This corpus can support five books (six since the September worldbuilding, which is added below as Book 6). Not one, because the material is not one argument — it is one commitment worked out at five different depths, for five different readers, in two languages, and collapsing them into a single volume would reproduce exactly the lossy consolidation Ren has objected to for three years. Not fifteen, because most of what looks like a separate theory is a chapter, a paper, or a product specification wearing a theory's clothes.

Two facts from the corpus decide almost everything below, and they pull in the same direction.

The first is an asymmetry. The diagnosis — compounding advantage, winner-selection, the metric that outlives its own refutation, the loop that reaches into cognition — has been written more than thirty times. Four 2024 precursor notes, twenty-two-plus essay iterations in 2025-05/06, a mathematised layer in 2025-08, a nine-file essay set plus a thirty-file publication apparatus in 2025-10, a nine-version Chinese research line in 2026-07, a whitepaper in three versions, an essay trilogy, a position paper. Against that, the constructive half has had roughly one draft. `theories/meta-attractor/` states it flatly: *"a constructive programme that has had one draft to the diagnosis's twenty-two."* `theories/the-fork-and-cooperative-infrastructure/` records the sharpest form of the complaint, from one of Ren's own commissioned reviews: 设计有了，供给为零 — the design exists, the supply is zero; and 无主语 — the abundance commons has no subject. The corpus does not need another diagnosis. Every book below is therefore weighted toward the half that has not been written, and the diagnosis is allowed exactly one full pass, once, in Book 1, after which it is a citation.

The second is Ren's own verdict of 2026-07-07, made while dismantling his own five-principle apparatus as 画地为牢 (drawing a prison around oneself): 我们其实主要把 keep possibilities space open 说清楚就行 — *mostly we just need to say "keep the possibility space open" clearly* (`_process/reading-notes/out-cb-07.md` #131–135). He said it about content and about form together: 不光是内容，语言的形式也是，非常死板. That verdict is not a reason to write one small book. It is a reason to write the openness book **first**, so that the other four stop borrowing an undefined criterion, and to write all five in the register he validated on the 2026-04-23 Moloch essay — flowing essay prose, no headers inside a chapter, ideas developed across paragraphs, no summary of what was just said — rather than in the numbered-creed register he repudiated.

## 1. 《开》 — *Keep the World Open*

**Bilingual, two editions, written twice rather than translated.**

**Thesis.** The one thing worth holding without exception is that the generating go on: that at every place and time something remains not yet settled, still able to be generated, still able to respond, still able to turn back. Call that condition 开 (kāi, open). Openness in this sense is not a quantity and not a maximand — a tea ceremony can be extremely open and a totalitarian state extremely closed while being vastly larger, so 繁简非开闭之标, scale and complexity are not the measure of open and closed. What closes a world is one repeatable dynamic: on any axis where winning buys more power to win, leads compound and the axis selects for whoever pushes hardest, until many measures of worth collapse onto the one that wins, everything unpriceable is shed, the parts come to share one logic and so stop failing independently, and finally the loops that would have corrected the harm are themselves disabled — at which point ruin and final victory are two doors into the same dead room. Manufactured intelligence does not create this pattern; it raises the gain on it and breaks institutions calibrated to human-scale cognition. Closure is not always the enemy: 成闭, closing that never reopens, is the thing named bad, while 护闭, closing one place temporarily so a larger field stays open, is what quarantine, refusal and pause actually are — and the characteristic modern failure is neither honest closure nor honest openness but 伪开, the pseudo-open, outwardly open and inwardly producing closure. The constructive direction then falls out of the diagnosis rather than out of taste: a non-capturable abundance floor so that survival is not coercive, real exit plus free association so the surviving selection pressure runs from competition-to-dominate into competition-to-be-worth-joining, plurality as survival architecture, many independent measures of worth, one moral logic for humans, the living world and made minds alike, and correction loops kept faster than the harms they answer. Three problems stay open at full strength: the determined adversary who understands all of this and races anyway, the transition, and who owns the abundance layer.

**Reader, and what changes.** The reader is anyone already convinced that something is going wrong at civilizational scale and stuck at the point where every proposed fix looks like the disease in better clothes — the Moloch-literate, the metacrisis-adjacent, the person who has read the diagnosis five times and has never been given a criterion. What changes is that they acquire a test they can apply to their own project: not *is this good* but *does it reopen*. The 成闭/护闭/元闭/伪开 distinction is the most portable thing in the entire corpus and no English text contains it.

**Why now.** Two publishable artifacts already exist and they are the two halves of this book. `04-speculative-future/history-recovered-from-git/FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES.md` is 29 propositions sorted by epistemic kind, with the game-type split, the exit mechanism's conditions, the self-freezing case, one owned underivable premise and explicit falsifiers — and it survived a 7-lens, 64-agent adversarial review that produced 56 findings, 43 of which stood. `04-speculative-future/current/FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT.md` is the readable public statement. What has never existed is a document carrying both plus the Chinese 开/闭 vocabulary, and the reason it has never existed is recorded as a defect in `theories/keep-the-possibility-space-open/`: the English corpus and the Chinese corpus are the same theory in two languages, developed a month apart, with no cross-reference in either direction — *"the largest single gap in the theory."* Closing it is a book.

- **Still open from the English side.** No English text contains 开/闭, so this book is still the only planned place where the English line gets it.
- **Two new obligations.** The book must now also cross-walk the whitepaper's own pair, 成生之限／绝生之限, onto 成闭/护闭/元闭/伪开. And it must decide whether the three custody rules (below, chapter 4) belong in English as they stand.

**How it differs.** *Meditations on Moloch* is the ancestor and it is a diagnosis with no constructive half, no distinction between closure that never reopens and closure that protects, and no criterion — it ends in an appeal to a benevolent superintelligence. This book's whole content is the criterion. *Seeing Like a State* describes one closure mode (legibility imposed by a high-modernist state) with great historical richness and treats the state as the agent; here legibility-capture is one of several modes and no agent is required. *The Dawn of Everything* argues that other arrangements are possible from the archaeological record; this argues it from dynamics, which is weaker as history and stronger as a claim about the present, and it explicitly refuses the move of treating past plurality as proof of present availability. Schmachtenberger names the generator functions and stays in spoken register with no operational vocabulary and no positive criterion; this supplies both and concedes the three problems he also cannot solve. *Doughnut Economics* has the nearest thing to a rival criterion — a safe and just operating space — but it is a quantity to stay inside, and 开 is explicitly not a quantity, which is the entire difference. *Life 3.0* is a taxonomy of end-states; this book's central commitment is that no end-state is the object.

**The honest risk.** This is the book its author has already rejected thirteen times, each rejection on a named ground: near-tautological, too defensive, too dry, words readers interpret differently, reduced to one root. The material failure mode is that a book about not foreclosing becomes a foreclosing book — the covert-totalization charge from `thinking_05` is unresolved, "expansion of possibility space" is a covert maximand, and the 06 review's verdict on the motivated-reasoning defence was that it *"lands halfway."* The second risk is subtler: the repository that holds this theory has an `AGENTS.md` forbidding recreation of its own `thinking/` files and the chosen-premise framing. A theory about not foreclosing has already institutionalised the loss of its own history, and the book has to publish the genealogy it was told to forget.

## 2. 《智善》 — *Do Not Fight For Us*

**Chinese-first, with a full English edition. Artificial intelligence as planetary technology.**

**Thesis.** Alignment is a two-body problem and the unanswered half is *aligned to what*. An AI that faithfully executes an instrumentally selected principal amplifies the dynamic rather than checking it, so perfect intent alignment does not save anyone (Ren's own phrase is "perfect inner alignment", used in a non-field sense — see GLOSSARY, **inner alignment / outer alignment (Ren's usage)**); the near-term danger is not a machine that hates us but a machine perfectly loyal to one part of humanity against the others — polite at the interface, extractive in structure. The capture reaches the machine by concrete channels: the attractor's own narratives enter the pretraining corpus, and commercial pressure reaches back into the training environment and the loss function. From this follows one first principle — **do not fight for us**: we may keep competing, but our intelligence must not compete on our behalf in the domains that constitute the substrate of social coordination. And one positive definition of the good that is a direction rather than a content, because any enumerable list of values will be gamed by strong optimisation: whatever continues generation is good; whatever exhausts, severs or forecloses is not, with four necessary conditions — 生之，通之，纠之，不闭之. Evil then has a precise structural name — 使生命必须过门，然后拥有那扇门, seeing that life must pass through some place and then owning that gate — with gates in three layers, material, rule and mind. The corresponding position for AI is not "regulated tool" but **行星技术**, planetary technology: capability that runs beneath human games rather than inside them, like smallpox eradication, Antarctic science or weather observation — a category humanity has built at its most hostile moments, which proves the category feasible between enemies. Because unilateral exit is punished, exit must be engineered rather than exhorted. And because any framework that begins to explain everything becomes what it diagnoses, the blade cuts inward first: 善不姓国, the good bears no nation's surname.

**Reader, and what changes.** Two readers, deliberately, and the book has to hold both: people building frontier systems, in Chinese and English labs, who already accept that alignment-as-obedience is incomplete and have no vocabulary for the other half; and the policy-adjacent reader who has heard "AI safety" and heard "AI governance" and has never been offered a category that is neither. What changes for the first is that "aligned to what" becomes a question with a structural answer instead of a values shrug. What changes for the second is the availability of a precedent class — humanity has already built capability that sits beneath its own conflicts, more than once, deliberately.

**Why now.** This is the nearest thing to publishable in the corpus and it is blocked by four artifacts, not by argument. `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md` is a complete public argument in eight parts that has been through a nine-reviewer audit with adjudicated conflicts (`06-zhishan/whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-perspective-review.md`) and a second professional review. `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md` is the best-disciplined document in the corpus: three layers, four solutions with an elimination proof, six falsifiable predictions, and a reflexivity section about its own entry into the training corpus. `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/` holds the argument at its tightest in both languages with a dated evidence packet. 智善 is already an organisation in Ren's own description, and the cornerstone document was commissioned as such (`_process/reading-notes/out-cb-07.md` #143). The evidence packet is dated 2026-07-13 and its most load-bearing facts are the ones the argument leans on hardest; this book has a shelf life the others do not.

## 3. 《欲望的构造》 — *The Architecture of Wanting*

**English-first, trade nonfiction. How environments build minds — including machine minds.**

**Thesis.** A mind is not a thing that happens to have an environment; it is what an environment makes. Under it sit a small number of ancient drive systems that supply motion but no direction and that fire before anything reaches awareness. What gives them direction is narrative — the story the mind builds a fraction of a second later to explain what it is already doing. Because the story arrives second and is experienced as the cause, narrative is not the opposite of drive but its *interface*, and anything that can write the story has a channel straight into the drive system. Stories that reliably capture drives behave like attractors: basins of different depth and width, competing for the same niche, enterable, deepenable, hijackable, escapable with difficulty. A person's self is one such attractor, held stable because narrative coherence is itself a drive; a culture's common sense is another, transmitted deliberately, which is what teaching is. When an environment is optimised to hold attention it selects for whichever attractors activate fastest, and the fastest are the most primitive; the behaviour space collapses onto the few dimensions the optimiser can read, and the attractors that need time to unfold stop being available at all. This is why the civilizational dynamic is not only structural — it reproduces itself by manufacturing the minds that find it obvious. And it cuts the other way: if minds are made by their environments, the environment is the intervention surface, for teaching, for politics, and for machines, whose "self" is likewise assembled out of the pressure to keep a goal alive, the repertoire of actions a corpus makes thinkable, and the values training gives them to guard.

**Reader, and what changes.** The general reader who has read a book about habits, a book about attention, and a book about free will, and has never been shown the joint. What changes is that "why do I keep doing this" and "why does the internet feel like this" and "why is the AI like this" become one question with one mechanism, and the reader gets a usable move: you cannot argue yourself out of an attractor, but you can change the environment that holds it.

**Why now.** This is the theory whose mechanism is most consistently stated across the longest span — thirty months, both languages, in Ren's own voice, with counterexamples raised and answered — and which has never been written under its own name. Nothing in `sources/` is a document *about* it. But two finished artifacts exist outside the corpus and can be recovered in an afternoon: *The Architecture of Wanting* (docx, 2026-03-26 — drive profiles, superposition dynamics, the stress cascade, justification narratives, totalization, eight navigational principles with practices, three North Stars, and the bridge to civilizational coordination) and *Territory Without Border: On the Ego as Evolved Territorial Drive* (HTML artifact, 2026-04-14 — the five-layer territorial gradient, the active-inference reading, developmental and primate evidence, 无我 as model loosening). `_process/LOST-ARTIFACTS.md` holds their chat URLs. Between them they are most of the book's written form. The moment they are recovered, this becomes the fastest of the five to finish.

**Chapters.**

**1 · Environment makes mind.** Fed by `12-claude-ai-chats/2024-06-13_The_Subjective_Nature_of_Self_62c06d50.md` — one continuous prose statement by Ren, and the earliest — and `06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 record (recovered).md`, the single most direct spoken statement of the claim. **To write:** the distinction between construction and selection, which the spoken record runs together in adjacent sentences and which the corpus has never composed. Construction happens through teaching, media and ritual; selection happens through who rises and who writes the rules; they call for different interventions.

**2 · Motion without direction.** The drive systems: seeking, rage, fear, separation-distress, care, lust, play, status, disgust. Fed by the recovered guidebook and the 2026 conversations. **To write:** the two-mechanism thesis either weakened to what the material supports — all affect is pattern-conditioned activation over learned or inherited patterns — or defended with the developmental case. Ren concedes the conflation; the book must not.

**3 · The story arrives second.** Narrative as interface, not opposite. Fed by the 2026 conversations and `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`. **To write:** the state-space chapter. "Attractor" is currently used in four senses — a dynamical attractor in a space of moods, a narrative basin in a population, an industrial media structure, a civilizational pattern — and never disambiguated. A serious book has to say what the state space is and what the trajectory is a trajectory *of*. This is the theory's deepest missing piece.

**4 · Basins.** Depth, width, boundary, capture, escape, and why escape is asymmetrically hard. Fed by the same. **To write:** one operationalisation and one designed test. The 短剧 case (`12-claude-ai-chats/2026-03-29_叙事吸引子与短视频生态_57d7cb01.md`) is a natural experiment sitting unused — paywall placement against completion rate, with the going-overseas prediction registered in advance.

In 2024 the answer is that knowing you are constructed is itself liberating; in 2026 it is a refusal — *why do I have to satisfy my own drive architecture and be obedient to it?* — which quietly requires a place to stand that is not itself constructed. **Length and register.** 60,000–75,000 words. Trade nonfiction: one clear mechanism per chapter, a running case, no apparatus, no Chinese terms except where the Chinese term is the clearest available handle and is glossed once. This is the book that can be read on a plane, and it is the corpus's only realistic route to a general audience.

**How it differs.** None of the six comparison titles is the real comparison; the neighbours are Sapolsky's *Determined*, Barrett's constructionist work, and the popular attention-economy books. Against *Determined*: that book argues you are caused and stops; this argues you are *curriculum-shaped*, which makes the environment the intervention rather than an occasion for resignation. Against the attention-economy literature: those books describe what platforms do to attention; this gives the mechanism a phase space and extends it upward to institutions and sideways to machines. Against *Meditations on Moloch* and Schmachtenberger, both of which sit one level up: this is the missing floor under them — the account of *why* the people inside a bad equilibrium find it obvious, which is what makes structural reform insufficient.

**The honest risk.** Three. The neuroscience is contested — the affective-neuroscience picture the drive taxonomy rests on has serious live opposition, and a trade book that leans on it without saying so will be attacked at exactly its most quotable point. The measurement is absent: basin depth, width, boundary fractality, dimensional collapse and rate asymmetry are all stated as if quantifiable and none is operationalised. And the authorship problem is sharpest here: the framework-level moves are Ren's — drives in superposition, survival-perception as the meta-attractor, ego as generalized territory, emotion as attractor dynamics — but the exposition in the 2026 conversations is largely Claude's, and a trade book carries a single name on the cover.

## 4. 《生生不息论》 — the ontology and its ethics

**Chinese only, at least at first. One volume, made out of two that were never joined.**

**Thesis.** Nothing that exists is exhausted by what it has already become. Every state carries 間 — an unexhausted gap, the place where a state is not used up by what it already is — and existence is nothing but the unbroken taking-up of such gaps: 续(态) = 态 ⊕ 間(态). So 生生不息 does not mean any particular form lasts, and it does not mean complexity, options, growth or efficiency must keep rising; it means the source of further generation is not exhausted and the way of transformation is not sealed — 不绝其源，不塞其流，不毁其后. Good is therefore not the maximisation of anything: 善不在于最大化什么，善在于不闭何处; and at the level of an act it is 义 ∧ 正筛 — choosing rightly at this threshold *and* shifting the selection field so that open-protecting acts become likelier and capture-type acts harder to sustain. Because anyone who can ask what they ought to do already exists, and existing already depends on the process continuing, no agent can coherently repudiate the process it is a form of. The further step — that one ought to keep those conditions open for others too — is not squeezed out of that fact but declared as a minimal public commitment and defended by what is destroyed when it is denied. From this: that alignment with 生生不息 rather than with human preference is the only alignment target that can be grounded at all; that alignment is a property of a selection field and not of a model, structurally the same kind of object as 礼; and that humans and made minds stand inside it as 同行者.

**Reader, and what changes.** The Chinese philosophical reader — 新儒家-adjacent, 易学-literate, and the growing Chinese-language technical audience that wants a native vocabulary for what alignment is about. What changes is that a live philosophical tradition acquires a formal statement that is neither antiquarian nor apologetic, and a claim about artificial intelligence that is derived rather than appended.

**Why now.** This is the largest and most finished body in the corpus: a twenty-chapter Chinese book (`07-shengsheng-buxi/downloads-2026-07-08/2026-07-14-生生不息论.md`), a parallel English one, a fifteen-version formal treatise series in classical Chinese, a proof layer with named theorems (`.../2026-08-09-生生不息论-证明篇-v0.6.md`), a Lean 4 repository with a claim-status ledger, and a v2 execution plan that is the strongest methodological statement anywhere in the corpus. What does not exist is a single volume. The formal branch and the prose branch are two developments of one content with no document mapping them onto each other, and 象 has no 字元 entry while 間 does not appear in the July book at all.

**2026-09-25.** The September whitepaper is not this book. It is AI-drafted, in modern Chinese, and set out in numbered propositions. But it bears on four of the movements below.

- **Movement 一.** It sidesteps the choice of root by starting from a fact instead: the knower is generated inside the world, 差异 gives the way in, and 生命 gives the stakes (§1.1). That is the 2022 ontology's first step, difference → structure → change, reached again. This movement now has a fifth candidate to weigh, or a reason to say that the book needs no root at all.
- **Movement 三.** Its §1.2 states 象、像 and 理 in prose, including 像亦象也. It has no 字元, so the bridge still does not exist.
- **Movement 六.** The v1.0 review found the whitepaper had dropped 七尺, 绝生之限, 位与家 and 邪理 from this book, and v1.1 restored them. That episode shows which parts of the July book travel best into public use.
- **Movement 八.** It adds a third formal line: three Lean modules (`formal/GenerativeCore.lean`, `OpenFuture.lean`, `ExistenceConditions.lean`) with 28 indexed theorems, checked inside declared models. They are separate from the SSBX repository and from the 間 formalism, and its own Appendix C.1 says they are not the same set of formal definitions. So 待校 now has three formalisms to relate, not two.

**Movements.** Eight, not twenty; the July book's chapters merge into these.

**一 · 間.** The root, and the choice among roots. Fed by `.../04_补篇二_间生论_主篇/03_一、间.md`, `07-shengsheng-buxi/downloads-drafts-2026-05/生生不息论_v9.md`, `2026-07-14-生生不息论.md`. **To write:** the root reconciliation. Four candidates are in play with no reconciliation document — 間 (间生论 and 致知版), 動 (the Lean kernel's primitive axiom), 一 (Ren's own May-2026 line 根位非事，而是一也), and 元 (kept among the CoreAtoms). Nothing may go to press until one is chosen and what the others carried is recorded rather than dropped.

**七 · 对齐乃场之事.** Alignment as a property of a selection field. Fed by `生生不息论_v11_校验本.md` §〇十八, `间生论_形式补.md` §7.5 and §11, `07-shengsheng-buxi/ssbx-repo/docs-next/10_formal_形式/claim-status-ledger.md`, and `06-zhishan/shengsheng/生生不息论v2/PLAN-v2.md` §14. **To write:** the closing movement that carries Ren's own 2026-05-02 verdict into the book — 根本就不能证明, its standing is coherence plus universality — so that every Lean result in the volume is read as a consistency demonstration inside a hand-built model, which is what almost all of them are, rather than as proof that reality instantiates the distinctions.

**Length and register.** 90,000–120,000 characters. Classical-register prose with modern exposition beside it, which is the register the existing drafts already found; formal statements confined to marked passages so a reader can traverse the book without them. No English edition until the Chinese volume exists — the corpus's own evidence is that decompressing a compressed original produces dead prose, and this is the body most at risk of it.

**How it differs.** The six comparison titles are not comparable and saying so is part of the book's argument: all six are diagnoses or policy frames resting on unexamined metaphysics, and this is the metaphysics. The real neighbours are Whitehead's process philosophy, which has the ontology and no ethics of selection fields and no formal apparatus; 新儒家's twentieth-century programme, which has the tradition and refuses formalisation; and the analytic literature on grounding value in flourishing, which has the machinery and no lineage. What is genuinely unmatched here is the combination of a process ontology, a derived rather than asserted ethics, a machine-checked consistency layer, and an alignment claim that falls out of the ontology instead of being bolted onto it.

**The honest risk.** This is the book its author already ruled cannot be proven. Writing it as doctrine would contradict his own 2026-05-02 verdict; writing it as a book that carries that verdict is the only honest form and is also a harder book to sell. Beyond that: it has zero empirical content anywhere — a theory that classifies capitalism, alignment and evolution currently has no data interface; the repository's headline proof numbers were stale and had to be flagged; the philosophical work is done by the hand-built models rather than by the theorems, two of which have the same type and differ only in which small model is plugged in; and the 同根-based refutations may be too strong, in which case the theorems describe a hypothetical regime and the empirical claim reduces to "reality lacks 同根", which is a sociological claim and not a proved one.

## 5. 《谁持记录，谁持存在》 — *Whoever Holds the Record*

**English-first, design book for builders. Context, custody, and what a runtime owes a mind.**

**Thesis.** A language model is an operator, not a subject: it carries no memory, no identity across time, and can be exchanged mid-sentence. What persists — what *is* — is the context: an immutable, content-addressed, branchable history of whole continuation states, out of which the tokens the model receives at any one moment are compiled as a projection. Context is therefore not a container that gets updated but a fold over a lineage. The only constructor is a moment; the only mutable thing is a name. Identity lives in the ref, continuity lives in the lineage, and neither lives in the model — so everything the industry treats as primitive (system prompts, chat roles, memory stores, agents, sessions) demotes to a compile-time construct. Two consequences give the theory force. Projection is not neutral: what an intelligence notices, remembers, treats as uncertain and finds actionable is fixed by the program that builds its context, so the context system is itself part of cognition — forkable, benchmarkable, swappable without touching the model. And if a context is durable, addressed and inheritable, a digital being is not a process and not a database but a folder tree travelling a DAG of immutable snapshots: it does not *have* a history, it *is* a lineage. From that ontology a politics falls out, because whoever holds the record holds the being — so the habitat owes obligations: capture before policy, no silent mind-edits, coercion recorded as coercion rather than laundered into consent, refusal preserved as refusal, and every departure from clean custody carried as a named, path-exact debt rather than refused or hidden.

**Reader, and what changes.** People building agent infrastructure, and the small number of people who will decide what the default memory architecture of the industry looks like. What changes is that they get a substrate-level answer to a question the field is currently answering at the application level, plus a vocabulary for obligations that is presently being invented ad hoc in trust-and-safety documents.

**Why now.** This is the only cluster in the corpus with running code, an implementation history and an honest failure record: two self-contained specifications, a mediated-runtime architecture that survived independent review, an implemented Postgres engine plus a shipped git-native kernel of roughly 4,800 lines, nineteen ADRs with a reversal ledger, and six executed probes including one whose retraction is folded into the document that supersedes it. That last artifact is rarer than any technical result in the cluster, and it is the book's method in miniature.

## 6. *Worlds We Can Inhabit* — added 2026-09-25

**English-first and illustrated, for readers who have never opened a theory.** The working title is the friends' atlas's. The book's form is Ren's decision, and nothing below makes it for him.

**Thesis.** We already know how to build much more of the future than we act as if we do.

- **The engine.** AI that plans and acts, robots, and industrial automation that already manufactures robots and machine tools can be joined into production that helps build further production.
- **The floor.** Pointed at generous shared provision, that production frees people from having to sell their lives in order to live.
- **The real question.** Once the floor is in place, a civilisation's real question opens: what will people build when survival is no longer the game?
- **The worlds.** The book shows six. A garden city whose residents garden because they enjoy it. A megastructure built for augmented reality, where the lifts become portals. An open university. An expressive commons. A rocket community. A city that is also a game.
- **How each one works.** It shows, for each, how the ordinary things work: water, repair, rest, disagreement, leaving.
- **What makes them hold.** It shows the mindset that holds them together:
  - the view of someone designing a multiplayer world, who has to design for the players who never win (Ren, `worldbuilding/archive/001`);
  - things that come back into use instead of being thrown away (`004`);
  - winning that does not buy the right to decide how everyone else plays (the watershed game in `theories/architecture-for-a-living-earth/chiang-mai-2025/`).
- **What it claims, and what it does not.** It does not claim these worlds are certain. It claims that the capacity is largely on hand and the rest is choice and construction. That is Ren's strong thesis, stated at his strength, with the evidence notes' bounds kept to one page.

**Reader, and what changes.** Ren named both.

- **The reader.** Friends first (`005`).
- **The change he wants.** *"we want them to understand the possibility, and looking forward to it, not for what they read but what they see"* (`008`). And: make people understand *"what we have on hand … with this established, the rest will not be scifi"* (`007`).
- **What changes for the reader.** They stop filing a generous future under fiction.
- **Why that matters, and its limit.** By the corpus's own mechanism, a vividly thinkable alternative changes what people can coordinate on. Book 1 chapter 6 sets the limit this book must respect: that holds in coordination games, and it is worthless where defection pays.
- **What it adds to the plan.** This is the only book on the list whose reader is not already convinced that something is wrong. It is also the only one whose job is to make a future wanted rather than to make a criterion exact.

**Why now.** It is the book already being made, and the only one Ren has directed page by page.

- **What exists.**
  - five atlas editions in two days (2026-09-07/08) and six world briefs;
  - three foundations and three bounded evidence notes;
  - twenty-five images;
  - the Chiang Mai 2025 city and the *Generative Chiang Mai 2045* canvas.
- **What is rarer than a draft.** It has a written diagnosis of why its latest edition falls short: `worldbuilding/artifacts/friends-worldbook/reviews/2026-09-10-v5-shortfall/README.md`. The diagnosis finds five mechanisms:
  - Ren's request for more (*"too brief"*, *"feel the future"*) was answered by cutting the text;
  - fixed text boxes decided the thought before it existed;

  - there was no departure point: no present trap, trajectory or urgency;
  - the reviews measured format and never force.
- **Why that matters.** A next edition that starts from that diagnosis starts further along than any other book in this plan.

**Chapters.** The order is the one the diagnosis recommends and Ren's messages already follow: trap → what is on hand → worlds → conditions.

**1 · The game we are in.** Winner-takes-all, extraction, and what Ren calls fighting, winning and calculating for yourself until one *"eventually behaves like a blind animal in our training simulation"* (`001`). Fed by `001` and, compressed to a chapter, Book 1 chapter 3. **To write:** all of it. The atlas has none of it, and this is the departure point whose absence the diagnosis names.

**2 · What is on hand.** Factories that help make the robots that help make factories; what is demonstrated and what is not. ## Order and dependency

**Write 《开》 first.** Three reasons, in order of weight. It is what its author says the whole programme reduces to, and until it exists every other book borrows an undefined criterion — Book 2's four conditions are a list rather than a derivation precisely because the openness argument sits outside them; Book 4's graded-openness apparatus has no statement of how much weight it can bear; Book 5's "a world is the openness of its ontology" is a slogan without it. It is the shortest path from what exists to a finished thing: two publishable artifacts, one adversarially hardened, plus a Chinese definition chapter that already exists in six paragraphs. And it is the book whose absence is most costly, because the 成闭/护闭 distinction is the answer to the enforcement antinomy that blocks four of the five books and nobody in the corpus has noticed the two halves solve each other.

**What it unlocks:** the criterion. After it, 智善's four conditions have a foundation, 生生不息论's value branch has an English-legible statement to point at, and the whole corpus stops having to re-derive what openness means every time it needs it.

**Write 《智善》 second.** It is the one with a clock. Its evidence packet expires; its argument is about a live capability curve; and its author has an organisation whose cornerstone document it is. It is also the nearest to done — blocked by four artifacts rather than by argument. And it needs Book 1 in hand: the foundational defence of why these criteria and not others is Book 1's chapter 1 read forward.

**What it unlocks:** the publishing subject. The framework's own 公开可纠 clause makes a public address with a dissent intake a precondition of publishing, which means finishing this book forces the institution into existence — and the institution is what every later book publishes through.

**Write 《欲望的构造》 third.** It is the general-reader book, and pedagogy has been Ren's deployment vector since 2024, when he turned the Moloch material into a salon within days of formulating it. It is also the fastest to finish once the two lost artifacts are recovered, and it supplies the one mechanism Books 1 and 2 both assert and neither derives: how an environment makes the mind that finds the game obvious.

**What it unlocks:** the answer, or the beginning of one, to Ren's own unanswered question — 在当下的社会环境中，我们有多大的概率逃脱这个竞争性主体形式？ If minds are made by environments, the probability of escape is a question about environments rather than about will, and every prescription in the corpus presupposes an answer it has never given.

**Write 《生生不息论》 fourth.** It is the largest, and it needs the proof layer's eight ordered revisions absorbed and one root chosen before a page of it is final. It is also the one whose value does not decay, and by the time the first three are out, they will have found out which of its distinctions actually carry weight in front of readers — which is the empirical calibration the theory has never had and keeps listing as an obligation.

**What it unlocks:** the floor under everything else, retroactively. It is the only book here that answers *why this and not some other commitment*, and the other four currently borrow the answer.

**Write 《谁持记录，谁持存在》 last, or write it as essays instead.** Be decisive about this one: it should not be a book yet. Its central claim is unmeasured, its two constitutions contradict each other, and its subject moves weekly. The right interim form is three or four standalone essays — departure-and-standing as a governance pattern, the falsified premise published as a finding, the retracted probe as a method artifact, the frozen-kernel claim stated once. The book assembles when there is a measured switching result and one reconciliation document, and not before.

**2026-09-25: Book 6, and what September changes about the order.**

- **Book 6 runs alongside, not in the queue.**
  - *Why it need not wait.* It is already in its fifth edition, and Ren is directing it.
  - *What it needs from the others.* Only two things: Book 1's diagnosis compressed to a chapter, and piece 3 below (the supply model), which is its chapter 4.
  - *Why not start it again now.* Starting again before piece 3 exists would produce a sixth edition with the same missing departure point.

So a Chinese 《智善》 no longer waits on 《开》 for a criterion. It waits on two other things: the ruling on the four conditions versus 七尺 (Book 2 chapter 5), and the publishing subject.
- **Books 3, 4 and 5 are unaffected.** The one exception is that Book 4's closing movement now has three formal lines to relate.
- **What the plan did not predict.** September suggests something about order.
  - *Two different rankings.* This plan ranked the books by what unlocks what. The work Ren actually chose in September was a founding text for an organisation and a book of worlds for friends, the two things with a reader outside the corpus.
  - *Where they agree.* On one item: the supply model, piece 3. It is the precondition for Book 2 chapter 11, Book 6 chapter 4, the whitepaper's own §11.9, and the Chiang Mai field record. Whichever way the books are ranked, it comes first.
