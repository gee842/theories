# Working in the theory repository

This repository is the primary home for Ren's theory and research. Start with `README.md`, `RESEARCH.md`, and `_process/BRIEF.md`.

- Write and revise substantive research here. Reuse the relevant existing study directory instead of creating another working copy in a dated chat workspace.
- Keep current formulations in `theories/`, study notes, proofs and reviews in `_process/research/`, and executable experiments in `experiments/`. Preserve imported originals and their provenance in `sources/`.
- `RESEARCH.md` is the current cross-branch index. Update it when a study is added, completed, or superseded, and connect substantive advances to the relevant theory README. Distinguish ongoing work from completed results and preservation from conceptual integration.
- Use repository-relative links inside documents. In messages to Ren, link to the repository file by its absolute path. Exported bundles are optional delivery snapshots, never the primary editing location.
- Temporary scratch and generated exports may live outside the repository. Before delivery, preserve any unique substantive notes, code, results, reviews, and figures in the appropriate repository location. Do not import dependency installations, interpreter caches, or redundant export archives by default.
- Preserve genealogy and original source bytes. Label portable reading editions and record link-only adaptations. Do not silently replace older results, erase counterexamples, or treat repeated copies as independent evidence.
- Distinguish Ren's words from AI elaboration; preserve Chinese and English as equally primary. Keep assumptions, scoped proofs, empirical evidence, and open conjectures distinct.
- Multiple sessions may work here concurrently. Inspect status before editing and committing. Preserve unrelated edits and commit only the work owned by the current task. Do not relocate active workspaces or rewrite another session's work to impose this layout.
- Verify links and preservation hashes when importing or reorganizing research. Rerun scientific checks only when substantive changes, dependencies, or unresolved concerns justify it; organization alone does not constitute new validation.
