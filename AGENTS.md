# Working in the theory repository

This repository is the primary home for Ren's theory and research. Start with `README.md`, `RESEARCH.md`, and `_process/BRIEF.md`.

**Current research goal:** follow [generative grounding](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). Seek a minimal generative foundation with universal explanatory scope wherever systems form, and derive organization, significance, learning, selection over selectors, concern and wisdom within that account. Connect each study to this derivation; distinguish foundational premises from supplied local-model inputs. Explain constructive and destructive regimes while preserving differentiated centers and plural possibilities. Universal favorable direction remains a further hypothesis, not the definition of the whole goal or an established result.

**Continuity toward completion:** keep this objective across sessions and handoffs until the [completion conditions](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#completion-of-the-unified-foundation) are met or Ren redirects the work. Select foundational studies by the unresolved derivation they can advance. State which transition is being addressed, which premises remain supplied, and what result would change the account. When a study closes, integrate what it establishes or rules out into the derivation map and `RESEARCH.md`, with the next unresolved bridge. Local completion, formal verification, source preservation and a finished exposition each have their own scope; none alone completes the unified foundation. Revise candidate explanations when evidence requires it, preserving the failed claims and counterexamples. Do not silently substitute a convenient model, application, or favorable-direction theorem for the governing goal. This follows [Ren's continuity directive](sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-unified-generativity-goal-and-continuity.md).
