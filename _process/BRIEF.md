# Brief for readers of this corpus

**Current research goal, 5 October 2026.** Ren asks us to find the generative root from which the many particular rules and relationships can be derived. The [governing position](../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) seeks a minimal foundation across system-forming reality, explaining organization, significance, learning, selection over selectors, shared concern and wisdom within the same account. Its [derivation map](../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#the-derivation-and-the-existing-work) connects existing results to the remaining explanatory tasks. Local models supply partial mechanisms; their positive anchors, objectives and favorable arrangements are further things to explain. Preserve plural centers, conflicts and histories within the common foundation. Universal explanatory scope and inevitable moral improvement are different claims. See [Ren's current directive](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-05-generative-root-research-goal.md) and the [current research index](../RESEARCH.md).

AuDHD cognitive profile treated as a system property. Many texts are AI-assisted drafts written from Ren's ideas; the ideas are Ren's, the prose often is not.

**How to read.** Distinguish (a) Ren's own claims and turns of thought (user messages, hand-written notes, poems, spoken records), (b) AI elaborations Ren accepted/kept, and (c) AI reviews/critiques. Prefer the latest formulation when versions conflict, but preserve what earlier versions had that later ones dropped (Ren has explicitly complained about lossy consolidation: "every time you update you also delete something important"). Chinese and English texts are equally primary.

**Style Ren holds.** Wisdom, elegance, rationality, completeness, self-consistency over dogma; mechanism first, conclusion after; no aphorism-first writing; concepts as handles not sacred vocabulary; affirmative over negation; systems-dynamics grounding; do not smuggle acceleration grammar; preserve plurality while seeking a common generative root; do not operationalize prematurely.
