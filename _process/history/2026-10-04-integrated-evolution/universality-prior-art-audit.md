# Universal constraints, prior art, and discriminating tests

4 October 2026. AI-generated research audit for Ren's continuing investigation of selection and the conditions of continued becoming. This is a targeted primary-source review and constructive mathematical assessment, not a systematic literature review, an originality determination, or empirical validation. No experiments were run. ## The strongest defensible universal core

The candidate principles survive if stated as **conditional necessities with identifiable premises**:

1. An activity that irreversibly depletes an indispensable, finitely buffered support cannot continue at a specified positive rate indefinitely unless depletion is offset, the support is replaced, or the activity changes its dependency.
2. Competitive success alone does not certify viability when the observations defining success leave viable and nonviable states indistinguishable.
3. Correction remains available only while the conditions actually needed to implement it remain available; their maintenance can be internal, collective, external, or achieved through substitution.

These are potentially useful restrictions on every model satisfying the premises. They are not yet a distinctive predictive theory. Scientific content comes from identifying indispensable supports, measuring their depletion and restoration, explaining how selection changes those rates, and predicting interventions before observing their consequences.

The distinction between **activity at a positive rate**, **mere nonextinction**, and **preserved ability to resume activity** is essential. A system approaching dormancy can have finite cumulative consumption and infinite mathematical lifetime without continued replenishment. A finite-duration project can legitimately consume reserves. An ideal conserved catalyst need not be replaced. External supply can maintain a subsystem that does not reproduce its own enabling conditions. A new technology can eliminate dependence on an old support. None refutes the restricted statement; all refute stronger slogans requiring every component to regenerate itself forever.

Chemical Organization Theory is particularly important for positioning. It explicitly distinguishes a cycle in which each consumed component has a production route from a network admitting simultaneous maintenance. Its example of interconversion with decay shows why a support graph can appear closed while every component vanishes. Its allowed static fluxes need not match the trajectory a given kinetic system reaches. That yields a useful hierarchy: **production paths → feasible simultaneous flows → dynamically reachable persistence → persistence under selection and institutional change**. The latter transitions are where a carefully specified extension could contribute.

## Three precise restrictions worth deriving and using

**Finite-buffer restriction.** If a support stock obeys \(\dot B=u-d\), viability requires

\[
\int_0^T(d-u)\,dt\le B(0)-B_{\min}
\]

for every horizon during which \(B\ge B_{\min}\). If total future replenishment is at most \(U_\infty\) and activity requires \(d\ge d_{\min}>0\), its duration is bounded by \([B(0)-B_{\min}+U_\infty]/d_{\min}\). This is accounting, not an independent law of evolution. The scientific question is whether the chosen \(B\), loss floor, and supply bound are independently justified and remain valid under innovation and substitution.

For multiple stocks, a nonnegative weighting can provide an impossibility certificate. If every admissible continuation at the required activity level satisfies \(y^\top\dot B\le-\epsilon\), with \(y\ge0\), while the protected weighted reserve is finite, no such continuation can last beyond that reserve divided by \(\epsilon\). This is a direct differential inequality, related to standard feasibility and duality reasoning. It accommodates alternative production routes if the admissible set actually includes them. Finding one feasible steady flux is weaker than proving that it can be reached before reserves run out.

**Observation restriction.** Let \(s(Y)\) be a success observation. If states \(Y_1,Y_2\) have equal \(s\) but different viability, that observation cannot correctly classify both. A success class containing a nonviable state cannot certify viability for every member. This does not imply that all indicators fail: a certificate or sufficiently informative observation can establish viability within a validated model. The research task is to characterize the missing information and measure the value of adding it.

**Implementation restriction.** Let controls satisfy \(u\in U(C,S)\), where \(C\) is competence and resources and \(S\) includes authority. An existence proof over controls establishes possible correction only. Actual correction also requires the institutional policy to choose and implement an admissible action in time. Maintaining the physical control channel does not establish its legitimacy, and granting formal authority does not establish competence or enforceability. These distinctions can become independently testable failure mechanisms.

## What persistence still fails to capture

Open-ended transformation can preserve a function by replacing its carrier, revise the function, or make a new function possible. Requiring every old component or capability to survive would prohibit many forms of development. Conversely, allowing arbitrary redefinition of “the function” after failure makes regeneration unfalsifiable. Specify in advance which functions are protected, which can be replaced, what evidence counts as functional equivalence, and who may authorize revisions. Permit explicit comparison between successor regimes rather than demanding identical coordinates forever.

A fixed grammar can represent unbounded programs or structures; therefore open-endedness does not require a state space outside mathematics. A finite fixed inventory of types, however, cannot establish indefinite structural innovation. Novelty counts alone are inadequate: randomness, exploits, and new forms of domination may increase novelty while reducing protected capacities. The [open-ended evolution analysis by Taylor](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883) is directly relevant here.

There remains an ethical gap between necessary conditions for any continuing process and obligations to sustain particular processes. Tumors, predatory organizations, and oppressive institutions also have maintenance conditions. People may choose to end an institution or retire a capability. Continued becoming can be a substantive philosophical commitment, but mathematics will not by itself decide whose becoming deserves protection, how losses are distributed, or which transitions are legitimate.

## Three discriminating experiments

The balance identities cannot be falsified while retaining their definitions. Their proposed **mechanistic applications** can and should be falsified. Predeclare parameter estimates, intervention contrasts, and failure criteria.

### 1. Separate favorable selection from support restoration

Use a public-good system with measurable abundance, producer share, and support stock. Randomize returns to production independently of direct support replenishment, and repeat at several initial absolute densities. Include a condition fixing composition, so changes caused by altered selection can be separated from direct ecological effects.

**Discriminating observations:** producer share rises without recovery at low reserves; direct replenishment rescues at fixed composition; or favorable selection rescues only after a predicted threshold or delay. These distinguish relative selection, absolute-stock limitation, and maintenance-flow effects. Conversely, if predicted thresholds fail out of sample, or direct replenishment has no effect despite measured support limitation, reject the specified coupling. The [Sanchez–Gore yeast experiment](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.1001547) supplies a biological precedent, not a prior run of this exact factorial design.

### 2. Separate correctness, competence, authority, and selected obstruction

In an AI or human experimental institution, match initial task performance and oversight budget. Independently vary training/practice of correctors and enforceable authority to act on verified evidence. Introduce standardized, independently adjudicated faults at later times. In a second layer, randomize whether successful actors may influence appointment or amendment rules; include exogenous replay and beneficial amendments.

**Discriminating observations:** faults detected but uncorrected indicate an implementation or authority failure; undetected faults with preserved enforcement indicate an epistemic failure; appointment control causally reducing later correction beyond replay supports endogenous obstruction. Restoration of authority alone versus training alone identifies different mechanisms. Equivalent correction despite the manipulation rejects the proposed vulnerability in that environment. Preregister legitimate targets so resistance to an illegitimate command is not labeled misalignment.

### 3. Separate renewal of function from preservation of its original carrier

Choose a repair or provisioning function with two independently verified ways to perform it. Remove the original provider. Randomize whether participants can access and develop a substitute, matching transition resources. Measure service delivery, recovery time, additional useful capabilities, and effects on protected stakeholders. Then withdraw the emergency subsidy and test another disturbance.

**Discriminating observations:** continuing function after provider replacement supports functional regeneration over identity preservation; failure after subsidy withdrawal reveals externally buffered continuity; successful repeated recovery indicates an established replacement process. Mere artifact proliferation without improved capabilities fails the innovation criterion. This tests a bounded case of constructive transformation; no finite run demonstrates unlimited open-ended evolution.

## Research positioning

The broad intuition has strong precedents. The opportunity is to connect four distinct layers that are often separated in applications: support feasibility, actual renewal dynamics, selection among practices, and the reproduction or replacement of institutions that keep intervention available. A useful contribution should predict which link fails, what observation exposes it, and which intervention repairs it before the relevant deadline. Preserve successful competition, legitimate retirement, substitution, and failed rescue as expected possibilities, alongside degeneration and regeneration.
