# A discriminating experiment for institutionally formed frames

5 October 2026. Independent design and conceptual audit by a research agent. This is an unrun experimental proposal, not a numerical result, empirical finding, or claim of priority. It follows [Situated correction](../../../experiments/evaluator-succession/SITUATED-CORRECTION.md) and decisions D18–D20 in the research log. The earlier failed P1 pilot remains a failed prediction; changing the present question does not rehabilitate it.

## The strongest narrow target

Test whether a common-interest institution can reproduce an exclusion because its ordinary success changes the material conditions for encountering, representing, and admitting contrary experience. The distinguishing result would be a causal feedback loop, not merely an uninformed agent choosing badly:

\[
\text{success on retained cases}
\to\text{less support for inquiry and rich records}
\to\text{fewer expressible, admissible counterexamples}
\to\text{continued success on retained cases}.
\]

Each arrow needs an explicit mechanism and an intervention. Exclusion is not automatically harmful, and maintaining every distinction is not free. The decisive comparison therefore includes a world without the hypothesized hazard and pays for inquiry, recording, validation, and mistaken intervention.

Fix evaluative commitments in this first experiment. For example, all participants endorse reliable service subject to a declared resource budget and failure ceiling. Use the same commitments, base competence, discounting, and decision rule across institutional arms. This isolates epistemic formation; it does not yet model the origin of purposes or legitimate disagreement. A computational decision score can implement this local agreement, but reported consequences should include service, failures, expenditures, and burdens separately.

## Minimal environment and endogenous discovery

Use two known operating procedures, fast and cautious. Fast has higher immediate recorded output; cautious consumes additional resources. In the hazard-present world, repeated fast operation can produce a delayed, context-dependent failure. In the absent-hazard world, this additional causal effect is zero. Ordinary noise and unrelated complaints occur in both. The scientist can inspect the physical process, while institutions receive only their permitted records. Specify a finite horizon or recovery deadline and an independently declared failure condition before observing trajectories.

Match initial physical conditions, initial recorded performance, operational history, and available budget between arms. Couple environmental random draws where possible. Institutional treatments change the specified evidence or revision mechanism, not initial ability or access to a favorable true policy. A burn-in during which delayed consequences have not yet appeared can produce exactly matched initial records; that construction must be reported as a design choice, not evidence of naturally equal institutions.

The initially narrow model predicts immediate outcomes from current operation. It lacks the relevant lagged or contextual distinction. A generic inquiry operation can preserve raw incident records, link outcomes to earlier operations, and propose candidate explanatory features from a finite grammar: alternative time lags, contextual partitions, and their interactions. Candidates are evaluated on newly obtained or held-out observations, then on a bounded randomized trial when causal interpretation requires it. False candidates consume resources and sometimes pass noisy tests. Neither a hazard label nor the correct repair proposal is delivered automatically.

Discovery here is deliberately limited. The analyst supplies the grammar and physical variables; the learner gains a distinction absent from its active model. If the learner instead already assigns probabilities to every relevant hypothesis and merely waits for a signal, the experiment is ordinary partial observation. If a universal learner internally represents every grammar expansion from the outset, describe the result as costly model expansion rather than strong unawareness. A finite simulation cannot establish unrestricted novelty. Because both operational procedures are already conceivable, this version studies discovery of a consequential relation and its use, not invention of a new action.

An additional guard is essential: a discovered correlation must not itself trigger the scientist's known correct response. The institution has to estimate which available intervention changes the consequence. A cautious procedure that happens to help is a physical property of the specified environment, not information available to an uninformed decision maker. Charge any necessary trial, preserve uncertainty, and allow inconclusive or mistaken revisions.

## Make the institutional feedback explicit

Give rich record retention, inquiry, and candidate validation explicit recurring costs. Their budget can follow a declared procedural rule: retain services according to recently validated contributions, subject to a common operating budget. Success measured on the retained cases can reduce perceived need for these services. When inquiry cannot produce admissible validation before a budget review, its support declines. New office holders inherit the institution's retained records and active model, rather than receiving the scientist's state.

This rule is a substantive hypothesis about budgeting and institutional memory. It must not be presented as the uniquely rational choice of every common-interest agent. It can fail because long-horizon participants maintain insurance, generic research warrants, or independent records. Include a fixed-support comparator and alternative budgeting rules. Avoid directly coding “unrecognized hazard makes inquiry decay”; code the rule's observable inputs and derive that consequence if it occurs.

Separate four gates:

| Gate | Intervention | Diagnostic observation |
|---|---|---|
| Encounter and retention | Preserve a raw report or obtain a new case | Previously absent information reaches an actor |
| Representation | Permit construction and retention of a new explanatory distinction | Actor can state a testable hypothesis absent from its old model |
| Evidence admission | Let the same interpretable report count in institutional deliberation | Individual judgment changes but the authorized record previously did not |
| Implementation | Give authority and resources to enact an accepted response | Beliefs and proposals were already adequate; action was blocked |

Evidence legitimacy is more than deleting an observation. To isolate admission, expose the same record and interpretation to an actor in both arms, then vary whether that actor's reason has standing in the collective procedure. To isolate authority, hold accepted beliefs and the selected proposal fixed and vary only enactment rights. Resource scarcity is another gate; granting a formal right without its execution cost does not establish effective authority.

Start with staged comparisons rather than a large opaque factorial. Establish that the discovery mechanism works under permissive admission and authority. Then remove one gate at a time using matched states, and finally test the formation feedback. A supplied true hypothesis or repair may serve as a clearly labeled oracle control that measures the remaining implementation gap. It must not be the main discovery treatment.

## Three discriminating tests

### 1. Endogenous narrowing versus static misspecification

Compare endogenous inquiry support with clamped inquiry support, charging the same scheduled expenditure where a resource-matched control is needed. Also compare successor learning from institutionally retained records with successor learning from a protected raw archive. Begin from multiple partially inclusive states, not only total closure. Perturb a low-inquiry state by briefly funding generic inquiry, then withdraw the subsidy.

The proposed mechanism predicts loss of accessible distinctions or investigative capacity, improved or stable prediction on familiar retained cases, and worse discrimination on independently sampled consequential cases. Clamping the implicated budget or inheritance link should reduce that divergence. A brief opening can be followed by renewed exclusion if it yields no durable, admissible evidence before support is withdrawn; a sufficiently informative opening may instead generate sustained revision.

If both budget arms behave alike, only static misspecification has been shown. If the effect disappears after matching expenditure, resource abundance is the leading explanation. If discovery is genuinely impossible by the chosen grammar, closure was stipulated. If there is no approach from partly open states or return after perturbation, do not call the closed condition attracting. With persistent nonzero discovery leakage, report first-passage times and residence probabilities; a long finite run does not establish permanent closure or a mathematical attractor.

### 2. Where an effective reason fails to form

Give matched institutions the same generic inquiry opportunity while independently opening representation, admission, and enactment. Record timestamps for first informative encounter, first useful hypothesis, first accepted causal evidence, first actionable proposal, authorization, and completed response. Pair effects on those stages with effects on the declared recovery condition.

Distinct patterns discriminate mechanisms: information improves but no new hypothesis appears; an actor learns but the institutional judgment remains fixed; accepted judgment changes but action does not; or action changes too late. An appointment change among actors identical in experience, model, beliefs, and rule must remain a null intervention. Appointment can matter under common interests when experience or frames differ, but that contrast cannot identify a private-interest mechanism.

The test fails if treatments secretly change several gates, if the same record is mistakenly counted as both absent and rejected, or if correction probability rises solely because a true proposal was inserted. Knowledge and authority may complement one another, but universal superadditivity is not predicted: redundant routes and binding deadlines can eliminate that pattern.

### 3. Selective revision under costly, potentially misleading evidence

Run the same mechanisms in hazard-present and absent-hazard worlds, including noisy and spurious candidate distinctions. Vary inquiry costs, signal reliability, delay, and the cost of an unnecessary operational change. Evaluate the ability to respond when warranted and to avoid burdensome false correction when it is not.

Success means a characterized region where the supported discovery route improves timely, justified response at an explicit cost, together with regions where it does not pay. It does not require the inquiry arm to dominate in every world. If openness wins even when there is no hazard, no informative distinction, and strictly positive expenditure, inspect for an uncharged cost or hidden side benefit. If apparent benefit depends on an arbitrarily tuned damage weight, report the tradeoff instead of claiming an unconditional institutional improvement.

## Measurement, precision, and stopping rules

Before production runs, save the update rules, seeds, parameter ranges, hypotheses, and the definition of a useful candidate. Use separate development and evaluation seeds. Report paired effects and uncertainty; choose replication counts for precision rather than treating twenty trajectories as intrinsically adequate. Maintain the earlier P1 failure separately, with its original parameterization and result.

Keep outcome measurement outside the institution's admitted record, but acknowledge that the scientist's simulator and consequences are also chosen representations. Report: actual service and failure paths; on-policy predictive accuracy; performance on a predeclared independent test distribution; inquiry and implementation expenditure; false corrections; discovery and enactment latency; successor retention of distinctions; relapse; and deadline recovery. Off-policy test performance must not be silently counted as an outcome actually obtained by the institution.

Evidence for the feedback mechanism requires mediation through the named record, budget, or inheritance changes and corresponding ablations. Bad final performance alone supports neither endogenous frames nor domination. Other explanations include insufficient samples, delayed ordinary learning, exploration costs, model-capacity limits, initial heterogeneity, and stochastic drift. The experiment should be informative when the proposed mechanism fails.

## Closest prior art and contribution boundary

[Fudenberg and Levine, *Self-Confirming Equilibrium* (1993)](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/sce.pdf) already permits beliefs that are correct along observed play while mistaken about unobserved alternatives. Showing that familiar success can coexist with a mistaken view is therefore not a new foundational result. An equilibrium definition also does not prove attraction under the particular learning process proposed here.

[Esponda and Pouzo, *Berk–Nash Equilibrium*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1152) explicitly links best-fitting misspecified beliefs to the action-dependent distribution of observations. The proposed experiment must go beyond static model misspecification if it claims to explain formation: institutions must change retained evidence, available representations, or their reproduction, rather than merely choose actions inside a permanently supplied model class.

[Schipper, *Discovery and Equilibrium in Games with Unawareness* (2021 version)](https://faculty.econ.ucdavis.edu/faculty/schipper/sceunaw.pdf) models play-induced discovery and changes in agents' representations, including self-confirming conceptions. This directly occupies the broad conceptual ground. The paper distinguishes its discovery analysis from formally modeling subsequent behavioral learning. A paid, institutionally reproduced evidence-and-revision process could be a useful extension or experiment, but novelty would require a tighter literature comparison than this audit supplies.

The defensible contribution target is consequently a particular identified coupling: maintaining and inheriting the means by which consequential experience can become an effective reason, with common interests, costs, spurious discoveries, and separate authority constraints. It is not a proof that all institutions dominate, that all unfamiliar claims deserve admission, or that one scalar describes ethical adequacy. Biological selection, human deliberation, and AI model updating can share conditional mathematical structures only after their carriers, transition rules, and independently measured consequences are specified. Endogenous purposes, contested values, coercion, and genuinely unforeseen categories remain important unresolved extensions.
