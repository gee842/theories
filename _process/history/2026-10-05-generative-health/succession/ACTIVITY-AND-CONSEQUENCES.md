# How effects participate in further activity

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [Ren's main question](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-activity-consequence-reorganization.md), under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **effects of activity → changed conditions of continuation → changed organization of subsequent activity**. This synthesis connects already established mechanisms and constraints; it reports no new simulation, theorem certification or empirical result.

## The explanatory relation

**An effect participates in changing activity when it changes an operative relation through which subsequent activity proceeds.** The effect need not first become a judgment, representation or reward. It can change the availability of a material, the functioning of a connection, the persistence of a pattern, or which further encounters are possible. These are particular realizations; none supplies the universal inventory.

The two parts of Ren's question are related within one history. Activity changes what is available or possible next. Where those changes reach the organization through which further activity is produced, they also change how continuation occurs. The operative organization is itself among the conditions that activity can maintain, damage, form or replace.

“Its own organization” is a claim about a continuing history and its relations, not an assertion that a primitive owner commands the process. Its effective range may overlap other ranges or change through the activity. An analysis must identify what continues and what changes instead of assuming a fixed actor/world boundary.

This yields three distinguishable questions about the same effects:

| Question | What must be explained |
|---|---|
| What can continue? | Which actual conditions enable, constrain or interrupt a specified continuation. |
| How will activity proceed? | Which operative relations produce the subsequent response, inquiry or formation. |
| What can change how it proceeds? | How earlier consequences participate in maintaining or revising those operative relations. |

The third does not demand an endless stack of supervisors. A connection that directs later construction can itself be formed or altered through interactions. A fixed encompassing law can describe that change. Explaining the origin and adequacy of a particular law remains a further question; the law's fixed description does not erase the changes it describes.

## From changed conditions to changed organization

An existing repair history gives an elementary example. Damage makes repair applicable when its other conditions hold; actual restoration removes the damage condition and restores the capture relation. Fresh capture still requires its source and storage conditions. The activity changes its own applicability. This requires no separate judgment that the repair has finished. Its event laws, components and support conditions are supplied by the local construction. See the [source reconstruction](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-INTEGRATION.md).

That example establishes consequential regulation, without claiming a learned revision of the repair mechanism. A stronger example appears in [autonomous method formation](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSEQUENCE-ORGANIZATION-MECHANISM.md). Actual acquired observations contradict every candidate produced through a first formed connection. The connection is removed and replaced. The successful trial result is then discarded: later candidate generation must traverse the retained replacement and recreate its result. Breaking the replacement blocks later generation despite the preserved historical acceptance label.

Here the consequence reaches the procedure that produces further candidate knowledge. The changed connection is used, not merely described as accepted. The available generator recipes, acquisition practice, compatibility test and construction mechanism remain supplied. They constrain what this particular history can explain.

A separate [matched method-revision history](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-INTERFACE-ARGUMENT.md) reaches subsequent encounters. Under matched physical circumstances and an identical receipt, permitting a method update changes acquisition from three readings to four and lengthens the complete native history. A matched write that leaves the method unchanged does not. The extra acquisition initially coincides with the narrower method's later prediction test, so this is a change of stopping point and total continuation, not immediate divergence of the primitive action.

That signal is misleading and the extra inquiry is unnecessary in the comparison. **Consequences can reorganize activity without making its continuation better.** This adverse case is part of the explanation. The two method histories are separate continuations from a shared acquired-H3 checkpoint; they are not spliced into one achieved developmental sequence.

## What distinguishes reorganization from a changed description

For a claim at a stated scale, identify four relations:

1. The operative arrangement before and after, specified by what it enables or constrains rather than by a success label.
2. The actual earlier effect and the route through which it changes that arrangement.
3. A subsequent difference in possibility, response or formation attributable to the changed arrangement.
4. The actual history and conditions connecting these stages, including any intervention supplied by an investigator.

These are explanatory requirements, not a universal learning algorithm. Mere persistence does not demonstrate reorganization. Depletion can change behavior without retaining a discrimination about earlier consequences. Passive remodeling can be real reorganization without intention or representation. A stronger claim of acquired discrimination must identify the relevant history, its retained effect and its later use. A recorded consequence that never reaches response cannot establish that specific causal connection, even though the consequence remains real.

An appropriate rival is response to current circumstances plus nonspecific change. Compare histories and retained mechanisms while preserving the present circumstances relevant to that rival. Do not demand identical complete physical states while expecting different learning: that would erase the retained difference under investigation. Nor does disrupting one redundant route establish that all consequence-mediated influence is absent.

One natural comparator supports the possibility of changing a change-producing relation. In acute rat hippocampal slices, earlier stimulation of one pathway altered the plasticity later induced in another pathway. The prior activity affected how later input changed the system. This was not a demonstration of improved organismal continuation; the persistent expression mechanism remained unresolved. [Hulme et al. 2012, primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6622209/); [preserved text and provenance](../../../../sources/39-consequence-organization-2026-10-06/README.md). This round reread the preserved primary text; live DOI retrieval failed and PMC returned a browser check. No figures or raw data were newly analyzed. The [existing comparator review](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSEQUENCE-ORGANIZATION-COMPARATORS.md) retains the induction controls and their limits.

## The relation to significance and health

An effect can matter to an activity before it is represented by that activity. Where continuation depends on a relation, disrupting and restoring that relation have different consequences for continuation. That dependency supplies the proposed organizational basis of significance. When the difference enters a response that changes the relevant conditions, it acquires an operative role in regulation. When retained consequences change how later responses are formed, the account can investigate learning and revision within the same history.

These transitions are conditional. Dependency alone does not guarantee that a corrective route forms, remains available or acts in time. Adequate evidence can coexist with blocked access. A changed procedure can become better at suppressing unwelcome consequences or sustaining one organization through another's exhaustion. The [continuity-and-correction result](CONTINUITY-AND-CORRECTION.md) already shows a specific case where, under an independently identified prefix requirement, every unchanged route to a response violates a condition it is meant to preserve. Selecting more favorably among those routes cannot remove their common obstruction.

The stronger candidate for generative health is therefore **renewing the conditions of continuation while preserving effective ways for consequential changes to correct how continuation is organized, where such correction is needed**. Renewal can involve repair, replacement, changed dependence or ending an incumbent practice. Stable conditions may permit an adequate fixed response; constant novelty or perpetual inquiry is not required.

This candidate directs the inquiry rather than settling its ethical grounds. Shared health requires following the differentiated lives, experiences and possibilities involved. The persistence of a larger organization cannot stand in for those histories. A general generative account explains harmful reinforcement as well as constructive correction; concern and wisdom require the further formation and justification of responsiveness to those consequences.

The recent [inquiry-sufficiency result](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-INTEGRATION.md) shows why a count of unresolved alternatives is insufficient. After readings `010`, two candidates agree on the next reading. That reading occurs and eliminates neither. The activity advances their positions, however, and the same two candidates now disagree about the newly next reading. Earlier knowledge remains true while its sufficiency for what follows changes.

The next developmental connection is **effects on the relevant continuing activity → acquired sensitivity to which differences or dependencies require inquiry → subsequent activity governed by that understanding**. In the separate prediction-method study, the existing method changes its inquiry duration under a fixed rule requiring one surviving candidate. It has not thereby acquired when further discrimination matters or revised that rule through inquiry's consequences. The old all-future prediction contract remains legitimate within its declared scope; one-step agreement alone does not authorize abandoning it. In the new concern history, the encounter limit is revised, while the significance assigned to two occupied sockets remains a supplied sufficient trigger.

A positive result must identify the relevant ongoing activity independently of a preferred answer, trace how effects on it alter an operative criterion, and show subsequent use of that changed criterion through the actual history. It must distinguish appropriate restraint from loss of access, and effective reconsideration from conformity to an observer's newly supplied target. Misleading and harmful revisions remain admitted possibilities.

The advance here is the connected explanatory answer and these discriminations, credited to their existing mechanisms. The further obligation is to explain acquired relevance through the relations between consequences, actual dependencies and available responses. It neither requires an evaluator outside generative reality nor postpones the already completed local admission-revision result.
