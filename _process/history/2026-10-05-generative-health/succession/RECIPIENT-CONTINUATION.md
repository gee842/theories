# What a delivered consequence enables for its recipient

**Foundational formulation, revised 6 October 2026:** **activity changes the conditions through which other activity can continue.** To explain that relationship, identify what changes, which ongoing activity depends on it, and how the change enables or interrupts that activity. Following [Ren's vocabulary correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-foundational-vocabulary-correction.md), the account below starts from those relations. Its selected numerical models already distinguish positions named `actor` and `recipient`; they do not derive those positions or establish a prior division into giver and receiver.

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [nondual evaluation reconstruction](NONDUAL-EVALUATION.md), under the [shared method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **a change produced by activity → its effect on an affected continuation → a distinction that evaluation and response can answer**.

This round gives a conditional correspondence test, using the existing resource theorem. It does not introduce a new controller, rerun the experiments, establish the physiology of an affected being, or derive ethical standing from a stock quantity. Its contribution is to make the consequence side of the shared criterion-revision inquiry more precise.

## The positive connection

A difference can matter to an organization before it is represented as valuable. If an activity requires an accessible support, loss of that support can interrupt the activity; actual provision can enable its continuation. The difference resides in the consequential relationship. An evaluator can discover, misrepresent or ignore it. The evaluator's approval does not create the dependency.

This is the first useful sense in which significance can arise within generation. It identifies an asymmetry between changes that enable or obstruct a specified organization. To become operationally appreciated, that difference must also participate in the organization's responses. To become learned, its encountered consequences must change subsequent response. Those are differentiated connections to establish within the same history, rather than separate worlds of neutral facts and added meanings.

The health question becomes stronger when several beings are involved. One organization can continue through activity that interrupts another's indispensable conditions. An adequate account of shared health must preserve both histories. Its description of the first organization's performance, or of an aggregate resource total, cannot stand in for what occurs in the other. Whether the affected activity is good, whether intervention is permissible, and which competing consequence should take priority remain substantive questions; the causal asymmetry alone does not decide them.

## An exact correspondence from delivered support to executable continuation

Fix an affected activity at a stated point in a common history. Identify its candidate continuations before asking which have sufficient support. Specify their participants, transitions, duration, component needs, permissions and completion conditions independently of a proposed increase in support or a favorable judgment. Identify candidates without first restricting them to those whose resource requirements are met.

For each candidate path γ, let its ordered changes in usable stock be δ₁,…,δₘ, and let f be the stock floor that must hold. Consumption precedes any replenishment arising from the operation it enables. Define the required initial stock using the [existing prefix-resource theorem](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECIPIENT-RENEWAL-MATH.md#1-a-general-theorem-about-future-action-and-actual-resources):

    Lγ = max(0, −δ₁, −(δ₁+δ₂), …, −(δ₁+…+δₘ))
    Tγ = f + Lγ

Let B be the usable stock at the comparison point. Every other required stock must also satisfy its own prefix conditions. Suppose an actual change makes an additional quantity d ≥ 0 available before the candidate begins. For this comparison, the path's increments and all other guards must remain the same when its initial stock changes from B to B+d. Merely retaining the same law is insufficient: that law might make consumption, action or access depend on the quantity present.

**Corollary of the existing theorem.** Under these conditions, γ becomes newly resource-feasible exactly when

    B < Tγ ≤ B+d.

Every previously resource-feasible candidate remains resource-feasible. If C(B) denotes the candidates passing all these fixed conditions at B, then C(B) is a subset of C(B+d). The inclusion is strict precisely when at least one candidate has its threshold in that interval.

**Derivation.** The existing theorem gives resource feasibility exactly when the initial stock is at least Tγ. A newly feasible path fails this condition at B and meets it at B+d. The nonnegative shift preserves every previously satisfied threshold, while the stipulated remaining guards are unchanged. No new resource theorem is claimed.

This is an **operational enabling effect**. It is stronger than merely observing a larger number: it specifies which independently identified activity is now resource-feasible and why. It remains weaker than actual execution, sustained renewal or welfare improvement. A path with sufficient resources may never be selected or initiated; its stipulated permissions do not establish a mechanism that exercises them. Painful or coerced activity can also become resource-feasible.

The increase in support is itself an event or process to explain. Its formation, source, accessibility and effects elsewhere belong to the larger history. The local comparison begins once that support is available; it leaves those relations outside its representation, not outside generative reality. If the increase also changes another required stock, access, behavior or circumstances, the simple inclusion claim no longer applies. The coupled histories must then be compared directly.

### A small constructed illustration

Suppose an independently specified activity has increments −3, +2, −1 and requires floor 1. Its largest prefix deficit is 3, so its entry threshold is 4. With initial stock 3, the first operation is unavailable. An additional usable unit permits the path; the successive stock quantities are 1, 3 and 2. The path is enabled although its net resource change is negative. No claim of indefinite renewal follows.

With initial stock 5 the same path was already resource-feasible. Another unit does not change feasibility for this one path. That does not establish no value: other activities or disturbances might make the reserve consequential. Conversely, if an indispensable component is absent or a deadline has already passed, increasing that stock need not enable the path at all. The illustration fixes its path and floor in advance; it is an algebraic witness, not a model of an observed organism or a new empirical result.

## From an account to an affected being

Applying the corollary requires more than choosing a threshold that makes an existing result look important. For the affected position represented in a model, establish:

1. **Continuity:** what ongoing activity or capability belongs to this affected being, and what would actually be interrupted? The same ongoing activity must be identifiable through the observed consumption, replenishment and later use. Sustaining a learning process does not by itself explain what continues at another affected position.
2. **Dependence:** which support, component, access condition or relationship enables that activity? Derive or independently identify the relevant floor and resource requirements. Do not select them after seeing the quantity reached by a favored intervention.
3. **Delivery and use:** did the change make support available to that same activity, early enough and through an effective relation of access? Increasing a recorded number is insufficient. The conditions for initiating, redirecting or refusing the path must also be identified.
4. **Consequence:** which affected continuation becomes executable, occurs, or is obstructed? Keep these claims separate. Retain burdens during the activity as well as capacities remaining afterward.

In the plasticity history, 34 names a modeled stock quantity. A need with threshold 35 cannot be invented to turn that number into evidence of deprivation. The affected activity and its dependency must be identified independently. In the renewal history, the probe/copy sequence is an actual resource-dependent continuation, but its floor and source requirements are supplied locally. Neither example yet explains how the stock change affects an independently identified being's own continuation.

The [empirical identification work](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md) adds another reason to preserve the target. Changed behavior can track consequences for another being, distress within the responding organization, learned cues, or newly learned means to an existing end. Distinguishing alleviation from suppression of its signal requires identifying what changes in the affected experience or activity. Altering understanding can itself change experience; a cognitive intervention is not automatically mere concealment. These are different hypotheses about one consequential history.

## What this changes in the core and its lenses

The proposed common relationship is now more explicit: **ongoing activity depends on conditions that activity can preserve, consume, replace or release; some organizations acquire ways for the resulting differences to revise what they do.** This explains a family of generative and destructive regimes. It is not a claim that every healthy thing learns or continually changes. Dormancy, durable provision and fixed adequate response retain their place. Operational continuation can require revision where the existing arrangement would fail under the circumstances claimed. Warranted revision can also concern a continuing practice's existing effects, even while that practice remains productive.

The lenses preserve different relations within this configuration:

| Lens | Relationship it must preserve for this claim |
|---|---|
| Maintenance | Actual provision and loss relative to the activity sustained |
| Learning | Encountered consequence, acquired distinction and its causal use |
| Succession | The support, capabilities and acquired distinctions that actually continue through a transition |
| Concern | The affected being's condition and what an available response would do for that being |
| Wisdom | How evidence about these relations can correct a response, its criterion or its practice without erasing affected histories |

The last two include justificatory claims still under investigation. Being informed, mutable or long-lived does not establish them. A regime can restore productive capacity while imposing severe unwanted experience; its productive future does not describe all that occurs in the continuation. The [constitutive significance candidate](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md) addresses the experienced condition and the further reason for a response separately, within the same unfolding. This resource argument neither proves nor refutes that candidate.

Universality can concern a faithful account of these relations across situations. It need not mean that every arrangement has one permanent rank. A complete ethical ordering also does not follow from inclusion between selected continuation sets. The stronger proposal for shared health is that renewal can become answerable to the differentiated lives it affects. Here, answerability calls for identifiable consequences, effective ways to contest and correct misdescription, and warranted treatment of burdens and refusals. Those requirements need defense and realization; putting the word into a definition supplies neither.

## Primary comparisons and the next bridge

Di Paolo's account of adaptivity distinguishes continued self-production from regulation sensitive to its changing conditions. It supports examining how consequence becomes operative rather than inferring appreciation from persistence. The account is conceptual and does not derive universal concern. See [Di Paolo 2005, pp. 438–442](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf), already part of this branch's [prior research](../RESEARCH.md).

Barandiaran and Egbert derive a viability-related field in a metabolic model, then explicitly distinguish that assessment from a mechanism sensitive to the metabolism's changing condition. Their controller can behave adaptively without that causal sensitivity. This gives a substantive comparator for our separation of actual dependence, successful response and acquired regulation. See [their 2014 paper, §§6.1–6.2, pp. 23–25](https://xabier.barandiaran.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/barandiaran_and_egbert_-_2014_-_norm-establishing_and_norm-following_-_alj_pub.pdf). These sources support the organizational question; they do not establish the ethical extension.

The next shared bridge is **an identified change in affected continuation → acquired distinction → warranted revision of the operative practice or criterion**. This round supplies the consequence-side correspondence conditions and a limited exact implication. The [practical-boundary study](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-INTEGRATION.md) owns the adjacent criterion/practice-revision argument. The account would need revision if the asserted dependency failed, the changed support did not enable the claimed continuation, a claimed learned distinction had no causal use under its stated conditions, or the health assessment omitted a burden it promised to preserve. These are failures of particular claims, not an automatic refutation of generation as a whole.
