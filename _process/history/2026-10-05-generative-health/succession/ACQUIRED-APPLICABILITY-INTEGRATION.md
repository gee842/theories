Earlier work showed why the sensor model could remain correct while this use failed. The failure was returned to an outer experiment; the original learner did not acquire it and let it change its next undertaking.

The new extension installs a native consequence contact and a small applicability selector before the attempted use. In the primary history, a declared change reverses both readiness and its sensor coupling, so every sensor prediction remains true while readiness at a given learned reading is reversed. This is the earlier access law's admitted transformation, applied at a different, explicitly identified handoff. Its origin is supplied; the learner does not secretly receive its flags.

What changes is the relation through which that knowledge guides use: reading 1 no longer suffices for the attempted productive operation. The learner retains two explanations, a complemented use relation and absence of transfer, rather than declaring that failure proves inversion.

Those possibilities disagree about a probe at reading 0. The acquired relation therefore changes the next question actually selected. In the primary open apparatus, that encounter both distinguishes the possibilities and transfers the remaining packet into the continuing account. Its actual arrival supports subsequent receipt acquisition, updating and retention. The account does not spend an anticipated return before it exists.

## Failures remain operative

The saved study includes 17 named cases and a 48-entry grid over both learned prefixes, readiness/sensor/blockage combinations and three receipt modes. Named and grid conditions overlap; they are not 65 independent kinds of evidence.

Missing receipt halts without becoming observed zero. Denied first commitment preserves the old candidate family despite an actual attempted use and acquired but uncommitted receipt. A misleading positive receipt is especially consequential. The primary apparatus physically fails to transfer, yet the complemented receipt makes the controller retain the same-reading interpretation and stop. Its program completes while actual capture has not occurred. Thus the mechanism can preserve a false application; the outcome channel's calibration is substantive, not an invisible truth oracle.

Withheld source or fuel remains in the declared inert boundary reserve; it is not silently destroyed or made available to the controller.

The admitted use family, reliable reset/phase correspondence, stationary interval, recipe, finite search, two-attempt bound and correspondence of receipts to actual arrival remain explicit premises. The controller receives only the retained model, pending word, native callback and resource account. ## What this changes in the governing account

The [previous relevance analysis](DEPENDENCY-AND-RELEVANCE.md) gave a conditional relation between unresolved possibilities and executable responses. This implementation now realizes a bounded next step: acquired consequences revise the application of retained knowledge, and that acquired relation governs a subsequent encounter with actual support consequences. The required sensory knowledge remains intact throughout.

The generative point is that an activity's results can alter the organization through which earlier knowledge participates in further activity. A failure need not falsify every part of that knowledge. It can expose a failure in its relation to the undertaking, and that relation can itself become a subject of acquired correction. A fixed encompassing law can implement this change without an unlimited hierarchy of supervisors.

For the health investigation, this establishes one conditional component of correction. It does not prove that every continuation should be preserved, that a learned correction is always appropriate, or that a captured finite stock constitutes continuing health. Differentiated affected activity, concern and warranted scope remain substantive parts of the full account.

The stronger remaining connection is formation or revision of the admitted applicability family, evidence access and inquiry rule through wider consequences, including relations the installed family cannot express. The achieved native join should not be reopened merely by asking for another controller; neither should its supplied recipe be called the origin of all relevance. Continuing renewal, independently warranted intervention and empirical identification retain their separate obligations.
