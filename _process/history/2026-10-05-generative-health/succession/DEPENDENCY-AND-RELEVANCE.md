## 1. Relevance begins in a dependency

An effect can change what an activity depends on before any organization recognizes that change. If a particular continuation requires an enabling relation, an operation that removes that relation, without an adequate replacement, cannot preserve that continuation. Identifying the relation requires evidence about the activity, its timing and possible alternatives. Calling a quantity a resource or marking an outcome successful is insufficient.

This consequence relevance does not yet require inquiry. The condition may already be known; several unresolved situations may admit the same adequate response; or no available inquiry may resolve the difference while preserving the means to act. **A distinction matters to inquiry through the relationship between what remains unresolved, what responses remain possible, and what those responses would enable or interrupt.**

These are relations within the unfolding activity. A represented alternative, the organization that retains it, an affected process and the means of acting are differentiated through their histories and couplings. The analysis separates them to preserve differences in access, effect and control, not to introduce a neutral world awaiting an independent meaning-giver. Relevant effects need not take the form of an explicit representation; a claim about acquired understanding requires the additional retained mediation and use to be shown.

The continuation must be independently identified. Its dependencies cannot be chosen merely to validate the proposed criterion. Nor does a real dependency establish that preserving this activity is justified overall. Destructive organizations also have dependencies. Actual undergoing, legitimate intervention and competing consequences remain substantive relations to investigate, rather than consequences of the notation below.

## 2. A lens can preserve response without preserving every difference

Use the existing [inquiry-sufficiency representation](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md). Let a nonempty evidence cell \(B\) contain the configurations still admitted by accessible evidence, including the actual represented configuration. Let \(D(x)\) contain the same identifiable terminal responses that are executable from configuration \(x\) and meet an independently specified continuation contract on every admitted outcome. Prefix conditions, timing, affected identity and any justified restrictions belong to that contract. A terminal response ends this inquiry episode, not all subsequent activity.

The inherited common-response condition is

\[
C(B)=\bigcap_{x\in B}D(x).
\]

If \(C(B)\) contains a response, the represented uncertainty need not be removed to guarantee that particular contract. This is sufficiency, not a claim that further knowledge has no other use or that the response is best by every consideration. If the intersection is empty, no single terminal response can be guaranteed from the present evidence. That failure does not by itself make inquiry available or worthwhile.

For each response \(r\), define its domain \(U_r=\{x:r\in D(x)\}\). Then

\[
C(B)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists r\; B\subseteq U_r.
\]

**Proof.** A member of the intersection belongs to every \(D(x)\) on the left. This is exactly containment of the evidence cell in that response's domain on the right. The converse uses the same memberships. ∎

The domains can overlap. They cover precisely those configurations that admit at least one adequate terminal response. An analytical lens, or an actually acquired observation, supports this bounded response claim when each of its nonempty cells lies inside some response domain. This restates the [earlier common-choice result T13](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#t13-exact-sufficiency-for-permission-and-for-choosing) as a coverage condition; it supplies neither a free sensor nor an installed selector.

There need not be one coarsest adequate partition. Consider three configurations whose adequate response sets are

\[
D(1)=\{a\},\qquad D(2)=\{a,b\},\qquad D(3)=\{b\}.
\]

Both partitions \(12\mid3\) and \(1\mid23\) support a common response in each cell. Neither is coarser than the other. Their only common coarsening is the single cell \(123\), which has no common response. Thus no adequate partition is coarser than both. In this same example, sharing an adequate response is not transitive: configurations 1 and 2 share one, as do 2 and 3, while 1 and 3 do not.

A separate example shows a stronger collective obstruction:

\[
D(1)=\{a,b\},\qquad D(2)=\{b,c\},\qquad D(3)=\{a,c\}.
\]

Every pair admits a common response, but the three-way intersection is empty. Pairwise compatibility checks therefore cannot in general certify the whole evidence cell. These are abstract finite witnesses about the response relation, not added native histories in either existing learner. They challenge a proposed pairwise compression rule; they do not refute the fuller generative theory or claim mathematical priority.

This gives Ren's lens question a precise consequence: **what a lens must preserve depends on which consequential relations it is being used to support.** Several distinct compressions can preserve the same bounded continuation. A shared generative structure does not require one partition or one scalar ordering to replace all of them.

## 3. Inquiry can construct an adequate response—and change its conditions

The [accepted constructive sufficiency theorem](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md) gives a positive connection. Suppose the current cell has no common terminal response. Suppose an available inquiry preserves all specified intermediate conditions, and every nonempty observation branch has a common adequate terminal response. For each of the finitely many observations, supply either an impossibility certificate or a common response for its successor configurations. Form the policy: perform that inquiry, retain its actual observation, and select the corresponding response. This policy satisfies the declared contract under the theorem's execution conditions.

No ranking of all possible futures is needed for this construction. It requires a response for each live branch, not a numeric reward or a uniquely preferred response. Constructing those branch facts and embodying the policy are additional obligations; the theorem alone does not give them to a learner.

Crucially, a branch contains the **successor configurations produced by the inquiry**. It is not just a smaller list of unchanged possibilities. A test can distinguish the original situation while consuming what a later response requires. It can also restore a condition or complete part of the undertaking. The inquiry's effects must therefore enter the response calculation.

The existing [one-packet apparatus](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ATTAINABLE-MATHEMATICAL-QUESTIONS.md) makes this issue concrete: either of two questions is separately answerable, while their combined answer cannot be guaranteed under the unchanged law. There need not be a greatest feasible information state to acquire before considering what matters. Waiting remains an alternative when the actual law permits the relevant demand to arrive without losing access. This result and the overlapping-response examples concern different relations: attainable evidence and sufficient response. Neither implies the other automatically.

## 5. A discriminating test for acquired relevance

A stronger test should preserve the undertaking and change an actual dependency, such as whether an alternative support route really sustains the affected activity during further inquiry. Where possible, match accumulated depletion, occupied sockets and unresolved predictive alternatives. Make the changed coupling available through actual encounters and retained evidence; do not supply an evaluator's relevance flag as if it were the acquired mechanism.

The concurrent [applicability reconstruction](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/APPLICABILITY-REVALUATION-INTEGRATION.md) supplies a complementary constraint: accurate knowledge of an observation channel can remain true while its productive-use relation fails. Changing an undertaking's application need not mean changing every ultimate concern or erasing accurate prior knowledge. Its separate source and comparator evidence is credited at its own scope; it is not a completed composition with the response-domain argument here.

There is a precise restriction to test. Let \(Z(h)\) contain all information consulted by a specified rival policy at history \(h\). Let \(A(h)\) be the nonempty set of immediate choices that can be extended by a policy using accessible evidence to meet the same declared contract on every admitted outcome. Include every permitted choice, including stopping, declining or waiting when it can meet that contract. Representing immediate completion as a terminal choice is a notational convention, not an added native operation. Suppose

\[
Z(h_0)=Z(h_1),\qquad A(h_0)\cap A(h_1)=\varnothing.
\]

Then no policy whose next choice depends only on \(Z\) can guarantee adequate continuation in both histories. **Proof.** It must select the same operation in both, and no operation belongs to both adequate-first-operation sets. For a randomized choice with a finite repertoire, the same choice distribution cannot have its support contained in two disjoint nonempty sets; randomization therefore cannot restore a zero-error guarantee. ∎

This result requires independently established disjointness. If one operation remains adequate in both situations, unchanged behavior does not refute dependency sensitivity. It also requires the rival's information restriction to be real. A general fixed-law stateful controller can implement learned dependency sensitivity, so this cannot separate learning from every fixed controller. The comparison concerns which acquired relations enter selection.

To identify that entry, combine three observations: a real dependency change is encountered; its retained effects alter the operative inquiry or response arrangement; and disrupting their specified use removes the alteration while preserving storage and formation history. The disruption must preserve relevant input delivery and general response capacity; broad impairment would not identify this retained relation's specific contribution. The result would establish functional dependency sensitivity at the tested scope, not uniquely identify its internal representation. Independently observe what happens to the affected activity. A changed sample count, depleted stock or favorable report alone cannot identify the whole connection. Preserve missed signals, misleading signals, blocked revision and harmful revision as possible outcomes.

## 6. Consequence for the foundation

The general relation can now be stated more sharply: **activity changes the conditions of further activity; acquired sensitivity to those dependencies can change which distinctions guide its continuation.** Organizational significance is grounded in actual differences to what can proceed. Inquiry relevance concerns the distinctions needed for available responses. Learning concerns how encountered effects acquire and change an operative role in making those distinctions. These explanations articulate one process at different scopes; they do not posit three independent substances or require an endless hierarchy of supervisors.

The health candidate remains renewal of the conditions of continuation with effective correction where needed. This result clarifies what correction must be sensitive to; it does not equate health with perpetual inquiry, preserving every incumbent activity or maximizing the number of possibilities. Differentiated lives, actual experiences and justified constraints remain visible in the continuation being assessed.

The completed contribution is a conditional account of response-relative relevance, its compression limits and a discriminating intervention criterion, with existing acquired mechanisms credited. The next stronger bridge is **actual acquisition and use of a changed dependency relation to reorganize inquiry within one continuing history**. The underlying physical coupling, evidence calibration, represented continuation and legitimate scope remain explicit inputs until that bridge is realized or grounded. This note neither silently combines separate histories nor declares the unified foundation complete.
