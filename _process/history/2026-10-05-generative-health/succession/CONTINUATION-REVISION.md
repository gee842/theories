## A positive conditional corollary

Compare continuations from the same reached four-encounter state, preserving the actual stock, records and material at that boundary. Let x denote the model's affected stock. For a stated requirement f, define the local condition:

    C_f: x remains at least f at every event prefix from this boundary.

The [old source-level proof](CONTINUITY-AND-CORRECTION.md#a-prefix-invariant-before-the-first-new-debit) establishes that every P0 continuation reaching the existing response first contains x = 34. The accepted P1 faithful witness starts with x = 36, preserves 36 through revision and assessment, and changes it to 46 through the existing response. Its minimum is 36. Therefore, for every independently specified requirement

    34 < f ≤ 36,

no old-law response path satisfies C_f, while a new-law response path does.

**Proof.** Every old response path has the prefix 34 < f. Every prefix of the specified new witness has x ≥ 36 ≥ f, and the witness completes the response. ∎

The witness has actual fuel 14 → 13 → 12 → 11 during formation, inspection and commitment, then completes the inherited response at fuel 1. Material remains 68. It consumes the spare pair that could otherwise support later replacement. The result concerns the existence of this admitted path; it does not guarantee that any scheduler selects it or that every learned sign, channel or resource condition permits it.

P0 and P1 are different declared laws: the latter adds explicit operations. Within P1, commitment changes immediate admission, while its later witness was already reachable through commitment before that event occurred. This is not an enlargement of the all-time reachability set under an unchanged law.

## What this establishes about an affected activity

If an independently identified ongoing activity requires C_f, the old pathway violates a necessary condition and the new pathway avoids that particular obstruction. The floor is an application premise; it is not an input secretly selecting the runtime's favorable action.

Preserving a necessary condition alone does not establish that the activity continues. A positive continuation claim additionally needs independently supported sufficient conditions preserved by the entire history, or direct evidence of continuation. Other dependencies, access, timing, burden and consent can change while x stays above f. The model executes a stock-changing response; it does not subsequently execute an independently specified life or activity of the affected being.

The event scale matters. Buffering, substitute support or tolerance for a brief depletion can defeat a per-prefix floor interpretation. A threshold chosen merely to separate these observed quantities would not establish the relevant dependency; independent evidence is required. The earlier fully restoring continuation remains a control: restoring the final quantity does not undo an interruption when the independently established activity cannot tolerate it, but such intolerance must be demonstrated.

The saved `one_history` comparison begins its revision suffix at count four with x = 38, fuel 15 and one positive occupied socket. The first such event produces b−2. This is the same source-level induction as the earlier b = 36 proof, with b left as a parameter.

For the actual one-socket boundary, b = 38. Consequently an independently justified requirement

    36 < f ≤ 38

would make further original-law acquisition incompatible with preserving that condition through the response. Yet the installed faithful trigger does not revise at this boundary. This is a prospective dependency conflict the current sufficient trigger does not cover. The requirement starts at this checkpoint or becomes newly applicable there: the saved earlier four-encounter history already reached 36, so it does not establish uninterrupted preservation of f > 36 throughout the earlier history.

It does not refute the rule's narrower four-unit repair application, identify an actual physiological floor, or prove all P1 continuations blocked. Nor does it establish a missed available remedy: at the retained register four, one positive socket does not make the inherited assessment favor the response. Merely lowering the rewrite threshold would leave that further condition unresolved. The case shows why cumulative depletion and the practical relevance of further inquiry are different relations. The amount already lost cannot determine an appropriate stopping point independently of what continued activity requires and which responses are actually available.

## The next generative connection

The criterion's relevance is answerable to what its enactment enables or interrupts. If a requirement obstructs the continuation it is justified as supporting, its adequacy for that purpose fails. Further inquiry can still be warranted where an unresolved difference changes the safety, feasibility or adequacy of response. Those relationships must be established at the current situation and horizon; an observer's preferred score cannot supply them.

The next connection is therefore **effects on an identified continuing activity → acquired sensitivity to the relevance of further inquiry → retention, revision or withdrawal of the operative requirement**. This can be investigated through changes in actual connections, reconstruction or selection. It does not demand a new evaluator above every rule. In the present model, calibration, the two-occupied trigger, formation recipe and mandate remain supplied; the occupancy report alone does not explain their formation or justification.

Shared health additionally concerns the differentiated continuations affected by revision. The new path changes one support relation while using fuel and replacement material. Which tradeoff is warranted remains part of the application. A finite successful repair does not establish indefinite renewal, universal ethical direction or an automatic improvement in all affected possibilities.
