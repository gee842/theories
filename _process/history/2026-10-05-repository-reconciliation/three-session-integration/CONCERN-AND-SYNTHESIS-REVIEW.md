It does not derive the architecture menu, credit-assignment law, primitive valence, or commercial ecology from constitutive physical dependencies. These are inputs to the local theorem and targets for the governing derivation.

The settlement adds rooted reason admission and retraction. Its bounded feature construction is genuine relative to its initial primitives but does not establish unrestricted discovery or new ethical reasons.

The accepted [settlement integration review](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONCERN-SETTLEMENT-INTEGRATION.md), especially its “Mapping into the completion interfaces,” already supplies the correct physical bridge obligations:

1. Realize audit, inference, memory, control, attack, and receipts in one event/history law with actual materials, energy, time, computation, access, and every payment prefix. A cash balance is not an energy ledger.
2. Derive an actual process entering and retaining the strategic continuation. Subgame-perfect equilibrium existence does not establish attraction, spontaneous formation probabilities, or prevalence under noise.
3. Preserve common joint tests, time horizons, reliability, delivered successor state, and information. More admitted cases or money cannot be substituted for inclusion of a joint capability region.
4. Explain how the primitive significant condition comes to regulate the process, then how consequences modify its selectors and representations. The origin of wider concern belongs inside this connected explanation; supplied anchors are not foundational axioms of the whole program.
5. Keep ethical judgment and strategic implementation connected without assuming automatic compatibility. Removing game actions through a normative filter can change incentives and financing. A certificate for one reason is not a whole-person ranking, and its causal origin does not by itself justify it.

These requirements are a substantive research interface, not missing files. The later physical branch's exact resource/turnover work is complementary and should remain the current base. Neither branch's independent success supplies the other's premises for free. The useful integration is to carry the unmet premises forward as derivation obligations, with explicit adverse alternatives, rather than to repeat a favorable example or restart an already completed conditional game.
