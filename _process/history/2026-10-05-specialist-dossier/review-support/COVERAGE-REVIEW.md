## Required correction

In §12.1, the sentence beginning “Start a partition with identical labels and permissions” omits the observation maps. Section 12.1 initially defines outcome labels `L` and observations `O_i` separately. Equal outcome labels and permissions therefore do not imply equal observations. Initialize using the joint tuple `(L,(O_i)_i,permissions)`, or explicitly fold the observations into the retained labels. Otherwise the described refinement can return a partition that fails the contract in Proposition 8 even when all transition-block sums agree.

## Minor clarifications

1. **Terminology, §7.1.** The sentence prescribing “meta-attractor” for an attracting or persistent regime is a defensible mathematical convention. The latest v7.3 source also uses the name for the reflexive-selection mechanism and separately reserves *attracting* for a proved invariant regime. Marking the dossier's narrower convention explicitly would avoid appearing to overwrite that distinction. No mathematical result depends on the naming choice.
2. **Timing units, §6.** The rescue example says “one additional reserve unit” after measuring lifetime in time units. Either specify unit loss rate or say “reserve sufficient for one additional time unit.” The correction-at-floor convention has already been supplied.
3. **Traceability, §7.4.** The explicit `L_i`, `M_ij` and cost definitions repair the earlier condensation. Appendix C gives the exact current statement and proof source. This is adequate for self-contained review.

## Limits of this review

The review checks exposition and source fidelity, including the principal equations compared against their local sources. It is not exhaustive independent reproof, empirical validation, mechanized verification, or priority certification. More ambitious extensions—unrestricted purpose formation, decentralized synthesis, endogenous legitimacy and enforcement, universal intervention transport—are appropriately presented as open questions. No further expansion of scope is needed before sending this foundation to specialists for criticism.

Local source links in the main dossier were checked at review time; none was missing. The coordinating author may amend the identified sentence before delivery; the review preserves the reason for that correction rather than concealing it.
