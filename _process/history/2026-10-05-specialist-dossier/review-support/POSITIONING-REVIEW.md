## Verdict

**No blocking finding in the reviewed conceptual framing, normative limits, prior-art positioning or evidence language.** The dossier acknowledges an achieved generative foundation and definite mathematical realizations. It does not quietly replace that achievement with the stronger and unnecessary demand for a unique universal ontology. It also does not claim unrestricted causal completeness, empirical validation across all domains, or a moral deduction from survival.

The work is adequately specified for external specialist criticism as an integrated foundation with conditional results. This verdict is not external peer review, a novelty certification, or an endorsement of every mathematical proof or empirical calculation.

## Findings

1. **Central claim is constructive and correctly delimited.** Sections 1–3 state the recursive relation between consequential activities, changed enabling/selection conditions, and the mechanisms needed for continued correction. Sections 5–13 give equations, distinctions and preservation results rather than leaving the account as an unconstrained slogan. Section 2 explicitly distinguishes structural, conditional-mathematical and substantive-scientific claims. It rightly treats uniqueness of vocabulary as unnecessary while retaining a burden to constrain mechanisms.

2. **The inquiry argument avoids the earlier overextension.** Section 3.4 uses the necessary implication: a project requiring recurring judgement cannot be accomplished after irreversible loss of judgement capacity. It does not rely on the stronger converse that an indefinitely branching world automatically realizes judgement. It explicitly allows finite projects and endings and does not infer obligations to other participants from the speaker's existence. This is consistent with the careful portion of the earlier proof text and the v2 methodological plan.

3. **The normative premise remains visible.** Section 14 states the commitment to plural centres, standing, shared conditions and legitimate revision, and distinguishes physical consequences from changed standards. Stable prisons, harmful lineages and legitimate replacement prevent persistence from becoming goodness by definition. Section 11 correctly distinguishes expected feasibility, participation, coercive outside options and legitimacy. The proposed direction is a constrained partial ordering where justified, not a universal moral gradient.

4. **Prior-art positioning neither erases the contribution nor overstates it.** Section 17 and references R1–R8 accurately reflect the overlaps checked in [POSITIONING-MEMO.md](POSITIONING-MEMO.md). The organizational-maintenance idea, selection-environment feedback, performative prediction, bisimulation, polycentric governance and multi-stakeholder alignment are credited. The candidate contribution is the specified integration and its discriminating consequences. The statement that a familiar ingredient does not by itself dispose of an integrated claim is appropriate. No assertion of established priority remains.

5. **The empirical status is appropriately separated from mathematical results.** Section 16 identifies constructed calculations as such, retains failed and inconclusive outcomes, states the institutional reanalysis's identification limits, and identifies the prospective handoff design as unrun. The causal arrow from outcomes to maintained future correction is not reported as empirically established merely because rival models can be discriminated in a constructed example. Section 15 makes intervention-preserving cross-domain transport a validation target, not an achieved result.

6. **The finite integration is not presented as universal closure.** Theorem 1 joins faithful representation, possible safe action and actual installed-process recurrence under its finite contract. Surrounding prose preserves the distinction between a successful alternative policy and the policy actually installed. Section 12.3 explicitly distinguishes computational expressiveness from explanatory compression. The final paragraph accurately says the foundation is ready to be criticized, rather than calling it a completed general science.

## Nonblocking refinements for later publication

- A specialist submission should choose one or two discriminating consequences as its principal contribution; the comprehensive dossier currently serves the review task well, but its breadth would obscure a conventional article's main result.
- Keep the qualification attached to “general” when excerpting the abstract or central claim. The surrounding explicit scope is doing substantive work and should travel with quotations.
- When reporting §9.4 outside this dossier, continue saying that the mechanism permits **bounded formation and commitment revision with supplied grammar and dispositions**. The existing text already says this; a short publicity summary could otherwise suggest that unrestricted goal formation has been derived.
- “Alignment as reproduction of its conditions” remains a useful explanatory principle, but actual fulfilment of commitments is still distinct from possessing the conditions that permit fulfilment. Sections 4 and 13 already preserve that distinction and should remain linked in later summaries.
