# Integrated theory and the four remaining tasks

Start with [Alignment as the Reproduction of Its Conditions](INTEGRATED-THEORY.md). It integrates the mechanism, optimal decision, causal identification, and revision results while preserving the distinction between a proved conditional framework and an empirically validated general theory.

The earlier [canonical representation approach](../OPTIMAL-FOUNDATION.md), [mechanistic presentation](../FOUNDATION.md), and [unchanged original dossier](../FOUNDATION-ALTERNATIVE-ORIGINAL.md) are retained.

## What this package establishes

- [Mechanism mathematics](MECHANISM-MATH.md) derives exact conditions for positive local attraction, collapse, competing basins, and plural protected floors in a class of endogenous renewal systems. It includes a separate finite stochastic extinction result.
- [Adaptive optimum](ADAPTIVE-OPTIMUM.md) supplies an exact least operating budget and implementable observation policy for a finite model class, with paid inquiry, explicit amendments, and a qualified confidence-learning extension.
- [Identification and added value](IDENTIFICATION-AND-VALUE.md) specifies identifiable assignment effects, a mediation counterexample, a stronger discriminating intervention, and an optimal finite-budget experiment design.
- [Composition and scope](COMPOSITION-AND-SCOPE.md) gives sufficient joint continuation and establishment conditions, and distinguishes representation, policy, experimental, and scientific-model optima.

Two requested achievements still need new observations: identifying the proposed causal loop in an actual system, and demonstrating practical predictive or intervention superiority. The prospective handoff study is unrun. The existing failed and inconclusive findings remain in the evidence record. Procedure conformity also does not establish independent ethical legitimacy.

Run `python3 verify_synthesis.py` from this directory to reproduce those standard-library checks.

Reproducing the figure and numerical checks with `plot_and_check_mechanism.py` requires NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib. Numerical trajectories do not replace the analytical proofs.

[Integration review](INTEGRATION-REVIEW.md) records the cross-review and resolved corrections. [Mathematical author audit](MATHEMATICAL-REVIEW.md) distinguishes the integrating author's completed checks from preliminary cross-review messages. These are AI-assisted internal reviews, not independent human peer review.

[DOCUMENT-CHECKS.json](DOCUMENT-CHECKS.json) and [MANIFEST.json](MANIFEST.json) report file integrity and provenance for this addition. They do not certify empirical validity, originality, or a universally optimal scientific theory.
