## Mechanism proofs checked

The production assumptions in MECHANISM-MATH.md give a unique surplus maximizer when P′(0)>μ/η, and zero maximal surplus otherwise. The positive-equilibrium equation, two-distinct-root condition, ecological Jacobian signs, block-triangular practice eigenvalue, and full-system hyperbolic local-attractor criterion are consistent. At critical equality there is a double root; the memorandum explicitly excludes this equality from the global collapse dichotomy. The assumption P″<0 supplies the nondegeneracy needed by the local fold expansion.

The global-collapse proof properly separates finite and infinite accumulated support exposure. Its logarithmic comparison function has a uniformly negative derivative once mean effort is bounded below the threshold. This forces exponential capacity decline, which makes support exposure finite. The resulting interior limiting share is a fraction of vanishing capacity, not a surviving population. The converse in the strict noncritical dichotomy uses an open basin of the selected upper equilibrium; it does not claim that every noncollapsing trajectory has been classified.

The protected-floor inequalities are strict. Local stability plus strict margins supports the nearby all-time protection statement. The exact example gives 7/12<u<2/3 for E>3/2 and C>6; effort 5/8 satisfies both, while effort 1 fails the support floor. The depleted rectangle is invariant by inward boundary signs and gives a separate collapse basin even when the positive equilibrium exists.

The finite stochastic extinction proof has explicit bounded birth, bounded population, baseline independent death-clock, no-entry, and absorbing-zero premises. Its conditional block bound does not require independence across blocks. These premises are stronger than merely saying that all systems have losses, and the text preserves that distinction. The very small numerical endpoint values are not treated as certified precision or as finite-time deterministic extinction.

The uncertainty-robust shortage certificate is conditional on the original nonnegative stock accounting, solution existence, the production upper bound, and no additional unmodeled supply or creation terms. Reversing its inequality does not establish successful control.

## Adaptive and compositional proofs checked

The finite optimization uses a common actual observation history, retains each unresolved persistent model label, and takes worst supported-path resource cost. Its backward induction therefore avoids both a hidden-state action oracle and an unjustified expected-cost rectangularity assumption. Hard safety, terminal completion, actual permissions, paid diagnostics, and implementation costs have separate roles. Randomization cannot reduce the worst-path budget because every positive-probability action must meet the same hard obligation.

The confidence extension uses controlled likelihoods from hidden-state filtering. For any fixed true candidate, the mixture-to-candidate likelihood ratio is a nonnegative supermartingale, including cases where an alternative puts mass on observations impossible under that candidate. The maximal inequality bounds true-model exclusion. Physical success is only claimed on the retained-true-model event; the exceptional fallback is not described as inherently safe. Optimality remains restricted to the declared retention certificate, not every policy with a given failure probability.

The infinite-horizon corollary is restricted to a finite sufficient configuration with history-independent updates. Bounded renewal obligations can be encoded by counters; arbitrary eventuality and unrestricted likelihood histories are not silently included. In the integrated text, the finite recursion begins from safe terminal states and works backward with explicit stage indices.

The composition and switching results are sufficient one-step inductive invariants. They require joint protected constraints, compatible joint resource spending, actual local observations, nonempty disturbance sets, defined transitions, and initialized membership. They do not establish optimality among all correlated viable regions, formation of the installed controllers, or safe establishment from outside the certified set.
