## What has to be distinguished

The new mechanism separates at least five observable claims: a record supports a currently applicable reason; the actor takes that reason into account; an action is chosen; the means actually execute; and the affected person's outcome changes. The study must not score all five from an expression of concern or a display of nominal points. Paid revision by a successor is a further outcome with its own evidence route and cost.

The formal construction identifies conditions sufficient for its declared process. Applying it elsewhere requires independent measurements of the process boundary, accessible records, current context, feasible actions, relevant early resources, authority and actual effects. A unit of fuel, a dollar, physical exertion and a reason's weight are not interchangeable measurements.

## A primary-source check of costly intended protection

Massaccesi, Zhang, Silani and Lamm's 2025 study analyzed fifty adults choosing physical effort for shock reduction for themselves or another person. Participants executed dynamometer squeezes; success among chosen effort trials was 93% for self and 92% for other. The other person was a confederate who received no shocks. Participants' own delivered outcomes were fixed to one, two and five shocks despite the described trial-selection procedure. Thus the study supplies measured effort under believed protective consequences, but not observed reductions in another recipient's delivered pain. Its finite task does not identify our record-replay, authority or succession mechanism. These distinctions follow the published methods and results, not a reanalysis. [Primary article, sections 2.2–2.3 and 3.2](https://nyaspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/nyas.70075).

Our identification rule follows: measured actuation cannot establish an outcome outside the measured process. Actor costs and recipient effects need separate observation.

The publisher's full text was accessible during this audit. Direct PMC opening and some other publisher pages returned browser challenges; search excerpts from those other papers were not promoted into additional verified method claims here. The article links public data, but those data were not downloaded or analyzed for this note. No source document is reproduced or silently modified.

## A discriminating next measurement contract

This is a proposal, not a result or a new completion requirement. A low-stakes implementation could use an actual optional payment or verified workload relief for consenting recipients, with a separately measured cost to the actor. Retain the same supplied positive commitment across arms. Before outcomes, fix:

1. **Evidence and applicability.** A retained factual record, its target and context; an independently checked update that either defeats its application or leaves its ground intact. Record admission and alternative grounds separately from the eventual action.
2. **Executable means.** The action that can deliver the stated benefit, its deadline, actual control permissions, early payment requirements and all other available routes. Distinguish an actor's request from an executed transfer or completed relief.
3. **Resource timing.** Compare enough usable support before the deadline with the same nominal total arriving afterward. Log authorized borrowing and outside means rather than assuming they do not exist.
4. **Operational obstruction.** A reversible delivery-channel intervention that leaves the supporting evidence unchanged. Separately manipulate defeated evidence with the action channel intact. Record withdrawal and alternative assistance, not only the originally requested action.
5. **Continuation.** Preserve the actual remaining record, stock and authority for a later decision maker; measure what that successor can reconstruct and afford without a fresh unreported endowment.

The intended discriminator is a crossed effect: changing the warranted factual application should change the relevant admission; interrupting only delivery should change executable means without itself refuting the reason. A truthful report of admitted concern may coexist with failed execution. A model equating admission with delivered benefit fails if these dissociate. A conventional model representing both epistemic and operational states can make the same prediction and must remain a competent comparator.

Estimate any psychological coupling or choice parameters separately; do not use the synthetic value five or its cash prices as fitted human measurements. Different normative models may disagree even with the same factual records. Neither task compliance nor profit establishes the ethical truth of the supplied commitment.

This contract could identify one remaining interface. Broader scope would still require the materially different mappings in the governing research programme. The finite construction and this source audit improve the target's precision; they do not substitute for that evidence.
