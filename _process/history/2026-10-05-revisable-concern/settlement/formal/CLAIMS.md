## Executable record

[check_process.py](check_process.py) uses only the Python standard library and exact rational arithmetic. [results.json](results.json) records:

- 4,096 four-node directed graphs; 10,368 closure-round checks; 24,576 single-certificate retractions compared with independent rooted-path supports.
- 162 joint authority-cut scenarios, including strict loss, tie and strict protection around the shared bottleneck.
- 24 strict parameter sets solved as complete finite games, with 1,216 episode states, 13,128 cold product choices, 44,928 warm guard choices and 1,324,056 operator-response comparisons, including 1,279,128 voluntary-safeguard comparisons. These are compared decision options, not independent empirical samples.
- One full six-episode witness, all payment prefixes, each patient's actual outcome, and eight four-episode adverse controls. The witness ends at reserve 67; its actual total patient harm is 2, exclusively outside the then-admitted set. The controls include profitable exclusion, weak concern, audit refusal, delayed startup and a case where the stronger denial-robust finance condition fails but the observed finite equilibrium still protects.
- Twelve exact permanent-capture horizon comparisons and one individual-concern counterexample.

[check_feature.py](check_feature.py) separately constructs 231 expressions, performs 1,000 candidate-row evaluations, and proves the selected four-literal expression is minimal in that finite syntactic grammar by enumeration. [feature-results.json](feature-results.json) records all eight predictions and the positive prices for assembly, labels, evaluation and replay. The full game is not rerun with a different hidden audit cost: the refinement's explicit bill is exactly the prior u=1.

Finite checks corroborate exact examples and inspect implementation. They are not proof-assistant verification or empirical validation. The general theorem is the written conditional proof.

## Review repairs

An independent internal review identified that arbitrary gate prices could make a cheaper superset gate change the opponent's safeguarding bill and break the stated continuation. The report now assumes monotone subset prices and selects the exact admitted set when equally priced supersets tie. The executable witness already had strictly increasing subset prices; its arithmetic and outcomes did not change.

The [independent checker and saved results](../review/results.json) use a separate forward-state/backward-value implementation with no author-code imports. It reproduces the six-episode witness, all balances and operator value 269/32, and both unadmitted-patient harms; checks nine further games, including empty admitted sets and discounts 2/3 and 9/10; reproduces four failure controls; and checks 220 monotone subset-price cases while preserving the original nonmonotone counterexample. A separate implicant-coverage argument corroborates the four-literal minimum in the feature refinement. Internal independent AI review is not external peer review. The reviewer found no remaining theorem blocker after the stated repairs.
