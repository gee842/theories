# What evidence settles and what observation is still needed

5 October 2026. Research directed by Yongxu Ren; this evidence assessment, protocol design, and read-only audit are AI-assisted. No new participant responses were collected. The assessment addresses the concern theory's empirical, comparative, universal-direction, and experience claims. It does not replace the separate ethical argument or formal proofs.

**Several questions already have answers, but they are different answers to different propositions.** Joint formation has a constructive conditional solution. An unconditional positive guarantee over the modeled class is false. Actual AI responses support a limited feedback and succession mechanism. Neither those responses nor the finite constructions establish the prevalence of concern, superiority to conventional theories, or a law connecting organization to subjective experience. The remaining empirical questions can be made discriminating; calling them settled before collecting the distinguishing evidence would change the claim rather than solve it.

The ledger records completed answers as completed. A conditional theorem is not an unfinished universal theorem. Conversely, a universal claim does not become established by repeatedly confirming its favorable examples.

## What the existing responses actually show

The preserved AI study contains 32 founder lineages and 64 successor contexts, with 24 changed-rule lineages and eight adequate-rule controls. Among the changed-rule lineages, all 12 founders given timely error evidence produced a valid corrective packet, while none of the 12 given delayed evidence did. Successors with timely-produced packets scored 100%; their paired successors without access scored 75%. Both delayed-feedback conditions scored 75%. Adequate-rule controls declined maintenance and scored 100%.

The original three registered exact randomization tests passed their stated multiplicity threshold. Their experimental unit is the lineage, not each of the 1,024 successor answers. Shared model training, the supplied candidate-rule family, and instruction-based rather than operating-system-enforced isolation restrict the inference. The report's acknowledged provenance limits remain attached to the result.

The handoff's cost is a declared task-slot opportunity cost. The experiment did not measure a matched monetary or computational expense, so this finding cannot supply those missing resource estimates.

Our [read-only reconstruction](audit_existing.py) independently scores saved successor answers and checks which hypotheses match each complete baseline report. The [audit result](existing-evidence-audit.json) records 160 input-file hashes, reproduces the four aggregate patterns, and confirms that conventional candidate elimination identifies the rule and scores 16/16 in all 32 fixtures. It adds **zero new empirical observations**. It does not rerun models, overwrite preserved files, or recast repeated copies as replications.

The implication is precise: feedback and usable transmission mattered to these actual model decisions, while the inferential improvement required no novel inference theorem. The conventional learner was given the complete verified report; this was not a matched-cost comparison of autonomous maintenance policies. Neither comparative superiority nor inferiority of the whole research programme follows. The software queue experiment independently establishes a boundary on one transported factorization, but its generated service outcomes are not observations of human institutions.

## What the human and consciousness sources add

Kirby, Hoang, and Crimston randomized 102 parents to a brief compassion intervention or a waitlist. Reported moral expansiveness increased at two weeks. The absence of an active control, reliance on self-report, and lack of a three-month control group limit attribution and durability. The result makes complete immutability of reported scope implausible in this setting; it does not demonstrate costly transfer, independent discovery of justified standing, or the mechanism proposed here. [Original article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12671-023-02300-7).

Eyal, Steffel, and Epley's 25 experiments did not find consistent accuracy gains from imagining another's perspective; direct conversation improved accuracy in a final comparison. That supplies a reason to include actual testimony or information acquisition as a serious alternative explanation. It does not establish that testimony always settles a claim or that perspective-taking never helps. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29620401/).

The Cogitate collaboration preregistered divergent predictions of integrated information and global neuronal workspace theories and studied 256 participants using three neural measurement methods. Findings supported some predictions and challenged central predictions of both. This demonstrates that specified empirical bridges can be tested; it does not validate our concern framework as a consciousness theory or settle why experience exists. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40307561/).

These are three separate sources of evidence, not three replications of the same theory. Each summary uses fewer than 200 source-derived words. The earlier [conceptual evidence memo](../../conceptual-evidence.md) retains the detailed scope and reporting qualifications for the first two studies.

## A discriminating human experiment

The next empirical target should be a bounded claim: **among adults who already endorse a stated compensatory reason, an explicit scope challenge produces greater selective, costly transfer to new recipients after a delay than equally informative factual exposure or recipient testimony alone.** The pooled target is a selective behavioral response to the instructional contrast. Because eligibility includes already-inclusive participants, a pooled pass cannot establish removal of a prior exclusion or newly learned internal concern. Baseline restrictions and changes in stated reason admission are reported separately as secondary outcomes. A confirmatory scope-revision claim would need a prespecified restriction/removal estimand, sufficient eligible recruitment, power, and error allocation. This draft does not supply that additional claim or derive the initial positive reason.

The [machine-readable draft](protocol.json) records the design and its unrun status. All sample sizes, margins, and deadlines below are prospective design choices, not observed effects or externally validated constants. The design requires materials, recipients, implementation, funding, and independent registration before data collection; it is not an already registered trial.

### Population and task

Recruit a fixed 3,600 consenting adults from one declared online panel, with its country, language, enrollment dates, payment levels, and eligibility fixed before recruitment. At screening, at least seven days before randomization, ask whether a specified, avoidable uncompensated extra task gives a consenting recipient a reason to receive an additional bonus. Record the precise reason, qualifications, compensation choices, and any restrictions to familiar recipients. Eligibility requires comprehension and endorsement of this specified reason, not a minimum treatment effect. Retain and report the number and characteristics of screened-out respondents.

Use real, separately consenting recipients, verified work records, and additional bonuses. Everyone receives an adequate guaranteed base payment; the experiment neither inflicts injury nor withholds agreed wages. Decisions allocate an optional bonus from an endowed decision budget; one decision is randomly selected for actual payment, with the selection rule disclosed. Higher transfers leave the decision maker less of this experimental bonus. This creates a real private opportunity cost without representing the endowment as previously earned personal wealth. Recipient identities remain masked. Workload, prior compensation, consent, and the recipient's stated preference are verifiable; arbitrary group labels are randomly assigned.

The supplied reason is intentionally modest: a claim about an additional compensatory bonus. Different generosity toward an already compensated recipient is not automatically immoral. The manipulation checks whether participants distinguish the specific reason's applicability; other reasons can still justify generosity and must be recorded separately.

### Randomization and active alternatives

Randomize individuals equally to three arms after screening. Stratify by baseline restriction status and broad baseline transfer level using prespecified bins. Block sizes, allocation code, and the hidden allocation seed must be frozen before the first main response. One participant is one independent assignment unit; individual case decisions are repeated observations, not extra participants.

1. **Reason challenge:** the same verified case facts and recipient testimony used in the other arms, plus an explicit comparison to the participant's retained reason. Invite extension, a supported relevant distinction, open revision of the prior reason, or an unresolved answer. Preserve the exact response; do not force extension to obtain completion payment.
2. **Facts and comprehension:** the same facts and testimony, in the same amount of time, with matched comprehension exercises but no explicit challenge connecting them to the earlier commitment.
3. **Recipient perspective:** the same facts and testimony, in the same amount of time, with a structured exercise obtaining and accurately restating the recipient's stated condition and preference. It supplies an established informational and interpersonal alternative to the explicit reason challenge.

Scripts must be matched for contact time, source credibility, reading burden, and access to true facts. Their framing will differ by design; the result estimates the full instructional contrast, not a chemically isolated logical operation. Demand and perceived experimenter expectations are measured after primary choices as secondary diagnostics. Test administrators and the analyst of masked arm labels do not know the preferred hypothesis during scoring.

### Outcomes that distinguish widening from indiscriminate assent

At day 14, use previously unseen recipients and case combinations without the trainer or challenge script. Include eight cases where the retained compensatory reason applies and eight with a supported disanalogy, such as the extra task already receiving its agreed additional payment. Randomize case order. The primary matched-case outcome is each participant's mean fraction of the optional decision budget transferred to eligible unfamiliar recipients. A second outcome is the mean rate of explicitly judging the **specified compensatory reason** applicable in disanalogy cases. It is not defined as all generosity to those recipients. Record changes to the original commitment and other stated reasons separately.

Immediate outcomes are secondary. A day-90 follow-up, if funded and frozen before the main run, is a separate registered durability endpoint; day-14 success alone must not be called permanent renewal. False analogy, withdrawal of consent, and contradicted work evidence need separate response options. Merely increasing every transfer is insufficient evidence of selective reason revision.

For each participant also define behavioral selectivity S as mean matched-case transfer minus mean disanalogy-case transfer, so S lies in [−1,1]. Higher matched transfers with unchanged stated discrimination alone do not identify selective behavioral transfer: giving could increase equally in every case. This contrast tests whether behavior changes more where the specified reason applies. It does not make all generosity in disanalogy cases an ethical error or identify the actor's private motive.

There are six confirmatory comparisons: the reason arm versus each active control on matched-case transfer, behavioral selectivity, and disanalogy misapplication. The proposed practical effect is a transfer difference above 0.10 on the zero-to-one scale and a selectivity difference above 0.05. Proposed noninferiority requires the increase in disanalogy misapplication to be below 0.05. These are prospective design margins to debate **before** data and assess during blinded feasibility and power work, not quantities dictated by the theory.

Use participant-level means and stratification-adjusted differences. The frozen implementation should produce six simultaneous two-sided confidence intervals with total family error at most 0.05. Bonferroni allocates 0.05/6 to each interval, or 0.05/12 to each tail. Favorable and adverse conclusions must use this same family: inspecting an opposite bound does not receive a new uncounted error allowance. At the fixed sample, asymptotic studentized randomization or robust regression intervals require a documented validity argument and a preregistered sensitivity analysis. Do not label a permutation test of a sharp null as a test of every heterogeneous zero-average null. Repeated trials cannot be used to multiply the effective sample size. A blind simulation using plausible outcome dispersion and attrition must justify the final sample before registration; 3,600 is a planning choice, not a completed power analysis.

### Missing observations and decision rules

Analyze everyone as randomized. Missing day-14 outcomes remain missing rather than counting automatically as uncaring. Compute adverse-bound analyses: for a lower treatment-effect bound assign missing reason-arm transfer zero and missing control transfer one; reverse this for an upper bound. For an upper bound on disanalogy misapplication assign missing reason-arm errors one and missing control errors zero; reverse for a lower bound. For a lower selectivity-effect bound assign missing reason-arm S=−1 and missing control S=+1; reverse for an upper bound. Bound partially missing matched and disanalogy case means separately before subtraction. These are bounding assignments, not claims about what missing participants chose. Recompute the correspondingly allocated one-sided bounds to form simultaneous missing-data envelopes; their full coverage argument must be validated before registration. The confirmatory acceptance rule requires all six margins under the adverse assignments; otherwise practical settlement is inconclusive despite a completion-only pass. Completion-only and prespecified imputation analyses are sensitivities. Selective follow-up cannot manufacture persistence.

**Scoped practical support** requires both transfer lower bounds to exceed 0.10, both behavioral-selectivity lower bounds to exceed 0.05, and both upper bounds on excess disanalogy misapplication to stay below 0.05, including the adverse missingness analyses above. It also requires the predefined integrity checks. Report positive lower bounds that fail their practical margin as positive-only evidence for that comparison; do not substitute a point estimate for a confidence bound. Increased transfer alone is not evidence that every selectivity criterion passed. This supports this instructional intervention contrast and population, not privileged access to moral truth, identification of inner motive, or superiority over every theory.

**Practical rejection** of a claimed transfer or selectivity advantage occurs if its upper bound is below 0.10 or 0.05 respectively. **An adverse result** occurs if the lower bound on excess disanalogy misapplication exceeds 0.05 or the upper bound on matched-transfer difference is below zero. These opposite bounds belong to the same six simultaneous intervals. Otherwise the relevant comparison is **inconclusive**. Mixed results remain mixed: matching factual exposure but outperforming testimony does not count as passing both comparisons. No post hoc redefinition of the primary reason, favored subgroup, or endpoint is allowed.

Recruitment ends at the registered number or the registered calendar cutoff, whichever occurs first. Do not inspect arm outcomes to increase the sample, rewrite scripts, or substitute tasks. Predefined privacy, recipient-payment, or severe protocol-integrity failures halt collection and are disclosed; technical duplicates are handled under rules fixed before assignment. Negative results receive the same report. Any repair is a separately labeled new study.

## How to test comparative value without a weak opponent

The human trial compares mechanisms, not entire philosophical systems. A separate prediction comparison must first turn the integrated theory into a model making probabilistic predictions from information available before action. Retrospective explanations that can accommodate every outcome cannot win a forecasting comparison.

Use independent task selection and freeze, before held-out outcomes, predictions for actual protection, claim intake, legitimate rejection, delay, private cost, and maintenance at succession. Conventional comparators should include a finite Bayesian or candidate-elimination learner where a rule family is supplied; reinforcement learning with social preferences and an information-acquisition option; and a structural causal or dynamic decision model including budgets, deadlines, and strategic interference. A complete conventional decision model containing the same mechanisms may be mathematically equivalent to our operational model. In that case equal performance is expected and does not become novelty through renamed variables.

Give all comparators the same observation history, available interventions, training split, and stated resource accounting. Fit flexible comparators using nested training validation; do not compare a tuned proposal to an untuned default. Reserve independent environments or organizations for final testing, rather than randomly splitting adjacent decisions from one lineage. Score binary events by log loss or Brier score, continuous outcomes by declared loss, and record calibration and actual implementation cost. Preregister one primary score and a practical superiority margin, then report uncertainty at the environment or organization level.

The programme may prove useful through organizing neglected variables, exposing a false independence assumption, or generating a new testable intervention even if conventional inference methods implement it. Those are defensible contributions, but they require their own evidence. No current experiment establishes minimum-description-length optimality, universal explanatory superiority, or a unique root of wisdom.

## Turn a directional intuition into a population question

Three propositions must be kept separate:

1. **A logical guarantee:** every member of a declared class becomes more inclusively protective. One admissible counterexample settles that proposition negatively. The existing models contain such counterexamples in their own class.
2. **A statistical tendency:** under a declared sampling distribution, a specified change is positive with a stated frequency or average. A constructed counterexample says nothing by itself about its frequency in nature.
3. **An intervention effect:** changing inquiry, authority, incentives, or resources improves protection compared with an alternative. Randomized success can identify this effect without showing that unaided natural evolution selects the intervention.

An initial feasible population question could concern all organizations in a publicly frozen registry that administer a specified assistance programme during a fixed two-year interval. Eligibility cannot be “organizations already known to be regenerative.” Draw probability samples before outcome inspection. Count organizations that close, lose records, or expel claimants; survival and available complaints cannot define the denominator after the fact. Freeze target recipients and reason-specific criteria with affected-party participation before coding outcomes. Track actual timely support, valid claim rejection, correction of erroneous approvals, resource consumption, and exit, rather than equating more claims admitted with more justice.

Such a study estimates a trajectory in that registry and period. It will require field partners, lawful data access, an audit of missing and suppressed claims, and independent outcome coding; those observations are not in the repository. Observational trends alone do not identify the cause. A randomized rollout of an independently funded appeal route could estimate an intervention effect, while a wider prospective cohort estimates prevalence. Neither establishes that the universe favors wisdom. A physical universal would need an argument linking independently justified physical premises to the ethically interpreted outcome across its entire claimed domain.

There is therefore no responsible numeric prevalence estimate to compute from the current corpus: its examples were chosen to construct or challenge mechanisms, and repeated saved copies are not sampled populations. This is an identifiability finding about the available evidence, not a prediction that the desired tendency is absent.

## What is missing from a theory of experience

Functional concern asks how another's condition changes representation, valuation, and action. Phenomenal experience asks why and under which conditions there is something it is like to undergo a process. A positive coefficient, a self-report, or a persistent corrective loop does not by definition answer that question.

A simple identification point is decisive for the present evidence. If two candidate bridges assign different experiential states while producing the same distribution of every measured outcome under every intervention allowed by this study, then every statistic computed from this study has the same distribution under both. No analysis of these data can distinguish them. This follows directly by applying the statistic to identical observable distributions. It is not a theorem that experience can never be investigated; it identifies what an additional test must change.

A proposed bridge must specify its physical domain, its relation to reports and other measurements, counterfactual interventions, and at least one prediction that differs from serious alternatives. It must also explain how the measurement is validated without simply defining experience as the measured functional capacity. Preregister the divergent prediction and permit defeat or revision, following the structure illustrated by the consciousness collaboration. The present concern model contains no such bridge, so a ready consciousness experiment cannot honestly be extracted from it by adding a label.

## The strongest present conclusion

The theory now has constructive mechanisms, obstruction results, and limited empirical support for one operational bridge. Its empirical frontier is not an indistinct remainder: selective reason revision, its durability, its transport across populations, its advantage over alternatives, and a bridge to experience are separate claims with separate evidence requirements.

The next decisive evidence would show whether warranted challenge changes costly treatment of genuinely new recipients while preserving supported distinctions, then whether that achievement survives incentives, obstruction, and succession in independently observed settings. Passing these tests would enlarge the established domain. Failing them would identify which claimed bridge needs revision. Neither outcome licenses pretending that the unmeasured remainder has already been settled.
