## Contributions and composition

Three agents developed disjoint formal, normative, and evidence branches. Two additional agents independently reviewed the formal game and the normative/evidence work. The coordinator compared their premises, revised the empirical protocol, integrated the theory, preserved provenance, and connected the result to the existing repository. All substantive files remain in the established research study or its canonical theory and source directories.

The formal branch answers an actual joint-choice question. It does not just attach a fixed update rule to a desired protective action: earlier and later selves, the persistent operator, cheaper audit products, selective gates, suppression, payment order, and successor reconstruction belong to one finite game. Its infinite existence statement relies on the written discounted-tail proof, not a long finite simulation.

The normative branch defends a positive ethical kernel and derives finite consequences for choice under grounded disagreement. Its rule does not prove the truth of model admission or select one action in every conflict. The coordinator explicitly rejected a tempting shortcut: imposing that ethical action filter on the strategic game can change the equilibrium and finances. The combined chapter therefore explains their interface without asserting an unproved general compatibility theorem.

The evidence branch retains both favorable and limiting evidence. The saved AI experiment is evidence of a task-specific correction-and-handoff mechanism. Rescoring and checking its source files add no observations; complete evidence also lets a conventional rule learner solve the task. The future human protocol is preparation for empirical inquiry, not present settlement of concern's causal origin.

## Findings that changed the research

| Finding | Resolution | Preserved limit |
|---|---|---|
| An arbitrary cheaper superset gate can change the operator's safeguarding bill and break the proposed continuation. | Assume monotone subset prices and select exactly the admitted-set gate when supersets tie. Keep the counterexample in the independent review. | The theorem is sufficient and repertoire-dependent; it is not a theorem for arbitrary gate markets. |
| A forced-open commitment was insufficiently explicit about withdrawal. | Specify that it removes both later protection and withdrawal, while the operator can still refuse. | Another commitment technology needs its own game. |
| The infinite proof needs finite branching and stable continuation state. | Require a finite extra-product menu; contexts and opportunities are exogenous and independent of earlier harms and record choices. Production starts after audit/control setup. | No endogenous context discovery or unmodeled setup harm is settled. |
| The current audit can reconstruct the complete finite record. | State that durable provenance is actually selected, but inherited storage's causal necessity is not proved. | Strong memory dependence needs another reconstruction constraint. |
| A favorable aggregate concern weight can conceal sacrifice of one admitted patient. | Use a sufficient individual concern margin and test all jointly excluding attacks. Retain every patient's actual outcome. | Two harms outside the witness's admitted set remain visible. |
| Increased giving everywhere could pass the original human protocol. | Add matched-minus-disanalogy behavioral selectivity and two prespecified comparisons. | The contrast does not prove motive or the ethical wrongness of other generosity. |
| Opposite statistical verdicts used directions outside the original confidence family. | Use six simultaneous two-sided intervals, including both directions and missing-data envelopes. | The actual interval implementation, coverage validation and power analysis are still required. |
| An average behavioral effect can come from already-inclusive people. | Narrow the pooled claim to selective costly behavioral response; record baseline restrictions and changed admission separately. | A confirmatory claim of boundary removal needs its own prespecified estimand and design. |
| Neutral evaluative significance does not identify the direction of concern. | Define direct concern as counting the other's benefit in favor of action and harm against it, apart from private consequences. | Functional orientation does not explain phenomenal experience. |

## Verification actually performed

The formal author checked 4,096 four-node directed graphs, 24,576 single-certificate retractions, 162 authority cases, and 24 complete finite-game parameter sets with exact rational arithmetic. Independent reconstruction uses forward reachable balances and backward value tables, with graph reachability for cuts, importing no author model functions. It reproduces the six-episode reserve path `5, 16, 26, 37, 47, 57, 67`, discounted operator value `269/32`, and total harm 2. It also checks nine further games, four failure controls, 220 monotone-price cases, and a separate lower bound for the finite feature construction.

The ethical author checks all 24,389 ordered triples of the 29 preorders on three actions, with 146,334 renaming comparisons. The independent reviewer instead constructs 355 preorders on four actions and checks 126,025 ordered pairs, including 143,288 maximal-witness checks. These are different finite reconstructions of the stated order properties, not independent evidence for moral truth.

The evidence author and reviewer independently checked the 160 listed source hashes. The reviewer also rescored all 1,024 saved successor answers; the experimental unit remains the founder lineage, not an individual answer. Its [independent checker](check_normative_evidence_independent.py) checks the repaired six-comparison alpha arithmetic. It does not implement or validate the future human trial's intervals.

The coordinator checks repository-relative links, JSON readability, agreement between the reported witness and results, the protocol's unrun status and practical thresholds, and ownership of staged changes. The resulting [release check](../release-checks.json) records the actual checks and final file hashes. These checks support the integrity of this delivery; they do not increase empirical confidence in the broader theory.
