# Detecting a consequential distinction missing from the current model

AI-assisted mathematical development, 5 October 2026. This is a conditional discovery and correction result within the shared operational specification. It is not a claim to a complete ontology, an automatic direction of nature, or a theorem of unrestricted invention.

**A model can answer every question currently put to it and still merge situations that require different responses.** A missing distinction becomes detectable when a physically obtainable extension of inquiry produces different recorded consequences in those situations. The positive result below gives a concrete, paid search that finds every covered distinction with a nonzero response gap, with a simultaneous error guarantee. It does not require the old model to produce an anomaly first, or assume a probability that a nominated candidate is useful.

The corresponding requirement for the larger theory is substantive: maintain access to ways of challenging the current repertoire, retain the evidence that makes a difference actionable, and pay for revising the organization that uses it. Neither correct prediction within an existing vocabulary nor continued operation alone supplies those conditions.

## 2. A missing distinction is relative to an actual question and interface

Use the preparations, observations, implementable protocols, recorded laws, resource ledger and functional tests of [the specification](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-SPECIFICATION.md). The learner, its apparatus, records, surroundings and support belong to the joint situation. The notation below partitions roles in that situation; it does not place an observer outside it.

Let \(\mathcal E_0\) be a currently available family of paid experiments. An experiment includes its preparation, apparatus construction where needed, actions, observation rule, horizon, resource use and protected conditions. For two preparations \(p,q\), define

\[
p\sim_{\mathcal E_0}q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal L_p(T_e)=\mathcal L_q(T_e)
\quad\text{for every }e\in\mathcal E_0,                 \tag{D1}
\]

where \(T_e\) is the transcript handed to the current operational interface. If that interface can read a preparation identifier, it is part of the transcript and can already distinguish the preparations; acquiring access to a previously unread research record is an interface expansion. Compare protocols admitted at both preparations, or explicitly include an unavailable-attempt outcome. A marginal success probability is not a substitute for the joint transcript law when later actions can depend on the transcript.

A current representation \(r\) can merge \(p,q\). An operational witness against its sufficiency is a common, physically implemented experiment \(e\), together with an accessible way of obtaining the two contexts, for which

\[
r(p)=r(q),\qquad
\Delta=\left|\mathbb E_pY_e-\mathbb E_qY_e\right|>0,
\qquad 0\le Y_e\le1.                                 \tag{D2}
\]

The preparation tag or candidate context feature must be available before the measured outcome. Otherwise the proposed split can amount to sorting by the answer after observing it. A tag can remain in a paid research record while being absent from the current operational summary. If the grouping information was erased, its recovery requires a new physical measurement or preparation, if either is available.

If \(e\notin\mathcal E_0\), (D2) need not be an error on any old question. It exposes the boundary of the old representation. If \(e\in\mathcal E_0\), it instead refutes the representation's claimed adequacy on its own domain. These are different findings, both useful.

A distinguishing program may compose existing actions, construct a new sensor from available parts, inspect a previously retained joint record, or build a composite feature from a supplied grammar. The theorem does not require that the useful program already be labeled useful. It does require its finite construction to occur within the admitted repertoire. Creating an experimental apparatus can itself change physical capability; the comparison must include those changes and costs rather than attributing everything to learning.

No joint hidden value for incompatible experiments is assumed here. Each \(e\) has its own physically recorded law. The classical sequential sampling assumptions used below are additional assumptions about the executed trials, not an ontology of all possible measurements.

### Necessary information, not necessarily a pre-existing anomaly

For any two models of the whole experiment, let \(P_0^T,P_1^T\) be their laws for the accessible transcript, including the learner's adaptive choices and randomization. Every detector \(D(T)\in[0,1]\) obeys

\[
\mathbb E_1D\le\mathbb E_0D+
\|P_1^T-P_0^T\|_{\rm TV}.                              \tag{D3}
\]

This follows directly from the defining variational bound for total variation. If the transcript laws coincide, detection power is no greater than false-alarm probability. More computation on that same transcript does not remove the limit. A new informative intervention, observation, or accessible record can remove it; merely renaming the model cannot.

The E/U construction in prospective revision already proves that an old-data anomaly is not a necessary precursor of a useful missing question. Therefore an anomaly-only trigger cannot be the general discovery mechanism. A continuing challenge route is required whenever its covered possibilities include such cases.

## 3. A concrete positive discovery theorem

### Operational premises

Fix a current representation \(r_0\). Enumerate candidate programs \(k=1,2,\ldots\). A finite enumeration is a special case. Each program specifies two obtainable contexts \(j\in\{0,1\}\) merged by \(r_0\), a common assay, and an implementable candidate distinction. The construction instructions and costs can be supplied without supplying the answer to the assay.

The following are independently checkable obligations of an application, not conclusions of the theorem.

1. **Coverage and interpretable context.** A distinction of interest has some finite index in the enumeration. Its two context preparations or selection rules and its assay have a fixed interpretation. Feature construction can occur inside the program; the candidate is frozen before fresh validation. Enumerating programs with verified safety certificates is one possible implementation. This does not imply that all safe programs can be recognized, or that every future concept belongs to the grammar.
2. **Repeatable recorded response.** For every fresh trial of program \(k\) in context \(j\), the bounded outcome \(X\in[0,1]\) satisfies
   \[
   \mathbb E[X\mid\text{all information before that trial}]=p_{kj}.
                                                               \tag{D4}
   \]
   These means stay fixed while this claim is tested. Conditional independence is sufficient but unnecessary. (D4) allows bounded martingale noise. It excludes unmodeled depletion, transport shifts or changing context composition that change the response. Such effects require a different joint preparation or a revised target, not a claim that the concentration bound repaired them.
3. **Safe continuation and actual support.** Every finite scheduled prefix can be executed, including actual setup, safe preparation or reset, waiting, memory, computation, apparatus upkeep, candidate construction, and reserved correction costs. Each job delivers an entry from which the next job is permitted and feasible. The schedule has finite realized duration through every finite stage and preserves its declared protected conditions. An expected budget alone does not suffice. Neither unbounded memory nor free reset is implicit.
4. **Correctability.** A detected distinction has a physically available representation update and accessible readout. Updates for any requested finite collection are jointly implementable, retain previously needed information, and are funded before the final validation is bought. Separate availability of mutually incompatible measurements would not establish that joint readout. Installing a distinction does not automatically authorize a new control action or show that every other prediction is correct.

These assumptions do not say that every candidate succeeds, or that a candidate is useful with a supplied probability. They say what can be tested, what the recorded observations mean, and what the implementation can afford. The only truth condition on a particular omission is the positive gap \(\Delta_k=|p_{k1}-p_{k0}|>0\).

### The search

At stage \(m\ge1\), execute every program \(k\le m\), obtaining \(m\) **fresh** trials in each context. Let their empirical means be \(\widehat p_{kj}^{(m)}\). Fix a total error allowance \(0<\delta<1\), and set

\[
\delta_{km}=\frac{\delta}{k(k+1)m(m+1)},\qquad
r_{km}=\sqrt{\frac{\log(4/\delta_{km})}{2m}}.             \tag{D5}
\]

Declare an operational omission only if

\[
\left|\widehat p_{k1}^{(m)}-
\widehat p_{k0}^{(m)}\right|>2r_{km}.                    \tag{D6}
\]

This schedule is deliberately simple rather than sample-optimal. It can prioritize other candidates between the required jobs, provided fresh-data validity, costs and eventual completion of each required stage are preserved. It never requires an archival anomaly to admit a candidate.

### Theorem D1: sound discovery of every covered positive-margin omission

Under the four operational premises, with probability at least \(1-\delta\):

- every declaration (D6), over all executed programs and stages, refers to a genuine response difference;
- every covered witness with \(\Delta_k>0\) is detected after a finite stage;
- every finite set of such witnesses can be detected and its funded distinctions installed after a finite physical time.

The last statement uses the explicit correctability and finite-duration premises. Without them the first two are guarantees about evidence only.

**Proof.** Conditional Hoeffding's lemma for a bounded variable with conditional mean \(p\) gives the exponential bound for the sum of the centered outcomes; iterating conditional expectations over \(m\) trials yields

\[
\Pr\{|\widehat p_{kj}^{(m)}-p_{kj}|>r_{km}\}
\le2e^{-2mr_{km}^2}=\delta_{km}/2.
\]

Apply the union bound to the two contexts and all \(k\le m\). Since

\[
\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{m}\delta_{km}
\le\delta\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{n(n+1)}\right)^2
=\delta,
\]

there is one event of probability at least \(1-\delta\) on which every interval is correct. On that event, an observed gap greater than \(2r_{km}\) entails a true gap greater than zero. For fixed \(k\), \(r_{km}\to0\) as \(m\to\infty\). Once \(r_{km}<\Delta_k/4\), the observed gap is at least \(\Delta_k-2r_{km}>2r_{km}\), so the witness is declared. The schedule reaches that finite stage under the physical premises. Taking the maximum of the finitely many stages needed by a finite witness set, and applying the funded updates, proves the last claim. ∎

This simultaneously covers all fixed enumerated witnesses; no additional union over the witnesses is required. It does not supply a finite stage at which the process knows that no further consequential omission exists. Retaining a growing family of distinctions requires the growing implementation capacity charged in premise 3. A finite physical implementation can instead use a finite enumerated family or a declared finite target set.

If candidates are adaptively created, the same argument applies only with a valid conditional fresh-test construction: freeze the context rules, assay and interpretation before obtaining its validation data, give each created program its own error allocation, and preserve (D4). Repeatedly changing a candidate in response to its validation outcomes is not covered by the simple block calculation.

### Finite deadline with declared coverage and margin

Suppose the relevant witness has index at most \(K\) and gap at least \(\gamma>0\). A sufficient final stage is any \(M\ge K\) with

\[
\sqrt{\frac{\log[4K(K+1)M(M+1)/\delta]}{2M}}
<\frac\gamma4.                                        \tag{D7}
\]

Such an integer exists and can be computed before the run. At stage \(M\), all witnesses among the first \(K\) whose gaps are at least \(\gamma\) are detected on the same good event. The inequality follows from the largest radius among \(k\le K\); it does not claim this conservative schedule is statistically efficient.

Let \(b_{km}\) cover program construction and setup, \(c_{km}\) bound the full cost of each of the \(2m\) trials, \(v_{km}\) cover validation, records and upkeep, and \(d_{km}\) reserve the cost of any allowed correction. A conservative full work bill is

\[
W_M=\sum_{m=1}^{M}\sum_{k=1}^{m}
\bigl(b_{km}+2m c_{km}+v_{km}+d_{km}\bigr).              \tag{D8}
\]

Already retained apparatus or records can reduce the actual bill, but the claim must then charge their ongoing maintenance and any capacity constraint. A correction that has already been installed need not be installed again; reserving \(d_{km}\) each time is only a worst-case bound. Scalar work accounting does not replace hard material, time, protection or permission constraints.

### Linking inquiry to renewed support

In the already proved two-support model, operation and inquiry satisfy

\[
\dot c_i=\alpha_i u_i-\delta_i c_i,\quad c_i\ge c_i^*,
\quad u_1+u_2\le1,
\]

and the exact nonmaintenance work frontier is

\[
B(T)=T-p_1(T-t_1)_+-p_2(T-t_2)_+,
\quad p_i=\delta_i c_i^*/\alpha_i.                      \tag{D9}
\]

Under that model's additional assumptions of divisible resumable jobs, no job decay, feedback at completion, and no extra calendar waits or sampling changes, \(W_M\le B(T)\) pays for stage \(M\) while maintaining both supports. Thus (D7) and (D8) give a concrete confidence-and-deadline condition on top of the maintenance law.

If \(1-p_1-p_2>0\), every finite work bill is eventually affordable in that specified work model. If \(p_1+p_2=1\), the lifetime allowance is only \(p_1t_1+p_2t_2\), even though both capacities can remain alive indefinitely. Maintaining the possibility of inquiry is therefore weaker than financing enough inquiry to reveal a particular omission. Unbounded scalar work alone still does not establish the other physical premises of D1.

### Safety is a separate simultaneous obligation

The main theorem assumes pathwise protection for the admitted jobs. If instead job \((k,m)\) has conditional protection-failure probability at most \(\beta_{km}\), including preparation and correction, with \(\sum\beta_{km}\le\beta\), and the process safely aborts on failure, the joint probability of safe completion and the stated statistical guarantees is at least \(1-\beta-\delta\), provided the bounds hold at every reached entry. This is the specification's continuation principle plus a union bound. It does not license an irreversible harmful experiment merely because its harm probability has been assigned a budget.

## 4. When the missing distinction is consequential

A statistically detectable difference need not change anything that the current task requires. Consequence must be established against an independently specified test, constraint or decision problem.

### A protocol can cross a reliability threshold

Suppose for a common installed protocol \(\pi\) and specified functional event \(E\),

\[
\Pr_p^{\pi}(E)\le\alpha-\gamma,
\qquad
\Pr_q^{\pi}(E)\ge\alpha+\gamma.                         \tag{D10}
\]

Fresh confidence intervals narrow enough to lie on opposite sides of \(\alpha\) certify a consequence of the omitted distinction: the same protocol cannot be transferred across the coarse cell with the asserted reliability. Equation (D6) alone certifies a gap, not a threshold crossing; the latter needs those additional interval comparisons.

This does **not**, by itself, prove that \(p\) lacks a capability that \(q\) possesses. The specification's capability region quantifies over every admitted protocol. A different protocol could succeed at \(p\). Certifying exclusion from that region requires a uniform upper bound over the admitted class, for example an exhaustively validated finite protocol library with a common error budget, or a separate impossibility theorem. Otherwise the finding concerns this protocol, or a lower bound on a capability region.

### A sharp decision consequence

Consider two contexts merged by the operational summary, occurring with probabilities \(w_0,w_1>0\), \(w_0+w_1=1\), and two admitted actions \(A,B\). Let expected losses lie in \([0,1]\), and suppose

\[
L_0(B)-L_0(A)\ge\eta>0,
\qquad L_1(A)-L_1(B)\ge\eta.                           \tag{D11}
\]

These causal action losses require their own identification: appropriate interventions or a justified physical model, rather than an arbitrary observational association. Other available observations must not secretly identify the context; otherwise the proposed coarse policy restriction is false.

Every policy choosing \(A\) with one context-blind probability \(q\) incurs regret, relative to choosing \(A\) in context 0 and \(B\) in context 1, of at least

\[
\eta\{w_0(1-q)+w_1q\}\ge\eta\min(w_0,w_1).             \tag{D12}
\]

The first expression counts the probability of using the wrong action times its loss gap. It is affine in \(q\), so its minimum is the smaller endpoint. Thus a missing distinction can impose an unavoidable loss on every policy using only the old representation, not merely on one badly chosen policy.

If the installed distinction is usable before acting, misclassifies the context with probability at most \(\epsilon\), and adds per-use cost \(c\) in those **declared loss units**, its expected loss is at most the ideal context-sensitive loss plus \(\epsilon+c\). Consequently its gain over every coarse policy is at least

\[
\eta\min(w_0,w_1)-\epsilon-c.                          \tag{D13}
\]

For \(n\) applications with the same conditional bounds, and a sunk inquiry/installation cost \(D\) expressed in the same units, the net expected gain is at least

\[
n\{\eta\min(w_0,w_1)-\epsilon-c\}-D.                   \tag{D14}
\]

This is a sufficient benefit condition, not a proof that all knowledge pays for itself. If energy, protected lives, time and money are not commensurable in the task, retain the physical ledger and constraint vector; do not manufacture a moral conversion rate to use (D14). Losses and action permissions are supplied and justified separately.

The formal bridge is now explicit: **fresh contrast → failure of a stated compression → an available refinement → a verified task consequence → funded implementation**. Skipping any arrow can turn a true observation into an unsupported claim of wisdom or generative improvement.

## 5. What no unrestricted guarantee can provide

These obstructions preserve the positive target by specifying what its support and coverage conditions must accomplish. They are not counted as proving the desired universal positive direction.

### Finite work cannot guarantee finding an arbitrarily located witness

Let candidate experiment \(e_j\), \(j=1,2,\ldots\), cost at least one unit. In world \(W_0\), every candidate returns 0. In world \(W_j\), every response is identical except that \(e_j\) returns 1 and reveals the stipulated consequential distinction. Take any possibly randomized adaptive procedure whose expected number of distinct tested candidates under \(W_0\) is at most \(N<\infty\).

Write \(q_j=\Pr_0(e_j\text{ is tested})\). Then \(\sum_jq_j\le N\), so for every \(\varepsilon>0\) there is a \(j\) with \(q_j\le\varepsilon\). Couple the procedure's randomness in \(W_0,W_j\). Until it tests \(e_j\), the transcripts coincide and therefore its subsequent choices agree. Its transcript laws have total variation at most \(q_j\le\varepsilon\). By (D3), a detector with false-alarm probability at most \(\delta\) under \(W_0\) detects that alternative with probability at most \(\delta+\varepsilon\).

Thus there is no uniform finite-budget guarantee over all locations of a consequential omission, even in this elementary deterministic family. The pointwise eventual guarantee in D1 and the finite bound with supplied \(K\) in (D7) are consistent with this obstruction.

### A finite number of noisy trials cannot resolve every positive gap

Even for one fixed candidate, compare Bernoulli outcomes of means \(1/2\) and \(1/2+\varepsilon\). Couple each trial with the same uniform random variable. A mismatch occurs with probability \(\varepsilon\), so over at most \(n\) informative trials transcript total variation is at most \(n\varepsilon\), including adaptive choices coupled until a mismatch. For any fixed \(n\), choose \(\varepsilon\) arbitrarily small. Equation (D3) again forbids a uniformly powerful test of every nonzero gap. A declared margin, or unbounded continued sampling, is necessary for the corresponding uniform claim.

### Accessible truth need not be safely discoverable

If two situations have identical laws for every uniformly safe experiment, while the only distinguishing probe irreversibly violates a protected condition in one situation, a sure-safe process cannot use that probe to distinguish them. This follows from (D3) restricted to the safe experiment family. Different permissions or an additional protective technology might change the family; their availability is a new physical and normative premise.

Similarly, a past distinction with no remaining trace and no distinguishable future experiment cannot be recovered by refining present language alone. This is the retained-information limit from the causal foundations, not a claim that the past never mattered.

### Verification does not supply the formation of the verifier

D1 constructs an admitted search policy and proves its performance. It does not establish that the shared renewal network will spontaneously build its apparatus, finance its schedule, preserve its records, or install its conclusions. An autonomous application must exhibit those transitions under one common law, and compare outcomes from the declared initial ensemble with all formation and failure branches included. Giving the process the policy after the fact would reopen the same supplied-architecture gap encountered in the physical studies.

Nor does a countable program grammar guarantee coverage of every conceivable meaning or purpose. A finite stopping certificate that the model is complete for an unrestricted future question set is not delivered. The complete claim here is relative: covered operational distinctions, their paid validation, and their specified consequences.

## 6. A small exact check: composition exposes an omitted future

The [exact checker](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/discovery/exact_check.py) and [recorded results](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/discovery/exact-check.json) provide a transparent finite witness, not empirical evidence or a simulation of universal discovery.

There are four preparations and one supplied primitive \(u\):

\[
a\xrightarrow{u}c,\qquad b\xrightarrow{u}a,\qquad
c\xrightarrow{u}d,\qquad d\xrightarrow{u}d.
\]

The recorded target is whether the endpoint is \(d\). Programs are finite compositions \(u^\ell\). The exact response table is

| Preparation | No move | One move | Two moves | Three moves |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| a | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| b | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| c | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| d | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

A representation of the currently asked zero- and one-move endpoint questions merges \(a,b\) without error. The composed test \(u;u\) separates them. The primitive dynamics did not change, and the useful composition was not a new primitive. The old representation was sufficient for its finite endpoint-question family; it was **not** a valid recursively closed Markov quotient. Recording the full next preparation would already have supplied additional information, which this deliberately restricted old interface does not record.

The check enumerates the four programs of lengths 0 through 3. Each costs one unit to construct. Each of its four trials costs two units for preparation/reset, \(\ell\) units for actions, one for readout and one for retaining the preparation-tagged record. Installing the refined response table costs four more. Total cost is

\[
4+4(4+5+6+7)+4=96.
\]

Every prefix is payable from a declared 100-unit budget while reserving the final installation bill. In the already supplied maintenance model with both supports at their floors and \(p_1=p_2=1/4\), the work clock provides exactly 96 units by time 192. These prices, reset capability, deterministic transition law and support dynamics are assumptions of the check, not measurements or a claim that real tests have these costs. One observation per preparation is exact only under the stipulated deterministic model; it is not the noisy-sampling guarantee of D1.

The checker also verifies (D12)–(D14) for an independently supplied two-action loss example. With context weights \((1/3,2/3)\), wrong-action loss \(1/2\), context error at most \(1/60\), and access cost \(1/30\), the per-use lower gain is \(7/60\). For 900 uses, pricing the 96-unit inquiry bill at one unit of the declared loss gives a net lower bound of 9. This explicit conversion belongs only to the example. It does not identify physical work with ethical value.

All checker assertions use integers and exact rational arithmetic. The script verifies the finite table, program costs, installation reserve, loss bounds, and finite prefixes of the statistical error allocation. It does not constitute a machine proof of the countable theorem, establish the physical sampling assumptions, or show spontaneous emergence of the enumerator.

## 7. What is advanced, and what remains to be established

The original question was how an important possibility absent from the current model can be noticed. This note closes a named formal interface: a known class of current-model omissions can be detected by an explicit prospective search, with no anomaly gate and no supplied good-nomination probability, then connected to a warranted refinement and a funded task improvement. Existing finite feature construction explains one kind of candidate production; existing support renewal explains when the search bill can be paid; the theorem states precisely how those pieces compose.

It also changes the proposed root in a useful way. A process can preserve its present organization while exhausting its ability to ask an informative new question. Continued generativity capable of correction therefore needs more than reproduction of current structure: it needs support for obtaining new discriminating contact with its surroundings, for preserving that contact as usable information, and for changing the organization when the evidence warrants it. Whether those conditions are formed and maintained by a given physical process remains an empirical and mechanistic question.

Three meanings stay separate:

- **Discovery:** the process acquires warranted evidence for a distinction absent from its representation.
- **Physical creation:** it constructs an apparatus, action route or relationship that changes what can actually be done. Discovery may enable this, but an updated description alone does not do it.
- **Ethical recognition:** it judges a being or relationship to matter and changes its obligations. A difference in recorded response does not establish moral standing or its absence; the concern and settlement studies supply separate arguments and premises.

The next decisive application is to give a physically implemented learner a fixed paid interface and a prospectively declared held-out class containing anomaly-free omissions. Measure the whole chain: construction, maintained support, experiment selection, fresh evidence, retained context, safe correction and delivered consequences. Compare an anomaly-only policy and the explicit challenge schedule under the same initial resources and protected conditions. Failure to cover a witness, inability to pay for its trials, altered sampling law, loss of its records, and failed installation must remain distinct recorded failures. This tests an explanatory mechanism instead of defining the successful ensemble as the one that discovers.

No result here proves a universal spontaneous tendency toward discovery, increasing joint capability, or 善. Nor does an information-theoretic obstruction establish that such a positive tendency is impossible in a narrower, physically identified class. The open obligation is to derive and validate the formation and renewal of these discovery conditions under a common physical process and a declared initial/support ensemble.
