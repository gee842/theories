# Paid formation and turnover of alternative selector updaters

6 October 2026. AI-authored mathematical contribution to the [governing derivation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#accepted-work-sequence). The named connection is **a revisable selector whose updater is installed externally → alternative updater mechanisms formed, used, maintained and replaced within the same resource history**. This follows the [selector-revision construction](SELECTOR-REVISION-MATH.md), where random restart and directed failure feedback were separate, fixed experimental arms.

This round also adds an explicit paid connection from [actually formed molecular response variants](FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-MATH.md). It does not identify those molecules with algorithms. The retooling reaction, binary updater repertoire and maintenance chemistry are declared additional local laws. What is established is a conservative embedding of the original preparation events, exact transfer of their actual resources and material, and a continuation in which an updater changes and demonstrably governs a changed selector.

## 1. Alternative updaters are material state

Retain the previous selector with orientation m∈{0,1} and lifetime ℓ. It routes sensory bit b to b XOR m. The hidden plant condition z makes b XOR z productive; the controller never reads z or the reversal schedule. Add updater type U∈{R,D}, updater lifetime j, and an indicator recording whether a selector has ever been formed. Both updater and selector initially have zero lifetime and no active structure. Their two material backing slots are recyclable.

The two supplied updater implementations control **the next paid selector assembly**:

- R samples either orientation with equal probability.
- D chooses the opposite of an expired selector's retained orientation. If no selector has ever existed, it samples either orientation equally, just as R does.

The expired orientation is a physical residue accessible to both implementations. The directed updater is not told which channel is productive. In a noiseless stable condition, failure of one of two complementary mappings makes the other useful; noisy capture and subsequent condition changes can invalidate that inference.

The module's effect can be isolated while holding all other current state fixed. At a depleted selector with a retained orientation, R permits reconstruction of that same orientation, whereas D does not. The formal event law checks this difference. Merely occupying an updater slot or receiving return before this residue-informed choice does not demonstrate that its distinctive updating behavior has been tested.

Let g be updater formation cost, c selector assembly cost, d sensing/response construction cost, r selector renewal cost and u updater maintenance cost. Their maximum lifetimes are J and L. The primary values are

\[
\boxed{g=2,\quad c=1,\quad d=2,\quad r=u=1,
\quad G=6,\quad J=4,\quad L=2.}
\tag{1}
\]

Here G is energy in each source packet. All prices are positive and G≥r+u. The laws are supplied discrete costs, not molecular measurements.

Each exposure follows the declared order:

1. If updater lifetime is zero, pay g to form R or D with equal probability. Insufficient fuel leaves the organization unable to proceed.
2. If selector lifetime is zero, pay c and let the **currently active updater** choose its orientation. Both selector and updater must be active for a trial.
3. Prepay d, then perform one trial. A capture transfers G from the existing source to fuel and pays r+u; it restores both lifetimes and preserves both types. Without capture, both positive lifetimes decrease by one.

Previously paid formation is never refunded if a later payment cannot be made. Every opportunity consumes one of the finite source packets; unsuccessful or inactive opportunities waste it. At a condition reversal, the organization retains its actual fuel, selector, updater, residues and remaining source. The high-level rule that proposes R and D, their action definitions, their material slots and the return-maintenance coupling remain fixed architecture.

J=4>L=2 allows an updater to remain present long enough to govern a further selector reconstruction after two failures. This addresses a specific identification problem: a module whose only use was choosing the first selector has not yet exhibited the difference between R and D. A separate synchronized-lifetime J=L diagnostic supports the exact calculation below, but it must preserve the expired selector residue so the newly formed module actually makes a residue-informed choice.

## 2. A paid entrance from the previous formation law

The predecessor state records free A and H, a resident, the repaired site, a local fuel bit f, common fuel F and source energy E. Let A(s) and H(s) count all actual A and H, including the resident. The adapter is enabled only when:

\[
\text{site intact},\quad A(s)\ge1,\quad H(s)\ge1,
\quad f=1,\quad F\ge h,
\tag{2}
\]

with adapter cost h=1. Thus both response types were actually formed, repair actually occurred, and a source unit was actually captured. The adapter first pays h from **common** fuel, then retools the existing structures and makes the existing local unit available to the new compartment. Its incoming fuel is

\[
F_{\rm in}=F-h+f.
\tag{3}
\]

Requiring F≥h prevents the local unit from silently paying a reaction that, by definition, must precede its mobilization. This order is an explicit interface assumption.

One A and one H become backing material for two recyclable slots. If the predecessor has total material M, the adapter retains the remaining M−2 units. The new module and selector begin absent, so their g and c formation bills remain to be paid. The adapter does not install either functional mapping or updater for free. A later module disappearance returns its slot to precursor; the coarse model tracks its occupancy/lifetime, not detailed stoichiometry.

The predecessor's E was an energy count, whereas a new packet contains G units. Therefore source conversion is

\[
H_{\rm in}=\lfloor E/G\rfloor,\qquad e_{\rm rem}=E\bmod G.
\tag{4}
\]

The remainder stays in the ledger and is unavailable to the declared packet interface. It is neither silently discarded nor treated as another full packet. Combining (3)–(4) gives the exact interface identity

\[
\boxed{F_{\rm in}+GH_{\rm in}+e_{\rm rem}+h=F+f+E.}
\tag{5}
\]

The original depleted mixed-copy witness had F=f=E=0. It cannot pay this adapter. Its earlier captured state also had F=0 and cannot pay h from common fuel, even though f=1. The new study declares larger stocks before any preparation, rather than repairing that shortage with a handoff reset.

The primary preparation starts with M=2, F₀=18, E₀=97, neither response present and the site damaged. It uses the predecessor's zero-loss reference: equal unit formation, association, repair and capture coefficients, local copying enabled, with bulk copying, damage, wear and leakage absent. The adapter is taken when its guard becomes true. In this particular two-material reference, a qualifying state has no precursor and no remaining old reaction with positive propensity, so this priority suppresses no competing positive-rate predecessor reaction.

All qualifying preparations have A=H=1, an A resident, an intact site, f=1, F=14 and E=96. The adapter spends one common unit and transfers the existing local unit, producing **F_in=14 and sixteen six-unit packets**, with zero source remainder. Both new structures are still absent.

For this reference the probability of reaching the interface is exactly 2/9. The successful prefixes partition into:

|---|---:|
| H forms, A forms, A associates, repairs | 1/12 |
| A forms, H forms, A associates, repairs | 1/12 |

After these prefixes capture is the remaining reaction and occurs with probability one in the embedded jump chain. Their sum is 2/9. The competing prefixes include H occupying the only site, formation of two identical variants, and repair before the alternative forms. Those histories remain failures of this interface, not discarded observations. The coefficients cancel in the listed ratios because the competing unit-rate propensities have the same current fuel factor at each race.

## 3. One joined trace and two conservative interfaces

The formalization defines a phase-tagged state containing either a predecessor state or an updater-bearing compartment. Old-labelled transitions between old states are **exactly the existing `FormedSelection.Step` relation**, with no rewritten old event. A paid connect event relates an actual old state to its entrance (3)–(4). New-labelled events then use the updater law. Original preparation traces embed into this combined relation, and continuation traces concatenate through the actual paid entrance.

Consequently, for every such joined history,

\[
\boxed{\mathcal E_{\rm final}
+\text{new event expenditure and packet waste}
+h+\text{old event expenditure}
=\mathcal E_{\rm old\ initial},}
\tag{6}
\]

where the new energy includes fuel, all remaining source packets and its retained remainder. The material count M is preserved. Retooling changes which subsequent event law applies; it is an added physical assumption. The theorem proves composition under that assumption, not that real A/H chemistry naturally realizes digital routing modules.

The second interface projects new events onto the previous selector law. A selector assembly, trial, reversal or expiry maps exactly to its previous counterpart when that law's renewal price is **r+u**, because both current structures are maintained. Thus any new trace segment with **no updater-formation event** projects to an old selector trace at that combined price, with the same fuel and source at every point. Updater formation is an extra paid event and cannot simply be erased from a full-history projection. Directed choice restricts the old assembly possibilities; matching the previous unbiased stochastic kernel additionally requires R and a module that does not become unavailable. These are precise conditional interfaces, not a claim of whole-process stochastic equivalence to the earlier random-only mechanism.

A checked whole witness uses the primary initial stocks:

**old H/A formation → A association and repair → actual capture → paid adapter → R formation → selector formation → four productive trials → reversal → two failures → R reconstructs the old mapping → two further failures → D formation → D reconstructs the opposite mapping → productive trial.**

The updater has changed, its new rule has actually been used, and the new selector's consequence renews it. Final fuel is 9 and seven six-unit packets remain. Whole-history accounting is

\[

+59\text{ (new costs/waste)}+51\text{ (remaining energy)}.
\tag{7}
\]

This is an admissible constructive history, not a representative average. The executable comparison must average over all preparations and subsequent formations, including the 7/9 that never commission an updater-bearing compartment.

## 4. Sharp synchronized-expiry result

For an analytical diagnostic only, set J=L. Immediately after a reversal, suppose the old selector is known to have existed, is now unproductive, and both structures have residual lifetime k∈{0,…,L}. The k=0 case retains the expired selector orientation. Let inherited fuel be F and H source opportunities remain. Conditions stay fixed, productive capture is noiseless, and G≥r+u.

The k inherited trials fail and exhaust both structures. Each fresh bundle then pays g for a fair R/D module proposal and c for the selector it constructs. Given the still-obsolete orientation:

\[
\Pr(D\text{ and useful selector})=\tfrac12,\quad
\Pr(R\text{ and useful selector})=\tfrac14,\quad
\Pr(R\text{ and obsolete selector})=\tfrac14.
\tag{8}
\]

A failed bundle leaves the same obsolete orientation after L failures, so the same alternatives recur. The distinction is produced by the updater's rule on the retained residue, not by assigning D a higher score. If there is no prior orientation, both updaters initially choose uniformly and (8) does not apply to that first assembly.

After exactly j unsuccessful new bundles, the next useful trial requires

\[
\boxed{C_j=kd+g+c+d+j(g+c+Ld),\qquad T_j=k+1+jL.}
\tag{9}
\]

All charges precede the first usable return. Define C₀=kd+g+c+d and B=g+c+Ld. Let N=0 if F<C₀ or H<k+1; otherwise put

\[
N=1+\min\!\left(
\left\lfloor\frac{F-C_0}{B}\right\rfloor,
\left\lfloor\frac{H-k-1}{L}\right\rfloor
\right).
\tag{10}
\]

Exactly N possible first-success bundle positions fit the actual reserve and horizon. Thus

\[
\boxed{\Pr(\text{first recovery})=1-4^{-N}.}
\tag{11}
\]

For N>0, first success belongs to D with probability

\[
\boxed{\Pr(D\mid\text{first recovered fresh bundle})=\frac23.}
\tag{12}
\]

Indeed, at each permitted position j the D and R success masses are respectively 4⁻ʲ/2 and 4⁻ʲ/4. Their ratio remains 2:1 when summed over the same affordability window. The module proposal was unbiased; its distinct effect on paid selector assembly, followed by actual return-funded maintenance, creates this conditional retention bias.

The result concerns **fresh bundles at a particular synchronized entrance**, not unconditional updater frequency, fixation, superiority on every history or the J=4,L=2 primary experiment. It also does not assert broad concern: the retained mechanism obtains local return under the declared plant.

Using g=2,c=1,d=2,L=k=2 and H=8 gives C₀=9 and B=7. Recovery probability is zero for F<9, 3/4 for F=9,…,15, 15/16 for F=16,…,22, and 63/64 for F≥23. The eight-opportunity horizon permits only three fresh bundle positions. Higher reserve cannot overcome that time bound under this proposal law.

The deterministic pre-return affordability equivalence is Lean-checked through the earlier sequential-payment relation. Grouping adjacent formation charges as g+c is exact because both are nonnegative, there is no inflow between them, and both must precede the trial. The random probabilities and the correspondence between the event history and these bundle prefixes are separate mathematical arguments, checked against finite enumeration rather than formalized in Lean.

## 5. Comparators, failure and what is still supplied

A conventional two-level finite-state feedback controller can carry `(U,j,m,ℓ)`, use exactly these proposal rules and costs, and perform the same return-conditioned maintenance. Its physical-state kernel is identical. Renaming the mechanism cannot produce a performance advantage. This equivalence is a positive check that the proposed explanation has explicit operational content.

In the noiseless synchronized diagnostic, an always-D module law with the **same formation and maintenance prices** obtains recovery with probability one whenever the first bundle is affordable and timely. The endogenous fair R/D law obtains 1−4⁻ᴺ, and the always-R law obtains 1−2⁻ᴺ at the same complete bundle costs. This establishes a specific effect relative to the weaker updating rule while retaining the stronger conventional comparator. It gives no claim that random variation outperforms a correctly installed directed architecture.

Added architecture can make continuation impossible. From an entrance where both structures are absent, at least g+c+d fuel must be prepaid before the first trial; its future return cannot pay those startup bills. Charging only c+d would supply the updater for free. Likewise, the old depleted witness cannot pay the adapter. These are architecture-dependent obstructions, not evidence that every learning mechanism has those exact prices.

With noisy capture, a failure can falsely implicate a useful mapping, and D's forced reversal may be harmful. With unequal lifetimes, a newly formed updater can receive returns from an already active selector before it has governed any new proposal. Its occupancy or maintenance then does not identify the consequences of its own updating rule. Measures should separately count actual residue-informed proposal uses, updater changes, subsequent captures, and all initial failures. Outcome-blind renewal and updater-type clamping are interventions on this supplied architecture, not matched biological processes.

What is newly connected is concrete: actual preceding formation and capture supplies the material and energy for paid formation of update-rule variants; an updater changes inside that continuing organization; its rule determines a subsequent selector; that selector's material consequence renews both structures. The general trace and ledger cover the whole interface, and a complete witness makes each link actual.
