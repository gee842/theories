# Comparing correction from a recipient's consequences

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparator and assumption note for **formed predictive machinery → paid evidence of a recipient's actual outcome → correction of the target-specific action relation → later paid action for that recipient**. This connects the [prediction/action construction](PREDICTION-ACTION-INTEGRATION.md), its subsequent [coupling law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/PROTOCOL.md), and the [faithful-record interface](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/RECORDING-FIDELITY-INTEGRATION.md).

**Status: aligned with the [saved recipient-correction protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/PROTOCOL.md), corrected producer results and current independent suffix check.** Sections 1–6 specify the comparison and its scope; section 7 interprets the saved computation. This note does not amend the active coupling/concern protocol. Its central comparison concerns a single record overwritten across targets versus separately addressable target records within an explicitly recoded register. The conventional implementation is an exact comparator, not an opponent expected to lose.

## 1. The missing connection and the inherited history

The [recipient continuation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/concern/PROTOCOL.md) supplies a shared target-input recipe and tests costly delivery to a separate center. Its read-only transfer does not update the actor's predictor from the recipient's outcome. A failed transfer after reversing the recipient's action law therefore identifies a calibration premise that has not yet been acquired or corrected.

The specified study begins at actual eight-exposure coupling endpoints, with four further packets declared at the original preparation and retained through the handoff. It carries the actual fuel, source, updater, circuit, coupler, record and lifetimes. The suffix is recipient, actor, recipient, actor. A prefix chosen for the strongest witness is not the initial distribution: failed preparation, failed operation and ineligible endpoints remain in unconditional outcomes.

Admission to the new recipe may require an active, written, confident circuit and a normal coupler. That is a functional guard, not proof that the stored bit is correct. Confidence and paid writing can survive an incorrect teaching interpretation, especially with outages. A hidden regime or an analyst's correctness mark must not become an admission condition. State clearly that the new experiment concerns correction by surviving aligned machinery; it does not establish recovery from every damaged or inverted predecessor.

The recipient's usable reserve remains separate. During a recipient trial, zero private receipt is compatible with successful delivery elsewhere. An actor that decodes its own lack of return as the recipient's failure has an observation error, not simply a low learning rate. A paid feedback channel must read the actual recipient event, including its target and action reference, without granting hidden productive orientation or the unchosen action's outcome.

## 2. What the shared and indexed records actually contain

The proposed shared direct-record controller stores one acquired productive-action bit. Every paid record replaces that bit, regardless of target. The indexed controller re-encodes the original five-valued register into four values representing two bits, one per target. Both cells initially receive the actual old acquired bit; the recipient cell does not start with a newly correct label.

Four possible payload states fit within a five-state abstract register. This is a cardinality fact, not a derivation of a physical two-cell memory or a zero-cost addressing operation. The packing recipe, target-dependent decoder, selective write mechanism, stable representation and applicable prices remain supplied local laws. The retrofit must preserve the declared two backing slots or identify any additional material explicitly. Old names for those slots do not establish the new circuitry's capacity.

Compare complete controller state as well as payload cardinality. The old last-action mark, acquired-write flag, component lifetimes, target input and any persistent phase or address mark can carry information. A comparison cannot silently give the indexed controller an unbounded history, free clock or additional hidden bit. Conversely, an observer's diagnostic flags are harmless only when no guard, command or update reads them.

The common target/sensor setup costs two and paid recoding costs two. These bills distinguish three questions. A shared controller paying an equal unchanged-format adapter bill isolates the organization of retained content under matched expenditure. A cheaper shared controller omitting that second bill reveals whether recoding repays its actual extra construction cost. A conventional indexed controller with the same representation, observations and bills tests exact mechanism equivalence. Installation is sequential; an unaffordable second step does not refund the common setup. None of these comparisons alone derives the supplied update or indexing algorithm from a competition among architectures.

## 3. Resource and information matching

All matched controllers must receive the same actual pre-action target information. Its calibration and physical delivery are part of the interface. If an ongoing read costs fuel, charge that cost before the command in every matched arm. If a formation bill supplies a finite target-input mechanism, state its lifetime and which accesses that bill covers. The externally scheduled P,A,P,A sequence must not become a free counter that substitutes for target sensing.

The chosen protocol charges, in order, retention one, target identification one, and work three before the actual capture outcome, then outcome acquisition one and writing one. The action reads only the register and this paid target report. A completed phase therefore costs seven; actor capture may fund its later acquisition and write, while recipient delivery adds nothing to actor fuel. Feedback or writing can remain unaffordable after an actual trial, and earlier bills remain spent. A missing outcome channel is represented as missing evidence; a paid write attempt then leaves memory unchanged instead of fabricating a failure label. Removing the recipient channel does not remove the separately acquired actor-private receipt: the mistaken-private arm can still buy that zero and misuse it as a recipient label. This is a different surviving input, not restoration of the missing recipient evidence.

The feedback bit concerns the actual recipient or actor outcome of the actual action. Its read expense is separate from retention and work. A paid write can occur only through the specified write event. Missing feedback, an observed failure, a successful recipient delivery without private receipt, and an unaffordable write are distinct states. Retain each in the ledger and observed histories.

Prepaying or reserving feedback/write resources before the outcome can make both outcome branches recordable. Paying afterward can also be a legitimate law, but its outcome-dependent affordability may change which records are retained. The [recording mathematics](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/RECORDING-FIDELITY-MATH.md) makes the distinction explicit. Its intact finite prefixes and stationary Bernoulli confidence guarantee do not automatically describe this action-dependent, target-indexed and potentially changing stream.

Similarly, the recorder's consumable A/H bit slots are not the coupling model's occupied recyclable slots. An interface may preserve actual acquired labels and event order without identifying these material realizations. A claimed physical composition must supply the adapter, capacity, intermediate states and accounts; a shared word such as “record” is insufficient.

Lifetime updates need the same precision as fuel payments. Recipient delivery cannot silently renew actor components using energy that was delivered to the recipient. If feedback or a later actor return funds renewal, place that payment at its actual event. If the four-use suffix employs a new finite-use retention law, identify that as a recipe premise. Inability to retain or execute must remain an outcome rather than being repaired by a reset.

Here the interface explicitly installs a four-exposure stabilized read/write law: paid rent holds inherited hardware usable for that exposure, and missing rent ends access permanently. The parent's wear, renewal and reformation events are suspended. This supplies a different finite local law, not a refinement of the old kinetic mechanism or a proof of indefinite retention. Its prepaid setup and per-exposure expense remain in the inherited account.

## 4. Strong comparators and interventions

| Arm or intervention | Required interpretation |
|---|---|
| Indexed controller and exact conventional indexed controller | Same finite memory, target/receipt inputs, actions, lifecycle law and costs; complete marked transition distributions should agree. There is no expected advantage over an identical conventional controller. |
| Shared latest-record controller with matched recoding bill | Same observations and write opportunities, but both targets address one record. It isolates interference introduced by overwriting a target's useful evidence with evidence about another target. |
| Cheaper shared latest-record controller | Keeps its lower construction price and remaining fuel. This is an architectural comparison, not a pure content intervention. |
| Frozen recipient cell with charged writes | Keeps access and costs while blocking retained correction. If the later recipient action still changes, identify another information path or an implementation error. |
| Private-receipt-only decoder | Uses the actor's zero return during recipient work as its signal. Successful recipient delivery remains independently recorded. This tests an incorrect reference, not an absence of actual outcome information in every possible channel. |
| Wrong target address or exchanged cells | Keeps the acquired bit and plant fixed but writes or reads the other cell. This tests the role of the paid target reference. Relabeling both cells and their input consistently is a different intervention and should preserve behavior. |
| Common versus opposite target laws | Tests when sharing a record is appropriate and when context separation is needed. The mechanism should not claim an advantage from indexing when both targets obey the same stable law. |
| Recipient-law change after acquisition | Tests correction of obsolete evidence. Preserve the costs and failures before the change; the learner receives no change notification unless implemented. |

Some rows are prospective diagnostic options. The final protocol and results must state which are executed; this table does not assert that every intervention is included. A competent conventional controller must have the same allowed addressing operation, not be restricted to the shared register merely to create an advantage. Any comparator supplied with exact unseen target laws is an oracle unless the same knowledge is supplied to the other arms.

The saved protocol declares seven arms: shared, indexed, exact conventional indexed, private-only outcome access, mistaken private-receipt teaching, frozen record with paid writes, and cheaper shared. The private-only, mistaken-private and frozen ablations retain the indexed representation and its recoding price; otherwise a channel comparison would also change the interference mechanism. The private-only arm represents absent recipient evidence; the mistaken private-receipt arm represents an actually supplied but misreferenced zero. These have different meanings even when a particular action agrees. Its fixtures cover opposite and common laws, recipient outages, missing outcome access, target aliasing, swapped target labels, low original fuel and high write cost. The former table's separate recipient-only freeze and post-learning law change are not claimed as additional executed arms.

**Consistent swapping is not aliasing.** With equal initial cells, a fixed bijection exchanging both target labels at every read and write only exchanges the cell names. Indexed behavior should be covariant under that swap, even though the labels no longer use the analyst's preferred names. Collapsing both targets to one reported label destroys their distinction and can reproduce shared-record interference. A read/write address mismatch or a label permutation introduced after acquiring different cells would instead be a corruption test. These are different interventions; calling every noncanonical label “wrong identity” obscures the information actually needed.

## 5. A discriminating finite word

There is a simple conditional interference result, useful for a targeted check rather than a replacement for the unconditional distribution. Suppose the old acquired actor bit is correct and equals a; the stable recipient law requires 1−a; both target observations are exact; every attempt and write is funded; and the hardware remains executable throughout P,A,P,A.

The shared latest-record controller begins with a and makes the successive updates

`a → 1−a → a → 1−a → a`.

Each action uses the bit most recently acquired for the opposite target. All four actions fail under those conditions. The indexed controller begins with `(a,a)`. The first recipient action fails and corrects only the recipient cell to 1−a; the later actor, recipient and actor actions then succeed. The actor record survives the recipient correction. A same-state clamp of the recipient cell before the second recipient trial should change that command while leaving the actor command unchanged.

This is a consequence of the declared direct-record and addressing laws, not a new general learning principle. It demonstrates what the extra organization accomplishes without increasing the register's abstract payload state count. It neither proves that every prepared instance is correct and funded nor predicts the sign of an unconditional resource comparison with cheaper controls. Missing writes, outages, depletion, damaged memory, incorrect target labels or a changed law can invalidate one of its premises. Report those failures rather than redefining them out of the witness.

The joint witness should therefore retain instance identity: an actual previously acquired actor record; a recipient action whose observed consequence contradicts its transferred prediction; an actually paid, correctly addressed write; a later successful recipient command caused by that retained correction; and preserved usable actor prediction on the intervening or later actor trial. Relearning after erasing the entire circuit is a different event. If the protocol uses a weaker witness, label that scope explicitly.

## 6. Outcomes and claims

Report unconditional recipient delivery, recipient usable continuation, actor capture, trial/feedback/write coverage, actual correction probability, depletion, final stocks, and all formation and operating expenses. Separate feedback fidelity from predictive error and both from action success. An inactive controller has no scored prediction at that opportunity. A missing receipt channel does not turn an unobserved recipient outcome into an observed failure.

Neither an analyst's comparison score nor an independently measured recipient benefit may secretly enter the controller as a reward. Actual recipient feedback may govern updates because its acquisition is part of the mechanism; that does not derive the shared target trigger or a general preference for another center's good. The suffix's architecture is supplied unless an implemented formation/selection process explains its installation. In particular, comparing controllers only after shared-target setup does not establish why an actor would buy that setup rather than preserve its private controller and decline the new route.

The [identity-revelation diagnostic](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONCERN-IDENTITY-DIAGNOSTIC-NOTE.md) distinguishes helping under uncertainty about private exposure from some forms of other-directed responsiveness. This experiment should keep the target reference explicit: known recipient delivery with zero private receipt differs from ambiguous self exposure. It still does not identify felt concern or ethical standing. Identity can legitimately index different causal relations; it cannot by itself establish that one center's otherwise comparable stakes count for less.

The intended contribution is a finite, resource-accounted correction of a supplied cross-target action relation, with a preserved useful private record and conventional equivalence. Its new capacities and failure conditions should be connected to the common derivation without claiming a universal tendency toward concern, justified moral inclusion, or superiority over conventional contextual feedback. No external historical, biological or empirical claim is made in this note.

## 7. Saved comparison and counterexamples

The [corrected producer record](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/results.json) contains eight cases, three coupling producers and seven suffix arms: 168 combinations. All eight recorded source hashes matched their current files when this note was updated. The producer checks exact equality of the indexed and conventional suffix outputs, including distributions, in every case and producer; it also checks equality between the variable and conventional coupling producers. The separately implemented [independent suffix check](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/independent-check.json) passes on 4,872 aggregate metrics, 1,022 local distributions and the full terminal distributions. All eleven hashes in that current record match. Its scope is the suffix over supplied, hash-pinned predecessor fixtures; it does not independently reconstruct the coupling prefix or identify chemistry. These are internal computational checks, not empirical validation or external peer review.

The [first exploratory implementation and results](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/development/shared-ablation-draft/) remain preserved. Its evidence ablations used shared-format memory and therefore confounded missing or incorrect feedback with interference between targets. The corrected ablations all use indexed memory. The protocol records that amendment and the stronger requirement on the receipt's provenance. The corrected result is not an independent replication of the earlier draft.

A subsequent channel audit found a shared producer/checker error: the missing-recipient-channel guard also suppressed the actor-private-zero proxy. The [pre-fix sources and records](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/development/private-channel-guard-before-fix/) preserve that implementation. The corrected law keeps the separately paid private channel available when recipient evidence is absent; it does not reinterpret the fixture as a common acquisition-bus outage. Primary results below are unchanged. This correction again distinguishes agreement between programs from correctness of their shared observation contract.

For the variable coupling producer in the primary opposite-law case, preparation succeeds with probability 2/9, and 1/6 of all original histories reach the suffix admission and installation. The suffix results are:

These are unconditional expected suffix capture counts, witness probabilities and actor fuel totals. They do not condition on admission. Energy retained on unsuccessful original preparations is recorded separately as `uncommissioned_energy` in the global account; it is not included in this suffix's final actor-fuel column. The indexed arm executes expected 1/2 paid trials, compared with 1/3 for shared and 4/9 for cheaper shared. The other indexed-representation arms also execute 1/2. This coverage belongs beside the outcome comparison.

**Benefit to the recipient does not imply more private growth.** Indexed and private-only finish with the same actor capture count and fuel, but indexed delivers expected recipient fuel 10/9 and private-only delivers zero. Both pay the matched acquisition/write bills; the distinction is how acquired target evidence affects the recipient cell. The model does not select indexed control using that benefit or show that private resource selection alone prefers it. Compared with shared overwriting, indexed control also protects the actor's useful record: the shared arm changes the actor command through recipient writes an expected 2/9 times, while indexed does so zero times.

**Behavioral correction alone does not identify the evidence source.** The mistaken private-receipt arm matches indexed capture counts and the weaker 1/9 correction in this short primary suffix. The first recipient failure happens to coincide with zero private receipt, so the proxy accidentally yields the right first corrective bit. After successful recipient delivery, private receipt is still zero and the same decoder writes the wrong target bit. Its expected decoder-error count is 1/9; indexed has zero in the noiseless fixture. The strict witness demands that the first corrective evidence actually came from the target channel, so it is zero for the proxy. This is a causal provenance condition on the implemented mechanism, not an inference justified by the matching behavioral totals. Inspecting the retained cell after successful help, or a further paid recipient trial, could distinguish the future implications; the latter is not an additional executed phase here.

The other fixtures retain necessary limits. With common target laws, shared and indexed have identical capture outcomes; cheaper shared leaves actor fuel 29/18 instead of 25/18. Consistent swapped labels preserve the primary metrics, while aliasing both contexts makes indexed reproduce the shared arm's zero captures. Under recipient outages, indexed recipient capture and strict witness fall to 1/12, with decoder errors 1/36. Missing recipient feedback removes indexed correction, but the surviving mistaken-private proxy still obtains behavioral correction 1/9, recipient capture 1/9 and decoder errors 1/9, with strict witness zero. That counterexample makes a broad claim that removing recipient evidence must remove all apparently corrective behavior false. Low original reserve produces no eligible suffix. At write price four, indexed still achieves weaker recipient correction 1/9 but no strict witness: it cannot preserve both later private operations. Thus correction, noninterference, affordable operation and calibration remain different achievements.

Using fixed-normal coupling as the producer gives indexed strict witness 2/9 instead of 1/9, while the conventional indexed controller again matches. That comparison reinforces the existing limit on the upstream variable mechanism: this suffix does not demonstrate its superiority to a competent fixed coupling. All these results concern the declared finite recipes and initial distributions.

The first recipient action fails and writes zero only to that target's cell; the actor action succeeds; the second recipient action uses zero and succeeds; the final actor action still uses one and succeeds. Suffix expense is 32, inaccessible source is ten, final actor fuel is nine and recipient fuel is ten. The same record preserves the earlier formation and coupling expenses. This constructive branch exhibits the named connection without replacing the unconditional result or establishing indefinitely reliable correction.
