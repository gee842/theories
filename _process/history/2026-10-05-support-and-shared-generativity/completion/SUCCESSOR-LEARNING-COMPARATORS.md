# Comparing learning by a copied receiver

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparator and assumption note for **actual paid transmission → receiver acquisition and record revision → paid corrected action → transmission of the revised record**. This advances the explicit remaining boundary in [recipient-supported transmission](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md). The [accepted implementation review](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION-IMPLEMENTATION-REVIEW.md) establishes one copied receiver's action and retention; that action is read-only and does not exercise the copied feedback route.

**Status: accepted within the declared finite scope by the [independent internal review](SUCCESSOR-LEARNING-REVIEW.md), with the [saved successor-learning protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/PROTOCOL.md), producer results and independent continuation check aligned.** Sections 1–6 state the contract and boundaries; section 7 interprets the executed finite comparisons. The selected scope is deliberately finite. The receiver's inherited route `gamma` governs new paid feedback and writing; its record `q` changes. This round does not reassemble or select a new `gamma`. Mechanism revision remains a separately priced, material task rather than another name for updating the record.

## 1. Actual starting state and additional premises

The primary receiver must be the actually retained child of the existing transmission law, with its copied route, copied bounded register and consumed seed retained. Admission requires an installed retained child, bounded payload and enabled recipient actuator; a merely present copied tuple is insufficient. The faithful witness has original actor fuel eight, recipient fuel ten and child fuel zero after its first action and retention. Its exhausted source remains exhausted. Failed older histories may still contain unused source or an occupied incomplete receiver; those stocks remain accounted for and do not resurrect an inadmissible child. A fresh installed learner with reset fuel would not establish this connection.

The primary continuation declares five extra ten-unit packets and one further receiver component bundle from the beginning of the extended experiment. Four packets support two unchanged-law warmups followed by a changed-law trial and a recovery trial; the fifth supports a new receiver's first action after recopying. These are additional finite inputs, not a refill of the original packets. Preserve their unused or lost amounts on every failed history. The new bundle is the declared blank carrier and routing part of the transmission interface, not free material extracted from occupied actor slots or the parallel typed A/H record construction.

With n warmups, the protocol declares n+3 extra packets. Thus the zero-, one- and two-warmup fixtures change both the number of earlier productive opportunities and total available supply. They test the consequences of earlier change and route-specific funding; they are not equal-total-resource comparisons against one another. Arms within each fixture receive the same stocks and opportunities. The primary extended energy is 211, comprising the parent's 161 and fifty newly declared source units. Missed learner opportunities lose their scheduled packets, while the final copying packet remains inaccessible and unspent if copying cannot begin.

The current receiver is supported by the recipient through an explicit paid interface. A probe charges retention one, target access one and work three before its return, then acquired outcome one and writing one. Capture transfers ten source units into recipient fuel. The recipient floor is one. These payments use actual available stock; neither anticipated capture nor the floor can prepay them. For each fee, the protocol uses existing learner cash first and transfers only the missing amount from recipient to learner before spending it. This is movement of existing fuel, not an additional energy source. Missing rent permanently ends access; failure to afford a later stage preserves its already paid current state for a later explicitly funded probe.

Invoking the inherited route's update function does not by itself construct the receiver's sensor, write apparatus or retention lifetime. The extension must declare how the copied carrier executes those functions, which payments hold it accessible and when access is lost. The old read-only retention interval is not indefinite stabilization. The update arithmetic and receipt transduction remain supplied even when their paid execution is newly demonstrated.

The same named recipient remains the target. No new equivalence between that recipient and the receiver's own condition is inferred from moving the template to another carrier. The paid target reference, action reference and actual receipt determine the decoder's meaning. The original actor receives no share of recipient capture in this continuation.

The primary matched FROZEN and CLOSED states are explicit route-substitution interventions on the retained faithful endpoint. They are not additional actual route-formation histories with the primary route's probability. Equalizing a record, resource and lifecycle state is useful for a causal comparison, but its intervention must remain visible.

There is also a stronger ancestry check available through the parent's `same_law` fixture. Under that full fixture, a FROZEN route can actually form with a correct inherited estimate, generate recipient support, be copied and act successfully. Run that preparation and continuation as its own experiment alongside TARGET. Carry its actual stocks and probabilities: its recipient reserve can differ from the primary ten-unit reserve. Do not reset it to ten to manufacture a matched lineage. A full same-law comparison can establish genuine formation ancestry; the primary endpoint substitutions isolate mechanism differences at a shared state. Neither should be reported as the other.

Changing `gamma` after the copy is a different intervention from exercising inherited learning. The ordinary continuation should preserve it exactly. An external tester may substitute a frozen or closed route for a control, but the positive witness cannot use that substitution as if the receiver autonomously revised its own machinery.

## 3. What changed conditions discriminate

In a stable noiseless environment, the correct frozen record and the learner can issue the same actions and obtain the same return. That tie is a required control. It shows that successful expression of inherited information does not by itself exercise learning.

The decisive change must enter the plant without a controller notification. The receiver issues its now-obsolete command, receives the actual consequence through its inherited route, pays for a write, and changes its stored command. A later paid action succeeds under the new law. A frozen or inaccessible record should remain obsolete under the same resource and information opportunities.

Only an actually acquired outcome can enter the update. The analyst may mark that a reversal occurred or that the first action was wrong, but those marks must not be policy inputs. The intended label rule identifies the productive orientation only for the supplied noiseless complementary binary plant. With an unavailable packet, the same observed failure can occur under a correct action. An outage can make the update worse; the comparison must retain that result rather than grant the learner a hidden cause-of-failure label.

The new copier should read the current actual `(gamma,q)`. It must not inspect a correction witness, fidelity tag, decoder-error score or desired route identity. A subsequent new-receiver action can then distinguish transmission of the revised value from transmission of the old value. This demonstrates revised-state inheritance, not yet a second receiver's own exercised learning cycle. Another change, acquisition and write in that receiver would be needed for that stronger claim.

The old-record control is a template-substitution law. Matching read, assembly and write prices does not create access to a historical value. The implementation may use the parent's explicitly supplied public-template intervention, provided it names that altered source. It must not silently keep an extra old-value register in the learner or claim that an expired donor-read window remains open. This comparison does not require or establish an actual archive mechanism.

The saved protocol uses that public-template law with an experimenter-supplied original code. Its write clamp likewise uses an explicitly supplied constant from the original receiver, rather than a new runtime archive. Both are altered causal laws. Diagnostics may preserve the original value in the experiment's record without turning that record into information supplied to the ordinary controller.

## 4. The primary funding boundaries

The following calculation is conditional on a retained correct TARGET receiver, a noiseless warmup, the specified seven-unit complete probe bill, a recipient floor of one and the stated copy prices. It is not a bound over arbitrary architectures or initial lineages.

Each fully paid successful warmup adds three recipient units. Starting at ten, two such warmups give sixteen. A subsequent failed changed-law probe costs seven and leaves nine. The corrected successful probe costs seven and returns ten, leaving twelve. The original actor stays at eight throughout.

Let B denote recipient stock immediately before the changed-law failure. To complete the failed probe and then prepay the recovery action, require

`B − 7 ≥ floor1 + rent1 + sense1 + work3`,

so **B ≥ 13**. A return after recovery can then pay its acquisition and write. Merely having enough money for the failing action is not enough to learn and use the correction.

After failure and successful recovery, recipient stock is `B−4`. Operational recopying needs a one-unit transfer to the current receiver for its donor-read hold, plus recipient use, template acquisition, assembly, writing and a five-unit seed, while preserving floor one. The payments total ten units before the new receiver's return: one for donor hold, four for recipient use/read/assembly/write, and five transferred as seed. With the protected floor, the required stock is eleven. Hence **B ≥ 15** for that full path through seeding and use. Mere template writing occurs earlier and can be affordable below this threshold. This price includes an actual donor-hold payment by the new donor; it does not reuse the original actor's earlier payment or credit that actor with later recipient production.

These two thresholds separate the planned controls. No warmup leaves B=10 and cannot fund recovery under this sequence. One successful warmup gives B=13: correction, use and writing a copy become possible, while the seed for that copy remains unaffordable. Two give B=16 and fund the full chain. They are the minimum integer number of these successful warmups needed from ten for this specified operational recopy route. Under noisy or failed warmups, “two opportunities” does not guarantee sixteen units.

In the positive trace, the recipient's twelve units after recovery finance that next generic copying/use sequence. The donor-read transfer moves one existing unit into the current receiver; the original actor remains eight. After the new copy's correctly addressed action and retention, recipient stock is eleven. The continuation's five packets, failed packet, probe expenses, transfer, copy expenses, component occupancy and remaining stocks must all appear in one account. A favorable terminal balance alone does not prove every prefix is affordable.

The declared positive continuation accounts for inherited eighteen plus new source fifty as `68 = 39 expense + 10 lost source + 19 final resources`. The seed and donor-hold funding transfers are not counted as dissipative expense until spent. A wrong old-code substitution may still leave enough spare recipient fuel for retention after its failed action. That possibility is important: the new retained-carrier count need not discriminate faithful copying of the correction from retention of an obsolete response.

## 5. Witness and falsification contract

The strict joined witness should require the following ordered occurrences in the actual continuing carrier:

1. Paid copying creates and retains a receiver with the donor's route and acquired record.
2. The inherited record actually governs a command before the condition changes.
3. After the unannounced change, an obsolete command produces an actually acquired consequence.
4. The inherited route executes a paid write changing the relevant cell while preserving its other cell and `gamma`.
5. The changed cell governs a later paid successful action.
6. Generic paid template acquisition and copying transmit this current state to a further carrier, whose paid action expresses the corrected cell.

Keep receiver learning, corrected action, completed recopy, successful new action and retention as separate counts. A copy may form but fail to act; a receiver may learn but lack fuel or parts for transmission. These distinctions are especially important around the one-warmup boundary.

A same-state write clamp should remove record correction while retaining the real failed return and the same bills. A current-versus-old-template intervention should alter the final action without changing copying's physical price. Neither intervention is a reward fed back to the process. If the learner changes its command without an acquired record or paid write, identify the extra causal path. If the frozen controller corrects at the imposed change, inspect whether the phase index, an externally supplied new answer or another uncharged state variable entered its command.

Recipient support, preserved actor stock and carrier count remain separate outcomes. The positive result would establish exercised successor learning and finite retransmission under the supplied route. It would not establish selection for truthful circuitry as a class, repeated population growth, an origin of the recipe, private willingness to participate, ethical standing or a universal favorable direction.

## 6. Scope within the parallel work

The primary missing connection is receiver use of inherited learning capacity, beyond the accepted one-action transmission result. The intended code belongs in the sibling successor-learning study and consumes actual transmission outputs. It must not modify the accepted transmission law to erase its read-only limitation.

The new receiver protocol's stabilization, receipt access, paid writes, transfer permission and additional finite supply remain explicit premises. A finite passed account does not provide unbounded donor readability or succession. Changed `q` with unchanged `gamma` exercises inherited learning; changing the route or selector itself requires a new formation/repair law, material bill and comparison.

The declared fixtures include stable conditions, zero and one warmups, recipient-source availability three quarters, missing recipient evidence, absent grandchild parts, another change immediately before grandchild action, and the actual same-law parent with stable or changed successor conditions. Ordinary, conventional, write-clamped and old-code-copying laws are compared within each fixture, with actual uniform route formation and the three upstream producers preserved. Same-endpoint FROZEN/CLOSED substitutions are separate diagnostics; they must not be mixed into the actual formation probabilities.

This work does not construct authenticated archives or convert current reasons into effective protection. Those parallel studies have different state, evidence and action interfaces. Any later join must preserve their actual inputs rather than borrow the words “record,” “reason” or “successor” as an equivalence proof. No new external historical, biological or empirical claim is made here.

The current record has no universal advantage over an old one when their target references differ in time, and retention alone again fails to distinguish productive use.

### Actual frozen ancestry and the private-zero short burst

The saved same-law inputs supply a genuine formation comparison beyond endpoint substitution. For the variable producer, actual TARGET, CLOSED and FROZEN routes each contribute admitted probability 1/36 and enter with recipient stock twenty and the same correct target estimate. Under stable successor conditions, each contributes productive and retained grandchildren 1/36 and expected final recipient stock 25/36. Under the unannounced change, TARGET contributes correction and productive retained grandchildren 1/36; CLOSED and FROZEN can still commit copies but have no productive grandchild. This establishes exercise of inherited learning against an actually formed correct frozen alternative under the stated matched target/closed/frozen entrance conditions.

The actual PRIVATE_ZERO lineage must keep its different history. It contributes admitted probability 1/36 but enters this same-law continuation with recipient stock ten. In its two-probe stable continuation, a first successful action is followed by a false update driven by private zero; the next action fails and another zero happens to restore the old correct bit. Its actual trace thus has a successful short burst and a locally plausible corrective write, without a faithful target-evidence update. It commits a copy but lacks its seed. After an immediate reversal, its first failure likewise makes private zero accidentally identify the right opposite action, but the remaining stock cannot fund the recovery trial. Neither event satisfies the actual-target-evidence correction witness.

This failure also produces a negative comparison for indiscriminate updating. In the stable same-law mixture, clamping all routes to their initially correct records increases productive grandchildren from 1/12 to 1/9, by preventing the private-zero route's destructive updates. That result does not make truthful TARGET learning worse than its own stable frozen control; it shows that “allow updates” is not equivalent to “learn from correctly referenced evidence.” Actual route differences, inherited reserves, retained code and coverage must all stay visible.

The computation therefore supports the narrow result: an actually copied TARGET receiver can exercise its inherited feedback route, pay to revise and use its record, and transmit the revised state into a productive retained descendant. Its route configuration remains fixed throughout. The evidence establishes neither route reassembly nor a general advantage of learning, newest records, current retention or faithful circuitry under every changed condition.
