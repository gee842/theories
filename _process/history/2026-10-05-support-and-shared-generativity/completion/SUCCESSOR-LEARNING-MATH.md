## 4. Sharp liquidity conditions

Let \(B\) be recipient stock at learning entry. Assume \(n\) warm-ups have actually succeeded with all seven-unit bills paid. Their ten-unit returns give

\[
W=B+3n.
\]

This equation conditions on those completed operations; it does not prove their occurrence or availability for arbitrary starting states and horizons. With zero child cash, even the first successful warm-up requires stock at least 6 to prepay its five-unit work prefix above the floor. A large formal value of n cannot repair an unaffordable entrance.

After reversal, the failed correcting probe costs7. The next corrected action needs rent1, sensing1 and work3 **before** its return. The complete pre-return price is therefore12, in addition to the protected floor 1:

\[
W\ge13.
\]

Lean proves this exact ordered-payment equivalence, using a general result that a consumption-only price sequence is affordable while preserving floor \(L\) exactly when its sum plus \(L\) does not exceed starting stock. A future packet cannot pay any earlier part of this prefix.

If the corrected probe succeeds and its remaining acquisition/write fees are paid, recipient stock becomes

\[
W-7+10-7=W-4.
\]

Before the grandchild can return anything, this stock must fund child-as-donor hold1, recipient use1, read1, assembly1, record writing1 and seed transfer5. Those payments total 10. Preserving floor 1 requires

\[
W-4\ge11
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
B+3n\ge15.
\]

The Lean copy-prefix theorem checks this equivalence. Execution sufficiency additionally requires the already corrected bounded template, active material, the receiver components, the reserved packet and correct target correspondence. In this route those conditions are provided by the joined enabled witness. The seed covers the grandchild's five-unit work prefix; the successful ten-unit return then funds its hold.

At the actual \(B=10\), zero warm-ups cannot fund corrected use. One warm-up reaches13 and can enable correction and its use, but leaves recipient 9 before copying and cannot complete that transmission prefix. It can still pay for and commit a copied record before its seed transfer fails; record copying and an operational grandchild are distinct completion conditions. Two warm-ups reach16 and complete the declared route. Lean proves that two is the least integer \(n\) satisfying the complete-copy threshold for starting stock 10. These are sharp thresholds for this payment order and architecture, not lower bounds on every possible learning mechanism.

## 5. What distinguishes learning from a retained correct answer

A frozen route with the same initially correct code ties the actual-target route during the stable warm-ups. When the law changes, both initially fail, but the frozen route retains its code. Its second changed-law probe fails again and leaves recipient 2. This counterfactual same-state comparison isolates exercise of the learning function; it is not an ordinary formation history of a free altered route. The executable study additionally compares correctly formed frozen lineages from the parent's same-law fixture, preserving their different inherited stocks.

Clamping the learner's post-failure code back to its inherited code, while keeping the already paid history, removes the subsequent successful correction. Lean checks that this clamped probe is still executable and ends at recipient 2. The clamp is an externally altered causal law, not an additional free archival memory inside the learner.

Clamping only the copied donor template to old code 1 makes the grandchild's action fail. Crucially, recipient 2 can still pay the one-unit hold while preserving its floor, so the wrong grandchild remains retained at recipient 1. The formal counterexample includes the enabled hold. **A retained carrier is not necessarily productive, accurately updated or evidence-faithful.** Productive action, copying and retention therefore remain distinct outcomes.

The fixed route \(\gamma\) is preserved throughout learning and copying. What is acquired anew and inherited is the target-action table \(q\), and thus the command mapping. The construction exercises heredity of a learning capacity and its newly acquired response. It does not show that the update algorithm or its sensor architecture revises itself.
