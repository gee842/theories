# Independent review: costed revision of an embodied selector

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review for the requested next connection from [formed responses and differential reconstruction](FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md) to a mechanism that changes which response can be reconstructed. This is a research review, not external peer review or empirical validation.

**Review status: accepted as a conditional example of paid, within-compartment replacement of an input-to-response mapping through physical return.** The checks reproduce all 55 declared case-arms exactly. The material mechanism is equivalent to a conventional random-restart controller and inferior to the stronger directed controller on captured packets in the primary comparison. This advances the repository's previous fixed-selector construction; it does not establish historical originality, general performance superiority, learned objectives or the completion of the unified foundation.

## Acceptance criteria

The requested result must represent a continuing organization's rule-bearing structure, rather than merely count replacement organizations. At least two physically distinct selector states must alter access, response choice, or copying probabilities. Their formation, operation, maintenance and replacement must consume declared resources. Those states may be simple; a two-state actuator is not an evaluative representation or general learner merely because it changes its behavior.

The environmental intervention can change the consequences of an action. The controller must not read the intervention's phase, date, or intended useful label. Its state must change only through the physical consequences it can actually encounter. An observer may calculate adaptation and survival statistics, but those statistics must not appear in a transition as an undeclared reward or oracle.

Resources and identity must continue through the intervention. The primary analysis must retain failed initial assemblies, failed probes, slow revisions and extinction. A conditional analysis among survivors is permissible only as a separately labeled diagnostic. The full history cannot be reset to a convenient useful module or well-funded reserve at reversal.

The conventional comparison must receive the same physically accessible observations, initial resources, proposal opportunities, operation prices and replacement prices. It should include a competent consequence-responsive controller, not only a fixed or blind mode. A conventional controller with the same state and transition kernel necessarily yields the same distributions; equality is an admissible outcome and prevents an unjustified superiority claim.

## Prespecified failure and intervention checks

| Check | What it must distinguish |
|---|---|
| Change which response restores usable support | Consequence-mediated reorientation from a scheduled mode switch. |
| Keep conditions fixed | Useful retention from gratuitous turnover and exploration overhead. |
| Disconnect the return-to-maintenance route | Effects of usable energy from effects of its routing to selector persistence. |
| Misroute the maintenance effect or preserve a misleading signal | Adaptation from capture or maladaptive retention. |
| Increase revision cost or reduce reserve | A paid recovery mechanism from unlimited free search. |
| Remove reassembly | A revision benefit from differential performance of an initially favorable mode. |
| Include all failed histories | Whole-process recovery from selection of successful trajectories. |
| Reproduce the mechanism as a conventional controller | A new causal implementation from an unsupported claim of a new class of feedback. |

The reserve obstruction deserves an explicit statement: where there is no other route to usable fuel, a history with less remaining reserve than the cost of necessary replacement plus its first potentially useful operation cannot recover through that route. The applicable inequality depends on the final event law; costs already sunk before reversal must remain sunk.

## Claims that this milestone cannot settle by itself

An embodied selector can change under fixed physical laws without the laws themselves changing. That can be a valid first instance of changing an effective selection mechanism. It does not automatically explain the origin of its repertoire, the mapping from return to maintenance, generalization to unseen response types, revision of the maintenance rule, represented objectives, or selection of new learning algorithms.

Local support can favor an effective private selector while damaging other centers. Neither private persistence nor adaptation to a reversal derives shared concern or wisdom. The [governing roadmap](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) keeps those later derivations open.

## What the construction actually changes

The [protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/PROTOCOL.md) was recorded in-session before the numerical comparisons. It is not an external preregistration. The physical selector orientation `m` changes the map from sensory cue `b` to reconstructed response `b XOR m`. A hidden plant orientation determines which response can capture a packet. The code's assembly policies cannot read this hidden orientation, the reversal clock, observer witness flags or future packet availability. Cue sensing and response production have a combined declared cost of two fuel units in the primary law.

Usable capture replenishes reserve and pays one unit to renew the same selector's lifetime. Missed capture wears that selector. Expiry permits paid reconstruction, with both possible mappings offered at equal probability by the primary chemistry. Earlier expenditures and the continuing compartment state remain intact at reversal. This is a changing effective response selector under fixed physical laws. Its finite menu, template memory, assembly rule, return-sensitive renewal rule, sensor and persistent scaffold remain supplied inputs.

The primary causal witness requires capture using one mapping before the first boundary, a paid orientation-changing reconstruction after the boundary, then capture using the other mapping. All three must belong to the same history. This excludes apparent adaptation caused solely by a formerly ineffective fixed mapping becoming useful when conditions change. The witness is counted from the original absent-selector start, including histories that never form or recover.

The conventional directed arm starts without knowledge of the useful mapping. After expiry it constructs the opposite mapping, using the same expired-orientation residue that the random arm can observe. It has the same assembly opportunities, fuel prices, hidden-condition prior and actual return information. Its directed coupling is an explicit alternative implementation, not an oracle. No competitive arm can replace a still-live selector. The resulting comparison is within this shared actuator and timing constraint; it does not optimize over every physically possible controller.

## Independent numerical and accounting checks

The [independent implementation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/independent_check.py) does not import the producer. It uses a compact state containing reserve, selector and witness information, with probability-weighted additive moments for expenditures and counts. The producer instead retains those event counts within each full state. This different representation independently reconstructs all **55 case-arms and 13 exact rational metrics per arm**, including the within-compartment witness. All agree exactly.

The independent recurrence checks local fuel conservation, nonnegative successors, lifetime bounds, valid paid orientation changes and exact probability normalization. Its **48,190 transition branches** and **5,482 summed compact terminal-state instances** are mathematical checks across the declared models, not distinct empirical observations. The local identities telescope to the source ledger: unused, mismatched and unusable packets spill to waste on the external exposure clock. An assembly payment remains spent even when it leaves insufficient reserve for a trial. No inactive path obtains an uncounted future store.

The producer separately verifies exact equality of the complete material and conventional-automaton state distributions for every declared case. The independent implementation verifies all their reported metrics through its own recurrence. The saved [verification record](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/independent-check.json) hashes the producer, its runner, the protocol, result record and independent checker; hashes match the producer record and remain unchanged during the independent run. The conditional first-recovery formula was additionally reconstructed by a separate finite-event recursion over **85,932 parameter combinations**. It is not the noisy-case recovery formula and does not replace the all-history comparisons.

## Results that constrain the conclusion

In the primary comparison, every arm begins with nine reserve units, no selector, twelve potential four-unit packets, and the same initial uncertainty. The hidden condition reverses after four exposures.

| Arm | Expected captured packets | Probability of the joint revision witness | Final reserve sufficient for next operation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Material random renewal | 9/2 | 3/8 | 3/8 |
| Conventional random-restart automaton | 9/2 | 3/8 | 3/8 |
| Directed failure feedback | 6 | 1/2 | 1/2 |
| Fixed mapping | 2 | 0 | 0 |

The final column means that reserve could pay the next trial, including assembly if necessary. The finite source has ended, so it is not a demonstrated survival probability. All values are unconditional. The primary construction establishes actual within-compartment revision and improvement over a fixed-map ablation, while the stronger conventional comparison prevents a claim that this chemistry improves on conventional feedback.

Two adverse regimes are especially informative:

- **Two reversals:** fixed mapping captures an expected four packets and ends ready with probability one half; material renewal captures only `15/4` and ends ready with probability one quarter. A mapping that persists through a temporary adverse condition can become useful again. No general direction or blanket superiority to persistence follows.
- **Initial reserve three:** every capture-gated competitive arm has zero revision-witness probability and zero final readiness. Outcome-blind renewal nevertheless has witness probability `12775/131072` (about 0.0975) and readiness `3319/32768` (about 0.1013). Some blind histories avoid renewal payments and retain enough reserve for a later replacement. The ablation changes the coupling and therefore realized expenditure; this is evidence of a cost tradeoff, not a matched-spending proof that blind renewal is generally better.

Noisy usable packets also reduce successful revision. Even a correctly matched response can miss a packet, wear out and be replaced by a wrong mapping. Directed feedback performs at least as well as material renewal on captured packets in these eleven declared cases, but those cases do not establish dominance for all noise, delay, support or environment processes.

## Integration judgment and next obligation

The concrete milestone is met: a rule-bearing structure forms at a price, controls response reconstruction, receives consequence-dependent physical maintenance, and can be replaced within the same resource-bearing organization after conditions change. A sharp recovery threshold exposes when that route is unavailable. Matched conventional comparisons and explicit adverse regimes survive the review.

This is a new conditional process with a common causal trace interface. It is not an exact continuation or kinetic refinement of the previous formed-variant molecular process. Constructing this selector token and its couplings from the previous study's actual constituents remains an unresolved joining task. The accepted local example cannot be counted as a completed end-to-end derivation from that chemistry.

The expected new result was allowed to fail. Here the mechanism is exactly expressible by an existing kind of finite feedback controller, and the stronger directed controller does better in the primary environment. The useful contribution is the explicit supplied-premise ledger, the connection from return to costed mapping persistence/replacement, and the checked resource-and-time constraint. Historical novelty of that connection or bound is not established by this review.

The next foundational obligation is to explain formation or alteration of the coupling that decides which mappings to reconstruct, or to connect this paid turnover to a separately specified learning architecture without silently providing its objectives. Empirical identification and comparison with biological implementations remain open. No ethical or universal-favorable-direction conclusion is gained here.
