# Independent review: a copied learner corrects and retransmits

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review of the [successor-learning protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/PROTOCOL.md). The named connection is **an actually retained copied learner → newly paid recipient evidence → corrected paid action → copying and productive use of its revised record**.

**Accepted within the declared finite model.** This extends the actual transmission output instead of substituting a freshly initialized learner. It demonstrates execution of the inherited feedback route and transmission of a newly acquired response. The route itself remains fixed. Recipient financing, continued access, sensing, update arithmetic, copying machinery and additional finite inventory remain supplied local inputs.

All eighteen pinned source/data/checker hashes still match at final review.

## Conditions that make this a connection

The primary faithful input is an actual retained child with original actor reserve eight, recipient reserve ten, learner cash zero and template `(TARGET,1)`. Its old source is exhausted. Admission also requires a bounded installed payload and enabled recipient actuator. A committed tuple without retained working access is not admitted. All original preparation, route formation, copying and retention failures remain in the unconditional distribution.

The extension declares five new packets and one new receiver bundle at the extended experiment's initial boundary. It does not refill old stocks at the transition. Nonadmitted histories keep the new source unused, including failed original preparations. Once an admitted learner's probe clock starts, missed scheduled opportunities lose their packets. The final copying packet stays unused until actual grandchild work. Any inaccessible old auxiliary source and all old material/latch stocks remain separate and unchanged.

Each fee is funded by an explicit recipient-to-learner transfer, using existing learner cash first, and then spent. The recipient floor remains one. A future capture cannot finance an earlier unpaid stage; acquisition and writing occur after work and have separate bills. Original actor cash is never pooled into the new learner account. The inherited low record cell still concerns that original actor; it is not silently reinterpreted as the descendant's private condition.

Continued access is supplied by a new finite stabilization law: a paid retention fee permits the probe and its possible write, and a later probe needs another fee. Missing retention ends access irreversibly. This is not a refinement of the predecessor's earlier wear kinetics or a proof of indefinite readability. The recipient remains the same named resource recipient, not a newly derived organized center or an ethical subject.

The policy reads the stored code and the paid target reference. Actual consequences reach updating through the inherited route. Hidden plant orientation, reversal time, decoder-error counts and witness flags do not choose actions or writes. Missing target evidence leaves the code unchanged; private zero is retained as a different available signal, including when the recipient channel is absent. The complementary noiseless plant makes failure informative about orientation; packet outages invalidate that inference and remain in the results.

## Positive result and material boundaries

On the primary faithful lineage, recipient reserve follows `10 → 13 → 16 → 9 → 12`: two productive probes, an unannounced-change failure with a paid corrective write, then productive use of the changed command. The original actor cell and feedback route remain unchanged. A subsequent explicit transfer funds the learner's donor window. The existing generic copier reads its actual current template, and a paid grandchild action expresses the corrected response. Final reserves are original actor eight and recipient eleven, with both descendants' cash zero.

The new account is `18 inherited + 50 reserved = 39 spent + 10 lost + 19 remaining`. Transfers are not counted twice as expenditure. Including the complete earlier history gives total 211. Every prefix, including incomplete receiver construction, is represented; terminal conservation alone would not establish affordability.

For a correctly calibrated target learner after `n` actually completed productive warmups, recipient reserve is `B + 3n`. Correction followed by paid productive use requires at least thirteen; operational retransmission including seeding requires at least fifteen. At inherited ten, one warmup can enable correction and commit a copied record, but the seed is unaffordable. Two warmups permit the full specified route. Productive warmups must themselves be affordable: with a positive warmup count the first needs at least six recipient units. These are payment-order and architecture bounds, not universal learning minima. Different warmup fixtures also have different total packet inventories, so they are not equal-total-resource comparisons.

## Controls and counterexamples

Primary unconditional strict probability is **1/36** for the variable upstream producer and ordinary continuation, including all original failures and uniform route formation. Exact conventional controllers agree on the complete distributions. There is no demonstrated advantage over that matched conventional implementation.

- Clamping paid writes to the inherited code eliminates the primary correction. Clamping only the copied template to the inherited code preserves learner correction and committed copying but eliminates grandchild capture. These are explicitly altered causal laws with an experimenter-supplied old value, not free archives or spontaneous route changes.
- The wrong old-code grandchild can still be retained, at recipient reserve one. Retention therefore does not identify productive action, current information or faithful learning.
- One warmup permits correction and a committed copy but not an operational grandchild. Missing new components blocks copying despite correction. Missing recipient evidence eliminates the strict chain.
- With learner packet availability three quarters, strict probability falls to **3/256**. Productive grandchild probability is **15/1024**, which includes histories outside the specified correction witness. Successful action and that causal history remain distinct.
- A second reversal before grandchild action makes the recently corrected code obsolete again. Ordinary copying then has no grandchild capture, while copying the old code can succeed. Newness alone is not a general measure of useful information.
- The actual same-law predecessor supplies correctly formed TARGET, CLOSED and FROZEN children with their real recipient reserve twenty. They tie under stable conditions; TARGET alone corrects after reversal among these matched inherited states. These are actual formation histories, distinct from explicitly labeled same-endpoint route substitutions.
- In the stable same-law mixture, clamping records increases productive grandchildren from **1/12 to 1/9** because it suppresses destructive private-zero updates. This does not defeat truthful learning's stable tie with its own frozen control. It defeats a general claim that allowing any update is beneficial.

## Review correction and preservation

Before independent acceptance, review found that the initial continuation output omitted the old material projection and could lose a paid grandchild latch when construction failed before committing a template. Energy totals and success counts could remain correct despite this missing physical state. The current distribution retains immutable old carrier/part/latch stocks plus the grandchild's actual latch and construction stage. No additional state becomes a policy input.

The [initial projection snapshot](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/development/partial-copy-state-before-fix/) preserves the superseded source, protocol, inputs and results. The final independent run compares the corrected full states. Numerical agreement on scalar outcomes alone was not treated as sufficient acceptance of the continuation contract.

Generic transformation identities alone do not establish enabled traces; the separate joined witness checks the relevant guards. The theorem joining total 211 takes earlier accounting as a premise rather than reconstructing stochastic ancestry.

The result closes a finite developmental connection: an actually copied capacity is exercised, its acquired response changes through consequences, and that response reaches another productive retained carrier. It does not show a revised update algorithm, renewed source or replacement parts, indefinite succession, selection for faithful routes, voluntary recipient financing, or ethical standing. Those supplied interfaces remain obligations for the generative foundation. Parallel material-recording, recipient-renewal and reasons-to-protection studies are separate mechanisms until an explicit state and resource join is checked.
