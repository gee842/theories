## Paid access precedes the replacement decision

An ambiguity in the frozen protocol could have allowed the head's formation or read to be guarded by an unread analyst-level diagnosis. The active owner's [before-execution clarifications](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/revision/PROSPECTIVE-CLARIFICATIONS.md) resolve this prospectively: every completed, bound monitored prefix pays assembly1 and read1 before the acquired result can arm replacement. Unresolved or conflicting evidence retains those costs; an explicitly unmonitored/no-repair comparator declines that access.

This matters for the TARGET first-frame case, whose failure-and-flip observation is unresolved. It cannot receive free classification just because the analyst knows its route. The five-unit grant is likewise released after a completed first prefix in monitored and ordinary controls; eligibility does not encode which alternative will succeed.

Swap must consume the acquired authorization, preserve the actual packed record and original-actor reference, and invalidate the old monitor binding/summaries. An earlier diagnostic cannot authenticate the changed route. A known break between paid read and swap must cancel permission. The functional head, finite phase state and binding law remain supplied; this does not construct arbitrary identity tracking or infer ethical authority to replace a route.

## Preserve material and failed execution semantics

The old installed routing part must become retired inventory exactly once. Immutable ancestor data records provenance, so the accounting projection must not count that same part both as an additional installed part and as retired material. The new route part, monitor cells/head, revision head and separate successor bundle require distinct inventories.

The proposed wrapper uses a **banked-source stopping law**: a pre-work interruption leaves unconsumed packets and ends the schedule. The earlier successor-learning runtime instead expires the current packet on pre-work failure and continues its scheduled clock. Prices, receipt decoding and paid-write equations can be reused; unchanged full runtime semantics cannot be claimed. The revision protocol explicitly chooses the new wrapper, whose incomplete prefixes require independent checks.

The inherited copier internally supplies its nominal packet. A caller must debit its declared copy bank exactly once and carry unused probe source separately. The active clarification correctly specifies a guarded missing-packet adapter: preserve earlier copy/seed/sensing payments, then block work when source is absent. Its nominal funded trace must agree with the unchanged copier. A zero-source test cannot silently mint ten units or substitute a zero-valued packet outcome.

## Acceptance status and the next bridge

Separate read-only review confirmed the first-frame projections and the five-unit bound. The access and missing-copy-source concerns are resolved at the prospective specification level. Actual route construction, acquired permission, partial failure distributions, material accounting and the guarded copier still need the revision study's implementation, proof and independent review. This note does not mark that work complete.

The finding strengthens the governing derivation in a narrow way: paid observation and paid revision must compose through an affordable earlier boundary, with new provision visible. A successful replacement path would then connect learned evidence to a changed learning mechanism and actual inheritance. Its useful persistence, recipe origin, shared evaluation, justified concern and general empirical reach remain further obligations. Retaining an ineffective route under spare resources and reversing its apparent advantage after environmental change remain required countercases.
