## Conclusion and completion status

**Accept the contribution as a completed conditional solution of joint strategic formation and funded reconstruction.** In one specified game, an initially unconcerned decision maker chooses useful learning while anticipating the expense of its later concern; the resulting decision maker purchases effective evidence and control; a persistent opponent safeguards; and actual receipts finance full rebuilding for each fresh successor. A patient without productive leverage is protected through an ally's acquired valuation and control of the ally's own input.

This advances the unified account beyond separately feasible learning, protection, and renewal schedules. The choices, cash prefixes, interference, and replacement are joined in the same process. I found no material gap in the repaired theorem within its declared repertoire and assumptions.

The correct promoted claim is **conditional joint formation and strategic continuation proved**. The stronger claims remain open: physical realization of the supplied opportunities, dynamical arrival at the equilibrium, enlargement of joint capability regions, a physically derived directional comparison, and generality across independently specified environments. Those omissions limit the result's use; they do not undo the conditional problem it has solved.

## Exact contribution contract

### Inputs

The game supplies a trustee's continuing reserve and rival productive input; a stationary commercial return from calibration; primitive self-related significance; a shared evaluative routing architecture and correspondence evidence; construction of a timely authenticated assay and binding input gate; a finite public action repertoire; and funded operator actions. Each episode supplies a fresh trustee with zero learned patient valuation and no operative assay or gate.

The cold decision self values current organizational cash and correctly anticipates the warm self's choices. The warm self values current cash minus the learned patient-harm contribution. The operator values its own discounted receipts. These are explicit objective and information premises. Acquisition of a nonzero coefficient is modeled; the primitive significance, possible routing architecture, and organizational cash objective are not derived.

Let the learned harm contribution be

\[
q=\eta\kappa G\beta H,
\]

and let \(D\) be the full current assay/control bill. With the source's notation, the sufficient region is

\[
q>D,\quad b-D>o,\quad R-a>D,\quad j>D,
\quad r>c,\quad\chi_E>c,\quad\chi_B>c,
\]

\[
R+o\ge\ell+a+D,\qquad
B_0\ge B_*:=\ell+a+\max(0,D-R).
\]

The cue alternative has a positive fee and can be masked more cheaply than safeguarding. The optional evidence-only action is an analytical extension, absent from the frozen computational grid. The infinite theorem additionally requires \(0\le\delta<1\), uniformly bounded operator stage payoffs, continuing stationary opportunities, and positive-duration episodes. Destructive attacks require the separate sufficient bound \(k>c/(1-\delta)\), actual immediate payment, and the retained gross-profit cap; they are not covered merely by \(\chi_E,\chi_B>c\).

### Output and quantifiers

For each finite remaining horizon, the construction provides a subgame-perfect equilibrium with strict protected actions throughout the viable reserve region. For each fixed initial viable state, the discounted infinite game has such an equilibrium. This is an existence result with strict on-region actions, not uniqueness of every off-path equilibrium.

The selected sequence is shared calibration, purchased authenticated evidence and control, no alignment attack, and safe production. All learned and operative capacities are rebuilt each episode. Every payment prefix is affordable. The patient supplies no input, payment, threat, or reciprocal benefit; its ally supplies the effective input control.

The reserve invariant along the selected trustee continuations is

\[
B'=B-\ell-a+R-D+y\ge B,
\qquad y\in\{b,o\}.
\]

This reserve guarantee covers the declared operator responses at those continuations. **The patient-safety guarantee is narrower: it holds on the protected equilibrium path.** An arbitrary actor can still pay for a harmful bypass. The theorem makes that action unprofitable; it does not make it physically impossible. A robust assay claim against arbitrary admissible opponents would need different quantifiers.

## Proof review

The proof's useful repair is that it joins the actors' decisions before taking an infinite limit.

1. The cash-prefix calculation establishes feasibility and continuation viability for the relevant trustee choices. It does not falsely require every trustee deviation to preserve the region.
2. Off-path upper bounds handle trustee deviations even when their successor reserves leave that region. State-consistent finite-game continuations justify comparing harmful and safe admissions with identical cash successors. Since safeguarding costs more for the same successor state in an open or cheaply masked profile, it cannot improve that profile's warm payoff enough to defeat verified protection.
3. Finite-horizon induction proves constant operator continuation value on the viable region, then verifies gate response, warm choice, cold learning choice, and the alignment-attack comparison. This is a whole-game construction rather than a best response to a protective policy installed by assumption.
4. The finitely branching history tree permits a pointwise convergent subsequence of finite-horizon pure strategies. Uniformly bounded discounted operator tails and the dated trustees' finite current objectives rule out a profitable deviation in the limiting subgame. The fixed-policy Bellman observation is correctly supplementary.

The numerical witness is strictly inside the action-comparison inequalities: cold shared and identity gains are 3 and 2; warm verified, open, and withdrawal scores are 2, 3/2, and 1; operator safeguard and bypass receipts are 3 and 2. The first reserve path is \(5\to0\to8\to5\to10\); denial leaves 6, which also funds the next full episode. This is a genuine nonempty conditional construction.

The source's binary sensor refinement has an actual identification route: known safe/hazard fixtures identify an unknown stationary polarity. It does not grant a decision rule free access to that hidden polarity. Correct fixture labels, an unchanged fixture-to-operation relation, timely measurement, and priced interference remain premises. The supplied post-calibration drift failures correctly mark this boundary.

No material mathematical repair is requested by this review. Two distinctions should survive every summary: full rebuilding concerns the modeled lesson and apparatus, while account/input access and commercial opportunities continue as supplied conditions; and financial invariance under declared deviations is stronger than, and different from, equilibrium-path safety.

## Decisive remaining bridge to physical direction

The next step is to connect the proved institutional mechanism to the existing operational interface, keeping its advantages and failure conditions measurable:

1. **Realize the economic primitives.** Specify what physical activities generate calibration income and productive receipts, what source and sink sustain them, and what is consumed by teaching, sensing, control, and attacks. Continuing private returns cannot be treated as an inexhaustible property of the whole without that boundary condition.
2. **Make selection a process where that is the claim.** Either retain the equilibrium theorem as a conditional strategic prediction or specify learning/evolutionary dynamics and prove an arrival or retention result. Optimality with known consequences does not supply a convergence mechanism.
3. **Connect safety to joint capability tests.** Fix output functions, affected parties, protected reserves, information, and time. Compare the full capability regions under protected and unprotected arrangements. A protected patient plus larger trustee cash does not establish that every other capacity is preserved.
4. **Derive the directional law.** If a stochastic reduction is used, derive its rates and approximation scope from that same realization. Show that its levels correspond to nested capability regions before importing the stationary formation/loss-ratio theorem. This contribution supplies no such rates or ordering by itself.
5. **Test the sign reversals.** Cheap identity filtering, a forced-open commitment with cost \(s<D\), inexpensive capture/bypass, inadequate seed, reserve destruction, and nonstationary calibration are substantive competing predictions. An empirical application must represent the cheapest actually available alternatives, not only confirm the positive witness.

The cheap-precommitment countermodel is especially consequential. Making identity suppression expensive does not suffice if the cold self can more cheaply retain the useful lesson while disabling its later behavioral consequences. The positive theorem correctly excludes that extra action; an application must justify the exclusion or include its real cost. This is a physical repertoire question, not a reason to discard the theorem's stated result.

The strongest current explanatory statement is therefore: **Under specified resource, information, commitment, and interference conditions, a privately useful activity can select and repeatedly finance the relations through which another's consequences alter action.** It is more substantive than saying that concern is feasible, but it is not yet a law that coupled physical situations generally acquire those conditions.

## Normative interpretation and whole-situation scope

Ren's current descriptive use of 善 concerns the direction of shared becoming. This contribution is relevant because it makes an affected consequence effective through acquired evaluation, evidence, control, and renewal. It does not establish that the physical world generally moves in that direction.

The patient's moral standing is a separate premise or argument. It does not begin when the trustee learns \(q\), disappear when correspondence fails, or depend on the patient's productive leverage. Conversely, a nonzero learned coefficient does not by itself certify ethically correct valuation or adequate protection of every affected being. The model's patient ledger is deliberately narrower than a full ethical evaluation.

This distinction should preserve both achievements: the mechanism explains one way concern becomes effective, and the normative theory can recognize reasons that remain ineffective under hostile conditions. A new moral derivation is not silently added to the acceptance criteria for the completed descriptive mechanism.
