# Comparing consequence-dependent selector revision fairly

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparator study for Ren's request to construct a **costed selection mechanism that changes through its own consequences when conditions change**, and compare it with conventional selection/feedback under the same resources and information. This continues the [formation-to-retention integration](FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md) and the [existing originality audit](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/ORIGINALITY.md). It addresses the named connection **formed responses → a variable access mechanism → consequence-dependent maintenance and revision of that mechanism**. The literature search is targeted, not systematic, and does not certify historical priority.

**Main finding:** failure-triggered variation and consequence-dependent stabilization are established forms of adaptive feedback. A finite, materially implemented example can close a missing derivation in this repository without exceeding that existing explanatory class. Its strongest conventional comparator is a controller with the same observation interface, internal state, transition law and physical expenses. Where the laws agree, the comparison should establish exact equivalence. A useful further result would identify a resource threshold, a failed abstraction or a discriminating intervention; a win over a fixed or uninformed rule alone would not establish a new theory of learning.

## 2. A comparison contract before interpreting a gain

The declared construction uses a selector bit m mapping an observed cue b to response b XOR m. The plant's hidden regime z makes response b XOR z productive. A trial costs two fuel units. A productive capture makes a four-unit incident packet usable; one unit pays renewal and resets the selector's lease to two. A failure to capture reduces the lease. At expiry, one fuel unit assembles a new selector, with each orientation proposed equally. The old orientation remains an accessible physical mark. Initially the selector is absent and the hidden regime is equally likely to take either value. The main schedule reverses that regime after four exposures and continues to exposure twelve, carrying all resources forward. The primary capture probability is one; a three-quarter capture probability supplies a noise comparison. These are uncalibrated local inputs, not empirical measurements.

The selector never reads z, the correct response label or a change notification. Its cue mapping changes only through its own expiry and paid reassembly. Actual return and reserve support upkeep directly. The uniform proposal law itself is fixed. The accompanying mathematical and executable study supplies the complete state, event ordering, source/use/waste ledger and checked numerical results; this note does not replace that specification.

A valid matched conventional implementation must satisfy all of the following.

1. **One continuing initial condition.** Both arms start with the same usable fuel, unconverted incident supply, materials, response population, absent or present selector, and component condition. Count all formation failures. Do not start the conventional arm after a favorable formation history or reset fuel at the environmental change.
2. **The same physically available observations.** State an observation map. Any cue, occupied site, return product, wear signal or accessible reserve quantity used by one arm must be available to the other under the same cost and delay. Neither controller receives the latent environmental phase, the correct route label, unobserved counterfactual return or a change-point notification merely because the simulator knows it. A plant's reaction propensity may depend on the actual environment without granting that information to the controller.
3. **The same realizable actions.** Both arms can construct, maintain or replace the same structures. The physical action menu, action availability, failure law conditional on an action, assembly times, maintenance opportunities and costs must match. The distribution over available orientations may differ: this is the controller policy being compared. Only the exact-emulation comparator also matches that distribution. Directed assembly requires a supplied alternative coupling that chooses an orientation; it cannot be described as the identical immutable uniform-assembly chemistry merely choosing differently. If reassembly recreates the same route, that attempt still pays its assembly cost.
4. **The same state and memory budget.** An electronic controller with exact counters, a clock, parameter estimates and unlimited history has more memory than a one-bit material selector. Such a controller is an informative stronger reference only if the mismatch is stated; it is not a matched implementation. Conversely, forbidding the conventional controller the internal state that the material selector already has weakens the baseline unfairly.
5. **The same accounting convention.** Charge formation, failed work, upkeep, changes and loss of usable fuel in both arms. Incident support becomes usable only through the shared capture law. Conservation or a monotone ledger must distinguish physical expense from an observer's numerical penalty. Charge opportunities or events, not only successful changes. If sensing and bookkeeping are idealized as free in the candidate, apply that same idealization to its matched comparator and state it.
6. **The same horizon and endpoints.** Report active time, delivered response, material continuation, reserve use, number of revisions, and lag after changed conditions separately. Exclude neither early collapse nor unsuccessful formations. Conditional performance among survivors is supplementary; the primary comparison averages over every history from the declared preparation.
7. **The same intervention.** A source change, return diversion or maintenance disruption modifies corresponding plant channels in both arms. An intervention-aware oracle is a ceiling, not an equal-information baseline.

Matching initial budgets does not require forcing equal realized spending after the policies diverge. That would remove part of the causal effect. Compare the full distributions of spending and outcomes, with the common feasible action and cost contract held fixed.

## 3. Three distinct comparison roles

### A. Matched adaptive feedback: the primary conceptual comparator

Represent the conventional controller as a finite automaton. Its internal mode records the same installed routing structure and condition; it receives the same accessible signals. Its output commands the same paid maintenance or reassembly transitions. Do not supply a success label that bypasses the physical return path. This is a costed Ashby-style realization rather than a claim that Ashby's book contains this exact reaction network.

The concrete equivalent controller is a **return-gated random-restart automaton**. It retains its mapping by the same paid renewal on actual return, loses the same lease on failed capture, and samples the same uniform orientation on paid reassembly. If this representation has the same state transition kernel as the material description, the result is equivalence, not an unsuccessful experiment. The implementation then establishes how this recognized adaptive mechanism can be paid for and can fail under the stated law.

For a discrete-time model, let \(X\) and \(\widetilde X\) be complete states, including the plant, and let \(h\) be a bijective redescription. Suppose corresponding initial laws agree and, for every reachable state and next state,

\[
\widetilde K(h(x),h(y))=K(x,y).
\]

Require corresponding observed signals and cost/endpoint marks to agree as well. Then the probability of every corresponding finite history agrees: each is its initial probability multiplied by the same sequence of transition probabilities. Consequently all corresponding bounded finite-history statistics agree. Applying a common intervention to corresponding channels preserves this conclusion when the resulting kernels still agree. For a finite continuous-time model the corresponding generator and marked-event intensities must agree; the jump probabilities and exponential holding-time laws then agree. Merely matching an aggregate generator can lose marks on parallel channels, so cost and intervention labels also need checking.

This is an elementary probability argument, not a novelty claim. It gives a decisive requirement: **a predicted advantage over an exactly matched conventional redescription signals an implementation mismatch, an observation/cost mismatch or an error.**

### B. Alternative adaptive rules: behavioral performance comparators

The concrete stronger comparator uses **directed replacement after expiry**: its first assembly samples either orientation equally, but every subsequent expired selector is rebuilt in the opposite orientation. It uses the same two-orientation action menu, opportunity to act, one-unit assembly cost, two-unit trial cost, one-unit renewal cost, accessible old orientation, cue, reserve and actual return. It does not read the hidden regime or the schedule. The selective assembly architecture is supplied, just as the candidate's uniform assembly architecture is supplied. A matched action-level model does not by itself prove equal molecular implementation complexity; the comparison should state this idealization.

With reliable return and a sufficiently persistent regime, repeated failure can make the opposite orientation a better choice than a fresh uniform sample. With noisy return, expiry can result from an unlucky sequence while the current orientation is still appropriate; opposite reconstruction can then discard a useful mapping. Preserve both the noise and no-change cases. Do not equate expiry with a direct observation of the hidden regime. The completed primary comparison does favor this directed rule on captured packets and the revision witness, as reported below. This does not establish optimality over all accessible controllers or every possible environment.

A fixed-rate random reassembly rule is a further useful alternative if implemented through the same opportunities and costs. Tune any free parameters using declared training fixtures, or report all parameters in a small available class rather than selecting a weak instance after seeing results.

A loss to this comparator does not erase the mechanistic construction. It limits any efficiency claim and may identify which additional memory or timing mechanism the next derivation must explain. A gain is specific to the controller class, information and parameter region tested.

### C. Ablations and oracles: identify causes and limits

Fixed routing, disabled revision, free revision, outcome-blind retention and reversed return coupling are useful controls. They do not by themselves constitute a strong conventional adaptive comparator. A controller given the hidden environmental phase is an oracle; its performance can bound opportunity, but cannot establish a fair learning comparison.

A passive population mixture is also a different question: replacement of compartments can mimic aggregate adaptation without a continuing compartment revising its own route. Track compartment identity and its internal route transitions to distinguish these effects.

## 4. A sharp physical intervention and the inference it permits

The principal identification target is **the return from a route's actual consequences to the future availability or maintenance of that route**. The [previous evidence note](ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-EVIDENCE.md#proposed-discriminating-observations--unrun) already distinguishes this from persistent material, induction and selective survival.

A useful model intervention is to divert a produced usable-return packet into inaccessible waste while retaining the upstream response attempt and any informational reporter. Record the packet in the ledger: the intervention removes access, not total accounted material. In a paired rescue, restore access through a declared externally funded channel with exactly recorded amounts and timing. Neither intervention informs a controller that the route is correct or incorrect. Compare the candidate with the resource-aware conventional controller under these same interventions.

If return-dependent maintenance and later routing change under diversion and recover under rescue, that identifies the implemented physical return channel. It does not distinguish the candidate from a conventional controller that explicitly includes that channel. If a reporter-only or reward-only abstraction predicts the same future for histories that have different remaining resources, this intervention can falsify that abstraction. The result would justify a richer state description, not a general claim that reinforcement learning or control theory cannot represent the process.

The distinction can be stated exactly. Suppose two full states have the same proposed abstract state, but one has sufficient resource for the next paid assembly while the other does not. If an observable assembly event has positive probability from the first and zero from the second, no single next-step kernel on that abstract state reproduces both conditional laws. This is a failure of the proposed state compression. A history-aware model or one that adds the missing resource coordinate may remove it. Do not claim two histories are informationally indistinguishable if their observed returns or accessible reserves already differ.

## 5. A resource bound worth deriving in the actual model

Before a first useful return, suppose failed candidate trials cannot replenish the resource used to try again. Let \(B\) be available reserve, \(b\) a declared protected reserve, and \(c>0\) the total cost of a completed formation-and-test trial. In a simplified model with exactly these costs and no extra losses, at most

\[
N=\max\{0,\lfloor (B-b)/c\rfloor\}
\]

trials can be completed before violating the protected reserve. If proposals succeed independently with probability \(p\), and enough time is available for every affordable trial, the probability of at least one successful trial is exactly \(1-(1-p)^N\). Extra losses or a deadline can reduce that probability. A response arriving after collapse does not count as successful adaptation.

This is an elementary yardstick, not an asserted bound for an event law that has not checked these assumptions. It suggests a useful stronger target for the actual model: derive the exact reserve threshold for one successful revision, then the finite-horizon probability and cost of adaptation from the complete stochastic law. Include initial assembly and wrong-route expenditure explicitly. A threshold conditioned on a selected favorable entrance must not be substituted for an unconditional guarantee from absence.

The value of such a result would be its account of a concrete obstruction and a matching feasible path. Resource-limited search itself is established, and the comparison must not call this formula a novel universal learning bound.

The witness requires pre-boundary capture, a paid post-boundary change of mapping, then capture with that changed mapping. It therefore distinguishes an actual consequential revision history from an unchanged mapping becoming productive because the environment changed. The identical conventional automaton reproduces the material arm's complete recorded state distribution. The stronger directed comparator obtains more captures and a higher witness probability in the primary fixture. These are unconditional comparisons from the absent-selector start.

The results retain adverse regimes. With two reversals, fixed mapping captures an expected 4 packets against the material arm's 15/4, although its revision-witness probability remains zero. At initial reserve 3, the outcome-blind renewal intervention captures 2653/1024 packets, about 2.591, against the material arm's 2. This blind intervention changes the maintenance coupling and its realized expenditure; it is an ablation, not an equal-policy-resource-use control. Different endpoints answer different questions. Neither consequence-coupled renewal nor more selector revision is a universal performance improvement.

The [mathematical account](SELECTOR-REVISION-MATH.md) replaces the generic search yardstick above with a sharp conditional recovery calculation for the actual lease and payment ordering. It retains the reserve and time needed to exhaust the obsolete selector and try a new one; the conditional result is not substituted for the unconditional startup comparison. Physical return diversion and matched rescue in §4 remain proposed interventions; the executed outcome-blind renewal arm should not be mislabeled as those tests.

## 7. What counts as progress after comparison

The round can establish a real local explanatory advance if its declared dynamics create a route, let it control response access, make its consequences affect its own maintenance, and replace it within the same continuing organization after conditions change. That would close a premise that the previous population-selection construction supplied.

The round does not by this fact establish open-ended invention, evolution of the revision algorithm itself, a learned world model, generalized credit assignment, plural concern or wisdom. The possible routing architectures, wear/return coupling, assembly rule and physical environment remain local inputs. Explicitly distinguish a **variable first-order selector** from a **variable rule for revising selectors**. The latter requires an additional changing mechanism and consequence path.

The comparative conclusion must report equivalence and failure cases as prominently as advantages. A checked bridge inside this theory is one kind of progress. A new scientific theorem or prediction relative to the strongest predecessor is another. The present comparator audit supports the first kind of work and specifies tests for the second; it does not certify priority.
