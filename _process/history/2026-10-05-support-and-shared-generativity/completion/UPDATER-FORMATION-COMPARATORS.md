# Forming and replacing mechanisms that revise selectors

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparator and interface audit for the next connection in the [governing derivation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md): **paid formation of an updater module → changed selector-revision rule → consequences that alter that updater's own maintenance or replacement**. This continues the [selector comparison](SELECTOR-REVISION-COMPARATORS.md) and [formation-to-retention construction](FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md). The implemented design makes updater identity variable between random and directed selector revision. This note now includes a comparison against the completed producer calculation and its protocol. Independent mathematical, implementation and formal review retain their separate scope in the companion documents.

**Comparative position:** mechanisms controlling modification and transmission have long been objects of selection, and learning rules themselves have long been learned. The proposed advance inside this repository is to give such an updater a paid formation and maintenance history within the continuing organization, and to connect that history to the preceding formation process. Adding another level is insufficient by itself. The complete hierarchy still admits an ordinary finite-state description; equivalence to that description is expected.

Conversely, the title “selection over selectors” does not establish novelty. This is a targeted comparison, not a systematic priority review.

## 2. What must actually vary

Distinguish three objects in the event law:

- The **selector** maps a currently observed cue to a response.
- The **updater** determines how an expired selector is reconstructed: for example, uniformly choose an orientation or reconstruct the opposite of the accessible previous orientation.
- The **updater formation and turnover law** determines which updater is constructed and how it persists or is replaced.

To advance beyond the preceding study, the second object must be an actual, changing state-bearing component with formation and maintenance costs. Merely renaming an experimental policy parameter as u leaves it externally supplied. At least one permitted history must change updater identity within the continuing compartment, and that change must alter a subsequent selector-transition probability under otherwise matched physical conditions.

The third object's law can remain a legitimate supplied local premise. State that boundary. Making u variable does not establish an indefinitely self-revising hierarchy or a derivation of every updating mechanism. Nor does it establish a represented objective or a world model.

“Fixed” describes updater identity, not an immortal or preinstalled controller. If an installed-at-start arm is also useful, separately charge or expose its installation endowment; use it as a developmental reference rather than silently giving it the absent-start comparison's status. A molecular gate cannot be charged only to the proposed arm while the conventional arm receives free operation, or omitted only from the proposed arm.

Policy choices may differ over the shared feasible actions. The event opportunities, costs and failure law conditional on an action must match. Identical proposal probabilities are required for exact emulation, not for the competing fixed-type policies. The actual assembly couplings implementing those choices remain explicit assumptions.

Use the same accessible return, reserve and physical residue in all arms. A simulator may know the hidden environment, but no policy receives it or the reversal schedule. If an updater retains a history bit that the selector did not retain before, give its comparator that same bit and charge its storage or state that both models idealize it as free.

Higher directed-updater occupancy alone is not evidence of learning: differential survival, different hardware costs, or a favorable initial mixture can also produce that statistic. Where possible, count an ordered same-compartment witness: consequence history, updater replacement, altered selector revision, then a subsequent effect. Retain every failed preparation and depleted branch.

## 4. Transferring the formation process to a gate

The preceding mixed formation witness consumes four common-fuel units before capture and its one captured packet during copying. Its terminal usable reserve cannot be silently identified with the nine-unit initial reserve in the selector study. Additional support or changed preparation is permissible only as a declared input with the resulting histories recalculated.

A valid interface needs the following ledger.

| Supplied or derived item | Required record |
|---|---|
| Updater repertoire and molecular meaning | State which formed species realizes each updater and how it gates actual transition probabilities. A label-to-code correspondence alone supplies no chemical implementation. |
| Gate precursor and formation | Declare whether it is inherited, produced by the preceding network or externally supplied. Charge construction and preserve failed assembly. |
| Residual energy and material | Carry actual common/local/source fuel, precursors, occupancy and damage across the boundary. Do not turn total uncaptured source into usable fuel without a capture transition. |
| Formation endpoint or handoff time | Specify a stopping rule or deterministic time before inspecting outcomes. Include histories that never reach the required state. A selected witness is not the entrance distribution. |
| Timing and lost opportunities | Preserve elapsed time and any packets already lost during formation. A new trial clock or source stream is a separate supplied resource unless explicitly composed. |
| Observer access | Establish which physical quantities the updater encounters. Successful formation, efficient repair or a theorist's “useful” label is not automatically a sensed input. |
| Identity and ancestry | Keep the compartment and relevant material history through updater construction and replacement. Population replacement is a distinct mechanism. |
| Event-law compatibility | Prove the mapped entrance supports the actual gate operations with their prices. If formation and operation are different laws, state the physical handoff mechanism and its cost. |

The unconditional composition test is simple. If the formation process induces exit-state measure μ, and R(x) is the continuation success probability from the **actual** exit x under the gate law, the joined probability is the sum or integral of R(x) against μ, with unsuccessful formation histories assigned their appropriate continuation value, usually zero. If some histories have no exit, μ is a subprobability measure; their missing mass must not be renormalized away. If time matters, x includes elapsed time and remaining opportunity.

This identity follows from conditioning and is not a new theorem. Its force is methodological: evaluate the continuation at each inherited state, not at a single favorable substitute. An exact refinement would additionally preserve relevant traces and event marks; equal means or a common fuel bound do not establish that stronger relation.

A meaningful first test is a boundary pair: one reachable exit can pay for gate formation plus its first operation, while a second exit one fuel unit below the sharp requirement cannot, under otherwise specified conditions. Check that the joined process and conventional hierarchy both predict the failure, and that the original formation process assigns the reported probability to these exits. If all affordable exits have zero formation probability, the claimed bridge has not been built even if the gate works from a manually funded state.

## 5. Counterfactual tests and interpretation

Hold the current selector and accessible environment fixed while intervening on updater identity. The next expired-selector reconstruction distribution must change. This establishes the updater's causal role instead of treating it as an observer's annotation.

Then intervene on the physical route from realized return to updater maintenance while retaining the response and resource accounting. The proposed mechanism predicts altered updater persistence only through its declared coupling. Effects through simple energy deprivation, shared maintenance or the selector's own renewal must be distinguished. A return packet allocated to two components cannot be spent twice. Where upkeep competes, the priority or allocation rule is another supplied mechanism.

A higher updater lifetime can improve retention in one regime but obstruct revision after a change. It can also consume the reserve needed for immediate action. Retain these possible adverse outcomes rather than define success as persistence of the currently favored updater. Compare noisy and changing conditions with fixed conditions; a decline in exploratory renewal may be locally adaptive and still impair later recovery.

None of these interventions separates the material description from an exactly matching conventional hierarchy. They can separate competing causal couplings or show that a proposed abstraction discards a necessary physical state. The new explanatory obligation is the actual formation-to-updater connection, not an escape from ordinary state-transition mathematics.

## 6. The implemented interface and comparative result

The final [protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/PROTOCOL.md) and [model](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/model.py) use the old formed-variant channels with material count M=2, common fuel F=18 and source E=97. Bulk copying, wear, damage and leakage are zero in this preparation reference; the other old channel rates remain one. Both response variants and the repaired site are initially absent. The completed jump-chain calculation retains all absorbing outcomes.

Commissioning occurs with probability 2/9. Its entrance has one A and one H, a repaired site, one local captured unit, common fuel 14 and remaining source 96. No old channel remains enabled at that entrance. The declared adapter consumes the two formed constituents as backing for two recyclable module slots, costs one common-fuel unit, and makes the local unit accessible. Thus operating reserve is 14−1+1=14, and the remaining source becomes sixteen packets of six units. This is an explicit conversion law; accounting consistency does not establish chemical realization of that law. The other 7/9 of preparations remain in the unconditional calculation with zero commissioned capture or revision witness and their unused energy recorded. The separately retained F=4,E=1 low-stock fixture cannot commission; it is not reset to the funded entrance.

The sixteen exposure opportunities begin when commissioning opens the valve. They are not a shared deadline including random preparation time. This scope avoids a hidden claim that the preparation can finish rapidly, but it also means that elapsed-time comparisons await a fuller timing model. The larger initial stocks are a new supplied preparation, not fuel appearing at the interface.

The updater has lifetime four and costs two units to construct; the selector has lifetime two and costs one. A trial costs two; capture pays one unit to renew each component. The six-unit packet is counted once, leaving a net gain of two on a successful paid trial. An updater must be present for selector construction and operation. Its longer lifetime can let it govern a residue-informed selector reconstruction before the updater expires. Every updater policy shares this gate, cost and lifetime contract. Fixed-R and fixed-D arms also start absent, construct their hardware and pay for renewal and reconstruction.

The producer's [exact results](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/results.json) report twelve cases and four arms. At inspection its recorded hashes matched the protocol, model, checker and imported predecessor files. The primary reversal occurs after exposure four. Its all-preparation results are:

The zero witness for fixed kinds follows from the witness's requirement that updater identity change; it is not a failure of adaptation or resource capture. Fixed directed hardware captures more than the variable updater in this fixture. The exact hierarchy agrees on the complete recorded state-and-moment table, as required by its identical transition law. These results support a paid formation-and-turnover construction, not superiority over conventional feedback.

The strong witness requires an old updater instance that both captured before the boundary and actually governed residue-informed selector reconstruction; a paid post-boundary change of updater kind; and actual governance of selector reconstruction followed by capture under the new instance. The two required old-instance events need not occur in a specified relative order. This records functional use of both mechanisms, rather than an idle label change. The unconditional probability 1/72 includes the 7/9 failed preparation mass; the probability conditional on commissioning is 1/16 and must not replace it.

A **post hoc derived contrast**, not a prespecified additional arm, compares variable updater identity with an equal mixture of the two fixed-identity hardware arms. Both begin with the same R/D mixture and maintain the hardware at the same prices; the frozen mixture reconstructs its initially selected kind thereafter. Linearity gives its expected capture as half the fixed-R value plus half the fixed-D value. This isolates the consequences of permitting updater identity turnover, including extra resource depletion. It is not a comparison with an immortal gate.

Such maintenance is not, by itself, evidence that the newcomer's update rule caused the useful response.

The repertoire R/D, rule proposing those kinds, adapter, two module slots, upkeep coupling and starting stocks remain supplied. The concrete bridge established by the construction is paid preparation and within-compartment change of a mechanism that changes selectors. It does not derive the full formation chemistry, open-ended algorithms, evaluative representation, shared concern or favorable universal direction.
