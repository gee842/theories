## A new consequence: equilibrium does not select the useful label here

The equilibrium obstruction in [useful retention](USEFUL-RETENTION-MECHANISM.md) applies to the integrated reversible model, and its extra premises can be verified.

**Connectivity.** From any state with fixed N=4 and H_f+L_f+F+GF_0+GF_1=B+3, dissociate complexes, convert R through C, and wear each material carrier to D. Leak all free F to L_f. If H_f remains, use the positive reverse C-wear reaction to make a temporary C from D, capture each remaining H_f into F, leak it into L_f, and finally wear C. This is a finite positive-rate path to the common all-D/all-L_f state. Every used chemical edge has a positive reverse, so reversing such paths connects every state in this conserved class. No absent bound-reproduction pair is needed for this construction.

**Symmetry of the measure.** The conserved class and assigned potentials are invariant under exchanging labels 0 and 1 on A,G,GF,YG. The detailed-balanced measure

\[
\pi(n)\propto\frac{\exp[-\varphi\cdot n]}{\prod_s n_s!}
\]

is therefore symmetric under that swap, although the generator need not be. Connectivity gives a unique stationary law. Thus E_pi[A0]=E_pi[A1], and E_pi[A0/(A0+A1) | A0+A1>0]=1/2. Corresponding swapped organization events have equal equilibrium probabilities. Faster type1 copying changes forward and reverse kinetics together; it cannot change this stationary measure while the stated potentials remain fixed.

The finite-time branch advantage and equal long-run equilibrium weights are compatible. This is an exact counterexample to promoting that advantage into lasting preference for the useful type. It does not rule out selection in a maintained nonequilibrium process, different physical free energies, expanding systems or selected finite time windows. Those conditions need an actual retention/propagation mechanism and a new sign calculation.
