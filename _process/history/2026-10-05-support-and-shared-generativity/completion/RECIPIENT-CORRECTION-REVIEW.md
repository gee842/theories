# Independent review: paid recipient evidence and separate correspondences

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review of the [recipient-correction protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/PROTOCOL.md). The connection examined is **an actual recipient outcome → paid correction of its action correspondence while preserving the actor's acquired correspondence**. This supports the concurrent coupling-to-concern work; it does not replace that work or establish ethical valuation.

**Accepted scope:** a finite, explicitly retooled suffix can acquire recipient feedback, correct an initially transferred but unsuitable command, and preserve the actor's separate command. The result is conditional on the supplied target interface, register interpretation, stabilization law and actual predecessor fixture. It does not derive another center's organization, concern, channel selection or physical circuitry.

These are exact finite checks, not independent empirical samples. Every imported source, the fixtures, producer results and independent checker are hash-pinned before and after the run.

The inherited input is read from [actual predecessor fixtures](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/fixtures.json). The audit checks that preparation starts with twelve source packets plus the initial local-capture unit, that exactly eight operational packets precede the suffix, and that four remain. Original and prefix laws differ only in their declared source/horizon bookkeeping. Every terminal preparation mass, prefix conditional ledger and final suffix mass is retained. Failed preparations retain their actual unused stocks; nonadmitted commissioned histories lose the four unavailable opportunities.

This checker does not independently reconstruct the coupling prefix's reaction and operational kernels. The producer replay checks those fixtures against that producer, while the independent suffix check treats the pinned fixture as its interface. Acceptance of a full predecessor scientific claim remains the scope of its own study. The combined accounting confirms that this suffix receives actual recorded successor stocks without refilling them or dropping their failed histories.

## Conditions needed for the causal interpretation

Admission requires an active, written, confident circuit, active updater and active **normal** coupling. This explicitly restricts the question to correcting target correspondence after a usable output route exists. It does not repair inverted couplings or justify their removal from other studies. Primary admission probability is 1/6 of original histories for the variable producer, versus 2/9 commissioning probability; both remain visible in unconditional results.

The interface and recoding each cost two actor units. Both packed cells initially copy the actual inherited actor bit. Recipient truth is not installed. The packed representation uses four values of the existing five-valued q register; it adds a functional recipe, not a third declared material slot. The last-action field remains in the physical state but these policies do not use it as hidden target memory.

Every active exposure pays retention, target identification, work, outcome acquisition and writing in order. An earlier unaffordable stage cannot borrow against a later capture. Recipient capture credits only the separate recipient reserve, so it cannot fund the actor's subsequent evidence or writing. Missing evidence leaves the payload unchanged even if a sham write bill is paid. A private-credit proxy is explicitly a different, potentially erroneous channel.

The action function uses the installed payload and paid reported target. The external P,A,P,A schedule selects the actual target, not a free policy input. Consistently swapping both target labels is therefore a relabeling; merging them is an information loss. No analyst metric, hidden productive orientation, future outcome or unpaid target label chooses an action or write.

The new four-exposure stabilization protocol is a substantial supplied assumption. Paid retention holds all admitted hardware usable through each exposure, and the predecessor wear/renewal/reformation law is suspended. This is not a kinetic refinement of the old hardware dynamics. The finite recipe is explicit, paid and irreversible on unpaid retention; it cannot establish indefinite maintenance or the natural availability of such stabilization.

The recipient in this suffix is a distinct target and separate usable-resource account. Its internal organization, maintenance dependencies or a later recipient-use operation are not constructed here. Treating deposited support as evidence of derived organizational significance, consciousness or moral standing would exceed the model.

## Independent causal and resource checks

For an admitted circuit with a correct inherited actor bit and opposite recipient law, installation leaves both cells with that actor bit. The first recipient action fails; paid target evidence changes only the recipient cell. The two actor actions remain productive, and the later recipient action succeeds. In contrast, a shared register with perfect records repeatedly overwrites the other target's command under this alternating schedule.

The evaluator separately verifies:

- An actual recorded fixed-normal predecessor supports the complete strict witness; no fresh learner state is substituted for this positive history.
- Inherited actor reserve twenty is the sharp boundary for the specified installed P,A,P,A route. Nineteen cannot initiate the final actor work. At twenty, the ending reserves are actor eight and recipient ten; setup plus operation costs thirty-two, and one packet ten is wasted: `20 + 40 = 8 + 10 + 32 + 10`.
- A same-law first recipient success with only enough actor fuel to initiate work can deposit ten at the recipient while leaving no actor fuel for outcome acquisition or writing. This branch later loses access; recipient support does not become an implicit actor subsidy.
- An affordable first installation payment followed by unaffordable recoding remains spent. There is no refund, unpaid predictor or later memory resurrection.
- Paid writes under distinct target reports preserve the other cell. A consistent label swap preserves output metrics, while target aliasing permits recipient writes to corrupt the actor command.
- The private-zero proxy can produce the right first revision after a failed recipient action, yet writes a wrong label after successful help. Its weaker behavioral correction is not evidence that the recipient channel was acquired.
- Removing the recipient channel does not remove the actor's private-zero signal. The corrected missing-channel comparison keeps that proxy available at its declared price.

The producer's additional 102 boundary cases check both actor orientations and inherited reserves zero through fifty. These agree with the independent route boundary and the separate setup, work-only, observation-without-write and paid-write checks. Changing the reserve in a boundary fixture is explicitly a conditional intervention; it is not counted as another observed predecessor history.

## Results and their adverse implications

Primary values below are unconditional over original preparation and the variable coupling producer.

| Suffix policy | Recipient captures | Actor captures | Strict evidence/correction witness | Final actor reserve |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Shared record | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1/18 |
| Indexed / exact conventional | 1/9 | 2/9 | 1/9 | 25/18 |
| Indexed, recipient evidence unavailable | 0 | 2/9 | 0 | 25/18 |
| Indexed, private zero used as recipient receipt | 1/9 | 2/9 | 0 | 25/18 |
| Indexed, frozen record | 0 | 2/9 | 0 | 25/18 |
| Cheaper shared record | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The indexed controller and conventional indexed lookup have exactly the same complete distributions. The fixed-normal predecessor yields strict correction probability 2/9; the variable predecessor yields 1/9. The cheaper shared recipe's failure under opposite laws does not make an elaborate record universally preferable: with equal target laws it obtains the same support while retaining more actor fuel.

The strict witness's provenance requirement is necessary. The private-zero proxy matches the indexed arm's recipient captures and weaker correction probability during this short primary suffix, but produces erroneous teaching after success. It therefore has strict probability zero. Behavior over the short sequence alone does not identify the causal information channel or establish future reliable prediction.

The negative private-selection result is equally concrete: indexed, private-only and frozen policies spend the same actor fuel, receive the same actor returns and retain the same final actor reserve, while recipient support differs. The proxy also matches that actor balance. Private reserve retention therefore supplies no preference for genuine recipient evidence in this fixture. Better recipient information and recipient benefit cannot be promoted to a derived private-payoff or ethical selection mechanism.

With recipient availability 3/4, indexed strict correction falls to 1/12 and erroneous decoded labels have expected count 1/36. Missing recipient evidence, aliased target identity and low initial reserve each eliminate the indexed strict witness. Write price four leaves weaker recipient correction at 1/9 but eliminates the full noninterference-and-use witness because the final actor action becomes unaffordable. Same target laws require no corrective reversal, so strict probability zero there is not failure of useful behavior. Consistent target-label swapping preserves the primary outcomes.

## Review corrections and preservation

The first exploratory implementation used shared-format records for the private-evidence and frozen ablations. That mixed an evidence-channel change with a representation change. Review separated them: the current ablations use the same packed representation, installation cost and target inputs as the indexed arm. Strict witness provenance now distinguishes an actual recipient receipt from a private-zero proxy, and actor interference is measured at the actual reported actor context. The [initial exploratory snapshot](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/development/shared-ablation-draft/) preserves the earlier source, protocol and results. These changes are disclosed; the first run is not treated as independent confirmation of the final comparison.

A subsequent channel guard incorrectly treated an absent recipient channel as also suppressing the private-credit proxy. That shared interpretation affected both numerical implementations. The corrected guard distinguishes the two signals, and a direct regression confirms that removing recipient access leaves the private-zero arm unchanged. Both implementations and every comparison were rerun. The [superseded guard snapshot](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/development/private-channel-guard-before-fix/) preserves the rejected version. Agreement of implementations did not detect the semantic distinction by itself.

## Formal scope and remaining derivation

Its standalone interface proves that zero-new-probe exact behavior for both queries under four independently possible binary target-law pairs needs four distinguishable **complete accessible controller states**. It does not prove that the inherited physical organization has insufficient capacity. Four codes already fit its five-valued abstract q register; other state or fresh observations would also change the lower-bound premises.

In particular, if the opposite-law relationship itself is supplied in advance, one stored actor bit plus a target-dependent inversion already suffices. The package checks this counter-regime explicitly. The primary opposite-law fixture alone therefore cannot establish the four-state lower bound, and failure of the tested target-ignoring shared-record policy is not failure of every one-bit context-dependent controller.

The same package checks packed writes, target relabeling versus aliasing, recipient-receipt decoding under the noiseless plant, private-credit ambiguity, and local paid observation/write feasibility. A failed completed-pair option does not make observation free: a separate theorem retains its sunk acquisition charge. These are finite information and payment interfaces. Neither the predecessor stochastic process, full stabilized suffix, all distributions nor the twenty-unit whole-route bound are refinements proved by this Lean package.

The [mathematics](RECIPIENT-CORRECTION-MATH.md) and [comparator assessment](RECIPIENT-CORRECTION-COMPARATORS.md) correctly separate those scopes. The next explanatory obligation is not simply to enlarge the register: it is to explain the formation, reliable access and retention of recipient-specific evidence and action evaluation when private stock alone does not favor them. This interface advances connected correction while leaving concern and the universal generative foundation unfinished.
