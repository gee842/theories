# Ideas from hb-01 (2024-03-18 to 2024-11-03)

This is the seedbed batch. Almost every later theory in the corpus is already present here in raw, pre-terminological form — but stated in the vocabulary of 2024: Moloch, attractor, positive feedback loop, metacrisis, paradigm, narrative, mental model, second-order cybernetics. Ren is doing four things at once: (1) building a mechanism for how societies select power-holders by whichever single value they tokenize as power, and why that selection is self-intensifying to collapse; (2) building a counter-move — plurality of societies, exit rights, redesigned games, autonomous infrastructure; (3) organizing the physical instantiations (Wamo, Wamotopia, Invisible Garden, 706 Chiang Mai, Edge City Lanna); (4) reading hard — cybernetics, autopoiesis, biosemiotics, anarchism, macro-history, metamodernism, Kant, Déjacque. Two things not yet present: any Chinese-classical vocabulary (no 生生不息, no 易, no 大同), and any notion that AI could be a *moral* participant rather than a *cognitive* prosthesis. The 2024 AI is a pattern-recognizer that lets us finally *see* attractors.

## Recurring preoccupations

**The selection mechanism.** This is the spine of the batch and it is refined at least six times. March 2024: complexity outruns comprehension. April 2024 (Chinese, Moloch speech): single-value optimization + better strategist → convergence. May 2024: wealth concentration as positive feedback loop + discourse-power second loop. July 2024 (Chinese essay, twice — 07-14 and re-translated 09-15): *social systems differ by which individuals they select into power*, and capitalism selects capital-optimizers while totalitarianism selects political fighters — same machine, different token. July 15: "the current system is selecting power optimizers... this pattern is also the core generator function that lead to metacrisis and moloch." August 11: the de-personalizing breakthrough — "not the capitalists... but the optimization of the capital itself." October 30: an eight-step second-order-cybernetic derivation ending in loss of alignment with the system's own autopoiesis. November 3: the same derivation generalized from capital to power in eleven steps. That November text is essentially the meta-attractor theory in its first complete form, eighteen months before the vocabulary arrives.

**Narrative as the load-bearing substrate.** April 11 (money as narrative) → June 13/26 (the psyche essay: phenomenology + narrative = your reality; media as "the technology of delivering narratives"; narrative evolutionary fitness favouring fear) → July 26 (selfishness as a *paradigm*, not an idea) → July 29 (capitalism as religion, finance as ritualized gambling) → October 30 ("the root causes are in our thinking, our world views"). He never separates the systemic mechanism from the narrative one: winners get discourse power, discourse power rewrites what counts as success, and that recruits the next generation of optimizers.

**Plurality with exit.** April 16 (everyone can live in their own utopia) → July 29 (the game-design turn: the problem is the game, not the ideology) → July 31 (a common layer with many societies on top) → November 3 (2045: infallible autonomous infrastructure making exit always available). The arc moves from "everyone can have their utopia" to a specific architecture: an agreed substrate, plural societies above it, and guaranteed exit — including the freedom to build a bad one.

**Wamo as empirical proof.** May 17 → June 11–13 → August 1 → November 3. He keeps returning to one claim: a narrative produced consensus, consensus produced action, and 1000+ people coordinated at city scale with no organizer, no funding, and no authority. The organizational move he never lets an editor drop is that the team returned the money and dissolved itself first.

**Reading program.** Cybernetics and autopoiesis cluster tightly in late April–early May 2024 (Uexküll, Maturana/Varela, Bateson, second-order cybernetics, strange attractors) and then resurface as the *analytic frame* in October 2024. Anarchism and utopian socialism cluster in August (Déjacque, Fourier/Owen failures, Mauss, Lévi-Strauss). Manifesto studies run all July.

**The AI question changes shape.** March–April: is AI conscious, do I know it? April 16: AI as the pattern-recognizer that lets us finally see dominant attractors. June 22: agent identity as a compressible narrative handed forward. July 10–14: alignment requires knowing what value is; "maybe we should just let AI take over?" July 15: alarm that AI rhetoric is powerful enough to bewitch people. October 30: AI barely appears — the mechanism has become the object.

## Style and method notes

**He resists compression, explicitly and repeatedly.** This is the earliest documented instance of the complaint the BRIEF records: "very Anticlimactic, can you not reduce my length?" (2024-05-13). Two weeks later he catches a lossy rewrite by name: "the previous one has a lot great concepts, like The Technology Amplifier and The Cognitive Lag, and The Complexity Trap. they are missing in you latest attempt and the context is somewhat digressed from my original text. you can output as long as you like... but my points should not be left out or modified" (2024-06-26). And: "can you rewrite it into an long essay, make sure you don't lose information, you can write as long as needed" (2024-07-13).

**He corrects his own arguments before anyone else does.** "i think some parts of the reasoning is not good enough to conclude on its collapse" (2024-03-18) — and then restates the claim in weaker, truer form. He asks for adversarial review of his own text: "is the first essay i asked you to translate a good argument? where is not good, or sufficent?" (2024-04-16). He asks a model to find flaws in a position he has just proposed: "Can you come up with ways to find flaws in the hard problems arguments?" (2024-04-16).

**He treats vocabulary as a decision.** "i feel the word self is not adequate word here, do you have any other better suggestions? i was think of mind" → "ok change it to psyche" (2024-06-26). Same instinct: "it is not crony capitalism but it works in similar ways and lead to similar consequences" — the phenomenon needs its own name (2024-08-11).

**He audits provenance.** "did you invent 'convergent dominance' or does it originate from other source?" (2024-07-28); "is this your version of 'financially literate' or is it from the book" (2024-08-11).

**Mechanism first, rhetoric second — but he studies the rhetoric deliberately.** July 2024 is an extended manifesto laboratory: he analyses the Communist Manifesto's style, extracts a ten-point stylistic spec, and applies it to anti-cyberpunk, anti-neoliberal and anti-selfishness texts. Then he flags the danger of what he has just built — AI-generated persuasion good enough to "蛊惑人心" (2024-07-15).

**He iterates the same core text across many conversations rather than editing one file.** The Chinese Moloch speech appears in four conversations on 2024-04-16 alone; the psyche essay in two (06-13, 06-26); the Chinese metacrisis essay twice, three months apart (07-14, 09-15); the Wamo history in five. Each pass adds one increment. This is his revision method, and it means the *latest* version is not always the fullest.

**He asks for the same content in many registers on purpose** — Sadhguru style, Mélenchon style, demagoguery, poetic, inspiring, popular science, teaching, lecture, academic paper, scripture — and then judges which register survives the argument. "can you make this demagoguery?" followed immediately by "how about inspiring style?" (2024-05-13).

**He rejects work that flatters him.** "not good, rewrite in english, assuming you are a scientist yourself and follows your training" (2024-07-14); "I am not convinced, can you provide specific examples?" (2024-07-17); "you didn't get what i mean" (2024-05-13); "some text are repetitive, and you didn't clearly understand that we want to first overview the last wamo and explain its spirit" (2024-05-17).

**Where he endorses rather than originates, he says so plainly** — and the endorsements are substantive: "this pattern is also the core generator function... all the point you brought up in your response is excellent! I think you have an understanding on what I was trying to say" (2024-07-15); "great!
