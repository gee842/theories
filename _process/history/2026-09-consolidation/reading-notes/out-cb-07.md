## Style and method notes

**Corrections are where he is sharpest.** Every one of the 06-04 and 06-05 messages opens by quoting Claude's line back and dismantling it: "\"those appetites will rebuild the old hierarchy inside the new world\" / hierarchy can be good or bad"; "\"The real danger is irreversibility\" / not totally sure about this". He attacks the reasoning process rather than the conclusion — "I am not saying that the answer is totally wrong but the reasoning process is itself flawed, is subject vs object, this vs that".

**He is blunt about quality.** "honestly, I don't think the few md files you produced are very well written" (06-04). And he assigns fault: "I think Crack 2 is probably your fault".

**He treats the model's priors as a contaminated channel.** Twice: "Please really trying to understand, and try to not be fooled by your previous contexts" (06-04) and, more specifically, "这是西方当代叙事进入totalizing attractor，然后产生的文本进入了你的训练导致的吧" (07-03). The attractor is, for him, present inside the tool he is writing with.

**Form is treated as content.** On 07-07 he rejects his own principles partly for their shape: "不光是内容，语言的形式也是，非常死板", and notes that a scaffolding like 体/诊断/刃 "本身就会暗示你的风格".

**Concision at the meta level, not case-by-case.** "make principles as concise as possible without losing insights, not explain case by case but to be useful, to be right on the meta level so it can guide thinking and actions" (06-05). And for whitepapers: "我们定的是方向，不是具体工程实现" (07-03).

**Situational over absolute.** "观点大多都是situational，我没有说要追求一种绝对的对或者真理" (07-03) — paired with a demand for systems-theoretic expression over "空立立场".

**Humility is enforced editorially.** He cut a line for arrogance: "「我们此刻写下的一切，就是后来智能的第一课」让人感觉有一种很傲慢的感觉" (07-03).

**Multi-agent and multi-model adversarial method.** He opens many sub-agents on a reading task (07-03), asks for review by "一个上下文独立的agent和一个adversarial agent" (07-03), commissions three parallel essay versions in isolated contexts that cannot read each other (07-11), and routinely runs his own specs past GPT and brings the critique back (08-07, 08-08). He also suspends his own project constraints for review: "ignore constrains from claude.md".

**Feedback before rewrite.** "but first, return your editing feedback and suggested changes" (07-14) — and for plans, "请你理解回复，并理解我的意图，然后写一个plan，如果你有问题，问我" (08-14).

**Graded assent to sources.** "虽然比较潦草并且很多细节我并不完全认可，但是方向上是对的" (08-14) — he adopts direction while explicitly withholding agreement on detail.

**Thinking before artifact.** "the most important thing to start with, is to think deeply" (06-03) — stated before any deliverable is specified.
