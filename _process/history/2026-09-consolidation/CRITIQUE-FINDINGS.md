# Critic findings

Five independent critics audited the organised corpus on 2026-09-04, plus one repair agent. Findings are grouped by lens and severity. Where a finding has been acted on, it is marked.

## Lens: structure — the organisation judged as a working instrument: whether the fifteen-way split is right, whether anything is misfiled, whether the cross-cutting layer earns its place, and whether the folder is navigable and usable at 80–370 KB per document

**What was checked.** Read in full: README.md, MAP.md (176 lines), _process/BRIEF.md, _process/COVERAGE.md, _process/THEORY-TEMPLATE.md, and the structural skeletons of TIMELINE.md, GLOSSARY.md, OPEN-QUESTIONS.md, BOOKS.md, _process/THEORY-DIGEST.md, sources/SOURCES.md, _process/reading-notes/README.md. Extracted the full heading tree of all 15 theories/<slug>/README.md and all 17 synthesis-*.md files and diffed them against THEORY-TEMPLATE.md. Ran quantitative checks: per-README size and claim counts (60–610 numbered claims each); source-group citation histograms per theory (establishing that 12 of 15 theory folders map ~1:1 onto a sources/ group); an orphan scan of every file in 9 source groups against the canonical layer (result: essentially zero orphans — coverage is genuinely complete); validation of all 17 cross-theory synthesis links (all resolve); a count of reading-notes citations from the canonical layer (342); and a diff of source files cited in synthesis-*.md but not in the sibling README. Read the Claims subsection structure, Genealogy and Relations sections of meta-attractor, zhishan, selfishness-spiral and alignment-selection side by side to test the "four theories or one" question; read OPEN-QUESTIONS §2 contradictions against the corresponding theory Open-questions sections and GLOSSARY entries to test whether the cross-cutting layer restates or adds; opened sources/05-alignment-and-moloch/2026-08-22-development-within-understanding-position-paper.md to test whether it belongs where it is filed. Verdict on the headline question: the four diagnosis folders are one theory in four vocabularies, MAP.md and BOOKS.md both already say so and rule correctly against merging them — the defect is not the split, it is that the ruling is invisible from inside the folders. The cross-cutting layer earns its place decisively: OPEN-QUESTIONS adds verdicts the theory files lack, BOOKS refuses eleven of the fifteen as books, GLOSSARY tracks provenance per term. Deliberately not reported: the missing single canonical diagnosis document, the 无主语 gap, and the vocabulary collisions — all are already named as work items in MAP.md and BOOKS.md and are writing tasks, not organisation defects.
