# Targeted review of EXPLORATION.md

Verdict: coherent, substantially well bounded, and consistent with the literature note. Three substantive refinements would prevent the central research claim from becoming weaker or broader than intended. No numerical error found in the displayed three-action example or the resource threshold.

## Recommended changes

1. **Lines 126–128: make the proposed tendency comparative rather than existential.** “Some generative processes develop” asserts an instance of possibility; it does not yet support the following sentence's claim to be stronger than possibility. It also leaves “representations of others' stakes generalize” potentially too close to the desired outcome unless representation is independently measured. Use the stronger formulation already present in conceptual-note.md §8: within a declared population and horizon, increasing accurate cross-position experience and independently measured representational transfer should increase the frequency of costly protection for novel powerless recipients relative to matched controls, without directly rewarding that test behavior. Declare failures—better prediction without greater protection, increased exploitation, or loss at succession—as evidence against this specified bridge. This remains a hypothesis, not an established effect.

2. **Lines 21 and 71–94: distinguish the theorem's narrower dependent variable.** The new resource model establishes conditions for relative growth and paid formation of a supplied responsive protection type. It has no evolving direct-concern variable, no expanding recipient class, and no representation-accuracy variable. Therefore it is not yet a prevalence theorem about wisdom or increasing ethical inclusion. Line 21 should say the constructions establish conditional prevalence/possibility for responsive protection, while a direction toward wider wisdom remains the research hypothesis. Consider naming §71 “A conditional theorem for the spread of responsive protection.” The existing caveats about supplied repertoire and trial rule are good and should remain.

3. **Line 5: formation of recognition is different from acquisition of moral worth.** “Eventually worthy of concern beyond their usefulness” can imply that causal learning makes the affected being worthy. Replace with “eventually recognized as warranting concern beyond their usefulness.” That preserves the distinction between moral standing and the natural development of recognition, defended later in the document.

## Checks and limits

- The static comparison is correct: at c=4, d=6, e=2, H=10, protection is uniquely chosen precisely for v>0.2; equality ties protection with bypass. Increasing d above e does not remove the bypass incentive.
- The displayed multiplication comparison is algebraically correct: multiplying W1>W0 by B(B+K)>0 gives λb(Bq+K)>B(c+λa)+KA. Positive relative advantage is correctly distinguished from absolute growth.
- The occupation-time fraction is correct under the stated independent regenerative assumptions with finite positive conditional mean durations. Classification and the lifetime advantage are properly kept separate.
- math-note.md was not yet present at the initial read; I inspected verify_math.py and math-checks.json, but this review does not certify the still-unavailable prose proof. The mixed-composition statement requires valid positive two-way trial rates. The small-trial dominance statement concerns the long-run composition first, then the limit as the trial fraction vanishes; it need not hold at a fixed short horizon starting with the favored type absent. Preserve those qualifications in the mathematical note.
- Literature synthesis respects the differences between collective formation, choosing supplied institutions, harmful punishment, and strategic extraction. The newly cited Stewart–Plotkin and Xu abstracts also support the scoped contrast at line 120: [population evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.7205) versus [human–computer experimental payoffs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.02378). No universal conflict should be inferred, as the draft correctly says.
- The proposed transfer test is evidence for generalized concern, not unique psychological identification. If implemented, retain the conceptual note's controls for reputation, anticipated reciprocity, demand effects, and removal of the observer's own distress. This is a research-design condition, not a reason to weaken the proposal into unfalsifiability.
