## Research question

Which arrangements of causal processes can maintain, discover, and revise the arrangements through which they act? Does including observers, models, policies, evaluative purposes, and institutional revision within an interconnected causal account provide a foundation with informative constraints?

## Scope and working decisions

- Treat the claim that this is the furthest foundational level as a conjecture with distinct ontological, explanatory, empirical, and ethical readings.
- Use a finite stochastic model as a deliberately limited formal laboratory. Do not equate this model with universal physics or claim that all causation has DAG form.
- Separate physical realization, operational causal modeling, universal causal closure, determinism, and completeness of knowledge.
- Require an explicit intervention family, observation interface, temporal scale, resource boundary, and outcome criteria before calling a causal account informative.
- Separate internal changes to an observer's representation from analyst changes of coordinates.
- Distinguish state augmentation from a substantive explanatory result. Universal recoding can be exact and still add no prediction.
- Preserve normative commitments explicitly. Persistence, causal influence, novelty, and self-maintenance do not establish legitimacy.

## Completed findings

The final synthesis supports causal embedding as an explanatory discipline, without accepting terminal ontology, universal closure, a universal DAG, or normative grounding. Fixed larger dynamics can implement changing effective mechanisms; this state augmentation is a representation construction, not an explanation of unrestricted novelty.

The formal appendix establishes action-wise quotient conditions, a finite-horizon total-variation bound, observational and counterfactual underdetermination examples, query-relative finite-memory limits, a restricted exposed-prediction impossibility, finite-trace fitting vacuity, and descriptive/evaluative underdetermination. These are established ideas or elementary derivations, not novelty claims.

Worked examples distinguish physical wiring, represented wiring and goals; they also separate sensing, evidence standing, deployment access, budget, and endogenous amendment. The paid amendment trajectory includes a fixed program that reads the record and blocked gate before requesting a rule change. Its amendment infrastructure remains a declared short-horizon support assumption.
