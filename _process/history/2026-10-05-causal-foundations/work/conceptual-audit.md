# Conceptual audit: internal observers, revisable institutions, and the limits of a causal foundation

Research memo for the dedicated causal-foundations session, 5 October 2026. This is a critical synthesis and a collection of elementary constructions, not an originality claim or empirical validation. The reference workspace /Users/ren/theories was read only.

## 1. Strongest defensible formulation

**When an account attributes observation, inference, evaluation, action, or revision to an agent or institution, those capacities must be compatible with their realization in the world being explained. For the causal question and time horizon at issue, either model the relevant realization and support, or declare which capacities and supports are held fixed as boundary assumptions. Then ask whether the claimed response remains feasible when those assumptions are relaxed.**

This formulation captures Ren's insight without requiring a complete world model. It prevents a correction theorem from quietly assuming unlimited memory, free experiments, an unchanging evaluator, automatic recognition of evidence, or access to action after that access has disappeared. It does not require every abstract model to simulate brains or assign a cost to every operation. A supplied policy is often a useful approximation; the scope error is treating a result conditional on that supply as establishing the supply itself.

The question becomes informative when sharpened to: **Which arrangements can preserve or restore a specified ability to diagnose and alter their own consequential mechanisms, under declared disturbances, information channels, budgets, deadlines, and evaluation criteria?** Different carriers may take over; indefinite survival of a named organization is not the target by default. Suspension can be appropriate if reopening remains possible and current inquiry is not worth its cost.

This is stronger than saying that everything changes. It is weaker than proving physicalism, universal causal closure, an observer-free description of all reality, or the moral authority of persistence.

## 2. Four claims that must stay separate

| Kind of claim | Defensible content | What has not been established |
|---|---|---|
| Ontological commitment | The relevant observers, policy carriers, and institutional actions participate in one causally connected world; their effects have realizations. | That causation is metaphysically primitive; all reality is physical; every event has a sufficient cause; there is one complete classical state; every observer's experience reduces to that state. |
| Explanatory framework | Include feedback from actions to evidence, representation, evaluation, access, resources, and subsequent actions when it matters for the question. | That one universal causal graph or one fixed decomposition is adequate for every question. |
| Empirical theory | A particular mechanism predicts how manipulating a channel, resource, revision rule, or deployment gate changes specified outcomes. | Any prediction from bare causal embedding alone. |
| Ethical grounding | Explicit commitments determine whose interests, rights, agency, and future opportunities should count; causal analysis assesses consequences and feasibility. | That connectedness, self-maintenance, survival, intelligence, or capacity to revise supplies those commitments. |

“The furthest we can go” therefore has several readings. It may mark a methodological stopping point: no unexplained outside chooser is needed in a particular model. It is not an established terminal ontology. “Causality” itself admits competing accounts, and “how changes change” leaves open what individuates changes, what alternatives count, what makes a dependence causal, and how probability and temporal order are interpreted. A general language of causal processes can underwrite many incompatible substantive theories.

## 3. Hidden assumptions in realization and closure

1. **Participation is weaker than sufficiency.** Saying cognition makes a causal difference through neural, bodily, technological, and social processes does not by itself establish that a complete specification of physical variables fixes every mental fact. No thesis about consciousness is required for the proposed institutional tests.
2. **Local modeling closure differs from physical causal closure.** A model that uses an external disturbance process is intentionally open. Expanding its boundary may explain that disturbance, but no finite study is obliged to include the universe. Unexplained residuals challenge the chosen model before they challenge physical closure.
3. **Closure does not entail determinism.** A physical causal account may specify probability laws, with realization not fixing a unique outcome. Conversely, a deterministic transition law alone does not supply intervention semantics or establish causal direction from data.
4. **One world does not imply one available view.** Agents can occupy different locations, retain different records, possess different permissions, and have incompatible interests. Shared causal participation neither gives them identical observations nor a common evaluative standpoint.
5. **Realization is not one-to-one identity at every level.** An institutional policy can be distributed across documents, software, trained people, and enforcement practices. A change of carrier can preserve the policy; a small physical edit can change its effective rule. Which difference matters depends on declared tests and interventions.
6. **Mathematical variables are not automatically internal representations.** A variable called belief or goal needs a specified interpretation and, for a mechanistic claim, evidence about how it is used. A scientist can describe a system as optimizing some functional without the system encoding that functional or learning a model.
7. **Endogenizing a variable does not endogenize its meaning.** Adding a goal-state update G(t+1)=J(G(t),e(t)) explains a pattern of evaluation changes only after J and the interpretation of G are supplied. It does not explain why that sequence is justified. Likewise, adding a “new concept” state does not demonstrate generation of an unforeseen concept if its meaning and transition were inserted by the analyst.
8. **A modeler's position is not an ontological exception.** This memo and its authoring process also have realizations. Including that fact does not require the memo to contain an exhaustive representation of itself. The model is a finite product used in a larger practice, not the whole practice.

The strongest version of physical closure would need a precise definition of “physical,” the relevant notion of causal sufficiency (deterministic or probabilistic), and criteria for evidence against it. Conservation laws by themselves do not discharge all of these philosophical requirements. The present program can progress without resolving them.

## 4. Elementary propositions and counterexamples

### P1. A bare dynamical representation imposes no restriction on a finite trace

For any observed sequence x(0),...,x(T), define the augmented state z(t)=(t,x(t)) for t=0,...,T and define F(z(t))=z(t+1) for t<T, with F(z(T))=z(T). Then projecting the orbit onto its second coordinate reproduces the sequence exactly. Values of F away from the observed trace can be arbitrary if a larger state space is desired.

Thus “there exists a state space and an update law containing the observer” is not, by itself, an empirically discriminating claim. The construction is an elementary representation, not evidence of the true mechanism or a causal model. Discriminating content requires restrictions chosen independently of the realized trace: locality, admissible interfaces, transition families, complexity or memory limits, intervention predictions, or independently measured supports. Allowing post hoc boundary, variable, and law changes to absorb every failure makes the framework uninformative.

### P2. A descriptive process does not uniquely select its evaluation

Fix a transition model and two reachable outcomes u and v. Attach evaluation J with J(u)>J(v), or evaluation J' with J'(u)<J'(v). If neither evaluation is constrained by additional normative premises, the descriptive transition equations do not select one ordering. The same claim holds when the descriptive model includes all actual evaluative utterances: it then explains which judgments occurred, but the inference from occurrence to justified authority still requires an additional premise.

This is a formal underdetermination observation, not a proof that moral facts cannot exist, that values are arbitrary, or that causal evidence is irrelevant to ethics. Once commitments are specified, causal evidence can refute a factual premise in a practical argument. A stable coercive institution or an accurate surveillance apparatus is an immediate counterexample to “successful self-maintenance implies legitimacy.”

### P3. Public exact prediction can fail by feedback

Suppose a device must publicly announce a bit p that is guaranteed to equal an agent's next action a. Suppose the agent can read the announcement in time and can implement a=1-p. Then the requested guarantee p=a is inconsistent. This two-line diagonal construction uses no chaos, ignorance of initial conditions, or lack of causal realization.

Scope matters: it assumes a public prediction, an agent able to negate it, and an exact guarantee over that response class. It does not rule out accurate private forecasts, coarse predictions, conditional predictions, probabilistic calibration, restricted agent classes, or useful self-models. “Everything is physically realized” does not imply that any subsystem can furnish every correct prediction about the whole.

### P4. Feedback and persistence do not establish discovery

A two-state oscillator z(t+1)=1-z(t) has feedback and indefinite change but no supplied diagnostic or proposal-testing function. A thermostat can reject temperature disturbances without revising its target or sensor. A self-maintaining institution can alter its criteria to make unfavorable evidence inadmissible. Conversely, an institution can rationally retire a obsolete practice while maintaining the public function through a successor.

Accordingly, the core question needs operationally separate predicates: response, inference, discovery within a hypothesis class, effective-mechanism revision, evaluation revision, and justified improvement. None follows merely from recurrence or from including all variables in one equation.

## 5. Worked institutional example: evidence, admission, and enactment

This finite episode is deliberately simple. It isolates causal roles; it does not generate its rules or explain their legitimacy.

There is a hidden state theta in {0,1}, equally likely. An institution must choose action A in {0,1}. Its declared task loss is 3 times the indicator A differs from theta. Its incumbent policy is A=0, yielding expected loss 1.5. A paid diagnostic costing 1 creates a correct durable record Y=theta. Installing a record-responsive policy costs another 0.2. The model ends after one operational decision; retention and staff availability for that horizon are declared boundary assumptions.

Let H in {0,1} represent whether this type of evidence is admitted by the operative review procedure. Let K in {0,1} represent whether the review body has actual deployment access. Neither variable denotes ethical legitimacy. With sufficient remaining budget, define R=H*K for installing the policy and set A=Y when R=1, otherwise A=0. If the diagnostic was run, its cost has already been incurred. This gives:

| Procedure | Diagnostic cost | Installation cost | Expected task loss | Total expected loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No diagnostic; incumbent action | 0 | 0 | 1.5 | 1.5 |
| Diagnostic, admission and access | 1 | 0.2 | 0 | 1.2 |
| Diagnostic, evidence excluded (H=0) | 1 | 0 | 1.5 | 2.5 |
| Diagnostic, no deployment access (K=0) | 1 | 0 | 1.5 | 2.5 |

The arithmetic follows by enumerating theta=0 and theta=1. Exact rational enumeration in this session returned total expected losses 3/2, 6/5, 5/2, and 5/2; both announced-bit cases in P3 and every binary trace of lengths 2–5 in P1 were also checked. These small enumerations check the illustrations; the written constructions supply the general arguments. Admission and deployment have distinct realizations and can be manipulated separately. The same true evidence and same declared loss can coexist with different actions. The institution might model theta perfectly and still lack the route to act. Knowing this does not show that the reviewer should possess authority in every institution; that is a further normative and practical question.

Make the gates revisable by letting an existing amendment channel install H'=1 or K'=1 at cost k. If a gate is blocked, the diagnostic is already purchased, the remaining budget covers k+0.2, and revision happens before the decision deadline, the expected task-loss reduction from revision is 1.5 and the incremental total benefit is 1.3-k. Hence k<1.3 is sufficient for positive expected benefit under these supplied rules. The amendment channel is itself a causal mechanism with a declared access condition; it is not a magical meta-action. If no such channel is available, a beneficial revision may be unreachable. One can in turn model how that channel is maintained, or state a boundary assumption; indefinitely adding levels without constraints gives no additional prediction.

Four types of change are now visible:

- **Ordinary state change:** the record changes from empty to Y=theta while institutional rules stay fixed.
- **Effective-mechanism change:** H or K changes, altering how the same record can affect deployment. At a more inclusive level this can be ordinary state change in a fixed amendment mechanism.
- **Representation change:** a linked record or contextual distinction becomes representable and usable; merely renaming Y cannot cause the improved response.
- **Purpose change:** the operative loss function changes, for example by newly counting harms to people omitted by the earlier review procedure. This can be implemented in documents, software, and behavior, but the fact of implementation does not establish its justification.

The final purpose-change example is a conceptual extension, not part of the numerical table. All numerical comparisons use the same supplied loss. The model's “causal structure changed” claim is relative to the institution's effective interface and intervention tests; it does not require fundamental laws to change.

For an AI analogue, replace diagnostic with model evaluation, Y with a retained evaluation record, H with the evaluation admission procedure, and K with access to deployment or rollback. The model does not presume that evaluator agreement equals alignment. A system that can edit its pass criterion instead of correcting harm can improve its measured score while degrading the externally specified objective.

## 6. Primary precedents checked, with their limits

The following sources were inspected online on 5 October 2026. Each source summary is intentionally narrow; none is offered as proving the entire synthesis.

1. **Conant and Ashby (1970), “Every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system.”** The original paper connects successful simple regulation with a model relation in its particular setup. Its title must not be used as an unrestricted theorem that every effective organism or institution learns an explicit predictive world model. The simplicity and success requirements, definition of model, and assumed regulator/system setup do substantive work. [Original paper, DOI 10.1080/00207727008920220](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207727008920220); [inspected original PDF](https://theisticscience.com/papers/tree/BioRegulation/Conant-IJSS1970.pdf).

2. **Ashby, Design for a Brain, second edition (1960).** Ultrastability is relevant precedent for a system whose slower reorganization changes its effective responding organization. Section 11/11 also directly cautions against equating richer connectivity with better adaptation: preserving partial successes can require that some parts do not affect others. This supports examination of feedback architecture rather than a slogan that everything should be connected. The archive search exposed that section; this session did not inspect the complete book. [Author's archive PDF](https://www.ashby.info/Ashby%20-%20Design%20for%20a%20Brain%20-%20The%20Origin%20of%20Adaptive%20Behavior.pdf).

3. **Wolpert (2008), “Physical limits of inference.”** The paper formalizes devices whose setup and conclusions occur in the same possible universe histories about which they infer, and proves restricted impossibility results. The setup highlights that embodiment can limit joint inference independently of particular physical laws. These are formal results for declared inference-device definitions, not proof that all scientific prediction, self-description, or comparison of theories fails. P3 above is a separate elementary illustration, not a restatement of the paper's full theorems. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/0708.1362v2); [published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physd.2008.03.040).

4. **Virgo, Biehl, Baltieri, and Capucci (2025), “A ‘good regulator theorem’ for embodied agents.”** In deterministic discrete-time coupled machines, the paper relates invariant regulating sets to consistent belief interpretations and a normative map. Its construction depends on an observer's interpretation and admits trivial models, including a doorstop example. The theorem's interpretation of a system as having a model is therefore not evidence that it internally learned a rich predictive representation. The authors explicitly restrict the result's scope and say the stochastic extension is not obvious. This is a recent preprint, used as a checked formal precedent rather than consensus. [Primary text, especially definitions 3.1–3.3, theorem 3.4, and section 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06326v1).

5. **Kolchinsky and Wolpert (2018), “Semantic information, autonomous agency, and nonequilibrium statistical physics.”** The paper defines a technical kind of semantic information through the causal contribution of correlations to a viability function. The definition declares a viability function, information measure, intervention family, boundary, timescale, and initial distribution. The useful lesson is to make those choices explicit. It does not establish that all linguistic meaning, truth, moral value, or legitimate institutional purpose reduces to self-maintenance. The distinction between their technical definition and ordinary semantics is necessary in using this precedent. [Primary text, sections I.2 and IV–VI](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.08053).

6. **Hume (1740), A Treatise of Human Nature, 3.1.1.27.** The passage demands an explanation when reasoning shifts from descriptive premises to an ought. That supplies a historical warning against deriving a duty to preserve or expand a process merely from its causal dependence. The present argument does not require accepting all of Hume's metaethics. [Original text](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/3/1/1).

Maturana, Varela, Rosen, autopoiesis, and organizational closure are possible further precedents, but no precise theorem from them is asserted here. The parent research is separately auditing organizational closure. Its meaning should not be conflated with physical causal closure or complete statistical isolation.

## 7. How this proposal could fail or become uninformative

The foundational commitment as stated is too broad for a single experiment to falsify it. A concrete application should instead preregister what can fail:

- If an allegedly necessary evidence channel is removed but correction proceeds unchanged, the necessity claim fails unless a specified substitute route accounts for it.
- If manipulating admission or deployment access does not change the predicted response after information, resource, and timing effects are controlled, the claimed gate mechanism is unsupported.
- If an intervention that supposedly changes an effective mechanism has the same input/output consequences on every declared test, the claimed effective change has no demonstrated content at that level.
- If a capacity measure predicts recovery only because “capacity” was defined as whatever produced recovery, it is circular. Define its tests, carriers, and access independently of the outcome.
- If performance disappears when matched expenditure is controlled, a claim about superior revision architecture may reduce to a resource effect.
- If a system improves the current metric through evaluator revision while worsening independently specified protected outcomes, the inference from self-revision to warranted improvement fails.
- If every counterexample triggers unrestricted redefinition of the system boundary, goal, timescale, or hidden state, the proposal is an interpretive vocabulary rather than a testable theory.

A useful first study would match diagnostic information and budgets, independently vary evidence admission and enactment access, and record actual policy changes before a fixed deadline. A second would make access and resources degrade and test paid restart or succession. Only thereafter should the program claim to explain maintenance of the amendment process itself. Purpose revision requires a separately declared criterion for evaluating changes and room for disagreement about that criterion.

## 8. Fit with Ren's existing record

The read-only README frames the target as maintaining transformation when its enabling conditions can be damaged or renewed. The claim register already separates exact identities, conditional results, empirical hypotheses, proposed definitions, and normative commitments. This audit preserves that discipline.

- U05 already supplies a precise information limit: observationally indistinguishable states may require incompatible safe interventions.
- U12/U13 and the frame-formation snapshot show how experience and retained representation can reinforce or eliminate a practice under a supplied update law. Those are conditional mechanisms, not unrestricted concept formation.
- U24/U25 and the inquiry-renewal snapshot distinguish value, institutional credit, and continued access; paid restart can matter more than uninterrupted operation.
- N01 explicitly protects shared agency and legitimate revision as a normative commitment. It should remain an explicit commitment after the causal reframing.

The existing record's most promising next move is therefore to endogenize a particular previously supplied revision support, then derive and test consequences. Merely replacing all earlier variables with a universal causal vocabulary would add no comparable result.
