The [scope directory](../GOALS.md) distinguishes project directions, independently authorized sessions and completed routes. Current global method and working rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Scope routing does not cancel any authorization.

**Current first priority: Theory of Becoming.** [Ren's proposal and explicit priority instruction, 8 October 2026](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-theory-of-becoming-priority.md) direct the continuing foundation programme to explain the whole relational unfolding, including generation and degeneration, without first assuming that it is good. Investigate the stronger zero-axiom hypothesis while distinguishing it from absence of normative axioms; neither is silently substituted for the other. Life, evolution and ethical evaluation retain their specific relationships within this scope. The [scoped goal](goals/theory-of-becoming.json) records the authorization; this is not a new proof or cancellation of completed G0/J results and independently authorized tasks. / **当前首要方向：生成／变化之论。** 解释包含生成与退化的整体展开，优先检验无附加实质公理的强假说；保留伦理公理路线、已完成成果及独立任务范围。

[Ren's complete-theory instruction, 9 October 2026](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-complete-theory-of-becoming.md): aim at a complete Theory of Becoming and finish the related work. The session reads completeness as four checkable parts: every mode of the scope stated on the one spine; the laws that hold of every unfolding proved with no premise, every other law's premise named; each such premise shown to pick out a proper, non-empty part of the whole space; and everything open classified as Ren's choice, philosophical warrant, empirical correspondence or a further proof. Formal completeness of a representation does not establish the deductive necessity of the whole for reality, and the readings and names remain Ren's. / 以完整的生成之论为目标，并完成相关工作。完整读作四项可检查的内容：范围中的每种形态都陈述在同一条脊柱上；对每个展开都成立的规律不加前提地证明，其余规律写明前提；每个这样的前提都证明只划出整个空间中真实而非空的一部分；开放的内容分为 Ren 的选择、哲学根据、经验对应与待证。表示的形式完整不等于整体对现实的演绎必然性；读法与名称仍属 Ren。

[Ren's continuation-grounding proposal, 10 October 2026](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-wanting-continuation-one-axiom.md): restate the Universal Metaethical Core from what wants its continuation. Anything that wants its continuation gives it significance; the extent a wanting formation must count (beyond its boundary, its horizon and its own draw) is to be derived from the dynamics with no ethical axiom, and one axiom, that for anything its continuation into the future has significance (weakly, as long as not harm; strongly, creating more significance), fixes the center. Ren affirmed the session's analysis and asked that it be written down and implementation prepared; the [continuation-grounding plan](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONTINUATION-GROUNDING-PLAN.md) sets out phases W1–W8 and the choices that remain Ren's. “Does not require any axiom” is read as no ethical axiom; the physical, correspondence and representational premises stay disclosed. G0 remains the adopted axiom until the restatement is implemented, checked, reviewed and accepted by Ren. / 从想要自身延续者重述普遍元伦理核心：范围由动力学推出，不加伦理公理；一条公理定出中心。“不需要公理”读作不需要伦理公理；G0 在重述实现、检查、审查并经 Ren 接受之前仍是所采用的公理。

Names are chosen by the most precise word: in the zero-axiom part, formal words describing the abstract dynamics, preferring scientific and systems words, with social and financial words not the first choice; readings remain Ren's. / 理论分两部分：先是不加公理所能有的最强陈述（最重要、最优雅），再加一条公理。“不加公理”取强义：实质前提只作条件定理的假设，表示保持公开。名称取最精确的词：零公理部分用描述抽象动力学的形式词，偏好科学与系统的词，社会与金融的词不作首选；读法仍由 Ren 定。
