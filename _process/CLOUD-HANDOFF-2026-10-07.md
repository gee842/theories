## What is already established, and at what scope

**Conditional foundation:** the recorded five-condition assessment accepts the candidate at its stated conditional scope. It includes formation, operational significance, acquired response and revision, recipient evidence, formed evaluative influence, warranted bounded applications and practical wisdom. It retains constructive and destructive regimes. Do not repeatedly reopen its completion by imposing a new universal apparatus or treating every later extension as an original prerequisite.

**Mechanisms:** contact formation, contextual recording/binding, testing experimental access, acquired calibration, architecture construction, revision of candidate generators, inquiry selection/synthesis and finite supported succession already have accepted results. Some are joined through actual histories. The reconciliation maps each to its implementation, proofs and limits. A premise supplied in one local example is not evidence that the entire theory lacks an explanation of that capacity. Conversely, a result in another branch does not automatically supply this example's exact parts, histories or resources.

**Ethical candidate:** goodness concerns the quality of the whole unfolding—what it realizes and how it changes the conditions of further differentiated realization, renewal and completion. This is a substantive candidate, not an established identity between all generation and goodness. Persistence, reproduction, information, coordination, fulfilled preference, maximum options and successful learning are not sufficient ethical measures.

**Integral and future field:** compare causally supported whole continuations, using scalar expectations or temporal integrals only where justified. Whole appraisal need not begin with independent individual utility atoms. Present/terminal goods, history, uncertainty and real relationships remain. Do not count mutually exclusive futures as co-occurring, or count a downstream good again as its enabler's value-to-go. Finite differences of divergent totals require careful limits; oscillating comparisons need not yield a total order.

The newer future-field work includes changes to the conditions generating possibilities. Probability transfers can preserve a defended better–worse ordering without fixing a cardinal scale. Entropy and endpoint probabilities omit ethically relevant relations. Under warranted procedures, an initially fair selection can generate a particular delivery claim and legitimately close options. The mathematical and causal results do not establish the standing or authority premises themselves.

**Situated judgment:** a bounded performance/recovery comparison defends relinquishing a genuinely improving shared achievement when continuing seriously impairs ordinary living and relevant alternatives fail. This uses an explicit ethical priority. A stable wider concern can explain changing the concrete pursuit; the example neither demonstrates acquisition of that understanding nor proves creation of a new ultimate ethical standard.

## What remains genuinely open

1. The ethical identification: why the proposed whole realization merits favorable or adverse appraisal. Calling it genuine good, harm or fittingness already carries content needing defense. A richer causal description can improve factual adequacy without supplying this warrant.
2. Universal scope and practical force: which justified grounds extend beyond prior membership, roles, agreement or the actor's existing concern, and what follows as a reason, permission, obligation or authority. These conclusions are distinct. No complete ranking of all possible lives is a prerequisite for a contributory minimum.
3. The stronger assumption-free foundation and its explanatory dependencies. Conditional universality is a real result, not the same achievement. Standard logical foundations, law choices and interpretation cannot disappear by renaming premises as definitions.
4. Claim-specific composition and empirical identification where a stronger application asserts them. Follow actual missing interfaces, not a generic ever-growing list. Preserved failures, conventional equivalence and uncertain empirical results are part of the evidence.

The [universal ethical minimum](../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) contains defended bounded claims about relief, rescue and latitude. Read its scopes rather than reducing the entire project to bare agnosticism. Conversely, these arguments are not a deduction of universal ethics from descriptive dynamics.
