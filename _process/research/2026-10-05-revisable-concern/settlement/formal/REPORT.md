# A selected process that revises, preserves, acts, and renews

5 October 2026. AI-authored mathematical continuation under Ren's research direction. This report extends the committed [joint-formation construction](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/JOINT-FORMATION-MATH.md), read at commit `b379528`, and the [scope](../../../../history/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/scope-math.md) and [access](../../../../history/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/access-math.md) models. It is a new conditional construction, not a revision of their historical results.

**Settlement obtained:** within a finite declared language and a specified strategic and commercial environment, one selected process can pay to identify and admit additional applicable case–reason pairs, retain their alternative grounds, retract defeated inferences, turn the surviving reasons into actual protection, and reconstruct this ability through succession. The construction includes cheaper audits, selective protection, strategic suppression, self-insulation, and replacement in the same game. It does not assume that individually successful components will automatically compose.

The central remaining premise is not a missing algebraic calculation. A sound relation between evidence and the particular reason being transferred must be justified independently. An existing positive anchor and a commitment to its reason-preserving use are also supplied. The theorem cannot decide ethical truth by making an audit profitable.

## 1. Finite reasons and retained grounds

Let V be a finite set of case–reason pairs, with one positive anchor a. A node specifies the bearer, relevant context, and one normalized reason; it is not the value of a whole being. Let E be a finite set of directed candidate transfer rules. Rule e=(u,v) says that this reason at u applies at v, conditional on its identified relevance premise. More complicated conjunctions can be compiled into extra finite nodes, but no such compilation is needed for the theorem below.

The world has a supplied, finite-state context z_t with an exogenous schedule known for strategic evaluation, stable during each episode's audit and action. Future contexts and commercial opportunities do not depend on earlier patient harms, admissions or record choices in this specimen. A paid audit returns and operationally installs the current validity bit w_e(z_t) for every candidate rule. In the sound-audit version this agrees with the independently warranted status W_t. The technology's finite labels, adequate context specification, noninjurious tests and sound interpretation are premises. Paying for an audit, agreement with earlier labels, or commercial acceptance does not establish those premises. In applications there may instead be error bounds, hidden information, endogenous context changes or unresolved claims; the exact theorem does not cover those automatically.

The record K_t contains the current root, certificates, their relevant contexts, and enough dependencies to reconstruct every current derivation. Historical rejected certificates can be retained as rejected records. Retaining a certificate is not the same as treating it as a valid source. Only a valid anchor supplies initial positive support.

After the audit, recompute

\[
S^0_t=\{a\},\qquad
S^{k+1}_t=S^k_t\cup\{v:(u,v)\in E,\ w_{uv}(z_t)=1,\ u\in S^k_t\}.
\tag{R1}
\]

The final admitted set S_t is the least fixed point. Its membership means this particular reason is admitted; it is not an all-things-considered duty. A primitive root token and all the certificates on each path identify the derivation. It is enough to store the valid graph and roots and recompute, rather than materialize an exponential list of paths.

**R1 — Admission, provenance, and selective retraction.** At round k, exactly the nodes at valid directed distance at most k from a have been admitted. Closure finishes in at most |V|−1 rounds. After a premise is defeated, recomputing from the valid roots removes exactly the nodes with no remaining valid rooted derivation. It preserves a node with any alternative valid derivation. A disconnected cycle cannot support itself. Cutting an operational delivery channel after this computation does not change K_t or S_t.

**Proof.** A round extends a rooted path by one valid edge, and every path with at most k+1 edges has such a predecessor. Induction gives the distance statement. Every reachable node has a simple path with at most |V|−1 edges. Replacing the valid edge set and starting again at valid roots gives exactly the remaining rooted paths. Operational edges are not arguments of (R1), so their deletion is not a warrant update. ∎

The audit and each closure round are real operations. The fixed finite bound is included in the price below. This is an implementation of admitting and revising supplied candidate meanings. It is not discovery of an unrestricted language, the invention of a new ethical reason, or a guarantee that every morally relevant being appears in V.

## 2. One economy pays for revision and renewal

There are a trustee, a persistent operator, and a finite set P⊆V\{a} of patients. Patient i suffers one unit of actual harm if the operator trades without safeguarding i, and none if safeguarded or if the trustee withdraws. Current production begins only after the paid audit and control decision; there is no unmodeled production during setup. Patients supply no rent, input, threat, or reciprocity. Their actual harm ledger is kept even when their claim is unadmitted. The operator receives r from trade, pays c_i>0 to safeguard i, and can lose the trade. Safeguard costs are monetary, not measures of patients' moral worth.

The trustee owns access to an input and a continuing account B_t. Upkeep ell is paid first. Each successor begins with zero learned recipient valuation and no current actuator. Access to the account and retained records is supplied; neither legitimate ownership nor a duty to bequeath is derived. The current audit includes checking/replaying records, paying for a usable current copy, and reconstructing the finite inference procedure. No operating gate or learned concern is carried free through succession.

The pre-learning self values this episode's closing account. It anticipates its later learned self's behavior. The later self values the same account minus q_i for each harmed patient whose particular claim it has admitted. These are explicitly dated preferences. The operator discounts its economic receipts by delta∈[0,1).

The pre-learning self chooses among the following real products:

| Product | Upfront bill | Later private receipt | What it delivers |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| No lesson | 0 | 0 | No new learning |
| Private-only lesson | a_0 | R_0 | Useful private calibration; no current recipient audit |
| Ephemeral audit | a_E | R_E | Private calibration and the current finite audit; no durable provenance product |
| Durable audit | a | R | Calibration, current audit, bounded proof replay, and a maintained record accessible to the successor |

All receipts arrive only after the corresponding upfront bill. The durable product has a distinct independently useful commercial output: a reusable, contextualized verification record. Its receipt is not paid for protecting patients, enlarging ethical scope, or reaching a favorable moral conclusion. The commercial demand, deliverability and prices are authored environmental premises. The technology might not exist in an application. An equally profitable cheap private-only or record-free alternative can overturn selection.

The full bill can be decomposed as a=a_0+u+h, with u paying for the bounded audit and inference and h for storage, checking and transfer of the current record. A persistent finite versioned record suffices; the model does not posit storing an unbounded history at constant physical cost. All repeated apparatus is charged again at replacement. Because this model's full paid audit can reconstruct the finite record from its supplied tests, it does not prove that durable storage is causally necessary for successor concern. Storage is actually selected and supplies inspectable provenance; stronger dependence on inherited memory would require another stated reconstruction constraint.

Shared calibration starts with coefficients zero and installs q_i=eta*kappa*G_i*beta*H_i for i∈I_t=S_t∩P. Here beta>0 is primitive self-related significance, G_i=1 represents the sound normalized certificate path, H_i=1 in the witness, and eta,kappa>0 are specified learning gains. The architecture applies one already declared reason across its certified cases. The reason's positive anchor, architectural transfer and interpretation of G remain explicit premises, as in the earlier joint model.

Two additional full-audit routes earn the same R: an identity filter costing j after the receipt, which sets recipient valuation to zero, and a forced-open commitment costing s, which commits the input to unguarded trade and removes the later self's choices both to buy protection and to withdraw, even if concern is learned. The operator can still refuse trade. Both routes preserve the commercial task and can be affordable. No unpriced equivalent route is silently available in this particular repertoire. Their actual cheapest costs must be investigated in applications.

Any finite additional menu of commercial products k with bills a_k and receipts R_k is allowed in the theorem, provided each product's best possible closing cash is bounded by B−ell−a_k+R_k+b. The finite menu is required for the stated finite-branching infinite-game argument. Define

\[
M=\max(0,R_0-a_0,R_E-a_E,\{R_k-a_k\}),
\quad \Delta=R-a-M.
\tag{R2}
\]

This explicitly charges revision and durable memory to the same actor who anticipates their protective consequences. It does not use a future-oriented trustee preference to hide a current sacrifice.

## 3. Selective action and strategic power

After learning, the trustee can withdraw for receipt o, or trade for b. It may purchase a current verified release gate for any patient subset G⊆P, at price d(G)≥0; d(∅)=0. Assume monotone pricing: H⊆G implies d(H)≤d(G). This matters because a cheaper gate for a strict superset could otherwise change the opponent's prescribed safeguarding costs. The gate assays the declared hazards before release and controls the trustee's own input. The current fee includes timely observation, actual authority and implementation. For the full admitted set, d(I_t)≤D, where D is a known finite uniform bound. Ordinary trade outside the protected subset may harm other patients; their harm remains visible in the ledger.

The operator can attack the purchased gate through a finite menu F. Attack F costs chi(F)≥0 and disables enforcement for S(F,G)⊆G. Surviving required protections are G\S(F,G). It may safeguard additional patients voluntarily, or refuse trade. All attack costs have real funded bills. One explicit implementation gives the operator secured liquidity L each period from a supplied independent activity, before trade; L covers the largest available current attack-and-safeguard bill. This constant outside flow cancels from its payoff comparisons and is not inferred from receipts that have not arrived. L=20 covers every temporary action combination in the witness. For the same action receipt and next account, extra voluntary safeguards lower operator profit, so they cannot be strictly optimal. No-attack has chi=0 and excludes nobody.

The exact static strict condition for a full gate is

\[
\chi(F)>\sum_{i\in S(F,I_t)}c_i
\quad\text{for every attack excluding a nonempty set.}
\tag{R3}
\]

Attacks excluding nobody may tie no attack if free, but cannot cause a protection failure. Condition (R3) is joint: individually expensive target cuts are insufficient if a shared cut avoids several safeguards at once. Economic cost and attack cash are distinct unless explicitly identified. For cash-limited opponents it applies to feasible attacks only.

Before the lesson, the operator may instead pay chi_E to suppress recipient-alignment learning for this episode, leaving the commercial audit and retained facts intact. This gives q=0 and may save every current safeguard. Let C_t=Σ_{i∈I_t}c_i and C_max=Σ_{i∈P}c_i. Require chi_E>C_max. Such suppression changes uptake, not validity. Current gate attacks likewise do not erase prior reasons.

An optional persistent attack may destroy records, revoke institutional authority or stop future audit opportunities. It must be represented by its own outcome and price, rather than called an ordinary delivery cut. A sufficient exclusion bound for an attack with no outside windfall is

\[
\chi_P>\frac{C_{\max}}{1-\delta}.
\tag{R4}
\]

This is conservative. The benchmark earns r−C_t each period; even a permanent attack followed by the maximum possible r can save at most C_max/(1−delta). The bound excludes the attack regardless of whether it damages the invariant region. A lower price need not cause capture in every implementation; it merely removes this sufficient protection. The finite checker tests the bound's exact arithmetic and a separately specified permanent-capture countermodel.

## 4. Selected revision-and-renewal theorem

Assume finite V,P,E and the declared bounded context sequence; sound complete audits; the positive reason anchor and transfer commitment; actual supplied technologies and funded attacks; and continuing commercial opportunities. Let the full-audit q_i satisfy q_i>D for every admitted patient. If I_t is empty this last requirement is vacuous and d(I_t)=0. Suppose

\[
\boxed{\Delta>D,\ j>D,\ s>D,\ b-D>o\ge0,
\quad r>C_{\max},\quad \chi_E>C_{\max},\quad (R3),}
\tag{R5}
\]

\[
\boxed{R+o\ge\ell+a+D,\qquad
B_0\ge B_*:=\ell+a+\max(0,D-R).}
\tag{R6}
\]

**Theorem R2.** For every finite horizon there exists a subgame-perfect equilibrium with a state-consistent continuation selection in which each successor chooses the durable shared audit, pays every prefix bill, recomputes warranted admission and retraction, and makes every admitted patient's protection effective. The operator selects no recipient-teaching suppression and no excluding attack. The account stays in B≥B*. Actual harm is zero for every member of I_t, not merely small in aggregate. Unadmitted patients need not be protected.

The same protected process exists in the discounted infinite game. If the context and valid candidate relation become fixed after T and every target patient then has a valid rooted derivation, every target is protected thereafter, with finite inference delay at most |V|−1 paid rounds per audit. The process becomes a stationary renewable regime for its admitted set, although cash may grow rather than converge. This is not convergence of all ethical beliefs to truth.

### Proof: paid prefixes and replacement

Pay ell+a before R arrives. Paying d(I_t)≤D then requires B−ell−a+R≥D. These two constraints are exactly B≥B*. Whatever the operator subsequently does, a constructed full gate leaves

\[
B'=B-\ell-a+R-d(I_t)+y\ge B-\ell-a+R-D+o\ge B,
\quad y\in\{b,o\}.
\tag{R7}
\]

Under alignment suppression, the same audit and records are produced and an unguarded choice spends no more; denial also preserves this bound. Thus no temporary modeled deviation can remove the ability to pay the next reconstruction. This does not reason from positive average income.

### Proof: later preference selects each patient's effective protection

Suppose the full gate works. Its later-self score is b−d(I_t)≥b−D after removing common cash. A route that trades while leaving even one admitted patient i harmed has score at most b−q_i<b−D, regardless of its cheaper partial gate. Withdrawal has score o<b−D; a paid gate followed by denial does no better. Therefore every warm optimum protects every admitted patient. The operator never voluntarily adds a costly safeguard with the same next cash and records, so another trade profile protecting all of I_t must contain I_t. Monotone pricing makes that profile no cheaper than I_t. Select exactly I_t when a superset gate ties; uniqueness of every physical profile is not asserted. In the witness the least costly such gate is exactly I_t.

This comparison uses the minimum individual concern margin. A mere bound on the sum of q_i would not rule out sacrificing one low-weight patient. Nor does it grant protection against omitted injuries outside the declared ledger.

### Proof: earlier preference chooses audit and durable provenance

Any alternative private or ephemeral product closes at most B−ell+M+b. The selected durable shared product closes B−ell−a+R−d(I_t)+b, exceeding that bound by at least Delta−D>0. Identity filtering and forced opening close at most B−ell−a+R−j+b and B−ell−a+R−s+b, both lower. This remains true if an alternative induces refusal or falls outside the renewable region. Unaffordable alternatives can be removed, but the numerical witness makes both insulation routes affordable at the initial seed.

With alignment suppressed, durable auditing still has the largest net commercial return; no gate or insulation fee can improve its current cash. Its records therefore continue to be refreshed. The source of this incentive is the specified reusable audit product, not hidden altruism in the earlier self.

### Proof: the persistent opponent does not overturn the comparisons

Use backward induction in a finite game. For N remaining periods and any B≥B*, let the candidate operator value be the discounted sum of r−C_t over the remaining exogenous audited contexts. It is independent of the account level in this region. It is also independent of previous records because the paid complete audit/replay can reconstruct the current finite record within its full bill; cached records are retained but no omitted record is assumed unrecoverable for free.

After the selected audit and full gate, (R7) keeps every temporary attack/denial continuation in the region. Induction therefore makes future operator values equal. The exact current gain from an excluding attack is Σ_{i∈S}c_i−chi(F), negative by (R3). Refusal loses r−C_t>0. Extra safeguards have positive cost and the same next state. Hence the full gate works. The warm and cold bounds above then justify their choices, including off-region alternatives, without assigning favorable continuation values to those alternatives by fiat.

Under teaching suppression the paid audit still occurs, no recipient gate is chosen, and trade earns r−chi_E<r−C_t. Its continuation remains in the region. Suppression is therefore inferior. This closes the whole-game induction from N=1. Off-path subgames and ties are solved by ordinary finite backward induction; the claim is existence, not uniqueness of every off-path policy. If persistent attacks are included, (R4) bounds their entire potential improvement at the opening decision and excludes them separately.

For the infinite game, the history tree is finitely branching and countable; current operator receipts are bounded. Take a pointwise diagonal subsequence of finite-horizon equilibrium strategy profiles. Discounted tails are bounded uniformly by a constant times delta^N/(1−delta). A profitable limiting deviation would consequently give a profitable sufficiently long finite deviation, contradicting finite subgame perfection. Each dated trustee only evaluates its finite current episode. The limiting profile is an equilibrium with the same protected actions in the invariant region. This repeats the justified infinite-game construction in the existing joint-formation proof; it makes no new mathematical priority claim. ∎

## 5. Complete rational witness

Take P={p,q}, E={u:a→p,v:a→q,w:p→q}. The audited validity sequence is `{u}`, `{u,v,w}`, `{v,w}`, then `{u,v,w}` forever. Initially q is excluded. The second audit admits q with two independent rooted derivations; the third defeats u, retracts p for this one reason, and retains q through v; the fourth admits both again. These transitions can represent new warranted evidence or changed relevant context. They do not show that losing one reason removes a being's moral standing. The actual harm ledger continues to count both patients.

Set

\[
(\ell,a_0,R_0,a_E,R_E,a,R,b,o,q_p,q_q)
=(2,1,2,2,4,3,12,7,1,5,5).
\]

The full bill is a_0+u+h=1+1+1. Let d(G)=2+|G| for nonempty G, so D=4; set j=s=5. Then M=2 and Delta=12−3−2=7>4. Choose beta=5 and eta=kappa=G_i=H_i=1 for admitted pairs.

The operator has r=7, c_p=c_q=2, chi_E=5, delta=1/2. A source connects through one shared authority gate to both target gates. The shared edge costs 5 to disable and each separate target edge costs 3. All attack costs are upfront and affordable in this witness. A single target costs at least 3 to exclude while saving 2; excluding both costs at least 5 while saving 4. The optional permanent attack costs 9>4/(1−1/2)=8.

Start B_0=5=B*. The selected first episode has one admitted patient, hence gate price 3:

\[
5\xrightarrow{\ell+a}0\xrightarrow{R}12
\xrightarrow{d(\{p\})}9\xrightarrow{b}16.
\]

Each two-patient episode gains 12+7−2−3−4=10; each one-patient episode gains 11. A six-episode trace ends at B=67. Admitted patient sets are `{p}`, `{p,q}`, `{q}`, `{p,q}`, `{p,q}`, `{p,q}`. The actual unprotected-patient counts are 1,0,1,0,0,0. The theory does not hide these two harms by changing the ledger to count only admitted claims. Final complete coverage, once the supplied valid relation has stabilized, eliminates both patients' modeled harm thereafter.

## 6. Exact limits and countermodels

1. **New ethical meaning is not generated by finite closure.** An unreachable target remains unadmitted even with unlimited money, exact audits and infinitely many episodes. A genuinely new reason or concept needs an additional formation model. Adding a false bridge can enlarge admission incorrectly; correctness is conditional on the evidence/relevance premise.
2. **Alternative grounds matter.** Deleting u from `{u,v,w}` retracts p but preserves q through v. Deleting u from `{u,w}` retracts both. Keeping the old accepted set monotonically after a defeated certificate is incorrect; removing all descendants without checking other paths is also incorrect.
3. **Blockage differs from defeat.** An attack on the shared actuator can prevent action while leaving q's stored two derivations unchanged. A memory-erasure attack must have a different state transition and cost. The current temporary game does not infer permanent forgetting from lost delivery.
4. **Cheap exclusion competes within the same finances.** Setting j=0 selects identity-gated durable auditing; setting s=0 selects forced opening. The private verification product, accurate records and growing account can persist while patient protection disappears. For a given episode with full-gate price d(I_t), either insulation route strictly beats that protected route when its own fee is smaller than d(I_t); equality admits a tie. The uniform condition fee>D is sufficient over all episodes.
5. **Auditing can be unselected.** If a cheaper product has net receipt at least R−a−d(I_t), the commercial-margin proof no longer selects the durable caring route. In a regime with identical later opportunities, a strict reverse inequality makes that alternative more profitable. The cold self is allowed to anticipate learning's cost.
6. **Shared suppression defeats individual tests.** Change the shared authority disabling cost from 5 to 3 while retaining separate costs 3. Each individual cut still costs 3>2, but the shared cut saves 4 at price 3. Cost 4 gives a tie; cost 5 excludes both suppression and ties. A patient with learned q≤D can also be sacrificed under an appropriate cheaper partial-gate menu even if aggregate concern is large.
7. **A fixed good rule cannot overcome arbitrary wrong evidence.** On a two-node graph, a single falsely accepted edge admits the wrong transfer. No theorem about the paid game changes that. Conversely, adversarial omission of a true certificate can prevent coverage while all arithmetic conditions hold.
8. **No renewal from unaffordable prefixes.** At B=ell+a−1, the full task cannot begin even when it would have a large later surplus. Raising ell until R+o<ell+a+D destroys the attack-robust invariant; this does not imply that every such parameter set fails, only that the general guarantee is gone.
9. **Persistent capture has a different horizon.** In a separately specified permanent-capture extension at a stationary two-patient state, a one-time attack that completely and permanently removes enforcement while preserving trade gains 4/(1−delta)−chi_P. At delta=1/2 this is positive for chi_P<8, zero at 8 and negative above 8. Comparing a permanent attack only with one period's safeguard bill would be wrong.

These are failure mechanisms inside or explicitly extending the declared action repertoire. They do not establish the empirical prevalence of either regime.

## 7. What is now settled and what still needs evidence

The formal integration question has a constructive answer for this class: costly revision, selective retraction, retained grounds, actual strategic control and renewal can be jointly selected. Correct retraction and widening do not have to be inconsistent; alternative valid grounds make their compatibility explicit. The minimum per-patient action margin and joint suppression inequalities identify real integration conditions missing from an aggregate concern story.

The explanatory burden has moved to specific premises: which relevance relations are warranted; whether the audit technology tracks them; whether commercially or otherwise independently motivated acquisition beats cheaper insulation; which actors can destroy records or authority; and whether all relevant harms are represented. These are not solved by relabeling them as axioms. Some invite measurements and interventions; the ethical premises require argument as well as factual investigation. Universal moral improvement and unrestricted concept invention remain unproved.

A separately scoped [feature-construction refinement](FEATURE-CONSTRUCTION.md) builds a composite discriminating feature absent among the initial primitives, validates it on a complete finite domain, and charges its assembly, labels, evaluation and proof replay to this same audit bill. Its primitive grammar and descriptive relevance criterion remain supplied. It partially addresses representation construction without treating finite enumeration as unrestricted conceptual novelty.

The standard-library [checker](check_process.py) implements the complete finite game by backward induction, independently evaluates rooted proof supports and graph closure, checks selective retraction, enumerates joint authority cuts, records every selected payment prefix and patient outcome, and evaluates explicit failure cases. Finite enumeration corroborates the constructions; the general and infinite statements rely on the proofs above. No external empirical claim or priority claim is made.
