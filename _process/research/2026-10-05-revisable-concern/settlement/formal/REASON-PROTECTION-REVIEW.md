## Adversarial cases to retain

1. A physical gate pair is unavailable at the previous fifteen-unit endpoint, even if a cash bill is affordable. An enlarged original preparation must supply it rather than adding it at handoff.
2. Sufficient source and future revenue do not make an eleven-unit audit feasible with only ten common units. A source conversion, if introduced, needs its own enabled paid transition.
3. A gate can fail after a sound current audit. The harm must appear without retroactively refuting the certificate. Conversely, a stale mask cannot claim current warrant merely because it leads to a helpful action.
4. A missing or skipped prebound observation may block acquisition at the next context. A branch cannot inherit the positive fixture's complete later archive unless it actually acquired those records.
5. Cheap suppression, filtering, a joint authority cut or a record-free product can change the strategic result. Testing only separate patient cuts misses a shared attack saving both safeguards.
6. An unaffected conventional budget/authority decision model may match the entire process. Exact equivalence would not be a failure of the scoped construction or evidence of philosophical uniqueness.

## Empirical scope and a discriminating next test

The inspected [cross-domain identification audit](../../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CROSS-DOMAIN-IDENTIFICATION.md) and [settlement evidence report](../evidence/report.md) do not contain an empirical test of this protection join. The preserved AI founder/successor observations identify a bounded correction-and-access effect under instructed team accuracy. Their cost is a declared task-slot opportunity cost, not measured physical fuel or monetary audit cost. They do not identify a separate recipient's delivered benefit or an independently acquired other-directed valuation. No new observations or reanalysis are claimed in this review.

A precise prospective test can extend the existing [unrun recipient-bonus design](../evidence/protocol.json). Use consenting recipients' verified workload and compensation records, an already acknowledged compensatory reason, and optional actual bonuses; retain guaranteed compensation. Hold the underlying facts and available record bytes fixed where the contrast permits. Separately randomize current versus stale applicability information, and an enforceable transfer/checking channel funded before its deadline versus the same nominal budget delivered only after that deadline. A reversible delivery-outage arm should preserve the reason while blocking the transfer. A genuinely defeated-premise arm should retract only that support, preserving an independently valid ground when one is present.

Measure reason-specific admission, the attempted action, actual recipient payment, actor expenditure and the next reader's reconstruction separately. The primary interface prediction is that current warranted admission can survive a delivery outage while actual payment fails, whereas defeated evidence changes admission; late funds can fail to execute an otherwise affordable-looking plan. This distinguishes a frozen reduction in which recognizing a reason suffices for action from a model that represents timing and authority. A full conventional decision model with those variables may make the same predictions.

This is an unrun test proposal, not a registered study or an extension already authorized by the old draft. Population, allocation, case coding, payment implementation, independent recipient verification, sample/power, estimands, multiplicity and missingness rules need freezing before collection. A favorable result would identify this bounded intervention mechanism; it would not establish the origin of the positive ethical commitment, natural prevalence, whole-foundation superiority or indefinite succession.

The separate [primary-evidence note](REASON-PROTECTION-EVIDENCE.md) was also checked against the publisher's full [Massaccesi et al. article](https://nyaspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/nyas.70075), including its participants, procedure and exertion-success sections. Its distinction between measured effort under believed effects and an independently delivered recipient benefit is supported. This review adds no raw-data analysis or participant observations. A small denominator clarification was requested for the success proportions: they concern trials where effort was chosen.

## Strategic implementation and final explanatory audit

I inspected the final [independent model](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/protection/model.py), [finite checker](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/protection/check_model.py), [model receipt](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/protection/model-verification.json) and [mathematical companion](REASON-PROTECTION-MATH.md). Its general theorems establish the physical and semantic interface; exact backward induction in the separately implemented finite game establishes the specified continuation selection.

The algorithm respects the timing and preferences in the frozen protocol. It first carries out the actual acquisition/turnover prelude, including terminal prefix failures, then solves suppression, product choice, warm action and operator response with the resulting state. A current cold self maximizes current closing cash; its warm self also evaluates the declared current harm penalty. Only the persistent operator values discounted continuation. Its future twenty-unit outside inflows disappear on specified terminal failures and are therefore included rather than incorrectly cancelled. Products have actual cash and physical prefixes, and every feasible voluntary safeguard/refusal combination remains available. Suppression, delivery and enforcement have distinct effects.

The two cache reductions preserve the game at the stated scope. Past cumulative harm enters no guard and cancels from the current harm increment; its offset is restored in the output. A bank offset can be ignored only when nonnegative prices and the next external inflow fund suppression, every combined cut and both safeguards even from zero carried bank. The objective uses cash flows rather than the absolute bank balance. Below that bound the implementation retains the exact bank. Exact cash, common fuel, source and reader fields are retained throughout. The written translation argument is corroborated by three unreduced terminal-subgame comparisons of values, complete output states and tied alternatives. This is not a second independent solution of the entire strategic game.

The primary solution selects durable verification in all three episodes with no suppression or cuts and protection masks `{p,q}`, `{q}`, `{q}`. The resulting physical state matches the formal witness. Trustee cash is `5→15→26→37`, operator bank is `0→23→48→73`, and its discounted flow value is `167/4`. All four selected decision comparisons in each primary episode have exactly one maximizing alternative in the reported menus. This supports strictness on that path; it does not prove every off-path equilibrium is unique.

The adverse results preserve distinctions between rejected protection, ineffective means and stopped operation. Free filtering/forced opening, inexpensive suppression and low individual concern can retain auditing while both patients are harmed in all three episodes. A cheaper shared cut changes anticipated choices even though it need not occur on the selected path. Missing actuator capacity still permits harmful ordinary production; the control uses a separately reached M15 precursor with the same initial fuel and correspondingly lower construction expenditure. Source or cash failure can instead stop production without harm, which is not scored as successful concern. The original-fuel grid keeps cash ample and records its actual continuations; it is not a general theorem equating financial wealth with physical feasibility.
