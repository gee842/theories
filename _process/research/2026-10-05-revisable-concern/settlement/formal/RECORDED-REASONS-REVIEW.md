# Independent review of paid records, reason certificates and replay

6 October 2026. AI-authored adversarial interface review for the active connection **paid retained observations → context-sensitive reason certificates → rooted revision → charged successor replay**. This is a collaborating-AI review, independent of the new implementation and finite checker, not external peer review. The reviewer authored an earlier material-selector package and reviewed the later material-updater and material-predictor interfaces.

**Verdict: accepted at the finite paid certificate/replay interface.** I inspected the original protocol before numerical computation, reviewed the implementation as it developed, and checked the final source, formal and independent-model records. The new result replaces a direct current-audit input with one constructed, paid producer under explicit assay, context, anchor and relevance contracts. It does not derive those contracts, transfer the old settlement equilibrium, or complete the unified foundation. The design questions below remain as review provenance; the final resolution and frozen receipts follow them.

## Inspected predecessors and the actual gap

The [recording source](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/formal/RecordedEvidence.lean) proves that complete, paid recordings retain exactly the emitted outcome word, with material slots, escrow, pending states and energy accounted for. Its observed Boolean is an outcome of the declared teacher: `false` transfers two source units and `true` transfers none. Neither value intrinsically means that a reason-transfer edge is ethically or descriptively valid. Record fidelity cannot supply that semantic mapping by itself.

The existing [settlement R1](REPORT.md) defines rooted closure on a finite reason graph and gives a written proof of provenance and selective retraction. Its [checker](check_process.py) compares closure with independently enumerated rooted proofs. R1 is not an imported Lean theorem. A new Lean rooted-path or least-closure theorem would be a new formal result. The settlement currently supplies a complete sound audit of current edge-validity bits, valid positive anchors, the relevance relation and its context interpretation. The named task is to construct an instrumentally useful interface replacing part of the direct audit input, without claiming to derive those ethical grounds from cost or persistence.

## Conditions for accepting the new connection

| Interface | Required distinction or construction | Claim that would remain unsupported |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome to query | Bind each record position to a declared assay, target edge, context and polarity before its outcome. Preserve that binding through storage and replay. | Relabeling an arbitrary saved bit after seeing it as evidence for a desired edge. |
| Query to certificate | State the calibration and relevance conditions under which a retained observation warrants the particular edge, including how current context is observed or supplied. | Paying for a decoder makes it sound, commercial acceptance establishes ethical relevance, or a true receipt is automatically a true certificate. |
| Current interpretation | Distinguish certified positive, certified negative, unknown, stale and inaccessible evidence as appropriate to the stated protocol. | Missing evidence means the edge is false; a false edge means the target node, person or all other reasons are false. |
| Rooted replay | Start each recomputation from valid supplied roots and derive admissions through current positive certificates. Prove the path/closure relation under the actual replay schedule. | Starting from old non-root admissions lets a disconnected cycle retain itself; one successful path establishes complete replay. |
| Revision | Recompute after changed evidence or context, preserving every surviving independent rooted derivation. | Removing all descendants of one defeated edge, or retaining every old admission regardless of its current grounds. |
| Material and information | Retain actual slots, archive, pending record, metadata/workspace backing, precursor and spent components. Declare the finite decoder/replay recipe and capacity. | Free archive copying, unexplained scratch memory, slot reuse not permitted by the parent law, or fuel payment alone establishing constructed hardware. |
| Payments | Charge setup, context interpretation, certificate operations and replay at their actual prefixes from inherited usable stores. | Future commercial receipts or inaccessible source fund a currently unaffordable interpretation. |
| Successor | Specify what persists, what resets, how access is obtained and what reconstruction/replay costs are paid. Preserve the actual residual state. | A fresh successor arrives with installed reasons, unused components or a copied archive for free. |
| Access and action | Keep evidence/actuator access separate from validity. A delivery cut can prevent installed uptake or protection without refuting a retained reason. | Lack of effective access proves the certificate false or silently removes an affected center from the harm ledger. |
| Wider consequence | Identify the exact downstream contract supplied and any changed audit bill. | Automatically inheriting the old settlement equilibrium, protective policy, empirical identification or infinite renewal result. |

The metadata and functional architecture may be explicit finite supplied recipes. Acceptance does not require deriving chemistry without laws or deriving ethical justification from bare observation. It does require distinguishing those recipes from the actual formation, use, inherited evidence and paid replay that the new result claims to construct.

## Concrete adversarial cases

The actual recorder's two-record endpoint retains `[false,true]` with common fuel three, source fourteen and no blank slots. A four-unit interpretation cannot be funded by adding source and common fuel into one liquidity scalar. An otherwise comparable positive history must put the additional common unit into its actual initial state or provide a separately enabled conversion; it cannot reset the endpoint.

The old recording law has no overwrite or reclamation event. A later context's new observations therefore need unused formed slots or an explicitly extended material law. Changing a saved outcome in place while citing the unchanged recorder would break the composition. An immutable external plan can be a premise, but it does not prove physical metadata production or protected provenance.

For the existing three-node graph with edges `a→p`, `a→q`, `p→q`, start with all three positively certified. Defeating only `a→p` removes that particular rooted reason at `p` while `q` remains grounded through `a→q`. If only `a→p` and `p→q` were originally present, losing `a→p` removes both non-root admissions. A disconnected `p↔q` cycle never bootstraps positive support. These are reason-level results, not verdicts that either patient lacks moral standing.

A stale or missing observation should be distinguishable from a calibrated current negative observation, even if neither currently licenses an edge. An access-blocked successor can have a different installed set from an adequately funded, accessible successor while the same retained certificate remains warranted. Any theorem equating installed reasons with all true or warranted reasons needs additional coverage and access premises.

For actual contextual revision, acquire the first context's records and perform its audit, change the environment's context, acquire new observations in remaining slots, and perform the new paid audit. Preloading later-version bits may define a useful configured fixture, but it does not by itself establish acquisition after a context change. A fixed query layout can be compiled into the finite supplied decoder recipe; it is not learned metadata. Context sensing needs its own event or an explicit premise. The plant may depend on a hidden context while the reader acts only on its paid observation; direct access to the hidden validity table cannot be described as acquired interpretation.

Context change should retain the old installed state and its version until a new audit acts. Sound-current-admission claims must require matching context and completed replay, or account explicitly for the partial/stale state. The protocol must specify what happens if context changes after a query but before response, or during replay. A stable-episode premise is legitimate if explicit; changing a tag alone does not prove that premise.

Publication freshness also depends on the audited evidence. Appending a previously missing current-context record can enlarge available closure without changing the context tag. Exact current-closure claims must invalidate publication before such an append, or retain the audited prefix/snapshot in the claim. An older installed mask may remain sound while incomplete. The coordinating reviewer requested an explicit partial-audit → same-context append check; the proposed phase discipline can implement the required invalidation if current publication requires the completed phase.

Conflicting duplicate records need a declared rule. Unique planned slots per edge/context/assay avoid one ambiguity; a larger language needs a priority, freshness or conflict policy. Accepting any saved positive regardless of a later relevant negative cannot implement selective retraction merely by naming the new observation a defeater. Such policy and soundness conditions belong in the theorem interface rather than only in favorable fixtures.

## Concrete design now under review

The frozen protocol uses three nodes with root zero, three candidate edges, two contexts, and six fixed unique query slots. It interleaves acquisition of the first three bits, a paid audit, a context change, acquisition in the remaining slots, a new paid audit and a cold operational successor's replay. A capacity-fifteen preparation forms eight A and seven H, associates A and repairs; six A/H pairs form the actual record slots and the remaining pair forms a finite reader/workspace under a declared recipe. The positive record convention is explicitly calibrated separately from the old recorder's energy-return convention.

The eleven-unit audit charges activation, current-context sensing, three decodes and four path checks with lengths one, one, two and two. Those four simple paths cover the possible non-root destinations from root zero on a three-node graph; nonexistent graph edges fail the corresponding check. This supports a finite three-node coverage theorem, not automatically the general arbitrary-finite-graph R1 result. The original fuel eighty and source twenty yield the now-verified account `100 = 17 preparation + 6 slots + 1 reader + 12 recording + 33 audits + 31 remaining`, with final common thirteen and source eighteen.

The successor reuses the retained physical reader and archive, clears its operational learned state, and repays activation, sensing, decoding and replay. This can establish charged operational reconstruction from inherited records. It must not be described as manufacturing a fresh reader, renewing damaged hardware, or proving indefinite material maintenance. Calibration, the fixed query layout, the finite workspace capacity and the positive anchors remain supplied contracts.

The protocol explicitly supplies a reliable interlock that invalidates an active audit after context change, while retaining the old published mask and version. This resolves the design-level freshness concern by a declared additional law; it does not derive the interlock or context sensor from payment. Query and context guards strengthen the recorder's admitted histories. Their steps may project exactly to old recording steps without every arbitrary old recording history lifting back. A matching context version must identify an adequate stable relevance condition under the assay contract; reusing a label alone would not establish freshness in an application.

In source review, a general calibration invariant must cover pending latched data as well as committed records. A merely structurally valid configured latch could otherwise commit a wrong bit. Reached handoff or pending-calibration hypotheses must remain visible. Likewise, exact per-record-step projection does not justify deleting reader/audit operations from a complete history: their real fuel and component changes remain between recording segments. The whole wrapper history needs its own ledger.

## Final source and semantic resolution

I read the complete final [ReasonReplay source](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/formal/ReasonReplay.lean), [formal checker](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/formal/check.py), [formal receipt](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/formal/verification.json), [new compiler audit](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/formal/verification/ReasonReplay.txt), [independent finite checker](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/check_model.py) and [model receipt](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/model-verification.json). I also read the finalized [mathematical companion](RECORDED-REASONS-MATH.md), [canonical account](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECORDED-REASONS.md), [package README](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/README.md) and updated [reading guide](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/START-HERE.md). Their validation counts, semantic scopes and accounts agree. The reading guide explicitly keeps the remaining cross-package composition obligations open.

The central semantic theorem now actually joins the contracts. `paid_recorded_reason_sound` starts with an authentic inherited history and a completed paid audit. Authenticity includes the pending latch and is preserved by every admitted step. Decoding recovers the appropriate retained current-context bit; positive calibration gives the corresponding edge-specific fact; the independently supplied relevance rule transports a supplied root reason along the rooted path. The theorem does not merely re-assume that the final decoded graph is already semantically sound.

Negative calibration is separate. The first negative-decoding theorem identifies a false assay bit. `calibrated_negative_refutes_only_edge_fact` additionally requires that this negative output implies the relevant fact is false. Positive-only calibration cannot establish that by contraposition. Neither theorem concludes that the target's reason, the recipient, or all alternative grounds are false. This preserves the distinction from positive-only recipient qualification, where an outage zero remains unknown.

`rooted_iff_supported` and `closure_exact` establish three-node rooted reachability for arbitrary edge relations, including cycles and self-loops. The paid endpoint theorem connects that result to the actual decoded available evidence. It does not establish completeness for unavailable truths, unrestricted graph sizes or all ethically relevant cases. `Mask.has` includes the supplied logical root even for `emptyMask`; that mask has no non-root admissions. A cold operational successor's absence of installed reasons is represented by `installed = none`, not by treating `emptyMask` as a literally empty root-inclusive set. Paid activation and phase guards precede operational reconstruction.

The current-publication predicate requires the completed phase and matching context. Recording is blocked in that phase, and reopening withdraws completed status. The final proof concerns completed audit endpoints; it does not assert that an arbitrary forged state with phase ten and a matching tag is authentic. The supplemental reached-history check establishes why the tag alone fails after a same-context append. Its old mask remains sound but incomplete until another paid audit.

The original recorder's slots, archive, escrow and pending state remain actual components of the wrapper. Reader assembly removes one free A/H pair and counts those two units separately as reader backing. Combined validity checks the recorder and reader together; the new pair is not turned into unformed precursor or a spare record slot. General step/trace ledgers retain material and expenditure. The full staged witness begins with actual `FormedSelection` preparation, and all three audits inherit their true predecessor. The fixed-order audit is feasible exactly when a valid dormant, accessible state with reader and idle writer has at least eleven common units. Source is not usable audit liquidity.

Recording is strengthened by prebound query/context/assay guards. Its admitted recording steps project to the unchanged parent steps, but arbitrary old observation words need not lift. Reader operations genuinely change the account between recording segments. The successor reuses the same physical reader and archive, pays eleven again and retains the resulting shortage: one further replay is affordable, but the next successor cannot complete another with only two common units despite eighteen source units.

## Independent checking and preserved amendment

The independent checker separately implements the physical equations and compares the four-path reader with a work-list closure oracle that the physical process never reads. It passes 1,728 ordered graph/certificate cases on the non-self candidate-edge grid, 23,296 configured audit cases, fourteen actual lower-endowment preparation histories and sixteen original boundaries. Of the configured audits, 1,792 complete, including 1,152 with an unknown current certificate. Those configured states are not all claimed to have actual preparation histories. The Lean rooted theorem has the broader self-loop scope; the finite graph grid is correctly labeled as a subset.

The actual liquidity histories use original common fuel thirty through forty-three, construct the same apparatus and acquire `111`. Their first audit completes exactly at original common forty-one or above; no endpoint is replenished. The main history records the two contexts in order, preserves the defeated route and surviving independent ground, and reconstructs the latter for a cold working successor. Every intermediate physical step is checked. These are deterministic conditional calculations, not probabilities or empirical observations.

The publication-freshness diagnostic was requested after the original grid had run. Its [original protocol, freeze receipt and first-run receipt](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/development/publication-freshness-review/README.md) are preserved, and the amended freeze explicitly discloses that sequence. One supplemental check is counted separately from the sixteen original boundaries; no event law changed. It shows that the same-context append enlarges available closure and that another eleven-unit audit is required. These preserved receipts are provenance, not additional independent evidence.

Visible metadata mismatch is an auxiliary diagnostic outside the immutable positive query layout. Corrupting trusted bindings or bits can mislead this reader when calibration/authenticity fails. The package does not claim universal forgery detection. Likewise, cuts to access or delivery do not rewrite the archive or refute a proposition: an early access cut prevents sensing/decoding, while a later cut can leave already cached replay possible. The delivery flag records a distinct interface condition; no patient-protection actuator is constructed here.
