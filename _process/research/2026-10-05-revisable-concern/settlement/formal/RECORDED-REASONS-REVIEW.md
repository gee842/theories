This is a collaborating-AI review, independent of the new implementation and finite checker, not external peer review. The reviewer authored an earlier material-selector package and reviewed the later material-updater and material-predictor interfaces.

**Status: design review in progress; no new implementation accepted yet.** The planned code location is a `reason-replay/` package within [the existing recording study](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/). This review will record the final source and verification receipts after they exist. Its present purpose is to expose the assumptions before they become hidden in an apparently complete result.

## Inspected predecessors and the actual gap

The [recording source](../../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/formal/RecordedEvidence.lean) proves that complete, paid recordings retain exactly the emitted outcome word, with material slots, escrow, pending states and energy accounted for. Its observed Boolean is an outcome of the declared teacher: `false` transfers two source units and `true` transfers none. Neither value intrinsically means that a reason-transfer edge is ethically or descriptively valid. Record fidelity cannot supply that semantic mapping by itself.

The existing [settlement R1](REPORT.md) defines rooted closure on a finite reason graph and gives a written proof of provenance and selective retraction. Its [checker](check_process.py) compares closure with independently enumerated rooted proofs. R1 is not an imported Lean theorem. A new Lean rooted-path or least-closure theorem would be a new formal result. The settlement currently supplies a complete sound audit of current edge-validity bits, valid positive anchors, the relevance relation and its context interpretation. The named task is to construct an instrumentally useful interface replacing part of the direct audit input, without claiming to derive those ethical grounds from cost or persistence.

## Conditions for accepting the new connection

| Interface | Required distinction or construction | Claim that would remain unsupported |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome to query | Bind each record position to a declared assay, target edge, context and polarity before its outcome. Preserve that binding through storage and replay. | Relabeling an arbitrary saved bit after seeing it as evidence for a desired edge. |
| Query to certificate | State the calibration and relevance conditions under which a retained observation warrants the particular edge, including how current context is observed or supplied. | Paying for a decoder makes it sound, commercial acceptance establishes ethical relevance, or a true receipt is automatically a true certificate. |
| Current interpretation | Distinguish certified positive, certified negative, unknown, stale and inaccessible evidence as appropriate to the stated protocol. | Missing evidence means the edge is false; a false edge means the target node, person or all other reasons are false. |
| Rooted replay | Start each recomputation from valid supplied roots and derive admissions through current positive certificates. Prove the path/closure relation under the actual replay schedule. | Starting from old non-root admissions lets a disconnected cycle retain itself; one successful path establishes complete replay. |
| Revision | Recompute after changed evidence or context, preserving every surviving independent rooted derivation. | Removing all descendants of one defeated edge, or retaining every old admission regardless of its current grounds. |
| Material and information | Retain actual slots, archive, pending record, metadata/workspace backing, precursor and spent components. Declare the finite decoder/replay recipe and capacity. | Free archive copying, unexplained scratch memory, slot reuse not permitted by the parent law, or fuel payment alone establishing constructed hardware. |
| Payments | Charge setup, context interpretation, certificate operations and replay at their actual prefixes from inherited usable stores. | Future commercial receipts or inaccessible source fund a currently unaffordable interpretation. |
| Successor | Specify what persists, what resets, how access is obtained and what reconstruction/replay costs are paid. Preserve the actual residual state. | A fresh successor arrives with installed reasons, unused components or a copied archive for free. |
| Access and action | Keep evidence/actuator access separate from validity. A delivery cut can prevent installed uptake or protection without refuting a retained reason. | Lack of effective access proves the certificate false or silently removes an affected center from the harm ledger. |
| Wider consequence | Identify the exact downstream contract supplied and any changed audit bill. | Automatically inheriting the old settlement equilibrium, protective policy, empirical identification or infinite renewal result. |

The metadata and functional architecture may be explicit finite supplied recipes. Acceptance does not require deriving chemistry without laws or deriving ethical justification from bare observation. It does require distinguishing those recipes from the actual formation, use, inherited evidence and paid replay that the new result claims to construct.

## Concrete adversarial cases

The actual recorder's two-record endpoint retains `[false,true]` with common fuel three, source fourteen and no blank slots. A four-unit interpretation cannot be funded by adding source and common fuel into one liquidity scalar. An otherwise comparable positive history must put the additional common unit into its actual initial state or provide a separately enabled conversion; it cannot reset the endpoint.

The old recording law has no overwrite or reclamation event. A later context's new observations therefore need unused formed slots or an explicitly extended material law. Changing a saved outcome in place while citing the unchanged recorder would break the composition. An immutable external plan can be a premise, but it does not prove physical metadata production or protected provenance.

For the existing three-node graph with edges `a→p`, `a→q`, `p→q`, start with all three positively certified. Defeating only `a→p` removes that particular rooted reason at `p` while `q` remains grounded through `a→q`. If only `a→p` and `p→q` were originally present, losing `a→p` removes both non-root admissions. A disconnected `p↔q` cycle never bootstraps positive support. These are reason-level results, not verdicts that either patient lacks moral standing.

A stale or missing observation should be distinguishable from a calibrated current negative observation, even if neither currently licenses an edge. An access-blocked successor can have a different installed set from an adequately funded, accessible successor while the same retained certificate remains warranted. Any theorem equating installed reasons with all true or warranted reasons needs additional coverage and access premises.

For actual contextual revision, acquire the first context's records and perform its audit, change the environment's context, acquire new observations in remaining slots, and perform the new paid audit. Preloading later-version bits may define a useful configured fixture, but it does not by itself establish acquisition after a context change. A fixed query layout can be compiled into the finite supplied decoder recipe; it is not learned metadata. Context sensing needs its own event or an explicit premise. The plant may depend on a hidden context while the reader acts only on its paid observation; direct access to the hidden validity table cannot be described as acquired interpretation.

Context change should retain the old installed state and its version until a new audit acts. Sound-current-admission claims must require matching context and completed replay, or account explicitly for the partial/stale state. The protocol must specify what happens if context changes after a query but before response, or during replay. A stable-episode premise is legitimate if explicit; changing a tag alone does not prove that premise.

Conflicting duplicate records need a declared rule. Unique planned slots per edge/context/assay avoid one ambiguity; a larger language needs a priority, freshness or conflict policy. Accepting any saved positive regardless of a later relevant negative cannot implement selective retraction merely by naming the new observation a defeater. Such policy and soundness conditions belong in the theorem interface rather than only in favorable fixtures.

## Concrete design now under review

The mechanism author proposes three nodes with root zero, three candidate edges, two contexts, and six fixed unique query slots. It interleaves acquisition of the first three bits, a paid audit, a context change, acquisition in the remaining slots, a new paid audit and a cold operational successor's replay. A capacity-fifteen preparation would form eight A and seven H, associate A and repair; six A/H pairs form the actual record slots and the remaining pair forms a finite reader/workspace under a declared recipe.

The proposed eleven-unit audit charges activation, current-context sensing, three decodes and four path checks with lengths one, one, two and two. Those four simple paths cover the possible non-root destinations from root zero on a three-node graph; nonexistent graph edges must fail the corresponding check. This supports a finite three-node coverage theorem, not automatically the general arbitrary-finite-graph R1 result. Exact final costs and states are pending source verification.

The proposed successor reuses the retained physical reader and archive, clears its operational learned state, and repays activation, sensing, decoding and replay. This can establish charged operational reconstruction from inherited records. It must not be described as manufacturing a fresh reader, renewing damaged hardware, or proving indefinite material maintenance. Calibration, the fixed query layout, the finite workspace capacity and the positive anchors remain supplied contracts.
