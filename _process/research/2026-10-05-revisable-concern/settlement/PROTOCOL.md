## Acceptance conditions

1. A positive formal result must derive the chosen protective process under its actual incentives and cash timing. Independent components that cannot coexist do not count.
2. Revision must preserve sound retained grounds through blocked access, retract only defeated support, and retain independent alternative derivations. Generalization within a supplied language must remain distinct from unrestricted invention of new concepts.
3. An ethical conclusion must show its positive grounds and what follows from them. Merely invoking consistency cannot exclude universal indifference. Persistence, productive usefulness and controllability do not by themselves create moral standing.
4. Empirical prevalence and superiority require discriminating observations. An authored toy model, internal AI review or benchmark protocol is not a new empirical validation.
5. Unconditional guarantees and statistical tendencies have different quantifiers. A counterexample in the stated class defeats a guarantee; prevalence in actual nature requires an independently specified population and evidence.
6. A full account of subjective experience is not supplied by assigning a valence number to an agent. Any claim about experience must state what has actually been explained and what remains unidentified.
7. Final status must distinguish proved conditional claims, reasoned normative conclusions, refuted formulations, evidence-supported claims and open obligations. Remaining obligations cannot be marked complete merely because this research round ends.

## Review and preservation

The three research agents write in disjoint `formal/`, `normative/` and `evidence/` subdirectories. The coordinating agent reviews their assumptions and integrates the result. Completed branches are cross-reviewed before delivery. New substantive work is saved in the repository, with links to primary sources and existing results. Only this team's files and deliberate index additions are committed.
