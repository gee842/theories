# Ethical settlement claims and assumptions

5 October 2026. Claim register for [the foundation](FOUNDATION.md). Arguments, mathematical consequences, evidence, and incomplete entailments have separate statuses.

| Claim | Status | Grounds and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Severe unwanted harm supplies a pro tanto reason in shared deliberation. | Positively defended ethical commitment. | Grounded in the loss to its subject; denial owes an answer to that loss. This is the substantive normative bridge, not a deduction from persistence or self-interest. |
| Bare identity does not cancel an otherwise unchanged reason. | Defended consistency commitment. | Preserves relevant relationships and competing claims; does not itself imply positive concern. |
| Agency and informed refusal provide independent reasons. | Separately defended commitment. | Practical authorship is not exhausted by comfort. Exceptions need justification; suffering alone does not entail this premise. |
| Consequential power owes effective answerability. | Defended commitment plus implementation consequences. | Hearing, relevant response, usable challenge and correction where promised; no unlimited veto. |
| An actor can reject a justified reason. | Conceptual distinction. | Motivational possibility and universal persuasion are distinct from the reason's justification. A consistent egoist's denial contests the substantive bridge, rather than generating a mathematical contradiction. |
| Usefulness, labels, silence and scarcity cannot by themselves erase admitted claims. | Conditional ethical conclusions. | Requires accurate case descriptions and the defended kernel. Additional relevant reasons can change action without erasing standing. |
| Robust dominance leaves a nonempty set with final witnesses. | Proved finite result N1. | Finite nonempty actions and models; reflexive transitive model relations. Does not validate model selection or rank every pair. |
| Costless protection can dominate despite uncertain patienthood. | Proved finite result N2. | No worse in every warranted retained model, better in at least one. Genuine competing burdens can defeat the premise. |
| The remainder respects identity renaming. | Proved result N3. | The entire relevant structure, relationships and model set are transported; invariant context needs closed model set. |
| Fully symmetric scarce allocation has no deterministic invariant selector and a unique invariant lottery. | Proved result N4. | Finite k-of-n allocation, full symmetry, feasible and ethically permissible lottery. Equal chances do not settle asymmetric or incomparable conflicts. |
| Observationally identical states with disjoint protection sets defeat a uniform guarantee. | Proved result N5. | The specified observation domain and exact indistinguishability; no claim that actual consciousness evidence always has this structure. |
| Sentience evidence can be explicit, plural and uncertain without being empty. | Scoped primary-source support. | Birch et al. 2021 report and Schnell et al. 2026 assessment, linked in the chapter. No automatic detector; no derivation of subjective experience. |
| Nonexperiencing nature has direct value. | Not entailed by this kernel. | Kernel admits extensions with and without that direct value; additional grounds can be argued. Duties concerning nature through patients or relationships can already exist. |
| Every unresolved conflict has a unique justified ranking. | Not established and not required by finite result. | Particular cases may be settled with additional grounds; universal ranking needs a stronger theory. Incompleteness is not equal endorsement of every action. |
| The proposed seven-step procedure produces ethically correct institutions. | Not established. | Procedure is specified and finite comparison checked; application depends on evidence, legitimate authority, represented reasons, feasibility, execution and review. |
