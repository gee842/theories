## What has actually been implemented

The code checks fixed finite graph closure, numerical uptake, deadlines, edge additions, leakage and retention witnesses, deletion attacks, all optimal exclusion outcomes, controller liquidity, and supplied reinforcement options. Independent programs reconstruct Boolean closure from satisfying assignments, uptake from explicit walk paths, and selected access calculations through a different method.

The broader challenge protocol also discusses relevant disanalogy, revision of the ethical anchor, direct admission of a new kind of reason, recipient agency, and plural conflict. Those are not all implemented in one general deliberative machine. The six-case experimental design in the conceptual memo remains a proposed next test; it was not run as a human, institutional, or AI behavioral experiment here.

## What the model cannot justify by itself

A graph can implement an unjust map perfectly. A self-interested controller can choose valid reasons strategically. A coherent rule can be ethically wrong. A connected channel can be too slow. A durable record can preserve a false conclusion. No single measure of connectivity, consistency, retained value, or survival certifies wisdom.

The result supplies a more explicit account of revision and its enabling conditions. It leaves the truth and adequacy of ethical premises answerable to philosophical argument, affected experience, empirical evidence, and further criticism.
