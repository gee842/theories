- **Blocked transfer:** the first failure retains complement and absent arrival; the second zero identifies absent arrival within the admitted family. The controller does not infer a unique inversion from one failure. Both packets can be lost while diagnosis is correct.
- **Missing receipt:** the attempt and waste occur, but absence of a receipt does not filter candidates as if zero had been observed. Final retention remains incomplete.
- **Misleading receipt:** the primary physical failure is reported as a positive arrival. The controller retains identity and stops despite receiving no fuel and wasting a packet. Completed interpretation is therefore not an oracle for actual success.
- **Interrupted updating or retention:** already incurred effects remain; an acquired-but-uncommitted observation is not a completed learned update, and a completed update is not the final retention milestone.
- **Sensor, reset and selected outside-family changes:** the supplied examples produce a sensory mismatch and stop before extraction. These controls do not establish detection of every outside-family law or later change. Finite agreement can still fail to exclude untested alternatives.

With only one available packet, the first failure is acquired and the changed question selected, but the next extraction cannot occur. With no available packet, even the first extraction fails. Inert reserves do not repair these failures.

It identifies prospective use through this interface, not a unique microscopic cause.
