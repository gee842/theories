## The positive connection

A difference can matter to an organization before it is represented as valuable. If an activity requires an accessible support, loss of that support can interrupt the activity; actual provision can enable its continuation. The difference resides in the consequential relationship. An evaluator can discover, misrepresent or ignore it. The evaluator's approval does not create the dependency.

This is the first useful sense in which significance can arise within generation. It identifies an asymmetry between changes that enable or obstruct a specified organization. To become operationally appreciated, that difference must also participate in the organization's responses. To become learned, its encountered consequences must change subsequent response. Those are differentiated connections to establish within the same history, rather than separate worlds of neutral facts and added meanings.

The health question becomes stronger when several beings are involved. An actor can continue while an affected recipient loses an indispensable activity. An adequate account of shared health must preserve that distinction. It cannot substitute the actor's performance or an aggregate resource total for what actually happens to the recipient. Whether the affected activity is good, whether intervention is permissible, and which competing consequence should take priority remain substantive questions; the causal asymmetry alone does not decide them.

## An exact correspondence from delivered support to executable continuation

Fix a recipient at a stated point in a common history. Identify a family of candidate continuing activities before asking which are affordable. Specify their participants, transitions, duration, component needs, permissions and completion conditions independently of the proposed grant or the analyst's favorable judgment. A candidate must not mean an activity already affordable at the initial balance.

For each candidate path γ, let its ordered resource increments be δ₁,…,δₘ, and let f be the floor its account must retain. Debits occur before the returns they enable. Define its reserve threshold using the [existing prefix-liquidity theorem](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECIPIENT-RENEWAL-MATH.md#1-a-general-theorem-about-future-action-and-actual-resources):

    Lγ = max(0, −δ₁, −(δ₁+δ₂), …, −(δ₁+…+δₘ))
    Tγ = f + Lγ

Let B be the actual recipient balance at the comparison point. All other required accounts must also pass their own prefix checks. A delivered grant d ≥ 0 arrives before the candidate begins and is accessible to its operations. For this comparison, require the candidate's increments and nonliquidity guards to remain the same when the opening balance changes from B to B+d. Keeping a law's name unchanged is insufficient if that law introduces a balance-dependent fee, liability, action or restriction.

**Corollary of the existing theorem.** Under these conditions, γ becomes newly affordable exactly when

    B < Tγ ≤ B+d.

Every previously affordable candidate remains affordable. If C(B) denotes the candidates passing all these fixed conditions at B, then C(B) is a subset of C(B+d). The inclusion is strict precisely when at least one candidate has its threshold in that interval.

**Derivation.** The existing theorem gives affordability exactly when the opening balance is at least Tγ. Newly affordable means that this comparison fails at B and holds at B+d. The nonnegative shift preserves every previously satisfied threshold, while the stipulated remaining guards are unchanged. No new resource theorem is claimed.

This is an **operational enabling effect**. It is stronger than merely observing a larger number: it specifies which independently identified activity is now resource-feasible and why. It remains weaker than actual execution, sustained renewal or welfare improvement. A funded path may never be selected or initiated in practice; the candidate's stated permissions must not be confused with a mechanism that exercises them. A painful or coerced activity can also be newly affordable.

The grant belongs to the larger history. Its source, delivery, debits and any restrictions must be accounted for there. This local comparison begins after delivery; it does not put provision outside generative reality or cancel costs to another position. Where the extra stock changes other accounts, access, behavior or the environment, the simple inclusion claim is unavailable and the actual joint traces must be compared instead.

### A small constructed illustration

Suppose an independently specified activity has increments −3, +2, −1 and requires floor 1. Its largest prefix deficit is 3, so its entry threshold is 4. At balance 3, the first operation is unavailable. A delivered, unrestricted unit permits the path; its balances become 1, 3 and 2. The path is enabled although its net resource change is negative. No claim of indefinite renewal follows.

At balance 5 the same path was already affordable. Another unit creates no new affordability for this one path. That does not establish no value: other activities or disturbances might make the reserve consequential. Conversely, if an indispensable component is absent or a deadline has already passed, increasing the balance need not enable the path at all. The illustration fixes its path and floor in advance; it is an algebraic witness, not a model of an observed organism or a new empirical result.

## From an account to an affected being

Applying the corollary requires more than choosing a threshold that makes an existing result look important. For the recipient represented in a model, establish:

1. **Continuity:** what ongoing activity or capability belongs to this affected being, and what would actually be interrupted? The referent must persist through the observed debit, receipt and later use. Maintaining the responder's learning programme does not automatically identify the recipient's own continuation.
2. **Dependence:** which support, component, access condition or relationship enables that activity? Derive or independently identify the relevant floor and costs. Do not select them after seeing which balance the favored intervention reaches.
3. **Delivery and use:** did the consequence reach the same recipient in usable form, early enough, with the required access? A credit assigned by the investigator is insufficient. Who can initiate, redirect or refuse the path belongs in its actual conditions.
4. **Consequence:** which recipient continuation becomes executable, is performed, or is obstructed? Keep these claims separate. Retain burdens during the activity as well as capacities remaining afterward.

In the current plasticity history, 34 is a modeled resource balance. This note does not manufacture a recipient need with threshold 35 in order to declare deprivation. The needed recipient activity and its dependency must be independently established. In the renewal history, the financed probe/copy sequence supplies an actual resource-dependent continuation, but its floor and source requirements remain supplied locally. Neither example yet constitutes the full recipient-side correspondence proposed here.

The [empirical identification work](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md) adds another reason to preserve the target. Changes in behavior can track recipient consequences, the actor's own distress, learned cues or an existing preference's newly learned means. Distinguishing genuine alleviation from cue suppression requires identifying what changes for the recipient. Altering understanding can itself change experience; a cognitive intervention is not automatically mere concealment. These are different hypotheses about one consequential history.

## What this changes in the core and its lenses

The proposed common relationship is now more explicit: **ongoing activity depends on conditions that activity can preserve, consume, replace or release; some organizations acquire ways for the resulting differences to revise what they do.** This explains a family of generative and destructive regimes. It is not a claim that every healthy thing learns or continually changes. Dormancy, durable provision and fixed adequate response retain their place. Operational continuation can require revision where the existing arrangement would fail under the circumstances claimed. Warranted revision can also concern a continuing practice's existing effects, even while that practice remains productive.

The lenses preserve different relations within this configuration:

| Lens | Relationship it must preserve for this claim |
|---|---|
| Maintenance | Actual provision and loss relative to the activity sustained |
| Learning | Encountered consequence, acquired distinction and its causal use |
| Succession | The support, capabilities and acquired distinctions actually received by a successor |
| Concern | The affected being's condition and what an available response would do for that being |
| Wisdom | How evidence about these relations can correct a response, its criterion or its practice without erasing affected histories |

The last two include justificatory claims still under investigation. Being informed, mutable or long-lived does not establish them. A regime can restore productive capacity while imposing severe unwanted experience; its productive future does not describe all that occurs in the continuation. The [constitutive significance candidate](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md) addresses the experienced condition and the further actor-addressed favor separately, within the same unfolding. This resource argument neither proves nor refutes that candidate.

Universality can concern a faithful account of these relations across situations. It need not mean that every arrangement has one permanent rank. A complete ethical ordering also does not follow from inclusion between selected continuation sets. The stronger proposal for shared health is that renewal can become answerable to the differentiated lives it affects. Here, answerability calls for identifiable consequences, effective ways to contest and correct misdescription, and warranted treatment of burdens and refusals. Those requirements need defense and realization; putting the word into a definition supplies neither.

## Primary comparisons and the next bridge

Di Paolo's account of adaptivity distinguishes continued self-production from regulation sensitive to its changing conditions. It supports examining how consequence becomes operative rather than inferring appreciation from persistence. The account is conceptual and does not derive universal concern. See [Di Paolo 2005, pp. 438–442](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf), already part of this branch's [prior research](../RESEARCH.md).

Barandiaran and Egbert derive a viability-related field in a metabolic model, then explicitly distinguish that assessment from a mechanism sensitive to the metabolism's changing condition. Their controller can behave adaptively without that causal sensitivity. This gives a substantive comparator for our separation of actual dependence, successful response and acquired regulation. See [their 2014 paper, §§6.1–6.2, pp. 23–25](https://xabier.barandiaran.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/barandiaran_and_egbert_-_2014_-_norm-establishing_and_norm-following_-_alj_pub.pdf). These sources support the organizational question; they do not establish the ethical extension.

The next shared bridge is **an identified change in affected continuation → acquired distinction → warranted revision of the operative practice or criterion**. This round supplies the consequence-side correspondence conditions and a limited exact implication. The [practical-boundary study](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-INTEGRATION.md) owns the adjacent criterion/practice-revision argument. The account would need revision if the asserted dependency failed, delivery did not enable the claimed continuation, a claimed learned distinction had no causal use under its stated conditions, or the health assessment omitted a burden it promised to preserve. These are failures of particular claims, not an automatic refutation of generation as a whole.
