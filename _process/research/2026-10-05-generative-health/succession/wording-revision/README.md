## The explanatory correction

The foundation begins with activity forming and changing the conditions of further activity. Its effects can continue and participate in what follows. Particular organizations, roles, records and capacities enter through the relations that establish them. “Activity” does not presuppose intention; “conditions” does not presuppose a transferable stock. Generative activity can have constructive or destructive consequences.

The actual prose now distinguishes these levels:

- General explanation uses activity, differentiation, relations, consequences, formation, transformation and continuation.
- A local mechanism identifies the quantities and operations it actually represents. Record updating and fuel consumption are described separately. Depletion, replenishment and restoration replace monetary metaphors where no money is modeled.
- Ethical descriptions retain actual experience, agency, consent and warranted concern where these are the subject. A modeled stock is not renamed life or welfare.
- Genuine economic arrangements retain money, payment and liability. Exact identifiers, quoted source wording, numerical results and meaningful conditions remain traceable.

English and Chinese formulations are AI-authored and labeled as such in the [grounding account](../../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) and [shared method](../../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md#keep-foundational-language-at-the-right-level). Process, theory, mathematical model and an internally formed model have distinct explanatory roles.
