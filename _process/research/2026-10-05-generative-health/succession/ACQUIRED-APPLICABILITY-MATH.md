For \(\tau=\sigma\), the sensor remains \(H(q)\). Keeping the same transition and reset law therefore preserves the sensor-language correspondence for every finite word. Changing both bits from 0 to 1 at an explicitly declared boundary changes readiness while preserving that correspondence. The boundary transformation is supplied by the local construction; its occurrence and origin are not inferred from a failure receipt.

Correcting its application is genuine learning, but it is not evidence of a physical dependency changing during that earlier history. ## 2. Learn a use relation without a universal reward

For this finite representation only, let \(b\in\{0,1\}\) be the reading predicted by the retained machine for an executed word, and let \(y\) be the acquired arrival receipt. Use the supplied family

\[
f_{\mathrm{same}}(b)=b,\qquad
f_{\mathrm{opposite}}(b)=1-b,\qquad
f_{\mathrm{absent}}(b)=0.
\]

These are prospective relations between a learned reading and an operation's outcome, not amounts of value. The last relation identifies no arrival at either reading within this interface; a physical diagnosis of its cause requires the admitted apparatus law. The source's two blocked parity configurations can share this prospective use description without establishing that their complete histories are identical.

Given a current family \(F\), an actually acquired pair \((b,y)\) updates it to

\[
F'=\{f\in F:f(b)=y\}.
\]

This familiar filtering rule is supplied. The new implementation claim concerns its actual input, retention and effect on subsequent inquiry in the existing learner's history, rather than invention of a learning algorithm or origin of the family.

**Conditional retention of the actual relation.** If the actual use relation belongs to \(F\), remains stationary during the episode, the predicted reading class corresponds to the enacted phase, and the receipt faithfully reports the actual arrival, then the actual relation belongs to \(F'\). The proof is substitution of the actual observed outcome into the membership condition. Induction preserves this property across completed updates. An unavailable observation or interrupted write is not a completed update. A false receipt, incorrect phase correspondence or relation outside the family defeats the stated premises rather than supplying an undisclosed truth oracle.

## 3. The first consequence changes the next question

The inherited first use aims at reading 1. A faithful arrival gives \(F'=\{f_{\mathrm{same}}\}\); the concrete one-capture undertaking is fulfilled and further applicability inquiry can stop. This is a local completion condition. It does not establish that every wider use has been learned or that the source should be exhausted.

A faithful nonarrival instead gives

\[
F'=\{f_{\mathrm{opposite}},f_{\mathrm{absent}}\}.
\]

Both retained alternatives predict another nonarrival at reading 1. At reading 0, their predictions differ. Thus a further encounter at reading 0 can distinguish the live alternatives and can itself transfer the remaining packet in the open, complemented case. This is a change in which experiment is selected through acquired consequences. No change to \(H\), to the original sensor-language contract, or to the actual arrival law is made by the learner's update.

Two acquired nonarrivals leave only \(f_{\mathrm{absent}}\). These follow by evaluating the three displayed functions.
