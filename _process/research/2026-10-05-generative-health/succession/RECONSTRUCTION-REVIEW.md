# Constructive succession within one generative history

6 October 2026. AI-authored conceptual reconstruction and scope review responding to Ren's objection to the inference from a ranking reversal to a limit on universal evaluation. This adds no experiment, rerun, controller, theorem or physical realization. The existing results keep their declared scopes.

The governing method is the [project-wide nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). This review credits the [existing nondual core](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) and [acquired-perspective reconstruction](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-TREE-MECHANISM.md); neither is being introduced here. The named connection is **organizational dependency → operational significance and regulation**, approached backward from this branch's supplied target, search and support premises.

## The corrected inference

The access experiment preserves an inherited package and opening balance, then changes whether extra inherited functions enter a particular enumeration procedure. Some specified next functions are solved only with active extras; others only with dormant extras. Consequently, a fixed ordering of these two procedures cannot agree with their probability of success under every distribution over the supplied next functions.

That is a result about a declared projection: next-task success for these procedures and costs. It does not establish that a universal generative criterion is impossible, that every evaluation must originate outside the process, or that circumstances and significance are fundamentally separate. Nor does replacing the distribution with a purportedly universal scalar explain what makes a function matter. The missing relationships must be reconstructed and tested on their own content.

## What one existing sequence actually connects

The [model](../../../../experiments/constructive-succession/model.py) and [protocol](../../../../experiments/constructive-succession/PROTOCOL.md) preserve this chronology:

1. A successor receives the actual serialized source, paid cached truth tables and balance left by its predecessor. Earlier private search state is absent. The inherited source is inspectable and contains reusable dependencies.
2. A stipulated work flow is added. Sixteen units are charged for base service and eight for the complete next target table. Uncached inherited functions can be reevaluated for a further charge.
3. The active repertoire shapes the actual candidate order. Each paid trial computes a NAND composition; acquired function tables affect which later compositions are available. The current target affects stopping, attempted installation and some archive rankings.
4. A changed installed program requires separate exhaustive verification, installation and affordable handoff. Failure to solve the new target preserves the old installed program. A found answer can remain unaffordable to install.
5. Handoff copies selected dependency-closed source and tables, charges the same continuing balance, discards untransmitted state and produces the next successor's actual starting package.

Construction, record, access, action and inheritance are therefore interdependent aspects of this computational history. The closing balance is the opening balance plus the given flow, less service, target access, recomputation, trials, validation, installation and transfer. A change in search can change both inherited content and remaining resources. No later stage receives a reset balance or predecessor knowledge that was not transferred.

These are computational source structures under a declared cost scheme. Calling the source “material” does not establish physical component formation, measured energy use or reproduction of the search machinery. The distinction between constructor and constructed program is a functional distinction in this execution; the experiment does not derive an experiencing subject/object division. Likewise, analytical comparison belongs to the actual research practice, while its development and resources lie outside this local simulation's representation, not outside generative reality.

## Which significance is constituted, and which remains supplied

| Relationship | Established within this execution | Local input or remaining explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Source and computed function | Each generated node has its function under the same supplied NAND semantics; paid cached records preserve earlier computations. | The primitive semantics, interpreter competence and reliability of computation are given. This is not discovery from uncertain consequences. |
| Archive and future access | Actually transferred source and records determine what this successor can retrieve and which combinations enter search. | The access and inheritance policies are imposed across arms, not acquired or revised within a lineage. |
| Target and regulation | Exact target comparison really guides search stopping and installation. It is not solely a label assigned after the run. | Why this truth table is a need, service or worthwhile end is not derived. Paying eight units reveals its whole specification. |
| Expense and continuation | Search, installation and transfer consume the balance used by subsequent activity; resource dependence is real within the model. | The work flow is supplied independently of task success. A flat service charge does not represent a measured service consequence. |
| Published success and health | The success count faithfully reports satisfaction of those specified tables under the algorithm and budget. | Satisfaction is not shown to restore an independently specified organizational dependency, benefit a recipient or justify the goal. |

Solving a task can conserve resources by stopping search and can change installation and transfer costs. It would therefore be wrong to say success has no effect on continuation. The precise omission is that **the output's service consequence is not what generates the incoming flow or demonstrates fulfillment of a need**. The model represents paid task pursuit and its resource consequences; it does not derive the operational significance of that task from a maintained organization's actual dependencies.

This distinction matters for the full account. The existing [acquired-perspective history](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-TREE-MECHANISM.md#2-三个方面来自同一次活动的共同方程) already connects an actual work receipt to resource change, retained action reference and later paid writing. A subsequent corrected command can obtain support for the same organization. That provides an established conditional example of the relationship this circuit experiment omits. It does not physically compose that learner with these circuit successors, derive the Boolean task distribution, or justify arbitrary targets. The two constructions must not be presented as one already joined history.

## Substantive rivals and revision conditions

**Enumeration and budgeting account.** A conventional finite synthesis account preserves the same package, target specification, code semantics, costs and outcomes. It explains the matched-access result through queue order, duplicate checks, affordable search and installation. This is a sufficient local mechanism, compatible with the generative reconstruction. If the broader language predicts no additional difference at this scope, the experiment cannot establish its unique explanatory superiority. A claimed missing causal link would require a specified observable difference, not a change of labels.

**Unrestricted capability account.** A richer archive cannot reduce the feasible possibilities if all former policies remain available at the same costs and extra material can be ignored freely. The actual experiment instead changes what a fixed procedure automatically searches, or charges preservation costs. Its negative results therefore revise the claim that more retained material automatically improves enacted performance; they do not refute the distinct conditional claim about free, genuinely optional capabilities. Conversely, an account identifying generative capacity solely with stored component count misdescribes the matched-package outcomes.

**Task satisfaction as organizational good.** This identification would need an independently specified maintained organization or recipient condition, an actual causal relation from output to that condition, and a history in which information and selection respond to it. Present records do not supply those conditions. This is an omitted relationship in this representation and an unresolved connection for this branch, not evidence that the reconstructed foundation cannot explain such relationships. An alleged connection would fail if success were assigned after the fact without an output effect, if funding were silently reset at the handoff, or if the benchmark objective were simply renamed an internally formed need.

**Universal criterion claim.** Contextual ranking reversals do not defeat a criterion whose application concerns the actual relation among dependence, response and continuation. Such a criterion still needs independent content: what configuration it assesses, what effects it preserves, and which differences would change its judgment. A proposed identification must be narrowed or rejected if it treats an unmet dependency as met, confuses a retained answer with executable support, or suppresses a relevant affected center merely because its loss is absent from the chosen score. Merely assigning two arbitrary scores to an unchanged condition tests the score's freedom, not the fuller constitutive claim.

## What this branch now contributes

The branch supplies a constraint on adequate representation: **a claim about effective continuation must retain the relevant connections between what is inherited, how it is accessed, what actually happens and what support reaches the successor**. Same stored material or nearly equal success totals can omit consequential differences in that relationship. This constrains how the core is articulated; it does not add an autonomous branch to an already complete foundation.

The next interface is conceptual and evidential before it is architectural: identify a concrete dependency and its output consequences, then determine how that consequence becomes available to response selection under the same resources and chronology. Existing acquired-perspective work supplies part of this interface and should be reused at its own scope. The origin and revision of the chosen target, faithful access to its consequences and continued support for its use remain explicit obligations. This review corrects the inference and identifies those obligations without inventing their fulfillment.
