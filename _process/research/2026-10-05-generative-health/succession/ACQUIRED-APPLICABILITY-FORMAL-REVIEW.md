## What the formalization represents

The [event law][law] represents the actual continuation boundary, learned sensory machine, physical apparatus, native effects, acquired observations, committed interpretation, operation tariffs and controller phases. It supplies meaningful operations rather than accepting arbitrary successor states, observation values, candidate lists or fees as event arguments. Charges are computed before their effects; insufficient fuel or capacity denies the operation. Actual extraction precedes acquisition of its receipt, so an arrival can fund subsequent observation, while a predicted arrival cannot fund the attempt that would produce it.

The encompassing law remains fixed while the acquired candidate family changes the selected next encounter. This is a genuine local reorganization under a supplied rule. The formalization does not require an additional controller to establish that fact. It also does not derive the origin of the three-function family, contact, search rule, tariff or two-attempt boundary.

The [generic proof module][proofs] is separate from the concrete fixture equations. It establishes conservation of source and fuel, the correspondence between actual transfer and credited income, preservation of the sensory machine, and the connection between admitted event sequences and the shared `Generative.Trace` relation. Candidate retention is conditional on agreement between the acquired outcome and the candidate. The ambiguity and identification results concern the declared three-function family; the misleading-receipt counterexample remains admitted.

Its additional selection results apply to arbitrary retained sensory machines: with the complemented-or-blocked family and adequate support, the scan selects a negative-reading word and skips a positive-reading word. Separate results establish installation of the selected word, the record-preserving reader clamp, outcome-dependent stopping, and the effects of interrupted commitment or final retention. These are generic conditional statements, not merely a restatement of the finite saved outputs.

The [physical grounding module][grounding] derives a member of the family from the declared sensor/extraction coupling, proves its calibration, and connects actual extraction and faithful updating to it. This is an analyst-side witness about the represented law; it is never supplied to the controller. The available-packet condition matters because a failed extraction can retain an earlier actual-arrival flag. The observation equalities express the required common encounter correspondence; the temporal realization is checked in the event history rather than inferred from those equalities alone.

These statements do not convert truth retention into an unconditional property of record formation. The actual relation must belong to the family, remain applicable during the episode, and have a faithful observation at the enacted reading. The source implementation checks the predicted reading against the fresh native reading before extraction. Missing evidence, a denied write and a completed but misleading write retain their distinct consequences.

## What the finite certificates check

The [independent correspondence checker][checker] reads the existing saved artifacts and projects their protocol operations. Each of the 65 fixture theorems checks six claims together: the event word satisfies the formal guards; it equals the word selected by the formal policy; every projected stored state matches; the acquired sensor, receipt and actual-arrival coordinates match; the final error matches the native halt reason; and the final formal state has no further selected event. These are kernel-checked equations, rather than an unchecked report from a native evaluator.

Checking policy selection matters because the guarded relation allows some schedules beyond the implemented policy. In particular, its cleanup event is admitted whenever a route is open. A certificate that checked only the existence of a guarded path could therefore miss a scheduling discrepancy. The additional `policyWord` equality closes that issue for the represented histories; general conservation results retain their wider declared-law scope.

The projection covers 2,033 native snapshots through 1,008 formal operations. Of their endpoint states, 973 are directly observed and 35 unsuccessful-extraction intermediates are reconstructed from the following receipt-charge snapshot by undoing only that charge. The checker separately verifies 916 successful microcharges against the exact tariff. These counts are different descriptions of one saved study, not independent experimental replications.

The acquired observations are separately bound to actual native returns. A committed record does not stand in for an observation that was acquired but never written. The projector checks that the emitted uncommitted pair agrees with the native read and probe returns. It derives actual arrival from the physical transfer change, independently of the possibly misleading reported receipt. The formal volatile coordinates are not an assertion that the native controller has additional durable memory.

The resulting correspondence is directional and finite: these source-bound histories satisfy the separately stated formal event semantics and policy. It is not a compiler-refinement theorem for every Python input. Logging strings, duplicate audit snapshots and charge microstates are not each individual Lean transitions. Their interpretation and the Python projection remain reviewed executable components outside the Lean kernel.

## Necessary precision in the correspondence claim

The primary and clamped branches have the same projected physical, controller and accounting state at the matched checkpoint, including the acquired record and its expenditure. Their formal `Config.clamp` fields differ from the start as representations of the later external intervention. Thus they are not literally the same full formal `State`. No autonomous formation of that intervention is claimed.

The formal preparation sets workspace to the route requirement, while the native procedure adds that requirement to its entry workspace and later restores the prior value. The saved routes enter with zero workspace, so the represented histories agree exactly. This convention does not establish correspondence for arbitrary nonzero-workspace Python entry states.

The learned machine and apparatus configuration are bound separately across every native snapshot. Their absence from the compact numeric projection does not license alteration. The original acquisition and preparation remain hash-bound ancestry supported by the previous independent native check. This extension does not reprove every earlier formation event in Lean or join unrelated branch histories.

The generic scan theorem establishes that the query computation is independent of apparatus `Config`. It is not a complete information-flow theorem for the entire controller: `Body` also contains physical coordinates, and the policy includes the explicit intervention mode. The source audit and finite correspondence justify the narrower report that the implemented selector does not read hidden apparatus truth. Actual support constraints can still affect whether an intended inquiry is executable.
