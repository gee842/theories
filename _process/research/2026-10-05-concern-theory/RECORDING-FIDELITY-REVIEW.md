# Independent review: formed records and evidence fidelity

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review, separate from the recorder's implementation and formal/mathematical authors. This is not external peer review or empirical validation.

**Decision: accepted within the stated finite, supplied-law scope.** The executable comparison, formal-artifact inspection and mathematical/prose review passed. The accepted interface is **actual constituent formation → paid record capacity → faithfully retained observations usable by an existing statistical decoder**. It does not derive the decoder, indefinitely renewable memory or concern for another center.

## The physical contract

The [protocol](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/PROTOCOL.md) composes an actual feasible history of the old formation law with a new, explicit slot recipe. For `K` slots, `2K+1` initially unformed constituents become `K` free A, `K` free H and an A resident. Formation spends `2K+3` energy units. Binding each A/H pair into a blank slot spends a further `K` common units. The resident remains separate from the slot material; the old local energy has already been spent on copying. No part or energy receipt is counted twice.

The three-event recording operation has a real intermediate state. Before observing the outcome it reserves a blank slot and pays two common units, burning one and retaining the other as write escrow. Both possible receipts are source-backed before admission. The next admission remains disabled until the pending operation finishes. Observation changes source and common fuel without creating energy; commitment spends escrow and appends exactly the latched bit. The phase, reserved slot and escrow must remain visible in any representation of interrupted operations.

These conditions establish paid feasibility and completed-history correspondence under a declared schedule. They do not imply that a scheduler must complete an enabled write, that memory resists arbitrary damage, or that the old capture chemistry itself supplies the new two-unit source-to-common routing. The slot recipe, transduction, routing, completion schedule and finite no-erasure interval are explicit local inputs.

The old teacher correspondence is deliberately one-directional on admitted states. The recorder requires source at least two and available material capacity; the parent `prediction_step` can execute a realized loss with less source and does not model slots. Equality of a completed recorder macro with one parent update therefore does not make their entire transition systems equivalent. With two slots, the actual word `[0,1]` leaves common fuel three and source fourteen but no blank slot. A fuel-only or count-only representation would lose this obstruction.

The selected formation word is a feasible paid history, not a probability claim about spontaneous commissioning. Comparisons are conditional on that preparation while preserving its actual expenditure.

## Sampling, omissions and the counterexample

The full rule retains the entire completed raw word. With the supplied stationary independent potential outcome stream, its finite retained evidence has the prefix relation needed by the previously established simultaneous confidence guarantee. That guarantee is applied on the original stream. It does not require observations to remain independent after conditioning on the eventual stopping length.

The halt control omits the first zero and then stops permanently. Its retained word is still a literal prefix preceding that zero. The simultaneous event covering all original prefixes covers this selected prefix. In contrast, omitting a zero and then appending later observations breaks the prefix relation. Paying the same two-unit bill for every attempt does not repair the evidence distortion.

For the prescribed `K=8`, `T=32`, `β=1/4` preparation, the inherited stocks after assembly are common fuel 65 and source 64. They back every potential attempt through the finite horizon. The reused certificate has `L(8,8)=3/8`, and no shorter retained word crosses the strict threshold `1/4`. The independent exact calculation gives:

| Rule | Probability of the false threshold certificate |
|---|---:|
| Full record | `1/65536` |
| Omit zero, then resume | `10469065127243216839/18446744073709551616`, approximately `0.5675291577` |
| Omit first zero, then halt | `1/65536` |

The resume probability is exactly `Pr(Binomial(32,1/4) ≥ 8)`. Its large violation concerns applying the old slot-only certificate to a new retained sampling law. No new estimator or general advantage over conventional estimation is established.

The one-trial selection identity has a narrower scope: conditional on retention, the probability of one is `βr₁ / ((1−β)r₀ + βr₁)`. For interior `β` and positive retention, it equals `β` if and only if `r₀=r₁`. This is not a necessary condition for every valid confidence procedure: the halt control is an explicit exception to that broader claim. Outcome-dependent stopping and repeated outcome-dependent omission must remain distinct.

The [mathematical note](RECORDING-FIDELITY-MATH.md) preserves these quantifiers. Its local retention identity, stationary repeated-resumption argument, deterministic word induction, completed-macro equivalence and pathwise prefix transfer are valid at the stated scopes. The final wording also correctly distinguishes funding every potential attempt from actual admission: finite slots and the halting policy can still prevent further trials. The three rules share a price per attempt, not equal total expenditure or workload.

Nor does omitted evidence become inaccessible to every observer. At completed boundaries this particular ledger identifies raw ones by `(F_ready−F)/2` and attempts by `(F_ready+E_ready−F−E)/2`. An observer with those stock measurements, original references and a suitable implemented decoder could recover sufficient counts. The demonstrated failure is specific to the declared slot-only decoder.

## Independent verification method

The [independent checker](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/independent_check.py) imports no producer module. It derives stress endpoint families by binomial, negative-binomial and geometric word counts, separately from the producer's state dynamic program. Its confidence inversion uses integer polynomial multiplication. It independently executes the actual formation word, assembly and reserve/observe/commit updates, checking bound material, escrow and global energy at every intermediate state.

As an internal cross-check of the oracle, all 96 potential five-bit words across the three rules in a smaller funded preparation reproduce the independently derived stopped-word endpoint families. The two-slot test covers all four two-bit words, blocked admission during either pending phase, insufficient fuel/source, exhausted slot capacity and the contrast between terminal omission and an interior omitted outcome followed by resumption.

The frozen producer comparison **passed**: all 298 endpoint rows agree with 51 independently derived endpoint families; nine aggregate quantities agree exactly for each of the three rules. All 43 saved primary, control and escrow states match the independently derived phase laws. The actual preparation traces, capacity counterexample, exhausted-source and exhausted-fuel preparations also agree. The [saved independent record](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/independent-check.json) pins the checker, producer, protocol, results and supporting source hashes.

The separate Lean artifact inspection **passed**. Its fresh-build record audits 33 new declarations, with 18 root and 43 old formation dependency audits. The current source, checker, toolchain, saved outputs and available local compiler match the recorded hashes. This inspection did not perform a second compilation. The formal checker requires every newly declared theorem to receive an axiom audit, rejects admitted proofs and additional axioms, and allows the disclosed standard dependencies `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`.

The formal positive-law definitions and proofs agree with the reviewed Python phase semantics. The observation invariant carries a latched but uncommitted bit separately from sealed records. At idle endpoints it yields the complete record theorem. The macro equivalence retains the entire physical successor and its admission guard, and the concrete two-slot proof joins actual predecessor preparation to assembly and two writes. The material and escrow proofs apply at intermediate states. Controls, Bernoulli probabilities, the confidence theorem, scheduler completion and a formal refinement of Python are outside the Lean result. The executable comparison does not expand that formal scope.

## Derivation consequence and remaining obligations

This is a substantive finite premise discharge: complete evidence retention, previously assumed downstream, receives an actual material predecessor, a paid implementation and a discriminating failure under the same resource setting. It remains a conditional construction under supplied assembly and sensing laws.

Finite capacity cannot establish asymptotic reliability requiring an unbounded number of retained observations. Renewal, evidence transfer through succession, calibration after dependency change, construction of the certificate/decision machinery and acquisition of shared evaluation remain separate obligations. A fresh lifetime confidence allowance cannot be silently granted at restart or handoff. A truthful record of private resource consequences also does not determine felt valence, ethical reasons or another center's significance.

The [integration account](RECORDING-FIDELITY-INTEGRATION.md) correctly separates this stationary Bernoulli evidence from the parallel [prediction/action design](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/PROTOCOL.md), whose clipped register and action-dependent, changing target obey different laws. Equal numerical records do not establish a common interpretation or justify transferring a stationary confidence result. Joining them requires a compatible target and paid observation/retention contract. This study supports the next central connection—consequence-dependent formation and revision of prediction-to-action coupling—without claiming that recording fidelity already produces that coupling.
