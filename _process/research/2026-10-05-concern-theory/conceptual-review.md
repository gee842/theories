# Conceptual review of the concern theory

5 October 2026. Independent conceptual review of `THEORY.md`, `START-HERE.md`, and the evidence memo in this directory, with the dynamics appendix consulted to check the stated scope. Main documents were read only. Line references below refer to the reviewed versions.

## Verdict

The candidate is coherent at the level it actually constructs: a paid, maintained learning mechanism for functional other-directed valuation, with explicit transfer assumptions, action thresholds, causal-efficacy revision, and succession. The documents correctly separate moral standing from observer recognition and distinguish the origin of concern from its justification. They do not claim universal moral direction or full empirical validation.

Three small substantive clarifications are recommended before delivery. They do not require changing the formal models. One further wording improvement would make the starting primitive clearer.

## 1. Define the favorable direction of concern, not only responsiveness

**Locations:** `THEORY.md` lines 7–11 and 284; `START-HERE.md` line 5.

The organizing definition currently says that concern is responsiveness to another's stakes and that another's condition matters when it changes what a process regards as worth doing. This identifies evaluative inclusion but does not yet distinguish concern from hostility. A sadist can represent another accurately, give their condition direct motivational weight, correct its own actions against actual outcomes, and preserve the process across generations. Its responsiveness has the opposite sign. An exploiter also makes decisions responsive to another's condition, although the route is instrumental.

The positive costs assigned to harm in the formal model resolve this at the model level. The conceptual definition should express the same orientation.

**Suggested local replacement for the organizing sentence:**

> Direct concern treats another's harm or frustrated interests as a reason against an action, and their benefit as a reason in its favor, apart from the action's private consequences for the evaluator. Wise concern can correct its understanding of those interests and of what its actions actually do.

This characterizes pro-recipient motivation without making good intentions infallible, identifying morality with a welfare sum, or denying legitimate conflicts and boundaries. If the shorter term “concern” is retained, state once that this is the other-regarding sense used here. Preserve the distinction between this psychological/functional definition and normative standing.

For `START-HERE.md` line 5, “becomes significant” should also be qualified as **“acquires motivational significance within a process.”** Otherwise the opening can momentarily imply that the recipient lacked significance until recognized, despite the correct clarification at line 20. The title can remain as the user's natural question; the opening sentence supplies the precision.

## 2. Restrict completeness to the instantiated mechanism and declared interfaces

**Locations:** `THEORY.md` lines 15–17, 40–64, 156–158, 234–245; `START-HERE.md` lines 3 and 16.

The completeness distinction is good, but “no essential update ... left unspecified” can be read as applying to the whole conceptual architecture. The diagram includes criticism revising recognition and evaluation. The formal specimen implements causal-efficacy correction through `z`; it holds the feature repertoire, correspondence targets, shared routing, and normative scope fixed. Reflective revision of those latter features is discussed constructively but not implemented as an update rule. Section 7 itself correctly says that implementation needs explanation.

**Suggested one-sentence addition after the completeness definition:**

> This closure is relative to the declared model interfaces: the specimen implements efficacy correction, while the formation or revision of its feature repertoire, evaluative routing, and moral scope remains a further mechanism to explain.

An alternative is to replace “mechanistic completeness” with **“model-relative mechanistic completeness”** and add the same distinction where the diagram is introduced. This preserves the real achievement rather than downgrading it to a mere plan. The model makes its supplied inputs visible and derives consequences; it simply does not yet instantiate every arrow in the larger architecture.

The wider definition in `conceptual-evidence.md` is compatible: an architecture can identify every explanatory obligation without having experimentally or formally closed all of them. Avoid treating completeness of the map of obligations as completion of every obligation.

## 3. Correct the empirical label in the main evidence paragraph

**Location:** `THEORY.md` line 251.

Change **“acquisition of concern for a trained target”** to **“learned shifts in valuation of a trained target.”**

The Zhang–Hackel adult experiments support modulation of target valuation and restricted transfer. They do not isolate the first appearance of concern from an initially absent state; participants bring developmental histories and substantial baseline responsiveness. The evidence memo already makes this distinction. Matching the main paragraph to it prevents the zero-initial-coefficient model from borrowing empirical support for a stronger origination claim than was tested.

The same paragraph's “persistence after reward withdrawal” is acceptable as a topic label, provided it is not read as long-term retention or evidence that material reward caused the persistent motive. The Warneken–Tomasello result compares later helping after different prior reward conditions; the memo correctly describes the direction.

## 4. Optional precision about learned significance

**Locations:** `THEORY.md` lines 85–87; `START-HERE.md` line 9.

The model begins with an intrinsically adverse teaching signal, so not all significance is absent at time zero. What begins at zero is the learned coefficient through which the feature influences this modeled evaluation route. The main closure table already calls it a “predictive adverse-value coefficient.”

If revising for maximal clarity, describe the first step as:

> Learn an action-value association from an already significant experience; the learned coefficient can begin at zero.

This is a terminology improvement, not an objection to the construction. A conditional theory is entitled to explicit primitives; its new explanatory result is the acquisition, transfer, and maintained behavioral effect of learned valuation under those primitives.

## Standing, justice, and scope checks that pass

- Section 7 clearly states that moral standing predates and exceeds observer recognition. In particular, failure to share familiar features does not establish absence of standing. It therefore does not turn the `G=0` transfer limitation into a moral exclusion.
- Standing is not conditioned on productivity, reciprocal power, or the ability to finance the helper. Sections 9 and 11 make the material bridge optional and expose external support.
- The bearer-independence argument identifies its substantive normative premise rather than deriving impartiality from physical symmetry or survival. Relevant differences and additional relational duties are permitted.
- Concern, correct outcome prediction, efficacy, authority, solvency, and succession are separately tracked. Solvency does not certify moral adequacy.
- The widening theorem is appropriately confined to finitely many specified, separable recipient classes with joint affordability. It is not presented as guaranteed discovery of every unfamiliar class.
- Stationary maintenance and revision after harmful change are treated as different regimes. The possibility of harm during detection and initial acquisition remains visible.
- The theory takes an effective route through the joint situation as its causal unit. Modeling an actor does not imply an isolated self or eliminate recipients' different perspectives.
- “Candidate,” “conditional,” and the final universal-direction limitation are meaningful and consistent with the actual mathematical results.

The 30-word allowance per paper exceeds the relevant source-specific clauses in the memo's short synthesis paragraph and the main's evidence-roadmap paragraph. Those paragraphs combine distinct studies; unrelated clauses are not attributed to every source. All source-derived summaries therefore remain under 200 words per source across the reviewed main and memo. Their causal and normative extensions are explicitly marked as the present proposal rather than attributed research results.

The main cites only the separate Yoshida–Man arXiv preprint; that paragraph is 99 whitespace-delimited words including the present-model comparison and link label. The four empirical source URLs occur only in the evidence memo; `START-HERE.md` has no external source URLs. No duplicate empirical case description was found in the main. No long direct quotations appear.

The evidence memo's two critical qualifications were checked against the full papers: Zhang–Hackel's Experiment 3 overall choice result was nonsignificant, with the described exploratory awareness moderation; Li–Tomasello's disagreement effect carried to the same-session test, whereas the justification-request effect did not. They should be preserved in any shorter retelling.

## Whether an invented dialogue would help

No additional dialogue is necessary. The existing concealment-versus-help example already gives the reader a concrete way to distinguish feeling successful from benefiting another. Another invented dialogue risks suggesting that reflective moral acquisition has been formally implemented when it has only been mapped. If the introduction needs one plain example, reuse that existing contrast briefly rather than add a new evidential-looking story.

## Review boundary

This was a conceptual and source-use review, not a new full mathematical audit. The suggested changes are read-only recommendations for the root author. No main or canonical files were edited.

## Author response during review

The root author reported applying the pro-recipient orientation and empirical-label corrections, as well as separating welfare harm from resource compensation and declaring faithful reinstallation of the architecture at succession. A reread confirms that the opening, core definition, model characterization, and introductory reader guide now give the concern orientation explicitly. The model-relative completeness clarification above was then sent to the author as the remaining recommended scope edit. The unqualified “becomes significant” in the reader guide and the primitive-versus-learned-significance wording are minor precision suggestions, already constrained by the guide's later explicit standing and primitive-capacity statements.
