## What the word “complete” covers

The mathematical specimen declares initial conditions, update rules, the action comparison, real recipient effects, paid criticism, stock timing, and succession. It is complete as a **conditional mechanism for these variables**. Its architectural rules, teaching signals, class repertoire, validation scaffold, and resource environment remain explicit premises. The prose theory proposes a wider architecture that also includes reflective moral learning, agency, conflict, and institutional criticism; that wider architecture is not an already validated general theory.

The appendices contain complementary regimes. The stationary favorable-ecology theorem, changed-efficacy correction, associative acquisition example, and productive-capacity extension do not become a single universal theorem by appearing together. Their interfaces and differing assumptions are stated.

## Scope that remains open

- The origin of primitive felt significance and the relation between functional signals and experience.
- How shared valuation routes, legitimate critic access, and their maintenance become installed and retained under competition.
- Learning reliable correspondence across very different bodies, needs, languages, and possible forms of experience.
- Correct generalization to genuinely new recipient classes and newly understood stakes.
- Deliberation among plural claims, legitimate authority, refusal, and uncertainty without reducing every value to one score.
- Noisy and strategic evidence, environmental change, capture, and recovery after interruption.
- The empirical prevalence, robustness, and historical direction of these mechanisms.

A failed bridge should narrow or revise the theory. It should not be relabeled “insufficient wisdom” and excluded from its evidence.

## Verification status

General mathematical claims rest on the stated proofs. Finite exact checks corroborate closed forms, thresholds and ties, succession, cost timing, and counterexamples. Internal review examines conceptual scope and reconstructs selected mathematics independently. None of this is external peer review, proof-assistant certification, or new empirical validation.
