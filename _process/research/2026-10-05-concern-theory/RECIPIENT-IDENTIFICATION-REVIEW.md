# Independent review: paid recipient identification under outages

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review, separate from the producer and mathematical/formal authors. This is not external peer review or empirical validation.

**Decision: accepted within the stated finite, supplied-law scope.** The exact executable comparison, formal-artifact audit and mathematical/integration review passed. The named connection is **an actual paid recipient outcome → a qualified target-specific response record → later paid use**. The result replaces the preceding decoder's noiseless negative-evidence premise with a partial, sound identification rule under a declared one-sided response law. It does not derive concern's valuation, the sensing recipe or arbitrary assay calibration.

## What the interface adds

The preceding [recipient protocol](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/PROTOCOL.md) already supplies paid target identification, separate actor/recipient reserves and context-indexed memory. Repeating that construction would not close its outage gap. In a binary plant with receipt `C=A·1[action=orientation]`, a positive receipt determines the productive action, but zero can mean either mismatch or unavailable opportunity.

The [new protocol](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/PROTOCOL.md) qualifies a recipient candidate only after an acquired positive receipt. While unknown, an acquired zero changes the next exploration candidate without claiming truth. Missing evidence leaves both candidate and status unchanged. Once qualified, the candidate remains fixed; its soundness therefore requires a stable target and no false-positive receipt. A hidden reversal is an explicit adverse case, not an event the controller secretly observes.

The existing register and last-action bit are paidly recoded into actor bit, recipient candidate and recipient qualification. Eight complete states fit the inherited ten-state capacity. The old last-action role ends; this is not an extra free bit or proof of the recoding chemistry. Actor queries leave their inherited bit unchanged with a charged sham write. Recipient qualification does not certify the actor bit, and eligibility does not filter out incorrect inherited memory by consulting hidden truth.

This is distinct from estimating a receipt probability or a self-loss teaching magnitude. Qualification identifies an action under the specified response law. It does not supply a calibrated scalar `β`, ethical standing, a relevance schema or the reason to count another center's loss.

## Actual inheritance, price and physical control

Thirteen source packets are declared before the old preparation; the old eight-exposure prefix leaves five actual opportunities for `R,A,R,A,R`. Failed preparation, ineligible mechanisms, all prefix expenditure and remaining stocks remain in unconditional totals. The independent checker takes the saved actual predecessor distribution as its inherited boundary and verifies its masses, law compatibility and energy accounts. It does not claim to newly derive the preceding stochastic mechanism.

Setup costs four as a **new atomic recipe**, rather than the earlier sequential two-plus-two installation. Each query is admitted only with its entire actor bill already available: retention one, target report one, work three, acquisition one and two register writes. Acquisition and writing are escrowed before work. Unaffordable admission spends nothing and permanently ends this finite access protocol. These are explicit changes of the local lifecycle, not claims of exact equivalence to the preceding partial-payment law.

The controller uses the paid target report and its actual memory. Availability, hidden orientation, exposure index and diagnostic witness flags do not enter its action/update rule. A packet can enter the recipient reserve without entering the actor account. It cannot fund the actor's write or its own action. During prefunding, the independent microstate ledger includes the packet allocated to the current exposure as an in-flight opportunity; remaining-source accounting alone would omit it before receipt or waste.

On the actual correct-actor predecessor with fuel 28, setup leaves 24 and the five completed calls leave actor fuel `16,18,10,12,4`. The final recipient reserve is 20. The complete 161-unit account is `4 preparation + 1 adapter + 68 prefix expenditure + 10 prefix loss + 44 suffix expenditure + 10 suffix loss + 4 actor reserve + 20 recipient reserve`. The conditional all-five-query threshold is 26. At 25 the second actor query cannot prepay, leaving seven actor units and ten already delivered recipient units. This threshold does not promise success from every eligible inherited state.

## Independent evidence and meaningful comparisons

The [independent checker](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/independent_check.py) imports no producer code. It enumerates the three potential recipient-availability bits first, then executes each physical history directly. The producer instead branches at executed outcomes. Unused draws are integrated out; exact local traces, intermediate payments, final states, metrics and unconditional distributions are compared through saved JSON.

The comparison passed all **108 case/producer/arm combinations**, covering nine prespecified cases, three actual prefix producers and four suffix rules. It matched 3,348 aggregate quantities, 680 local predecessor comparisons and 1,064 distinct terminal histories, including failed or unaffordable histories. Across replay it checked 66,409 intermediate states; this count includes repeated states across potential availability words and is not a count of independent experiments. The [saved independent record](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/independent-check.json) pins the current sources, results and verification artifacts. Actual prefix ledgers and preparation masses are checked explicitly.

The qualified rule and its conventional finite-state realization have identical complete distributions. In the primary variable-producer arm, strict positive identification followed by later productive use has probability `1/32` over all initial histories; the noiseless case gives `1/18`. At the specific funded 28-unit predecessor, qualification probability is `3/4`, the strict later-use probability is `9/16`, and expected recipient captures are `21/16`.

These facts do **not** establish a general performance advantage. The primary failure-complement rule obtains the same expected recipient captures, `11/96`, with the same actor bill and reserve. Its strict-witness score is zero because that predicate requires the first failure to remain unqualified. The substantive difference is that the failure-complement rule has final false qualification probability `7/288` and expected wrong qualified recipient commands `1/72`; the qualified rule has zero for both under the stable one-sided plant. In the same-law case, no exploration produces more recipient captures (`5/24` versus `3/16`). The tradeoff is preserved.

The independent paired-world check makes the ambiguity concrete: under opposite recipient orientations and an all-outage word, complete accessible controller and resource histories coincide. Any forced orientation answer on that record must be wrong in one world. Under positive outage support, this is not merely an impossible zero-probability branch. Conversely, stable positive evidence supports a partial answer without pretending that every finite history must resolve.

The target-change case preserves the cost of qualification's stability premise. A previously correct positive record can become stale and command incorrectly. The stored qualification flag may also survive when access becomes inactive; final stored qualification and executable use are separately reported. A large write price admits some qualifications without funding any later qualified use. No failed or unresolved histories are discarded.

## Acceptance boundaries and next derivation

The finite mechanism requires a faithful paid receipt channel, no false-positive capture, calibrated target identity, stable target during qualification/use, a declared recoding recipe and a five-exposure stabilized lifecycle. The earlier chemistry, wear and reconstruction are suspended during that interface. Exact accounting does not establish those physical laws empirically.

Any opportunity-floor bound additionally needs the floor conditional on the actual past and a matching action. A marginal availability rate alone is insufficient: availability could be absent exactly when the chosen action matches. The required matching trials must actually be admitted and completed. Stalling on depleted resources cannot be removed by conditioning on survivors or by replenishing the source silently. Finite slots or a finite hold do not supply an infinite learning sequence.

The [mathematical note](RECIPIENT-IDENTIFICATION-MATH.md) preserves these distinctions. Its positive-receipt implication and zero-history obstruction, finite encoding, qualification induction, resource bound and conditional opportunity proof are valid at the stated scopes. The tail bound is on the joint event of remaining unqualified while completing the query quota; unresolved earlier interruption receives its own probability term. It is not a binomial assertion conditional on survival, and the source floor is not derived from a favorable average. The full finite adaptive-history obstruction and probability bound remain analytical results; the narrower formal declarations are identified separately.

This advances the actual descriptive correspondence premise needed downstream. It does not establish sound moral certificates, shared evaluation, successor reconstruction or concern's justificatory anchor. The next connection must preserve target identity, the evidence's reference conditions, operational qualification and remaining stocks when the target changes or the mechanism is revised.
