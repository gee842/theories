## A failure that separates funding from fidelity

The prespecified comparison constructs eight slots from the same old law. Original common fuel eighty-four and source seventy-two leave common fuel sixty-five and source sixty-four after preparation and assembly. These stocks can pay every possible trial through a horizon of thirty-two. Three record rules share the same starting stocks and two-unit bill per actual trial:

| Record rule | What the slot-only decoder receives |
|---|---|
| Complete recording | The first eight outcomes, then acquisition stops. |
| Omit zero and resume | Ones are sealed; a zero spends the write bill but returns the slot to blank. Acquisition continues until eight ones or the horizon. |
| Omit the first zero and halt | A genuine prefix before the first zero, or eight ones if capacity fills first. No acquisition resumes after the omission. |

For a stationary independent target with probability one quarter of outcome one, eight retained ones give the earlier lower certificate `3/8`, using lifetime error allowance `1/10`. The full recorder produces that false certificate with probability `1/65536`. The omit-and-resume rule produces it with probability `Pr(Binomial(32,1/4) ≥ 8)`, approximately `0.567529`. The same confidence formula is being applied to a different retained sampling law.

The halt control distinguishes terminal omission from repeated selective retention. Its stored word is still a literal prefix of the original stream. Simultaneous coverage over all prefixes therefore covers the selected prefix, even though its length depends on outcomes. No new confidence allowance is granted on stopping.

The [mathematical account](RECORDING-FIDELITY-MATH.md) also identifies the selection effect within one trial. At a given pre-trial history, if outcome-dependent retention probabilities are `r₀` and `r₁`, the probability of one conditional on retention is `βr₁ / ((1−β)r₀ + βr₁)`. With a nondegenerate target and positive retention, this equals `β` exactly when `r₀ = r₁`. This is a statement about that conditional distribution, not a necessary condition for every valid confidence procedure. In particular, conditioning a stopped prefix on its eventual length can bias its observations; its simultaneous confidence guarantee still follows from coverage over the original stream's prefixes. Complete preallocated recording is sufficient, and suitable outcome-independent thinning can supply another sampling interface.

This failure concerns the declared slot-only interface. An observer with accurate original and current stock measurements could reconstruct attempted and loss counts from this particular ledger. Those measurements, references and computations would need their own implementation. The omitted records do not imply that every conceivable observer is unable to recover the evidence.

## What this advances in the common derivation

The accepted connection replaces one supplied downstream condition—faithful finite evidence retention—with a material construction, payment order and completed-event account. It also gives a failure mechanism within the same resource setting: continuing selective recording can defeat an otherwise valid confidence procedure. Physical persistence and accurate prediction therefore remain distinct capacities to explain.

Finite slots supply a finite-prefix confidence interface. They do not supply the indefinitely growing evidence count needed for the earlier asymptotic learning theorems. Memory renewal, transfer to successors, continuing resources, calibration after dependency change and the construction of the decoding machinery remain further obligations. None can be obtained by resetting counts or granting a fresh error allowance at succession.

The parallel [prediction/action design](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/PROTOCOL.md) uses a finite predictor and its correction under a different declared law. Its clipped register, changing action-dependent target and ambiguous receipts are not this stationary Bernoulli stream. The two constructions cannot be joined by identifying their numerical records. A valid connection must preserve target meaning, paid access, retained information and actual intermediate states.

The next central connection is consequence-dependent formation and revision of the prediction-to-action coupling. This recording result supports that task by specifying what a trustworthy retained feedback route must supply and how selective retention can fail. The remaining material connection must carry the evidence and its reference through mechanism revision, renewal and actual action. The separate route from prediction to shared evaluation must explain both widening and exclusion while retaining the affected centers. A truthful record of private losses does not itself establish concern for another center.
