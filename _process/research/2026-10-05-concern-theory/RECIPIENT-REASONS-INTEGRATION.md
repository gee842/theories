## What the reason means

Let `G` be a supplied objective to support this particular recipient. The root means `G`. The two other nodes mean, respectively, `G` together with compatibility of command zero or command one with the recipient's response law. They are alternative action-specific conclusions concerning the **same** recipient, not two new patients or an allocation of moral standing.

Under the existing binary receipt law, compatibility can be stated precisely:

`Compatible(Z,a) := for every availability v, receipt(a,Z,v) = v`.

Thus a compatible command succeeds when the modeled opportunity is available; the claim does not assert that an opportunity will be available. Paid inference from a sound certificate of `Z=a` can establish this compatibility. The factual-to-instrumental connection should therefore follow from the physical law rather than an arbitrary new ethical edge table. Whether `G` is justified, how it becomes a maintained objective and what competing reasons should prevail remain separate explanatory obligations.

This distinction matters at the actual endpoint: the actor has four working units and no source packet. A sound reason can be available while another support action is physically unavailable. Effective protection remains the next parallel interface, with its own resource and authority requirements.

The names `p` and `q` in the reused finite graph do not identify these route claims with the older reason-replay study's patient-case claims. An action consumer must retain their actual meaning: which command is compatible with this same recipient's law, relative to `G`. Transporting the mask to a differently interpreted protective gate is another typed interface, not a justified consequence of matching node indices. The later action must also retain the source-zero, working-four endpoint or explicitly derive added capacity from an earlier declared endowment.

## Preserve the information in negative certificates

The acquired cache answers the prebound predicates `Z=0`, `Z=1`, `Z=0`. Its entries are correlated. A present positive receipt can yield either a true or a false answer to such a predicate; nonreceipt itself remains unknown.

The initial design considered using only positive cache entries as direct edges. Review found that this would discard useful information: `Some false` for `Z=1` already establishes `Z=0` under binary exclusivity. Removing a later positive record would not remove that earlier knowledge. The frozen protocol therefore included explicit paid complement inference before its first numerical execution.

The circuit pays to inspect each of the three existing cache entries. For a predicate `Z=d`, a known true answer yields orientation `d`, a known false answer yields its complement, and unknown remains unknown. The newly formed reason workspace backs three bounded normalized values. Their agreement can supply the two orientation facts needed by the action graph. With no known value, neither route is warranted. Contradictory values trigger a separate conflict state and supply neither route; this guard does not detect all consistently wrong evidence. Under the fixed sound target law, conflicting normalized values should be impossible.

The graph retains the three-node pure closure interface: root→route0, root→route1, root→route0. Derived certificates describe inferred orientation facts, not additional observations. The third derived edge is always unknown; neither duplication nor a derived fact counts as independent evidence. The formal claim is exact closure in this stated language and sound instrumental interpretation. A finite direct lookup can compute the same route decision more cheaply, so the path circuit's tariff is not a universal lower bound or a claim of superior decision performance.

## Numerical evidence and semantic limits

Independent reproduction matches **54 comparison arms, 666 actual continuing histories, 1,404 exact aggregate metrics and 13,746 consumer microstates**. The independent oracle was written before inspecting the implementation and separately checks all twenty-seven three-entry partial-Boolean vectors, paid reasoning prefixes and actual larger preparation. The matched conventional producer agrees exactly.

For the primary variable producer, each cycle publishes a nonroot compatible route with probability **3/8**. The stricter identification/recording/two-replay history has probability **9/64**. These probabilities condition on the new controlled construction and keep all subsequent failed and ineligible outcomes. A completed root-only audit is distinguished from failure to finish; neither is mislabeled an acquired action conclusion.

Selective corruption of a stored action bit before the second read creates a conflict with probability **9/64**. Consistently corrupted prior tags instead produce a false route with probability **3/8** without conflict. These controls violate the integrity premise and show why a consensus of repeated readings cannot supply it. Historical truth, current calibration and the justification of `G` remain separate.

A supplied lookup circuit uses the same factual cache, three paid inferences and invalidation rules. Its one-unit final lookup makes the reason bill five rather than ten, yields the same completed masks and retains ten more units after two cycles. The generic path catalogue therefore has no claimed algorithmic superiority; its value here is a checked interface to existing rooted reasoning.

An incomplete first factual cache blocks the scheduled reason activation even after reserve transfer. This is not a proof that another paid factual reading is impossible. Missing workspace likewise preserves its unbound constituents and saved assembly unit. The two-cycle schedule can pay for a second factual read after an earlier reason failure; it is a declared finite schedule, not an optimal spending policy. The accepted independent review retains these distinctions when assessing completion.
