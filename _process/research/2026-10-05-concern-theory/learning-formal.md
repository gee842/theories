# A conditional formation theory of learned concern

Research memo, 2026-10-05. This extends `work/direction-frontier/math-note.md` without changing the canonical theory. The earlier model took a concern weight as supplied. Here that effective weight starts at zero and is generated by two distinct learning processes: learning the valence of a self-experienced adverse state, and learning a correspondence between differently situated states. Their transfer into action depends on an explicit shared evaluation architecture. The model is complete within those declared mechanisms; it is not a proof that all minds acquire them or that every extrapolated judgment is warranted.

## 1. Variables and the explanatory boundary

For a candidate recipient j, let H_j(a) be an independently specified adverse outcome under action a. This is a modeled outcome variable, not whatever an observer happens to find distressing. Whether that variable measures the recipient's relevant stakes is an empirical and ethical question outside the scalar toy model.

The actor has separate components:

- A world model predicts H_j(a), private consequences, and the efficacy of actions.
- An intrinsic self teaching signal β>0 registers a specified adverse state when experienced by the actor. The sign and existence of this primitive valence are supplied; its learned predictive coefficient is not.
- A learned coefficient w_t≥0 predicts the intensity of that self teaching signal from a unit adverse-state feature.
- A learned correspondence g_{j,t}∈[0,1] measures how strongly the recipient's represented state matches that feature in the **evaluation input**. Correspondence evidence is structural or predictive evidence, not a reward for choosing protection.
- A shared evaluation route uses v_{j,t}=g_{j,t}w_t when comparing recipient outcomes. This routing rule is an explicit architectural premise. The same learned world model can coexist with an identity gate that routes only self outcomes into evaluation.

The word “concern” below means this functional contribution of another's represented condition to action evaluation. It does not assert the actor feels compassion, recognizes rights, or possesses a complete moral theory. Positive concern also need not determine the final action: competing costs, conflicts, and delivery failure remain possible.

This supplies a mechanism for acquired valuation rather than merely renaming a fixed v. It does not derive the intrinsic β, the capacity to learn, or the choice of shared routing from valence-free physics. A conditional mechanistic explanation may take these as primitives; a universal account would need further explanation of their prevalence and limits.

## 2. Why self learning cannot force other-directed valuation

Let a linear learner evaluate a feature x∈R^d by f_θ(x)=x^Tθ. Training consists of Xθ=y, where rows of X are training features and the system is consistent.

**L1 — Exact held-out identifiability.** All training-consistent parameter vectors assign the same evaluation to a held-out feature x_* if and only if x_* lies in the row span of X.

**Proof.** If x_*=X^Ta, then x_*^Tθ=a^TXθ=a^Ty for every solution. If x_* is outside the row span, its orthogonal projection z onto ker X is nonzero and x_*^Tz=||z||²>0. Given any solution θ_0, all θ_0+tz fit the same training data, while their held-out evaluations vary as x_*^Tθ_0+t||z||². ∎

The unrestricted real-valued theorem permits arbitrary held-out values. Under nonnegativity or boundedness constraints, the admissible interval can shrink; lack of identification can remain, but arbitrariness must not be claimed without checking those constraints. A sufficient nonnegative counterexample is X=(1,0), y=β, x_*=(0,1): θ=(β,0) and θ=(β,β) both fit perfectly and assign different nonnegative held-out values.

There is a stronger architectural ambiguity. Consider the same injury intensity H and two candidate evaluation features:

\[
\phi_{\mathrm{shared}}(j,H)=H,\qquad
\phi_{\mathrm{gated}}(j,H)=\mathbf1\{j=\mathrm{self}\}H.
\]

Every self training sample has the same feature and prediction in both models. On another's identical injury, the first transfers learned value and the second assigns zero. Both can have an identical, perfectly accurate separate world model of all injuries. Hence neither accurate world prediction nor arbitrarily much self-only training logically forces shared evaluation.

A minimum-norm solution, simplicity penalty, or representation prior can select one extrapolation; it is then the data **plus that inductive rule**, not the self data alone, that select it. Physical relabeling covariance does not eliminate the self gate: “value only the current actor's state” is itself a uniform actor-relative rule.

## 3. Learning the value signal from zero

Take a unit feature x_s with ||x_s||=1. Repeated self experiences produce the teaching target β. Starting at θ_0=0, apply error correction with memory decay:

\[
\theta_{t+1}=(1-\delta)\theta_t
 +\eta u_t x_s(\beta-x_s^T\theta_t),
\]

where u_t∈{0,1} indicates an available, paid teaching or calibrated rehearsal event, η>0, δ≥0, and η+δ≤1. Because the update keeps θ parallel to x_s, write θ_t=w_tx_s. Then

\[
\boxed{w_{t+1}=(1-\delta-\eta u_t)w_t+\eta u_t\beta.}
\]

The teacher is an experienced or accurately recalled self outcome, not an externally assigned reward for being altruistic. Calibrated rehearsal need not mean producing new suffering; it can mean revisiting retained outcome information. Its fidelity and resource costs need an implementation.

For an evaluation feature x_j, the transferred coefficient is

\[
f_{\theta_t}(x_j)=w_t\langle x_s,x_j\rangle.
\]

Thus shared features yield transfer; orthogonal identity-specific features yield none. A negative inner product could yield an opposed evaluation, so nonnegative transfer is an additional property of the present representation class, not a general theorem about all learning.

**L2 — Acquisition under sustained teaching.** With u_t=1, put r=1−δ−η∈[0,1) and W=ηβ/(δ+η). Starting at zero,

\[
w_t=W(1-r^t).
\]

It is nondecreasing and converges to W. With no forgetting (δ=0), W=β. Positive forgetting reduces the maintained value unless teaching strength is increased.

**Proof.** The scalar recurrence is affine with fixed point W. Subtract W and iterate. ∎

For arbitrary u_t and w_0∈[0,β], the interval [0,β] remains invariant: each update is a nonnegative combination of w_t and β, with coefficient sum 1−δ≤1. This bound will be useful when teaching pauses.

## 4. Learning recognition and transferable correspondence from zero

A fixed g_j would leave recognition supplied. Instead, let σ_j∈[0,1] denote the target correspondence supported by independently observed structural or cross-position evidence. The evidence may concern common causal signatures, matched role transitions, or an independently validated feature alignment. It is not defined by whether the learner chooses to help.

Let s_{j,t}∈{0,1} indicate an observed and paid correspondence-training episode. The learner uses

\[
\boxed{g_{j,t+1}=(1-\kappa s_{j,t})g_{j,t}
 +\kappa s_{j,t}\sigma_j,\qquad 0<\kappa\le1.}
\]

This is descent on the local squared alignment error (g_j−σ_j)²/2 at observed episodes. Starting at g_{j,0}=0,

\[
g_{j,t}=\sigma_j\left[1-(1-\kappa)^{N_j(t)}\right],
\qquad N_j(t)=\sum_{k<t}s_{j,k}.
\]

The formula assumes exact, stable training targets. With noisy or biased targets, this equation becomes a different stochastic or misspecified learning problem; the exact convergence result should not be carried over without extra assumptions.

The parameter can be shared by a feature class rather than assigned separately to each identity. A novel recipient mapped by an independently checked recognition procedure into an already learned class inherits that class's g. A novel recipient outside the learned classes need not receive any positive transfer. Generalization therefore depends on a specified feature map and its domain, not on novelty alone.

Crucially, accurate correspondence in a **world-model representation** need not enter the evaluation route. The present mechanism learns the feature correspondence used by a shared value predictor. An otherwise identical identity-gated architecture can learn all structural correspondences yet refuse their use in action valuation. This distinction prevents the construction from quietly equating knowledge with care.

## 5. Exact formation and protection threshold

Use the earlier three-action comparison for one recipient: perfect protection costs c_j and leaves harm zero; ignore costs d_j and leaves H_j>0; bypass costs e_j and leaves H_j. All are feasible and affordable at the decision date. Put b_j=min(d_j,e_j). Learned evaluation adds v_{j,t}H_j to either harmful action, where

\[
v_{j,t}=g_{j,t}w_t.
\]

Protection is uniquely preferred exactly when

\[
\boxed{g_{j,t}w_tH_j>c_j-b_j.}
\]

Equality is a tie, not guaranteed protection. If c_j<b_j, protection is already privately preferred at t=0; that case does not demonstrate newly acquired concern.

**L3 — Acquired protective choice from initially zero value and recognition.** Suppose u_t=s_{j,t}=1 at every step, w_0=g_{j,0}=0, and all costs and correspondence targets remain fixed. Then

\[
v_{j,t}=\sigma_jW(1-r^t)[1-(1-\kappa)^t].
\]

The learned recipient valuation is nondecreasing, and a finite date of unique protective choice exists if and only if

\[
\boxed{\sigma_jW H_j>c_j-b_j.}
\]

In particular, when c_j≥b_j, σ_j>0 and a sufficiently large learned value can move the actor from nonprotection or indifference to strict protection even though there is no reward for protecting that recipient in the training rule.

**Proof.** Substitute the two closed forms. Both nonnegative factors are nondecreasing and converge to their stated limits. If the strict limiting inequality holds, convergence gives a finite crossing. If it fails, every finite value is bounded above by a nonprotective limit, so no strict crossing occurs. This includes equality at the limit. ∎

This theorem does not insert a positive v_j as an initial condition. It does supply a positive primitive self valence β, a shared evaluation architecture, opportunities to learn, informative correspondence evidence, and feasible actions. These are the causal conditions whose presence and acquisition require examination in an application.

**L4 — Conditional widening and its exact ceiling.** For finitely many recipient classes whose costs, H_j, and σ_j remain fixed, and which receive the sustained learning specified above, the set of uniquely protected classes is nondecreasing. Its eventual union is exactly

\[
\{j:\sigma_jWH_j>c_j-b_j\}.
\]

If the set is finite, all classes in this union are protected after some finite common date. Classes outside it never become uniquely protected through this mechanism. For infinitely many classes, individual finite crossing does not imply a single finite date when all are protected.

**Proof.** Each class has a nondecreasing score and the exact crossing criterion L3. For the finite case, take the maximum of finitely many crossing dates. ∎

The result is conditional monotonicity under fixed evidence and an unchanging model. Corrective evidence can properly decrease an erroneous match; rising costs, conflicts between recipients, action loss, identity gates, forgetting, or budget failure can shrink the protected set. Widening is therefore a possible learned trajectory, not a universal learning law.

## 6. Can self-relevant evidence favor a shared architecture?

The shared routing premise need not be opaque, but its origin must not be assumed solved. A minimal model-selection result illustrates one possible pressure.

Suppose a self-relevant adverse state appears in context A and then in a held-out self context B. A shared predictor trained to convergence in A predicts β in B. A context-gated predictor trained only in A predicts zero in B. If the independently observed self teaching target in B is β, their squared validation losses are respectively zero and β². A selector minimizing that validation loss strictly favors sharing. If the true target in B is zero, the ordering reverses.

This is an exact demonstration that transfer architecture can be favored by accurate prediction of self outcomes across contexts without rewarding protection of another. It is not proof that real learners use this selector or that self-context transfer extends correctly to a recipient's states. Extension to context C requires an invariance assumption or new validation. Nothing in finite A/B evidence uniquely determines all possible C targets; L1 describes the residual underdetermination.

Simplicity or data-efficiency arguments consequently need a declared task distribution and architecture cost. A shared predictor can be efficient under invariant targets and systematically wrong under heterogeneous targets. There is no distribution-free theorem that the broader feature class is always better.

## 7. Forgetting and the need for actual renewal of learned concern

After teaching stops, w evolves as w_{t+L}=(1−δ)^Lw_t. If learned correspondence also decays at rate ε while unattended, its contribution is g_{t+L}=(1−ε)^Lg_t. Then

\[
v_{t+L}=v_t[(1-\delta)(1-\varepsilon)]^L.
\]

For a strictly positive protection threshold T=(c−b)/H, any nonzero decay eventually removes a finite initial margin during a sufficiently long unattended interval. When T≤0, private incentives can preserve protection independently of concern; when T=0 and decay factors remain positive, positive but vanishing concern still passes the strict threshold at every finite date. Those cases should not be conflated.

**L5 — Exact periodic rehearsal level.** Fix g>0 and let a=1−δ, r=1−δ−η. One teaching event is followed by L−1 no-teaching updates, then the pattern repeats. The attracting post-teaching value and pre-teaching minimum are

\[
w^+_L=\frac{\eta\beta}{1-r a^{L-1}},\qquad
w^-_L=a^{L-1}w^+_L.
\]

The limiting cycle maintains a unique protective preference at every phase if and only if

\[
gw^-_LH>c-b.
\]

A strict positive limiting margin implies the same property after sufficiently many cycles from any initial value in [0,β]. At equality, no strict-margin conclusion follows. Initial formation can still require an earlier interval of affordable protection or tolerated nonprotection.

**Proof.** From one post-teaching value x to the next, the map is x↦r a^{L−1}x+ηβ. Its coefficient lies in [0,1), so it converges to the displayed fixed point. Within a cycle values decay monotonically until the next teaching step; their smallest decision-phase value is w^-_L. The threshold test follows. ∎

A high average teaching frequency does not guarantee a maintained margin. Consider blocks of n² teaching steps followed by n unattended steps, for n=1,2,.... Their limiting teaching frequency is one, but the unattended gaps are unbounded. For δ>0, the value at the end of gap n is at most β(1−δ)^n and tends to zero. Thus positive thresholds fail on a subsequence despite almost all dates being teaching dates. Gap bounds or an equivalent explicit memory-maintenance condition matter more than average exposure alone.

Rehearsal, observation, computation, and preservation consume resources and attention. The companion dynamics closure must charge them before claiming continued action, and must account for succession, inherited recognition, and copied weights. An unimplemented recurrence is an explanatory component, not an installed process.

## 8. Corrective feedback must distinguish recipient outcome from observer relief

The learning target and the feedback channel determine what is reinforced. If repair and masking both remove the observer's distress cue but only repair changes H_j, a learner trained solely on that cue cannot treat cue removal as evidence of recipient recovery. A cheaper mask can then dominate despite improved internal prediction and reduced observer distress.

The relevant missing information is not fixed by calling the cue “empathy.” Recipient condition, observer sensation, action efficacy, and model confidence need separate state variables. A feedback channel must contain information that discriminates protection from successful concealment, and its interpretation must remain open to correction. When the two actions produce identical observations for every available experiment, no estimator based solely on those observations can identify which one protected the recipient.

This is a structural identification limit, not a claim that every report is reliable or every inaccessible condition is unknowable. The companion closure develops the action/outcome/critic and budget version. The present learning theorems do not assume that relief inside the observer is a faithful measure of benefit outside them.

## 9. A numerical formation witness

Take β=2, η=1/2, δ=1/10, κ=1/4, σ=4/5, H=10, c=6, and b=1. Then W=5/3, r=2/5, and the protective threshold is T=(c−b)/H=1/2.

At t=1, w=1 and g=1/5, so v=1/5<T: protection is not preferred. At t=2, w=7/5 and g=7/20, so v=49/100<T: it remains unpreferred. At t=3, w=39/25 and g=37/80, so v=1443/2000>T: learned protection becomes uniquely preferred. Neither w nor g started positive. Training used self adverse outcomes and structural correspondence; no direct protective reward appeared.

If an identity gate fixes the valuation route to zero for this recipient, the same world-model learning and self experiences produce no other-directed term. If σ=0, correspondence learning also leaves v=0. If the recipient is accurately recognized but the teacher is maintained too weakly to satisfy L3, protection never becomes uniquely preferred. These are distinct failure modes.

## 10. Status, tests, and what remains outside the model

This is a constructive conditional bridge: an embodied self teaching signal can be learned, a cross-position representation can be acquired, their shared action-value use can generalize beyond trained identities, and the resulting preference can cross an actual protection threshold. The proofs also locate precise obstruction points and maintenance conditions.

It does not infer moral entitlement from feature similarity, infer subjective experience from an arbitrary signal, derive intrinsic valence from neutral dynamics, prove that shared routing is universally selected, prove correctness on every novel being, or establish survival of the machinery that implements these recurrences. It models one adverse dimension with separable feasible actions; conflicts, rights, unknown stakes, and multi-dimensional flourishing require a broader deliberative layer.

All mathematical results above are derived directly. No empirical neuroscience or psychology claim is relied on. The adjacent `check_learning.py` and `learning-checks.json` provide independent exact rational checks of closed forms, threshold crossings and ties, representation ambiguity, periodic memory levels, and the validation counterexample. General claims rest on their proofs, not on finite checks.
