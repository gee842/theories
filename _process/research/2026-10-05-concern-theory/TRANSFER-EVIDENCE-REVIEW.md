# Independent review of the noisy-evidence concern interface

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review, independent of the mathematical-note and executable-model authors. The named connection is **actual paid consequence records → maintained estimates → an affordable, explicitly routed later concern decision**. This is mathematical and executable verification, not external peer review or empirical identification.

**Status: accepted at the conditional reliability-interface scope.** The [mathematics](TRANSFER-EVIDENCE-MATH.md), [finite experiment](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/transfer-evidence/README.md) and [integration](TRANSFER-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) agree on what is established. Exact repeated teaching targets can be replaced by specified noisy records under additional evidence, retention and support conditions. The finite construction implements only the self-related target coordinate: correspondence and efficacy remain supplied as g=z=1. It does not acquire cross-center correspondence, derive shared evaluation or finish the origin of concern.

## 1. Why this is a useful additional connection

The preceding [paid teaching review](../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-REVIEW.md) showed that a finite mean above a threshold does not put every individual history above it. The present mathematical result is stronger at a different scope: under noisy constant-gain teaching, even indefinitely funded observations can lose every positive concern margin infinitely often. Thus the earlier exact-teacher durable-choice theorem cannot be imported simply by replacing its fixed teacher with the mean of a noisy physical signal.

The repository's [adaptive-evidence study](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/reproduction-test/adaptive-evidence/FORMAL.md) already established cumulative-evidence consistency, lifetime statistical certification, repeated fixed-batch errors, reset bookkeeping, memory costs and stale evidence after change. Those general methods are reused here. The new contribution is the concern-specific recurrence obstruction, signed joint-score contract and actual resource-state connection from the existing paid teacher to a later action. It is not a new generic confidence-sequence method or another claimed origin of the updater.

## 2. Mathematical audit

**Constant gain.** T1 is valid for its declared fully funded iid observations, bounded nonnegative targets, nonzero probability of a zero reading and contraction r<1. Independent disjoint blocks supply arbitrarily long zero runs, each driving w below any positive threshold. With a>b, H>0, g∈[0,1] and z≤1, the bound z[b+Hgw]≤b+Hw then gives infinitely many failed strict choices. The positive threshold requirement matters: private benefit alone, exact nonzero teachers or a different observation class can change the conclusion. The theorem does not say constant gain is useless for tracking change. Infinite actual funding is an explicit condition, absent from the finite closed-source example.

**Accumulation.** T2 correctly uses almost-sure convergence of the three retained sample averages, followed by continuity of the installed decision score and a strictly positive limiting margin. Its fourth-moment estimate is sufficient: bounded centered independent terms give E[(mean−truth)^4]≤3/n², so fixed-error tail probabilities are summable. Different acquired counts are permitted when every required count tends to infinity. Cross-stream independence is unnecessary. Record retention, calibration, continuing acquisition and action affordability are necessary premises, not consequences of convergence.

**Anytime certification.** T3 correctly inverts nested binomial upper-tail tests on a finite dyadic grid. At one count, L>p implies the true-parameter upper-tail p-value is below that count's allowance. Because the candidate tests are nested, no additional union over grid points is needed. Summing α/[3n(n+1)] over all counts and three streams gives the stated lifetime allowance. The one-stream executable fixture instead uses α/[n(n+1)]; it cannot spend that full allowance independently on three unknown coordinates.

The binomial calculation is unconditional on reaching a particular count. Stopped acquired histories are prefixes of the stipulated fresh stream. Conditioning the analysis on solvent survivors would generally change that distribution. All-count coverage permits funded adaptive inspections without restarting the error allowance. It does not permit hidden outcome-based filtering or a change in the observation law.

The signed efficacy bound ℓ_z=2L_z−1 requires the declared calibration between that operational record and Q. Requiring ℓ_z>0 makes multiplication by the nonnegative lower estimates a valid lower bound for the full score. Multiplication without this gate is not generally monotone in the required direction. T4's shrinking grid and polynomially allocated error probabilities support consistency under the stated stationary Bernoulli law. Eventual almost-sure certification and a 1−α bound on ever falsely certifying are different assurances.

**Finite zero-error obstruction.** T5 is valid after the author made its finite/countable-transcript domain explicit. If every finite accepting transcript possible under a helpful world is also possible under a harmful one, a nontrivial finite deployment cannot have zero harmful-world probability. Adaptive stopping does not remove that overlap. A generalization to continuous records would require an appropriate finite-history absolute-continuity condition; atomwise positive-probability language alone would not suffice there.

**Choice versus outcomes.** When Q is a deterministic aggregate efficacy, its recipient-outcome statement follows under that model. When Q is a conditional mean effect, the certificate concerns the model score and expected benefit. It does not guarantee improvement for each individual recipient. The notes retain this distinction and do not promote a confidence bound into a guarantee against all harms.

## 3. Independent executable check

The [independent checker](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/transfer-evidence/independent_check.py) imports no producer module. It directly enumerates every acquired binary outcome word, ending each word at the first unfundable observation or the fixed horizon. It carries common fuel, source, paid count, loss count and the exact order-dependent EWMA numerator. Its certificate calculation multiplies integer probability polynomials and inverts count cutoffs; the producer uses binomial sums.

This differs from the producer's count/resource dynamic program and weighted-subset pruning. All five complete reported case dictionaries match exactly, including every endpoint, policy probability, stopped-history statistic and lifetime false-certificate probability. The [saved independent record](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/transfer-evidence/independent-check.json) covers:

- **209,495 acquired outcome words**, including failed and resource-stopped histories;
- **680 fixed-count coverage cases**: n=1,…,20 and true probabilities 0,1/16,…,1, under both one-stream and three-stream allowance splits, with monotonicity in observed success count;
- terminal-action payment and outcome ledgers, exact mass conservation, and the identical acquisition laws but different action resources at initial reserves sixteen and seventeen;
- the paid sixteen-ones/five-zeros history and the positive-probability finite certificate at the true-score boundary.

The three-stream coverage cases preserve and supersede the mathematical author's 340 matching scratch calculations. They test the finite bound, not a complete three-stream physical implementation or a proof of the infinite theorem. Producer, protocol, README, upstream transition, saved producer results and checker hashes were frozen and verified during the independent run.

The source code reuses the unchanged upstream paid-prediction transition for acquisition and adds a separate terminal action that spends one available common unit. Return from that action is zero. No previous fuel reserve is reinstated, no stopped history receives a target, and a later update is not credited to an earlier decision. The finite circuit and its operation/write/computation price are declared local premises; counter capacity and table availability are not a derivation of hardware formation.

## 4. Results that limit the conclusion

| Tested boundary | Independent result | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Sixteen funded observations, β=1/2 | Delivered-action probabilities: EWMA 29271/32768; raw count 63019/65536; certificate 697/65536 | Confidence control is very conservative here; it does not maximize recipient benefit. |
| Initial reserve sixteen versus seventeen | Same certificate preference 1/256; delivery respectively 0 and 1/256 | An additional actual action unit matters even when acquired evidence is identical. |
| β=1/8, reserve three | Raw-count action 169/512; EWMA and certificate action zero | A randomly stopped sample average cannot substitute for an exact target. |
| True-score boundary β=1/4, funded | Terminal false-threshold certificate probability 16249/4294967296 | Finite positive-confidence certification is not zero-error identification. |
| Sixteen ones followed by five zeros | Fuel eleven, source thirty-two, lower bound 5/16, EWMA below 1/4 | Accumulation and constant gain respond differently; a changed generating law invalidates old stationary coverage. |

Every delivered repair physically helps the terminal recipient in the finite fixture because efficacy is supplied as one. Consequently, a false score certificate is not an example of a harmful repair, and withholding uncertified repair leaves the recipient's adverse outcome. At β=1/2 the certificate's expected terminal outcome is about 3.96, compared with about 0.154 for raw count. The integration correctly preserves this cost of caution. Absence of a statistical certificate does not establish absence of a stake or permission to ignore a recipient.

Likewise, the paid negative word can occur under the stationary law or after a change to β=0. Under the first interpretation it demonstrates a finite EWMA threshold failure despite adequate funding. Under the second it demonstrates a stale certificate outside its assumptions. It is not a counterexample to the stationary coverage theorem.

## 5. Composition and acceptance boundary

The exact count/resource projection does not preserve the original EWMA's internal state or commands on each history. Different orderings with the same counts can have different EWMA values. The producer reconstructs that policy's conditional frequencies statistically, and the independent word enumeration verifies them. This is a finite distributional calculation, not an exact controlled-history refinement or a Lean-certified end-to-end composition. The other branch's controller-refinement results must not be credited automatically to this projection.

The accepted result supplies a reliability contract for a downstream premise. It still assumes the relevant observation class and calibration, meaningful correspondence evidence, shared evaluative routing, protected retained records, an updater, and usable resources. Its primary executable comparison assumes g=z=1; the general signed three-coordinate theorem is analytical. It does not establish a new acquired correspondence assay or explain why an identity-gated evaluator becomes shared.

Infinite accumulation additionally needs growing sufficient records, their maintained substrate and continuing work. A finite register sized for sixteen observations cannot supply that premise. Succession must preserve the records, model provenance and lifetime error allowance; repeated rereading or resetting α cannot create fresh evidence or a renewed guarantee. Drift, masking and heterogeneous recipients can invalidate calibration even while the arithmetic remains correct. Probe-induced harms retain their separate ledger and are not erased by a correct posterior conclusion.

The next genuine bridge is to implement the observation/calibration and retained-evidence roles using actually formed mechanisms, then compose their acquisition, maintenance, updates and action costs in one continuing process. Changing dependencies require paid detection and correction with explicit delay and residual risk. Shared evaluation, warranted recognition beyond private usefulness and justified ethical reasons remain further derivation obligations. No blocking error remains in the accepted conditional results; the unified foundation remains ongoing.
