## Named connection and result

This contribution addresses training consequences associated with represented recipient affect → subsequent target-directed choice. It advances empirical discrimination under completion requirement 5 and constrains the real-system interpretation of the acquired evaluator under requirements 2–3. It adds no new requirement and does not replace the central causal/ethical join.

The fixed primary estimate among 333 author-eligible participants is +0.4979466 percentage points, with an approximate participant-level 95% interval [−4.2459882, +5.2418814]. Three prespecified computational sensitivities retain intervals spanning zero. No meaningful equivalence margin was set; no absence, equivalence or favorable-direction conclusion follows. The primary remains unchanged after observing the estimates.

The four assignment cells and both component differences remain visible. Timeout means no target-liked selection, not an observed target-disliked choice. The personally optimal reversal comparison requires the measured response adjustment. The [identification note](IDENTIFICATION.md) proves that identity and gives an observationally equivalent value/expectancy pair. It is an illustrative mathematical counterexample, not an empirical fit or a Lean theorem.

## What remains supplied or unidentified

Adult cognitive capacities, prior valuations, the trained target representation and the experiment's incentive instructions are supplied. Subjective private-payoff expectancy is unmeasured. Choice about a represented recipient is not independently verified recipient benefit. The experiment lacks a neutral unpaired training baseline establishing value from zero, and it does not test transfer to a new target. Post-choice awareness does not define a randomized mediator intervention. No new awareness subgroup outcome analysis, GLMM reproduction or learned psychological mechanism is claimed.

The consequence of this result is a narrower evidence claim for Experiment 3, not rejection of all acquired evaluation or acceptance of its unique mechanism. The explicit value/expectancy ambiguity would change if additional independently justified measurements or interventions excluded one account. A stronger connected foundation must then explain how the operative evaluator forms, how its target interpretation is justified, and how it remains effective and corrigible under actual resources and succession. Existing ethical premises and the five completion conditions are unchanged.

After the evaluator's acceptance in commit `23148d6`, the [canonical report](../../../../theories/becoming/history/REWARD-LEARNING-AND-RECIPIENT-CHOICE.md#one-discriminating-contrast-that-would-change-the-account) makes one future discriminator explicit: contingent versus yoked training followed by a new-recipient choice under characterized personal payoff, factual meanings and relief conditions. Its inference requires independently justified expectancy/model bounds and does not uniquely exclude all latent mechanisms. It remains unrun. This aligns the empirical result with the accepted finite evaluator and its still-supplied transfer recipe, without adding an evidence-collection project merely to enlarge the archive. Actual successor access and fresh efficacy correction remain the central causal continuation; the human evidence does not itself implement those interfaces.

Canonical maps describe this as accepted retrospective empirical computation and bounded identification. They must not turn its uncertainty into a positive causal value-acquisition finding, infer no learning, or count the preserved study and reanalysis as independent datasets.
