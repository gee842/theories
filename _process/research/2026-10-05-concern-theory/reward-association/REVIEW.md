The sole primary standardized training contrast in liked responses per 32 offered decisions is **0.00497947**, or **+0.50 percentage points**, with an approximate participant-level 95% interval **[−4.25, +5.24] points**. Its current-congruent and current-incongruent components are −0.38 and +1.37 points. The three fixed sensitivities also have intervals containing zero: all saved respondents −0.31 points, answered-trial denominator +0.34, and RT-qualified offered-trial denominator +1.47.

These results do not establish a reliably positive transfer effect at this scope. They also do not establish equivalence or no learning: no meaningful equivalence margin was declared, and the intervals retain both signs. The sensitivity family remains descriptive and individually nominal. Neither a stronger unplanned subgroup nor another outcome has replaced the primary result.

The four assigned cells supply independent participant means; repeated trials do not inflate the sample size. The two current-contingency strata have equal weights even though their cell sizes differ. Missing responses are no liked selection, not disliked selection. Personally optimal responding is `L` when current rewards favor liked choices and `R−L` otherwise. Direct participant optimal summaries give a +0.43-point unchanged-minus-reversed contrast; the declared response-rate adjustment explains its difference from +0.50. The independent exact identity holds in all four analyses. This is not reproduction of the paper's mixed-logistic coefficient or its inference.

## Identification and foundation scope

The current task has independently assigned training and decision conditions and a shuffled fixed 24/8 feedback schedule. The analysis preserves those source meanings. The author-eligible sample is selected using post-assignment completion, missed responses and attention. Random assignment therefore does not automatically identify the full randomized population effect in this selected sample. The all-saved sensitivity cannot restore unsaved and unobserved participants.

The identification note's counterexample is sound within its declared model class: a training-dependent target-value term can be offset by a changed, unobserved private-payoff expectation while retaining the same conditional choice law. Holding the response process and recorded feedback law fixed then yields identical finite task histories. Including post-task questionnaires in the equivalence additionally requires a common conditional reporting law given those observable histories; it does not establish equivalence under every independently validated instrument measuring the latent terms. The bounded-expectancy construction states the needed interior slack. This is a nonidentification result, not evidence that either particular mechanism operated. Actual reward records do not uniquely specify subjective beliefs or their updater.

The reporting-law qualification was explicitly added after the numerical results during final review. The [original pre-output note](development/IDENTIFICATION-before-reporting-clarification.md) is preserved at its frozen hash. The checker verifies that original against the pre-output receipt and separately binds the final note; it does not retroactively change what was reviewed prospectively. The [final prose receipt](review-receipt.json) binds those accepted versions. This collaborating-AI check is internal review, not external peer review.

The task concerns a represented, already familiar target across new scenarios. It does not establish new-recipient transfer, the emergence of evaluation from zero, durable concern, felt valence, or a retained physical learning apparatus. The own lottery and recipient gift were stated incentives; no identified fulfillment ledger verifies actual delivery. Post-task awareness is a descriptive variable, not a randomized awareness intervention or a valid way to rescue a primary causal claim.

Congruent and incongruent training can associate reward with opposite target outcomes. Thus even an identified transfer mechanism would not establish favorable direction or ethical standing. The empirical checkpoint constrains how the acquired-evaluation-to-choice bridge may be described: this chosen full-sample contrast remains uncertain, and the value-versus-belief distinction remains open. The engineered evaluative-uptake model's equal-factual-information comparison, patient-good premises and justified action claims retain their separate conditional scope. The next scientific obligation is a discriminating account or intervention for those remaining mechanisms, not promotion of the present uncertain contrast into a completed derivation.

## Review of the unrun discriminator and parallel-result alignment

The final canonical text specifies one future randomized contrast: contingent target-state reward training versus yoked noncontingent training, followed by choices concerning an untrained recipient. Matching reward and target-state marginals, validating factual meanings and using new perceptual materials makes this a concrete proposed test of transfer beyond the trained identity and cues. It is explicitly unrun and is not an additional frozen analysis of these data.

Its identification conditions are necessary to the stated inference. Equal objective payoffs do not guarantee equal subjective expectations; bounded expectation differences also require bounded payoff sensitivity and a fixed or bounded alternative decision law. Unrestricted demand, attention or baseline terms could otherwise explain any result. An effect exceeding a prespecified restricted rival's bound would challenge that rival, not uniquely identify concern across every latent-mechanism class. Abstract cue generalization and anticipated personal relief remain explicit alternatives where not excluded. A precise null would constrain this transfer route, not all learned concern. No source, estimator or outcome result was changed to add this prospective discriminator.

The summary of the separately accepted finite evaluator matches its canonical scope: paid acquisition from a zero register, retained causal influence and two selected actions, with transfer sign and scale supplied. This review does not revalidate that other package's experiments or proofs. Cross-target transfer within a human learner is distinct from the next actual-successor and fresh-efficacy connections. The new paragraph preserves that boundary and does not count the proposed experiment as executed evidence or as completion of those connections.
