# How another being comes to matter

We can establish a **complete conditional mechanism** and use it as the foundation of a broader theory. We have not yet established a complete empirical theory of every form of concern.

The central idea is that another’s condition acquires motivational significance within a process when it changes what a process treats as worth doing. Concern specifically counts the other’s benefit in favor of action and their harm against it, apart from private consequences. A useful theory must explain that orientation, its correction, and its continuing support.

The new [theory](THEORY.md) follows six connected achievements:

1. **Learn significance.** An already significant experience teaches an action-value association; its learned coefficient can start at zero.
2. **Recognize another's stakes.** Evidence connects the condition to someone differently situated.
3. **Transfer value into choice.** A shared evaluation route lets that recognition affect action. Knowing alone does not ensure this transfer.
4. **Learn what helps.** Outcomes for the recipient correct the intervention, even when correction means stopping an intended kindness.
5. **Widen justified concern.** Relevant reasons travel beyond familiar identities, while differences in needs remain visible.
6. **Renew the capacity.** Resources, memory, criticism, effective authority, and succession keep concern able to act and learn.

The mathematical advance is that concern is no longer a positive constant inserted into the model. Both learned significance and recognition begin at zero. Their product can cross an exact protective-action threshold. A second result joins this acquisition to efficacy learning, actual costs, forgetting, and lossy transmission: it gives exact conditions for eventual protective preference at every phase of later generations.

The limits are equally informative. The same training can fit both broadly shared and identity-gated concern. Learning can optimize concealment instead of benefit. Accurate concern can go unfunded. A mature practice can fail immediately after succession. These are separate failure mechanisms with different remedies.

The theory does assume primitive significance, a learnable architecture, access to warranted evidence, and feasible support. It explains what those conditions generate; it does not prove that all natural processes acquire them. Someone's moral standing is also not created by the observer's ability to recognize it, and it does not depend on their productive usefulness.

**The proposed root of wisdom:** let the conditions of those affected change your understanding and conduct, keep that responsiveness open to correction, and renew the means through which it can remain effective.
