## Why the general result holds

The strengthened invariant separates truth, provenance and completion. Known normalized entries agree with the fixed orientation. Working route bits have the corresponding command meaning. During a nonzero reason phase, the reader is complete, each normalized entry is unknown or the signed interpretation of its current cache address, and the working mask equals the completed path-prefix fold. A true current flag entails the entire current-publication structure. Activation and cold turnover clear the relevant computation; any reader operation reopens phase zero and invalidates currentness.

Consequently the publication equals closure of the normalized evidence actually retained by the inference events. It need not equal closure of every informative cache cell. The checked ordinary trace that pays ten reason units while withholding all three accesses correctly publishes only the implicit supplied root. This is a substantive limit on completeness, not a counterexample to soundness.

`PublicationBridge` discharges the archive-fact premise using an actual enabled history from the empty recipient archive and its ready producer, then composes with either an unread reader or the actual paid factual word. It does not assume the older `Assayed` truth vector. Material formation and physical backing remain inherited conditional interfaces; their entire stochastic execution is not newly formalized as a single Python-to-Lean refinement.

The cost proof counts **publish events**, even if later invalidated. The progress potential is zero before reading, fourteen after a complete factual read, and twenty-four immediately before publication. Every enabled event satisfies `credit(next) + 24·publish ≤ credit(current) + charge`. Resetting can discard progress; it cannot manufacture it. Thus arbitrary admitted histories satisfy `24N ≤ spent` from unread initialization, or `24N ≤ spent + 14` at a genuine completed handoff. With reader six and reserve `F−94`, the inherited ledger gives `F ≥ 74+24N`. This is a necessary bound for this protocol's tariff, not a universal price of reasoning or a claim that any sufficient aggregate budget ensures completion.

## Counterexamples and nonclaims preserved

- **Fabricated progress:** a sound archive/cache and backed workspace with forged phase eight and a wrong working mask can publish falsely for no additional cost. The Lean negative theorem and executable trace preserve this failure. Authentic initialized reachability is necessary for the positive argument.
- **Fabricated cache:** a genuine archive plus forged complete cache can pass the ordinary paid reason sequence and publish a wrong route. The missing premise is initial cache agreement, not sufficient payment.
- **Consistent corruption:** the preserved action-tag fault can yield paid, internally agreeing but wrong evidence. Thirty-six actual boundary occurrences violate `Facts`; the other 1,296 satisfy it, sometimes vacuously. These labels do not certify physical channel calibration. Later parent consumer faults are separately marked and are not hidden inside an immutable-archive event law.
- **Stranded resources:** a genuine partial reader with fuel three, reserve twenty-eight and two completed reads cannot afford the next four-unit read; funding is disabled until completion. Its complete graph has twenty-one states and eighty-two edges, with no publication. This refutes sufficiency of the aggregate thirty-one units under the current transfer rule.
- **No liveness:** free context/reset loops can continue without publication. Safety and a necessary resource bound imply neither termination nor fair access.
- **Fixed semantic premises:** target changes and objective changes are absent from this kernel. Reinterpreting a correct route under the opposite target defeats its meaning. Choosing false `G` defeats even the implicit root; this is a logical premise countermodel, not a simulated valuation experiment.

Two actual implementation boundaries are especially important. The frozen Python `reason_cycle` helper lacks the Lean phase-zero activation guard: a direct second call spends another ten units and republishes without a fourteen-unit reread. The preserved before/after receipt and inspected source demonstrate why the general Lean count bound cannot be asserted for every Python call sequence. The normal driver inserts cold rereading, so its accepted scheduled results are unaffected. The Python incomplete-cache control also transfers twenty-eight reserve units into reader fuel three where Lean `fund` is disabled. Neither discrepancy was fixed by silently changing the parent.

The boundary projection is correspondingly limited. Lean's empty mask retains an implicit root whereas the initial/cold Python mask explicitly has no root bit; the numerical mapping compares the two command bits and keeps `G` as a separate premise. Python does not retain all formal reader fields, so their values are reconstructed from actual paid prefixes. Exact world/archive hashes establish physical continuity, not equality of every runtime field.
