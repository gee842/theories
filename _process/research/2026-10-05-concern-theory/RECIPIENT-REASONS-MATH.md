The transition is **actual retained consequence evidence → paid interpretation

## 1. Signed facts about one binary target

At address `i`, the actual paid cache contains `None` or `Some b`. Its
prebound designated orientation is `d_i`, with `(d_0,d_1,d_2)=(0,1,0)`.
The preceding actual-acquisition/archive theorem proves, under its fixed
sound target law,

\[
 \operatorname{Some}(b)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b=[Z=d_i].
\]

A false factual certificate is useful: it came from a positive physical
receipt identifying the opposite action. It is not a physical zero receipt
misread as a negative orientation claim.

Define signed normalization by

\[
 N_d(\operatorname{None})=\operatorname{None},\qquad
 N_d(\operatorname{Some}(b))=
 \operatorname{Some}\bigl(b?d:\neg d\bigr).
\]

**R1 — general finite evidence theorem.** For any finite list of sound signed
facts about the same binary `Z`, every known normalized value equals `Z`.
Consequently all known values agree; a list with at least one known value
identifies `Z`; it cannot contain both normalized zero and normalized one.

*Proof.* For each observed fact, substitute `b=[Z=d]`. The four possibilities
for `(Z,d)` give the same normalized value `Z`. Mapping over the list preserves
this property. Any selected first known value therefore equals `Z`; two
known values equal each other. Their simultaneous equality to false and true
is impossible. The arbitrary-list theorem is separate from the finite
three-entry hardware implementation and carries no constant-cost claim.

The implemented three paid inference reads store the normalized values in
the new reason workspace. Unknowns remain unknown. Agreement on `z` produces
only the two derived route certificates

\[
 [\operatorname{Some}(\neg z),\operatorname{Some}(z),\operatorname{None}].
\]

No known value produces three unknown certificates. Disagreement also
produces three unknown certificates but sets a distinct conflict flag.
Conflict is impossible for sound fixed-law inputs. Corruption can create a
visible disagreement or a consistently wrong result; unanimity alone does
not verify calibration or integrity. These operations are inference from
existing observations, not additional independent observations.

## 2. Relevance follows the receipt law; the objective does not

For the existing no-false-positive binary receipt law, define

\[
 C(a,Z,V)=V[a=Z],\qquad
 \operatorname{Compatible}(Z,a)\iff\forall V,\ C(a,Z,V)=V.
\]

Compatibility is equivalent to `a=Z`. It means that the action matches this
recipient law whenever an opportunity is available, not that availability
or an affordable next action is guaranteed.

Let `G` be an explicitly supplied recipient-support objective. The meanings
of the three nodes are

\[
 R(\mathrm{root})=G,\quad
 R(p)=G\land\operatorname{Compatible}(Z,0),\quad
 R(q)=G\land\operatorname{Compatible}(Z,1).
\]

Use the fixed star `root→p`, `root→q`, `root→p`; its third certificate remains
unknown. A positive derived first or second certificate identifies exactly
the compatible command. Therefore relevance from the supplied root to that
route is proved from the actual receipt law. It is not an arbitrary premise
that an orientation bit is some unrelated reason edge.

**R2 — instrumental closure.** Sound informative signed evidence gives exactly
the compatible route. All-unknown evidence leaves only the supplied root.
The existing `closure_exact` and `closure_semantic_sound` then establish the
corresponding instrumental meaning for every admitted node.

The star has no new multi-hop dependency. Repeated observations of one
orientation provide redundant observational support, not independent ethical
grounds or a second reason path. Payment and compatibility do not prove `G`,
moral standing, or that a generated objective is worth pursuing.

## 3. The actual paid consumer

The continuing state retains the whole `PaidArchiveReader` state and hence
the whole archive, actor/recipient accounts, pending records, source and
protected stores. The factual cache is borrowed, not copied. One newly formed
A/H pair backs the declared finite reason recipe: three normalized values,
conflict flag, graph/control state, working mask and publication. Its

A completed current factual cache is required before reason activation.

root. Three separately paid inference reads spend one each. The unchanged

redundant on this star. Publication is allowed only after all three inferences
and all four checks. Thus this consumer costs ten. This is its declared
implementation price, not a universal lower bound on reasoning.

Every factual-reader reopening invalidates current reasoning, even when its
context tag is unchanged. The same finite interlock handles context change.
No free unbounded generation counter is installed. Historical bytes or
partial masks do not constitute a current completed publication. The law
keeps every spent unit and unfinished state on an unaffordable path.
