## Semantic boundary

Under a stable binary target and no false-positive capture, a present positive
receipt for action a identifies that orientation. For a prebound predicate
`Z=d`, it supports `Some(a=d)`. A positive opposite-action receipt can therefore
support `Some false`; zero itself cannot. Incomplete, absent, stale and invalid
frames remain unknown. The runtime reader uses the actual cell values and
paid context token, not the observer's hidden target.

The three predicates are `Z=0, Z=1, Z=0`. They concern **one** recipient
orientation, so the complete truth vector is101 or010; they are not three
independent edge facts, two recipients or two observed contexts. Query and
target identity are supplied by fixed physical addresses and a bounded pointer;
the four payload bits do not secretly store arbitrary provenance strings.
Undetectable relabeling or a false channel lies outside the soundness premise.

The [parallel reason consumer](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/PROTOCOL.md)
already uses partial certificates in its reasoning layer. Its original physical
Bool archive and total `Assayed` premise do not directly accept these frames.
A compatible handoff still needs an explicit factual-predicate/edge mapping,
positive anchor, relevance rule and paid reason operations. A true orientation
certificate does not establish future opportunity, need, ethical standing or
benefit from every action. Success-only logical identification is not an
unbiased estimate of success frequency.

The local recipes for cells, protected routing, bounded metadata/cache,
calibrated context sensing, invalidation and finite completion remain supplied.
The construction neither proves their evolutionary formation/retention nor
infinite durability. These are precise remaining premises, not reasons to deny
the completed finite event-to-record interface. The [integration](RECIPIENT-RECORDING-INTEGRATION.md)
correctly names paid certificate consumption by the reason mechanism as the
next downstream connection and keeps evidence-route transmission separate.
