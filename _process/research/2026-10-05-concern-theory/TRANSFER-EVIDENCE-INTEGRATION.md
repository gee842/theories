## The premise this work addresses

The [earlier paid-teaching construction](../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md) produces an operational teaching signal from measured resource consequences. It also shows that an expected learned coefficient above a choice threshold need not put individual histories above that threshold. The [concern dynamics](dynamics-formal.md) use exact repeated teaching targets and an exact efficacy assay to prove eventual all-date choice and immediate suspension after an adverse assay. A noisy physical producer cannot supply those conclusions merely by matching the teacher's expectation.

The [new mathematical note](TRANSFER-EVIDENCE-MATH.md) gives the missing reliability conditions. It reuses the programme's [earlier sequential-evidence methods](../../../experiments/history/2026-10-05-general-framework/reproduction-test/adaptive-evidence/FORMAL.md); accumulating evidence and lifetime error control are not new statistical discoveries here.

## What changes in the derivation

First, constant-gain learning with independent noisy targets that can be zero loses every positive coefficient margin infinitely often almost surely, even when all observations and updates are funded. When private benefit alone is insufficient, the corresponding concern decision fails infinitely often too. This is a distinct obstruction from resource exhaustion or unpaid rehearsal gaps.

Second, retained sample averages can supply an eventual strict choice margin under stationary calibrated evidence, a strict true-model margin, continuing paid sampling and preserved sufficient records. This is a statement about the actual sequence of decisions outside a probability-zero set. It gives neither a deterministic learning deadline nor a guarantee for every recipient outcome.

Third, finite binomial lower bounds can certify the signed joint score at adaptively chosen funded decision dates with a declared lifetime error allowance. The efficacy coordinate permits harmful interventions. The lower-efficacy bound must be positive before the product of lower bounds is used. A finite positive-confidence decision is not a zero-error conclusion: overlapping helpful and harmful observation laws rule out that stronger inference.

The [executable interface](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/transfer-evidence/README.md) is a bounded comparison using the actual preceding paid-prediction law. Its one-unknown-coordinate fixture keeps correspondence and repair efficacy supplied. It therefore checks how acquired operational teaching reaches a later paid decision; it does not demonstrate acquisition of the entire three-coordinate architecture.

## Finite results and their interpretation

All three policies receive the same actual resource-loss observations. The original constant-gain estimate, the acquired count average and the finite confidence bound then enter separate next-action decisions. Each repair must pay one further common-fuel unit and changes the supplied terminal recipient outcome from four to zero. The source, spent training costs and failed histories remain in the account.

With sixteen opportunities, mean target one half and initial common reserve thirty-three, repair is delivered with probabilities `29271/32768` under the original update, `63019/65536` under the count average, and `697/65536` under the confidence gate. The last rule is highly conservative at this horizon: controlling erroneous score claims does not maximize recipient benefit. Here repair has supplied perfect efficacy, so withholding an uncertified repair still leaves the recipient's adverse outcome. A missing certificate does not establish absence of a stake or an ethical permission to ignore it.

Initial reserves sixteen and seventeen produce identical acquired target histories and paid-count distributions; their remaining common stocks differ by one unit. Both give confidence-gated preference probability `1/256`, but only the latter can deliver those repairs: the former has spent its final common unit before the decision. A statistically valid record cannot finance its own use.

With mean target one eighth and initial reserve three, the first loss stops acquisition while leaving one action unit. A raw count average prompts repair with probability `169/512`, even though the true declared score is below the choice threshold. The original update and certificate do not cross it. This is a counterexample to treating a randomly stopped sample average as an exact downstream target; the repair itself remains physically beneficial under this fixture's supplied efficacy.

The paid history of sixteen one-targets followed by five zero-targets leaves eleven common units and thirty-two source units. The original estimate falls below one quarter, while the retained confidence bound remains `5/16`. That word is possible under the stationary model. It can also follow a change to a zero target, where the retained positive certificate is stale and its stationarity guarantee no longer applies. These are alternative generating laws, not contradictory interpretations of the same theorem.

## The retained boundary

The independent checker imports no producer module and enumerates 209,495 acquired outcome words. All five complete case dictionaries match, with 680 additional exact coverage cases and the paid negative witnesses. The [saved record](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/transfer-evidence/independent-check.json) includes source hashes. General probability claims rest on the mathematical proofs; finite enumeration neither proves the infinite results nor supplies their physical support. No new Lean theorem or empirical result is claimed by this study.

The count/resource projection omits order-dependent EWMA memory. Its policy frequencies are reconstructed and independently checked statistically. This is not an exact controlled-history refinement or proof that the construction branches already implement the same learner.

This work replaces repeated exact numerical teaching with explicit statistical and material conditions. It does not derive what a correspondence measurement means, why another's condition enters evaluation, the legitimacy of a reason, or the formation of the sensors and record-processing machinery. The operational teacher's interpretation as experienced valence also remains open. A correct coefficient can still enter an identity-gated or inverted action route.

The assumptions have different roles. An observation law and actual support are local physical inputs to identify. Reliable record retention and selection of a suitable update rule are organizational capacities to connect to the ongoing construction studies. Shared evaluation and justified recognition of other centers remain downstream explanatory tasks. These should not be collapsed into one unqualified claim that learning produces concern.
