## Review repairs

The final formulation incorporates the following changes:

1. It distinguishes mere evaluative significance from **concern's favorable orientation toward the recipient**. Hostility can also be responsive to another's condition.
2. It restricts mechanistic completeness to the declared specimen. Efficacy revision is implemented; the origin and revision of feature classes, shared routing, moral scope, and authority remain obligations of the wider architecture.
3. It distinguishes already significant primitive experience from the learned action-value association that begins at zero.
4. It labels the adult associative evidence as shifts in an already situated target valuation, rather than empirical proof of concern originating from zero.
5. It states the unrestricted-parameter domain for the identifiability theorem, the efficacy learning-rate domain, and bounded transmission fractions.
6. It separates recipient harm from resource expenditure and compensation. Paid probes require their own outcome accounting; compensation does not erase harm. The fixed-audit counterexample retains harms imposed by testing, and a separate gate stops deployment and testing after an exact nonpositive-efficacy warning.
7. It states that architecture and audit procedures are faithfully reinstalled at accounted cost in the succession specimen; their evolutionary or institutional reproduction is not thereby derived.
8. It distinguishes the masking example's private cue-relief channel from its recipient-effect channel, rather than treating masking as an ordinary failed repair.
9. It handles the zero-denominator edge of the harmful-action stopping bound and preserves the productive-revenue equality case where cumulative deficits can be finite.
