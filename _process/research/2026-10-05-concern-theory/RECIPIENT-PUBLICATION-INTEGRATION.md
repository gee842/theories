# Current recipient reasons under arbitrary enabled histories

6 October 2026. AI-authored extension of the accepted [recipient-reason construction](RECIPIENT-REASONS-INTEGRATION.md), following the [generative-grounding goal](../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The named connection is **initialized actual evidence and reasoning machinery → trustworthy current publication despite interruption and restart → a usable contract for downstream action**.

**Status: accepted at the stated event-law scope after formal verification, finite exploration and separate internal review.** The [prospective protocol](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/publication-safety/PROTOCOL.md) was frozen before state-space exploration. Parent sources and accepted receipts remain unchanged. This extension studies the existing guarded event law; it does not install a new action, objective, observation channel or physical fault model.

## The premise being discharged

The accepted semantic proof followed a prescribed sequence that reads all three available cache entries and completes its four path checks. Its event law also permits interrupted reads, omitted inference access, cache reopening, context changes and cold resets. A later consumer should not have to assume that every preceding attempt followed the successful sequence merely because the publication flag is current.

The new theorem concerns every finite enabled history from an initialized consumer, backed by a valid recipient acquisition/recording history. Its invariant preserves the meaning of any published route, the information actually used and the payments actually made. It retains the cases in which a publication is withheld or correctly contains only the supplied root.

This is a generalization over histories of one declared mechanism. It advances compatible composition in the governing derivation, without establishing the adequacy of the entire generative foundation.

## Three different guarantees

**Semantic soundness.** A current published route implies compatibility of that command with the same recipient's fixed binary response law, relative to the supplied objective `G`. The reader's cache agreement and archive facts, sound normalized entries and sound working mask form the proved invariant. Unavailable access writes unknown, rather than inventing a negative fact. A reader event can invalidate use while leaving historically correct values in memory; under the fixed target those old values need not become false.

**Completed interpretation.** The published mask equals the rooted closure of the evidence actually accessed during the completed inference episode. It need not equal the closure of every fact in the full cache. Withholding both informative entries from a sound cache can yield a completed root-only publication. Truthfulness and completeness relative to all available information are distinct claims.

**Accounted publication.** Count actual publication events, rather than repeated inspections of a retained publication flag. The progress account assigns credit to completed paid reading and reason steps, consumes that credit at publication, and discards credit when work is reset. It yields a bound on the number of publications in an arbitrary enabled history, without first assuming that a known number of complete words was charged.

The old implementation charges factual reading fourteen units and reason computation ten. The proved bounds are `24N ≤ spent` from an unread initialized reader and `24N ≤ spent + 14` from an already completed factual handoff. The latter preserves the first factual read as inherited paid work. With common endowment `F ≥ 94`, the accepted handoff has reader six plus protected reserve `F−94`; combining the bound with its ledger again implies `F ≥ 74+24N`, now with `N` measured from actual publication events. Interrupted attempts may consume resources without producing a publication.

These tariffs belong to the declared event protocol, which requires renewed reading before another publication. They are not universal costs of reasoning or of acting repeatedly on an unchanged conclusion. The earlier cheaper lookup remains a valid comparator.

## Initialization, calibration and use cannot be omitted

A sound archive and available hardware alone do not initialize the computation. A fabricated state with phase eight and an incorrect working mask can satisfy the publication guard without having performed inference. The theorem must start from the actual initializer or an independently proved invariant, not merely a claimed completion flag.

The objective `G`, fixed target law and faithful recorded facts remain premises. A fully paid computation can publish a false instrumental interpretation if its supplied root is false or its evidence is consistently corrupted. Neither agreement nor expenditure supplies ethical justification. Changes to target meaning or objective require a further validity mechanism; they are not among this event law's physical transitions.

Cache/context reset guards protect the claim of a current completed computation. This does not show that every such guard is necessary for truth under a fixed target: an invalidated historical answer may remain correct. Likewise, arbitrarily many free context changes can prevent completion. A safety invariant must not be presented as a termination or fair-scheduling theorem.

The archive and underlying world are retained unchanged during this consumer's event histories. Concurrent new acquisition, changing physical targets, storage corruption and actuator interference need explicit further transitions and proofs. The finite model does not silently gain these capabilities by admitting arbitrary orderings of its existing events.

## The executable boundary is part of the result

The parent Python driver is a prescribed scheduler, whereas Lean defines an event protocol. They were never claimed to be fully equivalent. In particular, Python's callable `reason_cycle` helper has no phase-zero activation guard: direct repeated calls can reuse a completed cache without the intervening reread required by the Lean event law. The normal driver inserts cold reset and paid rereading. Python also transfers reserve in an incomplete-cache negative control where the Lean funding event is disabled.

The new finite checker reconstructs the guarded event kernel explicitly and binds its starting signatures to the accepted physical histories. It cannot certify arbitrary Python API call sequences by importing the parent scheduler. The helper counterexample is preserved as a limit of representation. The frozen parent driver and helpers are unchanged.

## Checked evidence and acceptance conditions

The [fresh formal build](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/publication-safety/formal/README.md) passes 37 new theorem audits and 370 inherited audits across seventeen modules. It includes the unchanged-law induction, an actual-archive initialization corollary, and a formal fabricated-publication counterexample. Only the disclosed standard Lean axioms are used. The [mathematical account](RECIPIENT-PUBLICATION-MATH.md) separates semantic, provenance and accounting premises.

The [finite kernel audit](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/publication-safety/README.md) retains all 666 inherited history references as 1,332 unread/handoff boundary occurrences, plus 768 declared synthetic inputs. Fifty physical signatures yield twenty-nine decoder graphs, with 68,536 reachable states and 302,972 transitions. These counts describe the declared finite union; repeated references are not independent empirical trials. There is no history-depth cutoff. Thirty-six actual boundary occurrences violate the stated factual-soundness premise; those cases retain their payment checks and explicit semantic failures. Passing the factual predicate, including vacuous cases with no known positive records, does not certify a physical channel's calibration.

The [independent checker and receipt](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/publication-safety/independent-check.json) reconstruct every actual boundary pair, the synthetic grid and all twenty-nine graph digests using a separately written event kernel, path-count computation and strongly connected component algorithm. It checks 3,958 inherited reader-prefix events and 300 physical decoder observations. The [scope review](RECIPIENT-PUBLICATION-REVIEW.md) accepts the result as a collaborating-AI internal review, not external peer review. Source, fixture, compiler and result hashes bind the claims to the inspected versions. The decoder quotient retains every original history reference and all kernel observations, including failures. The arbitrary-history theorem comes from Lean induction; the finite exploration does not establish it by itself.

An enabled counterexample from an initialized, sound, fixed-target state would refute the proposed invariant or reveal a translation error. A paid current publication unequal to its actually accessed closure would refute the completion claim. A publication-count path exceeding the budget would refute the progress argument. Each would require revising the proposed result while retaining the counterexample; restricting the schedule after the fact would not establish the intended all-enabled-history claim.

A contradiction involving corrupted calibration, an unproved initial state, changed target laws or unrestricted helper calls instead identifies the missing premise or a different process. It is still substantive evidence against the stronger unqualified claim. Neither kind of failure may be hidden by merging it into a favorable average.

## Relation to the full roadmap

This extension replaces the prescribed-schedule premise with a reachable-publication contract for the declared protocol. It supplies a stronger input to the parallel reason-to-action study: what the conclusion means, which accessed evidence supports it, and what payment is necessary before it can be published.

It does not fund another action at the reached actor-four/source-zero endpoint. It also does not form or justify `G`, revise the observation/learning route, establish legitimate authority, renew the hardware, or validate a cross-domain empirical mapping. Those remain coordinated parts of the common derivation. The next physical join carries the actual command conclusion into funded action for the same recipient; the generalizing work must continue to expose the laws and evaluative premises that make that action warranted.
