# Formed variants, paid mixed histories and the root: Lean scope

6 October 2026. AI-authored formal development of the next connection, **response formation → differential retention under one actual resource history**. The [formal package](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/README.md) verifies the deterministic event structure needed by that connection. It does not certify the separate expected-selection calculation.

## What is newly checked

The single-site law contains two damage-coupled formation reactions, type-preserving copying, paid association and repair, local fuel access, material wear and fuel leakage. There are no initial response variants or installed associations in the primary witness. Formation, copying and repair all compete within one continuing history.

The 53 new Lean declarations establish five substantive constraints:

1. Every valid finite path preserves total variant material and has exact total-fuel and common-fuel spending ledgers. Copies cannot exceed initial common, local and incident support together.
2. A strict natural-number rank bounds the number of events. Indefinite active renewal is impossible with these closed finite stocks, independently of stochastic waiting times.
3. Restoring the site from an absent, vacant, damaged start requires at least three common-fuel units. Restoring it while retaining the alternative type requires at least four: the second variant cannot be supplied free at a handoff.
4. A type cannot first appear without its own formation reaction, and copying requires an already existing template of the copied type. Under the explicit no-resident-wear restriction, the alternative cannot access local fuel or produce a local copy from the initial preparation.
5. A mixed paid witness and a repaired-site inheritance counterexample both inhabit the same declared event law and the existing causal root.

The mixed witness uses M=3, F=4, E=1 and follows **H formation → A formation → A association → repair → capture → A copying**. It ends with A=2 and H=1. The four common units pay both variants' formation, association and repair; the captured packet pays the copy. This actual trace strengthens a one-founder witness by retaining the alternative within the productive history. Path existence is distinct from the expected effect across all histories.

The counterexample uses M=2, F=5, E=1. After both types form, A associates and repairs, then wears. H associates with that same site, captures support and copies, ending with A=0 and H=2. Resident wear preserves the repaired site's condition and fuel as specified by the law. The alternative's inheritance is therefore not a reset or an externally awarded benefit. It shows why the no-resident-wear access invariant cannot be silently promoted to all histories.

## Exact integration and interpretation

The formal state is `(aFree,hFree,resident,intact,localFuel,F,E)` with one site and fixed material M. Site condition and local storage are Boolean; precursor is derived from remaining material. `FormationRoot.lean` proves an exact two-way correspondence with `Generative.Trace`. Resource constraints, bootstrap requirements and the mixed witness transport into the root, and the original root theorem supplies concatenation through the actual intermediate state.

The lower bounds come from invariant paid-stage potentials, not from an analyst's success score inserted into a transition. A particularly useful bound counts the three paid A stages together with the presence of H; its sum with remaining common fuel cannot increase. This isolates what a downstream mixed-variant comparison must actually inherit.

These proofs cover the sixteen baseline event labels and their guards/updates. They do not formalize propensities, stochastic calculus, expected frequency, numerical enclosures, or a Python-to-Lean refinement. They exclude added catalytic-consumption events and altered negative-control rules unless separately verified. The `SupportOwned` definition is a physical access invariant, not an ethical entitlement or a general claim that productive actors retain benefits.

Formation chemistry, two available types, efficacy differences, association specificity and template copying remain local premises. The work closes a concrete accounting and trace-composition interface; it does not explain the origin of this chemical repertoire or derive a mechanism that revises its own selector.
