Required comparisons distinguish different questions:

- Ordinary no-monitor versus the full monitor/revision policy: total intervention benefit, including all observation costs and changed state history.
- Monitor with no repair: effect of replacement beyond acquired diagnosis, preserving access to the same later grant/source.
- Equally paid same-route replacement and each of the four proposed routes: effect of physical rewiring, rather than expenditure or privileged resources.
- Exact conventional transition table: whether the construction adds predictive content beyond an ordinary finite controller with the same state and laws.
- Missing parts, missing/unknown diagnostic access and interrupted stages: actual affordability and material dependencies.
- Broken binding, false/stuck credit contact and operational-channel loss: reliable evidence versus false evidence or absent evidence.
- Source outages and target changes: behavior-class diagnosis versus calibration of the productive command and continued relevance.

## 5. Empirical mapping and the limits of existing evidence

The repository already contains relevant primary-source audits. [Ceroni's engineered burden-feedback study](PHYSICAL-EVIDENCE-CERONI.md) provides an example of physically sensed burden, costly regulatory machinery and burden relief. It does not demonstrate a controller diagnosing and replacing its own update algorithm. The [yeast stress-response and memory audit](ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-EVIDENCE.md) separates synthesized protection, persistent material, altered future responsiveness and selection among surviving pre-existing states. The [RNA-network evidence](PHYSICAL-EVIDENCE-RNA-NETWORK.md) concerns changing specificity and interactions under externally renewed populations and compartments. Neither population selection nor inherited recipe induction is evidence of the present within-carrier replacement mechanism. These sources constrain the proposal; they do not validate the joined chain.

A direct physical test would use an explicitly engineered finite controller and plant. Independently log recipient credit, pre-command and post-write registers, write completion, route-component identity, resource transfers, reconfiguration work, later commands and actual copying/use. The diagnostic must acquire a paid physical signal during the event, not read an experimenter's saved correctness field. Independently calibrate contact specificity, missing-event handling and target addressing before evaluating revision benefit. Measure observation and reconfiguration expense rather than assigning the abstract fuel numbers to a biological process.

The strongest causal tests preserve the same carrier and acquired record through a measured route change; clamp only the swap while matching information and provision; then test changes in subsequent updating and actual resource-funded succession. Follow all failed preparations and lineages to separate within-carrier change from preferential survival of pre-existing variants. Stable, outage, changed-target, cue/credit mismatch and broken-binding regimes distinguish the claimed mechanism from fixed feedback, passive carryover and externally selected controller variants.

These are proposed measurements, not a claim that the full assay has been performed or that every intervention is already technically available. No new biological calibration or empirical validation is added here. The remaining foundational premises include the head's functional recipe, calibrated observation, participant funding permission, fixed repertoire/proposal law, target reference, finite leases and schedule. The result can establish a conditional connected mechanism while leaving the origin and justification of its meta-level selection rule open.
