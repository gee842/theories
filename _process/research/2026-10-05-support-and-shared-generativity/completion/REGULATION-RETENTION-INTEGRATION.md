# First conditional derivation gate: completed scope and next interface

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration of the mathematical construction, executable verification, Lean development and separate internal review. This audits the written [first acceptance gate](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#first-acceptance-gate), not the five completion conditions for the whole foundation.

**Decision:** the conditional causal-loop milestone is met for the declared seven-event law. It explains damage → paid response formation → repair → accessible fuel → additional copying, with explicit conditions for benefit and failure. The next central task is to compose response formation and differential retention of competing variants under one continuing resource history, then derive variation and revision of the selection mechanism.

## Gate audit against the actual requirements

| Written requirement | Evidence and scope |
|---|---|
| Define organization and continuation independently of the response | The capture scaffold and continuation observables C and fresh captures are defined separately from G and copy count in the [mathematical law](ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-MATH.md). The experiment preserves both, and exposes their tradeoff. |
| Start without the successful regulator installed; declare formation and variation | G initially equals zero. Damage-catalyzed formation consumes precursor and usable fuel. The available reaction repertoire is fixed and stated; stochastic event histories vary, while variant invention is not claimed. |
| Account for sensing, construction, operation and replacement | D is the damaged site's actual catalytic availability; sensing is bundled into the paid encounter rather than read by a free external classifier. Every formation, repair and copy consumes fuel; wear returns precursor without fuel. This is a supplied coarse chemistry, not an identified molecular energy model. |
| Preserve actual successor conditions | Arbitrary-path Lean ledgers and trace composition retain state and expenditure. The executable break/restore interventions pass the full distribution through the disruption interval with no refill or restart. |
| Break and restore the dependency and signal | Capture and cue disruption/restoration are checked. Damage, wear and leakage accumulated during interruption remain in the successor state. |
| Disrupt response and retention with appropriate comparisons | No repair, equally priced ineffective repair, no copying, no formation, wrong cue, constitutive formation and source-burning arms distinguish causal links. Per-event prices and initial resources are matched; unequal realized spending remains an outcome. No copying still permits fresh fabrication, explicitly distinguished from templated retention. |
| Give success and failure conditions; preserve adverse outcomes | The positive copying comparison, reversed no-source comparison, low-fuel bootstrap trap and finite-stock event bound are established. Removing copying can improve repair and capture. The original network's failed regulator remains preserved. |
| Do not use the observer's assay as a hidden reward | Rates depend only on the declared material state and fixed coefficients. Copies, captures and site occupation are recorded outputs, not inputs to selection. |
| Give an explicit route toward changing learning mechanisms | Shared-pool neutrality shows the missing differential-return route. The [selection extension](CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md) gives a paid local-access witness and an exact criterion, with selector variation and its formation identified as the next derivation rather than assumed completed. |

The gate permits supplied physical laws and formation mechanisms. Requiring unique minimality, empirical universality, discovery of the entire repertoire or completed learning here would add requirements belonging to the wider foundation. This decision therefore closes a conditional milestone without declaring those further obligations fulfilled.

## The next result must join the actual processes

**Subsequent result, 6 October 2026:** the [formed-variant integration](FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md) meets this preparation obligation under a newly declared joint law. Both variants form from absence and can undergo mixed-population copying without a resource reset. This is not an exact kinetic composition of the two older laws. The historical gap below explains the construction's target; formation and revision of the selection mechanisms themselves remain open.

The formation law starts without G but has one supplied response type. The selection extension begins with two existing types, unformed associations and extra starter fuel. Its useful relative-retention effect is a valid separate conditional theorem. Its preparation is not yet an output of the first law.

The next construction must produce alternative response types, pay their formation costs, and carry the resulting states into competition under one law. It must retain common-resource neutrality, potential exploitation and work-induced losses alongside useful local return. A formal or computational adapter that merely installs the contest preparation would leave this explanatory gap intact.

After that, let access, sensing or copying mechanisms themselves vary and be reconstructed. Test a formerly useful dependency becoming unproductive, tracing how its consequences change those mechanisms. Only then can a result supply more of the learning and revision premises assumed by the existing concern and wisdom studies. The unified foundation remains ongoing.
