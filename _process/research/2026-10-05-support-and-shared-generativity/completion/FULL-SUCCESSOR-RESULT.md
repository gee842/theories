Let tau be first entry and sigma the first such complete turnover. Two previously declared events are evaluated from the same original preparation and at the same absolute H10:

- Complete turnover: sigma≤10.
- Reserve-restoring complete turnover: sigma≤10 and F_sigma≥F_tau.

The second predicate concerns the first complete turnover. A later richer visit after a poorer first successor fails this predicate and is not substituted for it. Both events include failed preparations and failed replacement in their denominator. The inherited reserve remains part of the actual entry state.

Classify each event by the gate at first entry, j=0 or1. These are endogenous history classes within one common reaction law, not different intervention arms or a free choice to begin with an installed organization. Terminal gate type is recorded separately in the floating diagnostics.

These opposing comparisons concern two different functions. They rule out a common probability ranking across these functions, but do not by themselves prove incomparable full capability regions at a single reliability: the two contrasts sit at different absolute probability levels. No broader protocol class or useful-type prevalence follows from the history-class comparison.

## What this closes and what it leaves open

The former missing interface—complete formation followed by actual first replacement across its entrance ensemble—now has a common stochastic construction and a bounded finite-horizon calculation. It retains types, reserves, timing and all failure histories. The result concerns replacement of functional carriers while conserving/reusing their material, rather than loss of all original atoms.

Restoring F is not restoring the external source or undoing waste production. The maintained source remains a boundary assumption. The old finite-source reversible certificate stopped at seven captures and does not automatically transfer to this unrestricted event. A reversible extension needs its own error analysis and may permit restart after boundaries that are absorbing here.

Neither event guarantees uninterrupted operation of every role before completion or continued operation afterward. Indefinitely repeated renewal, an identified attainable chemistry, a whole joint-capability order and spontaneous useful prevalence remain unproved. The earlier finite-lineage extinction limitation still applies. This model should now serve as a precise gain/failure reference, rather than be mistaken for evidence of reliable universal regeneration.

The physical-data branch supplies a different next step: [partial identification of a joint RNA function](RNA-PARTIAL-IDENTIFICATION.md). It separates a task-preserving microscopic ambiguity from missing joint information that changes the task, and provides an exact conditional conventional prediction. That task is net population replacement with retained association, not renewal of every original carrier or the supplied apparatus. Moving to it requires an explicit bridge, independent calibration and a matched outcome test, rather than reuse of the word renewal.
