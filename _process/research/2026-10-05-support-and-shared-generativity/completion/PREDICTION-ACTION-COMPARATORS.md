# Comparing acquired prediction and its paid use

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparison contract for **formed, revisable machinery → acquired consequence-based prediction → correction after a dependency change → paid action within the inherited material and support history**. This advances the next connection named by the [updater integration](UPDATER-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md), using the [paid teaching contract](CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md) and [controlled-refinement result](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md). It is a design and interpretation note until the companion implementation reports its exact law and checks; no numerical advantage is asserted here.

The comparison must distinguish four achievements: acquisition of an informative consequence record, persistent predictive change, revision when that relation changes, and causal use of the prediction in an affordable action. A favorable consequence or an altered response by itself establishes neither retained prediction nor that prediction's use.

## 1. The target and the learner's actual information

Define the predicted event before defining a score. Suitable targets include delivery of a support packet on a specified future operation or failure to meet the actual prepayment requirement for a specified continuation. These are different targets. A decline in a fuel count does not automatically mean the continuation failed; formed parts, accessible source and action order also matter.

A forecast must name its action, future opportunity and reference condition. An old receipt can establish that support was delivered previously while being irrelevant to whether the now-consumed source can supply another operation. Changing the reference silently can make a correct stored prediction appear wrong, or an obsolete record appear current. Preserve the receipt/reference distinction already demonstrated by the paid teaching study.

List the complete input to the update, including actual action, acquired return, accessible stock or wear variables, prior memory, and any elapsed-time information. An internal physical coupling is an input even if it is not called a sensor. The learner must not receive a hidden regime, the correct response label, a reversal notification, a counterfactual outcome or an observer-computed causal contrast. State any known plant model, calibrated noise rate and prior; model knowledge is also supplied information.

In a deterministic binary plant, an action plus its observed consequence may identify a hidden condition through a declared decoder. With unreliable capture, a failure may instead result from an unavailable packet. Do not retain a noiseless decoder as an exact teacher merely by calling the readout consequence-based. The [teaching identifiability criterion](CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-MATH.md#2-exact-acquisition-and-the-stronger-paid-tree-condition) requires the target to be determined by the accessible record for an exact label; otherwise the appropriate object is a conditional prediction with residual uncertainty.

Prediction of the chosen action's observed return is not identification of the unchosen action's counterfactual return. That additional claim requires a justified structural relationship, paid interventions or separately identified evidence. The action-routing rule that uses a forecast remains another implemented coupling; prediction accuracy alone does not select its sign.

## 2. Competitive controls and architectural references

The numerical study should specify a small strong set before inspecting results.

| Comparator | Function | Matching obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Exact conventional predictor/controller | Represents the same record, memory update, predictor and action route as an explicit finite controller. | Equal initial state, observation law, transition probabilities, memory precision, costs, failure channels and source history. Complete recorded distributions must agree. |
| Conventional fixed Fast/Slow predictors | Fast replaces the register from the latest acquired teaching record; Slow moves one register step toward that record. Both use the same declared forecast and action decoder. | Same observations, register precision, write/read opportunities and hardware costs. Fixed rule identity does not remove wear, assembly or renewal costs. |
| Frozen-updater predictor | Holds update-rule identity fixed while allowing ordinary paid predictor changes. This role is supplied by the fixed Fast/Slow arms in the chosen design. | Same updater hardware costs, expiry and reconstruction. It is different from freezing the predictor register itself. |
| Direct-record controller | Uses the most recently acquired record directly rather than inserting an additional learned coefficient before action. | No fresher record, free probe or privileged plant state. Charge its actual record storage and action costs; a cheaper route remains a legitimate architectural comparison. |
| Fixed or no-memory response | Does not use acquired predictive memory. | Useful ablation, not the strongest conventional learning comparator. |

The exact emulator is the central test of an explicit mechanism, not an opponent expected to lose. A change of vocabulary cannot produce a performance advantage over an identical state-and-observation kernel.

A direct-record controller may use fewer writes or require less memory than a retained predictor. Do not force it to incur a pointless predictor-write expense merely to equalize spending. Instead report both its natural implementation and, if useful, a separately labeled price-matched intervention. Matching resources means the same endowment and available physical prices, not forcing identical realized expenditures after mechanisms diverge. If direct response succeeds with two prepaid units while probe → predictor write → first action needs three, that can expose an ordering cost of the proposed architecture.

Conversely, a baseline granted perfect history, an accurate latent-state model, arbitrary precision or a free clock is stronger than a one-register formed mechanism. Such a reference can be informative, but it is not resource-and-information matched unless those resources and assumptions are shared or priced. An oracle that reads the hidden environment is only a ceiling.

## 3. Interventions that identify different links

Keep the full source and material ledger in every arm, including unsuccessful assembly and measurements. Interventions start from stated shared physical states or common preparations. A laboratory intervention may be scheduled at the environmental change, but the schedule must not become information supplied to the learner.

| Intervention | What remains fixed | What it can establish or refute |
|---|---|---|
| Freeze predictor writes | Sensor, actual action/return and physical acquisition law remain active; use a charged sham write if isolating content. | Whether changing retained predictive content contributes beyond sensing and passive carryover. A separate cost-saving freeze tests a different combined intervention. |
| Erase the accessible record | The actual return still changes fuel and the real plant exactly as declared; replace only the selected information channel. | Whether the updater depends on that acquired distinction. Reserve, wear and direct maintenance couplings must also be audited: they may still carry the same information. |
| Invert or miscalibrate the record | Real capture and source debit are unchanged; only the acquired teaching content changes. | Whether the learner follows the readout and can be led into a wrong prediction. Physical repair and apparent signal improvement remain separate. |
| Freeze updater identity | The rule-bearing hardware still forms, wears and is renewed; predictor values can still update under that rule. | The contribution of revising the learning mechanism, distinct from ordinary predictor learning. |
| Clamp the stored prediction | Hold plant, acquired record and hardware fixed at a declared intervention state; change only the memory used by the actuator. | Whether the written predictor causally changes the next command. A clamp is an analyst intervention, not a training target available to the process. |
| Disconnect or invert prediction-to-action coupling | Keep prediction and its acquisition fixed; alter the actuator's use of it. | Accurate prediction and effective action are distinct. A restored predictor cannot rescue a severed actuator by definition alone. |
| Bypass the predictor with the acquired record | Preserve the actual observation and feasible action menu; pay the bypass's own cost. | Whether the retained predictor is needed for this task or imposes avoidable delay/cost. This is an architectural alternative, not a pure content clamp. |

A full-channel erasure claim requires identifying every influence through which the world affects the update. Erasing a reporter while fuel-dependent gating still changes memory is not an observation-free controller. This follows the complete-observation requirement of controlled refinement.

The stronger causal witness is a continuing history in which a formed mechanism acquires a consequence, pays to change retained predictive content, records a forecast before its target occurs, encounters changed conditions without reset, corrects through newly acquired evidence, and uses the revised memory in a later paid action. A matched memory clamp should change that action. Keep failed versions of each link.

## 4. Measurements that do not become a hidden reward

Use a vector of separately interpreted outcomes rather than one scalar called generativity.

1. **Formation and acquisition coverage:** probability that the sensor, updater and record form; probability of each planned acquisition/write; and the numbers of inactive and failed histories.
2. **Forecast quality before outcome:** for a binary event Y and a recorded probability q, compute squared error (Y−q)² only after the event resolves. Neither the error nor the hidden condition enters the learner unless an explicit physical mechanism acquires it. For nonprobabilistic memory, score the stated forecast decoder rather than assuming the register already is a probability.
3. **Correction after change:** prediction trajectory and elapsed paid opportunities until a prespecified calibration or forecast-error condition is met. Censoring due to depletion is recorded as noncompletion, not removed. A false alarm in unchanged conditions is a separate adverse outcome.
4. **Decision-time reliability:** probability that the actual stored value meets the declared decision condition while the system has the parts, fuel and source to execute its action. An expected coefficient above a threshold does not give that probability.
5. **Operational effect:** unconditional paid-action completion, real packet delivery, preserved continuation, resource use and material losses. A lower signal or error score is not itself repair or continued activity.
6. **Functional predictor-use witness:** probability of the ordered acquired-record → paid retained change → forecast → revised paid-action history, with instance identity retained.

Different controllers can choose different actions and thereby observe different labels. Their selected-action prediction errors then answer different conditional questions. Where a cross-controller accuracy comparison is needed, declare a common paid diagnostic operation or account explicitly for the different sampling laws. Do not install the unchosen action's outcome merely to complete a score table.

Report acquired-sample error together with coverage and the all-preparation operational outcomes. Assigning zero error to an inactive history can make a controller that never acts appear accurate; dropping inactive histories can produce survival bias. A cumulative all-history error, its number of scored opportunities and a conditional per-observation error may all be useful, but none can replace the others.

The score is an observer's assessment. A measured prediction-error channel can legitimately affect learning only if its operands were acquired, the computation is implemented, and its price is represented. A precomputed difference between the two counterfactual futures is not such a channel.

## 5. What a joined proof must preserve

The material predecessor and the predictor cannot meet through names alone. Choose either a typed consumable construction or a declared recyclable-slot interface and state its requirements for a sensor, record, updater and actuator. Earlier updater backing slots are not automatically enough to instantiate additional predictive memory. If a new adapter or write mechanism is supplied, say which premise it discharges and which functionality it still assumes.

The [controlled-refinement theorem](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md) gives a useful sufficient contract for deterministic finite histories: exact world projection and lifting from actual predecessors, compatible complete observations, and preservation of inherited controller memory. Resource accounts also require their own transported costs and credits. An equality of world traces alone is insufficient.

That theorem uses matching action granularity. If many molecular events realize one observation/write/decision operation, prove an explicit trace mapping with intermediate states, failures and expense. Similarly, stochastic equivalence needs matching kernels or a separate distributional argument; the existing deterministic theorem does not establish it.

For the learning recurrence, retain the actual acquisition/write indicator u. The existing exact moment identity contains E[uY] and E[uw]. Resource-dependent sampling can make E[uw] differ from E[u]E[w], even with calibrated teaching on every acquired round. A deterministic funded schedule and a fully funded physical realization must be shown before importing the simpler mean recurrence. Environmental change can additionally change the target distribution, so its reference and calibration must be re-established rather than assumed constant.

The intended local contribution is therefore a checked connection between a formed changing mechanism and an acquired, retained, corrected predictor that actually governs paid behavior. It need not beat a conventional predictor to establish that connection. Any efficiency or explanatory advantage must be stated against the exact controller class and interface tested. Prediction of private support does not yet generate shared evaluation, another center's significance or the concern theorem's normative conclusions.

## 6. The specified finite predictor and the claims it permits

The selected [protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/PROTOCOL.md) starts from actual old formed updater and selector instances and adds a paid functional retrofit. The declared old preparation has common stock 24 and source 121, yielding common reserve 20 and twelve ten-unit source packets at a successful paid adapter entrance. Old updater and selector assembly still cost two and one. The predictive retrofit costs three more units, leaving primary operating reserve 14 from the actual 20-unit adapter entrance; it cannot appear merely by renaming the old module. Payments are sequential: failed later construction does not refund an earlier formed prototype. The two recyclable material slots now implement the additional memory and decoding functions through a supplied recipe. This reuse preserves the coarse material count but does not derive the recipe's molecular realization.

The retrofit maps an old R updater to Slow and an old D updater to Fast. That mapping is an explicitly new physical implementation; the old random-restart and directed-restart semantics did not already constitute these predictor updates. The circuit stores q in {0,1,2,3,4}, initially two, and initially retains the actually formed old selector orientation as a tie-breaking mark. Thereafter last records the actual previous executed action. The action is zero below q=2, one above q=2, and follows that mark at equality. The declared forecast of capture is q/4 when the actual chosen action is one, and 1−q/4 when it is zero. It is recorded before that operation's return.

A completed operation produces its actual capture indicator. The acquired teaching bit is the chosen action on capture and its opposite on failure. Fast writes q=4Y; Slow moves q one bounded step toward 4Y. Under the supplied noiseless complementary-channel plant, the action and consequence identify the productive orientation, so the teaching bit has an exact operational interpretation. With packet outage noise, a failed matching action produces an incorrect inferred orientation. That case tests a corrupted teaching channel, not an exact hidden-state measurement. The register decoder q/4 is a specified forecast rule; Slow's intermediate values are not automatically calibrated Bayesian probabilities.

After required hardware assembly, an active circuit must prepay one unit of memory rent; failure to pay destroys its accessible record and blocks that trial. Work and sensing then require three prepaid units, followed by a further unit for a write after the receipt if it is affordable. A captured ten-unit packet pays two units of combined selector/updater renewal before the writing affordability check. Writing precedes end-of-trial wear. Thus acquiring a transient receipt and paying to retain it are separate events; missing writes remain part of the history. The circuit and updater have lifetimes six and two. Updater replacement costs two and retains q; circuit reconstruction pays its base cost plus the retrofit and resets the predictor and tie-breaking prior as declared. A hidden reversal after six opportunities does not reset the plant, source or reserve or notify a controller.

The eight declared arms are `variable`, `conventional`, `fixed_fast`, `fixed_slow`, `direct_record`, `frozen_register`, `inverted_record`, and `inverted_action`. The `direct_record` route retains the latest decoded payload bit with retrofit price one while retaining the declared writing and rent expenses. It still needs initialization and lifecycle state; the price is a supplied cost for that simpler finite circuit, not a derivation of its minimal physical memory. It is a cheaper architectural comparison; `fixed_fast` is the stronger comparator at the same five-level memory and retrofit cost. `frozen_register` uses charged sham writes, `inverted_record` changes only the label used in updating, and `inverted_action` changes the output coupling. Freezing memory is therefore not a disguised source of saved write fuel. Other interventions described above remain proposals unless the final protocol implements them.

Forecast error concerns **the actual capture outcome of the action taken**, while the teaching bit concerns **an inferred productive orientation**. These are connected exactly only through the declared plant. The noisy case must keep that distinction. Neither a hidden productive orientation nor a counterfactual return may enter the predictor through an evaluation-only field. An action-inverted arm must score the forecast for its actual executed action, not for the action it would otherwise have chosen.

The strict correction witness additionally excludes a fresh circuit's prior and a reset followed by relearning. The same circuit must have made an acquired nonneutral forecast and captured before the first boundary; use its retained old action unsuccessfully after that boundary; pay to write a change that alters its subsequent command; then execute the changed command and capture. Rebuilding the circuit resets a witness in progress; replacing only its updater does not. Completed witnesses remain historical outcomes. This distinction is essential: successful recovery after memory destruction is not correction of that retained prediction.

The eight declared parameter cases retain primary reversal, no change, two changes, three-quarter packet availability, smaller original common reserve, a higher write price, shorter circuit life, and lower packet yield. Brier loss is reported with trial coverage and its ratio to expected paid trials explicitly labeled a trial-weighted diagnostic. Decoder errors may be measured against the enumerator's hidden orientation to test misspecification, but that variable must not enter the update.

The direct-record comparator may succeed by bypassing the five-level memory's additional construction expense. Such a result would identify that cost or ordering burden. It would not show that physically retained evidence is absent: the one-bit route has its own paid memory. Conversely, a variable updater's successful correction supplies no broad advantage unless it exceeds the matched fixed-rule alternatives on the declared outcome with all formation and depletion histories retained.

The parallel [paid transfer-evidence study](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/transfer-evidence/README.md) supplies a finite later-action reliability interface under its stationary Bernoulli teacher and additionally supplied counters, certificate table and shared routing. It explicitly preserves false finite certificates, resource stopping and failure after model change. Its confidence guarantees cannot simply be attached to the present reversing, action-dependent and outage-corrupted teaching law. A downstream concern connection would need a target/record mapping, valid calibration and sampling conditions, paid memory/terminal action, and the still-supplied correspondence and evaluation coupling. The present capture forecast does not silently replace those premises.
