The design under examination makes updater identity variable between random and directed selector revision. No numerical outcome or acceptance of the final implementation is asserted in this note before its companion checks are available.

**Comparative position:** mechanisms controlling modification and transmission have long been objects of selection, and learning rules themselves have long been learned. The proposed advance inside this repository is to give such an updater a paid formation and maintenance history within the continuing organization, and to connect that history to the preceding formation process. Adding another level is insufficient by itself. The complete hierarchy still admits an ordinary finite-state description; equivalence to that description is expected.

## 2. What must actually vary

Distinguish three objects in the event law:

- The **selector** maps a currently observed cue to a response.
- The **updater** determines how an expired selector is reconstructed: for example, uniformly choose an orientation or reconstruct the opposite of the accessible previous orientation.
- The **updater formation and turnover law** determines which updater is constructed and how it persists or is replaced.

To advance beyond the preceding study, the second object must be an actual, changing state-bearing component with formation and maintenance costs. Merely renaming an experimental policy parameter as u leaves it externally supplied. At least one permitted history must change updater identity within the continuing compartment, and that change must alter a subsequent selector-transition probability under otherwise matched physical conditions.

The third object's law can remain a legitimate supplied local premise. State that boundary. Making u variable does not establish an indefinitely self-revising hierarchy or a derivation of every updating mechanism. Nor does it establish a represented objective or a world model.

A molecular gate cannot be charged only to the proposed arm while the conventional arm receives free operation, or omitted only from the proposed arm.

Policy choices may differ over the shared feasible actions. The event opportunities, costs and failure law conditional on an action must match. Identical proposal probabilities are required for exact emulation, not for the competing fixed-type policies. The actual assembly couplings implementing those choices remain explicit assumptions.

Use the same accessible return, reserve and physical residue in all arms. A simulator may know the hidden environment, but no policy receives it or the reversal schedule. ## 4. Transferring the formation process to a gate

The preceding mixed formation witness consumes four common-fuel units before capture and its one captured packet during copying. Its terminal usable reserve cannot be silently identified with the nine-unit initial reserve in the selector study. Additional support or changed preparation is permissible only as a declared input with the resulting histories recalculated.

A valid interface needs the following ledger.

| Supplied or derived item | Required record |
|---|---|
| Updater repertoire and molecular meaning | State which formed species realizes each updater and how it gates actual transition probabilities. A label-to-code correspondence alone supplies no chemical implementation. |
| Gate precursor and formation | Declare whether it is inherited, produced by the preceding network or externally supplied. Charge construction and preserve failed assembly. |
| Residual energy and material | Carry actual common/local/source fuel, precursors, occupancy and damage across the boundary. Do not turn total uncaptured source into usable fuel without a capture transition. |
| Formation endpoint or handoff time | Specify a stopping rule or deterministic time before inspecting outcomes. Include histories that never reach the required state. A selected witness is not the entrance distribution. |
| Timing and lost opportunities | Preserve elapsed time and any packets already lost during formation. A new trial clock or source stream is a separate supplied resource unless explicitly composed. |
| Observer access | Establish which physical quantities the updater encounters. Successful formation, efficient repair or a theorist's “useful” label is not automatically a sensed input. |
| Identity and ancestry | Keep the compartment and relevant material history through updater construction and replacement. Population replacement is a distinct mechanism. |
| Event-law compatibility | Prove the mapped entrance supports the actual gate operations with their prices. If formation and operation are different laws, state the physical handoff mechanism and its cost. |

The unconditional composition test is simple. If the formation process induces exit-state measure μ, and R(x) is the continuation success probability from the **actual** exit x under the gate law, the joined probability is the sum or integral of R(x) against μ, with unsuccessful formation histories assigned their appropriate continuation value, usually zero. If some histories have no exit, μ is a subprobability measure; their missing mass must not be renormalized away. If time matters, x includes elapsed time and remaining opportunity.

This identity follows from conditioning and is not a new theorem. Its force is methodological: evaluate the continuation at each inherited state, not at a single favorable substitute. An exact refinement would additionally preserve relevant traces and event marks; equal means or a common fuel bound do not establish that stronger relation.

A meaningful first test is a boundary pair: one reachable exit can pay for gate formation plus its first operation, while a second exit one fuel unit below the sharp requirement cannot, under otherwise specified conditions. Check that the joined process and conventional hierarchy both predict the failure, and that the original formation process assigns the reported probability to these exits. If all affordable exits have zero formation probability, the claimed bridge has not been built even if the gate works from a manually funded state.

## 5. Counterfactual tests and interpretation

Hold the current selector and accessible environment fixed while intervening on updater identity. The next expired-selector reconstruction distribution must change. This establishes the updater's causal role instead of treating it as an observer's annotation.

Then intervene on the physical route from realized return to updater maintenance while retaining the response and resource accounting. The proposed mechanism predicts altered updater persistence only through its declared coupling. Effects through simple energy deprivation, shared maintenance or the selector's own renewal must be distinguished. A return packet allocated to two components cannot be spent twice. Where upkeep competes, the priority or allocation rule is another supplied mechanism.

A higher updater lifetime can improve retention in one regime but obstruct revision after a change. It can also consume the reserve needed for immediate action. Retain these possible adverse outcomes rather than define success as persistence of the currently favored updater. Compare noisy and changing conditions with fixed conditions; a decline in exploratory renewal may be locally adaptive and still impair later recovery.

None of these interventions separates the material description from an exactly matching conventional hierarchy. They can separate competing causal couplings or show that a proposed abstraction discards a necessary physical state. The new explanatory obligation is the actual formation-to-updater connection, not an escape from ordinary state-transition mathematics.
