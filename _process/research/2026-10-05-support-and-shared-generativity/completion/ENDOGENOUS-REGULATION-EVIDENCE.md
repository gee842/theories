# Evidence interface: from dependencies to endogenous regulation

6 October 2026. AI-authored primary-source audit for Ren's [accepted derivation sequence](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#accepted-work-sequence). This supplies empirical constraints and proposed discriminating observations for **dependency → accessible internal difference → formed response → changed continuation → changed retention**. It does not validate a new model or close that entire connection.

**Finding.** Natural stress-response systems supply identifiable physical routes from impaired organization to regulatory activity and newly synthesized protection. Existing experiments also separate persistent protective material, altered future responsiveness, and selective survival. These are different mechanisms. None of the sources inspected here demonstrates the origin of a successful regulatory architecture from its absence, or an endogenous rule that retains newly formed responses because of their realized consequences.

## Primary sources and their actual scope

### 1. A physical internal signal: yeast Hsf1–Hsp70 regulation

Zheng et al., *Dynamic control of Hsf1 during heat shock by a chaperone switch and phosphorylation*, eLife 5:e18638 (2016), [article and DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.18638), [author-hosted paper](https://www.bu.edu/khalillab/_papers/zheng-elife2016.pdf).

Hsp70 associates with Hsf1 before heat shock, dissociates during the acute response, and later reassociates. Biochemical interaction, a competing Hsf1 decoy, and chaperone coexpression support a titration mechanism connecting folding demand to transcriptional control. The model links Hsf1 activation to production of additional Hsp70. Phosphorylation contributes to sustained activation beyond the minimal titration model. Excess Hsf1 activity impairs growth; Hsp70/Hsp40 coexpression partially rescues that impairment. See Figures 1–3 and 6, Results and Discussion.

This is the best immediate interface for a signal physically accessible to the process: occupancy of a needed component affects a regulator. It is induction within an inherited cellular architecture, not discovery of that architecture. Hsf1, Hsp70, promoters and expression machinery already exist. Regulatory activity is not a direct measurement of total viability, and the experiments do not identify a complete resource ledger or prove outcome-dependent retention.

### 2. Retention is not necessarily evaluation: yeast stress memory

Guan et al., *Cellular Memory of Acquired Stress Resistance in Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, Genetics 192:495–505 (2012), [primary full-text record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3454879/), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22851651/).

After mild salt exposure, elevated peroxide resistance persists through several generations. Long-lived Ctt1 catalase synthesized during exposure contributes to this persistence and passes to daughters. Induced Ctt1 expression without salt also produces persistent resistance. A separable response involves faster transcription and reacquisition of resistance after repeated exposure: deleting NUP42 disrupts this acceleration while preserving initial acquisition and the persistent-resistance memory under the reported conditions. See Figures 1–6 and corresponding Results.

These interventions distinguish material carryover from changed future responsiveness. Neither result shows that a response is retained because the organism experienced its beneficial outcome. Exposure history can induce persistence before a later survival challenge. The machinery and genes are supplied; externally induced expression is a causal intervention, not evidence of endogenous invention. Nutrient provision, dilution by division and elapsed time belong in any model of this memory. Its energetic price and long-run advantage were not jointly identified here.

### 3. A competing explanation: survival of pre-existing states

Levy, Ziv and Siegal, *Bet Hedging in Yeast by Heterogeneous, Age-Correlated Expression of a Stress Protectant*, PLOS Biology 10:e1001325 (2012), [primary article](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001325).

Prechallenge slow growth and Tsl1 abundance predict heat-shock survival. TSL1 deletion and an acute challenge support a protective contribution beyond a correlation with growth. Phenotypic distributions recover after growth of sorted populations, supporting reversible state heterogeneity. See Figures 3–4, Box 1 and Methods. The paper explicitly distinguishes demonstrated heterogeneity from the untested hypothesis that selection produced that distribution as an adaptation.

Thus an enriched protective state after stress need not mean that individual cells learned from successful action: the stress may have removed less-protected cells. Slow growth and resistance also need separate endpoints. Their association does not by itself identify a molecular synthesis cost. Age, initial state, growth, survival and descendants should all be observed rather than replacing them with one population-average reporter. Laboratory heat schedules and sorting are external interventions; neither is a naturally formed evaluator within the cell.

### 4. Engineered comparison already audited: paid burden relief

Ceroni et al., *Burden-driven feedback control of gene expression*, Nature Methods 15:387–393 (2018), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4635). The existing [full repository audit](PHYSICAL-EVIDENCE-CERONI.md) is authoritative for inspected methods and source-data limitations. Burden-responsive feedback can relieve a larger expression burden, while costly controller designs can worsen growth. This gives a useful cost-and-relief comparator. The controller is deliberately supplied, and antibiotic-supported cultivation does not demonstrate its spontaneous origin or evolutionary retention. Do not reuse its data inconsistencies as resolved calibration inputs.

## What the evidence permits us to model

Use a **physically coupled signal** as the near-term interface: a change in a process's constitutive dependency changes an internal state that can act on a response. The model must declare that coupling and its formation. An observer's label such as “bad state” must not be an extra input that silently chooses the useful response.

Keep three retention variables distinct:

- **Material retention:** a response component remains, partitions or decays.
- **Mechanism retention or modification:** later response production changes within a continuing lineage.
- **Population retention:** differential survival or reproduction changes the abundance of lineages or states.

Any of these can matter for future continuation. Only a specified causal connection from realized consequences to the latter two establishes the stronger claim of consequence-dependent response selection. A protein that persists because its degradation is slow does not, on that fact alone, establish learning. These distinctions are our explanatory requirements, not additional findings attributed to the papers.

The sources concern different interventions and mechanisms. Their results cannot be spliced into evidence for one complete causal chain. In particular, neither synthesis of new copies from an existing program nor a low reporter level establishes a starting state without the successful regulatory architecture.

## Proposed discriminating observations — unrun

The following is an identification contract for a future model and experiment, not a preregistration or a claim that these interventions are already technically available. Each perturbation needs a manipulation check, an explicit account of its collateral effects, and matched or measured resource consequences.

| Question | Comparison and observations | What would distinguish the explanations |
|---|---|---|
| Does the signal arise from the relevant dependency? | Change actual demand while monitoring internal signal, response, damage, resources and continuation; separately perturb the signal without the demand change. | Signal-driven responses should track the manipulated coupling. Direct damage effects must be distinguished from regulation. |
| Is new response construction causally necessary? | Begin with measured response abundance; selectively interrupt its construction, restore it, and measure actual material and growth costs. Compare with matched inactive material. | A reporter rising without changed continuation is insufficient; a rescue must recover the relevant function under stated costs. |
| Does experience change a surviving lineage? | Follow the same lineages before exposure, through response and after recovery; measure baseline heterogeneity and death. | A within-lineage mechanism change can be separated from survival-driven shifts in population composition. |
| Is persistence the explanation? | Estimate dilution, partition and decay; interrupt retention after matching initial response and first-episode outcome. | If later behavior is fully predicted by remaining material, a new evaluation mechanism is unnecessary. |
| Do outcomes alter retention beyond cue history? | Compare matched cue and response histories with experimentally altered consequences; specify the internal route by which those consequences could be sensed. | Retention differences attributable to that route, lost on route disruption and restored on rescue, would support consequence dependence. No effect supports a cue-driven or passive-retention account. |
| When does regulation become harmful? | Vary formation expense, lag, recurrence and cue reliability, retaining actual successor states. | Prespecified failure regions should include expenditure without repayment and obsolete responses; mere positive reporter output cannot count as success. |

A consequence manipulation can itself create a new signal or resource difference. Those differences must be measured and modeled rather than assumed away. Likewise, selective death is an outcome-dependent retention mechanism at population level, but it must not be renamed individual learning.

## Comparative and verification obligations

Compare at least an unregulated process, fixed constitutive response, ordinary negative feedback, passive carryover, and pre-existing heterogeneity with differential survival, as applicable to the chosen mechanism. Fit or bound their parameters from calibration observations before examining the eventual claimed gain. The new account must state a prediction or structural constraint these alternatives do not already supply. Shared vocabulary alone adds no empirical discrimination.

The candidate construction needs independent measures of internal signal, formed material, effective action, continuation, retention and resource use. Declare a horizon and an independently specified continuation task; do not use the analyst's endpoint as the cell's hidden reward. Retain unsuccessful formations and negative regimes. A conventional model may win this comparison and still provide a mechanism that the wider generative account must incorporate.

**Contribution to the roadmap:** the mechanism branch now has a concrete physical-signal comparator and explicit ways to distinguish induction, persistence, learning and selection. The unresolved bridge is still the formation of a response architecture and the causal return from its consequences to its retention or revision, under accounted costs. These sources constrain that bridge; they do not supply a completed proof of it.
