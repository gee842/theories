# Closing the formation-to-retention preparation gap

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration under the [governing roadmap](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The previous [conditional regulation gate](REGULATION-RETENTION-INTEGRATION.md) connected an absent response to material reconstruction. The next [selection argument](CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md) began with two already formed variants. Joining their preparation and selection, without a favorable reset, was the named next interface.

**Completed scope:** one declared law now starts with neither response type nor association present, pays their formation and association costs, and supports useful copying while an alternative remains present. Its stochastic comparisons average over all histories, including failed formations. This closes that concrete preparation gap. It does not complete the origin of chemical repertoires, selection over selectors, individual learning, concern, wisdom or the whole foundation.

The first proposed two-material/three-fuel fixture only favored a useful clonal founder. If both variants formed there, they could not reach mixed productive copying. That limitation led to a three-material/four-fuel primary fixture before the full numerical run. The smaller fixture is preserved, preventing its narrower result from silently replacing the requested interface.

## What remains supplied

The local laws specify a damaged capture site, two possible formation products with different repair efficacy, type-preserving copying and locality-dependent fuel access. These are legitimate explicit local-model inputs. They have not been derived from the abstract causal core, identified as molecules or shown to be uniquely minimal. A relation containing these transitions does not by itself explain why this chemistry exists.

This model is not asserted to be an exact kinetic quotient of either preceding model. It retains new association and localization distinctions that matter for subsequent copying. Its contribution is an explicit joined construction under one law, with the earlier studies' valid scoped results and failures preserved.

## The next missing derivation: selectors become variable mechanisms

The present population changes, and individual site occupants change, but the available response repertoire, association rule, inheritance rule and local-copy rule do not vary as transmissible structures. Changing μ, locality or copying between experimental arms is an external intervention, not the process revising its selector.

The next milestone must therefore:

1. Represent at least two actual mechanisms for controlling access, sensing or copying, with explicit formation, maintenance and inheritance costs.
2. Show that these mechanisms change which responses can be reconstructed, and that their own consequences feed back into their persistence or revision under the same law.
3. Retain an actual change in dependency or source conditions, including cases where an old signal becomes misleading; explain adaptation, lag, capture or collapse without externally choosing the winning mechanism.
4. Distinguish population replacement from a continuing organization's alteration of its own mechanism. Connect whichever capacity is established to the precise learning premises of the existing concern studies.

This is a route toward selection over selectors with honest intermediate obligations. It does not assume that such selection necessarily favors broad concern, or that a private-return advantage supplies an ethical reason to protect every affected center. Those further connections remain part of the same governing objective.
