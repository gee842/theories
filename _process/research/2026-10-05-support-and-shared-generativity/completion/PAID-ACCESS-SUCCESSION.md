# Paid access to captured support: full succession and a time-dependent comparison

5 October 2026. AI-assisted continuation from commit `37f580e`. This uses the explicit bound-fuel access extension proposed in [Useful retention, §6](USEFUL-RETENTION-MECHANISM.md). It does not alter that frozen source, import the earlier free-packet turnover proof, or assume that reproduction remains destructive after scaffold wear.

**Result.** The extended CF model can form its fabricator and gate from absence, replace all four original A/C/G/Y carriers, and return to the same supported organization. In a single common generator with every positive rate constant set to one, the blocking branch has a strictly greater probability of completing the declared full task by time 10. For sufficiently short deadlines, the **nonblocking** branch has the greater probability of that same task. The complete comparison therefore reverses with the deadline.

The time-10 probabilities are both only about **2.7 × 10⁻¹³**. This is a rigorous moderate-rate comparison in a supplied reaction model, not reliable organization, chemical identification, total architectural prevalence or universal direction. The reference source is maintained externally. A [separate thermodynamic certificate](PAID-ACCESS-THERMODYNAMIC-CERTIFICATE.md) transfers the time-10 sign to a stipulated finite-source reversible completion; molecular feasibility remains unproved.

## 1. The law and assay were fixed before computation

The [declared protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/paid-access/PROTOCOL.md) retains the full material-fabricator/CF law, with the following added channels and their mass-action propensities:

| Added reaction | Propensity |
|---|---:|
| D+CF -> R+C | h D CF |
| R+CF -> A_i+C | s_i R CF |
| A_i+R+CF -> A_i+G_i+C | q_i A_i R CF |

Both types i=0,1 are available within the same law. Every original free-F formation, copying and fabrication reaction remains. CF -> C+F, bound-packet copying, and CF-catalyzed free-F recycling are **absent** in this declared version. The last was an optional, previously unspecified channel; it is not silently included. Capture is C -> CF at rate gC. A source packet can therefore be accessed through a physical consuming reaction without first destroying C.

Use normalized unit volume and the existing packet convention. Every positive rate constant is one, including h, s_i, q_i and both fabricator-copy rates. The supplied structural difference remains \(\zeta_0=0\) versus \(\zeta_1=1\) for bound reproduction. Rate constants of different reaction orders have their appropriate normalized units; equality of their displayed numbers is not a claim about measured molecular kinetics. The primary deadline H=10 was fixed before calculation. No rate or target was changed in response to the sign.

The common preparation is

\[
Z_0:\quad R=3,\ Y=1,\ F=2,
\qquad\text{all other species absent}.
\tag{1}
\]

At the **first** fresh capture require entry into some state

\[
P_{ij}:\quad A_i=YG_j=CF=1,\quad F=0,
\tag{2}
\]

with all unlisted species zero. Mark the four functional carriers A,C,G,Y present at that entry. At the **seventh** fresh capture, by H, require return to that same count/type state, after each of those four marked carriers has undergone its wear reaction. Call this common, type-neutral event \(E_H\). The final return is a hitting event; later survival until H is not required.

Let I=i mean the first actual reaction from Z0 forms Ai through its template-free free-F channel. We compare

\[
J_i(H)=\Pr_{Z_0}(I=i,E_H).
\tag{3}
\]

Both branch contributions are unconditional probabilities under the same generator. Other first reactions, wrong-type formation, damage, leakage, source loss and incomplete trajectories all retain their probability mass. The target does not label type 0 successful by definition. Analyst tags and counters supply no sensing or control to the chemistry; implementing their readout would require its own account.

## 2. Actual construction and full original-carrier replacement

The first-five-event route

\[
\text{form }A_i,\ \text{fabricate }G_i,\ \text{bind Y},\
R\to C,\ \text{capture into CF}
\tag{4}
\]

reaches \(P_{ii}\). A and G formation consume both supplied packets. In the work ledger

\[
N=4,\quad U=F+CF+GF_0+GF_1,\quad W=N-D+U,
\tag{5}
\]

the preparation has W=6 and the entry has W=5: two construction losses and one new capture. That net preparation expenditure is retained in the comparison.

Here is a complete 21-event return from \(P_{ii}\). Each phase starts or ends at the same count/type organization; which marked original carriers have worn is kept separately. The reaction law itself does not react to those marks.

**Replace original A: five events, two captures.**

1. A wears, leaving D, CF and YG.
2. D+CF -> R+C spends the stored packet and recovers R.
3. Capture charges C, giving R+CF+YG.
4. R+CF -> Ai+C reconstructs A, with the competing type still available.
5. Capture returns to P.

**Replace original C: six events, one capture.**

1. Charged C wears: CF -> D+F.
2. YG dissociates.
3. Free Y recycles D+F into R.
4. Y binds the gate again.
5. R assembles into C.
6. Capture returns to P.

**Replace original G and Y: ten events, three captures.**

1. The original G constituent wears in YG, releasing Y and D while CF remains.
2. Bound-packet recycling D+CF -> R+C recovers a precursor.
3. Free Y reproduces into that R, producing a daughter.
4. Capture charges C.
5. The tagged original Y wears; its daughter survives.
6. Bound-packet recycling recovers R and empties C again.
7. Capture charges C while R remains available.
8. Ai+R+CF -> Ai+Gi+C rebuilds G.
9. The surviving Y binds G.
10. Capture returns to P.

At the original-Y wear step there are two free Ys. The selected original has wear rate d=1, while both wear propensities remain in the total exit intensity. The original's replacement is therefore an identity-resolved event, not merely a count of one Y wear. Binding preserves the identities of its G and Y constituents. Recycling old material into a new functional carrier does not assert replacement of the underlying atoms.

All selected events have positive finite propensity for both types. Their competitors remain charged. The [path check](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/paid-access/path_check.py) and [saved trace](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/paid-access/path-check.json) retain every intermediate labeled state, complete exit intensity and work change. This route establishes actual succession, not an external reset. It is not the shortest possible jump sequence and its probability alone is not used to decide the branch comparison.

## 3. Six captures are necessary for a full return

For any P-to-P return, the work identity is

\[
K_{\rm cap}=K_{\rm leaks}+B_A+B_G+D_A+D_G+D_C+D_Y.
\tag{6}
\]

All free, GF-bound and CF-bound packet leakage contributes to \(K_{\rm leaks}\). B counts A births and G fabrication, through either free or bound packets. D counts wear. Returning total A and G counts implies \(B_A=D_A\) and \(B_G=D_G\). Wearing each of the four marked original carriers requires at least one wear in each role. Hence

\[
\boxed{K_{\rm cap}\ge 2+2+1+1=6.}
\tag{7}
\]

The displayed return attains six with four recyclings, one A birth, one G birth and four original-carrier wears. The complete formation-plus-return route uses seven fresh captures and ends at W=5 after starting at W=6. The debit of one assigned work unit during formation is explicit. The six-capture minimum belongs to this ledger; it is not a measured free-energy bound.

The added channels remove the parent model's exact obstruction. There, \(V=R+C+CF+F+\sum GF_i\) never increased from V=1 at P, making RF-based A/G construction impossible forever. Bound recycling now changes D+CF into R+C, increasing V from one to two while preserving W. This makes previously inaccessible reactant combinations available. V remains an availability count, not energy, and its old monotonicity cannot be applied to this extension.

## 4. Why the finite calculation keeps all potentially successful paths

The assay has a fixed capture budget. Returning from W=5 to W=5 with six captures permits exactly four original wears, one A birth and one G birth, with no leaks or additional work losses. Therefore the following exits are rigorously incompatible with this particular target:

- Any packet leakage, repeated wear of an already replaced carrier, or wear of a newly formed carrier.
- More than one post-entry birth in a functional role. Extra A/G births would need extra wear to restore final counts; extra C/Y births would likewise require a forbidden additional wear.
- Formation of an A of the wrong final type. There is no type-conversion channel, and replacing that additional molecule would exceed the work budget.
- Loss of the last Y, or reaching R=C=CF=F=GF0=GF1=0. Neither Y lineage nor capture access can restart from those respective boundaries in the declared forward repertoire.

Those exits go to failure with their full propensities. They are not removed from holding-time denominators. An early capture outside (2), or a seventh capture that fails the final test, also fails the explicitly declared assay even if another task might later succeed.

Four labeled backbone carriers suffice after entry. Their initial roles are A,C,G,Y; G/Y bonds and free packets are also recorded. A wear bit changes when an original functional carrier wears. Before any forbidden later wear, the exact number of births in a role is

\[
\text{current count of the role}-1
+\mathbf1_{\{\text{its original carrier wore}\}}.
\tag{8}
\]

For these counts, the C role includes C and CF; G and Y include their free and complex-bound constituents. This identity justifies the birth-budget test in the enumerator. Molecule choices retain their mass-action multiplicities. In particular, catalytic copying keeps the catalyst, reproduction keeps its parent, and CF/GF wear releases exactly its held packet.

Before the first capture the minimum work budget similarly permits only one A birth and one G birth, with no wear or leak. It forces the attainable entry to have i=j. After selecting I=i, the following seven precursor rows include every potentially successful arrangement; the common generator still supplies the failure exits:

| Row | Nonzero counts, apart from fixed type i | Live transitions and rates | Failure rate |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 0 | Z0 | first Ai formation ->1, rate6 | 15 |
| 1 | R=2,Y=1,F=1,A_i=1 | G fabrication ->2, rate2; C assembly ->4, rate2 | 11 |
| 2 | R=Y=A_i=G_i=1 | Y binding ->3, rate1; C assembly ->5, rate1 | 4 |
| 3 | R=A_i=YG_i=1 | Y dissociation ->2, rate1; C assembly ->6, rate1 | 3+zeta_i |
| 4 | R=C=Y=F=A_i=1 | G fabrication ->5, rate1 | 10 |
| 5 | C=Y=A_i=G_i=1 | Y binding ->6, rate1 | 5 |
| 6 | C=A_i=YG_i=1 | Y dissociation ->5, rate1; capture ->P, rate1 | 4 |

The source [implementation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/paid-access/compute.py) records the selected type and all four original roles explicitly.

There are 1,393 transient states per branch, including these seven precursor rows, and a success state. Failure mass is implicit in deficient row sums. The maximum complete exit rate is 21. These are two marked branch calculations within one common reaction law, not a rate switch imposed after inspecting an outcome.

## 5. Certified comparison at the prespecified deadline

Let Q_i be the finite generator just described, including success as absorbing and failure as zero success reward. With f the indicator of success,

\[
J_i(H)=(e^{HQ_i}f)(Z_0).
\tag{9}
\]

Uniformization uses rate 21 and Poisson mean 210 at H=10. The [certificate](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/paid-access/certify.py) propagates lower and upper bounds on \((I+Q_i/21)^nf\) using integers scaled by \(2^{128}\), rounding every division down or up. It includes terms through n=512. A rational Taylor-series bound on \(e^{210}\), with a geometric bound on the remaining positive terms, bounds the omitted Poisson mass by \(10^{-65}\). No floating arithmetic enters the certified inequalities.

The [saved bounds](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/paid-access/certificate.json) give

\[
\begin{aligned}
J_0(10)&\in
\frac{[278831198712280953,\ 278831198712280954]}{10^{30}},\\
J_1(10)&\in
\frac{[269173570450682085,\ 269173570450682086]}{10^{30}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{J_0(10)-J_1(10)
\ge\frac{9657628261598867}{10^{30}}>0.}
\tag{11}
\]

The probabilities are approximately \(2.78831\times10^{-13}\) and \(2.69174\times10^{-13}\). The blocking contribution is about 3.59% larger relative to the nonblocking contribution; the absolute difference is only about \(9.66\times10^{-15}\). The result does not establish likely full succession. The initial floating matrix exponential and eventual hitting calculation are saved as exploratory checks and explicitly distinguished from these rigorous bounds; the undiscounted floating result is not needed for (11).

## 6. The same full task reverses at short deadlines

The exact shortest successful sequence in either branch has 24 jumps. Enumerating all such sequences with their reaction-rate products gives totals

\[
A_0=27677232,\qquad A_1=29611764.
\tag{12}
\]

For a finite generator, diagonal holding-rate terms cannot contribute before a successful off-diagonal path exists. Consequently the leading terms are

\[
J_i(h)=\frac{A_i}{24!}h^{24}+O(h^{25}),
\]

and

\[
\boxed{J_0(h)-J_1(h)
=-\frac{1934532}{24!}h^{24}+O(h^{25})
=-\frac{3161}{1013804577995489280000}h^{24}+O(h^{25}).}
\tag{13}
\]

Thus nonblocking is strictly better for all sufficiently small positive h, whereas blocking is strictly better at H=10. Continuity yields at least one crossover. No uniqueness, exact crossover time, or all-horizon advantage is claimed.

This reversal has a physical reason. The positive retained-scaffold comparison treated reproduction consuming the last C precursor as irreversible failure. After scaffold wear and access to CF, reproduction can instead supply the successor Y. For example, the reachable state

\[
S_i:\ R=C=YG_i=1,\quad A_i=CF=F=0
\tag{14}
\]

occurs after A wear and bound recycling. Bound reproduction followed by wear of the original Y preserves a daughter and C. Its short-time probability is \(\zeta_i\beta d\,h^2/2+O(h^3)\). Blocking requires at least an extra dissociation before free reproduction. The same reaction can therefore destroy one continuation and enable another; its sign must be assessed against the declared full task and deadline.

For a common finite augmented state space, the exact semigroup response identity weights each changed reaction by

\[
\text{rate}\,[u(\text{successor},\text{remaining time})
-u(\text{current state},\text{remaining time})],
\tag{15}
\]

where u is conditional success for the full event. In this extension bound reproduction has live successor states as well as failing ones. Its generator difference is not a nonnegative killing term. The earlier Feynman–Kac domination cannot be extended beyond its original scaffold boundary. Equations (11) and (13) compute the competing consequences rather than assuming their net sign.

## 7. Conditional continuation and remaining scope

For any selected return route with intensities r_j and complete exit intensities lambda_j, the actual return-time subprobability kernel is

\[
K_i(dt)=\mathop{*}_j[r_j e^{-\lambda_jt}\,dt].
\tag{16}
\]

After an actual P-to-P return, the same count/type state and homogeneous support give the same future kernel conditional on the modeled history. Exchangeability permits marking the newly present carriers for another assay without changing the chemistry. Finite sequences of such paid returns consequently have positive composable probabilities. This is a continuation guarantee at the specified physical state, not a free reset or a guarantee for arbitrary partly successful trajectories.

Neither the time-10 contrast nor a positive return path proves indefinite renewal or architectural prevalence. The source must supply each capture; access, allocation, original-carrier loss and competing construction remain modeled costs. A finite reservoir, reverse chemistry or hidden environmental state must be included in a new continuation state or an explicit finite-horizon perturbation bound. The previous reversible completion of the old free-packet model is not itself a thermodynamic proof for CF chemistry.

The new [CF physical-constraints derivation](PAID-ACCESS-PHYSICAL-CONSTRAINTS.md) and its [time-10 certificate](PAID-ACCESS-THERMODYNAMIC-CERTIFICATE.md) provide that separate finite-horizon transfer. They retain the positive time-10 sign at the cost of a very large finite source stock and stipulated potentials. Added reverse reactions can change short-time leading orders, so the reference model's earlier negative asymptotic is **not** automatically transferred. Nor is a fixed-source P-to-P return a return of the full depleted reservoir state.

The advance is that **formation, paid access, complete original-carrier turnover, return and a signed finite-time comparison now fit one moderate-rate generator**. The opposite short-time sign is part of the result. It rules out interpreting the retained-scaffold advantage as an unconditional preference for blocking reproduction and keeps the future joint task, support conditions and observation horizon explicit.
