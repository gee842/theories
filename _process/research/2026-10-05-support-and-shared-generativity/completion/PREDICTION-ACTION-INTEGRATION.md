## Results that constrain the explanation

All commissioned primary learners in the variable, conventional, fixed Fast, fixed Slow and direct-record arms complete the strict correction witness; unconditionally this is **2/9**. The specified full word is only one contributing path, with probability 1/72. Variable expected captures are **19/9**, fixed Fast **7/3**, fixed Slow **17/9**, frozen register **2/3** and each inverted arm **1/9**. The conventional implementation exactly matches the variable one. Under outages, Slow slightly exceeds equally priced Fast instead.

With original reserve 12, the elaborate circuit cannot fund a first trial, while the cheaper record circuit obtains 11/9 expected captures. Along the specified three-write correction route, twenty inherited common units produce a corrected register but cannot fund its use; twenty-one suffice. These are actual ordering/liquidity obstructions, not total-energy shortages that future source alone resolves.

The inverted-action arm has trial-weighted Brier loss 3/32, below the normal variable arm's 109/768, yet captures only 1/9 instead of 19/9. Its observation coverage is also lower: expected paid trials 2/3 versus 8/3. Accurate prediction of self-produced failure is compatible with a destructive action route. The loss is an analyst readout and a policy-dependent sampling statistic, not the mechanism's reward or a common held-out superiority score.

This makes the remaining action-coupling premise concrete. Forecast acquisition does not itself determine why the forecasted productive route is chosen. Formation of a normal controller under a supplied recipe proves its realization, not the origin or selection of that recipe over the inverted alternative.
