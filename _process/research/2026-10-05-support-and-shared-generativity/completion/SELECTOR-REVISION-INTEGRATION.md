# Integrating costed selector revision and its conventional comparison

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration under the [governing research path](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md), following [Ren's request](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-costed-selector-revision-request.md).

**Completed local milestone:** a rule-bearing selector changes its cue-to-response mapping within a continuing compartment, through paid wear, reconstruction and actual return-funded retention under changed conditions. A sharp reserve/exposure recovery boundary is established. A conventional automaton exactly matches the mechanism; directed feedback performs better in the primary comparison. This is a conditional constructive result and a useful limit on novelty claims.

| Required connection or test | Result and scope |

| Costs and consequences inside the process | Assembly, sensing/response construction and selector renewal consume actual reserve. Capture supplies finite source fuel. Failure, unaffordable probes and packet spill are retained. |

| Sharp obstruction | Recovery after k obsolete trials needs at least kd+c+d fuel and k+1 exposures. Each additional failed candidate costs c+Ld and L exposures. Exact random-reassembly recovery probability follows from the affordable timely candidate count. |
| Negative regimes | Fixed mapping wins on capture count with two reversals; outcome-blind paid renewal can win at low reserve by avoiding maintenance expenses that otherwise make later revision insolvent. |
| Scientific scope | Conditional explanation and exact bound, with targeted prior-art comparison. No historically new theorem, better learning algorithm or empirical identification is established. |

## What is still supplied and what follows next

The previous [formed-variant result](FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md) formed response types under a declared chemical law. This study supplies a new physical abstraction with cue transduction, selector residue, renewal lifetime and proposal chemistry. It is not an exact extension of that molecular law, and there is no demonstrated kinetic map showing the former process constructs the latter's architecture. Their common trace/accounting interface does not remove this remaining formation-and-composition obligation.

Selector orientation is variable; the rule that proposes orientations and the coupling that renews them remain fixed. Directed feedback is an installed comparator, not a mechanism generated by the material process. Consequently the next central connection is **formation and consequence-dependent retention/revision of alternative update mechanisms themselves**, starting from explicit material costs and the preceding formation resources. The construction should retain the demonstrated insolvency and misleading-feedback failures and compare with competent directed feedback. It must distinguish construction of a rule-bearing component, tuning a fixed rule, population selection among rules and revision inside a continuing organization.

Conditional operational teaching signals and information-flow constraints are also being developed in the parallel learning work. Formation of their measurement/update architecture, compatible composition with this selector process, shared transfer and evaluative action routing remain open; this study does not claim that those parallel results have completed these connections. Local adaptation does not itself establish broader concern, justified standing or favorable historical direction. The unified foundation remains ongoing.
