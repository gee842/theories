# Independent review: formed machinery, acquired prediction and paid action

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review of the next connection after [updater formation](UPDATER-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md): **formed, revisable sensing/updating machinery → acquired prediction corrected after dependency change → an actual paid action using that prediction**. This is not external peer review or empirical validation.

**Status: accepted as a finite conditional construction after correction of one shared implementation error.** The criteria below were recorded before the new comparisons. The final independent reconstruction agrees with every prespecified case and retains the adverse outcomes. Acceptance does not establish identified chemistry, calibrated inference over arbitrary plants, superiority over conventional feedback or shared concern.

## Existing results that the next construction must retain

The [paid teaching interface](CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md) supplies conditions for an acquired operational target, a paid memory update and valid sampling. An acquired target is not automatically an action preference. The [transfer-reliability interface](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/TRANSFER-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) further distinguishes a mean coefficient, an individual history's choice, a statistically justified decision and the resources needed to execute it. Its stationary evidence guarantees cannot be retained after a change without accounting for that change and the actual correction rule.

The [controlled-refinement result](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md) requires compatible complete observations and retained controller memory in addition to world-history correspondence. Resource correspondence does not supply omitted sensing or writing costs. An observation's order may matter to an estimator even when aggregate counts agree. Several physical reactions implementing one update need a corresponding account of their intermediate states and expenditures.

The updater construction provides recyclable backing slots under a declared conversion law. The parallel [material selector construction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/MATERIAL-SELECTOR-COMPOSITION.md) instead consumes typed parts into incorporated inventory. These are different material interfaces. The new construction must choose an explicit one and carry its stocks, rather than borrow whichever accounting convention is convenient at each stage.

## Required causal and material connection

The sensor, record, updater and action coupling must become operative through specified formation events. Their new capacity cannot appear through a Boolean flag while leaving its required material and fuel uncharged. A multifunctional module and a bounded register are legitimate coarse structures if their capacity and assembly law are explicit inputs. The model must not quietly gain a third hardware slot or unlimited cost-free memory from the two-slot predecessor.

The actual prepared or commissioned state must be the new mechanism's input. Previously spent fuel remains spent; failed preparations remain in unconditional statistics. A demonstration conditional on a favorable entrance must be separately labeled and cannot replace the actual preparation distribution. Additional source must be declared at the original preparation or enter by an explicit, accounted event.

The prediction target must be stated before selecting a policy: for example, the probability of a usable return under a particular chosen action. Teaching input must come from an actually acquired consequence, tagged with the action and reference conditions when these matter. An analyst's hidden regime, future outcome, preferred action or desired score cannot be an undeclared observation. If missing return can also reflect packet noise, it cannot be decoded as an opposite regime without proving that inference's assumptions.

Acquisition, observation processing, writing, retention, revision and use need declared prices and timing. Return occurring after an action cannot pay that action's earlier initiation. A record obtained without enough remaining support for writing or action is a retained failure. Measurement accuracy alone does not establish an operational learner.

## What counts as prediction correction and causal use

The model must distinguish prediction, updater state and selected action. A response change without a retained predictive variable is insufficient for this milestone. A predictive variable that changes but never affects a paid command is likewise insufficient.

A strong functional history should contain an acquired prechange prediction, the same retained prediction becoming misleading after an unannounced dependency change, paid new evidence and an update, and a subsequent paid action changed by that revised memory. Its actual consequence must remain in the history. Destroying a record during module replacement and learning from an empty register is a different achievement from correcting an inherited prediction; both may be useful but must be distinguished.

No update, forgetting rule or reset may read the externally supplied change clock. A reset may follow an actually available signal, paid component replacement or another declared accessible event. That causal route and its costs must remain explicit.

An intervention that clamps or scrambles the predictive state while preserving the plant, acquired observations and payment rules should distinguish causal use from a correlated observer statistic. Report actual action events and outcomes in each history, not only the predictor's mean or the proportion of surviving learners whose means look favorable.

## Comparators and required counterexamples

Use an explicit conventional learner with the same resources, evidence and hardware opportunities. An exact conventional realization can legitimately tie the material description. Also retain a competent direct-record response when it can act on the same acquired signal before a more elaborate prediction-and-write sequence. Different realized spending is a consequence to measure; equal initial resources do not imply equal completed updates.

Required adverse regimes include insufficient formation/startup reserve, unaffordable writing or action, misleading/noisy evidence, stale confidence after change, and a useful learned estimate routed into an unhelpful action by an alternative coupling. A model need not make all these comparisons favorable. A negative result can establish the cost or information boundary that the next proposed explanation must meet.

The closed milestone may be a constructive example, an exact obstruction, a sharper interface condition or a discriminating intervention. Its chemical repertoire, sensor calibration, update arithmetic, action preference and memory capacity remain premises unless this very construction explains them. Private predictive accuracy and local return do not establish shared concern, moral standing or wisdom.

The [saved independent record](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/independent-check.json) verifies all **64 case-arms and 1,728 reported metrics**, every preparation terminal probability, trial-weighted forecast loss, exposure coverage, source hash and whole-history energy identity. Its continuation audit visits 8,754 memoized states and 9,904 positive branches across the comparisons; these are implementation counts, not independent experimental samples.

The preparation reaches the paid interface with probability 2/9 and reserve twenty from original common stock twenty-four. The remaining 7/9 of histories retain their actual unused energy in the unconditional ledger. The post-commissioning horizon does not include the physical time spent preparing. The new functions occupy the two supplied recyclable backing slots and are installed by actual paid old updater/selector formation followed by a three-unit retrofit. This is a new functional recipe with a conserved inherited resource history, not a derivation of a molecular sensor or an equivalence of old R/D algorithms with Slow/Fast writing.

Independent targeted checks additionally verify:

- The ten-exposure complete joined witness, including paid updater replacement while retaining the same predictive circuit. Its conditional prefix probability is 1/16 and its unconditional prefix probability is 1/72. It ends with fuel eighteen and two packets ten; the account is `145 = 4 preparation + 1 adapter + 102 operational expenditure/waste + 38 remaining`.
- A register-only clamp with all other physical state unchanged changes both the command and actual capture. The strict witness's before/after command comparison holds the just-executed action fixed, so changing the tie-breaking `last` field cannot impersonate a causal register write.
- An unavailable matching packet and an available mismatching packet have identical locally accessible transitions, expenditures and receipt. Only the analyst's decoder-error label differs. The record alone cannot distinguish their hidden target.
- Commissioned reserve ten permits a first trial; eleven permits its first failed observation to be written. Three Slow corrective writes, one required updater formation and initiation of the corrected action need twenty-one units; twenty cannot initiate that action. Future return cannot pay an earlier missing charge.
- Paid prototypes survive an unaffordable retrofit without refund or repeated base charge. Updater replacement retains circuit memory; circuit destruction erases unfinished acquisition/correction flags while preserving a previously completed historical witness.
- An active circuit still owes retention rent when its separate updater has expired and cannot be replaced. One remaining fuel unit buys one exposure of retention; the next unpaid exposure destroys the record.

## A shared interpretation error and its correction

The first producer and independent implementation both placed the inactive-updater guard before circuit retention. As a result, an active circuit could retain memory without paying rent whenever its expired updater could not be replaced. Their numerical agreement did not establish conformity with the written protocol. The final protocol audit identified this shared error: retention is an obligation of the active circuit, while an active updater is an additional requirement for a trial.

Both implementations now charge or fail retention before checking whether an updater permits trial, and both include an explicit regression for this boundary. All 64 comparisons were rerun. Capture, correction and forecast readouts stayed the same; some stopped-history reserve and active-memory readouts changed. The [superseded source/results snapshot](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/development/retention-order-before-fix/README.md) preserves the earlier bytes and labels them rejected. This was a correction to a prespecified law, not a change of parameter cases after inspecting their outcomes.

The endpoint formerly called `p_acquired_active_memory` is now `p_written_active_memory`: a paid sham write does not establish acquired information. It measures active memory after a paid write. The stricter correction witness separately requires acquired nonneutral memory, paid correction and later productive use. This distinction is necessary when interpreting frozen-register comparisons.

The complete joined witness establishes that an actual updater change can contribute to correcting the same retained prediction. The aggregate correction probability does not require an updater-type change on every qualifying history; fixed Fast and fixed Slow can also qualify. The material and conventional representations have the same complete distributions. The supplied variable updater does not outperform the equally priced fixed Fast architecture in the primary plant. The cheaper direct-record arm explicitly pays less for its supplied retrofit and cannot be presented as an equal-price advantage for a more elaborate predictor.

The adverse regimes matter to the result. Original common stock twelve cannot fund the expensive circuit's first trial, while the cheaper direct-record arm obtains 11/9 expected captures. Packet yield six leaves the variable arm with zero strict correction probability. Short-lived circuits can destroy a prediction before it is corrected: fixed Slow has zero strict correction probability in the lifetime-two case despite later captures. At packet availability 3/4, the decoder generates false labels, and fixed Slow slightly exceeds fixed Fast in captures. This is a conditional robustness difference, not a general ordering of update rules.

The inverted-action arm has a smaller conditional Brier loss than the useful variable controller while obtaining only 1/9 expected captures and much less trial coverage. Accurately predicting the failure of one's chosen action does not supply a useful action policy. These scores are collected under different endogenous action/stopping distributions; they are not a shared held-out calibration test. The q/4 forecast decoder is supplied, and the experiment does not infer that it is calibrated simply because q is bounded or the noiseless receipt identifies the target.

## Formal and explanatory boundary

The [formal verification record](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/formal/verification.json) reports 34 new audited declarations under Lean 4.29.1. Its dependency report includes the standard `Classical.choice`, `propext` and `Quot.sound`; this is not an absence of all logical foundations. The new relation covers a deterministic-capture, affordable-write subprocess with explicit rent, work/receipt, writing and wear/renewal phases. It establishes the actual formation/retrofit interface, retained-state reversal, finite register bounds, whole ledger, decoder ambiguity, route-specific affordability and complete correction/action witness. It does not prove refinement of the entire stochastic executable, skipped-write or circuit-rebuild branches. The exact probability claims come from separately checked finite enumeration.

The accepted advance is therefore specific: **one declared formed organization can acquire a consequence prediction, retain it while its updater changes, revise it after an unannounced dependency change, and use that paid revision in a later paid productive action, without resetting its resources or receiving an observer's score as its teaching input.** The architecture, two-slot capacity, conversion recipe, exhaustive noiseless target relation, update arithmetic and action-return preference remain supplied local premises. Conditional resource accounting connects this result to its predecessor; it does not by itself derive those premises from the root.

The remaining interfaces include a physically identified realization of the added sensing/register/routing functions, inference when identical records have different causes, transfer under justified observation and memory refinement, and a mechanism connecting one center's useful forecasts to other centers' significance. None is completed by private predictive accuracy, a successful local action or standard logical verification. The [mathematics](PREDICTION-ACTION-MATH.md) and [comparator review](PREDICTION-ACTION-COMPARATORS.md) preserve these limits.
