## The transition and the reason for selecting it

The accepted paid route-revision mechanism changes an installed updater while preserving its acquired record, then exercises and transmits the changed mechanism. Its physical observation and replacement laws are explicit local premises. Repeating another financed cycle would not itself establish that a real system instantiates those interfaces.

This round therefore addresses **consequences and their costs → altered learning behavior → warranted identification of an operative updater**, advancing the existing empirical-support requirement. It combines one retrospective public-data calculation with a mathematical identification argument. It does not import a new biological mechanism into the construction or claim a combined human/synthetic execution.

## Contribution to the common account

The study narrows an empirical inference that the formal construction leaves open. Different subsequent behavior can arise through changed input encoding, available writing, latent memory or an operative updater. A representation that preserves causal interventions preserves an identified updater contrast; arbitrary hidden-state naming does not identify that contrast.

This improves the mapping from the common causal account to measurable systems under completion requirement 5, while keeping the mechanism claims of requirements 2 and 3 conditional. It does not promote the source's opportunity-time manipulation into the synthetic model's fuel ledger, component turnover or successor inheritance. Human task objectives and mature learning capacities remain supplied by this experiment. The paper's trial-level fits and controls remain cited source evidence rather than newly reproduced results.

The next empirical obligation is an assay identifying the retained mechanism's contribution under independently characterized inputs and write opportunities. The next constructive obligation remains earned support and maintained use of the observation/revision architecture, where a downstream contract requires it. Formation and justification of shared evaluative concern and compatible action through succession remain central parallel work. The five governing completion conditions are unchanged; this bounded checkpoint does not complete the foundation.
