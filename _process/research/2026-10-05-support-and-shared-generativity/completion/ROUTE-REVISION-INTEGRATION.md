## Which downstream premise is replaced

The accepted successor-learning process allowed the record to change and be copied while its update route remained fixed. The diagnostics study identified update-behavior classes but originally supplied its observation interface. The subsequent formed monitor and [first-commit handoff](../../2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ROUTE-REVISION-RESOURCE-REVIEW.md) supply actual paid stored evidence, resource stocks, material and ancestry. This study consumes that output to change the installed route and exercise it, rather than renaming record correction as meta-learning.

The constructive chain is:

**actual retained descendant → monitor assembly and first paid observation → paid diagnostic access → equally guarded physical route variation → unchanged paid probes → actual donor funding, copying, child action and hold.**

The same record persists across the swap. What changes is how later acquired outcomes revise it. Its actual post-probe route and record enter the inherited copier, preserving source, parts, actor/recipient separation and all paid failures.

## Failures preserved in acceptance

1. Late target reversal can retain a wrong PRIVATE_ZERO descendant while TARGET has corrected its record but cannot afford copying. Recipient benefit, truth, financial continuation and bare retention are not interchangeable.
2. Stable fixed responses and ordinary proxy histories can avoid the monitoring/revision bill. The equally provisioned whole-history comparisons can favor them.
3. Extra buffers can retain a misleading updater. Immediate copying can retain an unproductive child; two probes can permit a written copy without enough seed.
4. Missing source, parts, contacts, funds and binding block different paid prefixes. A missing copy packet can preserve a seeded child at target sensing, with cash 3 and no invented receipt.
5. The monitor reports updater behavior, not an ecological truth oracle. Missing opportunity can provoke an unnecessary replacement; changing contexts can reverse which candidate earns a return.
6. Conventional finite-table reconstruction exactly matches. The contribution is a causal and resource-preserving composition, not numerical superiority over that equivalent controller.
