## What changed in this integration

The supporting-condition work now has an explicit place in the common theory: it supplies physical renewal mechanisms, capability comparisons and a conditional stationary direction theorem. Faithful reduction and installed-process certification supply requirements for connecting those mechanisms to actual outcomes. Concern and revision studies supply conditional developmental mechanisms at a different level. Their shared vocabulary does not establish a shared stochastic realization.

The new specification adds an operational protocol-to-outcome interface, information-constrained joint capability assays, and two elementary conditional propositions for sequential composition and capability transfer under approximation. These statements expose how results can compose; they do not discharge the missing physical bridges.

The first integrated target is the existing shared-resource model. The broader positive goal remains open. No new experiment was run merely to perform this integration, and earlier saved checks are not counted as new evidence.

The [new stochastic realization](STOCHASTIC-REALIZATION.md) adds a declared event model for that target. It proves nonexplosion, exact material and work-equivalent accounting, and almost-sure absorption for finite material count under positive assembly/wear/leakage and bounded capture. Its energy count is unbounded, so the proof uses a dominating immigration–death process and repeated stopping-time trials rather than a finite-state shortcut. This is a new scoped mathematical construction, not an observed physical law. Functional assays, identified physical rates and directional comparisons remain unfinished.

**Subsequent progress:** the [joint-capability bridge](JOINT-CAPABILITY-BRIDGE.md) now derives finite-horizon joint probabilities from that same event law. It supplies positive construction results and certified incomparable autonomous-run regions at a common reliability. Identified physical rates, broader intervention capacities and universal direction remain open. The [completed joint-formation review](JOINT-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md) separately accepts the strategic formation and reconstruction theorem, without treating equilibrium existence as physical prevalence.

**Physical regulation and later concern integration:** the [regulator realization](PHYSICAL-REGULATION.md) adds fuel binding, inhibition and paid assembly to the same process. The [lag theorem](REGULATION-LAG.md) proves a construction delay and initial reserve loss; the [prespecified comparison](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/README.md) finds lower joint success in all 192 constructed-versus-absent comparisons. This numerical negative result is retained as a boundary of the proposed architecture, not generalized to all regulation. The [completed concern-settlement review](CONCERN-SETTLEMENT-INTEGRATION.md) accepts paid revision, retained grounds and funded succession in a repaired strategic theorem. It supplies no new physical identification or empirical observations.

The [finite-generator response theorem](REGULATION-RESPONSE.md) gives an exact event-to-joint-test contrast and an overflow enclosure for the original process. It is a proved diagnostic using standard semigroup calculus, not an independent reason for a favorable sign or a proof of future-region stochastic dominance. Its role is to locate and explain which physical changes contribute to the measured effect before seeking a general mechanism-class theorem.

**Opportunity and evidence audit, subsequent advance:** the [structural audit](REGULATION-OPPORTUNITY-AUDIT.md) distinguishes free fuel from retained productive work. It proves a restricted storage coupling and a conditional kinetic gain/failure inequality, with the physical implementation of that inequality still open. The [response calculation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/response/README.md) and [independent review](RESPONSE-DIAGNOSIS-REVIEW.md) confirm the regulator's negative net effect and identify irreversible loss when construction uses the last productive unit. Conditional values near the artificial packet boundary need their own error control. This is a post-outcome explanation of a known negative result, not a new prospective directional test.

The [Ceroni evidence audit](PHYSICAL-EVIDENCE-CERONI.md) now reviews the full accepted methods and selected supplementary data. It supplies a bounded empirical opportunity mechanism—expression burden can impair host growth, and paid feedback can relieve it or introduce excessive cost. Installed regulation, formation from absence and evolutionary retention are distinct claims. Exact sources and observed label/summary discrepancies are retained; the evidence neither identifies our G process nor closes the empirical universal-direction interface. The updated specification requires a demonstrated opportunity-loss mechanism and delivered-continuation assays before treating a reserve decline as a reason to construct regulation.

## Conflict and dependency ledger

| Issue | Resolution/status |
|---|---|
| Deterministic active attractor versus finite extinction | Different state spaces and limits. Retain both. Choose finite-time, survival-conditioned, restart-driven or unconditional asymptotic target explicitly. |
| Shared regeneration versus a harmed participant | Different tests. Include the participant's protected condition when claimed; aggregate production does not supply it. Ethical justification is additional. |
| Feasible capacity versus actual use | Capability quantifies over implementable protocols; installed dynamics predict the protocol actually constructed and used. Connect through a formation/selection mechanism. |
| Stationary advantage versus immediate advantage | Different horizons. Existing rates give an explicit short-time failure of dominance despite stationary improvement. |
| Physical coexistence versus nested capability regions | Unproved bridge. More Y can coincide with less C; test joint functions instead of equating population level with generativity. |
| Learned concern versus natural universality | Learning and strategic models have supplied routes and positive/negative regimes. They do not establish their prevalence or universal selection. |
| Finite vocabulary versus noticing an omitted possibility | Admitting new cases within a grammar differs from building a new sensor, action or representation language. Common extended assays and physical construction are required. |
| Free fuel versus future productive function | Recycling converts fuel to usable material in the existing law. Reserve readiness is a valid distinct test; later delivered production needs its own continuation assay. The restricted storage theorem is not a full-network ordering. |
| Useful regulation versus supplied regulation | A physical sensor and controller can be constructed yet reduce joint success. Derive the loss route it prevents, response delay and net consequences; the kinetic inequality alone supplies no physical implementation. |
| Published feedback benefit versus independent prediction | The reviewed biology supports conditional installed feedback, with both gains and costs. Known outcomes, ambiguous source labels and unmeasured controller states cannot supply independent identification or a new held-out validation. |
| Useful paid episode versus spontaneous useful architecture | The allocation theorem constructs G under a supplied reaction law and supported preparation. A reachable favorable state cannot be conditioned on for free when comparing the full preceding process; selection and inheritance need their own dynamics. |
| Fresh delivery versus terminal retention | The paid theorem tests a first protected capture by a deadline. Its success state can subsequently decay. The sequential assay instead requires productive survival throughout its full interval. Neither guarantee silently supplies the other. |
| Complete turnover versus reliable renewal | A four-packet physical path replaces all original carriers and returns; its numerical lower bound is tiny. The all-path damaged-state upper bound separately exposes unreliable repair. Positive path existence cannot be treated as typical behavior. |
| Formed fabrication versus useful selection | Fabricator states remove a free channel switch in the N=4 model. Equal formation opportunities and greater delivery contribution still coexist with negative local frequency drift for the useful type. |
| Reversible episode versus maintained source | One finite source carrier preserves a stopped first-capture test. It does not fund the four-capture turnover, and reverse reactions remove the old absorbing boundary. The three constructions need a new common-model proof before composition. |
| Direction versus stationary turnover | A fixed nonconstant capability map cannot improve from every state of a finite irreducible process at every time. Selected preparations, inter-arrangement stationary comparisons, expanding systems and explicit survivor conditioning have different quantifiers. |
| Classical stochastic model versus theory of everything | Classical specialization is useful but incomplete coverage. General interface does not derive quantum, relativistic or fundamental physical law. |
