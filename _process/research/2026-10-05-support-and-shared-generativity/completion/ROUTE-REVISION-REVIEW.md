### Scientific conclusion and remaining premises

For the ordinary variable-producer ancestry under the designated primary reversal, actual behaviorally changed, productive retained succession has unconditional mass **1/72**, preserving failed ancestors and uniform proposals. Productive retained children overall occur at **1/24**, versus **1/36** for monitoring without repair or ordinary no-monitor operation. This is a finite causal result under the stated law, not an empirical probability or a universal improvement theorem.

The controls are decisive limits. In the controlled stable proxy ancestry, ordinary no-monitor operation produces productive retained children at **1/9**, exceeding monitored blind replacement's **13/144**. A late reversal can fund and retain an incorrectly recorded child while preventing a corrected learner from copying. Immediate copying can preserve wrong records; extra buffers can preserve the proxy; two probes can create an unseeded copied template. Failed copying leaves the old donor and its route present. Consequently the study does not identify existence, retention, truth and generative benefit.

The typed local ledger starts at the retained ancestor: total88 ends as original actor8, recipient11, new dissipation49 and lost packets20. The complete numerical primary mixture starts earlier with total246 and retains predecessor costs. These are distinct accounting boundaries, not two competing totals for one state.
