# The paid teaching interface and its downstream contract

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration under the [governing
roadmap](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md).
The transition addressed is **consequence-sensitive activity → acquired
teaching evidence → maintained prediction → inputs to the concern results**.
The concurrent selector-revision study addresses how an effective selector
changes. The present study establishes what is required to treat a consequence
as a learning input and to compose that input with downstream theorems.

## Result and scope

The [mathematical interface](CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-MATH.md) separates three
obligations: identifiable evidence, affordable acquisition and use, and a valid
sampling/learning law. Its [executable specimens](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/README.md)
retain physical state and costs through each obligation. The
[independent review](CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-REVIEW.md) supplies the exact
verification status and counterexamples. This is a conditional operational
teaching interface; it does not complete the origin of the learner or concern.

An internally measured consequence can replace an unexplained numerical target
for **prediction of that identified consequence**. It cannot silently replace
the original concern model's felt or motivational interpretation, choice of
shared evaluation, or ethical argument. Those remain separate questions.

## What the concern results can now use

| Downstream premise | What is supplied here | What is still required |
|---|---|---|
| An acquired target for a self-related consequence | A task-specific dependency contrast and a budget-loss teacher generated by declared physical transitions | Actual sensor formation, calibration and applicability beyond the supplied mechanism class |
| The coefficient-update recurrence | Exact paid-update moment identity; the earlier mean recurrence under a feasible deterministic teaching schedule and conditional calibration | Origin and maintenance of the updater; noisy mean predictions do not supply pathwise coefficients |
| A learned input used in action | A supplied observation-only controller and coefficient-clamp intervention | Explain why that action coupling forms and persists; identical prediction permits contrary policies |
| Available teaching over time | A finite funded schedule, and a counterexample when funding selects the acquired histories | Sustained renewal, target stability and sampling identification under actual ongoing conditions |
| A maintained positive anchor for later valuation | No such general anchor is established by a mean learning result | Reliability at the relevant decision dates, justified interpretation, and revision of erroneous anchors |
| Recognition and evaluation across distinct centers | Existing transfer and identity-gating counterexamples remain applicable | Acquired correspondences, shared evaluative routing, recognition beyond private usefulness, and justified reasons |

The funded three-round specimen has mean coefficient 37/128, above 1/4, while
only half its histories have a coefficient strictly above 1/4. Thus substituting
the mean into the concern theorem's strict-choice inequality would overclaim
protection. The scarce-reserve specimen has a different all-history mean, 7/32;
among histories retaining enough common fuel for another payment, the
coefficient is zero. This is a concrete reason to preserve failed histories and
their resource conditions when joining learning to continuation.

The gate specimen also distinguishes information from timely use. Three common
units fund probe, coefficient write and first repair before any capture; two do
not. Two nevertheless fund a direct-record response followed by capture and
later writing. The obstruction belongs to the specified architecture and
ordering. It does not establish a universal inefficiency of learning or require
every effective response to pass through the supplied predictor.

## Connection to the common formal account

The parallel [accessible-revision interface](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/LEARNING-INTERFACE.md)
already proves that identical complete controller inputs cannot produce
different required revisions from the same inherited memory. The numerical
target criterion here is a specialization of that identification issue, not an
independent discovery. This study adds explicit paid writing, target-reference
changes, stochastic sampling and nonlinear downstream-threshold boundaries.

The concrete formation law is now connected to the universal support bounds by
[FormationSupport.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/FormationSupport.lean).
The [formation accounting extension](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/README.md)
reuses that bridge and adds a common-fuel boundary, reaction/leakage separation,
exact cancellation of capture credit against its source debit, and an explicit
zero-fuel-cost event. These are compatible accounts of the same process,
rather than separate evidence that learning has emerged. In particular, a
generic positive-demand event-count theorem cannot be applied to damage and
wear merely because other events consume fuel.

## The next connected task

Join a consequence-revising selector to an actual acquired and retained target
under the same event law. Its output must supply the next mechanism's input
without reinstating a source, clearing failed histories or assuming the meaning
of an old signal survives a changed dependency. Prove the claimed kind of
learning: changes in a response, a predictor, an effective selector and an
update architecture are distinct achievements.

Then address the remaining concern premises explicitly: how self-relevant
prediction enters evaluation, how correspondences with other centers are
acquired and corrected, and why shared evaluation can develop or fail. Private
support return can motivate a causal mechanism without settling another
center's standing. The unified foundation remains ongoing.
