# Independent review: formation and replacement of updater mechanisms

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review of the next connection after [costed selector revision](SELECTOR-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md). The named obligation is to make alternative reconstruction/maintenance mechanisms actual costly structures, while connecting their formation to the earlier response-formation process. This is not external peer review or empirical validation.

**Status: accepted as a conditional joined construction under an explicit additional conversion law.** Actual predecessor formation, capture and remaining resources now fund an updater-bearing continuation without a reserve reset. A paid change of updater kind can change the selector-reconstruction rule within the same compartment. The new adapter and available updater implementations remain supplied chemistry. Conventional equivalence and a stronger fixed-directed comparator prevent a claim of a new class of learning or general superiority.

## The required backward connection

The old process has seven coordinates: free A, free H, resident type, intact access site, local fuel, common fuel and remaining source. A joined construction must preserve their actual successor values. A supplied extra molecular reaction can legitimately assemble a new structure, but the reaction repertoire itself remains an input. Paying for an instance of an assumed architecture does not derive the architecture's chemical possibility.

At least one formed precursor, catalyst, or repaired access relation from the predecessor must actually enter the enabling conditions or material transformation for the new structure. Merely placing a fresh controller beside an old process and renaming its newly initialized reserve does not establish this connection. Any future support stream must belong to the declared initial resources or enter through explicit source events with their own account.

Material must also balance. An atom transformed into an updater or selector cannot remain available as free precursor or an A/H molecule. If a module occupies material, the effective precursor count used by old copying and formation channels must reflect it. A local fuel unit cannot pay a common-fuel bill without a declared access or transfer event. One predecessor source unit cannot silently become a four-unit packet.

When new reactions are disabled and new structure coordinates are absent, the old event law should reappear with exactly the same guards, updates and, where claimed, rates. This is a conservative embedding. It does not imply that every active extended history projects to an old history: occupied material, new uses of fuel and new structures may change which old events are available. Any stronger projection claim needs its own argument.

## A prior successful path that cannot finance the extension

The preceding primary mixed-copy history begins with `M=3,F=4,E=1` and ends with two A molecules and one H, no free precursor and no common, local or source fuel. No positive-cost assembly can begin at that endpoint. This is a required retained obstruction, not a failed theorem to hide by resetting reserves.

A positive example may start from a larger declared original reserve or obtain further support through an explicitly extended event law. The additional resource must stay visible in the comparison and in the actual preparation path. A source that cannot be accessed before architecture formation cannot finance the formation's earlier payment merely because it will become useful afterward.

## Required forward distinction

The updater state must change the conditional law for selector reconstruction or maintenance, rather than be another name for the selected response orientation. Switching between random reassembly and directed reconstruction is an admissible finite example if their physical structures, formation and maintenance are explicit. A witness must show the updater being formed, used to change the selector proposal law, replaced within the same compartment, and used again under the new law. Mere frequencies of independently supplied controllers are insufficient.

The proposed updater's own formation and retention law will still be supplied unless a further mechanism explains it. Adding one finite layer must not be described as open-ended recursive learning, learned objectives, or completion of selection over all selectors. Exact conventional realization remains a valid comparison; a fixed directed controller with the same prices and information remains a required strong comparator where physically available.

All histories must retain formation failures, depletion, misleading feedback and adverse timing. An observer's score or revision-success metric must not be read by the updater. Source/expense/material ledgers, conservative-embedding claims and whole-history probabilities require independent checks before acceptance.

## The accepted preparation and interface

The [protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/PROTOCOL.md) was declared in-session before the numerical comparison. The primary preparation uses the existing reaction law with two material units, 18 common fuel units and 97 source units. Its productive coefficients are one; bulk copying, damage, wear and leakage are set to zero. These larger original stocks and the zero-loss reference are explicit supplied conditions, not a restoration of the earlier depleted fixture.

The independent implementation represents free precursor, free A/H, resident A/H, intact/damaged site and three fuel locations separately. Exact recursion over the original preparation gives commissioning probability **2/9**. Every successful entrance contains resident A, free H, the repaired site, one local fuel unit, 14 common units and 96 source units. The three successful prefix classes have probabilities `1/12`, `1/12` and `1/18`. Every other absorbing outcome is retained, with its actual unspent resources.

There is no competing positive-rate old reaction at a qualifying entrance. Both material units are occupied, the site is intact, the local store is full, and the nuisance rates are zero. Taking the adapter there does not suppress an old race. This is an eventual embedded-jump-chain calculation; it does not bound the elapsed time of preparation. The new exposure clock begins only upon commissioning. The seven ninths that never commission do not silently receive fresh fuel or a running new valve.

The adapter consumes one actually formed A and one H into two recyclable backing slots, pays one unit from common fuel, retools the repaired site and explicitly mobilizes its local fuel unit. The commissioned reserve is therefore `14−1+1=14`. The remaining 96 source units become sixteen six-unit packets. There is no source-unit multiplication and no inaccessible local fuel paying the adapter's earlier charge. Both functional structures must still be formed at their subsequent prices.

The interface remains conditional on an added conversion reaction and a coarse material interpretation. The two backing slots and the undifferentiated retained inventory are conserved in that declared representation; this is not an identification of molecular stoichiometry or a derivation of digital structures from real A/H molecules. The old depleted endpoint remains impossible to commission. Independent boundary checks find zero commissioning at original common reserve four or below, even with plentiful source; at reserve five the interface can be paid but that does not imply enough money to form and operate both new structures.

## Functional updater changes and truthful witness bookkeeping

The primary updater lifetime is four, while the selector lifetime is two. Thus a current updater can govern a reconstruction from an expired selector's residue before the updater itself expires. Random reconstruction and directed reversal are distinct conditional laws on that same accessible residue. Their first orientation proposal is identical when no selector has existed yet; the model does not count that initial choice as a test of their distinctive updating behavior.

The strong witness requires a capture before the first condition boundary, an actual residue-informed proposal by that same updater instance, a paid change to the other updater kind after the boundary, a proposal governed by the new instance, and a capture that pays its maintenance. Current-instance use and capture flags are reset at every updater formation. Global labels such as “R was used once somewhere” cannot falsely qualify a later R instance. None of these observer flags controls a proposal, price or renewal decision.

The primary witness probability is **1/72 from the original unformed preparation**. An independent calculation predicted this before inspecting the result: commissioning contributes `2/9`; initial R with an initially useful selector contributes `1/4`; an unsuccessful R reconstruction after reversal contributes `1/2`; formation of D, which reverses the retained obsolete selector, contributes `1/2`. Their product is `1/72`. An initially wrong R that corrects itself before reversal has spent more reserve and cannot finance this same later failed-block route. The code retains that path-dependent insolvency rather than assigning every survivor a common reserve.

Updater changes alone are insufficient. With initial reserve twelve, some paths change updater kind but none satisfies the strong witness. With synchronized lifetimes two, updater changes are common but the previous updater never survives long enough to govern the needed residue-informed reconstruction. The strict witness remains zero in that declared case. A zero witness for fixed D likewise does not mean poor adaptation: it means the updater kind never changes, which is part of this witness's definition.

## Comparisons and failures that constrain the claim

| Primary arm | Expected commissioned captured packets, from original preparation | Strong updater-revision witness | Final readiness |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Variable updater | 335/144 | 1/72 | 17/96 |
| Conventional hierarchical automaton | 335/144 | 1/72 | 17/96 |
| Fixed R hardware, fully paid | 61/36 | 0 | 1/8 |
| Fixed D hardware, fully paid | 26/9 | 0 | 2/9 |

The variable mechanism improves captured return over fixed R in this preparation and remains inferior to fixed D. Comparison only with fixed R would confound the value of allowing updater changes with the fact that half the initial variable updaters are already D.

A clearly labeled **post-result derived contrast** addresses that confound. The fair mixture of the two fixed-type arms preserves the initial R/D uncertainty and their paid hardware while preventing kind changes. Variable formation captures `5/144` more packets than this frozen mixture in the primary case. With original reserve 24, the difference reverses to `−1/48`. Those exact contrasts use the already declared arm results; they are not additional preregistered outcomes. They show that permitting type changes can help or hurt under the same resources and initial type uncertainty. No universal benefit of updater variation follows.

The twelve primary cases show zero inherited-selector maintenance by a previously unused updater. A subsequent **supplemental counterpath**, combining short updater lifetime with noisy return, establishes why that credit problem nevertheless matters. Fix the hidden condition at zero, start from commissioned reserve 14, set updater lifetime one and selector lifetime two. R forms selector zero and captures; an unavailable packet then expires R while that selector remains active; D is paid to form and receives another capture from the inherited selector before governing any reconstruction. The successive reserves are `14→13→11→11`. The exact conditional path probability is `9/512`. This is a valid paid possibility, not an observed effect in the primary matrix or an unconditional rate of such inheritance.

The [supplemental diagnostics](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/derived-diagnostics.json), their twelve linear contrasts and this path were independently checked. They retain their after-results status. Together with the strong-witness definition, they prevent updater presence or maintenance from automatically being interpreted as evidence that its updating rule produced the current return.

## Integration judgment

This round closes a specific gap left by the selector study: the new structures can now be reached by an explicit paid conversion from the actual old formation process, and alternative update rules can themselves be formed, used and replaced within its continuing resource history. The transformation law, available implementations, persistent scaffold and rule choosing those implementations remain supplied. The result does not make that repertoire emerge from an otherwise unchanged molecular law.

The main demonstrated advance is a constructive interface with exact constraints and counterexamples. It is conventionally expressible, does not outperform the strong directed benchmark, and establishes neither historical originality nor empirical identification. A further useful connection must explain richer changes to the updater repertoire or connect these physically funded histories to the separately specified measurement, prediction and evaluative-action architecture, while retaining its information, cost and credit-assignment obligations. Concern, wisdom and the whole unified foundation remain open.
