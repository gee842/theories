# Independent review: formation and replacement of updater mechanisms

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review of the next connection after [costed selector revision](SELECTOR-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md). The named obligation is to make alternative reconstruction/maintenance mechanisms actual costly structures, while connecting their formation to the earlier response-formation process. This is not external peer review or empirical validation.

**Status: acceptance criteria recorded; proposed event law and results pending.**

## The required backward connection

The old process has seven coordinates: free A, free H, resident type, intact access site, local fuel, common fuel and remaining source. A joined construction must preserve their actual successor values. A supplied extra molecular reaction can legitimately assemble a new structure, but the reaction repertoire itself remains an input. Paying for an instance of an assumed architecture does not derive the architecture's chemical possibility.

At least one formed precursor, catalyst, or repaired access relation from the predecessor must actually enter the enabling conditions or material transformation for the new structure. Merely placing a fresh controller beside an old process and renaming its newly initialized reserve does not establish this connection. Any future support stream must belong to the declared initial resources or enter through explicit source events with their own account.

Material must also balance. An atom transformed into an updater or selector cannot remain available as free precursor or an A/H molecule. If a module occupies material, the effective precursor count used by old copying and formation channels must reflect it. A local fuel unit cannot pay a common-fuel bill without a declared access or transfer event. One predecessor source unit cannot silently become a four-unit packet.

When new reactions are disabled and new structure coordinates are absent, the old event law should reappear with exactly the same guards, updates and, where claimed, rates. This is a conservative embedding. It does not imply that every active extended history projects to an old history: occupied material, new uses of fuel and new structures may change which old events are available. Any stronger projection claim needs its own argument.

## A prior successful path that cannot finance the extension

The preceding primary mixed-copy history begins with `M=3,F=4,E=1` and ends with two A molecules and one H, no free precursor and no common, local or source fuel. No positive-cost assembly can begin at that endpoint. This is a required retained obstruction, not a failed theorem to hide by resetting reserves.

A positive example may start from a larger declared original reserve or obtain further support through an explicitly extended event law. The additional resource must stay visible in the comparison and in the actual preparation path. A source that cannot be accessed before architecture formation cannot finance the formation's earlier payment merely because it will become useful afterward.

## Required forward distinction

The updater state must change the conditional law for selector reconstruction or maintenance, rather than be another name for the selected response orientation. Switching between random reassembly and directed reconstruction is an admissible finite example if their physical structures, formation and maintenance are explicit. A witness must show the updater being formed, used to change the selector proposal law, replaced within the same compartment, and used again under the new law. Mere frequencies of independently supplied controllers are insufficient.

The proposed updater's own formation and retention law will still be supplied unless a further mechanism explains it. Adding one finite layer must not be described as open-ended recursive learning, learned objectives, or completion of selection over all selectors. Exact conventional realization remains a valid comparison; a fixed directed controller with the same prices and information remains a required strong comparator where physically available.

All histories must retain formation failures, depletion, misleading feedback and adverse timing. An observer's score or revision-success metric must not be read by the updater. Source/expense/material ledgers, conservative-embedding claims and whole-history probabilities require independent checks before acceptance.
