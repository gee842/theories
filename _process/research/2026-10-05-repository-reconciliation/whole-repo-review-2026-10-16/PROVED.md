This section lists what the repository actually establishes and how firmly. It covers the pure Lean core, the periphery, conditional results, hand arguments and empirical analyses. Each headline result gives its Lean declarations, its conditions, a status and an honest assessment: is it substantive, definitional or near-trivial, and does the prose (the opening, the chapter, records) claim more than the proof shows? Declarations are in `experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/` unless another path is given. "Chapter" means `theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md`.

**Status legend**
- **core**: Lean-checked in the pure core (prelude only, empty axiom report).
- **periphery**: Lean-checked outside the core. It may use `propext`, `Quot.sound` or `Classical.choice`, Mathlib or Lean 4.30.0-rc2.
- **conditional**: checked, but it takes a normative or substantive premise as a hypothesis.
- **argued**: a hand proof, finite computation or philosophical argument, with no proof assistant.
- **empirical**: an analysis of data.
- **claimed / unverified**: no checkable artifact exists in the repository.

**Assessment tags**
- **S**: substantive.
- **E**: elementary but real (counting, accounting, pigeonhole).
- **D**: definitional or near-trivial (`rfl`/`Iff.rfl`, one-line unfolding, the conclusion restates a hypothesis).
- **K**: a known classical result reproved constructively.

### 2.11 What "zero axiom" means here (foundations critique)

1. **It means an empty Lean axiom report for source declarations, not zero premises.** The core avoids `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`, and that is a real constructive discipline. Every substantive premise enters as a theorem hypothesis instead. Any axiomatic theory can be restated this way (the deduction theorem), so this form of "zero axiom" alone excludes nothing. The chapter keeps "axiom" and "premise" apart (line 58). AGENTS.md, GOALS and README shorthand often do not.
2. **The premise-free laws are definitional or arithmetic**: `change_readings` is six `.rfl`; `over_stretch` and `keep_pace` are counting. The chapter itself says they "exclude no unfolding". Every dynamical law is conditional on named premises or on representation commitments.
3. **The representation builds in more than its declared commitments.** The declared ones are discrete steps, ℕ amounts and a shared index. In addition:
   - one global linear step order, although §0 lists a definite global causal order as "not assumed"; indefinite causal order is a principled counterexample to the shared index;
   - every condition is a scalar ℕ stock, and dependence is threshold admission (need ≤ level);
   - change is quantity exchange, and the rest relatum absorbs any imbalance, so every level sequence is a run of exchange;
   - a fixed smallest unit, with dissipation losing ≥ 1 unit per uptake;
   - a pre-given type of formations, so the "generative space" is inclusion within a fixed menu.

   Several headline laws depend on ℕ integrality: closed systems come to rest, novelty needs gain, bounded parts forget, and frequency rather than strength sets runaway. With real levels a bounded part can be new at every step (irrational rotation), and record `laws-at-finer-units` shows rest fails at refining units. The OPENING generalizes these laws without the qualifier.
4. **The unity theorems are encodability results**, not evidence of one relational ontology: `World.unity`, `Contextual.world`, `Frame.world`, `Shown.run`, and P130's `run_world`. They show the representations encode one another. The families are still joined by correspondence (792–1,019 theorems apart).
5. **The decidability boundary is the genuinely deep part.** `law_iff_em`, `lpo_of_finite_ends`, `not_through_sensitive_iff_rule` (WLPO), `vanishDecision_iff_lpoRule`, `refutable_passes_iff_markov`, `eitherThroughout_iff_llpoForDecided`, `origin_of_propositions_iff` (DNE), `runs_iff_selection` (DC), `plans_iff_choice`, `matchesParts_of_compact_iff_em`, and the counting-and-listing equivalences together map exactly where each classical principle enters a law of change. This is the most original and best-proved line in the repository, and it is under-credited relative to the constructive reverse-mathematics literature.
6. **The metaethical results are near-definitional.** `significant_iff` is propagation along connectivity. `Unrestricted` makes sig ≡ ⊤. `significance_undetermined` toggles a free parameter. The fold is logically clean: the theory now **asserts no ethical axiom**, and its normative content becomes an open question of warrant, located by the decomposition at the step of invariance. But the result that would most change the ethical route has not been attempted: making significance a function of `World` data, for example from registration or wanting continuation.
7. **Representability is the open crux.** The open record `theory-of-becoming-foundation` correctly says that "every becoming is representable as the spine represents worlds" is unproved. Most objections above concern what the representation omits or adds. Under the nondual method they are not refutations of the full account, but neither do they support the opening's universality.
