## Assumptions that physical renewal results do not discharge

The existing [integration contract](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/JOINT-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md), especially its input contract and I1–I6 table, remains the correct boundary:

1. **Significance and transfer.** Primitive self teaching, informative correspondence and a transferable evaluation architecture remain supplied. Learning a nonzero recipient coefficient is explained within that architecture; the architecture's origin, scope and ethical warrant are not derived from chemical persistence.
2. **Continuing institutional position.** The controller inherits an account and access to a rival input. It values current account cash. This does not derive property/access rights, a predecessor's willingness to bequeath resources, public legitimacy, or free choice of controller roles.
3. **Actual resource production.** Calibration receipts, production income and fallback receipts require continuing opportunities and material support. Cash-prefix feasibility is not a complete energy/material account. A separate reaction network cannot discharge these inputs without a mapping of states, units, costs, sources, deadlines and delivered functions.
4. **Known and restricted options.** Actors know the declared game and anticipate its choices; the theorem does not derive discovery or convergence to that equilibrium. A feasible cheap forced-open commitment can defeat protection even if the explicit identity filter is expensive. The cheapest actual alternatives must therefore be represented in any application.
5. **Evidence and effective control.** The finite sensor refinement supplies known fixture labels, stable calibration-to-operation correspondence, timely observation and priced bypass. Post-calibration drift is a retained failure. Formal reaction accounting does not prove trustworthy interpretation or operational authority.
6. **Quantifiers of protection.** Reserve invariance covers declared responses along selected controller continuations. Patient safety holds on the protective equilibrium path. A harmful paid bypass remains physically possible. Equilibrium existence is neither protection against every admissible adversary nor attraction, evolutionary spread, or empirical prevalence.
7. **Maintenance and direction.** Every fresh controller repays the modeled lesson and apparatus, but stationary commercial conditions and the continuing role remain. Physical formation/turnover results have their own preparations, return kernels and rare-event limits; they do not establish indefinite reliability or universal favorable direction for this institutional process.

The integration should retain both achievements: a completed conditional joint mechanism and the unfinished generative explanation of its starting conditions. Neither a physical positive example nor a physical failure turns the institutional theorem into a universal conclusion. The next foundational bridge remains the formation of consequence-sensitive, revisable regulation from actual organizational dependencies, connected to the later learning and concern mechanisms with explicit interfaces.
