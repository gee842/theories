# Research studies and verification

The [current research index](../../RESEARCH.md) is the reading path across the recent theory, studies, proofs, reviews, and experiments. It identifies current work and evidence limits without moving the historical study folders.

This directory holds research development and its supporting records. Current integrated formulations live in [theories](../../theories/becoming/README.md); executable experiments live in [experiments](../../experiments/README.md); unmodified imported sources and provenance live in [sources](../../sources/SOURCES.md).

When continuing a study, edit its repository files directly. Dated chat folders and downloadable bundles are historical working or delivery copies. Refer to the [repository working conventions](../../AGENTS.md) and update the single research index when a branch advances.
