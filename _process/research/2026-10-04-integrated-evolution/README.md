# Research on the conditions for continued becoming

This is the working record for Ren's research program connecting what spreads to what makes continued becoming possible. It records findings, decisions, rejected claims, and the next questions. The north star is to discover and test universal structural constraints that can help human coordination and AI development under incomplete knowledge. A flexible collection of models is a starting instrument; it is not the final ambition.

Ren explicitly requested this direction and a continuing record on 4 October 2026. The elaborations and proofs here are AI-generated research assistance. Claims become stronger through derivation, counterexamples, prior-art comparison, and empirical tests; agent agreement alone does not validate them.

## The foundational target

Find constraints on how transformation can continue when the conditions enabling it can themselves be consumed, damaged, replaced, or regenerated. Explain how selective success changes those conditions and the ability to detect and correct their deterioration. Make the human commitment to shared agency, legitimate revision, and future generations explicit.

The 5 October reading intervention extends this target to how purposes, perceptions, conceivable responses, and standards of judgment are formed within a situation. A system's production of experience and interpretation belongs inside the explanation. Improving estimates within a supplied frame does not by itself explain how that frame can be challenged or revised; see the [situated correction framework](../../../experiments/evaluator-succession/SITUATED-CORRECTION.md).

The first candidate principle is conditional and applies across material implementations: ongoing activity that depends on exhaustible enabling functions must maintain access to those functions through renewal, substitution, or supported inputs. Success at spreading does not establish that this condition is satisfied. The dependency network, timescale, activity level, and system boundary must be identified independently.

Universality is a research target. Exact identities, impossibility results over declared model classes, empirical regularities, definitions, and ethical commitments have different evidential status. No single scalar measure of goodness or inevitable evolutionary direction is assumed.

## Questions driving the next results

1. Which constraints on renewal and attainable correction hold independently of detailed implementation?
2. When do corrective capacities remain viable when their own repair, information, resources, and authority are endogenous?
3. How much can be certified with incomplete observation, and which extra observations change the available interventions?
4. When does selection favor or undermine mechanisms satisfying these constraints?
5. How can succession and functional novelty be represented without reducing becoming to persistence of a fixed identity?
6. How do institutions produce the experience, frames, and purposes through which they are evaluated, and when can consequential experience become an effective reason to revise them?

## How findings are retained

Each substantive result receives a claim identifier, assumptions, a proof or evidence pointer, counterexamples or failure conditions, and a stated relationship to existing research. Hypotheses remain hypotheses when a numerical illustration works. A failed prediction stays in the record. A later revision records what changed rather than deleting the earlier formulation.

Research memos and calculations live here. Completed research snapshots enter `sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/`, with entries in `sources/SOURCES.md` and a link from the relevant canonical theory. Routine exploration does not silently rewrite the ontology or normative commitments.

## Fourth continuation cycle

On 5 October 2026, an implemented learner generated contextual policies from records and tested them on fresh randomized cases. It did not receive the true hidden rule or a prevalidated proposal. The frozen held-out evaluation covered 256 independent seed clusters, fourteen arms, and two hazard conditions, with paired random inputs across arms. These are 7,168 correlated trajectories, not independent replications.

- [Experiment and reproduction record](../../../experiments/situated-discovery/README.md): frozen inputs, four mechanism tests, complete development and evaluation records, analysis, and an inspected figure.
- [Mathematical audit](candidate-discovery-math.md): fresh-data error control, information and funding constraints, and the validity of the implemented cash-delayed trial schedule.

Protected inquiry validated and deployed a useful policy in all 256 hazard-present worlds; denying standing or deployment authority preserved the same validated evidence but prevented deployment. No policy passed in the protected absent-hazard worlds, while inquiry imposed a cost. Severe funding withdrawal was unable to pay for even one test by construction; less severe schedules eventually succeeded but differed in timeliness. Exact financing constraints explain that part of the result. The supplied grammar, common purposes, calendar funding rule, and free persistence of linked records remain limitations. This is a synthetic mechanism study, not an empirical validation of institutions or alignment.

Cap 32 raises the mean above a million and gives 99.914% survival through 1,000 rounds. Crediting only one context's otherwise unchanged evidence shortens cap-eight lifetime to 20.3577. A paid floor or predictable allocation can maintain inquiry; null controls expose continued expense and recurrent false adoptions. ## Next research decision

Give participants one concrete, funded action they control after refusing: a private backup service that reduces their own outage loss without repairing the shared plant. Allow the same backup during cooperation. Compare an optional allocation from actual earned output into private custody with an equal nominal but revocable pooled earmark, keeping initial resources and physical technology fixed. Track continuing loss, refusal loss, private backup access and joint working cash separately. A change in acceptance margin D−C decomposes as ΔD−ΔC: worse refusal can manufacture willingness without improving continuation. Calling this coercion additionally requires a defended entitlement, not just an inequality.

Private custody reuses the present irreversible-transfer primitive and remains an explicit assumption; it does not explain protection against confiscation. Do not add independent diagnosis while the refusal branch forbids every action that could use its information. Do not merely rename existing future contingent plans as promises: a new credible claim or loan must change enforceable actions and have an accounted cost. This next test is unrun. Formation of purposes, legitimacy and the reproduction of enforcement remain explicit open questions.

### Earlier next-step proposal, retained for continuity

The next mathematical step is one joint model of the rules governing observation, allocation, and retirement. Current practice shares are endogenous, but the copying response, production split, and handoff convention remain fixed within their respective modules. Explain how a rule is chosen, financed, enforced, and replaced through explicit returns and uptake, including the maintenance of the authority doing the choosing. Distinguish individual incentives, collective selection, and deliberate institutional choice rather than assigning all of them the same update rule.

The next experimental design should manipulate support, actionable information, and enforceable authority independently at matched current performance. Predict before running it which intervention restores timely correction. Include replacement of a failed provider and withdrawal of temporary subsidy, so preservation of a form is separated from regeneration of a function. No such empirical run has yet been performed.

Before a larger experiment, use the smallest integrated mechanism that separates these alternatives. The three modules are not evidence that one set of equations already explains all domains. Their value is the independently testable failure modes and intervention requirements they expose.
