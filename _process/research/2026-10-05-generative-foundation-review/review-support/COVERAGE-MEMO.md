## 1. Minimum explanatory spine

A complete exposition should connect six distinct operations: processes produce consequences; those consequences alter enabling conditions and the distribution of carriers; altered conditions change future actions, observations, representations and selection rules; these changes can retain or disable correction; replacement transfers or loses the means of continuation; situated evaluation judges the resulting consequences through explicitly declared commitments. None requires that everyone optimize, that a system maximize one scalar, or that institutions have the same goals as their participants.

The general state architecture `z=(x,e,n,r,phi)` is a role schema, not five independent substances or a minimal ontology. A variable can perform several roles without being counted several times. A finite model must retain sufficient memory. An ecosystem need not possess represented goals. The common causal structure does not establish the same numerical dynamics in every domain.

Sources: [general architecture, lines 17–58](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/README.md:17); [multilevel state, local information and plural relations](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/systems-dynamics/review/MULTILEVEL.md:13); [mechanism-first canonical revision](/Users/ren/theories/theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/README.md:303).

The dossier must distinguish four closures:

1. **Representation closure:** retained states preserve the declared consequences of interventions.
2. **Actual dynamical closure:** the installed process stays within declared acceptable outcomes.
3. **Renewal and response:** actual recurring events restore or transmit capacities, rather than merely preserving a label.
4. **Normative and institutional justification:** whose interests count, which changes are legitimate, and how disagreement is treated. Mathematical closure cannot supply these commitments.

## 4. Latest selection-of-selectors statement in one consistent notation

Use v7.3 only. Let `q>=0` be influence capacity, `p` carrier share, and `y=p ybar` its fixed domain profile. An appropriable fraction `chi in [0,1]` of the rule support produced by this capacity benefits its own lineage. Support begins at zero and evolves during the declared selection horizon `T_h` as

\[
\dot z_i^\chi=\varepsilon_i\left(\chi q\sum_j\widehat b_{ij}y_j-d_i z_i^\chi\right).
\]

Set

\[
L_i=1-\frac{1-e^{-\varepsilon_i d_i T_h}}{\varepsilon_i d_i T_h},\qquad
\widehat G_{ij}=\frac{a_i\widehat b_{ij}}{c_i d_i},\qquad
M_{ij}=c_iL_i\widehat G_{ij}.
\]

The horizon-averaged net return is

\[
\Delta\pi(q)=\chi q p^2\bar y^\top M\bar y
-f p^{\xi_f}q-\frac{\gamma p^{\xi_v}}{1+\eta}q^{1+\eta},
\]

where `eta,gamma>0`, `f>=0`, and `xi_f,xi_v in [0,1]`. Consequently

\[
\partial_q\Delta\pi
=\chi p^2\bar y^\top M\bar y-fp^{\xi_f}-\gamma p^{\xi_v}q^\eta.
\]

For a positive benefit coefficient and `f>0`, initial investment pays iff

\[
p>p^*=
\left(\frac{f}{\chi\bar y^\top M\bar y}\right)^{1/(2-\xi_f)}.
\]

At or below this threshold the concave return excludes a positive net gain from any positive `q` in this fixed trait family. With `f=0` and a positive appropriable channel, the initial derivative is positive. Under faithful transmission, selection changes mean influence capacity through `Cov(q,Delta pi(q))`; strict increase requires nondegenerate trait variation and an increasing return on its occupied support. The quadratic factor is **share squared**, not influence capacity squared.

This is not a universal claim that every winner must seek domination. It requires appropriability, persistence over the selection horizon, a suitable carrier size and cost law, a real selection/transmission mechanism, and the frozen competitive background of the comparison. Rival response, costs changed by reform, other trait dimensions, institutions preventing harmful execution, and altruistic or collective provision can alter the conclusion. Support shared equally cancels in the relative advantage. A fixed current gradient is not the total derivative of a real intervention and not an equilibrium theorem.

## 5. Formation and commitment revision must not disappear in a mathematical condensation

The latest bounded construction does **not** hold every goal fixed. A harmed participant can lodge a linked complaint; its admission creates a route to inquiry even when aggregate predictions were accurate. The new inquiry uses scarce work slots and linked fresh records. A finite generator constructs an attribute-conditioned expression; admission of its category and authorization to execute it are separate gates.

In parallel, the construction supplies a concrete experience-dependent reflection rule. An admitted account of previously unaddressed harm changes a participant's proposed harm ceiling from `.30` to `.20`. An environmental warning changes the burden steward's proposed ceiling from `.50` to `.45`, or `.40` for a supplied severe-warning category. A disclosed proposed tuple becomes binding only through the stipulated endorsements and burden-steward veto. Disagreement remains recorded if the tuple is infeasible.

This can be stated exactly in the toy task: physical harm is `H=(a XOR X_j* XOR theta) XOR noise`, where the attributes are fresh independent fair bits and noise has probability `nu<1/2`. Blind action cannot protect every context below harm `.5`, even if an oracle tells it the true causal feature. With the correct conditional distinction installed, context-wise harm `<=r` and action burden `<=b` are jointly feasible iff

\[
b\ge\frac{1-\nu-r}{2(1-2\nu)}.
\]

At `nu=.1,r=.2`, this threshold is `.4375`: the revised burden ceiling `.45` is feasible and `.40` is not. Better representation can enlarge attainable responses while stronger commitments shrink the acceptable set. Neither is reducible to changing weights in one fixed utility.

The underlying response menu, formation disposition, initial concerns, political standing, information interpretation, signature/veto rule and enforcement are supplied. The construction demonstrates bounded formation and changing commitments; it does not derive unconceived concepts, legitimate consent or the origins of evaluative subjectivity. Endpoint agreement cannot identify voluntary learning versus threat-induced adaptation. Record consequences under both old and new standards and preserve the grounds of revision.

## 7. Skeptical-review checklist: substantive omissions to avoid

- **Do not make money the ontology.** Include material throughput, labor/time, ecological support, standing, executable rights, information, skill, culture and succession in their own units. External inputs are legitimate; hidden inexhaustible maintainers are not an explanation.
- **Do not replace actual dynamics with an optimizer.** Existence of a safe or jointly feasible policy does not show that installed selection, participation and authority implement it. State which theorem concerns which process.
- **Do not omit the meta level.** Selection over practices, changes to their selectors and cognition/formation are distinct paths. The latest influence-gradient result is a useful restricted second-order mechanism, with its premises explicit.
- **Do not promise a universal attractor shape.** Positive feedback can be regenerative, destructive, saturated or transient. Demonstrated bistability, stable equilibria, rare-event failure and competing basins are different objects. Scope a mathematical attractor to its equations and region; do not infer inevitable convergence or a global scalar potential.
- **Do not conflate informed action, executable representation and normative agreement.** The formation example provides independent obstructions in all three. The same physical process can receive a better verdict after a standard is relaxed.
- **Do not freeze goals by accident.** Represent partial evaluations and evolving commitments, with a behavioral formation mechanism when modeled. The latest example does change commitments; universal purpose formation and legitimacy remain open.
- **Do not use average success to erase distribution or chronology.** Expected-vector feasibility, conditional participation, pathwise harm, a permanent refusal option and late funding answer different questions. Publicly disclosed lottery outcomes cannot be averaged away at a refusal gate.
- **Do not infer consent from a larger acceptance margin.** If the margin is refusal loss minus continuation loss, worsening refusal can increase it without improving continuation. The current experiment supplies the refusal institution and ownership; independent post-refusal activity and protection against confiscation remain a next target.
- **Do not equate endpoint sustainability with safe transition.** Include maturation, pending work, bridge losses, delay, temporary protection, durable changes and ordinary maintenance. A sufficient certificate's failure is not an impossibility proof.
- **Do not equate causal closure with good reproduction.** Ancestor closure identifies relevant state under supplied mechanisms. Actual recurrent-class tests address continuation. Normative commitments identify what should count as protected; they are neither inferred from recurrence nor guaranteed by writing them into labels.
- **Do not claim all modules already form one theorem.** Deterministic ODEs, robust information-set safety, finite probabilistic path properties and convex expected-policy feasibility use different semantics. A unifying exposition should give explicit module interfaces and scope, then identify which joins have actually been constructed.
- **Do not overread the uniqueness results.** Unique least parent closure and unique coarsest deterministic strong quotient exist relative to a declared class. They do not select one fundamental ontology or prove all physical/social dynamics arise from a unique rule list.
- **Do not overread the compiler.** Finite rational-kernel completeness is an expressivity result, not evidence that a compact causal explanation has been found for every world. Open-ended representation is possible with finite rules and unbounded state; computability and observation limits remain.
- **Do not erase rival models or negative evidence.** The harm-dependent turnover example earns an intervention prediction beyond its specific calibration channel. Real-world claims require the corresponding identifying evidence. Current public evidence does not yet establish the entire reproduction sequence.
- **Do not imply biological or ethical identity across domains.** Cancer illustrates conflict across levels under a patient-centered reference; it provides no entitlement for institutions to treat people as disposable cells. Different domains require independent causal maps, affected parties, boundaries and intervention semantics.

## 8. Recommended exposition completeness criterion

A specialist should be able to reconstruct: the generative claim; the independent normative commitment; the state and event architecture; the relation between lower-level adaptation and changes to selectors; the conserved or depleted supports; information and formation limits; implementation and participation gates; successor transmission; attractor and transition claims; exact formal closure results; present empirical status; and failure conditions for each transfer claim.

That would make the dossier complete as a reviewable presentation of the current theory. It would not assert that every scientific problem named by the theory has been solved. The current programme already possesses a generative core and substantial conditional consequences; its precise universal and domain-specific extensions remain propositions to evaluate rather than extra adjectives to attach.
