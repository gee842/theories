# From individual recourse to correction of a governing rule

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting contract for **affected-party evidence → individual correction → warranted revision for similarly affected people**. Robodebt, 2015–2023, is the historical test case; the [primary-source audit](GOVERNANCE-RECOURSE-EVIDENCE.md) owns the historical findings and their limits. This note supplies no current legal advice, new apparatus, scientific run or general theorem about institutions.

**A correct appeal need not correct the process that generated the decision.** The case record can change while the operative rule, evidence standard, implementing authority and future production remain unchanged. The missing connection is the justified transfer of the *reason for correction*, its relevant scope and the capacity to act on it. An appeal count or a formal right to object does not establish that transfer.

## 1. What should transfer

The [governance assessment](GOVERNANCE-LITERATURE.md) already distinguishes nominal participation from funded, consequential challenge. The [application account][application] requires a factual application relation, warranted grounds, authority and competing-claim comparison. The [settlement's R1][settlement] retracts defeated derivations while preserving independent support. The present contribution is the institutional interface between those existing claims, rather than another proof that feedback needs implementation.

| What the review establishes | Appropriate scope of inference |
|---|---|
| An allegation is independently shown false or inapplicable | Reject that allegation on its stated grounds; do not infer that other complainants or the entire review channel are unreliable. |
| The available evidence leaves the allegation unresolved | Preserve uncertainty and the applicable burden of justification. Missing evidence is not proof of falsity, and inability to appeal is not agreement. |
| An individuating fact changes this person's case | Correct that case; investigate whether the same factual issue recurs, without assuming everyone shares it. |
| A premise or inference used across cases is defeated | Identify decisions depending on that same premise in its actual context; withdraw the defeated support and reconsider them, preserving any independent valid grounds. |

A changed disposition alone does not reveal which row applies. A decision can be withdrawn without resolving the contested general reason. A rule can also be defective even where its output happens to be correct. Conversely, a person-specific exception need not defeat the underlying rule.

“Similarly affected” therefore means sharing the *relevant dependency*, not merely a demographic label or an adverse outcome. Scope includes the rule version, evidential method, governing standard, relevant dates and any material exceptions. A later change of law or circumstances does not automatically establish that an earlier decision was wrong. Similarity must be justified independently of the desired number of remedies.

## 2. A concrete revision criterion

**Where a warranted review defeats a premise necessary to a decision rule's justification in a specified domain, that premise must cease to count as sufficient support for dependent decisions in that domain.** A decision retains support only through another independently valid basis, or after a justified replacement inquiry supplies one. This applies the existing selective-retraction rule; it does not declare every dependent factual conclusion false.

For example, inability to justify an asserted amount using one inference is distinct from establishing that no amount could be justified using adequate independent evidence. Nor may the institution preserve the original conclusion merely by renaming its defeated basis. The response must respect the governing evidential burden: a person need not be able to prove the opposite before an unsupported adverse claim loses its authority, where that authority requires affirmative justification.

There are two separate thresholds. Credible evidence that a shared premise may fail can warrant investigation, escalation or temporary restraint under an independently defended risk and authority rule. Establishing a particular cohort remedy requires the relevant further grounds. A review decision's binding force and its evidential significance are different: absence of binding general precedent does not make a reasoned warning epistemically irrelevant; a warning alone does not confer unlimited remedial power.

This criterion leaves room for justified disagreement, differentiated remedies and urgent interim protection. It neither generalizes every successful appeal nor requires people to continue bearing a disputed burden until an exhaustive institutional audit finishes.

## 3. The actual handoff

An institution claiming rule correction should be able to exhibit five connected entries:

1. **A retained reason record.** Identify the original decision, evidence, correction, rationale, uncertainty, reviewing body's authority and relevant time. Preserve dissent and superseded grounds. Share enough reasoning to detect recurrence while respecting claimant privacy; distributing entire personal files is not required.
2. **A justified dependency scope.** Identify which operative inference was challenged and which pending, historical and future decisions use it. Distinguish verified matches, disanalogies and unexamined cases. Appeal records alone cannot establish coverage of people unable or unwilling to complain.
3. **An authorized response.** Name who can change guidance, halt a process, investigate, reconsider decisions or deliver redress, and the limits of each power. Identify escalation and independent challenge when the first reviewer lacks that jurisdiction. Technical access to a rule is not legitimate authority to rewrite it.
4. **Resources and implemented change.** Fund evidence access, assistance, investigation, staff, software/manual changes and delivery before claiming completion. Record the version actually used, the date it took effect and what happens during the delay. An approved instruction and a budget allocation are not evidence that affected people received the change.
5. **Scope-bound verification and reopening.** Check that the defeated rationale no longer generates covered decisions, that relevant independent grounds survive, and that ordered remedies reach their recipients. Retain unresolved coverage and further routes of challenge. A future rule change does not itself repair past collections, delay or other consequences.

These entries reuse the [paid recorded-reason interface][replay]: a fact supports a particular reason transfer under supplied calibration and relevance, and an inaccessible implementation does not refute a retained reason. They also reuse the [faithful-reporting contract][reporting]: a desired institutional outcome must not silently determine what the evidence is reported to show. Neither an agency's preferred recovery target nor a preferred exoneration substitutes for the warranted interpretation.

## 4. What would count as success or failure

The narrow empirical claim is that **a shared-ground correction changes subsequent treatment of relevant nonappellant cases through a documented rule revision**, while preserving material disanalogies. Evidence needs linked reasons, versions, dates, implementation and outcomes. Before/after improvement alone leaves staffing, caseload and other reforms as alternative explanations; this contract supplies no causal estimate from the historical record.

The claim fails at its stated scope if the institution accepts the shared defect but continues relying on it in covered cases after the claimed implementation date; if only appellants receive the change; or if the alleged rule revision exists only in guidance while operational decisions remain unchanged. Blanket reversal across relevant disanalogies would not demonstrate this reason-preserving mechanism, although a separately justified compensation policy might legitimately have broader scope. Falling complaint counts cannot certify correction when access or willingness to complain has fallen. A sound contrary interpretation should revise the scope or the alleged defect, not be suppressed to protect the programme's reputation.

Individual relief, prospective rule repair and delivered retrospective remedies are separate achievements. Each may be valuable before the others are complete. None certifies that all affected people or grounds have been identified. [Theorem R2][settlement] assumes a complete sound current audit over its declared finite candidate set, stable application, actual authority and funded protection. Historical appeals supply fallible, selected evidence; they do not discharge that entire premise. Unreviewed harms remain in the account, and retaining a person's standing does not require pretending their particular allegation has been established.

The constructive governance connection is therefore effective **reason transfer with bounded authority and actual fulfillment**. It protects plurality by carrying relevant grounds across cases while retaining differences, rights, uncertainty and further challenge. Institutional persistence or a successful appeal service alone is insufficient evidence that this connection works.
