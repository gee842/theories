# Physical scope originality and measured performance of the alignment foundation

Prepared 5 October 2026 for Yongxu Ren and specialist reviewers. Three agents investigated physical coverage, scientific priority and measured-system comparison, exchanged findings, and reviewed one another's work. The lead agent integrated the conclusions, inspected the main proofs and primary competitors, and independently checked the saved benchmark. This is internal AI-assisted research, not independent human peer review.

The three questions now have substantive answers. **The existing physical premises are not universal; the broad generative principle has extensive prior art; and the available measured comparison does not establish superiority.** The investigation also produced a useful mathematical extension, located more precise contribution candidates, and specified the empirical comparison that would test them. Earlier proofs remain valid within their assumptions. Their correctness does not establish universal physical coverage or scientific priority.

## Physical coverage is conditional and can be broadened

The original lifetime theorem needs maximum lifetimes. A measured half-life or mean lifetime cannot be substituted for a maximum. Exponentially distributed lifetimes have positive survival probability at every finite time. This limits the deterministic branch, while the balance-law branch and a stochastic extension can still apply. Other physical cases challenge different premises: equilibrium activity does not itself establish productive wear, and functional structures can form from prepared precursors without a live instance of the resulting function. The [physical report](PHYSICAL-COVERAGE.md) supplies primary sources, boundaries and a domain-by-domain matrix.

The main mathematical advance replaces a deterministic deadline with an explicit survival probability. Let \(N_i(t)\) count active instances of support type \(i\). Assume finitely many types, finite initial expected population \(m_0\), nonexplosion and integrable counting-process compensators. For every allowed history and intervention, creation rates are bounded by \(BN\), while losses are at least \(\Lambda N\), with positive diagonal \(\Lambda\). No creation occurs without a tracked parent, so extinction is absorbing. Joint replacement events are allowed; independence is not required.

If the support graph is acyclic and \(\lambda=\min_i\Lambda_{ii}>0\), the proved bound is

\[
\Pr(T_{\mathrm{ext}}>t)
\le\min\!\left\{1,e^{-\lambda t}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}
\frac{t^k}{k!}\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}B^k m_0\right\}.
\tag{A1}
\]

There is also a finite upper bound on expected extinction time. A serial chain with one instance replaced by the next at exponential rate \(\lambda\) attains both bounds. For three serial types at rate one,

\[
\Pr(T_{\mathrm{ext}}>t)=e^{-t}(1+t+t^2/2),
\qquad \mathbb E T_{\mathrm{ext}}=3.
\tag{A2}
\]

The chain still functions after time three with probability approximately 42.3%. Its survival probability falls to 5% at about 6.30. This exactly soluble example shows why adding three mean lifetimes does not produce a hard expiration deadline. It also shows how the corrected theory can make a useful quantitative prediction.

The proof uses established positive-system and first-moment comparison methods. Its contribution here is to broaden the project's applicable model family, not to establish priority for the method. Weak cyclic networks can also fall under a subcritical extinction bound. Conversely, the presence of cycles need not give survival forever: some noisy systems face eventual absorbing failure despite effective maintenance. Horizons and tolerated failure probabilities consequently do real mathematical work; they are not arbitrary entries in an evaluation checklist.

## The originality claim must become more specific

The [originality assessment](ORIGINALITY.md) contains a sixteen-row claim map, primary references and the actual search queries. It finds direct precedents for the broad principles. This assessment is stronger than simply observing that the work has ancestors: several predecessors already integrate substantial parts of the proposed foundation.

For example, a [reaction-network account of autopoiesis and cognition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystems.2023.104937) already joins self-maintenance, response to perturbation and predictive cognition. [Joint sensor and system maintenance](https://doi.org/10.1002/nav.21763) already treats the deterioration of observation quality and adaptively coordinates repair. Multilevel human and automated control, selection–environment feedback and maintaining successor corrigibility also have direct antecedents documented in the report.

The resource proof's general technique is a storage certificate. After changing coordinates in the channel state, the maintenance allocation cancels when the transformed reliability reserve and execution reserve are added. That inherited method is valid and useful. The particular exact result remains worth reviewing: a conditional error floor, continuous readiness, separate execution protection, an iff feasibility threshold, and a finite failure bound valid against every admissible allocation policy.

The narrow candidate claim is therefore:

> When observation quality and executable service depend on coupled maintenance resources, a declared standard of continuous correction can be impossible despite adequate present or scheduled performance. Explicit informational and temporal requirements can yield sharp feasibility boundaries and policy-independent exhaustion bounds. Endogenous selection can determine which maintenance regime is installed.

The exact formula was not located in the purposive search. That is neither certification of novelty nor evidence of duplication. The appropriate next priority question concerns that particular theorem and application, especially constrained reliability and maintenance-control literature. The broader integration must demonstrate a useful consequence beyond what its predecessors already supply. Merely putting existing principles into one vocabulary is insufficient to establish foundational novelty.

## The new measured comparison does not show an advantage

The [measured assessment](MEASURED-COMPARISON.md) reuses an archived microbial dataset with real biomass outcomes. It does not fit the renewal differential equations. Instead, it tests a restricted predictive implication: whether composition summaries add useful information beyond current biomass and recorded environment.

The protocol was saved before this new run, but all outcomes had appeared in earlier analyses. This is exploratory reuse, not an untouched test or independent replication. For each of four source inocula, fitting and tuning use only earlier cycles from the other three sources. The test predicts cycle six from cycle five in the held-out source, with six conditions per source and 24 test conditions total. Each matched library offers persistence, a training mean, ridge regression and nonlinear kernel regression with the same tuning opportunity.

Both libraries selected persistence in every fold:

| Primary held-out error | Baseline library | Library with composition |
|---|---:|---:|
| RMSE in OD600 units | 0.047725 | 0.047725 |
| MAE in OD600 units | 0.032974 | 0.032974 |

The 24 primary predictions are identical. The declared expectation of improving both metrics fails. This is not a precisely estimated biological equivalence: the algorithms chose the same predictor, there are only four source blocks, and the outcomes were reused. It neither proves composition irrelevant nor falsifies the mathematical theorems.

The fixed ridge-only comparison improves with composition, but both ridge models perform worse than persistence. The kernel-only comparison worsens with composition. Promoting the favorable ridge result would evade the primary question about competitive baselines. The previous analyses remain preserved, including their mixed primary result and favorable secondary result; different test sets mean their numeric scores cannot be compared directly with these.

No available dataset identifies the entire claimed causal chain from consequences to renewal decisions to maintained information/execution to successor performance. The existing human studies contain component evidence and substantial assignment or measurement limitations; the simulations test stipulated mechanisms. Neither can establish the stronger intervention claim by reinterpretation.

## What an informative next test must distinguish

A new label cannot beat an exact optimum computed using the same true model, information, policy class and loss. The useful scientific possibilities are more concrete: identifying omitted capacities or dependencies, collecting better measurements, predicting transfer with justified restrictions, finding a simpler certificate, or obtaining better decisions with limited data and computation. Standard control methods may implement the resulting insight.

The measured report develops a three-stage test from the existing handoff design:

1. Randomize timely error information and operative access to a sealed correction package; replace the worker and measure independently scored successor responses. Repeat a second handoff to test reproduction over a declared finite horizon. Factorial interaction alone is not causal mediation.
2. Before confirmatory outcomes, lock forecasts from a strong history-based model, an established joint-maintenance control model, and the specific proposed model. Give them identical usable information, interventions and tuning opportunities. Test genuinely different intervention predictions on held-out lineages and disturbances.
3. On a separate cohort, randomize the locked policies under equal resources and permissions. Assess success by deadline while retaining the separate protected constraints. A better aggregate score cannot compensate for violating a required constraint.

This is a concrete prospective specification, not an executed or fully powered experiment. Interference rules, exact resource accounting, sequential assignment probabilities, a primary estimand, implemented comparators, and independent power inputs must be settled before running it. No new human, biological or deployed-AI intervention was conducted during this assessment.

## Revised central claim and completion status

The defensible central claim is now:

> Alignment can be studied as the continued attainability of independently justified responses under changing information, execution and support conditions. Where those conditions are vulnerable, explicit loss and provision laws impose bounds on continued correction. Where current practices alter which practices and supports persist, endogenous selection shapes future attainability. The applicable physical mechanism, the acceptable requirements, and the comparative intervention value must each be established.

This retains the generality that motivated the project. It does not force every physical case into a renewal loop or make survival a proof of truth or legitimacy. Its scientific value will depend on identified models and discriminating consequences.

The team has completed the requested assessment and a feasible measured benchmark. It has not completed universal empirical validation, theorem-priority certification or a real-system intervention trial. The investigation supports a more physically credible family of conditional results and a narrower, reviewable contribution space. It does not support presenting the work as a completed universal science of alignment.
