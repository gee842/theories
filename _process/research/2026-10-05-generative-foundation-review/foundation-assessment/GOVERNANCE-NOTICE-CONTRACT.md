# Correcting an institution's operative instructions

6 October 2026. AI-authored application contract for **warranted rule correction → corrected recipient decision context → effective response and redress**. The [source audit](GOVERNANCE-NOTICE-EVIDENCE.md) owns the historical interpretation of the October 2022 Ombudsman report, Recommendation 8, §§1.281–1.291. This note adds no empirical outcome, legal advice, apparatus or general theorem.

## The missing handoff

An institution can revise its internal evidence practice while people continue organizing their choices around its earlier letters, deadlines or stated requirements. An internal rule update therefore need not correct the institution's external influence. Whether particular recipients relied on a notice, understood an update or changed conduct requires separate evidence; it cannot be inferred from a document's existence.

The [recourse contract](GOVERNANCE-RECOURSE-CONTRACT.md) already withdraws defeated shared grounds while preserving valid independent support. [R1](../../2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/formal/REPORT.md#1-finite-reasons-and-retained-grounds) supplies that dependency distinction. The [application account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md#correct-command-withdrawn-application) separates standing, efficacy, application, authority and feasibility. Here the additional application is communicative: which earlier institutional representations still shape the recipient's practical options?

## Conditional correction criterion

Suppose a warranted review defeats or withdraws an institutional requirement within a specified scope, and a separately justified responsibility obliges the institution to correct its own materially misleading or obsolete directions. Then updating the internal rule alone does not discharge that responsibility. The institution must implement the correction required by that responsibility's actual content, audience, timing and reasonable-access conditions.

This ground can involve legitimate undertaking or responsibility for an imposed burden; it is not derived from mere influence. The existing [assurance analysis](../history/root-foundation/HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#12-assurance-can-create-a-claim-that-outlasts-inquiry) already distinguishes correcting a proposition, performing a notification undertaking and repairing harmful reliance. This contract applies those distinctions rather than deriving a new universal duty of notice.

The correction must identify the superseded requirement and its scope, explain what now applies, and distinguish remaining obligations and uncertainty. An unresolved future policy need not prevent an accurate status notice or correction of an already defeated assertion. Defeating one evidential demand does not establish that every underlying liability is false. Conversely, an institution cannot leave its unsupported demand operative simply because an independent claim might later be established. The applicable burden of justification and legitimate authority remain necessary inputs. A later prospective policy change also need not imply that every earlier decision was wrongful.

## Five distinct achievements

| Achievement | Evidence needed at the claimed scope |
|---|---|
| Authorized revision | A warranted change, competent decision maker, affected rule/version and effective date. |
| Corrective communication | Accurate replacement guidance and correction of relevant outstanding instructions, with a justified recipient scope. A public announcement alone may not reach earlier addressees. |
| Actual access | Delivery or another usable channel appropriate to those recipients, including relevant assistance and accessible formats. A send log does not prove receipt or comprehension. |
| Usable choice | The corrected options can still be exercised: necessary time, assistance and authorized handling of intervening deadlines or consequences. Understanding an unavailable remedy is insufficient. |
| Redress | Each warranted reconsideration, payment, restoration or other remedy actually occurs. Notice of a possible remedy is not its performance. |

These are distinct success claims, not a requirement to prove every recipient's mental state before taking useful action. Staff changes, revised forms and notices can each be partial progress. Institution-wide coverage, recipient receipt, understanding and resulting choices need their own evidence. Reasonable communication efforts can satisfy a specified duty without guaranteeing universal understanding; that narrower claim must be stated honestly.

People who do not call must not disappear from the declared affected population. Establish coverage through the relevant notice and rule dependency, preserve uncertain matches and disanalogies, and record unsuccessful contact. The responsible body may need authorized escalation, assistance or interim restraint; technical ability to send a message creates no unlimited remedial power. Resources, privacy and competing claims constrain implementation without erasing outstanding reasons.

## Success and falsification

A claim of completed recipient-level correction fails if a covered noncaller still reasonably treats the defeated requirement as operative after the claimed correction, because the institution's superseded instruction remains their available guidance. This is a proposed test, not an asserted historical observation. A disproven scope match or independent valid requirement changes that assessment. Reduced call volume does not establish correction.

The [claims/action interface](../../../../theories/becoming/history/CLAIMS-AND-PAID-ACTION.md) preserves the conclusion: revised internal knowledge, delivered notice, enabled choice and fulfilled remedy are different achievements. Unknown fulfillment stays unknown; notification cannot erase a valid independent claim or certify redress it has not delivered.
