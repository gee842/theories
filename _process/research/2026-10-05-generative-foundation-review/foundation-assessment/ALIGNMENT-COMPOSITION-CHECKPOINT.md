## Strongest connected account

An organization's own future can require external support and discriminating information without representing an ethical “outside.” When a controller cannot distinguish possibilities with no common adequate action, it cannot guarantee adequate response across them. Informative channels or usable retained/inherited distinctions can remove that obstacle. This supports Ren's connection between external integration and cognition at a stated boundary, task and horizon; it does not imply fresh observation at every instant or concern for everyone supplying support.

The accepted constructions now show more than that necessity: under specified laws, formed machinery acquires and revises consequential knowledge; paid learned content affects another recipient's treatment; a distinct successor retains access, obtains new evidence and changes its means. Resources, target meaning and failed histories survive the staged joins. Under separately defended application premises, this can realize warranted instrumental responsiveness. The same conditions explain informed exclusion, costly suppression, retained mistakes, resource failure and strategic exploitation. Persistence therefore constrains the account without selecting its moral direction.

One precise application boundary remains. A qualifying inquiry receipt already fulfills an independently fixed one-service claim before every faithful supported selection entrance in the accepted successor class. The evidence exists, but the evaluator does not use fulfillment to withdraw that application. Later delivery cannot demonstrate satisfaction of a still-pending claim there. Independent recurring grounds could justify it; a new controller identity or technical epoch cannot invent them. Generic application correction is already accepted elsewhere. Combining it with this evaluator is necessary only for a claim that this particular lineage performs that correction—not a mandatory new sensor or general controller.

[Plasticity][plasticity] is live prospective work, with no accepted construction or outcome in this snapshot. It targets formation of an initially absent operative evaluative connection. Even success would not derive patient-good, legitimate authority or priority from conditioning. Broader concern and wisdom still require a substantive account of justified relevance, revision and effective correction under their actual limits. The remaining synthesis must resolve those central claims and their evidence, not merely rename a bounded construction the full objective or require unlimited new apparatus.
