**The strongest supported cross-domain statement is conditional and differentiated:** external conditions and accessible evidence can affect regulation, learning and action; acquiring information, giving it evaluative influence, authorizing action and delivering correction are separately fallible connections. The records support those distinctions in particular systems. They do not establish one observed developmental chain, inevitable ethical convergence from self-preservation, or a uniquely superior algorithm. ## Smallest material gap for a deployment claim

For a **specified deployment**, the missing evidence is an independently inspectable implementation of the claimed correction-to-action interface: an applicable correction changes subsequent authorized decisions for affected recipients, survives the claimed handoff/change conditions, and produces verified effects under its real costs and failure modes. A matched conventional implementation must receive the same information and opportunities. This is a missing evidential interface, not a promise that one successful trial certifies broad deployment.

The present successor already retains a potentially qualifying fulfillment record, but does not use completion to withdraw a service recommendation. A claim of that specific capacity needs evidence of its justified interpretation and choice influence; another sensor is not automatically necessary. Patient-good, authority and conflict standards must be defended independently. These obligations attach to stronger claims already contemplated; they add no foundation gate and do not require extending the completed ceiling pilot.
