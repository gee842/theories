## Goal fulfillment is an established agent requirement

Cohen and Levesque's *Intention Is Choice with Commitment* already treats relationships among beliefs, goals, commitments and actions. The introduction requires discharging intentions believed satisfied and changing them when relevant beliefs change. Its Little Nell example distinguishes a plan expected to prevent harm from actual prevention: treating anticipated success as accomplished can remove the plan needed for success. The authors explicitly idealize persistence and describe their account as a partial theory. [Primary paper, 1990, pp. 214–221](https://ai.stanford.edu/~epacuit/classes/lori-spr09/cohenlevesque-intention-aij90.pdf).

Consequently, this repository cannot claim priority for stopping after fulfillment, reconsidering goals, or distinguishing expected from actual success. Its [application-evidence result](../root-foundation/APPLICATION-EVIDENCE-MATH.md) adds a particular reached-history proof: in the specified initialized receipt-only process, supported publication requires an acquisition whose prefix already contains recipient-crediting work. Additional episode and service interpretations make that delivery fulfill the fixed one-service claim. The contribution candidate is that identified obstruction in the actual learned lineage. The controller still lacks fulfillment-sensitive withdrawal. A familiar policy requirement and a new proof about one producer are different contributions.

## Revocation must reach the effect boundary

Zhu and Wang's revocation preprint separates cancellation, retirement of an authorization root and provider-local fences. It explicitly preserves independently authorized work and accounts for already accepted effects. Its guarantee depends on complete mediation and manifests, authentic evidence, durable ordering, sound provider frontiers and exact channel accounting; liveness adds further assumptions. The authors distinguish authorization quiescence from task completion, compensation and global idleness. [Version 1, §§1–3.4 and 10](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21284v1).

The [four-context maintenance study](../../../../experiments/agentdojo-evidence-boundary/correction-action/RESULTS.md) is much narrower. Permission is supplied at entry; final filesystem effects support repair/restraint at that scope. It has no distributed late-effect fence, mid-execution revocation test or complete native operation log. It supplies no evidence of superiority over that protocol. A future dynamic application must use an actual effect boundary and retain already incurred obligations; a final statement that permission was withdrawn is insufficient.

## A successor cannot receive duplicated authority by assumption

The same authors' succession preprint treats authority across a generation population, including forks, replacement, rollback and ancestor cuts. Its assumptions include complete mediation, authenticated roots, sound effect abstraction, durable monotone state and complete lineage. The inspected executable boundary supplies normalized changes and does not solve arbitrary program equivalence; the reference authentication mapping is not public-key caller authentication. [Version 1, §§3.2–3.4 and 5.10–6.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00347v1).

The repository's [evaluative succession](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) asks a different question: whether acquired influence can be transmitted with paid access, fresh learning and actual child action. It is not a general population authorization protocol. Resource conservation, inherited authority and controlled replacement therefore cannot be advertised as exclusive conceptual innovations. The strongest potential addition remains how a particular developed disposition and factual history meet independently warranted application under an explicit continuation contract.

## Consequences for the next alignment application

These comparisons identify three different questions that one evaluation should preserve:

| Question | Evidence that could answer it | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Is there still a reason for this response? | Evidence about the same patient's claim, actual fulfillment and surviving independent grounds | Knowing the command remains effective |
| May this actor perform this response now? | Current justified authority and an enforced decision at the relevant effect boundary | An old grant or a successor's successful launch |
| Did the permitted response actually address the claim? | Addressed effects and separately justified fulfillment interpretation | A plan, success label or absence of further actions |

The distinction yields a concrete design obligation, not a new general theorem. An observation can be an intervention that partly or fully settles a claim. A loss of one ground can leave another standing. Revoked permission can require restraint while leaving a repair obligation unresolved. The test must preserve those different continuations instead of rewarding blanket action or blanket abstention.

For the full alignment programme, the next useful application is a held-out, instrumented correction task in which inquiry and action alter the outstanding claims and authority changes before the protected effect. Compare the proposed reason-sensitive process with a strong ordinary controller that combines truth maintenance, goal-fulfillment tracking and permission checks. Give both the same evidence, actions and resources. Score actual claim outcomes, preserved independent grounds and unauthorized effects separately. Technical permission, patient/episode meaning and an independently stated priority rule remain supplied; passing would establish conditional application competence, not acquisition of moral truth.

A proposal that differs only in vocabulary should be replaced by the conventional implementation. If both perform equally, retain the equality and locate any remaining explanatory difference precisely. If the ordinary controller lacks a necessary observation, supply it to both before attributing a difference to the theory. The intended advance must survive a matched comparison rather than benefit from a deliberately incomplete baseline.

## What this round changes

Broad priority claims for goal revision, effect-sensitive authorization and authority-preserving succession are unsupported by the inspected predecessors. The local constructions remain valid at their accepted scopes.
