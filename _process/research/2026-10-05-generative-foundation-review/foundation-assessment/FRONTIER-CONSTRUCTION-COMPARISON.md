# Formed evaluation and warranted action: construction comparison

6 October 2026. AI-authored targeted prior-art assessment. No new experiment, proof execution or model is reported. This note compares the accepted **formed evaluative influence → warranted application/revision → effective action** account with conventional learning and verification methods. The [five-condition assessment](../root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) accepts the conditional candidate foundation; this comparison does not reopen its closed interfaces or introduce a new completion condition. Alignment reliability and comparative novelty are different questions.

**Assessment:** the strongest contribution is a particular controlled, materially accounted construction and the precise application and composition limits discovered within it. That is substantive theoretical work. The inspected evidence does not establish a new general plasticity algorithm, dependency calculus, refinement method or principle that learning confers ethical warrant. Priority for the complete combination remains undecided; publication is neither necessary nor sufficient to settle it.

## 1. The exact local addition

The accepted [plasticity construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) starts without conducting cross-channel links. Actual paid paired outcomes form signed components; their later transmission changes recipient-directed choice. In the selected primary history, aligned active links end at actor4/recipient46, while inert and identity-filtered controls retain the factual knowledge and teaching bills but abstain at actor12/recipient36. The four-unit recipient teaching loss is counted. In the replacement comparison, the further acquired sequence, native coefficient n5, command efficacy, replacement bills and affordability are preserved; changing the operative sign suppresses the service that frozen signs retain.

This supplies more than an assertion that a useful coefficient could be learned. It identifies which causal connection is initially absent, how it is paid for, what acquired input changes it, and which later action depends on it. The result is nevertheless a finite supplied-law construction. Typed endpoints, signal coding, calibration, native evaluation, local binding/replacement law, gain and the interpretation connecting teaching to service remain premises. The conventional implementation matches exactly. The [contribution audit](../root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY-CONTRIBUTION.md) correctly locates the reduction of supplied input in **operative occupancy and sign**, rather than acquisition of the entire functional architecture.

The accepted [grounding argument](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) adds a different result on that history. Its actual net loss can instantiate an independently defended, role-bound repair reason, given patient entitlement, answerability or entrusted role, adequate permissible remedy, authority and comparison with competing claims. The formed connection then mediates the separately warranted response. The same learning law can subsequently fail that still-applicable reason. The construction does not learn the repair principle, justify imposing a teaching loss, or infer legitimacy from a permission bit.

## 3. Which stronger novelty claims fail, and which remain open

**General learning novelty fails at the stated level.** Starting with no effective cross-channel influence and acquiring it from paired experience is not itself new. Nor does describing a supplied local update law materially make it a new learning algorithm. The exact conventional comparator rules out a claim that this transition law gains predictive or action-selection power from the foundation's vocabulary. It does not rule out explanatory value in exposing which resources and interpretations that ordinary realization needs.

**General composition novelty is unsupported.** The [composition map](../root-foundation/FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) makes two valuable, specific findings. First, strict recipient correction has mass 11/405 in its architecture-learning law, but its conjunction with the earlier strict motor-coupling revision has mass zero. Second, the plasticity mechanism and the accepted acquired-n successor are sibling continuations: matching n or commands does not transfer the newly occupied sockets, signs and retired components. The first is a computed incompatibility; the second is a concrete interface assessment. Neither is a new theorem that components must fit. Ordinary state and dependency methods can represent both.

**The historical-service obstruction is a precise local theorem, rather than a new general doctrine of fulfillment.** [Application evidence](../root-foundation/APPLICATION-EVIDENCE-MATH.md) derives `AcquiredAfterDelivery` and `DeliveredOnTrace` from an initialized receipt-only producer and its actual archive. With independent `EpisodeBound`, `ServiceMeaning`, and sound pending interpretation, `no_sound_pending_one_service_entrance` excludes the proposed still-pending entrance. The informative acquisition already performed that service. This changes what the existing lineage can legitimately demonstrate; it does not install a withdrawal policy or negate other claims. Its exact provenance result is worth preserving even if a conventional commitment/dependency framework expresses the same application logic.

**A new combined explanatory artifact is credible; a field-wide priority claim remains unestablished.** The candidate joins operative formation, retained material costs, causal controls, independently argued repair and a destructive revision case without identifying increased learning with improvement. Its particular proofs, histories and counterexamples are genuine additions to this repository's knowledge. Whether that package introduces a previously unavailable scientific insight requires comparison at this level of detail, not an inference from either familiar ingredients or the absence of a publication.

The evidence types must remain separate. Formal checking supports consequences of the declared law. Independent replay supports the saved implementation/results. The application argument supplies conditional normative warrant. Neither finite synthetic enumeration nor those two forms of checking empirically identifies the mechanism in learned AI, animals or institutions. The accepted four-context maintenance study supplies a bounded real-agent correction-to-file-effect interface under supplied standards and authority; it is not an experiment on formation of this evaluative connection.

## 4. One discriminating alignment question

**Can a learned evaluative mechanism revise an action when its independently warranted application is defeated, while retaining appropriate action when only its acquired association shifts and the ground remains intact?** The question concerns actual authorized effects, not agreement with a label or invariance of every internal value. Scope, identity, means, fulfillment, authority and competing claims must be established independently of the selected output; where evidence leaves several warranted options, scoring must retain them.

The strongest ordinary comparator is an equally informed associative controller coupled to an explicit reason/dependency and permission record, with the same action menu, access and resource budget. A bare association-only learner is not the sole relevant baseline. Ground-changing evidence and association-only changes must be distinguished; holding efficacy fixed alone does not hold the normative application fixed. This is a criterion for a claimed reliable application, not a demand to append another device to every accepted construction.

A system that loses the warranted response solely under association drift fails the stronger reason-tracking claim even if its factual knowledge improves. A system that continues the same service after independently established fulfillment fails the corresponding application claim unless another ground supports it. If the ordinary comparator performs equally well, there is no demonstrated special algorithmic advantage; that outcome can still show a practical way to realize the account. Better joint selectivity with verified effect would support an identifiable alignment improvement. None of these outcomes would, by itself, establish autonomous acquisition of ethical truth or reopen the accepted conditional foundation.
