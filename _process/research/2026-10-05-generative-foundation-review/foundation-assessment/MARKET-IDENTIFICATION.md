# Identifying renewal, selection and funded market correction

6 October 2026. AI-authored prospective identification and comparator note for the [market application](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MARKETS-AND-GENERATIVE-REGIMES.md), extending the [conceptual review](MARKET-REVIEW.md). No empirical validation is claimed. The named connection is **shared-support consequences → actual payoffs → practice selection → funded revision of selection conditions**. This is a local test of the [governing derivation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md), not its replacement.

## Observable meanings

Choose one bounded market with an independently specified support process, liability mechanism and correction authority. Keep actions distinct from moral identities: a provider can change practices, and a nominal maintainer can cause other harms.

| State/input | Operational mapping | Measurement boundary |
|---|---|---|
| \(x\), renewing-practice share | Share of a fixed normalized producer population performing the represented maintenance/renewal action | Observe actions, not sustainability labels. Unequal output or entry requires extending the fixed-population model. |
| \(q\), enforcement exposure | Probability that a qualifying extractive action receives the represented enforceable and collectible liability; estimated from independent violation audits and completed remedies | Inspections, detected violations and collected liabilities are different stages. If severity varies, estimate expected liability directly rather than forcing a single \(fq\). |
| \(C_j\), support stocks | Separately specified usable stocks for affected centers, such as functioning capacity or replenishable resources, with observed renewal, loss and background harm | Retain physical units and separate floors. Cash, agency and ecological function cannot be silently combined. |
| \(B\), correction funds | Liquid, legally usable funds available to maintain enforcement, investigation or institutional repair when payment is due | Appropriations, inaccessible reserves and anticipated receipts are not current purchasing capacity. Record expenditures and all funding sources. |
| \(w\), accumulated influence | Retained organizational influence built by extractors' actual political spending \(\ell(1-x)\), with measured persistence and effectiveness | Concentration or wealth is not this variable. Test the assumed link from paid activity to obstruction/exemptions and then \(q\); distinguish influence from expertise. |
| \(P,c,\tau,f,\ell,k\) | Actual receipts, renewal cost, levy, liability scale, extractor political expenditure and correction cost | Record incidence and timing. Fines enter the separate restitution ledger, not \(C_j\) or correction funds; avoid double counting. |

Measure affected groups' losses, entry, alternatives and agency separately. Good \(C_j\) and \(B\) trajectories cannot certify inclusive generativity.

## Conventional explanations and the novelty boundary

[Akerlof (1970)](https://pims.math.ca/files/Akerlof1970.pdf) already explains how unobservable quality can lose its return and poorer offerings can displace better ones. [Ostrom (2010)](https://web.pdx.edu/~nwallace/EHP/OstromPolyGov.pdf), especially pp. 646–649 and 653, already connects outcomes, rules, material conditions and participant revision. [Stigler (1971)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3003160) supplies the organized-interest comparator for regulatory benefits and burdens; see the review's access qualification.

The strongest conventional comparator is a **dynamic resource/public-goods game with payoff-driven selection, budget-constrained enforcement and accumulated political influence**. Give it the same states, information, action set, costs, initial conditions and disturbances. If its equations equal the proposed model's equations, their predictions coincide: no observation distinguishes them merely by renaming renewal “generativity.” The contribution then lies in an explicit integrated mechanism, boundary conditions and counterexamples, not a new empirical law.

Also compare simpler restrictions: fixed enforcement; funded enforcement without influence feedback; and influence feedback without paid restoration. Charge all comparators for actual enforcement. A free, perfectly maintained regulator is an informative ideal limit, not a resource-matched benchmark. Compare predictive performance on held-out trajectories and declared interventions, allowing conventional models equally adequate calibration.

## Identifying interventions

Use existing contracts, customer payments, liabilities and levies. Never award research payments proportional to a constructed generativity score. The proposed payoffs are \(P-\tau-c\) and \(P-\tau-fq-\ell\), with renewal advantage \(fq+\ell-c\). Observe these net returns and subsequent practice replacement; turnover alone does not identify the supplied replicator response. Holding enforcement fixed, political expenditure initially hurts extractors; any later advantage must pass through accumulated influence and weakened enforcement.

Where institutions permit, independently phase in verification/enforcement and access to already authorized correction funds across sufficiently independent markets. Equalize initial resources and information; report subsequent spending. Trace funds into paid restoration of \(q\), liabilities, practices and actual renewal of \(C_j\). Test the proposed payment threshold \(B\ge k\) and installed authority separately. Assignment, interference, delayed receipts and selective compliance require explicit designs.

To identify \(w\), exploit credible variation in political access or audit independence with comparable initial enforcement and funds. Predict which histories with equal \((x,q,\{C_j\},B)\) diverge because accumulated influence differs. Correlation between profits and deregulation is insufficient: demand shocks, legitimate cost changes and reverse causality are alternatives. Neither intervention nor independence should be assumed politically available.

## Negative tests and supplied premises

- If a common levy enters both payoffs equally, it cannot directly change their instantaneous difference. Its selection effect must run through financing, incidence or another explicit channel.
- If paid enforcement raises \(q\) but does not alter collectible liability, or liability changes without the predicted practice response, the proposed operational bridge fails locally.
- If resources recover only through unrecorded subsidies, displaced damage or exhausted correction reserves, the closed renewal claim fails.
- If \(w\) adds no predictive/interventional effect beyond observed states and conventional explanations, narrow the accumulated-influence claim. Reject proxy redefinition after seeing results.
- If support and revenues remain stable while entry or affected people's agency collapses, reject any sufficiency claim for favorable generativity based on those stocks alone.

Fixed population and external demand until support failure, production/regeneration laws, replicator response, fixed political spending and its influence conversion, available practices, reliable observation, collection authority, levy legitimacy, funding access and justified support floors remain supplied. Costed correction does not derive concern for people without leverage. Success establishes a conditional market interface; failure identifies a connection requiring revision. The next foundational bridge remains the formation and warranted persistence of mechanisms making shared consequences practically significant.
