## Mechanism review

The [governing position](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#contrasting-regimes-inside-one-explanatory-framework) already requires specified dynamics before basin language carries a mathematical claim. The [formal core](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-CORE.md), F8–F10, separates feasible control, installed performance and stochastic persistence. A desirable region, an available remedy and a process that actually carries out that remedy are distinct.

The [unified theory](../../../../theories/becoming/history/THEORY.md), sections 7–8, supplies private-retention/shared-depletion and recurring-correction funding counterexamples. The [formation study](../../../../theories/becoming/history/FORMATION.md), “Standing and influence have different grounds,” permits sustained production with unaddressed harm to a dispensable patient. These counterexamples prevent market persistence or renewable productive capacity from certifying inclusive generativity.

The [meta-attractor history](../../../../theories/meta-attractor/README.md), especially “The power formulation,” identifies the proposed second-order mechanism: advantage changes control over future evaluation and correction. Historical unconditional claims about narrow optimization inevitably winning retain their provenance; the new application uses conditional language. Competition, concentration and rule capture must be causally distinguished.

The proposed directions also apply to regulators and alternative providers. More monitoring or more regulation can consume scarce resources, obstruct entry or entrench an incumbent. Success requires actual renewal, legitimate protection and retained capacity for correction at the stated scope.

## An identifiable next study

A repair marketplace offers a prospective bounded case. Independently assess durability, maintained skills and capacity for later repairs; record actual revenue, repeat purchases, contractual commitments, retention and entry. Trace proposals and authority for changes to ranking, guarantees and dispute procedures. Do not make a researcher's generativity score the payment mechanism.

A phased intervention could vary trustworthy durability information while using ordinary customer payments, then compare a conventional remedy with an equally resourced arrangement that also protects funded, accountable rule revision. Measure verification and governance costs, interference between market segments, price changes and exclusion of smaller sellers. A causal comparison needs a feasible assignment strategy and sufficient independent markets or segments; a before/after improvement alone cannot identify the mechanism.

Test separately whether information changes actual rewards, rewards sustain useful providers, and evidence changes rules despite incumbent interests. The recursive claim specifically predicts an effect of who can convert success into control of evaluation and correction, beyond first-order incentives. Vary or identify that channel directly. If it has no incremental effect under the declared conditions, the proposed additional mechanism loses support locally. If a conventional institutional model explains the result equally well, retain the mechanism and narrow the distinctiveness claim.
