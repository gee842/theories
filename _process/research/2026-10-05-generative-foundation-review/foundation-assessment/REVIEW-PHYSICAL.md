## 3. Prospective causal design

The proposed staged design is suitable as a basis for development. It separates three scientific questions: whether report timing and operative handoff change measured outcomes, whether locked models forecast randomized intervention outcomes accurately, and whether a locked policy improves outcomes under random assignment. It also correctly avoids interpreting an A-by-B interaction or a regression on chosen maintenance as an identified mediation effect.

The report calls this a prospective specification rather than a complete, powered trial. The following details must be fixed before treating it as execution-ready; none changes the status of the completed exploratory comparison.

| Required finalization | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| State whether participants may appear in multiple lineages; specify interference, shared communication, and assignment restrictions | Lineage-level analysis does not ensure independent units if people or information cross lineage boundaries. Randomization and uncertainty must match the actual cluster structure. |
| Fix report content, access windows, delivery time, generic-orientation content, and the resource accounting in both handoff arms | The B contrast estimates the specified package of differences. Equal information-processing time must be imposed or measured if the intended claim distinguishes content from additional exposure or assistance. |
| Prespecify how the second report-timing assignment depends on first-handoff history | Sequential randomization can identify history-conditional effects, but its probabilities and eligible histories must be logged. Effects on survivors alone would select on prior treatment. |
| Freeze the main estimand for repeated handoffs, complete missing-output rules, and joint failure/constraint criteria | Two successful handoffs, average successful cases, and survival of a lineage are different outcomes. Protected constraints should not be traded away after observing a favorable mean. |
| Specify and implement the competing root and standard joint-maintenance models before the independent confirmatory cohort | A vocabulary is not an intervention forecast. If both instantiate the same policies and probabilities, the contribution is an embedding or simplification, not predictive superiority. |
| Set a worthwhile effect and collect independent or blinded variance/delivery estimates before fixing sample size | Many tasks within a lineage do not replace independent lineages. No adequately powered superiority claim is currently supported. |

For the mechanism phase, random assignment to report timing identifies its intention-to-treat effect on recorded maintenance; assignment to operative package access identifies the effect of that assigned access bundle on successor performance. Neither alone identifies the causal effect of the founder's self-chosen maintenance quantity. A randomized standardized package can test a particular content or quantity intervention, but it does not automatically identify every mediator in natural practice selection.

For prediction, a proper score for the predeclared failure event is appropriate, with predictions logged before outcomes. A randomized intervention aids identification of its outcome distribution, but superior prediction still requires a prespecified out-of-sample comparison, uncertainty appropriate to assignment clusters, and fair information access. For policy choice, entire-lineage randomization is sensible if interference is controlled or explicitly modeled. All assigned lineages should remain in the primary intention-to-treat analysis, with failures to deliver or respond counted as prespecified.

## 4. Overall disposition

The measured report is appropriately restrained and useful. The proposed causal study can close an important empirical gap after development, assignment, outcome, and power details are fixed. It cannot presently be described as executed, powered, or as evidence of comparative success. The physical extension and the adverse empirical result are compatible: exact conditional mathematics and unestablished real-world comparative advantage are distinct scientific findings.
