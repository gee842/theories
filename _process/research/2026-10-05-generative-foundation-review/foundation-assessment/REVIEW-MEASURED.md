# Independent review of the stochastic physical extension

5 October 2026. Reviewer: measured-benchmark agent, separate from the physical-coverage author. Reviewed [PHYSICAL-COVERAGE.md](PHYSICAL-COVERAGE.md), principally Theorem P1, its sharp witness, and the conditional survival/information scopes. This is an internal mathematical review, not external peer review or an empirical replication.

**Disposition: the principal stochastic theorem and sharp witness are valid under the stated assumptions. One small observation-space clarification is requested in equation P11.**

1. **Expectation dynamics.** Unit creation/loss counting processes with predictable absolutely continuous compensators and the stated finite-interval integrability give an absolutely continuous mean. History-wise bounds imply \(m'\le(B-\Lambda)m\) almost everywhere. Simultaneous parent-loss/successor-creation events do not invalidate this marginal expectation equation. Independence is unnecessary.
2. **Positivity and comparison.** \(A=B-\Lambda\) is Metzler; its exponential is nonnegative. Setting the a.e. nonnegative deficit \(r=Am-m'\) gives \(m(t)=e^{At}m_0-\int_0^t e^{A(t-s)}r(s)ds\), proving P4. Entrywise monotonicity of Metzler semigroups justifies comparison with \(B-\lambda I\).
3. **Graph step.** Finite directed acyclicity, including absence of self-loops, implies \(B^n=0\) after topological ordering. The finite exponential expansion P7 follows; acyclicity at each time separately would not establish it for a changing graph, a limitation the report correctly states.
4. **Population versus survival.** With all tracked counts zero, P3 forces every creation rate to zero. Under the nonexplosive absolutely continuous counting model this is an absorbing state. Consequently survival to t equals a positive integer total population at t, and Markov's inequality gives the required bound. A demanded function can fail before total extinction, so the theorem is an upper bound on supported availability, not a guarantee of service.
5. **Infinite time.** The finite exponential polynomial tends to zero. This proves almost-sure finite extinction without interchanging an infinite-time mean limit with a pathwise assertion. Tonelli's nonnegative tail integral gives P6. Finite mean initial population is explicitly assumed.
6. **Sharp serial witness.** One active type advances along the chain at independent exponential rate \(\lambda\), with the final death leaving zero. Creation and loss compensators meet P3 with equality, there is exactly one instance before extinction, and the sum of n holding times is Erlang. The P5 probability bound and P6 expected duration both attain equality. A finite mean lifetime never supplies a hard maximum.
7. **Weak-cycle extension.** For nonnegative B and positive diagonal \(\Lambda\), the subcritical spectral criterion is the established nonsingular-M-matrix condition for \(B-\Lambda\) to be Hurwitz. It supplies an extinction upper comparison. The report correctly refrains from reversing it into a survival guarantee.
8. **Conditional block failure.** P10 follows by conditioning at each block endpoint. Its all-policy conclusion needs the stated uniform lower bound conditional on every surviving history; the report does not infer that bound merely from reachability under one controller. It does not require independent blocks.
9. **Conditional information scope.** The binary Bayes-risk formula P11 correctly incorporates the prior conditional on history and requires genuinely opposing appropriate responses. For a general observation space, however, the displayed sum is not literally defined. Add “for a finite or countable observation alphabet” before the formula, or give \(\int\min\{(1-p_h)f_0(y),p_h f_1(y)\}\,d\mu(y)\), with both likelihoods dominated by \(\mu\). This is a notation/domain repair, not a change to the result.

No new numerical fitting or physical experiments were required for this review. The physical upper/lower rates, support interface and function-to-support implication remain empirical application premises; validating their algebra does not validate them in a measured organism or institution.
