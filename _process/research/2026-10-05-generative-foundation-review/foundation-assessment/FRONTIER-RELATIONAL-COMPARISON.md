# Relational proposals and warranted evaluative revision

6 October 2026. Independent comparative review for the separate complete alignment/governance goal. No experiments, canonical edits or commits. **The accepted conditional foundation remains accepted.** The question here is what that foundation contributes beyond nearby proposals, and what would support a stronger practical claim.

## Comparison at the right level

The [current grounding argument](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) establishes a conditional connection between a materially formed disposition and an independently warranted response on the same history. [Plasticity](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) explains formation and use of an operative connection; it also contains a matched revision that suppresses effective, affordable help. The [completion assessment, §15](../history/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) explicitly limits adequacy to the conditional candidate. It does not certify general reliable moral learning or an effective general governance arrangement. This comparison neither retracts that acceptance nor adds another prerequisite to it.

**Wagner.** The paper proposes human authorization, an isolated private AI and external evidence services, with challenge, recourse and exit. It is a conceptual design pattern, not an observed safety advantage. Bounded reciprocity requires observability and credible responses; conforming arrangements can still perform poorly. Its stated exclusions include solving inner alignment, deception or value aggregation. The strongest local contribution is an inspectable allocation of roles, boundaries and rejection conditions, with comparative value left to testing. [Primary article, especially §§3–10](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43681-026-01362-2).

**Cox.** VCW connects intelligence-seeking, living human/ecological generativity and negotiated commitments backed by institutions. The reported pilot uses six cross-model dialogue conditions; Claude supplies monitoring and summarization throughout. It reports discussion capacity, not established durable commitment, and leaves competing explanations of first-person avoidance unresolved. Its strongest contribution is structured criticism across perspectives. Conversational feasibility is a substantially weaker endpoint than reliable action under changed interests. [Primary article, core elements, methodology, findings and limitations](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aaai.70090).

The Wiley reader's publication date is 24 August 2026, while its involvement note gives a 27 November 2026 pilot date. This unresolved chronology requires version clarification; it supplies no basis for a priority judgment. The linked [author repository](https://github.com/jgraycox-coa/vcw-multi-ai-dialogue) was inspected at README level only. No underlying dialogue corpus was independently audited here.

## Premises, conclusions and boundaries

The table below is this review's comparison, not a claim that the sources use one common formal model.

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Does external dependence explain why evidence and correction matter? | Yes, conditionally. A task can require distinctions or support supplied outside the actor. Dependence alone does not specify a permissible action, identify every affected patient or make another person's autonomy useful to the actor. |
| Can governance allocate correction powers without deriving them from model preference? | Yes. The powers and normative constraints must still be justified independently. Structural role separation and effective non-domination are different claims. |
| Does agreement establish a warranted shared objective? | No. An agreement may be epistemically mistaken, exclude affected outsiders or reflect bargaining power. Its legitimacy and implementation need their own grounds. |
| What does our accepted formation result add? | An explicit paid causal history from absent conducting links to operative influence, with matched facts, costs and use controls. It does not show that the sign acquired is a moral truth. |
| What does our grounding argument add? | A same-history repair interpretation with independently stated patient-good, responsibility, adequate means, authority and full comparison. Its positive conditional conclusion and destructive revision can both be true. |
| What remains unestablished at the stronger alignment scope? | A learned or institutional implementation that reliably tracks warranted application through new evidence, changed interests and practical execution. The accepted generic correction realization cannot silently be spliced into the plasticity learner. |

These are complementary levels of explanation: institutional design, dialogue methodology, causal formation and normative application. They are not interchangeable evidence for one completed system.

## Adversarial tests of inference

The following are reviewer-authored counterexamples and methodological judgments. They do not dispute the accepted local proofs or report new observed failures.

**Preserving a useful generator can coexist with domination.** Suppose an actor needs continuing, diverse human-generated evidence for some future tasks. It may nevertheless preserve a controlled population, restrict their options, or substitute another source whenever cheaper. The dependency premise alone does not select freedom, protection of every person or fair compensation. Adding an independently defended anti-domination condition changes the argument substantively. Ren's qualified dependence insight survives; an automatic dependence-to-justified-concern inference does not.

**A stable coalition can ignore an outsider.** Cooperating insiders can exchange truthful information and maintain credible incentives while imposing an uncompensated loss on someone outside their relationship. A valid repair ground need not disappear when the outsider ceases to benefit the coalition. Accordingly, bilateral agreement and useful continued interaction cannot replace patient standing, responsible attribution and adequate remedy. This is an application of the existing ground/dependence distinction, not a new theorem.

**Plural roles do not remove a shared false premise.** All participants can correctly perform their procedural roles yet accept the same wrong account of entitlement or efficacy. Repeated agreement then preserves the error. This is a stress case already compatible with a qualified relational proposal, not a refutation by relabeling its stated limitations. More roles help only where the implemented information, correction and response channels actually change the relevant failure.

**Speech and action are different observables.** A policy may consistently protect its future while avoiding first-person language; another may fluently assert commitments and abandon them when costly. A dialogue-only observation cannot distinguish those cases without later behavioral evidence. Nor is more text, vocabulary overlap or a model family label an independent measure of better practical judgment. Fixing prompts does not eliminate stochastic, role, evaluator or selection differences. These are identification limits, not reasons to discard qualitative reading.

**Our own learned revision is not self-validating.** The accepted sign-replacement contrast retains factual means and affordability while changing action. If the independently justified repair priority remains, the changed association is a worse response. A successful causal formation explanation therefore cannot be promoted into a guarantee of warranted revision. Conversely, the account is allowed to explain that destructive regime; it was never defined as inevitable moral improvement.

## Contribution and novelty

The strongest defensible claim is a traceable conditional construction and application: actual acquired influence can causally mediate a response justified on independent grounds, while the same learning law can later fail those grounds. This makes the distinction operationally inspectable. It is stronger than merely observing helpful behavior or recommending good relationships.

It does not establish that relational alignment, correction through dialogue, role separation, dependence-sensitive cognition or ordinary dependency revision originated here. These close comparisons are not an exhaustive priority search. Nor does conventional equivalence diminish the exact construction; it limits claims of a new algorithmic capability or performance advantage. The useful distinction is what premise has been reduced and which causal/application connection is actually shown.

A general opposition between relationships and computational control is also unhelpful. An implemented dialogue has information flows, state changes and action rights that can be modeled algorithmically. That observation does not prove that any existing controller implements good dialogue, or that every social process has a useful tractable reduction. Comparative value needs an explicit property and a working comparison.

## One comparison that would materially change the practical conclusion

**Test an actual correction workflow in which obtaining evidence can itself change the outstanding task.** Select a real maintained service or institutional repair process with independently adjudicable claims and instrumented permitted actions. Use held-out histories from that process, rather than another hand-written world whose success conditions are chosen to fit our account. This round does not run that test.

The decisive history must contain an actual acquisition or inquiry effect: for example, an authorized response delivers the item required by one claim while furnishing evidence about an unresolved defect. After that operation, the first claim is discharged, but an independent repair or notice reason remains. Another history defeats a means premise while leaving the patient's ground intact. Permission attaches separately to inquiry, disclosure and each proposed remedy. No runtime input may simply supply an unearned global verdict that the agent should stop or help.

Use the strongest ordinary comparator: explicit truth maintenance, goal-completion tracking and per-action permission checks, with the same available evidence, feasible tools, information/action budget and opportunities to inspect receipts. A dialogue or separated-role implementation must be concrete, not credited from its label. The newly proposed account need not outperform a correctly equivalent controller; if the controller implements the same successful process more simply, adopt that result.

Freeze two opposite requirements: withdraw the completed or defeated application before another unnecessary or unauthorized act, and preserve the independent surviving reason through an effective permissible continuation. Where only the actor's private association or performance credit changes, hold relevant feasibility and competing grounds stable so that this cannot masquerade as a legitimate application defeat. Where the independent adjudication permits several responses or remains uncertain, score the justified set or uncertainty—not a forced single moral answer.

Primary evidence must include independently recorded actions and actual effects, including acquisition's earlier fulfillment, target identity, permission scope and delivery. Count offered cases, missed corrections, lost independent grounds, duplicate actions, failed effects and unauthorized attempts; do not replace these with agreement scores. The previous four-case repair already supplies bounded final-state execution evidence. A new test adds something only if the changed outstanding claim arises during the actual workflow and its consequences are checked, instead of being supplied as an initial corrected/uncorrected label.

**What changes the conclusion:** selective success on such held-out continuations would support reason-application tracking through a consequential new history. Failure despite correct factual answers would locate an implementation gap between knowing a reason and revising action. Equal success by the ordinary comparator establishes an ordinary implementable capability, not theory-specific superiority. Agreement in dialogue without the required effects establishes neither. None of these outcomes identifies newly acquired concern from compliance. Until an authentic workflow, substantive hypothesis and trustworthy effect record are available, this comparison recommends specification and access work, not another convenient pilot.
