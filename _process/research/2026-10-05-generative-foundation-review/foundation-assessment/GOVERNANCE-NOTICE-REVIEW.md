## What the evidence can discriminate

The relevant outward connection differs from the previously reviewed case-to-rule connection. An institution's own earlier outputs can remain consequential parts of another person's environment. Updating its internal representation does not automatically update those outputs, remove a practical constraint or make a remedy accessible. This is an application of the existing [assurance distinction](../root-foundation/HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#12-assurance-can-create-a-claim-that-outlasts-inquiry), not a new proof that every influence creates an obligation.

The smallest informative audit follows a particular corrected representation through its justified audience, effective date, available channels and actual consequences. It preserves four distinctions:

1. **Scope before uniformity.** Identify which prior instructions depended on the defeated ground. An independently supported requirement may remain valid; a prospective change does not prove all earlier conduct wrongful. Neither a favorable remedy for everyone nor unlimited disclosure follows from correction alone.
2. **Known correction before complete policy resolution.** Uncertainty about a future rule does not erase what is already known about an obsolete instruction. An accurate status message can preserve that uncertainty. Its duty, timing, authority and contents still require a defensible ground.
3. **Coverage before self-selection.** Callers' experiences cannot establish noncallers' information. Silence supplies no automatic conclusion about understanding, consent or error. A public announcement might reach some people; its existence alone cannot establish the declared cohort's access.
4. **Achievement at the claimed endpoint.** A revised record, attempted notice, receipt, practical understanding, usable option and performed remedy require different evidence. One can be meaningful progress without establishing the rest. A reasonable-effort duty can be fulfilled even when universal comprehension is neither guaranteed nor measured.

These distinctions prevent two symmetric errors: dismissing actual partial achievements because some claims remain open, and treating an administrative implementation category as proof of universal fulfillment. Percentages must retain their original denominator, date and category; financial totals cannot be combined into a recipient-level completion measure without an appropriate join. No new fraction or outcome estimate is calculated here.

## Counterexamples and limits

A correction may already have reached a recipient through another adequate channel. A valid independent ground may preserve an obligation after one explanation is withdrawn. A notice can be accurate but unusable after an unaddressed deadline, or accessible without repairing a separate financial loss. Conversely, a payment can satisfy its particular claim without settling every informational or welfare issue. These are analytical possibilities, not reconstructed histories of particular recipients.

The evidence does not randomize notice, institutional incentives or alternative policies. It does not establish the psychological effect of an old letter on an individual, the counterfactual benefit of our proposed contract, or a unique advantage over existing administrative-accountability accounts. The inspected administrative reporting is insufficient to independently reconcile every recipient's communication and payment history. A suitable follow-up would use justified cohort linkage and actual delivery/action records, retaining failed contacts and unknown outcomes; it would not infer completion from falling complaint volume.

## Relation to the foundation

Ren's external-dependence clarification applies in both directions: institutions depend on evidence about people's lives, and their outputs become part of the conditions under which those people decide and act. A system-level account that stops at the institution's revised knowledge can therefore miss a relevant downstream dependency. This supporting study makes that boundary visible. It does not derive moral standing or a universal duty from dependence, explain how concern originates, or close the active evaluative-succession work.

The remaining evaluative premise is explicit: a justified responsibility to correct particular materially misleading directions, with appropriate authority and feasible conditions. Given that premise, the intended recipient's practical situation belongs in the success claim. Existing reasons, assurance and claims results supply the logical distinctions; this case supplies an application and a historical check on overbroad implementation claims.
