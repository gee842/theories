no transition law, controller or Lean result. The six source-derived native

**Finding.** The existing regulation and formation sources already instantiate
operative dependency sensitivity without a goal flag or analyst reward entering

## Three claims that need different support

An instantiated operation can have a **constitutive operational necessity**: a
condition without which that operation cannot occur under its actual law. In
the native model, repair consumes common support; source energy alone does not
perform it. This is a relation among activities and their conditions, whether
or not anything represents it. It establishes what continuation requires
conditionally, not that every available continuation must be chosen.

An **operative response or selection** additionally needs an actual causal
coupling. A damaged site can catalyze the formation of a repair constituent;
its repair can change capture and the copying of that constituent. These
changes can occur without a separate variable naming success. A description of
an enabling condition does not install this coupling. Conversely, a supplied
local reaction law does not imply that the response is outside generative
activity or that an infinite chain of approving controllers is needed.

**Ethical warrant** is a further substantive question. A process that preserves
itself can burden or obstruct other activity; a beneficial effect can outlast
its initiator. More copies, retained common fuel and successful repair are not
already an ethical ordering of the whole configuration. The
[future-good integral](FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md) represents consequential reach
only with justified valuation and causal conditions. It cannot become an
unexplained reward signal that supplies the mechanism this round is explaining.

## What the original regulation actually derives

The [response-formation law](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-MATH.md)
already has the sequence

`damaged site → formation of G → repair → capture → copying of G`.

Damage is an actual reactant condition in `D + P + F → D + G`, with rate
`κ D P F`. It is not a costless report sent to a separately prescribed
controller. Repair changes `D` into `C`; `C` admits capture; captured usable
support enters the actual copying reaction `G + P + F → 2G`. The measured
capture, intact-site and copy observables do not enter the propensities.
The source therefore already closes a conditional dependency-to-response and
return-to-retention loop. It supplies the reaction repertoire, sign of repair,
scaffold, capacity and copying specificity; it does not derive this chemistry
from unspecified activity alone.

The [executed controls](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/README.md)
preserve the distinction. Without incident support, effective repair can reduce
copying relative to disabling repair. With the original source, disabling
copying increases capture and intact-site occupation in the stated comparison.
Thus response retention and the process it supports can compete. The still
earlier [constructed inhibitor](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/README.md)
also has the intended local fuel response while worsening every declared joint
success comparison against its absence. Correct local feedback is not a
whole-process improvement theorem.

The [formed-variant extension](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/README.md)
then forms A and H from absence at equal rates and costs. Association and local
support connect actual repair to differential copying in its primary regime.
Its supplied local-copy chemistry recognizes the current resident template,
not a label meaning useful. H can inherit a repaired site after A is lost.
The preserved source-dependent reversal and appropriation path are therefore
substantive limits, not exceptions to remove from a positive narrative.

## Native vacancy feedback and its exact limit

Use the unchanged active, zero-repair-loss
[native source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/process.py).
Write `P = M − population`, `C` for intactness, `D = 1−C`, `L` for the local
store, `F` for common fuel and `E` for source. With the current resident active,
the relevant propensities include:

| Native operation | Propensity under the supplied law |
|---|---|
| Form either A or H | `μ D P F` |
| Repair | `ρ D F` |
| Capture into local store | `g C (1−L) E` |
| Local A copy | `β P L` |
| Bulk A copy | `b A P F` |

These formulas are read directly from `channels`, with no new objective.
At full occupancy, both copying rates vanish. Wear creates precursor vacancy
and can reopen copying; filling the vacancy closes it. Damage opens repair and
damage-coupled formation when their other reactants are present. Repair closes
those operations and can reopen capture. This is actual state-dependent
susceptibility and response, not an assertion that every enabled operation
will happen next. Competing rates, unavailable inputs and finite support matter.

The [existing abundance-generator derivation](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-MATH.md#2-an-unconditional-statistic-and-its-generator)
already identifies which mechanisms affect relative abundance. For total
counts `A,H` and `Z=A−H`, equal formation cancels and

`generator(Z) = β P L [1(resident=A)−1(resident=H)] + (b P F−δ) Z`.

Repair affects this through actual subsequent site, local-support and occupant
history, rather than by adding a favorable score to `Z`. This is an inherited
source result, not a new selection theorem. Its expectation need not be positive
in every regime, and the finite-stock law does not support indefinite activity.

There is a particularly direct limit at an actual boundary of the
[acquired-port construction](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/continuing-dependency/acquisition/README.md).
Immediately after two cells and the reader form, its plant is

`(free A 35, free H 36, active, intact, local 1, common 290, source 216)`.

Six other constituents remain incorporated in memory/control, so the effective
plant capacity is 72 and precursor vacancy is zero. The following native
possibility was first checked by hand and is now included in the separate
six-path replay; the original fixed twenty-case evidence remains unchanged:

1. `wear_h` gives `(35,35,active,intact,1,290,216)` and creates one vacancy.
2. `copy_a` gives `(36,35,active,intact,0,290,216)` and fills that vacancy.

The first guard requires a free H, which is present. The second requires an
active resident, local support and a precursor, all present after the wear.
Both preserve total material with the same six bound parts. One unit of local
support is consumed. Full occupancy is restored, but H falls by one while A
increases by one. Thus this native feedback does not constitute restoration of
the lost kind or preservation of the earlier A/H composition. The extra demand
to restore specifically A after A wear in the executed study is a declared
continuation contract, not a general implication of vacancy.

This is a representation-specific limit, not evidence that no richer
organization can maintain differentiated parts. A model of such maintenance
would need actual coupling to the missing kind and its reconstruction path.

## What the acquired port result can and cannot inherit

The [accepted acquisition](ACQUIRED-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md) retains native
reaction effects, occupied memory, resource history and actual later repair.
Its records identify which accessible port preserves common support. Its
`select(read_values)` function implements the supplied rule that uses that
distinction, and its finite loss/damage schedule supplies the particular
continuation. It does not implement spontaneous native event selection or the
formation of this selector rule from its later consequences.

A bounded source audit makes this lifecycle limit exact before fuel exhaustion.
At the actual ordinary `reader_completed` checkpoint, common fuel is still 281,
but both cells are filled, both have been read, the reader exists, its phase is
`installed`, and no trial is pending. In the
[acquisition source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/continuing-dependency/acquisition/model.py),
further cell/reader assembly fails the capacity/existence guards; binding needs
a blank cell; contact and commitment need a pending trial. Activation needs
phase `cold`, reading needs `active` or `read0`, and installation needs `read1`.
No declared erase, reset, expiry or component-reconstruction event returns this
state to those phases. Thus no successful declared nonnative continuation can
revise its cells, read values, installed selection, reader presence or phase;
native continuation outside a bound contact also preserves them. This is the
local apparatus's one-use acquisition lifecycle, not an impossibility of
consequence-sensitive revision in the foundation or in the earlier selector
studies. It is a source-level guard argument, not a new execution or a total
API theorem: some Python helpers debit resources before their later assertions,
so invalid calls caught outside the declared protocol are not modeled physical
events. The accepted protocol never makes those calls.

Restoring the stochastic native competition while leaving the port controller
unchanged would alter the prior experiment's timing and access contract. Native
copying could then occur without a commanded port, and waiting could permit
changes previously excluded. Such a composition may be valuable, but the old
reader contrast cannot be transported without checking those new alternatives.
The discrete event law alone gives possibilities; the CTMC adds supplied rates
and a distinct realized-event distribution.

The [selector-revision law](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SELECTOR-REVISION.md)
already supplies a stronger operative pattern: actual capture renews a mapping's
material lifetime; failures allow expiry and reconstruction. Its response
mapping can change within a continuing compartment without a numerical
performance score selecting the winner. The
[updater construction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UPDATER-FORMATION.md)
likewise forms and replaces alternative mechanisms that generate later
selectors. These are completed conditional antecedents, not absent capacities
that this note proposes to invent.

Their actual material composition nevertheless uses a staged adapter and a
different source/maintenance law. The
[material-selector interface](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/MATERIAL-SELECTOR-COMPOSITION.md)
explicitly ends concurrent preparation chemistry; the updater adapter additionally
makes local support accessible as common fuel and introduces packets financing
renewal. Importing that return law into the current native history would remove
its central no-common-replenishment relation. A conserved numerical sum cannot
justify exchanging those chemically different operational roles.

That existing composition also shows why the old organization cannot silently
fix every future requirement. Its H constituents, which did not repair the
original site, later assemble the alternative mapping that captures after a
regime reversal. Their operative role changes with the formed arrangement.
Maintaining the old proportion or favoring the old repair type is therefore not
a general substitute for explaining what the actual subsequent activity needs.

## The narrow next connection

The unresolved join is **consequences of native activity → retention or
reconstruction of the formed control coupling that governs that activity**.
To advance it, identify an actual component and its continuing reaction needs,
then show how encountered consequences alter the component's formation,
replacement or range of operation. The change must govern a later encounter in
the same material history. The old selector/updater results supply a conditional
pattern; the native acquired-port model supplies an actual learned relation and
occupied apparatus. Their common physical realization is not yet given.

The concurrent [remaining-dependency construction](REMAINING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md)
already uses acquired off/on effects to change a maintained relay relation
under its declared later-load contract. It is a different actual history and
an established conditional contribution. The native coupling question here
neither denies that achievement nor requires every local realization to be
concatenated into one executable sequence.

This cannot be supplied by assigning a desired repair value, adding a universal
future-good reward, or scheduling an arbitrary number of maintenance calls.
The current port apparatus has no modeled wear or reconstruction mechanism;
its final common reserve is zero, and no native event replenishes common fuel.
It therefore cannot simply be declared able to fund a further reader or
replacement operation. A new material coupling or a genuinely compatible
earlier boundary would have to be exhibited, with every changed role and stock
preserved. The absence is in this local representation and composition, not a
proof that requirement formation is impossible within activity.

The necessary adversarial comparisons follow from the existing sources: a
fixed informed response that already suffices; loss of H filled by A; productive
effects retained by a noncontributing occupant; a regulator whose maintenance
depletes its replacement reserve; source loss making repair unproductive; and
finite successful completion followed by exhaustion. A new realization that
only favors its selected statistic or silently restores common fuel would not
close the proposed native join. A real component-level coupling that revises
which differences govern operation while retaining these alternatives would.

This reconstruction locates an operative requirement in actual conditional
organization before any explicit representation of a task. It also preserves
the additional work needed to explain acquired scope, differentiated maintenance
and ethically warranted action. The next study should address that specific
formation relation; another already-prescribed controller would mainly repeat
the conditional learning results already established.
