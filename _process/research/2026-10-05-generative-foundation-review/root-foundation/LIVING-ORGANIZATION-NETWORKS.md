# Organized support, formation routes and catalytic networks

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored primary-source comparison for the [living-dependencies investigation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/LIVING-DEPENDENCIES.md). The question is how retained or reconstructed organization enables resumed activity. This note assesses a theoretical comparator; it does not present a new biological experiment or a new mathematical proof.

## Primary source and reading scope

Mike Steel and Wim Hordijk, *Tractable models of self-sustaining autocatalytic networks*, [arXiv:1801.03953v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1801.03953v2), dated 27 March 2018. Direct HTML reading covered the abstract, introduction and §1.1, including the RAF/CAF definitions. The displayed opening of §2 was also inspected, but its graph-theoretic results are not used here. The separate PMC opening was blocked; no independent reading of that version, cited experiments, raw data or figures is claimed.

The source distinguishes a reaction set whose reactants can be generated from supplied building blocks and whose catalysts are represented in the resulting network (RAF) from an ordering in which the necessary catalyst is already available when each reaction first occurs (CAF). The former can require initially uncatalyzed reactions; the latter excludes that startup requirement. This is a useful distinction between eventual catalytic organization and the conditions of its actual formation. A retained catalyst also has a different role from material consumed by an operation. These definitions do not alone specify an individual biological recovery history, quantities, spatial organization or a guarantee that a reaction occurs. No source theorem is re-presented as newly Lean-checked in this study.

## Implications for the current account

The following is our methodological inference, rather than an attribution of the living theory to this source. A proposed necessary organizing condition must be tested against **all admitted ways of producing, retaining and reactivating it**. An active complex may be absent while an inactive protected complex remains. Both may be absent while retained subunits can assemble. A slower uncatalyzed route, chemical import or another organized pathway can also defeat a claim that one particular intact precursor is indispensable.

Consequently, an absence invariant for a selected class of organization has a precise interpretation: it applies only if each admitted route into that class already requires a member, and no actual entry by another route occurs. The desired reusable inference concerns an actual history and a stated class of structures. It cannot show that all organization must originate from already complete organization, or that abiotic formation is impossible.

Similarly, a reaction graph that lists a possible formation route does not show its execution. Consumed inputs and retained catalytic participants must fit the same reached state at the same event. Later products cannot enable their own earlier formation, and alternative individually feasible paths cannot be combined by resetting their common inputs. These requirements explain why the present work follows actual histories and structural prerequisites.

The molecular, active/inactive and provision distinctions are analyses of relations within one unfolding. They do not posit an independently constituted organism using an independently constituted world. A reaction law supplied to this local representation remains a relation whose physical formation and adequacy can be investigated; it is not placed outside generative reality.

## Credit to the existing account

The repository already represents retained catalysts, consumed substrates, typed formation and repair in [TypedRenewal](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/formal/TypedRenewal.lean), and permanent loss marks in [CarrierTurnover](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/formal/CarrierTurnover.lean). [Turnover and closure](TURNOVER-AND-CLOSURE.md) already distinguishes durable suppliers, unbounded loss, changing types, conjunctive support and spontaneous formation. Their original validation scopes remain unchanged.

A further generic reaction vocabulary would not by itself advance the goal. The worthwhile addition is a general actual-origin criterion across a declared family of reactions, connected to an independently measured difference between retained competent organization, disturbed organization and resumed production. That combination can explain why adding accessible substrates need not repair a lost operational relation, while retaining genuine formation and replacement routes. It still requires biological correspondence; a new name for “alive” would provide none.
