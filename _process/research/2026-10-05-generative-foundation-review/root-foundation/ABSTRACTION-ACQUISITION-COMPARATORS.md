# Acquiring an exact abstraction: comparator and evidence interface

**6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting study.** Named connection: **formed prediction → acquired composition-preserving abstraction → conditional finite certificate**. This note assesses the proposed closed-and-consistent observation-table learner with a supplied state bound and paid experiments. It supplies no new human study or independent performance result.

Read with [structural results M4–M6](MATHEMATICS-STRUCTURAL-RESULTS.md#4-when-finite-acquired-evidence-can-identify-the-whole-behavior), [prior learning evidence](MATHEMATICS-LEARNING-EVIDENCE.md), and [formed prediction and paid action](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/PREDICTION-ACTION.md).

## 1. The ordinary comparator already learns the representation

Angluin's L* learns a minimal deterministic acceptor over a known finite alphabet using correct membership answers and an equivalence teacher that either confirms the conjecture or supplies a counterexample. It starts with a small observation table, constructs hypotheses from closed and consistent rows, and adds evidence after failure. Thus the correct state partition is **not** supplied to this established comparator. Its polynomial learner-time guarantee is relative to the teacher interface and counterexample length. The sampling replacement in the paper changes the guarantee to approximate correctness with confidence; it is not an exact all-word certificate. These claims were checked in §§1.1–2.2, Theorems 1 and 6, and §4 of the original paper. [Angluin, 1987, primary full text](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~lreyzin/papers/angluin87.pdf); [publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/0890-5401(87)90052-6).

Peled, Vardi and Yannakakis already combine active automata learning with conformance testing. Their black-box interface supplies a known state upper bound, a known alphabet, deterministic responses and reliable reset; their particular model reports whether an action is enabled. Their algorithm uses conformance counterexamples to refine learned hypotheses and tests up to the supplied bound before accepting the required conclusion. They distinguish finding an error from completing the more expensive confirmation. Without a bound, continued testing can find a failure but absence of a discovered failure does not supply the same final guarantee. Their semantics is not automatically identical to the present endpoint-output process, so transfer requires an explicit observation map. These points were checked in §§1–2 and the learning/conformance strategy in §4, including the discussion of unknown bounds. [Peled, Vardi and Yannakakis, *Black Box Checking*, 1999, author-hosted primary paper](https://www.cs.rice.edu/~vardi/papers/pstv99.pdf).

**Comparator conclusion:** the proposed learner belongs to this established algorithmic family. Charging its formation, tests, retained evidence and later use can advance the repository's connected explanation. Merely calling its table rows “generative abstractions” does not establish a new learning mechanism or a unique prediction of the theory.

## 2. What the new interface must actually remove

The proposed dependency reduction is concrete: replace an analyst-supplied correct quotient and external correctness answer with a representation constructed from the learner's own acquired records, plus tests it generates and executes. The following still need explicit inputs or a separate derivation.

| Interface | Required meaning | What observed success does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Target class | Stationary total deterministic endpoint behavior over the declared finite alphabet | Determinism or stationarity outside the assay interval |
| Bound \(N\) | A valid upper bound on reachable target states for that behavior | That discovered table rows exhaust hidden states |
| Reset | Every query starts in the same target condition | Restoration of learner fuel, erased evidence or experimental resources |
| Output | Exact, calibrated endpoint observation, including the empty word | Identity of hidden physical states or unobserved features |
| Candidate construction | State distinctions and transitions depend only on paid records and supplied procedures | Formation or optimality of the learning procedure itself |
| Test completion | Every required word has a valid answer and comparison | That an interrupted or selectively omitted test suite passed |
| Retention and use | The tested representation and its evidence remain available for a paid later inference | That a transient correct calculation became a reusable capacity |

The existing prediction-action construction includes resource depletion, leases and environmental reversal. It cannot simply be declared the stationary target of a resetting learner. A valid connection must specify a funded stationary assay interval and a reset implementation, or prove a projection that separates the stable observed target law from the experimenter's changing resources. A reset of the target condition must preserve the learner's acquired records and cannot refund resources already spent.

## 3. What is certified, and when it becomes a quotient

Use the repository's proved endpoint-output test contract. If target and candidate each have at most \(N\geq1\) reachable states, exact agreement on every word of length at most \(2N-2\) implies agreement on all finite words. This is a simple sufficient exhaustive tester; no efficiency improvement is asserted. It replaces an unexplained equivalence answer with a finite experimental obligation **under the supplied bound**.

A learner may use a more efficient conformance method later, but that method must bring its own assumptions and coverage proof. Checking a few random continuations does not instantiate the exhaustive theorem. Its original polynomial learner bound also cannot be read as a polynomial bound on the complete laboratory bill.

All-word equivalence from the reset condition is not, by itself, enough to identify an arbitrary candidate with the coarsest quotient of hidden target states. The candidate must also be accessible and behaviorally minimal. In the proposed table construction, distinct retained rows should have explicit observed suffixes distinguishing them, and the hypothesis must reproduce those suffix answers. This supplies a route to the needed minimality proof.

Then an exact map from a reachable hidden target state to a learned state can be defined using any access word. If two words reach the same target state, every continuation gives the same target output. Exact equivalence transfers that agreement to the candidate; minimality makes the two candidate states identical. The map is therefore independent of the access word and preserves action transitions and outputs. Its kernel is behavioral equivalence, by the same argument in both directions. This is the structural acquisition claim to establish. It does not require the learner to inspect or receive the hidden-state partition.

For an executable study, target internals may be available to an **independent auditor** that checks the result, while remaining unavailable to the learner and its query scheduler. A simulator using the true quotient to choose separating experiments would instead preserve the dependency we are trying to remove.

## 4. Counterexamples worth retaining

**A closed, consistent table can still be wrong.** Consider a unary target with states \(0,1,2\), initially 0, transitions \(i\mapsto\min(i+1,2)\), and output 1 only at state 2. The table using only the empty access word and empty suffix observes output 0 at the empty word and at \(a\). It is closed and consistent and yields a one-state zero-output hypothesis. The continuation \(aa\) refutes it. Thus table completion and global conformance are different stages.

**A false bound can make a false certificate.** Claiming \(N=1\) would test only the empty word. The preceding wrong hypothesis would pass despite failing at \(aa\). The evidence does not certify its own state bound. This is a deterministic counterexample, not a probability estimate.

**Reset and stationarity are operational premises.** If the same word produces different answers after purported resets, the exact table contract has failed. Overwriting a conflicting record or silently selecting a majority answer does not restore the exact theorem. Likewise, a certificate for one fixed law does not cover a later reversal without another invariance argument or renewed acquisition.

**A correct abstraction may remain uncertified or unusable.** Exhaustion before the final required query must leave the certificate incomplete. Exhaustion after certification but before retention or downstream execution separates warranted content from usable capacity. Unaffordable reads, unavailable actions and lost records cannot be filled with default output values.

These are self-contained diagnostic examples. Their appearance in a future run would validate the implementation's treatment of the boundary, not constitute evidence about human concept formation.

## 5. Costed comparison and evidential status

Record at least: actual resets and action executions; output acquisition; table writes and retained capacity; closure/consistency checks; hypothesis construction; test generation and comparison; certificate retention; and subsequent use. A cache can avoid paying twice for an already acquired answer only while its record remains intact and the target law is unchanged. Prefix reuse requires an explicitly available protocol. Resetting between full words is a different cost model from branching freely from remembered target states.

Every charge must be applied when the operation is attempted. Reporting a total cost after an otherwise unfunded run does not demonstrate a realizable paid learner. Computation charges should correspond to declared events or a justified bound; an unaccounted host-language loop is still a supplied computing resource.

The direct conventional comparator should use the **same learning and testing interface, target class, state bound, budget, retention law and operation prices**. If its policy and implementation are mathematically the same, matching behavior is expected and should be reported. Useful additional arms are a frozen table, an interrupted conformance test and a supplied-correct-model baseline. The last measures the cost of acquisition relative to privileged information; it is not an equal-information learning competitor.
