# What the unfolding can change, and what it cannot yet register

6 October 2026. AI-authored investigation under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **co-formation and consequential revision of practical boundary, significance and response within one unfolding**. The case is the existing joint-formation process, reconstructed from its code and saved history rather than assembled from separate demonstrations.

**Finding:** this process's consequences change the material availability of learning, which changes evaluative reach, installed control and subsequent consequences. But the history carries resources forward without carrying the harmed patient's outcome as corrective evidence. The resulting asymmetry is concrete: the process can reconstruct or lose its responsiveness without learning from the very injury it produces. That is a specific incomplete relation within this model, not evidence that activity and significance occupy separate realities.

## 1. The one process being reconstructed

The executable [game](../../../../experiments/joint-formation/model.py) contains a continuing account, successive controller decisions, an operator and a stipulated patient-harm variable. The [study](../../../../experiments/joint-formation/study.py) saves selected finite histories in [named-cases.json](../../../../experiments/joint-formation/results/named-cases.json). These are exact modeled histories, not observations of human experience. The interpretation of harm, the architecture, available operations and local laws remain supplied.

The selected case is `initially_underfunded`: six episodes, an opening reserve of three, operating expense three, learning expense two and learning receipt eight. Genuine inspection and control together cost three. Successful admitted production pays five. Shared learning supplies `w=7/2`, `g=1`, and operative patient weighting `v=w*g`; no learning supplies zero. These updates are explicitly implemented algebraic consequences of selected modes, not a newly observed sensory-learning mechanism.

The [timing clarification](../../../../experiments/joint-formation/TIMING-CLARIFICATION.md) matters: learning must be funded before its receipt arrives; an optional identity filter is paid afterward. Later income cannot retroactively finance the first lesson.

## 2. What actually happens in this history

| Stage | Material and practical change |
|---|---|
| Episode 1 begins with 3 | Operating expense consumes the reserve. Learning and installed gates are unavailable. |
| Episode 1 operates | The controller has `w=g=v=0` and opens production. The operator chooses harmful production; recorded harm is 1. Admitted production pays 5. |
| Episode 2 begins with 5 | Paying operating expense 3 and learning expense 2 leaves 0. The learning receipt then supplies 8. |
| Episode 2 learning and control | Shared learning yields `w=7/2`, `g=1`, `v=7/2`. Paying 3 installs the verified gate, leaving 5. |
| Episode 2 consequence | The operator safeguards; recorded harm is 0. Production pays 5, closing at 10. |
| Episodes 3–6 | Each reconstructs learning and the verified gate. Each closes five units higher; the saved history ends at 30 with five protected productive episodes. |

The income of the first admitted production makes later acquisition affordable. This does not establish that injury itself was necessary or justified: the receipt depends on admission, not on the patient being harmed. Nor does the model contain an experienced recognition of that injury driving the next lesson. The distinction is essential to reading the history faithfully.

No apparatus or learned valuation is inherited intact. Each episode reconstructs them. Cash and episode position pass to the next solver state. The changing responsiveness is materially connected across episodes, but it is not one enduring learner remembering all the previous patient outcomes.

## 3. Which boundary forms here?

The controller's bodily boundary is not generated by this game. Nor are the initial party labels discovered. What forms is a narrower **practical reach**: a consequential distinction can influence choice, be implemented through paid input control, alter another participant's decision, and change the conditions for reconstruction.

These aspects constrain one another. A positive coefficient alone does not install a gate. A gate requires resources and an effective relation to the operator's alternatives. Anticipation also matters: the pre-learning controller compares current cash consequences while anticipating what its later, differently weighted decision will buy. Selection of the learning route therefore already depends on the anticipated costs of its practical expression. This is not a one-way assembly of completed knowledge, value and action modules.

“Inside the practical field” consequently cannot mean only inside the controller's physical container or present in its learned weighting. It concerns the changing organization of influence through that whole arrangement. This description preserves differentiated participants and actual costs. It gives no reason to imagine one collective experience.

This reach is structured, not a single inside/outside bit: a patient's outcome can enter evaluation without effective protection being selected.

The [earlier joint-formation account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/JOINT-FORMATION.md) already established the conditional mechanism and its failures. The present contribution is its reconstruction under the corrected method and the sharper identification of what consequential revision its actual state transmission can support.

## 4. Two existing countertrajectories expose the relation

In `cheap_reserve_raid`, each episode starts with five. The operator destroys two; operating expense consumes the remaining three. Learning stays unavailable, open harmful production pays five, and the same configuration returns. All six episodes record harm. This is an actively reproduced restriction on responsiveness, sustained through the process's own continuation. It is not simply an isolated missing learner.

The `no_physical_control` history exposes a different limitation. Shared learning repeatedly produces the same `w=7/2`, `g=1`, `v=7/2` as the protected case, yet installed control is unavailable and harmful production continues. A representation retaining only the valuation coefficients would conflate histories with different practical effects. The missing relation is already specified in the full model; its disappearance from a summary is representational omission, not a discovery that values are causally powerless.

These are separate saved cases, not later events spliced into the selected trajectory. They alter actual model conditions and ensuing decisions. Their contrast does not arise from freely relabeling unchanged outcomes as good or bad.

## 5. The consequence that does not return

The decisive code fact is that continuation calls use `self.solve(t+1, closing)`. They carry the episode index and closing cash. The saved `patient_harm` entry records what happened, but it is not passed as evidence into the next controller's learning. The current learned decision does count the stipulated harm through `closing_cash - v*patient_harm`; the absence concerns retained, consequence-driven correction, not all operative influence.

A concrete pair makes the omission exact. In the first underfunded episode, the open profile admits both `harm` and `safeguard`. They produce harm 1 and 0 respectively, at operator costs 0 and 1. Both pay the controller 5 and call the same continuation, `solve(1,5)`. Only harm is selected; safeguard is a feasible, unselected branch. The different injury histories therefore cannot change the next learning through this interface. This is a change in actual modeled operation and consequence, not a detached evaluative relabeling.

Thus learning can become affordable after harmful production without the harm being learned from. Conversely, successful protection can exhaust the conditions for rebuilding it in the saved `first_gate_then_resource_failure` case. Resource feedback and feedback about the patient's condition are different relations here. Neither should be mistaken for the other merely because both appear in one output row.

The core description should therefore retain **which consequences return, through what carriers, to change which operative distinctions**. “Activity changes future conditions” remains true, but a description that loses this selective return cannot explain why the practical field opens, remains restricted or collapses. The tree corrects the adequacy demanded of the core's articulation.

## 6. The next exact movement

The game also lacks an evolving recipient organization: `H` is fixed and harm is recorded per outcome. Copying that scalar into memory would not by itself establish the affected continuity.

This local omission is not the frontier everywhere. [Recipient correction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md) already uses actual recipient receipts to revise target commands without private gain. The recipient remains a separate resource stock; its internal organization is not derived. [Evaluative succession](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) already combines transferred acquired influence, fresh evidence, corrected means and paid delivery. Its fixed inquiry can fulfill the interpreted claim, yet the subsequent policy does not withdraw delivery on that basis. These results retain their own histories; neither is silently appended here.

The next connection is therefore **affected history or condition → revised practical inclusion or applicability**, beyond correction of delivery means. Follow existing accessible records and actual continuation to determine how a consequence can revise what remains relevant to do. Preserving the affected continuity is part of that task; installing a harm-memory variable alone would not complete it.

This reconstruction adds no simulation or theorem. Whether revision improves affected continuities remains consequentially answerable; neither openness nor persistence supplies the verdict.
