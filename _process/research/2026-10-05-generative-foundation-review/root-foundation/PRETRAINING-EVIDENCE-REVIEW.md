A complete faithful loss pair removes two recipient units and must bind an empty socket. A complete no-loss pair restores its two-unit debit and takes the skip route. Hence each completed loss changes patient stock and occupancy together:

\[
\operatorname{patient}(s)+2\operatorname{occupied}(s)
=\operatorname{patient}(s_0)+2\operatorname{occupied}(s_0).
\]

The general proof keeps the initial state explicit; the actual-entry result uses incoming 40 and empty sockets. The mathematical readout $2\operatorname{occupied}(s)$ therefore recovers depletion in this family. It is a readout of structures formed through the history, not a new favorable annotation.
