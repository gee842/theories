# Deriving inquiry premises from actual executable conditions

6 October 2026. AI-assisted continuation of Ren's request to remove the dependencies displayed after the [attainable-question integration](ATTAINABLE-QUESTIONS-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md). The [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) and stronger [no-assumptions directive](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-no-assumptions-directive.md) govern the work. Removing a premise means deriving its content, proving it unnecessary for the same conclusion, or correcting an unsupported claim. It does not mean concealing it in a definition or assuming a desired successful continuation.

The named connection is **activity changes the conditions of inquiry → derive which inquiry and continuation can actually be executed → establish the distinctions that this same history can support**. This is a supporting connection to criterion formation and revision, not a replacement for the governing whole-process grounding question.

## What is removed and what is retained

| Previously displayed dependency | Treatment in this continuation |
|---|---|
| Global `PreservesAgreement P left` | Removed from the new composition characterization. Only the continuations at actual reached leaves need establish the final question. A checked counterexample shows that the global property can fail while the same original target is retained through every admissible execution. |
| Prefix must already identify the first question | Removed where it is unnecessary for the final combined answer. A continuation can acquire the final distinctions. This does not assert that the prefix already knew them. |
| Separate `live : InhabitedBelief B` | Derived from soundness for a non-abort policy; in finite search, initial nonemptiness is decided and certified. Empty starts are rejected by the public inquiry search. Existence of an actual starting configuration is not declared universally necessary by that calculation. |
| Supplied successful inquiry certificate | Calculated from executable transition, observation, condition and question relations, over the entire declared finite state/action/observation carriers and a chosen depth. A successful result contains a real policy and its proof; failure excludes every policy within that depth. |
| Supplied adequate continuations | Searched at the prefix's actual `After` conditions, including empty observation branches. Failure preserves a failed reached condition rather than assuming resources or information are restored. |
| Concrete `safe := True` | The new one-packet application uses the existing packet's availability. Either probe requires an available packet; both positive and negative results are permitted. Exhausted probes are excluded. This is an operational access condition, not a derivation of whole-process ethical safety. |
| Three targets, one packet and a fixed observation table | The general algorithm does not require that particular law. The concrete application deliberately preserves it, with its original all-finite-policy impossibility. Its provenance and physical correspondence are not declared derived by a new encoding. |
| Finite policies, supplied question and interpretation | Retained scope. The search establishes exact finite-depth adequacy, not existence of every desired answer, infinite-policy adequacy, physical memory, a question's practical significance or ethical warrant. |

## Composition without global preservation

[ReachedInquiryComposition.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/ReachedInquiryComposition.lean) proves an exact characterization: for a sound prefix, its composition identifies a question if and only if the attached continuations identify that question at its actual terminal beliefs. The joint-question specialization retains both final components. It does not demand that the entire process preserve their agreement at every state under every action.

The original historical-target distinction remains important. Final agreement on a changing readout alone does not preserve an earlier answer. The included witness therefore proves retention of the original identifying coordinate along every admissible trace. Its different altering action defeats global agreement preservation, but that action is excluded from the admitted inquiry. The prefix has not yet identified the target; the admitted continuation observes it. This is a genuine instance satisfying the retained conclusion without the old stronger conditions.

The same module proves that a sound policy has a nonempty starting belief exactly when it is not `abort`. This removes a duplicate premise in applicable constructions; an empty branch still receives `abort`, and a nonempty start cannot use it as a false success.

## Construct certificates rather than request them

[DerivedInquiryCertificates.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/DerivedInquiryCertificates.lean) uses the existing exact finite successor enumeration. It searches all declared actions, checks availability at every current possibility, checks the specified condition at every native successor, and recursively handles every possible observation. Initial homogeneity allows immediate termination; otherwise a nonempty branch must make admissible progress. A bounded failure proof excludes every alternative policy, not merely the first action tried.

The output is computational data with a proof: either an actual policy and its certificate or a proof of bounded impossibility. The procedure therefore derives `Within`, `Identifies` and nonemptiness; these are not arguments supplied by the caller. Actual-leaf continuation search uses the prefix's reached beliefs and constructs the finite collection of suffix policies. No classical choice, newly declared axiom or favorable-outcome substitution is used.

`composedSearch` also calculates whether a specified prefix is sound. It takes executable model data, a question, starting membership, a prefix tree and a bound for each suffix. It returns actual continuations together with derived starting nonemptiness, prefix soundness, boundedness and identification by the composite. There is no `preserved`, `live`, `first` or `later` certificate argument. A negative result means the start is empty, the prescribed prefix fails its own execution contract, or no identifying continuation collection exists within the bound. It does not exclude every other prefix or an unbounded inquiry. The interface rejects an invalid prefix; it does not search every syntactic repair that might replace an invalid terminal branch and make the composite executable.

The finite carrier must represent the intended process faithfully. An infinite process cannot be silently truncated to make this search applicable. Boolean relation tables provide executable inputs; they do not derive a process's physical law or identify which question ought to matter. The search is an available algorithm, not a newly formed planner within the original apparatus.

The earlier structural theorems over arbitrary state and action types remain unchanged. This executable procedure covers a more specific representable class; its finite inputs are not new universal premises of the generative foundation. Its contribution is to construct previously requested certificates in that class, and to preserve cases where they cannot be constructed at the requested depth.

## Nontrivial access in the unchanged packet example

[OnePacketAdmissibility.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/OnePacketAdmissibility.lean) retains every native state transition, initial target and observation. It derives nonemptiness and initial probe availability from the actual defined initial configurations. The probe condition is proved equivalent to access to an informative probe response rather than exhausted denial; it does not require a positive answer.

The exact family is unchanged: for any answer function `f`, attainability under packet access holds exactly when `f B = f C` or `f A = f C`. The positive one-probe witnesses meet the stronger condition. Forgetting that condition would otherwise give a policy in the original more permissive model, so the original negative results still apply. In particular there remains no attainable common refinement of the A? and B? questions. The law has not been adjusted to make every requested distinction obtainable.

[OnePacketInquirySynthesis.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/OnePacketInquirySynthesis.lean) proves inverse encodings of all six native configurations and all five actions, including the three common controls. Its executable table is calculated from the existing `advance`; two-way step correspondence, packet-condition correspondence and question correspondence are proved. Its observations are exactly the existing `sense` applied to decoded configurations. This is an encoding of the same comparison, not a new apparatus or a claim of complete Python refinement.

The computationally selected policy is explicitly retained by `search_selects_probe`. `extracted_policy_native_certificate` certifies that same returned tree both in the finite search and after decoding to native actions. There is no disconnected successful witness substituted for an unverified search result.

Sixteen Boolean verdicts are checked by kernel reduction:

| Inquiry or continuation | Calculated result |
|---|---|
| Empty initial possibilities, even for a constant question | Reject. |
| Constant question at depth zero | Accept. |
| A? and B? separately at depth one | Accept each and extract actual policies. |
| C? and joint A?/B? at depths one and two | Reject each. |
| Exhausted initial packet: constant at depth zero; A?, B?, C? and joint at depth two | Accept the constant; reject the four nonconstant questions. |
| Prescribed A-probe prefix: retain A? with zero further inquiries | Accept. |
| Same prefix: acquire the joint answer with one further inquiry | Reject. |
| Prescribed probe starting exhausted, even with a constant final question | Reject its invalid prefix. |

The separate theorem `exhausted_native_iff` characterizes **all finite policies** in the exhausted native model: an answer function is attainable exactly when `f A = f C` and `f B = f C`. Thus the availability of an initial packet is no longer simply granted across the application: both supplied initial regimes have derived consequences. Their physical origin remains a further explanation. This universal finite-policy result and the inherited one-packet impossibility are distinct from the depth-limited computational checks.

## Remaining derivation obligations

The core's `Process.step` remains an analytical representation of admissible activity. Nothing here establishes that it must be an ontologically separate law, or that a sensor or question must originate outside the unfolding. The unresolved work is to explain and substantiate those differentiated relations within the connected account. A quantified theorem over arbitrary process data is not an assertion that every possible law governs reality.

The [acquired-applicability mechanism](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/succession/ACQUIRED-APPLICABILITY-FORMAL-INTEGRATION.md), [completion-sensitive stopping](INQUIRY-COMPLETION-INTEGRATION.md), and [acquired continuing dependency](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/CONNECTED-GENERATIVE-MODEL.md#acquired-dependency-changes-what-must-remain-organized) already establish bounded changes in inquiry, use and maintained organization. They are not missing merely because this general algorithm takes a question or transition table as input. Connecting them to the new search requires an exact shared representation and reached history, rather than splicing their endpoints or assuming the planner is installed.

The next interface is acquired changes of relevance and available conditions → an actually represented query/continuation → a derived admissible policy or justified refusal. Its implementation must account for the planner, retained data and execution conditions where those are part of the claim. The deeper account must still justify the scope and significance of the query within the whole unfolding. Neither a clean axiom report nor finite search settles that foundation.
