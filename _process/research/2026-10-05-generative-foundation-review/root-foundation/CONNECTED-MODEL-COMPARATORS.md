# Comparators for one connected operational history

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting comparison for [Ren's connected-model direction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-one-connected-generative-model.md). This is a targeted reading, not a novelty certification or a new empirical experiment.

## Primary sources and access

**Composition.** Abadi and Lamport, [*Conjoining Specifications*](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/Conjoining-Specifications.pdf), 1995. The author-hosted PDF's introductory composition discussion, printed pp. 511–513, distinguishes circular safety reasoning from a mutually conditional eventuality that never occurs. Their temporal assumption/guarantee treatment preserves the order of violations. The [author publication page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/conjoining-specifications/) describes the two composition/decomposition results. This reading does not independently reproduce their general proof.

**Self-modification.** Everitt, Filan, Daswani and Hutter, [*Self-Modification of Policy and Utility Function in Rational Agents*](https://www.hutter1.net/publ/selfmod.pdf), 2016. The abstract and §§2–3, especially Definition 3, distinguish actions affecting the environment from their selection of later policies. Named policies have an interpretation; beliefs, utilities and the admissible modification structure are supplied. The paper explicitly idealizes modification access. Reading these definitions establishes a close antecedent for representing changing policies within one interaction history, not an assumption-free origin of those policies. No result from the paper is reproduced here.

These readings are link-only. No local original-source bytes or software artifact were obtained. Derived descriptions above remain limited to the inspected passages.

## Comparative implications for the current connection

The existing [composition map](FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) already distinguishes shared histories, conditional transports and sibling realizations. The local [protocol composition](PROTOCOL-COMPOSITION-MATH.md) and controlled-refinement work already cover generic flattening and observation/memory preservation. Repeating those results, or importing every module into one file, would not explain how their actual interfaces connect.

The selected bridge instead analyzes the existing acquired-evaluation successor. It must locate a reached learned state, its actual action preparation, the current permission boundary and the subsequent paid effects in one continuing history. Permission before work and permission after work have different consequences. A later cut can block fresh work without erasing an earlier transfer or preventing completion of its already funded record. Those are exact questions about the supplied law, not a claim to a new general authorization calculus.

The whole-model requirement also concerns the origin of the mechanisms. A transition relation containing a controller, a modifier and an access gate can represent their interaction while leaving their origin unexplained. Likewise, an invariant imposed by a one-way revocation law does not derive why that law exists or why its authority is legitimate. There must be a separate account of learned formation, practical grounds and warranted governance wherever those stronger claims are retained.

## Identification and adversarial evidence

A proof about the actual finite law can establish that all admitted continuations obey the stated boundary. It cannot establish that a real agent's complete behavior is contained in that law. Empirical identification would require independently checked event coverage, actual grant timing, effects, costs and unmediated escape routes. The earlier [learned-agent maintenance experiment](../../../../experiments/agentdojo-evidence-boundary/correction-action/continuation/RESULTS.md) has a supplied broker and a different implementation; it cannot be silently treated as this synthetic learner's continuation or as independent validation of every formal event.

Useful adversarial cases include a stale favorable record after a cut, a cut between reservation and physical work, a cut after work but before recording, an attempted unauthorized new probe, and partial or unavailable evidence. Same endpoint balances do not establish the same causal history. Endowed resources, the controller's signal interpretation and the practical significance of recipient credit require their own justification.

Exact conventional execution of the same law is a realization, not a competing prediction. Added explanatory value must come from a newly connected actual interface, an eliminated dependency or a previously unrecognized failure. The stronger connected and assumption-free objective remains open until its remaining premises are discharged.
