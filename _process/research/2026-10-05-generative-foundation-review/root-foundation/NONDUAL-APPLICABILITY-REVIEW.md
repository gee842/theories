## The strongest account supported by the existing configuration

The [generative account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#from-interacting-differences-to-consequential-organization) already proposes that events acquire organizational significance through their consequences for the relations sustaining further activity. The [relational formulation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/THEORY.md#3-a-situation-with-differentiated-centers) places the observer, instruments, activities and affected centers within that explanation. A representation distinguishes these relations for a question; it does not establish their fundamental independence.

The strongest local reconstruction begins with a continuing configuration whose capture, loss, repair and reproduction relations can be identified independently of the label “successful regulation.” A damaged capture site, its incident source, the assembled response, accessible resources and the products of their interaction belong to that configuration together. Need here means a specific impairment or shortfall in those continuing relations. Its organizational significance is the difference it makes to their continuations. The word does not yet assert felt suffering, an ethical entitlement or an overall obligation to preserve the organization.

An accessible difference can be the affected condition itself. In the [original seven-event regulation law](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-MATH.md), the damaged site D catalyzes paid formation; G, D and F participate in repair; repair changes D into intact C, making capture available. No classifier first declares damage meaningful and then directs an otherwise unrelated plant. The physical relation produces the response opportunity and is changed by the response. This law supplies the reaction repertoire and does not explain its origin. Its positive content nevertheless exceeds a retrospective observer reward: the recorded continuation and copying counts do not appear in a propensity.

The [existing formed-selection law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/FormedSelection.lean) makes a related configuration discrete. Repair requires an active resident, a damaged site and common fuel. It restores the site and spends fuel. The repaired site no longer satisfies the repair condition and can capture support if local storage is empty and a source remains. Capture fills that storage and thereby excludes another capture until local copying or leakage empties it. These are actual changes in relations of admissibility under the unchanged law, not evaluation labels supplied by a new stopping mechanism.

This gives a precise positive meaning to local completion: the interaction transforms the condition that called for, and enabled, this response. Its completion can change what activity is relevant next. Repair, capture and consumption are differentiated aspects of this activity; none needs an independent completion judge in this construction. An externally retained report may later inform another process, but it does not create the repaired site's condition.

Need and executable response remain distinguishable within this configuration. Damage can persist after the active resident wears away or repair fuel is exhausted. In that case repair is unavailable, but restoration has not occurred. Conversely, restoring the site removes its damage even if fuel remains. The positive account therefore tracks which actual relation changed; it cannot identify every disabled response with a completed need. Permission, where a later model represents it, requires the same distinction.

## One restoration can end while renewal remains relevant

Completing one repair does not complete all regenerative activity. The site may remain intact while local fuel is used and further capture becomes available. If damage later occurs, repair becomes applicable again, conditional on a resident and affordable fuel. A trace-level balance of intact state, damage and repair can establish that a further repair after restoration requires actual intervening damage. A corresponding storage balance can distinguish capture, local use and renewed availability. These balances articulate a relation already present in the event law; they do not derive its chemistry.

Historical accomplishment, current condition and continuing capacity must remain distinct. A record that support once arrived can stay true while the supported relation subsequently changes. Likewise, identifying a finite requested service does not imply that all of the recipient's needs end when that service is delivered. For a genuinely finite service, its scope can be formed and maintained through the actual request and agreement; for an ongoing organizational dependency, the relevant distinction can be enacted by restoration, consumption and damage themselves. Neither requires an evaluator outside generative activity.

The local model does not establish indefinite renewal. In FormedSelection, capture enters `localFuel`; it does not replenish common `fuel`. Formation, association and repair consume common fuel. The existing `trace_common_ledger` and `no_infinite_event_path` therefore prevent describing this as a fully self-funding or indefinitely regenerative repair system. The seven-event law and the sixteen-event law also have different resource routes. Their instructive resemblance is not an exact kinetic composition or a shared concrete history.

## Substantive rivals and revision conditions

Rival accounts must first preserve the independently specified configuration, history and target relations. When the proposed test changes a mechanism or source, that is a declared intervention applied to the same inherited preparation, not evidence that two different complete situations are identical. The following comparisons challenge identifiable claims rather than freely changing a value predicate.

| Rival account or adverse case | What is held fixed, and what would decide the issue |
|---|---|
| The response stops only through exhaustion, not restoration-sensitive regulation. | The original actual repaired endpoint has zero common fuel, and its captured endpoint has zero source. Those observations alone do not separate the rival. General guard proofs can establish the structural dependence on intactness or storage even with spare resources; an actual paid witness with spare fuel/source would demonstrate the distinction without a resource reset. |
| All apparent “significance” is ordinary chemistry described using a chosen organizational vocabulary. | Preserve every reaction, history and continuation difference. The positive account must explain why capture/restoration identifies an actual dependency independently of counting G as success. If both descriptions then make identical claims, no experiment discriminates their vocabulary or establishes a unique metaphysics. The conditional causal result remains; an extra normative or phenomenal conclusion does not follow. |
| Damage is a reliable indication of when repair benefits the process overall. | The seven-event law already preserves the same repair and sensing chemistry with an exhausted source. Repair still restores C but fails to yield new support and reduces copying relative to the disabled arm. This defeats unconditional benefit and requires the account to include actual source and opportunity relations. It does not falsify the fact that C was repaired. |
| Completing a successful event makes further service inappropriate. | Keep the actual ongoing dependency, restoration and later consumption/damage in view. Earlier delivery and renewed need can coexist. A claim about a finite agreed service must preserve that agreement; it cannot silently become a claim about the whole continuing organization. |
| A closed return loop necessarily favors the contributor or grounds its entitlement. | The same FormedSelection repertoire admits `repaired_site_can_pass_to_alternative`: resident wear and paid replacement allow the nonrepairing alternative to use the restored site. Preserve the entire history, costs and effects. This challenges an inference from local functional closure to reliable contributor selection; no actor's entitlement is established by the access bit or by calling a site owned. |
| Preserving the organization is automatically beneficial to every affected center. | Preserve its actual maintenance relation and any independently identified injuries to others. A damaging organization can repair the machinery through which it harms them. The organizational account must describe both consequences rather than erase one into aggregate persistence. The present chemical model contains no affected sentient patient or injury evidence, so such a case tests a broader application rather than refuting its local ledger. |
| A damaged component is necessarily an unmet functional need. | Hold the independently identified continuation capacity fixed and test actual alternative routes, redundant sites or a change of activity. If a bypass preserves the claimed capability, the particular component was not indispensable at that scope. This revises the proposed dependency or boundary rather than adding an arbitrary negative judgment. |

The useful test programme therefore measures or proves restoration-dependent response suppression, consumption-dependent reopening, the actual return route, the contributions of alternative routes, and costs to differentiated centers. It preserves the initial organization and resources across each intervention and reports failures. A stopped action is not enough to identify completed need; a prior receipt is not enough to identify current restoration; persistent copying is not enough to identify maintained shared activity.

## Acceptance of the proposed formal continuation

The mechanism proposal is useful if it remains an additive statement about the original laws: exact intact/damage/repair and storage/capture/consumption balances; the resulting guard changes; an actual resource-accounted witness where claimed; and the original root trace interface. The complementary patient-credit independence result should explicitly retain translated credit values and accounting rather than calling the two full states equal. It demonstrates an omitted recipient relation, not the irrelevance of actual patients.

The two analyses must retain their distinct concrete histories. Shared root notation enables comparison, but does not itself join the repaired-site process to the recipient-successor process. No claim that this closes the entire need-to-concern or alignment derivation is accepted. Direct material sensitivity is not yet acquired understanding; useful regulation is not yet ethical justification; methodologically beginning within one unfolding does not discharge a supplied physical law.

This review changes the interpretation and next research question without erasing earlier proofs. The positive advance is that a current consequential condition can govern and be transformed by a response within one activity, so local completion and continuing renewal need not be imported as a separate evaluation layer. The next task is to reconstruct which of those relations the recipient model must faithfully represent and how its actual learning, recording and action participate in them. Empirical identification, formation of that relation and ethical significance keep their own scopes.
