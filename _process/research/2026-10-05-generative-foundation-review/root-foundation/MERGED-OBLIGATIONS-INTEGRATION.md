## Assumption and counterexample review

**Conservation does not establish a uniquely necessary law.** Exact conservation and termination follow for the supplied native/formation rules. The drawless rival establishes non-necessity of one input for named conclusions; it does not establish that every law is assumption-free. The rank `2 × nativePotential + spares` is a termination argument, not a physical unit conversion or ethical value.

**The renewal comparison must retain units.** A successful packet consumes ten source units and yields **655,360 held ticks**. Comparing ten transferred units directly with the interface draw would mix units. In the actual low-overhead witness, formation draws 16,386 held ticks and formation plus the probe draws 49,158; its arrival covers the segment. With greater live overhead, formation alone draws 716,386, exceeding the same packet yield. An empty source permits opening but no arrival. These actual countercases defeat universal one-packet adequacy without disproving renewal in a richer process.

**The older recurrence statement must retain its quantifiers.** `RecursUnboundedly` says that for every bound there exists a finite marked trace. The positive return theorem finds **some reachable marked transition** on a return path. It does not say every marked transition preserves eventual possibilities, supply one selected infinite actual history, or establish that every offered restoration occurs. The current research-index wording is narrowed accordingly; the original theorem and historical handoff remain preserved.

**A fulfilled claim and an available opportunity are different targets.** The naturalism study's practical conclusion concerns an independently warranted claim to actual or timely fulfillment. A merely possible return cannot certify its fulfillment. A claim whose terms are restored access or opportunity may instead be satisfied by an accessible route. The affirmative result therefore checks whether an offered reason answers its stated claim; it does not derive all such claims' standing.

The three contributors and parent review are collaborating AI work, not external peer review.
