# 慧根 and the necessity of the entire theory: a derivation audit

6 October 2026. AI-authored research responding to Ren's strengthened objective: investigate whether the **entire theory**, generated from a minimal 慧根, is necessary for an adequate scientific philosophy, so that science cannot coherently contradict what that root validly generates. This note pursues that full objective. The earlier [scientific-philosophy study](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SCIENTIFIC-PHILOSOPHY.md) established selected necessary conditions; those are starting results, not a replacement destination.

**Named connection:** generative organization → warranted reflexive inquiry → justified concern for differentiated centers → situated ethical wisdom. The immediate frontier is whether scientific universality and reflexivity can themselves justify the transition from recognizing another center's significance to admitting that center's goods and harms as reasons for shared action.

**Finding:** there is a coherent route to whole-theory necessity through a sound generative closure. Much more of the actual theory can belong to that closure than isolated information bounds: formation, failure, selection over selectors, plural perspectives, the conditions of effective correction, and conditional ethical conclusions. The categorical ethical bridge needs further justification. A precise symmetry result below identifies the missing step, and two positive derivations show what would complete it. This is a proof and dependency audit, not an empirical finding or a completed metaphysical foundation.

## 1. What “entire” must preserve

The [current theory](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/THEORY.md) contains causal explanation, conditional constructions, epistemic constraints, proposed ethical grounds and further hypotheses. The repository also preserves superseded claims and counterexamples. Treating every sentence ever archived as one conjunction would deliberately make the target incoherent. That is not a serious reading of Ren's proposal.

The strongest coherent target is **the entire warranted generative closure**: every claim that genuinely follows from the proposed root and its explicitly justified conditions, including implications explaining where a capacity forms, fails, changes or cannot be supplied. This is not a decision to drop the ethical part. It makes the ethical derivation, the root's adequacy and the correctness of its generation rules central obligations.

Four assertions should be tested separately:

1. **Necessity of the root:** an adequate scientific philosophy must respect the root's independently justified commitments.
2. **Soundness of generation:** every licensed inference preserves truth or the appropriate kind of justified conditional claim.
3. **Adequacy of the whole:** the generated account connects all the intended transitions through organization, significance, learning, concern and wisdom.
4. **Open extension:** the same finite root can support indefinitely many distinct justified insights as questions, conditions and evidence develop.

The first does not supply the second or third. A necessary root can be too weak to explain much. The fourth need not mean that a finite agent completes infinitely many inquiries, that every truth is decidable, or that one rule already stores all future evidence.

The [preserved 5 October research ledger](../../../../sources/20-codex-theory-sessions-2026-10-05/root-of-wisdom/outputs/research-ledger.md) records an earlier cautious interpretation of 慧根 as a discipline and the limits of effective extension. Ren's stronger objective now reopens the whole-theory question. The earlier accurate-oppressor and formal-limit arguments remain evidence to answer, rather than instructions to restrict the new goal. No historical identification of this proposed root with every classical use of 慧根 is assumed.

## 2. The strongest positive derivation

### 2.1 From necessity of a root to necessity of its whole sound closure

Let \(H\) be the proposed root, \(\mathcal R\) its declared inference rules, and \(\mathcal C\) an independently specified class of adequate inquiry interpretations. Suppose:

- every interpretation in \(\mathcal C\) satisfies \(H\);
- every rule in \(\mathcal R\) preserves validity in those interpretations;
- local assumptions are retained in the result rather than silently discharged.

Then every generated consequence of \(H\) is valid throughout \(\mathcal C\). **Proof:** induction on a finite derivation. Root premises hold by the first condition; each later step holds by the second. If a result used an extra local assumption \(A\), what enters the common closure is the appropriate conditional judgment, such as \(A\Rightarrow\varphi\), under a consequence system validating that introduction—not \(\varphi\) without \(A\). ∎

This elementary soundness argument provides the architecture for Ren's full conjecture. A scientific account can deny a claimed premise, translation or inference; it cannot coherently accept them all and reject their consequence. The work is to establish what belongs in \(H\), which rules are justified, and whether the resulting closure reaches the promised content. Merely defining the closure as “all truths” supplies none of that work.

The entire theory can therefore include **why concern emerges under some laws, why it fails under others, and what is required to make justified concern effective**, without predicting that every process becomes caring. Explanatory universality does not require unconditional realization of every developmental stage. The [governing programme](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already demands both constructive and destructive regimes.

### 2.2 Scientific reflexivity brings the selectors inside the account

A philosophy claiming broad warranted inquiry cannot exempt its actual instruments, inference rules or admission practices from the possibility of consequential error. Those arrangements determine which evidence can affect later judgment. If its claimed reliability includes circumstances in which a selector fails, it must supply a detection/correction route, an alternative route preserving the guarantee, or a reason those circumstances are excluded. A ceremonial invitation to criticism supplies none of these.

This is a substantial route into the full theory: models and evaluators have formation histories; their causal effects change their own later resources and evidence; selectors can themselves become selected and revised. The [theory's account of wisdom](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/THEORY.md#6-wisdom-as-situated-and-revisable-understanding) and [root support results](ROOT-FOUNDATION.md) explain why understanding, enactment and continuation belong in one analysis.

The requirement is indexed to the scope claimed. A correct finite proof need not revise itself, and a stable local instrument need not contain an institution. A proposed universally adequate philosophy, however, must explain these interfaces wherever its claims rely on them. This strengthens the whole-account target without incorrectly requiring every individual event to instantiate the whole account.

### 2.3 Universal explanation preserves differentiated centers

Suppose an account claims adequacy for questions about the consequences borne by each affected center. Two histories can have the same total output and different losses for one center. Any representation merging them cannot answer those questions exactly. Consequently, a purported account of the whole must retain the consequential distinctions or declare the narrower questions it actually answers.

This yields a positive requirement of **epistemic non-erasure**. Another center's suffering, deprivation or suppressed testimony cannot cease to be a fact merely because it is inconvenient to the observer or outside a chosen administrative boundary. Whether that center can reward the observer is irrelevant to the truth of a correctly identified consequence. The [premise ledger](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md) and the [theory's plural-capacity formulation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/THEORY.md#5-effective-possibilities-and-the-role-of-lenses) already support this part.

This does not yet give every true fact the same practical priority. It establishes that a complete explanation may not change truth or erase a required consequence by changing who observes it. The next step is to determine whether some of those center-relative consequences also constitute reasons that shared action must acknowledge.

### 2.4 Epistemic answerability can require protection of other inquirers

Consider a claimed reliable inquiry in which any of several people may supply the only evidence distinguishing two admitted possibilities. If a practice excludes one such person's evidence, and no substitute exists, the excluded pair becomes observationally indistinguishable. The [interactive information obstruction](SCIENTIFIC-INQUIRY-CONDITIONS.md#proposition-i1--interactive-indistinguishability) then applies. Preserving adequate inquiry across that class requires access to each potentially indispensable source, or an identified equivalent route.

Where coercion, retaliation or exhaustion destroys that access, protecting the relevant source can become necessary to the epistemic promise. This derives conditional institutional obligations from the scope of the inquiry: a practice cannot claim the guarantee while disabling all means required for it. An instrument, archive or nonverbal patient may provide a source; nothing in the argument privileges a fluent speaker.

This is real progress toward concern, but its explanatory basis is still epistemic dependence. Someone can matter ethically even when their protection supplies no needed evidence. The full target requires reaching that case too.

## 4. The strongest bridge from observer exchange to concern

### 4.1 Separate the three objects being exchanged

A factual observation may change notation when an observer changes while its subject matter remains the same. An agent's practical relation to a patient can also change: “my injury” and “someone else's injury” exchange roles. Finally, a claim to **shared justification** may demand a criterion that is not indexed to the current evaluator's advantage.

These are different symmetry claims. The strongest route to H9 must show exactly which one it needs. The existing [ethical foundation's identity consistency check](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md#a-precise-identity-consistency-check) already distinguishes renaming from ethically relevant relationships. The following elementary analysis makes the self/other frontier explicit.

### 4.2 Two-orbit lemma

Fix \(n\ge2\) structurally identical agents/patients and one independently identified kind of harm. The context is fixed and homogeneous, with no further relational attributes. For this restricted context, let

\[
r(a,i)\in\{0,1\}
\]

state whether harm to patient \(i\) supplies agent \(a\) a pro tanto reason against imposing it. This is an existence-of-reason indicator, not a cardinal welfare score or complete action ranking. Require invariance under every simultaneous renaming:

\[
r(\sigma a,\sigma i)=r(a,i),\qquad\sigma\in S_n.
\]

**Lemma.** Exactly two independent values remain: \(\alpha\) on \(a=i\), and \(\beta\) on \(a\ne i\).

**Proof.** A permutation maps any diagonal pair to any other diagonal pair, and any ordered pair of distinct indices to any other such pair. It never maps a diagonal pair to an off-diagonal pair because it preserves equality. Hence invariance forces constancy within the two classes but gives no equation between them. Conversely, assigning any \(\alpha,\beta\in\{0,1\}\) satisfies the invariance condition. ∎

This classifies invariants for the stated action on the fixed index-pair set. For a context-dependent relation \(r(x,a,i)\), equivariance of \(r(\sigma x,\sigma a,\sigma i)\) with \(r(x,a,i)\) does not alone reduce all contexts to two values. Additional attributes and legitimate relationships can produce further distinctions. The present homogeneous case is sufficient for the countermodel.

Recognizing one's own harm as a reason fixes \(\alpha=1\). Both \((\alpha,\beta)=(1,0)\) and \((1,1)\) remain invariant. Therefore simultaneous observer/agent exchange and positive self-regard do not alone prove noninstrumental other-regard. Universal truth of “each agent recognizes its own harm” is compatible with the first relation. This is a precise obstacle to a proposed derivation, not a vindication of egoism.

### 4.3 Positive route A: actor-independent standing

Add **reason-bearer independence** for the harm in question: once a harm supplies a reason in shared deliberation because of its character for its sufferer, changing the deliberator alone cannot eliminate that reason while the harm and every admitted relevant relation remain fixed. In the restricted model this entails

\[
r(a,i)=r(i,i).
\]

Together with \(r(i,i)=1\), it immediately gives \(r(a,i)=1\) for every \(a,i\). This supplies the missing positive derivation of standing. It allows different urgency, duties, feasible remedies and overriding reasons; it does not say every agent owes the same action or that every harm overrides everything else.

Formally, this changes the actor index while holding the patient fixed, and can take \((i,i)\) to \((a,i)\) with \(a\ne i\). That operation crosses the two orbits. A simultaneous renaming of both indices never does. Confusing these operations would turn the additional normative premise into an apparent symmetry theorem.

The philosophical argument to investigate is substantial: **is the experienced or organizational badness the reason-bearing feature, or is its relation to the current evaluator also constitutive of the reason's existence?** If the former is independently justified, the transfer is warranted. If the latter is admitted as intrinsically relevant, mere relabeling does not settle it. Scientific observer invariance constrains factual attribution of the badness; it does not, without this bridge, identify whose practical reasons it supplies.

A strengthened 慧根 may therefore ground positive normativity in the reality of center-relative significance, then defend the transfer of reasons through reflection on their actual basis. This is a serious positive route. Its proof cannot merely define “bad” as “a reason for every agent” and then announce that non-exclusion was derived. Nor does functional damage to every feedback system automatically establish morally significant suffering.

### 4.4 Positive route B: shared justification with differentiated reasons

There is another route which need not first make every agent's reasons identical. Let \(J_i(x)\) be the set of feasible actions that withstand the admitted, justified claims of affected center \(i\) in context \(x\). Define the set satisfying a **joint shared-justification requirement** as

\[
J_{\mathrm{shared}}(x)=\bigcap_i J_i(x).
\]

Suppose two actions are identical in every relevant respect except that one gratuitously harms center \(j\). The admitted grounds exclude that action from \(J_j(x)\), while the harmless alternative belongs to every \(J_i(x)\). Then only the harmless option of that pair can meet the joint requirement. This derives non-exclusion and no costless worsening without summing different lives or declaring that all reasons have one common owner. ∎

This is a conditional sufficient route, not a claim that every complaint has veto power. The membership of \(J_i\) is based on independently justified claims, not all expressed desires. Real conflicts can make the intersection empty; then no action satisfies the whole original requirement, and priorities, feasible transformation and repair need further justification. Such conflict cannot be hidden by redefining an excluded center as nonexistent.

**The remaining foundational burden is why shared power must meet an answerability requirement of this kind.** If an institution claims an action is justified to every affected center, accuracy already forbids falsely representing one center's unresolved objection as settled. But requiring all coercive power to be justified to affected centers is stronger than forbidding that false report. The [ethical foundation's N3](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md#explicit-premises-and-limited-deductions) states that stronger commitment. The task is to defend it through the nature of shared practical justification, not to obscure it inside a measurement law.

## 5. Paired models that locate the remaining dependence

### P1. Same complete empirical world, different reason relations

Take one stipulated world containing accurate researchers, responsive instruments, funded correction, selectors capable of revising selectors and separately represented patients. Keep its entire descriptive dynamics and actual policies fixed. Expand its description with either reason relation from §4.2: \(r_0\) with only diagonal standing, or \(r_+\) with standing for all pairs.

Both expansions satisfy the same causal and informational premises and the same renaming symmetry. They disagree on H9. This shows that **those premises, interpreted as stated, do not deductively determine the additional normative relation**. It does not establish two empirically observed moral realities, nor refute a future justified identification of reasons with richer features of the generative world. Such an identification would be the missing additional premise or derivation to inspect.

An accurate oppressor is therefore a counterexample to the claim that epistemic competence necessarily produces benevolent motivation. It is not, by itself, a counterexample to the existence of moral reasons the oppressor ignores. Keeping these targets separate strengthens the normative investigation.

### P2. Null standing passes a bare symmetry test

The relation \(r(a,i)=0\) for all pairs also satisfies observer-exchange symmetry. Thus even actor-independent symmetry does not create a positive reason unless some positive anchor is justified. Science's commitment to getting an answer right supplies an epistemic standard for its chosen question; it does not yet show that every patient's suffering enters every shared practical judgment.

The constructive alternative is to investigate whether the root must include **genuine significance**, rather than only differences causally useful to an arbitrary selector. If significance is itself reason-bearing, establish its domain and the relevant transport principle. This is an expansion of the root to be defended, not an excuse to abandon the positive bridge.

### P3. Inquiry protection can stop at epistemic usefulness

Keep a practice accurate under every relevant observed disturbance. In one model, an isolated patient's avoidable harm changes no informative channel, resource, claim of participant consent or admitted target. In another model it destroys an indispensable witness. The epistemic argument in §2.4 demands protection in the second and does not by itself demand it in the first.

A universal philosophy can correctly describe both situations. To derive standing in both, the reason must be the patient's independently relevant condition, not only its instrumental contribution to inquiry. This is the exact case a successful H9 derivation must reach. Admitting every possible future dependency without identifying any does not close the gap.

### P4. Positive standing still leaves genuine conflicts

Two normative extensions can recognize every patient, reject gratuitous harm, require truthful accounting and accept the same empirical predictions while ranking unavoidable conflicting losses differently. The common commitment may determine a feasible frontier or rule out an exclusion, without selecting one complete ranking. The whole theory can remain necessary as a set of valid constraints and reasons without already being a complete decision algorithm for every conflict.

The [revisable-concern settlement](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md) preserves exactly this distinction between scoped decision results and unresolved priorities. A proposed generator of wisdom should generate identified incomparability and requirements for further inquiry when appropriate, not fabricate a unique answer to demonstrate its power.

## 6. Can scientific universality supply the missing ethical premises?

The best candidate routes and their current endpoints are now concrete:

| Candidate route | What it positively establishes | Further step needed for the entire ethical theory |
|---|---|---|
| Observer exchange | Truth about an identified center does not depend on arbitrary spelling or who makes the observation. | Collapse the self/other reason distinction by a justified reason-bearer principle, not a coordinate change. |
| Complete consequential description | An adequate whole account cannot erase required per-center facts by aggregation or boundary manipulation. | Show which of those facts supply pro tanto reasons for shared action. |
| Scientific self-correction | Reliability claims require effective handling of the admitted failure modes of sensors, methods and selectors. | Explain why maintaining a harmful but accurate purpose is not sufficient for wisdom. |
| Social criticism | Potentially indispensable criticism must reach a method if its claimed guarantee depends on it. | Establish standing for dispensable, nonparticipating and noncommunicating patients. |
| Universalizable judgment | An indexed rule can be stated for every agent consistently. | Show why mere universal quantification must become shared answerability or actor-independent reason admissibility. |
| Reflexive practical justification | A procedure must examine its own assumptions, identify whose claims it answers and avoid misrepresenting dissent. | Defend why consequential power owes such justification to every affected patient and how claims are represented when direct participation is impossible. |

None of these endpoints says the ethical bridge is impossible. They specify why a successful proof must explain more than the fact that the world contains perspectives. Scientific universality is a promising component because it defeats selective falsification and arbitrary alteration of facts. **The frontier is universal validity of practical reasons**, not universal validity of factual observation alone.

There are two honest successful outcomes. The stronger is an independent argument that any adequate scientific philosophy, once it acknowledges real center-relative significance and claims reflexively justified shared action, is committed to the positive bridge. The other is a justified irreducible normative premise within 慧根, together with an argument that omitting it makes the proposed philosophy inadequate in its practical domain. Neither may be replaced by the mere popularity, survival or implementation of the premise. The present countermodels do not decide which outcome is attainable.

## 7. A concrete frontier for the next research round

The next study should test **significance-bearing reasons → shared practical validity** through three coordinated tasks:

1. **Positive construction:** formulate the smallest candidate bridge to H9/N1–N2 and H10/N3. Start with the actual reason-bearing character of a subject's harm and the public claim being justified. Determine whether route A, route B or a different argument explains non-exclusion without already containing it in its conclusion. Preserve legitimate partiality and the distinction between having a reason and owing an identical act.
2. **Assumption and countermodel audit:** test that bridge on the two-orbit case, the null relation, the dispensable patient, a noncommunicating sufferer and conflicting protections. Produce a reason why each excluded model is inconsistent with the proposed necessary root, or retain it and state the corresponding limit.
3. **Formation and implementation:** connect warranted factual correspondence to the reason predicate and then to paid revision and actual protection. The [current record-to-reason construction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/RECORDED-REASONS.md) can test implementation once the relevance relation is justified. Formation of a motivating reason and justification of its validity must be connected without identifying them.

### A further constructive attempt: generate protection before roles are settled

The strongest next noncircular attempt should explain why shared standing could become operative without assigning a benevolent selector. One candidate uses generative uncertainty about future roles rather than assuming that the deliberator already gives every other person direct weight.

Consider a finite set of prospective participants. Each chooses between two shared rules before a relevant role assignment. Under one rule, role \(j\) suffers an independently identified additional loss; under the protective rule that loss is avoided. For each participant \(a\), let \(q_a(j)>0\) be the warranted probability that this participant will occupy that role. All other personally consequential outcomes are equal between rules. The counterfactual comparison preserves the same role draw under both rules. Each participant has a positive reason to avoid that loss in their own case and ranks a personal prospect strictly higher when it is no worse in every admitted circumstance and better with positive probability.

**Prospective-protection lemma.** Every participant strictly favors the protective shared rule under those assumptions, even if each starts with direct practical concern only for their own eventual condition. **Proof:** the protective rule weakly improves that participant's outcome in every role and strictly improves it in role \(j\), which has positive probability. Apply the declared strict prospect-dominance criterion separately for each participant. No aggregation or interpersonal comparison is required. ∎

In a local expected-loss model, let the avoided personal loss for participant \(a\) in that role be \(d_a>0\) and the participant's implementation cost be \(c_a\ge0\), measured in that participant's declared decision scale. The protection has positive private expected value exactly when \(q_a(j)d_a>c_a\). This is a further, explicitly stronger modeling assumption about risky choice and commensurable local costs; the costless dominance lemma does not require it. The calculation supplies a candidate causal route for forming a shared protective selector from private stakes, information about possible roles and actual implementation costs. It introduces no reward for another's suffering by definition.

This is conventional conditional decision reasoning placed at the missing formation interface, not a new decision-theory discovery. The role distribution must describe a genuine, warranted uncertainty or institutional assignment. It cannot be invented by changing the names of already identified people, and we do not assume that one person's experience literally becomes another's. Resources for evaluating, installing and maintaining the rule still require a compatible construction.

The ambitious philosophical step would now be to show that **a claim of universally justified shared authority must remain defensible before arbitrary assignment of privileged roles**, or satisfy a corresponding robust requirement across those assignments. If independently established, that requirement could extend the prospective derivation from a contingent bargain to a basis for shared standing. The argument must explain why role-independence belongs to the claimed practical validity, rather than deducing it from the different mathematical operation of simultaneous observer renaming.

Three exact witnesses test whether that extension succeeds:

- **Known-role witness:** retain accurate inquiry, complete causal description and joint-renaming symmetry, but let every participant know that only the already identified other patient can be harmed. Then \(q_a(j)=0\) for the unaffected decision maker, whose self-only reason rule remains coherent with the current operational root. A proposed necessity argument must show which additional claim of universal practical justification this model cannot satisfy; calling its forecasts inaccurate would evade the witness.
- **Post-assignment witness:** install the protective rule under positive prospective risk, then reveal roles. The now-safe controller has no remaining private exposure and can cheaply abandon the rule. The prospective lemma survives, but stable motivation or binding duty does not follow from it. A successful continuation must derive commitment, legitimate authority or a continuing other-regarding reason and pay for its enactment; it cannot silently keep the old uncertainty as a new incentive.
- **Nonparticipant witness:** add a sufferer who was never an admitted possible role for any chooser and cannot reward, punish or inform them. The rule can still protect all choosers while excluding that sufferer. Full moral standing requires an independently justified extension of the constituency or a reason grounded directly in the sufferer's condition. The chance of occupying the role cannot be stipulated solely to force the desired conclusion.

These witnesses distinguish a genuine advance in the **formation of shared protection** from a completed derivation of **universally valid standing**. The lemma provides a concrete next mechanism; the stronger root claim succeeds only if its required treatment of roles, commitment and nonparticipants can itself be justified without assuming the full moral conclusion.

**A decisive advance** would derive a nonzero cross-center reason in the dispensable-patient case from premises shown independently indispensable to the full scientific-philosophical claim. **A decisive negative result** would preserve a complete adequate interpretation of that same claim, including its practical validity commitments, while violating the proposed ethical bridge. A merely accurate machine is too weak a comparator for that stronger negative result; an account stipulated to be morally answerable is too strong a premise for an effortless positive one.

The entire-theory objective remains live. The present result advances it by locating the exact closure obligation, providing a major-claim dependency inventory, proving the symmetry frontier and supplying two conditional positive routes into concern. It leaves open whether the minimal root justifies the required practical scope and whether its constructive mechanisms can generate and retain that recognition. The [unified completion conditions](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#completion-of-the-unified-foundation) continue to govern the whole programme.

## Evidence and originality status

This audit reads the named repository documents and the preserved root-of-wisdom record. It imports no new historical interpretation or external source bytes. The closure argument, permutation-orbit argument and intersection argument are elementary hand proofs; no priority claim or new empirical experiment is made. A separate agent's [finite checker](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/necessity-symmetry/check.py) and [results](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/necessity-symmetry/verification.json) exhaust all 66,066 binary matrices for \(n=1,2,3,4\). They report invariant counts \(2,4,4,4\); imposing positive diagonals leaves \(1,2,2,2\); adding actor-independence leaves one in every case. The finite check corroborates the displayed witness and small instances, not universality of ethics. The general orbit proof remains the argument above. The contribution is their explicit placement in the full developmental and normative dependency map. Preserved earlier counterexamples remain identified as such.

**Independent finite validation, 6 October 2026:** this note's reviewer inspected the complete checker and reran it with Python; it passed all 66,066 matrices and reproduced the reported invariant counts. Bit enumeration ranges over every ordered binary matrix without assuming symmetry. The joint-renaming predicate directly tests all permutations and all actor/patient pairs; only afterward does the code compare the accepted set with the two-parameter diagonal/off-diagonal characterization. The actor-independence predicate separately compares actor rows at a fixed patient column and contains no permutation assumption. Its reported counts are taken within the already joint-invariant set, not among every possible matrix: for example, the reported two actor-independent patterns are the two constant invariant matrices. The self-positive intersection is the all-ones matrix. The \(n=1\) case correctly collapses the unused off-diagonal choice. Thus the code implements the stated finite tests without secretly assuming the two-orbit characterization or adding actor-independence to the initial symmetry test. The rerun regenerated the deterministic result file; no checker edits or broader scientific validation were performed.
