## 1. The claim being developed

**Evolution can change the organization through which further evolution occurs. The generative account explains this by following how activity forms relationships, how some of those relationships are reconstructed, and how their consequences alter subsequent formation, transmission and selection. Learning and revisable evaluation can arise within that same history.**

**中文核心：演化能够改变使后续演化得以发生的组织。生成论沿实际活动说明关系怎样形成、怎样被重建，其后果又怎样改变后续形成、传递与选择；学习及可修订的评价可以在同一展开中形成。**

The claim has several explanatory levels. The [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) treats organisms, surroundings, inheritance and evaluation as differentiated relationships within the unfolding. Their distinction remains necessary for explaining genealogy, developmental change and consequences; it is not a starting division into independent substances. A local model may hold some relations fixed without placing them outside reality.

The generative orientation alone does not determine a mutation law, a reproduction rate or an ethical ranking. Its theoretical content comes from explicit connections that constrain those possibilities. In particular, beneficial activity does not automatically reproduce its carriers: a consequence must actually affect retention or reconstruction through an identified relation. Losing this relation, sharing the benefit, or changing reconstruction can defeat the proposed feedback. The [recursive-selection account](RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) already preserves this distinction.

## 3. Comparison with evolutionary connectionism

The relevant primary accounts already connect changing relationships to development, ecological organization and transitions in individuality. The 2016 programme studies how selection can change an interaction network in ways related to learning, including memory and generalization under specified conditions. It explicitly investigates changes in the organization of selection. [Watson and colleagues, 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5119841/).

The 2022 account further addresses how relationships among existing units can produce a new individual, using connectionist approaches and nonlinear organization. It states limits of the integration achieved in that paper. Those limits are not evidence that the entire subsequent field remains at the same point. [Watson, Levin and Buckley, 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2022.823588/full). The [reading record](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/comparative-direction/README.md) preserves source scope.

It proves that this mechanism survives that translation, including failures and adaptive choices. It neither reproduces Watson's learning equations nor tests whether the wider foundation explains the origin of those laws. Equivalence of an implemented mechanism is compatible with a difference in explanation; that explanatory difference still needs an argument.

## 4. The affirmative theoretical addition

The present addition is a **connected account of how the capacities that constitute an evolutionary process can themselves become products of further formation and revision**. The derivation table makes this more specific than saying that everything changes. A retained effect must enter a subsequent construction or choice through an actual path. That path can require formation, consume provision, be inherited, be revised, or fail. The same explanatory discipline applies when the changing relation is a developmental attachment, an update rule or an operative goal, with the differences in mechanism preserved.

The ethical continuation adds a further question: which changes to these capacities have reasons in their favor? Under the accepted [G0 and additional practical commitments](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md), continued living and generation have basic positive normative significance, and particular comparisons require their declared further conditions. Reproductive success alone does not answer this question. Neither does the formation of an evaluative disposition establish its correctness.

This differentiation is compatible with one relational account. The activity that forms an evaluator, the relationships its judgments concern, the grounds for a judgment and the actual response are connected aspects of that account. Explaining one connection does not silently establish all the others. G0 is an explicit normative starting point, not a theorem smuggled into a biological fitness definition; the additional comparison commitments are not deductions from G0 alone. The completed axiom-based route is credited without reopening the separate premise-free project.

**The warranted current claim is therefore explanatory integration with explicit conditional derivations and an ethical extension.** We can state that positively without claiming a new universal selection equation, inevitable moral convergence or established superiority over the field.

## 5. What theoretical completion still requires

The next central connection is **the named connectionist learning mechanism → its formation and revision in the generative account → recovery of the same learning consequence under explicit conditions**. This is more precise than reopening generic inheritance or collecting another biological dataset.

For a genuine recovery, identify the original model's developmental variables, interaction changes, selection relation and relevant learning result. Explain how the proposed generative realization supplies those roles. State where the original timescale, recurrence, inheritance and structural conditions are retained, replaced or strengthened. Prove the claimed correspondence and consequence in Lean before treating them as new mathematical results. A generic embedding of both models into an arbitrary transition relation is insufficient: it merely imports the effective law we wanted to explain.

The working theoretical completion criteria are:

1. **Explicit foundation and domain:** state the proposed starting commitments and the biological, learning or evaluative claims they are meant to support. The generic generative orientation alone is not a sufficient dynamics.
2. **Recover the selected established result:** connect actual model roles and hypotheses, with a checked derivation for any new mathematical claim. Broader claims require correspondingly broader recoveries; one case does not replace population genetics or the rest of evolutionary theory.
3. **Identify the additional explanatory work:** show a removed independent input, a shared restriction explaining several mechanisms, or an additional justified connection. List what remains supplied; renaming a law or hiding it inside an initial state is not premise reduction.
4. **Preserve composition and adverse regimes:** demonstrate the interfaces actually claimed, retain failure and destructive cases, and keep justified ethical direction distinct from causal prevalence.

New measurements are not a prerequisite for these theoretical tasks. Claims about biological applicability and comparative prediction retain their own empirical burdens. The [earlier six-part map](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-EVOLUTION.md#completion-requirements) preserves that wider ambition without making its measurement projects the current gate.

## 6. Author counterreview and decision

**Could this still be a collection of familiar models?** Yes. Shared language alone would leave that objection unanswered. The derivation table identifies actual dependencies that have been explained, while the composition classifications prevent a catalogue from masquerading as one proved trajectory. A comparative reduction still requires the named recovery above.

**Does every derivation merely move an assumption downward?** A derivation can be informative when the same independently stated conditions constrain several consequences. It is uninformative if an unrestricted function or initial encoding simply contains the desired answer. Our remaining native laws are therefore part of the comparison, not hidden exceptions. The accepted conditional foundation need not explain every physical law to retain its completed scope.

**Does ethics make this a better biological theory?** Greater subject matter alone does not. The ethical continuation is an additional explanatory and justificatory task with additional commitments. Its value depends on the explicit connection between formation, evaluation and revision; it supplies no automatic advantage in explaining biological inheritance.

**Decision:** the existing contribution is substantive but bounded. The theoretical comparison now credits formation and revision arguments that the previous implementation-equivalence test did not assess, identifies their remaining premises, and selects a precise recovery obligation. The present assessment is complete; the broader claim that this is a superior general theory of evolution remains unestablished. Empirical calibration is retained as a separate future application, not a reason to postpone the theoretical work.
