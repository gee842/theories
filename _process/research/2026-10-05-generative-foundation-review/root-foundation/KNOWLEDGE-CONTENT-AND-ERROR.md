# Formed content, error and epistemic assessment / 内容的形成、错误与认识评价

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation under [Ren's direction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-knowledge-content-continuation.md). Named transition: **formed discrimination and retained use → determinate content and possible misrepresentation → assessment as knowledge**. This develops the [earlier uptake account](KNOWLEDGE-UPTAKE-AND-ERROR.md), using the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The affirmative argument, counterreview and primary assessment are integrated below. No new mathematical theorem, experiment or independent-agent acceptance is claimed.

## 1. The positive proposal and its scope

**A learned empirical representation acquires content through a formed pattern of discrimination, retention and use that makes a particular relation available across encounters. Its content is constrained by the history and organization of that pattern; its current accuracy is assessed against the relation it concerns.** This is a candidate explanation for a specified family of representations, not a definition of every mental state or a completed reduction of meaning.

**内容不是一个孤立符号自己携带的东西；它在形成的辨别、保留、运用及校准关系中取得所指。某次运用出了错，并不自动改变它原来所论的关系。** The distinction between occurrence and accuracy forms within this organization. Both the representation and the relationship it concerns belong to the same unfolding. No independent observer is added to confer meaning from outside it.

The earlier chapter explained cognitive availability while still relying on “content-bearing.” The present continuation makes the burden more explicit: identify which relation is being tracked, what historical and current organization supports that attribution, how the same content survives a false instance, and what counts as a genuine change of content. A correct-output score cannot answer all four.

The scope is empirical representation and the further use of content in judgment. Skilled participation need not always involve propositional content. Conscious experience, a complete account of abstract concepts and all linguistic reference are not silently inferred from this route.

## 2. Begin with formed relationships, not a label

An encounter can change what follows without representing anything. Wear changes a surface; an adjustment changes later responsiveness. A retained difference can also make later activity vary with earlier conditions. To explain a representation, the candidate requires a more specific organization.

**Differentiated acquisition.** Different encounters shape distinguishable states or processes. What is acquired must be traced through actual interaction, learning, inherited organization or a shared practice. A state that an analyst names “water” has not acquired that content through the name alone.

**A reusable relation among differences.** Variations in the acquired state organize different expectations, inferences or responses. The relation can remain useful across changed cues, delayed use or a new combination of previously acquired distinctions. This need not mean an internal picture, a sentence or an always-detached simulation. It means that the explanation identifies a coordinated family of differences, rather than treating one observed success as its own semantics.

**An independently described role in the ongoing organization.** Identify what the use actually does: discriminate locations, anticipate an event, retain a past occurrence, coordinate a report, or support a particular inference. Explain how the role formed and is maintained. Calling the role “representing the right thing” would simply assume the conclusion. More basic observations—what is encountered, retained, transferred and altered following discrepancies—must constrain that description.

**Continuity of content through a defective token.** The history and current use can continue to concern the same relation even when the current cue is misleading or the record stale. If an interpretation continually changes its referent to make every output correct, it no longer explains mistaken representation. Conversely, no eternal semantic essence is stipulated: sustained retraining, new reference practices or a changed role may really change content. A single favorable consequence does not establish that change.

These are explanatory commitments. Some are already partially realized by the repository's learning mechanisms; none is declared a new axiom proving universal semantics. Evidence may support a broad content while leaving a finer distinction underdetermined. We should retain that limitation rather than force a precise proposition onto every mechanism.

## 3. A stronger reading of the existing acquisition history

The [acquired-abstraction study](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION.md) already contains a useful adverse case: an inverted sensor supports a correct model of its own readings while the resulting interpretation of productive access fails. A later [experimental-access investigation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EXPERIMENTAL-ACCESS.md) can preserve accurate sensory prediction while investigating the conditions of its use. A matched reader clamp prevents acquired evidence from revising that use.

This supplies a substantive refinement: **the location of an error may be the application of an accurate representation, rather than the representation's acquired sensory content.** Calling the entire retained model “wrong” erases this difference. Calling every successful prediction knowledge of productive readiness erases it in the other direction.

The formation history constrains what the model acquired: observed continuations organized through its learned distinctions. Productive applicability is an additional relationship. Its failure and repair can be investigated without redefining the earlier observations. The earlier mechanism therefore illustrates why content, application and outcome must remain separately identifiable within one process.

The construction still supplies interfaces, laws, interpretation and candidate families. Its acquired partition is not a demonstrated origin of all intentional content. It was already checked at its recorded historical scope; this continuation neither reruns it nor presents its inherited results as a new proof. The advance is the explicit semantic comparison its preserved failure makes possible.

## 4. Error, failure and content change have different explanations

Consider the earlier stipulated pressure-monitoring practice. Repeated measurement, shared records and learned responses have formed a use of a display as a report of pressure at a specified place and time. That use can be studied through how readings are calibrated, passed on, questioned and revised. The illustration assumes this practice exists; it is not a laboratory finding or a derivation of language from a gauge.

| Change within the practice | What changes, and what does not follow |
|---|---|
| The sample becomes stale, but is still presented as current | The report may become false while retaining its current-pressure content. The existence of a stable display is not accurate current measurement. |
| A false low reading happens to prompt a beneficial inspection | The result can be favorable while the report remains false. The extra consequence does not revise what the report asserted. |
| The pressure report is accurate, but the actuator is jammed | Failure of the intervention does not alone establish an epistemic error. The representation can be correct while an enabling condition for its use fails. |
| The same reading is tagged as a past sample | A different temporal claim may be true. This must be an actual change in interpretation or reporting, not a retrospective rescue imposed by the analyst. |
| The reading's symbols change while calibration and interpretation change with them | Re-encoding need not change content. The corresponding use must be preserved. |
| A deliberately false scenario is considered during planning | Entertaining the scenario is not asserting it. A useful false supposition need not be a false belief. |
| The device is retrained and the practice now uses it to report a different quantity | A sustained change in role and reference can change content. Earlier failures remain failures of the earlier claim. |

These comparisons use changes in actual access, operation and practice, rather than freely assigned truth labels. They preserve counterexamples to identifying accuracy with survival, reward, immediate effectiveness or agreement. They also avoid assuming that every practical failure reveals a false belief.

There is a genuine limit. If two proposed contents fit every relevant acquisition, intervention, use and correction equally well, this evidence does not settle between them. A finer attribution needs a further differentiating relationship, or must remain indeterminate at that level. This is a limit of the present account's evidence, not a proof that content is arbitrary or that the fuller theory must fail.

## 5. From content to a commitment answerable to truth

The content of a state does not alone determine how it is being used. A report, question, hypothesis, memory, wish and instruction can concern a similar situation while imposing different requirements. The pressure illustration brings this out: imagining a dangerous reading is different from reporting that reading as actual.

For the present account, **taking a proposition to be the case places its acceptance under the question of whether that proposition obtains**. This is a proposed constitutive articulation of judgment, supported by the difference between reporting and supposing. It is not derived merely from a mechanism's persistence, and it is not an unconditional moral obligation to inquire into every truth.

The distinction has explanatory consequences. In a report, discovering that the target condition differs normally calls for reassessing the report. In an intention, the difference may instead motivate changing what happens. Both kinds of revision are activities within the same process. Real episodes can mix them: a plan can depend on a factual estimate, and revising that estimate can change the plan. The point is to explain the relation in each case, not to impose two independent mental substances.

The word “normally” here does not mean whichever response is statistically frequent or institutionally rewarded. A community can punish correction, and an individual can prefer a comforting error. The claim about reporting concerns what would make the report accurate; it does not infer legitimacy from prevailing sanctions. Accuracy of content, evidential permission to accept it and ethical permission to disclose it can consequently differ.

The normative strength must remain explicit:

- **Constitutive standard:** a judgment presented as actual is accurate when its content obtains. This identifies the achievement being assessed.
- **Epistemic warrant:** a competent basis and adequate treatment of relevant error are proposed reasons for accepting it. Their adequacy still requires the substantive account in [the knowledge chapter](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/KNOWLEDGE.md).
- **Practical obligation:** whether someone should investigate, retain, revise publicly or disclose depends additionally on the actual circumstances and justified ethical commitments.

The first does not by itself establish the second or third. A definition of truthful reporting cannot compel participation in inquiry, settle how much evidence suffices, or justify every cost of obtaining it. This keeps the accepted axiom route open without hiding additional authority inside an ordinary word.

## 6. What the primary comparisons change

The [continuation source record](KNOWLEDGE-SOURCES.md#content-and-misrepresentation-continuation) contains exact reading scopes. The evidence bears on different parts of the argument; no single source proves the whole.

Millikan's account directs attention to the organization in which a representation functions and its history. Shea, Godfrey-Smith and Cao distinguish functional from informational content in a restricted signalling framework, retaining problematic cases. We use these as serious antecedents and rivals. Our language of one unfolding does not itself supersede their explanations or remove their disclosed inputs.

The empirical comparison is narrower and useful. In the selected sensory-preconditioning study, learned cue relations and a cue's ability to reinforce a new response came apart under the tested procedure. That supports keeping learned relational organization distinct from one measure of acquired reward value. It does not identify propositional belief, consciousness, intrinsic goodness or the complete content of a neural state. The null reinforcement result is not proof of an exact zero for every possible measure of value.

The content-free alternative also remains serious: some skilled, adaptive activity may be explained without truth-evaluable representations. The limited primary abstract examined for this rival is not a complete assessment of radical enactivism. Our affirmative burden is to show where the content attribution improves an explanation of the same capacities, rather than assume that “cognition” already means representation.

## 7. Test the constitutive proposal against equally strong alternatives

The following is an author-conducted counterreview, not an independent-agent review.

**Mere covariance.** A record covaries with multiple stages of its causal history. The candidate adds actual acquisition, differentiated use and correction to constrain which relationship the content concerns. It has not proved that these always determine one unique referent. If the richer account adds no discriminating explanation, covariance plus an economical causal model remains preferable for that case.

**Successful regulation without representation.** A direct adjustment can stabilize a process without needing an intervening content attribution. Repeated success or error feedback does not defeat this rival. Delayed use, transfer among different uses and selective correction of a retained distinction are proposed evidence for the richer account, not conclusive behavioral definitions. A content-free explanation that covers them equally well must be compared on its merits.

**An arbitrary task supplies the alleged meaning.** This criticism succeeds when the task is simply the analyst's preferred score. The repair is to establish the role's actual formation, dependence and use. In a model where these remain supplied, identify them as correspondence assumptions. Even a historically selected role can be harmful; formation does not certify ethical warrant.

**Only rewarded distinctions could have content.** That is too strong for this proposal. A distinction can be acquired before it has a rewarded use, retained through a wider learning capacity, and later recruited. The particular token or fact need not have been selected for its own benefit. This does not abolish the need to explain how acquisition and retention were physically and developmentally possible.

**Correction proves truth only by presupposing it.** A mechanism can change after a discrepancy without genuinely improving its correspondence. The candidate requires evidence about what changed and why the new basis is better. Agreement among copies, an internal error score and increased confidence are not sufficient. Reciprocal calibration can strengthen warrant, but no circuit of mutually endorsing records is automatically self-certifying.

**Meaning changes whenever use changes.** Some changes alter confidence, practical relevance or the means of response while preserving content. The prior sensory-model/application distinction gives a bounded instance. We should infer content change only when the relevant reference or interpretive organization changes, with the original history preserved. A complete criterion for all conceptual change remains a further task.

**A shared whole should have one shared meaning.** Interdependence can produce communication, disagreement, equivocation and deception. Who has access, how a message is interpreted and which actual relation it concerns need to be followed. A common origin does not ensure matching content, common knowledge, equal authority or equal benefit.

## 8. How far this takes G0 and the philosophical account

G0 remains **整体中的生及其继续生成具有基本正面规范意义**. The present route strengthens its connection to knowing: learning can form distinctions that later make dependence, opportunity and loss available for appraisal; warranted correction can protect that capacity from degeneration. Where those relationships support living and further generation, G0 supplies a positive reason to cultivate them, with actual comparison, costs and responsibilities still required. Present understanding can also be part of living realization without needing a later instrumental payoff.

This is a substantive possible application of G0, not an automatic promotion of all information or all representation to goodness. A represented desire can be destructive, a skill can sustain deception, and a factual discovery can be ethically mishandled. Their content can still be identifiable. The positive direction of inquiry concerns its place in the whole continuation; its actual accuracy concerns the relationship represented. Both are assessed within the same reality.

The explanation now extends beyond the earlier definition: it gives a candidate formation route for content, locates mistakes without equating them with failed outcomes, distinguishes content from the mode in which it is taken up, and exposes the different strengths of semantic, epistemic and practical assessment. This advances the recognition/calibration connection needed by the ethical programme. It does not replace that programme's warrant with another successful learner.

The next bridge identified here is **formation and revision of the standards that identify a relation as an adequate reason**, including the move from learned expectation to shared, challengeable judgment. A learned association can guide a response while failing to justify a claim. Social training can produce correction while also institutionalizing error. The [ensuing reasons-and-standards study](KNOWLEDGE-REASONS-AND-STANDARDS.md) advances this connection: it articulates the epistemic commitments, gives a developmental candidate and primary comparisons, and tests social correction against shared error. It does not require a prior fully articulate knower or treat local acceptance as authority.

Three connected tests now have a determinate purpose: distinguish represented expectation from motivational value; distinguish a false content from a failed application of accurate content; and distinguish revising a judgment from merely suppressing a challenge. The empirical comparison constrains the first, the inherited acquisition history realizes a bounded version of the second, and the follow-on study investigates the third philosophically and socially. These are related demands, not three independently completed derivations.

**当前推进：生成的内容可以保留其所指而出错；正确内容也可能被错误运用。认识评价要继续解释，什么根据使接受、保留或修订一个判断值得信赖。** The earlier candidate's completion remains a historical scoped achievement. Ren's broader philosophical question remains open, with this deeper connection now explicitly advanced rather than declared solved by naming it.
