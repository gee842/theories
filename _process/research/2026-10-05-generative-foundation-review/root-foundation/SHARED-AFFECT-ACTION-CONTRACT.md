# From shared affective responses to recipient-directed action

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting contract for **self/other consequence representation → evaluative influence → actual recipient-directed action**. This is an identification audit, not a new apparatus, scientific run or proof of concern's origin. The active evaluator remains with its existing owner.

**The useful additional claim is pathway-specific:** a population identified independently by its self/other responses contributes to how recipient consequences influence choice. Shared neural responses alone establish neither that contribution nor its ethical warrant. The present sources offer complementary evidence, but do not jointly constitute a selective causal test of that entire claim in the same preparation.

## 1. The exact gap in the existing account

The [learning theory](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/learning-formal.md), §§1–5, already separates learned self-value `w`, correspondence admitted into evaluation `g`, and effective influence `v=gw`. Its L1 and identity-gate counterexample establish that accurate shared representation does not force shared evaluation. The [valuation identification note](RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md), §§2–3, already separates recipient sensitivity from efficacy beliefs, motor costs, subjective motives and value acquisition. Reproving either result would add nothing.

The [current uptake construction][uptake] makes equal factual access with different evaluative routing an explicit comparison. A biological analogue must distinguish the same functions rather than identify a firing-rate correlation with its supplied routing bit. The [application contract][application] separately requires a patient-good relation, a defended ground, authority and competing-claim comparison. Neural overlap supplies none of those ethical premises.

The new measurement distinction is between **represented information**, **its downstream readout**, and **its causal contribution to action**. In the existing linear account, a neural feature vector `r` could inform evaluation through a readout `θᵀr`; observing `r` does not identify `θ`. A decoder constructed by an analyst demonstrates accessible information, not that the animal uses that decoder. Opposite firing changes in “anti-mirror” cells need not imply negative concern: downstream signs and interactions matter. Likewise, a positive neural correlation is neither the scalar `g` nor an estimate of `w`.

Even neural–action association leaves alternatives: activity can mediate sensory discrimination, attention, action preparation or valuation; a common process can produce both activity and action. A selective intervention offers a way to distinguish causal contributions, with further exclusions needed to call the contribution evaluative. Observational causal identification is also possible under stronger, defended assumptions. These are applications of the existing identification limits, not a new universal impossibility theorem.

## 2. The source interface

The [evidence audit](SHARED-AFFECT-ACTION-EVIDENCE.md) owns the Wu 2023 design, task order, selection, cell/animal linkage and access findings. The [independent review](SHARED-AFFECT-ACTION-REVIEW.md) owns the Carrillo 2019 and Schneider 2020 comparisons and their primary citations. Those findings supply contact with real biological responses and recipient release, alongside live attention and action-selection alternatives.

These studies cannot be multiplied into one causal chain: a region-level freezing intervention in one study does not experimentally manipulate the action-related cells in another. Their useful joint contribution is to locate a plausible biological route and a discriminating alternative. They do not constitute one observed history of self-value acquisition followed by its causal transfer into help.

## 3. Minimal causal contract

Let `A` denote a specified recipient-directed choice, with delivered recipient consequences recorded separately. Let `H` vary an independently identified recipient stake or benefit while preserving the declared private payoff distribution, opportunity, action demands and information reliability. Let `I` be assigned intact versus selective perturbation of a neural population or output pathway localized without using the test choices.

The primary contrast is whether the intervention changes the recipient-stake response:

\[
\Gamma=\{E[A\mid do(I=0,H=h_1)]-E[A\mid do(I=0,H=h_0)]\}
-\{E[A\mid do(I=1,H=h_1)]-E[A\mid do(I=1,H=h_0)]\}.
\]

This is an interaction estimand, not an experiment already present in the cited archives. Randomization or another defended assignment argument, stable intervention definitions, common support, and controlled carryover are required. Trials and cells remain nested within their actual animals and intervention units.

A nonzero interaction identifies a changed recipient-conditioned action response under those conditions. Interpreting it as an evaluative-route contribution additionally requires:

- Independently verified targeting, with a decision-period manipulation distinguishable from effects on initial sensory acquisition or later movement.
- Preserved discrimination of the recipient condition, identity and action efficacy, tested with informative measures and prespecified equivalence tolerances. A nonsignificant impairment test does not establish preservation.
- Relevant self-choice, nonrecipient action and movement controls, including the possibility that reduced defensive freezing releases action rather than weakens concern.
- Independent evidence of action's actual recipient effect. Opening, contact, observer relief and recipient recovery must not be substituted for one another.

Attention can be a causal mediator of the perturbation. Regressing out post-intervention attention or arousal does not automatically isolate valuation; it changes the estimand and needs additional mediation assumptions. Nor does an intervention need to eliminate every internal feeling to count as recipient sensitivity. The [existing repair-versus-concealment contract](RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md#3-what-the-contrast-cannot-identify-unaided) owns that distinct target-tracking requirement.

There is no universal sign prediction for `Γ` at one arbitrary cost. Moving a choice boundary can increase a probability difference by releasing a ceiling, or erase it at a floor. A directional claim needs a declared choice model and adequate cost/stake support. In the [existing efficacy model](RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md#1-the-distinctions-in-the-actual-model), if an intervention changes only `v` to `λv`, `0≤λ<1`, the strict-protection boundary `a*=z(b+Hv)` becomes `z(b+Hλv)`, a shift of `−zH(1−λ)v`. Equivalently, for `z>0`, normalized extra cost `a/z−b` must be strictly below `Hv`, then `Hλv`; the boundary is a supremum, not an included maximum. This follows immediately from the existing protection inequality; it adds no new model. It is useful as a boundary-shift prediction, conditional on unchanged beliefs, efficacy and other costs.

A recipient-specific effect with stable self-choice calibration is compatible with changed routing `g`; proportional self/other changes are compatible with a shared coefficient `w`. Neither pattern uniquely identifies those parameters without an independently calibrated measurement/readout model. A broad lesion cannot be declared a `g` intervention by naming it one.

## 4. Smallest adjudicable observation and revision rule

For the existing Wu recordings, the smallest possible raw-data contrast is **within animal and independently classified unit, pre-opening activity for a rat versus the same opening with toy/empty contents**, using a fixed window and retaining mirror, anti-mirror and comparison units. Cross-task assignment must use declared stability criteria independently of the action response being tested; electrode proximity alone cannot establish a same-unit join. This tests recipient-conditioned recruitment during the action beyond a simple identical-opening account. It remains an association conditional on opening; matched movement alone does not identify valuation. All offered trials, nonopenings, latencies, classifications and animal/unit identities must also be preserved to expose selection and avoid treating cells as independent animals. No raw-data analysis is warranted from published curves alone.

The stronger primary causal contrast is `I×H` above, with actual delivery and informative perception/efficacy controls. It could reject an invariant-action alternative or undermine a claimed route when a precise, successfully targeted perturbation has no predicted effect over the relevant choice range. A null result would not prove absence of concern, given redundancy or compensation. An effect explained by altered discrimination or movement would revise the **evaluative** interpretation while retaining a causal role in information use or action. A selective effect satisfying the contract would support this route's contribution, not its uniqueness, acquisition from zero, universal necessity or ethical correctness.

The source studies therefore inform a supplied biological implementation premise in the foundation. They do not close the genesis of shared routing. The next useful connection is identifying a readout that carries independently understood recipient consequences into choice while preserving epistemic competence; another invented controller or another overlap statistic would not discharge that obligation.
