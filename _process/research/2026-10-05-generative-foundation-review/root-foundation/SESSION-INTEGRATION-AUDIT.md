## How to use the map in further work

Before claiming a new result, locate its family and state the existing theorem being extended or applied. Identify the changed hypothesis, new conclusion, new realization, correspondence evidence or counterexample. If none changes, classify the work as reuse, review or exposition. Keep imported audits separate from newly introduced declarations.

For a claim spanning branches, record the producer's actual output, the consumer's assumptions and the map preserving their meaning. A common vocabulary is insufficient. If a general theorem already applies directly, cite it; retain a concrete example only for its application or diagnostic value. Preserve historical failures and proof receipts.

No scientific claim, normative premise or theory-chain status changes in this consolidation. Therefore the independently maintained bilingual generativity-chain data and its pending source-review hashes are preserved. The work adds a navigation and reuse layer, not a new foundation or an exhaustive semantic-equivalence certification.
