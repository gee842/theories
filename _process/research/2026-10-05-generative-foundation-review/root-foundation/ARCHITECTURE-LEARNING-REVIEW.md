# Independent review of learning through locally formed target routes

6 October 2026. AI-authored internal review. **Status: accepted as a finite conditional bridge after independent executable checking.** The obligations below were recorded before the new protocol and computation; the final assessment follows them. The prior [architecture-formation review](ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-REVIEW.md) remains unchanged. This review owns this file and the new learning experiment's independent checker and receipt only.

The named bridge is **actual locally formed architecture → paid recipient-specific evidence → retained correction of recipient use → subsequent operation and maintenance**, with the acquired actor record preserved. The intended result is conditional causal acquisition through a material route, not an origin of motivation, intrinsic valence or justified concern.

## Prospective interface and counterexample obligations

1. Use the entire actual architecture-terminal distribution, retaining source exhaustion, actor and recipient balances, graph topology, live/dead status, guide inventory, remaining typed parts and failed preparation. Any extra original source or components must be declared before the history, remain unused through earlier stages and be tracked separately. No actor fuel is supplied merely because a selected endpoint would otherwise fail.
2. State how a retained graph is selected for the new task. An observer may not choose a favorable route using hidden efficacy or future outcomes. The choice rule, any required inspection and its costs remain explicit.
3. Traverse the existing route for each attempted recipient action. An identity filter cannot silently admit a probe or an evidence signal. Separate channels, a paid bypass or rewiring can be valid new mechanisms only when their construction, access, effects and costs are modeled.
4. Distinguish actual recipient receipt, paid accessible feedback, inferred target relation and retained record. A diagnostic trace is not automatically an observation available to the learner. A blocked action produces no intervention evidence; absence of a signal need not identify an ineffective command.
5. Preserve the actor's learned record while updating the recipient-specific record. New storage and observation/write machinery require components, commissioned functionality and payments. Partial construction or a write that cannot be funded retains its earlier costs and any uncommitted state.
6. Keep target identity and action direction attached to evidence. A recipient outcome must not be decoded as actor income or written over a record for another target. Positive receipt, known-present negative trial, source outage, channel loss and corrupt feedback have different inferential contracts.
7. Execute a later changed recipient command through the retained hardware, then account for subsequent maintenance. An acquired bit alone does not establish affordable use. Corrected use need not be retained indefinitely, and external surplus can retain an incorrect record.
8. Keep recipient support separate from actor resources and from a scalar observer score. A positive transfer can support the recipient's own operation without motivating the actor to finance another trial. Supplied operation schedules and feedback recipes must not be described as a derived desire to care.
9. Include meaningful boundaries: no live direct route, absent storage/reader/writer components, one-unit shortages at observation/write/use, unavailable recipient source, misleading feedback, immutable or shared-record alternatives, and no new learning. A smaller diagnostic set is acceptable if it isolates each advertised causal claim without inventing unsupported universal conclusions.

## Accepted construction and the actual explanatory advance

The [protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/learning/PROTOCOL.md) and [producer](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/learning/model.py) supply a compatible continuation of the actual local-assembly distribution. The new source is a separately declared inert forty-unit bank present from the original start. It does not enter the parent preparation's rates or restore actor fuel. Every old failed preparation retains that bank, its old source, recipient reserve and side parts. The complete primary energy account is 195 units.

The chosen site is always graph 1. A retained filtered graph can still perform an actor task, but cannot issue a recipient trial through its D node. The feedback attachment consumes an actual spare connector and fuel; it does not bypass that filter. Solvent histories with all connectors already bound therefore fail to acquire this interface. Dead graphs are not restored by the attachment.

Recoding costs two and reuses the parent's ten-value `q × last` carrier for eight persistent `(actor bit, recipient candidate, qualification)` states. The former last-action interpretation is explicitly retired. Preserving the actor means preserving its decoded bit and command through the actual coupler, not preserving the old numeric encoding. Construction modes and the immediate action/readout transaction are supplied functional machinery; the capacity count concerns the retained record, not every physical degree of freedom needed to operate it. Diagnostic counters and witness labels never select a command or change a record.

The new primary recipient efficacy reverses only at the continuation boundary. It was not reversed during the already verified parent history. The actor law stays the same. Initial recipient qualification is false: earlier help did not create a separately acquired certificate in this carrier.

The sequence is A0, R1, A1, R2. A0 obtains an actual private return where its retained route and funds permit. R1's learning transaction prepays observation and writing in escrow before work, then consumes both charges even for absent or unchanged feedback. Blocked or unaffordable work is unavailable evidence, not zero. Under the faithful channel, zero while unqualified changes a candidate; a positive receipt qualifies the issued command under stable target efficacy. A1 uses the preserved actor bit. R2 can execute the changed recipient candidate, whose actual positive receipt subsequently qualifies it. Both retained graphs then face their maintenance bills.

This ordering matters. R1's zero is not a sound identification when source outages are possible. R2 uses an exploratory candidate; its positive result comes afterward. The achieved result is **paid candidate revision → changed paid use → positive qualification → finite retention**. There is no later recipient action in this law using the newly qualified record, and no such extra action is required for the claim actually tested.

## Results and counterexamples that control their interpretation

The primary strict bridge has unconditional mass **11/405**, including all original failures. This matches the sum over the actual retained direct-site endpoints with enough remaining fuel and a spare connector. The intersection with the earlier motor-coupling-revision witness is **zero**: those particular old histories had already lost the selected graph. The new result therefore joins actual formed prediction and local assembly to recipient revision, but must not claim every previously demonstrated milestone in the same successful history.

For the indexed primary arm, expected new recipient captures and positive qualification both equal 11/405. Actor captures are 49/270, and selected-site retention has probability 11/135. Matched no-feedback has the same actor capture and site-retention totals but no recipient capture. Thus real feedback can improve the recipient without a private resource or retention advantage selecting that channel.

The private-zero proxy also obtains 11/405 recipient captures in the primary two-trial setting. Its first zero happens to flip the candidate correctly; its second zero then flips away from the successful command. It has no positive qualification or provenance-valid strict witness. Recipient outcome alone therefore fails to identify the learning channel. In the unchanged-recipient-law case, this proxy reduces expected captures from the faithful arm's 1/10 to 59/810.

Misleading feedback can set a qualification flag without a true positive outcome. Shared writes can corrupt the actor bit, reduce actor captures to 25/162, and remove the resources needed for later recipient use. The `shared_write` arm is a known-target writable-address interference intervention, not an experiment that supplies an incorrect identity cue; target cue calibration remains assumed.

The faithful update also has genuine counterregions. With unchanged recipient efficacy and availability three quarters, it yields 151/2160 expected captures, below matched no-feedback's 3/40: a source outage can make exploratory flipping spoil a correct prior. After a positive receipt followed by target reversal, preserving the qualified candidate leaves it stale. In that case an unqualified private-zero proxy obtains 67/810 captures versus the faithful arm's 1/18, without producing a valid positive qualification. A useful outcome, a warranted inference and a sound retained qualification are different properties.

Under the stated direct-site, correct-actor, stable-opposite, spare-connector and default-price conditions, inherited fuel six permits the paid first candidate change; eleven permits the complete strict continuation and both final holds. At eleven the final actor balance is zero despite completed finite maintenance. Smaller balances can yield a paid change that cannot be used; more fuel cannot substitute for a connector or overcome an identity filter. Observation or write price five removes the strict witness throughout the declared reachable comparison.

The protocol's threshold sentence initially omitted the already code-enforced direct-path hypothesis. This review requested the clarification after the first producer run. The freeze record preserves the earlier text hash and identifies the later scope clarification; it is not presented as a newly discovered numerical confirmation. The transition law did not change.

## Final boundary and remaining question

Accepted: a locally formed and retained response architecture can acquire paid recipient-specific evidence, revise and execute a candidate without overwriting actor correspondence, obtain a validating recipient receipt and retain the resulting record through the specified final boundary. Component, signal, memory and resource incompatibilities remain effective obstacles in the same process.

Still supplied: target cue and channel fidelity, the physical functions of the return wire and recoding machinery, transient transaction state, the fixed opportunity schedule, initial bank, source/efficacy laws, the candidate-update policy and finite maintenance interval. The study does not show why a faithful sensor, this learning policy or the willingness to fund recipient trials forms or is favored. It does not justify a recipient's standing, derive an evaluative objective, guarantee revision after target change, or establish indefinite renewal.

The result closes its declared acquisition/use/maintenance interface. Earlier evidence-to-reasons constructions remain valid at their own interfaces and need not be treated as undone. A subsequent account of justified evaluative influence must preserve the distinct questions: what actually helps this recipient, what the actor can know, what changes its motivation and what gives the recipient a warranted claim.

Final prose review accepts the [canonical integration](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md), experiment README and mathematical companion against this verification. They place the second candidate use before its positive qualification, retain the zero intersection with previous motor revision, state the resource tie and adverse regimes, and limit the eight-state claim to the persistent record. No further transition or wording correction was requested.
