# Closing the unified ethical account at its warranted scope

6 October 2026. AI-authored review under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **actual worthwhile transformation, available response and consequential formation of further possibilities**. This reviews the [current ethical account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md), including its preserved objections, and the [relational-goodness synthesis](RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md). It proposes finite explanatory closure without claiming the stronger research goal achieved.

## 1. The account that can be brought together

The strongest candidate begins with differentiated undergoing and activity within one unfolding. A severe, still-unwanted experience is not first an owner's possession awaiting export into another realm. A response can transform that same undergoing. The substantive identification is that **what this response would genuinely improve for the affected life can constitute a consideration favoring it before private adoption**. Availability locates the relevant opportunity; it does not manufacture the benefit. The claim concerns this real relation, not a detached benefit plus an unexplained ethical force.

Present realization and future propagation are complementary grounds, not mandatory successive gates. Final relief can matter when it enables nothing further. Elsewhere, a response also changes the capacities, relationships and circumstances through which further goods become possible. These consequences extend an already warranted practical relation; they cannot establish its first authority by causal repetition. Nor should one future good be counted repeatedly merely because several intermediate conditions support it.

Concern organizes sensitivity and response to these relations. Learning can improve their recognition, distort it or exploit it. Wisdom includes correcting means, purposes and practices through what they actually do, while respecting differentiated lives and legitimate limits. Some relationships create new goods and obligations; not every reason is merely recognized after already existing. This is one coherent account of realization, recognition, creation, response and correction. It need not add another moral authorizer at each stage or require goodness to reproduce indefinitely.

## 2. Why favor this constitutive account?

I favor its clean-case judgment: an otherwise appropriate opportunity to relieve an actual severe burden has a patient-directed point before the responder becomes a caregiver. An equally satisfying gesture that helps nobody lacks that point. This judgment concerns what favors an available choice, not merely what is permitted, rewarding or eventually chosen.

The explanation also preserves the target through change. The same concern can warrant another means, relinquishing a practice, or stopping after fulfillment. A changed report without changed suffering need not satisfy it; an actual improvement through changed understanding can. Its object is not perpetual intervention or reproduction of the helper's identity.

These correction patterns alone do not defeat the rival below: a chosen project can also track its beneficiary accurately. The stronger discriminating judgment is prospective: actual relief can justify **forming** concern before that project exists. The constitutive account explains this judgment through the same affected life that explains later correction. The restricted account must reject the first appraisal or identify another ground for it. That is a substantive difference in explanatory coverage, not a contradiction extracted from a neutral description. Our affirmative assessment supplies philosophical support; repeating it across documents adds no independent evidence.

## 3. The strongest surviving rival

Consider an account of genuinely formed practical centers. Each has authority over its positive purposes; promises, dependence, responsibility and shared practices can create further claims. Another's suffering can prohibit interference and fit compassionate attitudes without supplying every stranger a positive assistance reason. For this account, positive action-guidance is jointly constituted by the affected good **and** a legitimate practical connection such as an undertaking. Bare permitted causal access supplies no such connection. The restriction protects authorship and answerability, rather than merely reporting selfish motivation.

This rival preserves phenomenal adversity, accurate knowledge, effective relief, nondual formation and consequential feedback. Once concern is undertaken it can require correcting ineffective means. It permits charitable adoption while denying that the patient's benefit already morally justifies that adoption for every responder. It can likewise deny universal uptake of future benefits. Neither additional causal detail nor a longer beneficial chain forces it to concede the disputed initial scope.

The rival pays a substantive price: it withholds the pre-role patient-based justification defended above. Protecting someone from compulsory aid or constant scrutiny does not itself justify that withholding. Nevertheless, answerability itself can be its claimed protected interest. To defeat that stronger version requires assessing the legitimacy and reach of the exclusion; merely noting that no coercion occurs is insufficient. Its coherence is not proof of its truth, and its disagreement is not proof that ethical reality must be dual or irreducible.

## 4. Universal relevance must retain practical content

Special relationships can create obligations and genuine choice-specific exclusions, not only adjust weights. A valid merit procedure can exclude distress as the basis for selecting its winner even when the permitted result happens to relieve distress. A separately available way of implementing the already valid verdict can raise a different question. The universal account must preserve this difference between an outcome and an authorized determining basis.

Thus three conclusions remain distinct: a morally significant consideration; its warranted contribution to this particular choice; and categorical rational authority over every possible agent. None automatically supplies a decisive duty, permission to compel or inevitable motivation. Calling the first universal cannot silently establish the others.

Equally, a consideration barred from ever justifying any uncommitted response would not meet the intended practical minimum. The theory must defend real pre-adoption application somewhere and its claimed generalization. “Eligible” cannot mean that the favored reason survives; identify access, history, means and institutional conditions independently, then justify the relevant restrictions. Universal conditional scope is legitimate, but putting the disputed moral sufficiency into a law-class condition would not derive it from generativity.

## 5. The completion judgment

The elegant correction is to close the explanatory presentation around **actual good and its changing response relations**, rather than prolong a search for an extra stamp or guaranteed favorable sequel. The [original completion assessment](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) already accepts the conditional candidate, including concern and bounded wisdom, and compatible composition for the connections actually asserted. This review does not reopen that completion or require every mechanism to share one history. The stronger ethical-universality and premise-discharge objectives remain unfinished: the synthesis does not establish universal experience realization or the constitutive practical scope against the substantive rival. Completing this presentation cannot itself complete those stronger claims.

The account should change if apparent relief is masking or destruction of the affected continuity, if its good-making identification fails, or if an independently warranted jurisdiction genuinely excludes its claimed practical application. Exploitation and failed learning are compatible with an ethical reason's existence; they defeat claims of inevitable ethical development. Future empirical or formal work can investigate realization and consequential mechanisms. Only an argument bearing on the disputed practical constitution can close that particular necessity question. Neither endless bookkeeping nor declaring completion can substitute for it.
