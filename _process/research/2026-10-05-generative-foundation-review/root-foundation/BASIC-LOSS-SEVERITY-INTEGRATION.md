The bridge is **G0-grounded basic claims and warranted descriptions of impairment → an interpersonal comparative reason**. Neither G0 alone nor the existing indexed inclusion comparator supplies that bridge. BC2 declares its additional commitment rather than treating a profile of loss as already an all-things-considered verdict.

Most directly, [generative loss comparison](GENERATIVE-LOSS-COMPARISON-INTEGRATION.md) already establishes conditional transport through matched loss reduction for the same actual lives under its supplied preorder: 16 checked theorems and 23 audits at that study's scope. It does not justify moving protection between different lives merely because their profiles can be described in comparable terms. BC2 concerns that normative connection. ## 2. Affirmative argument / 肯定论证

The whole is the shared history in which differentiated living, impairment and response occur. It is not a further patient compensated whenever some participants benefit. Gravity is investigated through the depth and duration of lost basic activities, actual support during impairment, and reachable adequate continuations. Newly formed support may restore adequate living without restoring a former functional pattern. This is continuity with the acquired-support account, not a novel definition of restoration.

An intervention's actual protection must be distinguished from overall disadvantage. Otherwise one could allocate merely by a label describing who is worst off, without explaining what the action does; conversely, counting improvement alone could erase prior exclusion. BC2 does neither: it supports a restricted reason comparison after materially different histories, duties, burdens and rights have been assessed. The strongest baseline-sensitive alternative remains open outside that scope.

The additional positive commitment is that equal concern for basic living should respond to an independently warranted difference in preventable destruction when one-to-one protection is otherwise comparable. This is stronger than saying both claims exist. The draft went further and endorsed deterministic allocation. This round withdraws that inference: explaining why a difference matters does not yet explain why it defeats a chance claim. A lottery can acknowledge the gravity reason without accepting that stronger judgment.

The communication-support example is deliberately constructed: both losses are basic, reliable adequate protection is available for either person, initial deprivation is comparable, and one unprotected continuation includes substantially prolonged serious exclusion without effective replacement. Reverse temporal losses and differentiated obligations are explicitly absent. BC2 supplies a pro tanto reason favoring prevention of that continuation, while allocation still requires an account of fair chances. These conditions neither certify an actual service allocation nor show that all impairments are mutually comparable.

## 4. Substantive author counterreview / 作者反审

**The conclusion is hidden in “more serious.”** The described comparison concerns what living is lost, for how long, with what reachable support; it does not yet say who must receive the shared response. A rival can grant every description and the comparative reason, yet still defend a lottery. BC2 therefore does not carry the further force of a final allocation verdict. Choosing dimensions only after selecting a favored person invalidates its application.

**A worse condition should always take priority.** The strong alternative gives disadvantage ethical weight; it need not be caricatured as blind to benefits. This study leaves that comparison open where baseline disadvantage supplies a distinguishing reason. It rejects only treating a baseline label as a complete account of what a particular response protects. An inability to cure never establishes that care, relief or participation no longer matters.

**Long-term impairment matters less if a person adapts.** Actual adequate reorganization can change the loss profile, and must be assessed. Accommodation to deprivation, suppressed demands or an institution's declaration that someone has adapted does not by itself establish adequate continuation. Conversely, insistence on reproducing an old function can misdescribe a successfully transformed life. These are correspondence questions with normative significance, not automatic verdicts about adaptation.

**Irreversible deprivation must always dominate.** The proposed rule does not compare a minor irreversible change with overwhelming reversible suffering solely by reversibility. The communication case holds initial seriousness and support comparable and adds sustained serious exclusion. A unique basic loss on the apparently temporary side withdraws the verdict. No universal hierarchy among depth, duration and reversibility has been derived.

**Even a graver claim deserves only a higher chance.** This objection changed the draft. The argument establishes a reason favoring actual protection against the graver loss, but has not justified withdrawing every chance from the other position. The revised result does not choose a lottery formula either. Fair-chance constraints need a substantive account; they cannot be assumed away by calling the loss difference significant.

**Then one slightly graver loss defeats arbitrarily many lesser basic losses.** That conclusion is not supplied. The present explanation of BC2 concerns one-to-one comparisons; BC1 counts equivalent protection. No composition theorem or universal lexical rule is asserted. The two commitments identify the next joint problem rather than silently settling it. A complete framework must also explain how its judgments depend on actual feasible alternatives.

**New commitments simply patch every case.** Naming BC2 is not a justification. The common reason offered is responsiveness of shared basic protection to actual preventable destruction; the source comparison and rival test its reach and cost. This round declines further thresholds. The next advance must connect the principles, or show which must be narrowed, rather than indefinitely append unrelated verdicts.

## 5. Nondual and claim-scope audit / 非二元与范围审计

The analytical distinction between baseline, intervention and outcome preserves different roles within the same support history. It does not postulate an independent chooser confronting independently constituted patients. An individual's formed access and another's corresponding care activity may share one realization; describing each must not duplicate an additional life or injury. Where the intervention also changes other participants' basic conditions, the simple one-to-one scope is no longer the full description.

The unresolved connection is substantive normative composition, not a missing proof that evaluation belongs to a separate realm. Descriptive uncertainty can independently defeat a proposed application. The completed contribution is the defended gravity explanation and its pro tanto scope within BC2; universal necessity, full ordering, motivational convergence and reliable deployment are not claimed. This study creates no mathematical proof claim requiring a new Lean run. Any subsequent mathematical integration must be stated precisely and actually checked.
