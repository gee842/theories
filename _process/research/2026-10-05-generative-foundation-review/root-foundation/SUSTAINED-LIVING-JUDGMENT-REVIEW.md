## Why the normative connection works at its stated scope

`frontier_sustains` starts with an eligible failed alternative and obtains an eligible restoration retaining every basic protection held by that alternative. `protection_anchor` then uses the supplied lexicographic protection commitment to establish strict improvement. Consequently a frontier candidate cannot fail the chosen target. `collective_sustaining_requirement` separately requires a nonempty frontier. This avoids both hiding continuation in eligibility and deriving duty from an impossible menu.

The scope is deliberately sufficient, not exhaustive. It does not rank every genuine conflict. If restoring one basic claim removes another, the inclusion premise can fail. A stronger aggregate-output rival can accept the entire causal story while rejecting J2's priority. The candidate acknowledges this rather than redescribing the rival's productive success as unreal. G0 supplies favorable standing; J2 supplies the priority; J3/J4 constrain legitimate selection and assignment. No hidden scalar welfare weights appear. The two-unit spare-capacity thresholds in the example are clearly stipulated, not inferred human burden measurements.

The strongest remaining correspondence premise is `protect a claim ↔ target a`. It would be circular in application if protection meant only “whatever this model successfully outputs.” The candidate instead requires independently established living consequences, alternatives and contributor protection, and expressly denies that a reactor product proves living well. This is an identified empirical and substantive obligation, not an omitted normative axiom concealed by Lean.

A related application discipline is essential: `ds`, `need`, initial stocks and batch lists can vary by plan. A plan cannot evade a continuing claim by assigning it zero demand or an easier deadline. Variation must preserve the independently identified target, or represent a separately justified change such as ended demand, informed refusal or warranted closure. The candidate's independent-target and changed-purpose provisions support this discipline; the proof cannot enforce it from unconstrained functions alone.
