The present work advances **supported differentiated organization → cognitive discrimination and response → changed conditions of subsequent shared activity**. The earlier 21 theorems are credited. Exact controlled refinement and protocol composition remain completed conditional interfaces. ## Affirmative account

1. Part–whole organization is established through actual participation, arrangement and reciprocal support; it is not just containment or a statistical summary. Anatomical, functional and ecological relations can overlap without having the same boundaries.
2. Cognition is activity within this organization. Acquired distinctions and retained effects enter anticipation and response selection. Bodily and ecological changes affect those activities, and selected activity alters their later conditions. This leaves human deliberation, consciousness and particular neural implementations to their substantive explanations.
3. A description must preserve what its target depends on. Amounts alone can omit organization; present physiology alone can omit consequential history; a body-only boundary can omit shared support and other participants. These are specific omitted relationships, not refutations of the fuller relational theory.
4. Approximate correspondence must include changed action effects. The new finite-horizon result makes that dependency explicit; the threshold counterexample defeats an attempted inference from small estimation error to small response error without added conditions.
5. Four primary studies constrain cell-to-choice, anticipation, microbial-to-choice and activity-to-community connections. Their integration is an evidence-informed explanatory architecture, not a new empirical discovery or one quantitatively fitted trajectory.

## Substantive counterreview

This review is by the same authoring agent. It is not independent-agent verification.

**Does saying “one process” explain any mechanism?** No. The phrase sets the method; identified pathways, interventions, event laws and correspondence do the explanatory work. The diagram alone is not evidence. The chapter now identifies actual cellular signaling, acquired history and altered ecological conditions rather than treating monism as their derivation.

**Has the account merely renamed standard physiology and feedback control?** The general error theorem is an elementary stability estimate, not claimed mathematical novelty. A conventional mechanistic account retaining anatomy, neural history and ecological feedback can reproduce the proposed explanation. This is a successful comparator, not a rival rejected for using analytical distinctions. Comparative superiority remains unestablished. The contribution here is integration into the repository’s generative account and the explicit distinction between constitution, causal dependence and representational adequacy.

**Does the hierarchy wrongly make every body an organ of a single ecosystem?** The chapter separates anatomical inclusion from ecological participation, allows overlapping networks and retains distinct living lineages. No centralized ecosystem decision-maker or common reproductive interest is inferred. Coexistence, competition and loss remain possible within one unfolding.

**Are mind/body dualism and a homunculus returning through the policy symbol?** The formal state can include the relations realizing sensing, retention and selection. A policy is a supplied account of that activity in this theorem, not an extra entity standing outside it. The episode obtains its retained bit through a sensing transition. However, capacities and rules are still supplied; the new witness does not explain their origin. Existing formation studies are relevant interfaces, not claimed completed biological realizations.

**Does the tiny witness establish the requested biology?** No. The support count, Boolean sensor and scheduled events establish causal distinctions inside a declared model. They do not formalize an organ, energy balance or ecosystem renewal. Biological mechanisms are supported separately by primary experiments. The chapter labels this gap as incomplete representation/correspondence, not failure of actual biological organization.

**Can close states still choose differently?** Yes, the checked positive-δ counterexample does exactly that. Threshold margins provide a sufficient repair for a specified policy. If no margin or action-effect bound is warranted, the new horizon estimate cannot be used to certify the closed loop. Matching choices also does not establish that the choice is wise or good.

**Does favorable bodily support prove favorable whole generativity?** No. Another participant can lose availability, and the primary ecological example retains reduced diversity. Ethical appraisal must consider actual differentiated effects under the explicit normative route; neither mass balance, local success nor information accuracy supplies G0 or an undisclosed scalar weighting.

**Would evidence change the proposal?** If an alleged channel intervention has only nonspecific effects, its explanatory localization weakens. If a history-free physiological description predicts the stated interventions as well as the proposed retained-state description, the added cognitive variable has not earned necessity for that task. If changed activities do not change shared conditions in the tested regime, that return connection must be restricted. A well-matched conventional account succeeding is credited rather than excluded.
