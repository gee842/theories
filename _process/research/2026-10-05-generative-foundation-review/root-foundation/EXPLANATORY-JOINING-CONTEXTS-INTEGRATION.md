## 1. Corrected target and precise new bridge

The question is what different equally adequate accounts of the **same formation and continuation** must retain. It is not whether all possible complete worlds are isomorphic. Earlier `ExplanatoryUniqueness` already establishes uniqueness for exact/reduced presentations of a fixed answer family. `ExplanatoryComposition` already derives a composition factor for concatenation contexts and a supplied language L. `ExplanatoryJoining` and `ExplanatoryLocalUnits` already recover local boundaries from joining relations and check finite-history realizations. These are reused results, not new discoveries.

The new connection is **one partial joining law → its generated continuation questions → unavoidable relational distinctions and induced common operation → a checked all-context characterization for actual admitted histories**. There is no independent terminal truth predicate L in this construction. The outcome asked about is the possibility of realizing a finite arrangement through the same joining relation. The comparison is among accounts of that same activity, including a detailed full-history account and a smaller interface account.

## 2. How questions are formed and what remains supplied

A ternary relation `J x y z` records x and y joining with result z. It describes a declared fragment composition, not an ontologically external controller. A `Context F` contains one open position; it is built by adjoining another fragment on the left or right of an earlier context. This uses the same fragment sort F and the same J at every node. The mathematical hole marks where a comparison is made. It is not an independent observing subject.

`Realizes J c x z` carries an actual joining witness at every node of the finite arrangement, including each shared intermediate result. `Possible J c x` asks whether some such realization exists. A failed arrangement has no witness. We do not attach an arbitrary “good/successful” predicate after construction. `splice` composes contexts, and Lean proves that realizing a spliced arrangement is exactly realizing its inner part and continuing from **that same intermediate result**.

This semantics concerns possible realization according to J. It does not prove that every mathematical context is presently accessible, that a physical inquirer has generated or read it, or that the possibility was actually executed. No actor/environment ontology is introduced, but absence of such fields is not a proof of universal nondual constitution.

F and J remain mathematical inputs. In the concrete instantiation, F is restricted to admitted finite histories of a supplied process, rather than independently chosen points with inequality called generation. The existing proofs reconstruct local interface roles from joining under their stated laws. The present study does not derive the ultimate origin of the event repertoire or process law. Calling a local input immanent would not discharge that obligation.

## 3. Unavoidable distinctions before any minimality requirement

Define `Same J x y` by agreement on every generated joining context. This includes all finite nesting depths, not a fixed bound on tests. The two one-step contexts already ask whether x can join a fixed partner on either side. Lean proves that contextual agreement preserves joint feasibility when either or both compared fragments are replaced.

Let an account give exact answers to those possible-realization questions. If it merges x and y into one interpreted code, then all their contextual possibilities must agree. Therefore any actual context realizable with x and unrealizable with y forces different codes. The requirement follows from the independently specified arrangement and joining witnesses; it is not assumed as a structure-preservation field on the account.

An account may retain additional differences. `Reduced` is not part of `Exact` and is not imposed on all sufficient explanation. Every exact account has a unique functional matching from its **used** codes to the canonical context profile, and every canonical profile is represented. This common-factor correspondence is formed from the same fragment histories; it does not require a bijection between all positions of independent worlds or even between the ambient code types.

This is mathematical content recoverability. An arbitrary exact reading function is not yet an acquired or implemented explanation. The earlier [inquiry study](EXPLANATORY-INQUIRY-INTEGRATION.md) supplies a separately scoped example of formed records, calibration, correction and reuse in one history; its premises and completion status are reused, not reopened or generalized by assertion.

## 4. Why the common content carries an operation

`Compatible J R` requires related fragments to support matching left and right joins, with the resulting fragments still related, in both directions. It is stated directly in terms of activity, not a primitive answer table. Lean proves any such relation preserves every generated context, even when J has multiple outcomes.

If J is functional—one fixed pair has at most one composite—then contextual agreement itself is compatible. A realization determines the result from which the outer continuation proceeds; this is the key step in the converse. Hence `Same` is precisely the greatest joining-compatible relation in this regime. Reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity are also proved; the compatibility characterization does not start by assuming that the rival relation is an equivalence.

Now induce the code-level operation by actual joining witnesses. Its domain exactly recovers the possibility of joining the encoded fragments, without requiring reducedness. With functional J and a reduced exact account, its result is unique. Reusing the earlier relative matching theorem, two reduced exact accounts have a unique same-fragment-aligned correspondence on used codes that preserves and reflects this operation. No separately supplied homomorphism between accounts is assumed.

This is **unique common operational content for the declared task**, not a claim that the entire underlying history or every branch has been recovered. Functional composition is a stated condition for the converse and unique-output result; it is not a universal determinism axiom. The forward compatibility and exact-domain claims retain their broader scopes.

## 5. Joining whole histories closes the concrete correspondence

Use the earlier `Admitted P`: a finite fragment with an actual `Trace P` witness, including its beginning, event sequence and end. `traceJoin P` concatenates two admitted fragments only when their actual boundary matches. Each witness remains in the same supplied P. These histories can contain learning, records or resource changes when those are represented in P; this study does not append a second process to do the observing.

The joining of two **fixed histories** is functional because their concatenation is fixed. P itself may branch. Distinct outcomes are represented by distinct witnessed histories; no theorem assumes a unique future of the world.

The new all-context theorem proves:

`Same (traceJoin P) x y ↔ x.start = y.start ∧ x.finish = y.finish`.

The forward direction uses the already established idle-fragment probes. The reverse is new at this scope: equal interfaces form a joining-compatible relation, so they preserve **every nested joining arrangement**, not only the original local probes. Thus those probes are complete for this explicitly defined operational question. They are admitted histories; their mathematical availability does not establish unrestricted physical access to every boundary.

Every exact account of all such arrangements must retain these interfaces. A concrete interface account is exact and reduced; a full-history account is also exact but need not be reduced. The latter keeps which event occurred. In the inherited loop process, a false-labelled history and a true-labelled history are different yet agree in every joining arrangement. Lean checks both facts and checks that the full-history account is not reduced. This is a positive example of different descriptions retaining a common necessary structure, not another purported refutation of common-foundation uniqueness.

The earlier budget history, which can be used once and cannot self-join, remains a real compatibility failure at its original checked scope. If the process state omits an effect relevant to the claimed continuation, the theorem applies only to that representation; it does not make the omitted effect unreal or refute the fuller account.

## 10. Branches arise from the same joining and recover compatibility

`model J` turns a join into a continuation: from x, adjoining a on the left means `J a x z`, and adjoining it on the right means `J x a z`. Both x and a are fragments in the same J. Left/right name argument positions; the action type `Side × F` packages these roles without adding a second kind of substance. The atom vocabulary is empty: every nonlogical question is built from joining itself. The code does not infer the genesis of J or F merely from sharing a sort.

Branch questions include negation, conjunction and continuation. The important distinction is between two possible paths and one result that retains both possible paths. For example, “there is a result after joining a which can still join b and can still join c” contains one result witnessing the conjunction. It does not assert simultaneous execution of b and c, their probabilistic independence, or duplication of a destructive specimen. Nor is semantic negation a physically acquired absence certificate.

The checked adapter `bisimulation_iff_compatible` identifies matching these left/right continuations with the existing direct joining compatibility. `finite_outcomes_image_finite` passes the explicit fiber condition to the existing `ExplanatoryBranching.same_is_bisimulation`. Consequently, agreement on all branch questions is the largest joining-compatible relation. This is a reuse of the existing modal recovery proof, not a new proof of the general Hennessy–Milner theorem.

`holds_ask` translates every previous single-hole context into a branch question with a continuation. Hence branch agreement implies all-context agreement, with no finite-fiber premise needed for that direction. It does not follow that the former linear question family alone recovers all multivalued branching. The existing branching witness already established the danger for trace-only accounts; that completed result is credited at its original scope.

## 11. Recovering possible results and translating the questions themselves

Compatibility first replaces one input and then the other; the intermediate result correspondence composes. `join_result_matches` therefore matches any result of `J a b` by a branch-equivalent result of `J a' b'` whenever both inputs are branch equivalent. No preferred outcome is selected as a law of nature.

Let `Lift p J` be the already defined relation on codes witnessed by actual joins. With finite outcome fibers, exact branch answers and reduced codes, `operation_lifts` proves:

`Lift p J (encode x) (encode y) c ↔ ∃ z, J x y z ∧ encode z = c`.

The forward direction matters: **every actual input pair x,y with these codes** can realize the proposed output code. The construction cannot justify an outcome only by switching to incompatible representatives. It does not claim that an arbitrary prescribed native z with that code is an outcome; recovering each original ternary fact would require the stronger saturation condition studied earlier.

The existing exact/reduced correspondence theorem now gives a unique same-fragment-aligned correspondence between the used codes of two accounts, preserving all possible output classes. Redundant exact accounts still possess a canonical factor and are not required to be reduced. “Unique” here concerns that aligned correspondence; it is not uniqueness among all symmetries, vocabularies or independent world models.

`questions_survive_encoding` closes a further relation that should not be left outside the activity. It translates every partner mentioned in a question through **the same encode function**. Evaluating that question in the induced joining relation has exactly the original answer. This includes nested conjunction, negation and continuation. Thus the common representation internally supports these questions instead of relying on an untouched external supply of concrete partners. Mathematical evaluability still does not produce an apparatus that has acquired every answer.

## 12. Whole finite joining trees and a real multivalued witness

`Tree` combines fixed leaves using binary joining. `tree_lifts` proves that a code-level realization of the encoded tree exists with output c exactly when that same tree of the original specified fragments has a realization whose output encodes to c. The proof applies representative-wise `operation_lifts` at each internal node. A finite composition therefore cannot become possible solely by mixing representative witnesses at its successive levels.

This is a tree-shaped relational arrangement. Repeated equal leaves are repeated occurrences in that syntax; the theorem does not license copying one physical resource. It also does not establish shared-node diagrams, cycles, probabilities, actual scheduling or an infinitely completed execution. The repository's joint-realization and resource results retain their distinct scope and are not reopened or silently imported as consequences.

The finite witness uses **one J** with a shared root and a split root. Joining the shared root with `enter` produces a fragment retaining `left` and `right`; joining the split root produces either a left-only or a right-only fragment. Lean checks both individual two-step paths at both roots, and checks that only the shared root answers the shared-continuation question positively. This round does not separately claim an all-context equivalence proof for these two roots; its witness proves precisely these path and branch facts.

More strongly, the two split outcomes are branch distinguishable. Every exact account must distinguish them, and the canonical minimal account still has **two different possible output codes** for the same input codes. `canonical_operation_still_multivalued` rules out having secretly made the common relation functional. The same witness satisfies `operation_lifts`; genuine multivaluedness and the recovery conclusion coexist.
