## 1. Corrected target and precise new bridge

The question is what different equally adequate accounts of the **same formation and continuation** must retain. It is not whether all possible complete worlds are isomorphic. Earlier `ExplanatoryUniqueness` already establishes uniqueness for exact/reduced presentations of a fixed answer family. `ExplanatoryComposition` already derives a composition factor for concatenation contexts and a supplied language L. `ExplanatoryJoining` and `ExplanatoryLocalUnits` already recover local boundaries from joining relations and check finite-history realizations. These are reused results, not new discoveries.

The new connection is **one partial joining law → its generated continuation questions → unavoidable relational distinctions and induced common operation → a checked all-context characterization for actual admitted histories**. There is no independent terminal truth predicate L in this construction. The outcome asked about is the possibility of realizing a finite arrangement through the same joining relation. The comparison is among accounts of that same activity, including a detailed full-history account and a smaller interface account.

## 2. How questions are formed and what remains supplied

A ternary relation `J x y z` records x and y joining with result z. It describes a declared fragment composition, not an ontologically external controller. A `Context F` contains one open position; it is built by adjoining another fragment on the left or right of an earlier context. This uses the same fragment sort F and the same J at every node. The mathematical hole marks where a comparison is made. It is not an independent observing subject.

`Realizes J c x z` carries an actual joining witness at every node of the finite arrangement, including each shared intermediate result. `Possible J c x` asks whether some such realization exists. A failed arrangement has no witness. We do not attach an arbitrary “good/successful” predicate after construction. `splice` composes contexts, and Lean proves that realizing a spliced arrangement is exactly realizing its inner part and continuing from **that same intermediate result**.

This semantics concerns possible realization according to J. It does not prove that every mathematical context is presently accessible, that a physical inquirer has generated or read it, or that the possibility was actually executed. No actor/environment ontology is introduced, but absence of such fields is not a proof of universal nondual constitution.

F and J remain mathematical inputs. In the concrete instantiation, F is restricted to admitted finite histories of a supplied process, rather than independently chosen points with inequality called generation. The existing proofs reconstruct local interface roles from joining under their stated laws. The present study does not derive the ultimate origin of the event repertoire or process law. Calling a local input immanent would not discharge that obligation.

## 10. Branches arise from the same joining and recover compatibility

## 16. A common refinement without a shared fragment carrier

For each J, an `Occurrence J` consists of three fragments and an actual `J first second result` witness. `Position J` displays either a fragment or one of these witnessed joins. Entering a join through a role and leaving it through a role are relations derived from J. The common question vocabulary uses only these six role movements, Boolean conjunction/negation and continuation; it has no native fragment constants or atomic valuation.

This analytical distinction between a fragment and a witnessed joining does not posit two fundamental kinds. Each displayed join is constructed from the same fragments and J. Conversely, using one representation does not derive J's real interpretation. A path in this incidence graph is a relational inspection path, potentially traversing a result back to its formation, not automatically a physically executable temporal history.

`match_incident` reuses the existing modal recovery theorem. Given equal role-question answers at x and y, and finitely many joins incident to y at each role, an actual join incident to x is matched by **one** join incident to y whose three positions all have matching answers. This condition is `LocallyFinite`: finite joins around a fragment/role, not the preceding weaker finite-results condition for a fixed input pair.
