## 1. The connection being advanced

Use the completed accounts of [projection and risk](KNOWLEDGE-PROJECTION-AND-RISK.md), [inquiry priorities](INQUIRY-PRIORITIES-AND-G0.md), [warranted acquisition](WARRANTED-ACQUISITION-INTEGRATION.md), [reform and evidence](REFORM-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) and [practical judgment](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md). They are not reopened. The [collective-destruction result](COLLECTIVE-DESTRUCTION-INTEGRATION.md) already shows how locally attractive selection can damage shared support. This study addresses a different relation: changing the joint pattern of possible failures even while every component's individual failure rate improves.

The universal inference being tested is deliberately limited: does improving each differentiated capability's reliability suffice to improve the conditions of their joint continuation? The answer is negative under the declared local interpretation. This rules out that shortcut in a general alignment account; it does not refute G0, nonduality, or the value of continued generation.

## 2. One arrangement, differentiated vulnerabilities

Two routes sustain a specified continuing activity. They are roles within that activity, distinguished by what disruptions affect their support. They are not fundamentally independent entities. The modeled continuation needs at least one route; losing both prevents it in the modeled period. This does not mean the universe ends, or that the activity must be preserved indefinitely.

Ten equally weighted possible events are indexed 0–9. These are an explicitly supplied finite law, **not ten collected observations or a forecast inferred from their frequency**. In the original arrangement, events 0–1 disable the left route, and 2–3 disable the right. A reform assigns both to support disabled only by event 0. A revised arrangement retains the left vulnerability at 0 and gives the right a distinct vulnerability at 1.

| Arrangement | Left fails | Right fails | Both fail | At least one fails |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dispersed | 2/10 | 2/10 | 0/10 | 4/10 |
| Shared after reform | 1/10 | 1/10 | 1/10 | 1/10 |
| Revised support | 1/10 | 1/10 | 0/10 | 2/10 |

Every number follows from the same event law and route responses. The common vulnerability is a specified causal relation, not an ethical predicate arbitrarily flipped in a weak model. The first reform improves both marginal rates while making complete interruption possible. The revision removes that modeled joint failure without worsening either marginal rate. A positive intervention is therefore available in the supplied menu: the initial counterexample is not a stopping point.

The revision also increases the chance of *some* route failure compared with shared support. Calling it a universal improvement would erase this consequence. Whether losing just one route affects actual lives, creates repair burdens or impairs distinctive participation must be investigated. If the routes are interchangeable supports and the remaining route fully sustains every relevant realization without extra burdens, the joint improvement supplies a much clearer practical reason. If each has non-substitutable functions, the same mathematical table describes a different ethical case.

**名称上的多样性不等于不同的失效条件；同属一个过程也不等于总会一起失效。** The relevant description concerns actual vulnerability and support, not the number of boxes in a diagram.

## 3. Enough information without complete information

The generic table has four nonnegative integer weights: neither fails, left alone fails, right alone fails, and both fail. Let total weight be N, left failure weight L, right failure weight R, and joint failure weight B. Lean checks the exact characterization:

\[
\text{a compatible table exists}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
B\le L,\quad B\le R,\quad L+R\le N+B.
\]

For N > 0, division by N gives the familiar finite joint-probability bounds. The formal theorem is the integer statement above, including constructive attainment of every permitted integer B; it is not a new measure-theoretic result. The total and marginal premises must themselves be justified for an application.

This gives a positive route through incomplete awareness. A warranted upper bound on either marginal also bounds their joint failure. If that bound meets an independently justified practical limit, exact dependence need not be known to establish that particular numerical requirement. A checked example has positive joint failure weight and still meets its supplied bound. Neither zero risk nor full distributional identification is a general prerequisite.

At the same time, the shared and revised arrangements have identical marginal totals and different joint totals. No estimator receiving only those two totals can return the exact joint count for every supplied mode. That failure is repaired by retaining jointly indexed consequences: the actual pair of readings at event 0 distinguishes those modes. It is a fresh prospective distinction; recovering the pre-reform history is unnecessary. The proof does not guarantee when that event will occur or that a controlled test is affordable or permitted.

After a further reform, an old estimate may still be usable with a justified transfer bound. Lean checks a simple sufficient implication: if the new joint weight is at most the old weight plus an allowance, and that sum is within the budget, the new weight is within the budget. The drift premise is explicit. This is elementary bound composition, not a learned drift law. Fabricating the allowance, interpreting observed frequencies as exact probabilities, or treating a changed support relation as automatically stationary would invalidate the application.

## 4. How understanding and goals develop together

An operative success criterion such as “reduce each route's error rate” can initially track relevant gains. New joint evidence can reveal that it omits a condition of the very activity it was meant to support. Reflection can then revise both the organization and its evaluative description:

1. Retain the established individual improvement and the activities it benefits.
2. Add the shared failure relation and what becomes unavailable when it occurs.
3. Seek or form alternative support, rather than merely relabeling the previous report.
4. Assess the new arrangement's actual costs, permission, vulnerabilities and effects on distinct lives.
5. Continue under justified risk, retaining consequences that can reopen the judgment.

These are differentiated moments of one activity changing its conditions. The observer, operational goal and channels of correction are among those conditions, not an external arbiter added afterward. The model formalizes the local risk relations. Earlier acquisition and goal-revision results supply completed conceptual/mechanistic connections; this module does not simulate a full intelligence spontaneously discovering its own variables.

Under G0, continued living, understanding and further formation supply positive reasons. J1–J4 preserve actual losses, basic protections, bounded undertakings, authority and timely decision. Together they explain why joint interruption can matter without elevating a chosen institution, implementation or endless self-preservation into the final end. A goal may change from maximizing marginal reliability to sustaining a warranted range of participation and learning while limiting shared vulnerability. This is an argued application of explicit commitments, not a theorem that all intelligence adopts this revision.

## 5. Push the comparison before declaring a wall

**First repair: obtain the missing joint information.** Joint observations and mechanism investigation can improve the relevant estimate. Partial bounds can already suffice for some decisions. Statistical uncertainty is therefore not the present stopping reason.

**Second repair: change the support relation.** The revised arrangement supplies a concrete mathematical witness. Its actual formation, support costs and permission remain application premises. When such a repair is warranted and feasible, pursue it; do not manufacture a tradeoff by excluding it.

**Third repair: distinguish matched consequences from crossed basic risks.** If a change reduces the probability of a specified adverse realization while all other ethically relevant consequences, burdens and permissions really are preserved, there is a defensible comparative reason for it. Extending an actual-outcome comparison to probability is a normative step; this study offers the reason that reduced exposure to the same actual loss favors the already-affirmed living relation. It does not derive a universal expected-value rule from G0. The scope excludes hidden support costs, non-substitutable functions, excluded participants and changed obligations.

A stronger proposal—always minimize the chance of complete interruption—fails as a general decision rule. It could require unlimited present deprivation to preserve an optional undertaking. J2 already rejects that use of optional continuation. Conversely, always minimize the expected number of failed routes ignores cases where their shared absence removes recovery, inquiry or genuinely basic continuation. Counting alone does not settle those cases either.

**Fourth repair: retain J4's practical resolution.** Broaden feasible plans, examine responsibilities and urgency, and use legitimate bounded deliberation when a decision is due. This can justify proceeding without certifying a unique best act. A choice made under justified risk does not erase the losses that occur; support and repair duties persist where warranted.

These repairs advance the account, but do not provide a universal comparison when genuinely basic stakes cross. The particular unresolved case is a reduction in the chance of losing shared basic support that increases exposure to other basic losses, after affordable alternatives, relevant evidence, permissions and existing responsibilities have been considered. The threat must be to actual living conditions; continuation of the present organizational carrier is insufficient.

For clarity, Lean checks a diagnostic calculation on the original versus shared tables. Assign one unit per failed route and k units for losing both. At k = 1 the shared arrangement has smaller expected diagnostic loss; at k = 3 the dispersed arrangement does. The exact shared-preference condition is k < 2. **These weights are not our ethics.** They locate an extra judgment; they neither warrant a conversion rate nor prove that both scores satisfy J2 or the full theory. In particular, “set k very high” would conceal the question instead of resolving it.

**本轮新的墙：当共同基本接续与其他基本生活风险真实交叉时，什么理由能够证成风险之间的优先，而不偷偷加入兑换率或无限接续优先？** The current explicit commitments do not yet supply that substantive priority for every such case. The general conflict programme was already open; this continuation identifies its specific dependence-sensitive risk form and supplies a checked test case. This is an unresolved comparative justification within the account, not evidence of a contradiction, an axiom-free-proof demand, or a theorem forbidding continued action. A public additional commitment may resolve a specified case, but needs its own reasons and counterreview; calling it “generativity” is not sufficient.

## 10. A checked comparison with real costs

Consider two available support plans. Under a supplied law, favor-A produces basic-loss risks (10%, 40%) at A and B; favor-B produces (40%, 10%). The losses have matched gravity and duration, no simultaneous occurrence in this example, and no omitted difference in costs or permissions. The represented continuing activity and the two affected basic claims must actually fit that description. These are constructed assumptions, not field data or clinical advice.

Select favor-A on x out of n equally weighted selector outcomes, with n positive. When each plan retains its stipulated conditional risk law after selection, the exposure numerators, sharing denominator 10n, are:

\[
r_A=x+4(n-x),\qquad r_B=4x+(n-x).
\]

Lean checks the following general ticket-family results with n = 2k: total exposure is fixed; x = k gives both numerators 5k; a common bound on both is attainable exactly when it is at least 5k; and x = k is the unique allocation attaining this least bound. Probability interpretations require k > 0. This establishes the arithmetic of 25%/25%, including all allocations in this supplied family, not the ethical validity of minimizing the larger exposure in every context.

The explicit native law supplies ten danger events. Under favor-A, event 9 produces A's loss and events 5–8 produce B's; under favor-B, events 0–3 produce A's and event 4 produces B's. An equally weighted product of plan choice and danger has twenty cases, five losses for each position and none simultaneous. The probability of some loss stays 50%, as in either pure plan. This checks the relevant joint pattern instead of assuming that equal marginal totals preserve it.

Under RS's application conditions, balanced exposure has a fairness reason over an arbitrary pure choice. In the specified menu it uniquely minimizes the greater exposure. This can support selecting it when no further reason defeats it and legitimate authority is present. It does not claim that fairness independently determines every all-things-considered choice. The formal model deliberately does not encode an `EthicallyBest` flag.

**有损分担也可以有根据。** 原来获10%风险的一方提高到25%，原来承担40%的一方降到25%；损失没有消失，最大暴露却降低。它给出了某些基本风险交叉情形中的实质比较理由，并明确承担当中真实的增加。若形成了对双方都更好的新支持，原有菜单上的唯一最优不再有排他地位。

## 11. Fair plan frequencies do not establish fair risk

The same native responses expose a failure of implementation. Suppose the selected plan is favor-B on danger events 0–4 and favor-A on 5–9. Each plan is selected half the time, but each affected position now faces loss in five of ten cases: **50%/50%**. Some loss occurs every time. Equal plan frequencies therefore do not certify the product-law calculation or meet RS's no-added-harm condition.

This counterexample is not a demand for metaphysically independent agents and worlds. Selection and hazard are differentiated relations within the same unfolding. The relevant premise is their actual joint law: observing the selector's marginal frequencies leaves that relationship unidentified. A random-seeming procedure, institutional announcement or balanced selection log is insufficient. Future joint observations can correct the description; reliable design and conditional calibration can support prospective reliance without waiting for complete historical reconstruction.

Nor is independence itself a universal ethical goal. If a reliable, timely and permitted distinction reveals whether the danger event lies below 5, the reverse rule selects favor-A below 5 and favor-B otherwise. Lean checks that neither loss occurs at any of the ten events. Both plans still have equal marginal selection frequencies. Thus **the same apparent 50/50 rule can accompany 25%/25%, 50%/50%, or 0%/0% actual exposure**, depending on what selection is related to. The available distinction, its acquisition cost and its reliability remain explicit premises; the theorem does not conjure sensing into existence.

This is a concrete revision of a selection rule itself. Before the additional distinction is available, an equal mixture can have a fair-exposure reason. When informed selection becomes adequately supported and feasible, retaining the old mixture can impose avoidable loss. Continuing intelligence should revise its operative criterion from preserving the former procedure to realizing the underlying basic protection with the new evidence. The earlier lottery is not retrospectively unjustified solely because a better capability later forms; any actual losses and warranted responsibilities remain.

## 14. Push beyond symmetry: fairer exposure can add basic harm

The first extension is tested rather than promoted to a universal rule. Keep the two basic losses equally serious and the claims equal, but change what support can achieve: the two plans now produce (10%, 40%) and (80%, 10%). A genuine mixture choosing the first plan 70% of the time gives (31%, 31%). It lowers the largest exposure from 40% to 31%, but raises the probability of some basic loss from 50% to 62% in the native law. No simultaneous loss occurs, so this is an increase in the chance of an actual basic loss, not merely an analyst's changed score.

Four additional checked theorems establish the least common bound and its unique attaining allocation for every n = 10k ticket family, preserve the two pure-plan rates and the 31%/31% tradeoff, and connect the calculation to a one-hundred-case joint law. Here favor-A retains the earlier responses; under favor-B events 0–7 cause A's loss and event 8 causes B's. The full mixed law gives 31 losses at each position, zero joint losses and 62 cases with some loss. Probabilities, implementation and equal stakes remain supplied conditions.

This is a substantive counterexample to extending RS into “always minimize the largest basic risk.” The **arithmetic optimum is proved**, but RS's preserved-loss-profile premise fails. It cannot certify this new mixture as ethically preferred. Conversely, always choose the smaller expected number of basic losses would retain (10%, 40%) and leave the fairness concern unanswered. Neither proposal can be installed just by saying that both lives matter.

That rival is consistent. The argument for RSL is that justified fair treatment can have some resilience to a limited opposing reason, while actual protection remains a source of reasons for limiting the cost. ## 17. A local allowance cannot renew itself by changing the reference

The normative consequence is conditional on the existing warrant: if an argument supports only a bounded whole departure, describing each step as locally small cannot enlarge what it supports. ## 21. A checked acquisition and tracking law

Let rₜ be a real-valued aspect of the changing situation, yₜ an acquired reading concerning that aspect, and xₜ the retained estimate. These are projections of one history. They need not be statistically independent; actions and measurement can affect r and y. The supplied update is

\[
 x_{t+1}=q x_t+(1-q)y_t,\qquad 0\le q<1.
\]

This simple installed rule is a witness, not a necessary architecture of intelligence. It does not secretly read rₜ when updating. Its error eₜ=|xₜ−rₜ| satisfies the checked inequality

\[
 e_{t+1}\le q e_t+w_t,\qquad
 w_t=(1-q)|y_t-r_t|+|r_{t+1}-r_t|.
\]

The second term explicitly includes change between reading and subsequent use. A probability interpretation of r requires additional correspondence and range conditions. A single observed loss is not a precise reading of its probability. If y is itself a risk estimate, its calibration must support the asserted bound. This is a deterministic bound theorem, not a theorem of independent sampling, unbiased estimation, concentration or almost-sure convergence.

The same inequality supports three distinct results:

1. **Finite-prefix bound.** If wᵢ≤(1−q)B throughout the used prefix, then eₙ≤qⁿe₀+(1−qⁿ)B. The observation and drift bounds are needed only before n. No indefinitely funded execution is assumed for this conclusion.
2. **Bounded continued tracking.** If the inequality continues and B≥0, for every ε>0 the error eventually stays below B+ε. For supplied fixed reading error η and drift bound d, the checked premise is (1−q)η+d≤(1−q)B. This is an upper band, not a claim that the actual error must tend to B or cannot do better.
3. **Convergence of error.** If wₜ→0 and the update continues indefinitely, eₜ→0. The proof does not require rₜ itself to converge. It therefore supports improving understanding of a changing situation, rather than only learning a frozen initial condition. It proves a sufficient condition; a better predictive update may cope with variation that this simple rule cannot remove.

These bounds do not assume ethical improvement as a reward. q is a supplied update coefficient, and e is a diagnostic discrepancy with an independently specified projection. The support and opportunity to acquire readings remain application requirements. The existing paid-feedback result explicitly prevents identifying arbitrarily long funded prefixes with one already funded infinite history.

## 22. Retained uncertainty can become sufficient for action

\[
 E_{t+1}=qE_t+b_t.
\]

## 23. Convergence must survive the right failure tests

The new module retains three checked failures alongside its affirmative results:

- **Stable false belief.** Constant misleading readings of zero with initial estimate zero leave the estimate zero forever, while the actual quantity is one. Error remains one. Stability or self-agreement is not accuracy.
- **Continuing change with lag.** Even perfect readings, immediately adopted for the next step, leave error one for a quantity increasing by one each step. This is an unbounded real-valued condition example, not a probability law. It exhibits a lag of this update, not impossibility for a predictor with a warranted trend model.

Shrinking error alone therefore need not produce the conservative finite certificate at a boundary. The positive margin in §22 has real work to do. New capabilities may also change which relationships need representation. The scalar theorem applies to its stated projection; it does not cover every newly relevant loss or support relation. Changed descriptions need justified correspondence and newly supported error bounds. This is a limit of this representation, not evidence that generative development itself must stop. ## 27. From acquired records to a bound on the next effect

In a supplied local response family, a probe v changes a represented condition according to

\[
 r_1=r_0+\theta v+d_c.
\]

Here θ is the actual response coefficient during the calibration episode, d_c is its remaining disturbance, and the acquired readings are y₀ and y₁. The selected description records the measured response

\[
 \widehat\theta=(y_1-y_0)/v,\qquad v\ne0.
\]

Lean proves: if the two reading errors are bounded by η₀ and η₁ and |d_c|≤D_c, then

\[
 |\widehat\theta-\theta|\le(\eta_1+\eta_0+D_c)/|v|.
\]

This obtains the estimate from readings, rather than supplying the true coefficient to the predictor. Zero probe admits no response distinction in this one-step law. Exact readings and zero disturbance give the exact coefficient for any nonzero probe. Neither result is a universal theorem about experiment design: another channel or a different response law may supply other information.

A later action u has its own actual consequence r′=r+θu+d. The checked calibration-to-action theorem carries the preceding coefficient bound into a bound on the difference between the predicted increment θ̂u and the actual increment. **The same response coefficient at calibration and use is an explicit transfer premise.** A single probe does not establish that the law remains constant, excludes other causes or covers a new action regime. Probe support, permission, delay and actual disturbance/readout calibration also remain supplied. A large probe is not ethically warranted merely because the denominator can improve the coefficient bound.

The response-aware update now uses the predicted increment pₜ:

\[
 x_{t+1}=q x_t+(1-q)y_t+p_t,\qquad0\le q<1.
\]

The new error theorem replaces the whole change with the **unpredicted part**:

\[
 e_{t+1}\le q e_t+(1-q)|y_t-r_t|+
 |p_t-(r_{t+1}-r_t)|.
\]

No independence of commands, readings and condition is assumed. A command chosen from the retained estimate is included if the supplied actual law and bounds hold along that history. The theorem does not synthesize or approve that command policy.

If pₜ=θ̂ₜuₜ, the current coefficient error is at most ρ, command magnitude at most U and remaining disturbance at most D, Lean checks the further bound

\[
 e_{t+1}\le q e_t+(1-q)\eta+\rho U+D.
\]

A warranted bound on this residual feeds the **same retained-radius update** from §22. Its soundness is newly checked for the predictor. The unchanged prior convergence theorem is imported: a vanishing total residual supports convergence of the actual estimation error; accessible nonnegative vanishing residual bounds support convergence of the retained radius. The entire actual change need no longer vanish.

## 28. A continuing change can be predicted, while increasing action can defeat precision

The checked witness uses an exact unit probe taking a condition from 0 to 3. Its two readings produce θ̂=3. \]
