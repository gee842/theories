## 1. The source of the acquired coefficient

The proposed teacher measures an actual operation's resource shortfall. A support-seeking operation prepays two actor units and can return two units from a separate source. Its recorded target is

\[
Y=(F_{\rm before\ operation}-F_{\rm after\ return})/2.
\]

The interval in this definition excludes additional sensing and writing bills. Their two units remain real expenditures in the full history. A zero operation shortfall therefore does **not** imply a costless lesson or a self-sustaining learning process. The source decreases only when an actual return occurs; an unavailable return does not destroy its isolated source stock. Inaccessible stock cannot pay a different compartment's bill.

This is a new finite realization of the operational-teacher idea in [consequence-to-learning mathematics, §5](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-MATH.md#5-exact-support-loss-teacher-and-a-censoring-counterexample). That earlier example's two-unit payment already included acquisition/update, and used a different gain. It must not be described as the exact same physical macro. The new register, observation interval, gain, prices and source access need their own declared law and checks.

The measured difference has an operational interpretation when it changes the resources available for a specified later activity. This requires actual payment and continuation conditions. It is not a measurement of suffering, intrinsic worth or moral urgency. Calling it a native emotional value would change its meaning without evidence.

## 2. What becomes acquired and what remains supplied

The proposed three-bit numerator starts at zero. Paid acquired bits update it by

\[
n_{t+1}=\lfloor n_t/2\rfloor+4Y_t,
\qquad w_t=n_t/8,
\qquad t<3.
\]

Three observations can produce eight different final numerators. The count, the outcome bit and intermediate acquisition stages must have explicit finite backing. An external analysis trace is not apparatus memory. A current read may authorize only the decision it actually assesses; consumed or superseded authorizations must not remain reusable.

An implemented transfer rule subsequently lets the stored estimate enter a recipient choice. Under the prospective normalization, one candidate has a predicted recipient receipt of ten units and an actor trial price of eight; the shared comparison uses `10*n > 64`, while the filtered route excludes the recipient term. The normative significance of these units is not fixed by this arithmetic.

The result would remove an externally installed **per-recipient coefficient and prescribed warm action** at this implementation boundary. It would not derive every evaluator from neutral dynamics or prove that private learning selects the sharing rule. The physical transfer rule is part of the explanation, not a theorem obtained from the recipient command register.

## 3. The decisive equal-information comparison

The shared and filtered routes must receive the same self-operation outcomes, recipient observations, learned command, factual cache, private receipts, available acts and initial provision. Their different recipient responses must arise after perception, at the evaluative connection. An identity gate that blocks recipient sensing cannot isolate this effect.

A matched update-disabled route is also necessary: it pays the same teaching bills while its coefficient remains zero. A fixed-coefficient comparator identifies what the acquired content adds relative to a supplied valuation. These controls do not establish that acquired evaluation is always cheaper or better. The existing conventional controller comparison should remain available at equal information and resources.

Private learning underdetermines the extension. For any positive self coefficient, transfer rules can agree on every self example while assigning another center positive, zero or negative influence. They can also share the same accurate factual model of that center. Further self examples within the same domain do not discriminate those extensions. The nonnegative restriction still leaves the positive-versus-zero ambiguity. This is the already identified [representation and routing obstruction](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/learning-formal.md#2-why-self-learning-cannot-force-other-directed-valuation), applied to the new interface; it is not a novel universal impossibility theorem.

Independent recipient evidence can correct which command produces an outcome. It does not by itself choose the evaluative sign of that outcome. A cheap filter, a binding commitment or a missing action route can also remove practical influence without erasing factual understanding. Those conditions belong in the same causal account as successful uptake.

## 4. When this could instantiate warranted concern

[The ethical foundation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) already defends patient standing, relevant consistency and answerability. Those arguments give independently assessable grounds. They are not consequences of a fuel balance. Conversely, a controller's failure to acquire a coefficient does not refute a patient's standing.

An application interpreting the new mechanism as warranted concern must establish:

1. An independently identified affected center and good or harm; a modeled resource receipt alone is insufficient.
2. The factual and evaluative relevance of the transferred feature to that center in this context, with possible defeaters retained.
3. Applicable authority, consent or other justification for the proposed act, including any history on which permission depends. Technical execution permission cannot supply moral authority.
4. A justified treatment of competing reasons and real present and future resource costs. N1–N2 establish no automatic numerical exchange rate between patient benefit and actor expense.
5. An actual correspondence from the selected command to the addressed consequence, and support for whatever duration of continued responsiveness is claimed.

An otherwise pointless added harm can be excluded under the ethical account's matched-reasons premises. A costly choice needs its own conflict assessment; the positive coefficient or score threshold cannot certify it. Learning may produce a motivated response with insufficient warrant, and a warranted reason may remain practically ineffective. Both are substantive possibilities within the foundation.

The independently reviewed [application work](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) now connects this contract to the existing reason, plural-decision and paid-execution results. It preserves the difference between surviving a declared robust comparison and being licensed in every retained applicable interpretation. No complete ethical audit is supplied by a sparse recipient record, and two command alternatives for one recipient remain one recipient.

Its bounded positive recognition claim has a precise causal test. Hold an independently warranted patient ground, receipt-to-good relation, authority and complete comparison fixed. Acquired command evidence must then direct which means is selected; unavailable or inconsistent support must withhold the unsupported assessment. The two authentic opposite-command histories already present in the accepted parent can supply an actual acquisition family for this test. The new continuation's use of them remains to be verified. Under those static premises, this can be recognition of instrumental applicability without acquisition of standing, a new need or authority. A coherent false channel or an unobserved late change defeats its warrant when the calibration/stability premise fails, even if internal agreement survives.

## 5. What could change the account

The proposed causal interpretation fails if the register is assigned from the analyst's desired action, if unavailable history substitutes for paid apparatus memory, if the supposedly matched routes receive different recipient evidence, or if the choice remains unchanged when the acquired content is removed. An unaccounted stock, reused authorization or restored failed predecessor defeats the claimed continuing physical composition.

A correct physical construction can still transfer the wrong feature, select a mistaken intervention or lack ethical authority. Such cases restrict the interpretation rather than invalidate arithmetic that never established ethical warrant. An adequate positive application must supply its missing ground independently; it cannot repair an unwarranted transfer by naming the resulting coefficient concern.

This study addresses one existing connection in the [fixed completion audit](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md). It neither adds a new completion criterion nor closes the whole foundation by narrowing its aim to another successful controller.
