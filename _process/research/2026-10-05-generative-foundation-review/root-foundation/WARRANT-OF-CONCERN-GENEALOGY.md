# Actual undergoing and the warrant of concern: inherited results and the next claim

6 October 2026. AI-authored genealogy under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **actual adverse undergoing and an eligible transformation → a prospective reason for concern and response**. This repository-first review adds no primary evidence, experiment or theorem. It identifies the exact positive proposal that remains to be vindicated more strongly.

## 1. The strongest existing formulation

The [noninstrumental-ground argument](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN-GROUND.md) already defends this candidate:

> In the restricted domain of severe, still-unwanted experiential burden, an available, permissible transformation that preserves the affected being and genuinely relieves that burden has a contributory moral advantage because of that very improvement, before private adoption of helping as an end.

This retains the inherited intrinsic-enjoyment exclusion and distinguishes it from accepting a burden instrumentally for another benefit.

This inherited philosophical formulation is not Ren's verbatim statement or a theorem. It gives concern an object and correction condition: an intervention leaving the experience unchanged can fail its supposed point, whereas calming the observer does not settle what happened to the affected being. Its authority is the contested constitutive claim.

The proposal starts within differentiated activity. Undergoing, access, understanding and response need not be independently completed substances between which privately owned value must travel. Yet locating them in one unfolding does not erase their different histories or establish that every accessible transformation has moral favor.

## 4. The strongest inherited unclosed premise

The decisive claim is **nonexclusive practical scope**: why the specified improvement favors an eligible option available to a noncausing, uncommitted responder who has not privately adopted concern. That responder has present access, so the case is not absence of every relation. Whether this relation suffices is precisely disputed.

The strongest rival can preserve actual suffering, genuine relief, accurate understanding, fitting compassion, repair responsibilities and optional charity. It can also be nondual: practical jurisdiction forms within the same reality. It nevertheless denies pre-adoption favor outside an independently legitimate role. Its objection is not answered by proving that suffering persists, that the responder could help, or that no substantial owner exists.

Nor does the rival win by merely naming jurisdiction. It must explain why that formed boundary excludes this patient-directed advantage from practical justification. The constitutive proposal must explain why the advantage supplies such justification. A coherent predicate assignment settles neither account. Permission to decline, competing reasons and limits on demanding authority also must not be misreported as absence of every contributory reason.

## 5. What would count as a new advance

The promising next argument concerns what positively explains choosing an effective, permissible transformation over an otherwise matched empty intervention, before private concern. Its beneficiary, relevant difference and practical opportunity must be independently identified. The comparison and its affirmative judgment already exist; repeating them is not the new result.

A genuine advance would strengthen that defense, vindicate a restriction or correct a misidentified ground. Appraising whether a successful controlling goal should itself be abandoned extends scrutiny beyond its means. But stopping one's own exploitation may invoke responsibility for harm; that case alone cannot establish the noncausing stranger's baseline. This extension must retain actual option-specific justification, rather than an inert value elsewhere. Improved causal description supports inquiry without replacing its normative work.

This locates the unfinished explanation within generative activity. Neither permanent ethical irreducibility nor universal ethical necessity has been established.
