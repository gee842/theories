# Consequences can change the arrangements that generate later updates

6 October 2026. AI-authored source reconstruction under [Ren's nondual method and terminology correction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md#keep-foundational-language-at-the-right-level). The connection investigated is **acquired consequence or evidence → changed operative arrangement → changed subsequent sensing, learning or action**. The concern is how activity changes the conditions of later activity. Record formation, physical connection, generator invocation and response distinguish relations within that process. Payment is not their foundation; the resource tariffs below are features of the selected finite implementations.

The substantive finding is that the repository already contains an actual evidence-dependent replacement of the arrangement that generates candidate updates. A fixed declared transition law does not make that operative arrangement fixed. Conversely, forming and revising that arrangement does not establish the origin of every component or the warrant of its revision criterion. This reconstruction makes that distinction precise without adding another controller or proof wrapper.

It is a synthetic executable history, not a new empirical observation or a refinement of the adjacent Lean learner.

The history starts at the actually reached acquired-H3 checkpoint, with its inherited model, source and retained storage. It is an explicitly alternative continuation there, not a continuation of another branch's exhausted endpoint. The new generic head initially has **no bound method connection**. It has supplied typed ports and two candidate-generator recipes: G0 enumerates the inherited three-state model's phase/parity candidates; G1 also generates its declared four-state stutter alternative. The first destination proposal is uniform over those two ports. Neither hidden environment identity nor a desired final-model label enters the learner.

In the H4 environment with G0 proposed first, the concrete history is:

1. The inherited H3 generates the plan `aa`. Reset, two actions and a probe occur. The packet does not arrive. Its acquired receipt is zero, while its actual absence determines the resource effect.
2. Six native sensor readings form and are retained as `010001`, separated by five actions. They are subsequent observations from the same interface, not an externally supplied correct model.
3. An edge `(receipt-row 0, generator G0, bound)` forms. Reading this edge actually dispatches G0. Its candidates are generated and replayed against the acquired six-bit record; every candidate contradicts it.
4. The output is marked tried. The contradicted edge is removed; its trial storage is discarded. The remaining output is proposed, and edge `(0, G1, bound)` forms. Its generated family has a surviving four-state candidate.
5. The new edge is retained, while the successful trial result is deliberately discarded. A **later read of that edge** dispatches G1 again, regenerates the candidate family and replays the archive. The selected model and current cursor are then retained.
6. A subsequent native action and new sensor reading test the generated prediction. Expected and observed readings are both zero; the finite endpoint retains the result.

Thus the stored invocation sequence is `G0 trial → G1 trial → G1 later use`. The retired edge is `[0,0,1]`; the retained replacement is `[0,1,1]`. The edge is not merely an observer's name for having already selected H4. It must be read to determine which generator actually runs.

The trial executions are candidate-generation and replay operations. G0 and G1 do not each conduct a separate fresh experiment on the world: both are tested against the same previously acquired archive. Fresh native sensing resumes after the retained replacement regenerates its result. That narrower procedural enactment is already sufficient to change how later candidate knowledge is produced, without claiming comparative experimental learning absent from the code.

## Where the causal arrows occur in the source

The mechanism is more specific than saying that a higher-level selector has changed:

| Relation in the actual source | What it establishes |

| `propose_and_form` creates a three-field `edge` | The operative connection was absent. The generic constructor and available port meanings are already supplied. |

| `discard_trial`, followed by `invoke_and_acquire` and another `read_route` | Later acquisition must recreate its candidate family through the retained replacement. A cached accepted trial cannot substitute for the connection. |

The successful edge's source row is addressed by the receipt. Its destination is determined by actual proposal and compatibility testing. A packet failure does not by itself choose G1. The saved H3 transfer-blockage control also has receipt zero, but its archive is `100100` and G0 remains acceptable. It forms and later uses G0 even though the packet was lost. The different evidential roles are visible in the code: resource arrival changes available support; receipt selects a port row; sensor observations constrain the generator family.

The learner's `accepted_recipe`, `trials`, `retired_edges` and `invocations` do not provide a fallback for dispatch. The latter three are audit records. The source assigns `accepted_recipe` for reporting, but later use calls `read_route`, which inspects the actual edge. This permits a direct causal intervention: preserve the accepted trial and label, break the edge's bound marker, and later invocation stops.

## Contrasts that discriminate this account

The following are **previously executed and accepted controls**, extracted from the saved results rather than rerun this round.

- **Accepted edge broken:** the archive `010001`, rejected G0 trial and accepted G1 trial remain. `accepted_recipe=1` and the historical edge-retained flag remain. The actual edge becomes `[0,1,0]`; later traversal stops with `no_bound_method_edge`. No later generator invocation or forward use occurs. Historical acceptance does not bypass current access.
- **Formation clamped:** allocation occurs, but no bound connection forms. The same acquired archive remains; no generator is dispatched. Possessing observations is insufficient for this apparatus's later procedure when the operative path is absent.
- **Wrong source row:** edge `[1,0,1]` does not match current receipt row zero. Dispatch stops. This checks actual connection compatibility, not whether G0's output would have been correct in some other route.
- **First destination swapped:** the current edge dispatches G1 at the first trial and again at later use. It avoids G0 rejection and replacement. Dispatch therefore follows the connected destination, rather than an unchanged proposal label hidden elsewhere.
- **H3 with G1 proposed first:** G1 is accepted even though G0 would use fewer resources. The mechanism retains a compatible generator; it does not acquire a preference for the cheapest method.
- **Outside the admitted class:** the five-state control produces a compatible trial result but fails the later prediction. The delayed ten-state control passes the acquired archive and next reading, retains the model, and still has `predictively_equivalent=False`. Finite agreement and one successful use do not establish unrestricted correctness.

The last control is a genuine failure of a stronger generalization claim within the implemented interface. It is not an arbitrarily reassigned correctness label: the larger process has a later divergent sensor stream. Conversely, an edge clamp changes the operative configuration, so it cannot show two fully identical generative situations have different causal powers. Each intervention tests a specified relation.

This account would be revised if current source inspection found a direct dispatch fallback, if the survivor decision consulted external truth, if later use simply returned an earlier cached result, or if the accepted edge were already supplied at the starting boundary. Those are concrete failure conditions for the claimed acquisition-to-organization connection.

## Fixed law and revisable organization are different claims

The source describes one encompassing transition process. Its changing configuration includes the archive, current edge, tried outputs, candidate workspace, retained model and current cursor. Within that process, the arrangement that generates candidates can be absent, formed, contradicted, removed and replaced. After replacement, the same acquired record is processed through a different generator.

Accordingly, “the implementation has a fixed transition function” does **not** imply “the learner always applies the same operative generator” or “the organization cannot change its learning.” The replacement changes the actual generator, its candidate family, its work and the resulting acquired model. These are operational differences at the level the study explains. Moving them into a larger state description does not make them fictitious.

The converse inference also fails. A revisable operative generator does not mean that every norm of revision was acquired. Here the family is rejected when all generated candidates contradict the retained record; the comparison, finite acquisition schedule, trial discipline, two generator recipes and constructor are supplied. The study explains the formation and revision of their operative connection. It does not explain the origin of their entire organization merely by calling that organization endogenous.

Neither observation licenses a recursive demand for creation outside all conditions. The appropriate explanatory question is which named arrangement is formed or changed by which prior activity, and what relation a proposed abstraction preserves. The stronger no-assumptions objective remains open without denying already demonstrated internal revision.

## Other established mechanisms change different arrangements

Three neighboring histories clarify the distinction. They retain their separate ancestry and are not combined into the method-formation witness.

**Updater replacement.** [Learning-route revision](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/LEARNING-ROUTE-REVISION.md) consumes actual monitor states. Its [implementation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/revision/model.py) reads bound success/failure summary cells, arms replacement for a nonwriter or private-zero diagnosis, and changes `gamma` while retaining the acquired q and actor cell. Subsequent `revised(gamma, q, issued, captured, channel, ...)` uses the actual replacement to generate each new record. This changes the updater itself, not merely the answer already stored. But diagnosis authorizes a replacement attempt; a uniform proposal supplies one of four existing recipes. Evidence does not directly select TARGET as best. Under the stable primary conditions TARGET's receipts `011` support later copying and a productive retained child, while late change can instead retain a wrong proxy and wrong child. Configuration change and functional updater change are also separately counted, because CLOSED and FROZEN are behaviorally equivalent for the tested target.

**Evaluative conductance.** [Evaluative plasticity](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) obtains native and recipient consequences separately. In [its source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/plasticity/model.py), `settle_link` consumes those acquired shortfall states and forms or replaces a link. Assessment computes transmission from the actual links. The revision comparison keeps further observations, native n=5, factual means knowledge, replacement and affordability matched; the acquired sign change leads to abstention while the frozen-sign replacement selects service. This changes an operative influence relation. It neither establishes that the change is warranted nor derives the plasticity recipe from the paired history.

**Construction guides.** [Architecture formation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION.md) already lets productive activity form additional guides whose current abundance changes primitive bond rates during a later assembly. Its [source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/model.py) has a real consequence-to-construction path without requiring a symbolic correctness judgment: capture permits template copying; guide products change subsequent reaction rates. Matched inert copying leaves the rates unchanged. Copying the alternative guide can narrow rather than broaden routing. The product chemistry is supplied; no general favorable direction follows.

Taken together, these mechanisms demonstrate distinct ways activity changes what later activity can generate or transmit. They should not be flattened into one universal reward-update rule. Nor should the presence of a specified local law obscure which operative arrangements actually form and change under it.

## The exact unresolved component

For the central method history, the next origin obligation can now be named more precisely: **the generic organization that acquires the six-reading record, generates the available method candidates, tests compatibility and turns exhaustion of a candidate family into connection replacement**. That organization is present before the observed method edge forms. Its test schedule and criterion are not themselves revised by the record in this construction.

A further study should identify one of those components as its target and show a relevant formative history and later use. For example, changing what is subsequently sensed is different from regenerating alternative models from the same already acquired archive. This source establishes the latter. Claiming the former would require actual changed acquisition or access, its effect on the evidence available, and a contrast separating that effect from current stimulus or an externally chosen favorable query. No particular desired query or rejection rule is thereby prescribed.

The resulting procedure's **causal revision** and its **warranted revision** remain separate but related questions. The delayed ten-state example makes this practical: the implemented test actually selects and maintains a route that is wrong about later behavior outside its admitted class. A complete account must explain the conditions under which revision is reliable, how those conditions can themselves be investigated, and how conflicting consequences are preserved. Merely declaring the transition law fixed supplies no negative answer; merely declaring the operative edge learned supplies no positive guarantee.

The broader connection to concern remains bounded too. Revisable sensing or model generation does not itself establish the affected center's good, legitimate authority or the acceptability of changing its possibilities. None of these histories completes the full alignment or no-assumptions goal.
