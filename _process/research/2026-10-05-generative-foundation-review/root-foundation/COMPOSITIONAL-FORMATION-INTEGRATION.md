## 2. Positive explanation and nondual scope

Earlier activity can leave an interpretation available while the connections needed to carry it out are absent. Conversely, construction can produce a usable operation that passes the available examples yet fails elsewhere. These possibilities require different accounts of acquisition, construction and use. Neither a retained approval nor a correct abstract description substitutes for the relation through which the next output occurs.

The local model distinguishes reference demonstrations, retained samples, construction and response by their causal roles. The supplied reference law generates demonstrations; the samples constrain acceptance; placed connectors determine later composition. These are relationships within one stipulated history, not independent worlds of meaning and mechanism. The [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) requires preserving those connections while admitting what the representation omits.

One important consequence is that evidential adequacy depends on the operations that can actually be constructed. The earlier interpreter admitted forward and reversed ordering. The new construction also admits repeating either argument. When the two primitive effects coincide, a pair of atoms cannot distinguish these alternatives. An expression joining a repeated atom to a single atom can. The ambiguity concerns actual alternative operations and outputs; it is not created by freely toggling a predicate called understanding.

## 3. Formal correspondence and supplied premises

[CompositionalFormation.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/CompositionalFormation.lean) imports `CompositionalAcquisition`. The generator assembles the four two-connector routes: repeat the first argument, first then second, second then first, or repeat the second argument.

`fromLearner` carries the actual training records and remaining support into a construction frame with no installed program. This is an alternative implementation refinement of the earlier learner, not a theorem that every abstract code lacks an implementation. `place` consumes one declared part and one support unit; `inspect` tests all retained samples and consumes one unit; `discard` consumes one more and clears the attempted route without restoring parts. `search` tries generated routes in supplied order. `respond` uses the installed program and charges the earlier output-length convention.

`develop` reads lexical evidence from the original three-record prefix while checking candidate operations against the entire accumulated sample list. `encounter` appends a further actual reference demonstration under its declared charge. `reconsider` tests the present route again, discards it if rejected, and returns to construction. The demonstration is supplied through a reference interface; it is not secretly obtained by running the defective operation and calling its output correct.

The central general results have exact hypotheses:

- `formed_operation_correspondence`: the proper constructed route has the same output as the admitted reference law for **every finite expression**, proved by structural induction.
- `accepted_generated_program_transfers`: with distinct primitive effects, a generated two-connector route that fits the original faithful records transfers to every expression.
- `extra_probe_restores_transfer_for_generated_routes`: fitting those records **and the asymmetric demonstration** suffices for every expression across the generated route family, without requiring distinct primitive effects. This is an extensional result; internal order need not be identified when its effects coincide.
- `equal_primitive_effects_preserve_extent` and `indistinguishable_reversal_preserves_every_expression` explain that last distinction through the actual constant effect traces, rather than assuming all mechanisms must be uniquely identified.
- Accounting is preserved through placement, assembly, inspection, rejection, search, additional encounters and reconsideration. Placement also preserves the sum of remaining and used parts.

Concrete checked histories connect the general results to operation. With 18 initial support units and four parts, training consumes seven, a repeated-first route is built and rejected, a forward route is built and accepted, and an untrained expression executes using the four remaining units. Reversed reference order requires another failed proposal and therefore more support and parts. Missing parts, partial assembly, insufficient execution support and damage retain distinct failures.

With equal primitive effects, the first duplicate route fits the original records yet produces the wrong length on a novel expression. A fresh asymmetric demonstration exposes it. In the repaired history, 27 initial units and six parts support training, initial mistaken acceptance, the new encounter, rejection, a repeated failed proposal, successful reconstruction and a further untrained response. Seven units support initial acquisition, sixteen are spent before the final response, and four remain for that response. The search does not remember rejected proposals for optimization; its repeated construction and cost are preserved.

Without the additional encounter, the wrong operation remains installed. With the encounter but no spare construction parts, the discrepancy is recorded and the old route rejected, yet repair fails. These are substantive failures of those actual continuations. They do not refute cognition generally or imply that every real repair requires this stock convention.

## 5. Developmental and evolutionary evidence

These are primary-source comparisons, not experiments or data reanalyses by this project. No original publication bytes were imported. The formal connector apparatus is not claimed as the implementation of the participants' cognition.

**Kirby, Cornish and Smith (2008).** The published methods/results and accepted manuscript were inspected. Artificial languages passed through chains of adult learners. In the first experiment, increased learnability could accompany loss of distinctions. In the second, an experimenter-applied filter blocked ambiguous labels in training, and compositional structure developed. Thus transmission alone must not be credited with the supplied expressivity pressure. These results concern changes in culturally transmitted languages among already capable adults; they do not establish the biological origin of that capability. [Primary article](https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.0707835105); [accepted manuscript](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/8776820/cumulative_cultural.pdf). The [2017 correction](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1702129114) reports correction of supporting Table S7; that notice was inspected, but the table was not reanalyzed.

**Raviv, Meyer and Lev-Ari (2019; online 2018).** The primary abstract and publisher section summaries report compositional structure developing in groups of four communicating in alternating pairs, without generational turnover. Multiple partners and an expanding meaning space were included. This challenges the necessity of transmission to new generations for this outcome; it does not eliminate developmental or inherited contributions. Full-text retrieval failed, so detailed controls, effect estimates and the relative causal contributions of the two pressures are not independently assessed here. [Institute-hosted primary abstract](https://www.mpi.nl/publications/item2642791/compositional-structure-can-emerge-without-generational-transmission); [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010027718302464).

**Inference for the present theory.** Similar mature structure can arise through importantly different histories. The proposed generative explanation must identify which relations change learning opportunities, which change communication, and which change recurrence across successors. Calling all of these higher-order selection leaves that discriminating work undone. The first study also supplies a real adverse constraint: successful transmission can reduce expressed diversity. Neither replication nor learned regularity entails richer generativity or G0/J-based ethical justification.

## 6. Author counterreview

**Does this simply rename a supplied interpreter?** The recursive evaluator remains supplied, and the route is represented symbolically. The precise addition is an initially absent route whose placed connectors control later recursive output. This is a constructive interface result, not a material origin of all cognition. Conventional program synthesis, developmental learning and neural implementations receive their explanatory credit; no uniqueness or algorithmic superiority is claimed.

**Does more evidence prove arbitrary induction?** No. The theorem quantifies over the specified four-route family and uniform recursive law. A route or context-sensitive operation outside that family may fit the same records and fail elsewhere. The predecessor's nonuniform counterexample remains a result. Construction enlargement requires renewed evidence assessment; the new probe is not universally sufficient.

**Is reconstruction just an analyst's correct answer?** Candidate acceptance reads the accumulated samples, and execution reads the installed route. The checker does not insert the desired route. Nevertheless, faithful reference access, query choice, comparison, the search procedure and charges are supplied. Formation of those relationships requires other studies and explicit correspondences.

**Did the source comparison establish evolution of this cognitive mechanism?** No. Adult cultural tasks constrain routes to shared structure. They neither identify the formation of the Lean apparatus nor establish selection for a particular inherited psychological trait. The biological, developmental and cultural explanations must be joined in the particular domain claimed.

**Has a local success displaced the full objective?** The [coverage ledger](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/COGNITION-LEARNING-AND-EVOLUTION.md#1-the-full-target-and-its-completion-obligations) continues to include perception, broad inference, motivation, affect, social understanding and conscious experience. No change to their completion status follows from these proofs. This is author counterreview, not independent-agent acceptance.
