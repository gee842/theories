# Paid recipient learning on a formed graph

This note supplies one finite interface missing between [architecture formation](ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-MATH.md) and recipient-specific correction: the same actual graph and resource history must permit construction of an evidence path, a paid change of its response, further action, and retention. The [experiment](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/learning/README.md) preserves the complete parent distribution. A faithful feedback recipe, target addressing and memory recoding remain supplied. The conclusion is existence of a constrained mechanism, with exact failures, rather than a derivation of those premises or a universal favorable tendency.

## State, chemistry and inherited history

Let an actual architecture endpoint be

\[
x=(F,B,S,q,\ell,c,G_0,G_1,h_0,h_1,W,\ldots),
\]

where actor fuel and recipient reserve are distinct, \(S\) is the depleted old source, \(c\) the actual retained motor coupler, \(G_i\) explicit local graphs, \(h_i\) retention bits and \(W\) unbound connectors. Every original preparation failure and every commissioned endpoint is retained. A separate inert bank of four packets of size ten is declared before preparation and carried through the parent. Thus the original resource total is \(30+121+4+40=195\), with no actor refill at the interface.

The supplied finite clock is A0, R1, A1, R2, always using site 1. Its local graph must actually transmit the target cue. In particular, a recipient cue cannot traverse the actor-identity comparator. Construction after A0 consumes one actual connector and one fuel for a return wire, then two fuel for memory recoding. These are distinct payments: a consumed wire can remain without a completed recoding. Neither adds a recipient path around a blocked graph.

The original carrier has \(5\times2=10\) physical states. A supplied paid recoding uses eight states:

\[
q=2a+r\in\{0,1,2,3\},\qquad \ell=k\in\{0,1\}.
\]

Here \(a\) is the preserved actor bit, \(r\) a recipient candidate and \(k\) qualification. Initialize \(a=r=q_{\rm old}/4\) and \(k=0\), using the actual acquired actor record. The old meaning of \(\ell\) is retired. This is a capacity-compatible implementation, not a claim that physical states select their own interpretation for free. It makes no general minimal-memory claim for independently varying targets.

The motor reads

\[
u_A=a\oplus c,\qquad u_R=r\oplus c.
\]

Unrecoded histories retain the actual old command decoder. The target cue supplies consistent graph, read-address and write-address semantics. The stored indexed flag and physical wire must accompany any later continuation: packed \(q,\ell\) cannot be passed to the predecessor's old decoder. Earlier guide copies predate this learned relation and do not transmit it merely by persisting.

## Paid observation and the restricted soundness claim

After rent and sensing, a recipient trial with completed retrofit requires enough fuel for work plus observation/write escrow. In the primary law this is \(3+1+1\); the preceding rent and sensing cost another two. The transaction reserves observation and write before issuing work, then spends both irrespective of outcome. If admission fails, the earlier two payments remain spent. No unpaid persistent evidence buffer or favorable-outcome-only write appears.

Let \(V\) be hidden packet availability and \(\theta_R\) hidden recipient efficacy. The actual consequence is

\[
C_R=V\,\mathbf 1[u_R=\theta_R].
\]

Only this packet can enter the recipient reserve; it never enters actor fuel. A separate supplied physical harm law depletes the recipient by at most one on a wrong issued command. Correct-command outage causes spill without this extra harm. The one-unit recipient operation after capture is scheduled, not claimed impossible from its prior reserve.

The faithful paid update is

\[
(r',k')=
\begin{cases}
(u_R\oplus c,1),&C_R=1,\\
(1-r,0),&C_R=0,\ k=0,\\
(r,1),&C_R=0,\ k=1.
\end{cases}
\qquad a'=a.
\]

Absent route, work, wire or acquired feedback is \(\bot\), not \(C_R=0\). The matched no-feedback intervention consumes the same escrow and leaves the register unchanged. The issued action used by the update is supplied through the immediate motor/readout transaction, not recovered from a hidden efficacy label.

**Restricted positive-receipt lemma.** With faithful feedback, no false-positive capture, and unchanged coupler and recipient efficacy, a paid positive update makes all subsequent reads of that retained recipient bit issue the effective command, until a later write or physical loss. Indeed \(C_R=1\) entails \(u_R=\theta_R\), and \((u_R\oplus c)\oplus c=\theta_R\). The actor bit is invariant because recoding initially copies the acquired actor bit and every later primary write leaves \(a\) unchanged. This proof covers either actual coupler orientation; primary correct normal couplers are an outcome of parent history, not an eligibility filter.

The lemma does not justify zero-based identification. The observations \((u,C_R=0)\) are compatible both with a wrong command and with a correct command during outage. Flipping an unqualified candidate is therefore a fallible exploration policy. Qualification records a past positive correspondence; an unobserved later reversal can make it stale. The model's same-law outage and reversal cases instantiate these limits.

## Exact affordability and one complete microscopic word

Assume default prices, a direct recipient-transmitting site 1, active predictor, both graphs retained, one spare connector, an effective inherited actor command, and a stable recipient law opposite that actor command. With entry actor fuel \(F\), the successful boundaries are

\[
F\xrightarrow{A0}F+4\xrightarrow{\rm wire+recode}F+1
\xrightarrow{R1}F-6\xrightarrow{A1}F-2
\xrightarrow{R2}F-9\xrightarrow{\rm two\ holds}F-11.
\]

R1's command is wrong, so it cannot receive income; its real zero flips the candidate. A1 needs five units before its packet arrives, hence \(F-6\ge5\). At \(F=11\), every later admission and both holds are affordable. Thus a first paid candidate change requires \(F\ge6\), while this complete strict continuation has sharp threshold \(F\ge11\). These statements require the route and material conditions as well as the scalar balance. They are not universal entry thresholds for arbitrary graphs or records.

The stored witness uses the actual five-reaction preparation word with probability \(1/12\), an eight-exposure acquired-predictor/normal-coupler word with regime prior included in probability \(1/8\), and local assembly word probability \(4/45\). The second graph pays first for an unused D→P bond and then for R→P; its actual handoff is

\[
F=11,\quad B=22,\quad S=0,\quad W=1,\quad
G=(\mathrm{RP},\mathrm{DP+RP}),\quad h=(1,1).
\]

The inert bank remains 40. After the continuation, actor fuel is zero, recipient reserve 30, actor bit remains one, recipient bit is zero and qualified, and both graphs have been paid through the final boundary. The microscopic word has probability

\[
\frac1{12}\frac18\frac4{45}=\frac1{1080}.
\]

The entire commissioned preparation family reaching the same subsequent word gives \(1/405\). The full energy identity is

\[
195=4+1+68+10+37+2+31+10+1+1+0+30,
\]

in order: preparation, adapter, predictor/coupler spending and spill, architecture spending and recipient operations, learning spending and spill, new recipient operation, damage, final actor and recipient reserves. At each intermediate learning event,

\[
F+B+K+E_{\rm escrow}+E_{\rm spent}+E_{\rm spill}
 +E_{\rm patient\ operation}+E_{\rm damage}
 =F_{\rm entry}+B_{\rm entry}+40.
\]

The connector identity is \(W+\mathbf1[\text{wire}]=W_{\rm entry}\); original graph bonds, guides, other parts and historical costs are preserved. Actual solvent endpoints with no spare connector fail the retrofit. A nominal correct bit cannot erase either obstruction.

## Distributional result and what it identifies

Under the primary full-history distribution, paid recoding has mass \(47/810\), first candidate change \(31/810\), and strict continuation \(11/405=22/810\). The latter requires real zero evidence at R1, paid recipient change with actor record preserved, subsequent A1 capture, R2 real positive receipt and final site-1 hold. The exact conventional state-machine implementation has the same full terminal distribution.

Faithful and matched absent-feedback arms have identical private fuel, actor capture and retention outcomes, while recipient captures are respectively \(11/405\) and zero. Thus recipient benefit is physically produced without supplying a private selection advantage for channel fidelity. An actor-return proxy obtains the same primary capture count but not real-recipient-evidence provenance. In the same-law case it wrongly flips after recipient success, reducing captures from the faithful \(1/10\) to \(59/810\). Writing the recipient update through a shared actor address also breaks actor cash flow: primary recipient captures become zero and retained-site mass falls from \(11/135\) to \(22/405\).

At availability \(p\), the primary first command remains wrong, so the first zero is assured whether or not a packet was available. Conditional on a solvent successful route, the changed candidate captures at R2 with probability \(p\). Thus the actual primary strict mass is \((11/405)p\), including \(11/540\) at \(p=3/4\). By contrast, with same efficacy and outages, exploratory faithful updating obtains \(151/2160\) captures versus no-feedback \(3/40=162/2160\). Feedback plus this update policy is not uniformly beneficial.

After positive R1 receipt followed by a hidden efficacy reversal, the qualified response is preserved on R2 zero and fails: faithful captures are \(1/18\). The private-zero proxy happens to outperform it in that deliberately changing regime, with \(67/810\). This is a counterexample to unlimited qualification, not evidence that the proxy tracks recipient outcomes. High write or observation price, low original resources, absent connector stock and no formed architecture preserve zero strict-success regions. Filter-copy formation yields the smaller strict mass \(5/324\).

Crucially, the joint primary mass of an earlier **motor-coupler revision witness** and this strict learning witness is zero. The successful word contains acquired prediction, a formed coupler, locally constructed architecture and recipient correction, but not the earlier successful motor-revision event. Earlier revision histories have insufficient remaining resources. Separate existence witnesses cannot be concatenated into a funded full development path without this check.

## Remaining derivation

The generative connection advanced is actual retained organization → paid target-specific acquisition → finite candidate revision → differentiated further action and retention. The physical causal order and all costs are realized; the recipe that makes those operations possible is still a local premise. Production and selection of faithful channels, correct target addressing, improved inference under outages/reversals, transmission of the new relation through later construction, and a viable history that also includes earlier motor revision remain open. This finite result neither supplies a normative objective nor establishes universal concern or completion of the foundation.
