## The independently specified target

An exact question about a finite initial label set determines a partition: labels lie in the same block when they have the same answer. A finer partition asks for at least as much initial-label information. The common refinement, called the join in that information order, asks for both answers together. Labels remain attached to evolving candidates so that identifying present behavior is not substituted for answering the original question.

Attainability means that one admitted observation-history policy terminates with the correct block for every candidate. The relevant operations include their effects on future inquiry. A list of counterfactual tests is not already an executable joint procedure. The earlier [one-packet review](INQUIRY-CONTINUATION-REVIEW.md) establishes the precise native correspondence and the resource-sensitive obstruction.

Under the present permissive contract, any coarser question can be answered by executing a policy for a finer one and reporting the containing block. This supplies downward closure. The abstraction grants the required finite output projection and does not model a new physical implementation price for each answer label. A future concrete controller would have to account for that projection and its retention where relevant.

## The exact one-packet obstruction

At a fixed acquired phase, sensing parity and reset condition, the four causes A, B, C0 and C1 have native probe responses

| Probe | A | B | C0 | C1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Learned output 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Learned output 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

Each probe consumes one packet on either outcome. With one packet and the full initial belief, the only attainable partitions are the indiscrete question, `A | B,C0,C1`, and `B | A,C0,C1`. The two nontrivial questions are incomparable. Their common refinement `A | B | C0,C1` is not attainable. Thus the attainable family is downward closed but has no greatest element and is not closed under joins.

This conclusion is stronger than saying a particular algorithm happens to choose poorly: any first informative probe leaves, on its negative branch, initial alternatives that differ on the other question and cannot be separated afterward. It is also narrower than denying semantic identity or objective distinctions. The questions concern independently specified native response profiles; the obstruction arises from the packet-changing law, not arbitrarily attached interpretation labels.

With zero packets only the indiscrete partition is attainable. With two packets all five coarsenings of `A | B | C0,C1` are attainable, and that three-block partition is greatest. Any question separating C0 from C1 remains unattainable at every packet count under the unchanged native law. A result about the three native behaviors must not be presented as identification of four distinct hidden causes.

## Composition requires actual continuation conditions

A sufficient restoration theorem may execute a policy for one question, retain its answer, restore an appropriate initial testing configuration and execute a policy for a second. For this to establish attainability of their join, restoration must preserve the historical targets and retained answer; all steps must be executable for every remaining candidate; and enough support must remain for the complete composition. The second policy must not branch on hidden state or silently receive a fresh specimen.

Pairwise join closure then yields a greatest attainable partition for a finite initial label set by joining the finitely many attainable partitions. That is a conditional closure argument. Actual phase reset in the apparatus does not restore its source, so the restoration premise fails there. Reusable experiments and repeatable support cannot be inferred merely from separate availability of the two original procedures.

## What this means for relevance and criterion revision

No greatest attainable partition does not imply that relevance is arbitrary or must be supplied by an external meaning-giver. A particular continuing activity may depend on the A-question while another depends on the B-question. The result limits what can be jointly acquired in the declared history; it does not determine which dependence should govern that history or exclude its endogenous formation.

Demand timing must be fixed before a claim is tested. If the desired A- or B-question is announced before the packet is consumed, the appropriate probe answers it. If it is announced only after an irreversible first probe, a negative branch can leave the other question unanswerable. Where waiting for the demand while preserving the packet is available, that waiting policy avoids this obstruction. The local law contains no automatic urgency or passive loss that would justify inventing a duty to probe first.

The next constructive criterion claim would need a causal comparison preserving acquired evidence, current candidate configurations and further opportunities while changing the retained criterion actually read by the inquiry gate. Otherwise altered stopping may be explained solely by ordinary belief refinement or a moved cursor. A change in which questions the criterion requires would also need an independently traceable relation to the continuing activity. The acquired counterexample can establish a previously omitted dependence; it cannot, without additional correspondence, certify every unobserved future demand.

The hardest controls remain common wrong model families, miscalibrated consequence channels, stale contexts after a coupling changes, retrospective rewriting of the task, and a harmful revision that preserves the programme while damaging what justified it. The present finite partition result does not solve those controls by attaching a preferred score. It supplies one exact interface that any further construction must respect.
