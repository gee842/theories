His criticism of accomplished degradation invokes higher experience and comparison with what might have been (§IV); his positive comparison invokes intensity and God's aesthetic ordering (§§V, VII). These substantive commitments are not established here from generativity alone. The chapter supplies a contentful comparison, not authority for our ethical ground.
