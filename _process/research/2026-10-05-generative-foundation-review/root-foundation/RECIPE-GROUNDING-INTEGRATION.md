## The explanatory change

The [previous calibration continuation](CALIBRATION-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) installs a reader whose successful contact already returns the intended bit. Its missing origin is more specific than a general absence of learning: the project already has formed selectors, learned connections, method replacement and constructive inquiry synthesis. Those results cannot simply be spliced into this reader's history. They constrain what the next actual construction must accomplish.

A raw signal does not need to have the intended interpretation before interaction begins. Write the physically addressed binary condition as `x`, the raw transduction as `f`, and the reader's later decoding as `d`. The operational requirement is

\[
d(f(x))=x.
\]

This equation preserves a specified physical distinction. It does not assign an independent truth label to an otherwise unchanged mechanism, nor treat a representation and its target as fundamentally separate substances. The raw signal, retained connections, addressed condition and later use are differentiated relations within one interacting history. Their separation in this analysis preserves whether a changed signal still carries the distinction needed by that use; it omits the origin of the primitive transduction and construction laws.

Two contrasting reference encounters can acquire `f(0)` and `f(1)`. A reference is an actual imposed physical condition with an acquired record bound to that intervention, not an auditor's answer about the old load. The construction writes a connection from each acquired raw value to its imposed condition. If both records address the same raw value but require different outputs, this family cannot supply a decoder. If they address different values, the resulting connections implement the unique inverse on the entire binary domain.

The inverse is therefore selected by encountered constraints. It is not supplied as the successful member of the entrance state. The four possible complete two-row tables arise by independently choosing the two output assignments. This is completeness of the declared wiring representation and freely composable assignment grammar. The actual bound-reference schedule only constructs the inverse selected by its records or leaves partial/conflicted wiring; it does not enumerate or reach all four complete tables. Neither result claims that every possible physical reader has been generated. The generic row-writing machinery remains a lower-level construction premise.

This acquires **signal calibration**: how a raw contact output preserves its addressed binary input. It does not establish **weak-to-full calibration**: whether a weak test predicts the later full operation. The latter remains precisely the relationship that the downstream learner may find contradicted. Correctly decoding a failure is not repairing the failed operation or learning its untested alternative.

## What selection explains here

This is acquired selection and formation of operative wiring. It is not a new population-selection result or a proof that accurate readers necessarily out-reproduce inaccurate ones. A distinction can become operationally significant because the continuation depends on preserving it, without supplying a numerical reward for truth. But that explanatory relation does not itself create an affordable calibration encounter or a functioning constructor.

Identity and inverted raw transduction can both support the required use. Constant transduction cannot preserve both input conditions through any decoder. An intact reference history can be followed by a changed transducer or a differently bound target; the old acquired recipe can then be internally consistent and wrong in use. These are substantive failures of transfer conditions, not evidence that every acquired recipe is reliable or that agreement alone establishes its meaning.

The reference arrangement is part of the generative history represented by this extension. Calling its law local does not put it outside reality. Nevertheless, this model supplies its ability to impose the two states and preserve their binding. Replacing a prescribed correct reader with an acquired decoder reduces a specific functional dependency; it does not derive that reference arrangement from nothing.

## What would change this account

An acquired output used before its supporting write, a controller consulting a hidden outcome or transduction table, an uncharged necessary operation, or a purported continuation with incompatible ancestry would invalidate the particular constructive claim. A physical realization in which reference and target transduction differ would defeat its calibration transfer, even if the reference records themselves were correct. A broader constructor or informative interaction could overcome a limit proved only for the present grammar and interface.

This study advances the explanation of a particular functional recipe. It leaves the lower physical relations visible so that the next derivation can target them, rather than mistaking their relocation for elimination. The accepted conditional foundation retains its scope; the stronger no-assumptions foundation is not established by this construction.
