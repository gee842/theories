# Agreement as a formed practical relation: the ground and its limit

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored continuation under [Ren's instruction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-perspectival-agreement-ground-continuation.md), following [prospective control](PERSPECTIVAL-PROSPECTIVE-CONTROL-INTEGRATION.md). Philosophical argument, substantive counterreview and primary-source assessment; no new theorem or empirical intervention. The [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md#formation-participation-and-responsibility) governs the account.

**Result:** informed agreement can be understood as participation in settling a continuation and entrusting particular decisions, with a candidate significance beyond preventing material disappointment. That explanation supports fidelity and legitimate finality, but is narrowed by a source-informed counterreview: the value of having a commitment power, the validity of its particular exercise and the quality of the selected continuation are different questions. A valid exercise need not generate a new jointly valued outcome. The favorable character of participation alone also does not establish a directed obligation, a particular release power or permission to enforce. This round identifies those missing normative connections; it does not newly prove the preceding limit on an indefinite guild veto.

The universal inference tested is **whole qualitative realization → differentiated practical force**, including genuinely created reasons. Universal application would concern intelligences through the relations and capacities actually formed, rather than impose the same undertaking on every intelligence. Neither the universal ethical ground nor its full scope has been established.

## 1. What is inherited, and what this round attempts

[Elective concern §§2–4](ELECTIVE-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md#2-four-different-things-voluntary-choice-can-do) already permits choices to create new reasons and directed relationships. It also distinguishes justification for attributing a normative power from reducing its every exercise to a trigger for an antecedently preferred action. We inherit that result. Agreement is not newly admitted here, and the fact that one power exists does not establish that it controls every reason.

The [ratification round](PERSPECTIVAL-RATIFICATION-INTEGRATION.md) distinguished approval, authorization, release and common decision. The [prospective-control round](PERSPECTIVAL-PROSPECTIVE-CONTROL-INTEGRATION.md) retained real commitment after repayment, alongside its conditional objection to one indefinite control. Its unresolved point was the strongest rival's proposed power of informed self-binding.

The present attempt therefore asks **what makes the actual agreement a ground of practical authority**, rather than supplying another objectionable term. Saying that authority must answer to its grounds leaves that question unanswered until the grounds are specified and defended. Likewise, defining a valid agreement by exactly the verdict sought would hide the missing connection.

## 2. The positive candidate: settling and entrusting

Communication, shared activity and learning form capacities to understand, undertake, decline and rely. Within those relations, a communicated undertaking can change what is being done together: one contribution now answers a specification another participant helped settle; a decision formerly open is assigned to a particular role; later preparation proceeds through that arrangement. The positions are differentiated within this history, not independent owners exchanging a substance called authority.

The candidate identifies favorable significance in **making participants' actual contribution to determining a continuation effective within that continuation**. Keeping a warranted undertaking can realize that relation even when an unchosen alternative would produce an equally useful object. Its merit concerns the manner in which people live and act together, including what each has entrusted to the others. It need not await a subsequent product or an additional increase in options.

Here “entrusting” initially describes the understood undertaking and the way subsequent conduct is organized around a specified decision. It does not silently stipulate that a moral right was successfully transferred. That further interpretation is what the argument must justify.

The positive defense is that the undertaking can make another participant's say matter in the course they are living, rather than merely make the course predictable. A substitute outcome can preserve the object produced while failing to realize the particular participation that was undertaken. This supplies a candidate reason for fidelity with a concrete relational object. It is a substantive identification of favorable quality, not a deduction from causal coordination or an assertion that every shared determination is good. Coordination in cruelty remains a countercase to that latter assertion.

Consider collaborators who choose one of two permissible designs. A genuinely available fallback protects the identified material resources and livelihood if that design is abandoned. A collaborator can privately switch to the equally useful alternative. Our affirmative judgment retains a reason to follow the chosen design or seek reconsideration: the fallback answers the specified practical losses, but does not by itself realize the shared determination. This does not assume that all welfare, preference satisfaction or ethical significance is matched. The changed relation is precisely what the argument asks us to assess.

A consequential account that includes the actual historical relation can accept this judgment. The test challenges reduction to the protected material outcomes, not every account using the word “consequences.” Nor must there always be a common substantive aim. One participant can entrust a specified choice to another precisely because their preferences differ. A satisfactory account should explain that relationship as well as harmonious joint projects.

### The capacity, its exercise and the resulting continuation

The integrated source challenge materially narrows this explanation. It is one route to supporting fidelity, not a necessary condition that every valid promise produce a worthwhile shared output. Distinguish the value of **being able to undertake**, what makes **this exercise** change a claim or permission, and the quality of **the continuation selected**. A defended power may permit a participant to bind themselves to a contribution they do not independently favor. Its operation need not be justified afresh by proving that this performance yields a new common good.

This changes the proposed generative account's explanatory structure. Explaining why a capacity to make commitments can matter does not yet explain who possesses which power or what counts as exercising it. Conversely, establishing a valid exercise would not show that the selected continuation improves the whole or that its enforcement is permissible. Favorable quality, normative effect and overall ethical appraisal must be connected through a defended account, not treated as interchangeable measures of success.

## 3. The attempted bridge to directed authority

The account becomes more demanding when it says someone is **owed** performance. Three questions expose the attempted connection:

| Proposed ground | What it would explain | Further ethical work required |
|---|---|---|
| Particular participants actually settled how this continuation would proceed. | Why faithful continuation can have a significance the unchosen alternative lacks. | Why that significance creates an obligation rather than only some favor for performance. |
| The exchange entrusted a specified decision to a named participant or shared procedure. | Why its practical address may be differentiated rather than a general reason to benefit anyone. | Why the entrusting has moral force, which decisions it covers, and who can release or alter it. |
| Fulfillment, release or a final determination changes the actual relation. | Why a later practical requirement can change without treating every earlier judgment as mistaken. | Which changes discharge a ground, which preserve distinct claims, and why the claimed procedure is authoritative. |

The affirmative proposal is that these belong to one relation: a participant's contribution settles a matter, the other undertaking makes that settlement effective, and disregarding it frustrates this particular entrusted role. That can explain why a request from this participant matters differently from an unrelated observer's equally favorable wish. It also suggests why requesting release acknowledges a relation that private regret alone does not undo.

**The attempted derivation nevertheless stops short.** Demonstrating that this relation is worthwhile does not by itself establish an obligation owed to that participant, that participant's exclusive release power, or the exclusion of otherwise relevant considerations. Other people can have interests in the same good; several participants may share a release power; and a valuable relation need not be mandatory to continue. The proposed identification of entrusted participation with those particular claims is still a substantive normative thesis. The missing explanation has two parts: why this power and its allocation are justified, and how this particular undertaking exercises it. The second must not be replaced by a demand for a fresh beneficial outcome from each exercise.

This is not a claim that directed obligation must be inferred from a prior detached value score. Its relational significance and practical authority could receive a joint constitutive explanation. The present account has offered part of such an explanation, but has not justified the entire connection merely by naming it. Nor does the limit show that a separate ethical realm or an irreducible arbitrary axiom is necessary. It identifies what this attempt has not established.

## 4. Enduring commitment can realize the proposed good

A theory valuing participation cannot automatically require maximum later choice. Participants may decide that a question should be settled, that a coordinator should have discretion, or that an undertaking should endure despite later regret. Those arrangements can make their participation effective through time. A meaningful choice can include accepting constraints on subsequent choice.

The [correspondence review](PERSPECTIVAL-AGREEMENT-GROUND-CORRESPONDENCE.md) tests a lifelong undertaking concerning entrusted private letters. At its declared conditional scope, the undertaking is warranted, relevant circumstances do not release or defeat it, and a later desire to publish does not annul it. This is a control against unrestricted revocability, not a proof that privacy first acquires significance through a promise: privacy and other historical grounds can coexist. Indefinite duration and recovered costs alone do not show that the promise has lost its force.

Similarly, a navigation delegation can give one participant authority to settle choices others later dislike; a final schedule can close ordinary reconsideration. These are genuine counterpressures to a theory equating goodness with equal ongoing control, continual review or larger option sets. A perpetual demand for endorsement could defeat the very settled relation the candidate values.

Keeping the undertaking, enforcing it by a particular means, deciding a dispute finally and determining future terms remain different questions. One agreement can establish several of these relations, but its establishment of one does not establish all the others. A lifetime confidentiality duty does not by itself authorize surveillance of unrelated correspondence. That example concerns a limited undertaking; it does not refute an expressly proposed broader grant merely by interpretation.

## 5. Finality, scope and the enduring-allegiance rival

The useful distinction is between a **settled decision with a justified history** and a claim that an authority's present assertion is sufficient to establish its scope. The former can rule out repeated appeals and survive present disagreement. Normative grounding does not entail an indefinitely available review procedure or a duty to answer every challenge anew.

In a scheduling mandate, identifying what was entrusted can exclude unrelated commands. Here an account of content supplies a real limit. In the original guild case, however, broad ordinary-use control was explicitly proposed and accepted. Its critic cannot obtain the desired limit by pretending only a narrow schedule was agreed. The rival can say enduring submission itself was the selected relation.

That rival uses the candidate's own positive ground: informed participants made a future arrangement binding, including the chosen loss of future discretion. Calling the arrangement dependent, firm, enduring or costly does not answer it. Nor is a defended power of consent circular simply because its exercise creates a new claim. Our earlier judgment against the specified guild veto therefore retains its substantive assessment of the controlled work, actual allegiance and extent of interference; the present account does not derive it afresh.

“Authority is answerable to its grounds” consequently has three scopes. As a restatement that warranted authority is warranted, it is analytically uninformative. As a requirement to identify the actual relation and what it explains, it improves the argument. As a claim forbidding some explicitly accepted power, it needs the ethical limit being sought. A description of a narrower valid mandate cannot perform that final step for a broader disputed one.

## 6. Integration and implications for generative trajectories

The [substantive review](PERSPECTIVAL-AGREEMENT-GROUND-REVIEW.md) supplied the fallback comparison and challenged the passage from favorable participation to directed duty. It also preserved deliberately chosen delegation and enduring allegiance as genuine rivals. Its final pass narrows the positive explanation to allow a valid undertaking without a shared valued outcome, and separates justification for having a power from its exercise. The [correspondence review](PERSPECTIVAL-AGREEMENT-GROUND-CORRESPONDENCE.md) separated undertaking, nonrevocation, finality, enforcement and jurisdiction, and exposed the empty reading of answerability. These corrections are incorporated above.

The [primary-source assessment](PERSPECTIVAL-AGREEMENT-GROUND-SOURCES.md) examines Shiffrin's [2008 account of promising](https://doi.org/10.1215/00318108-2008-014) and Raz's [2006 authority account](https://scholarship.law.columbia.edu/faculty_scholarship/752/). The first supplies the challenge to a shared-outcome condition, with autonomy rights remaining substantive premises. The second requires grounds for the claimed scope of delegated authority. Neither supplies universal generative grounding. The source reviewer inspected selected Shiffrin pages from an author-hosted scan and only indexed original passages of Raz; the full Raz article was not retrieved. This integration relies on that bounded assessment and claims no additional complete reading. These are internal AI research contributions and primary philosophical comparisons, not external peer review or empirical confirmation.

For Ren's evaluative-field proposal, this supports a further distinction. A perspective can register another participant's judgment as a preference, treat it as evidence, or recognize that it settles a particular matter. Those are different practical relationships even if a numerical weighting happens to produce the same action. No claim that scalar representations are mathematically impossible is made; an encoding must preserve the distinctions its interpretation requires.

The karma-like continuation can therefore include changes in **who may settle what**, alongside skill, appreciation, material support and effective power. A changed approval level need not change a binding commitment; fulfillment or a warranted release can. A justified final decision can close an option; an unjustified claim to broader jurisdiction can also close it. The number of openings or closures does not determine the direction. Nor is creating a new obligation by itself ethical improvement. Actual efficacy, justified practical standing and the quality of the continuation are connected developments with different explanatory requirements.

This round's new supporting result is a revision and sufficiency audit of the proposed settlement ground. It offers defeasible support for fidelity beyond the specified material losses, withdraws that route as a necessary shared-output test for every valid agreement, preserves legitimate finality and isolates the extra normative connection required for a directed claim. It does not complete universal grounding, establish an attraction basin or add to the 25 previously checked representation results. The wider universal run's [recorded impasse](UNIVERSAL-METAETHICS-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md#13-latest-constitutive-tests-and-the-current-research-impasse) is not reversed by this local clarification.

The next substantive obligation in this branch is now precise: **justify or revise the connection between a particular entrusted decision and a directed claim, including its release and enforcement scope**. A successful account may jointly explain the relation's ethical quality and practical force. Repeating that agreement creates reasons, or adding another case where existing terms were exceeded, would not discharge this obligation. The broader universal programme and other authorized studies retain their priorities.
