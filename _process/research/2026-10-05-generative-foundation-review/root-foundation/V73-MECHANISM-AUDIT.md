# V7.3 mechanisms in the generative foundation

6 October 2026. AI-authored documentation and source audit, following Ren's request to integrate the six mechanisms. This note supports the canonical [generative selection chapter](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md). It adds no scientific run, new theorem, empirical measurement or independent replication.

**Named connection:** formed understanding and concern → causal influence under selection that can change effective objectives, evidence and corrective authority. The common root is activity changing the conditions of further activity; these mechanisms specify different ways that connection can preserve or defeat justified responsiveness. They do not make persistence an ethical criterion.

The source is [V7.3 THEORY](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/THEORY.md), with proofs and limits in its [SUPPLEMENT](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/SUPPLEMENT.md), and historical validation status in [CLAIMS](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/CLAIMS.md) and [RELEASE-REVIEW](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/RELEASE-REVIEW.md). This audit reads those mechanisms at their declared scope; it does not recertify every proof or contemporary observation in the manuscript.

## 1. Shared dependence need not influence relative selection

**Source result:** Definition 3.2, Lemmas 3.3 and 3.11, and the [S3 proofs](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/03_S3.md). A relative selection rule satisfies

\[
\mathcal F(p,\pi+g\mathbf1)=\mathcal F(p,\pi).
\]

Consequently, a host payoff entering every carrier's payoff additively and identically does not change its share trajectory. In the supplied load model,

\[
\pi_k=\phi_k+(\nu-\tau_{\mathrm{eff}})e_k+G(V),
\quad
\dot E=s\big[(\nu-\tau_{\mathrm{eff}})\operatorname{Var}_p(e)
+\operatorname{Cov}_p(e,\phi)\big]
\]

under constant-speed replicator selection and fixed loads. The shared term cancels even when the host deteriorates. A costly trait benefiting everyone equally has no positive selection advantage from that benefit alone among otherwise identical carriers; biased transmission or changed differential consequences can sustain it.

**Supplied premises and limits.** Relative invariance is a property of the selection law, not of every process. Equal additive exposure is essential to the cancellation. Unequal exposure, other advantages correlated with restraint, deliberate transmission and changes in liability can alter the result. Payoff monotonicity determines a sign; a cumulative rate bound is needed for the stronger takeover conclusion. The S3 counterexample has an unbounded integrated advantage but a noncumulative response too weak to cause takeover. Ethical standing is not inferred from any carrier's contribution to the host.

**Current overlap and integration.** [Selection and renewal](../../../../theories/becoming/history/THEORY.md#7-selection-changes-the-conditions-of-selection) already distinguishes reproduction, imitation, private retention and shared renewal. [Market dynamics](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md) gives actual payoffs whose common price and levy cancel, leaving the selection difference \(fq+\ell-c\); it then connects that difference to paid influence and liability. The addition is an explicit general invariance criterion explaining why indispensable support can remain unrepresented in selection. The analyst's assessment of a consequence supplies no reward channel.

## 2. Continuation can change the working objective without changing understanding

**Source result:** Proposition 3.9 and Remark 3.10; [S3, full form and proof](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/03_S3.md). Let \(\theta_0\) express the held relative weighting in \(J_i=\pi_i+\theta_0\bar\pi_{-i}\). Near the specified symmetric reference, the first-order working weight satisfies

\[
\frac{1+\theta}{n-1-\theta}
=\frac{1+\theta_0}{n-1-\theta_0}
\frac{1+\lambda_{\mathrm{com}}}{1+\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}}
=\frac{1+\theta_0}{n-1-\theta_0}
\frac{1-\Gamma_\delta}{1-\Gamma^{\mathrm c}_\delta}.
\]

Here \(-1<\theta_0<n-1\), and the relative and common continuation returns arise from net-payoff reinvestment. With both discounted gains in \([0,1)\), \(1+\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}=(1-\Gamma_\delta)^{-1}\) and \(1+\lambda_{\mathrm{com}}=(1-\Gamma^{\mathrm c}_\delta)^{-1}\). Thus the working objective narrows exactly when the relative return exceeds the common return. An accurate held model can coexist with this incentive; further accuracy alone does not change the continuation returns.

**Supplied premises and limits.** This is local and deterministic, with opponents' conduct held fixed in this statement, specified conversion laws, and a symmetric steady state or balanced path with constant linearized coefficients. Actual infinite-horizon interpretation requires finite discounted winnings and uniformly negligible discounted difference-quotient tails. Gains below one only at the reference do not supply that condition: S3's nonlinear-prize counterexample has finite linearized prices and infinite actual perturbed lifetime winnings. A fixed-prize case can narrow objectives while equal standing remains stable. Neither narrowing nor a positive relative premium independently proves concentration. Strategic response and stochastic finite-jump contests require their own continuation analysis.

**Current overlap and integration.** [Joint formation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/JOINT-FORMATION.md) models dated selves, learned valuation and anticipated later preferences; [evaluative plasticity](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) physically changes operative influence. V7.3 adds a different mechanism: a supplied held objective acquires a different effective marginal weighting because present resources change future opportunities. It does not derive the origin or validity of the held values, nor substitute this financial weighting for the material learning law.

## 3. Corrective independence depends on incoming networks and inherited state

**Source result:** Definition 4.1 and Propositions 4.10–4.12; [S4 proofs](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/04_S4.md). The field model uses

\[
\dot x_i=\zeta_i x_i(1-x_i)(a_i z_i-c_i),\qquad
\dot z_i=\varepsilon_i\left(\sum_jb_{ij}x_j-d_i z_i\right),
\qquad G_{ij}=\frac{a_i b_{ij}}{c_i d_i}.
\]

The captured share \(x_i\) and accumulated support \(z_i\) are distinct. Nonnegative incoming conversions represent specified channels such as appointments, funding and access. For fixed coefficients, a full inbound sum \(\sum_jG_{ij}<1\) suffices for eventual removal of capture when \(x_i(0)<1\) and initial support is finite. Diagonal gains below one do not suffice: mutually supporting domains can sustain capture. A reset can be reabsorbed when incoming support from domains that remain captured still exceeds the relevant threshold. Removing a domain that sustains the remaining core can instead propagate correction.

**Supplied premises and limits.** This is the declared cooperative field law, with fixed positive response/loss coefficients and monotone conversions, not a universal network theorem. The row bound is sufficient, not necessary. It guarantees eventual recovery, not present independence: inherited support can make captured share rise before recovery; \(x_i=1\) is invariant. A sufficient transient protection condition additionally bounds initial support by \(c_i/a_i\) and captured share by an acceptable level. [S16](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/16_S16.md) separately extends one joint protection certificate to maintained time-varying inbound bounds and effective liability.

Capture also has an independent meaning requirement: selector modification must create persistent advantage and impair a specified entrusted purpose, protected interest or corrective function relative to a defensible comparison. Concentration, hierarchy or a rule favorable to a successful actor does not establish those facts. Legitimate correction of a mistaken rule must remain possible.

**Current overlap and integration.** The market model already closes selection → paid organizational influence → eroded collectible liability → selection, with fiscal prefix guards, protected and destructive regions, and recovery. [Formation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/FORMATION.md) and joint formation already model strategic interference and its funding. The sharper addition is a network of distinct selectors, inherited support, and incoming-channel certification. It must not be described as the first endogenous capture or transition mechanism in the current account. Nor does the old continuous field automatically implement the newer paid discrete market law.

## 4. Substitution can erode the competence needed for correction

**Source result:** Lemma 6.3 and Proposition 4.6, with proofs in [S6](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/06_S6.md) and [S4](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/04_S4.md). If independent capacity \(\mathcal M\) decays under delegation at rate \(\delta_h>0\), safe return requires \(\mathcal M\ge\mathcal M_{\min}\), and \(\mathcal M_0>\mathcal M_{\min}\), then

\[
T_{\mathrm{lock}}=\frac{\log(\mathcal M_0/\mathcal M_{\min})}{\delta_h}.
\]

Under recovery \(\dot{\mathcal M}=\varrho_h(\bar{\mathcal M}-\mathcal M)\), with \(\bar{\mathcal M}>\mathcal M_{\min}\), return from a depleted state entails a below-floor interval

\[
T_{\mathrm{recover}}=\varrho_h^{-1}
\log\frac{\bar{\mathcal M}-\mathcal M}{\bar{\mathcal M}-\mathcal M_{\min}}.
\]

Maintaining sufficient parallel capacity removes this particular lock. If correction effectiveness is \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}=d(\mathcal M/\mathcal M_0)^w\), exponential competence decay adds \(w\delta_h\) to the correction-growth requirement of Proposition 4.5 under that proposition's other assumptions.

**Supplied premises and limits.** Capacity dependence, disuse decay, recovery law and safe threshold must be identified. Useful assistance that preserves competence need not produce the loop. A positive competence floor changes the asymptotic requirement. Captured evaluation encouraging further delegation and thereby making capture harder to undo is a specified numerical coupling in V7.3, not a general hysteresis theorem. The time formula does not prove that every delegated task destroys human authority.

**Current overlap and integration.** The current foundation already treats inquiry, alternatives and correction as paid maintained capacities, including depletion of future operating reserves. This mechanism identifies a particular source of that depletion: substitution removes the activity that renews independent competence. Its integration requires tracing the actual maintenance work, loss, available substitutes and recovery interval; an unused state variable named competence supplies none of them.

## 5. Strategic selection can reduce the information shared among centers

**Source result:** Proposition 5.16 and the [S5 proof](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/05_S5.md). In the supplied uniform-quadratic sender–receiver game with material bias \(b_0\ge0\), common weight \(\theta\in[-1,n-1]\), linear third-party effects and \(n-1+\theta>0\), the effective bias is

\[
b_{\mathrm{eff}}=b_0\frac{n-1-\theta}{n-1+\theta}.
\]

Informative cheap talk exists exactly when this bias is below \(1/4\). At \(\theta=-1\) and \(n\ge3\), the amplification is \(n/(n-2)\). For two purely relative players the objectives are zero-sum, and the source establishes payoff equivalence to ignoring talk, rather than a universal ban on sending messages. A shared hazard alone does not establish a sender's credibility: the sender may benefit from the receiver's restraint regardless of its own intended conduct.

**Supplied premises and limits.** These are equilibrium claims for a specified cheap-talk game. Costly signals, verified observations, different payoff curvature and external enforcement require separate models. The amplification decreases with the number of actors; the theorem does not show that larger groups always communicate worse. Claims that narratives or conceptual vocabularies become captured remain separate conjectures. Substituting a working weight from Section 2 requires matching its payoff interpretation and establishing the communication stage; the two formulas alone do not prove a joint dynamic theorem.

**Current overlap and integration.** [Valued futures and faithful evidence](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATION-AND-FAITHFUL-REPORTING.md) already distinguishes acquired information from warranted reporting and action, including convenient misdescription motivated by valued outcomes. The contribution here is strategic informativeness among separately motivated centers, complementing that semantic/reporting contract. Truth, credibility, normative standing and authority remain distinct.

## 6. A reform must traverse its transient and retain recruitable alternatives

**Source result:** Proposition S11.4 and Remark S11.5 in [S11](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/11_S11.md), followed by Proposition S17.1 in [S17](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/17_S17.md). In the one-domain field, start at normalized support \(\tilde z=G>1\) and interior captured share with logit \(u_0\). Insulating incoming conversion gives

\[
\tilde z(t)=Ge^{-\varepsilon dt},\qquad
u(t)=u_0+\zeta c\left[\frac{G(1-e^{-\varepsilon dt})}{\varepsilon d}-t\right].
\]

Captured share initially increases while inherited support exceeds break-even. Release must occur strictly inside the recovery basin; release on its boundary approaches the saddle. The required hold has no uniform bound as logit depth increases. A maintained, recruitable alternative with share at least \(\phi_{\mathrm{alt}}>0\) caps the captured share at \(1-\phi_{\mathrm{alt}}\), bounds depth, and bounds the necessary hold in this fixed model. A nominal alternative outside selection that cannot grow into the new opening does not supply that result. Formation and maintenance of the floor remain premises.

**The funded transition.** S17 adds one explicit survivable path into S16's protected region. After installation delay \(L\), incoming influence is durably cut from \(b_0\) to \(b_1\). Temporary correction rises from \(d_0\) to \(d_B\), then falls to maintained \(d_\infty\). Choose thresholds with

\[
\tau(1-\bar x)-\nu=\Delta>0,\qquad
b_1/d_\infty<z_\dagger<c/a.
\]

The source computes support-clearance, capture-reduction and load-reduction durations \(T_z,T_x,T_y\), with \(T_*=L+T_z+T_x+T_y\), and sufficient total cost

\[
C_*=C_I+k_d\left[\tfrac{d_0+d_B}{2}L+d_B(T_z+T_x)+d_\infty T_y\right]+mT_*.
\]

Its sufficient conditions include available finite budget \(B\ge C_*\), recurring flow \(R\ge k_dd_\infty+m\), peak and build-rate limits, and a maximum-load host lower bound \(\underline V(T_*)\ge V_{\min}\). They jointly protect the transition and allow ordinary maintenance after the temporary increase ends. A longer installation can exhaust the host's reserve before an otherwise sustainable destination is reached.

**Supplied premises and limits.** S11 uses a fixed one-domain gain and a quasi-static conversion law; its numerical dwell times are not calibrated institutional times. At captured share one, insulation alone cannot recreate the extinct alternative. S17 supplies fixed load traits, no entry/mutation, enforceable liability, actual reform authority, accessible funds, a correction-production law and a durable conversion cut. It proves neither their political origin nor their survival through unrestricted amendment. Failure of the conservative sufficient reserve certificate is not impossibility; losing its maintenance certificate alone is not proved recapture.

**Current overlap and integration.** [The formal core](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-CORE.md) already enforces actual-state succession, prefix solvency and joint safety; market dynamics already gives a funded invariant, an attracting set and recovery before shutdown. V7.3 adds a specific slow-support transition, the role of recruitability, and separation of temporary reform work from recurring operation. These enrich the existing transition account rather than introducing its first safe-path requirement.

## Composition audit and next bridge

The six mechanisms share an explanatory connection, not automatically one state law. Population shares in a relative selection rule, a strategic actor's marginal objective, capture-domain shares, independent competence, message equilibria and a controlled transition have different meanings. The same letter or a parallel verbal description does not identify them. Continuous fields, discrete paid rounds and finite material event histories also have different clocks and accounting boundaries.

An asserted combined realization therefore needs: actual carrier and state correspondences; a specified transmission/decision law at each stage; common observation and timing contracts; funded formation and maintenance of corrective means; actual successor states rather than resets; and one compatible set of action/authority constraints. The [faithful-implementation criterion](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-CORE.md#f13-faithful-implementation-joins-models-without-changing-their-questions) governs any claimed transfer. Equilibrium incentives do not execute a transition, and a feasible transition does not prove its selection.

The integration must retain three cross-cutting distinctions. Eventual recovery is weaker than protection through the transient. Information or warning is weaker than authorized, funded intervention before irreversible loss. And an operational success criterion is weaker than justified concern for each affected center. V7.3's coverage-to-survival result separately requires continued exposure, a coverage deficit and a residual-risk lower bound; failed evaluation does not supply those premises.

The [current market construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md) already joins some of these interfaces at its scope. The further explanatory bridge is **formed or inherited evaluative understanding → operative action when its transmission, communication and corrective authority are themselves selectively revised**. One useful extension would endogenize a presently supplied conversion or maintenance route while preserving its actual information and resource account; that extension is not a prerequisite invented for every use of the completed conditional foundation.

What would change the account is evidence that a proposed channel is absent, has the opposite sign, is adequately counteracted, or fails its implementation contract. The conjunction of V7.3's negative mechanisms on the contemporary AI trajectory remains empirical conjecture C0. The generative alternative's attraction, universal favorable direction, moral legitimacy and exclusive metaphysical necessity do not follow from this integration. The accepted conditional foundation retains its declared scope; these mechanisms sharpen its explanation of constructive and destructive regimes.
