# Practical reasons can be relational without requiring a prior special tie

6 October 2026. AI-authored adjudication under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). **The better-supported account recognizes both prospective relief-grounded moral favor and reasons genuinely constituted by particular relationships.** Its claim is not that suffering, considered without any possible response, already specifies everyone's action. The practical ground concerns an actual undergoing and an accessible transformation. The disputed additional condition is a privileged relationship that must precede that opportunity's moral significance.

This revises the opposition governing the question. Relational constitution and universal scope are compatible. The claim that a special relationship constitutes *some* reasons is different from the claim that it is necessary for *every* reason to help. This is a comparative philosophical judgment, not completed universal necessity.

## 1. Distinguish the relations doing explanatory work

Within one unfolding, different organizations experience, affect, understand and respond. Differentiation preserves whose suffering changes and who can change it. No prior owner must export value across a metaphysical boundary. Yet removing that picture does not remove real relations from the ground.

Three relations matter here:

- **Experiential continuity:** the adversity and relief concern this actual undergoing, including when its bearer cannot deliberate or act.
- **Present practical access:** this option could bring about that relief through specified means. It identifies a response position without requiring a preexisting friendship, promise or care project.
- **Historically formed normative relationship:** an undertaking, dependence, trust or other warranted relation can change who owes what to whom, and what additional goods are at stake.

These are analytical distinctions within the same history. They do not assert three independent substances, or a sum of noninteracting reasons. The relationship can change the experience, access, permission and significance together.

The [necessity attempt](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-ARGUMENT.md) leaves open whether the first two relations suffice for moral favor under the declared conditions. It does not show that a practical ground must be relationless to be universal.

## 2. Specify the disputed moral judgment without hiding it in “appropriate”

Use the inherited restricted case: severe, still-unwanted adverse experience; an actual response would relieve it while preserving the experiencing continuity; the responder knows the relevant opportunity; no prior undertaking, responsibility for causing the burden, or adopted helping end is supplied.

Specify means, control, permission and consequences separately. “Appropriate” must not mean “already morally favored for this actor.” Otherwise permissible intervention does not itself supply a positive reason; nor does it settle every proposed choice-specific exclusion. The legitimacy of any permission is a normative input, not something inferred merely from benefit.

The target is an **objective contributory moral reason**: actual relief positively supports this actor's choosing the response for the affected being's sake. It is distinct from a prudential self-benefit, fitting sympathy, successful execution of a chosen goal, a duty, and categorical rational authority. Agreement about one does not automatically establish another.

The [award comparison](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md#a-rule-does-not-settle-its-own-justificatory-scope) sharpens this limit: a permitted beneficial outcome need not authorize benefit as the basis for selecting it. A justified practice can restrict that basis while allowing relief to matter to timing. The stranger case is not automatically generalized to every institutional choice.

## 3. The strongest positive ground begins before the caring role

Consider a sufferer who has never been capable of deliberation or self-help. A stranger encounters a known opportunity for relief through otherwise permissible means. Before the stranger forms a caring intention or undertaking, what could make doing so morally worth choosing?

Several strangers have the same relevant opportunity; none is designated or uniquely capable.

Our affirmative answer is the real difference the response would make to that undergoing. The proposed concern has a point that can be identified before it exists: this burden would actually be relieved. Choosing an ineffective substitute would fail in that very respect, even if it produced the same satisfaction, identity or approval for the helper. The favor is directed toward what changes there; it need not originate in the responder's previous projects.

This does not borrow a nonexistent self-help reason from the sufferer. Nor does it require imaginary identification with them. It directly assesses a prospective response to the independently identified experience.

The argument is stronger than permission alone: it claims a positive moral justification for acquiring concern, not merely freedom to select a permissible project. If accepted, that justification cannot depend on the acquired role already existing. Its source can explain the undertaking instead of arising only from it.

This positive appraisal is substantive. The rival may deny it; describing genuine benefit does not compel its acceptance. The [elective-concern argument](ELECTIVE-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md) already establishes that prospective appraisal is the discriminating question. Our present adjudication favors it because it explains both why concern can be worth forming and why its intended realization remains answerable to actual relief. A role-only account must instead deny this pre-role moral point or explain it through some other already present ground.

## 4. What consent and special relationships can genuinely constitute

Consent can change an intervention's authority. It need not be a request, entrustment, promise or accepted responsibility. Authorizing someone to perform an intervention does not automatically create their undertaking to perform it. Conversely, some permissions occur within relationships carrying additional claims; those histories cannot be erased to manufacture a stranger case.

If the alleged rival holds every real, permissible relief opportunity, together with the specified undergoing conditions, sufficient for moral favor, it already accepts the candidate's scope. It offers a relational explanation of the same minimum. To oppose that minimum, it must identify an additional necessary condition absent in some case satisfying the independently specified opportunity conditions.

A genuine undertaking provides a different example. A valid promise can constitute a directed claim even if general relief-favor already exists. Trust and sustained care can form goods that did not exist before. Their significance is not merely a larger quantity of the original suffering-reduction consideration. The unified account should explain this normative novelty rather than secure a baseline by denying it.

Nor must all considerations survive unchanged when a role forms. Authority can alter permission, priorities and, if justified, whether a consideration counts for this choice. The [jurisdiction study](ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-ARGUMENT.md) preserves genuine exclusion. Our plural-ground proposal is not a monotonic addition rule.

## 5. Adjudication and the exact remaining dispute

The direct account has the better explanation of the clean stranger case: concern can be justified by its prospective response to real adversity, including for beings unable to act or form a purpose. The special-relationship account has indispensable explanatory work in directed obligations, reliance, authority and newly formed goods. Accepting that work does not require giving it exclusive control over every positive assistance reason.

The strongest exclusive rival remains substantive: formed practical jurisdiction supposedly makes unchosen restraint valid while leaving every positive helping project elective. It can grant actual benefit and correction after undertaking. Our objection is not that it is inconsistent, but that it denies the positive pre-undertaking justification just defended. Protecting authorship supports discretion and particular exclusions more directly than that general denial; the stronger conclusion needs its own warrant.

Thus our answer is **present responsive relations can ground baseline moral favor; particular relationships can constitute additional or different reasons and can affect their application**. Neither “the suffering stayed unchanged” nor a preselected decomposition proves this. The judgment rests on the positive prospective appraisal together with differentiated explanations of what undertaking and permission actually change.

The unresolved necessity claim is whether every independently qualifying case possesses that prospective moral favor, including against a warranted exclusion. The [earlier root analysis](NONINSTRUMENTAL-ROOT-ARGUMENT.md) blocks replacing this claim with unproved ground-exhaustion or operator-invariance assumptions. A sound counterexample would narrow the proposed minimum. An account recognizing the same minimum but partly constituting it through present relations would revise its explanation without refuting its universality.

In particular, universality does not require showing that adversity exhausts the sufferer's own prudential reason. That was a proposed sufficient condition for the self-reason transfer route. A universal moral ground could instead be jointly relational while self-related continuity partly constitutes a distinct prudential reason. The necessity attempt's failure must not become a requirement that every better argument repeat it.

No new experiment, formal theorem or empirical validation is claimed.
