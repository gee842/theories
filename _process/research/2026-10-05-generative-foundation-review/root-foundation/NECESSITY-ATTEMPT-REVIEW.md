# Can the reason in one's own suffering establish universal scope?

6 October 2026. AI-authored constructive audit under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), continuing the [warrant inquiry](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md). The target is a contributory ethical reason for every relevant response position under independently specified experiential and opportunity conditions. It is not inevitable caring behavior, compulsory assistance, or a uniquely necessary ontology. No new formal theorem or empirical result is claimed.

## 1. The strongest substantive rival

Consider an account on which practical authority is partly constituted by the organization and continuity of a particular life. Its suffering is objectively adverse; its practical activity has reason to protect that same continuing life without first choosing a self-care project. Other lives have equally real adversity. Their relief is genuinely beneficial, regardless of this agent's attitudes.

This account nevertheless denies that every benefit supplies every capable agent a reason. Responsibilities, undertakings and legitimate relationships can make another's condition a ground within this agent's practical authority. Otherwise aid can be permitted and freely adopted. After adoption, the genuine benefit determines success and corrects mistaken means. The account can also recognize constraints against causing harm. It need not deny experience, require selfish pleasure from every act, or treat arbitrary wishes as authoritative.

Its proposed justification appeals to formed participation in practical decisions: each life is a differentiated locus of projects, control, consequences and answerability. The account treats that relation as constituting some reasons, not merely weighting a universally shared list. This is a substantive proposal whose normative adequacy remains disputed. Its causal genealogy alone would not vindicate it.

Such a rival can be nondual. The relevant continuity need not be an indivisible substance or primordial owner. A common process can form distinct lives and their relations. Universal application of “each has these reasons regarding their own continuity” is compatible with unequal interpersonal address. Renaming every position consistently preserves this rule. Global covariance therefore does not refute it.

## 2. What “my pain supplies a reason by itself” establishes

Grant the clean case of actual severe, still-unwanted experiential adversity and a permissible, effective response. Three claims differ:

1. The undergoing itself is adverse for its bearer, without an external score creating that adversity.
2. The bearer's reason for relief does not require an antecedent desire, project or reflective ownership judgment.
3. The complete normative ground contains no self-relation beyond identifying the undergoing and locating an available response.

The first two do not establish the third. A reason can exist independently of someone's recognition while involving a real relationship to them. An infant's lack of an ownership concept does not erase differentiated undergoing. Similarly, answering “because it hurts” can identify the salient ground without exhaustively listing every constituent of its practical address.

If the third claim is independently warranted, the actor-local restriction introduces new normative content. It cannot be recovered merely from a fact already excluded from the complete ground. That is a genuine conditional consequence. But if the rival grounds the own-pain reason jointly in adversity and continuity, it has not first conceded ground exhaustion and then arbitrarily appended a gate. The disputed issue is which account identifies the ground correctly.

The direct account has an attraction: the adverse feature explains what relief accomplishes, while capacity locates how it may be achieved. The rival must explain why continuity determines the existence of all baseline favor, rather than special duties, priority, discretion or implementation. This explanatory challenge favors further investigation; it is not already a proof that continuity can never have the stronger role.

## 3. What the candidate routes actually require

| Route | What it establishes or pressures | Additional commitment required for universal scope |
|---|---|---|
| Experiential identity | Identifies an adverse aspect without an added observer penalty | That this aspect constitutes an action-ground across the claimed response positions, not only local badness |
| Ground locality | Keeps the same experiencing history and relief target while the operator changes | Normative sufficiency of those preserved relations; physical locality does not establish it |
| Perspective exchange | Exposes special pleading tied to particular names and can improve understanding | That reasons survive changing the actor–bearer relation; global exchange preserves egoism |
| Own-pain practical reason | Supplies a plausible desire-independent reason in the self-related case | Ground exhaustion, compatible reason kinds, and coverage of beings never able to act for themselves |
| Normative counterfactuals | Relief ceasing to help defeats that claimed point of the response | That dependence on actual benefit is sufficient, rather than one constituent of a relational ground |
| End revision | Supports assessing a selected purpose by consequences beyond its own success test | A warranted appraisal independent of that purpose; responsibility-based criticism alone leaves stranger assistance open |

These are substantive dependencies, not a demand for an external authorizer. The [earlier root argument](NONINSTRUMENTAL-ROOT-ARGUMENT.md) already shows why anchors and operator transport cannot be counted as an eliminated premise merely by reformulation. The present test identifies the ambiguity in the proposed own-pain anchor more sharply.

An imagination exercise does not independently supply the missing transport. “Were I undergoing that, I would have a reason” preserves the self-relation. “Were I uncertain who would suffer, I would choose mutual protection” can instead supply a prudential policy under uncertainty. Neither alone establishes what reasons remain for an informed, unrelated noncauser in the actual case. Conversely, the absence of motivation after perspective taking does not show that no reason exists.

## 4. The positive minimum that survives

The direct relief judgment remains defensible: actual improvement can contribute to an available choice before care is adopted. It explains why concern can be worth forming, why an ineffective intervention requires correction, and why continued intervention can lose its original point. Its object is the affected occurrence rather than an actor's approval. Those connected explanatory virtues support the candidate; they are not fresh observations proving necessity.

One exclusion rationale can be rejected decisively at its limited scope. Protecting a project from compulsory sacrifice does not by itself establish that permissible incidental relief contributes nothing. The latter conclusion needs a reason-excluding power, not merely permission to decline. Genuine choice-specific exclusion and concerns about moral answerability remain possible; the [closure audit](ETHICAL-CONSTITUTION-CLOSURE.md) preserves their scope.

A stronger autonomy rival grants unchosen restraints against taking control of another life while reserving positive projects to their author. It grounds restraint in noninterference, not an unrestricted welfare principle. Its concession defeats the blanket claim that reasons concerning others always need prior adoption; it does not yet establish assistance. The relevant pressure is whether protecting self-direction explains zero favor for incidental relief that does not invade those protections. If moral answerability itself allegedly invades them, that is a further claim to defend. The restraint–assistance distinction cannot be erased by calling both relations nondual.

An informed manipulator can acknowledge the reason and defy it. That possibility threatens reliable uptake, not the acknowledged reason's truth. A rival denying the practical scope must instead defend its restriction while preserving the adverse experience, actual benefit and relevant opportunity. Neither a free ethical-predicate toggle nor a successful harmful process supplies that defense.

The most discriminating remaining question is therefore whether the self-relation is **constitutive of the baseline reason**, or instead explains privileged access, special responsibility and priority around a reason grounded more broadly. Counterfactual comparisons must preserve the actual target and vary that relation, not merely change names. A claimed ground-exhaustion argument must explain why the relation is normatively redundant rather than assume it.

## 5. Verdict

Universal necessity is not established by this attempt. The own-pain route succeeds conditionally if its full-ground claim is justified; desire independence, nondual formation and global symmetry do not supply that justification. Nor has the rival been vindicated: the proposed authority of its restrictive relationship remains a substantive burden.

The advance is a precise adjudication of the proposed shortcut, alongside continued positive support for the minimal relief ground. It locates the unresolved explanation within generation, without declaring ethics permanently irreducible or requiring a unique metaphysics before conditional universal research can proceed.

Reading scope: direct repository argument and method review; no additional primary-source access, theorem compilation or scientific rerun.
