## What an implemented result could identify

A finite construction can establish that a specified local assembly law, operating on an actual inherited predictor and accounted parts, produces a shared route with positive probability; that productive history changes a measured construction distribution; and that actual consequences pay for finite retention. Each claim needs its own recorded events. Showing that one installed architecture performs well does not identify its origin. Showing more retained instances does not distinguish more births from fewer losses.

The decomposition to record is: **attempted assembly → completed architecture before use → first operation → post-operation retention**. Probability denominators must include abandoned assemblies and inherited failures. Report conditional results for reachable, well-funded states separately from unconditional results across the whole inherited process. An altered initial distribution is an intervention unless its alteration is produced and paid for within the history.

Local component laws, signal meanings, actuator addressing, prices, environments and initial stocks remain supplied unless an earlier accepted construction actually provides them. Reusing names or similar balances does not establish that two studies share the same state. A successful authored fixture is an existence result for its declared law, not an estimate of its biological prevalence.

A modeled recipient's reserve and paid operation are declared functional proxies. Biological welfare requires an independently justified outcome measure; an energy ledger alone does not supply one.

## Three primary comparators and their limits

**Organizational history can change accessible variation.** Parter, Kashtan and Alon, [*Facilitated Variation: How Evolution Learns from Past Environments To Generalize to New Environments*](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000206) (2008), is a computational study of logic circuits and RNA secondary structures. Under modularly changing goals, evolved organizations produced accessible variants suited to related tasks; the advantage did not generalize to unrelated random goals. See Results, especially “High-Fitness Phenotypes for Past Goals…” and “Evolution of Novelty…”. Encoding, mutations, goal structure and fitness are supplied. The comparison motivates measuring the distribution of variants before further selection and testing environmental transfer. It does not identify the evolution of helping or ethical concern.

**Experience can reconfigure an existing self–other action mapping.** Catmur, Walsh and Heyes, [*Sensorimotor Learning Configures the Human Mirror System*](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~ascch/Celia%27s%20pdfs/2007sensorimotor.pdf) (2007), pp.1527–1530, compared compatible and incompatible observation–execution training. Matched visual and motor exposure with changed pairing reversed the muscle-specific motor-evoked response measured during observation. Sixteen participants were selected from forty-four for clear initial physiological effects. This supports causal modification of an existing mapping; it does not demonstrate the complete developmental origin of that machinery. A motor response to another's movement is neither valuation of that person's welfare nor costly help. Existing sensorimotor capacities and plasticity are supplied.

**Regulation can develop within dependence on another organism.** Moriceau and Sullivan, [*Maternal presence serves as a switch between learning fear and attraction in infancy*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1560090/) (2006), Figs.1–3, found that twelve-to-fifteen-day rat pups learned avoidance after odor–shock pairing alone but preference with maternal presence. Hormonal and neural interventions probed the mechanism; corticosterone infusion restored aversion despite the mother's presence. This supports caregiver-dependent regulation before mature independence. It does not establish the common origin of caregiver and infant, absence of self/other discrimination, or origin of caregiving. Approach despite painful pairing also prevents identifying socially supported learning with uniformly beneficial development. Co-development of differentiated centers and shared machinery remains a proposed branch, not this experiment's finding.

These sources concern different levels and systems. They constrain the proposal; they do not jointly constitute an observed continuous progression from basic matter to concern. Primary text and article methods were inspected; raw data, supplementary analyses and replication histories were not reanalyzed.

## Required interventions and observations

| Contrast | Required intervention and matching | Decisive observation and remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Production before recipient outcomes | Manipulate accessible templates, compatible ports or local reconnection opportunities before novel recipient use. Match existing function, elapsed time and total accounted stocks; record any support needed for matching. | Count newly formed graphs before their first outcome. A difference here identifies production bias under the intervention; a difference only after operation can instead be differential persistence. |
| Changed generator versus replay | Compare actual task-funded changes in templates/sites with a replay that matches the initial proposal distribution but prevents those changes. Give replay the same observations, charged operations, timing and available resources. If equalization requires subsidy, declare it separately. | Test whether measured generator changes predict later construction frequencies on held-out histories. Merely changing fuel or giving one arm extra assembly attempts does not isolate the generator. |
| Productive consequence versus passive persistence | Block the specified consequence-to-maintenance path while preserving the pre-outcome architecture and recording the resulting resource difference. Separately use a declared resource-matched control to test whether the effect is mediated by affordability. | Birth counts can remain equal while finite retention changes. Resource-mediated retention is a valid mechanism; resource matching must not erase that mechanism and then be interpreted as its ordinary continuation. |
| Shared prediction versus shared response | Preserve information about both targets while interrupting only the route from recipient condition to evaluative/action selection; include restoration of that route. | Equal predictive performance with different recipient-sensitive action rejects the sufficiency of shared knowledge. Neural overlap alone cannot serve as this intervention. |
| Recipient benefit versus actor return | Independently vary actual recipient benefit, actor expense and compensating actor return. Audit separate receipts and permit a known-other, zero-compensating-return condition. Separately vary escape/concealment and cheap identity gating. | Recipient-directed action must track independently measured recipient consequences after the private-return route is removed. Continued helping of an indispensable partner alone does not establish this endpoint. Subjective relief cannot simply be declared absent. |
| Recipient correction and destructive transfer | Reverse action efficacy, change a recipient's stated preference or refusal, and test a new recipient with new cues. Retain false correspondence, harmful sharing, cheap gates and incomplete assembly when the local law allows them. | Correction toward actual recipient outcomes supports revisable response. Persistence of old behavior despite contrary evidence limits the claim to cached association or a previously useful route. |

For biology, construction counts require an independently observable structural or functional change, not only a fitted latent label. For an artificial system, preserve the full local event trace, paid intermediate states and component identities. Adult behavioral tests begin with substantial prior capacities and values; successful transfer would not mean concern arose from none.

## Conventional equivalence is part of the contract

A conventional stochastic state machine can include the same parts, local transition law, construction events, history and resource accounts. Such a saturated comparator must reproduce every distribution of this finite construction. Agreement is a correctness check; the result cannot distinguish the accounts by vocabulary or defeat conventional control in general.

The informative restricted competitors are a fixed proposal distribution, a generator varying only with current liquidity, differential retention without changed production, a shared predictor with private-only action, and a cue-value route that ignores recipient correction. Give each the same observation access and initial budget; distinguish expressive limits from arbitrary parameter restrictions. Fit or specify them before evaluating held-out perturbations. If a rival reproduces all tested observations, preserve that equivalence and narrow the claim.

The construction's potential contribution is an explicit mechanism and accounting constraint: the proposal distribution itself can have a paid material history. A broader empirical claim requires observing the corresponding mechanism in an identified system and showing that the interventions above discriminate it from restricted rivals.
