## Application and completion limits

This is inherited causal influence through retained supports, a new learner's means correction and selected physical delivery. Child-specific technical grants do not establish ethical authority. The finite birth/access recipe, positive-feedback law, calibrated decoder, inquiry schedule, feature transfer, private-cost conversion, external support and stability are supplied local inputs. No human phenomenology, native ethical standing, self-financing ecology, indefinite succession or universal favorable direction follows.

The [application contract][application] identifies a decisive limit. If an independently fixed one-service claim counts the successful second inquiry, every faithfully reached known-command entrance in this construction already has that fulfillment evidence. The current evaluator has no application-withdrawal rule. The later selected receipt therefore cannot be described as responding to that still-pending claim. This limits ethical interpretation without invalidating the causal construction; it also prevents retrospective episode definitions from manufacturing a favorable application.

No new empirical experiment is performed here. The result does not complete the unified foundation or add new mandatory apparatus requirements. It addresses the named inheritance/correction/action interface; justified evaluative application and revision, general regimes, empirical scope and the integrated premise/result/failure account retain their existing completion obligations.
