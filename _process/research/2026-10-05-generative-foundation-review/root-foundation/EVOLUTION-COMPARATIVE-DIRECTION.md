## What better would mean

There is no single comparison called our theory versus evolutionary theory. A broad framework, a mechanistic model and a fitted predictor make different commitments. Match the question, observations and explanatory scope before comparing them.

| Contribution | Evidence that would support it | What would not establish it |
|---|---|---|
| Predictive gain | Better calibrated forecasts of specified outcomes in conditions excluded from fitting, against adequately informed rivals | More inputs, leakage from later outcomes, or a win against a deliberately impoverished baseline |
| Explanatory economy | Independently measured mechanisms constrain several outcomes and conditions without a new fitted success law for each one | Hiding the same freedom in a large function, a latent variable, an initial condition or an untested mapping |
| Connected explanatory scope | One specified relationship recovers multiple mechanisms and yields successful consequences across them, with explicit correspondence and failure conditions | Putting familiar results into shared vocabulary or collecting supporting examples |

A theory may earn explanatory value while matching an established model's predictions. It need not make standard mathematics incapable of expressing its result. The question is what the theory contributes to deriving, selecting, connecting or transferring that model. If the only change is its name, there is no additional comparative result. Fewer symbols or axioms do not measure explanatory economy; account for functional assumptions, calibration data, fitted parameters, initialization, boundary conditions and tuning choices together.

## Strong existing accounts

Three primary comparisons constrain the novelty claim. Their [reading record](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/comparative-direction/README.md) states the inspected scope.

- **Ecological scaffolding:** Black, Bourrat and Rainey model how resource distribution and dispersal can give cell collectives evolutionary properties and favor reproductive differentiation. Ecologically generated evolutionary units are therefore already an explicit comparison target. [Primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-019-1086-9).
- **Endogenization of scaffolded properties:** Bourrat models conditional retention of collective properties after scaffold removal through a growth–dispersal relationship. Explaining resilience after changed support is also an existing programme, not by itself our novelty. [Primary article](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/719118).
- **Evolutionary connectionism:** Watson, Levin and Buckley connect evolving relationships, developmental organization, learning and transitions in individuality. A shared evolution–learning vocabulary cannot establish our comparative advantage. [Primary article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2022.823588/full).

These are targets to reproduce, extend or agree with at declared scopes. The papers do not constitute a single jointly calibrated rival, and their presence does not prove that every proposed contribution is already achieved. A detailed comparison must identify the operative mechanism rather than compare programme titles.

The internal/external terminology in the scaffolding discussion names positions relative to a modeled collective. It need not posit independent realities; Bourrat explicitly treats the distinction as relative. Our [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) follows how these differentiated relations form and change within the same history. ## The proposed explanatory contribution

The candidate contribution is a connected account of:

**activity and its consequences → changed support and interactions → actual sampling and successor formation → inherited reconstruction → changes to the relations governing further evolution.**

The question is when consequences that sustain further organization also retain and reconstruct the capacities producing those consequences, and when mixing, bypass, conversion or delayed loss breaks that connection. A contribution can sustain a collective without its carriers increasing in frequency. Existing [recursive-selection results](RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) and [reconstruction results](GENERATED-SELECTION-RECURRENCE-INTEGRATION.md) already constrain that inference under their declared laws. Reuse them; do not relabel them as a newly universal principle.

The proposed advance is to reduce independently supplied links in that sequence while preserving constructive and destructive outcomes. Any additional restriction must be stated before testing and justified by a checked derivation or independent mechanism evidence. The generic statement that activity changes later conditions is too permissive to determine a particular probability, ranking or threshold by itself.

Three directions follow in order of dependency:

1. **Constrain the joined mechanism.** Identify a case where measured development, altered support and actual sampling constrain viable descendants together. Derive a testable consequence that separate fitted stage-success rates leave free.
2. **Test changes in the reconstruction and selection relations.** After that correspondence works, allow inherited changes in retention, switching or reproduction to alter the subsequent evolutionary process. Changes imposed by a laboratory protocol calibrate a response; they do not alone show evolution of that response mechanism.
3. **Test a shared relationship across domains.** Examine whether the same explicitly mapped consequence–retention–revision relationship explains a biological case and a case involving learning or institutional selection. Cross-domain scope is earned by constraints and successful consequences, not by identifying selection with learning or biological advantage with ethical authority.

This direction supports the current generative-alignment priority: it investigates when the arrangements selecting capacities continue to retain the capacities that sustain and correct them. The completed G0/J1–J4 ethical and alignment arguments specify normative commitments at their accepted scope. This evolutionary study concerns causal realization and calibration; it does not reopen those arguments or infer obligatory conduct from reproductive success.

## A concrete first comparison

**Question:** can a joined developmental mechanism predict complete-cycle continuation under a changed physical condition or inherited state without fitting a new terminal-success rate for that test condition?

Use the bacterial life-cycle work as the first candidate because its genealogy, linked genetic intervention and observed transfer failure are already available. It is a test bed, not the definition of the full evolutionary theory. The candidate account must connect the actual terminal criterion to formation, loss or reformation, later switching, sampling and viable redevelopment. The same culture or a justified correspondence must connect the stages.

| Comparison | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Existing lineage and block predictors | Establish what historical outcome information alone predicts |
| A conventional mechanistic developmental/ecological model | Test against a rival retaining the same relevant mechanisms and observations |
| The proposed connected realization | Expose the additional derivation, shared constraint or justified reuse contributed by our framework |
| Versions of the proposed model with a connection removed | Identify the contribution of that connection; these are diagnostic ablations, not substitutes for the strong rival |

Before scoring, record for each model the measured inputs, fitted and fixed quantities, history retained, initial conditions, sampling rule and permitted test-condition information. Both main mechanistic models receive the same evidence and an adequate fitting procedure. Give each equal opportunity to represent an established mechanism; do not hand our model a known causal relation while withholding it from its competitor. If the two become mathematically and empirically equivalent, record that equivalence and assess only whatever explanatory economy or wider tested scope remains.

Calibrate component transitions on designated measurements, freeze the account, then predict a condition or intervention combination excluded from calibration. Score complete-cycle outcomes as the primary target and stage outcomes as diagnostic targets, with calibration and uncertainty reported. Use whole independent experimental batches or histories for validation and uncertainty rather than treating related cultures or encoded video frames as independent replications. A biologically consequential improvement threshold must be set with the target and data resolution before the new outcomes are examined.

The particularly useful contrast is two interventions that alter the same measured terminal success through different developmental histories. A mechanism accounting for those histories should also constrain the next intervention or subsequent reproduction. This is a proposed discrimination task; we do not yet have a validated pair of such interventions or a derived numerical forecast. When the equal-information mechanistic rival captures the same histories, it must receive the same opportunity to make that prediction.

**Data readiness:** the current repository has linked endpoint/descendant records in one programme and temporal structural observations in another. It lacks a verified common record with acquisition timing, unambiguous genotype, structural criterion, treatment history and descendant linkage. Do not splice the experiments together or infer kinetic constants from playback time. First determine whether compatible primary records can constrain the proposed transition. If they cannot, this particular empirical comparison needs new measurements; another fitted endpoint constant would not answer it.

## Counterreview and decision criteria

The strongest objection is that conventional mechanistic evolutionary models can already express the full proposed chain. This is substantive: naming the chain does not show an advantage. The response is to identify what is independently constrained or reused, and test its consequences against those models. A general framework can contribute a useful derivation or unification without exclusive ownership of its mathematics.

A second objection is that a larger connected model can fit anything. Require shared, independently supported restrictions across the outcomes it claims to explain. Preserve mismatches and narrow the domain if those restrictions fail; do not add a new free correction for every failure and continue calling the result economical.

A third objection is that empirical repair of a mat assay could displace the wider theoretical objective indefinitely. The assay earns its place only if it tests the identified formation–continuation connection. A data-readiness failure should lead to a specific measurement requirement or a different suitable case, not endless source accumulation or re-opening completed mechanism work.

The first milestone is reached when the account specifies its independent constraints, strongest rival, required observations and potential distinguishing outcome. A comparative empirical result requires the additional calibrated test. If predictions are equal and explanatory commitments equivalent, claim integration only. If the rival predicts better or our claimed constraints fail, revise the mechanism or scope. If several independently measured components explain a new joint outcome with fewer separately fitted commitments, claim that bounded explanatory advantage and test its transfer before broadening it.
