# Acquired consequences change subsequent inquiry within a supplied interface

6 October 2026. AI-authored source reconstruction, following the project-wide [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **an acquired consequence changes the operative organization of subsequent acquisition, and that organization changes actual further observation**. This continues [consequence and organization](CONSEQUENCE-ORGANIZATION-MECHANISM.md). It extracts an additional causal implication of already checked histories; it introduces no controller, event law, scientific rerun or Lean theorem.

The strongest matched source already contains a positive connection. A retained consequence changes the generated candidate family. The changed family remains ambiguous after readings that would have completed the narrower inquiry, so the continuing process performs another action/read cycle before retaining its model. This establishes acquired control of inquiry duration. It does not establish formation of a new sensing contact, a new primitive action, or a justified choice of which questions matter.

## 1. Four distinctions needed to state the result

| What can change | Meaning here | Established source |
|---|---|---|
| Internal diagnostic context | Which retained histories and action suffixes distinguish candidate states | Acquisition adds the counterexample's prefixes and a separating suffix |
| Executed inquiry | Whether and when another native action/read is performed, and which phase is reached before an assay | Method-revision matched controls; diagnostic movement based on acquired belief |
| Semantic scope | The class of action/observation questions whose answers define an exact representation | Supplied alphabet, family and scope; changing a finite table does not itself enlarge this class |
| Physical access | The actual coupling that makes an observation or transformation possible | Present apparatus contacts are supplied; the reviewed learner diagnoses and uses them but does not construct a replacement |

These are distinctions within one unfolding, not four independent realms. A changed diagnostic context can alter future action; an altered contact can change what observations distinguish. The source must establish the particular connection being claimed. A fixed transition law does not imply a fixed inquiry organization, while a changed inquiry organization does not automatically mean a new physical interface.

## 4. The unresolved connection is now narrower

The repository already realizes **acquired evidence → changed candidate organization → changed conditions for further native inquiry**, alongside the previous round's stronger **archive rejection → revised operative construction connection → later generation through that connection**. It is incorrect to describe all inquiry as fixed merely because the full transition law or primitive command vocabulary remains fixed. These are established conditional mechanisms, with unsuccessful and misleading cases preserved.

One broader component still missing in this reviewed chain is an acquired reorganization of the **contact or question scope itself**, followed by an accountable relation between the new observations, the retained claims and the affected continuation. For example, a diagnosed sensor ambiguity would have to participate in actually altering an observation contact; the resulting readings would then need to discriminate the competing explanations under that altered coupling. A manually changed fault flag or externally selected `probe=True` does not establish this connection. Nor would adding a desired repair branch establish its derivation.

The evidential criterion is concrete: hold the relevant prehistory and available organizational resources comparable; identify the acquired distinction that mediates a later change of contact, transformation, or question-generation arrangement; show the changed arrangement is actually used; and test the retained predictions against consequences under both the changed and unmodified arrangements. A retained claim may require translation, restriction or withdrawal when the coupling changes. A richer quotient follows automatically only under its stated retained action/question laws; arbitrary interface change need not preserve those laws. The continuing problem and actual effects must remain independently identifiable, rather than being defined as whatever the new readout declares successful.

These are requirements on a further explanatory connection, not an assertion that such a connection cannot occur without an external meaning-giver. The present omission is localized to what these sources represent and enact. The origin and adequacy of the apparatus, candidate grammar and consequence-to-revision relations remain questions for the larger generative account. No conclusion about universal ethical direction or completed alignment follows from the inquiry mechanism alone.

The [integrated argument](INQUIRY-INTERFACE-ARGUMENT.md) selects a narrower next central connection: **how the consequences of inquiry form or revise its operative criterion of sufficiency**. In the current source, the candidate family and actual stopping point change, while the rule to continue until a singleton remains does not. Establishing acquired revision of that rule is distinct from repeating the already established stopping-point result. Contact formation remains a broader supporting component; it is not substituted for this selected next relation. The criterion's origin need not be represented by an endless hierarchy of additional controllers, and inserting a desired stopping rule would not explain it.
