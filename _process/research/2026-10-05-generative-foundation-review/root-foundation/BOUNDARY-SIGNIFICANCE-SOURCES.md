## 2. What this changes in the question

Three short textual anchors matter: **氣已不貫**, **猶是二物有對，以已合彼**, and **不可對此箇别有天地**. In our reconstruction, the first directs attention to a failure of participation; the latter two challenge a starting architecture that completes the terms independently and only then asks how they can meet. This is a conceptual comparison, not an identification of 氣 with cash, information or neural signals.

The resulting research question is more precise than asking whether everything connects. A part may remain anatomically or historically identifiable while its effects no longer enter a particular response. Conversely, a process outside a selected container can participate in maintaining or disturbing that response. We should examine which relations make the boundary effective, what persists when one relation fails, and how the configuration can be repaired or redirected.

This does not make current responsiveness the test of moral standing. If excluding a consequence automatically removed its significance, an organization could render every neglected patient irrelevant by maintaining its own insensitivity. The proposed comparison instead distinguishes a relation's failed uptake from the disappearance of what it concerns. Whether repair is warranted remains assessable through the actual affected histories; it cannot be inferred merely from increasing connectivity.

Nor should the source's strong unity claim be weakened into the observation that bodily parts exchange signals. Its ethical confidence and cultivation account remain substantive commitments. Our mechanism comparison explains selected conditions of participation, not why unimpeded participation necessarily realizes 仁. Keeping differentiation, consequences and legitimate refusal visible is part of assessing the larger claim.

In `free_identity_filter`, private learning remains while `g=0`; the patient's harm persists in the modeled outcome although it is absent from the warm controller's valuation. These are different ways practical participation can fail.

The classical comparison does not explain these numeric results. It changes which relation needs reconstruction: valuation, consequential influence, control and renewed support cannot be collapsed into one inside/outside label. The same continuing process can sustain production while selectively preventing the conditions under which another's consequences would affect its conduct.

## 4. The boundary of the reconstruction

The recursive model passes only `(t, cash)` to the next episode. It reconstructs episode-local learning from the supplied recipe; it does not carry a previous patient's harm record into later corrective learning. Material feedback changes what can be rebuilt. Patient-outcome learning across episodes is absent from this mechanism. This is a specified explanatory omission, not evidence that knowing and acting inhabit independent realms.

The [recipient-correction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md) and [evaluative-succession](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) branches already establish conditional recipient evidence, corrected means and a distinct successor. Their opening result accounts were checked here; no new proof review is claimed. Thus retained consequential learning is absent from this game's interface, not from the entire tree. The next test should connect an affected continuity and an actual consequence carrier to revision of practical inclusion, applicability or a retained selector, beyond choosing corrected means within a fixed helping schedule. It must preserve costs, erroneous attribution and warranted restriction. Adding a harm-memory scalar alone would not explain the affected organization or justify the revision.

The contribution is thus a reconstruction of practical boundaries as maintained relations whose failures can be discriminated. It does not establish generated bodily individuality, phenomenal suffering in the game, universal moral necessity or Cheng's full unity claim. Those limits preserve the strong proposal as something to investigate rather than silently replacing it with a weaker slogan.
