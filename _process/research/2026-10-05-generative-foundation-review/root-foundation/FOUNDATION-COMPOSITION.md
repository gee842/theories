## What the connections jointly explain

The accepted work explains a development in which **an organization changes the conditions of its own later responsiveness**. Formed access makes support available; paid repair and reconstruction alter which arrangements survive; acquired records alter commands; replacement can change the rule that updates a record; local construction changes which targets can be encountered; and paid inheritance can preserve access to acquired influence while a distinct child learns a changed recipient relation.

These are causal dependencies, not a ladder of increasingly favorable labels. A component can be formed but unusable, a predictor accurate but connected to a harmful command, a recipient model correct but excluded from evaluation, and an inherited disposition active after its original practical reason has expired. The same accounting explains why these failures are possible: components, liquidity, evidence, routing, control and inheritance are different conditions, with actual interactions between them. Their conjunction is informative only when the meanings and outputs at each interface fit.

The map uses four classifications:

- **Joined history:** the producer's actual output is consumed in the checked continuation. Where the proof uses staged isolation and an exact preparation projection, that qualification is explicit; this is not automatically one unrestricted stochastic event law.
- **Conditional transport:** a proved or argued interface carries a property when its input contract is met. It does not manufacture those inputs or supply a new physical history.
- **Separate realization:** a completed study supplies an analogous capacity or complementary contribution under another law. No direct material handoff is asserted or required merely for it to contribute.
- **Unresolved interface:** a specifically advertised next use still lacks an input or causal connection. This does not reopen the completed producer or establish an unlimited requirement for more apparatus.

The distinction is productive: an action can change the conditions that made it worth repeating. A response architecture sensitive only to efficacy can continue a once-useful response after that episode's claim is fulfilled. This is a specific difference between retaining an influential disposition and maintaining its justified applicability. It belongs to the same generative account because action transforms future evidential and practical conditions; it is not established merely by increasing memory, receipts or aggregate survival.

## 4. Common regime statement and its proper generality

The accepted results support this architecture-level statement:

> For the declared process classes, consequential activity can alter later formation, evidence, action routing and inheritance. Where their actual interfaces compose, those alterations change which responses can be acquired, used and continued. Which centers benefit depends on the separately identified consequences and practical grounds; continued private functioning does not determine that answer.

This is a synthesis of checked dependency relations, not a new theorem that all listed conditions are globally necessary or jointly sufficient for every instance of helping. Voluntary protection, for example, can bypass a normative audit in its declared action law. A different calibrated evidence channel can overcome a trial-dependent observation restriction if it is actually supplied. General [protocol completion][completion] and prefix-liquidity contracts can be applied to matching subprotocols; neither installs a controller, proves learning success nor turns a scheduler's `finished` label into physical completion.

The constructive and adverse regimes concern changes in **actor operation, recipient functioning, evidence access, effective corrective control and future reconstruction**, each over a stated horizon. Broad construction can increase encounters; filtering can remove them. Faithful evidence can correct means; late change can defeat them. Private returns can finance a child or sustain exclusion. A new child can preserve access to acquired influence or inherit error. These are mechanisms and conditional intervention contrasts, not a proof of exactly two attractors or inevitable favorable selection.

The [empirical scope synthesis][empirical] identifies materially different observed connections while preserving conventional alternatives and incomplete valuation identification. Those studies are neither successive stages of one observed lineage nor independent replications of the whole foundation. Exact conventional equivalence limits novelty claims without erasing the explanatory value of preserving formation, accounts, evidence and affected centers in one map.

The remaining synthesis work is to defend the common claim at this scope, apply its ethical premises explicitly, and use the existing evidence at its identified level. A new mechanism is required only for a retained central claim whose actual input/use remains missing. Neither requiring every contribution to occupy one hardware trajectory nor omitting concern and wisdom would improve this account.
