## The connection being advanced

Organization → acquired discrimination and retained effect → selected, materially possible activity → changed conditions of subsequent activity. This supplies a correspondence requirement for practical judgments about effects; it does not independently establish normative reasons or identify avoidance with ethical goodness.

A biological organ is an arrangement of differentiated cells whose exchange, polarity, mechanical support and signaling sustain coordinated activity. A scalar total alone omits these relations. A body is formed through developmental and physiological integration of such arrangements. Ecological participation follows contact, consumption, excretion, transmission and reciprocal changes, and crosses anatomical boundaries. A bacterium in an intestine need not thereby belong to the animal's developmental organization. These are accountable distinctions within shared activity, not independently existing domains.

The new formal model represents selected consequences of such relations. It does not derive epithelial organization or organogenesis from a primitive cell repertoire. Biological evidence supplies examples of realization; the theorem supplies exact consequences of the stated representation.

## Checked construction and its premises

[Lean source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/systems-complexity/analysis/OrganizedScaleLoop.lean) · [fresh receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/systems-complexity/analysis/organized-scale-verification.json) · [checker](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/systems-complexity/analysis/check_organized_scales.py).

A joint state records two represented intestinal regions, a contacted material patch, available and spent work units, a retained signal, contact, an ordered signal route and an opposing response drive. These coordinates are related by the event law. Retention is itself a continuing material effect in the interpretation; it is not something outside the bodily process.

Each local signal stage maps input `x` to `g*x`. `relay_product`, `relay_append` and `grouped_route` prove serial composition, including grouping consecutive stages into larger functional descriptions. A zero stage interrupts this specified route. The gains and route are supplied; this positive linear chain is not a measured connectome, an arbitrary network theorem, or proof that removing one real neuron eliminates all parallel routes. The order of multiplication is immaterial in this particular linear model.

Ingestion transfers one available patch unit to the anterior region while contact holds. Transit transfers it to the posterior region; release returns it to the represented patch. Acquisition, when funded, increases retention by anterior burden times route capacity and spends one work unit. Withdrawal requires retention at least the threshold plus opposing drive and another work unit. An approach event permits renewed contact when the opposing drive exceeds retention and support remains. A context event changes that drive. Thresholds, unit costs, the mapping of context to drive, nondecaying cumulative retention and the event schedule are explicit local assumptions.

Theorems prove, for every finite event history, conservation of the declared material count and of available-plus-spent work. These are separate bookkeeping units. Neither is a fitted metabolic energy balance; ingestion, transit, release and context change have no work charge in this local law. Material replication, digestion and microbial growth are omitted.

The four-event example begins with patch 3 and fuel 4. With gains `[1,1,1]`, it ends with anterior burden 1, patch 2, fuel 2, spent 2, retention 1 and no contact. With `[1,0,1]`, it ends with anterior burden 2, patch 1, fuel 3, spent 1, retention 0 and continuing contact. The unspent work in the second history is not an ethical advantage; the account retains distinct consequences rather than maximizing their sum.

## Biological correspondence and reanalysis

[Evidence and source limits](ORGANIZED-SCALES-EVIDENCE.md) distinguish tissue interventions, sensory learning, motor execution and shared conditions. [Reproducible numerical results](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/biological-scale-correspondence/results.json) contain 268 diameter measurements and 135 occupancy fractions. Extraction agrees cell by cell between two libraries; means and sample standard deviations agree between two numerical implementations. These checks certify extraction and calculation, not biological replication, randomization, causal identification or the accuracy of the published measurements.

The studies narrow the gap from an unspecified gut-to-activity arrow to identified cellular interventions and signaling. Their different organisms' conditions, pathogens, assays and individual histories must not be silently spliced into one trajectory. The selected data contain no matching identifiers connecting intracellular activity, retained learning, locomotion and subsequent microbial population dynamics. The Lean signal, retention and work units have no estimated calibration to these measurements.

## Substantive counterreview — same author, not independent

1. **Merely labeling coordinates as scales?** The executable law connects transfers, signal composition, retention, costs and contact, and proves a concrete view. This is stronger than a nesting diagram. It still abstracts regional burdens and signal stages; actual epithelial topology, differentiation and organogenesis are not formalized.
2. **Assuming the desired action?** The schedule requests stages, while withdrawal success is computed from acquired retention, opposing drive and remaining fuel. Failed histories are preserved. Schedule formation and the retention rule remain supplied.
3. **Could ordinary mechanisms explain the evidence?** Yes. Established physiology, coupled dynamical models and learning models can implement the same architecture. No comparative superiority or unique necessity of generative terminology follows.
4. **Does the simple relay match the biological findings exactly?** No. Positive linear gains omit inhibition, saturation, branching, oscillation and partial compensation. Published interventions support involvement under specified conditions; a zero-link theorem is not their quantitative biological effect.
5. **Does one process erase conflicts?** No. Material redistribution, remaining fuel, retained effects and contact are separately visible. Long-term pathogen survival and other affected organisms are not represented; whole ecological or ethical improvement cannot be inferred.
6. **Does successful source reanalysis close the experiment?** No. Reusing published data tests calculations. Missing joint measurements cannot be supplied by a proof or by pairing unlabelled columns across figures.
