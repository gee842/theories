# Which relation does a consequence warrant correcting?

6 October 2026. AI-authored source reconstruction under the
[nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md)
and the [conditional-universality scope](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope).
Named connection: **retained consequences → context-sensitive revision of the
relation maintained → changed activity**. No controller, transition law,
experiment or Lean declaration is added. The new source comparison below reads
three already accepted relay histories without importing or rerunning a model.

**Recommendation.** The existing tree already realizes several forms of this
connection. The next useful question is more specific: when an application
fails, what evidence identifies the relation that should be corrected? The
failure can defeat a combined prediction while leaving its sensory model or
earlier completion claim true. Changing an output, changing an application
distinction, repairing contact, replacing an inference method and revising an
undertaking have different grounds. The represented situation must preserve
those grounds rather than equate every change with correction.

## A conditional correction principle, with its constructive limit

The general restriction is elementary. Let two admitted histories have the
same accessible record R but different failed component relations. Any
deterministic diagnosis or revision selection that receives only R returns
the same result in both. If a uniquely correct fault diagnosis differs, it
cannot be correct in both. Randomization cannot give a sure distinct diagnosis
from the same input either. This is an application of the existing
[common-response and inquiry results](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md), not a new
impossibility theorem for correction in general.

The conclusion must be scoped to diagnosis. A common robust repair might work
without identifying the cause, or an action adequate in both situations might
make further diagnosis unnecessary. Withholding unsupported repeated use may
be appropriate in both under a separately justified continuation contract;
this note neither installs that response nor rules it out. At these relay
endpoints the last source packet has actually been consumed. No further
productive load is available through that unchanged source interface. This
does not imply that retaining a corrected interpretation is worthless, nor
that another physical organization could not supply later activity.

The positive counterpart is equally important. When the relevant independent
relations are established, an actual contradiction can localize a correction.
In the accepted application-refinement history, H110 remains correct for its
sensor language. Two correctly bound records `(q0,0)` and `(q1,1)` concern
states with the same current sensor output. Under a stable deterministic
application law, no output-only application map fits both. The revised
distinction is required by the declared application claim, while the sensory
achievement is preserved. The already proved three-state uniqueness lemma
additionally assumes preservation of old separations; it is not a unique
ontology or a general mandate to split every category.

Method formation has the analogous boundary. Contradiction of every generated
candidate against the retained archive requires rejecting that family as an
account of that archive, assuming faithful readings and the specified candidate
meaning. It does not select the cheapest adequate generator or certify truth
beyond the admitted class. The saved expensive-G1 and ten-state-impostor cases
show why the operative rejection criterion and warranted unrestricted use
cannot be collapsed.

Thus a substantive next derivation should identify **which observation or
intervention connects the failed continuation back to the disputed relation**,
and show that its acquired result governs a later revision while preserving
claims that remain supported. It should preserve the alternative of a common
adequate response. Merely installing “if failure, flip” would choose a repair
site and direction in advance; merely requiring every possible premise to be
perfectly certified would replace the finite causal problem with a regress.

This is one explicit interface for the conditional-universality programme:
law properties concerning truthful contact, stable addressed relations and
reachable distinguishing operations support correspondingly scoped correction
claims. Where those properties fail, the same generative explanation must
account for misdiagnosis, retention of false calibration, or inability to act.
Their adequacy and physical origin remain substantive questions. No single
recipe, implementation or score is thereby required across all realities.

The question of which undertaking is warranted is not settled by any of these
instrumental corrections. Maintaining a relay can enable a load whose wider
effects are harmful; reconstructing a carrier can displace another activity.
Whole-process ethical assessment must retain those differentiated consequences.
The current comparison improves localization of a causal or epistemic correction;
it does not derive a universal purpose or ethical ranking from failed transfer.

## Subsequent source-semantics correction: an existing channel can separate the pair

A further audit of the already accepted
[formal event law](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/remaining-dependency/formal/RemainingDependency.lean)
narrows the earlier interpretation. The fixed Python executor's off/on schedule
does not exercise every contact request admitted by that law. A claim that the
apparatus requires a newly added separating reader was too broad: a selectable
contact already exists at the general event-law level. The earlier 16-event
collision remains exact for the saved schedule and records.

`Config.channels` is a pair of contact transformations. The event `.contact r`
selects `cfg.channels.get r` independently of the relay tag in the pending
datum. The separate `.write s` decodes the datum against context one and setting
`s`. Choosing contact zero for a setting-one datum therefore does not relabel
that datum as setting zero. The
[formal correspondence notes](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/remaining-dependency/formal/FORMAL-NOTES.md)
explicitly distinguish this total requested-event law from the saved Python
scheduler. The saved-fixture translator maps `false_dependence` to faithful
channel zero and a flipping channel one; `calibration_break` has two faithful
channels. This is declared source semantics, not solely an accidental choice
of an unchecked Python integer.

Consequently the existing law admits this seven-request continuation from
each saved final state:

`command true; challenge; contact false; write true; read; commit; retain`.

It issues a new actual weak challenge at relay one, passes the correctly tagged
datum through existing channel zero, rewrites the setting-one slot, and then
uses and retains the result. It reads no private audit state. The exact source
tariffs at 34 live cells give:

Each request is funded from the actual endpoint's 428018 units; the remainder

In `false_dependence`, the actual weak-on response is zero. Channel zero passes
it unchanged, so the retained pair becomes `[0,0]` and the existing consumer

the weak-on response is one; the pair remains `[0,1]` and relay one remains

original records can be extended by a funded, already represented inquiry that
distinguishes the two failed cases and changes operative selection in one.

This does not establish that the learner chooses that inquiry, knows channel
zero to be faithful, or explicitly diagnoses a faulty channel. The channel
independence and fidelity are supplied in this law; physical identification of
that selectable access retains its own scope. The event word is a proposed
external schedule, not the saved executor spontaneously revising its tests.
The changed slot records a weak-response correction; it is not an acquired
final-load outcome. Ordinary success and broken calibration remain identical
through this new weak test, and no exhausted packet is restored. A full-load
diagnosis or resumed service does not follow.

The primary remaining connection is therefore **acquired grounds for choosing
and using a distinguishing inquiry**, together with acquisition and warranted
use of the final-load effect for calibration. Absence of all separating contact
capability is no longer the right diagnosis. This revision preserves the
earlier insufficiency of the fixed record, the possibility of common adequate
responses without fault localization, and every completed learning result.
