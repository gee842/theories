# Generated self: primary comparators and the remaining derivation

6 October 2026. AI-authored research for the connection **generative organization → a usable self/world distinction**, serving the [ethical-minimum investigation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) and the [nondual grounding question](NONDUAL-GROUNDING-QUESTION.md). Ren's claim that “I” arises within the generative account is the hypothesis investigated; the interpretation and arguments below are AI elaboration. This is selected primary-source reading and conceptual synthesis, not a new experiment, a systematic literature review or a completed consciousness theory.

## 1. What the research can presently support

The productive direction is to explain the organization of a distinction before treating its familiar first-person form as foundational. A self-model may identify a continuing center of sensation and action; it need not be operated by another self concealed behind it. However, three claims require separate assessment: self-representation is constructed; experience has no independently existing substantial owner; and the subject/object distinction is never fundamental. A successful mechanism for the first would leave the stronger ontological claims to further argument.

“Generative model” also has two meanings here. Ren's governing project seeks an account of how organization, experience and evaluation arise within reality. A neural generative model is a much narrower candidate mechanism: organized neural activity predicts causes of sensory input. The latter can be an object explained within the former. Equating them would substitute one supplied neural architecture for the universal foundation being sought.

The named connection is therefore divided into organization, practical partition, experienced ownership and reflective identity. This prevents a finding about one transition from prematurely closing the others. The governing [derivation map](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#the-derivation-and-the-existing-work) remains the framework into which a tested mechanism must fit.

## 2. Four distinct explanatory targets

The following are analytical distinctions for this study, not a source-established developmental sequence.

| Target | What needs explanation | What would not suffice |
|---|---|---|
| Self-maintaining organization | Processes that renew a differentiated organization and regulate consequential conditions | Merely drawing a boundary around a system |
| Bodily self-model and ownership | Representing a body or capacity as belonging to this organized center; using that representation | Showing that a body exists, or that an observer can identify it |
| Minimal perspectival experience | There being an actual situated undergoing, whether or not articulated as “mine” | An internal state estimate, classifier label or behavioral report alone |
| Narrative “I” | A temporally extended identity involving memory, interpretation, commitments and social recognition | A momentary ownership judgment or action-correspondence calculation |

These targets may interact recursively. A formed self-model can alter attention, bodily regulation and subsequent learning. That causal feedback does not make the model an unconditioned origin. Conversely, a maintained organization need not already have an autobiographical identity. Explanatory dependence should not be confused with a single chronological story in which one completed layer appears before all others.

## 3. Three primary readings

The source summaries below are deliberately bounded. Detailed source-derived content is confined to these cards; subsequent sections develop the repository's own proposed reconstruction and tests. Reading dates are 6 October 2026. The cited original texts remain available at their source locations; this note is not an archival import of their bytes.

### Apps and Tsakiris: a proposal for learned self-recognition

Matthew A. J. Apps and Manos Tsakiris (2014), *The free-energy self: A predictive coding account of self-recognition*, *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 41, 85–97. [Accepted manuscript](https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/ws/files/16919653/Apps_Tsakiris_Free_energy_Accepted_version.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2013.01.029).

**Read:** §§3–5.3 and 7–8; especially PDF pages 9–15, 20–24 and 31–33, whose printed manuscript pagination is one lower. The proposed mechanism learns cross-sensory regularities and represents bodily identity probabilistically; contextual expectations can alter attribution. Mirror recognition illustrates acquisition rather than requiring prior knowledge of visual appearance. This is an original theoretical account, not new experimental evidence. Its caveats explicitly acknowledge little direct evidence then for self-related prediction/error processing and missing direct tests of probabilistic self-representation. Its explanatory object is self-recognition, not the origin of organisms or consciousness. Those historical caveats must not be recast as a statement about the entire literature in 2026.

### Seth, Suzuki and Critchley: presence and agency need separate explanations

Anil K. Seth, Keisuke Suzuki and Hugo D. Critchley (2012), *An Interoceptive Predictive Coding Model of Conscious Presence*, *Frontiers in Psychology* 2:395. [Full primary article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00395/full), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00395.

**Read:** “An Interoceptive Predictive-Coding Model,” “Predictive coding in the AIC,” “Testing the Model,” and “Summary.” This hypothesis article links presence to informative interoceptive prediction, not merely zero error or absent input. Agency and presence are modeled as interacting but neither necessary nor sufficient for each other. The authors distinguish behavioral from phenomenal measurement and identify direct neural tests still needed at publication. They explicitly leave fuller treatment of selfhood for extension. Its value here is a proposed mechanism and discriminating distinctions, not an experimentally established equation between inference, experience and an “I.”

### Suzuki and colleagues: ownership changes under controlled feedback

Keisuke Suzuki, Sarah N. Garfinkel, Hugo D. Critchley and Anil K. Seth (2013), *Multisensory integration across exteroceptive and interoceptive domains modulates self-experience in the rubber-hand illusion*, *Neuropsychologia* 51, 2909–2917. [Original full article supplied through an author's ResearchGate profile](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Sarah-Garfinkel/publication/256330406_Multisensory_integration_across_exteroceptive_and_interoceptive_domains_modulates_self-experience_in_the_rubber-hand_illusion/links/00b7d52c80d3072780000000/Multisensory-integration-across-exteroceptive-and-interoceptive-domains-modulates-self-experience-in-the-rubber-hand-illusion.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2013.08.014).

**Read:** §§2–4, printed pages 2910–2916, including questionnaire and figures. In 21 participants, heartbeat-synchronous visual feedback on a stationary virtual hand increased ownership ratings and proprioceptive drift relative to asynchronous feedback; drift averaged 3.16 versus 1.02 cm. Cardiac synchrony did not produce a significant difference in the movement condition. Cardiac-condition correlations with individual interoceptive sensitivity were nonsignificant. The experiment manipulates attribution within existing experiencing adults. Drift is a localization measure; neither it nor an ownership report alone establishes the origin of subjectivity or uniquely selects predictive coding over other integration mechanisms.

## 4. A constructive route without an internal observer

The following is a candidate explanatory construction, not an implemented model or additional finding of the papers.

Start with a persisting organization that can undergo changes, retain some consequences and affect some subsequent events. Do not initialize a variable labeled “the true self.” Instead, specify several distinguishable signal streams and transformations with actual causal access. Recurrent intervention can reveal which changes reliably accompany a command, which continue without it, which precede breakdown and which are correctable through available capacities. Those regularities can support a learned grouping useful for regulation.

For example, suppose a perturbation changes one set of accessible signals and its later consequences are recurrently altered by one action channel. A retained model can begin predicting their joint trajectory. A different signal cluster may vary independently and respond to a different channel. The model need not first decide whose signals they are to learn this difference. A practical distinction between directly regulated organization and less directly controlled surroundings can become one product of learning.

Calling a learned grouping “self-related” is justified only insofar as the grouping performs the relevant work: forecasts consequences, guides correction, supports transfer and updates when relations change. The English label does none of that causal work. The relevant mechanism consists of state transitions and their consequences. No further spectator has to examine the representation before its state can affect a response.

This would at most explain a functional partition. To derive experienced ownership requires an independently warranted account of how that mechanism relates to an actual undergoing. To derive a narrative “I” requires additional capacities and histories. Neither transition is completed by enlarging the classifier's vocabulary. Nor does a correct self-model imply complete self-knowledge: it may represent only those relations its organization can detect and use.

A successful partition can also be revisable without being fictitious. The distinction can track real dependencies under some conditions and fail under others. Formation and reality are compatible: a river channel is formed and nonetheless redirects water. The analogous claim here is limited to causal organization; the analogy itself supplies no theory of experience.

## 5. What remains supplied, and how to expose it

The proposed construction presently supplies distinguishable channels, a capacity for retained modification, an intervention repertoire, initial organization and some workable relation between consequences and retention. Each is an outstanding explanatory interface to the broader foundation. A designer's reward for “identify your body correctly” would supply precisely the classification being explained. Likewise, a training dataset already divided into self and world would demonstrate supervised reproduction of the partition, not its endogenous acquisition.

An informative implementation must expose the source of its learning criterion. If a classifier is retained because it improves actual reconstruction, the causal link from prediction to reconstruction must be specified and tested. If it is retained because an analyst likes its score, that is an external selection process. Both can be studied; they answer different questions. Biological plausibility cannot be obtained merely by naming an optimization objective “survival.”

Three counterexamples make the task less trivial. First, a remote instrument can be highly controllable without becoming part of a body. Controllability alone cannot settle ownership. Second, synchronized environmental signals can share a cause without belonging to one organism. Correlation alone cannot supply individuation. Third, two interacting organizations can exchange resources while retaining different histories and vulnerabilities. Mutual dependence alone cannot erase their differentiation.

The next test should therefore contrast learned causal grouping with a fixed boundary, a correlation-only rule and an externally supervised labeler. Change the controllability and coupling independently; test novel interventions and losses of access. Require evidence that the partition improves an actual regulatory capacity under declared costs. Record cases where the most predictive grouping differs from an anatomical boundary. These are proposed acceptance tests, not experimental results already obtained.

## 6. What follows for the ethical question

The research removes one unnecessary starting demand: a theory need not explain all significance as a decision made by a primitive owner. The [nondual note](NONDUAL-GROUNDING-QUESTION.md) can therefore ask about adverse occurrence and response without first establishing a metaphysically independent “I.” This is an explanatory opening, not yet the normative bridge.

The sentence “good and evil do not depend on I” has a defensible target if it denies that first-person ownership, self-interest or endorsement creates moral force. It has a different and much stronger meaning if it denies that the actual character of experience, situated capacities or consequences matters. The current ethical minimum needs the former direction while continuing to investigate the latter dependencies. Removing an ownership label should not be confused with removing an actual burden.

A constructed self-world boundary does not establish that every boundary is morally irrelevant. Consent, accumulated exhaustion and the ability to undertake a worthwhile activity concern continuing capacities and consequences. Their justification can be reconstructed without permanent substances, but it cannot simply be deleted. Equally, no ownership classifier can determine whose suffering counts by assigning or withholding a “mine” label.

A destructive organization could acquire accurate self-models and exploit others effectively. Such a case would challenge an inference from successful self-generation to necessary benevolence. It would not by itself refute a reason for relief that the organization ignores. The distinction between the existence of a reason and the causal formation of responsiveness to it must remain explicit.

## 7. Result and next unresolved bridge

The selected comparators make a generated self-representation a serious mechanistic research direction. They leave open the ontological status of a subject, the origin of perspectival experience and the derivation of narrative identity. They also leave the moral force of suffering and relief for direct argument. No favorable ethical direction follows merely from the adjective “generative.”

The next central connection is **formed organization → noncircular acquisition of a usable partition**, connected backward to its supplied retention and intervention machinery and forward to the identification of experiential adversity. A result showing useful acquisition without supplied ownership labels would strengthen the proposal. Failure under independent interventions would reveal that the apparent partition was a classifier convention or an exploitable correlation. A result requiring prior perspectival organization would locate a deeper premise to explain, rather than authorize its removal by terminology.

The ethical continuation should keep occurrence, access and consequences fixed while varying ownership descriptions, then separately vary real continuity and burden. That separation is the immediate way to test Ren's insight while preserving plural centers within one generative account.
