## From material to broader access

[ExpandingInquiry.lean](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/ExpandingInquiry.lean) extends the existing four-role installation and three readers. At depth zero each reader has its local support. A supplied parent map at each further level determines that support's parent. Roots at a deeper level are obtained by composition. Consequently, readers that already share a cause cannot become separate merely by following its parents farther upstream.

The installation initially has depth-zero access and no loose adapters. Fabrication converts two material units into one adapter. Extension consumes that adapter and one further material unit, connects the next parent-following test path, increases reachable depth by one, and clears the old effect records. Without an adapter, extension is disabled. The parent map is semantic structure: neither fabrication nor the admission gate reads its hidden identifiers. Its relation becomes visible through the effect of a pulse at the newly installed endpoint.

The adapter and connection law are explicit local modeling inputs. A constant construction cost, indefinitely repeatable manufacture, permission to extend, and a stable available parent connection are not consequences of generativity in general. The model abstracts engineering dimensions, distance, wear of installed paths and formation of the manufacturing ability. Access is materially accounted and changes which intervention can occur, but this is not a verified hardware design or a derivation of arbitrary invention.

Each round performs two ordinary cycles, fabrication and extension, then a three-position survey. Before each pulse, two ordinary cycles replenish material and restore all four support stocks to three. A pulse interrupts the tested readers and reduces every plant stock by one. Ordinary work is disabled while the pulse is held. Observation forms the outage record, and restoration releases the pulse and executes the inherited support cycle. These actions therefore share the installation's actual capacity; testing is not insulated from its support conditions.

## Preserving the means to finish a test

Preserving stocks above a floor is insufficient by itself. A pulse could leave the installation established yet unable to afford restoration. `unreserved_pulse_would_strand_restoration` exhibits this: starting with twelve material units, the raw pulse leaves eleven, enough for the represented equipment floor but insufficient for the restoration event's additional unit. The implemented protocol refuses that start.

The protocol instead requires fourteen material units before a pulse, thirteen before another observation, and twelve before restoration. This reserves the three units for interruption, observation and release while retaining the eleven-unit operating reserve. Each allowed event preserves the needed restoration reserve when a pulse is held. `interrupted_history_can_restore` proves that restoration remains executable after any permitted inquiry history starting with the invariant and reserve. Repeated observations cannot spend the reserved last unit. This is restoration availability, not fairness: the theorem does not force a scheduler to execute it.

Pulse duration is abstracted as the specified one-unit stock loss, and observation has its stated material cost without additional stock wear. An unbounded delay or a broken restoration path is outside this guarantee. The supply law resumes with the inherited restoration cycle. These timing and delivery conditions must hold for an application.

## Repeated expansion and bounded warrant

`expansion_continues` proves an actual `Generative.Trace`, preserved support and restored pulse status for **every finite number of rounds**. `expansion_reach` proves that the reachable depth increases by precisely that number. This is a parameterized Lean induction, not an enumeration of a few horizons. `expanded_records_complete` and `acquired_at_reach` connect the completed survey to the actual newly reached dependencies. All earlier records are invalidated at extension; certification resumes from the new effects.

Ordinary work and restoration reuse the existing physical operation and carrier replacement. Fabrication, installation and interruption are new material-consuming events in the same process. The successful repeated trace does not require an inexhaustible initial stock: recurrent supply finances the next round. Continuing supply and feasible construction remain assumptions. There is no general finite-resource perpetuity claim.

A useful positive result follows from the nested dependency law. If readers remain separate at tested depth `h`, they were separate at each lower level `d ≤ h`; a lower merger would persist to `h`. `bounded_gate_sound` therefore warrants a two-report certificate against one common-root fault at **any covered lower level**, provided unaffected readers report the actual proposition. `bounded_gate_available` retains availability when those unaffected readers respond. The gate reads current effect records, pulse status and reports. The proof's actual fault level is not secretly handed to that gate.

This is a single common-root report fault within the covered range, not arbitrary simultaneous failures at different levels. The nested hierarchy is restrictive: it does not represent every overlapping dependency graph. A separation certificate also does not follow merely from reaching a depth; the observed effect matrix must actually separate the readers. A shared cause can correctly make the certificate unavailable.

## The expanded evidence changes an actual response

The concrete example has separate local supports and a common parent one level higher. The local survey admits an all-yes majority under its local interpretation. After an adapter is manufactured and installed, the broader survey reveals the shared parent and blocks that same majority from licensing withdrawal.

`expansion_to_response_is_actual` connects construction, the new survey, one permitted withdrawal from full stocks, the common-root misleading reports, and the corrective response in one trace. The intervening withdrawal leaves each stock at two. The revised gate selects the supporting fallback and restores all four to three. Acting on the raw majority would withdraw again and leave stocks at one; `actual_corrective_effect` preserves this failure comparison. The plant is not reset between inquiry and response.

The common-root fault now lies within the reached depth, so the generic response guarantee applies. Before extension it lay outside the local certificate's premise. The earlier certificate was not a universal guarantee that the new observation somehow contradicted; it had a narrower scope.

## What continued inquiry can and cannot settle

For every finite depth `n`, compare two explicitly constructed towers. One keeps the three supports separate at every level. The other has the same structure through `n`, but merges them immediately beyond it. `every_finite_record_has_hidden_extension` proves that their complete pulse-effect records through `n` are identical. The proof gives the classifier the entire finite effect table, more information than the current retained survey alone.

`no_finite_certificate_of_unrestricted_separation` then rules out a classifier that both accepts this finite record as complete separation and is sound about separation at **all levels of every admitted tower**. This is a limit for the stated open family and evidence channel. It is not a claim about every conceivable measurement or theory of causation.

The positive counterpart matters: `continued_expansion_exposes_each_hidden_level` proves that the merger placed just beyond any finite `n` is revealed after `n+1` rounds. Every particular hidden merger in this family can be reached. What the finite record cannot supply is a warranted announcement that no such merger remains anywhere beyond it. Continued learnability and detectable completion are different properties.

**The stopping boundary is now precise:** within this open hierarchy, construction and inquiry can keep expanding without producing a final certificate from finite effects alone. A justified restriction on the relevant failure range, independent evidence of terminal structure, or a different observation relation could change that conclusion. Choosing and warranting such a practical range is still a substantive connection; appending another successful finite survey does not discharge it.

Unrestricted separation is not a prerequisite for ordinary justified action, and it is not a desirable ontology of independent things. The theory remains relational and interconnected. The bounded soundness and availability results already support action under stated relevant faults. The unmet claim is only the stronger inference from finite testing to unlimited coverage. No assumption-free causal completeness requirement is imposed on the whole meta-attractor programme.

For “sliding into” a regime, this yields a candidate diagnostic distinction: repeated agreement within existing tests, the capacity to construct a more discriminating test, and the capacity to restore activity afterward should be assessed separately. Loss of either construction access or restoration reserve can close an investigative route before current reports change. The formal examples establish these conditional mechanisms; they do not provide an empirical warning score or prove that selection inevitably suppresses inquiry.

## Primary comparisons and proof record

**Eberhardt (2008), [Almost Optimal Intervention Sets for Causal Discovery](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~fehardt/papers/Eberhardt_UAI2008.pdf).** The introduction and opening model assumptions were reread. Identification depends on the model family and available interventions; the cited complete-space bound assumes causally sufficient variables and ideal independence information. This supports keeping the coverage restriction explicit. Our nested support model proves no improvement to his discovery algorithm or general graph bound.

**Choo, Shiragur and Uhler (2024), [Causal Discovery under Off-Target Interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08229).** The primary abstract was read. It explicitly models the difference between an attempted intervention and the random set actually affected. That distinction exposes a retained premise here: a constructed path must deliver the specified pulse. Our deterministic delivery and restored-support proofs do not address their stochastic off-target model, and no comparative performance claim is made.
