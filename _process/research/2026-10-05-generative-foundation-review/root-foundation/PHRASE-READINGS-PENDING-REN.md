### 6. D13 · “not harm”

**Current AI reading** ([D13](DELEGATED-CHOICES.md#d13-不损--not-harm)).

- It is neither a filter on which continuations have significance nor a claim of one continuation on others. It is a relation between the levels with and without a part.
- Its boundary is the margin: a part loses a continuation exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin (`Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`). This is the form of the “phase transition with a clear boundary”.

**Reason.**
- As a filter, it would cost nearly every life its significance, since almost all life continues by ending other life.
- As a claim on others, it would add a premise of claims (relations, not rights).
- Retaining adds no premise under the continuation condition, and its boundary is the phase transition.
- The stepwise reading would count a preemptive drawer as harmless (`Preemption.not_undermining_yet_loses`).

**Would change it.** Reading “not harm” as a constraint on acts rather than a comparison of courses.
