# Necessity attempt: identify the full ground of the reason

6 October 2026. AI-authored response to Ren's request to try to solve universal ethical necessity, following the [current warrant inquiry](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md) and [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The named connection is **actual adverse undergoing and an accessible, otherwise permissible transformation → a contributory reason for an unrelated, uncommitted responder**.

**Result: the attempted necessity proof does not close.** It supplies a constrained argument against a general adoption requirement, and identifies the exact full-ground claim that a self-relief argument would need. These are substantive scope findings within the positive investigation. They neither prove the restrictive alternative nor show that ethics requires an ingredient outside generative reality.

## 1. The positive argument attempted

The [argument](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-ARGUMENT.md) begins with one unfolding in which experiencing, understanding and possible response differentiate. The same activity can form protective boundaries, caring practices and exploitative purposes. Their actual emergence does not, by itself, settle their justification.

For this attempt, grant the earlier experiential identification: a specified severe, continuing, still-unwanted episode has an adverse aspect for the being undergoing it. Identify an accessible transformation that genuinely relieves that aspect, preserves the affected continuity and uses otherwise permissible means. These concessions isolate the practical-scope question; they are not new derivations of experience or of its local significance.

The favorable judgment remains that what relief actually accomplishes can justify choosing it before helping is an adopted end. The response's point concerns the changed undergoing, not a newly assigned reward, the observer's comfort or the successful reproduction of a practice. This judgment explains how concern can be worth developing. It can also explain correcting an ineffective response and relinquishing an end that requires continuing distress.

The closing attempt then uses the strongest autonomy objection against a blanket exclusion:

1. If another person's consent and practical independence give a stranger reasons against coercing them, some reasons concerning another already apply without adopted concern.
2. Therefore lack of adoption cannot be a general prerequisite for all such reasons.
3. Where the stated protections are preserved, the rationale against interference does not itself exclude every favorable contribution from permissible relief.
4. Therefore actual relief supplies that contribution.

Steps 1–3 constrain a rival that accepts the first premise. **Step 4 does not follow from them without a further positive account of the ground.** Removing one proposed exclusion is insufficient to establish the reason's existence. A strict egoist can reject step 1; a more serious autonomy rival can accept it and defend an assistance-specific restriction. This attempt does not reclassify failure to help as causing the original suffering.

## 2. Why the restrictive account remains a substantive rival

The rival can treat a differentiated life's authority against interference as real, while treating positive practical projects as matters for its author. It can grant actual suffering, real relief, accurate understanding, special obligations and optional charity. Its claim is that the benefit does not give this uncommitted stranger a positive moral reason before an appropriate relationship or undertaking exists.

This proposed jurisdiction forms within the same reality. No substantial soul, independent ethical realm or factual ignorance is required. Nevertheless, its normative authority is not established by its causal formation. Protecting a life from compelled sacrifice does not prove that every optional benefit is reasonless. The rival still owes an account of the stronger exclusion, including whether admitting contributory favor necessarily brings an objectionable demand for answerability.

The direct account gives a positive explanation of concern's point; it does not win merely because this study has not accepted the rival's justification. Equally, the rival's consistency does not vindicate its restriction. Necessity requires a warranted account of the relation, not agreement with a preferred notation or a contradiction obtained by defining adequate agents as caring.

## 3. The own-suffering route exposes the precise additional claim

The [review](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-REVIEW.md) distinguishes three propositions:

- The episode is genuinely adverse for the experiencing being.
- There can be a reason for self-relief without a prior self-care project or reflective ownership judgment.
- The complete ground of that reason contains only the adverse undergoing and eligible transformation; a self-relation adds no constitutive condition.

The first two do not establish the third. A reason can be independent of recognition while still involving a real relation. Saying that relief matters because an experience hurts need not enumerate the whole ground of its practical address. A rival can propose that the ground jointly includes adversity and formed continuity. It has then offered a different constitutive explanation, rather than conceded a complete ground and appended an arbitrary gate afterward.

If the third proposition were independently warranted, restricting that same baseline ground to self-related response positions would add content excluded from the established ground. That conditional consequence is sound. The earlier [root attempt](NONINSTRUMENTAL-ROOT-ARGUMENT.md) already showed why replacing universal concern with anchor and transport premises does not reduce its normative content. This round applies that discipline to the proposed self-relief anchor; it is not a new operator-transport theorem.

Removing a fundamental owner, exchanging names or imagining oneself undergoing the episode does not establish the required completeness. The generative formation of selves leaves real continuities that a rival may argue are normatively significant. The task is to assess that significance, not erase differentiation to obtain the desired conclusion.

## 4. The nondual source comparison narrows the target

The [focused primary-source continuation](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-SOURCES.md) distinguishes equal altruistic obligation from the weaker existence of some contributory reason. A criticism of equal concern does not establish zero concern. The inspected Harris discussion also entertains moderate benevolence alongside special concern under an ownerless-stream account; that possibility is conditional, not a proof of our ethical minimum.

This matters to the present attempt: universal scope need not mean identical strength, interchangeable lives, compulsory service or maximization of one total. The candidate preserves those distinctions. Conversely, its weaker target still needs a positive reason; merely rejecting equalization does not supply one. Historical comparison can clarify the inference but cannot lend its authority as a derived result of this foundation.

## 5. What would actually solve the remaining connection

The task is now to vindicate one of the competing accounts of the ground: does the actual adversity and the available transformation supply a baseline practical reason, with continuity and role explaining further priority and responsibility; or is such a relationship necessary for the baseline reason to exist at all? These are competing accounts of differentiated relations within one unfolding.

The favorable account remains our defended candidate. Universal necessity would require independently establishing its constitutive sufficiency and applicable scope. A justified exclusion would instead require narrowing it. Further source testimony, a helping controller or a rephrasing of the inference would not discharge that obligation by itself.

This is a completed attempt with an unsuccessful closure step, not completion of the governing goal. The broader programme's conditional universality across independently specified law classes remains separate from both this ethical ground and a uniquely necessary ontology. No new scientific run or formal proof is claimed.
