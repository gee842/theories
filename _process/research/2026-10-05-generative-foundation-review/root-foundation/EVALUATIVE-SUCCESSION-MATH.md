The [prospective version][prospective] is preserved unchanged. The [protocol][protocol], [literal interface][interface] and independent oracle were frozen before implementation at commit `85fb419ccdf1ccf7d3120fa1c99bbcf178bc179f`, following accepted evaluative uptake at `23148d6692effd99185f8afd769423fb2d26a517`. ## 1. The actual predecessor and the new law

The selected predecessor is `EvaluationWitness.final`, with its actual staged ancestry. The donor has completed its teaching and two evaluation opportunities. Its strict endpoint has recipient60, native source6, no usable actor fuel, no spare pair, a locked old bank and a finished port. The new construction must extend this exact endpoint. It must not initialize a favorable child and merely attach an old history as a label.

The declared nine-pair kit supplies a new data carrier, a new RI controller, four frame cells, a bounded window controller, a one-use evaluation port and a retained-support/reader-binding adapter. These are declared finite recipes. Their functionality and capacities do not follow from the pair count alone. The new child can acquire and revise its own q/qualification; the donor's q and retired controller cannot change or act after activation. Access to retained n carries acquired influence without claiming that the child experienced the donor's private losses.

The old `FormedRecipientRoute` copier supplies an actual-read/commit proof pattern. Its route/q template, recipient-paid process and known-availability zero decoder do not constitute the proposed RI handoff. The new handoff copies the actual bounded q as an **unqualified candidate**, not a current publication, action token, authority or correct model of the child's private conditions. The actor bit remains historically indexed to the original actor and is never queried for child-directed work.

## 2. Exact ownership and energy decomposition

For an inherited `AcquiredEvaluation.State s`, abbreviate:

- `f := s.parent`, the `FreshRevision.State`;
- `a := f.parent`, the `RecipientAction.State`;
- `r := a.reasons` and `c := RecipientAction.core a`.

Expanding the accepted definitions gives the energy decomposition used by `EvaluativeSuccession.entry_accounts`:

```text
AcquiredEvaluation.energy(s)
 = c.hardware.fuel + c.patient + 10*c.packets + c.escrow
 + r.reader.world.budget + r.reader.world.escrow
 + r.reader.fuel + r.reserve
 + a.commandReserve + a.readerReserve + a.sourceReserve
 + f.bank.budget + f.bank.escrow
 + f.actorReserve + f.readerReserve + f.sourceReserve
 + s.training + s.source0 + s.source1 + s.source2
 + s.later + s.laterSource.
```

Thus preserving only donor actor fuel and old packets is insufficient in a general handoff. Both historical writer budgets/escrows, reader fuel, reason reserve and every protected parent reserve need custody. Retired snapshots containing these numbers cannot also be summed as an independently owned module energy.

**The revised protocol/interface adopts the full quarantine rule:** move every unused old monetary compartment into separately typed inert custody at retirement, including old reader fuel and reason reserve. Preserve the native source compartments separately under their old isolation rule. Move the one physical reader/workspace to the child, cold-clear its active cache/publication at paid binding, and fund child reading only from the new designated24. Old cash remains owned and counted but cannot silently subsidize a child shortage. This resolves the first draft's open balance-custody choice. `entry_accounts`, `step_energy` and `trace_energy` verify the accounting; the actual retirement/binding witness verifies its reached use.

For an admitted donor, the core is ready with zero core escrow; both writer phases are idle with zero writer escrow; native teaching is closed; and no candidate, command, evaluation or reader payment is pending. The implemented `QuietReader` admits either a genuinely unused cold cycle (inactive, no token, next0, accessible cache indices0–2 empty, cold/empty reason workspace with no publication), or structural completion (active factual reader, a token present, next3, reason phase9 and a publication present). The completed branch deliberately does **not** use `PaidArchiveReader.Complete`, which would add old token/current-context equality absent from the frozen admission law. Neither branch requires a positive mask or a correct old command. The serialized model classifies omitted fields from actual history; this is not an invented positive-publication test. Interrupted donors keep their whole state and obligations outside the admitted continuation.

After retirement the global energy is counted directly as:

```text
one shared patient
+ child fuel + 10*child allocated packets + child feedback escrow
+ new writer budget + new writer escrow
+ one live reader fuel + one live reason reserve
+ all remaining new protected common/source compartments
+ all separately retained old inert common/source compartments
+ the three retained native source compartments.
```

Raw source remainders are counted and cannot pay common-fuel bills. Transfers subtract from their originating compartments. In particular, do not add donor RI.energy to child RI.energy: both views could contain the same patient. Likewise, retained donor wrappers cannot expose an alternate live `.reason`, `.read`, `.fund` or actuation path. Their historical publications may exist in an analyst's catalog; they are not operational inputs. Before retirement there is one live donor reader; afterward there is one live child reader. A proof lens may reconstruct a nested ancestor state, but that lens creates neither a second reader nor another balance.

The material invariant belongs to this declared 60-unit retained architecture and counts its nine new pairs once across held and occupied custody. The actual preparation/projection proves that the reached inherited donor has 60 units; this does not assign a 60-unit footprint to every arbitrary `AcquiredEvaluation.State`:

```text
old retained matter + new free A + new free H
  + 2*(new carrier + controller + frame cells + window + port + access) = 78.
```

Assembly changes custody and spends one fuel; it does not destroy the installed pair. A missing component remains in its declared unavailable custody. A pending-copy latch belongs to the supplied child controller and is not another free carrier.

## 4. Implemented minimal architecture

The checked package uses three modules:

1. `SuccessionPreparation`: the enlarged M78 formation/assembly projection, protected nine pairs and common66/source30, and an exact accounting frame around the accepted actual donor history.
2. `EvaluativeSuccession`: the new finite handoff/access law, one live core and reader, account/material invariants, actual archive/reader projections and general selected-use conclusions.
3. `SuccessionWitness`: one composed actual continuation from `EvaluationWitness.final`, together with scope-relevant boundary witnesses. The stochastic mixture remains a separate independently checked model.

The interface uses a direct record containing one RI core, fresh-bank fields, retained native support, retired donor payload/material, old-account quarantine, new components/reserves and one reader/reason workspace. `PaidRecipientReasons.State Unit` is sufficient for the maintained reader: its generic laws do not need another stored physical world. An archive lens combines the actual RI core with the new bank when applying the accepted producer laws. The old archives are retained records and immutable metadata, not a second active reader or core. At candidate write the donor becomes retired; before the later paid access binding, a historical old publication may still be stored, but no child-current predicate or event may use it.

The event law retains literal interface guards rather than silently importing a different ancestor guard. In particular, the built idle evaluation port on a ready funded child can pay its one-unit read attempt even when support is unknown or n is inaccessible; that attempt consumes the port instead of yielding a renewable free retry or n=0. A successful assessment requires renewed access and current agreement with child q. Permission/delivery can block inquiry or work without removing facts or learned influence. Reader progression after bank locking is independent of a stalled RI core and cannot repair that core. Partial reader/reason payment never grants a complete publication.

The proof implementation uses existing lemmas at their natural scope: RI validity/energy and receipt identification; `RecipientArchive` actual-emission soundness; generic paid reader/reason invariants; the bounded shared threshold; and the existing command/effect argument. Reconstructing a full nested `AcquiredEvaluation.State` solely to call one lemma is optional. It must not add runtime structure or conceal mismatched tariffs, access guards or ownership. The implemented `State` contains no nested live `AcquiredEvaluation` or `FreshRevision` machine. Historical records are immutable provenance, not alternative spendable worlds.

## 5. General interfaces and the actual composed conclusion

General conditional lemmas establish exact new-bank, paid-reader and selected-action projections; conservation; retained native/history data; actual emitted-record factual soundness; initialized consumer assurance; and selected same-recipient effect under explicit observation-to-work stability. They require the traces and initialization premises stated in their types. `SuccessionWitness.actual_staged_composition` and `actual_effect_from_fresh_evidence` supply and join those inputs for the actual reached donor and selected continuation. This is not one unrestricted theorem about every structurally admitted donor or every numerical case. Its physical consequences have **no ethical G premise**:

1. The child retains actual access to the donor's learned n and its ancestry, with no new native lessons assumed.
2. New facts and the current supported command come from the child-produced bank; old qualified q and publications supply no current certificate.
3. The paid assessment chooses help/abstain exactly from the declared route and retained n, without rewriting q, facts or patient stock. Shared and filtered controls agree on those inputs until evaluation.
4. An actually enabled full selected trial delivers10 to the **same** recipient, preserving the historical actor bit and the retired donor controller.
5. Every prefix preserves material and satisfies energy-after plus actual fees/waste equals energy-before. Misses, partial writes and interrupted phases retain their real consequences.

The separate `warranted_instrumental_meaning` corollary assumes a warranted G and its application premises. `PublicationBridge.initialized_trace_assured` is generic in both the reader world and G: instantiating it with `True` yields the technical compatible-command result; instantiating it with the independently supported G attaches that ground to the current instrumental meaning. G is never a fuel, birth, access, admission, assessment or receipt guard. An actual physical receipt neither proves G nor needs G in order to occur.

No theorem may require n=7, a correct donor command or an old positive publication as a structural handoff premise. The concrete witness can derive n=7 from the accepted actual teaching history. A comparison with an actual n=0 donor derives that value from its own recorded history; it is not an equal-private-history intervention.

The child learns new means while retaining acquired evaluative influence. These results do not revise ethical standing, justify G or demonstrate the child's own private affective learning. Probe receipts and the evaluation-selected receipt must remain distinct in the conclusion and interpretation. A probe may satisfy a separately interpreted claim before selected use; another resource delivery is not proof that another unmet need exists.

## 6. Checked arithmetic and failure boundaries

The actual preparation uses M78/common360/source230, total590. Its extension of the accepted476 is formation18 plus protected services66 plus source30. The service account is assembly9, candidate read/write2, two probes16, writer5, reader/reasons24, evaluation2 and selected use8. Unknown initialization belongs to the paid new-child recipe; calling the old reset separately would add its existing two-unit charge.

`SuccessionWitness.complete_accounts` verifies the strict changed-law account:

```text
590 = 410 accepted historical expense/loss + 18 new formation
    + 66 new service + 10 failed-probe packet + 86 retained,
86 = recipient80 + isolated native source6.
```

The account comes from the enabled joined trace and generic energy theorem. `spent` includes the failed packet, so its new-history total is76 =66 services+10 loss. The actual milestone assertions verify ordered guards and separate compartments, not only the final sum.

The formal controls establish fixed probe/action capacity, recipient-only targets, cut-only guard changes, matched factual/core/native inputs under filtering, separation of permission from reasons, a paid unknown-n read and after-read access invalidation. The generic normal-cost conventional table equals the shared threshold on n≤7; this is not a learning-necessity result. The full 29-config experiment additionally covers alternate actual donors, both command orientations, restricted funding, outages, shortages, stale substitution and scope/permission/delivery interventions. Those finite numerical histories and their independent reconstruction retain their own scope.

Review found and preserved two early source-law defects: a probe authorization could permit more than one completed RI operation, and external guard events allowed free restoration. The final `probeRunning`/archive-flush separation and false-only cut guards repair them. Proof-development failures and interrupted elaborations are retained. Generic reader and route-frame lemmas resolved costly closed-history normalization without changing the event law or replacing actual states with configured fixtures.
