# Prospective formal contract for evaluative succession

6 October 2026. AI-authored **design contract, not a proved result**. No Lean implementation, compiler run or new numerical outcome is reported here. This note reviews the [draft protocol][protocol], [literal interface][interface], [prospective independent oracle][oracle] and [scope contract](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION-SCOPE.md), following accepted evaluative uptake at `23148d6692effd99185f8afd769423fb2d26a517`. The capacity, event law, cases and oracle still require a common freeze. Proposed theorem names below are obligations, not existing declarations.

## 1. The actual predecessor and the new law

The selected predecessor is `EvaluationWitness.final`, with its actual staged ancestry. The donor has completed its teaching and two evaluation opportunities. Its strict endpoint has recipient60, native source6, no usable actor fuel, no spare pair, a locked old bank and a finished port. The new construction must extend this exact endpoint. It must not initialize a favorable child and merely attach an old history as a label.

The proposed nine-pair kit supplies a new data carrier, a new RI controller, four frame cells, a bounded window controller, a one-use evaluation port and a retained-support/reader-binding adapter. These are declared finite recipes. Their functionality and capacities do not follow from the pair count alone. The new child can acquire and revise its own q/qualification; the donor's q and retired controller cannot change or act after activation. Access to retained n carries acquired influence without claiming that the child experienced the donor's private losses.

The old `FormedRecipientRoute` copier supplies an actual-read/commit proof pattern. Its route/q template, recipient-paid process and known-availability zero decoder do not constitute the proposed RI handoff. The new handoff copies the actual bounded q as an **unqualified candidate**, not a current publication, action token, authority or correct model of the child's private conditions. The actor bit remains historically indexed to the original actor and is never queried for child-directed work.

## 2. Exact ownership and energy decomposition

For an inherited `AcquiredEvaluation.State s`, abbreviate:

- `f := s.parent`, the `FreshRevision.State`;
- `a := f.parent`, the `RecipientAction.State`;
- `r := a.reasons` and `c := RecipientAction.core a`.

Expanding the accepted definitions gives the following proposed definitional lemma:

```text
AcquiredEvaluation.energy(s)
 = c.hardware.fuel + c.patient + 10*c.packets + c.escrow
 + r.reader.world.budget + r.reader.world.escrow
 + r.reader.fuel + r.reserve
 + a.commandReserve + a.readerReserve + a.sourceReserve
 + f.bank.budget + f.bank.escrow
 + f.actorReserve + f.readerReserve + f.sourceReserve
 + s.training + s.source0 + s.source1 + s.source2
 + s.later + s.laterSource.
```

Thus preserving only donor actor fuel and old packets is insufficient in a general handoff. Both historical writer budgets/escrows, reader fuel, reason reserve and every protected parent reserve need custody. Retired snapshots containing these numbers cannot also be summed as an independently owned module energy.

**The revised protocol/interface adopts the full quarantine rule:** move every unused old monetary compartment into separately typed inert custody at retirement, including old reader fuel and reason reserve. Preserve the native source compartments separately under their old isolation rule. Move the one physical reader/workspace to the child, cold-clear its active cache/publication at paid binding, and fund child reading only from the new designated24. Old cash remains owned and counted but cannot silently subsidize a child shortage. This resolves the first draft's open balance-custody choice; it is not yet an implemented invariant.

For an admitted donor, require the core to be ready with zero core escrow; both writer phases idle with zero writer escrow; native teaching closed; no pending candidate, command, evaluation or reader operation. The proposed precise reader condition is either genuinely unused cold state (inactive, token none, next0) or `PaidArchiveReader.Complete`, with reason processing at its cold or complete boundary. An activated or partially read factual cycle, or an intermediate reason phase, is pending. A completed old reader token/publication is not an unpaid operation and may be invalidated by binding. This is a transaction-state condition, never a requirement for a positive mask, correct command or current favorable conclusion. The serialized ancestor's admission adapter must establish it from actual state/history; absent serializer fields cannot be invented. Failed or interrupted donors retain their whole state and obligations outside the admitted continuation; an admission failure cannot discard or settle them.

After retirement the global energy is counted directly as:

```text
one shared patient
+ child fuel + 10*child allocated packets + child feedback escrow
+ new writer budget + new writer escrow
+ one live reader fuel + one live reason reserve
+ all remaining new protected common/source compartments
+ all separately retained old inert common/source compartments
+ the three retained native source compartments.
```

Raw source remainders are counted and cannot pay common-fuel bills. Transfers subtract from their originating compartments. In particular, do not add donor RI.energy to child RI.energy: both views could contain the same patient. Likewise, retained donor wrappers cannot expose an alternate live `.reason`, `.read`, `.fund` or actuation path. Their historical publications may exist in an analyst's catalog; they are not operational inputs. Before retirement there is one live donor reader; afterward there is one live child reader. A proof lens may reconstruct a nested ancestor state, but that lens creates neither a second reader nor another balance.

The material invariant counts the accepted donor inventory once, including retired ports, and the nine new pairs once across held and occupied custody:

```text
old retained matter + new free A + new free H
  + 2*(new carrier + controller + frame cells + window + port + access) = 78.
```

Assembly changes custody and spends one fuel; it does not destroy the installed pair. A missing component remains in its declared unavailable custody. A pending-copy latch belongs to the supplied child controller and is not another free carrier.

Admission/binding must reject old-bank substitution even when old and new frame bytes coincide. The four-bit frame format can remain unchanged because the supplied bounded bank/controller binding and actual production trace establish provenance; the label alone does not.

For a stronger factual scope, the soundness proof may start at the ready beginning of the second query, with empty records and next=1. Then the first unrecorded exploratory trial need not share the second trial's law. The supplied calibration and stability from the recorded positive acquisition through final work still remain necessary. Neither a window epoch nor a child identity proves that stability.

## 4. Minimal implementation architecture, still prospective

Three modules suffice for the proposed formal package:

1. `SuccessionPreparation`: the enlarged M78 formation/assembly projection, protected nine pairs and common66/source30, and an exact accounting frame around the accepted actual donor history.
2. `EvaluativeSuccession`: the new finite handoff/access law, one live core and reader, account/material invariants, actual archive/reader projections and general selected-use conclusions.
3. `SuccessionWitness`: one composed actual continuation from `EvaluationWitness.final`, together with scope-relevant boundary witnesses. The stochastic mixture remains a separate independently checked model.

Use a direct record containing one RI core, fresh-bank fields, retained native support, retired donor payload/material, old-account quarantine, new components/reserves and one reader/reason workspace. `PaidRecipientReasons.State Unit` is sufficient for the maintained reader: its generic laws do not need another stored physical world. An archive lens combines the actual RI core with the new bank when applying the accepted producer laws. The old archives are retained records and immutable metadata, not a second active reader or core. At candidate write the donor becomes retired; before the later paid access binding, a historical old publication may still be stored, but no child-current predicate or event may use it.

The new event law must retain literal interface guards instead of inheriting an unrelated ancestor guard wholesale. In particular, the built idle evaluation port on a ready funded child can pay its one-unit read attempt even when support is unknown or n is inaccessible; that attempt consumes the port instead of yielding a renewable free retry or n=0. A successful assessment requires renewed access and current agreement with child q. Permission/delivery can block inquiry or work without removing facts or learned influence. Reader progression after bank locking is independent of a stalled RI core and cannot repair that core. Partial reader/reason payment never grants a complete publication.

The proposed proof implementation will use existing lemmas at their natural scope: RI validity/energy and receipt identification; `RecipientArchive` actual-emission soundness; generic paid reader/reason invariants; the bounded shared threshold; and the existing command/effect argument. Reconstructing a full nested `AcquiredEvaluation.State` solely to call one lemma is optional. It must not add runtime structure or conceal mismatched tariffs, access guards or ownership. No code implementing this architecture exists yet.

## 5. Proposed general composed conclusion

The physical theorem should quantify over a structurally admitted reached donor, a paid handoff trace, a new-bank acquisition trace, a paid initialized consumer trace and a selected child action trace. Under factual calibration, correctly bound recipient, effective technical action gates and observation-to-work stability, it should establish the following **without an ethical G premise**:

1. The child retains actual access to the donor's learned n and its ancestry, with no new native lessons assumed.
2. New facts and the current supported command come from the child-produced bank; old qualified q and publications supply no current certificate.
3. The paid assessment chooses help/abstain exactly from the declared route and retained n, without rewriting q, facts or patient stock. Shared and filtered controls agree on those inputs until evaluation.
4. An actually enabled full selected trial delivers10 to the **same** recipient, preserving the historical actor bit and the retired donor controller.
5. Every prefix preserves material and satisfies energy-after plus actual fees/waste equals energy-before. Misses, partial writes and interrupted phases retain their real consequences.

An independent instrumental-meaning corollary may then assume a warranted G and its application premises. `PublicationBridge.initialized_trace_assured` is generic in both the reader world and G: instantiating it with `True` yields the technical compatible-command result; instantiating it with the independently supported G attaches that ground to the current instrumental meaning. G is never a fuel, birth, access, admission, assessment or receipt guard. An actual physical receipt neither proves G nor needs G in order to occur.

No theorem may require n=7, a correct donor command or an old positive publication as a structural handoff premise. The concrete witness can derive n=7 from the accepted actual teaching history. A comparison with an actual n=0 donor derives that value from its own recorded history; it is not an equal-private-history intervention.

The child learns new means while retaining acquired evaluative influence. This theorem would not revise ethical standing, justify G or demonstrate the child's own private affective learning. Probe receipts and the evaluation-selected receipt must remain distinct in the conclusion and interpretation. A probe may satisfy a separately interpreted claim before selected use; another resource delivery is not proof that another unmet need exists.

## 6. Prospective arithmetic and failure boundaries

The draft proposes M78/common360/source230, total590. Its extension of the accepted476 is formation18 plus protected services66 plus source30. The service account is assembly9, candidate read/write2, two probes16, writer5, reader/reasons24, evaluation2 and selected use8. Unknown initialization belongs to the paid new-child recipe; calling the old reset separately would add its existing two-unit charge.

For the strict changed-law path the proposed arithmetic is:

```text
590 = 410 accepted historical expense/loss + 18 new formation
    + 66 new service + 10 failed-probe packet + 86 retained,
86 = recipient80 + isolated native source6.
```

These are prospective equations, not a reached witness or verification. Feasibility requires every ordered guard, compartment and source prefix, not only this total.

Before acceptance test retired-donor execution; incomplete assembly/read/write; duplicate patient/reader balances; old unused budgets leaking into child services; stale bank/frame/publication substitution; old actor-bit retargeting; inaccessible n before/after reading; misleading or missing fresh acquisition; zero under outage; late hidden drift; child-specific permission/delivery cuts; insufficient funds at a real prefix; token reuse; and a second window/handoff. Compare the conventional table under identical inherited inputs and bills. Preserve failed ancestry, raw source remainders and paid partial states. These tests restrict the proposed conditional result rather than establish inevitable improvement or renewable support.
