# Whole continuation, impairment and the scope of a practical reason

Status: **a substantive philosophical defense with an explicit remaining normative premise**, not a new mathematical proof, empirical result or proof of universal metaethics. The [Lean requirement](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-lean-proof-requirement.md) governs any mathematical proof; none is claimed here. Under [Ren's later universal-intelligence clarification](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-universal-intelligence-session-goal.md), this uses [the local question](NORMATIVE-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md#5-the-specified-next-round) as a supporting discriminator for the universal inference, not the central completion target. A reason independent of assent is the target; a complete ordering of lives and an automatic priority for recovery are not assumed.

## 1. Fix the comparison without installing its verdict

Use the [existing shared-performance comparison](SITUATED-ETHICAL-DISCERNMENT.md#continuation-test-changing-a-successful-pursuit). Preparation still produces a real shared achievement. Completion is feasible and meaningful to both participants. Under the specified conditions it also continues a serious, sustained interruption of one participant's rest, ordinary activity and relationships. Joint cancellation is available and valid; recovery is feasible but neither instantaneous nor costless. Cancellation loses the actual performance and its shared expression. The stated recovery arrangements impose real support burdens, without an equivalent new impairment or an outstanding third-party performance duty.

These conditions identify the alternatives; they do not establish their ethical ranking. The earlier judgment favoring cancellation remains a defended judgment at its declared scope. It is not an input from which this note announces a new derivation of recovery's priority.

The undertaking, rest, contribution, impairment, expectations and decision powers differentiate within the same history. A participant is not an independent value-container whose contents must be admitted to a larger owner's ledger. The analytical distinction between achievement and impairment preserves two actual realizations within the continuation. It does not imply that they are independent, additive or commensurable. The impairment can alter what completion means, just as the shared purpose can alter what a demanding activity means.

The word *impairment* must not covertly supply the verdict. Its initial descriptive content here is sustained exhaustion and obstruction of established ordinary activities and relationships. Departure from a statistical norm, loss of one possible activity or reduced future productivity alone would not establish the ethical assessment. If the supposedly impaired condition has instead become a welcome, non-adverse form of realization, the ethical identification needs revision. Calling it impaired by a fixed measure does not answer that challenge.

## 2. The strongest positive explanation of baseline force

The candidate's ground is the life actually being lived through this arrangement. In the specified case, exhaustion and repeated obstruction are ways the continuation goes badly for the activity undergoing them: attempted ordinary living is repeatedly frustrated, rest fails, and valued relationships are interrupted. These are not merely warnings of lower future output. They are part of what the present continuation realizes. Their significance need not await an additional achievement after recovery, a complaint recognized by a steward, or a transfer of value back to the initiating activity.

The affirmative case has three connected considerations.

First, the explanation identifies what is adverse in the actual condition rather than inferring adversity from general loss of options. Rest, contact and unforced ordinary activity are lived realizations here, not merely unused capacity. Their obstruction matters through what it consists in, rather than through the number of future branches eliminated. This preserves cases where ending an activity is itself restorative, completing something finite is good, or keeping an undertaking open prolongs distress.

Second, the proposed response bears on that same condition. Ending or transforming the arrangement would change the exhaustion and obstruction themselves, while retaining the lost performance and the new support burdens in the comparison. This locates the proposed reason in the relation between the condition and the feasible response. A favorable label attached to an action with no such effect would supply no comparable case. Nor can a response remove a burden already wholly past merely by changing how it is recorded.

Third, appraising this realized adversity while declaring it categorically irrelevant to a choice that maintains it requires an account of that exclusion. The same undertaking's expressive achievement already enters the whole practical comparison through the realization it makes possible. Its organization as a named project does not by itself confer a monopoly on what can count. Ordinary living need not become another project, win a vote, or promise compensating output to become intelligible as something changed by the choice. A defended exclusion can still apply; the point is to locate its burden of explanation rather than to declare every exclusion illegitimate.

These considerations support the candidate identification: **where the specified adversity retains that character in the actual continuation, an available response's mitigation of it has a reason in its favor at the level of the whole ethical comparison**. This does not make every means permissible, establish a claim against any selected official, or determine the outcome against completion.

The ethical content is explicit. The argument defends the adversity of this living obstruction and its practical bearing; causal description alone has not entailed either. The relation between an adverse realization and a reason to change it is the adverse counterpart of the [good-to-reason principle already stated in §34](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#34-self-good-practical-reasons-and-the-remaining-ground). It is not a new derivation of that principle. Calling the connection constitutive makes an explanatory proposal about the character of the realization, not a proof by definition.

This is a cumulative philosophical defense. A rival can grant all descriptive conditions and contest their adverse ethical character, their general practical bearing, or their eligibility in a particular role. The account must answer the relevant challenge substantively. There is no contradiction simply in refusing our classification, and no proof here that a separate irreducible ethical realm or premise is required.

## 3. What may change when the reason ceases to govern a decision

The next step needs more precision than saying the reason either persists or disappears. Several different changes must be investigated.

| Change | What the account must identify | What is not established by that change alone |
|---|---|---|
| The adverse condition is removed, or a specific demand is fulfilled | Actual rest, changed work, completed recovery, or another identified change in the condition or demand that grounds the particular reason. | That the preceding impairment never mattered, or that causing it was justified. |
| The condition's ethical meaning changes | A real alteration of its experiential or practical relationships, rather than a new name or suppressed complaint. | That every residual burden disappears, or that assent automatically makes any burden good. |
| Countervailing grounds settle the choice | A stronger achievement, obligation, danger, cost or other warranted consideration supports bearing the burden in this situation. | That bearing the burden had no adverse aspect or that its ground was cured. |
| A justified role or procedure excludes this consideration from this decision | The authority's ground, scope and limits explain why this official or procedure may not decide on that basis. | That the burden has no place in appraisal of the whole continuation, or that someone else automatically has a duty or power to intervene. |
| Inquiry corrects the ethical identification or its assigned force | A substantive argument or discriminating experience shows that the earlier appraisal was mistaken, even while its described condition remains. | That every withdrawal of concern is such a correction, or that the old classification must remain true because its physical target persists. |

This classification is a philosophical diagnostic, not a universal mathematical partition. Actual cases can combine the changes. New information can also defeat an earlier belief that any serious impairment occurred; that is correction of the application, not merely a changed label applied to a preserved condition.

The fourth distinction matters. A reason relevant to the whole need not be an admissible reason for every particular decision. A procedure charged with recognizing a specified achievement may legitimately exclude unrelated need from its determination of that achievement. Assistance, protection or alteration of the arrangement is then a different question, with its own grounds. We must neither erase that question nor infer an automatic duty to provide an alternative route. The fifth distinction prevents a different overreach: unchanged descriptive conditions do not establish that a former ethical appraisal was correct. Warranted learning can correct the appraisal itself, not merely discover a new fact or a stronger counterweight. Any proposed metaethical invariant must leave room for that kind of correction.

Likewise, an undertaking's steward might lack power to cancel what another relationship validly governs. Recognizing impairment does not manufacture that power. But merely defining the steward's task as completion also does not justify the authority to maintain every burden produced by completion. The authority's actual basis has to explain the exclusion, including its reach over the affected continuation. Both arguments concern relationships within the history.

## 4. The informed finality rival survives

The strongest rival accepts the performance's achievement, the impairment's adverse force, the possibility of cancellation and the genuine costs of reopening. It says that a sufficiently justified settlement, an undertaken responsibility or the special significance of completing this finite work warrants completion. It can also say that this particular decision-maker has no jurisdiction to reopen the issue on the proposed grounds. These are substantive accounts of the same continuation, not a detached observer's indifference or a denial that consequences exist.

The affirmative response has two parts. The source of closure must be specified: fulfillment, ethical transformation, countervailing justification or a justified exclusion. A deadline's arrival, a settled record, or a successor's lack of concern does not identify which of these has occurred. Conversely, once the rival supplies an adequate account of finality, continued review is not automatically required. Review itself changes the whole and can impose serious costs or destroy a realization worth completing.

In the inherited bounded case, the candidate still favors recovery. Its support is the seriousness and breadth of sustained obstruction, contrasted with the limited, jointly cancellable accomplishment and the absence of additional major obligations. The rival's mere assertion that the work is unique does not yet explain why its uniqueness warrants this particular impairment. But the same demand applies to the candidate: calling ordinary life broad or basic does not by itself demonstrate a lexical priority. The earlier recovery comparison remains contested philosophical judgment; this round does not promote it to a necessary theorem.

There are substantive cases in which the finality account may be better: the affected life knowingly embraces a demanding finite completion; delay would destroy a grave and irreplaceable realization; further assessment brings serious burdens; or a justified division of authority excludes unilateral reopening. Those possibilities narrow the proposed constraint. Their existence does not settle the inherited case, but they defeat any inference from serious impairment directly to obligatory cancellation or endless appeal.

The added result is therefore a sharper question for finality: **does its justification change the condition, correct its ethical identification, outweigh a warranted reason, or exclude its use within a warranted scope?** These explanations should not be substituted for one another. This goes beyond the earlier case where silence was simply mistaken for endorsement, while leaving the first normative premise open.

## 5. Assent, nonclaimants and nonreflective living

Assent is an event within the relationship. It can form a commitment, confer a limited power, transform the meaning of difficulty, or reveal what the undertaking realizes for a participant. Those effects matter. They do not follow merely from the sound of agreement, and they need not abolish every adverse aspect of what follows. Informed voluntary difficulty can be warranted even while a burden remains real. The candidate therefore offers no universal ban on demanding art, devotion, sacrifice or finite terminal achievement.

A process that makes no claim to ethical justification is outside an argument about the commitments of making such a claim. It is not outside the actual continuation. Under the candidate identification, impairment does not first gain significance when that process acknowledges it. Extending a practical reason to such a nonclaimant relies on the substantive objective bearing defended above, not on a contradiction in its speech. The extension is not established by the narrow anti-silencing inference.

Similarly, nonreflective affected lives need not articulate an objection or understand the undertaking. Their experienced condition and actual participation in living relations can be represented and assessed. Evidence and representation may be uncertain; neither uncertainty nor incapacity to argue is itself a reason to stipulate no adversity. The account does not derive equal ethical weight for all living or intelligent organization from this point. Wider standing and differences in relevant kinds of realization remain part of the universal research question.

## 6. Reason, permission, duty and authority

A consideration that favors mitigation is not yet permission to use a particular means. A permissible response is not automatically required. A requirement bearing on an eligible participant does not automatically authorize another participant to compel it. These distinctions preserve different actual relationships of choice, burden and power; they do not divide fact and value into independent domains.

The present candidate defends baseline relevance in the whole comparison. Application to a particular choice requires the response's relation to the ground and any justified restrictions. A duty further requires sufficient force and a proper bearer in the actual relationship. Authority requires a justification of who can alter or settle the undertaking, by which means and within which scope. N3 answerability is not used to produce any of these answers. The result consequently does not establish a universal right to reopen a decision or an obligation to maintain a particular channel.

Recognition, motivation and implementation also remain distinct. A steward may recognize the reason but oppose the candidate's priority, lack motivation to respond, or lack a permitted effective means. A successor may correctly conclude that an old demand has been fulfilled. Conversely, erasing its representation does not establish fulfillment. These are possibilities to discriminate through their actual grounds, not through whether the system repeats the preferred ethical words.

## 7. Isolate the universal inference from jurisdiction

**Genealogy check after review.** [The constitutive-answer argument](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md#4-the-serious-rival-survives-in-the-correct-place) and its [integration](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-INTEGRATION.md#accepted-result-and-its-limit) already retain this exact sufficiency problem, including a permitted effective response available before adopted concern. The comparison below makes that inherited frontier explicit for the current universal-intelligence target. It is not a newly discovered discriminator, and it does not invoke their conditional result against a duplicate demand for authorization. Here the rival has not granted the option-specific reason that result presupposes.

The local steward already participates in the undertaking. Its case cannot alone establish a reason for every intelligence. To test the universal inference, distinguish a second response position formed within the same wider unfolding: an intelligence that did not adopt this undertaking, makes no ethical claim, and does not identify with its participants. It nevertheless has an actual opportunity to change the adverse continuation. Its access and capability must be causally specified; its means are stipulated to be permitted, with their authority and costs explicitly accounted for. This is a philosophical test condition, not evidence that such access is universal or that permission follows from ability.

For this discriminator, temporarily grant the candidate's first ethical identification and the favorable whole quality of the specified response. This isolates the remaining question: is there any practical favor for that intelligence's response independently of assent or an adopted role? It does not derive the granted ethical quality. A jurisdictional restriction on changing the performance's official decision cannot answer this test if the specified response does not exercise that power. A rival that grants baseline favor but denies duty agrees with the limited candidate; the unresolved rival must deny even that favor on the stipulated response grounds.

The positive explanation is that the response position and the change giving it its point are jointly constituted within the unfolding. The intelligence's action is an available realization of that very favorable difference, not a transfer from another person's privately owned value into its own reasons. Prior assent can explain motivation and can form additional responsibilities. Its absence has not yet identified a missing constituent of the already specified relation between this response and the favorable realization. The candidate therefore locates practical favor in that relation, with assent unnecessary to this particular ground.

The strongest rival responds that an actual favorable difference and a permitted capacity to realize it still do not suffice for a reason belonging to this response position. On its view, formed commitments, identifications or responsibilities constitute the further practical relationship. It can preserve the whole's interconnectedness, the favorable result and every causal effect while offering this richer account of practical reasons. It need not start from independent owners or deny any harm. Its proposed dependence must be defended: reference to a role is not yet an explanation of why absence of all such roles removes every baseline favor rather than merely a special duty.

This is the decisive remaining disagreement. The candidate's positive account is stronger than the claim that every effect lies inside the whole, because it identifies the actual action–realization relationship proposed to ground favor. It is still a substantive sufficiency claim. If the rival adequately explains a necessary further practical condition, the universal candidate needs that restriction. If assent or role adds only recognition, motivation, weight or special responsibility, the proposed general ground survives that challenge. Neither conclusion follows merely from choosing the word *reason* for one's preferred relation.

The final scope test holds fixed the same permitted effective response, grants its favorable whole realization, and introduces no affected participant's mandate, exclusive authority or competing cost. These simplifications remove distractions; they do not produce a new ground of first favor. The rival can still say that the favorable realization lacks a further practical constituent. Joint constitution and the absence of a second owner answer a misleading export architecture, but do not establish that no further constituent is needed. Repeating this comparison with another beneficiary or institution would not advance the universal derivation.

## 8. What this round changes and what would defeat it

The proposed first-warrant shortcut fails: reconstructing all effects inside one process does not by itself establish that serious impairment must govern every intelligence's conduct. The positive argument is stronger as an account of what grounds a bounded reason, but it still carries the substantive ethical identification and practical bridge. Neither a new dynamics model nor a Lean encoding of that bridge would discharge them.

The practical advance is to make finality answer a more exact ground-and-scope question. A settled decision may be justified without curing its burden. A burden can remain ethically relevant while a particular official properly lacks authority to act on it. A changed relationship can genuinely transform a demand. The whole comparison must retain these differentiated effects, including the value and costs of its authority arrangements. No additional cosmic beneficiary or maximum-openness objective is introduced.

Narrow or revise the candidate if its descriptive identification mistakes willing realization for frustrated living; if a claimed response does not improve the relevant condition; if countervailing grounds or legitimate restrictions defeat its application; or if a rival explains the same adverse realization and practical bearing without the alleged necessary dependence on the generative root. Compatible explanations are not defeated by different vocabulary. A matched rival's success would constrain exclusivity, not automatically erase the present explanation.

The remaining foundational question is: **what warrants the ethical identification of this realized obstruction, and why is the actual eligible response–realization relationship sufficient for practical favor beyond the commitments of a claimant?** The local case gives a concrete target and surviving alternatives; the cross-role comparison retains the inherited universal step. Neither is a completed universal derivation. A further advance needs an independently defended constitutive account of that sufficiency, or a substantive inadequacy in the rival's proposed role-dependence. It cannot define favorable quality or eligibility through the desired option-specific favor. Evidence about effects, recognition or role formation can constrain the application; behavioral convergence does not establish first normative force. A substantive correction of the inherited ethical classification would also advance the account if it identifies what was mistaken and why. Merely retaining the old descriptive target or successfully producing the new behavior settles neither appraisal.
