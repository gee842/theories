## 1. The general explanatory direction

Living organization can depend on a relation remaining operative while actual interactions change that relation. Its continuance then involves retained tolerance, effective compensation, or a transformation of how the function is realized. What needs explanation is the particular relation and its changing conditions; life is not defined as whatever continuously consumes a supplied resource counter.

The evidence-selected mechanism concerns a formed membrane, material transfer across it and the activity maintaining a physiological state. Compartment boundaries and their different conditions are relations within the same unfolding. The formal separation into material amount, transport and available work preserves selected balances; it omits the formation of the membrane and transport machinery, detailed chemistry, geometry and other living functions. Those omissions delimit the derivation rather than placing its supplied laws outside generative reality.

This yields a common research pattern: **identify an independently recognized living realization; explain its operative relations and inherited provision; derive when actual interactions exceed that provision; identify effective compensation or a changed realization on the same history.** The consequence need not be a new form at every step, renewal by every participant, or preservation of every constituent.

\[
x'+q=x+\ell,\qquad e'+kq\le e+r.

\[
(B-x')+\ell=(B-x)+q,
