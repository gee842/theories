## 1. The general explanatory direction

Living organization can depend on a relation remaining operative while actual interactions change that relation. Its continuance then involves retained tolerance, effective compensation, or a transformation of how the function is realized. What needs explanation is the particular relation and its changing conditions; life is not defined as whatever continuously consumes a supplied resource counter.

The evidence-selected mechanism concerns a formed membrane, material transfer across it and the activity maintaining a physiological state. Compartment boundaries and their different conditions are relations within the same unfolding. The formal separation into material amount, transport and available work preserves selected balances; it omits the formation of the membrane and transport machinery, detailed chemistry, geometry and other living functions. Those omissions delimit the derivation rather than placing its supplied laws outside generative reality.

This yields a common research pattern: **identify an independently recognized living realization; explain its operative relations and inherited provision; derive when actual interactions exceed that provision; identify effective compensation or a changed realization on the same history.** The consequence need not be a new form at every step, renewal by every participant, or preservation of every constituent.

## 2. The mathematical connection now established

The [new module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/LivingRealization.lean) represents an arbitrary core process and an arbitrary family of dependencies. For one index, let (x) be the inside amount, (B) its fixed operative ceiling, (q) active export, (ell) net unpowered inward transport, (e) available work support, (r) supplied support, and (k) the declared export cost. The physical-model premises are

\[
x'+q=x+\ell,\qquad e'+kq\le e+r.
\]

Both endpoint amounts must remain below the ceiling. From these premises the checked proof derives

\[
(B-x')+\ell=(B-x)+q,
\qquad H'+k\ell\le H+r,
\qquad H=k(B-x)+e.
\]

The first account concerns actual compensatory export. The second combines available tolerance with support that can fund further export. **(H) is an accounting potential, not a claim that unused physiological tolerance is stored biochemical energy.** The representation uses natural-number units; the exact correspondence, including conversion cost, is supplied.

The three checked boundary witnesses admit inward transport without pumping while tolerance remains, compensation using retained work without new input, and an idle interval followed by compensation through the same reached state. ## 4. Boundaries that matter to the conclusion

The formal ceiling defines a **maintained regime**, not life itself. The biological source reports restoration after depolarization before irreversible failure. A temporarily disturbed but recoverable living history can therefore fall outside the restricted theorem. Bounds are checked at represented endpoints; continuous-time safety between them is not proved.

The formal (ell) is net unpowered inward transport and is nonnegative on each modeled transition. Unpowered outward transport must be included in this net, never discarded or truncated. Histories with negative net unpowered flux, signed stochastic fluctuations or changing volume require an extended account or an independently justified correspondence. This round proves neither. Gross inward flux alone cannot be used to demand additional work while ignoring passive relief.

The work account also does not infer a universal ATP price. Cost can even be zero in the abstract interface, in which case excess cost-weighted load provides no support-necessity conclusion. The stochastic mortality model in the primary paper is not an individual deterministic deadline, nor a proved probabilistic refinement of this Lean module.

Finally, local input is not automatically fresh whole-level formation. It may draw on earlier chemical provision or material released by other activity. The included-store theorem retains that dependence. Actual release, conversion and loss elsewhere remain part of a faithful larger account.

## 7. Extension: passive relief and actual bounded endpoints

It does not identify returning to that range with recovery of living organization.

For an arbitrary actual finite history, let \(I,O,Q,R\) total passive inward transfer, passive outward transfer, active export and supplied work. The new transition laws compose to give

\[
x_f+Q+O=x_0+I,\qquad e_f+kQ\le e_0+R.
\]

These are finite quantities in declared natural-number units; signed net transfer can go either way. Only active export is charged under the supplied work law. No intermediate amount bound is needed. With the endpoint premise \(x_f\le B\), the checked consequence is

\[
kx_0+kI+e_f\le kB+e_0+R+kO.
\]

Thus actual attainment of the bound constrains the history that reaches it. If \(x_0+I>B+O\), active export must occur. If \(kx_0+kI>kB+e_0+kO\), actual support input must occur. Both conclusions have a contributing event on that same history. Passive relief and inherited work are credited before additional input is required. The term \(kO\) is accounting credit, not work generated by passive transport.

Including a provision store satisfying \(d'+r\le d\) yields

\[
kx_0+kI+e_f+d_f\le kB+e_0+d_0+kO.
