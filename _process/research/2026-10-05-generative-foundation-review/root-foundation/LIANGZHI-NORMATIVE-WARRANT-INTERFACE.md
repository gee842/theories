This note examines its contribution to the first-warrant question; it does not duplicate that implementation or count its results as new universal proofs.

**Result:** the new evidence and transmission interface specify a possible source of epistemic support for the whole-grounded account. They do not yet establish calibrated ethical judgment or universal ethical truth. This is a reviewed argument and evidence assessment, with no new theorem, Lean declaration or empirical experiment.

## 1. The connection the new direction makes available

The existing [reliability investigation](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#34-experiential-reliability-and-the-necessity-of-generated-claims) already permits warranted extension from independently supported operation of an identified judgment-forming process. It does not require a separate proof of every later judgment. Nor does an evolutionary history automatically vindicate or defeat that process. These are inherited conclusions, not this note's discoveries.

The new [formation argument](LIANGZHI-EVOLUTION-ARGUMENT.md#3-信用既有结果精确选择本轮增量) distinguishes a template's reproductive success from the response organization actually reconstructed in its successors. That distinction matters to epistemic warrant: a reliability-bearing capacity must actually be transmitted and developed if its earlier performance is to support later judgments. The same label, the same outward helping action or a larger lineage does not identify that capacity. The relevant organization includes what is detected, how the actual circumstances affect its appraisal, and how it corrects an ineffective or mistaken response.

The positive candidate is one relationship doing two connected kinds of explanatory work. Actual undergoing, dependence, fulfillment, control and possible transformation may constitute ethical significance under the proposed whole account; those same relationships may also shape a capacity to discern and respond to them. Intelligence and its discernment form within that activity. There is no external observer installing a value after the fact, or a private owner transferring a value across a boundary.

If the account of ethical character is defensible and the proposed calibration is supported, this can give **defeasible warrant** for judgments in the demonstrated range. It can also answer a specific allegation that the capacity's genealogy is disconnected from its purported grounds. A favorable genealogy does not establish either conclusion by itself.

## 2. Joint explanation is a legitimate route to investigate

The ethical identification need not be completely proved before formation evidence can contribute to its justification. A joint explanation can receive support when it accounts for actual distinctions, errors and corrections better than competing accounts. This permits evidence to strengthen or revise the proposed constitutive identity, rather than only calibrate access to a permanently fixed ethical premise.

For example, an account locating the point of a response in an actual continuing dependence has a different proposed explanation from one locating it in an inherited signal. Changing the dependence while preserving the signal, or changing the signal while preserving the dependence, can help distinguish the corresponding mechanisms. The experiment must establish that these changes really preserve what they claim to preserve. Their ethical interpretation also needs argument. Need-sensitive conduct alone need not express an ethical judgment, and an informed controller can use accurate understanding for manipulation.

Thus joint support does not mean mutual self-certification. Defining the ethically correct response as whatever survives selection, then presenting survival as evidence of correctness, leaves no independent test. Equally, declaring every departure from the preferred verdict a malfunction protects the proposal from the comparison it needs. The whole account must retain real costs, authority, self-good, finite completion and legitimate nonresponse when identifying what would count as a correction.

This methodological point does not lower the final objective from universal proof to an interesting explanation. It identifies evidence that can advance the justification of a premise needed by that proof. A probabilistic or abductive case must be reported at that epistemic strength; Lean checking of a conditional consequence does not upgrade it to necessity.

## 3. What the current evidence actually changes

The new branch's [source assessment](LIANGZHI-EVOLUTION-SOURCES.md) reports three bounded primary readings. Root and this note's source reviewer inspected that assessment rather than independently rereading the empirical papers; no additional primary verification is claimed here.

These are distinct studies, not observations of one complete evolutionary chain. They add evidence about formation, range and reconstruction to the dossier. They supply neither measured ethical accuracy nor a demonstrated correspondence transferring accuracy from a different judgment task.

An immanent rival can accept all three findings and explain the capacities through success in particular histories of transmission and development. It can allow accurate care, local noninstrumental concern and accurate manipulation. The universal candidate cannot claim these observations discriminate against that rival merely because they fit its own story too. The distinctive question is what governs correction when familiar cues, transmission success and the proposed whole ethical ground no longer coincide.

## 4. The next actual inference, without another generic bound

The new interface must retain the following connections together:

1. **A specified appraisal or response capacity.** Identify its content and organization; do not infer a moral proposition from an observed animal helping act.
2. **Actual reconstruction.** Show which sensing, updating and response relations survive or change through the same transmission and developmental history. Counted descendants alone do not establish this.
3. **Relevant calibration.** Support correct discernment of the relationships invoked in the ethical explanation, and explain how their ethical appraisal is warranted. Accuracy about a signal or recipient location does not automatically transfer to normative judgment.
4. **Supported extension.** Establish why the tested organization and conditions apply in the new case, retaining changed history, authority and available responses. Apparent improvement must not come from deleting difficult cases or affected lives from the evaluated domain.

Existing [valuation bridges](../../../../experiments/valuation-development/lean/ValuationBridges.lean), [valuation completion](../../../../experiments/valuation-development/lean/ValuationCompletion.lean) and [situated response](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/noninstrumental-concern/SituatedPractice.lean) already supply relevant conditional machinery. More specifically, the accepted actual successor below already connects inherited evaluative influence to new evidence, revised means knowledge and action. What remains for this normative interface is the correspondence to justified ethical discernment and its supported extension. A further generic accuracy bound, or reconstruction of that existing successor, would not establish the ethical identification.

This also fixes the role of counterfactual tests. Signal changes can test cue dependence; changes in actual need can test consequence sensitivity; changes in inherited organization can test reconstruction; changes in authority can change a warranted response despite identical benefit. No one test measures all four. The normative account must explain the significance of these differences rather than require more intervention under every condition.

### What this selects for the universal investigation

The relevant epistemic hypothesis is now more precise: **development and transmission can reconstruct sensitivity to changes in actual ethical grounds, with independently supported applicability beyond the tested history.** A successful test must distinguish that sensitivity from changing command efficacy, signal familiarity, access, resources or motivation. The ethical account and that calibration may still receive joint support, as §2 explains.

However, an engineered implementation of a supplied verdict supplies no independent evidence that the verdict is true. A relational rival could accept the particular service responsibility and its fulfillment while disputing the universal ethical identification, and could implement the same correction. These existing successor results therefore establish neither a new first warrant nor an impossibility result for universal ethics. Calling their `G` supplied describes a limitation of these formal inferences; it is not an argument that every fuller constitutive account must import an external moral principle.

For this session, **defending the ethical/calibration hypothesis is the central obligation**. More module composition remains supporting work within the Liangzhi programme unless it supplies a stated discriminator bearing on that hypothesis. There is presently no reason to construct another generic learner, add a sensor by default, or count another helping outcome as progress in the universal proof. The preceding next-step wording is narrowed accordingly; the original normative objective is unchanged.

There is still a specific, nonduplicative mechanism question for the formation branch. The new transmission model copies chemical response types; it does not copy the successor's evaluator, reader, archive, command or access architecture. The accepted successor instead uses a declared kit of nine additional pairs and isolated resources, and its final state cannot repeat the handoff. Sharing a formation ancestor does not join these laws automatically. A further composition would have to explain how actual response consequences change later assembly, development and transmission conditions for those architectures, then retain their demonstrated capacities. It need not require a private copy where maintained shared support suffices. That would advance the formation explanation at its stated scope; it would still require the ethical/calibration argument above to bear on universal warrant.

## 5. Relation to universal validity and completion

A capacity could contingently arise in some social histories while discerning a ground valid wherever its complete conditions obtain. Universal validity does not require every intelligence to have the same evolution, recognize the same facts, or comply. Conversely, even universal occurrence of a disposition would not by itself establish its ethical correctness. This distinction preserves the user’s full domain without making universal evolutionary convergence a new mandatory gate.

The present root question remains why the independently described whole relationship constitutes ethical quality and differentiated practical significance. Formation evidence can bear on a jointly defended answer, but does not presently discharge it. The five-condition generative criterion remains the substantive candidate; neither mere retention nor a helping count replaces it.
