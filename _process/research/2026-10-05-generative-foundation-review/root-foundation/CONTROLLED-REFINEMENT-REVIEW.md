# Independent review: retaining observations through an exact representation

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review of the [formal implementation](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/ControlledRefinement.lean), its [mathematical companion](CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT-MATH.md), [canonical account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md), checker and saved build. This is a separate analytical and artifact inspection, not external peer review, a second compilation or empirical validation.

**Decision: accepted within the stated finite-history scope.** Exact world representation together with compatible observations transports the behavior of the same supplied deterministic controller while retaining its actual memory. The paid counterexample establishes that exact world possibilities and matching resource costs alone do not suffice. This closes a representation interface; it does not construct a learning architecture or complete the material-to-learning derivation.

## The checked implication

`LocalExact` requires every abstract step to lift from **each actual concrete predecessor** in its fibre, and every concrete step to project. It does not collect individually possible edges from different representatives and concatenate them as if one representative could perform them all. No global surjectivity of the state map is needed: the statement starts at the image of a specified concrete state.

The additional observation condition is imposed on admitted concrete edges. In the abstract-to-concrete direction of `closed_loop_local_exact`, the proof first lifts the world edge from the current concrete predecessor. Observation compatibility then identifies the lifted edge's feedback. Pairing its successor with the abstract successor's memory satisfies the same update equation and gives precisely the required projected endpoint. The converse projects the edge and uses both components of endpoint equality, including memory. It does not assume a deterministic world or a unique concrete successor.

`controlled_trace_exact` correctly applies the existing root's finite-history theorem to that new local relation. Its lifted endpoint is some reachable concrete state with the specified abstract image and exactly equal final memory, not every state in the fibre. The same action/observation word is retained. `controlled_feasible_exact` existentially quantifies the endpoint; it concerns possible histories of the installed controller, not guaranteed success under every nondeterministic outcome or optimization over controllers. The two chaining results compose world maps and observation equalities through their actual common intermediate description.

The premises are sufficient. Unselected or unreachable edges can make a globally imposed condition stronger than needed by a particular controller. A separate distinction matters: ignoring an observation difference may preserve **command-only behavior**, but does not itself preserve that difference's label in the stronger action/observation-history contract. The mathematical companion explicitly distinguishes these claims. Changing observation alphabets, identifying different memories or treating many molecular events as one update requires an additional mapping. The present theorem uses one shared action alphabet and step granularity.

## The counterexample and its resource meaning

The concrete world carries a Boolean bit and fuel; every command preserves the bit and consumes one fuel unit. Its fuel-only summary is locally exact and therefore preserves every uncontrolled action word and its projected endpoint. Both descriptions satisfy the same declared unit-expenditure account.

The supplied controller probes once, retains the observed bit and selects its subsequent command from that memory. Starting from the true bit and two fuel units, the concrete process executes probe then right. The erased channel executes probe then left. The proof excludes an abstract probe-then-right trace for **every** choice of observation labels and endpoint, so the failure is visible in commands as well as labels. A separate theorem proves that no deterministic channel on the erased state can reproduce both concrete bit observations on their identical projected probe edges.

This is a counterexample to representation compatibility, not evidence that either response causes harm, that the abstract world lacks the right command physically, or that a predictor has learned a previously unknown law. Probe and memory hardware remain supplied. The unit charge is an explicit abstract event cost, not an identified molecular account. More generally, transporting a resource inequality only preserves its declared valuations; identifying it with an independently specified material budget requires matching reserve, expenditure and credit. Missing sensing, construction or maintenance costs cannot be recovered by the theorem.

## What the next construction still has to establish

The theorem supplies a contract, not an already established mapping between formed response variants, typed-part selector assembly, recyclable-slot updaters and paid prediction. Their actual world transitions, observations, memory and costs must satisfy it, or a weaker explicitly scoped relation must be proved. Stochastic probability, elapsed time, fairness, infinite continuation and empirical calibration require additional arguments. The origin and consequence-dependent revision of the supplied controller remain separate obligations.

The [identity-revelation concern note](CONCERN-IDENTITY-DIAGNOSTIC-NOTE.md) is preserved as a **proposed future diagnostic**, separate from these checked results. It has no implementation, scientific verification or empirical validation. A future material realization of that intervention would need to retain its identity evidence, timing and matched resources in the appropriate controller interface; this theorem does not establish that realization or validate the diagnostic. Connecting acquired prediction to evaluation of another center's stake remains part of the governing research goal.
