These are supplied local rates, not empirical biological estimates. The carrier production consumes equal numbers of free precursors and activity-support units; the support multiplier in the earlier law is not a replenishment of this stock.

The current state retains loose carriers by source arrangement. `weakCapacity` is the lesser of the available weak-source A and H counts divided by four, using integer division; `strongCapacity` is the corresponding strong-source capacity. `generatedTable` puts these capacities in the faithful diagonal channels. `reconstruct` invokes the old assembly with that computed table. The recipe operates only when the maximal batch is nonempty and enough activity support exists for the whole batch. Partial-batch alternatives are not admitted by this added recipe, although the inherited assembler can represent them.

`reconfigure` changes specified counts of currently active arrangements from weak to strong or strong to weak, consuming one activity-support unit per change and retaining the eight carriers per arrangement. It represents a change of organization, not creation of carriers. The interpretation of the two configurations and the local rate change is supplied; there is no molecular derivation or measured mutation rate. Reconfiguration is a permitted event, not a policy optimizing the eventual outcome. Source tags track which arrangement produced a carrier, not a complete individual genealogy.

The event list records operation, reconstruction and reconfiguration. No state reset, fresh precursor or activity-support replenishment occurs between events. These are differentiated operations and quantities within one history. A fixed formal law that represents possible reconfiguration is not an ontologically external selector; its empirical adequacy and deeper explanation remain questions.

The finite-history bound is specific to the declared positive event costs and absence of replenishment. It is not a general theorem that activity always consumes a common scalar resource, or that all real evolution must stop after finitely many events.

### Exact conventional recurrence

`cycle_population_correspondence` assumes a successful `operate → reconstruct` cycle and initially empty loose A/H pools for both source arrangements. Writing initial arrangement counts as W and G, it proves the daughter's counts are:

```
W_next = 7 * W
G_next = floor(33 * G / 2)
```

This is an exact deterministic two-type population recurrence on that slice, not a newly discovered general evolutionary law. The success premise includes both real precursor and support guards; the bare recurrence does not supply them. Pooling carriers within a source class allows remainders from different arrangements to combine. Thus `16 * G` is not the correct general strong count. Residual carriers outside the empty-pool slice must remain in the full state, rather than being silently discarded to reuse this formula.

The correspondence establishes matching counts whenever its assumptions hold. It neither proves empirical superiority nor represents a competitor unable to add resource guards or developmental state. Such additions can reproduce the same explanation, a possibility the broader comparison must assess.

This is emergence of one operative configuration from a supplied repertoire, not open-ended evolutionary innovation or formation of life from unorganized material.

The erosion case makes loss of the coupling operational: the next carrier production uses the configurations actually present. It is not a freely changed success annotation. This is an effect of the inherited all-at-once production guard and higher total output requirement, not evidence that a real organism with a stronger cooperative organization must fail. At zero support reconfiguration fails; insufficient carriers prevent reconstruction; a 23-daughter maximal batch does not run on 22 support units.

## Substantive self-review

This review is by the implementing author, not an independent agent review.

**Does the construction explain a reconstruction rule's evolutionary origin?** No. It replaces independent table choices with capacities determined by the actual history under a faithful source-associated recipe. Faithfulness, source retention, batching and conversion rules remain premises. The word generated applies to table entries and actual configurations, not to the full mechanism's origin.

**Does reconfiguration introduce a hidden favorable selector?** The model permits both directions and checks the loss case. It assigns neither a reward nor an ethical predicate to the outcome. Nevertheless, the permitted transformations and demonstrated event schedules are supplied. It would overclaim to infer spontaneous prevalence or selection for a reconfiguration rule from these witnesses.

**Does recurring production establish shared renewal?** No. The predecessor's opportunity multiplier increases descendant formation while finite precursor and support stocks decrease. The arbitrary-history bound makes this limitation explicit. A restoration mechanism must operate on the same state before the account can claim continued renewal.

**Are the comparison models weaker by construction?** A recurrence omitting carrier/support variables has less information, but conventional theory can include them. The exact correspondence is positive integration; superiority needs an additional explanation or test that survives an equally informed rival. Recasting established dynamics in generative language alone does not meet the goal.

**Does the formal split recreate independent substances?** Arrangement classes and source pools describe positions and retained effects in one process. They preserve productive origin and material accounting while omitting detailed genealogy, contact geometry, movement and heterogeneous exposure. Those omissions limit application; they do not establish that the fuller relational proposal cannot explain them.

**What would change the account?** An empirical case with the claimed four-A/four-H recipe but a different reconstruction count would challenge that application. More plausibly, actual association, leakage or division rules will differ, requiring replacement of the local recipe. A conventional connected mechanism matching every additional prediction would defeat a claimed unique predictive advantage, while leaving any warranted shared explanation intact.
