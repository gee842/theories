# Before further teaching: what the existing learner retains

6 October 2026. AI-authored mechanism study under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), continuing the [reached four-pair question](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-INTEGRATION.md). Named connection: **actually acquired consequences → evidence available before further teaching → reconsideration of that practice**. This round establishes an exact information relationship within the existing law; it installs no new event, controller, sensor or ethical predicate.

**Result:** in the specified completed first-four-pair histories, occupied learning sockets identify net recipient depletion exactly. The actual count-four state retains evidence of a four-unit reduction even though its transient outcome bits have been cleared. A claim that it needs another recipient-paid lesson merely to make that past quantity reconstructible would therefore be mistaken. Reconstructibility does not establish an installed cumulative readout, understanding of responsibility, or permission for the next teaching operation.

## 1. Start from the actual continuing configuration

The [new source][proof] imports the unchanged `PlasticityWitness` and uses `PairedPlasticity`'s existing state, event law and costs. The starting state is `PlasticityWitness.entry .active true`: the actual inherited `RevisionWitness.consumed` core plus the already declared plasticity preparation, empty sockets, recipient stock 40, actor fuel 10, training provision 22 and revision provision 12. The `true` parameter enables a six-pair schedule. It does not create new resources at the four-pair boundary.

The selected boundary is the result of the existing `aligned` four-pair word. `actual_count_four_prefix` connects it to a history of paid pairs, and `prefix_is_actual` turns that history into the original `Generative.Trace`. `count_four_retains_exact_loss` establishes count four, native coefficient four, cleared own and recipient latches, two occupied sockets, recipient stock 36 and mathematical depletion readout four.

This extends the prior reconstruction with new proofs about retained information. It does not reclassify the old final stock as welfare or treat the source name `patient` as phenomenal identification.

## 2. An accountable projection from retained learning structures

Each faithful pair pays for own and recipient operations and reads their actual return bits. If the recipient records a loss, completion requires binding an empty socket; with no recipient loss, completion requires skipping binding. Before count four, replacement is unavailable. `nativeWrite` requires the pair to have reached the settled phase and then clears the two transient bits.

Define the structural projection

\[
O(s)=\operatorname{occupied}(s.link0)+\operatorname{occupied}(s.link1).
\]

This does not discard a richer retained loss ledger to manufacture ambiguity. The sockets are the actual learned structures: the transmission calculation reads them, binding tests their vacancy, and later replacement tests their presence. Their occupancy survives native writing. The proposed projection selects a property of those operative retained structures, rather than the externally supplied lesson word, source-account history or analyst's patient balance.

It also does not identify all possible internal access with one scalar. The machine retains native data, phases, addresses, means knowledge, components and budgets as well. The new result is stronger than necessary for a full retained-record decoder: this small projection already determines the specified past net quantity under the fixed provenance. Conversely, the supplied `occupied` function also occurs in analytical material accounting; that occurrence alone would not establish a paid cognitive read of its numerical sum.

The new `depletionReadout` is a mathematical readout of the existing structures. No existing event calls it. Establishing that a state contains an exact code is different from showing that the learner has acquired and uses the code's cumulative interpretation. The protocol and initial boundary are premises of the theorem, not facts silently attributed to the learner's reflective knowledge.

## 3. General complete-prefix balance

The new `Prefix` relation describes a finite sequence of **complete faithful non-replacement pairs**. Each append must be runnable under the original law and start with count below four. This excludes arbitrary interleavings, later replacement, selected service, missing acquisition and unfinished pairs. It is a class of existing histories, not a replacement process law. The general balance itself allows any initial state for which those steps are admitted.

For every such prefix from `start` to `end`,

\[
P(end)+2O(end)=P(start)+2O(start),
\]

where \(P\) is the existing recipient account. A successful return exactly replaces that pair's two-unit debit and adds no socket. A recorded loss leaves the two-unit decrement and adds one occupied socket. The paid binding guard prevents overwriting an occupied second socket and silently forgetting another loss.

For the actual empty-socket entrance with recipient 40, this gives

\[
40-P(end)=2O(end).
\]

`same_retained_sockets_same_depletion` rules out two histories in this class with equal occupancy but different final recipient balances. `retained_readout_exact` proves the subtraction form. The conclusion is net teaching depletion relative to the known entrance. It is not an unrestricted estimate of absolute stock, all prior injury, total welfare, compensation by another process or the meaning of a different episode.

The first-four-pair restriction is genuine. At an unfinished pair, a debit can already have occurred while its return, read and binding are pending. The socket code then need not describe current net depletion. The theorem is tied to complete acquisition/settlement, rather than changing the accounting target after a failure.

## 5. Later turnover does not establish total forgetting

Occupancy alone need not increase when a later loss replaces an already occupied socket. But replacement increments the physically retained `retired` count. The new `replacement_pair_retains_depletion` proves that a paid recipient-loss replacement pair conserves

\[
P(s)+2\bigl(O(s)+s.retired\bigr).
\]

The actual continued witness has recipient 34, occupancy two and one retired part: the extended structural expression still accounts for six units. These are a general local replacement-pair theorem and the actual continued-state check. They are not an assertion about arbitrary future event words or an installed interpretation of retired parts. They prevent an occupancy-only scope limit from being inflated into absence of retained history in the full state.
