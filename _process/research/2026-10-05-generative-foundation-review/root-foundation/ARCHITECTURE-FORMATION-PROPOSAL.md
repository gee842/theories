# How organization produces its possible architectures

6 October 2026. AI-authored proposal responding to [Ren's question](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-architecture-formation-question.md). **Original proposal status, preserved: conceptual development with an elementary conditional calculation and identified scientific comparators; the complete construction had not yet been executed.** This advances the named connection **formed organization → production of alternative response scopes → viable development → consequence-mediated retention → effective shared response** within [generative grounding](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md).

**Subsequent disposition, 6 October 2026:** Ren requested completion of the next step. The [local architecture construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION.md) now implements paid local fabrication and bonding, broad/filtered response paths, consequence-funded guide copying, another actual assembly and finite retention, with independent checks. This closes a declared conditional instance of the proposed mechanism. It reuses one inherited predictive relation rather than acquiring distinct target records; recipient-specific learning, co-development of centers and shared regulation, component-law identification and ethical justification remain open. The prospective comparisons below remain preserved with their original broader scope.

## The production of alternatives needs its own explanation

An organization changes which further organizations can form. Its components, accumulated skills, retained examples, available connections and surrounding supports make some reorganizations easier, some expensive and some inaccessible within the available time and resources. Its activity also changes those conditions. The alternatives subsequently exposed to selection therefore have a causal history of their own.

Production, developmental filtering and retention can interleave. Early retention can change which unfinished structures complete, and current organization already carries earlier selection histories. Distinguishing these causal roles does not impose a universal sequence in which all formation finishes before any selection begins.

This develops an existing commitment of Ren's theory. The [selection and generative-conditions account](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-04-selection-and-generative-conditions.md) already makes variation and transition depend on the current organization and its enabling conditions. The [formed-updater result](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UPDATER-FORMATION.md) constructs a change to a selector's proposal mechanism. The missing result is specifically how a route that can respond to another center is generated and retained. Another axiom declaring that generation favors care would leave that mechanism unexplained.

The proposed addition is:

> **Organized activity changes both what happens and what can be formed next. By producing, reusing and modifying the machinery of response, it changes the alternatives available to later learning and selection. The scope of responsiveness—what can become effective in a system's action—is itself a developing feature of that organization.**

Here “what can be formed next” concerns actual parts, construction paths, time, information and support. It does not mean that a successor has more unrestricted all-time reachable states than its predecessor. Under fixed laws, the predecessor could already reach those states by passing through that successor. What changes can be immediate access, cost, delay, likelihood or attainable performance within a specified budget.

## Five questions that a finished explanation must answer

| Question | What supplies an explanation |
|---|---|
| Why does the architecture arise? | Actual assembly, recruitment, duplication, reconnection or learning events generate it from prior organization. |
| Why does development finish? | Intermediate states can obtain or retain the support, parts and information needed to continue. A favorable completed design cannot pay an earlier unaffordable bill. |
| Why does it persist or spread? | Its actual consequences change maintenance, reconstruction, transmission or replacement through an identified mechanism. |
| Why does it respond to this recipient? | That recipient's condition reaches the retained route and changes an executable action, with its meaning and target preserved. |
| Why is the response warranted? | The recipient's relevant claims and the proposed action withstand factual and ethical scrutiny. Formation and persistence do not certify this. |

An observed abundance cannot distinguish easy production from long retention. An accurate shared predictor can remain connected to a private-only action rule. A persistent structure can be subsidized, and an active response can harm its target. These are different explanatory failures.

The [functional concern construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-DEVELOPMENT.md) gives an actual inherited route through learning, motor revision and paid recipient support, but supplies its shared target recipe. The later [recipient-transmission construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md) advances this: evidence-routing programmes are physically installed before use, and recipient resources can fund copies and their finite retention. Its uniform draw over four named routes still supplies the distribution of architectures. Neither accepted result should be discarded; the new task works backward from that remaining input.

## Reuse can generate sharing before helping supplies a return

A plausible route starts with machinery useful in an earlier task. Later inputs can recruit that machinery through local connection rules. This can produce a broader response scope before a benefit from helping the new target has occurred. The new architecture need not begin as a complete proposed programme called “care for others”.

For example, a constructed response module already receives a local deficit signal, produces a command and is retained through productive activity. A new compatible input can attach to an available input port; its target address must reach an appropriate actuator. Separate records can retain what works for the two targets. The resulting graph may use the same operation for both while preserving their different conditions. An inhibitor or ownership-sensitive gate can also form where the local rules permit one.

The constructive explanation must derive the completed graph from those local operations. It cannot hide the entire other-directed response in a primitive named “attach recipient”. Port compatibility, target interpretation, action direction and source access each need a stated local mechanism or an explicit remaining premise. Reducing a whole architecture to independently specified component laws is a substantive step; it does not derive those laws from nothing.

Three different kinds of sharing must remain visible:

- Shared prediction or representation can reveal that the same causal relation applies to different targets.
- Shared action or evaluation routing can let a condition at either target activate a response.
- Shared maintenance or transmission can let several centers sustain the machinery that supports them.

The first does not entail the second. The third need not involve repayment to the original actor. The [recipient-correction result](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md) also shows why reusing an operation must not be confused with overwriting different targets in one undifferentiated record. Sharing can require more careful differentiation.

Selection can retain a bundle of capacities without separately rewarding every later use. A general route could be retained through a range of earlier productive tasks and respond to a new recipient by generalization. That is a candidate historical explanation of a particular unpaid response. Its persistence after repeated costs remains another question, especially when cheap gating is available. It does not establish that helping is merely strategic, that the actor experiences compassion, or that the recipient's standing depends on the architecture's historical advantages.

## A small calculation makes the change in formation bias explicit

Consider a local assembly law with two already formed reusable motifs. One is a response motif with an available compatible input, present in amount \(e\); another is an identity-filtering motif, present in amount \(i\). For this illustrative law, the next sensor docking has independent competing exponential rates \(ae\) and \(bi\), with the amounts fixed until docking, \(a,b>0\), \(e,i\geq0\) and \(ae+bi>0\). If both motifs are absent, no docking occurs and this conditional probability is undefined. The probability that docking uses the response motif is

\[
p(e,i)=\frac{ae}{ae+bi}.
\]

Suppose prior productive activity actually finances and completes one more response motif, consumes its required parts, and leaves the incoming sensor's formation conditions otherwise as specified. Conditional on reaching that resulting state,

\[
p(e+1,i)-p(e,i)
=\frac{ab i}{(ae+bi)(a(e+1)+bi)}>0
\]

when \(i>0\). The equality follows by bringing the two fractions to a common denominator. Conversely, adding a filtering motif decreases the response-docking probability when \(e>0\). When \(i=0\) and \(e>0\), adding response motifs does not increase an already unit conditional probability.

The relevant insight is causal: prior activity can change the production distribution of future architecture by changing actual reusable material. No recipient reward occurs in this calculation. The docking law, component functions and prior funded-copy event are supplied conditions; docking alone does not establish useful wiring, recipient interpretation or ethical concern. Other changing quantities, depletion, delays and competing events require a larger model. Its probability is conditional on the stated reached state, not the unconditional chance that the whole developmental history succeeds.

This is an elementary illustration of a possible mechanism, not a new universal selection theorem. Its empirical value depends on identifying the motifs, measuring their formation and testing whether changing them changes assembly as predicted. A measured final frequency could also arise through differential loss after formation. The two mechanisms require separate observations.

## Shared regulation need not be added to an originally private self

The private-learner-first history is a useful specimen, not the required beginning of every generative process. A relational account also permits differentiated centers and a common regulator to form together. Parts of their regulation can depend on one another before an explicit ownership discriminator exists. An exclusively self-directed scope may then be a later specialization requiring its own construction and explanation.

This suggests two developmental branches: an inherited private pathway expands through reuse; or an initially shared regulatory process differentiates into target-specific capacities and selective gates. Neither requires treating the centers as one subject. Their separate activity, vulnerability and consequences must remain identifiable even if the regulator does not represent that distinction.

Social dependence can sustain support, control, asymmetric depletion or common failure. Protecting an indispensable partner also differs from protecting a center that supplies no compensating return. The latter claim needs an intervention on contribution or return, with the resulting support conditions still accounted for. The absence of an identity gate is not a theorem that everyone matters equally. The [self/other symmetry analysis](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/HUIGEN.md) already shows why consistent renaming can leave self and other in different evaluative classes.

## Existing science supports parts of this route

Parter, Kashtan and Alon's [facilitated-variation study](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000206) is a computational comparator. Under systematically recombined goals, evolved circuit and RNA organizations produced variations better suited to related new tasks. The benefit did not extend generally to unrelated random goals. Encoding, mutation and fitness were supplied. This supports a conditional relation between organizational history and future variation; it does not establish concern.

Catmur, Walsh and Heyes's [sensorimotor training experiment](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~ascch/Celia%27s%20pdfs/2007sensorimotor.pdf) changed existing observation–action mappings through incompatible training. Sixteen participants were selected from forty-four for clear initial physiological responses. Matched exposure and changed association support a role for learning in the mapping. Motor responses during observation are not welfare valuation or costly help, and this is modification of existing machinery rather than its complete origin.

Moriceau and Sullivan's [maternal-regulation experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1560090/) gives a developmental comparator. In twelve-to-fifteen-day rat pups, maternal presence changed learning from an odor–shock pairing; hormonal and neural interventions probed the mechanism. It shows that developmental regulation can depend on another organism before mature independence. It studies already differentiated organisms; it does not establish their common origin, formation of a common regulator, absence of self/other discrimination or the origin of caregiving. Approach despite painful pairing also cautions against equating socially supported learning with beneficial direction.

The separate [evidence assessment](GENERATIVE-DEVELOPMENT-EVIDENCE.md) considers acquired social valence. Its adult experiments do not establish the emergence of costly concern from no prior valuation. Together these sources identify component mechanisms and boundaries; no one study establishes the full proposed progression.

## The next construction and its decisive comparisons

Start from the actual learned predecessor and a declared stock of typed parts. Specify local assembly operations before inspecting which complete networks they produce. Shared routing, separate routing, identity filtering, conflicting records and omission must arise through the same allowed operations rather than an external draw over desirable programme names. Parts, fuel, timing, observations and unfinished assemblies remain in the history.

Measure production, completion, use and retention separately. Let genuine task consequences change the number or accessibility of templates and connection sites. Then measure whether those changes alter the next round of construction. No analyst's final performance score may choose the next architecture or repair an insolvent intermediate.

The main discriminators are:

1. **Change assembly scaffolds before novel recipient outcomes.** Match currently available function and accounted resources while changing which connection sites or templates are accessible. An altered distribution of initial constructions would identify a production effect rather than subsequent reward for helping.
2. **Freeze or replay the generator.** A replay can match the first construction distribution while preventing later template changes. Keep source and exposure schedules and non-generator operational laws matched; account for intervention costs, component stocks and timing. If the processes later diverge, test whether measured changes in assembly conditions explain the difference beyond those resource and timing effects. A conventional model with the same complete law should still match; the contrast is with a restricted fixed-generator explanation.
3. **Separate shared knowledge from shared response.** Preserve predictive information while varying the evaluative/action connection. Measure costly action for a known other, recipient outcomes and cheap suppression. Equal prediction with different protection would reject the sufficiency of shared cognition.
4. **Vary developmental ordering and partial support.** Compare private-first and shared-regulation histories, retaining all developmental costs. A completed circuit's performance cannot substitute for a viable route to its formation.
5. **Retain destructive competitors.** Cheap gates, excessive sharing, wrong correspondence, copying errors and failed construction must be admitted where the component law permits them. Changing availability of these competitors should change the claimed retention conditions.

These are proposed tests. A failed assembly law, absent production effect or cheap bypass would revise the candidate explanation. Evidence that the same sufficient conditions travel across identified material systems would support broader scope; a successful authored fixture alone would not.

The completed conceptual addition is an explicit object of explanation: **how organization produces and reshapes its available response architectures**. The next unresolved result is a constructed and tested shared-response path from local assembly through actual retention. The formation of motivation and the justification of its scope remain connected questions with distinct evidence requirements.
