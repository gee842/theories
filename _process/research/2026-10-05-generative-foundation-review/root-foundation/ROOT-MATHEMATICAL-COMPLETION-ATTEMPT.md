# Attempting universal coverage and necessary common grounding

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored philosophical completion attempt following Ren's request to establish both remaining claims: **independent generative principles cover all mathematics**, and **every adequate alternative must depend on the same root for mathematical constitution**. The [preceding witness investigation](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-UNIVERSALITY-ARGUMENT.md) supplies a positive starting point, not a substitute objective.

**Decision:** the present root does not yet warrant either universal conclusion. This note executes a stronger positive argument and a concrete comparison before reaching that decision. An explicit enriched candidate can be proposed without importing a list of target truths; no formal consistency result for that global candidate is claimed. Its extra logical, existence and fullness principles, however, are not consequences already established from the root. Its matched structural rival can preserve the mathematical content without accepting the alleged direction of ultimate dependence. These findings do not prove that a universal generative foundation is impossible. They prevent this completion attempt from being reported as a completed proof.

This follows the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The arguments concern relationships within one unfolding, not independent realms of objects, minds, constraints and activity. New mathematical results are accepted only through checked Lean declarations. The candidate constructions and extrapolations below are philosophical proposals and proof obligations, not unverified mathematical theorems.

## 1. Fix the two claims before trying to prove them

**ALL** asks for more than a scheme that works whenever a suitable mathematical interpretation has already been supplied. For each mathematical subject in the intended scope, the root must warrant its admissible objects or possibilities, its identity and operations, and the relevant logic and interpretation. A plural root may distinguish incompatible local regimes. It must preserve their interpreted questions rather than merge them into one undifferentiated truth value.

**MUST** asks for more than agreement on all results. An adequate alternative might reproduce the same objects, transformations, proofs and truth conditions while proposing another account of why those conditions obtain. The claim to prove is that a full alternative cannot retain the target without sharing the root's constitutive dependence, up to a justified equivalence. Merely defining an adequate account as one that accepts the root would beg this question.

The intended subject is not limited to effectively decidable theories, physically enacted constructions or mathematics already known by a learner. An atemporal generative constitution is admissible as a candidate. Conversely, its breadth does not authorize hiding complete truth in a primitive witness selector or calling every newly needed principle another name for the root.

## 2. State a root rich enough for the attempted universal argument

The [current positive root](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#1-the-positive-candidate) treats beings, activity and understanding as differentiated formations whose relationships enable and constrain transformation. The core and tree are not separate realities. Concrete organization may improve our articulation of the root rather than merely instantiate a permanently fixed sparse model.

For the mathematical completion attempt, consider the following explicit enrichment. These are proposed constitutive resources, not declarations that the current causal model has already derived them.

| Resource proposed for the root | What it supplies to the attempted construction | Present warrant |
|---|---|---|
| Typed differentiation and substitution | Determinate positions, composable operations and the preservation of identity through substitution. | Particular forms are realized and checked in existing constructions; unrestricted necessary scope is not established. |
| Finite formation and renewal | Arbitrarily extended finite configurations, generated expressions and proofs. | Supported conditionally by inductive constructions; their background logic and unbounded range remain explicit. |

| Interpreted consequence | Explicit truth conditions, quantifier ranges and logical operations appropriate to each admitted regime. | Particular semantic accounts are checked; their universal coverage and constitutive dependence on the root remain open. |

This candidate is not a hidden table of all mathematical sentences. Its principles describe kinds of formation, extension and interpretation. A short law can have consequences not enumerated in its statement. That is an actual explanatory possibility, illustrated by the existing equality evaluator.

But a law can carry substantive content without being a truth table. Full profile admission, unbounded renewal and a logical interpretation remain commitments even when stated briefly. We must assess them rather than count their words as evidence that their content has been derived.

## 3. Execute the strongest argument for ALL

The favorable argument starts with mathematical identity. If a mathematical subject is exhausted by its structural role, an account need not create an additional bearer behind that role. Independently specified formation and extension rules can identify the role and determine its interpreted consequences. The equality case already demonstrates this form of explanation without asking a general semantic selector for the answer.

Extend that thought to full structural profiles. At a fixed admitted level, selecting which earlier positions stand in a relation describes a candidate relation. Instead of demanding a finite recipe for every such selection, a full-profile principle admits the entire collection appropriate to that level. Higher formation can then take those relations as positions in further structures. Stratification is intended to avoid the demand that one domain code all its own predicates without enlargement. Semantics is likewise indexed to the specified language and level rather than asserted to be one unrestricted same-level truth predicate.

This is a serious candidate for broad coverage. It permits uncomputed and individually undefinable objects without equating mathematical possibility with finite physical construction. It can in principle describe different mathematical regimes as differently constrained formations. It also exposes its content: full profiles, higher-level admission and interpretation are explicitly doing work.

The decisive attempted justification is **no arbitrary exclusion**. If the proposed subject is all coherent profiles at a level, and a profile satisfies every independently fixed condition, exclusion would change that subject. In that conditional setting, the fullness demand has force. A specified completion cannot retain its exact role while arbitrarily deleting required realizers.

The inference fails if that conditional is promoted into a universal existence principle. Before the subject is fixed as the full profile space, the absence of a stated exclusion does not establish that every profile exists in the intended mathematical universe. Nor does the description of a profile select the level at which its totality is admitted. The [completion-warrant investigation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-MATHEMATICAL-ADMISSIBILITY.md#when-the-subject-warrants-completion) already separates an independently warranted admission contract from mere finite compatibility. Genuine global conditions survive that distinction.

There are consequently two honest versions of the proposed argument. We can **adopt** full-profile and stratified existence principles as foundational commitments, then investigate their consistency, coverage and consequences. Or we can claim to have **derived** them from the prior root, in which case a further argument must establish their necessity. The present work supports the first as an intelligible research candidate. It has not completed the second.

This is not the claim that supplied principles are outside generative reality. Their source and relation to other activity may be immanent. The unresolved question is why the proposed root requires precisely this fullness rather than a different admitted regime. An expanded description of the whole may include the answer; merely expanding the word *root* until it includes the needed answer would not explain it.

## 4. A universal conditional schema, and its exact force

There is an actually warranted universal result available within the current mathematical scope: the checked witness criterion applies to every finitary signature and every embedding satisfying its explicit atomic and existential-witness conditions. The [formal witness development](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/formal/WitnessHull.lean) supplies the proof. This is quantified mathematical scope, not a collection of tested examples.

Its foundational use is conditional in a further sense. An independently specified family of extension operations may establish the witness condition without consulting target sentence answers. The equality construction does so at its declared language. A proposed richer family must be tested similarly. The universal theorem can then carry that concrete adequacy to all formulas, including formulas not individually examined in establishing the recipe.

That constructive test has now advanced beyond equality. The [dense-order development](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/order/formal/DenseOrder.lean), with [actual Lean verification](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/order/verification.json), supplies an executable evaluator for every formula built from equality, strict order, negation, conjunction and quantification, under explicit dense-linear-order and no-endpoint conditions. Its rational realization generates candidates from zero, existing parameters, a unit before or after a parameter, and a midpoint. Formula correctness and truth agreement across the stated order class are checked; closure under the specified before/between/after operations also yields a truth-preserving generated substructure. This demonstrates another independent witness recipe rather than a semantic-answer selector. It neither derives the order assumptions from the root nor proves countability, all mathematical admission or unique grounding. The receipt records 33 theorems and 58 declaration audits. This is concrete local progress within the completion attempt, not evidence that its unrestricted coverage obligation has disappeared.

This gives a substantive research schema: **derive the subject's relevant atomic interpretation and an independently specified adequate extension mechanism, then use the checked semantic theorem for all formulas in that subject**. It does not define adequacy as allegiance to our ontology. The formal condition concerns actual witnesses and interpretation, while independence of the recipe requires a separate dependency audit.

The schema is not the requested universal completion. It says that any subject meeting these conditions receives the stated result. ALL additionally says that every mathematical subject obtains these conditions from our root, including subjects not covered by that finitary first-order semantics. The universal implication does not produce all its antecedents. An alleged witness scheme that simply asks whether each sentence is true would supply them at the cost exposed by the checked tagged-selector audit.

Thus the schema avoids target-answer import **where its independently justified recipe does**. No theorem in this round says that every possible mathematical regime has such an independently derived recipe. The distinction is the actual coverage obligation, not a demand that the theory somehow reason from a position outside all mathematics.

## 5. Execute the strongest argument for MUST

The favorable necessity argument is stronger than “a rival also uses our words.” A determinate mathematical account must preserve the relevant identity, admissibility and consequence relationships. If a rival denies all such differentiation while retaining the same mathematical content, it has not described that content. Where operations and substitutions constitute the subject, removing them removes the subject rather than merely changing its presentation.

Now suppose ALL has been achieved: the root supplies the complete adequate structural roles, including their interrelations. An alternative genuinely covering all the same mathematics must preserve those roles, including the maps and interpretations relevant to the claims. Differences solely in names or redundant carriers do not then constitute a new mathematical subject. This provides a serious argument for common **constitutive content** rather than for one privileged notation or an independently existing bearer behind every role.

The attempted last step is: since the alternative must retain that content, it must accept that the content depends on the root in the same ultimate way. That step does not follow from the preceding argument. The indispensability of a relationship does not determine which account of its explanatory priority is correct. If the root is defined as only that indispensable mathematical content, the necessity claim becomes narrower than the substantive common foundation under investigation. If it includes a particular direction of constitution, that direction still needs defense.

Inseparability does not by itself repair the step. Interdependent positions may be constituted together; their identity need not precede their relationships. This gives an affirmative reason to investigate whole structural constitution. It does not show that the whole's complete mathematical character follows from the present minimal generative principles, or that its character could not instead be an irreducible relational basis. Neither account requires independent subjects, inert objects or a fundamental split between constraint and activity.

The claim that all mathematical reasoning takes place within the unfolding also does not establish the missing priority. It addresses the formation of reasoning and its representations. The candidate rightly includes this history. The mathematical truths at issue need not depend on any particular reasoning episode. The account still has to justify the constitutive relationship rather than turn necessary conditions for describing a truth into its exclusive sufficient ground.

## 6. A matched alternative that directly contests the inference

Consider a structural-modal account of the same whole. It preserves every formation operation, admitted profile, identity condition, interpretation, generated practice and mathematical consequence claimed by the enriched candidate. It explains how particular roles and their relationships constitute mathematical subjects, and how actual learning and proof develop within their wider activity. It does not import detached observers or insist that laws are outside reality.

Its distinct proposal is that relational possibilities and their logical articulation specify what the allowed formations are. Those resources are not claimed to arise from a smaller independently justified root merely because they can be expressed as formation conditions. This is a different account of explanatory priority, or an explicit refusal to assert a priority not yet justified. It does not change a free grounding predicate in a model while ignoring the fuller theory; it preserves the actual relationships offered as the target and asks which proposed dependence has been explained.

The affirmative account can respond in two ways. It can give a reduction showing that the rival's resources arise from an independently justified generative basis with fewer independent commitments. That would be genuine progress toward MUST. Or it can demonstrate that the rival's full explanation is already the same constitutive account under a faithful translation. In the latter case there may be no substantive rivalry. The translation must preserve more than final verdicts: it must retain how admission, identity and interpreted consequence are explained.

Neither response has yet been completed for every mathematical regime. Naming the rival's full structural basis *generative* achieves a terminological reconciliation, not automatically the first reduction or the second equivalence. Conversely, the rival does not win merely by calling its resources primitive. It has its own explanatory obligations. The current result is that the necessity argument has not excluded this adequate-looking alternative or established the promised equivalence; it is not a certification that the rival is the finished correct metaphysics.

This comparison also clarifies the meaning of uniqueness up to equivalence. If equivalence is only agreement on mathematical truths, the result does not decide ultimate direction. If equivalence is defined to require the desired root-dependence, showing that every adequate rival falls within it is exactly the disputed claim. An informative argument must justify its preservation requirements independently, then show that the proposed translation meets them.

## 7. Why the global boundary is relevant without becoming an impossibility proof

The [checked coverage boundary](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/global/formal/CoverageBoundary.lean) tests one explicit interpretation of ALL: coding every predicate on a domain by an element of that same domain while admitting the relevant negated diagonal. Its hypotheses identify the exact self-coverage claim. The same development distinguishes covering predicates over an earlier domain with a larger code type and extending a code family while preserving its former interpretations.

This result does not refute the atemporal common-root proposal. A stratified or schema-based account need not make the excluded self-coding demand. It does show why writing *all* cannot silently turn one selected code space into a self-enclosing mathematical totality. Moving to a larger level can answer that boundary; the new level's existence and interpretation remain part of the explanatory commitments.

The boundary therefore improves the completion attempt's architecture without completing its universality. No claim that every mathematical truth is computable, finitely nameable or representable by an element of one fixed domain is required for the philosophical target. Avoiding those overclaims leaves ALL and MUST substantive rather than automatically contradictory.

## 8. Exact completion decision

The attempted enriched root is an explicit candidate for a universal foundation: state formation, identity, extension, completion and interpretation together; allow non-effective and stratified scope; and identify mathematical content with the resulting roles. Its full global axioms have not been specified and checked for consistency. This is a positive proposal whose content can be investigated. It is not dismissed because it is nondual or because mathematics is used to study its own constitution.

The two requested achievements are nevertheless **not completed under the current warranted premises**. ALL still needs justified full admission and semantic coverage beyond the independently established cases. MUST still needs an argument that every target-preserving adequate account shares the proposed constitutive dependence, rather than only the mathematical content. The strongest arguments tested above do not discharge these requirements: no arbitrary exclusion is conditional on an admission contract; transcendental indispensability supplies necessary content; inseparability favors a relational whole but does not determine its ultimate explanation.

Adopting the enriched principles may produce a useful conditional foundation. Adding an axiom that every mathematical subject is its expression and every adequate rival depends on it would make the desired conclusion formally available. It would also put both disputed achievements into the premises. The current work does not do that, and does not report local checked progress as completion of the requested universal claim.

The remaining burden is no longer an unspecified demand for another example. It is a choice between concrete kinds of further justification: derive a currently supplied global admission or semantic principle; explain why a specific alternative's constitutive account fails while preserving its target; or establish an independently motivated equivalence of complete explanatory resources. A result of one of these kinds would change the universal assessment. A richer local witness construction contributes evidence and technique, but alone leaves that assessment unchanged.

This is the finished assessment of the present completion attempt, not a proof that the stronger goal can never be reached. The theory's valid conditional mathematics and its substantive generative-structural interpretation remain intact. The user-requested universal claims remain open rather than being weakened and marked achieved.
