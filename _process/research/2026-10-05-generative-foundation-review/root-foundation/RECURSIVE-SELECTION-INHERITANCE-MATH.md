# Reconstructing the selection arrangement and operating its successor

7 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [recursive generative selection](RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION-MATH.md), supporting Ren's request to pursue its recursive implications and assess completion. **New result: a checked finite connection from actual productive operation through material reconstruction of the coupling arrangement to the successor's own operation.** The law of reconstruction and its origin remain supplied. This does not establish self-sustaining recursion, universal evolutionary convergence or universal normative grounding.

## 1. Which inference this advances

The preceding Lean study established that collective demographic success does not automatically increase the frequency of the response generating shared support. A favorable coupling can overcome a within-collective disadvantage; an exact one-generation threshold was checked. It explicitly left formation and inheritance of that coupling outside its interface.

This round adds the next finite connection: **the output of an actual coupling arrangement supplies carriers for reconstructing arrangements, and those reconstructed arrangements actually govern the next operation**. The sequence uses one initial stock throughout. The successor's output is not assigned from the favorable parent's label; it is computed from the coupling actually formed.

This credits existing results rather than claiming that heredity or development first entered the theory here. [Liangzhi evolution](LIANGZHI-EVOLUTION-MATH.md) already separates template success from transmitted response; [developmental reconstruction](LIANGZHI-DEVELOPMENT-MATH.md) already checks reconstructed response operation into a further generation with source exhaustion. The present study imports the immediately preceding demographic law and adds a distinct reconstruction of its **coupling arrangement**. It does not import or claim physical identity with the entire material developmental mechanism. The broad account in [GENERATIVE-SELECTION](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) remains the conceptual connection.

The universal inference being tested is whether a productive arrangement necessarily reproduces its own selection conditions. The answer here is conditional: actual output can enable those conditions to be reconstructed, but conversion during reconstruction can instead increase a different arrangement. This is an evolutionary premise relevant to the grounding inquiry, not a derivation of ethical authority from reproduction.

## 2. One represented process and its declared mechanisms

The [source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/RecursiveSelectionInheritance.lean) directly imports `RecursiveGenerativeSelection`. Each active arrangement consists of eight response carriers, organized into the predecessor's two collectives `(3,1)` and `(1,3)`. The weaker arrangement uses the prior law with `base=1, strength=1`; the stronger uses `base=1, strength=3`. “Stronger” names the greater coupling coefficient, not an ethical property or an assumed reproductive winner.

An `operate` transition calls the prior `pooled` function for whichever arrangements are actually active. A weak arrangement creates `(28,30)` response carriers, and a strong arrangement creates `(68,66)`. Each new carrier consumes **one free precursor** and **one activity-support unit**. Both are separate finite stocks, initially 3000 in the matched histories. Operation requires enough of both and at least one active arrangement. Previously active arrangements become retained inactive material; they do not disappear from the accounting.

An `assemble` transition reconstructs an active arrangement from four carriers of each response. This recipe supplies the subsequent division into `(3,1)` and `(1,3)` collectives. Those eight carriers move from loose carrier counts into the assembled arrangement; they are not destroyed or counted twice. Each assembly consumes one activity-support unit but no new precursor. Loose carriers not assembled remain present. Their source tags record which coupling actually generated them; the current recipe does not permit arbitrary cross-origin mixing.

These are differentiated relations within one represented history, following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The shared state contains both material and activity-support accounts; it does not divide an independent agent from a supporting world. The original opportunity multiplier and the new finite activity-support stock are **different quantities**. The multiplier remains a supplied operational relation; it neither replenishes the finite stock nor replaces the cost guard.

The source and activity conversions, initial stocks and seed organizations, group recipe, coupling repertoire and transmission rules are explicit local inputs. The model does not derive universal positive costs, a common currency for all conditions, biological material composition, or the origin of the recipe. The initial arrangements and their organization are supplied; this round does not begin from unorganized absence.

## 3. Actual inheritance and the exact condition

The first operation of one weak and one strong arrangement yields the two stated carrier pools. Within the declared source-preserving recipe, their response counts permit at most **seven weak-origin daughters and sixteen strong-origin daughters**. `assembly_row_limits` proves these upper bounds for every actual admitted assembly with the relevant source counts. Both capacities are attained in each matched history.

A transfer table records **source coupling by row and reconstructed coupling by column**:

| Source | Reconstruct weak | Reconstruct strong |
|---|---:|---:|
| Weak source | `weakToWeak` | `weakToStrong` |
| Strong source | `strongToWeak` | `strongToStrong` |

For an actual assembly with no prior active arrangements and full row totals seven and sixteen, `fully_assembled_strong_share_rises_iff` proves:

> The reconstructed strong arrangements outnumber the weak arrangements exactly when `strongToWeak ≤ weakToStrong + 4`.

Because the initial arrangement counts are one of each, this is an increase in strong-arrangement frequency over the initial `1/2`. It is an exact condition for these capacities and recipe, not a universal inheritance threshold. It explicitly distinguishes the greater output of a source from the prevalence of its reconstructed coupling. The analogous row/column distinction in the earlier Liangzhi work is credited; this round's increment is that both the table's carrier availability and its successor operation occur in the same checked history.

The transfer rules are supplied recipes. Their actual entries are admitted only if the required carriers and activity support exist. The model **does not evolve or select the transfer table itself**, explain why faithful reconstruction forms, or infer a general advantage of preserving it.

## 4. Two complete matched histories

Both histories start with 3000 precursors, 3000 activity-support units, one weak arrangement and one strong arrangement. No resources enter later.

The common first operation produces 192 new carriers, uses 192 precursors and 192 support units, and retains the two seed arrangements as inactive material. The next operation reconstructs 23 daughters from 184 of those carriers, retains the other eight loose carriers, and consumes 23 support units. Both histories now have 2808 precursors and 2785 available support.

| Result | Faithful table `(7,0;0,16)` | Conversion table `(6,1;15,1)` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reconstructed weak / strong arrangements | `7 / 16` | `21 / 2` |
| All four transmission channels positive | No | Yes |
| Strong arrangement share compared with initial `1/2` | Higher | Lower |
| New response carriers from the daughters' actual operation `(A,H)` | `(1284,1266)` | `(724,762)` |
| Total newly formed carriers in that second operation | `2550` | `1486` |
| Final free precursor | `258` | `1322` |
| Final available / used activity support | `235 / 2765` | `1299 / 1701` |
| Final inactive eight-carrier arrangements | `25` | `25` |

The second operation invokes exactly the coupling each daughter received. The conversion history's descendants therefore use 21 weak and two strong instances of the prior law, despite the strong source's greater first output. No favorable second-generation ratio is assumed to hold: the actual reconstructed counts enter the operational function.

The final loose response counts include the eight carriers left over from assembly. They are `(1288,1270)` in the faithful history and `(728,766)` in the conversion history. This distinction prevents reporting a retained carrier as newly produced. Both final states retain total material **3016**: the initial 3000 precursors plus 16 carriers already organized in the two seeds.

`faithful_two_generation_history` and `converted_two_generation_history` check every operation/assembly equality and the stated comparisons. The second witness also checks that every transfer entry is positive. Higher realized output in the faithful branch draws more of the finite sources; it is not a proof that this branch indefinitely renews them or has a universal efficiency advantage.

## 5. General connected accounting and effective failure guards

The material measure is

\[
M=\text{free precursor}+8(\text{active weak}+\text{active strong}+\text{inactive})
  +\text{all loose response carriers}.
\]

`operation_accounts` and `assembly_accounts` prove, for every admitted transition, both conservation of `M` and conservation of **available plus cumulatively used activity support**. The account of used support is a record of consumption, not reusable fuel.

`connected_two_generation_accounts` composes these results over an actual `operate → assemble → operate` history. Its hypotheses are actual operational equalities under the guards; it does not license arbitrary endpoints or insert a fresh stock at the generation boundary. This is a generic connected-history result, not just a numerical comparison of the two selected endpoints.

`insufficient_source_or_excess_assembly_fails` checks five meaningful obstructions: the seed cannot operate with only 191 activity-support units, cannot operate with only 191 precursors, cannot assemble eight weak-origin daughters, cannot assemble seventeen strong-origin daughters, and cannot complete the 23-daughter assembly with only 22 support units. A named successor or stronger coupling bypasses none of these conditions.

## 6. What closes and what remains open

This closes the predecessor's **finite two-generation interface**: output carrier pools now fund an explicit reconstruction, changed inheritance can reverse the coupling's relative prevalence, and the reconstructed coupling actually runs again with all stocks and residual material carried forward.

It does not close the origin or selection of reconstruction rules, repeated renewal of the source stocks, emergence of further coupling types, changing boundaries or timescales, empirical realization, indefinite favorable recursion, or ethical warrant. The two transfer recipes were chosen for a matched comparison; the model does not discover or reward either because it is named generative. Nor is the weak arrangement or converted response shown to be wholly degenerative or ethically bad.

A finite operative chain contributes to the accepted conditional foundation. It cannot replace the stronger requirement to explain how the relevant laws and supporting relations themselves form, nor the first-priority transition from whole generative realization to justified ethical quality and practical force. A claim of completed universal metaethics would remain unwarranted by this result.
