## A. Ren's central terms

### A1. 生生 (D1) and the generative space (D22)

**The reading now.** D1: "生生 is read as the generative space: the widening of what can form" (`Web.Widens`); the three scopes of 7 October are scopes of what counts as a formation. D22: widening reads Ren's "these are space where generatively happens" and "leads to more futures(flourishes/plurality)".

**What it keeps.** Ren's confirmed reading of "more". On 10 October Ren answered "yes to both readings". The first was that possibilities are what can form next, and that one future is more than another when it keeps everything the other has and adds some ([source](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-axiom-route-levels-and-possibilities.md)). Widening adds no premise and goes one way. It does not count a malignant formation as generative, because the formation's part lessens what others can form (`Ranking.malignant_possible`, `Ranking.possible_contest`).

**What it loses.**

1. *A space and the widening of a space are different things.* One is where something happens; the other is a relation between two courses. The chapter names 生生 three ways:
   - "life in the whole and its continuing generation" (§6.1, and AGENTS.md);
   - "the arising within the whole and its going on generating further" (§10);
   - "the generative space, the widening of what can form" (§8.3, from D1).

   The first two say what 生生 is. The third says how its direction is compared.
2. *The part of 生生 that generates its own continuation.* Ren's treatise 生生不息论 v1, from Ren's zhishan repository (imported July 2026, [SOURCES](../../../../sources/SOURCES.md)), defines it under the heading "生生：生成者生成新的生成" ([text](../../../../sources/06-zhishan/shengsheng/生生不息论v1/生生不息论.md)):
   - "“生生”因此不是一个输出接着一个输出，也不是现有形式的无限复制，而是**generation generating generation**：这一次生成的结果，又保存、恢复、传递或创造了下一轮生成的条件与生成者。普通生成改变接下来的状态；生生还改变接下来能够形成哪些状态，以及世界能否继续发现新的形成方式。"
   - Its diagram has ordinary generation take the current state to the next state. 生生 takes (current state, 可成为之域, 生成能力) to (new state, new 可成为之域, new 生成能力).

   Ren said the same on 10 October: "being generative is its existence creates more possibilities of becoming - more future, not less, it is about number of formations, renewal capacity, or richness of relations, and it is about if the continuation of becoming continues itself". Widening compares only one component of that triple, what can form, between two level paths. A part that opens one more formation once, and loses nothing, widens.
3. *Two of Ren's measures are not read.* "Renewal capacity" and "richness of relations" (10 October) are open in chapter §8.2 ("What can form next"). The same holds for the treatise's 生成能力 and for the rising complexity of 7 October.

**Proposed reading.** Three terms, each with its own carrier. None adds a premise.

- **生生 is generation generating generation.** What forms preserves, restores, transmits or creates the conditions and generators of further forming, so the course changes what can form and the capacity to form along with the state. The core states its structure in laws, not in one predicate:
  - with nothing given from beyond, what can form is opened only by what the supported give ([support-from-the-supported](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/support-from-the-supported.md), D35, which already reads Ren's "continues itself");
  - where arising depletes finitely many conditions, unceasing generation needs renewal made by formations within the unfolding (`Web.Unceasing.regeneration_recurs`, [record](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/unceasing-generation-needs-renewal.md));
  - a whole whose parts pass persists only through the generation of its parts ([record](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md));
  - a renewing formation does not consume (`Web.renewing_not_consuming`).

  The glossary row for 生生 already gives no Lean anchor; this keeps it so. A single predicate would be a further proof, claimed on the board.
- **The generative space is what can form now.** The treatise again: "在当下，生显为尚可成为的可能性空间；在时间中，生则是潜在结构实现为象、并重构下一轮可能空间的过程". Ren confirmed reading possibilities as what can form next (10 October). Ren's sentence of 11 October defines the region where something's continuation widens this space (D22, kept).
- **The direction, "more 生" and "more future, not less", is compared by widening** (`Web.Widens`), with the four cases of D2: generative, neutral, degenerative, mixed. The treatise's "生生维护的不是选项数量" agrees: widening is inclusion, not a count of options.
- The rest of D1 stays: the three scopes as scopes of what is counted, courses compared on possibilities, and 生生 as the object of the ethical direction rather than any new organization.

**What would change it.**
- Ren saying 生生 names only the direction. D1 then stands, with the types corrected: 生生 is the widening, and the generative space is what is widened.
- A case that renews the conditions and generators of its own further forming, which Ren judges not 生生.
- Ren adopting renewal capacity or richness of relations as the measure of "more". That would be a premise beyond widening, since it orders mixed parts.

**Question for Ren.** Is 生生 the generation that generates generation, with widening as the comparison of its direction? Or is 生生 the widening itself?

### A2. 生生不息 (D5)

**The reading now.** D5 reads 生生不息 at the whole as "generation that does not cease, arising that recurs through renewal". In a bounded whole whose rest is idle this is return, and endless novelty needs gain from beyond every bounded part (P111).

**What it keeps.**
- The level of the whole: Ren, 7 October, "一种整体的……方向" ([source](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md)).
- No demand for growth. The treatise: "真正的“不息”不是数量的无限增加", and "生生不息的核心不是“永远扩张”，而是**不绝其源，不塞其流，不毁其后**". Ren on 25 September: "we can grow exponentially till we met our need, but we can't compounding forever" ([source](../../../../sources/16-claude-code-directions/2026-09-25-the-mind-not-the-approach.md)).
- The treatise's 不息, "不是永不消亡，而是不使生成之源枯竭" (its section heading), which is arising that recurs through renewal.
- The theorems of `Becoming.Gain`.

**What it loses.** D5 counts as 生生不息 a whole that returns for ever. Two of Ren's texts exclude that:
- the treatise: 生生 is "不是现有形式的无限复制";
- Ren's answer of 7 October: "生生不息 - 一种整体的，不断创造更多生的方向，而这个方向产生的可能性，多样性越高，复杂性/复杂结构的复杂度可以不断的上升，就是一个好的方向".

The core has the case:
- `Recycle.closed_unceasing` is a closed cycle whose total stays one and in which arising never ceases. Under D5 it is 生生不息, though nothing new can ever form in it.
- More generally, under the first law a finite whole whose rest gives nothing net stays within its stock (`Flow.whole_bounded_of_rest_idle`), so with natural-number amounts it reaches only finitely many configurations. It keeps revisiting configurations it has had (`Flow.whole_recurs_of_rest_idle`).

Such a whole copies existing forms. Its source has not dried up, but complexity cannot keep rising in it.

**Proposed reading.**
- **不息 keeps D5's reading.** Arising recurs through renewal: the source of generation does not dry up. This is the treatise's 不绝其源, with its five tests as necessary conditions.
- **生生不息 is 生生 going on at the whole with more still able to form.** It is not a return into copies of existing forms. Complexity can keep rising, though nothing requires it to rise.
- **P111 then says what it needs, without redefining it.** Under the first law, a family new at every step is given more than it takes, from the rest or across its boundary, at arbitrarily late steps, and the same holds for every bounded family containing it (`Flow.new_beyond_every`). That is inflow from beyond, not growth.
- **The consequences:**
  - A bounded whole whose rest is idle has 不息 only as return, and return falls short of 生生不息.
  - A part fed from beyond, as the Earth is by the sun, can have it as long as the inflow lasts.
  - The whole itself can have it only if it is not a finite whole under the first law.
  - Whether the actual whole is such a whole is empirical correspondence (chapter §8.7), not a reading.
- **Scope.** Return is forced here by natural-number amounts, a commitment of the representation. With real amounts a bounded whole need not revisit a configuration exactly, and what replaces return there is open (§8.5, the scope of representation).

**What would change it.**
- Ren counting return as 生生不息. D5 then stands.
- Ren requiring novelty at every step rather than more still able to form. The same theorem gives what that needs.
- Ren reading 不塞其流 alone as the criterion. A closed cycle's flow is not blocked, so that reading favours D5.

**Question for Ren.** Does a whole that returns for ever, copying forms it has had, have 生生不息?

### A3. "Entangled" (D39 (4)), and the scope of D39 (3)

**The reading now.**
- (4): "how entangled a whole is reads as how strongly its exchanges are coupled to each other's levels", with quantum entanglement declined.
- (3): "a held difference keeps arriving". It arrives without bound through a chain, and a loop of strength one doubles it every step.

**Ren's sentence** ([source of OPENING](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-truth-exists-opening.md)): "It is clear that at this moment in time, the whole earth has become a highly entangled system, everyone country, every person, is heavily coupled into it, which is also the reason why you are reading this."

**What (4) loses.** The sentence has two clauses, and D39 reads both by one measure.
- "Heavily coupled into it" is strength: the theory's adopted term "couples into" (the N1 renaming) with a large strength.
- "A highly entangled system" says something of the whole: its couplings are tangled, they come back.

Strength alone does not say that. A chain of strong couplings with no loop is heavily coupled and not entangled. What it carries to a condition is fixed by finitely many orders of response (`carriedWithin_exact_of_no_loop`, in `Becoming.Loops`).

**Proposed reading (4).** Two clauses, two structures, both already in the core.
- **"Heavily coupled into it"** is coupling strength (D39 (1), kept).
- **"A highly entangled system"** means the couplings lie on loops through the whole. What an act's difference does then comes back around, and no finite order of responses fixes it.
  - With every condition listed, where no loop lies upstream a finite order is exact (`carriedWithin_exact_of_no_loop`).
  - A fed loop whose couplings hold at every step leaves every order inexact downstream of it (`carriedWithin_lt_of_loop`).
  - "Highly" is then the strengths along the loops and how often they act. Whether a difference runs away depends on both: a loop of recurring couplings need not run away (`Frequency.Sparse`, P123).
- **Still declined:** identification with quantum entanglement. What the two share is that the whole does not separate into parts computed apart. That is an analogy, not an identification.
- **Why it matters.** It joins the word to what OPENING argues. In an entangled whole the consequences of an act return to the actor through the loops, and only a loop can make a difference run away (P118, as Ren took it on 16 October; see B8 for its scope). Interdependence, in the ordinary sense, is mutual dependence, which is a loop of length two.

**D39 (3), its scope** (review item 48). Keep it with the review's scope. "A held difference keeps arriving" is shown under a law that takes nothing and so creates amounts (`ExchangeLaw.carrying`); under the first law it is not shown.

One limit is already in the core. In a finite whole under the first law whose rest gives nothing net, levels stay within the stock (`Flow.whole_bounded_of_rest_idle`), so the difference between two such courses stays within it too. Unbounded arrival needs amounts brought in from beyond, or created. That is the theory's form of Ren's "we can't compounding forever".

**Questions for Ren.** Read "a highly entangled system" as loops, with strength as how highly; or as strength alone? Does "a held difference keeps arriving" stand with its scope?
