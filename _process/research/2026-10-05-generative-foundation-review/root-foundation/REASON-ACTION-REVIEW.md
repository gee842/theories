## Formal process defect found and repaired

Initial formal inspection found that the generic wrapper admitted
`.execute (.sense .actor)` after a recipient-bound import. Authorization
checked the recipient register but not the physical trial's target. A legal
old-wrapper word could therefore rent, sense the actor, work and settle while
crediting the actor and leaving the intended recipient unchanged. The numerical
implementation always used a recipient target, so its tests did not expose
this broader process defect.

The root requested repair of the law rather than merely narrowing prose.
The author added `RecipientOnly` to reason-mediated and voluntary events:
sensing must name the recipient, and reserve/work/acquire/write must retain
the actual recipient phase. This includes settlement after a cut, preventing
an already configured actor-target phase from being passed through that port.
