## Primary comparison read by root

Federico Echenique, Antonio Miralles and Jun Zhang, **“Fairness and efficiency for allocations with participation constraints,”** arXiv:1908.04336v3, May 2020. The [record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04336) retains the earlier title including “probabilistic”; root read the abstract and introduction of the [v3 primary full-text HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.04336v3), not its complete proofs.

The paper studies allocation with supplied reservation utilities, where ordinary envy-freeness can conflict with participation. Its proposed fairness criterion limits justified envy by reference to those constraints. This is a useful comparison for why existing participation conditions can shape allocation. It does not establish that an observed fallback is ethically protected: reservation utilities and preferences are model inputs. We do not adopt them as primitive private values or import the paper's existence results as proofs of our framework. Our task is to explain which continuing support is warranted before such constraints can represent it. An independently supplied constraint is not thereby ontologically external to the generative history.
