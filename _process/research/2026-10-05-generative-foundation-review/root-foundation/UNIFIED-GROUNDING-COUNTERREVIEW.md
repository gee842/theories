# Counterreview: patient value, practical uptake and eligible actors

6 October 2026. AI-authored assumption and counterexample review for Ren's continued investigation of unified grounding. This note addresses **an actual experiential burden → a reason for an unrelated capable actor**. It develops the exact challenge posed by the [phenomenal-identity investigation](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#32-phenomenal-identity-practical-scope-and-the-necessary-root), while preserving the [accepted conditional foundation](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate). This is an original conceptual analysis, not an empirical finding, external peer review or new formal verification.

**Finding:** present effective control is a real actor–patient relation, even without antecedent responsibility. The proposed bridge must justify why that relation makes an independently identified patient good practically relevant. Its strongest opponent need not deny patient value, fitting concern, evidence or special duties. Two different restrictions remain to be assessed: requiring a prior normative relationship before a burden supplies an action reason, and restricting reasonhood to actors with an appropriate route of practical receptivity. The latter need not deny a universal patient-grounded moral claim. Neither restriction is established merely by being formally consistent.

## 1. Preserve the claim under test

Fix a patient p undergoing a still-unwanted aversive episode e. Grant, for this investigation, that ending this episode is good for this patient at this time. Do not infer that judgment merely from resource receipt, withdrawal behavior or an observer's unpleasant reaction. Fix an actor a who did not cause the episode, undertake its care, acquire its advantages or receive a legitimate delegation to remedy it. The actor has reliable evidence and an available permissible intervention that would end this same episode. Relevant costs, competing claims and possible harms must be recorded rather than stipulated away after a comparison fails.

The target is some **objective contributory practical consideration addressed to a for p's sake**: what the intervention does for p favors that intervention even without an antecedent helping desire. Calling this a moral reason states the proposed target rather than settling whether an internalist must accept it as a reason in that sense. It is not a conclusion about equal weights, a duty to act regardless of cost, deserved blame, a directed claim enforceable against a, or the psychological inevitability of helping. A simple case with no contrary reasons tests the ground without resolving those additional issues. A case with a small cost can still test whether any patient-directed reason exists, although observed refusal cannot identify its absence.

Calling a “unrelated” means no independently established prior responsibility or special tie. It does not mean that no relation exists: a now controls an available difference in p's condition. The work is to explain the practical significance of **that current relation**, not to erase all relations and transfer a free-floating value between persons.

## 2. A richer rival than an indifferent machine

Consider a view with these commitments:

- The episode really is bad for p, independently of a's preference, and its relief would be good for p.
- The facts warrant accurate recognition and fitting compassionate concern by anyone who understands them.
- Evidence can correct the actor's beliefs and affective appraisal. The actor accepts epistemic requirements and can revise practical commitments when their grounds warrant revision.
- Causing unjustified harm supplies reasons for restraint and repair. Parenthood, valid undertakings and legitimate delegation can supply additional patient-directed reasons. Special duties are real rather than disguised private benefits.
- Positive assistance reasons nevertheless require a further actor–patient relation: self-concern, responsibility, an undertaking or a comparable independently justified tie. Mere feasible control is insufficient.

This rival denies a baseline action reason in the specified noncauser case, not merely a decisive duty. It need not deny a reason to feel compassion or to refrain from making matters worse. It can acknowledge that helping would improve a genuinely valuable condition while denying that every such improvement addresses every potential helper.

The distinction is logically available at the declared scope. Let B describe the admitted patient burden, C an effective permitted means, F fitting concern, and H the rival's relevant prior relation. The rival can accept B and C, assign F to every informed actor, and make the particular assistance reason depend on B, C and H. Simultaneously renaming actors, patients and their relationships preserves this rule. Epistemic reasons and nonmaleficence can remain separately valid. Hence those admitted premises alone do not force the assistance reason where H is absent. This is a hand counterinterpretation of those premises; it is not a demonstration that H is a morally justified exclusive gate.

The exclusive-gate claim carries a substantive burden. Showing that responsibility adds a reason, or strengthens one, does not show that its absence removes every reason. A relation can justify unequal priority without justifying a zero baseline. Nor does distinguishing action from omission itself establish which reasons accompany each. The rival must explain why current permissible control over this admitted burden is insufficient, while the affirmative account must justify why it is sufficient for at least some practical relevance.

## 3. Concern and the use of means have different thresholds

“Fitting concern” can identify different achievements:

1. Correctly understanding and affectively registering the patient's burden.
2. Appropriately wishing or desiring that the burden end.
3. Having a reason to adopt relief as a practical end within one's available activity.
4. Having a reason to use a particular available means to that end.

These should not be collapsed. Compassion can fit an irreversible loss when no remedy exists. A fitting wish can concern an outcome whose pursuit is impermissible. Instrumental rationality concerning adopted ends does not automatically require adopting every fitting wish as an end. The present permissible-control case removes those two obvious obstacles, but the removal alone does not prove the transition from 2 to 3.

This locates a useful affirmative target: **when an independently good outcome for a patient is an appropriate object of concern, and an actor has a permissible effective way of bringing it about, does that fact supply some reason for practical uptake?** The justification should name why permissible ability changes the appropriate response from acknowledgment alone to possible action. It need not claim that every fitting attitude obliges action or that prior responsibilities become irrelevant.

A rival recognizing compassion while rejecting all practical uptake is not formally inconsistent. It can distinguish appropriate affect from the ends governing action. But describing the distinction does not justify it in the matched case. The proposed ground should be assessed for its explanatory adequacy: does the same patient burden explain both responses, and what makes the additional restriction appropriate? This is the precise question on which operator substitution can exert pressure; adding more factual accuracy alone cannot answer it.

## 4. Moral claims and an actor's deliberative access can diverge

A different rival accepts a patient-grounded moral demand that applies to any suitably situated operator, yet restricts an actor's practical reasons to considerations accessible through an appropriate route from that actor's motivations. It may therefore accept a claim addressed to a resistant actor while denying that the actor possesses a corresponding reason in its restricted sense. That disagreement cannot be reported simply as absence of universal ethics.

Keep three predicates distinct: M for the objective moral claim or standard concerning the actor, R for the actor-addressed contributory reason in the sense under investigation, and A for the actor's capacity to appreciate or act upon it. A particular theory may identify or connect these, but it must defend those connections. Replacing a disputed R with M does not refute an internalist account of R; treating a failure of A as defeat of M presupposes the contrary account.

The eligible actor class must therefore be fixed independently. Reliable evidence, ordinary deliberative competence and physical ability do not by definition include endorsement of the proposed patient-ground. Conversely, current refusal does not establish inability to understand it. Defining “capable” as already possessing correct universal concern would make the claimed extension uninformative. An appropriate acquisition route must also have independently defended constraints: permitting any arbitrary change of motivation would trivially allow installation of the desired concern. The useful test asks whether the defended practical capacity itself supplies or warrants an acquisition route, and whether that route addresses this patient's condition rather than merely producing generic approval.

## 5. What a controlled comparison must preserve

For an operator-substitution argument, hold fixed the actual patient, episode, sufficient patient-good ground, relevant effect, permissible means and defeaters. Change who can use the means, while explicitly tracking any change in self-relation, responsibility, legitimate role or practical capacity. Physical sameness of the effect does not itself establish normative irrelevance of these changes. That relevance is what the argument tests.

Three outcomes would be informative:

- A justified factorization of the baseline reason through patient burden and permissible effective control defeats the exclusive historical-role gate at that scope. Special relations can still add weight or distinct responsibilities.
- A justified relation essential to this particular reason defeats the proposed factorization, without showing that no other patient-directed reason exists.
- A demonstration that the substituted operator preserves the relevant capacity but lacks a necessary practical uptake route challenges a claimed constitutive route. Mere refusal or absence of an installed positive association does not demonstrate that lack.

Do not confuse a reason to avoid creating a burden with a reason to relieve a burden encountered independently. An experimenter can accidentally add the missing relation by instructing a participant to care for a beneficiary, obtaining an undertaking, or deploying an agent under an entrusted assistance task. Helping in that setting may realize a genuine duty, but it does not discriminate the noncauser baseline from the special-role explanation. Historical agency, authorization and task meanings must be retained alongside the measured behavior.

## 6. What this changes in the unified investigation

The current non-entailment result concerns a particular descriptive formalization. It does not establish that value requires an additional physical substance, that every experiential identity is false, or that normative and generative explanation must remain separate. A successfully defended identity may constrain interpretations that an impoverished language left open. It must still preserve the actual practical role justified rather than slide from intrinsic badness to unlimited action address.

The new discriminating result is the separation of **patient-ground, appropriate response, adoption of a practical end, and use of means**, together with the difference between **an objective moral claim and its deliberative availability**. These distinctions identify exactly what a current-control or operator-substitution argument must establish. They also show why existing role-bound repair and learned influence are genuine achievements without settling the stronger claim by accumulation.

The next review should test a positive account of practical uptake against both restrictions above. It should credit any defended baseline immediately without demanding a unique complete ranking, and preserve a failed factorization if a relevant actor relation defeats it. No new simulation, scientific rerun or formal compilation is needed to establish this conceptual discrimination. The present note changes no acceptance status of the conditional foundation.
