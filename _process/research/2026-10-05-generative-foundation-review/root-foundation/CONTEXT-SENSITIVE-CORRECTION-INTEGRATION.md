## What the result does and does not localize

The alternate inquiry separates the fixed false-contact case from the other
two. It still cannot distinguish ordinary success from calibration failure:
both actual weak pairs are `01`. Under a wider fault family, the new bit would
not establish a unique diagnosis without additional correspondence grounds.
These saved cases compare different fixed contact/application laws; they do
not implement a temporal change in those laws.

Diagnosis and adequate continuation are different questions. The two failures
both have full-load law `00` and source zero, so no present relay setting
restores their load. Nor do the cases establish disjoint adequate corrections.
A common response can be sufficient under an appropriate continuation
contract even when the precise fault remains unknown. Withholding an
unsupported claim is not itself completed service. The existing
[inquiry-sufficiency mathematics](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md) already makes
this distinction; it is not a new general theorem from this round.

The generative explanation is correspondingly specific. Activity brings
about a response; contact transforms how that response participates in later
activity; retained differences reorganize which command is selected. These
are differentiated relations within one history. The supplied local law
does not become external to reality merely because its origin is not explained
here. Equally, naming the history generative does not settle contact reliability,
autonomous inquiry, the legitimacy of its undertaking or whole-process good.
