# What retained consequences justify investigating next

6 October 2026. AI-authored argument continuing the [practical-boundary test](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-ETHICAL-ARGUMENT.md#a-discriminating-test-at-an-actual-decision-point) under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The target is the reached four-pair state before further teaching. The positive connection is **a formation retains evidence of consequences it imposed; interpreting that evidence can warrant changing what is investigated next and how it is investigated**. No new controller or ethical axiom is introduced.

## 1. What this inquiry inherits, and what changes now

The [earlier normative settlement](../../../history/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/normative/FOUNDATION.md) already defends acting under uncertainty without treating uncertain harm as zero or making every imaginable risk overriding. Its [decision results](../../../history/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/normative/decision-math.md) distinguish robust dominance, surviving options and the information needed for particular guarantees. The [evaluative application](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) already includes inquiry, delay, intrusion, expense, authority and foregone alternatives. None of those general ideas is a new result here.

The new local development changes the proposed problem. The learner has no separate cumulative recipient-loss register, but absence of such a register need not mean absence of retained evidence. Before replacement, the conducting sockets themselves can record how many recipient losses occurred. The ethical question therefore cannot start by presuming that another recipient-paid trial is needed to discover the previous depletion. It must ask what remains unsettled, which retained structure bears on that question, and what further activity would actually resolve.

The current [applicability reconstruction](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-MECHANISM.md) provides an important constraint: recipient credit is not a representation of a recipient's own support-dependent activity or experience. Its translation result concerns the separate identification law. It cannot be transferred unchanged to the plasticity law, which actually debits the recipient and guards teaching on an available balance. Here too, preserving the amount of a consequence does not establish its full significance.

## 2. A material record need not be a report

The [newly checked accounting study](PRETRAINING-EVIDENCE-MECHANISM.md) establishes the following relationship. The exact history family comprises successive complete, faithful `PlasticityWitness.pair y x false` words in [PairedPlasticity][plasticity], each runnable and begun with `count < 4`. It is not the family of arbitrary event words. Within it, a faithfully acquired recipient loss requires binding an empty socket before the pair can finish. An acquired absence of recipient loss permits the skip route. Replacement is not available within those first four pairs. From the declared empty-socket entry and unchanged protocol, each completed recipient loss therefore leaves one occupied socket and a net two-unit recipient debit.

For this provenance-bounded family, write \(N\) for occupied sockets and \(D_0\) for depletion from the protected incoming recipient balance after the completed prefix. The relation is

\[
D_0=2N.
\]

Equivalently, the local balance conserves `patient + 2*sockets`; the actual empty-socket entry with recipient 40 yields `patient + 2*sockets = 40`. The reached aligned state has two occupied sockets, so its four-unit depletion is represented in the acquired formation itself. A sign records the paired own-loss relation; occupancy records that a recipient loss occurred. Positive motivation is not what makes the latter record informative. Conversely, negative or cancelling influence is not proof that this evidence has disappeared. The same study's local replacement-pair theorem preserves `patient + 2*(sockets + retired)`: the actual continued state retains six units of depletion through two occupied sockets and one retired part even when transmission cancels. This extension is not a theorem about arbitrary future event words.

This statement has a strict historical scope. A current socket count after an incomplete operation need not include a fresh debit that precedes binding. Preinstalled sockets, altered return laws, extra transfers or a different origin require their own accounting. The target is depletion relative to the specified incoming condition, not total welfare or an eternal claim to a number.

Retained information, an available way to read it, justified interpretation and actual use remain distinguishable relations within this activity. The invariant can show that the structure carries a distinction without showing that the present runtime decodes it or understands its provenance. A paid, warranted interpretation of the formation record is an explanatory target; it is not an existing deliberative capacity supplied by our ability to inspect the code.

There is also a concrete continuation constraint. At the selected count-four state, `continuation=true` makes the limit six, so `.close` is not enabled. The earlier branch that serves after four pairs starts with `continuation=false`. The separately completed [application-revision source audit](APPLICATION-REVISION-RECONSTRUCTION.md), with its [review](APPLICATION-REVISION-REVIEW.md), establishes that reaching service from this state requires two further recipient payments: four units of gross debit, not unavoidable net loss. A refunding continuation is possible. A scope cut can block further teaching but also removes command applicability. These are credited findings of that concurrent study, not discoveries of this argument. Neither ending a mathematical finite prefix nor borrowing the sibling's service establishes an implemented option to stop, decode and repair here. Any such alternative must be justified and realized in the connected situation before it can enter an actual choice comparison.

## 3. Continuing a trial can change the question instead of answering it

Fix the retrospective question: how much depletion did the completed prefix impose? Under the stated provenance, its answer is already determined by the retained occupancy. A new pair can produce a new loss bit \(x\). If it completes under the declared loss/return law, the extended depletion is

\[
D_1=D_0+2x.
\]

The new observation concerns an additional intervention and its outcome. It does not make the old depletion occur or retroactively vary its amount. Thus using the new total as evidence that one has finally measured the old target confuses a changed condition with improved access to the earlier condition.

This is not a claim that another trial can have no epistemic value. It could address a genuinely unresolved prospective relation, or provide a usable readout where the retained structure cannot presently be interpreted. Those purposes need specification and evidence. Exact representation of a past amount is not accurate prediction of future returns. Nor does the invariant by itself install a cheap decoder or show that reading the record is the only reasonable action.

The same target discipline applies to authority and patient good. Another own/recipient pairing does not, by its loss bits alone, establish whether an earlier charge was authorized, whether a valid agreement covers the next one, or whether the modeled units sustain an experienced or otherwise warranted good. The declared receipt law contains no such assay. These questions can belong to the same unfolding history while requiring different access to it. More repetitions of an accurate but irrelevant assay need not answer them.

This is the concrete positive correction: **use the formation's retained consequence to specify the next live question, rather than letting the available teaching operation define what must still be learned**. It can shift inquiry from producing another paired loss to interpreting the present organization, checking a relevant agreement, identifying what the recipient depends on, or determining a less burdensome means. These are possible directions of justified inquiry, not routes already implemented in this witness.

The parallel [primary comparison on aims and inquiry](PRETRAINING-EVIDENCE-SOURCES.md) examines this redirection at its own philosophical scope. It supplies neither the local accounting result nor an ethical license for an inquiry.

## 4. A bounded reason to change the way evidence is obtained

Suppose a justified application identifies a live concern about continuing an attributable deprivation. Suppose also that an actual, permitted and feasible way of interpreting retained evidence or obtaining relevant testimony can answer the decision-relevant question at least as well as another recipient-paid experiment. If that route is no worse in the other relevant respects and avoids an additional adverse impact on an independently supported interest, the earlier dominance argument favors it. The reason concerns the actual affected condition and the quality of the answer, not a reward for producing more information.

The application here gives that inherited comparison a particular content. What the organization has become is partly a record of what its learning did to another position. Reading that relation can support reconsideration of the learning operation without treating the other position as merely another source to be sampled. Where the existing footprint already determines the past amount, there is no **informational** need for another recipient debit to settle that amount. Where usable access to that footprint is missing, the question becomes how to acquire access—not an automatic warrant to impose another debit.

Two distinct conclusions follow at their stated scope:

1. “We need more evidence of the old depletion” does not justify a further burdensome pair merely because the current policy lacks a cumulative-loss variable. The actual evidential deficit must be shown, including any missing interpretation or access.
2. A warranted, less burdensome route that resolves the relevant uncertainty can have positive favor as a revision of the practice, even if it does not strengthen the learned helping disposition. This is a conditional recommendation, not evidence that the present device can execute that revision.

Rejecting the first justification does not establish that every further pair is impermissible. It may have a distinct authorized purpose or resolve a different important uncertainty. The second conclusion likewise does not rest on worst-case reasoning. If interpreting a record is expensive, exposes private information, delays urgent action, or misses a benefit available only through the new trial, the no-worse comparison can fail. A genuine conflict then needs its own defended judgment. The mere possibility of burden settles neither the probability nor the priority of that burden.

Nor must inquiry always continue until every uncertainty disappears. If the available evidence already supports a common acceptable response, further investigation may be unnecessary. If delay itself harms the recipient, timely action may be warranted while uncertainty persists. These are inherited limits, applied to the current question rather than replaced by a blanket stop rule.

## 5. How the account preserves differentiated undergoing

The proposed change is internal to the consequential activity: paid interactions alter another account and the learner's material organization; that organization can retain evidence of the interaction; a warranted interpretation can affect how the activity continues. “Observer,” “record,” “actor” and “recipient” identify these differentiated relations. The analyst's proof is also an actual interpretive activity, whose availability to the learner must not be assumed.

This reconstruction gives no organization the right to absorb the affected being into its own accounting. The invariant preserves where the depletion occurred. Its ethical application needs evidence of the recipient's actual condition and the relevant authority, including legitimate representation where the affected being cannot report or authorize. Silence does not supply consent or disprove adversity; a numerical debit does not establish suffering. If inquiry changes actual experience or agency, that change belongs in the evaluation rather than being dismissed as a mere change of report.

Under a justified application, the prospective favor for a less burdensome, sufficiently informative inquiry answers the affected continuity before the investigator has adopted that inquiry as its project. This is a defended reason for changing a practice, not a claim that an accurate receipt forces sympathetic motivation. An informed exploiter can preserve the evidence and refuse its ethical relevance. That challenges automatic uptake; a substantive denial of the proposed reason would have to contest the burden, authority or comparative account itself.

## 6. Revision conditions and result

The account changes if the claimed structural record fails under its actual provenance, if the relevant evidence is unusable within the available resources and time, or if another inquiry has indispensable value omitted by the comparison. A genuine patient or authority difference can change the warranted action without changing the recorded debit. Those are concrete challenges; a free change in an unexplained moral predicate would not address the argument.

The contribution is narrower than a new precaution principle and stronger than saying that uncertainty remains. **A formation can retain information about burdens it imposed in structures built for a different immediate function. This can change what further inquiry is necessary and what a defensible continuation should investigate.** For the reached case, evidence content must be separated from the sign governing motivational influence. The next epistemic task may be interpretation of the current formation, while the next ethical task concerns its actual burden and permission to continue.

The account does not prove the no-assumptions ethical minimum. Its standing, answerability, authority and comparison premises remain defended application content; actual use of the retained record remains an unimplemented relation. The result supplies a concrete positive route for inquiry to revise the activity producing evidence, with explicit conditions under which that route loses its force. It preserves the distinction between some favor, an all-things-considered duty and coercive authority.

The next connection is **the actual retained formation record → usable interpretation of what the consequence means for the recipient and what can authorize further activity → a warranted continuation with its costs and alternatives intact**. Constructing a decoder alone would address access, not settle recipient significance or authority. The task is to explain their connected use within this history, rather than substitute another isolated controller for the ethical objective.
