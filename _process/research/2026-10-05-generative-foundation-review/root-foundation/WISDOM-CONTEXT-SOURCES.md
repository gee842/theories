## Analects XI.22 / 《论语·先进》“闻斯行诸”章

Read the Chinese passage and displayed English translation in the [primary-text reproduction](https://confuciuspedia.com/lib/the-analects-of-confucius/xian-jin/), specifically the exchange beginning 子路问“闻斯行诸” and its explanation. The [Chinese Text Project passage index](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=gb&node=1377&remap=gb&show=parallel) search result corroborated the final explanation; direct CText page retrieval failed. This is not manuscript collation or an assessment of the reproduction site's scholarship. Some editions number chapters differently; the opening words identify the passage.

The explanation is explicit: “求也退，故进之；由也兼人，故退之。” Both ask about acting on what they hear. The reply to Zilu also invokes living fathers and elder brothers. Therefore the comparison must preserve personal tendency and relational consultation; it cannot turn the passage into an anonymous numerical average. The shared educational purpose is an interpretive reconstruction, not a supplied measurement or a theorem of the words. The source supports context-sensitive pedagogical counsel; it does not specify the regulation law, a universally optimal teaching policy or G0.

对应结论：相反劝告可以有公开的差异根据；父兄关系与培养方向须保留。数学例只检验这种结构的相容性与信息要求，不把人的性情当成液位，也不替经典补上未经说明的目标函数。

## Cicero, De officiis I.10 §§31–32 and I.13 §39

Read both selected Latin and Walter Miller's English in the [1913 edition reproduced by Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/47001/47001-h/47001-h.htm). I.10 connects changed duties to injury and common interests and discusses harmful promises and a child's serious illness. I.13 praises Regulus's fidelity despite lethal personal cost. The latter was found during contextual reading after the protocol; it is an adverse comparison, not a prediction scored as a success. The account concerns Cicero's argument, not the historical accuracy of the Regulus story.

The changed-circumstances pattern fits the proposed relational structure. A rule reducing every duty to the promisor's safe continuation fails to preserve this source. Injury, common interests, family claims and fidelity need substantive interpretation and comparison. The reading neither resolves tensions between the passages nor licenses importing their verdicts into G0/J. No modern legal advice is inferred.

对应结论：不能从“改变处境会改变义务”推出“有个人损失便可撤回”；也不能从形式相似推出伦理根据一致。保留不合简化模型的守信案例。

## Dijkstra, EWD418, §§2–3 and concluding remarks

Read the author's [Guarded commands, non-determinacy and a calculus for the derivation of programs](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/transcriptions/EWD04xx/EWD418.html), dated 26 June 1974 in the archived transcription. The predicate-transformer account combines enabled alternatives with obligations for every admitted outcome and gives sequential composition. Its conclusion distinguishes the calculus from an automatic invention method. This is established technical precedent for the finite `Assured` construction; no mathematical priority is claimed. Its program semantics do not provide our physical law, target warrant or an explanation of historical wisdom.

对应结论：常规程序语义已经表达同一推理模式。这是共同结构的明确比较者；本轮证据无法据此排除它，或证明当前生成理论是唯一表述。

## Source assessment

The two classical readings support a narrow common form while preserving different reasons. Dijkstra supplies a rigorous rival presentation of the formal pattern. No passage is evidence for actual water regulation, a learned controller or a universal ethical convergence. Familiar examples selected by an AI are not a blind historical test, even though the test conditions were written before the retrieved passages were read. The more demanding prospective test remains explicitly unperformed.
