## Accepted scope

The elementary word construction identifies one-symbol finite words under concatenation with natural numbers under addition. Multiple-symbol words map onto lengths only when the alphabet is nonempty; that map forgets content and order. All finite words are admitted syntactically. A physical process need not admit their execution.

The quotient operation \([u][v]=[uv]\) is well defined exactly when the equivalence relation is a two-sided congruence. Sufficiency follows from representative independence; necessity follows by applying well-definedness to equal input classes. The threshold example is valid: \(\varepsilon\sim a\), but appending \(a\) separates their classes. These are elementary arguments, with no mathematical novelty claim.

Review narrowed an initially broader phrase about reusable mathematical operations. Congruence is necessary for this exact quotient operation; approximate abstractions, dependent operations and nonquotient representations are not excluded.

The existing per-state finite-trace theorem has a stronger operational preservation contract than this word quotient. Quotient existence alone supplies neither legal physical composition nor lifting from every actual state, and neither criterion by itself preserves probabilities, policies or timing.
