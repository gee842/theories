## 1. What is universal here

An admissible result quantifies over every process satisfying stated hypotheses and every history licensed by that process. Its universality is independent of whether the states represent chemistry, machinery or an institution. The interpretation in any particular domain must establish the hypotheses; it is not supplied by the theorem's quantifiers.

The antecedent must describe local transitions or independently identifiable support relations, rather than defining a generative system as one that already continues forever. Conditional conclusions about infinite continuation may then take an infinite execution as a premise to derive necessary conditions. This is a valid obstruction theorem: a process that fails those necessary conditions cannot have that execution. It does not construct an execution or guarantee that one is reachable, likely, controllable or beneficial.

The mere existence of some numerical account is insufficient. Assigning zero reserve, zero demand and zero credit satisfies the balance for any process. Assigning equal demand and credit can also make arbitrary steps balance by definition. An explanatory application must identify those quantities independently and establish the nonzero or unbounded demand premise where used. The proved conditions are useful sufficient antecedents for the particular bounds; they are not certified weakest conditions for every realization of organization.

The proof should retain the original [actual-successor interface](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeCore.lean). Adding provision changes the bound from the old strictly decreasing reserve theorem; it does not permit restarting a component with a replenished reserve that its predecessor never supplies.

## 2. Premises that cannot be hidden

| Hypothesis or distinction | Why it matters | Counterexample if omitted |
|---|---|---|
| Nonnegative accounted reserve and a local expenditure/provision inequality | This is the material premise from which finite-history accounting follows | A hidden stock or unrecorded conversion permits apparent expenditure without recorded provision |
| Unbounded cumulative expenditure, or a positive discrete lower cost when event count is used | Infinitely many events do not alone require unbounded replenishment | A zero-cost self-loop continues indefinitely; positive real costs can form a convergent series |
| Positive provision rather than an event merely labelled provision | A named supply event can contribute nothing | Infinitely many zero-amount deliveries add no reserve |
| Necessary conditions distinguished from sufficiency | Ample supply can coexist with inaccessible or broken machinery | Fuel delivered to a process with no enabled productive event does not cause continuation |
| A specified boundary for reserve and provision | Inflow across a subsystem boundary can consume a finite stock in its environment | A locally renewed machine may deplete the larger preparation |
| Every later support formation has an earlier live enabling parent | The ancestry argument applies only when unsupported arrivals have been excluded | Immigrant supports or independently formed supports can sustain an acyclic subsystem |
| Finite lifetime bounds and a bounded dependency rank | The rank argument rules out indefinitely extending a chain within bounded depth | An immortal basal support or an unbounded acyclic sequence evades the conclusion |
| Natural-number formation times with strictly earlier parents, where used | This supports induction and excludes instantaneous circular construction | A simultaneous mutual formation law would need a different event model |

These are failures of broadened claims, not failures of the stated conditional theorems. The contrast is useful scientifically because a violated prediction points toward a missing dependency, inaccurate measurement, an inadequate system boundary or a false premise.

Natural-number reserve is a discrete accounting choice. Positive cost in a real-valued model does not automatically supply a common lower bound. Natural-number event indices are also distinct from elapsed physical time: arbitrarily many events can fit into bounded time under a law that permits accumulation. Conversely, a bound on support expiry in an explicitly interpreted time coordinate is a time bound under that interpretation.

## 3. What support recurrence does and does not mean

An episode of a support is a particular occurrence with a birth and expiry. A support type is a repeatable role or class. A relation between types does not itself cause an episode to form; the episode law must identify an actual live parent when formation occurs.

A strictly increasing bounded rank on parent-to-child type dependencies limits ancestry depth. With bounded episode lifetimes, it therefore limits the latest possible support. This supports a conditional conclusion about the failure of such a rank certificate when support exists arbitrarily late.

Do not strengthen that conclusion to a checked finite-graph cycle theorem unless the implementation also proves that every finite acyclic dependency graph has the required rank. A rank bound and an arbitrary finite graph are different formal inputs. A graph containing all permitted dependencies may also contain unused edges; a graph cycle is weaker than a realized, perpetually feasible sequence of formations.

When a cycle is identified at the type level, it is not necessarily a return of the whole physical state. New support tokens can descend through repeated types while reserve changes monotonically and every individual token eventually disappears. Their ancestry remains directed forward in time. Feedback across repeatable roles is distinct from backwards causation and from a closed full-state trajectory.

Even a realized type cycle is insufficient for continuation. Initial activation, fuel, compatible quantities, timely encounters and lack of irreversible damage remain relevant. A cycle that reconstructs one organization may also damage another. The cycle alone establishes neither collective benefit nor learning.

## 4. Empirical identification contract

No new experiment is reported. An application of the results should supply the following records before the abstract conclusion is promoted into an empirical explanation.

1. **Specify the state and boundary.** Identify the organization, environment, counted supports, reserve units, time convention and events. Keep omitted stock and coupled systems visible as explicit scope limitations.
2. **Check local accounting.** Measure reserve before and after each relevant event, delivered provision and expenditure, with uncertainty. If the theorem requires an inequality for every admitted event, a fitted mean balance is insufficient. Stochastic deviations require a probabilistic theorem or a proved pathwise bound.
3. **Identify actual enabling dependencies.** Track individual formations and their proposed live parents. Disrupt the parent while holding alternative supply routes as stable as possible, and test whether the child can still form. A correlation, common cause or genealogical label alone does not identify a necessary enabling relation. Substitutable parents require an explicitly disjunctive condition.
4. **Check lifetime and rank premises independently.** Determine whether lifetime bounds are hard bounds, empirical maxima or distributional estimates. Record external admissions, spontaneous formation and changes of type classification. Do not infer acyclicity from the absence of a cycle in a short observation window.
5. **Compare the same successor preparation.** Retain damage, reserves, pending obligations and support ages across intervention boundaries. Do not compare a damaged successor with a freshly initialized baseline and attribute the difference to a learning mechanism.
6. **State what would revise the account.** Sustained activity with apparently exhausted reserve challenges the ledger or accounting boundary. Support beyond the rank/lifetime bound challenges a dependency, lifetime or rank premise. These discrepancies do not by themselves demonstrate novel physics or validate the larger ontology.

The distinguishing prediction comes from the identified mechanism and its quantitative antecedents. Bare common-root monism has no additional intervention prediction supplied by these mathematical bounds.

## 5. Comparators and contribution

The reserve result should be compared directly with cumulative accounting and ranking-function arguments: summing local inequalities yields a finite-history restriction, and a bounded nonnegative quantity cannot fund unlimited positive consumption without sufficient additions. The support result should be compared with a bounded-depth dependency argument: if every later occurrence needs an earlier parent and each generation has a bounded duration, bounded ancestry depth bounds the time of the last occurrence.

These elementary mathematical patterns are not claimed as newly discovered mathematics. Their contribution here is an explicit interface between different mechanisms, a formal audit of what their continuation claims assume, and preservation of the actual successor conditions used by subsequent studies. A result adds explanatory content when the local antecedents are independently grounded and rule out a proposed history; renaming a standard inequality generativity does not add a new prediction.

The [regulation and retention integration](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REGULATION-RETENTION-INTEGRATION.md) remains a separate mechanism achievement. It gives a supplied chemical law under which damage, paid response formation, repair and material copying are coupled. The present universal bounds can constrain such a law once its actual ledger is mapped to the abstract premises. They do not by themselves establish that the mapping is exact, derive the chemistry, or show that copying, renewal and learning are equivalent.

The concurrently developing formed-variant selection study must be assessed and integrated through its own review. Its uncommitted artifacts were visible during this review and were neither changed nor used as already accepted evidence.

## 6. Source-specific semantic findings

The initial source inspection covers [ResourceContinuation.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/conditional/ResourceContinuation.lean) and [SupportNecessity.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/conditional/SupportNecessity.lean).

`AccountLaw` states `reserve t + demand s a t ≤ reserve s + credit s a t` for every allowed edge. `SupportTrace` augments the original finite trace with exact sums and a count of positive-credit events. Those sums retain the actual intermediate states; they are not functions of the event word alone. `support_trace_balance` and `execution_interval_balance` are the finite and arbitrary-suffix cumulative bounds. `componentwise_execution_balance` keeps each indexed support separate and introduces no scalar welfare aggregate.

`account_law_pullback` transfers the account through a forward step-preserving map with the same event labels and accounts obtained by composition with the state map. This is enough for a necessary bound; the theorem does not assert observational or probabilistic exactness. `support_cycle_covers_demand` correctly specializes the cumulative balance to a return to the same whole state.

`support_trace_append` and `repeat_accounted_cycle` compose actual accounted histories and repeat a supplied closed history any finite number of times, with proportionate demand, provision and event counts. The repeated cycle's state boundary must include every condition necessary to justify that repetition; an omitted exhaustible source cannot be made inexhaustible by forgetting it. This is a positive conditional realizability result for closed histories, separate from the necessary resource inequalities and the episode type-graph cycle question.

`unbounded_demand_requires_unbounded_credit` has the required unbounded-demand antecedent. Infinite event count is used only by later unit-demand results. `infinite_unit_execution_credit_window` establishes an explicit positive-credit event in the next inherited-reserve-plus-one events. `execution_interval_frequency_bound` adds a delivery cap before bounding the required count of credit-bearing events. These are necessary constraints on a supplied infinite execution, not existence or policy guarantees. No reward, optimizing controller or learning rule appears in these definitions.

`EpisodeSystem` is a supplied catalogue of support occurrences with kind, natural-number birth and inclusive expiry. Every positive-time birth has an earlier live parent with a permitted type edge. `expiry_le_potential` derives a deadline from a local lifetime bound and an edge-transfer inequality. The rank corollaries construct that deadline from strictly increasing type rank and a uniform lifespan bound. `bounded_rank_deadline` uses an explicit rank into `Fin n` and concludes `t ≤ n * L` for any live episode. It does not require finitely many tokens or a finite episode catalogue.

`typeProcess` embeds the permitted kind-edge relation in the root's `Process` interface. It does not provide an exact event-level refinement from the episode catalogue to a physical birth process. The prose must preserve that scope. `strict_rank_excludes_nonempty_type_cycle` establishes the rank obstruction to cycles; the converse finite-acyclic ranking result is not included.

The support countermodels correctly distinguish a renewing episode catalogue from an expiring one with the same permitted one-kind self-cycle. The infinite acyclic chain respects the parent and lifetime requirements while admitting support at every natural time, making the bounded-rank premise indispensable. The separate external-arrival statement exhibits why later births without internal parents fall outside the episode contract.

The [renewal examples](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/conditional/RenewalExamples.lean) supply complementary checks. A two-event use/restore cycle has expenditure two and provision two, with restoration explicitly receiving enough to pay its own unit cost and leave one unit for later use. The same law admits a terminal abandonment branch from the same initial ready state. This is an existence construction and a nondeterministic fork; no controller, probability or inevitable continuation is derived. The strict net-depletion model receives one unit on every event but demands two and has no infinite execution. The zero-demand process permits infinite recurrence without provision. The bulk-delivery model has no uniform cap on delivery size and has a checked finite history with one arbitrarily large receipt followed by a correspondingly long reserve-consuming run. This preserves the distinction between cumulative amounts and counts of delivery events.

No blocking semantic problem was found in these source statements. The [support mathematical companion](CONDITIONAL-SUPPORT-MATH.md) was also inspected and accepted, including its explicit treatment of inclusive expiry, unequal lifetimes, natural time, actual parents, and unformalized general cycle extraction.

The initial [canonical exposition](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/CONDITIONAL-UNIVERSALITY.md) had one boundary mismatch: it described expiry as exclusive and excluded functioning at the deadline, while `Alive` uses inclusive expiry and the theorem excludes only times strictly after the deadline. The reviewer requested a prose repair to match the checked contract. The final version now states inclusive expiry and excludes functioning strictly after the deadline. Its additional local-potential statement, explicit episode-catalogue scope, and requirement to identify an organizational function's dependence on a live episode are consistent with the source. The accounted-cycle repetition and audit counts also match the final implementation.

The final canonical statement is accepted. It preserves the distinction between local balance and event ordering, fixed arbitrary maps and meaningful empirical interpretation, bounded rank and general cycle extraction, and necessary conditions and sufficient mechanisms. It explicitly requires both interfaces to be established on the same implementation before combining the resource and support constraints; their separate proofs do not automatically construct joint feasibility. This completion is a checked contribution to the unified foundation, not completion of its regulation, learning, concern and wisdom derivations.
