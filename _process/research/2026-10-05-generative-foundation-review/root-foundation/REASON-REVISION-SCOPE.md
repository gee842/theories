## The connection to the root

An organization may preserve the result of an earlier successful interaction. That preservation can enable useful action while the relation remains stable, and produce systematic error after the relation changes. Both trajectories use the same memory and update law. Explaining their difference requires the retained relation, access to new consequences, and the means of changing and using memory within the continuing process.

The accepted identification mechanism qualifies a recipient command after a positive acquired receipt. Once qualified, its update preserves that command even after zero feedback. This was appropriate for the stated stationary law with outages: an unavailable opportunity should not erase established correspondence. Under a changed law the same protection can preserve an obsolete command. The earlier theorem was conditional on its stationary target; it was not refuted by moving outside that condition.

The new construction implements a finite paid reopening, new acquisition, retained evidence, new instrumental reasons and subsequent action. Its investigation is scheduled independently of whether the world changed. This supplies a correction route under stated provision and timing conditions. It does not yet explain when a system discovers that investigation is needed. That distinction identifies the existing explanatory scope; it does not create a requirement for an infinite hierarchy of monitors.

## A positive record has a time-indexed conclusion

Write the physical receipt rule as

\[
r_t=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v_t=1\;\land\;a_t=z_t,
\]

where \(a_t\) is the issued recipient command, \(z_t\) the physical binary correspondence and \(v_t\) opportunity availability at work. Faithful acquisition and recording preserve the issued command and actual receipt. A retained positive therefore warrants \(a_t=z_t\). This is a consequence of the declared physical and measurement law; the stored label does not certify its own source or truth.

If final use occurs at \(u\), an imported command \(b=a_t\) is compatible at use only under an additional link such as \(z_u=z_t\). Together with \(v_u=1\), that yields another positive receipt. When \(z_u\ne z_t\), a historically sound record can instead guide a wrong current command. Historical truth, current applicability and available opportunity are distinct conditions.

The new local window identifier records an apparatus operation. It cannot prove \(z_u=z_t\), infer the world's change time or establish the reliability of its own acquisition channel. A useful stability assumption may be justified by physical protection, independently supported regularity, a sufficiently short use interval, or a separately bounded risk model. None is silently supplied by calling a reason current.

For this reason, the construction keeps the old bank immutable and reads the new bank separately. The inherited `PaidArchiveReader.Facts z` asserts that every positive frame has command \(z\), without filtering by epoch. Combining opposing historical positives from different laws would violate that predicate. Separate bank selection preserves the theorem's actual premise; it does not erase the older evidence or certify the new law's permanence.

## Failure and missing observation are different from disconfirmation

Under the displayed law, a zero receipt is compatible with both an unavailable opportunity and an incorrect command. A missing acquired result conveys still less. Consequently the finite apparatus may use a zero as a reason to explore while declining to publish the claim that the old command is incompatible. Exploration is an action policy; a justified factual conclusion has a stronger evidential condition.

This distinction makes the no-change/first-outage comparison decisive for the proposed mechanism. Starting from the correct old command, the first zero can switch an unqualified controller to the opposite command. A second zero then supplies no positive command evidence. Publishing a confident new command merely because two paid probes occurred would confuse expenditure with information. Repeating the investigation can help only under additional opportunity, sampling, persistence and funding conditions; payment alone supplies none of them.

Conversely, a positive second receipt can identify the command even when the first probe was not persistently recorded. Choosing that second query in advance is essential to the stated sampling design. The external analysis file can contain the first probe for verification without granting the apparatus free access to it. A richer two-frame apparatus could retain more usable evidence at a different material and payment cost. The sparse design makes no optimality claim.

## Why undisclosed later change cannot be solved by a better label

The existing [learning-interface theorem](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/LEARNING-INTERFACE.md) proves that the same deterministic controller, initial memory and complete feedback history yield the same next command. Its precise formal result is `Generative.Learning.same_feedback_excludes_incompatible_futures` in [ControllerRevision.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/ControllerRevision.lean).

Apply that principle to two continuations identical in all information accessible before the next action. One preserves the correspondence; the other silently flips it after the last positive observation. If their compatible next-command sets are disjoint, one issued command cannot satisfy both. Additional internal agreement, a fresh local epoch, or another read of identical old information does not distinguish the continuations.

This is an application of the existing general theorem, not a new proof that all prediction is impossible. The disjointness and identical-observation conditions matter. Where one safe command works in both worlds, where a new observation distinguishes them, or where a protected relation remains stable, the obstruction does not apply. The deterministic statement also does not automatically establish a numerical bound for arbitrary stochastic policies.

The corresponding fresh-channel control has a separate interpretation. A false positive can mimic a physical success to a reader restricted to that channel. Such a trace violates the faithful-acquisition premise. An ordinary outage can preserve that premise while withholding a positive. Reporting these together as generic noise would hide which inference failed and which further evidence could help.

## The effect on completion

The accepted finite bridge supplies a previously missing epistemic continuation: the actual reason/action carrier can use new consequences to replace instrumental conclusions after changed conditions, with inherited failures and all costs retained. The late-drift and outage controls identify why that ability can still fail. These are constructive and destructive cases inside the same declared process.

This does not itself establish the formation or justification of objective G. The reason continues to have the form “given support of this recipient, this command is compatible with it.” Explaining how another center's condition first becomes influential in evaluation, and defending its standing in shared deliberation, remain connected but distinct tasks. The parallel architecture-formation study addresses the former; the ethical foundation and revisable-concern work supply arguments and conditional downstream requirements for the latter.

An adequate unified account does not need every possible revision apparatus to be derived one after another. It needs informative general conditions, an actual compatible construction of the advertised capacities, preserved failure boundaries, and evidence appropriate to the claimed scope. This study must be integrated under those fixed requirements. Its count of modules, probes or proofs is not a new completion criterion.
