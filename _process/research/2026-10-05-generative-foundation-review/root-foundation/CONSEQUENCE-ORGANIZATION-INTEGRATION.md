## The core changes through what the branches disclose

The preceding [participation study](PARTICIPATION-CONSEQUENCE-INTEGRATION.md) established that a learned command can change enacted participation without changing graph topology. The present [mechanism reconstruction](CONSEQUENCE-ORGANIZATION-MECHANISM.md) identifies a stronger relation already implemented by autonomous method formation: acquired observations cause an operative generator connection to be rejected and replaced, and subsequent acquisition must traverse the replacement. This is credit for an existing result, not a newly discovered capacity.

The generative description must preserve that difference. A statement that activity changes the conditions of further activity must include changes in the organization of learning, rather than confining all acquired change to responses within an unchanging arrangement. At the same time, different changes have different reaches: replacing a candidate generator, changing an updater, adjusting influence and changing which encounter is available are not interchangeable.

This is reciprocal core/tree articulation. The branch constrains what the general account must preserve; the general question selects the relevant relations within the branch. Neither is a completed entity to which the other is attached from outside.

## Why an unchanged description does not require an unchanged learner

A single transition description can include the current connections, records and candidate workspaces in its changing configuration. Earlier activity changes a connection; later activity reads that connection and therefore proceeds differently. Describing both transitions with one rule does not erase the actual connection change. An equivalent larger fixed controller can represent the same history, including the replacement. Such equivalence challenges an exclusive claim to a new computational class; it does not refute the operative change.

This removes a particular descriptive regress: evidence-dependent revision need not be denied until a new controller above every controller has itself been learned. The source already demonstrates the claimed local revision. It does not remove questions about why these processes occur, how their components form, or whether their revisions are justified. Those questions require named relationships and evidence, not a general inference from fixed mathematical notation to fixed organization.

Here the named remaining components are the acquisition schedule, candidate repertoire, compatibility test and machinery translating exhaustion of candidates into replacement. They are supplied to this local construction. That limitation locates an explanatory obligation; it does not locate those processes outside generative reality. Calling them immanent would not explain them either.

## The next unresolved bridge

The next shared connection is **acquired consequences affecting the practice through which further evidence and response become available**. The method history regenerates alternatives from a fixed archive; it does not change what will be sensed next. The [separate service-access reconstruction](APPLICATION-REVISION-RECONSTRUCTION.md) makes the stronger practical question concrete: an existing continuing learner can change influence, yet its actual four-pair state still requires two further recipient acquisition cycles before service becomes accessible. Possible refunds prevent treating gross debit as necessary net loss. A different influence score alone does not revise this relation.

The [retained-evidence result](PRETRAINING-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) already supplies structural information about prior net depletion. Consequently, another historical loss counter would not close the bridge. The further account must explain how relevant retained consequences become usable grounds for changing acquisition or access, how the changed arrangement actually operates, and how recipient continuation and authority constrain that change. The [recipient-continuation analysis](../../../history/2026-10-05-generative-health/succession/RECIPIENT-CONTINUATION.md) constrains the consequence side without identifying resource delivery with a whole life or with welfare.

For that claim, a discriminating result would preserve the actual reached history and show consequences altering subsequent inquiry or access, with later use depending on the altered arrangement. A comparison should distinguish this from a changed answer under the old protocol, a predetermined schedule, or an external experimenter supplying the desired choice. Where warranted concern is claimed, recipient significance and legitimate scope must enter the explanation without an analyst's favorable score silently selecting the mechanism.

This remains one connected research direction with different unresolved relationships. The existence of a revisable method does not establish its reliability: the earlier expensive compatible method and delayed false-agreement control remain. Causal effectiveness does not by itself establish justified concern or governance. Conversely, those unfinished obligations do not undo the local formation and revision already demonstrated. No universal favorable direction, no-assumptions foundation or completed alignment is claimed.
