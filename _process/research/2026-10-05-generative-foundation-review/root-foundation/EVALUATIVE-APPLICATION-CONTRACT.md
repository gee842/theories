# From acquired recipient facts to a warranted application

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting contract for the named connection **self-consequence learning → paid evaluative transfer → recipient-sensitive choice**. This note maps existing records to the patient-relative application requirements in [HUIGEN's finite contract](HUIGEN-FULL-THEORY-AUDIT.md#applicability-transfer-and-costly-choice). It adds no experiment, physical component, ethical foundation or proof. The evaluator described below is a **prospective construction**, now specified in its [draft protocol][draft], which remains pending root and independent review, unfrozen and without a numerical run at this reading. Its fields and design arithmetic are not accepted results.

The narrow contribution is an explicit reference interface: what the accepted factual endpoints can supply, what the proposed learned coefficient would add, and what must still be independently established before interpreting an action as warranted concern. The [downstream evidence contract](RECIPIENT-EVIDENCE-DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT.md) and [semantic transport result](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SEMANTIC-ACTION-COMPOSITION.md) already establish the general distinction. The present map does not repeat their generic composition theorem or HUIGEN's positive-weight/cost argument.

## 1. Bind an application to its actual subject and history

Reuse [ClaimLedger.AddressedClaim][ledger]: `patient`, `context`, `episode`, `ground`, and `addressee`. These identify the claim being assessed; they do not identify a command merely by reusing its bit value. The ledger's `admitted` predicate requires independent grounding. Its `certified` predicate concerns fulfillment, not the strength of a learned coefficient or the truth of every premise. `unresolved` means admitted but uncertified, not false, harmed, disqualified or unworthy.

For this application, attach the following **assessment fields by reference**. These are specification and verification fields unless an actual paid implementation is separately provided. Their presence in this note gives the controller no new observation, record cell or authority.

| Field | Required content and boundary |
|---|---|
| Subject and bearer binding | The continuing recipient and the actual actor/addressee; independently bound sensor, command destination and lineage. A name copied with an incoming token is not an independent destination check. |
| Context and episode | Relevant observation and use windows, physical target-law/stability assumptions, publication generation and assessment horizon. A current epoch label alone does not prove a stable world. |
| Acquired factual record | Actual source frame/register, observation channel and paid acquisition trace; known command, qualified candidate or explicit unknown. Distinguish positive, zero and unavailable evidence. |
| Patient-condition interpretation | An independently described condition of this recipient and a warranted relation from the measured consequence to improvement, worsening or unresolved effect **for this recipient**. State whether this concerns resource support, experience, agency or another specified good. |
| Ground and applicability | A defended actor-addressed ground, scope, current applicability and relevant defeaters, together with the evidence warranting recognition. Record unknown applicability separately from rejection. A resemblance to the actor does not itself supply standing or equal moral magnitude. |
| Authority history | The legitimate permission relied on, its source, subject, action/domain, time and possible revocation; or an explicitly supplied settled-permission premise. Keep the apparatus's scope/permission/delivery bits separate. Later approval cannot manufacture an earlier grant. |
| Feasible comparison | Complete declared alternatives, including abstention and any available private option; actual liquidity, source and material access; each alternative's relevant actor and recipient consequences. State a defended qualitative ordering or an independently defended/supplied conflict margin. |
| Acquired influence | Initial evaluator state, actual native teaching consequence, paid read/write/transfer, retained coefficient, target gate and choice effect. This is causal provenance; its existence does not justify the transfer. |
| Execution and effect | Actual enabled event word, recipient destination, all charged expenses and returns, final measured effect, and the interpretation needed to certify the addressed claim. Failure to act leaves that claim's fulfillment unresolved unless other evidence settles it. |

The exact source must remain identified when a field changes. New evidence can alter the command or whether a particular ground applies without being an operation on general standing or prior authorization. Such changes require separate typed revisions and their own warrant. Nothing here requires all historically admitted claims to remain actionable forever: expiry, fulfillment, a genuine defeater or changed responsibility must be represented with its actual scope.

## 2. What the accepted endpoints actually provide

### Fresh recipient reasons: the proposed physical parent

The formal primary counterpart of the proposed entry is [RevisionWitness.consumed][witness], immediately after the fresh paid consumer and **before** the old `read`, `install` and recipient trial. For the numerical distribution, the draft instead takes each actual saved prefix immediately before its first attempted `paid_command_read` or `command_read_unavailable`; a history with no attempted read retains its last actual pre-`continuation_complete` state. Source case, producer, row, prefix index, state hash and original mass remain bound. Failed histories are not replaced by the formal witness. This is not the old final state with the final action's expenses refunded. The accepted witness supplies:

- Actual fresh acquisition through the continuing recipient core. The fresh bank is sparse: addresses zero and two are absent, while address one contains the actual positive command-zero record. An absent slot supplies no negative factual verdict.
- A qualified recipient command and a current unique publication supporting command zero. `RecipientReasons.Meaning G z .p` is `G ∧ Compatible z false`; `.q` concerns command one for the **same recipient**. Neither node is a second patient.
- Under the declared faithful, stable recipient law, a warranted instrumental conclusion taking `root : G`. The theorem does not derive G, a patient's adverse condition or a warranted value magnitude.
- Actual reader exhaustion and paid history. `actual_reason_revision` records energy60/material42 at `consumed`; `actual_new_acquisition` and the consumer trace retain the underlying ten actor units, forty recipient units and one ten-unit packet. The old `useWord` would cost ten: two for read/import and eight for the recipient trial. These inherited facts are not new evaluator balances.

The [recipient action port][action] restricts the imported use to the recipient, preserves the actor register, and keeps recipient receipt out of actor fuel. Its `scope`, `permission` and `delivery` fields express supplied operating conditions. They do not establish the patient's consent, representation or another legitimate basis of intervention. The fresh law's observation-to-use equality remains a premise even when the publication is current.

### Architecture-learning: a different accepted endpoint

[Architecture-learning](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) supplies another concrete route to acquired recipient correspondence. In its [actual model][architecture], `indexed=true` changes `q` to `2*actor + recipient`, while `last` records recipient qualification. The issued command is the selected bit XOR the retained motor coupling. The wire, retained site, graph transmission, actual fuel and separate bank constrain use. In the primary arm, recipient writes preserve the actor bit; a faithful positive receipt qualifies the recipient candidate under the stated calibration/stability conditions.

Those bytes are **not** the fresh-reason core's encoding. That core uses `RecipientCorrection.packedAct`/`packedWrite`, with the recipient in the other bit position, and its imported qualification can have reason-import provenance. Decoding both with one numeric rule would change meaning. An adapter must preserve the actual command, coupler where present, target, qualification provenance and paid access; a guide copy does not copy the subsequently acquired recipient evidence.

The architecture endpoint observes capture, not independently identified welfare. Its `patient_use` is a stipulated operation that the initial recipient reserve could already fund; capture was not proved necessary for activity. Its primary trace has no third recipient action using the newly qualified record, and its strict recipient-correction history has zero intersection with the earlier strict motor-revision witness. These limits rule out borrowing a stronger whole-history witness. No physical composition of that endpoint with `RevisionWitness.consumed` or the prospective evaluator is asserted here.

The selection inequality is an operational law rather than a patient-condition measurement. In particular, acquiring a positive `n` is not the same event as discovering that this patient's current condition falls under a defended relief ground. A construction can establish acquired recipient-sensitive control even if the interpretive fields remain unknown. ## 4. Patient-relative calibration and factual correction

The smallest additional application test is to hold the actual receipt/command evidence fixed while varying an independently warranted patient-condition interpretation. In one stipulated context the modeled receipt supports this recipient's identified need; in another, it is irrelevant or worsens that recipient's condition. The actor's native-loss observations, learned `n`, raw receipt and command efficacy can be identical. A selector using only those inputs must give the same output, even though the supplied patient-relative grounds may warrant different application judgments. This is a conceptual underidentification case, not evidence that the actual resource model secretly contains both welfare laws.

That case exposes the missing field without a new generic cost theorem. The missing input is a calibrated relation to **this patient's condition**, not another positive coefficient. Independent patient evidence may support such a relation even where the recipient differs from the actor. Importing the actor's own significance map by identity is an egocentric projection unless that transport is warranted. Conversely, a warranted map need not require identical experience, response command or physiology; the relevant relation must be specified.

A prospective positive application uses the same claim identity and authority history before and after correction. New evidence may show that the proposed command no longer improves the relevant condition, so that command-specific support becomes false or unknown. It need not remove the patient from standing, reverse an earlier valid permission, or endorse the patient's every appraisal. If the evidence also bears on current permission or the general ground, update those entries explicitly with that separate justification. Preserve the previous record's context rather than overwriting its past meaning.

For fulfillment, reuse `ClaimLedger.record`: sound execution certificates extend `certified` while preserving the independently admitted set within the declared episode. The predicate connecting actual outcome to fulfillment must contain the patient-condition interpretation; recording the token cannot prove that interpretation. Correcting applicability is not automatically a `record` operation, and this ledger's fixed-admission theorem does not prove that a particular reason survives every factual change. Missing evidence, unavailable funding, an abstaining learned policy and a false command-benefit prediction remain distinct outcomes.

## 5. Bounded completion and provenance

A finished causal study should report its paid acquisition/transfer/use history and controls, preserving the same recipient and all failed or inactive states. Its interpretation should then cite the relevant application fields above and state which remain supplied or unknown. For a positive **counterfactual warranted application**, an independently defended patient-good map, actor-addressed ground, legitimate authority and complete comparison must be assumed explicitly; the causal result cannot certify them through its favorable action.

This note does not duplicate the active [recipient-valuation empirical lane](RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md), add a new experiment, or claim that neural/behavioral transfer proves ethical concern. It supplies an inspectable reference map for that lane and the prospective evaluator. Equal operational predictions from a conventional controller locate the contribution in the conditional developmental/interface account, not in a novel controller or a demonstrated ethical necessity.
