## 1. Start within the unfolding being explained

Activity changes relations that continue into subsequent activity. Some differences become available through sensing or signaling; some persist through changed connections, practice, recording or reconstruction; some affect what can act, develop or recur. These consequences jointly form the relatively enduring organizations later described as a learner, a model, a collective, its parts and its selective conditions. Neither independently constituted value owners nor a single scalar reward are starting primitives.

Ren's proposal identifies an important recurrence within this history: differences in what activity accomplishes change the organization of later activity, including how later differences will be generated, detected, evaluated and carried forward. The same relationship can participate in learning during a life, development, institutional revision and differential transmission. Such participation need not form a fixed hierarchy or share one clock.

This reconstructs the proposal more strongly than “successful things survive.” A causal explanation names the consequence, the path through which it changes organization, what persists into later activity, and which changed activity results. The previous description of formation, retention and use as three generally unconnected stages was too broad. Their generic connection is already instantiated in the repository. The open developmental correspondence concerns how particular formed and retained relations are recruited in particular tasks.

## 3. The affirmative inference and what it explains

The strongest defensible formulation is:

> Consequences of organized activity can change which operative relationships are strengthened, maintained, reconstructed or replaced. Those relationships shape later discrimination and action. Where that organization also changes how variation, evidence and continuation occur, the consequences can revise the process that does the selecting.

The causal work lies in its interfaces. First, an actual outcome must reach a modifying or transmitting operation. Second, that operation must alter something operative rather than just an analyst's score. Third, the changed relation must remain available or be reconstructed when subsequently required. Fourth, later activity must actually use it. The results in §2 already realize these relationships at particular scopes. They supply a common explanation of why formation can have lasting, revisable consequences without making retention permanent or use automatic.

There need not be a separate selector for every selection event. The physical and social organization through which variants have different consequences can perform selection without someone representing it. A learned model may subsequently make those relationships available for deliberate inquiry and revision. This explains a substantive difference between being shaped by consequences and modeling how those consequences arise.

It also removes an apparent regress. An explanation of how one learning procedure changes can start from already operative development, reproduction or practice, then show how those relationships form a revised procedure. It need not derive the whole of reality before explaining that change. Where the claim concerns the origin of those earlier relationships too, their formation remains a further named obligation; it is not an independent ontological realm.

### Different implementations retain different mechanisms

Backpropagation, reproduction and organizational evaluation can realize the common connection without sharing an algorithm. Backpropagation computes derivative information through a differentiated computational structure; an optimizer uses that information to modify parameters relative to a specified objective. Reproduction carries differences through development and differential offspring formation. Institutional assessment operates through records, judgments, rules, participation, authority and opportunities for future activity. Identifying the common relation does not establish that a brain uses backpropagation or that an institution computes a gradient.

The directly retrieved abstract of [Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams (1986)](https://doi.org/10.1038/323533a0) supports the narrow claim that changing connections to reduce task error can form useful internal features. Direct publisher-page retrieval failed; this review does not claim a fresh full-paper reading or technical replication. The inherited comparative work already addresses evolutionary connectionism. Its [primary theoretical account](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5119841/) explicitly studies changes both to what variation is generated and to how alternatives are evaluated. That explanatory credit must be retained; a common vocabulary here is not a priority or superiority claim.

For meritocratic organization, “merit” must be connected to actual contribution through a revisable assessment procedure. An assessment that rewards a signal regardless of contribution is still causally effective selection; its existence does not show that it identifies what enables shared continuation. Conversely, appropriate participation, care, rest or supported dependence need not be translated into individual competitive productivity before belonging within generative organization.

## 4. What makes this an explanation of cognition

Selection can explain the prevalence or maintenance of a capacity without explaining its operation. Cognition requires identifying what the organization distinguishes and what those distinctions make possible. A useful cognitive account connects access, retained or reconstructable structure, differential response, anticipation and, where appropriate, representational and inferential use.

The [existing content account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/KNOWLEDGE.md#how-content-can-form-and-remain-mistaken) supplies the affirmative semantic connection. Acquisition and use constrain a relation the model concerns across encounters; calibrated predictions and transferable implications make that relation available in later activity. A representation can be wrong about the same referent rather than having its content redefined as whatever happened to be rewarded. Changing a reward, a belief's content, the available evidence and the motivation to use it can therefore produce different changes.

This is more than a demand to attach another label to selected structure. It yields substantive contrasts: retained information can be inaccessible to the current response route; a successful shortcut can fail when a relevant relationship changes; acquired structure can support an untrained combination; evidence can revise one claim while preserving others. Existing checked constructions provide restricted instances. Their learning and content mechanisms are explanatory achievements even though natural-language understanding and consciousness are not finished.

The proposal should also retain learning without a represented reward. Covariation can reorganize accessible structure, and a later task can use what formed. A claimed higher-level evolutionary account of that plasticity must identify inherited differences and their consequences; naming any retained regularity “rewarded” after the fact would add no causal explanation. Selection need not consciously identify its target, while a claim of explicit identification requires the cognitive organization that performs it.

## 5. Whole-to-part effects and long-run direction

Ren's whole-to-part insight is correct as a causal possibility and already has concrete mechanisms: the consequences of an organized configuration can change opportunities for its constituent relationships to continue or be reconstructed. Those consequences may also favor a mechanism that preserves the connection between contribution and continuation. This is stronger than selection concerned only with an isolated self.

Three outcomes must remain distinct:

1. **Absolute continuation:** a capacity remains present or is reconstructed.
2. **Relative representation:** its carriers or realizations form a larger fraction of some specified successor population.
3. **Wider renewal:** the configuration maintains or enriches the actual conditions of further differentiated life and activity.

None defines the other two. A contribution can be maintained at a stable or declining fraction while the configuration it enables expands or renews. A nonreproductive or terminal role can support recurring organization. Relative-frequency increase is therefore relevant to the specific claim that an inherited variant spreads; it is not a universal requirement of generative alignment. Conversely, a variant can spread while damaging wider conditions. “What selects the whole also selects its parts” must identify the operative developmental, transmission, access or maintenance relation, rather than equate all three outcomes.

The strongest positive long-run argument concerns configurations in which continuation repeatedly depends on actual renewal, relevant variation remains available, and that dependence remains connected to the reconstruction of the organization responsible. Under such conditions, renewal can influence which learning and selection arrangements recur. No universal scalar score or requirement that each part independently reproduce is needed.

This argument does **not** make mere duration a corrective mechanism. Earlier export can reproduce a pattern before its damaging consequences arrive. Other activity can keep renewing the conditions from which a damaging pattern benefits. Association can be lost between contribution and inheritance. A renewing alternative can disappear before it establishes. These are actual causal vulnerabilities already reconstructed in the [prior counterreview](RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION-REVIEW.md), not freely toggled predicates in an unrelated ontology.

The long-run statements require precise scope. Continued activity cannot use an indispensable relation after that relation has ceased and no replacement operates. This dependence claim supports the necessity of some sustaining relationships in an indefinitely continuing configuration. It does not identify an optimum, guarantee that a particular renewing variant spreads, establish correction before irreversible losses, or show that every constituent becomes ethically aligned. A whole containing continuing renewal and recurrent damaging activity is not evidence that completely renewal-free activity lasts forever; it instead limits the inference from continuing renewal somewhere to alignment everywhere.

Nor does the largest whole require an external population of competing wholes. Its internal variation, differential continuation and changing conditions can explain selection within it. Recurrence of one connected whole does not alone establish Darwinian reproduction of that whole.

**Negative inference resolved at its appropriate scope:** feedback, recurrence and simultaneous selection do not by themselves license an unconditional claim that evolution must eventually produce universal generative alignment. The positive conditional mechanism remains explanatory. The existing one-round frequency counterexample specifically defeats automatic contributor spread, not every stronger asymptotic claim. Any new mathematical claim covering arbitrary histories must be checked in Lean with its actual renewal and transmission law; this review does not manufacture such a theorem from a finite example.

G0/J supplies the already authorized normative route. These dynamics can explain how an orientation forms and remains effective without re-proving the adopted axiom or equating ethical warrant with whatever a selection arrangement retains. Generativity is not restricted here to possibilities already independently declared good; the actual living and further generation, its differentiated realizations and its losses remain the object of explanation and appraisal.
