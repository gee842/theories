# Present endorsement, formed dependence and the scope of change

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored continuation under [Ren's instruction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-perspectival-ratification-continuation.md), following [the trajectory round](PERSPECTIVAL-GOOD-AND-TRAJECTORIES.md#authorized-continuation-findings-and-integration). **Scope: philosophical adjudication and correspondence review. No new mathematical proof or empirical intervention is claimed.** The existing 25 Lean results retain their earlier scope; they are not ethical premises for this argument.

The universal inference tested is **whole qualitative realization → differentiated practical significance**, here where an arrangement participates in forming the perspective that later endorses it. We retain the [current scope correction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#universal-scope-and-substantive-conclusions): universality does not prescribe the same positive response at every position. The inquiry must explain the grounds of actual differences without granting every formed boundary its own authority.

## 1. What the continuation changes

The earlier round already distinguished prospective formation, present acquired goods and transition responsibility. The concurrent [whole-practical-ground round](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-GROUND-INTEGRATION.md) already credits imposed dependence and invited reliance as candidates for additional directed responsibility; it also preserves the limit of those historical grounds. This round does not rename either result as first universal warrant.

The new test concerns **which practical change an actual present affirmation effects**. A person can value a relationship, authorize a specified continuation, release a particular claim or participate in a shared decision without doing all of them at once. These are roles within a formed practice, whose significance and legitimate scope need explanation. They are not independent owners' primitive powers supplied at the foundation.

Two overstrong criteria are rejected at the outset. Dependence does not automatically nullify agency: relationships can form the skills and understanding through which commitment becomes possible. Nor does one person's dissent automatically defeat a shared decision. A justified procedure can settle matters within its scope even when some participants disagree. A theory making independence or unanimity prerequisites would misdescribe these constructive possibilities.

## 2. The specified history

Continue the learning-association case. A shared training practice has produced actual skill, attachment and a meaningful craft. Its declared and, for this local comparison, warranted terms provide that demonstrated competence receives a truthful portable record. People have organized their activity in reliance on that undertaking. Their skill is real; issuance is feasible; no relevant competence uncertainty, confidentiality duty or safety restriction remains unresolved.

The office subsequently withholds the completed record unless a participant accepts a new period of service. That condition was not among the accepted terms. The new service is useful to the association, but in this constructed comparison no overriding necessity or other independently warranted ground defeats the delivery claim or justifies adding that condition. A real application would need evidence for those claims; merely failing to identify a defeating ground would not establish its absence. Some participants sincerely want to remain; others value the association yet object to the condition. These attitudes may coexist within one person.

The point is not that all credentials, all exit costs or all work requirements have the same status. The local history distinguishes a completed undertaking from a proposal for further participation. The warrant of the undertaking and its limits remain substantive inputs. New evidence that the record would be false, that a legitimate unresolved requirement remains, or that withholding is independently justified would change the case.

This is a whole relational history: skill, assurance, reliance, control and choice formed together. The office's causal role in producing the skill explains part of the dependence. That role alone does not settle the further question of what demands the office may now impose.

## 3. What the affirmative argument warrants here

The candidate favors honoring the completed undertaking and considering the new service on its own actual terms. Its ground combines the worthwhile realization already achieved, the reasonable reliance organized by the undertaking, and the office's continuing control over whether the promised recognition is made usable. Making that recognition unavailable solely to obtain a further concession uses the very reliance the undertaking invited to alter what participation demands.

This is a substantive explanation of the proposed constraint. Its force does not depend on whether the office currently prefers to honor it. It does depend on the warrant of the undertaking, the absence of a defeating ground and the scope of the claim. A bare historical contribution, an analyst's boundary or a reported preference could not supply those qualifications by itself.

The argument accepts several different continuations:

| Specified continuation | Adjudication at this case's declared scope |
|---|---|
| Deliver the truthful record and offer explicit further service terms; a participant knowingly accepts. | The earlier obstruction is removed. The new acceptance can authorize the specified participation, subject to other affected claims and the warrant of those terms. |
| Keep withholding the record and cite sincere enjoyment of the association as agreement to further service. | Enjoyment identifies a real good but does not establish that the additional condition has been authorized. The completed undertaking still supplies the stated ground for delivery. |
| Keep withholding the owed record; a fully informed participant expressly accepts further service and signs the demanded release to obtain it. | The participant's reasonable choice can express meaningful willingness to continue. That choice does not by itself legitimate the office's condition or settle the repair claim. The contested premise is that withholding this owed support cannot supply authority to exact its release; explicit assent alone does not answer that premise. |
| Deliver the record; the participant stays while expressly preserving a particular repair claim. | Continuing participation and retaining the claim can coexist. Staying alone does not state that the claim is released. |
| A participant competently releases a specified claim under conditions that warrant the release. | That claim may be discharged within the actual scope of the release. A general rule that every historical claim must persist would be too strong. |
| A justified common procedure settles a limited shared schedule despite current dissent. | The decision may bind within the procedure's warranted jurisdiction. Its authority comes from the actual shared arrangement, not an inference from another person's approval. |
| The association is dissolved immediately to remove dependence. | Ending control also loses actual craft, relationships and support. Their loss requires appraisal; dependence alone does not warrant dissolution. |

These are philosophical adjudications under stated grounds, not observations, legal determinations or formal theorems. Specifying a warranted release or procedure does not derive its first warrant. Those cases test the scope of the affirmative proposal and prevent it from defining all inconvenient consent as invalid.

## 4. The strongest ratification rival and the response

The rival accepts that an earlier expansion of control was unjustified but argues that informed present commitment can constitute a new legitimate relationship. This is a serious possibility. People need not remain governed by every earlier preference, nor must a practice forever lack authority because its history contains a wrong.

The affirmative response therefore asks what has actually been affirmed, by whom, with what understanding and practical opportunity, through which warranted authority, and with what effects on other claims. A new participation agreement, release of a repair claim and authorization of a common decision can have different contents and grounds. None is automatically impossible; none is established solely by the presence of sincere satisfaction.

An actual new authorization may change what may now be done. A valid release may change what is now owed. Neither conclusion, without a further argument, establishes the appraisal of the original choice. This does not freeze the earlier verdict: new evidence, later consequences or a warranted practice of ratification may change a current assessment or institutional status. The point is to identify the relation doing that work, rather than let one affirmative attitude answer every temporal question.

The strongest remaining disagreement concerns whether particular ratification conditions are adequate under dependence and which claims can be released or settled collectively. This comparison identifies that dispute rather than declaring that dependence settles it. A universal account must explain the relevant ethical differences, including where affirmation has real constitutive force and where it is insufficient.

## 5. Distinguishing an obstacle from the end of a relationship

The portable record is a discriminating feature because making it available need not erase the acquired craft or require recreating the association elsewhere. By contrast, a shared friendship or a particular collaborative practice may cease in its existing form when someone departs. No change of label preserves all such goods. A reason to remove an unjustified obstacle is not automatically a duty to reproduce every lost relationship outside its actual participants and conditions.

Even an intrinsic relational loss is not automatically exempt from care. If particular assurances invited dependence, or a promised transition was part of the undertaking, those grounds may favor support. If honoring them is impossible or imposes competing serious burdens, the scope needs reassessment. The relevant distinction is between actual grounds and feasible responses, not between a universally costless exit and a universally permissible penalty.

Removing the office-added condition also changes the whole relationship: it can alter trust, felt security and appreciation. We can preserve identified features such as skill, the truthful record and access to an offered continuation; we cannot honestly claim every good remains unchanged. Issuance of a record alone does not establish a usable alternative. Where removal also makes a relevant and adequately understood alternative genuinely usable, persistence of endorsement weakens the claim that continued enforcement of this obstruction is necessary for present staying. It does not erase the obstruction's historical formative effects, exclude other dependence, or certify all permissions, claims and earlier choices. Changed endorsement does not by itself prove that the earlier attitude was insincere or that the new one is finally authentic.

This corrects the earlier counterreview's less precise suggestion that persistence weakens a claim of approval being produced entirely by foreclosure. A formative effect can outlast its generating condition. The diagnostic concerns continued enforcement and current choice under changed conditions, not recovery of a socially untouched preference or disproof of the formative history.

## 6. Consequences for evaluative fields and generative trajectories

The result refines the relation between subjective good and whole appraisal. Felt attachment, understood preference and practical authorization can interact without being identical. A formed perspective can contribute to a good and also participate in a justified change of its terms. Neither its acquired origin nor its present stability supplies a complete ethical verdict.

For the meta-attractor investigation, a candidate mechanism is the conversion of successful participation into control over the conditions of further recognition, followed by treating the resulting approval as authorization of wider control. Each transition needs a causal and normative account. Some justified common institutions also reproduce their authority, so reproduction alone cannot diagnose capture. This round does not prove attraction, stability or formal containment in the generative model.

For learning and self-modification, changing which considerations influence choice need not change the grounds themselves. Conversely, fulfillment, valid release, a changed relationship or corrected understanding can change what remains warranted. A proposal to preserve ethical significance through revision must track these changes; neither frozen verdicts nor approval by a newly formed evaluator suffices. This applies the inherited ground-sensitive revision requirement, rather than announcing a new universal invariant.

The karma-like continuation therefore includes changed capacities to undertake, fulfill, revise and end relationships, together with the actual goods and burdens those relationships realize. Less dependence is not invariably better; stronger reliable commitment can be constructive. The direction must concern how the whole continuation is organized and warranted, not one scalar radius of concern or count of available exits.

## 7. What is gained and what is still supplied

The bounded result is an adjudication of the original rival: **present ratification can matter without acquiring unlimited scope**. Dependence does not erase its force; competent approval does not automatically supply every permission, waiver or collective mandate. The undertaking case supplies a positive reason to remove the specified added condition while preserving possible new commitment and genuine acquired goods.

Its remaining ethical inputs are the significance of the affected realization, the warrant and scope of undertakings and releases, legitimate shared procedures and the treatment of competing claims. The account explains their relation in this history but does not derive all of them from bare generativity. The universal target remains open; this case is a discriminator of a proposed whole ground, not its replacement.

The next connection should test the legitimacy of forming control over an acquired good under accurate disclosure and prospective agreement, without a breached promise. Compare a good intrinsically dependent on continued shared participation with a deliberately imposed condition on using a separable skill or support. Agreement's validity and reach must remain questions, not premises defined by the desired answer. Simply deleting the undertaking premise and retaining this verdict would repeat the earlier imposition case. The new comparison must retain actual power, formation, competing goods, legitimate completion and effects beyond the arrangement; it must identify what makes the proposed control warranted or unwarranted within that whole.

## 8. Integrated review and primary-source assessment

The [substantive review](PERSPECTIVAL-RATIFICATION-REVIEW.md) adjudicates five constructed continuations with explicit normative inputs. Its main corrections are now incorporated: valid specific release can end a claim; a warranted shared procedure can bind present dissent within scope; real dependence does not automatically invalidate commitment; and the hardest contested case includes informed explicit assent while owed support is withheld. The latter tests the office's authority to impose the condition, rather than diagnosing the participant as unable to choose. Additional directed responsibility remains an inherited conditional ground, not a new derivation of universal first warrant.

The [correspondence review](PERSPECTIVAL-RATIFICATION-CORRESPONDENCE.md) distinguishes an intervention removing a specified obstacle from one destroying or transforming the shared relationship. It also corrects the inference from persistent endorsement: effects of earlier formation can survive removal of an obstacle, and another route must actually be usable before the intervention tests current practical choice. Its matrix preserves prospective authorization, release, delegated discretion and retrospective appraisal as different targets. It recommends no new theorem: the existing formal representation results can retain those targets only after their relevant history and comparison have been supplied.

The [primary-source assessment](PERSPECTIVAL-RATIFICATION-SOURCES.md) supplies three independent challenges, not three confirmations of this theory. Khader's account allows autonomous endorsement of an oppressive arrangement, so ethical criticism need not deny the participant's agency. Christman's relational account challenges the sufficiency of noninterference where support helps constitute or sustain agency. Wenner examines uncontrolled power over options, which sincere endorsement alone does not characterize. Their wellbeing, autonomy and nondomination standards remain substantive proposals; none is imported as a proved generative invariant. The source note separates inspected author-manuscript passages from the narrower indexed-primary-text access available for Christman.

Together these reviews narrow the ethical claim while making its practical differences more exact. The account permits real present formation of authority and real discharge of responsibility; it rejects inferring their entire scope from a favorable attitude or behavior. The established result of this round is this defended conditional adjudication and the corrected trajectory diagnostic. No new experiment was conducted, no mathematics was needed to encode the desired verdict, and universal ethical grounding remains unresolved.
