The counterexample contributor independently read the root-authored canonical synthesis in full against the three companion notes, the inherited transport argument and the accepted foundation's declared scope. **Decision: accepted as a scoped conceptual and source-comparison advance. No blocking issue remains in the reviewed version.** The work identifies and tests the exact explanatory burden of patient-ground plus eligible present control. It does not establish that this conjunction suffices in every eligible case, prove the necessity of universal ethics, or reopen the completed conditional candidate.

## What is established and what remains a proposal

The synthesis gives the candidate positive explanatory content: the actual patient's condition supplies the proposed significance, while the actor's permissible effective opportunity supplies a practical relation to it. This preserves separate patients and an external success condition for concern. Ending the helper's discomfort can therefore fail the patient-ground, and learning can improve a means belief without creating the patient's significance. These are genuine attractions of the explanation, but restricted rival accounts can share them. The canonical text does not present them as a sufficient proof of universal practical address.

The combined discrimination is the principal advance of this round:

| Contribution | Reviewed scope |
|---|---|
| Distinguishing current control from prior responsibility | An independent actor has a present causal relation to the patient. Its normative sufficiency requires argument; showing that responsibility adds reasons does not make responsibility necessary for every reason. |
| Separating concern, practical ends and means | Fitting sadness or a fitting wish cannot silently supply a reason to pursue relief. Ordinary means–end reasoning begins after an appropriate practical ground has been justified. |
| Two different rival restrictions | An exclusive prior-role gate differs from a restriction on the actor's deliberative access. The latter can admit patient-grounded moral claims while disputing categorical reasons; it need not deny universal moral significance. |
| A primary naturalistic comparator | Manne supplies a concrete philosophical alternative distinguishing patient claims and actor reasons. The source is evidence of an articulated argument, not an experiment establishing its truth. |
| A sharper comparison contract | Hold the actual patient, episode, relief target, permission and relevant consequences fixed while changing the operator and tracking the changed relation. Task assignment must not smuggle in the special role whose absence is being tested. |

The patient's condition plus eligible present control remains the defended candidate to investigate. Neither the naturalistic comparator nor a formal closure rule certifies that candidate. The strongest next question remains why the patient's admitted burden supplies the unrelated capable actor an objective contributory practical consideration, without a prior desire to help. Defining the eligible actor as already possessing the intended ethical outlook would assume that conclusion.

## Inherited work is credited at its existing scope

The finite transport result and its converse diagnostic are inherited from [reason transport](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md), especially §§2–3. Positive anchors and warranted preservation implications entail the reasons reachable through them; an uncovered target can remain false in the least interpretation of those particular premises. This is not a proof that an uncovered moral reason is false, or that arbitrary interpretations of an impoverished vocabulary describe morally admissible realities.

The canonical version correctly credits this inherited mathematics. Actor substitution preserves the same patient and episode. Starting only with self-relief or legitimate-role cases requires a justified transition beyond that restriction to obtain the independent-actor case. A directly justified independent-actor ground is another possible route and need not pass through a self-case. Universal actor coverage does not create an anchor for a patient who never was a deliberating or effective actor. These qualifications prevent the transport method from becoming an unnecessary mandatory sequence for every grounding argument.

The earlier [phenomenal-identity study](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#32-phenomenal-identity-practical-scope-and-the-necessary-root) already distinguished experiential constitution, practical address and strength. This round builds on that result rather than claiming to discover those distinctions from nothing. Its added content is the combined assessment of practical uptake, the two restrictions and the new source comparator.

The [accepted role-bound repair](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) and [root completion assessment](FOUNDATION-ROOT-ACCEPTANCE.md) retain their completed conditional scopes. A formed disposition can mediate an independently warranted response, and changed learning can suppress that response. The current round neither withdraws those results nor turns them into autonomous discovery of ethical validity. Responsibility or an entrusted role remains part of that repair application. This extension addresses the stronger independent-actor grounding question.

## Supplied assumptions and retained limitations

The reviewed statement keeps the following inputs visible: identifying an actual patient and the relevant experiential condition; justifying the particular patient-good or harm; correctly identifying the action's effect; permission and relevant competing considerations; the eligible practical capacity; initial reason warrant; and any normative preservation or practical-uptake principle. The finite argument propagates justified grounds only if its own hypotheses are warranted.

There is no inference from common physical origin to universal ethical authority. Equally, placing a normative premise in a present formalization does not establish its ultimate irreducibility. A richer constitutive explanation could constrain interpretations left open by the sparse formal language. The current work establishes neither that every such explanation must fail nor that the favored one has succeeded.

Practical applicability, access, motivation, actual conduct, a decisive duty and justified blame remain different questions. Patient willingness can matter to permission without defining moral standing. A patient's imperative having a satisfaction condition does not establish its validity. Special duties can remain significant without being exhaustive. Baseline reasons, if established, would still leave answerability, conflicting claims and necessity of the joint foundation to investigate.

No behavioral test in the proposed comparison table has been run. Helping frequency cannot establish normative authority; equal fuel or identical action labels cannot establish a complete matched comparison. No scientific study, Lean compilation, source-data reanalysis or implementation test was added for this review.
