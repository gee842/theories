# Independent review of the constructed learning interface

6 October 2026. Internal collaborating-AI review, separate from the formal and model authors. The named connection is **actual formed variants → paid construction of selector architecture → costed operation and revision through consequences**. This is a mathematical and conceptual review, not external peer review or physical identification.

**Status: proposed finite construction contract accepted in scope; final source and verification audit pending.**

## What the incoming results actually supply

The existing `FormedSelection` law supplies paid formation of A and H types, association, restoration of an access site, local capture, and localized copying. Its seven state coordinates and material bound belong to the actual successor history. It does not supply a sensory transducer, XOR response routing, finite selector lease, or return-to-renewal coupling. The [selector-revision study](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SELECTOR-REVISION.md) supplies those operations under another explicit local law.

Construction can make the existence of a functioning selector conditional on actual formed parts and their paid joining. Its functional recipe must remain declared. Payment for an assembly label, without required parts or causal operation of the resulting structure, would only move the unexplained architecture into a new name.

The exact earlier mixed-copy witness exhausts its initial fuel and finite source. It therefore cannot fund a new positive-cost assembly. A positive composition must exhibit a more generously funded history from its original state and carry every earlier expense to the handoff. The exhausted witness should remain an explicit counterexample to the proposed inference that every successful formation automatically supports learning.

## Acceptance conditions for a genuine conditional bridge

1. **Continuous energy account.** Assembly, trials, renewal, loss, and discarded or inaccessible support must remain in one account. The existing source uses unit energy while the selector example uses four-unit packets. Packet conversion must account for this denomination and any remainder. The new state cannot retain the old source while also claiming a duplicate packet store.
2. **Continuous material account.** A formed part consumed into a module cannot remain a free part or reopen precursor capacity for another use. The old carrier/site and unused material must remain recorded or explicitly disposed of. A module's material occupancy must constrain continued use of the old formation law where that law remains available.
3. **Causal participation.** Assembly requires actual parts from the prior process and positive support. The resulting hardware gates operation. Its absence, wrong orientation, and insufficient funding have operational consequences under the same law.
4. **Exactness at the claimed interface.** A correspondence to `SelectorRevision` must retain guards, actual successors and event costs for the operations included. Restricting to a ready-device subspace is legitimate if stated. Erasing assembly phases or imposing a lock that changes the event menu is not equality of the complete two processes.
5. **No hidden selection oracle.** An existence witness may choose a useful construction as a permitted event sequence. That proves possibility, not that the process selects the useful construction or learns its recipe. Both useful and unhelpful branches must remain admitted. Scores computed by the analyst must not control transitions.
6. **A bounded explanatory advance.** New joining, transduction and upkeep laws remain inputs. A conservative extension can compose them with actual formed material; it does not prove that the earlier reaction catalogue entailed them. Alternative updater architecture and its own consequence-dependent formation remain a later obligation unless directly constructed here.

These criteria refine the compatible-composition requirement in [generative grounding](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The separate [updater-formation review](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/UPDATER-FORMATION-REVIEW.md) records the adjacent task of constructing alternative mechanisms that alter selector proposals or maintenance.

## Contract accepted for implementation

The proposed construction has two stages. The preparation stage executes the existing formation law. The running stage retains free A and H, the old resident and access-site state, local fuel, common fuel, source remainder, and all parts incorporated into active or spent modules. Two free A parts can be paid to join a false-orientation selector; two free H parts can be paid to join a true-orientation selector. Old incorporated material remains unavailable for reuse after the module's lease expires. The functional joining recipe and selector operating law are explicit new local premises.

This is a substantive availability constraint: equal energy with different typed part inventories can admit different reconstruction histories. The recipe must affect an operation's guard and actual material successor; an analyst's successful-orientation label cannot supply it. A general correspondence to the existing selector process is a forward simulation. Lifting a selector history back to the construction additionally requires enough parts of each orientation. The guards and successors of trial, regime reversal and packet expiry can agree exactly even though the complete event menus differ.

The preparation is suspended at the handoff. The result therefore concerns a finite staged composition, not simultaneous activity under a conservative extension of the entire old reaction catalogue. Grouping unit source into larger packet denominations preserves the amount but does not establish a physical packet-making reaction. The implemented staged state carries the hidden regime from initial preparation through handoff unchanged. That environment is an input; no new condition is selected opportunistically when constructing the operational state.

The more informative candidate witness uses actual local capture and copying: from five material units, twelve common fuel units and eighteen source units, form H twice and A once, associate A, repair, then capture and locally copy A twice. This leaves two free A, two free H, one active resident, seven common fuel and sixteen source units. Both H formations must precede repair because the old law permits formation only at a damaged site. The initially suggested order placing an H formation after repair was rejected on that guard; a correct conserved ledger alone would not have made the history executable.

The binding recipe explains how a supplied response-selection module can become present only after construction using earlier material. It does not derive sensory transduction, response semantics, the renewal coupling, or the recipe's physical possibility. Formation and consequence-dependent change of alternative updater rules remains the adjacent research task. These limits should appear in the canonical theory as well as in the implementation note.

## Final audit

The first source draft and mathematical contract were reviewed directly. They preserve free, resident and incorporated matter; the source denomination includes a strict remainder bound; local fuel is retained but not silently converted into spendable common fuel. The operational relation is the old selector guard conjoined with required parts, followed by the exact old selector successor and part consumption. The staged relation admits the old formation steps only during preparation. Its positive witness executes the corrected local capture/copy history and consumes all four free parts across its two assemblies. No conceptual blocker was found in that contract.
