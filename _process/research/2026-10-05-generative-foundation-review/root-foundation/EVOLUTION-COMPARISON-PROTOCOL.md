## Question and possible outcomes

Does the completed sampled-developmental reconstruction mechanism impose a predictive constraint unavailable to an equally informed conventional dynamic-network implementation? Give both accounts the same actual cells, inherited retention state, founder history, bonds, finite stocks, event occurrence, sampling requirements and transfer allocations. Compare all fields and failure, not only a favorable endpoint.

The rival will be an explicitly authored conventional network-and-stock implementation of these mechanisms, not an attribution of new code or results to Watson and colleagues. A directed bond is a physical association in this model, not a neural weight or a full regulatory network. Literature comparison separately examines the actual developmental, ecological and reproductive scope of evolutionary connectionism. Neither a vocabulary difference nor a omitted resource variable in a selected paper is a field-wide impossibility result.

Success outcomes are: a justified additional restriction with a distinguishing consequence; a correspondence establishing equal consequences at this scope; or a mismatch requiring diagnosis. Do not tune a rival to be weaker, claim an empirical gain from synthetic histories, or demand a positive superiority result. A correspondence must prove the particular transition implementations agree, rather than assume a generic commuting diagram.
