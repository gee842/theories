# Whole realization and its practical articulation

**Scope correction on goal continuation, 7 October 2026:** the current [universal-scope audit](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#universal-scope-and-substantive-conclusions) distinguishes universal applicability and endorsement-independent validity from positive assistance at every qualifying opportunity. This round had overidentified the stronger assistance conjecture with the completion criterion. Its aid-specific arguments and surviving rival remain tests; an independently warranted role-sensitive account could itself articulate universal differentiated significance. First ethical identification and the account's universality remain unproved. This correction changes research selection, not the checked mathematics.

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored affirmative investigation for the [universal-intelligence target](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#session-target-universal-meta-ethics-of-all-intelligence), following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The proposed connection is **whole realization, including the activity of selecting its continuation → practical appraisal at a formed opportunity to act**. The question is whether this constitutive reconstruction establishes practical scope, rather than merely relocates its unresolved premise. This note introduces no mathematical proof claim or empirical finding. It is not a completed universal ethical derivation.

**The strongest affirmative formulation is that practical significance can be the whole's ethical quality articulated at a real point of continuation. It need not be a value owned in one place and subsequently authorized somewhere else. But calling this articulation a restriction of whole appraisal does not yet establish that every independently good realization supplies a reason at every relevant point of choice. The proposed identity still requires warrant.**

## 1. What is already established and is not a new route

The [constitutive-answer argument](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md) already rejects a mandatory second authorization after option-specific favor has been justified. [Grounding §34](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#34-self-good-practical-reasons-and-the-remaining-ground) already states the good-to-reason principle with its substantive and jurisdictional dependencies. The [whole-continuation integration §3](WHOLE-CONTINUATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md#3-the-universal-sufficiency-audit) expressly rules out presenting another costless, uncommitted-helper comparison as a new universal derivation.

Likewise, [the integral account §7](FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#7-whole-appraisal-can-precede-its-temporal-expression) already allows whole appraisal to precede temporal contributions. Beginning with the whole is not a new discovery. The [prospective-imposition argument](UNIVERSAL-RESPONSE-COMPOSITION-ARGUMENT.md) already locates a selecting activity within the consequential relationship it may create. It challenges blanket antecedent-role exclusivity but preserves an assistance-specific rival. [Self-ground §66](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#66-normative-powers-and-assistance-specific-exclusion) has also closed the attempted regress from a role's authority to universal assistance.

The present attempt therefore tests a more specific proposal: **can evaluating the actual choosing activity as part of the same whole make practical reasons a faithful restriction of the whole appraisal, without importing their ethical force into that appraisal first?** Its new contribution is a correspondence and noncircularity audit of that proposal. Its positive argument develops the inherited constitutive candidate; it does not count repeated support as independent evidence.

Two concurrent investigations sharpen this limit. [Immanent self-assessment](UNIVERSAL-WARRANT-SUFFICIENCY.md) already places selection standards within the whole and distinguishes inescapable inclusion from practical authority. [Prospective ground constitution](PROSPECTIVE-GROUND-CONSTITUTION-ARGUMENT.md) already answers a temporal objection to pre-assent assessment and rejects self-authorization through forming a role. Both were directly read during this round. The present reconstruction must not report either conclusion as a new ground. No additional affirmative warrant is claimed merely by redescribing them as whole-appraisal restriction.

## 2. Reconstruct the complete practical relationship

The relevant whole includes ongoing living, material and social relationships, developed capacities for distinguishing alternatives, the activity of deliberation or selection, and what their exercise actually forms. An intelligence's access, retained understanding and power to alter a continuation have histories within this unfolding. They are neither an independently complete subject nor a detached spectator's control panel.

A continuation must retain the selecting activity itself. Its use of entrusted powers, its actual uncertainty, delay, coercion, consent and formation of further dependence can change what is realized. A comparison between terminal outputs that omits those relations is not the promised whole comparison. This qualification does not assume that every mentioned feature has the same ethical importance, or that the complete universe must be represented before any judgment is warranted.

An available option names a possible exercise of an actually formed capacity within these relations. It does not mean an already permitted or required exercise. When a response is ineffective, inaccessible or destructive of the very realization it purports to support, its relation to that realization changes. This is why the practical comparison is answerable to actual conditions rather than to a chooser's preferred description alone.

There are several questions about this one situation:

| Question | What its answer must preserve |
|---|---|
| What has favorable or adverse ethical character? | Actual realizations and the relations constituting that character, including terminal and nonproductive realizations. |
| What counts in favor of a particular available response? | That response's relation to those grounds, its actual scope and any warranted exclusion from this choice. |
| What may or must be done? | Relevant competing considerations, legitimate authority, means, uncertainty and constraints. |
| What is recognized or enacted? | Formed understanding, motivation, selection and actual consequences, including failure. |

These are distinctions among explanatory tasks, not independent kinds of reality. A favorable outcome does not by itself license an intervention; the restriction of a permission does not by itself erase every favorable consideration.

## 3. The affirmative constitutive argument

Consider actual relief, understanding, finite achievement or self-care whose favorable ethical character has been independently defended in its full relationship. The proposal is that this character can articulate itself practically through an available activity that realizes or sustains it. The point is present before private endorsement when that activity already stands in the relevant relationship. Endorsement can disclose the point, create another relationship or fail to respond to it; its occurrence need not manufacture the original significance.

The strongest explanation has three connected parts.

First, what choosing makes real is among the grounds of its appraisal. Where practical judgment purports to answer what this continuation would actually realize, excluding that realization merely because it is absent from the current selector's adopted objectives changes the question being answered. This supports criticism of an appraisal claiming whole adequacy while silently reporting only success against an incumbent purpose. It does not imply that every intelligence already makes that claim.

Second, the proposed practical address is located in the relation of continuation itself. The response does not first receive a private portion of good and then export its force. At this point of unfolding, this exercise of capacity would constitute or support that realization. A different causal route, a changed permission or an altered historical relation can change the practical judgment. No return to an initiating individual is required by this explanation.

Third, formed roles need not be the sole origins of that address. A commitment can make reliability, invited reliance or a new shared activity part of the whole. Those are genuine additional grounds, rather than optional labels. But where the reason offered for adopting the commitment is precisely what its activity would make possible, the constitutive account explains that prospective point through the same realization that later explains correction of its means. An exclusive role account has to withhold that pre-adoption point or ground it differently.

The last claim is an affirmative assessment, not a contradiction proved against every rival. The [existing elective-concern counterreview](ELECTIVE-CONCERN-COUNTERREVIEW.md) already explains why normative powers need not merely activate antecedent duties. The present argument must not turn the possibility of grounds before adoption into the premise that all reasons must predate a role. Genuine reason creation and pre-role favor can coexist.

This reconstruction has explanatory economy: one actual relation can explain why a response has a point, why inaccurate means are corrigible, why fulfillment can end its occasion, and why concern can require relinquishing a harmful practice. Yet a formed project can also display the latter three patterns. Only the defended first, pre-adoption appraisal distinguishes the candidate from the surviving rival. The economy supports a candidate identity; it does not establish its universal necessity.

## 4. What a restriction of whole appraisal can and cannot supply

There is a sound methodological idea here. If a whole appraisal already contains the warranted grounds of a particular choice, assessing that choice must retain them faithfully. Moving a descriptive boundary, renaming components or representing one selecting process at a finer scale cannot justify dropping its retained grounds. Real changes in access, delegation or authority are different cases, because they change the appraised relationships. The [current formal integration](UNIVERSAL-METAETHICS-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md) already treats complete reason reference and justified compatibility as obligations.

The proposed shortcut nevertheless has two distinct problems.

**Comparative quality can be too thin.** An ordering of whole continuations expresses comparisons. It need not separately express the grounds favoring each option, a permission to decline, or a rule excluding a consideration as the determining basis of one decision. Two alternatives can each have reasons in their favor even when one is better overall. Conversely, an overall comparison does not say which particular consideration may determine an entrusted choice. Merely including the chooser in the description does not put all these distinctions into the appraisal's output.

A plural-response account can also connect the same realization with warranted appreciation, protection or enactment without reducing them to one ranking. Such an account is a rival to a ranking reduction, not necessarily to the universal target: if it grants the relevant endorsement-independent contributory moral favor, disagreement about ranking has not defeated that favor. The assistance-specific exclusion in §5 disputes the stronger positive-response conjecture; it does not by that fact refute universal differentiated meta-ethics. Categorical rational authority, decisive obligation and maximization must not silently replace the narrower target under review.

The established merit-procedure example illustrates the substantive issue: a warranted selection procedure can exclude relief of distress as the basis of its award, while a different, independently available response can be favored by that relief. The whole must include how this procedural jurisdiction was formed and why it is warranted; it must not treat the procedure as an unexplained sovereign domain. A single outcome ranking would still fail to state this distinction unless given an appropriate interpretation. This is inherited ethical content used as a representation test, not a newly derived merit principle.

**A richer appraisal can contain the missing premise.** Suppose whole appraisal is enriched to state every response-specific favorable consideration, permission, obligation and exclusion. A faithful restriction can then recover those judgments at their actual positions. That is useful organization. It does not establish the judgments' warrant if the enrichment simply supplies them. Calling the richer object “the whole” cannot turn supplied practical significance into a deduction from independently justified comparative quality.

A formal example contrasting one order with different detailed ground records can test this information loss. It would show the insufficiency of the selected representation. It would not show that the same fully specified ethical reality permits arbitrary changes in practical truth. That stronger claim would require a substantive rival preserving the proposed constitutive ground, as in §5.

There is also a concrete overlap question for any proposed restriction construction. If each representation carries a different choosing occurrence, its full continuation descriptions may have little actual overlap. A claim that local appraisals agree on common content must identify the shared comparison, context and complete ground, rather than infer a substantive ethical agreement from an overlap condition that barely applies. Conversely, erasing the choosing occurrence to force overlap can discard precisely the historical responsibility in dispute. This is a correspondence question for the chosen representation, not a theorem that every whole-ground account lacks cross-position content.

This yields the exact correspondence obligation for the affirmative route: **state whether whole appraisal is only comparative quality or already a ground-structured appraisal of actual selecting activity, and identify the warrant of whichever practical content the latter adds.** The two can belong to one constitutive explanation. They must not trade meanings during its argument.

## 5. The substantive rival survives within the whole

The strongest rival grants developed intelligence, whole causal membership, actual adversity, effective relief, terminal good and the ethical significance of restraints against imposing injury. It also recognizes special responsibilities that arise without voluntary adoption. Its controversial restriction concerns positive assistance outside those relations: another realization's favorable character becomes a reason for this deciding activity only through an appropriate formed responsibility or undertaking.

For this rival, the whole includes differentiated practical authority. An undertaking is not a metaphysically isolated private value; it is a historically formed way of organizing answerability, claims and control. Its account can allow favorable appraisal of relief as a realization, while denying that this alone favors every physically available assisting choice. It can accept that the choice itself occurs within the whole. Ontological inclusion therefore does not by itself dislodge its practical restriction.

The affirmative objection remains substantive. Protecting authority over undertakings can justify permissions, special duties and legitimate limits. It does not yet explain why **all** favorable consideration for an available beneficial response must disappear before such an undertaking exists. The relation between response and realization offers a direct explanation of why freely undertaking it could have a point. But the rival can reject that particular explanation without denying every unchosen reason, every ethical quality or all whole appraisal. That rejected pre-role judgment is the disputed ground, not an already conceded premise.

An imposition comparison does not by itself settle this difference. Selecting a harmful arrangement and declining to remedy an independently arising burden both participate in future formation, but their histories and powers differ. The rival can place normative significance on the contemplated formation of adverse conditions. The affirmative account must explain whether that relation adds a responsibility to a common ground or supplies a reason absent elsewhere. Describing both as selections does not resolve that issue; the [prospective-imposition study](UNIVERSAL-RESPONSE-COMPOSITION-ARGUMENT.md#6-what-would-change-the-next-action) already preserves it.

Thus the rival is neither refuted nor vindicated by the restriction proposal. It offers a genuine account of the actual differentiated relationship, not a freely toggled reason predicate in an impoverished model. Defeating it requires a warrant for the broader practical ground or a substantive failure of its restriction.

## 6. First ethical quality and the risk of self-certification

Putting deliberation into the whole raises a further obligation. If a continuation is called favorable partly because it contains a **justified** choice, and that favorable appraisal is then used as the first proof that the choice has a reason, the argument has reused the disputed judgment. An independent account might vindicate both together through a constitutive explanation, but merely inserting “fitting response” into the description does not provide one. This is a risk in this reconstruction, not a claim that every mutually explanatory account is circular or invalid.

The positive route should instead identify what is alleged to constitute favorable realization: the actual manner of living and relating, fulfillment and obstruction, finite completion, formed understanding and the conditions their activity creates. It must defend the ethical character of that configuration. Successful reproduction, increasing options, accurate control, coherent endorsement or unrestricted integration cannot independently serve as its measure. The repository already retains dominating organizations, constructive endings and final nonproductive good that challenge those shortcuts.

Terminal good is especially important here. An actual finite realization can be ethically significant even if no subsequent intelligence recognizes it or no later improvement follows. The prospective selector's role cannot be made a necessary ingredient of its occurrence. Conversely, identifying its favorable occurrence still leaves the disputed practical scope when an opportunity to realize it exists. These are connected questions within one history, not an ethical object waiting for an external subject to activate it.

A nonclaimant intelligence is a separate scope test. It can form purposes, learn and select without claiming to give an adequate ethical account. A contradiction in a claimed whole appraisal would constrain such an appraisal; it would not by itself establish an ethical reason addressed to every intelligence that makes no such claim. Universal warrant must concern the actual relation regardless of assent. Recognition, motivation and compliance cannot be smuggled into the target under the term “intelligence.”

Learning and self-modification raise the same point over time. A later appraisal can correct an earlier ethical misidentification, as the [whole-continuation round](WHOLE-CONTINUATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md#2-what-this-changes-about-candidate-invariants) establishes at its philosophical scope. A shared whole does not make the incumbent judgment infallible. A claimed invariant must therefore concern warranted grounds and legitimate transformations, not unchanged approval or identical selected actions. Its substantive warrant remains to establish before a preservation proof can certify its implementation.

## 7. Executed comparison: formation of dependence, withdrawal and new support

The [concurrent prospective study](PROSPECTIVE-GROUND-CONSTITUTION-ARGUMENT.md#6-what-changes-the-next-action) specifies the next substantive discriminator. This section executes a philosophical comparison of its competing explanations. Following the [terminology correction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md#formation-participation-and-responsibility), it names the formation and transformation of dependence directly. This descriptive relation neither installs an independent originating author nor settles its ethical force. These are constructed histories, not empirical observations. No formal realization or numerical proof is claimed.

Fix a developing arrangement in which a living continuity undergoes a severe, still-unwanted burden when a particular enabling condition is absent. Some organized activity has acquired control of a route that can sustain that condition for a specified interval. Exercising that route takes limited activity and foregoes a specified alternative undertaking; these burdens remain in every comparison. Sustaining the condition supports the same identified relief. Its effectiveness and the current opportunity to exercise it are held fixed at this selected level. The complete histories, authority and meanings of that opportunity are deliberately **not** declared identical.

The choosing and affected continuities are descriptive differentiations within that arrangement. Neither is installed as an independently sovereign owner. The affected life need not formulate a claim or participate in the choice. Relevant permission, uncertainty and effects on other continuities remain explicit questions, rather than being settled by the fact of physical control.

| History at the opportunity to continue | Actual differentiating relationship | What the account of how dependence forms has to explain |
|---|---|---|
| **Introduction.** The selecting activity previously disabled an independently available route while reorganizing the arrangement for its own undertaking. The present burden arises through that alteration. | Its past contribution changed the condition that ordinary living could previously rely on. Earlier loss remains part of the history even if support now resumes. | Why this interference gives a directed reason to restore or compensate; mere chronological precedence cannot supply that reason. |
| **Withdrawal after reliance.** The activity established a supply, invited reliance, and induced abandonment of a workable alternative. It now considers ending supply. | The present dependence was formed through its undertaking and the actual displacement of an alternative. | Why the dependence and reliance constrain ending or require a supported transition; the supply's current usefulness alone does not identify this additional ground. |
| **Declining new support.** An adverse change arose without either of those contributions. The activity has now acquired a usable route but has made no assurance, displaced no alternative and accepted no undertaking. | Current capacity and the attainable relief are present; the identified historical responsibility relations are absent. | Whether the response–relief relation already gives any favorable consideration, rather than only an opportunity whose point depends on a new undertaking. |
| **Ending limited support without creating dependence.** The activity temporarily sustained the condition. Its announced interval ends, no assurance of continuation was given, and no independent alternative was abandoned because of its supply. | There is past beneficial participation, but the reliance and foreclosure specified in the second row are absent. The announcement's authority still requires appraisal. | Whether having supported once establishes the same directed continuing responsibility, or whether this history resembles new support on that particular ground. |

The fourth row is essential. A rule treating every withdrawal as equivalent to the introduction of a burden would erase how the dependence arose. A rule treating all nonrenewal as mere inactivity would erase the second row's actual formation of reliance. Neither action/omission vocabulary nor a selected snapshot therefore states the complete proposed ground. Likewise, the fact that assistance would change the future occurs in all four descriptions and cannot by itself explain the rival's differential scope.

The affirmative adjudication is that **actually creating this dependence can matter to what the selecting activity now has to answer**. Displacing a workable alternative and inviting reliance can give the continuing opportunity a directed significance that freshly discovering the same capacity lacks. This is a substantive normative judgment about those actual relationships, not a deduction from causal centrality. The first two histories can consequently require an explanation of restoration, compensation, transition or justified release that the third does not require on that historical basis. The fourth does not inherit every feature of the second merely because both involve ending a supply. Whether another ground still favors continuation remains open in both.

This is a positive account of differentiated practical address within one whole. The address need not begin with an independent owner or a private preference. It can form as the actual organization of continuation changes. It also shows why an available response's grounds need not be exhausted by the quality of its matched relief: historical formation can explain an additional reason, its direction and its possible discharge. The alleged normative work of dependence and reasonable reliance is identified, not derived without ethical premises.

Now apply the strongest rival rather than the crude role restriction. It grounds restraints on imposition in the security of already unfolding lives and grounds withdrawal responsibilities in actual established dependence and reliance. It denies that bare access to a new beneficial route constitutes either relationship. This explains a genuine contrast among the rows without appealing to isolated substances, current selfish preference or an arbitrary temporal baseline. It does not need to deny the relief's favorable realized character.

The remaining point is precise: explaining an **additional directed responsibility** in the first two rows has not yet explained why the third has **no contributory favor at all**. The affirmative candidate finds such favor in what the newly available continuation would realize. The rival instead treats a responsibility-forming relationship as constitutive of practical address. Neither the explanation of historical difference nor common present efficacy settles that disputed exclusivity. A defended permission to decline, even under a moderate burden, would not alone settle it either. Conversely, calling the role gate unnecessary merely because relief is available would repeat the original sufficiency claim.

The comparison therefore changes the proposed universal argument's form. Universal grounding must preserve real formation of differentiated responsibilities; it cannot treat roles as irrelevant labels or assume that every practical position is interchangeable. But an account seeking to exclude all initial favor for new support needs an explanation of that exclusion beyond the origin of special responsibilities. This is a narrowed burden of explanation, not a proof that every such exclusion fails or that the favorable baseline has been universally established.

### The self-modification test

Continue the reliance history, with its invited reliance, displaced alternative and current adverse condition still present. The selecting activity now changes the organization through which it registers the undertaking, suppressing the influence of that history on its future choices. Assume for this test that the earlier account had independently warranted a continuing responsibility. The update changes present uptake; it does not restore the abandoned alternative, end the dependence, fulfill the undertaking or establish a legitimate release.

If a rival makes present maintained uptake alone constitutive of responsibility, it must explain why this update removes a responsibility whose identified ground was the continuing historical relationship. The affirmative judgment is that the responsibility survives that particular update while the relevant relationship remains. Its practical implementation may fail; its ground has not been answered merely by that failure. This applies the [existing warrant argument](NORMATIVE-WARRANT-ARGUMENT.md) to an actual change of practical organization, rather than asserting that every revision must preserve every old judgment.

A history-sensitive rival can agree: formed address can continue after recognition or endorsement disappears. It can then permit legitimate correction, fulfillment, transfer or release to change the responsibility on different grounds. That repair rejects exclusive dependence on present uptake but still allows the rival to deny new-support favor. The test therefore supports **history-sensitive warrant distinct from currently operative orientation** at this declared premise, while leaving the universal initial ground unresolved. It is not a claim that all memory loss is blameworthy or that every disabled representation must be restored.

## 8. Result and the exact next test

The affirmative architecture is coherent: **ethical realization and practical address may be differentiated articulations of one relation of continuation**. It permits pre-assent significance, formed special responsibilities, self-good, terminal good and legitimate refusal without a value-transfer between independent owners. The relevant selecting activity belongs inside the appraisal, not outside its target.

The attempted proof by restriction does not complete that architecture's ethical warrant. In the thin reading it may omit practical content; in the rich reading it may contain the very content to be grounded. Including the actual choosing activity also requires a noncircular explanation of any “fitting” or “justified” quality attributed to it. These are specific findings about this attempted inference. They do not show that an irreducible external ethical principle is necessary, or that universal grounding is impossible.

The next substantive test is consequently fixed more tightly than another clean helping case. Defend an account of the **same actual continuation relation** that explains its ethical character and practical articulation together, then assess whether historical responsibility is a constituent of that first favor, an additional ground, or a justified exclusion. The independently identified relation must retain finite realization, permission, nonreflective affected living and the nonclaimant intelligence. If the account needs an already favorable option judgment to certify its whole quality, or needs the already approved whole quality to certify that very option judgment without further explanation, the attempted closure has not advanced.

The executed comparison of how dependence forms above gives differentiated responsibility a positive relational explanation and rejects present uptake as its sole measure where a continuing historical ground has already been warranted. Its history-sensitive rival survives and still disputes initial favor for new support. The next decisive advance would therefore have to justify that exclusion or the broader sufficient ground, retaining the actual differences already established. Our representation audit does not replace that task with a new demand to prove whole inclusion. Neither the assistance-specific conjecture nor an optional route to categorical rational authority exhausts the still-unfinished universal account of differentiated practical significance.

The universal claim remains unproved. The gain is an explicit account of which proposed reduction was attempted, what it would have to preserve, and why neither an order restriction nor an unexamined rich appraisal discharges its remaining premise. Formal verification may certify the representation and correspondence claims under explicit hypotheses; it cannot certify first ethical warrant merely by representing it.
