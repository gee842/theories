## 2. Affirmative explanatory argument

A learned representation is useful beyond an isolated episode when the relations it preserves can participate in further organized activity. Combination provides one route: acquire what primitive operations do and how a reusable operation composes them, then apply that organization to a new structure. The new structure need not have been encountered as a whole. Its interpretation remains answerable to the actual law under which the components and composition operate.

The explanatory work is done by identifiable dependencies. Primitive observations constrain effects; an ordered composite encounter discriminates two composition procedures; the records enter formation of an interpreter; the later query is processed through that interpreter; and execution depends on available support. Failure can be located in any of those relationships. The same decomposition does not make a symbol, its referent and an interpreter three independent ontological realms. They are differentiated roles in the common local history under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md).

This account explains a restricted productive capacity. It does not establish that every kind of cognition involves explicit compositional symbols. The grammar may be represented symbolically, implemented by a network or distributed through an organized practice; those are further correspondence claims. A generic assertion that a neural network can approximate the interpreter would not supply such a correspondence.

Within the larger theory, the developmental question concerns how this operation becomes available and why these encounters occur. The evolutionary question concerns which inherited and reconstructed organizations sustain or alter such learning across descendants. Explaining a learner's current generalization is not already an explanation of either origin. Conversely, the existing origin mechanisms remain relevant evidence when their exact interfaces are connected.

## 5. Primary evidence and comparisons

These are targeted source inspections, not a systematic review, new experiment or reanalysis. URLs identify the originals; no original publication bytes were imported by this round. The sources concern different populations and tasks, so their results are not one observed cognitive-evolutionary history.

**Lake and Baroni (2023), learned systematic generalization.** Directly inspected the primary article's introduction, behavioral results and modeling description; the earlier [mathematics-learning assessment](MATHEMATICS-LEARNING-EVIDENCE.md) retains the broader benchmark limits. Participants learned pseudolanguage instructions and answered new combinations; the authors compared their behavior with several models, including a network trained across compositional tasks. Our inference is that acquired compositional use has a concrete empirical target and serious neural comparators. The new Lean construction neither reproduces that network nor models the full human error pattern. It supplies a conditional acquisition/transfer relationship and exposes its grammar assumptions. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10620072/).

**Hattori et al. (2023), biological meta-learning.** Directly inspected the author-hosted paper's opening and first results pages, printed pp.2182–2183. The study separates improvement across sessions from trial-by-trial adaptation in a mouse reversal task, and reports a role for OFC CaMKII-dependent plasticity in the former. Its model and mice differ in how they exploit reward-independent choice history; the authors do not present the artificial network as an exact brain model. Our inference is that changing the organization supporting faster learning is a concrete biological comparison, while algorithmic identity requires further evidence. No raw neural data, source statistics or full intervention methods were independently reanalyzed here. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://komiyamalab.biosci.ucsd.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/s41593-023-01485-3-1.pdf).

**Ben-Ami Bartal et al. **Objection: a record is saved, but the code bypasses it.** The first draft computed interpretation alongside the stored batch. This establishes dependence on the record interface. The recorder's fidelity and the reader's construction remain premises, and the batch does not model physical interleaving.

**Objection: exact rule identification is demanded too strongly.** Correct. When both primitive effects coincide, order may remain unresolved while a new expression is still answered correctly. A checked witness preserves this case. Exact recovery is sufficient for the general result, not claimed necessary for every use. This parallels the existing knowledge account's distinction between resolving a task and identifying every mechanism.

**Objection: three examples cannot establish unrestricted productivity.** Correct. The nonuniform rival agrees on those examples and fails outside them. The all-expression theorem is conditional on uniform compositionality, rather than a proof of that premise from the sample. Biological or developmental evidence must warrant the application range. This is a limitation of the local explanation, not a refutation of every immanent account of semantic formation.

**Objection: this is a conventional interpreter.** Accepted. Ordinary symbolic implementations receive full credit, and learned networks may implement relevant relationships. No novelty or superiority follows from generative vocabulary. The contribution is the connection, exact dependencies, supported execution and failures within the shared derivation.

**Objection: the evolutionary explanation is still missing.** For this interpreter, yes. No population, variation mechanism, reproductive law or developmental formation of the grammar was added. The broader chapter states that obligation and preserves the existing evolutionary-organization results. A claim that this interpreter is an evolved adaptation would require new evidence and is not made.

**Objection: competent interpretation need not be understanding or consciousness.** The proof is about interpretation at a defined interface. Philosophical sufficiency requires the richer content/use/grounds account and appropriate comparisons; experiential realization is a further unresolved requirement. No extra invisible `Understands` flag is demanded, but neither is a local output function promoted to a complete mind.

**Objection: combining source studies creates an illusion of one causal chain.** Their populations, interventions and explananda are explicitly separated. They constrain candidate links and do not jointly establish one lineage or one cognitive architecture. The current full objective remains incomplete.
