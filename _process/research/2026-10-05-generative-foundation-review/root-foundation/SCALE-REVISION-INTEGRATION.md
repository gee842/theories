## Selected transition and affirmative result

The prior [21-theorem approximation study](RELATIONS-ACROSS-SCALES-PROOF.md) gives conditions under which descriptions preserve particular judgments. The present study makes acquired evidence alter the operative description and subsequent response in one declared history. Its conclusion is conditional: a calibrated revision can change a missed response into an enacted response, provided the needed time, operating work and support remain available. It supplies a correspondence/implementation premise for practical judgment, not a new normative ground.

In the quantitative witness, activation changes dependence; a probe records the changed total, inspection obtains a consequential coordinate, and revision carries the record through the elapsed stage. The view's forecast determines the support dose. A matched clamp retains the same data and costs but prevents that coordinate from entering the forecast. Endpoint conditions differ. This is an actual causal comparison within the supplied law, not two independently assigned success predicates.

## Substantive counterreview by the same agent

1. **Useful mechanism does not establish necessity.** `fixed_response_also_protects` defeats a claim that refinement is required in the primary example. Selecting between fixed response and further inquiry under varied dependencies remains open. Neither route has established universal superiority.
2. **Mismatch does not identify its explanation.** A changed total can have several causes. The installed second-coordinate sensor and switch supply a correct repair for this law. A general inference to that repair has not been proved. The law also keeps the acquired decrement stable through the schedule; arbitrary intervening changes need renewed calibration.
3. **Revision has an opportunity cost.** Work 3132 pays for the three inquiry/revision operations and leaves no work for response. Incorrectly ignoring revision delay produces an unjustified current forecast. Querying is not automatically the better response.
4. **Calibration and feasibility differ.** Bias −3 defeats the zero-margin revision. Margin 3 recovers this biased case; applying margin 3 with the accurate sensor instead requires four support units and fails against one available unit. Conservative information can be honest but unusable under the installed policy.
5. **Refusal is not protection.** A funded response refused for bad context, stale data or insufficient support still advances the declared drift and can lose the condition. A funding denial executes no stage; the stopped controller thereafter stutters. Neither convention asserts that a real body stops changing or is safe after a halt.
6. **No complete biological or normative account.** The source is finite and unrenewed, `b` is held fixed, the channel and law are idealized, and participants beyond these roles are absent. The threshold is supplied. There is no measured cell–organ–body loop, selection over newly formed sensors or universal ethical inference here.

这些限制保留真实失败，但不把局部表示的遗漏直接宣布为整个理论不可能。下一检验应使竞争解释、查询途径和回应都在同样的可得信息与资源下比较；生物对应按已有身体／生态研究继续，不能只给变量换上生物名称。
