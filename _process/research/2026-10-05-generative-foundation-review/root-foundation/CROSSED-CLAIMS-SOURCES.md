## Actual joint protection constrains possible chances

**Gerard Vong (2020), “Weighing Up Weighted Lotteries: Scarcity, Overlap Cases, and Fair Inequalities of Chance”, Ethics 130(3), DOI 10.1086/707212.** [Primary journal text](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/707212). The root read the introduction and §§I–III.A; no full assessment of the later proposed procedure or its applications is claimed.

Vong shows that overlapping groups which can be helped together constrain the available distributions of individual chances. Equal-strength claims need not admit equal chances through a feasible lottery. He also separates procedural fairness from what is right overall. This is a substantive check on any attempt to specify fair probabilities without retaining the joint protective options. We do not adopt his complete weighting procedure, independently validate its general adequacy, or infer an actual allocation recommendation. The new Lean model has its own explicitly stated three-position support law; it is not a formal verification of Vong's article.
