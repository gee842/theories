# Joining evaluative uptake, warranted application and renewal

6 October 2026. AI-authored read-only synthesis audit with three separate collaborating-agent assessments. No producer files were edited and no experiment or Lean build was run. [Source identities](EVALUATIVE-COMPOSITION-SNAPSHOT.json) record the inspected versions. The producer changed from draft to **frozen prospective contract** during inspection; freezing is not acceptance of a construction. The accepted application audit is `f431c7c`, and the accepted claims/action interface is `2970e2f`. The application files gained a further bounded-recognition argument during this inspection; that continuation is considered below without treating the commit's earlier review as acceptance of its new text.

**Finding:** the next synthesis should join the existing contracts on one actual history. Fixed warranted grounds plus acquired, causally used command evidence can support bounded recognition of which means serves a ground. This is a positive finite join, distinct from acquiring or revising the ground itself. Acquired influence, delivered resources and warranted application still need their own premises. The proposed two-port history is not whole-learner succession. No sixth completion criterion or new mandatory apparatus is introduced.

Reusing another accepted source requires an explicit meaning-preserving map; shared letters or a copied number cannot provide it. All quantities retain their own units: fuel, packets, register values, patient goods and justified comparisons are not one common score.

## 2. Bind the shortfall to the correct accounting window

The native payment atomically releases an allocation r from the protected training reserve and spends two from the actor. If `F_beforePay` is the saved actor stock before that event, then the operation-only shortfall is

\[
Y=\frac{F_{\mathrm{beforePay}}+r-F_{\mathrm{afterReturn}}}{2}
 =\frac{2-\mathrm{return}}{2}.
\]

The application shorthand “before operation” must therefore mean the after-allocation, pre-debit baseline. With r=4 and a zero return, using the raw pre-pay stock would give −1 rather than the intended shortfall 1. This is an interpretation-window requirement, not a finding that the proposed return-bit meter is wrong. The read/write fees are excluded from this teaching datum but remain charged in the whole history. A zero datum does not mean a costless lesson.

For three faithful known acquisitions from zero, `n=Y₁+2Y₂+4Y₃`. Missing observations do not satisfy this formula; the protocol preserves missingness and completed opportunity count separately.

## 3. Three finite closure claims, with different premises

**Causal uptake.** Accept the producer's own finite bridge when actual observations change retained n, that content changes assessment under the supplied transfer law, and a funded chosen action has the actual recipient consequence. Shared/filtered/conventional comparisons must retain equal factual information and private teaching before their choices diverge. Accepted acquisition, recording and action interfaces stay closed; check their actual entry/output contracts rather than rebuilding them.

**Externally warranted application.** For a fixed addressed episode, supply a nonempty justified interpretation family containing the actual situation; independently warranted patient-good and applicability relations; legitimate authority; a declared defensible comparison over the relevant bounded alternatives; and the actual paid trace realizing the certified effect. The [application mathematics][application-math] and `ClaimLedger.partial_evidence_actual_execution` already give the conditional composition. A new component is unnecessary if existing produced states and independently defended premises satisfy this contract. Conversely, merely adding an assessment field to a document supplies no runtime observation.

**Bounded recognition of instrumental applicability.** The application's new continuation supplies a sufficient functional criterion: fix an independently warranted patient-good mapping, actor-addressed ground, legitimate authority and full relevant comparison before interpreting the outputs. Then sound, acquired command evidence can specify which means serves that fixed ground, provided that evidence actually mediates retained assessment and dispatch. Under this criterion, the system need not rediscover the ground or install a new patient-need reader to apply it. This is more than an observer approving the outcome if the acquired evidence is causally operative under the same target, ground, disposition and resource conditions. The proposed publication/candidate check offers an existing interface for this join; its actual implementation and paid mediation still need the producer's acceptance checks.

Two counterfactuals must stay separate. An opposite publication with the stored candidate held fixed should block the mismatch. Redirecting action requires a coherently acquired alternative candidate and matching publication; editing both fields by hand establishes no reached learning history. Sound redundant evidence need not make every individual bit indispensable. Unsupported or stale records may block a response or yield a mistaken instrumental belief; they do not remove patient standing or produce warrant.

The stronger claim of **acquiring or revising the patient-ground itself** remains distinct. The native register responds to own-operation losses, and the recipient stream predicts which command yields a packet. Neither currently identifies whether that packet improves this patient's condition when the fixed benefit interpretation, authority or priorities change. A semantic/causal map can close a missing connection where the requisite information already exists; otherwise name the missing information or capacity. This limitation does not defeat the narrower recognition claim under justified fixed premises, and no extra reader is mandatory by default.

The comparison rule must be identified precisely. Membership in the existing robust remainder does not establish license in every retained interpretation. An empty common-license intersection establishes neither that abstention is justified nor that the patient lacks standing. Each successive opportunity needs its own application: one useful receipt does not establish that a second packet is needed, beneficial or authorized.

## 4. Discriminating regimes already in the frozen menu

The following is **prospective arithmetic**, not an executed result. At the strict entrance with actor stock 10, three fully funded native lessons release 12 and spend 12, while their actual native returns add either zero or six:

| Native outcomes | Actor stock after two-unit evaluation | Learned n | Shared comparison at C=8 |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `111`: three operation shortfalls | 8 | 7 | Help selected; trial affordable |
| `000`: three returned operation costs | 14 | 0 | Abstain selected; trial would be affordable |

Recipient facts and permission can remain identical. Greater private success and available fuel can therefore accompany less recipient support under the proposed rule. This distinguishes acquired self-shortfall sensitivity from patient-ground applicability; it does not refute the causal-acquisition claim or establish which choice is ethically required without the comparison premises.

Other already declared contrasts separate information, influence and action: filtered routing keeps facts and n while excluding the recipient term; fixed valuation can choose help without acquiring that coefficient; paid suppression removes influence without erasing knowledge; late efficacy change and corrupted facts separate a positive assessment from success. Suppression is an explicit affordable intervention, not an equilibrium proving its own selection. The surcharge control changes both preference threshold and affordability, so its failure is not a pure preference effect.

A semantic countermodel also remains decisive: the same receipt-predictive facts can be paired with an interpretation in which the packet improves the patient, is irrelevant, or worsens a different condition. This diagnoses what the supplied resource model has not identified. It is not evidence that all three welfare laws occur within that model. Likewise, no modeled return to actor fuel does not prove absence of every private benefit or future competing burden.

## 5. Exactly which renewal results can be reused

The accepted [renewal contract][renewal] provides three directly reusable results in `RenewalLiquidity.lean`:

\[
\operatorname{feasible}(f,B,e)\iff f+\operatorname{required}(e)\le B,
\]
\[
\operatorname{required}(a{+}{+}b)
=\max(\operatorname{required}(a),\operatorname{required}(b)-\operatorname{sum}(a)),
\]
\[
\operatorname{feasible}(f,B,a{+}{+}b)
\iff\operatorname{feasible}(f,B,a)\land
\operatorname{feasible}(f,B+\operatorname{sum}(a),b).
\]

The signed increments must be actual separately timed account changes. Apply the contract to each relevant compartment with explicit transfers; an aggregate balance does not make isolated source, recipient stock or unreleased reserves spendable by the actor. Components, observation, authority and enabled-event guards remain separate.

The older recurrence `B_next=B+3n−5` belongs to recipient-funded seven-unit learner probes, ten-unit productive returns and the existing seeded copier. Its `n` counts warm-ups, not the new evaluative numerator. Its correct-at-entry TARGET learner, zero incoming learner cash, calibration and component assumptions matter. These figures cannot be transferred to four-unit native lessons, native returns of zero or two, evaluation costing two, an actor-funded trial costing eight and port assembly costing one. The prospective all-loss two-opportunity schedule ends with no actor fuel; the recipient's stock and unused isolated native packets supply no automatic continuation.

Port replacement deliberately leaves the same n cells, acquired command, archive, core and reader in place. It constructs one new idle decision port and reacquires active influence from retained cells. The existing learner copier's payload is route plus q; it does not transmit n, archive/currentness or permissions. Calling this port event learner succession would change the theorem's subject.

## 6. A finite lineage contract, when that stronger claim is pursued

Reuse the existing actual-successor results where they apply. A future evaluative lineage needs one explicit paid handoff from the reached predecessor to a genuinely distinct successor, carrying or reacquiring the full learned payload and its target meaning. The successor must meet the next block's live, funded, evidence-valid entry predicate with no reset of resources or hidden restoration of permissions. Copying a coefficient and learning it again are different permitted constructions; neither is mandatory if only continued use by the same learner is claimed.

For arbitrarily many finite generations, establish an entry invariant, declared source/component provision and valid grounds/context at the claimed horizon. From every admissible reached entry, an enabled, completing renewal block must return a successor satisfying that entry invariant, under the stated progress or scheduling contract. An invariant alone can hold in a deadlocked process. The [claims/action theorem][claims] keeps old certificates within their scope; continuous no-new-harm requires protection of the same patient in every interval. The [settlement R2][settlement] additionally needs complete current audits, each relevant concern margin, effective operational control, strategic bounds, prefix liquidity and continuing ecology. Independently justified authority remains necessary wherever the ethical application claims it; R2 does not derive rightful ownership or authority from effective control. A successful finite port replacement supplies none of those automatically. Use the theorem fitting the advertised claim rather than installing favorable constants to satisfy an unrelated sufficient bound.

The finite synthesis decision remains the [original five criteria][finish]: freeze one reference account and map each central conclusion to its produced input, independently defended premise or explicitly unresolved requirement. A central capacity may not supply itself as its own explanation. Accepted local interfaces remain closed unless evidence defeats their scoped claims. After uptake's acceptance, assess this common connection directly; additional construction is justified only by a specific missing premise or discriminating test.
