## What the anti-regress inference establishes

An encompassing description of the process can remain stable while the configuration that participates in later operations changes. The interpreter reads the currently formed connection; it need not obtain a fresh act of approval from an additional observer before that connection has causal effects. A finite witness with an acquired observation, a revised connection and a later changed invocation is enough to refute the inference that fixed laws make such revision impossible.

This rejects a **descriptive controller regress**: the claim that every operative revision must be performed by another separately choosing controller, requiring another above it indefinitely. It does not settle every possible regress of justification. Why a comparison is reliable, what its evidence concerns, and whether a response is permissible remain substantive questions about the same unfolding. Moving those questions into a larger transition table neither answers them nor makes the demonstrated connection formation disappear.

The account should therefore name the particular relation whose formation remains unexplained. “Something in the implementation is still specified” is insufficient to show that the demonstrated organization did not form. Conversely, “some organization formed” is insufficient to explain every specified relation. A law supplied to this local model is not thereby external to generative reality; its origin and adequacy remain open at the appropriate scope.

## Rivals that preserve the phenomenon

**A compiled fixed controller.** A larger fixed-state implementation can reproduce the same acquired observations, edge formation, interventions, later invocations and effects. This rival preserves the local phenomenon. It challenges a claim that generative vocabulary alone gives a unique explanation or additional predictive power; it does not refute formation of the operative connection. The current source already defines the mechanism through stable computational rules. More than vocabulary would be required to establish explanatory priority for a proposed foundation.

**A compatibility mechanism rather than improvement in general.** H3 with G1 proposed first retains G1 even though G0 is cheaper. The delayed ten-state process matches the archive and next prediction yet later diverges from the retained model. These are implemented, non-arbitrary rivals to stronger optimization and unrestricted-truth claims. They retain genuine acquired evidence and actual method revision. Conditional warrant is strongest where the declared class covers the actual reading process, observation/execution are faithful and stable at the relevant times, and the required operations remain feasible. The existing [formation mathematics](AUTONOMOUS-METHOD-FORMATION-MATH.md) states those conditions; neither a negative label nor its absence supplies them.

**Correlated self-return rather than warranted concern.** Plasticity's actual sign rule can produce inhibitory influence from further observations. A learner may therefore revise how recipient evidence influences choice without acquiring an adequate account of the recipient's adversity, outstanding claim or alternatives. This rival preserves the physically realized acquired influence. It tests the inference to concern rather than denying that acquisition occurred. The current cumulative recipient-stock abstraction still omits the recipient's own ongoing organization; that omission is not proof that such organization lies outside the generative account.

**Revision inside a rigid access protocol.** The [application reconstruction](APPLICATION-REVISION-RECONSTRUCTION.md) identifies a different limit in the very history under discussion there. At the continuing four-pair state, the help expression is already true, but closure requires six pairs. Any subsequently initiated existing service requires two additional acquisition debits first. The source's count and continuation relations cannot be revised by the available events. This is a concrete local obstruction, not a claim that all fixed laws obstruct revision. Its refunding continuation also prevents replacing gross debit with unavoidable net loss. Delivery permission and authority to continue teaching remain different relations.

## Conditions for a further explanatory advance

The next work should identify a particular consequence-to-organization relation whose source is currently supplied, rather than adding an unspecified “meta” layer. The required distinction is between an acquired effect that changes operative organization and an observer's favorable classification of that effect.

1. Fix the affected organization and phenomenon independently of the desired improvement: for example, the acquisition sequence and service-access gate of the actual continuing four-pair configuration. Preserve which participant bears which consequence and which response remains possible.
2. Show how a consequence participates in formation or alteration of that relation, through actual available activity. A detached score, a renamed state or an externally selected event word cannot stand in for that explanation.
3. Establish subsequent causal use. Retaining the proposed mediator must change later sensing, updating or action under matched relevant conditions; damaging or replacing it must have the corresponding scoped effect. Matching must preserve the relevant circumstances without erasing the mediator being tested. Graph rewiring, explicit self-labels and a new symbolic controller are not universal requirements.
4. Explain what constrains the revision and what it can get wrong. Coverage, attribution, latency, conflicting consequences, feasible alternatives and differentiated authority matter where the particular target requires them. Merely naming a change endogenous cannot make it warranted.
5. Give a condition that would require revising the account: dispatch bypasses the formed carrier, the rule reads an inaccessible verdict, the supposedly acquired relation was present at the chosen entrance, matched causal effects do not depend on the carrier, or the response only moves the burden elsewhere while its asserted target remains unchanged.

For the method witness, the sharper unresolved relation is its generic acquisition/generation/testing organization. For the application witness, it is the access protocol that keeps an outstanding response conditional on further acquisition. These are different explanatory targets. A future study may investigate either; a proposal is not evidence that their histories have already been connected. Neither requires postulating an infinite stack merely to acknowledge the finite changes already established.
