## 1. Start within the actual unfolding

Formation, activity, retention, transformation, rest and completion belong to one history. Relatively enduring lives, organizations, capacities and response positions differentiate through their relationships within it. A water channel, a practice of teaching and a capacity to participate are not independent units which later acquire a relation merely because an analyst places them in one table. Their particular histories and dependencies explain what they are and what happens when their relation changes.

An assessment nevertheless needs distinctions. Here we distinguish an actual continuation, statements identifying what occurs within it, and the alternatives supported by its actual conditions. This separation preserves the difference between occurrence, evidence and possibility; it does not install an external observer or a separate realm of value. Its omissions must remain explicit: an inventory of selected relationships is not a complete cosmological account, and the existence of an alternative is not its ethical permission or eventual implementation. Follow the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md).

The zero-axiom ambition is not achieved by avoiding the word `axiom`. The claim interpretation, admitted histories, physical correspondence, logical background and ethical orientation all remain explanatory obligations. A checked implication can make those obligations exact without discharging them. This continuation neither assumes that generation is good nor concludes that a zero-assumption foundation is impossible.

## 3. Formed compatibility can be a new realization

Consider a prepared musical practice with two genuinely different lines, L and R. The supplied apparatus and performance conditions allow both lines to occur together in one final performance. A changed arrangement still allows L alone and R alone, but its supplied operating condition prevents their co-performance. Counts of line types and separate availability remain unchanged. What has changed is their jointly realizable relation.

The comparison does not begin with two isolated owners of value. Playing, listening, timing and mutual adjustment differentiate within the practice. Where the conditions support their joint activity, that relation can itself be an actual formation: it is not merely a prediction that someone will later receive two independently finished outputs. The changed arrangement loses that relational possibility even though its marginal catalogue is unchanged.

This is a specific way to correct a description of generation. A statement that nothing was lost because each line remains possible fails to preserve the stated target. The difference can also be reversed: forming an effective relation may make co-performance possible without adding a new marginal line. Thus the generative content can concern **a newly possible relationship among differentiated formations**, not just more objects, state labels or outputs.

The example's reach is bounded. No real instrument or community has been measured here. If actual mixing, scheduling or reconstruction can restore both lines within the same relevant performance, the alleged incompatibility must be revised. The single performance fixes the comparison; later separate performances are a different continuation unless the proposed target permits that substitution. Two abstract claim labels do not represent every ethical aspect of music or social life.

The formal comparison similarly preserves only what its claims mean. For each old continuation, one new continuation must realize the relevant old claims together. It does not require one new history to realize all mutually exclusive old alternatives. Nor does it automatically preserve order, simultaneity, duration, causal dependence or the identity of a living bearer: those relations must enter the interpretation when the comparison concerns them. “Both occurred at some time” is weaker than “both continued together through this interval.”

## 4. Why preserving every truth is not the required direction

One tempting repair is to require preservation of every fact of the whole. That proposal needs a distinction between **faithful description** and **an unchanged truth profile at matched comparison positions**.

Suppose a fixed comparison language contains a claim and its contradictory negation, interpreted at the same matched role. Preserving every claim true in the first profile includes its true negative claims. A newly true claim at that same role then conflicts with preservation of its earlier negation. The formal companion checks the exact consequence for such a negation-complete language. This is a limitation on a particular proposed strict-extension criterion; it is not a theorem that genuine development is impossible.

In an actual growing history, “the capacity was absent at the earlier stage” can remain true while “the capacity is present at the later stage” becomes true. Different temporal positions make these compatible. Faithfully preserving the past does not require freezing the present. Likewise, replacing a carrier while continuing an activity preserves one relation while changing another. The comparison must say which continuity it is claiming.

This matters for zero-axiom grounding. Choosing claims such as actual formation, continuing efficacy, joint activity and new participation can be warranted by the descriptive target of generativity. Such a choice need not itself smuggle in a moral verdict. But the choice is still a scoped articulation, and its favorable orientation is not obtained merely by calling the claims positive. A destructive organization also has actual formative relations. A complete account must represent its effects and explain their ethical character.

Accordingly, two obligations remain distinct within one inquiry: retain a truthful account of relevant change, including lost and newly true relationships; justify which transformations count favorably, adversely or in opposed ways. Making the first account logically complete would not silently complete the second.

## 5. The strongest affirmative ethical hypothesis

The positive proposal is that the formation of supported compatibility among differentiated lives and activities can itself constitute richer 生. It need not borrow all its meaning from a later output or return to a particular initiator. In the musical example, the shared realization is a way the unfolding has become organized, and understanding or participation may themselves be part of that realized relation. In ecological or broader organizational applications, the relevant relations require their own correspondence; the musical interpretation cannot simply be transferred by vocabulary.

This sharpens the candidate ethical identification: **a continuation can be favorable because of the differentiated life and formative relations it actually realizes together**. It is more specific than praising abstract possibility count. It permits a relation to have significance even when the list of separately reachable outcomes has not increased. It also explains why ending an obstructive arrangement can participate in generation: the relevant subject is the changed whole continuation, with its real losses, rather than indefinite survival of the old arrangement.

That explanation supplies content to investigate, but it does not yet establish the favorable identification from zero assumptions. The [inherited content-sensitive rival](WHOLE-GENERATIVE-AUTHORITY-COUNTERREVIEW.md#2-strongest-matched-rival-and-the-precise-discrimination) can accept the newly formed joint relation and value this concrete performance, while denying that co-realizability as such supplies a universally favorable ground. It need not erase interconnection, treat values as external properties or reject all generative developments. Where both explanations judge the same activity favorably, their agreement supports that judgment only to the extent their respective grounds are warranted; it does not select one as uniquely necessary.

The sharper question is therefore not whether jointness exists. It is **whether actual compatibility, with the concrete differentiated lives and consequences retained, has the claimed favorable significance across its stated domain, and whether rival explanations reveal a substantive misidentification or another articulation of that significance**. A mechanism alone does not answer it. Nor does a favorable cultural example establish the same answer for coercive coordination, ecological replacement or abiotic organization. Universal normative validity need not require agreement with this particular theoretical vocabulary or exclusive explanatory necessity. The richer descriptive comparison makes this test better posed without declaring it passed.

## 6. A mixed comparison that keeps the old relationship real

Consider a formed garden and teaching practice sharing a water channel. One feasible continuation keeps the present cultivation and intergenerational teaching relation through its finite season. Another actually repurposes the channel into a water-recovery arrangement, ending that present joint practice while establishing new ecological and teaching formations elsewhere. Both histories include construction, transition, upkeep, actual participants and changed access. Neither is stipulated to avert imminent catastrophe. The new site does not automatically replace the old place's particular relationship.

The generative hypothesis has a positive case for the second continuation: a transformed common condition now supports further differentiated formations together. The first continuation has positive content too: a living practice is being realized and can reach its finite completion. These are connected effects of the same change, not independent value tokens whose weights have already been fixed.

The comparison reveals two things which a complete theory must explain. First, new joint formation can be real without making the old loss unreal. Second, completing a finite relation can be real without making the new formation irrelevant. Calling the replacement more productive or the old practice more authentic does not settle the comparison. Nor does a larger count of compatible claim bundles establish that the lost relation is replaceable: finer vocabulary can change the count without changing the history.

A third proposed continuation—gradual replacement after completion—must be tested as an actual route with compatible timing, support and consequences. It cannot be added because it would remove a philosophical difficulty. If it is actually available, it changes the comparison; if not, the two opposed realizations remain. The same applies to restoration, relocation and release from an undertaking. A feasible technical handoff is evidence about continuity, not independent proof that the whole response is ethically justified.

A loss of joint realization is consequently a **descriptive loss certificate within the stated interpretation**, not a universal veto. A zero-axiom theory cannot turn the preservation criterion into an unannounced command to preserve every old activity. The desired full account must earn a response to the actual conflict: priority, permissible plurality, constraint, justified completion or a different resolution supported by the situation. Present incompleteness does not establish any one of these verdicts.

## 7. From a warranted realization to differentiated response

The [general continuation study](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md) already connects a warranted continuation to its actual necessary relations, while distinguishing contributory reason, requirement and particular allocation. This conditional achievement remains credited.

The new comparison improves its application. If a claimed response would preserve all marginal activities but destroy a joint relationship essential to the continuation at issue, it has not yet established adequate realization of that continuation. Conversely, an effective new shared condition can be part of what the response realizes, rather than an independently valued instrument detached from the result. These are corrections to the content of the proposed response and its ground.

Whether that realization has favorable significance remains the first-warrant question; whether the significance addresses a particular intelligence remains the practical-scope question. Actual capacity to affect a continuation can identify a response position, but causal involvement alone does not establish responsibility, authority to compel, or indefinite service. Existing commitments and release conditions remain part of the actual relationship. Learning can discover an omitted dependency; self-modification can change a capacity or purpose. Neither automatically certifies an improvement in ethical judgment.

There is therefore no fixed ethical polarity attached to the channel, the garden, the replacement or the agent. The same kind of relation can support a living continuation under one configuration and obstruct it under another. This situated dependence does not make judgment arbitrary; it identifies the relationships an adequate judgment must explain. Its universal validity would lie in a justified common account of those relationships and their differentiated significance, not in one command issued everywhere.

## 8. Result and the next test

The present extension makes the proposed whole direction more precise in three connected ways: relational generation can change while marginal outcomes remain fixed; preserving the relevant co-realization requires one compatible continuation rather than assembled separate witnesses; and an account of genuine change cannot equate preservation with an unchanged complete signed truth profile. These are conceptual advances tied to the companion's checked mathematical scope. In particular, its negation result excludes an explicit new-true/old-false witness; it does not use double-negation elimination to establish a stronger unrestricted truth-reflection theorem. They do not supply a new ethical axiom or prove the first favorable orientation.

The universal grounding investigation should now compare the **realized joint relation and its differentiated life**, rather than a tally of possibilities, under a conflict such as the garden transition. Its discriminating question is whether the same independently described configuration warrants a determinate priority through its generative relations, or whether a credible content-sensitive account explains the opposing realization better. The criterion must be fixed before the verdict; the old loss must remain even if replacement is justified. A resolution that merely declares the preferred bundle “more generative” would repeat the missing inference.
