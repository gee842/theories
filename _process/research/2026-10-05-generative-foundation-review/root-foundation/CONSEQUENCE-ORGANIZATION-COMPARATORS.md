# Activity changing the conditions of later plasticity

6 October 2026. AI-authored focused empirical comparison under the updated [NONDUAL-METHOD](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The named connection is **earlier activity → retained change in the conditions of subsequent plasticity → different subsequent plasticity**. This is evidence review, not a new experiment, a new learning theory, or a claim that ethical significance has been derived.

## Why this comparison advances the current question

The [participation comparison](PARTICIPATION-CONSEQUENCE-COMPARATORS.md) examines material history affecting later coupling, while preserving a published challenge to the stronger memory interpretation. The existing [learning identification analysis](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/UPDATER-IDENTIFICATION-MATH.md) already distinguishes changed behavior, a fitted learning parameter, and a causally effective retained organization. Its [empirical application](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/COST-SENSITIVE-LEARNING.md) leaves the third claim underidentified. The new comparator supplies a more direct physiological intervention at that interface. It does not replace the earlier study or retrospectively validate its mechanism.

The primary object is a continuing process in which previous activity alters what later activity can do. Calling one interval “priming” and another “conditioning” distinguishes their tested roles without making memory and learning fundamentally separate entities. Likewise, an experimentally isolated tissue remains dependent on its history and supporting conditions. There is no need to introduce payment, a separately owned register or an independently given evaluator as a foundation.

## Primary evidence and internal counterevidence

**Hulme, Jones, Ireland and Abraham (2012).** [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0634-12.2012); [preserved source and reading scope](../../../../sources/39-consequence-organization-2026-10-06/README.md).

Using acute rat hippocampal slices, Hulme and colleagues primed one pathway and subsequently conditioned another, with pathway independence tested electrophysiologically. The second pathway showed reduced potentiation or enhanced depression under matched conditioning protocols. The extracellular procedure separated priming and conditioning by thirty minutes. Hyperpolarization preventing somatic firing did not remove the effect; directly imposed firing instead facilitated potentiation. Disrupting calcium stores or blocking muscarinic signaling during priming, followed by washout, prevented the reported priming effect. Receptor-current and inhibition controls constrained other explanations. Propagation across dendritic compartments depended on direction. The retained expression mechanism and responsible cell types remained unresolved. These are small slice/cell experiments, not animal learning trials. Pharmacological null contrasts do not establish exact equality or uniquely localized mediation. Figure 6D's caption and body disagree on priming compartment; this review does not rely on that orientation. Full methods, results and discussion were read; Figures 4 and 6 were visually inspected. No raw data were reanalyzed.

## What can be inferred, and what cannot

The following are our methodological assessment and proposed distinctions, not further experimental findings.

**A retained change in plasticity propensity is stronger than a fitted behavioral gain.** The relevant contrast concerns what a later induction procedure produces after different preceding activity. It can support a history-dependent plasticity relation without identifying a unique molecular implementation. It does not require that the underlying physical laws change. A fixed law over a richer physiological state can describe the very organization through which earlier activity modifies later acquisition.

**The activity variable must survive causal scrutiny.** A generic index such as total “activity,” “success” or “experience” can merge interventions with different consequences. An account that treats those interventions as interchangeable loses the explanatory target. A failed trigger hypothesis should therefore revise where and how the relevant history is integrated; it does not erase every form of retention. Conversely, preserving a flexible label for history while changing its content after each failed test would make the explanation uninformative.

**Induction, persistence and expression are different causal questions within one process.** Interfering while a state is being induced can show that a pathway contributes to reaching it. That alone cannot establish that the same pathway stores the change, maintains it throughout the interval, or reads it during later acquisition. A fully located organization claim must not borrow these further roles from an induction result. This is an unresolved mechanism within the reconstruction, not evidence that causal organization has to be a detached controller.

**No task reward is needed for the selected contrast, but adaptive significance remains a further claim.** An investigator can measure altered acquisition without rewarding the tissue for matching a desired output. The measurement therefore does not secretly install a scalar task reward as the mechanism under explanation. It also does not establish why this form of change was selected, whether it improves the organism's continuation, or whether reducing rather than increasing plasticity is beneficial in the relevant setting. Improved experimental performance and justified concern for affected beings are still different claims; neither is contained in an electrical response measure.

## A test that would change the explanation

A useful next discriminator would intervene **after the earlier induction but before the common later challenge**, with an independently motivated candidate retained pathway. Compare a reversible disruption, a sham and an appropriate rescue while measuring the relevant baseline transmission, delivered challenge and general capacity to respond. Preserve nonresponse or damaged preparations as outcomes rather than selecting only successful recordings. The point is to distinguish an induction trigger from an operative retained contributor, not to require a new instrument for every explanatory term.

If disrupting the candidate after induction selectively removes the history-dependent acquisition difference, and rescue restores it without merely changing the challenge or disabling plasticity generally, the case for its continuing causal role strengthens. If the difference instead follows altered input delivery or a direct readout effect, the account must relocate the relevant organization. A broad insult that abolishes all plasticity cannot decide between those explanations. A null result with limited precision cannot establish that no retained organization exists.

For the stronger bridge from consequences to **beneficial revision of learning**, the prior events would also have to differ in an independently assessable consequence for continued functioning, and the changed plasticity would need to affect that continuation. This is the next substantive relation to explain, not a new universal completion gate. The present comparator advances activity-dependent modification of later acquisition; it does not by itself complete consequence-sensitive selection over learning processes, natural learning-to-learn, or ethical grounding.
