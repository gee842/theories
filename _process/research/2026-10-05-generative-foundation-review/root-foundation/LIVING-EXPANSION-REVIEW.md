# Expanding joint provision from retained organization: review

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored independent review for [living dependencies](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/LIVING-DEPENDENCIES.md), following the [transition review](LIVING-TRANSITION-REVIEW.md). It applies the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md): compartments and their shared provision are differentiated within the same history. This review does not independently reread the primary papers or rerun Lean. Final source and receipt inspection is recorded below.

**Candidate advance:** distinguish the repeatedly reusable support of past activity from the provision required by currently coexisting living activity. If independently justified disjoint requirements exceed inherited availability, explain the actual intervening increase without defining living organization as whatever must constantly replace itself.

## 1. The inference worth making

The candidate concerns actual joint provision, not an accumulation of descendant labels. A participant can act repeatedly, pass to a successor or be redistributed. Counting each past operation or birth as another permanently consumed copy would manufacture a shortage. The target instead specifies a reached collection of relevant centers, their variable requirements and an actual nonduplicating assignment of available participants.

Nonduplication must be warranted for the selected relation. Some enabling conditions are shared; a common transport route, signaling condition or other provision cannot simply be given one exclusive copy per named center. The partition argument only charges what the described activities really require separately. This does not assume that the centers are otherwise autonomous or independent.

The proposed general connection has two parts: justified current allocations constrain the current inventory; the actual reaction history explains when that inventory increased beyond its inherited value. A successful witness of an earlier increase is weaker than molecular genealogy or proof that the particular newly formed copies supply every current activity. New effective copies can arise by activation, assembly, redistribution into the counted scope or import. They are not newly created matter, and additional cells or copies are not necessarily novel diversity.

An excess in a finite reached state is already informative. The argument need not claim that a real population grows infinitely or that a universal physical clock determines its threshold. Conversely, indefinite succession with bounded joint requirements does not satisfy an expanding-demand premise merely because its number of historical births increases.

## 2. Strong scope-preserving alternatives

| Alternative or distinction | What the account must retain |
|---|---|
| Serial use and succession | A stable-size lineage may reuse or transmit provision. Historical birth count cannot replace current joint requirements. |
| Shared enabling relationships | Exclusive assignment is justified for selected participants, not imposed on every condition of living activity. |
| Greater inherited stock or different arrangements | Multiple inherited copies, unequal partition and alternate functional organizations can support more activity before a particular shortage. A fixed universal one-copy rule is not inferred. |
| Alternative provision | Import, complementary activity, activation and reconstruction can supply the relevant function where actually available. A selected molecular species is indispensable only within a justified application scope. |
| Changed target | Division, replication, areal elongation, biomass accumulation and viable retained function can diverge. Failure of one does not prove failure of all. |
| Dormancy and nonexpansion | Retention can suffice without growing joint demand. These remain legitimate living modes, rather than being excluded to rescue a universal expansion claim. |

These alternatives do not refute an independently established shortage. They delimit which shortage has actually been shown. The question is whether a real alternative supplies the specified activity under the compared conditions; merely imagining an unspecified alternative does not defeat ordinary causal evidence.

## 5. Full-goal coverage and completion standards

The four [stable requirements](LIVING-TRANSITION-REVIEW.md#1-what-counts-as-progress-toward-the-whole-goal) remain: independently identify continuation, establish actual insufficiency where necessity is claimed, connect an effective response to subsequent function, and warrant the scope claimed across stages and scales. They require a general argument with adequate real-system correspondence. They do not require a separate theorem for every organism, exhaustive empirical data, full kinetic calibration or exact molecular pathwise refinement of ordinary experiments.

The emerging account has three complementary applications: maintained activity under actual compensating demand; retained or dormant organization and its functional recovery; and expanding provision for jointly continuing activity. The present argument can strengthen the third application without replacing the whole objective with expanding populations. Evidence for one need not impose its particular clock, threshold or endpoint on another.

After the formal and empirical review, the next decision should be an integrated completion assessment of this common account. Additional work should follow a remaining substantive explanatory gap, not the mere possibility of making measurements or models finer. Calibration can improve quantitative prediction without being a prerequisite for every warranted qualitative dependency claim. An unqualified claim that all living continuation necessarily reconstructs in every interval would still be unsupported and would contradict the account's retained-provision scope.

Universal eventual insufficiency for every possible living continuation is a stronger recurrence claim, not an extra completion gate silently added to Ren's explicit living-dependencies direction. Completing a general account of actual formed dependencies, their retained efficacy and the conditions requiring an adequate response would not complete that stronger claim or the unified foundation. The completion decision should identify exactly which account has been established, rather than treating optional finer correspondence as an indefinitely moving endpoint.
