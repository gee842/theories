## 1. The substantive clarification

The [specified performance](SITUATED-ETHICAL-DISCERNMENT.md#continuation-test-changing-a-successful-pursuit) realizes a worthwhile finite achievement while seriously obstructing ordinary living. Joint cancellation permits supported recovery but loses that achievement. These are constructed conditions, including the absence of an equally grave shifted burden or outstanding third-party performance duty. They are not empirical findings, and they do not by themselves decide the ethical comparison.

The positive account locates the reason for recovery in the actual frustrated living and in the response that would change it. Recovery need not produce another performance or benefit an independent owner to have significance within this whole. The finality rival accepts the adverse aspect but argues that a warranted finite undertaking can justify bearing it. Its challenge therefore survives accurate representation of every consequence. Whole inclusion is necessary for this comparison's fidelity; it does not settle the comparison's ethical content.

The adopted clarification distinguishes changes previously liable to be grouped as one kind of resolution:

| What changes | Consequence to investigate |
|---|---|
| Actual condition or fulfillment | Rest, relief or fulfillment can end the ground of a particular prospective demand, without erasing past injury. |
| Ethical meaning within the relationship | A genuinely changed realization can alter its appraisal; endorsement alone does not establish the change. |
| Countervailing justification | A burden can remain adverse while bearing it is warranted overall. |
| Scope of inquiry or decision | A justified endpoint or jurisdiction can restrict further assessment or this decision's permissible basis; no automatic duty elsewhere follows. |
| Correctness of the former appraisal | Inquiry can show that the alleged adversity, reason or scope was misidentified, even when the described condition remains unchanged. |

These are overlapping philosophical distinctions, not an exhaustive partition or decision procedure. Removing a complaint from a record alone establishes none of them. Conversely, unchanged descriptive conditions do not establish that the former appraisal was true. This last correction is essential: a preservation rule must not turn an incumbent ethical judgment into an infallible premise.

Consent, demanding finite achievement, costly review, shifted burdens, nonreflective affected lives and self-good without productive return survive the review. The bounded recovery preference remains a substantive, defeasible judgment. No general duty to cancel, reopen, maximize options or prolong inquiry has been derived.

## 3. The universal sufficiency audit

The universal question concerns any intelligence with an independently specified actual opportunity to respond. It does not require all intelligences to have identical access or duties. Affected nonintelligent living remains within the ethical question. Capability, access and permitted means must not be defined as already owing the desired response.

The strongest candidate says that the actual relationship between this response and a favorable whole realization supplies some practical favor before adopted concern or assent. The strongest rival grants that favorable realization and permitted effective response, yet proposes that a formed responsibility is constitutive of a reason addressed to this intelligence. Both accounts can describe roles as differentiated within one unfolding. Calling the rival dualist does not answer it.

Its proposed rationale—protecting the authority to form and pursue undertakings—can explain special duties, permission to decline and restrictions on powers. It does not yet explain why **every baseline reason** disappears in a stipulated case without competing costs or interference. The candidate's reply is substantial: the beneficial response already explains why adopting concern could be worthwhile. But that reply still defends, rather than demonstrates the necessity of, the response–realization relationship's normative sufficiency.

The final audit found this to be the same frontier already preserved in [the constitutive-answer argument](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md), [its integration](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-INTEGRATION.md) and [counterreview](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-COUNTERREVIEW.md). The present practical bridge also inherits [§34](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#34-self-good-practical-reasons-and-the-remaining-ground). Removing costs, prior assent or role commitments sharpens the inherited case without supplying a new inference. Describing access as jointly constituted does not itself establish that it suffices. Once option-specific favor is established, demanding another authorization for that same favor duplicates the first question; a rival withholding that favor has not conceded its premise.

The meanings must remain stable. If “favorable whole quality” already means “a reason for this intelligence's response,” the proposed inference contains its conclusion. If it identifies the realization's ethical character independently, the practical sufficiency question remains substantive. Likewise, the rival cannot define responsibility as whatever has a reason and then use absent responsibility to explain absent reasons.
