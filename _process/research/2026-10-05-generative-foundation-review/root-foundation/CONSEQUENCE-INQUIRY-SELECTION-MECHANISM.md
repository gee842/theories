## 6. A positive relevance result for the existing inquiry priority

The subsequent additive [InquiryRelevance proof](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/remaining-dependency/formal/InquiryRelevance.lean) addresses a narrower positive question left explicit above. Let `r` be the physical relay setting actually used, and let `f` be its faithfully acquired full response. All four weak-response tables `W` remain possible within the calibration posterior. The question is whether the current weak response agrees with this acquired full response at `r`: `W(r) = f`.

A proposed one-coordinate weak query at `q` is sufficient precisely if any two weak tables producing the same answer at `q` agree on the truth of that local question. The theorem proves

\[
\bigl[\forall W,V,\ W(q)=V(q)\Rightarrow
  (W(r)=f\ \Leftrightarrow\ V(r)=f)\bigr]
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q=r.
\]

At `q = r`, the acquired bit directly settles agreement. If `q ≠ r`, take `W` to equal `f` at both settings and change only coordinate `r` in `V` to its complement. The two tables give the same off-target answer but opposite answers to the local question. This is a counterexample within the stated complete family, not a freely toggled predicate outside it. The proof also characterizes the full posterior as exactly the weak/full pairs with full response `f` at `r`: it contains eight pairs, and either weak query with either answer leaves four. Equal unweighted candidate counts therefore do not establish equal adequacy for this question. No distribution or entropy-optimality claim is needed.

Here `q` is the **physical test setting**, not the observation-route selector. The accepted contact-zero correction keeps physical setting 1 and chooses contact route 0. The relevance theorem does not select that route or establish its reliability. Its full outcome is historical and its weak observation is current; stationarity is additionally needed when a discrepancy is interpreted as refuting an earlier weak report.

This gives a conditional epistemic justification for the existing used-setting priority, instead of treating it as justified solely by a generic disagreement scan. The runtime still has that priority installed; its acquisition or formation has not been newly derived. Sufficiency here presumes one faithful coordinate observation, the stated candidate family and no additional side information. It establishes neither that inquiry must occur, nor physical affordability, global optimality, unique fault diagnosis or ethical worth.
