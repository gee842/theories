# Whole-process grounding: inherited resources and the new claim

6 October 2026. AI-authored source and scope map under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). This is a repository reconstruction, with no new external research, experiment or theorem. **The new target concerns ethical significance within the whole affected unfolding, without making already warranted private stakes its required starting point.**

## 1. What the correction already establishes

[Ren's direct correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-goodness-without-return-to-self.md) rejects return to an initiating self as the foundation's measure. The [goodness-without-return integration](GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md) already restores this direction, crediting the earlier relational-goodness work. It distinguishes transmission from return: a later benefit can remain when the initiator no longer controls the response. Actual handoff, changed needs and usable correction matter; preserving a routine or lineage does not certify its goodness.

That integration also already grants actual care and onward care while asking whether their arrangement displaces a third person's protection. Repeating that comparison is not new evidence. Its unfinished philosophical use is to explain why the quality of the affected unfolding, including these relations, has ethical significance before routing everything through an individual's prior reasons.

“Whole” must preserve the lives, dependencies, experiences, powers and burdens differentiating within it. It does not designate a larger owner, one cosmic experiencer, a sum of private payoffs, or an institution entitled to sacrifice its parts for persistence.

## 2. Three constructive resources

**Joint continuation.** The [health answer's joint-feasibility section](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/ANSWER.md#shared-health-requires-joint-feasibility-and-standing) rejects combining incompatible individual certificates. [Formal core F2b](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-CORE.md#f2b-joint-sustainable-capacity-and-a-failure-deadline) makes this precise: under its fixed convexly implementable activity law, indefinite protection is possible exactly when one common activity meets all protected losses and resource constraints. When none does, a joint shortage yields a finite deadline. Its separation multipliers are expressly not ethical weights. This supports examining one executable configuration rather than adding separately imagined successes; it does not validate the protected floors.

**Consequential revision within the configuration.** [Formal core F9](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-CORE.md#f9-the-joint-installed-process-including-its-own-revision) certifies the actual installed process, including changing evaluators and rules. Covered transitions must preserve a common invariant and relevant recurring events, with shared resources and permissions compatible. An unused revision variable or a different available good controller is insufficient. The theorem does not prove the legitimacy of the specification. It supplies a precise interface for the whole-process proposal without making every ethical good recurrent or every mechanism part of one implementation.

**Comparison without a universal sum.** [Revisable concern §4](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md#4-acting-before-every-disagreement-is-resolved) derives a strict partial order from dominance across grounded accounts. It preserves a nonempty undominated remainder without a common cardinal scale. The accounts and the normative use of dominance remain warranted premises; unresolved choices require further grounds. The [scoring review](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/succession/SCORING-SCOPE-REVIEW.md) separately explains why crossing task profiles defeat a fixed arrangement-only score, not every universal relational criterion. Whole-process assessment can be context-sensitive and incomplete without becoming arbitrary.

## 3. Counterregimes the candidate must explain

The [health counterexamples](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/COUNTEREXAMPLES.md#adaptive-tyranny-is-not-a-marginal-exception) compare indefinitely renewed capacities `(2,0)` and `(1,1)`. Equal totals conceal failure of a declared floor for one party. A globally available reform may also be unavailable to that party because another controls authorization. More options or self-revising machinery alone cannot resolve either difference; the example itself does not derive equal floors from physics.

The [ethical foundation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md#counterexamples-that-the-unified-theory-must-retain) preserves the dispensable victim, regenerative predator and destructive rescue. An organization can learn and renew while maintaining avoidable suffering; ending its activity can protect affected life. Its explicit standing premise must not be silently imported as a derived result in the new inquiry. These counterexamples defeat renewal-only sufficiency, not every richer constitutive account of generative reality.

## 4. The precise new contribution to seek

The promising candidate is that ethical quality concerns **how an unfolding realizes, obstructs and transforms differentiated lived possibilities together**, including their continuing relations. Repair can change that whole configuration without returning value to its initiator. Actual suffering and fulfillment occur within it; comparison need not begin with independent owners and then aggregate their interests.

The new work must defend why those relations constitute ethical improvement. Descriptively including everyone does not yet establish their significance; guaranteeing a favorable certificate assumes its criterion. A strong rival can preserve the complete differentiated history while denying that joint continuation or removal of an obstruction exhausts ethical assessment. Competing goods, legitimate restrictions and ending a life or practice cannot be settled by “more generation.”

The [accepted conditional foundation](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate), including bounded concern and wisdom, remains complete at its scope. This inquiry develops the stronger ethical constitution; it adds no mandatory controller or universal aggregation requirement.
