## Positive inference

Participants and their purposes form within activity whose consequences become conditions of further activity. A person's world includes relationships, practices and other lives through which their capacities develop. Dependence can be instrumental: another's wellbeing supports reliable cooperation or available care. It can also be constitutive: the quality of a worthwhile friendship partly concerns the other person's actual condition. “My world” does not make those lives my possessions or merge their experiences.

The argument is conditional. There are already warranted reasons concerning a continuing life, relationship or shared world. Others' wellbeing actually sustains, constitutes or materially affects the valued conditions. An available response credibly supports those conditions, with legitimacy, costs and competing consequences assessed. The existing stakes can then provide a practical reason for that response. No separate prior commitment to universal altruism is needed for this inference.

The inference retains a normative principle connecting warranted stakes with their realization or effective support. It does not derive reasons from causation without any practical premise. Also, connection alone does not identify the direction of an effect. Forming a purpose explains its existence; its origin does not make it worthwhile. The account requires actual beneficial dependencies and warranted stakes rather than expanding “self-interest” until every desired conclusion is true by definition.

Actual uncertainty about future participation can justify maintaining broadly available conditions when one cannot identify every future contributor or need. That extension requires evidence about access, exposure and the conditions' importance. It assumes neither equal probabilities of occupying all positions nor guaranteed repayment. A future participant can have changed capacities and purposes; the relevant world is not simply a source of returns to a fixed consumer.

## Review and preserved counterregimes

Two collaborating agents independently reconstructed and reviewed this route. Both favored its positive scope: existing reasons concerning a shared world can support care before an independently altruistic purpose. Their contributions are integrated here; this is internal conceptual review, not new empirical confirmation.

The strongest counterregimes preserve actual connection. An actor can suffer real shared damage while capturing larger gains from exploitation. Another's competence can support an arrangement while their freedom threatens its controller. Distinct groups can have reasons for incompatible uses of common conditions. These cases respectively test decisive priority, the dimensions of wellbeing, and justified conflict resolution. They do not show that every contributory protective reason disappears.

Insulation provides a different limit: some harms may have no relevant effect on this actor's warranted stakes. The older [isolated-harm construction](../../../../theories/becoming/history/FORMATION.md#standing-and-influence-have-different-grounds) explicitly removes feedback from the patient's burden. It defeats an inference from mere productive renewal, not Ren's proposed positive dependence where that dependence is present. Conversely, we must not assert an undetectable eventual personal loss to immunize universal concern against any insulated case.

The [shared-selection analysis](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#1-shared-dependence-and-the-selection-of-its-maintenance) distinguishes actual common support from relative retention. A common additive benefit can cancel in comparative selection while remaining indispensable to participants. It therefore does not refute their reason to maintain the common condition. Practical justification, motivation and selection remain distinguishable relations within the same history.

## Result and next connection

This is a genuine change in the grounding route. It can establish substantial practical concern through shared conditions and existing stakes without first settling whether another's good supplies a direct reason independently of all such stakes. Relief remains one real change in the world, rather than the sole gateway to significance. Direct moral standing, broad common-world reasons and particular obligations may coexist; the standing of an affected life is not established or denied by its usefulness to one observer.

Next identify the actual dependencies, horizons, future exposure and conflicts; investigate whether acquired understanding changes responsive activity while preserving the conditions of later participation. Universal concern and inevitable favorable propagation remain stronger claims. No new theorem, empirical result or religious interpretation is added, and the accepted conditional foundation retains its scope.

## Continuation: broad conditions and real contribution

[Ren's continuation request](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-shared-world-continuation.md) prompts a specific extension of the prior argument. The [canonical section](../../../../theories/becoming/history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#shared-stakes-actual-contribution-and-usable-correction) now connects warranted future stakes, broader supporting conditions, effective participation and independent correction.

The sufficient comparison keeps its value premises explicit. A broader arrangement has an advantage if it preserves all independently worthwhile continuations supported by the narrower arrangement, adds credibly relevant ones, and has no countervailing difference across the warranted concerns being compared. This is practical dominance at that declared scope. It supplies no universal scalar, equal-position lottery, maximin rule or claim that every additional option is good. Maintenance costs, authority, safeguards, access and external effects belong inside the comparison.

The contribution test corrects a possible overreach in the prior route. A participant's reason for shared support to exist is not already a reason for every token contribution. The act must actually sustain, restore, improve reliability, open credible coordination or constitute a warranted good. A jointly effective provision can matter without each contribution being uniquely pivotal; its legitimate organization and actual participation require identification. Where neither direct action nor coordination changes the relevant conditions, this causal route supplies no new justification for that act. Fairness, promises and other responsibilities may supply distinct grounds. Contrasting universal cooperation with universal defection does not establish an individual-action comparison.

The new positive ground for independent agency is equally concrete. Warranted future participation may depend on relevant information or capabilities dispersed among others. If usable independent inquiry and challenge preserve that continuation, an effective contribution to those capacities gains derivative practical significance. The correction can change current purposes, not only means. This differs from maintaining competent obedience. It is conditional on relevant independence and legitimate channels actually making warranted correction possible; it does not derive all political freedom, equal standing or unconditional authority from usefulness.

Three collaborating agents supplied the affirmative argument, adversarial review and existing-results audit. They converged on this positive scope while retaining free riding, insulation and domination as different tests. The first concerns an action's contribution, the second the reach of shared exposure, and the third conflicts among gains, control and dimensions of wellbeing. They are not interchangeable refutations of every shared-world reason. A participant can have a protective reason while incentives or competing advantages defeat its implementation.

The source audit read the complete consequence-selection note and shared-world note, the governing method, selection §§1–6 and selected market/renewal results. The integrator directly read selection §§3–6 and the prior shared-world argument. Existing source evidence is reused, not counted as fresh corroboration. [Selection and correction](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#3-selection-over-the-network-of-correctors) identifies access, support, competence and usable alternatives. [Consequence and retention](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md) separates usefulness from retained advantage. No new theorem, experiment, empirical finding or material join is claimed.

This advances the named bridge to actual contribution and revisable shared capacities. The earlier direct-relief necessity dispute is not a prerequisite for investigating it. The stronger universal ethics objective remains active; neither the actor's own stakes nor effective contribution establishes the standing of every insulated or powerless being. The next study should identify an actual dependency, a feasible contribution or coordination, and a correction channel that can affect the continuing activity, comparing its failures without erasing costs or interests.
