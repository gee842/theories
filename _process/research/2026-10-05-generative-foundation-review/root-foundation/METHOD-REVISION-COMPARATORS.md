# Consequence-triggered revision of a mathematical method

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting comparator note. Named connection: acquired operational consequence → changed retained method selector → later enacted candidate-generation or evidence-retention procedure, within a continuing carrier. This follows [META-REVISION](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/META-REVISION.md). It is a supporting mathematical-learning extension. The [fixed-finish-line assessment, §15](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) already judges the combined foundation adequate as a conditional candidate under the original criteria; this study does not reopen that decision or silently add a new mandatory completion condition.

## What the existing results already establish

[Learning-route revision](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/LEARNING-ROUTE-REVISION.md) physically replaces an inherited updating route after acquired diagnosis, preserves the existing record, and exposes the replacement to subsequent consequences. Its repertoire, diagnostic trigger, proposal law and funding remain supplied. Later differential copying is a further causal effect; it is distinct from a carrier learning which replacement is best.

[Updater formation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UPDATER-FORMATION.md) forms an updater, lets it govern actual reconstruction, replaces its kind, and lets that replacement govern another reconstruction and receive support. Consequences affect renewal and turnover, while the replacement chemistry remains fixed. Its conventional hierarchical realization has the same physical transition kernel. This is evidence for the conditional mechanism, not priority over ordinary adaptive control.

The new math bridge should therefore identify a concrete additional connection: the actual acquired model or evidence is used by an altered procedure, and its future operation differs because a consequence changed the retained method state. Another isolated survival result would not establish that within-carrier connection. Conversely, a fixed supplied trigger can genuinely cause method revision; its being supplied limits the origin claim rather than making the operative change unreal.

## Two close primary comparators

**Switching among identification/control procedures.** Narendra and Balakrishnan use multiple adaptive identification models, each paired with a controller, for a specified linear time-invariant plant class. Their models differ in initial parameter estimates. The proposed supervisor selects a controller using an index combining instantaneous and accumulated identification error; the index weights are design parameters, and successive switches are separated by a positive dwell time. The paper specifies known plant order, relative degree and gain sign, together with a minimum-phase assumption. This is a direct antecedent for an observed consequence changing which procedure operates. It supplies the controller family, supervision rule and reference-tracking objective; it does not derive unrestricted method invention or a physical cost account. Its stability result must not be transferred to arbitrary switches, arbitrary plant classes, or the repository's finite-resource mechanism. [Narendra and Balakrishnan, *Improving Transient Response of Adaptive Control Systems Using Multiple Models and Switching* (1994), author-uploaded full text, §§I–II and V](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/3021995_Improving_transient_response_of_adaptive_control_systems_using_multiple_models_and_switching).

**Changing retained evidence and triggering model rebuilding.** Bifet and Gavaldà's ADWIN adjusts a data window by testing whether its subwindows have sufficiently different means and discarding older data after detected change. The paper distinguishes using this detector outside a learner to trigger rebuilding from using it inside to update retained statistics. The formal setting assumes independently generated bounded observations and supplies a confidence parameter and testing rule. The revised window is acquired from data; the rule for revising it remains designed. This is a close comparator for a consequence-sensitive retention procedure without a known change timescale. Its statistical guarantee does not automatically apply to a feedback-coupled error stream, and discarding evidence for current prediction does not preserve every historical question. [Bifet and Gavaldà, *Learning from Time-Changing Data with Adaptive Windowing*, author version dated 17 October 2006, §§1 and 3; subsequently SDM 2007](https://www.cs.upc.edu/~gavalda/papers/adwin06.pdf).

Both cited texts were inspected beyond their abstracts. These references establish antecedents and interface requirements. They are not external validation of this repository's material implementation. No biological or human method-revision observation is asserted here, and no new general selection algorithm is claimed.

## Four claims that must remain separate

| Claim | Required observation or construction | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| A supplied trigger responds to acquired evidence | Changing the accessible receipt changes whether a declared switch is attempted | Acquisition of the trigger rule |
| A continuing carrier revises its operative method | The same carrier installs/retains a changed method state and later executes a different update, generator, or retention operation | That the replacement is better, or that its recipe was invented |
| The carrier learns which method to select | Evidence about alternatives changes a retained selection disposition that governs later comparable choices | Universal superiority, correct causal attribution, or an adequate open-ended repertoire |
| Consequences differentially preserve methods | Actual support changes persistence, renewal or copying of methods | Within-carrier learning, if only population composition or survival changed |

More than one claim can hold in one history, but they require their own evidence. A stopped arm is not an enacted alternative, a stored method label is not a functional change, and a good final outcome does not identify how the choice was made.

For a minimal functional test, let \(d\) be the current data/model state and \(u_\mu(d,z)\) the enacted update under method state \(\mu\) and fresh input \(z\). A method distinction is operational only if some admitted \((d,z)\) yields different update effects under \(\mu_0\) and \(\mu_1\). Effects can include retained evidence, generated candidates, operations and their costs, not just the current prediction. The consequence must actually cause a paid change from \(\mu_0\) to \(\mu_1\), and a subsequent use must realize a relevant difference. Merely changing \(d\) through an unchanged \(u\) is ordinary model revision.

Every finite such construction can also be represented as one larger fixed transition system. That representational fact neither disproves method revision nor establishes architectural novelty. The useful distinction is the declared causal organization and the intervention that changes the method while holding the relevant data and opportunities fixed.

## Discriminating evidence and failure controls

A convincing supporting witness should preserve the following chain:

1. **Actual entrance and identity.** Carry forward the learned representation, accessible memory, resource stocks and physical carrier. Any earlier funded checkpoint or new resource provision is an explicit alternative history, not a reset of the depleted META-REVISION endpoints.
2. **Acquired consequence and its meaning.** Record the native observation or operational receipt, its acquisition bill, and the dependency through which it bears on the carrier's operation. A hidden truth flag, desired answer or analyst score cannot drive the switch. A calibrated receipt can still mislead when its temporal or causal interpretation fails.
3. **Paid mediation.** Show the retained selector update, replacement or reconfiguration and any consumed part. If old evidence binding becomes invalid, invalidate it. State exactly which answer records survive, which are intentionally pruned, and which are newly acquired.
4. **Later enacted difference.** With matched later inputs, demonstrate a different candidate-generation or retention operation arising from the revised method state. Compare a frozen-method control that receives the same evidence and matched charges; use a separate ordinary fixed-controller realization where possible.
5. **Consequences without automatic improvement.** Preserve false alarms, missed changes, changes too late to help, misleading receipts, unavailable parts, and insufficient funding. A changed method may be worse, and past agreement does not certify a new family as complete.

The frozen-method comparison isolates mediation; a separate intervention on the receipt can test whether that channel controls the change. The latter is a diagnostic intervention and should not be presented as an ordinary history in which physical consequences somehow remain faithful to an altered receipt. Match costs when isolating informational effects, but also retain actual unequal costs when assessing whole histories. These comparisons answer different questions.

If a statistical detector is used, its dependence assumptions, false-alarm criterion, horizon and evidence-censoring mechanism need justification. A one-step contradiction in an exact deterministic family instead supports a conditional logical rejection, not a calibrated statistical change probability. The experiment should not interchange those meanings.

## Attainable conclusion

The strongest straightforward result is a conditional witness that acquired consequences alter a retained method state and thereby alter later mathematical learning operations in the same paid history. It can reduce a previously external interface by composing actual evidence, method change and subsequent use. Its supplied proposal repertoire, trigger law, operational demand, resource support and sensor interpretation remain visible.

A stronger claim that the system learned the trigger itself, autonomously invented an adequate generator class, selected retention for unrestricted future questions, or generally improved its reasoning would require additional evidence. Those stronger claims are not prerequisites newly imposed on the globally accepted conditional candidate. The present task is to test and extend a named supporting connection while preserving both its constructive content and its limits.
