## Named obligation and nonduplication

The proposed connection is **one actual paid predictor exposure, including every unsuccessful outcome → its actual successor → the next exposure under the same continuing account**. This discharges a documented implementation boundary in [the predictor protocol](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PREDICTION-PROTOCOL.md), within completion requirement 3. It supplies no new hardware, evaluative objective, ethical ground or source of continuing provision.

A generic probability inequality alone would not close this obligation. [Sequential certification P1](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-SPECIFICATION.md#5-composition-and-abstraction-two-interface-propositions) already states a compatible history-adapted composition bound. [Repeated-renewal composition](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REPEATED-RENEWAL-COMPOSITION.md) already retains actual successor, duration and charged costs in a subprobability kernel, retains failed preparation, and derives finite iteration and accumulated approximation bounds. [The formal core](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FORMAL-CORE.md) already separates recurring finance, information-constrained feasibility, installed-process recurrence and implementation. [Recipient renewal](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-RENEWAL.md) instantiates actual descendant learning and exact prefix liquidity under supplied finite source/component banks.

The useful new result must therefore establish an actual formal/executable boundary for the existing callable predictor, rather than present another favourable kernel or repeat a general bound. It would reduce the gap between the declared probabilistic execution and its checked formal description. It would not derive maintenance of a diagnostic channel, unlimited reliable sampling, sustainable provision or concern.

## Existing laws that must not be silently identified

The old [numerical predictor](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/model.py) implements `advance`: a complete scheduled exposure with attempted gate formation, possible base construction and retrofit, rent, possible work, an actual receipt, optional paid writing, and lease changes. Its state and moments contain both operative fields and observer-only historical information.

The existing [PredictionAction](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/formal/PredictionAction.lean) relation and [PredictionProtocolBridge](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/PredictionProtocolBridge.lean) cover a different, expressly narrower interface: deterministic completed payable-write trials with explicit phases. The old completed-block theorem is correct within that scope. It does not assign stochastic probabilities or erase its incomplete physical prefixes.

The source differences are material:

| Event or boundary | Existing callable exposure | Existing payable-trial relation |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled packet without completed work | The uncaptured packet is lost at the end of the exposure, including no-work outcomes. | A packet is consumed only by work; an earlier blocked phase does not consume it. |
| Work completes but writing is unaffordable | The exposure returns a state with unchanged score, updated executed-action/lease fields and an unpaid-write mark. Later calls receive that state. | The state remains at the observed phase without a completed macro step. |
| Rent cannot be paid | The callable clears the circuit's stage/lifetime and specified memory/history fields. | The rent transition is disabled. |
| Later apparatus formation | Subsequent calls may reform a gate or build/retrofit a circuit when enabled. | Formation is outside the committed four-event trial. |
| Outage | Matching action can still return zero; the existing decoder can then consume a misleading label. | The admitted work law uses deterministic availability. |

An exact overlap can be proved on installed, live, rent/work/write-enabled deterministic trials, with the corresponding parameters, packet debit and operational state projection. Outside that overlap the new result must retain explicit non-equivalence. Merely assigning probability to the old possible paths cannot reproduce the callable's different abort, expiry and opportunity laws.

## Required source-semantic contract

1. **Declare the state projection.** Preserve fuel, gate kind and lease, circuit stage and lease, bounded score, last executed action, and any retained write-status field included in the claim. Name every excluded observer flag and show that its omission does not affect later operative transitions. Exact agreement on this projection is not equality of every original Python field or historical metric. Reconstructing excluded strict-witness statistics needs a separately specified observer extension.
2. **Preserve the full law and its information boundary.** Gate/prior choices, comparator arms and availability probabilities must match the existing law. Hidden regime enters the plant; it cannot select the controller's proposal, action or admission. The `after` flag is historical reporting information in the old callable, not free change detection. A fixed environment or declared reversal schedule must persist across calls; it cannot be resampled at a macro boundary merely to simplify the proof.
3. **Use a total outcome law.** Preserve all initial mass through construction failures, no-work exposures, failed rent, unavailable or incorrect work, skipped writes, successful writes and expired apparatus. Joint atoms must retain the actual successor with the claimed receipt/attempt/status and cost marks. No-work has no physical receipt; it must not become an acquired negative observation. Coincident atoms may be combined only by summing their weights.
4. **Keep failure and continuation distinct.** An unsuccessful exposure is not necessarily a permanently dead system. Conversely, a returned object is not necessarily ready for the old completed-trial protocol. Absorbing a state is justified only by the declared downstream stopping rule or a proved absence of relevant continuations. A conditional-on-write or conditional-on-capture distribution cannot replace the full law.
5. **Keep the source clock and accounts.** The callable consumes one scheduled opportunity per exposure even though its `State` omits the bank coordinate. Composition must explicitly carry the finite remaining schedule/source account, or state the equivalent outer ledger. Every outcome obeys the existing equality between entering fuel plus the supplied packet and ending fuel plus expenditure and waste. Formation, maintenance, work, write and loss must not be charged twice or cancelled by a later receipt. A new call cannot create an unbudgeted packet.
6. **Specify one implemented composition.** Finite schedule composition must feed each actual outcome into the next call, preserving the joint association among memory, leases, fuel and outcome. One inaccessible-history-specific choice per row is not an implementable common policy. Independent fresh random choices are allowed only at the stages supplied by the original execution law; no independence of success and successor resources is inferred.
7. **State the proof/software boundary.** Lean can prove the normalized declared weighted law, its invariants, overlap and composition. A separately written finite reconstruction can compare that law with the existing callable on a declared domain. Those are different forms of evidence. Neither implies a theorem about arbitrary Python execution, every possible input, all scientific claims of the parent model or physical realization of its recipes.

- Preserve the no-work source-loss and unpaid-write continuation counterexamples to blanket equivalence with the earlier Lean trial. Report the exact successful overlap rather than changing either frozen predecessor to manufacture agreement.
- Keep an exact conventional comparator. Finite probabilistic composition is established mathematics; the claimed contribution is a checked application to this mechanism and its failed histories, not a superior algorithm or new universal probability principle.

With a live Fast predictor, score/action zero, regime zero, primary rent/work/packet/maintenance prices and availability one half, the first capture leaves fuel six and the first miss leaves fuel four. It is **not a corrected-memory witness**: the initially correct score remains zero, including after the later paid Fast write. Each row has probability one quarter and sums to twenty-eight. These hand calculations still require the planned independent callable comparison before numerical acceptance.
