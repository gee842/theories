# General foundational warrant: independent critical assessment

8 October 2026. Independent agent research for Ren's instruction to solve the remaining limit after the systems and variation studies. This initial assessment investigates the general inference itself. It adds no mathematical theorem, Lean run, empirical experiment or impossibility result. A completion-stage assessment of the new affirmative account will be added separately if that account is supplied for review.

**Provisional judgment:** a general account need not certify every attribution made in its name. The previous remaining-limit sentence should be corrected if read that way. There is a defensible general requirement to preserve the relationships constituting an independently identified explanatory target. That requirement does not yet establish that the present generative formulation supplies a complete explanation of every actual system or warrants every physical possibility it represents. The stronger no-assumptions target must not be silently replaced by the first, narrower achievement.

**一般适用的说明框架不等于所有具体归属都自动正确。** 系统说明须保留其所解释的实际组织关系，这一要求有一般根据；具体关系、可行变化及更强的普遍结论仍须取得相称根据。下面区分真正修正了什么、哪些竞争说明仍成立，以及什么可以诚实地宣布完成。

## 1. Fix the claim before testing rivals

I read the [canonical root](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope), [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), [stronger-foundation chapter](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ASSUMPTION-FREE-FOUNDATION.md), [systems account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION.md), [system-warrant continuation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/SYSTEM-WARRANT.md) and the earlier [direct constitutive argument](CONSTITUTIVE-WARRANT-ARGUMENT.md). Their scope is broader than a single mechanism but narrower than the claim that every event must make a difference forever.

The first and third proposed root commitments are permissive: reality **permits** consequential differentiation, and interactions **can** compose into arrangements including recurrent ones. Identified examples already support these existence and possibility claims. Requiring every system to instantiate recurrence, learning, closure or the same native architecture would strengthen them without authorization. Their evidential support is not invalid merely because a generic theorem lists the mechanism as a parameter.

The second commitment is more demanding: occurring activity depends on participating conditions and relations, whose organization constrains changes and can itself change. Its general explanatory content has to be distinguished from three possible overstatements:

- that every prior event contributes to every later event;
- that one representation identifies every actual constitutive participant and physical possibility;
- that all systems are necessarily produced by earlier systems in one privileged temporal formulation.

None of these follows merely from identifying systems or admitting a generative explanation. The canonical scope does not require them. Static, finite, destructive and externally maintained cases remain eligible.

## 2. What can be established at the general level

An explanatory target can be identified through held shape, coordinated movement, assembly, acquisition, operation, loss or other independently accessible features. These descriptions already concern relationships, but they do not decide which mechanism realizes them. An explanation of that target must account for those features or give grounds for revising their attribution. An account of a coordinated response that omits the coordination, for example, has changed the explanandum rather than supplied a competing explanation of it.

This yields a general **target-preservation requirement**. Its ground is the content of the undertaking: explaining the identified organization is different from answering an easier question selected afterward. It does not require that every local explanation answer every question about a whole history. A selected endpoint prediction, a structural equilibrium explanation and a formation-history explanation have different legitimate scopes.

When the target includes actual formation of capacities, its relevant earlier and later relationships belong to the explanatory content. The explanation may articulate them sequentially, simultaneously or through whole-history constraints. It need not add an outside lawgiver connecting independently constituted activity and conditions. However, saying that the relationships belong to one unfolding does not establish which attributed relationships are present or which variations are physically realizable. Nonduality removes an unwarranted ontological split; it does not give every proposed attribution equal evidential support.

This is a positive class-level result. Its universality is the applicability of the preservation requirement to the corresponding kind of explanation. It is not a new theorem that every model advertised as generative is correct, nor a certification that the present root exhausts all relevant kinds of system explanation. Those claims have different content.

## 3. Strong rivals and their exact force

### Whole-history constraint account

The strongest apparent rival describes an organized history by its allowed joint configurations and relationships, without treating earlier states as ontologically producing later states. It can retain assembly, operative use, interruption, alternatives and continuing support. If it preserves all the relationships relevant to the same target, it is an admissible articulation under the current nondual theory. Sequential notation has no automatic explanatory priority over it.

This is neither an empirical proof of the rival's ontology nor a new unrestricted equivalence theorem. The conclusion is a scope judgment: denying a special metaphysical direction of production does not by itself deny the relational content the present core claims. The rival becomes genuinely opposing only if the generative thesis adds the stronger claim that temporal production is uniquely fundamental. That stronger claim has not been established by the existing preservation proofs and is not needed for their results.

Calling the whole-history account generative is defensible only at the shared relational content. It would become empty annexation if the vocabulary concealed a disagreement about admitted possibilities, actual participation or explanation. The common content should be stated directly and the remaining disagreement left visible.

### Common driver, correlated record and changed causal location

A rival can preserve observed coordination while locating its operative condition in a common driver. It can preserve a prediction while denying that the predictive record was read. It can preserve final recorded outcomes while locating an error in recording rather than in the earlier operation. These challenge particular constitutive attributions while leaving the independently specified observed target intact.

They are not automatic refutations of a richer account that already has discriminating operative evidence. They identify what further content the richer claim needs. Conversely, the theory cannot declare such alternatives impossible merely because all activities occur within one unfolding. The whole can contain common causes, unused records and misleading measurements.

The completed [model-revision study](MODEL-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#5-two-distinct-limits-on-what-the-repair-establishes) already provides the precise formal case: production failure and downstream recording corruption agree on final readings for every input in the declared finite interface. A suitable intermediate observation distinguishes the specified alternatives. This is an existing checked target-relative nonidentification and a positive localization route; it is not a new theorem or a hypothetical global skepticism manufactured for this review.

### Replay of outputs

A replay preserving a selected output list does not necessarily preserve which alternatives coexist at a reached condition, actual operative uptake or provenance. It therefore cannot defeat an explanation of those richer features merely by matching outputs. The existing [branching study](EXPLANATORY-BRANCHING-INTEGRATION.md#4-a-matched-target-that-exposes-the-missing-correlation) gives a checked distinction: identical complete feasible-word answers can conceal different common-condition alternatives.

The correction also constrains the affirmative account. It cannot expand the target to all relational facts after every challenge. The features requiring preservation must be independently pertinent to what the account says it explains. Once the target is fixed, a competitor preserving only a strict subset is inadequate to that target; a competitor preserving the whole remains eligible.

### Static and accidental organization

A static arrangement does not need recurrent reconstruction to constrain its allowed configurations. A temporary ordered appearance does not automatically establish an enduring integrated organization. These are scope distinctions, not counterexamples to a theory that already allows static and transient systems. In each case, the claim of actual organization has the content supported by the arrangement and its relations; stronger historical or modal claims require more.

This review found no independently established actual system that both retains the full declared formation/organization target and refutes the minimal relational core. It also found no proof that all possible contrary accounts reduce to that core. Neither absence of a discovered counterexample nor reinterpretation of a few rivals establishes that unrestricted conclusion.

## 5. The inference that must not be smuggled in

The following claims differ:

1. Some independently identified organized activity exhibits formation and continuing participation.
2. An explanation undertaking to account for such organization must preserve its relevant relations or justify a correction.
3. The framework can investigate any proposed system without demanding a privileged boundary or one local architecture.
4. The present root explains every actual system at the relevant scope and has warranted its own recognition, inferential use and modal claims without any merely supplied commitment.

The first three have substantive support at the scopes described. They do not automatically yield the fourth. A framework's openness to investigation is not the same as explanatory completeness. A target-preservation requirement does not determine that the present list of root commitments is sufficient. The fact that recognition and inference occur within the whole does not establish the correctness of every use of them.

The fourth is also stronger than any sensible requirement to prove every application before using the theory. Specific attributions can be fallibly warranted and corrigible. A universal foundation could permit such corrigibility while establishing more basic common content. Correcting an excessive all-applications guarantee therefore removes a mistaken gate; it does not, by itself, finish the independently requested no-assumptions task.

No endless regress is imposed here. An actual construction or encounter can provide a direct ground, and a general philosophical argument can justify scope without inspecting every case. The unresolved matter is the content and reach of the offered ground, not its failure to come from an even earlier proposition. Nothing in this review proves that a stronger grounding is impossible.

## 6. Viable completion statements

**Can be accepted:** the general systems account explains how relational organization supplies an accountable target, how its boundaries depend on the relationships relevant to that target, and how formation and operation can earn possibility claims. Completed local proofs and evidence show this has more content than renaming arbitrary transitions.

**Can be accepted:** the purported need for a global certificate making every particular attribution correct should be retired. General scope, warranted token application and complete modal characterization are distinct claims. The completed evidence-guided revision account already explains bounded correction; it need not be rebuilt as a prerequisite to this statement.

**Cannot presently be accepted:** an assumption-free proof that the three working commitments supply the common explanatory content claimed throughout actual system-forming reality, or that a causal production ontology is uniquely necessary. The first is the independently authorized stronger task; the second must not become an extra gate when it is not claimed. Neither demands deduction of all physical constants or all microscopic details. The existing exact representations, branch recovery and scope corrections do not establish those stronger claims.

**What would change the assessment:** an argument supplying independently warranted common explanatory content and showing why a proposed system explanation could not omit it while preserving its target would strengthen universality. For a claimed physical or modal extension, a justified connection between the established relations and that extension would earn its scope. A genuine competing explanation of the same content could instead require revising the root. Another theorem about a newly supplied local mechanism would not alone decide this general question.

This assessment concerns the independently authorized descriptive foundation. It neither reopens the completed explicit G0/J ethical route nor claims that factual explanatory adequacy yields normative authority.

## 7. Completion-stage review of the affirmative argument

I read the revised [affirmative argument](GENERAL-FOUNDATION-ARGUMENT.md) and [primary-evidence assessment](GENERAL-FOUNDATION-EVIDENCE.md), together with the completed model-revision and branching studies cited above. **Accepted at their stated positive and limiting scopes; no required repair remains in the reviewed versions. This does not accept completion of the stronger assumption-free foundation.** This reviewer provided substantive feedback before the final version, including a request to distinguish target fidelity from actual realization and to remove an excessive all-applications certification test. It is an independent assessment with disclosed collaboration, not a blind review.

The revised argument advances beyond the initial requirement merely to keep an explanatory target fixed. Its second-commitment argument earns three general constraints:

1. **Constitutive identity:** at the justified explanatory resolution, a change of names alone does not change the realized organization. A claimed organizational change requires a difference in realizing relationships or a correction of their attribution. This does not identify every property of a system with an arbitrary summary.
2. **Joint realization:** contributions that share conditions must fit together in the actual arrangement and timing. Separately successful realizations that require incompatible shared conditions do not establish joint realization. Alternation, replacement and a finer account can resolve an apparent incompatibility; simultaneous operation is not required everywhere.
3. **Continuing organization:** whenever the claimed organization continues, its constituent roles remain realized, through retained or transformed relations or a realizing replacement. Losing one route is not necessarily losing the organization; absence of every realization of an indispensable role is. This does not require perpetual activity, universal depletion or recurrent rebuilding.

These constraints have general force because they concern the content of organization and its actual realization, not because a preferred local mechanism is imposed on all systems. They reject incompatible witness-splicing and disappearance of a genuinely constitutive role without replacement. Their force is not cancelled by the need to establish which relations constitute a particular organization. That latter attribution can be directly and fallibly grounded; it is not thereby arbitrary. The argument therefore closes a genuine **constitutive reading of the second root commitment**, alongside the existing grounds for the permissive first and third commitments.

The direct immanent route also deserves a positive answer. An exhibited construction can ground its result without invoking a second occurrence outside the unfolding. An inference preserving the established content can be justified through that preserving relation, rather than through an endless demand for earlier propositions. The existing ground-formation and formal transport work supplies scoped realizations of this route. What has not been supplied here is an argument that this route has already grounded the common causal and modal account at the full stronger scope. The need for that argument must not be redescribed as a need for infallible recognition in every application.

The repaired failed inference is now precise: **a necessary condition of realized organization does not by itself supply the general explanation of the activity through which organization forms and transforms or its possibilities change.** An impossibility conclusion concerning incompatible constitutive requirements can be warranted, while the sufficiency of a particular preparation or the general range of physical transformations remains open. The distinction is necessary versus sufficient explanatory content at the intended scope; it is not actuality versus an arbitrarily demanded prediction of every future.

The positive case would be weakened if the remaining issue were restated as either “all correspondence is groundless” or “every model application must be certified globally.” The reviewed text avoids both. Actual controller participation and experimental formation already have their retained evidence. Completed model revision earns renewed reliance within its domain and preserves genuine extension limits. Those results are directly reused.

No new Lean theorem was needed to check this philosophical argument. Its references to necessary joining, derived variations, branch matching, actual use and bounded model revision retain their previously checked mathematical and evidential scopes. I did not recompile unchanged proof files or recertify the whole repository. The review accepts no new theorem about unrestricted metaphysical equivalence, physical necessity or the impossibility of further grounding.
