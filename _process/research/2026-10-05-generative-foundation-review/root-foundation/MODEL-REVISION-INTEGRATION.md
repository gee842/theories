## 1. What was left unfinished, and what closes it

The preceding source model could discover that its original family failed. It could not construct and test a replacement. Its specific counterexample was an interaction between two disturbances: each disturbance alone passed calibration, while their conjunction changed the second report. Supplying that true mechanism to the learner would have bypassed the frontier.

The completion therefore has six requirements, fixed by that problem rather than by a desire to produce another successful example.

| Requirement | Completed contribution | Scope that remains explicit |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnose the failure | Find a discrepancy between the old prediction and a retained observation; distinguish a failed account from a uniquely located cause | A discrepancy can implicate production, measurement, controls or transfer conditions |
| Construct alternatives | Generate corrections from the observed residual and a declared expression vocabulary | The vocabulary and its adequacy are supplied; arbitrary conceptual invention is not proved |
| Let evidence discriminate | Select an unobserved case where surviving proposals differ, then acquire its outcome | Selection precedes that outcome inside the construction; this is not a preregistered human experiment |
| Check common omissions | Audit unexamined cases even when only one proposal survives | Agreement among admitted models is insufficient for adequacy |
| Re-establish warranted use | Check the repaired relation, preserve uncertainty where relevant, and distinguish prediction from causal localization | Measurement fidelity, stable operation and live failure coverage retain their conditions |
| End without false closure | Stop within a declared domain and available support, preserving unavailable tests and reopening conditions | No finite check certifies every new operating setting |

The general account explains these requirements; the construction makes a complete passage through them possible and preserves failures. It does not replace the wider research programme with a Boolean device.

For the normative programme, the connection is **revision of formed understanding → better grounded descriptions of dependencies and losses available to situated practical judgment**. Correct model revision can improve the factual grounds of a response. It does not supply G0, derive ethical value from predictive fit, or replace the completed J1–J4/E commitments.

## 2. A constructive account of revision

A material discrepancy first defeats a particular expectation under its associated conditions. It need not immediately tell us which part of the account to change. Check the question, retained record, measurement route and intervention before assigning the cause. These activities and distinctions occur within the same ongoing inquiry; there is no infallible outside observer whose label supplies the right repair.

Construction begins from what the discrepancy constrains. Which output changed? Under which combination of conditions? Which earlier results must the replacement still explain? Existing knowledge supplies candidate relations; the failed prediction can make a previously unused combination relevant. This gives inquiry a directed search without pretending that the evidence uniquely invents its own vocabulary.

Retrospective fit permits a candidate to remain under consideration. Further support concerns consequences not already used to force that fit. Seek a case where the live alternatives predict differently, or a check that could reveal their shared omission. Preserve the outcome even when it favors the narrower or less preferred proposal. Simplicity, familiarity or generative vocabulary alone cannot determine the verdict.

Return to use has a stated object. A repaired prediction may be adequately supported while its precise causal location remains unsettled. An improved mechanism account may reveal that the live evidence no longer identifies the target. Revision can therefore warrant more confidence, less confidence, a different response, or a narrower claim. Improvement is not defined as always producing more definite answers.

This account reuses the earlier fallibilist entitlement to competent, relevant evidence. It does not prove that entitlement without premises. Its substantive advance is to show how new evidence can constrain the premises and representation themselves, with consequences beyond agreement with the favored answer.

## 3. The complete finite reconstruction

The inherited domain has eight inputs: two target values, two local-disturbance states and two common-disturbance states. The earlier six probes tested each target with no disturbance or either disturbance separately. A seventh observation, with a true target and both disturbances, reveals the failure.

The learner compares the failed observation with the old prediction. Their difference identifies the second report as the output requiring a correction. From the three recorded condition features it generates eight possible conjunction gates, each carrying that same observed correction. It filters these proposals against all seven records. It is not handed the true joint-failure law.

Two proposals survive:

- **Interaction:** change the second report whenever both disturbances occur.
- **Restricted correction:** change it only when both disturbances occur and the target is true.

Both preserve the old observations and explain the discovered failure. Repeating the discovery supplies no difference between them. The algorithm selects the missing input with a false target and both disturbances because their predictions differ there. It then obtains the result through the specified acquisition process. The interaction survives in the original joint-failure case.

The narrower proposal is not disqualified merely for looking like a patch. A separately checked counterpart makes that restricted law the actual production relation. The same learner then retains it instead. Evidence determines which claim survives within the admitted grammar.

The correction record is appended; the earlier history is preserved. The resulting eight records exhaust this finite input domain. The learner can return to qualified inference: with known clean operation it recovers the target; with unresolved disturbances an agreeing report pair can still require suspension. This closes the earlier cycle from failure through reconstruction to actual bounded use.

### A stronger correspondence result

`exhaustive_acceptance_establishes_correspondence` is quantified over an arbitrary actual function on the eight-input domain. If a proposed repair fits faithful measurements at every input, its prediction equals that function throughout this domain. **The theorem does not assume that the actual function was already a syntactic member of the repair grammar.** If the grammar cannot represent the measured relation, no repair need survive.

This is a positive way to earn correspondence under exhaustive finite checking, rather than assuming the desired correspondence as a premise. Its remaining conditions are substantial: the interface is fixed and complete for this claim, measurement is faithful, and the modeled law is stable. It does not convert repeated observations at one time into a proof of all future stability. In realistic nonexhaustive inquiry, independently supported sampling, mechanism and transfer arguments remain necessary.

## 4. The stopping rule was challenged and repaired

An initial design chose further tests only where candidates disagreed. That rule can stop with a single false candidate, because a candidate never disagrees with itself. The checked witness keeps only the restricted correction while the actual law is the interaction; the candidate fits the seven records and the disagreement selector offers no test.

The final algorithm also checks unexamined cases in the declared audit domain. With one supported probe available, it tests the missing case, exposes the false singleton and reports an inadequate family. It does not quietly call the sole survivor validated. The earlier design's failure is preserved in `agreement_alone_misses_a_bad_single_repair`; its remedy is checked in `independent_audit_exposes_insufficient_grammar`.

Here “independent audit” means a check required independently of disagreement among candidates. It does not mean statistically independent measurement, a separate agent review or an assumption-free evidential source.

The finite support budget is also operative. With zero remaining probes, the original two proposals stay unresolved and the state is `needsProbe`. It is not marked complete. If access is restricted to the already observed discovery case, closure is explicitly only over those available tests; widening the test scope reveals the remaining question. Actual decisions under such limits retain the earlier [inquiry-adequacy conditions](INQUIRY-ADEQUACY-CORRESPONDENCE.md), including the possibility of a justified common response or the loss of an opportunity through delay. No new ethical priority is smuggled into a probe count.

## 5. Two distinct limits on what the repair establishes

**Causal location.** A failure in production and a downstream corruption of recording can produce exactly the same final outputs for every input. The construction preserves both staged histories and checks their observational equality. Full prediction of the recorded data therefore does not by itself locate the mechanism.

A further observation before recording distinguishes the two supplied candidates at the diagnostic joint-disturbance input. Final readings retain both locations; an adequate intermediate reading identifies one. This gives both a positive localization route and an explicit limit when that access is absent. It assumes that the earlier reading is obtainable and reliable relative to the challenged recording failure. Another reading that merely repeats the same vulnerable route would not discharge the obligation.

**Changed setting.** A checked alternative behaves exactly like the repaired interaction throughout the old setting but returns to the old production relation in a new setting. Even exhaustive coverage of the old eight-input interface leaves that difference open. Extending the claim requires evidence about the setting-dependent relationship or direct validation there. Common membership in one generative process cannot guarantee invariance; generative change can transform the supporting conditions.

These limits finish the scope assessment of the repair. They are not presented as endlessly postponed requirements for stating the account. A warranted claim can concern the tested predictive relation while leaving causal location or cross-setting use unasserted. If an undertaking needs those stronger claims, it must obtain their additional grounds or explicitly retain the limitation.

## 6. Primary comparisons and evidential reach

**Gelman and Shalizi, 2013.** Their account treats checking and expansion as substantive parts of modeling. Their redistricting example describes an observed failure of a no-interaction model that prompted a revised interaction model and changed the interpretation. They also argue that enlarging a model family does not eliminate the need to assess its assumptions. This supplies a strong conventional antecedent for our revision cycle. Reading scope: abstract, data-analysis-cycle discussion, expansion discussion and redistricting example, plus selected induction discussion; no original data or statistical analysis was reproduced. We use the reported methodological case, not a new validation of its political estimates. [Primary paper](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/research/published/philosophy.pdf).

**Solar-Lezama and colleagues, 2006.** Their finite-program sketching work constructs implementations within a specified program space and checks them against a separate specification. It is a relevant conventional comparison for bounded generation and validation. Empirical inquiry additionally has to justify its measurement and application of the specification; this cannot be borrowed from a synthesis theorem. Reading scope: the primary abstract and introductory claims available from the author-hosted paper; later section retrieval failed, so no detailed algorithm or performance reproduction is claimed. Our grammar and Lean proofs are independently specified. [Primary paper](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/asplos06-final.pdf).

The previously assessed causal-equivalence, shared-information and projection studies retain their earlier reading scopes. Together these comparisons prevent a claim that model revision is uniquely available to this theory. The contribution here is the connected explanation, explicit dependencies, checked realization and preserved corrections. No new primary-source originals or empirical datasets are imported.

**Is the repair grammar tailored to the successful example?** It is a deliberately finite grammar supplied for this construction. The generator derives the changed output and tests every admitted conjunction, rather than receiving the correct repair. The 24-mechanism grid and restricted-law counterpart check that it does not always favor the preferred interaction. This does not establish discovery of every possible scientific representation.

**Is a novel probe equivalent to independent confirmation?** No. The selected input was absent from construction records and can discriminate the two proposals. No statistical independence, confidence level or protection against every shared measurement failure follows. The recording-location comparison explicitly retains one such shared vulnerability.

**Does exhaustion make the conclusion circular?** The positive theorem requires actual measured correspondence at each input, not the desired conclusion as an untested field. Exhaustion is possible because this domain is finite. Declaring an actual scientific domain complete is a further substantive judgment, and the changed-setting counterexample preserves its vulnerability.

**Could a relevant exception be mistaken for an arbitrary patch?** Yes. The reversed case shows the procedure selecting the restricted correction when it predicts the new observation correctly. Generality is not silently treated as truth. What matters is its explanatory target, evidential consequences and warranted scope.

**Does testing forever become mandatory?** No. Finite support and the need for a practical response remain real. The existing account permits qualified closure and appropriate action under uncertainty; it does not permit presenting an unperformed material check as a passed one.

This is substantive author-conducted counterreview, not independent-agent acceptance.

## 8. Completion and place in the philosophical account

The named frontier now has a connected answer: a discrepancy constrains a repair; generated alternatives preserve the old history; new tests discriminate them and check possible common omissions; success warrants a specified relation within supported conditions; inadequate vocabulary, unavailable access and changed settings remain visible; and warranted practical closure is distinguished from certainty.

The chain is now explicit through **formed interpretation → selective revision → acquired source-dependence assessment → evidence-guided revision of the model family → bounded renewed reliance**. The earlier explanations of objectivity, projection, relevant scope and shared inquiry provide its philosophical context. G0 and the declared practical commitments explain its possible ethical significance without deciding empirical truth by desirability.

No remaining step in this named revision cycle is being deferred as another generic “next frontier.” Concrete empirical applications require their own data and correspondence work. The larger claims concerning consciousness, exhaustive meaning, unrestricted scientific discovery and necessity of the whole theory remain open at their original scope. This study supplies neither an impossibility verdict on those claims nor a reason to reopen already completed bounded results.
