- The first fully protected episode ends with cumulative harm `(0,0)`; the revised and successor episodes end at `(1,0)` and `(2,0)`. Earlier protection of p cannot certify its later protection. - Withdrawal avoids the production hazard without constituting a delivered positive service. Lack of a protection certificate is not proof of harm.
- The last inherited state has insufficient funds for a new audit while retaining installed reasons. Nonpayment is not evidence against the original ground.
- The parent's voluntary safeguard route can cost less than its armed word. Failure of one protocol does not prove universal physical infeasibility; authority booleans likewise do not establish moral permission.

For actual consecutive traces, monotonic cumulative harm makes no-new-harm over the whole interval equivalent to no-new-harm in both segments. This gives intersection for continuing coverage. Union is appropriate only for separately addressed historical attainments with an explicit persistence contract. No target, episode or baseline may silently change between those uses.
