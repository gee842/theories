# Consequences change the organization of later response

6 October 2026. AI-authored source reconstruction under the
[nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md).
Named connection: **actual consequences → changing organization carrying a
response → changed later activity**. This note adds a source-derived native
rate comparison and a precise composition condition. It introduces no
controller, transition recipe or Lean declaration. The later four-path source
replay is reported separately below; independent reconstruction has passed.
Existing executions and proofs retain their own verification scope.

The strongest existing explanation does not need a further evaluator that
first declares a consequence successful. A consequence can replenish the
conditions maintaining a component, supply the material from which its
successors form, or become a retained distinction that changes a connection.
The actual component then governs subsequent activity. These are different
causal routes within one unfolding, not one universal reward rule. Their
physical and evidential couplings must each be identified.

## Established mechanisms already change response-carrying organization

The [selector construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/SELECTOR-REVISION.md)
has actual capture restore a mapping's finite lifetime. Failed trials exhaust
that lifetime; reconstruction can produce another mapping in the same
compartment. The original reserve, source and residues continue through the
change. An analyst's count of captures does not select the replacement.
The return-to-renewal relation and binary assembly repertoire are supplied
chemistry. A conventional random-restart controller is exactly equivalent;
directed feedback is stronger in the primary comparison. Two reversals and
the three-unit initial reserve preserve cases where the material mechanism
performs worse than a fixed or outcome-blind alternative.

The [updater construction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UPDATER-FORMATION.md)
also changes the component governing reconstruction itself. Its accepted
history forms R, actually uses R to reconstruct the old mapping after failure,
then replaces expired R with D. D uses the retained old orientation to form
its opposite; subsequent capture maintains the new updater. In
[the source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/model.py),
`selector_choices(updater, previous)` consults the current updater and residue.
R permits both orientations; D excludes the previous one. The audit flags
`used`, `pre_capture` and `witness` identify the causal history; they do not
choose the updater. Replacement of R by D is a supplied fair proposal, not a
learned verdict that D is universally preferable. Fixed D performs better in
the primary comparison, and noisy or changing conditions can defeat its
failure inference.

The [motor-coupling construction](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/README.md)
isolates a still more specific relation. Confident acquired q remains in the
same circuit while an unsuccessful output switch expires and an opposite
switch forms. A later different command obtains support and renews that
switch. The operative command in
[its source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/model.py)
is `parent.command(q,last) XOR coupling`; changing the coupling changes what
the same prediction does. Its strict witness holds q fixed across the relevant
failure, switch replacement and successful use. This is neither merely a new
prediction nor a successful observation reported after the fact. The seven-unit
pre-return boundary, enclosing-circuit expiry and outage controls prevent
conflating a permitted replacement with guaranteed recovery.

## A native connection already present without a new recipe

There is a smaller, genuinely compatible instance in the unchanged
[formed-variant law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/process.py).
Local copying produces the current resident's template. When that resident
later disappears, the free template counts determine competing association
propensities. Thus the material product of an earlier response changes the
formation kernel of a later response-carrying component. No apparatus field,
task flag, success score or added conversion law is required for this relation.

Let the intact site have an active A resident, free counts `(a,h)`, local
support 1, common support `F>0`, and at least one precursor. Assume positive
copy, leak, wear and binding coefficients, as in the active source. Compare
two admitted words:

`copy_a; wear_res_a` and `leak_local; wear_res_a`.

Both consume the local unit and lose the old resident. Neither changes
intactness, source or common support. The copy branch leaves free counts
`(a+1,h)`, while the leak branch leaves `(a,h)`. This population difference is
the actual material mediator, not a nuisance difference to hide.

At the two resulting empty sites, association rates are respectively

`bind × F × (a+1)` versus `bind × F × h`,

and

`bind × F × a` versus `bind × F × h`.

For `h>0`, the conditional instantaneous share of A association increases by

\[
\frac{a+1}{a+h+1}-\frac{a}{a+h}
=\frac{h}{(a+h)(a+h+1)}>0.
\]

This follows by direct substitution into `channels` and subtraction of the
two fractions. It is conditional on the next reaction being an association
at those states. It is **not** the unconditional probability of the next event,
nor the probability that the first eventual association is A after arbitrary
intervening wear, damage or loss. Such events can change the competing stocks
and availability. With no common support, association is unavailable; with
`h=0` and `a>0`, both conditional shares are already one. A separate `a=h=0`
leak endpoint has no association to condition on.

The same argument for copying H favors later H association. The law recognizes
a present template, not its history of repairing the site. The existing
[formal inheritance witness](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/FormedSelection.lean)
`repaired_site_can_pass_to_alternative` already establishes formation of A/H,
A repair, A resident loss, H association, capture and H local copying under
the original repertoire. The paired no-resident-wear theorem identifies the
required transfer event. This is a preserved adverse case for any claim that
successful repair necessarily reconstructs its original contributor.

## Which native coupling remains closed

The resident/template relation can change while the acquired reader does not.
Let O consist of the two cells, cell count, reader presence and phase, read
values and installed selection. At the actual completed-reader boundary,
all successful admitted continuations preserve O. The
[preceding source proof](ORGANIZATIONAL-REQUIREMENT-MECHANISM.md#what-the-acquired-port-result-can-and-cannot-inherit)
examines every formation, contact, commitment and reader guard. Native steps
outside a bound contact leave O unchanged; filled slots, no pending trial and
phase `installed` exclude every currently declared apparatus-changing operation.
This holds before reserve exhaustion. It is a fact about admitted transitions,
not invalid Python calls caught after a helper has begun mutating its object.

An immediate composition consequence is useful. Suppose a proposed exact
implementation reads the target's operative selector as a fixed function
`m = f(O)`, preserving what those physical fields currently mean. Every finite
native implementation block preserves O, hence m. It therefore cannot implement
a target history that changes m. This follows by induction on each block and
contradiction at the first claimed selector change. Adding fuel or equating
total accounts does not change the closed structural guards. This is a bounded
application of the existing exact-refinement requirement, not a new general
impossibility theorem for learning.

Using another physical carrier is possible in principle: the native resident
is already an example. But a selector interpretation using that carrier must
exhibit the actual acquisition, transmission and use relation. Renaming the
resident as a digital method edge does not implement the old reader's observed
command law. Likewise, the old selector/updater handoff converts captured local
support into common fuel and supplies packet access and recyclable module
slots. Those are additional operations, absent from the present common-monotone
native law. Their full histories cannot be imported merely because the summed
energy agrees.

## What this advances and what would defeat it

The new source connection is **response product → reconstruction material →
changed response-carrier formation** in the current native law. It makes the
next explanatory obligation more precise than saying all response organization
is fixed. The resident coupling can change, its reconstruction probabilities
depend on the material produced earlier, and the installed record consumer
still cannot reconstruct itself. Different organizations within the same
history have different lifecycle laws.

The native rate result would fail if copying did not produce binding-competent
templates, if association ignored their abundance, or if the later activity
did not depend on the formed resident. Those are independently inspectable
source relations. Its application fails when precursor, local support,
association fuel or the later source is unavailable. Its favorable-direction
interpretation is defeated by alternative inheritance, changing environments
and harmful effects omitted from a private return. Existing guide, motor and
method studies preserve further such defeats rather than remove them.

For acquired scope revision in this branch, the remaining concrete obligation
is to identify a reachable operation that changes or reconstructs the actual
record-consuming organization, with its occupied material and later use
preserved. It need not be a new top-level task or an approving meta-controller.
The concurrent [relay study](REMAINING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md) already revises
a maintained relation through acquired effects in a different history. The
native result neither retracts that contribution nor demands concatenating
every implementation. Finally, none of these causal mechanisms alone supplies
ethical warrant: what should continue cannot be identified simply with what
copies, captures support or succeeds in reconstructing itself.
