## The explanatory connection completed here

The starting problem is how a new recurring organization arises within activity that already has some reproduction and transmitted variation, and how the resulting organization changes later evolution. The explanation must not require collective selection to produce the first collective, copy a completed group into every generation by stipulation, or infer inheritance from persistence of a structure alone.

The account has five connected steps. An inherited change first alters an operative relation such as daughter separation. Actual development forms new associations and consequences. Reproduction then carries a capacity for rebuilding those relations into successors. Subsequent development reconstructs organization with actual material and activity support. That organization changes the production, association and retention of further variants, which can in turn revise its developmental or reproductive relations. Collective evolutionary properties can therefore be outcomes of earlier processes. They are not all unexplained initial labels.

The new formal step is **actual sampling → carried developmental state → reconstructed bonds across transfers**, with explicit provision and removal accounting. The [previous developmental study](DEVELOPMENTAL-ASSOCIATION-INTEGRATION.md) already checked association formation and reconstruction by a detached descendant within a culture. The [generated recurrence study](GENERATED-SELECTION-RECURRENCE-INTEGRATION.md) already computed reconstruction from carrier pools and recovered conventional recurrences. Neither is reopened or claimed as new. The extension now follows the selected cell and its redevelopment in the same admitted history instead of supplying the next culture's retention independently.

This is a mechanism-based synthesis with a new checked connection, not a uniquely new principle that development can evolve. Existing biology supplies important parts of the account. The first emergence of any chemically reproducing organization remains a distinct origin problem; the chapter identifies that boundary explicitly. There is no claim here to explain every origin from unspecified initial conditions.

## Substantive author counterreview

1. **Does the explanation assume the organization it claims to form?** It begins with existing cell reproduction for the specific transition. Daughter bonds arise from development after an operative change; collective reproduction at the new level is not assumed as the cause of their first formation. The first origin of reproducing chemistry is outside that model and remains explicitly distinct.
2. **Is success hidden in the sampling rule?** The attached-cell criterion is a declared intervention. Its favorable effect cannot prove that all natural conditions reward attachment. Relaxing the rule and revising inherited retention expose different continuations. A population-level benefit still requires an independently justified sampling and competition account.
3. **Is heritability merely defined?** Copying an operative state is a local mechanistic assumption with a biological intervention correspondence. The checked consequence is that subsequent structure is rebuilt from that carried state and support, without transferring the old bonds. This is not a molecular derivation of heredity or a proof that every collective trait is heritable.
4. **Is persistent form confused with evolutionary stability?** No-revision inheritance does not include competitors or establish their relative propagation. The stronger stability comparator is credited. Revision and support failures remain explicit countercases; founder ancestry is not genetic identity or universal alignment of interests.
5. **Is renewal smuggled into a reset?** Fresh provision is debited from named finite reserves, and discarded stocks are recorded. This prevents free regeneration within the checked history. It does not explain formation or indefinite renewal of the reserves; the completed support account retains that wider question at its own scope.
6. **Does the framework defeat a weak rival and overclaim?** Count-only inadequacy is explicitly an observation limit. Conventional graph and developmental models can retain the same distinctions. No quantitative superiority, uniquely necessary vocabulary or universal favorable evolutionary direction is established.
7. **Does one generic process erase differences?** Mutation, developmental construction, aggregation, sampling and reproduction have different operations and evidential requirements. The encompassing process provides their relation, not a license to call them all selection. Local separations state their preserved relations and omitted detail, following the nondual method.

This is an author counterreview plus actual compiler and dependency checking, not an independent-agent acceptance. No outside reviewer or new experimental replication is claimed.
