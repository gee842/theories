## Activity forms differentiated conditions

The relevant history includes respiration, oxygen transport, cell division, matrix production and attachment, plus the vessel and experimental provision. Oxygen-rich and depleted regions are differentiated through those interactions. They are not two pre-existing independent domains subsequently connected by an organism. Atmospheric access and medium preparation remain actual supporting activities; explaining a gradient does not explain the origin or renewal of every supporting condition.

The [Koza et al. primary study](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/koza-2011/README.md) reports gradients forming before detectable optical-density growth; respiration inhibition slows their development. Oxygen manipulation changes the reported 48-hour WS-versus-SM-13 competitive-fitness mean from **1.23 ± 0.15** to **0.12 ± 0.07** (standard errors, eight microcosms per condition). The experiment uses 6 ml medium in 30 ml vials at 20°C. These results support a link from activity to generated spatial conditions to conditional selection. The preserved eight growth summaries are a separate assay comparing WS with ancestral SBW25; they must not be treated as the competition's raw observations. The authors' niche-construction account already explains this link. [Source extraction and checks](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/ecological-selection/mat-conditions/README.md) retain its scope.

This changes the status of requirement 3: activity-generated selection conditions now have direct experimental correspondence in this branch, alongside the separately recorded switching-to-cycle connection. Their integration into a single calibrated history remains open. It would be inaccurate to describe every selective condition here as merely imposed by changing a laboratory protocol; it would also be inaccurate to call these two papers one followed population.

## Access and structural persistence

The [Spiers and Rainey primary reading](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/spiers-2005/README.md) reports a weak WS-5 mat despite retained cellulose expression and no detected difference in several growth/attachment comparisons. Mixed strains partly restore strength. Developmental EDTA exposure reduces strength, whereas addition to mature mats produces no detected change over two hours. The strength assay adds glass balls until the mat breaks, sinks or detaches.

The resulting explanatory constraint is historical and relational: present material abundance or a genotype label may omit how structural interactions formed and how they respond to disturbance. The mutation is pleiotropic, and the measurement proxies and nonsignificant results do not establish equal material states. Partial complementation supports a distributed contribution to structure; it does not by itself demonstrate reproduction of a new inherited collective. A limited mature-mat observation does not prove permanent resistance. These qualifications preserve the positive evidence while preventing stronger causal conclusions than the interventions warrant.

## Substantive counterreview

A constant favorable advantage for the WS phenotype conflicts with the reported change across oxygen treatments. A predictor that keeps only genotype identity also fails our earlier dated transfer. However, an ordinary ecological model retaining respiratory consumption, oxygen transport, structural development and the assay rules can explain the new constraints. These observations therefore improve the content that our unified theory must recover; they do not establish a predictive advantage over that adequately informed rival.

The physical mechanism could still fail to explain the particular date effect. Storage, handling, within-label changes or other conditions may be responsible. Respiratory inhibition also has effects beyond a perfectly isolated oxygen intervention. A successful interpretation must be supported by measured exposure and structure, rather than choosing an unobserved oxygen value after seeing each outcome. The proposed contribution to test is whether measured developmental history reduces independently fitted stage probabilities and supports prediction under changed conditions.

## The next decisive measurement

The next connection is **observed mat development and loss → independently constrained phase-I persistence → linked switching and descendants**. Having checked the focal supplementary assay methods and table scope, seek independent public time-course data for formation, collapse/detachment, oxygen access, culture geometry and handling, retaining actual culture and batch identities. Any new experiment needed to fill a remaining gap must be identified as such, rather than simulated observations supplied as evidence.

A useful test must discriminate initial absence from subsequent loss. Oxygen profiling can disturb a developing mat, while the cited load test deliberately destroys it. A same-culture continuation test therefore needs a validated nondestructive observation or an explicitly modelled intervention. Matched sister cultures can calibrate a destructive assay, but they do not become the original culture's observed trajectory. Keep these sampling relations and any uncertainty in the mapping visible.
