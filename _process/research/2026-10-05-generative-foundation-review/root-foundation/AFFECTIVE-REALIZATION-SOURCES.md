The [author-hosted PDF](https://www.lucabarlassina.org/uploads/7/7/1/0/77107295/beyond_gb.pdf) is preserved unchanged in the existing source collection, with its hash and version in the linked manifest.

**Access:** the integrating agent read the complete main text, printed pp. 274–283, and notes; this source-audit agent independently checked pp. 274–276. **Source points:** on p. 276, “me” in the reflexive imperative concerns an experience type rather than its current token. Section 6.1 permits combined world-directed and reflexive content. The argument compares reflexive imperativism with Carruthers’s evaluativism through phenomenology and imagination-based decision-making. It reports no new empirical study. These refinements distinguish type-directed affective content from the episode’s other possible contents.

## Our assessment and limits

An affective imperative’s content or uptake does not establish ethical authority. Kauppinen supplies a substantive perfectionist explanation to examine, not a premise-free derivation. Neither comparison derives phenomenal realization from our dynamics or establishes C2: patient-directed practical force for an eligible independent responder. No underlying empirical original was independently evaluated for the philosophical comparisons above. The next test concerns constitutive experiential direction, genuine relief and continuity; eliminating a capacity cannot simply be counted as benefiting its bearer. Adding no Lean axioms does not remove these substantive explanatory obligations.

## Reward pursuit and measured affect

6 October 2026. New link-only empirical comparison for [the future-integral investigation](FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#pursuit-realization-and-the-appraisal-of-the-whole); earlier reading scopes above remain unchanged.

Peciña, Cagniard, Berridge, Aldridge and Zhuang, “Hyperdopaminergic Mutant Mice Have Higher ‘Wanting’ But Not ‘Liking’ for Sweet Rewards,” *Journal of Neuroscience* 23(28), 9395–9402 (2003), [author-hosted article](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/berridge-lab/wp-content/uploads/sites/743/2019/10/Pecina-et-al-hyperDA-mutant-J-Neurosi-2003.pdf), DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.23-28-09395.2003.

DAT-knockdown mice showed enhanced acquisition and directed reward pursuit, without higher overall positive sucrose taste reactions; reactions were lower at the highest concentration. Motor and distraction analyses supported the incentive interpretation. The study began with twelve males per genotype; three mutants died during initial food deprivation, after which the protocol changed. Cereal pursuit and sucrose reactions were separate assays. Mutation effects and adaptations remain possible; a nonsignificant contrast does not establish equivalence. Behavioral markers do not directly establish conscious pleasure or ethical value.

Root and the source contributor read extracted main text, printed 9395–9401 through the conclusion, including methods, results and captions. No figure facsimile, cited-study verification, raw-data reanalysis or source-byte import is claimed.
