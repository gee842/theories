**Decision: accepted at its stated philosophical scope, with no blocking issue.** The round defends a narrow patient-directed baseline as a defeasible ethical judgment and removes an unnecessary possible route requirement. It does not derive that baseline from the causal root or prove universal rational agreement, full patient coverage or the necessary joint foundation.

## Scope checks

- **Judgment versus derivation:** the canonical lead and final premise ledger explicitly say that actual eligible relief supplies the proposed favoring ground and that its necessity remains unfinished. Positive warrant is presented through the benefit, the intervention's merits and correction conditions. Mere permission, factual ability and failure to find a counterexample are not made sufficient grounds.
- **End adoption:** attitudes, ends and means remain distinguishable, but the original sequence is expressly treated as a possible route rather than a mandatory chain. An objective act reason can justify concern or an end without waiting for their adoption. The inherited repair/mislearning case supports this ordering only at its own role-bound scope; it is not silently exported to a stranger.
- **Counterexamples and target strength:** grief, respect, ineffective assistance and harmful means challenge different claims. A prohibition need not erase every beneficial consideration, and an allowed act need not possess patient-directed favor. Positive reason, permission, decisive duty, demand authority, accountability and motivation remain distinct.
- **No-conflict relief:** the actual patient, unwanted burden, independently warranted benefit, effective intervention and permission are retained. The affirmative judgment concerns the patient's improvement, not the mere absence of opposition. The text does not infer that all empirical rescue cases satisfy those stipulations.
- **Modest-cost comparison:** actual relief is contrasted with an otherwise matched empty intervention while the actor's cost and other relevant grounds are controlled. This identifies the proposed patient-directed justification without asserting that useless activity is always irrational, that aid is compulsory, or that the comparison was performed experimentally. The linked notes expressly preserve play, exploration, expression and agency as possible independent goods.
- **Strongest motivational rival:** lack of an explicit helping intention does not exclude broader commitments, a relevant desire or a sound route of deliberative access. The direct account remains a substantive position about what grounds practical favor. Competing explanations are not rejected merely for withholding the preferred label or failing to motivate an observed actor.

These checks support the narrower advance: a reasoned affirmative judgment may precede end adoption, while the explanation of its authority remains open to challenge. They do not establish an unrestricted promotion principle. The next connection is the constitutive ground of the patient-to-actor favoring relation, assessed against a motivation-sensitive alternative. Recognition and learned implementation must be evaluated separately.
