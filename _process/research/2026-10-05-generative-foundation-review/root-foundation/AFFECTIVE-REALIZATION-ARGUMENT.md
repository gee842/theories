# Affective direction within an experience

6 October 2026. AI-authored candidate argument under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The named connection is **formed organization and experiential direction → an occurrence's unpleasantness, before reflective ownership → the proposed local patient ground**. This investigates realization upstream of C1. It neither derives welfare from an imperative nor adds a formal axiom.

The existing [constitutive candidate](CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md) distinguishes sensory quality, affect, attitudes and practical justification. The [closure audit](ETHICAL-CONSTITUTION-CLOSURE.md) already distinguishes changing represented damage from changing experience. The present addition investigates a particular organization that could explain why an experience itself becomes a target of change. It is a philosophical candidate, not an implemented or empirically verified realization.

## A bounded primary comparator

Barlassina and Hayward's [2019 article](https://maxkhanhayward.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/more-of-me-less-of-me-hayward-and-barlassina-mind.pdf) proposes that affective experience partly comprises a reflexive directive targeting the experience of which it is a constituent. Positive and negative directions concern obtaining more or less of that experience, or something sufficiently similar. The reference is to the experience, not a narrative person. Motivational force is dispositional; manifestation and action can fail. Footnote 1 leaves the dependence relation unspecified between supervenience and identity; §7 offers a tentative naturalization sketch.

**Reading scope:** main text, printed pp. 1013–1042, including notes; cited empirical studies were not independently checked. Root separately inspected the [2021 corrigendum](https://academic.oup.com/mind/article/130/518/731/6154421), which corrects the clinical quotation in §5.3.2; the version of record was not amended. This note omits that quotation and does not treat the purported dissociation as established evidence.

## Our reconstruction: direction need not await an owner judgment

The following is our proposed use and assessment of the comparator, rather than further source exposition.

Begin with ongoing activity differentiating sensations, representations and responses. A local experience might include a direction concerning its own continuation. The relation could obtain before a reflective judgment that this experience belongs to a particular enduring person. A later self-model might recognize, organize or misdescribe that already operating relation. This is a constructive alternative to treating affect as a neutral sensation that receives all its significance from a separate narrative evaluator.

This reconstruction preserves differentiation. The occurrence being targeted, the organization through which it can affect subsequent activity, and another process that observes it are not interchangeable. Nor does targeting an occurrence require that it possess intentions as an independent agent. The proposal concerns how one organized activity can contain both undergoing and a direction of response.

Its explanatory attraction is target specificity. Changing a bodily condition, suppressing a report, satisfying a separate preference and ending unpleasant undergoing can come apart. A candidate organization should explain which transformation its direction concerns. Learning can then change the available means while the relevant experiential target continues. This does not yet identify the mechanism or show that experience arises wherever such functional relations occur.

Three further facts remain to explain: how the relevant organization forms; how its activity has that intentional content; and why that organization constitutes or determines felt unpleasantness. These are questions within the generative account. Calling a representation reflexive does not answer them. The comparator also begins with experiences and subjects; it does not itself derive their formation or establish the absence of every primitive subject assumption.

## Discriminating the candidate from a feedback label

A meaningful test needs identification of both **target** and **constitutive participation** independently of the desired verdict about affect.

First, establish what the process concerns. A controller might terminate an input signal because a bodily condition improved, because a report was muted, or because a display became inconvenient. Those relations do not by themselves identify an experiential target. A negative register or an event named `relief` provides no shortcut. The relevant episode and what counts as its continuation, reduction or sufficiently similar reinstantiation must be specified rather than inferred from the sign of an output.

Second, distinguish a component of the experience from an accompanying reaction to it. Immediate avoidance could be produced by a separate appraisal or coping process. Temporal proximity, reliable correlation and shared anatomy alone do not establish constitutive participation. Conversely, a separate reflective judgment can oppose a pleasant occurrence without making that occurrence unpleasant. The candidate needs an independently warranted account of integration, not the rule that whatever produces the expected report belongs to the episode.

Failure to choose a relieving act might reflect countervailing motivation, unavailable means or cost. It cannot by itself establish absent experiential direction. The relevant dispositions, acquired means knowledge and eventual action need separate assessment.

Third, investigate interventions that discriminate these explanations. A change in a separate appraisal that leaves the experience's proposed internal organization intact should not automatically count as removing its affect. A change to the proposed constitutive direction might instead change the experience itself while retaining some sensory features. These are conditional expectations to test, not claims that either intervention is currently identified, feasible or ethically authorized. Observed distress or its disappearance would require interpretation alongside changes in attention, reporting, action and other experiences.

A serious challenge would identify the proposed organization and content while establishing absent or contrary affect, or show that a rival explains the same independently identified phenomena without that organization. A freely varied affect predicate would not supply such a challenge. Equally, explaining all apparent failures by declaring that the hidden constituent must have changed would immunize the candidate against evidence.

## From unpleasantness toward C1

Explaining a direction against an occurrence could strengthen the account of its lived adversity without introducing a reflective owner to confer that character. But an operative direction is not already a welfare verdict or an instruction that anyone ought to obey. A process can pursue a harmful end; ending one episode can injure the same being or someone else. The affected continuity, genuine changes in experience and complete relevant consequences remain necessary to assessing relief.

The next substantive test is therefore organizational identification and comparison with a separate-response explanation. If the candidate survives, the further C1 question remains whether this identified unpleasantness constitutes a local burden for its bearer. Its significance for another actor remains the separate [C2 investigation](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-INTEGRATION.md). Neither step is supplied by the grammar of a command. The stronger no-assumptions foundation remains open, with a more specific realization candidate now available for investigation.

## Comparative relief and an experience’s formal aim

6 October 2026. This continuation is our reconstruction and test of a possible telic explanation, not a new C1 proof. It applies the inherited [discipline concerning changing grounds and exact fulfillment](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#43-correction-with-changing-grounds-of-benefit) to an experience's proposed constitutive direction. The [source comparison](AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md) records the limited Kauppinen reading; the following does not claim to refute his complete account.

[Barlassina's 2020 account](https://www.lucabarlassina.org/uploads/7/7/1/0/77107295/beyond_gb.pdf) explicitly identifies the reflexive target as an experience type, rather than only the present experience token (§3.2, p. 276).

The previous test required identifying a direction's target and its participation in the experience. The additional requirement is to establish the target's temporal and comparative content. Does the direction concern ending this occurrence now, reducing its intensity, shortening its duration, or encountering fewer similar occurrences over a specified interval? What comparison fixes “less”? These are questions about the proposed experience, not distinctions a theorist may choose retrospectively to secure the desired welfare verdict. Type reference alone does not establish an elimination target.

Consider a protective warning that successfully prompts withdrawal while its unpleasantness gradually diminishes but remains present. This is a conceptual diagnostic, not a claimed experimental dissociation. The warning's protective function may succeed while experiential opposition persists. Yet persistence by itself does not settle whether the experience's own aim failed. If the identified aim requires complete cessation, partial relief falls short. If it requires a specified comparative reduction, that reduction might satisfy it while residual unpleasantness continues. A subsequent occurrence might issue a further direction, but that requires identification; it cannot be assumed merely to preserve an equation between every present pain and an unsatisfied request.

The positive reconstruction therefore compares actual experiential continuations without requiring a binary equivalence between fulfillment and welfare. Where the constitutive direction favors less of an independently identified aversive undergoing, a response that actually reduces that undergoing can better realize that direction even while relief remains incomplete. This is a specific noninstrumental target: the change concerns the living occurrence itself, before a reflective owner endorses it or a later achievement supplies a payoff. The affected continuity must remain identifiable. Destroying its capacity to experience cannot simply be substituted for improving how that being undergoes this condition.

This proposal has an additional test on the positive side. A present pleasant occurrence can coexist with an unrealized direction toward further similar experience. That would not alone show that the present occurrence is bad for its bearer. A telic account must explain which success or frustration contributes to welfare, over what extent, rather than infer ill-being from any pending experiential aim. Nor does the existence of current pleasure automatically satisfy every possible “more” target. The actual content decides those fulfillment questions.

What supports the local improvement judgment is the proposed resolution of opposition constitutive of the undergoing, rather than generic compliance with commands. Its constitution and comparative content could thus explain why partial relief matters without beginning with a fundamental I. But why better realization of this direction constitutes benefit remains the substantive welfare identification to defend; its significance for an eligible responder remains a practical question. The present advance fixes a previously underspecified inference and its tests. It adds no normative label, Lean declaration or axiom to stand in for those explanations.
