## 1. The exact argument under review

The affirmative investigator supplied this proposed sequence during coordination:

1. Formed intelligence's activity belongs to the whole realization and changes its future conditions.
2. An adequate assessment retains the actual effects relevant to its scope, beyond the current purpose or named participant.
3. Preserving actual formed life while realizing further distinct life through those relations has a favorable generative aspect.
4. That aspect favors effective situated responses through which it is realized, independently of endorsement.

The first two claims prevent a false description of the subject. They can expose an omitted loss, actual dependency, unsupported forecast or misleading boundary. The third identifies ethical quality; the fourth specifies its practical significance. Both concern the same realization. No separate value realm is needed to distinguish the explanatory work they perform.

The decisive step would be a reason why fuller inclusion of the real history warrants **this favorable orientation**, rather than merely requiring that its actual differentiations be assessed. Calling the first two claims ontologically unavoidable does not perform that work. Calling the additional formation intrinsically good states the proposed identification; calling it an expression of the same good can explain its unity but still needs the identification to be warranted. If “adequate assessment” already means assessment according to the proposed ethical direction, the completing step has instead been included in its premise.

This does not require a second approval for an already justified reason. If a relation has actually been shown morally favorable for this response, asking for another stamp granting that same favor repeats the conclusion. The live issue is whether the favorable relation and its scope have first been established. Obligation, authority to direct another participant, permission to compel, rational authority and motivation remain further distinguishable claims. A universal account need not make all of them coincide.

## 2. Strongest matched rival and the precise discrimination

The inherited **content-sensitive realization account** retains actual expression, understanding, formation of capacities and purposes, living interdependence, succession, irreversible losses and constructive endings. It can recognize an activity's worth through what that whole relation actually realizes, without independent value owners, private return, fixed original purposes or an external ethical substance. It disputes whether generic formation, actuality or additional differentiation supplies a further sufficient ethical ground.

This rival is compatible with much of the broad generative programme. It becomes an opponent only when the affirmative claims a stronger generic sufficiency. Describing both accounts as generated does not select between them. Neither does the rival's intelligibility establish its truth or the impossibility of the affirmative identity. The needed discrimination is a demonstrated difference in what the accounts explain: a misdescription of actual realization, a justified correction one account cannot explain, or independent grounds for its proposed qualitative identification. A comparison on which both accounts agree is support for that comparison, not by itself proof that one complete account is necessary.

The inherited **relation-sensitive practical account** also accepts the full actual formation and can grant its favorable quality. It asks which actual relation addresses which response: present contribution, access, dependence, a valid undertaking, release or a formed domain of decision. Those relations must themselves be justified; their historical formation alone confers no exclusive authority. The rival becomes substantive only where it denies that the favorable realization contributes any reason at the position in question. If it merely limits the duty or the power to command, it has not challenged a candidate that asserts only a favorable reason.

The corresponding practical test must therefore fix the disputed conclusion before comparing accounts. Retain the same actual supported further life, the response's contribution, costs, affected continuities and the relevant role history. Compare the affirmative ground with a rival that accepts those facts yet offers an independently defended restriction on **initial practical relevance**, if that is what the affirmative claims. A change in indifference alone is not such a restriction; a hypothetical restriction is not evidence that it is legitimate. Conversely, denying a power to compel does not refute initial favorable relevance. The existing completion-and-succession comparison already poses this test; repeating it with new names would not be new evidence.

No additional participation criterion is installed here as the definition of ethical admissibility. Actual participation is among the relations to explain and appraise. Its moral role is not proved merely by ensuring that the example contains it.

## 3. What the new constructive endings actually add

The latest capacity study contributes genuine structural and accounting distinctions, as recorded in its already checked package. When only two raw parts remain, ending the spanning connection releases a part required for another three-part product; existing L and R remain. With three raw parts, Z can instead be formed while retaining the connection. Ending is therefore necessary for that further product in one represented state and unnecessary in the other. The longer history also changes the earlier work-efficiency comparison. Those corrections prevent false practical descriptions before any overall ranking is attempted.

The matched release-then-L and release-then-Z histories hold the source, expenditures, remaining support and old products fixed, while differing in the additional structure. This exposes the organizational differentiation claim more precisely. It does not decide it: the products have no subsequent represented activity, and a changed addressed structure is not yet a demonstrated difference in lived, ecological or practical quality. This reviewer inherits the recorded formal validation rather than rerunning it.

For the present completing attempt, the strongest implication is that a general ethical direction can consistently include **ending a particular arrangement as part of further formation**. That need not be an ad hoc exception. But the fact that the bar's products survive release does not show that ending a living center preserves its ongoing self-good, experience or relations in the same way. “Preserve actual formed life” must retain those distinctions and specify its scope; otherwise it hides the very losses the whole account promises to include.

The inert-structure comparison is not a new mandatory gate. Ren allows different conclusions across biological, lived and broad organizational scopes. Nor is the claim about guaranteed-never-used capacity restored. The relevant test is the actual quality and continuation of the proposed realization. A narrower favorable conclusion is available in principle without declaring every distinct arrangement intrinsically superior.

## 4. Can a universal partial direction handle mixtures without new moral axioms per case?

A universal ethical account need not produce one scalar score or a complete ranking of every pair. This was already explicit in the grounding audit and is retained here. A common account could identify favorable and adverse aspects, defend some comparisons, preserve genuine conflict and recognize permissible plurality. Declining a ranking can be an accurate outcome rather than a failure to notice the whole.

That flexibility does not itself justify the dimensions, their orientation or their practical scope. Calling the result a partial direction leaves the first identification to defend. In particular, lack of an established ranking does not automatically mean equal goodness, permission for either response, absence of responsibility or a right to impose one response on someone else. Each is a different conclusion. This is a conceptual scope distinction, not a newly proved order-theoretic result.

Nor does a common direction require a fresh moral axiom for every repair or ending. A defended relational ground can have different consequences as its actual dependencies and realizations change; the [general requirement argument](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-ARGUMENT.md) already explains that conditional structure. The factual contribution of release can change between the two-part and three-part cases without changing the ethical ground. However, applying that ground is different from proving it universally. The observed change in means necessity cannot serve as a proof of first ethical quality.

For a mixed or finite-ending case, the useful question is therefore whether the same defended account explains the changed realized quality and scope. It should neither silently prequalify 生 as already good nor label every conflict incomparable merely to protect the proposal. If no warranted comparison is available, preserve that limit. If a legitimate completion is favored, explain what it completes, continues and loses. No perpetual productivity, unanimous purpose, horizon extension or guaranteed evolutionary success is added as a requirement.

## 6. New authorized route: an explicit ethical ground and its general application

After the preceding audit, this reviewer read Ren's [exact new paragraph](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-generation-axiom-and-future.md), the [canonical reconstruction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#an-explicit-ethical-starting-point-and-the-generative-conditions-of-a-future) and [capacity integration §15](GENERATIVE-CAPACITY-INTEGRATION.md#15-生成的规范起点与未来的构成条件). Ren explicitly allows an axiom that generation matters and connects seeking a future with its actual generative conditions. An axiom-based account is therefore an authorized positive route. It need not await a premise-free proof or another endorsement from Ren.

The minimal quality statement in the canonical reconstruction is: **life within the whole and its continued formation have basic positive normative significance**. This is an AI articulation of Ren's input, not a verbatim additional user sentence. Its force is normative; it is not the descriptive proposition that every occurrence has a generative history. Taking it as the shared ethical ground removes the demand to establish independent intrinsic value for each repair, new structure or local enabling condition.

To make its practical scope explicit, a general theory must also say how this favorable significance bears on response. A defensible articulation is: **a response has a defeasible generative consideration in its favor insofar as its actual contribution realizes or sustains that positive direction within the whole relation being assessed**. This is a proposed interpretation of the normative ground together with the already defended [same-realization practical argument](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-ARGUMENT.md#4-the-general-conditional-practical-argument). It is not derived from causal influence alone. If practical force is included in the chosen axiom's formulation, label it openly; if the axiom states quality alone, retain the general philosophical articulation rather than claiming that an empty logical inference has assigned reasons.

This need not become two unrelated foundations or a moral axiom for each situation. The same ethical ground concerns what the response actually helps form. The situated information specifies the realization, the contribution, its alternatives and its full effects. The following general schema is accepted at that explicit-premise scope:

| Step | General content | What an application supplies |
|---|---|---|
| Ethical starting point | Whole life and its continuing formation have basic favorable significance, with the proposed practical articulation stated openly. | Which actual living and formative relations are at issue; no new isolated-value certification. |
| Actual dependence | A continuation occurs only through its real conditions and available alternatives. | A faithful dependency account, horizon and feasibility evidence. |
| Situated reason | An effective contribution can count in favor of a response through that same positive realization. | Its actual contribution, support, losses, affected continuities and response position. |
| Stronger requirement | If the continuation remains required in the full situation, satisfying it cannot omit every necessary realization. | Whether the stronger requirement applies, including responsibilities, burdens and legitimate means. |

The checked general continuation theorems already supply operational implications for the dependency step. In particular, demand beyond inherited support requires addition or route change within the actual demand window; an inherited route unable to add support must change if that demand continues. The action-exclusion theorem likewise rules out achieving the specified continued history through an action whose every successor eliminates it. None needs to be reproved separately for every instance. A real application must still meet the theorem's hypotheses; the new ethical axiom does not create a missing implementation or adequate replacement.

The practical articulation does not require private adoption of each instance. Within the proposed ethical theory, an indifferent response can still have favorable or adverse generative significance. Recognition and motivation remain distinct. But the axiom does not make every intelligence responsible for every dependency, and a favorable contribution does not itself allocate a duty or authority over another position. Those stronger results must preserve the actual relation. This is a scope distinction within the general theory, not a request for a separate ethical starting point in every case.

The same account can accommodate finite endings. A local arrangement's cessation may be part of the supported continuation; when another route already supplies that continuation, its existence alone does not favor the cessation. When finite completion needs no further replenishment, an infinite-renewal result does not add that demand. Stable regeneration can also realize the positive direction without creating a novel type each time. These are applications of one ground to different dependence relations, not exceptions to universal productivity, because no universal productivity command was established.

**What is now accepted:** an explicit general normative starting point, a stated account of its practical articulation and already checked dependence theorems provide a coherent route to conditional situated reasons and requirements. The older review's unresolved premise-free step no longer stops this work. A single minimal ground may leave mixed comparisons or allocation questions undecided; that is its current explanatory scope, not a reason to append an arbitrary verdict per scenario. A complete theory would need a justified general treatment of those conflicts. The present scope does not establish unconditional authority for all possible intelligence, exclusive necessity of this axiom, a complete ethical decision procedure or the stronger no-assumptions objective.

Its general consequences preserve alternatives, finite horizons, actual contribution and the stronger premises of duties. This reviewer found no blocker at that declared axiomatic scope. “Foreclosing” must concern the actual enabling relations and supported alternatives under assessment, not every imagined future or a command to preserve every unchosen route; the draft's feasibility and contributory-reason qualifications provide this limit.
