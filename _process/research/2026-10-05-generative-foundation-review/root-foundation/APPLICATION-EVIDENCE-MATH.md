## Named connection

The accepted succession history joins acquired evaluative influence, a distinct child, fresh paid correction and current instrumental reasons. That does not establish that a further instrumental use is still needed. In its receipt-only bank, a correct command can become known through a successful inquiry that already delivers to the recipient. ## Operational result, independent of ethical warrant

Let an initially ready producer run from an empty physical bank, retaining a fixed frame format and the existing bounded address law. Let the paid reader/reason consumer start with exactly that resulting archive, empty initialized working state and its original budgets. If its endpoint is a current publication supporting one of the non-root commands, the following are derived:

- Some accessible frame is current, present and positive. Exact selected-input/closure invariants and completed cache agreement establish this; semantic `Assured` alone is insufficient.
- That same stored index/frame came from an actual emitted recipient acquisition in the producer trace. Archive writes cannot invent its payload, and subsequent reading cannot substitute a different bank.
- The acquisition's own prefix contains an actual work event crediting the recipient ten physical units. The proof explicitly excludes a preloaded positive worked phase by ready initialization. Thus the historical delivery is proved from the event law, not added as a receipt-interpretation assumption.
- The paid consumer retains its exact ledger: terminal reader/reason energy plus the performed charges equals its original fuel plus reserve. Producer payment and source loss remain governed by the imported ledgers, not by a newly chosen price.

`AcquiredAfterDelivery` retains acquisition order, the exact archived index/frame, the bound header, actual step, prefix and suffix. `DeliveredOnTrace` identifies the actual work transition and its recipient credit. The generic result concerns this receipt-only producer/decoder/publication class; it is not a theorem about every possible reason system or every empirical observation.

## Conditional service interpretation

“Current” in the generic result means current publication in the declared initialized reader context. `ReceiptOnlyEntrance` does not require that context to equal the terminal producer bank’s `world.context`; it does not derive empirical freshness or sense episode identity. The actual successor instance uses the matching fresh bank. Historical completion of a fixed service can remain true after context changes when its independently justified interpretation makes that appropriate.

An episode is not supplied by matching a context bit. In the imported producer law, a context-change event clears the current query and makes the producer non-live; ordinary core events cannot restore that state, while a positive emission requires it to be live. That quarantine prevents a positive archived acquisition after such a change, but it does not identify the service episode. `EpisodeBound` is a separate premise binding actual work positions, before states and successors in the declared trace to a fixed recipient and predeclared service episode. `ServiceMeaning` says that the credited work in that episode completes the specified service. Neither is a new runtime flag or a proof that packets measure welfare.

Under these interpretations, the physical result entails `Met`. If a separately sound pending assertion satisfies `Pending → ¬Met`, the same entrance cannot satisfy `Pending`. This is the exact scope of `no_sound_pending_one_service_entrance`.

The operational proof needs no ethical root `G`. Whether the patient has standing, whether this receipt counts as a benefit, what priority its service has, whether authority is legitimate and what further action is warranted remain independent normative/application premises. No selected-choice premise is smuggled into the proof of historical receipt or completion.

## Actual joined history

The concrete instance reuses `SuccessionWitness.second_probe_trace`, its actual new bank and `actual_consumer_trace`, stopping at `consumed`. The second query credits the recipient from 60 to 70 before the 24-unit reader/reason cycle. At the current publication the recipient still has 70, actor fuel is 10 and one source packet remains. The accepted later selected trial is therefore feasible, but its necessity for a one-service interpretation is not established by knowing its efficacy.

The new proof does not refund or alter that accepted history. It does not reopen an old archive, change the learned coefficient, exchange sources, remove the optional act, or construct a withdrawal policy. Its conditional corollary shows why a proposed comparison requiring both this exact informative entrance and a sound still-pending version of the already-completed service cannot be realized under those interpretations.

## What changes in the foundation account

This supplies a concrete distinction between learning how an act works and establishing that its application is still warranted. It closes the narrow provenance question without choosing a favorable action in advance. It neither establishes constant abstention nor eliminates other service episodes, continuing care, persistent protection, different recipients or independent grounds. Those require their own explicit applicability and fulfillment conditions.

The broader concern/wisdom synthesis can now cite an actual counterpressure on automatic application: the process that teaches instrumental efficacy can itself change the need to which that efficacy might be applied. Whether a system learns or represents that changed need is a different construction; this theorem does not claim to have built it.
