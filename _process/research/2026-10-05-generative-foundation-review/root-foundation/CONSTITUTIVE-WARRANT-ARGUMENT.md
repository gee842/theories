## 2. The strongest affirmative argument

**Begin with an independently identified episode.** Fix what is being explained through assembly, acquired records, maintained or changed organization, subsequent operation and actual failure. The episode is not first defined as an instance of generativity. The [controller and biological evidence](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-EVIDENCE.md) constrain particular instances; the original constructed calibration episode remains a different realization with its own status.

**Identify roles through their relations.** A component, an operation, a retained arrangement and a condition name differentiated contributions within this episode. Their names do not create the differences or place activity and its conditions in separate realities. The [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) requires keeping this continuity accountable.

**Trace a formed arrangement into actual participation.** Where an operation forms or reorganizes an arrangement, and that arrangement enters a subsequent operation, the earlier effect participates in the latter's conditions. The warrant is the identified continuation of that arrangement through its actual construction, use, interruption or replacement. No extra enforcer outside the process is required to connect two independently constituted domains. This explanatory step still requires evidence that the claimed continuing relationship is the one actually present.

**Test the attribution without impoverishing the target.** A rival must either retain that demonstrated relationship, provide a different supported account of it, or show that the alleged participation was misidentified. Agreement on one output sequence alone does not preserve documented operative use. Conversely, the existence of a record alone does not show that it was used. Common causes, redundant routes, overwritten records and full resets can require revising a particular attribution; they are not excluded by a label.

**Earn joining from the same continuation.** Identified fragments join through the actual arrangement connecting them. Separate possible fragments need not compose when their support, timing or other relevant relations conflict. A whole-history constraint can preserve these relations as well as a sequential presentation. Recurrence is credited where it is demonstrated; a finite or destructive ending does not contradict the declared scope.

**Generalize the explanatory requirement.** Every account undertaking to explain an independently established formation must preserve its relevant consequential distinctions, operative relations and compatible continuation, or provide an adequate alternative that corrects their attribution. This requirement is independent of implementation and terminology. Its support is the content of the phenomenon being explained, not an `Exact` field that merely stipulates the conclusion.

This argument gives substantive warrant to identified constitutive relationships and to their necessary preservation. It does not justify treating every uninvestigated formation as already identified in the required relational detail.

## 3. The precise scope passage still unearned

Three claims must remain distinct:

The remaining passage is therefore specific: **from warranted attribution in identified formations and a general preservation requirement, to warranted attribution and coverage throughout the foundation's intended class.** Fixing the class as “everything whose prior activity forms later conditions” would place the disputed content in its entry condition. Conversely, allowing any arbitrary record sequence to count without explanatory consequences would weaken the proposal to representability, which the canonical programme explicitly rejects.

This is not an argument that evidence is inferior to deduction, or that every legitimate ground must be an earlier proposition. An exhibited operation can warrant a claim directly; the existing [ground-transport study](GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#11-derive-correspondence-through-formation-and-state-its-exact-remainder) gives a checked instance and relative propagation. The present problem concerns the warranted content and reach of those grounds. Recognizing an occurrence is not automatically correct recognition of every relation attributed to it.

## 4. Test the strongest shortcuts and rivals

**An enacted denial.** A grounds-responsive challenge may itself use acquired effects in subsequent activity. This can defeat a denial that participation ever occurs. A critic can nevertheless acknowledge that participation and dispute the universal scope inference. Moving from the first result to the second would change the claim being established.

**An exhaustive split.** An earlier effect participates or it does not. Admitting both possibilities can make an explanatory scheme broad enough to describe them. It does not establish positive participation in every case. The core already allows irrelevance, erasure, static and destructive regimes, so those cases discipline the scope rather than refute the theory.

**An atemporal whole account.** A description retaining acquisition, use, correction, support loss and warranted replacement relationships survives the test. This is an admissible articulation of the core's content, not a counterexample to it. Rejecting it would add the unrequested requirement that one temporal presentation be uniquely fundamental.

**Another exact mathematical reconstruction.** The recent effective-law theorem derives a summary law and complete finite-history correspondence from the original mechanism. That is a completed result. Its proof does not establish the original mechanism's substantive warrant throughout actual system-forming reality. Repeating the construction at another level would not, by itself, supply the missing scope argument.
