## The constructive result

The [self module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GeneratedSelf.lean) derives a relation from the existing process, initial condition and history:

`Residual P initial past future := Feasible P initial (past ++ future)`.

It describes which finite futures remain compatible with the declared past. The boundary theorem retains the actual intermediate-state constraint: compatibility means that some endpoint of the past permits the future. An observed label history may pool several endpoints; existence of a continuation is not the ability to control it.

The exact-summary theorem gives a necessary-and-sufficient criterion. A proposed record admits an interpreter preserving all these finite futures exactly iff equal records never merge histories with different residual profiles. The constructive reverse direction supplies an existential interpreter without a choice axiom. It does not show that the interpreter is computable, affordable, acquired or physically installed. Common history extension preserves profile equivalence, giving a consistent update relation.

This exact contract can test a claimed representation. It is stronger than many useful task-specific or approximate models need, and is not a definition of selfhood. The [mechanism note](SELF-LEAN-MECHANISM.md) also identifies the complementary existing local result: an actual material-backed memory update changes a later command in `ConstructedPrediction.lean`. That result retains supplied memory, decoder and coupling recipes; the new profile theorems do not derive those recipes or add a sensory boundary.

## What the counterexamples establish

The existing Boolean toggle process gives an actual transition from false to true while every finite future word remains possible. Its empty and one-event histories therefore have identical residual profiles. No readout that depends only on these profiles can recover both current endpoints. The failed claim is that this profile automatically constitutes a complete current-state representation. Some useful self-models may properly ignore phase; this result does not deny their possibility.

The same law also has an empty history and a two-event cycle ending at the same state. Order by history length and order by its converse are both reflexive, transitive, invariant under action renaming, and independent of attached Boolean annotations. They rank the histories oppositely. Their actual law, endpoints, histories and residual profiles are held fixed while the proposed orientation changes. This proves that those specified coherence/invariance conditions do not select a growth direction. Event count has not been identified with welfare, adversity or morality.

The [orientation module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/EthicalOrientation.lean) also derives a factual continuation-inclusion preorder, its monotonicity under the core's synchronous product and its preservation by exact feasible-word projection. Separately, the toggle cycle rules out a strict transitive state ordering that improves on every allowed event. The latter concerns inevitable improvement along every step, not the existence of an ethical distinction between better and worse acts.

These counterexamples do not assert that every process must form a self. A generative regime account should also explain conditions without selves. The unpointed-law symmetry result excludes one absolute distinguished state respecting every symmetry; it leaves local, historical and plural centers open. Details and the rejected phase-model proof route are preserved in [the ethical study](SELF-ETHICS-LEAN-GROUND.md) and [independent review](SELF-FOUNDATION-LEAN-REVIEW.md).

## Next unresolved connection

The formation task remains **actual organized interaction and retained learning → an acquired, revisable self-related representation that changes action**. It must distinguish own production/regulation from relevant external activity without receiving the correct boundary as a label or reward. The existing material-backed learner is a concrete interface to work backward from; its decoder and acquisition recipes still need grounding. The residual criterion is available to audit an exact-continuation claim, not to impose that particular task on every self-model.

The ethical task remains **actual experiential adversity → a justified reason for an eligible response**, before ownership or private endorsement. A sound exclusive-ownership argument would narrow the claim; a counterexample within a predeclared experiential domain would challenge its constitutive ground. Conversely, a model that merely reproduces a preferred label or maximizes an analyst's score would not establish the bridge.

The root occurrence/law question and the empirical identification of experience remain upstream obligations. These results sharpen where completion must occur while preserving the stronger goal. No assumption is silently declared solved because the formal integration passes.
