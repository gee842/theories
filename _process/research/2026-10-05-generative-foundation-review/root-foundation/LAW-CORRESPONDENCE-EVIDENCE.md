## 2. Experimental formation of subsequent biological conditions

Koza et al. (2011), *Environmental modification and niche construction: developing O₂ gradients drive the evolution of the Wrinkly Spreader*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ismej.2010.156), supplies a different kind of evidence. This assessment directly read the preserved [primary article](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/koza-2011/article.html), especially methods sections `sec5`–`sec9`, results `sec12`–`sec16`, and the discussion. Its [source manifest](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/koza-2011/manifest.json) identifies preserved bytes, retrieval, reading scope and missing raw data.

The methods report sterile medium equilibrated before inoculation, with a profile confirming no initial gradient; calibrated microprobe profiles are then acquired after inoculation. Early gradients form while no significant optical-density increase is detected over the first three hours. The separate respiratory intervention slows gradient development, and respiration measurements support oxygen removal exceeding replenishment in the static preparation. No detected early growth is not proof of exactly zero growth, and sodium azide is a metabolic intervention with effects beyond a perfectly isolated oxygen change.

Oxygen availability is also varied in the competitive assay. The reported 48-hour WS-versus-SM-13 relative-fitness mean changes from **1.23 ± 0.15** under high oxygen to **0.12 ± 0.07** under low oxygen, with eight microcosms per condition and standard errors. The source's competition methods (`sec9`) identify initial and final counts and the comparison formula. These are relative growth measures, not probabilities of mat persistence. The [existing extraction and checks](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/ecological-selection/mat-conditions/README.md) distinguish the two competition summaries from the eight separate growth summaries.

This supplies experimental constraints on **activity forming spatial conditions that participate in subsequent selection**. The conditions are differentiated through consumption, transport, organization and atmospheric access in the same material setting. The [mat-conditions integration](MAT-CONDITIONS-INTEGRATION.md#activity-forms-differentiated-conditions) correctly credits the original niche-construction explanation. An ordinary ecological account preserving these relations is a successful comparator; generative terminology has no demonstrated exclusive predictive advantage.

Keep the actual evidence boundaries: no raw individual sensor profiles or competition counts were obtained; gradients and competition belong to related assays, not one traced culture with every quantity measured; respiratory inhibition is not a unique-mediator proof; the reported numbers cannot calibrate different later genotypes or life cycles without transfer evidence. The source's summary statistics and the earlier extraction are credited at their original scope. This assessment neither reruns the authors' statistics nor independently reproduces their biology.

## 3. What this discharges and what it does not

The two cases answer the philosophical worry that “prior activity forms later conditions” is only a definition encoding a desired conclusion. The proposed relationship has independently documented instances: measured values feed actual retained calculation, and performed biological manipulations constrain formation and consequence. An account erasing those relationships would lose the target's documented operation or experimentally supported differences. A global or atemporal account preserving them remains eligible. Replaying a prearranged output table could match one record; it would need further evidence to replace the documented implementation or account for supported variations. The evidence does not rule out every imaginable rival.

The new mathematical effective-law work has a different role: derive a summary transition law and exact correspondence from the unchanged native `FormedGrounds` law, rather than receiving a second independent summary law and an unproved `Exact` field. **`FormedGrounds` is neither the TCLab apparatus nor the bacterial microcosm.** These empirical cases do not turn its Boolean comparison, finite support, learning repertoire or fresh-review architecture into measured biological or hardware facts. They substantiate the broader constitutive connection; any claimed empirical implementation of that particular construction needs its own mapping.

Thus a defensible completion statement is: **the explanatory preservation obligation is connected to derived mathematical correspondence and already evidenced operative mechanisms; local correspondence is no longer described as wholly missing.** This does not mean all mathematical inputs and evidential commitments disappear. Source fidelity, measurement interpretation and scope are warrants to assess, not conclusions that Lean can obtain from an empty empirical record. Nor should explaining the entire origin of physical law, uniqueness of generative vocabulary or indefinite continuation be introduced as new conditions for crediting these completed connections.
