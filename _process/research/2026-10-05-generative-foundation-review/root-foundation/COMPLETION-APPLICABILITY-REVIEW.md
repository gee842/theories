## A positive characterization that the evidence can support

For a specified claim and interpretation family, completion recognition can be exact only when the relevant acquired record distinguishes compatible histories that disagree on applicability. Conversely, constancy on those evidence classes permits a mathematical classifier. The existing generated-self/outcome-profile results already establish the generic form of this observation criterion; novelty cannot rest on renaming their variables.

A useful further specialization retains the actual process history, the patient/episode/ground index and the current evidence relation. A completion certificate is sound when every admissible history/interpretation compatible with that evidence fulfills the same claim, and the actual case is independently shown to belong to that family. Actual compatibility prevents an empty evidence class from certifying anything by vacuity. An ambiguous class permits withholding a completion verdict; absence of a certificate is not a certificate of continuing need.

Where the independently justified ground is specifically an unmet one-service demand, established completion defeats that application. Other grounds, other episodes and the underlying patient standing must remain represented separately. Neither the information criterion nor an exact selective-discharge identity supplies this normative interpretation, a total ranking of actions, an acquired decoder or its physical effect on the existing controller.

The strongest useful progress under an unchanged law is therefore a precise combination: establish what the actual retained history certifies, identify the missing query/claim binding, derive whether the current mechanism can respond to that distinction, and connect these results through an explicit evidence-to-application contract. If the existing law cannot change its selector, a theorem cannot manufacture such a change while preserving that law.

## Adversarial cases and acceptance requirements

| Case | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Positive receipt for another episode, patient or ground | Physical delivery and matching context bits do not alone establish completion of this claim. Carry justified binding, including temporal scope. |
| Absent, suppressed or inaccessible receipt | Unknown evidence must not become a negative fact or a completion certificate. Paid reading can fail without changing the underlying situation. |
| Zero receipt with unavailable means or source | A failed outcome can have more than one cause. It neither proves the opposite efficacy law nor, by itself, that the relevant claim remains unmet. |
| Historically valid receipt after context change or renewed need | Earlier service remains a historical fact. Its applicability to the current claim requires the right episode/horizon; do not silently transport it into a new request. |
| One claim fulfilled while another ground remains | Discharge the fulfilled demand without deleting another recipient, independent ground, continuing duty or patient standing. |
| Permission revoked while need persists | Permission constrains eligible execution; it does not prove completion or falsify benefit. Conversely, retained permission does not show an unmet demand. |
| Execution stops only because fuel or a port is exhausted | This does not demonstrate recommendation revision. A causal comparison must expose the recommendation's own dependence while retaining relevant means and resources. |
| A new interpretation makes the observed action look correct | Fix the admissible interpretation family independently of the desired result. Coverage and normative meaning remain explicit obligations. |

## Selected mechanism proposal

The proposed all-history invariant is substantive: in the unchanged successor law, native data are retained and route has no modifying event; therefore `help` is invariant. If established, every admitted assessment along the actual shared-route, native-seven lineage still returns the help branch. Physical evidence and corrected means can change without this completion-sensitive revision becoming available. Revocation can block execution while leaving that internal assessment unchanged. This is a structural limit of this particular supplied mechanism, not a theorem that learning or grounded recommendation revision is universally impossible.

The invariant is specifically about this internal `help(route,n)` assessment. It is not an invariant of every recommendation, command or action. Current command meaning, scope, permission, lifecycle, available port and actual execution can change in the same law. An all-history statement about what happens **if an assessment is admitted** also does not guarantee that another assessment will become enabled or be scheduled. The actual accepted first assessment supplies a nonvacuous instance; later lifecycle guards remain intact.

Root has elected to reuse the existing positive contracts rather than add another generic classifier or ledger theorem. The integration should keep three requirements explicit: grounded addressed meaning, physically acquired discriminating evidence, and an actual mutable path by which that evidence can affect selection. Existing acquisition histories provide actual records; the exact-summary and claim-ledger results organize the information and soundness obligations, with practical grounds and episode meanings still independently supplied. None installs the missing completion-sensitive path in this successor. Their reuse is a positive characterization of what a proposed continuation must preserve, not a new proof that every requirement has been discharged.

The proposed ten-versus-twenty comparison must have a precise scope. The same physical delivery can satisfy a one-packet service description and leave a two-packet description unfinished, where the requirement is absent from the physical record supplied to the proposed decoder. This is an ambiguity across distinct externally specified services. The twenty-unit unfinished case does **not** satisfy the old `ServiceMeaning` premise that any qualifying ten-unit work fulfills the claim; it cannot be presented as a counterexample inside that older one-service theorem. It also does not identify welfare with packet quantity or establish that either service requirement is normatively justified.
