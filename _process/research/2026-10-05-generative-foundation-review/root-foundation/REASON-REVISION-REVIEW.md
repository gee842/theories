It must not counterfeit a completed `PaidRecipientReasons` publication or be described as an unchanged trace of that reason protocol. The direct voluntary comparator can use its actually updated candidate without buying factual/reason reading or import, retaining those savings. Compartment-shortage controls preserve the same original total endowment and hold displaced units idle. They test usable funding and account restrictions, not smaller total resources. The actor cannot spend recipient funds or unconverted source. Interrupted parents cannot be repaired by reopening; missing assemblies retain their material and saved assembly cash.

False fresh acquisition is a calibration-violation control. It can generate misleading evidence even when every payment and structural guard succeeds. Disagreement between old and new records alone does not identify which channel or law changed.
