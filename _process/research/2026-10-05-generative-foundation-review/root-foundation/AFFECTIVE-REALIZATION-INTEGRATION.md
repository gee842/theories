# Formed affect before a narrative I

6 October 2026. AI-authored research under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The connection investigated is **formed regulation and acquired distinctions → an experiential episode with affective direction → local adversity and warranted response**. This supporting study specifies the experiential interface of the accepted derivation sequence. It adds no generative law, ethical axiom or claim that the complete foundation has been established.

**Result:** the target of an aversive direction and its participation in an experience are now explicit constitutive questions. Neither requires beginning the investigation with a narrative owner. We have a specific candidate and a substantive rival, with conditions for discriminating them. We have not derived experience, its badness for a bearer, or another actor's reason from the existing Lean dynamics.

## The three comparisons and their different contributions

The [positive argument](AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md) directly examines Barlassina and Hayward's experience-directed reflexive imperativism. The [review](AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-REVIEW.md) uses Martínez's body-directed rival: a separate coping process could explain experience-directed action. Both descriptions can concern differentiated activity within one generative history. Their disagreement cannot be settled by equating observed avoidance with either proposed content.

Prueitt, Sezer and Sacchet's [2025 allostatic account](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niaf049) connects bodily boundaries, adaptive directives and self-formation within embodied activity. It explicitly leaves affect's nature open (p. 11), presents predictive processing as a debated heuristic (p. 5), and reports no new data (p. 13). Its Pratyabhijñā comparison loosens the limited self while retaining a wider “I” (pp. 9, 11). It does not establish disappearance of all subjectivity. The full 15-page article was read through the [PDF mirror](https://philpapers.org/archive/PRURPA-3.pdf); underlying empirical studies were not independently inspected.

The [source record](../../../../sources/37-ethical-minimum-primary-2026-10-06/affective-realization/README.md) distinguishes preserved originals, corrected quotation status and link-only access. None of these papers is treated as independent empirical confirmation of our foundation.

The allostatic comparator does not adjudicate reflexive versus body-directed affective content. Its contribution here is a shared embodied organization and revisable boundaries; its neutrality about affect must remain visible.

## Our constructive integration

Start with the unfolding whose differentiations the theory must explain. A formed organization can acquire ways of regulating bodily conditions; it can also acquire representations, appraisals and means of changing its own activity. The additional hypothesis is that some organized activity is experienced and that its aversive direction belongs to its experiential constitution. Reflective ownership may then represent this organization rather than create every aspect of its significance. This order is a hypothesis about dependence, not a claim about every developmental timetable.

The new explanatory obligation has two coordinates. **Target:** does the direction concern a bodily change, the episode's continuation, another appraisal, or a report? **Participation:** is that direction part of the affective episode, or a further response to it? These coordinates describe distinguishable relationships, not fundamentally independent substances. A bodily condition can matter through an integrated experience; an experience can occasion a further response. A complete account must explain their actual connection, including cases where a separation is misleading.

This yields a more demanding positive test than adding an ownership variable. Explain how the episode and its target are differentiated through activity, how the directed component participates in it, and how learned means can change while the target remains identifiable. Then determine whether altering that organization changes felt affect, preserving relevant bodily content, competing motivation and capacities of expression where the proposed comparison requires them. The review's conditional interventions are research specifications; no selective manipulation or new result is claimed.

The comparison also keeps three senses of success apart. A directive can obtain its represented target; an organization can take it up; a response can be justified. Formation of the first two relationships does not certify the third. This is a distinction within the unfolding whose connections still require explanation, not a declaration that experiential and ethical facts occupy separate realities.

## Completion and revision conditions

An independently identified separate-response mechanism that explains the same phenomenology could defeat or narrow the reflexive candidate. A realized constitutive mechanism with discriminating evidence could strengthen it. Either result would change the account upstream of C1. Unpleasantness that is worthwhile overall does not by itself defeat a claim about local adversity; absence of a narrative ownership judgment does not by itself establish it. Genuine relief must still concern the actual undergoing continuity, with other consequences retained.

Even a successful realization explanation would leave the [ethical necessity investigation](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-INTEGRATION.md): why does that patient's permissible relief have practical force for an eligible independent responder? The present gain is a specific explanatory candidate before narrative ownership, not proof of the universal ethical minimum or an assumption-free ontology.

## Formal aims and comparative relief

The [new source comparison](AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md) adds a telic-perfectionist candidate for C1 and sharpens the earlier reflexive-content account. Kauppinen proposes that frustration of the formal aim of a self-defining experiential capacity constitutes unflourishing. His argument expressly uses the further identification of unflourishing with badness. Our bounded inspection does not establish that identification or constitute a complete assessment of the chapter. The separate, recently published Chow reply remains abstract-only here.

The [positive continuation](AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#comparative-relief-and-an-experiences-formal-aim) and [review](AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-REVIEW.md#comparative-direction-pending-aims-and-present-adversity) isolate an inference our reconstruction cannot make without further support: a comparative direction toward less experience does not alone establish an absolute elimination target or its time of fulfillment. An aim can be pending; a response can reduce an ongoing burden without ending it. These are differences within one history, not separate models with freely toggled valence. Success of an occurrence’s request and realization of a capacity’s formal aim must themselves be connected; neither can silently substitute for the other. Partial relief alone does not rule out residual unflourishing on an independently defended capacity-level standard.

Our constructive proposal retains an independently identified experiential opposition and compares its actual continuations. Where partial relief better realizes that direction, it can be investigated as a patient-side improvement before complete cessation. The argument still must explain why this better realization constitutes welfare improvement; ordinary command fulfillment cannot supply the answer. This proposal does not assume that intensity, duration, recurrence and different people's conditions share a scalar measure.

Type reference also needs a scope. Referring to an experiential kind does not, just by its grammar, identify every bearer as a target or give every responder a reason. A content may concern this undergoing continuity's future instances of the kind. Even a wider content would still require justification of its practical force. The formation of that scope belongs inside the generative explanation, without importing a narrative owner or erasing real differences between lives.

For the Lean interpretation, the existing numerical `burden > 0` test does not encode an imperative's target, comparison or fulfillment time. The model's service ending with zero burden therefore cannot establish that every remaining positive quantity is a frustrated experiential aim. That implication would require the very semantic and welfare grounds under investigation. No new predicate, event law or axiom is added to conceal them.
