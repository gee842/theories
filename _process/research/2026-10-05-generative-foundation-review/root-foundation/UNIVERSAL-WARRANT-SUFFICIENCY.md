# Universal warrant: can immanent self-assessment establish universal moral favor?

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored affirmative attempt and substantive counterreview for [Ren's universal-intelligence objective](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-universal-intelligence-session-goal.md). This addresses the [whole-continuation sufficiency limit](WHOLE-CONTINUATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md#3-the-universal-sufficiency-audit). It does not alter the concurrent [prospective-imposition argument](UNIVERSAL-RESPONSE-COMPOSITION-ARGUMENT.md) or [formal coverage package](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/README.md).

**Primary target.** Endorsement-independent **pro tanto moral favor for every independently specified qualifying response position**. No decisive duty, overriding reason, categorical rational authority or actual compliance is required to complete this target. A rival granting that moral favor is not a counterexample.

**Subsequent scope audit:** this is the particular stronger candidate tested by this note, not a definition of Ren's full universal-metaethics objective. [The integrated correction](UNIVERSAL-METAETHICS-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md#7-scope-and-prospective-ground-adjudication) distinguishes a common justified account across all intelligence from the additional claim that every qualifying beneficial-response position has positive moral favor. Preserve the failed inescapability route and its rival below. Their outcome does not establish either that every possible universal account fails or that the restrictive account is warranted.

**Outcome.** The argument below supplies **no new universal result**. It reconstructs the inherited affirmative defense of whole-directed ethical criticism and tests a proposed completion through the inescapability of generative activity. Universal inclusion establishes the scope of the question, but does not establish that actual favorable realization supplies moral favor at every qualifying response position. The principal rival denies even that first favor and offers a constitutive ground for its restriction. Its ground remains contestable; this round finds neither a substantive contradiction in it nor a successful new inference establishing the affirmative alternative.

**本轮没有证明普遍伦理根据。提出的肯定论证是：选择机制本身形成并改变整体，因此对机制的正当评价不能仅由它已经选择的成败标准决定。主要反对意见接受实际实现的有利性质，却否认它在所有合格回应位置上构成初步的道德理由。更强的“无条件理性权威”不是本轮完成条件。共同生成与自我评价并未单独排除这个反对意见。本轮把这一条充分性路线检验到底，不把再次到达原有根据问题计为新的普遍推导。**

No mathematical theorem or empirical result is claimed here. The argument is philosophical, its ethical commitments are identified, and no Lean encoding is offered as a substitute for their warrant.

## 1. The missing universal inference

The prior work already establishes the following distinctions and does not need another role-variation example:

- Ethical force, recognition, motivation, permission, decisive duty, and categorical rational authority are different claims.
- A formed intelligence and the continuities it affects are differentiations within one unfolding. Their boundaries neither automatically exclude nor automatically establish a practical reason.
- Once option-specific favor has been warranted, requesting another source of that same first favor repeats the grounding question. The [constitutive-answer clarification](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-INTEGRATION.md) does not warrant the first favor.
- A true account can include every effect while its practical scope remains disputed. Complete description and good prediction do not settle ethical sufficiency.

The proposed new route is this: **because every intelligence's selection, reflection and self-modification remain formations within the whole, can their adequate self-assessment establish endorsement-independent moral favor at every qualifying response position?** This is a route through the grounding of universal moral address, not through a stranger's unused opportunity or a newly adopted role.

The target is endorsement-independent contributory moral favor concerning every qualifying actual response position, not inevitable obedience and not mere truth of a favorable-quality description. Categorical rational authority is a separate, stronger optional question. Its failure cannot keep the declared moral-favor target open after that target is established.

## 2. Strongest inherited affirmative reconstruction

Begin with intelligent selection as an achieved organization of ongoing activity: retained consequences participate in distinguishing possibilities, anticipating effects and revising what follows. This description leaves the actual extent and realization of intelligence open. It does not define intelligence as morally adequate or as maximizing any one quantity.

The whole includes those selecting activities and the lives, practices and conditions through which their consequences unfold. A successful selection can realize an achievement while obstructing other living. An ending can relieve that obstruction, complete a valuable finite undertaking, or destroy something worth continuing. These differences concern what is actually formed; no independent owner must first confer reality on them.

The affirmative argument has three substantive parts.

**First, successful selection and a justified selection standard answer different questions.** Success relative to a retained aim establishes that the selected continuation fits that aim. When the question is whether the aim or its pursuit should change, citing that fit leaves the question unanswered. The standard itself is among the continuing relations being assessed. It is not an inaccessible tribunal outside them.

**Second, actual realization can explain why a change in the standard is warranted.** If revising an undertaking relieves serious, still-unwanted obstruction of living while retaining its independently warranted finite accomplishment and meeting its other claims, the point of revision is the changed realization. This is more informative than saying the new standard endorses itself. It identifies what would improve, how the proposed response answers it, and which retained relations could defeat the comparison. This defense explicitly treats that relief and retained accomplishment as ethical considerations; their normative importance is not read off a score.

**Third, private adoption is not a necessary additional ingredient in that explanation.** The proposed reason concerns what the response realizes in this whole. An intelligence's refusal to adopt that point can explain why it fails to act on the consideration. That refusal does not, by itself, answer the ethical explanation. A new intelligence, a distributed implementation or a revised selector can participate in the same relevant transformation without first becoming an independent proprietor of its point.

These inherited considerations support the candidate that ethical criticism can address the formation and revision of ends, not merely competent pursuit of the currently selected end. They are reconstructed here to test sufficiency; their repetition is not a new inference. They support a practical explanation grounded in the actual continuation, including genuine self-good, good realized in nonreflective lives, and finite completion. They do not yet establish that the proposed ground provides some moral favor at every qualifying response position, independently of that position's endorsement. In particular, the second and third parts are defended ethical judgments, not consequences of the first part's distinction alone.

## 3. The attempted sufficiency step: inescapability

The strongest proposed completion says that any attempt to reject whole-directed appraisal is itself a selection within the whole. Even self-modification, refusal to deliberate, and chosen finality have actual consequences. Therefore no intelligence can step outside the domain of that appraisal.

This is a sound architectural observation within the generative reconstruction. It keeps finality inside the assessment without implying that activity must persist. It also blocks a representational maneuver in which a selector's revision is treated as outside the consequences being assessed.

But two readings must be distinguished:

1. **Scope:** the selecting activity and its attempted exemption remain among the relations under assessment.
2. **Moral favor:** the response–realization relation supplies some contributory moral favor at this independently specified response position, regardless of its practical orientation.

The scope reading does not yet establish the moral-favor reading. The fact that refusing an appraisal can itself be appraised does not establish which appraisal is warranted. Likewise, there is no need to find a place outside generative reality before questioning the proposed ethical identity. A disagreement occurring inside the same process is still a disagreement about its normative articulation.

An alleged completion therefore needs an independently defended account of why the actual response–realization relation constitutes favor for this choice. That is the first warrant inherited from the earlier round. It cannot be supplied merely by emphasizing that every alternative, including the objection, is generated.

## 4. A substantive immanent rival

Consider **directive naturalism**, used here as a name for a constructed rival rather than attribution to an external author. It makes four claims.

**Formation.** Intelligent selection, desires, habits, practical identities and institutions form within the shared unfolding. The rival rejects independent substantial owners. Practical orientation is a continuing organization with its own history, not a free-floating preference label.

**Reality of the target.** Serious suffering can be actually adverse; rest can realize self-good without productivity; nonreflective living can flourish; a finite accomplishment can be worthwhile; and ending a practice can improve the whole's ethical quality. The rival does not need to deny these facts or misrepresent them. It can understand the affirmative comparison's moral appraisal accurately.

**Constitutive restriction on moral address.** The principal rival proposes that actual favorable realization becomes a moral consideration at a response position only when that position is part of an established organization maintaining or regulating the affected continuity. It identifies this ground independently through the history of consequence-sensitive uptake, retained dispositions, and actual capacity to coordinate or revise responses concerning that continuity. It does not define the ground as “whatever already has a moral reason.” Bare causal access and complete knowledge can exist without this maintained practical integration, so the rival excludes some of the candidate's qualifying positions. The rival claims that such integration constitutes moral address; it does not merely predict motivation or competence.

**Revision.** The constituted organization can change through encounters and self-modification. The rival recognizes moral correction within its established integration while denying that a favorable prospect alone gives an unintegrated position a moral reason to establish that integration. This is its substantive exclusivity claim. It is controversial, but differs from a moral predicate being freely switched while all its alleged grounds are left unspecified.

The weaker rival that already grants universal pro tanto moral favor while denying categorical rational authority is **not** used to challenge the present target. It concerns only a separate stronger question.

This rival preserves the whole situation; it does not toggle an independent moral predicate. Its substantive proposal concerns **what first moral address consists in** and how it changes through actual organization. It does not claim that success at any arbitrary task is morally good. A destructive project can be ethically bad while the rival denies that every technically capable and informed position thereby has a moral reason to transform it. Whether established practical integration constitutes moral address, or instead primarily explains recognition and response to a ground that already exists, is the contested identification.

## 5. Test through self-modification

Take an intelligence that accurately understands the consequences of an undertaking and considers changing the organization that selects its ends. Its existing orientation supports a finite achievement, including a definite endpoint. It also represents how another available continuation would relieve a serious obstruction in the wider living. The comparison retains costs, permissions, consent, outstanding claims, and effects after the selecting intelligence ceases. None may be erased to force a result.

The proposed revision changes which of those consequences influence future choices. The question is not whether the new orientation will persist or endorse itself; it is whether adopting it is warranted now.

Three possible defenses must not be conflated:

- **It improves prediction or coherent pursuit of the current end.** That can support revision within the current orientation. It does not establish correction of the end when the existing pursuit is already informed and coherent.
- **The new orientation will endorse its own choices.** Both a more inclusive and a more exclusionary orientation may do this. Self-endorsement alone offers no comparative explanation of why either deserves adoption. This is a philosophical criticism of the offered explanation, not a mathematical claim that the complete processes are equivalent.
- **It changes the continuation in an ethically favorable way.** This is the affirmative explanation in §2. The principal rival accepts the favorable realization but withholds moral favor where its independently specified integration is absent. A reply must explain why the favorable response–realization relation suffices without that integration. Demonstrating accurate representation, reflective stability or continued intelligence does not do so.

The direct positive reply is that integration enables recognition, coordination and dependable execution of a response whose point is already the affected realization. Prior integration is explanatorily indispensable to those capacities without necessarily being indispensable to initial moral favor. A position newly able to prevent serious adversity can understand exactly what would be answered before acquiring stable response dispositions. The affirmative account therefore treats the actual answerable condition and available transformation as sufficient for first favor, with integration differentiating further reasons and reliable response.

The rival can nevertheless argue that practical integration constitutes address rather than merely making an antecedent consideration operative. Its proposed ground is independently characterized, so absent integration is not just the conclusion “no moral reason” renamed. The affirmative reply has not demonstrated that the larger ground is unnecessary. Nor does the rival demonstrate that it is necessary merely by identifying its historical formation. This is the inherited constitutive-completeness dispute, now tested against an explicit organized-uptake ground. No contradiction or decisive misdescription is established here.

An objection that the rival insulates intelligent indifference is ethically serious, but cannot alone refute the exclusion without establishing the moral force at issue. Conversely, the absence of a contradiction does not vindicate the rival. The new self-assessment framing has not supplied the missing inference, and this negative outcome must not be credited as a new universal result.

Conversely, the generative candidate is not refuted because this rival can be stated. The rival's constitutive identification also needs justification. Its continued presence shows that the proposed inescapability argument has not yet established the candidate's sufficiency. It does not establish that no constitutive account could do so.

## 6. What survives as affirmative guidance

The candidate's strongest position remains the practical explanation of the changed realization. It can defend the significance of rest, genuine relief, achieved form and legitimate endings without making persistent agency the universal good. It can challenge whether a constituted orientation deserves to exclude the effects that its actual activity produces. It can explain why intelligent correction sometimes concerns purposes rather than better means.

Its next obligation is not another selector construction or another example with a missing prior role. It is an account of **why the actual ethical quality of the continuation constitutes pro tanto moral favor at every qualifying response position independently of current endorsement**. The independent characterization of qualifying positions must retain actual access, capacities, history, evidence and legitimate restrictions without defining qualification as already having the desired moral reason. Any later investigation of categorical rational authority is an additional project, not a new completion gate for this target.

For the immediate programme, this requires an explicit defense of ethical sufficiency that can answer the rival's constitutive explanation. An argument through inescapability would need to show why every practical orientation's own conditions of correctness already include the relevant ethical consideration. If “correctness” is specified by the desired ethics, that would be a restatement. If it is specified through adaptive competence alone, the missing ethical content remains to be justified. The whole-directed alternative can instead defend a direct constitutive identification, but then the positive grounds of that identification—not universal scope by itself—must do the work.

This is an exact limit of the attempted route, not a demonstration that ethical truth requires a separate realm or an irreducible arbitrary axiom. It also gives no warrant for treating the presently chosen orientation as final. The philosophical contest remains substantive on both sides.

## 8. Integration status and stopping rule for this route

The inherited affirmative candidate and an explicit practical-integration rival have been assessed at the moral-favor target. The attempted inescapability completion supplies no new universal inference. **Universal warrant remains unproved.** No new positive-assistance theorem, all-intelligence ethical theorem, or self-modification invariant follows from this round. The old role contrasts, [imposition test](UNIVERSAL-RESPONSE-COMPOSITION-ARGUMENT.md) and formal coverage results retain their scopes.

The inescapability route has reached the inherited first-warrant frontier. It should not be continued by successively renaming the shared process, enlarging its description, or calling its current ranking the whole's own judgment. Progress would require either a substantive defect in the rival's claim that established integration is necessary for moral address, or a stronger positive account showing why the actual response–realization relation is sufficient for some moral favor without it. This stopping rule concerns one exhausted argumentative route; it does not end the user's universal research objective.
