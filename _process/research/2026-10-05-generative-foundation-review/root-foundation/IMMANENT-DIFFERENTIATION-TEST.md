## 1. The exact affirmative route

The previous necessity argument can appear to leave a static organization waiting for a further law to move it. That is not the only admissible beginning. An actual operation may already include differentiation, retention, transformation and changed access. The account should start with that full occurrence where it is warranted, rather than remove its relations and then complain that a disconnected inventory cannot recover them.

The candidate tested here is therefore:

> Actual activity is internally differentiated. Its relatively enduring terms, their interaction and the distinctions through which the activity is known are articulations within it. The formation and continued operation of those relations can ground an explanation without an additional actor or causal connection outside the unfolding. Can this immanent differentiation itself establish the common process law's unrestricted foundational warrant?

This is our candidate argument, not a quotation or an already accepted doctrine. The final question retains the stronger scope. No definition of *system* is changed to make the answer automatic.

The independently identified target remains organized activity and how it changes subsequent conditions. It is not merely the occurrence of an assertion about activity. Existing controller and biological evidence already establish actual participation at their documented scopes. The present question is whether a general constitutive argument carries those grounded relations into the required common explanation.

## 2. New primary reading: the proposed passage is explicit

Nishida's *善の研究* offers a direct attempt. Part II.1 starts from immediate occurrence while questioning independently posited mind and matter. II.3 treats experience as active, rather than an aggregate of passive atoms. II.4 presents differentiation as development of an implicit whole and extends that form to nature through the claim that reality is not outside consciousness. II.5 moves from relational contrast and unity to necessary internal development; II.6 extends unity beyond a single episode. The 1936 preface retrospectively distinguishes the book's standpoint from his later account. This is a substantive grounding proposal, not merely a slogan about nonduality. It is also not identical with Ren's theory. [Primary text](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000182/files/946_74853.html); [preserved original](../../../../sources/nishida-zen-no-kenkyu/aozora-original.html); [provenance](../../../../sources/nishida-zen-no-kenkyu/PROVENANCE.json).

Reading scope: the prefaces, selected paragraphs of II.1, II.3, II.5 and II.6, and II.4 in full. This extends the repository's [earlier reading](GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#4-direct-presentation-can-be-a-ground-without-being-an-earlier-theorem), which concerned the prefaces and I.1. It does not assess all of Nishida's book or his later philosophy.

James provides a substantive comparison. In “A World of Pure Experience,” §§I–II, connections belong to experience as much as distinctions do, but kinds of connection differ. In “The Experience of Activity,” he distinguishes encountered activity from its causal attribution; further inquiry can relocate the activity attributed in an initial experience. He explicitly presents his methodological principles as postulates. He also gives parts explanatory priority over the whole; that stance is not adopted here. His distinction among connections tests the proposed universal inference while retaining real relations. It does not itself provide an assumption-free replacement. [Primary collection](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/32547/32547-h/32547-h.htm); [preserved original](../../../../sources/james-radical-empiricism/gutenberg-original.html); [provenance](../../../../sources/james-radical-empiricism/PROVENANCE.json).

Reading scope: essay II §§I–II and selected passages of essay VI corresponding to printed pp.158–170 and 181–188. These are philosophical arguments, not new empirical observations. The source editions are preserved byte-for-byte; no original-edition textual collation is claimed.

## 3. Reconstruct the strongest contribution before criticizing it

Three positive contributions survive the comparison.

**A beginning need not be an inert item.** Where activity is directly encountered or established through an operative history, its occurrence and connectedness are part of the ground to investigate. Replacing it with a list of independent elements would discard relevant content. A formal result showing that a bare carrier does not determine dynamics would then concern the weakened representation, not that fuller target. The [existing deeper-root results](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#3-distinction-underdetermines-dynamics) keep exactly this limitation.

**Distinctions can be formed within activity.** A retained contrast, changed response or acquired capacity can establish a differentiated role. The role need not belong to an independently constituted subject facing an independently constituted environment. Its scope comes from the relations that establish it. This applies both to distinctions acquired in understanding and to new differentiated organization; those two achievements must still be told apart. Recognizing an existing possibility is not automatically creating it, while a newly formed operation need not have been available earlier merely because the completed history can now describe it.

**An established operative connection does not need a second, unexplained connection behind it merely to count as operative.** If the acquired quantity actually participates in the calculation, explaining its acquisition, retention and use can explain that participation. Its having an explanation within one unfolding is not a reason to add a causal power outside it. Conversely, the claim of actual use still needs its grounds: a correlated record that was never read does not acquire that role through a description of the whole.

The third point retires a possible regress, not the stronger objective. It credits actual sufficient explanations of identified operations. It does not declare every felt connection correct or assume that a common conceptual schema already explains all organization. The [actual evidence assessment](GENERAL-FOUNDATION-EVIDENCE.md) remains the relevant achieved correspondence, and the [model-revision study](MODEL-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) already explains bounded correction when an attribution fails.

## 4. Where the proposed universal derivation adds content

The following is our analysis of the candidate's warrant, not a quotation or a claim that the cited authors use this terminology.

| Step being attempted | What the earlier ground supports | What needs an additional argument |
|---|---|---|
| Actual differentiation → relational articulation | The established differences and connections belong to the occurrence being explained. | That our present articulation is complete at every claimed scale. |
| Comparison of distinctions → a shared context | The comparison itself brings its terms into a real relation. | That this relation is the operative relation producing the compared processes, or determining their continuations. |
| Internal change → development of an implicit whole | Formation can be explained within the actual relations, including relations previously omitted by a narrow description. | That every later possibility or outcome is already fixed by one implicit completed whole. |
| The evidence for reality occurs within inquiry → a single universally applicable development form | Knowing is an activity within what is being investigated. | That its mode of presentation establishes the constitution of everything it can concern. |

The problem is not the absence of an external guarantee. It is a strengthening of the content attributed to the same relations. A shared comparison has actual content, yet it does not automatically identify a causal route between what is compared. A successful organized development supplies a realization, yet it does not show that every development has the same necessary form.

A charitable reconstruction need not claim that a completed future is encoded in advance. *Implicit* might instead mean that the present has capacities whose realization remains open and conditional. On that reading, the candidate better preserves stochasticity, failure, interruption and novelty. But its explanatory work then depends on identifying those capacities, their conditions and their transformations. The word *implicit* has not by itself derived them. This leaves a substantive route to pursue without claiming it is already completed.

The identification of all reality with that work's account of experiential activity cannot simply be inserted as a missing premise. This does not mean the contents of a private mind: the point is the proposed scope of the account, not a reinstated subject/world division. Nor can necessary self-development be treated as true because an encountered episode is unified. The [current root](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) does not require either identification: it admits stochastic, static, finite and destructive regimes. Testing this proposed route therefore does not disprove our root when the additional commitments are not adopted.

## 5. A genuine alternative at the same explanatory target

An alternative can retain the full established operation—its actual acquisition, uptake, alteration of shared conditions and subsequent effects—while treating wider continuations through the relations and constraints warranted for that situation. It can also include how the inquiry and its distinctions formed. It need not divide knowing from reality or deny that organization changes possibilities.

What it withholds is the claim that the unity of this occurrence proves a universal principle of necessary development. Withholding that inference preserves the target already established. It is consequently a serious test of the proposed route, unlike replaying a thinner output list or freely changing an unrelated verdict bit.

This alternative is compatible with much of the generative theory. That matters: it tests an inference offered to ground the theory, rather than pretending to be a discovered counterexample to every generative claim. Agreement on actual formation is a result to credit. The disagreement concerns whether that agreement itself warrants the additional universal conclusion.

The stronger reply would show how the actual operative relations ground the common law's causal and modal content, with an argument applicable at the claimed scope. Saying that the alternative already uses relationships establishes shared relational content; it does not yet provide that reply. Insisting that the alternative name an independently existing substrate would wrongly impose the dualism the method rejects.

## 6. Author counterreview

**Does this reject direct grounds because they are not deductions?** No. An occurrence or construction can provide a ground. The analysis credits connected activity rather than reducing it to a bare sensation. The issue is which wider content the ground warrants.

**Does it deny that the comparison itself connects what is compared?** No. It accepts that connection and asks what kind it is. Changing a description of a relation into a stronger causal or modal claim requires grounds within the same unfolding.

**Is the whole being reduced to a chosen local fragment?** No. The study tests whether the stated inference reaches the whole. It neither declares a fragment exhaustive nor demands that the whole be assembled from independently given atoms. Wider relations can correct the local account.

**Does the alternative deny the richer established target?** It expressly retains actual operative participation where established. Correlation alone, a copied record or a bare sequence is not substituted for that participation. Existing source-location and branching distinctions remain operative tests.

**Is nonduality equated with a consciousness-only ontology?** No. The primary proposal is assessed as a possible route, not adopted as the meaning of Ren's monism. The alternative's role likewise does not authorize replacing Ren's project with James's entire philosophy.

**Is “no extra causal glue” a new universal theorem?** No. It rejects an unsupported demand for a second relationship behind an already established relationship. It neither certifies the first attribution nor proves a general modal completeness result. Existing construction and transport proofs retain their actual hypotheses.

**Has the study changed the goal to fallible scientific practice?** No. It uses completed actual explanations as grounds and tests one argument for extending them. The assumption-free common foundation remains the requested end state. This assessment is progress toward it, not a declaration that a weaker goal is enough.

This is substantive author-conducted counterreview. No new independent-agent review is claimed, and the previous package's independent acceptance is not transferred to this study.

## 7. What changes in the next investigation

The route examined here can no longer be cited as if nondual presentation alone had derived the universal process law. Its positive contribution is retained: actual activity can supply connected ground, differentiated roles can arise within it, and an adequate local operative explanation need not invoke a separate causal layer.

The next specific test is **whether an independently identified operative relation can ground the modal restrictions attributed to it, and how that form of grounding earns the common law's scope**. This differs from deriving dynamics from a deliberately inert carrier, and from announcing that all actuality is internally connected. It must preserve stochastic and destructive cases and must distinguish capacities becoming realized from a presupposed completed outcome. The existing derived-domain and model-revision results are inputs, not mechanism gates to reopen.
