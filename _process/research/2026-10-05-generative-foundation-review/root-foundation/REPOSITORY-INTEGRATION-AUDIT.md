## What remains from V7.3 and earlier versions

The [coverage inventory](V73-AND-PREDECESSORS-COVERAGE.md) is still more extensive than the six-family organization of the canonical selection chapter and claims register. Priority additions include:

1. **Several compounding channels:** capacity, rule support, provision, sharing and diffusion have different effects; local instability, divergence speed and continuation value must remain distinct.
2. **Decisive deviation and response timing:** detection, authorization and execution can fail before effective correction arrives; the punisher can also seek a decisive position.
3. **Oversight topology and correlated failure:** serial versus parallel organization and common blind spots require their own reliability arguments. Reviewer headcount does not establish independent failure probabilities.
4. **Bounded objectives without actual stopping:** bounded success benefit need not bound resource acquisition as its cost falls. This predecessor result connects directly to the current inquiry-sufficiency frontier, but its economic mechanism is not the same as the learner's current rule.
5. **Private-type participation and correctable authority:** efficient outcomes, truthful reporting, voluntary participation and legitimate authority are different conditions. Retain the original information and incentive conditions.
6. **Whole institutional paths and nested selection:** compare capture, coercion, transition, fallback and continuation on compatible histories; connect selection of policies to selection of their reward-setters and institutions. Preserve the net-cost and transmission qualifications rather than assuming every safeguard is selected out.

Other inventoried connections include represented self versus material dependence, distinct reward-sharing and convertibility mechanisms, and local reform gradients versus implementable coordinated change. Some overlap current work and need precise cross-integration rather than a new theory label.

The earlier [Lean-interface audit](V73-LEAN-INTERFACES.md) remains the detailed starting point for missing discounted-return, capture-network, competence, strategic-communication and institutional-transition mathematics. The current targeted source review found no replacement establishing all these results. Existing structural trace, projection and continuation theorems do not supply those mechanisms' domain assumptions. Their eventual formalization must preserve the original statements and conditions; a convenient finite example is not a substitute.
