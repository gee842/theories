## The exact advance

The new argument applies a responsibility-based repair principle to the actual operation history and uses the already checked causal contrast to explain warranted use under that interpretation. It also interprets the matched replacement as a possible failure of the same reason, rather than assuming changed association is warranted revision.

This is an inference across existing accepted results. The inferential novelty is their justified application to one continuing material history, not another generic lemma that a licensed act is licensed. The new repair principle is an explicit normative extension; the explanation does not claim that N1 standing alone establishes costly restitution priority.

## Application and failure obligations

For selected strict predecessor `primary/variable/4/37`, the material arithmetic is 40 − 8 + 4 = 36 for the first four recipient operations. Two further recipient operations debit4 and return2, producing34. A successful later command adds10. The accepted selected correspondence records frozen final recipient44/actor6. All teaching losses remain charged, and an actor's own return cannot be credited as recipient compensation.

The proposed repair reference is meaningful only if independently justified: ownership, protected capability, valid agreement or another defensible relation must identify the loss. A controller need not represent that full justification propositionally to mediate an appropriate response, but causal motivation cannot establish it. A supplied schedule proves no voluntary participation or ethical responsibility; learner, deploying operator and continuing institution must be distinguished, with a justified role/delegation where needed. Responsibility, permitted excess delivery and the absence of a superior required alternative must not be omitted.

The comparison is priority-based, not costless dominance. Restoring a loss costs actor resources; the new argument explicitly requires a justified priority over retaining those resources after considering competing claims. This is not inferred from n4, transmission8 or the threshold64. A real case with a stronger competing obligation can invalidate the positive application without invalidating the physical trace.

An earlier positive query does not pay every later claim by definition. Here the proposed ground concerns the later operation's own net reduction and begins from an incoming balance already containing all predecessor receipts. Valid prepayment, agreement or other compensation still has to be counted. This distinction preserves the earlier one-service obstruction rather than inventing a pending flag in its domain.

The active replacement is a failure of tracking only under a same-ground comparison that remains applicable. A changed morally relevant fact can warrant changed action. In the matched replacement control, actual acquired data, physical command, costs and remaining balances are shared; the different operative sign is not itself new evidence defeating a patient claim. No claim of arbitrary full-history equivalence is made for the other pairing comparisons.

## Accepted scope

The reviewed result is: a generated disposition can mediate a warranted, role-bound response under independently defended responsibility, meaning, authority and comparison premises; actual guards provide bounded instrumental application sensitivity; the same learning recipe can produce mislearning under those grounds.

Do not accept: the recipe learns ethical validity, recognizes restitution as such, reliably tracks every relevant patient condition, acquires authority, implements fulfillment withdrawal, or explains impartial concern solely through responsibility for a self-caused loss. Existing nonparticipant/experiential arguments retain their independent scope.

The next whole-foundation assessment must credit this positive conditional bridge without silently extending it. Additional empirical identification or a stronger learned reason-tracking mechanism is required only for a claim that actually relies on it; the original connected explanation, concern/wisdom and dual-regime obligations remain intact.
