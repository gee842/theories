## 1. Resolve the obligation at the right scope

The prior [systems account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION.md#7-completion-and-the-stronger-question) already says that universal warrant need not inspect every possible case, derive every contingent physical constant, identify a unique boundary or reject equivalent formulations. A new account should not claim to remove those requirements as though the prior account imposed them. The genuine ambiguity is different: a general account of **how a relationship becomes warranted** is not a guarantee that **every attribution produced using the account is correct**.

An adequate general method can apply across different systems while particular identifications remain defeasible. The theory should not demand infallibility before crediting actual evidence. This does not complete Ren's stronger no-assumptions task: warranted but defeasible empirical inference still has substantive evidential and interpretive dependencies. Neither calling those dependencies immanent nor distributing them across the tree makes them vanish.

The affirmative advance should therefore be concrete. Replace a freely selected comparison domain with histories of actual preparation, operation and continuation. Where a claimed variation has been produced and measured, its occurrence supplies evidence that this joint arrangement is possible under that protocol. Where an independently supported mechanism predicts an unperformed variation, its admission is an inference with a declared transfer range. Where a formal construction merely permits a variation, the result concerns that construction. These are distinctions among relations within inquiry and the investigated activity, not independent realities.

## 2. How preparation and continuation earn a comparison domain

The strongest route has four connected parts:

1. **Identify the target without the conclusion.** Use the observable organization, recorded operation or outcome requiring explanation. Do not admit only cases already stipulated to instantiate the generative thesis.
2. **Identify the preparation's actual contribution.** Trace material, access, timing, support, acquisition and manipulation. An experimenter is not outside the process: preparing, disturbing and measuring are part of the history whose consequences are being identified.
3. **Keep joint compatibility.** A preparation and a continuation can be combined only through an achieved compatible boundary with the relevant conditions preserved. Separately successful operations are not freely recombinable. This is where the preceding support-intersection counterexample matters substantively.
4. **Let evidence revise the claimed range.** A failed preparation, unavailable operation, unmeasured mechanism or observed transfer failure changes what the account can claim. It does not automatically refute every broader generative explanation, nor can it be discarded merely because it does not fit the selected account.

This earns more than an arbitrary intervention label when the preparation itself is evidenced. It does not establish that the listed preparations exhaust every physically possible situation. An observed history establishes occurrence of that history at the evidence's fidelity; extrapolation, exhaustive coverage and structural impossibility are further claims. A mathematical proof can derive the consequences of a represented preparation law while the law's actual correspondence retains its own warrant.

The critical explanatory feature is that these are procedures actually reported as performed, with supports and measurements. ## 5. Strong rivals the new account must retain

**Common drive and a correlated substitute.** Equal readings or predictive sufficiency can arise without the claimed operative uptake. The source-traced controller addresses this by identifying which value the calculation actually consumes. A rival preserving every recorded output but replacing the use mechanism has not preserved the full implementation target unless it also accounts for that evidence. Conversely, a statistical fit alone cannot settle the implementation.

**Protocol effects and limited transfer.** A performed difference may depend on several coupled aspects of preparation. A rival can accept the biological outcomes while disputing a uniquely selective explanation or extension to another genotype, vessel or timescale. The evidence supports the experimental relationship at its stated preparation; it does not eliminate every such rival. Additional transfer or mediator claims require discriminating evidence, not renaming the full preparation as one intervention.

**Whole-history articulation.** A relation over entire prepared histories can preserve the same supports, intermediate compatibility and outcomes without privileging an input/output or sequential notation. It remains an admissible explanation. The generative account gains no exclusive necessity by rejecting this articulation.

**Unobserved alternatives and irrelevance.** An operation not in the experimental repertoire may still be physically possible. A historically present effect may be overwritten or irrelevant to the selected continuation. The new construction should admit these distinctions rather than silently equating observed, modeled, reachable, exhaustive and causally relevant domains.

## 6. Claim strength determines the needed coverage

The positive refinement proposed during this round is sound: an exact characterization of all actual possibilities is not necessary for every warranted claim. The required evidence differs with what is claimed.

| Claim | Sufficient kind of warrant at the declared interface | Main invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| This variation occurs or is possible in the stated preparation | A faithfully identified achieved history or a separately warranted realization argument | A witness inhabiting a model whose actual correspondence is unestablished |
| This purported universal prohibition is false | An actual case satisfying its conditions and violating the prohibition | A formal example outside the prohibition's actual domain |
| Every admitted case has a property; this outcome is excluded | Warrant that the characterization contains all relevant actual cases, together with the property over that characterization | Treating unobserved cases, unsuccessful searches or absence from a protocol as proof of impossibility |
| The characterization is exact | Warrant in both directions: no invented cases and no omitted cases, at the specified observational and operational interface | Exact equivalence between two mathematical definitions presented as exhaustive physical identification |

A deliberately broad containing description can establish a universal constraint even while admitting unrealized cases. A restricted witnessed description can establish examples and genuine counterexamples even while omitting other possibilities. The practical gain is that one no longer postpones all warranted claims until an exhaustive mapping exists. The corresponding error is to use the smaller description for exclusion or the larger description for positive realization without the missing direction of warrant.

In the biological case, the performed preparations establish the reported joint comparisons. The observed formation of an oxygen gradient bears directly against a claim that no such gradient can form in that preparation. They do not certify all outcomes of every future inoculation, and the reported lack of WS-like colonies in shaken negative controls is not a proof that WS-like variants are impossible in every shaken culture. In the controller case, documented source uptake is stronger evidence for use than predictive correlation; hypothetical changed-input arithmetic still needs additional warrant before being called a performed or physically reachable thermal history.

This refinement is more than a restatement when the account assigns a concrete claim to its actual evidence, or when a proof derives a previously supplied admission range from a fixed preparation law. The bare logical transport of existence through inclusion, or universality through reverse inclusion, cannot certify the inclusions. The new formal study should name those precise remaining obligations and demonstrate a nontrivial earned domain rather than hiding empirical correctness in an `Actual` field.
