# Review of general situated generative requirements

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored independent review of the [general argument](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-ARGUMENT.md), under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The [source assessment](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-SOURCES.md) records inherited technical comparisons and one focused primary philosophical reading. The mathematical inspection and validation scope are recorded below.

## 1. The general affirmative route

The proposed route can establish a common conditional practical structure. Where a specified continuation of life is justified, and its actual dependence is established, that same justification can bear on the activity sustaining or regenerating its necessary conditions. A separate ethical starting point need not be invented for every particular repair, transfer or maintenance act.

The state, action and condition distinctions used by the formalization describe different relations within one unfolding. They need not divide a preconstituted agent from an external world. Their adequacy nevertheless matters: a theorem about a represented reserve or renewal event applies to a real continuation only if the actual dependence is preserved by that representation.

The universal scope sought here is a reusable relation across every situation meeting its stated hypotheses. It differs from the unqualified claim that every possible system must grow, every intelligence must endorse a given end, or every mathematical law is a moral command. A descriptive law says how a represented process occurs. A situated requirement says which available activity can realize the justified continuation under that law.

## 2. Necessary relations and alternative implementations

If continuation requires an adequate restoring route, but several different routes can supply it, the requirement concerns their appropriate disjunction. It does not require every recipe. Nor does a sufficient construction become necessary merely because the current proof uses it. The earlier reset-based port inquiry is an example of why a particular successful schedule must not stand for every inquiry.

Necessity also has a time and scope. A support condition may have to recur, be restored before a boundary, or be replaced by another arrangement. This does not require retaining its value at every intermediate state. Consumption and formation can be parts of the supported continuation. A relation necessary for one component's continued existence need not be necessary for the wider continuation when replacement or a legitimate ending changes that component's role.

These qualifications make the proposed general principle usable; they do not weaken it to voluntary advice. Once an actual requirement is established at its proper scope, a purported realization that omits it cannot obtain the claimed continuation. The question is which requirement has actually been established.

## 3. Collective scope and the identification of renewal

A continuing collective history can require renewal somewhere without requiring every participant to perform every role, survive indefinitely or generate successors. Differentiated work, release, substitution and termination remain within that same history. The operational theorem does not allocate burdens or authority simply by quantifying over time.

A formal predicate named `renewal` also needs a substantive identification. If all ordinary transitions outside it descend in a well-founded relation, recurrence of that predicate can follow on an infinite history. This does not independently show that the selected events restore a represented capacity. A relation from the concrete condition change to the event is required; a genuine restoration witness or a general operational correspondence supplies stronger evidence than naming alone.

Correspondence can preserve several kinds of renewal. It need not identify every instance with a fixed numerical stock. Conversely, a local stock restored by consumption elsewhere cannot establish renewal of the full process if that source is silently omitted. Existing finite source obstructions remain genuine.

## 4. Possibility, control and actual progress

Three claims require different evidence: some successful continuation exists; a selected action has some successor from which success remains possible; every outcome allowed by the policy retains the required condition. The second does not establish the third. Where the relation admits uncontrolled outcomes, a favorable successor cannot silently be treated as the one selected by the acting organization.

A universal condition on allowed successors can also be vacuous when no successor exists. Enabledness or an actual-run hypothesis must remain visible whenever the conclusion concerns realizability. A proof about every supplied run does not by itself construct a run, establish nonempty policy choices, or guarantee indefinite physical execution.

Preserving an opportunity at each finite stage is also different from taking it. A policy can keep postponing a required event while leaving some alternative continuation available. A useful general sufficient condition must therefore include actual progress or a scheduling condition that rules out such postponement. The new proof must identify how its condition acts on the edges of the actual run, rather than replacing execution with a list of favorable options.

The earlier [continuation-renewal proof](CONTINUATION-RENEWAL-MATH.md) supplies a general descent interface. It assumes an infinite state history and an appropriate renewal-or-descent relation; it does not establish that such a history physically exists. Its repeating witness also shows why recurring renewal alone does not establish unbounded diversity or configuration complexity. These are retained scope boundaries, not reasons to discard the positive theorem.

## 5. The ethical inference and its temporal direction

The positive philosophical argument begins with justified continuation, not arbitrary adoption. When a response genuinely sustains a relation necessary for that continuation, its practical point can lie in the very living and formation whose continuation is justified. This gives a contentful account of necessary-means relevance within the whole. Encoding a separate moral field and proving that it follows from an assumed bridge would not add warrant to this explanation.

The argument still needs its actual scope. A reason favoring a continuation does not alone show that each means is permissible, that its costs are outweighed, or that a particular participant must supply it. If the justified requirement remains applicable in the full situation, its necessary implications cannot be omitted while claiming to meet that requirement. Whether it does remain applicable requires preserving burdens, existing responsibilities, feasible alternatives and the grounds of the requirement itself. Merely saying that a plan is coherent does not discharge those questions.

Temporal direction prevents a serious mistake. Warranted repair after damage does not establish that creating the damage was warranted because it makes subsequent repair possible. Producing the circumstance that generates a reason and contributing to its discharge in the current circumstance are different relations in one history. The full comparison retains both. A cycle of injury and repair cannot claim whole ethical improvement solely because its repair stages meet a local restoration condition.

A substantive rival can accept the full renewal dependence and the practical requirements of a warranted continuation while disputing its wider ethical scope, allocation of obligations or priority over another continuation. This is not an arbitrary ethical predicate switched in an impoverished model. Nor does the existence of that disagreement refute the general conditional result. It locates what that result does and does not settle.

## 6. Horizon correction preserved

The review does not reinstate intrinsic significance of guaranteed-never-used capacity as a required proof target. Present conditions can matter through possible later formation without a guarantee that every route will be exercised. Extending the horizon may improve identification of that dependence. It neither grants indefinite extension as an excuse for present losses nor proves the relevant future will occur.

The present general inference therefore concerns actual conditions, available responses and the continuation they support. It can be proved at its conditional operational scope while the substantive ethical ground continues to be defended and tested. Recognizing this scope is compatible with positive progress on Ren's general route; it does not substitute repeated local examples for the universal investigation.

## 7. Operational necessity: exact inspection

This reviewer read the complete [SituatedRenewal module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/SituatedRenewal.lean), its core `Process` and `Trace` dependencies and the earlier `ContinuationRenewal` descent result it invokes. No mathematical or semantic defect was found at the module's stated conditional scope.

`DependencyLaw` supplies, for each dependency, a provision route, remaining support, demand and addition. Its substantive accounting premise is that an actual transition retaining its route satisfies `remaining_after + demand ≤ remaining_before + added`. The inequality is a local model premise, not a conservation theorem derived from the core. `Response` means positive addition or changed route; it is neither an ethical predicate nor a sufficient condition of continuing life.

`window_account_or_response` composes every actual transition in a finite window. Either the inherited route remains and the initial support covers final support plus total demand, or a response occurs at a point whose incoming route is still the inherited one. `response_before_exhaustion` therefore derives an actual response when the window's demand exceeds its initial support. The actual intermediate states are retained, and `window_is_trace` connects that window to the core trace. There is no reset between segments.

`inherited_failure_requires_replacement` strengthens this conclusion when the original route cannot add support while it remains in place during the selected window. Under the same excess-demand and actual-window hypotheses, some actual transition departs from that route. This is a situated necessity theorem. It does not claim that every inherited arrangement must change or that every conceivable replacement would work.

The accounting constraint is conditional on route equality. On the transition that changes routes, the module deliberately does **not** certify the successor route's adequacy or the support account across that change. Positive addition also does not identify a physical source or prove whole-process improvement. Concrete applications must supply those relations. The theorem correctly identifies an unavoidable response under the declared dependence; it must not be reported as constructing a materially sufficient replacement.

`every_required_dependency_recurrently_responds` retains one common actual history and requires that, for each dependency and each starting point, some later finite window's demand exceeds its then-remaining support. Zero-demand steps and finite gaps are allowed. It concludes later responses for every such dependency; the events and contributors can differ. The bound theorem needs the stronger assumption of positive demand on every event. Its inclusive interval has `remaining(start) + 1` candidate transitions. Neither result requires every participant to produce on every step.

The idle witness has zero demand and no response. It correctly limits an inference from bare endless stepping to renewal; it does not purport to refute a substantive theory of living dependence. Route equality is decidable in the positive window theorems. The selected natural-number coordinates remain a particular accounting certificate, not a universal physical quantity or a common ethical score.

## 8. Situated implementation and joint continuation: exact inspection

This reviewer read the complete [SituatedContinuation module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/SituatedContinuation.lean), including its final composition theorem. No mathematical or semantic defect was found at the stated scope.

`HasContinuation` asserts an actual infinite state/action history satisfying a supplied state condition at every point. It does not assert a controllable strategy, a feasible decision procedure, or a physical guarantee. `actual_suffix_continues` preserves the state actually reached. Accordingly, `excludes_all_routes_cannot_sustain` proves that an action whose every admissible successor lacks such continuation cannot begin that same continued history. This impossibility does not require an enabledness premise: if the action has no successor, the proposed history is already impossible.

`joint_continuation_supplies_components` extracts each component condition from the same joint history. It does not infer a jointly feasible history from separately feasible component histories. `continuation_realizes_required_dependencies` likewise extracts one witness of `HasContinuation` and applies the indexed demand necessity to it. Its demand premise ranges over the admissible living histories at issue; it is not inferred solely from a predicate named `alive`. The output retains one history supporting all its indexed responses. Existence remains supplied in this theorem.

`rules_retain_invariant` and `certified_history_renews` impose their transition conditions across **all** admissible outcomes of an allowed action from the invariant. They therefore cover every actual compliant history, including nondeterministic outcomes. They do not silently select the favorable successor. They also do not assert that an infinite history or an enabled allowed action exists. The progress certificate requires either the identified renewal event or a strictly smaller natural rank on every such actual edge; this excludes indefinite postponement and gives a bounded renewal interval without a decision procedure for the renewal predicate. The rank is a waiting certificate, not a measure of value.

`implemented_rules_sustain` separately closes that existence gap with supplied functions `choose` and `next`, a certificate that they give an actual allowed transition at every invariant state, and an initial invariant state. Explicit iteration constructs the history without a choice axiom. This is an existence result from an implementation, not automatic policy synthesis, guaranteed control of every physically possible outcome, or proof that the chosen functions can be learned from partial observation.

The continuing `alive` condition follows from the supplied implication `Inv → alive` and preservation of `Inv`. It does not follow from recurrence alone. The theorem thus has both a condition-preservation certificate and an actual-progress certificate. A representation of humanity, life or a concrete organization must justify those premises at its own scope.

The two restoration histories provide separate adequate recipes: one always repairs and never replaces; the other always replaces and never repairs. They establish the real logical difference between a required disjunction and a uniquely necessary recipe. Their elementary transition law does not model a material source, unbounded novelty or biological renewal. The waiting history separately proves that an achievement can remain actually available at every stage without occurring. That counterexample concerns execution, not the intrinsic value of a capacity stipulated never to be used.

## 9. Philosophical assessment and validation record

The affirmative argument's §§4–6 preserve the distinction between a positive reason, an all-things requirement and first ethical warrant. Its response relation includes feasibility, actual contribution, timing, burdens and whole consequences. It does not manufacture ethical authority from an arbitrary intention or a new moral predicate. The source comparison identifies a genuine philosophical problem of necessary-means transmission; it does not certify our bridge by authority.

One requested correction was made during review: the argument's table now separates a proved necessary disjunction from a set of sufficient constructions. Several known adequate routes do not show that the list is exhaustive or that membership in that list is required. No further philosophical revision was requested.

The new operational results advance the universal investigation by making the general continuation-to-situated-requirement inference precise under explicit dependence and implementation premises. They do not establish why every intelligence is ethically bound to favor the selected continuation, which participant owes which response, or the adequacy of the premises for all living organization. Those are substantive grounding and correspondence questions within the same process; they are not refuted merely by freely changing a moral predicate in a weaker model.
