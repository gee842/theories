## An actual trial changes admission

[AdmissionRevision.lean](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/AdmissionRevision.lean) imports the unchanged reproduction network. Four roles have activation threshold two, stock capacity three and one unit of wear per cycle. The separate reciprocal arrangement returns two units to each operative partner; withdrawing returns supplies none. The existing physical cycle consumes a revision unit, accounts for support conversion and replaces the four carriers, with four parts and eleven material units arriving per cycle.

Previously, the supported admission rule always redirected withdrawal to the separate arrangement. The new learner starts with no installed loss estimate and no pending observation. From stocks (3,3,3,3), an actual withdrawal trial leaves (2,2,2,2). It records the before and after readings, consuming one material unit. A subsequent learning event reads that retained pair, installs the coordinate-wise observed loss and consumes another unit. It cannot learn before a record exists. The reserve moves from 11 to 21 through the trial, then to 20 through recording and 19 through criterion installation. No event creates its evidence by declaring a proposed criterion correct.

The criterion permits withdrawal only when every current stock is at least **two plus its acquired loss estimate**; otherwise it selects the separate return arrangement. Current-stock access for this guard is supplied and exact. The trial-reading channel below can be biased even while this current-stock access is accurate. Cycle accounting covers the stipulated revision/control event, not measured CPU or sensing costs. The operation also requires the represented checking organization still to be established.

With faithful trial readings, the acquired loss is one. The rule now permits withdrawal at stock three and restores returns at stock two. `conservative_loss_preserves` proves preservation for every established stock vector whenever every acquired estimate is at least one. `trial_learning_and_reproduction` joins the actual trial, record, learning and every finite continuation in one generative-core trace. Each later operation refines to the existing physical revision-and-operation events; its carrier generation advances accordingly.

`alternating_stock_for_all_time` proves that the faithful continuation has stocks two at every even time and three at every odd time. This is a temporal organization of support, not a permanently fixed set of active return routes. After two continuation cycles its material reserve is 31, compared with 23 under two always-return cycles from the same post-learning installation. That finite material comparison neither proves long-horizon optimality nor assigns ethical superiority. The two histories also have different intermediate stocks and resilience margins.

The trial can be performed safely because the initial stock margin is three. The learning theorem does not authorize arbitrary experiments at threshold two. Generalization from this trial depends on the supplied stationary one-unit withdrawal law and the operative fallback. This model acquires an estimate and a resulting admission threshold; it does not invent an unrestricted model class or choose its own normative target.

## Misleading evidence can disable further checking

The alternative trial-reading law reports `min(3, actual stock + 1)`. It is an explicit saturating offset, not a Boolean declaration of falsehood. Actual stocks three and two both report three. The retained pair therefore produces estimated loss zero.

At the actual post-trial stock two, the installed zero-loss criterion permits another withdrawal. This is an enabled, charged event in the same learner process. Stocks become one. Material and replacement parts remain sufficient, but the established checking organization is gone; `damaged_criterion_cannot_operate` proves that the next learned operation is disabled. The model does not silently keep executing a disembodied decision rule after its represented support fails. The biased monitor first changes its report to two when actual stock is already below the operating threshold.

Accurate current-stock knowledge did not save this rule: it tested an inaccurate estimate of the consequence of withdrawing. This is a specific mechanism by which a system can slide out of recoverable checking while its reported trial result and material reserve look favorable. It is not a universal warning signal or a theorem that every downward stock change is harmful.

The failure has a scoped repair. Adding a warranted one-unit error allowance restores the conservative condition, and continued preservation is checked. The allowance is an additional justified bound, not something inferred from the saturated readings themselves. Existing bounded-fault and model-revision work supplies other conditional repairs. These successes prevent this single fault from being misrepresented as the final wall for all learning.

## The inference wall

A separate pair isolates the remaining identification problem. In both physical alternatives, selecting the separate return arrangement executes exactly the same cycle. In one, the withdrawal command actually removes the returns. In the other, that command leaves a bypass return arrangement operating and accounts for its material use through the same physical cycle. The difference concerns enacted support routes, not a freely toggled value judgment.

`protected_histories_identical` proves equality of the **entire installation history for every time** under continued separate-return selection, including stock, material, parts, carrier generation and installed arrangement. Nevertheless, from stock two the untried withdrawal loses the established region in the coupled alternative and preserves it in the bypass alternative. `no_protected_history_classifier` proves that a function of that selected history alone cannot classify both correctly. The full history is deliberately stronger evidence than a real observer usually obtains.

This reuses the established logic of observational nonidentification, now at the criterion's own selected repertoire. It is not a novelty claim and does not say that all possible interventions fail. A discriminating trial, route inspection or a warranted restriction on the physical alternatives can change the conclusion. The earlier diagnosis-and-repair construction already executes such discrimination under declared margin and resource conditions.

**The wall reached is an invalid inference: recurrent operation and agreement with its selected evidence do not establish that the criterion remains adequate under untried changes.** More time on the same restricted history does not supply the missing distinction. The open connection is how an organization acquires and maintains warranted coverage of relevant effects, common faults and feasible challenges while its selection pressures also change those testing relations. Replacing this requirement with “trusted evidence,” “independent reviewers” or “the correct criterion” would assume the missing explanation.

This is a scoped theoretical wall, not a demand for field data before any more theory. Specified architectures, revisable fault envelopes and actual interventions support further conditional research. No impossibility of self-correction, universal generative explanation or normative justification follows. Nor does the theorem undermine the completed G0/J account: a criterion's operational availability and its evidential adequacy are implementation questions; whole ethical appraisal retains the reasons already established and the actual effects those reasons concern.

## Primary comparison and substantive counterreview

Fudenberg and Levine's [Self-Confirming Equilibrium (1993)](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/sce.pdf), introduction pp. 523–525, already distinguishes beliefs supported along selected play from beliefs about unvisited contingencies. It explains why observation and experimentation assumptions matter. This supplies the established comparison for the selected-history limit; our role network is not their game model and proves neither equilibrium learning nor a new equilibrium concept. The preceding reproduction study's autocatalysis and institution-maintenance comparisons retain their scope. The repository's [partial-compromise study](FAULT-TOLERANT-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md#7-primary-comparisons-and-author-counterreview) already credits quorum and enforcement methods, including actual common-fault coverage and protected implementation. We reuse those results rather than rename a new guardian as a solution.

The author counterreview changes the interpretation in five ways:

1. **Is the criterion really acquired?** Its numerical loss estimate comes from retained trial readings and changes the actual next operation. Its update algorithm, preservation target and fallback are supplied. This is bounded acquisition, not the origin of every standard or motive.
2. **Is temporary withdrawal itself destructive?** Only in the wrong phase or without the required support. The checked alternating history is a positive counterexample to requiring every return route at every step. Organization includes timing and recoverable margins.
3. **Does abundant material imply successful correction?** The biased run retains material and parts yet disables the checking roles. Support must reach the relevant activity; an aggregate reserve does not establish that relation.
4. **Does one bad sensor prove universal impossibility?** No. A justified error allowance repairs this case, and the existing fault results cover declared deviations. The wall concerns inferring that coverage from recurrence or the restricted evidence alone.
5. **Does the model establish ethical adequacy?** No. Maintaining these four roles can support justified checking or an oppressive arrangement. The existing G0/J comparison of actual living effects remains necessary; the model does not make its floor an ethical axiom.
