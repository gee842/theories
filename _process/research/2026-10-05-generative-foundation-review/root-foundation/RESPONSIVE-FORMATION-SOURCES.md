## 3. Our reconstruction: forming the pathway is consequential

The following is our analysis, not a further doctrine attributed to Wang.

The current generative proposal should explain more than the existence of complementary capacities. Two people may have controls that could jointly relieve a burden, yet lack a reliable way to coordinate their exercise. An invitation, exchange of evidence or agreed sequence can change that situation. The responsive relation is something they form through actual interaction; it is not already present because an analyst can imagine their combined action.

The comparator identifies a useful contrast within that formation process. Communicating an accurate concern does not guarantee that the approach makes a sound response accessible. An intervention may alter trust, attention, available deliberation and the meaning of participation. A process that begins with helpful intent may thereby undermine its proposed target. The historical passage supplies a philosophical example of this possibility, not measured evidence of an effect in our case.

Accordingly, the explanation should retain three separate questions: is the proposed transformation warranted; can these participants actually form an effective relation; and does the manner of approaching them preserve the relevant authority and other stakes? Improvement on one question need not improve the others. A manipulative invitation may recruit efficiently while corrupting what makes the response acceptable.

## 4. Correction must be able to change the proposal

A stronger responsive relation permits more than feedback about how to obtain agreement. Evidence from the affected participant or prospective contributor may show that the proposed outcome is mistaken, the means are unsafe, the authority is absent, or another route is preferable. Correction must be able to change or terminate the plan, including correcting the person who initiated it.

This does not make every refusal or report infallible. The relevant response may require further inquiry into misunderstanding, conflicting evidence or serious risk. Nor does disagreement by itself show that the invitation was coercive. The point is that the interaction must not count only information useful for securing the initiator's original end.

| Proposed comparison | What it distinguishes |
|---|---|
| Accurate information with room to question or decline | Formation that remains answerable to its actual conditions |
| The same information with humiliating pressure | Whether the approach damages uptake or adds a competing injury |
| Pleasant persuasion based on false benefit claims | Acceptance without warranted direction |
| Reliable counterevidence that changes or ends the proposal | Revision of the target or route, rather than compliance optimization |

These are conceptual controls for the next study, not completed findings. They preserve the adverse episode and distinguish its genuine relief from participant agreement, public harmony or a successful team formation.

## 5. Metaphysical and normative limits

Wang's account treats cultivation as realizing and clearing an already authoritative capacity. Our project must explain how actual response capacities form and how their judgments become warranted. A modern mechanism for openness or feedback would not prove his stronger identity of moral knowing and heaven-endowed nature. Equally, treating his account as nothing but useful persuasion would discard what it claims to explain.

The source also begins with established human relations and responsibilities. It cannot by itself establish a stranger's reason to enter a new coordination. The present positive proposal remains that a feasible, appropriate formation can be favored by the genuine relief it would enable. Its ground is that prospective transformation, not organization for its own sake. A coordinated harmful project can satisfy the same mechanical conditions.

The resulting constraint is concrete: a generative account of concern must explain **formation that remains correctable about its purpose**, alongside differentiated participants and consequences. Removing an ultimate owner does not remove the need for that differentiation. Whether this form of responsiveness is constitutively warranted by adversity remains the central ethical question.
