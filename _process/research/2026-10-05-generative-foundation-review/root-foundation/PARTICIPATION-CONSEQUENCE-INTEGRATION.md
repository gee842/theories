## What the empirical comparison adds

The [primary-source study](PARTICIPATION-CONSEQUENCE-COMPARATORS.md) examines a living network in which interaction changes the material that subsequently carries flow, alongside a bodily-extension study that adjusts effective movement. These supply distinct tests for structural retention and changed range. They do not instantiate the repository's program or establish that all relevant capacities have been learned.

The central memory interpretation faces a published primary criticism concerning evidence of experience-dependent retrieval. The comparison preserves both the measured remodeling and the remaining causal identification question. A response governed by present conditions can differ from one governed by a retained history; a stateful physicochemical mechanism can itself realize the latter. Requiring a separate nonphysical controller would misdescribe the proposed explanation, while naming physical hysteresis memory cannot settle stronger claims of understanding.

The resulting test is more demanding and more appropriate than demanding two identical organisms: trace distinct prior encounters into retained organization, compare specified later opportunities without conditioning away the proposed memory, and establish how the retained change mediates later activity. Preserve alternative explanations from residual stimulation, changed energy, injury and pre-existing flexible responses. These are prospective identification criteria, not interventions performed in this round. The next step must preserve that achievement and investigate **how relevant access, target differentiation and the acquisition practice itself form and become revisable through their consequences**, while connecting delivered support to an affected organization's actual continuation where that is claimed.

The last clause is consequential. A resource number can change without the representation showing its use, restoration or renewed dependence. A learner can obtain correct evidence about another target without private incentives favoring faithful uptake. Both facts belong inside the same generative account; neither authorizes inserting a benevolent criterion or declaring ethical significance external to reality. The [parallel practical-boundary inquiry](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-INTEGRATION.md) studies warranted revision of the practice producing consequences and retains its separate ethical obligations.

The earlier proof that a given abstraction preserves a declared scope remains intact; it is not a claim that this scope exhausts learning or life.
