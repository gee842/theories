## Finding and contribution

The strongest useful next result is **selective correction under an identified dependency**, rather than another demonstration that an arrangement can change. A maintained application connects an acquired contact, its target and context, an inference about another operation, and that operation's actual effects. A failed application can defeat this combined inference while leaving the sensory model, earlier reports and a previously completed activity intact. It need not identify which of the contributing relations failed.

The relay comparison gives a substantive example: two actual histories preserve the same undertaken full-load transfer, original learner and recorded diagnostic pair, but failure occurs through different physical relations. This is not an arbitrary change to a predicate called `Correct`. Its useful contribution is the precise localization of what the established record does and does not support. The general information argument is inherited, not a new theorem about the origin of correction.

The governing conditional candidate has already been accepted at its [fixed completion scope](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate). A further local construction is warranted when it closes this identified interface, not because every demonstrated controller must acquire another controller above it. This round does not retract that acceptance or complete the stronger no-assumptions goal.

## Exact conditional scope, without a new outside scorer

Let H be an admitted nonempty family of actual histories, containing the actual history, and E their retained available evidence. Let d(h) identify the disputed physical relation, where that relation is independently specified. Exact diagnosis from E exists only if, and in the finite setting exactly if, d is constant on each evidence class. The necessity follows because a function of the same input has the same output. Sufficiency assigns the common diagnosis to that class.

For correction, replace d by D(h), the set of currently executable responses that meet the same independently specified continuation contract, including their effects and relevant prefix conditions. One common evidence-based response exists exactly when each reached evidence class has a nonempty intersection of its D(h). A response means the same operation or implemented contingent policy, not a different hidden-state-dependent plan with the same desired outcome label. Empty compatibility is model mismatch, not vacuous permission.

These are applications of [T13](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#t13-exact-sufficiency-for-permission-and-for-choosing), the [outcome-summary factorization result](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/OutcomeContinuation.lean) and [dynamic inquiry sufficiency](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md). They should not be counted as newly discovered general principles. Inquiry changes the current configurations and available responses, so its successors must be propagated before recomputing adequacy. A static shrinking set of old world labels is not sufficient.

There is substantive constitutive content at the stated scope: under faithfully bound opposite outcomes and a stable deterministic application relation, a claim identifying both conditions with one response cannot remain true. The accepted refinement study realizes that incompatibility and its operative replacement while preserving H110. No separate reward score is needed to make incompatible predictions incompatible. But identifying the contact and target, justifying stability, forming the replacement and judging the wider undertaking are additional matters within the same activity. This does not derive every corrective standard or ethical reason from logical consistency.

## Rivals and the constructive interface

The relay pair **does not** establish disjoint adequate repair sets. Both failures have full-load law `00`: neither present relay setting delivers the load. Both also end with the last source packet consumed. Unique diagnosis would not itself produce an executable successful transfer. An altered supported mechanism, a later source or a different undertaking would be a changed continuation, not a fact already established by these cases. The purpose-correction no-creation result applies directly to any mere filter of the unchanged possibilities.

A common adequate response can sometimes make fault identification unnecessary. Abstention or suspension must be assessed against the actual undertaking; it is not automatically successful load delivery. Conditional on acquiring final failure, a common justified epistemic response here is to withdraw the guarantee from this diagnostic pair to this full-load outcome, retaining the historical reports and independently supported earlier claims. This is a consequence for an interpretation, not a newly implemented withdrawal rule or a demonstration that the load was fulfilled.

The smallest separating observation for these two fixed source histories is independently established access to the actual weak-on response: it is one in `calibration_break` and zero in `false_dependence`. Repeating the same weak test through the same deterministic faulty contact does not separate them. A faithful final-load observation distinguishes success from failure, but not those two causes. Neither observation alone proves a complete unrestricted model of future faults or excludes simultaneous defects.

A future positive continuation should therefore identify its contact's physical relation and support, bind the observed event and context, distinguish at least these retained alternatives, and exhibit the acquired result changing a later application or inquiry. It should preserve H and the earlier completion unless new evidence actually addresses them. If a common adequate response is available, that remains a legitimate rival. If the capacity to acquire or act has been exhausted, preserving uncertainty and the actual boundary is preferable to manufacturing another favorable suffix. These requirements identify a specific explanatory interface, not a mandate for endless certification of every premise.
