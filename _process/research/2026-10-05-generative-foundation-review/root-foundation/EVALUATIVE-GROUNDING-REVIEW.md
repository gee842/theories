## The positive inference and its exact strength

The argument connects two separately supported claims:

1. An independently warranted patient-resource relation, responsibility history, adequate permissible remedy and complete priority comparison establish a reason for this actor to perform this repair.
2. The actual paid plasticity history forms an initially absent evaluative connection, which works with separately acquired command knowledge to produce that same action.

The resulting conclusion is that a **generated disposition mediates a warranted role-bound response under those application conditions**. This is more than noticing a favorable endpoint: the active, inert and filtered contrasts identify the new connection's contribution while preserving acquired facts and teaching bills. It is less than proving that the actor understands restitution as restitution, that the associative recipe tracks moral reasons generally, or that its rule is selected because it is morally reliable. The draft preserves these distinctions.

The connection learned here is operative evaluative influence. The command's efficacy was learned earlier. The paper correctly avoids describing the new link as newly acquired efficacy, a moral proposition, or a proof of the repair principle.

## Does the comparison premise assume the conclusion?

The priority premise already does substantial normative work: it supports repair over retention when no sufficiently weighty competing justification defeats that priority. It must not be represented as a consequence of the sign, n value, threshold or subsequent service. The drafts explicitly call it a normative extension and do not claim that standing alone establishes costly restitution priority.

This is not formally circular. The responsibility, entitlement, remedy and competing-reason criteria are specified independently of which action the learner selects; they can condemn a learned output or decline to license it. The separate causal derivation then establishes that this formation history realizes the independently recommended act. The inferential addition is the actual generated mediation and its identified failure regime, not a second derivation of ethical priority from a physical ledger.

The limit matters: the conditional argument does not establish that the synthetic recipient really has the proposed entitlement, that the excess service is wanted, or that all competing claims have been surveyed in a real application. Those are substantive applications to establish, not empty terminology and not new requests to build another controller. If they are simply asserted in a particular case, that case has only a stipulated ethical interpretation. The document instead makes them explicit obligations and offers reasons for the bounded repair principle.

A local explanation may use independently defended normative premises without deriving them from bare causal difference. It may also use declared component laws without generating each law through an infinite prior apparatus. It cannot silently describe either premise as a newly acquired capacity. The inspected drafts respect that boundary.
