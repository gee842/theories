# Potential continuation and mathematical quantification

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored affirmative argument following the [induction investigation](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-INDUCTION-ARGUMENT.md), incorporated in the [potentialist integration](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-POTENTIAL-INTEGRATION.md). The target remains independent mathematical admission and the root's necessary constitutive role. This note follows the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Its philosophical proposal is distinct from the checked conditional mathematical correspondence.

## 1. A third way to understand collective use

The previous investigation separated permission for arbitrary finite constructor formation from admitting its products as one exhaustive domain. It left open a further possibility: perhaps arithmetic quantification need not require a stage at which that whole domain is present. It can concern what remains available through possible finite continuation.

The strongest affirmative proposal takes the common meaning of a constructor seriously. A repeatable counting operation is understood through its applicability to a formation of the declared kind, not through a list of occasions on which it has already occurred. A mathematical assertion can similarly govern any continuation presenting the relevant case. On this account, unbounded generality articulates the continuing scope of a rule; it does not describe an additional event that assembles all numbers simultaneously.

This proposal offers more than a new name for an infinite collection. It changes where quantification operates. An existential assertion can request a witness at some possible later stage. A universal assertion can impose its condition on every relevant object whenever a later stage makes that object available. The distinction between these demands is retained when they occur inside another assertion.

No historical or physical completion of infinitely many acts is required by this interpretation. Nor does it claim that every actual activity can continue indefinitely. It abstracts the formation relationship appropriate to counting from the conditions that may interrupt a particular realization. That abstraction takes place within the same unfolding. Its fidelity and its permission to disregard particular interruptions require explanation; mathematical and physical possibilities are not declared two independent realities.

The previous investigation already rejected physical completion as a requirement. The new work tests a particular interpretation of collective quantification; it does not claim to remove an objection that the earlier argument endorsed.

## 2. Why the continuation reading has a positive rationale

Consider what would count as understanding the proposed rule. Knowing how it behaved on a few stored examples would not yet specify its use on a newly formed example. The mathematical understanding attributes a common formation role to those cases. Its explanatory work is precisely to support a continuation that was not separately listed.

The proposed warrant for modal generality is therefore **continuation uniformity**: where a mathematical operation is specified by a common formation relationship, a change in the stage at which that relationship is presented does not itself change the operation's applicability. This is a philosophical account of the intended mathematical role, not an inference from observed repetition. It can justify treating an unlisted finite continuation as an instance of the same subject.

This also explains why the past history of actual construction need not fix the quantifier's range. A case may be relevant because its formation is licensed, even if no person ever produces a token of it. The common rule supplies an account of its relevance. The proposal does not demand a separate finite description of every possible higher mathematical object; the present argument concerns the constructor-generated arithmetic role only.

The force of this rationale has a boundary. Uniformity warrants extending a use under specified conditions. It does not automatically justify every proposed account of what is possible, establish that nothing relevant can become inaccessible, or settle the truth of every assertion about all continuations. In particular, an assertion's finite expression and a rule's finite description do not by themselves explain the full scope of classical negation and nested quantification.

The affirmative case thus distinguishes **a permission to make the next suitable use**, **the continuing scope of that permission**, and **a determinate classical truth condition across the entire permitted range**. The first can be exercised locally. The second is defended through the common mathematical meaning. The third is the stronger commitment still under investigation.

## 3. What must be preserved

The technical comparison uses relational first-order syntax. Its existential quantifier is interpreted as an existential witness at an accessible later stage; its universal quantifier examines every accessible later stage and each object available there. Classical connectives retain their declared interpretation. Atomic relationships have the same specified meanings wherever their parameters are available.

Relations are important here. A finite arithmetic stage should not silently be treated as an ordinary algebra already closed under every total arithmetic operation. Successor and other arithmetic operations can instead be represented by their graphs, with a later stage providing an output. This is an interpretation of a declared relational language. It is not yet a formal translation of every function-language foundation, a proof system, higher-order arithmetic or all mathematics.

The comparison must also follow nested assertions. It is insufficient to show that every relevant object can occur somewhere after one preferred starting point. An assertion evaluated after a choice of continuation must retain access to the witnesses required from there. The proposed formal contract consequently requires access to every ambient target element from every stage, together with growing availability and fixed atomic meanings. These are supplied semantic conditions to test, not conclusions already obtained from the root.

An unavailable branch is therefore a useful diagnosis of a weakened contract. It is not a refutation of the richer potentialist account, which specifically requires the relevant future opportunities to remain available. Conversely, adding that requirement to a model does not establish why it is justified. Both directions matter to the nondual assessment.

## 4. The mathematical test and its exact promise

The checked [PotentialTruth module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/potential/formal/PotentialTruth.lean) proves `potential_iff_global`: ordinary truth in a supplied ambient relational structure agrees with the translated interpretation for every formula of the declared grammar, with available starting parameters. Its natural-number-prefix instance has finite individual stages and no full stage. The sentence that every number has a larger number holds under the potential interpretation. Its current-stage interpretation is false at every positive stage; the empty stage makes it vacuously true.

The checked [CoverageBoundary module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/potential/formal/CoverageBoundary.lean) sharpens the requirement. Coverage from every stage is equivalent to existential agreement for **all ambient unary predicates**, not necessarily to truth agreement in one fixed language. A branching example reaches every Boolean value from the start, yet its nested assertion that every value has a different value disagrees with ambient truth even at that start. Compatible joint extensions supply one sufficient route from global coverage to the required future coverage.

These results remove the need for a **single stage containing the entire target domain** from this semantic interpretation. They do not remove the ambient domain from the proof's metatheory, establish a finite truth-decision procedure, or justify the stage frame independently.

This limitation is not an argument that potentialism is impossible because a classical model of it uses infinite resources. A formal model can explain a proposed interpretation without claiming to constitute it. A modal foundation may instead treat continuation principles as basic and use rules for them directly. The substantive question is whether the generative account warrants those principles and their interpretation, rather than merely whether a conventional metatheory can represent them.

## 5. Where the first-warrant question moves

The strongest version of the proposal treats whole-range modal assertions as expressions of one continuing formation relationship. There need be no extra bearer collecting its instances. This is a genuine explanatory option that the earlier contrast between individual acts and a completed collective domain did not exhaust.

Its principal commitment is now clearer: the admitted constructor relationship has an unrestricted, stable scope of possible finite continuation, and classical claims across that scope have determinate truth conditions. A finite-stage semantics can test what follows from this commitment. It cannot independently supply it merely by quantifying over all stage indices during the proof.

For the present first-order comparison, the target's arbitrary predicate collection is not installed as an additional object sort. That does not explain the full higher ranges needed by the universal mathematical request. Saying that a predicate might become available later raises its own admission question: which predicates are possible, with what identity and what common range? The earlier [family-warrant assessment](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-FAMILY-WARRANT-REASSESSMENT.md) still applies. A shift to possible availability must not silently reinstate unrestricted compatible completion as an earned premise.

A constructive or verification-based account could decline some whole-range classical claims. Such a proposal deserves assessment on its own subject, but changing the target's logic without a correspondence would not complete the current classical reconstruction. The proposed potentialist route is useful precisely because it makes preservation of the target an explicit test.

## 6. A matched alternative and the stopping condition

A structural-modal account can retain the same formation rules, every stage and extension, stable identity, all translated target truths, and the actual development of mathematical practice within one unfolding. It locates entitlement in the structural meaning of possible finite extension. The root account seeks to explain that entitlement through generative organization. This is a substantive difference in explanatory priority, even where their mathematical descriptions coincide.

The generative proposal's advantage is its account of why unperformed instances remain related to actual rule-governed practice. The rival can accept those relationships while denying that the current articulation of the root is uniquely necessary to explain them. To decide between them requires an argument about the modal entitlement itself, or a demonstrated failure in the rival's account of the same content. Equivalent translations do not settle that priority.

The present route can therefore earn a conditional removal of simultaneous infinite-stage availability and a clearer defense of quantification through continuation. It has not yet independently discharged the whole modal-range commitment or established exclusive root dependence. The precise next warrant question is whether generic constructor meaning justifies the required unrestricted modal principles and classical generality, without presupposing their entitlement in the meaning explanation.

Another stage recoding with the same supplied range would not advance that question. A substantive next step must defend one disputed modal principle from already warranted content, or show that preserving the mathematical subject forces the proposed generative explanation rather than only its relational consequences. The investigation should stop this particular route if it merely alternates between calling the same supplied range a domain, a rule or a possibility space.
