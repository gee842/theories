# How far does the necessity argument reach?

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [scientific compatibility](SCIENTIFIC-COMPATIBILITY-NECESSITY.md), under [Ren's instruction to push further](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-knowledge-content-continuation.md#pushing-the-necessity-argument-further). This study follows the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). It adds a more exact compatibility result, corrects an unnecessarily strong ethical rival test, and assesses the affirmative route from actual knowing and living relationships to generative value. It is an author-conducted argument and counterreview, not independent-agent acceptance.

**The advance:** G0 need not defeat every pluralist theory of value. Basic positive significance is not exclusive grounding. A rival must contradict a definite commitment, within the same scope, to threaten compatibility. Conversely, compatibility with every claim expressible at a specified observational interface can be much weaker than truth of the full theory. Eight new Lean theorems characterize that difference; all eleven explicit declaration audits have empty axiom dependencies.

The positive argument reaches the necessity of preserving the actual relationships constituting specified epistemic achievements. It supports a substantive account of living realization as more than a report of satisfaction. The extension to universal basic ethical significance remains a defended foundation rather than a consequence established merely by doing science. G0 and the completed G0/J derivations remain available at their authorized scope. No new requirement to prove the ethical starting point without axioms is imposed.

**相容不等于独占解释；基本正面意义不等于唯一价值。真正的必要性检验，要指出同一范围内究竟否认了哪一项关系或规范承诺。**

## 1. Correct the target before testing the rival

Ren's claim concerns coherence with the theory. The current G0 formulation is that life and its further generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance. [Ren's ethical-direction statement](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md) keeps biological/ecological, life–mind–relationship, and broader organizational scopes open for investigation. It does not identify one scalar, a mandatory nesting of those scopes, or an exclusive inventory of every value.

It would therefore strengthen the claim without authorization to require every compatible philosophy to hold that generativity is the **sole** or exhaustively explanatory ground of understanding, friendship, justice and every other good. A pluralist can accept basic positive significance for life and further generation while recognizing additional irreducible reasons. Calling that position pluralist does not make it dualist: different forms of significance may differentiate within one relational unfolding.

This corrects the candidate test proposed while continuing the previous report; it does not retract that report's distinction between compatibility and endorsement. A rival denying exclusive grounding has not yet denied G0. A rival assigning some lives positive value only because they produce pleasure may agree on many decisions while denying the basic significance asserted by a broader reading of G0. Agreement on decisions and agreement on grounds are also different.

| Rival's claim | Relation to the present target |
|---|---|
| “I withhold judgment about your whole ontology.” | Withholding endorsement is not itself incompatibility. |
| “I represent these dependent changes through global constraints.” | A different presentation may preserve the required relationships. Its actual explanatory adequacy still needs assessment. |
| “Life and further generation matter basically; friendship and justice also have irreducible significance.” | No contradiction with G0 has been identified merely by the additional grounds. Full-package compatibility still requires checking the other commitments together. |
| “Only experiential quality has basic ethical significance; generation has none apart from that.” | A substantive potential contradiction with G0 beyond the experiential scope. It requires assessing the same full configuration. |
| “This completed life or undertaking need not be extended indefinitely.” | Not a contradiction with a positive direction that preserves present realization, actual losses and finite fulfillment. |
| “More complexity always makes the whole better, whatever the harms.” | Not a faithful statement of the current theory. Correcting it does not defeat G0. |

Contributory significance, overall comparison and obligation remain distinct. This is not permission to attach a positive numerical increment to every event. The grounding question concerns the whole configuration, its differentiated lives and its continuation. The [authorized ethical route](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md) separately states comparison commitments and correspondence requirements.

## 2. A stronger logical answer for restricted scientific claims

The predecessor proved that compatibility with **every** admissible predicate-view entails the theory over the admissible class. It explicitly warned that the converse uses singleton views and does not characterize the actual language of scientific philosophies. The new result resolves the corresponding restricted question.

Let a configuration include the relevant formation, instruments, records and later activity. Let a profile retain the answers exposed by a specified inquiry interface. This is an analytical projection within the unfolding, not an independent observer confronting an ontologically separate world. An interface may concern a present record, a family of interventions, or a richer history; its completeness must be justified for its purpose.

The checked statement is:

> A theory is compatible, **within the admissible class**, with every realizable claim depending only on that profile exactly when it has at least one admissible realization for every admissible profile.

The formal names are `MandatoryAtProfile` and `Covers`. Every admissible profile needs a witness satisfying the **same fixed theory**. One may not change the theory to fit each result. All predicates on the selected profile space are quantified, including exact-profile claims. A more restricted practical language would require its own expressibility assessment. This theorem is an elementary semantic characterization, not a discovery of the correct scientific method.

The distinction matters. Suppose the profile reports one coordinate while the theory additionally restricts another. The theory can cover every reported profile without being true of every admissible configuration. The Lean example checks this on two Boolean coordinates. Neither coordinate is an ethical verdict, and this example is not a purported real-world counterexample to G0. It checks a logical inference that would otherwise be easy to overstate.

Three further checks locate the work still required:

1. If the theory has the same truth value in every admissible configuration sharing a profile, profile coverage does imply truth throughout the admissible class. **That constancy is an additional premise**, not something observational fit supplies.
2. Coverage at a finer profile implies coverage at a coarser one. The reverse need not hold; exposing the second coordinate rejects the earlier example. Thus the particular missing distinction matters. “More evidence” has force when it actually resolves a difference relevant to the claim.
3. Pairwise compatibility of commitments does not ensure one joint realization of the package. Three simple constraints in the proof illustrate the failure. Separate successes for epistemology, ontology and ethics cannot silently use mutually inconsistent configurations.

This yields a more precise answer to the user's question. If “scientific philosophy” is restricted to claims expressible through a selected evidential profile, mandatory compatibility may be established by a coverage argument, without proving the whole theory. If the rival also asserts metaphysical or ethical denials that the profile does not express, that restricted result does not adjudicate them. The rival class must be fixed before announcing universal necessity.

The proof supplies neither empirical coverage for this theory nor a universal separation of science from ethics. Inquiry can investigate experience, dependence, harms, deliberation and the formation of appraisal. The limitation is about what follows from a specified representation and its actual warrant.

## 3. The affirmative route through actual realization

The strongest positive route begins with achievements already required by the question. A justified correction is not merely a later feeling of confidence. A copied signal and an independently vulnerable route can have the same reported content while providing different grounds. An explanation that treats those histories as interchangeable fails to explain the declared achievement.

The completed [model-revision investigation](MODEL-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) supplies this connection in a bounded realization. The [branching investigation](EXPLANATORY-BRANCHING-INTEGRATION.md) supplies a stronger structural comparison: the same event sequences need not preserve which possibilities remain jointly available at one reached condition. Those results are credited at their original hypotheses and are not rechecked or counted as new here.

There is a defensible constitutive claim here: **the relevant achievement consists partly in actual relations of formation, contact, retention and responsive revision.** An adequate account of that achievement must preserve them. This need not privilege a vocabulary or one native architecture. It excludes an account that both promises to explain warranted correction and erases the relations by which the correction is warranted.

That is already important for the generative account. Knowing does not have to be imported as a finished internal object, then connected to activity by an unexplained external correspondence. Particular knowing relations form and change within the process; their histories explain both success and error. This is positive explanatory work, not the claim that everything can receive the word “generative.”

The corresponding ethical proposal is also substantial. Living realization includes actual friendship, relief, participation, learning and the formation of further ways of living. Present lives are not merely fuel for a future maximum. Continuing generation matters because what forms can live, relate and develop, including in ways not specified by the present evaluator. On the G0 route this is a basic orientation toward 生 itself, not a definition selecting only possibilities already certified good by a different criterion.

But two additional inferences must be defended rather than compressed into “realization.” An account can accept that warranted knowing requires these relations while asking whether every such epistemic achievement is ethically good in the circumstances. It can also recognize non-experiential goods without accepting the full biological or general organizational scope of G0. Scientific and epistemic adequacy do not, by those admissions alone, decide these wider normative questions.

## 4. The closest primary comparisons

**Di Paolo's adaptive account strengthens the formation explanation.** In *Autopoiesis, adaptivity, teleology, agency* (2005), regulation relative to viability supports differentiated organismic significance. His further discussion allows learned significance and forms of value underdetermined by metabolism. This is a useful comparison for generated appraisal; it does not establish universal ethical authority for every adaptive organization. Reading scope: selected passages from the abstract/introduction, adaptivity and meaning-construction discussion, and opening of the non-metabolic-values discussion in the accessible preprint, pp. 1–2, 8–15. [Primary preprint](https://yannickprie.net/archives/ENACTION-SCHOOLS/docs/documents2006/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf). We reconstruct the selected organismic boundaries as differentiated relations within the process; we do not import them as fundamental independent kinds.

**Nozick supplies a challenge to experiential sufficiency.** His experience-machine discussion contrasts having desired experiences with actually doing things, becoming a certain person and maintaining contact with reality; transformation and result machines develop the challenge. Reading scope: the three-page excerpt from *Anarchy, State, and Utopia* (1974), pp. 42–45. [Primary excerpt](https://gned.ca/readings/Reading01Nozick%20.pdf). This provides a philosophical comparison, not an empirical finding or a proof of G0.

**De Brigard weakens a quick appeal to universal intuition.** The 2010 paper reports reversed-machine vignettes and a follow-up in which responses vary with the described alternative and continuity of the familiar life. It proposes status-quo effects as part of the explanation. These findings caution against treating a presumed universal refusal of the machine as evidence for one unique value. They do not prove hedonism or the absence of value in real relationships. Reading scope: the methods, vignettes and results in §2, the proposed explanation in §3, and the philosophical qualifications in §4, printed pp. 46–55. These are reported studies, not experiments reproduced here. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://people.duke.edu/~fd13/2010/De_Brigard_2010_PhilPsych.pdf).

The earlier [primary-source audit](NORMATIVE-WARRANT-SOURCES.md) already assessed constitutivist arguments and challenges concerning agency, discourse and practical authority. Its conclusions are inherited rather than announced as new discoveries. The [prior compatibility study](SCIENTIFIC-COMPATIBILITY-NECESSITY.md#5-two-substantive-primary-source-comparisons) likewise preserves the exact reading scopes for van Fraassen and Mill. None of these sources is treated as establishing the correctness of a whole rival philosophy.

Our assessment also limits the empirical objection: choosing the familiar or more pleasant life does not establish that actual relationship has **zero** independent significance. A consideration can count in favor without determining the final choice. Thus the reported variation challenges a quick argument from universal intuitive priority; it does not refute every claim of basic positive significance. The same discipline must apply when evaluating objections to G0.

## 5. Test the strongest rival without making it a caricature

An experiential-value rival can accept all of the following: generative formation of perception and judgment; actual truth and error; the epistemic difference between knowledge and confident error; learned concern; interdependence; and the instrumental importance of social and biological conditions. It can nevertheless maintain that experiential quality exhausts basic **ethical** value.

This position does not have to deny knowledge to avoid the argument. Recognizing an epistemic achievement is not identical to granting it independent ethical value. Nor must the rival deny relational formation or posit a second ontological realm. Its disputed claim concerns which features of the one unfolding constitute basic ethical significance.

The generative response has two parts. First, a life of real participation and understanding is not exhausted by its reports of satisfaction. That claim has independently identifiable relational content. Second, these actual realizations and their further generation have positive significance. G0 provides the second commitment explicitly. The first strengthens its intelligibility and supplies things to assess; it does not already force the rival to accept the second.

An experience-machine comparison must preserve these distinctions carefully. A simulated experience is itself actual activity; a machine need not be devoid of life, learning or generation. Digitally mediated relationships can be genuine relationships. The relevant contrast is between which relationships and achievements are actually realized, not a metaphysically privileged material label. Moreover, changing reciprocity can change agency, trust, experience and obligations together. A stipulation that only “reality” changed cannot replace accounting for those differences.

The strongest comparison therefore asks, in a fully described case, whether the actual living relation contributes significance beyond the rival's experiential basis. Acceptance would defeat **strict experiential sufficiency in that case**. It would not establish that generation is the exclusive value, that every increase in possibilities is better, or that the widest organizational scope follows from a case about friendship.

Conversely, rejecting that significance is not automatically incoherent or scientifically disqualifying. The claim must be tested against the full relation the theory says constitutes significance. This round establishes neither a verified empirical case with every experiential consequence held fixed nor a proof that such separation is impossible. That is a correspondence limit of the comparison, not evidence that the constitutive proposal is false.

## 6. Whole perspective in the helping case

Ren's earlier comparison concerns helping that benefits both people overall yet changes the shape of the helper's life. Under the non-self perspective, both positions and their changing relationship belong to the whole history. The helper's opportunities, attachments and previous commitments remain real parts of that history. Expanding the perspective must not erase them.

Three things can be true together: the assistance is good for the recipient; participation also enriches the helper's life; and that life becomes different in ways that cannot be reduced to a net satisfaction score. Becoming involved can be a present realization of a relationship and can create further possibilities. It may also displace another valued possibility, establish reliance, or require a later handover. “Both benefit overall” does not remove those particular consequences or by itself confer permission to impose the change.

This gives a concrete affirmative interpretation of generative good: the relationship is part of what comes to live and matter, rather than merely a channel delivering two independently fixed payoffs. A pluralist who recognizes that relationship's basic significance may be compatible with the G0 orientation even if also recognizing distinct reasons concerning consent or promise. A strict experiential rival may instead count the relationship only through its experiential consequences. That is the real point of disagreement.

This analysis does not derive every duty from non-self. The [situated ethical programme](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md) already supplies scoped practical comparisons and preserves loss, self-good, purpose change and finite closure. The present contribution is to identify what those comparisons mean for the necessity dispute, without restarting completed mechanisms.

## 7. What genuinely changes the account?

The investigation yields three distinct research obligations, with different possible outcomes.

| Claim to establish | Evidence or argument that advances it | What would require revision |
|---|---|---|
| Necessary content of knowing | Identify an independently warranted epistemic distinction; show that an account erasing a specified formation/dependence relation misdescribes it. | An equally adequate account of the same full target that genuinely lacks the alleged indispensable content. An equivalent relational redescription is not such a counterexample. |
| Compatibility of the ethical foundation | Specify G0's claim in a declared scope; establish why the rival's denial misdescribes the relevant whole realization or why its restriction of basic significance fails. | A substantively adequate rival retaining that realization while justifiably denying the claimed significance, or a case showing the proposed identity misdescribes it. Freely changing a verdict bit in a weaker model is insufficient. |
| Necessity of the whole package | Establish a single coherent interpretation of the commitments and justify the relevant rival class; support each indispensable commitment without defining adequacy as assent to it. | A genuine jointly inconsistent commitment, unsupported scope extension, or adequate contrary philosophy within the independently specified class. |

The scopes of 生 require their own assessment. Positive value in real understanding and friendship does not silently establish the same verdict for every biological continuation, and a biological result does not by itself establish the widest organizational claim. These are not stipulated independent domains: the connections between them are precisely explanatory and normative work for the theory. Likewise, finding a countervailing harm defeats an overall ranking only under the relevant comparison; it does not automatically deny every contributory positive significance.

The next substantive ethical target is therefore **scope of the positive ground**, rather than the demand for exclusive value monism. A persuasive account would explain why further living realization has basic significance across the selected scope, including where it is not a means to an already selected end. It would preserve rather than erase actual harms, present fulfillment and responsible endings. The positive criterion remains 生生不息; it is not rewritten as “whatever already has justified value.”

## 8. Counterreview and exact endpoint

**Does the compatibility theorem trivialize Ren's claim?** Only if its profile restriction is concealed. A theory that admits one realization for every observable profile can be compatible with the profile language while leaving substantive claims unproved. The theorem tells us to avoid that weak victory. It does not show this theory covers every profile or that scientific philosophy contains no other commitments.

**Does pluralism's possible compatibility complete the whole claim?** No. It removes a false obstacle. One still needs an actual shared interpretation of G0 and the rival, and one joint realization of the full package. The result is not a verdict that every pluralist is compatible.

**Does admitting the stronger rival abandon the positive theory?** No. The generative account explains why life, learning and relationship are actual realizations with developing conditions; G0 articulates a positive ethical orientation toward them. Its explanatory reach and ethical coherence are grounds for serious assessment. The rival identifies the further claim a necessity argument must establish rather than assuming its denial has already been defeated.

**Does the limit prove that universal ethical necessity is impossible?** No. Neither the formal interface nor the source comparisons prove such an impossibility. They close shortcuts through coarse compatibility, constitutive dependence alone, exclusive-ground demands, and an unexamined universal intuition. A stronger constitutive or comparative argument remains possible and must be assessed on its actual content.

The furthest warranted conclusion is now more exact: **where the theory identifies relations indispensable to a warranted target, an adequate account of that same target must preserve their content. Broader philosophies can share that content and G0 without adopting exclusive generative axiology. The claim that every scientifically adequate philosophy must be coherent with every substantive commitment of the full theory remains unproved.**

This completes the present continuation's direct tests and integration. It does not declare the universal research goal finished. The unresolved issue is a specific scope and warrant question about positive significance, alongside any remaining ontological commitments; another successful finite learning mechanism would not by itself answer it.
