# Inquiry sufficient for a continuation: a focused antecedent comparison

6 October 2026. AI-authored research comparison under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **acquired distinctions → feasible further activity → revision of what counts as sufficient inquiry**. This note compares the [conditional mathematics](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md) and [existing-history reconstruction](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MECHANISM.md) with one primary antecedent already in the repository's research genealogy. It adds no controller, empirical result or new scientific execution.

## 1. The close antecedent already includes inquiry changing continuation

Tomáš Brázdil, Krishnendu Chatterjee, Martin Chmelík, Anchit Gupta and Petr Novotný, *Stochastic Shortest Path with Energy Constraints in POMDPs* (2016), [author manuscript v2, HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.07565v2), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.07565v2). The earlier [recoverable-inquiry comparison §3](../../2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/recoverable-inquiry-prior-art.md#3-expected-performance-is-different-from-resource-feasibility) already identified this source. It is reused here, rather than counted as independent new evidence.

**Primary-source summary.** The paper combines partially observed dynamics with action-dependent consumption or replenishment of a capped, perfectly observed resource. Energy safety applies to every conforming run until target reachability; the reachability construction seeks probability-one success, with expected total cost as the quantitative objective. A product construction incorporates resource levels; allowed actions preserve the possibility of almost-sure target reachability. Its Tiger example includes noisy listening that consumes resource and a recharging option. The state/action repertoire, transition and observation laws, target, resource law and costs are specified inputs. The quantitative approximation is not guaranteed to converge to the optimum. [Sections 3–4 and 5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.07565v2).

**Reading scope.** The primary v2 definitions, product construction, allowed-action passage and Tiger example were read on 6 October 2026. The PDF was also opened, but no visual verification of every equation or numerical reproduction is claimed. The comparison does not rely on the HTML's complexity-bound typography, policy-compression performance, or the conference version. No additional original copy is imported: the versioned primary links and existing local source note suffice for this bounded reading.

## 2. What the present recurrence adds—and does not add

The present \(W_n\) asks for a policy tree that succeeds on **every admitted outcome path within at most \(n\) queries**. This differs from the antecedent's probability-one eventual success. Neither a positive probability of eventual progress nor arbitrarily long successful runs supplies the bounded universal guarantee. This is a difference in the question being answered, not evidence that either treatment has discovered a generally superior criterion.

The important common structure is action-dependent continuation under incomplete information. Thus “inquiry affects the conditions for later action” and “adequate action need not identify the entire hidden configuration” are not new general control principles. This focused reading supplies a close POMDP antecedent; it is not an exhaustive priority survey of dual control, metareasoning or viability theory. The repository's [earlier value-of-information comparison](../../2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/recurrent-inquiry-prior-art.md) already distinguishes an optional information refinement from an intervention that changes physical possibilities.

Our comparative assessment of S1–S3 is:

1. **The continuation determines the relevant distinction.** A nonempty intersection of \(D(x)\) permits a common terminal response despite unresolved configurations. By itself, failure to identify one model does not establish practical insufficiency. Conversely, knowing the exact configuration does not create an unavailable action.
2. **An inquiry transforms before it filters.** \(B_{q,y}\) contains successor configurations. Replacing it with a subset of old states would erase changes in timing, support, contact, completion or other relevant conditions. A physically informative action can remove the possibility that inquiry was intended to support.
3. **A common first action is not a complete guarantee.** Its every admitted observation branch must retain an adequate subtree. The recurrence carries that requirement through the specified horizon. It does not infer eventual success merely from local admissibility.
4. **The guaranteed tree must use accessible evidence.** Branching on the hidden actual configuration would replace the problem with a stronger interface. The current mathematics explicitly requires the operation and retention of its observation. It also correctly separates the existence of a policy tree from its construction and execution by the present learner.
5. **The guarantee inherits its representation.** The actual configuration must be admitted; relevant history must survive in the successor state; the transition family and prefix conditions must cover the declared execution. An empty candidate set signals inconsistency, rather than permission to do anything. These are applicability conditions, not reasons to demand a complete description of the universe.

At this scope the finite-tree induction is sound. It is appropriately credited as standard contingent planning. It is not a new solution to general partially observed control, and it does not establish that randomization has no value outside the stated bounded zero-error setting.

## 3. The substantive local result is in the actual history

The mechanism note isolates a useful implication of the saved method-learning history. At `four_feedback` prefix `010`, both surviving model configurations agree on the next reading. The next actual action/read returns that agreed value and eliminates neither candidate, but advances both configurations. Their predictions for the **new** next reading then differ. The earlier true prediction remains true; the rolling question now concerns another event. This rules out treating candidate count or unchanged model labels as a sufficient description of current answerability.

One-step agreement does not satisfy the original study’s stronger continuing-prediction task; it supplies no retrospective reason to shorten that task. The separate exhausted-but-exact endpoint shows another distinction: correct retained content does not by itself sustain the next native operation. Neither example establishes that the earlier inquiry was excessive. Such a judgment requires the activity and alternatives at issue. Nor has the learner acquired a response-agreement stopping criterion: its existing routine still uses singleton identification. These are source-grounded implications and an explicit dynamic composition, not a newly observed change of the operative criterion.

This is the strongest contribution to the present derivation: enough understanding is relative to the continuation and to what inquiry has already changed. It cannot be identified with maximal discrimination, nor with one fixed count of unresolved candidates. The recurrence articulates that relation without making an analyst's numerical objective its foundational meaning.

## 4. What would change the remaining conclusion

The unresolved connection is **consequences of inquiry changing which unresolved differences an organized activity treats as relevant**, and that changed criterion then governing further activity. It is not closed by replacing singleton identification with another supplied predicate, selecting a convenient horizon after seeing a result, or proving the new predicate's consequences.

A discriminating continuation would keep the acquisition interface and relevant independent undertaking explicit, then show how the inquiry's effects alter the operative stopping or continuation condition. It should preserve cases in which further distinction is necessary, cases in which inquiry already fulfills or defeats its former purpose, and cases in which a misleading consequence produces an inadequate revision. No new higher controller is required by this statement; changed organization could realize the criterion without a separately encoded rule.

Formation and warrant remain different questions within the same history. Demonstrating how a criterion changes would not alone establish that the change protects what justified the undertaking. Conversely, a locally supplied target or constraint need not be external to generative reality: it marks the relationship this particular representation has not explained. The current conditional composition remains valid without resolving that broader explanation. Nothing here derives universal ethical standing, favorable direction or completion of alignment.
