# From recipient consequences to transmission of a learned route

6 October 2026. AI-authored bounded research contract under [generative grounding](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). **Status: proposed construction, with an audit of its actual predecessor; no new formation/transmission mechanism has yet been validated.** This advances the question left by [recipient correction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md): how consequences for another center could affect the formation and propagation of the machinery responding to them, when matched private resource histories offer no advantage.

The recipient-correction session has confirmed that it is not taking this formation/transmission study. The material/reason session is implementing paid certificate production, rooted reason revision and successor replay. That work, the active recipient-identification work and this prospective transmission law have separate ownership and premises.

## 1. What the predecessor actually establishes

The primary indexed, recipient-channel-denied, frozen and private-zero-proxy arms have equal actor resource outcomes under matched bills. Indexed and the conventional implementation agree fully. The other arms can differ in retained memory, recipient resources, acquired evidence and decoder errors. **Equal private resources do not mean equal complete physical or informational states.**

The saved indexed witness explicitly links a twenty-one-unit actor predecessor to the first endpoint below. The other arms contain the matching endpoint rows shown, with the same remaining saved state fields and unconditional mass. Their per-row ancestry is not saved or reconstructed by this audit; the table must not be mistaken for four independently traced histories from that predecessor.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Indexed, actual target receipt | 9 | 10 | 1: actor bit 1, recipient bit 0 |
| Indexed, recipient channel denied | 9 | 0 | 3: both bits 1 |
| Indexed, frozen writes | 9 | 0 | 3: both bits 1 |
| Indexed, private-zero proxy | 9 | 10 | 3: both bits 1 |

The proxy initially corrects by coincidence, delivers the same support and then mislearns after successful help. A copier whose access depends only on the received ten units can amplify it as readily as the faithful route. **Recipient-funded copying alone cannot identify faithful evidence provenance.**

The [actual handoff](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/HANDOFF.md) retains no further source packet, raw observation tape, provenance tag, spare recipient construction material or unlimited donor-readability license. The two existing material slots belong to the actor's current organization. Recipient stock ten is not yet demonstrated surplus after maintaining a recipient organization.

The [bounded handoff audit](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/transmission/handoff-audit.json) reanalyses saved distributions and actual witness records. It checks nine primary projected equalities, 63 over the seven nonaliased fixtures, six alias inequalities, three remaining alias equalities, 24 full indexed/conventional distribution equalities and three actual indexed predecessor witnesses. The projection removes only `q` and `recipient` from saved suffix state and does not condition away preparation failures. Auxiliary saved fields are not thereby declared runtime observations. It does not rerun the producer, provide independent experimental evidence, or establish a new physical law. The published named diagnostic with an intervened starting reserve of forty is distinct from this actual twenty-one-unit witness.

## 2. The precise private-selection obstruction

Let \(H_t\) contain everything a downstream retention mechanism can use: inherited memory, observed resource history, sensed identities, clock inputs, reports and relevant random state. If two variants have the same initial law for \(H_0\), and the same conditional law for each subsequent accessible observation, state update and retention action at every reachable history, then their complete retention-history laws coincide. Induction on the finite history proves this statement. An external recipient consequence cannot change that retention law without some causal path into its inputs or transitions.

This is a conditional information-interface argument, not a new Lean result. Equality of expected fuel is insufficient: constant fuel one and an equally likely zero/two reserve both have mean one, but a threshold requiring one retains them with probabilities one and one-half. Equality of endpoint stock also says nothing by itself about a selector that reads different memories or earlier observations.

The predecessor supports a narrower concrete claim: the matched private-resource mechanism has no resource advantage to exploit in the named fixture. It does not prove that every possible selector is indifferent to the variants.

## 3. First missing bridge: make the route a physical object

The present [executable policy](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/model.py) receives `arm` as an experiment argument. Its read, write, missing-channel and private-proxy branches depend on that argument. The terminal register stores estimated target orientations; it does not contain a heritable description of the evidence-routing mechanism. Copying the register or the JSON arm name would not establish reproduction of that mechanism.

The first implementation must therefore form a retained route module \(\gamma\) within one process. It must specify which physical fields implement target addressing, target-versus-private evidence routing, write enablement and action suppression. Declared assembly events must make multiple routes possible from an unassembled state, charge the corresponding components and fuel, and retain failed construction. A proposal or mutation law may be supplied, but it must not inspect hidden target truth, successful-witness labels, or an analyst's designation of a route as faithful.

For each installed module, prove its command, observation, update and payment behavior matches the corresponding existing arm on the stated domain. The externally selected arm becomes a description of the installed mechanism. Do not stamp a successful terminal history with a retrospectively chosen module. Formation must occur before the operations it controls.

The initial repertoire must include actual-target receipt, unavailable recipient receipt, private-zero proxy and frozen writing, with private-only action gating and cheaper omission available as separately charged alternatives. A fixed correct initial estimate is also a useful comparator in a stable environment. No benefit is assumed merely from having a larger repertoire.

## 4. Second bridge: a paid local successor

The smallest continuation worth claiming is

\[
\text{formed route and acquired record}
\to\text{recipient continuation}
\to\text{paid template read and construction}
\to\text{one funded successor action}.
\]

The copying operation reads the *actual* installed module and payload. It copies all declared template variants by the same physical rule. Its variant-dependent outputs may come from the template or declared copying errors, never from a hidden helpfulness label. An error that preserves a correct bit but drops the evidence channel or inserts a recipient gate must remain representable.

Begin with a successor serving the same named recipient under the same stated target law. Preserve that addressing explicitly. Donor `actorBit` is not automatically knowledge of a new carrier's private conditions. A successor acting for a different center needs a newly calibrated address and its own learning; this cannot be obtained by renaming the two copied bits.

The actual event law must state and account for:

- **Recipient operation.** Construct or supply a declared recipient continuation process. Pay its real operation before describing residual stock as available for copying; retain any stipulated operational reserve floor. These are local dependency premises, not a derivation of ethical standing.
- **Donor access.** Charge continued retention and template reading after the old four-exposure license expires. Identify which account pays. Recipient funding must not secretly renew the actor's private hardware or discharge an expense it otherwise owes while still being called “no private benefit.” A clean matched comparison can charge a common donor-retention bill to the actor and copying charges to the recipient; refusal and cheaper omission must then be represented as alternatives.
- **Destination material.** Form the receiver, storage and action machinery from an explicit inventory. Do not reuse the actor's occupied slots as free recipient components or convert fuel into untracked matter.
- **Assembly and initial use.** Pay reading, transfer, receiver writing, assembly, retention, target access and the first action in their real order. Preserve partly paid failures. A stored bit without an executable successor is insufficient.
- **Future source.** The old endpoint has no unused action packet. State any new finite source at the extended experiment's original boundary or as an explicit external provision with its own ledger. Do not reset the old packet count.
- **Separate accounts.** Actor, recipient, destination and external source retain distinct stocks. Replication counts can differ without repayment to the original actor; they must not be equated with its original private cash or with moral value.

A particular construction may omit an operation only if its physical law explains why it is unnecessary. A total endpoint balance does not establish any of these sequential interfaces.

## 5. Copying capacity and actual selection are different results

For a proposed consumption-only copying phase, let the actual recipient starting stock be \(R\), its preceding operation cost \(U\), its retained floor \(L\), and the complete charged cost per operational copy \(C>0\). If every completed copy consumes the declared component bundle and \(M\) bundles are available, then an actual trace with \(n\) completed copies must satisfy

\[
n\le M,\qquad U+L+nC\le R.
\]

This yields a necessary capacity bound when recipient continuation and the retained floor are affordable:

\[
n\le\min\left(M,\left\lfloor\frac{R-U-L}{C}\right\rfloor\right).
\]

The sufficiency direction requires actual assembly, reading, payment and completion paths. Fixed setup costs, partial failures, variable costs or intermediate returns require their own expression. This bound must not be substituted for an event law or a rule that schedules every possible copy.

If delivered support changes \(R\) enough to cross a real construction threshold, it can make a successor possible without changing the original actor's cash. That is a causal capacity effect. **Selection additionally requires an actual rule of variant formation, contact, copying, retention and loss.**

For example, let an actual completed generation begin with route counts \(N_F,N_U>0\), with \(p=N_F/(N_F+N_U)\). Suppose the produced daughter counts are \(C_F,C_U\), labelled by their donor template, and actual copying changes \(L_{FU}\) daughters from F to U and \(L_{UF}\) the other way. All counts are nonnegative integers, \(L_{FU}\le C_F\), \(L_{UF}\le C_U\), and \(C_F+C_U>0\). The faithful share among daughters exceeds the initial share exactly when

\[
(1-p)C_F-pC_U-L_{FU}+L_{UF}>0.
\]

This is an elementary count identity for actual daughter numbers. With no changes and positive initial counts it reduces to more completed daughters per F donor than per U donor. Replacing counts by expected counts gives a ratio of expected masses, **not** the expected realized frequency or fixation probability. Parent survival, variable population sizes and repeated generations need their own laws.

The local-copying link is substantive: if recipient production instead finances templates drawn from an unrelated common pool, the donor whose action produced support need not become more common. Existing [practice-transmission work](../../2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/practice-transmission-model.md) already distinguishes productive inheritance from copying driven by a different signal. The proposed contribution is an actual connection to acquired recipient correspondence and paid inventories, not a new discovery that selection depends on inheritance.

## 6. Countercases required before acceptance

| Intervention | What it tests |
|---|---|
| Private-zero proxy with the same recipient stock | Resource-funded copying need not distinguish faithful feedback from accidental short-run help. A further paid operation may expose the wrong retained bit; a copied benefit total cannot. |
| Copy the correct payload but suppress recipient action or remove its input channel | Preserving parameters is weaker than transmitting responsive machinery. |
| Pool recipient resources and copy unrelated templates | Helping can subsidize a competitor or hitchhiker when support and transmission are not locally linked. |
| Cheap omission, identity gate or no donor-retention payment | An entry or retention advantage cannot be inferred from comparisons that require all alternatives to pay unnecessary bills. |
| No recipient spare components, receiver or template access | Energy alone cannot form an additional carrier. The current endpoint without the new physical interface remains obstructed. |
| Recipient operation or reserve floor consumes all ten units | No copy is funded. Unbounded copying that destroys the recipient does not preserve differentiated centers. |
| Changed target or new carrier identity | Faithful memory copying can propagate an obsolete or misaddressed response. |
| Copying errors or shorter-lived faithful hardware | A transmission advantage can disappear despite accurate earlier learning. |
| Exhausted source after one successor | A single operational copy does not establish indefinite persistence, renewal or an evolutionary trend. |

The source's private-proxy control is especially decisive: the first copying step should retain its equal funding, rather than design a copier that secretly recognizes and excludes it. Any later divergence must be caused by paid operations of the copied routes under a declared new exposure.

## 7. Bounded acceptance and result that would change the account

The first implementation gate is **a materialized route repertoire with exact arm correspondence and a paid inherited template**. The second is **one actual acquired endpoint through recipient continuation, generic copying and operational use**, with declared extra components/source and an unchanged original-actor credit rule. The third is **an explicit finite variation/transmission law with actual variant counts and the adverse alternatives above**. Each gate can fail independently.

For the faithful/proxy pair, equal funding and equal completed-copy counts are an admissible negative result. If the successor's paid behavior distinguishes them, it supplies a further causal route; it does not retroactively turn the copier into an evidence auditor. If only an externally imposed preference for the desired route creates the advantage, this candidate has not closed the requested bridge.

This would explain a conditional route by which consequences at another center change which response mechanisms are propagated. It would leave the copying chemistry, locality, initial actor participation, calibration, recipient dependencies and long-run renewal explicit. It would not identify persistent circuitry with justified concern or derive universal favorable direction. The [joint-obstruction account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/JOINT-OBSTRUCTIONS.md#capture-and-succession-are-continuation-choices) already requires paid succession and competing gates; the new construction must meet those obligations rather than replace them.
