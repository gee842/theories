## Finding: a coherent relative foundation can be completed

The strongest defensible candidate is **consequential differentiation through admissible transformation**. A concrete account specifies the conditions that matter and the possible transformations between them. An activity is consequential when it changes which subsequent activities or outcomes are possible under the declared observational and intervention contract. Conditions, observers, representations and selectors can all be realized within such an account; none must be an external chooser or universal scalar objective.

For finite discrete history semantics, this becomes a particularly small presentation: a type of situations and typed primitive transitions between situations. Event labels and endpoints are retained in the existing finite-trace interface. Histories can then be generated from the primitive transitions. Composition is concatenation through their matching actual boundary. Forgetting the labels after constructing these histories gives the **least reflexive-transitive endpoint relation containing the primitive transitions**. Any map preserving the primitive transitions into a reflexive-transitive target relation also preserves the endpoints of every finite history. This least closure is unique pointwise.

This is positive closure of a mathematical layer: the presentation needs no separately installed endpoint-reachability relation, reward, learner, observer, recurrence or favorable direction. It also states what the foundation cannot supply: the concrete primitive transitions are inputs. Uniqueness of the least closure does not choose those transitions, make them occur, or select one physical realization of the formal structure. A stronger universal property for a free category retaining distinct path witnesses is not claimed by the new module.

The distinction between **presentation completeness** and **explanatory completeness** matters. A completed presentation gives unambiguous primitives, constructions, consequences, boundaries and integration maps. The unified explanation still has to derive the origins and changes of regulators, learning mechanisms and shared concern under identifiable material conditions. Completing the former is a genuine finished result rather than a declaration that the latter has been achieved.

## Relative minimality and dependence

An absolute minimum number of primitive symbols is not an invariant criterion: a record can package several independent inputs as one symbol. The useful question is which distinctions or laws can be removed without changing the explanatory contract.

| Proposed ingredient | What the smaller presentation establishes | What remains supplied |
|---|---|---|
| A distinction | Different continuation possibilities certify a state distinction | The situations and their possible transitions; the existence of nontrivial distinctions is not a theorem about every type |
| Transformation | A typed transition records a possible event between conditions | Which events are admissible, their rates or probabilities, their actual occurrence and causal interpretation |
| Sequential composition | Finite paths and their associative concatenation are constructed | Adequacy of the situation boundary, including relevant memory and resources |
| Recurrence | A return path is a defined, testable special configuration | Existence, accessibility, costs and sustaining adequacy of any cycle |
| Joint interaction | A combined mechanism can be represented by an appropriate joint law | The actual coupling, resource sharing, synchronization and compatibility of components |
| Operational significance | A difference in continuations is causally consequential | Receiving, response and retention mechanisms that make a difference matter within regulation |

Nontrivial differentiation does not determine dynamics. The same two-element carrier can support no events, only state-preserving events, or events that switch its elements. The same distinction therefore admits incompatible dynamical laws. Conversely, an inhabited event relation can exist on a one-element carrier: event occurrence alone does not force distinct endpoints. A two-element carrier with only identity transitions also shows that differentiated conditions need not undergo changing conditions.

A transformation need not alter its own future admissibility. A fixed law can generate repeated activity indefinitely while leaving an operative selector unchanged, as the existing boundary model shows. Accordingly, the deepest interface **admits** activity changing future conditions; it does not assert that every event must do so. Strong generativity needs a witness of consequential change and later mechanisms, not merely membership in the interface.

Sequential composition does not entail recurrence. A one-way finite chain satisfies the same rules and terminates. Recurrence in turn does not entail reconstruction, learning, broadening concern or favorable direction. Those require their own mechanisms and premises.

The empty path is the identity for composition. It represents no event, not a newly postulated physical operation that costs zero. A path in a type preserves its chosen transition witnesses; an existential trace proposition records that such a path exists. These are different levels of information. A bridge between them must say exactly which labels, endpoints and existence facts it preserves.

## Four historical claims repaired without losing their contribution

The preserved operation-monism chapter contains a productive idea: study a common construction before choosing a particular realization. Its product tower is useful as such a parametric construction. The following stronger inferences should remain recorded as rejected or restricted claims.

1. **The product operation eliminates the seed.** It does not. For a declared seed \(X\), the recursion \(T_0=X\), \(T_{n+1}=T_n\times T_n\) fixes each subsequent carrier. Empty seeds remain empty; singleton seeds remain singletons. Parametric dependence on a seed does not turn that seed into a consequence of the operator. The original phrase “the empty starting point” must not identify an arbitrary seed with the empty type. The inspected `rtower_no_typeclass` theorem supplies a type as a witness of `Nonempty (Type u)`; it does not prove `Nonempty (RTower X k)` or produce an inhabitant of that carrier.
2. **Iteration of products is already the genesis of physical differences.** The operation constructs spaces of tuples. Even where the number of configurations grows, it supplies no occurrence law, probability, interaction or physical process traversing those configurations. A construction index becomes physical time only through an additional interpretation and law.
3. **The carrier is a fixed point and objects collapse into operations.** A sequence of iterates does not itself establish an equivalence \(T\cong T\times T\), nor identify an object with the operation acting on it. Calling a tower an operator-generated presentation is sound. A fixed-point claim or an object–morphism identification requires an additional stated construction and proof.
4. **A two-mark encoding establishes one substrate across physical, logical and experiential domains.** A two-element inductive type can encode a binary distinction. The associated maps do not derive a Hilbert-space law, a semantics of experience, a causal coupling or ethical standing. Additional structure is part of the realization, and faithfulness must be judged against that structure. Merely assigning two names to domain elements is insufficient.

These repairs retain the useful genealogy: common constructions can expose invariants and prevent accidental dependence on labels. They withdraw the unsupported inference that a common construction has produced its own seed, all dynamics or the ontology of every realization.

## What monism positively commits to

Three different claims should not share one evidential status.

**Common presentation** means that several accounts use a common mathematical interface. It aids composition and comparison, but can also encode unrelated systems; even a disjoint union of unrelated graphs has one graph presentation.

**Explanatory continuity** means that claimed observers, values, models and selectors have carriers and causal roles within the described world or its explicitly declared environment. An explanation cannot introduce an unaccounted choice when it reaches the stage of learning, or a free informational channel when it reaches understanding. Open boundaries are permitted; their supplied contributions remain visible. This is a substantive discipline that the present root can impose.

**Ontological monism** is the stronger proposal that all the relevant kinds of being belong to one reality and admit such connected grounding. Shared notation and a closure theorem cannot by themselves establish it. In particular, they cannot choose between two physical interpretations that make the same predictions for the whole declared experiment.

The coherent monistic candidate is therefore a common causal reality with differentiated formations, rather than the claim that all things are one undifferentiated object. It preserves plural centers and their sometimes incompatible continuations. A center's predictive or productive contribution does not determine its ethical standing. An adequate mechanistic account of acquired reasons does not make every acquired reason justified.

## No-axiom audit

“No custom axiom” is a check on proof construction: no new unproved proposition was installed to make a desired theorem pass. “No axiom dependencies” is a stronger, declaration-specific report from the checker. Neither means that definitions, hypotheses, type theory and inference rules have disappeared.

Defining a type with two constructors supplies a formal distinction. It does not prove that actual reality has two distinguishable conditions. Defining a relation supplies a possible dynamical model. It does not establish the relation's physical adequacy. A conditional theorem may require no custom axiom while taking the entire relation as a parameter.

The positive objective is thus a small explicit foundation with derivations replacing unnecessary independent assumptions. A finite coherent realization can refute an alleged contradiction in the candidate relative to the proof system. It cannot certify its unique empirical or metaphysical truth.

## Discriminating consequences and the next connection

The candidate is informative when it enforces obligations that a proposed explanation could fail:

- If two situations admit different declared continuations, a purportedly exact summary cannot identify them. The existing Lean projection theorem supplies this consequence.
- If a claimed integration uses a successor reserve, signal or controller different from what its predecessor supplies, its path does not compose without an additional event. Independent successful examples are insufficient.
- If the same carrier and descriptive distinctions admit conflicting future laws, the law has not been derived from those distinctions alone. The formal countermodels identify the missing input.
- If a transformation is said to produce learning, its retained change must affect subsequent responses. If it is said to produce selection over selectors, the changed mechanism must actually alter what is sensed, evaluated, retained or varied.
- If all proposed future activities require more of an exhaustible reserve than the real history leaves, renaming those activities generative does not make them feasible. The existing depletion and support theorems supply explicit conditional bounds.

The next central connection is **formation and differential retention of competing response variants within one actual resource history**, followed by causal revision of the mechanisms that generate or retain variants. The new deeper layer should map to the repository's accepted dependency-to-regulation work rather than restart that study or install a separate philosophical track. For the deeper layer itself, the continuing extension is to preserve rates, probabilities, observation access and implementable policies when interpreting the same primitive transitions. Finite existential path faithfulness alone does not preserve those richer contracts.

## Acceptance status

The proposed conceptual specification is accepted at the relative finite-history scope above. Its positive content is typed consequential transformation, generated finite histories, exact accounting of inherited conditions and explicit dependence of every stronger capacity on an actual mechanism. Absolute primitive minimality, seed-free ontogenesis and formal proof of metaphysical monism are not accepted claims.

The four new modules were inspected for semantic correspondence:

- [ProductTower.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/ProductTower.lean) proves exact preservation/reflection of inhabitedness, emptiness and subsingleton status through each finite product depth. The retraction from a selected leaf to a diagonally embedded seed also proves that an existing distinction persists. It does not assert that larger product carriers have no additional tuples.
- [DynamicUnderdetermination.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DynamicUnderdetermination.lean) supplies genuinely incompatible empty, static and alternating laws on the same state/action carriers. Its singleton-loop witness correctly separates event occurrence from endpoint differentiation. These are underdetermination results relative to carrier data, not an impossibility theorem about derivation from richer laws.
- [GeneratedPaths.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/GeneratedPaths.lean) establishes the least endpoint relation, its pointwise uniqueness and preservation under a step-preserving interpretation into a reflexive-transitive relation. Its no-step result correctly leaves equality as the entire closure. Labels are forgotten in `Reach` only after a finite trace has been constructed; no free-category or path-multiplicity theorem is asserted.
- [HistoryRealization.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/HistoryRealization.lean) constructs an exact finite-word realization of a supplied prefix-closed language admitting the empty word. The state is the complete declared action-label history. Applying this construction to an existing process preserves its finite word language from the selected initial state. It can collapse original hidden branches, so it does not preserve the original statewise transition structure, probabilities, observations, intervention abilities or policy guarantees. It avoids a finite-memory prerequisite for this specific language contract, not every physical state-sufficiency question.

No blocking semantic issue was found in these stated contracts. This is semantic review plus verification-record inspection; a separate second compilation was not performed because the substantive sources were unchanged.
