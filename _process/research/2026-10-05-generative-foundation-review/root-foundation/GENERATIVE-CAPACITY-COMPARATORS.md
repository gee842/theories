## 1. What the existing results already establish

[ContinuationRenewal](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/ContinuationRenewal.lean) supplies conditional recurrence: an infinite history whose nonrenewing transitions follow a well-founded relation cannot have a renewal-free tail. Its alternating witness renews the same two configurations; recurrence does not establish increasing organizational variety. The theorem does not construct the infinite history or independently identify the exhausting relationship in nature.

[RenewalExamples](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/conditional/RenewalExamples.lean) already contrasts a supported cycle, terminal loss, inadequate repeated provision, zero-enactment recurrence and arbitrarily large one-time provision supporting finite runs. Its restoration brings a declared quantity. Repeating these examples with different names would not explain formation of a new generative capacity.

The newer [RegenerativeDiversity](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/RegenerativeDiversity.lean) adds actual prefix networks, retained outputs distinguishable on a common input domain, coherent finite construction histories, and copying without repertoire expansion. It therefore moves beyond a bare event count. Its exact limits are relevant:

- `reconstruct` requires a supplied channel, positive material, a spent interface and a network of positive depth. Depth one suffices for later reconstruction; increasing depth is not shown to improve the reconstruction mechanism itself.
- The channel is selected by the process parameter. Its formation is not derived merely because an assembled stage is required to operate it.
- `available + spent = received` accounts for delivered material. `intake` does not debit a represented source, so this is not closed accounting of the wider history.
- Prefix depth describes the configured dependency chain, not minimal computational complexity. Distinguishing inputs demonstrate functional difference; physical availability of every input pattern and the cost of accessing taps require a correspondence.

These are declared model interfaces, not defects in the proofs. Source inspection here does not claim a new compilation. The comparisons below ask which further relationships would change the result or its ethical interpretation.

## 2. Arbitrarily elaborate completion versus recurrent formation

**Common configuration.** Use the same initial seed, assembly recipe and ordered supply of lots. Each lot contains two structural parts and one unit of usable work. Track unconsumed source lots, captured parts, constructed organization, spent work and the interface that admits later lots. These are differentiated relations within the represented whole; the source is not an unlimited benefactor outside it.

**Batch continuation.** Gather finitely many lots; commission assembly at a stated work cost; close the intake relation; construct successive layers until captured support is exhausted. A larger finite gathering period can support a deeper final artifact. A history that gathers forever must be recorded separately: it has not thereby performed all the terminal constructions available after different stopping times.

**Recurrent continuation.** Commission from the same initial history. Each actual construction leaves or reconstructs an interface through which later lots can be captured and further layers formed. Record the work and parts used for both construction and interface formation. Compare prefixes receiving the same quantity and kind of supply; differing capture times and unused stocks remain visible.

**Discriminator.** The claim is about repeated completed formation in one history, not the union of incompatible terminal histories. The created structure must actually participate in the next enabled transition. A phase flag alone cannot identify that participation. Conversely, a fixed constructive recipe can legitimately supply the transition law; deriving that recipe's physical realization is a further task.

**Boundary test.** Replace the supply by a finite prefix followed by no arrivals. Retaining a port must not create missing material or work. An indefinitely supplied example would establish a conditional possibility of continuation, not infinite growth from finite support. The batch artifact's finite completion is not thereby worthless or ethically defective. The ethical candidate instead receives a precisely specified difference in continuing generation to appraise.

**Empirical identification.** Follow completed formations across rounds, source depletion and the next-round operation of the formed interface. A matched intervention disabling that interface while preserving the material supply tests its causal role; any collateral damage must be recorded. Fresh researcher-installed interfaces or newly initialized samples cannot be counted as autonomous re-formation of the same interface history.

## 3. Reproduction, repertoire and changed generative competence

**Common configuration.** Begin after one assembled functional stage, with the same finite lots, deadlines and admissible inputs. Compare spending each additional lot on another copy of that stage versus extending a network while retaining earlier taps. In the present prefix model these operations can be compared after equal two-unit intakes; they leave different artifacts even when both restore the assembly interface.

**Discriminator already available.** More copies increase multiplicity without increasing the family of input–output functions. Extension adds a function distinguishable from each earlier prefix on the common domain, with earlier taps retained. Count distinct operational relations, not merely different names, state encodings or arbitrary strings.

**Further interface still needed.** A larger output repertoire is not automatically greater ability to *form* later organizations. Specify a later construction whose enabling relations use the new output or arrangement. Under the same available material, time and input conditions, remove or bypass the newly formed relation and test whether that construction remains possible. If an unchanged central assembler can perform all the same constructions at the same cost, greater output diversity has been established, but the proposed additional generative competence has not.

This comparison does not demand novelty outside every fixed grammar. New effective capabilities within a fixed law can be real. It does require stating whether the result concerns distinct outputs, new compositions, shorter feasible paths, or a genuinely enlarged finite-budget repertoire. A later configuration reached from an earlier one does not thereby add states outside the earlier configuration's unrestricted all-time reachability set: its continuations were already reachable through that configuration. The relevant change is in what is enabled now under specified support and time conditions. An unavailable distinguishing input must not be silently supplied by the observer.

**Empirical identification.** Measure actual output responses and later construction under controlled inputs, including maintenance and access costs. A sequence catalog alone does not establish functional variety; functional variety alone does not establish reuse in formation. The intervention must distinguish these successive claims.

## 4. Central generation versus capacities formed in its products

**Common configuration.** Two arrangements produce the same initial set of artifacts from the same budget. In one, later formation passes through an enduring central assembly relation. In the other, some formed products embody interfaces through which they participate in later assembly. Charge their construction, communication, coordination and maintenance to the common history. Products are not presumed independent owners; their different operational roles arise from these relations.

**Positive test.** After initial construction, introduce a specified interruption of one assembly route or a changed supply location. Compare which further forms remain executable, through which actual connections, by what deadline and with what losses. A product-supported route may preserve formation after the former center becomes unavailable. A central arrangement with equivalent redundancy may do equally well. Dispersed interfaces may instead consume the materials needed for further formation.

**Equivalence control.** If the two arrangements preserve all relevant feasible productions, dependencies, control relations and costs, merely redescribing one as distributed supplies no generative or ethical advantage. If products can also alter their assembly rules, represent and test that additional power; physical dispersion alone does not establish it.

**Ethical use.** The relevant candidate difference is how formed relations sustain and transform further generation. Centralization is not stipulated bad or decentralization good. Where living participants occur, effects on their actual activity, understanding and burdens belong to the same comparison; increased network throughput cannot erase them.

## 5. Nonbiological generation and cross-scope displacement

**Clean broad-scope case.** Within a declared nonbiological configuration, compare retaining unassembled parts with using a fixed finite supply to form a network that produces operationally distinct structures and reusable interfaces. Record consumed work, retained parts, created structures, waste and lost alternatives. No living beneficiary or future service to humans is built into the description. This tests whether organization-generating organization is itself a favorable realization under the broadest scope. Resource use remains a real difference; the case does not obtain goodness by stipulating that every consequence is costless.

**Connected variant.** Let a represented supply also maintain a biological renewal cycle. Allocation to the organizational network now changes that cycle's actual replenishment. Compare a regime that exhausts its enabling stock with one that supplies both continuations, stating how the additional provision or reduced use is achieved. Hold the network's structural score fixed where possible so that a changed biological effect is not hidden by its score. Conversely, preserve any genuine organizational achievement in the exhausting regime rather than defining it out of existence.

**Discriminator.** The first case supplies a candidate positive judgment without biology; the second tests relations between authorized scopes. Neither increasing a network score nor noting biological loss by itself settles the full ranking. The theory must explain whether the changes admit common improvement, a qualified favorable aspect or a real conflict. A simple-life/complex-network exchange is therefore an ethical comparison to investigate, not a formal refutation of Ren's whole criterion.

**Empirical identification.** Identify shared support routes, measure both organizations' formation and depletion, and distinguish source conversion from relocation of costs. An observed increase in one scope cannot establish that unmeasured effects in another are absent. These scenarios are proposed model comparisons, not observations about an actual ecology or nonbiological system.

## 10. Reconfiguration and the scope of constructive ending

7 October 2026. AI-authored independent review of [ReconfigurableRoutes](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/ReconfigurableRoutes.lean) and the [ethical continuation §11](GENERATIVE-CAPACITY-ETHICAL-ARGUMENT.md#11-continuation-productive-reconfiguration-and-a-reason-to-end-a-particular-arrangement). The connection under review is whether the end of a previously productive arrangement can itself participate in further differentiated formation, and what that establishes about its ethical significance. This is not another test of a supplied objective's persistence.

### The changed law and what it actually preserves

The extension retains the old states and operations and adds three operations: release, formation of an AC dyad, and completion of Z. Z is the ordered tree `((a,c),b)`. At the common address `[left,right]`, Z yields c, L yields b, and R has no atom. The new formation therefore has an explicit structural discriminator. No claim about semantic novelty, life or increased functional complexity follows from this address test alone.

Release is now an actual event requiring a spanning bar and one available work unit. It removes the bar, returns one part to the unassembled stock, increments spent work and preserves both completed products and any pending assembly. It is not the earlier model's matched configuration map. The new trace accounting includes the initially supplied lot, the bar, pending assemblies and completed products. Because parts are a fungible natural-number stock, this is material participation at the level of accounted quantities and operations; it does not trace the identity of a particular physical particle. Intact recovery, subsequent role assignment, the AC recipe and synchronized joining efficiency remain supplied local laws.

The old steps and histories embed forward through `Event.old`. This is a positive inclusion result. An enlarged operation repertoire need not preserve old impossibility results, so those results cannot be imported merely by calling the extension conservative. The new work bounds and material-starvation results must use the enlarged process, as the source does. No independent agent, owner, evaluator or external source of value is introduced by the local accounting decomposition.

### Material necessity differs from a reason to select Z

After the two-use prefix of the source `(9 parts, 6 work)`, the model retains L and R, the spanning bar, two unassembled parts and three work units. With no further source lot, a history that excludes release cannot complete another selected product: it never has three parts available across stock and partial assembly. Release followed by AC formation and Z completion reaches the third product and consumes the remaining work. The prior realizations remain. This directly answers the earlier merely hypothetical Z objection with an actual continuation.

However, this does not make Z the only further supported formation. From that same state, release followed by ordinary L assembly also consumes three work units and the recovered stock. The resulting endpoints have the same remaining stock, work, historical expenditure and absent bar, and each retains the two prior products plus one new product. The substantive difference is a third structure versus another instance of an existing structure. This is a stronger comparison for the next ethical inquiry than comparing Z with a permanently retained, unusable bar: it holds the dismantling and support accounts fixed. It does not resolve the choice by output count, and a structural type count alone does not justify a universal preference for novelty over repetition.

A distinct source `(10 parts, 6 work)` leaves **three**, rather than two, unassembled parts after the same L/R prefix. Here Z can be formed directly while retaining the bar; release is unnecessary for that realization. The reviewed ethical §11.4 now explicitly preserves this route. The direct-Z endpoint retains one work unit and the bar; release-then-Z retains one unassembled part and no work. A further synchronized L instead retains two work units and the bar. No two additional three-part products can be completed from this state's total material account. Thus there is an actual choice, but it is not accurately described as Z versus retaining the bar. The earlier necessity inference was a representation error about the available route and has been corrected, not an ethical counterexample to the fuller proposal.

### The longer history revises an earlier advantage

For a list of k L/R formations followed by Z, the coupled history includes formation of the bar, k synchronized uses, release and two ordinary Z operations. Its work use is k + 4, including both arrangement and release; every final product accounts for three parts, and no bar is retained. The separate route's comparison requires two work operations per completed product. At k = 2 the checked source supplies a full ordinary history with the same endpoint as the coupled history, not merely equal output counts. At k ≥ 3 the expanded bypass bound rules out completing the same number within the coupled allowance. The finite-support efficiency claim thus changes when the later conversion is included.

The small-source control is equally important. With three parts and two work units, direct Z formation is supported. Attaching the bar first leaves two raw parts and one work unit; releasing it uses the final work, and the enlarged process cannot complete a product from that premature commitment. This is a genuine loss under one supplied law and common source, not a freely toggled ethical predicate. It defeats any general recommendation to specialize first or the suggestion that recoverability alone erases formation cost.

### What the affirmative ethical argument adds, and what it still owes

The positive advance is a reasoned account of **generation through transformation of its own conditions**. The achieved common relation need not remain in its present configuration for the history to continue forming differentiated organization. On Ren's proposed positive criterion, a supported further realization can supply a practical consideration for changing that relation, including ending its synchronized operation. This does not require independent value owners, private return to an initiator or an additional good stipulated outside the unfolding. The ethical section properly keeps earlier realizations, actual support and displaced routes in view.

The concrete controls narrow that consideration. Release is necessary for *any* further selected product in the two-part case, but this does not select Z over repeated L. In the three-part case the proposed further Z already has a route retaining the bar, so Z by itself does not favor release. These findings improve the factual and relational grounds of practical judgment. They do not independently establish the substantive identity between further differentiated generation and universally favorable ethical quality.

The remaining rival can accept every formation and accounting result, as well as constructive endings, while challenging the weight of additional differentiation compared with repeated realization and supported remaining conditions. A nondual account must explain the actual quality and practical significance of these different continuations. It need not add a separate moral substance, and no conclusion of impossibility follows from the local model's omission. But promoting this fixed tree repertoire, its type count or its work economy to a universal measure would leave the central warrant supplied.

The present bar has no represented experience, ongoing self-good or dependence of a living center; its earlier products survive release. Applying this result to ending a life would therefore change the relevant phenomenon. The ethical continuation explicitly retains that scope limit. No new empirical evidence was introduced, and no general law of physical recovery or synchronization is inferred from the executable recipe. With the raw-two/raw-three correction retained and the matched repeated-L control included, no further substantive blocker to this bounded continuation was identified. Universal normative grounding remains unresolved.
