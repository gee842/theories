# Continuation grounding: restating the Universal Metaethical Core from what wants its continuation

2026-10-10, Asia/Bangkok. Written by the AI (Claude Opus 5.5) at Ren's instruction. This is a plan: nothing enters the core and no result is registered; the [feasibility sketch](../history/root-foundation/development/continuation-grounding-sketch/README.md) is kept as a development draft. G0 remains the adopted axiom until the restatement is implemented, checked, reviewed and accepted by Ren.

<a id="0-结论--summary"></a>
## 0. Summary

**English.** Ren's proposal splits the core in two.

1. **Extent, with no ethical axiom.** Anything that wants its continuation gives that continuation significance from where it stands. Following the want, it must count what its continuation depends on: beyond its boundary, beyond its horizon and beyond its own draw. The dynamics derives this extent:
   - under balance and dissipation a continuing finite family is not closed, and, enclosed around, something is conveyed into it from beyond;
   - no finite horizon separates renewal from consumption;
   - draws each within renewal can together exceed it and end what depends on the shared condition at its need; a want that outlasts what the condition holds must count the joint draw (qualified in W8).
2. **Center, by one axiom.** The dynamics fixes the extent, not the center. The derived extent runs one way, to what the self depends on; it counts others as conditions and as supply, not as beings that want; it ends at the horizon of the want; and a careful totalizer satisfies all of it. The axiom's one job is to put a center at every point.

Each half removes one duality: the first a closed self, the second a privileged center.

**Formal content (sketched, no axioms):**

- Where every living claim lies in some position's own region (it is held), G0 is exactly S0 at every position with standpoint neutrality N_std; neither connection nor N_rel is used (`significant_iff_held`).
- In general, where every living claim is reached through formed relations from a held one, G0 is S0 at every position, N_std, and N_rel only into claims no position holds (`significant_iff_reached`). Each part has a countermodel; none can be dropped.

So for what wants, the AI reads Ren's one axiom as standpoint neutrality (pending Ren): what has significance from any center has it from every standpoint, and no center is privileged; a center at every wanting point is S0. (W8 correction: the earlier text said “Ren's one axiom is standpoint neutrality”, attributing the AI's factorization to Ren; nor is N_std the only completion, since on a connected whole where every standpoint holds a living claim S0 with N_rel also gives G0.) The existing characterization starts from one seed and needs connection and N_rel to carry significance everywhere. The new one places a seed wherever something wants and needs only N_std to make the seeds common. N_rel is left for lives no standpoint holds: a dormant seed, an unconscious patient or someone who no longer wants to go on, when no one else counts them as their own, and wholes that do not want as wholes.

**Weak and strong forms.** The weak form (“as long as not harm”) matches the current readings of `living`, all of which read “no damage” (不损). The strong form (“needs to create more significance”) is the whole's further generation, the direction of 生生不息, which `living` does not yet contain. The working structure is the AI's suggestion, affirmed by Ren's “exactly”: the weak form for each formation, the strong form for the whole. That is G0's own shape, living and further generation, built from below.

The plan has eight phases, W1–W8 (§5), six choices for Ren (§6), and turns with the parallel session on shared files (§8).

<a id="3-范围动力学推出什么--extent-what-the-dynamics-derives"></a>
## 3. Extent: what the dynamics derives

The extent half applies one premise of practical reason, that wanting an end gives reason for what the end requires, to the dynamics. What follows is what continuation requires. It does not say that continuation is good.

<a id="31-边界之外没有封闭的自我--beyond-the-boundary-no-closed-self-continues"></a>
### 3.1 Beyond the boundary: no closed self continues

**Checked.** Balance says each change of a level is what is given minus what is taken. Dissipation says that across a step at which a finite family is closed, each formation gives it no more than it takes from it, and less whenever it takes. Under both, a finite family on which taking recurs is not closed at arbitrarily late steps; enclosed around from some step on, it draws in from beyond itself at arbitrarily late steps (`Flow.taking_draws_in`, [no-finite-part-bears-taking](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/no-finite-part-bears-taking.md)). Embodied arising is itself replenishment and opens a family the same way (`generation_draws_in`, [generation-is-replenishment](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/generation-is-replenishment.md)). A balanced, dissipative world with finitely many standing at each step, whose arising is embodied, comes to rest once every step from some point is enclosed around what stands (`World.comes_to_rest`, [continued-change-needs-openness](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md)).

**New (W1, sketched).** The same law stated as conveyance. A formation present at a step *conveys* from `c` to `d` when it takes from `c` and gives to `d` (`Flow.Conveys`); a family is *fed from beyond* at a step when something outside it is conveyed into a member (`Flow.FedFromBeyond`). With equality decided, a step at which nothing beyond the family is conveyed into it draws nothing in (`Flow.inward_of_not_fed`). So a continuing family cannot, from any step on, be fed from nothing beyond it (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`). Ren's sentence needs this form: a system that continues does so together with what lies beyond it.

**New (W2, sketched).** Read with one kind of relatum, conveyance relates two events at a common step when something is conveyed between their relata (`Flow.conveyance`; `living` is the stepwise reading, unchanged). Under that reading the family's region is not a cut in the formed relations: a relation leaves it at arbitrarily late steps (`Flow.boundary_crossed`, `Flow.Cut`, `Flow.region_not_a_cut`). The boundary is formed and real, yet it closes nothing that continues. Whether conveyance counts as a formed relation, beside interaction, living on and descent, is Ren's choice.

**Scope.** Dissipation does the work. Without it a closed family can generate without end: two relata hand a unit back and forth (`Handing`), or a closed cycle keeps its total (`Recycle`). So “the pure self is an illusion” is a theorem under dissipation, not a law of every unfolding. Whether actual systems dissipate in this sense is empirical correspondence.

<a id="32-视界之外僵硬的自我--beyond-the-horizon-the-rigid-self"></a>
### 3.2 Beyond the horizon: the rigid self

**Checked.** For every horizon `h` there are two runs that agree before `h`, one renewing and one consuming. A reading decided by the first `h` steps that credits every renewing run credits some consuming run (`finite_reading_credits_consumption`, [becoming-finite-horizon](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/becoming-finite-horizon.md)). A reading of amounts, drawing at a step no more than is renewed, is fixed by the history through that step and withholds every draw beyond renewal before the horizon, in the renewing run as in the consuming one (`amount_reading`). Under the supplied wear law, consumption forms more at first and a one-step count prefers it; past a bound set by what was held and the renewal rate, every continuation within renewal has formed more than every exceeding one ([renewing-consuming-formation](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/renewing-consuming-formation.md)).

**New (shared with W3, sketched).** On the spine, `n` consecutive steps of joint excess need a level of at least `n` at the start (`Flow.excess_bounded`). The time during which excess can go on is bounded by what was held.

**Reading.** A self that judges by the outcomes visible within a window credits consumption. This is not a lack of information that more foresight would cure, since no finite window of outcomes separates the two. What separates them is counting the draw against renewal. A formation that wants its continuation without end must count that way. This is the formal content of “unable to see longer horizon”.

<a id="33-自身取用之外总体化的自我--beyond-ones-own-draw-the-totalizing-self"></a>
### 3.3 Beyond one's own draw: the totalizing self

**New (W3, sketched).** Each formation present can take from a condition within what the step gives it (`Flow.WithinOwn`) while together they take more (`Flow.WithinJoint` fails; `Pair.own_not_joint`). Taking jointly within what is given keeps the level (`Flow.joint_keeps`). Each step of joint excess lowers it by at least one (`Flow.excess_lowers`), so joint excess cannot outlast what was held, and in the two-formation case no web balances the flow at all (`Pair.no_web`).

**Planned (W3).** Build a support-gated commons on the spine. The renewer depends on the condition and gives only while active (`Acting`). Joint excess drives the level below the renewer's support, the renewer stops giving, and every formation depending on the condition at such a need ends, a taker among them where it so depends. The periphery has this in one wear-law class (`RenewingFormation.joint_excess_ends_shared_formation`, `jointly_exceeding_parts_end_shared_formation`). The core's support-gated law (`RelationalRenewal`) and `Becoming.Substitution` show the sustainer losing its support for good once a single consumer exceeds renewal ([substitution-preserves-consumption](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/substitution-preserves-consumption.md)). W3 states the joint case on the spine and connects to these through an instance theorem where one exists, and an interpretive link otherwise.

**Reading.** The meta-attractor selects formations that take more: they form more at first, and a count over a step prefers them. Each counts its own draw. Many of them together exceed renewal; the shared condition ends, and with it what depends on it at such a need, the winners among them where they so depend. A formation that wants its continuation beyond what the shared condition holds must count the joint draw on what it depends on (W8: `n` steps of joint excess need only a level of `n`, and `Mortal`'s want is fully met amid joint excess): “the dynamics that happens beyond what the "self" sees”. This is what the want requires, not a verdict passed on it.

<a id="34-推出的范围达不到的--what-the-derived-extent-does-not-reach-w4"></a>
### 3.4 What the derived extent does not reach (W4)

Following a want gives reason to secure what the wanted continuation needs. Four limits follow from that form. W4 builds a witness for each:

1. **One way.** The derived extent runs to what the self depends on, not to what depends on it. *Witness:* a downstream formation lives on what a self gives; the self stops giving, the downstream formation ends, and the self continues unaffected.
2. **As supply.** It reaches others as conditions. Where sources are replaceable, it reaches them as supply, not as particular beings: being fed from beyond is necessary, while no particular source is. *Witness:* a family fed alternately from two sources, either of which can be removed. Under N_rel, valuing reaches each source (`Flow.valuing_reaches_source`), but that is the axiom's relational half, not the want. (W8: the implementation used `Lone`, which shows only that no source condition need recur, the one sun doing all the conveying; the two-source witness remains open, and the claim is reduced to: being fed is needed, no source condition need recur.)
3. **The horizon of the want.** A want for one's own continuation over a finite life gives no reason concerning what comes after its end. *Witness:* a formation active for finitely many steps draws down a shared condition; another formation that depends on it ends after the first has ended.
4. **The careful totalizer.** A formation can meet every derived requirement and still end every life it does not need. It is fed from beyond, counts its draw against renewal and keeps the joint draw within renewal. *Witness:* a valuation with S0 at every position and transmission along what each continuation requires, in which some living claim has no significance from some standpoint. (W8: the first implementation used transmission into each own region, which follows from S0; the witness now has each standpoint's significance closed along feeding, `Flow.FedBy`.)

So the dynamics fixes the extent of a system, not its center. If the fourth witness could not be built, because the derived requirements forced standpoint neutrality, the center would follow from the dynamics. That would be a stronger result, and it would change the account (§9). (W8: as first implemented the test could not fail, since flow and web predicates never constrain significance. With closure along feeding it constrains significance; the line holds along dependence, and not along every formed relation.)

<a id="4-中心一条公理--center-the-one-axiom"></a>
## 4. Center: the one axiom

<a id="41-形式内容--formal-content"></a>
### 4.1 Formal content

In `Becoming.Metaethics`, a valuation has positions, claims, `living`, `related`, `own p` (the region of formed relations position `p` counts as its own) and `sig p` (significance from `p`'s standpoint).

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| S0 at `p` (`SelfGood p`) | `p`'s own living claims are significant from `p` |
| N_rel (`transmits`) | significance passes along formed relations to related living claims |
| N_std (`shares`) | a living claim significant from one standpoint is significant from every standpoint |
| G0 at the level of claims (`Significant`) | every living claim is significant from every standpoint |

The sketch adds two definitions:

- a life is **held** when some position counts it as its own (`Valuation.Held`, all living claims held);
- it is **unheld** otherwise (`Valuation.Unheld`).

It proves the following, without axioms:

- **Held lives.** `significant_iff_held`: if every living claim is held, then G0 ↔ (S0 at every position) ∧ N_std.
- **Reached lives.** `significant_iff_reached`: if holding is decided and every living claim is reached through formed relations from a living held claim, then G0 ↔ (S0 at every position) ∧ N_std ∧ (N_rel into unheld claims).
- **Minimality.** Three countermodels:
  - `OwnStandpoints.standpoint_neutrality_is_needed`: each life is held and valued by its own standpoint only, so each center is a center only for itself;
  - `Unheld.held_is_needed`;
  - `Reached.transmission_is_needed`.

Decided holding is a logical commitment of the representation, disclosed like decided equality.

**Planned instance (W5).** Read a web stepwise. Let each formation be a position whose own region is its own events (`own f e := e.1 = f`). Every living event is then held, so G0 there is S0 at every formation with N_std. Neither connection nor a correspondence for `related` is needed. This is the formal shape of “anything that wants its continuation”, where every formation wants its own.

<a id="42-与现有刻画的关系--relation-to-the-existing-characterization"></a>
### 4.2 Relation to the existing characterization

`significant_iff` holds on a connected whole with some living claim: G0 ↔ Seed ∧ N_rel ∧ N_std ([metaethical-core-characterization](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md)). That route starts from one seed and needs connection and N_rel to carry significance everywhere. Much of chapter §6.3 secures connection: the correspondence discipline, living on and descent. The new route seeds every wanting point and needs only N_std to make the seeds common. For held lives, connection and N_rel drop out; they keep a role only for unheld lives.

Both characterize the same claim-level content. Neither set of grounds implies the other in general, but where the whole is connected and every life is held, both are equivalent to G0. The existing result keeps its scope.

The shape of rivals in chapter §6.2 persists. In the new form the principal rival accepts S0 everywhere and limits significance to each position's own standpoint. That is a rule kept inside a formed boundary, and it is exactly what the axiom denies.

The evaluator route ([self-good-reachable-g0](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/self-good-reachable-g0.md)) found that S0 with monotonicity and tolerance gives a standpoint-indexed instance on reachable generation. It left unindexed G0 needing a bridge from standpoint-relative to normative significance. The restatement names that bridge as the one axiom, and shows that on held lives it is all G0 needs beyond S0.

<a id="43-弱式与强式--weak-and-strong-forms"></a>
### 4.3 Weak and strong forms

**Weak form, for each formation.** Ren: “doesn't have to be positive, we could argue as long as not harm”. All current readings of `living` read “no damage” (不损) and none contains further generation (chapter §6.3):

- whole-run: active and not consuming in the run;
- stepwise: active and not undermining;
- relation level.

So the weak form is the axiom over these readings: continuation that does not damage another's has significance. A compatible second reading: ending or undermining a held continuation enters judgment as a loss through C, which ranks lossless extensions. A deontic reading (added in W8): each continuation's significance is a claim on others not to harm it, rather than a filter admitting only undamaging continuations; it is not yet formalized, and the choice is Ren's. Read as a filter, the stepwise `living` puts “not harm” inside S0 (`Totalizer.keeper_not_living`).

**The level of harm.** Almost all life continues by ending other life.

- At the level of members, nearly all eating is harm.
- At the level of lineages (`Becoming.Identity` has lineage and member levels) or of renewal, taking within renewal is not.
- At the level of the whole, harm is undermining the whole's continuation.

This is Ren's choice.

**Strong form, for the whole.** “continuation into the future needs to create more significance(being generative)”. This is G0's second clause, further generation. It is also the direction in Ren's answer on ethical quality: 生生不息, 不断创造更多生的方向, with rising possibility, diversity and complexity. Formal readings:

- more without end (`Flow.More`), which needs replenishment and renewers without eventual bound ([more-needs-renewers](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/more-needs-renewers.md)) and a world that does not come to rest ([continued-change-needs-openness](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md));
- arising that recurs (`World.ArisingRecurs`);
- possibility, diversity and complexity, which are still open.

Direction, continuing capacity, per-step monotonic growth and scalar maximization are different claims. The strong form is read as direction unless Ren says otherwise. It can be met only where the world stays open, and that is the extent half's conclusion seen from the whole.

**Working structure.** The weak form for each formation, the strong form for the whole.

### 4.4 Draft of the restatement

> Anything that wants its continuation gives that continuation significance from where it stands. Following that want, it must count what its continuation depends on. <a id="45-公理之后仍在的--what-remains-after-the-axiom"></a>
### 4.5 What remains after the axiom

- **Non-ethical premises.**
  - physical: balance, dissipation, embodiment, enclosure;
  - correspondence: which relations are formed (conveyance, interaction, living on, descent) and which formations want;
  - the reading of wanting (S0);
  - the step from wanting an end to having reason for what it requires;
  - the representation: discrete steps, natural-number amounts, decided equality and holding.
- **Normative warrant.** The axiom asserts that standpoint-invariant significance is normative significance. Its philosophical ground stays an open item, as G0's does now (chapter §8).
- **Practical judgment.** The axiom gives every held continuation significance. It does not decide between conflicting continuations; C, L and J1–J4 do, together with the chosen level of harm.

“This closes the whole” therefore reads as follows: G0's content on held lives needs no further ethical premise. Practical judgment, correspondence and the non-ethical premises stay open as before.

## 6. Choices for Ren
