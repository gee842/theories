## Two original experiments

### Ratcliff, Denison, Borrello and Travisano (2012)

“Experimental evolution of multicellularity,” *PNAS* 109:1595–1600; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [published early-edition PDF](https://websites.umich.edu/~zhanglab/clubPaper/02_10_2012.pdf).

**Access:** all six PDF pages read as extracted text, including captions and procedure descriptions. Supplementary methods, movies and raw data were not read; this is not a complete protocol audit.

**Observed formation:** initially unicellular yeast evolved snowflake clusters under selection for rapid settling. Ten replicate populations were dominated by this form after 60 transfers. Enzymatically separated cells regenerated clusters through incomplete separation after division. Descendants therefore reproduced the association; experimenters did not repeatedly assemble its members. Clusters released multicellular propagules and required growth before reproducing. Subsequent divergent settling regimes changed reproduction size and juvenile duration.

**Inheritance and supplied recurrence:** three populations derived from one evolved population retained the phenotype through 35 transfers without settling selection. This supports persistence in that background, not indefinite stability. Researchers supplied nutrient-rich medium, daily transfer and preferential transfer of rapidly settling material; the first week used passive settling, subsequently brief centrifugation. Evolved organization changed what underwent this filtering, while the experiment retained the schedule. Postdivision development makes close relatedness a consequence of the formed association rather than a fresh assignment of members.

### Herron and colleagues (2019)

“De novo origins of multicellularity in response to predation,” *Scientific Reports* 9:2328; [DOI and article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-39558-8), [publisher PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-39558-8.pdf).

**Access:** all nine PDF pages read as extracted text, including Methods, pp.6–8. Supplementary videos and underlying datasets were not examined; no reanalysis was performed.

**Observed formation:** two of five *Chlamydomonas* populations exposed to *Paramecium* evolved multicellular organization within approximately 750 asexual generations; controls did not. The resulting life cycles varied. Some isolates formed strictly clonal clusters within maternal walls and released multicellular propagules; others also captured free-swimming cells in extracellular matrix. Do not describe every evolved association as clonal. Traits persisted in cultures maintained for four years without predators. Existing cellular capacities could be recruited into the new organization.

**Selection and supplied recurrence:** experimenters supplied crossed founders, predators, light and medium. Weekly transfer mixed cultures and diluted samples into fresh medium; it did not sort or assemble clusters. A separate protection assay used rotifers rather than the original predator, measuring growth through absorbance. It supported protective effects, with that assay scope. The experiment couples ecological interaction to inherited changes in recurrence, while leaving laboratory maintenance supplied. Success in only some populations preserves contingency rather than universalizing the transition.

## What the evidence positively advances

**AI reconstruction:** the stronger generative proposal can treat selection conditions as historically changeable relations. A developed association can determine which descendants remain together, what counts as a reproductive event and how effects of constituent activity are transmitted. These are candidates for mechanisms by which a local advantage for collective continuation becomes a more persistent developmental relation. This is a substantial answer to the objection that collective organization must always be an investigator's static partition.

The scientific target is a history: earlier interaction changes development; development reconstructs association; association changes subsequent exposure and differential reproduction; inherited changes participate in later cycles. Each connection needs evidence within the relevant history. Combining separate papers into this explanatory pattern is a research reconstruction, not observation of a single unrestricted recursive process.

The distinction between experimentally supplied conditions and evolved organization is methodological. It identifies which relationships the experiment allows to vary and explains, and which it maintains for the comparison. Investigators, cultures and physical conditions are differentiated activities within the same reality. An experimental boundary does not establish a separate ontological “environment,” nor prove that supplied conditions cannot themselves have a generative history. Equally, including them within reality does not yet explain that history.

## Remaining universal inference and decisive next test

The proposed recursive extension requires more than the existence of inherited grouping. It must explain when forming and maintaining such recurrence actually favors wider future formation, when alternatives erase that advantage, and whether the relevant response can arise before irreversible loss. A destructive trajectory need not wait for a higher-scale correction to become available. A whole whose components interact is also not automatically a reproducing population of wholes; differential continuation at that scope requires its own identified mechanism.

The next discriminating test should therefore follow one coupled history in which a previously supplied condition of selection becomes an inherited outcome, then examine whether that outcome survives changes in its original advantage. Track formation, transmission, reconstruction and changed consequences together. Include a rival history with comparable immediate success but deteriorating renewal, and a history where renewal benefits occur without the organization increasing. These are proposed requirements, not experiments performed here. They test the strongest hypothesis without demanding that every constructive effect be immediate or monotonic.

For universal meta-ethics, the premise advanced is **the possibility of an endogenous history for capacities that sustain further organized activity**. The still-substantive inference concerns why the resulting whole configuration warrants ethical affirmation and practical reasons for all intelligence, including where a locally successful orientation would knowingly refuse them. Neither inherited persistence nor increasingly effective selection by itself answers that question. This does not divide facts and values into separate realities; it distinguishes causal formation, justified appraisal, recognition and motivation within the same unfolding.

The evidence strengthens a particular causal connection. It supplies no defensible estimate that the universal finish line is near. No new mathematical theorem is claimed, and no Lean verification is represented by this source assessment.
