## 1. What changes in the explanation

The [previous round](COMPOSITIONAL-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md) formed and repaired a compositional operation. Its additional evidence came from a supplied reference demonstration. Here a participant's installed operation produces the demonstration, consumes its own support, and changes another participant's retained sample. The recipient reconstructs an operation through the existing construction procedure and can subsequently become a demonstrator. No hidden program is copied into the recipient.

This closes a particular **operation → public consequence → acquired sample → reconstruction → later public consequence** connection. It distinguishes three outcomes: faithful reconstruction, reconstruction that changes the teacher's operation because the encounters underdetermine it, and transmission that faithfully retains an operation which fails an independently specified task. A successful communication chain is therefore insufficient evidence of either task adequacy or ethical quality.

This is a local cognitive-developmental connection within the wider theory. The inherited [transmission](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/evolution/ConsequenceTransmission.lean) and [developmental reconstruction](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/evolution/development/DevelopedResponse.lean) already explain consequence-sensitive access and formation of an operative response after copying. They retain their native material correspondence and original checked scope. The new module imports the compositional apparatus and supplies its communication interface; it does not import those chemical models or assert that their state spaces are identical.

## 2. Relations within the common history

The communicative roles differentiate through actual access and use. One participant currently supplies an output; another receives and retains it. After reconstruction, the latter can supply outputs in turn. The boundary is not a fundamental speaker/world or mind/culture split. The model retains the relation between installed operation, output, support, sample and later response while omitting bodily development, attention, trust formation and the creation of the encounter schedule. Its local mature starting condition does not explain the origin of a mature human.

The same received trace can have different explanatory roles. It may accurately record what someone demonstrated yet poorly indicate the operation they possess. It may accurately indicate that operation yet fail to support a useful task response. Or it may disclose a discrepancy that the recipient lacks the conditions to repair. Evidence about cultural transmission, task correctness and the formation of epistemic reliability must retain these differences. The [knowledge account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/KNOWLEDGE.md) already distinguishes acquired content and grounds; this construction instantiates one social dependence rather than redefining knowledge as agreement.

A cultural convention is not inherently false because its mapping differs from another convention. The adverse cases below are assessed against an independently specified composition task, with a concrete required output distinction. If that task is removed, the formal result is a difference between operations, not a free-standing verdict that a community's convention is erroneous. Likewise, loss of a task distinction is not a complete G0/J ethical assessment.

## 4. Lean results and retained failures

The [checker](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/check_compositional_transmission.py) and [successful receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/compositional-transmission-verification.json) record **27 new named theorems / 45 explicit declaration audits**, with **151 inherited audits** rebuilt separately: 34 correspondence, 52 acquisition and 65 formation. The new dependencies are 12 declarations with no axioms, 18 with `propext`, and 15 with `propext` and `Quot.sound`. Sources, checker, statement and dependency logs are retained per run. Generated declarations compile but are not separately counted. Admitted proofs, new axioms, unsafe code and `native_decide` are excluded.

The results answer distinct questions:

1. **Does the evidence actually come from another operation?** `delivery_uses_actual_response` identifies the appended sample and remaining sender support. `teacher_program_changes_received_evidence` changes the actual demonstration when the installed route changes. `absent_response_does_not_fabricate_sample` preserves a failed encounter.
2. **Is support retained through the communication history?** `send_preserves_joint_support` and `lesson_preserves_joint_support` account for each participant's remaining and spent support. The subsequent `develop` accounting is inherited. Reception and execution charges are local conventions, not measurements of human effort.
3. **Can hidden source records bypass communication?** `learning_uses_sender_interface` proves that this recipient's result depends on the sender's available response interface. Unsent records and construction history do not secretly install a program.
4. **Can a reconstructed operation recur?** With distinct atomic effects, a generated two-connector route and sufficient sender support, actual demonstrations reconstruct that program and leave enough recipient support for another basic lesson. `every_cohort_reconstructs_the_transmitted_operation` proves this for every finite number of successions under the stated provisions. For the original law family, `every_cohort_retains_all_expression_correspondence` preserves the installed operation's meaning on every finite expression. It does not assert sufficient support to execute every arbitrarily large expression.
5. **Can stable transmission retain a genuine task failure?** Yes. The selective route repeats the first argument and ignores the second. It merges two expressions whose required task outputs differ, yet is faithfully reconstructed in every cohort. Extra support alone does not correct that content. The task distinction and actual response functions are explicit.
6. **Can transmission change a competent operation?** With equal primitive effects, a correct teacher's basic demonstrations underdetermine its route. The recipient constructs a duplicate route. `initial_limitation_can_change_and_then_stabilize_operation` proves that this change persists through every later finite cohort under the unchanged schedule, and `stabilized_change_can_fail_the_original_task` retains the failure. The extended demonstration prevents the initial misreconstruction in the checked comparison.
7. **Can correction occur without succession?** A peer's actual asymmetric demonstration enters an existing participant's records and triggers the inherited reconsideration/construction path. The corrected participant then executes a further untrained expression. An erroneous peer can instead preserve the error; receiving contrary evidence without enough support can fail to produce an installed operation.

The actual formed-teacher example connects the earlier acquired records and assembly to a new learner and a further learner. The old 18-unit example had enough support for its selected response but cannot complete the three-item teaching schedule; two delivered items remain and reconstruction fails. Additional preparation support permits teaching. This distinguishes competent use from supported social reproduction of that competence.

## 5. Primary evidence and its limits

These are targeted primary-source readings, not a systematic review, raw-data reanalysis or new human experiment. No publication bytes were imported. The formal apparatus is not presented as the implementation of any participant's cognition.

**Senghas, Kita and Özyürek (2004), emerging Nicaraguan sign language.** The [archived accepted manuscript](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/66237/20/WRAP_Senghas_Ms.pdf), especially the cohort comparison and discussion, describes later cohorts separating and sequencing motion components more often than the first cohort. This supports a target involving learners' reconstruction of expression, rather than exact reproduction of preceding forms. The authors discuss reciprocal shaping of languages and learning capacities as an evolutionary possibility, not an observed genetic history. The archive differs from the original abstract in its retrospective number of years; this account does not rely on that duration. The published article itself was not accessible through the retrieval tool. [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1100199).

**Methods qualification for that study.** The [supporting methods](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/66237/3/Senghas_SOM.pdf) describe 30 signers across three exposure cohorts and ten hearing speakers. The manner/path integration analysis used qualifying expressions from 33 participants; seven had produced no expression containing both. Participants narrated to peers, and the hearing comparison considered co-speech gesture. Exposure history, cohort and age at testing were not randomized. Our inference is that this is evidence about changing organization across histories, not a clean isolation of one innate mechanism or a controlled transmission of a single variable. No statistics were independently recomputed.

**Substantive criticism remains an evidence obligation.** A [published comment by Russo and Volterra](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15994513/) and an [author response](https://www.mpi.nl/publications/item57336/response-comment-children-creating-core-properties-language-evidence) were identified. Their full texts were not retrieved; no invented summary or claim to have resolved that dispute is made. The limited source assessment above therefore does not certify the manuscript's broader universal interpretation.

**Kirby, Tamariz, Cornish and Smith (2015).** The [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25966840/) describes simulations and laboratory experiments linking structure to pressures for compressibility and expressivity. This is a substantive existing explanatory competitor and source of mechanisms, not evidence invented by our terminology. Only the abstract was inspected this round; detailed model assumptions and comparative fit are not independently evaluated here. Our proposal preference is a supplied search order, not a derived compression pressure; equivalence to those mechanisms is not established. The [previous round's Raviv comparison](COMPOSITIONAL-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md#5-developmental-and-evolutionary-evidence) prevents promoting generational turnover into a necessary condition for every emergence of composition.

Taken together, the comparisons change the next question: explain how development changes the available segmentation and reconstruction operations, and how common activity changes which distinctions matter and become learnable. The present proofs connect use to others' input but hold the reconstruction procedure fixed. They neither duplicate the source experiments nor settle that remaining developmental connection.

## 6. Author counterreview

**A chain of well-funded identical learners is not evolutionary psychology.** Accepted. It supplies one necessary candidate pathway from performance to later learning. The distributions of learning capacities, their bodily development, varied histories, biological reproduction and population competition remain separately explanatory. Existing biological organization results are credited and not relabeled absent.

**Does the all-cohort result hide infinite free provision?** The 25-unit/eight-part endowment is explicit for each recipient. The theorem concerns every finite prefix conditional on those inputs. It makes no claim that a finite stock supports unlimited generations, or that replenishment occurs automatically.

**Does the successful reconstruction theorem assume success?** It assumes a fixed route family, distinctive atomic effects, faithful encounters and sufficient support. It does not assume the installed result. The lesson generates the samples through `respond`; the search forms and tests candidates; the conclusion identifies the result. The identical-atom and interrupted-lesson cases preserve failures outside those hypotheses.

**Is every stable difference an error?** No. The formal adverse comparison has an independently specified task output, not a preference for the teacher's convention. Stability alone neither selects the task nor establishes its ethical authority. The same implementation could serve other aims, requiring a different assessment.

**Does the module explain why people attend, trust or teach?** No. Contact, schedule, recording and search are supplied. A peer correction here establishes a pathway, not a theory of testimony, intentional pedagogy or mental-state understanding. Describing it as a complete social mind would overreach.

**Does this replace the full target with a language toy?** The [full coverage ledger](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/COGNITION-LEARNING-AND-EVOLUTION.md#1-the-full-target-and-its-completion-obligations) remains authoritative. Perception, broad inference, affect, motivation, social understanding and conscious experience are not completed by these proofs. The source comparison also identifies what this model fails to explain, so the next work cannot merely repeat successful transmission.
