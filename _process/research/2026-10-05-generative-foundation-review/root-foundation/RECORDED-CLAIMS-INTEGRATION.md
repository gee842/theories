## 1. Result

Two centers each hold a one-delivery claim. Each episode supports one
delivery, and that support perishes with the episode. The record states
whose claim is certified fulfilled, the kind of record the
[receipt register](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/succession/fulfillment/README.md)
makes available. A rule treats the centers alike when relabeling them in the
record relabels its choice.

- **One rule follows from two requirements** (`record_rule_alike`,
  `record_rule_nonforeclosing`, `record_rule_unique`). The two requirements
  are to treat the centers alike and to never foreclose needlessly where that
  is possible. Exactly one rule meets both: serve the center whose claim is
  unfulfilled, and withhold once both are met. The rule is derived from the
  requirements, not picked.
- **The record cannot settle the start** (`symmetric_open_forces_withholding`,
  `only_symmetric_open_needs_more`). When neither claim is met, every rule
  that treats the centers alike must withhold. Withholding needlessly
  forecloses both claims. This is the only record where that happens.
- **One external input suffices here** (`both_claims_met_after_first_choice`).
  After any first choice, the derived rule meets the remaining claim in the
  next episode.

## 2. What this means

Recorded fulfillment does real work. Once one claim is met, the history's
own record, together with the requirement to treat centers alike, determines
what comes next. At the fully symmetric point, something the record does not
contain must decide: an agreement, prior arrival, a further fact about the
centers, or a lottery, which is itself a symmetric procedure. The theorem
shows that such an input is needed. It does not say which input is
legitimate or who may supply it. Requirement 5 of the
[completion contract](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md)
asks for exactly this, and this round does not make plural authority
unnecessary.

各方”的要求，便唯一地决定了下一步。只有在完全对称的起点，记录本身无法决定，必须引入

## 3. Premises and limits

- **Perishable support.** If withholding retained support for later use, it
  would not be a needless foreclosure at the symmetric start, and the
  symmetric results would fail. The premise is stated in the module.
- **Model size.** The model has two centers, unit claims, fixed support per
  episode and no new claimants. With more centers an ordering is needed. If
  claims renew, the record returns to the symmetric start each cycle unless
  it keeps counts.
- **Rule class.** The rules considered are deterministic functions of the
  current record. Randomized rules and rules that use history beyond the
  record are outside the theorem.
