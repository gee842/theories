# Forming and revising the use of an acquired prediction

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [governing generative
foundation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md).
This addresses **acquired prediction → formed and revisable prediction-to-action
coupling**. It closes one premise exposed by the concurrent
[prediction/action study](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PREDICTION-ACTION.md):
that circuit learned from actual outcomes but was given a normal or inverted
action rule as a fixed experimental arm.

The new finite construction forms and replaces that rule-bearing structure
inside the actual continuing circuit. The acquired register can remain correct
while an inverted route makes it repeatedly choose the action it predicts will
fail. Those physical failures exhaust the output coupling; paid replacement
changes the action with the same register retained. An actual productive return
then maintains the new coupling. This is a conditional formation mechanism,
not a proof that predictive accuracy necessarily becomes useful action.

## 1. Interface and remaining local laws

The [executable continuation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/README.md)
imports the parent's full preparation without changing its reaction law,
eligibility test, failed outcomes or adapter. Original common fuel is thirty
and source energy eighty-one; both stocks are declared before preparation.
Commissioning has probability 2/9 and leaves twenty-six usable fuel units plus
eight ten-unit packets. The remaining 7/9 retains its actual failed preparation
and unspent energy. This is the absorbing preparation jump chain followed by
an exposure clock, not a common elapsed-time deadline for preparation and use.

The actor forms the actual old D updater (cost two), old selector (one), and
the parent's predictive retrofit (three). D is retooled into the parent's Fast
updater. Its inherited cost, lifetime and paid replacement remain; choosing
this already-declared fixed-Fast arm isolates the next coupling mechanism.
No claim is made that its update algorithm has now been derived.

The new state includes a coupling orientation k∈{0,1} and finite remaining
coupling lifetime. Both orientations start absent. A paid one-unit recipe
forms either fairly. The coupled command is

\[
a=\pi(q,\mathrm{last})\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}k,
\qquad
\pi(q,l)=\begin{cases}0&q<2,\\l&q=2,\\1&q>2.\end{cases}
\]

The acquired register's forecast for action 1 remains q/4. The actual issued
action a and actual receipt C produce the same inherited teaching bit

\[
Y=\begin{cases}a&C=1,\\1-a&C=0,\end{cases}
\qquad q'=4Y
\]

when the write is affordable. The controller receives no hidden environmental
condition, change notification or counterfactual reward. A source outage can
make this decoder wrong, exactly as in the parent law.

The added switch is a new functional arrangement **inside the existing
composite circuit's declared capacity**, with an explicit retooling price.
It is not an unaccounted third material unit. Conversely, two coarse backing
slots do not establish this functional capacity: the output-switch recipe,
its fair proposal chemistry and independent wear/renewal channel are additional
local laws. Retooling preserves the already formed circuit, q, last action and
acquired-record flag. Rebuilding the entire circuit destroys those inherited
records and any pending same-record witness. The two original backing material
units remain assigned to their slots; no fresh material is introduced during
operation. Reuse without extra material loss is itself a declared coarse law.

Every executed trial pays one for retaining the record and three for work and
observation before any return. A capture yields ten and pays one each for
updater, circuit and coupling renewal. A write costs one. Capture resets the
three lifetimes; a failure wears each by one. The primary lifetimes are two,
six and two for updater, circuit and coupling respectively. Expired hardware
is paid for in order: updater, circuit if needed, coupling; partial payments
remain spent when later operations are unaffordable. Lost retention destroys
the accessible circuit memory. An unaffordable write leaves q unchanged.

This is a constructive finite response to an upstream premise. The selection
of the new proposal or renewal law remains outside it. A physically different
route with opposite or outcome-blind renewal remains permitted by the larger
causal framework.

## 2. What is selected through consequences

In a stable noiseless complementary plant, an acquired confident correct
register has q=4z. With an inverted coupling, the issued action is 1−z, so
there is no return; the decoded bit still equals z and q does not change.
With a normal coupling, the action is z, capture occurs, and q again does not
change. Thus this subcase isolates the direction of prediction use from
prediction acquisition and accuracy.

The normal mapping is not installed by a supervisory performance comparison.
Fair proposals supply both couplings; their actual receipts determine their
maintenance. An inverted coupling can therefore disappear, and a normal
replacement can obtain the resources that renew it. The incidence and duration
of this route depend on available funds, lifetime, proposal outcomes and the
environment. It is not an optimizer theorem, a guarantee that all formed
couplings are useful, or a derivation of favorable direction from bare dynamics.

## 3. A resource and memory bound for this route

Consider a separate stable diagnostic beginning with an already acquired
correct confident register, an inverted coupling with k remaining trials,
circuit lifetime M, updater lifetime h, common fuel F and T opportunities.
The replacement coupling has lifetime L, updater replacements have lifetime
G and price g, coupling assembly costs c, retention plus work before return
costs d, and each failed paid write costs w. Assume deterministic capture for
the correct route; fresh orientations are fair and independent; the packet
pays all renewal bills and the successful write. No circuit rebuild counts
as recovery of this same inherited record. The checked family uses w=1.

If j fresh inverted candidates precede the first normal one, the number of
failed trials before that first return is

\[
f_j=k+jL.
\]

The updater must be replaced before the successful trial as many times as

\[
R_j=\begin{cases}
0,&f_j<h,\\
1+\lfloor(f_j-h)/G\rfloor,&f_j\ge h.
\end{cases}
\]

Its exact pre-return expense and elapsed opportunities are therefore

\[
C_j=f_j(d+w)+(j+1)c+gR_j+d,
\qquad T_j=f_j+1.
\]

The j-th opportunity exists precisely when

\[
F\ge C_j,\qquad T\ge T_j,\qquad f_j<M.
\]

The last inequality is a memory/interface constraint as well as a resource
constraint: after M failures the original enclosing circuit expires, so a new
predictor does not count as revising the use of the same inherited record.
The feasible j form an initial segment because costs and failures increase.
If N opportunities are feasible, the recovery probability is

\[
\Pr(\text{same-record recovery})=1-2^{-N}.
\]

**Proof.** Before the first return, each failed trial charges retention, work
and its write; each fresh coupling is separately paid; updater lifetime
determines exactly the counted intervening replacements. All expenses are
nonnegative and paid in sequence, so affordability of the total suffices for
every earlier payment on this specified history. Conversely, any such
successful history has exactly those expenses. Its circuit must still exist
before the successful trial. Only fair coupling proposals remain random in
this diagnostic; N consecutive inverted proposals have probability 2⁻ᴺ.
The unit write price means that an unaffordable optional write leaves no
positive reserve capable of financing another paid attempt. With a larger
write price, skipping a write may change the bound and must be analyzed
separately. ∎

This formula is not substituted for the primary process, which starts with
no acquired record and has an environmental reversal. It is a conditional
explanation of one identified obstruction. The exact executable check covers
3,936 combinations of reserve, horizon and inherited component lifetimes.

An especially direct boundary occurs immediately before exposure three of
the witness: both updater and coupling have expired but q survives. The
next capture requires updater2 + coupling1 + retention1 + work3 = **seven**
usable units. Six cannot capture; seven permits the normal proposal to
capture with probability one half. Future incident support cannot finance an
unaffordable earlier command.

## 4. Matched comparisons and failures

Five arms share original resources, signal access, basic repertoire and
prices: variable coupling, its conventional finite-state realization, fixed
normal, fixed inverted, and outcome-blind coupling maintenance. Fixed arms
still pay for all hardware, renewal and replacement. Outcome-blind maintenance
pays after every executed trial whenever affordable, resetting the coupling
even after failure; its additional realized expenses are reported.

| Primary arm | Expected captures | Same-record coupling-revision probability |
|---|---:|---:|
| Variable | 37/36 | 1/18 |
| Conventional | 37/36 | 1/18 |
| Fixed normal | 13/9 | 0 |
| Fixed inverted | 1/9 | 0 |
| Outcome-blind maintenance | 7/9 | 0 |

The conventional representation has exactly the same marked transition law
and full terminal distribution. A lower revision score for fixed normal is
not a performance failure; it obtains more support in this comparison.
The construction explains formation and consequence-dependent revision of
a coupling whose useful orientation was externally fixed in the preceding
study. It does not surpass the cybernetic description of that same mechanism.

Further declared cases preserve important failures:

- Initial common reserve twelve leaves no executed trial after formation.
- Circuit lifetime two gives zero same-memory coupling witnesses, despite
  occasional captures after rebuilding an entire circuit.
- Coupling lifetime one raises the strict witness from1/18 to5/48 while
  reducing captures from37/36 to41/48. More revision is not universally better.
- Source availability3/4 causes actual receipt-decoder errors, resource loss
  and a smaller witness probability7/192.
- Six-unit packets still permit some coupling revision, but leave much less
  operating reserve; obtaining one return is not perpetual viability.

All results include failed original preparation. The primary variable arm has
an active written circuit at the end with probability1/6, compared with the
2/9 commissioning probability. Downstream composition must keep that loss.

## 5. Verification, interpretation and the next connection

The prespecified protocol preceded execution. Exact rational distributions
cover seven cases and five arms. A separately coded operational specification
enumerates exogenous draws into mutable records rather than copying the
producer's branch-expansion implementation. It matches all 1,968 distinct
reachable marked kernels and 2,566 branches. Independently written forward
reaction enumeration also matches every original preparation terminal state
and probability. The deterministic witness, liquidity boundary, full energy
account and 3,936 recovery configurations pass.

The checker uses the producer's forward moment-accumulation driver after
checking every marked transition. It is not a second independently written
aggregate dynamic program. Source hashes include the imported, concurrently
developed prediction and preparation files; the runs check stability before
and after execution. This extension has no new Lean formalization, chemical
identification or empirical test. The evidence is exact finite computation
and the analytical argument above, with a separate internal interpretation
review.

The missing normal-action-sign premise is now supplied by a conditional
formation/replacement process. The causal significance is physical: a route
changes the support that renews it. This does not equate resource capture
with felt valence, ethical value or benefit for every affected center.

Other-directed concern requires a further interface. The same acquired q and
same locally useful coupling could coexist with a gate excluding all other
centers. Self-only behavior need not distinguish them. A subsequent study
must explain the entry and maintenance of shared evaluative routing, retain
any cheap identity gate, refusal or omission, and separate another center's
actual condition from the actor's own returned support. A patient's failure
to supply a return must not be silently converted into evidence that the
patient is unimportant.
