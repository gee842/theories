## Participation is physically effective, but its learned revision is narrower

The [architecture parent](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/model.py) already has consequence-mediated construction. A private capture pays to copy a guide; actual guide count changes primitive bond rates in the next assembly. With adequate resources, P-guide copying raises direct-route production from 1/2 to 2/3, matched inert copying leaves it 1/2, and D-guide copying makes it 1/3. This is a real process by which effects change later architecture production. It is not an analyst's success score selecting a completed graph. The guide's copying and bonding chemistry remains supplied, and the direction can narrow as well as broaden responsiveness.

That actual structural history should not be collapsed into the later acquired recipient record. The guides were produced before the recipient-specific correction. Their persistence does not copy the later addressed bits. In the learning suffix, the original `check_state` explicitly permits mutation of only `fuel`, `patient`, `raw_w`, `q`, `last`, `active` and `retained` among parent coordinates. Graph edges, guide identities/counts, receptor/comparator nodes and motor coupling stay fixed. `construct` is called once before R1. Neither subsequent recipient observation/write can alter those structural fields or call another graph-formation round.

This is a source-level localization of the missing connection, not a universal impossibility result. The implementation contains **formed participation → acquired consequence distinction → changed enacted participation and effect** and an earlier **productive effect → changed guide abundance → changed later formation distribution**. It does not yet make the acquired recipient distinction reconstruct its structural route, select a construction recipe or revise its targeting criterion. Combining the descriptions in a common wrapper would not add those stronger causal paths.

The recipient side is also limited. `patient` undergoes actual credit and damage, and a scheduled debit after capture. Its prior reserve could already pay that debit. No modeled recipient organization loses or regains a maintenance capacity because this support changes its state. The achieved causal effect on a differentiated reserve is therefore more specific than changed continuation of an affected living organization. This is a representation omission to address, not a refutation of a richer nondual relation.

## The next identification contract

A useful next study should identify an actual physical variable that both retains an acquired consequence distinction and changes participation or effective access in the continuing organization. It need not add a symbolic register if the altered organization itself stores the difference. The identifying evidence must show the following connected relation, with the original organization and resource history retained:

1. A particular encounter has a consequential effect and induces an accessible, persistent change; inherited cues or a supplied favorable state alone do not establish acquisition.
2. The changed physical organization alters a later response or contribution through a specified causal route. Merely reading out a retrospective classification does not establish its use.
3. A matched contrast on that retained change separates the history-dependent response from current stimulus, general activity, support amount and direct injury caused by the intervention. Any intervention that also destroys necessary machinery must retain that competing explanation.
4. The affected continuing organization has an independently identified dependency. The claimed change must concern its actual capacity, restoration, impairment or renewal, not just a renamed scalar receipt.

These are identification obligations, not a proposed controller or a promise that one implementation can independently manipulate every variable. If evidence shows that the alleged memory is only a by-product while another retained variable controls the response, the representation and causal claim should be revised. If the effect is immediate stimulus-following without a persistent history-dependent difference, the acquisition claim should be narrowed. If a record affects action but never participating structure, the existing narrower success remains rather than becoming failure by a changed finish line.

The governing foundation also has to explain formation and revision of the relevant signaling and structural mechanisms. Supplying a local law is not evidence that its ingredients exist outside generative reality; calling it immanent is not a derivation of that law. The no-assumptions objective remains unfinished.
