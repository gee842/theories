## 1. Informative grounding rather than representation alone

The revised §7 distinguishes a presentation of possible histories from independently identified account, information, implementation and retention conditions. Its positive explanation is that the enabling structure, acquired record and updater are themselves constructible, usable, vulnerable and transmissible states in the declared process. The capacity need not be installed as an unexplained chooser outside that process. The local laws still supply functional recipes, variation, feedback channels, transfer relations and initial provision; the section does not claim to derive these laws from bare difference or composition.

The consequences are informative because removing one identified condition can change a continuation even while another apparently favorable quantity remains:

- [Local-store accounting][learning] permits arbitrarily much common fuel while still requiring actual capture before expenditure from an initially empty local store. This is a distinction under the stated law, not a claim that resources could never finance a newly built access mechanism under an extended law.
- The same deterministic controller, inherited memory and complete observations give the same commands. Two incompatible continuation requirements cannot both be reliably addressed while those inputs remain identical. The required observation equality includes material channels, not merely matching verbal reports.
- [Controlled refinement][refinement] supplies an exact-history interface and a counterexample: preserving world possibilities and fuel costs can still erase a distinction that changes a learned command. Observation and memory preservation are sufficient at the declared granularity; not every ignored bit must be preserved for every controller.
- [Protocol completion][completion] separates correspondence of completed operations from an actual completion guarantee. Enabledness, preservation of the admitted domain and strict internal progress give a scoped sufficient result. They do not create funding, impose scheduler fairness or turn a failure boundary into success.
- In [consequence-retention selection][selection], useful repair can increase total reproduction while equal per-capita copying and loss leave the useful type's stopped expected fraction neutral. Differential access or loss supplies the missing selection relation. Paid localization can favor the producer; turnover or catalytic loss can remove or reverse that advantage.

These restrictions and constructive instances are more than a conjunction of desired capacity names. They state what cannot be substituted at a specific interface and identify a causal route that changes the relevant dependency. They are not a new omnibus theorem, proof of universal favorable selection, or proof of empirical novelty over a conventional model with the same kernel.

3. **The acquired evaluator enters before the parent's alternative final action.** The map uses `RevisionWitness.consumed`, retaining the old paid evidence/reason history and actor10/recipient40/one-packet state. It neither refunds an executed action nor claims the separately backed n/port construction is free.
4. **The selected successor comparison has a common actual donor.** Direct read-only inspection of the accepted saved results confirms that `changed/shared/variable/11`, `changed/identity_gated/variable/11` and its conventional comparator have predecessor `signals_111/shared/variable/11`, each with mass 9/128. At publication all have q2, actor10, source10 and recipient70, with retained n7. Both evaluative routes pay two; shared then pays eight and delivers ten, while filtered retains actor8/source10/recipient70. The conventional path matches shared. The shared ledger is `162 = 66 service + 10 failed-query loss + 86 retained`, where retained resources are recipient80 plus isolated native6. This is a post-perception routing intervention, not endogenous selection of the shared route, impaired information, or a newly derived unrestricted construction probability.
5. **Renewal and correction retain their own laws.** The recurring TARGET/recipient-financed renewal account is not silently substituted for the retained-n successor's eight-unit RI law. Generic prefix-liquidity facts can be transported after their actual deltas and guards are supplied. Likewise, [reason protection][protection] is a separate physical and strategic realization: Lean proves the physical/semantic traces, while a checked finite game selects actions under supplied dated valuations and economic conditions. Its cold replay reuses retained hardware; it is not a new physical learner.

The map correctly distinguishes physical delivery, evaluation, justified application and actual policy use. Patient-good meaning, an actor-addressed ground, legitimate authority and the complete relevant comparison are independent application premises. Neither n nor a technical grant establishes them. A query can already fulfill a predeclared one-service claim; the current successor policy does not use that fulfillment to withdraw the recommendation. The map records this unresolved use without reopening generic correction or requiring every contribution to inhabit one literal hardware trajectory.
