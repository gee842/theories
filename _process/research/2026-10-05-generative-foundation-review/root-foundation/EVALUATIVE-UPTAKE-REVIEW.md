The [frozen protocol][protocol] addresses actual consequence acquisition → retained evaluative influence → paid action for the same recipient. The new coefficient begins at zero at an actual saved pre-import prefix of the accepted recipient-revision process. Failed parent histories, exact ancestry and original probability remain. This does not restart the actor, refund an executed action or estimate a new unrestricted M60 assembly distribution.

The independent [checker][checker] reconstructed all 29 cases, 261 mixtures, 3,306 histories and 40,200 recorded prefixes, including 8,352 aggregate metrics, 3,071 formation-prefix comparisons and 152 exact inherited catalog entries. It verified each actual M60 formation, paid assembly, exact old-preparation projection, original compartment allocation, one continuing core, typed matter and whole energy476. It reconstructed native metering, every acquisition/write, actual assessment, recipient trial, interrupted prefix, replacement and endpoint from the law rather than importing the new producer transitions. The exact descriptive [reporting][reporting] was checked against these histories. The first reconstruction passed without changing the first numerical outcomes or repairing the oracle in response to an outcome mismatch.

The original independent oracle was written before inspecting the new implementation or outcomes. [Its initial receipt][oracle-initial], [corrected prospective guards][oracle-guards] and [full schedule][oracle-full] preserve that sequence. Permission/delivery were moved outside factual evaluation, and the bounded replacement guard was corrected before protocol freeze. A separately recorded [interface adaptation][oracle-interface] maps the prospective law to the later explicit executing mode, absent-port representation and close events. It preceded outcome inspection. The checker imports only the already accepted parent independent checker for inherited law, never the new producer's transition functions.

The [numerical receipt][numeric] records current source hashes. Supplemental checks added after the primary reconstruction compare 1,102 histories before the first actual policy assessment, 18 actual fixed-seven/learned-zero contrasts, 18 permission-preserves-assessment contrasts and 18 reached retired-port states with sufficient fuel and source. Their latter obstructions therefore do not depend on exhausted accounts. These are validation checks, not additional prespecified experimental arms.

## Adversarial checks and causal interpretation

Fifteen supplemental mutations or independent guard counterchecks were rejected. They include trace-consistent wrong loss-window metering with unchanged private charges; dissipation of supposedly inaccessible native source while preserving the scalar energy total; redirection of recipient receipts to actor funds; rewriting qualification during teaching; duplicated active cores; reuse of the held pair; premature source financing; disagreement between acquired command and current facts; actor-target substitution; reuse of a funded consumed port; and substitution of historical evidence with a recomputed local hash. Native-write repetition, repeated return and an unfunded return were separately blocked by the independent law. Full ancestry is checked against the accepted source histories, so changing only local identifiers or hashes cannot establish provenance.

These tests support a specific causal conclusion: equal recipient information and equal private training need not produce equal recipient influence. Shared and filtered arms have identical paid inputs before evaluation. The filtered arm can retain n7, the same valid command, enough stocks and the same private history while abstaining. A literal conventional table agrees with the shared comparison. The learned all-return controller retains n0 and abstains, whereas the matched fixed-seven evaluator still acts without falsifying the actual learned register. Thus acquired content changes the supplied decision mechanism; it does not select the transfer recipe itself. Negative transfer yields abstention in this two-action menu; the study does not thereby demonstrate active hostility.

The modeled teacher observes an operation's own two-unit shortfall, excluding subsequent read/write fees. It is not a measurement of subjective suffering or ethical importance. A no-return source packet remains isolated; it is not silently consumed to create the lesson. The strict continuation realizes:

`476 = 351 inherited prefix + 26 new construction + 12 native service + 10 first evaluation/action + 11 replacement/evaluation/action + 66 retained`.

The retained66 comprises recipient60 and native source6, with actor fuel zero. Recipient funds never pay the actor's bills. Permission loss preserves knowledge and positive assessment but prevents action; false feedback or silent law change can preserve internal agreement while defeating actual receipt. These distinguish valuation, calibrated facts, permission and fulfilled effect.

## Formal law review

I inspected all public `Event`, `Enabled` and `advance` branches in [AcquiredEvaluation][formal], rather than checking only the selected witness. The preserved [initial lifecycle findings][findings] were material: an exposed voluntary action constructor bypassed acquired assessment, and a consumed port did not yet establish closure of the first opportunity. These were corrected before acceptance of the formal package. The revised public law has no unrestricted voluntary constructor. The only RI event route requires an executing, due, currently supported command except for settlement of already occurred recipient work. It inherits recipient-only targeting. Reaching a configured worked state is not claimed to establish its own earlier acquisition history.

Explicit first-due, first-closed, second-due and finished states now govern the lifecycle. Scope/reason invalidation does not itself close an opportunity. Closing retains pending reads and the actual physical phase; replacement requires the first opportunity closed, consumed/absent old port, native completion, ready core, held pair and payment. A pending read or interrupted nonready core cannot become free replacement. Current command equality no longer unnecessarily requires an old qualification flag. Permission and delivery remain action conditions, not truth conditions.

The selected [actual witness][witness] extends exactly `RevisionWitness.consumed`. Its proved normal form is equal to that endpoint, not a newly initialized successful actor. New preparation, exact projected old preparation, old paid query/consumer history and new continuation are collected under an explicit staged isolation contract. The proof of actual recipient effect requires genuine publication provenance, separately supplied G and equality of the observation and use laws. A current tag or learned positive coefficient cannot replace these premises.

The Lean and numerical scopes differ. Lean covers the normal eight-unit service, a selected exact orientation and controls, and a restricted routing law. Its fixed-seven constructor is an exclusive route; the numerical model permits fixed valuation combined with other switches. Early port flags and scope-cut representations can also differ while their current guards forbid unauthorized evaluation/action. The full 29-case stochastic mixture is independently checked numerically; it is not established as a universal refinement of the Lean process. The [fresh formal receipt][formal-receipt] passed all26 modules with77 new audited declarations and630 total audits. All23 inherited sources match accepted parent `38df6f6`; the current source and log hashes match the receipt, and the checker confirmed no source changed during the build. Four new declarations have no axiom dependencies; the other73 use only standard `propext`, `Quot.sound` and/or `Classical.choice`. There are no custom axioms, admissions, warning-bearing accepted builds or native proof shortcuts. The [selected runtime crosscheck][crosscheck] matches12 named milestones, preparation and the actually reached opposite-command history. I checked its projections against the inspected theorem statements; it is a concrete comparison, not a theorem of all-case refinement. Its one reporting correction accurately names the zero-charge cold invalidation following a paid evaluation read; the original wording and unchanged comparison payload are preserved, without rerunning numerical or compiler outcomes.

## Interpretation and completion boundary

The [scope note][scope], [canonical account][canonical], [mathematical companion][math] and package documentation correctly distinguish the causal construction from ethical justification. The sign, scale, learning update, cross-center feature transfer, authority, opportunity schedule and interpretation of recipient stock remain local inputs. Private calibration admits positive, zero and negative transfers with identical self observations; deriving their unique ethical sign by assuming a positively transferred utility would be circular. The independent ethical argument and [application contract][application] are neither absent nor derived by this numerical coefficient.

A bounded recognition join is supported by the reached primary histories with opposite acquired commands (variable-producer paths4 and11), the unavailable-fact control and the mismatched-command obstruction. The implementation supplies acquired evidence, a matching instrumental response and actual addressed execution. Reading this as warranted recognition additionally assumes an independently justified patient-good relation, ground, authority and a complete relevant comparison; those meanings are not produced by the shortfall register or proved by its threshold. The two-action test menu does not itself establish the completeness of a normative comparison.

The new result supplies acquired operation-shortfall content to a materially formed evaluator and shows its influence on actual paid choice. It removes an installed coefficient and prescribed helpful action at this boundary. It does not establish native phenomenology, grounded G, necessary inclusiveness, superiority to conventional control or a strategic reason to retain the shared route. The later event replaces an evaluator carrier while retaining the same learner and archive; it is not whole-learner succession or evidence of a newly arising recipient need.

No empirical observations were collected. Synthetic cases and kernel proofs do not complete the cross-domain empirical obligation or the unified foundation. The fixed completion conditions remain unchanged: integrate this operational contribution with independently justified applicability, correction and succession where existing contracts suffice, and maintain explicit common premises, alternatives and evidential limits. These are the governing obligations, not an invitation to add arbitrary new apparatus gates.
