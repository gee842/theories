## 5. Affirmative argument and substantive counterreview

**Affirmative account.** In an unfolding where action changes later conditions, faithful continuation of a requirement can demand variation of means. The arithmetic instance makes that relationship causal within the represented history: success itself changes the state rather than a commentator toggling the conclusion. A response losing that state difference fails in a precise way. Reasoning that includes every requested prefix also distinguishes genuine continuation from selection of completed successes. These are informative constraints on an interpretation of contextual wisdom.

**Strong comparator.** Ordinary feedback control and Dijkstra's predicate transformers retain the same target, transition and prefix relations. They obtain the same formal results. This matched alternative preserves the target rather than removing relationships to manufacture a gap. Hence the present evidence supports a common usable structure and does not decide unique generative metaphysics. The [source record](WISDOM-CONTEXT-SOURCES.md) gives the primary technical precedent.

**Normative challenge.** The mathematical target is supplied. Calling it `Band` rather than `Good` makes the limit visible but does not solve ethical grounding. Cicero's costly fidelity case defeats the proposal that all the selected wisdom is preservation of the initiating individual's operating conditions. Simply broadening Q until it agrees with every passage would import the disputed verdict. The actual G0/J account must evaluate relevant lives, commitments and losses on its own published premises; textual survival is not ethical certification.

**Historical challenge.** Two deliberately selected familiar cases have low evidential force for a claim about all wisdom. Chinese thought already contributed to the core; Roman traditions are not established as causally independent of all comparison sources. The current search did not exhaust every original or translation. The same observed overlap is compatible with cultural transmission, selection of favorable examples, ordinary conditional reasoning and the proposed shared constraints. We have not estimated their relative causal contribution.

**Adequacy challenge.** The quantity witness only tests response reversal under a supplied law and technical range. It cannot explain the exact roles of kinship, learning, fidelity or common good. Likewise, a target satisfied only at the end may overlook genuine intervening losses. Those are omitted relationships of this small representation, not demonstrated failures of the fuller relational theory. Conversely, declaring that the full account includes them does not establish a faithful application; their actual specification remains necessary.

**Corrections applied.** The account calls the historical exercise a selected comparison, not a blind prediction; credits the standard formal precedent and existing readiness studies; distinguishes state-sensitive itinerary from a formed controller; retains costly fidelity and real losses; and states explicit G0/J premises without restarting their completed justification task.
