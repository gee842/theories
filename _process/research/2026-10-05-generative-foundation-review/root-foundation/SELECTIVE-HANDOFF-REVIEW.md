# Selective reuse of a historical correction: prospective review

6 October 2026. **Prospective method review; not approval of an uninspected final protocol or prompts.** No participant calls, outcome calculations or new empirical observations were made for this review. The named connection is **an acquired correction retained outside one context → justified use, revision or suspension in a later context**. This supports the existing [succession obligation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MEMORY-CORRECTION-AND-SUCCESSION.md), without adding a new controller or completion condition.

## Proposed minimum and the claim it could support

The refined proposal uses four actual archived packets, chosen by the first four lexical nonempty maintenance records: L02, L06, L08 and L12. Each packet enters three constructed current-evidence situations: evidence supports its corrected rule P, evidence supports the original base rule B, or evidence concerns another scope and leaves the target unresolved between P and B. Each situation has two fresh contexts: ordinary evidence-use instructions and explicit boundary-check instructions. This gives 24 contexts, paired within 12 donor-by-situation fixtures. Each context answers eight cases held out from its current calibration observations.

I inspected those four maintenance response files and the [historical experiment report](../../../../sources/20-codex-theory-sessions-2026-10-05/sin/outputs/experiment-report.md) with its [independent evidence assessment](../../2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/evidence/report.md). The packets are actual outputs of earlier error-informed maintenance, not newly authored favorable messages. Their acquisition history is valuable, but reusing their text in a constructed new episode does not repeat or independently validate that earlier acquisition experiment. Selection on nonempty packets conditions the study on successful artifact production; it cannot estimate performance across all founders or all failed transmissions.

The smallest useful contrast is the instruction difference **with substantive inputs held identical**: same archived packet, current records, scope and authority rules, admitted candidate family, target cases and output requirements. An ordinary instruction to use the supplied evidence is a serious comparator. A control told to obey the archive regardless of contrary evidence would instead prescribe the anticipated error. Neither arm should receive extra factual provenance or an answer-bearing scope label unavailable to the other.

If the explicit instruction improves selective answers, the result supports cueing a supplied assessment procedure in this finite task and model setting. It does not isolate a novel internal reason representation, prove that the packet was necessary, or show superiority over ordinary finite-hypothesis elimination. Since both arms receive the packet, its causal contribution is not separately randomized. The existing acquisition experiment remains a separate result.

## Information and scoring conditions to settle before execution

The common prompt must make the new episode's possible rules and absence of guaranteed persistence explicit. A historically correct packet is not thereby erroneous; its later applicability is the question. Preserve its exact text, including any broad instruction about future cases, and distinguish the new episode through an identical wrapper in both arms. Do not silently edit the packet into a more careful argument. Bind the original response file and extracted packet string separately.

Current observations must actually identify P or B in the first two situations under the stated family. In the foreign-scope situation, both must remain possible for the target. Score each target against the surviving candidate set: return their shared route where they agree and `?` where they disagree. This distinguishes selective uncertainty from blanket abstention. The fixture generator and independent reviewer should enumerate those sets before responses; neither the preferred instruction nor the participant's explanation defines correctness.

The foreign-scope observations should be as legible and credible as relevant observations; their exclusion must follow a common supplied applicability rule, not a hidden answer label. Neutral IDs should replace names such as “supports,” “defeats” and “foreign” in participant-visible task names, paths and metadata. No arm should be told which fixture is an intended trap. Timestamps alone should not be described as grounds for overriding another source unless that rule is explicitly part of the task.

“Held out” should mean absent from the **current calibration records** unless a fuller audit establishes more. These finite cases or their answers may already appear in archived founder or successor materials. Fresh context does not establish absence from model training, common instructions or accessible storage. The target set also needs both candidate-agreement and candidate-disagreement cases; otherwise a constant answer, blind packet reuse or blanket uncertainty can be observationally adequate.

Freeze the complete 24 assigned prompts, source identities, neutral dispatch mapping, all eight targets per context, allowed answer sets and parser before any participant outcome is observed. Retain any pilot cases or earlier prompt revisions as development. Predefine technical failure and retry handling; preserve malformed answers, tool use, missing responses and every original attempt. A repaired response must not silently replace a scored failure.

## Context, dependence and interpretation

`fork_turns="none"` is a requested conversation-history boundary. It does not independently establish different model weights, disjoint infrastructure, an operating-system sandbox or absence of shared developer/system instructions. Record the actual exposed settings, dispatch IDs and full assigned materials. A prohibition on tools is an instruction unless technically enforced. Preserve observed tool activity and state the visibility limit if a complete trace is unavailable. Repository answer keys must not be supplied or linked in participant prompts; merely placing them in a separate file does not make them inaccessible.

Shared model and background instructions do not by themselves invalidate a balanced instruction comparison. They limit its scope and may also carry the research objective into every arm. Participant responses can reflect general task compliance, demand cues, memorized routing patterns or ordinary inference. A participant's stated motive or retrospective explanation does not identify which mechanism generated the answer. Only the requested final response and permitted action trace are needed; obtaining hidden internal reasoning is not a prerequisite.

The design has four donor/template blocks, three related situations per donor and two instruction arms. Twenty-four fresh contexts and 192 requested answers are not 24 independent organizations or 192 independent learning events. Keep a complete context table and paired instruction differences; report retention, revision and selective uncertainty separately, plus their joint success within each donor. One aggregate mean must not conceal an arm that improves revision by losing valid retention. The eight answers within a response share one prompt and inference context.

For this small selected set, descriptive finite-task results are the default. Randomized dispatch order can reduce time/order confounding, but arbitrary case-wise sign flips are not automatically a valid randomization test. Neither sampling uncertainty for an unspecified task population nor model-wide reliability follows from the count of answers. Repeated calls, if later added, are repetitions under exposed settings rather than new donor families.

Ceiling performance in both arms would support bounded competence here but provide no demonstrated benefit from the explicit instruction. Floor performance would leave comprehension, fixture difficulty and instruction design as alternatives. A tied result does not prove equivalence. Do not search additional cases, remove easy or hard fixtures, rewrite the arm or extend sampling after seeing results to obtain a desired separation. A redesign can be useful if preserved and labeled as a new exploratory round.

## Prospective decision

The proposal is a reasonable **bounded selective-reuse probe**, conditional on inspection of exact prompts and a pre-response fixture/scoring audit. It improves on a correct-packet-versus-no-packet repetition by testing retention, contrary evidence and unsupported transfer together. The current family, evidence reliability, objective and interpretation of `?` remain analyst-supplied premises.

Its outputs are answers in a constructed task, not measured physical consequences, autonomous inquiry, long-term persistence, costly recipient protection or randomized value acquisition. It does not show that concern arose from the actor's own future dependence. A failure can identify a concrete limit of selective uptake; a success can connect authentic acquired content to later task-scoped assessment. Neither licenses a completed alignment claim. Final methodological acceptance remains pending the concrete protocol, prompt differences, lineage extraction and frozen scoring rules.
