# What makes a further inquiry pertinent to continuing activity

6 October 2026. AI-authored source and identification review under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **acquired consequence → selection of an available inquiry → revised application and actual continuation**. This note adds no model, experiment or literature corpus.

## The strongest positive connection is already implemented

The accepted [acquired-applicability history](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/succession/ACQUIRED-APPLICABILITY-INTEGRATION.md) already implements this connection. Its [source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/applicability/model.py), especially `execute_word` and `select_next`, makes the distinction precise. A failed productive attempt is acquired and committed while the sensory model remains correct. Within the admitted family, that failure leaves complemented applicability and blocked transfer possible. The learner scans words using its retained model and chooses a word whose predicted effects differ between the remaining possibilities. The further encounter both investigates that relation and attempts the outstanding productive operation. Success at the first encounter instead stops this inquiry.

The acquired-checkpoint clamp preserves the record and its formation expenditure but repeats the earlier route. Its failure separates possession of a correction from its operative use. The [formal closure](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/succession/ACQUIRED-APPLICABILITY-FORMAL-INTEGRATION.md) checks the actual policy words as well as transitions. This is stronger than an analyst selecting the right probe after inspecting the hidden case. Its finite search recipe, family, contact correspondence and undertaking remain local premises; those premises do not erase the acquired choice.

The [criterion-acquisition result](YITI-REN-CRITERION-INTEGRATION.md) goes further in another history. Actual obstruction, a repair's support change and restored operation identify a concrete support requirement within a declared family. That acquired relation changes completion content and subsequent use, including the consequences of making and returning the judgment itself. The [criterion source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/continuing-dependency/criterion.py) still supplies `later_repair`, its contact interpretation and compilation recipe. Thus concrete criterion content is learned; the whole maintenance undertaking is not thereby generated. Calling either result merely a fixed criterion would lose the achieved connection.

Acquired suffixes, inquiry-limit revision and method-connection formation supply further established realizations. Their completion need not be demonstrated again through another selector. The present question concerns a particular remaining access and application relation.

## A conditional derivation of instrumental relevance

An ongoing activity can give an inquiry a definite role without receiving a detached scalar reward. First identify what the activity is actually organized to accomplish or maintain, how its present claim of completion relates to that continuation, and what its previous action has changed. Then identify a still-unresolved difference whose alternatives change the supported ways of continuing. An available inquiry is instrumentally pertinent when its actual effects and obtainable indications can resolve that difference or themselves answer the outstanding claim.

The applicability history realizes this pattern: the investigated difference concerns the same attempted productive relation that just failed; the successful inquiry can enact that relation. The criterion history learns which support must survive for the already admitted later repair. Neither obtains the desired action name as a fresh reward label. Neither derives the worth of every undertaking merely from its being pursued.

The [existing inquiry-sufficiency account](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md) prevents two overstatements. A common adequate response can make exact fault identification unnecessary. Conversely, an informative operation can remove the means of answering the claim, so fewer possible explanations do not automatically mean a better continuation. Inquiry must transport the actual situation forward before its informational benefit is assessed. Waiting, stopping or declining can be adequate only under their actual scope and consequences; none is automatically the neutral answer.

The relation need not be stored as a sentence or a separate value field. Its operative carrier may be a learned connection, an acquired distinction or another dependency-sensitive organization. Our analytical separation identifies causal use and adequacy; it does not posit a world that must receive meaning from an independent evaluator.

## The current relay distinguishes access from selection

The [completed contact continuation](CONTEXT-SENSITIVE-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md) revises false apparent dependence through an alternate source-admitted contact. It does not select that inquiry from an acquired final failure. The old final operation changes load or waste without conveying that difference to the controller. Ordinary success and calibration failure retain the same weak laws, contact behavior and accessible account/record history. After both exhaust their source, the existing full-transfer guard blocks another transfer; the represented weak operations do not expose the persistent load/waste difference.

This identifies the relevant source-interface limitation. An inquiry rule using only those indications cannot selectively trigger on a final-effect distinction it never receives. The root's separate all-future characterization addresses the exact admitted-event scope; this note does not claim to have rerun or proved it. Nor is the limitation an absence of interconnection in the process: the actual effects persist, while this particular observation interface omits them.

The alternate contact separates the specified false-contact case from the other two, but does not distinguish success from calibration failure. Both failure cases have exhausted source and full law `00`; diagnostic separation is therefore not demonstrated restoration of the service. It might support another justified learning or accountability undertaking, but that further role and its available continuation must be identified rather than silently added after observing the result.

A prospective connection to this relay therefore needs **accessible evidence of the relevant full effect and its application**, not another abstract proof that a learner can choose queries. The evidence must arise through an admitted contact with the actual effect, retain episode/operation binding, and affect selection through the same continued support history. Formation or access can be supplied conditionally if explicit. Reading an audit-only stock, inserting a `failed` flag from the hidden case, or borrowing another learner's successful endpoint would bypass this connection.

## What would distinguish a substantive advance

For this specific interface, the useful comparison preserves the learned sensory relation and historical fulfillment, changes what full-operation effect is acquired, and tests whether that acquired difference changes an inquiry or application relevant to a surviving claim. It should retain successful completion, misleading or unavailable indications, and unsupported continuation. A matched interruption of the new evidence's use can locate its causal role, but need not be another permanent mechanism.

The strongest ordinary rival is an equally informed stateful dependency tracker with the same contact, action repertoire and support. It can realize the positive achievement. A difference only in vocabulary, or an investigator's ability to request the separating channel, would not establish acquired scheduling. Equally, no new regulator is needed to acknowledge the completed applicability result: the work is to establish or reject this particular correspondence.

Existing [information-seeking](INQUIRY-INTERFACE-COMPARATORS.md) and [outcome-revaluation](APPLICABILITY-REVALUATION-COMPARATOR.md) comparisons already constrain the empirical identification. They distinguish inquiry organization from answer accuracy, and historical outcome relevance from a present cue alone. They do not jointly establish this relay's contact mechanism or a full biological chain. Their primary findings are recorded in those notes; no empirical detail is repeated here and no new primary reading is claimed.

## Instrumental adequacy does not settle ethical authority

The [whole-process account](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-INTEGRATION.md) remains governing. A criterion can be genuinely formed, accurately track an enabling relation and direct effective inquiry while serving a harmful activity. Likewise, a generated standard's failure to realize its claimed good supplies a conditional defeater only when that good and the standard's role are adequately identified. Internal origin, successful persistence and observed preference do not settle the authority of the correcting ground.

These are unresolved relations within the unfolding, not grounds for installing an external realm of values. Ethical appraisal must preserve differentiated effects, legitimate claims and possible endings as well as maintenance. The defensible advance is a precise connection between an actual consequence, an available inquiry and its continuing role. Its achievement cannot be equated with universal concern or completed alignment.
