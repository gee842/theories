## 2. Bounded primary-source assessment

Giuseppe Attanasi and Aldo Montesano, *The Value of Endogenous above Exogenous Information in Irreversible Environmental Decisions*, November 2011 [author manuscript](https://publications.ut-capitole.fr/3372/1/10.01.307.pdf).

Read the abstract, introduction, §2.1 and the conclusion, plus the adjoining generalizability discussion. The model concerns two periods, irreversible resource development and uncertain future benefits. Information can arrive independently of development or through development itself. Its principal specification supplies linear net benefits, probabilities and risk neutrality; it reports parameter regions where adding endogenous learning increases preservation relative to exogenous learning alone. This is conditional optimization, not a universal injunction to wait or preserve.

Our inference: an inquiry proposal must identify how information becomes available and what the inquiry changes. Treating observation as free, accurate and timely is a substantive simplification. The paper neither grounds the positive ethical standing of generation nor validates our proposed mixed-comparison principle. No empirical replication or mathematical recheck of this paper was performed.
