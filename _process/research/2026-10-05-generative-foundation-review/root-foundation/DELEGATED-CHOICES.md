## 0. 怎样读这份记录 / How to read this record

**一项决定做什么、不做什么。** 它把一个读法从“待 Ren”改为“采用”或“不采用”，写明理由与会改变它的东西。它不改动任何 Lean 陈述或已检查的定理，不给任何规律加前提，也不把读法变成证明；不采用的读法，其定理照旧保留。Ren 改动一项决定，改的是读法，不是数学。

**What a decision does and does not do.** It moves a reading from “pending Ren” to adopted or declined, with its reasons and what would change it. It changes no Lean statement or checked theorem, adds no premise to any law, and turns no reading into a proof; the theorems of a declined reading stay. If Ren reverses a decision, the reading changes, not the mathematics.

**所用的标准（AI 的）。**
1. **Ren 的原话优先。** 原话已定的，照原话并注明；几种读法都合原话时，取 Ren 其他原话与已检查结果所支持的。
2. **不加前提。** 取不加前提的读法：不加权重、次序、特殊的层次或边界，不加主张、所有或代表。
3. **已检查的结果能定的，由它定。** 被定理排除的读法不采用。
4. **确切范围。** 读法只以其定理的范围采用；类比作为类比采用，不作为同一。
5. **关系优先。** 不采用引入分立的价值所有者、特殊中心或一方对另一方之主张的读法；总体化的读法以关系实际做什么回答。
6. **不丢失。** 不采用的读法与其理由记录在案。

**The standard used (the AI's).**
1. **Ren's words first.** Where Ren's words settle a reading, the decision follows them and says so; where several readings fit them, it takes the one Ren's other words and the checked results support.
2. **No added premise.** It takes the reading that adds no premise: no weight, order, privileged level or boundary, and no claims, ownership or representation.
3. **What a checked result can decide, it decides.** A reading a theorem excludes is declined.
4. **Exact scope.** A reading is adopted only with its theorems' scope; an analogy is adopted as an analogy, not as an identification.
5. **Relation first.** No reading is adopted that brings in separate owners of value, privileged centers, or claims of one party on another; totalizing readings are answered through what the relation actually does.
6. **Nothing lost.** Declined readings and their reasons stay on record.

**问“某读法是否是 Ren 的读法”的项。** 决定只说理论是否采用它为自己的读法，不说 Ren 持有什么；Ren 持有什么是 Ren 的陈述（§10）。

**Items asking whether a reading is Ren's.** The decision says only whether the theory adopts it as its reading, not what Ren holds; what Ren holds is Ren's to state (section 10).

## 1. 生生与生成的空间 / 生生 and the generative space

### D1. 生生的读法 / How 生生 is read

所在：第 8 节 Ren 的选择第一项；[ranking-under-readings](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/ranking-under-readings.md)、[becoming-stepwise-reading](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/becoming-stepwise-reading.md)、[becoming-renewal-reading](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/becoming-renewal-reading.md)、[metaethical-core-characterization](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md)。

**决定。** 生生读作生成的空间：能形成者的扩大（`Web.Widens`；以公理为条件，即保留且增加有意义者，`Web.widens_iff_retains_adds`）。Ren 2026-10-07 回答的三种范围（广义的生；生物生命与生态的繁盛；一切能够持续形成新组织的生成过程）读作“什么算作一个形成”的范围：每种给出一个空间，各有其扩大；在每种范围中，生生是那个空间的扩大，不是其中任何一项形成本身。G0 与 C 共用这一读法：继续之间以可能性比较（§6.12）。`living` 的各读法、`Flow.More` 与生起复现保留为所计者的读法，不作为生生的定义。
**理由。** 一、Ren 以方向所产生者说它：“一种整体的，不断创造更多生的方向，而这个方向产生的可能性，多样性越高，复杂性/复杂结构的复杂度可以不断的上升，就是一个好的方向”（2026-10-07，[原话](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md)），以及“being generative is its existence creates more possibilities of becoming - more future, not less”（2026-10-10）；扩大能形成者正是如此，而可能性读作接下来能形成的，Ren 已确认。二、不加前提：以扩大读“更好”，C 在水平路径上对 `living` 的每种读法是规律（`Web.paths_comparison_widens`）。三、它不把任何新组织都算作生生（G0 的对象是生生）：恶性增殖者的事件本身被计入（`Ranking.malignant_possible`），但它的部分使他者能形成的变少，所以不扩大；共享排在以消耗取胜之上（`Ranking.possible_contest`）。四、Ren 说三种范围“我觉得这几个都很有意义，都可以是，并且可能会得到不同的范围”：作为范围保留它们，正合此意。
**会改变它的。** Ren 另指生生的读法；一个扩大而 Ren 判为退化的部分，或相反；Ren 采用一个给混合部分定序的度量（“更多的未来”是偏序，互相超出者不排序）。

Where: section 8, Ren's choices, first item; the records above.

**Decision.** 生生 is read as the generative space: the widening of what can form (`Web.Widens`; with the axiom as a condition, retaining and adding what has significance, `Web.widens_iff_retains_adds`). The three scopes of Ren's answer of 7 October 2026 (生 broadly; biological life and ecological flourishing; every generative process that keeps forming new organization) are read as scopes of what counts as a formation: each gives a space with its own widening, and in each 生生 is the widening of that space, not any one formation in it. G0 and C share this reading: courses are compared on possibilities (section 6.12). The readings of `living`, `Flow.More` and arising that recurs stay as readings of what is counted, not as definitions of 生生.
**Reasons.** (1) Ren names the direction by what it produces: “一种整体的，不断创造更多生的方向，而这个方向产生的可能性，多样性越高，复杂性/复杂结构的复杂度可以不断的上升，就是一个好的方向” (7 October 2026, [words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md)) and “being generative is its existence creates more possibilities of becoming - more future, not less” (10 October 2026). Widening what can form is that, and Ren confirmed possibilities as what can form next. (2) It adds no premise: with “better” read as widening, C is a law on level paths for every reading of `living` (`Web.paths_comparison_widens`). (3) It does not count every new organization as 生生 (G0's object is 生生): a malignant formation's events are themselves included (`Ranking.malignant_possible`), but its part lessens what others can form, so it does not widen; sharing ranks above winning by consumption (`Ranking.possible_contest`). (4) Ren said of the three scopes “我觉得这几个都很有意义，都可以是，并且可能会得到不同的范围”; keeping them as scopes does that.
**What would change it.** Ren naming another reading of 生生; a widening part Ren judges degenerative, or the reverse; Ren adopting a measure that orders mixed parts (“more futures” is a partial order, and parts that each exceed the other stay unranked).

### D2. 生成的、退化的 / Generative and degenerative

所在：第 8 节第二项；[space-and-its-parts](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/space-and-its-parts.md)、[four-cases-of-a-part](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/four-cases-of-a-part.md)、[coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace.md) 及以活动读法陈述的普查与计数记录；第 8 节表中“接下来能形成什么”一行。

**决定。** 提议中的“生成的”“退化的”指生成的空间的两个方向，在两个层次上读，余量定理把它们联结起来。对一个部分，即它对能形成者做什么：有它的水平扩大无它的水平处是生成的，反之是退化的，互相包含是中性的，互相超出是混合的（§6.13，`Web.Widens`、`Web.Keeps`、`Web.Mixed`，四种情形两两互斥）。对一个形成，即它对所参与关系的继续形成条件做什么：是更新的形成与消耗的形成（Ren 2026-10-09 要求以关系结构分开这两种形成，[原话](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-renewing-consuming-without-axioms.md)；`Web.renewing_not_consuming`）。两层由余量联结：部分的净汲取在余量之内时保留，超出时失去（`Flow.retains_iff_within_margin_at`、`Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`）。活动的读法（生起复现与止息复现）不采用为“生成的、退化的”：有限世界中两者是同一部分，而 Ren 说“generative和degenerate虽然都在一个becoming model上，但是他们不overlap吧”；生起／止息仍是活动读法的名称。这也回答“把四种情形读作生成、退化与中性”：扩大是生成的，缩小是退化的，保持不变是中性的；混合不是中性，是需要度量的情形。
**理由。** 四种情形互斥，所以生成与退化不重叠，如 Ren 所要求；其中不出现意义、立场或边界，是第一部分的读法；Ren 2026-10-10 说“there are also neutral and generative dynamics that can be identified (degenerative/neutral/generative)”，三类恰是扩大、保持与缩小。
**会改变它的。** 一个 Ren 判为生成而缩小能形成者的部分；Ren 只以形成的层次（更新与消耗）为定义。

**Decision.** The proposal's “generative, degenerative” name the two directions of the generative space, read at two levels that the margin theorems join. For a part, what it does to what can form: it is generative where the levels with it widen those without it, degenerative where those without it widen those with it, neutral where each includes the other, and mixed where each exceeds the other (section 6.13; `Web.Widens`, `Web.Keeps`, `Web.Mixed`; the four cases exclude one another). For a formation, what it does to the conditions of continued formation of the relations it takes part in: renewing and consuming formations (Ren asked on 9 October 2026 that the two be separated by relational structure, [words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-renewing-consuming-without-axioms.md); `Web.renewing_not_consuming`). The margin joins them: a part retains where its net draw stays within the margin and loses where it exceeds it (`Flow.retains_iff_within_margin_at`, `Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`). The reading of activity (arising recurring, ceasing recurring) is declined as the reading of “generative, degenerative”: in finite worlds the two are one part, and Ren wrote “generative和degenerate虽然都在一个becoming model上，但是他们不overlap吧”. Arising and ceasing remain the names of the activity reading. This also answers reading the four cases as generative, degenerative and neutral: widening is generative, narrowing degenerative and keeping the same neutral; mixed is not neutral but the case that needs a measure.
**Reasons.** The four cases exclude one another, so generative and degenerative do not overlap, as Ren requires; no significance, standpoint or boundary enters, so it is a reading of Part I; Ren said on 10 October 2026 “there are also neutral and generative dynamics that can be identified (degenerative/neutral/generative)”, and the three classes are widening, keeping and narrowing.
**What would change it.** A part Ren judges generative that narrows what can form; Ren defining the pair only at the level of formations (renewing and consuming).

### D3. 第二个“生”的范围；以消耗为条件的生起 / The scope of the second 生; arising that depends on depletion

所在：第 8 节第三项；[unceasing-generation-needs-renewal](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/unceasing-generation-needs-renewal.md)。

**决定。** 第二个“生”的范围是所采用范围（D1）中能形成者：其所要求者被一个继续的水平满足的形成，活跃与否，包括休眠的与尚未形成的。以消耗为条件的生起不单独归类：它在有它的继续扩大能形成者处是生成的，缩小处是退化的，由比较决定（“更坏”经 C）。
**理由。** 给单个生起归类需要另一前提，并会重叠（同一事件既生起又消耗）；比较不加前提就决定它，并把分解、食腐与死亡之后的接续留在说明之内，而不本身记功；按 [unceasing-generation-needs-renewal](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/unceasing-generation-needs-renewal.md)，生起消耗有限多个条件时，不息的生成需要更新才复现。
**会改变它的。** Ren 把“生”限于活的形成或三种范围之一（空间随之缩小，规律不变）。

**Decision.** The second 生 ranges over what can form within the adopted scope (D1): formations whose requirements a course's levels meet, active or not, including dormant ones and ones not yet formed. Arising that depends on depletion is not classed on its own: it is generative where the course with it widens what can form and degenerative where it narrows it, as the comparison decides (“worse” goes through C).
**Reasons.** Classing single arisings would need a further premise and could overlap (one event both arises and depletes); the comparison decides it with no premise and keeps decomposition, scavenging and succession after a death within the account without crediting them as such. By [unceasing-generation-needs-renewal](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/unceasing-generation-needs-renewal.md), where arising depletes finitely many conditions, unceasing generation needs renewal to recur.
**What would change it.** Ren restricting 生 to living formations or to one of the three scopes (the space narrows; the laws are unchanged).

### D4. 体现 / Embodiment

所在：第 8 节第四项；[generation-is-replenishment](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/generation-is-replenishment.md)。

**决定。** 体现的关系项（恰在立着时交换）读作“生”：其活就是其立着的形成。生成不限于体现：所持的条件、休眠的形成与关系，凡在能形成者之中，都计入。体现仍是用到它的规律的前提，不是定义（[ZERO-PREMISE-PLAN](ZERO-PREMISE-PLAN.md) §1.4）。
**理由。** 可能性的读法计入形成，活跃与否（§6.12），所以把生成限于体现会去掉休眠（种子库）与所持的条件；P107 把体现列为部分的一种状态（立着而不交换者休眠）。
**会改变它的。** Ren 只把“生”读作体现的生命。

**Decision.** Embodied relata, which exchange exactly where they stand, are read as 生: formations whose living is their standing. Generation is not restricted to embodiment: held conditions, dormant formations and relations count wherever they are among what can form. Embodiment stays a premise of the laws that use it, not a definition ([ZERO-PREMISE-PLAN](ZERO-PREMISE-PLAN.md) §1.4).
**Reasons.** The possibility reading counts formations active or not (section 6.12), so restricting generation to embodiment would drop dormancy (seed banks) and held conditions; P107 lists embodiment as a state of a part (what stands without exchanging is dormant).
**What would change it.** Ren reading 生 only as embodied life.

### D5. 接续与生生不息的层次 / Succession and the level of 生生不息

所在：第 8 节第五项；[identity-across-steps](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/identity-across-steps.md)、[whole-persists-through-generation](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)、[coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace.md)。

**决定。** 接续经延续的已形成关系读：交互、以之为生、世系与输送（D9、D12）；工作读法中的任期如 [identity-across-steps](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/identity-across-steps.md) 的定理所说经接续延续。生生不息在整体的层次上读，如 Ren 原话“一种整体的……方向”：不止息的生成，即经更新而复现的生起。在其余闲置的有界整体中，这是回返，不是无尽的新（`Flow.whole_recurs_of_rest_idle`、`Flow.whole_not_new_of_rest_idle`，P111）；无尽的新需要来自每个有界部分之外的增益（`Flow.new_beyond_every`）。谱系、成员与家族是整体之内读延续的层次。
**理由。** Ren 的原话；P111；Ren 2026-09-25：“we can grow exponentially till we met our need, but we can't compounding forever”（[原话](../../../../sources/16-claude-code-directions/2026-09-25-the-mind-not-the-approach.md)）；部分短暂时持续的整体需要部分不断生起（[whole-persists-through-generation](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)），所以在整体的层次上“不断创造”成立，消除了只读作谱系延续时与 Ren 原话的张力。
**会改变它的。** Ren 把生生不息读作无尽的新（那就需要无界的增益，P111 给出其确切形式），或读在一个成员或一个谱系的层次上。

**Decision.** Succession is read through the formed relations of continuation: interaction, living on, descent and conveyance (D9, D12); a tenure on the working reading continues through succession as far as the theorems of [identity-across-steps](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/identity-across-steps.md) say. 生生不息 is read at the level of the whole, as Ren's “一种整体的……方向” says: generation that does not cease, arising that recurs through renewal. In a bounded whole whose rest is idle this is return, not novelty without end (`Flow.whole_recurs_of_rest_idle`, `Flow.whole_not_new_of_rest_idle`, P111); endless novelty needs gain from beyond every bounded part (`Flow.new_beyond_every`). Lineages, members and families are levels within the whole at which continuation is read.
**Reasons.** Ren's words; P111; Ren on 25 September 2026: “we can grow exponentially till we met our need, but we can't compounding forever” ([words](../../../../sources/16-claude-code-directions/2026-09-25-the-mind-not-the-approach.md)); a persisting whole whose parts are transient needs parts to keep arising ([whole-persists-through-generation](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)), so at the whole's level “不断创造” holds, which removes the tension with Ren's words that reading it only as a lineage's continuation had.
**What would change it.** Ren reading 生生不息 as endless novelty (it then needs unbounded gain, whose exact form P111 gives), or at the level of one member or one lineage.

## 2. 终结、侵蚀、开放与所持条件 / Endings, underminings, openness and held conditions

### D6. 终结之步的汲取与侵蚀 / Drawing across an ending, and undermining

所在：第 8 节第六、七项；[life-as-condition-of-life](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/life-as-condition-of-life.md)、[space-and-its-parts](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/space-and-its-parts.md)。

**决定。** 逐步读法的定义不增不减，它的判定作为定理保留：在承担自身的体现关系项终结之步对它的汲取，断裂它自身的依存（`Embodied.ending_breaks`），所以在逐步读法中汲取者的事件不活，对每个汲取者都如此，不另划一类汲取者；对持续不变的依存的侵蚀，使依存者在该步之后不活跃（`Web.Dependent.inactive_below`），依存者原本活跃时这是它的终结（`Web.Dependent.ceases_below`）。这些判定不作为对汲取者的估值，即不据此判定哪些汲取者更坏。终结或侵蚀是否使一个继续更坏，由可能性的比较决定：比较逐步计水平所容许者，活跃与否，看它使能形成者失去什么，不看对事件的判决。
**理由。** 判定是定义的定理，不是另加的选择；比较已不用 `living`（§6.12），为它另划汲取者的类，是给一个不用于比较的读法加前提；“更坏”经 C。
**会改变它的。** Ren 在比较中恢复 `living` 的某个读法，或为逐步读法另划汲取者的类。

**Decision.** The stepwise reading's definition is neither widened nor narrowed, and its verdicts stay as theorems: drawing on a self-bearing embodied relatum across the step at which it ends breaks its own dependence (`Embodied.ending_breaks`), so on the stepwise reading the drawer's event is not living, for every drawer, with no narrower class of drawers chosen; undermining a dependence that persists unchanged leaves the relatum depending not active after the step (`Web.Dependent.inactive_below`), its ending where it was active (`Web.Dependent.ceases_below`). These verdicts are not taken as valuations of the drawers: they do not decide which drawers are worse. Whether an ending or an undermining makes a course worse is decided by the comparison on possibilities, which counts at each step what the levels admit, active or not, by what it removes from what can form, not by a verdict on the event.
**Reasons.** The verdicts are theorems of the definition, not further choices; no comparison uses `living` any longer (section 6.12), and choosing a class of drawers for it would add a premise to a reading no comparison uses; “worse” goes through C.
**What would change it.** Ren reinstating a reading of `living` in the comparison, or choosing a class of drawers for the stepwise reading.

### D7. 开放与增益 / Openness and gain

所在：第 8 节第八项；[continued-change-needs-openness](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md)。

**决定。** “开放”指在每个有限家族之外的交换（如 `exposed` 中净量为零）；流入不是开放，是增益，两者分开：持续的变化需要开放（[continued-change-needs-openness](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md)），新需要增益，在第一律之下增益来自其余或跨过边界（P111，`Flow.rise_from_rest_or_open`、`Flow.new_beyond_every`）。
**理由。** 定理把两者分开使用：`exposed` 净量为零而开放；把开放读作流入，会使“持续的变化需要开放”弱于已证者，并把它与 P111 证明新所需的增益混同。
**会改变它的。** Ren 以“开放”指净流入（读法随之为：持续的变化需要外部的交换，新需要流入；定理不变）。

**Decision.** “Openness” names exchange beyond every finite family (as in `exposed`, at zero net). Inflow is not openness but gain, and the two are kept apart: continued change needs openness ([continued-change-needs-openness](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md)); novelty needs gain, which under the first law comes from the rest or across the boundary (P111, `Flow.rise_from_rest_or_open`, `Flow.new_beyond_every`).
**Reasons.** The theorems use the two apart: `exposed` is open at zero net. Reading openness as inflow would make “continued change needs openness” weaker than what is proved, and would merge it with the gain that P111 proves novelty needs.
**What would change it.** Ren meaning net inflow by “open” (the readings become: continued change needs exchange beyond, novelty needs inflow; the theorems are unchanged).

### D8. 所持条件被表示时的估值 / Valuation where a held condition is represented

所在：第 8 节第九项；[reserve-defers-ending](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/reserve-defers-ending.md)。

**决定。** 估值用可能性的读法（D1）：储备作为使一个形成跨过休眠仍在能形成者之中的条件被计入。依存转化到所持条件上的读法是动力学的读法（由构造满足 `Dependent`），`living` 的读法不再用于估值。
**理由。** §6.12；可能性的读法计入形成，活跃与否，这正是储备所保住的。
**会改变它的。** 同 D1。

**Decision.** Valuation uses the possibility reading (D1): a reserve counts as a condition that keeps a formation among what can form across dormancy. The reading in which dependence transfers onto the held condition is the dynamics' reading (it satisfies `Dependent` by construction), and the readings of `living` no longer value.
**Reasons.** Section 6.12; the possibility reading counts formations active or not, which is what a reserve keeps.
**What would change it.** As for D1.

### D9. 以之为生、世系与新的生起 / Living on, descent and new arising

所在：第 8 节第十项；[living-on-and-descent](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/living-on-and-descent.md)。

**决定。** 以之为生与世系采用为延续的已形成关系（在用到该补全时 N_rel 沿之传递的关系，接续经之而读，D5），不采用为直接的估值。带来新的生起是否使结果更好：它恰在扩大能形成者处排在其不在之上；不增加可能性的形成与其不在不分高下（`Ranking.possible_tie`），理论不另加次序来分：可能性优先也会把一次休息排在不间断的活动之下（`Ranking.Rest.rest_ranked_below`），把停顿算作损失。超出扩大的、使新的生命存在的价值，留在理论之外（§6.6）。
**理由。** 直接的估值加一个权重（前提），并给延续本身记功；作为关系，它们不带权重地进入范围与意义。
**会改变它的。** Ren 采用对世系的估值，或一个度量。

**Decision.** Living on and descent are adopted as formed relations of continuation (relations along which N_rel transmits where that completion is used, and through which succession is read, D5), not as direct valuations. Whether bringing about new arising makes an outcome better: it ranks above its absence exactly where it widens what can form; a formation that adds no possibility ties with its absence (`Ranking.possible_tie`), and the theory adds no order to break the tie, since possibilities first would also rank a rest below uninterrupted activity (`Ranking.Rest.rest_ranked_below`), counting a pause as a loss. The value of bringing new lives into existence beyond widening stays outside the theory (section 6.6).
**Reasons.** A direct valuation adds a weight, a premise, and credits continuation as such; as relations they enter extent and significance with no weight.
**What would change it.** Ren adopting a valuation of descent, or a measure.

### D10. 逐步读法、以消耗取胜与关系的纪律 / The stepwise reading, winning by consumption and the correspondence discipline

所在：[becoming-stepwise-reading](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/becoming-stepwise-reading.md)、[condition-correspondence-discipline](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/condition-correspondence-discipline.md)、[metaethical-core-characterization](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md)。

**决定。** 一、逐步的 living 以“不侵蚀”为其读法，与整段读法、关系层读法并列；比较不用它（D1）。二、对以消耗取胜的判决是对进程的判决：在可能性的比较之下，阈值之前的事件被计入，不被扣下（`Ranking.malignant_possible`），排序来自部分失去的。三、`related` 的对应纪律保留交互（一方汲取、另一方以正的水平依存于同一个可能使其失败的条件）；共同参与作为另一选择留在核心上。公理读作 `Unrestricted`（D16）时，G0 不再需要沿 `related` 传递，这一选择不影响 G0。四、[metaethical-core-characterization](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md) 的读法选择，就比较而言由 D1 回答；其哲学论证仍开放（§10）。
**理由。** 三个候选连同其见证都保留；比较以可能性进行时，选择其中哪一个不再有结论依赖于它；公理直接给出意义，纪律只对另一种补全起作用。
**会改变它的。** Ren 取共同参与；比较中用到 `living`。

**Decision.** (1) Stepwise living keeps “not undermining” as its reading, beside the whole-run and relation-level readings; no comparison uses it (D1). (2) The verdict on winning by consumption is a verdict on the course: under the comparison on possibilities the events before the threshold are included, not withheld (`Ranking.malignant_possible`), and the ranking comes from what the part loses. (3) The correspondence discipline for `related` keeps interaction (one draws on, and the other depends at a positive level on, one condition that can fail it); co-participation stays on the core as the alternative. With the axiom read as `Unrestricted` (D16), G0 no longer needs transmission along `related`, so the choice does not bear on G0. (4) The reading choice of [metaethical-core-characterization](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md) is answered for comparison by D1; its philosophical argument stays open (section 10).
**Reasons.** The three candidates are kept with their witnesses; with comparison on possibilities, nothing concluded depends on choosing among them; the axiom gives significance directly, so the discipline matters only for the other completion.
**What would change it.** Ren choosing co-participation; a use of `living` in comparison.

## 3. 想要延续者与一条公理 / What wants its continuation, and the one axiom

### D11. S0、公理的辖域与补全 / S0, the range of the axiom and the completion

所在：第 8 节第十一项；计划 §6 第 1、6 项；[held-lives-g0-is-centering](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/held-lives-g0-is-centering.md)。

**决定。** 一、S0 是“想要”的读法，从“规范前提”一栏移出（计划 §6.6）。二、公理的辖域是任何东西，如 Ren 的原话：“if we want to add one axiom, that is for anything, its continuation into the future has significance”（2026-10-10），也如 §6.8 所读；这是 Ren 的原话所定，此处只是记录。所以无人持有的生命（休眠的种子、昏迷者、不再想活下去的人）从每个立场都有意义。这一意义不决定任何人该做什么：延续之间的实践判断，以及它与一个人自己意愿的关系，仍由 C、L 与 J1–J4 处理（计划 §4.5）。三、在被持有的生命上的两种补全中，采用 S0 加 N_std（立场中立），因为 `Unrestricted` 给出的就是它（§6.8）；连通整体上的 S0 加 N_rel（`significant_of_transmits`）保留为另一种。
**理由。** 一、提议的“anything that want's its continuation to have significance”就是每个想要之点上的种子；二、Ren 的原话，以及 Ren 2026-10-10 的“so there isn't really who's continuation matters, which requires axiom”；三、N_rel 需要连通，是另一前提。
**会改变它的。** Ren 把公理限于想要者（那时无人持有的生命只经已形成的关系到达）。

**Decision.** (1) S0 is the reading of wanting, moved out of the “normative premise” column (plan §6.6). (2) The axiom ranges over anything, as Ren's words say, “if we want to add one axiom, that is for anything, its continuation into the future has significance” (10 October 2026), and as section 6.8 reads it; Ren's words settle this, and the record only records it. So lives no standpoint holds (a dormant seed, an unconscious patient, someone who no longer wants to go on) have significance from every standpoint. That significance decides nothing about what anyone should do: practical judgment between continuations, and how it meets a person's own wish, stay with C, L and J1–J4 (plan §4.5). (3) Of the two completions on held lives, S0 with N_std (standpoint neutrality) is adopted, because it is what `Unrestricted` gives (section 6.8); S0 with N_rel on a connected whole (`significant_of_transmits`) stays as the alternative.
**Reasons.** (1) The proposal's “anything that want's its continuation to have significance” is the seed at each wanting point. (2) Ren's words, and Ren's “so there isn't really who's continuation matters, which requires axiom” (10 October 2026). (3) N_rel needs connection, a further premise.
**What would change it.** Ren restricting the axiom to what wants (lives no standpoint holds are then reached only through formed relations).

### D12. 推出的范围与输送 / The derived extent and conveyance

所在：第 8 节第十一项；计划 §6 第 4、4a 项；[derived-extent-limits](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/derived-extent-limits.md)、[conveyance-crosses-the-boundary](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/conveyance-crosses-the-boundary.md)、[continuing-self-fed-from-beyond](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continuing-self-fed-from-beyond.md)。

**决定。** 一、推出的范围不用公理，向后沿依存，到一个延续所依存的（供给闭包），向前沿所及，到其交换所到达的（所及闭包）；每个已形成的关系只经 N_rel 进入，在另一种补全中。Ren 2026-10-10 的“i think there are two evolution attractor forces, one is for the self(life, and what's within that life, within ecosystem and beyond), and another one is the whole, which is also a force of evolution. and everything exists in between. so this is a relation that is self recursive - system and subsystem”（[原话](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-centers-within-the-dynamics.md)）经五个闭包读：自身、供给闭包、影响闭包在系统之内层层相套，所及闭包也在系统之内（[levels-of-closure](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/levels-of-closure.md)）。二、输送是已形成的关系，与交互、以之为生与世系并列。
**理由。** 一、沿每个已形成的关系，范围会包含一个形成所触及的一切，那既不由动力学推出，又是总体化的（[derived-extent-limits](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/derived-extent-limits.md)）；依存与所及是定理所推出的。二、它使一个持续家族的边界是形成的、实在的，而不是关系中的切断（[conveyance-crosses-the-boundary](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/conveyance-crosses-the-boundary.md)），读出 Ren 的“how much does one consider something to be a part of its system dynamics”。
**会改变它的。** 沿另一种关系不用公理推出范围；一种把 Ren 不算作相关者联系起来的输送。

**Decision.** (1) The derived extent, with no axiom, runs backward along dependence to what a continuation depends on (the feeding closure), and forward along reach to what its exchange reaches (the reach closure); every formed relation enters only through N_rel, in the other completion. Ren's “i think there are two evolution attractor forces, one is for the self(life, and what's within that life, within ecosystem and beyond), and another one is the whole, which is also a force of evolution. and everything exists in between. so this is a relation that is self recursive - system and subsystem” (10 October 2026, [words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-centers-within-the-dynamics.md)) is read through the five closures: the self, the feeding closure and the influence closure nest within the system, and the reach closure lies within it too ([levels-of-closure](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/levels-of-closure.md)). (2) Conveyance is a formed relation, beside interaction, living on and descent.
**Reasons.** (1) Along every formed relation the extent would include whatever a formation touches, which the dynamics does not derive and which totalizes ([derived-extent-limits](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/derived-extent-limits.md)); dependence and reach are what the theorems derive. (2) Conveyance makes a continuing family's boundary formed and real, no cut in the relations ([conveyance-crosses-the-boundary](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/conveyance-crosses-the-boundary.md)), which reads Ren's “how much does one consider something to be a part of its system dynamics”.
**What would change it.** A derivation of extent along another relation with no axiom; a conveyance relating what Ren would not count as related.

### D13. 不损 / Not harm

所在：第 8 节第十一、十二项；计划 §6 第 4b 项；[significance-retained-within-margin](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/significance-retained-within-margin.md)。

**决定。** “不损”读作保留：有一个部分的水平保留无它时有意义者处，部分不损（§6.8）。保留计入尚未形成者（`Preemption`），不读作逐步读法的不侵蚀（那只计已满足的依存）。它既不是过滤（决定哪些延续有意义），也不是一个延续对他者的主张：它是有与无一个部分的水平之间的关系。其边界是余量：部分恰在其净汲取超出余量处失去一个延续（`Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`），这就是 Ren 所说“a phase transition with a clear boundary, not some "outside will or belief"”的形式。失去经 C 进入判断，C 排序无损的扩展。
**理由。** 作为对成员延续的过滤，几乎每个生命都会失去意义，因为几乎所有生命都经终结其他生命而延续；作为对他者的主张，它会加上主张的前提（关系，不是权利）；保留在公理之下不加前提，其边界就是 Ren 的相变；逐步读法会把先占的汲取者算作无损（`Preemption.not_undermining_yet_loses`）。
**会改变它的。** Ren 把“不损”读作对行动的约束，而不是对进程的比较。

**Decision.** “Not harm” is read as retaining: a part does no harm where the levels with it retain what has significance without it (section 6.8). Retaining counts what has not yet formed (`Preemption`); not harm is not read as the stepwise reading's not undermining, which counts only dependences met. It is neither a filter on which continuations have significance nor a claim of one continuation on others: it is a relation between the levels with and without a part. Its boundary is the margin: a part loses a continuation exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin (`Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`), the form of Ren's “a phase transition with a clear boundary, not some "outside will or belief"”. A loss enters judgment through C, which ranks lossless extensions.
**Reasons.** As a filter on members' continuations, nearly every life would lose significance, for almost all life continues by ending other life; as a claim on others it would add a premise of claims (relations, not rights); retaining adds no premise under the axiom, and its boundary is Ren's phase transition; the stepwise reading would count a preemptive drawer as harmless (`Preemption.not_undermining_yet_loses`).
**What would change it.** Ren reading “not harm” as a constraint on acts rather than a comparison of courses.

### D14. 损的层次与形成的个体化 / The level of harm and the individuation of formations

所在：第 8 节第十一、十二项；计划 §6 第 2 项；[held-lives-g0-is-centering](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/held-lives-g0-is-centering.md)、[derived-extent-limits](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/derived-extent-limits.md)、[whole-first-and-bounded-centers](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-first-and-bounded-centers.md)。

**会改变它的。** Ren 只以成员或只以谱系个体化；一个在可能性之间权衡的度量（另一前提）。

**The level, answered by Ren (not the AI's decision).** Harm is read at the level of the whole, a weaker version within the system one is in, then the self (Ren, 10 October 2026, see section 0), with “the whole” the actual whole, never any center's model of it, and comparison by inclusion never licensing an imposed ending. **Decision (the AI's): the individuation of formations**, which fixes what counts as a loss (section 6.8). Formations are individuated at both levels `Becoming.Identity` gives, each with its use. (1) To class a part as degenerative, neutral or generative (D2), formations are counted as lineages, kinds that renew: taking within renewal is then no harm, and the margin of renewal is the phase boundary of D13. (2) A member's ending is still a loss of that member's continuation: no comparison by inclusion licenses it, and a part that adds its own continuation while ending a member is mixed, unranked without a measure.
**Reasons.** Counting only members would make nearly every eating a harm, with no phase boundary at renewal, against Ren's three classes; counting only lineages would let a member's ending count for nothing, a totalizing reading; with both, Ren's phase transition has its place and no ending is licensed.
**What would change it.** Ren individuating only members or only lineages; a measure trading possibilities against each other (a further premise).
