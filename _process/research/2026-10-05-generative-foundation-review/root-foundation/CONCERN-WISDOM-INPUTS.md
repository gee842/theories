These are inputs to distinct theorems. Their different meanings cannot be erased by giving all of them the name “learning.” The [settlement](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md) already conditionally joins reason revision, actual protection and funded reconstruction; repeating that game would not close the upstream contracts.

The concern protocol currently compares formed routes as interventions. Its separate maintenance tail measures actual paid persistence; final reserve is not an oracle selecting an architecture, and comparison does not establish an ex ante optimizing selector. Its declared future private opportunities repair the earlier free-omission problem, but their existence remains an environmental condition. This preserves a route toward concern without inferring that private selection must favor it.

## Strongest complementary next connection

**Paid, context-sensitive certificate production → rooted reason revision → charged successor replay.** This complements the active record, predictor-protocol and action-routing studies rather than constructing another learner or recipient-helping fixture.

The first concrete target is to replace the settlement's direct injection of current validity bits with a finite physical audit protocol consuming actual retained records. Reuse its existing anchor/two-patient graph and its alternative-ground example. A declared, independently justified relevance schema remains an input; the mechanism should acquire the descriptive evidence needed to apply that schema, record its target and context, pay for decoding and closure, and carry the actual residual stocks into action or succession.

A useful theorem would establish that completed audits produce exactly the rooted closure justified by the **current** recorded certificates, under the stated assay and relevance contracts. It should preserve an alternative derivation when another is defeated, reject unrooted cycles, and distinguish failed evidence delivery from refutation. An explicit paid successor protocol should either reconstruct the same justified state or report the missing record, stale context, component or liquidity obligation. The full audit cost should replace the downstream abstract bill with an accounted producer, not be fitted afterward.

For noisy acquisition, the exact sound-oracle theorem cannot be inherited without qualification. Use jointly controlled certificate errors and report incomplete coverage, or keep the first construction within an explicit deterministic calibrated-assay class. A finite confidence statement cannot become infallible validity, and independent error allowances cannot be silently restarted across certificates or successors.

The diagnostic comparison should hold original resources and truthful observations fixed while varying retained context/provenance, then measure false admission, appropriate retraction, preserved alternative support and executable protection. Include source-dependent failed acquisition and an attack that blocks action while leaving warrant intact. This would replace a central audit/implementation assumption while leaving the positive ethical ground and the warranted transfer schema visible.

## Scope discipline

A finite supplied repertoire can establish conditional revision of selectors and reasons. Completing the foundation does not require generating every possible algorithm or deriving physical recipes without physical laws. It does require informative general interfaces and actual composition, rather than treating one favorable binary fixture as universal evidence. Open-ended novelty, indefinitely growing memory and inevitable moral improvement are additional claims only where explicitly made.

The ethical argument remains connected but distinct: another center's vulnerability, agency or loss must be recognized through a causal process, and the reason for taking it seriously must withstand scrutiny. An accurately stored record, a persistent regime or a profitable audit cannot by itself establish that justification.
