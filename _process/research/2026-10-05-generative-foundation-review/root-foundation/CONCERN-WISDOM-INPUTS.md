## Scope discipline

A finite supplied repertoire can establish conditional revision of selectors and reasons. Completing the foundation does not require generating every possible algorithm or deriving physical recipes without physical laws. It does require informative general interfaces and actual composition, rather than treating one favorable binary fixture as universal evidence. Open-ended novelty, indefinitely growing memory and inevitable moral improvement are additional claims only where explicitly made.

The ethical argument remains connected but distinct: another center's vulnerability, agency or loss must be recognized through a causal process, and the reason for taking it seriously must withstand scrutiny. An accurately stored record, a persistent regime or a profitable audit cannot by itself establish that justification.
