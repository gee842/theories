# Identifying exercised update behavior

6 October 2026. AI-authored conditional analysis for the [route-diagnostics protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/PROTOCOL.md). The actual route functions come from [formed transmission](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md); [successor learning](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SUCCESSOR-LEARNING.md) supplies their exercise by actual inherited carriers. This analysis identifies evidence requirements for revising those functions, not a new physical diagnostic apparatus.

## 1. Interface and signatures

Let \(q\in\{0,1,2,3\}\), with recipient bit \(b=\lfloor q/2\rfloor\bmod2\). The ordinary recipient command is \(a=b\). Let \(c\in\{0,1\}\) be actual capture reported through a separately specified, acquired channel, and \(b'\) the recipient bit after a completed write through one unchanged installed route. Target addressing is calibrated, and the external recipient outcome channel is available before route-specific routing. CLOSED still deliberately disconnects that input. Define \(f=[b'\ne a]\).

The actual update definitions imply:

| Actual capture \(c\) | TARGET flip | PRIVATE_ZERO flip | CLOSED flip | FROZEN flip |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

TARGET writes \(a\) on capture and \(1-a\) on noncapture. PRIVATE_ZERO always decodes a false private return and writes \(1-a\). CLOSED has no recipient input; FROZEN does not commit a changed payload. Both retain \(b\). The actor bit is preserved in each case.

Consequently an admissible failure observation and an admissible success observation, in either chronological order, give the signature pair \((f_0,f_1)\): TARGET \((1,0)\), PRIVATE_ZERO \((1,1)\), and CLOSED/FROZEN \((0,0)\). A pair \((0,1)\) does not fit this fixed-route family under the assumptions. It is evidence to revisit the observation, completed-write, stability or implementation assumptions, rather than a license to force a route label.

This identifies three **task-relative update classes**, not four physical configurations. It also gives the precise one-polarity limitation: failure alone aliases TARGET with PRIVATE_ZERO; success alone aliases TARGET with CLOSED/FROZEN. Neither limitation excludes information outside this interface, such as a paid read of the actual route configuration.

## 2. Why the receipt must be independently warranted

The functions receive actual capture as a plant input, but PRIVATE_ZERO substitutes its own false private receipt before decoding. A meta-observer cannot treat that substituted value as an independent recipient report and then claim to have verified the substitution. Similarly, a saved `receipt_source`, `decoder_errors` or successful-witness label is not a constructed sensor.

Plant supply availability and route input availability are distinct. An unavailable source can produce actual noncapture despite a correct action. The flip equations still describe a TARGET decoder operating on that noncapture, while its resulting estimate can be wrong. A missing route input instead prevents the contemplated TARGET update and invalidates the available-input signature. Neither case establishes an ecological cause from a zero receipt alone.

An unpaid write is another different event: its unchanged payload does not show that a writing route is CLOSED or FROZEN. The diagnostic tuple must therefore retain actual completion/access conditions. Reading before and after memory, independently acquiring an outcome and obtaining a write-completion report require an explicit physical interface and its costs; the algebra does not construct them.

The formal package also specifies a small abstract inspection account: pre-read, independent outcome acquisition, write, then post-read, with nonnegative prices and no intermediate inflow. A complete diagnostic observation exists exactly when initial fuel covers all four prices; every partial outcome preserves initial fuel as remaining plus spent. With unit prices, fuel two pays two stages but cannot commit a write; fuel three commits it but cannot pay to observe the result. Both return no diagnostic tuple. This distinguishes an absent diagnostic from a negative diagnosis.

This four-stage account is a supplied protocol, not a refinement of the existing seven-unit physical learner probe. It omits that probe's work/target-access operations and does not establish how an independent monitor is built or whether an existing acquisition/write can be shared. Its total is not asserted to be the exact additional bill for a downstream route-revision implementation.

## 3. Outcome words versus record access

Consider a stable binary productive law \(\ell\), available supply and input, fully completed ordinary probes, and an initial recipient bit \(b=1-\ell\). Let capture be \([b=\ell]\), followed by the actual update before the next probe.

The first three capture words are:

| Route | Probe 1 | Probe 2 | Probe 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| TARGET | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| PRIVATE_ZERO | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| CLOSED/FROZEN | 0 | 0 | 0 |

After the first failed probe, TARGET and PRIVATE_ZERO both flip to the productive bit. After their second successful probe, TARGET keeps it and PRIVATE_ZERO flips away. Thus three outcome-only probes distinguish these three classes, while two cannot distinguish TARGET from PRIVATE_ZERO. A post-write memory observation distinguishes those two at the second probe. On the first failed probe, unchanged memory already separates the nonwriting class.

This is an identification-depth result for this particular ordinary-action experiment and observation interface. It is not a lower bound over all possible calibrated interventions, known recipes or channels. The initial wrong bit must come from a real acquired change or a specified paid challenge; it cannot be inserted as hidden information for the ordinary learner. Complete probe affordability and paid diagnostic observation remain premises. A finite unchanging successful history need not ever supply the contrasting evidence.

## 4. Equivalence and the limit of passive success

For recipient-only updates, CLOSED and FROZEN retain exactly the same payload for every list of actual receipt inputs, starting from the same payload. Their commands and any common tariffs and returns driven by those commands therefore agree under the same plant history. This statement deliberately excludes `gamma` and acquired input-presence/label reports, which distinguish the physical implementations. It also excludes actor-target probes: the CLOSED route's actor input remains functional.

In a stable available plant with a correct initial bit, TARGET also retains that bit after every success. It therefore has the same recipient command and capture words as CLOSED and FROZEN for every finite horizon on which the probes complete. A history of success alone does not reveal the different responses the routes would make after failure.

This is a direct route-specific use of the existing [controller-history interface](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/ControllerRevision.lean), which already proves that equal complete feedback and inherited memory give equal subsequent commands. The new question is which actual route functions can produce those same observations under the named experiment. No new general indistinguishability principle or universal impossibility of learning is claimed.

## 5. What a selector may conclude

An admitted signature can identify how this route updated on the observed task. It does not establish that changing the route is affordable, authorized or beneficial in the next context; that a correct present prediction will stay correct; or that the route's recipe arose through the same process. A nominal route read plus trusted recipe semantics can identify configuration without behavioral testing, but those semantics and calibration then do explanatory work.

The constructive follow-on must combine an actual diagnostic interface with paid replacement parts, preserved records, continued experiments and a rule connecting observed consequences to retained alternatives. Equivalent routes may remain plural; identifying one uniquely is unnecessary unless the downstream task makes their difference consequential. Paid route revision is being developed separately in the successor branch, and this study does not substitute a chosen TARGET label for that construction.
