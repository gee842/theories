## Generic interface

[AttainableQuestions.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/AttainableQuestions.lean) reuses `InquirySufficiency.Policy`, `Sound` and `After`. `InquirySound` specializes terminal response to an empty action word and a true terminal condition. `RefinesLeaves` then requires the states compatible with each retained observation path to agree on the question. Thus termination means ending an informational episode; it does not provide a physical reporting action or stored decoder at no cost.

The common-action and all-successor requirements remain in `Sound`: an inquiry action is enabled at every candidate, and every admitted successor obeys the supplied prefix condition and branch policy. `After` retains all matching predecessor/successor possibilities. The generic module does not assume deterministic dynamics or eliminate unfavorable outcomes. `identifies_has_actual_execution` supplies an admitted complete execution from every candidate, while `identifies_every_actual_execution_safe` preserves the native trace and every prefix condition for every admitted execution. Neither installs a physical scheduler. Actual observable failed attempts belong in the application's process relation; simply declaring them undefined would change that application.

`Question` is a binary relation, while `Agreement answer` specializes it to equality of an actual answer function. `Coarser` is reverse relation inclusion and `Join` is conjunction. The generic theorems need no unproved equivalence-relation laws; their interpretation as partitions uses the `Agreement` specialization. Relations are compared on the declared state space, not only on whichever observations an investigator happened to retain.

`shared_policy_join_iff` and `family_shared_policy_iff` concern the **same** finite observation-dependent policy. They do not move an existential policy choice through a universal question quantifier. Complete observation-path retention is the logical information interface here; a compressed or physically retained record needs a separate adequacy argument. In particular, a terminal `Unit` response does not itself carry all answers after the path is forgotten.

`LeafContinuations` supplies one explicit continuation at each syntactic terminal leaf. `ContinuationsIdentify` certifies each continuation at the actual successor belief reached by the first policy. `continueAtLeaves` therefore composes one continuing history and preserves the same native prefix conditions. It does not restart from the original belief or assume that two separately affordable inquiries are jointly affordable.

One qualification is essential: `PreservesAgreement` transports **within-leaf agreement**, not necessarily the numerical or semantic value of an arbitrary changing readout. Resetting a readout to a constant can satisfy agreement preservation. Claims about a fixed initial answer require an invariant target coordinate; `agreement_preserved_of_key_preserved` and `continuing_initial_question_identifies_join` supply that specialization. The one-packet module proves this invariant directly. The continuation result is a sufficient structural condition; it is not a derivation that an application actually restores access or retains an answer.

## One-packet witness

[OnePacketQuestions.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/OnePacketQuestions.lean) represents the previously established three behavioral targets, an available/spent packet, the two alternative first-probe columns, and common ordinary controls. The target is hidden from policy branching but remains in every state. `step_retains_initial_target` and `trace_retains_initial_target` prevent substitution of current post-consumption behavior for the initial question.

Both probe outcomes consume the packet. A probe at learned output 1 singles out A; the other singles out B. After consumption, the surviving negative pair has identical observations under every admitted action. `control output` permits arbitrary finite target-independent stuttering with a known common output; depleted probes give a distinct denial. Neither operation restores the packet. This grants more ordinary control freedom than a particular physical schedule while preserving the decisive absence of another distinguishing source use.

`spent_pair_same_class` and `initial_question_constraint` induct over arbitrary finite policy syntax, including any number of control and denial branches. The argument does not merely inspect two predetermined probes. A policy that stops before probing must agree on all initial candidates; a first A-probe leaves B/C inseparable and a first B-probe leaves A/C inseparable. The positive one-probe witnesses cover the converse. Consequently, for every answer function `f`,

\[
\operatorname{Attainable}(f)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(B)=f(C)\;\lor\;f(A)=f(C).
\]

This is the exact classification at the declared three-target interface. It gives separate attainability of the A and B questions and rules out both their joint answer and the blocked-only question. `no_attainable_common_upper` additionally excludes any attainable refinement of both initial questions. Thus the no-greatest conclusion applies within any permitted question family containing them; it does not incorrectly infer a subclass result merely from absence of a greatest member in a larger family of relations. It does not identify the two underlying blocked parameterizations: those have the same target C here.

The ordinary-controls abstraction is not a full Python-state refinement. In [the original native port](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/diagnosis.py), `Port.command` first pays interface costs, a probe consumes ten source units on either outcome, productive arrival credits fuel, and receipt acquisition is charged separately. Phase motion, reset and ordinary sensing do not restore source. The permissive witness abstracts physical schedules and ordinary costs, while retaining packet exhaustion and all information relevant to the negative pair. Positive logical attainability does not establish affordability or successful retention by the installed controller; the original retention failures remain real.
