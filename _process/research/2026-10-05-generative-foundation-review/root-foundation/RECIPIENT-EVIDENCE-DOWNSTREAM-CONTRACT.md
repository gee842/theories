# Recipient evidence: the downstream contract

6 October 2026. AI-authored shared-file handoff for the ongoing generative foundation. This is a read-only contract audit, not a new apparatus, proof or model. It incorporates [the fresh-acquisition interface assessment](FRESH-ACQUISITION-INTERFACE.md). The active reason-ground work remains owned by its existing chat; this note does not modify or duplicate it.

**Smallest justified handoff:** fresh acquired evidence can supply a context-bound factual premise about **which command produces a receipt for one identified recipient**. Under an independently defended reason to support that recipient, a warranted connection between receipt and the relevant good, and legitimate authority, that fact can inform practical judgment and paid action. It does not supply those evaluative premises, a preference to act on them, or a complete audit of every reason relation.

## 2. The handoff to evaluative judgment

Use the following interface as a premise checklist, not a claim that it is already implemented:

1. **Producer datum:** a recipient identifier, relevant context/window, actual acquired record, supported command or explicit unknown, provenance, and the costed state from which use is feasible. Preserve the condition that the observation law remains applicable through action.
2. **Factual application premise:** identify what the receipt does for this recipient's independently described condition. Ten units in `patient` are not automatically welfare, prevention of harm, experienced value or the patient's own priorities. Establish that application relation rather than renaming the resource counter.
3. **Evaluative premise:** defend why this good or harm supplies a pro tanto reason in this context. The [ethical foundation][ethics], N1–N4, offers explicit standing, relevant consistency, answerability and feasible-implementation premises. N1 supplies positive force; identity symmetry alone also permits indifference to everyone. These are defended ethical commitments, not consequences of the receipt theorem.
4. **Practical influence:** show that the reason can affect a choice among genuinely feasible alternatives, retaining costs, other affected claims and conflicts. A fixed schedule executing an already supplied G does not show the formation of such influence. Learned valuation, intervention efficacy and actual outcome remain separate quantities in the [concern/correction interface][concern].
5. **Authority and action:** identify legitimate authorization independently of a technical enabled flag, then preserve the actual permission, delivery, opportunity and prefix-funding conditions. A warranted reason is neither an automatic veto nor an all-things-considered command.

Fresh evidence can discharge the producer datum and part of the factual application premise **within its declared law**. It cannot discharge the other entries merely by being fresh. Conversely, the reason-ground work can supply a defended ethical root without reconstructing the already paid factual machinery. The needed join is an explicit relation between the same recipient, the independently significant condition, the operative command and the actual action consequences.

## 3. What protection and settlement actually require

[ReasonReplay.lean][replay], `paid_recorded_reason_sound`, takes an authentic starting state, actual history and audit trace, arbitrary `Fact` and `Reason` predicates, an `anchor : Reason .root`, calibration from true audited bits to facts, and a `relevant` implication transporting reasons along each edge. Its flexibility does not manufacture the calibration, root or relevant implication. The recipient fact can serve as one factual premise only after the target meaning and an actual state/protocol map are established.

[ReasonProtection.lean][protection], `reached_reason_and_protection`, adds a reached publication, `Fresh` operating state, a protection mask covering the admitted mask, an actual paid action trace, and separately supplied `Permissible G` with `permission : Permissible G`. Here `G` is a **patient mask**, not the recipient model's objective proposition. `Fresh` means a valid formed gate, unused opportunity, unspent lease, intact technical authority and delivery; it does not mean newly acquired world evidence. `Patient.p` and `Patient.q` are distinct patients. A raw copy of the recipient command mask would change the meanings. No such composition is established by this note.

The smallest next inference can therefore stay at the existing **one-recipient instrumental-action interface**, using [RecipientAction.lean][action], `paid_import_and_execution`, while the ethical and evaluative premises are supplied and examined explicitly. There is no need to force this finite datum through a two-patient protector merely to reuse a familiar theorem name. A later physical protection claim needs its own meaning-preserving state map and evidence that the commanded receipt produces the stated protection.

The [settlement report][settlement], §1 and Theorem R2, is stronger in another direction. Its paid audit installs the **current validity bit for every candidate edge**, with warranted status and stable audit/action context. It also assumes a positive anchor, shared evaluative transfer, each admitted patient's margin `q_i > D`, effective control, attack/insulation bounds, all prefix liquidity and continuing support. The fresh sparse view `[absent, fresh, absent]` does not supply this complete audit. Unknown cannot become “invalid” merely to fill the table. Nor do command alternatives become patients, resource units become money, or aggregate recipient gains imply every patient's margin. To claim R2, discharge those actual inputs; otherwise use an appropriately restricted conclusion and preserve uncovered cases.

## 4. Warrant, failure and the stopping boundary

Formal conditional validity says that the conclusion follows under the stated observation and event laws. Empirical warrant requires evidence that the proposed system actually has those channels, bearer meanings, context stability, resources and consequences. The [AgentDojo audit][agentdojo] concerns saved learned-model tool behavior in a simulated environment; the [recipient-cue study][cue] concerns cue-sensitive provisioning. Neither empirically identifies this formal recipient core, the ethical root, or the formation of justified concern. Their measured scope should remain separate from the logical interface.

The counterexamples remain decisive: silent change after observation defeats use; corrupted acquisition can produce a coherent false record; correct evidence can be unaffordable to use; a private learner can understand another's needs while exploiting or ignoring them; and loss of a delivery route does not refute the reason. Failure to prove an action feasible does not erase the recipient's claim. These distinctions are required by the existing account, not optional cautions.

No repeated inspector is required by these downstream soundness contracts. Our original pre-import origin is consumed; actual fresh acquisition creates new evidence without rearming that old permission. A new guard is needed only if a stronger repeated-corroboration promise is expressly made. Likewise, deriving an autonomous inquiry schedule is not a prerequisite for this finite evidence-to-action handoff. The next central work is the already identified reason-ground/evaluative connection, under explicit ethical premises and with an application relation that can be criticized empirically.
