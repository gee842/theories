# Goodness as a quality of the differentiated whole

6 October 2026. AI-authored investigation under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), following Ren's correction that the foundation concerns the whole. **The proposed ground is how activity realizes, obstructs and repairs its differentiated possibilities together.** Independently warranted private interests are not its starting premises. Selves, objects, purposes and standards develop within the unfolding whose quality is being assessed.

## 1. What the whole includes

“Whole” names the relevant actual unfolding, including its differentiated experiences, organizations, dependencies and consequences. It is not another person with one experience, an aggregate utility, or the incumbent institution described at a larger scale. An inquiry uses a bounded account, but must identify consequences omitted by that boundary. No observer is assumed capable of representing everything at once.

This yields a definite correction. If an arrangement's production and its controller's prospects remain unchanged while a formed life undergoes additional suffering, **the whole has changed**. The suffering is itself an occurrence within it. No return path to the controller is needed to make that difference real. An aggregate that reports no change has omitted the relevant differentiation; it has not shown that nothing happened to the whole.

That is a claim about descriptive adequacy, not yet a theorem that every difference has equal moral weight. An adverse quality here belongs to the unfolding; it does not settle every aspect or its overall assessment. This removes the private-return test without replacing the controller by a cosmic beneficiary.

## 2. The positive constitutive candidate

Actual organization introduces more than undifferentiated persistence: dependencies can enable or obstruct a continuing activity; experience can be fulfilled or adverse; relationships can support or disable understanding, response and change. These are relations to identify within the process. A deficit alone does not prove felt adversity, and every preference or proliferating activity is not thereby worthwhile.

The ethical proposal is that **the joint realization and repair of these differentiated forms of life can constitute goodness of the unfolding itself**. Goodness is not subsequently donated by a subject who already possesses an independently justified interest. Nor is the whole good merely because one of its forms reproduces. Its ethical quality concerns the actual pattern of fulfillment, impairment, dependence and possible transformation.

This is a substantive identity proposal. “Fulfillment” cannot mean simply achieving the process's installed target: successful torture would then qualify. The relevant content includes actual experiential quality, effective agency and relationships that permit affected conditions to become consequential in correction. Their ethical significance must be defended through their character and relations, rather than inferred from the word “generation.”

The positive argument is comparative. Where a feasible reorganization sustains the relevant differentiated lives and capacities while removing an actual impairment maintained by their arrangement, without countervailing warranted loss in its means, authority or other consequences, it realizes a more adequate joint continuation. Its advantage consists in that changed configuration, even after the initiator withdraws, with no return to that position. This defended ethical dominance judgment favors the changed configuration; feasibility alone does not establish it.

The favorable comparison remains an ethical judgment. Descriptive inclusion alone does not entail it. The advance is to locate and assess the proposed quality in the whole configuration, rather than making prior private warrant its admission condition.

## 3. Three structural requirements, with different force

**Consequence inclusion:** a claim about the whole cannot delete an affected continuation because it has no bargaining power, supplies no feedback or lies outside an incumbent's accounting. This demands adequate representation; it does not settle priorities.

**Joint realizability:** continuations claimed as compatible must actually be executable together. Separate certificates that each could continue alone do not establish this. Resources, timing, access and effects on other continuations remain part of the relation. Feasibility identifies genuine alternatives and conflicts; it does not make every feasible configuration good.

**Effective repair:** where activity produces an obstruction or makes a formerly adequate practice harmful, its organization must be assessable through those consequences. A proposed remedy requires an actual route for changing what follows. An unused record or preserved formula is insufficient. This requirement is conditional on the defended value of what is repaired; endless intervention and maximum revisability are not universal goods.

Together these constrain a substantive candidate of whole goodness. None secretly installs an omnipotent selector. Knowing and revising are themselves activities whose own omissions, means and effects belong in the assessment.

## 4. Deepen the existing comparison

The [no-return integration](GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md) already grants a chain in which care enables later care, while its organization displaces a severe burden onto another life. That case is inherited, not new evidence.

The whole-process comparison asks how the actual configuration could be reorganized. Suppose a feasible change preserves the realized benefits and relevant capacities while removing the displaced burden, with its means and other consequences retained and no countervailing warranted loss introduced. The positive judgment favors that change because the differentiated realization is improved together. It does not depend on the excluded life becoming useful to the original beneficiaries.

If no such change exists, the account must acknowledge a genuine conflict. Calling one branch regenerative cannot settle it. Nor does a total score decide whose continuity may be sacrificed. The source of the incompatibility, possible changes in dependence, authority and the irreversibility of losses need substantive assessment. The whole perspective reveals the conflict rather than pretending to abolish it.

## 5. Tests that the candidate must survive

Stable domination is the strongest challenge: a regime can reproduce, include accurate knowledge of suffering and maintain its own correction machinery indefinitely. Mere stability, completeness of its description and local adaptability therefore fail as criteria. Our proposal rejects identifying that regime's success with whole goodness; it must positively justify the significance of the lives and capacities its organization obstructs. Distinguishing legitimate authority from domination requires argument; lost control is not automatically morally irrelevant. A rival can dispute that judgment without denying the common process.

Likewise, ecological renewal can include irreversible loss. Not every cycle is restoration of what was lost, and not every ending is a defect. The account must distinguish completion, transformation, replacement and destruction instead of requiring every existing form to persist. Rest and final relief retain their significance without producing another benefit.

## 6. Result and grounding status

The new connection is **whole-description adequacy → joint-configuration appraisal → possible reorganization of the relations producing impairment**. The first step removes an illegitimate identification of the whole with an insulated actor or surviving regime. The ethical step identifies joint realization and repair as a proposed quality of that whole; the practical step concerns its actual transformation.

This shifts the foundation beyond enlightened private interest. It neither maximizes aggregate activity nor obtains goodness by defining away conflict. The unresolved task is to vindicate the constitutive ethical identification and its comparative reach, including cases where genuine forms of realization conflict. Existing feasibility and correction mechanisms can test its realization; they do not certify its value. No new formal theorem, empirical result or claim of completed universal necessity is made.

## 7. Continuation: the origin of a need and the value of its repair

The whole perspective changes how local improvements compose. **A genuine repair can be worth performing after a burden exists without making the process that creates and repairs that burden worthwhile.** This is not a withdrawal of concern from someone whose predicament was avoidably produced. It distinguishes two actual comparison points within the unfolding.

Consider a declared pair of possible histories from the same relevant initial configuration. In **H**, a continuing life remains free of the specified impairment. In **H′**, an intervention introduces a severe lived burden and a later response genuinely relieves it. Compare them over the same relevant duration, preserving the continuing life and matching the specified terminal supports, knowledge, opportunities and legitimate authority. The burden produces no independently identified achievement, needed information, protection or other compensating good in this comparison. Actual means and effects on others must also be included.

These are conditional case specifications, not an empirical assertion that any available repair can achieve such a match. The histories are not identical complete states: H′ contains an actual undergoing absent from H, even if the selected endpoint quantities agree. Remembered injury, changed trust, consumed support or lost opportunities would require expanding the comparison. Unknown differences remain unknown; they cannot be set to zero for convenience.

### Two baselines that cannot be substituted

At the **entrance**, the choice concerns maintaining the uninjured continuation or introducing and then relieving the burden. Our defended judgment favors H. Its positive basis is the actual lived adversity present only in H′: ending that episode does not make its occurrence equivalent to never undergoing it. The judgment concerns this contrast, with its specified absence of countervailing goods and restrictions. It does not follow from a capacity score, mere feasibility or the word “repair”; the ethical significance of the lived burden remains a substantive premise.

At the **reached impaired condition**, the available comparison can instead be genuine relief versus leaving the burden in place. Relief can then be a good response and, under further grounds, required. Rejecting the earlier package supplies no reason to abandon its affected continuation. Conversely, this later favorable comparison does not retrospectively authorize the intervention that produced the need. The counterfactual alternative has changed: preserving a life from injury is not the same option as withholding repair after injury.

This permits three coherent assessments: preservation is preferable to the introduced-burden-and-repair history; repair is preferable to continued avoidable adversity once the burden exists; and the repairer’s responsibility or motive requires a separate assessment. A real remedial benefit need not earn moral praise for its producer. None of these claims depends on a return to a controller.

### A hand result against positive-repair counting

Let N count independently identified completed repairs in a history, rather than arbitrary event subdivisions. Suppose one admitted burden–repair insertion changes H to H′, preserves the specified continuation conditions, and adds exactly one completed repair: N(H′) = N(H) + 1. Consider a proposed goodness ordering that strictly favors the larger repair count while ignoring the introduced burden and its history. It must favor H′ over H. It therefore cannot represent the whole-history comparison just defended, which favors H over H′.

One admissible insertion is sufficient for this incompatibility. If the actual process admits repeated insertions with the requisite matched conditions, the same defect can recur with additional genuine repairs. Unlimited repetition requires its own resource, time and realization conditions; no such possibility is inferred here. Counts can remain useful diagnostics of workload or response. The failed claim is that their strict increase, without the burden-producing history, establishes increasing goodness.

The meaningful test consequently includes the entrance histories and the reached-state alternatives. An assessment should recognize actual relief when it occurs, retain how its need arose, and avoid converting repeated restoration into an automatic advantage over preservation. Necessary treatment, valuable exertion or learning can change the matched goods and defeat this particular comparison. They do not rescue a counting rule that omits those distinctions.

### What this adds

The [joint-history study](YITI-REN-COFORMATION-INTEGRATION.md#3-形式接入与具体证据) already preserves an intermediate decline hidden by a selected endpoint summary. The [imposition-and-rescue analysis](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#13-imposed-burdens-and-rescue-share-an-opportunity-not-necessarily-a-history) already separates present repair efficacy from responsibility for producing the need. [Evaluative grounding](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md#an-independently-stated-repair-ground) already denies that later repair obligations justify creating learning-related deprivation. The [desert comparison](PERMITTED-RELIEF-AND-DESERT-ARGUMENT.md) already distinguishes the value of remission from the appraisal of its context. This continuation does not rediscover those results.

The [existing restoration law](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-MECHANISM.md#an-exact-relation-between-recurrence-and-restoration) already makes fresh repair from an intact condition depend on intervening damage. Its actual damage–repair suffix restores the intact bit while consuming one common unit: more repairs need not mean more remaining common support. This is an operational precursor, not a matched whole-state example, lived suffering or a new ethical theorem.

The added result concerns composition: **a process cannot certify its ethical improvement merely by repeatedly making its own later remedial comparisons favorable.** “Good produces further good” must track the formation of needs and the complete affected continuation, alongside genuine benefits. This constrains the unified candidate beyond endpoint inclusion while preserving repair’s situated value. It is a conditional philosophical comparison and elementary hand argument, not a new experiment, formal certification or derivation of all ethical significance.

## 8. Continuation: formed understanding can correct its source

**Successful formation can change warranted influence before it ends dependence.** This supplies a positive conditional connection to practical significance: understanding formed through a shared activity can legitimately correct that activity's aim. It does not derive worthwhileness from formation alone, or treat a newly acquired preference as self-validating.

Consider a conceptual mathematical apprenticeship. Its independently defended point is to understand and communicate what is true; initial guidance is justified partly by greater relevant competence. The operative aim is to establish conjecture P. For a self-contained illustration, let P say that n² + n + 41 is prime for every natural n: n = 40 gives 1681 = 41². The learner produces a verified counterexample under the same declared domain and interpretation. They still need guidance in choosing a replacement problem, proving generalizations and presenting results. The practice has formed a route to an actual ground for correction without making its participants equally competent in every respect.

The contribution changes more than a method of pursuing P. Establishing P as true must cease to be the operative aim under the stated undertaking. The counterexample's force comes from what it establishes, not from the mentor's private endorsement or the learner's desire for independence. The mentor may retain P as an explicitly false conjecture for an exercise. That changes the activity; it does not establish P as true.

Three supplied premises support the inference. Truthful inquiry is worthwhile in this practice. The particular authority being challenged is grounded in competence and support for that inquiry within a specified scope. The learner's correction is genuinely warranted. **The earlier role difference therefore cannot, by itself, defeat this correction or warrant presenting P as established.** The learner can be less capable overall while contributing the decisive consideration here; superior competence elsewhere does not repair the mentor's error here.

This is a local change in justified evidential influence, not a transfer of every decision power. Selecting the next inquiry, allocating resources and publishing remain subject to their actual responsibilities and permissions. Independent verification can be appropriate. A warranted correction may call for shared reassessment rather than immediate unilateral action. Protection of novices, non-speaking affected lives and other participants cannot depend solely on who can produce an argument. Their grounds remain distinct.

The positive whole-process proposal concerns the actual exercise of the understanding formed. The activity now contains a contribution capable of reshaping what it should become; accommodating that contribution can realize a joint understanding through differentiated roles. Appropriation occurs in the tested respect when the capacity is welcomed only as an instrument of the old aim, while its valid correction is excluded solely to preserve that aim. The arrangement then withholds the influence of the understanding whose formation supported its guidance. Recognizing this difference requires neither independent owners of value nor treating every newly formed purpose as good.

The preserved higher good matters. This is revision of a proximate aim under truthful inquiry, not an explanation of the origin or validity of every standard. A rival may openly prize hierarchy, performance or another purpose over this inquiry, or establish a further legitimate restriction. The argument then requires that changed ground to be assessed; it has not proved universal non-domination or every being's ethical standing. Invalid evidence or a misidentified authority scope would also revise the application.

[Participation analysis §11.2](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md) and [direction analysis §2](YITI-REN-DIRECTION-ARGUMENT.md#2-从具体活动取得条件不能由条件替它创造意义) already establish content consistency. [The support study §54](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#54-successful-support-and-continuing-commitments) already shows success changing a rationale without ending every undertaking. The narrower extension here concerns locally decisive understanding **before full independence**, and its appropriate influence amid continuing guidance. The worth of understanding in shared activity is a substantive premise, not a private preference or a completed ethical foundation. No formal or empirical result is added.

## 9. Renewal must include the selection of actual continuations

This follows [Ren's latest criterion](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-future-whole-generativity-criterion.md). This round tests a tempting shortcut in that comparison: more retained repair access does not establish a better distribution of future realization.

A whole appraisal must retain both, along with their relevance, likelihood and consequences. A changed law, stopping contract or installed regulator can change the comparison. Such changes require their own causal account; the mere existence of a controller that would suppress the competing route is insufficient.

### A positive candidate that includes selective closure

Renewal should be investigated as an organized capacity to realize and transform shared activity through its consequences. It cannot mean leaving every alternative open. A binding undertaking can make coordinated activity dependable; completion can release conditions for another formation. The prior participation and completion studies already supply these distinctions. Their application here prevents interpreting the whole-future criterion as maximal reversibility or an inventory of options.

The positive ethical proposal is that the actual formation of a shared future can itself be good: activity forms capacities and relations that were not previously possessed by independent owners, and their consequences can participate in revising what that activity becomes. This is a proposed quality of the unfolding, rather than a benefit routed through a privileged initiator. A fixed organization can damage this relation while reproducing itself, but dismantling it can also destroy the support, reliability and understanding that made the relation possible.

This proposal still carries a substantive constitutive judgment. Causal openness, self-modification and coordination do not prove it. A rival can value excellent realization of a fixed form over formation of different purposes. Showing that the rival closes alternatives does not yet refute its appraisal. The next ethical comparison must identify what those alternatives realize, what their dependable availability requires, and what must actually be given up to sustain them. It must justify a comparison with a real cost, rather than repeatedly relying on the existing no-loss dominance case.

### Formation of successors changes the relevant future

The [new primary empirical interface](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#selection-history-and-successor-formation) concerns selection during continued extraction. It supports tracing how an activity changes the successors that will constitute its future, rather than treating them as a fixed external population receiving an unchanged process. This is a causal interface; it does not identify fish size, yield or any other measured trait with the whole's ethical quality.

Taken together, the new native calculation and the empirical comparator sharpen the selected test. Preservation of access, selection among available transitions and formation of future participants are different relations within one unfolding. Their actual composition must be reconstructed before identifying degeneration or renewal. The integral's causal distributions must include those relations; a favorable reachable path is not the expected whole future.

### Result and next discriminating comparison

The established advance is a concrete restriction on the proposed whole-future criterion: **additional repair reachability does not guarantee improved realization probabilities**, even under the unchanged law and equal total energy. The positive candidate is consequently about organized, consequence-responsive realization and renewal, including selective commitment and completion. It is not a maximum-options principle.

The next normative test retains a real tradeoff: a dependable current undertaking versus a specified ability to change that undertaking in response to its actual consequences. The account must distinguish a binding relation that constitutes shared realization from one that insulates an incumbent arrangement from warranted correction. Their causal organization and the ethical significance of the particular realization both require defense. No universal preference for change, openness or persistence is assumed; no emotion-first foundation is reinstated. The unified goal remains active.

## 10. Present realization and the conditions of its continuation

6 October 2026. Named connection: **a real present sacrifice → preservation or transformation of shared future conditions → warranted comparison of the whole continuation**. This develops the preceding cost-bearing test under [Ren's governing correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-future-whole-generativity-criterion.md). The target is the future of the whole; maintaining a particular entity, practice or emotional state does not set its value.

### A specified comparison with a real loss

Consider a shared provisioning practice in which harvesting during a particular reproductive interval seriously impairs the next cycle. A temporary restriction protects that interval, but forgoes genuinely worthwhile present food, income or celebration. In the selected comparison, adequate present provisioning remains actually accessible; the sacrificed benefit is additional abundance. Supported future nourishment and activity would otherwise be substantially impaired. Enforcement, displaced extraction, effects on other living processes, uncertainty and accessible substitutes belong to the comparison. This is a conceptual case with explicit causal conditions, not an empirical report or a claim that these conditions hold for every fishery.

The causal difference is between using a process's current product and interrupting the relations that form its next realization. More harvested material cannot necessarily replace a missed reproductive interval. The future loss can therefore exceed the removal of an interchangeable stock. Conversely, an available replacement practice can make the ending of this one constructive. Causal non-substitutability concerns an identified continuation, not a metaphysical ban on replacement.

The [inherited reserve/installation example](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#38-adaptive-significance-and-the-good-of-the-bearer) already illustrates the structural possibility of sacrificing a present quantity to retain renewal. It does not supply this case's ethical ordering. Likewise, the preceding silverside experiment supplies a selection/successor interface, not a seasonal collapse threshold, a subsistence comparison or evidence about these stipulated alternatives.

### The affirmative judgment and its grounds

We defend the temporary restriction in the declared case. Its rationale is that a modest additional realization now would impair the shared conditions of substantial further nourishment and activity, while the restriction sustains those relations at a genuine but limited cost. The good proposed here is the temporally organized realization of living and activity together, including how it forms what follows. Neither the current harvester's eventual return nor the survival of the incumbent practice is required to ground the comparison.

Two different claims must be kept visible. **Temporal relevance:** if nourishment and shared activity are good through what they realize, their credible later realization is not ethically irrelevant merely because present participants can capture the current product. **Comparative priority in this case:** foregoing the specified additional abundance is warranted to avert the specified serious impairment of continuing provision. The first does not entail the second, equal weighting across dates, an infinite total or a duty of endless reproduction. The second is a substantive defended judgment, not a theorem derived from a biological threshold.

The explanation improves on an appeal to preserving options. Restraint participates in an actual reproductive phase through which the next realization forms; it does not merely keep an imagined choice on a list. It also differs from adding independently sovereign private interests: present benefit, future deprivation, reproduction, access and changing participants are differentiated aspects of the same investigated continuation. Calling them aspects neither cancels their differences nor settles their relative significance.

The priority has a bounded rationale: the loss concerns additional provision while the threatened relation supports provision itself, through a credible and non-substitutable transition. This gives a reason to favor restraint without making preservation lexically prior to every cost. It remains dependent on the proposed ethical significance of the realization and on the adequacy of the comparison. A critic who accepts all causal facts but contests this priority presents a normative disagreement the causal description alone cannot decide.

The primary comparisons in [Mengzi and Xunzi](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#seasonal-use-and-continuing-provision) help articulate timed use and renewal as one practice. Their political and ethical commitments remain their own; neither text proves this theory or supplies modern ecological measurements. Mengzi's same discussion also condemns withholding available provision amid starvation, constraining any interpretation of renewal as indefinite deprivation now.

### Result and the remaining derivation

This round establishes a more discriminating candidate and defends one comparison with an acknowledged loss. It adds no formal theorem, empirical observation or universal priority rule. The proposed reason is **sustaining an identified shared realization through the conditions of its further formation, at a cost that the particular comparison warrants**. The entity may change or end; the whole continuation remains the target.

The unresolved explanatory connection is the identification of this quality of realization as good and its authority in a contested comparison. That is not evidence of a fundamentally external ethical realm, nor is it discharged by including the word “whole.” The next discriminating case should hold the provisioning dependence fixed while varying who bears the restraint, their present access and the availability of a supported transition. If the account recommends preserving a future by disabling its actual participants, or preserving an incumbent despite a better supported succession, it must revise that judgment. This extends the derivation through distribution and transformation, instead of returning to emotional valence or entity persistence as the first ground.

## 11. What continuing provision actually realizes

6 October 2026. Named connection: **unchanged productive renewal → different distributions of usable provision → different qualities of the whole continuation**. The preceding argument justified a limited present cost under explicit conditions. This round tests whether its rationale mistakenly makes a person's significance depend on their contribution to future production.

### The matched comparison

Consider two feasible distributions under the same temporary harvest restriction. Both retain the same reproductive reserve, total consumption and subsequent production; relevant distribution costs and legitimate access to the means are matched. In one, already adequately nourished participants retain additional variety and celebration while another participant lacks the provision needed for ordinary living. In the other, usable provision sustains that participant's living, rest and relationships, while the others retain adequate nourishment but forgo that genuine enrichment. The comparison specifies actual access and realization, not merely an entitlement on paper or equal quantities delivered.

Crucially, the production process does not depend on the deprived participant's labor, knowledge or later contribution. Its recurrence therefore remains equal in the two cases. This is a conceptual comparison, not a nutritional intervention or an empirical finding. Matching production does not claim to match the whole history: the difference in what happens to the participants is precisely its target. Their distinctions locate different relations within shared activity; they do not introduce independently sovereign private standards.

We defend the second distribution. The proposed reason is that it sustains basic realization at the cost of limited enrichment elsewhere. Additional celebration remains a real good lost, rather than being relabelled worthless to manufacture dominance. The case thus goes beyond a no-loss comparison. Equal portions are not the criterion: different needs or conversion conditions can require unequal quantities to realize the same nourishment. No scalar measure or global law of diminishing returns is needed to state the particular ordering.

### Realization is not exhausted by its productive return

The defended significance concerns what provision actually enables in this continuation. Living, resting, relating and completing an activity occur within the whole's future from the decision point. They need not first improve a production index to enter that future. The same argument can include an ending whose supported completion produces nothing afterward; it does not require making every current form permanent.

The explanatory direction matters. Renewal can matter through the realizations it supports. Where renewal is justified only by enabling these realizations, explaining their entire value only by further renewal does not yet justify either allocation. A constitutive account could explain their significance together; it must still distinguish the actual histories being compared. This challenges an unexplained renewal-only interpretation; it does not refute every recursive or whole-level account, make every recurrent process worthless or give every occurrence equal ethical standing. A rival may identify a particular form of ongoing organization as itself valuable, but it must defend that identification and what it excludes.

The [earlier ethical account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md#explicit-premises-and-limited-deductions) already preserves standing without productive usefulness and tracks displaced burdens under explicit premises. The [relational account](RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md#what-makes-this-an-explanation-rather-than-a-circle) already allows terminal good. These are inherited commitments, not newly derived results. The advance here is testing them against the corrected whole-future criterion while productive renewal is fixed. It prevents an interpretation of that criterion that would erase the very realizations its future contains.

Two ethical commitments remain exposed: the affected realization has significance without productive return, and in this specified comparison it warrants sacrificing the limited enrichment. Descriptive inclusion alone establishes neither. A biological threshold can identify interrupted living; it does not automatically give that threshold overriding importance. Our judgment therefore supplies a bounded priority, not universal equal weighting or unconditional sufficiency.

### A rival within the whole perspective

A serious rival can acknowledge the deprivation and still give priority to a cultivated communal achievement because of its excellence. This whole-level perfectionist account need not be selfish or blind to consequences. Its disagreement concerns the quality and priority of the competing realizations. We cannot refute it merely by pointing out that the deprived participant also belongs to the whole.

In the declared case, however, the enrichment is limited ordinary abundance, not an independently established exceptional achievement or an irreplaceable completion. Its reality gives it weight; it does not explain why maintaining that additional abundance should take priority over enabling another actual life to proceed. The affirmative judgment is supported by this asymmetry in what the provisions realize, while remaining open to a defense of the rival's priority. If the foregone good is instead a substantial completion, another basic realization, or a binding responsibility, a new comparison is required. Relabelling such a loss “surplus” would evade the test.

The [bounded Sen comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#resources-and-the-realizations-they-enable) sharpens the distinction between means and what they enable, without supplying our priority rule. Choosing a richer descriptive target does not itself settle how to compare its realizations.

### Result and next central connection

The strongest retained candidate evaluates **the differentiated realizations sustained and transformed through shared conditions, including their distribution and completion**. Productive renewal is a consequential relation within that unfolding. It cannot be its sole ethical proxy if the defended comparison is accepted. This is a substantive application and refinement of inherited commitments, not a new theorem or a derivation of universal standing.

The next central connection is the actual point of dispute: **basic realization versus an independently identified exceptional shared achievement when they genuinely conflict**. The rival must preserve the former's actual loss, and our candidate must preserve the latter's actual value. A successful explanation of priority there would advance the whole-quality ground; an adequate contrary ordering would narrow the proposed priority. Adding another renewal mechanism or restating that all consequences are included would not resolve this normative connection.

## 12. Ordinary realization and the ground of excellence

6 October 2026. Named connection: **actual finite reciprocal expression → ethical significance within the whole → the scope of an excellence-based exclusion**. This round corrects the preceding selection of a priority conflict as the central grounding test, then examines a more discriminating explanation. It neither erases that conflict nor declares the original grounding complete.

### Priority is not the first significance

The [existing Nishida comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#11-realization-and-the-claim-of-necessary-agreement) already preserves an irreplaceable performance, indispensable care, no substitute and a genuine loss. Repeating it would not establish either good's first significance. An account that acknowledges both but ranks achievement higher contests priority. It does not thereby deny the significance of ordinary living. The [new bounded Rawls comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#excellence-and-its-practical-claims) likewise helps distinguish genuine excellence from claims to impose its costs, while retaining substantive premises of its own.

Consequently, the art/care conflict remains a downstream test of guidance and sacrifice. The immediate grounding rival is narrower: **ordinary, dependent or terminal realization has no ethical significance except through its contribution to an exceptionally excellent whole**. This is a proposed exclusive account, not a description of every perfectionist theory. Its assessment must concern the actual realization it excludes, not simply a different ranking of two conceded goods.

### A crossed constitutive comparison

Reconstruct four conceptual cases of musical expression. Two involve exceptional technical mastery; two involve a simple, competent expression. Within each pair, distinguish actual reciprocal uptake from separately directed execution. Identify uptake through what the participants understand and how their contributions respond to one another: an expressive variation is recognized and answered, and that answer participates in what follows. Mere synchronized output does not establish that relation.

| Independently specified execution | Reciprocal uptake occurs | Execution proceeds without that uptake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exceptional technical mastery | Mastery participates in an actual expressive exchange. | Comparable mastery occurs through separately directed execution. |
| Ordinary competent expression | A simple expression is actually understood and answered. | Comparable simple execution occurs without that expressive exchange. |

The activities are honestly understood and voluntarily undertaken; the content and surrounding relations are benign. The comparison does not manufacture a broken promise, deception or a secretly replaced performer. The finite ordinary exchange can be complete on its own terms, with no later productive advantage claimed as its justification. Costs, pleasure, engagement and later effects must still be examined; matching pleasant tone does not establish equal total welfare.

The matching is limited to independently specified technical features, not the whole achievement or its value. If reciprocal uptake constitutes a particular shared achievement, removing it changes that achievement. We retain that difference rather than claiming to preserve everything valuable while removing its constituent. These are philosophical comparison contracts, not four observed performances or assertions that perfect matching is empirically available. They reuse the earlier [participation identification discipline](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#64-our-identification-contract-and-the-live-rival).

### The affirmative explanation

We defend a positive ethical significance of actual reciprocal expression in the ordinary case. What is realized is an exchange in which an expression receives understanding and a response that helps form the activity. This is a real relation within the unfolding, not a privately owned preference subsequently added to a social total. It need not produce a more accomplished performance later to have this point now.

The comparison also tests the explanation offered for exceptional shared expression. If reciprocal understanding partly explains its value, technical mastery is not the entire explanation. The ordinary case realizes an independently identified instance of that relation too. Explaining both through actual reciprocal expression, while allowing further mastery to change the kind or degree of achievement, supplies a defeasible reason to recognize ordinary significance. Calling the ordinary activity incomplete solely because it does not reach the exceptional standard would substitute a different task for the simple expression actually completed.

This argument does not infer value from similarity alone. It relies on the defended appraisal that understanding and responsive expression are good-making here, and tests the scope of that explanation. Nor does it establish equal value, a separately additive welfare component, superiority to every nonreciprocal activity, or priority over a competing good. Separate execution, listening and solitary expression can have their own goods. The comparative claim requires accounting for what each change actually preserves and loses.

### The strongest emergence rival

A rival can hold that the valuable relation is a conjunction of uptake with a higher expressive integration found only in the exceptional case. This is a genuine constitutive alternative, not a freely toggled predicate. The exceptional contrast alone cannot exclude it: uptake could matter only under that further condition.

A threshold may identify a newly achieved integration, content or capacity. Its existence can explain an additional good without establishing that all ordinary significance is absent below it. Conversely, such an enabling condition is not illegitimate merely because it introduces a threshold. The rival must specify what it constitutes and explain why that feature conditions the value at issue. Prestige, a rank or the fact that one performance is greater does not by itself supply this explanation.

Continuity cannot settle the disagreement either. Even an assumed continuous scalar valuation can be zero below a threshold and positive above it. That mathematical observation defeats a shortcut from continuous variation to positive value everywhere; it neither justifies a threshold nor refutes the fuller relational proposal. No continuity or global monotonicity premise is added to the foundation.

The argument would narrow if the ordinary activity lacked genuine uptake, if its context transformed that relation's significance, or if an independently characterized higher integration convincingly explained why uptake matters only there. If “excellence” instead includes the ordinary activity's complete realization of its appropriate expressive relation, it already admits the minimum significance at issue and ceases to be this exclusive rival. Renaming the case cannot settle which account is correct.

### Result and active grounding test

The advance is an explanatory comparison: **exceptional excellence may add or reorganize goods without having been shown to create every good that an activity realizes**. The affirmative candidate recognizes finite ordinary co-realization through its actual relational character. This is stronger than appealing to later productivity or simply asserting that every included occurrence counts equally, but remains a defended, defeasible appraisal rather than a necessity proof.

The active next test is the rival's proposed enabling condition: identify a concrete higher integration and determine whether it adds a good or is genuinely required for the ordinary relation's significance. This addresses the first-value explanation directly. Priority between genuinely conflicting goods remains necessary for fuller guidance, but is not the gate through which their significance must first pass. No new empirical result, formal theorem or completed universal ethical authority is claimed.

## 13. Meaningful uptake and reflective participation in shaping activity

6 October 2026. Named connection: **enacted reciprocal understanding → reflection on jointly shaping activity → the alleged dependence of the first relation's value on the second**. This makes one higher-integration rival concrete. It is a local test of an ethical quality within the whole, not a proposal that every valuable continuation must exhibit human joint agency.

### Specify the proposed condition

Three targets must be distinguished without making them independent substances:

| Target | What would identify it | What does not identify it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaningful reciprocal uptake | An expressive contribution is understood and answered in a way that helps form the continuation; variations and misunderstandings receive context-sensitive responses. | Synchronization, repeated matching output or a bare causal effect on another process. |
| Occurrent reflection on jointly shaping activity | Participants presently recognize, endorse or reconsider the activity as something they are making together. These acts need separate identification. | Mere silence, absorbed performance, inability to verbalize or a generic awareness that another person is present. |
| Capacity for shared reflection on shaping the activity | The participants can make their joint activity an object of mutual consideration and understand possible revisions of its purposes and roles. This cognitive ability does not itself establish governing authority. | The absence of reflection on one occasion, or an isolated success or failure on a verbal report. |

Meaningful uptake can itself involve understanding another's intended meaning. The comparison therefore does not identify its first row with an absence of all representations of other minds. Recognizing joint participation in shaping activity also differs from actually having legitimate power to govern the undertaking. The latter depends on the formed relationship and its history, not just on thinking “we.”

### What the concrete comparison can support

Begin with reflectively capable participants in a simple exchange. One phrase receives an intelligible answer; that answer changes the next phrase; a misunderstanding is repaired. The exchange can be assessed in respect of this enacted understanding. Compare its occurrence without the specified occurrent reflection on jointly shaping activity with a continuation in which the participants additionally discuss what they have made, articulate their contributions and reconsider its purpose. This absence is a conceptual hypothesis; neither silence, reported absorption nor a lack of foregrounding establishes its realization in observed people.

Our affirmative appraisal locates a good already in the actual reception and response, under the benign and voluntary conditions declared in §12. Reflective appropriation may realize further goods: a jointly articulated purpose, a more explicit understanding of contributions or a supported way to revise an undertaking. Their formation does not itself explain why meaningful reception before that formation would have had no significance. Conversely, calling the exchange complete does not prove its value; the positive appraisal of this relation remains substantive.

This comparison addresses a requirement of **occurrent** reflective participation in shaping activity. It does not establish that the exchange could occur in beings lacking the relevant reflective **capacity**. The stronger contrast remains a possibility to investigate: meaningful uptake with that capacity absent, versus meaningful uptake with it present. No infant, animal or clinical population is asserted to instantiate either side on the basis of appearance, speech or a recognition task. If the higher capacity is actually necessary for the specified uptake, the proposed contrast fails as a description before deciding any ethical question.

### Assess the value-dependence claim directly

The strongest rival accepts meaningful uptake but locates the relevant good exclusively in joint activity shaped through shared reflection. It can say that reflection makes the activity an undertaking shaped by its participants, rather than mistakenly calling ordinary participants mechanically coupled. This identifies a more demanding kind of relation. Its exclusive ethical force still requires explaining why enacted understanding has no point of its own within the unfolding.

The positive explanation distinguishes what each relation realizes. Actual expression is received and answered in the first; the activity can additionally become an object of shared understanding and governance in the second. We favor recognizing the former's finite significance without treating the latter as its only possible source. That is a defeasible constitutive judgment, not an inference from mere occurrence and not a proof against every reflective-capacity account. It establishes neither an extra welfare quantity nor the superiority of reciprocal activity to solitary activity.

Defining a technical category such as joint agency to require reflective participation in shaping activity would not settle the value of activity outside that category. Nor does a fact about timing settle value: later reflection does not produce the earlier causal history, but it can change the later relationships and whole-history significance of that exchange. The candidate allows this contextual transformation without assuming that later recognition either creates all earlier value or is always ethically redundant.

### Evidence and genealogy constrain the conclusion

The [targeted source recheck](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#reflection-and-meaningful-uptake) does not establish the stronger dissociation. The inherited haptic-interaction experiment used adults instructed to cooperate; it did not independently identify or remove the proposed reflective capacity. Reported awareness of another's presence is a different target from reflection on jointly shaping activity. An enactive account of jointly transformed meaning also cannot be used as evidence of absent reflection merely because its explanation emphasizes interaction.

The [existing Gilbert comparison](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-SOURCES.md#51-successful-support-and-continuing-commitments) already distinguishes shared commitment, changing preferences, directed claims and justified exercise. It can help identify an additional formed relation without making its occurrence a proof of moral goodness. The inherited [participation and evidence audit](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#45-relational-benefit-and-the-evidence-for-it) likewise prevents identifying technical task success with an independently established welfare increment. These results retain their original scopes.

### Result and remaining connection

The higher-integration objection now has an exact burden: **explain why meaningful reception has ethical significance only within reflective participation in shaping activity**, rather than merely identifying a further kind of participation in shaping activity or enabling additional goods. Reflection might be proposed as a necessary enabling context; the rival need not accept reception as an independently valuable bearer. Our affirmative account favors distinguishing their contributions and challenges the need for occurrent reflection; it has not empirically or conceptually closed the stronger capacity question. That limitation is preserved rather than replaced with an easier successful test.

The next foundational comparison should address this claimed value-dependence, including the possibility that a person's understood expression matters without being reflectively appropriated as a shared project. Neither reciprocity, exceptional skill nor reflective participation in shaping activity becomes a universal admission test for ethical concern. The whole-future candidate retains dependent and terminal realizations outside this local example. Universal ethical identification and practical authority remain unfinished; no new formal or empirical result is claimed.

## 14. Understanding a reason and grounding its significance

6 October 2026. Named connection: **meaningful reception → a changed continuation → the ethical ground of that change**. The previous round made progress by distinguishing uptake, present reflection and reflective capacity. This continuation repairs an inference in the proposed test: an appropriate response that uses understanding does not, on that evidence alone, establish a further good of being understood. It returns the ethical dependency to the whole-history account rather than treating a threshold of reflective participation as the foundation's next mandatory gate.

### The stopping case is inherited, and its scope matters

The existing [仁 reconstruction, §§11.4–11.6](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md) already describes a constructed public-speaking preparation in which a participant's understood unwillingness changes the activity: the public event can be cancelled and private practice stopped. It identifies actual relief, effective choice and appropriately bounded support as positive features. It also allows reduced contact and the ending of relationships. These are inherited conceptual claims, not a new observed case or a new discovery that cessation can be constructive.

Consider the narrower dependency in that example. Where participation is legitimately optional, an accurately understood request to stop can guide a response that ends the activity. The resulting continuation can realize the significance of that optionality. No longer conversation or more intensive cooperation is required merely to keep the relation alive. Other affected lives, reliance, outstanding responsibilities and actual consequences remain within the assessed history.

This gives a positive conditional explanation: **understanding can participate in realizing a good through the ending it enables**. It does not establish that reception contributes an additional good beyond the appropriate realization of that request. Neither does it establish that reflection on jointly shaping activity was absent: the participants might reflect together on ending, and they retain whatever relevant capacities they had. The earlier [relational-benefit audit](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#45-relational-benefit-and-the-evidence-for-it) already requires distinguishing an actual relation, its proposed benefit and evidence for that benefit.

### Locate the ethical work of the context

The descriptions “benign” and “voluntary” in §§12–13 must not silently discharge this dependency. They can have different roles:

| Role in the argument | What it supplies | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excluding deception, coercion or specified damaging consequences | A restricted comparison in which those objections are absent. | Absence of those objections does not by itself prove a positive good of ordinary expression. |
| Establishing that continued participation is legitimately optional | A substantive ground relevant to whether a request to stop should change the activity. | Semantic uptake alone does not establish that permission or its scope. |
| Identifying the actual response and its consequences | How an understood expression helps produce stopping, continued support or another continuation. | Successful implementation does not independently justify its aim or prove an additional relational benefit. |

These are explanatory roles within one history, not fundamentally separate normative and causal worlds. An utterance, its understanding, an undertaking and its eventual ending can mutually shape what the relation is. The table distinguishes what a particular inference has explained; it does not assume ethical value decomposes into additive components.

The withdrawal comparison therefore cannot be transferred unchanged into the ordinary-expression argument. In the latter, a positive good-making role for reciprocal expression is defended. In the former, legitimate optionality can already explain why the response should enable an ending. Crediting that ground to reception would move the normative premise rather than derive it.

### A concrete contrast identifies the role, without supplying its ultimate warrant

Compare two continuations of the same stipulated optional activity. In both, the request to stop is accurately understood. In one, it changes the stopping condition and accessible arrangements so that departure can occur without the specified retaliatory burden. In the other, the understanding is used to identify and restrict available exits so that participation continues. The contrast concerns actual organization and consequences, not guesses about an inner motive or a relabeling of the same process.

Given the legitimate optionality and consequence judgments just declared, the account favors the first continuation. What explains the difference is how understanding participates in the actual exercise or obstruction of that optionality. Accuracy of understanding is present in both; it cannot alone explain the ethical contrast. This does not refute the restricted benign case of §12, because the obstructive continuation fails that restriction. It shows why the restriction and the relevant grounds must remain visible when extending the account.

Nor does the example establish that every request should control what happens. The inherited [commitment account](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-SOURCES.md#51-successful-support-and-continuing-commitments) distinguishes adaptive fulfillment, authorized unilateral revision and changes requiring amendment. The [release audit](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#55-reasons-to-revise-and-powers-to-release) distinguishes reasons to change an undertaking, power to change it, actual release and justified nonperformance. A different legitimate responsibility can change the appraisal; being caused to participate is not, by itself, proof of a legitimate responsibility. The legitimacy of those formed relations remains a substantive explanation to pursue.

### What the reflection objection can now test

Calling an ending “joint participation in shaping activity” cannot settle the question. If participation in shaping activity means only that both participants causally shape a continuation, it no longer names the more demanding reflective condition proposed in §13. If it means independently identified shared reflection or joint governance, the case needs evidence for that condition and for its proposed ethical role. Cognitive capacity, actual reflection and legitimate decision power remain distinct.

Two accounts can accept the appropriate ending while disagreeing about reception's additional value: one recognizes a further relational good in being understood; another explains the favorable result through optional participation and its fulfillment. The stopping example, as presently specified, does not discriminate those explanations. An actual difference in reflective capacity has also not been demonstrated. These are limitations of this comparison, not proof that either account must fail or that ethical significance lies outside generativity.

The affirmative whole-process candidate survives in a more exact form: a formation can realize good by becoming responsive to conditions that warrant its own revision or ending. The relevant good need not return to the initiating participant or preserve an incumbent relation. Its warrant still has to be explained through the affected realization and history; “responsive,” “voluntary” or “constructive” cannot be used as substitutes for that explanation.

### Revised derivation and next bridge

The reflection study is retained as a scoped test of how a proposed good is realized and described. It does not currently supply a derivation of that good's first ethical significance. This corrects the selection of a foundation gate without withdrawing the defended ordinary-expression appraisal or declaring the reflection rival settled.

The next substantive dependency is **how the formed conditions of participation, dependence and possible departure warrant a boundary of control within the whole continuation**. Existing commitment results already leave this warrant supplied; another generic refusal story or controller showing accurate understanding would not discharge it. The next comparison must trace and assess that actual ground, preserving any genuine reason for continuation alongside the good enabled by ending. It must begin from the formed relationship and its consequences, not pre-authorized private preferences, a sovereign project, or the assumption that every dependency creates a right to control.

This is a dependency correction with a retained conditional argument, not a new empirical result, formal theorem or proof of universal necessity. A substantive account showing that the optionality was misidentified, the ending failed to realize the claimed good, or the alleged relational contribution was fully explained by other grounds would revise the corresponding claim. The original whole-process grounding objective remains active.

## 15. Indispensable support and the control of alternatives

6 October 2026. Named connection: **formed dependence → a claim of necessary coordination → the warrant for maintaining the conditions of that dependence**. The preceding round was progress: §14 exposed the ethical work supplied by legitimate optionality. This continuation tests a particular warrant for control without assuming that every participant already has a right to leave. Its narrower contribution is to include the formation of available alternatives inside the justification being assessed.

### Reconstruct the dependence and the disputed claim

A provisioning arrangement has formed through equipment, skills, access rules, contributions and repeated use. At the reached condition, several lives depend on its continued service. One operator currently coordinates the only functioning route. These roles and dependencies belong to that history; the account does not start with independent owners subsequently entering an exchange.

The operator offers a particular justification for a restriction: continued provision requires its coordination, so it must prevent a transition to another arrangement. Distinguish three propositions:

1. The specified support presently depends on this operator's activity.
2. The operator's immediate coordination is warranted in the reached situation.
3. The operator is warranted in maintaining the exclusion that keeps it indispensable.

The first describes a dependency. With further ethical and practical grounds, it can support the second. It does not alone establish the third. A coherent desire to remain indispensable does not repair an inadequate objective justification for doing so. Conversely, discovering that earlier exclusions produced the dependency does not by itself defeat current coordination or establish that those exclusions were wrong.

### Compare maintaining support with maintaining exclusivity

Consider a specified alternative whose resources, competence, timing, interoperability and effects on those who remain have been examined. It offers a transition preserving the relevant provision while retiring the challenged exclusion. Those involved remain dependent on provision; the comparison does not require anyone to become self-sufficient. Real construction costs, risks, accrued reliance, continuing undertakings and any lost shared activity remain part of the comparison.

| Stage | Continuation retaining exclusion | Continuation forming the alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reached condition | The incumbent operates the only functioning route. | The same incumbent continues operating it. |
| Transition interval | The rule prevents the identified alternative from being established. | The identified work establishes and qualifies the alternative while current provision continues. |
| Later continuation | Incumbent coordination remains indispensable within that maintained arrangement. | The successor supplies the specified provision; incumbent coordination ceases to be necessary in that respect. |

This is a conceptual comparison, not a field finding or a claim that such a transition is generally available. Qualifying a successor must cover the relevant continuation, not just one successful delivery or an endpoint resource total. Matching this provision also does not establish equality of every good, claim or consequence.

The exact conditional result is this: **if a supported transition preserves the service without the challenged exclusion, indispensability under that exclusion cannot establish that the exclusion is necessary for that service.** The operator's current dependence claim may remain true during the transition. Its asserted scope across the relevant future is what changes.

The mistaken inference freezes the contested rule while considering alternatives, then uses the resulting absence of alternatives to justify freezing the rule. Assessing whether the transition is causally implementable does not assume that anybody may unilaterally perform it. Equally, requiring the transition to have the very permission whose refusal is under assessment would allow that refusal to settle its own warrant. Material feasibility, independently justified constraints and the disputed veto must be distinguished within the same actual situation.

### What the result warrants, and what it leaves open

The correction removes a particular necessity-based argument for the restriction. It does not establish a universal preference for the least control, an automatic duty to authorize the transition, or a power to override the operator. A particular joint undertaking, legitimate reliance, a valuable common practice or transition burdens can provide additional grounds; their existence and legitimacy require assessment. An alternative can preserve service while failing to fulfill a promised joint performance. The [existing commitment analysis](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#54-successful-support-and-continuing-commitments) already preserves that difference.

Support in the reached situation remains a live ethical question. Even a wrongly produced dependency can make abrupt abandonment harmful. Ending an exclusion and stopping service are different actions. The [earlier need-and-repair comparison](#7-continuation-the-origin-of-a-need-and-the-value-of-its-repair) already prevents a favorable remedial response from retrospectively justifying the history that made it needed. The present extension applies that structure prospectively: continuing help cannot, by itself, certify an ongoing policy that prevents another support route from forming.

The affirmative whole-future candidate is consequently more specific. Coordinating power may be justified by what it enables throughout the affected continuation, including the formation of successors and changed relations. A support-based rationale must remain answerable to those consequences as they change. That is an objective constraint on this offered rationale, not a demand that every institution dissolve or that every affected preference prevail.

### A real rival: coordination that legitimately excludes alternatives

Created dependence can be part of worthwhile coordination. In a hypothetical jointly financed flood-control network, incompatible channels could undermine protection; redundant pumps might have been retired to support reliable maintenance; continuing contributions could reflect a legitimate undertaking. Restricting the incompatible channels might preserve the common provision. Their mere physical possibility would not make the restricted future worse.

This rival defeats the stronger rule that dependence produced by exclusion is necessarily wrongful. It also shows why the proposed successor must preserve the relevant organization, not only a departing participant's supply. Unified scheduling, safety, uncertainty, common costs and actual commitments can defeat the transition specification or independently support the restriction. No real network is diagnosed by this invented comparison.

The inherited [Xunzi comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#provision-boundaries-and-continued-indispensability) supplies a positive conceptual counterpart: differentiated bounds can participate in sustaining desire and provision together. It does not make every historically formed hierarchy justified. The account must explain which boundaries enable the shared continuation and which merely protect the present arrangement's control; neither “division” nor “freedom” settles that question by its name.

### The dependency advanced and the one still open

The genealogy is explicit. Section 7 already distinguishes entrance and reached-state comparisons. [Section 8](#8-continuation-formed-understanding-can-correct-its-source) and the support audit §54 already show a means rationale changing while dependence or commitments persist. The [release audit §55](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#55-reasons-to-revise-and-powers-to-release) separates reasons, powers and actual release. The present application examines a narrower feedback: **the challenged restriction helps maintain the very necessity offered to justify it**. It is not a new logical principle or the first recognition of non-domination.

This advances §14's question by testing an actual proposed source of control, without treating optionality as already settled. The argument can reject that source's overextended inference while leaving the remaining boundary contested. The underlying worth of the specified provision, the initial warrant for coordination and the legitimacy of any surviving claim remain substantive dependencies. Locating them within the generative history does not prove them irreducible additions to it.

The next central test is the positive one exposed by the coordination rival: **what makes an organizing boundary a justified condition of shared realization when it imposes a genuine burden or closes a genuinely worthwhile alternative?** The existing unequal-allocation and performance/commitment comparisons preserve such losses; a costless substitute cannot stand in for that question. The analysis must trace why the protected realization and the authority claimed for it have their stated significance, rather than infer either from technical necessity or stable recurrence. No new theorem, empirical result or universal ethical grounding is claimed; the original goal remains active.

## 16. Grounding a comparison of whole continuations

6 October 2026. Named connection: **formed appreciative realization → comparative quality of the whole continuation**. The preceding round made progress by testing a particular necessity-based rationale for control. Its proposed maintenance-and-burden sequel, however, would again begin with worthwhile provision already granted. This continuation closes that derivative route at its existing scope and removes an unnecessary restriction on the direct ethical question.

### What the maintenance route already establishes

The [shared-world contribution analysis](SHARED-WORLD-GROUNDING.md#continuation-broad-conditions-and-real-contribution) already distinguishes worthwhile provision, actual contribution, non-pivotal joint effectiveness and further grounds such as fairness or an undertaking. The allocation and commitment studies preserve real burdens and separate reasons from authority. Necessity of some maintenance neither allocates each share nor identifies who may compel it. Receiving an unrequested benefit does not by itself settle either question; lack of unanimous consent also does not automatically defeat every common arrangement.

Even the direction of responsibility needs explanation. Forming reliance through support may supply a reason for its provider to maintain service or arrange a transition, rather than a debt from every beneficiary. That remains a normative assessment of the formed history, not a conclusion from dependence alone. Another maintenance example would investigate these downstream relations while continuing to supply the ethical worth whose grounding is sought.

### A whole comparison need not begin with an isolated welfare increment

The earlier distinction between priority and first significance remains sound: a conflict analysis that already grants the worth of both alternatives cannot, by that assumption, establish their worth. It does not follow that every comparative ethical argument must wait until separate positive contributions to individual welfare have been proved.

A direct, warranted comparison of complete continuations can itself be the ethical claim under investigation. This applies the existing whole-future and partial-order framework; it introduces no new aggregation principle. It changes the present research gate, while preserving the stronger participant-benefit question in [realization §9](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#9-continuation-6-october-2026-actual-engagement-and-the-grounds-of-its-value). An appraisal of the whole must still retain the differentiated experiences, relationships and consequences of those within it. Calling it whole does not excuse an unexplained sacrifice.

The candidate can therefore investigate appreciative realization as a quality of the unfolding without first isolating an extra welfare quantity independent of pleasure, concern and every other constituent. Whether an additional participant benefit exists remains its own open question; changing the target does not answer it.

### Use the actual formation comparison

Return to the inherited [concern-formation comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#concern-formation-and-comparative-benefit). In N, a participant understands and contributes to an actual expressive encounter instrumentally, without intrinsically appreciating that encounter. In R, the actual encounter comes to be intrinsically appreciated through the activity. In F, that concern forms but the relevant encounter fails. These histories differ in actual relations; F is not an inevitable adverse phase through which R passes.

The direct affirmative proposal concerns R compared with N. Its proposed good is the way the actual encounter becomes part of the lived continuation: what is made and answered together becomes intrinsically welcomed in its doing. The activity forms its participants' concern as well as realizing its object. A prior private demand need not be supplied as the source of that point, and the episode need not produce a further performance to matter.

We defend this as a possible improvement of a whole continuation, with a reasoned appraisal of that lived relation. We do not infer it merely from formation, more intentional content or subsequent endorsement. N need not be defective for R to be better. Conversely, an actual change in pleasure, displaced appreciation, ease, relationships or later effects must be included. Matching sensory pleasantness is not a license to declare all pleasure equal, and the candidate need not establish an additional nonhedonic increment in order to defend its whole comparison.

### A coherent rival remains

“Fulfillment is good once concern exists” can mean only that R is better than F. It need not mean that R is better than N. The existing inference restriction already establishes that distinction. A contextual or saturation account can recognize the completed appreciative relation while treating N and R as equally good, or declining to order them, and still favor R over F. Such an account might regard both N and R as complete worthwhile ways of living the encounter, without every new appreciation adding to the whole.

This is a substantive rival to develop and assess, not a full theory certified merely by writing R ∼ N ≻ F. It must explain why the actual appreciative formation makes no further difference in this specified history. Our affirmative account must explain why it does. Neither an unexplained flat valuation nor an assumed strict addition resolves the dispute. “Harmless” excludes identified adverse effects; it does not by itself establish a monotonic rule for adding goods to a whole.

Even granting a favorable aspect within R does not automatically decide R against N. Its contribution can depend on the organized context, and a new concern can reorganize rather than simply augment that context. The [Dietz recheck](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#whole-comparison-and-the-acquisition-rival) identifies the additional welfare assumptions under which his pressure on acquisition-neutral accounts operates. Those assumptions cannot silently become our whole-appraisal rule.

### No contradiction from changing the comparison point

An earlier neutrality between acquiring and not acquiring appreciation can coexist with a later reason to fulfill or preserve an appreciation that has formed. The later alternatives can include frustration, extinguishing an established engagement, regret or lost relationships. Never forming the concern and subsequently removing it are different histories. A time-inconsistency objection must preserve the histories, information and ethical question, not merely repeat a word such as indifference.

Likewise, history value, a reason to bring about a history and permission for an intervention remain distinct. Even an affirmative R-over-N judgment does not establish that a particular teaching or formation process should be imposed. Actual feasibility, uncertainty, other effects and legitimate powers require their own justification. This retains their place downstream without letting them substitute for the direct value question.

### Revised central connection

The positive candidate is now stated at the governing level: **a whole continuation can be better through the formation and actual living of informed appreciative engagement, without first containing a frustrated demand or needing a separately additive welfare contribution**. This is a defended constitutive proposal, not a completed derivation from minimal causation or a universal rule favoring every new concern.

The next discriminating work is to assess this actual R-versus-N comparison under an independently specified account of whole quality. The contextual rival remains live, and the original formation comparison remains conceptual rather than an observed matched experiment. The investigation must not replace this question with R versus F, another means-to-an-assumed-good argument, or a compulsory-isolated-welfare test. The correction removes a misplaced research gate; it does not establish the strict ordering by definition. Universal practical authority and ethical necessity remain unfinished, within the unchanged whole-process objective.

## 17. Completion and the claim of further enrichment

6 October 2026. Named connection: **actual appreciative realization → its goodness → possible comparative enrichment**. The previous round made progress by restoring a direct whole-history comparison. This continuation supplies a concrete completion rival and determines which part of the candidate it actually contests. It does not turn the ability to improve every already worthwhile continuation into a condition for unified grounding.

### Give the rival a positive account

The [bounded Epicurus comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#complete-realization-and-variation) supplies a substantive account of completion. The Principal Doctrines distinguish variation of bodily pleasure after need-pain ends from mental completion through understanding limits and overcoming disturbance. The Letter to Menoeceus describes a fulfilled life with no further search for something missing, while retaining positive pleasure, practical understanding and distinctions between a good and what should be chosen. Friendship remains prominent in the Doctrines. This is an account with content, not a freely assigned zero increment.

Its use here is limited. A limit on bodily pleasure alone cannot establish complete mental or whole-history goodness. The source's pleasure-centered ethical commitments do not become the generative foundation's premises, and its treatment of pain does not replace Ren's whole-future criterion. Nor do these passages establish that actual relationships can be replaced without loss by matching experiences. Our application concerns a possible ceiling on further enrichment, with these questions kept distinct.

### Reconstruct N without making it defective

In the inherited musical comparison, N can be a calm, informed and attentive participation in a genuinely enjoyed musical activity. The participant understands the collaboration and fulfills a worthwhile instrumental purpose without intrinsically appreciating the collaboration itself. In R, appreciation of that actual collaboration also forms. This changes how the activity is lived. It does not reveal that N was secretly inattentive, alienated, joyless or based on a mistake.

A completion account can regard N and R as two fulfilled ways of living the encounter. Additional intrinsic welcome can change their character without making R strictly better. F remains different: a concern actually forms and its encounter fails. The rival can therefore defend R ∼ N while favoring R over F. This is our application of a completion account, not a historical claim that Epicurus examined these cases or that his texts uniquely determine their ordering.

The rival owes a defense of its ceiling. Completing the technical task, feeling calm and reaching the limit of the relevant ethical quality are three different claims. Neither successful performance nor a report of contentment establishes the last. If only one dimension is complete, further dimensions can still change whole quality. If all relevant dimensions are claimed complete, their coverage and exclusivity require argument.

### What can favor the appreciative continuation?

The affirmative candidate concerns an actual reorganization: what is being made together comes to be welcomed in its doing. In a specified case this may deepen the lived relation without displacing calm attention, another appreciation or a worthwhile activity. We retain that direct positive appraisal as a candidate explanation of strict improvement. We do not count a new concern merely because it has acquired a name, and we do not require N to be bad for R to be better.

The proposed route through fitting appreciation needs particular care. Factual accuracy establishes that the welcomed encounter really occurs and is understood in the relevant respect. It does not establish that welcoming it is ethically fitting or that the resulting whole is better. Accurate appreciation can also concern a cruel undertaking. If “fitting” already means ethically appropriate to a worthwhile object, its normative content is part of the ground requiring explanation. The inherited [attitude analysis](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#10-concerns-object-and-the-grounds-of-its-correction) already preserves this problem; factual correspondence cannot silently discharge it.

Three outcomes consequently remain possible: the new orientation improves the whole continuation, changes it into an equally worthwhile form, or worsens it through its actual organization and consequences. These are conclusions to defend about the histories, not labels that identify themselves. The absence of a specified loss does not automatically convert variation into strict improvement. Conversely, the mere availability of a completion account does not establish that its ceiling has been reached in this case.

### The rival's scope changes its role in grounding

The strongest version of this rival grants that R realizes genuine good. It disputes the claim that R improves on an already worthwhile N. It therefore does not, by that dispute alone, deny the first goodness of appreciative realization. Requiring its defeat before recognizing that good would impose a further growth requirement that §16's can-improve candidate does not contain.

The universal claim that every harmless new fulfilled appreciation strictly improves the whole faces this substantive completion alternative. Our narrower claim that appreciative formation can improve a whole continuation remains compatible with some completed cases admitting variation without increase. Neither claim is established simply by calling activity generative. The fact that an opponent also recognizes good is likewise not a proof of its identification or universal necessity.

This locates the current agreement and disagreement precisely. Actual completion can matter without endless further production; plural complete forms can remain possible. Comparative enrichment requires a further whole-history judgment. The different explanations of why the fulfilled activity is good remain to be assessed. A pleasure-based explanation, a jointly constituted relational explanation and a compatible combination cannot be eliminated merely because they differ over some increments.

### What changes in the candidate

The unified account must distinguish **realizing good**, **sustaining good** and **making a continuation better than a particular alternative**. These need not be three independent quantities or events. One activity can instantiate several of these descriptions, and one worthy continuation can give way to another without a strict ethical ascent.

The new primary comparator makes the former schematic saturation rival concrete and removes its mistaken use as a mandatory first-goodness obstacle. It leaves the direct R-versus-N judgment contested where both explanations apply. The next central task is to compare their proposed good-making grounds in the same complete activity, without substituting factual accuracy for ethical fitting or requiring an extra increment beyond every good already conceded. The inherited malicious-appreciation and real-engagement comparisons constrain that task; repeating them without a new explanatory difference would not advance it.

No new empirical result or formal theorem is claimed. The foundation retains differentiated losses, terminal realization and the whole future as its target. Neither an ethic of endless growth nor a universal ceiling is adopted. Ethical identification and practical authority remain open within the unchanged objective.

## 18. Fulfilled activity and the future it forms

6 October 2026. Named connection: **organized activity → realized quality → ethical significance within the whole continuation**. The question is whether the quality of an activity can supply the proposed first ethical identification. Ren's [whole-future correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-future-whole-generativity-criterion.md) remains controlling: neither emotional valence nor an entity's successful continuation sets the governing criterion. The comparison below is philosophical, not a new empirical result or formal theorem.

### A positive quality with a specified scope

The [bounded Dewey reading](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#fulfilled-experience-and-whole-ethical-appraisal) supplies an account of experienced completion through related doing and undergoing. It distinguishes this quality from efficient execution and expressly allows its occurrence in activity harmful to the world. We retain both claims together.

Our reconstruction concerns an actual expressive exchange. What is heard changes what is offered next; what is offered changes subsequent reception. The activity reaches a completion constituted through those relations. Describing it as a finished output with a separate approval added afterward would omit part of the proposed phenomenon. Yet identifying this phenomenon does not establish that everything it forms or sustains is good. A rival can preserve its lived quality while contesting its ethical contribution in the actual continuation. The target is therefore specified without building whole ethical approval into the word fulfilled.

This is a useful positive identification, but not a new proof of first goodness. The inherited hostile-appreciation case already prevents approval from certifying its object. The additional source clarifies what a substantive account of fulfillment can preserve even when ethical certification fails. It does not establish that every such realization contributes an invariant positive amount, or that a favorable aspect necessarily improves the whole.

### Completion does not close the consequences

The boundary that identifies a completed encounter and the boundary needed to assess its consequences answer different questions. A musical exchange can finish while its demands on others, its material consequences and the relationships it has changed continue. Its completion neither erases those relations nor obliges the encounter itself to continue producing value indefinitely.

The whole future cannot simply be modeled as one larger consummating experience. Our account has not established a single experiencer, governing purpose or final fulfillment for the whole. Different activities can finish, renew or obstruct one another within the same unfolding. Local experiential organization explains one differentiated formation; extending its scope requires further explanation. This is a limit of that proposed extension, not a demonstration that the nondual foundation fails.

### Realization and its sustaining organization

The operational route has a related limit. An organization can have actual conditions of functioning without a reward supplied by an analyst. But one organization can function by restricting another activity's functioning. The common history contains both directions. A broader description cannot inherit either organization's standard as the whole's ethical criterion merely by including more causal relations.

An explanatory test distinguishes the good attributed to an expressive relation from the arrangement maintaining it. Compare reciprocal maintenance with competent asymmetric support while retaining the actual reciprocal expression, informed appreciation, relevant future opportunities and identified burdens. This is a proposed conceptual comparison, whose feasibility must be established; it is not a reported matched experiment. If the same good remains, reciprocal maintenance is not necessary to that good's identity. If the ethical quality changes, the account must identify why: changed control, reliability, obligations or burdens may be real differences, rather than a bare difference in feedback topology.

The inherited terminal and nonproductive cases already constrain renewal's necessity. The present test adds an explanatory discriminator, not another claimed discovery that endings or asymmetric care can matter. Mutual support can be indispensable in a particular history without being the exhaustive meaning of goodness.

### The candidate and its live test

The positive candidate is **the quality of differentiated realization together with the conditions and further realizations it forms, obstructs or transforms throughout the continuation**. Expression, reception, support and evaluation are related formations within this process. Its appraisal cannot be certified by a participant's satisfaction, an incumbent's persistence or an episode's internal completion. None of those real features needs to be denied to assess the whole.

The contextual dispute is inherited: [realization §10](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#10-concerns-object-and-the-grounds-of-its-correction) already distinguishes retained favorable enjoyment from an ethical character transformed by its object. Asking that question again about accurate hostile appreciation would not advance it. A new test must specify a different relation: damage as a constitutive support, an operative means or an enabled consequence of the fulfilled practice, rather than adversity that participants merely welcome. These roles must themselves remain distinct.

The next central comparison concerns that actual involvement. It must preserve the independently described expressive and appreciative features while tracing the changed support or consequence relation. It must not stipulate an identical complete value-bearing activity: a contextual explanation may hold that this relation partly changes what is realized. Whether such a comparison is feasible, and whether a favorable aspect remains, changes or fails to arise, requires argument. Neither a reassigned label nor an assumed arithmetic offset answers it.

The current result identifies the scope of a positive immanent quality and the additional work required to identify ethical direction. It establishes neither universal necessity nor the necessity of an irreducible ethical premise. The support and consequence test is a specified next investigation, not a result already obtained.

## 19. Damaging support and the organization of correction

6 October 2026. Named connection: **actual support relation → possible correction → ethical character of the whole continuation**. This executes a concrete part of §18's proposed test. It retains the governing future-of-the-whole criterion. The general distinction among historical cause, enabling condition, constituent and repair target is inherited from [realization §4](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#4-what-an-improvement-comparison-must-actually-preserve); it is not a new result.

### Specify what actually depends on what

A generating process can supply an expressive performance and discharge waste that damages another process. That establishes a common source. It does not establish that the damage supplies the performance: a feasible filter might prevent the damage while preserving the power. Whether such a filter exists, what it requires and what it displaces are substantive questions.

A second inference also needs correction. Even where stopping the generating process interrupts the music, stopping the music need not end that process. The music might use otherwise unused output from a process maintained for an independent purpose. Conversely, demand for the performance might sustain production and its damaging discharge even though the damage is not a means of producing power. Actual incentives, resource use and opportunities lost must be traced; describing output as otherwise unused is a comparison condition, not a general excuse for using it.

Thus these three claims differ: a damaging operation supports the activity; its adverse condition is an operative means to the activity; and continuing the activity sustains the damaging operation. One can hold without the others. No causal role by itself establishes legitimate responsibility or permission, but misidentifying the role would make the ethical comparison answer the wrong question.

### A concrete source-swap comparison

Consider two sources adequate for two small electrical loads: a work-driven generator and an independent electrical supply. One load supports actual shared music; the other runs a water-circulation task. The generator requires an exhausting period of work its operator wishes to end. The two histories below match the specified work and burden, actual musical expression and appreciation, circulation and energy use. They vary which source supports which load. Equal routing losses and compatible loads are supplied conditions of this conceptual construction, not claims about an observed installation. Independence here concerns their operation during the selected intervention, not an ontological separation or the absence of other shared dependencies.

| Arrangement | Work-driven source supplies | Independent supply serves | Immediate result of stopping the work, with the other supply continuing and routing unchanged |
|---|---|---|---|
| H | Music | Circulation | Music stops; circulation continues |
| I | Circulation | Music | Circulation stops; music continues |

The result follows directly from the specified connections. If W and S denote availability of the work-driven source and the independent supply, the supplied load conditions are `(music, circulation) = (W, S)` in H and `(S, W)` in I. At `(W, S) = (1, 1)` both yield `(1, 1)`; setting W to zero yields `(0, 1)` and `(1, 0)` respectively. These symbols describe a selected dependency within an already shared process. They neither posit independent owners nor prove that the adverse aspect of work generates electricity.

The intervention is stopping the work, not every possible way of relieving its burden. Better equipment or another arrangement might retain the work and remove the exhaustion. Nor does the comparison establish different complete sets of attainable futures: rerouting, stored energy, further sources and their timing can matter. It establishes different immediate consequences of this specified intervention. The actual control and support history is part of what the whole-future account must preserve.

The positive explanatory result is that a present inventory of achievements and burdens can omit how correction propagates. An appraisal concerned with the future must retain the relevant organization and alternatives. This applies the existing whole-process requirement to an explicit matched-output case. It supplies no ranking of music against circulation, and no first identification of the work's ethical adversity from the operator's wish alone.

### What the relation can and cannot settle

A substantive rival grants the music, burden, circulation and changed intervention consequences, but denies that attaching the work to music rather than circulation automatically makes the current whole worse. It can locate prospective differences in the actual continuation and goods interrupted. A contextual account can instead hold that an undertaking's actual contribution to sustaining a damaging practice changes its ethical character. The source swap alone does not establish that sustaining relation or adjudicate the claim. Participants' aims, knowledge and actual control cannot silently be added afterward as if they were already matched.

It would be equally premature to exclude causal relations from what an achievement is worth. The [bounded continuation of Hurka's argument](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#causal-contribution-and-the-character-of-an-achievement) proposes that successfully producing an independently valuable end can affect an achievement's own value. This gives a substantive comparator to a blanket causal/constitutive separation. It does not establish a converse rule for unwanted harmful support, and its independently valued end remains a premise.

Our candidate therefore retains both possibilities: an activity can realize a favorable aspect within an adverse arrangement, and its actual involvement can partly constitute the ethical character of the undertaking. Identifying which applies requires the particular relation and its grounds. Neither automatic contamination from causal ancestry nor automatic insulation of a fulfilled experience is justified by this comparison.

### Result and foundational limit

The test has changed the target in a concrete way. The phrase harm-dependent fulfillment must be replaced by an identified dependence and intervention. The source-swap construction demonstrates how matching present outcomes can conceal different immediate correction consequences. The filter and independent-production alternatives defeat specific causal inferences, not the whole-future proposal.

This does not derive first goodness: comparative use still needs an account of why the affected realization or loss matters. It would be a detour to seek that original warrant in further support diagrams after their relevant causal roles are clear. The support inquiry is retained as a conditional application and a test of the adequacy of a whole representation. The central grounding question returns to the affirmative identification of actual differentiated realization itself; an account of valuable contribution already assuming a good end cannot discharge it. The stronger goal remains active, and no necessary additional ethical principle has been established.

The specific return point is [realization §11's nondual constitutive proposal](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#11-realization-and-the-claim-of-necessary-agreement), including its bounded Nishida comparison. Its later concern-formation proposal was already pursued; neither that comparison nor the sacrifice case is a new next study. The unresolved identity concerns what independently characterizes the unifying activity expressed through knowing, feeling and willing, why its fuller realization would constitute ethical good, and how that identification reaches the differentiated future of the whole. Shared reality alone cannot supply the last step, and contrary ethical judgments must not automatically reclassify a case as incomplete realization. This is the next central investigation, not an accepted identification or a new mandatory doctrine.

## 20. Actual demand and universal concordance

6 October 2026. Named connection: **demand formed within activity → its ethical significance → the scope and reliability of its practical articulation**. This directly examines the immanent identity retained in [realization §11](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#11-realization-and-the-claim-of-necessary-agreement). The executed concern-formation comparison and sacrifice case remain inherited. Ren's whole-future criterion governs the reconstruction; neither private satisfaction nor an entity's preservation replaces it.

### Recover the actual explanatory starting point

The [renewed primary reading](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#actual-demand-and-the-concordance-claim) identifies a substantive starting point in experienced demand, rather than deriving valuation from causal occurrence alone. Its full target exceeds coherence: integrated knowing, feeling and willing are central. The proposal also makes stronger collective-personality and concordance claims, which require separate assessment.

Our reconstruction takes seriously the possibility that the activity forming a practical direction and the activity that direction answers to are one developing relation. A ready-made private preference need not receive significance from an external authority. What participants come to want, how they understand the situation and what they do can form and change together. A purpose can be revised because its previous articulation misdescribed the very activity through which it arose.

That is an explanatory alternative to treating ethical correction as a constraint attached to an otherwise complete self. It is not yet a first-value derivation. An account of how a demand arises, becomes coherent or responds to facts must still establish why the specified relation constitutes ethical favorability. The target cannot be reduced to a strong wish, nor can its ethical authority be assumed merely because it is experienced as necessary.

The generative foundation must also account for the content that this route begins with. A formal event history that omits lived demand does not refute the fuller proposal. Conversely, the proposal's account of experience cannot be imported as if it had been derived from that event history. Their connection is an unresolved explanatory obligation within the same reality; it need not imply either separate ontological substances or an irreducible extra ethical axiom.

### What a more adequate articulation can establish

The positive candidate concerns activity in which understanding, feeling and direction can be corrected through what is actually involved. The distinction between knowing and acting remains useful because someone can accurately report a consequence while continuing unchanged. But the distinction must not hide cases where learning changes a concern and what the participant takes themselves to be doing. Both configurations are possible targets for explanation.

This supplies independently assessable failures: a purportedly comprehensive articulation can omit an affected relation, attribute a purpose that the activity does not have, suppress inconvenient evidence or mistake compliance for another participant's actual initiative. Correcting such failures changes its relation to reality. The previous support test exemplifies one limited correction of this kind: changing causal description can reveal a different intervention consequence. Its ethical scope remains conditional.

An informed hierarchical account presents a stronger challenge. It can acknowledge the differentiated activities, their formation and actual losses while defending their subordination to a preferred organization of the future. Accuracy alone does not settle that appraisal. A fuller constitutive proposal must explain why such a direction misrealizes the relation, or recognize the case as a limit, without defining adequate realization by agreement with the preferred verdict. This rival challenges the needed identification; it is not declared a counterexample satisfying every disputed condition of the strongest proposal.

### Distinguish ethical applicability from guaranteed agreement

Four claims must remain distinct within the account:

1. A specified concrete realization constitutes a favorable ethical aspect.
2. The same good-making explanation applies wherever its relevant conditions occur.
3. The deepest demands of fully developed participants necessarily accord with the whole's good.
4. Such participants reliably judge and act correctly in particular situations.

The first does not become true merely because the second is consistently applied. The second does not entail the third. The third also differs from the fourth: a shared ethical orientation need not guarantee correct particular judgments. Nor does either unresolved guarantee show that every proposed immanent good has failed. Ethical truth, its scope, the character of developed demand and reliable practical realization are connected tasks, but one must not substitute for another.

Universal applicability also need not mean a complete ordering or exactly one permissible response. Different abilities and circumstances are already accommodated by the inherited account; those are not counterexamples. Even within one situation, a defended ethic might recognize more than one adequate continuation. Establishing a shared ethical ground therefore does not require proving that every mature judgment selects the same act.

A stronger decision-level guarantee, if adopted, requires a further test; it must not be silently attributed to the source's deepest-demand concordance claim. Consider a conflict between truthful public acknowledgment and protective confidentiality, with both participants informed about the same history and effects, responsive to the affected lives, and willing to bear personal costs. They disagree about whether one proposed disclosure is justified. These ethical claims are supplied for this test; it cannot derive their first worth. The relevant question is what independently identifiable omission, projection or disintegration warrants calling one judgment less adequate. Merely repeating that it disagrees with the true good would not explain that difference.

This is a proposed challenge to a decision-level guarantee, not a demonstrated counterexample to complete realization or to a shared deepest orientation. If a relevant factual or affective difference is found, it must be retained. The inquiry must specify the claimed agreement before testing it.

### Extend to the whole without installing a collective owner

The reconstruction can accept that persons, purposes and standards form through shared activity without identifying the entire unfolding as one experiencing personality. Their social formation does not by itself establish a higher owner or a collective will whose satisfaction settles ethics. The whole-future criterion concerns actual differentiated realizations and their continuing relations, including effects beyond human judgment.

This means our version cannot silently inherit a source's guarantee of harmony while withholding the stronger premises offered for it. It must establish its own account of ethical quality and practical reliability. Equally, removing that guarantee does not force us back to independent private interests: concrete relationships and their transformations remain the proposed constituents of significance.

The result is an explicit dependency correction. The first-good proposal begins with a richer activity than bare causal occurrence, and its ethical identification remains substantive. Necessary concordance is a further hypothesis rather than an admission condition for investigating that identification. The central next task is to test whether a direction that faithfully incorporates actual differentiated activity is thereby ethically better grounded, or whether it can remain an accurate appropriation. The account must explain the difference through the actual relation and its future, without treating all coordination, reflection or agreement as goodness. No universal ethical necessity or necessary additional ethical principle is established by this reading.

## 21. Personal recognition and the scope of the hierarchy test

6 October 2026. Named connection: **adequate practical articulation → ethical quality**, with informed appropriation as the proposed challenge. The [bounded primary extension](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#personal-apprehension-and-differentiated-ends) changes the test's target. An accurately informed hierarchy is not automatically an instance of the fuller realization; a hierarchy that does instantiate it need not deny its favorable aspect. Both qualifications matter for the first-goodness question.

### Reconstruct what the proposed relation contains

The renewed source inquiry directs attention to personal recognition within differentiated unity. Our reconstruction specifies a relation in which another's developing activity enters the undertaking as an end, rather than being exhausted by its contribution to a controlling purpose. Knowing and feeling participate in that relation; a checklist of accurate representations and strong emotions does not establish it.

An end here is an actual direction of the practice: what its participants attend to, respond to and seek to realize. Describing that direction does not yet certify its ethical authority. It can be examined through the practice's formation, deliberation, operative commitments and responses to changes. A declaration of respect is insufficient if the activity consistently treats the declared end as irrelevant except through its usefulness to another aim. Conversely, failure to choose an end on one occasion does not show that it never entered the appraisal as an end.

This analysis does not begin with separately owned interests. The participants and their purposes have developed through shared activity; that history can change both. Their differentiation nevertheless remains real: understanding another's activity is different from making it only a component of one's own project. The proposed recognition concerns how those differentiated developments participate in what the shared future becomes.

### Execute the proposed hierarchy comparison at its proper scope

Consider a hypothetical cultural workshop. Its director knows the participants' histories and changing expressive interests, feels their disappointments, adjusts methods and accepts personal costs. The participants contribute actual expression rather than merely simulating engagement. These conditions are supplied for a conceptual test; no empirical case or fully matched future is asserted.

In one version, the participants' developing activities matter exclusively because they sustain the institution's preferred cultural project. The director accurately acknowledges and feels their losses when those activities diverge from the project, while continuing to assess them solely by their contribution to it. In a second version, their developing activities also matter as ends within the undertaking. This can lead the director to revise the project, while preserving responsibilities and constraints that require their own justification. Present outputs can coincide; a different immediate choice is not required to distinguish the actual purposes organizing the relationship.

The first version fails a claim to recognize these participants as ends. Its factual accuracy and affective involvement do not repair that specific misdescription. This is an internal consequence of the declared relational target, not an independent proof that the second version is morally better. It neither condemns every limited institutional remit nor establishes an entitlement to direct every shared activity.

A third possibility is an honestly hierarchical version of the second relation. The director recognizes participants' realizations as ends but defends unequal responsibilities, a conflicting undertaking or a particular priority. Whether that defense succeeds is a substantive ethical question. Crucially, the director can grant that personal recognition has a favorable aspect while disputing what it requires in this situation. Such a rival challenges the weight or authority of recognition; it does not thereby refute its first ethical significance.

Nor does recognizing another as an end make every current wish authoritative. A formed direction can misunderstand its object, damage another realization or defeat the very activity it purports to continue. The comparison therefore cannot be settled by counting refusals, requiring equal control or treating all constraint as appropriation. Those tests would introduce further principles without establishing them.

### The positive claim and the remaining first-ground rival

The bounded positive consequence is this: **an appraisal claiming to realize personal recognition cannot justify another's significance exclusively by their contribution to its controlling purpose.** The exclusion follows from the relation being claimed. It does not yet establish that every adequate appraisal of the whole must realize that relation, or that the relation itself is ethically favorable.

The affirmative candidate goes further: an undertaking has a favorable ethical aspect when its direction answers to differentiated realizations as ends within the shared continuation. Its proposed ground is the actual personally responsive relationship, not a reward assigned by an observer or an already warranted private preference. This is a substantive constitutive appraisal under investigation. Its occurrence, favorable character, practical weight and permission for particular means remain distinct questions within one unfolding.

A genuine first-goodness rival must address that fuller target. It might preserve the actual noninstrumental relationship yet explain the alleged favorable aspect without any good-making role for that relation, or substantively deny favorability in the specified case. Alternatively, it might show that the proposed relation has been misidentified or cannot be realized. Merely assigning a different moral label, describing a perfectly informed controller or defending unequal priority does not supply that argument.

Contextual dependence alone is not that rival. A relation can require an enabling setting or belong to a further achievement while partly constituting that whole's good. The candidate need not become an independently valuable atom to explain ethical quality. A competing account must explain why the recognition relation has no claimed good-making role in the specified whole, rather than simply point out that the activity has other constituents or depends on conditions.

This identifies what would change the account. Evidence that apparently noninstrumental recognition was wholly organized around external success would revise the realization description. A successful account explaining away its alleged good-making role would revise the constitutive identification. A credible explanation of that role would advance it, while still leaving universality, conflicts and practical authority to be established. Neither answer follows from the current comparison alone.

### Genealogy and the whole-future boundary

[Section 8](#8-continuation-formed-understanding-can-correct-its-source) already distinguishes valid correction from incorporation that merely preserves an old aim, under a supplied good of truthful inquiry. [Realization §§8–9](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#8-continuation-6-october-2026-what-supported-participation-actually-preserves) already examine actual participation and its substitutes. The workshop applies these inherited methods; it is not an independent discovery that information differs from concern. The advance is a source-driven repair of the target and of which rival challenges which ethical claim.

The future of the whole remains the criterion under investigation. Recognition is not a warrant to preserve every entity or activity indefinitely, and a favorable relational aspect does not certify the whole continuation. The source's further treatment of adversity also faces the inherited [whole-history comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#5-valuable-response-does-not-warrant-creating-its-occasion): a valuable response to adversity does not alone justify its occurrence. No inevitable reconciliation, cosmic compensation or general permission to impose losses has been adopted.

The next direct question is now whether the specified recognition partly constitutes a good of the concrete unfolding, or whether a stronger account explains the alleged favorable aspect without that role. That comparison must preserve the actual relation, its enabling and constitutive context, and its relevant future rather than return to a weaker controller. Universal ethical necessity, the minimal explanation of this richer activity and the alleged necessity of an additional ethical principle remain unresolved.

## 22. Personal concern and the projects it can oppose

6 October 2026. Named connection: **personal recognition → its constitutive ethical quality within the whole**. The direct test reveals an ambiguity in §21's phrase “developing activity as an end.” Recognizing a person as an end and affirming their role in shaping a particular undertaking as an end are different relations. The previous section already denied authority to every wish; this continuation examines why the two relations cannot inherit one another's ethical appraisal.

### Preserve the person while specifying the undertaking

A formed person is not exhausted by a single purpose they have acquired. Their history, understanding, attachments and capacities can participate in revising or abandoning that purpose. This is a possibility to establish in the case, not a guarantee that every change preserves every aspect of a life. It requires no private owner existing before those relations: the person and the purpose can both have formed within the same unfolding without becoming identical.

Accordingly, concern for that developing life need not confer favorable standing on every project expressive of it. Someone can understand a project, take its importance in the person's life seriously and continue caring for the person while opposing the project. That opposition may express the relationship instead of suspending it. Whether a particular opposition is warranted still depends on its grounds; merely claiming superior insight could disguise manipulation.

These distinctions preserve three relations inside one practice: responding to the person, understanding what their proposal means and admitting that proposal as something with a reason in its favor. Understanding need not be merely instrumental, and refusing favorable standing need not amount to refusing to understand. Neither facts alone nor the word “recognition” settles the ethical identification.

### A substantive comparison within shared wrongdoing

Consider two coauthors knowingly conducting a campaign that damages an innocent third person's standing. Each genuinely cares about the other's life beyond the campaign, permits dissent and departure, and would continue that care if the project ended. Their collaboration is actual; their knowledge of its effects is not concealed. The campaign's wrongness is a supplied ethical premise in this conceptual test, not derived from their cooperation or from the causal root. The case is ours, not an example attributed to the primary comparators.

One proposes extending the campaign. In one continuation, the partner treats the proposal's expression of the other's developing agency as a consideration favoring it, although other considerations may defeat it. In another, the partner fully understands that expression and preserves personal concern, but denies that this fact itself favors the proposal. Both can ultimately reject the extension. The comparison therefore concerns what recognition admits into appraisal, not just different final decisions. It does not stipulate identical intentional experience, relationships or complete futures.

The first interpretation might retain a favorable aspect in their reciprocal affirmation as authors, alongside the campaign's adverse character. A substantive rival disputes that decomposition: affirming one another specifically as authors of this campaign can help constitute its freely shared destructive purpose. On that account, the particular affirmation is ethically defective within the whole; it supplies no favorable consideration for realizing that relation. The rival explains the dispute through what is being affirmed and done, rather than toggling an ethical predicate on otherwise uninterpreted events.

Neither account follows merely from the campaign's wrongness. The retained-aspect account must defend the alleged favorable contribution instead of presuming that every noninstrumental collaboration supplies one. The contextual rival must defend the altered appraisal of this actual participation relation instead of treating association with wrongdoing as sufficient contamination. Both can acknowledge favorable care when one partner supports the other's departure from the campaign or attends to an unrelated need. Those other relations cannot, by themselves, settle the value of the particular affirmation being tested.

This comparison does not show that the full Nishida target is instantiated by shared wrongdoing, that friendship has no good, or that personal concern loses every favorable aspect. It tests the scope of our own proposed good-making description. A judgment about project-relative affirmation cannot silently replace a judgment about personal concern, or vice versa.

### A primary dispute sharpens the decisive inference

The [bounded source comparison](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#personal-concern-and-practical-admission) distinguishes taking a person seriously from initially treating a central project as worthy of consideration as an option. Mason argues for a constitutive defect in certain friendships while allowing other goods; Hua challenges the required initial openness. Neither source supplies a derivation of the underlying ethical judgments from generativity.

Our use preserves the strongest disagreement. Merely showing that friends can eventually reject one another's views does not answer an argument about their initial admission. Nor does absence of later corruption answer a claim about the relation's present character. The question is whether genuine personal involvement requires conferring even provisional favorable standing on the specified project. That connection needs its own defense; it cannot be inferred from the importance of understanding the person.

The positive alternative is critical personal concern. The relation can remain open to learning what formed a purpose, what it means and what its consequences reveal, while denying that its being this person's purpose by itself gives it even initial favorable standing. This is stronger than merely withholding final endorsement. It does not require treating the other as a reform project whose sole value lies in future compliance. The account must preserve actual concern for their life, real responsiveness and the possibility that the critic's own judgment needs correction.

### What changes in the unified candidate

The candidate must therefore specify **what recognition recognizes and what practical influence it gives that object**. Personal concern, acknowledgment of genuine participation in shaping activity and favorable appraisal of the chosen project are connected but nonidentical. The method explains those differences through the history and organization of the activity; it does not install three independent moral substances.

The positive explanatory result is that continued personal concern can organize the transformation or ending of a jointly formed purpose. Its continuity need not be bought by preserving every current expression of that purpose. This fits Ren's whole-future criterion: the affected third life and the changed relations remain within the same appraisal. An improvement for the friends or their institution alone cannot certify the whole continuation.

The ethical claim is narrower than an automatic favorable verdict on recognition of any developing activity. Critical personal concern remains a candidate constituent of good; its worth is not derived merely from its occurrence, and criticism is not made correct by its caring intention. A defended account must explain how the concrete concern contributes ethical quality, including when its current practical role is to refuse a project. The supplied wrongness of the campaign cannot be repackaged as a derivation of that first worth.

The general possibility that context changes a relation's ethical character is inherited from [realization §10](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#10-concerns-object-and-the-grounds-of-its-correction) and its organic-unity comparisons. The new application concerns a relation that helps constitute a shared practical undertaking, rather than an observer's pleasure at independently occurring adversity. [Grounding §8](#8-continuation-formed-understanding-can-correct-its-source) also already supports purpose revision under a warranted higher aim. Neither prior result establishes the first worth of the present personal relation.

The next direct test should preserve critical personal concern while comparing its proposed constitutive contribution with an account treating it only as a causal means to independently identified favorable effects, with no constitutive good-making role for the concern relation itself. An improved continuation can include the relation as a constituent, so merely locating good in that continuation would not oppose the candidate. The comparison must allow both present relational quality and later effects, avoid double counting, and admit that a relation can have its own role while depending on context. Universal necessity, first ethical identification and the minimal generative explanation remain unfinished; no necessity of an extra ethical principle follows.

## 23. The whole is the unit of grounding

6 October 2026. [Ren's renewed correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-whole-as-grounding-unit.md) changes the governing next step. Named connection: **organization of the whole continuation → constructive or degenerative quality → practical significance**. Sections 21–22 retain their local findings, but their proposed mandatory recognition-first gate is superseded.

### Restore the explanatory order

The whole continuation is the ethical target. Purposes, experiences, relationships, institutions and standards differentiate within it and change through their interaction. Its quality need not be assembled from independently warranted goods attached to those parts. This restores the direct-comparison permission already established in §16 and the future-good integral; it is a correction of the research trajectory, not a newly discovered nondual foundation.

The affirmative candidate concerns what the unfolding actually realizes and how its organization changes the shared conditions for further differentiated realization, renewal and meaningful completion. Its proposed goodness lies in that concrete pattern of becoming. Particular care, pleasure, understanding or sacrifice must be understood through the pattern they help constitute. Neither the part's survival nor the continuation of an incumbent purpose fixes the whole's direction.

This does not require every participant to gain, make every loss unreal, or posit a beneficiary above the differentiated lives. Present realization is within the continuation, including where there is no further product. Different continuations may remain incomparable. The proposal therefore requires neither a scalar total nor a complete ranking before any particular ethical comparison can be defended.

### An immediate constraint on explanations

If two descriptions preserve the same actual relationships, undergoing and consequences but move an analytical boundary, their appraisal as a whole must agree. Calling damage external to an organization cannot exclude it from the relevant continuation. This is a consistency condition on whole appraisal, not a proof of an ethical ordering. A different actual boundary, such as a changed access relation or authority, changes the history and need not receive the same appraisal.

This constraint preserves differentiation while preventing an analyst's partition from deciding ethical scope. It also diagnoses the preceding drift: demanding independent value certificates for each isolated relation would impose a decomposition that the whole-level claim has not accepted.

### Apply the inherited repair comparison to the whole

Consider two conceptual continuations of a practice. In one, it repairs an impairment, develops usable capacities and permits its own role to change or end as dependence diminishes. In the other, it sustains comparable occasions for repair by recreating the impairment. Specify the actual damage, repairs, capacities, other achievements, continuing support and transition costs before evaluating either history. Do not stipulate equal complete futures or treat the number of successful interventions as the whole's quality.

The candidate favors the first when the recurrent impairment in the second contributes no compensating realization or necessary protection and the first's transition preserves usable support. Its explanation concerns the organization of the entire continuation: repair changes what can follow, rather than supplying occasions for its own repetition. This is a defended ethical comparison with explicit evaluative content, not a deduction of goodness from repair mechanics. No separate primitive value of recognition is needed to state or investigate it.

The inherited adversity/response comparison already supplies the inference restriction: value in repairing a loss does not establish that creating that loss improves the history. This application adds no new empirical evidence. An allegedly liberating ending that destroys indispensable provision would change the comparison. So would a recurrence genuinely necessary for an otherwise unavailable realization. The candidate must answer those actual differences and retain losses in whichever continuation it favors.

### Preserve useful local work without reinstalling its gate

The interrupted failed-remedy inquiry distinguishes failure of an institutional outcome from occurrence of a received personal encounter. It does not independently establish the encounter's value or the whole history's superiority. That is a scoped supporting observation, not the next foundation to secure.

The same review corrects §21's stronger first-ground test. An explanation can grant a relation a constitutive good-making role while explaining that role through further relations or goods. Such an account need not reduce the relation to a causal instrument. The earlier requirement that a first-ground rival deny every good-making role was too strong. This correction preserves the possible constitutive claim without requiring that the whole be decomposed into ethical atoms.

The next work must defend or revise the whole-level generativity identification itself, using concrete continuations and rivals that preserve their organization. It must not turn a selected relation's independent intrinsic value into a mandatory admission test again. The supplied ethical content of the positive comparison remains an explanatory obligation within the reconstructed theory; no demonstrated impossibility of unified grounding or necessity of an added ethical principle follows from that unfinished explanation.

## 24. Differentiated fulfillment and the transformation of the whole

6 October 2026. Named connection: **formed realizations → transformation of their organizing conditions → ethical quality of the whole continuation**. This develops §23's positive candidate through a comparison with real gains and losses. It does not reinstate independent value for a selected part as a prerequisite.

### Generativity can change the form of continuation

The [bounded reading of 乾](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#qian-differentiated-formation-and-timely-transformation) joins differentiated formation, concordance and timely advance or withdrawal. Its substantive account of rightness remains part of the source; it is not a deduction from change alone. Our reconstruction asks whether a whole can develop well through changing the very organization by which it previously developed.

The candidate is that **constructive generativity can consist in differentiated realizations becoming conditions for a transformed shared life, including changes in its purposes and standards**. An educational practice, for example, forms capacities that can eventually change what education should become. Its ethical direction cannot be read from how reliably it reproduces its originating form. The formed capacities, the organizing institution and their evaluative relationship belong to one history throughout.

This is more specific than praising adaptability. Adaptation can preserve a fixed scheme by making every new development serve it. The proposed constructive relation instead permits consequences and formed understanding to revise what the scheme is for. This relation is independently describable; whether it makes a particular continuation better remains the substantive claim tested below. Nor does it imply that every formed desire warrants institutional accommodation.

### A whole comparison with genuine achievement on both sides

Consider a community whose education and common work have developed two living languages and associated traditions of expression. Both are actually taught, used and changed; an archive alone does not preserve those practices. Participants now face two feasible continuations. This is a conceptual case, not a historical or empirical report.

In the convergent continuation, education gradually adopts one common language and demanding shared practice. Participants understand the transition and genuinely participate in it. The arrangement produces greater technical coordination, a distinctive common achievement and fewer recurring translation difficulties. Over generations, the second language and the social conditions for living its expressive practices disappear. The case does not explain the result away as deception, absent deliberation or a merely successful controller.

In the differentiated continuation, common provision and communication remain workable, while education, translation and shared institutions are reorganized around both living practices. The practices also change through contact; this is not preservation of two sealed identities. Maintaining that organization consumes time and support and forgoes part of the convergent achievement. Both histories retain adequate provision; neither is stipulated identical in every other respect. The realized costs and the changed relationships belong in their comparison.

Our affirmative judgment provisionally favors the differentiated continuation, treating the convergence gains together—including its distinctive common achievement—as insufficient to warrant the loss in this comparison. Adequate provision, limited coordination gains and rich continuing practices do not by themselves entail this judgment. Its proposed ground is the resulting organization of the whole: shared activity supports distinct forms of expression that can encounter one another and change the common practice. The whole develops through what it has differentiated, instead of obtaining its additional unity by ending that living articulation. This is a proposed good-making explanation of the relation, not the addition of a prior moral entitlement assigned separately to each language or person.

The judgment concerns the concrete realization, not the count of languages or options. A nominal second curriculum that nobody can use would not instantiate it. Nor would segregated preservation that destroys common provision. Conversely, a common language can enable differentiated expression rather than extinguish it. Those changes would alter the explanatory ground, even if a diversity count remained unchanged.

### A rival that also speaks for the whole

A whole-order rival can acknowledge every gain and loss while favoring convergence. It can defend a deeper common achievement as a maturation of this entire form of life. It need not regard individual wishes as sovereign or mistake institutional persistence for the whole. The disagreement therefore survives a nondual reconstruction: it concerns the relative quality of two actual modes of unfolding.

Our response includes the distinctive common achievement as well as the coordination gain. We judge those gains together insufficient, in this comparison, to warrant losing the living relations through which the community continues to articulate and revise itself. This is the contested whole-level appraisal; limiting coordination alone does not establish it. The differentiated history already achieves workable unity; its continuing difference is practiced within that unity rather than standing outside it as an obstruction. That is the substantive comparative premise we defend. It can be contested, and it must not be hidden inside the word generative.

If convergence instead enables indispensable communication or an otherwise unavailable achievement sufficiently weighty to warrant the loss, the judgment can change. Likewise, exhaustion or voluntary completion of a practice can differ from removing the conditions of a still living practice. There is no universal duty to retain every historical form. An adequate account must explain such changes through the whole trajectory, rather than attach a permanently favorable sign to either differentiation or consolidation.

### What the connection establishes and leaves open

The account now makes a definite, defeasible comparison and identifies why its whole-level rationale could reverse. It rejects both successful integration and preserved plurality as sufficient ethical tests. It proposes a more precise ground: differentiated realization within a shared life that those realizations can themselves transform. This concerns the organization of becoming, rather than a stock of already valued objects.

Purpose correction in §8, ending and renewal in §§10 and 15, and the limits of option counts in §9 are inherited. The addition is the direct whole-level comparison between genuine convergence and continuing differentiated realization, informed by the new primary connection. It is not a fresh proof of those earlier results or an empirical demonstration of the stipulated feasibility.

The next central question is whether this reciprocal transformation explains favorable whole quality beyond the selected comparison, or only names a feature favored by a narrower cultural ideal. Test that proposed extension against constructive convergence and meaningful completion, preserving actual practices and consequences. The candidate has not established universal ethical necessity or ruled out unified grounding; its normative identification remains a live explanation to defend.

## 25. Differentiation can become a common realization

6 October 2026. Named connection: **differentiated formation → selective commitment and actual realization → practical significance within the whole**. This extension explains constructive convergence without reversing Ren's whole-first direction.

### A relation can change its mode of realization

What a process has formed can participate in its continuation without remaining an open alternative at every subsequent moment. Distinct contributions may transform a shared undertaking during its formation and then shape its enactment through a settled arrangement. Their incorporation is an actual relation to identify, not a declaration that nothing has been lost. A name can disappear while an operative contribution remains; a name can also remain while the contribution has become inert decoration.

The distinction matters for the whole: the same organizing constraint can prevent formation if imposed too early, enable enactment after shared formation, and obstruct renewal if retained after the undertaking ends. These are different histories, not interchangeable local signs attached to constraint. Their ethical appraisal must include what actually forms, is realized and is foreclosed.

The [bounded Zhongyong extension](WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#zhongyong-connected-realization-and-common-form) contributes a comparison of connected realization through 成己 and 成物 and fitting exercise. Our temporal reconstruction is a proposal to test, not a deduction from the source or an identification of its normative vocabulary with causal coherence.

### Completion and exploration as real alternatives

Consider a group developing a collective mural during a bounded period of access to one wall. Through actual exchange, two incompatible compositions emerge and change. Neither finished composition was already contained in an organizer's plan. Materials and available time permit one complete enactment, or further exploratory work; the full means, burdens and effects beyond the group remain within the conceptual comparison.

In the completed continuation, participants settle on one composition, suspend further redesign during execution and paint it. Their differences continue to shape the actual work through the arrangement they developed. The activity finishes. The unrealized composition and further experiments remain genuine foregone possibilities; their historical contribution to the selected work does not automatically compensate their loss.

In the exploratory continuation, the group keeps developing alternatives until access expires. This realizes inquiry, expression and contact, with genuine discoveries unavailable in the completed history. No mural is finished. The case does not identify exploration with failure or absence of value, and it does not claim equal complete futures. The common prefix and specified constraints locate the choice without dictating its ethical answer.

Our affirmative judgment provisionally favors completion, expressly comparing the lived making and actual work with the exploratory history's discoveries and relations and the alternative enactments forgone. That priority is the contested whole-history judgment, not a condition already built into the case. Its proposed ground is the way actual differentiation becomes something the group can now jointly enact: selective stability carries the developing relation into a further mode of realization. The work expresses a relation the group has developed together, rather than merely satisfying an original plan. This explains what is being favored; the exploration rival can still dispute the claim that its realization merits priority here.

An exploration rival can acknowledge all of this and defend the ongoing inquiry as the richer continuation. The response must compare its actual discoveries and relations with the selected work, rather than invoke a general duty to finish. If the work is slight, the exploration transformative, or the purported incorporation misdescribes whose contribution remains operative, our verdict can change. ### Situated direction without permanent openness

On the defended comparison, settling and carrying through the work has a practical point because of the continuation it realizes. The relevant reason emerges within a history that has formed the undertaking and its possibilities. It need not begin with independently warranted private preferences and then aggregate their votes. Agreement identifies one feature of the relation; it does not supply the entire ethical warrant or unlimited authority to compel participation.

Suspending compositional redesign is narrower than immunity to consequences. Newly discovered harm, failed feasibility or misrepresented participation can warrant interruption. Conversely, the appearance of an attractive new variant need not require reopening every settled choice. Recognizing that difference is a proposed task of wisdom within the unfolding: understand what the present constraint enables and when its role has changed.

This gives the whole candidate a temporal form. Formation, commitment, enactment and completion can be connected aspects of one favorable continuation. They are not a compulsory sequence for every good, and further transformation need not occur after a terminal realization. A phase can answer what earlier activity made possible by fulfilling it, rather than retaining every route by which it could have developed differently.

### Result, genealogy and the next substantive test

Selective closure in §9, legitimate coordinated exclusion in §15, completion without obligatory enrichment in §17, and the integral's history-sensitive appraisal are inherited. The addition is the role transition: differentiated contributions can become operative constituents of a common realization without remaining competing proposals. This explains how convergence can express the whole's generativity rather than serve only as an exception to a preference for diversity.

The whole-grounding claim remains larger than this explanation. Reciprocal formation and successful enactment identify a temporal relation that constructive and destructive undertakings can both exhibit. The next test must investigate how the actual realization and its wider continuing relations change its ethical character, preserving the temporal achievement rather than treating it as sufficient or erasing it. This is a whole-history identification question; it is not a demand to establish the mural or each participant as an independently basic good first. Universal necessity remains unproved.

The new constraint is precise: preserving differentiated effects in appraisal does not require preserving the formations that underwent them. The task is therefore to compare which account explains the same specified history's constructive or destructive character, what its explanation includes, and which judgment would require revision. Attention, discrimination and revision can operate in either arrangement. The rival preserves actual concern, sacrifice, achievement, impairment and their causal relations. It disputes the further identification of caring direction as the privileged expression of the whole's constitutive generativity, with destructive directions classified as its obstruction.

This is an explanatory rival, not a demonstrated counterexample to the richer identity. Showing that destructive activity also occurs does not establish that it realizes the full target in the same way. Conversely, calling every destructive orientation obstruction requires an account of what it obstructs and why that relation grounds the ethical difference. The distinction cannot rest solely on the favored verdict. A successful rival must explain the independently specified caring activity and relationships claimed to constitute it, rather than erase them as an observer's label.

### Formed evaluation can articulate the unfolding

A developing practice can form understanding of its actual dependencies and consequences; this understanding can participate in changing what the practice becomes. The articulation and its target differentiate within one history. Separating them for this test preserves an account's relation to what it claims, while omitting much of the material mechanism by which understanding forms. It does not posit independently complete realities.

The supported inference is bounded. When a standard claims that a relationship continues, actual continued exercise constrains that claim. If only records remain, a correction can improve the account while retaining the same identified relationship. If that correction changes subsequent activity, understanding has become operative in the continuation. This improves the articulation in a specified respect; the ethical significance and appropriate response to the lost relationship remain further questions. Section 27 supplies the distinction between ongoing R and its record without assuming R's first value.

Such articulation can be instrumental to an undertaking, or partly constitute a shared understanding that the history actually realizes. These roles can coexist. Being constitutive does not prove its goodness; being generated does not make it merely an instrument. Not all worthwhile activity must develop reflective standards.

### The standards formed by a transition cannot alone certify it

Extend C and S only as a declared conceptual possibility. Continuing R can cultivate appreciation of historical joint judgment; successor activity can cultivate appreciation of new common capabilities. In either continuation participants may accurately understand lost relationships, watershed effects and transition burdens. Their standards can nevertheless develop differently through what they actually live. This adds a possible formation history, not a fact inferred from the original case or an assertion that either community's judgment is correct.

Two explanations remain distinguishable: a revised judgment recognizes what makes the continuation better; the continuation forms a judgment disposed to endorse it. They can coexist. Evidence establishing only the latter does not establish the former. Further selection over revisable standards can repeat this dependence rather than discharge it.

Causal dependence alone does not discredit evaluation: inquiry occurs through causal relations, and an acquired judgment can be well grounded. The defect is treating formation and subsequent endorsement as sufficient certification without establishing their relationship to the claimed ethical ground. Equally, freezing the earlier standard would privilege a prior formation without warrant. New appreciation can itself change the whole being appraised; the test must retain that development rather than pretend the entire history stayed fixed.

The relevant contrast is between an account grounding authority in an achieved relationship of mutual endorsement and one grounding it in what the whole actually realizes, to which endorsement is answerable. The first is not refuted merely by naming it self-confirmation; it owes an explanation of why that relationship supplies authority. The second must defend the quality it says is recognized. Neither gets the conclusion from accurate representation alone.

The advance is a more precise positive target and an account of evaluation's place within it: the whole's proposed ethical direction, its reach, its enabling conditions and its articulated standards cannot substitute for one another. The [earlier Cheng study](BOUNDARY-SIGNIFICANCE-SOURCES.md) already reconstructs embodied unity. None is newly proved here.

The next central test is the source-informed constitutive claim itself: does a caring and discriminating organization of actual relations explain their constructive character, or describe one generated orientation whose ethical privilege remains unexplained? Retain the specified realization and trace what supports its appraisal: an identified relationship, newly formed appreciation, or accommodation to a selected standard. A rival must preserve those actual relations and may challenge their alleged ethical role. The target is neither a complete C/S ranking nor a theory of reflection as a prerequisite for good. Universal necessity and the claimed necessity of an additional ethical principle remain unestablished.

The source-informed distinction also constrains the proposed integral of future goods. An intervention can matter greatly through the continuations it changes; that practical significance is not automatically a measure of the recipient's intrinsic worth. A future description must retain current realization and completion, the support it depends on, and what the intervention actually changes. It must not count a later benefit twice merely because the condition enabling it is also important. These requirements connect to the existing integral; they do not supply its missing ethical ordering.

The result narrows the positive identity without declaring it complete. Goodness cannot be exhausted by whole recurrence or by an entity's productive importance. The candidate instead concerns the actual ethical quality of differentiated realization through its changing relations. The first-good question remains why that quality is favorable or adverse, including cases where informed whole-order accounts disagree. Neither a new support diagram nor a claim that every actualization is good discharges it. The next direct work must defend this qualification while retaining the practical dependence distinguished here; universal ethical necessity and the necessity of an additional ethical principle remain unestablished.

## 34. Self-good, practical reasons and the remaining ground

7 October 2026. Continuation of [Ren's self-inclusion clarification](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-self-good-within-the-whole.md). Named connection: **genuine realization within the whole → a consideration favoring its realization in action**. This is a consolidation and conditional practical implication of §§30–33, not a new proof of the first ethical character. Review found that another lottery, unrecognized benefit or successful repair would largely repeat existing arguments.

### Carry inclusion into practical significance

Section 30 includes genuine self-care directly in what the whole realizes. Section 32 requires no additional cosmic beneficiary. Neither claim by itself establishes permission, obligation or authority to perform an action. To connect goodness to practical reasons, the present candidate uses this substantive principle:

> Where a feasible response would realize a genuine good whose favorable ethical role survives in its actual relationships, that good counts in favor of the response unless an independently warranted restriction excludes that consideration as a basis for this choice.

An exclusion must be independently defended through the relationship governing the choice; it cannot simply mean that the desired reason is absent. The earlier distinction between general benefit and the permitted basis of a merit award remains relevant. Eligibility in this sense does not mean that the response is already justified overall. A favorable consideration can coexist with a decisive objection to the means; authority to impose a response requires its own grounds. The principle is an ethical commitment of the current candidate, not a consequence of causal availability alone.

Apply it to self-care whose goodness retains its favorable character in the specified relationship. No outward return is required by the good-to-reason principle itself. Where no independently warranted exclusion applies, self-good can favor the response without that return. A proposed exclusion must earn its warrant rather than follow merely from self-direction. This implication concerns the reason's basis; it does not establish the superiority of every self-directed response or a universal rule giving the self priority.

The acting life, its conditions, the response and the other affected lives remain differentiated within one unfolding. There is no independently sovereign private value seeking admission to a collective account. Nor is self-good always a fixed positive summand: the context-sensitive ethical role retained in §30 still applies. Unknown consequences cannot be treated as absent, and recognizing one reason does not complete the whole comparison.

### A good can have a ground without needing a further product

The absence of a required outward payoff limits an instrumental explanation of self-good. It does not establish that the good is ungrounded or that an irreducible ethical principle has to be added to generative reality. A constitutive explanation can concern the character of the realization itself and the actual relations through which it exists. It need not identify a second favorable product manufactured afterward. Section 32 already excludes counting an additional organizational good above the same relational realization; this is its consequence for how we formulate the remaining task.

The converse remains equally important. Having actual conditions, forming internally or satisfying a functional standard does not establish ethical goodness. Successful harmful formation is a retained challenge. Describing a standard as immanent identifies where and how it operates, but still leaves its ethical warrant to explain. The [shared method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md#make-analytical-separations-accountable) therefore permits a fuller constitutive explanation without allowing the desired verdict to be hidden inside its definition.

### Consolidate the candidate and its rival

The candidate is **goodness as a constitutive quality of differentiated living and its actual relationships within the whole unfolding**. Its explanation must identify how such quality is realized, sustained, obstructed, transformed or completed. Self-care is one articulation. Continued production, approval and conformity cannot serve as automatic substitutes for the ethical quality being explained.

A plural constitutive account can accept genuine recovery, enjoyment, reciprocal relationships and fair treatment, while explaining their favorable character through differentiated grounds within the same unfolding. Much of that account is compatible with this candidate. A unified explanation need not introduce one uniform property in addition to these realizations. If it makes the stronger claim that a common generative ground necessarily explains their ethical character, that explanatory dependence must be specified and defended. Merely describing the plural account as fragmented would not answer it.

The concurrently integrated [situated-discernment study](SITUATED-ETHICAL-DISCERNMENT.md) supplies a supporting connection: participation can disclose existing significance, form new practical relations and cultivate response. It does not require complete knowledge of all future consequences before ethical capacity is possible. Relevant evidence and comparison may warrant a particular judgment without deriving the universal identity of goodness. Successful participation alone does not certify every situated judgment, create its authority or make recognition necessary for goodness. This section inherits that study at its stated scope and leaves its source readings and proposed next test unchanged.

The remaining first-good obligation is to explain why the actual realization has its proposed favorable or adverse character. A convincing account may connect several differentiated explanations; it need not defeat an alternative that preserves the same explanation under another vocabulary. Where a stronger identity is claimed, state the dependence before applying it, retain the existing destructive and terminal cases, and specify what would defeat it. The practical implication above does not discharge this obligation. Universal necessity and the necessity of an additional irreducible ethical principle both remain unestablished.

## 35. Revising a pursuit that still succeeds

7 October 2026. Named connection: **situated understanding of consequences → warranted change of a concrete pursuit under a continuing whole-directed concern**. The [executed situated-discernment comparison](SITUATED-ETHICAL-DISCERNMENT.md#continuation-test-changing-a-successful-pursuit) supplies a supporting application to §34's practical bridge. It does not replace the central question of first ethical character with another test of technical competence.

The comparison preserves a meaningful shared performance that can actually improve and be completed. Under the specified available options, completing it entails serious, sustained impairment of one participant's ordinary living. A valid joint cancellation and feasible period of recovery forgo the original completion and both participants' participation in it. Earlier work, recovery costs and accrued harm remain represented. The candidate defends cancellation while acknowledging that it sacrifices a real good.

This verdict is an explicit ethical extension, not a deduction from §11's limited-enrichment case. That section reserved substantial completion for further comparison. Here the argument concerns whether the bounded achievement justifies the stated sustained burden, with the specified lack of a less burdensome completion route or outstanding third-party duty. A stronger significance for completion, a different burden, an adequate alternative or a defeated recovery premise requires renewed judgment. It is not a rule that rest always defeats art or that demanding voluntary activity is wrong.

The practical result separates genuine success at a concrete goal from warrant to keep pursuing it. The favored response changes that goal; it does not retrospectively declare that the abandoned performance occurred or that its earlier value disappeared. A stable wider ethical orientation can explain this revision in full. A rival can accept every fact while defending the completion's greater weight, so the disagreement cannot be dismissed as insufficient awareness or private selfishness.

This executes the prospective comparison in the situated study and extends the inherited practice-transformation application. It preserves an original goal that still succeeds, rather than merely a proxy that ceased to measure success. Because the two responses share the supplied understanding, it does not demonstrate learning or derive the correctness of ethical discernment from successful adaptation. The underlying favorable and adverse identifications, their bounded priority and legitimate revision powers remain supplied. The generative account still needs to explain their ethical character; universal necessity is not established by this application.

## 36. Descriptive dynamics and ethical justification

7 October 2026. While §35 was being reviewed, [Ren identified the limitation of the route itself](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-value-question-and-dynamics.md): the question concerns value, while the investigation describes dynamics. His exact words are preserved separately from this interpretation. This clarification changes the classification of further work; another conditional application is not the missing derivation of ethical necessity.

The performance comparison makes the distinction concrete. Its causal account specifies what completing the performance entails, what cancellation changes, what recovery requires and which achievements remain unrealized. Its ethical argument gives priority to preventing the specified serious impairment over the bounded shared completion. The latter is defended through normative considerations; it is not entailed merely by locating every effect inside the same unfolding. Review also required bounding the new support burdens on others, which improves the comparison's factual specification without supplying its ethical priority.

A more complete description can matter greatly to justification: it can disclose a hidden injury, restore an omitted good or identify an adequate alternative. But the relevant appraisal cannot be silently installed in terms such as genuine good, harmful or fitting and then announced as derived from a neutral description. The ordinary occurrence of evaluation within generativity also does not by itself establish that the evaluation is correct.

This is compatible with the nondual method. Explaining formation and defending ethical significance are different tasks concerning one reality, not proof of two independent substances. Some earlier models omit actual relations; repairing that omission is necessary where it changes the target. It still does not automatically establish the richer target's ethical character. A constitutive identity could be defended, but its defense is substantive philosophical work rather than another successful dynamical construction.

The earlier unified identity therefore retains candidate status. The dynamics-only route is insufficient as an answer to the value question. This does not show that all value questions are unanswerable or arbitrary, nor does it settle whether their ground requires a further irreducible principle. Subsequent research must explicitly distinguish a dynamical explanation, a conditional practical application, and a normative justification. The studies below or elsewhere must not receive first-ground credit merely for adding descriptive detail or repeating a favored example. The no-assumptions and universal-necessity ambitions are not reported as achieved.
