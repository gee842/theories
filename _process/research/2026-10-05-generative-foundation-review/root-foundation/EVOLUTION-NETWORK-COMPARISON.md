## The actual overlap with connectionism

Watson and colleagues' 2016 programme already addresses changes in developmental variation, ecological selection and reproductive relationships. Its models connect correlation learning with evolved organization, and it discusses both generalization and its limits. Reproductive organization and changing evolutionary units are therefore shared territory. Its integration section explicitly leaves a model freely joining all three forms of organization unfinished. These are claims about that publication, not a survey establishing the state of every subsequent model. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5119841/); [preserved original XML and reading record](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/comparative-direction/README.md#direct-comparison-reading).

The 2022 account investigates interaction structures supporting new individuality. Its authors identify remaining connections between collective characters, plasticity, reproduction and other individuating mechanisms. Our simple inherited bond model does not resolve that whole problem: it does not implement their correlation-learning or non-linearly separable developmental analysis. Their research cannot fairly be reduced to a network with fixed units or unlimited free resources. [Watson, Levin and Buckley](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2022.823588/full).

Thus three kinds of addition must stay distinct:

| Candidate contribution | Assessment after this comparison |
|---|---|
| Evolution changes relationships, variation and the units that evolve | Shared with the cited programme; not an established novelty of our theory |
| A particular actual sample carries inherited developmental state that rebuilds bonds under finite support | A checked connection in our account; representable with exactly the same commitments by the conventional comparator |
| A common explanatory sequence through formation, continuation, revision and concern | A broader integration project with completed conditional connections; comparative economy across those domains has not been demonstrated by this test |
| Ethical appraisal of what goals and selection rules produce | A substantive extension in question and commitments relative to the cited connectionist account; the completed G0/J argument is not derived from reproductive success or a biological prediction contest |

## Checked equality and its exact scope

The proof first establishes inverse state encodings and checks each primitive reaction and transfer. It then lifts these particular equalities through finite developmental words and mixed histories. The principal result is

`history events (encode world) = (DevelopmentalReconstruction.run events world).map encode`.

This equality includes `none`: an impossible sample, missing precursor or exhausted support fails in both implementations. Decoding recovers the original result, and every readout of that state agrees. No generic correspondence premise is assumed in place of checking the operations.

The adaptive theorem also allows an arbitrary supplied function choosing the next event from the current full state. Both implementations receive the same selector, interpreted through the encoding, and agree after every finite horizon. This prevents explaining the correspondence away as an artifact of a fixed timetable. It does not derive the selector's origins, establish stochastic population stability, or prove indefinite continuation. A new restriction on the selector would be an additional commitment to compare.

## What follows for explanatory contribution

The exact equivalence rules out presenting this particular reconstruction example as a predictive victory over an adequately informed network account. Adding support, inherited traits or real sampling only to our side would create an information advantage. With those relations provided equally, they constrain both accounts equally. The matched implementations also have the same supplied laws; fewer independently fitted commitments have not been earned here.

This does not show that explanatory frameworks have value only when their mathematics is inexpressible elsewhere. A framework can justify a mechanism, reveal a missing relation, unify results or support justified transfer even when another notation can express it. This test establishes representational and predictive equality under the matched assumptions. It neither proves that our whole framework is redundant nor awards it an unmeasured economy. A genuine further contribution must identify which supplied relation the theory now derives or constrains and why that constraint survives a fair comparison.

The surviving present claim is therefore precise: **we provide a connected, checked account of inherited reconstruction and its conditions, situated within a broader generative explanation and explicit ethical argument.** We have not added a demonstrated new biological law in this comparison. Causal revision of selection conditions remains shared territory; warranted appraisal and correction of the ends selecting intelligence is a different contribution.

The completed [practical judgment](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md) and [alignment-direction argument](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION.md) use G0 plus disclosed J1–J4 and correspondence requirements. The [connected goal-revision result](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md#current-generative-alignment-priority) already joins acquired goals, understood consequences, operative revision and finite reform. These completed results are reused, not reopened. ## Substantive counterreview

**The comparator is deliberately a re-expression, so why expect a difference?** We should not. It tests whether the current specific mechanism exceeds conventional representability with the same information, an implication readers might take from the previous contribution claim. Its equality is a useful correction of that implication, but cannot replace an empirical contest between independently motivated models. No universal superiority or inferiority conclusion follows.

**A conventional network is not the published connectionist theory.** Correct. The literature comparison establishes the conceptual overlap; the executable comparison establishes one exact conventional realization. They are separate arguments. This study does not claim a Lean equivalence to Watson's recurrent regulatory equations or validate his hypotheses about individuality.

**Could the finite cases miss an exceptional history?** Yes, numerical checks alone could. The correspondence theorem quantifies over arbitrary finite event histories and full states; the execution checks test the implemented adapter and separate representation. The theorem is still conditional on the specified local rules and shared selector. It proves neither that those rules exhaust real biology nor that a process continues forever.

**Does equality eliminate the contribution of explicit support or reconstruction?** No. Those relations can improve an account that omits them. The point is that the improvement belongs to the retained mechanism and is available to the adequately informed rival. The source of an explanatory advantage would need a further justified restriction or integration, not the removal of information from that rival.

This is a substantive author counterreview, not an independent-agent acceptance. No new external review or experiment is claimed.

## The empirical comparison remains a specific unresolved connection

The existing [microscopy audit](MICROSCOPY-CORRESPONDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) recovers scale and acquisition history but does not establish treatment-to-culture identity or later descendants. The separate lineage experiments have linked outcomes, but no verified common developmental record connects the two programmes. Combining them would supply a fabricated joint history. Existing inspected outcomes also cannot become an untouched test set after model selection.

The minimum complete test record must join the following for the same cultures and actual transfers:

1. Culture identity, inherited state, dated batch, treatment and inoculation history.
2. Calibrated structural observations with actual acquisition times and independently specified uncertainty in formation, loss and reformation.
3. The actual terminal criterion, sampled material and transfer allocation, including unsuccessful attempts.
4. Viable descendant formation and inherited redevelopment under the recorded support conditions.
5. A batch or intervention combination excluded from fitting, with the candidate restrictions and both rivals frozen before its outcomes are inspected.

The primary target remains complete-cycle continuation. Stage outcomes diagnose failure; batches, rather than movie frames, determine independent validation units. The equally informed conventional model receives the same measurements and fitting opportunity. A restriction counts as explanatory economy only if it replaces independently adjustable commitments without hiding them in an uncalibrated function, initial condition or data join.
