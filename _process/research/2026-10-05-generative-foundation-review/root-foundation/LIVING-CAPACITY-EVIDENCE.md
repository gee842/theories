# Partial organization, productive engagement and continued activity

8 October 2026. AI-authored primary-source assessment for the [living-dependencies continuation](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#8-organizing-relationships-and-actual-resumed-activity). This addresses **partially competent organization and joint support → sustained specified living activity**. It adds no mathematical theorem, numerical fit or experimental replication.

The useful advance is to distinguish the availability of participants, their actual participation and the duration and consequence of their activity. An account of continued living function must connect those relations. A count of retained structures alone cannot supply that connection; neither can a fitted measure of realized output automatically identify unused capacity.

Following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), catalyst, substrate and product are differentiated roles in the same transformations. The distinctions below preserve their joint occurrence and changing relationships. They do not introduce a self-contained organization facing fundamentally external support.

## 1. Primary evidence: productive engagement is inferred with a stated balance

**Dai et al., “Reduction of translating ribosomes enables Escherichia coli to maintain elongation rates during slow growth,” Nature Microbiology 2, 16231; online 12 December 2016, volume dated 2017.** [Primary manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5346290/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmicrobiol.2016.231).

The study measures elongation using induction assays corrected for initiation delay, with four additional fusion proteins supporting the LacZ result. Elongation remains above 9 amino acids/second at roughly 20-hour doubling and around 8 in early stationary phase. RNA/protein ratio, checked against proteomics, proxies ribosome abundance. An **active ribosome equivalent** is inferred from growth, protein content and elongation under steady exponential growth with negligible protein turnover. Its estimated fraction falls below 20% in slow growth; it is not a direct count of structurally competent particles. Abortive translation yielding unstable products counts as inactive. Sequestration, initiation inhibition and abortive translation remain possible explanations. Ternary-complex availability supplies a mechanistic interpretation of elongation; a fitted relationship does not identify one obligatory regulation route. The stationary measurement and steady-growth inference require separate scopes. The nutrient-upshift argument cites earlier work rather than providing a new recovery experiment. [Results, Fig. 1 and “Fraction of active ribosome equivalent”; Discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5346290/).

## 2. Primary model comparison: replacement need not increase net biomass

**Calabrese et al. (2022), “Protein degradation sets the fraction of active ribosomes at vanishing growth,” PLOS Computational Biology 18(5), e1010059.** [Primary article](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010059).

This theoretical study combines published ribosome/elongation data, including Dai, with degradation measurements from other studies. It is not an independent replication or a jointly measured recovery history. Its combined model includes inactive ribosomes and protein replacement: production can balance losses without net growth. “Maintenance” and “growth” partition synthesis, not fixed machine types. The reduction assumes balanced growth, negligible traffic/drop-off and specified binding kinetics; its bound/active identification is model-dependent. Turnover alone does not quantitatively explain the E. coli observations. Degradation estimates come from heterogeneous measurements; the authors interpolate across studies, acknowledge incomplete uncertainty and retain unresolved regulatory mechanisms. Their analysis identifies Dai's zero-growth point as early stationary phase reached after slow growth, not an indefinitely balanced zero-growth culture. These results warrant retaining replacement when interpreting output; they do not establish a positive degradation rate for every living constituent or interval. [Results, Boxes 1–2; Discussion; Methods, “Data sets” and “Data interpolation and extrapolation”](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010059).

## 3. Correspondence requirements for this programme

The following is our proposed interpretation discipline, not additional experimental findings or a newly proved formula.

| Question | What a correspondence must specify |
|---|---|
| What is present? | Which intact, impaired, assembled or separated configurations are counted, with preparation and observation uncertainty. |
| What could participate? | The operation and conditions under which a configuration is recruitable; “competent” is relative to these conditions. |
| What does participate? | Actual binding, activation and productive events, distinguished from potential recruitment. |
| What happens per participant? | Operation duration, completion, interruptions and reuse, rather than merely an available count. |
| What is produced? | The selected molecular or functional output and its units. An amino-acid incorporation rate is not a completed-protein count. |
| What continues? | The reached organization, retained products, loss, replacement and further enabling transformations over the declared horizon. |

For a prospective empirical interpretation of the formal batches, a batch must identify jointly assigned participants and shared inputs. A retained catalyst cannot be simultaneously assigned more copies than are available in that batch. Successive batches may reuse it after its release. This is an allocation question; a physical time interval additionally needs occupation durations and event timing. No number of abstract steps supplies seconds by itself.

A capacity bound and a measured utilization level therefore answer different questions. To use the latter to identify the former would require further observations or a justified recruitment model. In particular, an inferred small active fraction cannot simply be inserted as a permanently unavailable fraction of the formal organizing pool. Conversely, an available pool should not be assumed saturated merely because the model permits a productive event.

Actual output must also be connected to the target being explained. A trace producing a selected product need not preserve every relation required for another trace. Products can be retained, transformed, lost or involved in restoration. A continuation claim needs the reached conditions of its next activity, not only a cumulative output counter. This requirement leaves room for quiescent intervals, changed modes of functioning and limited episodes; it does not impose positive biomass growth on all living continuation.

## 4. Existing work and what would discriminate the next connection

The repository already has a [Weiße resource-allocation reconstruction](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/RESOURCE-ALLOCATION-BASELINE.md): its prior author inspected the primary paper, supplementary model and code, preserving substrate-dependent elongation, binding/release, growth closure and finite-batch accounting. This round reread that assessment, not those primary artifacts. Joint support and catalyst reuse are credited existing mechanisms, not discoveries of this note. Its historical algebraic validation scope is unchanged.

The [previous organization evidence](LIVING-ORGANIZATION-EVIDENCE.md) concerns B. subtilis protection, prepared translation and dimer resolution. Its preparations, organisms and interventions are not the E. coli histories here. A cross-study explanatory comparison is legitimate; combining the measured quantities into one supposedly observed trajectory is not.

The next useful correspondence would track **one specified activity across actual successive conditions**, measuring or bounding available configurations, engagement, completion time, shared inputs and relevant losses. A successful explanatory account should separate decreased recruitment from impaired participants or slower operation, and separate replacement from net accumulation. If different admissible mechanisms reproduce the selected measurements, the account must preserve that non-identification rather than choose its favored story by definition.

For the formal branch, jointly enabled operations and their actual ordering can advance this connection before a kinetic model is available. Any checked allocation/serialization result should be stated at that scope. A further claim about sustained biological throughput requires the timing and observation correspondence above; a further claim about self-maintenance requires actual production or preservation of the relevant enabling relations. These are identified explanatory obligations within the same process, not evidence that the generative account has substantively failed. Neither is universal ethical warrant established by this biological study.
