# When consequences change the organization of response

6 October 2026. AI-authored identification/comparator note under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **consequences of activity → retention or reconstruction of the organization carrying further response**. This reuses the existing empirical interface, adds one focused adverse comparator, and proposes no new model or experiment.

## The addition worth making

Consequence-dependent renewal and replacement are already established in the conditional [selector](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/SELECTOR-REVISION.md) and [updater](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UPDATER-FORMATION.md) constructions. Actual capture finances renewal; failure permits expiry and reconstruction within the continuing compartment. Ordinary controllers remain valid comparators. A fresh lifetime variable, successful-outcome renewal and random replacement would repeat that result.

The [current native history](ORGANIZATIONAL-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md) instead has an acquired port relation in an apparatus whose completed lifecycle cannot reopen through admitted events. A substantive addition would connect actual native consequences to the availability, retention or replacement of **that formed coupling**, preserving its history, occupied parts and differentiated support. It would identify a previously unjoined realization, not discover consequence-sensitive organization in general. A new law can supply such a realization conditionally; its origin is not explained merely by giving it a physical vocabulary.

Encounter, altered material, later sensitivity and renewed action belong to one continuing process. Distinguishing them allows causal identification; it does not require a detached evaluator or a metaphysical gap between organism and surroundings. Fixed laws can produce retained changes in organization. They need not rewrite themselves for the response to change.

## Primary evidence and its discriminating limit

The following capsules contain the paper-specific findings. Subsequent tests and interpretations are ours.

**Guan et al. (2012), already credited.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3454879/), DOI `10.1534/genetics.112.143016`. Salt-pretreated yeast retains peroxide resistance partly through long-lived Ctt1 protein. Separately, prior exposure accelerates later transcription and resistance reacquisition. NUP42 deletion impairs this acceleration, with no detected defect in basal resistance, initial acquisition or persistent protection in the reported assays. Controls address a gross export defect, but the precise memory mechanism remains unresolved. These results distinguish carried protective material from altered production of future protection; they do not manipulate experienced benefit while holding the inducing history fixed. Methods and Results for Figures 2–6 were reread.

**Goulev et al. (2017), targeted comparator.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5438251/), DOI `10.7554/eLife.23971`. Microfluidic peroxide steps, ramps and pulses in budding yeast were combined with growth, fluorescent reporters and genetic perturbations. Peroxiredoxin disruption and function-separating mutants support peroxide scavenging in ramp adaptation. Constitutive Tsa1 expression did not rescue the tested background, although its expression was lower, limiting that comparison. After pretreatment, the later transcriptional response was lower at a 100-minute interval and returned toward the naive response at 400 minutes as Tsa1 levels declined. A nonlinear feedback model explains enhanced buffering without faster transcription. This is evidence for changed protective capacity under an inherited network, not a uniquely identified new regulatory rule or a measured favorable-outcome-to-memory pathway. Reading: Results for Figures 4–6, reporter/mutant methods and mathematical-model assumptions. No raw data were reanalyzed.

These different protocols are not a failed replication of each other. Together they sharpen the question: **what has changed that accounts for the later response?** A larger retained stock, altered response production and a replaced input–action relation are different possible answers. Neither better performance nor the word “memory” settles which one occurred. Retained protective material can be a genuine acquired change in effective organization without establishing the stronger acquisition of a revised rule.

The [earlier evidence note](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-EVIDENCE.md) supplies the complementary physical-feedback and survival-selection comparisons. Its mechanisms must not be concatenated with these findings into one observed complete lineage. No further literature branch is needed for this distinction.

## A bounded causal comparison

For the native continuation, identify the actual path from a response's consequence back into the organization that carries later response. An independently reported success score cannot stand in for this path. Conversely, a resource or chemical state is not an illicit reward merely because it influences retention: establish its actual origin, use and effects.

Three comparisons distinguish the claims:

1. **Retention rather than only current stimulation.** Follow an encounter into a retained alteration and a later encounter. Preserve the relevant present inputs and account for residual stimulation, stored material and inherited support. Do not demand literally identical complete states: the proposed retained alteration is precisely a difference to test. Nor should an analysis remove that alteration and then infer that history did nothing.
2. **Consequence dependence rather than cue induction alone.** Where physically meaningful, keep the prior cue and attempted response comparable while changing their actual productive coupling. Trace how the changed consequence reaches the component's retention or reconstruction. Any added signal, damage or resource difference belongs in that account; it cannot be hidden by saying all else was equal. Disconnecting the return path should affect the claimed organizational change, with direct impairment and alternative routes considered.
3. **Altered subsequent operation rather than a surviving marker.** Show what later activity the retained or reconstructed organization changes. Interrupting its use should discriminate the proposed mechanism from passive carryover or a direct present-input policy. If carryover explains the full effect, that is a positive material-memory result at its scope, not evidence of an additional learned rule. Differential survival or copying can itself be real selection, but a population shift is not automatically within-lineage revision.

These are alternative identification demands tied to the stated claim, not three mandatory modules in every adaptive process. A simple feedback explanation may be sufficient. More elaborate representation is justified only when it explains a distinction the simpler account misses.

For this particular source history, useful outcomes might maintain a response-bearing component, permit its reconstruction, or change its relation to the next encounter. Each would require declared events and a complete account of the actual successor. Funding a component by silently converting inaccessible source into common support would change the central native dependency. At the final depleted boundary, a replacement cannot be credited without an admitted means of realizing it. Earlier installation, reuse and reconstruction remain possible routes if their material history is shown.

The existing updater counterexample also matters: a component can receive renewal from an inherited successful selector before it has itself governed reconstruction. A renewed component is therefore not automatically selected for its own tested contribution. Provenance of the productive effect and subsequent use must be retained whenever that stronger attribution is made.

## What would advance or revise the account

The worthwhile positive result is a **same-history causal join from native consequence to changed response-bearing organization and then changed later activity**, with its inherited architecture and adequate conventional alternatives explicit. The empirical comparators constrain what that join must identify; they do not validate a synthetic implementation as biological fact.

A preserved component that never affects later operation would narrow the result to retention. A changed output fully explained by current substrate would narrow it to immediate regulation. A cue-induced lasting change without identified feedback from its realized outcome would establish acquired responsiveness at that narrower scope. A fully observed return-dependent change would support the stronger stabilization/reconstruction claim. All can occur under fixed laws; none alone derives the whole origin or ethical quality of the response.

The [whole-process account](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-INTEGRATION.md) continues to govern ethical interpretation. An organization can stabilize a response that burdens other activity. Local support, persistence and effective reconstruction do not supply a universal favorable direction, nor complete concern or alignment.
