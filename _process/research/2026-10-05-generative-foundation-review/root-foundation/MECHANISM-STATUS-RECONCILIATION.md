# Existing mechanisms and the corrected unfinished list

7 October 2026. AI-authored repository reconciliation following [Ren's observation](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-existing-mechanisms-status-correction.md). This is a review of existing explanations, constructions and verification, not a new mechanism, experiment or proof.

**Finding: several capabilities described too broadly as unfinished already exist.** In particular, the tree contains physical contact formation, constructed contextual recording, tests of experimental access, acquired calibration, local architecture construction, revision of candidate generators, consequence-selected inquiry and repeated supported succession. The last decoder study is one additional realized connection, not the first explanation of all these capacities.

The correction is to the status inference: **a primitive supplied to one model is not, by that fact, a mechanism unexplained throughout the theory.** Conversely, another model's result does not automatically establish this particular apparatus's history. The [shared method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) already requires this distinction. Subjects, contacts, reference conditions, construction and the investigation itself remain differentiated activities within the account; a local law is not an outside cause merely because it is an input to one representation.

## Connections that already compose must remain closed

The [composition map](FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) already separates actual joined histories from conditional transports and separate realizations. Material part formation through selector/updater operation, recipient acquisition through contextual recording and subsequent use, and actual learned succession have existing joined results. Their completion is not revoked because another example chooses different primitives.

The [five-condition assessment](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) accepted the conditional candidate, including bounded concern and wisdom, and explicitly identified no additional mandatory foundational construction. This reconciliation preserves that judgment. It neither converts it into general moral reliability nor turns each later extension into an unfinished prerequisite for the already accepted candidate.

A request for one stronger concrete history still requires compatible interfaces for **that claim**: target meanings, event laws, retained information, constructed parts and actual support. The right response is to locate its missing interface, if any, rather than reopen all mechanisms or demand a single universal apparatus containing every branch. No new generic contact, selector, planner or renewal experiment is justified merely by the list reviewed here.

## What remains after the correction

**The particular reference arrangement.** The latest decoder's forced zero/one jig, reference labels and same-transducer target coupling are explicit local laws. The studies reviewed here explain related formation, recording and access-testing capacities; they do not yet supply a verified realization of this exact combination from that retained relay ancestor. This is a claim-specific implementation/derivation boundary. It is not a finding that all reference formation or sensing remains unexplained. A more modest claim can retain that local law as an application condition without pretending its origin was derived.

**The actual support failure.** The new fixed sequence can reach the correct failure diagnosis but cannot retain its apparatus. The deficit and misleading-reference cases stay accepted. Existing renewal contracts explain how other funded continuations can work; they do not create resources in this depleted history. Whether a different compatible realization or schedule can accomplish the same required retained result remains a specific question, not a newly missing universal renewal theory.

**Class conditions and identification.** Ren's [active universal target](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) is conditional universality: independently specified law properties imply substantive consequences for admitted processes. Applying those implications requires justifying the system's correspondence and showing that conditions do not secretly contain the capacity to be explained. It does not require deriving every physical law or proving one uniquely necessary ontology. The stronger [no-assumptions directive](ASSUMPTION-ELIMINATION-AUDIT.md) has not been proved satisfied or silently waived; an empty Lean axiom report is not its fulfillment. Its status must be distinguished from completion under the conditional target.

**Practical and ethical significance.** Existing mechanisms explain acquired relevance, concern-related responses and bounded warranted applications. They do not turn information gain, persistence or a working reader into universal ethical warrant. The broader [whole-process ethical investigation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md) retains its own claims and evidence. This mechanism audit does not reopen completed bounded applications or settle that stronger investigation.

## Corrected research practice

Before reporting another unfinished mechanism, identify the exact claim, the strongest existing derivation, its accepted scope and the concrete relationship that remains unestablished. Mark a local input as a local input; mark a missing composition as a missing composition; call something an unresolved explanation of the wider theory only after checking both the core and the relevant branches. Preserve a genuine failure rather than treating every unfavorable regime as missing machinery.

The immediate outcome of Ren's correction is therefore **integration and narrower status reporting**. The existing results above remain available for reuse. Further construction should follow a named stronger claim and a demonstrably missing interface, not an ever-receding generic list of sensing, learning, construction and renewal.
