## Contribution to the universal question

The proposal makes the scope and shape of evaluation something to explain within generative activity. An organized perspective forms through bodily activity, encounters, memory, practices and their continuing effects. Its surroundings and its own organization differentiate within that history; neither is an independently complete starting point. Activity can change both the conditions of further activity and how those conditions will subsequently be encountered and evaluated.

The universal inference under test remains **whole realization → justified ethical quality → practical reasons across differently formed intelligences**. Explaining why perspectives differ helps distinguish recognition, endorsement and implementation from warrant. It does not require universal reasons to produce identical judgments or behavior. It also exposes a possible failure: a claimed universal ground may actually be one formed weighting silently imposed on all others. The proposal supports investigating this inference; it does not settle it.

## What the field represents

These are distinguishable roles in one history, not separate causal and ethical realities:

| Role | Question | What an apparent boundary can miss |
|---|---|---|
| Actual consequence reach | Through which relations does an activity change subsequent conditions and realizations? | Unnoticed effects, delayed consequences, effects surviving the initiator. |
| Cognitive access and importance | What is registered, represented, anticipated or attended to? | A dependency may be unrepresented; a threat may be highly salient without being valued positively. |
| Felt or endorsed good | What is experienced favorably, preferred or treated as worth pursuing? | These can differ from one another and from accurate prediction. |
| Warranted whole appraisal | Which features of the actual continuation justify favorable or adverse judgment and practical response? | Neither a successful preference nor a wide field of attention establishes this warrant. |

A hard cutoff, smooth distance decay, several peaks, or strongly directional weights are possible representations of a specified role. A Gaussian-shaped function need not be a probability distribution; normalization, a metric and additive aggregation are further choices. Relational distance might concern dependency, familiarity or a learned category rather than geography. Which relation is intended must be specified. A reach relation may facilitate or impair continuation; cognitive importance is not necessarily affection, and preferential weights may favor another's loss. The proposed family therefore exceeds a single positive radial function.

This also distinguishes **good reaching me** from **another's good mattering to me**. The latter need not promise a return. Genuine self-good remains a realization within the whole and needs no separate outward payoff. Nor is whole appraisal simply the limit of an ever-wider self: it must retain differentiated effects without inventing a larger owner or one aggregate score.

Changing only the analyst's boundary preserves the actual history; changing an enacted perspective can change activity and its later ethical grounds. A broad perspective can disclose an existing loss, form a new relationship, or support more effective domination. Those outcomes must be distinguished.

## Trajectories and karma

Ren's proposed reading continues the existing causal-temporal account: **果为后因**. An activity changes what follows; those consequences participate in forming further activity. This can include changed capacities, habits, trust, dependence, practices, evaluative fields and the possibilities later participants actually encounter. Effects can branch, interfere, dissipate or persist after the initiator disappears. No guaranteed return, deserved suffering or automatic favorable endpoint follows.

The [future-field account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#future-fields-and-their-causal-organization) already tracks changes in whole continuations. Here the added emphasis is that a trajectory also transforms how it will be evaluated and which evaluations acquire influence. A trajectory may be becoming more capable of reproducing its own approving judgment while worsening what that judgment omits.

“Generative model manifold” is a useful provisional image. A formal manifold, smooth motion, fixed dimension or a global gradient is not established. Formation, dissolution and branching may require a different mathematical representation. Begin with a structured space of histories and retain the relations relevant to the question. Trace both realized quality and changed conditions for further realization; neither opportunity count nor endless productivity is a general measure of good. When effects conflict or long-run direction is uncertain, retain that structure rather than manufacture a scalar karma balance.
