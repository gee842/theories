# Formed practical boundaries: reconstruction of the existing histories

6 October 2026. AI-authored study under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **formed practical scope → encounter with a consequence its evaluation omits → revision of that scope, evaluation and response within the continuing history**. This note reads existing sources and their accepted evidence; it adds no controller, theorem, simulation or new certification of old checks.

**Finding:** the strongest existing mechanism already forms and revises effective recipient influence through paid encounters. It can also revise that influence toward exclusion while accurately retaining recipient consequences and means knowledge. The useful next inquiry concerns the justification and revisability of its inclusion criterion. Another example of a mutable coupling would repeat an established result.

## 1. What the fresh nondual work already covers

The contemporaneous [tree reconstruction](NONDUAL-TREE-CONTINUATION.md) and [mechanism note](NONDUAL-TREE-MECHANISM.md) already recover the actual `AcquiredPerspective` history: a real action produces a receipt, its paid recording changes a later command, and the subsequent effect renews the same organization. Reading the [source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/AcquiredPerspective.lean) confirms that the two-action forecast is an analytical readout of the existing score; the runtime command already uses that score. Neither the readout nor its exactness theorem creates a new target identity, subjective perspective or ethical ground.

[NondualFormation](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/noninstrumental-concern/NondualFormation.lean) supplies a different result. Links and active sources generate overlapping effective supports; cutting necessary links changes capacity, and a formerly affected node becomes a necessary contributor after rewiring. Those capacities are read from actual configured relations, rather than primitive Actor and Patient types. Its event repertoire has no acquired record, prediction update or evaluative selection. The node carrier, link/cut law and event sequence remain local inputs. The absence of a joint realization with the paid learner is an uncompleted composition, not evidence that the reconstructed theory fundamentally separates activity and significance.

These two results should retain their present ownership and scope. They neither need to be rediscovered here nor silently concatenated with the recipient histories below.

## 2. The connected learner already encounters a consequence its recommendation omits

The [connected model](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/CONNECTED-GENERATIVE-MODEL.md) follows an actual donor and distinct successor, fresh recipient inquiry, acquired command evidence, paid evaluation and actual delivery. Its selected successor retains the donor's acquired `n=7`; fresh acquisition changes the child's command while the retired donor remains unchanged. The single recipient account and remaining funds are carried through the history.

This matters because inquiry itself can deliver the relevant resource. Under the independently supplied one-service episode interpretation, the actual positive inquiry frame already supports fulfillment before the later selected delivery. The evidence is not absent. The actual inquiry raises recipient stock to 70; the subsequent selected work raises it to 80. These quantities are resource transfers, not measurements of welfare.

The [completion source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/CompletionApplicability.lean) identifies an exact operational dependency. `help` reads only the fixed route and retained native coefficient. Every admitted successor event preserves both, so `help_invariant_on_every_trace` holds even while factual registers, publications and technical permission change. This is an actual limitation of this particular law: it cannot make its recommendation completion-sensitive. It does not establish that a richer nondual account cannot relate fulfillment and further response.

Three distinctions follow. The acquired record can be available without affecting the relevant recommendation. A technical permission cut can block work without revising that recommendation. And learning a new command is not a revision of which recipient condition presently calls for a response. A proof-level claim certificate can clarify historical fulfillment without constituting a paid runtime update. The existing [applicability study](COMPLETION-APPLICABILITY-MECHANISM.md) already exposes these distinctions; they are not new discoveries of this note.

## 3. The stronger existing formation-and-revision history

The [accepted plasticity integration](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY-INTEGRATION.md) is the more useful adjacent mechanism for the present question. It uses the actual `RevisionWitness.consumed` predecessor with declared protected preparation and resources. Initially two materially backed sockets are empty and their conductance is zero. Paid own and recipient operations create consequences; separate paid reads acquire their shortfalls; paid binding forms signed connections. Later transmission through those connections changes the paid assessment and possible service to the same recipient.

The [operative source][plasticity] makes the dependencies explicit:

- `recipientPay` deducts two units from the existing recipient account; an actual return can restore two. Own operations have a separate account and source.
- The faithful reads convert actual return bits into loss bits. Missing recipient acquisition blocks binding and native writing; it is not silently converted into a known absence of loss.
- Recipient loss enables a binding or replacement. For the active route, `learnedSign` is positive when own loss is recorded and negative otherwise. The recipient result gates the operation; the own result determines the sign.
- The acquired native coefficient updates as `n/2 + 4*ownLoss`. Assessment compares `10 * n * (sign₀ + sign₁)` with 64. Resource costs, current command support, permission and one-use authorization still constrain execution.

Thus the operative field is not an analyst's description alone. Empty or occupied signed sockets causally affect assessment, and their formation consumes real modeled components and fuel. But the positions to which they connect, the loss meters, plasticity rule, threshold and schedule are supplied by this local realization. Saying so locates its explanatory start; it does not place those relations outside generative reality. The empirical and ethical interpretation of a recipient shortfall is not generated by calling the state field `patient`.

## 4. Locate the omission before asking for its correction

At least four practical boundaries can be distinguished within these configurations:

| Boundary at issue | What the existing model does | What a different claim would require |
|---|---|---|
| Effective ability to affect a position | Formed links and actual event guards determine capacities. | A common physical history joining the overlapping-support and recipient-learning realizations. |
| Access to consequences | Paid recipient acquisition and retained, addressed records distinguish outcomes. | Formation of a new target or access path if the relevant consequence is genuinely unrepresented. |
| Influence on assessment | Plasticity forms signed transmission; equal-information controls expose downstream exclusion. | An explanation of when this influence is appropriate, not merely present. |
| Criterion governing inclusion and revision | The given own-loss rule assigns signs; the connected successor's assessment inputs remain fixed. | A warranted revision of that rule or its applicability, carried into actual selection with its history and costs intact. |

In the plasticity witness, recipient effects have already entered causal activity, data acquisition and learning. The omission is therefore not wholesale invisibility of another center. It is the way the specified inclusion rule makes recipient influence depend on the learner's own loss. In the fifth pair, accurate recipient loss can help install an inhibitory connection precisely because no own loss accompanies it. Calling this an omitted consequence means that it is not granted the proposed patient-directed practical significance; it does not mean that the source erased the observation.

Nor should the update be called a prediction error without a corresponding predictive claim. An associative action policy can implement the given rule consistently. The ethical challenge is whether that rule appropriately answers the actual recipient condition, not whether its code miscalculates its own threshold. If the target were merely correlation-dependent conductance, this history succeeds. If the fuller target is warranted response to independently identified adversity, the consequence needs an additional account of practical significance. That account is to be investigated within the same situation, rather than appended as an unrelated observer's score.

## 5. A concrete next configuration toward the ethical minimum

Use the paired-loss configuration to fix the question. Keep the actual affected position, its continuing condition, the possible alleviating transformation, accurate acquired evidence, remaining means and permissions in view. Ask whether the own-loss-dependent rule is an adequate way of treating that condition. For this proposed criterion-correction claim, the encounter would need to change the rule that selects evidence's influence, not merely run its existing update again. Other warranted corrections may change only an application's current status while properly preserving its criterion.

The positive route needs two connected kinds of explanation. First, identify what the loss and possible service actually do for the recipient, including continuity of the affected process and any unintended burden. Second, defend why that difference supplies patient-directed favor and why a lack of corresponding own loss does not remove it. The latter is the ethical work. The former prevents an apparent revision from merely changing reports, increasing a proxy, appropriating the recipient, or transferring the cost elsewhere. Shared unfolding preserves these differentiated consequences; it does not turn them into the learner's private stock.

The existing [repair argument](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md#an-independently-stated-repair-ground) already supplies a bounded foothold because the teaching operation itself imposed the shortfall. Under warranted deprivation, answerability, remedy and comparison premises, it supports repair and already diagnoses the active replacement as an inappropriate revision. That application is inherited, not a new result of this reconstruction. Responsibility may belong to a deploying operator or institution; the learner's state fields do not establish its own responsibility. This route does not by itself establish the [universal minimum](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) for an otherwise independent responder. The noncausing, nonreciprocating case remains an explicit extension to defend, not something excluded by redefining eligibility.

A future same-history realization would have to preserve the acquired consequence and its target while changing the operative inclusion criterion through an identifiable, affordable process. It should discriminate real alleviation from report suppression and from the learner's own gain; retain a separate independent ground when another service is fulfilled; and preserve permission restrictions. These are proposed identification and realization obligations, not accomplished tests or additional moral axioms. There is no reason to start by constructing another isolated controller when the existing histories already locate these interfaces.

## 6. Result, limits and conditions of revision

This reconstruction changes the choice of next study. Generic formation of recipient influence, its plastic replacement and its downstream causal use are already established at a conditional scope. The complementary task is **consequential encounter → justified correction of the criterion governing practical scope**, with a causal continuation when implementation is claimed. A criterion can be genuinely formed and genuinely revisable while remaining ethically inadequate.

The selected plasticity and successor histories are siblings with different states and resource designs. The plasticity construction has not become the connected successor's new update by being cited beside it. AcquiredPerspective's own-support history and NondualFormation's support graph are likewise relevant contracts, not their undisclosed ancestors. This is missing composition in the representation. The justification of the own-loss rule, or an alternative patient-directed criterion, is an unresolved explanatory question. A preserved-target case showing the defended criterion misdescribes real adversity, permissions or consequences would instead be evidence against that specified account.

The present source-level finding would need correction if the accepted plasticity law in fact changed its own sign-selection criterion, discovered its recipient boundary rather than starting with it, or if the two advertised comparison states failed to share the acquired data and accounting claimed. The ethical interpretation would need revision if the modeled resource change did not realize the independently identified adverse condition, or if proposed relief lacked the stated effect or permission. These are determinate challenges, not unrestricted toggles of a moral predicate.
