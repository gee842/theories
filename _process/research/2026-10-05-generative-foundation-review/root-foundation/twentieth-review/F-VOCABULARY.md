## 1. Summary

1. **The words the main claims are stated in carry several meanings each (major).** "Whole" means three things: the space of all worlds (ch:60, "The whole is every world"), all the formations of one world (§6, "significance bounded by the whole"), and the theory's content (the necessity target). "Condition" means the relatum role, the continuation condition, the local hypotheses of a law, and a status ("a condition, not an axiom"). "Reading" has at least 8 senses, among them the status label "[AI reading]" and the Lean `reading`. "Law" is both a dynamics (`Law`, `ExchangeLaw`) and a theorem that holds of every world. "Selection" has at least 5 senses, among them subsets of premises and dependent choice. The same goes for "closure/close" (≥6), "keep" (≥7), "reach" (≥5), "rest" (≥4) and "return" (≥4, three Lean `Returns`).
2. **The primitives have no glossary rows (major).** None of these has a row in the 202-row glossary: *unfolding*, *relatum*, *formation*, *condition* (as a role), *level*, *need*, *support*, *standpoint*, *living*, *coupling / couples into* (D45: "coupling is the primitive"), *observation*, *generation*, *loss*, *law*, *history*, *pool* (149 uses), *seeking*, *mutual dependence*. "Relation" is defined only as the claim type of one reading of `living` (ch:1735). The Lean name `Unfolding` belongs to a legacy record with static dependence (`deeper/RelationalRenewal.lean:104`, 28 uses in core), not to the one unfolding.
3. **"Generation" breaks the theory's own rule (major).** AGENTS.md and D2 reserve "generation" for widening, the opposite of degeneration. Core uses `generation_*` (31 declaration names) and the module `Becoming.Generation` for embodied **arising**. Chapter §2, "Continued generation" (ch:407), still gives the superseded reading of the second 生 as arising, which D3 (revised 19 Oct) replaced.
4. **The ethics is still laid out in rounds, not as one account (major).** Section 6 follows the dates of its rounds. Six anchors (§6.1, §6.8–§6.12) still carry `g0`/`axiom`/`p39`–`p44`. The no-axiom order (widening, §6.13) comes after the conditional material. The point that living significance "follows immediately, by instantiation" is restated about 13 times. The whole `Valuation`/`G0` apparatus no longer enters any comparison. Once written out, the account has two premises: a scope premise (the continuation condition, which is the presupposition plus the step of invariance) and an order premise (C: widening is better). The text blurs them in three ways:
   - it says "what ranks lies in C **and in the step of invariance**" (ch:30, ch:1159, AGENTS.md);
   - it says the content of "calling the generative space good" **is** the step of invariance (ch:1125, ch:1430);
   - it says "C follows from the condition" by reading "better" as retaining and adding (ch:160, ch:997). That hides the ranking weight in the gloss of "better".
5. **Some live sentences contradict D1's revision.** §8.3's first bullet says "Living significance and C share this reading [of 生生]" (ch:1367); §8.4 and the glossary say `living` does not read 生生. Target 2 says 生生 acquires normative significance "through living significance" (ch:38). §7.5 says "one evaluation … by living significance" (ch:1282), while its own "Under what" paragraph says the ranking comes from C read as widening.
6. **The nondual vocabulary is mostly sound but has pockets.** Lean declaration names: about 0.8% carry social, valuative or financial tokens. Module names: 10 of 297 (3.4%). In a sample of 12 analytic distinctions, 8 give their relational basis. The ungrounded ones cluster in ethics and the collective: `Pos` is a primitive type of standpoints separate from formations; the chapter uses "free rider", "winning by consumption", "rules the winners write", "the game of relative position" and "the patient's side" (ch:163). By D54's own test, "a contest or rank" is not relational. Chinese terms are disciplined, but they conflict on 停息 versus 静 (D45(4) against D49(4) and ch:276; three different `RestsFrom`). 消耗 names both consuming and depletion.

## 3. What is sound and should be kept

- **Chapter prose has largely dropped the retired labels.** "Part I/II" appears only in the glossary (verified: 2 occurrences, both in the retired-labels table), and "G0" appears only as a Lean name. The single "Retired labels" table is the right device.
- **Lean names are overwhelmingly structural** (≈99% of declaration names). N5's table-driven renaming, with no aliases and statements unchanged, is the right precedent for F3 and F9.
- **The premise card** (§0) states the representation's commitments separately from premises, with standing. This is the model the ethics section should follow.
- **D45's "one account"** is the right standard, and its five links (one unfolding, generation, continuation and extent, knowing, alignment) are a good skeleton for the core vocabulary. The decomposition of the condition (§6.11) is exact and well placed in the literature.
- **The Chinese edition's 展开 / 形成 / 生成 split** and its separation of 可达 from 所及 and 封闭/闭包/闭合 are more disciplined than the English. The English should copy them.
- **The closing/opening crosswalk** (§10) maps earlier vocabulary onto laws and leaves every verdict out ("稳 and 元闭 are one structure with two verdicts"). It is exemplary nondual practice.
- **"`own` is a region, not an owner" and "relations, not rights"** are carried consistently. The theory has no ownership vocabulary in Lean (verified: no `owner` token).
