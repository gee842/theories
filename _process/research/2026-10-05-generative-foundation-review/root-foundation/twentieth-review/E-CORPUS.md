# Review E: redundancy and coherence of the document corpus and records

Reviewer E, twentieth review. Read-only, on `main` at 121ff8042 (main moved to b52f142b3 during the review; the counts below come from the working tree as read). Every count was produced by a read-only script or `git`/`grep` over the tree. Scratch: `scripts/E/names.py` (declaration-name index over the Lean sources). "Verified" means counted or read. "Judgment" marks an elegance call.

## 1. Summary

1. **Three or four layers say the same thing, and generated navigation outweighs the theory.** The English chapters total 3.2 MB (the canonical one alone 0.74 MB, about 99k words). Generated result views total 9.0 MB, record pages 6.7 MB and the study's top-level Markdown 7.6 MB. The canonical chapter states the whole four times ("In one sentence", "The theory stated in full", the "Premise card", "The theory stated whole"). Four module catalogues describe the same ~300 modules. Several live generated pages are snapshots of 8 October. (E1, E2)
2. **84% of record history is bookkeeping.** `history` is 58% of all record JSON bytes (8.8 of 15 MB). 9,647 of 11,541 history entries are rebind, repoint, drift or receipt notes. `census-balance` alone has 145. RESULT-WORKFLOW asks for one note per round and none for untouched records. (E3)
3. **Retired vocabulary survives through identifiers and secondary catalogues, not through the chapter.** 20 record IDs carry G0, axiom, Part I, registration or correction. The Lean README still describes modules in "registration" terms (20 times, e.g. the `Becoming.Selection` row). The non-verbatim parts of the REFERENCE file do too (114 times). Seven unmaintained Chinese editions carry G0, one under the title "how an adopted axiom is supported". The canonical glossary (§10) itself agrees with the Lean code (556 of 565 anchors resolve) and with its Chinese edition. (E4, E5, E6)
4. **Records split law families by landing and never use supersession.**
   - Families split across records: counting and census (≥11 records over 7 modules), sums over histories (4), a law along one run (3), settling ⇔ LPO (3), the laws of one step (3, two of them with contradictory titles).
   - No supersession: 0 of 279 records are `superseded`. 53 records still bind proofs to the 75 superseded periphery modules. One record's canonical source is the retired bridge.
   (E7, E8)
5. **The study and the working tree hold much executed or finished state.**
   - Of 135 top-level entries in the study, about 25 are live working state.
   - `development/` holds 64 landed-item kits (1,143 tracked files), plus 227 MB of ignored `.build` output.
   - Executed plans and answered decision briefs are still live.
   - 34 worktrees use 121 GB; 26 sit at heads already in main. (E10, E11, E12)
6. **Proposed minimal corpus** (§5): 9 root pages, 1 opening, 1 canonical chapter with a merged whole-statement, 1 glossary, about 18 statement-source chapters, records with compacted history, one module table and one generated register. Move about 14 chapters, 3 generated catalogues, about 60 study files and 64 development folders to history. Docs and tooling only, no Lean. Roughly 4–6 working days in all.
