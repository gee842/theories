## 1. Summary

1. **No completion criterion can be checked today.** "Unified, complete, enclosed, zero-axiom" is Ren's phrase (sources/…/2026-10-11-one-unified-theory-together.md), and the board uses it as its target (`_process/BECOMING-WORKBOARD.md:11`). Only "zero-axiom" has a check, the core axiom report. "Enclosed" is defined nowhere. "Complete" points to the four criteria of plan §9, written 9 October 2026. Nobody has audited them since, and criterion 4 still names a "Ren's choice" class that D45 abolished. Item 11 (ch:88) and "What this completeness is" (ch:1196) still claim completeness in that sense. §3 of the findings (D1) proposes a seven-part criterion that a script can check.
2. **The two halves of evaluation do not meet on the spine.** The ranking half, C read as widening (`Web.paths_comparison_widens`, ValuationFrames.lean:443), holds "for every reading of `living`", and `paths_extension_iff_widens` sets `living := True`. So the evaluative object `living` (active and not undermining), the subject of living significance, plays no part in any ranking. The one theorem in the core that relates undermining to the generative space is a counterexample: `not_undermining_yet_loses` (Significance.lean:557) gives a living event that narrows what can form. Widening also has no theorem linking it to renewing/consuming or to arising/ceasing (a scan of every statement found 0). D2's reading, "renewing and consuming at the level of formations", has no theorem behind it.
3. **Evaluation does not form within the unfolding on the spine.** Standpoints, regions and significance are supplied parameters. `Valuation (Pos C)` takes a separate type of standpoints (Metaethics.lean:130), and `Web.valuation` says "Standpoints, their regions and significance remain supplied" (Metaethics.lean:250–253). §5 is titled "How evaluation forms", but its evidence is history chapters and periphery records. "How a center forms" is listed as open in four places in §8.
4. **The modelling choices do work in places labelled "no premise".** Examples: `rising_recurs` ("in every unfolding … keep falling keep rising", ch:255), the §2 headline principle `falls_not_recurring` (ch:371), and `excess_bounded` ("joint excess cannot outlast what was held", ch:969). All three are facts about the well-foundedness of ℕ, and real amounts falsify them (the chapter's own `Halving` is the counterexample). The generative space counts only formations of a type fixed in advance, admitted at given thresholds.
5. **What would most advance the theory as a whole:** (A) restate the spine's laws over every law (or relation) of exchange, which means a definitional join of the two base families. This is the formal content of the first target, which §8.1 itself names. (B) Join the generative space to the dynamical classifications and to the evaluative object, so that one chain runs from the unfolding to the condition, to C, and on to alignment. The alignment direction (§7.5) is still bound to a 7 October periphery toy with ten theorems whose premise is "G0".

**The one or two items that would most advance the theory as a whole**

**(A) State the spine's laws over every law (or relation) of exchange, so the two base families join by definition.** My argument, a judgment:
1. It is the only formal formulation of target 1 that the repository itself offers (ch:1321: "what a reading of the exchange makes necessary is what holds of every law of exchange"). Settling it turns "laws of every world so represented, with separately chosen premises" into "laws of every unfolding read off its exchange". That is the precise "necessary content" the target asks for.
2. It is also the unification gap. `families_reach_each_other` is all False, and `World` "is still defined without `Process`" (ch:1349).
3. The groundwork exists: `ExchangePremises` (five premises are readings, three are states of the law), `Reading`, `Runs` (every base is a run), `Interleavings` (the step index comes from the relata's own courses), `CourseRoles`.
4. It makes premise independence mean contingency in becoming rather than "freedom of separately chosen fields" (ch:139).
5. Every downstream law gains a uniform statement, which is the elegance asked for.

Cost: Lean proof work, large but incremental, module by module, as the U and W batches were.

**(B) One evaluative chain: join the generative space to the dynamics and to the evaluative object (D2 with D3).** The ethics' ranking runs through widening, the evaluative theorems run through `living`, and the dynamics run through arising, renewal and undermining. No theorem crosses between these, apart from one counterexample. Settling it, by proving the run-class/widening relation under named premises and restating significance over admission, gives one chain: unfolding → what can form → its significance (the condition) → C → J → direction (D7). It also gives D2's reading a theorem or a counterexample family. Cost: moderate Lean work, plus a revision of D1/D2 and of §5–§6 prose.

The warrant of C and of invariance matters more philosophically, but no amount of formal work settles it. The theory already locates it exactly (§6.11), so it is not the item that most advances the theory as a theory.
