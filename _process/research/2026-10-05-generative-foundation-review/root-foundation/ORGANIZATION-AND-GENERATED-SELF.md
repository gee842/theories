# Organization before a represented I

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparator note for **organized dependency → regulation → a formed self/world distinction**. This supports [the generated-self investigation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATED-SELF-AND-ETHICS.md), without treating biological success as moral goodness.

## Primary comparator

Xabier E. Barandiaran, Ezequiel Di Paolo and Marieke Rohde (2009), *Defining Agency: Individuality, Normativity, Asymmetry, and Spatio-temporality*, *Adaptive Behavior* 17(5), 367–386, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/1059712309343819). Read the [author-hosted manuscript, version 1.0, July 2009](https://xabier.barandiaran.net/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/barandiaran_dipaolo_rohde_-_defining_agency_v_1_0_-_jab_20091.pdf), particularly §§2.3–2.4 and 4, manuscript/PDF pages 5–6 and 8. Accessed 6 October 2026.

Their original theoretical proposal explains minimal agency through interdependent processes that sustain an organization and adaptively regulate environmental coupling. Individuation and viability-related norms are generated by the organization; an observer's preferred success score is insufficient. The definition does not require a reflective self. It supplies adaptive organization and its dependencies, rather than deriving them from universal laws alone. Its minimal agency and biological normativity do not establish consciousness or moral worth. This is a conceptual comparator, not a new experiment or a demonstrated universal origin of agents.

## Repository reconstruction and tests

The following proposals are our own. They continue the existing [dependency and regulation study](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md) and [formed prediction/action model](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PREDICTION-ACTION.md).

An account can begin with processes whose continuation depends on other processes, rather than with a named owner of them. But declaring a dependency graph does not yet produce a maintained body, a regulatory response or a learned distinction. An actual realization needs flows, constraints, formation costs and transition laws. An organization that merely happens to persist has not thereby learned anything.

For self-model formation, make the target a usable grouping of causal dependencies, not a rewarded answer to “which objects are me?” An acquired representation should help the organization anticipate consequences of its own interventions, discover losses of control and revise its partition when coupling changes. A preset anatomical label would supply the answer. A score assigned by the investigator would supply an external selection criterion. A claim of endogenous learning instead owes the causal connection between retained modifications and the organization's actual regulatory activity.

Three controls are necessary. A reliably controlled external instrument can remain external; control alone does not settle bodily ownership. A shared stimulus can synchronize unrelated processes; correlation alone does not establish one individual. Two mutually sustaining organisms can remain differentiated; dependency alone cannot decide where one self ends. The generated boundary must earn its explanatory role through the work it does, rather than through the analyst's preferred outline.

This makes explanatory priority more precise: organization and some regulation can be specified without a reflective I. When a self-model forms, its causal influence can become part of that organization. Feedback from a generated representation is compatible with its being generated. None of these steps derives phenomenal perspective, an autobiographical identity or moral authority.

The next mechanism test is therefore acquired partition → improved actual regulation under independently changed coupling, compared with fixed-boundary, correlation-only and externally supervised alternatives. This is a proposed test, not a simulation already run. The ethical test remains independent: a destructive organization may pass every functional test. A self-maintenance criterion cannot decide whether its continuation should be supported.
