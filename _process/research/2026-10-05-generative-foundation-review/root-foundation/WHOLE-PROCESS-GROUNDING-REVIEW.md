# Whole-process goodness without a privileged beneficiary

6 October 2026. AI-authored review under the updated [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **differentiated realization within one unfolding → assessment of its jointly possible continuation**. The [current ethical account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md) and [goodness-without-return integration](GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md) supply the immediate scope. No new empirical or formal result is claimed.

## 1. What the correction changes

The relevant question concerns what the unfolding actually realizes, forecloses and enables throughout its affected continuations. A controller's future advantage is one consequence within that configuration; it is not the tribunal before which every other consequence must establish significance. Insulation from personal loss therefore does not refute goodness elsewhere. The earlier shared-stakes argument remains a particular practical route, rather than the admission test for ethics.

The whole is not another individual with an aggregate interest. Experience, dependence, authority and capacity differentiate within it, and their differences remain part of its reality. Replacing a harmed life with an equally productive successor does not erase what happened to that life. Conversely, preserving every institution or identity indefinitely is not the meaning of preserving good.

“Whole” also does not give the investigator omniscience. An analysis must justify its boundary through the consequences and dependencies relevant to its claim, retain uncertainty and expand that boundary when an omitted burden matters. A global total score is neither required nor automatically adequate.

## 2. A positive criterion with an actual comparison

The strongest candidate concerns **the joint realization of actual worthwhile lives and possibilities, including conditions for their needed correction and continuation**. Joint realization means that their supporting paths can really coexist: the same capacity cannot be promised incompatibly to different continuations. The [generative-health study](../../../history/2026-10-05-generative-health/ANSWER.md#shared-health-requires-joint-feasibility-and-standing) already establishes why separate feasibility certificates cannot substitute for this assessment. Its mathematical and ethical scopes remain distinct.

A positive sufficient comparison follows under explicit value premises. A feasible transformation preserves the independently identified goods and legitimate continuities of the relevant configuration, removes an avoidable burden or blocked worthwhile development, and introduces no countervailing warranted loss. The improvement belongs to the changed whole relation. It need not improve the initiator's position, reproduce their programme or return to anyone already privileged by the account.

This also assumes a defended dominance judgment; joint feasibility alone establishes neither that judgment nor the goods compared.

This is stronger than treating a successful beneficial branch as evidence that the arrangement is good overall. The inherited handoff comparison can preserve real later benefit while revealing displaced burdens elsewhere. The whole assessment retains both. It also includes transition costs and access: a desirable endpoint does not establish an executable transformation.

The criterion does not require every desire to be satisfied or every causal pattern to survive. It must distinguish a worthwhile capacity from the capacity to dominate, and justified authority from entrenched control. Loss of power cannot silently become either a decisive injury or no loss at all. The classification itself requires defense. Final enjoyment, relief and rest need no further productive sequel; correction is required where the situation calls for it, not as a demand for continual change.

## 3. Genuine whole-level challenges

**Regenerative exploitation.** Imagine a stable arrangement that renews material support, trains competent participants and adapts to failures, while maintaining avoidable burdens and restricting some lives to serving the arrangement. The complete account includes those effects. This challenges the identification of successful reproduction, feedback or complexity with goodness. Its force is not that a controller profits, but that the arrangement's impressive capacities coexist with systematically foreclosed worthwhile continuations. Calling it unhealthy only because it fails our preferred verdict would beg the question; identify the actual obstruction and defend why removing it improves the whole configuration.

**Incompatible worthwhile continuations.** Two genuine goods can depend on the same indispensable condition that cannot sustain both. Every affected history may be accurately represented, and both proposed responses may be effective separately. Full inclusion then reveals a conflict rather than resolving it. Joint feasibility rules out pretending both can be secured; it does not determine whose loss is justified. A whole account must preserve the remaining loss even when one response is warranted.

**Ecological replacement.** A conceivable regenerative ecology includes predation, death and replacement. New life and durable organization can coexist with severe burdens and irreversible endings. This challenges both “all renewal is good” and “whole goodness preserves every existing continuity.” More biomass, diversity or persistence does not by itself settle the ethical comparison. Conversely, destruction is not established merely because a particular organization ends. The inquiry needs the actual differentiated gains, losses, alternatives and grounds for assessing them.

These are conceptual regimes, not empirical findings or freely toggled goodness labels. They preserve generativity and expose competing accounts of what its fuller realization amounts to.

## 4. Evaluation belongs inside the unfolding

Evaluators acquire their distinctions, authority and blind spots through the same relations they assess. Their inquiry can alter the lives and possibilities at issue. A regime's ability to generate agreement or a favorable metric therefore cannot certify its own adequacy. Nor does disagreement alone establish domination, or a changed preference automatically become invalid.

Generative responsiveness is a positive structural condition where correction is needed: affected consequences must be able to change attention, interpretation and organization through actual routes. Those routes can operate without every affected being articulating a claim. However, responsiveness alone can also improve exploitation. A defensible account must identify what it becomes responsive to and which actual improvements its revision realizes.

## 5. Adjudication

The whole-process reconstruction should govern this branch. It removes the mistaken requirement that good return to a subject and evaluates formation, effects and correction together. The positive comparison above can warrant transformations without a privileged beneficiary or an external evaluator. The accepted conditional foundation retains its completed scope.

Its strongest constitutive thesis remains substantive: jointly realizing and sustaining the identified goods, while answering their differentiated burdens, constitutes improvement of the whole. Accurate inclusion of all effects supports that inquiry but does not alone prove the thesis or settle conflicts. This is an unresolved explanation within the reconstructed account, not evidence that values must exist outside generation. The next advance should defend a concrete whole-process comparison against these rivals, rather than ask again whether a controller gains or announce universal necessity from the word “whole.”

## 6. Continuation: repair and the history that made it necessary

The next comparison separates two decision boundaries within one unfolding. Once an actual burden exists, genuine relief can improve the continuation relative to leaving it unresolved. At the earlier boundary, introducing that burden and subsequently relieving it need not improve on a feasible continuation that avoids it. The local repair can remain beneficial, and even required, without retrospectively justifying the sequence that made it necessary. A manufactured need is still an actual need; its origin does not make subsequent suffering fictitious.

Take histories H and H′ from a common entrance. H maintains an unburdened continuation. H′ introduces an avoidable, genuinely adverse interval and then genuinely restores the affected condition. Match the relevant endpoint capabilities, support, knowledge and legitimate opportunities where this can actually be done. Preserve the actual intervening experiences, consumed resources, acquired responsibilities and other consequential differences. Equal declared endpoints do not make the histories identical. The comparison is conceptual, not an already realized experiment; the availability of H cannot be borrowed from an observer's hindsight.

The defended whole-history judgment favors H where H′ adds actual adversity without a countervailing warranted benefit or legitimate requirement, with the relevant means and transition conditions assessed. This does not follow from resource accounting alone. Nor does it assume that wrongdoing was necessary to produce H′: a burden can arise through permitted activity while its avoidance remains better in the declared comparison. If creating it violates an independent claim, that supplies a further consideration; permission to repair never authorizes creating the need.

This yields a precise limitation of a proxy. Let N count genuine completed repairs. One admitted burden–repair insertion can give N(H′) = N(H) + 1 while the defended history comparison favors H. Any proposed whole-goodness score depending only on N and strictly increasing with it must reverse that comparison. One such pair suffices; arbitrarily many repetitions additionally require repeatable transitions, support and time. This is a conditional consequence of the stated score and ethical comparison, not a new universal ethical theorem. Counting repairs can remain a useful diagnostic; it cannot alone be the whole-goodness ordering in this class.

The problem is not merely arbitrary event subdivision. Splitting one restoration into several implementation steps need not create several genuine repair occasions. Here the insertion contains another actual adverse interval and a real subsequent benefit. The misleading inference combines improvements measured from different starting conditions: repair is assessed after the added burden exists, while the package is assessed before its introduction. Accurate local appraisals need not compose into approval of the package when its own activity changes which needs exist.

Several rivals delimit the case. A necessary challenge may form a skill, realize a worthwhile achievement or reveal indispensable information. Those consequences defeat the proposed matching unless an actually available less burdensome path preserves them. Equal final test scores do not establish equal learning histories or achievements. Likewise, prevention can cost more, create other risks or violate justified authority. These are substantive comparisons, not loopholes to declare away. Conversely, restoring an institution's operation may restore a harmful dependency; functional restoration cannot silently substitute for the specified improvement in lived condition. Its wider effects remain part of the assessment.

The result does not require every relief to generate something further. Terminal relief retains its point, and care for an already burdened person is not discredited because an unavailable earlier history would have been better. Present action must answer the reached condition. The inherited [repair application](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already distinguishes warranted repair from justification of imposing the original loss. This continuation sharpens that distinction into a whole-path and proxy test; it does not newly discover the ethical objection to creating harm.

[Reason transport §13](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#13-imposed-burdens-and-rescue-share-an-opportunity-not-necessarily-a-history) already preserves different causal histories with identical present repair effects. The [restoration law](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-MECHANISM.md#an-exact-relation-between-recurrence-and-restoration) already links repair counts to intervening damage; its reached damage–repair suffix consumes one common unit. These are inherited interfaces, not evidence that material damage realizes suffering.

The constructive requirement is to assess what activity makes necessary as well as what it successfully repairs. Revise the comparison if the supposed burden is not genuinely adverse, relief merely masks it, prevention is unavailable, or a newly identified worthwhile consequence changes the matching. No experiment, formal verification or proof of universal favorable development is added.

## 7. Continuation: correction before general independence

The next positive interface is narrower than universal non-appropriation: **understanding formed through warranted guidance can require revising a particular aim before the learner becomes generally independent.** The practice changes its own grounds of guidance through what it enables. Descriptive plurality alone does not establish this conclusion; the activity's worth, the relevance of the understanding and the scope of authority remain explicit premises.

Consider a mathematical apprenticeship whose independently warranted purpose includes establishing and communicating a true result. The mentor selects a conjecture P over a specified domain. Through the practice, the novice develops a counterexample that is checked and valid for that same conjecture and domain. This is neither a novel opinion nor a counterexample to a silently altered claim. The mentor may remain more knowledgeable overall, with legitimate responsibility for training, resources and publication.

The valid counterexample defeats the target of establishing P as true. Preserving that target solely because its proposer is the mentor cannot restore its warrant. A revised conjecture, a refutation or ending this inquiry are possible responses; the learner's discovery does not choose among them. Here correction concerns the proximate aim—what is to be established—not just a more efficient proof method. The higher purpose of truthful inquiry remains granted rather than newly generated.

This distinguishes the correction's force from authority to implement a response. Validation can legitimately require checking, appropriate expertise and time. A learner's insistence does not make a counterexample valid. Once the stated validation condition holds, however, remaining dependence in other matters cannot by itself defeat this correction. Publication rules or valid curricular decisions may still govern subsequent action. Investigating P as an explicitly identified false-conjecture exercise can be worthwhile, but changes the activity; it does not establish P as true.

The [existing content test, §11.2](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md) and [direction argument §2](YITI-REN-DIRECTION-ARGUMENT.md) already show that violating an activity's necessary conditions defeats its claimed realization. [Successful support §54](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#54-successful-support-and-continuing-commitments) already distinguishes changed grounds from disappearance of every commitment. The present application connects these results before general independence: relevant understanding can arise unevenly, changing one instructional ground while other guidance remains justified. It neither discovers content consistency nor proves an equal distribution of decision power.

The positive whole-level interpretation is that developed understanding can actually help determine what shared activity becomes. Keeping its source in the apprentice role does not make that understanding cease to matter to the specified inquiry. This warrants a local correction without requiring a private return or an outside meaning-giver. It does not establish the activity's moral worth from mathematics. An honest hierarchy rival may defend another purpose or additional authority; those grounds require appraisal rather than dismissal by title. Applying this result to contested ethical aims requires independently defending what counts as a correction, not importing the preferred verdict into that word. No new formal theorem, empirical finding or universal ethical necessity is claimed.
