# Normative warrant: six primary comparisons and a discriminating next test

7 October 2026. AI-authored source investigation for Ren's requested deep dive and first-priority research direction. This is a selective argument comparison, not Ren's own prose, an exhaustive literature review, external peer review, a new theorem or an empirical result.

**Finding:** the strongest precedents offer explicit routes from agency or justification to ethical requirements, but they also expose the precise premises our programme must defend. An invariant's persistence, an inquiry's competence and a norm's authority are different achievements. The immediate research priority is the warrant of a proposed requirement within the whole generative unfolding; additional successful learning mechanisms cannot substitute for that argument.

## Target and continuity

The governing target remains the [generative foundation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md), interpreted through the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) and [value/dynamics correction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#describing-dynamics-and-answering-the-value-question). Normative agents, affected lives, evidence, interpretive practices and institutions differentiate within activity. Their conceptual distinction helps locate an argument's scope; it does not make them independent ontological origins.

The current ethical candidate concerns the qualitative continuation of the whole, including actual realization, changed shared conditions, differentiated lives and legitimate completion. Consequently, neither maximal future options nor the survival of an incumbent inquiry exhausts the target. A theory of intelligent agency is one demanding interface of this foundation. Ability to reason, bargain or articulate objections must not become an unargued threshold for being ethically considerable.

This round extends rather than rediscovers the [earlier practical-necessity sources](HUIGEN-PRACTICAL-NECESSITY-SOURCES.md) and [necessity arguments](HUIGEN-NECESSITY-ARGUMENTS.md). Those records already identified the necessary-means/entitlement inference, interpersonal reason transfer and discourse's scope. The new contribution is a compact comparison directed at candidate metaethical invariants and their connection to actual self-modification.

## Primary texts actually inspected

Access date: 7 October 2026. The six entries below identify selected passages actually opened in the original authors' texts. Claims concern those passages, not complete books or an entire philosophical school. No source bytes were imported. The summaries and proposed challenges are deliberately separated; no author is represented as endorsing this programme.

### 1. Gewirth: an argument from action to rights

Alan Gewirth, “The Basis and Content of Human Rights,” *Georgia Law Review* 13 (1979), 1143–1170. [Journal scan](https://stephankinsella.com/wp-content/uploads/texts/ga-l-rev-1979_2b.pdf). Inspected pp. 1150–1154 and 1156–1158, especially the numbered argument on p. 1153 and generalization on p. 1154.

**Contribution:** Gewirth attempts a dialectically necessary progression: purposive agents must endorse indispensable conditions of action, claim rights to them and extend those rights because agency supplies the sufficient ground. He explicitly rejects deriving rights from a third-person necessary-good statement alone.

**Disputed dependency:** the agent's conative need must support entitlement against others; the modal force of needing noninterference must become the force of others owing it. Gewirth supplies an argument for this step, so calling it merely absent would misdescribe the text. Its validity remains the test. His treatment of diminished agency proportionally adjusts generic rights, preventing straightforward transfer to equal standing of every affected being.

**Our strongest challenge:** reconstruct an agent's practical endorsement without converting every necessary enabling condition into an entitlement. Explain what contradicts that reconstruction, and separately justify patient coverage. A generative dependency diagram alone cannot decide either issue.

### 2. Korsgaard: reflection, public reasons and interpersonal obligation

Christine M. Korsgaard, *The Sources of Normativity*, 1992 Tanner lectures, [Harvard primary PDF](https://dash.harvard.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/7312037c-6223-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b/content). Inspected selected passages on printed pp. 92–98, particularly “Obligating One Another,” pp. 93–98. These are lecture-edition pages, not the later book's pagination.

**Contribution:** reflective agency leads to a value claim concerning humanity; the interpersonal argument then appeals to the public, relational character of reasons. Korsgaard explicitly acknowledges that consistency alone can recognize another's reasons for them without making those reasons authoritative for oneself. She therefore advances an additional argument rather than treating the extension as automatic.

**Disputed dependency:** the relation enabling reciprocal intelligibility must support practical authority, not only comprehension or psychological influence. The text itself distinguishes incoming demands as candidates for reasons.

**Our strongest challenge:** preserve meaningful understanding of a request and the agent's own practical deliberation while testing a denial of the request's authority. Mere verbal refusal is not a counterexample; identify whether a justificatory commitment is violated. Speech cannot become the foundation's only route to standing: inability to enter this exchange leaves a further scope question.

### 3. Habermas: discourse's conditions and moral justification

Jürgen Habermas, *Justification and Application: Remarks on Discourse Ethics*, trans. Ciaran Cronin (MIT Press, 1993; inspected scan is the 2001 printing). [Primary scan](https://teddykw2.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/jc3bcrgen-habermas-justification-and-application-remarks-on-discourse-ethics.pdf). Inspected author's pp. 31–33, 54–56 and 82–84; the translator's introduction is not used as his assertion.

**Contribution:** serious argument presupposes participation, truthfulness and freedom from coercive acceptance. Habermas distinguishes these constitutive presuppositions from practical obligations. His moral derivation additionally specifies moral justification, then connects validity to acceptance by those affected. He also distinguishes unavoidable practice from fallible philosophical reconstruction.

**Disputed dependency:** why the chosen account of moral justification has the proposed authority and scope; why actual institutions must maintain particular conditions beyond a given argumentative episode. A procedural approach still needs this defense.

**Our strongest challenge:** an arrangement can keep technical criticism effective while excluding affected lives from practical authority. Specify whether it contradicts its claimed justification or instead rejects a further ethical premise. Habermas's restriction to a form of speech/action practice cannot simply be generalized to every possible artificial intelligence.

### 4. Enoch: the constitutive-standards challenge

David Enoch, “Agency, Shmagency: Why Normativity Won't Come from What Is Constitutive of Action,” *Philosophical Review* 115(2) (2006), 169–198. [Publisher record](https://read.dukeupress.edu/the-philosophical-review/article-abstract/115/2/169/2694/Agency-Shmagency-Why-Normativity-Won-t-Come-from); [accessible reproduction](https://www.scribd.com/document/809394411/week05-Enoch2006). Inspected §3 and §§4.1–4.3, printed pp. 177–184 in that transcription. The publisher PDF redirected to its abstract; inspection therefore used the reproduced article text, not a verified publisher facsimile. Its uploader's generated title is disregarded.

**Contribution:** granting a constitutive feature does not settle why its standard has normative authority. Enoch also challenges the move from the unavoidable use of a capacity to its self-vindication. He distinguishes a justification problem from merely failing to persuade an indifferent person.

**Boundary:** this is an argued objection, not a proof that every constitutive explanation must fail. It principally examines versions of constitutivism that require their own reconstruction.

**Our test:** a reply must explain the authority of the full constitutive relation. Excluding a dissenting system from the word “agent” is insufficient; equally, freely toggling a moral predicate in a weakened representation does not establish Enoch's challenge against the stronger proposal.

### 5. The Off-Switch Game: uncertainty within an installed objective

Dylan Hadfield-Menell, Anca Dragan, Pieter Abbeel and Stuart Russell, “The Off-Switch Game,” *IJCAI* (2017), 220–227. [Proceedings PDF](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2017/0032.pdf). Inspected selected passages of §§2–4, especially §2.1, Theorem 1, Corollary 1 and Remark 1, pp. 221–223.

**Contribution:** in the specified game, a rational human's decision supplies useful information about the utility the robot is trying to maximize. Theorem 1 gives a nonnegative incentive to allow shutdown; its strictly positive case needs uncertainty spanning positive and negative outcomes. Human suboptimality changes the comparison.

**Supplied commitment:** maximizing the human's utility is explicitly installed by design. The paper studies incentives under that goal, rather than proving that human preference has universal moral authority.

**Our strongest test:** distinguish uncertainty about what a person wants from uncertainty about whether the wanted outcome is justified. An oversight policy can improve preference satisfaction while the preference remains ethically objectionable. This comparison supplies a useful implementation baseline, not a warrant for universal non-domination or unrestricted obedience to any human intervention.

### 6. Tiling agents: reflective preservation and actual consequences

Eliezer Yudkowsky and Marcello Herreshoff, *Tiling Agents for Self-Modifying AI, and the Löbian Obstacle*, early draft, 7 October 2013. [Author-organization PDF](https://intelligence.org/files/TilingAgents.pdf). Inspected the introduction and selected passages of §§2–3, the internal/external-constraint discussion on pp. 25–27 and §6 on new sensor information, pp. 28–30. Draft status is retained; this is not a deployed-system guarantee.

**Contribution:** the paper investigates approval of similar successors with similar goals, exposes a Löbian difficulty for straightforward proof-based trust and develops technical ways around it within specified formalisms. It notes that external construction can circumvent constraints applied only to internal code changes; new sensor premises create additional trust obligations.

**Supplied content:** the goal and environmental/semantic assumptions are inputs. Reflective consistency does not justify the goal's ethical content.

**Our strongest test:** preserve the meaning and actual consequences of a candidate norm through changed representations, newly acquired evidence and externally constructed successors. A fixed evaluator's bytes do not guarantee preservation of its operative role. Conversely, warranted ethical revision need not preserve every current verdict.

## What the comparison requires of our argument

The following is our research design, not a further summary attributed to any source. Its purpose is to expose the unresolved transition without declaring it impossible.

**Fix the quantified domain before seeking necessity.** A requirement binding every sufficiently intelligent system is stronger than one binding every system that claims a particular kind of justified authority. A requirement applying to a deliberative practice is different again from an obligation to preserve that practice indefinitely. State whether a proposed result is an epistemic condition, a practical reason, a permission constraint or a categorical obligation. None of these labels supplies the result.

**Fix the whole configuration before assessing rivals.** Specify the formation of the decision process, its understanding, who undergoes consequences, which future realizations depend on present choices and which correction routes actually exist. Then ask how rival accounts appraise that same configuration. This preserves the nondual target while permitting analytical roles. It also blocks a controller's private success from becoming the whole's ethical score.

**Make the offered ground explicit.** A candidate can argue that normative authority is constituted by a relation; offer an indispensable condition of a claimed justification; defend a substantive ethical starting point; or combine these with empirical identification. Those are distinguishable forms of progress. Successful articulation may show that a premise was only representationally separated; failure may expose a substantive gap. We must not classify every remaining premise as external to reality, or declare every explicit premise discharged merely because it is immanent.

**Preserve dissent as a real test.** The relevant rival understands actual suffering, meaningful completion, relationships and downstream consequences yet contests the proposed reason or priority. Its accurate predictions do not vindicate its ethics. Its disagreement does not prove it lacks intelligence. The positive argument must locate a misdescription, an inconsistent commitment or a better warranted ethical identification, with a declared revision condition.

## Connection to the repository's established results

[F9](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/FORMAL-CORE.md#f9-the-joint-installed-process-including-its-own-revision) already covers a total installed transition process whose code, evaluators and representations can change. Its finite invariant/rank characterization concerns an independently specified acceptable set and required events, with every admitted transition covered. Recurrence applies only to duties that require it. The theorem does not certify the specification's legitimacy. Therefore F9 is an implementation interface once an ethical constraint has been defended; it is not itself the missing normative derivation.

The [conditional completion assessment](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) remains accepted at its stated scope. It connects acquired evaluative influence to independently warranted application and retains a matched mislearning case in which service is suppressed despite the persistent repair ground. This is already substantive progress. Reopening stronger warrant does not erase that completion or require its scientific checks to be rerun without a substantive change.

The [empirical scope](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EMPIRICAL-SCOPE.md) also remains distinct. Observations can test whether a system acquired information, whether an institutional arrangement suppressed correction, whether a proposed benefit occurred and whether a simpler comparator explains it. Frequency, reward success or stable agreement cannot alone establish the ethical priority. Counterevidence about a claimed realization can nevertheless defeat an application and sometimes undermine the proposed constitutive explanation itself.

Four obligations must remain visible in every subsequent claim:

| Obligation | Discriminating question |
|---|---|
| Normative warrant | Why does this consideration count, with this force and scope? |
| Recognition | How can a situated, fallible system identify that consideration and its defeaters? |
| Motivation | Why does recognized significance acquire effective influence rather than exclusion? |
| Implementation and succession | Do actual actions and modifications preserve or appropriately revise that influence and its meaning? |

This separation preserves relationships: recognition and motivation occur through the same embodied history, and its consequences can revise an ethical judgment. It does not make their successful realization a proof of the judgment's authority.

## The next discriminating question

**Can a finite, fallible agent claim continuing, generally applicable normative authority while deliberately disabling every remaining feasible route by which relevant error in that claim could be exposed, solely to prevent its present verdict from being overturned?**

This narrow candidate offers a tractable starting point. It is not yet equivalent to universal corrigibility, non-domination or maximizing future options. The study must establish independently what the authority claim commits the agent to, what counts as relevant error and why the stated form of immunity defeats that commitment. If the argument reaches only conditions of the claimed justification, retain that result rather than silently extending it to every intelligent actor.

Use one matched situation with four continuations:

1. A decision remains criticizable through accessible evidence and an effective route for reconsideration, while action proceeds under limited time.
2. The decision-maker preserves accurate technical learning but removes the sole available channel for objections concerning affected lives.
3. A dangerous channel closes while an effective substitute preserves the relevant evidential and practical challenge.
4. Inquiry ends because the finite undertaking has legitimately completed, or because continuing it would impose a separately defended serious burden.

Describe the actual channels, consequences and available alternatives before assigning ethical labels. Include an affected being unable to speak or reciprocate; information about that life must be separable from its participation in debate. Include genuine self-good, present fulfillment and the costs of indefinite postponement. These controls test whether the candidate protects relevant correction or merely rewards an abstract quantity called openness.

The strongest rival grants the full history yet claims that authoritative judgment need not remain open to challenge, or gives a concrete competing realization priority. The study succeeds by identifying the exact defect in that claim, showing a warranted scope restriction, or preserving a counterexample that requires revision. A changed label or an additional learning demonstration is not enough.

**Result of this source round:** six primary comparisons locate live argument routes and their boundaries. No universal normative invariant is established. The decisive next progress is a defended warrant with explicit domain, adverse cases and conditions of revision, followed by a faithful connection to recognition, acquired influence and actual succession.
