# Retained organization and a changing living transition

8 October 2026. AI-authored primary-source assessment for [living dependencies, integration §10](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#10-retained-organization-through-a-changing-living-transition). This round investigates **formed organization and current provision → activity over a physical horizon → the conditions of subsequent activity**. It adds no mathematical theorem, experimental replication or fitted model.

The advance is an experimentally constrained initial-condition comparison and a more precise account of what further formation would have to explain. A productive continuation need not wait for new machinery, and a requirement for changed organization need not arise from destruction of old material. Following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), transport, translation, retained material and changed provision name interdependent relationships within the same unfolding. They are not independently existing system and resource domains.

## 1. Selected primary evidence

**Erickson et al. (2017), “A global resource allocation strategy governs growth transition kinetics of Escherichia coli,” Nature 551, 119–123.** [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24299); [author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5901684/); [institutional manuscript PDF](https://escholarship.org/content/qt5cn8q3j7/qt5cn8q3j7.pdf). DOI: 10.1038/nature24299.

Across E. coli carbon shifts, the study compares OD-based biomass trajectories with RNA, LacZ and proteomic time courses. In a separate transporter intervention, DctA pre-expression varies while the postshift expression setting is shared; early OD-derived flux changes, sometimes overshooting. The knockout retains a small initial response. These curves fit an initial collective translation-activity parameter. Pulse labeling separately compares pre-shift light protein with a spiked heavy reference: most of forty abundant proteins show no detected decrease over two hours; FliC declines. This is relative assay abundance, not constant per-cell copies. A flux-balanced allocation model explains coordinated remodeling through protein production and growth dilution. RNA reports composition, not individual ribosome recruitment. The proteomic series follows the principal shift protocols; the inspected text does not establish that every assay used one flask. The study does not measure individual ribosome occupation/release. [Main text, Methods and Extended Data 7 and 10](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24299).

## 2. What this contributes to the generative account

The following is our explanatory assessment, not a further source finding or a newly proved law.

The declared target is a population growth transition under specified conditions. The horizon concerns the transition and subsequent growth, not indefinite viability. Its measured output must retain its units: biomass accumulation, biomass flux and growth rate are different quantities; none is automatically a count of completed proteins, functioning ribosomes or viable descendants. This target is independently biological rather than defined by a requirement to renew.

An intervention on an initial organizing relationship can distinguish a causal dependency even when an instantaneous output is also sensitive to supply and recruitment. Matching a later induction setting does not make every later physiological condition identical. The appropriate inference concerns the changed transition under the compared preparations, not an exclusive route, a universal amount–benefit ordering or sufficiency for every subsequent function.

For this programme, **material retention, relative representation and functional sufficiency must be evaluated separately**. A retained catalytic participant can act repeatedly; transport events cannot each be charged as consumption of one transporter. A changing fraction cannot be equated with destruction. Conversely, continued material presence does not establish that the available organization supports a changed operation at its required scale. This comparison requires independently specified demand and supported alternatives. Neither a fixed protein fraction nor continuing growth can be made a definition of life.

The scientific result therefore strengthens a constructive possibility: an inherited configuration contributes to early activity while subsequent production changes the conditions of later activity. It does not yet identify a deadline at which every explanation using initial reserves, changed recruitment or changed operation becomes impossible. Observed new production and the necessity of that production answer different questions. The latter needs a justified insufficiency comparison; observing a growing curve alone does not supply it.

| Output → later enabling relations | Identify which actual products or changed relationships support the next specified activity. An aggregate output counter cannot identify this by itself. |
| Initial provision insufficient → further formation required | Bound usable alternatives independently, preserve adequate retention and recruitment, and state the target and horizon for which insufficiency is claimed. |

Matched preparations and destructive assays can provide coordinated causal evidence without tracking every molecule in one vessel. The stronger claim of a pathwise formal refinement requires an explicit observation account. This distinction avoids both dismissing ordinary experiments and silently manufacturing a single molecular history from different measurements.

The source's normalized retention assay was not independently reprocessed here. No exact absolute whole-culture inventory, constant per-cell count or zero degradation rate is inferred. In particular, a non-detection over a finite observation window cannot license immortality, and a different experiment's estimated turnover cannot be inserted as a necessary loss on this history.
