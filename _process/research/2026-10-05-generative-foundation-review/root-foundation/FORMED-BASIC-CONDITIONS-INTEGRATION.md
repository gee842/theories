**Result:** the basic character of a condition can depend on its role in a formed life. Learned or socially formed support is not excluded merely by its origin. Conversely, acquired attachment does not automatically establish basicness or an entitlement to preserve its present carrier. The appraisal must distinguish realization, serious impairment, timely substitution and the disclosed priority commitment.

## 2. Affirmative argument and explicit commitments

The argument begins with a single developing history. Bodily capacities, expressive practices, understanding, institutions and dependencies differentiate through it. Their acquired character does not place them outside the lives whose continuation is at issue. Nor does their common formation erase differences in what each participant can undergo or do.

The normative ground remains G0; J2 separately prioritizes independently established basic protection over optional enrichment within its stated scope. The existing interpretation of basic conditions includes serious impairment and minimally effective participation/self-care. A subsequently learned mode of expression can bear that role. Where its loss has that independently described consequence, excluding the claim solely because the support was acquired would introduce an additional origin-based restriction. This study gives no ground for that restriction. It also does not prove that any conceivable restriction is incoherent.

Three distinctions make this application informative:

1. **A need and its concrete support have different formation histories.** A communication need may predate an accessible shared practice. Protection does not begin only when someone learns a language. The practice can nonetheless become a specific indispensable support, and new relationships can create further particular dependencies.
2. **Basic functioning and maximum achievement differ.** Serious impairment of understanding or participation is the proposed ground; intensity of desire, sunk effort or lost excellence is insufficient alone. Actual thresholds remain contestable and must be defended before selecting a winner. The account does not downgrade every cultural or relational loss to optionality.
3. **The protected content constrains substitution.** Output equivalence, physical availability and usable timely succession are different. Relevant histories can be part of what must be retained. When succession really preserves the basic role, necessity of the old carrier can lapse while other genuine losses remain.

This is a philosophical application argument. It uses no newly encoded predicate to certify its own ethical classification. The inherited formal theorems apply only after their supplied meanings and conditions have been justified. There is no claim that an empty Lean axiom audit proves G0 or this interpretation.

## 4. Acquisition, admission and practical judgment

The candidate description should be formed through the actual differences requiring explanation: who can understand and respond to whom, in which consequential interactions, and through which sustained support. A larger vocabulary alone need not answer this. Reports, observed mutual uptake, earlier failed attempts and available alternatives can favor competing descriptions. Inquiry need not deliberately remove essential support to test its importance.

Positive formation is admissible under the defended G0 scope when actual expression and understanding are established. Basic classification needs the further case concerning serious impairment or minimally effective participation. A particular carrier's necessity needs still further evidence concerning usable alternatives and timing. J3/J4 determine responsibility and permission; admission of a basic claim does not assign it to whoever first noticed it.

This yields differentiated revision conditions. If added expression has no actual uptake, revise the realization claim. If participation survives through a timely alternative, revise the necessity claim while retaining other losses. If its importance is acknowledged but the lexical comparison is rejected, the dispute concerns J2. If available evidence leaves serious impairment unresolved, use the already completed account of live applications; do not silently treat uncertain basicness as either established or absent.

No actual full evidence history is newly reconstructed here. The result is a defended classification account and a source-constrained candidate case. Detailed correspondence to a policy, complete acquisition of the relevant application family and reliable motivation remain unestablished.

## 5. Substantive author counterreview

**“Stable universal capabilities can explain all this.”** Accepted as a serious rival explanation. A fixed commitment to participation can acquire historically changing applications. Our account does not require invention of a new fundamental value or show that conventional capability theories fail. The contribution is the connection to G0, already checked content extension and the changing support requirements of generative activity. It is not exclusive necessity of our vocabulary.

**“You merely define basic as deserving priority.”** Rejected as a method. Specify the impaired understanding, expression, participation or self-care, its severity, timing and alternatives before invoking priority. These descriptions can be challenged independently of J2. Their relevance is a substantive interpretation of the already declared basic-protection commitment, not a deduction from persistence or dependence. Borderline thresholds are not solved by naming them.

**“Socially acquired needs make every expensive preference compulsory.”** The concern changes the account if attachment alone is being used. That premise is expressly rejected. A lost excellence can matter without undermining basic life. Where an acquired condition does create serious impairment, its recognition still does not assign unlimited provision; responsibility, alternatives and competing basic lives remain relevant. Voluntary acquisition may affect assignment without making the current impairment unreal. This answer leaves hard distributive conflicts intact.

**“A dependence producer can manufacture moral claims and thereby win control.”** Actual harm can require care even after objectionable formation. That does not establish the producer's authority or the permissibility of having created the need. The protected role can be discharged through transformation or succession where genuinely feasible. No imaginary alternative licenses abrupt deprivation. Existing ratification and J3/J4 work supplies this distinction; this study makes it constrain basic classification and prospective formation appraisal.

**“Functional substitution destroys culture while claiming success.”** Accepted as a genuine danger to a coarse description. Particular memory, expression and relation can themselves be part of the loss. They cannot be assumed interchangeable, nor automatically assigned basic status. A claim of unchanged generic task output is insufficient. If a replacement sacrifices a fundamental relationship, retain that basic claim; if classification is unsettled, retain the uncertainty. No universal assimilation rule follows.

**“This language example smuggles a verdict in through scientific authority.”** The source warrants formation evidence only. It does not supply the withdrawal consequences, threshold, authority or a justified policy verdict. The theory explicitly marks those additional conditions. The round does not complete the empirical acquisition bridge left by evaluative extension.

**“Respecting formed life prevents every ending.”** No. A specific support can cease to be necessary, a responsibility can be discharged, and a life or undertaking can reach finite completion. Real losses may remain. Where no continuation protects all basic requirements, the account acknowledges conflict; it does not certify a painless resolution or override the care provider's life.

## 6. What has advanced and what remains

The new defended connection is **formation history → present basic-support role → warranted classification and possible revision**, under G0/J/E. A comparison must neither reset life to an imagined pre-formation baseline nor canonize its present carrier. Generativity can change later care requirements; prospective appraisal should therefore include foreseeable support and transition under existing bounded responsibility, rather than count acquisition while omitting its continuation conditions.

The next theoretical connection is the treatment of **competing, independently justified basic claims when all relevant descriptions are accepted**. The present J2 rule intentionally leaves crossed basic losses unranked. A useful continuation must defend a further comparison, a legitimate plural procedure, or an acknowledged limit without covertly reducing life to counts or reclassifying the disfavored claim. The broader acquisition and durable-concern programmes remain open in parallel. None of the completed formation, comparison, responsibility or living-scope results needs to be repeated as a prerequisite.
