## 1. The stronger affirmative attempt

The preceding [enacted-foundation study](ENACTED-FOUNDATION-INTEGRATION.md) separated a situated occurrence from its asserted content and checked the scope of finite-history evidence. It left the warrant of logic and recognition explicit. Here the attempt is to earn part of that warrant through **constructing what an inference claims to provide**, then examine whether the first grounds can be presented directly.

Two mistakes would stop this investigation prematurely. A theorem's parameter can represent a genuinely evidenced fact; its being a parameter does not show that the fact is arbitrary. Conversely, naming a datum “evidence” does not establish its adequacy. The task is to exhibit the relationship that warrants its use, including where a relation is immediately demonstrated rather than inferred from another statement.

We therefore do not adopt the extra requirement that every legitimate first ground must be a previously proved proposition. That requirement would already favor one theory of justification. Construction, direct presentation and an immanent reconstruction of the whole remain live candidates. Their being live does not establish that any one has achieved the requested foundation.

## 2. Inference as an actual construction of grounds

Consider a modest language with atomic claims, conjunction and implication. For each atomic index `a`, let `Ground a` be a type of candidate grounds. This type family is supplied, with no automatic physical or epistemic interpretation.

The implemented language can retain a ground, pair two grounds, recover either member, abstract a construction over a hypothetical ground, and apply that construction. Its context contains positions within a shared derivation, not fundamentally separate subjects and objects. An interpreter recursively returns a ground of the conclusion's interpreted type. The construction is explicit; no rule says that every generated assertion is thereby correct.

The new [Lean source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/InferentialGrounds.lean) checks:

- Pairing followed by either projection returns the corresponding original ground.
- Applying an abstraction evaluates its body with the supplied argument ground in the extended context.
- A closed atomic term exists exactly when its atomic ground type is inhabited.
- With an additional correspondence from atomic grounds to actual claims, a closed atomic term yields its corresponding claim.
- No intermediate representation can both accept an arbitrary `Unit` ground and return an `Empty` ground. This is the inhabited/empty test of an unrestricted introduction/elimination mismatch.
- The language's duplication term returns two references to the same ground. It does not execute an event twice or establish joint physical feasibility.

The positive achievement is exact **construction and preservation of grounds under these rules**. In a concrete use, projection is warranted by the ground that pairing actually retained, and application by the constructed function and its argument. This is more informative than announcing that the elimination rules are independently acceptable. It is an existing proof-theoretic idea instantiated here for the foundational audit, not a newly discovered logic.

The atomic characterization needs careful reading. It does not say that inference produces no knowledge, that a computed witness had to be physically preinstalled, or that all scientific inference reduces to this language. `Ground a` denotes a type, not a chronological inventory. The language also contains no supplied nonlogical operation for constructing a new worldly condition from another. Such operations can be investigated and their products can provide grounds; the present theorem does not forbid them.

Nor have we eliminated the logic used to check this account. The syntax, recursive interpretation, function and product types, structural reuse and induction are visible commitments of the metatheory. A representation of inference inside Lean does not prove Lean's own ultimate warrant. The result is a justified relative construction, not a premise-free certification of every inferential practice.

## 4. Direct presentation can be a ground without being an earlier theorem

The strongest presentational route begins with the occurring inquiry itself. Its occurrence need not be inferred from the truth of a comprehensive theory. A token-reflexive assertion, when actually made with the appropriate reference, can point to the occurrence that warrants its minimal existential content. Its force would arise from that exhibited occurrence, not from endorsing everything the token says.

This deserves stronger credit than “it is an assumption because formalization takes a witness.” A construction can supply a witness; a demonstrated occurrence can supply evidence. The formal place occupied by a ground is not its philosophical genealogy. The previous Lean theorem keeps actual enactment and interpretation visible because it audits their use, not because it has proved both ungrounded.

There is nevertheless a scope issue within presentation itself. The apparent content of a present memory and the history it purports to remember are different claims about the same connected inquiry. Immediate access to the former does not, simply by being called immediate, settle the latter. This is an epistemic distinction within one unfolding, not a second mental realm opposed to a physical one. A successful grounding would explain their connection rather than assume either total separation or universal identity.

[Nishida, *善の研究*](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000182/files/946_74853.html), the original preface and Part I chapter 1, provides a closely relevant attempt: the individual is to be explained within experience, and immediate experience can include connected activity rather than an isolated sensation. The chapter also distinguishes present remembering from direct access to the past. His later preface registers changes in how he understood the project. These passages support testing a rich, nondual presentation without treating a bare instantaneous atom as compulsory. They do not establish our universal ontology or identify all actuality with experience.

The candidate for our inquiry is therefore not “erase every actual differentiation until nothing remains.” It is: **attend to what is actually exhibited, and determine which part of our account is warranted by that exhibition.** Presupposition criticism should remove unsupported additions, not manufacture an inert starting point that the phenomenon never supplied.

The unresolved issue is whether this procedure can make its own recognitional and interpretive commitments fully explicit and justified. Requiring an endless previous theorem is not our answer. Equally, declaring the present occurrence “self-evident” cannot authorize every richer description of it. At present we have a serious candidate direct ground, not a completed unrestricted epistemology.

## 5. Three attempted passages to the whole

**Every challenge is itself generated.** This is a strong demand that the account include its own challenge and the relationships that make it an inquiry. But an actual generated challenge can question a proposed universal law without denying its own occurrence. To establish performative defeat, the theory must show that the challenge requires the truth of the particular disputed claim, not merely that challenge and claim have both occurred. The earlier occurrence theorem checks only its explicit token/occurrence correspondence.

**All actuality belongs to the whole, so its ground cannot be external.** This removes an unexamined external standpoint from the explanatory picture. By itself it does not specify which internal relations constitute the whole, whether every region interacts, or why the whole must continue. Those are additional substantive questions. A domain called “the whole” must not silently answer them by its name.

**Ending generation would itself be another event.** An ending can indeed be an event without supplying a successor to that event. The previous finite-replay result retains actual events and a terminal endpoint in its declared local models. It is not a model of every possible whole, but it shows why the argument needs an independent account of renewal rather than just counting an ending as activity. The distinction between local completion, transformation elsewhere and necessary whole-continuation remains essential.

These assessments do not establish a general impossibility of ultimate grounding. They identify what each affirmative argument has supplied and what its proposed further conclusion still requires. A global structural rival that retains the same histories and dependence relations remains relevant to exclusive explanatory priority; it must be tested at the fuller target, as the parallel explanatory-necessity study is doing.

## 8. Correct the scope before making a stronger claim a completion gate

3. **Composition:** when do outputs actually become conditions of further activity? Existing path and ground-extraction proofs earn relative composition, while the shared intermediate conditions and actual applicability must still be established.

A successful investigation must show which of these commitments is directly exhibited, which is constructively discharged, and which still requires a substantive account. ## 9. A direct test of mutual grounding

The next affirmative attempt lets a completed arrangement determine its starting content jointly. It does not demand that every node first receive an independently declared factual value. This gives serious credit to the whole-account route considered in the preceding chapter's Hegel comparison, without claiming to formalize Hegel's dialectic.

The new [MutualGrounding.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/MutualGrounding.lean) considers the constraint pair

\[
P\leftrightarrow\neg Q,\qquad Q\leftrightarrow(P\land Q).
\]

No separate atomic truth value for either symbol is an input to `coupled_determines`. From the jointly imposed constraints the proof obtains \(P\land\neg Q\). The pair is realized by `True` and `False`, and any two solutions have pointwise equivalent truth profiles. This is a positive example of determination by a whole arrangement. Simply saying that mutual relations can never determine a starting content would miss it.

The constraints, their interpretation and the ambient logic nevertheless remain part of the construction. To test the desired actual-world application, read \(P\) as an occurrence claim and \(Q\) as its absence, \(\neg P\). The checked `occurrence_reading_equivalent` proves

\[
\operatorname{Coupled}(P,\neg P)\quad\leftrightarrow\quad P.
\]

Thus this particular package of constraints has exactly the force of the occurrence claim under that reading. Solving the equations is genuine mathematical work, but it does not independently justify imposing them on actuality. The missing warrant has not disappeared into the mutual presentation. The result concerns this explicit candidate; it is not a theorem against every possible holistic foundation.

There is a second positive clarification. Suppose new descriptions are introduced through constraints, and every old case admits some extension satisfying them. The checked `total_extension_conservative` shows that a claim solely about the old case holds throughout all constrained extensions exactly when it holds throughout the old cases already. This conclusion does not need uniqueness. A further checked example gives a uniquely determined Boolean description at each old case while preserving that conservativity.

Consequently coherent implicit definitions can determine new meanings without making a new factual restriction on old cases. If the proposed foundation instead excludes an old case, that exclusion needs its substantive warrant. The formal “old case” domain is a supplied comparison range, not an independently established totality of actuality. There is no inference here that definitions cannot be informative, that mathematics cannot discover anything, or that all philosophical grounding is mere model restriction.

## 10. Consequential difference and the warrant of a particular dependence

The next test addresses **actual differentiated activity → the dependence attributed to it**, the second root commitment in §8. The affirmative claim is that an account must preserve the actual differences involved in the inquiry it explains. That claim can constrain an account without selecting one exclusive causal structure or vocabulary. The distinction matters for grounding: agreement that a consequence occurred, identification of its operative dependence, and a universal account of such dependence are different achievements within the same unfolding.

The [new Lean comparison](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DependenceWarrant.lean) gives this passage an exact, modest test. A realization has an inherited Boolean `u`, a mode, and two resulting coordinates `x` and `y`. In ordinary mode `x = u`. In reset mode `x = false`. The two proposed routes specify `y = x` and `y = u`, respectively. These are competing dependencies within one joint realization, not independently constituted subject and object domains. Boolean coordinates and the two-route class are supplied local representations.

**The positive and negative results belong together.** Every finite ordinary record agrees between the routes, even when it contains arbitrarily many changes of the inherited value. Both routes preserve the contrast between ordinary realization with `u = false` and with `u = true`. Neither is a static-output caricature. But with `u = true`, the specified reset yields `(false, false)` in the mediated route and `(false, true)` in the common-source route. In this declared class, that test's second result identifies the route exactly.

This comparison does not establish that a real experiment performs the represented reset, that no third route exists, or that the inherited coordinate was correctly identified. Those are the correspondence and class-admission conditions of the positive identification. A mode is a coordinate of the represented whole; the local model omits its formation. It does not place the mode or its formation outside reality. An expanded empirical account must explain what actually changed and which relations were retained.

There is also an exact presentation test. The simultaneous constraints on `x` and `y` characterize the very same realizations as the sequentially written evaluator, for both modes. This global relational account preserves the entire declared interface and is an equivalent articulation. It must not be counted as a refutation merely because it does not narrate a signal passing from one already separate thing to another. The two *routes*, in contrast, agree only on the ordinary observation interface; they disagree on the reset interface. This identifies precisely what their match preserves and omits.

### Source assessment and the stronger objection

[Hume's *Enquiry*, §4.16–21](https://davidhume.org/texts/e/4) distinguishes a past regularity from its projection into unobserved cases and asks for the intervening warrant. We use that question, without adopting his separate premise that cause and effect must be wholly distinct. His argument is a historical challenge to assess, not a completed disproof of every constitutive account. Our finite comparison independently states the scope it tests.

[Eberhardt and Scheines, *Interventions and Causal Inference*, p. 1](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/philosophy/docs/scheines/PSA2006.pdf), explicitly discuss the assumptions connecting interventions and causal discovery. This supports examining how an intervention operates rather than treating its label as its warrant. Our Boolean comparison neither imports their statistical theorems nor claims a new causal-inference method.

The strongest objection from the generative proposal is correct: **both routes are already generative, and a record of their values need not include the operative relationships of the full target**. Accordingly the countercase does not refute 生生, does not establish a nongenerative world, and does not prove that a witnessed actual dependence can never be directly known. It tests the narrower attempted passage from ordinary realization records alone to this particular dependency attribution. A richer encounter could warrant more; it must identify the further relationship actually encountered. Conversely, calling an ordinary record “the full process” does not silently give it the omitted reset behavior.

The affirmative advance is therefore an improved grounding criterion. Preserve established consequences; distinguish dependence from mere ordering; permit equivalent whole presentations; and justify a separating condition where a particular dependence remains contested. This does not make every specific physical law a mandatory a priori output of the foundation. It asks whether the root's asserted dependence has a ground appropriate to its own generality.

### The affirmative construction

The new [GroundRelationTransport.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/GroundRelationTransport.lean) reuses the existing typed-term language. A source value `x` is transformed by an explicitly given operation `f`. The same term is then formed from those transformed sources. For an atomic source, `R i x (f i x)` states the intended relationship between the two actual values. Compound correspondence is articulated recursively: paired values correspond component by component, and functions correspond when they return related results for related arguments.

These two presentations are positions within one specified transformation. They are not independent mental and physical realms. The model retains source values, pairing, projection, abstraction, application and variable use; it omits physical resource consumption, temporal delivery and empirical acquisition of the source relationship.

`extract_transport` proves by induction on the term that the resulting values correspond whenever the source and context values correspond. This covers arbitrary terms in the language, including nested functions, rather than a finite list of tested examples. No separate premise declares each completed compound result correct. Its relation is obtained through the way the construction proceeds.

There is an explicit case in which the source links are themselves constructed. Set `f x = (x, x)` and require both projections of the new representation to equal `x`. `paired_representation_transport` supplies these atomic equalities by reflexivity and obtains correspondence for every closed term. Thus **a relationship can be justified by the operation that constitutes it**, without an additional source-correctness hypothesis in this example. This is a proof about constructed values; it does not duplicate a physical event or certify an unrelated empirical proposition.

This resolves a real part of the earlier question. It would be too strong to say that every relation expressed by a model must remain a separately supplied, ungrounded interpretation. Here a specific relation is exhibited by a construction, and its preservation through further construction is checked. The retained language and metatheory remain explicit.

### The exact boundary is also checked

Write `R*` for the compound relation, `E` for the existing ground-extraction evaluator, and `map_f` for source transformation. The checked equivalence is

\[
\left[\forall A\;\forall t:\operatorname{Term}(L,[],A),\;
 R_A^*(E(t),E(\operatorname{map}_f(t)))\right]
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left[\forall i\;\forall x:L_i,\;R_i(x,f_i(x))\right].
\]

The forward direction tests the all-terms claim on single-source terms. The reverse direction is constructive propagation. This is an exact account of the burden for **all** admitted closed terms; it is not a claim that a selected term depends on every source in the language. A restricted application can require fewer links.

The equivalence settles where more work in this calculus can help. Source relations can be proved by a construction, as above, or investigated through a justified correspondence with another actual formation. Once grounded, the compound relation follows. Repeated composition alone cannot establish every initial link that the all-terms claim already entails. This does not show that the initial links are unknowable or that every possible assumption-free foundation is impossible.

### Separate three claims before attempting their derivation

The sentence “activity forms and transforms the conditions of further activity” can express different commitments:

1. **Consistent continuation:** after a history extends, a proposed continuation must fit that extended history.
2. **Consequential formation:** an extension can enable a continuation previously unavailable or remove one previously available.
3. **Universal operative grounding:** the relations identified in this way explain the actual formation and transformation of organized activity throughout the proposed foundational scope.

The first admits a precise necessary law within the finite-history representation. The second needs substantive content in what histories are possible, and can then be forced by that content rather than stipulated as another update rule. The third additionally requires adequate identification of actual operative relations and a derivation of the proposed common scope. No claim here requires each event to change the available possibilities, every possibility to grow, or continuation to be endless.

### Derive a common update rule from one history relation

Let \(H(w)\) mean that the finite event word \(w\) is admissible in the declared account. For interpreting this as a history language we require the empty word to be admissible and every prefix of an admissible word to be admissible. These are properties of the selected finite sequential representation, not a proof that every physical process is fully captured by it.

Define the **continuation profile** after \(h\) by

\[
C_h(v)\;\Longleftrightarrow\;H(hv).
\]

Here juxtaposition concatenates finite words. A profile answers the particular question whether a finite continuation is possible. It does not describe all material conditions, causal production, probabilities, success guarantees, or the constitution of the event vocabulary.

Then the following update law is checked:

\[
\boxed{C_{hu}(v)\;\Longleftrightarrow\;C_h(uv).}
\]

Both sides refer to the same concatenated history, and the proof uses associativity. There is no independently supplied profile-update law. Further, if two histories agree on every finite continuation, extending both by the same word preserves this agreement. These deductions show how a description of conditions can be derived from one relational history account, without introducing activity and constraint as independent ontological kinds.

The update identity alone does **not** assert that \(C_{hu}\) differs from \(C_h\). A change of position in a history is not already a change of continuation capabilities. Treating the identity as proof of that stronger claim would conceal the crucial step in the notation.

### An exact criterion for substantive dependence on history

The stronger result characterizes the boundary, for any alphabet and any prefix-closed history language containing the empty word:

> **Every admissible history has the same finite continuation profile as the beginning if and only if admissibility of a word is completely determined by the separate initial admissibility of each letter in it.**

In the unchanged-profile case, every finite combination and repetition of initially admissible event labels is admissible, and no initially unavailable label becomes available later. Conversely, if admissibility is exactly this unrestricted combination of initially admitted letters, all admissible histories retain the initial profile. The proof is constructive and uses pointwise equivalence; it does not obtain predicate equality through extensionality.

This gives a substantive conditional connection. Suppose the actual proposed history constraints include a sequence of initially admissible events that cannot occur in that order. Those constraints cannot be represented faithfully while insisting that every admissible prefix leaves the initial continuation profile unchanged. With a supplied decision procedure for admissibility, the checked proof locates an admitted prefix of that finite sequence immediately before its first blocked extension. That prefix has a changed continuation profile: a letter admitted initially is now blocked. “Admitted” means feasible in the account; the theorem does not assert that the prefix was actually enacted.

Formation has an equally direct positive witness. If \(hu\) is admissible but \(u\) is unavailable initially, then \(h\) has a different continuation profile: it enables a continuation initially absent. This is a condition for formation of a capability in the selected account, not a claim that a word's mere representation produces a physical capability.

These are more precise than the earlier phrase “joint realization requires compatibility.” They identify when constraints on joint sequential realization **force history-dependent possibilities**. The theorem does not infer such constraints from nothing, nor transfer sequential restrictions to simultaneous interaction without a warranted representation.

## 19. The whole deduction and the passage from inclusion to relation

9 October 2026. ### Begin with differentiation within the unfolding

The inquiry, its changing expressions, the relationships it addresses and its attempts at correction occur within the reality at issue. Begin with that articulated activity. Where a relationship holds, changes or ends, the account owes an explanation of the relevant determination and difference. A terminal loss need not be redescribed as hidden productivity; persistence need not be excluded because it fails a test for newly produced possibilities. Nor does differentiation turn its terms into independently constituted substances.

The whole claim is therefore not that every occurrence creates conditions for a successor. Its proposed content is that determinations and their differences are constituted within one relational unfolding, whose forms include the constructive, maintained, transformed and lost. These are descriptions of potentially overlapping relationships. They are not five independent forces, an exhaustive classification already proved, or a partition of a pre-existing carrier.

This formulation still contains a substantive claim. To say that the theory considers all actuality does not yet demonstrate that the actuality considered has this relational constitution. The distinction is between an undertaking and its justification within the same reality, not between a model and an inaccessible second realm.

### The direct deduction attempt

The strongest present argument can be reconstructed without an independently complete subject and object:

1. The occurring inquiry exhibits differentiated articulation: a claim is retained, compared, accepted or revised through particular relationships. The earlier enacted and formed-reference work earns limited instances of this participation. No deduction of all actuality from an isolated subject is required.
2. An account claiming the whole cannot place this actual inquiry outside the scope it undertakes to explain. Its consideration of denials, errors and losses also belongs to that scope. This earns a requirement of self-inclusion, not the truth of every included statement.
3. Where actual constitution is established, its explanatory replacement must preserve that constitution or justify correcting the attribution. An account cannot remove a relation from its representation and use the resulting omission as a refutation of that relation. This is the already earned constitutive fidelity argument.
4. The proposed final step says that all determinations of the whole are internally constituted moments of the unfolding, including those not presently identified by the inquiry. **The preceding steps do not yet exhibit that whole-scope constitution.** In this attempted argument, self-inclusion establishes where inquiry belongs and fidelity constrains an established target; neither supplies the unestablished part of the final claim.

This is a diagnosis of an unfinished philosophical deduction, not a new formal non-entailment theorem. No freely varied causal predicate or purported relationless universe is introduced as a counterexample. A general constitutive argument could supply the last step without surveying every occurrence, making all regions mutually accessible or deriving each contingent law. Its content must actually be shown. Our current attempt does not show it merely by using unrestricted words such as “all” or “whole.”

The closest positive candidate is that determinacy itself is relational: a determination is this through its distinction from other determinations. But its present articulation has two scopes to reconcile. A distinction made in expressing a claim supplies an actual relation of articulation. The stronger assertion concerns what constitutes the determination being articulated. Their connection need not be external or arbitrary; it can be earned, as local correspondence constructions already demonstrate. It is nevertheless the substantive step to establish. Treating the concept's contrast as sufficient evidence of every attributed constitutive relation would assume that step.

### Primary comparison: the category does not already supply a cause

The earlier [Hegel comparison](DEDUCTIVE-FOUNDATION-TEST.md#7-can-the-developed-whole-discharge-its-own-basis) considered retrospective grounding and immanent differentiation. This reading tests a more specific passage relevant to the corrected whole scope.

In [*Science of Logic*, §§132–134](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hl/hlbeing.htm), Hegel presents becoming through inseparable but differentiated being and nothing. The target is not two independently given things joined by an external operation. This remains a serious affirmative comparator, not a premise of Ren's account.

In [§169](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hl/hl083.htm), Hegel explicitly excludes reading this initial transition as a relation of cause, ground or condition: these require further determinations. Sections 176–183 distinguish coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be, then develop determinate being as a stable result. Section 140 also distinguishes abstract being/nothing from consequential differences concerning determinate content. These passages support preserving differentiation and stability in assessing his argument; they do not warrant transferring every causal claim into its opening category. We do not adopt the ethical indifference urged in §145, a necessary beneficial sequel to every loss, or Hegel's dismissive cultural generalizations.

[“With What Must Science Begin?”, §§109–114](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hl/hlbegin.htm), considers an analysis of beginning and then identifies its presupposition of a familiar concrete relation. Hegel's alternative seeks the development's internal necessity. This directly exposes the burden of treating “one relational unfolding” as a rich given and calling its analysis assumption-free. We retain Ren's relational starting method; we do not adopt Hegel's pure beginning as an instruction to erase actual activity. The relation between that beginning, its articulation and its eventual warrant must be demonstrated.

**Our inference from the reading:** the earlier demand for a universal causal-generative law cannot be justified simply by citing a deduction of a category called becoming. Conversely, the absence of such a law does not defeat the wider whole claim. The interpretation of the philosophical deduction and the later explanatory relations require their own explicit connection. The assessed passages sharpen this obligation; they are not an assessment or formalization of Hegel's entire system.
