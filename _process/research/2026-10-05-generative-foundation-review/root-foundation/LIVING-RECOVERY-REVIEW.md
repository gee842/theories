## 1. What the proposed extension can explain

The [previous result](LIVING-REALIZATION-PROOF.md) derives maintenance demand from transport and work coupling, but its represented transitions retain nonnegative net passive influx and satisfy the selected bound at both endpoints. It correctly makes no claim that leaving this regime constitutes death. Its omitted relations are nevertheless substantive: later outward relief can offset earlier inward transport, and recovery can pass through states outside a maintained range.

Accounting separately for passive inward flow, passive outward flow and work-coupled export permits the necessity claim to follow the actual balance across a finite history. Passive relief must reduce the remaining requirement for active export; it cannot be discarded and then counted as unexplained work demand. Requiring the selected bound only at the final state also allows an earlier excursion to remain part of the same history. These changes address identified coverage restrictions of the old account rather than merely rename its supplied depletion premise.

The resulting inference is conditional on an actual history and its final amount. It does not construct a recovery response, prove that the response is available from every disturbed state, or force its eventual occurrence. Neither an arbitrarily long permitted excursion nor a succession of possible recovery endpoints supplies an actual successful continuation. A budget sufficient to avoid an accounting contradiction is also not a sufficient physiological intervention.

## 2. The decisive distinction: amount restored, organization restored

The central correspondence question is whether the organization reached after the excursion can actually resume the independently specified living activity. A selected ion amount, polarization signal, cell volume, growth resumption and colony formation must not be identified without evidence. Their relationship is a biological question within the same unfolding, not a primitive split between a system and its conditions.

A substantive discriminating case is an excursion that leaves the measured amount back in range but damages the membrane, transport machinery or other organization required for the next activity. The balance of the selected material still has to be respected; its restoration alone does not document restoration of those relations. This is a proposed coverage test, not a newly proved countermodel or an assertion that the cited experiment observed that exact case. It identifies an omitted mechanism rather than freely toggling a predicate named “alive.” Conversely, genuine recovery cannot be ruled out solely because the selected amount previously crossed a local bound.

The next useful biological target is therefore **history-sensitive recoverability**: identify the jointly preserved or repaired organization, the actual compensating route, and the later function resumed through it. A source showing that a depolarized cell subsequently grows after nutrient restoration supplies more relevant evidence than a signal crossing alone. It still does not show that every such cell recovers, that the intervention works after structural rupture, or that passive relief alone performs the rescue.

The amount account can remain a necessary projection of this richer history. It need not become a complete definition of viability to be useful. A future richer representation should preserve its conservation account while making the operational differences between recoverable disturbance and irreversible loss consequential for subsequent transitions. Supplying a free survival flag would not explain that difference.

## 4. Review of the source

The complete [LivingRecovery source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/LivingRecovery.lean) was read alongside the inherited `Window`, `sumWindow` and core `Trace` definitions. A window retains every actual transition between the selected states; the sums run over those same transitions. There is no state reset or replacement of a prefix endpoint by a convenient recovery state.

For one dependency index, write the cumulative inward, passive outward, active outward and supplied-work quantities as $I$, $O$, $Q$ and $R$. The actual finite-history accounts are

\[
x_f+Q+O=x_0+I,
\qquad e_f+kQ\le e_0+R.
\]

`recovery_account` combines these with the supplied endpoint condition $x_f\le B$ to obtain

\[
kx_0+kI+e_f\le kB+e_0+R+kO.
\]

Neither the initial amount nor any intermediate amount must be below $B$. The proof avoids the old maintained-headroom subtraction. The term $kO$ credits outward relief in the accounting comparison; it does **not** assert that passive transfer creates work or converts a tolerance into stored energy. The cost remains a supplied, fixed natural-number coordinate for the index, including the possibility of zero.

`compensation_required` and `support_input_required` correctly require strict excess after respectively crediting passive relief, and passive relief plus inherited work. They obtain a positive event on that actual window. Their sufficiency is for the stated necessary-event inference, not for constructing a recovery trajectory. The actual work law and transport law remain premises.

`window_store` and `included_store_bounds_recovery` retain the final provision store and its depletion through every support transfer. The result includes passive relief without inventing an upper bound on it. It does not establish inexhaustible provision, formation of the supplying store, or a finite universal time of failure. Input to one coordinate still need not be newly formed whole support.

`recurrent_input_at_returns` uses an actual infinite history and an independently supplied bounded-endpoint excess-load window after every start, for every index. It does not produce those windows, their successful endpoints, or the continuing history. Its bound may vary with index, start and selected window; the theorem does not require an earlier crossing or return to a single fixed physiological regime. An application claiming such a target must identify it independently. The checked conclusion is arbitrarily late actual input on the given history, without a common clock or demand on every step.

`fromRealization` is an algebraic embedding of the old nonnegative **net** coordinate with no separately represented passive outward term. It does not reconstruct previously hidden gross flows, assert that actual passive outflow was zero, or change the physical meaning of the old source. The inherited maintained-window theorem simply forgets a restriction; it does not change an actual trace.

The three explicit witnesses retain different alternatives: passive relief with no work/input; an amount excursion followed by passive return; and an excursion followed by export consuming retained work. Both two-step witnesses retain the high intermediate state in their core trace. These are structural examples with stipulated transfer laws, not biological recovery simulations or parameter estimates. The interface has no damage, repair, machinery-formation or viable-function coordinate whose restoration could be inferred from them.

**Source verdict:** no mathematical or semantic blocker found at the declared finite-transfer scope. The extension advances correspondence by representing previously omitted transport directions and recovery-shaped paths, while the biological interpretation remains a necessary projection of a richer living history. The complete [proof note](LIVING-RECOVERY-PROOF.md) was then read and accepted at that scope. It expressly retains the variable-bound/no-prior-excursion qualification, algebraic versus physical embedding, passive-relief versus stored-work distinction, and the biological endpoint correspondence. No source correction was requested.
