## Minimum diagnostic interface, without an oracle

For these two specified, persistent alternatives, a faithful observation of a new weak **on** response would separate 1 from 0. That is an informational sufficiency statement, not an available native operation or a general diagnosis theorem. A concrete continuation would have to establish:

1. **Effect contact and binding.** The indication arises from the actual response, identifies weak versus full operation, setting and relevant context, and survives into the decision. Calling it “independent” does not prove its reliability. A disjoint failure route or other calibrated relation must support that assertion; installing a truthful bit by definition is only a conditional interface.
2. **Temporal applicability.** The weak relation has persisted over the tested interval, or the claim is restricted to its current state. A current zero alone cannot distinguish an originally false record from a formerly accurate record followed by change. Likewise, a current one and final failure identify a weak/full mismatch only within the admitted alternatives; unmodeled execution or observation failure may remain possible.
3. **Actual opportunity and retained support.** Specify how the contact, record and read occur from the reached state, with their materials, duration and effects. The saved source packet is exhausted. A diagnostic indication cannot be counted as restored service, and a fresh full-load experiment cannot silently obtain another packet. Weak challenges in this model use operating support rather than that packet, but a reliable new contact is not supplied by this fact.
4. **Selective downstream use.** The acquired distinction changes the affected application or next inquiry while retaining still-supported relations. Compare against the same retained history with this new evidence's consumer disconnected, if a causal-use claim is made. A changed audit description or chosen branch name is insufficient.

Repeating the old on-probe through the same faulty contact fails the proposed separation: the false-dependence case again reports 1. Agreement among repeated reports sharing that fault does not repair its provenance. Conversely, a failed attempt to obtain a new indication must remain unknown; it is not a negative response.

These are interface obligations for the particular diagnostic claim, not requirements that every regulatory process contain four modules. The existing executor does not implement this new diagnostic continuation. Earlier acquisition and correction architectures can provide candidate realizations only through a compatible history, not by importing their successful endpoint.

## Diagnosis is relative to the response still at issue

The pair has the same full-load law `00` and no remaining packet. It does **not** establish disjoint feasible corrective actions, or show that unique diagnosis is required immediately. Withdrawing reliance on the present on-setting as a certified full-load means can be common to both once its failure is faithfully acquired. Whether to investigate, wait or pursue a different undertaking depends on available continuations and the scope of the governing claim. Avoiding another unsupported transfer need not await identification of every fault.

For a stronger result, state which later responses differ across the still-compatible possibilities, and why no common adequate response answers the relevant claim. Then show that an available inquiry resolves a difference that matters without destroying the conditions of that response. If a common response remains adequate, identifying the exact fault may be useful but is not forced by this argument. This reuses the existing information-set criterion rather than claiming a new theorem.

An equally informed conventional dependency tracker can implement the same selective revision. Matching it is compatible with a positive causal realization; it does not establish a superior learning algorithm. Nor must an organization reconstruct a new rule whenever circumstances change: retaining a correct relation with a narrower application can be the appropriate correction.

## What the result could establish

The substantive advance would be a source-bound diagnostic contract, or its actual realization, that distinguishes **false retained evidence** from **accurate evidence whose application has failed**, while preserving independent valid structure. The present pair motivates that contract without proving a temporal dependency change: its different weak/full laws and contact fault are supplied case parameters. An implemented history of actual change would need its own admitted transition.

The [application account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) already distinguishes withdrawal of an instrumental application from loss of standing or other reasons. The [whole-process grounding](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-INTEGRATION.md) governs its ethical interpretation. Acquired correction can serve constructive or destructive undertakings. Showing which relation supports a load establishes neither that the load is good nor who may demand or authorize it. These remaining explanations belong within the unfolding; they are not evidence for an external normative realm.

## Source-interface clarification: an alternate contact request

6 October 2026. This addendum preserves the preceding review point, SHA-256 `eb678ed662b3f7fe9e84d2632b2d5a7f3fc285504becd79a2af7ac67a249228e`. Direct inspection of [RemainingDependency.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/remaining-dependency/formal/RemainingDependency.lean) identifies an existing formal possibility narrower than a newly constructed observation route. It corrects any reading of the earlier phrase “not an available native operation” as an impossibility under the existing event interface. The absence is from the saved executor's diagnostic schedule, not from all source-admitted request words.

In `advance`, `.contact r` applies `cfg.channels.get r` to the pending datum. Its argument selects the channel independently of the datum's relay field. Consequently, with adequate funding, `command true; challenge; contact false; write true` keeps the true-setting binding while passing the response through channel zero. The existing Python methods admit the corresponding calls: `contact(probe)` selects the injected fault by `probe`, whereas `write(setting)` checks the datum's setting. Neither implementation requires those arguments to coincide. In the two specified cases, channel zero is faithful; the resulting on-response is therefore 1 in `calibration_break` and 0 in `false_dependence`. Repeating `contact true` through the original faulty route remains nonseparating.

This is a genuine possibility of the declared charged event law and Python method interface, not a contradiction caused by a wrong-setting write. However, the [original protocol](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/REMAINING-DEPENDENCY-PROTOCOL.md) fixes contacts matching each issued test setting. It does not separately describe construction or physical selection of interchangeable observation routes. The Lean law expressly allows request words beyond the native scheduler; saved correspondence certifies the original words, not every interpretation of such alternatives. Calling this an **alternate contact request under the existing source interface** is justified. Calling it an already observed physical rerouting, a newly formed sensor or learned discovery of channel reliability is not.

Its interpretation as a selectable physical route remains conditional on that boundary interface. If the argument were intended only as bookkeeping for which test receives an injected fault, the alternative exposes an underspecified physical meaning outside the original protocol. Source syntax alone cannot decide that interpretation. Neither reading licenses silently revising the old protocol or dismissing the formal possibility as mathematically invalid.

Channel-zero faithfulness is known from the two supplied configurations; it has not been established for a broader failure family by the learner. Other faults, temporal changes or coupled contact errors can defeat the separator. Further, a different weak record does not by itself repair the full-load correspondence, restore the consumed packet, or implement a new corrective policy. This clarification supports a narrower same-law diagnostic comparison while preserving the preceding application and ethical limits. No new execution or proof build was performed for this note.
