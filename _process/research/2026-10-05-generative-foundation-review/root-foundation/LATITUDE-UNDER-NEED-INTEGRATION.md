## Positive result and its exact reach

The [argument](LATITUDE-UNDER-NEED-ARGUMENT.md) holds a helper's actual irreplaceable opportunity loss and each patient's severe unrelieved episode in the same history. Other providers actually default, and there is no reliable substitute. It defends preserving the necessary conditions of a worthwhile undertaking while meeting compatible duties through two new commitments:

1. The helper's complete life-integrating loss is substantially graver than each patient's complete bounded loss in the stipulated case.
2. Separate individually weaker claims do not, solely by their increasing number, require that graver irreversible sacrifice.

These judgments give the candidate permission determinate content. They are not entailed by equal standing, generative differentiation or the fact that projects have value. The positive rationale for the second concerns justification to the person bearing the graver loss. The demanding objection is that many real people can have jointly sufficient claims without any fictional collective sufferer. That challenge remains open, including theories that combine only serious or otherwise relevant claims.

The construction therefore does not establish a general agent-relative prerogative, a universal authorship floor, or moderate demands in every actual society. It supplies a constrained candidate with visible inferential commitments. If one patient's complete loss is as strong or stronger, this particular permission argument ceases to apply. Its failure alone does not prove a duty to sacrifice; that verdict needs its own sufficient grounds.

The [review](LATITUDE-UNDER-NEED-REVIEW.md) evaluates the same people, means and burdens under an individual-priority demanding account and a cumulative demanding account. ## Three restrictions that survive the dispute

**Symmetric historical accounting.** Replacing distinct one-off patients with one recurrently suffering patient can create a life-integrating patient loss. The helper's history cannot be bundled while that patient's is fragmented. A principle restricting aggregation between people supplies no permission to ignore accumulation within one person's life. This is a discriminating change to the argument's domain, not a mere reminder that cost matters.

**Compatible rescue remains required.** The proposed permission to retain an undertaking gives no exemption from an additional response satisfying the inherited bounded severe-rescue rule while preserving the same undertaking, rights, rest, attention, responsibilities and full history. This is a specific inherited duty, not a new general rule that every equal-cost improvement is mandatory. Merely equivalent output or equal scalar welfare does not establish the same protected goods.

**Permissions travel with full grounds.** The [transport analysis](LATITUDE-PERMISSION-TRANSPORT.md) distinguishes renaming persons while retaining the whole relevant situation from changing their rescue capacity, responsibilities or affected others. A permissible friendship for one person is not automatically a permissible token pursuit for someone with a different urgent opportunity. Equally, those differences do not make comparable goods intrinsically worthless. The claim about permission must identify which relevant feature changes its force.
