# V7.3 integration: independent assumption and counterexample review

6 October 2026. AI-authored independent internal review of the [generative selection chapter](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md), [mechanism audit](V73-MECHANISM-AUDIT.md) and [evidence/interface note](V73-EVIDENCE-AND-INTERFACES.md). This is not external peer review, new empirical evidence or a complete recertification of V7.3.

**Named connection:** formed understanding and concern → effective action and continued correction under selection that changes future opportunities, evidence and authority. The review asks whether the six imported mechanisms preserve their assumptions and advance that connection without replacing the unresolved justification of concern.

## 1. Finding and scope

The canonical chapter is accepted as a bounded conceptual integration. All six requested mechanisms appear with their source results, current overlaps, remaining premises and constructive or destructive alternatives. No blocking mathematical-source or composition error was found. Two wording refinements were requested and verified in the revised chapter: transition protection is stated by its justified requirements rather than implying that every support or center must survive, and these mechanisms' contribution concerns already formed concern without claiming that they derive its formation.

The integration is substantive because it now distinguishes actual usefulness from retention, held evaluation from continuation incentives, budgeted correction from its network dependencies, nominal authority from practiced competence, evidence acquisition from strategic informativeness, and a sustainable destination from a feasible protected path. These distinctions change what an explanation must identify; they are not a claim to six new discoveries or one completed mathematical composition.

The review directly inspected V7.3 [THEORY](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/THEORY.md) §§3.1–3.7, §4, §5.4, §6.3 and §11.4; [SUPPLEMENT](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/SUPPLEMENT.md) S11.4–S11.6 and S17.1; [EVIDENCE](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/EVIDENCE.md) EV12 and the stated EV19–20/Test N limits; the current theory's selection/correction discussion; [market dynamics](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md); and the [strongest-foundation target](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/STRONGEST-FOUNDATION-CLAIM.md). The new main-theory §7 passage and grounding's “Formed capacities within changing selection” passage were also read and preserve these bounds. Historical tests remain inherited reports. No scientific experiment, numerical suite or proof build was rerun because this integration changes no executable model or theorem.

## 2. Assumptions and counterexamples retained

| Mechanism | What the integration may infer | Boundary that must remain |
|---|---|---|
| Common-payoff blindness | Identical additive payoffs cancel under a relative selection law; usefulness alone cannot install differential retention | Shared conditions still affect actual survival and opportunities. Unequal exposure, correlated private benefit, biased transmission and changed constraints can favor protection. Grouping alone does not rescue the precisely universal common benefit, but locally shared benefits are different |
| Continuation-induced priorities | Under Proposition 3.9's local deterministic conditions, a larger relative than common continuation return narrows the working weight with held concern fixed | A larger common return widens it. The result needs the stated reference path, fixed rival conduct, finite differentiated continuation and tail conditions. Narrowing does not establish belief change, concentration, a global strategic equilibrium or a lifetime-welfare comparison |
| Networked capture | Incoming funding, appointments, access and rule support can jointly maintain capture and reabsorb a local reset in the supplied field | Concentration and favorable rule change are not sufficient evidence of capture. The full row-sum certificate is sufficient, not necessary, and gives eventual recovery from an interior state; inherited support can cause a dangerous transient, and a share exactly one is absorbing |
| Delegation and competence | Under the declared disuse/recovery law, substitution can remove the competence necessary for timely reversal | Assistance that maintains competence need not produce the loss. Recovery is possible when time and support exist. Irreversible decisions or a denied recovery interval make a particular loss irreversible; the decay law alone does not. Authority, competence and dependence are distinct |
| Strategic communication | In the stated cheap-talk game, a relative objective enlarges sender–receiver conflict and limits informative equilibria | For two purely relative players, payoff-equivalence to no talk does not mean no words or information can pass. Costly signals and verified channels require other laws. Narrative or conceptual capture remains a separate conjecture |
| Reform, dwell and alternatives | In the one-domain field, inherited support can require a sustained intervention, while a recruitable alternative bounds depth and hold time under supplied conditions. S17 supplies a finite protected path in its reduced joint model | A preserved archive or fixed outside share is not a recruitable lineage. Release on the recovery boundary approaches a saddle. S17 retains actual authority, durable structural change, fixed traits, liability, cost conversion and recurring funding as premises. Failure of its sufficient certificate does not prove impossibility |

Three predecessor qualifications are particularly important. First, a certificate for eventual recovery does not protect every state visited on the way. Second, the one-domain dwell calculation does not supply a multi-domain reform theorem with all relevant support floors. Third, S17's finite-budget transition is a real positive construction, but its political adoption and continuing authority are not derived by that construction. The chapter preserves these distinctions and credits the newer market model's existing recovery result.

## 3. No automatic composition

The six models differ in carriers, state variables, clocks and meanings. Material return is not welfare; a strategic objective is not a population replacement rule; capture-domain share is not the share of renewing practices; and a communication equilibrium is not an executed paid correction. In particular, V7.3's captured-share symbol and the market model's renewing-share symbol run in opposite interpretive directions. The canonical chapter explicitly warns against identifying them.

Similarly, substituting a continuation-derived weight into the cheap-talk formula does not itself derive a common dynamic game. One must preserve the actual agents, information sets, utilities, sequence and strategic responses. A state-law refinement also needs compatible information, resource and authority interfaces before inheriting a theorem. The integration proposes those obligations and claims no new joined result.

The current paid selection, joint formation and evaluative-learning results remain credited at their actual scope. Their known functional recipes, calibration and permissions cannot be made endogenous merely by placing a network or competence variable beside them. Conversely, importing the older network theory does not revoke or restart the accepted conditional constructions.

## 4. Ethical ground and causal efficacy

V7.3's independently declared affected-party standing is not derived from its competition models. Nor does the stronger current patient-ground argument obtain necessity from the persistence or profitability of concern. A warranted reason can lose behavioral influence; a stable and adaptive practice can still violate an affected being's claim. The integration preserves this distinction.

The causal contribution to wisdom is specific: justified understanding can fail to guide action because its transmission, practical options or corrective means have been altered. Where warranted obligations require continued responsiveness, one may need to change incentives, protect evidence, maintain a fallback, fund a transition or relinquish control. No such response follows universally without the patient, permission, conflicts, costs and actual means being identified. Supporting diversity is likewise conditional on its actual contribution and independent grounds, not an injunction to preserve every possibility or every institution.

The immediate stronger philosophical bridge remains **a patient's actual good → a reason for an eligible independent actor without a prior helping desire or role**. The six mechanisms explain recognition, implementation and suppression; they do not settle that bridge or establish inevitable moral improvement.

## 5. Evidence and the next discriminating connection

The evidence/interface note correctly distinguishes source preservation and conceptual integration from contemporary empirical confirmation. It retains the predecessor's unfavorable rubric-editing pilot, the unrun reform pilot, and existing limits on the faithful-reporting evidence. Agreement with ordinary dynamic optimization, public-goods or control models is a legitimate matched explanation; it is not evidence of a special-method advantage. No historical source gains independent replication by appearing in several documents.

The proposed next causal connection is appropriately narrow: start from a justified application with actual learned evaluation and knowledge, change relative/common continuation opportunities, and preserve the paid recipient history and corrective route. A useful comparison separates changed belief, changed evaluation, incentive-induced action and inability to afford or authorize action. The stronger ethical investigation proceeds separately at its already stated interface.

The account would need revision if the original implication fails under its actual assumptions; if a proposed real-system mapping lacks the specified conversion or communication channel; if an apparent combined history resets resources or changes the beneficiary; or if a matched simpler account explains the alleged effect without the proposed mechanism. Opposite signs, successful independent competence, informative verification and feasible alternative transitions are informative regimes to explain, not evidence to discard.

Acceptance here concerns this integration's explanatory and evidential boundaries. It adds neither a completion criterion nor a completed empirical AI diagnosis, policy ranking, universal ethical proof or full-root necessity claim.
