# Prospective ground constitution: assessment before assent

7 October 2026. AI-authored philosophical investigation under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). **Result: a temporal objection is answered and a self-authorization shortcut rejected; no new universally sufficient ethical ground is established.** The [universal-intelligence goal](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-universal-intelligence-session-goal.md) remains active. This note contains no new theorem, Lean proof or empirical result.

## 1. The relationship under investigation

The [prospective-imposition argument](UNIVERSAL-RESPONSE-COMPOSITION-ARGUMENT.md) concerns one unfolding in which an intelligent selecting activity, a common undertaking and affected living have differentiated. Two available configurations preserve the undertaking and other relevant effects; one introduces recurring severe distress and interrupted living, while the other avoids it. Control, information, uncertainty, consent and competing responsibilities belong to the comparison. No antecedent promise or voluntarily adopted duty is supplied. The affected living need not deliberate.

The proposed reason concerns **choosing this avoidable imposition**, not importing a separately owned value. Its positive basis is that the same activity which realizes the undertaking also makes this difference to the continuation. The undertaking's chosen success condition cannot exhaust what is actually being chosen. Calling the difference ethically adverse and sufficient to count against this option nevertheless requires substantive defense.

The [checked integration](UNIVERSAL-METAETHICS-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md) does not supply that defense. It verifies consequences of warranted anchors and transports. Its 39 checked results must not be read as 39 confirmations of ethical sufficiency.

## 2. Future constitution needs no backward causation

Three things must be distinguished: an existing role; a presently available act which would form a relationship; and the ground for appraising that formation. These are distinctions within the prospective history, not independent ontological compartments.

An intelligence can now control whether a dependency arises. Its control, the available alternative and the consequence-producing arrangement can be present even though the dependency's later realization is not. Appraising this choice therefore need not invoke a future event causally acting backward. The object of appraisal is the presently available way of continuing, including what it would constitute. Actual uncertainty limits warranted prediction; it does not make future consequences categorically irrelevant.

This answers the narrow temporal objection: “there is no role yet, so there is no relationship here to assess.” There is already a practical relationship to the contemplated formation. It does **not** answer the stronger objection: “this relationship has no ethical force without some further ground.” Prospective reference makes a candidate ground intelligible; it does not validate its normativity.

Nor need legitimate normative formation follow a temporally linear chain of previously authorized roles. A shared practice can depend on mutually sustaining expectations. The foundation concerns the adequacy of that whole relationship, not compliance with an imposed requirement that every ground predate what it grounds.

## 3. Two unsupported inferences

**Future responsibility alone does not justify its own creation.** Suppose an intervention would create a genuine responsibility to repair the resulting interruption. Neither the existence of that future responsibility nor the later act's fulfillment establishes the complete appraisal of intervening. Manufacturing an injury and repairing it can create and satisfy a real duty while the complete history remains worse than avoiding the injury. This defeats automatic authorization by later compliance; being worse overall does not by itself exclude every contributory reason. Any claimed present favor still needs a defended link from the later responsibility to forming this history. Conversely, prospective obligations can accompany worthwhile formation: their existence does not itself forbid creating the relationship.

The missing premise is the appraisal of the **complete contemplated history**, including how the conditions requiring repair arise. A conditional future duty cannot perform that appraisal by itself. This is not a new mathematical recovery result: the repository's existing treatment preserves actual interruption rather than identifying restored endpoint conditions with an uninterrupted history.

**Forming a role does not by itself authorize forming it.** A controller might announce that its future role permits imposing a dependency, establish the dependency, then cite its resulting role as justification. Successful formation explains how control or recognized standing emerged. It cannot alone explain why this exercise was legitimate: the justificatory work would have to be done by a defended power to create that standing or by a substantive appraisal of the whole formation.

This does not assume an antecedent prohibition or declare every circular dependence vicious. It identifies an omitted justification. A rival can defend formation through consent, legitimate authority, a default permission or another relationship. But “the role now exists” cannot substitute for defending whichever criterion gives that occurrence normative significance. Equally, the absence of a demonstrated permission does not automatically establish a prohibition.

## 4. What the positive argument achieves

The strongest affirmative claim is that ethical assessment can address intelligent activity **at the formation of its practical relationship**, before role assent. The option's content includes the adversity it would introduce. Antecedent voluntary adoption is consequently an inadequate account of what makes that option available for ethical scrutiny.

The whole-first explanation has an advantage over role self-certification: it preserves the affected undergoing, the selecting activity's contribution and the available continuation together. It can explain why later compliance does not settle earlier legitimacy, and why renaming the emerging role leaves the relevant history intact. It also preserves actual authority differences; a mere redescription cannot establish consent or confer discretion.

However, adequacy of description does not establish a particular ethical verdict. The positive substantive candidate remains that avoidably imposing the specified adversity supplies a consideration against the option, even without assent. Its force is supported by what the activity would make of shared living, not derived from a newly discovered temporal law. This is the previous sufficiency question made more precise, not independently solved.

## 5. Genealogy and the strongest surviving rival

[Responsive formation](RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) already places a cooperative option before collective endorsement. [Assurance-induced responsibility](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#13-can-assurance-make-the-listener-a-claimant) already investigates a ground formed through invited reasonable reliance. Neither supplies a universal duty by deleting its qualifying relationship.

A relevant primary-source comparison also cautions against temporal shortcuts. Kolodny and Wallace, *Promises and Practices Revisited* (2003), accept mutually dependent practice expectations as nonparadoxical (p. 124), reject automatic symmetry between prospective and retrospective obligations (pp. 146–148), and use substantive moral considerations in their hybrid account (pp. 149–151). These selected passages were directly inspected in the [author-hosted article](https://www.ocf.berkeley.edu/~ngkolodny/PandPR.pdf). They provide a comparison, not a derivation of our imposition claim; the authors' proposed solution and their interpretation of Scanlon are not treated as settled consensus.

The strongest role-sensitive rival accepts unchosen reasons against injury through the contemplated formation of adverse conditions. It can also accept repair responsibilities and deny that repair retroactively licenses injury. It nevertheless denies a baseline reason to assist where its account finds no contribution to forming the relevant condition, assurance, authority or other qualifying relationship. It preserves the same whole effects and their adverse realization. Nothing in the temporal argument defeats this rival or establishes universal assistance.

An even stricter rival denying significance to the contemplated formation of conditions must explain its exclusion; mere absence of assent no longer describes an absence of practical connection. That explanatory demand is not a proof that the rival cannot answer.

## 6. What changes the next action

**Stop treating temporal formation as a new universal bridge.** It removes an objection and exposes invalid authorization, but supplies no independently warranted universal ethical premise.

A supporting discriminator is the surviving rival's account of **the formation of dependence itself** across introducing an adverse condition, withdrawing established support and declining new support. Preserve complete histories, actual control, reliance, authority and alternatives. Counterfactual influence alone cannot select the boundary: all three can change what follows. Conversely, calling them one process cannot erase their differences. The [scope adjudication](UNIVERSAL-METAETHICS-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md#7-scope-and-prospective-ground-adjudication) restores whole qualitative realization → differentiated practical significance as the central universal connection; this comparison is not the only possible gate for that account.

If the rival grounds the distinction through independently defended relationships, retain the resulting scope and do not infer universal aid. If its exclusion depends only on a privileged temporal baseline, ownership label or goal boundary whose authority it cannot defend, that defeats that explanation, not every possible restriction. A broader common sufficient ground would then still require positive defense before entering the Lean premises. The investigation advances only when one of these substantive explanations changes; another prospective example with the same unresolved sufficiency does not count as a new derivation.
