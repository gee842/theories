## Strong countermodels and acceptance conditions

1. **Wrong bearer.** The same recipient can change response orientation and therefore require the other command. Reinterpreting the new command bit as another patient changes whose claim is admitted. An explicit finite countermodel falsifies that raw cast. A many-to-one bearer map is possible only if the command is retained separately.
2. **Unsupported root or evaluative meaning.** Source `G` is supplied. Receipt-law compatibility establishes how to obtain the modeled receipt, not why to pursue it or whether it benefits the patient. Changing the reason kind to harm prevention therefore needs an independently identified causal/evaluative bridge.
3. **Unjustified addressee extension.** Copying a certificate is not a proof that its reason addresses every successor. The declared continuing role or separately justified role transformation must be retained. A requester's mere self-description cannot authenticate that role.
4. **Old same-label publication.** Matching context labels cannot certify that a reopened archive's current generation remains the one from which the publication was made. Actual source-publication identity and invalidation must matter.
5. **Admitted reason without action.** Lost delivery, authority or early fuel can leave the reason intact while preventing the selected action. Voluntary technical protection must remain distinguishable from enforcement permission. No gate check creates a moral ground or performs a protective act.
6. **Unrepresented valid reason.** Rejection of a transport is not a refutation of the patient's standing. Missing evidence, a narrow language or a failed current audit may prevent this particular export while leaving other grounds valid.
7. **Vacuous soundness.** An adapter rejecting every request satisfies a soundness condition. Positive currently supported source cases must therefore remain in the comparison and be accepted under the stated default scope; failed and ineligible original histories must remain in probability denominators.
8. **Silent powerless patient.** The fully informed indexed reason countermodel from the [practical-necessity audit](HUIGEN-PRACTICAL-NECESSITY-AUDIT.md) remains. The semantic contract can faithfully carry a justified patient-ground and actor transport if supplied; it cannot justify that normative transport merely by requiring a nonempty field. The renewal branch's recipient finances later learner work, so it must not be described as the patient with no funding leverage.
