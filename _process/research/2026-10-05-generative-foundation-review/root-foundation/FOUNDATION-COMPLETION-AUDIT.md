## The active goal has not changed

The active goal is to **find the smallest adequate generative foundation from which organization, significance, learning, concern and wisdom can be explained together, and formalize the derivations connecting them**. Follow the full [governing position and five completion conditions](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#completion-of-the-unified-foundation). Conditional universality—specified properties of local laws imply consequences for every process satisfying them—is the accepted universal target. Application also requires adequate evidence that a system instantiates those properties.

A finished Lean package, finite specimen, source archive or exposition is a scoped contribution. None substitutes for the connected foundation and its claimed empirical reach. Conversely, completion does not require premise-free physics, a uniquely necessary ontology, an answer to every historical application, exactly two attractors, universal favorable direction, or deriving ethical justification from bare persistence. These are distinct stronger questions, not additional silent gates.

The five governing requirements remain unchanged:

1. Explicit common grounding, informative consequences and comparison with simpler alternatives.
2. Connected derivations through organization, operational significance, learning, selection over selectors, models of shared consequences, concern and wisdom.
3. Compatible composition retaining resources, information, timing, affected centers and actual successors.
4. Generative and degenerative dynamics, including learning failures, exclusion and domination, with centers and horizons explicit.
5. Discriminating support across materially different domains and an integrated, independently checked statement of premises, proofs, observations, ethical arguments, failures and conjectures.

## What could falsify the common account

A failure needs to be assigned to the claim it actually challenges:

1. **A local implementation fails.** An empirical A/H recipe, retention cost, source-access law or decoder may be wrong. This rejects or restricts that realization, not every generative account. Replacing the recipe is legitimate only when the replacement and its evidence are made explicit.
2. **A stated sufficient condition fails.** If independently established premises all hold but the derived outcome does not, the sufficiency claim, formalization or real-system mapping is wrong. An unnoticed upstream assumption cannot simply be added after seeing the result without changing the claim.
3. **The proposed root has no informative restriction.** If its alleged common explanation adds nothing beyond an arbitrary transition-system redescription and cannot state independent conditions under which a proposed mechanism fails, explanatory adequacy has not been achieved. Representability can survive while the research claim fails.
4. **A simpler adequate account is found.** An alternative with fewer independent premises and the same tested reach defeats a claimed minimality or explanatory advantage. It need not refute existence of the present coherent candidate. The comparison must retain observations, costs, affected centers and actual scope.
5. **A stronger directional inference fails.** An accurate regenerative predator, sustained domination or cheap self-insulation refutes a relevant favorable or ethical entailment. It does not refute a common account explicitly intended to explain those destructive regimes too.
6. **A purported shared interface fails.** If a mapping preserves world events but erases accessible evidence, target meaning, another center or an unpaid stage, composition has failed. The generic representation theorem does not rescue a particular inadequate mapping.

Empirical root-level falsification requires fixing the claimed common law class, scope and observation/realization criteria before outcomes. Unlimited hidden-state expansion can absorb any recorded history, so an unrestricted representation language alone is not a discriminating empirical theory. The foundational programme must attach itself to specific common constraints and intervention claims while retaining legitimate implementation diversity.
