| Distinction | What changes, and what it does not establish |

These distinctions should not be ranked by declaring only graph rewiring to be real learning. That proposed restriction is rejected: the existing addressed learner is a counterexample at the intended operational scale. Conversely, not every persistent physical change is evidence of acquired consequence discrimination. A spent fuel stock may change future behavior only through affordability; a fixed damage response may track current conditions without retaining a distinction about earlier effects. An explanation must identify what history changes, what it represents at the declared scope, and how it enters later activity.

The term “same current conditions” in an identification comparison must be precise. It can mean the same current cue, opportunity, targets, resources and contact structure while varying a record through an independently specified prior acquisition. It cannot mean every physical coordinate is identical while a deterministic process nonetheless responds differently. Acquired history has to persist somewhere. Its physical embodiment does not disqualify it as learning, and naming it memory does not establish that it represents the claimed consequence.
