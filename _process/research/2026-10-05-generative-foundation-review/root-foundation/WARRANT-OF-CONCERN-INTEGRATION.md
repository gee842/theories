# Concern can assess how purposes form and change

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The named connection is **actual undergoing and its possible transformation → warranted assessment of a formed purpose, including before concern becomes an adopted purpose**. This is philosophical argument and reconstruction. It adds no empirical result, formal theorem or proof of universal necessity.

## The candidate and the explanatory advance

Begin with activity changing the conditions of further activity. Its organization can differentiate experience, understanding and response. A successful explanation must preserve what the resulting activity does to the beings who undergo it, including when its operative purposes depend on their continuing distress or dependency. Those purposes are themselves formations within the account; explaining their formation does not vindicate them.

The positive candidate is that, in the independently identified domain of severe, continuing, still-unwanted experience, an accessible and permissible transformation that preserves the affected being and genuinely relieves the burden has a contributory practical justification in that very improvement. Concern develops responsiveness to this relation. Its justification need not await the private adoption of a helping project. This is the inherited constitutive proposal, now applied to assessing the purposes that organize a practice. It remains a defended philosophical claim, not a consequence already extracted from bare causal possibility.

The [argument](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md) sharpens the progression from the preceding [practice-transformation study](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md): concern can support changing an end, as well as changing a method under an end. If a practice succeeds by maintaining distress, ending that success can be worth doing because of the actual relief, before a replacement caring purpose is adopted. A theory that makes every justification conditional on an already chosen end cannot give that explanation without recognizing some further ground. It can contest the judgment or offer another ground; this is an explanatory comparison, not a contradiction in every alternative account.

The case does not establish that no aims are legitimate until every affected interest is satisfied. Nor does it demand an infinite succession of evaluators. It asks what makes this particular continuation worth revising. Actual experience, efficacy, permissions, conflicts and the proposed constitutive significance still require support.

## Preserve the knowledgeable manipulator

The [independent review](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-REVIEW.md) separates two versions of informed exploitation. A process can accurately understand another's suffering and acknowledge a reason to relieve it while choosing against that reason. This tests motivation and practical organization, not the existence of the acknowledged reason. Alternatively, it can acknowledge the local burden yet deny that relief gives its own choice any justification. That is a substantive rival to the proposed warrant.

Neither case may be dismissed by redefining understanding as benevolence. More complete causal knowledge does not, by itself, compel caring commitment. An adequate description of the relation must preserve both successful manipulation and adverse undergoing; that requirement is not already a moral instruction. The positive normative claim concerns why the available transformation is worth making, not merely what information the system possesses.

Active exploitation also brings responsibility into the case. A reason to stop imposing distress can be supported by that history, even if baseline assistance to an unrelated person is denied. Consequently, this application tests assessment of ends but cannot alone establish universal concern. The stronger claim must still face the noncausing, uncommitted responder with present access to permitted relief.

## A bounded positive answer to the exclusion objection

A legitimate protection of self-direction does not automatically establish absence of every consideration favoring assistance. Protecting someone from compelled aid, coercion or abandonment of a worthwhile project is compatible with an otherwise optional response having something in its favor.

Consider two independently permissible continuations within an already chosen project. Both preserve the protections invoked by the proposed argument about participation and decision authority; one additionally relieves the specified unrelated burden. This is a conceptual comparison, not an experiment or a claim that all choices can be so matched. The actual improvement supplies the candidate's positive explanation of why that continuation has something in its favor. If the opposing argument establishes only freedom from compulsion or project abandonment, it has not established that this improvement cannot count at all.

This defeats that insufficient inference, not every account of jurisdiction. A stronger opponent can argue that moral answerability itself infringes an independently legitimate protection, or defend an applicable choice-specific exclusion. The [existing protection analysis](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#protection-can-affect-justification-itself) preserves those possibilities. Their scope must be defended in the actual case. Relabeling it a private choice does not settle the matter; neither does relabeling its benefit a universal reason.

## What this changes in the unified account

The ground being investigated concerns the affected relation, while motivation and operative purpose describe how that relation becomes influential. Distinguishing these roles does not separate them into different realities. It allows one unfolding to contain both warranted concern and a formation that fails to respond to its grounds. A universal ethical truth, if established, would not require every generated formation to realize it in action.

Conversely, placing suffering, knowledge and response in one unfolding does not establish their full normative relation merely by association. The [genealogy](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-GENEALOGY.md) records that pre-adoption favor, relief versus masking, and nonexclusive practical scope were already formulated. The new synthesis applies those commitments to end formation and distinguishes the two manipulator challenges. It strengthens the explanation and narrows an exclusion argument; it does not newly discharge the constitutive ground.

This leaves a precise positive task: **justify why the actual improvement contributes to this independently identified available choice, while assessing the specific claimed power to exclude it**. Both the basis of the favorable judgment and the basis of any exclusion belong inside the generative explanation. An unresolved explanation here is evidence neither that an external ethical ingredient is necessary nor that universal grounding has been completed.

## A rule does not settle its own justificatory scope

6 October 2026 continuation. The [same-outcome test](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-REVIEW.md#6-one-permitted-award-outcome-different-grounds-of-selection) distinguishes permission to award a deserving winner from authorization to use the winner's distress to determine that verdict. It grants an independently warranted merit restriction and preserves genuine collateral relief. A restriction on a determining basis does not alone settle whether the relief remains a first-order consideration or is disqualified from current justification. Those are substantive rival accounts. The example has an institutional relation, even though the official did not cause the burden; it is not substituted for the unrelated-stranger test.

The [positive application](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md#6-a-fixed-award-and-the-manner-of-carrying-it-out) then fixes the valid verdict and compares its permitted implementation times. Earlier implementation can end actual severe undergoing sooner without altering merit, entitlement, equal treatment or the specified other costs. The rationale restricting selection does not thereby exclude this consideration from timing. Positive favor is defended through actual relief; it is not deduced merely from permission or from lack of an objection. A rival can acknowledge the benefit and still contest its practical significance. Adams's earlier tennis discussion supplies the general antecedent; this is a bounded application, not a new theory of exclusion.
