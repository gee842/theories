# Independent review of architecture formation

6 October 2026. AI-authored internal review of the continuation proposed in [architecture formation](ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-PROPOSAL.md). **Status: accepted as a finite conditional construction after an independently implemented executable check.** The prospective obligations below were written before the new local law and computation were supplied; the final assessment follows them. This review owns only this document and the new architecture experiment's independent checker and receipt. Other branches and shared indexes are outside its editing scope.

## Named claim and acceptance boundary

The proposed connection is **actual inherited organization → local assembly of alternative response scopes → paid operation and retention → changed production of a subsequent architecture**. It must explain more than assigning names to finished programmes and drawing one from a supplied menu. It need not derive its local physical laws from nothing or establish universal favorable direction.

The acceptance boundary is a finite conditional construction, with an independently implemented executable check. Origin, viable development, retention, activation and ethical warrant remain separate claims. A retained circuit's response to another center can establish functional other-directed responsiveness without deriving felt compassion, justified standing, general concern or its natural prevalence.

## Prospective adversarial obligations

1. **Assembly is a process.** Installed routing must follow from local component states and permitted operations, with actual intermediates, failed or omitted construction, typed stocks and spent resources. Primitive component functions and target semantics must remain explicit. Calling a complete shared programme a component would move the explanatory gap without closing it.
2. **The predecessor is real.** Keep the actual preparation and acquired predictor distribution, including every unsuccessful history. Carry its register, motor coupling, component life, resources and source remainder. Any enlarged initial source or new parts are declared before operation and remain accounted for; nothing is silently replenished at a handoff.
3. **Payments precede returns.** Trace every assembly, observation, action, renewal and copy payment. An affordable final total is insufficient if an intermediate step is insolvent. Inactive, unfinished and unaffordable paths remain in the distribution.
4. **Consequences remain differentiated.** Actor, recipient, accessible fuel, source and lost energy have separate accounts. A patient's delivery is not an actor receipt. A source-zero event is not evidence of the recipient's irrelevance. Supplied target correspondence and actuation laws cannot be called learned unless actual acquisition is executed.
5. **Retention has an implemented cause.** Count actual paid persistence, reconstruction or transmission. An observer's performance ranking or expected final reserve cannot select an architecture unless a mechanism uses that quantity. Extra stock can retain an ineffective circuit; correctness and retention are not definitions of each other.
6. **Production feedback reaches a second construction.** Prior activity must change actual templates, connection sites or another specified assembly condition, and a later construction must use that changed condition. A changed diagnostic probability without an executed later formation is insufficient for the proposed joined result. A fixed microscopic law may produce a changing effective generator; no infinite hierarchy of generator laws is required.
7. **Scope may narrow as well as widen.** Include omission and every suppressor admitted by the declared component grammar. Cheap inhibition, unsuccessful transfer, task conflict, depleted parts and unproductive copying must remain possible. A law-specific finite menu is legitimate; it is not closure over all conceivable architectures.
8. **Development and filtering can interleave.** Keep failures during construction, competition for parts and support, and consequences that alter later construction. No universal generate-first/select-later chronology is assumed. The inherited private-first specimen does not rule out a separate co-development of centers and shared regulation.

## Independent checker contract

After the prospective protocol is fixed, implement the new transitions separately from the producer's suffix. Shared public input datatypes and the existing independently checked predecessor law may be reused, with that dependence disclosed. Compare complete final-state and marked-event distributions, not only favorable means or a displayed witness. Reconstruct unconditional results with failed preparations retained, and check every prefix energy/component account.

The intended adverse comparisons include: no construction; depleted fuel and missing parts at each construction stage; missing recipient return; wrong target correspondence; cheap or free inhibition if explicitly admitted; unproductive but subsidized retention; and unchanged versus consequence-modified assembly conditions. Frozen-generator comparisons must preserve source and exposure schedules and account for intervention costs and stocks. A conventional implementation of the same complete transition law should match exactly. Divergence from a restricted fixed-generator explanation alone is not evidence of a new class of dynamics.

Read-only inspection of the current worktree found many active parallel edits. This review neither absorbs them nor interprets their presence as validation of this construction. The final receipt will identify the particular frozen sources and verification scope it actually checks.

## Implemented law and substantive advance

The [fixed protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/PROTOCOL.md) and [producer](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/model.py) meet the named local construction claim. Receptor and comparator fabrication precede individual directed bond events. R has one output port; its direct attachment to the inherited predictor competes with attachment through the identity comparator. The comparator-to-predictor bond can form before the receptor binds, including a real unused bond in a subsequently direct circuit. Fuel and connector scarcity can terminate an intermediate rather than merely reduce a finished programme's score.

This is more informative than the earlier draw over completed routes. Under sufficient resources, the graph law can be marginalized into a probability of each completed topology; doing so does not erase its derivation or make intermediate expenses optional. Under tight fuel or component constraints, the completed-only description loses relevant states unless it explicitly restores them.

An actor capture through either topology can pay for an actual guide made from a T precursor. The second graph's binding propensities depend on the resulting guide inventory. They do not read a success flag or an observer's score. The frozen arm spends the same copying price and consumes the same precursor into an inert product; the filter-copy arm makes a comparator-docking guide where its comparator template exists. Both narrowing and widening are therefore admitted under the same successful private activity and different explicit copying couplings. The copying chemistry itself remains supplied.

Two clarifications were made before the producer's first complete numerical run. Eligibility retains either inherited coupler orientation and never tests hidden correctness. The receptor receives a **supplied target request/cue**, not a derived support-deficit measurement: no independent deficit threshold is modeled. Later stages use their new receptor address rather than borrowing an older graph. Loss of predictor access disables subsequent productive assembly and all retained graphs, while every bound part remains in the account. These are declared phase laws, not conclusions about every material implementation.

## Independent numerical check

The [independent interpreter](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/independent_check.py) uses mutable ledgers, explicit node traversal and its own enumeration of payable primitive events. It does not call the producer's entrance, eligibility, topology, propagation, assembly or suffix functions to obtain expected suffix histories. Public datatypes and parameter values are shared comparison interfaces. The separate [handoff checker](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/handoff_check.py) reconstructs preparation and uses the previously independent coupling-event evaluator for the inherited prefix; that inherited independence is reused, not newly invented here.

The final [receipt](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/independent-check.json) passes:

- 390 comparisons from every reachable predecessor in ten cases and five arms, matching 2,966 complete event-name/state branch histories and exact terminal distributions;
- 78 matched first-round comparisons, with identical accounts and conditions after ignoring which guide product was made;
- 9,920 separately labeled configured reserve, stock, efficacy, coupler and intervention comparisons, plus 25 missing-capacity comparisons;
- 288 checks against independent closed-form assembly probabilities, including insufficient fuel or a single connector;
- all 50 unconditional aggregate and terminal-state laws, reconstructed with actual preparation probabilities, every failed preparation and the correct post-reversal regime.

Energy and all four typed material accounts are checked at every reference event. Failed preparations preserve their old source, recipient reserve and auxiliary stocks; commissioned histories lose their four scheduled packets whether operation occurs or not. Recipient captures never add actor fuel. The complete original energy account is 30 common +121 source +4 recipient =155 units in the primary case. Side-stock conservation is distinct from that energy account.

All eleven scientific/checker dependency hashes were stable during the final run. An earlier run reached the last assertion with every substantive comparison passing but detected a concurrently edited handoff-checker file; no passing receipt was written for that run. The checker was rerun after both implementations were frozen. These are internal exact computational checks, not a new Lean certificate or empirical replication.

## What the outcomes establish and exclude

The primary expected recipient captures are 17/108 for live copying, 5/36 for matched inert copying, and 13/108 for comparator copying. Probabilities of any recipient use are respectively 13/108, 1/9 and 11/108. Previous motor-coupling revision followed by recipient use has unconditional probability 1/36. All original failed preparations remain in those denominators. Conventional propagation has the same complete distributions as live propagation.

These differences establish a causal change in production and subsequent action under the declared component laws. They do **not** establish a total retention advantage for broad response: all three primary copying arms have 2/9 expected graphs still retained at the final boundary. More actor fuel also remains under the no-build intervention. Construction follows a supplied physical schedule; no-build is an ablation, not a voluntary choice generated by an optimizing actor in this model.

Retention is nevertheless an actual mechanism, not a relabeled balance. The first graph must pay again at the second boundary; a failed bill deactivates it without returning parts. Under maintenance price three, expected final retained graph counts become 11/54 for live, 5/24 for frozen and 23/108 for filter-copy. An actual reserve-21 predecessor can construct two direct routes and fund both recipient uses yet fail to retain the second graph. Avoiding the second recipient expenditure through a filtered route can keep both graphs payable. The selective effect of the maintenance bill can therefore oppose the production bias.

The harmful-transfer intervention leaves actor consequences and graph distributions unchanged while turning the live arm's expected 17/108 recipient captures into 17/108 units of recipient damage under its separately declared damage channel. Private effectiveness cannot identify beneficial transfer to that center. The model has no acquired recipient-specific evidence that could repair this error. This is a useful counterexample to treating shared response as automatically helpful, rather than a defect in the scoped construction.

The cheap-gate experiment changes both affinity and price; separate affinity-only and price-only cases prevent attributing their combined effect to only one mechanism. Scarce connectors, missing templates, low reserves, unavailable components and expensive fabrication retain actual paid partial failures. The declared grammar remains finite; this review does not claim closure over all possible biological or engineered suppressors.

## Final acceptance boundary

Accepted: actual inherited organization supports local assembly of alternative target scopes; private consequences finance finite maintenance and material guide production; the changed guides causally alter another actual assembly; the resulting architecture changes costly response to a distinct recipient. Production and retention are separately measured, and adverse regimes remain inside the account.

Still supplied: component functions and compatible ports, target cue and identity calibration, action-to-target correspondence, the fabrication/opportunity schedule, return-to-copy coupling, finite leases and side stocks. The finite read-only suffix suspends the predecessor's original wear/reconstruction rules. No recipient-specific learning, ethical standing, general motivation, population fixation, natural prevalence or co-development of centers is derived. The result is a completed conditional interface, not completion of the unified foundation.

The canonical integration was checked against these results. Two wording corrections were requested: use target request/cue instead of an unexplained deficit sensor, and keep easy production distinct from differential retention when describing why a route becomes common. Neither requires changing the verified transition law. The next composition should connect these locally formed routes to actual recipient-specific evidence and paid correction while preserving this endpoint's remaining components, records, guide inventory and depleted sources.
