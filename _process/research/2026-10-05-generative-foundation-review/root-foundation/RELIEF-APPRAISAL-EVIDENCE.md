# Relief, changed appraisal and what an assessment identifies

6 October 2026. AI-authored primary comparison for **a response changes undergoing and evaluation → warranted reconsideration in the same history**, continuing [the relief-and-reconsideration integration](RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-INTEGRATION.md). This investigation supplies an empirical identification constraint. It does not identify selective incapacity, establish any treatment's permissibility or discharge the universal ethical ground.

## A concrete disagreement about apparent improvement

**Verdam, Oort, Visser and Sprangers (2012), read in Verdam's 2017 thesis chapter 2.** The study reanalyzes 170 patients assessed before surgery and three months later. A retrospective “then-test” reassesses the earlier condition alongside the current assessment. General Health's raw mean stayed 2.96; estimated standardized change was +0.18 using the then-test and −0.15 using structural equation modeling (SEM). The latter found different post/then measurement relations for four of nine scales and changes beyond recalibration for three. Nonsignificant differences in latent means gave no evidence of recall bias, not proof of perfect memory. SEM involved researcher-guided model choices; the authors limit its results to methodological illustration, expressly declining substantive clinical conclusions. See the [institutional primary chapter](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/9797580/02.pdf), printed pp.26–38, especially Tables 2 and 5 and the final discussion. The whole main chapter, pp.23–38, was read as text; the journal version was not independently collated.

**Genealogy and contrasting method.** The earlier [Visser, Oort and Sprangers (2005) study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7722795_Methods_to_detect_response_shift_in_quality_of_life_data_A_convergent_validity_study) uses this same cohort. The then-test and SEM largely converged, but General Health's inferred direction differed. A third, individualized anchor task gave divergent results. Its discussion acknowledges the absence of a gold standard and possible task or administration effects. Read introduction, study-design and assessment methods, and focused results/discussion, printed pp.629–637; not a complete paper review. These are related analyses, not independent replications. The accessible author-uploaded text was read; its PDF link returned 404.

**Current conceptual constraint.** [Vanier and colleagues (2021)](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/302009163/s11136-021-02846-w.pdf) distinguish a measured construct from its scores, and changed appraisal from the resulting measurement discrepancy. Their proposed model has explicit assumptions, including conceptual distinctions and measurement validity. They acknowledge accounts in which adaptation transforms quality of life itself, and limit the suitability of their statistical conception accordingly (printed pp.3316–3320). This is a proposed theoretical model, not another clinical experiment.

[Original bytes, versions, reading scopes and hashes](../../../../sources/37-ethical-minimum-primary-2026-10-06/relief-reconsideration/appraisal-continuation/README.md) are preserved separately. No raw patient data were acquired and no analysis was rerun.

## The resulting change to our identification target

Giving answers about “then” and “now” at one later occasion does not establish a common appraisal frame. Our interface must therefore represent—or justify excluding—the relevant question and comparison context. This inference does not identify each person's mechanism. The study tests measurement invariance; it does not show that inquiry caused a change in experience or capacity.

Within the proposed relief history, three possibilities must remain open:

1. Understanding changes the undergoing itself, so an earlier burden genuinely diminishes.
2. New relationships, activities or priorities change what matters in the continuing life.
3. The mapping from an identified condition to a reported rating changes, while that condition remains.

These possibilities can coexist. They must not be identified from the direction of a score alone. None requires an unchanged, pre-social owner as the standard of authenticity. Equally, describing every altered answer as constitutive improvement would erase the distinction between an experience and an unreliable account of it.

The study's label “true change” names an estimate within a selected measurement model. It is not our ethical conclusion. Choosing a latent factor, its indicators and invariant anchors does not establish that everything excluded by that representation lacks value. A change in an activity's meaning may matter within the life even where a fixed questionnaire treats it as changed measurement. Conversely, newly described meaning does not guarantee the disappearance of an independently identified continuing burden.

The nondual reconstruction therefore follows the intervention, undergoing, appraisal and reporting as differentiated activities in the same history. A latent construct is an analytical representation, not a second hidden subject. Its adequacy depends on what it preserves and what the target requires. No fundamental split between neutral experience and an external evaluator is needed.

## What would identify selective restriction

Response shift supplies no diagnosis of incapacity. The previous argument needs a further relation: a specified live consideration cannot enter assessment of this response's continuation, because of an identified effect of that response. Evidence about access, actual understanding, assessment, expression and feasible action must discriminate that claim from reasonable refusal, a new warranted priority, altered wording, unavailable alternatives and valid settlement. The present studies do not test those functions.

A useful next empirical identification would follow relevant considerations across changing appraisal contexts and show which relations remain usable. An unchanged endorsement could be compatible with meaningful reassessment; a reversed endorsement could result from compliance or changed question meaning. Neither outcome settles capacity. A permitted reopening must be assessed through the relation it restores and its consequences, including any loss of genuine relief. It must not define success by agreement with an investigator.

This adds a specific requirement to the existing model interface: **preserve or identify the relation between the considered history, operative appraisal and report before interpreting a judgment's persistence or revision.** If that relation is stable, this source comparison supplies no basis for discounting the answer. If it changes through warranted learning, it supplies no basis for restoring the earlier standard. If independent evidence identifies response-produced exclusion of a live concern, the earlier prospective reopening argument becomes applicable, subject to its permission and proportionality conditions. The patient's good and its practical relevance to an independent responder still require the foundation's substantive justification.
