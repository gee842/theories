## 2. What changes actually matter for a sign judgment

Let the earlier channel be \(r_o(p)=g_o p+b_o\), with \(g_o\ne0\), and the current channel \(r_n(p)=g_n p+b_n\). Retain the earlier reference values. For target sign \(s\), the old reader now compares the sign of

\[
Q=s[r_o(1)-r_o(0)][r_n(p)-r_o(0)]
  =g_o g_n sp+g_o(b_n-b_o)s.
\]

`all_signs_transfer_iff` proves the exact condition

\[
\bigl[\forall p,s\in\mathbb R,\quad Q>0\iff sp>0\bigr]
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
b_n=b_o\ \land\ g_o g_n>0.
\]

Thus a changed gain magnitude can preserve every relevant judgment. `positive_gain_change_preserves_signs` gives an explicit gain-one to gain-two example. A changed offset or polarity cannot preserve this all-real-input sign interface. This criterion concerns strict sign discrimination, including arbitrarily small responses. A restricted task set or a known nonzero margin can permit a wider class of changes; no such wider characterization is asserted here.

This corrects an unnecessarily strong reading of the previous sufficient condition. The earlier proof used the same channel from calibration through use. What the actual task requires is preservation of the relevant discrimination relation. Nonduality does not make every internal change irrelevant, and a changed internal parameter does not by itself establish a loss of ability.

A block contains one current channel family and a finite task list. The procedure inspects at its entrance and then applies the admitted reader throughout its tasks. The common channel parameter means that the reference and task encounters share that relation within the block. If each block's gains are nonzero, the initial contacts/inhibition are connected, and the inspection reserve is at least \(7m\) for \(m\) blocks, the actual sequence yields exactly the same retained matrix and actual success count as applying the responsive update through those blocks. Seven units per block is a sufficient bound; unchanged relations consume six. `finite_blocks_conserve` preserves the separate inspection account through every finite block list, including blocked cases.

This strengthens a once-calibrated stable-channel construction to renewed correspondence across an arbitrary finite sequence of changed channels. It does not require one channel to persist forever. The given block partition specifies when checking is available and how long transfer is assumed. No numerical wall-clock detection bound follows without such timing information. The possibility or evolutionary origin of that inspection schedule remains distinct from its proved consequences.

Inspection has a continuing cost. Repeating a six-unit check for every task or every short block can cost more than the one-unit-per-four-task raw return advantage established in the earlier comparison. Large stable blocks may amortize checks, but the present theorem neither selects their size nor derives stable intervals from anticipated benefit. Operational returns and inspection fuel have distinct access laws; no economic advantage or self-funding claim is smuggled into the correspondence theorem.

**Does the exact affine model solve general monitoring?** No. The nonaffine alias in §3 preserves both reference outputs while changing the target response. Noise, uncertainty, reference degradation and silent rebinding are additional application conditions. The theoretical advance identifies what the present assurance depends on; it does not infer those conditions from agreement alone.

**Did we replace a success oracle with a change oracle?** The change decision follows from six acquired values and installed records. The opportunity to probe, the reference-producing relation and the inspection/commit capabilities remain supplied. This is a local reduction of the earlier dependency, not premise-free self-authentication or spontaneous invention of the probe.

**Do all detected changes require revision?** No. The exact transfer criterion preserves same-zero, same-orientation changes, whereas this implementation rewrites after any changed reference value. Its guarantee is conservative. That gap is a concrete possible improvement, not evidence that the current conditional guarantee is false.

**Does successful correction establish ethical alignment?** The proof concerns the causal reliability of a report controlling learning.
