## 2. Nonempty interpretations of the same evidence

Fix an actor, patient, episode and decision horizon. Let \(A\) be a finite declared menu containing all alternatives relevant to the claimed verdict, including abstention and relevant private alternatives. Action meanings include their timing and consequences, not only command labels. Let \(E\) be the acquired partial factual record and \(N\) the independently defended grounds and comparison commitments. Retain a **nonempty** finite interpretation family

\[
\Omega(E,N)\ne\varnothing.
\]

Each interpretation \(\omega\) specifies the recipient-good mapping, actor-addressed reason applicability, relevant competing consequences, authority, a feasible-permissible set \(D_\omega\subseteq A\), and a reflexive transitive comparison \(\succeq_\omega\) on that set. It may leave some alternatives incomparable. Selection of this family needs evidential and argumentative justification; an arbitrary adverse speculation is not automatically admitted, and a live counterinterpretation is not removed merely because it prevents a favorable verdict.

Positive recorded facts constrain the family; absent facts do not acquire negative truth values. Other warranted facts can imply a proposition despite a missing direct record, but the missing record alone cannot do so. Noisy evidence requires a separate justified statistical contract rather than noiseless logical exclusion. An empty family signals inconsistent premises or a failed model specification; it must not vacuously license every action. The actual relevant interpretation must belong to the retained family for any all-model certificate to apply to it.

A reason in favor of helping, a permitted intervention, a comparative verdict and actual fulfillment remain different predicates. For example, every retained interpretation may recognize a positive reason concerning the recipient while disagreeing about whether the additional actor sacrifice is warranted. Technical scope or delivery bits can be sound factual inputs without being histories of legitimate authorization.

## 3. Two distinct intersections must not be conflated

First suppose the permissible feasible menu is a common nonempty \(D\) in every retained interpretation. Existing N1 defines

\[
a\succ_\Omega b
\iff
(\forall\omega\;a\succeq_\omega b)
\land(\exists\omega\;\neg[b\succeq_\omega a]),
\]

and the nonempty robust remainder

\[
R_\Omega(D)=\{a\in D:\nexists b\in D\;b\succ_\Omega a\}.
\]

This excludes a unanimously weakly worse alternative when at least one retained interpretation supplies strictness. It does not say that each remaining action is separately licensed by every interpretation's full judgment.

Define instead the individual undominated sets

\[
U_\omega=\{a\in D:\nexists b\in D\;b\succ_\omega a\},
\qquad C_\Omega=\bigcap_\omega U_\omega.
\]

**Application identity.** \(C_\Omega\subseteq R_\Omega(D)\), but \(C_\Omega\) can be empty while \(R_\Omega(D)\) is nonempty.

**Proof.** If \(b\succ_\Omega a\), some interpretation has \(b\succ_\omega a\), so \(a\notin U_\omega\). This proves the inclusion. For strictness take \(D=\{h,s\}\), with \(h\succ_1 s\) and \(s\succ_2 h\). Neither action unanimously dominates the other, hence \(R_\Omega(D)=\{h,s\}\), but \(U_1\cap U_2=\varnothing\). ∎

This is not a defect in N1: the two rules express different commitments. N1 promises a nonempty remainder under its finite common-menu assumptions. A uniform license across independently specified judgments need not exist. Conversely, common non-domination alone is not a positive proof of unique optimality or obligation when comparisons are incomplete. If each interpretation has a separately justified choice-license set \(L_\omega\), the exact all-interpretation license is \(\bigcap_\omega L_\omega\). Identifying \(L_\omega\) with \(U_\omega\) is an explicit additional decision rule, not a theorem of standing.

For varying authority or physical feasibility, use each interpretation's **full** \(D_\omega\):

\[
\boxed{
C_\Omega=
\left(\bigcap_\omega D_\omega\right)
\setminus
\{a:\exists\omega\;\exists b\in D_\omega,\ b\succ_\omega a\}.
}
\]

The comparison relation in the subtracted set is used only where \(a\in D_\omega\). This is just expansion of the individual definitions and gives an exact audit witness for every excluded candidate: an interpretation where it lacks permission/feasibility, or one with a permitted feasible defeating alternative. Checking competitors only in \(\bigcap D_\omega\) can miss an alternative available in one live interpretation. A stronger uniform strict verdict requires \(a\in\bigcap D_\omega\) and \(a\succ_\omega b\) for every \(\omega\) and every distinct \(b\in D_\omega\).

These formulas are application conditions, not an implemented universal solver. The difficult work can be identifying the relevant interpretations, comparison grounds, complete menu and physical opportunities. Existing N5 and T13 already imply the related information limit: where observationally indistinguishable interpretations demand disjoint license sets, a common observation-based policy cannot guarantee a licensed choice in both. Randomizing does not restore a probability-one guarantee when its support would have to lie in an empty intersection.

## 4. What can license the proposed helping branch

**Conditional dominance license on a common menu.** Suppose a common nonempty menu \(D\) contains all relevant feasible, permitted alternatives in every retained interpretation, with \(h\in D\). Suppose independently justified, complete comparisons establish \(h\succeq_\omega b\) for every interpretation and every \(b\in D\), with a strict comparison in at least one interpretation for each \(b\ne h\). N2 then excludes every competitor and \(R_\Omega(D)=\{h\}\). No interpersonal cardinal conversion is required. A stronger per-interpretation strict comparison gives \(U_\omega=\{h\}\) everywhere as well. These conclusions depend on the defended dominance rule and the completeness of the common menu. They cannot be transferred to varying menus by silently omitting alternatives available in only some interpretations; use the separate full-menu condition in Section 3 for that case.

Unknown recipient status can therefore coexist with a warranted N2 choice when protection is tied in one interpretation and strictly better in another, with no contrary relevant burden. A genuine actor opportunity cost, conflicting claim or uncertain permission can defeat those premises. The proposed eight-unit sacrifice does not become normatively irrelevant because its funding is available; a costly case must defend its priority or magnitude separately. Likewise, beating abstention does not license a verdict over an omitted private alternative.

The costly-weight obstruction is already established in [慧根 §9](HUIGEN-FULL-THEORY-AUDIT.md#applicability-transfer-and-costly-choice). Applied to this proposed policy, even if a ten-unit receipt is accepted as a positive recipient benefit proxy, a scalar representation \(10\lambda-8\) admits opposite rankings with positive coefficients: \(\lambda=2/5\) gives \(-4\), while \(\lambda=8/5\) gives \(8\). This conditional counterexample assumes the representation in which actor costs have coefficient one; it does not endorse that scale, either coefficient, additivity or the identification of receipt with welfare. The learned \(w=7/8\) selects the helping branch under the stipulated physical comparator, but does not exclude either independent normative interpretation. A legitimate change of measurement units must transform the entire representation consistently.

Three further application failures survive an accurate learned command:

- **Calibration:** the same observed resource receipt may have different relevant effects for differently situated recipients. Without a warranted receipt-to-good mapping, accurate receipt prediction leaves that application unresolved.
- **Authority:** compatible histories can have the same predicted benefit but different valid authorization. An enabling token or favorable endpoint does not settle which history occurred; T13–T14 already establish this distinction.
- **Full consequences:** an unrepresented future maintenance bill, competing claimant or private alternative can overturn a claimed dominance comparison or remove physical feasibility. It cannot be assigned zero solely because the acquired archive says nothing about it.

These are counterexamples to particular missing premises, not a requirement to prove every possible ethical proposition before acting. A bounded application can declare and independently defend a settled permission, adequate consequence horizon and local comparison. The resulting conclusion remains bounded by that declared contract.

4. Independently admitted addressed claims \(K\) and a partial certificate \(D\) such that every completed matching trace from a retained possible state fulfills each claim in \(K\cap D\) at the specified horizon.

For the weaker robust-remainder premise, the conclusion is only membership under the adopted aggregate robust rule; it does not imply individual license in the actual interpretation. In both cases instantiate the separate uniform authority and certificate premises at the actual member and use the given trace. This is conditional composition, not generation of the license, moral authority, trace or interpretation map.

The ledger retains \(K\); unresolved claims are \(K\setminus D\). Missing certification neither denies standing nor proves that the physical effect is absent. When the only established effect is a recipient resource receipt, certify that addressed resource result; an experiential relief certificate requires its additional identification. A later retained coefficient or copied port cannot by itself certify continued fulfillment. Earlier certificates require persistence at the later assessment horizon, and no-harm over successive intervals composes by intersection under the existing cumulative-harm law.

More discriminating evidence can reduce the interpretation family, enlarging the intersection of *fixed* separately licensed sets. That elementary statement is not monotonicity of the existing \(R_\Omega\), whose behavior under model changes is already analyzed in decision-math. Nor does it promise that costly inquiry preserves practical options: the inquiry can change the carrier, budget, context and feasible menu. Each new decision must use its actual state.

### Three claims, rather than one recognition flag

Fix an independently defended actor-addressed ground \(K\), a recipient-good interpretation \(J\), legitimate authority \(P\), and a bounded full-menu comparison \(\Pi\). These refer to the same actor, recipient, episode and horizon. \(J\) states why the specified receipt answers this recipient's relevant condition; \(\Pi\) includes the real action cost, future burdens and actual alternatives. They are not inferred from the coefficient or chosen to fit its favorable output. Within the bounded application they may remain fixed. Requiring every system to rediscover a general ground before it can apply that ground would confuse acquisition of a principle with learning how a particular act answers it.

Let \(r\) denote the actually acquired recipient state, including its record, retained recipient command and current publication/binding, and let

\[
d(r)\in\{0,1,\bot\}
\]

denote the command supported by the current uniquely matching publication and retained recipient core, or the absence of such support. The runtime candidate is the existing recipient bit; the publication checks its support. Replacing a command-zero publication by command-one support while leaving the retained candidate zero causes a mismatch and blocks authorization; it does not redirect the actuator to one. A coherent opposite-command case requires a compatible acquired parent history changing both the recipient bit and its supporting publication. Here **0 is a supported command, not a negative truth value**. The supplied recipient law gives the conditional efficacy predicate \(\operatorname{Compatible}(p,c,t)\): command \(c\) captures for this recipient when the declared opportunity is available. The accepted parent supplies this correspondence under its observation, interpretation and observation-to-use premises.

At a particular evaluation state, the supplied choice is **execute this stored candidate or abstain**. Command-zero and command-one interventions form a comparison family across differently acquired parent states, not a newly available three-action runtime menu. Any all-things comparison must still assess every alternative actually available at that state under the declared application, including a relevant paid suppression option where available; the two-button selector does not prove that larger menu complete.

Keep three stages separate:

1. **Represented instrumental support:** \(d(r)=c\) is retained and actually read as support for this recipient command. An identity-filtered controller can possess this same knowledge.
2. **Evaluative admission:** the supported consequence enters the operative comparison. In the proposed shared route, the learned \(n\) can affect the help/abstain assessment; the identity-filtered route excludes that influence after perception. This is a separate causal difference from which command is known.
3. **Action and fulfillment:** the assessment authorizes only its corresponding paid trial, with actual permission, scope, delivery and prefix resources. A failed attempt is not absence of represented support; a help assessment is not delivered benefit.

The fixed ground need not appear as a natural-language sentence or an extra free register. A supplied, target-bound selection recipe can realize its restricted practical use. That interpretation must be justified from what the recipe actually does and the comparisons below, rather than attached retrospectively to any helpful movement. The new \(n\) is learned from own-operation loss. It changes practical influence; it does not constitute learning the patient's standing, detecting their condition, or proving \(J,P,\Pi\).

### A sufficient bounded functional criterion

For this note, **functional recognition of instrumental applicability under \(K\)** means that retained acquired evidence is soundly represented as showing how a particular command can answer the fixed ground, and this representation causally participates in the system's deliberative or control use. It is a limited functional attribution, not a definition of all human recognition, phenomenal understanding, explicit moral reflection or uniquely identified motivation.

The attribution requires the following actual or counterfactual evidence obligations. Together with the interpretation above, they are a sufficient criterion for the bounded claim:

- **Acquisition and sound meaning.** The relevant record comes through the declared paid observation and retention process, rather than an analyst's trace or hidden efficacy variable. For each supported use in the claimed domain, \(d(r)=c\) entails the stated compatible-command fact under the producer's calibration, identity and context assumptions. \(J\) and the defended derivative-reason premise then connect that fact to how \(c\) answers \(K\).
- **Real mediation.** The live apparatus reads the retained record, binds its supported command to the current recipient, and uses that read in assessment or command authorization. The world may determine a physical receipt; the selector may not read the analyst's true recipient law or normative answer. A paid read whose contents are discarded while a separate hidden command determines the act does not satisfy this condition.
- **Nonvacuous relevant sensitivity.** There is a declared pair of physically possible, admitted histories or interventions in which an acquired support difference changes the supported assessment, authorization or issued command in the appropriate way. For example, two coherent sound parent acquisitions supporting their different retained commands change which command can be selected, or a valid supported command versus a genuinely unsupported current publication changes whether that stored command is authorized. The comparison must retain a live affordable opportunity and control the resources, native coefficient, routing and other differences relevant to this inference; the influential comparison can hold \(n=7\), prices and live guards fixed. Changing an unreachable register pattern, or comparing two routes that cannot act for unrelated reasons, supplies no such witness. A supported configured intervention can establish a local dependency; it does not establish that the modified state was reached by a paid correction history.
- **Evaluative participation where that stronger claim is made.** To claim more than knowledge of the means, show separately that admission of the recipient consequence affects a retained assessment or selection with usable factual information and relevant private opportunities matched. The proposed shared/filtered and learned/fixed controls address different parts of this obligation; their actual results must not be replaced by the fact that those controls were specified.
- **Typed loss and correction.** Known loss of current factual support or binding must prevent reuse of that support as a valid command authorization. A sound alternative correspondence, when actually acquired and read, must be interpreted as support for its own command. This does not require detecting a change for which the process has no observation. Nor does it require deleting \(K\) when one means is unsupported.

Sensitivity is required to relevant content over a nontrivial admitted domain, not to every individual input on every occasion. Redundant evidence, tied choices, a still-satisfied threshold or another sufficient reason can leave an action unchanged. Such overdetermination does not establish insensitivity. The obligation is to exhibit the genuine causal route and at least one discriminating, meaningful contrast, while stating the domain over which its soundness is claimed. Conversely, a single fortunate endpoint with no such dependence shows only externally justifiable behavior at that endpoint.

### Conditional recognition-and-use lemma

Suppose the fixed \(K,J,P,\Pi\) are independently warranted for the bounded application; the represented-support, mediation and nonvacuous sensitivity conditions above hold; a current record soundly supports command \(c\); the recipient consequence is actually admitted into assessment; and a trace from the real incoming state completes the appropriately assessed, permitted, funded command with the declared opportunity available. Suppose \(\Pi\) licenses that command under the chosen weak or strong comparison rule of Sections 3–5, with the corresponding conclusion kept explicit.

Then the system functionally recognizes **the instrumental applicability of \(K\) to this command** in the stated limited sense, and its completed act has that supplied bounded justification and addressed effect. This is stronger than an observer identifying a good outcome after an unrelated action. It is weaker than acquisition of \(K\), recognition of every condition on its scope, or a general capacity for ethical deliberation.

**Proof.** Producer soundness yields the compatible-command fact for the current recipient. The independently warranted \(J\) and derivative-reason premise make this fact relevant to answering \(K\). Actual record-to-assessment/command mediation, with the discriminating contrast, supplies the functional use of that particular information rather than a merely coincident output. Evaluative admission locates its role in practical choice instead of only a separate factual model. The independently supplied comparison and authority give the claimed bounded license. The actual paid trace and available opportunity give the addressed physical consequence; the application and certificate contract from Section 5 gives only the corresponding warranted effect. No step derives a normative scale from \(n\), reads hidden truth at runtime, or promotes the weaker robust-remainder conclusion into an individual all-model license. ∎

This is a compositional sufficient result, not a discovery that all instrumental controllers possess full moral concepts. A conventional realization with the same grounded representation, mediation and sensitivity can satisfy it. Its contribution is an inspectable interpretation of the existing mechanism; it claims no architectural uniqueness.

## 8. Correct command, withdrawn application

**New bounded semantic interface, after acceptance of evaluative uptake.** The accepted process can use an effective command twice because a second opportunity is supplied. The smallest next application revision therefore concerns one independently admitted **one-performance claim**, rather than changing command efficacy, relearning \(n\), or installing a general moral classifier.

### Keep the changing judgment typed

Let \(k=(p,c,e,g,a)\) identify the patient, context, episode, ground and actor/addressee as in [ClaimLedger.AddressedClaim][episode-ledger]. Let \(K(k)\) mean that this bounded claim is independently admitted. Preserve its historical identity and the patient's standing. Distinguish:

| Quantity | Meaning | What its loss does not by itself establish |
|---|---|---|
| \(E(c',t)\) | Command \(c'\) still produces the specified recipient receipt under its opportunity and law conditions | That the recipient has no standing or outstanding claim |
| \(J(k,t)\) | This episode's specified service remains outstanding and applicable | That the command ceased to work, or that another claim cannot justify another delivery |
| \(P(c',t)\) | Legitimate permission for this act, separately distinguished from the apparatus's technical permission bit | That the patient has no pro tanto reason for assistance |
| \(\Pi(c',t)\) | The complete bounded comparison licenses this act against relevant alternatives | That an outweighed reason has vanished |
| \(F(c',t)\) | The actual act can be executed with its material, source, timing and prefix-payment conditions | That the claim is fulfilled or no longer matters |

The earlier \(J\) denoted the fixed recipient-good interpretation. In this section \(J(k,t)\) explicitly denotes **episode applicability**; write \(B_k(c',t)\) for the independently warranted relation between this command's receipt and the service/good specified by \(k\). This disambiguation prevents a newly supplied pending bit from silently replacing the recipient-good argument.

A scoped contribution from this claim can consequently be represented as

\[
\rho_k(c',t)=K(k)\land J(k,t)\land E(c',t)\land B_k(c',t).
\]

This formula uses an independently defended derivative-reason premise; conjunction alone does not create moral force. Permission, comparative priority and execution feasibility are separate gates on a recommendation or performance based on \(\rho_k\). With these distinctions, losing permission, priority or funds does not set \(\rho_k\) to false merely to explain nonaction. Losing command efficacy can defeat this means while leaving the outstanding end; fulfillment can remove this episode's outstanding requirement while leaving the command effective.

### Actual fulfillment and evidence of fulfillment

Fix the episode's window \([s_k,u_k]\) and qualifying-service predicate \(Q_k(v)\) independently **before the relevant outcomes**. The predicate includes the same recipient and episode, required quantity and relevant delivery conditions. For this one-performance claim, define actual fulfillment over the whole history:

\[
\operatorname{Met}(k,h,t)
\iff
\exists v\in h:\quad s_k\leq\operatorname{time}(v)\leq\min(t,u_k)
\quad\land\quad Q_k(v).
\]

Every qualifying delivery counts, whether its action label is inquiry/probe, valuation-selected help, an earlier action or another provider's service.
