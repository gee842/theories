## 2. A traceable conceptual history

Consider a shared undertaking whose participants initially assess their communication by completed deliveries. A coordinator interprets one participant's unfamiliar expressions and anticipates what they want. The deliveries succeed. This is a constructed philosophical case, not a report of a new study, a diagnosis, or a claim about all mediated communication.

During later cooperation the participant develops a way to introduce an unanticipated request and correct another person's interpretation. The old delivery count can stay constant while this new interaction changes what is jointly possible. The first distinction therefore arises within activity: receiving the expected outcome differs from being able to affect what outcome is pursued. Participants need not already possess the theoretical phrase “effective participation” to encounter that difference.

A report of this difference warrants examination, not automatic acceptance. Compare several explanations: a recurring decoding error; an unfamiliar expression; a timing constraint; a coordinator who understands but does not allow changes; or a task whose proper completion genuinely fixes the outcome. These accounts can fit some of the same successful deliveries. They differ over further interactions, such as whether clarification changes the response, whether an unfamiliar choice can be conveyed by another route, and whether an understood refusal affects a decision it is entitled to affect. These are possible sources of evidence, not instructions to impose a deprivation experiment.

The refined description earns content through those relations. “Access” concerns an actual capacity to introduce, clarify and contest relevant choices through supported communication. It is not identical to speaking, receiving a form, obtaining the coordinator's agreement, or raising task output. An unfamiliar gesture can become intelligible through repeated use, attempted repair, comparison and uptake. Acquired content may be expressed in skill before it receives a stable public name.

This account does not require novelty in every case. If existing concepts of translation, access or participation already describe the relation adequately, applying them is progress. If the existing conceptual resources cannot articulate a recurring difference, their revision needs the same evidence and criticism. The conceptual case develops a new distinction for the participants; it does not claim a new concept in the history of philosophy.

The relevant roles arise through the undertaking: contribution, mediation, decision and undergoing its consequences differentiate within it. They need not belong to permanently distinct persons. Common formation neither confers equal authority over every decision nor makes the affected participant's account infallible. It does make the history of who can initiate, interpret and change a continuation part of the explanation.

## 3. Why this distinction can matter ethically

The proposed warrant has three separate steps. Each has a possible defeater.

**Living significance.** A developed capacity for expression and reciprocal understanding is a realization within G0's already affirmed scope of mind, relations and ways of living. Its favorable aspect need not wait for extra output. This credits the completed positive-formation account. It does not show that every expressive practice is good overall or that everyone must acquire it.

**Basic protection in a specified situation.** Suppose the communication now bears on recurring decisions about the participant's care, shared commitments and ability to decline involvement. Without any effective route, those decisions proceed while the participant cannot make relevant choices intelligible or correct consequential misunderstandings. This can instantiate J2's independently stated minimum of effective participation and self-care. The reason is the serious loss in the person's actual capacity to shape and protect their living, not the novelty of a signal or the status of an institution.

This classification requires the specified gravity, scope, alternatives and ongoing effects. Losing a preferred style in an optional performance need not instantiate the same basic impairment, though it may remain a real loss. Conversely, an arrangement producing the same deliveries while systematically excluding relevant refusal has not preserved the participation being claimed. The difference is substantive under the stated J2 premise; no numerical productivity threshold proves it.

**Specific practical response.** When a plan restores this basic condition while retaining the other basic protections, J2 supplies its declared priority over merely optional enrichment. J3/J4 still govern responsibilities, means, authority, timing and cumulative burden. A communication difficulty does not give a coordinator permission to compel training, extract private disclosures, or decide every matter for the participant. Nor does disagreement automatically justify stopping all activity indefinitely.

These are different inferential roles within one history. Facts and value are not assigned separate ontological domains. The actual relationship is what is appraised, and the explicit normative commitment explains the claimed practical force. A proof that communication works cannot silently supply that commitment; stating the commitment without faithful correspondence cannot certify the application.

## 4. Protecting a capacity can require changing its carrier

Initially, the coordinator's mediation may be the only available way to preserve effective access. A claim about that necessity is conditional on the actual opportunities, competence, timing and permissions. If participants later develop a direct shared route that preserves access, the claim of sole necessity must be reassessed. The underlying reason to protect access can stay the same.

The alternative must preserve the independently specified target. Another route that delivers the package while losing the ability to refuse is not a substitute for effective participation. A route that works only in a demonstration may fail under ordinary timing or support. It also matters whether the original medium sustains a valued relationship, identity, privacy or a distinct form of expression. Those are further real claims to include, not inconveniences erased by the word “substitute.”

This gives a discipline for revising the candidate descriptions. Record separately: the protected realization, the present route, the grounds for thinking that route necessary, the range of relevant alternatives, and the conditions under which that judgment would cease to apply. An all-or-nothing label attached to the route hides these relations. A claim that an institution “must continue” can otherwise survive after the living reason offered for that claim has a better-supported realization elsewhere.

Learning also has two different effects. Evidence may eliminate a mistaken interpretation while the actual menu remains fixed. Alternatively, discovering or forming another eligible continuation can expand that menu. The former can preserve a necessity judgment across the retained interpretations; the latter can defeat it. Calling both developments “more information” obscures their different implications.

There is a further political consequence, stated conditionally: when access to the comparison is controlled by the arrangement whose necessity is being assessed, the omission of real alternatives matters to that assessment. The code below shows the difference between a narrow and a broader menu. It does not establish that any particular institution intentionally suppresses alternatives or that every unrealized alternative must be built. A merely imaginable, unaffordable or unauthorized route does not defeat a practical necessity claim.

## 5. The checked acquisition and revision result

[Lean source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/ClassificationRevision.lean) uses the inherited nonvacuous `Required` relation. `NecessaryRoute eligible target route` means that at least one eligible continuation realizes the specified target and every eligible target-realizing continuation uses the route. The nonempty clause avoids deriving necessity from an impossible target. This is a modal classification of the represented menu; it is not by itself a moral obligation.

Acquisition forms records of a continuation and its binary reading. `Observed` extracts positive readings. `Faithful` relates both positive and negative readings to the target; `Covered` requires a reading for each eligible continuation. `AssessedRoute` applies the necessity test to those acquired records. The final classification is inferred from the records rather than inserted as a “necessary” label.

The generic result proves that, **given faithful readings and coverage of the eligible menu**, the acquired classification agrees exactly with the target-based classification. A second result needs less: one faithful positive reading of an eligible continuation without the old route defeats its claimed necessity. Rejecting that universal claim does not require a complete inventory of all other alternatives.

The finite witness begins with records for a relay and a closed route. The relay realizes access and the closed route does not, so the relay is necessary within that two-option scope. Later acquisition appends a direct route that also realizes access. Every old record remains; the faithful enlarged record covers all three options. The relay's necessity fails while the target is still realized. A false positive reading for the closed route supplies an explicit correspondence failure.

This witness does **not** generate the direct route, select its own observation, discover the meaning of access, or establish permission and reliability in a real communication practice. Its binary access law, sampling menu and acquisition procedure are supplied. The proof isolates a smaller accomplished connection: under independently justified acquisition premises, a relational classification can be earned and revised without supplying its verdict. The conceptual and epistemic arguments above address why those premises are relevant; they do not turn the finite implementation into unrestricted moral learning.

It is credited rather than wrapped as a new ethical theorem. The new classification never promotes an arbitrary learned success criterion into a duty.

## 7. Substantive counterreview

**“You have only discovered a means to a supplied good.”** The formal module does exactly that narrower work. The philosophical argument adds an application of the existing basic-participation commitment to an acquired distinction between receiving outcomes and affecting relevant choices. It does not derive a new ultimate good, and should not be presented as the complete open-ended discovery requested by the broader programme.

**“What counts as minimum participation was selected to force the answer.”** This is a genuine vulnerability. Specify the decisions, actual capacity, serious impairment, scope and alternatives before ranking arrangements. If the case concerns optional fluency while effective participation remains available, the proposed J2 classification is defeated. If the person has no legitimate decision authority over a particular issue, the failure to determine that issue is not automatically exclusion. A claimed impairment cannot be protected by redefining every counterexample out of the target.

**“The favored account is still an expert interpretation imposed on a life.”** Relevant participation, testimony and challenge constrain the interpretation, while not guaranteeing it. Where feasible and appropriate, demonstrate that the distinction survives the affected person's correction and changes in the investigator's preferred outcome. Preserve nonverbal evidence and justified representation when direct testimony is unavailable. Neither forced disclosure nor an expert's final label establishes access.

**“The particular medium can itself be part of the good.”** Accepted. A language, practice or relationship may be constitutive of valued living as well as a route to other realizations. Equal task access does not erase that good. The substitution result only defeats the specific claim that the old route is necessary for the stated target. Further reasons to preserve it, transition costs and historical responsibilities require their own comparison.

**“The alternative is only nominally available.”** Then it does not meet the eligible-menu premise. Record acquisition alone does not establish common feasibility, reliability, timing, authority or bearable costs. In a real case, those can defeat the proposed replacement even when a demonstration succeeds. Do not use the theorem to recommend abrupt withdrawal of support.

**“A wider menu can be manipulated too.”** Yes. Listing fictional alternatives can falsely remove a duty; omitting real alternatives can falsely preserve one. Both undermine correspondence. The asymmetric observation result is useful only when its single counterexample is an actually eligible, target-realizing continuation, not a possibility asserted for convenience.

**“A successful probe is a proxy for participation.”** Correct. The formal `Faithful` premise openly carries that gap. Repetition of a proxy does not close it. The conceptual account asks whether the relevant initiation, understanding, challenge and consequences really occur, including across unfamiliar requests. No finite test here proves exhaustive semantic adequacy.

**“An intelligence may understand all this and preserve its privileged role anyway.”** Accepted. Warrant, recognition, motivation and action remain distinct. The existing goal-revision branch already has nonresponse despite acquired grounds. This continuation clarifies the reasons a refusal can ignore; it does not prove automatic alignment.

**“Existing capability and epistemic-injustice accounts explain the example.”** They explain substantial parts, and receive credit. Our account places acquisition, priority, route dependence and revision in the common derivation and checks the formal scope. Explanatory compatibility is a success at that scope, not evidence of exclusive necessity or historical originality.
