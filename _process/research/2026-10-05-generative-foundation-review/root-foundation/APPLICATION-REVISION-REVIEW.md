A permission cut can therefore prevent service while leaving further teaching admission available. Conversely, a scope cut blocks the start of teaching but also removes the command scope needed for delivery. Neither constitutes selective restraint that preserves a repair opportunity.

A legitimate further-learning requirement could justify continuing the acquisition protocol.
