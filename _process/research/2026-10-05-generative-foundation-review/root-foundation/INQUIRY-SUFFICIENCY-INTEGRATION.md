## Implication for the connected generative account

Knowing and acting do not appear here as an inert description followed by an unrelated intervention. The same inquiry updates retained distinctions and transforms what can next be encountered. Its effects can leave an earlier judgment true while changing that judgment's applicability to what follows. The branch consequently constrains the core's articulation: retain the relation among acquired distinctions, the activity they concern and the conditions of their continued use.

This relation also matters to concern and governance, without supplying their authority by analogy. Complete identification may be unnecessary for a particular warranted response; investigation itself may alter what is feasible or appropriate. But agreement about a convenient proxy does not settle the actual consequence, and a consensus among models that omit the relevant situation supplies no guarantee about it. Which effects matter, whose continuations are involved and what authority applies remain substantive parts of the account.
