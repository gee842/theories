## Substantive review

The canonical preserves three distinct obligations. An actual experiencing subject and episode must be identified. C1 proposes that a restricted experiential whole constitutes local badness for that subject. C2 proposes that the prospective eligible relief relation constitutes moral favor for the actor's option. The text explicitly denies that C1 alone entails C2. It does not define moral favor as bare causal relief or infer experience from negative reward, feedback or organizational persistence.

The revised patient domain retains the final argument's limits. It concerns a severe, still-unwanted aversive episode and excludes established intrinsic enjoyment or appreciation of that same occurrence that may alter its prudential status. Instrumental acceptance of an episode for another benefit is distinguished from that exclusion. The domain is not an operational consciousness classifier, and the enjoyment and whole-life objections remain genuine challenges. The account does not answer them merely by calling badness an aspect or by changing the domain after every objection.

C2 is explicitly prospective: moral favor, if grounded as proposed, exists before both endorsement and execution. Actual delivery is not required to create the prior reason. Recognition and implementation remain separate; acquired concern can be assessed against a ground without making every kind of concern learned. Permission, positive favor, decisive duty, rational authority and effective motivation are not collapsed.

The main advance beyond the inherited identity arguments is the timed comparison with elective concern. Private adoption and abandonment change the actor's mind while preserving the patient, opportunity and relevant external relations. The text does not pretend that complete physical histories are identical. A rival must distinguish a current attitude from a valid commitment that can survive repudiation; the disappearing-favor prediction applies only where its alleged condition legitimately ends. A separate promise or entrusted-role arm can introduce real new reasons.

The exact conditional challenge is sound: an account accepting the same prospective patient-grounded moral justification before adoption has already accepted the target moral favor. Denying that justification, accepting only permission, or locating it in an additional relation remains a substantive alternative. This clarifies the rival rather than proving its impossibility. Manne-style agreement on moral claims alongside disagreement on rational authority is preserved as a different dispute.

The candidate's positive defense identifies the patient's improvement, the merit of prospective relief, and correction against the actual patient. Its adequacy is not inferred merely from the rival's lack of a supplied counterexample. Choice may create further reasons; the question is whether it creates every patient-directed reason. The conditional connection to concern and wisdom retains costs, competing centers, authority, fulfillment and the distinction between patient relief and removal of a patient or report. It supplies no total ranking or maximizing command.

## New contribution, inherited work and evidence

The experiential and practical identities, favorable relief judgment and several implementation contrasts already appear in the self-ground audit §§29–32 and the preceding practical-uptake work. The canonical credits them. This round's specific contribution is their joint adequacy contract, restricted patient domain, private-adoption test and separation of realization, patient-sufficiency, practical-scope and implementation failures. It is not presented as a newly proved identity, mathematical theorem or independent corroboration of the same ethical argument.

The identification study supplies bounded empirical constraints, not moral certification. Rainville 1999 was read at complete-abstract scope; independence from the earlier 1997 work was not verified. The source investigator read Becker 2017's main article, whose combined painfulness/unpleasantness scale cannot isolate pure valence. Acceptance may change with experience rather than demonstrate its constancy. Neither study establishes C1, C2, artificial sentience or a universal realization criterion.

The coordinating investigator independently inspected Rainville's complete abstract and metadata. Its Becker attempt failed, so the detailed Becker assessment remains delegated. This integration reviewer read the entire source note without independently reopening either primary work. Temporary-download hashes are access receipts, not claims that original PDFs were imported. No new participant experiment, scientific rerun or formal compilation was performed for this round.

Two canonical precisions were requested and rechecked. The clean-domain paragraph now explicitly carries the enjoyment/appreciation exclusion instead of relying only on its link to the argument. “Concern is then an acquired orientation” was replaced with an assessment of acquired concern, expressly avoiding a claim that every concern must be learned. The prospective C2 wording and valid-renunciation caveat were also checked against the final companion argument.
