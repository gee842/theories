# Carrier succession, diagnosis and continued activity

8 October 2026. Continuation of [Ren's selected definition](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-meta-attractor-generative-integration.md), the [current account](../../../../theories/meta-attractor/GENERATIVE-REGIMES.md), and the first [integration](META-ATTRACTOR-INTEGRATION.md). This is an AI-authored argument and counterreview. It is not independent acceptance or empirical validation.

**The new connection is consequence return → reconstruction through different carriers → an executed probe → possible repair → further recurrence, within one actual history.** The passive-observation limit remains true, but does not prevent this constructive continuation. Diagnosis is itself activity within the regime: it changes the conditions whose reproduction is being investigated.

**新的连接是：后果回返 → 不同承载者的接续 → 实际辨认 → 必要修复 → 继续回返。** 识别活动与所辨认的过程处于同一历史；获取区别的同时可能消耗继续活动的条件。因此，“知道发生了什么”与“仍能实际回应”需要分别检验并连接。

## What is reconstructed

[CarrierDiagnosis.lean](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/CarrierDiagnosis.lean) imports the first construction's two return routes and the existing `Generative.Process`. A state records a selecting condition, carrier identity and productive variant, reconstruction parts, convertible material, intervention capacity, return route and latest observation. These are local, differentiated roles. Material conversion, replacement and observation connect them in each event; no independent observer-world ontology is asserted.

The represented organization is the relation among condition-dependent activity, output return and reconstruction of its successor. Its particular carriers alternate between yields of two and three material units. The common organization therefore preserves a causal role and a proved maintenance range, rather than an identical numerical output. Both variants lose one condition unit per cycle and can reproduce the selected region. The ordinary successor event consumes a beginning-stock part, changes identity and variant, consumes its actual material output, and then receives one part and three material units from a supplied continuation. The resource arrival is explicit; its wider formation is not derived here.

The `activity` route returns the selected activity's output. The `substitute` route transfers the same represented quantity under a direct condition-triggered supply law. Each consumes that material; neither obtains output without the represented debit. Cutting the named activity-return route diverts the first output while leaving the substitute transfer intact. The comparison isolates this route under a declared local law. It does not classify the substitute process as globally non-generative or prove that a wider organization containing it is not a meta-attractor.

Ordinary histories record condition, carrier identity and variant, parts, material, intervention capacity and receipts. Their equality across the two routes is proved for every time, including unready initial states. Thus adding these particular records does not by itself reveal provenance. The route-sensitive probe does distinguish them. A readout uses only its receipt—before and after condition, output capacity, intervention flag—and never reads the hidden route field. The formal correctness claim is relative to these two known architectures, exact observation and the stated isolated cut.

组织同一性在这里是相关作用关系的接续，并不要求所有数量相同。两种产出能力具有同样的维护作用，但资源消耗及具体轨迹不同。另一路来源只是所指定回返路线的实质对手；扩大研究边界以后，它也可能属于另一种回返组织，不能直接判为“非元吸引子”。

## Observation and recovery share material conditions

A probe consumes one intervention-capacity unit, one part and the carrier's emitted material. It receives no replacement inputs during that event. If the activity route is cut at condition three, the condition falls to two. A subsequent repair can transfer one remaining material unit into that condition, consuming one more intervention-capacity unit. Repair preserves the receipt of what actually happened; it does not retrospectively turn the probe into an ordinary successful cycle.

The field `funds` is an inherited local coding name for a discrete intervention capacity. No monetary transfer is represented. Its units are not interchangeable with the material stock or the selecting condition. The material balance explicitly includes worn, diverted and overflow amounts. Such additive accounting belongs to this example; it is not a foundational claim that all conditions admit one resource measure.

`Prepared` gives uniform sufficient conditions: selecting condition between three and eight, at least two parts, material at least the carrier capacity plus four, and at least two intervention-capacity units. Under these assumptions, the actual probe identifies either route, any necessary repair restores the recurrent region, a reconstructed carrier remains, and ordinary recurrence can follow for every finite duration. `actual_diagnosis_and_recurrence` constructs the entire event trace, including repair only when executed. Continued operation still depends on the ordinary supplied arrivals.

`Prepared` is sufficient, not necessary. With initial condition three, two parts, six material units and two intervention units, the activity-route probe lowers the condition to two; repair restores three, leaving three material units, one part and zero intervention units. The successor has yield three. Further ordinary conditions are five, six and eight, through different identities and variants. With only one intervention unit, the same immediate probe correctly identifies the route but cannot repair the drop. With only two material units, funding alone cannot restore it. These are checked counterexamples to identifying diagnosis with recoverability.

There is also a checked timing contrast. Starting with the same one-unit intervention limit, one ordinary cycle first raises the condition to four and replenishes material. A subsequent probe lowers the condition to three; no repair is necessary, and the result lies in the recurrent region. **Waiting can improve recoverable identification.** The first construction separately proves that waiting can increase the impulse needed for exit. The two results concern different operations and objectives; they do not jointly prove a numerical tradeoff in one physical implementation or a universal rule to wait or hurry.

在这个构造中，资源充分时可以取得来源区别、修复探测造成的下降，再由不同承载者继续回返。资源不足时，正确识别也可能留下无法维持的状态。一次等待可使探测不再需要修复；旧模型中等待却可提高退出代价。应按实际操作、时序及目的分别判断，不能把“越早探测越好”或“越久越稳越好”写成普遍结论。

## Counterreview and prior work

This continuation improves the first witness's abstract turnover: carrier variation now changes actual output and material use. It still supplies a two-variant successor recipe. It does not derive biological assembly, transmission of an institution's entire organization or an arbitrary equivalence among carriers. Those are separate realization questions. The organization signature must be specified independently in an application and retain the relations relevant to its claimed recurrence.

The diagnosis is not an oracle: source is hidden from the readout, and the observable equality proof makes the passive rival substantive. But the known candidate class, isolated intervention and faithful sensing are strong correspondence premises. Unrepresented routes, a changed intervention effect or a misleading receipt can defeat application. More formal correctness within this class cannot establish that the class contains an actual case.

Preserving the represented recurrent region is also not automatically desirable. A destructive institution can satisfy it. The theorem concerns a nominated continuation; ethical reasons to preserve, transform or end it still require the actual wider effects and the established G0/practical comparison. There is no implication that someone ought to restore the regime just because this model can do so.

The problem of informative action with recoverability is established in control and learning. [Berkenkamp, Turchetta, Schoellig and Krause (2017)](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.08551v2) connect exploration to stability and an initial safe policy under explicit continuity and model-calibration assumptions. Reading scope: abstract, introduction and §2. This is a primary comparator, not a theorem rechecked here or evidence for this particular model. Our discrete construction studies a specified return route across carrier succession, with material consumption and repair in one core history. No priority claim for safe exploration, active identification or dual control is made.

## What is now closed, and where the inference stops

The previously missing *conditional* join between substantive carrier variation, executed observation, repair and subsequent activity is now present. Repeating that requirement as wholly unimplemented would erase this result. Source-resolved field evidence, other forms of carrier construction, and broader model classes remain possible extensions, with their own required evidence.

The current wall is precise: a recurrence history, even with the listed resource and carrier records, does not determine which tested route reproduces selection. An intervention can cross this information limit only under additional assumptions about its target, execution and observation. The definition cannot itself supply those assumptions. This is a limit on unconditional diagnosis, not a failure to integrate meta-attractors into generative theory or proof that all possible investigation must stop.

The original request asks for that theoretical integration, higher-order selection, boundary conditions and recognition of approach. It does not require a newly measured field case or a universally calibrated warning instrument. Earlier wording that made a general empirically identified regime theory a mandatory completion gate went beyond the request. Its empirical cautions remain valid; its extra gate is withdrawn. The adopted definition remains intact, and its general explanatory content is not replaced by the numerical witness.
