## What changes in the connected account

an audit. The [new native continuation](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/INQUIRY.md)

present productive-use component. A detectable consequence of that event can
be acquired, retained and read, so the changed situation governs subsequent
inquiry. Acquisition itself consumes support and can fail after an effect has
already occurred. These are differentiated relations in one unfolding history.

This is stronger than an analyst announcing that a question has become
unnecessary: the actual controller stops on the acquired indication. It is
narrower than learning what counts as completion: the episode and its reading
rule are supplied by the declared local construction. Calling them supplied
locally identifies an unresolved provenance in this explanation, not an

uncertainty remains. Under false contact, the controller can mistakenly stop or
continue. The representation therefore preserves the connections among effects,
their acquisition and further activity without identifying every report with
its target. The inadequate abstraction would erase these differences or impose
complete identification as the only legitimate end of inquiry for this episode.
The original all-word sensory undertaking retains its distinct, stronger contract.
