# Mechanisms that make living continuation depend on renewed conditions

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored scientific continuation after Ren redirected the current session to **living dependencies**. It advances the previous [grounding assessment](LIVING-GENERATIVITY-GROUNDING.md) by identifying a mechanistic source of maintenance demand, rather than adding another generic depletion premise. The [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) governs the interpretation. Ethical grounding continues separately; this note makes no new normative claim.

**Main advance:** maintaining a physiological relation under actual transport can itself require compensatory activity. In a membrane-transport regime, continuing inward transfer changes the condition that the organism must retain; active outward transport counteracts that change and requires available work. The relevant dependence can therefore be traced to an actual exchange law and an operative range, rather than to an analyst's arbitrary countdown. This is a scientifically motivated, scoped mechanism. It is not a universal positive maintenance constant or a deterministic lifespan assigned to every cell.

The second mechanism concerns the transition from protected inheritance to active use. Repair can affect the fidelity and timing of resumed organization even when it is not required at every instant of dormancy. Specific repair pathways need not be individually indispensable. This prevents the common account from identifying living continuation with either immutable material retention or obligatory replacement of every part.

## 2. Discriminating observations and models

### Non-growing survival and collective provision

Schink and colleagues' 2019 experiments distinguish maintenance from new biomass production. Nutrients in the supernatant of a starving culture temporarily arrest viability loss; controls using fresh carbon-free medium or pre-starvation supernatant do not. Added dead biomass also supplies nutrients. Their model uses viable population $N$, maintenance demand $J_d=\beta N$, and recycled supply $J_s=-\alpha\dot N$; the reported exponential decay follows the supply–demand balance. A wasteful LacY transport activity increases death rate, whereas expression without its transported substrate does not produce the same effect. RpoS changes recycling efficiency. These are measured collective dependencies, not independent radioactive-style death clocks. The assays use colony formation and selected microscopy; the reported regime must not be extended indefinitely beyond its studied phase. [Primary publication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S240547121930198X).

### Why the maintained relation incurs demand

The 2024 study identifies ion homeostasis in carbon-starved *E. coli*: active export compensates inward ion transfer and maintains plasmolysis. Its single-effective-ion model is $\dot c_{in}=P(c_{in})(c_{out}-c_{in})-j_{pump}(c_{in})+D\xi(t)$. The coarse-grained steady-state account gives $j_{out}=P\Delta c$, transport demand $\beta=\kappa P\Delta c$, and recycling balance $\alpha\gamma=\beta$. These use explicit approximations, including rapid nutrient redistribution. Permeability and salt interventions test the proposed relation; impermeable osmolyte controls distinguish ion-transfer effects from osmotic stress alone. An osmotically balanced, low-salt medium improves survival. Depolarization can be reversed before lysis by nutrient readdition. The proposed death mechanism is stochastic escape; it does not assign a common deterministic expiry time. [Primary author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12442806/).

### Protected inheritance, active use and repair alternatives

Ibarra and colleagues find that loss of Nfo/ExoA slows spore outgrowth and increases mutation frequencies. Germination itself remains similar. Their controls address prior heat activation, damage already in stored DNA, and ordinary vegetative growth differences; added peroxide and other repair/protection mutants help localize the effect. Crucially, the tested deletions do **not** stop outgrowth. The authors discuss error-prone bypass as an alternative to repair. The evidence supports a dependence of faithful, timely recovery on particular protective and repair relations; it does not prove those enzymes indispensable for any continued life. Mutation frequency is assayed through rifampin resistance, not by complete genome sequencing. [Primary publication](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jb.01625-07).

## 3. What the common mechanism explains

The following is our synthesis, not a further experimental finding.

The starting point is a differentiated organization whose operation depends on a relation being maintained within an effective range. The membrane and its contrasting concentrations are parts of that actual organization, formed within the same history. They are not primitive independent domains called system and environment. The same interface that enables exchange also changes what later exchange and organized activity can do.

A loss or inward transfer matters because of its effect on that relation. Compensatory transport, reconstruction or a changed relation can prevent departure from the effective range. This explains why keeping an organization operational can require activity even when its total biomass does not increase. The needed activity is identified by a causal mechanism; persistence alone does not name it.

The scientific advance over a supplied natural-number rank is therefore the direction of explanation: **actual transfer changes a bounded operative condition; continued realization constrains compensatory transfer; that transfer is connected to work-support through its mechanism.** The rank or support account may then summarize those relations. A summary cannot establish the relations by itself.

This framework also explains why changing organization can lower demand. The necessity concerns an actual realization law. If another arrangement changes the relevant transfer, protection or support relationship, it can change the cost or timing of maintaining the living activity. The old law is not an eternal command to repeat the same operation.

The target must retain its fidelity and horizon. Continuing some viable descendant, retaining a particular functioning organization, and reproducing a specified sequence with low error are different targets. A successful altered continuation does not establish faithful retention; failed faithful retention does not establish that all living continuation has stopped. These distinctions are necessary for interpreting both experimental outcomes and later proofs.

## 4. A disciplined bridge to the general Lean interface

The companion formal investigator was given the source relations before choosing a theorem. The proposed bridge is an explicitly deterministic, discrete transport idealization. It connects a bounded internal amount, inward transfer, effective export and available work-support along the **same actual history**. Its purpose is to derive a dependency account from coupled transfer balances rather than label a freely supplied countdown “biological maintenance.”

The required correspondence is precise:

- A represented amount and its admissible bound must refer to an identified operative relation. A concentration model cannot be silently treated as an amount model when volume changes; fixed reference volume or an explicit volume account is required.
- Represented inward transfer must be identified under the actual gradient and transport regime. Unmodeled outward routes cannot be silently excluded when claiming necessity of one export mechanism.
- A coupling between export and available work is a scoped conversion law with declared units. It is not a universal cost of activity or an assertion that one ion always costs one ATP.
- Noise, changing geometry, multiple ionic species and alternative transport routes are omitted by this idealization. Their absence must remain visible; the source's stochastic mortality predictions are not thereby formalized.
- A bound on the operative relation is not automatically an irreversible death threshold. Restoration before irreversible failure and changes of regime require their own representation.

Under a successfully checked formulation, cumulative actual transfer can supply the demand term of the existing general necessity theorem. This note does not claim that compilation has occurred or assert a new mathematical result; the integrating report must use the final module and its actual verification receipt. ## 5. What recurrence would require beyond these studies

The common explanatory mechanism does not depend on a single positive cost at every moment. Retained conditions can suffice during a finite interval. The earlier [dormant-spore assessment](LIVING-GENERATIVITY-GROUNDING.md#4-a-primary-test-operative-inheritance-during-dormancy) already illustrates why contemporaneous reconstruction cannot be assumed everywhere. The [independent coverage review](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-COVERAGE-REVIEW.md) adds a focused primary analysis of long-lived constituents and heterogeneous exchange, preserving the distinction between turnover and growth-associated dilution. That reviewer read the Toyama study; this note credits the review rather than claiming a second primary reading.

Three propositions must remain distinct:

| Proposition | Evidence or proof needed |
|---|---|
| A particular continued realization needs compensatory activity. | An identified transfer/loss mechanism, an operative requirement and a warranted absence of adequate unrepresented alternatives. |
| Compensatory activity recurs along the actual history. | Actual continuing transfer or loss exceeds what retained provision can accommodate, with restoration genuinely recurring in the same history. |
| Every independently specified living continuation has such recurring demand. | Coverage of that domain by justified mechanisms, including protected retention, dormancy, changed realizations and succession. The present studies do not establish this universal statement. |

Stochastic loss deserves a separate scope. A measured decline in the viable fraction is not a theorem that every individual fails at a finite common deadline. Nor does a distribution fitted over an observed interval independently establish what occurs over all future times. A proof about all histories under deterministic balances and a probabilistic claim about a biological ensemble answer different questions. They can be connected, but the connection must be stated and checked.

Likewise, renewed support for one part can involve the ending of another. A whole account must preserve that source and loss instead of reporting only the supported part. This is a physical accounting requirement here, not a new ethical endorsement or prohibition. A local support loop should not be mistaken for unlimited renewal of the whole.

## 6. Current conclusion and next discriminating test

This round advances a real explanatory premise: maintained disequilibrium plus actual transport can explain maintenance demand, and phase-specific protection and repair can explain the quality of recovery from retained organization. The evidence makes both the affirmative mechanism and its boundaries more concrete. It does not establish that every molecule must turn over, that every living position must be productive at each moment, or that life has a universal deterministic expiration law.

The next useful test is correspondence under a **changed realization**: does a demonstrated reduction in transfer or change in protective organization preserve the independently specified living target while altering its maintenance requirement? The mechanism predicts dependence on the operative relation, rather than on elapsed time alone or a fixed quantity called “life's cost.” For the broad claim, domains that retain living capacity with very low activity are substantive tests of coverage, not cases to exclude by definition.

The new mathematical bridge should be accepted only at its checked transfer-law scope. Scientific coverage remains an empirical and explanatory task. This keeps the session on the living-dependency objective without reopening a generic ethical-warrant discussion or relabeling an old conditional theorem as a completed theory of life.
