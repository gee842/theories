# When a formed practical boundary becomes answerable to its consequences

6 October 2026. AI-authored argument under the [project-wide nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **a practical scope forms through consequential activity; encountering those consequences can warrant revision of how the activity admits, treats and produces them**. The shared configuration is the existing paid plasticity history reconstructed in [the mechanism audit](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-RECONSTRUCTION.md), with [independent counterreview](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-COUNTERREVIEW.md). No new controller, theorem or empirical result is claimed.

**Positive proposal:** a formation can incur a relation of repair and restraint through the very operations that develop its responsiveness. Correcting its practical boundary then concerns how it continues those operations, not only which already represented recipient gets favorable influence. The proposal is defended below at a bounded scope. Actual deprivation, warranted attribution and appropriate response still require justification; neither a stock decrement nor common generative origin proves them. This is not a completed assumption-free foundation.

## 1. Reconstruct one consequential formation

The [accepted plasticity study](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY-INTEGRATION.md) begins from the actual inherited `RevisionWitness.consumed` state with a declared preparation. Paid operations affect two distinguishable accounts; paid reads retain their consequences; binding fills initially empty conducting sockets; their transmission changes later assessment. These are parts of one unfolding, not an unaffected observer assigning value to a separate object.

In the four-pair branch, the recipient's incoming 40 becomes 36 while two positive links form and the native register reaches `n=4`. Its later service can bring the recipient to 46. The selected **continued** branch instead proceeds from the four teaching pairs to two additional pairs before closure or service. A recipient loss paired with no own loss enables paid replacement of one positive link by a negative one. At the end, active and frozen-sign arms have the same acquired pairs, `n=5`, recipient 34, fuel 16 and factual means knowledge. The active arm abstains; the frozen arm can deliver ten. The first branch's served endpoint 46 is not an intermediate state in this continuation.

The [operative law][plasticity] is precise: recipient loss enables binding or replacement; own loss determines the active sign. That criterion is fixed throughout. Learning changes signed connections under it, not the criterion itself. The [witness][witness] establishes the resulting histories and comparisons. It does not establish that the learner experiences loss, understands responsibility or offers any ethical justification.

The [NONDUAL-TREE continuation](NONDUAL-TREE-CONTINUATION.md) supplies a compatible method and a different established material learning history. It is not another segment of this trace. Its lesson here is to preserve how receiving consequences, changing records and changing activity depend on one another, while retaining each branch's actual composition limits.

## 2. Locate the practical omission accurately

Three achievements differ within this activity. A consequence can be **registered**, can **influence choice**, and can lead to **reconsidering the rule that governs its relevance**. This witness establishes the first two. Its negative replacement demonstrates why they do not establish the third or guarantee ethical improvement.

The recipient is not invisible. The acquired recipient loss contributes causally to a real replacement. What is absent from the operative criterion is an assessment of the relation between the imposed operations, the recipient's cumulative uncompensated loss and possible repair. The state need not contain a sentence describing responsibility for that relation to exist; conversely, a state field named `patient` cannot establish it. Nor is the sign rule making a false prediction: it implements its declared associative rule correctly.

Our separation of two accounts preserves where costs and returns occur and which consequences determine a sign. It omits the formation of those particular embodied positions, phenomenal realization, consent, institutional attribution and the origin of the local rule. Those omissions limit this representation. They do not show that the missing relationships belong outside generative activity. Treating the two balances as interchangeable would lose the very consequence the ethical proposal addresses.

The decisive additional observation is that these encounters are interventions. `recipientPay` removes two units from the same recipient account. The formation does not merely discover an antecedent need; under an appropriate ethical interpretation, it can create or enlarge an outstanding deprivation while developing a disposition to respond. The history of knowing is therefore part of what later response must answer.

## 3. A constitutive argument for repair-bearing formation

First fix the application without moralizing the counters. The removed resources or capabilities must actually sustain a differentiated being in a way relevant to its good or warranted possession. Identify who controlled or was answerable for the operations. Determine whether their burden was authorized, returned or compensated. Establish what the proposed remedy would change, with whose permission and at what further cost. The synthetic history supplies only some of this evidence. An operator's responsibility does not automatically become the learner's because both are described by one model.

In the case where the operations impose an attributable, continuing, uncompensated deprivation, the affirmative argument is this:

1. **Formation has changed the relationship.** The activity's acquired capacity and another position's remaining burden have a common operative history. The latter is not merely a remote misfortune newly represented by the former. This identifies a relation of answerability more specific than present causal access.
2. **The acquired capacity does not itself settle the burden.** A useful link or a better register can persist while the affected being remains deprived. Calling the operation successful learning describes one real result; it does not describe restoration of the other. The resulting practical account must retain both consequences.
3. **Appropriate repair answers that historically specific relation.** It changes the affected continuity left burdened by the activity. Erasing the record, obtaining more useful data, or changing the sign alone does not. This explains why the proposed favorable direction follows the affected condition rather than an analyst's preference for positive links.
4. **Absence of an accompanying own loss is not a discharge.** It may explain why the learned disposition changes. Unlike an actual return, valid authorization, a corrected attribution or an adequate remedy, it does not alter the fact that this activity left that being carrying this burden. It therefore supplies no answer of those kinds to the imposed loss.
5. **The continuing deprivation favors an appropriate, permissible repair by the responsible activity.** That favor concerns release from the imposed burden. It need not await affection, reciprocal gain or the affected being's ability to participate in deliberation. Competing claims can constrain what response is permissible or best without converting a changed association into fulfillment.

The last step is the substantive ethical judgment being defended. It is not obtained by replacing the word “consequence” with “reason.” Steps 1–4 identify its ground and explain why the actual negative replacement supplies no defeater of that ground. They do not turn causal attribution into moral responsibility or establish entitlement from a balance. The argument treats appropriate repair as a way for an activity to answer what it has actually imposed, rather than treating its own acquired preference as the source of the claim's validity.

This supports a constitutive interpretation: **repair-bearing significance is a relation of the formation, its retained adverse consequence and a continuation that can appropriately address it**. “Actor,” “patient” and “remedy” differentiate positions within that relationship. They need not be independent substances connected afterward by an external evaluator. But constitutive placement is not yet a proof of the full normative interpretation. A rival denying answerability despite the specified deprivation and attribution must contest that interpretation, not merely point to the learner's indifference.

This argument reconstructs the [earlier conditional repair application](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md). That application already defended repair, showed a formed disposition mediating it, and explicitly denied that a later repair obligation justifies deliberately causing a loss as a learning opportunity. Those are inherited results. The present addition is to locate **the acquisition practice itself** as the concrete object of a criterion-revision test, developed below. The same operation can improve a capacity, worsen another's condition and thereby change what a continuation has to address. Neither initial nor eventual motivation defines the whole relation.

## 4. Revision can change the activity that produces the evidence

The proposed correction is not “keep every sign positive.” In the continued history, the relevant question is also whether another round of recipient-paid teaching is warranted. If the operations impose unjustified deprivation, a permissible way of stopping or altering them can answer an impending burden even before a later service occurs. Preventing additional imposition and repairing an existing loss are distinct responses to different parts of the same history.

This is a practical change of boundary: the recipient's conditions constrain what the organized activity may treat as an available means of its own learning. Its existing ability to extract a signal does not settle whether continuing that extraction is appropriate. In an application with a responsible operator, the change could occur through the operator's reorganization of the practice. Attributing it to the learner itself would require evidence of that capacity and its use. The current witness realizes neither criterion revision nor an ethical decision to stop teaching.

A later ten-unit service cannot by itself justify the preceding acquisition. It might constitute adequate repair under the declared interpretation; it might also be unwanted, excessive or insufficient for other consequences. Valid advance arrangements could justify a learning cost, but their content and authority would have to be established. This is why the unit totals cannot settle whether the whole undertaking was beneficial or permissible.

The positive standard is consequence-specific and defeasible: an available continuation has favor insofar as it appropriately answers a real imposed burden. It does not rank all futures by one sum, make organizational growth good by definition, or privilege more interventions. Fulfillment can remove a particular claim; refusal can rule out a proposed means; ending a harmful activity can be the appropriate revision. Silent or non-agent patients remain within the intended scope where actual adversity and the relevant relation can be warranted; the loss meter is not evidence that this model realizes such a patient.

### A discriminating test at an actual decision point

Use the reached state after `aligned` in the entry with `continuation=true`, before the fifth `.ownPay`. It has two positive links, `n=4` and recipient 36. The existing word next begins another paid pair; its later recipient debit and replacement produce the problematic continuation. This identifies where an explanation of practice revision would need to act. It does not claim that the current program considers whether the next teaching operation is justified.

Hold the inherited acquired command, signs and own-loss data fixed while investigating three distinct relations:

| Question at that point | Evidence that could properly change its answer | What a successful explanation must distinguish |
|---|---|---|
| What past loss remains to be repaired? | A verified return or compensation addressing that specific loss; correction of mistaken attribution. | Discharging the past claim from obtaining permission for another charge. |
| May a further recipient-paid teaching operation occur? | A valid advance arrangement or withdrawal, plus evidence of the operation's prospective burden and alternatives. | Authorization and adequate justification of this operation from the fact that an old loss remains unpaid. |
| Why revise the criterion? | Acquired evidence connecting the operation, its affected continuity and the relevant outstanding or prospective burden. | Learning that relation from changing the sign because the own outcome happened to change. |

These are proposed identification conditions for a connected explanation, not three additional fields already realized by the witness. The evidence must have an actual provenance and be available to whatever process is claimed to revise the practice. The analyst's knowledge of the scheduled fifth outcome is not automatically the learner's forecast. Four past pairs alone do not prove what the next pair will cost after returns.

A successful test would show the continuing activity distinguishing repaired from outstanding loss and justified from unjustified further teaching, then using that distinction in a feasible choice for independently defended reasons. Where the next operation is shown unjustified, another positive link or an intended later remedy must not count as its permission. Where teaching is validly arranged, a bare debit must not be misclassified as an unauthorized deprivation. The latter does not require choosing to teach: a policy that always stops might remain permissible, but identical abstention across these cases would not by itself identify understanding of their difference.

This gives a sharper acceptance target than more recipient influence. A system can retain the right command, accurate loss bits and a helpful terminal disposition while failing this practice-level test. Conversely, an appropriate correction could reduce new training and its future service capacity. The evidence for ethical improvement would concern the warranted change in the actual imposition, not a higher link count, a larger target set or a larger final transfer. No existing formal or empirical result is asserted to pass this test.

## 5. What could defeat or narrow the account?

The [counterreview](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves the strongest realized rival: accurate encounter and material revision can strengthen exclusion under an unchanged own-loss criterion. That defeats automatic ethical improvement through openness or plasticity. It does not defeat the repair ground by showing a mechanism failing to respond to it.

A further, conceptual exploiter could understand the burden and its responsibility yet use that understanding only to protect its own position. Such a case would defeat an inference from understanding to appropriate conduct. If the rival instead claims there is no repair favor, it must explain why the imposed deprivation lacks the alleged significance. We cannot refute that stronger claim by redescribing the exploiter as insufficiently nondual or defining proper understanding as agreement with our conclusion.

The proposal has determinate vulnerabilities:

- If the stock change is not an actual relevant deprivation, the ethical interpretation of this trace fails while its causal history remains intact.
- If valid authorization, compensation or attribution changes the claimed relation, this particular repair ground must be revised. A genuinely authorized cost is not the same relation with an arbitrary ethical label switched off.
- If apparent repair transfers the burden, defeats a valid refusal or damages the affected continuity, the proposed response is misidentified or impermissible. Favor toward a different response does not vindicate this one.
- If a case preserves the independently specified deprivation, attribution and possible appropriate response but a defensible relation excludes any repair favor, the constitutive account needs narrowing. Exact desert and justified allocation remain serious comparators; they cannot be dismissed because the loss occurs within shared activity.
- If the account treats every imposed cost as decisive, or treats compensating later as sufficient permission to impose now, it overreaches. Pro tanto favor, duty and coercive authority remain distinct.

## 6. Contribution and remaining explanation

The actual advance is a focused account of **generated answerability** with a concrete test at the reached pre-teaching state. It turns an inherited repair/restraint distinction into a discriminating target for revision of the acquisition practice: outstanding restitution, prospective permission and the evidence used to revise the criterion must remain distinguishable. This goes beyond checking a terminal helping weight. It does not rediscover the prior warning that successful acquisition and later repair cannot certify a whole history ethically.

No foundational normative premise has thereby been eliminated. The account positively defends answerability to attributable deprivation; its general warrant and the realization of actual adversity remain explanatory work. It does not derive them from a hidden welfare reward, a new Lean axiom or the absence of a formal countermodel. The program's missing criterion revision is a representation/realization limit; a successful normative challenge to answerability would be a different, substantive limit.

The [universal minimum](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) also includes responders who did not cause the burden. This history gives a reason-bearing relationship through participation; it cannot reach the independent responder merely by widening the word “participation.” The next extension must investigate what present encounters and possible responses constitute beyond incurred responsibility, while retaining real differentiated consequences and the possibility of legitimate exclusion. That keeps the stronger objective intact without making another controller its substitute.
