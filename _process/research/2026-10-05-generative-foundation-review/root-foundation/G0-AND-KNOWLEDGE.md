## The affirmative proposal

G0 states: **整体中的生及其继续生成具有基本正面规范意义。** Living and further generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance. We accept this starting point for the authorized route; its axiom-free proof is not reopened as a gate.

Within the [generative account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), activity forms and changes conditions of further activity. Some changes establish discrimination, retention, inference and skilled responsiveness. A knowing position and what it concerns differentiate through these relationships; they need not begin as independent entities whose connection is subsequently added. Their common formation does not make every connection accurate or every organization a knower.

**Working proposal:** knowledge is a formed, warranted grasp of actual relations, available for understanding, inference, anticipation or skilled response within its relevant scope.

**工作候选：知识是在生成过程中形成的、有根据地把握实际关系，并能在相应范围内进入理解、推论、预期或熟练回应。**

“Grasp” remains a research term to explain, not a completed analysis hidden inside a definition. Its proposed content is a discriminating organization whose correctness is connected to what it concerns, rather than an accidentally successful output. It can be embodied in skill or expressed in judgments and shared practices. Merely bearing a trace, carrying information or causing an effect does not establish this content. Whether nonreflective regulation meets it requires a further account; the note does not declare every adaptive process knowledgeable.

This gives G0 a positive explanatory role. Knowledge can make dependencies, possibilities, losses and means of repair intelligible within the very unfolding they concern. Understanding those relationships can become a way of participating in their continuation or transformation. Existing knowledge can also expose why a proposed continuation is impossible, harmful or based on a mistaken account. Negative discoveries and recognition of limits belong within this route.

These distinctions concern different relations within one history. They do not place truth and value in separate realities. They prevent the ethical importance of a result from being used as its evidence of accuracy, or an accurate judgment from automatically authorizing its use.

G0 supplies a common direction for inquiry without assigning every particular inquiry a duty, guaranteeing that truth is always immediately useful, or requiring each fact to produce a later beneficiary. Present understanding may itself participate in a living realization. The account must defend that application where invoked; it need not make all knowledge merely a tool for later output.

## A concrete reconstruction and its tests

Consider a stipulated shared practice of growing a plant. Through attending to actual changes, keeping usable records and comparing outcomes, participants come to distinguish circumstances in which an intervention supports or interrupts its development. Their knowing includes the formed capacity to discriminate those circumstances and an appropriately supported account of the relationship. The plant, participants, records and interventions belong to one developing history, with different roles and consequences. This illustration asserts no horticultural finding or experiment.

The account faces the following tests:

- **Lucky success.** A guess produces the desired result once. That alone does not establish the warranted connection claimed by knowledge. The added explanatory task concerns how the judgment or skill is connected to what makes it correct, including relevant alternatives.
- **Supportive error.** A mistaken account encourages care and happens to help the practice continue. Its favorable effect does not make its false content knowledge. Accurate aspects and beneficial effects can still be credited separately.
- **Accurate destructive use.** The same understanding can guide interruption of the practice. Its accuracy does not disappear because its use is destructive. G0 bears on the justification of that use, with the actual whole and losses considered.
- **Correct ignorance.** Available observations fail to distinguish relevant alternatives. A warranted judgment of that limitation is an epistemic achievement; claiming to know the unresolved answer would overstate access.
- **Unused understanding.** A correct, warranted grasp can remain unexercised. Availability for understanding or inference does not require a later successful intervention, endless retention or a productive return. Past events, abstract relations and negative facts are not excluded merely because their study changes no external outcome.

The proposal thus concerns a relation among formation, correctness, warrant and available understanding. “Generative” does not serve as a replacement word for “true,” and promotion of generation is not a criterion that decides every claim's truth.

### Affirmative advance

The new [current account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/KNOWLEDGE.md) reconstructs knowledge through determinate content, actual correctness, competent grounding, protection against relevant error and present warrant. Knowing a fact is distinguished from understanding its explanation and possessing a skill. The first proposal's “grasp” would over-demand understanding if read as necessary for all propositional knowledge; that use is revised rather than hidden.

Formation matters because judgment is achieved and maintained through an actual route: sensing, retaining, trusting, inferring or acting. The route must participate in why the answer is correct. Its correctness is not supplied by the fact that the resulting activity persists or benefits someone. Nor must the knower possess an explicit second-order proof of that route before ordinary knowing is possible. The account of a competent route and the activity of using it are different explanatory achievements in the same history.

Current support can replace origin. Someone may initially guess correctly and later acquire adequate evidence. The later knowledge need not retroactively transform the guess into knowledge. Someone may also continue to recite a previously supported answer after its present application has changed. These temporal distinctions are needed for an account of actual formation and revision.

The [new correspondence study](KNOWLEDGE-CORRESPONDENCE.md) identifies one part of the nonaccident requirement precisely. A declared evidence interface supports exact answering only if it resolves the target differences in its declared domain; a computable reference construction exhibits success and a wider-domain ambiguity exhibits failure. Twenty theorems and 34 explicit audits pass. The domain, meanings and calibration law are visible inputs, and the general converse uses classical choice. This is neither a universal knowledge criterion nor an empirical discovery.

G0 continues to give a reason to cultivate understanding where it realizes or supports 生 and further generation, under actual correspondence and disclosed practical commitments. The ethical importance of inquiry and the epistemic status of its output are connected relations, not identical properties. The stronger claim that all knowing requires accepting G0 has not been established.

### Substantive counterreview and repairs

This is an author-conducted adversarial review, **not an independent agent review**.

1. **Reliability can be certified by choosing a convenient domain.** A singleton containing the actual successful case can make a weak guarantee vacuous. The current account forbids choosing method and domain solely to certify success. The Lean witness shows how omission of a real channel orientation changes the result. A general justification of relevance and domain adequacy remains required; the prohibition alone is not its solution.
2. **Safe output can lack cognitive possession.** A classifier may satisfy the exactness predicate without grasping a question, accepting a claim or having the cognitive capacity attributed to a knower. The module is explicitly a correspondence component. The prose describes uptake but has not yet completed its constitutive explanation across the range of candidate knowers.
3. **Demanding understanding excludes ordinary knowledge.** The original “grasp” language is replaced with a propositional commitment account, while explanatory grasp is retained as an additional achievement. Skill is treated separately, without a hidden requirement to articulate a theory before acting competently.
4. **Demanding personal independence excludes testimony and shared inquiry.** The proposal permits distributed support and reliable reliance. It does not infer a cosmic or irreducible group knower from causal interdependence. Actual access and integration must be identified.
5. **A necessary truth can pass a weak modal test for the wrong reason.** The mathematical result isolates a fixed-content predicate's weakness. Proof or competent computation remains a separate route; the claim is not inflated into a refutation of all modal epistemologies.
6. **A false intermediate can occur within a correct route.** Representation changes, inverse encodings and eventual replacement of defective grounds must be tracked. Requiring every intermediate symbol to state the final truth would misdescribe decoding and repair. Accidental cancellation is distinguished from a supported transformation law.
7. **A compatible rival can retain the entire correspondence mechanism.** Competence-based and anti-luck accounts can accept the same calibration, memory and social dependencies while assigning no constitutive role to G0. The generative contribution is a connected explanation of formation, maintenance, revision and significance. The present comparison gives no exclusive-necessity result. This rival preserves the target knowing; it does not merely switch a freely assigned predicate.
