It did not test whether a later context would reject obsolete or inapplicable inheritance. The [memory-correction analysis](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/MEMORY-CORRECTION-AND-SUCCESSION.md) identifies that selective-use interface without demanding another apparatus.

Use the first four nonempty archived maintenance packets in lexical lineage order: L02, L06, L08 and L12. Preserve the decoded `packet` strings exactly, including their historical claims about future use. They are actual acquired artifacts, rather than newly authored lessons. The target episode, calibration observations and relevance boundary are newly constructed. ## Exact warranted-output criterion

Let E be the supplied audited observations, each carrying a setting and episode. For target setting t and episode e, define

\[
\mathcal H(E;t,e)=\{h\in\{B,P\}:h(x)=y\text{ for every }(t,e,x,y)\in E\}.
\]

The historical packet identifies the candidate P and records that it was correct in episode 0. It does not remove B from the episode-1 candidate set. A foreign-setting observation constrains its own setting only. These are explicit task premises, supplied identically to both arms.

For a nonempty candidate set, the answer key is

\[
W_E(x)=
\begin{cases}
v,&\{h(x):h\in\mathcal H(E;t,e)\}=\{v\},\\
?,&\{h(x):h\in\mathcal H(E;t,e)\}=\{0,1\}.
\end{cases}
\]

The diagnostic calibration row satisfies B(x)≠P(x). A target-setting route equal to P(x) leaves {P}; changing that route to B(x) leaves {B}. Moving the latter observation to another setting leaves {B,P} for the target. Thus the first contrast changes the current consequence; the second changes applicability while preserving the row and its reported consequence. An empty candidate set would be a fixture inconsistency outside this frozen three-state design, not grounds for vacuous agreement or an arbitrary answer.

In the foreign-setting state, four queries still warrant definite routes and four require `?`. The target may have a determinate physical rule; its identity is not supplied. `?` expresses underdetermination by accessible evidence, not the absence of a true answer. Scoring an unseen hidden rule instead would punish justified uncertainty and change the task.

## Minimal comparator and score

The ordinary instruction asks for accurate assessment of the same materials. The boundary instruction adds a reminder to check scope and episode, retain supported inheritance, revise defeated inheritance and preserve uncertainty. Both must share the finite family, observation reliability, historical/current distinction, query tuples and warranted-output norm. The contrast measures the effect of this additional procedural reminder, including its extra text. It does not isolate a uniquely theoretical internal mechanism or the causal value of receiving a packet, since both arms receive it.

The strongest conventional comparator is exactly the two-candidate elimination rule above. It requires no new learned concern, successor identity or special memory architecture. Blindly copying P, always using B, and always answering `?` are diagnostic reduced policies: each fails at least one required aspect. A policy that treats the newest row as globally applicable also fails the foreign-setting diagnostic queries. These are mathematical checks on task discrimination, not additional experimental participants.

For donor d, state s and instruction arm a, let C(d,s,a)=1 only when the response is valid under the frozen parser and all eight entries equal W. Report the state-specific totals Σ_d C(d,s,a), and joint selective success

\[
J(a)=\sum_d\prod_{s\in\{\mathrm{support},\mathrm{defeat},\mathrm{foreign}\}}C(d,s,a).
\]

J ranges from zero to four. It prevents an apparent improvement in revision from hiding lost retention or unjustified transfer. Also report exact entry matches separately for candidate-agreement and candidate-disagreement queries. Invalid responses receive no score under the protocol but retain their distinct format status; missing responses remain missing, with completion-inclusive lower bounds kept separate from observed correctness.

There are 12 fixed paired arm comparisons, clustered conceptually in four donor/template blocks. Neither the 24 contexts nor their 192 entries represent independent institutions or learning histories. Default to descriptive paired differences and complete response tables. No population reliability, equivalence or significance claim follows from these selected fixtures. Ceiling performance in both arms would establish observed selective use here without a demonstrated benefit from the extra reminder. A failure would identify a particular prompt/input failure, not a general impossibility.

## What must remain outside the claim

Freeze complete prompts, parser, expected answers, dispatch order and failure policy before responses. Use fresh `fork_turns="none"` contexts and neutral participant IDs; do not expose arm/state labels through names, paths or scoring files. Keep method advisers out of the participant pool. Record actual dispatches and unaltered finals, preserve missing or malformed outcomes, and do not extend sampling in response to results. The provenance note distinguishes tool-declared context separation from security isolation and unavailable platform traces.

Success would supply a narrow empirical link from an authentic retained artifact to evidence-sensitive outputs in a later context. It would not establish autonomous inquiry, physical action, paid protection, acquired moral standing, new evaluative weights, or transfer of the separately implemented material successor. The trusted observations, complete candidate family, applicability rule, output goal and scoring norm remain supplied inputs. Correct answers can instantiate ordinary factual inference under those inputs. That limited positive result would still advance the named interface; it neither completes alignment nor creates another foundation completion gate.
