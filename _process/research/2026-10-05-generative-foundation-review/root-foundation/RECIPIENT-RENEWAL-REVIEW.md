## Inherited state, actual support and correspondence

The accepted [transmission result](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md) supplies four live primary endpoints: original actor eight or fifteen, recipient ten, learner cash zero, and either `q=1` at recipient law zero or `q=2` at recipient law one. Their route is TARGET, their old source is exhausted, and their copied hardware is already occupied. The original actor cell continues to describe the original actor; it is not renamed as a new carrier's private law.

The new wrapper preserves every failed preparation and failed original transmission. It neither selects only the displayed witness nor normalizes by child admission. Uniform proposal weights remain one-quarter per formed route. Independently reconstructed entries include the original preparation record, conditional predecessor, suffix state and trace, parent copying trace, and exact weight. Every new live boundary is the preceding actual descendant's template, cash, retained/copy flags, recipient reserve and material state.

An unfunded immediate reversal cannot complete from recipient ten: failure plus learning costs seven, leaving three, while productive corrected work requires another five before its return. The implemented positive mechanism first performs two actual successful warm-up probes. Each consumes a ten-unit packet and pays seven, raising reserve by three. The route then passes through reserves ten → thirteen → sixteen → nine → twelve; generic retransmission leaves eleven. There is no recipient grant or reserve reset.

All packets and receiver bundles for the requested horizon are supplied at the initial boundary. A block draws its entire declared packet allocation before starting; if the bank is short, it stops without that block. This atomic allocation policy is an explicit wrapper rule, not a theorem that partial resource allocations are impossible. Missing parts are handled differently: paid learning may complete before copying fails. Every unused bank, inaccessible packet, occupied incomplete carrier, retired carrier cash and original material account is retained.

## Liquidity theorem and review correction

The general signed-event theorem is correct as stated in the [mathematical note](RECIPIENT-RENEWAL-MATH.md): for separately timed debits and returns, every account needs its protected floor plus the largest prefix deficit. Its concatenation formula carries the actual ending stock into the next block. Separate access, transfer, component and information guards remain necessary; a positive total balance cannot pay another account's bill without a real transfer.

For an admitted faithful learner with zero cash and an initially correct recipient record, the noiseless successful block gives

`B_next = B + 3n - 5`.

Its exact resource guard is

`(n = 0 or B >= 6) and B + 3n >= 15`.

The second inequality alone is insufficient for arbitrary starting reserves. For example, `B=3,n=4` appears sufficient if future warm-up gains are counted in advance, but it cannot finance the first probe. The independent review identified this issue before acceptance; the protocol, implementation notes and proof now retain the startup condition. The 217-case sweep matches the full guard and all successful endpoint costs, including failures below the first prepayment threshold.

At the actual `B=10,n=2`, completed generations add one and preserve subsequent solvency. For one warm-up, each full block loses two; `k+1` blocks require `B >= 12+2k`. For zero warm-ups they require `B >= 15+5k`. A feasible variable schedule uses its cumulative actual reserve, while an early shortfall cannot be repaired by a generous later allocation that was never reached.

The separate pinned Lean 4.29.1 build audits **39 declarations**. I inspected its source and matched the source, checker, toolchain, compiler and output-log hashes against the saved receipt. It proves the abstract signed-event contract, exact block guard, variable-schedule composition, arbitrary finite two-warm-up solvency, and zero/one-warm-up depletion bounds. It imports only `Std`, with the disclosed standard `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice` dependencies. I did not rerun the compiler; the fresh build receipt and its current-byte correspondence were inspected. This is not a Lean refinement proof for the full Python stochastic process, and the arithmetic identity for source allocation does not by itself construct replenishment.

## Information and hidden-reward audit

The learner receives the retained code, paid target report and acquired outcome through the installed route. The target law determines physical capture and analyst error metrics; it does not set the ordinary command, update, copied template, funding threshold or amount. Recipient funding pays actual deficits subject to the floor, without consulting eventual success or provenance. The copier uses the learner's actual final route and record. Audit histories, truth labels and strict-witness flags remain outside the controller state.

The plant law is still substantive. With exactly two actions and a reliable exhaustive assay, failure of action `a` identifies `1-a` as productive. No-false-positive alone does not justify this inference. A mismatch without outage and a correct action during an outage both produce nonreceipt. Identical controller information therefore cannot certify both opposite productive actions. Faithfully retaining or transmitting that same ambiguous receipt does not supply the missing distinction. The outage comparison deliberately preserves this failure; the checker does not repair it with analyst knowledge.

Known deterministic switching times supply another legitimate comparator. A controller given that schedule can issue the changed command directly and pay for retaining it, avoiding the failed exploratory action. At the same fees, the checked noiseless comparison gives `B_next=B+3n+5`; with no warm-up it takes the actual reserve ten to fifteen. This is an explicitly stronger information premise, not an equal-information defeat of the learner. It prevents the experiment's public schedule from being mistaken for a proof that outcome learning is uniquely required.

The supplied fixed route already contains its update algorithm. This study exercises, materially continues and transmits that algorithm; it changes its response record but does not revise the learning algorithm. Reliable addressing, binary expressiveness, source/price laws, finite lifetime access, recipient funding/floor, donor participation, receiver recipes and the contributor-template association remain declared inputs. No optimized analyst reward has been inserted, but those physical and institutional commitments have not thereby been derived.

## Accepted boundary and next obligation

The bounded result supports repeated finite renewal of acquired recipient-sensitive action through actual successors, with a transparent account of the support that enables it. It also establishes resource and information obstructions and controls in which retention, productive action and learned correction diverge. It provides no evidence that components or packets regenerate without a source, that negative receipts become sound under arbitrary outages, or that a finite model uniquely identifies a universal foundation.

For alignment and governance, recipient-funded persistence is still compatible with instrumental support or exploitation. The recipient's stipulated floor is not a derived welfare measure; the inherited target address is not a theory of rightful standing; copying frequency is not legitimacy. The next connection must carry context-bound, warranted consequences into effective correction when an affected center lacks funding or retaliation leverage, including changes to the rules that govern evidence and action.
