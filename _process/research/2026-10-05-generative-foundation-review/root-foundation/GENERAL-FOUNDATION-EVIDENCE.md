## 2. Construction can earn a law's applicability

There is a concrete positive form of warrant here. Identify the arrangement, establish how it was formed and maintained, trace which operation it actually realizes, and derive the consequences of that operation while preserving the relevant conditions. The connection is established through the same organization being explained. It does not require an additional lawgiver or a coupling between independent domains called model and world.

For the controller, formation and execution history plus the retained program calculation identify a particular operation. A proved recurrence for that operation can explain the documented calculation because the correspondence has content: the variables identify the quantities actually read, retained and issued. For the microcosm, the preparations and experimental manipulations constrain which material arrangements and consequences the explanation may attribute. The evidence does not have the same exactness as software semantics, and should not be rewritten as though it does.

This is neither output agreement alone nor construction alone. A successful reproduction of an output may leave operative participation unidentified. A constructed arrangement may fail to operate, degrade or be bypassed. Actual formation, operation and consequences supply complementary grounds. Different sciences may warrant them through direct observation, provenance, interventions or independently supported constraints; experimental control of every system is not required.

The resulting conclusion can be general across an identified class of realizations. Its range follows the relations preserved across those realizations. A change in an irrelevant label need not defeat it; a broken causal route, changed law, exhausted support or unexamined joint interaction can. The ground for extension is the continuing organization and warranted constraints, not the mere absence of a counterexample in a finite sample. This is a philosophical account of explanatory warrant, not a new formally proved universal transfer theorem.

## 4. Exact statement of the remaining issue

Three conclusions should now be separated:

1. **Actual generative participation occurs.** This is supported by the independently documented relations above. It is not merely stipulated in the Boolean example.
2. **An adequate explanation must preserve the relevant content of the phenomenon it explains.** Existing preservation arguments and formal results give this obligation substantive consequences once the target and claim are fixed. A conventional or atemporal account can satisfy it.
3. **The proposed common account supplies an adequate explanation throughout every actual system in its intended scope.** The cited evidence alone does not establish this stronger completeness claim. More successful cases would provide support but would not, just by their number, constitute a deduction of it.

This remaining issue is narrower than saying that all actual applicability, all recognition or all inference remain groundless. Each particular grounds-responsive identification can be warranted without first certifying the universal reliability of every possible act of recognition. Conversely, acknowledgment of fallible but substantive warrant is not completion of the explicitly requested assumption-free universal foundation.

The strongest defensible advance is to **close the undifferentiated physical-applicability objection**: actual applicability has already been earned where the operative and evidential relationships are established; each proposed extension must identify what it adds. What is still distinct is a genuinely general argument for the root's explanatory coverage. No evidence in this assessment establishes an unrestricted metaphysical necessity, unique vocabulary or universal moral consequence. Nor does it prove such a foundation impossible.
