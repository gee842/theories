## 2. Checked general connection

`Within P realized s n bundle` means that one trace from `s`, of length at most `n`, reaches an endpoint realizing every claim in the bundle. It keeps one common endpoint; separate paths for different claims do not certify joint realization. `Eventually` means that such a finite bound exists, without fixing its size.

For an actual prefix `Trace P s p t`, the checked declarations establish:

- `within_after_prefix`: a bundle reachable within `n` steps from `t` is reachable within `length p + n` steps from `s`.
- `eventual_after_prefix`: a bundle eventually reachable from `t` was eventually reachable from `s` through that prefix.
- `no_new_eventual_bundle`: therefore there is no represented bundle that is eventually reachable after this prefix but was not eventually reachable before it, under the same law and meanings.

The last result is backward inclusion of existential endpoint possibilities along an admitted history. It does **not** say nothing forms, that the future has already occurred, that the process is deterministic, or that our current knowledge already describes it.
