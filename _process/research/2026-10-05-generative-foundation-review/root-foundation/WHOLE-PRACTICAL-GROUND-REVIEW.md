## 1. Reconstruct the proposal on its own terms

The deciding activity, available responses, consequences, experienced changes, formed relationships and subsequent revisions occur within one generative unfolding. A response is not outside the realization it helps constitute. Its circumstances include its formation, limits and effects. A whole appraisal can therefore include the ethical quality of deliberation and response itself; it need not appraise an endpoint left over after deleting action.

The proposed pullback is a representation: describe a feasible response by the whole continuation it would realize, then use the whole comparison on those continuations to articulate the comparison at this response position. A map between descriptions does not posit two ontological realms. Nor must a represented position begin as an independent owner: its access, control and responsibilities can be actual relationships formed within the shared history.

The strongest affirmative interpretation is **co-grounding**: a concrete relationship partly constitutes both the whole’s quality and a fitting practical articulation. That proposal deserves assessment directly. Demanding an unexplained second normative bridge after establishing their constitutive identity would repeat the same question. But stating the proposed identity does not establish it, and whole membership alone does not specify its content.

## 2. Three achievements must retain different scopes

| Achievement | What establishes it | What it does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Faithful re-expression | Preserve the actual response, its history, alternatives, grounds and practical meaning under a changed description. | Why the original ethical appraisal is warranted. |
| A common representation | Show the proposed local comparisons can be expressed by restrictions of one whole relation under exact compatibility conditions. | That the represented comparisons are true or that their common relation explains them. |
| Universal normative grounding | Defend why independently described relationships have the alleged ethical quality and practical force throughout the target domain. | Actual recognition, motivation or inevitable compliance. |

These are questions about the same reality. Their distinction is methodological, not a split between factual and ethical substances. A common explanatory ground could answer more than one question together. Its content still needs to be defended.

Two equivocations must be excluded. If whole quality already means the correctness of every situated practical verdict, pulling it back expresses those verdicts but does not newly explain their correctness. If quality instead identifies realization independently of a practical verdict, its significance for this type of response remains part of the candidate’s substantive explanation. Neither case proves that a separate ethical axiom is unavoidable; neither supplies the stronger no-assumptions result.

## 3. Representation rival: one unfolding, differentiated fitting responses

The rival grants that appreciative activity, protection, self-care, enabling cooperation and legitimate completion can be good within the whole. It also grants that acting and evaluating are among the unfolding’s relations. It disputes the claim that their ethical forms are adequately captured by a prior unqualified ordering of whole histories whose restrictions automatically determine all practical favor.

Its positive explanation is relational: what a response realizes depends on how it participates. Listening can receive an expression, joining can constitute a collaboration, refraining can respect a refusal, and intervention can prevent a serious imposed burden. These response forms are not interchangeable just because they concern the same worthwhile activity. Their distinction is formed through actual communication, dependence, permission and action. No independently complete subject or private value is introduced.

Consider an actual collaborative performance formed through invitations and mutual musical uptake. The performing activity’s quality can give grounds for attentive listening, for a participant’s contribution, and for protection against interruption. That does not yet identify one reason to prolong it, recruit more participants, reproduce the sound mechanically or displace another activity for it. The same candidate whole account may explain why these differ. The point is to require that explanation, not to stipulate the candidate must favor every expansion. This is a constructed philosophical test, not an empirical result.

This representation rival can grant the positive-response conjecture of some moral favor while disputing its reduction to one promotion ranking. In that case it is **not** a counterexample to universal moral favor; it challenges only the attempted representation and explanatory identity. The first-favor rival is specified separately in §5.

The rival does not erase whole ethical quality or simply toggle a moral predicate. It proposes a different account of how the same differentiated relationships ground different responses. It can accept some practical force everywhere an appropriate relationship is present while rejecting one uniform promotion rule. It can also defend a restriction on a particular intervention without denying all favorable significance. That distinction is essential because the universal target is not limited to obligation, and refuting an overstrong duty does not refute baseline practical favor.

**Affirmative reply worth keeping:** the whole relation can retain the type of response, actual participation and authority instead of maximizing a scalar. If it does so with independent ethical justification, the rival may become another articulation of the same account. Compatibility is a possible success. It must not be announced solely because both accounts can be encoded in one large data type.

## 4. New discriminating test: common notation versus common ethical content

The current proposal should be tested through a three-part comparison.

**A. Same practical relationship, changed analytical cut.** Describe an actual selecting activity once as a whole organization and once as a differentiated planner/executor arrangement, preserving the same controls, information, formed responsibility, response and affected continuation. The addressed practical claim must retain its exact ground and meaning. Renaming the chart cannot justify losing it. This applies the established nondual boundary discipline; it is not new ethical warrant.

**B. Changed practical relationship, shared whole setting.** Genuinely delegate discretion, invite reliance, withdraw permission or change who can intervene. Preserve the other relevant relationships explicitly. Practical scope may now change because the unfolding changed, even if a selected output or aggregate quality measure did not. A model that treats this as a mere coordinate change omits constitutive content. A model that retains it has a better representation, but must still defend the normative role of that content.

**C. Same content claimed across positions, genuine overlap.** Require a proposed universal relation to identify actual comparisons that its different articulations share. When a representation retains the entire deciding token in each history, local domains may become so separate that agreement on overlaps tests little. A proof of common representability would then constrain notation more than substantive cross-position warrant. Ask exactly which continuation pairs and complete grounds are shared, why they refer to the same claim, and which previously supplied local judgment can now be derived rather than merely copied.

Part C is the new diagnostic. It is not a proof that rich histories must have disjoint domains, and it does not authorize deleting actual roles to manufacture overlap. If there are no shared comparisons, the correct next obligation is to explain a warranted relation between different situations. Merely joining them as nodes is insufficient. If common comparisons exist, incompatibility matters only when practical meaning, context and grounds are the same. Distinct legitimate reasons may conflict without a mistaken representation; forcing them into a transitive total ranking would need an additional defense.

## 5. First warrant and universal coverage remain substantive

The principal first-favor rival is the [established-practical-integration account](UNIVERSAL-WARRANT-SUFFICIENCY.md#4-a-substantive-immanent-rival), alongside the [affirmative note’s assistance-specific rival](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-GROUND-ARGUMENT.md#5-the-substantive-rival-survives-within-the-whole). It can grant actual adverse and favorable quality, reasons against imposed injury and unchosen special responsibilities. For positive assistance, it nevertheless treats a historically maintained organization of consequence-sensitive uptake, response dispositions and coordination as constitutive of moral address. Bare effective access is insufficient on its account. This condition is described independently of the desired reason predicate, and its claimed necessity is substantively contested. The affirmative explanation treats it principally as recognition, implementation or an additional ground, while locating first favor in the response–realization relation itself. Neither calling it constituted nor calling it merely enabling settles that dispute.

This is a rival to first moral favor, not merely to overriding rational authority. It is not vindicated by being articulable or formally consistent. Conversely, a new common representation does not answer it when it copies its supplied local comparisons along with the affirmative account’s. The compared interpretations need their own ethical defense.

For the performance test, the positive ground could be the actual responsive realization and its place in the continuation. It need not be assembled from independently valued atomic preferences, and its value need not return to an initiating self. The account still owes a defense of why this quality is favorable rather than merely effective, faithfully understood or durably endorsed.

If the theory jointly explains its quality and practical articulation, no mandatory sequence from an isolated first value to a later reason is required. But a substantive account has to identify what is jointly explained. Calling the history completely ethical does not supply that explanation. Conversely, a rival cannot treat formed responsibility as whatever practical favor happens to exist and then explain absent favor by absent responsibility.

Universal quantification over an arbitrary formal position type also cannot identify its instances with every relevant actual intelligence. A successful account must specify how the relevant practical relationships arise, which transformations preserve them, and which genuine changes alter their force. Recognition, assent and installed preference cannot be used as the definition of qualification if the intended claim is independent of them. Nonintelligent affected living remains within the whole; no reflective-agency threshold of significance follows.

## 6. What would change the adjudication

An affirmative advance would identify a concrete relation whose ethical quality and fitting practical articulation are jointly defended, then establish its scope under actual changes of access, control and responsibility. At least one formerly supplied local practical judgment should follow from that ground through a checked inference with faithful correspondence. The rival must either preserve that relation and explain a different response form, show a genuine misdescription, or accept the articulation. This would be more than restating a clean uncommitted-helper case.

The candidate needs revision if a shared whole-quality representation erases why reception, protection, enactment or refusal have different practical meanings; if overlap exists only after deleting relevant history; or if its whole ranking is simply reconstructed from verdicts whose warrant it was meant to establish. Discovering that multiple defended response forms fit the same account would support a relational universal ground without requiring a unique scalar objective or total ordering.

A full completion claim is not justified by the present proposal alone. The defensible outcome is a sharper constitutive candidate and a nonvacuity test for its proposed universality. This is a limit of the argument presently supplied, not a theorem that universal normative grounding is impossible.

### Executed counterreview of the comparison of how dependence forms

The affirmative note’s [executed §7](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-GROUND-ARGUMENT.md#7-executed-comparison-authorship-withdrawal-and-new-support) has now been read in full. Its introduction, reliance-based withdrawal, new-support and time-limited-support histories are not falsely matched as identical wholes. The fourth history is useful: previous benefit alone does not supply the second history’s invited reliance or displacement of an alternative. Equally, describing withdrawal as inactivity does not erase those relations where they actually occurred. The announcement of an endpoint is explicitly not assumed to authorize it.

The strongest counteraccount explains restrictions on imposed adversity through the security of unfolding lives and directed continuation responsibilities through actual dependence and reliance. This is a genuine relational explanation of differentiated address. It can survive the move to one whole and can survive recognition that every alternative changes the future. The account still needs a warrant for its claim that absence of these special grounds excludes **every** first moral consideration for new support. Explaining additional responsibility does not by itself explain that exclusion. The affirmative account still needs a warrant for its broader sufficiency claim. This review accepts neither side by default.

The self-modification continuation makes a bounded change to the rival. If a historical responsibility was independently warranted and the relevant dependence remains, disabling present uptake does not by itself fulfill it, correct the original appraisal or establish release. A theory requiring only current operative integration must account for that retained relationship. A history-sensitive version can do so and still deny new-support favor. The result tests an explicit restriction; it does not establish an unlimited duty, universal blame for memory loss or a requirement to preserve every incumbent judgment. This is an application of the inherited warrant argument to the current-integration proposal, not a new universal normative invariant.

**Adjudication of the addition:** the four-history comparison supports a positive account of additional directed responsibility within the whole and preserves a stronger surviving rival. It supplies no contradiction in that rival’s assistance exclusion and no proof of the broader first-favor claim. This is substantive refinement beyond a representation audit, with its philosophical and supplied-premise scope intact.
