# Coverage review: from actual living realization to necessary renewal

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored independent conceptual and coverage review for Ren's redirected living-dependencies goal. The immediate task is biological and explanatory correspondence, not a new attempt at first ethical warrant. This note reads the [previous living-grounding study](LIVING-GENERATIVITY-GROUNDING.md), [ContinuationRenewal](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/ContinuationRenewal.lean), [SituatedRenewal](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/SituatedRenewal.lean) and the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). It adds one bounded primary reading. No new mathematical proposition is claimed as proved and no compiler was rerun.

**Recommendation:** develop a common account of **inherited functional realization, its actual loss or insufficiency, and effective formation or recruitment into the same continuing history**. Derive the shortage from independently identified biological relations. Do not take renewal-or-descent, universal turnover of all constituents, or positive cost per clock tick as the biological premise. The useful next advance is a correspondence that explains why retained organization fails to meet a continuing functional requirement, not another theorem assuming that it fails.

## 1. What the existing proofs already do

`ContinuationRenewal` proves recurrence on an infinite history when nonrenewing activity descends in a supplied well-founded relation. Its natural-rank specialization provides a bound under stronger hypotheses. The theorem does not establish the biological relation, positive demand, infinite existence or applicability to dormancy. The fixed-form witness also preserves the distinction between renewal and increasing novelty.

`SituatedRenewal` starts closer to a measurable relation: when a provision route stays unchanged, final support plus actual demand is bounded by initial support plus addition. Actual demand beyond inherited support therefore forces addition or route change within the selected actual window. Recurrence needs the further premise that such demand arises again after every starting point. Zero-demand gaps are allowed; route change does not certify adequate replacement.

The unresolved biological work is not the deduction from these premises. It is identifying an actual dependency and deriving the relevant insufficiency from its mechanism, measurement and continuing use. A molecular degradation rate, a decreasing isotope fraction and a support account are not interchangeable descriptions of that mechanism.

## 2. Distinguish the mechanisms before claiming necessity

| Candidate mechanism | What would support a renewal inference | What must not be substituted for it |
|---|---|---|
| Loss of functioning material or organization | An actual loss trajectory or justified loss law, together with a continuing functional requirement and insufficient retained alternatives. | A mean lifetime presented as a deterministic expiration date for every constituent. |
| Dilution during growth or division | Increasing required occupancy, concentration or distributed provision that the retained cohort cannot adequately supply. | A falling fraction of old label treated as direct evidence that its molecules degraded. |
| Consumption and restoration of a functional state | Independently established use and restoration of an operative condition, with actual supply and transformations included. | The claim that every catalytic or structural component must be consumed on each use. |
| Stochastic damage or failure | An identified stochastic model, relevant interventions or observations, and a conclusion at the model's actual probabilistic scope. | Positive risk, expected loss or an empirical decay curve promoted into descent on every admissible history. |
| Retained dormant capacity | Formation before the inactive interval, persistence of its operative relations, and actual reactivation or use when it occurs. | Counting retention as newly occurring reconstruction, or assuming the inactive bearer continuously produces its support. |
| Support supplied through other activity | An actual supported transfer, repair or functional substitution, with its sources and timing retained. | Calling the recipient self-renewing or moving the explanatory boundary until an unexamined supply disappears. |

These are alternatives and combinations within one process. They are not an exhaustive taxonomy of life. Their common subject is the adequate realization of a continuing activity; their clocks, carriers and causal obstructions can differ. A persistent scaffold and exchanging subcomponents can jointly realize one function. Reactivating a molecule changes its functional state without requiring a new molecule, and an actual replacement must preserve the relevant function rather than merely the name of its coordinate.

Stochastic models need a separate audit of their quantifiers. Finite expected duration, an almost-sure eventual event and a deterministic finite deadline are different proposed conclusions. None should be silently renamed a well-founded relation. This note makes no new probability theorem; any extension making such a mathematical claim requires its own checked proof and correspondence assumptions.

No supplementary methods, raw data, complete-paper reading or replication. PMC returned a browser check; retrieving later PDF text timed out. Cited studies were not independently read.

**Bounded source result:** rat pulse-chase measurements distinguish neuronal from glial-enriched nuclei. Histone H3.1 remains strongly labeled; pore subcomplexes show different exchange kinetics. **Our inference:** label loss needs causal interpretation. An enduring organization can contain inherited and newly incorporated contributions. Neither constituent exchange nor detectable synthesis alone establishes whole-structure replacement or adequate maintenance.

## 4. Exact coverage obligation

The target should be identified before asking whether it renews: a particular observed living activity, viable retained capacity, tissue function, lineage continuation or ecological relation. These are not interchangeable targets. Identification can use converging biological evidence; defining life as what necessarily reconstructs would settle the desired conclusion by classification. A sterile individual, dormant bearer or finite achievement must not be excluded simply because it does not meet a reproduction-based shorthand.

The coverage claim needed for general recurrence is stronger than observing that many parts renew and weaker than requiring every part to renew. After each reached stage of a claimed continuing history, identify a dependency still required by that continuation and establish why its inherited realization will not suffice without further effective formation. Its alternatives and sources must remain included. The witnessing dependency can change with development, succession or a changed activity. A retired role need not keep consuming support, but retiring its label cannot conceal a function that the specified continuation still requires.

Six correspondence obligations make that claim substantive:

1. **Independent target:** state what continues, how it is recognized, and whether the claim concerns actual operation, retained viability, growth or lineage succession.
2. **Functional dependence:** establish which relation is indispensable at the relevant stage, including adequate substitutes. Removal, rescue and interruption evidence can discriminate dependence from correlation when their other effects are controlled.
3. **Inherited realization:** identify what survives from the starting state and how it remains effective. Material identity, functional status and organization identity need not coincide.
4. **Insufficiency mechanism:** distinguish demonstrated consumption, loss, dilution or changed requirements. State the temporal and deterministic or probabilistic scope actually warranted.
5. **Effective response:** identify formation, repair, activation or recruitment that actually supplies the function. A changed label, mere synthesis or imported token without the needed integration does not establish adequate restoration.
6. **One supported history:** keep the provision, sources, losses, transitions and successor functioning in the same history. Do not reset its support when moving between individuals, generations or analytical boundaries.

No uniform global clock or common scalar across biological scales is needed. Conversely, naming a whole or enlarging an index set does not establish these six relations for it. The present sources can test particular mappings; they cannot exhaust all actual or possible living configurations.

## 5. A candidate common invariant across scales

The useful invariant is an **account of realized function through actual provenance and transformation**: at a reached stage, the effective contribution needed for the target must be explained by retained operative organization or by formation, restoration or recruitment that actually occurred in its history. Changes of scale must preserve those same contributions, sources and failures rather than create a fresh supply by redescription. This is a proposed correspondence discipline, not a new proved formal invariant.

For a protein complex, the account can distinguish stable organization from exchanged subunits. For a cell population, it must distinguish per-cell support from the provision sustaining surviving cells and new cells. For a tissue or ecosystem application, an incoming contribution must remain connected to its actual production, persistence or release elsewhere. These are proposed application requirements, not evidence that the same countable stock or turnover law already fits all scales.

The strongest next derivation would start with an independently supported functional-capacity relation and a provenance account, then show why surviving inherited capacity becomes insufficient under the actual continuation. It could obtain that obstruction from measured loss, increased functional demand, or their combination. Only then should the established general necessity theorem be applied. Newly supplied capacity must be distinguished from the actual material and work required to make it effective.

A cohort-based formalization may help if it does this work. It would be progress if it derives the required response from actual carrier survival, maintained functional adequacy and supported recruitment, and demonstrates a faithful map to the biological evidence. It would not be progress merely to rename `remaining`, `demand` and `added` as molecules while assuming the same unexamined exhaustion premise. Material cohorts alone may be inadequate for reversible functional activation; that case needs its state transformation represented explicitly.

## 6. Real limits and the recommended next test

The inherited [dormant-spore reading](LIVING-GENERATIVITY-GROUNDING.md#4-a-primary-test-operative-inheritance-during-dormancy) supports operative earlier formation without contemporaneous ATP-based reconstruction in the studied response. The new primary comparison supports long retention alongside heterogeneous replacement in active cells. These constrain claims of uniform, rapid or continuously local renewal. Neither is an observed indefinitely living process with no generative support whatsoever. No such empirical counterexample is identified here.

Likewise, a persistent local component is not a counterexample to a wider necessary dependency, and a source elsewhere is not an explanation until its actual contribution is traced. Conversely, demonstrating some turnover elsewhere does not show that it is necessary for the selected target. The issue is the causal connection, not whether renewal can be found somewhere in a sufficiently large description.

**Recommended common test:** use one independently established support or repair mechanism and compare inherited retention, functional loss and effective replacement through the same account. Preserve at least one slow or retained component and one nonuniform demand interval. Require the representation to distinguish loss from dilution and actual restoration from mere synthesis or a renamed route. Compare its predictions with the primary interventions and abundance/function measurements. This tests a reusable correspondence rather than adding another favorable cycle.

The concurrent evidence and formal investigators are pursuing nongrowing survival and the retained-realization interface. Their results should be integrated at their own reading and checking scopes; this note does not anticipate a successful universal proof. A result would change the account if a purported essential relation were bypassed, inherited organization sufficed over the claimed obstruction horizon, measured replacement failed to restore the function, or a probabilistic model failed to justify the deterministic necessity claimed for it. Those are substantive tests of the common derivation.

**Assessment:** the promising advance is a derived biological insufficiency relation with an explicit correspondence to functional continuation. The general implication from that relation is already available. Universal living coverage remains a research obligation, but neither every-part turnover nor a uniform maintenance clock is required to pursue it.

## 7. Independent review of the selected transport realization

The concurrent investigation selected a concrete transport route rather than the cohort proposal above. This reviewer read the complete initial [LivingRealization module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/LivingRealization.lean) and the [mechanism assessment](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-MECHANISMS.md). This is a meaningful alternative way to discharge part of the correspondence obligation: derive the support account from an actual transport balance, an operative range and a work-coupling law. It need not instantiate every mechanism in §§2–5 to be useful.

The module's `TransportLaw` provides inward and outward quantities, an internal amount, available work, supplied work support, a fixed ceiling and an export cost. Conservation of the represented amount and the coupling between active export and work are premises. The final `DependencyLaw` account is **not** a field assumed in that structure: `ion_account` and `support_account` derive it by combining those relations on the restricted maintained process. This genuinely connects the earlier general theorem to a more mechanistic law. The physical laws, conversion units and biological identification remain supplied, so the result is neither an assumption-free derivation nor a formal refinement of the source's stochastic concentration model.

The maintained restriction requires the internal amount to remain under its ceiling at the represented states. That is an operative condition, not a definition of life or irreversible death. Histories with a temporary excursion and later recovery can fall outside this restriction while preserving some actual living continuation. The source assessment reports recovery before lysis after nutrient readdition. It also reports that the tested Nfo/ExoA deletions impair timing and fidelity without abolishing spore outgrowth. These findings are inherited here from the investigator's stated primary readings; this reviewer did not independently reread those publications. They prevent a local maintenance target or particular repair pathway from being presented as all viability.

The derived quantity `supportedHeadroom` combines tolerance for additional internal amount with available work. It is an accounting potential, not a claim that unused tolerance is stored biochemical energy. Initial tolerance can accommodate inward transfer without immediate export, and retained work can permit export without new input. The explicit witnesses preserve these distinctions and include an inactive step followed by compensation through the actual reached state.

Natural-number amounts, a fixed ceiling and a fixed cost per index are scoped abstractions. Concentration, changing volume, multiple interacting ions and continuous-time excursions are not represented merely by using those names. Positive cumulative load remains an additional hypothesis for recurring input; neither positive load on every step nor replacement of every constituent is required. Zero cost is admitted by the type, so a claimed work-input necessity must actually satisfy the weighted excess-load premise rather than rely on a hidden universal positive cost.

`included_store_account` and `included_store_bounds_load` correctly retain depletion when a finite provisioning store is included in the same account. Transferring stored support to the selected component does not create additional whole support. A positive local input can be inherited supply or released material from another part; it is not automatically newly formed organization. The proof does not establish an inexhaustible source or an actually infinite maintained history.

A correspondence issue identified during review is now resolved by an explicit scope restriction. `inward` means **net unpowered inward transport over a represented transition**, restricted to nonnegative net values. Unpowered outward relief is included in that net; it is not omitted or truncated. `exported` denotes active, work-coupled export. Net-outward transitions fall outside this representation. The final source and [proof note](LIVING-REALIZATION-PROOF.md) expressly reject a general refinement of noisy paths or automatic reorganization of their intervals. This accepts a narrower physical regime rather than extending the gross-inward conclusion across unrepresented relief.

This reviewer reread the final scope comments and complete proof note.
