Selves, purposes and particular beneficiaries develop within the unfolding; their real differences remain consequential. This neither places value outside generation nor identifies one enlarged observer with the good of the whole.

## Distinguish transmission from return

A completed act can change another life and leave knowledge, capacities or arrangements available to subsequent activity. After an adequate handoff, the original individual may cease participating while those conditions remain usable. Later good is then realized in the lives that actually undergo it. It is not a benefit owned by the originator that must somehow return or survive as their possession.

This gives a specific comparison. Keep the originator absent in both continuations. In one, usable knowledge, support and warranted means of correction enable later participants to meet a changed need. In the other, an institution or formula persists but repeats a routine that no longer meets that need. Assess the actual effects on those participants and anyone else affected. Successful transmission concerns effective conditions for further good, not preservation of a name, program or lineage as such.

Absence of a return path and failure of a transmission path are different changes. Removing a reward to the originator need not remove a later benefit. Removing the only usable instruction, necessary support or means of correction can remove it. These are proposed causal comparisons, not evidence that every historical source can be removed without consequence. The initiating activity's continuing effects may remain indispensable even after its initiator no longer acts.

The test requires an actual handoff, an identified later need, effective access to retained conditions, a defensible account of the benefit, and the burdens and alternatives relevant to its assessment. It does not require every beneficiary to become a future producer. The inherited account continues to recognize a final relief, rest or enjoyment without demanding another downstream product.

## Test the whole affected continuation

The independent review grants the proposed propagation: care enables one participant to care for another, and both benefits really occur. Yet the arrangement may consume a shared capacity needed to relieve a third person's severe burden. The dispute concerns that complete configuration. Lack of selfish incentive is not the objection, and the benefits already realized do not disappear.

If a feasible, warranted alternative preserves those goods while avoiding the displaced burden, the organization should be assessed against it. If no such alternative exists, there is a substantive conflict to adjudicate. A successful beneficial path alone does not settle the quality of every branch or the justification of the whole arrangement. This applies the existing [joint-feasibility account](../../2026-10-05-generative-health/ANSWER.md) and [ethical derivation's displaced-burden discipline](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md); it is not a new premise-free necessity theorem.

Here an independently identified good means an actual relief, worthwhile capacity or relation established beyond simply labeling the activity generative. It does not mean an ontologically independent value attached by a primitive self. Likewise, a beneficial later result cannot automatically vindicate the means that preceded it. These qualifications test the proposed generative account from within the affected history.
