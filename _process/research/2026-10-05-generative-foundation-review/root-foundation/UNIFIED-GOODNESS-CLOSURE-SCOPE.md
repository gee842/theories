## 1. The strongest connected explanation

The common starting point is consequential activity: what occurs changes conditions of further occurrence. Organization, acting, undergoing, retaining, understanding and evaluating differentiate within this unfolding. An organization depends on some continuations rather than others; its activity can alter those dependencies, form responses, retain consequences and revise how later consequences matter. Distinct affected centers remain real. Common generation neither merges their experiences nor makes every continuation beneficial.

The following map states what the accepted work contributes. “Conditional” identifies the explanatory contract; it does not place supplied laws outside generative reality.

Specific functioning organizations and their laws require identification. A general presentation is not a proof that its particular chemistry governs every system. |

## 2. What completion now means

The [five original criteria](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#completion-of-the-unified-foundation) require explicit premises, connected derivations, compatible composition, constructive and destructive regimes, and discriminating support. The completion assessment accepts all five at the declared conditional scope. Its decisive addition is the connection between actual evaluative formation and independently warranted repair, rather than another ungrounded helpful score.

This acceptance includes concern and wisdom. It explicitly identifies no additional mandatory foundational construction at that scope. It also preserves mislearning: changing an acquired disposition can suppress a response whose independently justified ground remains. A successful learner need not become ethically better.

One later closure matters for current reporting. Under the original six-encounter law, the four-encounter state cannot reach service without further teaching. [INQUIRY-REVISION](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/INQUIRY-REVISION.md) now adds supported contact, reading and a genuine six-to-four requirement rewrite, followed by the original service. The old impossibility remains true of the old law. “This history has no operative protocol revision” is consequently obsolete as a description of the extended implementation. The origin and warranted coverage of its installed rewrite criterion remain open at a stronger scope.

## 3. How goodness belongs in the account

The positive ethical candidate identifies appropriate transformations of differentiated lives as constituting practical significance: relief, worthwhile agency, understanding, relationships, rest and legitimate completion. Concern recognizes and participates in these goods; it need not first manufacture their warrant by privately adopting them.

The [relational-goodness argument](RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md) adds temporal depth. A response can realize good now and change conditions through which later good becomes possible. It may restore voice, enable correction or transform participants' purposes. Ren's continuing-consequence analogy and its AI-drafted elaboration are documented in the [source genealogy](RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-SOURCES.md); no general religious doctrine is inferred.

This is a constructive synthesis, not a circular definition of good as whatever produces more good. Final relief needs no subsequent yield. Exploitation can reproduce; care can produce dependence; warranted refusal can interrupt activity. The prospective extension is conditional on a worthwhile possibility already supplying this responder a reason, together with credible permissible contribution to its conditions. Interconnection extends practical reach without proving the initial universal warrant or inevitable favorable propagation.

## 4. The stronger claims still requiring defense

The [ethical account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md) proposes that actual severe, still-unwanted experiential burden and an eligible response relation can jointly ground moral favor before an antecedent privileged relationship. This has a defended positive comparison with empty intervention and explains correction toward the patient. It is not yet a necessary conclusion of generativity. A serious rival can retain actual patient benefit while disputing this actor's baseline favor; legitimate jurisdiction can also affect a specific choice's justification.

The unresolved claims are therefore precise: experiential realization and the relevant constitutive identification; universal practical scope beyond independently warranted role-bound applications; stronger rational authority; and a uniquely necessary, assumption-free root. Broad empirical identification, autonomous selection of ethical standards and complete governance remain separate further achievements. Their absence is not evidence that ethical authority must ultimately be irreducible, nor that the accepted concern mechanisms are missing.

The defensible closure is a connected conditional foundation plus a coherent, substantive ethical candidate. Completing its exposition is possible now. Declaring universal necessity would require the further argument, not a renamed definition or another favorable model.
