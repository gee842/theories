## Formal contract and correspondence

`StepFrame P join` asserts primitive compatibility with all admitted left contexts. Together with a left unit, this gives:

- `Replacement P join x y ↔ Reach P x y`, for every endpoint goal in every declared context.
- `Contraction P join a ↔ Reach P a (join a a)`.
- An object resource calculus with identity, primitive steps, cut and frame constructors, **without a contraction constructor**. `Derives ↔ Reach` under the explicit frame hypothesis.
- `AdmissibleContraction ↔ Reach a (join a a)`, now for the actual calculus.
- Binary duplication suffices for every finite positive multiplicity; no exponential promotion, coherence of copying maps, infinite execution or full classical logic is inferred.

The first equivalence is elementary: choose the empty context and target itself for necessity, then use framing and path composition for sufficiency. It is a representation/admissibility result, not an independent explanation of why a physical process permits duplication. No novelty is claimed for this proof pattern.

In the stock instance, states count `(ready, provision)`, combination adds coordinates, and two declared events convert provision or spend readiness. `step_frame` is proved from the operational law. `budget_exact b n` quantifies over all natural numbers: a stock `(1,b)` reaches at least `n+1` ready supports iff `n ≤ b`. Leftover provision is retained in the witness. Conservation in this account is a consequence of the declared steps, not a universal law supplied by the phrase "generativity".

`paid_contraction` is deliberately typed as `Replacement (1,1) ((1,0) ⊗ (1,0))`. It does not have type `Contraction (1,1)`. `no_budget_contraction` verifies that every finite complete funded package fails duplication of itself. This prevents a resource hidden in a modality label from silently funding unbounded contraction.

`not_ready_before`, `ready_after` and the explicit four-state path establish formation of immediate spending capacity and later exhaustion of paid replacement. They do not establish newly generated unrestricted reachability. The general `replacement_backwards` theorem preserves this distinction for fixed target and fixed law.

The serial model is a separate declared single-port process, not presented as the same physical realization as the stock model. It has all finite `take; release` repetitions but forbids `take; take`. The interference control supplies a five-state process and a context operation with a left unit: bare duplication holds, framing and contextual contraction fail. It tests precisely removal of the frame premise. Its lack of physical detail is an omitted relationship in this small representation, not a refutation of the fuller nondual theory.

## Author counterreview and resulting corrections

1. **Is contraction merely encoded as a definition?** The operational law is separately stated; the object calculus does not include contraction. The equivalence establishes admissibility. Nevertheless, the semantic necessity argument is elementary and its generic version explicitly assumes framing. This is no proof that all actual processes supply it.
2. **Does restoration or renewal justify copying?** The checked serial counterexample says no for simultaneous availability. The earlier broad suggestion that repeatability might directly yield contraction is narrowed to a precise resource interpretation.
3. **Is provision silently duplicated?** The paid rule retains its different source package. Duplicating the whole funded package is formally excluded. Costs cannot disappear into the name of a resource or modality.
4. **Does formation create new unrestricted possibilities?** The first code commentary called the later replacement capability formed. That overstatement was corrected before delivery: preparation was already in reachability. Immediate readiness is formed; arbitrary finite prepared reachability propagates backwards. Both distinctions now have checked statements.
5. **Does the countermodel refute the whole generative account?** No. The interference control intentionally violates one hypothesis. It explains why omitted contextual relations matter; no free toggling of a predicate is presented as a substantive universal counterexample.
6. **Does Lean already use contraction?** Yes, Lean is the metalanguage. A proof about an explicitly defined object calculus does not derive Lean's own inference rules or foundation from zero assumptions. The object calculus is limited to resource expressions and endpoint goals.
7. **Does a path guarantee execution or preserve every effect?** No. Reachability is existential and permits a preparation prefix. Extra time, trace effects and adversarial interference need a richer contract. The current account keeps their omission explicit.
8. **Is there a frontier result?** Not established. The source comparison contains strong precedents even for dynamic sharing. Neither the number of Lean declarations nor integration with our philosophical terminology measures novelty.

This is author counterreview, not an independent-agent acceptance. No subagent was requested or used.
