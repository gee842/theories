The [frame analysis §10](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#10-imaginative-perspective-taking-reversible-frames-and-new-warrant) gives an elementary exact translation result and a counterexample to overreading it. Reversibility alone is weaker: exchanging two patient names and exchanging them back preserves the endpoint while permitting an incorrectly addressed intermediate action. The actual action boundary therefore needs a meaning-preservation condition.

This is compatible with learning. Evidence and reflection can newly warrant the applicability of an independently justified practical premise. The positive return condition concerns the **same actual-actor reason**. Fading attention or arousal alone does not refute an already warranted claim when its premises, applicability and supporting warrant remain, without an independent defeater. The person may nevertheless forget it or fail to act. Returning to an ordinary viewpoint need not restore an earlier erroneous judgment.
