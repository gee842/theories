## 4. Why this inquiry addresses the challenged application

The calibration continuation leaves eight response-table pairs after acquiring
one full outcome. Both possible weak tests split those candidates equally.
Counting eliminated candidates consequently does not justify preferring one
test over the other. The current implementation supplies priority for the
previously used setting.

The [relevance theorem](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/remaining-dependency/formal/InquiryRelevance.lean)
now gives that priority a precise conditional justification. Let `r` be the
setting of the actual prior operation and `f` its acquired full outcome.
Let `W` be a possible weak-response table. The question is whether

\[
W(r)=f.
\]

All four weak tables remain possible: the old reports are among the relations
being investigated, rather than independently certified truths. A single
faithful weak test at setting `q` determines this answer exactly when `q=r`.
At any other setting, two possible tables agree on the observed response while
disagreeing about this local application. The explicit proof uses a table
whose two entries are `f` and a second table changing only entry `r` to its
opposite.

For the actual failed on-setting operation (`r=1`, `f=0`):

| Possible weak table | Test setting 0 reports | Test setting 1 reports | Does the tested application's weak response agree with its full effect? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `00` | 0 | 0 | Yes |
| `01` | 0 | 1 | No |
| `10` | 1 | 0 | Yes |
| `11` | 1 | 1 | No |

Both tests halve the four weak possibilities, and each halves the eight full
candidate pairs. Only setting 1 answers this question. Here `q` is the physical
test setting, **not** the contact-route argument: the earlier contact-zero
correction also tests physical setting 1. A faithful route is still required.

This is ordinary target-relative identification applied to the actual
relationship challenged by the prior operation. It is not a new general
information algorithm or a proof that the physical policy learned its own
origin. It explains why a supplied priority is appropriate to this question
without introducing a scalar reward. Additional reliable weak facts,
correlations among the candidate tables, other query types or a different
question can change the uniqueness result. The actual consumer and its
formation still need their own realization.

The inquiry's subject matter is connected to an actual activity: a particular
relation was used, its effect was acquired, and the relationship between that
effect and the earlier indication is challenged. Which observation resolves
