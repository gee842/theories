## 1. What can actually be discharged

A dependency is locally discharged when an earlier result supplies the required input with its original meaning, actual history and material conditions. The antecedent does not disappear merely because it is removed from one theorem's argument list. Its provenance must be followed into the earlier result. This distinguishes genuine composition from hiding the same premise farther upstream.

Three inherited examples establish what successful local discharge looks like:

| Interface | Existing positive result | What remains upstream |
|---|---|---|
| Primitive event relation → finite sequential reachability | [GeneratedPaths](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/GeneratedPaths.lean) gives the least reflexive-transitive extension of the supplied events. | The event relation and its physical interpretation. This is a closure derivation, not a derivation of which events occur. |
| Proposed record → exact finite-continuation interpretation | [GeneratedSelf](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GeneratedSelf.lean), `exact_summary_exists_iff`, constructs an interpreter precisely when equal records preserve every relevant residual profile. | The record, actual internal installation and causal use. The constructed interpreter can be computationally unavailable. |
| Absent typed parts → formed predictive register → acquired correction → action | [ConstructedPrediction](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/construction/prediction/ConstructedPrediction.lean), `full_formation_prediction_corrected_action`, preserves actual earlier formation, paid retrofit, acquired memory and a later action in one staged trace. | Functional recipes, the five-state score, noiseless binary response law, component types and initial endowment. |

The third result is the strongest constructive starting point for this round. It already contains physical formation, retention, correction and use. What was missing was an exact account of **what the acquired memory represents about the organization that carries and uses it**. Proving that account avoids constructing another disconnected toy learner.

The concurrent [AcquiredPredictionRecord](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/construction/prediction/AcquiredPredictionRecord.lean) branch also already develops native record replay and next-outcome interpretation. Those results are credited to that branch. The present addition connects the existing material-formation history to both-action response-function exactness, loss of exactness after a changed dependency, actual correction and paid support renewal. Replaying or interpreting an acquired record is not claimed as a wholly new idea or result of this round.

## 2. Why the inquiry closure is not the missing foundation

[GenerativeClosure](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/GenerativeClosure.lean) distinguishes basis formulas, local assumptions and finite derivations. Its central equivalence is exact:

\[
\text{every closed descendant is necessary}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every basis formula is necessary}.
\]

The forward direction uses the basis constructor; the reverse direction is derivation soundness. This is useful because it prevents productive closure from laundering an unsupported starting point. It supplies no missing proof of basis necessity. `local_justification_discharges_context` likewise takes the necessary local justifications as arguments; it does not discover them.

[InquiryFamilies](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/InquiryFamilies.lean) proves substantive, genuinely distinct families of conditional constraints. Under its clipped-observation definition, threshold query `k` is answerable exactly when `k < capacity`. Under its spending relation, a finite action word is feasible exactly when its length does not exceed fuel. Both implications are proved from the displayed definitions. The underlying clipping and spending laws are declared local inputs, as the source explicitly states.

Consequently neither infinitely many descendants nor an empty context means infinitely many unexplained inputs have been eliminated. These files establish sound propagation and particular derivable contracts. They do not connect their capacity/fuel scenarios to the material learner, derive a self, interpret adversity or justify an actor-addressed reason. The needed next step is to supply one actual interface, not to add another theorem about closure size.

There is also no claim here that this proof calculus is complete for all semantic consequences or all mathematics. Its displayed inference rules and quantified domain determine its exact scope. No further expressivity counterexample is needed to decide the present route: the existing equivalence already identifies the unresolved obligation.

## 4. The strongest positive route in the unchanged learner

The [predictive law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/formal/PredictionAction.lean) connects the relevant quantities causally:

1. An action obtains a receipt exactly when its Boolean setting matches the environmental regime, under the supplied available/noiseless law.
2. The receipt changes the score through the installed updater. The updater uses the issued action and received result; the command does not read the hidden regime directly.
3. The same score determines the next command.
4. A successful receipt replenishes the same organization's fuel and, after the required paid write and finish, renews its selector and updater leases.

For score `q`, the action-conditioned response forecast is `q` for action `true` and `4 − q` for action `false`. Exactness at a corrected extreme score is not imposed as a success criterion: it can be proved against the unchanged `receiptOf` law and discharged in the actual reached corrective state. The existing command already consumes that score; the new analytical readout does not install another controller.

This is a partial, operational representation of action relative to the organization's support. It is stronger than calling an arbitrary retained bit a self-model, and more constructive than an impossibility claim based on an idle system. It remains weaker than a generated body boundary, phenomenal perspective, narrative identity or general self-knowledge. The internal score does not explicitly contain the whole mathematical function used by the analyst.

“Actual” in these trace statements means that successive states and enabling guards belong to one witnessed model history. It does not assert that this history occurred empirically, is selected autonomously among all admitted histories, or is inevitable. The formation, replacement and trial schedule remains the displayed finite witness. The generic write lemma characterizes an update's content; its enabled physical execution is supplied by the separate reached-state trace.

The actual event set also matters: `.work` executes the current command. It is not a command accepting an arbitrary hypothetical candidate action. Exactness for both candidates concerns the underlying response function. The positive control result is the reached corrective write, changed next command and enabled later action; it is not a proof that both alternatives are executable from the same unmodified state.

The acceptance test for the new [AcquiredPerspective](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/AcquiredPerspective.lean) bridge is therefore concrete:

- Preserve the inherited laws and display the actual formation/acquisition trace.
- Prove the acquired readout's exact support-effect meaning at a reached state, with the fast updater and correct record established by that history.
- Prove that a paid write changes the retained score and subsequent command while preserving the ongoing material carrier and already executed action.
- Carry that command through enabled paid action, writing and completion to actual support renewal; algebraic evaluation of a disabled event is insufficient.
- Keep noiseless availability, supplied coding and recipe boundaries explicit; retain outage ambiguity and the distinction between an informative forecast and an affordable act.

Source review caught a concrete intermediate failure: an early draft evaluated `.finish` directly from the observed post-work state, while the inherited guard requires the written phase. The corrected source supplies an actual paid `.write, .finish` continuation and proves its endpoint is the original `ConstructedPrediction.final`. It also supplies the staged formation-to-record trace. This is a composition correction, not a new physical premise. Final review and compilation status are recorded below.

## 5. Ethical and mathematical comparisons do not supply the missing ground

[RootGrounding](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/noninstrumental-concern/RootGrounding.lean) already proves

\[
\text{universal eligible-actor reason}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{event-anchor coverage}\ \land\ \text{eligible-operator locality}.
\]

It also connects that decomposition directly to the existing C2 contract. This is the exact dependency result needed here: deriving locality does not create the positive anchor, and assuming both conjuncts merely restates the disputed content. `candidate_satisfies_c2` in [GroundingContract](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/noninstrumental-concern/GroundingContract.lean) is a consistency witness for one definition of merit, not ethical grounding.

The parallel [constitutive ethical review](ETHICAL-CONSTITUTION-CLOSURE.md) reports no new C1 or C2 discharge. Accurate acquaintance with adversity can contribute to identifying the condition. Actual opportunity and permission must still be identified at the response interface. This observation must not be used to dismiss a possible direct ground of badness: C1 need not encode every actor opportunity. The unresolved inference remains whether the accurately identified badness, together with a genuinely eligible response, constitutes the claimed favor. Another decomposition of those inputs is not an answer. Independent review of its Sections 3, 6 and 7 accepts these distinctions and its explicit non-completion result; no new scientific validation is claimed.

The [mathematical reconstruction study](MATHEMATICAL-ROOT-FRONTIER-MATH.md) offers a useful model for a *successful scoped* reconstruction: natural operations across the entire specified category of deterministic action systems are recovered as unique finite action words. That removes an independent choice of operation once the full structural role is fixed. It still quantifies over a supplied structural family, maps and semantic background. The [grounding resolution](MATHEMATICS-GROUNDING-RESOLUTION.md) expressly retains the additional philosophical identity/priority commitments. This route does not currently derive actual dynamical laws or normative relevance.

The positive lesson common to both comparisons is precise: fix an independently warranted explanatory role, and show that its allegedly independent extra content is determined by the already established structure. The failure to avoid is fixing that role by including the conclusion one was asked to derive.

## 7. Decision

**Accept the acquired action/support representation and its composed paid continuation at the reviewed local scope.** It is the strongest presently identified positive discharge using the unchanged formal laws. Preserve the distinct ethical and root-law obligations; do not replace them with the existence of this learner.

The current outcome does not justify “complete foundation”, “assumption-free model”, “generated selfhood” or “derived universal ethical reason”. A genuine derivation of one of the remaining recipes or a justified response-directed ethical ground would change the audit's assessment. A larger theorem count, a renamed predicate or another general counterexample would not.
