# Systems, boundaries and constitutive possibilities

8 October 2026. Integrated response to [Ren's selected systems question](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-systems-foundation-question.md). The [current account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION.md) explains systems as differentiated relational organizations within an unfolding, with formation, persistence, interaction, transformation and dissolution all in scope. The [affirmative argument](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-ARGUMENT.md), [formal accounting](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-FORMAL.md) and [independent review](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-REVIEW.md) supply its complementary assessments.

**New result:** boundaries of an adequate description cannot generally be inferred by retaining only the coordinates that individually make a difference. Nor need all adequate descriptions have one least sufficient region. The actual domain of jointly admitted states matters. This advances warranted system description while exposing why predictive sufficiency alone does not establish actual constitution.

## 1. The explanatory advance and earlier completed work

The systems question makes the target more precise: identify an organization and the relations through which it has the possibilities being explained. A selected collection, an effective description and an integrated organization can all be called systems, but they support different claims. No single demand for autonomy, mutual maintenance, feedback or persistence is imposed on all of them.

Actual boundaries articulate relationships; they need not form one exclusive partition. The account must retain relevant crossing dependencies and distinguish organizational continuity from resemblance or mere replacement. The explanatory sequence follows actual formation into operation and further transformation; its strength comes from supported participation, not a definition stipulating generativity.

Prior exact-reduction and autonomous-law results in [systems complexity](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/SYSTEMS-COMPLEXITY-CHAOS.md), [formed overlapping ranges](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions), [effective finite-history correspondence](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-INTEGRATION.md), and [developmental reconstruction](EVOLUTIONARY-ORGANIZATION-INTEGRATION.md) are reused at their established scopes. The present result is not another proof of autonomous quotient existence or the first formation mechanism.

Let `Supports D read response B` mean that agreement on readings in region `B`, for states in admitted domain `D`, implies the same selected response. The domain, readings and response are explicit parameters. They represent analytical distinctions within one state and do not assert independent physical components. This definition expresses a question about sufficiency; no system's sufficiency is asserted merely by declaring it.

| Result | What is established | Exact limit |
|---|---|---|
| Sufficient regions can be combined by intersection under recombination | Construct an admitted intermediate state agreeing with the first state on one region and the second on the other; sufficiency then carries the answer through it | Recombination is a sufficient condition, not a necessary condition for every particular response |
| Full products admit that recombination | The splice is explicitly constructed when the first region has decidable membership | Full independent recombinability is proved for this mathematical domain, not assumed of physical reality |
| Correlated domain lacks a least sufficient region | On the two states `(false,false)` and `(true,true)`, either coordinate determines the first coordinate; the empty region does not | This concerns the declared question and coordinate representation, not a theorem that no physical boundary exists |
| Two distinct minimal sufficient regions | Each single coordinate is minimal among decidable candidate subregions | The separate no-least-support result quantifies over all regions, without this decidability restriction |
| Isolated-coordinate tests can miss joint contrast | No two admitted states differ in only one coordinate, while the two admitted states have different responses | No inference to absence of causal influence is licensed by that failed test |
| Formation earns a restricted-domain description | A `correlate` event establishes equality of coordinates, preserved by every later event and finite trace | The defined event law is supplied; this is not empirical system identification |
| Either coordinate preserves all finite possibilities on that domain | Equal selected readings identify the full admitted state, hence all finite event-word feasibility | Right-coordinate sufficiency fails in the unrestricted domain; adequacy depends on which states are genuinely admitted |

The concrete single-law trace is

`(false,true) →correlate (false,false) →flip (true,true) →pulse (true,true)`.

The first event establishes the correlation making either coordinate sufficient. **It does not itself enable the pulse:** the first coordinate remains false. The later flip changes pulse availability. Correlation is not necessary for pulse availability in the unrestricted process. Keeping these effects distinct avoids manufacturing a causal capability claim from a prediction theorem.

The result supplies a real correction to boundary analysis: independently varying every reading can introduce states unavailable in the organization under study; forbidding all such variation can instead conceal causal alternatives. Which domain is warranted is a substantive question. The formal result makes that obligation explicit rather than answering it through a type declaration.

Koza and colleagues' bacterial measurements and interventions support activity forming oxygen conditions that affect later growth and competition. Their related assays do not become one fully tracked history. The reviewer reread the preserved primary passages and retained inhibition and missing-raw-data limits. The support theorem therefore assists explanation without replacing mechanism identification. A redundant coordinate can reconstruct a response without being the operative source. Warranted constitution requires evidence about actual formation, continuity, uptake, interruption or substitution, including where intervention is unavailable or introduces additional changes.

## 4. Completion assessment

The systems question has a developed affirmative answer, a checked new consequence, actual evidence at identified scopes, and a substantive independent assessment. The account preserves common-drive alternatives, independent aggregates, static and temporary cases, externally maintained organizations, nested boundaries and eligible global descriptions. It supplies no universal sharp threshold separating every possible system from every collection.

**The stronger assumption-free foundation remains unfinished.** The systems formulation does not establish the general warrant for the actual relationships, admissible variations, recognition and inference throughout the proposed scope. Calling those ingredients “a system” would relocate the starting commitments rather than justify them. This is the unresolved passage identified by the earlier [direct warrant assessment](CONSTITUTIVE-WARRANT-ARGUMENT.md), now made more discriminating through the difference between predictive support and actual constitution.

No new foundational axiom is adopted, and no empty axiom audit is presented as a premise-free empirical or philosophical proof. No impossibility result about the stronger target follows. A general argument can in principle provide scope warrant without inspecting every case; unique boundaries, unique vocabulary, one architecture everywhere and endless recurrence are not extra completion gates.

**Next foundational test:** establish how evidence of actual formation and uptake warrants the admitted *joint variation* used in a constitutive claim, especially when correlated substitutes predict the same record. Existing mechanism identification gets credit; a new exact predictor alone would not answer this question. The theory changes if a supported alternative explains the target while correcting the alleged participation or the proposed range of variation.
