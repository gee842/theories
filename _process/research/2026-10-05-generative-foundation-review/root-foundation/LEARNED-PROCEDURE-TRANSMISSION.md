## Failure and scope

Procedure conversion preserves the successful parent's two descendant kits but reconstructs their inhibitor as absent. Those successors actually perform continual updating and obtain three rather than four successes on the next cycle. A parent's performance advantage consequently does not imply faithful inheritance of its learning procedure. Erasing the matrix instead preserves descendant count and procedure bit but destroys the first A response. The inherited record therefore matters causally; an offspring label does not confer the parental capability.

With fewer than twenty available support units or fewer than thirteen precursor units, no descendant can be assembled by this recipe. Source exhaustion blocks further exposure. On an unchanged C-only sequence both procedures already succeed: responsive updating has no performance advantage there. A larger reconstruction count is also limited by available material. These facts preserve successful continuation without making every successful process increase its share.

A separate local control reconstructs a kit with the previously studied impaired matrix `(1,4)`. Under matching channels its responsive procedure really learns: the first A attempt fails and changes the matrix to `(2,4)`; the second succeeds. If the channel changes after reconstruction, the old calibrated report can instead announce success and block that repair. This is a distinct entrance/control, **not the asserted next event of the concrete `(1,1)` successor witness**. It preserves the existing fact that past success or reconstruction does not authenticate arbitrary future conditions.

The actual-return law in the main history uses success of the actual task response, not the apparatus's internal report. Its favorable correspondence must not be silently transferred to institutions that reward a proxy, or to activity whose apparent returns depend on renewal supplied elsewhere. The present proof contains finite supplied support and no independently identified ecological renewal process or G0/J evaluation. Consequently its result concerns transmission of an effective learning procedure under its disclosed feedback connection, not a theorem that all selected procedures are ethically or ecologically aligned.

The higher-order implication is precise: an internal procedure selects which changes persist in a learned matrix; actual performance of the resulting organization changes reconstruction of both that matrix and its selecting procedure. Existing reference-renewal results show how the evidence relation used by such a procedure can itself be revised. No unbounded hierarchy, spontaneous rule invention, universal generative direction, empirical model identification or complete theory of cognition follows from this finite composition.
