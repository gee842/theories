## 2. Two primary readings

**Ostojić, Shaw, Cheke and Clayton (2013), “Evidence suggesting that desire-state attribution may govern food sharing in Eurasian jays,” PNAS 110(10), 4123–4128.** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3593841/), DOI [10.1073/pnas.1209926110](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1209926110).

Across seven males, sharing shifted after observing their partners eat one food to specific satiety; male prefeeding was held constant. The seen/unseen comparison and a separate observational-satiety test addressed immediate recipient cues and changes in the male's own appetite. The authors nevertheless leave the mechanism of information uptake unresolved, including cues during prefeeding. The evidence supports recipient-sensitive sharing in this task; it does not settle mental-state attribution.

**Ostojić et al. (2016), “Experimenter expectancy bias does not explain Eurasian jays' (Garrulus glandarius) performance in a desire-state attribution task.”** DOI [10.1037/com0000043](https://doi.org/10.1037/com0000043); [Cambridge repository record](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/handle/1810/291360), [accepted manuscript](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstreams/3d94f859-7b3d-4843-b6ba-cece8ee0a23b/download).

The test experimenter was blind to female prefeeding. Eight pairs entered; six contributed analyzed sharing data after exclusions. Sharing varied in the predicted direction, using a planned one-tailed test, p=.031. This same-program repetition addresses experimenter expectancy. It is not an independent replication. Its nonsignificant comparison with earlier results does not establish equivalence. The analysis also changed from proportions to food-count differences and reanalyzed earlier data.

**Inferential limit, our assessment.** Small samples and the targeted controls warrant a bounded behavioral comparator. No general mind-reading capacity, universal concern, stranger-directed reason, or reliable stopping rule follows. Specific satiety is food-specific; it is not fulfillment of every need. Nor does declining a particular response imply that its recipient has ceased to matter.

## 3. Identification contract: our reconstruction

The useful unit is the temporally extended relation: prior activity changes a recipient; access to that history can change subsequent participation. Describing only an actor's unchanged general disposition or an observer's final score loses the distinction the comparison investigates. Conversely, a response label alone cannot establish that the affected condition guided anything.

For the present research, three questions must remain distinguishable:

1. **What changed in the affected continuity?** Track its actual condition rather than treating a completed instruction or a larger counter as sufficient evidence of benefit.
2. **What consequence became available to the responding process?** Preserve its source, timing and possible ambiguity. Analyst knowledge is not automatically an acquired distinction.
3. **Which participation can change?** Distinguish changing the kind or amount of a response, declining further acquisition, and preserving access to an outstanding remedy. Evidence for one is not evidence for all three.

These are investigative differentiations within the unfolding relation, not separately complete ontological components awaiting an external value assignment. Their connection must still be explained. The comparison would lose its identifying force if an unchanged actor state or experimenter cue fully explained the response, or if the measured recipient manipulation failed to change the relevant condition. Neither primary work derives the formation or general reliability of the entire relation.

The empirical comparison does not show that jays revise such a protocol. It instead sharpens what a future identification must preserve: a changed affected condition may call for reorganizing participation rather than merely retaining a generally favorable response. Whether further inquiry is justified, which remedy remains appropriate, and who can revise access require their own warranted assessment and realized control.
