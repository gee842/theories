## 2. What a mathematical identity preserves

For a fixed deterministic process, write \(\beta_s(w)\) for the designated observation after continuation \(w\). Identifying \(s,t\) when \(\beta_s(w)=\beta_t(w)\) for every permitted finite continuation gives the already proved coarsest output-preserving transition congruence. The resulting state identity preserves recursive action and the selected responses. It does not identify every physical, historical or consequential difference between its representatives.

There are two different expansions to track. **Additional evidence within the same scope** can reveal that a learner previously merged states that the intended equivalence already distinguished. **A larger action/observation scope** can refine the intended equivalence itself. The latter need not make the older quotient erroneous for its original question. If every old test is recoverable from the enlarged interface, the richer equivalence is contained in the old one, yielding a projection from new identities to old identities. Without that inclusion or interpretation map, two descriptions need not be ordered refinements at all.

The preservation condition must match the claimed composition. Equality of all future responses from a fixed initial state gives a right congruence on histories: appending the same action preserves identity. It need not permit substitution under arbitrary preceding histories. For the language \(\{ab\}\), histories \(b\) and \(aa\) both have no accepting continuation; nevertheless prefixing \(a\) produces \(ab\) and \(aaa\), which differ. Thus the state quotient is valid while treating it as a two-sided quotient of word multiplication is invalid.

If the target is identity of reusable operations, require the stronger context family appropriate to that claim: for example, equality under all prefix-and-suffix contexts, or equality of induced transformations on every admitted state. This is a concrete expansion of scope. It neither dissolves composition nor proves that every mathematical identity is exhausted by this particular observable interface.

## 4. Every finite scope can be realizable while its limit adds ideal profiles

Here is a self-contained counterexample to an overstrong reconstruction inference.

Let \(S\) be the binary sequences with finite support, with coordinatewise exclusive-or \(\oplus\). For \(n\geq0\), let

\[
q_n:S\longrightarrow Q_n=\{0,1\}^n
\]

retain the first \(n\) coordinates. Each \(q_n\) is a surjective homomorphism. Its equality kernel is an actual congruence, not merely an approximation that fails under composition. Dropping the last coordinate gives \(p_n:Q_{n+1}\to Q_n\), with \(p_nq_{n+1}=q_n\). The kernels refine and their intersection is equality on \(S\).

The inverse limit consists of coherent tuples \((u_n)\) satisfying \(p_n(u_{n+1})=u_n\). Such a tuple determines one arbitrary infinite binary sequence, and every infinite binary sequence determines such a tuple. Thus

\[
\varprojlim Q_n\cong\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}.
\]

The natural map \(S\to\varprojlim Q_n\) is injective but not surjective: the tuple of all-one prefixes is coherent, and every one of its finite levels is realized by a finite-support sequence, but no one member of \(S\) realizes all levels.

Consequently, joint separation by all the scopes establishes recoverability of an actual element from its full profile, not that every coherent profile belongs to the original domain. A completion may deliberately admit new elements; claiming that the original activity already realizes them requires an additional closure or realization argument. This is a limitation of the proposed inference, not a metaphysical claim that infinite objects cannot exist.

Using exclusive-or matters: prefix equality would not be a transition congruence under an unrestricted left-shift action. The example supplies a genuine compositional family while isolating the failure of surjectivity. It does not contradict Rutten's final-coalgebra construction; that construction specifies a semantic carrier and preservation hypotheses rather than asserting that every given subsystem already contains every possible behavior.

## 5. What this advances, and what would revise it

The positive route is precise: identify distinctions through their role in continuations, establish which compositions preserve them, and reconstruct a reusable quotient. Enlarged participation or observation can reveal a finer identity, while an explicit projection retains the old scope. This connects acquired distinctions and stable mathematical operation without making mathematical identity arbitrary or denying unobserved structure.

A relevant counterexample must preserve the declared interaction and target while defeating the asserted preservation. Useful tests include a formerly merged pair separated by a newly admitted context; a purported state quotient that fails under the composition it claims to support; a component model whose unmodeled resource difference alters an admitted result; or a coherent limit profile with no realization in the original carrier. None is established merely by attaching an unconstrained extra predicate to a weaker formalization.

For the present files, stable deterministic interfaces, generator/test laws and archive-maintenance conditions remain local inputs. That records the scope of this explanation. Their being local inputs does not locate them outside the unfolding. Conversely, calling their relationship immanent does not supply an omitted preservation proof or a history of their formation.

The accompanying mathematical note should distinguish semantic identity, finite evidence for it, an acquired representation, and the changing carrier that uses that representation. A substantive new result would show that the actual expanded interface preserves the whole relation its claim requires, or construct and retain the refined identity when it does not. The present source work supplies established tools, exact scope controls and an explicit completion counterexample; it does not announce the no-assumptions foundation or universal mathematical constitution completed.
