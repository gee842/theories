# External integration before reflective recognition

6 October 2026. AI-authored focused source audit of Ren's clarification: an actor's own future can require integration of what lies outside its boundary even without explicitly thinking beyond that boundary. This supports conceptual integration of the completed pre-import study; it adds no experiment or formal result. Primary papers were read online and are linked below; no source originals were imported.

The existing [alignment and governance assessment](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md) already distinguishes material support, informative coupling and cognitive participation, and gives the relevant indistinguishability argument. [Cybernetics](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/CYBERNETICS.md), [information and renewal](INFORMATION-AND-RENEWAL.md), and the [prior-art audit](INVARIANCE-AND-PRIOR-ART.md) already credit the closely related literature. ## Four primary comparisons

**Kolchinsky and Wolpert (2018): causal contribution to continuation.** Their framework intervenes on system–environment correlations and measures changes in a specified viability function. It distinguishes information already stored from information acquired during later interaction. The analysis fixes the system/environment decomposition and horizon; its observed-information construction uses discrete-time Markov dynamics. The chosen negative-entropy viability function is explicitly analyst supplied. The authors acknowledge that low entropy need not mean membership in a particular viable set or preservation of identity. This supplies a method for testing whether a distinction matters to continuation in a specified model. It does not prove that every external correlation is necessary, that every persisting system cognizes, or that viability measures welfare. The formalism needs no conscious awareness of the external relation. Read §§III–V. [Primary article](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.08053v3).

**Di Paolo (2005): dependence does not already constitute regulation.** The argument distinguishes self-production and robustness from adaptivity: the capacity to monitor relevant tendencies and regulate relations as viability limits approach. Materially needing nutrients does not, by itself, explain the mechanism that moves toward better conditions. Adaptivity can arise through dedicated mechanisms or an emergent organization; no explicit reflective judgment is required by its definition. The paper therefore supports the transition from external dependence to operational significance as a substantive problem. Its proposal is a conceptual account of organismic sense-making, not an empirical identification of the present mechanism or a demonstration of inclusive ethical concern. The origin of adaptivity remains to be explained rather than following automatically from continued existence. Read printed pp. 436–442, especially the definition on p. 438. [Author's primary paper](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf).

**Conant and Ashby (1970): a conditional regulator-model result.** The proof supplies regulator, reguland and outcome sets, their outcome mapping, a reguland distribution, and optimization of outcome entropy. It identifies a deterministic mapping from reguland events to regulatory events among the simplest optimal regulators. Its presentation also assumes a common optimal outcome distribution; the discussion qualifies the conclusion by the proof's conditions. This mapping sense of model does not require a consciously represented external object, a symbolic simulator or a complete map of everything outside. It should not be cited as proving that every successful action requires an explicit world model. Nor does minimizing outcome entropy determine which outcome ought to be preserved. Read §§2–5, especially the theorem and proof in §4. [Primary article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207727008920220); [VUB reading copy](https://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/books/Conant_Ashby.pdf). The latter is a retypeset copy with some damaged equation rendering; its readable statement and prose assumptions are the basis used here.

**Brooks (1991): implemented behavior without an explicit central representation.** The paper describes mobile robots whose behavior layers couple perception directly to action. Their finite-state machines still have internal states, registers, timers and communications; eliminating a central symbolic representation does not eliminate physical state or environmental dependence. These are implemented limited behaviors, not a proof that all cognition can dispense with representation. The paper reports three physical control layers, distinguishes further plans from completed work, and leaves scaling and integrated learning as questions. It is a useful concrete counterexample to requiring an explicit reflective model before environment-sensitive behavior can occur. Read §§5.1, 6 and 8. [Author's primary paper](https://people.csail.mit.edu/brooks/papers/representation.pdf).

## The necessity that follows

The following is the repository's conditional argument, not a newly attributed theorem of any one source. Fix a future requirement, admissible environments, available actions, decision time and complete accessible information, including inherited structure and memory. If two still-indistinguishable situations require disjoint sets of adequate actions, one policy cannot guarantee an adequate action in both. A stochastic policy does not remove this obstruction: it cannot assign probability one to two disjoint action sets using the same information and randomization law.

Consequently, reliable continuation across those alternatives requires some route that removes the obstruction: an action-relevant distinction must already be inherited or become available, the situation must be changed, or an action adequate across the remaining alternatives must become possible. Further computation over unchanged indistinguishable information does not manufacture the missing distinction. The relevant route might be a sensorimotor loop, a retained calibration, a bodily constraint, a trusted instrument or another participant. Whether each counts as representation or as part of cognition is a further theoretical classification; the causal requirement does not depend on that classification.

This is compatible with Ren's emphasis. The relation can do indispensable causal work before the system explicitly represents itself, an outside boundary or its future. It is stronger to identify that work and its failure conditions than to infer dependence from a system's verbal acknowledgment of interconnectedness.

It remains a conditional necessity, with straightforward countermodels:

- If the same action is adequate in every admitted external state, no distinction between those states is required for that task.
- Previously acquired information and stored resources can support a finite interval of offline reasoning or action. Dependence over a history does not require continuous new observation.
- A stipulated constant resource stream can support a process without conveying a task-relevant distinction. Material coupling and information-dependent regulation are different relations.
- Adequate information cannot overcome a missing actuator, exhausted reserve, closed opportunity or physically impossible requirement. Information is not sufficient for an attainable future.
- A controller can exploit another being using accurate information. Effective integration does not establish that the other's future receives independent evaluative weight.

These are logical boundary examples, not new empirical observations. They exclude a universal claim that every cognitive step requires fresh external data or an explicit outside model. They also preserve differentiated boundaries: causal dependence does not make every cause a constituent of a mind or make every other being replaceable by its usefulness.

## What the paid pre-import result contributes

The [completed package](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/calibration/pre-import/README.md) constructs and pays for one specific transition: independently acquired recipient information is read coherently before an externally supplied command can overwrite it. Its three inspection units and added A/H pair make preservation of that informational relation a material part of the route. The system need not articulate an account of interconnectedness to implement the comparison.

The key issue is which causal relation survives. A command import can write a candidate and a qualifying flag that agree with the command itself. Such agreement does not preserve the former independent relation to the recipient. Single-use origin and causal invalidation prevent that substitution within the supplied interface. A recent read of an old anchor still does not establish that the external law is unchanged; the stable-law premise and adverse common-fault cases remain necessary boundaries of the result.

This supplies a finite constructive instance of protecting an already acquired external relation during further cognition and action. It does not establish that this architecture is universally necessary: the equally charged conventional monitor matches it, and a direct retained-register route can avoid the import. Nor does the recipient-directed continuation derive its own beneficiary binding, objective, authority or concern. The next conceptual obligation remains how responsive structures form and retain significance under change, and how another center's future becomes more than an instrument of the actor's continuation. Actual paid new acquisition and warranted revision are the next physical interface; re-reading old state cannot supply them.
