# Retained organization and the resumption of protein synthesis

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored primary-source assessment for the current [living-dependencies inquiry](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/LIVING-DEPENDENCIES.md), following the [recovery assessment](LIVING-RECOVERY-EVIDENCE.md). This note investigates one discriminating mechanism; it neither defines all life by this mechanism nor announces a new mathematical proof.

**Advance:** material provision and retained functional organization need separate, connected representation. The selected experiments distinguish a reactivatable configuration from an impaired preparation under comparable supplied reaction conditions. They therefore constrain the proposed transition from retained organization to resumed activity more directly than restoration of a physiological amount alone.

## 1. Primary evidence: protection tested by subsequent function

H. A. Feaga and colleagues, “Ribosome Dimerization Protects the Small Subunit,” *Journal of Bacteriology* 202 (2020), e00009-20; [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7186458/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.00009-20).

In *B. subtilis*, purified stationary-phase ribosomes were supplied at the same nominal 100 nM concentration to a ribosome-free translation mixture with a reporter template. Wild-type preparations produced reporter within ten minutes. At thirty minutes, preparations from an HPF deletion and a binding-capable but dimerization-defective HPF mutant produced respectively 97% ± 2% and 91% ± 7% less protein. Proteomic and structural analyses identified depletion of ribosome-associated S2/S3; whole-cell S2 abundance was similar between wild type and the deletion strain, and tested rRNA appeared intact. Free cytoplasmic S2 was an inference, not direct localization. Removing the inhibitory factor RsfS did not rescue the defect. The read account contains no direct S2/S3 re-addition rescue. Residual translation remained; these preparations were not demonstrated to contain zero competent complexes. Equal nominal loading does not mean equal intact functional loading. The result supports a role for assembly and protection, without isolating one structural change as the complete explanation. The prepared mixture supplies other reaction components; it is not autonomous cellular recovery.

## 2. Primary evidence: release of retained organization

H. A. Feaga and J. Dworkin, “Transcription regulates ribosome hibernation,” *Molecular Microbiology* 116 (2021), 663–673; [primary author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8628635/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/mmi.14762).

Purified stationary-phase dimers supported reporter production in a prepared ribosome-free PURE reaction; a no-ribosome reaction was the negative control. In cells transferred to fresh medium, dimers resolved within five minutes. Rifampicin blocked resolution; a resistant *rpoB* strain retained resolution during treatment, supporting an effect through transcription inhibition. Fusidic acid did not prevent resolution, separating this initial transition from a requirement for new protein synthesis. Competition between newly synthesized mRNA and HPF is the authors' mechanistic proposal, rather than a directly observed displacement trajectory. Their in-vivo assays also found no significant translation attenuation from HPF deletion or ectopic expression at the tested stages, despite altered dimerization. Thus dimer abundance alone is an inadequate inactivity classifier. These experiments do not establish long-term proliferation after complete loss of translation machinery, or a universally necessary release route. The cell-free functional assay and the cellular inhibitor assay are different experiments, not a tracked single-cell molecular history.

## 3. The connection this advances

The target is independently specified activity: production of a protein reporter in a declared reaction context. It is not a favorable label attached to an amount. The proposed explanatory connection is that earlier formed relations remain capable of participating in a later productive interaction, or are transformed into such relations before production occurs.

The explanatory unit includes the preparation, template, participating machinery, reaction conditions, interventions and measured output. Differentiating a catalytic complex from a consumed substrate identifies their different roles in that interaction; it does not make them fundamentally separate kinds. A laboratory preparation boundary is an intervention and measurement boundary within the same material history.

For the generative account, retaining organization can count as retaining a condition of future activity without requiring constant new production by every bearer. However, calling a configuration retained must not automatically classify it as recoverable. That classification needs an admissible transformation to the specified activity, supported by a mechanism or independently tested function. Mere molecular abundance cannot supply it by definition.

This adds an applicability connection to the existing continuation work. The earlier amount and work accounts constrain supply on a history. They do not identify every relation that makes a reaction possible. The present evidence motivates representing competence and actual transformation alongside provision. It does not discard the quantitative accounts or turn every dependency into an additive stock.

## 4. Requirements for the proposed organization model

The following are our formalization recommendations, not further empirical findings or newly proved theorems.

| Representation requirement | What it must preserve |
|---|---|
| Joint enabling | The required configuration, accessible substrates and other declared reaction conditions participate together. A favorable quantity alone is not a success certificate. |
| Inactivity and impairment | Distinguish an active configuration, a configuration with an admitted reactivation route, and material that lacks the represented competence. Avoid identifying these states solely by a count or marker. |
| Catalytic participation | A catalyst can remain after an operation; it need not be consumed once per output. A toy model that does consume it must explain that different interpretation. |
| Actual timing | Reactivation or reconstruction must occur before the operation whose availability it explains. A possible route is not an event that has already happened. |
| Alternative formation | Admit each relevant reconstruction, import or alternative assembly route explicitly. A selected protection route is not the definition of all competent organization. |
| Scope of absence | An empty-pool theorem concerns the declared organizing pool and admitted transitions. It must not silently become a statement about every physical route, every timescale or life's origin. |

An account of origin across an arbitrary admitted reaction family would improve on another isolated catalytic example: it could identify whether later competence derives from retained organization or an actual admitted formation route. Its substantive premise would be the adequacy of that family for the selected activity and interval. Biological evidence for impaired preparations does not itself verify complete absence or rule out unrepresented slow formation.

Nor should the model erase partial competence. A binary illustration can distinguish two extreme histories, but interpreting a population requires deciding what its state represents. Residual output, heterogeneity and delayed recovery are different from categorical impossibility. Any later quantitative fit must be independently undertaken; none is claimed here.

## 5. What would revise this account

The relevant rival is not a model with an unconstrained success predicate. It is an alternative explanation of the same independently measured activity. A bulk-supply explanation would need to account for the functional differences after the preparation comparison; an organization explanation must survive controls for accessible substrates, inhibitors, carryover and measurement normalization.

A decisive extension would track loss and restoration of a specified organizational relation together with resumed function under matched conditions. A restoration intervention that repairs the proposed relation but fails to restore the activity would challenge its sufficiency. Successful activity while the alleged indispensable class is genuinely absent would challenge its necessity, provided hidden retained components and alternative routes are assessed. These are prospective discriminating tests, not results reported by this note.

The outstanding explanatory task is how the relevant configurations themselves form, remain competent, and participate in successive stages. This round supplies a concrete constraint on that inquiry. It does not explain the origin of translation, establish complete biological recovery, or derive all living dependencies from the bare generative core.
