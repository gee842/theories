## The positive connection is already partly realized

The existing native law contains actual condition-sensitive reconstruction. Let `P` denote available precursor, `d=1−c` damage, and `r` the resident. In the [formation process](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/process.py), the relevant propensities are:

| Native event | Propensity and physical restriction |
|---|---|
| Local A copying | `β P local`, when `r=A` |
| Bulk A copying | `β₀ Acount P F` |
| Repair | `ρ F`, when `r=A` and `d=1` |
| Formation of either type | `μ d P F` |
| Capture | `g E`, when `c=1` and `local=0` |

At full material occupation copying is disabled. Wear makes precursor available; copying consumes it and removes that copying opportunity. Damage enables repair and formation; repair removes their damaged-site condition and enables capture. Capture fills the local store and removes its own enabling condition until copying or leakage consumes that store. These are changes of native events and propensities, not an observer's success labels. They require neither an extra evaluator nor a represented injunction to maintain a count.

The earlier [regulation construction](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-MATH.md) separately joins damage-coupled response formation, repaired capture, newly captured fuel and additional response copying. Its [review](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-REVIEW.md) already tested ineffective repair, capture interruption, copying removal and actual-successor restoration. This is positive evidence for a declared endogenous material feedback. It must not be recast as merely a supplied success predicate, nor claimed as a new discovery here.

What this does **not** establish is that every continuation condition becomes sensed, repaired or selected, or that the resulting activity maintains the whole prior organization. The scope follows the actual encounter and reconstruction pathways. Their supplied chemistry is an explanatory input within this model; it is not thereby external to generative reality.

## Exact same-law test: vacancy response is not restoration of every type

|---|---|---|
| `wear_a → copy_a` | `(2,1)` | Occupation and the original type counts |
| `wear_h → copy_a` | `(3,0)` | Occupation, with H replaced by A |

Each wear event creates one precursor. The resident remains A and local fuel remains one, so `copy_a` is enabled. It consumes that fuel and precursor, restoring full occupation. No new sensor, count target or type-preservation rule has been introduced. Both words have positive path probability under the default positive rates; neither is asserted to be the only or typical realized path.

This exact hand derivation identifies a real scope boundary: the native encounter responds to available precursor and the resident template. It does not identify which type was lost or reconstruct an arbitrary earlier composition. The second word genuinely changes the organization. Calling it restoration of occupancy is accurate; calling it restoration of the original A/H organization is not. The type labels here do not establish experience or ethical standing, so this is not itself a proof of domination or harm.

The test would change if a demonstrated retained relation directed reconstruction to the lost type, or if the relevant continuing activity required only occupation rather than type composition. Those are different explanatory claims to identify, not reasons to erase either history.

## Other substantive rivals that preserve the relevant activity

**A damaged capacity need not be repaired for every continuation.** With an active resident, a damaged site, one local unit, no common fuel and one available precursor, `copy_a` remains enabled. It completes one replacement without repair. With sufficient common fuel, bulk copying can also proceed at a damaged site. These are finite continuations, not indefinite viability. They preserve the source-capture-specific necessity of an intact site while refuting a general demand that every damaged capacity must be repaired before any useful activity can continue. The [exact dependency frontier](CONTINUING-DEPENDENCY-MATH.md) already retains both routes.

**A response can remove its own occasion.** Once repair makes the site intact, that repair event is disabled until another actual damage event. An intact site may capture and support copying without undergoing another repair. The existing [nondual applicability study](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-INTEGRATION.md) already proves the event-count relation requiring renewed damage before renewed repair. Stopping this particular response can therefore realize its present function. The [completion-sensitive inquiry](INQUIRY-COMPLETION-INTEGRATION.md) separately shows a supplied finite undertaking ending while other questions remain unresolved. Neither is evidence that all further activity or reasons have ended.

**Different continuation ranges need not be ordered by aggregate support.** The earlier same-ancestor pair leaves, after copying, common/source stocks `(1,215)` or `(0,216)` with all other plant coordinates equal. Both have aggregate energy 216. The former admits damage followed by repair; the latter does not. Conversely, the latter admits 216 repetitions of `wear_a → capture → copy_a`, opening and refilling one copying vacancy on each cycle, whereas the former's source supports at most 215 captures. These are distinct event-specific capacities, not two evaluations freely assigned to one unchanged configuration. Their significance depends on the actual activity in question; the larger count of captures is not automatically the better whole continuation. A scalar energy total loses both distinctions.

**An operating response can become mismatched to its source.** In the earlier retention law, the instantaneous repair propensity `ρGDF` does not consult the incident-source stock `E`. At otherwise matched states, repair can therefore occur with or without replenishing source. The accepted analytical comparison shows an advantage in expected copying for the source-present witness and a disadvantage when `E=0`. A constitutively endogenous response need not assess every condition of its own usefulness. Explaining its actual operation is different from explaining reliable adjustment of its scope.

**More response retention can compete with more supporting activity.** The existing retention review reports that removing copying increases expected usable captures and intact-site occupation in its primary comparison. Copying is a real retention route but also consumes conditions needed by other activity. The old numerical findings are credited here, not rerun or treated as a general monotonicity theorem. They rule out identifying every increase in retained response with an improvement in every independently specified continuation output.

**Shared dependence need not selectively favor its maintenance.** In the [two-type contest](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md), equal per-capita common-pool copying and wear give zero stopped-frequency drift even when only A repairs. Localized capture can instead favor the type actually occupying the repaired site; turnover can let H inherit it. The later [formed-variant construction](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-MATH.md) has ongoing formation and a different abundance statistic, so the earlier frequency theorem is not transferred to it. Actual retention depends on access and losses, not solely on which response produced a supporting consequence.

**Finite organization can exhaust.** The native rank strictly decreases at every admitted event. Its finite stocks therefore bound active event histories; terminal waiting is not renewal. The earlier positive-loss retention result also yields eventual disappearance of sites and regulators under its stated finite-source law. Local response formation and successful repair are compatible with eventual extinction. They do not establish a drive, capacity or obligation for indefinite persistence.

**Self-maintenance is insufficient for whole-process ethical direction.** The existing [selection account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) preserves success-driven impairment of independent correction, while the [market model](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md) distinguishes continued production from depletion of a nonessential affected person. These are separately declared models, not conclusions about the chemical toy. They supply substantive rivals to the ethical leap: actual organized persistence can coexist with suppressed capacities or burdens elsewhere. The [whole-process account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#the-origin-of-a-need-and-the-value-of-its-repair) also preserves the difference between repairing an existing burden and vindicating the arrangement that creates it.

## What an operative requirement claim must identify

There are related questions, not independent ontological realms:

1. **What depends on what?** Identify the activity, relevant duration and actual enabling relation. A conditional necessity for capture does not imply a necessity for every kind of copying or for indefinite survival.
2. **What actually responds?** Trace how the changed condition alters formation, event access, retention or revision through the process. A detached assay can describe this path but must not supply a hidden reward or command.
3. **Which continuation becomes effective?** Explain why those particular responses and their consequences are retained or enacted in the current organization. Preserve alternatives, inherited support, competing uses and misaligned feedback. A causal reconstruction need not claim that the process represents its requirement explicitly.
4. **What warrants preserving or changing it?** Identify the actual lives, purposes and relations at stake. Causal effectiveness and endogenous formation do not answer this ethical question by themselves.

The [purpose-correction mathematics](YITI-REN-PURPOSE-MATH.md) already distinguishes a supplied `K,G` continuation contract, existential feasibility, restriction to viable routes and a realizable policy. An observer can calculate which transitions preserve a completion without those transitions being selected by the native organization. Conversely, an operative response may exist without computing that predicate. We should credit both facts rather than demand an infinite sequence of further controllers because a law remains stable.

For the next substantive claim, fix the activity and rival descriptions before implementation. Show that an actual changed dependency alters the process carrying the response, then that the response changes the independently identified activity and feeds into its own continuing availability or replacement. A matched interruption or alternative pathway should separate the named mediator from inherited support or a directly acting common cause. This is an identification discipline, not a universal requirement that every function have a unique, individually necessary component: redundancy and alternative realizations remain admissible.

Most importantly, a new undertaking variable should not count as explanation merely because it changes. The stronger target is an actual reorganization of which relations are being maintained or completed, caused by encountered consequences, with its continuing effects demonstrated. A claim about learned revision needs retained acquired history to matter beyond present reactants; the native vacancy example alone is condition-sensitive reconstruction, not learning. Stable laws can support that further revision, and an ordinary stateful controller remains a valid implementation description.
