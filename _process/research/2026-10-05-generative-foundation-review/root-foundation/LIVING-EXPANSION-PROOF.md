# Disjoint current provision and an actual prior pool increase

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored continuation of the [inherited-provision test](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#coverage-audit-and-next-decisive-test). Source: [LivingExpansion.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/LivingExpansion.lean). The target connection is **finite inherited organization → provision for coexisting differentiated continuations**, including when additional provision is necessary despite persistence of older material.

**Result:** explicit, nonduplicated assignments of current occurrences yield a general required-copy bound. If those actually provided requirements exceed the initial selected count, the supplied reaction history contains a prior reaction that strictly increased that count. The witness retains its actual prefix, transition and suffix. **14 new named theorems / 19 explicit declarations pass Lean 4.29.1, with 94 audits across the three-module closure.**

The main theorem **assumes the selected endpoint requirements are actually provided**. It does not derive physiological success from an allocation certificate, define life by a threshold, or promise that an expanding continuation will succeed. A prior increase can be activation, assembly or import. Its existence does not identify the ancestry or causal contribution of that event to particular current participants.

## 2. Current assignment structure

At a reached inventory, choose one participant species with count `count`. The occurrence indices `0` through `count - 1` represent distinct **current** copies. They are local enumeration positions, not persistent molecular identities. Reusing index `0` in a later state does not prove retention of the same molecule.

`CurrentProvision count centers required assigned` states:

1. `centers` lists distinct currently selected centers.
2. Each selected center has at least its independently specified number of required copies in its assigned occurrence list.
3. Concatenating all assigned lists produces no duplicate index.
4. Every assigned index is present in the current pool: it is below `count`.

Requirements may differ by center, may be zero and need not exhaust the available pool. Neither balanced partition nor equal growth is assumed. The structure does **not** contain the desired inequality between summed requirements and the pool count. `requirements_le_assigned` and `distinct_subset_length` establish the two relevant counting steps, and `current_provision_bound` derives the bound.

This is a representation of **disjoint current provision**, not a claim that every enabling relationship must be exclusively localized. Shared metabolites, transferable support, sequential service and alternative arrangements can invalidate an attempted physical interpretation of that exclusivity. They must be represented or allowed by the application. A selection of many historical births does not by itself supply a current provision certificate.

Following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), the centers are differentiated relationships within the same unfolding. Their labels and boundaries do not confer independent fundamental status. The code does not construct those biological boundaries; the application must identify why these are currently distinct positions of provision and why assigning the same physical occurrence twice would misdescribe them.

## 3. The actual history and the necessity inference

The imported reaction process already requires admitted recipes, adequate input multiplicities and exact reached-state updates. Inputs and outputs are finite lists over arbitrary species. A retained catalytic copy can occur on both sides without being newly created or consumed by every use.

`IncreaseOccurs` records an actual decomposition of the original reaction word, an actual prefix to the selected reaction, the reaction itself, and an actual suffix to the final state. The selected count increases across that reaction, and its recipe has more selected output occurrences than selected input occurrences. `reached_increase_has_actual_event` proves that a net endpoint increase entails such a witness.

`expanded_provision_requires_actual_increase` combines that history with the current assignment bound: if the total actually provided current requirements exceed the initial selected count, an increasing reaction occurred within that history. Thus persistence of all older material would not remove the requirement for additional provision where the declared disjoint current requirements exceed it. No age, wear hazard, maximum lifetime, per-use destruction, growth rate or physical duration appears in the theorem.

This is a **necessity statement conditional on achieved provision**. It does not prove that a pool increase happens merely because an observer wants more descendants, nor that an increased pool produces a functioning descendant. If the requirements cannot be met, the selected continuation may fail or change. The theorem does not infer which outcome occurs.

The witness establishes occurrence of a count increase, **not genealogy**. That particular increase might later be lost, and another event might supply the current copies. No identity, use or contribution of an individual earlier copy to an individual current center follows. The general result also does not select one obligatory formation recipe. Increasing functional count may involve activation of retained material, assembly, recruitment or import; it need not create new material or synthesize every constituent.

## 4. Inherited bounds and preserved alternatives

`trace_bound_without_increasing_recipes` audits every admitted recipe's selected outputs against its inputs. In that declared no-increase repertoire, the final selected count cannot exceed the initial count. `inherited_provision_bound` therefore bounds the summed current requirements by the inherited count. The recipe audit is substantive: adding a productive assembly, activation or import route may invalidate it.

`uniform_requirements_lower_bound`, `uniform_current_provision_bound` and `inherited_uniform_frontier_bound` cover the special case where each currently selected center has an independently specified common minimum. The checked conclusion is `minimum × numberOfCurrentCenters ≤ availableCount`, or the inherited count in the no-increase regime. The minimum is supplied and need not be positive; a positive choice is not a universal law that every living center needs one copy of the chosen species.

Three small checks distinguish the intended scope:

- `finite_reserve_allows_unequal_provision` assigns two copies to one center and one to another from a four-copy pool, leaving one unused. Finite inherited provision can be sufficient without immediate reconstruction. The check establishes an allocation certificate, not a biological success assay.
- `three_required_centers_exceed_two_copies` rules out a disjoint assignment of one copy to each of three current centers from two copies.
- `successive_snapshot_assignments_reuse_an_occurrence` permits separate single-center snapshots to assign the same locally enumerated occurrence. This illustrates why historical births cannot be added as simultaneous requirements. It supplies no unmodeled transfer mechanism or molecular identity across snapshots. The earlier allocation module separately contains an actual sequential catalytic-reuse trace.

An alternative route that avoids a selected copy requirement changes the application's premise. The formal conclusion is not a disjunction proving that every possible route must increase this chosen pool. To claim necessity across alternatives, the correspondence must cover those alternatives or identify an independently warranted common requirement.
