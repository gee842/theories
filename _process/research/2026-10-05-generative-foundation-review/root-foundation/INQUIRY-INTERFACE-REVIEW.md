## Positive result and concrete challenge

An inquiry can change how it encounters, distinguishes and responds without ceasing to concern its continuing situation. Its continuity need not be identity of every action, question, answer or criterion. The account must instead explain the actual historical connection and the relations claimed to survive the change. It must also acknowledge what no longer survives. Such an explanation belongs within the activity; it does not require a fundamentally separate evaluator that bestows meaning on otherwise unrelated stages.

The concrete challenge is that **continuing an inquiry is not automatically conservative refinement of a mathematical interface**. The [new identity account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/IMMANENT-MATHEMATICAL-IDENTITY.md) supplies a canonical projection only when the larger interface preserves the underlying process and the old action/question laws. A practice may change those laws, stop using an old indicator, or change the processes with which it interacts. Keeping an old label does not meet the theorem's premises. Conversely, lack of that particular projection does not make the changed practice unrelated or meaningless.

Even newly available participation need not correspond to an analyst enlarging the mathematical action set. Forming a connection can make an existing partial action executable in a later state under an unchanged encompassing law. The behavioral abstraction already retains action definedness. Enacted sampling, changed feasible participation and enlargement of the description's scope must therefore be distinguished rather than inferred from each other's names.

The strongest positive mathematical constraint is already [I2a in the supporting note](IMMANENT-MATHEMATICAL-IDENTITY-MATH.md#corollary-i2a--strict-refinement-cannot-be-recovered-from-the-old-class-alone): when richer identification strictly separates an old class, its actual finer class cannot be recovered from that old class alone. New interaction, other retained distinctions or warranted restrictions on the possibilities must do the work. Picking a finer representative supplies a guess, not a recovered distinction. This result concerns all information actually available for recovery; ignoring an existing archive to manufacture insufficiency would misapply it.

## What must remain accountable across a claimed revision

There is no demand to preserve all old questions forever. The obligations depend on the continuity being claimed.

**The actual history.** Later activity must inherit or encounter the effects it is said to revise. Reconstruct which observations formed, which operations occurred and what persists through the change. An analyst cannot restore an earlier configuration, replace the environment and call the resulting success a continuation of the depleted or transformed endpoint. A prospective alternative from an earlier actual checkpoint is legitimate when identified as such.

**The reference of retained evidence.** An earlier observation concerns a particular interaction at its acquisition time. It can remain true while losing its former predictive use. The [method-formation source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/methods/formation/model.py) retains H3 because the new generator is interpreted relative to it. Replaying the six-bit archive recovers the phase at its last reading; after another action, the separately committed cursor is needed. Treating the old archive as a current reading would silently change its referent. An adapted interpretation may be warranted, but its relationship to the earlier interaction must be explained.

**The exact claims carried forward.** If an earlier operation is claimed interchangeable with a new one, preserve the relevant admissible contexts, outcomes and definedness, rather than only its output at one entrance. The identity note already distinguishes reached-state equivalence from operation equivalence. If only a narrower property is retained, state that property. A read that changes the target or consumes a particular support is an operation in the relevant history; it cannot be treated as an unchanged external peek when those effects matter.

**The concern or problem being continued.** A practice can change both its method and its local evidence of progress while remaining answerable to what those methods were meant to enable. This is not necessarily an immutable scalar objective. If the broader aspiration is also revised, identify that change and its grounds rather than claiming success on the old aspiration by redefinition. The people or processes involved need not remain unchanged either; their actual development and differentiated consequences are part of the connection.

**The possibilities excluded by the revision.** Abandoning an unnecessary test can be appropriate. Disabling a challenging observation can also make a practice appear successful while concealing its failure. To distinguish them, track the condition the discarded question concerned, whether it still matters, and what evidence supports its replacement. Removing a report is not automatically removing its target. A changed understanding may, however, genuinely change the target experience or relationship; that possibility must be investigated rather than dismissed as mere relabeling.

These are commitments of particular preservation claims, not a new universal checklist defining every legitimate inquiry. An open transformation can preserve historical continuity while rejecting earlier identifications. It should not claim an exact mathematical invariance that it has not established.

## Practice transformation tests the stronger meaning

The [practice-transformation argument](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) is compatible with this account. Its point is that a practice's own effects can change what repeating it does. Historical usefulness does not become false merely because repetition loses its warrant. The reported teaching change illustrates a possible continuity of concern through altered exercises and indicators; its causal and ethical success is not independently demonstrated by the historical report alone.

A substantive rival preserves observation-dependent adaptation while organizing it around programme compliance. It can respond intelligently to resistance, alter its questions and produce continued participation, yet obstruct the capabilities or possibilities that originally justified the practice. This rival does not arbitrarily toggle a value predicate. It identifies a different actual relation between participation, acquired dispositions and life beyond the exercise. To meet it, the positive account must identify those consequences rather than making compliance the definition of adequate continuation.

Another rival accepts the same acquired distinctions while retaining a fixed inquiry recipe. The source's adaptive table and phase-conditioned movement can be described by such a recipe. That rival preserves their causal achievements and challenges only a stronger claim that the repertoire or inquiry law itself formed. To establish the stronger claim, identify the previously absent or different operative relation, its formation from accessible consequences and its subsequent use. A renamed phase variable or an experimenter's new query is insufficient.

Neither challenge demands an outside source of meaning. They require the account to preserve the actual relationship it claims to explain. Nor does changing an inquiry erase earlier effects or outstanding grounds for response. Stopping an exercise and answering what it has already done remain distinct.

## Accepted scope and conditions of revision

The positive connection already supported is that acquired discrepancies and observations can organize subsequent diagnostic contexts and action sequences, and a retained method change can alter the duration of subsequent native acquisition under specified laws. This can continue the same inquiry through its actual history and retained references. Conservative enlargement of the exact semantic interface is a stronger, separately testable relation. Transformation of the process or the concern is different again and needs an explicit account rather than an automatic projection.

The account would need correction if later dispatch bypassed the supposed acquired state, if a supposedly new question was only an analyst's annotation, if an old record silently changed its acquisition epoch, if operation substitution failed in an admitted surrounding context, or if a claimed richer identification was selected from a coarse summary with no distinguishing interaction or retained information. A wider trace showing the allegedly unavailable distinction was already accessible would instead narrow the proposed obstruction.
