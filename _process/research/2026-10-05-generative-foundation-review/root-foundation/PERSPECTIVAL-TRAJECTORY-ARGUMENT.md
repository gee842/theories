# Forming perspectives and appraising their trajectories

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored affirmative investigation under [Ren's authorization](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-perspectival-trajectory-research-direction.md). This continues [the initial integration](PERSPECTIVAL-GOOD-AND-TRAJECTORIES.md), rather than replacing the universal normative objective with a theory of preference development. **Scope:** philosophical argument and specified comparative cases. Mathematical results belong to their separately checked companion; none of the ethical conclusions below is claimed as a Lean theorem or empirical finding.

## 1. The central connection

The generative account should explain not only which outcomes a perspective favors, but how activity forms the perspective through which subsequent outcomes will be encountered and evaluated. Its trajectory can therefore change all of the following together: actual realizations, the conditions of further realization, access to those conditions, practical weighting, and the influence by which some evaluations shape later activity.

These are analytical roles within one unfolding. A relatively enduring center, its encounters and its surroundings must be identified through the relations being maintained and transformed. Naming several coordinates does not establish independent foundational kinds. A local law can preserve a selection/observation distinction while leaving the formation of those roles supplied; a complete explanation must account for that formation.

The universal question is **why the qualitative realization of this whole history gives practical reasons across its differently formed intelligences**. A theory explaining how a preferred direction wins is not yet a justification of that direction. Conversely, explaining evaluative formation is relevant: it can reveal a concealed dependency in an argument from endorsement to warrant, or show that a changed perspective partly constitutes a new actual good.

## 2. A field needs a specified relation

Ren's Gaussian-like suggestion may represent declining transmission, diminishing familiarity, selective attention, expected benefit or concern. These have different referents. A dependency can have large consequences through a rarely noticed link; a familiar threat can attract intense attention; a distant life can matter without benefiting the one considering it. No general sign, metric, normalization or additive aggregation follows from describing these relations as fields.

A richer field can be asymmetric and history-dependent, with several centers or a sharp boundary. Its shape need not determine its ethical quality. A stable commitment can rightly concentrate effort; a broad picture can aid domination. Whole appraisal also preserves actual self-good. Treating every bounded orientation as an error would erase precisely the differentiated agency the theory must explain.

For mathematical work, a deterministic evolution and a perspective readout may be supplied as one local representation. Then ask which future distinctions the readout preserves. Two histories can look equally successful now and contain different conditions for later continuation. Such a failure diagnoses the selected representation, not the nondual theory as a whole and not the first ground of ethical comparison. Richer fields must be tested against actual targets, rather than praised for their width alone.

## 3. The new discriminator: creation, present care and transformation

The inherited [formed-evaluation argument](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#28-generative-direction-and-the-formation-of-its-standards) already observes that a trajectory can create standards that later endorse it, and that a new appreciation can genuinely change what is realized. The earlier [formed-appreciation account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#formed-appreciation-and-added-good) also distinguishes an acquired good from warrant for producing it, while [elective concern](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#what-choosing-to-care-can-create) recognizes formed responsibilities. We do not rediscover those results. The next comparison connects three practical questions:

1. Before a perspective-forming arrangement is chosen, what favors or opposes bringing it about?
2. Once its effects exist, what acquired goods and adverse conditions now matter?
3. Which available transformation responds well to that reached situation, including the losses and new conditions it creates?

The candidate claims these questions can have different answers without inconsistency. A cultivated attachment can be real and valuable even if the arrangement that produced it was unjustified. Ending that arrangement can require care for its effects. This possibility tests appraisal of whole trajectories more sharply than either freezing the initial preferences or treating final endorsement as sufficient.

### Specified comparison

Consider a common workshop in which people acquire skill, shared understanding and meaningful relationships. The organizers can establish two forms of apprenticeship. Both can achieve the specified competence and have an available route to completing the work. Equal competence does not establish equal whole good: distinctive relationships or meaningful forms of participation may differ and must be assessed. These are constructed conditions for comparison, not measured facts about apprenticeships.

In **arrangement A**, instruction develops skill and pride while allowing credible evidence about severe strain to affect the schedule and distribution of work. Commitments can be substantial; unrestricted freedom to reopen everything is not required.

In **arrangement B**, access to belonging and advancement is organized around accepting a pattern of severe avoidable strain. Acquired standards treat enduring that strain as evidence of deserving one's place. Participants can become genuinely skilled, proud and attached. Some may accurately understand the strain and still endorse the arrangement. The case therefore does not depend on false reports, ignorance, absent enjoyment or a wholly fictitious good.

The contrast must specify actual effects, available alternatives, permission and costs. For the present comparison, the severe strain continues to interrupt ordinary living, the alternative supports the same specified competence without it, and there is no identified compensating achievement that requires it. If those stipulations fail, the verdict needs reassessment. Distinct histories are being compared; they are not claimed identical in every ethically relevant respect.

### Prospective appraisal

The affirmative argument against choosing arrangement B has three premises. First, severe interruption of ordinary living is an adverse aspect of this continuation. Second, the choosing activity has effective and sufficiently informed control over whether to impose it. Third, the proposed gain in endorsement and belonging does not adequately justify that avoidable interruption when the specified less burdensome formation is available. These are substantive ethical claims with actual relational referents, not consequences of mere connectedness.

Their positive support concerns what the arrangement makes possible and what it demands as the price of participation. It trains a practical dependency whose reproduction is served by favorable interpretation of its burden. The reason under consideration addresses the intelligence choosing that dependency before assent to the resulting role has formed. This develops the [prospective-imposition investigation](UNIVERSAL-RESPONSE-COMPOSITION-ARGUMENT.md); its additional target is the formation of the future appraisal itself.

Later endorsement supplies evidence of an acquired disposition and may express genuine goods. It cannot, by itself, establish the adequacy of the earlier choice: the alternatives, imposed burden and causal production of the endorsement are also part of that choice's grounds. This does not invalidate all internally formed judgment or all adaptation. Every competent appraisal has a history; causal origin alone is neither disqualification nor vindication.

### Appraisal after formation

Once skills and relationships have formed, their value is not cancelled by criticism of their origin. A reform that preserves relationships and meaningful completion while changing the damaging condition has a different profile from an abrupt shutdown that destroys them. The former can be favored in the specified case while the original choice remains criticized. The past burden also remains in the history after reform; its later correction does not make it never have occurred.

The reason to care for these acquired goods need not become a reason to preserve the whole mechanism that produced them. If a particular attachment requires continuing an injurious practice, the actual conflict must be assessed; attachment is neither automatically worthless nor automatically decisive. Present participants should not be required to erase their own experience to make the critique intelligible.

The reached situation thus introduces practical grounds that were not present in the same form before its formation. Present need alone does not retrospectively authorize manufacturing that need. Valid later ratification or forgiveness can change current permissions or claims without establishing that the earlier informed choice was justified. This is the round's proposed temporal distinction, not a complete theory of responsibility or a deduction of all its premises.

## 4. The substantive rival and the constructive narrowing control

The strongest rival accepts the burden, its avoidability, acquired attachment and the organizers' role. It argues that disciplined commitment, informed willingness or the particular shared achievement makes the formation worthwhile. A second rival treats the affected participants' informed final endorsement as sufficient for its legitimacy. These are substantive positions, not failures to recognize interconnectedness.

The first rival can change the comparison if it identifies a genuine achievement or warranted commitment absent from the proposed alternative. It must show why that difference justifies this burden; naming discipline cannot supply its own priority. The second must explain why endorsement settles the earlier comparison despite its causal dependence on an arrangement deliberately making a damaging condition central to belonging. The affirmative account must in turn defend the adverse significance and scope of the proposed constraint. It cannot win by defining the rival's choice as defective intelligence.

The [counterreview's harder rival](PERSPECTIVAL-TRAJECTORY-COUNTERREVIEW.md#5-strongest-rival-preserving-the-same-history) concedes an earlier unauthorized expansion of control but claims that competent present ratification creates a new legitimate association. It also notes that some losses on departure end a particular shared relationship rather than express a penalty the association owes compensation for. Answering it requires identifying actual authorization, the significance of dissent, portable support and losses intrinsic to ending the relation. The earlier matched-competence comparison does not settle this case. The counterreview consequently compares continued terms, abrupt dissolution, costly transformation and restoration, with no option defined as best.

Preserve the constructive control: a person can knowingly undertake a bounded difficult practice, acquire appreciation for it, narrow attention during its completion, and rightly decline further reassessment for a time. The activity may create a genuine good unavailable without that commitment. Nor must every good increase later productivity. These features defeat any proposed criterion equating maximum revisability, minimal burden or widest concern with universal good.

The proposed discriminator therefore concerns the actual formation relationship, alternatives and differentiated consequences, including their practical authority. A mere dislike of changed preferences would condemn learning and cultivation as well as capture.

## 5. How a meta-attractor can enter this trajectory

The existing meta-attractor mechanism adds a further relation: successful activity acquires influence over the conditions of subsequent selection. In the workshop case, an arrangement could reward those most willing to reproduce its evaluative standards and give them influence over admission, teaching and assessment. That would be a candidate pathway through which present success shapes future perspectives. A contrasting arrangement could use success to strengthen independent assessment of both achievement and burden.

These are hypotheses about mechanisms, not diagnosed attractors or inevitable outcomes. To call either an attraction basin requires a specified evolution, domain, notion of approach and checked argument or adequate empirical evidence. To recover an older model within the broader generative account requires an explicit correspondence; neither naming the variables nor drawing the feedback loop supplies it.

Broad held concern may still be ineffective when selection penalizes its exercise. Conversely, narrow initial concern can coexist with arrangements that protect broader consequences. A credible test must therefore vary the practical selection relation as well as the field's stated shape. This preserves the current manuscript's distinction between held appraisal and effective incentives.

## 6. Karma as a history that changes its own continuation

The continuing-effects account, already present in [Ren's genealogy](RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-SOURCES.md), can retain four linked questions along a trajectory:

| What changes | What to preserve in the account |
|---|---|
| Actual realization | Whose living, experience, relationship or finite achievement changes, and how? |
| Further conditions | What support, dependence, opportunities or obstacles continue, including after the initiator leaves? |
| Perspective formation | What becomes visible, salient, valued, habitual or difficult to question? |
| Influence over selection | Which evaluations acquire the practical capacity to shape future formation and response? |

This is a research profile, not four additive moral quantities or a proof that these exhaust the whole. It permits one trajectory to improve present achievement while damaging renewal, or to make a constructive reform temporarily costly. It preserves mixed and incomparable cases. A direction may be defensible over one interval without being monotone, and a stable global direction cannot be asserted without further grounds.

The propagation of effects does not require repayment to the initiator. A damaging institution can persist, and an act of care can have value without securing its own reproduction. Consequently, ethical appraisal of a trajectory must not be inferred from which pattern wins or how much activity compounds.

## 7. What this advances, and what remains open

The advance is the connected comparison of **prospective inducement, acquired present goods and responsibility during transformation**, including genuine losses that cannot simply be erased. The appraisal of perspective formation itself is inherited from the earlier studies. The before/after/transformation comparison supports a candidate continuity requirement: new judgments should answer to the actual history and changed grounds, rather than gain authority solely through their own successful production. Its applicability and force remain to be justified across intelligence; this is not a proved universal invariant.

The checked mathematical companion supplies a different contribution: conditions under which a selected representation preserves future comparisons. It can show a missing relation in a projection without deriving the ethical comparison it is meant to preserve. The primary-source assessment tests the plausibility and limits of reciprocal formation. Neither replaces the normative argument.

The next central question is whether the candidate constraint on choosing perspective-forming arrangements follows from the same whole ground that warrants care for their actual effects, and whether the informed-endorsement or disciplined-commitment rivals justify a restriction. If the argument merely repeats a supplied anti-injury premise, report that scope. A new dynamical witness cannot convert that premise into universal proof.
