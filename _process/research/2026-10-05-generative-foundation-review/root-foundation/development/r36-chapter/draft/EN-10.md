## 10. Glossary

(a) holds the core terms, one sense each; (b) indexes the formal terms the sections use, each with the first sentence of its meaning and its Lean anchor; (c) explains the retired labels and Lean names, and is the only place in the chapter that does; (d) and (e) cross the chapter's vocabulary with the corpus glossary and the earlier texts' vocabulary of closing and opening. Readings marked as the AI's are recorded in the [decision register](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md). Five words are kept to one sense each in this chapter: *whole* is everything in the one unfolding (the class of represented worlds is the *space*); *condition* is a relatum in the role of what others depend on, and “the continuation condition” is that proposition's fixed name, a law's local premises being its *hypotheses*; *law* is a rule of change, a checked statement being a *theorem*; *reading* is an interpretation with its standing (process, frame and world are *views*, and a quantity is *read off* what it is built from); *selection* goes through couplings (subsets of premises are *premise subsets*, and choice principles are *choice*).
