## 4. Collective dynamics

Collective activity is explained from actual relations first. It does not presuppose opposed interests, bargaining, who gains more, or a fixed common utility ([the reminder on common problems](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-collective-destructive-attractors.md#common-problem-solving-and-awareness)); actual conflicts and differences remain. A collective is a region of formed relations (§5), not a unit with an interest of its own. None of the laws below is an axiom or says "must"; whether an actual collective meets a law's conditions is empirical (§8.7).

### 4.1 Joint consumption, markets and the meta-attractor

**In words.** Joint consumption has no single consumer: the consumer and the formation that loses support both take part in the drawn condition, and difference-making belongs to the joint activity. Where the influence the successful gain weakens the constraints on the strategies that produced the success, present advantage changes future selection ([meta-attractor](../../meta-attractor/README.md)).

- `consumption_relates`: consumer and loser both take part in the drawn condition; with `jointly_consuming` (§2.1), the class belongs to the joint relation.
- *Joint difference-making*: under overdetermination, withholding any single party's part, the loss happens all the same, while the relational reading still counts each drawer as consuming; in a gradual depletion no finite set of events makes the whole difference, while the breaking event makes the difference to that break.
- *Substitution does not certify renewal*: a party continuing by consumption can turn to other conditions, and the one that lost does not recover (`stepwise_includes_substitution`, §2.1).

So under relational neutrality, a party continuing by consumption cannot place the one it ends outside significance on the ground of unrelatedness (§6.3), and “my share makes no difference” does not remove a drawer from consumption. None of this yields responsibility or duty. Market selection stays within the generative regime only where successful activity renews the conditions on which further exchange and life depend and those affected can still discover damage and change the arrangement ([markets and generative regimes](MARKETS-AND-GENERATIVE-REGIMES.md)); the periphery bounds checked are continued operation under a protection certificate, the exact reach of partial correction, a supported finite transition, and an interval of positive seeds finite support cannot rescue.

### 4.2 Mutual dependence and its pace

**In words.** Read the closures of §2.5 as the whole: a flow shares conditions throughout where every system is the whole, is mutually dependent where every influence closure is, and mutually fed where every feeding closure is. Each gives the one before it and neither converse holds; mutual dependence is not mutual benefit. Where the couplings carrying such dependence act at every step around a loop, the most difference that can arrive grows exponentially both ways.

- `Flow.mutuallyDependent_iff_reach`: a flow is mutually dependent exactly where every reach closure is the whole, so whatever a formation affects can, at some remove, affect it back. The clearer's web shares throughout while the dweller lies beyond the clearer's influence closure (`Clearing.shares_not_mutual`); in `Drawing` two formations each take from what the other requires and neither feeds the other (`Drawing.mutual_not_fed`).
- `Flow.MutuallyDependent.unrestricted`: under each reading, significance bounded by the matching closure is the continuation condition (`Flow.SharesThroughout.unrestricted`, `Flow.MutuallyFed.unrestricted`), extending §2.5's one common condition to any web of mutual feeding. Used in §6.3.
- `exponential_around`: around a loop coupled at every step a chain advances one coupling a step (`CoupledAlways.chainInTime`), R turns multiply what reached the condition by at least 2^R (`carried_pow_around`), and with strengths at most M and gaps at most D it is at most D·(1 + M)^t (`carried_le_pow_always`); from every condition to every condition where couplings are mutual at every step (`CoupledThroughout.exponential`). A selection with transmission raises the carriers' ratio at least as (a/b)^n (`share_ratio_pow`).
- `Flow.Balances.half_stands`: under balance, with nothing given, a draw exceeding everything drawn before it leaves more than half the starting stock at every step it is met, and lasting n steps needs a stock of at least 2^n − 1 (`Flow.Balances.doubling_needs`); in `DoublingDraw` four of seven units stand at the last step, then none.

*Readings* [AI reading, D39, revised 19 October 2026]. “A highly entangled system” reads as mutual dependence, sharing throughout the weaker reading and mutual feeding the stronger; every link of affecting then lies on a loop through the whole. “Exponential pace” reads as growth around loops acting at every step, and in a selection as growth of the share ratio. “Little time to react” reads as `half_stands` with `Web.break_not_bounded`: what stands gives no warning. That a dependence acts as a coupling by level with positive strength at every step is supplied; whether the Earth is so is not measured.

### 4.3 Relative position, and what a collective's continuation needs

**In words.** A part's position read against the others' sums to zero by counting: its rise is the rest's summed fall, and no change raises every part. Position does not protect: a joint excess ends a part at its end through its dependence, whatever its position. A collective's continuation is asked of runs, not of positions: keeping a part is not renewal, there is no number of the whole, conditions are joined rather than multiplied, a counter-loop has to reach how the rule reads, and the invariants are laws, not axioms.

- `RelativePosition.change_balances`: across every change of amounts a part's rise in position is the rest's summed fall, no change raises every part (`RelativePosition.not_all_rise`), and every part can draw more while one falls (`DrawingMore.grow_and_fall`; on a flow with no hypothesis, `Flow.drawn_positions`).
- `Flow.ends_at_race_end`: with balance, a joint excess that every part joins ends a part at its end exactly through its dependence; with renewal gated the greatest parts end with the condition for good (`Flow.race_ends_greatest`, `Flow.greatest_end_for_good`; in `Race` the part that rises in position ends with the rest). Ungated renewal restores the part (`Restored.returns`); a greatest part that does not depend on what it draws down continues (`Clearing.greatest_continues`), and what it ends is a continuation with significance only under a significance (`Clearing.loss_needs_significance`): under the condition it counts from every standpoint, under none it does not.
- `Renewable.kept_part_not_renewal`: no course leaves the guard “the focal formation is active” from the start, while one run renews and another consumes the sustaining formation's condition; whether no course leaves a guard is a fact of the configuration, renewing a fact of the run.
- `Viability.kernel_and_iff` with `ChoicePoint.no_rule_decides_joint`: where every course is counted, no course leaves two guards together exactly where no course leaves either, the whole truth of “any factor at zero makes the product zero”; where continuation goes through choices, each condition can be kept while the two cannot (`ChoicePoint.each_not_joint`), and no rule on the separate answers decides the joint one.

### 4.4 Giving that waits on giving, tolerance, loops, pace and regulation

**In words.** Where giving waits on giving, withholding with memory stays and spreads, and without memory it returns around every loop; more waiting never gives more. For every law that keeps inclusion the worst start is total withholding, and from it only a formation giving whatever it waits on restores, exactly into the formations every long enough chain of waits passes it. A loop closes a difference only through a link giving back less than it received. A rule changing only at reading steps fixes what it holds at stretches of 2N − 1, and a level held from both sides settles exactly where the response does not overshoot.

- `withholding_absorbs` with `withholding_returns`: with memory one withholding ends all giving within `d` waits from step `d` on, and withholding stays and spreads (`waitsAll_withholding_stays`, `withholding_spreads`); without memory it returns around every loop at every multiple of its length; under both laws more waiting never gives more (`waitsAll_more_waiting`, `answers_more_waiting`). In a ring of three a formation giving whatever it waits on restores all from the third step, and no earlier (`Ring.givesFirst_restores_exact`).
- `worst_start` with `restores_iff`: for every law keeping inclusion a formation gives at step `n` from every start exactly where it does from total withholding; under the tolerating law (`tolerating`; tolerance zero is waiting on all) total withholding is kept without a formation sparing all it waits on (`total_stays`), and with one giving first and sources decided, a formation gives from every start exactly where no chain of `n` waits clear of sources leads into it, a loop clear of sources never restored (`clear_loop_withholds`). Tolerance absorbs without restoring (`kept_set`, `thin_absorbed`, `Triad.total_kept`); the fault-envelope certificate read as a law of giving keeps a set giving exactly where envelopes are separated (`certified_available_iff`); on a web the tolerance law is a run (`web_tolerating`).
- `closing_needs_less`: where every link gives on at least what it receives the difference never falls (`never_closes_of_no_less`); a return map below what it receives closes within as many turns as the difference, exactly (`closes_within_exact`); a cut closes at once only where every later link gives nothing for nothing (`cut_closes`, `cut_reopened`); with whole-number factors a loop closes at the first turn, keeps, or runs away (`nothing_between`), and closing gradually needs a link that is not a multiplication (`gradual_needs_another_link`). The self-loop's return map is the carried amount (`selfLoop_carried`, `selfLoop_gap`); the equality around longer cycles is not shown. This answers for loops §7.7's question which law closes rather than widens a difference.
- `pace_law`: a two-level course is a run of one law (`paced_run`); a rule that never reads is unreached and loses what it loses (`rule_unreached`, `never_reads_loses`); a fast difference reaches the rule only at a reading step (`rule_until_reading`), regaining what a fixed rule loses needs a change of rule (`Switching.changing_regains`), and a stretch of `L` steps fixes every pair of held-rule courses with at most `N` states exactly when `L ≥ 2N − 1` (from §3.3); a course read whole is fixed by its first `N + 1` steps (`whole_fixed_exact`).
- `Flow.withinGap_iff_between` with `Regulation.no_narrower_band`: with balance the exchange departs from evenness by at most the gap exactly where the next level lies between `m` and the level, the bound being exactly no overshoot; within it, opposing, the level settles at `m` (`Flow.regulates`, `Flow.settles`, `Flow.restores_exactly`), and beyond it an opposing exchange crosses `m` (`Regulation.Overshooting.oscillates`; one-sided restoring stays away, `Regulation.OneSided.above`). A rule reading `ℓ` steps late keeps no band narrower than `2 (ℓ + 1) d`, attained by the late return (`Regulation.lateReturn_band`); a late relay overshoots by `ℓ M` and settles exactly where `a + (ℓ + 1) M ≤ b + 1` (`Regulation.relay_threshold`).

Not derived: that a given system regulates, how late it reads and its step; one policy keeping a band from every start; the lumping of several regulated levels into one; real factors.

*Readings* [AI reading, D48]. Cooperation among strangers is giving that waits on giving across chains longer than any acquaintance; more waiting never giving more is the structural price of complexity. The spiral “others will deceive me, so I deceive first” is withholding with memory, which absorbs; around a loop without memory withholding returns. Kept promises, enforced contracts and institutions acting whatever was last received are laws that give first: the law changes, the state alone does not. Tolerance is the slack that lets cooperation survive scattered defection, and it does not rebuild trust lost everywhere; a cycle of mutual reliance with no source giving first stays withheld. An exchange of harms is a loop: answering exactly in kind keeps the difference, answering more runs away, and a conflict closes only where some link gives back less (compensation, arbitration, absorption, or a cut followed by links giving nothing for nothing); with whole-number strengths nothing closes gradually, which is why the account's conflict institutions are links of another kind. Stability with adaptability is a slow level reading the fast one at stretches of at least `2N − 1`, as constitution is slower than law and law than policy; that this layering is good is the comparison's to say, not the law's.

### 4.5 Passing, shared codes and capture through the rule

**In words.** A pattern outlasting its carriers lasts only through passing, and ends within a span once passing stops. A code formed between formations by hearing makes no form: the start and the order of hearing fix the form reached, no form is privileged, and only a centre, a preferred form or an order of hearing breaks a symmetric disagreement. Where a rule of selection is read from the share it selects, what holds the outcome is how the rule reads, not the rule's value, which every reading step sets again.

- `Web.ByPassing.ends_within`: a pattern present from `T` on whose carriers carry it briefly has a carrier beginning within every `d` steps (`Web.begins_within`, in double-negation form), under the passing premise every beginning is a passing (`Web.ByPassing.begins_passed`), a step with no carrier is followed by none (`Web.ByPassing.empty_stays`), and where passing stops the pattern ends within `d` steps; `PatternRelay` attains each bound. The passing premise is supplied, as transmission is (§2.4).
- `run_agrees_every_start_iff`: hearing makes no form (`run_held`), agreement is kept exactly through the hearings closed for it (`kept_iff`), an order brings every start to agreement exactly where it gives every formation one origin, and any form in use can become the shared one (`run_shared_iff_in_use`). On a path whose ends each follow the least-indexed neighbour, the ends never agree (`Path.never_agree`); a law privileging neither forms nor formations never resolves a symmetric disagreement (`never_agrees_of_both`, `TwoFormations.three_agree`). An imposed code merges what codes told apart, and only a form kept outside brings it back (`outside_needed`); a reading written back into records rests at once and is barred only by records kept from before (`kept_bars`, `added_never_replaced`).
- `favouring_locks` with `uniform_locks`: where a selection does not lower a target's share under a rule that favours it (`Raises`) and the rule read at a reading step is a monotone function of the share (`ReadsShare`), with no rule turning against the reading the share falls at no step and the course is locked from the step after its first reading step; where the reading favours at every share, every course is locked from the step after its second, exactly (`Second.second_exact`). A perturbation keeping the rule's direction is absorbed at the next reading step (`perturbation_absorbed`), one reversing it and held past the threshold locks the other way (`Reversed.not_absorbed`), and monotone two-level laws keep the order of courses (`order_kept`). This mirrors `Law.perturbation_reabsorbed`: there the state is re-absorbed because the step does not read it, here the rule's value because the reading does not. That an actual rule reads the share it selects, monotonically, is the premise `ReadsShare`; it is where “the winner shapes the rule” of `Becoming.Capture` enters, as a premise, not a conclusion.

*Readings* [AI reading, D48, D56, D57]. Forming the next members is passing a pattern across brief carriers, and passing carries whatever it carries: patterns of capture pass as patterns of care do; nothing in the law says which are worth passing. The second-order failure the account names, corruption taking over courts, press, statistics and audit, is a loop whose rule-reading reaches the couplings that would carry the difference back, the routes of 明 themselves, and the third order, rewarding those who break the routes of correction, a selection favouring narrowing what couples in, so that the gap of §7.2 widens as capability grows. “Stable is not good” is the theory's own starting point. 名无固宜，约定俗成谓之宜 is the order of use breaking the symmetry, 荀子's 王者制名 a centre, 书同文 an imposed code whose merges stay unless a form is kept outside (“古文由此絕”), and the 說文's 止戈为武 a reading written back into its own evidence, barred only by the bone and bronze forms in which 止 is a foot. 韩非子's 二柄 (「今君人者释其刑德而使臣用之，则君反制于臣矣」) is a rule of selection read from the share it selects, 韩非's framing of it as control between opposed interests kept as a divergence; 管子's 据有余而制不足 is the proportional reading; 商君书's 壹赏、壹刑、壹教 is one reading favouring one way at every share. None of these says whether a shared, imposed or locked code or loop supports or damages 生生.

### 4.6 Responsive transformation

**In words.** Understanding, purposes and action are reorganized through changing relations and consequences (因应转化): activity alters the conditions of further activity, some effects become available to discernment and memory, and acquired understanding enters evaluation and changed practice. A warranted direction is not an assured outcome: a local mechanism can guarantee an effect within a range without guaranteeing that the range, its interpretation or the reasons for it persist through every later change.

The relations to examine are consequences becoming discernible, acquired grounds staying applicable, understanding changing operative purposes and practice, support continuing through a transition, and evaluation staying answerable to living consequences; each has its own standing, and together they are not a proved checklist. The direction is not the maximization of change, flexibility, information or compliance: constant revision can destroy the continuity learning needs, and persistence can sustain care or entrench a mistake. Revising an interpretation of the good gives no AI authority to exempt itself from warranted oversight; the bridge to corrigibility is the relational capacity for such transformation, including forming and revising the judgment about what should change.
