### 6.1 The generative space, with no premise

**In words.** Possibilities are what can form next from what exists. One course widens another when its levels admit every formation the other admits and, at some step, one more; a part is generative where the levels with it widen those without it, degenerative where the reverse. Widening is an order with no premise, and a part does one of four things: widen, narrow, keep or be mixed. The boundary is exact: a part closes something only by a net draw beyond the margin by which the levels without it meet a need, and opens something only by a net give. No significance, standpoint or boundary enters.

- `Web.Widens.trans` with `Web.Widens.not_reverse`: widening goes one way and is transitive, and the four cases exclude one another with no classical logic (`Web.Widens.not_keeps`, `Web.Widens.not_mixed`, `Web.Keeps.not_mixed`). That every part falls under one of the four is the classical step, not stated in Lean. Witnesses: a giver from beyond widens (`Inflow.widens`), the clearer narrows (`Clearing.narrows`), a conveyor is mixed (`Transfer.mixed`); mixed parts are not ordered by inclusion, and ordering them needs a measure, a further premise.
- `supported_iff_within_margin` with `Balances.withheld`: withhold a part with the rest of the exchange as it was; the levels without it are unique where they exist, and each level with what the part took equals the level without it with what the part gave, so a formation is supported exactly when the part's cumulative net draw stays within the margin (`supported_of_net_give`, `admits_of_net_draw`; finding the condition uses classical logic). A part giving back at least what it takes closes nothing; one giving back exactly what it takes changes nothing. Where requirements do not loosen, a formation gains support only through a rise given by someone supported (`gains_from_supported`), and where nothing beyond gives, the unsupported stays so (`unsupported_stays`, each premise needed by `Restoring`). Used in §6.4 (the boundary of not harm).
- `Flow.widens_iff_within_margin_at`: with balance only at the conditions each formation requires before the step, the levels with a part widen those without it exactly when its net draw stays within the margin for every formation they admit and, at some step, its net exchange brings within the margin one they do not; the boundaries of retaining, losing and adding what has significance hold alike, for any significance (`Flow.retains_iff_within_margin_at`, `Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`, `Flow.adds_iff_within_margin_at`).
- `Web.paths_comparison_widens`: on level paths an extension with every claim counted is exactly a widening (`Web.paths_extension_iff_widens`), so with “better” read as widening C holds for every reading of `living`. The proof is an unfolding: the normative content of C has moved into calling widening better, which this theorem does not show (§6.5, §8.5). Used in §6.5 and §7.5.
- `Web.includes_iff_within_every`: the comparison over the whole is the comparison within every region at once; a region's comparison takes the region as an input, the whole's takes no boundary. What holds within a region holds within every region inside it, and nothing lies beyond the whole (`Web.AdmitsWithin.narrow`). The clearer, active and supported throughout, closes nothing within itself and its source and closes the dweller beyond them (`Clearing.loses_beyond`, a witness for that “can” in one constructed world with the clearer stipulated active): “only non-degenerating dynamics continue indefinitely” fails for parts, while the whole has nowhere to push the loss.

*Represented boundaries.* A formation renews over a region where it is active and keeps every dependence on the region's conditions it takes part in: over a region covering everything it takes part in this is renewal at the step; over its required conditions it keeps its own support yet may undermine another; over a region omitting a required condition it can lose its own support (`Omitted`). The whole is never any center's model of it: no configuration of a frame whose claims are its own configurations realizes every pattern of claims about them (`Frame.self_incomplete`, §3.3).

*What widening counts.* `Web.Widens` compares which members of the given type of formations `F` the levels admit at the web's thresholds (§0, block A), so a course that brings about a kind of formation or a requirement outside `F` widens nothing on this measure, and 生生, read as creating the conditions and generators of further forming, can exceed what widening registers; widening over a world that grows by attachment (`Flow.GrownBy`, §2.5) is open (§8.1). *Regrouping* keeps the process and only merges claims: where members of a group hold alike in the configurations read, each content is recoverable and the extension comparison is unchanged; where requirements are decidable merging is lossless under every level test exactly when members have the same least requirement at every step, which characterizes admitted content, not the identity of lives; two feasible courses of one law of exchange show a grouped description making mixed gains and losses look alike, an omission of a description, not a refutation ([argument and counter-review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ETHICAL-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md#relational-comparison-content)). *The open-form result*: `Becoming.Handoff` connects the finite formation witness to the common definition of positive relevance (`Run.novel_by_handoff`, `Run.relevance_by_handoff_of_unrestricted`), with the significance condition an explicit hypothesis; a formal connection, not a new principle ([completion audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RISK-CONDITIONING-INTEGRATION.md#spine-bridge-completion)).

### 6.4 The continuation condition

**This section is conditional: the theory does not assert its condition.** It takes the condition as a hypothesis and states what follows and what does not.

**In words.** A significance says, for a standpoint, a step and a formation, whether from that standpoint the formation's continuation at that step has significance. The continuation condition says that nothing restricts it: from every standpoint the continuation of anything has significance at every step. It is boundary neutrality applied once, to the significance of continuation. It fixes scope, whose losses count; it gives significance, not a positive valence; and by itself it orders nothing.

**Instantiation, said once.** `Unrestricted sig` is `Neutral` applied to `sig`, and `Neutral rule` is `∀ i, rule i`: under the condition `sig` holds of every standpoint, step and formation. Every theorem below that takes the condition as a hypothesis (`livingSignificance_of_unrestricted`, `Valuation.premises_of_unrestricted`, `Web.retains_iff_includes` and the rest) is an instantiation of that universal statement; counting such theorems counts uses of it, not dependence on further content. A statement scan of the core on 19 October 2026 finds 30 to 33 theorems taking it as a hypothesis and 24 to 31 stating it in their conclusion (the equivalences that characterize it, the decomposition, the witnesses where it fails, `bounded_rival`), the numbers depending on whether hypotheses stated as section variables or under a `∀` are counted (on 17 October: 26 and 20 in the core, 7 in the bridge, which left with the F3 sweep; the [inventory of 11 October](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/PART-II-INVENTORY.md#31-用到公理的-22-条--the-22-that-use-the-axiom) found 22 of 157 cited theorems took it). Import order is not dependence: `Becoming.Significance` and `Becoming.Metaethics` are imported, through `Becoming.Interdependence`, by 48 other core modules, among them `Becoming.Loops`, `Becoming.Observation` and `Becoming.Onward`, which use no theorem taking the condition; the layers follow imports, not the line between any significance and the condition.

- `Valuation.premises_of_unrestricted`: under the condition, on any reading where a claim's significance is some formation's continuation, living significance holds at the level of claims, with self-significance everywhere, both neutralities and, wherever something lives, a seed, with no connection used; the stepwise, relation-level and whole-run readings are such readings (`Web.significant_of_unrestricted`, `Conditions.significant_of_unrestricted`). Positive relevance read from significance is the least relevance satisfying living significance, holding of exactly the realized living content (`relevance_least`), so every use of living significance in core and periphery is discharged (`livingSignificance_of_unrestricted`, `Extension.new_relevance`, `SecuredContent.contingent_newContent_of_unrestricted`, `securesNewContent_by_bound_of_unrestricted`); characterizations and countermodels are unaffected. Living significance is therefore a condition, not an axiom, and needs none of its own.
- `unrestricted_iff_livingSignificance`: on a frame whose claims are formations at steps, each realized somewhere, the condition is exactly living significance with every claim living and positive relevance read as significance from every standpoint; on the level paths of every web this holds outright (`Web.unrestricted_iff_paths_livingSignificance`). On level paths the condition, significance and C read one object, admission (`Web.paths_one_object`): the condition widens living significance's scope to anything and weakens “positive” to “has significance”. Used in §7.5.
- `Web.retains_iff_includes`: under the condition, retaining what has significance (`Web.Retains`) is inclusion and adding (`Web.Adds`) is exceeding, alike from every standpoint (`Web.adds_iff_exceeds`, `Web.retains_agree`); widening is retaining and adding, keeping the same is retaining both ways, mixed is adding both ways (`Web.widens_iff_retains_adds`, `Web.keeps_iff_retains`, `Web.mixed_iff_adds`). So the generative space read as significance loses nothing, and the condition adds no rule. The margin laws of §6.1 hold for any significance (`Flow.Balances.retains_iff_within_margin`, `Flow.Balances.loses_iff_beyond_margin`); the condition only makes them cover every formation.
- `Preemption.not_undermining_yet_loses`: what has not yet formed counts. A drawer taking a condition's unit before any dependence on it forms is living on the stepwise reading, yet its part narrows what can form, and under the condition it loses a continuation with significance. Undermining is a sign of loss where the levels without the part meet the dependences (`Web.UnderminingOn.not_retains_unrestricted`, through `Web.exceeded_of_below`), and renewal at a step is no sign of keeping (`Preemption.within_renewal_yet_loses`). Over the whole run, with only active formations taking from what they draw on, a part drawing only on conditions renewed over the run keeps what can form (`Flow.Balances.not_narrows_of_renewed`), and one giving no more than it takes where a dependence is consumed narrows (`Flow.Balances.narrows_of_consumed`); keeping is not widening, and a part can consume over the run and keep (`Untaken.consuming_keeps`). These run-class laws take no condition.
- `Clearing.clearer_loses_dweller`: under the condition the clearer's part loses the dweller from every standpoint, while the levels without it admit every formation and the clearer is supported throughout, so withholding the part ends nothing (`Clearing.admitted_all_without`; a witness in one constructed world). Without the condition, with each formation's significance stopping at its dependence, the centers disagree (`Clearing.centers_disagree`). Under the condition what retains in the whole retains within every region (`Web.Retains.within`), the fewer continuations have significance the more is retained (`Web.Retains.of_fewer`), and a bounded significance retains what is admitted within its region (`Web.retains_bounded_iff`). Used in §6.5.

**What the condition does not settle.** Under it significance is the same everywhere, so every narrowing loses something with significance, predation, apoptosis and ending a cancer alike; such parts are mixed (`Transfer.unordered`), and telling harm from what is not harm among them needs a measure. No ending is licensed: a path retaining what has significance along another ends none of it (`Web.Retains.not_added`), and the giver's part retains and adds (`Restoring.Inflow.retains_adds`). On level paths C holds because “better” is read as widening (§6.1), not because of the condition. A, C, L and the practical commitments stay disclosed conditions; the level at which formations are individuated (members or lineages) fixes what counts as a loss; which reading the `living` of living significance names is a choice (`Preemption` shows two differ); and the warrant of the condition is open (§8.5), with no route attempted that would make significance a function of the world's own data. A significance bounded by the self is a premise too: Ren wrote that the self “can not justify itself without some sort of "belief"”. `bounded_rival` proves only that a significance bounded by regions holds the rule within its region and is not the condition where something lies outside; that such a rival is consistent with every law of the dynamics holds meta-theoretically (§5).

### 6.5 Scope and order: the step of invariance, C and practical judgment

**In words.** The ethics rests on two premises with two roles. The continuation condition fixes scope: exactly decomposed, it is the self's presupposition (from a standpoint, the continuation of what lies within its boundary has significance) joined to the step of invariance (that presupposition does not change with how the self's boundary is drawn). That step is the condition's content beyond the self; it extends the scope to losses that return to no one, and it ranks nothing. C, the widening comparison, fixes order: calling widening better is the only premise that ranks courses. Courses are compared on possibilities; lifting (L) and the practical commitments (J1–J4) carry the comparison to responses and choice. Neither premise is asserted, and no ethical result follows from the condition alone.

- `unrestricted_iff_presupposed_along`: along any relation the whole is an admissible boundary of every self, and the condition is exactly the presupposition sub-true (holding as the boundary is drawn somewhere, `SubTrue`) and boundary-invariant (`BoundaryInvariant`) at every standpoint; generally where the whole is admissible (`unrestricted_iff_presupposed`), and from covering (`unrestricted_of_presupposed`). Super-truth (`SuperTrue`, after Fine 1975; sub-truth after Hyde 1997) is invariant sub-truth (`superTrue_iff`), and what lies within the self however its boundary is drawn is exactly the closure (`superTrue_member_iff`). Logically the decomposition is A ⇔ P ∧ (P → A), given A ⇒ P, with supervaluation bookkeeping: it locates the contested step and derives nothing beyond it. The step runs both ways (`not_subTrue_of_invariant`); where the whole is admissible it is the conditional from the presupposition to the condition (`boundaryInvariant_iff_conditional`); given the presupposition everywhere and every formation within some closure it is standpoint-invariance, the rule of standpoint neutrality (`boundaryInvariant_iff_standpointInvariant`; apart otherwise, `OneCounts.forms_apart`).
- `presupposition_scopes`: route (b) and the condition are one presupposition with “however the boundary is drawn” at narrow and wide scope. Sub-true along a relation, it is significance over the closure (`subTrue_significantWithin_iff`): along the empty relation a standpoint's own good, along the total relation the condition (`subTrue_empty_iff`, `subTrue_total_iff`); route (b)'s significance is the least making every presupposition sub-true (`bounded_closure_least`). Sub-true along affecting it compares every part within the reach closure as the condition does, using neither invariance nor super-truth (`Flow.Balances.presupposed_reach_comparison`; along sharing, the system, which is the stronger premise, `Flow.presupposition_order`). Where every formation lies within each closure, invariance is trivial and the step has moved into drawing the self's boundary closed under the relation (`unrestricted_iff_presupposed_of_whole`, `Flow.unrestricted_iff_presupposed_of_common`). Each premise is needed (`Clearing.without_presupposition`, `Clearing.without_invariance`, `Clearing.with_fixed_boundary`).
- `Ranking.possible_extends_iff`: C read on possibilities, including a formation wherever a course's levels admit it, is under the condition retaining and adding what has significance. It ranks the absence of an overdrawing formation above its occurrence, restraint above overdraw, also with a third formation, and sharing above overdraw by consumption (`Ranking.possible_ranks`, `Ranking.possible_restraint`, `Ranking.ThirdParty.possible_extends`, `Ranking.possible_contest`), withholding no event (`Ranking.malignant_possible`), and a course nowhere lower is never ranked below (`Web.not_added_of_le`). It leaves unranked a formation drawing within renewal, which adds no possibility (`Ranking.possible_tie`): the value of bringing new lives into existence, not treated. Possibilities first, then a reading, would rank it but also rank a pause that adds no possibility below uninterrupted activity (`Ranking.Pause.pause_ranked_below`), a further premise.
- `articulation_iff_lifted`: comparing responses with several outcomes needs a lifting rule, and under the conservative L (“every outcome is better than every outcome”) the comparison, the reason, the survival of coordinated choice and continual improvement having no survivor hold on every frame, the functional situation being exactly the deterministic case. L is substantive: a response with one good and one bad outcome is incomparable both ways, and transitivity needs a feasible intermediate response.
- `Direction.chosen_keeps_basic`: under J1/J2 (“basic conditions first, then living gain”, `Lexical.Frame.Above`; on a frame's steps `FrontierFrames.Priority`, the options being what one move reaches), a chosen move keeps the basic admissions some move keeps, and with options listed and comparisons decided the keeping is required (`FrontierFrames.Priority.kept_required`, `Direction.kept_required`); a choice at every step needs weak excluded middle (`FrontierFrames.choice_exists_iff_weakly`). On a world J3's joint plan at a step is the move, and a sole giver to what a formation requires is required and active (`FrontierWorlds.sole_giver_required`, `FrontierWorlds.sole_giver_active`, `FrontierWorlds.Discharged.no_residual`). Used in §7.5.

Under the condition, A read with bare significance gives a reason for any new content, living or not (`BareSignificance.bare_forces_reason`), so A must be read on living content (`articulates_total`), and then the universal reason follows with no seed, neutrality or connection premise (`universal_reason_of_unrestricted`); A remains a premise, itself a neutrality premise. What C ranks under a reading of `living` (`Becoming.Ranking`): a continuation without a formation extends the one with it only if the reading withholds every event of that formation; under the whole-run reading the absence of an overdrawing formation ranks higher, under the stepwise reading an event at whose step every need is met is included and C no longer decides (malignant growth and overdraw by consumption are instances; `Immediate` where support is overstepped at once); restraint ranks above overdraw under both readings (`Ranking.restraint_ranks`, `Ranking.malignant_restraint`) and can be lost with a third party (`ThirdParty`). FR, finite responsiveness, is an additional, revisable comparison commitment, neither theorem nor axiom, whose formal content is `Becoming.LossMatching`. Other named commitments of the practical line are stated in their chapters with their records, each additional and revisable: BC1, BC2, LC, LR and CP in [basic conflict judgment](BASIC-CONFLICT-JUDGMENT.md), NP in [choice and generative context](CHOICE-AND-GENERATIVE-CONTEXT.md).

**What it does not do.** It proves none of self-significance, relational neutrality, standpoint neutrality, A, C, L or the practical commitments, binds no position that rejects them, and does not treat the value of bringing new lives into existence. Normative warrant, recognition, motivation, compliance and actual preservation are different claims; so are direction, continuing capacity, per-step monotonic growth and scalar maximization. Covering, sub-truth and invariance are supplied as every significance is (§5); along a relation the presupposition already carries transmission along it, from the bare own good of the empty relation to the condition of the total one.
