## 2. Organization, renewal and evolution

### 2.1 Renewal and consumption belong to runs

**In words.** A formation renews where every condition it depends on or draws on keeps supporting every formation that depends on it, and consumes where it draws on a condition from which some dependent loses support for good. The two exclude each other without exhausting the cases, and the class belongs to the run, not to a configuration or to the formation's own course: persistence, success and growth do not certify renewal.

- `falls_not_recurring`: an amount counted in whole units that never rises from some step on cannot keep falling. It is the whole-number instance of the least-loss law (`Amounts.rests`, §1.3); over dense amounts a halving stock falls for ever (`Halving.approaches_without_reaching`). Depletion with no run, recurrent renewal by a rank, and drawing without renewal are its cases. Used in §4.3 (constraint 1).
- `Renewable.class_belongs_to_the_run`: in one process, from one start, the focal formation is active at every step; in one run it renews, in another it consumes the sustaining formation's condition and that formation ends. The earlier module outside the spine has the same point (`own_trajectory_does_not_decide`). Used in §4.3.
- *No finite history decides the class, in `Renewable`*: for every horizon two runs agree before it, and after it one renews and the other consumes, so a reading decided by finite prefixes that counts every renewing run counts a consuming one (`whole_run_includes_overdraw`); a reading by amount, taking no more than is renewed, is decided by finite histories and withholds every overdraw (`amount_reading`). This is shown for this process, not for every process; what finite stretches decide in every process is §3.2.
- `jointly_consuming`: two formations each within renewal together consume a shared condition and both end; the class belongs to their joint relation. Used in §4.1.

The stepwise reading [AI reading, D10] reads an event as within renewal where its step keeps every dependence it takes part in (`RenewingAt`), and reads `living` stepwise as "active and not undermining", undermining being drawing that leaves a condition below a need once met in the dependence's unbroken tenure (`MetAbove`). It differs from the whole-run reading both ways (an escalation, a brief dip), withholds drawing after a loss, and has a cost: `draws` carries no amount, so drawing exactly the renewed amount is withheld too; its witnesses are in [becoming-stepwise-reading](../results/records/becoming-stepwise-reading.md). Return grades renewal: kept, returning within a period, returning without bound, never returning; consumption is exactly some dependence never returning (`consumed_iff`, unfolding the definition). In a family of laws where renewal depends on the renewed condition, a loss beyond the threshold does not come back (`no_return_below_support`); a supported route of contribution can repair the relation (periphery); a consumer that substitutes continues by transforming its dependence and is still consuming, the sustaining formation ending (`stepwise_includes_substitution`); in one class of wear laws the two classes are dynamically distinguishable (periphery).

### 2.2 Continued generation, more, and the two roles of one relatum

**In words.** Unceasing arising needs renewal, and more without end needs replenishment with no eventual bound, given by formations within the unfolding. A formation is a condition of others and of itself, so generation is itself replenishment: an embodied relatum arises only as another gives to it. Under dissipation no finite part bears recurring uptake on its own.

- *Continued generation* (`Becoming.Arising`): a formation arises across a step when it is inactive before and active after it. In a balanced flow, positive uptake at arbitrarily late steps rules out replenishment being zero from some step on (`uptake_needs_replenishment`); where an amount never rises and every arising lowers it, generation is not unceasing (`dissipation_arising_not_recurring`), while a closed cycle with a constant total sustains it (`Recycle.closed_unceasing`). Unceasing arising is strictly stronger than recurring renewal: a tide the moon renews rises with nothing arising (`Tide.renewal_without_arising`).
- *More* (`Becoming.Flow`, `Becoming.Population`): read as "for every number, from some step on at least that many formations take", more rules out any eventual bound on replenishment (`More.replenishment_unbounded`) and on renewers where each gives a bounded amount (`More.renewers_unbounded`); the renewers may be absent at infinitely many steps (`Pulse.pulsed`). Under a census of active formations, more forces unceasing arising (`More.unceasing`); without the census, with repetition or with takers never active it does not (`Dormant`, `Repeated`, `Inactive`). The replenishment needed forms a strict hierarchy, and the renewers can be the takers themselves (`Cycle.closed_more`).
- *No finite part bears taking* (`Dissipative.opens`): under dissipation a finite family on which taking recurs is not closed from any step on, and enclosed around it draws in (`taking_draws_in`); with finitely many conditions in all, taking does not recur. In one exchange a rise comes with a fall at the same step (without dissipation, `Brood.rise_without_fall`). Where no step is closed dissipation is vacuous and a renewer gives while taking nothing (`Spray`); the opening exchange may be unrelated to the family (`SprayShuttle`). Used in §2.3 and §4.3.
- *Arising is given* (`Embodied.arises_given`): in a balanced flow where only active formations give, an embodied relatum arises only as an active formation gives to it, possibly a source depending on nothing (`SourceSeed`, `EmbodiedSource`); so generation is replenishment, and where embodied generation recurs in a finite family the family is not closed from any step on (`embodiedArising_opens`, `embodiedArising_draws_in`, `embodiedArising_not_recurring`). Without dissipation (`Shuttle`) or embodiment (`Flicker`) these fail; burning fresh fuel meets every premise (`Throughput`). Which condition is which formation is supplied by the shared index (`relabel`).

The requirement follows the rise of level, not activity: a seed can be given its level while dormant and arise later with nothing given (`Provisioned`), and waking is not coming to stand (`Sleeper`); a reserve defers the end without escaping it (`Becoming.Reserve`). A bee and a flower each depending on itself and the other are connected with no third condition, while where each depends only on the other neither valuation relates them (`Mutualism.apart_embodied`). Drawing on an embodied relatum at the step it ends is breaking, whatever ends it (`Embodied.ending_breaks`, `Predation`, `Wilting`): a verdict of the stepwise reading, not a valuation of the drawer, since comparison is by possibilities [AI reading, D6].

*Continuity across dormancy and identity.* Given a reading of holding, `Web.persisting` reads activity by it, and the class of a formation that holds throughout does not change (`Web.steady_held`, `Web.renewingIn_held`, `Web.consumingIn_held`); succession at a step where nothing holds is not constrained (`Dormancy.held_both`), and both ends of a census must read holding alike (`Web.persisting_census`). A re-identification keeps balance exactly where what it continues lies within the conservation relation (`World.balanced_reidentify_iff`), and with equality decided `World.fixed_iff`, `World.arises_fixed_iff` and `World.ceases_fixed_iff` give when identity, arising and ceasing are fixed under every naming keeping balance; balance can admit different arisings at once (`Lineage.world_readings`, `Hibernation.world_readings`). Not proved: classes under splits or merges, and sufficient conditions for a mixed census.

*Readings* [AI reading, D3, D4, D6]. The second 生 of “不断创造更多生” (“unceasingly creating more 生”, Ren's words) is read with the scope of what can form; only its “unceasing” is stated here. Embodied relata are read as 生, so generation giving rise to generation is one formal content of the relation between the two 生. More uptake needs replenishment given within the unfolding, borne by an unfolding that goes beyond every finite part; sustaining a single taker already is (`Lone`). Rises in possibility, diversity and complexity are other readings of “more” and are stated through widening (§6.1). The reading of holding and conservation as continuation are the AI's.

### 2.3 Joint excess, taking all, and what is fed from beyond

**In words.** What a family takes without end it is fed from beyond; draws each within what a step gives can together exceed it, and a joint excess that brings a level below the renewers' need ends them and everything depending on the condition at that need. Taking all that is given excludes every other taker, and a take that rises at every step exhausts what it takes from and ends.

- `Flow.fed_from_beyond`: under balance and dissipation a finite family, listed once, from which taking recurs and which is enclosed around from some step on, cannot from any step on be fed from nothing beyond it. It needs natural-number amounts; with real amounts losses can shrink geometrically and it fails. What is needed is being fed, not the recurrence of a source (`Lone.fed_not_recurring`). Used in §4.3 (a collective fed from beyond depends on what lies beyond).
- `Flow.excess_bounded`, `Flow.joint_excess_ends`: each step of joint excess lowers the level by at least one unit, so it cannot outlast what was held; where renewal is given only by formations depending on what they renew, a joint excess bringing the level below their need ends them and every formation depending at such a need (`Exceeding.all_end`; with one taker all continue, `Alone.all_continue`). Own draws within what is given can jointly exceed it (`Pair.own_not_joint`, `Exceeding.own_not_joint`). Used in §4.3 (relative position does not protect).
- `Flow.continuing_excludes`: a full taker (`Flow.TakesAll`) that continues leaves no other formation taking from the condition at each of `level + 1` steps; full taking alongside another taker ends every formation depending at the matching need, the full taker included (`Flow.full_taking_ends`), and a taker active only while it takes ends, the ending derived (`Flow.continuing_ends_taker`). Of `Exceeding`'s three courses, each taking one keeps all three supported: the first's continuation does not need the second's ending, taking all does (`Excluding.three_courses`), and the courses are alike by admission (`Excluding.admission_alike`), so the loss is the member's continuation [AI reading, D44].
- `Flow.seeking_ends`: a take more than now (`MoreThanNow`, §3.4) rises by at least `n` in `n` steps (`more_accumulates`), so from a condition given at most `B` a step it takes all that is given after `B + 1` steps, and under balance a formation that takes so while active is not active through a bounded window; where its activity does not resume, it ends, derived from balance and the bound on what is given alone. Where renewal depends on what it renews, the seeker exhausts the condition for every formation depending at the matching need, renewers included (`Flow.seeking_exhausts`). In `Alone` a taker taking all without its take rising continues (`Alone.takes_all`): what ends the seeker is the rising take. Used in §3.4.

What the derived extent does not reach. It runs one way, to what one's own continuation depends on: where one stops giving, a formation living on that giving ends while one continues (`Downstream.one_way`); it ends with the want (`Mortal.after_its_end`); and a clearer meeting every requirement derived here, active throughout, renewed, fed from beyond and drawing jointly within what is given, still exhausts the dweller's habitat (`Clearing.undermines_within_renewal`). The witnesses' endings are stipulated in their activity and shown consistent with balance, support and dependence; the general theorems derive endings.

### 2.4 Evolution and transmission

**In words.** Evolution names specific mechanisms (transmission, reconstruction, variation, selection), each explained through its own relations; it is not a synonym for every change. On the spine, descent is defined by taking part, giving and coming to stand as a condition, with no valuation.

- `price`: Price's equation in counting form, an arithmetic identity for any finite family of parents and offspring; the weighted total is not the selection term proper (`selected_uniform`).
- `unsupported_childless`: where only active formations give and activity needs support, an unsupported formation leaves no descendants, and the parents' weighted need is bounded by the level (`selected_le`).
- `transmitted_supported`: where descendants take up their parents' dependences (`TransmitsWithin`, a premise), a transmitted need exceeds the level at the giving step by at most the bound of transmission (`Slack`); in `Drawdown` transmission, variation, selection and the feedback of activity on selection lie in one unfolding. Used in §3.4 (`share_rises`) and §4.5 (passing).

Transmission is inheritance and need, and is independent where it binds (`Becoming.Transmission`). Fitness is not defined; reproductive rates, mutation, drift and population dynamics are not covered. The periphery mechanisms (inherited structural copying, formed copying and its correction, growth-constrained fragmentation, inherited organizational rules; standard Lean axioms) keep their own scope, and no correspondence between them and this content is proved; the growth case is restated on the base in `Becoming.Cuts` (§2.5). Prepared material joins recurrent chain formation (periphery): a copy of a word produces the word exactly when the pool supplies it and the support covers what it draws, and where every unit draws d from one stock and becomes two, n rounds from k units run exactly when the stock covers d·k(2^n − 1) (`Copying.built_iff`, `RecurrentAssembly.supported_rounds`). See [the theory of evolution](THE-THEORY-OF-EVOLUTION.md) and [evolutionary organization](../history/EVOLUTIONARY-ORGANIZATION.md).

### 2.5 Closures: feeding, influence, reach and system

**In words.** A formation's continuation is fed by those who give to what it requires, affected by those who exchange with what it depends on, and reaches those whose conditions its own exchange touches. The closures under these relations are nested: feeding within influence, influence and reach within the system. Feedback carries at most the influence closure, a part's exchange reaches into the reach closure, and the comparison with and without a part is decided within what the part reaches.

- `Flow.beyond_gives_nothing`: a formation's feeding closure (`Flow.WithinFeeding`) is the least region closed under feeding that holds it, and nothing beyond it gives to what a member requires. Under balance, a formation taking at every active step from a condition nothing supported gives to is active for at most the condition's starting level of consecutive steps: consumption ends with its feeders (`Flow.Balances.consumption_ends_with_feeders`). In `Overshoot` an ending returns through a feeder (`Overshoot.ends_through_feeder`), while in `Clearing` the dweller lies beyond the clearer's closure and ends while everything within it continues (`Clearing.dweller_beyond`). Used in §5 (each formation's own region along feeding).
- `Flow.withinSystem_of_withinInfluence` with `Flow.withinInfluence_of_withinFeeding`: the five levels (own events, feeding, influence, reach, system) are closures under one seed–edge construction (`withinClosure_iff_closure`), each inclusion can be strict, and influence and reach point in opposite directions (`Clearing.levels`; in `Trophic`, wolves lie beyond a forest's feeding closure and within its influence closure, `Trophic.mediates`). One common condition makes every system the whole (`Flow.system_whole_of_common`), and one condition every formation requires and gives to makes every feeding closure the whole and bounded significance the condition (`Flow.feeding_unrestricted_of_common`). Used in §4.2 and §6.3.
- `Flow.Balances.uncoupled_beyond_influence`: what feedback carries. Nothing beyond the influence closure exchanges with what a member depends on (`Flow.beyond_influence`), so where a part lies beyond it, support within it is as without the part; in `Clearing` the clearer's support is the same whether or not it clears, while the dweller ends (`Clearing.uncoupled`). Levels follow their exchanges (`Flow.Balances.levels_agree`, `Flow.Balances.agrees_iff_even`). Used in §7.3.
- `Flow.Balances.comparison_within_reach`: for any significance covering what the part has reached before each step, the levels with and without the part retain and add exactly as they include and exceed each other, so significance bounded by a region closed under affecting compares every part within it as inclusion does (`Flow.Balances.closed_comparison`); for any two flows starting alike the comparison is decided within what their difference reaches (`Flow.Balances.comparison_within_difference`). Covering is enough and not needed (`Margin.reach_without_misjudgment`); a part with a member beyond a system can be misjudged (`Trophic.mixed_part`); with the rest fixed, effects through others' responses do not show (`Trophic.mediated_uncoupled`, `Trophic.responsive_mixed`); where a part gives what others take there may be no levels without it (`Clearing.no_levels_without_source`). Used in §6.3.
- `Cuts.closure_unchanged_iff_bypassed`: removing links leaves every closure as it was exactly where each removed link is bypassed; in a relation grown by attachment every link separates its ends, and a join frees exactly what it crosses (`Cuts.joined_cut_unchanged_iff`); on the base, each condition of a world grown by attachment is needed (`Flow.GrownBy.condition_needed`). `GrownFragmentation`'s certified cuts are periphery instances.

Earlier giving reaches a later need through a condition that stands throughout the stretch: the closure across steps contains the closure at one step, storage can make it strict (`Provision.through_store`), the exact condition for meeting a need under local balance is `m + taken ≤ level + given`, and withholding both of two redundant feeders can leave no balancing path (`Redundant.withheld_both`); carrying characterizes a possible retention, not the actual source. In `Clearing` the region along dependence is exactly each formation's feeding closure (`Clearing.dependenceRegion_iff`). Not derived: actual retention, a stricter notion of carrying, how a center represents the closure, and whether a formation knows beyond its feedback. A part is a decided family of formations, so one act is compared through a second flow (§2.6).

### 2.6 Response, propagation and loops

**In words.** Where the rest responds, a difference goes where couplings carry it; given the rest's law, the counterfactual of an act is the course the same law generates. How much difference arrives is itself a course, the carried amount, attained by a law and so the most that can arrive. It expands by orders of responses, a sum over routes back in time; a finite order is exact exactly where no loop of the couplings that go on lies upstream, and the most difference that can arrive grows faster than every polynomial only through such a loop, which forms by closing and need not run away.

- `counterfactual_is_course`: with the rest following one law of what has stood, two balanced flows starting alike with the part acting alike have the same course (`counterfactual_fixed`), so the counterfactual is the course the law generates, and a determined law steps beyond a run's history exactly at the moves it never takes (`ExchangeLaw.counterfactual_beyond_shown`). Where the law reads through what couples into each formation (`ExchangeLaw.Through`), the rest responds through its couplings (`ExchangeLaw.Through.responds`), so the comparison is decided within the responsive reach (`Flow.Balances.comparison_within_response`), the dual of the coupling closure (`Flow.withinResponse_iff_coupling`), and sensitivity is the least coupling where the law reads finitely many conditions (`Becoming.Sensitivity`). Used in §3.1 and §7.2.
- `most_difference_arrives`: a coupling strength is the measure sensitivity lacked (`ExchangeLaw.RespondsWithin`; a square law has none, `Propagation.Square.not_respondsWithin`); every gap is at most the carried amount (`gap_le_carried`), which the carrying law attains (`carried_attained`), positive exactly along a route in time of positive strengths (`carried_pos_iff`). A held difference keeps arriving without a loop (`Propagation.Chain.keeps_arriving`). Used in §4.2.
- `finite_order_iff_no_loop` with `carried_eq_carriedWithin_add`: order j is exact up to step j and where no loop lies upstream (`carriedWithin_exact_of_no_loop`); a cut at order j loses exactly what j + 1 responses carry from the carried amount, for every schedule (`Becoming.Remainder`), and the orders are sums over routes back in time, a cut being a truncation (`routeSum_eq_carriedWithin`, `Becoming.Routes`); with the same strengths at every step the carried amount is polynomial exactly where no loop lies upstream (`polynomial_iff_no_loop`), and the self-loop attains every bound (`Loops.SelfLoop.attained`). Used in §7.2 (law 8).
- `forms_by_closing` and `break_carried_nowhere`: a loop forms at the step a coupling closes a chain (`onLoop_succ_iff`), not foreseen from the amounts before it (`Closing.Onset`); loops lie within what a law reads (`not_onLoop_unread`), and for every law reading a stock only below a threshold, raising the stock changes nothing up to the break, where the loop through it forms (`ExchangeLaw.ReadsBelow.standing_no_loop`, `Thresholds.Draw`). A difference reaches a formation only along routes of reading from where it lies (`apart_only_downstream`), and observing is the end of such a route, formed by closing (`route_forms_by_closing`, `Measure.loop_at`). Used in §3.4 (the break of a runaway) and §7.3.
- `runaway_of_roundTrip` with `Frequency.Sparse.polynomial`: every return at least doubles the carried amount (`carried_doubles_roundTrip`), frequent returns make it outgrow every polynomial, and a self-loop coupling only at sparse steps stays polynomial, so a loop of recurring couplings need not run away; the rate counts the steps at which couplings act (`carried_le_count`), and that every schedule settles is exactly the dichotomy `RecursOrStops` (`settled_iff_recursOrStops`), which gives LPO (§8.6). Used in §4.3 (constraint 6) and §7.2.

The rest of the line, each with its record. *A law of the whole and no part* (`Becoming.Tail`): a counterexample to the least coupling exists exactly where a rule decides of every Boolean sequence whether it is false throughout, the weak omniscience rule, and the tail law takes the LPO rule (`not_through_sensitive_iff_rule`, `vanishDecision_iff_lpoRule`). *History sums* (`Becoming.HistorySums`): support, splitting, exact orders and the steps between; an exact order needs the alternatives to commute (`HistorySums.grouping_needs_commuting`). *Coupling by level* (`Becoming.LevelCoupling`): up to double negation, `obs` is exactly where some law reading through the couplings couples a level into a formation within its need (`not_not_obs_iff_respondsByLevel`), while through a threshold only a crossing passes (`ExchangeLaw.Through.not_respondsByLevel`, `crossing_passes_iff`); a crossing where admission is not decided is not shown. *A move formed within the unfolding* (`Becoming.MoveFormation`): a move is the part's own exactly where it is formed through the part's configuration.

Not derived: the strengths themselves and a least strength; real amounts and strengths below one (damping); continuous time; laws reading infinitely many conditions at a step; which of the bound by returns and the bound by coupling steps is met beyond the self-loop; the spectral radius of given strengths; that formations respond only through their couplings; and, beyond the coupling sense, that observation is no second relation (the conjecture of [observation-is-a-route](../results/records/observation-is-a-route.md), §8.1).
