### 8.5 Philosophical warrant

- **The deductive necessity of the whole**, with no added substantive premise: that its determinations are internally related moments of one unfolding, not a collection of instances ([foundation §9](BECOMING-FOUNDATION.md#9-the-content-of-the-whole-deduction)).
- **A stronger universal practical warrant**: grounding reasons without an externally supplied end.
- **The necessary relational foundation of scientific inquiry**: the core's indispensability from independently stated achievements.
- **The grounds of living significance, A and the neutralities**, none independently reviewed. On held lives what living significance adds beyond self-significance lies in standpoint neutrality, on a connected whole where every standpoint holds a living claim in relational neutrality instead; under the condition living significance is an instantiation, so its ground is the condition's; route (b) claims no warrant.
- **What the support for living significance contributes.** Four kinds of support count comparatively, none as proof: explanatory unification (care, repair, creation, learning, renewal, rest, finite fulfilment and constructive ending from one ground), discriminating content (renewing and consuming formation separated by descriptive content where a one-step count ranks them the other way), discrimination against rivals, and fruitfulness for alignment. The formal checks contribute inference, premise footprint, dynamical distinctions and independence; none verifies the starting commitment, and the dynamical theorems are sign-neutral ([history](../history/G0-WARRANT.md#2-支持的四种方式--four-kinds-of-support)).
- **Calling the generative space good**: is widening better (the warrant of C, which orders; C's no-loss extension is widening by definition, so the definition carries no warrant), and does significance stop at no boundary (the warrant of the step of invariance, which fixes scope and orders nothing)?
- **The scope of representation.** Can every becoming be represented with discrete steps and natural-number amounts? The shared index is read off own courses for local exchanges (§1.4), so what remains is whether every becoming has such a local law with finite courses; where it cannot be so represented, the spine is extended (items 1 and 6).

### 8.6 The logical commitment

The core assumes no principle of classical logic (§0, block A). Where a law needs one, the principle is its own hypothesis, or the law is proved equivalent to it; each calibration below is a result only where it says something about the unfolding, and otherwise a lemma of the law that needs it.

- **Decidedness is local** (`Becoming.Decidedness`): a count exists exactly where what it counts is decided (`counts_iff_decided`). The counting laws (`World.enclosure_bounds_change`, `World.counting_under`) take decided activity for every relatum; over counted censuses (`Becoming.CensusCounts`, `Becoming.CensusBinding`) the laws under the premises take none. The [decidability inventory](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DECIDABILITY-INVENTORY.md) lists every theorem still conditioned on it.
- **Excluded middle**, asked of every case, is exactly each of: the finite-world law that arising recurs exactly where ceasing does, without decidability (`World.law_iff_em`, `World.law_decides`; with EM as hypothesis, `World.law_of_em`, `Carries.chain_ends_of_em`), a lemma of Prop-valued activity, the witness `World.undecided` putting an arbitrary proposition at one step; a fixed number of relata up to indiscernibility (`World.numbers_indiscernible_iff_em`); deciding likeness of finite frames (`alike_decided_iff_em`); branching tests fixing likeness without decided steps (`alike_of_sameBranchingTests_iff_em`); matching parts by joint tests without stability (`matchesParts_of_compact_iff_em`); every recipe or program as one test (`Recipe.holds_after_iff_em`, `diamond_correct_iff_em`); the robust comparison under every classification (`classification_robust_iff_iff_em`); forming and holding making inheritance (`split_iff_em`); every undominated member having a property wherever each lacking it is bettered (`undominated_has_iff_em`); the disjunctive remainder's density (`orNotAdheres_dense_iff_em`).
- **Weak excluded middle** is an undominated member of every nonempty listed menu under every irreflexive transitive relation (`exists_undominated_iff_weakly`), on frames the choice at every step (`FrontierFrames.choice_exists_iff_weakly`): where whether one move betters another is open, a step need leave no move known to be chosen. It is the one classical step of the practical commitments as first proved (`finite_frontier`).
- **Double-negation elimination**, asked of every process, is the origin law with a proposition as the gate (`Gated.origin_of_propositions_iff`), a refutable reading holding where adhered to (`refutable_holds_iff`), and passing either way between refutable and closed classes (`Stretches.refutable_closed_iff`, `Stretches.closed_refutable_iff`; for every order, `Ideals.refutable_closed_iff`, `Ideals.closed_refutable_iff`); and the step from verifiable to refutable complement (`Ideals.verifiable_compl_refutable_iff`).
- **LPO** is given by a never-supported formation with a carried reserve having a last active step (`Web.lpo_of_ending`), a list of every member that ever holds (`Listed.lpo_of_few`), naming the step from which a finite world rests (`World.lpo_of_finite_ends`), finding the juncture of every loss (`lpo_of_junctures_found`), every run of every finite-world process settling (`LastChange.lpo_iff_settles`, `Settling.OneWay.lpo_of_settles`), locating where a run stopped (`Limit.Sequences.eventuallyFalse_lpo`), and reaching within one step in the search witness (`Lasting.Search.reaches_iff_lpo`); it is exactly telling whether a history ends or goes on (`Stretches.cut_decided_iff_lpo`, `HistoryIdeals.cut_decided_iff_lpo`): telling whether a history ends is deciding whether it changes again. So the core states the weaker forms, “not active at arbitrarily late steps” and rest that cannot be refuted (`World.rests_unrefuted`, `World.comes_to_rest_unrefuted`, `World.rests_unrefuted_enclosed`, `Flow.change_stops_unrefuted`, `Amounts.rests_unrefuted`). At or above it: deciding vanishing gives LPO (`lpo_of_vanish_decided`) and as a rule is exactly the LPO rule (`vanishDecision_iff_lpoRule`, `LPO.of_rule`); every schedule settling is exactly `RecursOrStops` (`settled_iff_recursOrStops`), which gives LPO (`RecursOrStops.lpo`, `lpo_of_settled`) and is excluded middle at level Σ⁰₂ (Akama, Berardi, Hayashi and Kohlenbach 2004), strictly stronger.
- **WLPO** (`WLPO`, `WLPO.of_lpo`) is given by a law of what has stood differing under histories agreeing on all it is sensitive to, with numbered conditions (`wlpo_of_not_through_sensitive`), and by a law of the whole and no part (`wlpo_of_insensitive_differs`); as a rule, a counterexample exists exactly where a rule decides whether a sequence is false throughout (`not_through_sensitive_iff_rule`, `changedByNoCondition_iff_rule`, `WLPO.of_rule`). So the core states the least coupling only for laws reading finitely many conditions under each history.
- **Markov's principle** (`Markov`): asked of every process, the reading of tests is refutable exactly under it (`refutable_passes_iff_markov`); on runs, from verifiable to refutable complement (`verifiable_compl_refutable_markov`), and no method refutes settling in the limit under it (§3.2).

### 8.7 Empirical correspondence

### 8.9 Objections not yet answered

Each stands against the account as stated, with what would answer it.

- **Agent-relative reasons and partiality.** The step of invariance asks a self to count anything's continuation as it counts its own; Williams argued that agent-relative reasons and partial attachments are not defects awaiting universalization (“one thought too many”). §6.5 locates this at the step and does not answer it. *Would answer it:* an argument that the step holds, or agent-relative weights beside the condition and C with theorems saying where partiality may decide.
- **Why “anything” has significance**, a hurricane or a tumour included. §6.4 shows this ranks nothing, not why significance attaches at all; the scope comes from the condition's wording. *Would answer it:* a reason for the scope not resting on the wording, or a narrower scope (what wants its continuation, §6.2) argued with its consequences checked.
- **The externalism of A.** Internalists deny a reason where no motive or sound deliberation connects to it. *Would answer it:* an argument for A against internalism, or the account restated internally with the theorems that survive.
- **Graded status, priority and constraints are not modelled.** Significance is two-valued; graded moral status, prioritarian weighting and deontic constraints have no representation, and “every rival is the same rule inside a boundary” is a framing of countermodels (§6.2). *Would answer it:* such representations on the spine, with theorems saying where each agrees with C.
- **Which function is learned.** The laws of §7.1–§7.4 hold for every selection through the couplings and say nothing about which function training installs, inductive bias, goal misgeneralization or deception. *Would answer it:* results on which law a trained system's acts come to follow, joined to the learning laws, and evidence for identified installations.
- **The internal-relations thesis is open** (item 1). *Would answer it:* the passage the first target describes.
- **Every review so far is by AI.** *Would answer it:* independent human review of the Lean statements against the prose and of the philosophical arguments, beginning with §6 and §7.
