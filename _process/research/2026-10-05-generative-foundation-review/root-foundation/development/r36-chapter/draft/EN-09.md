## 9. The formal spine

All new formal results are stated on one spine; where it cannot express a result it is extended rather than another model built, and existing modules connect by instance or equivalence theorems. Since the F3 sweep (19 October 2026) the core states the content of every periphery module that was in the checker's run, with the excluded middle, choice, extensionality, stability or sections their proofs used taken as hypotheses (72 theorems). One package and one checker are not yet one web of dependence: the [theorem-connectivity measure](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/THEOREM-CONNECTIVITY.md#11-r32-the-measure-stops-rewarding-restatement), rerun on each landing, finds on R34's tree 476 of the 11,401 spine theorems apart from both base families; each of the 340 roots among them stands apart for a stated reason (one constructed model 205, a support lemma 80, a general structure 34, a restatement 9, logical strength alone 10, a calibration 2), and 176 statements reach the base only through a law of their own module. Since R32 the measure counts no named one-line restatement as a connection; the earlier reruns are logged in [its §10](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/THEOREM-CONNECTIVITY.md#10-reruns-on-each-landing-17-19-october-2026).

**Rings.** The checker's lists fix them, and the [package README](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/README.md) describes them.

**Three layers on one base.** `import Becoming` brings in the whole core. The import tree starts at the foundation and `Becoming.Prelude`, which imports only `Init` and states once each lemma on numbers, truth values, lists and options; `Becoming.World` (the records of the unfolding with no premise among them) and `Becoming.Laws` (the notions every law uses, stated once) import the frame; modules stating laws that need no record of the unfolding import nothing or the prelude only. The layers are computed from each module's source and imports by [`layers.py`](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/r31-support/layers.py): L0, the base (`Becoming.World`, `Becoming.Frame`, `Becoming.Prelude`), 3 modules; L2, the 16 modules whose source names the condition or a valuation (`Unrestricted`, `Valuation`), from `Becoming.Metaethics` and `Becoming.Significance` on; L1, the other 258. No module imports one of a higher layer, which the script checks; fifteen core modules reach `Significance` or `Metaethics` through imports (138 before R31, [study §222](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#222-layers-and-one-prelude-r31); [§223](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#223-forwarders-copies-and-reading-modules-r32); [§225](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#225-one-evaluative-chain-r34)).
