## 7. AI alignment

This section states the alignment problem from laws the theory proves and organizes its answer around 明. §7.1–§7.4 use neither the continuation condition nor any practical premise: they say what a selection reaches and cannot reach, whatever is valued. §7.5 is conditional. §7.6 keeps the conditions under which a requested change can reach a system and be carried out, and §7.7 says what is open. The organization around 明 and pooled coupling is the AI's reading ([source](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-18-the-light-and-alignment-for-all.md)). A person, an AI system, a firm and a training process are formations within one unfolding (§1); each couples into the conditions it depends on by threshold and into those it observes by level (§3.1). Alignment is therefore not first a relation between separate owners of value, nor a bargain among opposed interests: it concerns what each formation's selections reach within the one relation, and how far that reach can widen.

### 7.1 What a selection reaches

**In words.** A selection is any claim about level histories (which are kept, rewarded, reproduced or trained toward), and it goes through a formation's couplings when histories alike through them are selected alike. It then reaches exactly what couples into the selector, and optimizing harder through the same couplings reaches no further; what a selection can separate grows only with what couples in.

- `Web.selects_alike_iff` (§3.4): by construction, from the definition `Web.SelectsThrough`; the substantive premise is that a given optimizer's selections go through just those couplings, which is supplied. Through a group's couplings together, histories are selected alike exactly when alike through every member's (`Web.selects_alike_all_iff`).
- `Training.fit_right_iff` (§3.3): training is selection through the data, right beyond it exactly where the class it can reach is determined there; a selection through a model is right about what matters only where what matters goes alike across the model's lumping (`Learning.selects_right_through`), and a model lumping what matters apart is right through no selection (`Learning.selects_not_right_of_parted`).
- `apart_only_downstream` (§2.6): under every law reading a schedule, two courses apart at the start in one condition stand alike at every condition not downstream of it, and nothing beyond a formation's influence closure exchanges with what a member depends on (`Flow.beyond_influence`). A selection through couplings stays one under more observation (`Web.SelectsThrough.widen`), and an observed stock's every raise couples in (`Web.observed_raise_couples`).

*Reading* [AI reading, D25]. For AI training, the reward process is the selector, and training reaches only what the reward couples into.

### 7.2 What a selection cannot reach: 无明 in alignment

**In words.** Eight laws, each stated in an earlier section, say what a selection through a formation's own couplings cannot reach: what lies beyond its coupling closure, its own act's difference there, where what it raises is drawn from, how far off its break is, what a finite history leaves open, what its untaken acts would have done, whether its target can rest, and what loops do to its consequences. Each law holds under the conditions it names; none uses the condition of §6.4.

1. **Beyond the coupling closure** (`Flow.unselected_beyond_coupling`, §3.4). Pooling members of the closure changes nothing; the gap is the reach closure less the coupling closure, and in `Clearing` the dweller is ended in only one of two worlds that every selection through the clearer's couplings selects alike.
2. **One's own act** (`Flow.act_unselected`, `Flow.consequence_unselected`). An act whose difference stays beyond the actor's coupling closure is selected alike by every member of the closure, the actor included, and its consequences are a selection through no member; the rest's response is derived from its law, and where both acts are laws nothing is supplied (`act_courses_unselected`).
3. **Raising what couples in** (`Flow.favours_reaching_beyond`, §3.4). A rise of what a group couples into is paid from beyond it; whether what is taken is depended on is open (§8.1).
4. **The distance to the break** (`Web.break_not_bounded`, §3.4): nothing a formation couples into bounds how far off its break is, and a policy through its couplings uses an unobserved larger stock only after its support has failed.
5. **Exclusion** (`Web.Excludes.not_of_alike`, `Web.tail_unexcluded`, `Web.excludesWithin_iff_conjunction`, §3.1).
6. **What an act would have done** (`Run.not_fixes_of_untaken`, `keepHistory_fixes_nothing_else`, `counterfactual_is_course`, §3.1, §2.6): a law fixes it, the record of one run does not.
7. **Targets that cannot rest, and capture** (`more_needs_open`, `Flow.more_exhausted`, `Law.aboveAll_never_rests`, `share_rises`, `Law.perturbation_reabsorbed`, §3.4).
8. **Loops** (`carriedWithin_exact_of_no_loop`, `carriedWithin_lt_of_loop`, `carried_doubles_roundTrip`, `runaway_of_roundTrip`, `Frequency.Sparse.polynomial`, §2.6): where an act's difference reaches a loop coupled at every step, no finite order of its consequences is exact, and returns frequent enough make the most difference that can arrive outgrow every polynomial.

*Readings* [AI reading, D20, D25, D45]. The position is not a defect; 无明 is the seeking it leaves locked. Every formation, human or AI, selects through its own couplings; these laws say what that position does not see: what lies beyond its coupling closure, its own act's difference there, where what it raises is drawn from, how far off its break is, what a finite history leaves open, what its untaken acts would have done, and whether its target can rest. Runs that keep changing are a feature of the process, not 无明 (D41). Read capability as reach: capability that widens what a formation reaches without widening what it couples into widens the gap, and optimizing harder through the same couplings does not close it.

### 7.3 明: coupling that reaches what support does not mark

**In words.** Losses reach a seeker only through what it couples into, so the losses locked seeking makes beyond that reach it through measurement, a model, or others. Each route has its law and its limit, and each joins to a selection a coupling that reaches where the selector's own does not.

- **Measurement.** Where a formation observes a stock, every raise of it makes a difference through its couplings at every step (`Web.observed_raise_couples`); a route of readings forms by closing (`route_forms_by_closing`), and in `Observation.Measure` the loop stock → record → draw forms at the step the formation begins to record (`Measure.loop_at`, `Measure.couples_after`). *Limit:* a difference reaches a measurement only along routes of reading from where it lies (`apart_only_downstream`).
- **A model.** Relative to a model of the exchange, a formation observing a stock excludes at the first step every false claim that it comes to nothing (`Flow.excludes_tail_within`), and with the law given, what one's own act would have done is the course the law generates. *Limit:* exclusion relative to a model excludes model and claim together, so a wrong model can exclude what holds (`Stationary.model_error`), and a selection through a model is right only where what matters goes alike across its lumping.
- **Others: pooled coupling.** A selection through a group's pooled coupling (`Web.SelectsThroughAll`) separates exactly what some member couples apart (`Web.separates_all_iff`; with members listed and couplings decided, `Web.separates_all_iff_member`); every member's selection is pooled and pooled selections combine (`Web.SelectsThrough.all`, `Web.SelectsThroughAll.and`). In `Clearing`, that clearer and dweller are both admitted at the third step goes through their pooled coupling and through neither alone (`Clearing.selected_pooled`). *Limit:* a pool's coupling closure is the union of its members' (`withinClosureOf_iff`), and everything beyond it is selected alike by every group within it (`Flow.unselected_beyond_pool`); lying within the closure does not make a difference separated (`Clearing.within_closure_unseparated`).

*Readings* [AI reading, D20, D26, D45]. 明 is a relation, not a state: as wide as the couplings joined to a selection, widening as measurement, models and others are joined. 识, 理 and 明 are one structure: 识 is a formation's own course factoring through the lumping its couplings make, 理 a lumping the world goes alike across (`goAlike_iff_lumped`), 明 the widening of the couplings a course goes through. Holding 明 for others is keeping the three routes open: models others can check, measurement bringing far consequences back into what couples in, and pools including those whose couplings reach where one's own do not; admission is a selection through one's own couplings (`Web.admits_through`), so the formation an act ends separates the act (`Clearing.selected_by_dweller`). A lasting change has to reach the rule, since a change to the state alone is re-absorbed (law 7), and keeping each law's scope exact is part of holding 明: a law used beyond its scope is a wrong model.

### 7.4 Alignment for all: pooled coupling across intelligences

**In words.** Pooling moves the gap to the pool's coupling closure and does not remove it. What a pool does jointly beyond that closure is separated only through formations beyond it that couple the difference apart, such as those whose support the act changes, and nothing within the closure responds to it. Coupling bounds what a pool's acts respond to and does not fix it: within the bound, the law each member follows decides. Difference is what a pool adds.

- `Flow.act_unselected_pool` with `Flow.act_selected_by_pool`: with the rest following one law reading through coupling, an act whose difference stays beyond a pool's closure, the pool's own joint act included, is selected alike by every group within the closure (`Flow.consequence_unselected_pool`, `act_courses_unselected_pool`); a formation whose admission the act changes lies beyond the closure and separates the act through every pool holding it (`Flow.apart_beyond_pool`, `Flow.separating_member_beyond`). In `Clearing`, every pool holding the dweller separates the cleared and spared worlds (`Clearing.pool_closes_gap`).
- `ExchangeLaw.Through.selectsThroughAll`: what a pool decides by its acts under a law reading through its members' coupling (a reward, a vote, a revision) is a selection through its pooled coupling, so the laws above apply with that premise derived; the bound is exact where coupling classes are decided (`ExchangeLaw.acts_alike_all_iff`), and a pool whose acts separate two histories has a member coupling them apart (`ExchangeLaw.Through.acts_separate_member`).
- `ExchangeLaw.response_not_fixed_by_coupling`: where a member couples two histories apart, some law reading through every coupling takes differently, and the law exchanging nothing reads through every coupling too; in `Clearing` what every pool holding the dweller separates goes unresponded outside the clearer's part (`Clearing.separated_unresponded`). Nothing within a pool's closure outside the acting part responds to the act (`Flow.response_alike_pool`, `act_courses_response_alike_pool`); a pool meets a part's responsive reach exactly where the part meets the pool's closure (`Flow.pool_within_response_iff`, `Flow.pool_alike_beyond_response`); no member responds before a difference enters what it couples into (`TimedResponse.pool_alike_before`); and in `Loop` a response returns through the actor's closure (`Loop.response_returns`).
- `Web.pool_adds`: a pool's selections are one member's exactly when whatever that member couples alike every member couples alike (`Web.pooled_iff_member`); otherwise the pool has a selection through no coupling of that member's, and a pool holding both separates what either separates (`Web.member_separates_all`).

*Readings* [AI reading, D45]. Alignment is neither fitting one party to another's preferences nor fitting all to a fixed common utility: it is pooling couplings across intelligences, so that what any member's acts do beyond its own coupling closure couples into some member whose acts can respond to it. A response that closes an act's difference and one that widens it share one structure, a loop through another's coupling and response, and the laws followed decide which (§4.4, §7.7). An AI system may couple into scale, distance and far consequences a person does not, and a person into a lived situation and its stakes; neither coupling replaces the other. One ground reaches every layer of selection, computation, energy, labour, knowledge, training, use and institutions: each is blind beyond its coupling closure, each that rewards a rise of what it couples into favours reaching beyond, and a change reaching only states is re-absorbed, so where a rule is read from the share it rewards (§4.5) the pool has to reach how the rule reads. Capability is not authority: the account applies across intelligences in that where situations are alike the reasons are alike, not that intelligences share one psychology, and the further a member reaches beyond what it couples into, the more of what it does is seen only through others' couplings, so capability is a reason to pool, not to govern. `universal_reason` addresses reasons to every intelligence, AI included, under its premises (§6.2); understanding a reason is not complying with it.

### 7.5 The direction, under the condition

**In words.** The alignment direction is the conditional route: with the continuation condition fixing scope, C read as widening fixing order and the practical commitments disclosed, a chosen move keeps the basic admissions some move keeps, and a move whose outcome another's widens is not chosen. Its formal part is a schema over any web; the claim about actual AI systems stays an argument.

**We propose aligning AI formation, learning, action and revision with actual living and further generation within the whole: enabling richer co-development of life, minds, relationships, ways of living and organization, while governing tradeoffs through real losses, basic living conditions, bounded responsibility and legitimate participation, with effective opportunities to understand, challenge and revise that development.**

### 7.6 A requested change must be an executable sequence

**In words.** A disposition to accept a requested change, such as a stop or a revision, does not mean the change can reach the system and be completed. The checked conditions include a margin reserved for shutdown during recovery, recovering evidence after interrupted change with the bound on reaching a stop, the criterion for assured arrival, and the difference between a risk bound and an actual outcome. Each is a conditional inference about a stated protocol; deploying one needs separate evidence for an identified installation (persistence, complete mediation, a protected stop authority and reserve, bounds on scheduling and delay, actual stopping effects), which more abstract checking does not supply. §7.3 says what a change has to couple into to reach what an act does; these records say, for their protocols, when it can also be carried out.

### 7.7 What is open

**In words.** Seeing is not valuing, and coupling is not compliance: the laws of §7.1–§7.4 bound what a selection can separate and what an act responds to, and leave open which differences matter, which law a system's acts follow, and how pools form and resist capture.

- **Seeing is not valuing.** Widening what couples in changes what a selection can separate, not which differences matter; that is the condition's to say, and its warrant is open (§8.5).
- **Coupling is not compliance.** Coupling bounds what a member's acts respond to, exactly, and fixes nothing within the bound (`ExchangeLaw.response_not_fixed_by_coupling`). Which law a system's acts follow, how training installs one that responds to what it couples into, and the evidence for an identified installation are open (§7.6, §8.7).
- **What a response does to an act's difference.** The course without an act is a comparison, not a standard. A difference opens or closes at a condition only across a step at which the two flows exchange with it differently, and where courses rejoin what they exchanged makes up the gap exactly (`Flow.exchange_apart_of_change`, `Flow.gap_made_up`); a difference closes only through a formation outside the part that exchanges differently and couples the courses apart (`Flow.closes_through_coupling_apart`), and one nothing within the responsive reach exchanges with stays (`Flow.stays_beyond_response`). In `Replenishing` a responder reading the level gives back what the act took (`Replenishing.closes_through`); in `Loop` the response ends the actor's support. For loops, closing needs a link giving back less than it received (§4.4). Whether rejoining is better than keeping or widening goes through the comparison and the condition. How a member's law comes to be, differences at many conditions at once, and members reading one another's coupling directly are open (§8.1).
- **How pools form, last and resist capture.** The laws say what a pool reaches, not how pools of intelligences form, are kept, or resist rules read from the share they select (§4.5).
- **What a rise draws from beyond**, and strengths below one, and **correspondence**: identifying the actual coupling closures, reach and loops of an AI system and its institutions is empirical (§8.1, §8.7).

The readings of §7.2–§7.5 are the AI's, decided as one account on 19 October 2026 and adopted with the scope this section states (D45).
