## 1. Becoming: one unfolding

Sections 1–6 hold everything that needs no ethical premise: theorems of every world so represented, and, for evaluation, characterizations that hold for any significance. They stand whatever is decided about the warrant of the continuation condition, which enters only in §6.4. Premises appear only as hypotheses of the theorems that name them.

### 1.1 One unfolding and its views

**In words.** The start is one unfolding of relations: relata of one kind, with what each takes, gives, stands at, needs and draws on at each step. Process, frame and world are views of it, and each returns to it: a move is part of the configuration, a law is read off the history it governs, a claim is a relatum, and a configuration is how it relates. The base is one, the run; a world is a run by its type.

- *A history fixes the law it shows.* Every history is a run of the least law it is a run of, and what a law given before the history adds are only the moves not taken, which no record fixes (`shown_le_iff`, `shown_determined_iff`, `exists_law_iff`). Laws up to their dead ends correspond one to one with families of histories holding their splices (`family_iff`, `same_histories_iff`). Used in §2.6 (the counterfactual is the law's step beyond its history) and §3.1.
- *The move is part of the unfolding.* Read within the configuration the move changes no run, and the law leaves the next move open exactly where it was determined before with one move at each step (`withMove_determined_iff`). A world's move is its relata's exchange: a world balances exactly where its levels run by the law of exchange with the exchange as the move (`World.balances_iff_exchange`, by definition).
- *A world is a run, by its type.* A `World` is a run of the process admitting every step, whose configuration at each step is what the world is then and whose move is its exchange; `Web F C` is `Dependence Nat F C`, and the two views are one by `rfl` (`World.run_world`, `Run.world_webAt`). A determined law's notions (merging, returning, reachability, keeping every possibility) are its process's by definition (`Law.lossless_iff`, `Law.returns_iff`). Used throughout: one theorem about runs serves worlds.
- *A configuration is how it relates.* Configurations related by a bisimulation are alike, and a description makes sense exactly where it identifies the alike configurations; it is then unique and, where the frame is determined up to likeness, carries a law (`Alike.tests`, `identifiesAlike_iff`, `IdentifiesAlike.carries`). Used in §3.3 (理 as a lumping the world goes alike across).
- *Separation loses the relation.* Two parts are a pair by relation: describing them separately gives the whole back exactly where the whole is rectangular, and otherwise loses exactly the relation, which reappears as extra combinations; three parts can be related while every two are free (`separation_faithful_iff`, `separation_admits_extra`, `whole_not_in_pairs`).

The views in context always form one world, and a context-free description exists exactly where they glue (`Contextual.world`, `Contextual.fitsFree_iff`): `World` is the view where the views glue, not the start. Where each context requires a parity, a context-free reading exists exactly where no selection of contexts contradicts; then a mixture of context-free readings meets at most m − 1 of the m contexts on average, and readings in context meet all m (`ParitySystem.glues_iff`, `ParitySystem.mixture_le`; Specker's three boxes, the CHSH game and the Mermin–Peres square are instances). A condition's change is read by its standing and by the exchange alike exactly where the flow balances, and with the rest counted the two readings glue at every step (`Flow.changeReadings_glue_iff`, `World.unity`). A run of a process whose configurations are not webs is a world only through its claims, with loss (`Frame.world`, `Frame.world_fixes_run`). Not derived: the shared index of steps; whether a move is formed within the unfolding, and by what (§2.6, `Becoming.MoveFormation`); which law a formation takes its history to show; that configurations passing the same tests are alike (shown where frames are determined up to likeness, `alike_iff_sameTests`, and constructively for finite frames with decided steps, `alike_iff_sameBranchingTests`).

*Readings* [AI reading, D23, D32, D33, D43]. Law and history are one unfolding read two ways, the law read off the unfolding rather than set over it; the gap between law and history is memory, and the splice is memory's absence. Action is not a second kind of cause beside the unfolding; knowing lies within it; the names of configurations carry nothing. The root is the run: every view of the unfolding is a run. Two are a pair by relation, in what they can be together or in how they go on together (under a law a pair is a split that makes sense, `Motion.neither_alone`); the members are read off the pair, and a pair is the least split, not the least relation.

### 1.2 Theorems with no premise: change, return and attraction

**In words.** A determination comes to hold or ceases to hold across a step, and that one change, read of activity, dependence and standing, is arising and ceasing, a dependence forming and dissolving, and a condition coming to stand and ceasing to stand. Over any stretch the end with its falls equals the start with its rises, so in a finite family coming to be and ceasing keep pace. A law that merges nothing returns every configuration of a finite world; what does not return is drawn into a region it never leaves; and what a course loses it loses at a juncture.

- `change_readings` (by `Iff.rfl`) and `over_stretch`: the one change and the tally, with no premise. With amounts natural numbers, finitely many conditions that keep falling keep rising (`rising_recurs`); with finitely many formations and activity decided, arising recurs exactly when ceasing does (`World.arising_iff_ceasing`); endless novelty can arise without ceasing (`Accretion`) and endless members cease without arising (`Attrition`), so finiteness does the work. Used in §1.3 and §2.1.
- `Law.lossless_iff_returns`: within finitely many configurations a determined law merges nothing exactly where every configuration returns; keeping the history admits only that history, at the price that it never returns (`Run.keepHistory_shown`, `Becoming.Recurrence`). Used in §1.3 (rest belongs to laws that merge).
- `Law.drawn_iff_not_returns`: a configuration is drawn into some absorbing region exactly where it does not return; what is drawn in never comes back (`Law.DrawnInto.never_back`), and what can be had n steps on is an absorbing region into which every possibility lost by step n is drawn (`Law.reachable_absorbing`, `Law.lost_drawn`). Within finitely many configurations attraction is merging (`Law.lossless_iff_none_drawn`); an open world separates them (`Law.succ_drawn_lossless`, `Law.pred_drawn_keeps`). Used in §3.4 (capture is an absorbing region in the rule) and §4.4.
- `necessarilyDrawn_iff_not_returns`: under branching every process necessarily draws in exactly what does not return; keeping every possibility no longer gives returning (`Transit.keeps_not_returns`), and a merge need not lose a possibility (`OneWay.merge_keeps`).
- `loss_passes_juncture`: where `s` returns, every course from `s` to where `s` can no longer be had passes a juncture, up to double negation; finding it is deciding whether `s` can still be had, found where a list holds the configurations (`loss_passes_juncture_listed`) and, for every process, exactly LPO (`lpo_of_junctures_found`). Keeping only the steps after which `s` can still be had is the greatest sub-process keeping `s` (`keeping_greatest`).

Three further theorems complete the line. *Every origin passes the gate* (`Becoming.Gated`): where the steps not marked by a decided gate reflect a property, a course ending where it holds started where it held or passes a gated step after which it holds, nothing about the property decided (`Gated.origin`); with the gate a proposition, asked of every process this is double-negation elimination (§8.6). *Where a run goes on* (`Becoming.Onward`): within finitely many configurations a run starts exactly where a course as long as the list starts, exactly where a course leads to a return, with no decision and no choice (`run_iff_continues`, `run_iff_returning`); where configurations are unbounded a run is built by search where a test recognizes the moves (`searchedRun`); asked of every process it is exactly dependent choice (`runs_iff_dependentChoice`). *What a commuting law keeps apart* (`Becoming.Commuting`): a law commuting with a map that moves every configuration never brings a configuration and its image together (`Law.Commutes.apart`), so merging a difference a symmetry carries needs breaking the symmetry (`Law.Commutes.breaks`).

*Readings* [AI reading, D25, D32, D43, D52]. Lock-in is not returning: an attractor is the unfolding narrowing its own future. Within finitely many configurations whatever goes on without end does so by coming round, read off the unfolding with nothing chosen. 机 is a juncture; 护闭 is keeping and 成闭 losing at a juncture, one operation with no valuation, and many options do not make a juncture. A law that keeps a symmetry never erases the difference the symmetry carries. Which regions actual unfoldings enter, and a measure on branching courses, are not derived.

### 1.3 Premises carve parts: counting, rest and openness

**In words.** The theorems with no premise hold of every world; each premise of §0, block C, carves a proper part of the space, and what holds of a part follows from the premises that carve it. Counted, coming and ceasing balance on one list. Rest belongs to the part that dissipates with a least loss per change and a stock within finitely many such losses; continued change needs openness; and what goes on without end is fed from beyond every bounded part.

- *Counting where the counts exist* (`World.counting_under_counted`): a count is a relation existing exactly where what it counts is decided (`counts_iff_decided`, `World.ceasings_exist_iff_listed`); wherever the counts of a census exist they are a tally, and under each premise subset, under enclosure and in finite worlds the laws of counting hold of them for every property of the counts. With decided activity, under each of the 256 premise subsets the counting laws are the tally alone or the tally with nothing arising at a step where nothing is active (`World.counting_under`). Under a bounded census arising and ceasing recur together even among infinitely many relata; asked of every finite world without decidedness the law is exactly excluded middle (`World.law_iff_em`), a calibration of Prop-valued activity and a lemma of the decided law.
- *The rest theorem* (`World.comes_to_rest_unrefuted`): in a balanced, dissipative world with censuses of what is active and of what stands, whose arising is embodied and which is enclosed around what stands from some step on, that it comes to rest cannot be refuted; each premise is needed (`leaking`, `attrition`, `throughput`), and naming the step after which nothing arises is LPO (`World.lpo_of_finite_ends`). What brings rest is finitely many standing and active at each step with enclosure, not finiteness of the world (`rest_few`). Used in §2.3.
- *Rest needs a least loss* (`Amounts.rests`): over amounts that may be dense, falls of at least a least loss from a stock within finitely many of them do not recur; whole numbers are the instance, and the rest theorem is proved from it (`Amounts.rests_unrefuted`, through `Flow.change_stops_unrefuted`, `World.rests_unrefuted_enclosed`). Each premise is needed (`Halving.never_rests`, `Layered.never_rests`). Approach to rest needs only Archimedean amounts (`Amounts.few_large_losses_of_archimedean`, `Amounts.approaches_rest`), as exponential relaxation shows (`Halving.approaches_without_reaching`). Over finitely many configurations a loss above nothing needs a law that merges (`Law.loss_needs_merge`): rest belongs to descriptions that lump configurations and dissipate.
- *Openness* (`World.arising_opens`): in a counted, embodied, balanced and dissipative world, arising without end excludes being enclosed from any step on. The eight premises decide neither enclosure nor openness, and openness with them does not give arising without end (`exposed`, `holding`, `hatch`); a finite, enclosed, lossless world that never rests exists (`Passing.passes`).
- *Fed from beyond* (`Web.embodiedArising_fed_from_beyond`): a dissipative family enclosed around in which members keep arising cannot, from some step on, be fed from nothing beyond, in double-negation form; under the first law, more without end needs inflow from beyond every bounded part (`Flow.new_beyond_every`). Being fed needs no source to recur (`Successive.relay`). Used in §2.3 and §4.

Arising and ceasing are one part in finite worlds with decided activity, and the part in which every event is living crosses them (`World.living_crosses`): among finite worlds each recurrence pattern occurs both with every event living and with some event not living (`shuttle`, `grazing`, `mutualism`, `drawdown`). The refutation laws hold however a family is listed: 28 laws in refutation form need no listing once, six in `Becoming.Listing` and 22 as `_listing` corollaries (`Web.embodiedArising_fed_from_beyond_listing` among them); fixing every list's number up to indiscernibility in every world is excluded middle (`World.numbers_indiscernible_iff_em`). Open: the connection of listing to likeness of configurations (`Alike`).

*Readings* [AI reading, D2, D6, D43]. Theorems belong to the whole, premises to parts, and living is one part, not coinciding with arising. Arising and ceasing (生起, 止息) name activity starting and stopping; they are kept apart from “generative” and “degenerative”, which do not overlap, while arising and ceasing recurring coincide in finite worlds with decided activity. A count is a relation. The stepwise verdict on self-drawing at an ending is a theorem of the stepwise reading, and no comparison uses it.

### 1.4 The order of change and the step index

**In words.** What the theorems use of the shared index of steps is the order of change: exchanges whose footprints are disjoint commute, and only the partial order of joined exchanges shows. For exchanges acting locally the index is read off the relata's own courses, and beyond them it only orders exchanges that share no relatum.

- `Footprinted.sorts_iff`: commuting neighbouring exchanges with disjoint footprints keeps every premise and reaches exactly the renumberings that keep the partial order of joined exchanges; stuttering and removing idle steps keep the premises, censuses and totals. A least footprint exists exactly where idleness is decided, and a normal form needs a decision that distinct exchanges are not joined (`Becoming.OrderNormal`).
- `run_of_courses`, `unique_iff`: an exchange acts locally where it leaves the relata it does not touch as they were and changes those it touches as their states determine (`LocalLaw`); what a relatum undergoes is its own course (`ownCourse`), and an index is a list interleaving the own courses (`Interleaves`). Two interleavings giving every relatum the same own course reach the same configuration; the own courses fix the interleaving exactly where every two exchanges are the same or share a relatum. A scheduler completes exactly where some interleaving exists (`schedule_iff`).
- `world_balances`, `world_swap`: from a local law the interleaving builds a `World` of the spine, each step balancing; two interleavings of the same own courses give every relatum the same course of standings (`standings_of_courses`), and swapping two adjacent exchanges that share no relatum changes the world only at the step between them.
- `OneStep.every_iff`: a pattern of one step is realized by a world exactly where it obeys the law of every step and of each named local condition; for families chosen step by step, running down adds continuity counted at positive levels (`OneStep.step_runsDown_iff`).
- `Weights.sum_courses`: given weights with their laws and finite lists, the sum over histories is exactly the stepwise transfer, and exact preservation through a description is one-step lumpability (`Weights.lumped_iff`); truncation, cancellation, unkept memory and loops each have boundary witnesses. Used in §2.6 (the orders of a carried difference are such a sum).

Not shown: a Foata or unbounded normal form; continuous time; separate footprints for reading and changing; courses without end; a relation, step by step, between worlds of interleavings differing by more than one swap; whether every becoming has a local law (§8.5); counts of the active and capacity over whole runs.

*Reading* [AI reading, D30, D43]. The shared index of steps is a reading of the relations, not their ground: where every two exchanges share a relatum it is derived, and elsewhere it is a commitment of the representation, a choice among interleavings the configurations do not read.

### 1.5 The Changes and the Chinese sources

**In words.** The 周易's sixty-four configurations are a world the spine states with no premise, and its laws are instances of §1.2: the law of waxing and waning (消息) merges nothing, so every configuration returns, while a law that admits only a broken line merges and draws everything into 坤 (`every_returns_iff`); complements are a lumping the law goes alike across (`pairs_goAlike`), and the waning into 坤 is the law that breaks that symmetry (`Hexagram.wane_breaks`). The relations of the places are readings in context that glue to the whole configuration only where every site agrees (`halves_glues_iff`); the received order is the run of a table and of no symmetric rule (`runsOrder_not_commutes`). 248 passages of the Chinese sources are tied to the declarations they correspond to (95 agreements, 94 exact scopes, 59 divergences), with no Lean added.

*Readings* [AI reading, D47, D50, D51, D53, D54, D55]. 无往不復 holds exactly where a law merges nothing; 窮則變 is not a law; 生生之謂易 is a scope, since change is wider than 生生; 繼之者善 and the ranks of 天尊地卑 diverge, the dynamics determining no significance and the configurations carrying no rank. The relational start holds across the Chinese lines, their universal forms mostly need a scope, and four non-relational overlays (a prior ground, a nature fixed before relations, a will or single centre, a contest or rank) are each answered inside their tradition. The passage maps and their censuses are in [YI-ZHUAN-AND-BECOMING](YI-ZHUAN-AND-BECOMING.md) and [CHINESE-SOURCES-AND-BECOMING](CHINESE-SOURCES-AND-BECOMING.md).
