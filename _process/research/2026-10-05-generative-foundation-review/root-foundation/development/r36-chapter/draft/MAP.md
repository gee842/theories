## The theorem map

**In words.** The theory, stated whole, comes in four layers, each said once in the sections:
- **I. What holds of every unfolding and every law of change**, with no premise: the readings of one change, the tally as the whole law of counting (`counting_laws`), the laws of one step, and of every law of change that within finitely many configurations it merges nothing exactly where every configuration returns (`Law.lossless_iff_returns`), a kept history never returns, and a run is a course into a return (§1.1–1.2, §3.2). These laws exclude no unfolding beyond what the representation excludes (§0, block A).
- **II. What each premise adds**: the eight World premises are independent (§0, block C); the laws of counting under each of their 256 selections (`World.counting_under`), the rest theorems and openness (§1.3), joint excess and what is fed from beyond (§2.3).
- **III. Where the dynamics stop deciding**: whether actual unfoldings meet the premises, the step after which nothing arises, the order of mixed parts, where models and targets come from, and significance, which no represented dynamics entails (`Web.significance_undetermined`, §5).
- **IV. How evaluation holds for any significance**: living significance characterized with no premise, retention and addition within exact margins, widening as the comparison on level paths (§6.1–6.3); the continuation condition as a stated condition and the step of invariance (§6.4); C, L, J1–J4 and FR as the premises of the practical results (§6.5). The ethics rests on two premises with two roles: the condition fixes scope, whose losses count, and C alone orders.

Where each principle of classical reasoning enters a law of change is §8.6; what this completeness is, and is not, is §8.1. The declaration catalogue of the whole, every theorem's name and conditions, is kept in the [reference catalogue](../history/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING-REFERENCE.md#declaration-catalogue-of-the-whole).

**The table.** One row per record in the [result register](../results/RESULT-REGISTER.md): its standing, the premises its hypotheses name, and the section that states it. It is written from the records by [`assemble.py`](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/r36-chapter/assemble.py) and is not edited by hand. *Standing* is read from the record's kind and status and from its hypotheses. The premises of §0 blocks E–F a record takes are read record by record from its hypotheses (a mention there is often a negation, a history or another use of the letter); those of block C, and a supplied law of exchange, by the Lean names its hypotheses give. A theorem *without premise* takes none of them, though the representation's commitments (block A) and local hypotheses such as finiteness or decidedness (block B) may be among its hypotheses; *under stated premises* takes some; *the condition among its hypotheses* marks the records where some theorem takes the continuation condition, and a parenthesis says where only some do; *periphery* marks a record whose canonical source is outside the core. For assessments, arguments and targets the record states the premises. A record's own page gives its exact hypotheses, what it does not show and its counterexamples.
