## 5. How evaluation forms

**In words.** Positions, their regions and significance all form within the unfolding. A position's own region is a region of formed relations, not an owner, and the significance `sig` is the one evaluative parameter supplied. A standard's gaining influence, its recognizing its target, its being warranted and its being followed are four distinct questions; a formed standard can be wrong, and learning a disposition is not learning a reason. The represented dynamics contain no significance, so they do not entail any.

- `Web.selfValuation_own_least`: read with the formations as the standpoints, a formation's own region is its closure under a relation of the unfolding, its least boundary, and `living` and `related` are the web's stepwise reading; along the empty relation the own region is the formation's own events, along feeding the feeding closure (`Web.selfValuation_empty_own`, `Web.selfValuation_feeding_own`). Which relation a center's region is drawn along is read off the unfolding, not derived. Used in §6.2.
- `Web.significance_undetermined`: on any web with an event that lives, with the web unchanged, living significance holds where everything has significance and fails where nothing has. The theorem evaluates these two constant significances only. That no law of the dynamics constrains significance holds meta-theoretically, because significance is not a field of `World`, `Web` or `Flow`; it is not a stated theorem. It shows non-entailment by the represented dynamics; it is not evidence that becoming leaves significance undetermined, and it does not show that the condition is needed. This is the one place the point is stated; later sections cite it.
- `LivingSignificance`: realized living content falls under a relevance; read from significance, the relevance is the significance of realized living content, with no valence (`Frame.relevance`, `relevance_least`). At the level of claims every living claim has significance from every standpoint (`Valuation.Significant`). It is a condition, characterized with no premise in §6.2, and the condition of §6.4 restricted to living claims.

The coupling laws read one further supplied relation, `obs`, where a level couples into a formation by level, the second relation supplied beside the web's dependence (§2.6, §8.1).

*Routes of formation.* Evaluative uptake: acquired understanding forms evaluative influence and enters warranted application ([evaluative uptake](../history/EVALUATIVE-UPTAKE.md)). A warranted response under conditions: an initially missing evaluative connection can become the mediator of a warranted response to another being, and the same associative learning can later suppress the response without overturning the reason; the warrant rests on a defended normative premise, the repair principle of [evaluative grounding](../history/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) (an actor answerable for an identifiable uncompensated deprivation of another being's independently warranted resources or capabilities has an actor-addressed reason for appropriate repair), stated at a bounded scope and not derived from the dynamics. The evaluator route: self-good, with unrestricted monotonicity and tolerance, gives a standpoint-indexed instance of living significance on a single history, narrowed (periphery), and is unified in §6.2.

*Readings of comparison.* Comparing joint compositions needs relational information; single possibilities are not enough (periphery). Under the relational reading C ranks a consuming formation below its absence, while the wide reading by events does not decide (an earlier module, no axioms). Priority of basic conditions is independent of C and excludes every natural-number score (periphery). Comparison while the classification is undecided is in its record (periphery).
