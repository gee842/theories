# Toward one connected generative model

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [Ren's direction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-one-connected-generative-model.md): derive learning, concern, governance and alignment in one connected model, preserving the no-assumptions/no-axioms requirement.

**The whole requirement remains open.** This continuation connects an existing learned successor's actual history to the root's outcome semantics and removes avoidable named-axiom dependencies from an equivalent recipient-learning process. It does not claim that compiling a larger collection derives its supplied laws or ethical grounds.

## What one connected derivation requires

The existing [generative root](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) supplies a common account of events and their continuing conditions. A successful stronger derivation must explain the actual mechanisms that form, learn, revise, evaluate and govern through that account. Each later mechanism must use what earlier activity actually made available: physical resources, retained records, capacities, identities, timing and effective authority. A favorable endpoint from another branch cannot supply a missing intermediate state.

Three achievements have different scopes. A common representation makes mechanisms expressible together. An operational connection shows actual earlier outputs supplying later inputs without hidden resets. A foundational derivation explains why the capacities, laws and practical grounds are available. This round advances the second and reduces particular proof dependencies; the third remains governed by the stronger requirement. These distinctions do not add new completion criteria or weaken Ren's direction.

## Actual learning, influence, permission and effects

The accepted successor already carries acquired actor/recipient information, native evaluative influence, an actual archive, a paid reader and remaining resources into a distinct child. The [new continuation proof](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/ConnectedModel.lean) follows every admitted finite history after withdrawal of its technical permission.

Under this unchanged law, withdrawn permission remains withdrawn and no fresh recipient transfer occurs. Both routes to productive work are covered: the selected action and an inquiry that itself can deliver a resource. Archive operations must use their authenticated actual successor state. The proof therefore does not treat inquiry as causally inert or accept a submitted state as evidence of an actual transition.

The reached timing comparison matters. Immediately before productive work, the child retains learned register 2, acquired influence 7, recipient balance 70, fuel 3, escrow 3 and a remaining source packet. Withdrawing permission at that point preserves the records and resources while blocking the work. Performing the work first raises the recipient balance to 80. Subsequent withdrawal retains that achieved effect; previously allocated escrow still pays for acquiring and writing its record, lowering total energy from 89 to 86. No new recipient transfer occurs during this settlement.

The whole selected continuation is one root trace from the existing successor entrance, with retained native/history fields, energy accounting and material conservation. The outcome interface then proves that every admitted continuation from the actual post-work cut has recipient balance 80. This is an effect-sensitive connection, rather than an assertion that an event word remains possible.

The law permits only grant withdrawal, not regrant. Its permanence theorem therefore belongs to that law. An attempt may still be issued and consumed after withdrawal without protected execution. Neither technical gating nor recorded resource credit establishes legitimate authority, welfare or moral justification. The predecessor's formation and preparation retain the exact conditional interfaces documented in the earlier composition map.
