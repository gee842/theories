## A check before building: deriving the least loss (20 October 2026, LC1)

An AI check (one session, line C of the significance aim), made before any Lean, of whether the premise the rest laws turn on, a least loss per change counted (`Amounts.rests`; chapter §8.1 item 6), can be derived rather than supplied, and of three candidate routes: from finite distinguishability, from a stochastic version without a least loss, and from the second law in its fluctuation-theorem form. The test is SA1's fourth: a premise derived from something weaker. **Verdict: every route is a known result. Finite distinguishability does not derive the least loss; it makes it unnecessary, by pigeonhole, which is standard termination reasoning. Almost-sure rest without a least loss is in the literature on ranking supermartingales and random walks. Every physical least loss per exchange (Landauer, finite time, precision, finite bath) is a theorem of stochastic thermodynamics with premises of its own (an equilibrium bath, Markov dynamics, local detailed balance), and stating it in the periphery would only instantiate `DestroysAtLeast`. What was built (board item LC1, in `Becoming.LeastLoss`) relocates the premise on the spine and is claimed as known mathematics restated, not as new mathematics.** Searched 20 October 2026 (abstracts and publisher or arXiv pages; full texts not read except where noted).

| Candidate | Closest known results | Relation |
|---|---|---|
| (1) A least loss from finite distinguishability: an amount read off finitely many configurations | Termination by a ranking function into a well-founded set (Floyd 1967; Turing 1949); every strict order on a finite set is well-founded; the pigeonhole principle; Lyapunov functions on finite state spaces | A strictly falling amount never revisits a configuration, so falls are at most as many as the configurations read. This is the pigeonhole principle; the spine's form asks only a preorder, with no totality, decision or choice. It replaces the least loss rather than deriving it: with a decided total order the finitely many losses have a least one, but rest follows without it. |
| (2) Rest in expectation or almost surely without a least loss | Ranking supermartingales: an expected decrease of at least ε gives almost-sure termination (Chakarov and Sankaranarayanan, CAV 2013); the decrease's amount and probability parametric (McIver, Morgan, Kaminski and Katoen, POPL 2018, [arXiv 1711.03588](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.03588)); the symmetric random walk on the naturals reaches zero almost surely with no expected decrease (Pólya 1921); Doob's convergence of non-negative supermartingales | `EntropyProduction.taking_not_recurring_ae` is the whole-number case (each taker at least one unit) of the ε rule. Almost-sure rest needs no least loss in expectation where fluctuations reach the floor (the random walk), and approach in expectation is Doob's theorem. Nothing here is the theory's own; not built. |
| (3) A least loss per irreversible exchange from fluctuation theorems | Kawai, Parrondo and Van den Broeck, PRL 98, 080602 (2007, [arXiv cond-mat/0701397](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0701397)): the mean dissipated work is kT times the relative entropy of the forward and backward phase-space densities; Landauer (1961), with Esposito and Van den Broeck, EPL 95, 40004 (2011, [arXiv 1104.5165](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.5165)) far from equilibrium and Sagawa and Ueda, PRL 102, 250602 (2009) for asymmetric memories and measurement; finite time: Proesmans, Ehrich and Bechhoefer, PRL 125, 100602 (2020, [arXiv 2006.03242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.03242)); precision: Barato and Seifert, PRL 114, 158101 (2015, [arXiv 1502.05944](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05944)), an uncertainty ε costs at least 2kT/ε²; finite bath: Reeb and Wolf, NJP 16, 103011 (2014, [arXiv 1306.4352](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4352)), Landauer's bound reached only with an infinite reservoir; unattainability: Masanes and Oppenheim, Nat. Commun. 8, 14538 (2017, [arXiv 1412.3828](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3828)) | The second law in fluctuation form gives no floor by itself: dissipation is kT times the distinguishability of the process from its reverse, which a slow enough protocol makes as small as one likes. A floor comes from one of four further facts: a logical merge (kT ln 2 per bit), finite time (a cost falling as 1/τ), required precision (2kT/ε²), or a finite bath. Each is physics with its own premises; the theory adds nothing to them. |

**Where Landauer's count already stood on the spine, and what was missing.** `Law.loss_needs_merge` (a loss along an orbit within finitely many configurations needs a law that merges) and `withHistory` (every law, with its history kept, merges nothing; Bennett's 1973 history tape) were known results restated (SA1, rank of recurrence). The converse direction was not stated: inside a whole that merges nothing, what a part's merge costs. That is Landauer's own argument (1961) in counting form: Liouville's theorem, or a unitary law, conserves the number of distinguishable possibilities, so a memory narrowed must spread its environment (Bennett, SHPMP 34, 501, 2003, [arXiv physics/0210005](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0210005): "in a sense ... a restatement of the Second Law"; Ladyman, Presnell, Short and Groisman, SHPMP 38, 58, 2007, whose general proof Norton disputes). Its finite-rest form, that a part can be narrowed only to the whole's spread over the rest's room, is the counting shadow of Reeb and Wolf's finite-bath correction and of Masanes and Oppenheim's finite-resource unattainability.

**What was built** (`Becoming.LeastLoss`, pure core, empty axiom reports in scratch; the records name the receipt).

- `Amounts.rests_of_finite_reading`, `Amounts.falls_within_reading`: over any amounts, an amount that does not rise and is read off configurations a list holds falls strictly at most as often as the list is long, so its falls do not recur. `Halving.stock_unlisted`: falls that recur need a reading no list holds. The chapter's claim that rest needs a least loss becomes: rest needs a least loss or a finite reading.
- `Law.distinct_within`, `Law.Lossless.keeps_apart`, `Law.Lossless.narrowing_spreads`: under a law that merges nothing, `k` possibilities no two alike, read by a part and a rest that together tell them apart, number at most the part's count times the rest's count at every step: a part's narrowing is matched by the rest's spread. `Law.Lossless.reset_unlisted`: a part reset at every step inside a whole that merges nothing needs a whole no list holds. Witness `Reset`: the part merges at every step, the whole merges nothing, and the rest is the part's kept history.

**What this changes, and what it does not.** It changes where the least loss stands: the rest laws need it only for amounts read off configurations no list holds, and the periphery's Landauer floor (`EntropyProduction.DestroysAtLeast` at kT ln 2) now needs only the conversion of a count to energy (Boltzmann's S = k ln W, and heat at least T times the entropy a bath at temperature T receives) as its physical premise; the count itself is a core law. It does not derive a least loss in energy, a least loss in expectation, or the second law; it does not state the flow, web or world over any amounts. The `Marks` law of `Becoming.Recurrence` (a whole-number mark of every change) is a case of `rests_of_finite_reading`; deriving it there under board rule 9 would change `Recurrence` and recompile its 184 dependents, so it is left for the next change of that module.
