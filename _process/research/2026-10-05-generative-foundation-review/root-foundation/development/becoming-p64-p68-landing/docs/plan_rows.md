Done: `Becoming.Selection` ({N_SELECTION} theorems, L4, importing Testing and P62's Capture, changing no existing module), with no axiom; that a selection goes through registration is a condition written into the theorems. Two histories are selected alike by every selection through a selector's registration exactly when the selector registers them alike (`Web.selects_alike_iff`), and likewise for pooled registration. With the rest of the exchange fixed, selection and scoring are blind beyond the registration closure (`Flow.unselected_beyond_registration`). In `Clearing` the dweller lies within the clearer's reach closure and beyond its registration closure; selection through the clearer's registration misses its ending, and the dweller's registration and pooled registration select it (`Clearing.unselected`, `Clearing.selected_pooled`). In `Diversion` a flow reaching beyond the registration closure to raise a gauged store is selected, and is selected alike with raising the store without reaching beyond (`Diversion.favours`, `Diversion.blind_to_source`). The share's rise is an application of `share_rises` (`share_rises_through`). Reading (the AI's, pending Ren): the gap is the reach closure less the registration closure, closed only through dependence, observation or pooled registration; read for AI training, training reaches only what the reward registers. - **P65:** the comparison where the rest responds, the second proposal, answering the chapter's section 8 open item, the counterfactual in which the rest of the exchange adjusts, where the rest responds through its registration. Done: `Becoming.Response` ({N_RESPONSE} theorems, L4, importing Testing, changing no existing module), with no axiom; responding through registration (`Flow.RespondsThroughRegistration`) is a supplied premise, and the rest held fixed is a case of it. The responsive reach is the part's closure under affecting what is registered, the dual of the registration closure, and, registering support only with one flow, exactly the union of the members' reach closures (`Flow.withinResponse_iff_registration`, `Flow.withinResponse_supportOnly_iff`). In the trophic cascade the responsive reach is exactly the wolves, the deer and the forest (`Trophic.within_response_iff`); in a two-party loop the difference returns to the part's own support through the other's response (`Loop.returns`). Responses given by the dynamics stay open.
