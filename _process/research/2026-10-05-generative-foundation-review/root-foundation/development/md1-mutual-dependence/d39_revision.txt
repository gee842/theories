
**Revision (19 October 2026, board item MD1).** (4) is revised again. “A highly entangled system” reads as mutual dependence: every formation's influence closure is the whole, so every continuation can be affected, at some remove, by every other (`Flow.MutuallyDependent`, `Becoming.MutualDependence`). Sharing conditions throughout (every system the whole) is the weaker reading and mutual feeding (every feeding closure the whole) the stronger; each is strictly stronger than the one before (`Clearing.shares_not_mutual`, `Drawing.mutual_not_fed`). Under mutual dependence every link of affecting lies on a loop through the whole (`Flow.mutuallyDependent_iff_reach`), which keeps the content of the loop reading of 18 October and states it between formations. Where the couplings that carry the dependence hold at every step around a loop, the most difference that can arrive grows exponentially both ways (`exponential_around`), so “highly” is still the strengths along the loops and how often they act. “Heavily coupled into it” stays coupling strength. **What changed.** The 18 October text read “entangled” as couplings on loops of conditions, and the note on the opening had found that this ties entanglement to pace only one way. Mutual dependence is a relation of continuations, the theory's primary object, and the loop reading follows from it. **What would change it.** A sense of “entangled” that none of the three readings captures. Ren's reply ([words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-19-entangled-mutual-dependence.md)) is its provenance, not its warrant. The texts above are kept.
