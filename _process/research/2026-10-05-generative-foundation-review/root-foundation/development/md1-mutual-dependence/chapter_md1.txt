**Mutual dependence, and the pace it gives** ([Becoming.MutualDependence](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/MutualDependence.lean); record [mutual-dependence-and-its-pace](../results/records/mutual-dependence-and-its-pace.md)). Read the closures of §6.10 as the whole. A flow *shares conditions throughout* where every system is the whole (`Flow.SharesThroughout`). It is *mutually dependent* where every influence closure is the whole, every continuation open, at some remove, to the effects of every other (`Flow.MutuallyDependent`). It is *mutually fed* where every feeding closure is the whole (`Flow.MutuallyFed`). Each gives the one before it (`Flow.MutuallyFed.mutuallyDependent`, `Flow.MutuallyDependent.sharesThroughout`), and neither converse holds. The clearer's web shares conditions throughout while the dweller lies beyond the clearer's influence closure (`Clearing.shares_not_mutual`). In `Drawing`, two formations each take from what the other requires and nothing is given: they are mutually dependent, and neither feeds the other (`Drawing.mutual_not_fed`). Mutual dependence is not mutual benefit. A flow is mutually dependent exactly where every reach closure is the whole (`Flow.mutuallyDependent_iff_reach`), so whatever a formation affects can, at some remove, affect it back. Under each reading, significance bounded by the matching closure is the continuation condition (`Flow.SharesThroughout.unrestricted`, `Flow.MutuallyDependent.unrestricted`, `Flow.MutuallyFed.unrestricted`); for feeding this extends §6.10's one common condition to any web of mutual feeding. Where the couplings that carry such dependence hold at every step around a loop, a chain of them advances one coupling a step (`CoupledAlways.chainInTime`), and the loop returns what reached its condition at every turn (`returns_of_coupledAlways`). After R turns the most difference that can arrive there, and downstream, is at least 2^R times what had reached it (`carried_pow_around`, `carried_pow_downstream`). With strengths at most M and starting gaps at most D it is at most D·(1 + M)^t (`carried_le_pow_always`), so it is exponential both ways (`exponential_around`), and from every condition to every condition where couplings are mutual at every step (`CoupledThroughout.exponential`). A selection with transmission, carriers leaving at least a offspring and others at most b, raises the carriers' ratio to the others at least as (a/b)^n (`share_ratio_pow`). Under balance, with nothing given, a draw that exceeds everything drawn before it leaves more than half the starting stock standing at every step at which it is met, and lasting n steps needs a stock of at least 2^n − 1 (`Flow.Balances.half_stands`, `Flow.Balances.doubling_needs`). In `Doubling` four of seven units stand at the last step, then none (`Doubling.half_then_none`). *Readings* [AI reading, D39, revised on 19 October 2026]. "A highly entangled system" reads as mutual dependence, with sharing conditions throughout the weaker reading and mutual feeding the stronger. Under it every link of affecting lies on a loop through the whole, which was the content of the earlier loop reading. "Exponential pace" reads as the growth around loops that act at every step, and in a selection as the growth of the share ratio. "Little time to react" reads as `half_stands` with `Web.break_not_bounded`: what stands gives no warning. That a dependence acts as a coupling by level, with positive strength at every step, is supplied; it is where the formation-level readings and the condition-level laws meet. Whether the Earth is so is not measured.
