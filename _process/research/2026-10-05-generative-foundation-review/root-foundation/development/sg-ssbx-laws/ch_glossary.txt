| juncture (关节; 机, AI reading) | A configuration with a step after which a given configuration can still be had and a step after which it cannot. Every loss of a returning configuration passes one, up to double negation; finding it is deciding whether the configuration can still be had, which in general is the limited principle of omniscience. | `Branching.Juncture`, `Branching.loss_passes_juncture`, `Branching.lpo_of_junctures_found` |
| keeping / losing (护闭 / 成闭 at a juncture, AI reading) | The sub-process of the steps after which a configuration can still be had, and that of the others. Keeping keeps the configuration and is the largest sub-process that does on its course; losing never returns to it. Neither is a valuation. | `Branching.keeping`, `Branching.keeping_greatest`, `Branching.losing_not_returns` |
| commuting law | A law `T` with `T (σ x) = σ (T x)` for every `x`. Where `σ` moves every configuration, the law's runs from `x` and from `σ x` are never at one configuration at the same step. | `Law.Commutes`, `Law.Commutes.apart`, `Law.Commutes.breaks` |
| undecided (未定; the third value, 疑 in the earlier texts) | A value below true and false in information: what revision has not decided. Revision of finitely many sentences that speak only of one another settles at the least fixed point; where two-valued revision has no fixed point, a sentence stays undecided. | `Undecided.Value`, `Undecided.least_fixed`, `Undecided.undecided_of_moves` |
