**What a commuting law keeps apart.** A map `σ` that moves every configuration tells each configuration apart from its image. What does a law of change keep of that difference? This is stated with no premise ([Becoming.Commuting](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Commuting.lean)). Where the law commutes with `σ` (`Law.Commutes`), its run from `σ x` is the image of its run from `x`, so the two runs are never at one configuration at the same step (`Law.Commutes.apart`). To merge a difference a symmetry carries, a law must break the symmetry (`Law.Commutes.breaks`). No such law is constant (`Law.Commutes.not_drawn_to_one`). A run from what `σ` fixes stays where `σ` fixes (`Law.Commutes.keeps_fixed`), the fixed case of one structure whose free case is `apart`, as `Relabel.Equivariant.tied` is for selection. Rests and returns come with their images (`Law.Commutes.rest_paired`, `Law.Commutes.returns_paired`). Under an exchange, a run that holds a configuration's image `k` steps on holds every configuration's image `k` steps on and returns at `2k` (`Law.Commutes.paired_halfway`), and the law goes alike across the pairs (`Law.Commutes.pairs_goAlike`). Every map commutes with itself, so the diagonal's never resting is the case `T = σ` (`Law.Commutes.self_apart`, `Diagonal.revision_never_rests`). A process symmetric under `σ` carries reach, returning and, under an exchange, junctures, and every run has a mirror run never at the same configuration at the same step (`Branching.Symmetric.juncture`, `Branching.mirror_apart`). In the Changes, the complement moves every hexagram and the law of waxing and waning commutes with it. No number of steps brings a hexagram and its complement to one, and the six steps to the complement are the pair halfway round the return of twelve (`Hexagram.waxWane_apart`, `Hexagram.waxWane_halfway`). The going alike across the pairs is the general law's instance (`Hexagram.pairs_goAlike_of_commutes`), and the waning into 坤 breaks the symmetry (`Hexagram.wane_breaks`).
