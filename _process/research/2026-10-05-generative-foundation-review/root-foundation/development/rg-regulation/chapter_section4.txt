**A level held from both sides, and read late** ([Becoming.Regulation](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Regulation.lean); record [two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading](../results/records/two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading.md)). Let the joint exchange at a condition oppose the level's departure from `m` on both sides, taking more than it gives above `m` and giving more than it takes below (`Flow.Opposes`); `Flow.restores` has one side only. With balance, the exchange departs from evenness by at most the level's gap to `m` on each side exactly where the next level lies between `m` and the level (`Flow.withinGap_iff_between`): the bound is exactly no overshoot. Within it and opposing, the gap falls by at least one at each step where it is positive, and the level is `m` from the step its gap is used up (`Flow.regulates`, `Flow.settles`); from below, restoration reaches `m` exactly and stays (`Flow.restores_exactly`). Beyond the bound an opposing exchange crosses `m` (`Flow.crosses_of_above`, `Flow.crosses_of_below`): a witness opposes at every step and oscillates about `m` without reaching it (`Regulation.Overshooting.oscillates`), and one meeting the premise of `Flow.restores` with one side only stays away (`Regulation.OneSided.above`). Read late, regulation needs a band that grows with the lateness. A rule reading `ℓ` steps late (`Regulation.ReadsLate`) cannot tell apart two rests that differ only over the `ℓ + 1` steps after a step, and the levels they reach differ by what those rests exchange differently (`Regulation.late_spread`); so under every rest moving the level by at most `d` a step, no such rule keeps a band narrower than `2 (ℓ + 1) d` (`Regulation.no_narrower_band`), and the late return, moving back what the rest exchanged `ℓ + 1` steps before, keeps exactly that (`Regulation.lateReturn_band`). A relay pushing a step `M` for a reading below `a` and pulling one for a reading above `b`, read `ℓ` steps late, goes `ℓ M` past its entry into the band (`Regulation.overshoot`); once it crosses past `b` from an entry near `a`, it crosses past both ends without end (`Regulation.crossing_oscillates`), and, for a step `M > 0` and a band `[a, b]` with `0 < a` and `(ℓ + 1) M ≤ b + 1`, where the relay has a run from every start (`Regulation.relay_runs`, on `Law.orbit`), every start held below the band for its first `ℓ + 1` steps settles in it exactly where `a + (ℓ + 1) M ≤ b + 1` (`Regulation.relay_threshold`). Not derived: that a given system regulates, how late it reads, its step; one policy keeping the band from every start, and the lumping of several regulated levels into one, are open.
