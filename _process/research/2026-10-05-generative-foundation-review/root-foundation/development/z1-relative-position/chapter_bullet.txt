- **The game of relative position** ([relative-position-is-zero-sum](../results/records/relative-position-is-zero-sum.md)). A part's position read against the others' (its amount counted once for each part, against the family's total) is zero-sum by counting: across every change of amounts its rise is the rest's summed fall, no change raises every part, and every part can draw more while one falls (`RelativePosition.change_balances`, `RelativePosition.not_all_rise`, `RelativePosition.rise_needs_fall`, `DrawingMore.grow_and_fall`; on a flow, with no hypothesis, `Flow.drawn_positions`). Joined to joint excess, position does not protect: with balance, a race of joint excess ends a part at its end exactly through its dependence, a need above what the race leaves and activity only while supported (`Flow.ends_at_race_end`), and with renewal gated the greatest parts end with the condition for good (`Flow.race_ends_greatest`, `Flow.greatest_end_for_good`; in `Race` the part that rises in position across the race ends with the rest, `Race.greatest_ends`). Ungated renewal restores the part (`Restored.returns`). A greatest part that does not depend on what it draws down continues (`Clearing.greatest_continues`); what it loses is a continuation with significance, which needs a significance: under the continuation condition it counts from every standpoint, and under none it does not (`Clearing.loss_needs_significance`).
