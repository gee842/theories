= L-built @statement/zh
- 凡一个过程的运行所识的是同一历程之处，配置数为 N 时，记录从 2N − 1 起确定它，与确定的律一样。在分开之处，没有学习者，无论被给予什么（世界在内），在两个运行上都对；列出、可判定的过程中，被给予世界的学习者恰在不分开之处都对。
+ 凡一个过程的运行经映射是同一历程之处，配置数为 N 时，前段从 2N − 1 起确定它，与确定的律一样。在分开之处，没有外推，无论被给予什么（过程在内），在两个运行上都对；列出、可判定的过程中，依过程所建的外推恰在不分开之处都对。
= L-built @statement/en
- Wherever the runs of a process register one course, a record fixes it from 2N − 1 on with N configurations, as for determined laws. At a parting no learner, whatever it is given, is right on both runs; for listed, decided processes a learner given the world is right exactly where none part.
+ Wherever the runs of a process have one course through the map, a prefix fixes it from 2N − 1 on with N configurations, as for determined laws. At a parting no extrapolation, whatever it is given, is right on both runs; for listed, decided processes the extrapolation built from the process is right exactly where none part.
