= L-feature @statement/zh
- 所识的未来相同的配置给每个学习者同样的记录。被列表所持的世界中，某个学习者在每个配置上于目标静止，恰当这样的配置其目标从某步起一致；律使每个配置不变时，恰当目标经识同去。
+ 经映射的未来相同的配置给每个外推同样的前段。被列表所持的世界中，某个外推在每个配置上于特征静止，恰当这样的配置其特征从某步起一致；律使每个配置不变时，恰当特征经映射之归并同去。
= L-feature @statement/en
- Configurations with the same registered future give every learner the same record. In a world a list holds, some learner rests on a target everywhere exactly where such configurations have targets agreeing from some step on; under a still law, exactly where it goes alike across the registration.
+ Configurations with one future through the map give every extrapolation the same prefix. In a listed world, some extrapolation rests on a feature everywhere exactly where such configurations have features eventually agreeing; under a still law, exactly where the feature respects the lumping.

+ 外推从经映射之历程的前段提出特征之值：由配置出发之轨道经映射的历程（course）；从第 T 步起每步提出特征之值即从 T 起静止（RestsFrom），某个 T 存在即静止（Rests）。
= L-feature @laws/0/en

+ An extrapolation proposes a value of the feature from the prefix of a course through the map: the course through the map of a configuration's orbit (course); proposing the feature's value at every step from T on is resting from T (RestsFrom), and resting is resting from some T (Rests).
= L-miss @laws/1/zh
- 所识的未来相同的两个配置给每个学习者每一步同样的记录；所以在其目标不同之步，主张至多在一处对，不用判定；目标值可判定时，主张在一处被否证，而两处记录处处相同。
+ 经映射的未来相同的两个配置给每个外推每一步同样的前段；所以在其特征不同之步，所提出的值至多在一处对，不用判定；特征之值可判定时，所提出的值在一处不合，而两处前段处处相同。

+ Two configurations with the same future through the map give every extrapolation the same prefix at every step; so at a step where their features differ a proposed value is right on at most one, with no decision, and with the feature's values decided a proposed value misses on one while the two prefixes stay equal at every step.
