**What a method comes to say in the limit** ([what-a-method-comes-to-say-in-the-limit](../results/records/what-a-method-comes-to-say-in-the-limit.md), [Becoming.LimitVerification](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/LimitVerification.lean)). Settling and recurrence are neither verifiable nor refutable: no finite record counts for or against them. The next level is convergence. A method guesses yes or no at each horizon from the record so far; it verifies a reading in the limit where it converges to yes exactly on the runs inside, and refutes it in the limit where its yes recurs exactly there (`Limit.Verifies`, `Limit.Refutes`; identification in the limit, Putnam 1965, Gold 1967, in Kelly's form). A reading verified in the limit is a union of closed readings and one refuted in the limit a meet of verifiable ones (`Limit.yesFrom_closed`, `Limit.yesAfter_verifiable`); readings of tests and readings of some test succeeding are both (`Limit.noneFailed_verifies`, `Limit.someSucceeded_refutes`); meets are kept, of readings refuted in the limit by a joint guess that says yes where each guess has said yes since the last joint yes (`Limit.both_verifies`, `Limit.jointly_refutes`). Where configurations are told apart, "the last step changed nothing" verifies settling in the limit (`Limit.still_verifies`). On the process of two configurations with every step allowed, settling and its complement are dense, so settling is neither verifiable nor refutable, and under Markov's principle no method fixed by the record refutes it in the limit: the run that changes where the method says yes defeats every method (`Limit.Free.settles_not_refuted`). Recurrence is the other way round: refuted in the limit by the last move, and under Markov's principle verified in the limit by no method (`Limit.Sequences.lastMove_refutes`, `Limit.Sequences.recurs_not_verified`). Settling thus sits on three levels: no finite stretch verifies or refutes it, a method verifies it in the limit, and deciding it outright for every process of a finite world is the limited principle of omniscience (`LastChange.lpo_iff_settles`). Saying from a run's not recurring that from some step on its moves are `false`, or saying which of two guesses recurs from their disjunction recurring, each gives that principle too (`Limit.Sequences.eventuallyFalse_lpo`, `Limit.Sequences.either_recurs_lpo`): locating where a run stopped, from the fact that it does not go on, is deciding. Identification in the limit verifies and refutes each hypothesis in the limit (`Limit.identifies_verifies`, `Limit.identifies_refutes`); `Learnable.Rests` is such identification along an orbit through a map. Read on inquiry (the AI's reading): "this has stopped changing" is a claim inquiry can come to make by watching each step and keep making, though no step tells it that the claim has become final; "this changes again and again" cannot be come to even in the limit.
