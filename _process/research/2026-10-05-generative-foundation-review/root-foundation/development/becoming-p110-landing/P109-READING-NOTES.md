The zh text of each reading is in §131 (branch `p109-coupling` 632ac3f6, `BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md`).

1. **Acting and undergoing are one move read from its two sides.** Recommendation: adopt, qualified "relative to a given cut of the whole into part and rest; which cut is apt is not derived" (相对于给定的切分；哪种切分恰当不由此导出). Reason: in `coupled` the part and the rest take one move at every step (`Run.part_act_eq_rest_act`); an own move is the rest's input (`Run.rest_driven_by_part`); "whose" is a property of the formation relative to a projection (`partForms_iff`, `restForms_iff`), so it moves with the cut; some moves belong only to the relation (`Parity.neither_alone`). It carries "there are no two realities" to the move. Would change: a coupling not representable as a synchronized product with shared moves; in discrete steps asynchrony is representable (the rest's law takes the move with no change); continuous time open. Advise against: the actor as a kind of being owning its moves apart from the cut; undergoing as passivity or lesser standing; responsibility or desert readings; a Markov-blanket reading in which the dynamics fix the boundary.
2. **No move needs supplying from outside the whole.** Recommendation: adopt as a reading of representation, not an explanation of the moves: "the clock that forms the moves takes them as data" (是读法，不是说明：形成动作的钟以运行的动作为数据). Reason: every run of a labelled process is the part's course in a whole whose rest, a clock under a determined law, forms its moves (`Run.withClock`, `clock_determined`, `Run.withClock_restForms`); the counterfactual's supplied moves are of this kind (`ExchangeLaw.ofMoves_lumped_stage`); counterpart for moves of `World.withRest`; AGENTS' "a supplied local law is not thereby external to reality". Would change: nothing within the representation refutes it (`withClock` always exists), hence marked a reading; read as an explanation, the substantive claim is reading 6. Advise against: determinism; "the environment controls the agent"; denying openness; the clock as explaining anything.
3. **Whose move it is, is a claim about courses not taken.** Recommendation: adopt; best supported. Reason: one course admitted by the part's law and by the rest's, differing only where the course never goes (`TwoClocks.one_course_two_laws`); on a finite stretch with equality decided, no policy gives the moves exactly where a configuration recurs with the move varied (`no_policy_upto_iff`); where nothing recurs every reading fits (`lumped_of_new`); a part keeping its history reads every move as its own (`Run.keepHistory_formed`) but never returns. The P53 parallel is a correspondence of readings, not a theorem. Would change: an attribution fixed by one course's step-level relations shown not to reduce to the law. Advise against: attribution off one record without recurrence; "the record never refutes it" for a part keeping its history as evidence of authorship.
4. **Reading more, more is one's own.** Recommendation: adopt, qualified "registering more moves the cut; a part registering everything is the whole, where 'whose' no longer divides (`lumped_id`)" (识得更多即移动切分；识得一切的部分就是整体，在那里“谁的”不再有分别). Reason: a move formed through a reading is formed through every finer reading (`lumped_of_finer`). Boundary: registering is the configuration carrying the distinction (识), not cognition; the bound on what a part can register is not stated as a coupling theorem. Would change: "own" reread as formed by a policy the part itself formed. Advise against: "knowledge is control"; "the more one registers, the more one is responsible"; autonomy as total registration.
5. **Closed is not one's own.** Recommendation: adopt with exact scope: "closure counts only the differences the part's law registers" (封闭只计部分之律所识的差别). Reason: the witness part is a clock whose step ignores the move, closed against the rest while its move is the rest's (`TwoClocks.closed_not_own`); own implies closed where the part's law is determined (`PartForms.goAlike`). Expected exact boundary (not proven; offered as a follow-up): where the part's determined law tells moves apart and the whole can step at both configurations, closed implies own. Advise against: closure as authorship or agency.
6. **Novelty in what is done needs openness or growth.** Recommendation: adopt for determined laws, with the branching case: "where a law branches, which successor is taken is formed by nothing in the representation, and by reading 2 it is a move of a rest beyond the listed part" (对确定的律；律分支时，取哪个后继不由表示中的任何东西形成，按读法二，它是所列部分之外的其余的动作). Reason: moves of a whole, rest or part held by a list repeat within the list's length (`Run.moves_lasso_of_whole_held`, `Run.moves_lasso_of_rest_held`, `Run.moves_lasso_of_part_held`); moves that never cycle need a whole and a former no list holds (`Run.whole_unheld_of_never_cycles`, `Run.rest_unheld_of_never_cycles`, `Run.part_unheld_of_never_cycles`); every lasso is so formed (`Run.withLasso`, `withLasso_held`, `withLasso_part`). Stock-level counterparts: P111. Would change: without the branching qualifier the reading overstates. Advise against: novelty as good per se (the G0 object is 生生, not any new organization); openness as a transcendent outside; "bounded agents cannot be creative" in a social sense.

Not a reading choice: which cut of an actual unfolding is apt, and that any actual part's move is in fact its own; both stay open as not derived.
