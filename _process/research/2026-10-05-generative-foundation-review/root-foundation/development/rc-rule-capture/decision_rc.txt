### The readings of capture through the rule

Where: the chapter [CHINESE-SOURCES-AND-BECOMING](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/CHINESE-SOURCES-AND-BECOMING.md) (§8, and the passages 二柄, 据有余而制不足 and 壹 in its [passage map](development/ro-relational-sources/passages.json)); the canonical chapter, §4 ("Capture through the rule") and §10 (元闭); the record [capture-through-the-rule](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/capture-through-the-rule.md); study section {N} ([RC](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#{N}-capture-through-the-rule-rc)). Decided in the one account of D45, with the kinds of D47 and D54.

**Decision.** Adopted with exact scope. (1) **二柄.** 「今君人者释其刑德而使臣用之，则君反制于臣矣」 is read as a rule of selection read from the share of those it selects (`ReadsShare`): locked, the loop does not turn back (`locked_stays`), a perturbation of the rule's value that keeps its direction is absorbed at the next reading step (`perturbation_absorbed`), and only a change of how the rule reads leaves (`uniform_locks`). The passage stays a divergence: 韩非 reads the loop as control between opposed interests and narrows 德 to reward, while the theory states it as a relation between a selection and its rule. (2) **据有余而制不足.** Read by the proportional reading: the kind holding more is favoured by a rule read in proportion to what each kind holds, and holds more (`Counts.proportional_locks`); the kind holding less holds less (`Counts.proportional_locks_down`). The passage's reading as dependence on one giver (`withholding_absorbs`) stands beside it; its aim, to have no rival, stays a divergence. (3) **壹赏、壹刑、壹教.** Read as one reading favouring one way at every share: every course is locked from its second reading step, exactly (`uniform_locks`, `Second.second_exact`). Its reading as one pool of selection (`Web.pooled_iff_member`) stands beside it. (4) **"The winner shapes the rule".** Read as the premise `ReadsShare`, a stated hypothesis on the reading; what is derived is that such a rule holds the outcome, not that any actual rule reads the share it selects.

**Reasons.** Each reading names the declarations it rests on and says no more than they prove. The three passages state the loop the spine had in parts (`Becoming.Capture`, `Becoming.Pace`), and the laws that close it show which part of each passage is a law and which is an aim: the lock and its leaving are laws; control, rank and the absence of rivals are the passages' direction, kept as divergences.

**What would change it.** A reading of a passage, argued from the text and its commentaries, under which the rule it speaks of does not read the share it selects; a law of the spine that a reading contradicts; a statement of the loop with real-valued shares, rules that read more than the share, or many levels, under which the lock fails.
