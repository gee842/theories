
<a id="what-a-collectives-continuation-needs"></a>
**What a collective's continuation needs.** An account of social viability brought to the theory asks which constraints, violated long and systematically, end a society's capacity to maintain itself. It lists eight, and a ninth that unifies them: keeping the capacity to correct ([source](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-19-collective-continuation.md)). Here each is restated as laws of what a course does under stated conditions. They hold in cells, bodies, ecosystems, institutions, networks and collectives of intelligences alike, wherever these are so represented. None is an axiom and none says "must"; whether an actual collective meets a law's conditions is empirical (§8.7). Five changes of framing come first, each carried by a law ([study §193](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#193-what-a-collectives-continuation-needs-v)).

- **Keeping a part is not renewal.** The question is not whether a collective persists but whether what its persisting draws on renews, for it and for whatever depends on the same conditions. A collective is a region of formed relations (§5), not a unit with an interest of its own. In the renewable process of §2, no course leaves the guard "the focal formation is active" from any configuration; from one configuration one run renews while another consumes the condition of the sustaining formation, which ends (`Renewable.kept_part_not_renewal`). Whether no course leaves a guard is a fact of the configuration; renewing is a fact of the run. A collective can keep itself by consuming others, and continuing fed from beyond is a dependence on what lies beyond, not an independence from it (`Becoming.Continuation`). A society that oppression or extraction keeps going for centuries is this case: kept, not renewing.
- **There is no number of the whole.** "Local gain above zero, effect on the whole below zero" is located, not summed. An act whose difference stays beyond its actor's coupling closure is selected alike, with and without it, by every selection through any member of the closure (`Flow.act_unselected`, §7.2). Where the conditions around what a group couples into gain nothing, a reward for raising what it couples into is earned only where some formation takes from what no member couples into (`Flow.favours_reaching_beyond`). The loss lies at a dependence that breaks, at a position, and ranking basic conditions first excludes every natural-number score of them (§5).
- **Conditions are joined, not multiplied.** Where every course is counted, conditions decompose exactly: no course leaves two guards together exactly where no course leaves either (`Viability.kernel_and_iff`, and for a list, `kernel_allOf_iff`). That is the whole truth of "any factor at zero makes the product zero". Where continuation goes through choices of which course is taken, each condition can be kept while the two cannot be kept together (`ChoicePoint.each_not_joint`), and for every rule on the separate answers, the joint answer is not that rule of them (`ChoicePoint.no_rule_decides_joint`). So `Viability = Perception × Coordination × Correction × Regeneration` is a heuristic: the conditions interact through the choices that keep them, and the joint question has to be asked of the joint course.
- **A counter-loop has to reach the rule.** Positive feedback on power and wealth is a loop whose rule is written by those it rewards (the meta-attractor above; D26). Where the next configuration depends on the rule and not on the state, a change to the state alone is re-absorbed from the next step (`Law.perturbation_reabsorbed`). Term limits, audits, courts and antitrust are, so read, couplings that carry what an actor's acts do back into what selects the rule. The theory states this as pooling couplings (§7.3–§7.4), not as interests set against each other, and capability is a reason to pool, not to govern.
- **The invariants are laws, not axioms.** The account proposes, as its last step, a small set of "system survival axioms". The theory's direction runs the other way: each invariant below is a law of one unfolding under local conditions, proved with no axiom, and read in each domain by a correspondence. Homeostasis, checks and balances, the regulation of what reaches beyond, fault tolerance and carrying capacity are readings of these laws, not new starting points; which have been stated as instances is listed in §8.2.

**Giving that waits on giving** ([Becoming.Withholding](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Withholding.lean); record [giving-that-waits-on-giving](../results/records/giving-that-waits-on-giving.md)). Let the giving of each formation wait on the giving of others. With memory, where a formation gives while it gave and all it waits on gave, a formation gives at step `n` exactly where every formation within `n` waits of it gave at the start (`waitsAll_closed`). Withholding then stays (`waitsAll_withholding_stays`) and spreads downstream (`withholding_spreads`). One withholding ends all giving in a family reached from it within `d` waits, from step `d` on (`withholding_absorbs`). Without memory, where a formation gives exactly where all it waits on gave the step before, withholding passes out where no chain of waits is long enough (`gives_without_chain`) and returns around every loop, at every multiple of its length (`withholding_returns`). Under both laws more waiting is less giving (`waitsAll_more_waiting`, `answers_more_waiting`). In a ring of three, a formation that gives whatever it waits on restores every formation from the third step on, from every start, and no earlier (`Ring.givesFirst_restores`, `Ring.givesFirst_restores_exact`); no course of the law with memory regains giving (`waitsAll_gives_earlier`).

**Whether a loop closes, keeps or widens a difference** ([Becoming.Widening](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Widening.lean); record [whether-a-loop-closes-a-difference](../results/records/whether-a-loop-closes-a-difference.md)). Read a loop by its return map, the difference that comes back after one turn for the difference sent, and the course on runs of it. Where every link gives on at least what it receives, the difference never falls below where it began (`never_closes_of_no_less`). So a loop that closes a difference has a link that gives back less than it received, and the link is found (`closing_needs_less`). A return map below what it receives at every positive difference closes within as many turns as the difference, exactly (`closes_within`, `closes_within_exact`), and a closing loop merges two differences (`closing_merges`). Taking a constant off closes at the least turn the constant covers the difference (`sub_const_closes`, `sub_const_open`). A cut closes at once where every link after it gives nothing for nothing (`cut_closes`); otherwise a later link reopens it (`cut_reopened`). Monotone return maps settle within `d` turns or never fall (`settles_within`, `never_falls`), and doubling links run away (`runs_away`). With whole-number strengths there is nothing between: a loop closes at the first turn, keeps the difference at every turn, or runs away (`whole_strengths`, `nothing_between`). Closing gradually needs a link that is not a multiplication (`gradual_needs_another_link`). This answers, for loops, the question of §7.7 which law closes rather than widens a difference.

**How fast a rule may change** ([Becoming.Pace](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Pace.lean); record [how-fast-a-rule-may-change](../results/records/how-fast-a-rule-may-change.md)). Take a two-level course: a fast configuration steps by a rule, and the rule changes only at reading steps. It is a run of one law (`paced_run`). A rule that never reads is kept, and nothing the fast level meets reaches it (`rule_unreached`). Under it, what is lost stays lost and merges lose configurations (`never_reads_lost`, `never_reads_loses`). A fast difference reaches the rule only at a reading step, exactly (`rule_until_reading`, `Copying.rule_parts`), and regaining what a fixed rule loses needs a change of rule (`Switching.fixed_loses`, `Switching.changing_regains`). Given two values, a stretch of `L` steps fixes every pair of courses with at most `N` states that held rules generate exactly when `L ≥ 2N − 1` (`pace_law`, `dissolving`, `held_fixed`; from `Becoming.Identification`). A course of configurations read whole is fixed by its first `N + 1` steps, exactly (`whole_fixed`, `whole_fixed_exact`).

**A pattern lasts through passing** ([Becoming.Passing](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Passing.lean); record [a-pattern-lasts-through-passing](../results/records/a-pattern-lasts-through-passing.md)). Read a web's activity as carrying a pattern. A pattern present at every step from `T` on, whose carriers each carry it briefly (none at two steps `d` apart), has a carrier beginning within every `d` steps (`Web.begins_within`, an instance of `Web.Covered.arises_within`). Under the passing premise, that a carrier carried before or was given the pattern by one who did, every beginning is a passing (`Web.ByPassing.begins_passed`). The pattern is then passed across one of every `d` steps (`Web.ByPassing.passes_within`). A step with no carrier is followed by none with one (`Web.ByPassing.empty_stays`), and where passing stops, the pattern ends within `d` steps (`Web.ByPassing.ends_within`). `PatternRelay` attains each bound. The passing premise is supplied, as transmission is in `Becoming.Evolution`. Passing carries whatever pattern it carries: patterns of capture pass as patterns of care do.

*Readings* [AI reading, D48]. These readings place the laws in the account of collective activity and of 无明 (D26, D45); revising one changes no theorem.
- **Cooperation among strangers.** It is read as giving that waits on giving across chains of waits longer than any acquaintance. That more waiting is less giving is the structural price of complexity: each added reliance on another's giving is one more way withholding arrives. The spiral the account describes ("others will deceive me, so I deceive first") is withholding with memory, which absorbs; without memory, around a loop, withholding returns. What restores is a law that gives without waiting on the last step. Kept promises, enforced contracts and institutions that act whatever was last received are read as such laws. The law changes; the state alone does not.
- **Bounded conflict.** An exchange of harms is read as a loop, each side's response a link. Answering exactly in kind keeps the difference forever, and answering more runs away. A conflict closes only where some link gives back less than it received: compensation, arbitration, absorption, or a cut followed by links that give nothing for nothing (exit). With whole-number strengths nothing closes gradually; that is why the account's list of conflict institutions consists of links of another kind.
- **Stability with adaptability.** It is read as a slow level that reads the fast one at stretches of at least `2N − 1` steps: long enough that each stretch fixes the course it holds, and short enough that what the fast level meets reaches the rule. The account's ordering, constitution slower than law, law slower than policy, is such a layering. That this layering is good is the comparison's to say, not the law's.
- **Forming the next members.** It is read as passing a pattern across brief carriers. A generation that stops passing it ends the pattern within one span, and one step with no carrier ends it; nothing in the law says which patterns are worth passing.
- **The correction point.** The second-order failure the account names, corruption that takes over courts, press, statistics and audit, is read as a loop whose rule-writing reaches the couplings that would carry the difference back: the routes of 明 themselves. The third order, rewarding those who break the routes of correction, is read as a selection that favours narrowing what couples in, so that the gap of §7.2 law 1 widens as capability grows. "Stable is not good" is the theory's own starting point: becoming is not assumed good, generation and degeneration do not overlap, and keeping a part decides neither renewal nor what it does to others.
