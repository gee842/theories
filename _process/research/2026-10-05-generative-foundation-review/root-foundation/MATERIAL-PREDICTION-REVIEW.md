## What the join establishes

The operational core is exactly the existing deterministic `PredictionAction` state and successor law. The wrapper adds free A/H stocks, resident/site state, previously bound or spent parts, precursor and a separate register count. One register unit accounts for one A and one H. Retrofit subtracts that pair from free stocks without also adding it to the old bound counters. Subsequent updater formation consumes another free A/H pair and moves it to bound material; rent, work, writing, finish and reversal preserve these stocks. Writes reuse the formed finite register.

Every new operational step requires positive installed register capacity, the original event guard, and sufficient typed parts. Thus its projected step and trace are actual old steps and traces. Conversely, with an installed register, an old finite trace lifts **if and only if** it is feasible and each initial free stock covers the number of updater formations in its event word. Nonnegative consumption makes the whole-word bound sufficient for every prefix. The proof retains the original endpoint, including acquired score, phase, receipt, last executed action, source, fuel and leases. It does not establish unconditional equivalence with the coarse scalar-material model.

The register premise is explicit. An empty trace is possible without an installed register; the general iff is stated under positive installed capacity, and the independent finite checker handles the empty-word exception correctly. Arbitrary configured wrapper states need not have a formation history or matching coarse material label. The actual handoff and complete-history theorems supply that consistency for the reached construction; the result does not assert it for every possible record value.

The staged join retains the actual predecessor through the adapter, machinery formation and retrofit. The inherited adapter requires a valid material state, intact site, local support, one free A/H pair and its common-fuel payment. It converts the local unit once, packages source into quotient and remainder, and retains resident and precursor. The old reaction repertoire is suspended after handoff. This is a declared staged law, not a conservative concurrent chemistry or exact kinetic refinement.

The retrofit inherits the actual leases, last-action reference and support stores, charges three fuel units, and initializes the newly built five-state score to neutral exactly once. It changes the predecessor's functional recipe to the parent's numerical prediction law. Slow/Fast writing, the response decoder, register capacity and the route from prediction to command remain supplied laws. A material pair alone does not logically entail those functions. The wrapper introduces no hidden-regime read into assembly, command or updating; the regime affects the plant's actual work receipt under the parent's noiseless complementary response law.

## One actual history and its accounting

The witness begins absent and damaged with capacity eleven, common fuel 33 and source 121. Six H and five A formations precede association, repair and capture. This order respects the old formation guard: formation cannot continue after the site becomes intact. The endpoint has four free A, six free H, one A resident, fuel twenty, source 120 and one local unit.

The adapter binds one A/H pair. A Slow updater binds another pair, and the true-oriented selector binds two H. Retrofit then binds its separately counted register pair. The predictor begins with the exact old predictive state except that its inert material label is eleven, with one free A/H pair remaining. The label change is explicit and has its own trace-preservation theorem; fuel, packet size, phases and other parameters are not silently replaced.

Six productive trials acquire score four. After reversal, two failed Slow trials lower it to two and exhaust the updater's lease. The actual Fast replacement is paid from inherited fuel, consumes the final free pair, and preserves that acquired score and register. Its next failed trial produces a paid write that changes the next command while retaining the already executed action. The subsequent productive trial ends with fuel eighteen, two source packets, score zero and the corrected false command. The full actual event word is proved separately from the arithmetic theorem identifying the corrective write.

All eleven material units remain: resident one, bound A three, bound H five and the two-unit register. No free parts remain. The exact energy history is

\[

3_{\mathrm{retrofit}}+96_{\mathrm{prediction\ costs\ and\ waste}}+38_{\mathrm{remaining}}.

The predictive expenditure includes the cost of failed packets. Future source is not borrowed to fund a presently unaffordable event. The general whole-history ledger and this concrete witness both retain the original resource history.

It retains that witness's original `UpdaterFormation.primary` parameters inside an explicit legacy prediction-parameter value. The coarse retrofit is affordable and its material scalar is thirteen, but all usable pairs have been spent, so the typed retrofit is blocked. There is no illicit switch to the new predictor's packet size or lifetime. A separate configured no-register fixture preserves all eleven units by counting the unavailable register pair in old bound material; it is explicitly not claimed reachable.

The separately implemented finite checks cover:

The capacity-ten histories preserve the old predictive support/score trajectory until the Fast replacement, where fuel twenty-two, four packets and score two coexist with a missing A or H. The capacity-nine histories form the initial machinery but cannot build the register. These particular smaller-capacity traces are independent finite checks, not additional Lean witnesses. They complement the general typed-stock theorem and the separately proved legacy-state obstruction.

The finite checker reimplements the relevant formation and predictive event equations rather than importing executable producer transitions. It checks actual microstates and payments, including the source loss on failure and the distinction between executed action and the next command. The finite comparisons are conditional calculations under the supplied deterministic recipe; they are not new observations or an exploration of every possible law or stock size.

## Limits that integration must preserve

This closes a material dependency of the restricted predictive subprocess. It does not prove a refinement of the full Python predictor with outages, skipped writes, erasure, rebuilding or stochastic scheduling. In the formal law, unaffordable rent or writing blocks the relevant event; it does not implement that larger model's failure/reset behavior. Existence of the successful word gives neither a policy that selects it nor its probability, and finite component accounting does not provide indefinite replacement.

The finite clipped score is not the append-only outcome word of the recording-fidelity construction. No stationary sampling or confidence guarantee transfers to it. The separate completed-trial protocol result can inform a later interface, but this package does not itself supply a new protocol observation theorem or physically preserve an extra historical report for free.

Variable action couplers, learned cross-target calibration, recipient protection, reason revision and successor replay are not reached endpoints of this construction. The initial local recipe and fixed proposal possibilities remain assumptions even though their instances now have an actual paid material history. The [completion audit](FOUNDATION-COMPLETION-AUDIT.md) retains the common-grounding, connected-development and empirical obligations beyond this accepted bridge.
