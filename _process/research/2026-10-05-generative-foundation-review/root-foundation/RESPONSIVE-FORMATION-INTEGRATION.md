## Substantive result and corrections

Individual capacities and access are already supplied; formation does not occur from nothing. The ethical proposal is that opening this genuinely available relief route can positively justify the first step through its relation to actual adverse undergoing.

Assessment explicitly precedes A's decision to invite. Lack of a stable team would not by itself establish independence from A's helping end; the final text does not supply such an antecedent motive as its normative ground. A later intention can organize action without retroactively creating the earlier justification. Participants' eventual assent likewise is not treated as an already controlled resource or a source of the patient's entire stake.

The canonical correction distinguishes physical possibility, actual opportunity to open cooperation, evidence for that opportunity and completed relief. An unknown broken control can leave an evidentially reasonable attempt while defeating the claimed actual pathway. That is not achieved formation, and inquiry may have a separate ground. Later refusal need not retrospectively erase an earlier live option. Neither successful recruitment nor eventual relief retroactively validates coercion.

The overlapping A+B/A+C routes give the whole-relation candidate discriminating content. Their membership, timing and contributions can differ while addressing one occurrence. Duplicating a description or listing alternatives does not create another experiential stake. Differences in reliability, other people's burdens and permissions can still matter. Replaceability of a contributor does not establish replaceability of the sufferer's continuity. No common experiencer or unique collective owner follows.

The proposal must remain correctable about its target, not merely efficient at obtaining agreement. Evidence can revise or terminate a proposed purpose. Refusal and acceptance are not infallible descriptions; neither mechanically settles all ethical questions. Report suppression, predatory coordination, forced participation and destruction of the patient's continuity remain genuine tests rather than excluded examples.

The whole relation's favoring significance is still a substantive identification. It is not proved by naming a response, adding possible histories or successful causal coordination. The candidate offers a defended prospective judgment while preserving independently warranted restrictions. Its present formation conditions must not mean simply whatever ought to be formed. Earlier owner-independent significance, joint-formation mechanisms and self-model work are credited; no theorem, empirical result or verification of unrelated concurrent implementations is added.
