## Positive target without an endless purpose regress

The justified next question is how activity acquires and acts on changed applicability, not whether every ultimate purpose has itself been replaced. An undertaking can cease to serve its continuing concern; completion can remove a need, and successful formation can change what repeating a method does. A stable concern can therefore support retiring, constraining or replacing that undertaking. Conversely, an appropriate practice is not defective merely because it remains stable.

The [practice-transformation argument](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md#3-what-changes-when-the-aspiration-remains-stable) already establishes this conceptual distinction. The [nondual applicability result](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-INTEGRATION.md) supplies a local enacted restoration/renewal relation. The recent [discriminator study](DISCRIMINATION-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) and separate [inquiry-limit revision](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/INQUIRY-REVISION.md) establish acquired operational changes at their own scopes. None should be redescribed as no revision merely because a more encompassing law remains fixed.

The meaningful positive criterion is relational. Identify what an undertaking was doing for the actual activity before assessing its success. Follow how its consequences maintain, fulfill, obstruct or alter that relationship. Then identify how those effects enter and change the undertaking's subsequent application. This makes the proposed criterion answerable to an independently described history, without making an external score the source of its meaning.

Fulfillment, loss of applicability and inability to continue remain different. An internal completion marker, silence, resource exhaustion or cessation alone identifies none of them. Ending one task also does not erase an independent demand, outstanding burden or past effect. An explanation must preserve those differences rather than define every changed response as improvement.

No figure values were digitized, individual observations reconstructed or statistical tests repeated. Its controls support learned outcome relevance without uniquely determining an internal representation.

At that bounded scope, the comparison contributes empirical identification rather than merely a metaphor for the code. It supplies evidence for a naturally realized historical dependency that an adequate account must explain. It does not demonstrate the corresponding feedback mechanism in the synthetic learner, establish a uniquely generative explanation, or complete the derivation of the biological organization from the proposed root.

An ordinary mechanism with stable encompassing rules and acquired internal relations remains a strong compatible explanation. Treating every retained difference as merely another input would not disprove learning; it would relocate the same explanatory dependency into those inputs. Equally, a changed action alone does not establish an acquired evaluative proposition or a new encompassing purpose. The comparison appropriately avoids both claims.

There is no need to posit a universal scalar value variable. An empirical relation can be represented numerically at a stated scope without making that numerical representation the source of its significance. The relevant question remains what was acquired, what persists, and how it participates in actual selection. This is different from claiming that any learned attraction, aversion or priority is good for those affected or ethically warranted.

## Tests and alternatives that preserve the target

The [integration's comparison conditions](APPLICABILITY-REVALUATION-INTEGRATION.md#a-substantive-test-of-acquired-applicability) are appropriate. Matching must preserve the presently relevant circumstances while allowing the proposed acquired mediator to differ; demanding identical complete states would remove the very history effect under study. A valid intervention separates formation of a retained influence, access to it and its use, rather than identifying all three from the same changed endpoint.

A particularly strong rival preserves an institution's or controller's local indicator while undermining the activity it purportedly serves. That failure must be shown through the prior role and actual consequential relationship. A costly prediction exercise is not refuted by assigning it a retrospective duty to fund itself. Likewise, a system that learns to suppress complaints or retain attendance has not thereby learned to protect the continuing activity those indicators were supposed to inform.

The converse controls matter too. Relevant needs can remain, a learned revaluation can be mistaken, and deliberate persistence or adequate delegation can preserve the pertinent relationship. Indiscriminate abandonment is not evidence of appropriate applicability revision. Reopening inquiry can itself transform the ground being assessed, as the [relief/reconsideration continuation](RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-INTEGRATION.md#continuation-assessment-can-transform-what-it-investigates) already explains. Current adequacy must not manufacture a diagnosis of the past or retroactively authorize an earlier intervention.
