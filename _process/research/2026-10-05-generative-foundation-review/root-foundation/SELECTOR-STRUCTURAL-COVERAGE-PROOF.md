## The attempted shortcut and its exact failure

Reuse the inherited support law `protection(capacity, intensity) = (intensity ≤ capacity)` and secondary claims `gain(intensity, i) = (i < intensity)`. G0/J classification and this causal correspondence remain supplied; intensity is not a definition of life or goodness.

For `lower < higher`, `lower_intensity_favored_iff` proves:

> The lower-intensity plan is favored by J exactly when `lower ≤ capacity < higher`.

The lower plan preserves the basic condition which the higher loses. Its forgone secondary realizations remain real. This immediately locates a failure in retaining only extreme support descriptions: capacities 0 and 4 both miss the objection in which capacity 2 favors plan 2 over plan 3. `two_extreme_reviews_miss_middle_objection` checks that specific case.

The general result is stronger. If the summary still consists of threshold reviews of this same kind, all-menu certificate coverage requires preserving every originally admitted capacity. Plan pair `c, c+1` singles out exactly capacity `c`. `threshold_all_menu_coverage_iff_capacity_inclusion` proves the equivalence, using the earlier all-menu characterization. It does not require retaining duplicate labels, impose a memory or model-count lower bound, or assert that other kinds of exact representation are impossible. An interval description carrying an independently warranted no-gap law would be a different representation from merely retaining its two endpoint reviews; that additional construction is not claimed as checked here.

## The positive structural route

A bounded amount of exact evidence can nevertheless justify a conclusion on an unbounded plan domain **under a substantive whole-domain law**. Define `probe(c,k)` to return the truth of `k ≤ c`. The measurement is represented as exact; its physical formation, reliability and availability are not constructed.

`two_adjacent_probes_identify_capacity` proves:

> Success at intensity `n`, together with failure at intensity `n+1`, holds exactly for capacity `n`.

`two_probes_give_exact_certificate_on_every_menu` then proves exact correspondence between the live family consistent with those observations and the single identified review, for every menu and every natural-number plan intensity. No assumption that the final comparisons are identical is supplied. The proof first identifies the parameter from the observations and structural law, then transports the already defined J comparisons.

This is the positive distinction from finite auditing alone. The earlier family showed that success on any audited prefix does not itself certify the next case. Here a discriminating observation straddles a boundary, and the independently supplied law links that boundary to the entire represented domain. The theory need not demand preserving every old hypothesis or repeating every possible experiment after such an identification. Nor may it silently insert the law, noiseless interpretation or permission to perform the inquiry into a record of past success.

The two observations are given as a mathematical contract: success at `n` and failure at `n+1`. This is not a strategy guaranteed to find an unknown boundary with two inquiries from scratch. Finding `n`, inquiry costs, non-stationary support and an unlimited-capacity alternative are not solved by the theorem. Availability and permission to obtain a boundary observation are separate; a failed-condition record does not authorize inducing a basic loss to obtain it.

No empirical probes were executed. Finite identification of a supplied parameter is conventional mathematics, and earlier [model revision](MODEL-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) already has finite candidate selection and domain limitations. The addition here is the explicit connection from an identified threshold to arbitrary future menus on an unbounded plan domain, paired with the failed endpoint shortcut. The existing [faithful recoding result](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/WholePracticalRestriction.lean) remains credited; we do not add another generic recoding theorem. Identifying the parameter is the new structural input to faithful comparison in this example.

## Exact hypotheses and remaining boundary

The laws, probes, retained descriptions and judgments are representations of differentiated relations within one activity. None is thereby an external substance or an unchanging law of all life. The checked positive conclusion depends on:

- A common, stable threshold law throughout the interpreted plan domain.
- The exact relation between the two recorded outcomes and that law.
- Stable meanings of the represented basic condition and secondary claims.
- The same explicit J comparator and the stated menu interpretation.

The theorem does not establish these relationships for new forms of living, a changed support mechanism, a noisy record or a revised basic classification. Actual inquiry may consume time and support or alter what it investigates; those effects must be part of a fuller correspondence. A certificate about comparisons is still distinct from permission, implementation or guaranteed choice under uncertainty.

The research boundary reached by this structural attempt is therefore **warranted acquisition and continuing applicability of the structural law and its classification**. That boundary is not the demand to prove G0 without an axiom. The authorized ethical direction and completed practical principles remain available. Further useful work must identify actual or independently defended relations which can warrant such an application; another arbitrary favorable law would not discharge this dependency.
