# Independent review: shared affect and recipient-directed action

6 October 2026. AI-authored assumption review of **own/other consequence representation → evaluative influence → recipient-directed action**. This supplies an empirical interface for the existing [evaluative-uptake construction][uptake], not a new mechanism, numerical reanalysis or completed derivation of concern. The prior [identification note](RECIPIENT-VALUATION-IDENTIFICATION.md) already separates factual learning, efficacy, valuation, private cue relief and warranted standing; those results need not be repeated.

## Primary-source findings and reading scope

**Carrillo et al. (2019):** self-laser and observed-shock responses overlap in some ACC neurons. Equipment controls and a conditioned-fear comparison constrain simple alternatives. Cross-decoding is asymmetric across the broader tested population; reverse transfer succeeds in a selected dual-responsive subset. Regional muscimol reduces social freezing, not conditioned-tone freezing. This is neither mirror-cell-specific inhibition nor a helping test. The paper identifies [public processed data/scripts](https://doi.org/10.17632/rkt957v4n4.1) and request-only raw recordings. Read primary Results, Discussion and STAR Methods; no dataset analysis. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6488290/).

The [2019 correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6584327/) adds omitted surgical anesthetic/analgesic and animal-welfare reporting to STAR Methods. The authors state that results and conclusions are unchanged. It does not report corrected neural statistics.

**Schneider et al. (2020):** a Pavlovian self/other reward–shock task finds population responses compatible with attention, alongside outcome-specific cells. Responses depend on the actor's reinforcement context, including when the other receives no shock. Omitting the actor's outcomes removes both reward and shock, so this is not a pure threat-only intervention. The task provides no instrumental helping choice. Read primary Results, Discussion and Methods; data are available on request. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7541607/).

These are compatible constraints on different tasks and neural populations. Neither shared activity nor a population-average attention pattern settles what every individual cell represents. Public derived material, raw recordings and the later helping-study records have different access statuses; the parallel source audit owns that distinction. Access alone supplies no reason to rerun a completed analysis.

## What would distinguish the mechanisms

The following are prospective methodological judgments, not results attributed to either paper.

| Competing explanation | Discriminating intervention or observation | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A common sound, sight or motor event drives both responses | Vary stimulus modality and cue mapping; compare physically matched cue presentations with independently changed recipient condition; measure movement separately | Equipment-noise control does not match the recipient's full sensory reaction. A cue response may still carry useful learned information |
| Unsigned arousal or attention explains overlap | Cross valence with independently calibrated intensity, imminence and uncertainty; track autonomic and orienting responses | Equal freezing or equal stimulus counts do not establish equal arousal. Do not regress away treatment-induced attention as an ordinary pretreatment confound |
| Another's distress predicts the actor's own danger | Vary credible actor safety separately from recipient outcome, preserving reward availability, familiarity and action opportunity | A change after a bundled safety/context manipulation does not uniquely identify personal-threat coding |
| Shared representation supplies recipient-sensitive evaluation | Intervene on an independently localized shared population or projection during evaluation, with matched sensory and motor control interventions | Regional necessity and successful researcher decoding do not identify the readout used by the animal |

A neural response is not its own downstream meaning. Co-activation can support discrimination, attentional allocation, defensive preparation or choice. Likewise, inhibition can decrease freezing and thereby release movement; an increase or decrease in helping is not automatically an increase or decrease in concern. Suppressive versus excitatory neural coding cannot be identified with negative versus positive ethical value without the intervening circuit.

## Smallest nontrivial action interface

The useful next test is an interaction between a selective pathway intervention and the recipient consequence's influence on choice. It requires an independently localized candidate route, a demonstrably effective perturbation, retained recipient/outcome discrimination, and a choice in which action changes an independently verified recipient outcome. Recipient efficacy, private payoff and motor demands must be comparable across perturbation conditions. This is conditional pathway identification, not a demand to identify every psychological motive.

Use the existing identification note's recipient-stakes/cost comparison, now under perturbation and sham. Include recipient-irrelevant trials requiring the same action and non-social trials testing the relevant motor and sensory capacities. Separate observation, evaluation and execution periods when feasible. This helps locate whether the perturbation changes acquisition, subsequent influence or physical performance, rather than relabeling all three as empathy.

Preserved factual competence needs a specified tolerance and adequate measurement, not merely a nonsignificant control result. Both actual recipient delivery and actor cue consequences matter; the earlier repair-versus-concealment contract supplies that boundary. A recipient's improvement and the observer's internal relief need not be mutually exclusive causes.

Compare an identified decision boundary or a prespecified cost range with observable choice variation. Ceiling/floor responses can conceal or reverse a simple difference in choice probabilities; no universal direction of a high-minus-low recipient-stakes contrast follows from weaker influence alone. Analyze at the animal/assignment level, with repeated trials and cells nested within it. Independent localization and held-out tests limit selecting a neural population because it already correlates with the behavior being explained.

A selective change in recipient-sensitive choice, with the relevant controls retained, would support a causal contribution by that route. Loss of factual discrimination would instead support an upstream perceptual explanation. General motor impairment would leave the helping interpretation unresolved. A precise null after validated perturbation and adequate behavioral support would constrain necessity in that task, without excluding redundancy or other pathways. A formation claim would additionally require an identified change in this influence across learning; later physiological classification alone cannot establish that temporal direction.

## Consequence for the foundation

The model's transfer factor `g` describes how information enters evaluation; it is not the fraction of cells with overlapping responses. Even a selective circuit contribution would not identify `g` and learned self-value `w` separately, derive the sign of their extension to others, or establish moral standing. This study instead gives the existing equal-information/evaluation comparison a concrete biological target. It supports testing whether shared consequence information is actually used for another's outcome, while retaining constructive, indifferent and self-protective uses of the same external information.
