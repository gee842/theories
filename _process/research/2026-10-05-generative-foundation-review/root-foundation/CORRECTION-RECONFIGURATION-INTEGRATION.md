Its fault envelope was supplied at the reviewer level. This continuation removes that particular input by computing it from a more explicit account of shared causes and each reviewer's vulnerability. The underlying causal laws and root-cause coverage remain supplied.

The [joint-risk study](JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) already establishes why improved individual reliability can worsen joint continuation, and gives repairs and partial risk bounds. That result is credited, not recounted. The present question concerns a different interface: what an operational admission procedure must retain when changing who reviews and which failures can affect them together. No new discovery of correlated failure is claimed.

Within the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), reviewers, controllers, observations, credentials and reorganization are differentiated activities and dependencies within one changing arrangement. Distinct identifiers do not establish independent existence or failure. The representation retains shared causes, command consequences and resource changes. It omits the developmental formation of the monitor, detailed social relations and physical implementation. A supplied law is local explanatory input, not something outside generative reality.

## 2. From causal formulas to a fault envelope

Each registered reviewer's vulnerability is a finite formula. A leaf names a root failure or compromise condition. An `any` node means either input is sufficient for the represented vulnerability; an `all` node means both are required. These are failure formulas, so their interpretation is the reverse of a casual diagram of successful support.

The formula must be an engineering upper bound: outside its evaluated failure set, the relevant reader or issuer satisfies the response law used by the theorem. It need not predict that every exposed reviewer actually misbehaves. For Byzantine assertions, combining two systems does not by itself justify an `all` gate: a mechanism preventing either one alone from corrupting the response must actually exist. Shared power, common software, joint administrators or a common decision procedure can reintroduce a single sufficient cause. The same cause identifier may occur many times and in many formulas; no probabilistic independence assumption is used.

Given a nonempty finite family of admitted root-failure bounds, evaluate every reviewer's formula at every bound. Each resulting set is a reviewer-level possible fault set. This is the computed envelope used by the existing certificate test. An actual root-failure set need only be a **subset of an admitted bound**; exact enumeration of its particular subset is unnecessary.

Lean checks monotonicity for arbitrary finite formulas and transports it through the roster filter. Thus a reviewer outside a bound's induced fault set also survives every actual root set covered by that bound. A certificate supplies a supporter outside that bound; the declared faithful response law then warrants the attested statement. This completes the input-to-attestation connection without assuming a separate honest-reviewer count.

The executable `ready` test requires a nonempty root-fault family and, for every pair of admitted root bounds, a registered reviewer surviving both. Lean proves this equivalent to nonemptiness plus the inherited separation condition on the computed envelope. Under covered actual failures and responsive surviving reviewers, it supplies availability. Soundness does not require this availability condition. A conservative family may reject some physically workable arrangements; the test characterizes its declared worst-case envelope, not every possible real pattern of behavior.

## 3. A reorganization with a real corrective suffix

The protected local transition receives an explicitly authorized proposal, a candidate arrangement, a represented reorganization cost `c`, and a supported correction-path length bound `L`. For `n` candidate roster entries, it admits only if `ready` holds and both work and remaining time cover

\[
c+n(L+2)+3.
\]

The first term covers the supplied review/installation allowance; the second and third are inherited collection and command costs. Admission consumes `c`, installs the candidate, preserves the protected mandate and route tree, increments the authority epoch and transaction serial, and clears signed/in-flight/completion flags. The retained old credential is not silently reauthorized. A fresh command obtains current endorsement through the inherited interface. A rejected proposal returns `none`; the caller can retain the original value. This pure-function rejection does not model physical expenditure on a failed real-world proposal.

The generic `admitted_configuration` theorem verifies these changes and the remaining budget. `old_request_rejected_after_reform` excludes the old epoch. `admitted_budget_for_shorter_route` covers any actual path no longer than `L`. The main `admitted_reform_completes` theorem joins admission to the inherited guarded command execution: given current responses, faithful surviving readers/issuers, covered root failures and a qualifying path, the new arrangement actually completes the command within its retained mandate.

Authorization is an explicit supplied Boolean approval for this invocation, not a derived quorum decision about the proposal. An implementation must bind that approval to the actual candidate and parameters. The theorem does not infer legitimate reorganization from old signatures. Mandate *expansion* is deliberately not implemented here; its substantive reasons and authority must be justified separately. Preserving the old table also does not prove that table is just.

The transition is atomic with respect to the protected installation. The reorganization cost is supplied, not measured; formula evaluation, authorization, copying and synchronization must fit a warranted allowance in an application. There is no theorem of continuous service while moving machines, zero downtime, concurrent Byzantine agreement, or response under indefinitely delayed messages. The earlier [funded succession result](ALIGNMENT-CONTINUITY-INTEGRATION.md) retains its distinct material-carrier scope; its part and payload assumptions cannot be silently borrowed to certify this new physical transition.

## 4. Positive repair and retained failures

| Arrangement | Computed relevant fault sets | Availability test |
|---|---|---|
| Three reviewers, distinct single causes | `{0}`, `{1}`, `{2}` | Passes |
| First two moved under cause 0 | `{0,1}`, `{}`, `{2}` | Fails |
| Fourth reviewer with its own cause 3 | `{0,1}`, `{}`, `{2}`, `{3}` | Passes |
| Fourth reviewer shares cause 2 | `{0,1}`, `{2,3}` | Fails |

The empty fault set in the second/third rows records that one admitted root cause affects no current reviewer. It is harmless and retained rather than silently removed. The first and second rows have the same number of registered reviewers and admitted singleton root failures. The difference comes from changed support/control relations.

Retaining the original reviewer envelope after the merger accepts support from compromised reviewers 0 and 1. Recomputing the envelope rejects it. The protected admission rule rejects the merged configuration and admits the repaired fourth-reviewer arrangement. Merely duplicating the vulnerable support does not repair it. The formula example also verifies that two occurrences of the same cause under an `all` gate still fail together.

In the successful execution, reorganization consumes two units from twenty, advances epoch 7 to 8 and serial 0 to 1, and retains the shutdown-only scope. Reviewers 0 and 1 then withhold all replies. Reviewers 2 and 3 provide current observations and the exact new request; shutdown succeeds, running becomes false, serial advances to 2 and total spending is thirteen. This gives an explicit constructive ending, not an invariant requiring the AI to continue operating.

Twelve units can fund the two-unit reorganization alone but cannot fund reorganization plus the eleven-unit corrective suffix, so admission fails. Thirteen units and thirteen steps suffice in the represented boundary case. This reserve is attached to the admitted path bound and immediate protected suffix; unrelated later spending can consume it unless an implementation maintains the reservation.

Finally, a formula can include a shared cause 9 for every reviewer while the root-fault family omits that cause. `ready` passes for the listed bounds, yet cause 9 exposes the entire roster. This is a genuine model-coverage failure, not a refutation of the conditional theorem or of the wider generative account. A monitor that trusts a fabricated dependency description can similarly be misled; successful computation cannot certify its own correspondence.

## 5. Alignment interpretation and counterreview

Under the completed G0/J account, correction matters because actual lives, learning, participation and further formation can be harmed by the ends and selectors being implemented. A performance-improving reorganization can therefore defeat its own ethical purpose if it also removes the warranted capacity to expose and correct those harms. The practical consequence is to assess the proposed control relations together with the response they permit. This is a conditional application of explicit normative commitments, not a new axiom hidden in `ready`.

A numerical reliability test does not grant an institution a permanent right to preserve itself. J1–J4 still require actual losses, basic protections, consent/authority, bounded undertakings and timely judgment. Adding a fourth reviewer can impose burdens or worsen other basic conditions. A smaller protected monitor may be preferable for a particular accessible fact, as the earlier direct-scope guard already demonstrates. A legitimate decision can also retire a harmful arrangement. The formal result establishes what one chosen transition preserves; it does not establish that every failed candidate must be prohibited in all circumstances.

This is author counterreview, with no independent-agent acceptance requested or claimed.

| Objection | Assessment and resulting scope |
|---|---|
