## Mechanism and guard audit

The causal reading matches the intended narrow contribution:

- Exogenous native and recipient words enter `operation_return`, which creates actual receipts and stock changes. Hidden final efficacy enters only the final physical work comparison. Neither chooses a learned sign or assessment directly.
- Exactly two Boolean signal cells are reused. Each physical return is overwritten by paid acquisition into a shortfall, missing or empty mode. The adapter supplies those modes. The native update reads the acquired own cell; binding requires both acquired cells and reads their actual values.
- Empty sockets initially conduct nothing. The active route reads no installed positive-transfer tag. It sums the two actual live signs and multiplies by the actual native register. Inert products retain material at sign zero, while the identity filter blocks transmission after the same acquisition and construction. The conventional table is the exact finite alternative to the declared integer comparison.
- Primary binding consumes its addressed free pair and fee. Replacement is available only after four completed pairs in the six-pair configuration, with both sockets occupied and the reserved pair accessible. The oldest part is selected from the fixed finite binding identities; this is the declared two-socket ordering, not an inferred external chronological oracle. Frozen-sign replacement reads the still-present old sign, then retires and replaces the same amount of matter at the same tariff.
- A successful bond precedes native writing. Failed binding retains the earlier native value; a failed subsequent write retains the new graph and acquired cells. Count, phase and inventories prevent repeat payment, repeat binding or a new sample after that failure in the declared execution.
- The final read pays before checking applicability. Invalid scope consumes the port after the read and incurs no assessment fee. Positive assessment does not preempt the inherited failed-action branch: with source10 but fuel below8, the packet is lost and the core is deactivated without RI service expenditure. A source shortage instead consumes the port without packet loss.
- Final capture credits the recipient only. Feedback acquisition and writing spend the reserved escrow. The one-use port cannot authorize a second initiation. Transmission saved in `outcomes` is an observer diagnostic; subsequent control does not read it. Bills, waste and success markers are likewise not hidden reward inputs.

The finite port, comparison scale, endpoint functions, source ownership, measurement calibration and plasticity recipe remain supplied. This code can demonstrate an operative link formed through paid events; it does not derive why that analogy is accurate, why the architecture is selected, or why the resulting preference is warranted.

## Exact execution scope

The reviewed Python API executes the frozen schedules through `run_case`. It is not an arbitrary-interleaving event kernel. For example, `pair` relies on the initial admission made by `run_case`, and assessment/service perform several phases within one call. The finite menu changes scope only after training and before the final read; it introduces no asynchronous mutation between internal statements. A formal event law may state stronger invariants, but those must not become an unproved theorem about unrestricted direct calls to these Python helpers.

Preparation, account and material assertions occur throughout the selected histories. Such assertions are useful checks, not a replacement for independent comparison of complete states and event deltas. The oracle's distinct embedding of the live parent must be reconciled losslessly with the producer overlay. Differences in spelling or duplicate observer snapshots must not justify dropping physical latches, unfinished phases, parts, balances or failed ancestry.

## What premise has actually been reduced

The narrow supplied input is reduced rather than merely renamed. Initially empty sockets have no operative conductance. Paid physical events supply the two acquired bits; those bits change backed link occupancy and sign. Assessment then reads that live graph. No hidden world label or analyst success score installs the active connection. The inert and identity-filtered comparisons show that the same observations, native learning and command knowledge are insufficient for the active choice when the conducting product or its transmission is removed. The paid revision comparison shows that a changed connection can alter choice while native value, factual efficacy and action affordability remain matched.

The **capacity to acquire such a connection** remains a supplied local architecture. It includes compatible cross-channel sockets, the recipient address, feature and sign coding, calibrated acquisition, native evaluation, the plasticity chemistry, the later modality mapping, the comparison gain and available material and fuel. These are explicit conditional law premises. The result does not derive those premises from undifferentiated matter, establish that prediction or private persistence selects them, or show that their evaluative interpretation is uniquely correct. Calling the operative link acquired is justified; calling the entire shared evaluator self-derived would not be.

The whole-foundation implication is consequently limited but substantive: a retained connection into evaluation need not be installed as an already conducting local input, and its causal formation can explain opposed as well as favorable behavior. The live revision suppresses an affordable, physically effective recipient service, so acquisition and revision alone cannot warrant improvement. Generalization to other recipients and modalities, continued reliable correction under the altered architecture, empirical identification of this mechanism, and the separate justification of patient-good, authority and competing claims are not established here. This identifies the remaining scope of existing obligations; it adds no new universal completion requirement or mandatory apparatus ladder.
