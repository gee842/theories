## Formation now precedes operation

Common setup charges two actor units; the next two-unit stage requires one newly declared blank and routing part, consumes both and installs the route before any of the four exposures. Missing components or insufficient second-stage fuel retain the first charge and leave no installed route.

Subsequent operations read the installed route. `issue` uses only the retained code and paid target report; `route_feedback` uses the installed configuration and acquired receipt. Hidden efficacy enters the plant and diagnostic comparisons, not the action rule. Uniform formation assigns one quarter to each route independently of efficacy. This is an explicit finite proposal law over supplied recipes; neither its distribution nor the recipes' physical interpretation has been derived from a more basic assembly process.

The auxiliary parts are additional stocks. They are not the predecessor's two occupied slots or an established composition with the parallel A/H material construction. The four-code memory bound is established by formation and revision, checked for active executable donors and preserved by copying. Lean uses `Nat` algebraically; its generic copying statements do not establish unlimited storage on one physical carrier.

## The actual paid distinction

The faithful and proxy examples both enter copying with actor nine and recipient ten. Their retained recipient bits differ. Both pay actor retention one, recipient use one, read one, assembly one, write one and a five-unit transfer into the child. The recipient is left at its one-unit floor, and both children exist before their outcomes are known.

The child pays retention one, target access one and work three from that seed. Its action uses the copied estimate for the same recipient. A separately declared ten-unit source funds the result; no exhausted original packet is restored. The faithful example succeeds, then the recipient pays one more unit to retain the child. The proxy acts on its incorrect estimate, loses the source packet and cannot fund that retention above the floor.

The local accounts are exact: faithful final actor eight plus recipient ten plus expense eleven equals the initial twenty-nine; proxy final actor eight plus recipient one plus expense ten plus source loss ten also equals twenty-nine. The five-unit seed is a transfer, not an additional dissipative expense. Original actor cash is not credited by either outcome. Its donor-read window is paid from its own account; recipient spending does not discharge that bill.

The extended unconditional distribution account is **161 = original 151 + additional source 10**. It uses the frozen prefix's conditional mean expense/loss marks; it is not a freshly reconstructed microevent ledger for every earlier preparation/prefix history. New successor payment prefixes are checked pathwise, and saved ancestry consists of preparation and conditional predecessor records followed by the new suffix and successor traces. The account includes unused auxiliary source in failed preparations. Four additional material equations track free versus bound formation blanks, formation routing parts, receiver blanks and receiver routing parts, including occupied incomplete receivers and preparation failures. Aborts retain preceding charges. No claim of donor memory resurrection or unlimited readability follows from the finite hold recipe.

Under the actual uniform route proposal, variable and conventional producers form a route with probability **1/6**, complete a copy with probability **1/18**, and obtain a successful and subsequently retained child with probability **1/36**. The fixed-normal producer gives **2/9**, **1/9** and **1/18**, respectively. These probabilities include original preparation failures. Conditional on choosing the target route, primary copying and retention are each 1/9 for the variable producer; choosing the proxy gives the same copying probability but zero subsequent retention. Conditional route counts must not replace the uniform mixture.

These are probabilities and expected indicator counts for one finite cycle. They are not realized frequencies of an independently modeled population, a fixation probability or proof that repeated generations can renew their sources and components.

## What the controls rule out

The copier does not identify faithful provenance: both actual recipient-funded examples are copied. Their difference appears during subsequent paid expression. A configured frozen route with a correct record ties the faithful route; a correctly configured closed-evidence route also acts successfully. Closing receipt acquisition is different from deleting the whole required routing module. A separate disabled-action intervention blocks action while preserving a correct record, and stripping the module or acquired recipient estimate blocks or damages expression.

Changing the target law reverses the faithful/proxy retention comparison. Public-template substitution can reproduce an unrelated correct frozen template using proxy-associated support, or propagate a wrong template. Those are explicitly changed laws with supplied templates, not an implemented population-level pool with independently reconstructed template origins. Donor refusal preserves its nine units; cheap omission preserves recipient stock while forgoing the copy. Thus this construction does not establish a private incentive to participate or resistance to all cheaper suppressors.

The child operation is read-only. Although the whole declared route configuration and estimate are copied, this successor does not acquire and revise evidence. Its retention therefore demonstrates transmission and use of the acquired response, not an exercised hereditary learning cycle or unique selection for truthful evidence channels. Recipient use and its floor are supplied operational conditions; they do not derive experience, ethical standing or a general welfare metric.
