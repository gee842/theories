## 1. The central inference uses G0 directly

Fix one response and its admitted continuation domain V. Its realization inventory already describes that response; the response is not selected separately after learning which history occurs. The sufficient hypothesis is:

\[
\forall h\in V,\quad\exists q:\quad
\mathrm{Generative}(q)\land\mathrm{Realized}(h,q)\land\neg\mathrm{Before}(h,q).
\]

G0 then yields:

\[
\forall h\in V,\quad\exists q:\quad
\mathrm{PositiveRelevant}(h,q)\land\neg\mathrm{Before}(h,q).
\]

`contingent_generation_has_positive_ground` checks this implication. There is no comparison C, no `Better`, and no preservation-of-all-other-effects premise in the theorem. It does not infer any positive meaning from an arbitrary Boolean flag: G0 and the realization correspondence are precisely the declared normative and descriptive premises.

`certified_integral_supplies_contributory_reason` adds an explicit direct articulation:

\[
\mathrm{PositiveRelevant}(h,q)\Longrightarrow\mathrm{ContributoryReason}(h).
\]

The reason supports the common response through the aspect it brings about. This articulation is supplied openly rather than hidden in a definition of goodness. It does not grant permission or override another reason. The theorem proves its consequence, not a separate foundation of that articulation.

同一回应能够确定带来某种生成善，并不要求预先固定那种善的唯一形态。这里既没有把“每一种未来各存在某个不同动作”偷换成当前共同动作，也没有把有正面意义偷换成整体严格更好。该充分证书不是所有正当探索或风险行动的必要条件。

## 2. One arrangement, two different kinds of openness

The finite construction has one response `respond`. A pre-existing branch of activity, the productive relation's magnitude and the response together form a fresh relation. `freshLeft` requires retained `priorLeft`; `freshRight` requires retained `priorRight`. Thus fresh occurrence is defined through an actual relation to prior activity, not as independently owned output from an isolated subject.

Three candidate formation models supply magnitudes 0, 1 and 2. For **each fixed model**, both Boolean branch histories are feasible under `future`: this is the specified nondeterministic future law. A productive model therefore admits both fresh-left and fresh-right futures. They are real alternatives in that local law, not merely two epistemic descriptions of a single already fixed future.

`same_response_secures_generation_without_fixed_form` proves, for either productive model:

- Every admitted branch contains a genuine newly formed generative claim.
- No single fresh-left/fresh-right claim appears in every branch.

Separately, an acquired first reading supports two remaining magnitude hypotheses. Even after establishing positive generation, the inquiry does not know whether the magnitude is 1 or 2. A second reading can distinguish them. This remaining **epistemic** uncertainty differs from the **future-form** openness that persists within each model.

The local event/reading law is a supplied finite representation. Its positive ethical interpretation follows the declared generative correspondence; the theorem does not derive the biology or phenomenology of all life from these four claim constructors.

## 3. The finite integral neither double-counts nor decides the whole

`finiteIntegral` is a signed finite sum with unit measure. Its integration domain consists of relation-occurrence types in **one branch history**. The two mutually exclusive branch histories are never added as if both had happened. `positiveDomain` contains fresh-left and fresh-right once each, and `fullDomain` adds one burden occurrence; both lists are proved duplicate-free.

`positive_integral_has_positive_occurrence` proves that a strictly positive finite signed sum has at least one strictly positive contributing occurrence. The separate `positive_occurrence_has_actual_generative_correspondence` checks the concrete connection: a positive occurrence in the fresh domain is generated, actually realized and absent from the prior history. This output feeds G0 through the general certificate theorem.

For a productive magnitude k (1 or 2), every branch has fresh contribution k. The explicit burden contributes −(k+1), giving a full represented signed change of −1, compared with 0 before the response. `positive_integral_and_adverse_full_change` checks these values.

这个负担没有被删掉。算例表明，一个确实出现的正面生成方面可以与另一个不利方面共存；它不把这组人为指定的单位、加法或兑换率宣告为普遍伦理总量，也不声称这项回应最终应被允许。正面根据成立不需要先抹去负担；最后怎样回应继续受其他已知关系与实践原则约束。

The finite arithmetic is optional representation. Its general theorem needs the explicit sign-to-generativity correspondence; it does not make every positive numerical change good. Conversely, using no scalar integral would leave the direct G0 inference intact. The historical [future-good integral note](FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md) retains its original hand-argument validation scope; this file does not retroactively certify it.

## 4. Preserved possibilities and actual inquiry access

`response_preserves_joint_openness_and_query_access` directly applies the existing `faithful_history_transport_preserves_joint_bundles` theorem to the same branch history. Prior-left and prior-right realizations are retained where they held; no false common history is assembled by mixing witnesses from different branches. All represented old joint bundles remain possible. The `queryOpen` field remains true after the same response.

This concrete positive construction verifies joint openness and retention of the declared query-access field together; the general G0 theorem does not require all prior good to be preserved before any new good can count. The burden remains represented separately. **`queryOpen` is a retained field: `acquire` and `rule` neither inspect nor consume it in this module.** The new proof therefore does not establish an actually executed, access-gated query path. Interpreting that field as real continued access remains a correspondence premise. Earlier funded observation and correction models supply their own implementation results, not a new literally checked composition here.

## 5. Visible futures use acquired evidence and remain revisable

The first evidence is produced by the existing `StandardsBridge.acquire` function, using one available query and its actual supplied reading rule. The candidate repertoire has the three explicit local laws. The first reading removes the barren law while retaining the laws of magnitude 1 and 2. It certifies the positive future contribution throughout the remaining model/branch family, while the initial pre-response contribution remains zero. Merely filtering evidence therefore has not performed the response or created the new relation.

The final compiled application examples reuse `actual_rule_retained` and `acquired_evidence_faithful` to connect this acquisition to truth retention, and `PracticalJudgment.robust_at_actual` to transport the certified assertion when actual coverage is warranted. They are applications of earlier results, not new discovery of perception or generic learning. The repository's broader formed-reference, action/prediction and correction studies supply their previously checked scopes; their connection here is interpretive reuse rather than a newly verified implementation composition. No new universal sensing mechanism is claimed here.

`acquired_evidence_secures_good_and_leaves_both_opennesses` additionally checks:

- Both productive model hypotheses remain live after the first reading.
- The second question is initially unanswered, and its two possible answers select different productive models.
- Counterevidence to the first productive reading leaves the old surviving family inconsistent, causing abstention rather than silently retaining its certificate.
- A fresh inquiry with a barren reading retains the barren model from the original repertoire.

`wider_scope_can_require_certificate_retraction` shows that reopening the admitted scope to a barren possibility invalidates the prior positive certificate. That theorem does not itself warrant adding the possibility: a newly discovered condition or actual changed relation must justify the revision. It also does not falsely retroact a current change into a past truthful observation. This finite construction witnesses further discrimination and retraction; it is not a complete model of arbitrary future concept formation.

没有把“好的不确定性”定义为拒绝知道，也没有只把它做成永远缩小假设集。新证据可以使正面结论更有根据，仍允许不同新形态；当支持关系或所见范围改变时，旧证书可以撤回。这些变化发生在同一认识—回应—后果过程中。
