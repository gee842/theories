## Question fixed before construction

**How can the relations selecting an organization, including its correction arrangements, remain operative through activity that must itself be selected and reconstructed?** A perpetual protected guardian is not an explanation. Neither is an infinite sequence of newly named guardians. The candidate explanation is a finite organization of mutually enabling activity whose actual returns maintain the conditions of its continuation. Its establishment, persistence and transformation are distinct questions.

**研究连接：活动后果 → 选择及纠正条件的相互重建 → 组织的形成、接续与转化。** 纠正的承载、有效准入与支持关系都须参与实际过程，不以永恒保护者为前提。相互依存可以解释既有组织怎样维持，却不自动解释它怎样第一次形成，也不保证任意耦合都产生一个稳定整体。

This contributes an implementation account to generative alignment, not a new normative starting point. G0/J and the completed practical account retain their separate warrant. A self-reproducing circuit can sustain protection or domination; the circuit's persistence cannot settle its ethical quality.

## Derived maintenance and actual realization

The new [ReproductionNetwork.lean](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/ReproductionNetwork.lean) represents a finite, duplicate-free list of roles. An operative role converts material into support for specified other roles. Its activation depends on its current support stock meeting a threshold. Stocks undergo wear and have capacities. These are deliberately local, commensurable material units; legal legitimacy, ethical quality and all conditions of life are not reduced to them.

For stock vector s, role i contributes the specified amount to j only when i's threshold is met. The next stock at j is the lesser of its capacity and its remaining stock after wear plus incoming support. The maintenance certificate lists a lower stock vector, which roles it activates, and whether their actual returned amounts cover the required lower stocks after wear. `maintenance_step` derives preservation from those amounts. `maintenance_history` carries that result through all finite times. Preservation is a conclusion, not a field assumed by the certificate.

Actual execution has further conditions. Every cohort replacement consumes one beginning-stock part per role; the total support delivered is debited from a common represented material stock. Wear and overflow remain in the material balance. A generation identifier changes for each executed cohort. The supplied ordinary continuation replenishes the required parts and enough material for the maximum represented demand. `actual_history` checks an actual generative-core trace under those premises. Neither a maintained numerical stock nor an unexecuted simulated update suffices for that claim.

The construction abstracts detailed assembly and faithful transfer of a protocol. It establishes neither a biological body nor a new microfoundation for every institution. It also does not imply that every role is active in every core trace: that stronger description requires the activation premises. The examples satisfying the lower bound two have all their represented roles active; the zero-stock example does not.

**维护证书来自回返量、磨损、容量及实际启动条件。** 运行还必须有可用材料与承载者替换部件；零库存中存留一个结构描述不算该组织已在活动。耗损及溢出被保留。持续补给与具体重建规律仍是公开的局部前提，不被解释成生成整体之外的东西。

## Establishment, phase and causal contribution

The two-role instance represents a practice and the operative support for its selection or correction. Each role requires stock two, loses one unit per round and returns two to the other, up to capacity three. Neither role receives a permanently fixed supporting stock.

| Initial stocks | Checked continuation | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| (3,3) | Both stay at three while cohorts change under funded operation | Mutual reproduction can maintain the joint organization |
| (1,3) | Next stocks (2,3), inside the maintained region | A sufficient margin can establish joint operation from partial activation |
| (1,2) | (2,1), then (1,2), repeatedly | Mutual positive returns need not establish simultaneous operation |
| (0,0) | Stocks stay zero under ordinary return alone | A possible supporting architecture does not automatically bootstrap itself |
| (3,3), one return route severed | Both stocks are zero after five rounds | Each role's continued support is a real causal dependency |

The alternating case is not declared non-generative or non-recurrent. It is a periodic organization; the theorem excludes eventual entry into the different regime in which both roles remain simultaneously active. This matters for the definition: the temporal pattern is part of the organizational signature. A theory that identifies organization only with the set of roles would erase the difference.

The establishment operation transfers actual material into activation stocks. From a cold state with eight material units and two parts, transferring two units to each role leaves four material units for the first ordinary cycle. `establishment_then_actual_recurrence` joins that operation to every finite subsequent ordinary history. Formation is a funded predecessor event, not a logical circle in which each initially absent role simply presupposes the other. The conversion operation and initial materials remain premises; emergence from absolute absence is not claimed.

## Coupled regimes need not form one global regime

Four roles permit two separate reciprocal pairs, a one-way bridge between them, or a four-role ring. The elementary return law is unchanged. From stocks (3,3,0,0):

- Separate pairs retain that state: one recurrent circuit does not automatically recruit the other.
- A bridge supplies the second pair. After two rounds the stocks are (3,3,3,2); the bridge can then be removed and both pairs remain supported under the separate arrangement.
- The ring changes the dependencies. After three rounds the stocks are (2,2,3,3), so all four can continue under the ring's maintenance certificate.

The ring also admits a four-step return from (0,0,1,2), with a different first successor. Thus coupling does not imply only a fixed-point outcome. The structure and time pattern both matter. The bridge example is temporary assistance followed by independent maintenance; calling it a permanently indivisible global attractor would misdescribe its dependence.

The [finite exploration](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/reproduction-exploration.json) enumerates 1,024 initial-vector/network pairs and records repeat detection. Its cycle catalogues are exploratory implementation evidence. The formally checked claims are the concrete histories and generic certificates named in the Lean source, not an unreported theorem proving every numerical basin classification.

## Selection of supporting rules is consequential activity

The installed arrangement is now part of state. A revision consumes one material unit; the following operation runs the newly installed return structure. Parts, material, stock changes and generation remain in the same history. The allotment of eleven units covers the maximum ten-unit support demand plus revision. Repeated arbitrary revisions within the three supported arrangements preserve the established region under this explicit provision. The available fourth arrangement removes all return routes and fails the maintenance certificate. `arrangement_certificate_iff` checks that correspondence; the classification is not a freely toggled label.

An alternative local selector maximizes next-round retained material. Under this **supplied one-period objective**, withdrawing all return routes is always at least as good and is strictly better than every supported arrangement at the warm initial state. Three such revisions leave all four activation stocks at zero while the material reserve has risen from eleven to forty-one and three cohort replacements have occurred. The selector succeeds at its declared objective while destroying these particular operative conditions. This is not a proof of optimality over a longer horizon or a verdict on all material conservation.

A further protocol gives two represented authorization roles a practical part in admission. When both are operative, a proposal to remove all support is redirected to the supported separate arrangement; the other three proposals pass. When either authorizer is below threshold, that filter does not operate. The fail-open behavior is a supplied design choice and a retained vulnerability, not a universal law of institutions.

Starting from the established region, the accepted activity replenishes the authorization stocks. `admission_reproduced` and `arbitrary_proposals_preserve_warm_regime` prove preservation against every sequence of proposals in this finite class, including state-dependent proposals to withdraw support. `proposal_history_actual` and `proposal_refines_actual_events` connect the admission history to executed revision and operation. This removes the earlier premise that proposals themselves must already be favorable: the implemented, materially supported admission relation does work.

The counterexample is equally important. At (1,3,1,3) the authorization condition is absent, even though a supported revision could re-establish the whole circuit. Repeated proposals to withdraw support now pass and eliminate the stocks. Retained material alone therefore does not establish operative correction; its distribution into the required relationships matters.

This is a conditional explanation of the *availability* of admission, not a derivation of legitimate authority or of its correct criteria from nothing. The recognition procedure, its faithful implementation when supported, and the available proposal class are still supplied. The protocol cannot adjudicate arbitrary new arrangements by inspecting their names, and cannot exclude a separate bypass that its process does not represent.

## Primary comparisons and author counterreview

[Sigmund, De Silva, Traulsen and Hauert (2010)](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~hauert/publications/reprints/sigmund_nature10.pdf), all three pages including the model and limitations, already treats the costly reproduction of sanctioning institutions and second-order free-riding. Its results depend on participation and punishment assumptions; it also explicitly leaves the actual setup of pool punishment unspecified. The present work therefore makes no novelty claim for endogenous institutional support. It addresses accounted realization, role timing, changed return structures and admission availability in a different conditional model. The paper's preference for sanctioning is not adopted as a normative conclusion here.

[Steel and Hordijk (2018)](https://arxiv.org/html/1801.03953v2), abstract, introduction and §1.1, distinguishes collective catalytic support from construction using available reactants, and notes that initial reactions may need to proceed without catalysis. This directly supports separating an architecture's self-maintenance from its establishment. Our activation-stock model is not a chemical RAF model, does not reproduce its algorithms, and supplies no new priority claim for autocatalysis or modular organization.

The substantive counterreview is:

1. **A generic invariant in disguise?** The certificate contains independently specified threshold and flow inequalities, and the preservation conclusion is derived. It does not derive those local production laws or prove their field correspondence.
2. **Carrier labels alone?** Actual beginning-stock parts, material conversion, generation changes and core events constrain turnover. Assembly details and faithful protocol copying remain abstract; earlier concrete command-transfer results are credited, not silently imported as this implementation.
3. **A permanent guardian moved into a function?** The authorizers' *availability* is reproduced by the circuit and can fail. Their recognition criterion and faithful implementation remain supplied. This closes one dependency and leaves another explicit; it does not solve every kind of endogenous capture.
4. **The regime is good because it persists?** No ethical conclusion is encoded. The same topology can support domination. Actual living effects and G0/J comparisons are needed to call its preservation warranted correction.
5. **The material-retention rival is irrational?** It optimizes a disclosed short-horizon objective. It refutes an unrestricted sufficiency claim for that objective, not every long-horizon institution or cooperative alternative.
6. **Does this explain whole populations?** Roles can represent differentiated activities at several scales, but the proof is not a refinement of the separately studied population model. Individual learning, candidate reproduction and a maintained admission criterion still need to be connected rather than equated.
