## The connection reconstructed

The activity being explained is a configuration changing its own conditions of continuation. In the original regulation model, a capture site in its damaged condition cannot capture incident support. That same damaged site participates in paid response formation. A formed response repairs it, and the repaired site can capture support that can contribute to subsequent reconstruction. The source, accessible stores, formed components, damaged site and changing reactions belong to one configuration; sensing and response are not added by an outside evaluator reading a neutral object.

The claim tested here is narrow and positive:

> Where an actual transformation restores the enabling relation it acts upon, the resulting configuration changes the availability of repeating that transformation and the possibilities of further activity. Repetition becomes applicable again when its relevant condition actually recurs, while other activity may continue throughout.

For the declared material law, the target is independently identified by damaged/intact capture behavior and its support flows. We do not identify a need with whatever happens to trigger a response. The damaged site's relation to capture, the response's actual paid effect, and the changed subsequent possibilities are all represented and checked. There is no global welfare score, service quota or new `Met` field.

This is an account of operational significance at the stated material scale: being damaged rather than intact changes what this organization can next do, and repair changes that difference. It does not by itself establish experience, all-things-considered ethical warrant, legitimate authority or a universal favorable direction. The specified chemistry remains a local model input whose place in the larger generative explanation is still to be investigated; being local input does not make it external to generative reality.

## Source configuration and differentiated activity

The [original seven-event account](../../2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-MATH.md) represents intact and damaged conditions of the same site through `C + D = N`. Damaged-site encounters participate in fabrication and repair. Repair removes a damaged-site reactant and restores capture access. Its already preserved source-exhaustion reversal shows that this local restoration does not guarantee net benefit to every other activity.

The new proofs use the existing sixteen-event [FormedSelection law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/FormedSelection.lean), connected to the original `Generative.Process` and `Trace` through [FormationRoot](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/FormationRoot.lean). It represents the site's two conditions by `intact`, an actual resident, precursor inventory, common fuel, a one-unit local fuel store, and finite incident source. It is a conditional successor development of the earlier regulation work, not an exact kinetic identification of the two laws.

Three linked changes are already operative:

1. **Repair:** an active resident, damaged site and common fuel produce an intact site and consume one common unit. The same intact condition prevents further repair and damage-triggered formation. When the local store is empty and source remains, capture becomes available.
2. **Capture:** the intact site transfers one incident source unit into its local store. The filled store prevents another immediate capture. This is a represented occupancy relation, not a declaration that every future activity is finished.
3. **Local use:** an admitted local copy consumes the local unit and actual precursor. If the site remains intact and source remains, capture becomes available again. It does not require another repair episode.

The common and local fuel stores remain different parts of this configuration. Captured local fuel funds local copying; it does not replenish the common account used for formation, association or repair. Consequently the result is bounded continued activity, not indefinite self-funding repair. Existing finite-stock termination results remain in force.

## An exact relation between recurrence and restoration

[GenerativeRepairApplicability](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeRepairApplicability.lean) proves, for every admitted finite root trace:

\[
\operatorname{bit}(\mathrm{intact}_{\mathrm{end}})+\#\mathrm{damage}
=\operatorname{bit}(\mathrm{intact}_{\mathrm{start}})+\#\mathrm{repair}.
\]

These are event counts and the site's actual binary condition, not a reward or value assigned to the process. The identity follows by checking the original sixteen event rules and composing their actual intermediate states.

Starting intact, later repairs cannot outnumber later damage events. More precisely, **an admitted new repair following a trace from an intact state requires a damage event in that very trace**. Resource spending or the passage of time alone does not provide the missing material condition. A completed restoration therefore supplies a physically anchored distinction between repair episodes without an added service identifier or completion classifier.

The theorem also keeps recurrence open. It does not say that the relation can never be damaged again, that a later repair would be redundant, or that completion of repair ends copying, capture or other activity. Fresh disturbance changes the configuration and can restore the conditions for the repair reaction, provided the requisite resident and common fuel remain.

The proof depends on the declared one-site transitions. A model with gradual degradation, multiple distinct sites, preventive reinforcement or alternative repair routes would need a corresponding finer relation. It would be a scope error to call every further maintenance activity redundant because this particular binary repair has finished.

## What the recipient-credit model omits

The earlier [completion-applicability proof](COMPLETION-APPLICABILITY-MECHANISM.md) correctly establishes that the selected successor's internal `help` flag cannot change under its existing event repertoire. Under the nondual reconstruction, that is a genuine limitation of that implementation. It does not establish that significance must arrive from an ontologically independent evaluator or that restored conditions cannot themselves change applicability.

The [new projection audit](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/RecipientContinuationProjection.lean) localizes a deeper omission in its recipient-identification component. The `patient` field is accumulated resource credit. It enters the transfer update and total-energy accounting, but does not represent a recipient's own capture sites, ongoing activity, restoration state or use of those resources.

For any nonnegative amount, translating the initial patient credit by that amount:

- leaves hardware, packets, escrow, phase and active status unchanged;
- leaves every event's admissibility unchanged;
- commutes with the complete state update;
- gives an exact correspondence of finite traces and the same feasible event words.

Every trace from a translated start has an actual unshifted counterpart with the same events. This is stronger than inspecting one branch or merely observing that the field is absent from one guard.

The proof also retains the difference the model does represent: patient balance and total energy increase by the translated amount. A positive shift changes the recorded patient outcome. Thus we do not claim identical full outcome profiles or identify different resource distributions. The independence concerns modeled control and continuation possibilities, not every observable consequence.

The mathematical conclusion is that cumulative recipient credit does not affect this local process's subsequent operational opportunities. It is not evidence that an actual recipient's conditions are independent of delivered support. The model was built to study paid target correspondence and influence; it does not include the recipient organization whose dependence, restoration and renewed activity would make the significance of that support visible. Calling the coordinate `patient` does not supply those omitted relations, and proving its invariance does not disprove them in a fuller account.

Likewise, the earlier ten-versus-twenty diagnostic changes the question binding supplied to an unchanged projection. Its decoder obstruction remains correct. It does not show that the whole relevant generative configuration contains two freely interchangeable meanings, or require a meaning-giver outside that configuration. Requests, capacities and the relations that make them relevant can themselves form within the larger process; their representation and genesis must be investigated at the appropriate scale.

## Substantive rivals and limits

The following cases preserve the relevant physical distinctions while restricting stronger interpretations:

| Case | What it preserves and what changes |
|---|---|
| Source exhaustion | The same repair restores the site, but no available source means no immediate fresh capture. Restoration is real; this consequence does not imply all associated activity benefits. |
| Competing fuel uses | Repair spends common fuel that could support other formation or copying. The earlier no-source comparison gives an actual adverse retention regime under the declared law. |
| Appropriation after wear | The original same-law inheritance path lets the alternative resident use a repaired site. Effective restoration does not establish exclusive entitlement or inevitable retention of the repairing type. |
| Renewed disturbance | Later damage restores the repair condition. A rule that treats every future repair as repetition of a finished episode would misdescribe this configuration. |
| Other ongoing dependencies | Capture and local reconstruction can continue while the site stays intact. Completion of one transformation does not eliminate all support dependence. |

These are changes in actual conditions or meaningful contrasts within the represented process. They do not freely toggle an unrelated value predicate and call that a refutation of the fuller relation. They also prevent the positive result from being inflated into universal beneficial selection, a complete account of concern or an instruction to stop regeneration.
