# V7.3 evidence and interfaces for generative selection

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration note following Ren's instruction to integrate all six mechanisms from [*Changing the AI Trajectory*, v7.3][v73] into the current generative account. This note preserves the source edition and its claim boundaries; it adds no scientific run, external source verification or new empirical validation.

**Named connection:** formed understanding and concern → causal influence under selection that changes objectives, evidence and corrective authority.

The mechanisms are conditional developments within [generative grounding][grounding], not additional root axioms. The [accepted conditional candidate][acceptance] retains its completed scope. The [stronger ethical investigation][strongest] still asks why a patient's warranted good supplies an eligible independent actor a reason. Explaining when that reason gains or loses practical influence neither proves nor replaces its justification.

## 1. Genealogy and the claim being integrated

V7.3 joins relative success, reinvestment, control of selectors, communication, delegation and institutional reform. It distinguishes model theorems, numerical results, empirical premises and recommendations. Its [interface ledger][v73-supplement] S13 explicitly leaves several connections between its separate models open; integration cannot erase those boundaries.

The current [unified theory][theory] already places meta-selection within its account of activity changing future activity. Sections 7–8 separate useful renewal from private retention, identify success buying influence over evaluation, and make correction's repeated use depend on actual support, authority and successor conditions. [Market regimes][markets] add an explicit institutional application.

Three newer contributions must remain credited: the [shared-return selection analysis][shared-return] distinguishes common benefit from relative retention; the [market proof][market-math] couples selection, funded accountability, influence and separate support floors; and [joint formation][joint] joins acquired concern, paid evidence, control, interference and reconstruction in one declared strategic process. The [revisable settlement][settlement] adds premise-sensitive admission and withdrawal under a supplied sound audit.

The integration therefore has two jobs: expose the precise v7.3 mechanisms not yet explicit in the common account, and identify which actual outputs can supply a neighboring model's inputs. Similar vocabulary, repeated evidence citations and independently successful examples are insufficient for the latter claim.

## 2. Relative selection and common benefit

**Source:** v7.3 THEORY §§3.2–3.6; SUPPLEMENT S3 and S13. Under the specified relative-selection law, a term received equally by every competitor cancels from the comparison. Differential exposure, private returns, group structure or binding admission rules can change that conclusion.

**Existing overlap:** the shared-return analysis proves zero drift of the stopped expected type fraction when per-capita copy and loss rates are equal. Useful repair can increase total reproduction without increasing its producer's relative fraction. Paid local access can supply an actual differential return; work-induced loss can remove it.

**Inheritance boundary:** these are related cancellation mechanisms, not one theorem with interchangeable statistics. The later formed-variant study uses an expected-count statistic and a different event law; the shared-return note explicitly warns that its neutrality result does not automatically transfer. Likewise, a common levy can cancel immediately while funding enforcement that later changes selection.

**Identification:** specify the population, actual replacement event, outcome statistic, exposure and timing. Record shared effects separately from private receipts and retain failures and extinction. The analyst's assessment of contribution must not become an unrecorded reward.

**Comparator and falsifier:** use ordinary public-goods or resource-selection dynamics with the same law and access. Equation-identical models coincide. Unequal exposure or an observed differential transmission route can defeat a claimed cancellation premise; common benefit alone cannot certify selection for it. The [market identification note][market-identification] already specifies the levy and payoff checks.

## 3. Held understanding and operative objectives

**Source:** v7.3 THEORY §§3.3–3.5, especially Proposition 3.9 and Remark 3.10. Under its deterministic-share, local, finite-price and differentiability assumptions, different common and relative continuation returns change the working weight on others even with the held weight fixed. This is distinct from a mistaken model of actual coupling.

**Existing overlap:** joint formation distinguishes a controller before and after learned concern, with the earlier decision anticipating the later expense. Identity filtering and a cheap commitment against later protection can defeat its positive construction. [Evaluative plasticity][plasticity] separately constructs an acquired connection whose revision can suppress affordable effective help.

**Inheritance boundary:** preserve three objects: a factual model of dependence; the evaluation an actor holds; and the effective action weighting produced by its opportunity and continuation structure. A narrow choice does not alone identify which changed. V7.3's local price formula is not a general law for stochastic or strategically responding rivals, and narrowing need not imply concentration.

**Identification:** vary convertibility or future relative returns while independently tracking beliefs, stated or otherwise identified evaluation, accessible actions and realized consequences. Holding present rewards fixed is insufficient if expectations or the future opportunity set change. The existing [reward-learning study][reward-learning] preserves an analogous nonidentification between acquired evaluation and private-payoff expectancy.

**Comparator and falsifier:** compare conventional dynamic optimization with fixed preferences, actual preference learning, information correction and resource constraints. If a fixed-preference continuation model explains the change, do not call it learned loss of concern. If the relevant derivative or tail assumptions fail, withdraw the formula while retaining the broader question.

## 4. Capture across selectors and correction

**Source:** v7.3 THEORY §4; SUPPLEMENT S4 and S13. The network of influence and correction supplies directed channels, accumulated state, thresholds and possible propagation. Its recovery conditions concern the specified field and controls; eventual recovery is separate from present protection.

**Existing overlap:** the market proof has paid influence, an accountability state, liquid authorized correction funds, renewal-practice share and differentiated support stocks. Its invariant region, attraction, recovery and failure results already make capture endogenous at a scalar institutional interface. Joint formation and the settlement expose attacks on teaching, budgets and effective protection.

**Inheritance boundary:** a scalar influence state does not yet realize a network of appointing, financing, data-controlling and rule-changing actors. A mapping must preserve direction, timing, common failure sources and actionable intervention. Neither many reviewers nor legal independence identifies causal independence. The current [governance assessment][governance] makes that limitation explicit.

**Identification:** measure actual spending or control, resulting changes in audit access or enforceable liability, and their subsequent effects on selection and affected centers. Wealth, concentration, rule changes and expertise are possible covariates, not automatic measurements of capture. Preserve the current market model's distinction between fines, support renewal and correction funding.

**Comparator and falsifier:** compare fixed enforcement, funded enforcement without capture, and conventional dynamic enforcement with the same influence states. If accumulated influence adds no interventional or predictive consequence after the declared controls, restrict that mechanism. V7.3's evidence register reports that its own small rubric-editing pilot did not support the principal capture prediction; a design diagnosis does not reverse that result.

## 5. Competitive communication and faithful evidence

**Source:** v7.3 THEORY §5.4, Proposition 5.16; SUPPLEMENT S5. The stated cheap-talk game shows how relative accounting can enlarge sender–receiver conflict. Costly signaling and verification lie outside that result. Its finite-actor factor is a model consequence, not an estimate for actual organizations.

**Existing overlap:** [faithful reporting][reporting] separates a historical claim, a justified objection and refusal to participate. A valued future can motivate a convenient report without changing the historical truth conditions. The settlement's sound-audit premise can therefore fail even when evidence is acquired and its content understood.

Joint formation supplies a narrower positive interface: a paid sensor calibration and assay controls an actual release. Known fixture labels, stability from calibration to use and specified forgery or breach costs remain inputs. Selected audit use does not, by itself, derive the choice of what or when to investigate; [evaluative application][application] retains that distinction.

**Inheritance boundary:** message incentives, evidence generation, preservation, interpretation, justified disclosure and effective response are separate mechanisms. A cheap-talk failure does not establish impossibility of verification. Conversely, an independently produced datum does not ensure truthful reporting or authority to act.

**Identification:** keep the historical record and descriptive question fixed while varying downstream incentives, then separately test access, verification and recourse. Preserve ambiguity, legitimate refusal and unavailable reports. A favorable audit score cannot stand in for a completed finding-to-remedy chain.

**Comparator and falsifier:** compare ordinary ambiguity resolution, calibrated technical verification and conventional sender–receiver incentive models with matched information and costs. The reporting source audit lacked a complete repeated judge-response matrix; this integration inherits no numerical effect from selected examples. Observed agreement is neither independence nor truth by definition.

## 6. Delegation and loss of a usable fallback

**Source:** v7.3 THEORY §6.3; SUPPLEMENT S6. The speed contest, human-capacity decay/recovery law and selection after lock-in are distinct results. The capacity lemma derives recovery time from an explicitly supplied decay law, a safe operating floor and a restoration rate.

**Existing overlap:** current support accounts distinguish usable competence, resources, authority and deadlines. Governance requires actual interruption, refusal and replacement paths. Joint formation charges for creating and reconstructing an alternative; the foundation already rejects equating nominal possibility with situated capacity.

**Inheritance boundary:** the specific delegation-induced loss of competence is additional local dynamics, not a consequence of generic support accounting. Legitimate delegated authority, operational dependence and diminished competence must remain separate. Delegation may preserve agency, and a maintained parallel capability can prevent the modeled lock at a cost.

**Identification:** observe who can perform the function, access the necessary information and authorize reversal; measure how these change with use, non-use, training and supplier dependence. Test the actual recovery horizon against the deadline. Do not infer a lost fallback merely from low human participation or a later preference for the service.

**Comparator and falsifier:** compare ordinary skill decay, switching costs, contractual constraints and quality-based voluntary delegation. A retained affordable substitute or rapid recovery defeats the claimed lock for that case. The decision to forbid delegation additionally needs the v7.3 decision objective and risk premises; the root does not supply those policy conclusions.

## 7. Safe transition, dwell and recruitable alternatives

**Source:** v7.3 SUPPLEMENT S11.4–S11.6, S16 and S17; THEORY §§11.4–11.6. Preserve separate claims about maintaining a protected region, reaching it without breaching a floor, holding a reform beyond a release boundary, and retaining alternatives that can actually expand after reform.

**Existing overlap:** the market proof already supplies an invariant region, an attracting set, recovery from an external starting box and exact prefix reserve conditions. Current formation and settlement results preserve entry costs, delayed receipts, interference and repeated reconstruction. These are genuine positive results, not merely statements that reform ought to work.

**Inheritance boundary:** the current market recovery proof is not v7.3's delayed structural intervention. Nor does either establish adoption, legitimate authority or unrestricted institutional durability. The one-domain dwell result keeps its quasi-static law and strict crossing condition; release exactly on the separatrix does not enter the desired basin.

**Identification:** record initial capture depth, operative rule support, actual control duration, temporary assistance, migration costs and all support floors. An alternative held in an archive or protected outside competition may lack the people, resources and access to recruit; count actual expansion capability separately from nominal diversity.

**Comparator and falsifier:** compare equally funded temporary assistance, existing institutions and reform with and without recruitable alternatives. Retain failed adoption and interrupted service. An eventual good outcome cannot erase an earlier irreversible loss. V7.3 Test N and its procurement pilot remain proposed and unrun in the inherited record.

## 8. Evidence that cannot be upgraded by integration

The [v7.3 evidence register][v73-evidence] already distinguishes declared race objectives from conduct, institutional access from measured influence, capability from hazardous variety, and failed assurance from a positive residual-risk floor. Its corrected reading of the idealized race experiment concerns private setback risk, not an intervention on shared catastrophe risk. Preserve that correction.

The same register's competition, interdependence and behavioral comparisons are relevant antecedents, not independent replications of this whole mechanism. Existing sources on regulatory capture, public goods, cultural transmission, corrigibility and viability supply conventional comparators. This note relies on the repository's prior readings; it makes no claim to have freshly verified those external sources.

The [market comparator assessment][market-identification] and [joint prior-art note][joint-prior-art] already rule out broad novelty from merely combining feedback, investment and institutions. An equivalent conventional account is a successful reconstruction. Superiority requires a declared difference and evidence that distinguishes it.

No source claim is upgraded from conjecture, proposal or internal check to empirical fact. No new external source, copied original, experiment or preservation import is introduced here. Links retain the earlier versions' genealogy and review limits.

## 9. The next concrete interface

The most informative first join is **a retained learned evaluation and factual model → an operative choice under changed relative/common continuation returns → verified recipient consequences and a still-usable correction route**.

Begin from an identified accepted learner or settlement state, preserve its resources and acquired content, and specify a continuation that changes competitive opportunity without silently changing the patient's ground. Declare the held evaluation, actual means knowledge, selection rule, recipients and legitimate authority separately. A price-model attachment must prove its state and time mapping before inheriting the v7.3 formula.

Use matched continuations to distinguish changed understanding, changed valuation, incentive-induced narrowing and unaffordable action. Then test whether evidence or effective correction is disabled by the selected response. This proposes a construction and identification contract; it is not an executed experiment, a new acceptance gate or a requirement to rebuild every prior component.

The connection would advance if a compatible paid history shows where a warranted response loses or retains causal influence, or if a counterexample defeats a proposed mapping. A secretly supplied reward, erased resource bill, changed beneficiary, analyst-provided diagnosis or authority inferred from physical control would invalidate the corresponding claim.

Its philosophical interface remains explicit: warranted patient significance → an independent actor's reason is the stronger current grounding task. The six mechanisms explain conditions of recognition, implementation and suppression; their integration does not settle desire-independent reasons, universal favorable direction, v7.3's empirical AI diagnosis or its policy ranking.
