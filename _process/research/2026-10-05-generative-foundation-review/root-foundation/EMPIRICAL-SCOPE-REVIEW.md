2. Each cross-domain mapping identifies the measured relation and the theoretical input it does or does not instantiate. Changed response is not an identified changed updater; target-liked selection is not verified recipient welfare or uniquely acquired valuation.
3. Uncertain primary outcomes, adverse cases and exact conventional equality remain visible. Secondary or selected findings cannot silently replace a fixed primary contrast.

5. Each substantive inference names a comparison, measurement failure or counterexample that could revise its interpretation. A mathematically equivalent conventional description is not treated as an empirically defeated rival.
6. The final scope statement retains the full organization → significance → learning and selection over selectors → concern and wisdom task. It may restrict unsupported prevalence or unique-mechanism claims; it may not delete a central derivation or rename an assumed capacity as derived.

These are finite review criteria for this ledger, not new foundation requirements or demands for another experiment.
