# How relationships and variations acquire warrant

8 October 2026. AI-authored affirmative investigation of Ren's question: why do the identified relationships and admitted variations correctly characterize actual systems throughout the intended scope? This advances the [systems account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION.md) under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Its contribution is a substantive, claim-specific account of correspondence and variation warrant. It adds no foundational axiom and does not announce completion of the independently stronger assumption-free foundation.

**The positive answer is that an account earns its claims through the actual formation, operation and supported transformation of the organization it describes. Different claims require different coverage. An exhibited joint realization can ground an occurrence or capability claim; warranted constraints can ground a limit across a wider range; a complete characterization of possibilities requires both directions. Exact knowledge of every admissible state is therefore not a prerequisite for every justified claim.**

**正面回答：关系与变化的根据来自所研究组织的实际形成、运作及有支持的改变。不同主张要求不同的覆盖：已实现的共同过程可以支持发生或能力主张；有根据的约束可以支持一定范围内的界限；完整的可能性刻画才要求两方面同时成立。因此，不必先掌握一切可接纳状态，才能对真实系统作出任何正确说明。** 但既往实现不自动保证现在仍可实现，局部论证也不自动成为完全无假设的普遍根基。

## 1. State the obligation at the right level

The [earlier argument](CONSTITUTIVE-WARRANT-ARGUMENT.md) already distinguishes supported instances, preservation requirements and the stronger universal warrant. It expressly excludes exhaustive inspection of every case, one architecture everywhere, unique notation and inevitable continuation as completion gates. The present correction does not attribute those claims to it.

Its final phrase, “the range of admissible variation,” nevertheless needs a more discriminating account. Four different achievements can be obscured by that one phrase:

1. Establishing that a particular organized history occurred.
2. Establishing a presently available continuation under identified support.
3. Establishing a restriction shared by the relevant alternatives.
4. Exhaustively identifying which alternatives are possible and which are impossible.

An account can earn the first three to specified extents without having completed the fourth. Conversely, a few realized examples do not justify presenting the fourth as complete. The exact-adequacy interface is one useful mathematical contract; it is a stronger contract than many actual explanations need.

The [canonical scope](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) already allows different local laws, boundaries and empirical inputs. Its generality concerns common explanatory content across formation, maintenance, transformation and dissolution. It does not grant every proposed local account universal validity. The stronger request still concerns warrant for the foundation's own commitments; this distinction cannot be used to move those commitments into an unexamined list of local data.

## 2. Why formation and operation supply correspondence

The relation between an account and its target can itself be followed within the same investigated history. Acquisition, recording, comparison and revision are organized activities with consequences. This gives the correspondence question a concrete subject: which conditions produced a distinction in the inquiry, which distinction was retained, and which subsequent operation used it?

Consider the [documented controller](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-EVIDENCE.md#1-documented-acquisition-retention-and-issued-action). The preserved program retrieves a reading, passes the reading and the retained integral into a calculation, retains the updated integral and issues the resulting command. The variable name alone establishes none of this. The source order identifies the proposed operation; archive provenance connects that source to the reported run; reconstruction from the recorded inputs checks its consequences over the recorded updates. These connections constrain one another. A purported interpretation in which the integral is merely decorative must account for its documented calculation and consumption, rather than matching only a final output.

This is a reason for accepting the attribution, not merely a instruction to “test more.” Its warrant comes from a specific explanatory achievement: the account reconstructs the same differentiated operations that form and use the retained quantity and recovers their independently recorded consequences. The participating relation is thereby identified through more than an output resemblance. The existing evidence assessment states the retained source-fidelity boundary and does not certify unrecorded firmware behavior, underlying sensor truth or delivered heat.

A correlated external record might still predict the command. Successful prediction alone gives that record a different status from the integral that the documented program passes to its next calculation. A replacement explanation needs to preserve the operative evidence, show why that evidence has been misinterpreted, or provide a different supported implementation. Merely inventing a second predictor does not erase the first mechanism. Conversely, evidence of a bypass, overwrite or different executed program would change the attribution. These are substantive possible corrections because they concern the claimed contribution, not the terminology used to name it.

This grounding is relational throughout. The acquired reading, the sensor, the program and the archival inquiry are differentiated through their operations and connections. Their distinction does not put a complete observer on one side and an independently complete world on the other. Nor does belonging to one unfolding make their claims automatically accurate. A sensor can drift and a record can be mistranscribed within that unfolding; identifying the actual relation includes investigating those failures.

## 3. Admit joint variations through their formation

A state description often gives several coordinates: a configuration, a reading, a retained quantity and a support condition. Treating those coordinates as freely recombinable asserts something about the organization. It needs a reason. A preparation can impose a correlation, consume the support needed for a later action, or alter what an instrument reports. The [system-individuation result](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-FORMAL.md) shows why the resulting joint domain matters for explanatory supports.

The constructive source of an admissible variation is therefore a **joint preparation and continuation**. Follow how the relevant arrangement is reached, what is retained at the boundary, and which operations remain supported. Include the changes made by measurement or intervention. Separately obtainable parts do not yet supply a jointly obtainable arrangement, and two histories cannot be joined merely because their selected labels agree.

The resulting warrant has three useful forms:

| Ground | What it supports | Additional coverage needed for a stronger claim |
|---|---|---|
| A documented performed history | The organization and operation occurred together on that occasion, within the evidence's fidelity | Repetition now or elsewhere needs a justified transfer of the relevant conditions |
| A supported construction or operative mechanism | The specified continuation is feasible in the regime covered by that construction or mechanism | Physical implementation, available support and regime membership must be established in an actual application |
| A justified constraint on all alternatives in the claim's range | The alternatives ruled out by that constraint are excluded within that range | Exhaustive possibility identification also needs realizations for what the account declares possible |

A past success is especially easy to overextend. The preparation may have exhausted a reagent, destroyed a connection, depended on a vanished support, or changed through the very operation being tested. Its direct warrant is historical. A claim of current capability must connect the current organization to the enabling relations demonstrated by the earlier run. This transfer can itself be supported through maintained structure, measured ranges and a warranted mechanism; it cannot be supplied merely by repeating the same system name.

Failure also has a scope. A failed attempt may establish that this attempt failed. To conclude that the target is impossible requires showing why relevant alternatives are covered by the failure argument. A mechanism-based impossibility result can supply that coverage without trying every alternative; one unsuccessful trial ordinarily cannot.

## 4. Use partial coverage without presenting it as exact knowledge

An inquiry can have positively supported realizations and a broader, conservatively described range that retains all alternatives relevant to the stated claim. Between them lie possibilities whose status is unsettled. The two ranges perform different explanatory work.

The positive range supports existence claims and actual contrasts. If two realized situations have the same selected readings and different relevant continuations, that pair directly challenges the proposed sufficiency of those readings. No exhaustive catalogue is needed to expose that loss.

The conservative range supports a universal conclusion only when its coverage has a warrant. If the relevant constraint holds even throughout that broader range, unresolved detail inside it need not defeat the conclusion. A failed test within the broader range can instead arise from an unrealizable case retained for caution; that failure does not establish an actual counterexample. A total possibility claim requires a tighter account connecting feasibility and exclusion.

Here “conservative” has content: the investigation must explain why it has not excluded an actual alternative relevant to the claim. Bounds from material availability, demonstrated program branches, measured containing intervals or other established relations can do this at a declared scope. Choosing a convenient large set is not enough when its interpretation or coverage is ungrounded. Enlarging a representation also does not by itself make it an actual physical system.

This division lets an explanation remain informative under partial identification. It can say what was realized, what is supported now, what is excluded by an established constraint, and what remains unsettled. The unresolved alternatives remain part of the account. They do not become impossible through omission or possible merely because a model can name them.

These are the philosophical meanings of claim-specific coverage, not a separately claimed new mathematical proof. The companion formal work for this round is responsible for checking the exact inference rules and retaining the coverage conditions in their signatures. Formal transport earns no empirical warrant simply by introducing a predicate called “admitted.”

## 5. Why this supports general explanatory content

The account has general reach because its obligations follow the content of the explanatory claims, while its grounds are supplied by the actual organization under investigation:

- An occurrence claim concerns an actual realization and must retain the relations by which that realization is identified.
- A capability claim concerns an available supported continuation and must retain the conditions on which availability depends.
- A constitution claim concerns an operative contribution to the organization and must address formation and use evidence, rather than relying only on a substitute predictor.
- A necessity or exclusion claim concerns its entire stated range and needs coverage of that range.
- A continuity claim concerns which organization or history continues through the change and must preserve the continuity it asserts.

This generality does not require the same component types, event law or boundary in every case. A chemical preparation, an acquired command calculation and a social procedure can answer these questions through different actual relations. A static organization can have a supported configuration without changing during the interval of inquiry. A one-use organization can genuinely realize a capacity and then dissolve. An externally maintained organization remains an actual organization; its sustaining relations belong in the explanation where relevant.

Nor is this only a demand that a preferred vocabulary be used faithfully. It tells us what specific extra support a proposed inference needs and when less than an exact model is enough. The supplied construction can replace an independently postulated feasibility assertion; an operation trace can distinguish participation from a surrogate correlation; a containing bound can support a conclusion while leaving the exact domain unknown; a shared compatible continuation can replace arbitrary concatenation. These changes can alter the account's conclusions.

The core and branches therefore support one another without an automatic circular certification. Branches contribute occurrences, operations, constraints and failures that were not established by classifying them as generative. The core connects the forms of inference they justify and exposes omitted dependencies. When a branch defeats a proposed attribution or coverage claim, the common articulation must accommodate that result. Coherence among statements alone would not supply these relations.

An equally adequate global or atemporal account can retain the same organization, admissible continuations and grounds. That counts as explanatory agreement at this level. Exclusive priority of one sequential notation is not part of the argument.

## 6. Substantive rivals and their outcomes

**A merely correlated surrogate.** It may supply a sufficient predictor on a restricted domain. The constitution claim still requires the documented operative contribution. When the two accounts differ under a jointly supported reconfiguration, that comparison can discriminate them. When available evidence cannot discriminate them, retain the unresolved attribution rather than inventing a preferred cause.

**An impossible coordinate intervention.** A proposed independent change can destroy the very preparation whose capabilities are being explained. The comparison must include that disturbance. Its failure may concern feasibility of the intervention, not absence of the proposed relation. This does not protect the relation from criticism: bypass, replacement or another supported operation may test the same attribution differently.

**A wider domain containing unsupported cases.** It can be useful for a conservative proof if actual coverage is warranted. A failure in that enlarged domain is not automatically an actual failure. The direction of the claim determines how the extra cases matter.

**A record-only explanation.** It can match observed outputs and still leave documented construction and operation unexplained. That mismatch is an explanatory cost. If the purported operative evidence is itself erroneous, however, the record-only account may have identified a real defect; the richer account gets no exemption merely by calling its relations constitutive.

**The whole certifies all of its own descriptions.** Actual inquiry belongs to the same unfolding, but an erroneous description belongs to it too. Inclusion explains where inquiry occurs; discriminating formation, uptake and correction explains why some particular attributions have better grounds. The first does not replace the second.

**Every relation is brute, so no common account is possible.** This challenge must address the documented common forms of composition, supported operation and correction. Existing constructions and actual cases warrant more than arbitrary labels. They do not, however, prove that every reality must contain the same structures or that all substantive laws have now been grounded.

## 7. What is resolved, and the remaining stronger claim

The question now has an affirmative answer at the level of actual explanatory warrant. Correct characterization is earned by grounded relations and the coverage appropriate to the claim. Actual participation is supported through formation and use; admissible variation is supported through jointly realizable preparation and continuation; generalization uses an identified mechanism or justified containing constraints; partial knowledge can support real conclusions without pretending to be exact knowledge. These connections replace an undifferentiated request for “the correspondence” with identifiable grounds that can be supplied and tested.

This is more than an assurance that another study might someday find a correspondence. Existing controller and biological investigations already supply relevant grounds at their declared scopes, as assessed in the [evidence record](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-EVIDENCE.md). The present argument does not rerun them or extend their conclusions. In particular, related biological assays do not become one fully tracked culture, and documented command calculations do not become a universal physical-temperature model.

The argument also does not establish the stronger conclusion that all constitutive attributions, possibility ranges and acts of recognition throughout reality are already warranted. Its common inferential obligations are general; the positive actual content still depends on grounds that are identified in particular or generally established mechanisms. Under the independently stronger [assumption-free directive](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ASSUMPTION-FREE-FOUNDATION.md), the uncompleted obligation is to justify the root's substantive universal commitments and the inferential or recognitional warrant on which their application depends. The current account does not derive all of that from nothing, and it supplies no impossibility proof.

There is a useful distinction here. An established relation does not become an arbitrary assumption merely because later inquiry could correct it. Fallibility and lack of a ground are different conditions. Conversely, a well-designed correction procedure is not itself an assumption-free guarantee of all its starting identifications. The present contribution earns specified relations and inferences while keeping that stronger question intact.

**中文结论。** 本轮推进的是“何以有根据地说明”：沿形成与实际运用确定参与关系，沿共同准备与接续确定可支持的变化，按主张所需的范围证明其覆盖。局部实现在其范围内有正面根据；受支持的界限也可在精确可能性尚未全知时产生一般结论。先前的“可接纳范围”不应成为所有主张一律等待完整穷尽的门槛。仍须保留历史实现与当前能力、预测充分与构成参与、边界包含与精确等价的区别。这个说明没有把可修正性说成没有根据，也没有把一般检验方法宣告为已完成的无假设普遍根基。
