## Mathematical scope

The contract's private-retention argument is valid under its full hypothesis: equal initial accessible-state laws and equal conditional observation, update and retention laws at every reachable history imply equal finite retention-history laws. The saved endpoint projection alone does not establish that hypothesis for arbitrary selectors. In particular, different retained memories can be used by a newly supplied selector. The contract correctly distinguishes this argument from its narrower concrete resource comparison and makes no new Lean-proof claim.

The copying bound is necessary under the declared consumption-only accounting: after recipient operation and preservation of its floor, completed copies require both components and charged resources. It does not establish an enabled assembly path, usable donor memory, successful copying or a scheduler that realizes capacity. The stated affordability condition and caveats for setup, partial failure and intermediate returns are necessary.

The daughter-count identity is exact. Under the stated nonnegative integer bounds, the final faithful daughter count is `C_F - L_FU + L_UF` and the total remains `C_F + C_U > 0`. Comparing their ratio with the initial faithful share gives precisely `(1-p) C_F - p C_U - L_FU + L_UF > 0`. These must be actual daughters of an explicit generation. A ratio of expected counts is not an expected realized frequency, and the identity supplies neither a reproduction law nor multigeneration persistence.

## The substantive interfaces remain open

The current predecessor chooses evidence routing and write behavior through an external `arm` argument. Its two-bit `q` payload is not a heritable encoding of that mechanism. The contract appropriately requires formation of a retained module before the operations it governs, correspondence to the existing arms, and a generic copier that also copies suppressing or mistaken templates. Otherwise the desired phenotype would be hidden in a label or in the copier.

The faithful endpoint and matched private-zero-proxy endpoint both have recipient stock ten, despite different recipient estimates. Copying access based only on that stock cannot certify faithful evidence. The contract retains equal initial access and requires any later distinction to arise through paid operation or newly constructed evidence. Local transmission could favor recipient-supporting machinery over suppression without uniquely selecting faithful learning; those are separate claims.

The handoff has no unused action packet, free destination components, recipient maintenance-surplus proof or perpetual donor-access license. The two existing material slots are occupied actor backing. The proposed continuation must supply these missing interfaces with their own accounts and intermediate failure states. Its no-private-benefit condition also covers donor retention: paying an actor's otherwise necessary upkeep would be an indirect benefit even without crediting its fuel. Charging common donor retention to the actor can define a matched comparison, but refusal and cheaper omission must remain available before any entry or retention conclusion.

A successor begins with the same recipient and target-law contract. Copied actor memory is not automatically valid for a new carrier. Public pooling, unrelated template uptake, cheap gates, copying errors, exhausted recipient surplus, target changes and finite source loss remain required countercases. A single funded successor action does not establish indefinite renewal or justified concern. The copying chemistry, locality and initial participation remain supplied even if the proposed finite construction succeeds.

## Disposition

The requested lineage clarification, explicit count bounds and saved-state wording have been incorporated. No blocking error remains for the package's **prospective-contract and existing-evidence-audit status**. Its three implementation gates remain unmet: physically formed route alternatives, paid copying followed by actual successor use, and an explicit finite variation/transmission process. The next implementation should report failures at those gates rather than promote this review into acceptance of a transmission result.
