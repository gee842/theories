## Mathematical reconciliation

Let $q$ denote the represented common organization, including whatever operational relations, support and retained differences matter to the question. A justified articulation $q\mapsto(x,r)$ can expose differentiated operation and regulation. It must preserve relevant continuations, or retain the omitted alternatives. The new `no_closed_label_update` theorem checks the obstruction: if two full states have the same label but different next labels, there is no autonomous deterministic update on that label that represents both.

Within a sufficient articulation, choose a finite parameterization $r=w$ and explicitly supply a rule law $G$. Then $(x',w')=(F(x,w),G(x,w))$ has that law as its second coordinate; `joint_rule_coordinate` checks the exact embedding. This is the valid connection between generic joint dynamics and particular weight updates. It does not imply that every selection relation is a scalar score or that every changed organization can be represented by fixed proxy coordinates.

To identify the August covariance law with the October slow law, distinguish three steps. First choose an actual weight-update mechanism. Second justify its covariance or linear approximation. Third justify averaging over faster activity if making the slow reduction. Use $a$ for the August scalar gain and $r$ for the October rule vector; their shared historical symbol $\lambda$ does not identify them. Setting a coordinate update proportional to $Cw$ embeds a chosen law; it supplies neither an attracting compact rule region nor the stationary distribution required by the averaged account.

The following dispositions govern current reuse.

| Older claim or usage | Disposition and corrected scope |
|---|---|
| A meta-attractor necessarily turns means into ends and progressively eliminates alternatives | **Restrict to a proposed destructive subtype.** Neutral recursive reproduction need not do this. Terminalization, capture and loss of effective revision require their own evidence. |
| A scalar-valued function $\sigma:X\to\mathbb R$ makes $\partial F/\partial\sigma$ an ordinary scalar derivative | **Repair the representation.** Scalar output is not a one-dimensional space of rules. Supply a scalar parameter family or appropriate function-space differential before using that derivative. |
| Positive cross-product $\gamma$ explains formation/deepening and can be read as both links being positive | **Reject as a sufficient criterion.** Two negative links also have positive product. With identical positive off-diagonal coefficients $1/4$, diagonal coefficients $1/4$ halve the equal-coordinate mode, while $7/4$ double it. The full update matters. These are checked discrete algebraic comparisons, not new continuous-time stability theorems. |
| $\Delta w_i=\eta E[p_iR]$ is a Price/covariance selection law | **Separate supplied dynamics from an identity.** The raw moment includes $E[p_i]E[R]$. A constant proxy and constant reward can give raw moment 2 and covariance 0. An explicitly centered or covariance-consuming update is an additional mechanism, not a consequence of calling the rule Price-style. |
| $\dot w=aCw$ follows generally, with $w(t)=e^{aCt}w(0)$ and inevitable leading-mode dominance | **Restrict.** The exponential formula needs a fixed coefficient matrix; the derivation additionally needs its centering and linearization conditions. Directional dominance needs a relevant spectral gap and a nonzero initial component. The checked diagonal discrete comparison preserves a missing dominant component at every finite time. It does not constitute a Lean migration of the historical ODE claim. |
| Covariance supplies nonnegative proxy weights and a normalized terminal-value vector | **Reject without additional law.** Covariance may have negative off-diagonal entries. The checked covariance of two centered observations is `[[1, -2], [-2, 4]]`; its quadratic form is a square, yet an Euler update from positive weights can make one negative. Normalization, projection or a multiplicative rule changes the dynamics and must be stated. |
| Instantaneous covariance/eigeninformation suffices when the population changes | **Reject the shortcut.** The checked pair of symmetric matrices has order-dependent products. A changing matrix requires its changing history; instantaneous quantities alone are not the constant-matrix solution. |
| Starting with $w_i=1/k$, the exact initial rate equals the covariance row sum | **Restore scale.** The gain and $1/k$ matter. A common positive scale can preserve a ranking; it does not preserve numerical rates. A checked two-coordinate example distinguishes them. |
| A correlated CAOC block necessarily owns the leading mode, which is the system's terminal values | **Unestablished.** The entire covariance structure, measurement units, initial components and normalization matter. A mode of an assumed proxy process does not identify experienced concern, endorsement or ethical quality. No CAOC universality follows from the retained equations. |
| Adding lobby-ability/friction makes PDI a derived dominance exponent | **Treat as a proposed heuristic.** Define the variables, exclude division by zero, fix units and derive their entry into the actual update before making predictions. A row-sum statistic is not by definition an asymptotic exponent. |
| $e^{\lambda_1T}\le1/\rho$, $0<\rho<1$, ensures stability or decay | **Correct the interpretation.** The displayed allowance exceeds one. It is a finite-horizon amplification allowance under the specified constant linear model, not decay, basin depth, safety or an implementable governance certificate. The earlier opposite-sign versions remain errors; changing only the sign did not repair the prose. |
| Standard regularity and a small rate parameter automatically justify an averaged slow equation | **Incomplete.** State the fast long-run behavior, the averaging/tracking theorem and its hypotheses. Multiple fast regimes, retained history or changing support cannot simply be suppressed into a unique $\pi_r$. No averaging theorem is newly proved here. |
| A fixed-rule barrier or frozen-parameter Lyapunov calculation certifies the changing joint organization | **Incomplete certificate.** Specify boundary regularity, the joint invariant region and actual available update law. If a function depends on a changing rule, include that dependence in its total change. The historical barrier and composite-Lyapunov claims have not been migrated to Lean in this round. |
| A narrow optimizer necessarily outscores an equally resourced broader one | **Restrict to a comparison premise.** Equal resources do not alone identify the feasible processes, productive relations or costs. Optimizing the same supplied score over a superset of feasible options gives a weak optimum comparison, not automatic strict superiority of actual practices or inevitable population takeover. Relational production can make care, reliability or maintenance productive. The v7.3 conditional rule-capture formulation is retained; its §2.1 shortcut is not a universal foundation. |

**Primary-source check.** Price's [1972 extension](https://dynamics.org/Altenberg/LIBRARY/REPRINTS/Price_extension_AnnHumGenetLond.1972.pdf), pp.485–487, explicitly distinguishes product moments from covariance and mean products (A6), and the selection contribution from property change (A11–A15). Omitting the latter has conditions. This supports separating an accounting identity from a causal rule for institutional weights. The original [1970 publication record](https://www.nature.com/articles/227520a0) was checked for provenance; no inaccessible full-text claim is attributed to it.

Boyd and Lall's [autonomous linear systems notes](https://ee263.stanford.edu/lectures/lds.pdf) supply the standard fixed-matrix setting for the matrix exponential. They are background for restricting the old formula, not evidence that civilization follows that equation. ## Vocabulary that no longer carries hidden inferences

Use **meta-attractor** for the adopted neutral organization-through-reproduced-selection-conditions concept. Specify **destructive capture regime** where shrinking corrective possibilities is established. Use **revision-supporting regime** for an organization that actually preserves and uses the capacity to revise. A designed safe rule region is a separately specified mathematical target; calling it a meta-attractor does not prove it exists or that its safety criterion is justified.

The two old PDI expressions are not one variable: the August covariance/lobby/friction expression is a proposed growth-ranking statistic; the October expression combining observed decision weight and proxy–welfare correlation is a descriptive diagnostic. Neither is newly calibrated. Refer to each by its formula and source rather than an unqualified acronym. Likewise, source-specific CI/DE formulas and historical “capture percentages” have no unified numerical meaning established here. This review resolves their role in the present theory by excluding them from the foundation; calibration and equivalence are not silently declared complete.

The August cross-derivative product, the v7.3 contest loop gain and the initial core's recurrence threshold answer different questions. Preserve their model and symbol definitions. A threshold for retention is not automatically an attraction boundary; attraction is not automatically affordable recovery; correcting an output is not automatically the ability to revise its selection rule.

## The connected positive result

The inherited `FormedSelection` law contains no initially installed resident response. Damage, precursor, formation, association, repair and capture establish the local roles. `UpdaterFormation` already supplies a paid bridge and actual formation-to-replacement trace. This work credits that result and extends the previously supplied module-choice command through a retained consequence.

`CommonOrganization` adds a record of observed lifetime decreases, a paid receipt on each trial and two commands computed from available organization: `proposal` uses the record, and `orientation` uses the formed updater and current selector. Neither reads the hidden regime. Actual ineffective trials change lifetime; they consequently change the later module proposal. A directed acquired module then changes the allowed next response. A second reversal in the same history produces another successful response change.

The mathematical artifact demonstrates **one continuous, paid, relationally connected history**, not three diagrams joined by terminology. Formation uses the old event law. The bridge uses the old exact resource conversion. The later law adds one charged receipt per trial. The incoming source is 193 instead of the earlier witness's 97; this is an explicitly different initial stock, never a mid-history injection. Common initial energy is 211, with 4 formation expenditure, 1 bridge expenditure, 142 later expenditure and waste, and 64 final energy. Two material units remain accounted for. Generic step and trace ledgers and a finite-support trial bound accompany the witness.

The genuine comparators are a complete alternative receipt implementation that erases each acquired record, and an underfunded replacement state. The blind implementation reaches the same pre-replacement material organization, chooses the old updater, repeats the failed response and ends that comparison with zero operating fuel. The underfunded state cannot execute module formation. A correct record alone cannot finance a change.

This adds an actual join at a specified connection; it does not derive an unlimited hierarchy of learners. The more advanced testing, causal discovery, expanding access and method-generator results remain separate conditional realizations of further connections. Their contributions are mapped in the current account; no common physical law or compiled interoperation is claimed where it has not been built.

## Counterreview and remaining connection

**Does a supplied bridge manufacture the appearance of emergence?** It can if left implicit. Here the bridge is explicit, paid and inherited; its event changes the local organization law. The result joins two conditional descriptions through an actual admitted state, but does not derive molecular retooling. The account must not claim one unchanged chemical microphysics across the bridge. Removing the bridge leaves the original formation mechanism and does not magically produce the later updater.

**Is the recorder another unexplained controller?** Its interpretation and construction recipe remain supplied. Its use is nevertheless no longer an analyst's choice: the subsequent command consumes the retained consequence. This is an advance at a particular connection, not completion of apparatus formation. An equally effective fixed encompassing transition law represents these internal changes; that equivalence does not erase them or establish a novel computational class.

**Does regulation emerge only because a success label was inserted?** The inherited law connects matching response to support release and lifetime renewal. The new proposal reads a change in that material lifetime, not a supplied goodness score. However, why this lifetime is a reliable signal of the relevant relation is still an implementation/correspondence premise. No conclusion about concern or ethical correctness follows from it.

**Does the experiment choose its own favorable history?** The event schedule and candidate repertoire are supplied; eight successful trials finance the first adaptation, and six subsequent successes finance the next. The theorem establishes executability, not typicality, inevitability or autonomous scheduling. The general accounting and availability statements apply beyond that witness. A schedule or changing demand that outruns the available support can still fail.

**Has a finite example been called an attractor?** No. The joined model has no new basin or asymptotic certificate. It gives the previously missing record-to-updater handoff within actual formation and repeated revision. Existing recurrence proofs remain tied to their original models. A constructive recurrent regime requires additional support and dynamical evidence.

**Can revision preserve a harmful organization?** Yes. Causal effectiveness, revision availability and normative warrant are distinct questions within the same reality. This study does not reopen or replace the completed explicit-axiom ethical route; it supplies a mechanism that can implement differently warranted ends.

The current stopping boundary is specific: the account still supplies the apparatus making failures legible, the repertoire of replacements and the paid retooling relation. The next construction should let ongoing consequences form or revise one of those relationships while preserving actual ordinary activity, costs and failures. The existing method-formation and expanding-inquiry results are constraints and resources for that construction, not tasks to redo. These remaining interfaces are limitations of the joined representation, not evidence that regulation requires an outside cause or that other branches have never formed sensing or candidate-generating capacities. General foundation, empirical adequacy and full historical proof migration remain distinct unfinished questions.
