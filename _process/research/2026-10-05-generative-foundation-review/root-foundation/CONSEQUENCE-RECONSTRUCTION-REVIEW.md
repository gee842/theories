## A precise criterion, without an infinite controller regress

The relevant difference is functional and causal, not the distinction between a variable called state and one called rule. Any finite adaptive process can be represented by a stable encompassing transition law whose configuration includes its current organization. That representation does not establish that its operative organization is unchanged.

Identify the candidate component through what actually reads, assembles, transmits or reacts with it. For an explicitly delimited operation, let `K_m(·|c)` denote the response or reconstruction distribution induced by component configuration `m` under accessible current conditions `c`. This notation is an analytical description of the actual process, not a separate evaluator. A deterministic response is its degenerate case.

If two component configurations induce the same operation on every admitted condition in the claimed domain, their difference alone establishes no functional revision at that level. If their induced operations differ on a mutually admissible condition, the component can make an operative difference even though the encompassing law remains fixed. To claim an achieved formative connection, establish more than that counterfactual capacity:

1. Actual activity and its consequences form, preserve or change the component along the inherited history.
2. A later operation genuinely uses that reached component; no report label, unmodified parallel route or cached earlier result substitutes for it.
3. The changed operation reaches later activity under the stated realization conditions. Changed probabilities require probability-level wording; changed available operations, actual executions and universal guarantees have different scopes.

A matched intervention should preserve the relevant incoming evidence and execution conditions while interrupting the component's particular influence. It must not remove the whole capacity to act and then attribute the loss to a specific learned relation. Matching current conditions excludes the retained mediator being tested and need not freeze its downstream consequences. Some comparisons concern equivalent contexts rather than literally identical whole states; their correspondence must be justified.

This is an identification criterion for the named explanation, not a universal taxonomy of cognition. Fuel availability, template abundance, retained prediction and a formed edge can each participate in organization through different roles. A changed concentration is not disqualified because it is numerical; an edge is not established as functional because it is drawn as a graph. Conversely, merely naming an arbitrary stock a higher-level rule supplies no evidence of its role.

The criterion does not require every successful response to alter the apparatus, every learned component to change graph topology, or every revision law to have itself been learned in the same episode. Additional origin questions remain meaningful, but they must concern an identified component and a substantive explanatory target. Repeatedly declaring the encompassing law fixed does not demonstrate a descriptive regress.

## What would count as progress, and what would change the conclusion

The native comparison is worth pursuing because it uses an already formed state, original reaction laws, actual newly captured support and a physically operative future coupling. It can close a narrow consequence-to-reconstruction interface without another prescribed controller. It must be credited as a local articulation of existing copying and association mechanisms, not discovery of selection or acquisition of an ultimate purpose.

The decisive source checks are concrete. Copying must leave an available template after the original resident disappears. Association must actually depend on that template abundance. If instead later association ignored template counts, copied parts were unavailable for association, the nominal resident did not affect subsequent reactions, or an extra stock were inserted at handoff, the claimed bridge would fail or require revision.

The next stronger target should then be named at the appropriate level. Changing resident reconstruction does not by itself change a sensor's calibration, a reader's comparison rule or the continuation criterion. Conversely, the apparatus's fixed learned record does not erase native reconstruction below it. Meaningful rivals remain: a known fixed coupling that already works; retention of a once-useful component after conditions change; copying that narrows another type's access; correct organization that becomes unaffordable to use; materially retained but unread evidence; two differently named components that implement the same operation; and a productive local feedback that damages an affected process outside its own return. These rivals preserve actual processes and claims. They neither rely on freely toggled ethical predicates nor demand that every regime be favorable.

The completed studies establish real internal revision under stable laws. The proposed native calculation advances a different, narrower material link. Ethical warrant, empirical identification and the full no-assumptions foundation remain distinct further explanations within the same unfolding.

### What the projection counterexample establishes

Within each retention arm, the alternative associations followed by capture reach equal total A/H counts, intactness, local/common/source amounts, expenditure, capacity and apparatus. The resident placements differ. The checker sums **all** native channel rates into the same projected target: the target increments total A, consumes the local unit, preserves common fuel and increments expenditure once. Its rate is one from the A-resident state and zero from the H-resident state in the retained-copy arm; the corresponding rates are two and zero after local loss. A bulk A copy does not enter that target class because it changes common rather than local support.

Therefore this particular projection, which forgets resident placement while retaining those totals, fails the equality of projected outgoing rates required for an exact Markov description valid at both reached full states. This is the usual strong projection requirement. The argument does not exclude a history-conditioned description, a description that retains the resident relation, or every distribution-specific weaker abstraction. Nor does it establish an ethical priority of A or a universal requirement to preserve the original composition.

### A post-replay hand strengthening of the full-race result

The following algebra was proposed and independently checked after the first replay. It was not a prospective execution claim and adds no simulation or theorem certificate. At an empty, intact site with local support zero, use the source law's unit association and capture coefficients, and a common positive coefficient `ε` for bulk copying, wear, damage and leakage. Put `n=a+h`, `P=M−n`, with `F>0`, `E≥0` and `n+1≤M`. The complete exit rate is

`T(n) = Fn + E + ε[Fn(M−n) + n + 1 + F]`.

Increasing free A by one, with the other stated quantities fixed, changes the very-next-event A-association probability by

`F D / [T(n) T(n+1)]`,

where

`D = Fh + E + ε{F[hP + a(n+1)] + h + 1 + F} > 0`.

To check it, subtract the exit rates to obtain `T(n+1)−T(n)=F+ε[F(M−2n−1)+1]`, then expand `(a+1)T(n)−aT(n+1)`. Both denominators are positive. Unlike the association-conditional expression, this statement includes `n=0`, because other native events still give a positive exit rate. The particular copy-versus-leak followed by resident-wear realization needs room for both the prior resident and the copy: its empty-baseline precursor count must satisfy `P≥2`. The reached comparison has `P=2`.

This strengthens the sign claim for one immediate event under this specified family of rates. Different rates, unavailable association support, intervening changes or a different future task require their own analysis. It does not turn the local increased association probability into an eventual-retention, fitness, learned-reader or ethical theorem.
