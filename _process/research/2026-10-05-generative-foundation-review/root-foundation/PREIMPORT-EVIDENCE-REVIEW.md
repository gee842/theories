## The actual information and physical boundary

The calibration theorem derives qualified-command soundness from the original recipient updater: faithful positive acquisition under a fixed recipient law can establish the encoded command. It does not infer soundness from any arbitrary state with `last=1`. An inactive controller can still retain that evidence. Physical read access, its bill and its association with the same recipient remain additional obligations.

The action model's `import_command` writes the recommended bit into the recipient component of `q` and sets `last=1`. That new qualification is certificate-derived. It is not a newly acquired positive outcome, and the old independently acquired content has been overwritten. Therefore **the pair `(q,last)` alone cannot identify independent acquisition after an import**. A later check can simply compare a publication with its own stored copy.

The proposed monitor should begin at the actual parent handoff **before any first import**, with provenance established by the retained predecessor history. It must either be a one-shot mechanism whose transitions cannot rearm independence from `last`, or preserve a separate physically acquired anchor with paid storage and read access. A new monitor installed after import cannot restore the lost acquisition merely by initializing an `unused` bit. Repeated paid reads of the same imported state do not create independent evidence.

The same meaning must be preserved throughout: two commands for one recipient, not two recipients. The original archive remains limited to its actual three records. A new read of `q,last` is a new interface to retained memory; it is not a fourth archive acquisition or evidence of current-world stability.

## The minimal warranted treatment of disagreement

Separate an **epistemic classification** from a **chosen action policy**. Agreement, disagreement and insufficient basis are observations of a relation between records. A policy can temporarily withhold a certificate-derived command when its required corroboration is absent. Withholding is a supplied response rule; it does not by itself identify which source is false or prove that taking no action best serves the recipient.

At the narrow admitted scope, there is a conditional stronger inference. If the original acquired anchor is sound for the same unchanged recipient law, and a reason recommends the other binary command, that reason is incompatible with this law. Rejecting that reason is then warranted by the anchor's causal acquisition premise, not by agreement as a universal value. A direct learned-register alternative may already act correctly in this scope; the monitor must preserve that comparator rather than claiming to invent the command.

Outside that scope, the minimal honest result is **unresolved applicability requiring new information**, not an unconditional veto by old memory. Correct new evidence can conflict with a stale anchor. An externally supplied `fresh_correct` flag, a hidden target orientation or a label naming the intervention would merely reinstall the answer being sought. Calling a publication new or authentic also does not establish that its content is accurate or temporally applicable.

A genuine changed-world recovery must obtain evidence through an actual informative route. Under the existing no-false-positive law, an actually acquired positive receipt can warrant compatibility at that exposure. A nonreceipt alone remains ambiguous between a wrong command and unavailable opportunity. Paid controlled probing could potentially discriminate with additional assumptions and observations, but its actuation, permission, risks, recording capacity, resources and temporal scope must be explicit. The current exhausted archive and qualification-preserving updater do not implement that recovery merely by being reread.

Accordingly, a first material study may accept a **stable-law pre-import guard**, provided it also demonstrates the stale-anchor refusal countermodel and does not present refusal as resolved revision. The next bridge must then be actual new acquisition and warranted revision. An abstract fresh-evidence countermodel is useful to refute a universal veto; it is not a constructed new evidence source.
