# What forming an evaluative connection would establish

6 October 2026. AI-authored prospective scope and identification assessment under [generative grounding](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). This assesses the proposed finite plasticity construction before its outcomes. The protocol is being developed in the existing evaluation experiment's `plasticity/` directory. No numerical result, formal acceptance, empirical validation or ethical application is claimed here. This note neither changes the five completion conditions nor reopens an accepted local result.

The named transition is **paid native and recipient events → retained paired evidence → formation of signed input-to-evaluation connections → a later selected, paid recipient action**. The explanatory aim is to make the *instance and sign of the evaluative connection* consequences of an actual history, instead of installing the completed positive transfer route.

## 1. The input this construction can replace

[Architecture formation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION.md) already forms direct and identity-filtered paths through local fabrication and bonding. It explains why completed routing alternatives become available under its component and guide laws. [Architecture learning](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) then connects a reached graph to paid recipient-specific correction. These are substantial accepted constructions. Neither must be repeated merely to introduce another link.

[Evaluative uptake](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-UPTAKE.md) acquires a finite native consequence estimate from zero, then contrasts shared and identity-filtered evaluative routes at equal factual information. Its update, cross-center transfer, sign and scale are explicit recipes. The current proposal works backward from that last input: **can paired events produce physical connections whose subsequent transmission gives the recipient feature positive, negative or absent influence?**

The older [concern learning account, §§4–6](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/learning-formal.md#4-learning-recognition-and-transferable-correspondence-from-zero) already gives a correspondence recurrence and a conditional self-context model-selection argument. The [conceptual account, §5](../../2026-10-05-concern-theory/conceptual-evidence.md#5-two-elementary-formal-results-that-expose-rather-than-conceal-the-missing-bridges) already gives an associative value learner and the familiar-recipient underidentification example. Another unbacked coefficient update or proof that unseen recipients are underdetermined would duplicate those results. The new contribution must be the actual event, memory, material and action interface.

| A successful new construction would establish | What remains an input or separate claim |
|---|---|
| Previously absent link instances are assembled from parts after paid paired acquisitions. | Compatible endpoints, the local plasticity recipe and sufficient initial materials. |
| Which signed connections form depends on the acquired pairs. | Native feature coding, sign convention, temporal pairing window and what qualifies a pair. |
| The formed connections carry a recipient input into an existing evaluator. | The generic transmission and evaluation law, its scale, and the identity of the recipient input. |
| Equal native learning and factual knowledge can lead to different recipient-directed choices through those connections. | Accurate factual sensing, action efficacy, prices, authority and opportunity. |
| A later action pays its actual bills and has a separately addressed consequence. | Whether that consequence benefits a patient and whether acting is warranted. |

This is formation of an evaluative connection under a plasticity law, not formation of the entire capacity for plasticity. Encoding a rule called “become shared” as the link constructor would leave the intended input supplied. Conversely, a local coincidence/anti-coincidence rule with declared endpoint functions can give a genuine conditional construction even though its chemistry and availability remain assumptions.

## 2. The acquired pair must come from the process

Each native and recipient label must originate in a defined interval of the corresponding actual operation. A resource shortfall can be measured relative to the operation's payment and return; acquisition and write fees still belong in the whole-history ledger. A native loss must not be manufactured by destroying unused source, and a recipient return must not silently become actor income. Raw apparatus observations, learned estimates, teaching labels and the analyst's audit trace are different objects.

Pairing requires actual retained capacity. Specify which bit waits for the other, its target/episode binding, how an absent or interrupted observation is represented, and what pays for retention and commit. An inaccessible bit is not zero. The physical state must contain enough information for every later update; the update cannot consult a complete external trace after its apparatus record was lost. Outcome-dependent write failures and unmatched pairing must remain visible rather than becoming an apparently well-calibrated sample.

The two-unit paired recipient operation is a distinct modality from the inherited factual command's ten-unit efficacy assay. Its acquired bit does not inherit that command's calibration merely because both are binary. Source ownership, the physical relation being measured and the later input-feature interpretation must be stated separately. Two positive parallel connections also supply a specific multiplicity and gain; their aggregation and the comparison threshold remain part of the supplied evaluator's scale.

The initially empty link slots and their positive/negative products need a material account. If opposite observations consume different components or destroy previous links, preserve that history and expense. A connection cannot be used before its final required write or bond. A disconnected or worn structure cannot count as transmitting merely because a summary coefficient remains nonzero.

An inherited factual command `q` and the actual native teaching history should be identical across the principal paired comparisons. If the native register is updated during these trials, its value must arise from those same native records, not be assigned to a common endpoint afterward. Recipient training consequences may differ by design; their stock changes must be kept separately from the common actor account. Any later divergence caused by selected action is an outcome, not a reason to reset the arms to equal resources.

## 3. Contingency, exposure and feature meaning

Equal native histories alone do not isolate a pairing effect. If one arm exposes only recipient loss and another only recipient return, a marginal-exposure learner can explain their difference. The current prospective candidate holds the native word `1001` fixed and pairs it with recipient words `1001`, `0110` and `1100`. Each recipient word has two losses and two returns. The operations are to use the actual recipient stock and isolated return source, preserving equal endpoint accounts across these pairings. This is a declared design, not a computed result. It is a paired sequence/exposure contrast; matching counts does not erase sequence-sensitive alternatives or establish a general causal correlation estimate. The exact update's order dependence must remain explicit.

If the finite material budget instead permits only constant or unbalanced sequences, the study can still test a signed co-activation rule. It should call that result sequence-dependent link formation rather than a uniquely identified contingency effect. Conventional associative learners can have biases under uncentered inputs; a count of coincident positive events is not automatically a covariance estimate.

Neither correlation nor repeated agreement establishes a common causal dependency. The same pairs can arise through a common external source, an arbitrary signaling convention or a transient training arrangement. The generic recipe can consequently produce a reproducible but misleading transfer. Correct recipient action knowledge can coexist with that mistaken evaluation. Changing the relationship after training, or dissociating the presented feature from the recipient's actual condition, tests this limitation without changing what the original formation result proves.

The endpoints identify the scope: learning a connection for one supplied recipient feature does not yet learn a feature class or generalize to a genuinely untrained recipient. If a shared input code automatically covers every recipient, that invariance remains part of its representation. The result should not claim learned cross-target classification unless the classification itself is acquired and tested independently of identity.

## 4. Comparisons that locate the causal contribution

These are comparisons for the proposed interface, not demands for an open-ended new study.

| Comparison | What it can isolate | What must remain matched or disclosed |
|---|---|---|
| Aligned, opposed and mixed paired events | Dependence of link sign/strength on the acquired relation | Native history, acquisition rules, pairing opportunities; recipient marginal and sequence differences disclosed. |
| Enabled versus disabled link writing | Dependence on material plasticity rather than merely elapsed training | Observation and native learning remain intact. Match expenditure and component consumption with an inert product when claiming a material-matched control. |
| Acquired graph versus an identity gate after acquisition | Evaluative transmission after perception | Same observations, factual command, native value and link products; a gate that prevents recipient evidence is a different intervention. |
| Generic graph transmission versus independently implemented conventional law | Correct implementation of the same declared mechanism | Same complete accessible state, transitions, costs and missingness. Exact equality is a useful result, not comparative superiority. |
| Truthful training relation versus misleading or changed transfer | Limits of a formed association's applicability | Keep acquired links intact; independently state the later recipient consequence law. |
| Later contrary pairs with plastic versus frozen-sign replacement | Revision of the formed connection rather than change in native value alone | Continue the same native observations and updates. Both arms retire/rebind at equal cost; the control replaces the old sign while the active arm can change it. Preserve all retired and replacement parts. |
| No formation or frozen installed links | What acquisition adds relative to absence or a supplied disposition | Different savings or construction costs reported, not concealed as equal budgets. |

A conventional signed associative learner, implemented with the same finite registers and account, is the nearest algorithmic comparator. A stronger structural learner could also retain separate target models and learn when sharing predicts held-out outcomes; its hypothesis grammar, evidence and resource requirements would need equal treatment before any superiority claim. The present study need not defeat conventional learning to explain a previously supplied physical connection.

An equally accurate factual learner with an actor-only evaluation gate remains a crucial rival. Native prediction-error reduction does not uniquely prefer a recipient-inclusive evaluator when both routes make the same private predictions. If the proposal contains no native mechanism that differentially maintains those alternatives, it must not claim that private success selects the acquired recipient influence. Formation, present causal efficacy and differential retention are distinct results.

The current prospective revision appends native `01` paired with recipient `10` before the single final assessment. Native learning continues in both arms, with intended final register `n=5`; the active arm changes a positive connection to negative while the control pays to replace its old positive sign. This is a design assertion awaiting implementation, not a result. A previously considered single native-zero lesson would reduce the native register enough to make both comparison arms abstain; that would not isolate link revision. The current design avoids a special frozen-native regime and adds no second action port.

## 5. Functional interpretation and remaining ethical boundary

A later recipient opportunity without a compensating actor return can demonstrate present other-directed causal influence even when its acquisition depended on past native outcomes. Its reward history alone does not make the later influence merely an expectation of current repayment. But the physical action and retained evaluator must establish that distinction; a favorable analyst label does not.

Positive and negative links describe the sign of a supplied feature's influence. A negative connection can oppose the modeled recipient-support action, depending on the evaluation rule. That is not a proof of malicious intent, negative moral worth or felt pleasure in another's harm. Likewise positive influence does not identify compassion, a justified patient-good mapping or appropriate authority.

The [evaluative application contract](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) remains separate: patient standing, relevant good or harm, valid authority and comparison with competing claims require independent defense. This construction may explain how an influence arises under those conditions or outside them. It does not make the conditions true by producing a link. The active succession and ethical investigations retain their own interfaces; a newly formed connection is not already a transmitted, corrigible or justified one.

## 6. What would support or defeat the proposed claim

The conditional connection is supported only if an actual admitted history constructs the operative links from their declared absent state, the acquired pairs determine those changes through the local law, and a later paid action depends on their transmission while the relevant factual/native controls are preserved. All failed preparations, interrupted pairing, exhausted components and unaffordable actions must retain their original place in the reported scope.

The causal interpretation fails if the constructor reads the desired final action, if a “shared” route is toggled independently of its physical links, if a label is inferred from inaccessible truth or a final stock instead of its paid acquisition, or if matched arms differ in their supposed common factual/native histories. It also fails at the claimed use boundary if erasing or blocking all newly formed influence leaves the same selection for an unrelated hidden reason.

A correct result can still show no helpful action, destructive influence, no retention advantage or complete equality with the conventional implementation. Those are substantive outcomes of the specified mechanism. They should narrow its interpretation rather than trigger a new definition of success. No outcome from this finite construction establishes empirical prevalence, universal favorable direction or completion of the whole foundation.
