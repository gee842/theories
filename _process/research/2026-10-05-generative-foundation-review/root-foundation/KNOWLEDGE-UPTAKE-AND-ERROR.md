# Cognitive possession and relevant error / 认识的持有与相关错误

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [knowledge investigation](G0-AND-KNOWLEDGE.md#development-of-the-knowledge-track), following its two outstanding requirements. This is a philosophical reconstruction and author-conducted counterreview, not an independent review, new experiment or new mathematical proof. [Current account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/KNOWLEDGE.md) / [primary readings and access limits](KNOWLEDGE-SOURCES.md).

## The explanatory claim

The two requirements connect through **content**. To explain what someone knows, identify which relation their judgment concerns, how that content enters their activity, and how the route could mislead about that same relation. Neither a bare signal nor a success statistic supplies these three relationships. They differentiate within an actual developmental history; a ready-made subject confronting an independent world is not the starting point.

**认识的持有，是某种有内容的区别能进入实际的判断、推论或熟练回应；认识的根据，则关乎这种进入怎样受其所论关系制约。** 形成、持有、真实及根据不能合并成一个“生成成功”指标。也不要求知者先能说出全部根据，才允许他知道。

G0 gives positive significance to living and further generation. The wider generative account explains how knowing can develop within that unfolding. The epistemic account additionally concerns accurate content and its grounding. These are different explanatory contributions. The conclusion of this investigation is a candidate integrating them, with a negative conclusion about deriving knowledge's identity from G0 alone. It does not establish an exhaustive reduction of intentionality or a universally accepted analysis of knowledge.

## 1. From a retained effect to cognitive possession

Start with effects that alter subsequent activity. A changing temperature can alter a material; a record can preserve a difference; learning can make an earlier encounter affect a later response. These are enabling relationships. To attribute propositional possession, the account must explain more than that a later event has an earlier cause.

The proposed explanation has three connected parts:

1. **A content-bearing distinction.** The formation and use of a state locate what it concerns: this object, the present temperature, a remembered event, a mathematical relation, or another person's claim. Attention, tracking, measurement practices, learning and linguistic histories can constrain this relation. Raw covariance alone usually underdetermines it. The content must remain identifiable when the state misrepresents; defining it as whatever happens to make the output correct would abolish error.
2. **Integration into taking something to be the case.** The distinction can guide assent, a further inference, anticipation or response in virtue of its content. An utterance merely copied without comprehension does not meet this description. Possession can be dispositional: the person need not be using the knowledge now. Retention, reliable retrieval or reconstruction must actually be available, rather than present only in an auditor's archive. Perceptual judgment can also be brief; permanent storage is not a requirement.
3. **Coordination with other content and correction.** The capacity participates in a wider pattern of accepting, using, qualifying or revising judgments. This gives substance to the difference between accepting a claim and emitting a signal. It need not include explicit reasoning about reliability, conscious reflection, linguistic reporting, or a personal correction apparatus for every belief. A capacity may be established developmentally or socially and remain unexercised in a particular token.

These are proposed constitutive relationships, not three independent mechanisms or a numerical consciousness test. Their explanatory burden is to identify actual organization: which differences are acquired, which distinctions persist across changes of cue, what conclusions or actions they can inform, and which discrepancies alter acceptance. A finite stimulus–response table can realize some of these relationships; its being finite does not settle its cognitive status. Conversely, giving an arbitrary physical state a propositional name does not establish them.

This proposal permits elementary knowing without requiring understanding of its explanation. Someone can recognize a familiar person without articulating the diagnostic cues. Testimony can give a child a fact without giving the child a theory of testimonial reliability. An explanatory account of these capacities may be sophisticated even when their exercise is not. A reflective adult's ability to give reasons is one developed form, not the proposed origin of all cognition.

**What prevents an unargued thermostat verdict?** Reliable regulation establishes a particular control capacity. A further claim of propositional knowledge requires evidence for content and the integrated acceptance described above. A bare temperature switch's reliable transitions do not by themselves supply that evidence. A more elaborate artificial organization might supply it; no biological privilege or arbitrary complexity threshold is stipulated. Whether a borderline case has minimal representation, know-how or propositional belief may remain disputed. The account makes the disputed relationships inspectable instead of treating reliable output as the answer.

**What prevents a hidden inner interpreter?** Integration is realized in the differentiated routes themselves: acquisition, retention, inference, response and revision. We do not add a second person who reads the first person's state. This is a functional and historical explanation, not a completed elimination of every intentional concept. Its remaining empirical burden is to show that the proposed content relationships explain actual capacities better than rival descriptions of the same organization.

## 2. The inherited construction supplies a causal comparison

The [experimental-access and acquired-applicability account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EXPERIMENTAL-ACCESS.md) already provides a relevant bounded realization. An acquired sensory model organizes later tests; failed use qualifies its applicability and selects a further encounter while preserving the accurate sensory model. The matched reader-clamp comparison retains evidence and formation costs but repeats the failed route. Thus acquired content's availability to later selection makes a causal difference within that construction. This is stronger evidence of operational uptake than the presence of an accurate record alone.

The construction does not settle philosophical possession. Its candidate family, contact laws, selector and interpretation remain disclosed inputs. A misleading receipt can produce false completion. We credit the earlier checks at their original scope and do not call the model conscious, a universal semantic theory, or a fresh empirical discovery. Its contribution is precise: it realizes and tests one of the organizational relationships the candidate explanation requires.

The complementary archive case matters too. A record may be accurate but inaccessible to a particular person; another person with usable access may know through it. A retained capacity can be temporarily blocked without being destroyed, whereas an unrecoverable record does not remain someone's available knowledge merely because it once helped them. Distinguish an enabling condition's absence from the loss of the capacity itself. Access need not be immediate, effortless or internal to the body.

## 3. Which errors bear on which claim?

An error possibility matters through the **content and route of this judgment**, rather than because it can be imagined or because a preferred verdict requires excluding it. The following constraints make that proposal assessable without inventing a universal error probability.

| Constraint | What justifies including or excluding a candidate error |
|---|---|
| Fix the claim before assessing success | State referent, time, quantity, approximation and conditions. A conclusion about a display is not silently replaced with a conclusion about what the display measures. A successful conditional claim does not establish its antecedent. |
| Locate the operative route | Identify the actual perceptual, computational, testimonial or inferential basis, including any basis that now replaces the origin. Classify it through acquisition and use, not as “the method that produces this true answer.” |
| Include materially available ways that route misleads | Actual decoys, channel changes, shared-source errors, invalid inferential transitions or missing application conditions can matter even when the claimant has not noticed them. Their relevance comes from their bearing on the route and content. |
| Assess the grounds for the range itself | Acquisition history, calibration, the conditions of current use, corroboration with different vulnerabilities and adverse cases can support a range. Mere absence from the chosen model cannot establish irrelevance. |
| Preserve distinct conclusions | An unresolved error may require a weaker claim, lower confidence, further inquiry or suspension. Failure to settle the target need not undermine accurate knowledge of what was observed or why the evidence is insufficient. |

These constraints distinguish **truth**, **the person's reasonable position given available evidence**, and **whether the actual route amounts to knowledge**. They can diverge. Unknown decoys can make a true answer accidental without making the person's ordinary reliance culpable. Newly supplied misleading testimony can challenge warrant even if the original perception was accurate; deciding whether that challenge is defeated requires assessing the testimony's actual basis. The distinctions concern different relationships in the same case, not separate domains of reality.

A remotely conceivable universal deception is not automatically a failure of a specific evidence route. To challenge that route, an argument must connect the proposed alternative to its conditions or undermine the reasons for accepting those conditions. This is the candidate's fallibilist stance; it is not a proof that global skepticism is impossible. An actual deception cannot be dismissed merely because the claimant lacks evidence of it. Fallibility allows evidence to be responsibly used without guaranteeing that every apparently adequate attribution of knowledge is correct.

Nor do these constraints produce a single numerical boundary between knowledge and nonknowledge in every possible case. Borderline risk, method individuation and rival treatments of epistemic context remain philosophical disputes. What is supplied here is a positive way to justify and criticize scope, rather than an analyst's unconstrained choice of a successful domain. Treating a declared domain as metaphysically exhaustive would exceed the result.

## 4. One case ties possession and error together

Consider a stipulated pressure-monitoring practice; no engineering or safety recommendation is being asserted. A trained reader sees a display of 40 and can use that reading in a report. The display may have a stale sample, and the reporting practice distinguishes a displayed reading from present pressure.

- **Claim A: the display currently reads 40.** A clear, competent observation can support this narrow judgment even if the sampling channel is stale.
- **Claim B: the pressure is currently 40 within the stated tolerance.** The same visual observation does not suffice. Sample age, calibration and the transmission route bear on this content. A known stale sample is a live undercutter; excluding it because the screen looks normal would be arbitrary.
- **Uptake comparison.** Copying the characters without understanding their role does not give the copier Claim B. A reader who distinguishes the two claims, can use their content and has warranted reliance on the measurement chain may know through it without personally rebuilding the instrument.
- **Repair.** A current independent reading can replace a stale basis. It does not turn the earlier unsupported inference into knowledge retroactively.
- **Action.** A further decision requires its own purpose, consequences and responsibility assessment. Knowing a pressure does not alone determine what anyone ought to do.

This case preserves the target phenomenon in its rivals. The mere-trace account cannot explain the possession difference. The output-success account cannot explain why a stale but luckily correct reading fails to establish the broader claim. An excessively reflective account would exclude warranted reliance unless the reader could personally prove the whole chain. The candidate avoids these particular failures, while remaining open to a rival that better explains the same cases.

## 5. Different kinds of knowing require different realizations

For **perceptual knowing**, changes of perspective and conditions of access matter; a perceptual judgment need not include a theory of perception. For **testimony**, the grounds may be distributed, but communication, interpretation and warranted reliance must connect them to the recipient. Agreement among copies of one source does not create independent support. For **memory**, retention and present retrieval matter; the current fact that one recalls something is distinct from the remembered event's having occurred.

For **mathematics**, proof, computation and warranted testimony connect a judgment to an abstract relation. The relevant failure may concern a misread premise, invalid transformation or corrupted computation. There is no need to invent a metaphysically possible world where the same necessary proposition is false; the epistemic route can be defective despite its conclusion's necessity. For **know-how**, the target is a competent capacity with appropriate enabling conditions. A temporary inability to perform need not remove the skill. Explanatory understanding adds a grasp of dependencies; it is not a condition silently imposed on every fact known.

This differentiation answers why one exact-classification predicate is too narrow. The shared structure is grounded cognitive availability with appropriate correctness; what realizes it depends on the achievement attributed. The previously checked binary correspondence results retain their precise mathematical scope.

## 6. Counterreview of this continuation

This review is by the author, not an independent agent.

**Is “content-guided use” merely knowledge renamed?** It would be if successful knowing were the only evidence for it. The proposal instead asks about identifiable reference, retention, transfer between uses, incompatibility and revision, including false judgments. An incorrect judgment can possess content and uptake. Truth and epistemic grounding are further conditions. This avoids that particular circularity without pretending to have completed reductive semantics.

**Does correction become an impossible entry requirement?** The candidate attributes a developed capacity, not an obligation that each knowing token survive a newly performed test. Socially supported and elementary perception remain possible; an isolated, rigid token can be embedded in a competent capacity. Reliable correction somewhere in an organization does not warrant every claim it produces.

**Can an institution choose away inconvenient errors?** Its declared standard can itself be wrong. Actual failure pathways and counterevidence remain relevant even when officials exclude them. Content changes must be stated: “our procedure accepted this” is a different proposition from “this is true.” G0 offers no exemption for socially beneficial falsehood.

**Does this settle all relevance disputes?** No. It supplies reasons for admitting or excluding alternatives and explains the representative cases. It does not derive one complete ordering of possibilities from G0. Both a demand for such an ordering and the claim that it is unnecessary need further argument. Universal immunity to counterexamples is not claimed.

**Does G0 do constitutive epistemic work after all?** Consider accurate, warranted understanding used destructively, and a false belief that happens to help a life continue. Both belong within generative reality, and G0 can evaluate their contributions, but the epistemic verdict follows the respective content and grounds. Changing the ethical use need not change those grounds. This is a substantive separation within the same process, not a freely toggled formal predicate. G0 alone therefore does not supply the proposed identifying conditions of knowledge.

**What could overturn the candidate?** A persuasive case of propositional knowing without any integrated cognitive possession; systematic knowledge through a route unrelated to its correctness; or evidence that the specified content relationships add no explanatory discrimination over arbitrary registration would require revision. Conversely, accurate use surviving a change of cue while failing after disruption of the proposed integration would support that aspect of the explanation. These are proposed discriminations, not new experimental results.

## Integrated outcome

The earlier gaps now have a connected philosophical answer: cognitive possession is explained through formed content and its integration; epistemic adequacy is assessed through that content's actual grounds and the ways those grounds can mislead. The account supplies reasons, representative comparisons and revision conditions. It does not establish a final reductive theory of every possible knower or solve every dispute about error relevance.

**本轮得到的结论：生成理论可以解释知识怎样形成、被持有、得到根据、受到纠正并进入生活；G0 可以解释其中的正面伦理方向。G0 单独并不给出知识与错误的区别。** The research result is therefore an integrated, fallible candidate and a bounded answer to Ren's explanatory conjecture, not a G0-only definition or a necessity theorem for all scientific philosophy.

The connection to the wider programme is recognition and calibration: ethical judgment requires access to actual dependencies, possibilities and losses. This study explains what such access must involve without substituting it for normative warrant. Broader validation remains open research: investigate content and integration in actual cognitive cases, and challenge the proposed relevance constraints with matched rival explanations. No new Lean claims or scientific reruns are needed for this philosophical continuation.
