# Identifying recipient learning through a formed architecture

6 October 2026. AI-authored interface and identification contract for **locally formed target routing → paid recipient-specific evidence → corrected action and further paid use**. This follows [architecture formation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION.md) and works toward an actual composition with the functional interface established by [recipient correction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md). **Status: prospective contract; new execution and its independent review have their own evidence records.** No new biological evidence or empirical result is asserted here.

## What has to cross the boundary

The accepted architecture process ends with no original source packets. Its actual endpoints differ in actor and recipient reserves, active predictor access, graph edges, graph retention, guides, spent materials, unused components and the inherited record/coupler. The learning phase must start from those endpoints, including depleted and inaccessible histories. It must not select an attractive learned state and then fund it as if its construction had been free.

Any auxiliary packets or new components must be declared at the original start, carried unused through the earlier process and retained on failed preparation. This is an extension with additional stated resources, not a claim that the exhausted source regenerates. Original and auxiliary source accounts must remain distinguishable even if later summed for conservation. Recipient reserves must not enter actor fuel without an explicit paid transfer law; no such transfer can be inferred from the fact that help benefits the recipient.

The primary live architecture's surviving endpoints have already consumed their three template blanks. A new record therefore needs either explicit recoding of existing memory capacity or newly declared material. The old recipient-correction study provides a candidate paid recoding operation, not permission to reset the architecture. Retain the actual record and coupler, initialize new target cells only from accessible inherited information, and distinguish pre-coupler estimates from issued actions when writing evidence back.

Access also has a history. A retained direct path can admit a recipient cue; an actor-identity comparator blocks it. Missing or deactivated paths cannot observe recipient consequences merely because the experimental arm is called a learner. If several retained graphs are available, selection, combination or search among them needs an explicit policy and cost. Changes to the earlier rule that each new construction round addresses a new site must be declared at the phase boundary. Paid reuse of an old site is a new local operation, not an unnoticed continuation of that scheduling rule.

## What the accepted correction result contributes

Recipient correction demonstrates a conditional problem: learning the recipient's action correspondence in a shared latest-value record can overwrite a different actor correspondence. A paid target-indexed record avoids that interference under the declared signal and write laws. The prior proof's four-state lower bound concerns independently possible binary target laws. If the recipient is known in advance always to require the opposite actor action, a target-dependent inversion suffices. A numerical opposite-law fixture alone cannot establish the general information requirement.

The prior study also preserves an identification trap. An actor's zero private receipt can accidentally supply the right correction after an unsuccessful recipient action, then supply a wrong label after successful help. Short behavioral improvement therefore does not identify the actual recipient-evidence channel. A new join should distinguish delivered outcome, receipt source, paid evidence access, target report and committed record. An observer's complete execution trace is not itself a runtime sensor.

The new result would connect these operations to a locally formed route and its actual costs. It would not derive the sensor, context calibration, update chemistry, decision to keep helping, or ethical significance of the recipient's condition. The old primary evidence assessment remains relevant: [modifiable mappings and social valence](GENERATIVE-DEVELOPMENT-EVIDENCE.md) constrain parts of the mechanism without demonstrating this complete history. No additional external research is required to justify the present computational interface.

## The selected prospective interface

The [new protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/learning/PROTOCOL.md) declares a forty-unit inactive bank at the original start, giving primary total energy 195 while leaving the parent's preparation source and rates unchanged. It reuses fixed site 1 rather than selecting a favorable graph. Between an initial actor opportunity and the first recipient opportunity, a one-unit wire attachment consumes one actual spare connector; paid recoding then repurposes the existing `q × last` carrier. Its ten possible states can represent the eight combinations of actor estimate, recipient candidate and qualification. This capacity count does not derive the recoding chemistry or prove an unrestricted lower bound. The existing coupler remains part of the command and feedback translation.

The phase supplies stability over four declared opportunities and charges both still-retained graphs at its final boundary. This is an explicit local time contract. It neither silently reuses the old per-round schedule nor implies unbounded retention. A filtered site still blocks recipient work and feedback, including when another graph elsewhere would have passed them. The attachment, recoding and later escrow payments can fail separately, leaving real partial costs and materials.

The selected update rule treats an unqualified zero receipt as a reason to try the other candidate, not as proof that the other command is correct. A subsequent positive receipt can qualify the candidate under the supplied faithful-channel and stable-efficacy assumptions. Qualified records ignore later zeros that may be outages; this protects one ambiguity but can fail after an actual efficacy reversal. The same-law outage and post-positive reversal cases therefore delimit this specific rule rather than establish universally reliable inference.

## A strict event and its limits

A useful strict witness has the following causal sequence: a still accessible formed route admits the recipient interaction; the inherited command fails under a changed or distinct recipient law; actual recipient feedback is obtained and committed at declared cost; only the recipient estimate changes; a later prepaid interaction uses the revised command and succeeds; the actor estimate still supports the actor's different action. Every step must occur in the same retained history. Report this event's unconditional mass separately from success among installed or solvent learners.

The physical teaching signal cannot be the hidden target law. In a deterministic, always-available binary task, observed nonreceipt after an action can identify the alternative action. Under source outage, obstruction or sensor failure, it cannot do so without further information. Availability and feedback provenance therefore belong in the supplied premises and countercases. A wrong paid label is not made truthful by the energy spent acquiring it.

Actor-first opportunities may make the learning bills affordable. That schedule is a legitimate supplied condition, but a favorable final balance does not show that recipient-first acquisition was viable. Preserve unaffordable initial observations and writes. A positive private return from a later actor opportunity is also different from compensation caused by the recipient's benefit. Test those pathways separately.

Recipient stock and a scheduled operation remain declared functional outcomes. They do not establish biological welfare, felt concern or ethical warrant. Correcting an instrumental command is a narrower result than learning why a recipient matters.

These tests must retain routes that block help, misleading evidence, aliased identities, unaffordable writes and frozen records that happen to be correct. More retained controllers do not by themselves imply more faithful learning. A predictor that accurately forecasts failure but never changes its command also remains a necessary rival to successful correction.

## Conventional equivalence and acceptance

A conventional indexed controller with the same reachable graph, state, observations, schedule and payments should reproduce the finite implementation exactly. Such equality verifies the representation; it does not refute the generative mechanism. The informative rivals omit a claimed dependence: a frozen record, shared aliasing, private-only evidence, an outage-blind decoder or a fresh-reserve model that ignores construction history. Distinguish those restrictions from conventional learning in general.

Local acceptance requires independent reconstruction of the old preparation, coupling prefix and architecture distribution; exact preservation of parent branch masses; a separate ledger for any original-start auxiliary supply; a strict actual recipient-learning witness if one exists; preserved failures; matched ablations; and transparent limits where behavioral outcomes fail to identify evidence provenance. Reusing the already independent architecture interpreter for the parent is permissible and must be disclosed; the new learning suffix needs its own independent implementation.

An honest null result is possible: the actual retained graphs may be too depleted to learn, or the routes with enough actor resources may block recipient access. The account should then preserve that obstruction and alter an explicitly named initial resource, component law or schedule in a separate case. It must not repair the result by introducing an unreported actor reserve or knowledge-bearing record. Even a successful join leaves formation of the feedback channel, long-run selection for faithful learning and justified concern open.
