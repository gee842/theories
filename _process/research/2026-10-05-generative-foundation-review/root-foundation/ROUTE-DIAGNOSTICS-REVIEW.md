## What the signature establishes

The physical interpretation assumes a bounded record `q < 4`, an ordinary recipient action equal to its current recipient bit, calibrated recipient addressing, one unchanged installed route, an external outcome channel available before route-specific routing, and a completed write. CLOSED deliberately disconnects that input; external channel availability therefore does not mean every route acquires it. A separate warranted report supplies actual recipient capture. The classifier observes the action and post-write bit, with the stipulated before/after record access and completion evidence.

For `flip = (post-write recipient bit != issued action)`, the actual functions give:

| Route | Actual failure | Actual success |
|---|---:|---:|
| TARGET | flip | retain |
| PRIVATE_ZERO | flip | flip |
| CLOSED or FROZEN | retain | retain |

A success/failure pair identifies these three recipient-update classes. The formal classifier uses the same route parameter for both samples; a physical consumer must associate observations with that same route episode and recipient context. Route replacement between samples cannot be silently ignored.

Both outcome polarities are needed for uniform separation of all three classes. This is not a claim that every diagnosis needs two samples: one failure with no flip identifies the unchanged class, and one success with a flip identifies PRIVATE_ZERO. Failure alone aliases TARGET with PRIVATE_ZERO; success alone aliases TARGET with CLOSED/FROZEN. An incompatible pair should leave the fixed-family assumptions in question rather than force a label.

Readable `gamma`, input-presence reports, provenance, hidden target law and analyst scores are excluded from this classifier. A paid configuration read with trusted recipe semantics can identify nominal routes directly; that is a different evidence contract. CLOSED and FROZEN remain equivalent only for recipient command/record behavior and common charges under the same inputs. Their route identities and input-presence behavior differ, and CLOSED still has a functional actor-target update. No equality of full physical states or all experiments follows.

## Sequence depth and passive success

The ideal wrong-start experiment gives receipt words TARGET `011`, PRIVATE_ZERO `010`, and CLOSED/FROZEN `000`. This requires a stable available binary plant, ordinary actions, completed writes and a genuinely initially wrong recipient bit. TARGET and PRIVATE_ZERO alias through two outcome-only observations; a post-write record read can separate them at the second probe. The three-probe depth belongs to this named experiment, not every possible calibrated intervention or observation interface.

Conversely, a correct initial bit in the stable available plant leaves TARGET, CLOSED and FROZEN at the same successful fixed point for every finite completed horizon. Passive success need never exercise their different update capacities. Neither result provides the resources or event occurrence needed to obtain both polarities. A real changed context or funded challenge must supply informative exposure; the learner is not given the hidden desired answer or a free environmental-change notification.

## Payment, access and truth remain separate

The [Lean source](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/formal/RouteDiagnostics.lean) imports the actual `FormedRecipientRoute.revise` and packed record operations. Its hypothetical inspection pays pre-read, outcome acquisition, write and post-read in sequence, with nonnegative prices and no inflow. A diagnostic tuple exists exactly when the four prices are jointly affordable; every partial result preserves remaining plus spent fuel, without refunds.

With unit prices, fuel two pays the first two stages but cannot write; fuel three commits a write but cannot pay the post-read. Both return `observation = none`. The returned structure's `q`, `remaining` and `spent` describe physical outcomes; they do not themselves grant the classifier unpaid access. Only `Inspection.observation` is the stipulated diagnostic channel. The signature theorem separately requires `actual_report : receipt = actualOutcome`: payment does not prove report truth.

This inspection is supplied bookkeeping. It is not a refinement of the existing seven-unit learner probe, a constructed monitor, or a justified exact additional diagnostic bill. It omits that probe's work and target-access mechanism. Any reuse of acquisition, records or writing must be established by the later physical composition.

The failure distinctions are sound. An unpaid write cannot diagnose a nonwriting route. Missing operational input makes TARGET locally alias CLOSED/FROZEN. Unavailable plant supply can produce a genuine failure despite a correct action: TARGET still executes its specified flip, potentially making its estimate worse. Thus update-behavior identification does not infer ecological truth or failure cause. Saved `capture`, `receipt_source`, decoder-error and witness fields do not install an independent runtime observation channel.

## Acceptance boundary for the next construction

This result establishes what evidence would discriminate exercised behavior under the supplied law. The next bridge must construct independently warranted acquisition and affordable access, retain evidence across any reconstruction, pay replacement parts and continued probes, and connect consequences to the retained alternative. A failing route may lack the resources to diagnose or replace itself; successful dormant routes can remain indistinguishable until conditions change. Existing funding bounds cannot absorb those new obligations unchanged.

The evidence does not select TARGET by moral or physical necessity, explain the origin of its recipe, or show that replacement improves a future context. Equivalent mechanisms may remain plural. Concern, justified authority and differentiated centers are further requirements; behavior classification alone settles none of them.
