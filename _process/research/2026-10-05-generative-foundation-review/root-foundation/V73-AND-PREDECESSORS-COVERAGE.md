# What advanced, and what remains in V7.3 and its predecessors

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparison answering Ren's question about the latest Lean advance and further content in the earlier manuscript. This is a coverage and genealogy audit, not another theory integration or new scientific result. The source scope is the local Selecting the Selectors / Changing the AI Trajectory manuscript line, with targeted checks against the current unified theory. It is not an exhaustive audit of the entire historical corpus.

## 1. The actual advance in the latest Lean work

The [integrated public module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeModel.lean) establishes a more precise relationship between the root's laws and histories:

1. **Exact identification within the formal representation.** Pointwise equality of primitive step relations is equivalent to agreement on every finite labelled history with its starting and ending states retained. This does not discover the law from a finite observed sample.
2. **An exact finite classification.** For two Boolean states and one action, the specified total/deterministic/renaming-invariant package has exactly the static and toggle descriptions. This is not a classification of arbitrary generative systems.
3. **Two local requirements are derived.** Within that Boolean package, same-label reversal and unique predecessors follow from the remaining properties. They need not be independently included there. The totality, determinism, finite carrier and symmetry conditions have not thereby been derived for reality.
4. **A proved limit on summaries and composition.** Static and toggle permit the same finite action words but reach different states. Synchronizing either with the same other process on those action words preserves the ambiguity. Arbitrary state-sensitive interactions are outside this specific product statement.

The [verification receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/verification.json) records 20 new proof declarations, including helper and public-interface results, within 119 checked declarations. These are not twenty new theories. Their named axiom-dependency lists are empty, and no new primitive process law was asserted. The original model remains conditional on its supplied dynamics and the interpretation of its formal objects.

This is a mathematical advance in identification, local premise reduction and known limits. It is not yet the causal derivation of the V7.3 mechanisms, organization, learning, concern or wisdom from a premise-free root. The [integration record](LEAN-MODEL-INTEGRATION.md) gives the implementation scope.

## 2. The earlier six families were not an exhaustive inventory

It did not mean that every result in V7.3 or its predecessors had been incorporated or proved in Lean.

There is more. Some of it is already present in current theory in a different form; some is briefly acknowledged; some needs a new exact connection. The current [Lean-interface audit](V73-LEAN-INTERFACES.md) supplies the separate formal status.

## 3. What the versions contribute

The [V6 preservation ledger](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v6/THEORY.md#appendix-f-preservation-ledger) maps V5.2 results and records earlier ideas, including selection rather than greed, rule reproduction, information unable to change action, reform windows, trust formed through conduct, and unity that preserves differentiation. It is useful genealogy, not independent evidence for each surviving claim.

- **V3–V4 and early manuscript lineage:** the selective reproduction of rules, capture, loss of effective correction, contests, irreversible risk, delegation, cooperation and transition supplied much of the problem structure. Later versions preserve both these questions and failures of earlier universal claims.
- **V5:** more explicit decision standards, comparison of complete alternatives, strong counterexamples and separation of faithful assistance from a harmful multi-actor game. Its [change record](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v5/CHANGELOG.md) identifies withdrawn universal survival and cooperation claims.
- **V6–V6.2:** multilevel selection, two-channel accounting, stronger compounding analysis, the three erosions, and a function-based generative alternative. The [V6 ledger](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v6/THEORY.md#appendix-f-preservation-ledger) and [V6.2 change record](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v6.2/CHANGELOG.md) separate retained results from corrections and unproved interfaces.
- **V7–V7.1:** [sharpened mathematical conditions](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7/CHANGELOG.md) and [further separation of claims](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.1/CHANGELOG.md), including objective narrowing versus concentration, apparent assurance versus residual hazard, and institutional correction versus technical coverage.
- **V7.2:** [a protected joint region and correction through institutional change](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.2/CHANGELOG.md), conditional on maintained bounds and implementation.
- **V7.3:** [a funded finite path into that protected region](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/CHANGELOG.md), including installation delay, temporary intervention, structural reform, reserve and ordinary maintenance after release. Its procurement illustration and comparison are proposed and unrun; they are not demonstrated institutional effectiveness.

Much of the useful older material remains inside V7.3, sometimes in supplements rather than the main text. It should not be counted twice as independent evidence or restored in its unrepaired earlier form.

## 4. Corrections are part of what must be integrated

The [V7.3 counterexample ledger](../../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/14_S14.md) and change records rule out treating the following older overclaims as results:

- shared benefits or partial pooling necessarily reverse relative selection;
- compounding always narrows objectives, or narrowing necessarily means concentration;
- bounded utility necessarily gives a point of enough or stops hazardous growth;
- an assurance gap establishes a positive residual hazard, or every race necessarily ends in failure;
- a corrector beyond human understanding must fail;
- every institution within a contest is eventually reabsorbed;
- successful local correction, invariant viability, attraction and timely recovery are equivalent;
- common interdependence by itself proves universal moral concern.

Some of these have narrower valid successors; others remain hypotheses or interpretive claims. Their failed forms and counterexamples are constraints on further integration.

## 5. Research consequence and scope

The most useful next bridge is **formed learning and selector revision → the actual returns and authority that retain or replace those selectors**. The compounding, enforcement and nested-selection strands supply precise targets for working backward from existing conditional results. Their payoff, event, observation and authority dependencies must be derived or remain openly unresolved.

A second connected obligation is **available evidence → timely effective correction through replacement**, with coverage, correlated failures, funding and irreversible losses distinguished. The recent Lean result supports faithful representation of such histories; it supplies none of their actual dynamics.

A successful new study would change the account by deriving one of those missing links in the existing model, identifying its realizable conditions, or showing by a counterexample that a proposed universal claim must be narrowed. This comparison itself adds no axioms, code, new theorem or empirical result. No scientific suite was rerun.
