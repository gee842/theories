## 4. The smallest remaining general connection

**Formed and revisable understanding of another center → that center's condition gaining justified evaluative influence → effective, corrigible action through a continuing lineage.** Causal formation and normative justification are distinct parts of this existing obligation.

A finite acceptance contract can require:

1. **Specify the ground and its target.** Independently describe the recipient condition and distinguish receipt, organizational benefit, experienced harm, learned influence and justified reason. State what ethical premise is defended and what is still assumed.
2. **Explain evaluative uptake.** Use the already formed learning/assembly mechanisms to show how consequences change the recipient condition's influence on choices, including comparisons with equally informed identity-gated or private-only alternatives. Retain cases with no private reward and the conditions causing exclusion. An analyst's desired endpoint cannot choose the architecture.
3. **Discharge an actual downstream contract.** Identify which produced state supplies each causal input and which independently defended argument supplies the positive reason, warranted relevance and legitimate authority required by the applicable theorem. Show that the produced process can recognize and act on those grounds. Supply an explicit semantic/resource map and the applicable action margins, or state and justify an appropriate revised theorem. Do not identify private fuel, factual accuracy or persistence with moral value.
4. **Compose and challenge the result.** Carry current evidence, affected-center meanings, revision access and actual stocks through a constructive case and decisive destructive alternatives, including changed dependence or lost power at the claimed scope. Use existing verified interfaces where they already meet the contract. Conditional renewal requires a declared support law, not infinitely many simulated generations.

The [ethical foundation](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) already contains positive arguments for standing and answerability; it would be inaccurate to say no ethical argument exists. The evolving [慧根 account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/HUIGEN.md) still distinguishes a patient's own significance from a reason an unrelated deliberator must recognize, especially for a dispensable noncommunicating patient. That central extension needs a defended scope. `InquiryRoot.entire_closure_necessary_iff_basis` transfers necessity from a necessary basis; extending the closure cannot make an unjustified basis necessary or supply authority.

The causal construction can explain how concern arises or fails without proving ethical truth from bare persistence. The normative argument must defend why the identified condition counts as a reason; it cannot use successful reproduction as its proof. If a stronger universal entailment fails, preserve the counterexample and explicitly revise that claim. A nonentailment result does not magically count as a positive completed derivation of concern.

The smallest justified join is therefore a meaning-preserving, paid route from the acquired evidence actually available to the reasons and actions its chosen theorem licenses. Currentness must be supported by the declared context/stability law, not by an analyst's knowledge that change occurred. Forming and justifying evaluative concern remains a separate central connection even after that operational join succeeds.
