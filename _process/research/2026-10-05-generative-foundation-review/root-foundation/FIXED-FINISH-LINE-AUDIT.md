## 4. The smallest remaining general connection

**Formed and revisable understanding of another center → that center's condition gaining justified evaluative influence → effective, corrigible action through a continuing lineage.** Causal formation and normative justification are distinct parts of this existing obligation.

A finite acceptance contract can require:

1. **Specify the ground and its target.** Independently describe the recipient condition and distinguish receipt, organizational benefit, experienced harm, learned influence and justified reason. State what ethical premise is defended and what is still assumed.
2. **Explain evaluative uptake.** Use the already formed learning/assembly mechanisms to show how consequences change the recipient condition's influence on choices, including comparisons with equally informed identity-gated or private-only alternatives. Retain cases with no private reward and the conditions causing exclusion. An analyst's desired endpoint cannot choose the architecture.
3. **Discharge an actual downstream contract.** Identify which produced state supplies each causal input and which independently defended argument supplies the positive reason, warranted relevance and legitimate authority required by the applicable theorem. Show that the produced process can recognize and act on those grounds. Supply an explicit semantic/resource map and the applicable action margins, or state and justify an appropriate revised theorem. Do not identify private fuel, factual accuracy or persistence with moral value.
4. **Compose and challenge the result.** Carry current evidence, affected-center meanings, revision access and actual stocks through a constructive case and decisive destructive alternatives, including changed dependence or lost power at the claimed scope. Use existing verified interfaces where they already meet the contract. Conditional renewal requires a declared support law, not infinitely many simulated generations.

The [ethical foundation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) already contains positive arguments for standing and answerability; it would be inaccurate to say no ethical argument exists. The evolving [慧根 account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/HUIGEN.md) still distinguishes a patient's own significance from a reason an unrelated deliberator must recognize, especially for a dispensable noncommunicating patient. That central extension needs a defended scope. `InquiryRoot.entire_closure_necessary_iff_basis` transfers necessity from a necessary basis; extending the closure cannot make an unjustified basis necessary or supply authority.

The causal construction can explain how concern arises or fails without proving ethical truth from bare persistence. The normative argument must defend why the identified condition counts as a reason; it cannot use successful reproduction as its proof. If a stronger universal entailment fails, preserve the counterexample and explicitly revise that claim. A nonentailment result does not magically count as a positive completed derivation of concern.

## 5. Empirical scope and a finite release decision

The [cross-domain identification audit](CROSS-DOMAIN-IDENTIFICATION.md) maps bacterial burden feedback, actual AI handoff and represented-beneficiary learning. They support different portions of the account, not three replications of a full chain. The [recipient-cue analysis](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/RECIPIENT-CUE-GROUNDING.md) is a real independently checked retrospective reanalysis of 24 published preview pairs, with mean +4/3 pulls and explicit source/sample limits. It identifies cue-sensitive provisioning, not recipient-specific representation or moral concern. Calling the evidence merely simulations would understate it; calling it validation of the whole foundation would overstate it.

For the remaining advertised empirical mechanism, use the existing identification contract: retain accessible evidence, beneficiary identity, actual delivered effects, actor costs and alternative mechanisms, and test a contrast that separates their predictions. Existing data can suffice if they actually identify that contrast; a new experiment is not compulsory by definition. If evidence is unavailable, restrict the empirical claim transparently. Do not silently drop the foundational concern objective while calling a narrower controller demonstration completion.

An exactly equivalent conventional controller cannot be defeated by a discriminating experiment between the two identical laws. Preserve that equality. Comparative contribution can lie in newly exposed construction, interpretation, feasibility or cross-center conditions, tested against an explicitly restricted alternative with those omissions fixed before results. It need not be a superior universal algorithm.

A finite release decision can therefore ask: **Are the five original criteria met at the stated scope, with no central bridge still supplied as its own premise?** The answer remains no at this snapshot, principally because concern's evaluative/justificatory connection, its integrated continued use and the claimed empirical reach remain incomplete. The four active studies materially narrow those obligations if accepted. Afterward, assess the common connection and evidence directly; another gadget is warranted only when it supplies a demonstrably missing hypothesis or a discriminating test.

A final synthesis should retain one dependency/claim ledger, accepted interface receipts, shared meanings and remaining local-law/ethical/empirical premises. It need not reprove every implementation in one file, resolve every ethical application, prove universal favorable historical direction or establish unique metaphysical monism. No shared document was edited for this audit.

## 6. Follow-up: which evidence interfaces must actually compose?

A further source inspection on 6 October clarifies the boundary between the two active evidence studies. The fresh-evidence continuation starts from the accepted action history. The pre-import guard starts before the first import and consumes a single-use provenance permission. Fresh acquisition does not by itself install or rearm that guard. The two developments are complementary scoped continuations; their coexistence establishes neither one combined carrier nor a universal requirement to build such a carrier.

The inspected downstream theorems do **not** require repeated pre-import inspection or a second independent corroborating channel. `ReasonReplay.paid_recorded_reason_sound` requires an authentic starting state, an actual history and paid audit trace, calibration, a positive anchor, warranted relevance, and a reached publication containing the claimed reason. `ReasonProtection.reached_reason_and_protection` additionally requires freshness of the apparatus, coverage, an actual paid action trace and permission. Supplying those contracts is the obligation. Reusing a particular inspector is necessary only for a claim that expressly retains that inspector's defensive guarantee across later imports.

The immediate `RecipientAction.paid_import_and_execution` interface requires sound meanings for the working conclusions and enabled import/trial operations; it does not prescribe the source of that soundness. `ReasonProtection.Fresh` concerns the physical operating condition and should not be misread as an assertion of freshly acquired world evidence.

R2 is stronger in a different direction: its audit supplies **complete current validity for every candidate edge**, in a context stable during audit and action, and supports paid reconstruction, individual concern margins, effective control and the stated liquidity/incentive conditions. A sparse fresh record that correctly returns unknown does not satisfy that complete-audit premise. Nor do two command alternatives for one recipient acquire the meaning of two patients. To use R2, establish the full input mapping; alternatively prove an appropriately scoped theorem for partial evidence and preserve its uncovered cases. Either route addresses an existing premise, rather than adding a sixth completion condition.

The smallest justified join is therefore a meaning-preserving, paid route from the acquired evidence actually available to the reasons and actions its chosen theorem licenses. Currentness must be supported by the declared context/stability law, not by an analyst's knowledge that change occurred. Forming and justifying evaluative concern remains a separate central connection even after that operational join succeeds.
