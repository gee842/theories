# What formed evaluative plasticity adds—and does not add

6 October 2026. Independent contribution and premise audit, using existing accepted work and the current separately reconstructed finite plasticity executions. No new calculation, empirical observation or source study was performed for this note. The numerical comparison and corruption tests pass; package acceptance and any formal certificate retain their own review status. This note neither changes the frozen protocol nor adds a completion condition.

## 1. A real conditional construction, with a narrow derived object

The new transition is **paid paired consequences → an initially nonconducting, materially backed connection → its retained signed influence on one later recipient assessment**, with one paid change of that connection. This is more than relabeling the old live `shared` constructor. Initially both sockets are empty. They cannot transmit. A paid recipient shortfall encounter and the separate acquired own bit determine whether a real pair binds and whether it conducts positively or negatively. The final port reads the occupied components, not an analyst's benevolent-route label. Inert components pay the same construction bill without conductance; the identity filter retains knowledge and links but blocks their influence. The optional revision retires and replaces a component while matched native learning continues.

What arises is the **instance, occupancy and sign of an operative connection within a supplied functional architecture**. The sockets already designate a recipient input and a native-evaluation destination. The recipe already makes a recipient loss a contact opportunity, an own loss set positive conductance, and an own return set negative conductance. This is not derivation of those endpoints, the whole topology, the plasticity law or the meaning of positive influence. Formed material is not a proof that an arbitrary pair implements those functions.

A fixed microscopic rule producing changed effective wiring is a legitimate developmental explanation. Calling that an origin of all cross-center evaluative architecture would be too strong. The finite-state representation alone also cannot distinguish this construction from a conventional controller whose state contains the same paid component graph.

## 3. Functional and interpretive premises still supplied

The [frozen protocol][protocol] supplies the following content:

- A typed, stable recipient channel; two predesignated connection sockets; part identities; one spare replacement; calibrated own and recipient meters; and finite addressed latches. Missing acquisition is distinguished from acquired zero.
- Primitive own-operation significance and the specific update `n' = floor(n/2)+4Y`. Operation shortfall excludes later measurement/write bills, although all bills enter the resource account. This is an operational teaching convention, not an observation of suffering.
- The contact condition `X=1`, the signed plasticity recipe, retirement order, paired exposure schedule and phase isolation. No system-level choice of this learning activity or this rule is newly derived.
- A generic transmission/assessment port, unit link conductances, additive gain from two contacts, the scale `10*T > 8*8`, a one-use authorization and the inherited action law. Both positive contacts are needed for the primary threshold at n4; multiplicity is consequential supplied functionality.
- A transduction from the new **two-unit recipient-operation modality** to evaluation of the old **ten-unit recipient command**. The same addressed reserve does not prove that these operations share a causal or evaluative meaning. Neither operation establishes an unmet patient claim.
- Originally protected material, fuel and source; an actual controlled preparation word and suspended old formation after handoff. The enlarged unrestricted chemistry's assembly probability is not inherited from the old preparation.

These premises are visible, falsifiable local assumptions, not conclusions from undifferentiated generativity. Some are ordinary component laws a conditional explanation may take as inputs; others become central unresolved obligations if the release claims to explain their functional origin or warrant. No endless demand to derive every physical law follows.

## 4. What the comparisons discriminate

The primary own history is fixed. Aligned, opposed and mixed recipient sequences preserve marginal shortfall counts but generate respectively positive, inhibitory and canceling links. These are finite paired-sequence contrasts, not a causal covariance experiment or evidence that the association will generalize. The local rule is conditional coactivation/sign setting; its balance-specific covariance identity is not its general learning objective.

Within each pairing, active, inert, filtered and conventional arms receive the same actual training observations. Active versus inert identifies conductance after paid construction; active versus filtered identifies its downstream use; disabled native writing identifies the native magnitude's separate role. Missing acquisition, an inaccessible primary part, withheld funding, a missing prebacked packet and lost scope leave actual paid/failed prefixes rather than manufactured completed learning.

The optional revision is particularly informative: both controls continue to n5, pay the same turnover bill and retain the same evidence. Replacing the oldest positive link with a negative one makes the active graph cancel; copying its old positive sign keeps the frozen-sign graph positive. Thus the changed assessment is attributable to changed operative linkage, not frozen native learning or an omitted bill. **The revision reduces this recipient service under an unchanged efficacy law.** It demonstrates plasticity, not improvement, wisdom or correct moral revision.

The conventional table matches the entire finite decision law and saved outcomes. It is a strong positive comparator: the result survives ordinary computational realization and claims no predictive superiority over an equally specified conventional associative controller. A simpler account—paid bounded associative plasticity plus instrumental command knowledge—already describes this specimen. Its contribution to the foundation is the explicit developmental/composition interface and failure account, not a new exclusive learning algorithm.

The [independent comparison][check] retains all 836 histories and exact inherited masses, all physical states and accounts, and all advertised metrics. It does not privilege the successful conditional witness. The [mutation checks][mutations] reject the listed graph, acquisition, reset, receipt, resource and ancestry corruptions. They are finite artifact checks, not a proof that every conceivable implementation has the same behavior.

## 5. Claims that still do not follow

There is no empirical finding here that organisms acquire value by this rule, that correlation establishes another center's good, or that a controller has experienced concern. The recipient operations consume its real stock in the model; their teaching cost cannot itself justify imposing those operations on a patient. Benefit, harm, standing, consent, rightful authority, competing claims and the adequacy of the eventual service require the existing independent application premises.

The connection is to one preaddressed recipient. There is no new-recipient generalization, learned semantic identity, autonomous discovery of a shared feature, or calibrated transfer between the two operation modalities. The hand-built schedule is not an endogenous exploration policy. Accurate command knowledge can coexist with zero, inhibitory or excluded evaluative influence.

Nothing in this continuation selects the active plasticity rule because it benefits the recipient, generates the plasticity rule from a lower-level repertoire, or favors inclusive evaluation over a cheaper/private alternative. Those stronger claims require their actual selection mechanisms; they cannot be inferred from eventual material occupancy or a positive case. Existing selector-revision results remain available where their input contracts match, but they are not automatically this graph's successor history.

## 7. The next connected obligation

The next whole-foundation bridge is **acquired evaluative influence → warranted, revisable applicability → effective action**, using the existing means-knowledge, evidence, application and succession results. It must distinguish what the learner happens to favor from what the affected center has grounds to claim, and must let defeating evidence change the relevant application without erasing unaffected knowledge or standing. The already active application work owns that connection; this note does not propose a duplicate engine.

Concretely, the release account should identify which independently defended patient/episode/role/authority and full-comparison premises license use of the acquired link, which available evidence defeats that use, and whether the actual controller uses that evidence before an affordable choice. Already fulfilled service must not be relabeled unmet because a new evaluator or link appears. Existing conditional revision/application results should discharge what they actually prove; a physical combination is needed only for a retained claim of that particular continuing mechanism.

A central capacity remains open if it is still merely named or supplied as its own explanation. A conditional account does not remain open merely because some more detailed implementation can be imagined. Integrating the supplied local functions, the defended ethical interpretation, the actual compositions and the empirical claim boundaries is the substantive next assessment. No further apparatus is automatically required by accepting this result.
