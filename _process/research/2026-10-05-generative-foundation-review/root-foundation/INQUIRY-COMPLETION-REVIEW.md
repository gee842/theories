## Prospective acceptance and claim boundary

The [protocol](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/feedback/INQUIRY-PROTOCOL.md) identifies a bounded episode fulfilled by one productive extraction. This is the productive-use component inherited from the earlier undertaking, not the original novelty-constrained assay or the preceding refinement study's stronger post-split-use test. Its goal and scope must remain fixed before viewing outcomes.

The strongest expected claim is **acquired knowledge of actual completion controls whether a still-unresolved question is pursued**. The actual successful effect fulfills the episode; the retained receipt enables the learner to act on that fact. This can connect inquiry, changed conditions and responsive stopping without deriving a new purpose or requiring another controller above every fixed rule. It does not establish that an unasked question is irrelevant to all future activity, that stopping is uniformly better, or that the planning recipe itself was learned.

Acceptance requires:

1. Unchanged and accounted preparation, handoff and sensory acquisition, preserving H110 and its original conditional certificate. The supplied earlier handoff and shortest first-use policy must remain explicit.
2. Native contact and a completed slot write before the table contains an outcome. A denied contact must not promote the outer audit's known effect into an acquired fact. Actual arrival can fund only later operations.
3. The ordinary and disconnected-completion arms must share the same received positive outcome, table and charged completion reading before their subsequent behavior differs. A decoder intervention must not erase the record it purports to ignore.
4. Unknown phase outcomes remain unknown when the episode is reported complete. False-positive and false-negative contact must separate actual fulfillment from its acquired indication. Storage alone must not certify truthful completion.
5. Continue to report cases where more inquiry gains useful information or another actual arrival, especially readiness011. An episode-relative sufficiency claim must not become a general judgment that inquiry or additional options are harmful.
6. Preserve physical depletion, inaccessible receipts, failed writes, source denial and unsuccessful final retention. All-blocked readiness must remain admitted, so no unconditional guaranteed productive outcome is inferred.

The old [inquiry-sufficiency mathematics](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md) already permits an inquiry to fulfill its undertaking and distinguishes actual effects from accessible information. The present work can realize that connection in the existing lineage. It is not a new theorem about inquiry, a new learned sufficiency standard, or a derivation of ethical experimental authority.

### Causal result rather than a retrospective label

Both matched pairs, under readiness010 and011, share their first 18 extension events through the acquired positive slot write and charged completion read. Their earlier history, actual incoming support and retained table also match. Disconnecting only the completion reader then causes further inquiry. The ordinary learner stops with table[0,1,?] and source10; it has completed the specified productive-use component without knowing the final phase's readiness.

Under010 the extra inquiry consumes the final packet without another arrival. Under011 it produces another ten units and additional knowledge. Thus the reader controls whether a question is pursued, but stopping is not uniformly preferable for every future undertaking. This is a direct counterweight to interpreting local completion as global sufficiency or treating preservation of an unused packet as the only possible good.

The three productive primary worlds001/010/011 all fulfill the episode and retain their table under the specified nine-exposure, shortest-first conditions. The admitted000 world cannot fulfill it. This is a finite conditional realization, not an unconditional positive-outcome guarantee or a proof that the controller computed such a guarantee before acting.

This controller has no such inference-and-write route. Its retained-table interpretation must not silently become a complete information-state theorem.

False-positive first or second receipts cause reported completion without any productive event. A false-negative second receipt under010 produces the inverse mismatch: the effect occurred, but the table stays negative and the procedure continues until all slots are recorded. The source reports physical fulfillment only in the outer audit; that field does not guide the controller. A lost first contact can leave a retained blank table. These cases preserve the difference between an indication, truth and storage.

The resource controls also remain substantive. Exposure8 cannot fund installation despite greater source stock. Exposure9 with the original novel word cannot fund the next native reset. Exposure10 under001 can acquire the phase-one failure but has no packet for phase two, despite enough reserve to retain its incomplete table. None of these failures is repaired by changing a tariff, restoring source or importing an outcome from another history.

### Remaining explanatory scope

The protocol's task, completion interpretation, compact record format, interface correspondence, shortest-unknown-phase recipe, cost envelopes and risk remain supplied. The physical successful effect changes the episode's completion state; acquiring its indication enables the learner to respond. The study does not yet show acquisition of a new sufficiency criterion, creation of the episode's purpose, or formation of the recipe that generates the next inquiry. Nor does the successful finite episode establish indefinite renewal or discharge independently surviving duties.

The new compact rule has a different representation and cost schedule from the preceding refinement controller. Cross-study feasibility differences should not be attributed solely to one changed decision rule. The matched completion-reader interventions within this new implementation supply the narrower causal identification. Ordinary state-based control remains a valid realization of the same mechanism.

The core/tree account is advanced by an executed connection it must preserve: the same event changes material support, may fulfill a prior undertaking, and may or may not become a usable record governing later activity. These are distinguishable relations within that actual history. Their successful composition does not derive the local laws or establish interpersonal standing, universal moral priority or completed alignment.
