## The new derivation

The unchanged plant relation is `receiptOf action regime available = (action == regime) && available`. The executed action is available to the controller through its retained action field. The regime and realized availability bit are not thereby supplied as observations.

[ReceiptIdentification.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/construction/prediction/ReceiptIdentification.lean) characterizes exactly which regimes an action/receipt pair permits when either availability value is possible:

- A successful receipt identifies the regime as the executed action **at that trial**.
- A failed receipt permits both regimes. It may be a mismatch, or a matching action during an outage.
- The existing total decoder is correct exactly when availability was true or the action differed from the regime. Its error case is a matching action with unavailable support.

The mathematical partial decoder returns the action on success and abstains on failure. Its answers are sound and complete for unique identification from those two fields: every sound decoder must abstain on failure, and every sound complete decoder agrees with it. This is an interpretation theorem, not a replacement controller or a claim that abstention improves learning or survival. Different observations or a justified probabilistic model can support different conclusions.

For any finite record, compatibility with one fixed regime is equivalent to agreement with all its successful actions. All-failure records retain both candidates; opposed successes refute that fixed-regime account. This tests a proposed stable interpretation rather than assuming that the actual process is stable. The statements do not deny probabilistic evidence in failures, identify every formally possible channel record with an affordable execution, or imply a general impossibility of learning.
