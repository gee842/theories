## The transition addressed

Actual effects of inquiry change the configurations from which a response must remain possible. The preceding theorem characterized bounded successful policies, but its equivalence to the implication-only criterion required an explicit empty/live classification of every relevant successor branch. This continuation constructs that classification for a finite executable representation. It also removes all 21 standard-axiom dependencies retained by the structural entry on which the inquiry results depend.

These are two different reductions: a proof implementation no longer depends on named logical axioms, and a local classification obligation is now derived from executable representation data. Neither change alone supplies the origin, accuracy or authority of the represented law and continuation requirement.

## Classification is constructed, not postulated

[FiniteInquiryClassification](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/FiniteInquiryClassification.lean) represents the complete local carrier by `Fin size`. It constructs its exhaustive state list recursively and proves coverage. Filtering this list with executable membership produces an exact candidate list; exactness is a theorem, not an input certificate.

For an actual process `P`, initial candidates `B`, inquiry `a` and acquired result `o`, the successor list contains exactly those endpoints `t` for which

\[
\exists s,\quad B(s)\land P(s,a,t)\land\mathrm{sense}(s,a,t)=o.
\]

The construction searches predecessors and endpoints. It retains every allowed nondeterministic successor and does not select one favorable representative. Iterating executable membership yields `ClassifiedThrough` at every finite inquiry depth. Thus the existing implication criterion is equivalent to existence of a bounded sound policy without a supplied classification or enumeration proof.

The general interface accepts decisions for transition and initial membership. A Boolean-table specialization derives both decisions internally from the table and initial bits. Its arguments are representation data, the observation map, response-word map and declared prefix/endpoint predicates; there is no extra classification, coverage or decidability hypothesis. This specialization does not choose a particular physical table or assert that an arbitrary proposition-valued relation is decidable.

The [public finite entry](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeFiniteInquiryModel.lean) imports the existing inquiry entry and this extension. Its [full fresh-build receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/finite-verification.json) records **20 modules and 227 audits, all empty**: 212 inherited and 15 added (12 theorems, two decision kernels and the table adapter). It also checks that the public entry adds only imports.

## Premises discharged and obligations retained

| Item | Status after this continuation |
|---|---|
| The 21 inherited structural `propext`/`Quot.sound` dependencies | Removed while retaining the original statements and finite law. |
| Exact finite-state and successor-list coverage | Constructed and proved. |
| Empty/live classification at every finite inquiry depth | Derived for executable finite representations. |
| Transition and initial-membership decision proofs | Derived internally for Boolean representation data; explicit in the more general interface. |
| Correct finite representation of the relevant process | Still requires justification. An unbounded process cannot simply be truncated. |
| Observation meaning, calibration, response availability and relevant history/resource coordinates | Still local representation and realization obligations. |
| Prefix and endpoint requirements, their practical scope and warrant | Still explicit; no reward or ethical direction is inserted into the process. |
| Finding, installing, learning or retaining a policy | Not proved by this existence characterization. Arbitrary action/response spaces are not searched. |
| The common foundation's explanatory sufficiency | Still open under the [premise ledger](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md). Empty dependency reports do not establish it. |

Inquiry, acquired outcomes and subsequent response describe one transforming process. This analytical separation preserves native successor relations; it omits the formation of its representation and criterion unless those are separately modeled. A supplied local law is not thereby external to generative reality. Conversely, including a law in one representation does not explain its formation merely by renaming it generative.
