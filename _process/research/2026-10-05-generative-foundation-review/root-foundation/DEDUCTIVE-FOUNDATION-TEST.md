## 1. What changes, and what remains established

The [general foundation argument](GENERAL-FOUNDATION-ARGUMENT.md) correctly distinguished the root's permissive claims from claims that every system has feedback or repair. Its actual examples still warrant occurrence at their documented scopes. Under the newly explicit standard, however, that evidential achievement cannot be counted as the deductive necessity of the foundational capability claims.

| Root commitment | Established support retained | Obligation under the clarified standard |
|---|---|---|
| Consequential differentiation is possible | Documented retained-use operations and activity-formed conditions | Deductive warrant for the foundational possibility claim, including the interpretation and modal scope used |

This changes completion accounting, not the empirical findings. Nor does it impose deduction of each measured temperature, one mandatory notation, deterministic evolution, universal ethical convergence or endless survival. Application conditions can remain contingent. Moving a foundational commitment into those conditions would leave its warrant unresolved.

Historical passages that reject an *unrequested* deductivist completion gate now have dated scope. Ren has explicitly requested it. Neither automatically establishes the necessity of its actual-world interpretation.

## 2. The strongest affirmative actuality-to-possibility route

An actual realization provides a witness that its occurrence is possible at that realization, when the admitted comparisons include the actual case. A stronger argument might then say: because this witness exists, its possibility itself cannot fail. That second step deserves a direct test rather than an unsupported rejection.

The [Lean companion](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DeductiveNecessity.lean) uses a type of cases and an explicitly supplied comparison relation `admits`. These are bookkeeping for the proposed inference, not independently existing realms or a new process ontology. Write `R(a,b)` for an admitted comparison, and `P(a)` for the claim at a case. The meanings are:

\[
\Diamond_R P(a) := \exists b\,(R(a,b)\land P(b)),\qquad
\Box_R P(a) := \forall b\,(R(a,b)\to P(b)).
\]

The first positive theorem constructs the actual case as a possibility witness from `R(a,a)` and `P(a)`. The second proves that the same witness establishes necessary possibility if every comparison admitted from `a` admits `a` in return. The exact uniform result is:

\[
\bigl[\forall P\;(P(a)\to\Box_R\Diamond_R P(a))\bigr]
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall b\;(R(a,b)\to R(b,a)).
\]

The forward proof uses the predicate “is the starting case”; the reverse proof reuses that case as a witness. This does not assume the return condition and then conceal it. It recovers the exact condition for the proposed **uniform inference rule**. No full modal axiom system is imported.

The stronger uniform promotion of actual truth directly to necessary truth has a stricter characterization:

\[
\bigl[\forall P\;(P(a)\to\Box_R P(a))\bigr]
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall b\;(R(a,b)\to b=a).
\]

Thus “it is actual, therefore it is necessary” cannot be adopted as a general rule over this full predicate range while also admitting distinct cases. A fifth theorem constructs the precise separation for necessary possibility when an admitted comparison lacks the return: the starting-case predicate is actually possible but not necessarily possible. This is a general inference audit, not another finite controller or a claimed counterexample to the full theory.

## 3. What these deductions do and do not require of the foundation

The all-predicate quantifier matters. A **particular** foundational claim may have necessary possibility through other witnesses even if the uniform return condition fails. We do not impose return accessibility as a new mandatory completion gate on that claim. The results assess an attempted shortcut that purports to work from actuality alone for arbitrary content.

Nor is modal return physical reversibility. A later configuration's inability to restore an earlier configuration does not by itself refute metaphysical necessary possibility. Conversely, an equivalence relation among descriptions does not prove that a physical preparation remains available. The theory's current operational possibilities and unrestricted metaphysical possibility must not be substituted for each other.

If the relevant comparison relation and its scope can themselves be deductively justified, the positive transfer theorem becomes usable. Merely choosing a symmetric relation does not do this. Declaring all comparisons to be identical makes the stronger rule hold but does not justify excluding genuine variation. Empty comparison ranges can make necessity vacuous; the theorems do not create inhabited actual domains or justify their coverage.

This respects the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The mathematical separation tracks different claims within one inquiry. It does not posit a split between a mind and an independently constituted world, or between a passive substrate and an external law. The remaining issue is warrant for the intended application, not an inference that the law must be external to reality.

## 4. Does constitutive potentiality close the gap?

The strongest alternative treats possibilities as aspects of actual organization rather than as an additional law attached to bare things. This can avoid an artificial explanatory split. Its success still depends on deriving the operative and modal content attributed to that organization.

**Primary-source assessment, limited reading:** Barbara Vetter's [“Replies” (2020), pp.199–203](https://www.philinq.it/index.php/philinq/article/download/282/187/859) explicitly treats potentiality as primitive. Her response to Bird examines how conditional dispositions could ground nonconditional possibilities, lists three relevant assumptions, and leaves the complete solution open. She distinguishes a route to actualized potentiality from the further problem of nonactualized possibilities. This is a substantive comparator for immanent capacities, but it supplies no assumption-free deduction for our foundation. This assessment does not refute her programme or adopt her ontology.

Only those five printed pages are used here; the whole book and the entire symposium are not claimed as read. This is a link-only assessment, with no imported or modified source edition. Preliminary search results and abstracts for other works are not evidence for a completed foundational argument.

Our own constitutive argument must therefore identify which features of organization entail which possibilities. If “complete operative organization” already includes the very continuation law being sought, the deduction may be valid but the foundational premise remains. If it means only an independently established actual arrangement, a further argument must establish its modal consequences. The earlier [immanent-differentiation assessment](IMMANENT-DIFFERENTIATION-TEST.md) and [effective-law derivation](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) remain relevant at their stated scopes; they are not reopened as unfinished local mechanisms.

## 7. Can the developed whole discharge its own basis?

### The affirmative route must remain available

Nor does it require the actual whole to consist of independently justified pieces.

The positive question is more precise: can the completed proof family supply derivations of the starting claims whose expansion no longer uses those claims as premises? A developed argument might answer yes. Merely returning to a named starting claim does not answer either way; we have to inspect the instantiated proof obligations.

### A positive proof construction, with no root-truth premise

> For every root formula, a derivation with **empty reusable basis and empty local context**.

## 8. Does dependent origination supply the substantive deduction?

### The candidate and its actual target

The strongest remaining proposal need not begin with independently constituted things and then attach relations. It can begin with organization whose differentiated terms have their identities and capacities through how the organization occurs. On this proposal, conditions, occurrence and continuation are differentiated relationships within one unfolding. The proposed foundational conclusion is that formation and change necessarily have this relational constitution throughout the intended scope.

This is an affirmative candidate to investigate, not an adopted premise. Its target includes actual causal participation and constraints on further possibilities. It is stronger than the statement that an analyst can describe any event with a relation. It need not mean that every event is fully determined by earlier events, that all things directly affect all others, or that every organization continues indefinitely.

### Primary reading and its scope

The [CBETA edition of 《中論》 hosted by National Taiwan University](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/BDLM/sutra/html/T30/T30n1564.htm) identifies Nāgārjuna's verses, commentary attributed to 青目, and Kumārajīva's translation. Chapter 1 examines production and the relational attribution of conditions. Chapter 15 contrasts conditioned formation with an unmade, independent nature. Chapter 24 connects dependent origination, emptiness and designation while resisting the objection that emptiness destroys ordinary causation. Its commentary also denies treating emptiness as independently established. These passages support a comparison with constitutive dependence; they do not straightforwardly furnish an independent generative substance or an assumption-free deduction of our process law.

Reading: chapter 1, T30.0002b07–0003c05; chapter 15, T30.0019c20–0020c05; selected chapter 24, T30.0032c12–0033b27. The edition reports version 1.14, 5 November 2001. This is a link-only primary reading, not a Sanskrit collation or a claim to have read the entire work. Attribution to the commentary is kept distinct from attribution to the verses.

For a different grounding strategy, Leibniz's [*Monadology*, §§31–38, Latta translation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Monadology_%28Leibniz%2C_tr._Latta%29), states a sufficient-reason principle alongside noncontradiction, distinguishes necessary from contingent truths, and seeks a final ground beyond the contingent series. We do not adopt that move. It identifies additional argumentative work that would be required if our proposal used a similarly strong reason-for-every-fact demand. No conclusion about the whole of Leibniz's philosophy is drawn from these passages.

### What the constitutive argument earns

Consider an organization independently identified through connected operation. Its differentiation is not exhausted by a list of items considered apart from their arrangement. For example, where a retained state participates in a later operation, preserving a duplicate value while eliminating the operative uptake does not preserve that target. The [existing direct constitutive assessment](CONSTITUTIVE-WARRANT-ARGUMENT.md) and [general argument](GENERAL-FOUNDATION-ARGUMENT.md) already establish why explanatory fidelity must retain this content. Their result is reused here, not counted again.

The new assessment concerns the proposed further grounding. Treating the relevant organization as complete independently of the relationships through which it is constituted would remove part of what we undertook to explain. Looking for a wholly separate condition-maker behind those relationships would repeat that separation. Once an operative relationship is established, it need not receive an additional external connection merely to be operative. None of this makes the attribution automatically correct.

Likewise, the roles of condition and consequence need not belong permanently to different sorts of things. What one activity forms can participate in what follows; the same material or relation can have different roles in a changed arrangement. This role account preserves real differentiation and the possibility of irreversible loss. It does not turn all consequences into continuing conditions, and it does not identify a past relation's existence with its current efficacy.

The positive result is therefore a more disciplined constitutive explanation: investigate the formation and transformation of these roles together, without demanding separately self-sufficient carriers of the roles. The argument's scope remains the actual relationships established or deductively justified. A successful exclusion of a misdescription does not itself establish the full positive law over unexamined cases.

### Two stronger steps that must not enter unnoticed

**From absence of independent constitution to a particular modal law.** A rejection of self-sufficient organization does not yet specify which variations retain the organization, which transformations it permits, or why one stated continuation rule covers all relevant cases. If the explanation of dependence already includes that rule, the rule's content has been retained as a premise. If dependence means only that the whole is not an aggregation of independently complete units, the asserted restrictions on continuation still require an argument. This diagnoses the candidate inference; it is not a claim that the classical text accepts either simplified reconstruction.

**From a relational explanation to a sufficient reason selecting each outcome.** The canonical root explicitly permits stochastic activity. A necessary account of how conditions constrain possible changes therefore need not supply a necessitating antecedent for each actual outcome. Requiring such an antecedent would strengthen the task beyond its stated scope. Conversely, admitting stochasticity is not a warrant for any chosen transition relation or probability law. The organization of the alternatives, including any distribution attributed to them, still needs the warrant the foundation claims.

This corrects a risk in our language of “sufficient common explanation.” The word *sufficient* there concerns adequacy to the explanatory task, not a covert requirement of deterministic production. A bare statement that every fact must have a reason would be a new foundational commitment to justify, not a consequence supplied by the word *nondual*. We must not add that stronger principle simply to make the remaining research look deductive.

### A matched challenge and the strongest reply

A challenge can retain the documented formation, retained effects, uptake, changes of conditions and failures, while accepting that these are relations within the same unfolding. It can also accept the impossibility of preserving that very organization while deleting its constitutive relationships. What it withholds is the inference that rejecting independent self-sufficiency establishes every modal restriction asserted by the proposed general law.

This is a challenge to the **warrant of the extension**, not a counterexample asserting that the established dependencies never operated. It does not erase the fuller target into an output list, postulate an independently existing observer, or require an inert starting point. A successful reply must identify the constitutive content whose denial would misdescribe the claimed target across the intended scope. Calling the challenge itself relational shows that it shares relational content; it does not yet establish the disputed extent of that content.

Nor should the foundation respond by treating “the whole” as a new independently complete substance with intrinsic powers. That would relocate the unexplained self-sufficiency instead of explaining the differentiated relationships. This is a constraint on our proposed inference, not a claim that Ren's monism already makes that mistake. The nondual method remains active without being declared a proved ontology.

### Deductive status and the next argument

An important distinction prevents a false completion gate. A foundational law can be a necessarily valid universal conditional without requiring each contingent application condition to be necessary. The problem is whether its antecedent legitimately identifies the target or smuggles in the law to be derived. “For any organized formation, its continuations have these constraints” is a candidate needing substantive justification. “For any formation satisfying these constraints, these constraints hold” cannot perform that justification. The point is not to reject conditional form; it is to inspect its content.

**本轮没有把缘起改写为已被证明的宇宙公理。** 对已辨认的构成关系，不能先删去相依内容，再索取一个外在联结；但由此到所主张的全部可能性约束，仍须演绎根据。这里的充分说明不等于每个随机结果都由先前状态唯一决定，也不把整体变成一个新的独立自性实体。必要的条件命题可以成为根基的一部分；把待证规律放进适用条件则不能完成奠基。

No new mathematical proof claim, Lean module or experiment is introduced by this philosophical assessment. Sections 2 and 7 retain their actual checked scopes; their empty dependency lists do not validate this new constitutive argument. This is author counterreview, not independent acceptance, and not a proof that the stronger task is impossible.

The exact next inference is now **relational constitution → warranted constraints on continuations**, allowing stochastic, finite and destructive regimes. The proposed deduction must earn those constraints at the claimed scope without either assuming their full content or demanding an extra deterministic reason for every realized outcome. The active goal remains unfinished at Ren's deductive standard.

## 9. Necessary common structure and contingent local possibilities

### Correct the quantifier before attempting another deduction

The preceding section's references to “every modal restriction” and “the full positive law” can invite an excessive requirement: deriving the exact possibilities of every particular system from the foundation alone. The [canonical scope](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) explicitly retains actual laws, couplings and boundary conditions in applications. Ren's demand makes **each foundational claim** deductively necessary; it does not say that all contingent particulars must become foundational claims.

The correction concerns our test, not a relaxation requested from Ren. A necessary common law can constrain systems with different local continuations. We must justify the content and scope of the common law. We need not derive one exhaustive continuation catalogue shared by all systems, nor determine every particular catalogue from that law. Conversely, calling an unresolved foundational commitment a local parameter does not discharge it.

Three obligations therefore have different roles:

| Obligation | What would satisfy it | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Necessity of the common constitutive process law | A deduction of its substantive content for independently identified organized formation, with the interpretation warranted | Unfinished; necessary joint realization is already credited but is not the whole formation-and-change explanation |
| Adequacy of a particular continuation account | Warrant that the stated operations, couplings and allowed variations preserve the relationships claimed in that application | Completed for named formal constructions; actual examples retain their evidential scope and possible revision |
| Deduction of every exact local law from the foundation | A substantially stronger theory specifying every application's alternatives | Not a completion gate imposed by the current task |

“Independently identified” does not introduce an observer outside the process. It requires identifying the explanatory target without selecting cases solely because they satisfy the conclusion. Recognition and proof remain activities within the unfolding, with their own warrant obligations.

### What the recent results actually contribute

The [variation-warrant study](VARIATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md) derives an exact attained domain and finite-continuation correspondence from its native operations. The [jointness study](EXPLANATORY-JOINTNESS-INTEGRATION.md) explains why separate possibility descriptions can omit actual sharing, and proves specified correspondence and plan-lifting results under explicit conditions. These are substantive completed contributions. Neither must derive every local law in reality to remain valid.

For the foundation, their contribution is to expose what a common explanation must retain: formed relations, relevant restrictions and actual shared realization. A proof with an arbitrary transition relation can be valid uniformly across different local laws. That uniformity alone does not establish that the relation represents the relevant generative content, or that all organized formation has the proposed content. The unresolved inference concerns that content and coverage, not the mere presence of a law parameter in a theorem.

### Preserve formation and loss in the constitutive argument

One possible argument says that an organization cannot remain that organization while losing relationships essential to it. At most, this constrains **continuation as that organization**. The foundation also undertakes to explain transformation and cessation. It cannot reject those outcomes merely because they no longer instantiate an earlier organizational identity. Nor can the identity constraint explain by itself why a relation forms, stays operative, changes or disappears.

This is a scope distinction within the one unfolding. It does not posit enduring things on one side and externally imposed changes on the other. The positive task is to explain differentiated organization and the changes in its realizing relations together. Existing support-loss, replacement and destructive histories must remain admissible tests of that account.

### Counterreview and the remaining target

The strongest objection to this correction is that it could shelter every unexplained commitment among the local inputs. The answer is to retain the actual foundational claim register. Consequential differentiation, possible composition and the common formation-and-change relationship still require the deductive warrant demanded in section 1. They are not deleted or relabeled as mere application facts. Local variability is permitted; foundational necessity is not waived.

The next substantive question is consequently: **why must independently identifiable organized formation have the claimed consequential relationships through which activity forms or changes later conditions?** An answer must preserve actual formation, transformation and loss, and derive a common explanatory restriction rather than only rename a supplied transition relation. Exact local possibilities need further warrant only to the extent that the answer claims them.

**中文结论。** 根基主张必须演绎必然，不等于每个具体系统的规律、边界和全部后续选择都须由根基独自决定。不能把这个额外要求当作持续未完成的理由；也不能反过来将未证的共同生成关系改名为局部输入。已完成的局部范围与共同实现证明继续复用。真正尚缺的是：组织的形成为何必然具有所主张的后续条件形成关系，并涵盖转化及真实结束。

## 10. Does becoming itself establish operative inheritance?

### A stronger positive candidate

Perhaps the missing connection is not a separate rule added after formation. An unfolding might be temporally constituted: what has occurred enters what is occurring, so that further activity already includes the effects of its own development. On this proposal, there is no independently complete present awaiting a connection to an independently complete past. The question is whether this constitution can supply the claimed necessary generative relationship.

The proposal must not be reduced in advance to a sequence of isolated snapshots. It includes the actual transition and the formation of its differentiated terms. Equally, a temporal vocabulary cannot by itself certify the operative content it attributes. We must distinguish the positive claim from a representation that simply retains every preceding item in a list.

### Primary comparison and reading scope

Henri Bergson's [*Creative Evolution*, Arthur Mitchell translation, 1911](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/26163/26163-h/26163-h.htm), provides a direct comparison. In chapter I, pp. 1–11, he treats psychic states as interpenetrating change, rejects an unchanging ego joining separate states, and understands retained past through present tendencies rather than a storage faculty. He explicitly asks whether this account extends to existence generally, then considers systems within the whole. In chapter IV, the waiting-and-creation discussion, pp. 338–340, connects experienced succession with a claim about novelty in the concrete whole. These passages offer a substantive temporal account; they do not present a premise-free deduction of our universal systems law.

This is a link-only reading of those passages in the Project Gutenberg edition, ebook 26163, released 1 August 2008. It is not a French-text collation, a reading of the entire book or an endorsement of its historical scientific claims. In particular, our assessment does not silently adopt a cosmic consciousness or a necessary division between life and inert matter.

### What this changes in our own candidate

**Operative inheritance need not mean keeping an unchanged object or a readable record.** An earlier activity can change an arrangement's present tendencies, access, responsiveness or coupled relations. Its effect then participates through what the arrangement has become. No extra memory-bearing substance need be installed between earlier and later activity. This formulation keeps formation and present operation together, rather than first separating them and then demanding a third connector.

This is compatible with the existing [formed effective-law account](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-INTEGRATION.md), which earns a selected effective description from native operations. Its result is reused: sufficient present conditions need not be a literal copy of the whole history. Whether the selected description preserves what matters remains answerable to the actual operations and the explanatory question.

It also makes the treatment of loss more precise. Loss of explicit recall does not by itself establish loss of every operative effect; a changed habit or relation might remain. Conversely, an account must not declare every past event presently effective merely because the event still belongs to the history. Effects can be interrupted, superseded or cease to matter for a specified continuation. The account has to trace the relevant relation rather than make either inference from the word *memory*.

### The attempted deduction and its missing step

The strongest proposed argument is: formation is a real transition within one unfolding; the later organization is the achieved continuation of that transition; therefore prior activity necessarily participates in the conditions of further activity. The first two statements preserve more than a collection of unrelated observations. The final step nevertheless changes the question from **which development this is** to **what currently enables, constrains or produces its continuation**.

If “achieved continuation” already means that prior effects remain operative, the final step retains that meaning but has not independently established it. If it means historical belonging alone, the required operative content still needs justification. This distinction does not assert that the two relationships exist in separate realities. It asks whether one stated ground justifies the stronger relationship in that same reality.

A matched challenge can accept the actual transition, the formation of current dispositions and every established continuing effect, while withholding the claim that temporal constitution alone proves such efficacy throughout the intended scope. It need not detach the present from the whole or deny the investigated development. The best affirmative reply would identify the independently specified content of organized formation whose denial entails the required misdescription, without including operative inheritance in that content solely to secure the conclusion. That reply remains to be established.

### Do not strengthen the foundation to rescue the argument

The current generative root does not require complete preservation of all past content, irreversible change in every system, constant novelty, or unpredictability. Making these additional claims foundational would create new deductive obligations. Nor does a dependence on elapsed activity require that every outcome be undetermined. We therefore retain the temporal candidate's useful account of transformed efficacy while declining to use those stronger commitments as unproved steps.

The [existing variation result](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/VariationWarrant.lean), `normalization_does_not_preserve_original_word`, already distinguishes histories that reach the same selected endpoint. It concerns that native finite model and its selected state, not the full identity of concrete duration. Using it as a disproof of the stronger temporal account would violate the nondual method by weakening the target. No such refutation is claimed here.

### Result and next discriminating obligation

**Positive revision:** articulate retained efficacy as the changed organization of present relations and dispositions; do not make it depend on an extra register or unchanged carrier. **Remaining obligation:** justify why that operative relation is necessary for the independently identified class of organized formation, rather than inferred from historical belonging or experiential temporal continuity alone. This keeps section 9's scope: it does not demand an exhaustive local possibility catalogue.

**中文结论。** 过去活动的作用可以体现在当下倾向与关系的形成中，不必原样保存为一个物件或可读记录；回忆消失也不独自证明全部作用消失。但“属于同一生成史”与“仍参与后续条件”尚不能未经论证等同。必须给出这种作用接续在所主张范围内的演绎根据，不能为救论证而补入普遍保存、不可逆或不断新颖等公理。

This is an affirmative reconstruction, a scoped primary comparison and author counterreview. No new mathematical theorem or Lean run is claimed. Existing checked results retain their statements and source-bound receipts; they do not certify this philosophical inference. The stronger foundation remains unfinished, with no universal impossibility claim and no new independent acceptance.

## 14. A necessary constraint on relational formation

### Advance the missing connection through a general exclusion

Necessary joint realization alone did not explain how new organization forms. A stronger question is available: **can wholly independent transformations account for a newly formed restriction between independently identified aspects of one unfolding?** This asks about every transition relation of a specified form, rather than selecting another favorable mechanism. Independence is the candidate explanation being tested, not a primitive ontology or an axiom of the generative account.

Fix two aspects whose meanings remain the same over the comparison. Let the admitted joint domain be a predicate \(P(a,b)\). Call it *recombinable* (the Lean name is `Rectangular`) when any two admitted pairs can be mixed:

\[
P(a,b)\land P(a',b')\ \Longrightarrow\ P(a,b').
\]

This is a property of admitted possibilities, not a definition of organization, causal independence or statistical independence. The pair notation differentiates readings within one domain. It does not say that the readings have independent existence or that every real domain permits their recombination. The existing [system-individuation result](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md) has already shown why arbitrary independent variation cannot be presumed.

Consider any two transition relations \(L(a,c)\) and \(R(b,d)\). They may be nondeterministic, partial or destructive. Their independent joint relation admits exactly the pairs satisfying both. Its complete image of the initial domain is:

\[
Q(c,d)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists a,b\;P(a,b)\land L(a,c)\land R(b,d).
\]

**Deduction:** if \(P\) is recombinable, so is \(Q\). Take witnesses for two outputs \((c,d)\) and \((c',d')\). Recombine the first witness's first aspect with the second witness's second aspect. The initial-domain condition admits that mixed input; the two respective transition witnesses yield \((c,d')\). The proof works for arbitrary types and relations. It uses neither a probability distribution nor a particular event law.

Consequently, if the complete resulting domain has a genuinely new joint exclusion, **no factorization of the proposed transition relation into those independent aspect-wise relations can explain it**. This is an exclusion of every such factorization, not merely the failure of one guessed mechanism. Induction extends preservation to every finite sequence of independent transitions, even when the local relations vary at each stage. A nonrecombinable target therefore has no exact history of that kind from a recombinable initial domain.

### Why this is more than a renamed premise

The starting definitions do not declare that new organization needs interaction. They specify the proposed independent operations and an independently testable joint-domain property. The proof constructs the allegedly excluded mixed continuation. An account that both admits the two original continuations and denies the mixed one cannot retain all those independent-operation conditions. That gives a necessary explanatory restriction on formation, rather than merely repeating that whatever is defined as generative is generative.

The restriction is stable under separate injective recodings of the two aspects, proved in both directions. It is not invariant under arbitrary changes of the explanatory question. Replacing two readings with two copies of the same reading can create a diagonal displayed domain without a new operative coupling in the original process. The meanings of the aspects, the compared stages and the complete admitted domain must therefore stay accountable to the target. No unique smallest decomposition is required.

This result strengthens the existing [native diagonal-domain construction](VARIATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md): that construction exhibits one formation; the new theorem excludes the entire independent-transition class as its explanation from the full initial domain. The copying example is reused as an elementary mathematical control, not advertised as a new physical mechanism or a discovery of the underlying mathematics.

## 15. Preserve the unfolding while testing its aspect distinctions

### Why generating the domain again would miss the present question

The existing variation study already derives a complete domain from a specified formation law, and `HistoryRealization` already realizes a supplied finite-history language. Neither result needs repeating. Both retain the content of the underlying law or history language. Section 14 adds a general constraint on possible independent explanations, but its reference to two identified aspects carries substantive content. Its recoding theorem covered separate injective renamings of each aspect. Its discussion also allowed a duplicated reading to create a displayed constraint. The new test is stronger: **can a fully invertible change of representation alter the coordinate test while preserving the complete declared unfolding?** No event, state, history or alternative is deleted in this test.

### An exact reversible comparison

Let a state have two Boolean readings \((a,b)\). The first presentation has the transition

\[
F(a,b)=(0,b).
\]

It resets the first reading and retains the second. Its transition relation factorizes into two independent coordinate relations. Starting from every pair, the exact image is \(\{(0,0),(0,1)\}\), which permits recombination.

Use the invertible recoding

\[
E(a,b)=(a\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}b,b),\qquad E(E(s))=s.
\]

In the new coordinates \((c,d)\), the same transition is

\[
E(F(E(c,d)))=(d,d).
\]

This is the copying relation. Its image of the full carrier is diagonal, and section 14's theorem excludes **every** independent factorization in these displayed coordinates. Nevertheless, the recoding is its own inverse. Every state remains distinguishable, and the two presentations have exactly corresponding transitions. The previous unrestricted-variation assumption is not being erased to create the result: both initial domains are the complete four-state carrier.

The [new Lean companion](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/RelationalCoordinates.lean) checks the involution, injectivity, step equivalence, opposing factorization results and exact image correspondence. Reusing the original `GenerativeCore.trace_map`, it proves equivalence for **every finite labelled history** and for arbitrary endpoint questions decoded with their original meanings. A further theorem covers every infinite stepwise path. These are universal path results for these declared processes, not agreement on selected short simulations. No probability distribution, duration or extra intervention repertoire is claimed to be preserved when none was specified.

Thus the mathematical law and all of its histories, considered without a privileged coordinate interpretation, do not make displayed factorization invariant. It would be invalid to infer that one presentation has acquired a new physical interaction merely because its displayed update copies one coordinate into another. The relational reading \(a\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}b\) was introduced by the recoding; its dependence is real at that descriptive resolution, but is not by itself a second physical history.

### The positive invariant retains what the readings mean

This does not erase differentiated relations. The earlier first reading is recovered as \(c\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}d\); the earlier second remains \(d\). Applying those original reading meanings to the encoded image gives the same recombinable domain as before. The companion proves this instance and a general theorem: transporting both the admitted domain and its readings through a decoding map with a constructive inverse preserves the complete reading domain, hence preserves its recombination test.

That theorem does not require two independently existing objects or a uniquely smallest physical partition. It requires keeping the chosen question's meanings accountable to the unfolding. A new coordinate chart may mix those meanings; transporting them correctly keeps the original question fixed. An arbitrary fresh pair of displayed coordinates asks a different question.

This gives the nondual method a concrete formal check. Analytical distinctions can differentiate one process without constituting its primitive independent parts. Conversely, their being analytical does not make their actual operative roles fictitious or freely interchangeable. Those roles need their own warrant, and an equivalent account must carry them through its representation if they belong to the explanatory target.

### Counterreview and the exact effect on the foundational route

The comparison is not a counterexample to section 14. That theorem is explicitly relative to fixed aspects, and its proof is unchanged. Nor does the comparison refute the broader generative theory: the whole declared transition and its consequences are retained exactly. What fails is a proposed shortcut from **coordinate nonfactorization** to **an intrinsically identified causal division or newly formed physical interaction**, without warrant for the coordinate meanings.

The example also does not prove that actual roles can never be derived from a richer process account. Particular operations, their composition and their formed access relations can distinguish roles. That additional content would have to be exhibited and transported too; calling the displayed variables components cannot replace it. The [earlier system-individuation result](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md) already distinguishes predictive sufficiency from operative participation and rejects a mandatory least carrier. This new result concerns reversible descriptions of the complete specified dynamics, rather than only an incomplete observation or correlated output sample.

**Revised next bridge:** identify the relationships that differentiate operative roles within the unfolding, and show why their participation constrains continuation independently of the choice of presentation. Do not derive a domain from a supplied law and then treat the law's arbitrary coordinate split as that missing identification. The new transport theorem supplies a consistency test for such an account; it does not supply its actual constitutive content. The common process law remains to be deductively grounded.

## 16. Derive distinctions from an operation without choosing parts first

### Replace a separately chosen split with a derived comparison

Section 15 did not show that every distinction is arbitrary. It showed that displayed coordinate factorization cannot alone identify operative parts. A positive next step is to begin with a declared operation and derive which distinctions its consequences retain. This derives an operation-relative comparison; it does not yet derive the actual operation from an empty foundation.

For any operation relation \(R(s,u)\), two starting configurations have the same permitted successors exactly when

\[
\forall u\;\bigl(R(s,u)\leftrightarrow R(t,u)\bigr).
\]

This comparison does not require deterministic or total dynamics. The [new companion](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/OperationRoles.lean) proves that it is preserved and reflected by a faithful recoding covering the target states. The declared successor relation supplies its content; no coordinate decomposition or independent part inventory is required. Equal possibility support does not assert equal probabilities, internal implementations or histories omitted from the represented configurations.

For a deterministic operation \(F\), the comparison reduces exactly to equality of results, \(F(s)=F(t)\). This identifies distinctions merged by the operation's result. A later function of that same result cannot distinguish the merged pair. This last conclusion does not forbid another retained channel, fresh evidence about the earlier history, or further relations omitted from the chosen interface. It is not a claim that physical information has vanished from the whole.

Both the merged-result comparison and the fixed-state relation are proved covariant under recoding of the operation. Unlike the coordinate-factorization test, they do not change merely because a new chart mixes the displayed coordinates while preserving the operation itself.

### A constructive description of the retained distinctions

For operations satisfying the **local** condition \(F(F(s))=F(s)\), the result itself supplies a fixed representative. This condition is idempotence; it is not asserted as a universal law of activity. The companion derives that the attained configurations are exactly the fixed configurations and constructs

\[
\rho(s)=F(s)\in\operatorname{Fix}(F).
\]

A reading \(q\) is unchanged by the operation when \(q(F(s))=q(s)\). The new universal result is:

\[
q\text{ is unchanged}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\bar q:\operatorname{Fix}(F)\to V\;\forall s\;\bar q(\rho(s))=q(s).
\]

The factor is constructed by evaluating \(q\) on a fixed representative. It is unique pointwise, because every fixed representative is attained. The proof uses neither a chosen representative from an equivalence class nor a choice axiom. Equality of operation results also agrees exactly with agreement of all invariant state-valued readings; the converse uses the native output reading \(F\), whose invariance is proved from the same local condition.

This is more than naming an equivalence relation. It proves an exact characterization of every invariant reading and supplies its factor constructively. The retained description is determined by the specified operation rather than by choosing one coordinate in advance. It does not prove that such a reading is physically measured, that it is an independent component, or that it has normative significance.

### Apply it to the unchanged reset/copy operation

The companion identifies its two functions with the existing `ResetLeft` and `CopyRight` graphs; it does not replace their laws. Both satisfy idempotence by direct proof. The mixed-coordinate map intertwines them, and the merged-result relation is identical under that recoding.

In each presentation two inputs yield the same result exactly when their second readings agree. The original first reading is not invariant; the second is. Thus an operation-relative retained distinction can be derived and carried through the same representation change that altered the displayed independence test. This supplies a positive answer to that local part of section 15's challenge without making coordinates into fundamentally independent entities.

The earlier [system-support results](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md) still distinguish predictive sufficiency from operative participation. This new construction does not erase that boundary. Knowing the exact input/output operation can determine its invariant readings while still leaving open how a physical implementation forms, realizes or consumes them. Such implementation relations must remain in the target wherever the account claims to explain them.

### Controls against turning a local derivation into a universal premise

Bit-flipping is proved non-idempotent and can attain a state it does not fix. The fixed-representative construction therefore cannot be promoted to a universal account of every operation. The general successor-profile theorem remains available without that condition, but it has not thereby become a characterization of all retained physical organization.

A second checked control uses the unchanged reset process. Its two different retained results coexist with exactly the same feasible finite action words: every word over its single action is feasible from both states. Retained outcome differences therefore need not change action availability. Questions about outcomes and questions about enabled actions must remain distinct at their declared interfaces. The richer outcome distinctions are preserved, not erased to obtain the control.

**Counterreview:** the comparison begins with a declared operation and a state interface. If the operation is an arbitrary mathematical choice, the derived distinction is only relative to that choice. If an actual operation is independently warranted, the theorem can remove the need to supply an additional arbitrary split for this question. Neither case proves that all actual organization must instantiate one deterministic stable operation. Calling the mathematical comparison a derived role must not conceal the unresolved actual operation or substitute input/output coincidence for constitutive participation.

## 18. Does formed differentiation require an antecedent asymmetry?

### The direct affirmative argument and its exact question

One proposed route to necessary generativity starts from a newly differentiated organization: its formation cannot be explained by an account that treats all relevant alternatives identically while claiming that just this outcome must follow. This can place a real restriction on explanations. It might then be strengthened to say that the outcome's differentiation necessarily already existed among its antecedent conditions, establishing the operative inheritance required by the common law.

The strengthened inference needs testing. The canonical foundation permits stochastic, finite and destructive regimes. It must not silently equate the necessity of a law with uniqueness of each outcome. Nor is every organization a broken symmetry: the test below concerns a specified transformation and a target distinction, not a definition of organization or a classification of all actual systems.

Following the nondual method, the transformation acts on the configuration of one declared process. It does not introduce independent things that must subsequently be connected. Which transformation preserves the relevant relationships, what a configuration retains, and what counts as the same event remain accountable to the target. These are stated inputs, not universal physical symmetries assumed by the foundation.

### A necessary restriction for arbitrary finite histories

The [new Lean companion](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/FormationSymmetry.lean) reuses the original core's `Trace` and `trace_map`. Let \(g\) transform configurations while preserving every declared transition, with the event labels fixed. Suppose \(g(s)=s\), and a finite event word takes \(s\) to \(t\).

The preserved transition relation also admits that same word from \(s\) to \(g(t)\). Hence:

> **If the endpoint of this history is unique, then \(g(t)=t\). If \(g(t)\ne t\), the proof explicitly supplies a distinct permitted endpoint for the same initial configuration and event word.**

No excluded middle or choice is needed to exhibit the alternative. Uniqueness is required only for the declared initial configuration and word; global deterministic dynamics are a sufficient condition, proved separately by induction, not a prerequisite imposed on the whole theory. The checked negative form excludes the conjunction of symmetry preservation, a fixed start and a unique endpoint when the endpoint breaks the transformation.

When \(g\) is an involution, the whole endpoint domain is invariant under it. This is a statement about the **set of admitted outcomes**, not a statement that every member is individually fixed. The distinction remains even when the formation is retained through arbitrarily long finite continuations.

This is an implementation-independent necessary constraint within the stated process interface. It can expose a missing asymmetry, omitted event distinction or unjustified uniqueness claim in a proposed explanation. It does not prove that every actual process has this interface, a particular symmetry or a uniquely determined outcome.

### A formed distinction that persists without a prior selected branch

The scope control uses three configurations of one process: `none`, `some false`, and `some true`. Here `none` means **no selected branch in this interface**, not absence of all conditions, activity or organization. The single event can take it to either selected branch; once a branch is selected, subsequent events retain it. The whole law and admitted alternatives are supplied explicitly.

Exchanging the two selected branches fixes `none` and preserves the complete transition relation. Nevertheless, for every finite \(n\), there is a history of \(n+1\) events from `none` to either selected branch. Every finite continuation from a selected branch retains that branch. Thus the example includes a persistent differentiated result and later use of the formed state in the declared update relation; it is not a forgotten output label appended to an otherwise unchanged process.

Both individual results are asymmetric under the exchange, while their joint possibility domain is symmetric. The Lean proof directly refutes endpoint uniqueness. Therefore the proposed inference from a formed asymmetry to **the same antecedent asymmetry** fails for this declared relational model when uniqueness is omitted. The process is itself generative at its stated scope, so this is not a nongenerative counterworld or a refutation of the broader theory. No physical randomness, probability distribution, experimental realization or metaphysical indeterminism is claimed.

### A deterministic account can relocate the difference without erasing its burden

An alternative representation adds a Boolean seed within the same whole configuration. The update sends `(none, b)` to `(some b, b)` and retains a selected branch thereafter. Exchanging both the branch and seed commutes with this update. The refined process is deterministic, and each of the two retained branch histories has an explicit seeded realization for every finite length.

But `(none, b)` is not fixed by that exchange: the antecedent seed supplies the asymmetry. Every fine trace projects to an admitted coarse trace, while a fixed fine initial seed cannot realize the opposite coarse branch. The latter failure is checked explicitly. ### The constructed relation and its exact criterion

> For any two admitted histories with the same code, every finite suffix is admitted after one if and only if it is admitted after the other.

The checked control admits every Boolean word and assigns code `true` only to the history `[false]`, assigning `false` to every other history. All histories have the same continuation language, so the derived process is exact for finite words. Yet it permits the abstract edge `false --false--> false`, witnessed by `[true] → [true,false]`. Starting instead from the empty history, the same letter ends at `[false]`, whose code is `true`.

Consequently finite-word exactness does not require exact encoded endpoints. The core's stronger lifting condition remains correct and useful for its richer query contract; it is not a compulsory premise for this narrower one.
