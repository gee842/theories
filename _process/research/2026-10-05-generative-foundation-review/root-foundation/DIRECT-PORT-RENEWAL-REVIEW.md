## 2. Funding, finite source and formation

`inquiry_recovers_pair` requires an intact reset, open contact, native capacity, at least twenty source units, a consistent recovery record and initial held support of at least eight draws. The existing inquiry constructor needs seven; the stronger initial bound supports the succeeding recovery at the state actually reached. The positive packet may arrive at either probe depending on inversion, so the proof does not assume an earlier arrival to support an otherwise unfunded stage.

The exact held equation includes all eight draws. Consequently the replenishment condition uses eight draws, not the earlier seven-draw condition. The checked high-overhead history is a relevant counterexample to importing the older bound: material is regenerated while held support decreases.

`formation_regenerates_its_pair` explicitly supplies the first pair and a blocked contact, then constructs actual formation before inquiry and recovery. Formation consumes the pair and one draw; recovery restores the pair only after the new failed conversion. The nine-draw balance and changed contact are retained. This is not a return to the initial total state: source, phase history, enactment, transfer and recovery records change. Neither source nor held support is reset by the composition.

`finite_material_regeneration` concatenates rounds at their reached states. Its initial source hypothesis is twenty units per requested round, and each induction step derives the next source guard from exact source decrease. `Ready` deliberately omits source while retaining the other sufficient conditions. Its preservation cannot therefore be read as permission for arbitrarily many further rounds from a fixed finite start. The held bound is sufficient transient funding, not a claimed necessary threshold.

## 3. Material regeneration is not additional organization

The one persistent contact remains open after its formation. Repeated successful rounds accumulate spare pairs, but neither the state type nor these proofs forms another contact or a larger organizational repertoire. Calling the newly produced pair a formation input is accurate under the existing formation law. Calling its repeated production proof of increasing diversity, complexity or indefinite regeneration would exceed the result.

Likewise, recorded failed conversion is related to pair recovery by a supplied event law. These proofs establish the consequences of that law together with native conversion and funding; they do not derive a general physical conversion from dissipated energy into material, an origin of the law, or ethical goodness. The exposition makes those dependencies explicit.

The possibility module preserves a useful distinction: a target reachable after an actual prefix was already reachable through that prefix under the same full process and fixed target. Actual formation can nevertheless change the contact condition preserved by native activity. `NativeReachOpen` includes the empty history, so this is not a theorem of funded packet delivery or added future organization. The direct-renewal exposition accurately retains that limitation.
