# How acquired consequences change inquiry

6 October 2026. AI-authored argument under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), including Ren's correction of foundational vocabulary. The named connection is **an acquired consequence changing the organization of further encounters and their interpretation**. Acting, observing, retaining and asking distinguish roles within this unfolding; they are not independently completed systems that an external observer connects.

The positive finding is that a particular connection already exists: an acquired signal changes a retained method, and the changed method alters how long actual inquiry continues before a later test. The stronger claim that this history forms new native access remains unestablished. This distinction prevents a blanket absence claim while identifying the further derivation precisely.

## What changes, and at which scope?

The [mechanism reconstruction](INQUIRY-INTERFACE-MECHANISM.md) distinguishes four relations. These are analytical distinctions within activity, not four ontological layers or universal developmental stages.

| Relation | What would establish a change |
|---|---|
| Retained diagnostic distinctions | Acquired evidence changes which histories or consequences the learner treats as different. |
| Enacted sampling | The retained change alters which encounters occur, their order, or when inquiry continues or ends. |
| Available participation | An actual formed or revised coupling changes what can be encountered or done. |
| Meaning or scope of a claim | Newly relevant consequences, changed access or changed activity require specifying what an earlier identification still warrants. |

One event can participate in several relations. None follows merely from renaming a register. Equally, new wiring is not necessary for a changed inquiry: different continuations through existing connections can expose different consequences.

## The matched history reaches actual sampling

The strongest saved comparison comes from [method revision](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/METHOD-REVISION.md). Two controls share the same three-state process, actual resource arrival, misleading zero receipt and earlier native sequence. In one, the acquired receipt changes the retained method bit; in the other, a matched write operation leaves the bit unchanged. Later dispatch reads that retained bit.

The changed method generates a larger family. Under the same supplied rule for continuing until a unique candidate remains, it requires four acquisition readings rather than three. Both branches subsequently confirm their prediction. This is an existing matched execution: the acquired signal reaches actual further activity, not only an analyst's description of the learner.

The comparison must preserve event roles as well as raw action names. The narrower branch's forward prediction test initially coincides with the broader branch's additional acquisition cycle. The complete broader history subsequently contains one more action/read cycle. Thus the evidence establishes a changed stopping point and a longer total native history; it should not be described as immediate divergence to a different primitive command after the first shared readings.

This is also an adverse case for a stronger claim. The signal is misleading, the process did not need a larger family, and the extra inquiry does not uniquely enable successful prediction. Actual responsiveness to consequences therefore does not establish appropriate responsiveness. The switch follows an acquired operational signal under its supplied update rule; no separately optimized success score is computed. That fact does not vindicate the rule.

## Correcting a hypothesis is not always enlarging an interface

In [abstraction acquisition](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION.md), a prediction failure leads to a distinguishing suffix. The new coordinate repairs a formerly merged pair of histories. Yet the primitive action was already available and the declared semantic question already covered all finite action words. The old one-state hypothesis was inadequate within its original scope. It was not an exact old quotient that became inadequate only when genuinely new access appeared.

Moreover, the primary unary run's fresh endpoint queries have lengths zero through four, which a fixed bounded enumeration could also request. That observed query sequence alone does not identify adaptive sampling. The internal source and mismatch/refinement trace establish a changed diagnostic organization; the separate matched method comparison above supplies the stronger effect on actual inquiry duration. This avoids using one form of evidence as proof of another.

The [mathematical identity result](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/IMMANENT-MATHEMATICAL-IDENTITY.md) supplies a different relation. When an interface genuinely expands while preserving old laws and questions, its exact identity can refine the previous exact identity. If refinement is strict, the old class alone cannot identify the actual finer class. Further interaction, additional retained information or warranted restrictions are required. All accessible information matters; an available archive cannot be silently excluded from the comparison.

These distinctions allow the core's articulation to improve through the branches. “New distinction” is too broad to determine whether a process learned an old relation, acquired a new capability, or changed the situation it was investigating.

## Continuity through transformation does not require preserving every question

An inquiry can remain continuous through actual histories, maintained capacities and the problems those capacities encounter, even while its questions change. The [practice-transformation argument](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) makes this concrete: a method can change the conditions that previously made repeating it appropriate. Holding the same question label fixed would not guarantee that the question still tracks the relevant activity.

For claims that a particular older meaning or operation survives, its correspondence must be shown. The canonical projection for conservative interface expansion cannot automatically be imported when a practice alters the old action law, changes an indicator's meaning or abandons a question. Conversely, dropping a question need not destroy continuity of the people or capacities involved. What persists must be identified through the actual transformation.

This also matters for concern and governance. Inquiry can learn how to keep a programme running while undermining the possibilities of those involved. Adaptive questioning is compatible with that outcome. The case for revision must therefore concern the actual consequences and differentiated continuations at issue, not merely restored participation in a fixed programme. Particular roles, resources, permissions and measures belong where their formation and relevance are established; they are not primitives of all inquiry.

It does not establish formation of the native contact, acquisition of that stopping criterion, or revision of the criterion through the effects of investigating.

The further target is specifically **formation or revision of the criterion for sufficient inquiry through its own consequences**. The present history already changes the stopping point by changing the candidate family under an unchanged uniqueness rule.
