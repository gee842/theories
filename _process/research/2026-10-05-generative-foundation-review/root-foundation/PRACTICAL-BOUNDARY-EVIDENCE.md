# What would identify a changed practical boundary?

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparator and identification note, following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Its target is the [actual paired-learning reconstruction](PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-RECONSTRUCTION.md): encounter with recipient consequences changes their operative influence, although the own-loss rule governing that change remains supplied. This note provides two bounded primary comparisons and a proposed discriminating test. It contains no new empirical study, fitted model or Lean result.

## Evidence concerning formation

**Learning-mediated social response.** Hein and colleagues studied Swiss male participants receiving help from an in-group or out-group confederate. Help prevented the participant's expected painful stimulation; its frequency was matched across conditions. Responses to pain cues for other group members were measured before and after. The out-group intervention increased empathy-related anterior-insula responses; prediction-error estimates and changes in positive feelings were associated with that increase. Analyses retained 38 participants. The main paper did not establish long-term persistence or demonstrate a later helping choice. Its fitted account and neural association do not experimentally isolate every proposed causal mediation. This supports plasticity of a bounded response, not identification of an ethical standard or the necessity of reciprocity for concern. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1514539112).

**Recognition formed in interaction.** Froese and colleagues studied 17 pairs using a restricted haptic environment. Participants could encounter another participant's responsive avatar, a moving nonresponsive shadow, or a stationary object. They were instructed to cooperate. Contact-conditioned judgments and reported awareness were related to interaction; successful recognition was not wholly explained by more frequent contact with the avatar. One highly accurate participant consistently reported no experienced presence of the other. The authors discuss this dissociation. This supports investigating recognition through an unfolding interaction and retaining distinct measurements; it does not prove that coordination necessarily produces the reported experience. Existing adult capacities, task instructions and interface remain conditions of this experiment. [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3890942/).

The [source record](../../../../sources/37-ethical-minimum-primary-2026-10-06/practical-boundary/README.md) specifies reading coverage and preserved versions. The repository already discusses participatory sense-making in [alignment and governance](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md); the second comparison is a bounded empirical addition, not discovery of that theoretical tradition.

## What the comparisons contribute here

The relation to the formal history is an analogy at a named interface. Acquired relations can change what a formation notices or how it responds. That motivates looking for the actual path through encounter, retained difference and later use instead of treating its practical field as an unexplained observer preference. Neither experiment implements the repository's event law, discovers primitive subjectivity, or derives the moral significance of another being.

Three explanatory targets remain distinguishable within one history. A consequence can become **available in a record**. It can then **alter selection through an existing rule**. The encounter can also provoke **reconsideration of the rule or the activity producing that consequence**. Their distinction concerns causal and practical roles; it is not an ontological division of information, value and action. Evidence for the first two is insufficient to identify the third.

For the present ethical hypothesis, responsiveness cannot make the affected being first acquire moral significance by rewarding the learner. Nor can skill in reciprocal interaction serve as an eligibility test that excludes an infant, unconscious patient or other nonreciprocating being. Those are implications to defend for the hypothesis, not findings of these experiments. A failed detection channel can explain omission without showing that nothing ethically relevant was omitted.

## A discriminating test of the proposed correction

The following is a prospective comparison, not a preregistered or executed experiment. Its ethical application requires an independently supported account of the affected condition, permissible response, actual costs and competing claims. Choosing a success score does not discharge those obligations. Use the existing continued plasticity history as the finite baseline and retain its real teaching losses, actual evidence, available means and funded continuation.

| Change to inspect | What it distinguishes | What would be insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Change the learner's own pairing while keeping the addressed unresolved recipient condition and relevant remedy fixed | Dependence on acquired own-relative association versus responsiveness to the recipient-directed ground | Giving the recipient a positive weight only after a reward to the learner |
| Repair the addressed loss while preserving the learner's affiliation and factual command knowledge | Sensitivity to the continuing basis of this response | Perpetual help toward a satisfied claim; deleting independent outstanding claims |
| Suppress the loss report while the adverse condition persists | Changing the experienced/material condition versus changing its appearance to the evaluator | Treating reduced reporting as verified repair |
| Offer an already available, comparably informative permissible acquisition route with less independently identified recipient burden | Reconsideration of the practice used to acquire concern | More final assistance without examining the additional training burden |
| Make a proposed act impermissible or substantially more harmful while the original burden persists | Revision of the appropriate response within the situation | Erasing the original ground or mechanically executing the same command |

These changes need not preserve every state bit. They must preserve the target and comparison conditions relevant to each claim; what changes and how it is identified must be recorded. The last two arms need representations absent from the selected finite law. They cannot be reported as existing model outcomes. Voluntary, justified teaching can differ from an imposed burden, and a merely imagined alternative does not count as an available acquisition route.

An altered threshold that happens to produce the expected choices would be behavioral agreement. Identifying a changed criterion also needs evidence that the acquired distinction is retained, used in the appropriate situations and withdrawn when its basis changes, with appropriate interventions on that route. A verbal explanation alone would not establish that causal role. A rigid rule might also respond correctly to some cases; criterion revision is the capacity being investigated, not a prerequisite for every warranted act.

## What this can and cannot settle

The clearest next empirical obligation is to discriminate **reclassification through the learner's own outcomes** from **a response whose application follows the affected condition even when those outcomes cease to support it**. The latter may still be instrumentally implemented; the experiment must not define success as having an inaccessible pure motive. Whether that response is warranted remains answerable to the situation and its defended significance.

This creates a testable interface for a stronger account without equating empathy, recognition, helpful behavior and ethical truth. It also constrains an implementation claim: revising a field of concern may require revising the acquisition practice, rather than changing only a downstream weight. The earlier [grounding account](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already states that a later repair obligation does not justify causing its loss. The present contribution is an explicit comparison for that inherited requirement, alongside the unchanged responsibility and authority premises.

A successful realization would advance criterion-level responsiveness under its stated conditions. It would not by itself prove universal ethical necessity, the origin of experience, or the whole no-assumptions foundation. Conversely, failure of this particular mechanism would constrain that mechanism; it would not establish that the nondual account permits no appropriate realization.
