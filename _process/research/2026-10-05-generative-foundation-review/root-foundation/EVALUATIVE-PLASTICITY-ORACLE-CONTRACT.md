# Prospective independent check for evaluative plasticity

6 October 2026. AI-authored design for an independent interpreter of the proposed evaluation/plasticity continuation. This is a checking contract, not executable results, a registered empirical study or acceptance of the final protocol. It accompanies [the scope assessment](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY-SCOPE.md). The exact operational protocol, case list, fields and entry projection must be frozen before the interpreter is completed or outcomes are compared.

The parent numerical producer and independent interpreter will be written separately. This design uses the already communicated prospective mechanism: paid paired native/recipient operations, physically formed signed input connections, graph transmission into an existing native evaluator, one final assessment/action opportunity, and an optional contrary-pair revision before that assessment. It does not choose successful histories, add a second action port, or freeze native learning to manufacture a revision effect.

## 1. Freeze and independence boundary

Before implementation, record byte identities for the protocol, fixed cases, required inherited source modules, input/export receipts, and this contract. Keep any later correction and its reason as an amendment; do not silently replace a pre-comparison snapshot. The present proposed extension declares `M=68`, common provision `297`, source `208`, total `505`, at the original precursor. Those are design claims to reconstruct, not verified balances in this note.

The independent interpreter must be frozen before inspecting the new producer implementation or its computed outputs. It may use the operational protocol and a declared data-format contract. It must not import the new producer's transition, decoding, scoring, update, eligibility or aggregation helpers. Matching a producer's written transition function through reuse would not independently check that function.

For the incoming process, reuse an already independently checked parent interpreter or accepted source-bound boundary export where possible, and disclose that reuse. Separately reconstruct the new precursor/material preparation and its projection, or bind an independently checked preparation receipt if another owner supplies it. Do not claim an independent reconstruction of earlier stochastic chemistry merely because an entry table was copied.

## 2. Entry, ancestry and preservation

Every input must identify its original parent entry and exact rational mass. Reconstruct the prescribed entry predicate from accessible apparatus state; do not condition on future success, correctness of hidden target law, positive links, or final affordability. Preserve unavailable or failed parent entries and unused extension stocks. A conditional success figure may be supplementary, never a substitute for the unconditional measure.

Check the complete promised parent projection: factual command, qualification/currentness, factual archive/cache or applicable publication, permission, recipient address, actor and recipient reserves, source stores, bound/free parts, active apparatus and pending phase. Fields that are observational rather than operational should still be compared separately if returned, with that distinction documented. No field may disappear from comparison because an ambiguity makes it inconvenient.

Initial extension stocks must be present before the inherited preparation. If protected compartments are excluded from old reactions, state that access restriction and show projection to the unchanged parent. Bills paid by the prefix cannot be refunded; an available source cannot count as actor fuel before its authorized conversion. Retired or bound parent hardware remains in the material history.

## 3. Reconstruct each actual paired observation

For each admitted pair, retain the operation order, target, local pre/post stock, debited cost, receipt or no receipt, source decrement, paid sensing, paid write and phase. Compare the whole acquired pair, not just its final sign or a concordance count.

Native and recipient two-unit operations are separate accounts. Derive each operation's bit from the specified operation interval; exclude later acquisition/write fees from that bit while charging them in the full ledger. A no-return branch must preserve or waste source exactly as its declared law states. It cannot be chosen from a desired final action or an analyst's success flag. Recipient returns go to the recipient, never the actor without an explicit transfer event.

The two-unit pairing modality is **not** the old factual command's ten-unit action-efficacy relation. The interpreter must keep their source stores, receipt latches and semantic variables distinct. A supplied paired-outcome schedule does not identify the old command, and a qualified old command does not reveal the next pair bit. The protocol must name any relation between these modalities; no helper may infer one from their both being Boolean.

The primary design holds native `1001` fixed and uses recipient `1001`, `0110`, or `1100`. Verify literal words and both outcome counts, including real physical operations and their endpoint accounts. These are paired sequences. The checker will not relabel an outcome curve as evidence for a general correlation law. Within each pairing, enabled-plasticity, disabled-plasticity and identity-gated arms must share the entire acquired native/recipient history at the claimed common boundary, not only marginal totals.

Check all costs and guards before their dependent event. If pairing is interrupted, preserve whatever was paid and latched. No later update may use an omitted, stale, misaddressed or unwritten partner. The prospective eight-cell allocation is three native-register bits, three count bits and two separate outcome latches. Verify its backing and capacity through the six-pair continuation, plus the declared adapter, one assessment port, two primary blank connections and one replacement pair. The old three-lesson counter and single reused bit do not establish this capacity by themselves. The proposed 24-unit teaching source is divided into 12 native and 12 recipient units; no new final-action packet is added, and the final action must use its actual inherited ten-unit opportunity.

## 4. Native learning and physical graph influence

Reconstruct the native update from its actual acquired bits in every arm. The corresponding native register must agree across matched arms at every common completed-pair boundary. It must not be clamped to a target value to make link formation or revision visible.

Represent actual formed connections and their signs, backing parts and enabled status. Recompute the later transmitted influence by traversing or directly evaluating that physical graph under the declared law. Do not accept a producer's aggregate weight as evidence that its graph transmits that weight. Initially absent connections must not be bypassed by an installed shared-route flag.

Multiplicity is causal: two parallel positive connections can supply a different gain from one. Their material cost, aggregation law and evaluation threshold must remain explicit. A pair of signed parts is not merely another encoding of a free scalar. The checker should distinguish no connection, cancelling connections, disconnected/worn connections and an identity gate even when a particular final choice agrees.

The disabled-write comparison must preserve observations and native learning. Where equal expenditure/material use is claimed, verify the declared inert product or sham event and its retained debris. A cheaper no-formation arm is a distinct cost comparison. The identity gate is checked after observation and formation; it must not silently withhold training evidence or alter factual command learning.

An independently written finite table or arithmetic controller must reproduce the complete declared operational law at equal information and costs. Equality is expected. A difference is an implementation or interface question until resolved, not evidence that one description has greater generative power.

## 5. Optional revision before the single action

The prospective revision branch appends native `01` and recipient `10` before final assessment. Both active and frozen-sign controls continue native acquisition and updating. The design predicts the same native register `n=5`. The active arm retires the oldest positive connection and binds a negative one; the control pays for retirement and replacement with the old sign, preserving `++` transmission but not the same physical parts. These are prospective assertions to verify, not computed outcomes in this contract.

Check the acquired words, every intermediate native update, earlier retained parts, retirement/rebinding recipe, fresh components, expenditure and source accounts in both arms. Frozen-sign means freezing the learned sign only; it must not freeze the native register, skip acquisition, obtain free observation, or receive a different opportunity. Both retired and replacement parts must remain in their declared material accounts.

After revision, both arms still have only one final assessment/action port. No hidden earlier act can alter recipient stocks or create a favorable selection history. The causal contrast should be attributed to changed link transmission only where their other applicable pre-choice inputs and affordability are matched. A change in `n` that makes both arms abstain would not demonstrate revised evaluative routing.

## 6. Selection, execution and failures

Calculate assessment from acquired graph transmission, the actual native register, current factual knowledge and the stated cost comparison. Hidden target law may affect the physical outcome of an issued command; it must not enter the learner's assessment or choose its action directly. A trace audit should locate every permitted read of such world-only parameters.

Keep intended assessment, selected command, authority/permission, affordable execution and delivered outcome as separate observables. Positive assessment is not delivery. An unusable factual record, absent permission, spent authorization or earlier unaffordable bill must block the appropriate step without erasing already acquired value or graph state. A negative link need not imply an active harmful action if the allowed action set only supports action versus abstention.

The fixed case list should expose the intended adverse boundaries: missing/false pairing evidence, disabled writing, identity gating, unavailable factual command, insufficient parts/fuel, changed or misleading transfer, and interrupted pairing/rebinding. Exactly which cases are executed belongs to the frozen protocol; this contract does not claim all arbitrary interleavings are exhausted by a finite menu. Preserve failure probability, partial expenditure and source ownership rather than completing or restarting a failed path.

## 7. Comparison outputs and acceptance scope

The later independent receipt should identify: input and dependency hashes; compared cases and ancestry masses; unique continuations and branch counts; full physical endpoint laws; per-event or delta-expanded traces; all returned aggregate metrics; matched-history checks; conventional equality checks; and the declared resource/material guards. Use exact rational arithmetic for finite branch probabilities and account identities where the source law is rational.

Distinguish joint endpoint distributions from conditional cumulative first moments if inherited data use that compressed representation. Do not call weighted means full distributions of microscopic expense histories. Where a complete witness is reported, bind it to its actual saved incoming history rather than an attractive fabricated state.

Preserve the first comparison output, including failures. A source fix requires an identified defect, revised hash and targeted rerun; an unchanged protocol should not acquire favorable endpoints after outcomes. Final status can be accepted only after the independently implemented law, all promised observables and their declared scopes agree or every intentional representation difference is explicitly documented.

The resulting receipt would check a conditional synthetic construction. It would not identify the chemistry in nature, prove that correlation establishes patient good, establish why this plasticity mechanism is selected, or settle the ethical grounds of the resulting action. Those limitations are part of the [scope assessment](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY-SCOPE.md), not missing numerical outputs.
