## Primary evidence actually consulted

**Blount, Barrick, Davidson and Lenski (2012), “Genomic analysis of a key innovation in an experimental Escherichia coli population,” Nature 489, 513–518, DOI 10.1038/nature11514.** [Author-hosted original PDF](https://www.blount-lab.org/uploads/2/3/0/3/23037222/blount_et_al_2012.pdf). Read the abstract and printed pp. 514–516: actualization, engineered regulatory constructs, weak initial growth, reversion and refinement. A duplication connected an aerobically active promoter to a citrate transporter. The engineered single-copy configuration gave weak function in the tested background; loss and copy-number comparisons connected regulation to phenotype. This supports a newly operative relationship, not creation of new matter or a proof of inexhaustible novelty. The assembly witness is an explanatory comparison, not a quantitative reproduction of the genetic mechanism. We do not infer speciation or universal rarity from this experiment.

**Van Hofwegen, Hovde and Minnich (2016), “Rapid Evolution of Citrate Utilization by Escherichia coli by Direct Selection Requires citT and dctA,” DOI 10.1128/JB.00831-15.** [Primary publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26833416/) · [full-text location](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4800869/). Reading scope: indexed author abstract and indexed results passages; direct PMC access returned a challenge and the PubMed direct fetch was incomplete. The reported repeated evolution under different selection conditions limits any inference that the waiting time in the LTEE is an intrinsic constant of the trait. It does not remove the changed regulatory organization or refute contingency within the original protocol. The authors' broader interpretation of novelty is not adopted as an experimentally settled definition.

**Tim Taylor (2019), “Evolutionary Innovations and Where to Find Them,” Artificial Life 25(2), 207–224, DOI 10.1162/artl_a_00290.** [Author publication page](https://www.tim-taylor.com/paper-details/taylor2019evolutionary.html) · [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01883). Read the author's abstract and publication description. The preprint PDF was located but repeated text retrieval failed; a full-text reading is not claimed. Its distinction between exploratory, expansive and transformational novelty and its attention to cross-domain and compositional effects are substantive antecedents. Our unbounded endpoint family remains exploratory relative to its fixed chain grammar. The altered-constructor comparison expands a frozen installed repertoire, not all possibilities of its encompassing formalism. This source prevents treating a larger parameter value as proof of endlessly new kinds.

## Review outcome

The affirmative account survives these objections at its declared conditional scope. Its strongest supported claim is that real formation can change both functional kind and the operative means of further formation without choosing finished outputs from a finite menu, while description revision is governed by actual consequential distinctions. The contact witness is a separate interpretation of the binding account, not an undisclosed extra capability of the earlier constructor. It does **not** establish creation beyond every generative law or indefinite invention of qualitative kinds.

The contribution advances the descriptive premise of the normative programme and the novelty row of the evolutionary programme. It does not reopen completed finite formation or acquired-abstraction studies. Future evidence would revise the application if a supposedly new capability was already operative under matched conditions, the proposed binding intervention did not mediate the effect, uncounted support explained success, or the proposed refinement failed on its declared questions.
