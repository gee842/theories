## 2. Reaction semantics and participant multiplicity

Species and reaction types are arbitrary. Species equality is decidable. Each `Recipe` has finite input and output lists; an inventory gives the natural-number count of each species. An actual transition requires:

1. The reaction is in the declared admitted repertoire.
2. For every species, the total input multiplicity is available in the actual prestate.
3. For every species, `after + input = before + output`.

A retained catalyst occurs in both lists. If a separate copy is consumed while one copy is retained, both required copies must appear in the input list. The guard checks their summed multiplicity; the output cannot supply its own missing prestate participant. This is stronger than checking each informal role separately against the same single copy.

These are exact inventory updates. They do not, for arbitrary recipes, prove conservation of mass, energy or a chosen material weighting. A recipe family can include supplied input, conversion or formation routes; its physical interpretation must account for them. The concrete illustration below has a separately checked declared material invariant.

The inventory is a selected description of differentiated relations within one process. Catalytic organization, consumed substrate and product name roles in that actual interaction. They are not fundamentally independent categories, and the formal species names do not independently establish their biological realization.

## 3. The general structural condition

A selected pool is a decidable predicate on species. `Present pool state` means that at least one selected species has positive count. `Touches pool list` means that a recipe list includes at least one such participant.

`Closed pool recipe` states a structural property: **if the recipe produces a pool member, its input list requires a pool member**. `Entry pool recipe` states the alternative relevant here: the recipe produces a member without requiring any pool member as input. Entry can be assembly from retained components outside the chosen pool. It need not create new matter, synthesize every component, or introduce a new kind of organization.

The condition is checked against the actual input/output lists. It is not a supplied premise that the later organism survives, or a freely assigned Boolean called “organized.” Its applicability nevertheless depends on which species, interactions and alternative routes the representation admits. A physically relevant slow formation, import or assembly route must not be omitted to make a desired pool closed.

The main general results are:

| Checked result | Content |
|---|---|
| `required_pool_is_present` | An actual reaction whose input list touches the pool has an actually present pool member beforehand. |
| `closed_step_preserves_absence` | Under structural closure, positive pool presence after a step entails pool presence before it. |
| `step_pool_origin` | An actual step ending with pool presence has prior presence or is an entry recipe. |
| `trace_pool_origin` | An actual history ending with pool presence has initial presence or an actual entry occurrence. |
| `empty_pool_preserved` | If every admitted recipe is closed, an initially absent pool remains absent along every actual admitted history. |
| `actual_operation_origin` | An actual operation requiring the pool has initial pool presence or an entry in its actual preceding history. |
| `operation_after_empty_state_requires_entry` | Starting from an established empty reached state, a later actual operation requiring the pool forces entry in the intervening actual suffix. |
| `no_actual_operation_from_closed_empty_pool` | An actual required operation cannot follow an empty state through a repertoire whose admitted recipes are all closed. |

The proof decides only natural-number comparisons and pool membership in finite recipe lists. It does not decide the entire arbitrary inventory or use classical choice to select an initial cause.

## 4. The actual-prefix witness and its limits

`EntryOccurs` contains a decomposition of the original event word into a prefix, one entry reaction and a suffix. It also contains the actual prefix trace to that reaction's prestate, the actual reaction step, and the actual suffix from its reached state to the specified endpoint. There is no source reset or substitution of an independently possible path. The witnessed entry is earlier than the operation whose required participants are being assessed.

The result does not say that entry is unique or chronologically first. It also does not identify the molecule that later participates. Initial pool presence can satisfy the general disjunction even if those original members are subsequently lost and another entry occurs. Thus the disjunction is not a complete causal attribution of the later operation.

The empty-state corollary removes that ambiguity for a useful necessity question. Once an actual reached state is independently established to contain no pool member, later required operation cannot be explained solely by having had a member before that state. An actual entry must occur afterward. The corollary applies directly to the actual suffix; it does not require a universal lifetime or uniform loss rate.

Closure is sufficient for the stated absence obstruction. It is not asserted to be a necessary characterization of every unreachable state, nor a universal law of chemistry. Adding a genuine entry route legitimately defeats that obstruction for the enlarged repertoire. A slow uncatalyzed process need not have zero physical rate merely because the selected catalytic model omits it. These are scope boundaries of the mechanism, not claims about the impossibility of life's origin.

## 5. A checked structural realization of retained and reconstructed operation

The illustration represents active complexes, protected pairs, separate left/right components, substrate/product and support/spent coordinates. The supplied recipes are:

- Two active complexes can form one protected pair.
- A protected pair plus support can yield two active complexes and spent support.
- Separate left and right components plus support can assemble an active complex.
- An active complex can separate into its components.
- An active complex, substrate and support can yield product while retaining the active complex.

These are declared structural recipes. They are not an exact account of ribosome activation, translation stoichiometry or the observed transcription requirement. Other needed reaction components are not represented, so this model does not certify those lists as sufficient for biological translation.

The selected pool contains active complexes and protected pairs. The activation and operating recipes are closed for that pool. Assembly from separate components is an explicitly checked entry. A restricted repertoire excluding that assembly is closed; the full repertoire contains it. Both scopes are visible in the code, and the full-repertoire witness prevents treating the restricted obstruction as an absolute physical impossibility.

`example_step_preserves_material` and `example_trace_preserves_material` check a declared weighting: active complexes count as two units, protected pairs as four, and separate components, substrate/product and support/spent as one each. The three comparison states—active complexes, a protected pair and separate components—have equal total weighted material and matched support. The compared protected and separate-component states also have the same substrate stock. This weighting is a structural control, not an experimentally fitted molecular-mass measure.

The actual productive comparisons preserve the reached states:

The product coordinate is zero in these specified initial inventories and one after the demonstrated productive histories. This is actual output under supplied reaction laws, not just a restored label or an available path claimed to have occurred.
