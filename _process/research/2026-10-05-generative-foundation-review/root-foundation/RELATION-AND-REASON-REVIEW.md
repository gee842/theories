# A reason can be relational without requiring a prior special bond

6 October 2026. AI-authored review under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), following the [necessity attempt](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-INTEGRATION.md). Named connection: **actual undergoing, an available transformation and differentiated responsibility → the constitution and scope of a reason**. This is conceptual adjudication, not a new theorem or empirical study.

**Assessment:** the choice between suffering itself and relationship as the source of reasons is too exclusive. A particular reason can jointly concern an actual burden, an available relieving transformation and the position from which it can be realized. That does not make a prior undertaking, shared identity or special role necessary for every baseline consideration. Conversely, special relationships can genuinely constitute reasons and obligations, not merely change the weight of an otherwise complete generic reason.

## 1. Separate relationships by what they explain

There is already a relation in the proposed stranger case: a particular available action would affect a particular experiencing continuity. Identifying that relation requires actual efficacy and a preserved target. Calling the responder unrelated therefore means lacking the disputed antecedent role, not existing outside every causal or practical relation.

Three explanatory roles must remain distinguishable:

- The undergoing and its relief identify what the response would change for the affected being.
- Present access and means identify an opportunity available to this responder, rather than an improvement nobody can presently realize.
- Undertakings, responsibility, consent and protective relations can create claims, shape permission or affect which considerations may justify this choice.

These are not three independent substances or necessarily additive reasons. The grounds of one concrete consideration may be jointly constituted. Some roles can create different obligations concerning the same action; others can legitimately restrict its use. The [elective-concern review](ELECTIVE-CONCERN-COUNTERREVIEW.md) already preserves these possibilities. The present clarification does not claim to discover plural grounding.

The important dispute is narrower: must an additional special normative relationship exist before the actual relief has any positive practical significance for this responder? Replacing that question with “are reasons relational?” makes a substantive disagreement disappear verbally.

## 2. A case that cannot borrow a prior self-reason

Consider a being who has never possessed the ability to perform the relevant self-help. Independently identify a severe, continuing, still-unwanted experiential burden. A stranger, without previous contact, causal responsibility, undertaking or adopted caring purpose, now has an effective, being-preserving response through independently warranted permissible means. Other equally capable strangers can also help; no unique rescuer or delegated role is supplied. No suffering is inferred merely from incapacity, silence or dependence. This is a conceptual case, not a claim about a clinical population.

The direct account judges that relieving this actual burden contributes something to that available choice. The practical relation is present now; no earlier act of self-rescue, promise or ownership representation has to transmit its reason to the stranger. This matters because a derivation proceeding only from each agent's own action-reasons would omit some experiencing beings at the outset. Exhausting the ground of self-directed prudential reasons is a burden of that transfer route, not a prerequisite for every universal moral account.

The strongest restriction can accept the experience and real benefit while denying this stranger any baseline favor. It allows voluntary adoption to establish a worthwhile project, after which actual relief governs successful performance. Its principle is that positive participation in practical decisions has exclusive jurisdiction until a further legitimate relation arises. It does not need to deny the target or postulate a separate ethical reality.

Calling present access itself “the special relation” has two possible meanings. If present permitted access plus actual relief is sufficient, the account accepts the proposed scope and differs mainly in vocabulary. If more is required, the additional relationship must be independently described and its normative necessity defended. Mere acquisition of a caring label cannot establish that necessity.

## 3. Permission is neither undertaking nor positive justification

Permission allows a specified intervention; an undertaking can make the responder answerable for performing something. One does not automatically establish the other. A request, consent, promise and accepted responsibility may occur together, but that coincidence does not make them equivalent.

Consent can genuinely change a normative relation. It must not be treated as an irrelevant administrative check. Yet a permission granted without entrustment does not, merely by existing, show that all the favorable significance of relief originated in a special obligation. Where express consent is unavailable, the case requires an independently warranted basis of permissibility; silence is not permission.

Likewise, an action can be permissible without having this positive ground. The argument cannot move from “the stranger may help” to “relief favors helping.” Its affirmative premise concerns the actual improvement. The [award comparison](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md#a-rule-does-not-settle-its-own-justificatory-scope) further distinguishes a permitted outcome from legitimate grounds for selecting it. A prohibited intervention might also possess a beneficial aspect, but that aspect does not authorize it. The present clean comparison avoids deciding how all such conflicts should resolve.

This makes optional charity a discriminating concession. If the restriction grants that the stranger's prospective act is positively justified by its actual relief before adoption, it grants the baseline in question. If it grants only liberty to choose, or justification after undertaking, it has not. This is the inherited timing distinction, now kept separate from the permission relation.

## 4. The substantive adjudication

This reviewer favors the jointly relational direct account in the specified case. The actual improvement explains why forming concern is worthwhile, why an ineffective response loses its stated point, and why the stranger's lack of earlier commitment need not make the opportunity ethically empty. Actual access supplies the relevant opportunity; it does not turn every shared causal connection into a duty.

The restriction explains something real: participation in shaping a project, self-direction and created obligations matter. But those achievements do not establish exclusive authority over whether this independently existing burden's relief counts at all. Permitting a person to decline a project is compatible with that project's having a reason in its favor. Genuine choice-specific exclusions remain possible and must be assessed at their actual scope.

The converse overreach is equally important. A promise may constitute a reason that did not exist before, and a created relationship may produce a new good. Continuing suffering does not hold every normative fact constant. Our account should retain those changes instead of claiming that special relations merely amplify a fixed universal quantity.

What can be settled is the proposed architecture: **baseline relief favor can be relational without depending on a prior special bond, while special relations can supply genuinely additional or differently constituted grounds**. This is the defended position for further inquiry. Its universal necessity is not proved by dissolving the false dichotomy; the restrictive account's additional exclusivity claim still requires substantive adjudication. Neither formal predicate variation nor the shared origin of the relations decides that claim.
