The controller traverses the retained \(H\) to derive its first positive unary word, `aa`. The actual credit depends on physical contact, not on the receipt symbol.

The primary unchanged \(H\) reaches its positive state after reset and `aa`, and receives ten units. This environmental structural change is an exogenous assay condition. Allocating its possible representation later is not construction of a changed physical apparatus.

A missing or unaffordable receipt is not interpreted as zero. The costly-receipt control preserves actual ten-unit income and source depletion while preventing any selector update or generator dispatch. The output-one/productive-contact coupling, reset law and receipt channel are supplied physical assumptions. Abstract learned labels do not intrinsically mean support. The second route really performs additional model construction and stores its result. The first route does not generate that model. This is an enacted method difference, rather than an externally changed score or a label attached to the same execution.

Fresh sensor observations then select the current model. The receipt is not treated as a correct-family label. A zero receipt can lead to broader inquiry that ultimately selects the original \(H\). ## 6. Completion is not model correctness

The fresh audit is exact only when the actual reading process belongs to the chosen class and the stated channel/stability assumptions hold. In that case, candidate filtering preserves the actual current configuration, and a singleton is correct within the class. Three consecutive readings suffice for the six three-cycle candidates; six suffice for the fourteen-candidate combined class. The implementation can stop earlier when a singleton remains.
