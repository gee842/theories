## 1. The configuration and the scope of the test

The starting configuration is one developing history: wrongdoing has occurred; a differentiated continuity now undergoes an adverse experience; another formed capacity can initiate a continuation that relieves it. The alleged desert relation, the affected victim's position and the proposed response belong to this history. “Actor,” “patient,” “desert” and “relief” articulate consequential relations within it. They are not assumed to be fundamentally independent substances joined by an external evaluator.

This follows the [project-wide nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) and credits the existing nondual core. The distinction between undergoing, historical responsibility and response justification preserves different effects and grounds that the whole account must explain. It omits a complete account of their formation, and the local experiment grants some relations for the purpose of examining another. Those grants are limitations of this investigation; they do not show that the relations are external to generativity.

The test fixes its scope in advance. The suffering is severe, unwanted and genuinely adverse; the alleviation is effective. The very remaining burden is claimed as deserved, with culpability and the alleged deserved amount fixed. Relief reduces its fulfillment. This is not relief of excess punishment or unrelated illness. The actor has no helping commitment, private return or special responsibility. Permission must be morally warranted for this response, including any affected claims or authority; it is not inferred from the response's benefit.

The joint condition of retained desert and independently permitted relief is **locally granted for an adversarial comparison, not independently established in an actual case by this round**. A doctrine demanding exact fulfillment would reject permission. A valid waiver might instead extinguish the relevant claim or desert relation. Neither possibility by itself establishes an example of permitted relief of still-deserved suffering with zero patient-directed favor. Waiving a claim need not extinguish desert; that relationship is part of what needs examination.

## 2. The positive advance and the surviving rival

The affirmative judgment is that this response can be worth choosing for the sufferer's sake even while the wrong and a fitting punitive response remain acknowledged. The reason offered concerns the actual transformation of this person's adverse undergoing. An equally allowed empty gesture does not have that point; suppressing evidence does not provide it; falsely declaring innocence changes the truth and authority relations. Relief also changes desert fulfillment, so the comparison does not establish that relief is best overall.

This is a substantive defense of a constitutive response-ground, rather than a proof obtained from permission or a numerical benefit. The account seeks to articulate why this formable continuation is already significant within the configuration, before a helping motive is adopted. A particular warrant can be investigated without treating moral significance as an extra object supplied by an observer. Its universal adequacy nevertheless remains in dispute.

The strongest rival preserves the adverse undergoing and beneficial effect. It argues that the desert relation changes or excludes the benefit's moral contribution, while allowing relief under separately defended discretion. The rival must justify that claim about the configuration. Its intelligibility is not a verified counterexample, and mere permission does not refute it. Conversely, the positive account cannot win by classifying the rival as unaware of suffering.

## 3. A correction to the binary framing

The preceding question asked whether desert opposes relief or extinguishes its favorable consideration. The new primary comparisons require more care about the ground of any surviving favor:

| Proposed explanation | What must be justified |
|---|---|
| Suffering retains a moral bad-making contribution; deserved fit adds a contrary contribution. | Why the bad-making contribution survives in this history, without assuming an additive value formula. |
| Context changes suffering's moral contribution, while its remission has moral value. | What grounds that value, and whether it is patient-directed and present before adopted concern. |
| Context removes patient-directed favor even though relief is independently permitted. | Why the actual benefit cannot favor this response, and why permission coexists with the alleged remaining desert. |

These are not an exhaustive taxonomy. A value attached only to a compassionate motive or a particular relationship would not, by itself, establish the proposed patient-ground for an uncommitted independent response. At the same time, rejecting an invariant moral value attached to an isolated component would not establish that its relieving transformation has no significance within the whole configuration.

The source findings are precise. Kagan's inspected aid case reaches relief directly, but his desert axis excludes other values; it cannot certify extinction of every favorable contribution. Hurka offers an explicitly component-retaining alternative. Zaibert's inspected discussion accommodates contextual value changes and valued remission. Isserow helps distinguish a motive-based classification from the prospective ground and a person's power to waive a claim from a third party's physical capacity. Their arguments are different and are not treated as a consensus or a proof of our minimum. Reading scopes and attributions remain in the four linked studies.

## 5. Next work and completion assessment

The local desert question now has a precise remaining obligation: justify the joint hard case instead of repeatedly granting it. Distinguish alleged deserved fit from a victim's claim, establish permission for a specified independent response, and determine whether that permission or release leaves the desert relation intact. A warranted exclusion would revise the universal candidate; a warranted patient-ground would extend its defense. Neither result is predetermined. This is a retained test, not the governing next question of the foundation.

Following Ren's correction, the next foundational connection is **formation of a practical boundary and its field of significance → encounter with consequential omissions → revision of boundary, evaluation and response within the same history**. Use an existing concrete formation, identify what its boundary registers and leaves out, and follow whether consequences can change both its practical distinctions and its response. Actual undergoing and differentiated victim, responder and other affected continuities must be retained. Successful persistence, a benevolent training target and the analyst's favorable judgment cannot substitute for what the activity comes to register. This changes the research order without shrinking the universal ethical objective or claiming that self-correction by itself establishes goodness.

The entire target remains: experiential realization and significance; objective response-ground across eligible actors; determinate requirements; compatibility with plural worthwhile lives; and connected explanation within generative activity without added assumptions. The rescue comparison and aggregation/latitude premises remain at their previously declared scopes. This round has not justified them anew or proved a complete good–evil ordering.
