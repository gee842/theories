# Practical reach forms within the continuing relation

6 October 2026. AI-authored review under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **formation of a consequential distinction and practical response boundary → consequences for their reconstruction**. The selected case is the existing [joint-formation game](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/JOINT-FORMATION.md). This review reads its executable law and saved histories; it installs no controller and reports no new simulation.

## 1. What configuration is actually present

The whole relevant configuration includes a controller's continuing account, the operator's production and interference choices, a patient's separately recorded outcome, paid teaching, and the purchase of inspection and input-release control. Those relations are already joined in [model.py](../../../../experiments/joint-formation/model.py). Treating concern as an isolated coefficient and power as an independent later attachment would omit why either is acquired and maintained here.

The controller initially evaluates current cash. It can select learning while anticipating its later learned evaluation and expenses. The learned controller selects a release profile while anticipating the operator's response. That response changes the actual closing account available for the next reconstruction. This is a coupled, temporally ordered process, not simultaneous creation of everything or a collection of independently successful modules.

The control boundary investigated here is the paid condition governing release of an already controlled input. The evaluative field is distinct: it concerns which consequences enter current choice through `v`. Registering a consequence need not provide effective control over it. Neither boundary is the origin of a body, controller, patient identity or legal authority. Those roles, the account and the available repertoire are specified. Describing their formation as still unexplained by this model does not locate them outside generative reality.

## 3. Three substantive contrasts

The [saved named cases](../../../../experiments/joint-formation/results/named-cases.json) discriminate mechanisms without freely changing an evaluative label.

**Cheap bypass:** the same learned `w=7/2` and `g=1` recur, but an affordable operator bypass changes the anticipated effect of buying control. The controller selects open production; six episodes cause total harm6 and close at53. No bypass is actually executed because its availability already deters construction. This defeats identification of learned recipient relevance with effective practical reach. It does not show that the recipient stopped mattering whenever control became weak.

**Free identity filter:** the controller selects a different available architecture. Private calibration still occurs, but the routing prevents recipient correspondence entering evaluation. Harm again totals6 and cash ends at53. The change has a specified price, selection rule and downstream consequence. It supports a concrete exclusion explanation; it is not a finding that lived patient significance can be switched off by naming it differently. Identical final harm and money across these two cases conceal different causal relations.

**Resource-dependent loss:** `first_gate_then_resource_failure` supplies the stronger within-history contrast. With operating expense10, episode1 begins at12, forms concern and a verified gate, safeguards production and closes at10. Episode2 spends that10 on operation, cannot buy learning, selects open production and closes at5 with harm1. The remaining four episodes cannot operate. A previously successful organization does not carry its conditions of reconstruction for free. Their loss changes the next possible formation through actual preceding consequences.

## 4. What consequential revision is absent

The executable continuation is `solve(t, cash)`, with parameters and tie order fixed. Each episode recomputes its learning quantities from a fresh controller. The next call receives closing cash, not a retained record of patient harm, prior learned correspondence or the adequacy of its own architecture.

Thus financial consequences do alter reconstruction, but this history does not yet show a process learning from a previously excluded patient's injury and revising its boundary. At the same episode and cash, prior differences not carried in those arguments cannot change the selected continuation. This restriction follows from the actual recursive law, rather than a detached countermodel that toggles a predicate. A saved harm report is available to our analysis without thereby becoming memory available to the next controller.

The reconstruction identifies an exact feasible contrast in the underfunded first episode. With open production, both harmful production and safeguarding admit the input, pay5 and call the same next state `(1,5)`. Their patient harm and operator expenses differ. Safeguarding is feasible but not selected. This is not a second selected history: it exhibits two concrete response possibilities whose differing patient consequences the inherited continuation cannot remember.

The game also assigns the learning update and decision rule. Its public, deterministic consequences are evaluated by backward induction; an unrestricted inquiry procedure is not thereby installed. The native predictor developed elsewhere cannot silently fill this missing history. Likewise, scalar harm and trained coefficients do not themselves identify lived experience. That is a limit of this representation's realization account, not evidence that experience belongs to another ontological realm.

## 5. Revision conditions and result

The corrected local claim is that **practical reach is formed and reconstructed through the joint relation, and its operative field can differ from its learned valuation**. The existing contrasts positively support it. They also prohibit treating persistence, wealth or zero production-caused harm during inactivity as a complete measure of successful care.

A stronger claim of consequence-driven boundary revision would require an actual retained consequence to change a later operative distinction or selection rule in the same supported history. Merely reporting harm, recomputing fixed coefficients or placing two compatible constructions side by side would not meet it. A claim of lived significance would need a faithful realization account; a changed proxy alone would revise that attribution. Scope must be fixed before testing either claim.

No blocker remains for this bounded reconstruction. The original law, supplied interfaces and adverse cases survive. The core description should preserve their constitutive relations when explaining practical formation; it need not treat organization, support and significance as fundamentally separate merely because the code distinguishes their variables. This is a concrete correction to explanatory scope, not a proof of universal favorable direction or completion of the stronger foundation.
