# Conditional claims and their explanatory scope

6 October 2026. AI-authored synthesis of accepted work at Git commit `9a6a7795e9d8a8dd777c0973eadf2f4523615e48`. This note classifies existing results; it adds no theorem, model run, compiler run or empirical analysis. Pending evaluative-succession outcomes are excluded. Concurrent working-copy changes are not evidence for the claims below. The governing [completion conditions][G] and [premise ledger][P] remain the standard of explanatory adequacy.

## Five claims with different quantifiers

| Form | What must actually be established | Central accepted example | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Law-class necessity | Every history obeying an identified law has the stated constraint. | `Generative.Conditional.support_trace_bound` and `unbounded_enactment_requires_unbounded_arrival`. | Existence of a continuing history, formation of a regulator, or favorable direction. |
| Actual constructive history | A specified event word is enabled from the real supplied precursor and reaches the stated capacity with its accounts intact. | `FormedSelection.paid_formation_to_copy`, `witness_accounting`. | Typicality, a stochastic selection advantage, or indefinite reconstruction. |
| Selected behavior under a declared law | Acquired state is actually read by the installed selector and changes its paid decision in a controlled comparison. | `EvaluationWitness.matched_postperception_training`, `different_paid_decisions`, `exclusion_with_equal_facts`. | Uniquely identified human motivation, justified concern, or an inevitable tendency to help. |
| Warranted application | Sound acquired means knowledge participates in action under independently defended grounds, patient-good interpretation, authority and comparison. | “Conditional recognition-and-use lemma,” application mathematics §7. | Derivation of standing or ethical priority from a learned coefficient, resource balance, or permission bit. |
| Empirical identification | Observations discriminate the claimed mechanism from specified alternatives under justified sampling and measurement assumptions. | Recipient-value reanalysis and its fixed-evaluation equivalence/bounded-alternative results. | That a recorded choice contrast alone identifies changed evaluation, or that a conditional null envelope has already been empirically calibrated. |

### Necessity is informative only through its account premise

[ResourceContinuation][N] quantifies over arbitrary carriers and event relations. `SustainLaw` identifies nonnegative reserve, demand and credit on **each actual edge**. `support_trace_bound` yields spent plus remaining reserve ≤ initial reserve plus received credit. `unbounded_enactment_requires_unbounded_arrival` additionally assumes an actual infinite execution with unbounded cumulative demand. Credit may be conversion, regeneration or external provision at the specified boundary. The theorem does not select among them. Zero-demand stuttering remains possible, and an uninformative zero account supplies no substantive diagnosis. The [saved verification receipt][NR] records the fresh build and dependency closure.

This is a genuine universal conditional constraint, not a claim that all systems possess an energy variable with the chosen interpretation. Application requires identifying such a quantity and validating its edge law. Explanatory work then asks how the actual system constructs, accesses or loses the support that the constraint makes necessary.

### Existence supplies a mechanism without supplying its success frequency

[FormedSelection][C] starts its central witness with both variant counts zero. The paid word is `formH, formA, associateA, repair, capture, localCopyA`: repair permits capture, whose actual local fuel permits copying. `witness_accounting` retains both variants and accounts for all five expenditure units. The repertoire, precursor material, initial fuel and source remain supplied; the formed response and copy are reached rather than assumed active. Conversely, `repaired_site_can_pass_to_alternative` exhibits an alternative inheriting the repaired site after resident wear. Constructive and adverse histories therefore inhabit the same repertoire. The [receipt][CR] verifies these statements, not a probability assigned to either word.

### Actual selection remains distinct from its ethical interpretation

The [evaluation witness][S] has equal acquired register, recipient facts and resources before the shared/filtered comparison. Both pay the decision bill; `different_paid_decisions` derives help versus abstention from their respective installed routes. `actual_staged_composition` connects the declared precursor/assembly and inherited history to the evaluation trace through explicit boundary projections. It is not an erased single law for every stochastic ancestral path. `actual_reason_sensitive_effect` requires `root : G` and the applicable producer/recipient contract; it does not prove `G` from the coefficient. The [formal receipt][SR] and [independent review][SV] accept these separate scopes.

This explains a limited causal capacity: acquired consequences can influence selected recipient action, and equally informed exclusion can suppress that influence. The transfer sign, scale, update recipe and calibrated channel remain identified local inputs. A conventional realization with the same law is an admissible explanation, not a failed control. Retained data and paid port replacement in this accepted study are not whole-learner succession.

### Warrant is an additional, inspectable relation

The [application mathematics][A], accepted as a conditional interpretation study by its [review][AR], separates represented instrumental support, evaluative admission, action and fulfillment. Its §7 lemma requires independently warranted `K` (actor-addressed ground), `J` (patient-good interpretation), `P` (authority), and `Π` (comparison over the relevant full menu), plus actual mediation and nonvacuous content sensitivity. These are analytical premises, not extra axioms secretly established by the Lean runtime.

Section 3's **Application identity** is useful here: the intersection of individual undominated sets is contained in the robust nondominated remainder, but can be empty while that remainder is nonempty. Thus “not robustly defeated” cannot silently become “licensed by every retained interpretation.” Unknown support is not refutation of the recipient's claim. A physical receipt must still be related to the recipient's good; an actor's available funds do not make its sacrifice normatively negligible. Concern stays in the explanatory target precisely by retaining these obligations.

### Identification can become sharper without overstating the observations

The [saved Exp3 results][D], independently reconstructed in [DR], estimate a standardized training contrast of **+0.498 percentage points**, with 95% Welch interval **[−4.246, +5.242]**, among 333 author-eligible participants on the offered-opportunity scale. This is neither full randomized intention-to-treat identification nor an equivalence result. The [identification argument][I] constructs equal finite task-history distributions from changed evaluation and unchanged evaluation with a compensating unmeasured expectancy shift. Extending equality to questionnaires requires a common reporting law unless an independently validated latent measurement relation is imposed. The countermodel shows nonidentification in that class; it does not show which mechanism humans used.

The newer bound supplies a testable restriction rather than another observed effect. `OfferedChoice.signed_population_bound` and `mirrored_population_bound` in [B] prove, for a finite rational paired population,

\[
L_1-L_0\le R_1-(1-m)\max(0,R_0+R_1-1-\varepsilon).
\]

Here gates and conditional probabilities lie in `[0,1]`, uncovered paired mass is at most `ε`, and covered pairs have probability displacement at most `m`. `sharp_population_attains` proves attainability with the accompanying validity/account lemmas. Exact maxima in this bounded-probability class can be only suprema under finite logistic logits. `OddsEnvelope.quadratic_iff` in [O] is an exact rational pointwise equivalence; `certified_envelope` is a sufficient quadratic certificate, not a necessary discriminant condition. The [formal receipt][BR] and [review receipt][MR] distinguish these proofs from the separately reviewed real-logistic and statistical arguments in [M]. Independently bounding nuisance displacement and paired coverage remains a measurement obligation. Exceeding a justified envelope rejects a joint restricted alternative; it does not uniquely prove acquired concern.

## Restrict evidence claims without replacing the goal

A defensible restriction says: “this experiment does not identify retained mechanism revision,” or “this theorem applies when these physical and interpretive premises hold.” It preserves what the result establishes, identifies a discriminating next observation, and keeps the missing causal connection open. Universal explanatory scope does not require each empirical study to identify the whole foundation, nor premise-free derivation of physical chemistry.

An impermissible substitution assumes the central capacity being explained—an active learner, truthful acquired label, operative other-directed evaluation, or warranted patient-good mapping—and then reports its consequences as that capacity's derivation. Calling this input a law does not cure the substitution. Similarly, removing concern and wisdom because their evidence is difficult would change the governing goal. Conditional local construction is legitimate when the named capacity is actually formed/used and its supplied recipe is distinguished from it; claiming that the recipe's origin or applicability has thereby been established is not.

The proof trust boundary is equally specific. Saved axiom reports list `propext, Quot.sound` for the cited continuation bounds and paid decision/composition results; `propext` for the cited formation witnesses; and additionally `Classical.choice` for the reason-sensitive effect and rational population/odds results. These are standard Lean logical dependencies, not empirical or ethical premises. Absence of custom axioms or admissions does not eliminate hypotheses in a theorem statement. The application and identification arguments are reviewed mathematics, with their own stated assumptions, rather than silently promoted kernel proofs. No theorem count settles the fixed completion conditions.
