## 2. Affirmative argument

A reduction can omit many distinctions yet preserve exactly what is needed for a particular inquiry. The old exact theory already established special cases. The new error recurrence makes approximation useful without pretending exactness: it carries actual one-step defects and error sensitivity through a horizon. Time-dependent maps charge changes in description, while composition charges amplification at successive scales. The observable theorem separates dynamical approximation from calibration of what matters at a particular position.

The formal examples constrain this positive result. An equal total does not preserve every future after coupling changes, nor does exact conservation of total preserve the two-position support requirement. These failures follow from the same actual laws and measured coordinates. A lower-envelope summary is sufficient for one protection conjunction, so the account also defeats the overreaction that all aggregation is inadequate.

由此得到的不是“永远越细越好”，而是一个有范围的充分性判断：给定问题、实际关系、变化时域与可接受误差后，说明何种描述足够，以及哪种遗漏迫使修订。映射不是独立于现实的价值创造者；它可以正确或错误地表述同一展开中的关系。

## 3. Substantive counterreview

This is a same-agent counterreview; no independent reviewer or agent is claimed. It checks the completed argument against stronger rival explanations and against the source’s exact theorem statements.

| Challenge | Assessment and resulting scope |
|---|---|
| The error theorem merely assumes the desired answer. | Its hypotheses bound one-step defects and error propagation at reached pairs; its conclusion bounds full-horizon discrepancy against an independently recursive envelope. It does not assume endpoint accuracy. However, obtaining useful defect bounds can be difficult and remains a substantive identification task. |
| A bound checked on the actual path cannot justify a future decision. | Accepted. A post-hoc application is only a diagnostic. Prospective use needs bounds across the admissible histories/interventions, or separately warranted probabilistic coverage; neither is generated by the induction. |
| Conventional approximation theory already explains these results. | Accepted. The source comparisons below are antecedents, not inferior theories. The contribution is their explicit checked connection to the generative history and indexed practical correspondence, not mathematical novelty or superiority. |
| A pseudometric can hide a serious loss at distance zero. | Accepted. A dynamical metric alone is insufficient. The readout calibration and the index set are independent obligations. The exact-total counterexample exposes this issue without changing the underlying target. |
| Changing descriptions is only a coordinate change; an offset is artificial. | A correctly transported offset is harmless. The witness disproves only inference from separately frozen fits to an unchanged transported prediction. Real application must identify whether the map change is recoding, new organization, or different questions. |
| Total plus the second coordinate is not a meaningful reduction. | Accepted and stated. That repair restores full pair information. The result locates needed information; it does not claim compression. The minimum witness separately shows target-specific genuine compression. |
| An illustrative threshold of one imports a complete ethical theory. | Rejected as an interpretation of the actual claim. The example only proves failure to preserve a supplied conjunction. Its biological or ethical use requires justification of coordinates, units and thresholds. No welfare sum or universal threshold is derived. |
| A sufficiently large error budget makes the theorem useless. | Accepted. Useful adequacy also requires margins or precision goals. The routing lower bound proves that some summaries cannot meet a fixed tolerance; positive drift prevents inferring unlimited-horizon accuracy from small residuals. |
| Matching predictions ensures matching actions. | Not established. A changed observation can change a policy discontinuously. The existing controlled-refinement contract remains relevant; this module neither synthesizes a controller nor establishes all-intervention equivalence. |
| The formal core cannot represent changing organization. | Not supported. The core accepts the complete state and law; stored coupling or stage can be included. Current projection choices omit some relations, which is not a failure of the fuller ontology. Formation of an adequate projection remains an explanatory question. |

No theorem of generic downward causation, autonomous macro-agency, automatic scale discovery, universal renormalization or normative universality follows. A rival can accept every checked result while treating reduction as standard model analysis; the present study has not established exclusive necessity of the generative formulation.

## 4. Primary-source assessment

Sources retrieved on 8 October 2026. No publication bytes were imported, and the reading scope is restricted as stated. The mathematical results in this round are independently checked in Lean; the readings locate them historically and conceptually.

- **Tabuada, Ames, Julius and Pappas, “Approximate reduction of dynamical systems” (2007), [author arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.3804).** Read the abstract and bibliographic record. It explicitly studies reduction with hard error bounds using incremental stability. This supports treating bounded approximate reduction as established prior work. This limited reading does not certify all their hypotheses, a proof of their full theorem here, or its application to our changing maps.
- **Chorin, Hald and Kupferman, “Optimal prediction with memory,” Physica D 166 (2002), 239–257, [author-hosted original](https://math.berkeley.edu/~chorin/CHK02.pdf).** Read the abstract and §§1–2, printed pp. 239–242, especially the resolved dynamics and memory/noise terms in equations (6)–(7). Under their stated statistical projection assumptions, omitted variables can contribute through memory rather than an autonomous instantaneous law. This motivates a further route for retaining consequential relations. We have not formalized their operator construction, probability assumptions or memory equation.
- **Pola, Girard and Tabuada, “Approximately bisimilar symbolic models for nonlinear control systems” (2007), [author arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.0246).** Read the abstract and bibliographic record. The work gives approximate symbolic equivalence under incremental global asymptotic stability. It is a stronger control-equivalence comparator with its own conditions; our reached-history error theorem is not an approximate-bisimulation theorem.

The empirical status is unchanged: no cell, organism, community or institution has been newly fitted or experimentally tested in this round. A proposed application must identify its actual conditions and interventions rather than rebrand the two-coordinate witnesses as evidence.
