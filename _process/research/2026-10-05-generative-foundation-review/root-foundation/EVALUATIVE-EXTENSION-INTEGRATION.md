# Evaluative openness and judgment before agreement

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [Ren's request to push the theoretical frontier](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-goal-formation-and-revision.md#continue-the-theoretical-frontier), under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The current account is [the extended goal-revision chapter](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/GOAL-FORMATION-AND-REVISION.md#8-evaluative-openness-and-judgment-before-agreement).

**Result:** a formed intelligence can preserve its ethical grounds while revising the content and scope of its judgments. When new relations are not yet fully understood, it must distinguish agreement on a better replacement, agreement that a plan fails, and warrant for an actual choice under uncertainty. The first can be absent while the second is present. The third requires further grounds; neither comparison alone supplies it.

## 1. What is already established, and the precise continuation

The concurrent [concern-extension study](CONCERN-EXTENSION-INTEGRATION.md) already explains positive formation as an entry into concern and proves generic content-extension, reversal and incomparability results. This continuation imports its `ConcernExtension` module instead of independently maintaining the overlapping inclusion lemmas. Its achievement remains a completed result.

The [inquiry-priority argument](INQUIRY-PRIORITIES-AND-G0.md) already distinguishes a decision-relevant uncertainty from a demand to know everything. The existing `PracticalJudgment.Robust` interface and common-choice counterexample already establish that individually available answers need not give a common answer. We do not announce those as new discoveries. The [goal-revision](GOAL-FORMATION-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md), [reform-evidence](REFORM-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) and [correction-infrastructure](CORRECTION-INFRASTRUCTURE-INTEGRATION.md) mechanisms also retain their completed scopes.

The additional connection is **how an evolving evaluative description enters comparison across unresolved applications of the same grounds**. We extend both layers of J1/J2 together, distinguish preserved claim content from preserved verdicts, and check an error that can arise when deliberation first intersects all strict comparisons and only then selects its undefeated plans. This procedure can retain a plan that every live application defeats. That is a limitation of the claimed certificate, not a general refutation of decision-making under imprecise probabilities.

The universal inference advanced is conditional: a shared normative orientation across intelligence does not require identical operative goals, a finished application vocabulary, or agreement on one replacement before every negative judgment. This does not prove the universal warrant of G0, inevitable recognition of its implications, or the exclusive necessity of our formulation. The authorized axiom route remains in force; other sessions' stronger foundational investigations retain their scope.

## 2. Stable grounds with changing descriptions

Activity can form relationships whose significance was absent from an earlier practical description. It also forms ways of attending, interpreting, remembering and responding to those relationships. These are connected developments within one history. The account does not begin with an independent evaluator assigning values to an independently constituted world.

Four changes require different explanations. A renamed claim can preserve the same content. New evidence can correct an old claim. A newly formed or newly recognized relation can require an additional claim while all the earlier claims remain true. Finally, a change to G0 or the priority commitments changes the normative premises themselves. Neither acquiring a new preference nor expanding the claim vocabulary establishes that last change's justification.

For an extension to carry practical weight, identify the relationship, its actual realization and consequences, the grounds for recognizing it, and its relevance under the explicit G0/J commitments. Preserve earlier gains, losses and responsibilities. Classification as basic must be defended before selecting a winner; organizational persistence, popularity and novelty alone do not supply it. These requirements reuse [practical judgment](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md), rather than silently adding a new priority axiom.

The checked transport results specify three cases. Equal additional profiles preserve the exact old comparison. Retaining the previously disfavored plan's newly represented protections and secondary gains is sufficient to preserve an old strict preference; it is not necessary in every J2 case. A newly established basic protection lost by the formerly favored plan defeats that favorable comparison. Crossed optional gains can instead leave both directions undecided. Enriching an adequate description need not make choosing easier.

An illustrative stage change preserves an output advantage of two realizations versus one. A formed dependence now requires support supplied only by the lower-output continuation. Under the stipulated basic classification and J2, maintenance becomes favored while the forgone output remains a real loss. The prior and later judgments therefore cannot be represented by one stage-insensitive ranking. The **same unchanged `Better` rule on stage-sensitive descriptions does represent both**.

This finite witness stipulates the two stages and the changed need; it is not an executed emergence mechanism or autonomous discovery of a new concept. It compares continuations from their respective situations, without retrospectively deleting earlier losses. Creating a dependence has not thereby been justified. [Earlier perspectival work](PERSPECTIVAL-GOOD-AND-TRAJECTORIES.md) already distinguishes care for a formed need from ratification of its formation. No duty of endless maintenance is added.

## 3. Judgment can advance before a complete agreement

Newly relevant relations may be only partly understood. One supported account may identify one condition as decisive; another may disagree about that condition or the response that actually protects it. The possibilities here are competing descriptions of the same unfolding, not independently existing ethical worlds. Their admission must rest on substantive reasons, not arbitrary switches in a weak formalization.

Let the live applications be `w`, the actually eligible plans be `a,b`, and `b ≻w a` mean the inherited J1/J2 comparison under application `w`. There are three distinct questions:

| Question | Exact condition | What it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Is there one agreed improvement over `a`? | There is an eligible `b` such that every live `w` has `b ≻w a`. | A common strict comparison, conditional on the application family's warrant. |
| Does every application defeat `a`? | For every live `w`, some eligible `b` has `b ≻w a`. | If the actual warranted application is covered, `a` is actually defeated, even if the better `b` varies. |
| Is `a` undefeated in every application? | Every live `w` leaves `a` eligible and without an eligible strict superior. | A stronger certificate of uniform undefeatedness, not merely absence of one common superior. |

The first implies the second. The converse can fail. Also, being undefeated under the intersection of the comparison relations does not establish the third. These statements receive exact Lean proofs and a concrete J1/J2 witness, rather than an independently supplied arbitrary preference relation.

In that witness a newly recognized condition has two possible support routes. Each account treats one route as effective and the other as ineffective; omission is ineffective in both. All three plans are stipulated eligible and otherwise equal. Under the first account, the left response defeats omission. Under the second, the right response defeats omission. Neither fixed response strictly defeats omission in both accounts. A procedure retaining only common strict comparisons therefore calls omission undefeated, even though each application rejects it.

This particular witness is uncertainty about effective support, **not proof of irreducible normative disagreement or discovery of ethical standing**. The generic results also apply to differently classified profiles when their substantive interpretation is independently warranted. They do not justify treating unsupported classifications as live, nor demonstrate that the represented family covers an actual case.

There is no response undefeated in both accounts in the stated menu. That is not a reason to pretend there is one. Nor does it show that practical judgment is impossible. A timely discriminating inquiry, a newly feasible joint response, or an already justified procedure for uncertain choice can alter the practical problem. Their costs, support, authority and grounds must enter the account. The existing J3/J4 and inquiry results handle those conditional routes. This study does not invent a free observation, a freely combinable response, or a universal probability distribution.

## 4. What this adds to reflective intelligence and alignment

The proposed capacity is **to revise the extent of a practical claim without needing to settle everything first**. An intelligence may know why a current arrangement is no longer defensible under every supported account while remaining uncertain which repair will work. Conversely, a favored alternative may be supported only under a disputed classification. Reporting the former as indecision, or the latter as settled obligation, loses relevant structure in the judgment.

This matters when intelligence deliberately changes its formative conditions. An operative goal can remain successful on its old measure while new relations defeat its adequacy as a guide. Replacing it requires more than selecting whatever receives later approval. The developing account must preserve actual consequences, state which reasons survive across live applications, and distinguish the epistemic status of a proposed change from its implementation and authority. A shared generative direction can guide this work without selecting one operative goal for every formed position.

There is no new requirement to prove an infinite sequence of higher standards before every action. A bounded conclusion can be supported by explicit current premises and relevant coverage. There is equally no proof that those premises are unrevisable. If J2 itself changes, the mathematical consequences using its comparison must be reassessed; successful prediction, new terminology or a majority's endorsement does not by itself justify that normative change.

**规范开放不等于把一切尚未想到的判断都当成反对票。** 需要说明哪些应用已有根据、何种遗漏会改变结论、当前主张承担多大覆盖责任。可以先取得有范围的判断，也可以保留正面分歧；既不冻结现成目录，也不要求先完成全部伦理发现。跨智能的共同根据不自动成为相同动机、相同目标或同一行政命令。

## 5. Substantive counterreview

**A fixed evaluator can represent the whole change.** Accepted. The positive control explicitly preserves the same comparator across the stage change. Only one ranking that ignores the relevant stage difference is excluded. We prove no impossibility of fixed programs, historical utility functions, finite representation or conventional learning.

**The new basic claim was handed to the model.** Correct. The witness checks what follows if it is warranted; it does not discover its status. The argument identifies the outstanding acquisition and classification burden instead of declaring it complete. A correspondence showing that the dependence is optional, imposed illegitimately, or already covered by an alternative would change the application.

**A plan can be rational under uncertainty even if some known-state alternative is always better.** Correct. Actual defeat under a first-order comparison, robust certification, and justified choice under uncertainty are different questions. This study does not infer a universal prohibition from statewise inferiority or refute maximality as an epistemic decision criterion. An additional justified uncertainty procedure could select a plan without certifying it undefeated in every application. The exact negative conclusion is that the weaker certificate cannot establish the stronger one.

**The live descriptions may share the same omission.** Correct. The actual-application theorem explicitly requires coverage. An unanimously mistaken family cannot establish an actual ethical conclusion by consensus. Existing model-revision and reform-evidence studies supply failure cases and conditional correction mechanisms. Nothing in this proof settles open-ended adequacy of the family.

**Combining or randomizing the two responses may supply a common improvement.** Possible, with a correspondingly richer eligible menu and warranted treatment of risk. The example excludes neither possibility in reality. The actual capacity to combine, deadlines, divided provision and permissions must be established. Randomization does not automatically guarantee that a basic condition is met on every realized branch; a comparison in expectation adds different premises.

**Universal undefeatedness may demand too much.** Accepted. The finite example has no universally undefeated response. We present that as an informative limit, not a universal ethical admission rule. J4's bounded procedures remain available under their own grounds. Disagreement does not license suppressing alternatives until uniformity appears.

**This is established decision theory in a new vocabulary.** The logical distinction has substantial predecessors, credited below. The contribution claimed here is its checked connection to developing claim descriptions under the repository's explicit G0/J account, with inherited results reused. No global originality, empirical advantage or exclusive explanatory necessity is established.

- **Matthias C. M. Troffaes, “Decision Making under Uncertainty using Imprecise Probabilities” (2007).** Read the introduction, §§3.2–3.4 and §4 in the author's online text/preprint; the arXiv deposit is from 2018. ## 8. The next unresolved bridge

The next task is **warranted formation of the live application family itself**: how participation, evidence and criticism generate candidate distinctions about new forms of living; how those distinctions earn or lose basic/secondary status; and how a conclusion remains appropriately limited when relevant descriptions may still be missing. Existing feature construction, model revision, concern formation and access repair supply parts of that task, not an already justified full classification.

A successful continuation would trace at least one new distinction from actual interaction through grounds for its relevance and classification to a changed practical judgment, while preserving a serious alternative classification and specifying what evidence or argument defeats it. It must distinguish discovering a consequence from revising a normative commitment, and identify which independently justified G0/J premise the classification instantiates. Merely supplying the right new predicate or adding another fixed-choice controller would not close this bridge.
