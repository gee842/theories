## A minimal kind of distinction, with an explicit access interface

Consider two command ports whose fixed wiring is initially unknown to the
learner. One runs `capture; localCopyA`; the other runs `bulkCopyA`. The admitted
family contains the two swapped assignments. The physical plant still follows
the unchanged [sixteen-event law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/FormedSelection.lean).
The port wiring is a supplied interface to these events, not a newly acquired
physical law or an extra route from source into common fuel.

An encounter binds the actually issued port to a slot before execution. After
the recording allocation is withdrawn, a contact mechanism detects whether
common fuel changed across the completed native macro. Only that effect enters
the slot. Binding/commit costs must be excluded from the measured interval;
otherwise the recorder would misclassify its own consumption as a plant effect.
The learner receives neither the hidden wiring nor the producer's state dump.
The contact law, reliable action binding and comparison circuit remain supplied
realization conditions. An arbitrary externally selected bit is insufficient.

For the admitted family and completed calibrated encounters, the two bits are
`(1,0)` or `(0,1)`, where 1 means common fuel was preserved. Reading the positive
slot identifies the port that preserves the common unit needed by later repair.
This elementary implication concerns acquired port correspondence. The
[all-event common-fuel theorem](CONTINUING-DEPENDENCY-MATH.md) and the later
repair requirement explain why that difference matters; they are not learned
merely by observing two successful operations.

One truthful port observation already identifies the other under the stipulated
two-permutation family. Probing both ports is therefore a symmetric direct
evidence protocol with the same total training consumption in either wiring,
not a claim of minimal inquiry. Both training operations successfully replace
an A. A learner attending only to immediate reconstruction success would obtain
the same response from both; the common-preservation record retains a difference
relevant to the specified continuation.

## Matched controls and failure limits

The matched reader interventions retain the same assembled parts, native
training effects, completed bits and reader expenditure while clamping the
installed port. Across swapped wirings, each fixed port succeeds in only the
wiring where it accesses the local macro. Both calibrated acquired choices
complete the actual later repair. The wrong port restores A but cannot repair
after damage; it ends with common 0, source 215 and total expenditure 375.
The two record-inversion arms likewise select bulk and fail that later operation.
Clamping with 279 rather than 280 leaks leaves common 2, and both local and bulk
choices then complete both components. A known-native controller using available
local/source support before bulk copying is an appropriate
informed comparator and can succeed without this learning. No superiority or
universal necessity of acquired distinction follows.

The fixed twenty cases include lost contact, interrupted commitment, interrupted
reading, both-bit and port-0-only contact inversions. Lost contact and interrupted
commitment preserve the actual completed first reconstruction and escrow 1;
the latter additionally retains a latched bit. Interrupted reading retains both
records with common 283. Port-0-only inversion yields `[0,0]` or `[1,1]`; after
the fixed losses and wear, it stops with no installed command and common 1.
Damage is imposed only after completed reconstruction. These conventions were
fixed before execution.

Stale or misbound action records, changed wiring, inadequate reserves, missing
source and memory damage remain untested limitations here. The local macro
authenticates both native events. A captured local unit with no completed copy
would not be a completed reconstruction, and nonoccurrence cannot automatically
become a stored negative result without an additional interface.

Bulk copying remains adequate for the immediate replacement alone and, with
enough common fuel remaining, for replacement followed by repair. A damaged
site with an existing local unit can also copy without first repairing. These
native alternatives must survive the learned-port presentation. Restricting the
ports does not prove that no other native adequate route exists.
