6 October 2026. AI-authored mechanism study responding to Ren's request that learning, concern, governance and alignment mechanisms be derived in one connected generative model. This is a supporting connection within that goal; the no-assumptions/no-axioms requirement remains unmet.

The source is the already accepted `EvaluativeSuccession` process, which is an instance of the original `Generative.Process` and `Generative.Trace`. The [new Lean module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/ConnectedModel.lean) changes no original state, event, decoder, selection rule, resource law or gate.

The complete inherited source closure is the thirty-module [application verification receipt](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/succession/application/formal/verification.json), ending in `ApplicationEvidence`, `SuccessionWitness` and `EvaluativeSuccession`. These modules retain actual formation/preparation, learned actor/recipient registers, native acquired influence, paid archival production and reading, copying, intervention and action. The earlier [composition account](FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) classifies which antecedents are joined staged histories, conditional projections, separate realizations or missing interfaces. This study does not silently turn those classifications into one unrestricted physical law.

This is different from adding another synchronized product or re-proving finite protocol flattening. The new proof follows **every admitted finite continuation of this existing composite process** after a current permission cut, including its archive-production route, selected-action route, copy events and settlement events.

## Derived invariant

For any admitted one-step transition of the existing law:

1. If `permission = false` before it, permission remains false afterward.
2. The cut excludes recipient work in both the selected-trial route and the fresh-query archive route. Querying is not treated as causally harmless: it can itself perform productive recipient work in this law.
3. Every other admitted event preserves the recipient balance. In particular, acquisition and writing may settle work already performed without creating another recipient transfer.

Induction on the original `Trace` derives both statements for an arbitrary finite continuation. The same trace inherits exact retained native/history fields, its actual energy bill, and material conservation. No account is reset and no abstract memory is installed for the proof.

The bank event accepts a syntactic successor argument. The no-credit proof therefore uses its actual authenticated producer transition and the `RecipientArchive.Step` successor equality; it does not assume an arbitrary submitted successor preserves the balance.

This permanence follows from an explicit feature of the supplied law: permission-changing events only withdraw grants, and copying combines inherited permission with the supplied copy flag. There is no rule for issuing a new grant. The theorem therefore does not establish that legitimate permission can never be restored in a richer institution. A guarded regrant event would require a different, episode-sensitive invariant.

`attempt` is still enabled after a cut when its port is pending. Its operational rule consumes the port instead of beginning protected action. The result concerns new protected effects, not a universal prohibition on attempted commands.

Under its separately supplied episode binding and one-service meaning, that service is already met. Nevertheless, the existing selector performs another ten-unit work event while acquired influence remains unchanged.

The new combined statement places interpreted completion, the actual whole root trace, the earlier ten-unit credit and the later repeated credit together. It constructs no new withdrawal policy and imposes no new definition of ethical good. Repetition may be appropriate for another service or an independent reason; the obstruction concerns the declared one-service interpretation. The still missing mechanism is acquired, warranted change of **the applicability of the recommendation**, retaining means knowledge and independent grounds. A permission bit can block execution without learning that distinction.

## What would change the account

A valid continuation that restores permission or increases recipient balance after a cut under these exact unchanged laws would refute the new invariant. A concrete intermediate state where the stated paid settlement is disabled would refute the witness. Neither is repaired by discarding that history or inventing a replacement reserve.

For the governing derivation, a constructive continuation must next explain how an actually acquired episode/completion distinction changes the operative selector or its update rule, and why that revision is warranted, without erasing acquired means, unrelated reasons, costs or differentiated affected centers. Merely adding the desired completion-sensitive branch would supply the mechanism being explained. The origin of the event laws and independent ethical grounds remains a deeper obligation under Ren's stronger target.
