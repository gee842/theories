# Whole-process realization: two primary comparisons

6 October 2026. AI-authored comparison under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The named connection is **the character and relations of differentiated realization → the ethical quality of the unfolding they constitute**. This note tests a constitutive proposal; it adds no experiment, theorem or completed ethical derivation.

**Finding:** causal interdependence, successful operation and present fulfillment each identify something substantive, but none alone identifies a good configuration. The sharper positive task is to explain what is realized in a relation, what its continuation requires, and why its ethical quality warrants that continuation. A good response to an existing burden does not by itself make the burden worth preserving.

The [whole-process argument](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md) already separates consequence inclusion, joint feasibility and ethical appraisal. Its positive comparison preserves relevant lives and capacities while removing impairment without a countervailing warranted loss. The words *impairment*, *relevant* and *warranted* perform substantive evaluative work. This is explicitly acknowledged in the original argument, not a newly discovered hidden assumption. The missing grounding cannot be supplied by calling the resulting arrangement more complete or better integrated.

The [relational-goodness argument](RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md) also already rejects defining good solely through production of further good. ## 2. Moore: constitution, causal support and response to existing evil

**Source synopsis:** [§§18–22](https://fair-use.org/g-e-moore/principia-ethica/s.18) distinguish the value of a configuration from the sum of its parts' values. [§19](https://fair-use.org/g-e-moore/principia-ethica/s.19) contrasts a necessary causal means with a constituent of the valuable whole; [§21](https://fair-use.org/g-e-moore/principia-ethica/s.21) denies that reciprocal causal dependence itself establishes value. [§128](https://fair-use.org/g-e-moore/principia-ethica/s.128) offers substantive relational judgments about malicious enjoyment and punishment. [§§132–133](https://fair-use.org/g-e-moore/principia-ethica/s.133) distinguish the value of an appropriate response to existing suffering from a justification for suffering's existence. [§134](https://fair-use.org/g-e-moore/principia-ethica/s.134) presents the particular value judgments as revisable and warns against imposing desired systematic unity. These are philosophical judgments, not empirical findings.

**Our use:** the argument needs to preserve the actual relation constituting the proposed good. An episode of trustworthy companionship includes more than two independently pleasant states; correcting an episode of cruelty changes more than the total amount of activity. Conversely, mere dependence of two operations does not identify either relation as good. A shared process can realize either configuration. This is a test of what the proposed generative account explains, rather than adoption of Moore's metaphysics.

Moore's particular claims about punishment, ideal worlds and the total balance of value are not premises of our account. Nor do we adopt his account of intrinsic properties, his claim that all relevant comparison admits amounts, or a universal arithmetic of values. His causal/constitutive distinction must itself be reconstructed within continuing activity: a relation can be causally formed and also constitute the current phenomenon. Those descriptions answer different questions without placing constitution outside the unfolding.

## 3. Aristotle: present fulfillment and the quality of its activity

**Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics*, Book X, chapters 3–5, W. D. Ross translation.** Directly read these complete chapters in the [MIT Internet Classics Archive primary translation](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.10.x.html) on 6 October 2026. Reading relied on the HTML text; no Greek text, manuscript, alternate translation or complete work was checked. Incidental material elsewhere on the returned page is not additional relied-on scope. No original bytes were imported.

**Source synopsis:** X.3 rejects identifying all pleasure with replenishment after a lack. X.4 contrasts pleasure's present completeness with a process completed only when its product is finished, and describes pleasure as completing activity. X.5 relates different pleasures to different activities and assigns their ethical quality through the activity's quality. Its appeal to the virtuous person's judgment remains part of that account. These chapters therefore distinguish present enjoyment from successful pursuit of a future result, while retaining an evaluative account of which activity is worthy. They do not derive ethical quality from generic successful functioning.

**Our use:** enjoyment, understanding or connection may be realized in present participation. Their significance need not wait for a later product or a subject's further endorsement. This supplies a substantive comparator for the repository's existing protection of present good. It also exposes a potential circle: replacing *good* with *fulfilled activity*, and then distinguishing good from bad fulfillment by whether the activity is worthwhile, has relocated the disputed judgment. The account must say which features warrant the distinction.

No claim that Aristotle derives our nondual framework follows. We do not import his species hierarchy, standards of human excellence or broader political exclusions. The inspected text is a comparator for a specific relation, not authority for those commitments or for a universal realization theory of affect.

## 4. A sharper continuation test

The following is our proposed application, not a conclusion attributed to either source. It sharpens the inherited whole-process comparison by keeping the role of the alleged burden explicit.

| Role in the history | What must be preserved or tested | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| A burden helped produce an achieved understanding | Preserve that history and the understanding actually retained | Repeating or continuing the burden is necessary to preserve the achievement |
| Under the present arrangement, a burden enables a valued activity | Establish an actually feasible changed arrangement and its consequences | The dependency must hold under every available arrangement |
| A condition partly constitutes the activity being valued | Identify whether removing it preserves, transforms or ends that activity | The original good remains numerically and qualitatively identical after removal |
| Repair responds well to a burden that already exists | Preserve the reality and possible value of the response | Keeping the burden in place improves the whole by keeping repair active |

For example, care may foster understanding and confidence through responding to an actual illness. If the illness ends, this need not erase that history or what was learned. The current caring activity may nevertheless change, and some forms of practiced competence may need other conditions to continue. The comparison must identify those changes rather than asserting that every relevant good is unchanged. None of this establishes that continued illness is justified merely because it once occasioned care.

There is also a genuine rival to examine: a relation may allegedly be valuable partly because it responds to an actual burden or realizes deserved treatment. If removing the burden changes that relation, the disagreement concerns which changed configuration is warranted. It cannot be settled by claiming an unchanged collection of component goods, nor by declaring every lost control or satisfaction ethically irrelevant. The [existing desert study](PERMITTED-RELIEF-AND-DESERT-SOURCES.md) preserves this exact distinction between local relief, relational considerations, permission and an overall verdict.

The positive candidate survives these comparisons only as a claim about specified kinds of realization and their relations. The next result should defend why a particular transformed configuration improves the unfolding, or identify a genuinely warranted loss that narrows that conclusion. A list containing every consequence, a feasibility proof, or an account of why an evaluator learned its preference cannot substitute for that appraisal.

## 5. Scope and revision conditions

This note supplies two primary philosophical comparators and a more discriminating test. It adds no independent support by rereading earlier empirical sources and makes no claim to priority for constitutive or relational value.

The comparison would require revision if a proposed alternative silently changed the intended realization, if the burden remained necessary under the actual available conditions, or if an independently defended relational good was omitted from appraisal. A demonstration that target attainment, integration or recurrence can coexist with cruelty already defeats those features as sufficient tests; it does not defeat every richer account of differentiated realization. Conversely, naming that richer account *whole goodness* does not establish it.

The accepted conditional foundation remains unchanged. The unresolved connection is the substantive warrant for the ethical quality attributed to a specified configuration, including how to acknowledge genuine conflict without making the whole a larger owner or one total score.

### 6.4 Our identification contract and the live rival

Keep four questions distinct: what the task technically demands; what effort or unpleasantness this participant actually undergoes; what the participant contributes; and why the specified activity is worthwhile. Those relations can interact without being interchangeable. Expertise may reduce experienced effort while retaining precise control. A support can relieve distraction while leaving interpretation and execution to the performer. Another intervention can produce the same sound while bypassing the performer in the very role being assessed. The last distinction must be established from the actual organization of the activity, not inferred from the presence of technology.

For the proposed musical comparison, identify the intended role before matching outputs. Possible roles include executing a passage, choosing its interpretation, responding to another musician, directing a device, or knowingly enjoying a playback together. They need not have identical value or be mutually exclusive. Substituting one for another without notice differs from transparently choosing it. A playback participant can still listen, move, learn or accompany others; a claim of absent participation must specify what is absent.

The following are proposed identification contrasts, not experiments conducted or claims that perfect experiential duplicates are feasible:

| Comparison | What evidence would need to establish | What that evidence alone cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Support versus taking over | The actual routes through which the participant's timing, interpretation or correction shape the identified activity | That all aid diminishes agency, or that preserved agency settles the overall choice |
| Mastery versus replacement | Continuing competent responsiveness despite reduced felt effort, distinguished from a route that bypasses the specified skill | That ease is always improvement or that every skilled performance is ethically good |
| Responsive duet versus matched playback | Whether participants actually adjust to one another in the role at issue; nominally identical sound is insufficient | That every pleasure or welfare difference follows from this causal distinction |
| Redundant contribution versus sham control | Mechanism and role evidence where another process could have produced the same output anyway | That absence of simple “without this person, no sound” dependence proves absence of contribution |
| Effort versus aversiveness | Separate evidence about exertion, technical demands and experienced unpleasantness | That reducing one necessarily reduces the others or destroys the valued activity |

A warranted inquiry could use records of actual control paths, skilled adjustments and clearly specified counterfactual changes, together with participants' descriptions of what they are doing and undergoing. In redundant or overdetermined performances, a simple output perturbation is insufficient; the constitutive route and actual role matter. Transparent automation may become a medium of skilled participation instead of eliminating it. Neither self-report nor the observer's causal diagram independently establishes the ethical significance of that participation.

Two substantive rivals remain. An effort-based account can accept genuine relief and retained skill while claiming that the lost exertion had contributory value. The headache comparison contests this claim but does not prove its impossibility. A view locating welfare in experience or satisfied attitudes can grant the different causal routes yet deny an additional welfare contribution from actual skilled participation. It may still condemn deception or broken commitments on other grounds. Our case must defend the contribution it attributes, rather than portray either rival as denying the activity's physical facts.

**Result:** the useful constraint is preservation of the specified worthwhile participation, with its ethical standing independently defended. The target is neither maximum difficulty nor identical external output. Sometimes support makes the same participation easier; sometimes mastery changes what counts as a challenge; sometimes a valued activity is replaced or legitimately transformed. Distinguishing these outcomes advances appraisal without establishing a universal rank or a complete theory of achievement. No new formal or empirical result is claimed.

### 7.3 Our application: locate the actual disagreement

The following is our analysis of the duet, using distinctions already present in the repository. Within one unfolding, concern, understanding and effective contribution can jointly constitute a way of participating. Separating them for comparison does not claim they are independently manipulable or originate outside that activity.

An experience-only welfare account can accept every causal difference between responsive playing and an apparently identical preset rendering while denying an additional participant benefit. Its welfare judgment need not excuse deception or broken agreements. Conversely, a world-directed desire account can value actually playing with another because that is what the person cares about. Matching pleasure and belief does not match fulfillment of that concern. An informed-desire account can make the same distinction when the relevant concern survives its specified factual correction. This establishes the central narrowing: **dependence on an actual relation does not entail value independent of attitudes.**

The positive joint candidate therefore survives: informed concern realized through actual engagement can constitute a present good without requiring a later product. It is compatible with some desire and hybrid accounts. Competent activity alone does not yet explain why that activity benefits this participant, and a preference for reality does not uniquely identify an attitude-independent good.

A stronger claim needs a further comparison. Informed indifference about whether one's present contribution is operative would matter only if the account predicts an additional benefit from actual exercise even then. Matching current attraction does not match counterfactual informed desire; matching factual information does not match a morally idealized endorsement. State which attitude condition the rival uses before claiming to control it. Do not redefine indifference as defective solely because it withholds the desired verdict.

The comparison also cannot hold both factual knowledge of different operative routes and identical relevant beliefs fixed. A stipulated uncertainty about which route occurs is a different case. Its physical feasibility remains to be shown; it is not an experimental protocol. Consent, deception, commitments, anticipated discovery and future learning or opportunity can independently explain a preference or prudential difference. If they change, the inference must retain them rather than assign their effects to participation alone.

**Result:** this round reconciles the duet with inherited relational and attitude-sensitive arguments. It preserves actual engagement as an explanatory candidate while withholding the stronger inference to an independent increment of participant welfare. A defended comparison may support that increment, reject it, or reveal a different target such as achievement's impersonal value. None by itself supplies overall ethical priority or authority to impose participation. The next bridge remains the substantive warrant for the specified good-making relation.
