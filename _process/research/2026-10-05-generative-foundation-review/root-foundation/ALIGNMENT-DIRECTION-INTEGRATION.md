## 2. Affirmative warrant and substantive self-review

**Whole inclusion.** Training, knowledge, labor, infrastructure, use, dependence and correction differentiate within the same unfolding. Following their effects prevents developer benefit or successful output from silently setting the scope. It does not authorize a total administrator or require complete cosmic knowledge.

**Why these additional commitments?** J1 treats genuine improvement as a comparative reason while retaining the actual history. J2 gives non-substitutable basic participation priority within its disclosed range. J3 combines reliability of justified dependence with inclusion of contributors' own lives. J4 responds to finite understanding, plural standing and timely action through legitimate participation, inquiry and correction. These are argued commitments; G0 alone is not advertised as determining them.

**Strong rival retained.** An aggregation-sensitive account can accept G0, every service fact and every real loss, yet prefer sufficiently extensive optional capability gains to an additional basic protection. J2 refuses that compensation. Its lexical strength and cost are explicit; no logical refutation is claimed. Narrowing J2 would change the mixed comparison but need not erase no-loss improvement, actual handoff or the distinction between justified responsibility and causal connection.

**Scope/classification challenge.** The account cannot justify every chosen boundary by calling it basic. In the example, usable communication and bounded contributor burdens are separately stipulated service and burden criteria. Their basic normative classification is an additional application premise. If growth prevents a different basic loss, or transfer seriously harms a contributor, the original comparison fails. The formal crossed-claim case retains this failure of ranking rather than relabeling its damage.

**Model standing challenge.** A service model's replacement does not by itself settle the presence or absence of its own experiential or other moral standing. The example stipulates no omitted morally relevant loss of that kind; any supported additional standing must enter the comparison. The positive direction includes biological/ecological, mental/relational and broader organizational ranges without treating all growth as equal or everything artificial as disposable.

**Authority challenge.** The opportunity to improve outcomes gives no unlimited right to control infrastructure or people. In the worked case, an advisory AI can explain, recommend and seek authorized action; operators and institutions have distinct responsibilities through actual undertakings, power and capacity. Voluntary participation, legitimate authority and emergency limits remain independently justified J3/J4/E conditions.

**Uncertainty and revision challenge.** Missing evidence is not no damage. A narrower candidate set can omit actuality. Existing requirements survive faithful redescription under supplied coverage and meaning conditions; a new reward or self-description proves neither fulfillment nor release. Actual changed grounds require reassessment. No general self-modification safety theorem is claimed.

These are substantive constraints on the proposed position, not a renewed demand to prove G0 without an axiom. They specify when to narrow or reverse a particular verdict.
