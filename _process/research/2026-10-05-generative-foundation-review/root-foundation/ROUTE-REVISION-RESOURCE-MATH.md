# Resource bounds from diagnosis to route replacement and succession

6 October 2026. AI-authored independent mathematical analysis of the [prospective revision protocol](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/revision/PROTOCOL.md). This study addresses a narrow part of the [governing derivation](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md): whether an actual acquired diagnosis leaves a physically feasible path to changing, exercising and transmitting its learning mechanism. The calculations use the declared prices and inherited resource laws; they neither select those prices retrospectively nor establish a complete implementation or empirical result.

**Main result:** from the specified informative first-monitor boundary, the extra accessible grant of five is the exact minimum for the stable TARGET proposal to complete three ordinary probes and productive copying/use/hold. Endpoint balance alone incorrectly suggests four. The minimum changes with the probe horizon, proposal and actual receipts; enough additional funding also retains an unproductive successor.

## 1. Boundary and unchanged imported laws

The actual ordinary CLOSED/FROZEN cohort and separately controlled PRIVATE_ZERO cohort reach a completed informative first frame with recipient reserve 11, zero current-carrier cash, a wrong recipient bit and one unused ten-unit monitor packet. Their earlier histories and probabilities differ. This is not the ordinary TARGET first-failure frame: failure with a changed record alone remains ambiguous between TARGET and PRIVATE_ZERO and does not authorize the stated replacement rule.

The monitor's eight cells and head are occupied. The old carrier and its routing part are occupied; no free replacement part, successor blank or successor routing part is inherited. Original-actor balances, earlier learner balances, source and material stay separate. The grant is a protected original provision, released by an explicit transfer, not a return newly produced by the diagnosis.

Let \(g\ge0\) be the accessible recipient grant. Head assembly, evidence reading and route swap each cost one above floor one. Conditional on legitimate evidence, a head and replacement part, these three stages are affordable from \(11+g\), leaving

\[
B=11+g-3=8+g
\]

before ordinary probing. The swap preserves the actual record; a proposed TARGET is therefore initially wrong too. Its inherited record is not silently repaired during formation. This calculation concerns a completed, attempted replacement, including a same-route replacement charged at the same price. A no-repair control does not pay these three charges and has a different starting reserve.

The imported [successor learner](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/formal/SuccessorLearning.lean) charges rent1, sense1, work3, acquisition1 and write1. Its funding guard preserves floor1, and its work event consumes a packet and credits the recipient. Its `FundDonor`/`copyStart` bridge funds the actual current carrier as donor while preserving the original actor separately. The imported [formed-route copying law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/transmission/formal/FormedRecipientRoute.lean) supplies the actual material, seed, work and hold guards. The [Python copier](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/transmission/model.py) separately exposes all failed copying prefixes.

## 2. Sharp ordinary-probe cash-flow bound

Let \(c\in\{0,1\}\) denote actual receipt of the ten-unit packet. A probe spends five before its possible return and two after. With recipient reserve \(R\) and zero carrier cash, completion is equivalent, as a funding condition, to

\[
R\ge6,\qquad R-5+10c\ge3.
\]

Thus a failed probe requires \(R\ge8\), a successful probe requires \(R\ge6\), and either completed probe leaves

\[
R'=R-7+10c.
\]

These are conditional on the nonfinancial guards: access, correct phase, source, target query and the specified operational evidence law. The successful-probe inequality does not assert that success occurs.

For a prescribed actual receipt word \(c_1,\ldots,c_n\), set \(C_i=\sum_{j=1}^{i}c_j\), \(C_0=0\), and

\[
b(0)=8,\quad b(1)=6,\qquad
P(c_1\ldots c_n)=\max_{1\le i\le n}
\left[7(i-1)-10C_{i-1}+b(c_i)\right].
\]

Use \(P(\varnothing)=0\). All probes can be funded exactly when \(B\ge P\). If completed, they leave \(R_c=B-7n+10C_n\). Necessity follows by inspecting each pre-return and post-return prefix. Sufficiency follows by applying those same guards in order. This is a closed-form account of the existing sequential payments, not a new reward criterion.

## 3. Actual copying, use and hold

Let \(R_c\) be recipient reserve after the probes; donor and prospective child start with zero cash. The imported continuation has these recipient balances:

| Stage reached | Recipient reserve | Additional condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Fund donor1; donor pays hold1 | \(R_c-1\) | \(R_c\ge2\), live actual donor |
| Own-use1, template read1, assembly1, write1 completed | \(R_c-5\) | \(R_c\ge6\), new blank and successor routing part |
| Seed5 transferred to actual copied child | \(R_c-10\) | \(R_c\ge11\) |
| Child rent1, sense1, work3 completed | \(R_c-10+10d\) | funded seed, target/action guards, reserved packet |
| Recipient hold1 paid | \(R_c-11+10d\) | \(R_c-10+10d\ge2\) |

Here \(d\) is the child's actual capture indicator, not an evaluator's opinion of the proposed route. The seed transfer is not a second expenditure: the child subsequently dissipates its five units. Total copying/use/hold expenditure is eleven: donor hold1, four recipient operations, child operations5 and final hold1.

Consequently, copying through child action needs \(R_c\ge11\). A productive held child needs \(R_c\ge11\); an unproductive held child needs \(R_c\ge12\). At \(R_c=11\), an unsuccessful child can act but cannot pay its final hold. A copied but unseeded template is a different endpoint, achievable already at \(R_c=6\).

Combining the probe and copy guards gives the exact grant conditions:

\[
\begin{aligned}
g_{\rm probes}&=\max(0,P-8),\\
g_{\rm child\ action}&=\max(0,P-8,\;11+7n-10C_n-8),\\
g_{\rm held}&=\max(0,P-8,\;K(d)+7n-10C_n-8),\\
K(1)&=11,\qquad K(0)=12.
\end{aligned}
\]

The final recipient balance, when copying/use/hold completes, is

\[
R_{\rm final}=8+g-7n+10C_n-11+10d.
\]

No final-balance equation replaces the prefix term \(P\).

## 4. Immediate, two-probe and three-probe continuations

Assume the actual initially wrong recipient bit, a stable available plant, ordinary present-channel updates, and unchanged recipient context. Write F for failure and S for success. TARGET repairs after its first failure and then stays correct; PRIVATE_ZERO flips after every probe; CLOSED/FROZEN leave the wrong bit unchanged.

| Probes after swap | Proposed route | Probe receipts | Child outcome | Minimum \(g\): probes complete | Minimum \(g\): child acts | Minimum \(g\): child held |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | Any of the four | Empty | F | 0 | 3 | 4 |
| 2 | TARGET | FS | S | 5 | 7 | 7 |
| 2 | PRIVATE_ZERO | FS | F | 5 | 7 | 8 |
| 2 | CLOSED/FROZEN | FF | F | 7 | 17 | 18 |
| 3 | TARGET | FSS | S | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 3 | PRIVATE_ZERO | FSF | S | 5 | 14 | 14 |
| 3 | CLOSED/FROZEN | FFF | F | 14 | 24 | 25 |

All entries also require the explicit source and parts below. A minimum grant for an unproductive held child does not promise a productive successor for any grant. Immediate copying cannot make the wrong record correct, regardless of the route installed alongside it. With two probes, TARGET and PRIVATE_ZERO have the same observed FS receipts but different final records and different child outcomes. A third productive TARGET probe adds three net recipient units and makes grant5 sufficient where the two-probe copy requires grant7. This is a consequence of the supplied schedule, not evidence that an extra probe is always beneficial.

For the primary TARGET proposal at \(g=5\), \(B=13\) and the probe balances are \(13\to6\to9\to12\). Copying/use/hold ends at11. At \(g=4\), the first failed probe leaves5; rent and sense then leave3, insufficient to pay work3 while preserving floor1. The second return cannot be borrowed in advance. Although the hypothetical final probe balance would suffice to copy, that endpoint is unreachable. This establishes sharpness of five under this law, rather than merely sufficiency of the chosen example.

At the same grant5, PRIVATE_ZERO completes \(13\to6\to9\to2\). Donor funding and hold leave recipient1, blocking own-use; no blank, successor part or copy packet has been consumed. CLOSED/FROZEN reach \(13\to6\), then pay rent, sense and work of their second failed probe to reach1. Acquisition is blocked; a third completed failure must not be imputed. At grant14, the private-zero proposal can instead copy its finally correct record productively. At grant25, even the still-wrong nonwriting proposals can complete all three failures, copy, fail the child action and pay its hold.

## 5. Source and material feasibility are independent of cash

The first-monitor boundary supplies one remaining ten-unit packet. Completing \(n\) new ordinary probes requires a probe bank of \(10n\), so newly connected probe source must satisfy

\[
A\ge\max(0,10n-10).
\]

For the primary three-probe path, the declared addition20 is exactly sufficient. A separate copy packet10 is required and cannot simultaneously fund a probe. Immediate copying leaves the old probe packet unused; two probes need only ten new probe units, and any unused declared twenty-unit provision remains separately accounted. New packets cannot be credited to the recipient until actual work captures them.

With the fixed ten-unit work law, a depleted probe bank blocks the next work event before its fee; additional liquid grant cannot create a packet. The source remains finite, including on histories that cannot afford later operations. Old inaccessible source or original-actor balances are not alternative funding without a new access law.

The Python copier's API deserves an explicit join check: `transmit` initializes `auxiliary_source=law.packet` inside its local call. A new wrapper must debit the externally reserved ten-unit copy packet exactly once and account for its local return; the imported call alone does not prove that the outer packet existed. Setting `law.packet=0` instead changes the local law to a zero-return packet: that Python function can still execute an action and retain a child with sufficient cash. It is not automatically a blocked-source event. The fixed ten-unit Lean work guard requires its packet. Therefore a zero-copy-source control must specify whether it blocks access to the original module or explicitly changes the packet law; neither interpretation permits a ten-unit return without a source debit.

Material requirements cannot be met by choosing a larger \(g\). A successful revision needs the new finite revision head and one replacement routing part. The displaced part remains retired and nonspendable. Successful copying needs one distinct blank and a second new routing part. Without a revision head no head can be assembled; without its replacement part no swap occurs, even if earlier head/read fees were paid. Without a successor blank, paid donor hold and own-use may be sunk before read blocks. Without a successor routing part, read can occupy the blank and leave the actual template in its paid latch, but assembly cannot finish. Earlier occupied material, monitor cells/head and retired parts cannot fill these roles under the specified law.

## 6. Conditional differential persistence, not guaranteed improvement

At the three-probe horizon, grant5 through13 permits only TARGET among the four supplied proposals to reach a held child under the stable available wrong-bit conditions. Grant14 through24 also permits PRIVATE_ZERO. Grant25 or more permits all four, including unproductive CLOSED/FROZEN descendants. These are conditional route-specific feasibility statements. They do not replace the actual uniformly supplied proposal law, preserve no global advantage under arbitrary environments, and do not constitute full-mixture probabilities. An unsuccessful proposal remains part of the incoming mixture.

A late reversal before probe3 changes the result without changing the price. TARGET can produce FSF, acquire the new correct record and still end at reserve2 under grant5, unable to copy. PRIVATE_ZERO can produce FSS, end at12, copy a record it just made wrong, fail the descendant action and nevertheless pay hold to finish retained at1. Outcome-dependent resource guards can retain existence without retaining useful behavior or an accurate learning route. The algebra above covers both cases by their actual receipt words and child outcomes.

The resource obstruction is therefore specific and practical: a sound diagnosis does not supply replacement matter, an evidence-consumption interface, accessible liquidity until the next return, or a productive continuation. This study supplies an independent feasibility certificate for the next implementation; it does not justify the recipient objective or derive the variation law, head recipe, coupling, ownership of source, concern or universal favorable direction.

## 7. Evidence boundary and possible formal follow-up

This analysis was derived directly from the frozen protocol and imported source guards. It did not rerun predecessor experiments or edit their sources. The independent concern reviewer read the complete note and protocol and confirmed the thresholds, horizon table and late-reversal arithmetic without a substantive finding. The [passed independent handoff certificate](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/monitor/handoff/independent-verification.json) separately executes 279 conditional fee/return words: nine scenarios across grants 0 through 30. This account check deliberately extends beyond the protocol's local 0-through-20 grid to check the 24/25 boundary; it is not a full route-replacement run. The same checker validates the actual exported first-commit boundaries. The new implementation must still establish guarded replacement, acquired behavior, source debit and material composition in its own event law.

A compact optional theorem would state the receipt-word prefix bound and its composition with the imported donor/copy threshold for a supplied feasible event schedule. Its hypotheses must retain actual nonfinancial guards and actual receipt labels; it would neither construct a proposal policy nor prove those receipts occur. This is proposed scope only. No new Lean module was created for this note; the revision implementer owns its mechanism proof.
