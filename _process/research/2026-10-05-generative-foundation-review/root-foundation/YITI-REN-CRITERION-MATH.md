## What is acquired

The unchanged material process distinguishes common fuel, a local store and incident source. A successful native repair consumes one common unit and changes the damaged site to intact. The added contact computes a compact channel mask from the before/after support coordinates of this same occurrence. It does not store a complete state history internally. Its own consumption is a separate event, so it cannot be misclassified as the support used by repair. Complete state histories are retained in the external execution audit.

The supplied candidate family contains three one-unit, single-channel consumption relations. A candidate fits a receipt precisely when its selected coordinate decreases by one and the other two are unchanged. Filtering retains exactly the candidates fitting the acquired receipt. `compatible_candidate_retained` proves retention of any fitting candidate already in the family; `actual_repair_identifies_common` derives the singleton common-channel result from an admitted native repair, with no supplied desired answer.

This is an identification result within that declared family. `successful_repair_does_not_exclude_fallback` supplies a substantive rival: a law that behaves identically whenever common support is positive, but uses incident source when common support is zero. The same successful repair history cannot exclude this law. `fallback_has_a_genuinely_different_continuation` exhibits its different result at zero common support. The original material law excludes that fallback; the observed sample alone does not establish the exclusion.

## How it changes a completion judgment

Let \(A(s)\) be the actual A count and \(a_*\) the count bound to the undertaking before its specified wear. The old criterion is \(a_*\leq A(s)\). The revised criterion preserves that count component and additionally requires the support relation learned for continued repair. The operational relation changes from an empty requirement list to the acquired common-channel requirement. The maintenance undertaking and its admitted scope remain supplied; this is acquisition of its concrete sufficiency content, not the origin of every purpose.

`acquired_requirement_changes_completion_content` proves a sharp difference at one and the same state: when the count is restored but common support is zero, the old judgment says complete, the learned judgment says incomplete, and the count completion itself remains true. It is therefore neither a change of historical truth nor merely a different ranking between equally accepted actions. The actual mechanism must also read and use this difference; the finite-history section below verifies that additional obligation.

`learned_criterion_fits_repair` identifies the acquired common requirement with the unchanged repair resource prerequisite. The theorem separately assumes an active resident and current damage. It does not claim that fuel alone enables repair, that damage was learned as the only cause of blocked capture, or that capture's other guards may be omitted.

The local extension makes the final criterion reading consume one common unit. A prospective plan test must include this reading. `acquired_common_requirement_reserves_its_reading` proves the exact result: count restoration plus at least two common units before the reading is equivalent to passing the common-support criterion after its admitted reading. One unit is consumed by the read and one remains for repair.

`free_reading_would_misclassify_completion` preserves the boundary case. At one common unit, an uncharged judgment would accept the repair reserve, but the actual reading removes that reserve. The planned local route and the bulk route must therefore be compared after all their own operations, not with a free evaluator outside the unfolding.

The final native protocol also charges the finish operation that a positive judgment permits. Its stronger `native_judgment_and_finish_reserve_three` theorem therefore requires three common units before judgment: one for reading, one for the actual finish, and one retained for repair. The earlier two-unit theorem concerns the shorter reading-only continuation and is not used as the full native requirement.

## Scope preserved by the proof

The acquired completion criterion does not equate material copying or persistence with goodness. No Actor, Patient, Owner or moral Reason type is introduced; the selected coordinates describe differentiated relations in one process. The native material law, candidate family, contact correspondence, maintenance scope, encoding, assembly, inference and selection recipes remain local explanatory inputs. Their being locally supplied neither places them outside generative reality nor proves their necessity. The account advances an actual acquisition interface while preserving the separate requirements of experiential realization and universal noninstrumental justification.
