# Review: retained acquisition as a calibration interface

6 October 2026. AI-authored review/formalization role in the continuing alignment and generative-grounding work. Named transition: **acquired recipient feedback → qualified retained command → corroboration of a current instrumental publication**. This supports the separately developed physical command-use join; this role has not implemented or edited that join.

**Review judgment:** the calibration interface has a defensible conditional result. The original acquired controller record can detect the declared archive-tag-only corruption because that fault does not alter the acquired record. Agreement does not establish calibration when both channels share the fault, when the target law has changed, or when the publication was first copied into the record used to check it. The completed local result is an information/calibration interface, not a completed alignment architecture. Formal author compilation and independent conceptual review remain distinct; root review of this new proof is required before treating it as independently reviewed.

## What changed in the account

The previous semantic adapter correctly stated that typed matching records can carry a wrongly calibrated command. It would nevertheless be a mistake to infer that the actual system has no other relevant evidence: its retained `q` and qualification `last` survive outside the archive tags. The saved histories contain those registers. Restricting attention to the archive alone would erase information available to a suitably implemented reader and manufacture an overly strong impossibility claim.

The present conditional result uses that surviving information. Independence here means a **distinct fault route**: corrupting archive action tags leaves the original acquired controller register intact. It does not require statistically independent samples. The archive, reason graph and qualified controller may all ultimately reflect the same past physical exposures; duplicating those exposures does not produce independent ethical justification or multiple empirical replications.

The positive-receipt invariant is not a new unexplained truth flag. The existing recipient updater starts unqualified, retains a candidate after a positive acquired receipt and marks it qualified, and retains a qualified candidate thereafter. Under the supplied stable binary target law and faithful channel, a positive receipt identifies the compatible command. Missing observations do not qualify, and actor observations do not overwrite the recipient state. The new finite-replay proof imports that updater and composes its existing soundness result over a sequence of acquired observations.

This replay is a mathematical wrapper over already acquired observations. It does not implement the process that obtains or pays for them. Action `active` is deliberately not a readability requirement: retained knowledge need not disappear when its action path is inactive. Conversely, an abstract `read_access` flag does not construct a physical reader.

## Decisive counterexamples and the next join

**Check before import.** The developing physical command route proposes writing the admitted reason command into the recipient cell and setting `last=true` before exposure. The new `self_import_guarantees_agreement_without_calibration` theorem uses the actual existing cell-write operation and proves that this operation guarantees subsequent agreement for every imported command, including an incompatible one. A post-import check would compare the publication to its own copy. The acquired-anchor theorem therefore applies to the original retained register, or to a separately preserved grounded record with an accounted-for preservation/read path. Setting the flag true for command commitment is not the original positive-receipt provenance. A paid implementation must order the check before that write or separately preserve the anchor; the present abstract study does neither physically.

**Common corruption.** A finite countermodel retains the entire declared visible state—candidate `q`, qualification flag, source claim and readiness—while its two physical worlds require opposite commands. Agreement is true in both but compatible in only one. No decoder of that visible state can identify both physical laws. This is a countermodel after relaxing calibration assumptions, not an event claimed to occur in the sound stable-law baseline. Additional genuinely informative observation can break the equivalence. The general full-history information theorem already exists as `ControllerRevision.same_feedback_excludes_incompatible_futures`; the local result is its concrete calibration illustration, not a second general discovery.

**Stale acquisition and valid new evidence.** Qualification persists under the old updater after an unsignaled target change. Its old claim can corroborate and now fail; new independently warranted evidence can disagree with that old controller and now be correct. Disagreement rules out simultaneous sound old qualification, unchanged applicable law and a true new claim. It does not identify which premise failed. Thus this interface reports corroboration, disagreement or unknown; turning disagreement into an unconditional rejection policy would be an unsupported additional decision rule.

**Receipt is not welfare.** Even correctly calibrated compatibility supplies the modeled physical receipt, conditional on opportunity. It neither justifies the objective `G` nor proves that a receipt benefits the recipient, resolves incompatible claims, or creates legitimate authority. The earlier semantic-action countermodel already separates compatibility from evaluative good; it has not been erased or resolved here.

The inspected interface reports `calibration_established=false`, `read_implemented=false`, `executed=false`, and unchanged stocks. Those limitations are accurate. No actual-source-zero endpoint has been given hidden next-action funds, and no recipient reserve pays for the actor. The freeze and independent checker must remain the source of exact fixture counts and masses; their test outputs are not observations of a learned AI or social institution.
