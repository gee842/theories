# Whole ethical identification: repose, attenuation and achievement

7 October 2026. AI-authored substantive counterreview. This studies **whole qualitative realization → differentiated practical significance**, keeping the first ethical identification inside the whole inquiry. It follows the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), [current warrant integration](NORMATIVE-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md), and [corrected universal target](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#universal-scope-and-substantive-conclusions). The [source assessment](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-SOURCES.md) separates the fresh comparison from inherited work.

## 1. Candidate and genealogy

The strongest candidate is not that a separately existing chooser must promote a separately specified good. An activity forms participants, demands, sensitivities, capacities and authority; its unfolding can also change or end those relations. Ethical quality, if identified, belongs to that whole realization. Ending a demand may participate in the realization's quality rather than enter only as an external permission to decline promotion.

That constitutive possibility is already explicit in [whole grounding §§29–32](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#29-achieved-form-and-the-ground-of-ethical-direction). Rest, terminal good and selfgood are also inherited. The initially proposed contrast between repose and suppression of reports while injury continues would repeat the repository's rejection of misleading proxies. It is retained only as a rejected research shortcut, not counted as an advance.

The harder question is this:

> Can whole realization identify the ethical significance of a change that actually ends burdensome striving, produces genuine contentment, and reduces some capacities, without either dismissing that contentment or presupposing the superior worth of retained capacity?

This addresses first ethical identification. It does not require every opportunity for assistance to supply a positive reason. Nor does it require a complete ranking of all histories, inevitable compliance or proof that this formulation is uniquely necessary.

## 2. One history and three actual continuations

Consider a demanding shared practice. Through its history, capacities, sensitivities, expectations, reliance and patterns of effort have formed. Participants' lives and those relations belong to the same unfolding. A demander and someone undergoing demand are locally differentiated positions within it, not metaphysically independent starting points. Either position can change. Other affected lives and actual costs remain in the description.

The continuations below are philosophical specifications, not empirical or clinical claims. No assumption is made that the listed transformations are available in a particular real case. Neither a welfare scalar nor a collective purpose is supplied.

| Continuation | Actual realization | Question left for ethical identification |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Repose with retained capacity** | The demanding episode ends. Participants cease performing or answering its demands and experience genuine repose. Some formed capacities remain, although not currently exercised. | What is good in this actual repose, and does retained capacity add ethical significance in this history? |
| **B. Contentment through attenuation** | The history actually changes the striving and some capacities through which demands were felt. Contentment genuinely occurs. There is no continuing concealed exhaustion, falsified report or stipulated ongoing injury that decides against it. The earlier formation, the transition and the actual capacity loss remain in the history. | Is this attenuation a fitting transformation, a loss, both, or neither in a specified respect? Actual contentment cannot be dismissed as merely a score, and actual capacity loss is not already an ethical verdict. |
| **C. Bounded further achievement** | Participants undertake a finite further realization, retaining some burdens and accomplishing the activity. Its actual character and costs remain. | What warrants the achievement's quality and its bearing on the burdens? Actual assent does not alone settle authority; authority is not disqualified merely because participants were formed within dependence. |

The histories differ. Calling them the same because each ends quietly would omit the very formation the theory explains. But describing more differences does not by itself determine their ethical roles.

## 3. Executed discriminations

### Mere concord does not identify quality

B is not refuted by noting that harmony can be manufactured through false reports. The specified contentment is real. A criterion favoring successful elimination of experienced conflict can regard B favorably. To reject B, the whole account must identify something ethically significant in the transition, the capacities actually lost, other affected realization or the relevant alternatives. No such adverse significance follows merely from naming B “degradation.”

This is the point at which a richer whole proposal differs from a concord proxy. The proposal must explain the qualitative content it retains, not simply insist that it considers the whole. Conversely, B's real contentment does not establish that nothing of ethical significance was lost.

### Capacity preservation does not supply the missing comparison

Compare terminal variants of A and B, removing every productive sequel from the specification while retaining the present realization and its history. Retained capacity in A is still an actual disposition, not an imaginary object. But its ethical superiority cannot now be explained by a later achievement that does not occur. Such superiority would need a defended significance of the present capacity or of its actual history.

Separate a wholly dormant retained capacity from present understanding or appreciation of the completed activity. In the second case, A has a current relational realization that B may lack despite comparable contentment. That difference cannot silently be borrowed to defend the dormant case. Nor does identifying richer present content by itself establish its ethical superiority. The [earlier actual-relations adjudication](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#adjudication-of-the-proposed-discriminator) already allows a sufficiently rich experiential account to include actual historical and intentional relations; this distinction is an application of that result, not its refutation.

This does not prove that unexercised capacities never matter. It identifies the premise needed when they are said to matter here. Nor may the earlier aspiration settle the comparison simply because it existed first. A transformation can genuinely change what is pursued and enjoyed. The [current ratification study](PERSPECTIVAL-RATIFICATION-REVIEW.md) already examines formation, release and continuing claims; neither past sovereignty nor current endorsement is imported as decisive.

The reverse shortcut also fails: B's removal of striving does not establish that its resulting life is poorer. Some attenuations may be fitting. Treating every lost capacity to pursue a purpose as an ethical deficit would reinstate the generic end-capacity superiority examined in [HUIGEN §67](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#67-capacity-for-ends-and-the-first-ethical-ground).

### Constitutive identification has a real but bounded result

In A, ending the demand partly constitutes this repose. In B, changing the formed striving partly constitutes this contentment. In C, undertaking and completing the activity partly constitutes the achievement. These are different realizations of one generative history; none need be reduced to a later instrumental payoff.

Thus constitutive explanation alone does not privilege A. All three have it. The inference from “the whole relation constitutes this lived fulfillment” to “this is the ethically fitting realization” still needs the substantive identification of the relevant quality. This is a test of a particular explanatory move, not a theorem that constitution can never ground ethics. A sufficiently defended constitutive identity could answer it.

### Nonreflective life and the self remain inside the test

The same question can concern a nonreflective affected life, without verbal endorsement as the source of its significance. A helper's own repose or transformed striving can also be the realization considered. These variants prevent a prior helping role, public uptake or future usefulness from defining good in advance. They do not establish that every self-maintaining organization has experience or identical ethical standing.

## 4. Strong substantive rivals

An actuality-sensitive rival grants the complete history, genuine contentment, finite goods, selfgood, nonreflective life and differentiated agency. It accepts that the way a life is formed can alter its actual ethical quality. But it denies that unused end-capacity, greater articulation or more purposes is intrinsically superior in every case. It can therefore allow B without concealing its history or treating endorsement as infallible.

A realization-sensitive defense of A can instead argue that actual capacities, relationships or achievements have significance not exhausted by current feeling. It must identify that significance in this history. Merely adding the adjective “higher” supplies a comparative judgment, not its explanation. C sharpens the same dispute: achievement can have quality even with burdens, but achieving an end is not itself a certificate that the end or its means is worthwhile.

These positions can agree on many ordinary judgments while differing over the first quality asserted in the hard case. Their disagreement is not an arbitrarily toggled ethical predicate. They retain the same descriptions and propose different contentful accounts of what makes this living realization matter. Neither is credited as a completed universal metaethic by its availability as a rival.

The [Whitehead comparison](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-SOURCES.md#argument-synopsis-and-exact-reading-locations) helps identify the substantive commitment in an affirmative whole-process ordering. We do not import his ordering or treat it as a proof. Whole-process language must expose, rather than conceal, the content doing that comparative work.

## 5. Adjudication

The [affirmative argument §3](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-ARGUMENT.md#3-the-affirmative-co-grounding-attempt) offers a positive warrant, not merely a label: actual aversive pressure gives way to ease in the lived relations, and a preference for relief can respond to that difference rather than create its significance. Understood as a reflective and defeasible ethical identification, this is intelligible substantive support. The absence of an assumption-free deduction does not make it no argument. But the same contrast can occur in genuine B; this warrant does not yet resolve the disputed capacity, relationship or authority difference between the continuations. The possible context-dependent change of an aspect's significance must also remain, rather than treating every instance of pleasantness as an invariant favorable summand.

The easy quiet-versus-hidden-injury case does not settle this frontier. Genuine contentment after attenuation survives that objection. A whole account must therefore allow the contentment as actual while investigating the ethical significance of how it formed, what changed and what was lost. It cannot secure the desired answer by defining all lost striving as deprivation.

The test also clarifies why constitutive explanation matters without yet completing the first grounding: it identifies A, B and C as actual qualitative realizations, including their formation, instead of treating a prior helping purpose as the ethical reference point. But all three receive a constitutive explanation. That shared achievement does not yet warrant their ethical characterization or resolve the disputed significance of retained and attenuated capacities.

The precise residual is **the justified identification of the concrete qualitative relations and their differentiated ethical significance**. For this test, that includes whether a capacity's present place in living or its loss contributes ethically, when changed striving is fulfillment rather than deprivation, and how genuine contentment bears on that assessment. One might resolve these through a rich natural identity. No split between factual reality and an external value realm is required. The present comparison has not established that identity, nor proved it impossible.

An adequate universal account may permit some comparisons to remain incomplete and justify different practical modes. It need not invariably prefer A, condemn B, demand C, require maximal openness or turn every good into assistance at every location. Its validity must nevertheless rest on more than the location's endorsement or a restatement that the preferred realization is fitting.

No new Lean theorem or empirical result is claimed. Encoding the desired A/B verdict or proving that stipulated grounds survive a representation would leave the disputed first quality supplied. This round executes and sharpens that substantive test; it does not declare universal grounding complete or relabel the inherited terminal-good insight as new.

## 6. Joint ethical identification and the exclusivity test

7 October 2026, subsequent continuation. This assesses **the whole realized relation, its ethical character, the formation of discernment, and justified correction together**. It follows [HUIGEN §§68–69](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#68-explanatory-identification-and-the-evidence-for-the-whole-root), the [Liangzhi warrant interface](LIANGZHI-NORMATIVE-WARRANT-INTERFACE.md) and the [whole-unit correction](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#ending-an-activity-and-the-quality-of-the-whole-continuation). None requires proving an isolated feeling or capacity to be the first good before the whole account can receive support. The preceding attenuation comparison is not repeated as the foundational unit.

### The positive explanatory route remains substantive

The relations through which living is fulfilled, obstructed or transformed can also form the inquiry that comes to understand them. Critical understanding can involve changes in attention, testimony, participation, practical alternatives and response. These need not be independent observations subsequently assembled by a detached evaluator. The whole account can explain their formation and their bearing on the very realization being appraised.

This provides a legitimate route to jointly supporting an ethical identity and the reliability of its recognition. An account can be tested through what it explains about an actual practice, how it distinguishes correction from manufactured assent, and what its proposed grounds require when those grounds conflict with a successful inherited arrangement. The support need not await a separate completed proof of each component's value. Conversely, defining successful correction as whatever agrees with the proposed identity would prevent this route from testing that identity.

This route is inherited, not newly proved here. The new source comparison tests a tempting strengthening: that explaining genuine correction already selects this complete philosophical explanation as indispensable.

### A rich comparator blocks that exclusivity inference

The [Kitcher reading](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-SOURCES.md#4-joint-justification-and-a-pragmatic-rival) supplies a developed alternative explanatory order: progress, goods, methods and diagnosis are assessed together, with real conflicts and a substantive normative proposal. It preserves noninstrumental concern and criticism of prevailing practice. It therefore cannot be reduced to a controller maximizing stability or to a rule that existing jurisdiction certifies itself.

The source's different explanatory order does not by itself imply that it must approve an arrangement the generative account condemns. Nor does it make later actual approval the simple condition of earlier wrongness. No such concrete ethical disagreement was established in this round. Manufacturing one by replacing its normative proposal with unquestioned local authority would not be substantive counterreview.

Accordingly, the particular exclusivity route fails to discriminate: an account of correction that both explanations can support does not select one merely because it is called whole-grounded. This is a bounded explanatory assessment, not a new mathematical non-entailment theorem or an argument against the possibility of universal ethics. The pragmatic account could itself be another articulation of broad G. Universal warrant and the unique necessity of this formulation remain different objectives.

### Distributed contact is still a live affirmative answer

Our response to the source's discovery argument is that an absence of an isolated perceptual episode does not establish absence of contact with the relations being appraised. Extended conversation can change which actual consequences are understood, expose a mistaken account of a practice's organization, and enable comparison of interpretations. Several participants can contribute to that understanding; nobody need experience a single decisive moment of ethical perception. This gives the whole account an intelligible explanation of how inquiry could be answerable to something it does not merely invent by agreeing.

The argument is a possibility defense. Identifying such contact does not establish that the ethical appraisal is correct, that the relevant history actually proceeded in this way, or that the whole identification is necessary. Equally, that remaining work does not justify dismissing distributed inquiry as incapable of recognition. The positive route and its calibration must be assessed together at their actual evidential strength.

Temporal scope matters. The [fixed-past analysis](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#when-a-fixed-past-changes-present-significance) preserves real later authority, release and changed relationships. A question about whether a response was justified at its decision point differs from current appraisal of the complete history, including reform. Critical discovery must not be simulated by silently applying a later rule as though it already governed; genuine later transformation must not be erased by declaring all ethical significance immutable. Shared past occurrence alone settles neither question.

### What this continuation does and does not change

This comparison rejects an insufficient route to explanatory exclusivity while preserving a positive joint-warrant route. It does not supply the requested first universal warrant, an ethical counterexample to the whole account, or a new obligatory sequence of component-first proofs. It also finds no reason to impose perpetual challenge, identical reasons everywhere or convergence to one total ordering. Finite completion and warranted differentiated authority remain possible.

The next central obligation is to **defend the whole relation that makes a proposed revision ethically better, including how that very ground differentiates practical significance**, against an alternative that shares the full history but gives a genuinely different assessment. Specify the actual disputed consequence before selecting another illustration. If the accounts instead share the defended ground under different vocabulary, credit that common normative result rather than pursue unnecessary exclusivity. Evidence about how intelligence forms and corrects itself should bear on this joint claim; another source endorsing naturalism, another successful ethical controller, or a restatement of the unresolved frontier will not settle it.

This is a philosophical counterreview with a fresh primary comparison. No new Lean result, empirical calibration or universal proof is claimed.

## 7. Abiotic regeneration under the new Shengsheng criterion

7 October 2026. [Ren's explicit answer](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md) identifies a good direction through whole generation of more 生, generated possibility and diversity, and complex organization capable of further development. Ren also accepts several scopes of 生 as meaningful. The [current reconstruction](SHENGSHENG-DIRECTION-RECONSTRUCTION.md) therefore supplies positive content that the earlier review must now investigate. It cannot be vetoed by this review's prior refusal to infer good from an unspecified capacity count, nor weakened into “more forms already independently certified good.”

### A comparison at the widest scope

Within one generative history, consider a region in which no biological life or valenced undergoing is identified. Compare two continuations. In A, a simple configuration recurs. In B, new organizations form and alter the conditions under which subsequent organizations form; these processes actually regenerate support for further organization, differentiate forms and enable increasingly articulated organization. The difference concerns actual relations of formation, not longer descriptions, arbitrary state labels or a single complex terminal product.

This is a philosophical specification. Physical realizability and indefinite growth are not asserted. Biological life elsewhere is not displaced, and the proposed positive contribution of B is not explained by a future service to human beings, animals or other already valued lives. The assessing intelligence remains within the encompassing unfolding. Focusing on the region's proposed contribution does not declare the two complete universes identical in every other ethically relevant respect.

The sharpest variant stipulates that the region's development never involves valenced undergoing or serves a later lived realization. This is a correspondence condition of the thought experiment, not an empirical conclusion drawn from failing to detect experience or a mathematical consequence of omitting an experience field. The disagreement below concerns that specified variant; an account attributing experience to the very generative organization would instead contest its realization.

Nor does “abiotic” settle whether proto-life or purposiveness is beginning to form. If a proposed realization crosses such a boundary, describe that formation; do not make the comparison easy by stipulating that its regenerative organization can never count as nascent life. To isolate the widest claim, the assessment asks whether the specified generative relation itself contributes positively, without deriving that contribution from a further lived good.

### Affirmative judgment and its defeasible support

Under Ren's candidate, B has a positive generative difference in the broad organizational scope. It actually realizes more articulated formation and recreates conditions for further differentiated formation. A mere inventory of unrealized outcomes would not capture that difference. This is a definite application of the new criterion; returning only “some unknown G is missing” would fail to engage the answer.

The affirmative rationale concerns the character of the unfolding itself. In B, what has come about does not only persist as a completed arrangement: it participates in bringing forth forms and relations not exhausted by its current organization. The diversity and further formation are actual articulations of the whole, rather than benefits awaiting transfer to an existing owner. This supplies content for favoring B as a fuller generative realization, even when no prior demand is satisfied and no spectator enjoys it. It is a direct, reflective ethical judgment about that realization, not a deduction that event number, formal complexity or continuation as such must always be better.

There is also an explanatory reason to consider that judgment: it allows the emergence of new loci and modes of realization to matter without requiring an antecedently valued beneficiary to authorize each one. That avoids making the actual inventory of established lives the definition of everything that could matter. This is support for the candidate's breadth, not proof that every broader account is better or that the rival must secretly accept it. The [Moore extension](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-SOURCES.md#5-value-beyond-use-by-an-already-valued-life) provides a distinct comparison concerning world-quality without human contemplation; its beauty judgment does not certify our generative judgment.

### The substantive rival and the actual disagreement

A monist lived-quality account grants the complete abiotic history: self-reorganization, diversity, actual complexification and regeneration of conditions all occur. It also explains biological organization, awareness and practical concern as differentiated developments within the same process. It proposes that ethical significance is constituted where that unfolding has a lived or valenced character—where something is actually undergone as fulfillment, adversity or another meaningful qualitative relation. On its strict version, the specified abiotic increase, absent any such relation, contributes no ethical improvement in itself, although it is a real organizational achievement.

This is a different ethical verdict on the target contribution: the generative candidate favors B's regenerative character itself; the strict lived-quality rival does not. Their shared description does not create that disagreement by a freely varied Boolean. They offer different constitutive explanations of the ethical boundary. The rival must defend why lived quality is necessary; it cannot simply declare that only familiar living beneficiaries count. The generative account must defend why the specified regenerative character itself is favorable; calling the rival narrow does not establish this.

Several nearby positions must remain distinct. A rival requiring only self-maintenance may already admit B, so it would not supply this disagreement. An account that regards this network as newly living may agree with the judgment while disputing the scope label. A strict sentience restriction and a broader organismic account also have different targets. No empirical claim deciding those boundaries is made here.

The strict rival can take newly arising lives seriously once the relevant qualitative relation forms. It need not restrict value to lives already present or to human observers. Consequently, the affirmative's openness to new realization does not defeat this stronger rival by itself. The contested point is the **abiotic generative contribution before, or without reliance on, that additional relation**.

### Result and remaining universal argument

The new input yields an actual positive judgment and a substantive disagreement to investigate. This improves on the preceding explanatory-exclusivity comparison, where the strongest accounts had no established difference in ethical verdict. The inquiry can now address whether regeneration's creation of diverse, increasingly articulated forms is itself an ethical realization, rather than first asking which independent good its results serve.

This review favors retaining B's proposed positive contribution as a defensible candidate at the broad scope. It has identified its concrete content and a reason for taking beneficiary-independent realization seriously. It has not proved the identification universally necessary. The strict lived-quality restriction is neither assumed as the default nor established false. At narrower scopes, the same history may receive no positive verdict of that particular kind; that need not contradict the broader claim. Combining scopes requires an account of their actual relationships and conflicts, not a hidden sum or a forced choice of one scope.

Likewise, the positive contribution does not yet entail that every intelligence must produce B, that any cost is justified, or that existing forms should be sacrificed for indefinite complexification. Differentiated practical force remains to be explained from this whole direction, including actual feasible continuations and competing changes. Those qualifications delimit the inference; they do not replace the criterion with previously certified goods.

No new mathematical theorem or empirical result is claimed. A future checked formalization can distinguish repeated self-regeneration from one terminal construction under explicit laws. It would not, merely by encoding the direction, prove its universal ethical warrant. The present continuation provides the positive scope judgment and the genuine counterposition on which that warrant argument can now work.

## 8. Does the formation and correction of goods confer universal authority?

7 October 2026. This reviews the [affirmative continuation §9](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-ARGUMENT.md#9-can-the-formation-and-correction-of-goods-establish-the-universal-ground), after the [continuation study](CONTINUATION-GENERATIVITY.md) and checked [regenerative-diversity construction](REGENERATIVE-DIVERSITY-MATH.md). The universal inference tested is specific: **the relationships through which purposes, goods and their criticism form might thereby have universally favorable ethical standing, including authority over competing local appraisals.** Common formative origin, an intrinsic favorable aspect, and justified adjudication are distinct claims within the same unfolding.

### The positive comparison comes first

A supported plurality can develop new relations through its activity, differentiating how subsequent organization forms and participates in further formation. This is an actual realization, not merely a stock of unused alternatives or a service awaiting an independently valued beneficiary. Ren's criterion identifies that development as a favorable direction. This review retains that positive content. Following [Ren's framing question](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-situated-nondual-framing-question.md), an opposition between a controlling position and a burdened position is not imposed as its initial structure.

Compare supported steady plurality with supported further differentiation. Both already renew their conditions and sustain actual diverse activity and competent inquiry. The second also forms further generative organization. The comparison therefore removes two explanations that would otherwise obscure the disputed contribution: additional differentiation is not rescuing an inevitably failing continuation, and criticism is not absent from the steady history. These are philosophical comparison conditions, not newly established physical possibilities.

The strongest rival grants this complete formation. A monist lived-quality account can understand new purposes, concern, criticism and their changing standards as developments within the whole. Its favorable appraisals answer the concrete character of living together, including newly arising lives and finite fulfillment. In the sharper abiotic variant, however, it denies an intrinsic favorable contribution to additional regenerative differentiation that never bears on lived realization. Its own formation through generative inquiry does not conflict with that assessment. The disagreement concerns the specified contribution; neither history has been stipulated identical to the other in every whole consequence.

This is the substantive rival retained from §7, now tested against the stronger source-and-correction argument. Its ethical restriction remains a commitment requiring defense, not a default verdict. Conversely, the generative account's refusal to restrict significance to lived realization is a contentful extension requiring defense, not a mistake exposed simply by producing the rival.

### What the new inference accomplishes

The affirmative rightly challenges a fixed inventory of existing purposes as the final tribunal for every new realization. Generative development can form what is appreciated, as well as the capacities by which appreciation is criticized. This supports investigating a whole ethical identity jointly with its discernment. It does not require a sequence of independently certified component goods.

But the rival can also explain that joint development. Mere revision establishes that an appraisal changed; warranted correction additionally concerns its adequacy to the whole realization. If the source-and-correction argument means only the former, its description includes formation of competing ethical orientations. If it means the latter, it must defend the very relationship of ethical adequacy whose authority is sought. A successful constitutive explanation could do so. Ren's new criterion supplies a definite candidate for it; the fact that the criterion and its criticism have a generative history does not by itself vindicate the identification.

The [earlier capacity-for-ends audit §67](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#67-capacity-for-ends-and-the-first-ethical-ground) already establishes the scope of the related performative-contradiction objection. This round does not rediscover it as a new universal result. The present finding is that **renewal already satisfied, competent criticism already available, and additional generative enrichment** leave the specified disagreement intact. A formed inquiry can be worthwhile without thereby establishing a favorable aspect in every further extension of its formative conditions. Legitimate completion is compatible with recognizing the conditions through which it was reached; this is not a counterexample to every proposed pro tanto value of further formation.

### A subordinate conflict tests a different inference

Now let the further organization form through a change that ends some ecological regeneration and lived practices. Retain both the new formation and the actual ending, including how each changes shared conditions. An ending is not condemned merely because something ceased, and its loss is not erased by placing all the relations under the broad organizational scope.

Even if the new abiotic formation has Ren's proposed favorable aspect, that concession does not supply an exchange rule between these changes. A rule favoring development that is nonworsening in every admitted scope could guide a different case, provided its ethical warrant and the completeness of the represented relations were defended. In this case its nonworsening antecedent is absent. Neither a hidden scalar nor automatic priority for the broadest or the lived scope is supplied by common generativity.

The absence of a completed comparison is not proof of equality, incomparability or permissibility. Universal ethical validity need not deliver one total ranking; it must warrant whichever particular reason, limit or verdict it does claim. The existing [scope-composition results](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-RESTRICTION-MATH.md) preserve specified claims under their explicit hypotheses. They do not turn contributions with different scope into an adjudicated whole comparison.

**Assessment:** the affirmative §9 preserves the positive direction and accurately limits this attempted universal inference. Formation and criticism can jointly support ethical identification, but their common origin has not discharged the proposed identification's warrant or its cross-scope practical reach. This is a limit of the tested route, not an impossibility result, a rejection of the new criterion, or a requirement for ethical grounds outside generative reality. The next substantive defense must explain why this further whole realization itself warrants favor, and how that ground differentiates a concrete practical conclusion where needed. Restating dependence or adding another renewal mechanism would not answer that question. No new mathematical proof or empirical result is claimed.
