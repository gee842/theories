# Empirical claim families and their limits

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting note for the empirical claim ledger. This is a synthesis of existing observations, accepted analyses and mathematical results, not a new source audit, data analysis or study. The factual authorities below are the tracked Git blobs at **`9a6a7795e9d8a8dd777c0973eadf2f4523615e48`**, with byte hashes recorded in §8. Concurrent working-tree additions are not promoted into accepted evidence. The newer selective-handoff study retains its own evidence and acceptance record and is not counted here.

The seven families concern different portions of the proposed derivation: six observed connections and one mathematical discriminator. They are not replications of a complete developmental chain. A source report, secondary reconstruction, synthetic counterexample and Lean theorem have distinct evidential roles. Conventional equivalence remains a legitimate result; a comparison cannot distinguish two descriptions that predict the same observations under the same interventions.

## 1. Bacterial regulation: installed feedback can relieve a larger burden

**Observed support.** The preserved Ceroni audit [C1–C2] identifies an engineered *E. coli* route from burden-responsive promoter activity through guide RNA and dCas9 to repression of a burdensome construct. Optical density, GFP production per OD and target fluorescence are separate measurements. Tested designs can improve growth and the expression-capacity reporter; a high-cost design also improves that reporter while losing growth. The complete controller is laboratory supplied before the assay, not observed forming from absent architecture.

**Comparator and discrimination.** H2 alone measures the package effect; a scrambled guide probes intended repression; weak fixed induction tests a specified static attenuation alternative across temperatures. These controls support a conditional feedback mechanism. They do not exclude ordinary resource-allocation/control explanations or establish superiority over every fixed schedule. Antibiotic/preparation differences, unresolved late-batch labels and absent replicate identities restrict some quantitative comparisons. One reporter does not identify joint native capability, an energy ledger or evolutionary retention.

**Revision condition.** If independently aligned culture histories showed that the feedback benefit disappears against a specified matched attenuation/control under the advertised conditions, narrow that performance claim. If the protected reporter rose while a prespecified required second function failed, reject the stronger joint-capability inference. Neither result would revoke the already explicit possibility of costly or harmful regulation.

## 2. Actual AI correction and handoff: evidence and access can jointly matter

**Observed support.** The original task [A1] contains 32 founder lineages and 64 fresh successor contexts. In the 24 changed-rule lineages, all twelve timely-feedback founders produced valid packets and none of the twelve delayed-feedback founders did. Timely-packet successors scored 100%, versus 75% for their paired successors without access; delayed conditions scored 75%. Adequate-rule controls declined maintenance and scored 100%. The independent read-only audit [A2] reproduces saved scoring; it adds no model responses. The payment is one declared task slot, not measured computation or money.

**Comparator and discrimination.** The unchanged-rule default scores 12/16 on challenges; conventional candidate elimination given the complete verified report scores 16/16 on every fixture. The feedback/access contrasts challenge invariant maintenance and access-insensitive response accounts in this task. They do not establish superiority over conventional inference, autonomous financing of maintenance, repeated real-world succession or newly acquired concern. Team accuracy and the rule family are supplied; shared model settings and instruction-based isolation limit generalization.

**Revision condition.** A verified response/provenance failure would revise this particular observation. A prespecified independent task/model replication with no timely-feedback/access benefit would restrict transfer beyond the original setting. A saturated conventional learner matching the result does not refute it; only a separately claimed comparative advantage would be lost.

## 3. Beneficiary-specific learning: represented outcomes are not delivered welfare

**Observed support.** The accepted primary-methods audit [B1] records Lockwood et al.'s 31 analyzed male participants choosing symbols and receiving probabilistic point feedback in self, other and no-beneficiary blocks, with learning and computational-model comparisons. The represented other was a confederate. Participants believed choices affected payment, whereas their actual payment was fixed. The repository preserves this methods assessment and a primary-publication link; it does not preserve or reanalyze this study's raw participant data here.

**Comparator and discrimination.** The same task under different represented beneficiaries is a meaningful comparator for beneficiary-framed learning. It does not independently manipulate a newly formed evaluative route at matched factual knowledge. Reported learning in an other-beneficiary condition supports the functional use of that task framing, not the origin of the beneficiary representation, actual recipient benefit, costly personal sacrifice or newly acquired moral standing.

**Revision condition.** Failure of an independent, prespecified reconstruction to recover the reported learning/model distinction would narrow its computational attribution. To strengthen the evaluative interpretation, a contrast must separate accurate recipient prediction from recipient-sensitive choice; observed equal predictive competence with different choice can discriminate those roles. Merely replacing displayed points with another proxy does not establish delivery.

## 4. Recipient cues: remote social conditions can affect local food provision

**Observed support.** The accepted preview-based reanalysis [Q1–Q3] retains all 24 displayed focal-animal pairs. Food-access pulls over seven minutes average **4/3 more** in the cooperating-donor than neutral-donor situation, with a positive mean in every single-animal omission. The remote cue donor is distinct from the nearby food recipient. The original CSV bytes were not retrieved; the independently checked transcription is of a public display. The paper's reported 23 animals versus the preview's 24 pairs remains unresolved.

**Comparator and discrimination.** Same-donor paired conditions constrain an identity-only explanation. They do not match donor activity or arousal, identify a cooperation-specific chemical signal, or distinguish cue potentiation, generalized reciprocity and richer recipient representations. Pulls measure food access, not ingestion or sustained welfare. Missing donor/recipient identities and timing limit shared-unit and carryover adjustment; exact reference calculations are not recovered assignment probabilities.

**Revision condition.** Verified original-file differences would revise the transcribed-data result. If the contrast disappeared under matched activity/state controls, narrow the cue interpretation. Transfer under independently varied cue source, beneficiary and delivery conditions could strengthen recipient tracking; neither a positive pull count nor the current fixed arrangement already identifies that stronger mechanism.

## 5. Harm-sensitive action: the apparatus links choices to a recipient, with causal alternatives retained

**Observed support.** The accepted rat reanalysis [H1–H2] retains all twelve released male contingent-harm and fourteen male no-harm actors. The difference in changes in preferred responses per offered slot is **−16.69 percentage points**, decomposed into alternative responses **+16.41** and nonperformance **+0.28**. Every single-actor and recorded-label omission preserves the negative contrast. The documented apparatus makes the preferred action shock a distinct recipient while either completed action offers one pellet to the actor. The released free-choice log does not independently verify every delivered shock or total experienced harm.

**Comparator and discrimination.** NoHarm addresses drift and selection of the baseline-preferred lever more directly than a within-harm decline alone. Separating alternatives from nonresponse shows that this descriptive change is principally action replacement, not cessation. Group allocation and cage identity are unresolved, and recorded cohort labels do not overlap; no randomized causal effect or population interval is claimed. Conventional learned aversion to recipient reactions, changed contingency understanding and actor cue relief remain viable. Equal offered food is not equal realized food, effort or subjective cost.

**Revision condition.** A validated allocation/cohort account could strengthen or weaken causal attribution. A properly matched relief-versus-cue-suppression contrast could separate target tracking from a restricted cue-relief explanation; if choices tracked only concealment despite known unchanged harm, narrow the protective interpretation. The present data do not already decide that contrast or identify the origin of aversion.

## 6. Reward training and recipient choice: an uncertain assignment contrast

**Observed support.** The accepted reconstruction [V1–V2] recovers 385 saved respondents and the authors' 333-person eligible population. The primary equal-weight training contrast in target-liked responses per 32 offered decisions is **+0.50 percentage points**, with approximate participant-level 95% interval **[−4.25,+5.24]**; all three fixed sensitivity intervals include zero. A timeout is no liked response, not a disliked choice. The target is familiar, awareness is post-task, and eligibility is post-assignment; neither main population is complete recruitment intention-to-treat.

**Comparator and discrimination.** Congruent versus incongruent prior training is compared within each fixed current private-payoff condition. This establishes an uncertain bounded behavioral contrast, not equivalence or a positive full-sample effect. The accepted identification argument [V3] constructs the same observable choice law with changed latent private-payoff expectancy and unchanged target evaluation. Raw reward histories and a coarse post-task report do not uniquely determine subjective beliefs. Stated gift/lottery incentives are not a fulfillment ledger.

**Revision condition.** A well-calibrated new-recipient contrast exceeding the bound in family 7 would reject its specified unchanged-evaluation alternatives. A precise null against a prespecified meaningful transfer effect would constrain that proposed acquisition route, not all learned concern. Neither a favorable post-treatment awareness subgroup nor an unplanned endpoint may replace the preserved primary result.

## 7. Sharp identification bound: a tested mathematical discriminator, not new human evidence

**Established support.** The accepted analytical note and review [I1–I3] give a sharp offered-choice bound under a common paired population, score-displacement budget `D` on mass at least `1−ε`, and marginal response levels `R_0,R_1`. With `m=tanh(D/4)` and `H=max(0,R_0+R_1−1−ε)`, the restricted unchanged-evaluation class implies

`−R_0+(1−m)H ≤ L_1−L_0 ≤ R_1−(1−m)H`.

The finite rational formal package passes 27 theorem audits, including general sharpness and separate rational-odds results. Real calculus and statistical inference remain separately reviewed analytical proofs. Synthetic checks are not participant observations. No inspected empirical archive supplies the necessary validated score budget and paired coverage.

**Comparator and discrimination.** The alternative explicitly bounds changes in factual expectancy, private expectancy/sensitivity, baseline influences and decision scale while evaluation stays unchanged. Marginal response levels constrain selection; equality of response means alone does not establish equal individual gates. A simultaneous sampling region beyond the defensible envelope rejects the conjunction of those restrictions, not every possible unchanged-value explanation or the ethical adequacy of an acquired preference.

**Revision condition.** A counterexample satisfying the formal premises would expose a proof/specification defect. An empirical violation after the measurement/population premises are independently supported would reject the restricted behavioral class or its real-system mapping. Failed calibration, unbounded baseline changes or selected populations instead make the proposed application unwarranted; they do not become evidence for newly acquired valuation.

## Consequence for the final claim ledger

The observed bacterial, AI and behavioral results supply different empirical contacts with regulation, correction and recipient-linked choice. The sharp bound supplies a conditional test, not an extra empirical domain. None independently identifies the complete generative progression, natural prevalence, a common numerical value scale, inevitable favorable direction or ethical warrant.

These are scope boundaries for the existing five completion conditions, not new mandatory criteria. A final account may state the accepted conditional mechanisms and the supported empirical mappings while leaving stronger acquisition or universality claims open. New empirical work is required only when an additional retained claim needs evidence absent here. Further source collection or another outcome-removal reanalysis would be warranted by a named discriminator; it is not automatically the next foundational gate. This note neither declares the whole proof complete nor reopens accepted local results merely because stronger applications remain possible.
