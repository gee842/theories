## 3. The final unresolved connection has an answer

Round three supplied a criterion relative to a domain and requirements, leaving their warranted formation in shared inquiry open. The new answer is neither “include everything” nor “let the model decide.” It traces relevance through the claim, actual supporting routes, consequences and pertinent challenges. It permits opening an investigation on weaker support than accepting its explanation, so a not-yet-established alternative can challenge the existing field without becoming accepted merely by entering it.

It differentiates three kinds of standing. Relevant access bears on credibility; affected participation bears on practical inclusion through the existing declared commitments; justified authorization bears on the scope of decision. This prevents epistemic expertise from silently becoming sovereignty, and prevents ethical inclusion from silently becoming factual infallibility. New source comparisons and a source-reported case constrain the argument; no independent empirical confirmation is claimed.

Finally it supplies task-relative closure. Scope, material challenges, justified reliance and reopening conditions can be addressed without claiming all unknown possibilities have been eliminated. This is a fallibilist answer under the previously declared commitments, not an unexplained promise that future correction will somehow occur.

## 4. Adversarial review of the complete proposal

**The strongest objection: formation still does not by itself justify.** Accepted as a distinction within the same process. The account discloses epistemic commitments and G0/J1–J4 rather than pretending a dynamical description entails all authority. The affirmative result is an articulated relationship among formation, warrant, recognition and response. If the demand is a derivation with no substantive epistemic or ethical commitments, this proposal has not met it. The accepted G0 route does not require that stronger achievement before delivery.

**The relevance criterion could be used to exclude its own critics.** The proposal requires a challenge to the filter itself to be assessable; a fully developed accepted explanation is not the admission threshold. Nevertheless the institution can fail, and the philosophy has supplied no guarantee that those controlling it will comply. A real application must show how access and reassessment occur. Merely naming a procedure fair would fail E.

**A procedural account may make knowledge depend on politics.** Particular truths and some local knowledge can occur in unjust institutions. The argument about fair standing does not redefine them as false. It assesses claims of warranted shared inquiry and whole practical judgment, where exclusion can defeat support or wrong people. Truth, reasonable reliance, possession and ethical legitimacy remain distinct.

**Existing theories already explain much of this.** Correct. Competence epistemology, social epistemology and conventional decision theory are substantive comparators. Their overlap with the proposed relationships supports intelligibility and convergence; it does not prove exclusive novelty. A rival may explain knowledge and adopt a different ethical account. We must then assess its ethical grounds separately rather than dismiss its epistemology because it rejects G0 or J2.

**The entire philosophical account cannot be finished while consciousness and induction remain open.** The research does not claim an exhaustive solution to philosophy. It delivers a complete articulation of this candidate across the questions pursued, with those limits made visible. If “finish line” means a universally demonstrated or exhaustive foundation, it has not been reached. That stronger objective remains honestly unfinished; its name is not reassigned to the synthesis.

**A whole perspective can conceal actual loss.** The helping example and practical comparison retain the helper's altered life, unique opportunities and burdens even where both participants benefit overall. Non-self changes the explanation of how lives and purposes form; it does not cancel differentiated experience or permission requirements. Any application that erases those differences contradicts the account's method.
