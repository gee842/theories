## Precise hypotheses and correspondence

### Finite inquiry and concrete correction

For arbitrary types of represented state, query and action, an `Interface` supplies partial probe/response operations and an achieved-condition Boolean. Candidate states and plans are finite lists. A plan contains one query and one response for each Boolean answer. `run` obtains the actual answer before choosing the response. `choose` returns the first plan that succeeds on every member of a nonempty candidate list.

`chosen_sound` and `actual_covered_execution` establish success for a covered actual state. `chosen_exists_iff` and `no_listed_policy_iff` characterize existence relative to the list; `repertoire_complete` proves enumeration contains every query/response combination over the declared lists. They are standard finite robust-planning results. There is no least-cost or maximum-reward theorem: list order is a disclosed preference, and all represented response costs already participate in feasibility.

`protocol_exact` gives sufficient current signing/epoch, actual-route, three-work-unit and three-step conditions for issue/delivery/enactment, accounting for depletion and completion. Querying the unknown route adds a unit and step. The unknown-route example therefore needs four units/steps; statewise three-unit paths do not give a common policy. Shutdown changes `running` to false, replacement increments generation and update increments version. Authentication does not establish institutional legitimacy or cryptographic robustness. Version/generation changes are abstract enactment semantics; no arbitrary software update is certified correct.

The concrete command model holds route and authority constant during a normal run. Separate stage checks reject revocation before execution. Arbitrary asynchronous races, physical faults and harmful shutdown side effects need richer laws and G0/J application. The successor transaction assumes the predecessor remains available while installation completes; a continuously timed overlap mechanism is not derived. These are omissions in this representation, not dualist separations or refutations of the fuller theory.

The construction imports the actual `MarketGenerativity` law and its `rectangle_all_times` proof. `withMigration` changes the fee to `fee + migration`; all other changes are explicit in the worked financing example. `Carrier` adds identity, part stock, current command/authority, outstanding claim identifiers, receipts, route and provided/consumed work. `step` checks the transferred payload, calls the actual `EffectiveCommand` implementation and appends a receipt only after successful command completion.

`every_generation_executes` and `competitive_lineage_executes` use the stronger aggregate certificate, initial safe region, bounded disturbances, a positive initial part stock, one part replenished after each cycle, continuing command and current authentication. A changing ranked candidate list must contain a faithful candidate at each generation. The fixed admission rule then rejects earlier corrupt candidates. It is a preserved institution, not a derived motivation to adopt or maintain that institution against every incentive.

`lineage_projects_to_market` is an exact step/history refinement. The carrier is the corrective installation within the market arrangement. It does not give a microscopic reproduction explanation of the aggregate practice share. The mapping from paid fee to four units of command work is an explicit service-conversion law; money, work and replacement parts are not collapsed. Cumulative work provided and consumed both increase by four per generation. Payload equality preserves records and claim identifiers; ethical scope and the truth of those records require their original warrant, and carrying an outstanding claim is not discharging it.

The fixed standing non-shutdown command and credential explain the all-generation continuation theorem's scope. A separately checked shutdown completes and stops running. A more general stream of newly authorized commands could be connected by additional authority transitions; the present theorem does not freeze legitimate authority forever by normative fiat. Finite replacement supplies, unmodeled capture of admission or unavailable faithful candidates can prevent continuing execution.

## Substantive author counterreview

**“The planner knows the worlds.”** It is supplied a finite uncertainty family and transition models; it does not know which candidate is actual. A policy uses acquired observations. Coverage and model fidelity remain assumptions, and an omitted-harm counterexample demonstrates their consequence. This closes bounded plan selection, not arbitrary discovery.

**“Success was defined to be ethical.”** The general planner proves satisfaction of a specified interface. The reform realization separately names actual access/maintenance and learning operation, tied to inherited G0/J application. The code does not prove that these are the only relevant basic conditions. The command realization proves effective command execution, not that every authenticated command is good. Ethical justification remains in the explicit argument and correspondence.

**“You smuggled unlimited support into succession.”** The continuing theorem openly requires recurring parts and the stronger fee/levy/capacity inequalities. Its concrete state records supplied and consumed work. The old correction-only certificate and missing-part examples fail. Arbitrary finite indices under perpetual provision are not a claim of free actual infinity.

**“The selector just forces fidelity.”** Yes: the institution admits faithful proposals and must continue doing so. This is a sufficient implementation condition under arbitrary ranking of available proposals, not a proof that unregulated competition selects fidelity. Aggregate capture and disturbance bounds are inherited; capture of the admission machinery lies outside this model.

**“Preserving records freezes all values.”** The theorem preserves a current payload through transfer, not a universal prohibition on revision. The earlier goal-revision results explain warranted changes; this transfer model requires an explicitly authorized revision step before interpreting changed content as legitimate. A current-epoch check can reject stale authority. No AI is given a veto on authorized shutdown.

**“This solves safety during every instant of migration.”** The four command steps represent time and expenditure; the enclosing replacement transaction assumes availability of the predecessor until completion. Continuous overlap, new arrivals during a transaction, failures during memory copying and cryptographic adversaries are not proved. The paper states this granularity boundary.

**“A paper with many checked lemmas is a frontier contribution.”** The theorem count is a validation inventory. Finite exhaustive planning, control, refinement and preservation induction are conventional. General novelty and superior empirical performance remain unestablished. The four requested theoretical connections can be completed without making that stronger claim.
