## 1. What this comparison can establish

An account of scientific practice must distinguish a method's reproduction, the content of its retained evidence, and its reliability on independently specified questions. The [common premise ledger](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md) already requires this separation. Published metascience can identify parts of that distinction; it cannot establish that every scientific philosophy must accept our complete ontology or that the generative framework is uniquely minimal.

The claim tested by the sources is modest: **what gets selected, recorded and transmitted can diverge from what supports accurate inference**. The proposed generative contribution concerns an explicit connection among these mechanisms and their material feasibility. Selection theory, statistics and conventional control remain substantive competing or equivalent explanations.

## 2. Inspected primary sources

### E1. Method reproduction can favor unreliable methods in a declared model

Smaldino and McElreath (2016), [*The natural selection of bad science*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5043322/), DOI [10.1098/rsos.160384](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.160384). Their population model lets publication success affect inheritance of research methods. It produces methodological deterioration without deliberate fraud; replication does not by itself prevent that result in the modeled regimes. This supplies close prior art for selection over selectors and for the separation of reproduction from epistemic reliability. It does not show that all academic systems obey that law, or that replication never helps. **Access:** indexed primary abstract, introduction and section 4 excerpts inspected on 6 October 2026; the direct PMC page returned a browser challenge. No code rerun or complete-paper audit was performed here.

### E2. A simulation's own correction needs an independent check

[*Replication of the natural selection of bad science*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9943874/) reports an independent reproduction with a coding discrepancy affecting the transient evolution of replication rates; the authors report that the original central conclusion survives. This is a computational replication, not new observations of actual laboratories. Its relevance is procedural: even an explanatory model of scientific error can contain an implementation error. **Access:** primary abstract and introduction available in indexed results, inspected on 6 October 2026; direct access returned a browser challenge. We did not inspect or execute the reproduction code. This qualifies reliance on E1 rather than treating a second simulation as independent real-world confirmation.

### E3. The publication filter is associated with a different evidence distribution

Scheel, Schijen and Lakens (2021), [*An Excess of Positive Results*](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/an-excess-of-positive-results-comparing-the-standard-psychology-l-2/), DOI [10.1177/25152459211007467](https://doi.org/10.1177/25152459211007467). Their comparison included 71 Registered Reports and 152 standard psychology reports. For each article's first hypothesis, positive results occurred in 44% and 96%, respectively. This is a comparison of publication formats, not randomized assignment of otherwise identical studies. It is consistent with changed publication selection, while differences in questions, investigators or designs limit causal attribution. Positivity is not a direct measure of truth. **Access:** complete university-hosted primary abstract and bibliographic record inspected on 6 October 2026; the linked full PDF returned HTTP 403. No data reanalysis or full-text methods audit is claimed.

### E4. Published status and replicated evidence are different measurements

Open Science Collaboration (2015), [*Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26315443/), DOI [10.1126/science.aac4716](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aac4716). The primary abstract reports replications of 100 studies from three psychology journals: 97% of originals and 36% of replications had statistically significant results, with other reported replication criteria producing different estimates. These are not estimates that 64% of all psychology, or 64% of the selected propositions, is false. They identify a difference between originally published evidence and new measurements under replication designs. **Access:** primary abstract inspected on 6 October 2026. No individual-study audit, coverage claim for all science, or adjudication of subsequent methodological disputes is made.

## 4. A proposed discriminating test

**Status: design only; not run, preregistered or empirically validated.** Reuse the existing recipient-evidence and record interfaces rather than begin another disconnected resource game. The unresolved premise is whether a process can obtain and retain evidence about its observation channel, instead of receiving correct calibration from the analyst.

### Question and construction

Give an implemented inquirer two initially possible environments. In one, a binary target is unchanged and the sensor polarity is unchanged. In the other, the target and sensor polarity both reverse. Ordinary reports are identical in the two cases, but the correct target judgment differs. Initial memory, inherited priors, timing, resource reports, silence and all accessible side channels must also have identical distributions. A method choosing ordinary tests adaptively cannot distinguish the environments when every permitted ordinary action preserves that equivalence.

Add a **paid reference probe** whose source and correspondence are identified independently and whose outcomes differ between those cases. Its calibration is a new explicit premise; this test does not derive trustworthy reference standards from nothing. Store the probe's target, time/context, recipe identity and result using the existing paid-record protocol. Charge acquisition, writing, storage where relevant and successor decoding from the actual history.

Compare four available mechanisms: ordinary evidence only; faithful contextual probe record; the same probe with context removed or selectively retained; and a conventional inference/controller implementation with exactly matched observations, prior, interventions and expenditure. Retain a no-inquiry policy and, where useful, an explicitly privileged known-channel comparator. The latter is an upper benchmark, not evidence of what an uninformed inquirer can achieve.

**A distinct context contrast is required.** In the two stable worlds alone, deleting metadata that carries no additional information need not hurt. For the succession comparison, add two possible probe histories ending in the same current context: one record was calibrated in that context, while the other has the same recorded result from a different context whose sensor correspondence is not licensed for current use. Keep all other successor-accessible information equal after the stipulated transfer. The faithful record distinguishes warranted reuse from a need for a new probe or suspension; the result-only record does not. If actual polarity is tested, specify the cross-context changes independently and retain all ordinary target/sensor ambiguities. Score false certification and coverage separately: correct suspension need not deliver more immediate correct answers. Include unchanged-context metadata deletion as a control where no performance loss is predicted. This addition came from independent internal review; it corrects a design that otherwise would not identify context's role.

### Measures and prospective predictions

1. Declare the target, balanced two-world evaluation and action/abstention scoring before outcomes. Report error among issued answers and coverage separately; universal abstention is not successful discrimination. Also report paid failures and exhaustion.
2. With a forced binary answer and no distinguishing channel, average error over the balanced worlds cannot be below one half. A prior concentrated on one world changes that average bound and must be reported; lucky accuracy in the realized world is not uniform reliability.
3. An informative reference probe can lower the achievable error only if its information is accessible to the judgment mechanism. Observing it and deleting all informative consequences removes that advantage. A sufficient summary can preserve it without storing every raw detail.
4. A sensor-polarity certificate is not automatically a certificate of target stability. Successor reuse must preserve the proposition and conditions actually warranted. Context changes may justify suspension while leaving the historical record true.
5. More finance can make an informative probe executable; funding an uninformative probe cannot create discrimination. Different costs or feedback access cannot be hidden in a comparison of methods.
6. If the conventional comparator implements the same kernel and evidence contract, it should match. A better result requires an identified substantive difference; notation alone predicts no advantage.

The independently evaluated target is a measure of accuracy, not an assumed internal reward. Any actor-internal reason to purchase probes, protect inconvenient records or transmit them must be separately implemented and explained. Where private returns are equal, private selection cannot be invoked as the explanation for why faithful evidence persists.

### What would revise the account

Reliable discrimination under genuinely identical complete evidence laws would contradict the stated operational model or its theorem. An omitted cue, informative initialization or privileged diagnostic action instead revises the completeness identification. A failed contextual record may expose a mismatch in decoding or a missing context assumption. A successful alternative representation challenges a uniqueness claim only if the foundation asserts one; it need not violate the common information constraint.

If the remaining reference/calibration premise is repeatedly installed by the analyst, this study has not explained the origin of epistemic reliability. The next task would remain formation and checking of the channel, with declared independent resources and finite regress stopping conditions. None of these tests alone settles realism, induction, moral standing or the full unified foundation.
