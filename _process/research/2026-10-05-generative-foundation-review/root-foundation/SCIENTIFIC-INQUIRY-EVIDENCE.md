6 October 2026. ## 1. What this comparison can establish

An account of scientific practice must distinguish a method's reproduction, the content of its retained evidence, and its reliability on independently specified questions. The [common premise ledger](../../../../theories/becoming/history/FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md) already requires this separation. Published metascience can identify parts of that distinction; it cannot establish that every scientific philosophy must accept our complete ontology or that the generative framework is uniquely minimal.

The claim tested by the sources is modest: **what gets selected, recorded and transmitted can diverge from what supports accurate inference**. The proposed generative contribution concerns an explicit connection among these mechanisms and their material feasibility. Selection theory, statistics and conventional control remain substantive competing or equivalent explanations.

## 2. Inspected primary sources

### E1. Method reproduction can favor unreliable methods in a declared model

Smaldino and McElreath (2016), [*The natural selection of bad science*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5043322/), DOI [10.1098/rsos.160384](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.160384). ### E2. A simulation's own correction needs an independent check

[*Replication of the natural selection of bad science*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9943874/) reports an independent reproduction with a coding discrepancy affecting the transient evolution of replication rates; the authors report that the original central conclusion survives. This is a computational replication, not new observations of actual laboratories. Its relevance is procedural: even an explanatory model of scientific error can contain an implementation error. **Access:** primary abstract and introduction available in indexed results, inspected on 6 October 2026; direct access returned a browser challenge. ### E3. The publication filter is associated with a different evidence distribution

Scheel, Schijen and Lakens (2021), [*An Excess of Positive Results*](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/an-excess-of-positive-results-comparing-the-standard-psychology-l-2/), DOI [10.1177/25152459211007467](https://doi.org/10.1177/25152459211007467). Their comparison included 71 Registered Reports and 152 standard psychology reports. For each article's first hypothesis, positive results occurred in 44% and 96%, respectively. This is a comparison of publication formats, not randomized assignment of otherwise identical studies. It is consistent with changed publication selection, while differences in questions, investigators or designs limit causal attribution. Positivity is not a direct measure of truth. **Access:** complete university-hosted primary abstract and bibliographic record inspected on 6 October 2026; the linked full PDF returned HTTP 403. No data reanalysis or full-text methods audit is claimed.

### E4. Published status and replicated evidence are different measurements

Open Science Collaboration (2015), [*Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26315443/), DOI [10.1126/science.aac4716](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aac4716). The primary abstract reports replications of 100 studies from three psychology journals: 97% of originals and 36% of replications had statistically significant results, with other reported replication criteria producing different estimates. These are not estimates that 64% of all psychology, or 64% of the selected propositions, is false. They identify a difference between originally published evidence and new measurements under replication designs. **Access:** primary abstract inspected on 6 October 2026. ## 4. A proposed discriminating test

**Status: design only; not run, preregistered or empirically validated.** Reuse the existing recipient-evidence and record interfaces rather than begin another disconnected resource game. The unresolved premise is whether a process can obtain and retain evidence about its observation channel, instead of receiving correct calibration from the analyst.

### Question and construction

Give an implemented inquirer two initially possible environments. In one, a binary target is unchanged and the sensor polarity is unchanged. In the other, the target and sensor polarity both reverse. Ordinary reports are identical in the two cases, but the correct target judgment differs. Initial memory, inherited priors, timing, resource reports, silence and all accessible side channels must also have identical distributions. A method choosing ordinary tests adaptively cannot distinguish the environments when every permitted ordinary action preserves that equivalence.

Add a **paid reference probe** whose source and correspondence are identified independently and whose outcomes differ between those cases. Its calibration is a new explicit premise; this test does not derive trustworthy reference standards from nothing. Store the probe's target, time/context, recipe identity and result using the existing paid-record protocol. Charge acquisition, writing, storage where relevant and successor decoding from the actual history.

Compare four available mechanisms: ordinary evidence only; faithful contextual probe record; the same probe with context removed or selectively retained; and a conventional inference/controller implementation with exactly matched observations, prior, interventions and expenditure. Retain a no-inquiry policy and, where useful, an explicitly privileged known-channel comparator. The latter is an upper benchmark, not evidence of what an uninformed inquirer can achieve.

**A distinct context contrast is required.** In the two stable worlds alone, deleting metadata that carries no additional information need not hurt. For the succession comparison, add two possible probe histories ending in the same current context: one record was calibrated in that context, while the other has the same recorded result from a different context whose sensor correspondence is not licensed for current use. Keep all other successor-accessible information equal after the stipulated transfer. The faithful record distinguishes warranted reuse from a need for a new probe or suspension; the result-only record does not. If actual polarity is tested, specify the cross-context changes independently and retain all ordinary target/sensor ambiguities. Score false certification and coverage separately: correct suspension need not deliver more immediate correct answers. Include unchanged-context metadata deletion as a control where no performance loss is predicted. This addition came from independent internal review; it corrects a design that otherwise would not identify context's role.

### Measures and prospective predictions

1. Declare the target, balanced two-world evaluation and action/abstention scoring before outcomes. Report error among issued answers and coverage separately; universal abstention is not successful discrimination. Also report paid failures and exhaustion.
2. With a forced binary answer and no distinguishing channel, average error over the balanced worlds cannot be below one half. A prior concentrated on one world changes that average bound and must be reported; lucky accuracy in the realized world is not uniform reliability.
3. An informative reference probe can lower the achievable error only if its information is accessible to the judgment mechanism. Observing it and deleting all informative consequences removes that advantage. A sufficient summary can preserve it without storing every raw detail.
4. A sensor-polarity certificate is not automatically a certificate of target stability. Successor reuse must preserve the proposition and conditions actually warranted. Context changes may justify suspension while leaving the historical record true.
5. More finance can make an informative probe executable; funding an uninformative probe cannot create discrimination. Different costs or feedback access cannot be hidden in a comparison of methods.
6. If the conventional comparator implements the same kernel and evidence contract, it should match. A better result requires an identified substantive difference; notation alone predicts no advantage.

The independently evaluated target is a measure of accuracy, not an assumed internal reward. Any actor-internal reason to purchase probes, protect inconvenient records or transmit them must be separately implemented and explained. Where private returns are equal, private selection cannot be invoked as the explanation for why faithful evidence persists.

### What would revise the account

Reliable discrimination under genuinely identical complete evidence laws would contradict the stated operational model or its theorem. An omitted cue, informative initialization or privileged diagnostic action instead revises the completeness identification. A failed contextual record may expose a mismatch in decoding or a missing context assumption. A successful alternative representation challenges a uniqueness claim only if the foundation asserts one; it need not violate the common information constraint.

If the remaining reference/calibration premise is repeatedly installed by the analyst, this study has not explained the origin of epistemic reliability. The next task would remain formation and checking of the channel, with declared independent resources and finite regress stopping conditions. None of these tests alone settles realism, induction, moral standing or the full unified foundation.

### 6.1. What the comparison changes

**Our interpretive conclusion:** an adequate warrant can concern restrictions sufficient for a declared inference without reconstructing every internal detail of a device. This corrects an overly demanding bridge from the formal model to scientific practice. It does not show that the world has no further structure, that an unrestricted black box certifies itself, or that the cited methods eliminate every assumption. The evidence is compatible with the relational account and with conventional metrology and statistical explanations; it does not uniquely select our theory.

The relation to the shared spine is methodological, not a newly proved instance theorem. In particular, no correspondence from these photon experiments to `Flow`, its natural-number levels, or the repository's separate contextuality construction has been established here. The sources assess how an application obtains its conditions; they are not experimental confirmations of P92's quantitative bound or P94's residual theorem.

### 6.2. Three different burdens

| Declared achievement | Conditions whose warrant is at issue | What this achievement alone need not supply | What would defeat the application |
|---|---|---|---|
| A measured value adequate to a stated tolerance | Meaning of the measured quantity, applicable reference/calibration relations and uncertainty | A full microscopic reconstruction of the instrument | Evidence that the reference, calibration range or uncertainty budget does not cover its use |
| Rejection of a specified statistical family at a declared error level | Admissible law family, record construction, selection/termination rules and relevant physical restrictions | Identification of every internal mechanism compatible with those restrictions | An admissible explanation excluded without warrant, or record selection inconsistent with the test |
| Explanation of how a distinction changes a response | Operative paths, initial records and response conditions sufficient for the stated comparison | The complete historical genealogy of every component | A missing operative input or change that makes the proposed mechanism explanation inadequate |

This table is an author-supplied comparison of explanatory obligations, not a theorem that these categories exhaust science or have one minimal common assumption set. Unknown details may be left open only within the scope for which the inference remains justified. Warrant may be distributed across construction, calibration, theory and cross-checks; it is not required to be separately rediscovered by each scientist.

### 6.3. Counterreview and remaining test

The strongest objection is that the examples already rely on substantial physical and statistical knowledge. That objection succeeds against a premise-free empirical-certification claim. It does not turn such knowledge into exhaustive device knowledge or make it allegiance to our complete theory. Conversely, calling an assumption “task-relative” does not justify it: the evidence must support the particular restriction, and a defect can change the scope or force suspension.

The revised foundation question is therefore whether a **specified core commitment** survives adequate alternative explanations of the **same achievement**, including legitimate weakenings of its assumptions. If a rival preserves the operative content under a different representation, it challenges representational exclusivity rather than that content. If it actually dispenses with the disputed relation while explaining the same achievement, it challenges the proposed necessity. The distinction has to be decided in the case, not by renaming every successful method “generative.”

### E8. A physical protocol whose setting relation matters

### 7.1. An independently stated continuation task

For an apparatus offered as responding to a setting during an ongoing preparation, an appropriate task is to account for **which admitted setting commands can still be delivered and executed at the designated stage, and which subsequent readings are licensed**. Timing window, preparation class, command availability and tolerable error must be stated. This content is specified by the claimed experimental operation; it does not mention our core or require a particular graph notation.

E8 makes this a scientifically relevant question. It does not establish that its actual operation equals the repository's exact `Choosing.late` graph, that all possible command policies were tested, or that a specimen can be duplicated at an identical hidden state. Those would be additional claims. The source's particular protocol must not be enlarged to arbitrary adaptive control.

Three comparisons keep the question honest:

| Account under comparison | What it retains | Assessment for the declared task |
|---|---|---|
| A list of successful completed setting/readout sequences | That each listed sequence occurred or was possible somewhere | Does not by itself specify which settings remain executable at the particular reached stage; the existing native branching witness isolates this loss |
| A response account with command timing, preparation range and measured failures | How subsequent commands enter activity and which responses are supported | Addresses the task, subject to its actual calibration and coverage; graph branching alone does not establish this |
| A history-based account retaining relevant reached conditions, inputs and failures | The continuation information in another representation | Must be accepted if it answers the same warranted questions adequately; “history-based” does not mean it discards branching content |

### 7.2. A discriminating protocol, still unrun here

The prospective comparison fixes a preparation procedure and a stage before the selected command acts. It varies the subsequent command within the declared window, and records delivery, actuation, rejection, interruption and outcome. The demand is stage-relative response, not merely the presence of both successes in a pooled archive. Failed or refused commands stay in the denominator appropriate to the declared claim; outcome statistics and command-execution performance remain separate.

The adverse control is an apparatus or account that becomes restricted to one setting during preparation. To test that restriction, command assignment must not simply track which setting has already been selected. Relevant correlation, communication and selection assumptions need evidence. Otherwise a preselected implementation can appear responsive in the collected records. No universal statistical bound or claim of unrestricted independence is introduced here.

A third comparator uses complete command-conditioned histories with enough context to retain the same continuation information. If it succeeds, that is evidence against requiring our particular representational format, not evidence that operative relations have disappeared. Repeated preparations warrant only the preparation class actually supported; they do not copy an unknown individual state or execute incompatible measurements simultaneously on one specimen.

### 7.3. Result and limits of the source comparison

**Our assessment:** the independently meaningful content is the capacity to continue under the specified commands and conditions. The sources motivate and constrain that content, while the existing proofs explain why erasing common-stage alternatives can lose it. A flat archive's omission is not a substantive counterexample to the full relational account. Equally, the mere scientific relevance of one continuation question does not warrant every finite modal question, establish image-finiteness, or recover a universal bisimulation from finite observations.

This round therefore supports **task-relative preservation of continuation content** and rejects a blanket requirement to use branching notation. It leaves open the stronger inference from actual experiments to the complete modal scope needed by the structural theorem. There is no new experimental validation, new theorem or independent review; the earlier plans in §4 and this section remain unrun.

### E11. Intervention claims require a warranted comparison

Section 2.1 distinguishes observational associations from changes under intervention. Sections 3.2–3.2.1 make the invariance of the other structural functions part of the intervention model. The noncompliance example in §§3.5.1–3.5.2 distinguishes randomized assignment, treatment actually received and outcome, while stating exclusion and independence assumptions. **Access:** author-hosted primary PDF, printed pp. 99, 107–108 and 122–123, text inspected on 13 October 2026; no complete proof audit, raw-data analysis or medical recommendation. No external source bytes imported.

**Our application, not the paper's theorem:** command assignment, receipt, actuation and subsequent output need separate experimental meanings. An intervention modeled as changing a supplied source while retaining the rest's response rule requires evidence that the actual manipulation meets that description. We credit this established causal-inference distinction; it is not an original philosophical discovery or proof of our full core.

The earlier E8 timing report gives a concrete setting-operation context, but P104 has not mapped its physical timestamps, uncertainties, interface or all actual alternatives into the new discrete theorem. The new formal delayed witness is not that experiment. The existing `TimedFeedback` bounds also retain their separate source-age/drift interpretation; formal ordering is not a measured duration.

### 8.1. What the next comparison must retain

For the prospective apparatus comparison, retain preparation context; command assignment and its timing; successful and failed delivery; actual actuation; the readout deadline; and every eligible outcome, including refusal and interruption. Specify whether the claimed performance concerns assigned commands, delivered commands or completed actions. These are different denominators. Correlation between an earlier preparation and the later command must be included in the comparison, rather than erased by pairing already-different records as if they shared an initial condition.

The current theorem can serve as an application constraint once these correspondences are warranted. It cannot infer the complete interface, command source, matched initial conditions or a counterfactual law from a pooled success table. A failed mapping should narrow or change the application, not count as a refutation of the fuller relation merely because an incomplete representation omits it.

## 9. Demarcation without confusing science with success

### E13. Historical claims need not reproduce their past event

### E14. Replication outcomes do not directly classify scientific standing

Our proposed assessment asks how a claim is constrained and corrected through its actual evidential relations. The probabilistic-forecast and insulated-institution cases in §17.3 are author-supplied conceptual tests, not experiments reported by these sources. The suggested matched comparison in §17.4 is a next test, not a completed validation.
