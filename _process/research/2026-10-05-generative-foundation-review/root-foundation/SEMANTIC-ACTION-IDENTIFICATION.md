## The ambiguity actually found

The recipient consumer's nonroot nodes refer to command zero and command one for the same recipient. The parallel protection process's two safeguard bits refer to two patients. Their shared small-mask representation is insufficient to compose their meanings. Mapping the first nonroot bit to patient p and the second to patient q changes the apparent recipient when evidence changes the recommended command. Reversing that fixed permutation merely exchanges the two failures.

The independent reconstruction confirms fourteen primary variable histories: root-only mass 5/8 and each command route mass 3/16. All nineteen source hashes in that saved result matched during the preliminary inspection; the accepted finite audit instead reads preserved exact bytes. These are probabilities under the parent's supplied controlled preparation and subsequent finite law. They are not natural frequencies, newly collected observations, or independent replications of the parent study.

For a fixed intended recipient p, the naive route-to-patient mapping sends command-one advice to q on mass 3/16. This is half the histories supplying a command, while the 5/8 unresolved/root-only histories remain in the denominator. A many-to-one bearer map can retain p across both commands, provided it retains the command distinction separately. The counterexample therefore defeats the naive bijection; it does not prove that every adapter is impossible or that the existing producers malfunction within their enclosing setup.

## What the finite comparison must distinguish

The independently reconstructed comparator treats admissible transport as a relation between a source proposition and a destination request. It does not treat a display label, an artifact hash or the occurrence of a true bit as semantic authorization. The source's enclosing binding identifies the recipient, controller, supplied objective/root G, receipt law, proposition kind, role and context generation. The finite registry and admitted role transformations are supplied premises of this interface.

The source proposition can remain supported when a request concerns another recipient, actor or context. Such a request fails transfer rather than retrospectively making the original evidence false. Similarly, loss of enforcement changes that route's permission, while a permitted voluntary route may remain. Delivery and resource shortages affect operational prerequisites; they do not themselves defeat the factual command advice. The source endpoint supplies no next source packet, so the adapter cannot execute another recipient action.

The check must also be positively discriminating: both commands, ordinary same-role use, a same-role successor and a separately admitted role transformation must pass when their stated premises hold. Refusing every request would conceal the composition problem rather than resolve it. A conventional relational/typed dispatcher with identical information is a competent comparator and is expected to agree exactly.

Consistent source corruption is a separate limitation. Authenticity can show that these bytes are the bytes an observed source produced. Their interpretation still relies on that source's integrity, assay calibration and fixed meaning. A faithfully copied but miscalibrated command tag can pass byte and scope checks. The checker must not recover hidden true orientation to repair its runtime output; truth is available only to the outer assessment where the source evidence permits it.

An instrumental receipt claim is also weaker than a harm-prevention claim. Without a separately supplied causal relation between the receipt and the relevant patient's harm, the latter conclusion does not follow. Permission under an implemented policy is likewise distinct from justified political or moral authority. Neither missing implication is supplied by changing a token's proposition-kind field.

## Established conventional comparators

Bauer, Schneider and Felten's implemented proof-carrying authorization system separates authentication from access decisions. The client constructs a proof of the server's challenge; the server checks both the proof and the challenged proposition. Their session identifiers bind cached proofs to a particular session. This is a direct conventional comparator for request-specific proof checking and contextual reuse. Their supplied access policies do not establish the moral rightness of those policies. [Primary USENIX Security 2002 paper](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/events/sec02/full_papers/bauer/bauer_html/pcaproto.html).

Birgisson and colleagues' macaroons use cryptographic credentials with contextual caveats that constrain delegated access. Their 2014 paper includes a prototype and an authorization-logic account. It establishes prior technical machinery for limiting where, when and for what purpose authorization applies; our finite semantic adapter should not claim that contextual scope checking is novel. Its relation to justified concern and physically paid action is the research question here. [Primary NDSS 2014 publication](https://research.google/pubs/macaroons-cookies-with-contextual-caveats-for-decentralized-authorization-in-the-cloud/).

These sources were inspected as primary comparative evidence. No external implementation was benchmarked, and this package does not implement cryptographic authentication or a security protocol. A Python registry and source hashes supply a finite trusted provenance model only. No paper's implementation results are being counted as observations of Ren's proposed mechanism.

## What independent empirical identification would require

The existing [reasons-to-protection evidence note](../../2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/formal/REASON-PROTECTION-EVIDENCE.md) distinguishes belief, effort and another person's delivered outcome. The [saved AI-handoff audit](../../2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/evidence/report.md) identifies an instructed correction/transmission task without independently measuring patient welfare or authority. Neither dataset can retrospectively identify the present complete bridge.

A discriminating implementation needs independently checked recipient-to-sensor and recipient-to-actuator bindings. Cross recipient identity, command encoding and context generation while preserving the actual evidence; record scoped recommendation, permitted request, attempted actuation and recipient outcome separately. A same-label context reopening should test freshness, not be silently treated as unchanged circumstances. Cut only enforcement in another arm while retaining available voluntary means. Defeat the factual ground in a separate arm with delivery intact. The crossed effects distinguish semantic application from operational obstruction. A competent conventional model carrying those same variables may give the same predictions.

The observer must check which recipient actually receives an effect; reading back the request's own patient label is circular. Likewise, a signed metadata field proves no physical sensor/actuator calibration. Calibration errors, hidden alternative routes and unintended effects belong in the outcome record. For competing claims, preserve the separate claims and the infeasibility of their joint satisfaction; do not convert insufficient means into evidence that one claimant has no reason.

These requirements identify what the next physical and empirical connection must provide. They are not an unrun trial reported as an achieved result, and the present synthetic check does not derive the supplied positive ground G or a universal direction toward justified concern.
