## 2. The checked universal inference

For arbitrary positions, let B identify independently warranted instances, E identify justified unary implications at the preserved context, and D be the independently specified target domain. `Reached B E` is finite derivability from B by E. The [checked theorem](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/WholeGroundCoverage.lean), `universal_target_coverage_iff`, establishes:

\[
\bigl(\forall p, D(p)\Rightarrow\operatorname{Reached}_{B,E}(p)\bigr)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall R,\;\bigl(B\subseteq R\ \land\ E\text{ preserves }R\bigr)
\Rightarrow D\subseteq R.
\]

R ranges over Prop-valued interpretations of these inference premises. The forward direction is induction on a derivation; the reverse instantiates R with derivability itself. No finite position set, total causal graph, enumeration or completed infinite computation is required. The theorem proves neither that D has already been identified with every intelligence's relevant response claims nor that the supplied B and E are ethically warranted. An interpretation omitting an unreachable claim diagnoses incompleteness of these stated premises, not a possible world with arbitrarily altered moral truth.

The same module establishes when local certificates compose. Their claims must agree on overlaps, and every step must preserve the ground within its actual scope. They can then alternate scopes. A checked witness reaches a claim using two scopes that neither isolated closure reaches; another has complete domain coverage and a seed but still fails target coverage. These are logical tests, not a claim that contextual ethical reasons must all agree.

`history_reach_iff` retains occurrences within one history fiber. It prevents an argument from borrowing a warranted instance from a different history merely because the occurrence label matches. The index's intended real-world reference and the completeness of each reason key remain obligations. All local-to-global results are conditional on warranted compatibility and transport; they cannot manufacture those premises from contact or overlap.

## 3. The constructive correction

The inherited hand argument uses Boolean-valued interpretations and a condition requiring an explicit incoming edge into every relevant unseeded cut. Its classical scope remains intact. It must not be relabeled a constructive empty-axiom result without checking its logical steps.

The [new audit](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/CutCoverageAudit.lean) defines a precise related obstruction: a target-containing, anchor-free, reverse-closed cut. It proves constructively:

\[
\operatorname{HasCut}(p)\iff\neg\operatorname{Reached}(p),\qquad
\operatorname{NoCut}(p)\iff\neg\neg\operatorname{Reached}(p).
\]

The unrestricted upgrade from this `NoCut` to reachability, over arbitrary position types and premises, is equivalent to double-negation elimination. The proof reduces an arbitrary proposition to the seed of a singleton with no edges. Double-negation elimination is an explicit proposition in the equivalence, not an installed axiom. Decidable reachability suffices for an individual upgrade.

This does **not** identify the reverse-closed-cut condition constructively with the older explicit-crossing condition. Extracting an incoming crossing or a first entry can require membership decisions. Likewise, the older Boolean characteristic function of arbitrary reachability is not silently supplied here. Prop-valued closure gives the constructive exact inference theorem. This qualification repairs the proof's logical scope; it does not refute the earlier classical theorem or establish a barrier to universal ethical truth.
