## 2. Separate explanatory claims through the activity that supports them

These are investigative distinctions within one history, not independent kinds of reality or a compulsory ladder through which every kind of cognition must pass.

| Proposed achievement | What an explanation must identify | What remains unsettled by that achievement alone |
|---|---|---|
| Information is available in a recorded pattern | Which differences an investigator can recover, with an independently specified target and domain | Whether the organism's subsequent activity makes use of those differences |
| Acquired organization affects later conduct | Formation, retention and a supported causal route into the relevant conduct | Whether the selected local pattern implements the relation, enables its acquisition elsewhere, or supports attention and access |
| A representation can be mistaken about the same relation | Content continuity through defective instances, constrained by acquisition and differentiated use | Whether one uniquely detailed content is established, or whether the process reflectively assesses its grounds |
| A claim's support can itself be assessed | How provenance, access, applicability and counterevidence enter selective retention, suspension or revision | Whether the assessment is correct in this case, or gives a practical obligation to act |

The account should preserve all demonstrated achievements while making only the strongest warranted attribution. Neither neural decoding nor a single successful intervention licenses every later attribution. Nor does the absence of reflective argument erase elementary perception or skill.

## 4. A positive proposal: understanding through differentiated answerability

For the narrower target of **reflective empirical judgment**, the working candidate is that understanding a claim and its grounds consists in an acquired organization that makes the claim's implications and relevant grounds available across changes of use and challenge. Formation, retention, response and correction belong to the same developing practice. The representation need not be a sentence stored inside an isolated subject.

What gives this proposal content is selective consequence. Discovering that a source could not observe a condition can undermine reliance on its report without establishing the report's negation. Discovering that the condition differs can refute the report while preserving what it asserted. Changing what one wants can change one's response without changing the factual claim. These are different transformations of the organized activity, not three names for any decrease in an error score.

This candidate supplies an affirmative explanation: a fact about access can become a reason through its acquired bearing on a judgment, and the same access distinction can be reused when the speaker, sign or immediate interest changes. The distinction's role is demonstrated through that integration. No subsequent inner observer is required to read it again.

It is not a theorem that these relations suffice for every kind of understanding. The existing construction realizes a bounded source/correction connection; the wider generalization still needs comparison. Nor is reflective challenge a necessary condition for every instance of knowledge, content or animal cognition. A temporarily blocked capacity can remain possessed, and a person can understand a challenge while refusing to act on it.

**Strong countercase: informed deception.** Someone can track the actual condition, identify another person's evidential vulnerability and knowingly issue a false report. A theory that locates content solely in public correction or cooperation would misdescribe this activity. The content attribution must follow the whole relevant history, including planning, retained distinctions and selective use, while preserving the false report's public content. This is a constructed philosophical case, not a diagnosis of the animals or a newly observed experiment. It tests the criterion without switching an otherwise unexplained semantic predicate.

Deception also shows why recognition of a reason, endorsement, motivation and conduct require differentiated explanation. It does not establish the absence of a reason or obligation. The normative question remains what warrants the relevant demand under the actual circumstances and the declared ethical premises.

## 5. The next comparison is a pattern of selective changes

The following protocol is a proposed research target, not a performed experiment. It applies first to an already acquired reporting/inquiry practice; it does not claim to derive language from a bare cue apparatus. The practical illustration is a report about whether a shared water supply is available. The relevant records, inspection routes and reliance all form within the same ongoing activity.

| Perturbation | Candidate pattern to explain | A result that would weaken the proposed attribution |
|---|---|---|
| Change the desirability of using the water, while preserving its availability and the reporting route | Change the use or action while retaining the factual discrimination | Reports simply track the new incentive with no evidence that the availability distinction remains usable elsewhere |
| Show that the particular reporter lacked access, without new evidence about availability itself | Revise reliance or seek a check; do not automatically accept the opposite report | A generic negative cue produces negation regardless of whether it concerns access, desirability or the actual condition |
| Obtain relevant contrary evidence through an established, differently vulnerable route | Correct the asserted availability claim while retaining which claim was wrong | Rename the old content to make the previous report retrospectively correct |
| Re-encode the report while preserving the acquired reference and access relations | Transfer the appropriate inferences and challenges | Performance depends solely on familiar symbol shape |
| Keep the claim fixed but change which activity depends on it | Apply the same factual distinction while reassessing relevance and responsibility | Copy the previous action without assessing the changed dependence |

These are contrasts to investigate, not a pass/fail definition of mindedness. Measurement must distinguish factual response, practical choice and support assessment rather than infer them all from one action. In nonverbal cases, identify an appropriate behavioral route instead of treating silence as absence of understanding. In linguistic cases, fluent explanations alone are insufficient evidence of the claimed causal integration.

Compare a learned relational model, a sufficiently rich account of skilled participation without basic representational content, and a simpler cue-response account on the **same** histories and held-out perturbations. Match relevant learning opportunities, memory, support and access. Do not cripple a rival by excluding retention and then credit our account for remembering. Do not assume that the rival cannot accommodate transfer simply because it uses different philosophical vocabulary.

A rival that explains the whole pattern equally well defeats a claim of exclusive explanation. A rival that also preserves the full proposed content/grounds organization may instead be an alternative realization or description of it. Which situation obtains requires examining the accounts; renaming them does not settle it. Finite observational equivalence leaves additional causal differences open.

## 6. Substantive counterreview

This is an author-conducted review, not an independent-agent acceptance.

**A content-free account can retain serious normativity.** Hutto and Satne's proposal distinguishes basic directed activity from contentful practices and explicitly preserves truth beyond consensus. It therefore cannot be dismissed as equating correctness with social approval. Their account leaves origins work to be done; so does ours. The present evidence does not settle between their placement of content and a broader representational account. [Primary comparison](SEMANTIC-SUFFICIENCY-SOURCES.md#philosophical-rivals).

**A representational account need not entail a unified good agent.** Shea argues for mechanisms of coordination in representational accounts of agency. Our inference is that even coordinated representation and action leave the ethical quality of the resulting purpose to justify. Neither coherence nor one chosen scalar can silently become G0. That comparison also challenges any easy inference from a local neural representation to whole reflective agency.

**Calling selective change understanding may be stipulative.** The reply must show the explanatory work: why access changes support, why contrary evidence concerns the same claim, and why a changed purpose leaves some factual commitments intact. A model with these relationships merely assigned still has a correspondence burden. The current proposal offers a constitutive interpretation and an empirical comparison programme, not an independently established universal biconditional.

**A scripted mechanism could pass a finite battery.** Agreed. Tests discriminate specified accounts and vulnerabilities. Acquired transfer, causal interventions and differently vulnerable checks can improve evidence, but no finite list here certifies all possible histories. This is a limit of evidential reach, not a proof that every actual implementation lacks understanding.

**Failure to revise might reflect refusal or impaired access.** Agreed. Establish which relation failed. The account must preserve comprehension where it remains operative, including deliberate misreporting. Conversely, merely stipulating an unobservable comprehension does not repair a missing explanation.

**The learning evidence is too narrow for philosophy.** It is too narrow to prove universal philosophy, but it constrains a concrete premise: interpretation cannot be equated with a fixed acquired reward response. Its value here is that specific constraint and the causal acquisition evidence, not prestige borrowed from neuroscience.

**Conscious experience may be part of understanding.** That substantive position deserves its own argument. The present investigation neither proves an experience-free sufficiency claim nor treats the absence of an experience measure as evidence of absence. A further consciousness study must specify which experiential phenomenon it explains and what evidence discriminates the proposed relationship. No cosmic subject follows from a common process.

## 7. What this changes in the larger direction

The immediate task is now more precise than explaining how a symbol gets a label. It is to test whether the already constructed acquisition/correction route generalizes to differentiated assessment of grounds. This advances the recognition/calibration transition needed by the ethical programme. Which actual dependencies and losses are recognized matters to a justified response; improved recognition still cannot supply G0 or every comparison commitment by itself.

Necessity should be investigated at the level of the relationships that survive comparison. An account of scientific judgment must explain its target's relevant error and correction, including how support can fail while the question remains the same. It need not adopt every term, ethical premise or model in this repository unless an additional argument establishes that dependence. The existing [explanatory-necessity branch](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EXPLANATORY-NECESSITY.md) retains its relative mathematical results; this empirical study does not extend them into unique universal ontology.

Consciousness remains a further research front. The useful next question there concerns the formation and limits of particular experiential perspectives within shared activity, including differences between access, report and undergoing. Treating all integrated information or all generativity as conscious would decide the question before testing it. This paragraph sets direction only; it claims no new consciousness result.

The bounded stopping point of this round is reached: a positive semantic candidate, serious rivals, inspected causal evidence, four numerical checks and an explicit next discriminating comparison are integrated. **Universal semantic sufficiency, exhaustive consciousness and necessity of the whole theory are still open.** The broader frontier goal remains active. Further progress requires the specified comparison or a substantive constitutive argument that survives it, not another repetition of the same conditional mechanism.
