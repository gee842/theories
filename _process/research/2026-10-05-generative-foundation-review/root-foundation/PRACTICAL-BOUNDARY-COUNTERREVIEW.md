# Consequential learning can tighten a practical exclusion

6 October 2026. AI-authored counterreview of **formed practical boundary and field of significance → consequential omissions encountered → revision of boundary, evaluation and response within one history**. The shared configuration is the existing paid plasticity history reviewed below. This follows the [project-wide nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) and the [current core/tree correction](CORE-TREE-NONDUAL-REORIENTATION.md). No new controller, theorem, empirical observation or ethical axiom is introduced.

**Assessment:** acquired recipient consequences can genuinely form and revise operative influence while the rule admitting that influence remains organized around the actor's own outcomes. In the selected history, a faithfully acquired recipient loss participates in replacing a positive connection with a negative one; available effective service is then declined. This is a substantive rival to treating consequential openness and revision as sufficient for appropriate response. It does not refute patient-directed moral favor, prove that the actual learner recognizes and rejects a duty, or establish that every revisable boundary must have this structure.

## 1. Fix the history before interpreting its boundary

The [accepted plasticity integration](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY-INTEGRATION.md) already establishes paid formation from empty sockets, later use and a matched replacement failure. The [ethical application](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md#what-correct-revision-must-preserve) already interprets that failure conditionally. Neither result is new here. The present contribution is to test what the proposed practical-boundary reconstruction may infer from them.

The source is [PlasticityWitness.lean](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/plasticity/formal/PlasticityWitness.lean), especially `continued`, `actual_replacement_histories`, `matched_revision_accounts` and `revision_changes_assessment_not_native_data`. Its [event law](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/plasticity/formal/PairedPlasticity.lean) specifies the following configuration:

- The inherited parent is the actual `RevisionWitness.consumed` state. There is one recipient account, acquired command knowledge, supplied meters and sockets, training and revision provision, and a paid local binding law.
- Four aligned pairs form two positive links from empty sockets and yield native register `n=4`. The recipient's incoming 40 becomes 36; the actor has 14 available units before assessment. The primary four-pair branch can assess and deliver ten, ending with recipient 46.
- The selected **continued** branch instead permits six pairs. It extends the aligned training before closure and service. Its fifth pair acquires no own loss together with an actual recipient loss, pays to retire and replace the first positive connection, and makes that connection negative under the active rule. The sixth pair acquires own loss without recipient loss.
- Active and frozen-sign continuations finish training with the same six pairs, `n=5`, recipient 34, actor fuel 16, total energy 72 and material 68. The active signs cancel; frozen signs remain positive. After the same factual read and assessment, the active branch abstains while the frozen branch can pay for effective service.

The primary endpoint 46 is **not** followed by a new unexplained fall to 34. These are alternative continuations. The continued entry explicitly allows the six-pair schedule; the matched active and frozen branches share that condition. No patient replacement, resource reset or changed past receipt supplies their difference.

## 2. The strongest explanation preserves real acquisition

The rival account is not that the recipient was never encountered. The meter reads the actual acquired recipient outcome. The receipt participates in a paid event that changes material conductance and subsequently changes action. Nor is the account that the learner cannot act: the matched active continuation retains enough resources and correct means knowledge.

The decisive asymmetry is in the actual rule. An acquired recipient-loss bit enables binding or replacement, but the sign learned on that occasion depends on the acquired **own-loss** bit. Native updating also follows own loss. Consequently, the recipient's consequence can enter the system's practical field on terms whose favorable direction is set by another account's outcome. In the fifth pair, the very occurrence that adds a recipient loss makes a negatively signed replacement available because the paired own outcome has no loss. Cancellation then removes the operative support for service.

This is a concrete explanation of selective uptake. It preserves the acquired observations, the action-effect relation, the local event order, all costs and the same recipient. Nothing is accomplished by setting an otherwise free `Reason` predicate to false. The exclusion concerns this process's influence on selection; it is not yet a justified exclusion of a moral reason.

One might describe the organization as appropriating recipient information into its native response pattern. That is a proposed functional reading, not an empirical finding of intentional exploitation. The source does not establish a strategy to harm, an understanding of responsibility, a wish to profit from the recipient, or a mental denial of their significance. It also does not show the learner acquiring a proposition that own loss morally licenses or cancels another's claim. The actual rule is sufficient to explain the behavior without those stronger attributions.

The challenge therefore reaches an identifiable inference: **formation of broader causal influence, followed by truthful feedback-driven revision, is insufficient to identify the revision as an appropriate answer to the affected recipient.** It does not challenge the modest claim that such formation can mediate an appropriate response under justified application conditions.

## 3. Which boundary changed, and which did not?

“Boundary” should preserve distinctions within this one activity rather than give several achievements a common certificate.

| Relation being investigated | What this history establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Access to another account's consequences | Paid meters acquire actual recipient outcomes. | Acquisition of felt adversity, consent or the recipient's own assessment. |
| Operative influence on choice | Material links form, can be replaced, and alter assessment. | That the influence tracks a patient-directed reason. |
| Criteria governing that influence | The supplied own-loss-dependent sign rule acts on new acquired pairs. | Formation or revision of the rule that makes own loss decisive for the sign. |
| Legitimate scope of response | Technical scope and permission conditions constrain admitted acts. | Authority or standing derived from those flags, or a morally warranted restriction of the patient's claim. |

These are relations among the same operations, not independent ontological layers. Their distinction is needed precisely because the recorded recipient event and the learned sign belong to one causal history. Greater receptivity at the first relation can coexist with reduced favorable influence at the second, under an unchanged third relation.

The consequential omission is therefore not simply an unread recipient bit. That bit was read. The account must ask whether the history registers the relation between its own imposed operations, the recipient's cumulative uncompensated loss and the available remedy. The witness does not acquire or use that relation as such. Calling the existing negative replacement a revision of the *grounds of evaluation* would overstate a revision of the *effective disposition*.

This is not a demand for another independent controller. A richer connected formation might acquire and revise the relevant relation within its ongoing activity. The current representation leaves that explanation open; its omission does not establish that the relation lies outside generativity. Conversely, calling the process nondual cannot turn its present own-loss rule into an explanation of appropriate treatment.

## 4. The ethical challenge must retain its grounds

Under the preceding [repair application](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md#an-independently-stated-repair-ground), the recipient's remaining loss can warrant repair: the actor or responsible operator is answerable for imposing or maintaining an uncompensated deprivation of independently warranted resources or capabilities; the proposed remedy is adequate and permitted; and the complete comparison supports its priority. Under those conditions, the active revision leaves a warranted response undone while the frozen comparator delivers it.

This is a genuine conditional failure of appropriate uptake. It is not an assumption-free derivation of the repair relation. The causal fact that an operation removed units does not itself establish entitlement to retain them, responsibility for their removal, absence of valid agreement or compensation, or the adequacy of a ten-unit service. The source gives no actual phenomenal identification. The responsible agent could be a deploying operator or institution; its responsibility cannot be transferred to a learner merely because the learner's state contains the accounts.

These qualifications should not be converted into arbitrary alternate moral labels. A consensual expenditure and an imposed deprivation differ in their actual histories and relations. They are possible application distinctions that this representation does not identify, not counterexamples preserving an already warranted uncompensated-deprivation premise. Likewise, if the positive reconstruction independently defends that premise and its actor-addressed significance, it has made philosophical progress even though the local program does not encode the argument.

A fully informed exploiter supplies a further conceptual challenge: an organization can accurately understand the loss and its responsibility while adjusting its behavior only enough to protect its own position. Such a case would undermine a claim that factual recognition or even professed moral recognition necessarily produces appropriate response. It would not, merely by its refusal, undermine the reason for repair. This richer case is **not realized by the present witness**. The review does not import strategic intention to make the finite mechanism appear stronger.

The universal-minimum objective also exceeds this role-bound application. Showing how an organization should answer a loss it imposes does not establish the same ground for every independently situated actor who can relieve another's adversity. That extension remains a substantive part of the foundation, not a reason to discard the present bounded relation.

## 5. A discriminating continuation and revision conditions

The next explanation should answer a question fixed by this history: **what could make the recipient's continuing loss alter the criterion of response when its occurrence no longer parallels the actor's own loss?** Merely adding more encounters, more links or another successful service would not answer it. Nor would freezing positive signs be a general solution: fulfilled claims, changed means and valid refusal can require different responses.

Any proposed answer should separate three claims: the actual loss/response relation is warranted; the organization can acquire evidence bearing on that relation; and the acquired relation can revise later action on the same resource history. The earlier ethical application supports the first conditionally, while this witness supplies acquisition and disposition change of a narrower kind. Their coexistence is not already acquisition of responsibility-sensitive repair.

The following findings would change the assessment:

1. A claim that this very continuation revises its own-loss-based criterion must identify the actual revision and its later use. Changing a link under that unchanged criterion does not satisfy the claim.
2. An account claiming appropriate repair in the active branch fails if the independently warranted loss and priority remain applicable while the response is declined. Finding valid fulfillment, a different responsible party or a justified contrary priority would instead revise that application; it would not change the recorded physical history.
3. A richer proposed formation that distinguishes outstanding from fulfilled claims while preserving independent claims, legitimate refusal and real costs would extend the explanation. It must establish its actual connection rather than borrow a successful endpoint from a sibling study.
4. A claim that wider recognition necessarily improves treatment is defeated by a relevant history with warranted adverse recipient consequences and worsened treatment despite preserved recognition. For this witness the ethical premises remain conditional; intentional exploitation and universal moral knowledge are not established.

The round should therefore retain both positive formation and acquired exclusion in its account. The required revision is to the interpretation of an enlarged or mutable practical field as sufficient evidence of ethical correction. No new impossibility result follows. The substantive task is to explain and justify how the formed activity can answer the consequences for its differentiated participants, including where its inherited criteria obscure those consequences.
