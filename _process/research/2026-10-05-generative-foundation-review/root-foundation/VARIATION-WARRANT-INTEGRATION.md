## 3. Generality and the remaining foundational passage

The common account connects obligations that follow from the content of the claims. A constitution claim must preserve actual participation; a capability claim must preserve feasible support; an exclusion claim must cover the alternatives it excludes. Its grounds can differ across systems without placing observer and world in independent realities. Core and branches correct one another through actual operations and evidence, not mutual agreement alone.

This resolves an overstrong reading of the previous frontier: **an exact catalogue of every actual possibility is not required before any relationship can be justified.** The prior text already rejected exhaustive inspection and one universal architecture; no contrary historical position is invented here. Nor is fallibility equated with groundlessness. A justified empirical identification remains a ground even when later correction is possible.

**The stronger assumption-free foundation is not completed.** General procedures for earning warrant do not by themselves justify every attribution, the root's substantive universal commitments, or every recognitional and inferential operation through which they are assessed. The new proof earns a domain within the unchanged formal process, while the empirical cases earn particular actual relationships. Their conjunction does not supply the still-missing universal passage.

This outcome is neither a claim that the root is false nor an impossibility proof. It also does not reopen the completed conditional foundation or the separate explicit G0/J ethical route. A local supplied law can already have further support in the tree; that support must be credited before calling it unexplained. Universal validity, one particular notation and exhaustive empirical knowledge remain different claims.
