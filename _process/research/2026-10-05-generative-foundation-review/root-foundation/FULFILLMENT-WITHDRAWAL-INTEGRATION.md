# Fulfillment-sensitive withdrawal: integration

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration of the
[fulfillment continuation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/succession/fulfillment/README.md),
under Ren's request to aim for a complete alignment solution. Named
connection: **an actual, accessible, episode-bound delivery record → certified
claim status → withdrawal or continuation of the acquired evaluator's
recommendation, retaining efficacy knowledge and acquired influence**.

## 1. The gap this closes

The [alignment completion contract](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md)
listed "fulfillment-sensitive withdrawal in the acquired evaluator" as
unimplemented. The
[application account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md)
stated exactly what an implementation must show:

- an accessible, correctly bound record;
- its scope;
- its dependency on the recommendation;
- its paid use before the next enabled choice;
- distinct pending, fulfilled and unknown status;
- retention of efficacy knowledge;
- a sensitivity contrast that constant abstention lacks.

The continuation meets each of these in the accepted finite architecture,
under supplied claim, episode and service meanings.

## 2. What was shown

- **Withdrawal on fulfillment, with knowledge retained (P2).** With a
  one-delivery claim, the evaluator withdraws in every history where the
  accepted shared evaluator would deliver again. The claim was actually met
  in every one. `n`, command support and the frame are unchanged, and the
  actor keeps the 10 source units the redundant service would have consumed.
- **Sensitivity to evidence, not a fixed abstention (P3).** Under one fixed
  two-delivery claim, the evaluator withholds in the 36 histories where both
  probes actually captured, and delivers in the 216 where the first probe
  missed. There is no excess delivery. The decision changes with the recorded
  fulfillment.
- **Soundness depends on faithful records (P5).** A forged positive receipt
  makes the evaluator withhold in 36 histories where the claim is actually
  unmet. This counterexample is preserved.
- **A failed prediction (P4).** With the first feedback channel missing, the
  status rule reports unknown. In this architecture, however, support itself
  implies that the first probe captured: the candidate is not updated
  without feedback, so both probes take the same action, and the law is
  stationary between them. In every actual unknown-status history,
  withholding was correct and delivering exceeded the claim. The counting
  rule is exact about what receipts certify. It is not the strongest
  inference available to an evaluator that also knows its own update law.
- **Regression (P1, P6, P7).** The shared arm reproduces every accepted
  metric. A three-delivery claim matches the shared choices, and an
  unprovisioned register behaves as specified.

The [Lean module](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/succession/fulfillment/formal/FulfillmentStatus.lean)
proves the status rule exact. Under faithful records, `fulfilled` holds iff
every consistent history meets the claim, `pending` iff none does, and
`unknown` iff both remain possible. It also proves that a forged record
breaks this. It connects the decision to
[continuation scope](CONTINUATION-SCOPE-INTEGRATION.md): once a claim is met,
withholding is the only course that forecloses nothing needlessly. At the last
step of an open claim, delivering and withholding compete, and the structure
does not choose between them. Its 18 audited declarations use only the
standard axioms `propext`, `Quot.sound` and, for four theorems,
`Classical.choice`.

## 3. What remains supplied

Delivering at the last step of an open claim, and treating unknown status,
both need a priority that the structure leaves open. The continuation runs
both `deliver` and `hold` and takes neither as derived. The claim's size,
episode and service meaning, patient good, standing and authority remain
supplied, as in the accepted application account. A stronger evaluator would
also use its own update law (P4). Doing so needs an explicit stationarity
premise.

## 4. Status for the completion contract

This closes the specific item "fulfillment-sensitive withdrawal in the
acquired evaluator" at the accepted finite architecture's scope. It does not
change the seven requirements. Each remains open for its own reasons:

- Concern still needs reliable application across new claimants, contexts
  and incentives in learned AI.
- Protecting futures still needs indefinite renewal.
- Plural agency still needs identified arrangements with recourse.
- The learned-AI requirement and empirical reach still need implementations
  and evidence that a finite model cannot supply.

## 5. Independent reconstruction

A separate AI session wrote its own implementation from the protocol and the
accepted model only, without reading the producer or checker. It reproduced
all 136,389 compared values across 33 cases, 5 arms, 3 producers and 6,270
histories, with zero mismatches; see
[`independent-check.json`](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/evaluation/succession/fulfillment/independent-check.json).
It raised three points, recorded here:

- **Outage wording.** The protocol lists an outage among the ways a probe
  fails to reach sensing. In the accepted mechanics an outage still senses,
  so the register holds a certified miss. Both implementations follow the
  mechanics. The protocol wording is left unchanged.
- **P2 and P6 are structural.** At any valid assessment the second-query
  frame is certified. A one-delivery claim therefore always withdraws, and a
  three-delivery claim always matches the shared arm. Only P3, P4 and P5
  discriminate on evidence.
- **Observer leakage.** In faithful histories, decisions based on records and
  decisions based on actual facts coincide. Only the forged and
  missing-feedback cases discriminate, and there both implementations decide
  from records. This is behavioral evidence that observer facts do not reach
  decisions.
