# Relevant possibilities and standing in shared inquiry / 共同求知中的可能性与地位

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [Ren's finish-line request](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-knowledge-content-continuation.md#finish-line-request). Addresses the explicit frontier of [round three](INQUIRY-PRIORITIES-AND-G0.md#9-integrated-third-round-result): warranted inclusion of possibilities and justified standing under unequal access. Follows the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). This is philosophical argument and source assessment, with inherited checked mathematics; no new mathematical theorem or empirical experiment is asserted.

## 1. The affirmative connection

**A reflective inquiry is accountable both for its answers and for the boundaries through which it makes those answers available.** Those boundaries include the question, admitted alternatives, evidential routes, participants and consequences considered. Their warrant can be challenged through relationships that the current representation leaves out. They need neither remain fixed nor include every conceivable possibility.

The scope is reflective inquiry that claims to justify a judgment or shared response. An infant's perceptual knowledge does not require an articulated public procedure. Nor must every personal thought undergo collective review. The present argument concerns an additional achievement: sustaining a claim of warranted inquiry when pertinent challenges bear on its basis or application.

A mathematical domain is a representation of selected possibilities, not a fence around actuality. The third-round common-choice result supplies a consequence given a domain; it cannot establish that a decision's real consequences are exhausted by that domain. The earlier exact-correspondence result already distinguishes successful answering within a narrow domain from failure when relevant histories are admitted. Their hypotheses remain unchanged. We now explain what can warrant the selection and revision, without turning another formal predicate into its own ethical justification.

The knowing positions and their unequal access form within shared histories: bodily experience, practical participation, training, instruments, records, language and institutional control. No participant begins outside the process with a complete inventory. Correspondingly, no central compiler acquires the whole perspective merely by collecting reports. What can be known jointly depends on actual interpretation, access and appropriate combination; privacy and real burdens remain part of the activity.

## 2. What warrants admitting, bracketing or excluding a possibility

Begin with the content actually asserted. A claim about a present reading, a cause of that reading, and the consequences of acting on it need different error inquiries. Then reconstruct the routes supporting that content and examine conditions that could break or alter them. Relevant alternatives have grounds in those routes, in supported dependencies, in pertinent experience, or in observed failure. Mere verbal conceivability does not automatically make an alternative equally credible.

There are three useful dispositions. **Admit for assessment** when the possibility has a supported connection to the claim or its consequences. **Bracket explicitly** when its relevance is credible but available inquiry cannot yet settle it; qualify reliance accordingly. **Exclude provisionally** when there are adequate grounds for its irrelevance or defeat within the stated scope. Exclusion must state its grounds and reach. Not investigating everything does not authorize asserting that everything uninvestigated is impossible.

The support needed to open an investigation is different from that needed to accept its proposed explanation. A report of an unexpected effect can warrant a check even if its proposed cause is mistaken. A repeated practical difficulty can warrant revising a model's categories before anyone has a replacement theory. Conversely, an allegation does not become true merely because suppressing it would be wrong. This distinction lets criticism enter without treating every criticism as an established result.

An illustrative shared workshop makes the point. A machine meets its measured performance specification, yet people working beside it report disruption that the test did not ask about. Their reports need not refute the narrow performance measurement. They may defeat the wider assertion that the arrangement has no relevant adverse consequences. Investigating the reported pattern can then distinguish a changed operating condition, a previously unmeasured effect or an unsupported causal attribution. These are stipulated alternatives, not an empirical finding about an actual workshop.

A first-person report can have particular authority about what is experienced while leaving its cause, prevalence and appropriate remedy unsettled. An instrumental measurement can have particular authority about its calibrated target while leaving lived effects unsettled. Their scope differences arise within the same situation; they do not divide experience and measurement into separate realities.

The relevance judgment is fallible. The proposed warrant is an accountable history of selection, investigation and response to defeaters, not proof that the final inventory contains all possible surprises. General skepticism remains a rival to the disclosed fallibilist entitlement. A finite account's incompleteness is not itself a diagnosis of which ordinary claim is false; an identified omission bearing on that claim can be.

## 3. Three grounds for standing

| Ground | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant access or competence | Taking the contribution seriously as potential evidence, with credibility assessed in its scope. | Universal expertise, automatic truth, or authority to impose consequences. |
| Being affected in living, participation or continuing possibilities | Inclusion of the effects in practical comparison and the already declared standing to be heard and challenge relevant decisions. | Expertise about every causal mechanism or an unlimited veto. |
| A justified undertaking, procedure or delegation | A bounded authority to decide or coordinate, subject to its terms, actual capacity and review. | Truth by office, unlimited sovereignty, or permission to ignore valid objections. |

These are distinguishable reasons arising in one activity, and can belong to the same person. Technical expertise may make a report reliable while leaving a proposed allocation unjustified. Someone with little technical training may disclose an omitted condition. An authorized coordinator may need to act under disagreement while accurately preserving that disagreement and the remaining uncertainty.

The ethical standing in the second row is **not a new consequence secretly drawn from bare G0**. The existing [practical-judgment account, section 4](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md#4-谁承担什么--allocating-responsibilities), already declares affected participation and relevant knowledge as grounds to be heard, challenge and participate. J3/J4 distinguish that standing from unlimited veto and total disclosure. This study explains its epistemic connection and applicability; it does not claim to have independently derived that whole political commitment from an information theorem.

G0 gives a further positive reason for taking these relations seriously: understanding, participation and the lives affected belong within the affirmed whole. Under J1, a comparison that simply deletes a burden cannot claim improvement without countervailing loss. Under J2, optional gains cannot justify imposed basic deprivation in its stated scope. Under J3, responsibility and access are differentiated and bounded. Under J4, decision authority must itself be warranted. E requires that these descriptions actually apply.

Inclusion can therefore be both epistemically useful and ethically owed; the two explanations should be shown rather than assumed identical. A truthful but exploitative investigation can gain knowledge while wronging participants. A respectful process can remain mistaken. We need both distinctions to recognize the failures.

## 4. Exclusion can affect the question before it affects the answer

[Fricker's introduction](https://www.mirandafricker.com/uploads/1/3/6/2/136236203/introduction.pdf) distinguishes prejudicial loss of testimonial credibility from disadvantages in shared interpretive resources. It also argues that reducing reasons to power obscures the difference between warranted rejection and prejudice. We use the complete introduction, not an asserted reading of the book's detailed arguments. This provides a primary challenge to treating every relevant experience as already expressible in the dominant inquiry vocabulary.

Our resulting requirement is specific: when a report does not fit the available categories, assess whether the difficulty lies in the report, its interpretation, or the categories. Requiring a fully accepted explanation before allowing examination of an unexplained pattern can protect the very assumptions under question. Repeated observations, practical demonstrations, comparison of descriptions and supported changes of question can help; no single route is guaranteed to succeed.

This does not give every proposed category automatic authority. A new category can obscure variation, incorporate a false explanation, or facilitate domination. Its adequacy needs assessment through what it discriminates, what it makes intelligible, how it guides further inquiry and where it fails. Experience need not be publicly exposed in full to supply a relevant challenge; justified intermediaries and protected forms of communication can sometimes preserve access with fewer burdens.

Nonverbal, absent and future affected lives also cannot be excluded simply because they cannot enter a hearing. Their inclusion requires warranted accounts of dependence and consequences, and accountable representation where applicable. Neither a self-appointed spokesperson nor a computational forecast becomes authoritative by claiming to represent the whole. The framework supplies reasons and constraints; it does not uniquely settle every form of representation.

## 5. Inquiry needs effective criticism, not merely a permission to speak

[Longino's 1990 discussion, pp. 75–79](https://fitelson.org/piksi/longino.pdf), connects objectivity to criticism of background assumptions, public avenues, shared standards, responsiveness and intellectual authority among qualified practitioners. She distinguishes objective method from guaranteed truth. This is an antecedent and rival account with substantial overlap, not evidence of our theory's unique priority. The later 2002 formulation was not read for this study.

The present proposal requires a traceable route from a material challenge to reassessment. A process can allow testimony yet prevent it from changing the admitted cases, the interpretation of evidence or the practical decision. Listening then does not establish correction. Conversely, responsiveness can consist in a supported rejection of the criticism; it need not mean capitulating to every demand.

An inquiry adequate to its undertaking should be able to explain: what the challenge concerns; what bearing it has; what examination occurred or why it could not occur; whether the answer, scope, confidence or response changed; and what remains unsettled. This is an explanatory standard, not a demand for a public dossier about every private cognitive act. Records can be proportionate, privacy-preserving and directed to those entitled to assess them.

Effective access sometimes needs interpretation, time, education, material support or protection against retaliation. Equal formal permission can coexist with radically unequal capacity to use it. The allocation of these supports requires the practical commitments and actual circumstances. The theory does not derive a universal budget, meeting format or identical evidential weight for every speaker.

## 6. A primary case constrains the account

[Wynne's 1992 case study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235737617_Misunderstood_misunderstanding_social_identities_and_public_uptake_of_science) reports Cumbrian farmers' encounters with scientific advice after Chernobyl. Its discussion on pp. 286–287 connects inappropriate transfer between soil conditions with overlooked practical knowledge, including a criticism of the experimental arrangement. This is source-reported qualitative evidence; we have not checked the underlying measurements, interviews or cited experiments. It illustrates how someone can contribute relevant specialist access without acquiring authority over every disputed explanation.

The study constrains our theory in two directions. The qualification of an established expert cannot by itself warrant extending every claim to a new setting. Equally, a failure of expert advice does not establish every competing explanation offered by an affected group. A whole account must reconstruct particular evidence, dependence, uncertainty and consequences, rather than sort people once and for all into the knowledgeable and the ignorant.

This is not a new empirical confirmation of generative monism or G0. Conventional social epistemology can explain the same case. Its contribution here is a substantive test of the claimed connection between access, bounded competence and practical participation.

## 7. Finite closure without permanently closing the field

An inquiry can close for a stated decision when its operative grounds and known material challenges have been addressed sufficiently for that response under the declared practical standards. Adequacy may require a narrower answer, provisional action, a protective response across remaining cases or explicit acknowledgment that the requested assurance cannot be given. The earlier deadline analysis remains relevant: indefinite inquiry can eliminate the opportunity it was meant to inform.

This closure is defeasible. It records the scope of reliance and circumstances that would warrant reopening: a discrepant observation, changed supporting conditions, credible omitted testimony or discovery of a shared failure in the checks. Reopening is a responsibility under appropriate circumstances, not a promise of perpetual funded surveillance by every original participant. Who maintains it, for how long and with what capacity belongs to the undertaking.

Newness alone is not sufficient: repetitive defeated claims need not restart a closed decision. Nor is the existence of a deadline sufficient to suppress a material challenge. Urgency, the consequences of delay and the costs of examination require support. Different degrees of uncertainty can be tolerable for different actions without making the preferred factual answer true.

The boundary of an inquiry is itself an object of inquiry. This does not demand that every inquiry contain a completed inquiry into all of its own conditions. The existing fallibilist commitments permit warranted reliance while leaving particular routes vulnerable to pertinent criticism. No final self-validating certificate is claimed.

## 8. Substantive counterreview

**Could an authoritarian group get a particular answer right?** Yes. Truth and some local knowledge can occur under unjust organization. The proposal does not define inconvenient counterexamples out of existence. It argues that an undertaking claiming warranted whole comparison cannot justify excluding relevant grounds merely through control, and that ethical failures remain even where an answer is accurate.

**Could plural participation make inquiry worse?** Yes: distraction, duplicated sources, intimidation and misleading claims can consume attention or spread error. Participation is not an information maximization rule. Scoped competence, supported relevance, reasonable triage and practical limits remain necessary. Diversity can expose a vulnerability; its mere presence is no guarantee.

**Does triage reinstate the gatekeeper?** Some allocation of finite attention is unavoidable in the actual inquiry. The proposed response is to make exclusion challengeable, preserve grounds and provide a proportionate alternative route when the original filter is credibly implicated. This does not eliminate power or completely derive an institution. Unjustified gatekeeping remains a real failure possibility, including for our own framework.

**Is the account circular because it assesses standards using other standards?** It does not establish all knowledge without reliance on any capacity. It argues for local, defeasible correction through checks that can expose the particular failure at issue. A check that merely repeats the disputed assumption has not done that work. The general skeptical dispute remains open; asserting a completed absolute foundation would exceed the result.

**Do G0 and epistemic relevance already prove democratic institutions?** No. Affected standing is inherited from the explicitly declared practical commitments; forms of authorization and representation need further political justification and application. Claim-specific expertise and equal ethical inclusion do not entail one universal voting rule. The present account is compatible with multiple institutions and rules out no rival merely for using different terminology.

**Is this just social epistemology in generative language?** There is substantial overlap. Our contribution is the connected articulation from formed knowing positions through content, grounds, bounded inquiry and ethical comparison, with scope and failures preserved. Unique novelty, predictive superiority and exclusive philosophical necessity are not established.

## 9. Result for the philosophical account

The previous frontier now has a defended answer: relevant scope is warranted through supported connections and revisable exclusions; access, affected standing and authority are differentiated; criticism must have an effective route into reassessment; and practical closure retains its scope and reopening conditions. Existing mechanisms of learning and new question formation remain credited rather than restarted.

This advances the governing normative connection by explaining how an asserted whole comparison can remain answerable to omitted lives, dependencies and losses. It also identifies the inherited ethical commitments needed to give participation practical force. A representation can leave a relationship out without refuting the fuller theory; the theory itself fails in application if it protects that omission after its relevance is established.
