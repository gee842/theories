# Review: sufficient inquiry for an actual continuation

6 October 2026. AI-authored mathematical and assumption review under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **acquired distinctions → sufficient grounds and means for a particular continuation → reassessment after inquiry changes the situation**. The inquiry, its observations, its effects and the subsequent response belong to the same history. No separate evaluator, universal scalar reward or new controller is introduced.

## Positive bridge and existing results

**Inquiry can be sufficient for a response without identifying the whole process. Its sufficiency must nevertheless concern the actual continuation from the state the inquiry leaves.** Agreement about an earlier question does not imply agreement about a newly advancing question; more evidence does not by itself preserve feasible or warranted responses.

The common-choice result is already established. [Reason transport T13 and T15–T18](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#t13-exact-sufficiency-for-permission-and-for-choosing) separates identifying a whole permission correspondence from finding an action common to its evidence-compatible members. It also treats randomized choices, conflicting requirements and abstention. [Evaluative application §3](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-MATH.md#3-two-distinct-intersections-must-not-be-conflated) distinguishes intersection of individual licenses from the weaker aggregate undominated remainder; §5 already warns that inquiry can change the feasible menu. A new exposition should credit these results rather than announce that non-singleton knowledge can support action as a new theorem.

The useful further composition is dynamic: propagate each compatible configuration through the inquiry, including its physical and historical effects, then collect the configurations giving the same accessible observation. Evaluate continuation at those resulting configurations. Merely deleting inconsistent model names from the old set omits part of what the inquiry does.

The existing [scientific-inquiry conditions](SCIENTIFIC-INQUIRY-CONDITIONS.md) already require complete accessible histories, actual class coverage and current available actions. The [identity account](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/IMMANENT-MATHEMATICAL-IDENTITY.md) preserves definedness and relevant outcomes. [OutcomeContinuation](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/OutcomeContinuation.lean), especially `outcome_iff_actual_boundary`, composes a continuation from a reached boundary rather than a reset state. These are the prior interfaces to combine.

## The quantifiers must describe the same possible response

For compatible current configurations \(B\), let \(D(x)\) contain the response objects that are both executable from \(x\) and meet a separately specified requirement. At the stated scope, a common immediate response lies in

\[
C(B)=\bigcap_{x\in B}D(x).
\]

This is the inherited common-choice interface. Its objects must be actions or implementable continuations with consistent meanings, not just desired outcome labels. The statements

\[
\forall x\in B\;\exists r\text{ that works from }x
\quad\text{and}\quad
\exists r\;\forall x\in B\text{ that same }r\text{ works}
\]

are different. An outcome reachable separately in every hidden situation can require incompatible commands. Calling it a common outcome cannot supply the missing selection information. Conversely, a common feasible response can succeed without resolving a hidden distinction that does not affect its required consequences.

An empty immediate intersection does not rule out an adaptive inquiry that creates distinguishing observations before acting. A no-policy conclusion requires either an explicit bounded policy analysis or a premise that the relevant possibilities remain indistinguishable under every allowed continuation. Current equal observations alone are insufficient. Even then, absence of a uniformly guaranteed policy does not show that no action is justified under every other risk or evidential standard. The guarantee being tested must be declared.

## Contracts for a bounded dynamic criterion

A finite policy-tree characterization can express the further composition without adding a runtime policy. Let \(T(x,q)\) contain possible observation/successor pairs after an inquiry event \(q\). The evolved observation branch is

\[
B_{q,y}=\{x':\exists x\in B,\ (y,x')\in T(x,q)\}.
\]

The intended bounded recurrence says that success within zero further inquiry steps requires a common terminal response. Success within at most \(n+1\) steps requires either such a response now, or one common admissible inquiry whose every reachable observation branch supports success within \(n\). This is a standard finite robust planning decomposition, not a new theorem of control or a derivation of ethical criteria.

The following premises matter to its exact use:

- **No deadlock by vacuity.** The chosen inquiry must have at least one possible successor at every compatible starting configuration. Requiring a condition of every member of an empty transition set cannot make an unavailable inquiry successful. Failed attempts with effects must be represented by their actual transitions, rather than disappearing as undefined actions.
- **A common accessible choice.** Configurations in a branch represent the same complete available observation history. A single query is chosen from that history; it cannot depend on a hidden model identifier. Every observed distinction actually available to the policy must be represented, including informative availability or refusal signals.
- **Finite implementable branching at the claimed level.** A finite tree proof needs the stated finite observation/operation setting, or another argument supplying finite realization. Finite successor sets also preserve the stated finite domain of candidate sets. Existence of a branchwise mathematical selector does not install its storage, computation or use in the current learner. If only a restricted policy family can operate, quantify over that family rather than all conceivable lookup trees.
- **Inquiry effects and prefix conditions.** Admissibility must apply to the inquiry itself, not just its terminal result. Successor configurations must retain whatever earlier effects remain relevant: changed capacities, lost opportunities, continuing burdens, fulfilled requirements, authority and elapsed time. Where the judgment depends on the full history, include the relevant history in the configuration or the residual requirement. A good leaf cannot retroactively erase an impermissible prefix.
- **Terminal response semantics.** Membership in \(D(x)\) must include actual response feasibility and the required consequences, including every outcome when a robust guarantee over nondeterminism is claimed. A response label or existence of a favorable endpoint does not establish guaranteed control.
- **The inquiry/response distinction is functional.** An information-gathering action can already satisfy a requirement, change its applicability or cause an adverse effect. It must count when it does. The remaining response can then be empty where the independently specified completion condition licenses that; no second act is required merely because the first was named inquiry.

Under those contracts the induction is straightforward. A successful tree either ends in a common response or has one inquiry at its root. Its branch subtrees give the smaller-horizon conditions on evolved states. Conversely, a common admissible inquiry and successful subtrees compose to a successful tree because every actual successor appears in the matching branch. This establishes a conditional existence characterization. It does not derive the transition relation, class coverage, requirement or an operative learner that acquires and uses the criterion.

The bound counts the declared inquiry stages. It is not a universal time, energy or memory measure. Any such realization claim must retain its particular laws. Nor is worst-case success over all admitted nondeterministic outcomes identical to a probabilistic or risk-sensitive guarantee. The earlier T15–T18 results should be used at their own stated scope if that guarantee is intended.

## Static information refinement and changing activity differ

If observation only removes possibilities while every candidate retains the same response set, then deleting candidates can only enlarge their intersection. That is the earlier static result. It cannot be transferred unchanged to an encounter that moves the process, closes an opportunity, changes permission, or consumes conditions needed by the response. In those cases \(D(x')\) is a set at the resulting configuration, not the old \(D(x)\) with one model name removed.

Even an encounter with a correctly predicted result can change what needs to be distinguished next. The saved method history identified by the mechanism reviewer supplies a particularly clean source-level case. After readings `010`, its two remaining H3/H4 configurations agree about the next advance/read. Executing that step changes both cursors without eliminating either candidate, and they then disagree about the newly next reading. This is not loss of information about the fixed event just answered. It is a changed continuation and a changed relative question. A complete source-bound account must retain that distinction.

The separate exact-budget endpoint establishes a different limitation: a correct retained representation can coexist with no capacity for another native operation under that local law. This is the saved boundary fixture with a lower starting reserve: its recorded prior withdrawal is `167501/65536`, whereas ordinary `four_feedback` records no prior withdrawal and ends with that quantity remaining. It is not the ordinary history retroactively depleted at its endpoint. It does not establish that an earlier stop was available, justified or better. Such a comparative claim requires the real earlier state, a specified continuation purpose and its alternative feasible history. Combining a hypothesis set from one prefix with funds or permissions from another would not supply it.

## Grounding the requirement without defining the desired result

Writing \(D(x)\) does not determine why its members are acceptable. The underlying requirement must concern independently identified effects, relations or commitments, with a declared horizon and affected continuations. A factual target, permission, comparative adequacy and actual fulfillment remain different claims. They can be investigated within one process without being supplied by a detached observer or conflated with the learner's current score.

The [ClaimLedger execution theorem](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/recording/reason-replay/protection/claims/formal/ClaimLedger.lean), `partial_evidence_actual_execution`, already keeps actual membership, authority, execution and certificate soundness separate. Its persistence theorem also requires earlier certificates to remain sound after later events. The proposed dynamic account should preserve those distinctions. Missing certification is neither a false claim nor proof that fulfillment has not happened.

A substantive rival is a process that resolves its own classification problem while foreclosing the continuation that made inquiry pertinent. Another retains several possible models but has one response that preserves the relevant capability in each. These rival accounts differ in actual feasible consequences, not in freely reassigned approval predicates. The response-oriented account should recognize both: exact identification may be unnecessary in the latter case and practically insufficient in the former.

The reverse overreach also fails. A common acceptable immediate response does not imply that all further inquiry is wrong or unnecessary for every purpose. A future demand may distinguish the remaining models; further inquiry may itself be a warranted continuation. Likewise, an empty robust intersection does not create authority for harmful inquiry, erase an outstanding claim, or establish that waiting is harmless. The chosen target and guarantee must remain open to substantive criticism without being changed retrospectively to vindicate the implemented action.
