## 1. The precise connection tested

The semantic proposal concerns acquired organization making a claim's implications and grounds available across changes of use and challenge. Here we test one consequence: different changes should have appropriately different effects on judgment, assessed support and action. We do not define understanding as passing this battery.

The earlier `FormedGrounds` history already forms a decoder through two actual paired contacts, with inspection work, retained examples and later possible misinterpretation. This round imports that checked acquisition. It then projects the continuing practice into calibration records, two report slots, current assessments of their usability and a local purpose. Receiving a report, withdrawing reliance and changing purpose are events in that one history. The second slot does not by itself represent independent corroboration.

The projection preserves learned interpretation, the identity of retained reports, selective support withdrawal, conflict and differentiated response. It omits how source access is discovered, how reliance assessments become warranted, degrees of reliability, timing and costs, social participation, and the actual consequences of choosing. These omissions locate this model's boundary. They are not proof that the reconstructed theory cannot explain the omitted relations.

For the normative programme, the tested transition is **formed recognition and calibration of grounds → the factual understanding available to situated practical judgment**. It supplies an implementation constraint on recognition. It neither derives nor replaces G0, J1–J4 or E, and does not reopen their completed axiomatic argument.

## 2. Disclosed model choices and correspondence

| Element | Implemented relationship | Supplied condition or limit |

`Ground.supported` means that this bookkeeping procedure admits support. It does not mean the report is true or that accepting it is epistemically warranted. `Outcome.judgment` is an output of the model; it is not an independently established attribution of conscious judgment.

The code's two implementations receive the same records, calibration, usability assessments, purpose and event history. The first combines the two decoded reports directly. The conventional comparison computes whether there is admitted support for true and for false, then derives the same three support statuses and response. Neither has a target-truth oracle or additional observations.

## 3. Results and two corrections to the protocol

For illustration, let the retained proposition be that the relevant condition holds. The Boolean examples below vary both conventions and both contents in the checked statements, rather than proving only this wording.

| Change | Checked result | Consequence for the proposed test |
|---|---|---|
| Change the desired condition | Factual answer and support stay fixed; the selected response can reverse | Desire and factual commitment need not move together |
| Withdraw the only usable report | Suspend; retain its token and calibration | Loss of support is not evidence for the negation |
| Withdraw one of two agreeing usable reports | Keep the same definite answer through the other report | **Access loss need not cause suspension when an admitted alternative remains** |
| Add an opposite report while both are admitted | Suspend with conflict | **Contrary evidence does not automatically make the latest report win** |
| Withdraw one side of that conflict | Accept the remaining report under the declared aggregation rule | Correction requires a stated ground for excluding or subordinating competing support |
| Recode reports and the acquisition convention together | Preserve judgment, support and response | Raw shape alone cannot explain this transfer |
| Compare no support with conflict | Both suspend, but their support statuses differ | A single answer or action conceals a distinction the protocol needs to measure |

The third and fourth rows correct overbroad expectations in the earlier proposed protocol. The adequate target is conditional selective revision, not an automatic mapping from each perturbation label to one response. If a new route is already independently established as superior, an immediate revision can be warranted; the current equal-status model does not establish that superiority.

### Conventional comparison

The theorem `conventional_matches_all_states` proves equality of the whole `Outcome` for every state of this interface, including arbitrary calibration lists and usability assessments. `conventional_matches_every_history` consequently proves equality after every finite sequence of the defined events, from any initial state. This is stronger than agreement only on the finite executed cases.

Its philosophical scope is narrower than its mathematical quantifier might suggest. The two procedures share the decoder, state representation and event updater by design. The result establishes equivalence of these assessment implementations at that boundary. It does not compare two complete independently developed cognitive architectures, prove their full causal equivalence, or establish that one has no content. A conventional realization can instantiate the proposed relational account. The result blocks an inference from this test alone to exclusive explanatory necessity; it does not refute the theory's compatibility or relational explanation.

### A retained failure

`supported_copied_mistake` uses the inherited history in which a signal is reversed after accurate acquisition. The actual physical target remains false. Copying that misleading signal into both admitted report slots produces a definite true judgment classified as supported. This failure does not arise by freely toggling an unexplained `Understands` or `True` predicate; the false answer and its physical target come from the previously connected history.

The support mechanism works exactly as specified and still admits a false claim. Two records are not two independently adequate routes. This does not refute knowledge by showing that reports sometimes err. It identifies the explanatory work excluded from the current representation: warranted assessment of the routes by which records were produced, including shared vulnerability.

The shape-only diagnostic also fails under reversed encoding. This is an ablation of acquisition, not the strongest rival. The conventional comparison retains acquisition and passes.

## 5. Primary-source comparison and counterreview

Jon Doyle's *A Truth Maintenance System* describes recording reasons for program beliefs and revising those beliefs when assumptions change. The MIT record also identifies explanation and the guidance of action as uses of the retained reasons. This supports using a serious dependency-maintenance comparison rather than assuming selective revision belongs uniquely to our philosophical formulation. [Primary MIT record, AIM-521, 1979](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/5377b306-4ecc-4687-b1f5-78cbb4a0543a).

Reading scope is the primary repository abstract and bibliographic record, supplemented by the indexed text of Doyle's short 1977 IJCAI summary. A direct fetch of the author-hosted summary returned 404, and another proceedings PDF exceeded the browser tool's retrieval limit. This round does not claim to have read the complete 1979 paper or implemented Doyle's full system. Our two-support procedure is independently specified here; its proof follows its source code, not the authority of the citation. No new source-original file is imported. The previous neural-data checks remain credited and were not repeated.

**Is equivalence engineered?** Yes: the conventional comparison reconstructs the same admitted-support relationship through a different calculation. This deliberately tests the claim that the observable pattern alone distinguishes the philosophical account. It cannot settle a stronger comparison between rival acquisition, participation or experiential explanations. In particular, it is not a completed empirical test of Hutto and Satne's content-free account against a representational theory.

**Does the positive result merely restate definitions?** The aggregation policy and event updater are chosen. The checked consequences confirm their composition with the inherited acquired decoder and show exactly which invariants follow. They do not independently justify the policy as human reasoning or universal epistemology. The principal research advance is the corrected target and the exposed limitation, not treating a stipulated policy's consistency as semantic sufficiency.

**Are access and purpose secretly independent worlds?** No ontological independence is asserted. They name differentiated roles in one activity, isolated to identify causal consequences. The model omits their co-formation and the warrant for its interventions. Those omissions become explicit correspondence obligations; they are not concealed by the word nondual.

**Could understanding persist when the response fails?** Yes. The model prescribes assessment and response together; it does not model refusal, deception, fatigue or blocked execution. It therefore cannot infer lack of understanding from an actual person's failure to revise. Nor does success certify conscious experience.

**Does copied error defeat the full candidate?** No. The candidate already requires actual grounds and preservation of relevant failure pathways. The example shows why that requirement cannot be replaced by consistency among admitted records. A fuller account that explains the acquisition and warranted revision of reliance may resolve this model's omission.

This is substantive author-conducted counterreview. No independent-agent acceptance is claimed.

## 6. Next unresolved bridge

The next target is **acquired discrimination of source access and shared vulnerability → warranted, revisable reliance**. Reuse completed acquisition and review mechanisms. Add the relational difference this model omits: two reports can agree because two adequate routes converge, or because a single failure is copied. Distinct names or identical outputs cannot establish which history occurred.

A useful next comparison would hold the report tokens and immediate rewards fixed while varying inspectable production histories: genuinely separate checks, a copied report, a shared obstruction, and mistaken suspicion of copying. The account must explain how an acquired access distinction becomes available to assessment, when the distinction is warranted, and when the available evidence leaves the histories unresolved. A later outcome check can assess reliability without being supplied as an answer oracle during judgment. Independence must be relative to specified failure pathways, not an unqualified label.

What would change the account: a richer conventional or participation-based rival could explain the same acquisition and justified revision, reducing claims of distinctiveness further; or our proposed reliance criterion could endorse copied error or discard adequate corroboration under its own stated conditions, requiring revision. If the relevant histories are not discriminable through available inquiry, the account must preserve that uncertainty instead of asserting an omniscient whole perspective.

This narrows the frontier without repeating completed decoder formation or claiming that all knowledge requires explicit reflection about sources. General changes of practical dependence and responsibility in the original proposal remain outside this local preference model. Human or animal realization, exhaustive meaning, consciousness and necessity of the entire theory remain open.
