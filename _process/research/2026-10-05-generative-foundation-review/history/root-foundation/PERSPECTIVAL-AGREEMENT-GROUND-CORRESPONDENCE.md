# Agreement, stable commitment and the grounds of authority

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored conceptual and correspondence review following [the prospective-control integration](PERSPECTIVAL-PROSPECTIVE-CONTROL-INTEGRATION.md), the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) and [language correction](LANGUAGE-AND-FORMATION-CORRECTION.md). **Scope: philosophical reconstruction and constructed countercases; no new theorem, empirical result or completed universal grounding.**

**Finding:** agreement can create a real practical difference without creating every power subsequently claimed in its name. Binding commitment, absence of unilateral revocation, stable finality and immunity from all grounds are different claims. “Authority remains answerable to its grounds” supplies a substantive limit only when the ground, its scope and relevant changes receive an independent ethical explanation. Otherwise it restates the conclusion.

## 1. Agreement reorganizes one unfolding

Begin with people learning, communicating and participating in a practice. Through those relations they acquire capacities to understand a proposal, disclose uncertainty, decline, undertake a task and depend on another's undertaking. An agreement is an activity within that development. It can change who prepares, who refrains from making another arrangement, which performances become expected, and whose specified decision will settle a later matter. There is no need to begin with independently complete owners transferring a substance called authority.

Those differentiated consequences remain real. A communicated assurance is different from a private intention; accepting a proposal is different from enjoying its result; participation in setting terms differs from merely predicting them. Naming these relations describes how agreement can organize activity. It does not yet establish why an assurance deserves reliance, when acceptance creates an obligation, or what makes a decision authoritative.

The affirmative candidate must allow a further possibility: a warranted agreement **creates** a ground, rather than merely reporting pre-existing reasons or causing useful expectations. Someone can have a reason to perform because they undertook that performance, even when their present preference changes. Whether this force arises through fidelity, practical participation, assurance or a combination needs defense. Reducing all commitments to recovered costs or present aggregate advantage would prejudge that investigation.

## 2. Grant consent actual force in the countercase

Consider a shared archive containing personal letters. An informed participant freely accepts a lifelong undertaking not to disclose specified private material. The practice permits meaningful communication and sustained trust. For this constructed control, stipulate that the undertaking is warranted in its content and formation, and that the circumstances later considered do not supply a release or defeating reason. These ethical stipulations test an overstrong criterion; they do not prove every confidentiality agreement valid.

Years later, the participant prefers to publish a personally satisfying account. The initial work costs have been recovered, no new reliance is created that day, and the participant can competently explain the changed preference. Nevertheless, the stipulated undertaking remains a reason against the specified disclosure. Lack of continuous unilateral revocation is part of what the participants reasonably established. Its lifelong duration does not by itself erase meaningful participation in its formation.

This defeats a proposal that all continuing authority must be freshly endorsed, continuously revocable, or productive of additional benefits at each instant. It does not establish that others may use any method to prevent publication, inspect unrelated correspondence or dictate the participant's subsequent work. It also does not determine what follows under materially different circumstances, such as release by the relevant parties or a disclosure with another serious practical ground. Those changes require assessment, not automatic preservation or automatic annulment.

The example grants a genuine normative power locally. Calling it warranted does not establish its universal foundation; it prevents the present theory from defining commitment's force away while claiming to explain it.

## 3. Distinguish the powers a history may form

| Claim | Content requiring justification | What it does not itself establish |
|---|---|---|
| **Binding undertaking** | The participant has a reason or obligation concerning specified performance. | Permission for another to compel it by any means. |
| **No unilateral revocation** | A later preference or declaration alone does not end that undertaking. | Inability to fulfil, discharge, release or defeat it through a relevant change. |
| **Stable decision finality** | A warranted procedure can settle an issue despite continued disagreement. | Unlimited jurisdiction over other issues or immunity from defects outside its warranted settlement. |
| **Enforcement permission** | A particular response to nonperformance is warranted, with specified means and limits. | Every penalty, intervention or claimed remedy. |
| **Delegated discretion** | A role may determine some later terms within a defended jurisdiction. | Power to expand that jurisdiction merely by issuing a new demand. |

These distinctions do not deny that one agreement may confer several powers. If it does, their joint conferral must actually be explained. Conversely, inferring open-ended jurisdiction from a reason to keep one undertaking changes the conclusion without adding the required ground.

A scheduling control makes finality concrete. Participants can authorize a coordinator to choose a date among stated possibilities and accept that an announced date settles the matter. A disappointed participant's preferred date need not prevail; continual reopening can defeat the worthwhile coordination itself. The coordinator's selection need not maximize everyone's independent first preference. Yet this specific authority does not settle whether the coordinator may require unrelated future service. If broader discretion was expressly proposed and accepted, its broader warrant becomes the question; it cannot be refuted merely by pointing to the narrower control.

## 4. When “answerable to grounds” says something

There are three readings.

**Analytical reading:** legitimate authority extends exactly as far as legitimate grounds warrant. This is a useful reminder but supplies no criterion. Both the unlimited-self-binding rival and its critic can affirm it while assigning agreement different powers.

**Explanatory reading:** identify the actual content of the undertaking, its formation, participation, reliance, affected living and claimed decisions; explain why these relations give this role this authority. This makes the disagreement inspectable. It does not require public litigation of every judgment or each participant's continuous agreement.

**Substantive reading:** certain changes or claims cannot be covered by the authority that this particular ground constitutes. This can constrain a power, but only through a defended premise about that ground. A finite scheduling mandate excludes unrelated commands by its specified content. A fully disclosed lifelong ordinary-use veto presents the harder case: its content already includes broad control, so rejecting it requires an ethical limit on what agreement can authorize. Interpretation alone cannot supply that limit.

Stable finality can itself belong among the grounds. Answerability therefore need not mean that every dispute reopens the decision, that the affected individual retains a veto, or that authority survives only while it is instrumentally indispensable. A justified settlement can change which considerations settle action. Claiming immunity from **all** grounds is stronger: it treats the power's own assertion as sufficient irrespective of the ethically relevant conditions that establish its authority. Whether any particular proposed clause actually claims that stronger immunity must be demonstrated, not inferred from its duration or firmness.

## 5. Changes that the comparison must distinguish

| Later development | Exact question |
|---|---|
| Preference changes; the warranted undertaking and relevant circumstances remain. | Why would renewed preference alone discharge it? The lifelong control retains force here. |
| A decision settles a question within a warranted final procedure. | What does the settlement legitimately close? Continued disagreement need not reopen it. |
| A specified performance is completed or a warranted release occurs. | Which ground has thereby ended, and which distinct grounds remain? |
| Costs are recovered, but the agreed project or risk allocation continues. | Have all grounds ended, or only one instrumental explanation? |
| The holder introduces a demand outside the defended jurisdiction. | What additional authority supports the new demand? Effective enforcement cannot answer this alone. |
| A serious new circumstance appears. | Is it within the undertaking's accepted scope, an excuse, a defeating ground or grounds for another response? Novelty alone decides none of these. |

The last row preserves meaningful commitment under uncertainty. Calling every adverse development a new ground for revocation would undo the very allocation of risk that might have been warranted. Calling every imaginable development already authorized would assume the unlimited power at issue.

## 6. Universal contribution and remaining premises

The universal connection advanced is **whole formation → differentiated reasons and powers**, including reasons genuinely created by agreement. The account can explain why an unchanged preference is unnecessary for fidelity, why finality sometimes contributes to a good, and why a performance claim is not automatically jurisdiction over subsequent activity. These distinctions specify what a universal ground must explain across formed intelligences; they do not derive its ethical content.

The substantive inputs still require defense: the significance of practical participation and assurance; the normative power of an adequately formed agreement; the worth and limits of commitment and settled coordination; the grounds of release and defeat; and the additional conditions for enforcement or delegated discretion. The positive candidate locates these within what people actually form and live together. Calling them relational or immanent does not settle the self-binding rival's proposed scope.

No new mathematical result is needed. The existing [trajectory representation](../../../../experiments/valuation-development/lean/perspectival/README.md) can preserve supplied histories and comparisons; a ranking or a single consent flag would omit many of the distinctions above. Encoding “valid agreement” with exactly the desired permissions would prove only a conditional application. The next substantive test must defend why a proposed kind of agreement creates the asserted power, or why a specified power misdescribes the worthwhile participation, assurance and responsibility that could ground it. Mere appeal to answerability would not move the investigation beyond its present frontier.
