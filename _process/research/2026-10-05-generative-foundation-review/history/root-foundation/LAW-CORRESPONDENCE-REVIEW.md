## The precise connection being tested

Ren asks to finish the remaining supplied process law and correspondence after the unfolding-preservation study. The strongest interpretation compatible with the existing target is to justify the actual connections, not to remove theorem parameters cosmetically or rename a chosen simulator “actual unfolding.” The [foundation's corrected completion test](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ASSUMPTION-FREE-FOUNDATION.md#10-the-foundations-actual-content-fixes-its-completion-test) does not require endless continuation, one privileged notation or deduction of contingent physical constants from an empty context. It does require warrant for the foundational relationships and their claimed scope.

Three distinct obligations must therefore remain visible:

1. Derive a reduced process law and its correspondence from the unchanged native mechanism, without supplying exactness or a homomorphism as an additional proof premise.
2. Identify actual evidence for acquired activity participating in subsequent conditions, crediting the repository's completed empirical studies instead of repeatedly describing every correspondence as unestablished.
3. Preserve the stronger question of universal explanatory reach. Neither a derived abstraction nor several real instances alone establishes that universal result.

These are questions within one connected account. The native process, its summary and our investigation are not independent ontological domains. A mathematical projection can legitimately omit relations that are irrelevant to its declared future questions while remaining inadequate for different historical questions.

## Affirmative case and serious rivals

The formal proposal is a substantive improvement if the unchanged code proves that two native configurations with the same retained summary have the same successor summary for every original event. An effective step can then be derived from native transitions, and every effective history must lift from the actual chosen native start. The lifting requirement is important: an existentially collected edge relation can otherwise splice edges from incompatible representatives and invent a history. Explicit representative independence prevents that defect; it must be proved, not placed in a structure field.

This warrants the reduced law within the native construction. It does not explain why that architecture is physically instantiated or why its comparison operations have universal authority. A globally constrained account that preserves the same events, use of acquired differences and failure relations remains an equally admissible articulation. The result must not exclude it merely for lacking sequential vocabulary.

The empirical continuation has stronger grounds than another constructed example. The archived TCLab code reads the acquired temperature, passes it and retained integral state into the command calculation, then issues the resulting command. The published records and independent rational replay support that particular computational connection. A prearranged sequence matching only the logged outputs omits the inspected calculation and the publication's source provenance; to remain a rival to the full target it must contest or account for that evidence. Publication supports the reported historical realization; this review did not independently observe those laboratory runs or certify every firmware instruction. These records also do not identify the thermal response to every changed command. The preserved failure of residual transfer is directly relevant and must remain.

The separately studied oxygen-gradient experiments connect bacterial activity, formed oxygen conditions and changed selection through measured profiles and interventions. They supply a natural mechanism instance and already have a conventional niche-construction explanation, which deserves full credit. The respiration inhibitor is not perfectly specific, the assays are not one continuously followed trajectory, and neither a competitive-fitness ratio nor a growth summary determines an unrelated life-cycle survival probability. Those limitations narrow particular transfers; they do not erase the affirmative evidence that activity changes subsequent conditions.

## Defeat and revision tests

The formal claim fails if any native event makes equal summaries diverge, if an effective trace has no lift from a matching original start, or if an alleged omitted field affects a retained question. The historical claim fails if a future-sufficient summary is advertised as reconstructing acquisition provenance that the earlier equal-endpoint counterexample demonstrably loses.

The empirical claim requires revision if the archived controller calculation does not match the commands or does not use the alleged measured/retained input, or if the measured gradient and intervention results are misreported. A rival preserving the entire independently identified relation may give a different mechanistic or global explanation. Agreement on a short record alone does not establish such full equivalence, while agreement on all supported questions does not warrant rejecting a rival just for using different vocabulary.

The universal foundational claim would need a further argument establishing the reach of these relationships across its declared class, including static, finite, destructive and differently organized cases. Requiring a separately assumed transition relation to disappear from one local theorem is not a substitute for that argument. Equally, the mere presence of a parameter in a theorem does not establish that its supplied value lacks evidential warrant.
