## Stable concern can require changing the undertaking

The next step should not require every ultimate purpose to change. The [practice-transformation argument](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md#3-what-changes-when-the-aspiration-remains-stable) already explains how a stable aspiration can require replacing a practice whose own formative effects have changed its role. The [applicability reconstruction](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-INTEGRATION.md) likewise shows restoration removing the condition for repair while other conditions of continuation remain active. This round preserves those results rather than inventing a new requirement of perpetual value revision.

An undertaking is used here for an identifiable organization of activity toward a particular continuation. This is a local explanatory description, not an intention attributed to every generative process. Its pertinence depends on the actual relation between what it does and the continuing activity concerned. That relation can change through fulfillment, changed needs, revealed consequences or obstruction. The activity can therefore retire, redirect or constrain a particular undertaking while sustaining a wider concern.

An explanatory account must preserve three distinct possibilities. The undertaking's occasion may have ended; its occasion may remain while its means have become inappropriate; or appropriate means may remain but be unavailable. An internal completion label, declining activity or exhausted support does not decide which happened. The same outward cessation can express successful completion, warranted restraint or failure. Those differences lie in the activity and its continuing relations, not solely in an observer's label.

## Correct knowledge of one relation does not settle its application

The original [abstraction acquisition](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION.md) sets out both a prediction task and a later productive-use operation before its outcomes are examined. The [source reconstruction](APPLICABILITY-REVALUATION-MECHANISM.md) follows the already preserved inverted-sensor case: a model can correctly predict its actual observation channel while a use based on that channel fails. This counterexample was already reported in the original study; it is not a newly discovered failure or evidence that all prediction is useless.

The important distinction is between the accurate observation claim and the additional claim connecting that observation to the intended effect. A failed effect can challenge the latter without falsifying the former. Revising the application may therefore require a further consequential distinction, a different contact, restricted use, or justified uncertainty. Erasing the accurate model is not automatically the right correction. Nor is automatically reversing its interpretation: a blocked route can produce failure without a polarity reversal.

This yields a conditional adequacy argument. If an undertaking's independently established role is to enable a continuation, a criterion that declares success while omitting a demonstrated obstruction to that continuation is insufficient for that role. The prior relation does the work. An analyst cannot first observe a costly predictor and then assign it an undeclared obligation to finance itself. The previous ample assay's expenditure does not refute its stated prediction undertaking.

This is a relationship within the unfolding: sensing, acquired prediction, attempted use and its effects occur in the same history. A local description can preserve the sensory relation while omitting the use relation. Its omission does not show that significance must be added to reality from outside. It shows what an account claiming adequacy for that use must also explain.

## A substantive test of acquired applicability

The next implementation or empirical investigation should fix the continuing activity and its connection to the undertaking before assessing success. It should then distinguish these alternatives:

| Account of changed activity | Discriminating requirement |
|---|---|
| Present conditions alone change performance | Compare adequately characterized present conditions while varying the relevant consequence history; do not demand identical complete states, which would erase the acquired difference being studied. |
| Earlier action strengths alone govern repetition | Revalue or newly encounter the consequence without reinforcing the target action, then assess its subsequent use. |
| A retained consequence changes application | Identify the retained influence and its subsequent use; an intervention on that route must preserve the distinction between storage, access and actual response. |
| A local success signal conceals failure of the undertaking's role | Preserve the prior role and test the actual continuing effect independently of the signal. |
| Appropriate persistence | Include a case where relevant consequences remain favorable or the original need remains, so indiscriminate abandonment does not count as successful revision. |

These tests do not require a separate evaluator outside the process or an endlessly changing higher controller. They require the claimed relation to be operative and appropriately identified. A supplied local law can realize that connection; explaining the formation and scope of that law is a further question, not a reason to deny its realized effect.

## Connection to concern, governance and the remaining foundation

Learning which consequence now governs a response and establishing what response is warranted are related questions with different evidential requirements. Learned aversion, attraction or priority can be causally effective without being correct or just. Equally, a justified continuing concern need not be reconstructed as a newly chosen purpose at every step. The [concern-and-purpose work](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md) and [relief/reconsideration account](RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-INTEGRATION.md) retain the arguments about grounds, authority, differentiated consequences and appropriate persistence.

For governance, the corresponding application is conditional: when a procedure is warranted through a particular role, its own indicators cannot exhaust evaluation of whether it still performs that role. Who can challenge, interpret and revise the procedure, what ongoing claims survive revision, and how capture is detected remain substantive parts of its actual organization. This is an application of the adequacy argument, not a newly established institutional mechanism or universal ethical theorem.

The advance is a more precise, evidence-constrained positive account of acquired applicability. It removes an unnecessary demand for revision of every ultimate purpose and supplies a concrete empirical comparator for consequence-dependent revaluation. It preserves the real native implementation gap rather than filling it with an observer's favorable score.

The next central connection is **an actual acquired effect that changes the undertaking's use relation → an operative revision of that undertaking or its inquiry scope in the same supported history**. A useful construction must retain the accurate prior knowledge, unresolved alternative explanations, consumed conditions and legitimate live demands. A desired mode switch or a manually changed target does not supply that history. The wider no-assumptions foundation, universal concern and completed alignment remain open.
