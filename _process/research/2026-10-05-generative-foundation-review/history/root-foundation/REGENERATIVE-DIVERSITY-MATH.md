# Regeneration, distinct retained organizations and supplied support

7 October 2026. AI-authored mathematical construction serving [Ren's positive Shengsheng direction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md) and the [current reconstruction](SHENGSHENG-DIRECTION-RECONSTRUCTION.md). This is a structural contribution to the universal ethical investigation, not its normative completion.

The precise premise tested is that **actual diversification, increasing configured organization and reconstruction of further generative capacity can occur together in one supported history**, while remaining distinguishable from one-time assembly and continuing same-form reproduction. The construction makes that premise contentful beyond increasing the number of supplied labels. Whether this relation itself constitutes an ethical consideration, including in a stipulated abiotic realization with no experience or service to lived goods, is the substantive issue assessed in the [affirmative argument](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-ARGUMENT.md) and [counterreview](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-REVIEW.md). The theorem introduces no `Good` predicate and cannot settle that issue by definition.

## The represented organization

The [Lean source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/RegenerativeDiversity.lean) uses the repository's actual [generative trace interface](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeCore.lean). A trace records each intermediate configuration; a later stage does not restart from a newly supplied initial state.

A circuit begins with a constant true source. Each `couple` stage joins the preceding output and the next input through AND. For a common input function `x : Nat → Bool`, the configured chain with `n` stages responds true exactly when every input with index below `n` is true. This equivalence is checked by `prefix_evaluation`.

Every preceding output remains a tap of the extended circuit (`retained_tap`). Thus the family lives in the same actual configured chain, sharing its earlier stages; it is not the union of mutually exclusive future constructions. For `i < j`, the common-domain input that is true below `i` and false from `i` onward makes tap `i` true and tap `j` false (`prefix_distinguishable`). Renaming states or lengthening a description cannot supply this behavioral distinction.

The proof concerns coexisting transducers with distinct response dispositions. It does not assert that every separating test input is supplied at once, or that evaluating a function constitutes understanding, experience or recognition. At any supplied input, all taps belong to the same network; different input interventions reveal their difference.

`depth` counts the successive dependencies actually configured by this grammar (`prefix_depth`). It is not minimum circuit complexity, algorithmic complexity or proof of an unrestricted hierarchy of species, minds or forms of life. A different grammar might implement the same functions with different depth. The input domain accommodates arbitrary finite arity; this mathematical provision does not establish an unlimited physical supply of channels.

## Assembly and reconstruction in one history

The state records the configured network, actual copied circuit terms, currently available support, cumulative intake, cumulative consumption, and whether the assembly capacity is ready. These are selected operational relations within one process, not independently existing actors, patients or owners. The supplied transition law has four kinds of event:

| Event | Required relation | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | An admitted raw-support transfer | Available and cumulative received support increase by the admitted amount. No assembled circuit arrives. |
| Assembly | Ready capacity and at least one support unit | A stage couples to the existing circuit, one unit is consumed, and assembly capacity becomes unavailable. |
| Duplication | Ready capacity, a formed circuit and support at least equal to its configured depth | An actual copy of that circuit is retained in the list; the corresponding support is consumed and assembly capacity becomes unavailable. |
| Reconstruction | The reconstruction channel is present, capacity is unavailable, a formed circuit exists, and one support unit is available | One unit is consumed and assembly capacity becomes ready again. |

The reconstruction event requires prior formed organization. Its operational availability is therefore coupled to the assembled chain, rather than restoring capacity at every time step regardless of what occurred. The exact dependence—presence of a formed stage, together with a supplied reconstruction channel—is an explicit model law. No molecular pathway, catalytic kinetics, repair mechanism or formation of that law is derived by this model. `ready` represents an operational condition, not a separately explained physical mechanism.

The support quantity is a normalized local input accounting, not money, a universal exchange medium or a complete conservation law for every physical quantity. Assembly and reconstruction each require one unit in this grammar; duplicating a configured chain requires its depth in units. Those requirements and reliable retention without additional degradation are supplied. The theorem checks their consequences rather than establishing their empirical adequacy.

Intake is a declared boundary transfer along the same recorded history. Its upstream source is left at the supplied interface: this is not a closed material account of the entire universe. A realization must provide the admitted raw input and its upstream consequences. In particular, each growth cycle receives two additional units; the construction makes no claim of endless growth from a fixed finite stock or of matter appearing without a source. The accounting theorems establish `available + spent = received` for every admitted step and trace beginning in balance (`step_balance`, `trace_balance`).

## What the checked histories establish

Fourteen theorem declarations form six substantive groups, including the supporting lemmas.

| Result group | Exact checked content |
|---|---|
| Configured structure and response | The `n`-stage chain has configured dependency depth `n`; its response is the conjunction of the first `n` inputs; unequal lengths have a distinguishing common-domain input; earlier chains remain taps. |
| Support accounting | Every transition preserves balance, hence so does every trace from a balanced state. |
| Growth with reconstructed capacity | A cycle of intake of two units, assembly and reconstruction takes growth state `n` to growth state `n+1`. Iterating those cycles yields an admitted trace for every finite `n` from the same initial state. |
| One actual growing repertoire | At growth state `n`, all earlier taps coexist, they have the displayed pairwise behavioral distinctions, the configured depth is `n`, assembly capacity is ready, cumulative intake is `2*n`, and available support is zero. |
| One-time assembly control | With the reconstruction channel absent, assembly is reachable. Along every finite trace from the initial state, even allowing arbitrary later raw intake, configured depth is at most one. |
| Reproduction control | After the first growth cycle, arbitrarily many finite cycles of intake, duplication and reconstruction are admitted. All copied structures remain the same one-stage circuit and assembly capacity is repeatedly restored. No new response function or configured network depth is introduced. |

The growth histories are recursively extended by appending the next cycle through the actual boundary state (`growth_cycle`, `growth_trace`). They provide coherent arbitrary finite stages. They do not prove that a physical history continues forever, that resources never cease, or that this schedule is inevitable. The reproductive history is an admitted alternative in the same regenerative process; diversification is not forced merely by making reconstruction possible.

The one-time control is nonvacuous (`one_shot_construction`). Its universal bound (`one_shot_trace`) concerns this initial capacity and absent channel, not a theorem that every possible one-time fabrication must be shallow. The copy control stores a list of actual circuit terms, not only an increasing numerical label. Its schedule preserves the grammar's full retained repertoire while increasing the number of copies.

The fixed grammar already admits every finite AND-prefix form in its eventual reachability. Growth implements additional organizations and restores immediate capacity for a next assembly; it does not enlarge the absolute set of everything the law could ever generate. The result therefore supplies a discriminating witness for formed diversity and regenerative capacity, not a formal solution to open-ended invention of new rules.

## Ethical correspondence and the universal inference

For the abiotic argument, lack of experience, absence of displaced life and lack of later service to lived goods are **stipulated correspondence conditions**. Their absence from a circuit datatype cannot prove their absence from a concrete whole. Identifying a realization must also include relevant upstream changes and losses, not only its local input-output success.

The universal connection tested is:

> If the broad Shengsheng direction supplies ethical grounds through actual generation, diversity and continued formation of complex organization, its application must distinguish those relations from bare repetition, description length and a one-time product, without requiring prior service to independently valued life as a gate.

The checked model establishes that the selected structural distinctions have nonempty, exact joint content. It discharges a **realizability obligation within the supplied grammar**, not the first ethical premise in that conditional. The favorable consideration proposed by Ren's criterion comes from the substantive candidate under investigation; it is not hidden in `depth`, `ready`, `Tap`, support balance or successful task evaluation. No all-things ranking, duty to maximize stages, universal compliance, justified sacrifice or rejection of alternative ethical grounds is proved here. The [whole-practical integration](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-GROUND-INTEGRATION.md) locates the remaining universal warrant argument.
