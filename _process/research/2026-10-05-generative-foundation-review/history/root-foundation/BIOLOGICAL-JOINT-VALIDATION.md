# Specification for the remaining joint biological validation

8 October 2026. **Unexecuted measurement and analysis specification**, following [Ren's full-validation choice](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-relations-across-scales.md) and [the empirical results and limits](BIOLOGICAL-RENEWAL-INTEGRATION.md). This is neither preregistration nor evidence that an experiment has occurred. Laboratory implementation and any required institutional review belong to the participating researchers.

## Question and experimental unit

Test whether an acquired bodily response changes subsequent contact/dispersal, viable microbial growth and later living organization in a single defined worm–bacterial preparation. First establish the learning effect and observation maps **in that preparation**. The Pv1, PA14, commensal calibration and OP50-dispersal datasets are candidate precedents, not interchangeable populations. Do not assume their genotypes, RNA signals, reporters, growth parameters or errors transport.

The unit for allocation and primary uncertainty is the independently prepared plate/cohort, with experimental day and lineage retained. Individual tracks and neurons remain nested observations. Where measurement destroys an animal or interrupts movement, allocate matched sacrificial cohorts before outcomes are known; validate their comparability. Pooling embryos does not create an individual parent–descendant history.

All sample IDs must map to a real source record. A missing measure is missing; no generated values or cross-paper individual joins fill it. Record failed and ended histories as outcomes. Specify the actual finite horizon; no finite trial establishes endless renewal.

## Discriminating comparisons

Separate acquisition from present exposure with matched current conditions; compare an established acquisition/pathway perturbation with its appropriate control and rescue if justified in this preparation. Measure both the neural/tissue response and behavior because reporter elevation alone can dissociate from inherited avoidance. A molecular intervention may also change development or locomotion; measure those competing routes instead of declaring it a pure memory intervention.

Compare freely moving histories with appropriate exposure/dispersal controls. An azide-trapped choice cannot itself produce the subsequent movement loop. If used to validate initial choice, treat it as a separate matched assay and test correspondence to the beginning of an untrapped trajectory. Identify the consequences of assay-induced paralysis rather than extrapolating its endpoint to later occupancy.

Disentangle deposited viable cells, subsequent growth and consumption. Establish whether physical dispersal alone explains later growth, whether acquired activity adds a consequence, and whether the consequence returns through subsequent contact and reproduction. Retain nutrient and geometry dependence. Controlled finite growth and repeated renewal under measured replenishment are distinct endpoints.

## Analysis fixed before the confirmatory data

Use calibration/training days to specify observation and transition maps. Freeze coefficients, preprocessing, exclusions, the main horizon, missingness handling and primary endpoints before opening held-out days. Determine sample size from pilot plate-level variance and a biologically justified target effect/error; no arbitrary sample number or tolerance is installed here. If these choices remain unspecified, the study remains exploratory.

Compare current-condition, retained-history and equally expressive conventional coupled models using the same measured inputs, split and computational opportunity. Predict both next activity/contact and later viable microbial conditions; a good behavior-classifier score alone is insufficient. Hold out entire days/plates, then separately assess a predeclared intervention or changed condition. Use clustered uncertainty and sensitivity to missing/ended histories. Distinguish prediction, causal intervention and population-level transport.

Passage requires reproducible calibration, discrimination of the specified rival, accurate declared joint predictions, appropriate interventions and replication within the claimed scope. Improvement on one average loss is not enough if crucial feedback or loss endpoints fail. Report success/failure by endpoint and condition; do not widen tolerances after testing to secure success. If a conventional explanation accounts for all tested relations, credit it; do not claim that these experiments establish the exclusive necessity of our vocabulary.

## Formal and empirical completion

Lean can check a new mathematical inference once exact empirical premises and correspondence have been stated; it cannot create or certify unmeasured biological premises. Statistical residual intervals, mechanism-supported hard bounds and practical tolerances require different warrants. The existing [organized-loop proof](ORGANIZED-SCALES-INTEGRATION.md) remains a conditional construction.

The full-validation target should be revisited when the linked records exist and have been tested, not when this document is saved. Current missing prerequisites are a defined experimental preparation with demonstrated acquisition and ecological feedback together, calibrated observations in it, identified independent cohorts and the resulting joint measurements. No laboratory execution or author contact has been initiated.
