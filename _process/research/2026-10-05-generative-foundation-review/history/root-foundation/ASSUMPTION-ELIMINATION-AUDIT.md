## 1. What changes

Making a premise explicit is necessary for inspecting it, but does not eliminate it. A declared physical law, construction recipe, valuation rule or ethical ground remains an explanatory dependency wherever the conclusion uses it and the account under review has not established its support. Here a locally supplied input is not thereby outside generative reality; the audit must determine what the connected account already explains and what it still leaves unresolved. Moving that dependency into a definition, a type, a choice of admissible worlds or a theorem's quantifiers is not a derivation.

The earlier [premise ledger](../../../../theories/becoming/history/FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md) and [deeper-root account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md) defend a weaker, conditional standard: expose every commitment and identify what follows from it. Their results remain legitimate at that scope. That standard is insufficient as the final response to this directive.

The new requirement does not license deleting hypotheses from theorem statements, declaring an unexplained premise an axiom, or replacing a general claim with a favorable example. Nor does it license announcing that assumption-free grounding is universally impossible: the existing results establish narrower obstructions.

## 2. What Lean can and cannot certify here

A closed proof of “for every process P, if H(P), then C(P)” establishes an implication. It need not use any custom axiom, but it does not establish H for an actual process. A universal theorem without such a restriction may express a genuine structural invariant; quantification over a variable is not by itself a hidden hypothesis. Its explanatory scope must still match the conclusion advertised.

An empty Lean axiom-dependency report means the proof term has no declared axiom dependencies within the underlying proof system. It does not certify the proof system's own justification, derive the intended interpretation of a definition, establish an empirical correspondence, or eliminate a hypothesis that appears in the theorem's type.

Definitions are legitimate mathematical constructions. The problem arises when a stipulated definition is used as the explanation of why reality has that structure. Likewise, indexing an account by arbitrary laws can give universal coverage without deriving which law governs an actual system.

Ren did not grant an exception for logic or definitions. This audit therefore keeps the formal framework visible as a dependency to be understood; it does not declare that dependency discharged. Literal freedom from every presupposition has not been demonstrated.

## 3. The gap in the distinction-only route: distinctions do not select a dynamics

The actual [Lean core](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeCore.lean) defines a process by accepting a relation from state, action and next state to a proposition. Finite traces then compose events admitted by that relation. This derives closure properties from a supplied dynamics; it does not derive that dynamics.

The existing [dynamic-underdetermination source](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DynamicUnderdetermination.lean) fixes the same Boolean state carrier and unit action carrier, then defines three incompatible processes: no events, identity events, and toggling events. Its theorem “same_carrier_incompatible_laws” distinguishes the laws. “event_without_state_distinction” also exhibits an event in a singleton state space. Thus:

- a carrier of distinctions alone does not determine whether or how transitions occur;
- representing activity does not by itself force state differentiation;
- ruling out these alternatives requires content beyond the common carrier.

The [product-tower results](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/ProductTower.lean) similarly preserve the seed's nonemptiness and singleton limitation. Repeated products do not independently generate alternatives from an empty or singleton seed.

These are relative underdetermination results, not a theorem against every possible richer account of generativity. They challenge particular entailments, such as deriving occurrence or change from a carrier alone. They do not require the universal theory to exclude static systems or choose one dynamics for every system: a genuine structural result may cover all three. The existing [verification receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/verification.json) records the earlier successful Lean check; this audit inspected source and did not rerun or change that proof.

### A stronger finite test of proposed structural grounds

The new [DynamicSymmetry module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DynamicSymmetry.lean) proves that the same static and toggle laws are both total and deterministic, have unique predecessors, permit reversal of each step using the same action label, and preserve their full event relation under every bijection of the two states. Identity and Boolean negation witness that the quantified renaming class is nonempty and nontrivial. The laws nevertheless differ, and no state-label map identifies their step relations.

Thus these structural requirements do not derive nontrivial change or select between the two dynamics. This is a counterexample on a two-state carrier, not a characterization of all symmetric dynamics or an impossibility theorem for deeper grounding. The [scope and reproduction note](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DYNAMIC-SYMMETRY.md) and [separate verification receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/dynamic-symmetry-verification.json) document a fresh Lean 4.29.1 build with 20 new and 27 inherited audited declarations, all reporting empty axiom dependencies. It preserves the old countermodels and their original verification record. Independent source review accepts the finite theorem's scope; it supplies no empirical interpretation or missing generative law.

## 5. How to advance without hiding the gap

For each dependency, trace what supports it and whether that support independently explains the target. A derivation from another unexplained law merely moves the open obligation. A cycle of mutually conditional implications does not by itself supply independent warrant.

Three outcomes must remain distinct:

1. **Local discharge:** derive the needed content through the proposed generative account, tracing its upstream dependencies. This removes the premise at one connection; full assumption elimination requires that no independently supplied commitment remain doing explanatory work. Merely justifying or relocating an upstream assumption does not establish that stronger result.
2. **Regime classification:** derive when a constructive or destructive outcome follows, leaving the actual regime's determination explicit. This improves the account but does not alone satisfy the assumption-free requirement.
3. **Revision or withdrawal:** restrict or abandon a conclusion whose necessity the account cannot establish. Preserve the failed claim and counterexample.

For the distinction-only route, audit distinction → occurrence → changing conditions separately, using the idle/static/toggle alternatives as tests of its advertised implications. This does not prescribe the starting sequence for the whole foundation. The current [nondual continuation](NONDUAL-TREE-CONTINUATION.md) begins with the proposed generative unfolding and reconstructs its actual relationships before assessing what its representation omits or leaves unexplained. Its stronger claims still require support and genuine revision conditions; calling supplied content immanent does not by itself explain it. In parallel, the [strongest-foundation investigation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/STRONGEST-FOUNDATION-CLAIM.md) and [ethical-minimum inquiry](../../../../theories/becoming/history/UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) must track the grounds of practical significance without converting a desired conclusion into the definition of agency.

A result would change the account if it either justified discriminating dynamical content from the proposed root without importing that content, or showed precisely that an advertised entailment fails across admissible alternatives. The former advances the derivation; the latter requires narrowing the claim. Neither a completed local example nor a clean Lean build alone settles the full requirement.
