# Generator and retention selection: resource, information and causal review

6 October 2026. AI-authored independent review of **retained revised prediction → further use or controller modification → consequence-sensitive selection of generation and retention policies**. This follows the [model-family revision review](MODEL-FAMILY-REVISION-REVIEW.md) within the generative-grounding derivation.

**Decision:** the boundary audit is verified. The actual primary endpoints can make another confirmed prediction but cannot pay the stipulated final retention charge. Other completed configurations have different reserves, including two that can complete one more maintained use. The separate logical audit proves that no one-bit record can answer both independent future bit questions exactly on every history. Neither result implements a selector over generators or retention policies. That positive explanatory transition remains unbuilt.

## 1. Preserve the actual endpoints and the two alternative assays

The [protocol](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/PROTOCOL.md) replays the existing acquisition, access and revision chain. It does not retire that result and substitute a fresh task with a new reserve. The actual successful access branch has already consumed its last source packet before revision. Every completed revision endpoint considered here has source zero.

The two named revision continuations retain different actual payloads:

| Endpoint | Retained model | Reading parity | Current stored state | Live cells | Fuel |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Unchanged apparatus | Learned three-cycle, outputs `(0,0,1)` | 1 | 0 | 33 | `72475/65536` |
| Declared phase insertion | Generated cycle `0→1→3→2→0`, outputs `(0,0,1,0)` | 1 | 2 | 35 | `64123/65536` |

The physical state agrees with the stored state at these two handoffs. This agreement is verified by the external audit; the continuation predictor receives only its retained model, parity, cursor and native command callback. It does not receive the apparatus's hidden target or state. The old raw histories remain external records, not new learner inputs.

Two assays branch separately from each endpoint. One attempts the previously specified extra-controller installation; the other continues the already installed forward-use routine. They are alternative continuations, not two purchases from a shared reserve. An accepted installation adds eight cells under its supplied recipe; it does not thereby construct a working metaselector. The use assay does not reinstall or reacquire a model that is already retained.

These assays are externally requested comparisons. The study does not derive an internal decision to run one, a reason to prefer one, or autonomous scheduling of repeated use. Replaying the actual endpoint is a way to specify and verify an alternative continuation, not a physical rewind with later knowledge preserved.

## 2. Installation is unavailable at both named endpoints, not at every endpoint

The unchanged installation recipe charges fee 1, eight work units, eight new persistent cells, and rent on old cells, new cells and eight temporary cells for nine clock ticks. Under the existing local tariff its exact bill is

\[
C_{\mathrm{install}}(L)
=1+\frac{16}{64}+\frac{9(L+16)}{65536}.
\]

For the named three-cycle endpoint this is `82361/65536`, exceeding the actual reserve by `4943/32768`. For the named four-cycle endpoint it is `82379/65536`, exceeding the reserve by `1141/4096`. Denial occurs before installation changes storage or spends its proposed bill. The four-cycle cannot pay even the base fee; the three-cycle can pay that fee alone but cannot pay the whole specified operation.

The eight completed configurations among the preceding fourteen do not all have those same reserves. Two four-cycle configurations starting the revision assay at state 0 retain `144369/65536` fuel. They can pay this blank installation. The other six cannot. There is no justification for promoting the two primary failures to a universal impossibility of retrofitting a controller.

All prices are the declared symbolic machine costs. They are not measured energy or an empirical claim about the minimum physical cost of installing an arbitrary selector. A cheaper representation or a different already installed operation would require its own analysis.

## 3. Existing use has a stronger, more informative boundary

The installed forward-use sequence pays for planning, native action, cursor commitment, native reading, comparison, and final retention. The three-cell plan workspace remains charged across the intervening native commands and writes. Its total completed-use cost, with constant live storage \(L\), is

\[
C_{\mathrm{use}}(L)
=\frac{L+40}{64}+\frac{12L+34}{65536}.
\]

For 33 live cells this is `37591/32768`; for 35 it is `38627/32768`. Both exceed the named actual reserves, but the order of payments matters:

| Named endpoint | Paid further-use prefix | Remaining fuel | Denied final retention | Paid prediction comparison |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Three-cycle | `10075/16384` | `32175/65536` | `17441/32768` | Correct |
| Four-cycle | `315/512` | `23803/65536` | `18467/32768` | Correct |

Thus successful fresh prediction and affordable maintained use are different conditions. Neither branch is unable to do anything. Both can move the apparatus, commit the new cursor, acquire a reading and pay the comparison before the final denial.

The denial means that the stipulated maintenance/retention operation was not completed. The implementation does not erase the selected model or simulate its spontaneous destruction. Its updated fields and external receipt can still exist in the interrupted state. A false per-invocation retention flag must not be paraphrased as proof that every historical distinction has vanished. Conversely, a previous successful-retention flag must not certify the new invocation. The wrapper explicitly resets the invocation's retention, confirmation and receipt fields while marking the earlier acquisition as historical.

Across all eight fully retained in-family endpoints, the verified outcomes are:

| Endpoints, distinguished by revision boundary state | Count | Further fully retained uses | Further confirmed predictions | Terminal status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Three-cycle, initial state 1, either parity | 2 | 0 | 0 | Native action unaffordable after planning |
| Three-cycle, initial state 2, either parity | 2 | 0 | 1 | Final retention unaffordable |
| Four-cycle, initial state 2, either parity | 2 | 0 | 1 | Final retention unaffordable |
| Four-cycle, initial state 0, either parity | 2 | 1 | 2 | Second further use reaches comparison, then final retention is unaffordable |

These are counts over the stated finite configurations, not probabilities. The other six preceding configurations never completed their previous retention contract and are recorded as excluded endpoints. They are not silently promoted to successful handoffs.

No further packet arrives, and no productive operation occurs here. The branch identity remains

\[
145=89+C_{\mathrm{acquisition}}+C_{\mathrm{access}}+
W_{\mathrm{access}}+C_{\mathrm{revision}}+
C_{\mathrm{continuation}}+F_{\mathrm{remaining}}+S_{\mathrm{remaining}}.
\]

The installation alternative and use alternative each satisfy this identity separately. With finite stock, no new income and a strictly positive minimum native-command charge, indefinite repetition is impossible under this law. This is a conditional accounting result. It is not a universal theorem against renewal, cheaper methods or other physical support mechanisms.

## 4. Repeated finite agreement still does not establish family coverage

The previous outside-family five-cycle case from state 1 incorrectly retained a four-cycle model. The continuation preserves that actual five-cycle environment rather than replacing it with the selected representation. It completes one more maintained use and confirms two further predictions before failing its next retention payment. No prediction error occurs within that funded continuation.

This is a substantive negative control: using a wrong model again need not expose its error before the fuel runs out. Calling the model wrong comes from the auditor's knowledge of the supplied environment, not from an error signal secretly passed to the controller. The result does not show that every wrong model will survive every finite test.

The fourteen admitted three/four-cycle phase/parity possibilities have fourteen distinct six-reading signatures. Their first five readings produce only twelve distinct signatures. This verifies the earlier family-relative separation result. The delayed-continuation construction in the [proof note](META-REVISION-MATH.md) shows separately that any finite observed prefix is also compatible with infinitely many distinct finite-state continuations when the state bound is unrestricted. A fixed finite generated family cannot infer unrestricted coverage from that finite evidence alone.

The conclusion concerns finite generated candidates and finite testing. It does not deny that a finite program can describe an infinite model class or enumerate all finite automata given unbounded resources. Description, generation, identification and affordable use are different obligations.

## 5. The information countermodel supplies no reward or chosen policy

The separate logical assay has four histories \((x,y)\in\{0,1\}^2\), one retained bit, and a later question asking either \(x\) or \(y\). It checks all sixteen deterministic history encoders and all sixteen decoder pairs, for 256 complete designs. None answers both questions correctly on every history. The best total coverage is six of the eight history/question pairs.

This is a counting result about answers, not a uniform empirical demand distribution, a controller accuracy score, a delivered resource or a fitness measure. The executable apparatus does not select among these logical designs. The enumeration neither gives it a preferred future question nor wires logical correctness to fuel.

The impossibility requires the one-bit bound to include every later-accessible source of information about the history. A hidden retained transcript, history-dependent fuel level, timer, physical trace or uncounted shared random seed cannot be used to evade the bound. Subject to that accounting, the underlying theorem is simple: exact answers to all declared future questions require distinct memories for distinct joint answer profiles. Here the four profiles cannot fit in two states.

Retaining \(x\) answers every \(x\)-question; retaining \(y\) answers every \(y\)-question. This does not prove that no policy is best for a specified task law or cost criterion. It rules out a single one-bit design that preserves both independent distinctions exactly over the stated unrestricted demand alternatives. A narrower demand family, a supplied distribution, a larger store or a stated approximation criterion changes the problem.

The proof review found no substantive issue in the profile-sufficiency equivalence, finite profile bound, arbitrary-encoder obstruction, delayed finite-state continuation or explicitly defined causal-efficacy criterion. These are conditional mathematical constraints, not a theorem that concern or wise retention has already been generated.

## 6. What would count as the next positive bridge

A causal selector must do more than expose different scores in the research report. An acquired operational consequence must change a retained selector state, and that change must alter the later generator or retention procedure actually enacted. The sensing, write, storage, switch and later operation must all be paid. A changed policy label with unchanged enactment, or one arm simply losing all ability to operate, is insufficient as the positive witness of selection between available recipes.

The comparison must also identify what the procedure serves. If the only criterion is remaining fuel, all operations cost money and there is no required continuation or return, inactivity preserves the most stock. A genuine operational demand, deadline, dependency or receipt law is therefore a substantive premise. It cannot be replaced by the analyst's label that one generator is correct or one record is valuable. A record's proposed future benefit must be exercised through an actual demand and use, rather than counted only in an external score.

For a finite constructive test, an earlier funded checkpoint could be used as an explicitly prospective alternative. Its reserve cannot repair the completed endpoint, and competing policies cannot each receive that same full reserve within one claimed trajectory. A sustained mechanism needs a specified source of renewed support, its native access and feedback, and a checked resource/state join. Any new material, recipe, demand or calibrated channel must be disclosed. These requirements leave room for a positive mechanism; they prevent calling a supplied reward or supplied choice rule the missing derivation itself.

The present round deliberately stops at this boundary. It establishes no universal winner, new renewal interface, policy-change mechanism or autonomous origin of the stutter generator. The remaining transition is concrete: **available method variation → native operational consequences → paid retained influence → changed later method under continued support**.
