## Question and inherited results

The [previous wall](META-ATTRACTOR-ADMISSION.md#the-inference-wall) remains correct: recurrence and agreement along selected activity cannot alone identify the consequences of untried changes. The constructive next question is whether a testing organization can **acquire a relevant dependency description and maintain its adequacy through actual reorganization**, instead of leaving the testing arrangement permanently fixed.

We reuse [concrete handoff](CORRECTION-HANDOFF-INTEGRATION.md), [partial-compromise correction](FAULT-TOLERANT-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md), [relational correction](RELATIONAL-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md), and the meta-attractor's own [funded reproduction](META-ATTRACTOR-REPRODUCTION.md) and [acquired admission](META-ATTRACTOR-ADMISSION.md). Handoff already preserves a tested route through revision. Fault-aware certification already handles declared correlated deviations. The new join is **observed dependencies → admission of dependency changes → financed repeated reorganization → maintained evidence and actual response**. It does not introduce a new quorum or dynamic-update algorithm.

Within the nondual account, production, material reserves, observers, retained descriptions and changing routes are related activities in one history. Their analytical separation follows their different effects. A causal root here is a represented common source of deviation; it is not a metaphysically independent entity or a claim of absolute probabilistic independence.

## Explicit model and correspondence

[TestingReorganization.lean](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/TestingReorganization.lean) imports the actual earlier admission and reproduction modules. Its material plant is the existing four-role installation. Three reading positions depend on three represented causal roots, whose natural-number identifiers name those dependencies. The testing configuration retains partially acquired root readings and at most one staged replacement path.

The local fault model permits **one represented root to deviate in each response transaction**, possibly a different root in later transactions. Every reader depending on that root may give any answer or no answer. Readers outside it report the true supplied proposition when they answer; availability additionally requires their responses. Root faults concern these response streams. Structural inspection of a path's root is a separate, faithfully implemented primitive in this construction. It is not made reliable by voting among the same uncertain streams.

Three pairwise distinct actual roots imply that at most one reader is affected. Agreement by two therefore supports the represented proposition; the two unaffected responsive readers also provide availability. This is a single Boolean statement under a declared fault model, not distributed agreement, asynchronous termination or a general institutional legitimacy result. The source of a report must actually be the claimed reading position, and the response transaction uses a stable current state.

Initially the root records are empty. A paid inspection places the actual root of one live path into its record. The `Knowledge` predicate expresses correspondence between retained readings and the current paths; it is proved to persist under the defined events, not tested by an oracle inside the acceptance procedure. Operational acceptance reads the records, requires them to be complete and distinct, and checks agreement. The proof connects those records to the actual dependency arrangement.

Important premises remain: initial distinct causal roots, faithful structural inspection, complete mediation of relevant changes, the one-root fault envelope, responsive nonfaulty streams when availability is claimed, and the real availability of newly addressed sources. A fresh integer does not create causal independence in reality. The constructive supply law makes a requested root available at the stated local cost; applying it requires that correspondence. Shared material support can still fail outside the modeled supply law.

## Staged reorganization, costs and recurrence

The event protocol allows ordinary work, inspection, construction of a staged path, inspection of that path, and substitution into a reading position.

- Building a path costs two material units and produces no inspection record.
- Testing it costs one unit and records its actual root.
- Switching costs one unit. It requires a tested candidate and a complete current dependency record. The prospective record must still distinguish the three roots.
- Old live paths stay in service during construction and testing. Switching updates the path and its record together, retires the staged handle, and increments the revision counter.

A candidate root already shared with another position is rejected when it would merge those dependencies. An untested candidate is also rejected. These are consequences of executable guards, not favorable proposals assumed in advance. The switch is an atomic local step, as in the inherited handoff approach; the model does not prove a distributed implementation of that primitive. All relevant physical path changes must use the permitted transition relation.

Every charged event retains at least eleven material units for the existing installation's ordinary operation. Stock and parts support are preserved. Inspection and construction do not introduce independent elapsed-time decay; wear occurs in the inherited operation cycle. The costs are declared material accounting, not measured computational or institutional burdens.

The separate-return work cycle produces at least two units of spare material under the inherited eleven-unit arrival law. Two work cycles therefore finance a complete four-unit migration without reducing the starting reserve. `initial_to_arbitrarily_many_revisions` proves an actual generative-core trace from the original uninspected state through preparation, the three initial inspections and any finite number of these funded replacements. The worked family repeatedly replaces one reading dependency; arbitrary legal finite revision traces have the broader invariant guarantee. Continued source availability and replenishment are supplied, not proved from an empty world.

The three units of collection and the physical response remain in the account. This establishes implemented use of the maintained evidence, not just an unused certificate. ## A prospective boundary and its failure

Two configurations can agree in all current reports while differing in resilience. The checked comparison uses roots `(0,1,2)` and `(0,0,2)`. Before an affected root deviates, their complete report vectors agree. A subsequent deviation at root zero makes two readers in the merged configuration support the wrong answer. The dependency-aware gate rejects that merger before this adverse observation occurs.

This supplies a bounded meaning of “seeing the slide”: **loss of relevant causal separation can precede visible disagreement or task failure**. It is not a universal diversity score. The relevant separation is determined by actual shared causes and the admitted fault scope, rather than numbers of people, committees, labels or opinions. Different fault scopes can require different organizations.

The retained counterexample maps all three apparently distinct root labels to one unrepresented actual cause. That cause produces misleading agreement. At plant stock two, the nominal records accept an affirmative withdrawal and the physical response drives stock to one, disabling the established checking organization. This failure is outside the permitted accurate-root/mediated-change correspondence; it is a substantive counterexample to treating distinct labels as sufficient evidence of that correspondence.

The previous inference wall is therefore advanced, not erased. Adequacy can be carried through a specified history by testing dependencies and restricting how they change. **The remaining boundary is warrant for the structural read and causal-root model itself, including newly introduced common causes and changes that bypass the protocol.** Maintaining a record of an incomplete dependency map does not make it complete. This leaves room for richer discovery and fault models without declaring another permanent guardian or requiring an empirical deployment before further theory.

## Primary comparison and author counterreview

Malkhi and Reiter's [Byzantine Quorum Systems (1997)](https://malkhi.com/files/byzquorums-STOC1997.pdf), introduction and §3.1, explicitly motivates nonuniform failure sets through shared administration, location and hardware/software dependencies. It treats coverage of actual faults as an assumption. This is established groundwork for the present distinction between reader identities and common failure causes. Our three-reader statement, staged dependency substitution and material accounting neither reproduce its replicated-data protocol nor improve its quorum bounds. Read scope on 8 October: PDF pp. 569–570, introduction through the system-model section; the fuller comparison already recorded in the repository retains its own scope.

The strongest counterreview is that structural inspection has remained trusted while response streams may fail. Accepted: this round explains acquisition and maintenance of a specified causal description, not how every structural observation becomes warranted. The root-aliasing counterexample prevents that overclaim. The next study must constrain or investigate the actual structural channel and common causes, rather than merely certify more copies of its output.

A second objection is that all allowed changes already preserve the desired property. The implementation does not assume a favorable candidate: it reads the proposed path and evaluates a concrete prospective dependency arrangement. Failed untested and merged candidates remain explicit. The preservation theorem then checks the consequence of those executable guards. The enforcement of the guards is still a supplied local law; unrestricted rewriting is not covered.

A third objection is that testing costs could destroy the ability to act. The reserve guards prevent this in the declared accounting, and the two-work-cycle result finances continued migration. Unlimited investigation without replenishment is not promised. Source fabrication, elapsed-time decay during checking and full physical assembly remain correspondence obligations.

Finally, correct reporting can sustain an unjust organization. The represented proposition concerns a local operational margin, not the whole ethical appraisal. Neither reliable agreement nor a protected mechanism establishes legitimate authority or the goodness of its chosen ends.
