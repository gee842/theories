## The positive result

An intervention can genuinely relieve a burden while selectively impairing consideration of its own continuation. Current gratitude can be sincere and general agency retained. If a live, independently warranted concern is thereby excluded from assessment, a permissible, proportionate offer to restore that consideration can have a patient-directed ground. Its point is what it enables in the affected life. The responder need not have caused the original condition, promised assistance or expect private benefit.

The [argument](RELIEF-AND-REOPENED-EVALUATION.md) makes this prospective connection explicit. An identified activity or relationship matters; changing circumstances bear on its continuation; a restriction produced by the response prevents those circumstances from entering assessment of that response; an effective reopening can restore that relation. The existing constitutive candidate supplies the substantive claim that this patient difference can count in favor of an eligible responder's offer. The new argument specifies an application of that claim; it does not derive its universal authority from causation or from an added axiom.

This separates **an enabling benefit for the affected person** from **using that person as a means**. Reconsideration may matter because of the worthwhile activity it protects. Concern can nevertheless be noninstrumental toward the person: their actual continuation supplies its point. A universal intrinsic value of unused options is unnecessary for this limited route.

Successful reopening can leave the final decision unchanged. That is compatible with a new capacity to assess relevant considerations, but does not prove that capacity exists. Its causal relation to a declared range of considerations needs identification. The original preference has no automatic priority; the affected continuity, its formed capacities and the significance of their changes must be explained within the history.

The advance here locates the affected relation in **the beneficiary's response-altered ability to evaluate this very response**. It provides a bounded positive reason for reopening that relation and distinguishes it from repairing the original harm, reconstructing prior permission or restoring compliance with a programme. The case is philosophical; no patient or intervention has been empirically identified.

## Controls that can change the conclusion

Reasoned persistence, adequate delegation and valid self-binding are substantive controls. They can protect the relevant good without continuous personal reassessment. An alleged restriction must be distinguished from unavailable evidence, inability to express a considered judgment, lack of safe alternatives and inability to enact refusal. A disliked preference establishes none of these by itself.

The [primary comparison](RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-SOURCES.md) constrains both the diagnosis and the proposed remedy. Khader's account preserves agency under constrained options; Holton's defense of rational persistence retains an ability to reconsider. These are bounded author-manuscript readings, preserved with provenance. Neither supplies a premise-free welfare criterion or an empirical realization of the proposed case.

If reopening has no supported prospective role, if an adequate delegation already supplies the relevant responsiveness, or if reopening would defeat a justified settlement, the present argument may supply no favor. Costs, risks to actual relief, permission and competing claims belong to the comparison. Requiring agreement with an investigator or restoring an earlier preference by force would not satisfy the proposed target.

The [counterreview](RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-REVIEW.md) also preserves a stronger rival: a response's genuine relief might be disqualified from justifying that particular means when the means insulates itself against warranted reconsideration. This is distinct from an automatic balance of relief against an agency cost. The reopening argument need not settle that dispute about the original response, and an impermissible method does not refute a relief claim explicitly scoped to independently permitted means.

The current patient interface omits a patient-side appraisal process. ## Continuation: assessment can transform what it investigates

6 October 2026. The next substantive result distinguishes **warrant for a current continuation** from **warrant for a diagnosis of earlier impairment**. An inquiry offered to investigate the response can also change understanding, enable a new distinction, relieve fear or manifest a retained capacity. Successful performance after it may establish a presently usable relation without showing which of those histories occurred. [The positive argument, §8](RELIEF-AND-REOPENED-EVALUATION.md#8-inquiry-can-change-what-its-result-establishes) and [independent review, §8](RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-REVIEW.md#8-review-of-inquiry-that-changes-its-evaluative-target) defend this result and preserve retrospective diagnosis when adequate transition evidence is available.

This changes the earlier proposed test. The inquiry's own effects must be traced before its endpoint is called restoration. A ground may have ended, persisted or formed during inquiry. The relevance of a consideration must therefore be warranted in the reached situation. If a truthful explanation ends a fear, declining to treat that fear as still present need not indicate blockage. If a different worthwhile activity remains obstructed, its concern can remain live. A considered unchanged decision is still compatible with successful assessment.

There is also a positive route to action under incomplete historical knowledge. Where an independently justified present benefit, evidence and permission support a bounded offer, the offer need not wait for a definitive diagnosis of earlier impairment. It must not manufacture that diagnosis to justify itself. Equally, its current success cannot retrospectively authorize an earlier intervention. This is an application of the existing patient-directed candidate, whose universal ground remains unfinished.
