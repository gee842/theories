## 1. The inference attempted

The candidate is that a concrete continuation can constitute both its ethical quality and a fitting practical response within it. Choosing is among the activities realizing that continuation. An actual response can enact, receive, protect, sustain, transform or appropriately end a realization; promotion of more instances is not the only possible relation. Neither a scalar total nor an enlarged subject is required.

The question is therefore not how an independently owned value crosses a subject/object boundary. It is whether the full relationship explains its favorable or adverse character and its practical articulation, independently of the current selector's assent. One explanation could establish both together. We do not require isolated values to be justified first and then assembled into a whole.

The target is an endorsement-independent account of whole ethical realization and differentiated practical significance across intelligence. Some moral favor at every qualifying beneficial opportunity is a stronger candidate consequence, not the definition of that target. Reasons, constraints, permissions and warranted absence of a particular reason require their own exact content. Universal validity does not imply an identical command, inevitable compliance or allegiance to this vocabulary. Formation of understanding and motivation does not settle validity.

The [affirmative argument](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-GROUND-ARGUMENT.md) develops the stronger assistance candidate and its substantive rival. Within that subclaim, a rival granting moral favor but denying its categorical rational authority does not refute first favor. A role-sensitive account is not automatically a rival to universal meta-ethics: it could be a competing articulation if it independently justifies its ethical grounds and differentiated scope. The [counterreview](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-GROUND-REVIEW.md) now preserves that distinction.

## 3. The checked mathematical result

The [Lean module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/WholePracticalRestriction.lean) and [correspondence note](WHOLE-PRACTICAL-RESTRICTION-MATH.md) use `H` for continuations at a declared scope. For a formed position `i`, `rᵢ` describes which continuation an available option realizes, and `Lᵢ` compares two options. These are analytical presentations within one unfolding; the module does not derive their formation or certify their complete correspondence to actual histories.

The exact restriction contract is

`Lᵢ(a,b) ↔ Q(rᵢ(a), rᵢ(b))`.

A common binary relation `Q` satisfying every such contract exists **if and only if** the local comparisons agree whenever both realized continuations match. This result is constructive. It assumes no complete ranking, scalar aggregation, transitivity or desired ethical direction. It supplies no ethical warrant for either side of the contract.

The accompanying results identify three practical limits on using that representation as a universal argument:

| Checked result | Consequence for this investigation |
|---|---|
| Extensions agree on co-represented pairs; arbitrary extensions can disagree on an uncovered pair. Representing every continuation individually does not ensure pair coverage. | A universal index or an inventory of cases does not itself establish every needed comparison. Additional global constraints may determine otherwise uncovered pairs; they need their own grounds. |
| Three compatible, locally strict orders cover every pair but form a cycle, so no common strict order extends them. | Even pair coverage and local orderliness do not establish whole transitivity. This is a structural counterexample, not an ethical counterworld. |
| Two records induce exactly the same binary comparisons while differing in whether the lower option has any positive supporting entry. | A ranking alone need not preserve the grounds favoring an option. The finite numbers are an information-loss witness, not ethical weights or a model of the integral of the whole. |

Faithful recoding also preserves a comparison when both realized continuations and the common relation are preserved. Actual learning or self-modification can change relevant grounds, available options and justified appraisals; it is not automatically a recoding.

The universal inference advanced is conditional and exact: a warranted whole comparison cannot acquire a different verdict solely through a faithful change of presentation. It remains to justify the whole comparison, its practical interpretation and the relevant correspondence. A common relation constructed from supplied verdicts does not explain why those verdicts deserve assent.

There is also a nonvacuity requirement. If retaining distinct choosing histories makes the local domains disjoint, overlap compatibility can impose little cross-position content. Deleting relevant history to manufacture overlap would be equally unfaithful. The theory needs substantive relations between its differentiated positions, not just a single notation containing them.
