# Actual completion and the existing successor's applicability limit

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [connected-model study](CONNECTED-MODEL-MECHANISM.md), under Ren's request that all learning, concern, governance and alignment mechanisms be derived in one connected generative model. The stronger no-assumptions/no-axioms requirement remains open.

## Named connection

The connection is **actual acquired completion/applicability evidence → warranted change of the operative recommendation**, preserving the same learned means, accounts, recipient history and independent reasons. This study asks what the current process can already derive before adding a desired completion-sensitive branch.

The [new Lean module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/CompletionApplicability.lean) imports the accepted `ConnectedModel` and preserves every existing law. It derives a stronger limit on this actual successor: the internal `help` flag is invariant on every finite admitted history. More knowledge or a more exact proof of completion cannot by itself revise that flag under the current event repertoire.

This is a result about a particular causal dependency, not a theorem that learning applicability is impossible. The qualified `command`, access, scope, permission and execution can change. In particular, an attempt can be denied and consumed while the internal help flag remains true. The distinction prevents stopping through external gating from being mistaken for learning why a recommendation ceased to apply.

## The actual dependency and its invariant

The existing function has exactly these inputs:

| Existing route | Internal help flag |
|---|---|
| Shared | Whether `64 < 10 * native.n` |
| Filtered | False |
| Conventional | Whether `native.n = 7` |

Its action labels, learned means register, current archival records, recipient balance and current permission do not enter this function. Native influence `n` was acquired before the current successor continuation. The predecessor's proof already shows that every admitted successor event retains the native payload. The new proof shows that every such event also retains the route. The unchanged law has no route-update event.

It follows, by induction on the original `Generative.Trace`, that `help t = help s` for every admitted finite successor history from `s` to `t`. The proof includes query production, record writing, paid reason use, factual correction, copying, intervention and settlement. It does not restrict attention to the one favorable example.

Two supporting results state the exact dependency without adding a transition:

- Equal route and equal `native.n` give equal help flags.
- A changed help flag would require a change to at least one of those two inputs.

Both supporting proofs have empty named-axiom reports. They identify a necessary causal interface for this existing function; they do not justify changing either input or supply a rule that does so. Changing a route by fiat would install the missing selection rule rather than derive it.

A presentation preserving every original primitive edge inherits the same invariant. Refactoring decision functions to remove proof dependencies therefore cannot introduce a new learning capacity while remaining an exact presentation of the old law. A new capacity needs an additional actual transition or a changed causal dependence whose origin, resources and warrant are explained.

## Consequence on the reached history

The accepted actual successor entrance uses the shared route and acquired influence 7. Its internal help flag is true. Every reachable state retains that flag. More strongly, **every admitted assessment at a state reached from this entrance sets its port to `help` and pays its original one-unit assessment cost**.

This does not assert that assessment is always enabled, that a command is always supported or that work always executes. It says what an admitted assessment can select under this law. Current permission can still block work; absence of a qualified command can prevent assessment or use. These are existing causal channels, distinct from revising the internal evaluation in response to episode completion.

The actual learned endpoint is retained, not reconstructed. `SuccessionWitness.prefix_trace` supplies the same successor entrance, paid acquisition and reading history. At its consumed endpoint:

- the actual second query has raised recipient balance by ten units;
- the learned means register is 2 and acquired influence is 7;
- the internal help flag remains true;
- the next admitted paid choice produces the help port.

The result therefore connects completion-relevant consequences, learned means, acquired influence and an unchanging operative evaluation within the same history. It strengthens the previous single repeated-action witness into a law-wide limitation of this internal selector.

## Why the record also needs a question binding

The existing `RecipientArchive.Frame` records context, action, presence and receipt. The successor has no service quantity or episode identifier specifying which request a completion judgment answers. The original `ApplicationEvidence.EpisodeBound` and `ServiceMeaning` are explicitly external interpretation premises; equality of a context bit was never their replacement.

For a narrow diagnostic comparison, this study supplies two external quantity requests against the same actual second-query credit: ten units and twenty units. It defines quantity completion only as `required ≤ delivered`. This is a deliberately declared comparison, not a proposed definition of welfare, ethical standing, all services or all practical reasons.

The actual query credit is exactly ten. Consequently the ten-unit request is met and the twenty-unit request is not met. **The entire existing modeled endpoint is identical in the two questions.** No function of that endpoint alone can answer both questions correctly; neither can a function of any further summary of that endpoint. Even complete access to the existing state cannot supply an omitted argument identifying which request is under consideration.

This does not refute the old one-service theorem. Its `ServiceMeaning` premise says that the qualifying ten-unit delivery entails that declared service's completion. The unmet twenty-unit interpretation does not satisfy that premise. The result demonstrates missing query binding when a proposed decoder is required to cover both interpretations, not inconsistency in a fixed interpretation and not indistinguishability of every physically complete world description.

Nor does the result show that the current archive loses every fact needed for all completion tasks. The actual production and provenance results already identify a prior successful delivery. What is missing for the two requested meanings is the binding between that delivery and the requirement being answered.

## A scoped sufficient interface, and what it does not implement

Supplying both the declared quantity requirement and the actual attributed delivery makes the narrow comparison decidable. `quantityReadout` computes their comparison, and the all-input correctness proof has an empty named-axiom report. On the existing ten-unit delivery, the two supplied requests produce the appropriate different answers.

This pure readout is not called by the current successor and has not been installed as a controller or acquired register. Its comparison law does not establish the origin or accuracy of an attributed amount, the justified scope of a request, the legitimacy of its claimant, or the priority of this request against independent reasons. A completed service can remove one reason's present applicability without removing another reason or eliminating the recipient's standing.

The two results must therefore be kept together:

1. A completion judgment needs adequate binding of evidence to the claim it answers.
2. An operative recommendation needs a causal route through which such a judgment can change an input actually used by that recommendation.

Supplying a sound quantity answer alone leaves the invariant selector unchanged. Mutating a selector alone does not establish a warranted episode binding. Both connections, their physical costs and the applicable practical grounds belong to the remaining generative derivation.

## What would change the account

A valid existing successor trace that changes `route`, `native.n` or the help flag would refute the claimed invariant. An admitted assessment reachable from the actual entrance that selects the abstain port would refute the stronger reached assessment claim. A completion decoder correct for both diagnostic questions without receiving a distinguishing query argument would refute the non-identifiability result.

The next constructive bridge must produce and retain the relevant claim/episode distinction, connect it to actual acquired completion evidence, and explain a warranted paid update to the operative selection rule while preserving means knowledge and independent reasons. Existing paid record, dependency-ledger and selective retraction contracts are possible interfaces; their presence elsewhere does not establish that this actual successor invokes them. The present result identifies exactly what the unchanged law cannot supply, while leaving the stronger foundation's event-law and ethical-ground obligations intact.
