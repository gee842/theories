## The connection and the independently specified target

The connection is **activity transforms the conditions of its own applicability, while other or renewed dependencies can continue**. Begin with the actual coupled configuration: a damaged capture site, response material, common fuel, incident source, local storage and the admitted transformations among them. A response’s practical role is investigated through what those transformations actually make possible and consume. Neither an external success score nor an independently declared `Met` predicate drives this process.

The original FormedSelection law independently specifies that damage prevents capture, permits formation and repair when their material and fuel conditions obtain, and repair changes the site’s condition. Capture requires an intact site, available incident source and empty local storage. Local copying consumes that storage and available precursor. These are stipulated physical modeling laws whose genesis and empirical adequacy remain open; the present reconstruction concerns the relations they encode, not a derivation of the chemistry from logic.

Test the strongest structural reading: repair changes the configuration so the same repair cannot recur until actual damage; capture changes the configuration so it cannot recur until its local store is consumed or lost. These are different changes within ongoing activity. Completion of a restoration response does not imply the termination of every generative activity. No universal good, permanent need, patient standing or complete alignment follows from the event counts alone.

## Revision conditions and contribution to the root/tree account

A second admitted repair without an intervening damage would defeat the proposed no-repeat consequence under this exact law. A capture without release of the already full local store would defeat the corresponding storage consequence. A patient-stock change that alters an admitted recipient-identification event would defeat the proposed projection invariance. Independent evidence of bypass capture, unnecessary damage, misdirected repair or burdens on another continuing center could require a richer account of the relevant situation; those are substantive changes or rivals, not detached label toggles.

The intended contribution is to reconstruct one actual form of consequence-sensitive applicability already present in the tree and use it to revise how the core’s significance question is articulated. Shared Process notation is not a claim that the two concrete laws have been physically joined. The next wider question is how acquired representations and response capacities retain, discover and revise these consequential relationships, including effects on other centers, within the same developing history.
