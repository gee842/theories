## The identified relation

The actual ancestor and leak/wear continuation reach common fuel 1, source 216 and an empty local store. Capture/local-copy and bulk-copy both replace the lost A, leaving equal material counts and total energy 216. Only the former retains common fuel for repair after the stipulated later damage. Completed reconstruction and aggregate support therefore do not determine the remaining repair possibility.

This supplies a concrete target for acquired understanding: **which realized route changes a condition required by a later operation**. The learner must obtain and use that relationship, not receive a label saying “maintain,” “broaden the goal” or “choose capture.” Both routes remain adequate for current count replacement alone. Extending the undertaking to the stated later repair must be explicit before judging choice. This finite maintenance criterion neither establishes an ethical preservation duty nor derives a new ultimate purpose.

This is formal event provenance, not cryptographic authentication or an already realized sensor. The generic emission function still needs an actual contact/encoding specialization. Binding reserves four write units plus one binding unit, and the interface assumes available cells and an archive budget. Its four-bit encoding also limits what one frame can distinguish; it is not an arbitrary full-state recorder.

Useful evidence could bind a performed operation to acquired common/local/source effects and the relevant native context. A record need only preserve distinctions needed by the declared continuation, not reproduce the whole state. Missing contact, observed zero, stale binding and an unfinished record remain distinct. A producer's external state dump or the reachability checker cannot become runtime knowledge by being copied into a convenient field. Existing valid evidence may be reused; another sensor or repeated observation is not required by default.

## The learner changes the available conditions too

The reached common-fuel 1 boundary cannot fund even one further common-unit operation while retaining the unit required for later repair. Under the unchanged law, capture cannot replace that spent common fuel. The existing slot assembly alone consumes the reserve; its subsequent reserve/observe/commit macro cannot run from that state. The archive's separate budget cannot be introduced as free support.

Any earlier construction or retained evidence therefore needs its own actual ancestry and account. Moving assembly before the recorded 292 leaks does not preserve the old endpoint automatically. Earlier acquisition may be feasible, but its costs and changed material state must be carried forward. This is a specific obstruction under these recipes, not a universal positive cost of knowledge.

Memory parts remain occupied in later plant capacity guards. Deliberately incorporating free A/H into a record is organization, not automatically harmful wear that the count-restoration target must reverse. Fix which independently occurring loss is being replaced and count incorporated material consistently; otherwise the controller can be instructed to undo its own memory formation. Mathematical inspection of stored bits also does not itself implement the later reader or decision.

## Concrete acceptance of a future acquisition claim

1. Preserve the reached ancestry and specify the broader continuation independently. Do not combine separately funded inquiry, formation and material histories.
2. Trace native encounters through acquired, bound records to the operative dependency distinction. State the admitted channel/law family: finite evidence does not establish arbitrary future invariance.
3. Show that acquired evidence changes an actual choice or scope assessment while preserving independently supported work. A selective reader intervention should preserve formation, relevant contact and execution capacity; complete collapse identifies less.
4. Execute and account for the later operation, including decision costs. Preserve misleading contact, unavailable source, damaged starts with local support, and adequate bulk-copy alternatives. If a common adequate policy needs no additional distinction, do not invent a necessity-of-learning claim.

The [existing hierarchy](INQUIRY-COMPLETION-COMPARISON.md) already includes acquired inquiry revision and method formation. This interface adds their concrete dependency target, not a new architecture or repeated claim that consequence-sensitive revision is absent. Ordinary dependency-aware control is an admissible explanation and baseline. Broader ethical grounding follows the [whole-process account](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-INTEGRATION.md), preserving differentiated lives, authority, burdens and competing goods; common fuel and population counts do not settle that comparison.
