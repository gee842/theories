## Candidate and discriminating controls

The affirmative route investigates a local stability commitment: whether a sufficiently limited and independently described increase in one basic loss must immediately overturn BC1's established priority for protecting more otherwise equivalent lives. The ethical stability claim is an additional normative commitment, not a conclusion from the physical continuity of change. No universal numerical neighborhood, cross-form harm scale or empirical cutoff is presumed. A disturbance that crosses an actual living threshold must remain distinguishable from an arbitrarily renamed small change.

The generalization test concerns finite aggregation rules that select which claims count against which alternatives. A pairwise relevance cutoff can be mathematically specified and tested for cycles. Applying a cutoff relative to the whole menu can avoid a particular cycle yet require a fresh explanation: why may an unsatisfied strong claim remove a third claim from the comparison that actually determines the chosen alternative? Neither numerical coherence nor a renamed option supplies that explanation. A local candidate does not automatically become a global transitive ordering.

A separate positive connection concerns the formation of a new joint protective option. Chance arrangements must be implemented by the same feasible support relationships that determine who can be helped together. Test whether a new option can preserve every previously warranted protection chance and improve some, and whether requiring identical final probabilities can block that improvement. A chosen baseline chance is an input with its own justification; the model must not certify the status quo or derive fairness from normalization.

These mathematical controls represent positions within one shared provision history. They do not posit separate value owners, an aggregate supersubject or chances detached from their realization. The interpretation must retain actual losses, burdens, permission and timing. Chance improvement does not compensate an actual loser or remove the duty to carry out the selected response.
