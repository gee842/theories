## 1. Correct the form of the obligation

The phrase “independently justify every required whole family” should not suggest producing a separate finite recipe for every membership pattern. The desired justification may be one common explanation of an entire domain and its admitted relationships. An explanation of the conditions of a mathematical possibility need not be an account of a physical history that actually realizes it.

For example, the proposed next route uses one binary branching rule, independent of a target predicate, to describe finite choices. Its ambition is to explain a space of complete possibilities collectively. The question is whether that account independently warrants the complete space. Presenting each target through its own selected finite pieces would leave that question unanswered.

The existing finite-program boundary must also retain its exact type. `finitePrograms_miss_some_trace` in [PrefixSafety.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/completion/formal/PrefixSafety.lean) concerns `output : List Bool → Option (Trace Bool)`: a finite description has at most one complete trace as its output. It does not establish that a finite description cannot specify a domain, a branching rule or a relation with many possible outcomes. Applying that theorem directly to a collective domain explanation would change its contract. This is a scope correction, not a new proof or a claim that the proposed domain is already justified.

The target is therefore **a common warrant for the full range of possibilities**, including its interpretation at the required predicate levels. Neither an individual inventory of every pattern nor a completed physical enactment of every history is imposed as a necessary form of explanation.

## 3. Preserve the full target in the explanatory comparison

A matched structural account can accept the whole possibility space, the common formation rule, its relation to finite content and every established mathematical truth. It can also place the actual formation of mathematical practice within the same unfolding. It treats admission of the whole space as a structural commitment while disputing that a weaker generative description has independently warranted it.

That account still owes a justification for its commitment. Its availability as a comparison does not establish its correctness, the impossibility of the generative account or equality of explanatory merit. It shows why another truth-preserving correspondence cannot alone determine explanatory priority.

The stronger generative proposal must give a new reason that the relevant possibilities are constituted by its organization, or show that the rival's complete explanation expresses the same dependence. “Every compatible completion counts” is the disputed principle at this stage. Ren's nondual method does not require this principle to be false or to originate outside reality; it requires its proposed constitutive role to be assessed substantively.

## 5. Decision and condition for resuming this route

The useful advance is to fix the form of the stronger objective: seek one substantive explanation of the full range, without imposing a separate finite construction for every member. The proposed branching route does not yet provide that explanation. Its unresolved locality/exhaustiveness and whole-possibility admission requirements are already known.

Further work on this route is warranted when there is either an independently defended principle explaining admission of the whole range, with its premises compared against the full target, or a substantive argument selecting the proposed direction of constitutive dependence. A new principle must not obtain its apparent force by including the desired full range in its own definition. Another prefix construction or a more symmetric proper subfamily would not meet that condition.

This is a stopping conclusion for the repeated branching argument, not a proof that the universal project is impossible. The conditional higher-order reconstruction and relative assembly argument remain established at their recorded scopes. Full admission from the root, its extension across mathematics and exclusive constitutive priority remain unproved.
