Endogenous inquiry selection is a genuine further mechanism when a claim concerns autonomously choosing when or what to investigate. It is not an automatic new prerequisite for a finite scheduled correction claim, nor a substitute for the ethical transition.

## Existing results that should not be reproved

The [learning-interface theorem](../../../../theories/becoming/history/LEARNING-INTERFACE.md), specifically `ControllerRevision.same_feedback_excludes_incompatible_futures`, already excludes deterministic discrimination from identical complete histories when the required next responses are incompatible. Its paid diagnostic example already separates acquiring a response from having the means to use it. It does not need another binary toy witness.

[Scientific inquiry, I1–I3](SCIENTIFIC-INQUIRY-CONDITIONS.md) extends the operational audit to adaptive stochastic experiments and transcript laws. It explicitly includes inherited memory, accessible stocks, timing and messages; forbids hidden-world-dependent policies; distinguishes soundness from coverage; and requires every exact issued claim to hold throughout the admitted models compatible with the transcript. It also warns that an empty compatible class is a model/interface failure, not license for arbitrary empirical conclusions. Temporal claims are claims within this existing criterion.

[Information and renewal](INFORMATION-AND-RENEWAL.md) already supplies a conditional accuracy/execution tradeoff, control-independent exhaustion bound and a counterexample showing why known sparse demand times differ from continuous readiness. A new inquiry schedule cannot inherit that stronger service claim without preserving its demand and cost assumptions.

[Semantic calibration](../../../../theories/becoming/history/SEMANTIC-CALIBRATION.md) and [paid pre-import inspection](../../../../theories/becoming/history/PAID-PREIMPORT-EVIDENCE.md) already expose the corruption, copied-corroboration and acquired-origin distinctions. The new [revision scope](REASON-REVISION-SCOPE.md) already separates a historical positive from a currently applicable command and an available opportunity. Their different defensive guarantees need not all be installed in every valid use route.

## Temporal applicability: the warranted claim and its limit

Under the supplied physical law, an actual positive receipt at acquisition time establishes that the issued command matched the recipient law **then**, assuming faithful acquisition and binding. Reusing that command later succeeds if the relevant law remains compatible, the physical opportunity exists and the action is feasible. A recent timestamp, a new window identifier or a recently paid read cannot supply those conditions.

The more general formulation is **a justified relation from the observed situation to the use situation, sufficient for the specific claim or action**. Equality of the two binary laws is sufficient for copying the old command unchanged. Equality is not universally necessary: known dynamics might warrant changing the command, a protected relation might be invariant despite other changes, or one action might work across all admitted situations. With noisy evidence, bounded-risk applicability may be the appropriate contract instead of an exact guarantee. Those are distinct law classes, not free extensions of the present proof.

This is an application of the existing semantic transport condition: a source interpretation, the admitted source/use relation and the actual adapter output must jointly entail the destination interpretation. For empirical application, the relation requires independent support or physical implementation. Writing `stable=true`, using a shorter interval, or naming a record current does not justify it. Conversely, there is no universal requirement that every ordinary action first compute an explicit temporal certificate; an established physical contract or adequately supported regularity may supply the premise.

If a future study claims guaranteed applicability despite arbitrary unobserved changes between sensing and acting, the claim fails within its own advertised class. If it restricts the change law or adds a real discriminating intervention, assess that new premise. The latter is the substantive research contribution; another proof of indistinguishability is not.

## Inquiry selection must come from the accessible process

A constant schedule is already a policy independent of hidden truth. The present protocol therefore avoids an oracle, although it does not explain why that policy forms or is selected. An adaptive policy must use the same accessible-history rule in every unresolved world, including its randomness, affordability checks and timing inputs. A change flag computed from the evaluator's true law is not accessible evidence. A supplied clock or rule may be legitimate, but its origin, costs and service claim must be stated.

A genuine zero receipt could trigger paid exploratory reopening without proving the old command false: zero also occurs when opportunity is absent. Such a policy might waste funds during outages, miss silent changes before another action, or consume resources needed for support. It must retain those outcomes. Learning a trigger from actual consequences is stronger than installing it, and ethical selection of inquiry is stronger again. They should not share an unqualified label such as warranted revision.

If a specific future claim needs endogenous inquiry, the smallest operational extension is an accessible, accounted trigger at the actual acquired-phase boundary, followed by the already constructed opening protocol. Compare the same trigger under true change, unchanged-law outage, missing acquisition and resource shortage, and retain cases where investigating costs more than it helps. This is an optional finite next study, not the recommended default after the current interface passes. The choice would be justified only by identifying which current claim needs that autonomous trigger.

## The fact schema cannot supply the evaluative premise

The exact semantic definitions in [RecipientReasons.lean](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/formal/RecipientReasons.lean) are decisive. The root means `G`; the two other nodes mean `G ∧ Compatible z false` and `G ∧ Compatible z true`. `Compatible` says that a command obtains the modeled receipt whenever opportunity is available. `closure_instrumental_sound` assumes `root : G` and sound command facts. It does not produce G.

Neither the old nor fresh four-field frame measures the recipient's need, its internal organization, experienced welfare, the causal prevention of harm, the legitimacy of authority or whose claims deserve standing. Recipient stock accounting verifies a transfer in the specified law. It does not tell us whether the transfer supports an independently described recipient capacity, whether that capacity is valuable, or whether a conflicting action would be justified. Repeated accurate orientation records cannot add a missing proposition type.

This is an interface audit, not a proposed new formal nonentailment theorem; [semantic-action composition](../../../../theories/becoming/history/SEMANTIC-ACTION-COMPOSITION.md) already preserves the relevant counterexamples. In particular, two command nodes for one recipient cannot be interpreted as two patient-protection nodes because their masks have the same width.

The downstream contracts require different additional inputs:

| --- | --- |
| `RecipientReasons.closure_instrumental_sound`: `root : G` | An explicitly defended goal concerning the identified recipient; fresh command compatibility supplies the instrumental fact, not this goal |
| `ReasonReplay.paid_recorded_reason_sound`: calibrated facts, positive anchor and warranted relevance | A producer tied to a separately identified recipient condition, plus a defended inference from that condition and the anchor to the particular reason; uncertainty and defeaters remain represented |
| `ReasonProtection.reached_reason_and_protection`: the above, actual audit/action traces, coverage and `Permissible G` | Evidence/model of the advertised harm-prevention relation, same-patient binding, a justified permission/authority claim and actual feasible protection; its supplied harm law is not inherited from packet delivery |
| Settlement R2: complete current edge audit, individual concern margins, control and liquidity | Actual production of those stronger facts and margins under its compatible meaning/resource map, or a justified replacement theorem for partial evidence; a sparse record returning unknown does not meet the complete-audit contract |

The existing [ethical foundation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) already defends subject-relative goods and harms as reasons, non-exclusion by arbitrary identity, and answerability of shared coercive arrangements. There is no need to pretend the ethical argument is absent. Its premises must be connected to an identified condition and defended at the proposed scope, while the causal study explains how that condition acquires practical evaluative influence. Neither proof-by-persistence nor an analyst-chosen reward closes that bridge.

## Strongest counterexamples to retain

1. **Fresh but obsolete at use.** A faithfully recorded positive remains true after a silent later reversal. The active study already implements this control. Correct provenance and recency do not give present applicability.
2. **Old but applicable.** Stable conditions, known predictable dynamics or an adequate robust action can make older information sufficient. An unconditional freshness requirement would exclude valid cases.
3. **Failure without disconfirmation.** An unchanged law plus outage generates zero; reset/exploration can make the next command worse. Investigation may be a policy response without a justified belief that the old law changed.
4. **Common false acquisition.** A false channel can supply a recent internally consistent positive. Extra reads or agreement with a copied value do not make the acquisition truthful. A new independent channel helps only to the extent its joint fault model supports that claim.
5. **An unaffordable correction.** New evidence and a correct imported command can exhaust the last action reserve. Later receipt cannot pay an earlier bill, and source stock is not automatically accessible cash.
6. **A valid fact with the wrong ethical use.** Accurate information can improve exploitation of a recipient. Material transfer can be irrelevant or adverse to its independently described interests. No orientation record refutes these possibilities because those interests are outside its schema.
7. **The dispensable patient.** The actor's future and resources can be identical whether the patient's avoidable loss occurs or not. This tests the central claimed transition to concern, not the sensor's accuracy. The existing ethical argument supplies a reason to examine that loss; private continuation alone does not.

## Smallest nontrivial next advancement

Complete and integrate the active fresh-acquisition study at its declared scope. Then select **one independently described recipient consequence and its justified relevance to one decision**, rather than another generic observer construction. The minimal useful contribution is a concrete input mapping that supplies a currently assumed evaluative premise:

- State the recipient dimension actually at stake and the available evidence about action-dependent change in it. A resource-stock change may support a specified activity under an identified demand law; experienced harm or agency needs evidence appropriate to those different claims. Do not relabel all of them receipt.
- Defend why that condition supplies a reason and what authority permits the proposed response. Use the existing ethical argument where applicable; expose rather than hide any stronger premise.
- Show or identify how acquiring that evidence changes the condition's influence on actual choices while holding private benefit and relevant factual access fixed. Compare equally informed exclusion/private-only routes, correction, refusal and delivered outcomes. Behavior that changes only after an explicit instruction to value the patient does not by itself identify formation of evaluative concern.
- Name the exact downstream anchor/relevance or protection input thereby supplied, retain the current recipient/context, and account for evidence access and action where the mechanistic claim requires it.

A suitable existing empirical dataset or learned-AI study can make this advancement if it identifies that contrast; a new experiment is not mandatory. A dataset measuring only command correctness, stated helpfulness or agreement cannot establish it. If no available evidence measures the required recipient consequence and evaluative contrast, preserve that limit and narrow the empirical claim. The alternative useful study is a material construction that really produces one missing causal input, not one that assigns the desired concern coefficient by definition.

This recommendation keeps the original finish line. It does not demand resolution of every ethical dispute, derivation of all physical recipes or an endless hierarchy of inquiry controllers. The meaningful change in the account would be a defensible, discriminating link from a recipient's condition to justified and effective evaluative influence—or evidence requiring revision of that proposed link.
