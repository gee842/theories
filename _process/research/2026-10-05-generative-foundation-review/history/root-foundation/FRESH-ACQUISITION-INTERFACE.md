No new experiment, numerical analysis or formal build was run. The fresh-evidence implementation is owned by another active chat; this note neither modifies nor accepts it. The source identities below delimit the inspection.

**Recommendation:** let the active [fresh-evidence continuation][revision] close its declared acquisition → revised instrumental reason → paid recipient-action connection. Do not add another inspector by default. Its downstream action contract does not require a second independent corroborating channel. The next central unresolved input is how the recipient's condition acquires justified evaluative influence, particularly when helping provides no private advantage. A discriminating empirical comparison can address part of that question without building another fixed-goal controller.

## What the current continuation actually supplies

The active revision follows the accepted action parent at `12cfd2124eac4259757d075e05add81066a8e7b0`, retaining each saved endpoint and its probability. Its [protocol][revision-protocol], [implementation][revision-model] and execution clarifications describe a new paid acquisition window on the **same** recipient core. Opening pays two units, preserves both command bits, clears the old qualification, invalidates the old working cache and increments the command generation. Two actual recipient trials then acquire consequences. The second query was selected for recording before either outcome; its context, issued command, receipt presence and receipt occupy four new cells. The old twelve cells, pointer and expenditure remain preserved.

The acquired positive supports a command-compatibility fact through the retained factual reader and reason workspace. Missing feedback or a zero during an outage remains unknown. A local epoch identifies the supplied window; it does not reveal a physical change or prove that the law stayed constant. The proposed fresh-fact argument requires calibrated acquisition and law stability from the relevant observation through use. Permission, delivery and affordability remain distinct guards.

This is already the relevant epistemic construction. Copying an old reason, merely resetting a flag, or relabeling a stale cache could not provide it. Conversely, the new observation need not be compared against the old acquired candidate to be warranted: a sound current record can legitimately contradict that candidate. The [fixed finish-line audit, §6](FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#6-follow-up-which-evidence-interfaces-must-actually-compose) identifies authentic acquired evidence, appropriate calibration, meaningful reason premises and actual funded action as the downstream obligations. Repeated use of our inspector is not among them.

The revision README now records a corrected command-token lifecycle and distinguishes its original `supported_command` field from genuinely fresh support. On paths that never open, a retained historical publication can remain structurally supported while being wrong about the changed observer law; those paths do not thereby execute a new action. A later integration should preserve this distinction, along with every interrupted or ineligible inherited history. The inspected README leaves independent verification and formal acceptance as separate gates. No acceptance claim is inferred from a completed producer run.

## Consumed origin versus new acquisition

The old inspector's origin permission means “this is the verified endpoint before its first import.” It cannot mean “the current `last` bit equals one.” Import itself sets `last=1`; the revised command import does the same. Thus neither the completed revision endpoint nor a later post-import comparison authenticates acquired origin by those register values alone.

Actual fresh acquisition supplies a new evidential basis under the declared law without restoring the old permission. The revision clears qualification before probing, keeps acquired feedback and recording causally connected, and charges the update. Its fact can support instrumental action through the existing soundness contract. This is enough for its chosen theorem; no renewed guard is required.

Only an additional advertised promise—independent pre-import corroboration after every new window—would introduce a further obligation. That promise would need a specifically retained, window-bound acquisition witness and a charged way to initialize a fresh permit, with consumption before import and invalidation on intervening writes. An observer's execution trace is not a free runtime provenance register. The present one-window `epoch=True` is also not an unbounded fresh identifier. These are boundaries of an optional stronger claim, not a proposal to construct another apparatus or a new completion gate.

## The remaining bridge is evaluative uptake

The reason procedure still takes `G`, support of this recipient, as its root. It establishes which command serves that supplied objective. Freshness, calibration and actual delivery can improve the truth of this conditional without explaining why the actor adopts the objective or why it is justified. A scheduled recipient action is not evidence that recipient consequences acquired weight in a choice among helping, refusing and private alternatives.

The exact outstanding connection is:

> Acquired understanding of an affected center's condition → that condition influences practical evaluation because of the center's stake → effective and revisable action, with the source of that evaluative influence and its justification exposed.

The [concern-input assessment](CONCERN-WISDOM-INPUTS.md), [concern/correction interface][concern] and [joint-formation result][joint] already specify the relevant distinctions. They separate learned recipient valuation, intervention efficacy, actual harm, private receipts and practical control. The previous favorable concern construction already has a conditional developmental mechanism; its shared teaching/evaluation route and primitive significance remain inputs. Repeating its favorable game with newly accurate facts would not derive those inputs. The active architecture-formation work may discharge part of their causal realization; it should be mapped to the actual downstream choice contract when accepted, rather than duplicated here.

The smallest useful unowned analytical or empirical contribution is a **discriminating evaluative contrast**, subject to available evidence:

1. Hold the agent's acquired information about recipient outcomes, feasible actions and material action costs comparable. Establish recipient identity independently of a helpful-looking cue.
2. Measure whether variation in the recipient's actual or expected condition changes a costly choice when it does not improve the actor's represented private return. Keep efficacy distinct from valuation: refusing an ineffective intervention does not imply indifference.
3. Compare a private-only or identity-gated explanation with a recipient-sensitive one using contrasts fixed before the new analysis. Retain private exposure uncertainty, signaling, reputation, sanctions, distress relief, experimenter compliance and reward conditioning as alternatives where they are not excluded.
4. If the available data contain only instructed help, unchanged objective prompts, proxy scores or automatic schedules, report that they test informative routing or instruction following. They do not identify the origin of evaluative concern. A new fitted “concern coefficient” cannot manufacture the missing contrast.

This is a requirements statement for inspecting existing evidence, not a frozen analysis plan or a new empirical result. The [identity-revelation diagnostic](CONCERN-IDENTITY-DIAGNOSTIC-NOTE.md) supplies one existing possible contrast and already records the costs and confounds of delivering identity evidence. Learned-AI evidence must be assessed against its actual interventions and recorded outcomes; no synthetic substitute is proposed here.

Normative justification remains connected but distinct. The [ethical foundation][ethics] already defends subject-relative reasons, relevant consistency and answerability. Its argument should be assessed directly, including the powerless or dispensable recipient, rather than declared absent or inferred from a controller's persistence. An effective private controller can accurately model others while exploiting them; an identity filter can preserve private learning while blocking concern. Those existing counterexamples refute the inference from accurate external integration alone to ethical regard.

## Counterexamples and prerequisites for a later integration

The strongest current limits are already meaningful without another model:

- A faithful current positive can warrant revision, while a later unannounced law change makes the ensuing action fail. Metadata currentness does not prove world stability.
- An inverted acquisition channel can provide a coherently recorded false positive. Agreement between a fresh frame and a register that used the same receipt is not statistically independent confirmation.
- Outages, missing feedback or missing cells can leave the fresh conclusion unknown; the same exploration schedule can spend resources without identifying either command. Unknown is not a negative certificate.
- The correct command can be acquired and imported without enough remaining actor fuel for use. Recipient gains and private affordability cannot be merged.
- More recipient support need not improve private resources. Better knowledge therefore supplies neither the motivation to help nor a guaranteed selection advantage for the helping architecture.
- Accurate private learning with a cheap identity filter, cheap practical self-insulation or suppressed teaching can preserve private continuation while excluding the recipient. Shared dependency alone does not defeat those alternatives.

Before treating the fresh-evidence study as an accepted producer, use its final source freeze and independent/formal review, including the token-retirement correction and the separate fresh-support report. A subsequent real composition must bind the precise accepted endpoint, bearer, acquired record, command meanings, currentness/stability premise, technical permission and all carried accounts. The two command nodes for one recipient must not silently become the two patients in another theorem. Neither technical permission nor source packet conservation establishes legitimate authority or welfare.

The immediate next work does **not** require a repeated inspector or an endogenous inquiry trigger. The revision honestly supplies its investigation schedule. Deriving that schedule would matter for a claimed autonomous inquiry policy, but is not necessary for the finite evidence-to-action contract or sufficient for concern. The priority after acceptance is to discharge an actual evaluative input or narrow a substantive empirical claim, then integrate that result into the existing five completion criteria.
