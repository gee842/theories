## 2. Finite proof and finite execution evidence

The distinction that matters for continuation is exact. `allTrue_recurs` has a finite Lean proof term establishing an infinitary property: beyond every position there is another designated event. The same module proves that the class of all recurring traces has no finite bad-prefix characterization. A finite derivation from an adequate rule and a finite observed history therefore have different evidential roles.

This does not separate mathematical activity from reality. Forming and checking a proof, retaining observations and carrying out the activity described are related developments within the unfolding. The analytical distinction identifies which relationship supports a particular conclusion. A checked proof relates a formal conclusion to its hypotheses and definitions. An observed prefix records only that part of a history. Neither relationship can silently replace the other.

The safety/liveness classification is also derived from the properties of these same histories. It does not begin with constraints and flow as fundamentally different kinds. One asks whether exclusion of a whole history has an irremediable finite witness. Some admitted classes have that property and others do not. The full target fixes which class matters.

This is standard verification mathematics. The previous [primary-source audit](COMPLETION-WARRANT-SOURCES.md) records the domain and safety antecedents. Root additionally read Lamport’s author-hosted [1977 paper](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/proving.pdf), p. 125 and §III, pp. 134–136, in the retrieved text: it expressly develops proofs of liveness while keeping the relevant execution premises visible. This confirms the distinction between a liveness property and the method of proving it; it is not evidence for an ethical priority. No source bytes were imported and no mathematical priority is claimed.

## 3. What the result contributes to normative warrant

The first-priority inquiry asks why whole generative relationships have ethical force. This study cannot answer that by changing the definition of admission. It can expose a proposed preservation rule that fails its independently defended target.

Suppose a substantive ethical argument warrants ongoing response in a particular relationship. Its warrant, scope, exceptions and possible fulfillment must first be stated. If the requirement has a recurring or eventual component, retaining every finite opportunity for response does not by itself ensure actual response. The recurrence counterexample establishes that mathematical obstruction. Accurate technical learning or reported future possibility alone does not establish fulfillment; a checked adequate generating rule or refinement can supply a stronger guarantee.

Conversely, some undertakings legitimately end, some claims are discharged, and some channels should close. The completion theorem does not turn every ending into a violation, or establish an obligation to preserve every possible continuation. Applying unrestricted completion to a termination subject would change that subject. This preserves the ethical programme’s genuine self-good, finite fulfillment, nonreflective lives and actual burdens of continued inquiry.

The positive contribution is a requirement on a claimed **demonstration of preservation**: its interpretation must retain the complete requirement actually defended, including relevant global content and legitimate discharge. This follows from what it purports to demonstrate. It is not yet an independently grounded duty to institute that requirement, much less a universal duty applying to every intelligent formation.

## 4. Affirmative argument and substantive counterreview

The affirmative line has three steps. First identify the actual relationship that supplies the candidate reason, instead of defining the reason by the behavior of an installed evaluator. Next give its full temporal and practical content, including circumstances in which it ends or changes. Then check that represented revisions preserve that content, or establish that a substantive change defeats or fulfills it. The Lean package verifies selected mathematical links within this route; the first ethical identification remains the central philosophical task.

The informed-finality rival from the [normative integration](NORMATIVE-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md#4-the-rival-that-determines-the-next-connection) can accept every theorem here. It can acknowledge that silencing evidence does not answer it and that mere future possibility does not amount to fulfillment, while arguing that a particular achievement or a serious cost warrants ending further assessment. Resolving that dispute requires defending the ethical scope and priority in the actual case. No safety theorem, compactness theorem or axiom audit decides it.

Likewise, the earlier structural rival can reproduce the full completion mathematics, including its Lean proof. Formal uniqueness therefore remains uniqueness relative to the specified role. It does not become unique ultimate metaphysical constitution merely because its formal proof has now been checked.

This is a stronger evidential status for the mathematical claims and a more precise interface for ethical work. It is not a new affirmative proof of universal normative grounding. The remaining first-priority connection is still the practical force of the whole relationship against the informed-finality account. The mathematical branch supplies a criterion for detecting when a proposed implementation or abstraction has changed that target.
