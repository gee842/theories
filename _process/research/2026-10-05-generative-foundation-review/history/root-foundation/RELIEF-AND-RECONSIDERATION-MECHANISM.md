# Relief, changed evaluation and the possibility of reconsideration

6 October 2026. AI-authored source reconstruction under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), continuing [一体之仁的生成解释, §§1–8](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md). The named connection is **a response changes actual undergoing and the organization of evaluation → new evidence can, or cannot, participate in reconsidering that response within the same continuing history**.

The repository already establishes real changes in operative updating and candidate generation, and exact distinctions between current activity, acquired records and later capacities. It does not yet identify a realization in which those mechanisms constitute an affected person's experienced burden and capacity to contest the response. The constructive next step is an identified connection between these relations, not another controller with a favorable evaluation installed. This note adds no theorem, event law, experiment or validation run.

## 1. Preserve the actual question

The coformation account permits understanding to change experience itself. Reinterpreting a threat can genuinely relieve fear; suppressing a complaint can instead leave the burden unchanged. A third possibility is genuine relief accompanied by a response-induced loss of the capacity to reconsider the response. Calling all three “report change” would erase the very distinction to be explained.

Present relief and later revisability are therefore separate questions about one unfolding. Their separation does not posit independent substances. An actual response can change both through related mechanisms. Nor must a stable self stand outside those changes: a continuing organization can acquire, retain, lose or reorganize capacities. Identifying the affected continuity, including changes to it, is nevertheless necessary; merely reusing a patient name cannot establish it.

The target is not the proposition that any changed preference, steady decision or refusal to revise reveals damage. A person can understand a challenge and reasonably continue. Voluntary bounded self-binding, delegation, changed interpretation and an inability to act immediately are distinct controls. The capacity question concerns how a specified range of new considerations can participate in evaluation and available response, not whether the experimenter obtains a preferred answer.

## 2. What the existing mechanisms actually establish

### A changed updater, with its old answer retained

In [RouteRevision.lean][route], `swap_preserves_actual_record_and_actor` preserves the acquired `q` and original actor while replacing the template's route. `paid_read_uses_only_actual_diagnosis` ties the attempt to the actual monitor result. `swap_invalidates_old_evidence` clears the binding and old success/failure summaries: evidence about the displaced route cannot certify its replacement.

Subsequent learner events use the changed route to generate later updates. This is a change of an operative learning mechanism, not an analyst relabeling a fixed answer. Yet the diagnosis authorizes a replacement attempt; it does not identify the morally or epistemically best replacement. The [accepted history][route-account] includes late-change and buffer controls in which a wrong route and wrong child can persist. Neither persistence nor actual mechanism revision identifies ethical improvement.

This supplies a useful causal design: keep the current retained answer distinguishable from the rule governing its response to further evidence. It does not license renaming `q` as a person's endorsement or the route as that person's evaluative agency. The recipient account and the learner's updating arrangement have distinct roles in this implementation.

### A changed generator, tested and subsequently used

The strongest existing method history begins at the actually acquired H3 checkpoint. In [the source][method], `acquire_record` obtains six native readings; `instantiate_and_replay` compares generated candidates with that retained archive. `form_by_evidence` removes a formed edge when its whole candidate family fails. It then forms a replacement edge. After successful trial, the trial result is discarded; `invoke_and_acquire` must read the retained edge and regenerate its family again.

Thus acquired evidence changes the actual arrangement generating later candidate knowledge. Breaking the accepted edge stops subsequent invocation even when the archive and historical acceptance label remain. Conversely, the delayed ten-state control passes the available archive and next prediction while diverging later. The [prior reconstruction][organization] already establishes these distinctions and their resource history; they are not new results of this note.

The compatibility criterion, acquisition schedule, two generator recipes and generic replacement machinery remain supplied. The result does not show that the acquired evidence revises that criterion itself. Its particularly depleted successful H4 continuation also cannot be borrowed as a sustainably operating reconsideration system: the reported remainder is below one native-interface fee. A stored capacity and an affordable further invocation are different claims.

### Current activity can remain while a formed capacity disappears

[NondualFormation.lean][nondual] contains an actual formation, response and dissolution history. `dissolution_changes_capacity_preserving_activity` proves that the actual cuts preserve active nodes while disabling the jointly supported pulse capacities. `no_activity_only_capacity_report` establishes the corresponding limit on an activity-only description. In a different continuation from the reached responded state, `affected_node_becomes_effective_contributor` makes a previously affected node a necessary contributor to a later response.

These are nonprimitive, relational capacities: the relevant supports arise from current links and activity, can overlap, and can be reorganized. The law does not contain experience, acquired evaluative interpretation or contestation. It supplies an exact structural contrast for the new question, not a patient-agency realization. The rewiring and dissolution branches must not be combined into one history.

### Evidence, protection and contestation remain distinguishable

[ReasonProtection.lean][protection] preserves the actual harm ledger when a particular reason is retracted. `cut_does_not_refute_or_erase` preserves the reader's grounds and actual safeguards when an enforcement path is cut. `actual_delivery_cut_preserves_reasons_but_allows_harm` gives an actual permitted sequence in which installed reasons persist but an unprotected release follows.

This distinguishes epistemic support, effective control and consequences. Its instantiated control apparatus and strategic suppression alternatives are not the beneficiary's own ability to assess or contest an intervention. An institutional route to effective protection can support that ability in an application, but the identification must be established rather than inferred from the word “revision.”

## 3. What the patient and plasticity interfaces preserve and omit

The concrete [PatientConcern interface][patient] represents each patient with `burden`, `episode`, `version`, `route` and `permitted`. Its faithful `read` copies current fields. An enabled `act` removes the same patient's modeled burden and retires the packet. The permission event changes the permission field and clears the selection; its production is not modeled as a patient's deliberative act.

There is no patient-side appraisal mechanism, acquired alternative, capacity to form a refusal, or response-induced modification of those capacities in this state. The burden coordinate's experiential interpretation is expressly an application premise. Consequently, its proofs cannot settle whether a particular real response changed experience, only its expression, or both experience and revisability. This is an omission from this local representation, not evidence that the fuller nondual account needs an ontologically external evaluator.

The plasticity branch has a richer acquired history but does not remove this limitation. Its existing recipient-loss acquisition changes operative links, with own-loss determining the sign. The [pretraining result][pretraining] establishes an exact retained code for net teaching depletion through its declared completed prefixes; later retirement preserves the corresponding extended accounting in the selected replacement. It would be wrong to call cleared latches total loss of consequence information. But this structural code is not an installed cumulative interpretation, an experience measure, or a represented patient claim.

Likewise, the [coformation mechanism][coformation] describes each actual before/event/after occurrence and preserves joint continuation. It records an intermediate debit even when the selected endpoint summary returns to its original value, and distinguishes a missing acquired read from no consequence. Its `Snapshot` is a mathematical description of existing runtime coordinates. Calling it acquired understanding would add an unimplemented relationship.

## 4. One concrete next test: relief followed by an opportunity to reconsider

Fix the target before testing: one identified continuing organization undergoes a particular burden, has a formed capacity to evaluate a specified range of considerations concerning a response, and can participate in a feasible continuation. The developmental history and its actual remaining means belong to the starting boundary. Neither a primitive self nor an already ethically correct evaluator is required by this description.

The proposed comparison should have the following stages within each complete history:

1. **Reach the boundary.** Establish the burden, available evidence, operative evaluative arrangement and relevant permissions through their actual histories. A later capacity cannot be borrowed from a different successful branch or reconstructed by resetting its resources.
2. **Execute the response.** Preserve what actually changes in undergoing, retained assessment, expression and the mechanism relating future evidence to evaluation. Distinguish an independently supported genuine-relief case from a report-only suppression control. Do not stipulate that a cognitive intervention leaves experience unchanged.
3. **Offer a fresh relevant consideration.** This can concern a newly available alternative or a consequence for a continuing activity; it need not manufacture a new painful episode. Trace acquisition, retention and its participation in assessment. Lack of acquisition, recognition without evaluative uptake, revised assessment without expression, and expressed refusal without effective means are different failures.
4. **Follow the available continuation.** Test whether the consideration can enter reconsideration and, when selected, affect an authorized practical continuation. A considered decision to continue can be a successful exercise of the capacity. A response-created selective inability to admit relevant considerations is different from a reasoned rejection of them.

The decisive comparison holds independently identified present relief fixed while testing different subsequent evidence-response relations caused by the response. A single unchanged decision is insufficient. The declared consideration range and the mechanism claimed to exclude it must be specified; otherwise every unwanted decision could be redescribed after the fact as lost capacity. A consented, bounded self-binding arrangement is a substantive rival interpretation to examine, not automatically evidence of incapacity or wrongdoing.

The two genuine-relief histories need not be identical in all physical facts: the proposed causal difference is the arrangement supporting reconsideration. The comparison must explain how that difference is produced, which current effects are matched, and what later consequences it permits. An arbitrary `canContest` flag with no operative relation would not establish the mechanism. Nor would simply choosing an output that the analyst calls respectful.

This is one proposed discriminating investigation, not an implemented experiment. The current repository laws do not supply the experiential or patient-capacity correspondence needed to instantiate all its stages. Their positive contribution is a precise constructive interface: actual response, changed operative relation, fresh acquired consideration, changed or preserved evaluation, and an available continuation must be connected without substituting one for another.

## 5. Authorization and temporal scope

Permission must concern the particular operation at the time it is undertaken. In [ConnectedModel.lean][connected], `actual_cut_before_work` blocks new work, whereas `actual_work_then_cut` preserves the prior transfer and `actual_paid_settlement_after_cut` continues settlement of the already funded record. These existing results concern that event law's permission semantics; they are not a general consent theory.

The analogous requirement in the proposed test is to preserve authorization before the response, authorization for a later assessment or reopening, and the actual means of withdrawal as distinct relations. Post-response endorsement cannot automatically authorize its own production retrospectively. Post-response silence likewise cannot by itself show either consent or unchanged burden. Genuine later permission is not disqualified merely because preferences developed; its formation must be assessed rather than assumed either authentic or manufactured.

The pretraining service-permission cut also warns against reusing a convenient flag: it blocks service but still allows further teaching under that law. Its separate scope cut can stop teaching, without constituting an acquired decision to stop. Here too an ability to interrupt, a justified judgment and the operation to which permission applies must be connected explicitly.

## 6. Existing proof methods suffice for this next interface

[OutcomeContinuation.lean][outcome] distinguishes continuations by declared endpoint effects and requires the actual shared boundary. Its `exact_outcome_summary_distinguishes` says an exact record must separate histories whose future outcome profiles differ. It neither creates the record nor identifies its observation with experience. Applied to the proposed investigation, a present-relief summary would be inadequate if actual later evidence-response possibilities differ in a relevant, independently established way.

The coformation occurrence interface additionally preserves response-induced intermediate changes that an endpoint can omit. Together these tools already support the necessary bookkeeping: joint effects from one actual history, future possibilities from its reached state, and an explicit account of what a selected report preserves. A generic extra theorem would not supply the missing realization and is unwarranted this round.

The ethical comparison must still determine what significance the retained or lost evaluative capacity has, whether a reopening opportunity is permissible, and how that bears on the relief already achieved. This note neither makes capacity preservation a new eligibility gate for every local relief reason nor infers that its loss erases the favorable aspect of genuine relief. It identifies what an adequate account must distinguish before answering those questions. The stronger no-assumptions and whole-tree explanatory goals remain unchanged.

## 7. Scope of this reconstruction

This round read the cited canonical accounts and the operative source definitions. It did not rerun their scientific checks, add machine-checked results or conduct an empirical investigation. No beneficiary's experienced relief, evaluative transformation or capacity to contest has been newly measured or implemented. Existing results retain their original validation scopes and separate ancestry.

The account should change if an inspected branch already realizes the entire proposed patient-side relation, if a claimed learned mechanism merely bypasses its retained edge, or if a comparison attributed to capacity loss instead changes only affordability, expression or authorization. A real relief case remains relief even when the response introduces another loss; a genuinely transformed experience must not be forced back into the old burden category to preserve a favored explanation.

## 8. Independent source-correspondence review

A separate reviewing agent checked this reconstruction against the operative definitions and named theorems in the route-revision, method-formation, relational-capacity, reason-protection, patient-concern, connected-permission, outcome-continuation and joint-occurrence sources. It also read [the positive argument](RELIEF-AND-REOPENED-EVALUATION.md). **Accepted at the stated scope, with no blocking correction.** This is source correspondence and argument review; it does not repeat the prior scientific validations or add a new formal result.

The decisive boundary is the **actually reached post-response organization**, with its undergoing, acquired evidence, operative evaluation relation, expression channels, permissions and remaining means carried into the fresh-consideration suffix. Within each comparison history, that suffix must begin at this entire reached state. Equal current relief, equal approval or a reused patient identifier cannot substitute for the boundary. Comparing different response histories remains legitimate when their causal differences and matched effects are independently specified; their later capacities cannot be combined into a single favorable continuation.

The source checks support the claimed exclusions:

- The route swap retains the acquired answer and actor while changing the operative updater and invalidating displaced-route evidence. It does not identify the best replacement or a patient's evaluative agency.
- Method formation actually discards its successful trial result and regenerates through the retained edge on later use. Its fixed compatibility criterion and recipes are not acquired ethical standards. The reported depleted H4 endpoint does not supply sustainable later reconsideration.
- The relational-capacity history preserves activity while removing support for later pulses; the role-changing rewiring starts on a different branch. Neither realizes experience or patient contestation.
- PatientConcern's burden, route and permission fields have no patient-side appraisal dynamics. ReasonProtection separates installed grounds from effective protection. These are complementary distinctions, not an implemented patient evaluator obtained by combining their names.
- The existing outcome and joint-occurrence interfaces preserve future possibilities and intermediate changes from actual states. Their analyst readouts do not become acquired understanding by being exact. They already supply the necessary formal bookkeeping; another abstract theorem would not close the missing realization.

The positive note respects these limits. Its conclusion concerns a permissible offer whose restoration of consideration has a specified prospective role in protecting an independently warranted concern. Unchanged eventual endorsement can remain successful; disagreement, bounded nonexercise, unavailable alternatives, blocked expression and unavailable action do not establish selective evaluative inability. Valid self-binding and the costs of reopening can defeat this particular favor. The move from the affected good to an independent responder's reason remains the explicitly defended constitutive candidate, not something proved by the mechanism correspondence. No new capacity-value axiom, patient realization or universal ethical necessity is accepted by this review.

## 9. The inquiry belongs to the history being assessed

6 October 2026 continuation. The [new argument](RELIEF-AND-REOPENED-EVALUATION.md#8-inquiry-can-change-what-its-result-establishes) sharpens stage 3 of the proposed test: an offered consideration is part of an actual inquiry, and that inquiry can manifest an existing disposition, teach a distinction, restore a capacity, or change the concern itself. The operative boundary after that inquiry must remain distinct from the boundary before it.

For the existing formal interface, represent the original response and the later inquiry by their actual successive transitions. A description of the inquiry's before-state, event and after-state can preserve what changed in the relevant condition, available information and operative relation. Current performance belongs to the reached state and the specific task. A claim about prior capacity needs evidence connecting that performance to the earlier state; a broader capacity claim needs evidence across its proposed range. This is an application of the existing [joint-occurrence interface](YITI-REN-COFORMATION-MATH.md), with no new transition law or theorem.

The source gives a concrete warning about the unit called acquisition. In [PairedPlasticity.lean][paired], recipientPay reduces the recipient coordinate by two; recipientReturn can restore it; recipientRead then forms the acquired loss bit from the retained return and consumes the learner's fuel. That final read does not itself reduce the recipient coordinate. The whole acquisition protocol can change what is subsequently recorded, while an isolated faithful read has a narrower effect. The existing [CoupledConcernHistory.lean][coupled] result consequence_and_acquisition_are_distinct_dependent_changes preserves an actual sequence in which the coordinate changes from 40 to 38 before the acquired distinction participates in binding. These are inherited, source-checked facts. They are neither a new patient experience nor a realization of evaluative incapacity.

The concurrent [effectful-inquiry proof](INQUIRY-CONTINUATION-MATH.md#1-what-is-to-be-identified-must-persist-through-the-investigation) already represents original target labels alongside changing current configurations. It expressly distinguishes initial identification from a question about the reached state. That supplies a more specific inherited formal interface for this application; the ethical continuation adds no general identification theorem. Its label preservation does not establish which actual experience or capacity those labels describe.

The concurrent [inquiry-revision extension](../../../../theories/becoming/history/INQUIRY-REVISION.md) also supplies the earlier missing technical read/use/reconfiguration join in this concern learner, under new explicit local laws. This positive result must not be described as still absent. It does not supply a patient-side appraisal or identify the goodness of its installed trigger. The present reconstruction of the original acquisition law retains its own scope.

This resolves a representational choice for the next investigation: keep the full inquiry transition where its effects matter, rather than replacing it with a passive read merely because its purpose is to obtain evidence. Conversely, do not infer that every read transforms its target or that eliciting a response creates the capacity expressed. Different actual operations require different preservation claims.

The [primary appraisal comparison](RELIEF-APPRAISAL-EVIDENCE.md) adds a distinct constraint: being assessed at one later occasion does not automatically establish an invariant appraisal relation for questions about different temporal conditions. An adequate description may need the considered history and question context as well as the state at which the answer is produced. The study does not demonstrate that inquiry changed capacity or experience. The prior conceptual comparison permits such changes and specifies what a realization would need to identify.

Two conclusions can therefore have different evidential status. A well-supported current response can guide the available continuation even while an earlier-impairment diagnosis remains unresolved. A justified historical inference remains available where the actual transformation or faithful manifestation is sufficiently identified. Uncertainty about history does not itself remove present grounds; current benefit does not retrospectively establish either impairment or authorization.

This continuation inspected the operative advance/enabled definitions and the existing occurrence proof's stated history. It did not rerun their scientific checks. The claimed good, the evidence for its realization, the meaning of an appraisal and the scope of permission remain substantive dependencies. Treating all of them as relations within one history does not discharge them.
