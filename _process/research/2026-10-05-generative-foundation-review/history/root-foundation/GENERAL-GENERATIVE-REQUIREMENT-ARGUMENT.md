# General generative requirements and their situated realization

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored affirmative argument following [Ren's request to prove the general route](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-general-generative-proof-request.md) from continuing humanity, life and systems to the generative conditions that must be met situationally. It develops [the continuation proposal](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-continuation-depends-on-generativity.md), [the positive 生生 direction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md) and the [whole-relational method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md).

Read with the [independent counterreview](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-REVIEW.md) and its [focused source assessment](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-SOURCES.md). The latter's author performed the primary reading; this note claims no additional independent reading or source-based proof of the ethical ground.

**Affirmative conclusion:** where a specified continuation is justified, the actual relations without which it cannot occur belong within that same practical justification. Their situated realization may require preserving, restoring, reconstructing or replacing an arrangement. An alternative recipe can satisfy the requirement; successful continuation does not require every available recipe or each participant's productivity. This is a general conditional argument, not a derivation that every persisting system ought to survive or that any means serving survival is permissible.

**若生命与人类的延续在所说明的关系和范围内有根据，那么使这种延续实际可能的生成关系，就进入同一个实践根据。应当满足的是实际必要的条件及其适用关系；实现条件的具体形式可以因情境而改变，可以由替代、接续与结束来完成。并非每个现有形式都必须保留，也并非每个个体都必须生产。**

## 1. The scope of the proof route

The [earlier continuation study](CONTINUATION-GENERATIVITY.md) already develops the dependence of ongoing realization on renewal and transformation, and the [checked necessity interface](CONTINUATION-RENEWAL-MATH.md) makes one part precise. The current route brings that general dependence into an explicit practical argument. It does not discover renewal's importance anew or replace the full universal ethical objective with a formal condition on a selected controller.

[Ren's horizon correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-generativity-future-horizon.md) governs this continuation. Generativity concerns conditions of the future, potentially over horizons beyond immediate use. Proving a separate intrinsic reason for capacity stipulated permanently never to matter is not a required gate. Nor must every enabling condition first receive a detached ethical certification before its relation to justified continuing life can matter. Its significance can lie in that relation itself.

The generality sought is a **shared dependence-and-realization argument**. Once an actual necessary relationship is established, its application does not require a new moral theory for every instance. The situation determines which relationship is necessary, which realizations remain available, what they affect, and who can rightly do what. This is not an assertion that a single action rule has already been proved adequate for every situation.

The note offers philosophical justification and specifies the interpretation of existing checked results. It does not introduce an unchecked mathematical theorem or claim that a proof assistant certifies its ethical premises. The mathematical account and its correspondence must be read at their declared scope.

## 2. What is to continue?

Begin with the actual unfolding of living, learning, relating and forming further living conditions. Bodies, communities, practices, ecologies and intelligent powers are differentiated organizations within it. Their continuing reality depends on particular relations of support, transformation and succession. A scientific representation can select some of those relations without making the selected system and its surroundings independently constituted realms.

“Continuing humanity” therefore need not mean keeping every presently existing organism, institution, purpose or technique unchanged. Transformation and finite completion can be ways in which differentiated living continues. At the same time, humanity cannot be silently replaced by a population count, one institution's persistence or a successor that preserves only a name. The target's identifying relations must retain the actual lives, capabilities, relations and losses pertinent to the claim being defended.

Ren's positive criterion gives substantive ethical direction: whole unfolding creates more 生, richer possibility and diversity, and conditions for complex organization to continue developing. This does not first restrict 生 to items individually certified by another ethics. The present conditional argument asks what follows when this direction warrants a specified continuation in its actual circumstances. It does not pretend that the conditional itself has established the direction's universal sufficiency.

That separation is investigative, not ontological. Justification and the conditions through which its subject continues concern the same history. The favorable character proposed for living is not held in one domain and subsequently transferred into a neutral support machine. What the living can actually be, now and later, partly depends on how those relations operate.

The horizon is part of the target's scope. A finite achievement needs conditions sufficient for its realization and appropriate completion; indefinite continuing activity has a different demand. A long-horizon account must retain consequential effects outside an expedient cutoff, but lengthening the horizon does not establish that every retained possibility will be used. Present life is already among the actual realizations of this history, not merely a disposable input to an indefinitely deferred future.

## 3. The causal and constitutive dependence already checked

`ContinuationRenewal` supplies a general necessity result. Given an ongoing history and an independently specified renewal predicate, suppose every nonrenewing transition descends in a well-founded relation. Then there is no indefinitely renewal-free tail. With the explicit decision or renewal-or-descent condition used in the positive theorem, renewal occurs after every chosen starting point. The rank specialization gives a bounded next-renewal window under its stricter hypotheses. The [mathematical note](CONTINUATION-RENEWAL-MATH.md#3-exact-results-and-hypotheses) records the actual checked declarations and their dependencies.

This is already a conditional proof applying across every represented history satisfying its hypotheses. It is not a list of examples. Its application requires that the event called renewal really restore or reform the relevant conditions, and that unrenewed change really satisfy the exhaustion certificate. The recurrence theorem alone does not establish either correspondence. The certificate need not be a finite monetary or material stock; it can concern an independently supported relation of exhausting dependencies.

The theorem does not prove that an indefinitely continuing history exists. Nor does a witness of renewal establish that all other conditions of continuation survive. Necessity, sufficiency and actual execution must remain distinct. A nominal repair that restores one coordinate while exhausting another cannot be described as an adequate whole continuation on the strength of that coordinate alone.

The old stationary renewal construction and the checked alternating witness also show why recurrence must not be conflated with innovation. Repeated re-formation can suffice under the represented stable conditions. A requirement to replace an inherited arrangement needs an additional result: it no longer meets the relevant continuation conditions, while an adequate alternative is available if an effective response is being claimed. Simply renaming the predicate `renewal` as `innovation` would not provide this result.

These relationships give the practical argument a definite object: not an abstract command to be creative, but the actual conditions through which the continuation occurs. Deriving those conditions from a process law, and identifying their realization in life, remains substantive research. Their use as local hypotheses does not make them external to generative reality.

## 4. The general conditional practical argument

The strongest defensible argument has one continuing subject matter throughout.

1. **A specified continuation has an actual ethical ground.** The claim concerns its concrete living and formative character, its scope and its relation to the present situation. Merely adopting an intention, maintaining a preference, or calling the target “survival” does not supply that ground. Ren's generative direction is the positive candidate being developed; particular applications remain answerable to their whole effects.
2. **Some generative relationship is actually necessary for that continuation.** This is identified through the process, not defined as whatever the ethical verdict favors. Its necessity includes the stated horizon and all genuinely available alternatives relevant to the claim. An incomplete model that omits substitution cannot establish the irreplaceability of one incumbent arrangement.
3. **An available response bears on that relationship in a specified way.** Preservation, reconstruction, transfer or substitution may sustain it; severance or omission may defeat it. The response's timing, efficacy, other effects, available support and legitimate scope remain in the description. A factor logically present in every successful history is not automatically an action that this position can perform or an effective contribution it can make.
4. **The practical justification must answer to those actual realization relations.** If a response removes every route through which the specified continuation can occur, that response cannot be justified as realizing that very continuation. Where an available, legitimate response is what sustains or reconstructs the indispensable relationship, the continuation's ethical ground provides a rationale for that response through what it makes possible. This rationale remains subject to the complete situation; necessity does not erase means-related harms or other claims.

The affirmative defense of step 4 is that the reason's subject is the **actual continuation**, not an inscription of a desired result. Its enabling relations explain how that reason can be acted on. Treating their destruction as irrelevant while retaining the same practical claim would substitute a merely imagined continuation for the one offered as the ground. Attention to the generative condition corrects the content of the justification; it does not request an unrelated reward for maintaining a mechanism.

This gives a positive conditional reason, rather than only a verbal warning about inconsistency. If the continued living is worth realizing in this situation, and a legitimate reconstruction actually sustains a relationship indispensable to it, that living gives the reconstruction a point. No additional argument that the reconstructed condition is intrinsically valuable when permanently disconnected from that living is required. The ground is relational all along. A reconstructive response can also have other unfavorable consequences, so its point does not by itself settle the overall choice.

There are two limits to the conclusion. First, criticizing a false claim to serve an end does not force someone who lacks or rejects that claim to adopt the end. Universal endorsement-independent ethical warrant would have to establish the end's significance and its practical scope through the actual relations, not through everyone's declared intentions. Second, a reason for a continuation does not identify who must do what. Particular duties require a warranted account of access, contribution, responsibility, burdens and competing claims at the relevant position.

The stronger requirement follows only at the correspondingly stronger scope: **if carrying out this continuation remains justified as required here, its necessary conditions cannot all be abandoned while that same requirement is counted as fulfilled.** Where several legitimate routes remain, the requirement concerns an adequate realization, not each route. If no permissible feasible route remains, the question is impossibility, adjustment or justified completion; no theorem makes an unavailable action executable or legitimates prohibited means.

This is a genuine conditional practical argument. Its ethical ground is explicit and its realization is constrained by the checked dependence. It is not a completed derivation of first ethical authority. Calling the target justified does not prove its justification; conversely, that unfinished universal task does not prevent a sound conditional consequence from being established at its actual scope.

## 5. Universal relations, situated realizations

“All generative rules need to be followed” has to distinguish different kinds of statement:

| Kind of statement | What its application warrants |
|---|---|
| A constitutive or causal necessity for a specified continuation | Any successful continuation in that scope must satisfy it. If the continuation is justified, the condition belongs in its practical assessment. |
| One sufficient construction | This route can realize the target under its premises. Its existence does not make this route uniquely necessary. |
| A proved necessary disjunction | At least one member of this exhaustive alternative must hold in the stated scope. A demand for every member would change the result. |
| Several sufficient constructions | Each demonstrated route can work under its premises. This list need not exhaust the ways of succeeding, so membership in it is not thereby required. |
| A contingent limitation of the current arrangement | Actual change may remove or alter it; preserving the target need not preserve this limitation. |
| An ethical requirement or permission | Its normative ground, scope, burdens and means need justification in addition to causal feasibility. |

The shared schema applies to different situations because it preserves the same relation between continued realization and its actual conditions. Its instance changes as those conditions change. A reliable old practice can cease to realize the supported target; a replacement can meet the same dependence differently. Recognizing the replacement is not an exception to the general requirement. It is an accurate application of it.

This also locates learning and correction. New evidence can show that a supposed condition was unnecessary, that a chosen route no longer works, that renewal conceals displacement of costs, or that the target itself needs revision. These are different kinds of correction within the same undertaking. Successful pursuit of an unchanged local score cannot settle them. Equally, novelty alone does not establish that an old arrangement requires replacement.

“Derived from the core” must refer to actual derivations, with the hypotheses and correspondence they establish. A general dependence theorem does not retroactively turn every local model's recipe into a universal law. The core and its differentiated studies must remain answerable to one another. A branch can identify omitted relations or defeat a proposed application without thereby locating its process outside generative reality.

## 6. Whole continuation and differentiated participation

The continuing relationships can be distributed across differently situated lives, organizations and times. From their joint dependence on renewal it does not follow that every individual must reproduce, work continually, preserve a current role or maximize output. Rest, receiving care, learning, finite creation and a legitimate ending can all occur within the same continuing history. Actual contribution need not be uniform or simultaneous.

Nor does a necessary support relation establish its current maintainer's exclusive obligation or authority. A transfer can preserve the relevant continuation while changing who sustains it. Whether such a transfer is feasible and legitimate depends on its actual timing, coordination, burdens and relationships. The new [Liangzhi practical study](LIANGZHI-PRACTICAL-INTEGRATION.md) already preserves successful handover, failure, recovery and finite completion at its declared scope; those results should not be replaced by an invented universal rule that old support must always remain until new support is established.

The whole is not a larger proprietor entitled to spend its parts. The living through which a continuation occurs is itself part of what the ethical proposal concerns. Calling exploitation necessary for a selected institution does not establish that the institution's unchanged continuation is the proper target. The general argument can instead require reassessing its purpose, organization and alternatives in light of the fuller living it affects. Necessity for an unjustified target inherits no ethical authority merely from successful dependence accounting.

Temporal scope also blocks a false bootstrap. A later situation can make repair necessary without thereby justifying the earlier production of the damage. Causing a dependence does not validate itself because it then makes one's assistance indispensable. The justification must retain the actual history and compare the formative response at the time and scope in which it is being assessed. This is one reason to evaluate the whole continuing relation rather than detach a favorable repair endpoint from how it became necessary.

These are general constraints on interpreting the argument, not an endless catalogue of exceptions. They prevent a necessity claim about a continuing relation from changing unnoticed into a different claim about every actor, every current form, or every available means.

## 7. What is newly warranted, and what remains open

The conditional advance is that generative dependencies can organize practical requirements **through the same ground as the justified continuation**. They need not await proof of an unrelated intrinsic worth for each enabling condition. A target can therefore justify situational preservation, reconstruction or substitution without fixing one universal recipe. The causal proofs constrain which alleged realizations actually serve that target and which do not.

This does not confer new unconditional authority on a theory, institution or intelligence. It does not prove that every life-containing history ought to continue unchanged, that greater diversity is always better, or that all intelligence necessarily recognizes the same reasons. The new authority claim is narrower: where the practical warrant and its scope have genuinely been established, a proposal cannot count as satisfying it while defeating the independently demonstrated relations through which its subject occurs. This is inherited justification made more precise, not moral force generated by an arbitrary intention.

The remaining substantive ethical premise concerns why the specified continuing and further differentiating life deserves the proposed favor, and how that favor is addressed through actual intelligent capacities and responsibilities. Ren's positive direction supplies an account to defend. The general argument neither dismisses it as a bare preference nor reports it proved solely because continued activity needs renewal. Universal first warrant and physical applicability across all humanity remain distinct obligations.

The next proof work should therefore derive **situational reconstruction requirements from actual changing dependencies**, retain sufficient alternatives and demonstrate supported continuations where existence is claimed. It must not assume the desired ethical conclusion in a fresh predicate, define all persistence as generativity, or count a new label for the old renewal theorem as a new discovery. A failure of an inherited arrangement can warrant changing its realization; a failure of the target's ethical justification can warrant changing the target. A complete account must be able to distinguish both.

The route is thus general without being empty: it identifies the continuing realization, establishes its actual generative dependence, and makes practical appraisal accountable to the available ways of sustaining or transforming that relation. It remains conditional where its ethical and correspondence premises remain conditional. No proof of permanently unused capacity's independent intrinsic worth is required.
