## Chang and the meaning of an unresolved comparison

Ruth Chang, *The Possibility of Parity*, **Ethics** 112(4), 659–688 (2002). [Primary paper hosted on an academic's site](https://www.kevindorst.com/uploads/8/8/1/7/88177244/chang_the_possibility_of_parity.pdf). The root read the introductory comparison of positions at **printed pp.659–662**; no full assessment of the later arguments is claimed.

Chang distinguishes ignorance, incomparability, semantic indeterminacy and a proposed fourth evaluative relation, parity. Her challenge to trichotomy prevents us from simply treating every failure of the present comparison as established moral incomparability. We use that distinction as a scope discipline, not a proof of parity in our examples or endorsement of her full theory.

Our mathematical completion family may yield opposite rankings for a crossing profile. That establishes unresolved ranking within that specified family. It does not settle which philosophical account explains a real hard choice, nor prove that no further justified comparison can be supplied. This application is our inference from the distinction, not a new finding attributed to Chang.
