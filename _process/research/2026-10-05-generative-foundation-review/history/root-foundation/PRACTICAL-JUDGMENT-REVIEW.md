## 2. Substantive objection: limited sacrifice versus lexicographic priority

The formal `Better` relation and the prose's protection-superset rule have **lexicographic strength**: retaining every indexed basic protection and adding one wins regardless of the amount of secondary loss. That is stronger than rejecting unlimited sacrifice for optional expansion. The initial rationale—that optional output must not treat living conditions as indefinitely consumable—supports a protection limit, but does not on its own determine this precise priority rule.

A substantive rival preserves the same whole relational history, all affected positions and actual losses. It accepts G0 and basic significance, yet holds that sufficiently extensive **genuinely secondary, below-boundary** losses can outweigh one additional basic restoration. This is a finite comparison, not the unlimited-continuation rival already discussed in the candidate. No freely toggled ethical predicate or independently existing value-domain is needed for this disagreement. Nor may the objection be dismissed by automatically calling every sufficiently extensive secondary loss a basic deprivation: that would change the independently fixed case.

I requested that the author acknowledge the stronger commitment explicitly, assess this rival, and either defend the restricted lexical rule as an additional axiom or narrow the claimed rule and corresponding formal interface. Acknowledging this choice is compatible with the authorized axiom route. It prevents the mathematics from silently strengthening a weaker argument. This is the only requested correction before scoped acceptance.

## 3. Philosophical and nondual assessment

**G0 and additional commitments.** The premise ledger clearly marks J1–J4 as normative additions rather than derivations of their complete content from G0. No numerical welfare weights are hidden. J3's reasons concern justified reliance, changed alternatives, controllable obstruction, limited emergency response and shared provision; they are not a disguised assertion that every causal contribution entails liability. J4 openly adds authority, feasibility, allocation and procedural commitments. These are substantive proposed principles, not conclusions certified by a Lean theorem.

**Nondual method.** Capacities, affected positions, dependence and inquiry are described as formed within one unfolding. Separate formal types index local roles and relations without asserting metaphysically separate subject/object or fact/value realms. The whole is neither an aggregate owner nor a single administrator. The contributor's own life has standing without having to produce an outward return. These are appropriate local abstractions with their omissions acknowledged; the model does not purport to recreate the formation mechanism of every role.

**Four responses.** Preservation is conditional on actual support and justified alternatives. Transformation must repair the relevant living relations rather than merely raise output. Transfer distinguishes authority to withdraw from actual demand coverage and successor acceptance/capacity. Ending includes fulfillment, justified release, changed grounds and burdens, with residual losses retained. Combinations of the four modes are allowed, and finite life is not required to produce a successor. This is a general response account, not simply four names attached to an unchanged preservation imperative.

**Responsibility and discharge.** The most important distinction is between a task needing performance, a unique available contributor, and a legitimately settled assignment. Alternative allocations do not erase an existing justified undertaking, while an empty jointly feasible set cannot create universal duties through vacuity. Emergency capacity creates at most the explicitly adopted, bounded rescue responsibility. It confers no permanent sovereignty or unlimited service obligation. Release requires standing or justified procedure; earlier harms remain distinct tasks when still warranted. These conditions supply genuine content despite the absence of a universal numerical allocation formula.

**Conflict and authority.** The account does not pretend basic claims are always compatible. Its procedure includes timely inquiry, participation, urgency, limited authority, corrigibility and remaining repair. A lottery applies only under independently justified fair allocation of otherwise unresolved chances. This is a permissible general procedural account under J4, not a proof that any existing institution or convenient decision-maker is legitimate. Specific authority disputes still require case evidence and argument. That application obligation is explicit and does not leave one of the requested four response categories untreated.

**Meaningful remaining uncertainty.** Basic/secondary classification, severity, support capacity and legitimate authority can be wrongly described. The candidate supplies revision conditions rather than announcing correct classification by definition. Productive coercion retains its genuine generative gain while being assessed through separate protection and authority commitments. Irreplaceable care retains both the dependent person's need and the contributor's limits. These are substantive rival pressures, not counterexamples erased by redefining 生 as already ethically approved activity.

## 4. What the Lean results establish

The 30 theorems check the consequences of supplied predicates and relations. Several are short elimination or projection results. Their utility is to expose exactly what must be supplied, not to generate new ethical warrant from a long theorem count.

- `better_trans` and `finite_frontier` establish a strict transitive comparison and undefeated finite alternatives. The ethical use requires one stable, faithful classification over that menu. Pairwise changes of claim meanings would not be covered.
- `protection_anchor` and `mixed_repair_anchor` implement J2 once its interpretation is adopted. They do not establish that an actual repair restores life or that the sacrificed event is optional. `repair_retains_real_loss` preserves the stipulated event loss; it is not independent empirical validation.
- `supplied_ceiling_bounds_optional_gain` proves a consequence of an already supplied cutoff. It does not discover a cutoff or a universal finite sacrifice number. The prose discloses this correctly.
- `Required` includes nonemptiness as well as universality over admissible plans. Ethical admissibility is supplied by J3/J4/E, not proved by universal quantification. `unique_responder_required` presupposes warranted coverage; `assignment_respects_capacity` assumes the feasibility implication it projects. Joint feasibility is not derived from individual capacity. The exact two-task control correctly exhibits that distinction.
- Demand-indexed coverage permits a no-demand gap, but does not construct the successor. Settlement requires actual fulfillment or legitimate release. Those normative readings remain correspondence obligations.
- Robustness requires that actuality remain in the considered possibilities. Shrinking a belief set is not automatically improved knowledge. Faithful redescription preserves a requirement only when the admissible alternatives are covered and task meanings are retained. Arbitrary self-modification is not certified.

The source imports only `Std`. There is no theorem that silently treats G0 as proving J2–J4, and no claim that absence of custom Lean axioms implies absence of normative premises. The scope is an inference interface, joined by explicit correspondence to prior mechanisms, rather than a newly unified physical construction of every response.
