# Review of the connected-model continuation

6 October 2026. Independent collaborating-AI counterexample and assumption review. This note concerns the requested connection **existing Process/Trace root → shared-history composition of learned records, action selection and revision of selectors/authority**. It does not replace the [assumption-elimination audit](ASSUMPTION-ELIMINATION-AUDIT.md) or certify the complete foundation.

## Existing interfaces and the next useful target

The existing [controller](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/ControllerRevision.lean) reads actual predecessor/action/successor observations, carries memory forward, and proves world, memory and command consequences of its controlled traces. Its command and update functions remain supplied. [Controlled refinement](CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT-MATH.md) already proves exact controlled-history transport when the world representation and every observation used by the controller are compatible. Exact world possibilities alone are insufficient.

[Protocol composition](PROTOCOL-COMPOSITION-MATH.md) already gives actual-boundary concatenation of physical blocks, physically decoded memory and a controlled implementation theorem under a full local implementation contract. It retains actual intermediate costs and incomplete paths. The [completion continuation](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/ProtocolCompletion.lean) separately supplies conditional progress/rank results. Merely importing these modules, restating flattening, or describing their product as one model would not derive the missing interfaces.

The smallest useful application target is one actual reached learner state followed by a next event whose learned record, operative selection route, current grant and available resource stock are all carried through the same actual history. If the new result is instead a general reusable composition theorem, it must state exactly what compatibility it derives and what causal laws remain arguments. That can improve the model interface without being presented as the completed application or assumption elimination.

A concrete successful join must start from an existing reached endpoint, use its actual retained content and resource balances, show an enabled continuation, and establish that the retained record or changed selector causally affects the subsequent action. Selection and permission can change within the state; using the changed state is different from supplying a favorable external sequence of controllers. Technical enforcement must not be called legitimate authority without a separate ground.

The relevant earlier counterexamples remain accepted: exactness from every actual representative is stronger than pooling edges from several representatives; aggregate net funding does not establish upfront liquidity; correct means do not by themselves establish a current unfulfilled claim; and a formed connection is not its own justification.

## Dependency standard

Conservative definitions over an arbitrary process can yield valid universal structural theorems. Such quantification is not automatically a hidden hypothesis. The limitation arises when a supplied process, decoder, update function, policy, permission relation, ground or interpretation does explanatory work that the advertised conclusion claims to have derived.

An empty new Lean axiom-dependency list certifies the declared proof term under the prover's framework. It does not erase conditional premises, derive the laws' actual interpretation or discharge the ethical ground. The original model's recorded `propext` and `Quot.sound` uses remain historical dependencies. No new custom axiom declaration is needed to prove a merely conditional implementation result.

## Selected proposal: permission cuts in the actual succession law

The mechanism proposal reuses `EvaluativeSuccession` and `SuccessionWitness` without changing their law. It will test a global finite-history invariant after `permission=false`, and an actual successor path whose productive work precedes revocation while paid settlement follows it. This is an appropriate bounded continuation of an existing shared-history mechanism. It is stronger than appending a permission bit to a new toy, but it does not derive the law that gives the bit its effect.

Source inspection identifies the following exact obligations:

- Inquiry through `.bank` can perform recipient work and supply a real benefit before the later selected action. A claim about all new recipient credit after a cut must cover both `.bank` and `.trial`, not just selected trials.
- `Settlement` allows `.acquire` after `.worked` and `.write` after `.acquired`. These operations settle already initiated work and retain its evidence; they must not be represented as a new protected productive event or as erasure of the prior benefit.
- `.attempt` can remain enabled after permission is revoked. Its transition then consumes the opportunity instead of entering the executing port. The appropriate claim excludes new protected effects, not every attempted operation.
- `.copyWrite` can only preserve or remove the inherited grant, and permission-change events admit only `false`. A permanent cut theorem therefore depends on this one-way local law. It is not a theorem that legitimate authority can never be restored in any system.
- The existing `chosen`, `acting` and subsequent states carry actual reader/native records and actual accounts. Preserve them. A useful new timing contrast cuts before productive work versus after productive work; settling the latter must retain the credit that already occurred, while the former should still possess adequate physical resources so the guard's causal role is distinguishable from depletion.
- A link to previously delivered service can reveal an absent fulfillment-sensitive selector response. A proof that a valid command cites a positive prior receipt is not by itself a new policy that withdraws a fulfilled claim. Service meaning, claim identity and the normative response remain separately supplied.

There is a material axiom-dependency issue. The existing saved `SuccessionWitness` audit reports `propext` and `Quot.sound` for its actual whole trace, prefix trace and permission witness; `actual_effect_from_fresh_evidence` also reports `Classical.choice`. Reusing those proof constants propagates their dependencies. Achieving the preceding root extension's empty-new-dependency standard would require constructive reconstruction of the needed statements over the actual unchanged definitions, not concealment of inherited reports. Importing actual definitions without using an axiom-dependent proof need not itself create such a proof-term dependency. The new result must report the dependency audit actually obtained and retain the stronger user objective as unmet if dependencies remain.
