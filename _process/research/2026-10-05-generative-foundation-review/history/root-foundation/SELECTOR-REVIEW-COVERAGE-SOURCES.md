## 2. Abstract interpretation: a substantive predecessor

Patrick Cousot, “Abstract Interpretation”, *ACM Computing Surveys* 28(2), 1996, pp. 324–328. Read the principles discussion on printed pp. 324–326 in the [author-hosted paper](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cousot/publications.www/Cousot-ACM-Computing-Surveys-v28-n2-p324-328-1996.pdf).

The account explains sound approximation relative to a specified semantics. It separates choosing a soundness relation from constructing abstract operations, and describes compositional derivation under explicit hypotheses. Some abstractions preserve safety information while omitting other trace distinctions. This is a strong antecedent for our distinction between retaining every description and retaining the information required by a particular certificate. Our application does not establish a new general theory of abstraction. A mathematical semantics and its sound approximation also do not establish their correspondence to actual living relations or the warrant of G0/J.

The 1977 joint paper's [author summary](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cousot/COUSOTpapers/POPL77.shtml) was also read. Its scanned PDF did not provide usable text in this tool path; no full-paper reading or reconstruction of its proofs is claimed. The 1996 principles discussion is the substantive reading used here.

## 3. Reward-process modification: a distinct established problem

Tom Everitt, Marcus Hutter, Ramana Kumar and Victoria Krakovna, “Reward Tampering Problems and Solutions in Reinforcement Learning: A Causal Influence Diagram Perspective”, 2021 version. Read the abstract/introduction, §3.3 discussion, §§4.1–4.3 and conclusions in the [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.04734v5).

The paper distinguishes changing a reward function from changing its information inputs. It also considers model-dependent interpretation of rewards and states assumptions behind avoiding particular instrumental incentives. Its conclusion retains reward specification as an additional problem. This directly prevents presenting self-modification of assessment channels as our discovery. Our study asks which partial-order comparison certificates survive changes to their application family; it supplies neither an RL incentive theorem nor a solution to reward specification. Avoiding a tampering incentive and actually maintaining warranted semantic coverage are different achievements.

## 4. Evidence boundary

没有新增经验实验、受试者研究或真实部署评估，也没有建立发表优先权。构造案例用于核对论证与形式接口；文献提供强对手与既有方法，不证明当前方案优于它们。对新生活关系的取得、解释与基本分类仍需本理论已有的实质对应，并不能通过一个覆盖关系的名字完成。
