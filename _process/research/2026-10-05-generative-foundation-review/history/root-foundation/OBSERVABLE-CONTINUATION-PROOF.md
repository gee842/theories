## A common support mechanism with executable alternatives

One demand has two current connection modes. The fixed left route works in the false mode; the right route works in the true mode. The prior target is the old left route's protection. The candidate adds support in the other mode. Their event-level union therefore calls for protection in both modes, while old maintenance only requires preserving the first mode's success.

The local physical law offers three actions: left, right and, when in the independently permitted menu, genuinely parallel provision through both routes. A single route costs one budget unit; parallel provision costs two. In this stipulated complementary-connection law, parallel operation supports the demand in either mode. This is a supplied physical arrangement with a resource requirement, not evidence that parallel operation always succeeds or has this cost.

The existing planner has two queries: a blind query returns false at zero cost; a sensing query reads the current mode and consumes one unit. Its response branch uses only the returned Boolean. The hidden mode participates in generating the reading and the route's actual effect; the choice rule does not directly read it. Observation is faithful, and the connection mode remains stable through this read/use interval. These correspondence assumptions are explicit; no future outcome is revealed to an unfunded or blind rule.

`probe` and `respond` update the budget in the actual state. The observable success field is set by the route-effect law. It is not assigned merely because the planner selected a route. The success test checks the requested profile against that field. The physical transition functions are identical whether the target is old maintenance or the larger extension; only the requested success specification changes.

The last three statements are checked by explicit Boolean cases and kernel reduction of the finite implementation. They are finite witnesses, not substitutes for the general composition theorem. Under the two equally weighted represented conditions the common total is two. The exact source records event masses and strict comparison, rather than reporting measured empirical success rates.

The one-unit failure is restricted to the listed extension plans. **It does not make old maintenance impossible.** A correct paid reading would use up the only unit, leaving no funded response. A separately retained application example checks this failed `run`; another instantiates the generic composition with the funded sensing route's independently checked actual output. These two examples add no theorem count.

## Scope of realization

The positive results establish **one support delivery within the supplied window**, not uninterrupted service while querying. The model does not assert that intermediate states satisfy an ongoing continuous-support target. Such an undertaking needs intermediate-state requirements, a maintained old chain during acquisition, or another independently warranted realization. The target cannot silently be strengthened from one timely delivery to continuous provision.

The costs are local budget units. No separate physical clock or selector-search cost is modeled. The repertoire search is executable, but its runtime, the cost of constructing its model and its availability before a real deadline remain outside the proof. Menus supply independently permitted actions; physical capability does not establish permission.

The actual probe state shows deduction before response. However, `run` returns `Option State`: failure returns `none`, which does not carry a retained post-failure ledger. The checks therefore do not prove that failed attempts preserve a full expense history or that a caller cannot reset the initial budget for retries. No such retry guarantee is claimed.

Likewise, the parallel route is an explicitly available physical alternative in this local law. Its success does not follow in arbitrary systems merely by writing a Boolean disjunction. If shared resources, interference or correlated failures prevent both routes from working together, this law must change. If observations can fail or the relation changes before use, the already completed [fallible inspection](FALLIBLE-INSPECTION-INTEGRATION.md) and [situated tracking results](JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#20-situational-awareness-and-continued-convergence) provide separate conditional resources; this module neither reproduces nor automatically integrates their guarantees.

The maintained and newly protected events concern the same demand. Their normative standing and omitted burdens remain substantive obligations. A strict success-mass increase is not by itself an all-things-considered ethical ordering. The result tests implementation of the specified undertaking under the existing G0/J route; it does not derive a universal probability allocation or a new normative axiom.
