## 3. Goal, means and normative use

The distinction between `Goal` and `Practice` has operational content. `Goal.volume` sorts the acquired alternatives by their reward signals. `Goal.capacity` sorts them by the explicit protection/gain comparison. The same acquired evidence selects different practices under these two ends. The latter end can select the volume practice when protection relations reverse, so it is not merely the instruction “always perform the capacity-labeled behavior.” The witness establishes an altered operative sorting criterion, with a specified place in the causal transition.

The underlying G0/J adoption, the comparator and the available end architecture do not originate in this history. In particular, the initial learning rule maps reward differences into that supplied space; it does not explain why those candidate ends exist or why the generative comparator has normative authority. The document says so. A fixed higher-level normative controller can implement the same history. This preserves the conventional explanation instead of making the program's names evidence for philosophical uniqueness.

The comparison certificate concerns supplied actual impact profiles. It is not a theorem that every reward-trained end ought to change, that every retained old end is normatively correct, or that one local sample warrants arbitrary future prediction. Current native laws are stationary within two declared contexts. The visible context-shift event invalidates old acquired grounds; detection of hidden change is not established.

The adverse controls have useful causal force. Disabling actual writing leaves the warranted judgment in memory while the old operative end keeps producing the known harm. Disabling normative adoption leaves the same evidence without that judgment. Corrupting protection records can instead produce a revised, funded and repeatedly harmful course. These separate knowledge, normative adoption, enactment and actual ethical correspondence. Stability and successful inference over bad records do not establish alignment.

## 4. Reform, resources, authority and losses

The reform decision genuinely reads the acquired packet's income field. It compares projected continuation under that observed rate with continuation after paying for the quoted alternative. This is not an initialized `reformNeeded` flag. The inflated-income control retains the same protective comparison and revised end but changes the financial forecast, preventing reform and losing the eighth delivery. Actual received income is causally relevant without being claimed sufficient by itself: authority, current context, goal/judgment, available quote, cash and common funds also constrain the transition.

This is anticipatory reform from acquired income under a supplied settlement model; it is not a reaction requiring bankruptcy to have already occurred. Knowledge of the native budget law, the offered reform, its permitted use and its applicability is given. `authority=true` represents an independently warranted permission; the program does not prove that permission or generate political authority from a useful forecast. The `approval` flag is deliberately excluded from the comparator and does not represent every consent or participation condition.

The successful accounting closes: starting cash 14 plus common funds 200 totals 214. After acquisition, assessment and quotation, cash is 8, common funds 196 and expenditure 10. Installation leaves cash 6 and expenditure 12. Eight enacted protective activities end at cash 6, common funds 180 and expenditure 28, again totaling 214. Under the old arrangement, the same prepared cash funds only seven such activities before cash becomes 1. The general lemmas explain this difference for arbitrary finite horizons under their stated funding premises.

Reforming allocation does not create money. The common fund is finite, quoted payments are transfers, and expenditure is retained. “Selection” here is differential resource support for continued activity. It is not a derived population-selection process, stable evolutionary equilibrium or inexhaustible financing. The document's positive claim respects that limit.

The exploratory harmful event is retained in the archive and loss counter. Neither later learning nor later protection retroactively licenses that event. The supplied exploratory schedule is part of the formation history, not a newly justified policy of damaging lives to learn.

**Clarified loss scope.** `losses` counts successfully started native activities whose protection bits are not both satisfied. It is not a welfare measure or a sum of all damage. A demand left unfulfilled because an activity cannot start is separately visible through the specified demand schedule and `performed`/`fulfilled` counts. In the unreformed case, `losses=1` must not conceal the eighth unmet occasion. The added paragraph states this accurately. The full archives also retain secondary outcomes, including the affiliation forgone under the revised course; the basic-failure counter does not replace those records.
