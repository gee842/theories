## 1. The affirmative connection tested

The previous attempt did not obtain full admission from finite coherence, renaming neutrality or absence of an arbitrary exclusion. The current argument asks whether an already constituted relation grounds a derivative mathematical object through its own organization.

Given `R(i,x)`, the witness occurrences are pairs satisfying that relation. The new formalization identifies two occurrences exactly when they have the same output `x`. It then proves inverse correspondences between this quotient and the support of `∃ i, R(i,x)`. The actual inclusion into the base is retained. `projected_content_unique` establishes that a predicate with exactly these outputs has precisely the projected membership.

This tests an identity mechanism: the output object obtains its membership and identity from the given relation, while distinctions between alternative witnesses are omitted. That omission is accountable and does not introduce another membership choice. Index, witness and output are differentiated roles within the same relational organization; no independent observer or separate mathematical substance is required by the construction.

The affirmative philosophical step is to accept such a derivative role as an object in an appropriate extensional domain. The review accepts this as a substantive relative proposal, supported by the identity mechanism. It does not accept it as a theorem that the minimal root necessarily licenses every abstraction or every witness relation. Determinate content and object admission remain different explanatory obligations within the one process.

## 2. Exact admission interface

The formal admission condition applies to a presentation together with its output map into a fixed base. `support_admitted` assumes admission of the whole witness presentation, closure under the specified output quotient, and transport along inverse correspondences that preserve outputs. It concludes admission of the support presentation with its inclusion into that base.

Keeping the output map matters. A correspondence between carriers that ignores their values would not preserve the subject's membership role. The current interface retains that role while leaving its closure premises visible.

This theorem is not an automatic assertion of membership in a separately selected predicate-quantifier family. A declared correspondence from the admitted presentation to that family, and its warrant, remain necessary. The abstract relation range, quotient construction and classical witness selection are supplied to the mathematical explanation. Their local supplied status does not identify them as external to generative reality.

## 3. A formation rule independent of target membership

`parityStages` starts empty and extends stage `n` by the element `2*n`. The definition accepts no arbitrary target predicate. Lean checks its exact finite content, increasing stages and union equal to the even predicate. The relational module's `parity_projection` and `parity_iff_output` connect that recurrence directly to the witness quotient.

This explains the family through one common rule, rather than selecting its stages by consulting a supplied target predicate. It calibrates the proposed relative admission argument: already warranted natural-number indexing and arithmetic determine a whole relation, whose projected support has exactly the expected membership.

Natural-number arithmetic, the interpretation of the whole recurrence and the abstraction principle remain commitments. This does not prove that a physical history completes infinitely many stages. The earlier finite/cofinite family still admits every finite stage and excludes its union. That is a boundary for its weaker closure regime, not a full-target rival or a refutation of the root.

The theorem `parity_admitted` must be read carefully: it assumes the general finite-chain closure principle. Its assumptions already have full predicate strength on `Nat` when finite admission is given. The bounded positive contribution is the explicit recurrence and its exact abstraction, combined with the separately defended relative warrant; that theorem does not establish a weaker local warrant by itself.

## 4. Why countable indexing does not finish fullness

`full_iff_finite_chain` proves that full predicate admission on `Nat` is equivalent to admitting all finite predicates and the union of every ambient increasing finite chain. `chain_iff_directed_given_finite` connects this formulation to the earlier all-directed-family closure under finite admission.

For arbitrary `S`, `prefixChain S` selects the elements satisfying `S` below each stage bound. Every stage is finite, but selecting the whole chain already uses `S`. Its union reconstructs `S`. Restricting the index to natural numbers therefore does not independently justify all the selected relations.

The formation operation and the range of its available arguments need separate accounts. A justified particular relation supports its abstraction. A universal schema applied to every ambient relation carries the full range through those arguments. Calling a parameter-dependent expression uniform does not remove this dependency.

The sequence/fullness distinction was already present in [closure resolution P2](MATHEMATICS-CLOSURE-RESOLUTION.md#proposition-p2--which-union-closure-yields-all-subsets). ## 6. Result and next obligation

The approach has earned a defended relative connection: formation of a specified whole relation can explain the membership of a derivative extensional object through output-preserving abstraction. The concrete recurrence and quotient mechanism make that connection inspectable. This is more specific than demanding that every coherent possibility be included.

The attempted universal inference remains unsupported. The next substantive step must justify formation and admission of the whole relations needed for full coverage, including the passage to further predicate levels, or establish the proposed common dependence against the matched rival. A further target-dependent prefix representation would not advance that obligation. Relative abstraction can remain valid if the stronger universal programme ultimately needs revision.
