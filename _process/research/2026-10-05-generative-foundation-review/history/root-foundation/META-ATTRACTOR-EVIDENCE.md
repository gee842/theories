## Adaptive networks

[Gross and Blasius, Adaptive Coevolutionary Networks, 2008](https://arxiv.org/html/0709.1858v2). Read abstract, introduction and §II. The paper studies feedback between activity and changing interaction structure, including cases where activity modifies the topology that shapes subsequent activity. It supplies a substantive neighboring account. Such coupling supports the proposed explanatory architecture, but an arbitrary adaptive network need not reproduce a particular selection organization across carrier changes. No exclusivity claim for the meta-attractor vocabulary follows.

The HTML rendering carries an August 2026 rendering date; the article is the 2008 publication, as recorded in the [version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.1858). This assessment does not infer a new paper date from the renderer.

## Selection across scientific generations

[Smaldino and McElreath, The Natural Selection of Bad Science, 2016](https://arxiv.org/html/1605.09511v1). Read abstract, model setup, the reproduction paragraphs, payoff table, evolutionary results and discussion of institutional change. Successful simulated laboratories generate successor laboratories that inherit and mutate methods. The study supplies a concrete comparator for selective succession without requiring deliberate misconduct.

The publication incentives are supplied model parameters. This reading therefore supports selection and transmission across carriers; it does not independently establish that those carriers endogenously reconstruct the institution's evaluation rule. The literature maintained by the model can affect later replication opportunities, but any claim that this closes the exact proposed selection-condition loop needs a separate route-specific analysis. Treating all its results as demonstrated institutional capture would overstate it.

## Moving thresholds

[Wieczorek, Xie and Ashwin, Rate-Induced Tipping, arXiv v3, 2021](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.15497v3). Read abstract and introduction, including the distinction between changing stability and failure to track under changing inputs. This provides an established mathematical reason to distinguish state movement, changing effective conditions and rate-sensitive loss of tracking. Its hypotheses concern particular nonautonomous dynamical systems. The present discrete impulse-budget example is not a proof of rate-induced tipping, and the cited external results have not been re-mechanized here.

## Informative action and recoverability

[Berkenkamp, Turchetta, Schoellig and Krause, Safe Model-based Reinforcement Learning with Stability Guarantees, 2017](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.08551v2). Read abstract, introduction and §2, including explicit continuity, calibrated-model and initial-policy requirements. The paper treats informative exploration and maintained recovery capacity as connected requirements. This is established prior work, not empirical support for the meta-attractor construction. The [new continuation](META-ATTRACTOR-CARRIER-DIAGNOSIS.md) gives a discrete, resource-constrained instance for diagnosing a specific return route through carrier succession. Neither account makes stability equivalent to ethical goodness.
