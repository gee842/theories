## Empirical comparisons

**Sadacca, Wied, Lopatina, Saini, Nemirovsky and Schoenbaum (2018), “Orbitofrontal neurons signal sensory associations underlying model-based inference in a sensory preconditioning task.”** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.30373); [version 2 PDF](https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/30373/elife-30373-v2.pdf). Read introduction, results, discussion, relevant figure captions and analysis methods from the publisher's version 2 API record, checked against the PDF. A version 1 PDF was encountered first; conclusions use version 2. The principal relation is acquisition before cue-reward pairing, not an absence of every contextual or biological value.

The main text reports A/C p=.044 for its paired t-test and p=.017 for its ANOVA; Figure 1's caption instead gives p>.05. Different analysis descriptions may contribute, but the discrepancy is not resolved here. The [archived analysis repository](https://github.com/elifesciences-publications/OFC_SPC_17/tree/47f19e376dce13f56ad2171c467f47218098ae66) contains code rather than the raw inputs needed for this replication. Read README, `load_spc.m`, and selected test calls in `bo_behavior_probe.m` at that exact commit. The coexistence of ANOVA and paired-test code does not independently decide the result. No new 2018 computation is claimed.

**Hart, Sharpe, Gardner and Schoenbaum (2020), “Responding to preconditioned cues is devaluation sensitive and requires orbitofrontal cortex during cue-cue learning.”** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.59998); [PubMed primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32831173/). Read the publisher's version 2 introduction, results, discussion, methods and source-data captions. The published exclusions precede the supplied summaries; this round adds none. The intervention does not isolate representation from attention or identify the only effective temporal window. The devaluation subset is not a separate replication cohort.

**Executed data assessment.** [Analysis methods, source ranges and reproduction instructions](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/semantic-evidence/README.md); [machine receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/semantic-evidence/verification.json). Four selected reported statistics match rounded values. Their numerical reproduction supports source consistency; it does not independently confirm the semantic interpretation or causal specificity.

## Philosophical rivals

**Hutto and Satne (2015), “The Natural Origins of Content,” Philosophia 43, 521–536.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11406-015-9644-0); [author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/284246869_The_Natural_Origins_of_Content). Read the introduction, teleosemantic criticism, the sections distinguishing basic directedness and content, the social-origins proposal and conclusion. The comparison concerns their proposed division of explanatory work; it is not a comprehensive review of radical enactivism. Their text explicitly distinguishes truth from communal consensus and presents its natural-origins account as a programme needing further detail. This is a stronger primary comparison than the earlier book-abstract-only assessment.

**Shea, “Realist Representational Explanations of Agency Should Require Some Unity of Purpose.”** Read the [author's August 2025 accepted-manuscript PDF](https://www.nicholasshea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Shea_25_Repn_agency_AI_PhiMiSci_PrePr.pdf), selected introduction and §§6–7 (printed pp.14–18). The [author's publications page](https://www.nicholasshea.co.uk/publications/agency-and-behavioural-control/) lists publication in 2026; DOI 10.33735/phimisci.2026.12098. The final publisher version was not inspected. Shea distinguishes local representation from agency involving coordination among goals and behavior. Our comparison uses that distinction, not an attributed claim that coherence proves ethics or that all agency requires representations.

## Result of integrating the evidence

The empirical and philosophical comparisons bear on different claims. A contribution to learned inference, the attribution of content, reflective understanding and justified practical force each need their own argument, connected through the same activity. The evidence positively constrains a fixed direct-reward account and strengthens the acquired-relationship route. It does not decide every representational/content-free dispute. That residual comparison is the next substantive task, not an excuse to discard the acquired relation or declare the whole theory proved.
