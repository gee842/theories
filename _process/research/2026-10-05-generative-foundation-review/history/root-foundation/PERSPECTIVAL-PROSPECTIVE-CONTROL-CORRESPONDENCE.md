# Prospective control: separability, contribution and the actual alternatives

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored correspondence and conceptual review of the continuation selected in [the ratification integration](PERSPECTIVAL-RATIFICATION-INTEGRATION.md#7-what-is-gained-and-what-is-still-supplied), following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). **Scope: constructed philosophical comparisons and a premise audit. No empirical intervention, new mathematical proof or completed universal ethical derivation.**

**Finding:** disclosed agreement removes the earlier breached-undertaking argument; it does not settle every permissible term of formation. But portability of an acquired skill also does not establish that the formation history leaves no warranted claim. A defensible comparison must identify the good, the claimed power, the provider's actual contribution, and the feasible alternative at the relevant time. Otherwise either side can win by naming its preferred bundle as the good.

The universal inference under test is **whole formation and continuation → warranted differences in practical authority**. This note narrows a discriminating comparison. It does not infer a universal anti-control principle from a larger domain of explanation, or reinstate independent subjects who subsequently exchange value.

## 1. What the new case must genuinely change

The earlier case supplies a warranted undertaking to issue a portable competence record, followed by an additional condition. Its positive reason for delivery cannot be carried into this round after removing the undertaking. In the present construction, the provider accurately discloses the condition at entry, participants adequately understand its stipulated content and agree, and no later term exceeds what was disclosed. Whether that agreement warrants its condition is the question; it is not built into the description as “valid authorization.”

Training, joint work, tools, acquired discrimination and shared recognition form the provider and learner roles within a common history. The acquired capacity can later participate in activity beyond those original arrangements. The investigation asks which relations continue to matter and why. It does not require a capacity to become causally self-sufficient before the question is intelligible.

Accurate disclosure should also not mean impossible advance acquaintance with every future experience. The case can stipulate understanding of the arrangement, significant anticipated consequences and relevant uncertainty. It must distinguish that from already having the appreciation training will form. Neither perfect prediction nor an unexplained inability to anticipate personal change should settle the ethics of commitment.

## 2. Specify the controlled activity before appraising the condition

Use two different target descriptions rather than pretending they identify the same good.

**J — Continued common performance.** An ensemble cultivates skills, trust and a distinctive collaborative performance. Participation at a particular performance requires actual contributions and coordination by other members. A disclosed rehearsal commitment can be part of the work through which that performance becomes possible. Someone who leaves retains acquired musical capacities but cannot thereby require the others to reproduce the same shared performance on demand. Continued collaboration and independent performance are different realizations.

**S — Further exercise of a learned capacity.** A workshop teaches a general technique. Later a participant can demonstrate it using independently available tools, materials and opportunities; this exercise need not use the original workshop's continuing labor, confidential material, name or scarce equipment. At entry the workshop nevertheless required continuing affiliation as a condition of independent use. A coordinated recognition network makes that condition practically enforceable. No portable credential or unconditional permission was promised. Whether the network ought to enforce the condition is disputed, not an assumed legal fact.

For S, the power needs a concrete operation: refusing venue access, cancelling an otherwise usable recognition, demanding further affiliation activity, or another specified act. A sentence in an agreement is not by itself physical control of skill. Conversely, calling the imposed condition “membership-only learning” does not demonstrate that it constitutes the technique's successful exercise. The substantive rival may argue that a wider practice of loyal membership is itself valuable; that adds a good to be compared, rather than making the operational technique identical with it.

The targets can overlap. Outside exercise can preserve a technique while losing belonging; an inside performance can also include an unnecessary restriction on unrelated activity. Classify a particular relation and power, not an entire institution as intrinsically inseparable or wholly portable.

The concurrent [ethical-identification correction](WHOLE-ETHICAL-IDENTIFICATION-ARGUMENT.md) applies directly. The fact that a capacity was developed, remains available or can expand does not establish that its continued exercise is good. Ending an aspiration or genuinely attenuating a capacity can realize contentment. The present prospective-control comparison must therefore identify a particular worthwhile exercise and its actual consequences if preventing it is to supply a reason against control. It cannot use a generic capacity count or a requirement of perpetual self-development. Nor does a person's own worthwhile ending by itself authorize another office to impose the same ending: those are different histories and authority questions.

## 3. Four questions hidden by “separable”

| Question | Evidence or stipulated comparison needed | What an affirmative answer does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Can the specified activity occur without fresh contributions from the original provider? | Actual technique, materials, other participants and support routes; no silently required input. | That earlier contributions or warranted commitments leave no present claim. |
| Does the proposed alternative preserve the particular good being compared? | Whether the target is a technique, this shared performance, a relationship, recognition or something else. | Preservation of every good in the original association. |
| Can this participant actually use the alternative now? | Access, competence recognition, resources, timing, burdens and the enforcement network. | Ethical permission to ignore a term, or an obligation on someone else to fund the alternative. |
| What authority remains in the formation history? | Content and scope of agreement, contribution, reciprocity, effects on other participants, and their proposed justification. | A verdict merely from causal ancestry, consent, portability or present preference. |

These are analytical distinctions within one history. “Normatively separable” cannot be an extra factual premise meaning that the challenged claim has already disappeared; it would contain the conclusion under examination.

## 4. Investment cannot be erased, and cannot name its own limit

Provider contribution is part of the whole: instruction can consume time, forego other worthwhile activity, require continuing renewal and expose contributors to risk. Their actual good is not an inconvenient private remainder to be subtracted from the comparison. A restriction can help make a valuable training route feasible. Describing expenditure as sunk at a later boundary does not show that an independently warranted claim of reciprocity has expired.

Nevertheless, several different claims must not be collapsed:

- The contribution helped cause the capacity.
- A specified return was contemplated or agreed.
- Without some supported return, the training route would not be available.
- This particular form and duration of control is warranted.

The first three can matter to the fourth without deciding it. A lifetime veto, a fixed service period, payment of a disclosed amount, loss of access to genuinely scarce ongoing facilities, and a refusal to use the provider's name are different proposed powers. Their practical adequacy and normative scope require separate assessment. This is not a claim that one of those arrangements is always permitted or always impermissible.

Compare especially a **completed bounded contribution** with **continuing provision**. If instruction has been supported and the stipulated participant contribution completed, controlling unrelated later exercise needs its own defense. That observation does not prove that all legitimate claims were exhausted: the content and warrant of such claims remain disputed. If the provider continues to supply essential equipment or coordination, an alternative pretending that input is costless or already owned by the learner misdescribes the activity.

Cost recovery is narrower than completion of a commitment. A disclosed six-week collaboration can still have a worthwhile final performance, distributed risks and others' justified reliance after teaching expenses have been recovered in week three. The final weeks are not thereby an artificial penalty. Likewise, gratitude or lasting affection can survive settled expenditure without becoming an office's entitlement to demand any expression of them. Track a proposed reason, an agreed duty, and an enforceable discretion separately; neither having the first nor exerting the third establishes the second's content.

The strongest provider rival therefore survives mere portability. It can say that the accurately disclosed arrangement enabled worthwhile formation that otherwise would not have occurred, embodied a legitimate shared commitment, or supported future learners. The response must examine those particular grounds. Neither “you would otherwise have had nothing” nor “the technique can now travel” is a complete ethical answer.

## 5. Separate the time and author of the alternative

Three comparisons are often mistakenly treated as one:

| Comparison | Who can enact what, at which boundary? | Feasibility obligation |
|---|---|---|
| **Design** | Before training, the organizing practice can offer a different financing, membership or control arrangement. | Identify the resources and participation that actually sustain it. A less restrictive offer is not equally feasible merely because one can write it down. |
| **Present release** | After formation, specified decision-makers can stop enforcing a condition, possibly after an independently warranted completion or settlement. | Account for remaining obligations, continuing inputs, effects on other learners and the actual enforcement path. |
| **Present transformation** | Participants and others can build another practice, replace a support route or alter a constitutive relationship. | Include construction costs, lost goods, authority and transition time. Do not compare its eventual success with the original arrangement while omitting how to reach it. |

A participant's unilateral departure is not the same act as an organizer designing an open offer. A new subsidy available at the present boundary was not necessarily available when formation began. Conversely, a past funding necessity does not establish that its former control remains necessary forever. These are different claims about one developing history, not permission to choose whichever temporal baseline favors a verdict.

In the cleanest proposed S-comparison, the same training contribution can be supported under a disclosed bounded return, the nominated return is actually met, and independent exercise is feasible without further provider activity. Those are **construction premises requiring demonstration in an application**. Even then, the ethical claim that these facts defeat continued control is a substantive candidate, not part of the feasibility stipulation. If the only available open design destroys a worthwhile training route or shifts serious burdens onto others, the clean comparison fails; it does not follow that every restrictive offer is justified instead.

## 6. Disciplined adjudication matrix and counterexamples

| Specified case | What it tests or rules out | Remaining ethical question |
|---|---|---|
| J: the acquired good is this continuing collaborative performance, and the alternative requires others' ongoing contribution. | Defeats the claim that release preserves the same good solely because one member retains skill. | What commitments the actual collaboration warrants, and how withdrawal or completion should occur. |
| S: technique, outside support and reliable recognition are available; no continued provider contribution is needed. | Defeats an alleged technical necessity for permanent affiliation, if that was the stated explanation. | Whether historical contribution, reciprocal commitment or another ground nonetheless warrants the restriction. |
| A fixed disclosed participation period supports identifiable training and a common achievement. | Challenges any rule that all intentionally formed dependence or narrowed options are adverse. | Adequacy and limits of that commitment, including serious changes and affected claims. |
| Training costs are recovered early while a bounded performance commitment and others' justified reliance remain. | Defeats the inference from recovered costs to discharge of every restriction. | What remains due under that independently defended commitment; it does not authorize unrelated perpetual demands. |
| A proposed open alternative relies on money, tools or other people's effort that is not actually available. | Defeats the claimed practical comparison; the alternative is merely imagined. | The appraisal of the available arrangement, further provision or declining formation. |
| Bounded support and participant contributions are completed; continuing control is defended solely by having caused the skill. | Exposes the missing argument from contribution to the precise continuing power. | Whether a stronger justification can be supplied; absence of that one inference is not a complete universal prohibition. |
| Outside exercise retains technique but ends a valued shared identity or shifts burdens onto remaining members. | Defeats an assertion that every relevant good is preserved by portability. | Whether the change is still warranted and which transition response answers the losses. |
| All terms were accurately disclosed; the participant later rejects them after acquiring appreciation and dependence. | Rejects automatic invalidation from regret or changed evaluation; also rejects treating original assent as a timeless verdict. | Which commitment can bind this changed situation and why. Neither temporal perspective has automatic priority. |
| Enforcement stops and willing affiliation persists. | Can challenge the necessity of continued enforcement for present affiliation, if an alternative is genuinely usable. | The restriction's formative contribution, other dependence and the moral scope of current agreement. |
| A participant genuinely ends a burdensome striving and realizes contentment instead of further exercising the acquired technique. | Defeats a diagnosis of degeneration from lost exercise or capacity alone. | The transformation's actual good, authority, continuing commitments and effects on others. It does not certify every imposed restriction. |

These are proposed philosophical controls, not empirical examples of particular institutions. No case establishes a basin or predicts that restrictive practices must collapse. Some restrictions can persist and reproduce sincere approval. The ethical question concerns what that continuation realizes and what authority its organization warrants.

## 7. Premise ledger and the conclusion it permits

**Descriptive and correspondence premises in the constructed comparison:** content and understanding of disclosure; actual agreement; specified training contributions; the enforceable operation and duration of control; the target good; actual support and competent independent exercise; the available alternative at the named time; effects on other participation; and which contribution has been completed. None is inferred from a label such as “coercion,” “free choice,” “public technique” or “authentic association.”

**Normative grounds still needing defense:** why the realized capacities and relationships matter; what forms of commitment agreement can constitute; how contribution and reciprocal dependence can ground authority; limits on control of later activity; what burdens upon other participants count; when completing or changing a relation discharges a claim; and who may choose the transformation. No-breach is not a normative blank slate, but it removes the specific promise-based warrant used in the prior round.

The justified review verdict is therefore narrower than a categorical prohibition: **neither disclosed agreement nor operational portability alone determines authority over the later good**. The comparison can identify a false necessity claim, an unavailable alternative, an unacknowledged provider contribution or an unwarranted expansion in the object of control. Whether the best-supported remaining explanation grounds the ethical limit universally is the central unresolved inference. Defining a “fair investment return” or “valid commitment” to contain that limit would restate it.

## 8. Why the existing mathematics is enough for now

The [checked trajectory companion](../../../../experiments/valuation-development/lean/perspectival/README.md) can test whether a supplied readout retains distinctions relevant to a supplied comparison across specified actions. It cannot identify what constitutes this common performance, determine a fair return, or certify the availability and permission of an outside route. A representation would need to retain the formation history, remaining provision, action authority, named target and actual alternatives before a comparison-preservation question is well posed.

When coordination partly constitutes the good, “remove the condition” may describe a different activity with different realizations. It cannot be represented as toggling a constraint while holding every outcome and normative ground fixed. Conversely, the fact that a condition was included in one offered package does not make deleting it a logically impossible action; a different feasible design can be compared on its actual consequences. Identifying those action relations is correspondence work before any theorem of preservation applies.

No new Lean theorem is proposed here. Encoding an ethically adequate contract, a completed return or a permissible release as an input and proving a selected verdict follows would not advance the missing warrant. The present work is the substantive comparison and its admissible inferences. No external primary source or empirical finding is introduced; source claims are limited to the cited repository genealogy.
