## 1. A finishable question and its boundary

The local question can be finished: exhibit a declared process in which actually formed machinery acquires consequential predictions, constructs or replaces the way those predictions control action, and subsequently pays to protect another independently identified center that supplies no compensating private return. Account for information, material construction, inherited memory, resources and failed histories. Give adverse regimes and competent conventional comparisons.

A positive construction under explicit law conditions is a completed conditional result. It need not first derive the existence of every possible material law. Conversely, calling a new construction conditional does not excuse an unmentioned source, restored account, beneficial decoder or caring action rule. The assumptions determine what the result explains.

If final actor reserve is converted into a copying comparison, energy is only one required input. A literal offspring count also needs a declared finite stock of blank carriers or precursor, or an explicitly supplied continuing material source. With K available carriers and fuel price c, min(K,⌊F/c⌋) is a possible paid copying law; ⌊F/c⌋ alone does not create carriers from fuel. Repeated reconstruction in the same two inherited slots is renewal, not an unbounded population of simultaneous circuit copies.

Three separate achievements remain visible:

1. **Causal genesis:** a previously absent functional pathway forms, changes through actual consequences, and makes another's condition affect action.
2. **Calibration and understanding:** the pathway's representations and action predictions track independently specified conditions under the tested interventions. Accurate prediction is one component of understanding; a complete general theory of understanding is not established by a five-state register.
3. **Justified standing:** there are reasons to consider the affected being, including when this pathway never forms. This requires the ethical premises and evidence about the patient, not merely the pathway's historical success.

Formation of functional concern does not entail felt compassion. Accurate material-support prediction is not automatically experienced valence. Failure of the causal mechanism does not remove the patient's ethical standing.

## 2. Non-entailment beyond an identity gate

**Proposition G1 — Predictive and reconstructive competence do not entail other-directed protection.** Consider a class of causal models whose requirements constrain formation, available observations, predictive accuracy, memory maintenance and revision, but permit at least two action couplings with the following properties at an accessible decision state:

- the same accurate predictor describes both available actions;
- the actions have the same consequences for the actor's accounted support and privately valued outcomes;
- they have different consequences for a separately retained patient;
- either coupling is allowed, affordable and compatible with the preceding formation and learning history.

Then those requirements do not entail choosing the patient-protective action.

**Proof.** Keep the predecessor, predictor, retained records, physical action laws and costs fixed. Install the first admitted coupling to select the protective action and the second to select the harmful action. Both models meet the stated competence requirements and retain accurate action predictions. Their patient outcomes differ. Thus the premises admit a countermodel to the protective conclusion. If the class also permits a parameter controlling the coupling, a genuine revision mechanism can update that parameter toward either regime under appropriately declared retention laws; the presence of revision alone removes neither countermodel. ∎

The proposition does not assume the two complete world histories are identical: their patient outcomes are precisely the difference. Nor does it say every specific physical law admits both couplings. A class restricting the allowed coupling can entail protection, but that restriction is an additional explanatory premise.

The conclusion is stronger than a failure to generalize between identities. The learner may already know whose condition is at issue and predict it correctly. It can still invert the action route, disable an actuator, choose a cheaper mask, or learn to forecast the harms it deliberately produces. The predecessor's inverted-action comparison is one concrete warning: good prediction diagnostics can coexist with poor productive action. Its differing observation coverage means that comparison is not a common-task accuracy-superiority theorem.

This result leaves constructive work available. It requires identifying the particular laws that select and preserve a useful coupling; it does not show such laws cannot exist.

## 3. A criterion covering every affordable suppression route

The earlier identity-gate comparison is necessary but can be too narrow. An actor can retain a caring representation while making it ineffective. The completed joint-formation study already records one such defeat: a cheap commitment to open production or a budget lock can avoid later protective expenditure while preserving the useful private training receipt.

The following comparison covers an explicitly declared repertoire of entire feasible policies, including adaptive strategies and commitments. It is not an assertion that an analyst has enumerated every physically conceivable bypass.

Fix an initial decision history and the initial selector's evaluation of private receipts and expenditures. Use either a finite horizon or a discounted infinite horizon with integrable costs. All comparisons retain that **same** initial evaluation; utility offsets are not changed when a later preference changes.

Let C be a candidate development policy that installs a shared pathway and later protects a patient. Let S be any affordable alternative policy. Under a declared common coupling of external histories, record:

- ΔR(S): the difference in expected, appropriately discounted gross privately valued receipts and avoided private losses, S minus C;
- L(S): the expected protective expense of C that S avoids;
- D(S): every additional expense of S relative to C, including altered construction, commitment, gating, measurement, disabling, maintenance and loss of reusable material. Any negative cost difference belongs in this same account.

These quantities are accounting differences, not rewards assigned to patient welfare. They must partition the private difference without double counting. Define the resulting private evaluations by V₀(C) and V₀(S).

**Proposition G2 — Feasible suppression comparison.** Under that account,

\[
V_0(S)-V_0(C)=\Delta R(S)+L(S)-D(S).
\]

Consequently, C is not privately optimal whenever an affordable S satisfies

\[
D(S)<\Delta R(S)+L(S).
\]

For a finite declared repertoire consisting of C and alternatives S, C is uniquely privately optimal exactly when the strict reverse inequality holds for every alternative. Ties do not uniquely select C.

**Proof.** Expand the two expected private accounts and cancel common receipts and expenditures. The remaining terms are exactly the defined receipt difference, avoided protective cost and additional alternative cost. Maximizing the common initial criterion gives the comparison. ∎

This is an elementary theorem about a supplied initial decision criterion. It is not a universal model of natural selection, nor a claim that larger expected resources always imply greater material retention. Stochastic reconstruction must be compared using its actual transition kernel: a larger expectation alone does not establish the probability of surviving a liquidity threshold.

For the particularly transparent full-suppression case, all alternatives preserve the same gross private task returns, avoid the same eventual protective bill L, and incur suppression expenses D(S). With a finite nonempty menu put D*=min D(S). If useful acquisition yields additional private receipt G at acquisition cost A, and declining it has incremental value zero, the shared route is uniquely selected in this menu exactly when

\[
G-A-L>0,\qquad D_*>L,
\]

provided the relevant acquisition and payment prefixes are feasible. The full-suppression alternatives are uniquely worse than the shared route because their net gain is G−A−D(S). With an infinite menu, the exact condition is D(S)>L for every available S; inf D(S)>L gives a stronger uniform margin but is not necessary for pointwise strict preference.

The qualification about the menu matters. A partial suppressor may avoid only some protection, obtain different private receipts, or exploit a later favorable state. It belongs in G2 even when it does not fit the simplified D* formula. Declining learning, a cheaper architecture, deliberate ignorance, distorted correspondence, memory erasure, an ineffective gate and a prior commitment must each enter if the law permits them. Their feasibility includes resources at the actual date of construction; a cheap nominal bill may still be unaffordable before a receipt.

**Omission before construction is a distinct alternative.** If the acquired private predictor already exists and a patient-only interface is optional, declining to build that interface can preserve all private capability while saving both construction and protective expenditure. An expensive lock on an already-built interface does not make this earlier omission expensive. Past private training receipts are sunk at this choice. A favorable selection result must include omission, identify a future private capability actually lost by omission, or restrict its claim to retention of an already-formed shared interface. A paid successful patient continuation can remain a valid realization even when optional omission outperforms it on private resource retention.

### What G2 does and does not explain

The initial reason for acquiring the pathway can be private even when its later protective action has no private return. The actual training task pays G; protecting the patient does not. Shared physical implementation can make suppressing its later effect more expensive than tolerating the expense. That is a coherent conditional causal route to costly other-directed action without rewarding that patient-protective action during training.

The favorable expense comparison is a substantive local law condition. It must not be introduced merely because the desired conclusion needs it. A real application would identify the available implementations, their prices and the least expensive ways to prevent concern or its practical effect. If a free identity filter or actuator lock preserves G, the positive region disappears for the initial cash selector.

G2 does not imply the shared pathway never forms outside its optimality region. Undirected formation, inherited structures, mistakes, drift or other selectors can realize it. It proves what the stated optimizing selector can uniquely choose and what defeats that particular explanation.

## 4. Conditional realization without an analyst's benevolent reward

A constructive law class can satisfy the following conditions without awarding a reward for another's welfare:

1. A self-relevant activity produces actual observations and support. Its physical benefit funds formation, evidence acquisition and retained records. A source label meaning “good for others” is absent from this return law.
2. A paid circuit learns an operational dependency and changes a response coupling through actual return, wear or reconstruction. Its positive and inverted versions are physical alternatives when the repertoire admits both.
3. A separately specified sensor and correspondence mechanism connect another center's condition to a representation. The relation is validated by evidence about that center or a declared transport law; resemblance alone is insufficient.
4. A physically shared evaluation/action pathway makes the acquired significance accessible when the other center is represented. Its recipe, sign and possible suppressors are explicit. Shared architecture is not silently inferred from predictive accuracy.
5. A protective action has a distinct recipient effect and draws on the actor's inherited resources. The patient supplies no return, sanction, threat, reputational payment or later reciprocal benefit within the claimed horizon. Known identity rules out self-exposure ambiguity in the claim being tested.
6. Formation and persistence are supported by a declared causal selection or choice mechanism. If that mechanism is the initial private selector, G2 applies to its whole feasible repertoire. If it is material renewal, the claimed differential retention is established through the actual renewal law.

These conditions specify a sufficient architecture class; they do not prove all the conditions are selected in every environment. A finite implementation meeting them can close its own conditional question. It cannot claim the origin of its unmodeled primitive significance or transport law.

### Two legitimate claims that must not be blended

**Operational transfer.** A prediction of loss to a distinct organization causally changes action through a shared circuit, and the actor pays to prevent that loss. This realizes functional concern for the declared organizational dimension. It leaves experience and moral-patient status open.

**Learned valence transfer.** The existing concern model additionally supplies a self teaching signal β>0, learns w from that signal, learns correspondence g, and uses v=gw in action evaluation. Paid hardware can implement those recurrences, but the operational capture bit of the private predictor is not thereby β. β's interpretation and the sign of evaluative use remain explicit premises.

For a simple two-action choice with recipient harm H under omission, cost c under successful protection, and a zero-private-return patient, the declared shared evaluator prefers protection exactly when gwH>c. Learned w and g can cross that threshold from zero under the existing theorem. That proves an acquired functional contribution within a shared evaluator, not the derivation of shared evaluation from accuracy alone.

If the predictor is reused to estimate protection efficacy rather than valence, keep the roles typed: q predicts a consequence, w carries learned self-significance, g carries a supported correspondence, and the action coupling determines how they affect conduct. A finite circuit can store those roles in the same substrate only under an explicit capacity and update recipe. Identical letters or compatible numerical ranges do not establish an implementation.

## 5. Compatible composition through an actual acquired predictor

Let the predecessor process terminate or hand off at a stopping time τ. Its interface Xτ must retain the actual material state, resources, remaining source opportunities, timing, installed modules and their condition, observations, retained registers, and any evidence validity conditions the continuation will use. Let μ be the unconditional distribution of that interface, including failed formation histories.

A downstream concern mechanism is a partially defined continuation kernel K_x. Its legal domain A contains exactly the interface states from which its required initial recipe is available and all required opening bills are payable. Outside A, the model must record a declared fallback or failure; it cannot initialize a fresh concern experiment. Any preceding predictor construction already spent remains spent.

The following contract is sufficient for a conditional joined implementation:

- **Actual predecessor:** the first downstream state is the actual successor of the preceding material history, possibly after a separately charged adapter.
- **Resource and time preservation:** all construction, sensing, writing, retention, correspondence, suppression and action events respect payment prefixes and source timing. A future avoided harm is not cash. A future receipt cannot pay an earlier bill.
- **Information preservation:** the continuation receives only installed observations and inherited records. Hidden environmental condition, recipient identity or efficacy cannot arrive solely through an analyst's validation variable.
- **Semantic transport:** every reused prediction has an explicit target map. If q predicts own capture, a condition relates that event to recipient protection before q is used as a protection predictor. Changing recipient coupling without changing q is an adverse control, not an opportunity to provide the right label externally.
- **Action preservation:** the projected protective command is implemented by actual recipient-affecting events, with its full latency and expense. Several material events implementing one abstract update must retain their intermediate failure states.
- **Center preservation:** actor resource return, recipient condition, observer distress and scoring variables are distinct. Repair and concealment are not identified merely because they create the same reassuring observation.
- **Decision preservation:** the implemented acquisition, revision and action laws match the abstract law under the available information. Normative claims use an additional declared interpretation rather than being smuggled into transition marks.

**Proposition G3 — Composition and unconditional success.** Suppose those interfaces are realized and, for every x∈A, the actual continuation has probability p(x) of the specified concern witness. If no outside-A fallback qualifies as that witness, the complete process has success probability

\[
\Pr(\mathrm{witness})=\int_A p(x)\,\mu(dx).
\]

In particular, if p(x)≥p* throughout A, its unconditional success probability is at least p*μ(A). No independence of preparation and continuation is required.

**Proof.** Condition on the actual stopping-time interface. The continuation law is K_x by the stipulated material, information, action and timing contract. The law of total probability gives the integral, and the lower bound follows pointwise. ∎

This is a probability-accounting and implementation contract, not evidence that a particular pair of existing models already satisfies it. It prevents multiplying convenient success fractions from separately funded models, conditioning away formation failures, or transporting a downstream result to predecessor states lacking its premises. For deterministic relational traces, the corresponding argument is concatenation through the shared actual state; probabilities require their own kernel compatibility.

The [controlled-refinement result](../../../../theories/becoming/history/CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md) already establishes why retained world possibilities and costs are insufficient without observations and inherited controller memory. The additional target and center clauses here concern the proposed transition from private operational prediction to other-directed action.

## 6. A discriminating witness and the controls it requires

A joined witness should establish, in one history:

1. The relevant representation and response coupling begin absent or uninformed, then form and acquire their operative state through paid events.
2. A changed dependency causes a genuine correction or revision, retained in the same record or explicitly reconstructed with loss counted.
3. A known distinct recipient is represented through the declared evidence route. Protection is actually costly to the actor and supplies no compensating private return within scope.
4. The recipient's state changes which action is selected through the acquired pathway. Altering that pathway while holding the information and bills appropriately matched removes or reverses the effect.
5. The selected paid action actually improves the independently specified recipient outcome; an internal favorable score alone does not qualify.

Where claimed, the following interventions distinguish the explanation:

| Alternative explanation | Relevant matched comparison |
|---|---|
| Uncertainty that the actor itself is endangered | Supply valid recipient identity and remove residual private exposure without changing resources or efficacy |
| Distress relief or signal suppression | Distinguish actual recipient repair from equally reassuring masking; charge both interventions |
| Generic acquired predictor, caring route installed by fiat | Construct competing action/evaluation routes; trace what forms, renews or replaces them |
| Identity filter is the only considered bypass | Include affordable commitments, actuator disabling, memory erasure and budget locks from the declared repertoire |
| Copying more because it consumes protective reserves | Record actor reserve, recipient outcomes, module copies and subsequent feasibility separately |
| Shared chemistry assumed across targets | Change the recipient's transport relation while leaving the private predictor fixed; identify the resulting misgeneralization |
| Success obtained by selecting survivors | Report all initial histories and the integral in G3 |
| Prediction counted as understanding | Hold predictive competence fixed while changing its evaluative or action use |

Not every application must implement an unlimited list of interventions. The claimed mechanism determines which alternatives it must distinguish. A finite declared repertoire can be exhaustively checked; extension beyond it remains a separate scope claim.

## 7. Why the ethical bridge stays substantive

The [ethical foundation](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) supplies standing, relevant consistency, answerability and feasible implementation as explicit premises. They can be defended and criticized on their own grounds. No theorem here derives them from preservation of an organization.

An accurate regenerative predator meets many cognitive and material requirements while failing those ethical constraints. Conversely, a being can have justified claims when no guardian pathway recognizes it. The shared evaluator's feature similarity is a causal route by which concern can become effective; it is not a condition that makes the patient deserve consideration. Identity symmetry alone also permits universal indifference.

The proposed connected account therefore has two interfaces, neither dispensable. Causal development explains how reasons can become represented, revisable and effective. Ethical argument examines which represented considerations actually are reasons and whether their weighting, exclusion and use of power withstand scrutiny. Keeping those interfaces explicit is compatible with studying their historical interaction; it does not reduce the project to an externally imposed fixed moral score.
