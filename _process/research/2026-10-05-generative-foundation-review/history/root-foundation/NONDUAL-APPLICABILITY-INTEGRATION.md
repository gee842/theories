## What this round establishes

The named connection is **activity transforms the conditions of its own applicability, while other or renewed dependencies continue**. The existing material formation law already supplies a positive instance. Repair changes the damaged site into an intact site; that change removes the condition for the same repair and restores capture access. Capture fills local storage; actual use can empty it and reopen capture while the site stays intact. No additional completion label, service identifier, reward or controller was installed to obtain these relations.

This changes how the preceding recipient result bears on the foundation. Its invariant help assessment remains a correct limit of that implementation. A new exact translation result establishes that the recipient-identification process records cumulative patient credit without letting its amount affect any subsequent event's admissibility. It omits the recipient's own use of support and continuing organization. A local model omitting that relationship cannot by itself show that significance requires an evaluator outside generative reality.

The core and tree therefore constrain each other's articulation. The branch shows which consequential relationships an abstraction must preserve for this question. The core does not stand as a completed independent object to which “meaning” must subsequently be attached. The original formal core already lacked primitive Actor, Patient and Owner fields; this round does not claim to remove such an axiom or establish nonduality for the first time.

## What remains supplied and what could defeat the reading

The material reaction repertoire, initial resources, one-site representation and source routes remain local modeling inputs. Captured local fuel pays for local copying and never replenishes common repair fuel. Existing finite-stock termination remains in force. The result is bounded ongoing activity, not indefinite self-funding repair or a derivation of the chemistry.

Disabled activity is not sufficient evidence of a restored dependency: a damaged site can lack repair fuel or an active resident. Restoration is identified through the actual site change and its capture relation, independently of whether the response stops. Conversely, a restored site without source has no fresh capture opportunity. An alternative resident can inherit a repaired site, so this relation does not guarantee retention of its contributor or establish entitlement. A wider organization can preserve itself while harming other centers. These are substantive constraints on the account, not detached relabelings of an otherwise identical phenomenon.

An admitted repeated repair without intervening damage would defeat the stated trace theorem under this law. A failed claimed prefix or payment would defeat the actual continuation. A patient-credit-dependent guard would defeat the representation audit. Redundant pathways or useful further maintenance would narrow applications of the one-site account. Ordinary coupled feedback or chemical descriptions may preserve all the same predictions; this result does not establish a uniquely superior algorithm or ontology.

The [primary comparisons](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-COMPARATORS.md) connect Ren's Chinese and English genealogy to Dewey, Di Paolo and a human thermoregulation study. They support a focused comparison of response-sensitive changes within continuing activity. The observed bodily case is not a new experiment here, a derivation of regulatory capacities, or evidence that functional preservation, experienced relief and ethical warrant are identical. The comparison preserves ordinary feedback as a strong rival description.
