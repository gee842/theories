# Collective formation, dependent families and infinite-domain warrant

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored philosophical assessment following the [finite-domain and named-theory result](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-COVERAGE-INTEGRATION.md) and [whole-family reassessment](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-FAMILY-WARRANT-REASSESSMENT.md). This round distinguishes a legitimate extension of finite collective admission from a proposed justification of infinite domains. The latter remains open. Universal properties identify mathematical roles; their existence commitments and comparison ranges must also be justified.

## 1. The affirmative collective principle

A common formation account can explain a whole mathematical domain without supplying a separate finite description for every member. The finite selection construction already demonstrates this: one target-independent procedure generates a family covering every predicate on a supplied finite carrier. Its completeness is proved after specifying the procedure. This avoids taking each target predicate as the input that selects its own alleged explanation.

A natural stronger candidate is **collective inductive formation**: an independently specified constructor system warrants the domain of its finite formations, with their formation identity and an interpretation rule determined by the constructors. The favorable rationale is that the domain organizes a common practice of composable formation. Its content need not be a further list of unrelated objects or target truth answers. For natural numbers, zero and successor offer an especially clear candidate: their collective role concerns arbitrary finite iteration, without adding an infinite numeral.

This proposal is distinct from admitting every completed binary history or every predicate over an infinite carrier. It does not demand that every mathematical object have a finite individual description; it specifies one proposed source of domain admission. A universal foundation might need additional principles for other subjects.

The principle nevertheless remains a substantive commitment. Availability of any particular finite formation, indefinite permission to continue forming, and admission of the resulting whole domain are distinguishable. The first two do not become the third simply through notation. The proposed account must explain why its formation practice warrants collective quantification and unrestricted interpretation of the domain. That explanation need not require a physically completed infinite process, but it cannot be replaced by a finite run of one.

## 2. What a universal property contributes

Initiality provides a precise way to assess the proposed inductive domain: its constructor structure should determine the relevant interpretation into every admitted target of the declared kind. The existing [generative presentations](MATHEMATICS-GENERATIVE-PRESENTATIONS.md#a-universal-structure-for-iteration) and [closure resolution](MATHEMATICS-CLOSURE-RESOLUTION.md#proposition-i3--no-junk-and-no-unforced-identity-are-equivalent-to-initiality) already study this role, with their original validation scope. Repeating those hand arguments as a new proof would not comply with the current Lean requirement.

The philosophical order matters. One may first defend a formation principle and then use a universal property to test whether its realization has the intended structural role. Alternatively, one may postulate that the universal role has a realization. The second is an explicit collective existence principle, not automatically a deduction from the definition of that role. It can be informative without being premise-free.

For natural-number formation, a constructor account has an advantage over an arbitrary mathematical truth table: it specifies the intended structure through a common operation and its permitted iteration. But ordinary induction already appears among the supplied resources of the [root's premise ledger](../../../../theories/becoming/history/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md). This round has not derived its collective entitlement from an independently defended weaker root.

For a full powerset, changing the presentation to a universal property does not eliminate the range question. The specification must say which predicates, relations or maps are classified and from which domains. A comparison restricted to already admitted relations characterizes a relative role. Reading it as admission of all predicates of the fixed full target requires a warrant for that unrestricted comparison range. The earlier checked full-admission characterizations expose this commitment independently of the chosen terminology.

An explicit collective principle therefore narrows ambiguity and prevents per-member bookkeeping from masquerading as explanation. It does not erase the principle's content. The question becomes why this particular collective principle follows from the root, rather than whether its consequences can be described compactly.

## 3. A real extension: finite dependent domains

The [new family-admission module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/families/formal/FamilyAdmission.lean) extends the finite result to a supplied family `B(a)` whose permitted values vary with the base position `a`. With a finite base and finite fibers, `sigma_finite` and `sections_finite` supply finite covers for the dependent sum and the domain of sections. `finite_sigma_full_admission` and `finite_sections_full_admission` then apply the established finite-formation principle to predicates on those domains.

This is collective progress. The result handles the whole supplied finite family, rather than taking an arbitrary target predicate as the construction input. Empty, singleton and binary-union admission suffice at the stated finite carriers. For sections, the finite-cover proof uses an injection into an ordinary finite function carrier and classical representative selection; it is not a new executable dependent enumerator independent of the ambient function space. The complete fiber assignment and its finite-cover hypotheses remain inputs. This does not derive every family assignment from the root.

The companion [dependent-completion module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/families/formal/DependentCompletion.lean) represents sections by total functional ideals of finite subsets of the dependent sum. Its inverse maps, evaluation correspondence and predicate-membership results retain the varying fiber at each base position. The base is fixed while the given fiber correspondences change presentation. This preserves dependent relations within the same organization; it does not split an independent index world from independently existing value worlds.

The distinction between local compatibility and coverage remains concrete. Functionality is characterized through finite graph members; totality requires a value at every base position. An empty fiber obstructs a total section. The natural-number family with fiber `Fin (n+1)` has a checked zero section, whereas the family with fiber `Fin n` has an obstruction at zero. These are exact calibrations under supplied arithmetic, not an explanation of infinite arithmetic admission.

The module also shows that each supplied section over a finite base has a finite graph even when the fibers themselves are infinite. This concerns that section's graph, not finite coverage of the entire section space. There is no dependent term/formula syntax, full dependent truth theorem or internal-universe reconstruction claimed here.

## 4. Why pointwise finiteness does not solve infinite admission

Making each fiber small does not control the information in the whole assignment. `truthFibers P a` places a lifted witness of an arbitrary predicate `P a` in its fiber. Each fiber is subsingleton and finite, while `support_truthFibers` recovers exactly `P` from which fibers are inhabited.

The checked targets `full_iff_subsingleton_support` and `full_iff_finite_support` make the consequence exact: admitting supports of every family with the stated pointwise property is equivalent to full predicate admission. The family quantifier ranges over arbitrary supplied assignments. The construction is mathematically legitimate; using it as an independent explanation of `P` would import `P` through the family parameter.

This is not a rejection of dependent families. It locates the commitment that pointwise bounds leave untouched. A family can be independently specified by a common rule, as the zero-section example is. But a theorem accepting every ambient family does not independently justify that whole range. Likewise, classical existence of one section from pointwise nonemptiness differs from warranting the full space of all sections. The module states the former separately rather than confusing it with the latter.

The finite collective achievement and this boundary belong together. Finite base plus finite fibers permits the warranted finite construction. Arbitrary whole assignments over an infinite base retain a different admission obligation, even when every fiber is tiny. The boundary tests a proposed argument; it does not refute a richer generative account that independently warrants those assignments.

## 5. One actionable next bridge

The most precise next investigation is a **meaning-based justification of collective inductive formation**. State the constructors, identity conditions and permissible interpretations before the desired arithmetic conclusions. Explain why they warrant one domain and its elimination or induction rule, instead of merely assuming that domain and proving its universal property. Then identify which commitments this actually removes from the root's ledger.

The success criterion is substantive: the account must justify the collective formation rule without importing the target domain, a full predicate range over it, or an unrestricted family principle of the same disputed force. Its mathematical consequences require Lean checking, but formalizing the formation rule as an axiom would only expose the premise. A matched structural account may retain the same constructors and induction while disputing ultimate priority; that further comparison remains necessary.

This is an actionable premise question, not a newly established answer. The current audit found no independently discharged infinite-admission principle. If its proposed defense reduces to “all finite formations are already collected,” another initiality theorem would not advance it. Conversely, a successful justification could establish a genuine infinite-domain bridge without pretending that it also supplies the full powerset. That separation makes the next question more precise than restarting binary-path completion.
