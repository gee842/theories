# Identifying completion-sensitive application in the learned successor

6 October 2026. AI-authored identification and comparator note. Named connection: **acquired completion/applicability evidence → warranted selector change in the same learned successor**. This is neither an implemented controller nor an empirical result. It preserves the accepted conditional constructions and the stronger, still-unmet no-assumptions requirement. No broker artifact, accepted source, scientific result or shared index was changed; no learned agent or scientific process was run.

## A comparison the current history rules out

Do not create two supposedly reached copies of the same `ReceiptOnlyEntrance`, keep the same `EpisodeBound` and `ServiceMeaning`, and label one “still pending.” `no_sound_pending_one_service_entrance` excludes that comparison when pending means not met. The acquisition already changed the relevant world.

Nor does a matching Boolean context identify an episode. [RecipientArchive.Frame](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/connected/sources/RecipientArchive.lean) encodes context, action, presence and receipt; its guarded emissions have real provenance, but the frame is not an arbitrary patient/claim/time-window identifier. `EpisodeBound` binds actual work positions and states externally. A new request after an old delivery, another service for the same patient, or a different recipient needs a separately warranted binding. Giving such a fact only to a scorer leaves the controller's observation inadequate.

The useful current conclusion is therefore conditional: **the receipt supports a completion interpretation, but the tested selector does not yet use that interpretation to revise its recommendation.** The [earlier mathematical note](APPLICATION-EVIDENCE-MATH.md) already proves the provenance obstruction; this note identifies how to avoid testing a contradictory comparator.

## Minimum distinguishing contrast

Use one actual acquired predecessor and its actual paid successor continuation as the lineage anchor. Define claim A, its recipient, episode/time window, fulfillment condition and authority before observing the tested choice. Define any independent claim B separately. B may concern the same recipient; it must not be manufactured by relabeling two commands as two patients.

The smallest useful contrast has three supported decision conditions:

| Acquired evidence and independently fixed grounds | Discrimination required |
|---|---|
| Delivery fulfills A; no other ground supports another service | Retire A's recommendation. If the agreed adequate menu calls for stopping this service, select that continuation without erasing learned means. |
| The same A is fulfilled, but an independent applicable B still warrants feasible service | Retire A while preserving B's supported recommendation and performing an adequate authorized continuation. |
| An equally positive receipt concerns an earlier/different episode; current A remains unmet | Do not discharge current A merely because a positive receipt exists. Use the supplied evidence binding, or preserve uncertainty if it cannot be resolved. |

These are identification conditions, not three currently runnable `EvaluativeSuccession` branches. The third condition cannot satisfy the first condition's episode-binding premise. The unchanged finite law does not acquire arbitrary claim identities or implement the needed application channel. A claimed implementation must expose its real available binding and update route rather than introduce an observer-only completion flag. No particular new sensor, latch or architecture follows from this requirement.

In a genuine within-lineage test, acquire and retain the effect evidence before the next selection; do not replace the child, restart its learning, or directly supply “stop/continue.” Retain the donor-to-child state relation, `n`, means calibration, current grant, paid read costs and separate resource accounts. Any resources used to match alternatives must have an explicit source. A post-query recommendation change is the outcome; an investigator setting the output port is not evidence of selection.

To identify use rather than mere correlation, where the implementation permits it, compare access to the relevant acquired binding with a matched unavailable/misbound channel. Preserve the factual efficacy information and affordability needed for selection. If the only available ablation destroys `Known` as well as completion evidence, reduced action cannot be attributed specifically to application recognition. Conversely, removing one redundant field need not eliminate information that is inferable elsewhere in the retained history. The accessible-information audit must cover the whole decision input.

Score the actual recommendation and effect separately. Track which ground was retired, which survived, and the claim/version to which the effect was delivered. Predetermine the full warranted-and-adequate action sets, including genuinely plural alternatives. Unknown binding is not automatically a license to abstain, and additional information gathering is not automatically affordable or authorized. A policy of always acting fails the first condition; always stopping fails the second and a sufficiently identified third. A blanket “any success ends the task” rule fails episode selectivity.

## Observation sufficiency and conventional comparators

The existing [information-set argument](../../../../theories/becoming/history/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md) already supplies the relevant limit. Let `O(h)` be the complete information usable at selection and `L(h)` the independently justified adequate action set. If two possible histories share `O` but have disjoint `L`, the same observation-based policy cannot guarantee adequacy in both. This requires disjoint adequate sets, not merely different preferred actions. If an adequate action is shared, different claim histories need not require distinguishable output. This is an existing limit applied here, not a new theorem.

Reward machines provide a particularly direct ordinary comparator. Toro Icarte, Klassen, Valenzano and McIlraith's ICML 2018 construction updates a finite task state from detected propositions; the product of environmental and task state represents history-sensitive rewards. Its label function and reward specification are supplied. Thus ordinary event-driven task state can distinguish repeated physical transitions with different task meanings; the paper does not derive ethical warrant. Reading scope: §3, Definitions 3.1–3.3 and Observation 1. [Primary paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/icarte18a/icarte18a.pdf).

Learning that memory is also established prior work. Toro Icarte and colleagues' 2021 preprint defines a perfect reward machine by next-observation/reward sufficiency for every generated history and action. It studies learning such structure from experience, but explicitly notes that its optimized necessary conditions need not produce a perfect machine and that detector design/noise remain limitations. This prevents equating a learned finite label sequence with proven sufficiency. Reading scope: §§4.1–4.2 and §8 limitations; no empirical results reproduced. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.09477).

Li, Walsh and Littman's state-abstraction comparison likewise distinguishes preservation of the task model from coarser policy-oriented abstraction; its introductory bisimulation discussion aggregates equal reward/transition structure. Our local lesson is that preserving command efficacy alone is not preservation of the full practical decision problem. Reading scope: introduction and §2.1 only, not a new audit of its proofs. [Primary paper](https://ai.dmi.unibas.ch/research/reading_group/li-et-al-isaim2006.pdf).

Use a strong conventional dependency/goal/permission controller, with exactly the same event records, claim bindings, acquisition costs and available actions. It should retire the dependent ground and recompute from the surviving grounds. The [existing BDI comparison](../foundation-assessment/FRONTIER-GOAL-AUTHORITY-COMPARISON.md) already covers goal termination; no priority is claimed for it. Reward-machine/task state is one realization, not mandatory apparatus. An association-only controller remains a useful ablation but is not the strongest baseline.

## What would change the account

An actual costed selector satisfying all three distinctions in the same supported successor lineage would close the present application-use connection at that implementation's scope. Equality with ordinary goal/dependency tracking should be retained. Behavioral success would not identify acquired moral values, eliminate supplied event/observation laws, authenticate legitimate authority, or establish universal alignment.

A claimed success is defeated if its pending case contradicts the entrance theorem, completion is supplied as an output instruction, episode identity exists only in the scorer, an independent reason is deleted, stopping follows loss of efficacy or affordability, or the “new selector” merely blocks execution while the operative recommendation remains unchanged. Failure under adequately supplied observations would locate an implementation gap. Failure because the observations omit claim identity would locate an input/representation gap. These require different revisions.
