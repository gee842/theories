## What the actual source already distinguishes

The native [retention `channels`](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/model.py) takes a declared law and current material state. Damage changes response formation; response and fuel enable repair; an intact site enables capture; captured fuel can enable copying. Its downstream counters do not enter these event propensities. The [formed-variant implementation](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/process.py) likewise constructs channels before its `readouts`, event-count accumulation and `A_minus_H` calculation. The latter are assessment variables, not a runtime reward selecting reactions.

Thus an analyst's choice to report copies does not cause copying in these programs. An analyst nevertheless supplied the reaction specificity, accessible material, template inheritance and preparation. Demonstrating a process without a scalar reward input discharges one possible confound; it does not derive the origin of all those relations. Conversely, supplying a local law does not place that law outside generative reality or reduce its consequences to mere labels.

The [regulation result](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ENDOGENOUS-REGULATION-MATH.md) already connects absent response, formation, restored capture and reconstruction. The [variant result](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-MATH.md) adds formation of alternatives and consequence-dependent differential reconstruction. These are existing conditional derivations. They must not be replaced by a claim that organization produces no operative direction until an explicit goal variable appears.

The current n-copy/r-repair task asks a different question: which continuation meets an independently specified finite undertaking, and whether acquired port effects guide it. Its closure does not make that undertaking the source of the earlier native feedback. Neither does the native feedback establish that every supplied undertaking is generated by the organization.

## Four distinguishable identification claims

| Claim | Evidence that bears on it | Inference to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| A relation enables an activity. | Source guards and actual transitions identify what becomes unavailable when the relation fails, including alternative routes. | Any loss of fuel, count or component must impair every continuation. |
| The relation is operative in regulation. | Its change alters native formation, allocation or response; the resulting activity changes that enabling relation. | An observer's feasibility calculation is already an enacted policy. |
| Consequences alter subsequent regulation. | A retained change produced through encounters affects later response under suitably matched current conditions; interrupting its use changes that response. | A current-state reaction or analyst parameter change already demonstrates learning. |
| The continuation warrants support. | The account identifies affected activity and lives, what improves or impairs them, and the grounds and scope of response. | More persistence or copying establishes whole-process ethical direction. |

These are distinctions within an unfolding history, not independently existing observer, world and value domains. They can share a material mechanism. A requirement may be operative as a dependency-sensitive disposition without being represented as a sentence, a target count or a separate assessor. The third claim is required only when acquired revision is claimed; it is not an extra condition imposed on every instance of regulation.

## Concrete evidence that would change the next conclusion

Start from the existing source law and an identified activity, not a newly named benevolent score. Trace the actual channel through which its conditions change response availability and through which the response changes those conditions again. Preserve the precursor, resident, support location and competing events that make this connection possible. The [independent same-law review](ORGANIZATIONAL-REQUIREMENT-REVIEW.md) gives the immediate discriminator: vacancy enables copying, but the resident template determines what replaces the loss. Occupation restoration therefore does not identify recovery of the lost type or prior composition.

For a stronger **consequence-dependent revision of scope**, the evidence must identify the retained relation that changes which dependency receives a response. Compare its actual use with a matched interruption or another available route; do not merely set a new `required` flag from the desired conclusion. Retain the costs and material occupation of that relation where the source actually has them. No universal positive measurement cost or special memory architecture is presupposed.

The existing adverse regimes provide concrete adjudication rather than new gates:

- Repair can spend support without enabling further capture when the source is absent. A new claim of source-sensitive regulation must show how the relevant consequence reaches and changes the operative process. Existing fixed repair need not be falsely credited with this capacity, or dismissed as no regulation at all.
- Closing a copying vacancy can remove copying's present occasion; repair can remove its damaged-site occasion. More response is not always better regulation. Acquired suppression, if claimed, must be separated from ordinary substrate depletion or a disabled event guard.
- The same total support can leave incomparable repair and capture opportunities. A new claimed continuation criterion must identify the activity whose conditions matter instead of making aggregate energy, abundance or option count its unexplained universal objective.
- The variant study's mean fraction can increase without a copying advantage. Claims about selection must retain event-specific production/loss and unconditional histories, rather than select successful founders or infer the mechanism from a favorable summary.

A source audit should therefore report the operation-producing law separately from its assessment functions, the actual mediation path, its interruption, and the continuing consequences on unsuccessful as well as successful histories. Laboratory identification would additionally require independently identified reaction or physiological relations; these authored rates are not such measurements. An ordinary stateful feedback account that preserves the same causal organization remains a legitimate explanation. Merely redescribing it in foundational vocabulary would add no empirical discrimination.

## Focused primary antecedent

**Source capsule.** Di Paolo's *Autopoiesis, adaptivity, teleology, agency* (2005) distinguishes the continuation of self-production from regulation of threatening tendencies. Adaptivity concerns how an organism's ongoing processes discriminate and respond to its viability conditions; self-production alone does not supply that explanation. He leaves the historical emergence of this capability unresolved. This is a conceptual account, not an empirical demonstration of a mechanism forming its own regulatory criterion. [Author's primary paper](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf), pp. 436–440; reread as searchable PDF text.

**Our use of the comparison.** This already credited antecedent sharpens the derivation target: observer-visible dependence and actually operative regulation must be connected, rather than inferred from each other. It does not require a detached self-monitor, indefinite survival, or adoption of every part of the author's account. The [earlier comparator](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-COMPARATORS.md) remains the source for its wider discussion. No new source corpus is imported.

The legitimate next addition is a demonstrated change in **which actual enabling relation governs continuation**, arising through the identified organization or its acquired consequences. Existing native recruitment and cessation already establish a bounded part of that connection. Explaining broader formation and revision remains substantive; another fixed success label would not close it. Ethical scope continues to follow the [whole-process account](WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-INTEGRATION.md), including differentiated burdens and possibilities that local self-maintenance can fail to respect.
