# Others' wellbeing and the conditions of my continuing world

6 October 2026. AI-authored argument, review and integration following [Ren's correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-shared-world-grounding.md), under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **others' wellbeing → conditions of present and future participation → practical concern through already warranted stakes**. The [canonical development](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#others-wellbeing-and-the-world-in-which-we-continue) makes this a central route rather than treating the earlier direct-relief bridge as compulsory.

## Positive inference

Participants and their purposes form within activity whose consequences become conditions of further activity. A person's world includes relationships, practices and other lives through which their capacities develop. Dependence can be instrumental: another's wellbeing supports reliable cooperation or available care. It can also be constitutive: the quality of a worthwhile friendship partly concerns the other person's actual condition. “My world” does not make those lives my possessions or merge their experiences.

The argument is conditional. There are already warranted reasons concerning a continuing life, relationship or shared world. Others' wellbeing actually sustains, constitutes or materially affects the valued conditions. An available response credibly supports those conditions, with legitimacy, costs and competing consequences assessed. The existing stakes can then provide a practical reason for that response. No separate prior commitment to universal altruism is needed for this inference.

The inference retains a normative principle connecting warranted stakes with their realization or effective support. It does not derive reasons from causation without any practical premise. Also, connection alone does not identify the direction of an effect. Forming a purpose explains its existence; its origin does not make it worthwhile. The account requires actual beneficial dependencies and warranted stakes rather than expanding “self-interest” until every desired conclusion is true by definition.

Actual uncertainty about future participation can justify maintaining broadly available conditions when one cannot identify every future contributor or need. That extension requires evidence about access, exposure and the conditions' importance. It assumes neither equal probabilities of occupying all positions nor guaranteed repayment. A future participant can have changed capacities and purposes; the relevant world is not simply a source of returns to a fixed consumer.

## Review and preserved counterregimes

Two collaborating agents independently reconstructed and reviewed this route. Both favored its positive scope: existing reasons concerning a shared world can support care before an independently altruistic purpose. Their contributions are integrated here; this is internal conceptual review, not new empirical confirmation.

The strongest counterregimes preserve actual connection. An actor can suffer real shared damage while capturing larger gains from exploitation. Another's competence can support an arrangement while their freedom threatens its controller. Distinct groups can have reasons for incompatible uses of common conditions. These cases respectively test decisive priority, the dimensions of wellbeing, and justified conflict resolution. They do not show that every contributory protective reason disappears.

Insulation provides a different limit: some harms may have no relevant effect on this actor's warranted stakes. The older [isolated-harm construction](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/FORMATION.md#standing-and-influence-have-different-grounds) explicitly removes feedback from the patient's burden. It defeats an inference from mere productive renewal, not Ren's proposed positive dependence where that dependence is present. Conversely, we must not assert an undetectable eventual personal loss to immunize universal concern against any insulated case.

The [shared-selection analysis](../../../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#1-shared-dependence-and-the-selection-of-its-maintenance) distinguishes actual common support from relative retention. A common additive benefit can cancel in comparative selection while remaining indispensable to participants. It therefore does not refute their reason to maintain the common condition. Practical justification, motivation and selection remain distinguishable relations within the same history.

## Result and next connection

This is a genuine change in the grounding route. It can establish substantial practical concern through shared conditions and existing stakes without first settling whether another's good supplies a direct reason independently of all such stakes. Relief remains one real change in the world, rather than the sole gateway to significance. Direct moral standing, broad common-world reasons and particular obligations may coexist; the standing of an affected life is not established or denied by its usefulness to one observer.

Next identify the actual dependencies, horizons, future exposure and conflicts; investigate whether acquired understanding changes responsive activity while preserving the conditions of later participation. Universal concern and inevitable favorable propagation remain stronger claims. No new theorem, empirical result or religious interpretation is added, and the accepted conditional foundation retains its scope.
