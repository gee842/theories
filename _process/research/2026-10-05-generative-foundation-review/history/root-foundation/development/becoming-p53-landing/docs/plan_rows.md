Done: `Becoming.Experiment` ({N_MODULE} theorems, L3, importing Joint and Recurrence, changing no existing module), with no premise. Relative to determinacy, the record fixes a move's outcome exactly where the run has taken it (`Run.fixes_of_takes`); an untaken move is not fixed even with everything registered (`retarget`, `Run.not_fixes_of_untaken`). What one's act would have done otherwise is fixed only where the configuration returns (`Run.fixes_on_return`, `Run.not_fixes_other_of_new`). A run keeping its history is new at every step and fixes nothing about its acts but what it did (`keepHistory_new`, `keepHistory_fixes_nothing_else`). Through a projection the step factors through, one occasion stands for its class (`Run.generalizes`), and within finitely many projected configurations the projection returns (`Run.projection_returns`). A wrong projection misattributes, is never refuted by moves in step with the hidden cause, and is refuted by moves varied against it (`Confound`). Reading: replication is return, a control is the model that the step factors through the reading, and variation independent of what is left out is the structural form of randomization (the AI's, pending Ren).
