- **The exact remainder.** What one response carries from given levels (`responded`) and what j responses carry in turn (`responses`); giving is additive in the levels read (`carriedGive_add`), so the carried amount is the orders below j plus what j responses in turn carry from it (`carried_eq_orderBelow_add`), and a cut at order j loses exactly what j + 1 responses carry (`carried_eq_carriedWithin_add`). The loss falls as the order rises (`responses_antitone`), is at most the bound of `Becoming.Loops` (`responses_le_missBound`), and is exactly that for the self-loop (`Loops.SelfLoop.responses_eq`).
- **Where a cut loses.** One response carries something exactly where, at an earlier step, a positive level is coupled in (`CouplesAt`, `responded_pos_iff`); the orders below j miss something exactly where a gap is conveyed through j responses or more, each reading a level the one before had reached (`ConveyedThrough`, `orderBelow_lt_iff`, `carriedWithin_eq_iff`).
- **From every start.** Order j is exact from every start exactly where no chain of j + 1 couplings in time order ends there (`CoupledIntoBy`, `exact_every_start_iff`), and a finite order is exact at every step from every start exactly where those chains are bounded (`finite_order_iff_bounded`, `finite_order_uniform_iff`). No premise.
- **Couplings that come and go.** A fed loop of recurring couplings (`CouplingRecurs`) upstream leaves every order inexact (`carriedWithin_lt_of_recurring_loop`, `unbounded_of_recurring_loop`); where no coupling reads a level from step N on, order N is exact (`exact_of_reading_before`). Each coupling reads a listed condition, so a chain one longer than the list passes a loop with no listing of every condition (`loop_of_coupledInto_succ`, `no_chain_of_no_loop`).
- **Where the schedule settles.** The strengths from step N on (`strengthsFrom`) keep the couplings that read levels from N on, and the last j of a chain in time order of N + j couplings are such couplings (`CoupledIntoBy.tail`). Where every coupling from step N on recurs (`SettledFrom`), a finite order is exact from every start exactly where no loop of the couplings from step N on lies upstream (`bounded_iff_no_loop_from`, `finite_order_iff_no_loop_from`), with no listing of every condition; whatever the schedule, without such a loop the carried amount is at most a polynomial (`carried_le_polynomial_from`). `Loops.Once` reads the first step only, has no chain of two couplings in time order and has settled from the second step (`Loops.Once.reads_first`, `Loops.Once.no_chain_of_two`, `Loops.Once.settled`).
