**Capture through the rule** ([Becoming.RuleCapture](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/RuleCapture.lean); record [capture-through-the-rule](../results/records/capture-through-the-rule.md)). Let the fast step of a two-level course be a selection that does not lower a target's share under a rule that favours it (`Raises`), and let the rule read at a reading step be a monotone function of the share alone (`ReadsShare`). Where no rule turns against the reading, the share falls at no step from every start, and the course is locked from the step after its first reading step (`favouring_locks`). On counts, each carrier leaving `a + m` offspring and each other `a`, the margin `m` read from the share falls at no step from then on, and the share rises strictly wherever the margin is positive and carriers are present (`Counts.margin_course`, `Counts.rises_strictly_along`). Where rules can favour the others, the lock is a condition on the start: from a configuration whose rule favours and is not above what the reading of its share gives, share and rule fall at no step, reading steps included (`locked_stays`, `locked_rises`). Without it, a rule in force against the reading, read one step late, makes share and rule fall without end (`Alternating.alternates`). A perturbation of the rule that keeps its direction is absorbed at the next reading step (`perturbation_absorbed`); one that reverses it and is held until the share crosses the reading's threshold locks the course the other way (`Reversed.not_absorbed`). Where the reading favours at every share, every course is locked from the step after its second reading step, exactly (`uniform_locks`, `Second.second_exact`), so what leaves is a change of how the rule reads or of the selection. Two courses of a two-level law whose steps are monotone keep their order (`order_kept`): a perturbation lowering the rule delays the course and does not turn it (`perturbation_keeps_below`). What holds the outcome is how the rule reads, not the rule's value, which every reading step sets again from the share. This is the mirror of `Law.perturbation_reabsorbed`: there the state is re-absorbed because the step does not read it, and here the rule's value is re-set because the reading does not read it. That an actual rule reads the share it selects, and reads it monotonically, is a premise; it is where "the winner shapes the rule" of `Becoming.Capture` enters.

*Readings* [AI reading, D{D}]. 韩非子's 二柄, 「今君人者释其刑德而使臣用之，则君反制于臣矣」, is read as a rule of selection read from the share of those it selects: locked, the loop does not turn back, and only a change of how the rule reads leaves; 韩非's framing of the loop as control between opposed interests is kept as a divergence. 管子's 据有余而制不足 is the proportional reading, under which the kind holding more is favoured and holds more. 商君书's 壹赏、壹刑、壹教 is one reading favouring one way at every share, which locks every course from its second reading step. "The winner shapes the rule" is the premise `ReadsShare`, not a conclusion. None of this says whether a locked loop supports or damages 生生.
