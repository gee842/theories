C13 (“consuming an embodied relatum at a need of one ends it”) stood in the old claim under “Under Dependent:”. `Web.Embodied.ends_when_consumed` does not assume `Dependent`: it takes embodiment, a need of at most one and the level staying below that need after the step. Law 10 (the local law) states the hypotheses of `Flow.BalancesOn.arises_given`: balance across the step on a family holding the relatum, embodiment at the two ends of the step, and only active formations giving to it.
