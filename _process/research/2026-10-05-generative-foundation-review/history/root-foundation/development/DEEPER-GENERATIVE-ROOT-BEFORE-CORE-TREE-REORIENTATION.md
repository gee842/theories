**Question addressed:** what lies beneath “activity changes the conditions of further activity”? Can a substrate of distinction or one operation supply that dynamics, and what does an axiom-free proof actually establish?

**Result and scope:** this conditional mathematical and interpretive layer is complete: a space of distinctions does not determine its dynamics; given a declared relation of admissible events, finite continuation is generated by composition through actual intermediate conditions; its endpoint reachability is the least reflexive-transitive extension of those events. A history-language realization supplies exact finite-word feasibility without requiring a finite-memory state summary. The new package checks **32 declarations in four modules**, with empty reported axiom dependencies, and recompiles the 18 audited core declarations it uses. These results support a coherent causal candidate and delimit the earlier monist interpretation. They do not establish a uniquely necessary ontology or derive the actual laws of reality from logic alone. The whole unified foundation remains ongoing.

The elementary structural results are not claimed as novel mathematics. The contribution is their checked use to resolve a foundational ambiguity, preserve the valid older constructions, reject unsupported inferences, and connect the resulting premise ledger to the continuing mechanism derivation.

## 1. The positive candidate

**Subsequent development, 6 October 2026:** Ren narrows the universal target to consequences for every reality satisfying specified properties of its laws. The [conditional-universality extension](CONDITIONAL-UNIVERSALITY.md) proves such consequences for support accounting and dependency lifetimes. It develops the dynamics that this substrate analysis leaves supplied. A uniquely necessary ontology remains a separate philosophical question, rather than a prerequisite for completing that universal explanatory programme.

The proposed ontological stance is:

> **Beings, activities and understandings are differentiated formations within the reality they inhabit. Their relations enable and constrain transformations, whose effects can become conditions of further transformation. An explanation includes the formation and maintenance of its observers, representations and evaluations wherever those affect the process.**

> **存在者、活动与理解，都是其所处现实中的分化与组织。它们的关系使变化成为可能，也约束变化；变化的结果，又能成为后续变化的条件。观察、建模与评价本身，也须纳入这一生成过程。**

These are current AI-assisted formulations, not classical quotations or verbatim Ren. The proposal concerns a common explanatory root with differentiated realizations. It does not make all centers interchangeable, assume one cosmic subject, or assert direct interaction between every pair of things.

Ren's [earlier words](../../../sources/12-claude-ai-chats/2026-05-05_古文一元论与语言的逻辑收敛_2954cf42.md) distinguish this ambition from counting substances: “我说的一元当然不是基数，是所有概念回归同一root”; the same message says “因而一生万物即是真理，乃多元”. His wider favorable-direction claims in that conversation remain hypotheses under the current governing position. The [genealogy audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DEEPER-ROOT-GENEALOGY.md) separates those words from later AI elaboration.

“Process-relational monism” is a useful interpretation of this research stance. It is an explanatory commitment to investigate, not a consequence of putting many variables into one mathematical state. One state type can equally encode several disconnected processes. Shared notation alone does not establish a shared causal origin, connectivity, conscious standpoint or ethical conclusion.

The demand of immanent explanation is concrete: an observer's distinction must have an observation mechanism; a retained rule must have a carrier; a learned evaluation must have a formation and revision mechanism; a local external supply must remain an explicit input, or be included in a larger account. This does not require every study to derive fundamental physics. It requires the account to identify where its explanation stops.

## 2. The earlier substrate and what it supplies

The preserved [operation/distinction-monism proposal](../../../sources/07-shengsheng-buxi/ssbx-repo/docs-next/10_formal_形式/wen-substrate/03-operation-monism.md) studies a product operation and its iteration:

\[
\Sigma(X)=X\times X,\qquad T_0(X)=X,\qquad T_{n+1}(X)=T_n(X)\times T_n(X).
\]

It also uses carriers of the form \(\operatorname{Fin}(N)\to\delta\), with particular realizations of the distinction parameter \(\delta\). These are useful constructions of structured possibilities. Once the seed and construction rule are fixed, the subsequent carriers are determined.

The stronger inference that the seed, differentiation, temporal change or physical dynamics have thereby been derived is unsupported. The new [product-tower proof](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/ProductTower.lean) establishes, at every finite level:

\[
\operatorname{Nonempty}(T_n(X))\iff\operatorname{Nonempty}(X),
\qquad
\operatorname{Subsingleton}(T_n(X))\iff\operatorname{Subsingleton}(X).
\]

Here *subsingleton* means at most one inhabitant. The proof uses a diagonal embedding of the seed and a leaf projection back to it, together with induction through products. An empty seed stays empty; a singleton seed stays singleton. Products can organize distinctions already available in a nontrivial seed, but this operation alone does not create nontrivial alternatives from an empty or singleton seed.

This also clarifies an ambiguity in “a type exists.” A construction of the type \(T_n(X)\) is not a construction of an inhabitant of that type. The historical source's `Nonempty (Type u)` statement must not be read as `Nonempty (T_n(X))`. A type-level iteration index is construction depth; physical time or occurrence needs a further interpretation and law.

The earlier chapter's reported Lean status and its current inspectable source must be distinguished from the new independent build. Three actual source modules have now been [preserved with hashes and checkout provenance](../../../sources/26-deeper-root-code-audit-2026-10-06/README.md); their historical project build was not rerun. The genealogy records this snapshot and its limits. The older [universal-formal claim](../../../sources/07-shengsheng-buxi/ssbx-repo/docs-next/10_formal_形式/wen-substrate/07-claim.md) also explicitly permits some reality to lie outside formal articulation. Its scoped algebraic results, universal-formal interpretation and any ontology of all reality are separate claims.

## 3. Distinction underdetermines dynamics

Keep exactly the same two-state carrier, equality, product construction and one event label. Three admissibility laws are possible:

| Law | Admitted one-event successor | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| No-event law | None | No nonempty event history |
| Holding law | The same state | Recurrence without state change |
| Alternating law | The other state | State change and a two-event return |

The [checked countermodels](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/DynamicUnderdetermination.lean) place these laws on identical carriers. A space's distinct values and product structure therefore do not uniquely determine whether events occur, whether conditions change, or whether a particular return path exists. An alternating process further shows why recurrent change alone does not establish learning or productive novelty.

This is a relative underdetermination result: the stated substrate data admit incompatible expansions. It rules out obtaining a unique dynamics from those data alone. A stronger substrate might include additional dynamical constraints, but then those constraints must enter the premise ledger and be justified. Calling the package one operator does not remove its informational commitments.

## 4. A small causal presentation with derived continuation

For the existing discrete, labelled interface, take:

\[
\mathcal P=(S,A,E),\qquad E\subseteq S\times A\times S.
\]

\(S\) records conditions, \(A\) labels events, and \(E(s,a,t)\) states the admitted successor relation. The types and relation are model data. They need not be finite. Events need not be intentional actions, available commands, deterministic changes, or controllable choices.

The current [Lean core](GENERATIVE-CORE-LEAN.md) represents this as `Process State Action` and defines `Trace P s word t` by a zero-event history and extension by one supplied event. A finite history carries its actual intermediate conditions.

Define endpoint reachability by existence of an event word:

\[
s\leadsto_E t\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists w:\operatorname{Trace}_E(s,w,t).
\]

The new [generated-path result](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/GeneratedPaths.lean) proves that this is the **least reflexive-transitive relation containing every supplied event**. Every other reflexive-transitive relation that contains the events contains these reachable pairs. The least such extension is unique pointwise. More generally, an interpretation that carries supplied events into a reflexive-transitive target relation carries every generated finite history into it.

This is the positive completion result at this layer: finite sequential reachability needs no extra independent composition axiom once the supplied events and ordinary inductive construction are fixed. It is a relative leastness theorem, not a proof that this presentation is the uniquely minimal ontology. The endpoint relation forgets action labels and path multiplicity; the underlying `Trace` still retains the event word, and no free-category uniqueness claim is made here.

The zero-event identity is formal bookkeeping, not a claim that a cost-free physical no-op occurs. Sequential composition uses a common actual intermediate state. Parallel composition, resource competition, timing, correlations and cross-system effects still require the actual joint law. A physical model that forgets consequential history cannot justify concatenation merely by calling its reduced record a state.

## 5. Keeping history prevents an unexplained memorylessness assumption

Suppose a supplied language \(H\) specifies which finite event words are allowed, contains the empty word, and is prefix-closed. Construct a process whose states are the complete declared event-label histories. A step appends one event label when the extended history belongs to \(H\).

The [history-realization proof](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/HistoryRealization.lean) establishes that the feasible words from the empty history are exactly the supplied words of \(H\), with the reached history equal to that word. Thus a history-dependent finite admissibility specification has a faithful relational realization at this scope. This representation may merge different original hidden branches that have the same event-label history. It preserves word feasibility, not original branch identity, statewise probabilities or powers of intervention. The construction does not discover \(H\), compress it, produce an implementable learner, or provide durations or infinite-path guarantees.

Compression is a later operation with its own obligation. The existing core proves that exact projected one-step possibilities from **every** concrete starting state are necessary and sufficient for exact finite histories and projected endpoints. An omitted coordinate is dispensable only relative to the questions and consequences actually preserved. This connects the deeper presentation directly to the checked five-phase example and to the physical mechanism's actual-successor bridge.

## 6. Which premises remain, and which notions are derived?

| Item | Status at this layer |
|---|---|
| Logical identity, types, functions, products and induction | Resources of the chosen mathematical foundation; not a derivation of logic from physical reality |
| A nonempty or nontrivial physical domain | A commitment about an intended application; not forced by the generic interface |
| Admitted primitive transitions or allowed finite histories | Supplied dynamical content; not determined by bare distinctions or product structure |
| Finite sequential continuation and least endpoint closure | Derived from the supplied transitions and inductive construction |
| Consequential distinction | Definable relative to a declared continuation or intervention question; a permission difference implies a state difference |
| Observation, control and probability | Additional model structure where claimed; existential paths alone do not supply them |
| Physical occurrence, causal identification, cost and timing | Require material laws, preparations and evidence at the application boundary |
| Feedback, renewal and formation of regulation | Conditional properties/mechanisms; not properties of every instance |
| Learning, revisable selection, concern and wisdom | Further explanatory targets with existing partial constructions and explicit missing connections |
| Common-root ontology and universal adequacy | Governing hypothesis tested by connected explanation, not proved by one representation theorem |

This refines the three provisional premises in [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope). Within the declared finite-history framework, sequential composition is derivable. Physical composition and the possibility of particular recurrent regimes remain substantive questions. The revision does not erase the broader causal content of the earlier premise by mistaking mathematical concatenation for physically unconstrained combination.

There is no certified absolute count of primitives. An unlabelled graph can encode labelled events using extra vertices; a relation can be encoded as a predicate; histories can be encoded into states. Such translations change a presentation. A minimality claim must specify what the translations preserve and which background constructions they are allowed to use.

## 7. What “no axioms” establishes

A proof without custom `axiom` declarations can still quantify over a supplied process and assume hypotheses about it. An empty `#print axioms` list reports no declared axiom dependencies for that proof term within Lean's underlying type theory. It does not remove the inference rules, definitions, parameters or theorem premises, and it does not turn a formal interpretation into an empirical fact.

The [original core audit](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/verification.json) separates proofs with empty axiom dependencies from proofs using standard Lean axioms. The new [deeper-layer checker and record](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/README.md) report the actual dependency footprint of this extension. The old corpus's stronger slogans are not transferred to the new results or vice versa.

The target adopted for this conditional development was **no hidden premises, no unexplained capacity smuggled into the mechanism being derived, and an explicit justification or open obligation for every remaining commitment**. Ren's subsequent no-assumptions directive is stronger: the listed checks remain necessary but do not satisfy it. Premise-free derivation of the actual universe is not established by this development.

## 8. Explanatory content and tests

The common-root proposal earns content through compatible mechanisms, not through the ability to rename every event generation. The following tests connect this layer to research:

1. **Distinguish presentation from law.** Hold the represented carrier fixed and vary an allowed transition rule. A claim that the carrier alone predicts dynamics must exclude the incompatible expansions by independently justified conditions.
2. **Challenge a summary.** If two allegedly equivalent preparations have different admitted future responses, the claimed equivalence fails for that question. If a summary satisfies the exact local criterion but a finite-history prediction disagrees, at least one law, mapping, state-sufficiency or scope claim was wrong; the theorem identifies the consistency obligation.
3. **Carry mechanisms across boundaries.** The source of a sensor, rule, repair material or retained evaluation must be charged to a declared input or to a mechanism in the larger process. Moving an accounting boundary cannot make a dependency disappear.
4. **Test the link rather than the label.** Disrupt a proposed observation, response or retention coupling while retaining appropriate resource-matched comparators. Repeated success caused entirely by an installed controller or passive persistence does not establish the claimed formation of learning.

These are model-identification and intervention requirements, not new empirical results. The [genealogy and identification audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DEEPER-ROOT-GENEALOGY.md) records competing explanations and the findings that would revise the account. Bare monism has no distinctive intervention prediction by itself; the proposed mechanisms must supply one.

## 9. Integration and remaining connection

The deeper layer has a positive role: it identifies the shared causal object, derives finite continuation from its declared events, explains when histories can be compressed, and forbids treating a static distinction calculus as a derivation of dynamics. Its countermodels preserve the failed stronger route so that it cannot silently return under a new name.

The [independent internal review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DEEPER-ROOT-REVIEW.md) accepts these claims at their stated scope, separately from compilation. It checks the distinction between endpoint closure and path data, formal identity and a physical no-op, complete event-label history and hidden physical history, and product construction and actual dynamics. This is AI-assisted internal review, not independent human peer review.

This work does not reset the accepted research sequence. The [regulation/retention gate](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REGULATION-RETENTION-INTEGRATION.md) reached a conditional milestone under its declared chemistry. The unresolved deeper scientific obligation is to identify and justify common constraints across materially different mechanisms. A successful account may retain some domain laws as empirical inputs while deriving shared consequences. Unique metaphysical necessity, universal empirical adequacy and ethical justification require further arguments; none is obtained by reducing the number of symbols in the presentation.

## 10. Nondual core and derived positions

**Core formulation:** activity forms and changes relations that condition further activity. Undergoing, observing, acting and representing a self/world distinction are differentiated organizations within this activity. A complete explanation must account for their formation, maintenance and actual effects. This AI-authored formulation responds to [Ren's core/extension distinction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-nondual-core-and-subject-extensions.md); it is not a classical quotation or a completed metaphysical theorem.

The original `Process State Action` already imposes no subject/object split. `Action` labels an event and need not name an intentional act. Nonduality here makes that absence an explicit explanatory requirement: local actor, patient, observer and owner labels must have an account inside the history, rather than silently furnishing its fundamental partition. Existing practical indices did not assert independent substances. The new work develops their place in the architecture instead of claiming to have disproved a dualism formerly built into the core.

### Roles follow relations within the history

For an actual admitted history \(h\), a role description \(\rho(h)\) may identify which relations jointly enable an effect, which ongoing organization is affected, or which process can obtain evidence. A consistent annotation \((h,\rho(h))\) can be forgotten without changing the underlying history. The [formal extension](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-CORE-MATH.md) establishes this conservative interface and a relative exactness condition for role summaries. It does not turn an analyst's supplied readout into a physically learned self-model.

The constructive instance starts from homogeneous sites without connections, then forms complementary connections that enable effects. Two effective ranges can overlap, and removing an indispensable connection removes the corresponding capability. Thus the range is responsive to actual history rather than supplied as an eternal actor boundary. Its nodes, event laws and activation rules remain local-model inputs. Complementary causation alone does not constitute an organism, a consciousness, a moral patient or a self-maintaining organization.

The formal `ActualHistory` carrier retains a start, event word and endpoint with a `Trace` proof. It is not a readable log of every intermediate state and does not distinguish all nondeterministic branches sharing those data. Exactness is relative to the stated continuation and endpoint questions. A historical distinction absent from these data would require a richer carrier.

In a further actual continuation, a previously affected site becomes a necessary source of another effect after a new connection forms. This concretely separates a site's identity from a fixed subject/object role. The model's pulses can turn a site on or off; neither direction is declared ethically favorable.

The explanatory sequence distinguishes **causal participation, maintained organization, evidence access, control, acquired self-representation and experiential continuity**. These may overlap without coinciding. An instrument can extend control without extending experience; a coordinated response can span several organizations without forming one common sufferer. The [acquired-self bridge](GENERATED-SELF-AND-ETHICS.md#acquired-action-effects-and-organizational-use) concerns a later internal representation/use interface, under its existing supplied laws and partition. The new range construction does not retroactively derive that physical partition or fuse the two mechanisms into one developmental history.

### Concern concerns the continuation before it is assigned to an owner

The corresponding ethical question is: **how does actual adversity occur within generative activity, which feasible continuations genuinely change it, and how can situated capacities form to recognize and realize warranted change?** Patient and responder are then positions within this problem: a continuity of undergoing and an effective locus of transformation. Neither must be a primitive separate substance, and one process can occupy both positions.

The positive candidate locates the significance of relief in the actual transformation of undergoing, before a preselected owner's adoption or benefit. That changes the starting point of the explanation. It does not yet demonstrate that causal change is felt, that every persistence is good, or that actual relief yields every proposed moral conclusion. The [earlier C2 audit](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md#9-实际尝试从根基消除-c2) remains a local test for a developed ethical interpretation; C2 is not imposed on the core signature. Moving it into a predicate on continuations would not itself discharge its content.

Actual distinctions remain essential. Silencing a report can leave the burden untouched; relief here can coexist with injury elsewhere; consent, responsibility and cumulative loss cannot be removed merely by changing the boundary vocabulary. No scalar total value or privileged administrator of a Whole follows from nonduality. Indifferent, destructive and caring organizations must remain describable in the same generative framework.

### What this advances

The [reconstruction and premise ledger](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-CORE-RECONSTRUCTION.md) separate three levels: a role-free core architecture; a conditional construction of changing and overlapping effective ranges; and the stronger derivations of organization, selfhood, experience and warranted concern. The first is explicit in the present model, and the second has a checked realization. The third remains the continuing research task. Neither the formal interface nor the [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-CORE-SOURCES.md) establishes the necessity of the ontology.

The next named connection is **formed effective ranges → internally acquired and revisable distinctions of participation and consequence**, connected to the existing learned action-effect results. Experience and the ethical significance of the resulting continuations remain explicit concurrent obligations. [Integration and independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-CORE-INTEGRATION.md) record the finite verification and the conditions that would revise this account.
