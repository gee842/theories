### Significance, learning and concern

The candidate foundation describes activity changing the conditions of further activity. Its ethical development asks how another being's condition becomes a reason within a process, how that reason can learn and widen, and how effective concern survives costs, obstruction, and succession. Supporting conditions, recognition, valuation, justified standing, operational influence, and actual protection have distinct roles. Their origins and connections are central derivation tasks under the governing position above.

The completed studies supply conditional mechanisms and counterexamples. They do not establish a universal tendency toward wiser or more inclusive regeneration. The current [theory overview and genealogy](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/README.md) keeps that distinction explicit.
