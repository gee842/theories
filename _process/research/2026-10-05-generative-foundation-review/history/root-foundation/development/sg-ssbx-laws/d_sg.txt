### Junctures, commuting laws and the third value

Where: chapter §1, "Where a configuration is lost" and "What a commuting law keeps apart", §3, "A family of readings within what it reads" (the liar with a third value), and the glossary; the records [every-loss-passes-a-juncture](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/every-loss-passes-a-juncture.md), [what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart.md) and [revision-with-an-undecided-value](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/revision-with-an-undecided-value.md); study section {S} ([SG](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#{SANCHOR})). Decided in the one account of D45.

**Ren's words.** On 19 October 2026, asking that the earlier 生生不息 repository be read for what can be integrated: "please take a look of this … many insights can be integrated" (abridged, as quoted with the [review](SSBX-REPOSITORY-REVIEW.md) in commit `c599d83db`; the full message is not in `sources/`). Provenance; each reading stands on its law.

**Decision.** Adopted with exact scope. (1) **机 is a juncture.** The earlier text's 机, two continuations leading to different open futures, is read with the open future as one configuration still to be had: a juncture for `s` has a step after which `s` can still be had and one after which it cannot (`Branching.Juncture`). A profile of several possibilities at once is not formalized. (2) **护闭 and 成闭 at a juncture.** 护闭 is keeping, the largest closure that keeps `s` to be had again (`Branching.keeping`, `Branching.keeping_greatest`); 成闭 is losing, the steps after which `s` is never had again (`Branching.losing_not_returns`). They are one operation at a juncture, removing the losing steps or the keeping ones. (3) **What finding the place asks.** Every loss of a returning configuration passes a juncture, up to double negation, and finding it is deciding whether `s` can still be had; asked of every process, that is the limited principle of omniscience (`Branching.lpo_of_junctures_found`). The place of a loss is there on every course; where it is is known where reach is decided, as for configurations a list holds with decided steps. (4) **Many options make no juncture** (`Branching.no_juncture_of_keeps`, `Branching.Many.no_juncture`): two options are needed and are not enough. (5) **What a symmetry carries.** A law that keeps a symmetry moving every configuration never merges what the symmetry tells apart (`Law.Commutes.apart`); to merge it a law must break the symmetry. In the Changes, 消息 never merges 陰 and 陽 changed line for line, and the waning into 坤 breaks 錯 (`Hexagram.waxWane_apart`, `Hexagram.wane_breaks`). The earlier topology's "«一» structurally does not exist" and its table equating Borsuk–Ulam with the second law are not taken; the law is. (6) **The third value 疑.** The undecided value is what revision has not decided, not a third truth and not superposition (the review's "不确定 ≠ 不可知"). The earlier "三值保守律" (U ⇏ ⊤) is `Undecided.eval_undecided` for terms without constants, and along the run `Undecided.stays_undecided`. The diagonal's gap reappears as undecidedness (`Undecided.undecided_of_moves`, `Undecided.rest_undecided`). (7) **Not read into it.** No valuation: which configuration is worth keeping, whether keeping or erasing a difference supports or damages 生生, and which sentences ought to be decided are the comparison's to say (§6.15).
**Reasons.** Each reading names the declarations it rests on and says no more than they prove. The double negation and the LPO calibration are read as what finding a juncture asks of the unfolding, not as a gap in the proof.
**What would change it.** A formal profile of openness at a juncture that the reading through one configuration misses; a process in which every loss has its juncture found without deciding reach (which would need LPO there); a law keeping a symmetry that moves every configuration and merging a pair (impossible by `Law.Commutes.apart`).
