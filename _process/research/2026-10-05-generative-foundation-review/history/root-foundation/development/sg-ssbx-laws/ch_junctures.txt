**Where a configuration is lost.** On a branching course a configuration can be lost on one path and kept on another. Where is it lost? This is stated with no premise ([Becoming.Junctures](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Junctures.lean)). A configuration `x` is a juncture for `s` where it has a step after which `s` can still be had and a step after which it cannot (`Juncture`); a juncture can have `s` again and is possibly drawn in (`Juncture.reach`, `Juncture.possiblyDrawn`). Where `s` returns, every course from `s` to a configuration that can no longer have `s` passes a juncture at which it takes its losing step, up to double negation (`loss_passes_juncture`). The double negation says what the unfolding asks: finding the juncture is deciding, along the course, whether `s` can still be had. Where that is decided the juncture is found, and it is decided wherever a list holds the configurations and the steps are decided, by a reading of reach that only rises and settles by the list's length (`loss_passes_juncture_decided`, `reach_decided_listed`, `loss_passes_juncture_listed`). It is not found in general: finding the juncture of every loss of every process is the limited principle of omniscience (`lpo_of_junctures_found`). Keeping only the steps after which `s` can still be had (`keeping`) keeps `s`, returns to it exactly where the process does, and holds every sub-process that keeps `s` on that sub-process's course from `s` (`keeping_keeps`, `keeping_returns_iff`, `keeping_greatest`). What it removes on its course are the losing steps at junctures (`keeping_removes_at_juncture`), and the losing steps never return to `s` (`losing_not_returns`). A process that keeps `s` has no juncture on its course, however many options it has (`no_juncture_of_keeps`, `Many.no_juncture`).

*Readings* [AI reading, D{D}]. 机 is a juncture: one step keeps `s` to be had again and another loses it. 护闭 is keeping, the largest closure that keeps `s` to be had again; 成闭 is losing, after which `s` is never had again. They are one operation at a juncture, and neither carries a value: which configuration is kept, and whether keeping it supports or damages 生生, is the comparison's (§6.15). Many options do not make a juncture. Record: [every-loss-passes-a-juncture](../results/records/every-loss-passes-a-juncture.md).
