It is preserved for its limited causal content. The [governing account](../GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md) concerns good and its continuation beyond private return.

## Source and exact boundary

The [preserved working paper and access record](../../../../../sources/37-ethical-minimum-primary-2026-10-06/shared-world/README.md) supply these rules: five participants can invest at most ten tokens each per round; a token restores one infrastructure percentage point, capped at 100. Ordinary decline is 25 points; a shock treatment includes an 80-point decline. The supplementary p.6 table, visually inspected, gives zero delivery through 45 and positive delivery from 46. Its nearby prose says only “below 45”; our exact boundary follows the table.

The paper reports similar average performance alongside unequal outcomes and interprets earlier inequality as weakening later repair. This does not identify a particular trajectory below. The methodological limits are in the source record.

## Our permitted-state arithmetic

Let A–C be the three upstream positions, each with a ten-token contribution bound. Their maximum 30 can cover ordinary decline of 25. Conditional on the indicated level immediately before the shock:

| Starting level | Decline | Contribution | Result | Delivery capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 90 | 80 | A–C maximum 30 | 40 | Zero |
| 90 | 80 | A–C maximum plus 10 from D or E | 50 | Positive |
| 100 | 80 | A–C maximum 30 | 50 | Positive |

These are consequences of the inspected rules. They are not observed individual histories, a replay of the fixed disturbance sequence, or claims that an entire experiment reaches the indicated state. They avoid any issue about negative stocks or a zero clamp. Independent arithmetic review accepted the comparison; an earlier threshold reading of 45 was corrected after direct visual inspection and is not used.

The inference is that capacity sufficient for routine maintenance need not suffice for immediate restoration in every admitted state. The control also matters: a larger preceding buffer can remove this particular dependence under the same rules. Fresh later budgets mean failure this round cannot establish permanent inability to recover. No probability, willingness to contribute or obligation is inferred.

## Who bears a maintenance contribution?

A separate, conceptual comparison holds aggregate investment at 25 and keeps the subsequent water allocation fixed. One feasible contribution vector is (10,10,5,0,0); another is (5,5,5,5,5). The infrastructure input is identical, but D and E each relinquish five more tokens in the second allocation. Keeping their water benefit fixed prevents us from treating their contribution as evidence that their own situation improved. This is our arithmetic comparison, not an observed treatment or a claim that water extraction itself depletes infrastructure.

## Scope after the correction

Such comparisons can identify whose capacities enter a continuation and who bears its costs. They cannot establish that people matter only while useful to a subgroup, that preserving infrastructure suffices for goodness, or that an ethical reason must be a disguised future return. Even if a buffer removes the group's need for someone's contribution, that person's actual good and harm remain part of the broader generative inquiry.

The investigation is therefore retained as supporting causal material. It neither becomes the foundation's governing next question nor supplies a new Lean law, an ethical axiom, empirical replication or proof of universal necessity.
