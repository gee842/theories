- **Laws on the levels alone (`Becoming.Novelty`).** With no flow, balance, loss or finiteness of the world, each law needing one state of the part written into it: a family whose stock stays within a bound returns (`Web.revisits_of_bounded`, `Web.returns_of_bounded`); a family new at each of the steps `T, …, T + N`, `N` the number of configurations within the bound, exceeds the bound at one of them, found by checking those finitely many steps (`Web.exceeds_of_new`), so what is new without end gains without bound (`Web.unbounded_of_new`, realized by `Web.counting`); no reading of a bounded configuration gives a record that never repeats or a count without bound, so a bounded family exchanged with at arbitrarily late steps does not carry the count of its exchanges, nor does a finite whole (`Web.unread_of_unbounded`, `Flow.uncarried_of_bounded`, `World.forgets_of_bounded`); with loss at each change, the changes over a stretch number at most the stock, and change does not recur without end (`Web.changes_le_stock`, `Web.rests_of_loses`). Entropy's returning, resting and forgetting under the first law are the cases with the bound balance supplies (`first_law_cases`).
- **Premises read off the exchange (`Becoming.Reading`).** Activity read as exchanging and drawing as taking, only active relata give and only active relata take, and only what they draw on, for every flow (`Flow.readOff_acting`, `Flow.readOff_taking`). With the rest of the whole counted as a relatum, every world balances, and its balance is exactly the rest never active (`World.withRest_balanced`, `World.withRest_closed_iff`). Under a law of exchange, with requirement read as what the exchange responds to, activity needs support for every need, so all four tying premises hold with no premise (`ExchangeLaw.readWeb_dependent`, `ExchangeLaw.readWorld_glue`); requirement is a counterfactual of the law that no course fixes (`ExchangeLaw.requirement_beyond_course`). What remains of the eight premises are states of parts: whether the rest is idle (closure, for a law feasibility), dissipation, embodiment, bounded transmission and the decision to list what is active.
- **One unfolding, read in context (`Becoming.Readings`).** The readings in context of a run are always one world whose relata are the indexed claims, with no gluing, quantum registrations included (`Contextual.world`); nothing stands or is exchanged in that world. The standing and the exchange read one change alike exactly where the flow balances, so a world's balance is the gluing of two readings (`Flow.changeReadings_glue_everywhere_iff`); with the rest counted the two glue at every step, and every world is one run of the law of exchange (`World.unity`). Under the same standard of invariance, significance from a standpoint holds within some boundary only by holding for everything (`presupposition_iff_all_of_invariant`, `bounded_invariant_iff`, restating checked results of `Becoming.Boundaries`; not new results).
