= ALT @statement/zh
- 对相同已定的选项与给每个选项任何种类条目的模型：两个选项被每个经模型的选择选得相同，恰当模型使二者并列；至多选一个选项的这种选择能选出 k，恰当没有别的选项的条目等于 k 的。
+ 对相同已定的备择与给每个备择任何种类条目的映射：两个备择被每个经映射的选择选得相同，恰当映射使二者并列；至多选一个备择的这种选择能选出 k，恰当没有别的备择的条目等于 k 的。
= ALT @statement/en
- For options with decided equality and a model giving each an entry of any kind: two options are selected alike by every selection through the model exactly when the model ties them, and such a selection selecting at most one option can select k exactly when no other option's entry equals k's.
+ For alternatives with decided equality and a map giving each an entry of any kind: two are selected alike by every selection through the map exactly when the map ties them, and such a selection selecting at most one alternative can select k exactly when no other alternative's entry equals k's.
= ALT @laws/0/zh
- 交换两个选项（swap）是对合。选择、取值与选取经模型：交换两个选项的条目，即交换所选者、所取的值或所选取者。交换两个并列的选项，模型不变。
+ 交换两个备择（swap）是对合。选择、取值与选取经映射：交换两个备择的条目，即交换所选者、所取的值或所选取者。交换两个并列的备择，映射不变。
= ALT @laws/0/en
- Exchanging two options (swap) is an involution. A selection, a valuation and a choice are through the model when exchanging two options' entries exchanges what is selected, the values or the choice. Exchanging two tied options leaves the model as it was.
+ Exchanging two alternatives (swap) is an involution. A selection, a valuation and a choice are through the map when exchanging two alternatives' entries exchanges what is selected, the values or the choice. Exchanging two tied alternatives leaves the map as it was.
= ALT @laws/1/zh
- 并列的选项被选得相同；两个选项被每个经模型的选择选得相同，恰当模型使二者并列。见证是选出条目等于给定值的选项。
+ 并列的备择被选得相同；两个备择被每个经映射的选择选得相同，恰当映射使二者并列。见证是选出条目等于给定值的备择。
= ALT @laws/1/en

+ Tied alternatives are selected alike, and two alternatives are selected alike by every selection through the map exactly when the map ties them. The witness selects the alternatives whose entry equals a given value.
= ALT @laws/2/zh

= ALT @laws/2/en
- A selection through the model that selects at most one option selects no tied option, and none at a full tie of two or more. Holding an entry alone (Alone) is such a selection, so an option can be singled out exactly when no other option's entry ties it.
+ A selection through the map that selects at most one alternative selects no tied alternative, and none at a full tie of two or more. Holding an entry alone (Alone) is such a selection, so an alternative can be singled out exactly when no other alternative's entry ties it.
= ALT @laws/3/zh

+ 经映射的取值（如权重）给并列的备择相同的值，全并列时处处相同；取某值的备择是经映射的选择；常值经映射；两个备择被每个这样的取值取得相同，恰当二者并列。
= ALT @laws/3/en
- A valuation through the model, such as a weight on each option, gives tied options equal values, and every option the same at a full tie; the options taking a value form a selection through the model; a constant valuation is one; two options are valued alike by every such valuation exactly when tied.
+ A valuation through the map, such as a weight on each alternative, gives tied alternatives equal values, and every alternative the same at a full tie; the alternatives taking a value form a selection through the map; a constant valuation is one; two alternatives are valued alike by every such valuation exactly when tied.
= ALT @laws/4/zh

+ 经映射、至多选取一个备择的选取，其所选取者是经映射的选择；它不选取并列的备择，全并列时不选取。
= ALT @laws/4/en

+ A choice of at most one alternative through the map selects, through the map, the alternative it chooses; it chooses no tied alternative and withholds at a full tie.
= ALT @laws/5/zh

+ 映射是一个归并（Learning.Lumped）：经映射的选择是经其归并的选择，对要紧者（谓词 p 或取值 f）正确，只在要紧者经归并同去之处；要紧者分开映射所并的两个备择时，无一正确；要紧者从映射读出时，经映射读出即正确。
= ALT @laws/5/en
- The model is a lumping (Learning.Lumped): a selection through it is a selection through its lumping, right about what matters (a predicate p or values f) only where what matters goes alike across the lumping; where what matters separates two options the model lumps, none is right; where it is read off the model, reading it through the model is right.
+ The map is a lumping (Learning.Lumped): a selection through it is a selection through its lumping, right about what matters (a predicate p or values f) only where what matters goes alike across the lumping; where what matters separates two alternatives the map lumps, none is right; where it is read off the map, reading it through the map is right.
