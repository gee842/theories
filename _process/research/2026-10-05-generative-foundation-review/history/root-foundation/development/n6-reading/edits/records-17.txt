- Claims registered in contexts (Contextual S K C V): within k c says that context k registers claim c, and shows s k c is what k registers of c at configuration s. At s the registrations glue (Contextual.Glues) when some v : C → V agrees with every context on every claim it registers; this gives that any two contexts registering a claim agree (Contextual.Glues.agree), and where each claim has a context registering it the two are equivalent (Contextual.glues_iff_agree). Read with its context, every registration is a claim of a frame (Contextual.indexed), and the indexed frame fits every registration (Contextual.indexed_fits); a frame of context-free claims fits (Contextual.FitsFree) exactly where the registrations glue at every configuration (Contextual.fitsFree_iff). Where what a context can show depends only on the claims it registers (Local)
+ Claims shown in contexts (Contextual S K C V): within k c says that claim c lies within context k, and shows s k c is what k shows of c at configuration s. At s what the contexts show glues (Contextual.Glues) when some v : C → V agrees with every context on every claim within it; this gives that any two contexts a claim lies within agree (Contextual.Glues.agree), and where each claim lies within some context the two are equivalent (Contextual.glues_iff_agree). Read with its context, everything shown is a claim of a frame (Contextual.indexed), and the indexed frame fits everything shown (Contextual.indexed_fits); a frame of context-free claims fits (Contextual.FitsFree) exactly where what the contexts show glues at every configuration (Contextual.fitsFree_iff). Where what a context can show depends only on the claims within it (Local)
