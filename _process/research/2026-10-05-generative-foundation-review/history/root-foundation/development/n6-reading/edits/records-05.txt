= L-miss @statement/en
- Every learner has an open course refuting each of its claims; no record fixes the next value. In a world held by a list the refutations are bounded, coming to rest is unrefuted and happens only there, and when is not registered; learning over classes is the same learning.
+ Every extrapolation has an open course on which each value it proposes misses; no prefix fixes the next value. In a world held by a list the misses are bounded, coming to rest is unrefuted and happens only there, and when does not show; learning over classes is the same learning.

= L-miss @claim/en
- A course o : Nat → O is a sequence of registered values (such as the registration of a determined law's orbit); record o t is its record before step t, latest first; a learner is any claim pred : List O → Option O made from a record; a claim is refuted (Refuted) when it claims a value and the registered value differs; a hypothesis holds on a course (Holds) when it is refuted at no step. With no premise: for every course and step t, given two values, two courses share its record before t and take the one and the other value at t, and a claim made from that record is not right on both (no_record_fixes_next), and with values decided is refuted on one of them (claim_refuted_on_one); both are courses of worlds held by a list (holdFrom_lasso, no_record_fixes_next_finite). For every learner and every other moving each value, the diagonal course diagonal pred other x refutes each of its claims (diagonal_refutes), so on it the learner does not, from any step on, claim at every step and be right (diagonal_unrested); it is the registration of a law keeping its record, whose configurations no list holds (record_diagonal, diagonal_open). Learning by refutation (indexOn, learner: hypotheses H in order, moving on exactly where the one held is refuted; values decided) never passes a hypothesis that holds: where H j holds the index is at most j (index_le_of_holds) and the learner is not refuted at arbitrarily late steps (refutations_not_recurring); every hypothesis passed was refuted on the record at some step (refuted_before); one record gives one hypothesis and one claim (learner_through_record). Lassos (Lasso o i q: repeating from step i with period q + 1): where a determined law's orbit is held by a list l, every registration of it is a lasso with i + q + 1 ≤ l.length (lasso_of_held); every lasso is the registration of a world held by a list (lasso_realized). With the lasso hypotheses in diagonal order (lassos, pairAt, pairAt_place): on a lasso course the learner is refuted at most tri (i + q) + q times, claims at every step from step i + q + 1 on and is not refuted at arbitrarily late steps (lasso_learner_bounded), and that from some step on it claims at every step and is right is unrefuted (rest_unrefuted, a double negation); where from some step on it claims at every step and is right, the course is a lasso (lasso_of_rest); where the orbit is held by a list l it is refuted fewer than tri l.length times, claims at every step from step l.length on and is not refuted at arbitrarily late steps (held_learner_bounded), its coming to rest unrefuted (held_rest_unrefuted); its diagonal course is no lasso (diagonal_not_lasso); that a bounded count that never falls is constant from some step on is unrefuted (stable_unrefuted). With classes G c merged along the pairs (merged), where G c j holds the learner over the merged enumeration has index at most tri (c + j) + j (merged_bound), and the learner over every merging is unrested on its diagonal course (merged_unrested). Witness: on Alternate (false, true, false, …) the learner is refuted twice and right at every step from the third (Alternate.index_three, Alternate.rests).
