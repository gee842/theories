- # Learnable runs: what a learner reading a run's registration can learn
-
- `Becoming.Learnable` states what a learner reading a registration can learn along the orbit of a
- determined law. A determined law is a process of the spine (`Law.process`), and every run of it is
- its orbit from where the run begins (`Branching.law_run_state`); configurations of a determined
- law are alike in the frame that registers `reg` exactly where their registered futures are the
- same (`Law.alike_iff_sameFuture`). This module states the laws of `Becoming.Learnable` about the
- runs of the law and the likeness of the frame, and proves them through those laws.
-
- * **A run's registered record is its orbit's.** The record a learner reads along a run is the
-   record along the orbit from the run's first configuration (`prefix_eq`), so a learner rests on
-   a run exactly where it rests on that orbit (`restsFrom_iff`, `rests_iff`).
- * **Alike in the frame, learned alike.** Two runs beginning at configurations alike in the frame
-   that registers `reg` give every learner the same record at every step (`prefixes_same`); a
-   learner resting on both has targets that agree from some step on (`agree_of_rests_both`), and
-   where their targets differ at arbitrarily late steps no learner rests on both
-   (`not_rests_on_both`).
- * **What is read off the registration is learned along every run a list holds**
-   (`rests_through`), by the learner over the lasso hypotheses given nothing.
- * **Exactly.** In a world a list holds, some learner rests on the target along every run of the
-   law exactly where runs beginning alike in the frame have targets that agree from some step on
-   (`learnable_iff`); where the law leaves every configuration unchanged, exactly where the target
-   goes alike across the registration (`still_learnable_iff_lumped`).

### lean:ExactRecordRuns
= .
- # Exact readings of a run's registered record
-
- `KnowledgeCorrespondence` states, for histories, an evidence projection and a two-valued target,
- when a reading of the evidence answers the target soundly (never wrongly) or exactly (always
- rightly) on a domain of histories, and when the evidence resolves the target (histories with one
- evidence have one target). Here the histories are the runs of a process, the evidence at step `t`
- is the record a registration `reg` leaves along the run before `t` (`recordBefore`), and the
- target is a two-valued reading `val` of the run's configuration at `t` (`valueAt`). A reading of
- the record is then a learner at one step: for a determined law, a learner claims the target along
- every run from `T` on exactly where it reads the record exactly at every step from `T` on
- (`exact_iff_restsFrom`, through `LearnableRuns.RestsFrom`). This module states the periphery's
- general laws of these readings of runs and proves them through those laws.
+ # Exact readings of a run's prefix through a map
+
+ `KnowledgeCorrespondence` states, for histories, an evidence projection and a two-valued target,
+ when a reading of the evidence answers the target soundly (never wrongly) or exactly (always
+ rightly) on a domain of histories, and when the evidence resolves the target (histories with one
+ evidence have one target). Here the histories are the runs of a process, the evidence at step `t`
+ is the prefix before `t` of the run's course through a map `reg` (`recordBefore`), and the
+ target is a two-valued feature `val` of the run's configuration at `t` (`valueAt`). A reading of
+ the prefix is then an extrapolation at one step: for a determined law, an extrapolation proposes
+ the feature's value along every run from `T` on exactly where it reads the prefix exactly at
+ every step from `T` on (`exact_iff_restsFrom`, through `LearnableRuns.RestsFrom`). This module
+ states the periphery's general laws of these readings of runs and proves them through those laws.

-   domain have one record before `t` and different values at `t`, a sound reading gives nothing on

-   reading a second registration beside the first** (`finer_resolves`).
- * **A faithfully kept record keeps exactness** (`kept_exact`): reading the record through what a
-   retention keeps is exact where the retention is read back faithfully on the domain.
