### What a collective's continuation needs

Where: chapter §4, "What a collective's continuation needs"; the records [giving-that-waits-on-giving](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/giving-that-waits-on-giving.md), [whether-a-loop-closes-a-difference](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whether-a-loop-closes-a-difference.md), [how-fast-a-rule-may-change](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/how-fast-a-rule-may-change.md), [a-pattern-lasts-through-passing](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/a-pattern-lasts-through-passing.md) and [conditions-kept-together](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/conditions-kept-together.md); study section 193 ([V](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#193-what-a-collectives-continuation-needs-v)). Decided in the one account of D45.

**Ren's words.** On 19 October 2026, with a pasted eight-constraint account of social viability: "does our theories fully cover this?", then "please, spawn a swarm of subagents, think hard, let's fully complete this" and "I agree with your push back, let's think about how to resolve them elegantly" ([words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-19-collective-continuation.md)). His agreement with the pushbacks is provenance; each stands on its law.

**Decision.** Adopted with exact scope. (1) **Five framings.** Keeping a part is not renewal (`Renewable.kept_part_not_renewal`); there is no number of the whole, the loss lying at a dependence that breaks (`Flow.act_unselected`, `Flow.favours_reaching_beyond`); conditions are joined, not multiplied: they decompose exactly under every course and not along some course, and no rule on the separate keepabilities decides the joint one (`Viability.kernel_and_iff`, `ChoicePoint.no_rule_decides_joint`); a counter-loop has to reach the rule (`Law.perturbation_reabsorbed`), stated as pooling couplings, not as opposed interests; the invariants are laws, not axioms. (2) **Readings of the four laws.** Cooperation among strangers is giving that waits on giving; more waiting is less giving; the spiral of distrust is withholding with memory, which absorbs, and around a loop without memory it returns; what restores is a law that gives without waiting on the last step (kept promises, enforced contracts, institutions acting whatever was last received). Bounded conflict is a loop that closes only through a link giving back less than it received (compensation, arbitration, absorption, exit as a cut followed by links giving nothing for nothing); with whole-number strengths nothing closes gradually. Stability with adaptability is a slow level reading the fast one at stretches of at least `2N − 1` steps. Forming the next members is passing a pattern across brief carriers. (3) **The correction point.** The second order is a loop whose rule-writing reaches the routes of 明; the third is a selection favouring narrowing what couples in. (4) **Not read into it.** No "must": the laws say what courses do under stated conditions; that a layering, a law that gives first or a closing loop is better is the comparison's to say (§6.15), not the law's; passing carries whatever pattern it carries.
**Reasons.** Each reading is argued from the declarations it names, and each pushback is a theorem, not a disclaimer, so the account's own claims are checked where it makes them: its "any factor zero" half holds under every course, its product claim fails along choices, and its survival criterion is separated from renewal.
**What would change it.** A law deriving the waiting relation from the flow; a loop law with real strengths under which a multiplication closes gradually; a collective whose continuation does not go through choices (where joint keeping would decompose); a derivation of passing from descent.
