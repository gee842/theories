**Thresholds of waiting and the laws that restore giving** ([Becoming.Tolerance](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Tolerance.lean); records [restoring-giving-from-the-worst-start](../results/records/restoring-giving-from-the-worst-start.md), [thresholds-of-waiting](../results/records/thresholds-of-waiting.md)). Let a formation spare some of what it waits on: under the tolerating law it gives where all but at most its tolerance of what it waits on gave (`tolerating`), so a formation waiting on `m` and sparing `m - k` gives where at least `k` gave. Tolerance zero is waiting on all, with the course of the law without memory (`tolerating_zero`). Every one of these laws keeps inclusion: more giving now gives at least as much giving next. For every law that does, the worst start is total withholding: a formation gives at step `n` from every start exactly where it gives at `n` from the start at which nothing gives (`worst_start`). From total withholding giving only grows (`rises_from_withheld`), so what is restored by a step stays restored (`restored_stays`), and where no formation gives at the step after total withholding, none gives at any step (`withheld_kept`). Under the tolerating law, that formation is one sparing all it waits on (`gives_needs_sparing`, `sparing_gives`); without one, total withholding is kept (`total_stays`). Where one formation gives whatever it waits on (`givesFirst`) and the sources are decided, a formation gives at step `n` from every start exactly where no chain of `n` waits clear of sources leads into it (`restores_iff`); where no such chain is `L` waits long, every formation gives from step `L` on (`restores_from`), and a loop clear of sources is never restored (`clear_loop_withholds`). The ring of three is an instance (`Ring.restores_from_three`). Tolerance absorbs without restoring. A set of formations each waiting on at most its tolerance outside the set keeps giving, whatever the rest does (`kept_set`). A withholding that every formation spares passes out in one step (`thin_absorbed`). In three formations each waiting on the other two, one withholding spreads to all from step 2 with tolerance zero and passes out at step 1 with tolerance one, and with tolerance one total withholding is kept at every step (`Triad.spreads`, `Triad.absorbed`, `Triad.total_kept`). The certificate of `Becoming.FaultEnvelopes`, read as a law of giving (`certified`), keeps a set giving where each of its domains has a separated envelope admitting a fault set that holds all of its roster outside the set (`certified_kept`), and is available exactly where the envelopes are separated (`certified_available_iff`, `certified_blocked`).

Read onto collectives (the AI's reading, D48), tolerance is the slack that lets cooperation among strangers survive some defection. It keeps a group giving whose members rely on few outside it, and it absorbs a scattered defection at once. It does not rebuild trust once it is gone everywhere: from total withholding only a formation that gives whatever it receives restores giving, and it restores exactly the formations into which every long enough chain of reliance passes such a formation. A promise kept, a contract enforced or an institution acting whatever was last received is such a source. A cycle of mutual reliance with no such source on it stays withheld.
