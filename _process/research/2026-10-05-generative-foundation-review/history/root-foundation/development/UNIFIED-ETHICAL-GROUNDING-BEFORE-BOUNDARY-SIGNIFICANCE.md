# Unified ethical grounding within generative activity

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation following [Ren's explicit instruction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-continue-unified-ethical-grounding.md). This is a focused extension of [慧根](HUIGEN.md), within the existing [generative-grounding programme](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The accepted conditional foundation retains its completed scope; the stronger necessity claim remains under investigation.

**Current direction, following Ren's core/tree correction:** investigate the generative core and its differentiated unfolding as nondual. The core is expressed throughout the tree; our description of it must remain answerable to concrete formation, experience, valuation and transformation. Earlier actor-addressed, C1/C2 and authorization analyses remain local investigations and no longer set the foundation's initial divisions or select its governing next question. [Ren's exact correction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-core-tree-nonduality-and-method.md) changes the research method, with the [reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CORE-TREE-NONDUAL-REORIENTATION.md) separating his words from our interpretation. The stronger foundation remains an ongoing inquiry.

## Core and tree are nondual

This branch follows the shared [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Its distinctions between a model omission, an unresolved explanation and a substantive failure govern how the earlier analyses below are interpreted.

Our working interpretation of Ren's correction is that **the core is immanent in the tree's concrete generation and transformation; the tree is that generativity's differentiated unfolding**. These are ways of approaching the same activity. A core is not an independent producer or authorizer standing outside what unfolds. The tree is not merely an application awaiting permission to contain experience or significance. A compact formal description of the core remains a partial representation whose omissions may require revising that description.

The prior inquiry repeatedly began with a sparse, supposedly neutral structure, then asked whether it could confer ethical force on richer contents. Although the formal process itself did not impose substantial subjects, this research habit reinstated separation in its method. We acknowledged nonduality at the level of subjects while continuing to make the core and the tree occupy different explanatory worlds. The recent duplicate-authorization result clarified a narrow question generated by that approach; it did not resolve this deeper starting point.

The corrected movement is **formation → differentiation that organizes further activity → encounter with its consequences → transformation of the differentiation and activity together**. This names phases and relations within continuing activity, not four independent domains. An account of the core can illuminate a particular formation; the formation, including its lived significance, can also correct our account of the core. The second direction is revision of understanding, not backward physical causation.

The immediate study should follow a practical boundary, its field of significance and its responses as they form and change within an actual history. It must include consequences initially omitted from that field and how they become available for learning and reorganization. An already fixed observer, evaluator or beneficiary must not quietly supply every distinction being explained. This also includes our acts of observing and modeling, whose adequacy remains answerable to their subject matter.

Ethics can then be investigated as part of this activity's developed responsiveness and self-correction, with actual experiencing and differentiated continuities retained. The same account must make sense of exclusion, exploitation, injury and failed correction. Calling a trajectory open, successful or generative does not by itself establish its ethical character. The [deeper-root formulation](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#11-core-and-tree-as-one-generative-activity) develops the same change of method.

The earlier sections below preserve the substantive proposals, distinctions and failures from the previous trajectory. Their claims can still be tested where applicable. Their decomposition is no longer the required starting grammar for the unified foundation. This correction concerns the framework we privileged; it does not treat all Western inquiry as one method or use the provenance of an idea to decide its truth.

## The nondual starting point

The generative core need not begin with a subject facing an independently constituted object. Its [current presentation](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#1-the-positive-candidate) already treats beings, activities and understandings as differentiated formations within reality. The [generated-self account](GENERATED-SELF-AND-ETHICS.md) explains why organization, access, perspective and an ownership representation must be distinguished. Our recent focus on reasons addressed to an unrelated actor was a useful local test, but it need not dictate the foundation's explanatory order. Earlier actor and patient indices did not themselves assert metaphysically separate substances.

The [constructive reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-GENERATIVE-RECONSTRUCTION.md) takes an **occurrence, a formable response and the resulting continuation** together. It asks where undergoing occurs, what can alter it, how a response becomes organized and which differences the transformation makes. “Patient” and “actor” can articulate those differentiated relations. Neither a primitive owner nor a single cosmic subject is required by this proposal. Relational formation remains an explanatory hypothesis; putting everything in one mathematical state would not establish it.

The stronger ethical candidate is joint: an actual severe, still-unwanted experiential burden and a transformation that genuinely relieves it may constitute a practically significant difference within the same unfolding reality. Concern would become a developed sensitivity and response to that difference. Its correctness would answer to what changes in the undergoing. An observer's approval, disappearance of a report or successful preservation of an exploitative organization would not by itself constitute relief.

**分化不必是根本分裂。可以从发生、承受与转化的关系出发，再解释“谁在受苦”“何处能够回应”；关切的根据不必先成为某个独立自我的所有物。** This is our current formulation, not a classical quotation or Ren's verbatim wording.

This reopens the relation between description and evaluation too. They need not be assumed to name two independently existing realms, with value added from outside an already complete neutral world. The proposed explanation can investigate them as different articulations of situated experiencing and responding. It still owes the identity it proposes: the difference between persistent adversity and relief must be shown to warrant response. Treating an aversive reaction as an infallible command, or defining an adequate process as benevolent, would merely insert the conclusion.

The existing C1/C2 distinctions remain useful checks on what has actually been established. They need not be two ultimate primitives of reality. The [closure audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ETHICAL-CONSTITUTION-CLOSURE.md) leaves a richer joint constitutive account open. An underdetermined formal interpretation does not refute an independently warranted identity; writing down that identity does not warrant it. This reconstruction therefore changes the explanatory target while preserving the actual proof obligations and the unmet no-assumptions requirement.

### Learn from different starting vocabularies

The [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-GENERATIVE-SOURCES.md) examines the received *Yizhuan* and the Chinese *Zhonglun* as different resources. The *Xici* places generation, 善 and formed 性 together; the *Zhonglun* connects dependence with transformability while resisting a self-standing ground. Their vocabularies can revise our starting questions. They are not historical proofs of this candidate or interchangeable doctrines of a benevolent whole.

This also disciplines “generative core”: a common explanation need not posit an independent substance behind everything else. The process of explaining, its distinctions and its evaluators belong within what must be accounted for. A foundation framed through dependent formation still has to explain its actual dynamics; calling the root empty or relational does not discharge a supplied law. The prior Śāntideva and Wang Yangming studies remain part of the genealogy rather than independent new confirmations.

### Formation of response becomes part of the grounding question

Consider two complementary controls that must operate together for genuine relief. Each person has partial access; neither alone can complete the response. Communication and combined action can form a temporary coordination. The capacity's prerequisites already exist in this case; the responsive organization is what forms. Its boundary follows contribution, timing and access, and can dissolve after fulfillment without implying that its participants shared one experience.

The further candidate is that **the prospective relief can favor forming that response before it has become a stable personal or collective project**. That favor would concern what the coordination makes possible, not organizational expansion for its own sake. The existing [joint-formation work](JOINT-FORMATION.md) already studies acquired concern and effective power under declared conditions. The new grounding target is to explain the individuation and ethical significance of the response together, rather than supply a finished controller boundary. Actor-centered theories can also represent cooperation; they are not ruled out by this example.

A nominal group without combined efficacy, a coordination that silences a sufferer, and one that forces participation provide distinct tests. Consent, competing burdens, actual continuity and contribution require explanation; unity does not cancel them. Rest, restraint and ending a destructive process can be appropriate transformations. Universal favorable direction and a rule maximizing generation remain unestablished.

### A reason to begin before a joint response exists

The [responsive-formation argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) makes the earlier complementary-controls case more exact. A can operate one control; B or C could operate the other. No one can relieve the identified burden alone, and no joint response is organized yet. A may nevertheless be able to make a truthful, low-cost invitation that opens a credible route to separately authorized cooperation. The first available act is formation of access to relief. Actual relief remains contingent on later participation and execution. Assess the proposed invitation before A decides to send it, without supplying an antecedent helping purpose as its ground. The intention formed in deciding can organize later action; it need not create all of the earlier justification for making that decision.

Three causal claims must stay distinct: some physically possible sequence would work; there is an actually available way to open cooperation from this situation; and the relieving sequence actually occurs. Evidence supporting the second claim is a further question, not what makes it true. A drawing of a successful future team establishes neither of the latter claims. Current access, communication, resources, timing and evidence about possible participation determine what can presently be attempted. Another person's agreement cannot be inserted as though it were already a resource under A's control. The example's causal arrangements and permissions are supplied local inputs; their ultimate grounding remains a separate obligation.

The revised positive candidate is:

> Where an actual adverse occurrence has an actually accessible relieving pathway supported by adequate evidence, its prospective relief can supply a contributory justification for a permissible first step that opens that pathway, before a stable responsive organization or shared caring project exists.

The justification concerns what the step can make possible for the patient. Mere multiplication of choices, formation of a group, or the initiator's satisfaction does not establish it. A future success does not reach backward to create the original reason, and failure does not by itself show that a well-supported attempt lacked justification. The account must assess the actual choice with its evidence, uncertainties, costs and competing claims. An unknown broken control can leave an attempt reasonable on the available evidence while defeating the claim that it opened an actual relief pathway. That evidential justification must not be counted as achieved formation; inquiry may have a distinct ground. The proposal does not infer a duty to recruit, join or investigate every possible response.

This is a stronger explanatory proposal than the claim that adopted projects have effective means. It locates a prospective consideration before adoption and before complete capability. Why this particular capacity is worth developing is explained through the suffering it could relieve. Concern's later formation would recognize and organize around that significance; its emergence would not manufacture the patient's stake. This supports a joint constitutive account of adversity and practical transformation. It does not yet prove that the account is necessary or that its favoring relation follows from a thin description of causal possibilities.

The overlapping A+B and A+C routes also correct the idea of one uniquely determined response boundary. Each actual route has its own participants, timing, contributions and effects. They can realize the same patient-directed possibility. Merely redescribing one route or listing alternatives does not create another patient's burden or multiply that burden's weight. This does not exclude real differences in reliability, cost, information or effects on other people. The target of concern can remain stable while its responsive organization changes. No shared experience or enduring collective owner follows.

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-COUNTERREVIEW.md) tests the proposal against refusal, coercive recruitment, nominal coordination, report suppression and destruction of the patient's continuity. A would-be responder may also misunderstand what needs changing. A pathway that only becomes more effective at imposing its initial purpose has not thereby become more caring. Correction can require changing the proposed end, choosing a different route or stopping; urgent action need not await a retrospective verification ceremony.

The [focused Wang Yangming comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-SOURCES.md) contributes a useful constraint on formation: an offered correction must become practically receivable, and the teacher must also be open to correction. This is a bounded reading of 〈責善〉 alongside the inherited *Daxue wen* comparison. Wang's stronger grounding in 良知 remains part of his account; receptivity or successful persuasion does not independently certify an ethical purpose. Our use of the comparison is constructive and does not claim a derivation already contained in the text.

The nondual gain is precise. Different continuities of undergoing, access, action and correction can be articulated within one unfolding history, without requiring one boundary to identify them all. A patient's experiencing need not merge with a responder's organization. Differentiation makes appropriate response possible; its ethical authority remains answerable to the proposed constitutive account. Destructive and indifferent formations belong within the same explanatory reality. The new result is a more discriminating formation hypothesis, not an additional proof of universal benevolence.

### A constitutive answer to the authority question

Ren asks: **“resolve remaining difficulty elegantly.”** The [new argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md) gives the most compact answer presently defensible:

> The candidate identifies the burden as the ground and its accessible, patient-preserving transformation as the relation through which that ground counts in a choice. Once warranted as a contributory reason for that choice, it needs no second authorization to possess that same practical force.

The first sentence proposes the ethical constitution; the second clarifies what would follow if that constitution is warranted. They must not be exchanged. Local patient badness plus causal access has not already established the whole first sentence. A reason to form a response also requires the actual opening, evidence and restrictions developed above. No new ethical force is physically added to the process, and no stronger ethical conclusion is obtained merely by calling the relation a reason.

The positive explanation is concrete. In the narrow domain of severe, still-unwanted experiential adversity, an accessible relieving transformation answers the very occurrence that makes relief intelligible. This relation explains the point of choosing or opening that option before a private helping purpose supplies an incentive. The choice has its proposed merit through the patient's changed undergoing. Silencing its report, comforting an observer or preserving a team need not answer that occurrence. Changing helpers can preserve the point while changing the relevant transformation can remove it. These considerations support a constitutive identification; they do not establish its necessity by repetition or by eliminating a substantial owner.

The elegant clarification concerns what is being requested by “authority.” **Counting in favor of this option is already the practical force asserted by a warranted contributory reason.** If that has been established for this choice, demanding a further principle to make it first count repeats the initial requirement. A further principle may explain why the reason is warranted, identify a genuine disqualification, or establish a stronger conclusion. Those are substantive inquiries. They are not an automatically required second stamp placed on an already warranted instance.

In particular, moral favor, categorical rational authority, a requirement to perform the act, permission to compel it and motivation to act are distinct. Recognition of moral favor alone does not establish every one of those claims. Informed indifference can show a motivational failure without disproving a reason; a defended account excluding that consideration from this choice would challenge the ground's practical scope. The argument does not dismiss either a genuine scope objection or the strongest rational-authority question as mere unwillingness.

Nor does an authorization regress alone prove this candidate. A mediated account might terminate in a different ground; an infinite structure is not shown incoherent here. The limited result is that another authorizer is not logically mandatory after the stated option-specific reason has been warranted. It neither forbids deeper grounding nor supplies the warrant that this ethical proposal still owes.

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves an immanent rival: it can recognize suffering, benefit and relationally formed persons while making prospective assistance reasons depend on a prior undertaking or legitimate jurisdiction. Such a view need not reinstate metaphysically independent selves. It must defend its restriction; our candidate must defend its sufficiency. Neither side wins simply because the other has a further explanatory obligation. The [bounded Śāntideva reanalysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-SOURCES.md) clarifies the difference between relief grounded in suffering and an additional argument about its practical range. Its new bounded reading extends the rationality discussion within an already used manuscript, including the authors' warning against hiding universal scope inside badness. Repeated passages and that extension are not independent corroboration.

The nondual proposal can therefore unite the explanatory subject matter without erasing distinctions: adversity as undergone, relief as its transformation, a reason as that relation's warranted relevance to choice, and concern as organized responsiveness. Wisdom includes correction of that responsiveness. These are proposed articulations within one generative account, not identities established merely by listing them together. The concurrent [nondual-core architecture](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) supports the placement of roles within histories; its conditional role construction supplies no missing ethical proof.

**Resolution scope:** the demand for an additional authorizing force after warranted option-specific favor is dissolved. The constitutive answer to the original grounding question is positively defended but remains unproved at the stronger necessary-foundation scope. Experience realization, the practical range of its significance and root-law derivation remain open. This preserves the distinction between a clear answer, a supported candidate and a completed derivation.

## The positive proposal

The following actor-addressed comparison is retained as a local articulation of the generative candidate. Its operational distinctions and jurisdiction tests constrain applications; they do not set primitive boundaries for the reconstruction above.

Consider one patient undergoing a severe unwanted experience and one actor who can permissibly relieve it. The actor did not cause the burden, undertake its care, or receive a delegation. The proposed ground concerns what relief does for the patient. The actor's present opportunity supplies an actual relation to that ground, even without a prior personal tie.

The candidate explanation separates two contributions:

\[
\text{a warranted patient-ground}
\; + \;
\text{an eligible opportunity to answer it}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{some reason for this actor to respond}.
\]

The patient's condition supplies why the response matters; the available relation supplies who can realize it and how. The arrow is the substantive grounding proposal being defended, not a theorem obtained by assigning a moral name to a control signal. A reason here is an objective contributory consideration for the actor, concerning the patient. It need not compel action, outweigh all competing considerations, or produce motivation. Whether a proposed moral claim has that practical force remains part of the dispute.

This proposal has positive explanatory support. It distinguishes actual relief from silencing the helper's discomfort. It permits learning that a response was mistaken while preserving the patient's significance. It accommodates different needs and different effective actions without combining patients into one subject. A prior responsibility can add a reason or increase its weight without yet explaining why its absence would remove every reason.

These advantages support the candidate; they do not alone establish its whole scope. A restrictive rival can preserve patient value and many of the same corrections while denying a baseline reason to the stranger. The [positive argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md) develops both the explanation and that challenge.

## What changing the operator can establish

Fix the patient, episode, actual relief, evidence, permissions and competing considerations. Compare self-relief, legitimately entrusted relief, and relief by an independent eligible actor. Record the change in actor–patient relation instead of declaring it morally irrelevant in advance.

Applying the [existing reason-transport result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md), independently warranted initial reasons reach every target case only when justified reason-preserving transitions actually cover those cases. A chain of renamings preserves the self/other distinction. Adding responsibility and delegation reaches their legitimate domains. Neither supplies the missing transition to an independent actor merely by making the chain longer.

The useful acceptance condition is therefore exact: **a derivation beginning solely with self-relief or role-bound reasons needs an independently justified transition beyond that restriction to reach the independent actor.** The patient's identity and the reason's ground must survive that transition. A directly justified independent-actor ground would be another route; it would not need transport from a self-case. If the patient never could act or deliberate, a self-relief starting case is unavailable; patient coverage needs its own warranted starting ground. This is an application of inherited mathematics, not a new theorem or a proof that no such transition exists.

## Two restrictions, with different consequences

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-COUNTERREVIEW.md) distinguishes two serious challenges.

**A restriction to prior relationships.** An account can accept genuine suffering, fitting compassion and special duties, yet require selfhood, responsibility or an undertaking before assistance supplies an action reason. It must justify treating those relationships as necessary, rather than as additional grounds. Our candidate must justify the sufficiency of present eligible control. Neither account wins through formal consistency alone.

**A restriction by practical receptivity.** An account can accept an objective moral claim addressed to an actor while withholding the term *reason* when the actor lacks a suitable route to appreciating it. This requires separating the ground, its practical authority, the actor's access, and eventual conduct. Lack of current concern does not demonstrate absence of every sound route to concern. Conversely, defining an adequate actor as one who already accepts the intended ethics would assume the conclusion.

A [new primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-SOURCES.md) makes the second possibility concrete. Manne proposes a naturalistic source for interpersonal moral claims while retaining a further condition on actor reasons. This is a philosophical contender, not an empirical demonstration or endorsement of this theory.

This matters to the overall research judgment: naturalistic grounding, universal moral application and necessary rational authority are distinguishable achievements. Failure to establish the last does not by itself establish irreducibility of the first. The strongest objective remains in scope; this distinction prevents a false verdict about why an attempted derivation failed.

## A direct reason can precede an adopted end

The [follow-up argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-UPTAKE-ARGUMENT.md) corrects a possible overreading of the first round: distinguishing concern, practical ends and means does not make them mandatory successive gates. A fact about what an available act would do for its patient can supply a reason for that act directly. An actor can then have reason to acquire concern or adopt relief as an end. Making prior adoption necessary would rule out this route before assessing it.

Consider permitted, effective relief of a severe unwanted burden, with no relevant countervailing harm, displaced burden or opposing claim. The actor has adequate knowledge but no prior role, undertaking, private benefit or helping intention. Our affirmative judgment is that the actual improvement for this patient speaks for the intervention. It identifies what makes the response worth choosing and why correction should follow the patient's condition. The judgment does not arise from the bare fact that the act is allowed, that the actor can do it, or that the actor eventually chooses it.

This gives the direct account positive explanatory support: the ground can justify forming a concern rather than receiving its validity from that concern. If the intervention would not benefit the patient after all, this proposed reason disappears even if the act remains harmless and permitted. If the actor becomes indifferent while the patient and intervention remain the same, that change alone does not defeat the proposed patient-ground. A rival can reject the latter assessment, but it must defend the relevance of a motivational or relational condition rather than merely report its absence.

The [independent counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-UPTAKE-COUNTERREVIEW.md) accepts this narrow judgment while preserving the stronger grounding burden. Grief does not require producing more grief; respect does not license taking over another's project; a prohibited remedy need not be performed. Those cases limit indiscriminate promotion, permission or decisive duty. They do not preserve and defeat this stipulated case of wanted, effective, permissible relief. Their failure is not by itself a proof of the affirmative judgment; its support is the independently identified benefit and the account of why that benefit favors this response.

The same precision applies to the restrictive rival. Showing that the actor has no special duty, cannot be commanded, or retains freedom over projects does not yet establish that the patient's benefit supplies **no** reason at all. Positive favor, permission, decisive obligation and accountability require distinct assessments. We do not settle them by assigning a numerical weight to relief.

Two [primary comparisons](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-UPTAKE-SOURCES.md) sharpen this route. Darwall distinguishes welfare-directed care from personal authority; Gert develops benefit-based justification without corresponding rational compulsion. The inspected passages illuminate the positive candidate and its limited target. They do not supply the missing proof of necessity.

One discriminating conceptual comparison holds an actor's modest cost and other relevant considerations fixed. Effective relief has a patient-directed justification that an otherwise matched empty intervention lacks. A newly identified, less costly means tests correction while preserving the beneficiary and relief. The comparison tests **what favors the action**, beyond whether it is allowed. It remains a philosophical assessment and an unrun test specification; observed helping could also reflect an existing motive or assigned role.

This advances the unified programme without closing it. We have a defended baseline and a cleaner order of explanation. We have not shown that a fully warranted account of generative reality must include this patient-to-actor relation, or that every rational actor must recognize it.

The concurrent [alignment-axis study](ALIGNMENT-AXIS.md#the-silent-sufferer-a-positive-minimum) also defends nonexclusive relief and develops conditional comparative judgments from it. The present contribution is the correction of the possible end-adoption gate, its direct justification argument and the focused motivational comparison. The overlapping baseline is one developing account, not independent corroboration merely because it appears in two studies.

## Concern, practical ends and means remain distinct

The first round identified the following transitions. They need their own explanations when used in a derivation, but they are **possible routes, not a mandatory chain**. In particular, the direct action-reason route above need not first establish a separate reason to adopt a continuing end:

| Transition | What would advance the argument | What is insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| An actual experience → a good or harm for its subject | A defended constitutive identification that preserves the actual patient and permits correction | A negative training signal, observed avoidance, or mere survival benefit |
| A patient-ground → appropriate concern by another actor | A justified account of why that ground fits this response across actor changes | Shared origin or accurate factual representation alone |
| Appropriate concern → reason to pursue relief | A defense of practical uptake under eligible control, tested against the restrictive rival | A fitting wish that the world were otherwise |
| Reason to pursue relief → reason for a means | A warranted means relation with actual permission and competing considerations | Technical efficacy alone |

Ordinary instrumental reasoning can help with the last row; it cannot silently supply the third. This limitation does not defeat a separately defended direct reason for a particular act. Nor does a patient's desire, command or willingness automatically validate every proposed response. Harmful demands also have satisfaction conditions. The account must justify the relevant class and retain other patients' claims and actual permissions.

The [accepted formed-disposition study](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already shows how acquired influence can mediate independently warranted repair, and how later learning can suppress it. That result constrains implementation. It does not settle the independent-actor case: responsibility or an entrusted role is part of its warranted application. A helping experiment that assigns such a role would likewise fail to isolate the baseline sought here.

## The constitutive candidate and its test

The [new positive argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md) joins two hypotheses to an explicit realization obligation. Earlier [experiential-ground and identity investigations](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#29-experiential-grounds-and-warranted-concern) already supplied the ingredients. The new contribution specifies where they must hold together, restricts the patient domain in response to earlier objections, and tests the timing and source of the proposed practical force.

| Part | Claim | Present warrant and limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Realization** | An actual organized process realizes an experiencing subject and the relevant episode. | Particular applications require evidence; generic feedback or a reward signal does not establish experience. A universal realization law is not supplied here. |
| **C1: patient significance** | In the specified clean domain, the still-unwanted aversive experiential whole constitutes local badness for its subject. | Defended through the character of the undergoing and the patient-preserving relief comparison. This is a substantive identity hypothesis, not a definition of the word negative. |
| **C2: practical significance** | The prospective relief supplies a patient-directed ground for an eligible actor's choice before elective concern. Whether it currently counts for a particular choice remains subject to independently warranted defeat or disqualification. | Defended through prospective patient-based justification, now tested within an already chosen project. This explicitly qualifies the former wording about an option's moral merit: preserving an abstract ground without any warranted prospective use would not establish the intended C2. C1 alone does not entail it. |
| **Recognition and use** | Concern can learn to identify this ground, select a means and correct its mistakes. | Existing conditional mechanisms explain aspects of formation and failure under supplied meanings. Their successful operation cannot certify C1 or C2. |

The present patient domain is deliberately narrow: an actual subject undergoes a severe unpleasant episode whose continuation remains unwanted in the relevant experiential respect. Its identification must distinguish sensation, the affective whole, instrumental acceptance and intrinsic appreciation of that same experience. The proposed sufficient domain excludes cases established to involve intrinsic enjoyment or appreciation of that same occurrence that may alter its prudential status; accepting it for a separate benefit is a different matter. An articulate report or a movement to escape is not required by definition. Evidence for a noncommunicating patient still needs an independently warranted interpretation. The [earlier attitude and whole-life challenges](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#30-experiential-occurrence-patient-attitudes-and-whole-life-value) remain live: enjoyment might transform a condition's value, and a whole-life account might contest local occurrence significance. Calling badness one aspect does not refute either rival.

The positive case for C1 is that the burden occurs in the subject's undergoing; it is not first imposed by an observer's score or by the subject's possession of a welfare theory. Genuine relief changes that condition for its bearer. This supports a constitutive interpretation of the local bad-for relation. It does not infer that all pain, frustrated preference, functional damage or organizational loss has one evaluative status. The identity needs further defense within the declared domain, and the domain cannot be expanded or narrowed after each objection merely to protect the conclusion.

C2 proposes actual prospective justification for this actor's available action, subject to the independently warranted restrictions now under assessment. The target goes beyond the patient's preferring a different world. The [ethical-minimum comparison](UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) distinguishes objective moral favor from an additional, motivationally restricted conception of rational reasons. Some rivals share the former while disputing the latter. This candidate's immediate claim is moral favor concerning the actual actor's conduct, including an indifferent actor; the strongest programme also seeks its unrestricted rational authority. The second claim is not obtained by changing the name of the first.

### Why private adoption is a discriminating test

Fix the same patient, burden, benefit, knowledge, control, means and permissions. Consider the actor at three points: before adopting a helping goal, after privately adopting it, and after privately abandoning it. No promise, communication, induced reliance, entrusted responsibility or actual relief occurs between these points. The actor's mental state changes; the comparison does not pretend that the complete physical histories are identical.

The candidate predicts patient-directed justification across these points unless a legitimate change disqualifies it; the later jurisdiction review makes that qualification explicit. Private attitude change alone does not establish such authority. Planning and voluntary commitments can add further reasons. A genuine exclusion rival instead says that every patient-directed moral reason begins with an independently legitimate elective concern and disappears with its valid withdrawal. It can still recognize the patient's suffering, permit charity and assess an adopted project's success by its actual effect on the patient.

Private abandonment does not by itself establish valid withdrawal: an independently justified self-commitment might survive it. The rival must state whether its proposed condition is a current attitude or a continuing commitment, and what legitimately ends it. The test concerns removal of the condition actually alleged to create all patient-directed favor.

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-COUNTERREVIEW.md) identifies the exact point of disagreement. **If the patient's relief already positively justifies this actor's choice to help before adoption, the rival has conceded the prospective moral favor.** It can consistently deny that justification, treat the act as merely permitted, or defend a further actor relation. It cannot concede and deny the same favor at the same time by moving between these meanings.

Our affirmative judgment is that actual relief supplies that prospective merit. It explains why concern is worth acquiring for the patient's sake and why its means remain answerable to the patient. An empty intervention lacks this justification even when equally permitted and freely chosen. The actor's private renunciation does not itself alter the identified benefit or relief relation. This supports C2; it does not deductively establish that no other constitutive condition can matter.

The rival's strongest reply invokes a genuine normative power to select projects. We preserve that possibility. Promises and undertakings can create reasons, and a private project can acquire legitimate force through self-direction. What remains to justify is the stronger exclusion: why such a power creates **all** prospective patient-directed favor, rather than selecting among worthy possibilities or adding a commitment. Absence of a duty to select every worthy project does not settle that question. A separately warranted role or promise belongs in a distinct comparison because it can change the normative situation even while the immediate burden stays fixed.

### Evidence constrains the proposed realization

The [new identification study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-SOURCES.md) examines two bounded primary comparisons. Rainville and colleagues distinguish measured sensory and affective dimensions; Becker and colleagues measure acceptance alongside a combined pain/unpleasantness rating. The latter cannot isolate pure valence. These findings constrain proxies for the patient-ground; they establish neither C1 nor C2. See the [Rainville abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10467921/) and [Becker original article](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/72139/1/s41598-017-08454-4.pdf), with precise access limits in the study note.

The resulting identification requirement is concrete: record whether an intervention changes the actual experiential whole, its evidence, the subject's larger chosen package, or only an observer's response. Accepting an episode for another benefit does not by definition erase its local burden; changing one's appraisal can also genuinely change the experience. Neither possibility can be assumed merely from observed choice. Organizational success, reduced complaint and helpful-looking behavior therefore remain insufficient substitutes for identifying the proposed patient condition.

### The conditional connection to concern and wisdom

If realization, C1, C2 and the local application are warranted, the reason is grounded before its recognition. Acquired concern can then be assessed as an orientation toward an already significant patient condition. Such concern can be accurate, mistaken, absent or suppressed; this does not require every form of concern to be learned. A warranted means correction changes the proposed intervention; actual fulfillment ends this episode's current relief application; an unexplained change in the helper's learned preference does not redefine what happened to the patient.

These are inherited conditional consequences, now assigned to explicit parts of the candidate. Wisdom further requires adequate evidence, permission, consideration of other affected centers, effective means and warranted revision. The candidate supplies no complete priority rule or command to maximize relief. The actor's own goods remain among the goods to assess. Removing a subject cannot silently be entered as improvement of that same subject, and eliminating the patient's report cannot substitute for relief. Rest, restraint and relinquishing control remain possible warranted responses under the appropriate grounds.

The common-root ambition is thus specific: the realized patient condition and the action relation would themselves constitute the ethical ground, with learning explaining its recognition and use. We have not yet derived a universal experience-realization law, proved the constitutive identities, or shown that every adequate rival must preserve their joint content. Their present status as defended hypotheses is compatible with further unified grounding; it does not establish ultimate irreducibility.

## What choosing to care can create

The [elective-concern argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ELECTIVE-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md) now tests the alleged source of the gate, rather than inferring everything from the patient's remaining unchanged. Four effects of choice need different explanations:

| Effect | What may acquire a new ground |
|---|---|
| Selecting among already worthwhile possibilities | A settled plan; selection need not create the merits of the options |
| Internally committing to a project | A genuinely new practical reason, if the proposed normative power is successful |
| Forming a shared practice or relationship | New goods, vulnerabilities and responsibilities; the complete situation has changed |
| Making a valid undertaking | A directed claim with its own authority, history and release conditions |

The unified account should allow genuine reason creation to be investigated. It cannot require every successful exercise of agency to be mere recognition of a fully predetermined reason. Nor does being able to assess an exercise by reasons show that those reasons must guide every detail of the willing. A theory still owes an account of why the alleged power exists and when it works. That theoretical obligation does not decide in advance whether the exercise itself grounds a reason or merely activates an antecedent norm.

The [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ELECTIVE-CONCERN-SOURCES.md) makes this stronger possibility concrete. Chang's 2013 hybrid account combines given and will-based reasons; her 2020 account defends willing as a ground. Neither establishes that all patient-directed moral favor requires adoption. The source's general practical reasons must not silently become moral claims or permissions. Its difficult cases also prevent us from assuming that creating a contributory reason necessarily makes an option ethically acceptable. See [the 2013 chapter](https://www.ruthchang.net/s/Chang_CommitmentsReasonstheWill.pdf) and [the 2020 paper](https://www.ruthchang.net/s/Do-We-Have-Normative-Powers-offprint.pdf), with bounded access recorded in the comparison.

### The additional claim required for an exclusive gate

Establishing that commitment creates one reason concerning relief establishes neither that the same reason existed earlier nor that no differently grounded reason existed earlier. The exclusive rival needs a further claim: **every positive assistance consideration that could morally favor this actor's response must derive from an authored connection.** A reason-creating power and its legitimate source do not alone establish that all-encompassing scope.

This is the round's limited inferential result. It defeats an argument from some genuinely created reasons to the necessity of election for all assistance reasons. It does not establish a patient-ground by eliminating a competitor. The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ELECTIVE-CONCERN-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves a serious restricted rival: it accepts unchosen self-regarding, noninterference and role reasons, while reserving positive assistance to an authored connection. Showing that some reasons are given does not refute that position. The stranger-relief instance still needs its own argument.

Our positive judgment continues to favor C2. Actual relief can make a response worth choosing for the patient's sake before a new commitment exists. Self-authorship can support selection among worthwhile lives, particular commitments and legitimate discretion. Its importance does not by itself show that even contributory moral favor from an unchosen patient's burden destroys that authorship. A defense of protected decisions, limited duties or refusal rights therefore needs a further step before it becomes a defense of zero moral relevance. This is comparative support for the baseline, with the rival's narrower scope claim still open to substantive defense.

The [separate rescue study](UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md#from-moral-favor-to-a-bounded-rescue-requirement) assumes a moral contribution and argues toward a limited duty through further grounds. Its conclusion is not evidence for the baseline from which it starts. Likewise, our source comparison supports neither a general duty to adopt care nor a complete permission to decline it.

### A generative account can explain recognition and creation together

The resulting candidate has room for existing patient stakes, formed agents and historically created commitments. A new undertaking can produce a reason that was absent before; a new relationship can create something its participants now have reason to preserve. Those possibilities make agency part of the explanation without assigning every patient's significance to whichever end an actor currently selects.

This is a proposed common architecture, not a license simply to add every reason together. Establishing a new prior ground can change whether a purported normative power satisfies its conditions of successful exercise. Ground interaction, priority and exclusion need their own account. The relevant comparisons must therefore record patient contribution, created commitment and independently arising responsibility separately, including what validly ends each. Observed endorsement or increased helping identifies none of those normative sources by itself.

The counterreview adds a specific identification limit: if intentional helping is itself used to identify the alleged elective condition, selecting successful helpful actions also selects that condition. Their success and subsequent correction cannot then establish whether moral favor preceded adoption. The power needs independent characterization, and the available option needs prospective assessment before its exercise. This constrains the evidence; it does not settle the disputed moral ground.

The deeper task remains to ground both patient significance and legitimate creative powers within the generative account. A single explanation can have several reason-producing relations without being reduced to one source at the level of each particular reason. Whether that explanation is ultimately sufficient and necessary remains to be established.

## Relief within an already chosen project

The [assistance-jurisdiction argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-ARGUMENT.md) adds a comparison within the actor's existing purpose. An actor has independently chosen a legitimate project and is already informed and selecting its implementation. Two routes achieve that project equally well, including its relevant expressive character, complete-history costs, risks, commitments and opportunities. Both use otherwise permitted means. One also relieves an independently occurring severe, still-unwanted experiential burden; the other leaves it unchanged. The intervention has warranted patient permission and actual efficacy. No helping end, promise or responsibility is supplied. The comparison does not stipulate whether declining relief is ultimately permissible.

**Our affirmative judgment is that the actual relief can positively justify choosing the beneficial route for the patient's sake before selection.** The project need not be replaced by a new helping project. Equal costs do not produce the reason: the patient's actual improvement is the proposed ground. If the intervention provides no relief, that particular justification disappears. A favorable accident, a reward for the actor and suppression of the patient's report would not supply the same ground.

Choice of means still expresses authorship. The comparison therefore tests a specific inference: protecting an authored purpose against displacement does not by itself justify excluding beneficial consequences available within its implementation. A rival may defend wider jurisdiction over how a purpose is carried out, including which considerations can justify its implementation. That stronger claim remains open. We cannot establish its falsity merely by stipulating away ordinary costs, and we cannot infer prior concern from a helpful choice whose justification is the disputed question.

### Protection can affect justification itself

The [new primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-SOURCES.md) strengthens the objection. [Adams](https://www.npadams.prof/s/Adams-In-Defense-of-Exclusionary-Reasons-Penultimate.pdf) defends choice-specific justificatory exclusion; [White](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.northwestern.edu/dist/5/764/files/2016/10/Responsibility-and-the-Demands-of-Morality-JMP-final-2016-2kmy4j2.pdf) treats answerability itself as a possible burden. Neither establishes blanket exclusion of stranger relief.

Our account must distinguish four effects of protection:

| Effect | Consequence for the disputed ground |
|---|---|
| Permission to decline or a limit on inquiry | Helping can remain justified without being required or continually investigated |
| Exclusion from justifying a particular kind of choice | An otherwise significant consideration may not currently count for that choice; this is more than limited attention |
| Valid termination of a particular commitment | That created ground can end while other grounds remain to be assessed |
| A universal condition requiring adoption before any practical favor | This is the stronger barrier to C2 and still needs its own justification |

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-COUNTERREVIEW.md) accordingly preserves a genuine possible revision. A warranted choice-specific disqualification could defeat the proposed justification in one implementation even when the means are permitted and the benefit real. An account barring every uncommitted actor from ever choosing for the patient consideration would challenge C2's intended practical content. Retaining the name of a reason would not meet that challenge. Conversely, permission to refuse, protection from constant scrutiny and lack of responsibility for the original burden do not automatically establish either kind of exclusion.

Eligibility must not be defined as “a case in which our preferred reason survives.” Identify the relevant act, history, role or practice independently, then justify any claimed power to disqualify this ground. Assess whether the actual choice falls within its scope and whether the grounds for maintaining that scope hold. Changing the description to “my private choice” or “a rescue” cannot settle the matter. The clean project comparison supplies no established special procedure, but it leaves the general authorship objection available for argument.

### Test exclusion against the actual response

The [new fair-allocation comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PATIENT-GROUND-AFTER-FAIR-ALLOCATION.md) distinguishes taking a winner's relief resource, reopening a completed fair draw, and using a fresh independently permissible opportunity to relieve the person who lost. The last act need not undo either the procedure or its result. The affirmative explanation is the change in continuing adversity; a completed chance claim has a different satisfaction condition. A genuine restriction on the original allocation is not automatically a restriction on this new response. This is a positive local defense of patient-grounded favor, with legitimate wider exclusions still open to argument.

[The direct-justification study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DIRECT-GROUNDING-EPISTEMOLOGY.md) examines how a particular assessment could be warranted without a separately believed general bridge. It neither certifies this assessment nor removes the constitutive and universal grounding obligations. Recognition can be investigated within a formed history; its reliability cannot be established by scoring agreement with C2. The earlier invitation case additionally needs warranted evidence about the formable continuation rather than treating anticipated relief as already given.

The [completed local desert comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PERMITTED-RELIEF-AND-DESERT-INTEGRATION.md) now studies the particular adverse episode claimed as deserved, its history and a permitted relieving continuation together. The affirmative assessment concerns what that response accomplishes for the actual sufferer; the rival claims that desert changes or excludes this patient-directed significance. Neither is settled by permission alone. The study grants exact desert and permission locally and does not yet establish their coexistence in a realized case.

Its source comparison also corrects the earlier binary framing. A suffering component could retain bad-making significance alongside a desert consideration; context could change that component's moral value while remission has value; or patient-directed favor could be genuinely excluded. Valuable remission grounded only in a compassionate motive or special relationship would not establish a reason preceding that motive or relation. A negative desert ranking does not by itself decide among these accounts. These remain substantive tests within the differentiated configuration, not a claim that independently variable formal predicates disprove its proposed unity. The governing nondual formation study below retains priority.

### A practical boundary within the generative account

The developing [nondual reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-GROUNDING-QUESTION.md) permits actor, patient and project to name differentiated continuities within one reality. Earlier operational distinctions did not require substantial owners. A formed boundary can protect agency, coordination or sustained activity; its formation alone proves neither authority to exclude reasons nor absence of such authority.

The positive proposal therefore connects actual undergoing, an available transformation and the continuing activity within which it can occur. It seeks to explain both the significance of relief and the legitimate organization of response. The current comparison supports a prospective patient justification without a new helping purpose. The further account of choice-specific protection is a substantive grounding obligation, not an optional label or a derivation already furnished by shared origin.

## The next decisive connection

The next provisional study concerns **co-formation and consequential revision of practical boundaries, significance and response within the same history**. This selects a concrete inquiry, not a mandatory linear grammar for all generativity. Its distinctions may form together and must remain revisable. Core and tree are investigated together. The activity whose organization is being explained includes its own capacities to undergo, distinguish, evaluate and respond; these are not automatically independent additions because a sparse model initially omitted them.

Select a concrete formation already under study and reconstruct these relations together, identifying where existing work actually connects and where it only supplies compatible interfaces. Follow what sustains the boundary, what it registers or excludes, how consequences return, and whether its terms of attention and response can change. Preserve different affected continuities and the possible failure to register them. A supplied benevolent selector, a fixed neutral evaluator, or mere success at persistence cannot stand in for the explanation.

Earlier patient-ground and jurisdiction questions remain local tests for developed ethical claims. They no longer determine this branch's governing research order. Formal entailment and premise tracking retain their proper scopes: a result about a representation must not automatically become a verdict about the reality whose richer unfolding the representation leaves out. The actual generative account still owes its laws, experiential identification, distinctions and claimed directions; the no-assumptions requirement is not relaxed or declared complete.

Fix the proposed connection and its scope before comparison. A collection of disconnected histories cannot establish that the claimed relations formed together. A changed proxy or report without the claimed experiential change requires revising that identification. Improved internal regulation with worsened affected continuities requires revising any claim that it established ethical improvement. These are possible revision conditions, not outcomes declared in advance; the relevant evidence and rival explanations still need investigation.
