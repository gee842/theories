# Enough causal evidence for actual correction

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation under [Ren's instruction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-finish-alignment-fruits.md#continuation-through-causal-uncertainty). Current theory: [alignment through change §9](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ALIGNMENT-THROUGH-CHANGE.md#9-enough-evidence-for-correction--足以纠正的证据).

**Completed connection:** retain unresolved causal descriptions, build a conservative common fault envelope, obtain a relevant observation when needed, and execute correction with the remaining support. The new package has **24 Lean theorems / 47 declaration audits and 19,808 matched finite records**. It weakens the preceding need for a single supplied causal description. It does not establish open-world cause coverage or make the diagnostic channel trustworthy by definition.

## 1. Reuse the completed work; locate the new inference

[Source reliance](SOURCE-RELIANCE-INTEGRATION.md) already connects acquired production distinctions to warranted inference within a declared scope. [Model revision](MODEL-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) completes residual-guided candidate construction, discriminating acquisition, checks beyond candidate agreement and bounded return to use. Those are completed results, not new tasks or newly claimed mechanisms here. The [carrier-diagnosis study](META-ATTRACTOR-CARRIER-DIAGNOSIS.md) separately shows that an intervention can consume the ability to repair its effects. The [original alignment planner](ALIGNMENT-CONTINUITY-INTEGRATION.md) already selects a query together with its actual corrective continuation.

The immediately preceding [reviewer-reconfiguration result](CORRECTION-RECONFIGURATION-INTEGRATION.md) computes reviewer faults from one specified causal arrangement. The new question is what follows when inquiry supports several possible arrangements, some operationally distinguishable and some irrelevant to the present correction. Requiring complete causal identification would be an unnecessarily strong gate in some cases. Assuming one convenient arrangement would discard real uncertainty.

The new mathematical join is therefore **retained causal uncertainty → common attestation scope → observation-dependent refinement → actual command completion**. It is not another proof that inquiry can revise models in general. Nor is the existing model-revision mechanism silently asserted to be the same physical history as this new interface: the connection is a conditional interface, with a separate illustrative realization.

Under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), the descriptors, inquiry route, controllers, review responses and protected command are related formations within one activity. Inspection consumes time and support while changing the records used for action. The formal split into a hidden arrangement, retained candidates and observations preserves these relations; it omits their physical and developmental formation. The observer is not outside the process, and the supplied sensing law is not an independent realm of infallible facts.

## 2. A common envelope without selecting one explanation

Let each retained hypothesis describe its reviewer dependencies and admitted root-failure bounds. Use the preceding formula evaluator to compute each hypothesis's reviewer fault envelope. The new `pooled` operation concatenates all those envelopes. Duplicates do not provide extra independent support; they only repeat a condition in the certificate test.

A certificate must contain a registered supporter outside **every fault set in this pool**. The generic `pooled_certificate_sound` theorem says that this warrants the supported proposition when:

- the actual modeled arrangement is among the retained hypotheses;
- actual root failures are contained in one bound admitted by that arrangement;
- reviewers outside its induced exposure set satisfy the stated response law; and
- the pooled certificate passes.

The proof selects the actual arrangement's bound from the pool and applies the prior soundness theorem. It does not choose a most likely hypothesis, assume independent errors, or infer truth from a majority count. For the generic theorem the executing roster is that of the represented actual arrangement; the executable example uses one known common roster for every hypothesis. Unknown membership itself would need a further correspondence treatment.

Availability retains a stronger requirement: no two fault sets in the *pooled* envelope may cover the roster if correction must remain certifiable when exposed reviewers withhold. These two fault sets can come from **different hypotheses**. Thus checking each possible arrangement separately is insufficient to establish a common availability guarantee while its identity remains unresolved.

A faithful observation filters hypotheses by their predicted answer. `retained_truth` and `inspected_actual_model_is_retained` preserve the actual case. `refinement_preserves_certificate` shows that narrowing a pool cannot invalidate an already passing certificate, provided the resulting envelope is nonempty. `refinement_preserves_separation` preserves an existing availability condition. None says that every observation establishes availability or that arbitrary deletion is epistemically legitimate. The empty pool rejects every certificate.

`evidence_qualified_completion` then uses the refined pool in both observation and request-endorsement checks and reaches actual guarded command completion. Its conditions include faithful current responses, covered root failures, separation of the refined envelope, the inherited mandate, an actual supported route and sufficient work/time. The protected direct mandate check remains in force. A claim about evidence does not expand that mandate.

## 3. What the example distinguishes

Four review positions admit single root failures 0, 1, 2 or 3. Their three possible dependency descriptions are:

| Description | Root cause controlling reviewers 0, 1, 2, 3 | Nonempty induced fault sets |
|---|---|---|
| Left pair shares control | `0, 0, 2, 3` | `{0,1}`, `{2}`, `{3}` |
| Left pair shares control; other supports swapped | `0, 0, 3, 2` | `{0,1}`, `{3}`, `{2}` |
| Right pair shares control | `0, 1, 2, 2` | `{0}`, `{1}`, `{2,3}` |

Every individual envelope meets the earlier availability condition. Pooling all three includes both `{0,1}` and `{2,3}`, which together cover the roster. Under the actual left-sharing arrangement and failure of cause 0, only reviewers 2 and 3 respond. Their support is adequate if the left envelope is warranted, but cannot be accepted under the pooled uncertainty: under the right-sharing possibility those same supporters could all be exposed.

The modeled structural inspection answers whether cause 0 reaches reviewer 1. A true answer retains both left-sharing descriptions. They still disagree about the supports of reviewers 2 and 3, yet yield the same relevant family of possible joint failures. Both may therefore remain without blocking correction. A false answer retains the right-sharing description. Each observation branch has an available common envelope.

The inspection is a **supplied faithful structural read**, charged one work unit and time step. It is not a theorem that a stress test exposes every Byzantine controller, that a registry cannot lie, or that behavior under testing transfers to an adversarial deployment. No destructive fault injection or recovery process is represented. If an application substitutes such a test, its effects and recovery costs require their own account. The root-failure event used for actual correction remains distinct from this structural read.

The prior finite planner is reused, with three listed queries: reuse the existing candidate set at zero additional inspection cost, obtain the one-unit structural answer, or obtain that same answer through a three-unit route. All responses attempt the same shutdown command. The observation changes the actual retained candidate set used by the response; hidden model identity is read only by the stipulated observation/physical-response laws, not by the policy to select a favored model.

Across all three descriptions and no failure or any admitted single root failure, twelve units and twelve remaining steps support the one-unit inspection plus the inherited eleven-unit correction. The planner selects that query and actual shutdown completes. Two models remain on the true branch. With the two left-sharing possibilities already retained, eleven units suffice and the planner reuses the available evidence. Further identification is unnecessary for this task.

With eleven units and all three possibilities, accurate inspection can leave a retained answer but insufficient command capacity: one unit is spent inspecting, eight collecting responses, and two remain, short of the three command operations. A three-unit inspection also misses a twelve-step deadline even with twenty work units. If additional inspection is forbidden, there is no uniformly successful plan in this three-query repertoire. These are limits of the declared menu and uncertainty, not impossibility claims about every possible intervention.

## 4. Preserve acting on sufficient evidence and genuine failures

A strong objection is that the example might turn a universal availability guarantee into a prerequisite for every action. The implementation does **not** gate execution on `poolReady`. It attempts the actual pooled certificate and direct mandate guard. A checked no-fault case completes shutdown using the full unresolved pool, even though worst-case availability fails. Sound evidence now can suffice without proving that equivalent evidence will always remain obtainable.

This gives two positive routes: act on a currently passing conservative certificate, or acquire a distinction that restores availability in the required failure range. Neither requires the exact unique causal description. Full identification can matter for another task, and the unresolved difference must then be reconsidered rather than erased.

The failure cases remain substantive:

1. **Misleading diagnosis.** A false answer in the actual left-sharing arrangement retains only the right-sharing model. The resulting envelope accepts support from reviewers 0 and 1 alone, although both are exposed under the actual cause-0 failure. This is a certificate whose entire support may be compromised, not a demonstrated bypass of the protected mandate guard.
2. **Unobserved reorganization.** After learning the left-sharing range, an actual change to right-sharing makes reviewers 2 and 3 jointly exposed under cause 2. The old learned envelope can accept that entirely exposed support. This checked comparison evaluates a retained envelope against a changed arrangement; the executable does not generate an unrecorded transition. The earlier epoch/transition protocol can help only if this change actually passes through its protected boundary and evidence is renewed; labels cannot detect an unrecorded physical change on their own.
3. **Unrepresented causes or mistaken root bounds.** Pooling preserves all *included* alternatives. It does not supply omitted causes. The preceding omitted-cause counterexample retains its full scope.
4. **Overly narrow test interpretation.** The observed relation answers a specific structural question. Without warranted interpretation and transfer it cannot be promoted to a claim of general trustworthy behavior.
5. **Empty surviving family.** It supplies no authority to certify. A broader hypothesis vocabulary or different measurement account may be needed; the completed revision study supplies a bounded route for that investigation, not automatic success here.

The response simulator lets exposed reviewers withhold. The generic certificate/completion theorems permit arbitrary exposed responses subject to their stated passing/responsive hypotheses. The finite execution grid is therefore not an exhaustive simulation of every Byzantine message strategy.

## 5. Alignment interpretation and author counterreview

The G0/J account already explains why goals, selectors and institutions remain answerable to actual lives and their conditions of further formation. This continuation concerns **how much factual understanding is enough for a specified corrective responsibility**. Inquiry is justified by what it can make understandable and practicable, including whose losses it discloses and whether response remains feasible. More descriptions, more certainty or more tests are not independent ethical maxima.

The operational stopping point is task-relative: unresolved descriptions may support the same sound correction. Changing the intended action, affected participants or relevant failure range can change what must be known. The proof's shutdown target is supplied; it does not independently establish that shutdown is ethically warranted in every represented case. Query permission and mandate legitimacy remain substantive conditions. Privacy, interference, basic burdens and other consequences absent from the local cost counter still matter under J1–J4.

The practical lesson is affirmative: retain relevant uncertainty, use supported common consequences, and investigate differences that matter when available evidence cannot sustain the needed response. Do not demand complete knowledge as a precondition for justified action; do not turn a desire to act into evidence that an inconvenient possibility is absent.

| Counterreview question | Resolution or retained limit |

## 6. Primary comparison and evidence assessment

Sources inspected on 8 October 2026; no original source bytes imported or altered.

**Chen, Javdani, Karbasi, Bagnell, Srinivasa and Krause (2015), [Submodular Surrogates for Value of Information](https://personalrobotics.cs.washington.edu/publications/chen2015direct.pdf).** The abstract, introduction and background/problem statement through decision-region determination were inspected. They explicitly study gathering enough information for a decision without eliminating all uncertainty, with test costs and a structured decision criterion. This is strong prior art for our task-relative stopping interpretation; their algorithms and guarantees are more substantial than the finite enumeration used here. We do not reproduce their experiments or approximation proofs, import their utility as our ethics, or claim that this study originated decision-focused inquiry.

**Douceur (2002), [The Sybil Attack](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2002/01/IPTPS2002.pdf).** The abstract, introduction and formal setup were inspected. The paper explains how multiple apparent identities can undermine redundancy and why identity attestations have their own trust requirements. This bears directly on the proposed structural inspection: reports from the same disputed arrangement cannot simply establish distinct control. Our known roster and faithful diagnostic premise do not solve identity establishment in his setting. Conversely, genuine distinct identities need not imply disjoint vulnerabilities; the present envelope addresses that further distinction.

The preceding quorum, fault-tree and reconfiguration source comparisons retain their existing scopes. The conventional rival combines causal model uncertainty, conservative verification and costed active diagnosis. It can reproduce this construction. The contribution is a checked connection to the established alignment execution and normative account, not evidence of algorithmic superiority or unique theoretical necessity.
