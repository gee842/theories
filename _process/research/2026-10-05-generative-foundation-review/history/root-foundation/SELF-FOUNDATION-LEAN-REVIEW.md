# Review of generated-self and ethical-ground integration

6 October 2026. AI-authored independent source and entailment review. The governing task is to explain the generated self and non-subjective ethical significance within the generative account while honoring [Ren's no-assumptions directive](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-no-assumptions-directive.md). This review does not replace that requirement with the weaker requirement of no custom Lean axioms. It preserves the [self-formation acceptance criteria](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATED-SELF-AND-ETHICS.md#self-formation-as-a-requirement-of-this-foundation) and the [governing completion conditions](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#completion-of-the-unified-foundation).

**Named connections:** interacting organization → acquired, operative self-representation; actual differentiated conditions → justified ethical significance. A structural reconstruction of histories can advance the first interface. A proof separating factual invariance from normative interpretation can advance the second. Neither alone completes either connection.

## 1. The four standards are different

| Standard | Acceptance evidence | What passing it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| No new declared axioms | Inspect all new source and imports; reject `axiom`, unchecked declarations and admitted proof terms. | A theorem may still assume its desired conclusion in an argument or a structure field. |
| No new theorem axiom dependencies | Compile frozen source in a fresh directory, print dependencies for every new theorem, require empty lists, and preserve the exact old-source baseline for any inherited dependencies. | A closed conditional proof establishes its implication, not its antecedents or its interpretation. |
| No additional explanatory premise hidden in definitions or domains | Inspect theorem types, definitions, quantified classes and claimed interpretations together. A general invariant may use new definitions; a chosen learning law or a predicate named `good` cannot explain its own applicability merely by being defined. | Valid conservative mathematical extensions do not identify real organizations, selves, experience or moral significance. |
| The requested real-world foundation completed | Connect actual formation, retention and causal use of a self-model to warranted experiential and ethical identification, compatible composition, failure regimes and discriminating support. Trace rather than relocate every remaining explanatory dependency. | A successful local model, an invariant relation or an empty axiom report is insufficient. |

The first three tests must be applied to each advertised conclusion. Introducing a mathematical definition is not automatically an illicit assumption: one can prove a structural theorem about *all* processes without restricting their laws. The illicit step would be to advertise that defined structure as a realized self or moral ground without deriving or justifying that identification. Literal freedom from every presupposition, including the formal framework, has not been established.

The preserved original build contains `Quot.sound` and `propext` dependencies in audited declarations of `Depletion` and `RootRealization`. They remain inherited dependencies, not new additions in this round. An accurate formal claim is therefore that the new results have empty dependency lists and add no dependency to the audited model; the complete historical model is not reported as axiom-free.

## 2. What the inherited model actually supplies

[GenerativeCore](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeCore.lean) accepts a state type, an action type and a relation `step : State → Action → State → Prop`. Its `Trace` retains actual intermediate states. Exact path composition, synchronous conjunction and faithful representation are genuine consequences of this interface. The code explicitly states that observation, learning, significance and favorable direction are not entailed.

[GenerativeModel](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeModel.lean) currently integrates finite-history realization and the Boolean dynamics classification with that same core. Universal statements over a supplied process can be unconditional *structural* theorems; they still do not derive which law or history is actual. The existing law/history equivalence appropriately preserves endpoints. Static and toggle dynamics have the same feasible action words, so word feasibility alone can erase a consequential distinction in their endpoints.

The [history realization](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/HistoryRealization.lean) gives an exact representation of a supplied prefix-closed language. The complete history is mathematical state in that construction. No physical memory acquisition or paid maintenance follows from it. Likewise, the [least reachability closure](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/GeneratedPaths.lean) adds no primitive transition. With no steps, it gives only equality.

The [patient implementation](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/noninstrumental-concern/PatientConcern.lean) adds two named patients, episodes, positive quantities, commands, faithful capture, permission flags and paid operations. It is a legitimate local law. The code explicitly leaves its experiential and welfare interpretation to an independent application premise. Its fields are not generated by the default root merely because its process can be represented there.

The [grounding contract](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/noninstrumental-concern/GroundingContract.lean) distinguishes two interfaces. `C1` connects realized positive burden to badness; `C2` connects badness and eligibility to merit. `grounded_eligible_merit` takes both as hypotheses. `candidate_satisfies_c2` succeeds because candidate merit is defined as badness conjoined with eligibility. That is a witness to contract consistency, not a derivation of ethical truth. Its explicit `zeroMerit` construction already shows that the formal positive-quantity interpretation of C1 does not entail C2.

## 3. The strongest legitimate route from the present root

**A. Recover consequential organization from the supplied relation.** Derived relations may identify which states or histories have different continuations and what any exact representation must retain. These can be useful canonical structures rather than new imposed dynamics. Their claimed sufficiency must match the observation contract: preserving feasible words is weaker than preserving actual endpoints, branching, control, probabilities or affected centers.

Exact preservation of *all* future action words is also stronger than many useful self-models need. The necessity result is conditional on that representation contract. A finite, approximate or task-specific self-model may deliberately omit other distinctions. Failure to recover a changing coordinate does not by itself refute a stable representation of the continuing organization.

**B. Identify a precise self-formation interface.** A usable self-model must be acquired inside an actual history, retained in the process and causally alter subsequent action. It should represent something about the continuing organization and its relation to surrounding conditions. Correct own/world labels, a preinstalled observer, a preferred reward and an unexplained decoder cannot count as the derivation. A model of controllability can include tools or other bodies, so controllability alone is insufficient for bodily ownership. The appropriate next positive result would derive the relevant representation from independently specified interaction and retention mechanisms, while distinguishing the local discharge from whatever upstream law still supplies those mechanisms.

**C. Derive factual comparisons without prematurely calling them moral.** A consequence relation may order actual continuations or losses without reference to an observer's approval. Such an order is an objective structural fact of the chosen process. Capability preservation can help specify what a response changes. It does not yet settle which capabilities ought to be preserved or how conflicts between centers should be assessed: exploitative capacities also have continuations.

**D. Test the occurrence-to-reason ground at its actual interface.** The positive ethical proposal concerns actual severe unwanted adversity and effective permissible relief. Ownership descriptions and prior helping attitudes need not occur in that proposed ground. Its remaining burden is to justify why this occurrence and response constitute the claimed favor, rather than define a predicate that says so. The comparison must retain real continuity, consent, efficacy, complete costs and competing claims. Changes to those conditions may warrant changed judgments even when private endorsement is irrelevant.

These steps do not announce universal impossibility. Richer justified accounts may explain organization, representational formation or constitutive ethical significance. The present task can close exact structural interfaces and eliminate particular false entailments while retaining those constructive possibilities. Completing a stronger explanation requires the missing content to be supplied by derivation and justification, rather than by a renamed field.

## 4. Counterexamples that any completion claim must survive

| Proposed shortcut | Existing or immediate control | Exact lesson |
|---|---|---|
| A state/action presentation entails an acquired self. | The core permits an empty state type; a nonempty singleton with no events also has no nonempty history. | No universal nontrivial formation conclusion follows from this interface alone. A regime account may legitimately include absence of selves. |
| Distinction, totality, determinism, reversibility and symmetric labels determine transformation. | The checked static/toggle pair has these properties but different endpoint histories. | These particular grounds do not determine change. This does not reject richer generative grounds. |
| Same feasible words imply the same causal organization. | Static and toggle have all the same feasible words but different one-step endpoints. | A word-based representation needs an explicit sufficiency boundary. |
| Recurrence entails revised selectors or acquired self-knowledge. | The inherited fixed-selector witness changes phase indefinitely without revising its causally operative selector. | Recurrence alone does not supply learning. |
| An invariant choice is therefore the ethical choice. | Always-empty merit is invariant; opposed relations can share the same causal reduct when the normative relation is unconstrained. | Invariance is necessary for a specified irrelevance claim, not sufficient to select its ethical content. |
| Accurate relational self-understanding necessarily produces care. | A relational predator accurately models itself and its victims while exploiting them. | Self-understanding does not guarantee benevolent conduct; this does not disprove a reason the predator ignores. |
| More continuation is always good, or another center's continuation repairs this center's loss. | Exploitative organizations can persist; separate centers may compete for one resource; replacement need not relieve an existing burden. | Factual direction, moral evaluation and same-center continuity must remain distinct. |

The invariance and expansion tests require narrow interpretation. Failure of a globally distinguished, unpointed self-label under a symmetry does not exclude a local history-relative self. An actual organization or retained history may distinguish a position. Likewise, opposed formal ethical expansions establish underdetermination in the current signature; they do not establish that both are equally correct interpretations of reality or refute every possible constitutive ethical ground.

The empty/idle controls test only an unrestricted entailment from the sparse interface. They do **not** refute the intended regime-specific foundation, whose job includes explaining why selves form in some conditions and fail to form in others. Nor do they preclude joining the repository's existing concrete learning mechanisms to a carefully specified self-representation task. That route must identify which inherited mechanism supplies each required operation and what remains unexplained upstream.

## 5. Independent review of this round's modules

**GeneratedSelf: source review accepted.** The 23 theorem statements in [GeneratedSelf.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GeneratedSelf.lean) derive history-conditioned feasibility, its exact boundary-state decomposition, compatible updating and a necessary-and-sufficient condition for exact summaries. The local uniqueness, exactness and projection hypotheses are visibly conditional; no claim asserts them for every process. The existentially constructed interpreter need not be computable or implemented. The existing total toggle law supplies the exact current-state decoding counterexample and the limited unpointed-center obstruction.

Two requested comment refinements were applied: exact preservation is not required of every usable self-model, and the decoder counterexample does not require all self-models to recover every changing coordinate. The accompanying [mechanism note](SELF-LEAN-MECHANISM.md) correctly connects existing paid corrective-memory results as complementary local mechanisms without claiming that their decoder or own/world partition has already been derived.

**EthicalOrientation: source review accepted.** The 17 theorem statements in [EthicalOrientation.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/EthicalOrientation.lean) include a general factual continuation preorder, its synchronous-product preservation and its exact-projection contract. The closed comparison then uses two histories of the unchanged toggle process: no events and a two-event cycle, both from `false` back to `false`, with matching future-feasibility profiles. Ordering by event count and reversing that order yield opposed coherent, annotation-invariant comparisons. Both also ignore action renaming as comparisons; the code correctly says that such renaming need not preserve process admissibility.

This establishes the insufficiency of the specified coherence/invariance conditions to select the growth direction. It does not make history count a proposed ethical measure, make the annotation a generated self, or identify both orders as equally defensible ethical interpretations. The final cycle result rules out a transitive irreflexive *state* comparison strictly increased by every toggle event. It does not rule out history-based progress, non-strict comparisons, favorable regimes or moral improvement in another process.

An earlier development attempted to specialize factual continuation inclusion to the old renewal/terminal realization. The agent's dependency audit found that this would inherit `propext` through that old realization's definitions. It was excluded from the new empty-dependency theorem set without changing the historical law. This was a formal dependency-policy failure, not a counterexample to the old renewal result. The accepted module retains generic continuation facts and uses already existing constructive Boolean laws for its closed witnesses.

**Default entry point: source review accepted.** Three added theorems in [GenerativeModel](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeModel.lean) expose the exact-summary criterion, join actual changed endpoints with the profile-decoder obstruction, and connect the same history profile to orientation underdetermination. The final connection is a genuine shared mathematical interface: `SameProfile` unfolds to the residual proposition already proved in the ethical module. It does not compose physical self-learning with ethical realization. The five previous entry-point theorem statements and bodies are retained.

## 7. Acceptance decision and next connection

**Accept the formal integration at its stated structural scope. Do not accept a claim that the requested generated-self and non-subjective good–evil foundation is complete.** The new modules add no primitive process law, reward, patient-good interpretation, C1/C2 ground or declared axiom. Their conditional contracts remain conditional. No hidden own/world classifier is used to claim self acquisition; no mathematical order is called morally justified. This meets the local source and theorem-dependency standards while honestly retaining the stronger unresolved task.

The next positive connection is an actually acquired, retained and causally used representation of the continuing organization, using the existing concrete paid-learning mechanisms where they supply an interface and identifying all upstream recipes still required. Its adequacy can be task-specific; it need not satisfy the new exact-summary contract. In parallel, the ethical connection must justify the proposed relation between independently warranted adversity and an eligible response while preserving continuity, permission and conflicting claims. A justified constitutive ground or a genuinely derived formation mechanism would advance the account. Additional names, invariant annotations, favorable orders or a fresh axiom report alone would not.
