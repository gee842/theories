## The connection advanced

The [preceding study](META-ATTRACTOR-TESTING.md) acquired dependency records by faithful structural inspection, then preserved their usefulness through staged reconfiguration. This continuation weakens that particular access assumption: **delivered intervention → observed joint outage → acquired dependency partition → revised admission → physical response**. It starts with unknown dependencies, including initially shared ones. The operational gate never reads the hidden root identifiers.

This is an implementation connection for generative alignment: inquiry and correction need conditions that the selected activity can maintain, and the evidence must distinguish consequential alternatives. It does not derive G0/J from technical persistence. The existing reproduction, acquired-criterion and staged-testing results are reused. The parallel [fallible-inspection study](FALLIBLE-INSPECTION-INTEGRATION.md) already interprets contested structural reports without a designated infallible witness; it remains credited. Here the new evidence is intervention effects. We do not claim to have formally composed its fallible-report procedure with this process.

Under the nondual method, intervention, sensing, retained records, selection and material support are differentiated relations in one history. A represented root names a common causal reach, not a separate metaphysical substance. The analytical split preserves which readers a pulse or fault can affect and what testing costs; it omits embodied fabrication, the formation of sensing capacities and unmodeled upstream support. A supplied pulse law is local modeling input within the generative account, not a cause outside reality.

## Actual operations and explicit premises

[CommonCauseDiscovery.lean](../../../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/CommonCauseDiscovery.lean) imports the unchanged testing, admission and reproduction modules. Three readers support decisions about the existing four-role installation. The semantic state contains actual dependencies `roots`, initially empty effect records, any held pulse, the material plant and a revision count.

Pulsing reader `i` disables every reader sharing its represented root. Observation stores the actual outage vector, without exposing root names to the gate. Restoring removes the pulse. Each of these three operations costs one material unit. Two ordinary supported cycles precede each probe; the inherited return law finances the three-unit cost while retaining the eleven-unit operating reserve. A survey repeats this for all three positions: six ordinary cycles and nine units of probe expenditure. Its material stock finishes at least three units above the starting stock, and the plant remains supported at every permitted prefix.

These are substantive local premises:

| Premise | What it supplies; what remains unproved |
|---|---|
| Delivered, selective intervention | A handle for position `i` interrupts its represented shared support. No arbitrary physical access or collateral-damage immunity is derived. |
| Faithful outage observation | The recorded row matches the actual outage. It is not certified by the possibly faulty Boolean decision reports. |
| Stable relation during a trial | Dependence does not change silently between pulse, observation and use. Declared reconfiguration invalidates old records. |
| Transfer from pulse effects to decision faults | The same represented root partition governs the one-root report-failure law. An outage experiment alone does not establish this correspondence for every failure mode. |
| Supported fallback and supplies | The known `separate` arrangement can operate while readers are interrupted. The pulse does not also disable its material supply; inherited arrivals and parts remain supplied laws. |
| Initial support and bounded cost | Testing starts from an equipped, established installation, and each modeled event has its stated cost. This does not prove safe testing from arbitrary depletion. |

The plant's ordinary work, withdrawal and response have explicit traces under the inherited physical operation. Pulse delivery and observation are new charged events in the same `Generative.Process`; their internal engineering is not a proved refinement of an embodied sensor mechanism.

## What the acquired evidence warrants

`survey_records_all_effects` obtains the complete coupling matrix for arbitrary initial root assignments. It identifies an equivalence partition of these three readers under the represented law, not names of causes or an entire causal graph. `complete_records_identify_coupling` connects retained rows to that actual relation. No initial assumption of distinct roots is needed for the survey.

The gate requires restored operation, complete records, a separating effect matrix and two agreeing reports. `separate_effects_imply_distinct_roots` derives the required distinctness from the acquired effects. `learned_gate_sound` then reuses the earlier one-root fault result. `learned_gate_available` retains availability when the acquired effects separate the readers and unaffected readers respond. Missing or correlated records do not authorize the majority shortcut. This gate is deliberately conservative; it does not compute the most permissive certificate for every possible shared-root arrangement.

`fallback_available_during_probe` keeps a physical supported operation executable even if all readers share the pulsed root. `survey_actual` and `survey_preserves_response_capacity` join funding, interruption, recording and restoration. A configuration change charges two units, clears the records and increments the revision. `no_gate_after_change` blocks reuse of stale certification; `retesting_after_reorganization` constructs another actual paid survey from the changed configuration. Availability after reconfiguration still depends on what it discovers. A new label or a successful local pulse cannot guarantee universal independence.

## One history from discovery to correction

Compare actual dependencies `(0,1,2)` and `(0,0,2)`. Pulsing position 0 disables position 1 only in the second installation. The complete survey therefore distinguishes them through observed effects.

In the shared case, begin at the inherited supported installation, execute the funded survey, and perform one physically permitted withdrawal while all four stocks are three. This leaves all four stocks at two. Now a fault at root 0 causes the first two readers to support another withdrawal; the unaffected third reader correctly opposes it. The raw majority is wrong. The acquired coupling record blocks that majority from authorizing withdrawal, and the fallback operation restores all four stocks to three.

`shared_dependency_to_corrected_response` proves this entire sequence as one actual trace. `joint_history_preserves` covers every permitted joint history, under its report-law and resource conditions. `discovered_coupling_changes_actual_response` verifies the changed operation and restored stocks. `unaccounted_majority_would_lose_support` retains the matched failure: another withdrawal would leave stocks at one and violate establishment. The comparison does not reset the plant between discovery and response.

This adds a prospective diagnostic meaning to the meta-attractor account. Dependencies among the practices that judge and select activity can become concentrated before their current reports disagree. An intervention can expose that concentration and change selection while there is still capacity to act. Neither intervention nor withdrawal is prescribed for any actual institution by this formal example.

## The stronger rival and the wall

The counterexample has three distinct local branches in both organizations. In one, their upstream parents differ; in the other, all three depend on one parent. A local branch pulse affects only that branch in both. Lean checks identical complete local-effect matrices and identical effects for every finite list of simultaneous local pulses. A classifier supplied only that matrix cannot label the two upstream organizations differently.

Nevertheless, a failure at their actual upstream roots has different reach. The shared-parent case lets one fault corrupt all three reports, producing a wrong majority. A pulse at the upstream level would distinguish the organizations. These are explicitly different dependency structures with different operations, not truth labels freely toggled inside a weaker description.

**The wall is intervention coverage and transfer:** exhaustive results for the available local handles do not establish that every relevant common cause lies at that level. This invalidates the unconditional inference from a clean local survey to resilience against arbitrary upstream faults. It does not invalidate the conditional acquisition theorem, prove causal discovery impossible, or say the upstream test can never become available.

The next substantive connection would have to explain how accessible intervention relations expand or are otherwise supplemented, and why their results cover the failures relevant to action while maintaining observation and response capacity. Another repetition of the same local survey cannot discharge that premise. Existing fallible-inspection and reconfiguration work supplies conditional components, not this unrestricted coverage result. This is the stopping boundary of the present continuation; it is not completion of the whole meta-attractor theory or a new requirement to conduct an empirical deployment before doing theory.

| Has perfect structural knowledge merely been renamed? | Direct root inspection is removed, but selective delivery, truthful effect recording and pulse/fault transfer remain assumed. The gain is conditional acquisition through effects, not assumption-free causal knowledge. |
| Is success only refusal to act? | Soundness and availability both hold for separating surveys; the shared case executes the known fallback. General optimal permissiveness is not claimed. |

## Primary-source comparison

Sources read on 8 October 2026; no originals were imported or altered.

**Eberhardt (2008), [Almost Optimal Intervention Sets for Causal Discovery](https://www.auai.org/uai2008/UAI_camera_ready/eberhardt.pdf).** The abstract, introduction and opening model assumptions were inspected. The paper makes identification depend on the admissible causal models and intervention class; its discussion of standard bounds distinguishes causal sufficiency and idealized independence information. Our three-position partition experiment is much narrower. The useful comparison is that stronger observations do not remove the need to specify which causal alternatives and interventions they discriminate. No general causal-discovery bound or the paper's conjecture is claimed proved here.

**Ramasubramanian et al. (2017), [Growing a Protocol](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/conference/hotcloud17/hotcloud17-paper-ramasubramanian.pdf).** The abstract, introduction, sections 2–3 and opening section 4 were inspected. Their approach uses models of successful distributed executions to select failure experiments as protocols evolve. It provides direct prior art for targeted intervention and for treating resilience as a property across failure scenarios. This study gives no comparative algorithmic improvement. Its narrower contribution is joining acquired dependency effects, their cost and a consequential physical response in the existing generative history, while retaining a missed-cause counterexample.

Both comparisons limit the novelty claim and support taking intervention coverage seriously. Neither establishes the empirical correspondence of our pulse law, the theory's exclusive necessity, or universal ethical convergence.
