## 0. 问题 / The question

**中文。** Ren 的说法：称之为“哺乳动物的”，是因为寻求系统被锁定了：本性的一部分被后天所养成的东西劫持；人们所追逐的，按南亚传统与佛教的说法，是幻象。Ren 要求记下这一点，找出无明的边界条件，并加以证明。Ren 不完全同意“支配总是相对位置的度量，因而总是零和，永远不够”：在秩序无法达成一致时，可以由明白的人来掌握权力；支配也可以受到规范。但在西方，它几乎是“神”，处处被当作真理，连理性也成了自利。

AI 的读法（待 Ren）：
- **寻求**是以一个已形成的目标为方向的活动。目标在展开之中形成（遗传、学习、叙事、元吸引子的选择），不是从外给定的效用。“被养成所劫持”就是目标的这种形成。
- **追逐幻象**有两种读法：目标在静止时不能被满足（永远不够）；或者满足目标并不维持寻求者实际所依存的（追逐一个其边界不是实际依存的自我的扩大）。
- **边界条件**是精确的界线：一边是能在“够了”处静止的寻求（明），另一边是不能静止的寻求；以及这种幻象在何时被动力学纠正、在何时持续下去。
- **无明**读作使寻求锁定在这种目标上的不见。Ren 把无明作为边界条件所针对的对象；把它等同于这种不见，是 AI 的读法。
- **支配**本身不是幻象。Ren 批评的是争当“支配一切的最大者”，以及支配在西方被奉为“神”；受规范的支配，以及在秩序无法达成一致时由明白的人掌权，不在其内。

Ren does not fully agree that dominance is always a measure of relative position, always zero-sum and never enough: when order cannot be agreed, it can be for the one who understands to take power, and domination can be regulated. ## 2. 候选的边界条件 / Candidate boundary conditions

Each is a conjecture to be stated on the spine and proved in Lean on the pure core, or refuted by a witness.

**B1. Enough (completion).** Read a target as a condition on the state that the seeking is directed toward. Seeking can come to rest only at a state that meets its target with no further change. Three kinds of target differ:
- **A fixed condition**, such as a need or a level, can be met at rest.
- **"More than now"**, relative to the seeker's own current state, is met only by change, so never at rest. This is the treadmill, Ren's "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger".
- **"Above all others"**, relative to the others' states, is met by at most one formation at any state, so never by two or more at once. This is Ren's "the biggest that dominates". It is not dominance as such: power held within an order, regulated or taken by the one who understands when order cannot be agreed, can serve a target that the order meets.

Conjecture: a family of seekers can all come to rest exactly when their targets are jointly met at some state at rest within the margin (in the sense of `Flow.Balances.supported_iff_within_margin`). Self-relative targets never are, nor are relative targets for two or more seekers. The counting is elementary; the substance is in stating it of formed targets within the unfolding.

**B2. Expansion or exhaustion.** Under balance and dissipation, seeking that never rests keeps taking, and a finite family that keeps taking cannot, enclosed, go on fed from nothing beyond it. Conjecture: seekers locked onto targets that cannot be met at rest either keep drawing from beyond themselves, which is accumulation, expansion and colonization, or, enclosed, cease, by exhaustion rather than by reaching enough. Where activity needs support, the exhaustion ends the seekers themselves. This would be the formal content of "the whole Earth has become a highly entangled system": where nothing lies beyond, the pursuit ends.

**B3. Feedback.** The unfolding reaches a seeker's support only through what the seeker requires: support is admission by the levels of required conditions (`Web.supported_iff_admits`). Conjecture: the loss of its own support marks an illusion exactly where the seeker's cumulative net draw exceeds the margin on a condition it requires. That mark is delayed by at most its reserve and spread by joint excess. Where the cost falls beyond its dependence, no such mark ever comes, as with the free rider. Making "marks" into "corrects" needs a model of how a formed target is revised; the goal-revision results ([acquired-revision-of-pending-task](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/acquired-revision-of-pending-task.md) and its relatives) may supply it.

**B4. Seeing (明).** Conjecture: what support cannot mark, inquiry can. A seeker whose inquiry reaches its actual dependence, and the dependence of those its draw closes, can revise its target before the loss. An illusion whose cost falls beyond its dependence is then revisable only through inquiry, not through support. This would state the opening's "by one's own understanding, one can see" as a boundary condition: 明 is inquiry whose reach covers what support does not mark.

**B5. Hijacking.** Targets are formed and transmitted. Conjecture: where winning buys more power to win, formations whose targets cannot be met at rest take more, and transmit their targets more, until enclosure binds. This is the meta-attractor's selection of minds. On the AI's reading, Ren's "even rationality is self-interest" says the hijacking can reach the standard by which seeking is judged: the winners write the scorecard, including what counts as reason. It is the hardest of the five, and it needs the meta-attractor's results joined to the spine.

**The combined target.** 无明 locks in exactly where the target cannot be met at rest (B1), while the beyond still gives (B2) and the cost falls beyond the seeker's dependence or is deferred (B3), and inquiry does not reach it (B4). 明 is seeking whose target can be met at rest within the margin, whose costs return or are seen.

All five need no significance, so they belong to Part I, without axiom. Calling the locked-in seeking an illusion is a dynamic fact on this reading: the target cannot be met at rest, or meeting it does not sustain the seeker. Calling it suffering, or bad, would need Part II.
