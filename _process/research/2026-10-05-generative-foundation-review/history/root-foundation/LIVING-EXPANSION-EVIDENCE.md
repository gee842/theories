# Inherited provision across expanding living lineages

8 October 2026. AI-authored primary-source assessment continuing [the inherited-provision test](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#coverage-audit-and-next-decisive-test). The selected connection is **retained functional organization → continued multiplication → target-specific insufficiency when further formation is interrupted**. This adds no mathematical theorem, empirical replication or numerical fit.

The [previous transition assessment](LIVING-TRANSITION-EVIDENCE.md) established why retained organization and changed requirements must be followed separately. The present study is more discriminating: it combines a targeted interruption of formation, a measured inherited-protein proxy, observed descendants and a functional-localization proxy. It supports a scoped causal account without demanding that every molecule be tracked or that all possible biological alternatives be refuted first.

## 1. Primary evidence and version scope

**Choi, Lo, Cutler, Huang, Will and Wiggins, “Protein overabundance is driven by growth robustness.” Science Advances 12(12), eadz9623, 20 March 2026.** [Final publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41861001/); [final primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13004021/); [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adz9623). The accessible [arXiv v1, 21 August 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.11952v1), supplies the detailed supplementary reading below. These versions are one research lineage, not independent replications.

The final article reports A. baylyi knockout-depletion: descendants initially proliferate after removal of an essential gene. For fluorescent DnaN, dilution accompanies persistent replication-associated puncta; puncta disappear before filamentation. This supports continuing activity rather than merely partitioning previously replicated chromosomes. The earlier supplement measures corrected mean fluorescence against summed descendant area, not calibrated molecular copies. A matched nonessential knockout continues growing under selection. It also describes heterogeneous sister branches, interpreted as wild-type/knockout chromosome segregation; branch-specific fluorescence and morphology help distinguish them. Outgrowth precedes imaging, so the first frame does not identify an exact synthesis-stop instant. Critically, its DnaN division count plateaus while area continues increasing; the fitted area-arrest time is poorly constrained. Other targets' protein abundance is largely inferred from dilution. The observed target is therefore continued multiplication, not every form of growth or viability. No restoration intervention was found in the inspected material. [Final Results/Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13004021/); [v1 supplementary §§4.1–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.11952v1).

## 2. Affirmative interpretation and the rival it addresses

The following is our explanatory analysis, distinguished from the reported findings.

The target is **replication-supported multiplication of the selected lineage under the declared preparation**, identified independently of the proposition that it requires renewal. A lineage can continue other activities after this phenotype fails. This matters both for the causal account and for any later practical interpretation: arrest of one operation cannot silently become loss of all living significance.

The intervention changes the capacity to form a particular prerequisite while leaving already formed material available. Its delayed effect is positive evidence for inheritance: absence of a functioning gene is not equivalent to immediate absence of its product. The later phenotype, assessed with the depletion and localization observations, strengthens the claim that retained provision eventually fails to support the selected continuation under these conditions. It is more informative than observing decreasing concentration in an otherwise unperturbed growing culture.

The relevant rival must explain the whole selected observation pattern. A claim that only pre-existing completed products are being distributed differs from a claim that inherited machinery continues performing the operation. A localization proxy can discriminate those explanations without being an exact assay of each completed reaction. Its adequacy remains a correspondence question; the formal empty-pool theorem should not be substituted for that biological assessment.

Residual transcripts, continued expression from incompletely replaced genetic material, changed recruitment and unequal partition are possible components of an alternative explanation. They should be assessed against the intervention and branch-resolved observations, not excluded by saying “knockout” and not promoted into defeaters merely because they are conceivable. Ordinary causal evidence can support a target-specific requirement even when it does not identify an exact no-synthesis interval or every molecule's ancestry.

The warranted direction is consequently stronger than “dilution might matter,” but narrower than a universal expiry law: **formed material can support actual descendant activity, and interrupting its further formation can reveal later insufficiency for a specified expanding continuation**. This does not prove that every living bearer must produce the same material, that formation must occur in every interval, or that observed arrest is irreversible.

## 3. What the general account must preserve

Following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), progenitor, descendants, compartments and exchange routes are differentiated relationships within one history. A collection of already independent cells and a separate supply world is not the explanatory starting point. The relevant distinction concerns where the formed participants remain available and which ongoing operations they can jointly support.

| Relationship | Consequence for interpretation |
|---|---|
| Material persistence and relative concentration | Dilution need not destroy inherited molecules. The same material can become differently distributed relative to growing or multiplying activity. |
| Function and reporter | Fluorescence, localization, completed replication, division and viability answer different questions. A quantitative interpretation must state which one is measured or inferred. |
| Earlier activity and coexisting requirements | A historical division counter is not a count of simultaneous material demands. Continued distinct descendants and their actual operations determine whether the same participant could serve both. |
| Local provision and other routes | Retention, redistribution, uptake and new formation must be considered where actually available. A gene-specific intervention is not proof that every possible route has been removed. |
| Failed continuation and recovery | Later insufficiency does not itself establish death, irreversible loss or the success of a proposed repair. A restoration claim needs its own intervention or adequate mechanistic evidence. |

A repeated catalytic use is not a material consumption event. A bounded inherited pool therefore must not be modeled as losing one unit at every completed operation merely to obtain eventual failure. A justified application instead identifies how retained participants are distributed, how much relevant activity is simultaneously required, or why their operative concentration no longer supports that activity. Which description is appropriate depends on the mechanism and target.

This also preserves successful finite continuations. Retained provision that supports several generations is a genuine realization, not a merely apparent exception. An account of later insufficiency should explain both the earlier success and the later failure. A fixed threshold supplied to a local model may express one phenotype's requirement; it cannot define life generally.

## 4. Existing formal work and the next connection

The checked [organization-origin result](LIVING-ORGANIZATION-PROOF.md) tracks actual entry of a required organizing class. The checked [joint-allocation result](LIVING-ALLOCATION-PROOF.md) prevents incompatible concurrent assignments and credits later actual increases. Their hypotheses remain explicit. This assessment supplies a closer biological instance of retained provision and interrupted formation, not a calibrated integer inventory, an observed empty pool or a proof that a particular batch partition equals physical time.

The general [situated-requirement account](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md) already separates necessary additional provision from adequate retention and alternative routes. Applying it does not require another theorem for every gene. It does require identifying the operation and the relationship that becomes insufficient. The missing work is empirical and explanatory correspondence, not an inference that biological conditions originate outside generative unfolding.

The next integrated step should connect three already investigated modes: **ongoing maintenance, retained organization and recovery, and provision across expansion**. Earlier [recovery](LIVING-RECOVERY-EVIDENCE.md) and [organization](LIVING-ORGANIZATION-EVIDENCE.md) evidence already supplies restoration relationships at its own scopes. Those preparations must not be combined into one supposedly measured history, but neither must every new example repeat every earlier intervention. A DnaN rescue or exact copy calibration could strengthen this particular application; neither is a mandatory completion condition for the whole living account.

Further investigation should be selected by a missing relationship in that synthesis. A counterexample would matter if the specified continuation persists while its proposed necessary support is absent or inadequate, without an admitted alternative supplying it. Conversely, a validated alternative route should revise the proposed requirement instead of being excluded by definition. The synthesis must credit both successful inheritance and target-specific failure rather than automatically seek another gene-depletion case.

This round informs the physical dependencies that can enter situated practical reasoning. It supplies no ethical ranking, duty assignment or conclusion that multiplication must always continue. Nongrowing maintenance, dormancy, changed modes of activity and finite endings retain their separate scopes.

## 6. A complementary limitation within a growing compartment

Root-authored addition. **Mäkelä and colleagues (2024), “Genome concentration limits cell growth and modulates proteome composition in Escherichia coli,” eLife 13:RP97465, version of record 23 December 2024**, [DOI 10.7554/eLife.97465.3](https://elifesciences.org/articles/97465); [primary manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11666246/).

The study compares replication-arrested, single-genome filaments with division-arrested, multiple-genome filaments. An alternative replication-arrest preparation and stress-pathway controls test competing explanations. Genome dilution constrains estimated total transcriptional engagement even though polymerase abundance increases. Engagement is inferred from fluorescent abundance and mobility states, not a direct count of completed transcripts. Downstream translation and growth respond differently across media: poorer conditions delay the growth effect, with possible buffering mechanisms left for further tests. These are growth-scaling observations, not an all-or-none viability threshold. [Results, Figures 1, 3 and 6; Discussion](https://elifesciences.org/articles/97465).

**Our comparison:** a census of separated descendants cannot explain every increase in functional demand. Provision can become limiting within an enlarging compartment, and greater abundance of one participant may fail to compensate for another relationship. Conversely, altered operation can buffer a limitation without restoring the old ratio. A general dependency account must retain those responses. It cannot identify all requirements with one fixed copy per cell or infer failure of life from departure from exponential growth.

This evidence supplies an empirical contrast to the disjoint-copy abstraction, not a second instantiation of an exact integer threshold. The two studies are distinct preparations and histories. Their measurements are not combined to construct a purportedly observed reaction trace or a calibrated universal capacity law.
