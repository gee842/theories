## What advanced

The [new Lean module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/sufficiency/InquirySufficiency.lean) gives a constructive necessary-and-sufficient criterion for a bounded inquiry policy. A policy is independently defined as a finite branching tree of queries and terminal responses. The criterion propagates possible configurations through the actual existing process before filtering their observed outcome. It requires one response per acquired branch that works for all configurations still admitted on that branch.

The formalization preserves four connections that a claim of sufficient knowledge can otherwise omit:

1. Every query and every step of the terminal response must be executable at every still-admitted configuration. Merely exhibiting one favorable complete trace is insufficient.
2. Every admitted successor must preserve the declared intermediate condition, and every completed endpoint must satisfy the declared response requirement.
3. Impossible observation branches are distinguished from live branches with a continuation. An empty initial candidate set cannot establish usable sufficiency.
4. Actual execution uses the same `Process.step` relation, carries actual successors forward and follows the acquired observation branch. The proof establishes both existence of a complete execution and correctness of every admitted completed execution, including its intermediate conditions.

The uniform depth bound counts inquiries; the selected terminal response has its own finite action sequence. The theorem does not impose scheduling or turn an analytical readout into a physically acquired sensor.

## A logical dependency removed rather than assumed

Finitely many observation labels permit a direct construction of the finite table of branch policies. The proof constructs that table by induction, avoiding unrestricted choice. A library route that introduced propositional extensionality was replaced with an explicit finite-table constructor. The mathematical content is preserved; no process rule or desired behavior was added to compensate.

There is a separate constructive issue: finitely many observation labels do not decide whether an arbitrary predicate-defined branch contains a configuration. The core recurrence therefore carries **either proof that a branch is empty or a sufficient continuation for it**. The older implication form is proved equivalent when branches can be classified through the stated finite depth. An exact finite list of candidate configurations supplies a nonemptiness decision without equality testing or a logical axiom. Constructing faithful successor lists for a particular law remains that representation's obligation.

The [mathematical account](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-LEAN-MATH.md) states this refinement explicitly. It does not claim that arbitrary process relations have decidable reachability or that the classification interface is a necessary or minimal condition. The [review](INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-LEAN-REVIEW.md) checks both the positive proof structure and substantive adverse cases.

## What this does and does not derive

The new entry asserts no event law. It quantifies over the existing `Process` representation and proves relationships between explicit continuation contracts, finite policy syntax and actual traces. Every new declaration's axiom dependencies are audited by its separate checker; the inherited core's existing dependencies and sources remain governed by their prior receipt.

The observation interface, represented candidates, permitted response repertoire, intermediate condition and endpoint requirement are parameters of the theorem. They must be identified and justified when the theorem is applied. Calling these relations immanent does not derive their calibration, practical meaning, historical formation or authority. Conversely, their explicit use in a local representation does not imply that knowing, acting and significance originate in independent realities.

This completes the stated formal interface; it does not construct a newly acquired criterion in an actual learner. It contributes to the no-assumptions investigation by avoiding additional logical axioms in this derivation. It does not satisfy the stronger requirement that the entire generative foundation have no unexplained premises.
