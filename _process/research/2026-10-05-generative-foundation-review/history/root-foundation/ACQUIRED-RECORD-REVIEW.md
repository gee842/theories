## What is established

The new [outcome interface](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/OutcomeContinuation.lean) retains an endpoint readout on the existing `Process`/`Trace` relation. Forgetting the outcome recovers word feasibility. Exact interpretation of a proposed history record exists precisely when collisions do not merge different outcome profiles. Identity readout retains the full endpoint-trace relation and therefore determines the declared step relation. The pooled interpreter is an explicit existential mathematical relation, constructed without choice; it is not an implemented decoder or memory.

The three added [public model wrappers](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/GenerativeOutcomeModel.lean) state this criterion, its implication to the earlier feasibility contract, and the existing static/toggle separation. The additive entry point imports the unchanged shared `GenerativeModel` and the outcome interface, preserving parallel acquisition work. Their types and proofs preserve the distinction between feasible words and actual endpoint effects. The outcome contract is at least as demanding as feasibility; a constant readout need not make it strictly stronger. All-word exactness includes impossible histories, so a proposed partial operational record needs its domain identified before the generic contract is applied.

The [concrete module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/construction/prediction/AcquiredPredictionRecord.lean) adds the following genuine interface to the already established learner:

1. Every reached score equals replay of the actual consumed write inputs from the inherited initial score. Replay follows the unchanged updater recipe and does not alter the original process.
2. Equal initial scores, equal consumed write transcripts and equal final action references give equal next commands. The final reference is necessary because the tied score uses that field. The converse result only says differing commands require differing write transcripts when those other fields are fixed; it does not assert that every transcript difference changes action.
3. A paid work step installs the selected command as the executed action. The singleton-outcome interface therefore identifies its output exactly once that full state's work-enabledness is established. Equal acquired command information gives equal issued-action outcomes for the two separately admitted work steps.
4. The actual earlier beginning reaches the corrective observation through paid events. A paid write changes the retained score while preserving the previous action reference; the subsequent finish, rent and work execute the changed command and obtain a successful receipt. This same history instantiates the outcome relation, without resetting its memory, leases, stock or fuel at the join.

This is more than attaching an analyst's arbitrary summary to a process: the native `score` and action-reference fields are already read by its work event, and the proof traces the score back to the consumed inputs. The external transcript and replay function themselves are not installed inside the learner. The old existence of a successful correction history is credited; the new contribution is the general provenance/readout result and its exact endpoint-outcome connection.

## Counterexamples and retained dependencies

- A general trace may start in an observed state with an inherited or preloaded receipt. The replay theorem proves the provenance of subsequent score changes from consumed inputs, not that every input was acquired by a work event inside every arbitrary trace. The concrete beginning-to-correction witness supplies that acquisition history in its own case. The source comments now state this explicitly.
- Score and action reference determine a command, not all feasible futures. Phase, fuel, packets, leases and other physical fields retain their separate enablement roles. The equal-acquisition theorem compares the issued-action readout, not equal receipts across differing plant regimes. The successful receipt belongs to the identified concrete continuation.
- A receipt alone does not determine the productive orientation across different executed actions; the new closed counterexample proves this. The positive target-decoding result also depends on the unchanged noiseless, available-packet law. It supplies neither a posterior-calibration result nor the outage guarantee studied separately in the earlier executable comparison.
- The law admits event sequences; a feasible paid trace does not prove autonomous choice of the work/write schedule or Fast updater replacement. Within an admitted work event, the command is fixed by the retained native fields. Those are different claims.
- Existing [protocol counterexamples](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/PredictionProtocolObservation.lean) remain relevant: an acquired record may be unaffordable to write, and a completed correction can leave an expired updater unable to execute another cycle. An analyst's recovery of a consumed receipt from before/after scores is not an installed receipt sensor or a second internally retained copy.

The parallel [evidence review](ACQUIRED-RECORD-EVIDENCE.md) separately checks the outcome/feasibility distinction in finite states of the unchanged prediction law. Those computations are not new empirical observations, a refinement of every runtime branch, or a proof of self-formation.
