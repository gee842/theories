# 保持量词范围的普遍系统根基论证

8 October 2026. AI-authored affirmative argument for Ren's renewed instruction to solve the remaining foundational limit. This is a philosophical scope analysis, not a new mathematical theorem or empirical run. It applies the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md), preserves the independently authorized no-assumptions objective, and does not reopen the completed conditional foundation or separate G0/J ethical route.

**The three proposed root commitments do not all make the same universal claim. Actual instances already ground their permissive content. A general argument also grounds fidelity and necessary joint realization for independently identified organization. The remaining stronger passage concerns substantive causal and modal reach; it cannot be settled by quietly strengthening the permissive commitments or weakening that reach to mere description.**

**三个根基承诺的量词不同。实际例证已经为其中“能够发生”的内容提供根据；对已独立辨认之组织的保真要求也有一般论证。真正较强的连接在于因果与可能性约束的普遍说明，不能把“有这样的能力”偷偷加强为“处处如此”，也不能把后者削弱为任意描述。**

## 1. Read the existing commitments precisely

The [canonical minimal root](../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) states:

- Reality **permits** consequential differentiation: distinguishable conditions and relations can affect subsequent interactions.
- What occurs **depends** on participating conditions and relations; organization constrains later change and can itself change.
- Interactions **can combine**, including recurrent arrangements. Not every system is required to contain feedback, repair or reproduction.

The first and third include world-level capability claims. A warranted actual realization supplies a positive ground for possibility under its realized conditions. It does not need an induction showing that every system possesses that capability. Nor does an actual recurrence establish that the same preparation remains available now. The [previous variation-warrant distinction](../../../../theories/becoming/history/SYSTEM-WARRANT.md#2-match-the-grounds-to-the-claim) applies unchanged.

The second carries more than an existential observation if read as a common explanation of what occurs throughout system-forming reality. Its language needs disambiguation: a relation's being part of an achieved organization, its taking part in an operation, its being causally relevant to that operation, and its restricting other possible continuations are connected but different claims. The existing [systems account](../../../../theories/becoming/history/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION.md#2-constitutive-participation-differs-from-successful-prediction) already prevents replacing participation by successful prediction.

The broad task therefore must not be restated as three equally unsupported universal propositions. It also must not be announced complete merely because two permissive claims have actual instances. The common explanatory reach has its own content.

## 2. Actual evidence supplies a beginning, not a freely chosen axiom

The [retained-use evidence assessment](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-EVIDENCE.md#1-documented-acquisition-retention-and-issued-action) identifies a documented controller that acquires a reading, computes an updated integral, retains it, and passes it into the next command calculation. Program provenance, operative source order, archived commands and independent replay jointly support that limited attribution. These are grounds for an actual instance of consequential differentiation and recurrent composition. They are not merely examples satisfying a definition that contains the desired conclusion.

The [oxygen-gradient evidence](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-EVIDENCE.md#2-experimental-formation-of-subsequent-biological-conditions) independently supports activity altering subsequently relevant conditions. It retains the distinctions among performed assays, their measured outcomes and their reported limitations. It supplies another realization, not a license to treat every later biological development as already explained.

These grounds answer a real part of the foundational question: the proposed relations are not introduced only because the theory would like them to exist. They are accountable to documented operations and performed contrasts. Calling these grounds defeasible does not reduce them to arbitrary premises. Conversely, their actual evidential dependencies—source fidelity, recognition of the operations and inference from the records—cannot be erased by calling them immanent to the whole.

This argument reuses the already assessed empirical content. It claims no new independent retrieval, replication or source-binding check. The stronger task's treatment of recognition and inference is addressed below; ordinary empirical warrant and a completely assumption-free justification are not being identified.

## 3. A general constitutive argument that does succeed

Begin with a phenomenon identified without invoking the generative theory: a sustained shape, a documented calculation, a coordinated action, a prepared reaction, or a collective arrangement. Its actual characterization may include organization, continuity and operation established through different kinds of evidence. None of those words makes the attribution automatically correct.

Now consider an account that claims to explain that very phenomenon. If an independently warranted relationship is part of what happened, an account that simply discards it does not yet explain the full target. It must either preserve the relationship in its own presentation, restrict its explanatory undertaking explicitly, or offer a substantive reason to revise the attribution. This is the reason for fidelity. It does not depend on a privileged generative vocabulary or require the rival to retain the original variables.

The argument applies to any such target because its force concerns keeping the explanatory question fixed. It does not extrapolate a particular mechanism from several observed systems to all systems. A whole-history, ecological, computational or physical account can satisfy it in its own terms. A purported common-cause explanation may improve the account by correcting an alleged direct dependence; it must address the evidence for that dependence rather than merely match a smaller selected record.

This yields a universal explanatory obligation with substantive applications:

1. Preserve actual dependencies relevant to the selected claim, including their formation and subsequent use when those are established.
2. Preserve actual joint conditions; a collection of separately feasible operations is not yet a demonstrated jointly realized arrangement.
3. Preserve the difference between continuing an organization, reproducing a similar outcome, and replacing it through another history when that difference belongs to the question.
4. Preserve established failure, erasure and loss. Earlier participation does not make a relation perpetually operative.

The entries are consequences for explanatory practice, not an additional definition of reality. Their substantive content is supplied by the independently constrained target. This is why they can rule out particular descriptions without pretending to generate every physical fact from a vocabulary.

The existing checked [unfolding](UNFOLDING-PRESERVATION-INTEGRATION.md), [effective-law](LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-INTEGRATION.md), [system-individuation](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md) and [variation-warrant](VARIATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md) studies give formal instances with explicit carriers, laws, questions and correspondence. Their mathematics is credited without treating those signatures as an unexplained split between mind and world.

## 4. Push the second commitment beyond descriptive fidelity

The strongest affirmative proposal is a general **realization-dependence** argument. This name describes the argument below; it is not an added predicate that makes the conclusion true. Begin with an organization identified through warranted facts about how it occurs. Differentiate its constituent relationships at the explanatory resolution justified by that inquiry. The differentiation need not pick independent objects, a unique smallest region, or a privileged material carrier.

**First, constitutive identity has content.** If two descriptions retain the same organization-determining relations at that resolution, a bare change of labels does not establish that the organization itself changed. Conversely, a claimed change of organization needs a difference in its realizing relationships or a correction to what the chosen account treated as determining it. This does not claim that every property of a system, including every qualitative or modal property, is fixed by an arbitrary structural summary. Determining the relevant relations is part of the warranted identification, and the [no-least-region result](SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md) prevents making one smallest carrier mandatory.

**Second, realized organization constrains joint realization.** Where two contributions occur through a shared condition, they participate in one realized arrangement of that condition, with the timing belonging to the target. Describing one contribution under one incompatible shared arrangement and another under a different arrangement does not exhibit their joint realization. Joint realization can involve successive or alternating operations; it does not require every contribution to occur simultaneously. This is more than the instruction to preserve a record: it rejects a proposed constituent explanation when its separately supplied realizations cannot fit together in the organization it purports to explain.

For a simple illustrative case, two separately successful operations can each require the same presently indivisible support during the same interval. Those successes do not establish that both occur together. The decisive content is the independently warranted exclusivity and shared timing, not the words “support” or “system.” If the actual operation permits division, alternation or a replacement supply, the incompatibility must be revised. Similarly, two descriptions of one physical connection must agree about its actual realization where they overlap; treating them as independent inputs would change the investigated arrangement.

This reasoning does not impose classical deterministic dynamics on everything or declare every physically relevant boundary to be a complete state. The [joining study](EXPLANATORY-JOINING-INTEGRATION.md#4-a-genuine-failure-equal-interfaces-do-not-always-suffice) already demonstrates that matching represented interfaces need not suffice for actual admitted joining. Agreement is a necessary consistency demand at the retained resolution; physical feasibility can require additional relationships that the resolution omits. No sufficiency law is inserted here.

**Third, continuation inherits this dependence without fixing the carrier.** When the organization actually continues, its constituent roles are realized in that continuation, whether through retained relations, transformed bearers or replacement. If an identified route ceases and no other route realizes an indispensable role, that organization does not continue at that point. This allows finite completion, dormancy, reconfiguration, externally maintained activity and succession. It does not require an organization to choose continuation or imply that continuation is good.

This gives the generative account an implementation-independent necessary relation: continued organization calls for continued compatible realization of what constitutes it. Where inherited realization becomes insufficient, an actual continuing history must contain whatever changes make adequate realization possible. The established [general continuation results](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md#what-the-requested-chain-now-establishes) make stronger operational consequences precise under independently stated support/use laws. Their hypotheses are not replaced by this philosophical sentence. In particular, mere existence does not prove recurrent depletion or recurrent renewal.

The argument does not define *system* as an entity whose prior activity causes its future. It begins from separately established organization and follows what its realization requires. It explains why a constitutive relation cannot be declared absent while the same organization is claimed present without a realizing replacement or a revision of the attribution. It earns a necessary **constitutive** sense of the second commitment across implementations.

The strongest attempted final step is to argue that these facts already establish causal generation: because every realized continuation has compatible realizing relations, those constitutive necessities suffice for the theory's common causal and modal explanation throughout its intended scope. That step does not follow from the argument just given. Joint realization does not by itself identify a producing relation where one is claimed. A necessary condition for retaining organization does not make a continuation physically available or explain the transformation through which it becomes available. This does not require a specified antecedent for every system, exhaustive prediction, one ontology or complete knowledge of every possibility. The constitutive content is real and general; its claimed causal/modal strengthening needs its own grounds at the strength actually asserted. This is the precise boundary of the direct attempt, not a general impossibility judgment.

## 5. Why this does not yet establish the strongest generative claim

There are three possible conclusions, with different warrants:

**An achieved organization has the relationships actually constituting that organization.** This is an account of factual realization. Evidence that establishes those relationships can warrant the attribution. Once that content is independently fixed, removing it from an explanation changes or narrows the target.

**Those relationships participate in producing or maintaining the organization.** This adds operative content. It can be supported through construction, uptake, coupling, interruption and replacement. Mere simultaneous presence is insufficient; the evidence must distinguish unused records, redundant routes and common sources where those are relevant alternatives.

**Their organization constrains unperformed continuations throughout the proposed domain.** This adds modal and general content. A witnessed continuation supports its realized possibility. An impossibility or invariance claim needs grounds that cover the relevant alternatives. A mechanism and its realization can supply such grounds over an earned class; calling the arrangement a system does not supply them.

The affirmative constitutive argument establishes the first conclusion's fidelity requirement, earns the general necessary-realization constraint, and carries the second wherever actual operation has been established. It cannot alone establish the third merely by substituting “possibility” for “actuality.” Nor does the third require a complete inventory of all reality: a warranted general mechanism or constraint could support it. The question is whether that support has been given for the scope actually claimed.

This identifies the exact unearned inference:

> Every realized organization requires compatible constitutive relations; therefore this requirement itself grounds the theory's general explanation of how organization forms and transforms, and how its possibilities change.

The conclusion contains additional content. A necessary requirement on realization is not by itself an explanation of the activity through which realization occurs or its possibilities change. It can constrain that explanation without supplying its common causal and modal ground. This distinction does not demand certification of every individual application; the remaining target is the general explanatory connection actually claimed. Pointing out this difference is not a rival ontology and is not a proof that the root is false.

## 6. The strongest rivals and corrections

**“A system can be a mere grouping.”** The systems chapter expressly permits this broad descriptive usage. It would be wrong to infer actual integration or causal participation from membership in a chosen collection. Where the target is independently established organized activity, however, an arbitrary grouping fails to explain the organization unless it supplies the relevant relationships. This disciplines the attribution rather than defeating organized systems.

**“A static or terminal arrangement refutes generation.”** It does not refute the declared core, which admits static, finite and destructive cases. The appropriate account may identify how an arrangement was formed, how it is maintained, or where continuation ends. No requirement that every system actively reproduces itself follows. If no formation history is established, the account must leave that provenance open.

**“An atemporal account avoids the process.”** An account preserving all relevant formation, use and dependence relations is an admissible articulation. No general necessity argument should rely on forbidding it. A temporal sequence and a whole-history description can carry the same explanatory content.

**“Any challenge itself enacts the entire foundation.”** A consequential inquiry can exhibit participation and composition in that inquiry. This provides a beginning and rebuts a denial that such relations ever occur. It does not, simply through the critic's participation, decide the organization of every other system or justify every inferential practice. The stronger all-scope passage requires its own argument.

**“There could always be a hidden exception.”** Bare imaginability is not itself a defeater of a supported scientific explanation. The present limit does not demand elimination of every imaginable rival before ordinary warrant is credited. It asks that an asserted class-level claim have the particular argument or evidence its strength requires. A fabricated weaker model that forgets actual operative evidence does not refute the richer target.

## 7. Recognition and inference within the same unfolding

The examination, retention, comparison and correction that establish a relation are themselves actual activities. Their inclusion in the account removes an unnecessary picture of a detached observer crossing into a separate reality. It does not make them immune to error. Two mutually consistent records can share a defect; a valid formal transformation can begin from a misidentified input.

The existing [constructive correspondence work](GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#11-derive-correspondence-through-formation-and-state-its-exact-remainder) supplies a positive case: an operation can form a relation and exhibit it, and a checked transformation can preserve that relation. This does not require every ground to be justified only by an earlier proposition. Directly exhibited organization, construction and warranted uptake are legitimate candidates for grounds.

For the stronger objective, the question is not whether these grounds are allowed in principle. It is whether the content attributed to them and the transformations of that content have acquired the required justification. Demanding universal infallibility would be an additional gate; calling ordinary fallible justification “no assumptions at all” would silently weaken the user's request. Neither move is warranted.

The relevant residual obligation can therefore be stated more narrowly than “justify everything about every system”: justify the foundational inferential and recognitional operations actually used, and the additional generalization from grounded organization to the claimed causal/modal reach. Specific completed applications do not need to be reopened while that stronger investigation continues.

## 8. Assessment of the attempted resolution

This argument earns three positive advances in the accounting of the problem. First, the permissive content of consequential differentiation and composability is already supported by actual witnessed organization; it is not waiting for an all-systems induction. Second, preservation of independently established organization is a general explanatory requirement because changing its relevant content changes the target, rather than because a preferred definition declares every system generative. Third, constitutive identity, shared realization and continuing organization support an implementation-independent dependence constraint: organization cannot persist without a compatible realization of its constituent relations, including replacements where relevant. This constrains explanations and actual continuation without asserting sufficient physical access or a fixed material carrier.

The stronger foundation cannot be certified from these gains alone. The remaining issue is concentrated in the substantive scope of the second root commitment and in the warrant of the recognition and inference carrying that claim. An assumption-free completion would need an argument that establishes those commitments at their actual strength. This note supplies neither a new axiom nor an impossibility result, and makes no claim to have completed that task.
