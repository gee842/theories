## The result

Activity changes the conditions of subsequent activity. Organization, operational significance, learning and revision of selectors can develop through specified relations within it. Where differentiated lives and their worthwhile possibilities occur, the ethical candidate identifies actual good and harm as practically significant through appropriate available response. Concern organizes recognition and action. Its effects can change both the ground for another response and the capacities through which further response becomes possible. Wisdom concerns the adequacy and correction of this whole activity, including its purposes and its continuation.

This closes the exposition around one substantive candidate. It does not add an authorizing force after a reason has been warranted, require each good to produce another, or interpret the success of a learning mechanism as proof of ethical validity. Direct relief anchors a possible good even at the end of a life. Elsewhere, effects can form new relationships, capacities and goods. These changes belong within the same generative explanation while preserving real differences among lives.

The [positive argument](UNIFIED-GOODNESS-CLOSURE-ARGUMENT.md) identifies four remaining commitments: actual realization of the attributed relations, the constitutive value and practical-favor identification, independently warranted contextual legitimacy, and adequate correspondence between consequences and their representation. Disclosure does not discharge those commitments. The restricted relief case has the strongest developed ethical defense; a wider account of agency and flourishing requires its own warrants.

## Adjudication and its limit

The affirmative judgment concerns starting to care. Actual relief can justify forming concern before a prior bond or helping purpose. An equally satisfying performance that helps nobody lacks that point. The same affected life explains subsequent correction and cessation. This is positive philosophical support for one account spanning prospective adoption and later practice.

The [review](UNIFIED-GOODNESS-CLOSURE-REVIEW.md) preserves a substantive rival: formed practical centers may have a jurisdiction over positive purposes such that benefit and an undertaking jointly constitute an assistance reason. This rival can accept actual suffering, effective relief, nondual formation and feedback. It rejects the pre-role appraisal; it is not refuted by changing a predicate in a weaker model. Protecting self-direction does not alone establish its proposed zero-favor restriction, but we have not proved that every such restriction fails. The unified candidate remains favored rather than demonstrated necessary.

Legitimate relationships can also create obligations, goods and choice-specific exclusions. General moral significance, a permitted basis for this choice and categorical rational authority remain distinct. Prospective justification and realized outcomes remain distinct too. The review retains exploitation, coercive dependence, terminal relief and warranted protective refusal as discriminating cases rather than redefining them out of the account.
