# A typed construction interface for the existing predictor

6 October 2026. AI-authored mathematical companion to the [typed predictive construction](../../../../theories/becoming/history/MATERIAL-PREDICTION-COMPOSITION.md). Named connection: **actual formation of shared components → updater and selector construction → reserved finite register → paid prediction, correction and action with component-limited updater replacement**.

The operational target is the existing `PredictionAction.lean` relation, with its explicit payable-write phases. This is a conditional construction interface to that law. It does not change the predictor algorithm or inherit the broader Python experiment's outages, skipped writes, memory erasure, circuit reconstruction or probabilities.

## 1. What is preserved and what is newly required

The predecessor is an actual `ConstructedUpdater` state. It already carries the full coarse updater state, free A/H stocks, incorporated/spent A/H stocks, resident, site and retained precursor. Initial formation and assembly are unchanged. The predictor's retrofit additionally requires one free A and one free H, besides the old predictor retrofit's active hardware, seen-selector, coarse material and fuel conditions.

The retrofit pays the existing three-unit bill, removes one free A/H pair and records it in a **separate register inventory** `registerUnits=1`. That pair is not also added to `boundA` or `boundH`. The five-state register, receipt latch, decoder and response function are supplied functional recipes. Two constituents do not by arithmetic alone imply that memory capacity or wiring.

The new operational state contains the entire `PredictionAction.State`, including its physical state, retained score, pending receipt and phase. It additionally carries the actual typed inventory. The new register starts at the old neutral score two exactly once at retrofit. The existing selector orientation remains its last-action reference, while fuel, packets, leases, updater kind, regime and remainder are inherited with their specified payments.

During predictive operation, only `formUpdater` consumes additional free parts: one A and one H become incorporated/spent parts. Its fuel cost and all other effects are unchanged. Writing reuses the installed register and pays the old writing bill; it does not consume another component pair. Every admitted operational step requires a positive installed-register count. All events retain the register backing. No reclamation, new constituent production or covert conversion of the resident is introduced.

Thus material is

\[
M=A+H+B_A+B_H+2R+\operatorname{occupied}(\rho)+P,
\]

where \(R\) counts reserved register pairs and \(P\) retained precursor. Per-type bookkeeping preserves \(A+B_A+R\) and \(H+B_H+R\), with the resident's unchanged type accounted for separately. The bound inventories retain both current and spent assemblies; they do not assert that expired assemblies have disappeared or become reusable.

## 2. Exact lift of predictive histories

Let \(U(w)\) count later updater formations in an operational word \(w\). For a state \(r\) with an installed register and a specified coarse predictor endpoint \(s\), the exact criterion is

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\exists t:\operatorname{TypedPredictionTrace}(r,w,t)\land t.\mathrm{core}=s\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{PredictionActionTrace}(r.\mathrm{core},w,s)
\land U(w)\le r.A\land U(w)\le r.H.
\end{aligned}
\]

This is `ConstructedPrediction.material_trace_lift_iff`. The installed-register condition is an explicit hypothesis, not implied by coarse `material >= 2`. The empty word is always a trace; without installed backing no nonempty operational trace is admitted by this wrapper.

The proof projects each admitted step to the exact old predictor successor and sums its component costs. Conversely, nonnegative cumulative component costs fit every prefix when the whole sum fits. The old trace premise still checks **every** phase, lease, fuel payment, packet access and payable write. An aggregate energy inequality cannot replace these prefix conditions.

The component restriction changes the set of admissible histories without altering admitted commands, receipts or score updates. Finite free stocks bound the number of newly formed updaters, not the number of writes on a surviving reusable register or the persistence of an already formed mechanism.

## 4. Actual material and phase failures

The independent checker retains two actual smaller preparations with the same operational fuel and packets. Original capacity ten, fuel 32 and source 121 can form either four A/six H or five A/five H before the same association, repair and capture. After the same adapter, base assemblies and retrofit, the predictor begins with respectively zero A/one H or one A/zero H. Its coarse state still completes the old predictor word, but the typed process reaches the Fast replacement with **fuel 22, four packets and score two** and cannot form it. Earlier valid construction has exhausted a required type. Extra source energy does not release an incorporated part.

Two other actual preparations isolate the retrofit itself. Capacity nine, fuel 31 and source 121 can form three A/six H or five A/four H. Their paid base machinery has fuel seventeen, twelve packets and coarse material nine, so the old retrofit guard is true. One free component type is absent, and the typed retrofit is blocked. A coarse scalar material total cannot certify available register backing.

The formal package also retains a different genuinely reached obstruction: the previously proved typed updater witness ends with an operative selector/updater and fuel nine but no free parts. A predictor retrofit using the same base parameters is coarsely enabled and materially blocked. This uses that old witness's actual final state, not a fictional depleted initial state.

Payment order remains independent of parts. Starting a mismatched cycle with four common units pays rent and work and reaches phase `observed` with common fuel zero and eleven source packets. The receipt has arrived but its write is unaffordable, so the retained score is unchanged and no operational event in this restricted relation can complete that cycle. Its spent costs and pending receipt are retained. Future source cannot pay the missing present write.

This failure must not be conflated with the broader numerical predictor law. In the relation refined here, an unaffordable rent blocks the rent event; it does not erase the stored score. There is no skipped-write transition, outage, circuit rebuild or automatic forgetting in this formal subprocess. Installed backing and exact preservation of a field are not an empirical claim about indefinite physical memory retention.

## 6. What this supplies to the derivation

The earlier shared component inventory can now produce and sustain the particular predictive correction history under an explicit finite-register recipe. Updater replacement, predictor memory and later action share both their support history and their material obligations. A successful earlier assembly cannot be reused as free backing for a new function.

Physical wiring, finite storage capacity, transduction, the declared update repertoire and the response rule remain local-model inputs. Production/reclamation of future components, fallible calibration, the broader predictor protocol, new coupler construction, other-directed routing and successor reason revision are separate interfaces. The [downstream contract note](CONCERN-WISDOM-INPUTS.md) records why this material connection does not itself supply shared concern or justified wisdom.
