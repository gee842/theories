## The evidence changes the proposed route

The completed inquiry model does not schedule any autonomous maintenance after
its final retention epoch. Its remaining memory cells do not themselves create
an additional native obligation. Adding an arbitrary number of retention calls
would supply a new task while describing it as derived. This is an omission in
that local representation of continued organization, not evidence that purposes
come from outside generative reality.

The [existing material reconstruction](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-MECHANISM.md) already
contains repair, capture, consumption and renewed copying. Yet it does not imply
that capture is necessary whenever reconstruction continues. The same law has
bulk copying from common fuel. At the rich formed ancestor, that is a real
adequate route for replacing a lost A. Prescribing capture as the next learned
requirement would exclude an independently available alternative and build the
desired behavior into the experiment.

We therefore derive the actual requirement relation before claiming to teach it.
The [new exact characterization](CONTINUING-DEPENDENCY-MATH.md) gives the least
common support for a specified count-restoration task, retaining every original
event and both local and bulk routes. For an intact site it is
`max(n−L−E,0)`; for a damaged site it is
`min(max(n−L,0),1+max(n−L−E,0))`. Here n is the required A increase, L the initial
local bit and E the source, with an active resident and enough precursor places.
The target preserves H and the active resident but does not also require final
intactness or a full local store. Those would be additional continuation claims.

The disjunction is substantive. Repair can be unnecessary for current
reconstruction even while damaged; local support already present or common
fuel can suffice. In other configurations, reaching source through repair is
necessary. A condition's role must be judged through its actual alternatives
and the continuation being explained, rather than through one selected causal
chain or a favorable evaluator's label.

The new result does not install another controller. It identifies the dependency
that the next actual learning construction must acquire and use: current task
completion and total remaining support need not preserve the ability to meet an
independently continuing requirement. A supplied capture-first answer would
bypass this question; a generic ordinary planner with the appropriate acquired
relations remains a valid implementation.

Its archived records also occupy material that must remain unavailable for
ordinary plant copying. The established generic emission archive supplies a
provenance pattern, not a demonstrated physical contact for this application.

needed next connection: actual native effects → material-backed acquired

continuation. Formation, observation, retained history, reading and action must

extra common-unit operation would consume the repair reserve; earlier acquired
knowledge cannot be inserted for free after reaching it.

This leaves an acquired-recognition and composition obligation, not a new denial
of existing learning, inquiry-limit revision or method formation. Their accepted
results retain their scope. The count-maintenance criterion, local physical law
and disturbance schedule remain supplied at this study's scope. Their provenance
