# Naming formation, participation and responsibility directly

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. Editorial and conceptual clarification under [Ren's explicit request](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-generative-language-and-formation.md). This revision supports the universal investigation by preventing its disputed normative inference from being hidden inside terminology. It supplies no new theorem, experiment or ethical premise.

The ambiguous term had three different theoretical uses: contribution to forming an adverse condition; actual participation in an activity or decision; and effective direction of one's life. Those uses are now named according to their content. In the current support comparison, the relevant subject is how dependence forms and changes. Activities and their differentiated positions belong within that formation; a separate originating author is not assumed.

| Question | Current wording | What still needs explanation |
|---|---|---|
| How activity changes subsequent activity | Formation and transformation of the conditions of further activity | Actual relations, their history and differentiated effects |
| How support and available alternatives change | Formation and transformation of dependence | Sustaining, interrupting, displacing alternatives and inviting reliance |
| Whether an actual contribution shapes a decision or practice | Practical participation, joint participation, self-direction | Formation and exercise of the capacity; accurate prediction is not its exercise |
| Which response is justified | Responsibility, permission or a reason, with its defended scope | Normative warrant, competing considerations and legitimate discharge |
| What an operation requires in a local mechanism | Specified resource use or resource requirements | The actual quantity and law; no universal positive cost is inferred |

The support comparison retains its four histories and their distinct consequences. Describing their formation does not prove a duty, and explaining a special responsibility does not prove that all other reasons are absent. Likewise, practical participation does not automatically establish legitimate jurisdiction. These qualifications preserve the original claims' scope while making their dependencies easier to inspect.

The pass covers current exposition, associated research arguments and summaries. Context determines the replacement: self-direction and collective participation are not renamed as dependence, and actual monetary relations are not renamed as fuel. Code symbols, original sources, direct quotations, attributed source terminology, preserved development snapshots and past verification receipts retain their historical identity. Explicit anchor aliases preserve links to renamed sections.

No formal definition, theorem or experiment is changed by this pass. Earlier scientific checks retain their original source bindings; wording updates do not count as scientific revalidation. The saved patch and before/after hashes record this revision separately so older prose can be reconstructed rather than silently overwritten. Current documentation checks concern navigation, preserved material and the completed terminology changes.
