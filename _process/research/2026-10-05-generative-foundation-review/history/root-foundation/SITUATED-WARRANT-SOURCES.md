## 1. Greaves: actual history, expected comparison, and genuine conflict

Hilary Greaves, “Cluelessness,” *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society* 116(3), 311–339 (2016), [publication identity](https://doi.org/10.1093/arisoc/aow018). Read the [author manuscript](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~mert2255/papers/cluelessness.pdf), §§1–2 and §§5–8: PDF pp.1–5 and 9–18, including the footnote distinguishing credences from evidential probabilities. The 20-page manuscript is not the published 29-page pagination; no version-equivalence audit was performed.

**Source result.** Greaves separates actual consequence-betterness from expected comparison at decision time. Her simple-cluelessness response depends on a defended symmetry between opposite action–effect correlations: it concerns an expected difference, not actual cancellation of remote effects. Complex cases contain substantive, dissimilar reasons for systematic effects in opposing directions. Inconclusiveness does not establish equal probabilities. Her imprecise-credence discussion leaves rival connections to permissibility, and its conclusions remain tentative.

**Contribution and limit.** This gives the investigation a precise opponent to both automatic tail-cancellation and automatic paralysis. It does not justify an ethical scale, identify which evidence a participant ought to obtain, or settle permissibility under disagreement. Greaves's charity example is a philosophical test; this reading does not verify its demographic or intervention claims.

## 2. Friederich: examine mechanisms without deleting sensitive consequences

Simon Friederich, “Causation, Cluelessness, and the Long Term,” *Ergo* 12(17), 440–459 (2025). Read printed pp.442–457 from the [primary paper in PhilSci-Archive](https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/25043/2/ergo-7428-friederich.pdf); checked identity against the [author's institutional record](https://research.rug.nl/en/publications/causation-cluelessness-and-the-long-term/). The publisher's direct download failed.

**Source argument.** Causal dependence can vary in sensitivity to background changes. The distinction often illuminates simple versus complex cluelessness, but the paper explicitly gives exceptions and treats sensitivity as gradual. Systematic downstream mechanisms permit investigation; focusing on long-term interventions does not automatically avoid uncertainty, since their mechanisms interact with other mechanisms. The paper also argues against attributing ethical responsibility for highly sensitive unforeseen effects.

**Contribution and limit.** Mechanism sensitivity helps specify what to investigate. Its responsibility conclusion requires a further normative argument and must not become “sensitive losses have no ethical significance.” Being one contributor among many does not itself settle responsibility. The cited health, development and technology examples were not independently assessed here; their substantive policy conclusions are not adopted. This is a philosophical rival, not evidence that every systematic side effect is tractable or every speculative tail cancels.

## 3. Lorenz: a limit on forecasting has a specified target

Edward N. Lorenz, “Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow,” *Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences* 20, 130–141 (1963), [original university-hosted scan](https://cdanfort.w3.uvm.edu/research/lorenz-1963.pdf). Reopened that primary URL and directly reread printed p.141 using the repository's [preserved page image](../../../../sources/41-systems-complexity-2026-10-06/lorenz-1963-page-12.png). The [existing provenance record](../../../../sources/41-systems-complexity-2026-10-06/README.md) preserves the scan and earlier reading scope.

**Source result.** The specified nonlinear model and the conditional atmospheric discussion challenge indefinitely precise prediction from inexact observations. Lorenz explicitly leaves the atmospheric prediction horizon unanswered. This page does not establish that every real process is chaotic.

**Contribution and limit.** A failure of detailed trajectory prediction is not itself a result about practical or ethical ranking. Using chaos against a particular judgment therefore requires identifying which ethically relevant relationship becomes unpredictable and how that defeats the comparison. Neither useful coarse predictions nor their failure is established here. Sensitive dependence inside a specified model must also be distinguished from an omitted mechanism or an event outside that model's repertoire. No fresh chaos theorem or scientific replication was attempted.

## 4. Haasnoot and colleagues: adaptive plans retain supplied ends

Marjolijn Haasnoot, Jan H. Kwakkel, Warren E. Walker and Judith ter Maat, “Dynamic adaptive policy pathways: A method for crafting robust decisions for a deeply uncertain world,” *Global Environmental Change* 23, 485–498 (2013), [institutional copy of the published article](https://ris.utwente.nl/ws/files/247793059/Haasnoot2013dynamic.pdf). Read §§2–3, the case's introductory scope statement, and §§5–6: printed pp.487–490 and 495–496; also the abstract and introduction.

**Source method.** Objectives and success criteria are specified before pathways are evaluated. Monitoring, triggers and contingent changes join near-term action with later adaptation. A tipping point for performance differs from a trigger requiring advance preparation. Detection difficulties and implementation lead times matter. The Rhine Delta illustration uses simplifying assumptions, earlier studies and expert judgment; it is not a completed quantitative validation of the general method.

**Contribution and limit.** This supports representing an action together with its feasible observation and revision arrangements. It supplies no first ethical warrant for objectives, acceptable loss, authority or whose perspective governs. Robustness remains relative to the explored futures and indicators. The theory must assess monitoring burdens and justified closure separately; keeping pathways open does not itself establish good continuation.

## 5. Fowler and Christakis: interaction can propagate changed responses

James H. Fowler and Nicholas A. Christakis, “Cooperative behavior cascades in human social networks,” *PNAS* 107(12), 5334–5338 (2010), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0913149107), [primary article PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~knutson/bad/fowler10.pdf). Read study design and results on pp.5334–5335 and discussion/methods on pp.5337–5338. Supplementary analyses and raw data were not inspected or rerun.

**Source evidence.** The authors analyze earlier public-goods experiments involving 240 people, anonymously reassigned to groups without repeat pairings. Participants received contribution feedback. The analysis reports subsequent contribution changes along direct and indirect interaction paths, with both increased and decreased contributions spreading. It explicitly leaves wider propagation in natural networks open.

**Contribution and limit.** This supplies a bounded empirical reason to investigate changed responses beyond the original encounter. It is not a test of a detached bystander merely witnessing help: group members participated and shared material consequences. It does not identify every psychological mediator, establish lasting moral development, or validate a universal multiplier for assistance. Experimental contribution and experimental payoffs are not already whole ethical quality. Repeated use of the same data must not be counted as independent confirmation.

## What changes in the central inference

以下是本研究基于核查提出的推论与检验要求，不是以上作者共同主张，也不是 Ren 已接受的新公理。

**第一，判断所依赖的范围应按理由关系限定，不能只按时间远近切断。** 一次已知的依赖改变可能长期持续；一个很近的旁观反应也可能只有任意猜测。应写明哪些关系支持判断、对哪些扰动仍成立、哪些遗漏能够改变原理由。这样的范围说明承认认识有限，不把范围外的实际损失判为零。Greaves 与 Friederich 的分歧还提示：没有一项通用的“未知项归零”操作可以承担这份工作。

**第二，至少有三种不同的挑战，不能合并为一个风险分数。** 一是某后果是否会发生、如何传递；二是同一完整后果配置有什么伦理性质与优先关系；三是当时的证据、可行行动及调查条件是否足以支持这次回应。它们都在同一生成中形成：证据由活动产生，获取证据会改变关系，已经形成的评价也能影响接下来的行动。分别核查它们，是为了追踪错误发生在哪一步，不是重新建立事实／价值两域。事后发生的损失仍真实；当时无法知道也不等于当时已有充分根据。

**第三，对未知的回应本身需要比较。** “先调查”“先等待”“保持原状”会继续形成后果。DAPP 使这种时间连接具体，但没有证明调查总值得，或只要能修正就可先做。下一比较须保留观察的负担、反应提前量、可用修正能力及延迟期间的损失；不能把未来修正写成免费、必达的退出通道。一个等待确定性的安排若使可行的保护机会消失，其机会损失必须与立即行动的风险同列。

**第四，机制证据可以提高某条后果链在审议中的地位，不能直接给它伦理优先。** 支持扩散也能固化依赖；可学习的安排也可能传递排除；较稳定的路径也可能稳定地伤害。相关实验有助于反驳“帮助只改变两人当下状态”的表示，却不支持“传播越多越善”。单纯可想象的灾难与有机制支持、足以改变比较的损害应有不同的认识地位；区分二者的证据门槛仍需结合严重程度、可调查性和延迟后果辩护。

## Next discriminating connection

下一步宜检验 **同一支持活动怎样同时改变当前实现、后续能力、依赖结构与辨识后果的条件，进而产生不同的回应理由**。可以保持当前缓解需要的效果相近，比较两种后续安排：一种实际保留表达拒绝、改变支持或结束安排的条件；另一种使同等支持越来越取决于单一通道。必须同时计入第一种安排的学习负担、缓冲成本、可能失败，以及第二种安排可能有根据的稳定性和承诺。这里尚未断言前者总优。

这个比较有区分力之处在于：普遍伦理方向若能说明具体理由，应能说明何时能力与纠正条件的形成支持一种回应，何时这些条件的成本或无效性反而支持直接供给、稳定承担或正当结束。仅把这些目标交给稳健优化器，会重新供给本来需要辩护的伦理内容。原始文献已经帮助排除几种过快的推论，并指出可核查的关系；**从整体生成的伦理质量到有限参与者的充分实践根据，仍未因此完成。**
