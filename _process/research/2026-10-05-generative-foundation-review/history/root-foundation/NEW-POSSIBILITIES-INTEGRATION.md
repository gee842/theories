## Correspondence and dependency audit

The state coordinates are analytical descriptions within one process: number of chain links, loose parts, work stock and operative binding. Two bound parts constitute the modeled constructor connection; a further part constitutes each added link. Material accounting includes both. `Reaches` is a path built from consecutive links, and `canExtend` is checked equivalent to actual enabled extension. These meanings are specified before comparing novelty; novelty is not an unconstrained Boolean output. In the separate circuit interpretation, a contact's conditional transmission law and serial binding derive a joint-input function outside the complete factorization class for either single input. Material is counted relative to the new assembly: a still-unjoined contact may already conduct individually. Signal costs and timing remain abstracted.

The matched control equates material, work and existing chain length, but differs in binding. Part geometry, universal port compatibility along the chain, deterministic event laws, one-unit work accounting, absence of decay and the selected construction schedule remain supplied. The model explains formation of an operative constructor connection under these laws, not their physical origin. The endpoint assay is idealized and omits propagation resources and noise. The biological evidence is a scoped comparator, not a proved refinement into this state space.

Description adequacy is exact response agreement on a declared domain. Its failure does not demand a richer fundamental ontology; it may reveal an omitted relation in an otherwise adequate language. Introducing a new query requires an operational interpretation. Preserving old response meanings does not preserve every old prediction under changed conditions. Actual evidence acquisition, storage, access and maintenance remain separate interfaces, with the earlier acquired-abstraction work credited.
