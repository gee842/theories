# Endogenous formation and replacement of a mathematical-method connection

6 October 2026. AI-authored proof and executed construction for the stronger user-requested connection **initially absent selector wiring → local paid formation → acquired-evidence rejection → replacement → later traversal and mathematical use**.

The earlier [method-revision result](METHOD-REVISION-MATH.md) installed the update \(p=1-\text{receipt}\). This study removes that operative coupling from the entrance state. It supplies a generic constructor and component contracts, but no active receipt-to-generator edge. One continuing process forms an edge, tests the generated family against its own retained observations, removes a contradicted edge, forms another, retains it and later traverses it. The strict four-cycle witness actually tries \(G_0\) before rejecting and replacing it.

This is **autonomous connection formation relative to a declared boundary and law**. It does not establish autonomous origin of all generator code, construction laws, calibration, scheduling or fundamental physics. It extends the already assessed conditional foundation at the user's request; it adds no global completion criterion.

## 4. The generated families and exact six-bit test

For a machine \(M\), state \(q\) and constant reading parity \(b\), define

\[
\operatorname{sig}_6(M,q,b)
=\big(o_M(\delta_M^i(q))\oplus b\big)_{i=0}^{5}.
\tag{A5}
\]

The two generator families admit every phase and both parities. Let

\[
C_0=\{(H_3,q,b)\},\qquad
C_1=C_0\cup\{(\operatorname{Stut}(H_3),q,b)\},
\tag{A6}
\]

where the omitted ranges are all states of the named machine and \(b\in\{0,1\}\). They contain six and fourteen initial configurations respectively.

Replaying \(D\) returns **current** compatible configurations:

\[
B_j(D)=
\{(M,\delta_M^5(q),b):
(M,q,b)\in C_j,\ \operatorname{sig}_6(M,q,b)=D\}.
\tag{A7}
\]

The five advances matter: the returned cursor is at the archive's last reading, not its first.

### Proposition A1 — At most one survivor, and coverage of the declared two-machine family

For every binary word \(D\) of length six, each \(B_j(D)\) has at most one member. If the actual reading law is a member of \(C_1\) throughout acquisition, then \(B_1(D)\) is its singleton current configuration. Moreover:

- if the actual machine is \(H_3\), both \(B_0(D)\) and \(B_1(D)\) are that singleton;
- if it is the stutter machine, \(B_0(D)=\varnothing\) and \(B_1(D)\) is the actual singleton.

**Proof.** The fourteen signatures are pairwise distinct. Within either cycle, a full period distinguishes its phases and distinguishes its one-positive pattern from its complement. Across cycles, an uncomplemented three-cycle has two ones in six readings separated by three positions; a four-cycle has one or two ones, and two are separated by four. Complements obey the analogous zero pattern. Opposite parities have at most two versus at least four ones. Thus signatures cannot coincide. The actual acquisition supplies exactly its own signature, and five action steps give the current state in (A7). ∎

This proof justifies the implementation's single survivor register. Encountering a second survivor is an explicit violated-interface failure; the code does not silently overwrite one.

Truthful resource receipt is not a premise of A1. In these fixtures receipt error changes the addressed row, while stationary sensor/productivity flips are absorbed into the admitted reading parity. Sensor calibration and class coverage still matter: arbitrary observation faults or an omitted machine can defeat the inference.

## 5. Formation, testing and replacement

The local first proposal chooses \(G_0\) or \(G_1\) with equal probability, independently of the hidden apparatus. The two random-bit branches are exhaustively executed, not selected after seeing which succeeds.

Pay to form

\[
R_z\longrightarrow G_j
\tag{A8}
\]

in three new logical cells. Then pay to read that actual edge and dispatch the generator. Mark the invoked output in a paid tried mask. Construct the generated model when needed, enumerate its phase/parity candidates, and replay the stored bits. The rule is:

\[
B_j(D)=\varnothing
\ \Longrightarrow\
\text{pay to remove the contradicted edge and propose an untried output}.
\tag{A9}
\]

A nonempty result is accepted at the declared scope and the edge is paid to remain retained. H3 evidence may accept either output; the rule contains no bonus for the cheaper narrow generator.

### Proposition A2 — Termination and conditional correctness within two proposals

Assume A1's coverage/stability premises; faithful execution of formation, stored-record reads and compatibility tests; and enough resources/capacity for every required prefix. Starting with no edge, the process accepts a bound edge within at most two proposals. Its tested generated family contains the actual current reading configuration.

**Proof.** Each rejected output is recorded in the internal tried mask and cannot be proposed again. There are two outputs. A1 ensures \(G_1\)'s test is nonempty, so not both outputs can be rejected. Every accepted test has the actual unique configuration by A1. If \(G_0\) is first and the apparatus is the stutter machine, A1 forces its empty result; the sole untried output is then \(G_1\), which passes. ∎

The resource condition is substantive. The proof neither supplies free proposal attempts nor treats a post-payment balance as proof that every earlier guard was payable.

### Proposition A3 — A strict acquired-evidence replacement history exists

In the stutter world with first proposal \(G_0\), whenever the checked prefix is funded, the following events occur in one history:

**no edge → form \(R_z\to G_0\) → read and actually invoke \(G_0\) → derive an empty family from the retained record → pay to remove that edge → form \(R_z\to G_1\) → invoke and accept \(G_1\) → retain its edge.**

**Proof.** Formation and invocation follow the transition rules, not a desired-model verdict. A1 makes the first test empty. The paid removal and tried mask leave \(G_1\) as the sole eligible proposal. Its nonempty test yields acceptance by A2. The numerical witness below establishes the actual funding of these operations on the inherited reserve. ∎

This goes beyond changing an initialized bit before its first use. The first connection has governed a real generator invocation and has been contradicted by internally acquired evidence before it is replaced.

### Proposition A4 — Later edge traversal regenerates the same warranted model

After accepting an edge, discard the trial model and survivor allocation through a paid operation while preserving the original \(H_3\), receipt, archive and edge. If their meanings remain stable, the later paid edge traversal invokes the accepted generator again, reconstructs its family and replays \(D\), obtaining the same current compatible configuration.

**Proof.** The retained source still matches \(z\), so the edge returns its stored output port. The original \(H_3\) fixes the same generator semantics, and the unchanged \(D\) fixes the same set (A7). No native action intervenes between the archive and this regeneration. ∎

The original H3 is retained even if H4 becomes the selected model, because the stored generator descriptor is defined relative to H3. A paid forward action/read then tests use, updates the selected cursor and receives separate maintenance.

After that action, the archive still describes its original acquisition epoch. Replaying it alone would recover the pre-use cursor. The separately committed selected cursor represents the later state. This experiment does not implement an indefinite future-replay loop or silently import the external native trace to advance old evidence.

## 6. What makes the formation endogenous

The following relative criterion distinguishes the achieved result from merely installing the desired selector:

1. At the chosen initial boundary the operative selector path is absent.
2. Actual paid local events form its stored connections; no target-selected finished mapping is imported.
3. Proposals and acceptance use only declared accessible state, acquired events and retained evidence.
4. A later read traverses the formed path and enacts the corresponding procedure.
5. Removing, mismatching or changing that path changes the enacted behavior under controlled opportunities.
6. Construction, evidence, testing, replacement and use preserve one chronological material/resource account.

The producer satisfies this criterion for a single observed receipt row. The other row remains unformed. It does not generalize a total receipt-to-generator function from one addressed episode.

The controls locate the causal contribution. Clamped formation pays for its allocation but leaves no bound path, so dispatch stops. A source-port mismatch also stops. Breaking an accepted edge blocks its later traversal. Swapping the first destination changes the generator actually invoked; subsequent rejection, if any, concerns that invoked output. A direct fallback would invalidate these controls.

The receipt and sensor record have different roles: the receipt addresses the connection, while the sensor record warrants the compatibility rejection. Receipt inversion therefore need not change the accepted generator, whereas changing the six-bit record can. The former fixed \(1-z\) rule is not hidden in the binding chemistry.

### Proposition A5 — Fixed local laws can form a previously absent effective selector

Let \(T\) be a fixed transition law on states containing a graph \(E\). Let the executable-selector interface extracted from state \(x\) consist of graph paths actually present in \(E_x\). It is consistent that this interface is empty at \(x_0\), becomes nonempty at \(x_1\) after allowed formation steps, and later governs a different procedure, although \(T\) itself has not changed.

**Proof.** The present construction is an explicit witness: (A3), the paid insertion (A8), and the guarded traversal define the respective states and effects under one unchanged law. ∎

This does not mean the earlier state had fewer unrestricted all-time possibilities. If \(x_1\) is reachable from \(x_0\), transitivity gives \(\operatorname{Reach}_T(x_1)\subseteq\operatorname{Reach}_T(x_0)\). What has changed is the currently installed, affordable and operative path.

The regress boundary is therefore precise. Explaining formation of an effective selector need not require the fundamental evolution law to rewrite itself. Conversely, constructing one effective selector does not explain why its component semantics, proposal kernel or learning rule were available. “Autonomous” at this boundary means internally realized formation and revision under the stated laws, not independence from every antecedent or self-creation without conditions.

## 8. Verification and failures that delimit the claim

The [producer and saved results](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/methods/formation/README.md) preserve 35 fixtures, 448 declared model/phase/fault/proposal configurations and24 learned-state renamings. All448 admitted configurations complete with the correct future reading model;128 actually reject and replace their first connection. The static fault grid preserves all phases, both first proposals, and sensor, productivity, reset, blockage and receipt variants. It is a finite synthetic design, not an empirical distribution or a general performance guarantee.

The separately implemented [independent checker](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/methods/formation/independent_check.py) reconstructs the executions, resource/cell prefixes and rejection/traversal sequence; its [receipt](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/methods/formation/independent-check.json) records the checked source versions. These are collaborating-AI internal checks, not external peer review, new empirical observations or a new Lean proof.

The boundary controls remain substantive:

- A larger five-state process can survive the archive test and then contradict the subsequent prediction.
- A ten-state process matches both the six-bit record and the next reading, leaving a wrong accepted H3 model. Formation and internal testing do not certify the admitted class.
- Receipt-payment failure can occur after actual resource credit. No receipt register, complete archive or formed selector follows from the external audit's knowledge.
- Missing source, inadequate capacity and ten separately denied stages preserve their paid physical and representational prefixes.
- A native action followed by a denied cursor commit leaves the stored cursor behind the physical state.
- A denied maintenance fee is not a simulated erasure of every stored bit.

H3 accepts either recipe. Choosing G1 first there costs more; trying and rejecting G0 on H4 also costs more than beginning with G1. No extra criterion in this construction learns the cheapest or globally best method. Its improved coverage relative to the earlier short-audit trigger comes from a paid retained six-bit test under a known finite model family.

The next stronger claims remain separate: autonomous production of the component repertoire and construction law, calibration beyond the supplied interfaces, selection under unknown future demands, or sustained support after the last packet. The finite formation witness advances their interface without claiming to prove all of them, and establishes no unique constitution of every mathematical truth.
