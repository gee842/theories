## What the negative result changes

Repeated outcomes do not by themselves identify the mechanism reproducing their conditions. In one model the selected activity returns the support; in the rival, the current condition triggers a substitute source directly. The passive q histories agree at every time, while cutting the activity's return has different effects. A classifier of that history alone cannot distinguish the pair.

This is not a claim that all observations fail. It says that the observation channel must preserve the causal distinction. The counterexample rules out upgrading a recurrence score, apparent convergence or rising exit cost into an unconditional diagnosis. Once source correspondence, a calibrated state interval, timing and intervention feasibility are supplied, the interval theorem gives a constructive uniform correction certificate.

真实的识别障碍是：相同被动轨迹可以由不同的回返来源产生。扩大词汇或继续拟合同一观测不能消除这一差别；须取得来源关系、干预或其他能区分机制的证据。这个结论不否定元吸引子，也不将整个理论宣布为不可检验。

## Further applications and the inference boundary

1. **Other carrier architectures.** The current material construction allows two productive variants and proves preservation of their relevant causal roles. Biological assembly, institutional succession and other architectures need their own signatures and realization evidence. Existing material formation studies supply possible components, but their histories cannot simply be cited as this implementation.
2. **Observation with causal provenance.** Existing market variables have an operational protocol, but the new model has no selected measured counterpart. A real diagnosis needs linked records of outputs, selection-condition change and carrier succession, plus a credible counterfactual route comparison. Open literature supports neighboring mechanisms without calibrating this construction.
3. **Reachable interventions in an observed process.** The new model executes its route cut and repair with represented stocks and recorded consequences. A field implementation additionally needs warranted route correspondence, timing, authority, actual effects and support. Neither the interval theorem nor the new trace certifies these from repeated observations alone.

These are further realization questions, not additional gates silently appended to a conceptual exploration.
