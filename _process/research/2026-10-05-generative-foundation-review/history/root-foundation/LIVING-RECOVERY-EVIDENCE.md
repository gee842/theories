# Recovery after departure from a maintained condition

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored continuation of the [living-dependency mechanisms study](LIVING-DEPENDENCY-MECHANISMS.md). This round deepens its existing recovery evidence to assess a formal extension allowing temporary departures and later endpoint restoration. It does not introduce another ethical requirement or claim a complete mathematical model of biological recovery.

**Result:** the evidence supports retaining a distinction between departure from a maintained condition and irreversible loss of living capacity. However, recovery must be specified by its actual functional outcome. A proof about returning a represented amount below a bound is not automatically a proof of resumed life, restoration of the original physiological regime, or preservation through every intervening moment.

**Evidence:** after 14.4 hours of starvation, fresh medium containing 0.2% glucose was supplied. Cell length, membrane-potential indicator DiBac4(3), and PI staining were tracked; 5,256 cells with at least 2.5 hours of follow-up entered the analysis. The authors report repolarization and resumed growth in some depolarized cells. Recovered depolarized cells below the PI threshold had depolarized 1–2 hours before supplementation. No cell classified as PI-positive recovered in this assay. Some initially polarized cells subsequently depolarized. Recovery was assessed within the observation period; this is not a universal rescue deadline. The accessed account does not establish return to the prior starvation volume or plasmolysis, a matched-cell colony assay, or a direct ATP-flux measurement. The precise length-trace recovery classifier was not verified.

No experiment or analysis of the authors' numerical records was performed. This is a deeper reading of the same study, not independent new empirical evidence.

## 2. What a recovery claim must preserve

The question is not whether a marker can be assigned a favorable value again. It is whether the same continuing organization retains or reconstructs the relations through which the specified further living activity occurs. Which observations establish that claim depends on the target.

The following is our correspondence assessment. It separates the content needed by a biological interpretation from the relations present in the proposed mathematical representation.

| Candidate interpretation | What must be established |
|---|---|
| Restoration of one represented variable | Its value at the endpoint satisfies the chosen condition; the account retains the actual path and transfers. |
| Return to the original maintained regime | The target's meaning and relevant conditions are still those of the original regime, rather than being replaced by a different mode of activity. |
| Recovery into another viable regime | Identify the new regime's operative conditions, how it is reached, and how its subsequent activity is realized. |
| Continued capacity despite a temporary departure | Establish what remains capable of recovery during the interval; endpoint accounting alone does not identify it. |
| Irreversible loss | Identify a loss of the relevant realization possibilities, with the claimed intervention and temporal scope explicit. A threshold label alone is insufficient. |

This distinction matters even when the same mathematical bound is used at both ends. Its unchanged numeral does not establish unchanged physiological meaning. A changed organization can realize continued life differently; conversely, restoring one quantity can leave other indispensable relations destroyed. The appropriate representation must retain both possibilities.

## 3. Active support and passive relief are different contributions

The proposed [LivingRecovery module](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/LivingRecovery.lean) distinguishes passive inward transfer, passive outward transfer, active export and work-support. This is useful because actual relief need not all be attributed to one active mechanism.

A mathematical witness using passive relief tests the necessity of an overstrong claim that every successful endpoint requires new work-support. It is not a report that the biological recovery assay demonstrated that mechanism. Conversely, a response compatible with active work does not show which fraction of recovery is explained by each transport channel. That allocation requires additional mechanism and measurement.

The evidence assessed here motivates representing recoverability and changed support. It does not instantiate every field of that formal law. In particular, an empirical interpretation still needs the correspondence between the represented amount, its bound, transport magnitudes, work conversion and continuing functional organization. Naming a field “recovery” would not supply those connections.

## 4. Endpoint accounting and the path to it

The proposed extension allows intermediate states outside the selected endpoint bound. That removes an inappropriate requirement when the inquiry concerns restoration after departure. It also makes the retained scope especially important: a necessary budget for an endpoint is not a sufficient construction of that endpoint, and endpoint admissibility is not proof of uninterrupted functioning or continuous-time safety.

A longer recovery window may contain loss, compensation, changed support and reorganization. These must occur on the actual shared history. Restoring the initial state by resetting the model would erase precisely the loss and support relations the investigation is meant to explain.

An irreversible-loss relation is not supplied merely by omitting intermediate bounds. A biological realization would need to connect structural integrity, functional capacity, and the available interventions to the allowed transitions. Without that connection, the correct claim is a constraint on represented histories meeting the endpoint condition, not a theorem that a damaged organism can recover.

## 5. The stochastic correspondence remains separate

The previous scientific account includes a white-noise stochastic differential equation. The new proof uses finite nonnegative transfer quantities over discrete transitions. These are different representations. No integration scheme, limiting argument, probability model or pathwise correspondence has been established between them.

Accordingly, favorable stochastic effects cannot simply be renamed passive outward transfer and treated as if that completed a refinement. A finite physical transport model can be useful in its own right; it must state which actual transfers its fields represent. Its deterministic necessary conditions also do not give a recovery probability or an individual biological deadline.

This note asserts no new mathematical result about stochastic paths. It records the missing correspondence and preserves the distinction between the published model, the observations, and the independently proposed discrete law.

## 6. Contribution and remaining test

This round supports a more adequate living-dependency target: **retained organization can permit recovery after departure from one operative regime, and support can be changed in realizing that recovery.** Whether the result restores the old regime or establishes another one must be stated.

The next discriminating correspondence is to identify an independently measured functional recovery endpoint and the relations that remain recoverable along its path. One variable's restoration should then be tested as a necessary condition, sufficient certificate, or imperfect proxy, according to the evidence. The present reading does not settle that classification universally.

Formal claims belong to the final source-bound verification report. This note does not claim that a proposed proof has already passed checking. It also does not treat a checked transport budget as a complete biological rescue theorem.
