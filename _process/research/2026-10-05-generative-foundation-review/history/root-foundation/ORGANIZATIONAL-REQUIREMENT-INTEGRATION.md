## What changes in the derivation

The existing account should not be described as beginning with an inert plant
and then requiring a separate evaluator to make anything matter to its activity.
Its native organization already couples changed conditions to responses that
change those conditions again. Damage, available precursor, resident templates
and occupied stores participate in the same reactions. The analyst's reported
copy count does not govern their propensities.

This is **operative significance in a restricted causal sense**: a difference
changes what the organization does and what it can subsequently do. The account
does not thereby identify felt significance, a represented purpose or ethical
warrant. These distinctions concern explanatory questions within the unfolding;
they do not make three fundamentally independent kinds of reality.

The [earlier regulation result](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REGULATION-RETENTION-INTEGRATION.md)
already connected damage-coupled response formation, repair, capture and further
response copying. The [formed-variant result](../../../history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md)
then joined actual formation to differentiated reconstruction. We credit those
positive connections rather than create another controller to fill an invented
absence. This round clarifies which kind of continuation the actual coupling
can enact, and where its own revision is absent from one local apparatus.

## Restoration must be identified through its actual role

Vacancy-sensitive copying responds to available precursor and the present
template. It need not reconstruct the kind that disappeared. This defeats the
specific inference from restored occupation to restored type composition;
it does not make the previous composition a universal goal. The existing
[material-selector composition](../../../../theories/becoming/history/MATERIAL-SELECTOR-COMPOSITION.md)
already gives H constituents a new role in a mapping productive after regime
reversal. Which relation needs maintaining can change as organization changes.

Similarly, repair without source cannot restore source capture under this law.
It may still have another relevant role in a richer identified continuation.
Do not infer universal uselessness from this absent return. Completion can also
end a response appropriately: repair removes its own damaged-site condition;
filling a store removes its capture condition. Continued generation does not
require every particular response to remain active.

These are genuine differences in actual activity, not freely toggled predicates
on an unchanged phenomenon. They let the branch correct the core's articulation:
**activity can change the conditions, scope and occasion of further activity**.
This is broader than endlessly maintaining an existing thing. It still leaves
the origin and ethical quality of particular arrangements to explain.

## The next explanatory connection and its limits

For this material branch the specific candidate join is **native consequences →
retention or reconstruction of the formed control coupling → changed later
activity**. It should explain an actual component's changing relations and
availability, using the existing learned history and its occupied parts. A new
task flag or an arbitrary number of maintenance calls would mainly repeat a
prescribed controller. A demonstrated consequence-dependent replacement or
reorganization would advance this branch. A newly identified admitted rewriting
event would revise the lifecycle diagnosis instead.

At the foundation, the task remains to explain how such organizations and their
response scopes form, transform and become significant within activity. Local
laws and recipes are supplied to this representation; their provenance is an
unresolved connected explanation, not evidence that they exist outside
generation. No extra demand for a meta-controller at every level follows.

of warranted significance through whole continuations. It should not become an
