# Acquired distinctions within changing participation

6 October 2026. AI-authored identification argument under [Ren's nondual correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-core-tree-nondual-trajectory-correction.md) and the [shared method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The preceding [applicability round](NONDUAL-APPLICABILITY-INTEGRATION.md) made verified progress; it did not complete the foundation. This study addresses **formed participation → acquired distinctions of consequence → changed continuation**. Existing source laws and results are known before this reconstruction. Proposed empirical contrasts below have not been run.

## Begin with the continuing configuration

The explanatory object is a situated organization acting, being changed by the consequences, and subsequently acting differently through what has changed. A record, an effector, a resource return and an affected recipient identify relations in that history. They need not be initially independent entities joined by an outside observer. Nor does calling their shared process generative remove the obligation to explain the actual relationships.

The [material learner](SELF-ACQUISITION-BRIDGE.md) already connects actual formation, paid acquisition, changed command and support renewal. A stronger existing [formed-architecture recipient study](../../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/learning/README.md) also connects a retained graph, constructed return path, acquired recipient-specific response and later recipient effect. These achievements must constrain the next question. Treating their established connections as still absent would manufacture a gap.

Three uses of “participation” require different evidence:

| Description | What can change | What its observation alone does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Material organization | Components, connections, conductance, geometry, maintained records | Which available relation is actually exercised now, or whether a change was acquired through the relevant consequences. |
| Available interaction | Actions or paths the current configuration can support under stated circumstances | Which action occurs, what it affects, or what history selected it. |
| Enacted participation | The actual command, engaged route, current encounter and resulting effects | That the organization has acquired a faithful general model of other possibilities. |

These are analytical descriptions of the same course, not three ontological layers. Fixed graph topology can coexist with changed conductance, retained state, command, flow and affected continuation. A requirement to rewire edges before recognizing learned participation would exclude real changes by definition. Conversely, a new wire installed by a scheduled construction event does not demonstrate that acquired evidence selected its installation.

## What makes the distinction acquired and operative

For this inquiry, specify the consequence independently of the desired cognitive description: actual receipt, usable support, transported material, issued command or a measured effect on the addressed recipient. Establish what the retained configuration records through its actual formation history and what it does in later activity. An analyst's accuracy score is evidence about the proposed account; it must not secretly become the update signal.

An adequate identification should answer four connected questions:

1. Which actual earlier encounter changed a retained material configuration, with its payments, timing and failures preserved?
2. What difference from that encounter survives, and what transformation would instead erase, corrupt or alter it?
3. How does that retained difference affect subsequent action or coupling under specified present opportunities?
4. What consequence does the subsequent activity actually have, including effects that differ from the apparent success measure?

This is an evidential contract, not a universal definition of consciousness or an extra learning law. Its parts can be realized in the same structure. A tube's changed geometry can influence the next flow; a materially backed register can influence the next command. Neither must first become a detached symbolic account of an outside world. Simple hysteresis can satisfy some of these relations, so the inquiry must state what stronger sensitivity or generalization it means by learning and test that claim separately.

Matching “present conditions” in a causal contrast cannot mean matching the entire physical state including the proposed memory. That would remove the very difference under investigation. Specify the current stimulus, support opportunities, relevant resources and action repertoire being compared, and identify the retained mediator being allowed to differ. Differences in an affected recipient produced by the earlier history must remain in the account, even if they do not feed back into this particular local controller.

No one successful endpoint establishes all four relations. A preconfigured command can succeed; a misleading channel can accidentally select a useful action; accurate acquired knowledge can become unaffordable to use. Their distinction concerns actual provenance, causal use and current conditions, not the attachment of different success labels to an unchanged whole situation.

## What the existing controls already discriminate

The existing architecture-learning study supplies a particularly useful controlled contrast. The selected actual predecessor retains the same graph, inherited actor response, stocks and a spare connector. Faithful and paid absent-feedback arms preserve the reported actor expenditure and final retained graphs, while their acquired recipient records and subsequent effects differ. The original private-zero control can reproduce a favorable recipient outcome without faithful recipient evidence. These are already published repository results; [the mechanism audit](PARTICIPATION-CONSEQUENCE-MECHANISM.md) locates the exact sources and retained histories.

Together they support a specific causal conclusion: in this law, the consequence channel can change what later recipient command is enacted, beyond what is explained by actor fuel or mere retention of the same architecture. They also reject the inference that a favorable recipient outcome alone proves faithful learning. The controls do not prove that all other aspects of the recipient's history are identical, or that private selection will favor faithful recipient evidence. The source study explicitly finds no private-fuel or retention advantage for that channel in the matched primary comparison.

The return wire, addressed memory recipe and candidate update remain supplied local mechanisms. Its construction event occurs before recipient-specific trials; the experiment does not show recipient evidence autonomously constructing its own sensor or target categories. The successful path inherits a normal motor coupler. With the declared resources, the original experiment assigns zero primary mass to the stronger conjunction of earlier motor revision and the entire strict recipient-learning continuation. Retaining that failure prevents a sequence of separate successes from being reported as one completed lineage.

## Structural memory as a materially different comparison

The [primary-source comparison](PARTICIPATION-CONSEQUENCE-COMPARATORS.md) examines nutrient-associated remodeling in a living flow network. Its relevance is structural: the material changed by past interaction can itself participate in subsequent transport. This makes it a candidate empirical realization of the same acquisition/use relation without requiring separate memory and actuator components.

The case does not settle the whole identification contract merely by being biological. A historical imprint, an effect of current geometry, and proof that the earlier encounter caused that later effect carry different evidential burdens. A current network's structure may reflect multiple prior influences. The paper's measured changes, mechanistic model and unperformed history-control comparison must therefore be reported separately. The comparison is a substantive test of the framework's scope, not evidence that nature implements the repository's Boolean program.

The strongest rival can preserve a purely physicochemical account of the complete process. If it explains the same acquired persistence and later causal use, it is not refuted by renaming those changes cognition. Equally, physical realization does not defeat a limited functional memory claim. The discriminating question is which specified historical and future-response relations actually hold. Claims of conceptual understanding, conscious significance, learning a new target identity or moral concern need further evidence.

## Revision conditions and the remaining foundational task

Withdraw a claimed acquired distinction if the relevant record is installed correctly before the encounter or merely inferred by the analyst. Withdraw a claimed causal use if changing or withholding the proposed mediator leaves the stated later response unchanged under the relevant controls. Narrow a claim that observations identify faithful learning if a proxy explains those same observations; independent source and provenance evidence can still establish the faithful channel. Narrow the claimed relation if staleness changes its consequence or unsupported counterfactual generalization does the explanatory work. Retain actual costs and harmful effects even when the selected task succeeds.

This narrows the next derivation without imposing a new requirement that every learner explicitly classify self versus world. Acquired target individuation is one stronger question; consequence-sensitive control of an already addressed interaction is another, partially established one. For the recipient branch, the preceding credit audit also shows why the recipient's own continuation must eventually enter the explanation: a delivered quantity alone does not describe its use, restoration, renewed dependence or experience.

The outstanding connected explanation concerns how relevant access, target differentiation and practices of acquisition form and become revisable through their actual consequences, including consequences for other centers. It must explain constructive and destructive formation together. The proposed framework cannot count every successful private adaptation as aligned merely because both actor and recipient occur within one unfolding. Whether a response is warranted belongs within this same investigation, with its actual grounds and conflicts preserved; functional learning does not settle it by itself.

This round adds a more discriminating interpretation and cross-domain identification, while crediting the existing operational bridge. It introduces no event law, theorem or completed empirical intervention. The full no-assumptions objective, formation of the local laws, experiential realization and justified concern remain open.
