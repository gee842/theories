# Shared affective responses and recipient-directed action

6 October 2026. AI-authored identification audit of **own/other consequence representation → evaluative influence → recipient-directed action**. This examines the supplied transfer route in [evaluative uptake](../../../../theories/becoming/history/EVALUATIVE-UPTAKE.md). It complements the already reanalyzed [recipient-harm choices](../../../../theories/becoming/history/RECIPIENT-HARM-AND-ACTION.md), without treating two studies as one observed learning history.

## Primary-source summary — under 100 words

Wu et al. studied female rats and putatively matched neurons across helping and later laser-pain tests. Selection was 120 trained →33 openers implanted →26 good recordings →16 retaining opening →12 with stable cross-task units. Helping preceded self-pain testing. Figure 7 pooled seven mirror and seven anti-mirror units; their animal-level mapping is unpublished. Reported mirror activity preceded release; anti-mirror evidence concerned nose interaction. Motor-cortex activity preceded control openings too. Rat/toy/empty conditions were randomized. Food trained gate-opening. Data are available upon request, with figures and illustrative videos public. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2022.105865).

## What this connects, and what it leaves supplied

There is a substantive same-experiment bridge here: a recorded neural phenotype is related to an action that opens a real recipient's restrainer. It is stronger evidence of contact between representation and action than merely observing overlapping brain-region activation in separate populations. Nevertheless, putative cross-task unit identity is an empirical matching claim, not guaranteed cell continuity. An aggregate peri-event response is also not an animal-level estimate showing that animals with more shared response help more.

The temporal order prevents interpreting the experiment as an intervention in which the measured firsthand pain episodes teach a value that subsequently produces helping. It does not prove absence of earlier self-related learning in the animals' lives. Their prior history and the mapping from shared neural response to evaluative influence remain to be identified. Nor does a response that comes before an opening demonstrate that it causes that opening: a common process can drive both.

Food-based skill acquisition establishes an instrumental route to opening a gate. It need not teach the recipient's benefit directly, but it also means gate-opening is not a behavior beginning without reward history. Recipient release, anticipated social contact, contextual salience and actor relief can all influence the learned action. Internal affect alone does not make the motive selfish; conversely, calling a neural response “mirror” does not establish that it tracks the recipient's welfare when sensory relief and actual recipient benefit diverge.

## Independent units and assignment

Animal, neuron, session and opening event must remain distinct units. Cells within an animal share history and physiological state; multiple openings share the same actor and potentially the same recipient. An inference across animals must cluster or summarize at the animal level, preserving recipient pairing where known. Counting all cells or events as independent actors would supply precision the design has not earned.

The selection chain above conditions the neural helping result on successful training, recording quality, retained behavior and unit stability. A reanalysis should preserve the enrollment and exclusion ledger instead of silently generalizing the surviving sample to all trained rats. It should not further select animals by stronger conspecific preference, or discard failed openings when testing whether activity predicts selection.

Randomized object conditions help with local within-animal comparisons; they do not randomize a mirror-cell phenotype, a learned evaluative route or the animal's history. Before any reanalysis, the actual scheduling file would need to resolve trial-level versus block-level randomization and carryover. The per-cell animal mapping and inclusion criteria are necessary to determine whether an apparent phenotype difference is carried by a few animals or by selection of particular behaviors.

## Discriminating predictions

| Candidate claim | What the available evidence can bear |
| --- | --- |
| Shared response can be associated with an action benefiting another | This has direct reported empirical contact; it is not merely an analogy to shared representations. |
| Every social response is equivalent to recipient-directed help | The reported difference between interaction and release makes that indiscriminate prediction inadequate. |
| The reported neural pattern is entirely a generic gate-opening signal | The object and motor comparisons challenge this simple description locally; attention, expected contact and action preparation remain viable explanations. |
| A shared response necessarily produces helping | Neither necessity nor sufficiency follows from an ensemble association; individual cells, non-openings and perturbations matter. |
| Measured self experience caused acquisition of the recipient value | Not identified by this task order. A compatible transfer route is not its demonstrated developmental origin. |
| Anti-mirror activity is negative moral concern | Unsupported: excitation/inhibition correspondence does not supply the sign of a downstream value or its ethical meaning. |

The strongest causal test would alter an independently identified shared route while preserving recipient sensing, factual efficacy knowledge, motor capacity and private opportunities. A selective change in recipient-benefiting action would then need to survive controls for attention, actor cue relief and contact reward. Region-wide perturbation alone would not uniquely isolate that route. This is an identification requirement, not a proposed new animal experiment or a claim that the required intervention exists in this paper.

The published tests around Figures 6–7 compare responses with baseline within conditions. A significant response in one condition and a nonsignificant response in another do not themselves establish a statistically supported condition difference. The direct paired contrast below is therefore needed before treating the reported specificity as an independently demonstrated interaction. A null anti-mirror result is not proof that those cells have no causal contribution.

## Feasible retrospective contrast, conditional on obtaining data

A narrow initial analysis could compare each matched unit's pre-opening activity against its own baseline in rat, toy and empty conditions, then compare these within-unit contrasts across mirror phenotypes. Classification must be independent of the helping data and uncertainty in phenotype/unit matching retained. Separate excitation and inhibition patterns instead of assuming that pooling opposite response signs measures a single evaluative quantity. Use actor-level resampling or explicitly hierarchical inference, with leave-one-animal-out checks and all failed opportunities retained for a separate action-probability analysis.

This could test whether action-related neural activity distinguishes the recipient context beyond recorded motor/contact covariates. It would remain correlational. A stronger held-out prediction of opening versus non-opening would require comparable predecision windows for every opportunity, without selecting windows retrospectively around actions that already occurred. Neither comparison identifies an ethical ground or proves a uniquely shared-value mechanism.

**Required fields, not an observed public schema:** animal and recipient IDs; enrollment/exclusion reasons; electrode/region/unit identifiers; waveform and stability evidence across tasks; trial/block order; pain-stimulus and behavior timestamps; all opportunities and opening outcomes; condition identities; spike times or declared bins; movement/contact covariates; and unit-classification code. None should be invented from figure labels.

## Access and preservation

The [source package](../../../../sources/32-shared-affect-action-2026-10-06/README.md) preserves Wu's complete [Europe PMC XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9826941/fullTextXML), with HTTP provenance and article license. Reading covered the main results, Figure 6–7 captions, limitations, experimental subjects, helping/distress procedures, quantification and data statement. Direct PMC web opening produced a browser check; publisher opening returned 403. The indexed primary text and official XML supplied access.

No public trial/neuron table, data dictionary or unit-to-animal join was located. The XML lists `mmc1.pdf` and two video supplements; their listed roles are figures and illustrations, not a complete dataset. Exact-title/DOI searches did not locate another public archive. This is a bounded availability finding, not proof that data cannot be obtained. We did not contact the authors, download raw recordings/videos, digitize figure averages or calculate new outcome aggregates. No retrospective numerical reanalysis is presently justified.

Carrillo 2019 and Schneider 2020 are independently reviewed companion comparators. Their primary documents are preserved in the same minimal source package; their different tasks and perturbations must not be concatenated with Wu's observations into a single causal experiment. The central construction's learned register, cross-center route, costs and normative application remain separately supplied or established according to their own contracts.
