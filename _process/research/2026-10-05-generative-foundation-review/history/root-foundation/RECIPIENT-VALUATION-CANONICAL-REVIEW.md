## Interpretation accepted, with one bounded clarification

The report preserves the physical consequence in the original protocol: an actor's response could deliver a shock to its neighboring recipient. Absence of released per-event hardware receipts limits independently reconstructing delivery; it does not turn this apparatus into a symbolic score task. The report also keeps actual recipient experience distinct from physical exposure and does not infer total shocks from free-choice counts while ignoring forced trials.

The primary quantity is incidence per offered opportunity, with alternative action and nonperformance separately retained. The report makes no randomized-treatment, independent-cage, population-confidence or latent-preference claim. Baseline selection, disjoint undocumented SBL labels, unmatched female controls, selected yoking and unequal-force histories remain visible. Emotional mediation inside the actor is appropriately distinguished from a conclusion of selfishness. The model interface does not infer acquisition of `w`, `g`, shared evaluation, ethical standing or justified action from a changed lever response. The prior-experience comparison is useful evidence about the proposed transfer route while retaining its post-exposure baseline and alternative mechanisms.

One small clarification was requested: the three-pellet value of −0.76 percentage points is a **known-record preferred-response frequency change**, with one offered slot unknown. It is a lower bound on the fully observed preferred-incidence change; it should not be called a fully observed incidence estimate without that qualification. The report already preserves the blank and its three hypothetical completions elsewhere. No change to data, estimator or numerical results is required.

**Verdict:** numerically and conceptually accepted within its stated descriptive scope, subject to that presentation clarification. This adds bounded empirical evidence about external social consequences and changing action; it neither proves nor dissolves the remaining acquisition and ethical-ground obligations. A final report hash and closure of the wording correction will be recorded below.
