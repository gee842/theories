# Uniform assembly as a relative ground of mathematical admission

7 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. AI-authored philosophical argument continuing the [admission attempt](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-ADMISSION-ARGUMENT.md) and its [counterreview](ROOT-MATHEMATICAL-ADMISSION-REVIEW.md). The new proposal is **constitutive abstraction from an independently formed relation**, rather than another appeal to maximality. Mathematical calibration and boundary claims belong to the accompanying checked indexed-assembly development; the philosophical warrant remains distinct from its formal consequences.

## 1. A positive reason for the assembly rule

Suppose an index domain and a relation connecting its positions to a second domain are independently admitted. Write the relation as `R(i,x)`. An indexed union asks whether some position `i` is related to `x`. It retains incidence while omitting which index witnesses it. Index, membership and result are differentiated roles within this relational organization, following the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md); they are not separate substances subsequently joined by an observer.

The strongest affirmative argument is that this projection introduces **no independent membership choices**. Once the whole relation and its index range are fixed, the proposed result is fixed through them. Admitting the projected predicate need not add a bearer, a new membership assignment or a separate truth oracle. It recognizes a derivative role in already admitted organization.

Why should that role count as a mathematical object? For this extensional predicate subject, being an object means having the determinate membership role appropriate to substitution, comparison and quantification. The argument proposes that an independently constituted relation can ground such a derivative role through an explicit operation. Its justification is not that nothing prohibits the result. It is that the result's content and its dependence are specified together by the relation's incidence structure.

This supplies a substantive reason for accepting **relative projection closure**: when the index and whole indexed relation have been justified, accept their incidence projection as an extensional predicate. The membership requires no further selection beyond that relation, and the stated operation explains which memberships result.

The [relational-assembly formalization](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/assembly/formal/RelationalAssembly.lean) tests the identity mechanism directly. Its carrier consists of witness occurrences in the supplied relation. Quotienting by equal output forgets the index while retaining exactly the projected support; the inverse maps and `projection_iff_output` verify this correspondence. `support_admitted` preserves both the support carrier and its actual output map. It assumes whole-witness admission, output-preserving quotient closure and closure under inverse correspondences commuting with the outputs. Thus the membership role is retained. Inclusion in an independently selected predicate-quantifier range still requires the philosophical role-admission argument.

There is nevertheless a premise to defend. Determinate content and admission to a quantifier domain are different roles. A language can permit a formula without treating its extension as an object of every relation sort. The affirmative argument favors admitting the derivative predicate in a sort intended to contain extensional consequences of its admitted relations. It does not prove that every mathematically legitimate sort already has that intended closure. The philosophical commitment is explicit: independently grounded relational consequences may constitute derivative mathematical objects at the appropriate level.

## 2. Why independent formation changes the explanation

Uniformity alone is too weak. Every arbitrary subset can occur as a parameter in a uniform-looking membership clause. A single expression does not establish that its parameters or its whole relation were independently formed. The relevant distinction is between applying an admitted assembly rule and explaining the admission of its arguments.

The favorable case has an independently described index, a relation formed from already warranted operations, and an assembly operation whose incidence meaning is fixed. Here the explanation can run forward without first specifying the desired infinite membership pattern. The result is a consequence of that formation rather than the input used to select it.

The less favorable case starts with the target predicate, uses it to specify which finite pieces appear at each index, and then reconstructs that same predicate through their union. This is legitimate mathematics and useful representation. It does not independently explain admission of the target-dependent family. Moving its selection into a function argument does not reverse that explanatory direction.

“Independent” means independent of importing the content being explained, not detached from reality or without commitments. The natural numbers, their operations and the interpretation of existential projection remain resources to account for. This step can reduce a particular admission burden without deriving those resources from nothing.

## 3. Calibrate with the even-number formation

The even-number example is useful because it has a formation rule that does not ask an arbitrary predicate for membership answers. Begin with the admitted natural-number indexing and arithmetic operations. A finite stage forms an initial collection of doubled indices; a later stage extends that collection by the same operation. The assembled predicate is then governed by this common formation, rather than by an independently supplied table of even-number membership.

The [indexed-assembly development](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/constitution/assembly/formal/IndexedAssembly.lean) checks the finite stages, their increase and their assembled content through `parity_stage_membership`, `parity_stages_increasing` and `parity_union`. The relational module's `parity_iff_output` connects this exact recurrence to the witness quotient. The separate `parity_admitted` theorem assumes unrestricted chain closure; that strong antecedent does not justify itself. The bounded argument uses the recurrence and exact union together with the separately defended local projection principle.

The philosophical gain is a **relative admission explanation**. Given the accepted indexing and arithmetic formation, the rule determines the whole selected family without another unrelated choice at each position. Relative projection closure then recognizes its derived incidence predicate. The finite/cofinite restriction previously excluded the evens; this route explains a concrete stronger admission by naming the relation and operation that warrant it.

It does not require every stage to have physically occurred before the mathematical relation is admitted. It does require an account of the completed or schematic range over which the formation rule is interpreted. Acceptance of unbounded arithmetic and uniform rule interpretation is doing work. Consequently the example supports a bounded generative explanation; it does not settle the universal warrant for infinity or arbitrary completed families.

## 4. Test the move from this success to all subsets

The tempting generalization is that natural-number indexing is already admitted, every stage can be finite, and each chain can be described uniformly. Perhaps nothing further is required to obtain every subset of the natural numbers.

The checked boundary targets exactly that inference. **Given admission of every finite predicate**, closure under every increasing natural-number-indexed chain of finite predicates has full predicate strength on the natural numbers (`full_iff_finite_chain`). For an arbitrary target predicate, its bounded prefixes supply the relevant chain. The index and every stage are small in the specified senses; selection of the entire family still depends on the target predicate.

This result does not invalidate the relative projection rule. It distinguishes the rule from the range of arguments to which it is applied. Justifying a particular independently formed chain permits that assembly. Justifying all such ambient selected chains permits full admission. The former does not supply the latter merely because both use the same index type and union operation.

To complete full predicate admission through this route, the root must therefore justify every required whole indexing relation independently of the target answers. A finite rule with previously admitted parameters may succeed for particular subjects. Treating every arbitrary predicate as an available parameter would reinstate the full admission commitment. No claim that finite rules exhaust mathematical possibility is needed to see the difference.

The new attempt thus identifies a usable positive principle and its actual application discipline. It does not turn countable indexing into a reduced-strength foundation for the full powerset. The earlier closure result remains credited; the present test concerns whether an independently constituted argument can supply what a universal closure schema leaves assumed.

## 5. Necessity against the matched rival

A structural account can accept the same arithmetic formation, independently admitted relation, existential projection and resulting predicate. It can agree that the derivative role requires no additional membership choices. Its explanation says that the subject's relational structure warrants the projection; the generative account explains the structure through its formation and continuing articulation within the whole.

The affirmative account now has more to offer than a relabeling. Where the family is actually derived from a common formation rule, that rule explains its indefinitely many related instances without taking the completed target pattern as an independent input. An alternative treating every such instance as unrelated misses the connection being explained. This is a genuine explanatory achievement within the declared subject.

The matched rival need not make that mistake. It can preserve the same common rule and the same derivative dependence while declining to identify them with the unique ultimate generative root. The local success then favors a shared relational explanation, but does not establish the proposed root's exclusive priority. That further step requires either deriving the remaining structural resources or showing that the rival's complete account has the same dependence under a justified comparison.

## 6. Exact outcome of this attempt

The affirmative result is a defended **relative** principle: an independently warranted whole relation can ground its incidence projection as a derivative extensional object, without a further independent selection of the result's memberships. The even-number formation provides a concrete calibration of the needed independence. This is philosophical justification supported by checked mathematics, not a Lean theorem that the minimal root necessitates the principle.

The attempted universal inference remains unsupported at a specific point: availability of every whole indexed relation has not been derived. The natural-number chain boundary prevents confusing a simple index and finite stages with a weak family-admission requirement. Unique constitution likewise remains open against the rival that preserves the common formation and projection explanation.

Further progress through this route must therefore enlarge the independently justified repertoire of whole relations, or establish a general admission principle from separately defended root content. Repeating the projection operation on arbitrarily supplied families would leave that work undone. The current attempt earns a relative explanation and a precise test of its extension; it does not complete full mathematical admission or necessary root dependence.
