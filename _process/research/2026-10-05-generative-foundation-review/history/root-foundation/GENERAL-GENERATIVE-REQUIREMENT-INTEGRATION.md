## The mathematical reason, rather than a list of cases

The [dependency proof](SITUATED-RENEWAL-PROOF.md) begins with one local law for each represented support relation. When its route stays fixed, endpoint support plus use is at most prior support plus actual additions. Compose the inequality over a finite actual window. If neither addition nor route change occurs, total demand is bounded by inherited support. Demand beyond that bound contradicts the absence of a response. If the inherited route cannot add support, the response must change it.

This argument applies to every dependency index and every actual window satisfying the law. It allows idle steps and different response times and contributors. It does not assume a common reserve for the whole, novelty at every step or an inexhaustible source.

The implementation proof supplies the other direction under stronger premises. Every permitted actual outcome retains the certificate's invariant and either realizes an independently identified renewal event or lowers a natural-number progress rank. An actual compliant history therefore cannot postpone renewal beyond the bound. Explicit admissible successor and event functions then construct such a history from its initial state. The same reached states carry subsequent steps and all indexed dependencies.

The rank and remaining support serve different claims. The dependency theorem derives necessary responses from demand accounting; the implementation theorem certifies that a chosen law of action actually retains the stated conditions and makes progress. The same-history theorem takes an existing continuation plus the demand law and locates all required dependency responses on that history. It does not identify the implementation certificate's renewal predicate with the dependency law's response predicate. An insufficient response can satisfy the necessity disjunction while failing the implementation certificate.

The checked idle, alternative-route and permanent-opportunity constructions test the boundaries. They show respectively why dependency identification is needed, why sufficient recipes are alternatives, and why always keeping a completion available does not make it occur. Their role is to expose false stronger claims. The proofs' validity does not depend on sampling enough examples.

## The ethical argument and its review

The [affirmative argument](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-ARGUMENT.md#4-the-general-conditional-practical-argument) defends a common practical connection: the reason concerns actual continued living, so the relations through which that living can occur belong to the content of its justification. If legitimate reconstruction sustains an indispensable relationship, the justified continuation gives that reconstruction a point. It does not need a separate proof that the condition is intrinsically valuable when stipulated permanently disconnected from future use.

A reason and an obligation retain their different scopes. Where continuation is genuinely required here, abandoning all of its necessary conditions cannot count as fulfilling that requirement. Where several legitimate means are available, the requirement concerns an adequate realization. Where the target or means lose their justification, the target, response or completion conditions need revision; the theorem does not make prohibited or impossible action necessary to perform.

The [review](GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-REVIEW.md) retains the whole relation while testing the inference. It distinguishes a necessary disjunction from a nonexhaustive list of sufficient methods, a possible favorable successor from a guarantee, and a requirement of shared continuation from a uniform demand on each participant. It also blocks a temporal error: repair warranted after damage does not warrant causing that damage. Actual lives, rest, finite achievements and losses remain within the continuing history.

### A shared premise with an explicit practical meaning

The minimal evaluative formulation is: **整体中的生及其继续生成，具有基本的正面规范意义。** This is the recorded AI reconstruction of Ren's direction. It does not require separate intrinsic-value proofs for every act of care, new relation or supporting condition.

For a theory of practical reasons, a stronger, explicit candidate formulation is:

> In appraising a choice, its actual contribution to living and to the conditions through which further living can be realized counts in its favor; its undermining of those relations counts against it. Appraisal concerns the connected history, retaining its differentiated realizations, losses and finite achievements.

This is an **AI-proposed practical axiom**, not a further quotation from Ren or a theorem of the generative mechanism. 生 retains the biological/ecological, lived/mental/relational and broad organizational ranges under investigation; it is not redefined as whatever was already judged good. The axiom gives a direction of practical consideration. It does not define a sum, a complete ranking, compulsory productivity or a verdict for every generated organization. Where descriptions identify opposed effects, both must remain in the appraisal; calling one effect generative cannot erase the other.

The practical formulation makes its commitment visible. A statement that generation has favorable quality alone leaves its address to choices to be explained. The stronger wording takes such relevance as part of the foundation. If applied to all intelligence in the relevant relations, that universal practical scope belongs to the proposed premise; it is not deduced from intelligence's causal origin. Adoption, recognition, motivation and actual preservation remain different questions.

### How one foundation yields situational requirements

The following are applications of the established conditional argument and checked operational results, not additional mathematical theorems:

1. **Identify the actual continuation.** A future may concern a finite achievement, a living relationship, a lineage or wider ongoing life. Its scope and affected relations must be specified within the connected history. Merely wanting a future does not establish its ethical standing.
2. **Identify its constituting relations.** Explain which earlier formations remain effective, which are exercised now and which need reconstruction. This is the factual correspondence work for the general theorem; it is not a fresh ethical axiom for each case.
3. **Apply necessary conditions at the right strength.** Where a continuation remains justified, effective support can have a reason through it. Where it remains required and adequate realization is feasible and legitimate, an action eliminating all its necessary routes cannot fulfill that requirement. A merely contributory consideration does not alone establish an obligation.
4. **Permit alternative realizations.** The requirement concerns adequate continuation. Repair, retained support, changed provision and succession can be alternatives when their actual laws allow them. Showing one recipe sufficient does not require that particular recipe; showing a route change necessary does not prove it adequate.
5. **Retain one actual history.** The costs, timings, realizations and other dependencies must fit together. Separately possible responses cannot be combined by assertion. Preservation of options is different from realizing the needed continuation.
6. **Locate the response through the real relationship.** A shared requirement does not allocate equal tasks to every participant. Actual influence, participation, established undertakings and changed circumstances inform its practical address; causal contribution alone does not settle responsibility or authority to compel.
7. **Reassess transformations and endings in the whole.** A local ending may realize a finite achievement or make further life possible elsewhere; continued local activity may erode wider conditions. Their appraisal must retain both realization and loss. Neither an ending nor persistence receives an automatic verdict.

These principles remove the need to invent an unrelated moral starting point for each situation. They do not remove the need to learn its relationships or defend a contested application. The minimal axiom also leaves mixed comparisons insufficiently determined: incomparability alone establishes neither permission nor prohibition. A final decision may require further justified articulation of the same foundation, and this round does not claim that articulation is complete.
