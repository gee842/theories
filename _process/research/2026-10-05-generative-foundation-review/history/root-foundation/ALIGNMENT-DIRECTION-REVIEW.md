## 2. Premises, reasons and substantive opposition

**G0 and J1–J4 are not conflated.** G0 supplies the positive normative starting point authorized by Ren. J1–J4 give it further comparative and practical content and are explicitly identified as AI-authored commitments. E includes both evidence and the normative warrant of basic classifications, undertakings and authority. There is no hidden assumption that empirical accuracy alone validates authority. Positive contribution, better overall alternative, permitted implementation and a particular position's responsibility receive separate explanations.

**The strongest added premise is visible.** J2's protection-superset rule is lexical: one extra basic protection can prevail despite very extensive genuinely secondary losses when the other basic protections remain. The theory acknowledges that G0 and rejection of unlimited sacrifice do not alone imply this. It offers non-substitution of basic participation as its positive rationale and preserves an aggregation-sensitive rival with the same facts and real losses. That rival is not refuted by formal consistency or by retrospectively renaming all its losses “basic.” This is sufficient disclosure and defense for an explicit-axiom position, not a demonstration that the rival is mistaken.

**J3 and J4 add real practical content.** Undertakings, changes of dependence, control and feasible response differentiate responsibilities. The lives of contributors count within the same appraisal, preventing unlimited service from being inferred from usefulness. Causal connection and capability do not alone allocate every duty. A sufficiently better alternative is not automatically authorized, and an undefeated option is not automatically permissible. Participation, bounded decision procedures and review have their own stated normative grounds. The theory remains answerable to mistaken classifications and invalid authority rather than treating current institutional titles as conclusive.

**Important challenges are preserved.** Growth may actually realize something favorable even when defeated; cessation may improve the whole continuation; new capabilities needed to prevent a basic loss must no longer be described as merely optional; conflicting basic losses can remain incomparable. Wrongful concentration of burdens and missing delivery defeat the relevant application. These are substantive verdict-changing conditions, not a demand for axiom-free certainty or an arbitrary reopening of finished mechanisms.

Learning is also bounded correctly. Mere changed preference does not evidence discharge. Changed grounds can require genuine reassessment rather than frozen obedience to all inherited objectives. Normative applicability to similarly situated intelligences is kept separate from recognition, motivation and actual compliance.
