**Status:** accepted at the bounded scope below. Actual predecessor handoffs, generated-model semantics, chronological candidate updates, subsequent predictive use and resource prefixes have been independently checked. The positive result is a conservative fresh audit of present behavior. It is not recovery of the discarded historical transcript, proof of the origin of a new model grammar, or autonomous discovery of the need to expand the family.

## 2. Retrospective revision and a fresh present audit are different claims

A retrospective revision would ask which newly introduced hypotheses can explain the *past* observations. It cannot generally infer that answer just by treating every new hypothesis as having survived the old test. It would need appropriate retained evidence, a proved sufficient encoding for the enlarged question, or a conservative account of the histories still possible given its archive.

The chosen design instead performs a **fresh audit of current behavior**. It keeps the old learned model and old diagnosis as a scoped historical archive, admits current phases and reading parities afresh for two structural families, and purchases new observations. It does not pretend to know what the discarded transcript would have said about the new family.

Reopening phases and parities even for the old three-cycle is deliberately conservative. The old cursor is not silently used as a trusted present-state fact while boundary changes are being admitted. This loses some possible inferential advantage of old evidence, but makes the present audit's information contract explicit. The archived old candidate remains a statement about its earlier stage, not a continuously updated current-state estimate. It is retired during the explicitly paid compaction after a new singleton is selected; its historical content remains in external research records, not a free retained learner archive.

The new audit also does not identify physical readiness reversal, sensor reversal or reset fidelity. It represents their *observable reading parity* under the present sensor-only controls. Separating those older fault parameters would require the appropriate apparatus assumptions and consequence evidence again. A current behavioral-family verdict must not revive an earlier productive interpretation merely because its model has a positive output.

## 3. What the supplied generator adds

The retained three-cycle is reused as data. The proposed generator inserts one extra zero-output state immediately before the learned positive-output state. With the original learned indices, the old cycle is

```text
0 → 1 → 2 → 0       output array (0,0,1)
```

and the generated cycle is

```text
0 → 1 → 3 → 2 → 0   output array (0,0,1,0)
```

Its traversal word from the learned initial state is `0001`. The output array in numeric index order is **not** `(0,0,0,1)`; that is an isomorphic alternative labeling. Preserving the old indices avoids silently reinterpreting physical state 2 when the insertion is used as a boundary perturbation.

This stutter generator, its one-state insertion grammar and its installation recipe are supplied. Applying it to the learned transition/output table is a real use of acquired structure. It does not establish that the system invented the transformation or that all future discrepancies should be explained by a single added zero phase.

The fresh family contains every phase and reading parity for the three- and four-state models: \(2(3+4)=14\) alternatives. The choice of behavioral coordinates eliminates neither a sensor diagnosis nor a reset diagnosis, because those are not requested conclusions of this new audit. Their physical interpretation remains unresolved. The native test performs no reset and no productive probe.

The implementation should obtain the positive state's predecessor and construct the inserted cycle from the retained model, rather than substitute a target-specific four-state table. Renaming learned state indices should preserve the native command/read sequence and the family verdict, with inferred state indices transported accordingly.

## 4. A sufficient acquired test, with a sharp information limit for this recipe

For either family, collect successive endpoint readings separated by one action. Six readings suffice to separate every three-cycle rotation/parity from every four-cycle rotation/parity in this restricted family. The six-bit response sets are

```text
three-cycle:
001001 010010 100100 110110 101101 011011

four-cycle:
000100 001000 010001 100010
111011 110111 101110 011101
```

They are disjoint. At five readings the words `00100` and `11011` remain shared, so a uniform five-reading guarantee for this unreset action sequence would be false. A particular inherited case can distinguish the families sooner. This is a bound on the declared behavioral assay; it is not an optimality theorem over every possible sensor, reset or consequence policy.

A set-valued update should advance each candidate through its own model and retain only predictions agreeing with each new paid reading. A nonempty set confined to one family warrants that family within the supplied alternatives. Phase and parity should be reported only when their appropriate candidate labels also agree. Empty belief reports failure of the admitted family or trusted sensing assumptions; it must not manufacture a new model label.

The explicit new evidence matters. If a richer future family is later admitted, the current compact belief may again be insufficient for retrospective replay. Any retained fresh transcript or certificate must have a declared encoding, write cost and rent. The implemented continuation retains the selected model, parity and current cursor; its new raw transcript remains external. A reusable raw-evidence archive is a further, unimplemented interface. The external audit cannot silently become internal memory at the next handoff.

## 5. The positive and failure cases must preserve their ancestry

The successful probe branch has enough fuel to be a plausible positive continuation, but no source packet. It can test fresh sensor predictions; it cannot demonstrate another productive extraction. A new four-cycle in this branch is a declared boundary structural change, preserving the actual old state index 2 under the insertion above. An unchanged three-cycle is the paired control. The native environment retains its actual state; the learner receives all current phase possibilities, not that state label.

The actual historically undetected four-cycle branch is different. Its four-cycle was already present during the preceding failed interpretation, and its remaining fuel is only \(52327/65536\). A new installation fee of one unit alone exceeds that reserve. A successful revision obtained from a larger reserve is therefore an explicitly funded assay or different ancestry, not recovery of this historical branch.

There is a small genuine failure-to-revise witness within the old installed mechanism. Its retained template 3 at model phase 2 predicts a read of 1, while its actual four-cycle at physical phase 2 reads 0. Direct execution of one additional existing-controller `step('read')` is affordable: it empties the old family, leaves \(29509/65536\) fuel, and leaves 23 live cells after removing the candidate. The newly proposed installation is still unaffordable. This shows that discovering a problem and funding a new representational capacity are separate transitions.

That optional extension must treat the old `retained` Boolean carefully. The predecessor was designed as a one-shot diagnoser, and the flag remains true after a later direct `step` raises an empty-family exception. It is a historical flag in this extended call sequence, not proof of a currently retained valid diagnosis. A new wrapper must report the acquired refutation and current empty archive explicitly rather than trusting the stale flag.

The sensing-only branch keeps a real packet but only \(88177/32768\) fuel. Its physical reserve cannot be treated as accessible fuel before a paid transfer occurs. Under the selected no-probe audit, a funding failure must preserve that unspent source. Choosing to perform a risky productive action to finance later inquiry would be a different supplied policy with different consequences; it is not free financing of the current one.

## 6. Resource and memory checks required

The chosen recipe keeps the predecessor's 25 or 27 occupied cells, adds eight controller cells, pays a one-unit installation fee, and stores the generated nine-cell model explicitly. Candidate pairs, generated data, selection scans, committed overwrites and any fresh evidence record require their own work/write/rent charges. Scratch must stay live across native actions and readings, as in the preceding access study.

The accounting identity for every actual branch is

\[
F_0+S_0=C_{\mathrm{revision}}+F_{\mathrm{final}}+S_{\mathrm{final}},
\]

for this no-probe continuation. There is no new incoming credit or new extraction waste. All costs are prepaid from actual accessible fuel. If the learner runs out of funds after physically acting but before committing its state update, its last stored belief may be stale. Such an archive must not be interpreted as a completed current model.

The prior controller, scoped archive and acquired model remain occupied unless the protocol explicitly pays for and reports a compaction or retirement. Generating a new model is not permission to overwrite the old one for free while simultaneously relying on its content as the generator's input. A capacity denial must preserve prior expenditures and prevent the uncommitted change.

Unchanged abstract prices should be used for comparative branches. They are a local logical-machine law, not measured physical energy. A failed literal or factored representation is a result about that particular supplied recipe, not proof that every possible representation or diagnosis is unaffordable.

## 7. Planned independent checks and acceptance boundary

After the root protocol is saved, the independent checker should replay the actual access handoffs, verify occupied storage and resource carry, reconstruct the generated model without using its implementation helper, and independently compute chronological candidate compatibility. It should compare all retained outputs against the declared family/phase/parity questions, rather than demand fault labels the new sensor-only model does not support.

The finite suite should include unchanged and inserted-cycle continuations of the funded primary branch; the actual unfunded historical four-cycle and sensing-only branches; all declared rotation/parity cases under clearly stated provisions; an outside-family or drifting law; increased tariffs and capacity restrictions; and interrupted native/update boundaries. Learned-state renamings can test dependence on acquired structure rather than hardcoded phase identities. An ample mathematical assay must be kept separate from actual inherited affordability.

**Accept the local bridge** if generated alternatives use the actual acquired model, fresh paid evidence selects or rejects those alternatives as claimed, and every memory/resource prefix and failure status is honest. State explicitly that the family generator, restart policy and test-channel calibration remain supplied. Do not infer restored past evidence, open-ended autonomous model invention or renewed productive capacity from this bounded result.

The exact insertion-schedule budget \(687561/65536\) completes with zero fuel left. Removing another \(1/65536\) permits acquisition and confirmed use but prevents final retention. A separate derived prefix budget permits the physical forward action and denies its cursor commit; then `selected.state` is stale relative to the physical phase. The historical `acquired_retained` flag must not be read as a claim that this later cursor is current. No failed step restores the physical apparatus, old archive or spent fuel.

**Final decision:** the finite current-family reacquisition interface is verified. An actual acquired model supplies the structure for a new generated candidate, fresh paid readings select between alternatives, and funded cases retain and use the revised prediction. The study also demonstrates affordable error detection without affordable new installation and learning without affordable later use. It does not demonstrate autonomous invention of the generator, retrospective recovery of discarded evidence, retention of a reusable raw transcript, new productive capture or completeness of the enlarged family.
