## 1. What was missing and what is now supplied

The earlier Lean completion proves inverse, inclusion-preserving maps between every predicate on a supplied carrier and the ideal of its finite subsets. A domain correspondence alone had not yet been connected to the interpretation of higher-order formulas. The new work supplies actual syntax, binding, application and equality semantics, and proves their correspondence by induction. Formula truth agreement is a conclusion, not a field required of an input structure.

The two interpretations are formal presentations of related mathematical content within the same inquiry. Individual, relation and function types identify roles in that content; they do not posit fundamentally separate substances or an external meaning-giver. The distinction exposes what the construction preserves. The account still owes a justification for any admitted mathematical resources whose origin is not derived here.

## 2. Full relational second-order truth

`SecondOrder.lean` represents relation variables of every finite arity and finite contexts of individual variables. Its formulas contain individual equality, relation application, predicate equality, negation, conjunction, and quantification over individuals and relations. Existential constructors allow arbitrary finite nesting; universal forms have checked semantics. Empty carriers and zero-arity relations are permitted by the definitions.

One interpretation reads a relation as a predicate on tuples. The other reads it as an ideal of finite subsets of those tuples, with membership given by the ideal's union. `satisfaction_transport` proves preservation of every formula for corresponding environments and independently specified matching quantifier ranges. The proof handles both directions of relation quantification with the concrete `partsOf` and `unionContents` maps. Predicate equality is actual extensional equality; it is proved to correspond to equality of ideals.

`full_satisfaction_transport` and `full_sentence_reconstruction` specialize to every predicate and every ideal. Thus the result covers full relational second-order truth at its declared language scope, not merely selected definable predicates or tested examples. The module contains **25 named theorems and 57 declaration audits**.

## 3. Recursive finite higher types

`TypedTruth.lean` goes beyond the second-order milestone. Its types are generated by a base type, function types and predicate types. Their finite nesting is unrestricted: predicates of predicates, functions between higher predicate domains and predicates of function domains are included.

A supplied base bijection is lifted recursively. At a predicate type, the map uses the existing finite-subset ideal completion on the recursively interpreted carrier. At a function type, it uses forward and inverse maps to transport every function between those carriers. Both inverse laws are proved. No truth-preservation premise is added to this construction.

The term language contains typed variables, application and lambda abstraction. `term_transport` proves preservation of term interpretation. Formulas add equality at any type, membership, logical constructors and quantification at every represented type. `formula_transport` proves truth equivalence for all formulas with corresponding assignments; `sentence_transport` handles every closed sentence. The identity base bijection gives a reconstruction of the same base domain.

`formula_comprehension` additionally gives an ideal presentation of each formula-defined extension, including formulas that quantify over higher types. This is semantic comprehension using the already admitted full predicate interpretation. It is not an effective procedure for obtaining truth from observations or a formula-to-predicate term constructor. A closed higher-type eta example exercises lambda binding, application and quantification over functions from predicates to predicates of predicates. The module contains **18 named theorems and 40 declaration audits**.

The type grammar is explicit. It contains no dependent types, internal universe or distinguished truth-value object type. The result does not assert coverage of every possible higher-order formalism. Full arrow spaces remain supplied; only predicate types are recursively replaced by ideal completions. No reconstruction of every total function from finite function graphs is claimed.

## 4. Fullness is preserved, not independently derived

The second-order module checks selected-domain transport as well. `corresponding_fullness` proves that matching predicate and ideal ranges are full together. This prevents an interpretation shift from being hidden behind the replacement of predicates by ideals. It does not establish that every proper restriction must change some closed sentence's truth.

A concrete boundary is checked separately. On the same natural-number carrier with the same membership reading, the sentence “there exists a unary predicate holding of every element” is true with full predicate quantification and false when only finite predicates are allowed. `full_totalUnary`, `finite_rejects_totalUnary` and `finite_not_full` establish this result. The finite range is explicitly selected semantics, not a claimed Henkin model with comprehension. This is a genuine failure of that narrower range to preserve the indicated truth; it is not a counterexample to a root admitting full completion.

The positive construction therefore earns the conditional semantic claim without eliminating its admission commitments. All ideals remain available as predicates on a finite-subset basis. Full function spaces remain available at arrow types. Rewriting these domains faithfully does not by itself derive their availability from minimal generative principles.

## 5. Contribution to the stronger claim

The earlier report that the higher-order semantic connection was missing is now superseded at the two declared language scopes. Its remaining foundational claims are preserved: mathematical content can have a faithful generative completion presentation, while the warrant for its entire range and the direction of constitutive dependence require further argument.

A substantive structural account can accept every checked correspondence here. It may identify the completed role with the mathematical subject while leaving the root's ultimate explanatory priority open. The generative account could meet that challenge by independently deriving the admission principle or showing that the rival's explanation is the same constitutive dependence under a justified translation. These proofs extend semantic reconstruction to richer declared languages; they do not alone establish that explanatory reduction.

The next universal bridge is therefore **warrant for full predicate and function admission, and its dependence on the proposed root**. A model lacking predicate binders was previously a representational omission; that omission is now repaired. The unresolved universal justification is an explanatory obligation in the reconstructed theory. Neither should be confused with a proof of impossibility.
