# Meaning, verification and action: primary comparators

6 October 2026. AI-authored source comparison for the [semantic action-composition study](../../../../theories/becoming/history/SEMANTIC-ACTION-COMPOSITION.md). The comparison locates the technical contribution and its limits. It does not claim empirical validation or that established software concepts are newly discovered by this programme.

## Proof rules must preserve the consumer's intended property

Necula and Schneck's *Proof-Carrying Code with Untrusted Proof Rules* (2002) permits a producer to supply higher-level safety rules while requiring a proof that they enforce a trusted lower-level memory-safety policy. Its decoder and verification machinery retain explicit assumptions. The authors distinguish proof checking from the correctness of the reference interpretation. [Primary author-hosted paper](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~necula/Papers/sfpol_isss02.pdf), especially pp. 1–3 and 8–9 of the PDF.

**Application here, an inference:** accepting a source's internal proof is insufficient if the destination interprets its conclusions differently. The recipient's command-compatibility propositions must be mapped to the actual action request; they cannot be reinterpreted as independent patient-standing claims. A translation can be checked against an independently specified destination meaning. The choice and justification of that meaning remain additional obligations. The present small adapter is not a new proof-carrying-code architecture, and checking it does not validate its chosen ethical policy.

## The final outcome is an application-level obligation

Saltzer, Reed and Clark's *End-to-End Arguments in System Design* (1984) distinguishes delivery to a host from the application's completion of the requested action. Its reliability argument concerns the entire relevant path and explicitly depends on identifying the application endpoints. It also treats lower-level mechanisms as potentially useful rather than universally unnecessary. [Primary MIT-hosted paper](https://web.mit.edu/6.033/2002/wwwdocs/papers/endtoend.pdf), especially PDF pp. 2–5 and 7.

**Application here, an inference:** a read certificate, a compatible command request and an actual recipient consequence require different evidence. The protected endpoint must be identified with the recipient whose conditions supplied the reason. Byte fidelity and a successful local audit can contribute while still leaving an actuator, funding or delivery failure. No networking theorem establishes ethical standing; the comparison concerns the structure of the verification obligation.

## Meaning cannot be obtained solely by renaming tokens

Harnad's *The Symbol Grounding Problem* (1990) asks how formal symbol interpretation can be intrinsic rather than dependent on an external interpreter, and proposes connecting symbolic categories to nonsymbolic sensory representations. The accessible repository abstract supports this characterization. [Primary author record and abstract](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/250382/) and [repository PDF](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/250382/1/symgro.pdf). Direct opening failed during this retrieval; the indexed author-repository abstract and bibliographic record were available. No detailed full-paper reconstruction is claimed.

**Application here, an inference:** adding patient names, role names or hashes to an exported mask does not establish that those names refer to the actual patient or control mechanism. Our contract preserves an already interpreted relation under stated calibration and transport premises. It does not solve the general symbol-grounding problem, derive semantic content from strings, or establish that every cognition requires a new online observation. Ren's broader proposal concerns the constitutive external conditions of cognition; this study isolates one operational requirement when different interpreted mechanisms are joined.

## What the new test can discriminate

The proposed naive adapter holds source bytes fixed but changes their interpretation. The binding-aware and conventional typed dispatchers preserve both patient and command meaning under the same supplied context. A performance difference therefore identifies an interface omission relative to that naive comparator. It does not distinguish the foundation from a conventional system that already models the same relationships.

The strongest new evidence is the concrete incompatibility in this repository's actual proposed join, its exact frequency in the retained fixture distribution, and a nonvacuous corrected semantic interface. Its significance for alignment is conditional: a system pursuing the intended end must preserve that end's bearer, proposition and applicable grounds across its own transformations. Those requirements do not choose the ethically right end. The remaining physical and normative interfaces must be established on their own evidence.
