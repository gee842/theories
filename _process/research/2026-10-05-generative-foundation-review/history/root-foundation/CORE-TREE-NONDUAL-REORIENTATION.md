# Core and tree: correcting the order of inquiry

6 October 2026. AI-authored reconstruction following [Ren's exact correction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-core-tree-nonduality-and-method.md). This changes the governing method of this ethical-grounding branch. It is not another attempt to close the earlier reason-bridge contract on its own terms.

This note applies the project-wide [NONDUAL-METHOD](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md) to this ethical-grounding branch. The separately preserved [project-wide direction](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-project-wide-nondual-method.md) governs the shared project; this note is a branch-specific implementation, not a competing method or independent corroboration.

## What the correction reaches

Ren states that the generative core and generative tree are nondual. The preceding work had mostly applied nonduality to subject/object and patient/responder boundaries. It continued to treat the core as a sparse starting structure from which the richer tree had to obtain separate credentials. That preserved a methodological separation precisely where this correction asks us to investigate continuity.

Our working interpretation is:

> The core is immanent in the tree's concrete generation and transformation; the tree is that generativity's differentiated unfolding. Understanding, evaluation and revision are themselves formations within this activity and can change its subsequent course. Our descriptions of either core or tree remain partial.

This formulation is AI elaboration, not Ren's verbatim definition. It does not reduce the core to a convenient summary or posit a separate producer behind the tree. The same activity is investigated through its common generative character and its differentiated unfolding. Our descriptions remain partial and revisable. “Tree” here does not silently impose a mathematical unique-parent structure on relations that may overlap and feed back.

## The specific methodological error

The earlier formal process did not itself contain a primitive subject/object split. Its scoped proofs and countermodels retain their actual meanings. The error was allowing that deliberately sparse representation to fix the ontology and the agenda: whatever it did not entail was repeatedly encountered as an additional input, and the next question became how the thin core could authorize that input.

Premise tracking is valuable for a particular model. It cannot establish that what the model omits is separate from the reality being modeled. A countermodel to a sparse description demonstrates underdetermination of that description. It does not, by itself, establish an ontological separation between generativity and significance. Conversely, a richer proposed unity still needs an account; announcing that omitted content belongs to the core cannot establish its truth.

This drift had a concrete effect. Despite calling C1/C2 local checks, we repeatedly let them choose the next research question. The latest [constitutive-answer argument](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md) clarified a duplicate authorization demand after a reason was granted. Its narrow result remains valid, but it addressed a question generated by our decomposition. It did not resolve the user's more basic core/tree proposal.

We also tended to bring Chinese and Buddhist texts into a preselected moral-reason debate. The correction allows their questions and ways of articulating experience to alter the research architecture itself. No survey of “Western thought” is claimed here. The object of criticism is our particular default: separately fixed terms, a one-way derivation, and authority supplied across their boundaries. Geographic provenance does not decide a claim's truth.

## A different movement of explanation

The concrete research movement becomes:

**Activity forms a distinction; the distinction organizes further activity; consequences disclose its reach and omissions; responding changes both the distinction and the continuing activity.**

For example, a practical boundary can form around maintained coordination. That boundary shapes what is sensed, retained and acted upon. Its activities also affect conditions and continuities beyond what its present representation registers. Those consequences may become available within later experience and interaction, changing the boundary, its field of relevance and its response. Undergoing, valuing, attending and acting should be reconstructed together where they actually occur. None is made fundamental merely because a local model already has a variable for it.

There are two directions of inquiry here, without two separate realities. A proposed common generative account helps explain particular formations. The formations, including experience and evaluation, correct what that account says generativity involves. This is epistemic revision of our account, not a claim that later events physically cause their own earlier conditions.

The investigator belongs in this inquiry too. What we select as data, what a model preserves, and which distinction a proof tests are activities with conditions and consequences. Including them does not make every interpretation equally sound. It makes their adequacy answerable to the concrete differences they claim to explain.

## Consequences for ethics and the next study

The first question is no longer how a completed neutral core issues a moral warrant to a completed independent actor. It is how differentiated generative activity becomes sensitive to its conditions and consequences, forms significance and response, and can revise the organization of that responsiveness. The subject matter includes actual felt burdens and possibilities, not only survival counters or fitted behavior.

The next provisional study concerns **co-formation and consequential revision of practical boundaries, significance and response within the same history**. This is a chosen inquiry, not a necessary linear order imposed on the whole tree. Its distinctions may form together and must remain open to revision. Existing formation and learning studies supply scoped interfaces and limitations. Their unjoined pieces must not be presented as one realized process, and a supplied benevolent selector cannot serve as the origin of concern.

A useful comparison follows an organization that sustains itself by excluding consequential feedback and one whose organization changes through that feedback. Openness, persistence and successful revision are not themselves ethical verdicts. The reconstruction must preserve what happens to each affected continuity, including suffering, coercion and lost possibilities. It must explain destructive and constructive development within the same activity. It must also distinguish another process's experience from a modeler's projection of it.

Earlier relief comparisons, jurisdiction arguments and C1/C2 distinctions remain available when a developed claim needs those tests. They no longer determine the foundation's initial divisions or serve as its sole completion test. We are not silently abandoning rigor: the claim of unity, its proposed differences and its explanatory reach remain assessable. Nor does this methodological correction discharge the no-assumptions requirement. It changes what account we investigate and how the partial models answer to it.

Fix the proposed connection and scope before testing. Disconnected histories cannot establish that the claimed relations formed together; changed reports cannot alone establish changed experience; improved internal regulation cannot alone establish improvement for affected continuities. A substantive failure of one of these claims requires revising it, while an omitted relationship in a model can instead require revising the representation. These distinctions follow the shared method and do not insulate the theory from criticism.
