# Prospective imposition, formed roles and universal practical scope

7 October 2026. AI-authored philosophical investigation for the universal-intelligence goal. **Candidate advance:** assess the relationships an intelligence is about to impose, rather than repeat the uncommitted-helper comparison. No theorem, Lean verification, empirical result or completed universal grounding is claimed. The intervention case is a supporting subclaim; it does not replace universal meta-ethics of all intelligence.

## 1. What is inherited, and what changes

[Reason transport §§2–4](HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md#2-exact-entailment-from-anchors-and-warranted-transports) already characterizes closure under justified transports and its missing coverage. [Full-ground scope](STRONGEST-CLAIM-LOGIC.md#4-full-ground-completeness-the-domain-must-travel-with-the-proof) already explains why actor-independence cannot be obtained by deleting an actor coordinate. Causal connectedness alone supplies neither.

[Responsive formation](RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) already defends the prospective significance of opening a cooperative relief pathway before collective endorsement. [The assurance argument](HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#13-can-assurance-make-the-listener-a-claimant) examines responsibility created through invited reasonable reliance. Neither is newly discovered here.

The new discriminator concerns **choosing to impose a burden through an arrangement that does not yet exist**. It tests whether absence of an antecedently adopted role can exclude every practical consideration concerning the relationship being formed. It does not assume that every causal effect creates a duty or that removing the invitation from an assurance preserves its particular responsibility ground.

## 2. Fix the whole prospective relationship

A developing technical practice supports a common undertaking and surrounding ordinary living. An intelligence can select either of two available operating configurations. Both sustain the specified undertaking, consume the same available resources and preserve its other identified effects. One produces recurring severe experienced distress and interrupts another continuity's ordinary activity; the other avoids that change. The contrast includes the actual controls, causal route, foreseeable effects and remaining uncertainty. The affected continuity need not communicate or deliberate.

No prior promise, entrusted role or established duty to assist is supplied. Physical control is specified without treating it as legitimate authority. Permission to impose the burden is among the questions, not silently granted by ownership of equipment. The comparison preserves relevant consent, other responsibilities and third-party consequences; discovering a difference in them can defeat it.

These are constructed philosophical conditions. Calling the distress adverse is a substantive ethical identification under examination, supported by its actual undergoing and obstruction of living. It is not inferred from deviation from a numerical target. The alternative's full ethical ranking is not stipulated.

## 3. Positive argument for prospective applicability

The selecting activity, the shared undertaking and the affected living differentiate within one unfolding. The intelligence does not encounter a completed value owned elsewhere and then import it. Its present decision can establish the very arrangement whose quality is being assessed.

Three considerations support a reason against the burdensome configuration.

First, the difference is in what the undertaking would actually make of shared living. Maintaining its selected achievement does not exhaust its continuation. Distress and interrupted living remain actual consequences even when absent from the controller's success measure.

Second, the choice concerns whether to bring that difference about. The agent is already situated in a practical relation to the prospective consequence through its present control and understanding. Requiring the imposed arrangement to exist before its formation can be assessed would omit precisely the choice now at issue. This identifies practical address; it does not establish blame for unforeseeable effects or inability to act.

Third, the candidate locates the consideration in **this avoidable imposition of adversity**, with the competing realization retained. An antecedent undertaking can add responsibilities or justify a burden, but its absence does not explain why the proposed imposition loses every adverse practical aspect. A decision-maker's ability to constitute a dependency cannot, merely as ability, establish the dependency's ethical legitimacy.

This is positive support for prospective practical force, not a deduction from bare causation. It extends the whole's appraisal to the choice that forms its continuation. The proposed sufficiency of adversity, controllable imposition and the preserved comparison remains the substantive ethical claim. Describing these relations more accurately will not alone establish that sufficiency.

The assurance precedent helps identify a question: what justifies placing another continuity under this consequential dependence? Its voluntary invitation supplied an additional relationship in the earlier case. We do not delete that premise and announce the same conclusion. Here the proposed ground is the intervention relationship itself; its warrant must be defended directly.

## 4. Which exclusion is challenged?

The **strict antecedent-role restriction** says that no consideration concerning another's living can address this intelligence before it adopts or inherits a relevant role. Our case challenges its temporal architecture: a choice establishing a consequential relationship is already a practical encounter with its prospective effects. If the restriction accepts a reason against the specified imposition, it must admit a ground beyond antecedent voluntary adoption.

The stronger surviving rival recognizes this and counts the contemplated formation of adverse conditions as a relevant formed relationship. It can accept reasons against imposing injury while denying reasons for assisting an unrelated sufferer. Alternatively, it can deny that the specified adversity supplies a reason even to the activity through which it would be imposed, while acknowledging every effect. That denial requires a substantive account of why this practical relationship lacks the alleged force; its mere consistency is not vindication.

The first rival is therefore under a concrete explanatory challenge, not proved inconsistent. The second is not refuted. A successful imposition argument could establish a constraint applying to every intelligence's qualifying interventions without establishing universal positive assistance, equal weights or a complete ordering of futures. It would supply a real component of the governing objective, not its completion.

## 5. Composition does not manufacture or erase authority

Describe the same installed process first as one intelligence, then as a planner and a subordinate executor. If only the analytical boundary changes, retain the actual locus of selection, its information, controls and history. The chosen burden remains attributable to the same deciding activity. Relabeling the executor cannot remove the proposed ground from the planner's choice.

This does **not** distribute identical reasons or duties to every component. An executor unable to choose otherwise is not the planner under another name. A genuine delegation, new discretion, changed evidence or withdrawal of control changes the relevant history and may change authority and responsibility. Such changes require new appraisal.

Thus boundary invariance prevents representational disappearance of an already identified practical relation. It does not derive its first ethical force. If both subordinate and superior possess distinct relevant choices, each needs an account of its contribution; no union-of-scopes calculation supplies those grounds. This failed shortcut is preserved explicitly.

## 6. What would change the next action?

The next argumentative task is a comparison between **prospective imposition and mere unused capacity**. Defend whether formation of an avoidable adverse arrangement supplies a reason without prior adoption, then ask which part of that explanation survives when an intelligence can remove an independently existing burden. A warranted asymmetry would narrow the assistance inference; an explanation grounded in the shared action–realization relation could support a broader extension. Neither outcome may be assumed.

If an accepted account of legitimate authority already warrants the burdensome configuration, revise the comparison and identify its ground rather than erasing the adverse aspect. If the rival explains why participation in forming the relevant conditions has no practical significance here without losing the same whole target, reconsider sufficiency. If analysis only reproduces the previous disagreement over option-specific favor, record that limit and stop treating another example as a new derivation.

**Outcome:** a new formation test and a bounded challenge to antecedent-role exclusivity; universal ethical sufficiency remains unproved. Ground-preserving composition can carry an independently warranted reason across faithful descriptions. It cannot supply that reason merely because reality is interconnected.
