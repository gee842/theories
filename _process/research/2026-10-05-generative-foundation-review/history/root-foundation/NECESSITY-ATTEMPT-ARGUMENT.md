# Necessity attempt: can unchosen restraint ground unchosen concern?

6 October 2026. AI-authored attempt under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Target: **actual severe, still-unwanted suffering and an accessible, otherwise permissible relieving transformation supply some moral reason before the responder adopts concern**. The target is universal within independently specified conditions, not inevitable motivation, compulsory aid or an assumption-free derivation of all reality.

The strongest positive route treats the undergoing and its possible transformation as one concrete ground, then challenges an exclusion at the place where it claims normative authority. This round tests an additional connection: a rival appealing to autonomy already recognizes unchosen reasons concerning others. Can its own ground justify restricting those reasons to restraint? The attempted inference narrows a substantial exclusion argument but does not establish universal necessity.

## 1. Fix the occurrence before disputing its practical scope

Activity differentiates into an experiencing continuity and processes capable of affecting it. Their histories, experience and control remain distinct without becoming fundamentally separate substances. Actual relief changes that undergoing; silencing a report, pleasing an observer or dissolving the affected continuity is not the stipulated relief.

We retain the restricted experiential domain from the [constitutive-significance argument](CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md). Instrumental acceptance does not itself remove an adverse aspect; transformed enjoyment is not automatically treated as the same still-unwanted condition. No reflex, resource deficit or negative numerical sign independently establishes suffering.

Identify a real relieving option before its selection. The unrelated responder neither caused the burden nor undertook responsibility. Otherwise permissible means, effective access, affected continuity and relevant consequences are specified independently; legitimate choice-specific exclusion remains disputed rather than stipulated absent. To test the simplest case, hold competing material and project effects fixed. That does not settle whether moral answerability itself changes the normative situation.

The positive judgment is that genuine relief gives this option a point before caring is chosen. The reason can explain forming concern; it need not be produced by that formation. A knowledgeable person may recognize this point and resist it. Neither moral defiance nor an absence of empathy shows that the reason is absent.

## 2. The strongest attempted derivation

Start with four distinguishable claims:

1. **Adversity:** this actual undergoing is bad for its experiencing continuity, independently of the responder's approval.
2. **Realization:** the identified option would relieve that very adversity through otherwise permissible means.
3. **Unchosen restraint:** another's formed life can give an indifferent stranger a reason against coercing or commandeering it, without the stranger first adopting respect.
4. **Restriction requires its own ground:** practical jurisdiction cannot acquire normative authority merely because a boundary, preference or policy has causally formed.

The first is the inherited experiential identification, granted here to isolate scope. The second is a causal and permissibility finding. The third is an independently recognizable normative commitment of the autonomy rival; a strict egoist may reject it. The fourth distinguishes explaining the occurrence of a selector from vindicating it. None is presented as a theorem of bare generative activity.

Now attempt the closing steps. Since unchosen restraint is possible, prior adoption cannot be a universal requirement for reasons concerning another life. Since the relieving option does not commandeer either life, the rationale against commandeering does not exclude it. Its actual benefit remains. Therefore, the attempt concludes, that benefit supplies some reason for the option.

The first inference succeeds against a **general adoption requirement**. The second succeeds against the stated interference rationale. The final inference needs further warrant: defeating that exclusion does not itself turn an acknowledged benefit into an action-ground. The rival might recognize a different, assistance-specific jurisdiction. We must examine that possibility rather than conceal it inside “therefore.”

## 3. Why the positive conclusion remains compelling

The relieving transformation answers the adverse character already present in the unfolding. Nothing must travel from a private value-owner into a separate agent. Capacity locates a possible realization; it need not create the value being realized. In the clean case, the choice is improved in this respect by what it makes possible there, not by satisfying an antecedent wish here.

This also gives a reason for choosing to care. The proposal need not demand first cultivating compassion and only subsequently acting. An initially indifferent responder can choose an available response for its actual relieving point. The resulting intention expresses the reason; it need not retroactively create it.

These are substantive favorable judgments. Calling the transformation “an answer” cannot establish its practical scope by definition. The [earlier root attempt](NONINSTRUMENTAL-ROOT-ARGUMENT.md) already tested fixed-occurrence operator substitution and showed why ground sufficiency cannot be smuggled into it. The [warrant inquiry](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md) already tested correction of ends. Neither becomes a new proof here.

The additional pressure concerns autonomy's source. If respect is warranted because differentiated lives have real stakes in how activity affects them, the rival owes an explanation of why those stakes have exclusively prohibitive significance toward strangers. Preventing intrusion explains a constraint; it does not yet explain excluding every favorable consideration from a nonintrusive improvement. If instead agency has a distinct authority that disqualifies welfare considerations for particular choices, that authority and scope must be justified. A mixed account is possible. None follows merely from the formation of agency or the absence of a shared subject.

This does not collapse doing and allowing, or make every benefit equally weighty. An account could recognize distinctive reasons against intrusion while admitting weaker positive favor. Our proposed minimum needs only the latter, leaving obligations and demandingness for further assessment.

## 4. The strongest surviving exclusion

Consider a rival with this substantive account: differentiated lives possess authority against others taking control of them, while positive practical projects belong to their own authors. Unchosen restraint respects that standing. Assistance becomes a moral project through a valid undertaking; before it, another's improvement is a real benefit without supplying this stranger a positive moral reason. The rival can permit spontaneous charity and let actual benefit correct its means after adoption.

This is not a freely toggled moral predicate. It proposes an explanation of the difference between restraint and assistance through a formed normative jurisdiction. It preserves the undergoing, actual relief, access and nondual formation. Its normative explanation may be wrong, but it is not contradicted merely by the shared causal history.

This hypothetical rival is deliberately stronger than a defense of special concern for one's own life. Special priority can coexist with some favor for strangers; rejecting equal altruism does not refute our minimum. The [focused source adjudication](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-SOURCES.md) preserves that distinction. We do not attribute the zero-favor rival to the primary author it examines.

Our response is that freedom to choose projects and permission to decline do not establish zero favor. The [jurisdiction inquiry](ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-ARGUMENT.md) already distinguishes them. The rival's strongest reply concerns answerability: recognizing even favorable moral relevance allegedly subjects one's choice to an alien domain of assessment. Alternatively, legitimate authority might genuinely disqualify this consideration for this choice.

Neither reply is disposed of by saying the physical effort is negligible. Conversely, neither is established by describing distinct lives. Whether favorable justification necessarily brings the contested answerability, and whether a particular protected domain disqualifies incidental relief, require their own argument. A universal exclusion leaving relief permanently unable to justify choice would defeat the intended practical minimum; retaining an inert value label would not rescue it.

## 5. What this attempt establishes

The conditional result is exact: **a rival recognizing unchosen restraints cannot use lack of prior concern as a general barrier to other-directed reasons. A reason to protect against interference cannot, by itself, justify excluding favorable significance from a noninterfering relief opportunity.** The rival must supply a further assistance-specific ground. This constrains its account; it does not prove that no such ground can be warranted.

The positive constitutive account remains preferable in the clean case in this inquiry: actual relief can warrant concern's formation, rather than waiting for concern to confer significance. But the four premises above do not necessitate that conclusion. The unresolved claim is that the adverse-undergoing–accessible-relief relation itself suffices for prospective moral favor wherever the declared eligibility conditions hold. Establishing universal scope requires vindicating this sufficiency and addressing genuine exclusions, not another authorizer after a reason is already established; the [constitutive-answer argument](CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md) separates those questions.

The result would change if a defensible account showed why the same formed authority grounds unchosen restraint yet extinguishes all favorable relevance in the stated relief case, or if the constitutive account independently established that the preserved relation is the complete practical ground. The [counterreview](NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-REVIEW.md) also shows why desire-independent self-relief does not already establish that completeness. Categorical rational authority remains stronger than the moral favor targeted here.

**Universal necessity is not solved by this attempt.** What fails is a proposed inference, not the possibility of unified grounding. The remaining disagreement is substantive normative explanation within the unfolding, rather than an omitted transport mechanism between primitive selves. No new model, scientific run or empirical evidence is claimed.
