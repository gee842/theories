It reuses actual reported boundaries and physically decoded world/memory through the existing exactness contract. Accounted costs stay with their original micro-events.

The proof uses standard total-correctness methods, with primary antecedents linked in the canonical account. The new research contribution is a missing checked connection between this repository's protocol, controller and resource interfaces.
