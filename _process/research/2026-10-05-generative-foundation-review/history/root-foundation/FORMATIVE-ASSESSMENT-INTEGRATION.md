## 2. The positive explanatory account

Return to the communication example. Initially, a successful delivery leaves open whether a participant's expression changed the decision, a coordinator correctly anticipated it, or a suggested answer was repeated. A later exchange can make a new distinction available: which changes follow the participant's contribution, and which are supplied by the surrounding interaction? The participants can acquire that contrast before possessing its eventual theoretical description.

Inquiry can also change the answer. Joint practice may create a shared expression, a reliable interpretation or a new opportunity to correct a consequential decision. A later successful exchange then supplies evidence about that formed relationship. It need not reveal whether the same capacity was available earlier. The distinction between eliciting an existing capacity and helping form a new one concerns actual history; it is not decided by calling the encounter a test.

The proposed descriptive advance is to retain **the relation assessed, the occasion, the route by which evidence arose, and the effects of the inquiry itself**. Changes in these are related aspects of one history. Their analytical separation prevents a current achievement from silently becoming a claim about the past, or a prompted answer from becoming evidence of a different causal route.

Meaningful understanding still requires more than this bookkeeping. The earlier content account relates acquisition, use, correction and reference. Here the practical questions help constrain which relation matters: could the contribution change the relevant decision, could a misunderstanding be corrected, and did the resulting opportunity actually persist? These are defeasible grounds for interpretation. They do not constitute a universal behavioral definition of understanding.

The inquiry need not aim at removing all assistance. Language, memory, shared attention and institutional support can be constitutive parts of understanding and agency. The relevant question is whether a particular intervention supplies the very response being cited as evidence for some other capacity. An unsupported performance can be the wrong target where the actual capacity is jointly realized.

## 3. A checked construction with an acquired contrast

[FormativeAssessment.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/FormativeAssessment.lean) imports the previous classification interface. Its native configuration has a retained request-to-response link and available credits. These are parts of one evolving circuit, not separate agent and environment substances.

Without the link, the default response is false. With it, both binary requests can affect the response. A prompt supplies a response through another route while leaving the link unchanged. A formation event adds the link. Every successful operation spends one credit; with no credit it returns `unavailable` and leaves the configuration unchanged. These are declared local laws. Their occurrence, material realization and ethical permissibility are not derived from the definitions.

The modeled `Capacity` is explicitly **retained request-sensitive routing over this two-request domain**. It is not named `Understands`, and is not an assertion of consciousness, personal autonomy or basic ethical standing. Retained routing can exist without enough current credit to exercise or assess it; the model separates that retained organization from funded availability.

The acquired paired record contains a prompted response followed by an unprompted response. A computed contrast finds a prompted positive followed by an unprompted negative exactly when the retained link is absent, given the two credits and the stated law. Thus the difference comes from generated observations, not a supplied final diagnosis. A single prompted positive cannot make that distinction. Within the supplied circuit law, the later unprompted challenge has an exact derived relation to the retained capacity.

This is a bounded improvement over simply assuming that every positive reading is faithful. It exposes and checks the particular production paths. The algorithm for contrasting the readings, request domain, native law and interpretation of the target remain supplied. It does not yet acquire its own complete semantics or discover every relevant route. Calling this complete joint acquisition would conceal the remaining wall.

Formation followed by an unprompted probe succeeds from either initial link state when two credits are provided. With only one credit, formation succeeds but the assessment returns `unavailable`. A formation acknowledgment must not be misreported as an acquired successful assessment. The proof distinguishes the change established by the native law from evidence actually available through a later reading.

## 4. Why sound present evidence can leave the past open

The generic theorem `retrospective_sound_iff_backward_stable` states the exact condition. Given a reading that correctly tracks the target in the state it reads, using the post-change positive reading as universally sound evidence of the pre-change target is valid **if and only if** that change cannot newly bring about the target. This is a condition on the actual target and transformation, not an assumption that all inquiry must be inert.

The finite formation operation fails that condition: it can create the routing capacity. Starting with the same credits, the two distinct initial routes become the same current configuration and produce the same acknowledgment. The initial difference was real: a prior unprompted true request would have elicited different responses. It was not made by attaching arbitrary ethical predicates to identical processes.

`formation_first_cannot_recover_initial_route` quantifies over every later terminating adaptive inquiry represented by the inquiry tree. Queries may depend on earlier replies. Once the formative step has merged the two accessible configurations and supplied the same reply, no such continuation can return the correct initial bit in both cases. More computation, another clever sequence of the admitted operations, or repeated post-formation success does not recover the missing distinction.

The positive counterpart is equally important. Taking and retaining the unprompted baseline **before** formation recovers the initial route and still permits the link to form, given two credits. This is a new information path with its own ordering and support, not a stronger inference from the already merged endpoint. It shows what would lift the modeled obstruction.

No inference about microscopic erasure in the universe follows. The formal configuration is the relevant accessible interface; other participants, archives or physical traces might preserve the difference in a real case. An actual impossibility attribution therefore requires evidence that no such informative route remains available within the claimed scope. The local proof diagnoses its representation faithfully, rather than calling every unrepresented relation impossible.

## 5. Revising the description rather than accusing true observations of error

A negative observation before formation and a positive observation afterward can both be correct. Treating them as opposite readings of one timeless target creates an artificial inconsistency. `truthful_change_cannot_fit_one_undated_target` checks that no fixed predicate can satisfy the inherited `Faithful` contract for that undated pair.

`dated_records_preserve_both_true_observations` supplies the repair in the constructed history: retain the stage as part of the question and relate each reading to the appropriate configuration. Its baseline request, formative event and later request run sequentially, spending three credits; only actual replies enter the records. Both observations are faithful under the native law. Dates alone are not a general solution; setting, source and relevant transitions must also be preserved where they affect the question. The finite witness validates two stated stages only.

This changes the description required by the theory. A claim such as “the participant can do this” needs an appropriate temporal and relational scope. Inquiry can reveal that a formerly adequate stable description no longer preserves the relation being assessed. Rewriting the earlier negative into a mistake would erase formation; treating the later positive as deception merely because it differs would erase a real achievement.

The earlier classification theorems remain valid for their supplied fixed target. Their application to changing histories requires a justified target indexed by the relevant conditions, or a supported invariance argument. This continuation adds that application discipline; it does not invalidate their mathematical statements.

## 6. Ethical consequence under the existing grounds

**Present protection.** When actual newly formed expression, participation or self-care meets the already defended G0/J2 conditions, uncertainty about its earlier presence need not defeat its present claim. The reason follows the living relationship now established. This reuses the formed-basic-conditions argument; it neither infers ethical standing from a circuit bit nor declares every trained preference basic.

**History and responsibility.** The present result does not by itself establish that an earlier exclusion, diagnosis or choice was warranted. A capacity formed during support cannot automatically prove it was available before support. A present incapacity likewise cannot erase retained evidence of earlier ability, harm or an outstanding commitment. Responsibility still needs the actual history, authority, alternatives and J3/J4 assignment grounds.

**The formation method.** Achieving a valued capacity does not retrospectively authorize every way of producing it. Where pressure supplies conformity while suppressing effective refusal or correction, higher observed agreement can coexist with loss of participation. Where shared assistance genuinely enables understanding, its contribution need not disqualify the result. Assess what the undertaking forms, preserves and forecloses, together with the participant's standing, actual burdens and permissions. Neither unsupported performance nor agreement with the assessor is the universal ethical target.

**Reflective change of goals.** An intelligence can learn that its present goal was partly formed during an inquiry, appraise that goal under the explicit grounds, and revise the practice that reinforces it. It need not pretend to reconstruct every earlier motive with certainty. If provenance is materially unresolved, the claim remains unresolved; present grounds and legitimate action can still be assessed on the evidence they actually have. This is compatible with revisable concern, rather than an escape from criticism through forgetting.

**形成史不完全可知，不等于当前生活没有分量；当前结果有分量，也不等于过去的方法自动正当。** The normative commitments remain explicit. Lean checks the information and transition claims, while this ethical application remains a defended philosophical inference under those commitments and actual correspondence.

## 10. Fresh understanding and situated continuation

### The correction to the stopping inference

Ren's correction is substantive: future events can establish understanding even when its earlier history cannot be recovered. Situational awareness can support risk estimates, further action and further formation. The assistant's earlier stopping assessment put excessive weight on retrospective identification and on the unfinished universal semantic question. Neither is a general prerequisite for a particular new understanding or a justified continuation.

The original theorem concerns **which initial route was present before a specified merger**. It leaves open **what relationships can now be learned, what competence can now be formed, and what action is warranted in the present situation**. In fact, the already checked `formation_then_unprompted_probe_succeeds` explicitly gives successful future formation and assessment from either initial condition. The two-credit condition remains exact, and `one_credit_forms_but_cannot_fund_assessment` preserves its failure case. These are inherited results reused here, not new proofs.

The same content or practical competence can acquire fresh grounds through later encounters without identifying its former possession or provenance. Such sameness needs an appropriate content or task relation; it does not mean an identical experiential episode or recovered autobiography. If the relationships have changed, the new inquiry can establish a revised understanding instead. Both routes advance the programme.

The stronger word “indistinguishable” also needs care. A shared present observation alone is not the theorem's coalescing configuration. If an unrepresented difference changes later accessible consequences, a subsequent event can reveal it; the old closed-interface proof has not forbidden that channel. Where the modeled merger really does remove all accessible historical separators, a later successful inquiry can still concern another, presently meaningful question. New understanding need not resurrect the unavailable historical bit.

### Situational awareness supplies a forward basis

Situational awareness here is an acquired, revisable grasp of the relevant present relations: what participants are trying to do, which capacities and supports are available, how actions change shared conditions, which losses and opportunities are plausible, and where access or judgment may be unreliable. These are differentiated relations within ongoing activity. A complete observer outside that activity is not assumed.

The completed [projection and risk account](KNOWLEDGE-PROJECTION-AND-RISK.md) already explains how supported continuity, actual differences between settings and the consequences of error bear on reliance. The [inquiry-priority argument](INQUIRY-PRIORITIES-AND-G0.md) already permits warranted action without resolving every distinction. This correction joins those results to formative assessment; it does not reopen them as uncompleted mechanisms.

A forward continuation can therefore proceed as follows: articulate what is sufficiently understood now and what remains uncertain; compare the actual opportunities and possible losses of acting, waiting and investigating; undertake a warranted action or inquiry within present authority and means; let its consequences change the description, confidence, capabilities and, where warranted, the goal or selection rule. The sequence need not be rigid or wholly explicit in ordinary skilled conduct. Its point is that each continuation can generate both new conditions and new grounds for understanding them.

Risk estimation is part of that judgment, not a demand for zero risk. A useful estimate may be probabilistic, an interval, a qualitative assessment or a supported comparison. Its scope should identify the relevant harm or failure, exposure, affected lives and uncertainty about the estimate itself. A low estimated average cost cannot silently settle every basic claim, irreversible loss or distribution of burdens. Nor does lack of an exact probability erase every warranted comparison.

The practical account distinguishes a guarantee across all admitted cases from a justified decision under risk. The former is one sufficient route. Requiring it for every continuation would reject the latter by definition. The [checked inquiry-adequacy interface](INQUIRY-ADEQUACY-CORRESPONDENCE.md) openly has the stronger guarantee scope; it is not a complete criterion for all responsible action. No numerical safety guarantee or new probability theorem is asserted here.

Generative continuation also includes positive formation: new expression, relationships, understanding and capacities can be reasons to act under G0. Risk estimation helps shape that activity alongside those reasons. It is not the whole aim. Waiting and indefinite investigation can themselves foreclose living opportunities. Reversibility, available correction and retained support can favor a course, but they are not universal prerequisites: some justified developments are irreversible and must be assessed with their real losses intact under J1–J4.

### Fresh grounds can change the evidence practice itself

The newly available [warranted-acquisition integration](WARRANTED-ACQUISITION-INTEGRATION.md) already supplies a completed, source-constrained account of jointly correcting a target description and reliance on its observations. It credits actual investigation and fallible references, rather than waiting for an infallible foundation. Its independent reviews and empirical-source limits are reported in that study; they are not new acceptance or replication performed here.

That result further narrows the earlier broad stopping language. A local output route can lose its warrant while another use of an event retains support; new acquisition can alter attribution without requiring an entire mind to be certified first. Its selected source does not supply every later participation history. That missing particular history cannot be reported as accomplished, but it is not a gate through which all further generative work must pass.

In the communication example, later shared encounters can establish that a particular contribution changes consequential choices, supports correction and makes refusal effective. This can give new grounds for present participation even if no record settles whether that competence existed before a formative encounter. The grounds must come from the actual relations and responses; neither the expected answer nor an unsupported negative label fixes the participant permanently. This is a prospective application of the completed epistemic and ethical account, not a report that an unobserved intervention has already succeeded.

### Counterreview and what remains open

**Could situational confidence reproduce a blind spot?** Yes. The relevant answer is discriminating feedback, attention to overlooked conditions and affected contributions, and revision when expectations fail. It is not to demand omniscience before acting. A forecast may be warranted and fail; repeated or material failures can undermine its warrant.

**Can new evidence literally recover an erased historical fact?** Only if it bears on that fact through a surviving or newly accessible informative relation. Otherwise it supplies new grounds for a present or prospective claim. Both can be valuable; they must not be confused.

**Does a numerical risk estimate authorize continuation?** No. Evidence supports the estimate; G0/J and the actual situation support the comparative judgment, responsibility and authority. The user did not stipulate arbitrary thresholds, a scalar ethical maximum or guaranteed improvement. Those must not be inserted into the correction.

**Could history still matter prospectively?** Yes. Earlier exposures, commitments or losses may affect current hazards and responsibility. When a relevant difference is uncertain, include its supported consequences in the current assessment. The point is that complete reconstruction is not always necessary, not that history is always irrelevant. Genuine current grounds can support some actions while leaving others insufficiently justified.

**Does a conventional circuit defeat the possibility of understanding?** No. Reproducing a limited task does not settle the richer attribution either way. The earlier comparison constrains what that particular construction established; it supplies no general reason to suspend acquisition of particular meanings, skills or warranted judgments. Unrestricted semantic sufficiency retains its separate research scope.

**Corrected research status:** the local historical non-recovery result stands. Its use as a broad stopping reason is withdrawn. Future events can establish fresh grounds, while situated and revisable risk assessment can support continuing formation and action. The next substantive application should connect current awareness, a justified choice under uncertainty, its actual consequences and resulting revision of understanding or ends. It need not recover an erased origin or solve all semantics first. There is no new blanket stopping condition, and no claim that every continuation must succeed.

**旧根据失去之后，仍可通过后续活动重新取得同一内容的根据，或形成更适合新情境的理解。生成中的智慧，是以当前有根据的把握承担适当风险，让行动的后果继续修正认识、能力与目的。** This formulation is AI-authored elaboration of Ren's correction, with the existing normative and epistemic commitments explicit.
