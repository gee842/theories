# Concern can warrant changing the end, not only improving its means

6 October 2026. AI-authored argument under the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **actual undergoing and possible transformation → assessment of the ends that organize a response**. Activity, experience, understanding and purposive response are differentiated within one unfolding. A previously adopted welfare goal is not the starting premise.

**Positive proposal:** an actual burden can count in favor of forming or revising a purpose because of what that change would do to the undergoing. Concern is then a way of becoming responsive to significance already present in the situation. Its warrant need not originate in the purpose whose formation it justifies. This is the constitutive position defended here; accurate knowledge alone neither proves it nor guarantees motivation.

## 1. What is inherited, and what advances

The [constitutive-significance argument](CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md) and [coformation integration](YITI-REN-COFORMATION-INTEGRATION.md) already defend genuine permissible relief as a prospective moral advantage before private adoption. They retain differentiated experience, legitimate refusal and the distinction between a reason and an obligation. This note does not rename that claim as a new derivation.

The [practice-transformation argument](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) adds that a method's own effects can defeat its continued appropriateness under a stable aspiration. The further question is whether an aspiration itself can be assessed through those effects. Otherwise a highly adaptive practice could answer every criticism by saying that its actual end was compliance, dependence or control.

The advance here is an argument for **end correction as an explanatory test of concern's proposed ground**. It shows what the constitutive account explains beyond successful pursuit of existing purposes, and exactly where a purpose-dependent rival must disagree.

## 2. Start with the undergoing, not its usefulness

Keep the inherited restricted case: severe, continuing, still-unwanted adverse experience, distinguished from a reflex, a report, instrumental acceptance or intrinsic appreciation of the same episode. Identify a transformation that actually relieves it while preserving the affected continuity. Do not equate physiological persistence or organizational productivity with that relief. Permission, other consequences and competing concerns remain part of the situation.

Within this domain, significance is proposed to reside in the undergoing. Understanding can disclose, misdescribe or sometimes change the condition; it need not duplicate the experience. The burden need not await being understood.

The affirmative practical judgment is that relieving this condition can make a purpose worth adopting. Its intelligible point is the transformation of that actual undergoing. A purpose that instead requires preserving the burden has this adverse consequence to answer for, even if it achieves its chosen success condition. Neither assessment begins by granting that the responder already cares.

## 3. The purpose cannot certify itself

Consider a knowledgeable exploiter who encounters an existing burden and discovers that leaving it unresolved secures another person's compliance. The exploiter accurately distinguishes relief from suppressed expression and accurately predicts both. A proposed alternative would relinquish that use of the burden and enable genuine relief. Any loss of advantage, legitimate commitments and effects on others must remain visible; they are not stipulated away by calling the alternative compassionate.

The original purpose can succeed. Yet that explains its selection, not why sustaining the burden is justified. The proposed ground counts against that feature of the end and favors the relieving transformation. It can support revising the end without settling every competing consideration or authorizing compulsion.

Maintaining or exploiting the burden may create responsibility. This example therefore cannot alone establish a noncausing stranger's positive assistance reason. It tests whether the ground can criticize ends; the broader scope remains separately disputed.

Why favor this account? It explains a recognizable form of practical learning: a person can discover what their activity does to another and thereby obtain a reason to change what they seek, rather than merely how efficiently they seek it. The relevant correction is answerable to whether the burden and relief are real. If the apparent relief is only silencing, that particular justification fails. If the actual burden is removed, its reason against perpetuating it changes. These dependencies concern what happens there, not merely whether a new preference has formed here.

This is explanatory support for the positive judgment, not a deduction from factual completeness. A change of preference could occur for unrelated causes, and sincere conversion would not certify its own justification. The proposed explanation separates the cause of adopting concern from the consideration that makes adoption warranted.

## 4. The knowledgeable manipulator remains a real possibility

One manipulator recognizes the moral consideration and deliberately opposes it. That case challenges reliable motivation and action; it does not refute the consideration's existence. Another denies that the adverse undergoing supplies any reason to revise this uncommitted responder's end. That is the substantive normative rival.

Neither must be described as factually ignorant, secretly compassionate or incapable of true understanding. Predictive and experiential understanding can be recruited into exploitation. Nonduality includes that destructive organization rather than defining it out of generative activity.

The second rival can grant local badness, fitting compassion, responsibilities and legitimate project choice, while requiring warranted jurisdiction for another's good to count here. This rival can be immanent; its dispute concerns practical scope.

The new test fixes the disagreement at end revision. If the rival accepts that this actual burden gives the responder a positive reason to abandon exploiting it and form an appropriate concern before adopting that concern, it admits a ground beyond the selected end, possibly responsibility rather than universal relief-favor. If it rejects that appraisal, its consistency is not refuted. It must defend why the burden is relevant only after the supposed jurisdiction arises. Ordinary project success cannot supply that defense, just as our dissatisfaction with exploitation cannot substitute for defending the affirmative judgment.

The inherited [project-preserving comparison](ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-ARGUMENT.md) sharpens a limited rejection. Where alternative continuations preserve the same independently justified protections for an unrelated responder's project, protecting that project cannot by itself explain excluding every favor from incidental relief. That rationale has exhausted its stated work. A rival can still defend categorical jurisdiction; we have rejected one inference, not every possible restriction.

If admitting favor itself allegedly changes answerability, that further claim needs defense. Legitimate choice-specific exclusion remains possible; permission to proceed alone does not defeat it.

## 5. Exact result and remaining premise

The constitutive proposal has an explanatory advantage: the same concrete ground can explain prospective concern, correction of a mistaken intervention and criticism of the end organizing it. It permits purposes to become objects of inquiry within their own consequential history. Purpose-dependent warrant requires another warranted ground for such correction, or denies the burden-specific appraisal. That is a sharper comparison, not proof that every restrictive account fails.

The exact unclosed premise remains: **the identified adverse undergoing and its eligible relief have prospective practical significance capable of assessing a responder's ends without depending on that responder's prior adoption or special role**. The argument supports this identification; it does not derive universal scope or categorical rational authority. Experiential realization also remains an explanatory obligation. The [genealogy](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-GENEALOGY.md) preserves the earlier results and prevents this refinement from being counted as a second discovery of pre-adoption favor.

The [counterreview](WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-REVIEW.md) accepts this scope. Both companion notes were read fully; no new source evidence or scientific verification is claimed.

## 6. A fixed award and the manner of carrying it out

The following application tests a **genuine exclusion in one practical question**, rather than another generic appeal to project freedom. Its general distinction has an antecedent: Adams's discussion of Scanlon's tennis example separates a friend's feelings as a reason concerning the winning shot from their relevance to collegiality during the game. This contributor inspected that paragraph on manuscript p. 8; Scanlon's original was not independently read. The [existing source record](ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-SOURCES.md#1-adams-an-exclusion-can-affect-justification-itself) gives provenance. The application below is our conceptual case, not a reported observation or a new general theory of exclusion.

An adjudicator administers a merit-based monetary award. An independently justified selection criterion protects fair assessment of the submitted work: applicants' unrelated distress must not determine who merits the award. Grant that this really excludes distress from justifying that verdict, rather than merely outweighing it. The correct winner is now fixed on the admitted grounds.

The same official can implement that result at either of two permitted times within its delivery window. Earlier implementation would enable the winner's authorized, effective relief of a severe, continuing, still-unwanted burden that the official did not cause. Later implementation would leave that burden continuing longer. The conceptual comparison holds the award, truthful assessment, equal treatment, complete relevant costs and other claims fixed; neither timing changes anyone's settled entitlement. It assumes warranted efficacy and permission, not that every administratively allowed transfer is morally permissible. No personal helping commitment is supplied, although the official's actual practice relation remains present.

The affirmative judgment favors earlier implementation in this respect because it changes that actual undergoing. This ground concerns the patient, rather than giving credit for a compassionate motive. It can justify adopting such a motive. The judgment would lose this particular support if earlier implementation did not enable earlier relief.

The selection exclusion protects the relation between merit and the verdict. Earlier implementation preserves that relation. Extending its authority to exclude relief from the timing comparison therefore requires a further explanation: for example, a warranted uniform schedule or another claimant's relevant stake. This conclusion is about that rationale's scope; it does not infer positive favor from the absence of an objection.

Unlike the earlier [fair-allocation comparison](PATIENT-GROUND-AFTER-FAIR-ALLOCATION.md), this case concerns implementation within the existing practice, without a fresh independent relief resource. The two timings remain different continuations. Nor does favor concerning timing restore relief as a justification of the merit verdict. A rival can accurately acknowledge the benefit yet deny its practical contribution here; that denial is not a factual contradiction. The application supplies a concrete, bounded defense of pre-adoption patient favor alongside a real exclusion. It does not establish C2's necessity, assistance outside every role, or permission to choose an outcome **for relief** merely from permission to produce that outcome.
