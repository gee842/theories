## 2. What the handoff supplies and what it does not

The old circuit's declared memory is only five register levels and the previous action in two coarse backing slots. An observation table does not fit there by implication. The new protocol honestly adds an initially inert blank 256-symbol cartridge and a paid six-unit installation. The component is an additional supplied initial material input; the energy account can remain unchanged because the cartridge is not an energy credit. Its initial availability and the conversion recipe are not derived from the earlier formation experiment.

Retiring the old binary circuit prevents free duplication of its capabilities. Its `q`, hidden orientation and witness flags may remain in external audit records, but cannot teach the new partition. Thus the handoff establishes an actual formation and resource history for installing the learner. It does not show that the old binary prediction algorithm develops into observation-table learning by itself.

The new three-phase target is a supplied stationary assay. Its readiness bit is a permitted observation, not a label naming its hidden state or the correct equivalence class. The reset law and exact sensor calibration are supplied. Routing an original packet through the phase-2 port is a new supplied coupling law; it must not be described as the unchanged old capture law equating command with orientation. Readiness of a phase is distinct from the availability of a remaining packet.

The target quotient preserves that readiness behavior. It does not quotient away the resources of the whole coupled experiment: source depletion, learner fuel and memory determine what later actions can actually occur. Reset can restore the target phase while leaving those resource states changed.

## 3. Resource and capacity acceptance conditions

Installation leaves **30 fuel before any acquisition work or later capture**. All acquisition, retention, planning and pre-extraction action prefixes must be funded from the actual remaining reserve. A later ten-unit packet transfer cannot pay for its own earlier prerequisites. Source transfer decreases source by the same amount it increases accessible fuel; any discarded packet must instead appear as waste under the declared law.

The protocol's costs are an explicit symbolic tariff: reset, target action and endpoint reading each cost \(1/4\); work and additional persistent-cell writing use \(1/64\); retention uses \(1/65536\) per live cell per declared clock tick. Every event lasts its declared work count plus one tick, including events with no symbolic work. The final retention epoch also costs \(1/64\) per retained cell. These are not Python instruction counts or measured physical energy. The changed retention tariff was declared in the protocol before numerical execution. Its interpretation must remain a local-model premise.

An honest finite capacity account includes retained query words and answers, access/suffix sets, the candidate transition/output table, any simultaneously retained old and revised representation, and bounded temporary work. Unbounded Python objects, a growing counter or a saved transcript cannot become free internal memory. External audit logs are legitimate uncharged readouts only if inaccessible to decisions.

The separate-reset protocol has a useful exact lower bound. With unary alphabet and \(N=3\), the complete certificate queries five words of lengths 0 through 4, with ten total actions. Its experiment bill is

\[
(5+10+5)/4=5.
\]

With two actions, it requires \(1+2+4+8+16=31\) words and \(2+8+24+64=98\) actions, giving

\[
(31+98+31)/4=40.
\]

The latter exceeds the 30 post-installation fuel even before computation and storage. Cache reuse avoids duplicate experiments, but cannot avoid these distinct words under the stipulated separate-reset certificate. The binary regime therefore cannot complete on this handoff without a different funding or experimental law. Its failure should be preserved; improving tariffs after seeing it would change the declared experiment.

## 4. Hidden correctness and certificate boundaries

The learner must receive only its specified alphabet, output type, supplied state bound and acquired endpoint answers. It must derive a mismatch by comparing its own candidate prediction to a paid answer, and derive a new suffix distinction through those answers. Hidden phase identifiers, the target's transition table, a correct partition, an analyst's counterexample, and product-graph validation are prohibited learner inputs.

The all-word certificate needs both the target and candidate in its stated class. If the target has at most \(N\) reachable states and the candidate at most \(N\), agreement through length \(2N-2\) suffices. If the candidate has \(n\) reachable states instead, the corresponding comparison bound is \(N+n-2\) when a disagreement is possible. An implementation should either enforce the candidate's claimed bound or use the appropriate bound. Wrong-bound and corrupted-observation cases must not accidentally inherit a certificate theorem whose hypotheses they violate.

The target's stationarity, faithful reset, calibrated endpoint meaning and justified bound remain supplied. Counterexamples under false versions of these premises are useful precisely because successful learning does not verify those premises itself. The analyst may use the full target table to assess a completed answer, but that result cannot select the learner's next query or repair its candidate for free.

Historical correction and currently accessible memory also differ. A once-correct model may become unusable after failed retention. A successful historical acquisition flag must not be read as current capability. Failed write/capacity events leave the committed archive unchanged while retaining previously sunk charges.

## 5. Comparator, operational use and stop criterion

The expected baseline is ordinary observation-table learning with the same observations, ordering, storage and tariffs. If the two accounts implement equal transitions, they should have equal outputs and expenses. Equality establishes a joined mechanism under these premises; it supplies no distinctive algorithmic contribution. A separately reconstructed result is evidence against an implementation mistake, whereas running one function under two names is only a definitional equality check.

A frozen candidate can consume less while making incorrect continuation predictions. Its lower cost belongs in the result. Inactivity and failure to reach a test are not accurate predictions. Performance should distinguish attempted, completed, certified, retained and actually used models.

The later operational use should exercise an unqueried composition if the claim is reusable abstraction. Replaying an already queried accepted word demonstrates paid lookup use. A word beyond the acquisition test depth, selected through the learned transition structure and preceded by a paid reset when necessary, can provide the stronger use witness. Its success criterion is the actual declared resource transfer, not a hidden correctness score supplied as a reward during acquisition.

**Accept this local bridge only if** an actual paid mismatch leads to an acquired suffix distinction, the final model is minimally represented and all-word correct under valid declared premises, every operation and memory prefix is affordable, and a retained model governs an affordable later use. At least one prespecified regime may satisfy that contract while others fail. The result must remain conditioned on the assay's state bound, sensor/reset law, target question, architecture, cartridge and tariff.

**Stop this branch after that bounded test and comparison.** If the partition or correction is supplied by an oracle, a resource debit is missing, or a retained model cannot be used, do not report acquisition-to-use completion. If the conventional comparator matches, report the mechanism and its scope; do not launch further comparisons merely to seek theoretical superiority. This does not complete the wider causal origin of the architecture or the unified foundation.

## 6. Implementation review

The saved [model](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/model.py) exposes endpoint experiments to the learner, while its hidden target table is used only by the environment and post-run analyst checks. The learner's observed failure on `aa` adds the suffix `a`, separating the formerly merged empty and one-action histories. Its candidates have one and then three states. Its five distinct acquired words have lengths zero through four; the later plan `aaaaa` is absent from that archive and is computed from the retained transition structure.

Two omissions were repaired before acceptance. The first instrumented draft charged rent only to persistent storage during symbolic work, omitting temporary table storage and the duration of reset/action/read events. The repaired meter includes the peak of old/new persistent storage, held table workspace and temporary cells, and charges retention over `work + 1` ticks. This reviewer then found that command words remained live across query and use operations without their own rent outside the table envelope. The port now holds a command/cursor/receipt workspace of `len(word) + 2` cells throughout those operations, restoring the previous workspace afterward. Both repairs increase the accounted cost under the declared rates. Earlier draft bills are not accepted results.

- A separate calculation reconstructs every recorded charge from its work, persistent storage, workspace, temporary storage and tariff. This validates the arithmetic of the declared operation schedule; completeness of the schedule relies additionally on the inspected implementation and its stipulated macro-operation recipe. Under the fixed \(N=3\) protocol, 222 of those acquisition-and-retention schedules fit 36 units and 256 cells. The maximum bill is \(735{,}947/16{,}384\) and the maximum peak is 168 cells. Later productive use is excluded from that count. The enumeration is a finite labeled model class, not a probability distribution over naturally formed systems.

All twelve named fixtures also pass the independent ledger replay. Binary acquisition, fourfold tariffs, capacity 48 and installation with only five units fail without retained acquisition. Excessive final rent leaves a historical finite-test certificate but prevents current retained use. The frozen candidate discovers the same mismatch but refuses revision. The false-bound and broken-reset fixtures accept wrong models, as expected when the semantic premises are false. An inverted sensor yields the exact model of inverted readings but wastes its extraction packet under the true port meaning. These are recorded limits, not corrected by hidden information.

Two further fixtures are derived budget sensitivity checks, not prespecified population arms. Starting with precisely \(B\) funds the entire primary schedule, leaving zero before the ten-unit return. Starting with \(B-1/65{,}536\) retains the learned model but fails to pay `extraction_work`, so it cannot receive the prospective return. The actual witness still starts with its independently inherited 36 units.

The companion [proof](ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION-MATH.md) was independently reviewed for the table-evidence lemma, strict state increase after an acquired counterexample, and the \(2N-2\) certificate bound. Those arguments are sound under their stated hypotheses. The `certified` flag records completed finite tests under supplied premises; `retained` and actual `use` are separate outcomes. In particular, a paid certificate does not retroactively make failed retention a usable capability.

**Final decision:** the acquired-distinction-to-retained-use contract is met in the unary witness. The correct partition and analyst-selected counterexample are not learner inputs. The demonstrated connection is an actual resource ancestry plus new, explicitly supplied learning and experiment laws; it is not a semantic derivation of those laws from the old binary circuit. The ordinary comparator matches, and no distinctive algorithmic superiority follows. This bounded branch can close with its limitations preserved.
