## Scope and contribution

The named connection is **formed, evidence-responsive learning → warranted and revisable inquiry → revision of selectors and reasons**. The research examines necessary conditions of claimed epistemic capabilities while respecting the user's clarified direction: adequate scientific philosophy must cohere with those conditions. It does not substitute the converse for that conjecture, define scientific standing as agreement with the present theory, or infer strict containment of whole philosophical traditions.

**Completed at the bounded conceptual/comparative scope:** conditional necessity arguments, proof/assumption review, primary-text comparison, preserved countermodels, and a prospective empirical identification contract. The full necessity or uniqueness of the proposed minimal root remains unproved. The unified foundation and the actual calibration/reason/succession composition remain ongoing.

## What was checked

The critical reviewer independently read the mechanism note and evidence design. The reviewer accepted the finite adaptive-indistinguishability induction, exact binary total-variation calculation, positive-probability certificate condition, sensor-inversion pair and conditional stock bound under their stated premises. The primary-source numerical summaries in the evidence note were checked against the cited primary excerpts; observational comparisons were not treated as randomized causal evidence, and simulation replication was not treated as a real-world experiment.

The mechanism author separately reviewed the counterexample note and the canonical synthesis. The synthesis preserves a fixed finite prefix and unrestricted alternatives in its induction argument, does not assume a uniform distribution over infinite streams, and distinguishes an available informative experiment from an implemented decoder. The account fixes the same assumptions and meanings when assessing incompatibility. The author also checked the evidence design's information boundaries.

The source reviewer independently accepted the canonical philosophical framing and source table. In particular, the comment about Quine's causal account and normative obligations is the present study's comparative inference, not a quotation of Quine or an asserted settlement of later interpretation. The scope permits philosophical disagreement and does not infer that disagreement itself establishes pseudoscience. The canonical Lakatos addition follows the inspected primary passage on substantive integration versus linguistic reinterpretation.

These are separate collaborating-AI internal reviews, not external peer review or independent empirical replication. This record reports the scope actually reviewed. It does not turn agreement among agents into additional philosophical evidence.

## Corrections made during review

1. **Nonempty feasible-policy class.** The mechanism note now explicitly assumes a nonempty family when taking a supremum over inquiry policies. If inquiry is wholly unavailable, that is a capability failure rather than an undefined numerical advantage.
2. **Empty compatible-model class.** An observation outside every admitted model now explicitly triggers a model/interface mismatch. Vacuous logical entailment must not be implemented as permission to certify arbitrary empirical claims.
3. **Context intervention made discriminating.** The original prospective test compared removal of context metadata within two stable worlds. The reviewer correctly noted that noninformative metadata need not affect performance. The corrected design adds different calibration histories ending in the same current context, so the record's provenance matters to warranted reuse. An unchanged-context control preserves the case where deletion should make no difference. Sound suspension and answer coverage are separate measures.
4. **Scientific status separated from success.** The critical review and canonical formulation explicitly allow a scientifically pursued conjecture to be false or to challenge an alleged necessity. The compatibility requirement concerns adequacy under the established premises, and is not a sufficient demarcation criterion.

No attempted empirical or mathematical validation failed and was hidden. The design correction remains recorded because it changed what a future test could identify.

## Supplied premises and remaining obligations

The proofs supply a finite operational probability model, target alternatives, complete observation boundaries, common policies, and—in particular applications—law/calibration restrictions. The certificate result is semantic soundness relative to an admitted class, not an exhaustive analysis of knowledge. The support result assumes positive cumulative demand or the specified vulnerability laws. The literature supplies philosophical arguments, not a proof that our root is uniquely necessary. Ethical standing and warranted relevance remain separately argued premises.

The most immediate interface is the one shared by the active recipient-evidence and reason-replay work: a positive receipt under a no-false-positive law can qualify a correspondence, whereas nonreceipt may remain unknown. Honest record storage cannot convert that unknown into a sound negative assay. Current context, historical truth, accessible evidence, inferential support and executable action remain distinct.

The next mechanism should explain acquisition and retention of a calibrated correspondence, including a route that can detect relevant sensor errors, and carry its evidence through actual contextual reason revision and successor access. Any reference standard, diagnostic source or incentive to retain inconvenient evidence must be stated and paid for where applicable. The unrun design in the evidence note tests that interface; it does not assume its success or finish the full derivation.
