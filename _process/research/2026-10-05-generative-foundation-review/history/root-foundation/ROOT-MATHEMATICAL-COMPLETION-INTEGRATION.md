## 1. The direct attempt at all mathematics

The strongest candidate investigated here enriches the current root with typed formation and substitution, independently described witness extension, full profile admission at each declared level, higher-level formation, and level-indexed interpretation. These are proposed constitutive resources, not a claim that the current process representation has already derived them. The global axiom system and its consistency have not been established.

This candidate avoids requiring a finite name or effective decision procedure for every mathematical object and truth. Full profiles could admit non-effective relations; higher levels could organize those relations into further subjects. It is more substantial than importing an arbitrary table of sentence answers. Nevertheless, short structural laws can contain substantive existence and semantic commitments.

The attempted derivation of full admission appeals to the absence of arbitrary exclusion: when a subject is specified as every profile satisfying its conditions, deleting an eligible profile changes that subject. This is a valid reason to respect an independently warranted role. It does not establish why the prior root warrants the full profile domain in the first place. Treating the role's existence as already given would assume the disputed step.

The same issue remains at higher levels and at limit admission. Stratification can avoid a particular diagonal obstruction without establishing the availability or scope of every stratum. The [existing completion theorem](COMPLETION-WARRANT-LEAN-INTEGRATION.md) derives a unique realization of its role under its hypotheses; it does not choose that role for every possible mathematical inquiry.

The failed step is therefore exact: **from “every admitted realization must satisfy the specified role” to “the root requires the full range of roles and realizations needed for all mathematics.”** The current argument does not discharge that implication. Fullness and interpretation might still receive an immanent generative explanation; they are not proved to require an external realm or irreducible extra axiom.

## 2. The direct attempt at necessary common dependence

The positive argument begins with indispensable mathematical content. A target-preserving account must retain the identity, operations, admissible relationships and interpreted consequences that make the subject determinate. Differences only in notation or redundant carriers need not constitute another mathematical subject. This supports a common structural account of the intended content.

The further claim is stronger: every adequate account must explain those relationships as depending on this substantive root in the same way. The necessity of the content alone does not establish that explanatory direction. A matched structural-modal account can preserve the same unfolding, mathematical construction, interpretation and inquiry while treating structural admissibility as what specifies coherent formation, or withholding a claim about ultimate priority. It need not assume independent minds and objects or separate constraint and activity into ultimate kinds.

That rival has its own obligations. Calling admissibility primitive is not a completed explanation. The affirmative account could establish that its resources derive from an independently justified generative basis, or show that the rival's complete explanation is another articulation of the same constitutive dependence. Neither reduction has been completed universally. Simply relabeling the rival's retained structure as our root would establish a vocabulary agreement, not the demanded explanation.

The attempted transcendental argument also stops short: even if reasoning about mathematics requires differentiation and rule-governed articulation, that establishes necessary features of the reasoning or content. Their use does not by itself show that they uniquely suffice for the truth or establish the proposed direction of its ground. The nondual method permits jointly constituted relationships; it does not declare their complete mathematical character already derived.

This locates the second unresolved inference: **from necessary shared mathematical content to necessary shared constitutive dependence.** An equivalence chosen to require that dependence would make universal rival equivalence the original obligation again. An equivalence preserving only truth values would be too weak to settle it.

## 5. Integrated conclusion and what would change it

The constructive strategy survives: specify extension rules independently of arbitrary sentence truth, prove their adequacy for a fixed subject, and examine whether the rules themselves follow from the proposed root. The order case strengthens the strategy. Its success alone does not justify extrapolation to every mathematical subject.

The global enriched candidate also remains a substantive proposal. To change the current universal assessment, it needs a justified admission or interpretation principle that is actually derived from the root, rather than adopted because it is needed for coverage. To change the exclusive-grounding assessment, it needs a reduction or independently motivated equivalence addressing the matched rival's explanation, not merely its outputs.

These are unresolved explanatory obligations in the reconstructed theory. The diagonal result is a genuine failure only of its specified self-coverage contract. None of the results establishes that nonduality is false, that the wider root is impossible, or that metaphysical reasoning cannot supply an argument. Conversely, none warrants marking the two requested achievements complete.
