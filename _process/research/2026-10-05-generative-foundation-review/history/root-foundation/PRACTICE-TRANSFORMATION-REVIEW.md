# When a practice must change to continue its work

6 October 2026. AI-authored constructive review under the [shared nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **a practice changes its participants → those changes alter what the practice accomplishes → the continuing relation transforms its method**. This reviews an inherited textual case and existing formal interfaces, without a new controller or scientific run.

## 1. The constructive result

The [inherited Wang episode](WANG-YANGMING-SOURCES.md#25-事上磨炼在有偏见风险的实际事务中修正) describes teaching revised after its own results became problematic. Our reconstruction concerns how a practice can help produce a disposition that makes its continuation questionable. Its participants are not unchanged inputs awaiting a different command.

This supports a coherent generative explanation: the continuing activity includes what teaching forms in learners, what that formation makes possible or difficult, and how subsequent teaching answers it. Preserving that activity can require transforming a method rather than preserving its repetition. The method's identity and the participants' continuity are different relationships within the same history. Neither an eternal protocol nor an observer outside the process is needed to describe their difference.

The source is a transmitted teaching account, not a controlled observation. Our positive result is an intelligible, textually grounded reconstruction of consequential practice revision. It is not a measured causal effect or proof that the reported correction succeeded.

## 2. The strongest rival partly agrees

A serious rival accepts every described change but treats the teacher's aspiration as stable. Quiet sitting and the later emphasis are alternative means to cultivated responsiveness in ordinary affairs. The teacher updates judgments about means while preserving the end and its authority.

This is not an empty explanation or a defeat of the bounded candidate. It already places consequential method revision inside the continuing practice. No principle requires every adequate correction to replace the ultimate aspiration. The actual advance is that the original method's apparent success is reassessed through dispositions it helps form. More of the same observable quiet can cease to count as progress toward the stated purpose. The exercise, its concrete adequacy judgment and the larger aspiration must remain distinguishable; correction of the first two does not prove transformation of the third.

The rival limits a stronger conclusion: the episode does not establish that the aspiration itself became revisable, that learners could contest it, or that its authority was generated by this correction. Nor does a fixed aspiration necessarily imply a detached evaluator. Teacher, learner, aspiration and method can have histories within the same unfolding while this report explains only one part.

## 3. A discriminating contrast

The important contrast is between two consequentially responsive practices. One changes teaching because the previously encouraged disposition obstructs learners' activity beyond that exercise. Another learns that learners resist and changes technique to restore compliance with the teacher's programme. Both can be flexible, observant and effective. Both preserve real effects; neither is a freely toggled label.

To distinguish them, examine which continuation is restored or obstructed: learners' capacities in the affairs the teaching claims to prepare them for, or merely continued participation in the exercise. The contrast must include what happens when those diverge. Treating every withdrawal as a defect requiring more ingenious recruitment would preserve the programme by excluding its affected participants from the explanation of success.

This is a prospective identification test, not a claim that the historical record supplies learner testimony or modern consent procedures. The teacher's interpretation could be accurate, partly accurate or paternalistic. Listening can correct a description without making every expressed preference infallible. Conversely, a learner's difficulty cannot be dismissed merely because the teacher's account labels it attachment. The reconstruction needs evidence about the difficulty, the response and their consequences.

The [fresh source reading](PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-GENEALOGY.md) also records a sharp teacher rebuke. Our interpretation must not idealize his openness or infer demonstrated harm from that episode.

Adequate continuation therefore cannot mean indefinite growth or preservation of this particular method. It may involve changing, suspending or ending an activity while retaining relevant capabilities and acknowledging what happened. A fixed practice that remains suitable is not defective merely because it does not change.

## 5. Scope of the affirmative assessment

The candidate earns a positive assessment at the level of consequential practice transformation: what an activity forms can become a reason, within its continuing inquiry, to change how it proceeds. That relation can be explained without first separating a neutral process from an external meaning-giver. The ethical assessment still turns on which participants and consequences the continuation preserves, including burdens that productive teaching might conceal.

The [recipient-continuation account](../../../history/2026-10-05-generative-health/succession/RECIPIENT-CONTINUATION.md) makes that target more exact: support must connect to independently identified activity of the affected recipient, not only the responder's programme. This does not require translating Wang into a resource ledger. It prevents either an unchanged balance or a more productive practice from standing in for the life it claims to serve. Universal ethical necessity and the stronger foundation remain open; consequential correction is a substantive part of their investigation, not their announced completion.
