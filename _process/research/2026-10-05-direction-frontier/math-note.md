# Conditional direction, paid response formation, and the obstruction to universal inclusion

Date: 2026-10-05. Independent mathematical continuation audit. This is a research memo, not an amendment to the canonical theory. Its strongest positive result is conditional and local: a specified, physically payable response channel can form from zero within a supplied construction repertoire and spread when its retained-resource advantage exceeds sensing, repair, and successor construction costs **after the cheapest evasion or substitution is included**. This does not entail universal wisdom, impartial concern, or monotonically widening inclusion.

## 2. An exact directional criterion that does not assume a universal trend

Let two faithfully reproduced types have strictly positive populations and reproduction factors:

\[
n_i(t+1)=W_i(t)n_i(t),\qquad W_i(t)>0,\quad i\in\{0,1\}.
\]

Then

\[
\log\frac{n_1(T)}{n_0(T)}
=\log\frac{n_1(0)}{n_0(0)}
+\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\log\frac{W_1(t)}{W_0(t)}.
\]

**Proposition 1.** Type 1 approaches relative share one if and only if the cumulative log advantage on the right tends to positive infinity. This is an exact two-type criterion, not a claim that the criterion holds for a morally preferred type.

**Proof.** Divide the two recursions, multiply through time, and take logarithms. The share is the odds divided by one plus the odds. It tends to one exactly when the odds tend to infinity. ∎

A positive advantage at every date need not suffice: if the log advantages are positive but summable, the final odds remain finite. A larger arithmetic average reproduction factor is also insufficient. If type 1 alternates between factors \(1/2\) and \(3\), while type 0 always has factor \(3/2\), type 1's arithmetic average is \(7/4>3/2\), but its relative abundance is multiplied by \(2/3\) every two steps. There is no contradiction: products determine lineage growth.

For a trait \(g_i\), a census with descendant mean trait \(g_i'\) satisfies

\[
\bar W(\bar g'-\bar g)
=\operatorname{Cov}_p(W,g)
+\sum_i p_iW_i(g_i'-g_i).
\]

The first term is differential reproduction of the declared parental trait; the second is transmission or transformation. Neither term has a universally positive sign. This is an accounting identity until mechanisms determine \(W_i\), variation, and transmission. The existing fixed-trait argument is therefore a diagnostic, not a derivation of wisdom from selection.

Primary lineage: George Price, *Selection and Covariance*, Nature 227, 520–521 (1970), https://www.nature.com/articles/227520a0; Steven A. Frank, *Natural selection. IV. The Price equation* (2012), https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.1515 and https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2012.02498.x. ## 3. Causal return, bypass, and concern are distinct

Fix recipient harm \(H>0\). A decision maker has exactly three available actions:

| Action | Private cost | Harm to the original recipient |
|---|---:|---:|
| Protect | \(c\) | \(0\) |
| Ignore | \(d\) | \(H\) |
| Bypass the returned consequence | \(e\) | \(H\) |

Costs are nonnegative. The private decision rule minimizes cost. Bypass may mean avoiding liability, replacing a damaged supplier, leaving the interaction, suppressing a report, or moving the harm outside the maintained boundary; the model does not assume that any particular implementation is actually available in a given application.

**Proposition 2.** Protection is the unique private optimum if and only if

\[
c<\min(d,e).
\]

If a supplied evaluative weight \(v\ge0\) adds disvalue \(vH\) to each harmful action, protection is uniquely selected exactly when

\[
vH>c-\min(d,e).
\]

**Proof.** Compare protection's cost with both alternatives. Their lower evaluated cost is \(\min(d,e)+vH\). Strict inequality is necessary and sufficient for a unique minimum. ∎

At equality, protection ties the cheapest harmful action; it is available as an optimum but is not forced by minimization. When \(c<\min(d,e)\), even \(v=0\) selects protection. When \(c=\min(d,e)\), any positive \(v\) selects it uniquely, while \(v=0\) leaves a tie. Thus the threshold should not be stated as a universal requirement that a positive concern weight must exist.

Increasing the returned loss \(d\) stops changing the comparison after it exceeds \(e\). If \(e<c\), arbitrarily strong return through the first route cannot force protection. This is an open parameter region, not a zero-measure pathology. Perfectly representing \(H\) in a world model does not entail \(v>0\): representation supplies a fact; evaluation determines how that fact enters action choice. Nor does permutation-covariant physical dynamics imply impartial preference. A rule can retain a self/other or owner/outsider distinction under consistent relabeling.

The term \(vH\) is an evaluative term, not automatically a material expenditure. A physiological or computational implementation may itself have costs, but those must be modeled separately. This proposition supplies no origin theorem for \(v\), no selection theorem for general concern, and no guarantee that learned concern generalizes to new recipients.

## 4. A minimal paid model in which an effective response channel can be favored

### Supplied environment, repertoire, and timing

This is a deterministic population-mass model with divisible resources and construction. It can represent a continuum of trials or exact aggregate fractions. It is not an almost-sure theorem about finite, integer populations.

Every generation follows this order:

1. Each current parent obtains resource \(A>0\), measured **after** common upkeep, common constructor operation, and other costs identical between types. The recurring resource source is supplied; this is an open system, not a closed-universe model.
2. A fraction \(\lambda\in[0,1]\) of its interactions exposes an affected target to harm \(H>0\). Harm is scored independently of private resource success.
3. Costs of avoiding or responding to that hazard are paid. An installed channel can route a physically available warning to an attempted repair in time; the recipe, warning availability, and timing law are stipulated. The attempted protocol costs \(a\) on each hazard trial and succeeds with fraction \(q\in[0,1]\). A failed trial still pays its cost.
4. The parent's retained resources finance constructed successors, with construction costs charged before the next census. Parents retire; successors become the next parents.

Two construction recipes are in the allowed repertoire:

- Type 0 has no effective response channel. On a hazard it can accept private loss \(d\) or use the cheaper available bypass at cost \(e\). Either leaves the original recipient harmed. Write \(b=\min(d,e)\).
- Type 1 pays channel upkeep \(c\), attempts repair at cost \(a\) on hazards, and avoids both recipient harm and the private fallback loss when repair succeeds. A failed repair uses the same cheapest fallback \(b\).

All \(c,a,d,e\) are nonnegative. For branch-by-branch prefunding, \(A\ge c+a+b\) is a simple sufficient condition; the conclusions below require retained resources to be strictly positive. The model treats the stipulated precursor and actuation timing as part of the effective channel's specified implementation. It does not obtain information for free by assuming an omniscient decision maker.

The resulting retained resources per current parent are

\[
S_0=A-\lambda b,\qquad
S_1=A-c-\lambda a-\lambda(1-q)b.
\]

A type 0 successor costs \(B>0\); a type 1 successor costs \(B+K\), where \(K\ge0\) pays the additional assembly of its response channel. With faithful construction and no exploratory allocation,

\[
W_0=\frac{S_0}{B},\qquad W_1=\frac{S_1}{B+K}.
\]

**Theorem 3: net response advantage.** Given these mechanisms and \(S_0,S_1>0\), type 1 has strictly higher reproduction exactly when

\[
\boxed{\lambda b(Bq+K)>B(c+\lambda a)+KA.}
\]

**Proof.** Direct subtraction gives

\[
W_1-W_0
=\frac{\lambda b(Bq+K)-B(c+\lambda a)-KA}{B(B+K)}.
\]

The denominator is positive. ∎

This is noncircular: the trait is specified as a concrete recipient-protecting operation, while the advantage is derived from independently declared resource flows and successor construction costs. It does not define whatever reproduces as wise or regenerative. When \(K=0,q=1\), the condition becomes \(\lambda(b-a)>c\), the avoided private loss exceeding the channel's operating costs. When \(K>0\), successful current operation is not enough unless its successor construction can compete as well.

The condition concerns private continuation enabled by actual recipient repair. It can select protective behavior without a positive evaluative concern weight. It does not establish that private loss and recipient harm are always aligned, or that all affected recipients have such a channel.

Bypass bounds \(b\le e\). Consequently, increasing \(d\) alone cannot produce the strict advantage if

\[
\lambda e(Bq+K)\le B(c+\lambda a)+KA.
\]

This is the selection-level analogue of Proposition 2. Expanding causal interaction can increase opportunities for bypass or replacement as well as opportunities for repair.

## 5. Paid response formation from zero, within a supplied repertoire

A reproduction comparison by itself assumes the response type already exists. The following extension makes construction of that type an actual transaction.

Supply a fixed exploration fraction \(\mu\in(0,1]\) and a fixed experimental allocation \(\alpha\in(0,1)\). Each parent's **actual retained resources** are allocated as follows: fraction \(1-\mu\) to its own type, fraction \(\mu\alpha\) to constructing type 1, and fraction \(\mu(1-\alpha)\) to constructing type 0. Resource sent to a recipe buys successor mass at that recipe's stated unit construction cost. In particular, expensive variants buy fewer successors.

The construction repertoire and exploration allocation rule are supplied. This is not an explanation of arbitrary technology invention, the origin of a universal constructor, selection for \(\mu\), or why experimental resources are allotted with this \(\alpha\). It is an explicit theorem about paid trial constructions from an available repertoire, including construction of a response channel absent from the current population.

Let \(n=(n_0,n_1)^T\). Then \(n'=Mn\), where

\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
(1-\mu\alpha)W_0 & \mu(1-\alpha)S_1/B\\
\mu\alpha S_0/(B+K) & [1-\mu(1-\alpha)]W_1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

**Theorem 4: paid formation and conditional amplification.** Assume \(S_0,S_1>0\), \(B>0\), \(K\ge0\), \(0<\mu\le1\), and \(0<\alpha<1\).

1. Every successor is paid for: with \(C=(B,B+K)\),
   \[
   Cn'=S_0n_0+S_1n_1.
   \]
2. Starting from \(n_0>0,n_1=0\), positive responsive mass is constructed at the first successor census.
3. From every nonzero nonnegative initial population, the responsive share converges to a unique strictly interior share \(x_*\).
4. Holding all other parameters fixed and then taking \(\mu\downarrow0\), this stationary responsive share tends to one if \(W_1>W_0\), and to zero if \(W_1<W_0\).
5. At \(W_1=W_0\), the limiting ratio is the capital-adjusted construction baseline
   \[
   r_*:=\frac{x_*}{1-x_*}=\frac{\alpha B}{(1-\alpha)(B+K)},
   \]
   for every positive \(\mu\).

**Proof.** For statement 1, multiply each column of \(M\) by the corresponding successor construction costs and add. In column \(i\), all allocated fractions sum to one, giving exactly \(S_i\). Statement 2 follows because \(M_{10}=\mu\alpha S_0/(B+K)>0\).

For the remaining statements, all matrix entries are positive. After one census both populations are positive. Their ratio obeys

\[
f(r)=\frac{M_{10}+M_{11}r}{M_{00}+M_{01}r}.
\]

The fixed-point equation is

\[
M_{01}r^2+(M_{00}-M_{11})r-M_{10}=0.
\]

Its leading coefficient is positive and the product of its roots is negative, so there is exactly one positive root \(r_*\). Moreover,

\[
\det M=(1-\mu)W_0W_1\ge0,\qquad
f'(r)=\frac{\det M}{(M_{00}+M_{01}r)^2}\ge0.
\]

The sign of \(f(r)-r\) is positive below \(r_*\) and negative above it. Monotonicity of \(f\) prevents crossing the fixed point: starting on either side, successive ratios move monotonically toward it, remain bounded by it, and converge to a fixed point by continuity. At \(\mu=1\), \(f\) is constant and convergence occurs after one step. This proves statement 3.

For statement 4, put \(u=M_{10}\), \(v=M_{01}\), and \(h=M_{00}-M_{11}\). As \(\mu\downarrow0\), \(u,v\) approach zero proportionally to \(\mu\), and \(h\to W_0-W_1\). If \(W_0>W_1\), the positive root can be written \(2u/(h+\sqrt{h^2+4uv})\), which tends to zero. If \(W_1>W_0\), write it as \((-h+\sqrt{h^2+4uv})/(2v)\), which tends to infinity. Converting odds to shares proves the claim. At equality, substituting the proposed positive ratio into the quadratic proves statement 5; uniqueness finishes the argument. ∎

The order of limits matters. If \(\mu\) is set to zero before starting from an absent response type, it never forms. If positive \(\mu\) is retained, reverse construction prevents fixation at share one. Sending \(\mu\) toward zero after the long-run share is computed describes a family of processes; it does not itself give a schedule for decreasing exploration.

Nor is inclusion globally monotone: a population starting above its stationary response share moves downward toward it. Starting from zero produces a positive share, but when response has a disadvantage that share tends to zero with the supplied exploration budget. Exploration maintains those constructions; selection does not favor them.

## 6. Relative prevalence, absolute continuation, and recipient harm

Let total successor-construction capital represented by the current population be

\[
E=Bn_0+(B+K)n_1.
\]

The exact balance gives

\[
E'=Bn_0W_0+(B+K)n_1W_1,
\]

and hence

\[
\min(W_0,W_1)E\le E'\le\max(W_0,W_1)E.
\]

Thus if both factors are below one, capital tends to zero, even though one type becomes the relative majority. If both are above one, capital grows geometrically in the deterministic model. If the factors straddle one, these two bounds alone do not decide growth; one can use the dominant root of the explicitly specified matrix. None of these deterministic mass conclusions guarantees infinite survival of a finite stochastic lineage.

For an independently fixed exposure weighting, expected harm per current interaction is

\[
\lambda H(1-qx),\qquad x=\frac{n_1}{n_0+n_1}.
\]

A larger responsive share reduces this specified harm measure when \(\lambda Hq>0\). It does not necessarily reduce total harm when the number of interactions grows. It also leaves every omitted dimension of welfare, agency, externality, fairness, or future consequence unevaluated. This is one effective protection dimension, not a scalar definition of wisdom.

## 7. A constructive open-region counterexample, with both populations capable of growth

Set

\[
A=20,\quad B=10,\quad K=1,\quad c=a=1,\quad
\lambda=1/2,\quad q=1,\quad d=12.
\]

Then \(W_1=37/22\), and responsive selection requires

\[
b>70/11\approx6.3636.
\]

With cheap bypass \(e=2\), \(b=2\) and \(W_0=19/10>37/22>1\). With expensive bypass \(e=10\), \(b=10\) and \(W_1=37/22>3/2=W_0>1\). Increasing \(d\) from 12 to 100 changes neither case because bypass is already cheaper.

For \(\alpha=1/2\):

| Exploration fraction \(\mu\) | Responsive stationary share, \(e=2\) | Responsive stationary share, \(e=10\) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.05 | 0.163002 | 0.800656 |
| 0.005 | 0.019447 | 0.975145 |
| 0.0005 | 0.001976 | 0.997462 |

All displayed strict inequalities persist under sufficiently small parameter perturbations. Both types have reproduction factors above one, so the counterexample is not an artifact of comparing two vanishing populations. A growing system can select cheaper bypass over more effective protection. Conversely, when bypass is sufficiently costly, the same supplied response channel is selected and its harm reduction can spread.

## 8. What the model does and does not settle

The strongest warranted direction statement is:

> Within a supplied construction repertoire and an ongoing resource regime, actual paid formation plus cumulative retained-resource advantage can amplify effective protection. That advantage must survive response costs, successor costs, transmission losses, and the cheapest available evasion or substitution. These conditions can hold and can fail in open parameter regions.

A mechanism may therefore widen **effective responsibility** when others' condition is consequential, the consequence cannot be more cheaply evaded, information reaches an operative response, and preserving the affected party is a viable intervention. This is a conditional causal statement with explicit competing pathways.

Widening the causal graph alone is insufficient. A damaged supplier can become more consequential and simultaneously more replaceable. A model can accurately predict an external party's harm while assigning it zero independent disvalue. A growing coalition can maintain increasingly elaborate coordination while excluding expendable parties. None contradicts the local mathematics.

The outstanding foundation problem is the formation, persistence, and generalization of evaluative concern: why should a response weight extend beyond currently indispensable, visible, related, or reciprocating parties, including when bypass is easy? The static weight \(v\) makes this missing variable explicit but does not explain its origin. Models of learning, generalization, shared fate, recognition, institutional commitment, or inclusive representation could address it; their hypotheses and costs would need separate derivation and counterexamples.

Local counterexamples refute claims quantified over all systems or all trajectories. They do not by themselves refute every statistical conjecture about a specified ensemble of worlds. Such a conjecture requires a probability measure, a sampled class, an independently defined target observable, and a time horizon. Without those, “reality tends toward wisdom” has no unique mathematical meaning to test. The prior recurrent-class obstruction additionally rules out a nonconstant globally nondecreasing expected scalar on finite irreducible stationary recurrent classes, but does not cover arbitrary expanding, nonstationary, or infinite state systems.
