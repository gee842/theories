# The direction of reality: from shared becoming to wider, wiser regeneration

Research note, 5 October 2026. Prepared for the resumed direction inquiry. This is a constructive conceptual and mathematical proposal, with selected primary evidence. No canonical theory is edited. Its new distinctions and test designs are not presented as established universal laws.

## 1. Reconstruct the thesis at its strongest

Ren's proposal is more substantial than “survivors survive,” “cooperation helps,” or “we should build fair institutions.” The source statement connects natural evolution, the dynamics of the brain and other species, conscience, generativity, and competing directions called 善 and 恶. The latest correction makes the explanatory subject the whole differentiated situation rather than an independently complete individual.

The most charitable strong reconstruction is:

> The activity of reality can form organizations that participate in sustaining the conditions of further activity. In some of them, the shared situation becomes felt, represented, and reconsidered from within. Their capacity to understand consequences can become a capacity to revise how those consequences are produced, including consequences for other centers. The proposed deeper direction is a tendency for this reflexive generativity to become more adequate to the shared situation—wiser, and more inclusive of the centers through which that situation has stakes.

This does not say all events are benevolent, every organism must survive, or every limit must disappear. Its subject is a possible **direction in the organization of becoming**, including transformations of what counts, who can respond, and how responses themselves are formed. Biological survival and institutions are realizations or tests of portions of this question, not its definition.

“From within” matters. Moral understanding need not be an alien command added to a value-free natural machine. Felt harm, concern, recognition, deliberation, commitment and their revision can themselves be events in the same world. Distinguishing the warrant of a moral conclusion from the cause of a behavior does not divide reality into two substances. It only avoids treating one kind of evidence as automatically proving the other.

The explanatory challenge is therefore positive: **how does one center's condition come to matter within another center's regulation, beyond the first center's usefulness to the second—and how does that change become capable of correcting and renewing itself?**

## 3. Five different directions that must connect without collapsing

| Level | Direction being proposed | What would count as an explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Becoming | Products alter the conditions of subsequent transformations | An actual causal pathway, not just later events following earlier ones |
| Regeneration | Some produced organization replenishes vulnerable enabling conditions | Formation, turnover, work supply, and gain sufficient to offset losses |
| Wisdom | Consequential error or incompleteness can change models, actions, interfaces, and judgment | Discrimination, learning, executable correction, and correction of the correcting process |
| Inclusive responsiveness | Conditions of additional affected centers influence what the process protects or repairs | Transfer of concern into policy under cost, including centers without reciprocal power |
| Direction of reality | These developments have a systematic formation, persistence, accessibility, or prevalence bias | A specified ensemble/process, measured direction, causal mechanism and counterfactual comparison |

The last row is not established by defining the first four. Nor is the fifth meaningless until there is a cosmological theorem. It can be investigated in increasing scope, beginning with independently defined classes of driven, learning, interacting systems.

A major distinction within “inclusion” is necessary:

1. **Representation:** the process can predict what happens to another center.
2. **Valuation:** that consequence can count against an action even when it does not harm the decision maker.
3. **Execution:** the valued consequence can change what is actually done.
4. **Retention and revision:** the route survives cost, changing circumstances, and succession, while remaining open to evidence of its own errors.

A skilled exploiter may achieve the first exceptionally well. A compassionate but powerless observer may achieve the first two. A temporary rescue may achieve three without four. A universal wisdom hypothesis needs the transitions among these, not a single scalar called awareness.

## 4. The precise missing bridge: symmetry of the world is weaker than impartial concern

This is a simple but important formal distinction that improves the earlier discussion of nonseparation.

Let x=(x1,…,xn) be a constructed vector of comparable outcome qualities, and let i be the evaluating center. Physical relabeling symmetry naturally permits a family of evaluations satisfying

\[
U_{\sigma(i)}(\sigma x)=U_i(x)
\]

for every relabeling permutation sigma. The perfectly egoistic family

\[
U_i(x)=x_i
\]

satisfies this symmetry exactly. No center is privileged in the laws; each nonetheless privileges itself in its policy. An agent can model the entire web of dependence accurately while having this evaluation rule.

Therefore shared reality, physical symmetry, shared representations, and accurate perspective-taking do not by themselves erase a valuation gate indexed to “me” or “my group.” The crucial empirical transition is whether reasons and valences **transfer across the index of the evaluator**, not merely whether the model represents that index correctly.

A conditional proposition makes the stronger bridge explicit. Suppose a common evaluation J is invariant under permutations of otherwise equivalent centers, and increasing x1 alone strictly improves J at every admissible profile. Then increasing any xj alone strictly improves J: permute j into the first position, apply the premise, and undo the permutation. This is a reason-portability result under a substantive common-evaluation premise, not a derivation of that premise from physics. It neither imposes a sum nor fixes all conflicts, rights, or incomparable interests.

The promising question is now sharp: **what natural processes cause index-sensitive self-concern to develop portable other-regarding concern, and what preserves that portability when it is costly?** It is a question about learning, embodied valuation, social encounter and inheritance as well as institutions.

## 5. A positive mechanism hypothesis: valuation generalization and reflexive inclusion

A plausible bridge has five causal stages. These are hypotheses to distinguish experimentally, not a single established developmental sequence.

**Embodied stakes.** Some organizations have states that are better or worse for their functioning, and in sentient beings some conditions are experienced as good or bad. The presence and nature of experience require independent evidence; a resource stock is not automatically a suffering subject. Within this scope, value is encountered through the organization rather than only described from outside it.

**Recognition through shared structure.** Encounters with another center can recruit models and responses that originally tracked one's own conditions. The other remains distinct: their experience is not literally the observer's experience, and their interests need not match the observer's projections. Recognizing similarity without losing difference is a better interpretation of nonseparation than merging everyone into one total.

**Transfer into valuation.** Another's condition can enter action valuation itself, rather than serving solely as a prediction of retaliation, lost production, or the observer's immediate discomfort. This is the essential bridge. It can be investigated as how learned or evolved response dispositions generalize to strangers, new identities, or newly recognized beings.

**Reflective correction.** An agent can discover that an identity-based exclusion conflicts with its own existing reasons, that a protective response has harmed its intended recipient, or that a vivid reaction has obscured less salient suffering. The revision concerns both factual understanding and the scope/application of concern. This is a plausible interpretation of 良知 becoming more mature; intensity of emotion alone is insufficient.

**Reproduction through a changed situation.** Care can preserve another center's capacity to express, teach, reciprocate, create, and correct. Those changes can improve later understanding and support. The relevant feedback can thus be self-reinforcing without reducing its first act to expected self-profit. But this favorable loop must compete with concealment, manipulation, costly fatigue, domination, and the loss of the mechanisms carrying it.

The strong candidate is therefore **reflexive inclusion**: the situation forms centers able to represent and value consequences beyond their current boundary of concern, and those centers can revise the processes that form that boundary. This concerns the natural formation of wisdom, not simply compliance with an externally prescribed rule.

A key burden remains: similar representations can support vicarious distress, concern, retaliation, manipulation, or indifference. The theory must explain and measure the valuation transfer, not identify it by whichever outcome is later praised.

## 6. Protect, ignore, or bypass: a discriminating constructed model

The coordinating investigation supplies an especially useful minimal case. An action harms another center by H>0. Preventing that harm costs the decision maker c>0. Leaving it uncorrected produces private return loss d≥0. A bypass costs e≥0 and prevents the private loss while leaving the other center harmed. Let v≥0 be the action-value weight on that independently specified harm.

The modeled costs are

\[
C_{protect}=c,\quad C_{ignore}=d+vH,\quad C_{bypass}=e+vH.
\]

With strict preferences, protection is selected exactly when

\[
c<\min(d,e)+vH.
\]

At v=0, increasing the return of harm to the decision maker, d, does not force protection: if e<c, a cheap bypass remains preferable forever. Complete factual knowledge of H is compatible with this. The other center matters descriptively but not directly in the operative valuation.

This locates three different interventions:

- Increasing d changes private consequences or dependence.
- Increasing e removes or prices a way to shift those consequences away.
- Changing v changes how the other's condition counts even when shifting costs is possible.

The first two can improve behavior and may be important natural routes. But they cannot by themselves establish the formation of portable concern. The proposed thesis becomes more demanding—and more informative—when it predicts that v itself can form, generalize, and persist through reflective learning and succession.

This equation does not solve that formation problem. Supplying v, or using a learning rule explicitly rewarded for increasing it, would install the desired direction. An informative experiment must either derive v's effective change from independently specified learning mechanisms or measure its change under interventions not defined by the target moral score.

An especially important extension gives the actor two ways to remove their own distress signal: conceal the accurate evidence or repair the other's actual condition. If both end the observer's unpleasant experience but only repair benefits the affected center, preference for repair under a real cost distinguishes world-directed concern from mere removal of aversive input. Appropriate controls must distinguish concern from reputation, anticipated reciprocity, demand effects, and a belief that concealment itself is punished. This is a proposed discriminator, not a perfect psychological identification theorem.

## 7. Persistence filters can supply a real direction, but require an independent bridge

It would be too dismissive to call every persistence effect “only survivor bias.” A mechanism that changes persistence changes the actual time distribution of arrangements.

For a succession of episodes of two independently classified regime types, let the fraction of entering episodes of inclusive type I tend to q, and their mean duration tend to tauI>0; let the other type's mean duration tend to tauX>0. Directly dividing cumulative residence times gives the limiting occupied-time fraction

\[
f_I={q\tau_I\over q\tau_I+(1-q)\tau_X}.
\]

These limits can be assumptions of a stochastic formation process or properties proved independently for a specified deterministic sequence. No novel renewal theorem is claimed. If tauI>tauX, then fI>q for 0<q<1. This is a genuine temporal prevalence bias, even if new types are initially formed without a preference for inclusion.

But three questions remain separate:

1. What produces q, including the first formation of concern?
2. Does inclusive responsiveness cause the increased lifetime, or are both caused by an external subsidy or easier environment?
3. Does the classification track affected centers independently, or does it merely call long-lived patterns “inclusive” afterward?

The new renewal chemistry explains a persistence mechanism without ethical inclusion. The corrective institution explains a protected inclusive regime under supplied authority. A stronger direction theory must connect independently measured inclusion to formation, maintenance, or spread. Observing only the successful regimes cannot establish that connection. Conversely, explicit causal formation/lifetime models should not be dismissed merely because persistence appears in their explanation.

At the level of all reality, there need be no population of universes selecting the whole. Selection, learning, formation, and elimination can occur among relations inside one evolving situation. A hypothesis about their long-run prevalence still needs a stated measure: duration, spatial extent, number of maintained centers, or something else. Different measures can disagree.

## 8. Conditional hypothesis with a real risk of failure

Here is a stronger testable version than “when conditions favor wisdom, wisdom develops”:

> In driven systems with recurrent contact among differently situated centers, replaceable roles, learnable consequences, and affordable revision, increasing accurate cross-position experience and the transferability of consequence representations will, over a declared horizon, increase costly protection of newly encountered affected centers and the retention of their effective feedback—even when those centers cannot retaliate, reciprocate, or materially support the incumbent.

Every conditioning variable must be measured independently. “Affordable revision” means an audited resource/action constraint, not that the system has already chosen moral revision. “Transferable representation” means an independently measured generalization property, not a direct reward for protecting the test recipient. The hypothesis could fail if improved representations increase strategic exploitation, if role exchange produces only temporary insurance preferences, if concern remains confined to trained identities, or if costly concern disappears at succession.

This is a conditional direction hypothesis across a class of systems; it is not a definition of goodness and not yet a universal theorem. It preserves the ambitious mechanism: **reality developing ways to make more of its affected centers consequential within its own revision**, rather than simply maximizing total survival.

It should be compared with at least four rival explanations:

- **Instrumental dependence:** protection rises only when harm can return to the actor; it vanishes for powerless recipients or when bypass is cheap.
- **Representation without concern:** factual prediction improves, but the action weight on other's harm does not; exploitation may improve.
- **Local social conformity:** protection tracks the training group's approved categories or visible reputation and fails novel-recipient or private-choice tests.
- **Reflexive concern:** protection transfers to a relevant new case, survives known self-interest conflicts, and changes appropriately when evidence shows the intended help is harmful.

No single favorable experiment establishes cosmic direction. Convergent successes across distinct substrates and formation histories would support a structural tendency; counterexamples identify which couplings matter. If the effect requires directly supplying all the relevant other-regarding values, the claimed formation bridge has failed even if the final behavior is good.

## 9. Primary empirical anchors, narrowly used

These are fresh primary-source checks for this note. They support the possibility and separability of pieces of the bridge. They do not establish the entire proposed chain, a universal evolutionary history, or the philosophical truth of the ethical conclusion.

**Joint learning for self and other.** Rhoads and colleagues (2025) report three preregistered studies of learning when actions affect both oneself and others. An integrated-value model with target- and valence-dependent prediction-error updates characterized the data better than the tested simulation alternative. The relevance is that other outcomes can enter the learning process guiding choice, with measurable asymmetric weights. It does not show that accurate learning necessarily increases concern or that those weights naturally become impartial. [Primary article and abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41125577/), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64424-9).

**Representation and exceptional helping.** O'Connell and colleagues (2019) compared people who donated a kidney to a stranger with controls. They found differences in similarity of neural patterns for experienced and observed distress, with some differences altered by an empathy prompt. This associates self/other representation with costly altruism and distinguishes disposition from prompted ability. It is not randomized evidence that representational similarity causes moral concern or that all shared neural activation has one meaning. [Primary paper abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31341206/).

**Cultivation and transfer outside training.** Weng and colleagues (2013), in a randomized training study, found greater costly redistribution to a victim after compassion training than after reappraisal training, with associated neural changes. The reported behavioral comparison involved 20 versus 21 participants. This gives a limited causal example that a mental practice can alter other-directed behavior outside its training context. It does not establish unlimited generalization, persistence over generations, or a guarantee of improved judgment in every moral conflict. [Primary abstract and study figures](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23696200/).

**Role uncertainty and later endorsement.** Weidinger and colleagues (2023) studied choices of distributive principles under a veil of ignorance. Across five incentivized studies they report greater preference for prioritizing the worst-off and some evidence of retaining a choice after one's advantaged role became known; the figures show not every reported effect is individually significant in every study. This is relevant to the transition from role uncertainty to endorsed reasons under later self-interest. It does not establish one uniquely justified principle or a general change in concern for powerless outsiders. [Primary paper abstract and figures](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37094137/).

**An essential adverse case.** Singer and colleagues (2006) manipulated fair/unfair social interaction before observed pain. Empathy-related responses depended on that context, with marked reductions for unfair targets in male participants in the study. This shows that social evaluation can gate responses to another's distress; “more contact” does not by itself imply broader concern. The study does not warrant a universal claim about either sex or prove that neural response differences alone identify moral merit. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04271).

The evidence therefore makes the bridge scientifically plausible enough to investigate rather than merely a moral wish. It also shows why one should measure representation, valuation, behavior, and retention separately.

## 10. Discriminating research designs

### A. Information × consequence return × bypass × valuation transfer

Use a harmless computational environment first, with matched initial resources, actual action costs, public/private consequences, and a fixed independently declared harm measure. Vary: whether the actor observes valid harm evidence; whether harm returns privately; whether bypass is available; and whether its learned evaluator shares valence features across identities or keeps identity gates. Freeze every training reward and do not directly reward the held-out inclusive behavior if emergence is the target.

Architectural sharing is itself a supplied intervention. If a common valuation channel mechanically transfers an already learned negative weight to every new recipient, the result establishes conditional transfer, not that natural selection or learning must choose that architecture. A claim about formation of the architecture must let alternatives arise or be learned, charge their costs, and evaluate adoption without directly rewarding the held-out moral result.

Measure factual prediction, effective other-harm weight under tradeoffs, actual harm reduction, choice to hide evidence, and resource costs separately. Test novel centers that never assisted, rewarded, or punished the learner. Representation-only improvement with unchanged protection is a clear negative result for the valuation bridge. Increased protection explained entirely by d or e supports an instrumental mechanism, not an emergent v.

### B. Distinguish expansion from homogenization

Give centers different needs but matched standing in the declared test. A process that applies its own preferred treatment to everyone may be less adequate than one that asks and responds differently. Measure whether new information about the recipient changes the action appropriately, whether refusal remains effective, and whether direct experience can correct the observer's projection. An inclusive direction should preserve consequential differentiation, not merely increase one global “empathy” score.

### C. Endorsement after role revelation

Let actors learn under varying roles, then reveal a stable advantageous role with a known cost to keeping their previous protection. Compare whether a learned reason transfers after actual risk of personally occupying the weak role ends. Include stable-role and matched cognitive-effort controls. Persistent protection is evidence against immediate insurance alone; reputation and commitment costs must still be disentangled.

### D. A second and third generation

A successful episode is insufficient. Charge upkeep, transfer, training, verification and execution. Give successors only actually transmitted tools, memories, values and access. Test whether concern for untrained powerless centers survives, whether mistaken protections can be corrected, and whether the successor can finance onward transmission. If institutional persistence survives while direct concern disappears, the favorable bridge failed at retention.

### E. Formation and extinction denominators

Report every preparation, including no formation, exploitation, collapse, and stable indifferent regimes. Estimate formation probabilities and conditional durations separately. Do not count only already compassionate agents or already viable institutions. Match external work and resource subsidy. A positive residence-time effect and a positive formation effect are different discoveries.

These designs concern measurable functional behavior. They would not by themselves settle whether an artificial model has phenomenal conscience or moral patient status.

## 11. Counterexamples the stronger thesis should explain rather than define away

- A world can be fully causally connected while its centers have stable egoistic valuations. Relabeling symmetry does not refute this.
- Better inference of vulnerability can make extraction more effective. Information is an enabling condition for both care and manipulation.
- Care within one coalition can fund harm to outsiders. Larger cooperation is not equivalent to broader protected standing.
- Concern can form and then be selected against under cheaper imitation or replacement. The origin of a disposition does not ensure its retention.
- A costly compassionate act may end the actor's own projects while protecting another. A self-persistence score can misclassify it.
- Stable care can preserve the wrong practice through a mistaken model; maturity requires correction, not unconditional escalation of the same behavior.
- A finite system can exhibit long periods of wisdom and still eventually fail. That does not falsify a finite-horizon prevalence claim, but it does falsify an indefinite-sure version with the same assumptions.
- A positive local trend can coexist with a worse global distribution because activity expands into new victims or shifts costs across the observed boundary.

Allowing these does not empty the hypothesis. Its content lies in the independently specified conditions under which the favorable direction should be more likely, which stage should change, and which counterfactual intervention should remove that change.

## 12. The next conceptual advance

The earlier program already established that sustained activity needs support and that correction needs support. The next advance is not another proof of those facts. It is an explanation of **why support-producing reality sometimes grows a concern for the conditions of other centers, and how that concern becomes able to revise its own inherited limits**.

There are two complementary research burdens:

- **Natural formation:** explain the development of portable valuation, calibrated recognition, and practical correction through embodied learning, encounter, variation, and succession.
- **Reasoned adequacy:** examine whether the resulting concern correctly identifies who can be harmed, treats comparable reasons consistently, preserves relevant differences, and remains responsive when its own good intentions fail.

They can inform each other without collapsing. An evolutionary origin neither proves a moral judgment nor makes it arbitrary; a defensible moral reason can become a real cause within the very dynamics being explained. That is the strongest reading of conscience as shared becoming reflecting upon its own direction.

A concise candidate statement is:

> **The proposed direction is not merely that reality preserves what persists. It is that, under discoverable conditions, reality forms processes that can increasingly recognize, value, and revise their effects on the differentiated centers through which further becoming matters. Wisdom is this capacity becoming more adequate to the shared situation; inclusive regeneration is its maintained practical expression.**

Its decisive unfinished bridge is the formation and retention of valuation that transfers beyond usefulness, familiarity, and coercive return. The recent chemistry and institutional mechanisms make that bridge more concrete, but do not replace it. Primary research provides real examples of several intermediate capacities. A universal tendency remains to be explained and tested through their actual connection.
