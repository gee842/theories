# When regeneration can become wiser and more inclusive

5 October 2026. An exploratory continuation of Ren's unified theory, written with AI assistance. The constructions below are conditional results and research hypotheses. They do not announce a discovered law of cosmic moral progress.

The question is whether reality's generative activity does more than produce occasional cooperative arrangements: whether it tends to produce understanding, correction, and progressively wider concern for affected beings. The most promising route is to explain how the conditions that make others consequential also make their situation intelligible, capable of changing action, and eventually recognized as warranting concern beyond their usefulness to a particular actor.

This exploration identifies a precise missing bridge. **A system can become better at modeling others without becoming more concerned for them. The directional hypothesis therefore needs an account of how concern forms, generalizes, and retains its power over action.** Recurring feedback and selection can contribute, but their effects depend on what responses are available and which consequences guide selection.

## What a tendency would mean

Several meanings of direction should be distinguished before testing the claim.

| Meaning | What would establish it |
|---|---|
| Every trajectory becomes wiser at every step | A monotonicity theorem for a justified measure over every admitted trajectory |
| Eventually every viable process becomes inclusive | A convergence theorem excluding persistent alternatives, cycles, and loss of the required capacities |
| Wiser arrangements become more prevalent in specified conditions | A sampling frame, a measure of prevalence, a time scale, and a formation and retention mechanism |
| Greater effective inclusion becomes possible | An accessible construction with its resource and information requirements |
| Inclusive regeneration provides reasons for action | An ethical argument about whose consequences matter and why |

Our present models support some statements of the third and fourth kinds about specified protective arrangements. This does not yet establish increasing wisdom or expanding the range of recipients with protected standing. They do not support the first two without strong restrictions. The fifth is already an explicit ethical commitment of the theory. It can be investigated and defended without treating it as an empirical population trend.

“Statistical tendency” cannot mean that we select a few inspiring cases. Populations, domains, weighting, losses, and time scales must be fixed independently of the favored conclusion. Relative prevalence, absolute abundance, and improved treatment of affected beings can move in different directions. A growing upper limit of capability can also coexist with worsening typical outcomes.

## What continuation really selects

The unified theory already establishes that finite indispensable supports cannot absorb unbounded cumulative loss without provision or changed dependence. This creates a genuine physical constraint. It does not dictate one response to the constraint.

A process can replenish a support, change its function, reduce its need, replace a partner, move to another source, shift costs to someone else, or stop. Preserving the same center, preserving a particular relationship, and maintaining an aggregate function are different outcomes. A theory of direction must explain which of these responses is generated and retained. The earlier [selection analysis](../2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/selection-of-renewal.md) already proves that a support-maintaining practice can be viable yet vulnerable to replacement.

Longer persistence can nevertheless produce a real prevalence bias. For example, consider independent successive arrangements: an inclusive type forms with probability \(q\), has mean duration \(\tau_I\), and the alternative has mean duration \(\tau_X\). Under the ordinary regenerative assumptions and finite positive mean durations, the long-run fraction of occupied time spent in the inclusive type is

\[
\frac{q\tau_I}{q\tau_I+(1-q)\tau_X}.
\]

This is a conditional occupation result, not a moral interpretation of survivors. Formation probability and the causal source of any lifetime advantage need independent explanations. Sampling only survivors cannot supply them. The [conceptual note](conceptual-note.md) develops this distinction.

Enlarging the accounting boundary does not close the gap. At the scale of the whole, costs still occur somewhere inside it; their being included in an account does not cause every affected being to be protected. Relational existence requires us to track consequences. It does not guarantee that an actual selector gives them equal or positive weight.

## Why returned consequences are insufficient

Consider a deliberately small decision model. An actor knows that another being faces a loss \(H>0\). Three feasible actions exist:

| Action | Cost to the decision maker | Loss to the other |
|---|---:|---:|
| Protect | \(c\) | \(0\) |
| Ignore | \(d\) | \(H\) |
| Bypass the returned consequence | \(e\) | \(H\) |

All quantities are supplied nonnegative costs in this model. Bypass could stand for a particular feasible substitute or means of insulation; the model does not assume that all real harms admit one. All three actions are affordable here. A more complete model must include deadlines, uncertainty, and the costs of implementing each action.

If choice minimizes the actor's cost, protection is uniquely selected precisely when

\[
c<\min(d,e).
\]

Take \(c=4\), \(d=6\), and \(e=2\). The actor chooses bypass. Raising returned damage \(d\) to 12 does not change that choice. It can understand the other perfectly and adapt successfully while leaving the other's loss untouched. This is a counterexample to the logical implication from stronger feedback to greater protection; it is not a prevalence estimate.

Now let the other's loss itself enter the actor's evaluation with weight \(v\geq0\). The three burdens become \(c\), \(d+vH\), and \(e+vH\). Protection is uniquely selected exactly when

\[
\min(d,e)+vH>c.
\]

For the same costs and \(H=10\), any \(v>0.2\) makes protection uniquely preferable. At \(v=0.2\), protection and bypass tie. If returned costs already exceed the cost of protection, positive direct concern is unnecessary for this particular action. Conversely, protection without personal return can occur when concern is sufficient. The scalar is only an illustrative local decision weight; it does not define all moral value or legitimate every tradeoff.

This separates **knowing the other's condition**, **being affected by it**, and **caring about it**. They can interact causally but are not equivalent. Treating the concern parameter as supplied exposes the next explanatory obligation: how does the process produce and revise it?

## A conditional theorem for the spread of protective response

The [mathematical note](math-note.md) extends the comparison across generations. It connects successor production to actual retained resources, including sensing, repair, and the construction of a responsive carrier. It also allows a cheaper bypass.

This is a deterministic population-mass model with divisible construction and a supplied recurring resource source. Let \(A\) be available resource after common upkeep and common constructor operation; \(B>0\) the construction cost of an ordinary successor; \(K\geq0\) the additional construction cost of a responsive successor; \(\lambda\) the fraction of interactions with a harmful encounter, with \(0\leq\lambda\leq1\); \(q\) the fraction successfully repaired by the responsive type, with \(0\leq q\leq1\); \(c\) the operating cost of responsiveness; and \(a\) its repair expenditure per hazardous encounter, including failed repair attempts. The privately returned loss per unprotected encounter is \(b=\min(d,e)\), because the actor can choose the cheaper of bearing the loss and bypassing it.

Under the note's specified accounting and faithful inheritance assumptions, the two resource-funded multiplication factors are

\[
W_0=\frac{A-\lambda b}{B},\qquad
W_1=\frac{A-c-\lambda a-\lambda(1-q)b}{B+K}.
\]

Assume both numerators are positive and the action bills are prefunded; \(A\geq c+a+b\) is a simple sufficient bound in the stated timing model. Responsive carriers have a relative selective advantage exactly when

\[
\lambda b(Bq+K)>B(c+\lambda a)+KA.
\]

The proof is cross-multiplication by positive construction costs. The content lies in the independently specified accounting: successful repair saves returned losses, but operating and constructing the channel consume the same resources from which successors are made. A positive relative advantage does not imply absolute growth or survival.

The note then allocates a fraction of actual retained resources to a fixed exploratory mix of both candidate constructions. This makes first formation paid and possible from an initially absent type. In the specified deterministic population model, positive trial rates give an attracting mixed composition; as the fraction devoted to exploratory construction tends to zero, the larger-multiplication type dominates when their factors differ. This concerns the long-run mixture at each positive trial fraction; starting with zero trials can prevent first formation entirely. The construction repertoire, timely warning and actuation route, and trial rule remain supplied. The model does not establish survival of a finite stochastic population. The result explains their use and propagation, not arbitrary invention or the evolution of the trial rule itself.

This is a legitimate conditional bridge. A scientific model may take mechanisms as premises and derive an outcome from them. It becomes circular only if the purported explanation already assumes that wisdom or inclusion necessarily determines fitness. Here, the same equations also produce disappearance or rarity of protection. Increasing returned damage ceases to help when the bypass cost becomes the cheaper bound. The construction cost, repair efficacy, and actual availability of resources have independently inspectable roles.

## How concern could develop within the same reality

The descriptive and ethical questions have different standards of justification, but they do not require two separate worlds. Pain, attachment, learning, empathy, judgment, and moral reflection can be natural events within the relational process. An explanation of how care arises does not by itself settle whether a particular judgment is justified; neither does that distinction make care causally mysterious.

The constructive hypothesis is a sequence with feedback between its parts:

1. A center has states that matter to its activity and experience.
2. It develops representations of related states in others.
3. Some of those represented states come to influence its valuation and action directly.
4. Learning and reflection extend or revise that influence beyond initially familiar partners.
5. Practices, institutions, and inheritance preserve the capacity to question mistaken exclusions and pay for warranted protection.

Each step is substantive. A shared representation need not carry a shared valuation. Affection can remain local. Reasoning can rationalize favoritism. Institutions can suppress the very disagreement they were created to hear. Successors may inherit a rule while losing its interpretation or material support.

The [conceptual note](conceptual-note.md) examines primary studies of self–other learning and compassion training as limited evidence for parts of this pathway. The mechanism of greatest interest is **generalization of concern to a novel affected being who cannot reward or punish the actor**, followed by retention of that concern when it becomes costly. This connects directly to Ren's proposal that 良知, conscience, is a fallible capacity formed within the same generative world. It does not require declaring every evolved impulse morally reliable.

Relabeling symmetry alone cannot supply the missing evaluation. A population in which every actor values only its own outcome follows a perfectly uniform actor-relative rule. Equal application of that rule does not produce impartial concern. A claim of universal standing needs the further argument that comparable stakes provide reasons across changes of identity. Our theory already states that commitment; the new causal task is to explain its formation and extension.

## What the evidence currently contributes

The [targeted literature review](literature-note.md) gives five original studies and their limits. Yeast experiments show that a new reproducing collective can form under explicit selection. Human public-goods experiments show that participants can adopt and finance supplied sanctioning institutions. Those are positive mechanisms of formation and retention, not merely stories about already successful arrangements. [Ratcliff and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [Hilbe and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1315273111), [Lo Iacono and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgad091).

The same literature supplies important opposing mechanisms: punishment can target cooperative contributors, and strategic responsiveness can sustain unequal extraction of gains. [Herrmann and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1153808), [Press and Dyson](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1206569109). These studies do not measure universal ethical progress.

Even closely related results depend on the process under study. Generous strategies can be favored in specified evolving populations, while a human–computer laboratory study found extortionate strategies earned more than generous ones under its conditions. These findings concern different comparison procedures; they are not contradictory universal laws. [Stewart and Plotkin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.7205), [Xu and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.02378).

The inference we draw is that coordinated regeneration, responsive choice, and wider protection have real causal routes. Their conjunction and continued extension require explanation. The current evidence does not establish a representative trend across reality as a whole.

## A stronger and more testable hypothesis

A candidate comparative hypothesis is: **within a declared class of interacting learning systems with affordable revision, increasing accurate experience across differently situated roles and the transferability of consequence representations increases costly protection of unfamiliar affected beings who cannot reward or punish the actor, relative to matched controls over a fixed horizon.**

The system class and horizon must be fixed before testing. Affordability is an audited budget and action constraint. Representation transfer is measured by predictive generalization, separately from protection. The intervention must not directly reward the held-out protective response. This makes the conjecture vulnerable to failure: prediction may improve while protection stays unchanged or exploitation improves. The hypothesis is presently proposed, not established.

The proposed mechanism can in turn alter the selection conditions that initially produced it. Concern need not remain confined to what originally made it advantageous. This is a claim about formation and transfer under discoverable conditions; it does not require every process to follow that route or every extension to be irreversible.

The decisive tests separate changes that are often combined. Hold information about harm constant while varying whether the actor bears its consequences, can bypass them, has learned another's stakes, or can revise the rule used to evaluate them. Then introduce unfamiliar recipients with no leverage, remove the original reward, add real opportunity costs, and transfer the practice to successors. Measure accurate prediction, chosen protection, recipient outcomes, contestability, continued operation, and inheritance separately.

If protection disappears whenever personal return disappears, the result supports instrumental dependence. If it transfers to powerless unfamiliar recipients after the original reward is removed, it supports generalized concern. If the rule and its rationale survive paid succession and remain corrigible, it supports the stronger continuing-capacity hypothesis. Requiring the agent to maximize an externally supplied universal-welfare score would not test the formation of that concern.

The positive frontier is therefore precise: **how does a world that generates centers with stakes also generate a durable capacity to recognize those stakes across centers, let them change action, and correct the boundaries of that recognition?** The new costed model proves one conditional route for responsive protection to form and spread. The broader account of generalizing conscience remains the next substantive bridge.
