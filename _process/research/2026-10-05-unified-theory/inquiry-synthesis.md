## 1. The strongest common result

The sources support a theory of **situated, materially supported, revisable action**. An organization encounters the world through particular channels, retains particular distinctions, can enact particular responses, and changes some of the conditions of its later encounter and response. Its operation, investigation, correction, and transmission draw on finite supports. Some consequences matter for maintaining that organization; other consequences matter to affected participants whether or not the organization depends on them.

This is already stronger than a loose collection of virtues. The studies exhibit independently necessary failure points: a consequential distinction can be unavailable, discarded, inexpressible, misinterpreted, denied standing, unaffordable, too late, outside the action repertoire, or impossible to carry into a successor. Restoring one does not generally restore the others. A successful correction can also consume the means for subsequent correction.

The unification should therefore have three explicitly connected layers:

1. **Descriptive organization:** what produces, maintains, transforms, observes, and acts through what actual couplings.
2. **Epistemic and practical adequacy:** whether one executable policy can distinguish enough and act in time, within the specified uncertainty and resources.
3. **Ethical justification:** which consequences and participants deserve protection, and why the decision and revision procedures have legitimate standing.

These layers interact causally but are not interchangeable. Predictive success does not establish ethical goodness; persistence does not establish truth; aggregate renewal does not protect every participant; reflective language does not establish better inquiry.

The strongest defensible “root of wisdom” formulation is consequently a research proposal: **understand and responsibly revise the conditions through which one and others can know, act, continue, and correct consequential mistakes.** Its ethical terms require justification, and its practical advantage requires testing. It is not a theorem that this phrase uniquely defines wisdom.

## 3. A shared formalization that preserves the substantive distinctions

### 3.1 Joint state and experimental contract

Let a declared model contract be

\[
\mathfrak C=(\mathcal X,\mathcal U,\mathcal D,F,O,\mathcal M,\mathcal K,\mathcal L,\Gamma).
\]

Here `X` is a joint state space; `U` is a menu of physically and institutionally implemented interventions; `D` specifies permitted disturbances; `F` is a transition relation or stochastic kernel; `O` is the actual observation law; `M` is the admitted uncertainty class; `K` is an explicitly interpreted protection/continuation set; `L` is a vector of consequences; and `Γ` states admissible ways of revising this contract. Cost, time, and randomness conventions must be specified. The tuple is a syntax for joining mechanisms, not itself a mechanism or explanation.

A useful state decomposition is

\[
x=(e,z,\theta,r,m,g,\ell).
\]

`e` records consequential surroundings; `z` maintained participants and components; `θ` actual sensors, converters, skills, records, interfaces and installed control mechanisms; `r` resource stocks and pending obligations; `m` retained information and inference state; `g` enacted access, standing and authority; `ℓ` relevant lineage, commitment and historical records. One need not measure every coordinate. The analyst's joint state and the participant's accessible state must never be confused.

The next transition can change `θ`, `m`, and `g` as well as ordinary quantities. An observation is generated by the installed channel `O_θ`; executable actions depend on interface, resources, and standing. A richer model of the current interface does not itself install another interface. “Purpose revision” requires a concrete process that changes the operative criterion or rule; merely writing new weights does not explain how the change takes effect.

The analyst still supplies this model, its primitives, and its revision grammar. A computation that changes its program can be represented by a fixed higher-level transition rule over program states. Endogeneity of selected mechanisms does not eliminate all assumptions or certify ontological completeness.

### 3.2 One policy, not one answer per hidden world

Let `h_t` be the actual accessible observation-action history, including records of expenditure and public institutional choices when they are visible. A policy `π(h_t)` must be executable through this history. Its feasible set may be written

\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{adm}}(\mathfrak C,h_t)=
\{\pi:\text{every modeled possible payment prefix is funded, actions are authorized and available, deadlines are met}\}.
\]

Then a robust expected-consequence certificate has the form

\[
\exists\pi\in\Pi_{\mathrm{adm}}\quad
\forall M\in\mathcal M\quad
\forall j\quad
\mathbb E_M[L_j(\pi)\mid h_t]\le\tau_j.
\]

This is different from `∀M ∃π_M`: individually solvable worlds may be jointly unsolvable for an agent that cannot distinguish them. Expected protection, high-probability protection, and pathwise protection are distinct predicates. Hard resource feasibility is ordinarily prefix/pathwise even where the operating objective uses expectation. Probability-zero conditioning requires explicit handling.

A certificate is relative to the contract and class. A failed certificate identifies an obstruction within that class; a successful certificate does not protect against omitted worlds, validate its ethical commitments, or prove the procedure that generated the class is complete.

### 3.3 The equivalence hierarchy

The same elementary factorization fact ties together inquiry, self-modeling, abstraction, historical continuity, and missing possibilities. Given a representation `ρ:H→Z`, a definite question `q:H→Y` is exactly answerable from `ρ` iff it is constant on every fiber of `ρ`. This is a truth/representation result.

Decision adequacy is weaker. For finite losses and a common action meaning, a represented policy attains the model-specific optimum in every compatible model iff

\[
\forall z,\qquad
\bigcap_{M:\rho(M)=z}\arg\min_a L_M(a)\ne\varnothing.
\]

Unidentified losses can still admit a common optimal action. Randomization cannot repair an empty intersection for simultaneous **exact optimality**, although it can help minimax risk, expected constraints, or regret under different criteria.

Dynamical abstraction has another requirement: if `ρ(x)=ρ(x′)`, then merged successor/output behavior must agree for every allowed intervention (or for the specifically declared restricted policy class). An abstraction adequate for one fixed controller need not survive admitting a new intervention. For deterministic autonomous dynamics, the basic condition is `ρ(Fx)=ρ(Fx′)` whenever `ρ(x)=ρ(x′)`.

Historical adequacy is a separate question again. Two histories can have the same present reactor state and every same allowed future response while differing on uninterrupted presence or ancestry. Future sufficiency does not make the historical fact meaningless; it means that description does not retain its answer. A record, tracer, witness, or expanded boundary can change the information class.

**Recommendation:** preserve four different labels in the integrated theory: claim sufficiency, decision sufficiency, transition/output sufficiency, and historical/provenance sufficiency. Do not use a single unqualified word “equivalence.”

### 3.4 Multi-step correction is a viable course, not merely a safe move

For a finite adversarial safety model, let a belief `B` be all joint states compatible with accessible history, and require it to lie inside the acceptable set `K`. Let `P(B,a)` include every successor under the permitted worlds/disturbances, and let `U(B,a,o)` filter successors by the next actual observation. Define

\[
\Phi(W)=\{B\subseteq\mathcal K:\exists a,
P(B,a)\subseteq\mathcal K,
\quad U(B,a,o)\ne\varnothing\Rightarrow U(B,a,o)\in W\ \forall o\}.
\]

The greatest fixed point of `Φ` is exactly the finite-model set of beliefs from which a history-dependent controller can guarantee indefinite safety. At each surviving belief choose the witnessing action. A removed belief fails at a finite horizon; repeated deletion establishes necessity. Treat unavailable actions as failure rather than deleting inconvenient states. Include intermediate rebuild steps and payments in the transition; otherwise the recursion grants fictitious access.

This theorem gives the cleanest shared formal kernel, but only for the stated safety question. It does not guarantee discovery, improvement, fairness, eventual task completion, or nontrivial development. A prison or frozen system can be safe under a badly chosen `K`. Add reachability, recurrence, quality, participant, and normative requirements explicitly instead of silently attributing them to viability.

For paid finite-horizon inquiry with a supplied probabilistic model, the corresponding optimization is ordinary dynamic programming over augmented information/resource/time/interface state:

\[
V(s)=\min\{\text{feasible immediate action values},
c_q+\sum_y P(y\mid s,q)V(s_{q,y})\}.
\]

This captures complementary questions and justified stopping. It does not invent its hypothesis space or legitimize its loss function.

### 3.5 Generativity as produced change in effective capacity

A productive process is generative in a stronger causal sense when an output becomes part of the enabling mechanism for later production, observation, action, correction, or successor formation. Test the link by removing the produced component or disconnecting its use while holding the specified alternatives fixed. Mere persistence, stored objects, or correlation does not establish that link.

The full future reachability set under fixed laws cannot literally grow along its own trajectory: if `x′` is reachable from `x`, path concatenation gives `Reach(x′)⊆Reach(x)`. “More possible futures” therefore needs an **effective** reference: operations available within a horizon, budget, interface, information, rights, and protected conditions; or capacity relative to an explicit counterfactual baseline. Building a tool can enlarge immediately executable operations even though its construction-and-use sequence was already permitted by the full dynamics.

A suitable object is a set-valued **effective capacity profile** of attainable consequence and resource vectors, with question, horizon, disturbance contract, and protections attached. Set inclusion or Pareto comparison can be useful when these meanings align. There is no warranted universal scalar that combines all future possibilities, lives, inquiry, complexity, and goodness without further evaluative premises.

## 4. Results that should enter the unified theory intact

### 4.1 Reflection cannot manufacture missing evidence

The self-modeling study gives three useful negatives. Data processing alone cannot increase information about the same target under the stipulated Markov chain. Additional reflective layers can worsen feedback stability or confidence calibration. Nested and flat implementations of the same estimator were exactly behaviorally identical in 10,000 matched comparisons; repeating estimates simply added priced work in that setup. Neither a fixed point nor convergence implies truth.

The strongest general identification counterexample uses

`x_next = ax + u + g + w`, `y = x + b + ε`, `z = x + d + η`.

The transformation `(x,b,d,g)→(x+c,b−c,d−c,g+(1−a)c)` preserves every allowed observation and adaptive acquisition/action history in a shift-closed class, while changing an absolute target loss. More internal computation cannot discriminate the pair. However, known zero forcing with `a≠1`, a genuinely unbiased reference, or a fixed informative initial distribution can break the ambiguity. Importing the unrestricted impossibility into an anchored simulation would be an error.

With an omitted forcing term, an estimator can instead represent `x−g/(1−a)` as its state and absorb the displacement into both sensor biases. It may regulate that coordinate convincingly while missing physical zero. The source carefully does not claim convergence of every finite Gaussian-filter run. Its observations—roughly 90% nominal coverage under the correct zero-forcing cases but about 2.5% under hidden forcing—are descriptive benchmark outcomes, not universal rates.

**Integrated implication:** protected evidence access, informative experiments, adequate interpretation, and effective actuation are distinct. Monitoring confidence alone can fail because uncertainty is conditional on the model that omitted the cause.

### 4.2 Model success cannot be the sole permission to challenge the model

In the prospective-revision construction, even the joint routine record of counts, endpoint, and later outcome has identical law in worlds where a richer conditional response is beneficial or harmful. Full historical order alone can still fail: the decisive information is its linkage to the outcome of that same case. An anomaly-only gate has equal acceptance probability in observation-equivalent worlds.

The robust version is a total-variation bound: for any gate using its actual archive, power above false-admission size is bounded by the total variation between the **whole joint archive laws**. It is not automatically the one-record distance when many samples are acquired.

Candidate construction has a separate language bottleneck. In the bounded discovery example, all original predicates through two attributes lose operating value in a four-attribute hazard world, while a supplied extension through three or four attributes contains beneficial candidates. Demanding improvement at every intermediate refinement can block useful combinations. A schedule that enumerates deeper combinations removes this particular obstruction without knowing which combination is correct, but does not guarantee finite-sample discovery or introduce an unmeasured variable.

**Integrated implication:** access to investigation must sometimes be possible before the incumbent criterion can demonstrate expected benefit. This does not warrant believing or deploying an untested candidate. Preserve separate gates for question admission, candidate formation, evidence, institutional standing, and enactment.

### 4.3 Inquiry is productive expenditure with real opportunity costs

Scientific integration contains 1,920 runs (64 paired seed clusters × six environments × five policies), not 1,920 independent real institutions. All policies serve a fixed population of 700 requests per group, so lost service cannot vanish from the denominator.

In the lasting-opportunity world, the full loop reduces B failures from 309.05 to 40.75 on average and all 64 runs change the mandate and method. It ends with less cash. Stable simple tasks show no accuracy benefit and additional expense. Under falling income, full inquiry has 479.20 B failures (142.50 wrong services plus 336.70 unserved requests), compared with 310.59 failures and no unserved requests under no renewal. This is a policy failure, not proof that every protected-inquiry policy must fail.

One temporary-opportunity seed adopts a presently advantageous method, then cannot afford timely reversal after drift. It incurs 145 B errors versus 31 under ablations. Both accepted changes had positive expected advantage under their current review law. A valid local certificate can therefore coexist with long-run harm when policy change, reversibility and future funding are jointly omitted from the decision criterion.

Fresh testing freezes the **actual installed package**, candidate, current mandate, and recurring cost difference. A review receives `α_j=.05/[j(j+1)]`; conditional fixed-sample Hoeffding control plus the union bound gives a within-institution error bound under the declared independent stationary review law. This does not validate mandate ethics, recover sunk research expense, certify arbitrary future tasks, or guarantee post-drift advantage. The atomic stationary review is itself a consequential idealization.

**Integrated implication:** maintain a recoverable route to inquiry, with reserves and reopening conditions; do not define wisdom as maximum continuous investigation. Learning, maintenance, current service, and reversibility compete for shared resources.

### 4.4 Changing an interface changes what can matter and what can be done

The two-resource interface model uses one receptor, one transporter, and one converter; two rebuild steps consume reserve without intake. At capacity three, starting ready and full at a permitted phase entry, environmental phases of length at least four allow indefinite operation: rebuild consumes two, two productive steps restore them. Alternating three-step phases defeat every controller in the stated action/resource class even with foreknowledge. This is a sharp temporal/resource result, not just a smart-policy success.

Fixing the receptor while changing transport/conversion leaves independently varying processing properties indistinguishable. Fixing transport or conversion blocks use even with perfect knowledge. A permanently installed interface cannot cover the environment class, while the fixed overall controller with a changing interface can. This demonstrates changing effective organization, not escaping fixed algorithms or physical laws.

**Integrated implication:** some mistakes are repaired by new beliefs; others require reconstructing the actual relation through which evidence and action occur.

### 4.5 Nonseparation must preserve differentiated consequences

The boundary-balance identity is exact for any subset: internal antisymmetric transfers cancel; source terms and boundary transfers remain. Expanding the boundary changes classification but cannot erase a complete-account transfer. In a connected symmetric exchange network, no proper nonempty subsystem has exact autonomous dynamics for all preparations; changing the exterior can change its derivative at the same interior state.

These results justify examining hidden dependencies and including the observer where causally relevant. They do not establish that everything interacts directly with everything, that approximate autonomy is impossible, that the universe is a single subject, or that Buddhist no-self and an ultimate Self are equivalent doctrines. Universal applicability of an accounting rule is not omniscience of an observer.

Organizational persistence through replacement is rigorously illustrated, but lineage, original material, uninterrupted functioning, and numerical identity remain different relations. A present-state future model can erase real history; replacing a failed organization with an identical state need not establish uninterrupted continuation. Conversely, dormancy need not be destruction if recoverable carriers remain.

**Integrated implication:** a common world is compatible with different standpoints, responsibilities, protections, and refusal. Unity must not function as a license to absorb everyone's consequences into one aggregate objective.

### 4.6 Renewal does not generate moral standing by itself

The completion supplement makes the central counterexample concrete: both allocation rules maintain the same productive support while the extractive rule gives the incumbent more. The affected participant is dispensable to production and loses its reserve in six rounds under controller-only revision, while the system keeps producing.

An effective participant route protects it only through explicit filing, binding authority, and a renewed carrier. In the specified nine-round cycle, carrier funding must satisfy `f≥11/9`, prefunded storage `K≥3`, and participant filing threshold `h≥1+c+d`; the main case has `h=4,c=1,d=2`. Maintenance-only funding preserves an idle channel but cannot sustain its repeated use. Faster filing improves reserve margin while increasing the required recurring rate under this release rule.

These are distinct average-flow, prefix-liquidity, and delay constraints. Money cannot replace missing authority or missed participant time; authority cannot replace resources. A rights statement without an executable route can fail under unchanged production.

The mathematical result is conditional. The standing of that participant is an ethical premise. Permanently enforced restoration could avoid repeated claims, but the mechanism sustaining its enforcement would need its own account. The construction does not prove immunity to capture, and withdrawable funding/authority remain stated dependencies.

**Integrated implication:** ethically favorable generativity must be assessed at affected centers, including those a productive whole could discard. Their standing cannot be derived from usefulness to the whole.

## 6. What the evidence actually establishes

There are three different forms of work:

1. **Conditional mathematics:** identities, lower bounds, constructive policies, convergence results, and countermodels. Exact numerical enumeration checks implementations of these claims; it does not substitute for their general proofs or establish novelty.
2. **Synthetic program interventions:** frozen worlds, policies, costs, priors and seeds support causal comparisons inside those programs. Large episode counts improve precision for those declared worlds; they do not create ecological validity or independent confirmation across research traditions.
3. **Small AI reasoning comparisons:** these actually compare instructions on authored tasks. The first pilot has six context-level batches and 72 item responses. The active-inquiry pilot has six context-level batches, 36 episodes and 66 visited choices. All three conditions reach the ceiling in each study. Hidden inference effort and sampling seeds were uncontrolled, and complete post-loading tool abstinence was not independently auditable from the retained final-answer records. There is **no demonstrated special reasoning advantage** for the six-question discipline.

The scientific/inquiry/self-modeling numerical results are not human, biological, contemplative, or clinical experiments. The papers cited in source documents concern particular external mechanisms, not the full proposed theory; their contents were not independently re-read for this memo. No broad external factual claim should be upgraded merely because several AI-assisted session documents repeat it.

The present unification can be an internally coherent **research framework with proved modules**, not a verified best possible universal theory. Its comparative novelty remains unsettled. Established information theory, control, viability, decision analysis, statistics, organizational economics and enactive theory already supply much of the mathematics and vocabulary. Potential added value lies in the explicit common resource/authority/interface/history accounting and the testable integration, not a claim to have invented their component results.

## 7. Testable consequences that can discriminate the integrated proposal

The next tests should cross mechanisms and include explicit null/negative controls. The following are hypotheses outside their already constructed witnesses, not newly established facts.

1. **Resource protection × discrimination × enactment interaction.** Manipulate available inquiry reserve, access to diagnostic linkage, and authority to enact change independently. Predict that protected money alone fails when evidence remains equivalent or enactment is blocked, and that money helps only within a feasible temporal window. Falsifier of a proposed application: the intervention attributed to authority does not change any executable response, or the alleged evidence bottleneck is bypassed by existing accessible records.
2. **Reversal liability after correct adoption.** In controlled changing environments, compare a positive-current-gain acceptance rule with one also reserving switching/reversal cost. Predict different outcomes as drift rate, adaptation delay and reserve change. Include stationary tasks where extra reserve lowers present utility. Do not call this proof of a universally optimal precaution level.
3. **Latent-mode monitoring versus aggregate monitoring.** Hold intervention capacity and cost constant; vary whether the observation resolves an unstable internal difference. Predict the loss of robust stabilization precisely where detectability fails, with recovery only if the new signal reaches a sufficient actuator. Include stable hidden modes where additional monitoring is unnecessary.
4. **Prospective linkage under question changes.** Compare equal-cost archives retaining counts, order, and case-to-outcome links. Later reveal a predeclared distribution of new questions. Predict the model-specific sufficiency ranking and exceptions, including raw-record expenses and simpler sufficient summaries. Failure of the predicted posterior or decision equivalences falsifies the implementation/model claim.
5. **Grammar complementarity.** Compare candidate search that requires every parent candidate to improve against a budget-matched strategy admitting some jointly useful refinements. Use held-out interaction structures and pure-noise worlds. Predict gains only where the permitted composition language contains a useful combination; rare-group sampling or multiple testing may erase the net advantage.
6. **Capacity-preserving pauses.** Compare continual review, destructive shutdown, and paid recoverable dormancy under different change rates and restoration delays. Predict a regime where dormancy saves resources without missing the useful intervention window, and another where dormant capacity cannot resume in time. Current operating success alone should not diagnose the distinction.
7. **Participant protection independent of productivity.** In a bounded institutional experiment, give one affected center no production leverage while retaining a specified protection criterion. Vary effective standing and the funding of its corrective channel. Predict aggregate output may remain stable under deterioration, while protection tracks the operative route. Ethical legitimacy remains argued rather than inferred from an output metric.
8. **Wisdom framework against strong matched comparators.** Test whether the integrated procedure reduces consequential errors at matched time/inference budgets relative to explicit decision analysis, causal modeling, and ordinary expert practice. Include misleading pressure, harmless omissions, warranted retention, expensive inquiry, inaccessible evidence and contested goals. Report distinct harms, unnecessary costs, useful discoveries, revisions and scope errors; no single wisdom score by default. Existing ceiling pilots give no effect size for powering such a study.

For each application, prerecord the question, observation interface, proposed causal mechanism, rights/constraints, accessible actions, budget and deadline, sampling unit, comparison class and failure condition. This is the minimum needed to make the framework answerable to outcomes, rather than an administrative ritual asserted to create wisdom.

## 8. Recommendations to the integrating author

Use a short core statement plus explicit formal modules. The belief-state viability/attainable-correction structure can unify them, while conditional mechanism models retain quantitative content. Do not flatten the source genealogy into one equation that hides the differences between law discovery, model selection, concept formation, institutional legitimacy and physical reconstruction.

Preserve these negative findings prominently: both wisdom pilots show no comparative advantage; more reflection can be redundant or harmful; protected inquiry can bankrupt ordinary service; an accepted currently beneficial change can become harmful through delayed reversal; aggregate regeneration can coexist with participant loss; future prediction can erase historical truth; deterministic regenerative equilibria do not guarantee indefinite finite-particle persistence.

The highest-quality result is a foundation that states exactly what its mechanisms explain and where another premise is needed. Elegance comes from a small number of shared distinctions and theorem templates with explicit scope. “Optimal” should mean a well-posed conditional optimum, a Pareto frontier, or a specified robust guarantee. No source proves a total ordering of all worthwhile futures or a single final best theory.
