## Required precision corrections

### 1. F3's finite-energy sentence exceeds its integral bound

At the inspected version, FORMAL-CORE line 120 says:

> This prevents indefinite positive machinery activity from finite work supply in this model.

Equation F3.5 bounds the cumulative integral of C. It does not preclude C(t)>0 at every finite time. For a direct counterexample inside the stated model, choose `J=0,R0=0,C0>0`. Then `R(t)=0`, `C(t)=C0 exp(-bt)>0` for every finite t, while `b integral C=C0`. The derived limit is correct; its prose interpretation needs the nonvanishing-activity qualification.

Suggested replacement:

> This rules out unbounded cumulative machinery activity from finite work supply, and therefore rules out any positive long-run time-average of C in this model.

Alternatively use “indefinite activity bounded below by a positive machinery level.” This is the same family of precision issue already identified in THEORY §10's continual-consumption statement, but it occurs separately in the formal companion and should also be fixed.

### 2. F7 must retain the positive-delay convention from the source audit

At inspected FORMAL-CORE line 253, the general claim says filing is “followed by d X rounds” and derives `h-c-d`. The exact source theorem assumes **positive integral d≥1, counting the filing round**. The source audit found and repaired a real d=0 error because an immediate restorative receipt can replenish a filing debit before the end-round floor is assessed.

Suggested replacement:

> Let d≥1 be an integer number of X rounds counting the filing round. If filing starts at reserve h and costs c, then after those rounds and before restoration its minimum end-round reserve is h−c−d.

THEORY §8's general inequality should likewise say “with positive integral delay d counting the filing round,” or leave the general condition exclusively to the formal companion. Its specific two-round cycle is already correct.

These two corrections do not change any equations, verification results, causal conclusions, or core construction.

## Coherence and nondual whole

The initial object is explicitly the evolving joint situation rather than an already complete individual facing an independent environment. Centers are differentiated loci with distinct observations, needs, powers, and consequences. The accounting theorem supports boundary consistency while declining to prove universal direct coupling. ## Tautology and explanatory force

“Activity changes conditions for further activity” is a research orientation, not by itself a predictive physical law. Its scientific force comes from specifying which supports and relations do the causal work and predicting what fails when one is changed. The current text does that through chemical thresholds, inaccessible boundaries, information indistinguishability, recurring-cost cuts, prefix infeasibility, and experimentally distinguishable inheritance procedures.

The most important anti-tautology protections already present are:

- supports identified independently, with substitutes and intervention contrasts;
- a possibility profile tied to actual information, budgets, rights, and deadlines;
- installed operation distinguished from existence of a helpful alternative policy;
- mixed or empty feasible sets allowed rather than calling every outcome successful;
- explicit counterexamples where persistence, more options, more inquiry, or private success does not improve the declared continuation;
- the formal core's statement that unrestricted transition expressiveness proves no substantive continuation property;
- no claim that the normative specification justifies itself by having a feasible policy.

This is enough to make the manuscript a coherent formal research theory rather than an empty tautology, provided the completion statement stays at the level it currently uses. It does not establish a universally best ontology or empirical law of all becoming.
