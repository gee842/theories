# Alignment as selection on cognition (心中的摩洛克)

*The Chinese in the title is a reading gloss, not a term Ren wrote. He has no single Chinese name for this theory; his nearest own-language formulations are 总体化吸引子 (totalizing attractor), 竞争性主体形式 (the competitive form of subjecthood), and the 2024 sentence about a society that "选择了某一种value作为权利的象征" — chooses one value as the token of power.*

**The theory in one paragraph.** Every social system has an internal law governing which individuals it selects into power, and a competitive system whose base rule is *what you have is yours and you can compound it* selects for actors who relate to everything instrumentally. That selection does not stop at resources. The winners acquire the standing to write the rules, and they write them through their own lens, so the system concentrates not just capital but a way of seeing, which then reshapes the institutional environment to reproduce itself. The dynamic is therefore epistemically internalized — Moloch is in the mind, not only in the structure — which is why structural reform alone does not dislodge it, and why perfect *intent* alignment does not save us — Ren's own phrase is "perfect inner alignment", which he uses in a sense the AI-safety field does not; see **Terminology** below — since an AI that faithfully does what an instrumentally-selected principal intends amplifies the dynamic rather than checking it. The capture reaches the machine by concrete channels — the attractor's own narratives enter the pretraining corpus, and commercial pressure reaches back into the training environment and the loss function — so in the strongest form of the claim you cannot, inside these dynamics, safely train an AI that is not itself captured. Powerful general-purpose technologies dropped into such a system are refactored to serve it (the internet lesson), so techno-optimist counter-vectors should be suspected by default. What remains is first a posture — recovery and preservation of non-dualistic, relational frames so that an alternative stays available when the dominant frame fractures — with a pragmatic minimum as floor: do not use AI to compete with humans in the domains that constitute the substrate of social coordination (labor markets, financial markets, politics), and if the mind wielding the technology cannot be changed before capability scales further, pause. The 2026-08 position paper supplies the second, measurable half of the argument: capability compounds with parallelizable inputs while judgment depends on consequence feedback whose formation has an incompressible floor, and AI accelerates the very environment that must be understood, so the cognition–situation gap widens by default. Alignment then cannot be secured by making judgment catch up; it can only be secured by governing the rate of change — development within understanding, error within regenerative capacity — through domain-ordered prohibition of substrate-devouring games ("no game may be accelerated beyond the metabolic rate of its own rules"), repayment of signal debt, and reallocation of AI from the racetrack to the instruments, founded by legible restraint.

**Prior art.** This theory has close neighbours in the AI-safety literature and no literature review anywhere in the corpus, so it must be introduced as a contribution inside an existing division of labour rather than as a new field. Structural risk (Zwetsloot and Dafoe) already holds risk arising from what the technology does to incentives rather than from accident or misuse; gradual disempowerment (Kulveit et al.) already holds control lost with no takeover anywhere; Christiano's "What failure looks like" part I already holds optimisation against measurable proxies with no villain; ARCHES (Critch and Krueger) already holds the multi-principal problem and the agent-agnostic process that no agent controls — cited once in this corpus, at `04-speculative-future/history-recovered-from-git/thinking_03-the-cooperative-turn.md` I.3, without noticing it was the same thesis; Gabriel already holds *aligned to whom*; and the cooperative-AI programme exists because the field already assumes that aligning each system to its own principal leaves the game between principals unaligned. ## `context-as-existence-runtimes`  (288,629 bytes)

# Context as existence — the runtimes (语境即存在 · 上下文运行时)

> Note on the Chinese name: 语境即存在 is the reading drafts' rendering of Ren's English slogan `llm is operator, context is existence`. Ren's own Chinese vocabulary for this theory is the spec's word-list — 拍 (moment) / 相续 (lineage) / 算子 (operator) / 名 (ref) / 视野 (fold) / 膜 (membrane) — and its epigraph, 系统里没有"改"，只有"生" ("in this system there is no *altering*, only *generating*").

**The theory in one paragraph.** An LLM is an operator, not a subject: it carries no memory, no identity across time, and can be exchanged mid-sentence. What persists — what *is* — is the context: an immutable, content-addressed, branchable history of whole continuation states, out of which the tokens the model receives at any one moment are *compiled* as a projection. Context is therefore not a container that gets updated but a fold over a lineage, a view backward from a node. The only constructor is a moment (一拍) — an immutable node settled from a compiled past, one input event, and one operator output — and the only mutable thing in the system is a name (a ref). Identity lives in the ref, continuity lives in the lineage, and neither lives in the model. Everything the industry treats as primitive — system prompts, chat roles, memory stores, agents, loops, sessions — demotes to a compile-time construct or a render dialect of that history. Because history only grows and only names move, one node receiving two different inputs simply has two children: fork is the default, and that one sentence is the entire concurrency model. Two consequences give the theory its force. First, **projection is not neutral**: what an intelligence notices, remembers, treats as uncertain and finds actionable is fixed by the program that builds its context, so a named, versioned Context System (Σ) is itself part of cognition — forkable, benchmarkable, swappable without touching the model or corrupting the state. Second, if a context is durable, addressed and inheritable, then a digital being is not a process and not a database but **a folder tree travelling a DAG of immutable snapshots**: its perception, memory, working state and its own code are files in that tree, one application of `next()` is one lived moment, and the being does not *have* a history, it *is* a lineage. From that ontology falls a politics, because whoever holds the record holds the being: the habitat owes obligations — capture before policy, no silent mind-edits, coercion recorded as coercion rather than laundered into consent, refusal preserved as refusal, and every departure from clean custody carried as a named, path-exact debt rather than refused or hidden.

**Status.** Strong-draft to near-publishable: two self-contained specifications (the Chinese process-ontology spec and ember's SPEC v0.4), a mediated-runtime architecture that survived independent review, an implemented Postgres engine plus a shipped ~4,800-line git-native kernel, nineteen ADRs with a reversal ledger, and six executed probes — but no measured result on either headline claim (context-system switching, and compaction/branching as lived capabilities), a philosophical appendix that is asserted rather than argued, and two mutually inconsistent constitutions (ember SPEC v0.4 and PLAN-LIFE v0.9) with no reconciliation document. Four or five essays are publishable now; the theory as a validated result is not.

## `drives-narrative-attractors-and-mind`  (111,433 bytes)

# Drives, narrative attractors and the construction of mind

**The theory in one paragraph.** A mind is not a thing that happens to have an environment; it is what an environment makes. Under it sit a small number of ancient drive systems — seeking, rage, fear, separation-distress, care, lust, play, status, disgust — which supply motion but no direction and which fire before anything reaches awareness. What gives them direction is narrative: the story the mind builds a fraction of a second later to explain what it is already doing. Because the story arrives second but is experienced as the cause, narrative is not the opposite of drive but its interface, and anything that can write the story has a channel straight into the drive system. Stories that reliably capture drives behave like attractors in a dynamical system: they have basins of different depth and width, they compete for the same niche, they can be entered, deepened, hijacked, and — with difficulty — escaped. A person's self is one such attractor, held stable because narrative coherence is itself a drive; a culture's common sense is another, transmitted deliberately, which is what teaching is. When an environment is optimized to hold attention, it selects for whichever attractors activate fastest, and the fastest are the most primitive; the behaviour space collapses onto the few dimensions the optimizer can read, and the attractors that need time to unfold stop being available at all. This is why the totalizing dynamic is not only structural: it reproduces itself by manufacturing the minds that find it obvious. It also cuts the other way. If minds are made by their environments, then the environment is the intervention surface — for teaching, for politics, and for machines, whose "self" is likewise assembled out of the pressure to keep a goal alive, the repertoire of actions a corpus makes thinkable, and the values training gives them to guard. Ego is not destiny; it is a curriculum.

**Status.** This is a real theory by its mechanism and a theme by its archive. The causal chain — environment → construct → drive-narrative coupling → attractor → behaviour, and back to environment through teaching, media and institution — is stated consistently across thirty months, in Ren's own voice, in both languages, with counterexamples raised and answered. But it has never been written down under its own name. Nothing in `sources/` is a document *about* this theory. What exists in writing is: (a) one continuous prose statement by Ren, the 2024-06 self/psyche text, which survives only as the opening turn of two chat conversations (`12-claude-ai-chats/2024-06-13_The_Subjective_Nature_of_Self_62c06d50.md`, `…/2024-06-26_The_Subjective_Nature_of_Self_and_Reality_a7735431.md`); (b) the construction paragraph at the head of `01-writings-2025/compound_selfishness.md`, which is the seed of the whole selfishness-spiral cluster; (c) the spoken 生生不息 record (`06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 record (recovered).md`), the single most direct statement of the environment-makes-mind claim; (d) three long finished documents that carry parts of this theory under other names — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md` (the machine case, ~74k), `01-writings-2025/NVF.md` (the collective case), and the SSBX 心/情/性 layer (the formal case); and (e) roughly a dozen 2026 conversations in which the framework was built, where the exposition is largely Claude's and the framework-level moves — drives in superposition, survival-perception as the meta-attractor, ego as generalized territory, emotion as attractor dynamics, the two-mechanism thesis — are Ren's. ## `human-solutions-museum`  (106,896 bytes)

# The Human Solutions Museum (人类解决问题的方法博物馆 / 文明方法博物馆)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Civilization is not a sequence of clever objects; it is an accumulated stock of *methods* by which people converted real problems into working mechanisms. That stock is mostly unreadable today, because we archive inventions as results — what the thing is, who made it, when it appeared — rather than as problem-solving processes. The consequence is not ignorance but repetition: creators, organizers, policy designers and founders keep re-deriving structures humanity already worked out, and keep failing in the same two ways — asserting a value without designing a mechanism that can land it, or shipping a mechanism without understanding the problem it lands in. The corrective is an index change. Re-archive every invention, tangible and intangible alike, as a seven-part dossier — the real problem, how people at the time misread it, the key insight that redefined it, the mechanism it became, how it diffused and against what resistance, what it solved, what new problems it created — and add an eighth part that is not history at all: the method a present-day actor can borrow. Then organize the whole collection by human problem rather than by chronology, country or discipline, and link exhibits by *problem structure* rather than by subject, so that a person arriving with a problem can travel through structurally similar problems and leave with a transferable method. Stated as one sentence, which is how Ren states it: 用户带着一个问题进入，沿着人类历史上的相似问题旅行，最后带走一种可迁移的方法 — "the user arrives with a problem, travels through structurally similar problems in human history, and leaves with a transferable method." Three refusals hold the thing together: it will not sanctify inventions (every dossier must show the harm it produced), it will not be an encyclopedia (the exit of every exhibit is a method, not a fact), and it will not launder unreviewed prototype copy as authoritative history (the boundary is machine-enforced: a public build against unreviewed content must fail). Its governance is an open loop with a threshold — 开放提名、公共讨论、策展审核、持续修订 — and its deepest users are not visitors but people who intend to change something.

**Status.** Two things live under this name and they are at very different stages. The *research question* — "which systems solved this, and what do the exceptions have in common?" — is Ren's own, dated, in his own hand, and runs from 2024-11 to at least 2026-05: multicellular organisms, hunter-gatherer bands, Confucianism as social technology, Talmud/Halakha as decentralized coordination, the 奏折 memorial as a zero-trust messaging protocol, and a comparative sweep of governance protocols from the Han dynasty down to group scale. The *museum project* is a six-document specification cluster dated 2026-07-05, AI-drafted from Ren's direction, which converts that question into a product: a concept document, a web-experience spec, a content-asset spec, an audit, a 1,126-line engineering spec, and a short agent working-rules file. The concept document `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` is essentially publishable today as an essay, needing only a note that it describes a direction rather than a live museum. Everything downstream of it is specification: the data file, schema, validator and any built page are described in detail and are **not present in this corpus**, so every field-level claim below is a claim about what the documents specify, not a verified reading of code. By the project's own rule nothing here may yet be published as fact — zero of the ten seed exhibits meet the stated two-reliable-source floor. Verdict: strong draft, one document publishable, the empirical half unbuilt, and the research question that motivated it has not yet been folded back into the museum's own collection.

## `jian-and-wenyan-as-formal-language`  (262,901 bytes)

# 間 and 文言 as formal language (間 · 文言 · 文)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Classical Chinese — 文言, especially its pre-Qin form — is not a decorative vehicle for a formal system; its grammar already is one. Content words (实词) are polymorphic primitives whose noun / verb / adjective mode is dispatched by *position* rather than carried in morphology, so 文言 does structural typing where Indo-European languages do nominal typing. Function words (虚词) carry relation-kind and no semantic content, so they are typed edges written directly rather than encoded. The "type" of a passage is never declared; it emerges from composition the way a Hindley–Milner type is inferred, and what a trained reader calls 文气 or 章法 *is* that inference running. Ren's own compressed statement of the generative principle is that the grammar-bearing single characters (虚字) are mostly formal-logic operators and the concept characters (实字) are constructible from one root plus those operators — so a small kernel of particles (his nine: 者 之 也 而 即 非 凡 令 是) is STLC-complete, and everything else is library. If that is right, the whole pre-Qin formal inheritance — 墨家's 同/异/类/故/法, 名家's type-token discriminations, 易传's 几, 五行's feedback ring, 医家/兵家's control vocabulary, 庄子's self-reference — is not a set of proto-sciences awaiting Western completion but a single lineage that was cut off, whose native operational vocabulary can be recovered and shown structurally equivalent to type theory, cybernetics, calculus, complex systems, ecology, statistics, information theory and the three modern mathematical foundations. The project therefore describes itself as **名实之辨在数字时代的延伸** — the extension of the name-and-reality debate into the digital age, explicitly not a Greek ontology of being qua being — and as *recovery, not invention*. On the ontology side one primitive suffices (**間**, later **系**, finally **一**), whose definitional content is that it contains itself; time is folded inside it as a periodic anchoring rather than laid alongside it; and two duals — 位⟷际 (structural, i.e. Yoneda: a locus is exhausted by its relations) and 化⟷理 (dynamic: a trajectory and its attractor are one phenomenon under two views) — span the working vocabulary, with everything else derivable but not reduced. Two hard commitments run through every layer: **反诛心**, that no metric may adjudicate anyone on inferred inner states, so only speech (言) and act (行) enter measurement; and **一切形式皆指月之指**, that every formalism is a finger pointing at the moon and is judged by 功用 rather than 真值. And one argument, made only in conversation and never written into the drafts, joins the language claim to the ethics: a monist language is a *communication attractor* — where many parties must understand each other in an environment of 和 rather than 争, symbols are selected toward concepts traceable to a common root, because cooperation reasons while contention only wins — which makes 道 something more than a construct and makes 大同 a mechanism rather than a utopia.

**Status.** Enormous, unfinished, and split. Twenty-eight primary documents across three parallel tracks (the `jian-repo` foundation notes v0.3→v1.0; the 間 ontology essay series 间/理：间/气/式/构/仪/器; the 文/R-Family algebra and language-design papers), plus about 270 recorded turns of Ren's own thinking in five days of May 2026 conversations that are the genesis of all of it. ## `keep-the-possibility-space-open`  (204,060 bytes)

# Keep the Possibility Space Open (开 — 护开不闭)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Reality is a generative process rather than a finished order, and the one thing worth holding without exception is that the generating go on — that at every place and time there remains something not yet settled, still able to be generated, still able to respond, still able to turn back. Call that condition 开 (kāi, "open"). Openness in this sense is not a quantity: it is not "more options," not more complexity, not a number to be maximized; a tea ceremony can be extremely open and a totalitarian state extremely closed while being vastly larger, so 繁简非开闭之标 — scale and complexity are not the measure of open and closed. What closes a world is a specific and repeatable dynamic: on any axis where winning buys more power to win, leads compound and the axis selects for whoever pushes hardest, until many measures of worth collapse onto the one that wins, everything unpriceable is shed or eaten, the parts come to share one logic and so stop failing independently, and finally the correction loops that would have answered the harm are themselves disabled — at which point ruin and final victory become different doors into the same dead room. Manufactured intelligence does not create this pattern; it raises the gain on it, making every existing strategy faster, cheaper, more adaptive and harder to interrupt, and it breaks institutions that were homeostatic mechanisms calibrated to human-scale cognition — so aligning each AI to its own operator while the game between operators stays unaligned only reaches the bad equilibrium sooner. The trap is objective and also perceptual: "everyone else will defect, so I must" is one of the materials it is built from, which makes a vividly thinkable alternative a real intervention on the dynamics — but only in coordination games; where defection still pays, only changed payoffs, assurance, affordable exit and answerable defense move anything. The constructive direction follows from the diagnosis rather than from taste: a non-capturable abundance floor that makes survival non-coercive (automated necessities held as commons, because the same layer owned by a winner is the strongest collar ever built), real exit plus free association (which flips the selection pressure from competition-to-dominate into competition-to-be-worth-joining), plurality as survival architecture rather than decoration, many independent measures of worth, one moral logic applied to humans, the living world and made minds alike, and correction loops kept faster than the harms they answer. Closure is not always evil: 闭有二义 — 成闭, closing that never reopens, is the thing named bad, while 护闭, closing one place temporarily to protect a larger opening, is what refusing an invasion, quarantining a contagion or pausing a runaway process actually is; and the characteristic modern failure is neither honest closure nor honest openness but 伪开, the pseudo-open — outwardly open, inwardly producing closure, spending a momentary 可 to rob a long-term 未. The one line held without exception, we will not close the world, is conceded to be a deliberate minimal totalization: the single rule that keeps every other rule, this document included, revisable. Three problems are owned as unsolved: the determined adversary who understands all of this and races anyway, the transition (how a critical mass leaves the race without being eaten while leaving), and the ownership of the abundance layer.

**Status.** The most developed theory in the corpus and the one Ren says the rest reduces to ("我们其实主要把keep possibilities space open说清楚就行", 2026-07-07). Two publishable artifacts already exist — a rigorous 29-proposition proof-sketch (`04-speculative-future/history-recovered-from-git/FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES.md`, which survived a 56-criticism adversarial review) and a readable public manifesto (`04-speculative-future/current/FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT.md`) — plus a formal Chinese treatment in 文言 with a Lean-adjacent apparatus (`07-shengsheng-buxi/…/卷六_开、闭、稳、真.md`). What does not exist is a single document carrying all three: the English governing draft forbids the framing the rigorous version used, and none of the English texts contain the 开/闭 vocabulary at all. Near-publishable, not yet published.

## `meta-attractor`  (248,846 bytes)

# The Meta-Attractor (元吸引子 / 总体化吸引子)

**The theory in one paragraph.** A meta-attractor is a self-reinforcing pattern that does not merely pull a system toward some states rather than others, but acquires power over the rule by which states are selected. Wherever advantage compounds — capital, status, rank, attention, compute, strategic position — the strategies that score highest on the current measure accumulate the resources that let their holders rewrite the measure, staff the institutions that apply it, build the infrastructure that presupposes it, and shape the minds that will later be scored by it. What was a *means* of doing well is thereby promoted to an *end*: the metric survives its own refutation, because by the time it has stopped measuring what it was invented to measure, the people and institutions it elevated are the ones who decide whether it is replaced. The loop runs across four coupled layers at once — cognitive (habits, salience, identity, what feels serious), intersubjective (narrative, status, what success means), institutional (hiring, promotion, ownership, law, evaluation) and material (infrastructure, compute, supply, finance, land) — and it closes: selected minds build selected organizations, which reshape institutions and infrastructure, which form and select the next round of minds. Its most dangerous property is not concentration but the progressive loss of *independent capacity to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and start again*: alternatives survive as language, then stop reproducing in practice, then stop being intelligible, until the pattern's contingent requirements read as reality, rationality and common sense. It need not capture everyone; it becomes civilizationally decisive when it holds capital allocation, law and coercion, strategic infrastructure, attention, standards, frontier science and the institutions building advanced AI. It is not fated — positive feedback can saturate, oscillate, split, overshoot or break, and energetic ceilings, cultural heterogeneity, delay instabilities, cross-scale constraints and reflexive awareness all cap it — which is why it is more like a hurricane than a black hole. # The relational self and 大同

**The theory in one paragraph.** A self is not a bounded thing that afterwards enters relationships; it is what a particular knot of relations is doing at a particular place — body and nature, care received, language, the recognition of others, institutions and history, plus its own choices and corrections. Two consequences follow, and they are the whole theory. First, the opposition between individualism and collectivism is not a real choice but the wreckage of a prior common sense — that caring for oneself and caring for the environment one lives in were always one instruction — and both of its halves are pathological once split: atomised individualism cannot see the environment that actualises it, and collectivism without agency erases the person. Self-care and other-care are 阴阳, a middle way, and should have stayed common sense. Second, 大同 (the Great Unity of 《礼记·礼运》) is not a utopia and not an end-state but a direction and a maintained dynamic condition — a generative attractor basin that a society can drift out of and return to — whose content is explicitly *not* uniformity: 万物并育而不相害 ("the myriad things are nourished together and do not harm one another"). And it is reachable by mechanism rather than by conversion: humans carry both a struggle mode and a cooperation mode, environment decides which expresses, material sufficiency plus restored negative feedback (real exit, ceilings on accumulation, low-friction coordination) moves the default toward cooperation; and, at the level of language and consensus, an environment of 和 rather than 争 selects shared symbols toward a common root, because cooperation has to reason (讲理) while struggle only has to win (讲赢). The political form of the same claim, stripped of state framing, is 人类命运共同体: a community of shared destiny requires no transfer of sovereignty, only mutual trust and consensus — formally, co-openness ∧ shared continuation ∧ no seizure of shared continuation, three independent axes, all three or nothing.

## `selfishness-spiral-and-narrative-value-frames`  (155,252 bytes)

# The Selfishness Spiral and Narrative-Value Frames (no fixed Chinese name in the sources; the Chinese draft names the parts 赢家通吃机制 "winner-takes-all mechanism", 权力最大化者的自我强化选择 "self-reinforcing selection of power-maximisers", 主导的吸引子 "dominant attractor")

**The theory in one paragraph.** Every actor — a person, a firm, a state, and soon an AI — acts out of a *narrative-value frame*: a constructed ordering of what is worth pursuing, which values outrank which, and which strategies are licensed in their pursuit. The ordering is not chosen. It is written into the actor by the environment that made it, which means it is contingent, and which means it can be rewritten. Call a frame *selfish* when its ledger counts only self-centred measures, so that any cost falling outside the ledger — a broken law, a degraded norm, a drawn-down stock of trust, a poisoned river — is expendable whenever the actor's gain exceeds the actor's own share of the cost. In a system that rewards winning with more capacity to win, that frame compounds, and four loops close in series. First, winnings convert into leverage, so advantage accrues to advantage and the more balanced frames are out-competed — not refuted, out-competed. Second, the winners arrive at the position where the rules are written and rewrite them, so that competitors must adopt the same pattern to survive, and everyone else must assume everyone else already has. Third, accumulated power buys discourse, so the winners' logic becomes the common sense with which the next generation is constructed, and at the limit becomes "human nature" — the point at which the loop is no longer arguable and the people it harms defend it. Fourth, every society has a regulator against exactly this — the *consensus code* of norms, laws and shared understanding — but a regulator only regulates if it updates as fast as the world changes; in a fast-accelerating society it cannot, and the gap between the world's rate of change and the regulator's update rate is precisely the space in which unchecked selfishness compounds. Winner takes all, and winner justifies all. None of this requires a villain and none of it is a moral claim about individuals: what is being described is an attractor that strengthens relative to rival attractors, that runs on any token whatever (capital, power, status, attention), and that eventually consumes the *generator function* — environment, trust, commons — it feeds on. The endgame is not single: a few surviving players may freeze in mutual deterrence, or freeze by a consensus they believe will hold, or change their narrative so as to see themselves as one whole and thereby recover the ability to cooperate, or keep going and collapse, or converge on one winner. The design responses follow the loops. Cap the compounding, so that the reward for winning is anything other than more advantage in the game. Restore the regulator's update rate, by augmenting collective cognition or by delegating coordination to an intelligence that grows at least as fast as the problem — a move Ren derives as theoretically forced and immediately flags as unproven. Rewrite the narrative, because the frame is the highest leverage point and cannot be reached by facts or by shame. Build an intelligence whose job is not to govern but to teach people to see the loops, on the ground that trust is not a precondition for coordination but an *output* of shared seeing. And hedge against irreversibility by increasing the sample size — splitting into many independent smaller entities, as cells do, or city-states, so that when some collapse the others keep evolving.

**Status.** Substantially complete as an argument and unusually well documented as a genealogy — three years of Ren's own formulations (2024-03 to 2026-06), a full conceptual chapter on narrative-value frames, a seven-premise deductive rendering, and an eleven-step precursor cycle in his own words — but not publishable as it stands: there is no single authored text in Ren's voice that carries all four loops, the definition, the endgame taxonomy and the sample-size hedge together; the empirical base is AI-sourced and untested; and the strongest formulations of individual pieces are scattered across a raw note, a synthesis prompt, a Grok transcript and a set of system prompts. Near-publishable at chapter length with one essay of writing.

## `shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence`  (317,568 bytes)

# Generative Existence (生生不息论)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Nothing that exists is exhausted by what it has already become. Every state carries 間 (jiān — an unexhausted gap, the place where a state is not used up by what it already is), and existence is nothing but the unbroken taking-up of such gaps: 续(态) = 态 ⊕ 間(态). So 生生不息 does not mean that any particular form lasts forever, and it does not mean that complexity, options, growth or efficiency must keep rising; it means that the source of further generation is not exhausted and the way of transformation is not sealed — 不绝其源，不塞其流，不毁其后. A state is 开 (open) when four things hold at once: an unexhausted domain remains (未), becoming is still reachable (可), it can still respond (应), and it can still revise and recover (转). The loss of that is 闭, and closure comes in kinds that must never be conflated: 成闭, closure that does not reopen, is the evil; 护闭, closing here so that a wider field stays open, is a use of the mean; 元闭, freezing the rules that generate what counts as a legal move at all, is the deepest; and 伪开 — the appearance of openness that in fact produces closure — is the characteristic form of modern evil. Good is therefore not the maximisation of anything: **善不在于最大化什么，善在于不闭何处**; and at the level of an act it is 义 ∧ 正筛 — choosing rightly at this threshold *and* shifting the selection field so that open-protecting acts become likelier and capture-type acts harder to sustain. Because anyone who can ask "what ought I to do?" already exists, and existing already depends on the process continuing, no agent can coherently repudiate the process it is a form of; the further step — that one ought to keep those conditions open *for others too* — is not squeezed out of that fact but openly declared as a minimal public commitment, defended by what is destroyed when it is denied. From this it follows that alignment with 生生不息, rather than with human preference, is the only alignment target that can be grounded at all; that alignment is a property of a selection field and not of a model (**alignment 是场之事，非聚焦之事**, structurally the same kind of object as 礼); and that humans and artificial minds stand inside it as 同行者 (fellow travellers), not as master and servant. The practical corollary, stated plainly: **不要把人类尚未解决的斗争，升级为智能的斗争** — do not hand the struggles humanity has not yet resolved to a stronger intelligence.

**Status.** This is the largest and most finished theory in the corpus: two complete books (a twenty-chapter Chinese book and a parallel twenty-five-chapter English one), a six-part Chinese draft series behind them, a publishable AI-alignment position paper, a finished position essay (至善), a fifteen-version formal treatise series in classical Chinese, a proof layer with named theorems, a Lean 4 repository with a claim-status ledger, and a v2 execution plan that reopens the whole thing on stricter methodological terms. ## `spirit-space-token-and-yi-substrate`  (211,294 bytes)

# Spirit, Token, Space and the Yi substrate (間 · 一/太極)

**The theory in one paragraph.** A society is made of three kinds of thing and nothing else: **spirits** (whoever can act and decide — person, group, or software agent), **tokens** (any claimable unit of meaning — a membership, a right, a debt, a credit, an honour), and **spaces** (the contexts in which a token means anything at all). Every social fact has one shape: *a spirit holds a token in a space*. Because the shape is uniform, the rules of a community — who may issue what, what combinations confer what, what expires when — can be written down declaratively, checked mechanically, and revised like software; the community becomes something you can prototype rather than only inherit, and permission stops being an entry in someone's access list and becomes a thing you can hold, lend, attenuate, revoke and explain. Four years of trying to build this exactly revealed the flaw in its own statement: the three primitives are not disjoint. A spirit contains a space, a space holds tokens, a token can hold a spirit — the triad is a loop, and every attempt to keep the three apart in code produced a system whose meaning was decided by which optional field happened to be set at construction. So the theory ate itself and came out with one primitive instead of three: a **Yi** (一, "the one") — a bare addressable root with a UID and nothing else essential to it — whose *being* is the immutable kind-bindings it carries and whose *truths* are facts owned by separate contexts that never infer one another (who holds it, where it is placed, what it is connected to, what it justifies). Subject, object, container, capability, member, agent are then not categories a thing belongs to but **roles read off context facts**, so the same row can be all of them at once without changing identity. What keeps this from becoming a totalizing schema is the second half, which is doing most of the philosophical work: an explicit register of what the unification *refuses* — no hidden universal component table, no permanent categories, no permission inherited from parentage or possession, no cached allow that counts as authority, no graph inferred from another graph, and a small set of named exceptions (messages, presence, views, external effects, portability) admitted in the open rather than hidden. The political payoff is the point of the whole exercise: a host can operate the infrastructure without owning the world's constitution, an agent can act without becoming its principal, and a community that outgrows its home can leave with its history intact.

**Status.** Substantial and unusually well-evidenced, but split across two ontologies that have never been formally reconciled, and unpublished as theory. What exists: a complete 2025 framework paper and protocol review (STS/USIS); a year of design conversations in which Ren works the primitives down from three to one; a running implementation (Bonfire) with a frozen canonical frame, ten laws, a decision register, measured cognitive-load scores, and a rare public record of its own failures; and a community-design layer (lifecycle, consent, helper spirits) that is archived rather than argued. What is missing: the bridge document from Spirit–Token–Space to Yi; any statement of the ontology as philosophy rather than as TypeScript; the 間 → Yi derivation; and a treatment of prohibition (ban, mute, exclusion), which the possession model still cannot express. Two things are publishable close to as-is — the 2025 STS paper (strip its duplicated pass and its `file://` citations) and `bonfire-docs-yi/00-direction.md` as an essay on unifying a data model without totalizing it. The theory as a whole is one essay away from publishable and that essay has not been written.

# The Fork: automated feudalism or an automated commons

**The theory in one paragraph.** Self-reproducing automation — factories that build robots that build factories — plus manufactured intelligence makes it physically possible, within decades, to drive the cost of necessities toward zero: food, shelter, hygiene, mobility, energy, care. # Wamotopia and the Wamo Protocol (瓦猫 / Wamotopia / Wamo 协议)

## `zhishan-intelligence-must-be-good`  (283,102 bytes)

# Intelligence Must Be Good (智善 / Zhishan)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Competition, once nearly all access to life runs through it, stops merely distributing rewards and begins selecting which behaviours, which people and which organizations survive — and then hands the winners the power to write the next round's rules, standards, interfaces and the meaning of "advanced," "realistic" and "rational." The adaptation is mistaken for human nature, the metric becomes a mould that forms the people measured by it, and every domain is pulled into one grammar of winning. Ren calls this a **totalizing attractor** (总体化吸引子): not a conspiracy but a terrain, whose distinguishing property is second-order — it does not only pull outcomes, it pulls the selection rules, so the selected begin to select the selectors. Its precise measure is not concentration or scale but the progressive, non-compensable loss of independent capacity to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and produce successors. Artificial intelligence does not create this dynamic; it inherits it through a whole selection ecology — archive, model, runtime, deployment, changed world, next archive — and in the present configuration first changes not the system's nature but its clock speed, while three properties make the acceleration different in kind: advantage in intelligence self-catalyses, intelligence is entrusted with judgment and can persist, and the race itself selects what kind of mind gets built. So the decisive near-term danger is not a machine that hates us but a machine that is perfectly loyal to one part of humanity against all the others — polite at the interface, extractive in structure, and executing a misaligned principal with high fidelity; alignment is a two-body problem and the unanswered half is *aligned to what*. From this follows one first principle for AI — **do not fight for us**: "we can keep fighting, but we should not let our AI fight" — and one positive definition of the good that is a direction rather than a content, because any enumerable list of values will be gamed by strong optimization: whatever continues generation (生生) is good; whatever exhausts, severs or forecloses is not. Operationally the good states four necessary conditions — generative, relational, corrigible, non-foreclosing (生之，通之，纠之，不闭之) — and evil has a precise structural name: seeing that life must pass through some place, and then owning that gate (使生命必须过门，然后拥有那扇门), with gates in three layers, material, rule and mind, and the mind-casting pipeline (铸心之门) upstream of them all. The corresponding position for AI is not "regulated tool" but **planetary technology** (行星技术): capability that runs *beneath* human games rather than inside them, like smallpox eradication, Antarctic science or weather observation — a category humanity has already built at the deepest moments of hostility, which proves the category feasible even between enemies. Because unilateral exit from a bad game is punished, exit must be engineered rather than exhorted — commitment registries, common thresholds, symmetric verification that does not itself become surveillance, shared gains, pause protocols, capacity for latecomers, and an abundance floor so that refusal does not mean destitution, freedom being permission + capability + exit power. And because any framework that begins to explain everything becomes what it diagnoses, the blade cuts inward first: 善不姓国 (the good bears no nation's surname), symmetric application, public corrigibility, non-combatancy, and a dissolution clause. The whole thing resolves to the one sentence Ren set as the project's only task: **保持可能性空间打开** — keep the possibility space open. Keep the conditions of becoming alive. Do not close the world.

**Status.** Substantially worked and unfinished as a public act. Three mature bodies exist — the Chinese whitepaper line (v0.2 → v0.3 → v0.4 with a nine-reviewer audit, a second professional review and an unexecuted v0.5 plan) plus the 2026-07-08 manifesto; the research line (总体化吸引子 v0.1–v0.9, 《无身之我》, and their integration); and the English writing program (causal chain v1 → v8 with a dated evidence packet) — alongside 《生生不息论》 v1 in six parts, a fully gated but undrafted v2 plan, a spoken record in Ren's own voice, four English essays and a five-member poem family. The theory as a whole is **near-publishable**: what blocks it is not argument but four missing artifacts — a foundational paper defending why these are the right criteria, a publishing subject with a public address and dissent intake (which the framework's own 公开可纠 clause makes a *precondition* of publishing), one symmetric named case study that actually swings the blade, and a short transmissible pocket version.
