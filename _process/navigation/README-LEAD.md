The Theory of Becoming explains formation, persistence, transformation, degeneration and dissolution within one relational unfolding, without first assuming that becoming is good. It uses no Lean axiom: each law is proved under the conditions it names. Living significance, that every living claim has significance from every standpoint, is a theorem under one condition the theory states and does not assert: the continuation condition, that for anything its continuation into the future has significance (chapter section 6.15). With explicit practical commitments, living significance supplies reasons addressed to every situated intelligence and a normative framework for AI alignment.
