The durable direction is `docs/yi/00-direction.md`; the conceptual frame is
`docs/yi/01-frame.md`. `docs/yi/README.md` names the current owners; archived
numbered plans live under `docs/yi/archive/`. Implementation status comes
from `docs/yi/49-structural-arc/HANDOFF.md`, live code, and tests—not from the
older frame/pivot review history.

The mental model is intentionally small: Yi makes **being, having, doing, and
permitting** one composable substrate. A tie to a type is a trait; a tie to an
instance is a relation; a Yi offers ports; gates decide who may fire them.
Roles such as holder/held or subject/object are positions in a tie, not stored
types, and agency is a trait. Storage, indexes, streams, projections, and
fan-out may specialize below that authoring frame.

Treat conceptual claims as destinations unless production callers and tests
show the capability runs. The former proof-fixture, byte-pin, and
evidence-per-landing apparatus is superseded by
[[verification-regime-2026-08-14]].
