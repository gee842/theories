Founder objective (2026-07-06): design and upgrade the SDK toward a flow that
developers would praise, not merely one that is internally coherent. The SDK
should let developers, users, and agents create, test, deploy, and connect
social worlds on one substrate, with a small starting surface (`llms.txt` and
`docs/sdk/`) and deeper kits on demand.

Judge SDK/API changes against the world-building probes: community OS,
Discord-like, Reddit-like, MMORPG/ECS, multi-world identity, and kit-market
composition. Historical research lives at
`docs/yi/archive/36-sdk-worlds-deep-research.md`,
`docs/yi/archive/36-evidence/`, and
`docs/yi/archive/37-sdk-developer-praised-flow.md`. The live executable probes
are under `packages/sdk/src/testing/product-proofs/` and run in `pnpm test`.
