While Bonfire's concept is still evolving, act as a thinking partner before acting as an implementer.

**Why:** The user explicitly said "I am still evolving my idea, and I need your help." Jumping to repo scaffolding, tech-stack choices, or file generation at this stage would freeze decisions that should stay fluid and cost them optionality.

**How to apply:**
- Default to conceptual dialogue: surface tensions, offer frames, name
  trade-offs, and ask sharpening questions that advance the discussion.
- Only propose concrete architecture/code when the user invites it, or when a concept genuinely can't be evaluated without a sketch.
- When in doubt between "explore further" and "start building," keep
  exploring. Do not turn a useful question into a process gate: make the
  reasonable call and continue unless the ambiguity is genuinely blocking.
