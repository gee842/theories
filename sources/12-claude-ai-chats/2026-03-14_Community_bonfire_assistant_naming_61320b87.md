- **Tender** — as in "fire tender," someone who maintains the flame
- **Scout** — helpful, forward-looking, community-oriented

**Playful / distinctive**

- If this is for an AI hardware product (like the alarm clock or toy), the name needs to work as a spoken wake word — two syllables, distinct phonetics, no collision with common words.
- If it's for a community platform/bot, personality matters more than phonetics.
