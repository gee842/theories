Good, I have solid info now. Let me summarize the key points about Da Nang SIM card pricing and the airport premium.Here's the breakdown for Da Nang:

**City price:** Tourist SIM cards in Vietnam generally cost between 160,000 and 350,000 VND (~$6–15), depending on how much data and duration you want. A basic prepaid SIM itself is around 50,000 VND, then you add a data package on top. For a 30-day tourist SIM with a decent data allowance, you're typically looking at around $6–11 in city shops or official carrier stores (Viettel, Vinaphone, Mobifone).

**Airport price (Da Nang):** At Da Nang airport, tourists should expect to pay around 200,000–250,000 VND ($8–10) for 10–60GB over 7–30 days, which is roughly 30–40% more than in the city.

So for a 1-month plan, you're looking at roughly **$6–8 in the city vs. $8–10+ at the airport** — a premium of a few dollars for the convenience. As a general rule, buying in the city gets you about double the data for the same price compared to airport kiosks.

If you want to minimize cost, grab a Grab into town and buy from a Viettel or Vinaphone store (or a chain like Thế Giới Di Động). But honestly for a $2–3 difference and the convenience of being connected immediately, the airport kiosk is pretty reasonable — especially after a long flight. The staff there will also set it up in your phone for you.

One thing to note: the Da Nang airport SIM kiosks may not be available after 10 PM, so if you're arriving on a late flight, plan accordingly.
