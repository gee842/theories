 Cybernetics is a vast and complex field that encompasses the study of communication and control systems in living organisms and machines. It is an interdisciplinary subject that combines concepts from various fields, including engineering, biology, psychology, and computer science. To learn cybernetics, you can start by exploring the following topics:

1. Foundations of Cybernetics:
   - Study the works of early cyberneticians like Norbert Wiener, William Ross Ashby, and Claude Shannon.
   - Understand the basic principles of feedback, control, and communication in systems.

2. Systems Theory:
   - Learn about the properties and behavior of complex systems.
   - Explore concepts such as emergence, self-organization, and adaptation.

3. Information Theory:
   - Study the quantification, storage, and communication of information.
   - Understand the concepts of entropy, coding, and channel capacity.

4. Control Theory:
   - Learn about the principles of controlling the behavior of dynamic systems.
   - Study topics like stability, robustness, and optimal control.

5. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning:
   - Explore how cybernetic principles are applied in the development of intelligent systems.
   - Study topics such as neural networks, deep learning, and reinforcement learning.

6. Robotics and Automation:
   - Learn about the application of cybernetic principles in the design and control of robots and automated systems.
   - Study topics like sensor fusion, motion planning, and human-robot interaction.

7. Biological Cybernetics:
   - Explore how cybernetic principles are observed and applied in living systems.
   - Study topics such as neural networks, sensory-motor control, and bio-inspired robotics.

To deepen your understanding of cybernetics, consider the following resources:

- Books:
  - "Cybernetics: Or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine" by Norbert Wiener
  - "An Introduction to Cybernetics" by William Ross Ashby
  - "The Human Use of Human Beings: Cybernetics and Society" by Norbert Wiener

- Online Courses:
  - "Introduction to Cybernetics" by the Santa Fe Institute on Complexity Explorer
  - "Cybernetics: History and Future" by the University of Reading on FutureLearn

- Academic Programs:
  - Look for undergraduate or graduate programs in cybernetics, systems science, or related fields at universities with strong research groups in these areas.

Remember that cybernetics is a broad and evolving field, and there are many more topics and resources to explore as you delve deeper into the subject.
