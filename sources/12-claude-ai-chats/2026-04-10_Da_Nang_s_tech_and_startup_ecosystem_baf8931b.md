Good data.Da Nang's got a surprisingly active and fast-growing ecosystem. Here's the picture:

**Core industries:** The city has identified six priority sectors: high-tech/semiconductors/AI/digital industries, mechanical engineering and automotive, biotech and medicinal plants, logistics and free trade zones, high-quality tourism, and financial services/education/healthcare.

**Tech and startups specifically:** Da Nang is rising as a cost-effective destination for IT outsourcing and software development, benefiting from lower costs than HCMC and Hanoi. The startup ecosystem grew nearly 58% in 2025 and now ranks #766 globally, with 38 tracked startups. Still early compared to HCMC (#110) and Hanoi (#148), but momentum is real.

**Semiconductor and AI push:** The city launched a major data center at its Hi-Tech Park (1,000 racks, ~$32M investment) and opened Software Park No. 2 with capacity for 6,000 IT engineers, targeting semiconductor and chip design. US chip firms like Marvell, Synopsys, and Uniquify already have presence, alongside Japan's Renesas and Vietnam's FPT Semiconductor. There's also a $69M semiconductor Lab-Fab facility (VSAP LAB) in development.

**Ecosystem infrastructure:** The city has incubated 200+ startups and invested ~500 billion VND in a dedicated innovation space. The target is 3,000 startups and 50,000 private firms by 2035, with a permanent community of 5,000+ experts including 1,000 foreign professionals.

Given your hardware sourcing and agent infra work, Da Nang's interesting as a lower-cost base with growing semiconductor ties — though for deep Huaqiangbei-style manufacturing ecosystem density it's obviously not comparable to Shenzhen. The international cooperation pipeline with Shenzhen, Singapore, and Korea could be worth watching though.
