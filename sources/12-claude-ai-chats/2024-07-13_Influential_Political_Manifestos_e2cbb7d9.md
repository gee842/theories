**ASSISTANT** (2024-07-13T10:59):

 Several other manifestos have had significant influence throughout history, though the Communist Manifesto remains one of the most well-known. Here are some other influential manifestos:

1. The Declaration of Independence (1776) - While not technically a manifesto, this document had a profound impact on political thought and inspired revolutionary movements worldwide.

2. The Futurist Manifesto (1909) - This artistic and social manifesto by Filippo Tommaso Marinetti influenced avant-garde art movements and had political implications.

3. The SCUM Manifesto (1967) - Valerie Solanas' radical feminist manifesto gained notoriety and influenced certain strains of feminist thought.

4. The Port Huron Statement (1962) - This manifesto of the Students for a Democratic Society became a defining document of the New Left movement in the United States.

5. The Humanist Manifesto (1933, with later versions) - This document outlined a philosophical and religious stance that has influenced secular humanism.

6. The Unabomber Manifesto (1995) - While highly controversial, this anti-technology and anti-industrial manifesto by Theodore Kaczynski sparked discussions about technology's impact on society.

7. The Hacker's Manifesto (1986) - This short essay by The Mentor influenced hacker culture and ethics.

8. The Leap Manifesto (2015) - This Canadian document calls for a restructuring of the economy to address climate change and has influenced environmental movements.

These manifestos vary widely in their content, reception, and lasting impact. None have achieved the same level of global influence as the Communist Manifesto, but each has played a significant role in shaping ideologies, movements, or fields of thought.
