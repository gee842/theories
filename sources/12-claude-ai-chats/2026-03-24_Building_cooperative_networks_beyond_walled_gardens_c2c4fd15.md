This is not a prediction. This is happening now, and it changes everything about who gets to decide how humans connect with each other.

A payment system that tracks every purchase to build a profile it sells to people you'll never meet. Each one offers something you genuinely need — communication, information, navigation, commerce — wrapped inside a machine whose actual purpose is to convert your attention into someone else's revenue. You know this. Everyone knows this. We use them anyway because there was no alternative. The cost of building one was too high. That constraint is gone.

**Understand what these systems actually do to you.**

They don't just waste your time. They shape how you think. A network optimized for engagement feeds you what provokes you, not what's true. Over years, this rewires your sense of reality. You become more certain and less accurate. More reactive and less reflective. You feel more connected to strangers performing outrage and less connected to the person sitting next to you. This is not a side effect. This is the product working as designed. Attention is a finite resource and whoever controls the allocation of yours controls, slowly, who you become. You are being shaped by systems that do not care what shape you end up in, only that you remain malleable.

**Now look at what this does to us collectively.**

A society where every individual's information environment is optimized to maximize engagement is a society that cannot coordinate. You can't build consensus when everyone's feed is a funhouse mirror tuned to their specific anxieties. You can't solve shared problems when the tools you use to communicate are actively profiting from misunderstanding. We don't have a disagreement crisis. We have an infrastructure crisis. The medium through which we try to think together is poisoned, and we keep blaming each other for the symptoms.

The same AI that these platforms use to manipulate you can be used to build for you instead. Not in theory. Not as a startup pitch. As a functioning reality, built and owned by the people who use it.
