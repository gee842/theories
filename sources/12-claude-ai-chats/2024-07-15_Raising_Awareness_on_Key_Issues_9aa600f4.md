 This type of article is typically called an "awareness article" or "advocacy article."

The main purposes of such articles are to:

1. Raise public awareness about a specific issue or topic
2. Explain the importance and impact of the issue
3. Spark interest and concern in readers
4. Potentially call for action or change

In English, there are several other common terms used:

- Consciousness-raising article
- Public awareness piece
- Issue-focused article
- Social impact writing

These articles often appear in news media, social platforms, NGO websites, and other channels to draw public attention to important social issues.

**ASSISTANT** (2024-07-15T17:56):

 To prompt an AI to write a great awareness article, consider the following approach:

1. Specify the topic: Clearly define the issue you want to raise awareness about.

2. Provide context: Give the AI background information on the topic.

3. Define the target audience: Specify who the article is intended for.

4. Set the tone: Indicate whether you want the article to be formal, conversational, urgent, etc.

5. Outline key points: List the main ideas you want the article to cover.

6. Request a hook: Ask for an engaging opening to capture reader attention.

7. Include data: Ask for relevant statistics or facts to be incorporated.

8. Personal stories: Request the inclusion of hypothetical personal accounts or case studies.

9. Call to action: Specify what you want readers to do after reading.

10. Article structure: Suggest a format (e.g., problem-solution, Q&A, listicle).

Here's an example prompt:

"Write an awareness article about climate change. Target audience is young adults. Use a conversational tone. Include recent statistics on global temperature rise. Incorporate a hypothetical personal story of a climate refugee. Explain the main causes and potential solutions. End with a call to action for individual lifestyle changes. Structure it as an introduction, three main points, and a conclusion. Start with a hook that emphasizes urgency."

Remember to review and refine the AI-generated content to ensure accuracy and alignment with your goals.

 The tone of a manifesto is typically:

1. Assertive and bold
2. Passionate and urgent
3. Declarative and confident
4. Revolutionary or transformative
5. Idealistic yet concrete
6. Provocative and challenging to the status quo
7. Inspirational and rallying

2. Vivid descriptions of how capital accumulation undermines democracy and social stability
3. A vision of an alternative economic future that prioritizes equity and shared prosperity
4. A list of concrete demands or action items to achieve this vision

Writing guidelines:
- Begin with a striking, attention-grabbing opening statement
- Use strong, emotive language that conveys urgency and importance
- Employ short, punchy sentences and paragraphs for emphasis
- Incorporate rhetorical devices like repetition, parallelism, and climax to build momentum

- Include powerful metaphors or analogies to illustrate key points
- End with a rousing call to action that inspires immediate engagement
