   Explanation: This sentence explains the direct result of the disparity outlined in the first sentence.    Explanation: This sentence further elaborates on the consequences of the gap between the rate of change and the ability to understand and respond to it. As the disparity grows, decision-makers within governing organizations will find it increasingly challenging to make well-informed and suitable decisions, as their understanding of the situation becomes more limited and outdated.

4. "As this trend persists, the probability of sustaining the system will diminish, eventually reaching a point where collapse becomes inevitable."
   Explanation: This final sentence projects the long-term outcome if the trend continues unchecked. As the ability to make appropriate decisions declines, the likelihood of maintaining a stable and functioning system decreases.
