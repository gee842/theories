Three suffix patterns worth keeping in mind:
- **-ology** = the study of X
- **meta-** = X about X (one level up the stack)
- **-ic / -ics** = the principles or art of X

Worth noting that ontology and metaphysics overlap heavily — ontology is usually treated as the "what is there?" subset of the broader metaphysical question "what is the nature of what is there?" And axiology is the cleanest umbrella term when you want to talk about value-questions without committing to ethics-vs-aesthetics.
