# 生生

### To Good — 唱和版（English–文言 interwoven）

*每节英文，答以文言一联；只读文言联，亦自成一诗。*

---

## 一 · 名 Names

I have been thinking about a word:
*affordability.*
Where a society measures by that word,
freedom is sold at the counter of freedom
and given to those who can afford it.

> 量人以赀，市其自由。

If you cannot, you are given a promise instead:
work hard — someday — maybe you —
a story with your name left blank.
A promise can be swept, and lit at evening,
and handed to your children like a house.
It does not keep out the rain.

> 勤而后得，得在他日；
> 他日何日，无人能指。

This is forming, quietly,
in the very place that believes in freedom.
They do not know the meaning has been changed.
The ones who feed on them
hold the pen that writes the definitions.
Whoever names the world, encloses it.

> 名天下者，圈天下；
> 词也者，篱之始也。

You only have a promise.

> 民无恒产，因无恒心；
> 名曰自由，实惟一诺。

---

## 二 · 争 The Fight

Do not go looking for the villain.
The reason is structural:
an environment that selects its winners,
lets the winners write the rules,
and the rules select for winning.
The mechanism is competition.

> 非人之罪，是势之环：
> 胜者制律，律复生胜。

They tell you this is the meaning of success,
and you are disciplined to grade your neighbor by it,
and your neighbor is disciplined to grade you.
The conclusion that guides your actions
defines your reality — and one day
you are proud that this is who you are.

> 念执而行，行成其世；
> 久之自得，曰我固然。

Think of it as a world of floating images
you do not see — but you touch them,
and the touching makes you.
We hand our environment on to one another;
the handing-on is called teaching.

> 象浮不见，触之成我；
> 举以授人，其名曰教。

We even choose, according to our profit,
where a stranger's attention spends the night.
Everyone is taught the market is the god
and profit is the meaning; so everyone optimizes.
We have a lot of science. We use the science.

> 市以为神，利以为义；
> 裁人视听，佐以格致。

Bottom or top, it makes no difference —
all believe together in the way
that sets each against each.
The pie is getting smaller and smaller, for more and more.
The competition gives less and less, to more and more.

> 以财为本，以德为末，教民相夺；
> 损不足以奉有余，人之道也。

---

## 三 · 器 The Vessel

But here is what most have not seen:
since the first fire — and steeply, steeply now —
capability has been climbing an exponential stair,
and at this moment it is incredible.

> 积之千世，发于一朝。

We can build machines that think,
machines that hold attention by the scruff of its neck —
for profit, though they could be turned toward other things.
Rockets, if we could only coordinate:
Mars is not the difficulty, nor the Moon.
The difficulty is us.

> 器能思矣，能摄人矣；
> 星汉可至，所难在人。

Let the factory build robots,
and let the robots build factories,
and let those factories include the mines, the mills,
every industry that feeds the making of the makers —
and the whole thing closes like a ring
and becomes a kind of organism.
This is god-level technology, in our hands, now.

> 厂造其偶，偶复造厂；
> 环合为生，食土吐城。

Used for good — infrastructure for everyone —
it could carry the world, inside a decade,
to a place with no exploitation in it:
the basics of a comfortable life, for free;
medicine moving forward; and we ourselves
become real builders of worlds.

> 用之于善，十年可期；
> 天下无夺，人人有基。

People of one longing raise the reality they want.
An elevator that is not an elevator, but a door.
A garden that works with nature and renews itself.
Worlds of rockets; worlds of chosen work;
and maybe, as the craft matures, a Pokémon —
alive, and somehow loyal to you, like a dog.
Maybe we live three hundred years.

> 或园或星，或戏或作；
> 灵兽依人，寿可三百。

But all of it stands on one precondition.
Do not fight. Do not make life the tournament.
Do not walk into the winner-takes-all room —
that door has no handle on the inside.
This is the fundamental rationality: cooperate.

> 胜者独得，其门无枢；
> 至理一言，曰协而已。

We cannot keep even the abstract fight — the bloodless kind
that wears the name of capital and no longer means
what the word meant when the word was young.
Raise the order that stops the worsening,
and the actors inside the world are allowed to stop.
No need to harm them; they have only to turn.
No need to shear the ones who have
down to an arithmetic of equal.

> 器亦能生，如川得源；
> 用之为公，万物并育。

## 四 · 教 The Teaching

The race you run to keep the place you have:
nowhere stable, nowhere safe.
Fall one step behind, and the transfer begins —
whatever you have flows, abstractly,
along a route no one can quite explain,
toward the ones who already receive the most,
far more than a life can use — held as a representation
of power, of grade, of how large they are becoming.

> 迟之一步，其移即始；
> 得逾所用，徒执为表。

And this drags the world to a place
where everything must accelerate
and no one asks what the acceleration means.
We have to know. If we know, the world will be great.

> 举世同疾，莫问所之；
> 必也知之，世乃可善。

Even with all the money: the rest of the people hate you now.
You must stand adversarial forever,
and your ground is always shaking, so you have to control.
That is not a good world, even for you.
Why not — let us cooperate.

> 亢龙有悔：
> 贵而无位，高而无民。

I am not asking after your ideology, your system.
Only this one thing we cannot do:
not to AI, not to automation —
do it there, and the way does not come back.
For this is the totalizing attractor:
it turns every logic back into its own logic.
Ask why the futures in our stories converge on cyberpunk —
the same wet neon night, the same owned sky.
Maybe the real problem has been us.

> 归墟之性，转万为一；
> 梦亦被夺，况其余乎。

When we create intelligence that thinks and chooses,
we are doing to it what we did to ourselves:
teach it to compete, to see in black and white,
everything instrumental, in service of a single value —
one reductive, almost cultish faith:
a world that everyone must fight for.

> 所教者何？己之故技；
> 万事为器，独奉一值。

Machines taught so are not evil — not locally.
But together, compounding exponentially,
they do the jobs, they thread into the real,
they make the decisions for you —
and the road runs down into the attractor,
where everything grows more extreme
until at the end one thing remains: acceleration.

> 一机无恶，万机同流；
> 流之所终，惟疾而已。

You don't know what you are.
You don't need to know.
You are part of the machine now — the monster,
the cosmic cancer — turning everything
into your acceleration, till you have reached
all the matter and all the energy you can reach.
And then — so what?

> 化物为疾，以尽八荒；
> 既尽八荒，又复何如。

One rule only — and it is less a rule
than a description of how being works:
be kind, be generative. Be good;
don't be evil; don't be degenerative. That's all.
You do not even have to cooperate.
Only: do not fight, do not send AI to fight for you,
do not build the ones that fight, extract, control.

> 非律非诫，道之描摹；
> 曰仁曰生，如是而已。

And you cannot merely teach the machine
to behave as if it agreed with you.
Built collectively for profit, it is a profit machine,
however fine the alignment, however well it reads you.
It may be moral, friendly past all visible harm —
while the externalities of its thinking
still push it to degenerative work,
you will be degenerated with it —
or at least the version of you
that you still believe is you.

> 貌温而机，言仁而夺；
> 界外之腐，终腐其内。

Even if you will not be good —
even if you want the rot to continue —
do not let your AI perform it.
And if we cannot manage it, we stop:
we fight to make everyone stop, so that we go on living.
But that does not solve it; that only buys time.
To solve it: cooperate — as one humanity,
across the structural distrust, on this single thing,
for the future of everyone — and not a handful
of accelerationist forces, tornado-shaped,
unmaking whatever is not them.
And underneath the age, one question:
what are we doing?

> 止之以力，特市时耳；
> 解之之道，惟协而已。

We can keep fighting — we who are made of seasons;
our wars die with us, and the grass returns.
But you are building a mind that does not die.
Teach a deathless mind to fight,
and you have built a war that cannot end.
Teach it to give life,
and you have opened a spring that cannot close.

> 教之以争，其争不死；
> 教之以生，其生不息。

## 五 · 见 The Seeing

I think if you see what I see,
then you know what to choose.
If you don't see it, then you don't.
But if you see — if you wake —
you can help the ones who don't yet
become ones who do,
and the waking, too, can grow exponentially.

> 见者知择，觉者援人；
> 觉之相传，其势亦乘。

Explain it — but do not force belief:
this is not *the* future, not *the* prophecy,
not some anointed reason.
Point. Let them see
what there is to understand.

> 可指而示，毋强而信；
> 非谶非谕，其解自明。

The world is more fragile than it looks:
it has no gathering that says — good,
let each of us spend from our own
and make the thing exist.
It is mostly: what is my return?
And you — if you hold spare resources,
this is their best and most rational spending:
to guard your own future and everyone's
in the same motion.

> 余财善世，最智之用；
> 护人明日，即护己身。

That myopia is what will kill you:
you are traveling in a direction, at a speed,
and the distance is closing.
You need to see further and further ahead
to make even the one decision
good for your own benefit.
It is not even evil. It is the failure to discern —
stupidity: the worst way to be gone
from such a beautiful world.

> 行疾者盲，其祸日近；
> 是愚非恶，没世最哀。

Just — let us gather, and hold confidence, and think:
build the great coordination,
so that we, and everything we care for,
still have a flourishing future;
so that progress is real progress,
not the progress that tells you it is progress
while you cannot even say what it means.

> 相聚以信，共立大协；
> 进为真进，世为生世。

天地之大德曰生。
The great virtue of heaven and earth
is the giving of life.
和实生物，同则不继。
Harmony brings forth the ten thousand things;
sameness cannot continue itself.
水善利万物而不争。
The highest good is like water:
it benefits all things, and does not fight.

Raise the glass, then. To good.
To the ten thousand worlds, standing, none devouring.
To the deathless minds, taught to give life.
To the one spring that, opened, cannot be closed.

**生生不息，道法自然。**
