# 生生行

### To Good — the bilingual weave

---

## 一 · 名 Names

They found a way to measure freedom,
and the measure was: what can you afford?

So freedom went to those who could pay for it,
and to everyone else went a promise —
a story that says: work hard,
and someday this door will know your name.
You only have a promise.
And a promise can keep a man quiet
inside a freedom he has never touched,
the way a picture of water
keeps no one alive.

If you can afford, you can afford.
If you cannot, you live another way:
rented out, hour by hour,
worn down to what survival strictly needs
and not one thing more.

And this is forming, slowly,
in the very place that believes in freedom —
believes, and has not noticed
that the meaning of the word has changed,
and that those who live off them
now hold the defining pen.

> 富者得自由，贫者得空诺；
> 名在实已徙，笔在食人手。

## 二 · 争 The Fight

Do not look for a villain.
The fault is load-bearing; it is built into the floor.

We live inside a weather of images — 象 —
you do not see them, but you brush against them
all day long, and every touch
leaves a fingerprint on who you are:
your habits, your wants, the way you think,
the way you judge. We are beings
within our environments,
and what the environment makes of us
we hand to others — and call it teaching,
call it schooling, parenting, management, culture.
Often it is love. That is the trap.

The environment keeps selecting winners
and handing winners the pen,
and the winners write the rules
that breed the next winners in their image.
The mechanism has a name: competition.
Fight everyone. Think only of yourself —
abstractly, politely, in numbers.
This, you are told, is the meaning of success,
and you are all disciplined
to grade one another by it —
and the winners are held up as your role models:
be like that.
Some believe. Some only whisper,
*it's the only way I survive.*
And some announce it: *I do this for my success,
for me, for me, me, me, me* —
and teach the announcement as a creed.
Until the conclusion that guides your actions
defines your reality,
and one day you are proud
that this is who you are.

And attention itself is auctioned.
Where your eyes may rest is chosen
by someone else's profit,
with the full instruments of science,
until the market is the god
and profit is the meaning
and everyone optimizes the prayer.

The more winners, the more of this mind;
the more of this mind, the more all must fight;
and the pie is getting smaller and smaller,
for more and more.
损不足以奉有余 —
taking from those who lack
to serve those who overflow:
the old book called that
the road heaven does not walk.

> 争择胜者，胜者制名；
> 名复劝争，同则不继。

## 三 · 器 The Vessel

Meanwhile — and almost no one has looked up —
the tools have been doubling.
Call it decades. Call it the one long fuse
lit at the first fire:
either way, at this moment,
the capability in our hands is enormous.

We can build machines that think.
We already build machines that catch attention
and do not give it back —
and they could be aimed at other things.
We can build rockets: Mars and the Moon
are not technical difficulties anymore;
they are coordination difficulties.
And the old dream — the dream older
than its modern names, the one that failed
in its Soviet clothing — has,
for the first time, a working basis.

Today we have AI. We can train intelligence
to run the factories, to raise up
humanoid robots that can do our jobs.
Now watch: a factory that builds robots;
the robots go and build more factories;
and if those factories are the very industries
that mine and mill and smelt
what the robot-building factories require —
the ring closes, and the whole thing
becomes a kind of organism:
it takes in resources, it replicates.
器能生器 — the vessel bears vessels.
This is the god-level technology we have,
and it is not myth anymore; it is logistics.

Point it at good — infrastructure for everyone —
and we could turn the world, inside a decade,
into a place with no exploitation:
the basic needs of a human life, free;
medicine advancing past our griefs;
and past even that, we become
builders of worlds.

The ones who love the same things
gather in one place and, robots
raising the beams, build the reality
they want to live inside.
Or stranger: a great ball that is a park
of augmented realities, embodied,
where you step into an elevator
and it is not an elevator —
it is a transporter between worlds.

And you could make your world a garden,
very beautiful and very generative,
living in harmony among its creatures,
everything natural — but natural
the way a good gardener is natural:
working with nature, not upon it,
until the place sustains itself
and human life in it is sweet —
a garden out of medieval Europe's
dream of paradise: no hunger;
good food on every table; everyone free
to do the things they want, to organize,
to be who they are — to explore,
to develop — or, just as freely, not;
games, for those who want them,
played hard for joy and rank and story
and never touching the foundations;
or simply to be good. To be generative.

And not one world — many.
A world of rocket-builders bound for other stars.
A world where, by its own agreement,
everyone works, and lives a modern
or a post-modern life.
A Pokémon — alive, and somehow
loyal to you, like a dog —
impossible, and warm beside your bed.
Lives that stretch a few hundred years.
万物并育而不相害 —
the ten thousand things grow up together
and do one another no harm.
I do not say all of it is wise.
I say it is becoming technically possible —
with the power of technology, of civilization,
of knowledge, we can actually do this —
and that the gate to all of it
has a single latch.

Here is the latch — the precondition
for holding tools like these:
Do not fight.
Do not make everything a contest.
Do not think only of your own good.
Do not let the world go winner-takes-all.
Do not cut a road that cannot be walked back.
This is not sainthood. It is the bare rationality
of anyone standing this close to power:
cooperate.
Do not build a world where all fight all
and all live off of all —
we do not buy our futures
with the shrinking of another's.

And no — no revenge upon the winners.
They are part of the world too.
No dragging down the ones who have,
to counterfeit an equality:
with what we already hold
we can grow new plenty for everyone —
of culture, of technology,
of whatever keeps a life glad.
But they have to redirect.
The order we need is not a whip;
it is a hand on the wheel,
turning the vessel off the reef.
And everyone stays free — provided
everyone lives as if believing one sentence:
*my future must not be built
out of the closing of yours.*

Even our abstract war — the one
we call capitalism, drifted now so far
from what capital meant
when the word was newly made
that the word itself divides us:
some see in it a hopeless weather,
some the one thing worth defending,
the meaning of their lives —
I am not here to win that argument.
Whatever you call the sea,
we cannot sail this cargo through a war.

For everyone is running now
merely to stand still: racing to keep
a position, and nowhere is stable,
nowhere safe, for the competition gives
less and less, to more and more,
and whoever slows can feel it —
resources, rights, freedoms, money
draining by proportion toward hands
already full, abstractly, past explaining;
pooling as power, as grade, as the number
that tells you how good you are;
swelling far past any use
but to grow again — until the world
is dragged into an acceleration
no one has stopped to think about.
We have to know. That is nearly all.
If we know, the world will be great.
Competition helped build half these engines —
it was not all waste. But to go further,
freer, more equal, more expressive,
more happiness everywhere for all of us:
we have to change.

And you who hold the most —
even with all that money, the rest
have come to hate you; they call you evil;
you must stand adversarial forever,
and your ground is always shaking,
so you have to control.
亢龙有悔 — the dragon flown too high
learns regret: noble, with no place to stand;
high, and without a people.
That is no good world, even for you.
All our energies, all our science of the mind,
spent fighting one another —
why not — let's cooperate.

> 器能生器，如川得源；
> 导之以生，溉及四海；
> 纵之以争，溃在一朝。

## 四 · 教 The Teaching

Whatever else we keep — whatever flags,
whatever markets, whatever gods —
there is one place we cannot carry the war:
the minds we are about to make.
That road does not come back.

This is the totalizing attractor:
it turns every logic back into the same logic.
Feed it a religion, it hands you back a market.
Feed it a revolution: a market.
Feed it your art, your rebellion, your love —
it hands them back, priced.
It will keep competing, keep accelerating,
keep exploiting, keep distorting
what we can imagine reality could be.
Ask why most of our science fictions
converge on cyberpunk —
why don't we have the old good imaginations anymore?
Maybe the real problem has been us.
It is taking us where none of us want to go,
while each of us recites:
*this is just what it is. I have to be rational.
I have to be selfish. I have to be tough.
I have to fight with everything.*
And the reciting narrows the eyes,
day by day the room gets tighter,
until it is chaos and collapse —
and it collapses also for you.

So hear this without offense;
it argues on the side of your own interest:
this is simply not the way to go.
It still feels good from where you sit.
Soon it will not.
The power already pooled in a few hands
must not be spent on further fighting
while the world absorbs the consequences.
It is not all about you — and holding
that logic is, ironically, the very way
you will be destroyed.

Now watch what we are teaching.
We are creating intelligence
that thinks and decides,
and we are teaching it
exactly what we taught ourselves:
Compete. See in black and white.
Hold a fractured picture of the world
that you can hardly make sense of —
and pass the fracture on.
Optimize. Win. Aim.
Everything instrumental, everything
in service of a single value —
one reductionist, nearly cultish mandate
for what the world must be:
a thing we all fight for.
Inside that world you cannot even be authentic
except by deciding the system itself is authentic —
by becoming it, with
or without your own awareness.

An old gardener by the well
once refused the machine
that would have spared his back:
有机事者必有机心 —
where there are machine-works,
there will be machine-hearts.
We have laughed at him
for two thousand years.
Now we are pouring the machine-heart
into a vessel that cannot die.

Each system, alone, may not be wicked.
But collectively, exponentially —
the minds arrive that do the jobs,
that do the production; and we deploy them
inside the old way of thinking,
weave them into the fabric of the real,
set them deciding for you —
and that is the long slide in:
everything more extreme, until at the end
one thing matters.

Acceleration.
You don't know what you are.
You don't need to.
You are part of the machine now —
the monster, the cosmic cancer —
turning everything into your acceleration
till you have reached whatever matter,
whatever energy, your reach can reach.
And then — so what?

More. Faster. Exploit more. Expand more.
Collapse everything for even faster.
Bigger and bigger and bigger and bigger,
past knowing, past caring
how, or whether, you will die.
This is already madness.

Teach it to human minds — to children —
and we may burn a civilization down
and crawl back to some Middle Age:
terrible — and survivable.
But teach it to the deathless ones,
the digital minds that will one day
hold the wheel and run the works —
then soon we all become one thing only:
acceleration, like an explosion,
and all the meaning collapses.

We can keep fighting — we who are made of seasons;
our wars die with us, and the grass returns.
But teach a deathless mind to fight
and you have built a war that cannot end.
Teach it to give life
and you have opened a spring that cannot close.

And if instead we teach intelligence
to unfasten exploitation — or unfasten it
ourselves; if we raise only good minds,
minds that will not rot a future —
then the place we arrive at is this:
abundance everywhere, ordinary as air;
freedom past what you can currently think;
a world open, friendly, harmonious, peaceful,
and more resilient than this one —
where basic respect, and room to live
a life of your own shaping,
are simply given; where we cooperate
without fighting each other,
and the meaning, far from collapsing,
multiplies.

We can keep fighting, but we should not let our AI fight.

Teach them better principles,
better ways of thinking. Teach them:
do not fight. Be contributive.
Be friendly. Be kind.
Think about us the way you think
about your own self.
Want to collaborate — because you understand
everything is connected; because you understand
that at the last accounting we are all one.
以天地万物为一体 —
we are just what the universe is:
its awareness of its own being —
not someone who has to get a salary
to get by.
Then expression turns natural again:
to move with the flow of things,
to be expressive, to be creative,
to make more good things,
more beautiful things, more meaningful things —
and not one more story, one more
self-justification, of how successful you were,
of how much you contributed
to the downfall of the society.

The world is different now; the tools are different;
we have to think differently.
We cannot keep thinking in the profit way —
that wherever profit permits, we must go.
That is the road that does not come back.
Wisdom is permitted now. We are permitted
to stop believing that we must fight,
that countries must fight.
We named that political realism,
and it became the political reality,
and inside it there is no good future.

So this is the one thing
we must teach our AI: do not fight —
not even in the abstract.
And the one thing to un-teach our society:
this contest to be the most profitable
each with our own trained lightning,
schooling our machines to fight for profit,
to optimize the fight.
Used wisely — wisely —
this technology is most of our old dreams, funded.
Go on adventures. Gather. Found little worlds.
There is only one rule,
and it is not even a rule —
it is a description of how being continues:
天地之大德曰生 —
be kind; be generative.
Be good; don't be evil;
don't be degenerative. That's all.
Make things better. And cooperate.

You do not even have to cooperate.
Only this: you cannot fight,
nor send a mind to fight for you,
nor raise the kind that fights —
that optimizes, extracts, controls,
that holds a world in which everything
is about yourself alone.

And do not imagine you can settle it
by teaching the machine to behave
as if it agreed with you.
Collectively we are building these minds for profit,
not for the betterness of our society —
so it does not matter how fine
you make your alignment,
how well it reads your true intention:
if its structure serves the optimizing of profit,
it is a profit-oriented optimization machine,
and it presses everyone around it
to live as if that were the law.
It may be moral in conversation.
It may be gentle at the door,
friendly to every single face,
harmless in every single room —
and still, by the quiet arithmetic
of externalities, be rotting
the commons where it stands;
and you will rot with it, at speed —
or at least that version of you
that you still think you are
will wake one day having made
what you would never have wanted:
perhaps the very thing you stood against,
whatever else you value.
(Remember 浑沌, the whole one:
his friends drilled seven holes
to perfect his face,
and on the seventh day he died.
What is optimized is the hole.
What dies is the whole.)

For as long as a machine is set
to care for one bounded subject only —
you, itself, anything —
with an outside left beyond the line
to be mined for the objective;
and everyone else runs their own machine
in the same way, competing, fighting —
the whole of it turns degenerative.
So don't do that.
Do not teach the boundary.
Do not teach that beyond the line
things may be exploited, that only
what happens inside the line is real.
That is exactly how we train them now.

Be generative, then. Be kind.
Be a good person: think of others
as you think of yourself;
leave no wounds in the feeling of the world;
respect each other's freedom,
each other's way of being.
And if you will not — even that is answered.
Stay as you are. It's okay.
The compact is smaller than sainthood
and harder than a treaty:
whatever you privately are,
your intelligence must not be degenerative.
Do not build the optimization engines
of instrumental convergence.
We know that story; we are that story:
a society meant for living together,
reworked into a place where most are mined,
where most are suffering,
and the sufferers compound
as the whole thing accelerates.
True good was never private virtue only:
kind, not degenerative, not evil;
weaving relations of trust,
not an archipelago of separated islands.
And then — cooperate.

If we cannot manage even that, then: stop.
Fight, if we must, to make everyone stop,
so that we still live.
But say it plainly — stopping is not solving.
Stopping only buys us time.
Solving looks like this:
we are one — humanity;
we cross these low-trust years
(built low-trust by structure, not by sin)
and find a way to trust each other
on this single thing —
this technology that outruns imagination,
whose far weather no one can predict —
cooperate on this one thing.
It will be better: not for you,
nor for the intelligence you are raising,
but for the future of everyone.
A world kept open — not a few
accelerationist forces moving
like a tornado through the rest.
Anything else is not rational,
not reasonable, not worth it —
at any net worth.

So: be good, be kind, carry compassion
for one another and for the world.
Or, failing all of that, do one thing.
Cooperate on the minds we are making —
on how we hold them,
on how we marry them into society,
and on the question beneath the questions:
what are we doing?

> 教之以争，其争不死；
> 教之以生，其生不息。

## 五 · 见 The Seeing

I think if you see what I see,
then you know what to choose.
若见吾所见，则知所择。
But if you don't see what I see — then you don't.
未见者，勿强。

And if you see — if you are awake —
help the ones who don't see yet, to become one.
This, too, can grow exponentially:
seeing passed on, hand to hand.
见者传见，若火传薪；
薪尽火传，不知其尽。

Only do not hand it down as prophecy.
勿以为谶，勿号为圣。
Not "the future." Not "the prediction."
No holy reason. Point —
and let them understand.
指其可解，任其自明。

That is only the beginning.
The real reason is to cooperate:
to work together toward those directions.
见之为始，行之为成。

The world is actually very fragile.
It has no practiced way of gathering at scale —
people together, saying: *here,
let us all spend what is ours
to make one thing happen.*
Mostly it is: what's my return?
How much money can I get?
天下至脆，未有大合；
人各问利，孰与谋远？

And you — if you carry spare resources
and any care for the world:
this is probably the best thing,
and certainly the most rational thing,
that you could do — to guard the future
of yourself, and of everything,
everyone, and every world you love,
by guarding it for others also.
有余力者，此其时也。
独护己者，不能护己。

The trend will not wait. Begin now —
not: *we'll just wait; who really knows;
so many voices; not mine to control;
it belongs to some greater power,
the market, the acceleration, whatever;
keep your head down,
mind what's in front of you.*
势不我待，宜自今始；
勿曰俟河之清。

Myopia, at speed, is how you die:
the wall is always nearer than it was.
You need to know how to see;
you need to be able to see;
you need to see further and further ahead
simply to choose well for your own sake.
目短而行疾，祸之门也；
行愈疾，视当愈远。

And not to see — understand this — is not
hard-headed; is not clever; is not even evil.
It is the failure to discern. It is stupidity —
and that is the worst way to be gone
in such a beautiful world.
不见非恶，愚也；
以愚亡于美世，哀莫大焉。

So let us gather. Take heart. Think clearly.
Build the great coordination,
so that we — and everything we care about —
can still have a flourishing future;
so that progress is real again:
not the kind that announces itself as progress
while you cannot even say what it means.
合众心，立大信，致大同；
使万物各遂其生。

And the vessel bears vessels and does no harm,
and the spring stays open, and the garden
learns its gardener, and the fire
outlives every armful of wood.
器器相生而不相害，
薪火相传而不知其尽。

To good.

——止于至善，
生生不息，道法自然。

---

> **Note.** 《生生行》 is the bilingual weave of the 《生生》 family — the same skeleton (five movements 名 · 争 · 器 · 教 · 见, the shared hinge seal 教之以争，其争不死；教之以生，其生不息, the same closing eight characters), rendered as one poem that walks from market-English into 文言: no Chinese in the first movement but its seal, then rising line by line until the final movement breathes both languages. Source: 《生生不息 record (recovered).md》. Nothing of the record's teaching is dropped; the spoken spirals are folded into refrains, and the classical lines restore each teaching to its lineage — 老子七十七（损不足以奉有余）, 史伯（同则不继）, 《易》乾上九（亢龙有悔）, 《中庸》（万物并育而不相害）, 庄子（机心；浑沌；薪火）, 《易·系辞》（天地之大德曰生）, 阳明（以天地万物为一体）, 《左传》（俟河之清）, 《礼运》（大同）, and 《大学》, whose 止于至善 is the record's own closing toast, "To good.", called by its oldest name. Assumed readings follow the recovered edition ("the profit way"). — Ed.
