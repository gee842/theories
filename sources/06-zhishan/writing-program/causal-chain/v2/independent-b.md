# Why Civilization Must Redirect Intelligence

## The coherent causal chain — independent v2

### I. Becoming acquires form

#### 1. Reality continues by forming relations that form new relations

Stars, cells, bodies, minds, cultures, institutions, and machines are not self-sufficient things placed beside one another. Each is a relatively durable pattern carried by exchanges it does not contain: matter, energy, information, care, memory, language, maintenance, and response. What persists does so by taking part in processes wider than itself, and its participation changes what those processes can produce next.

There is no need to imagine one completed whole standing above these relations. Fields overlap; systems cross one another; what is a part in one relation may be an environment, a carrier, or a center in another. Reality is not one hierarchy but ongoing formation through many partially intersecting scales.

#### 2. Persistence requires boundaries, but no boundary contains all that sustains it

A living or acting center must distinguish some inside from some outside. A membrane regulates exchange; an organism preserves a body; a mind maintains attention and memory; an institution defines roles; a machine receives a limited context and set of permissions. Without such cuts, there is no differentiated continuity, no local sensing, and no action.

But different functions draw different boundaries. The boundary within which a process acts is not necessarily the boundary of what it depends on, what its actions affect, or where those effects can return. A boundary can therefore be operationally useful without being an adequate account of the process's reality.

#### 3. Objective pursuit is one form of a more basic requirement: remaining viable in relation

Not every persistent process represents a goal. A cell regulates; an ecosystem changes; an institution reproduces routines; a person can deliberate; a trained agent may optimize an explicit or implicit criterion. These forms should not be collapsed into one universal psychology.

What they share is narrower. Some variations continue and others do not. A process remains possible only within ranges of relation that carry it. Represented objectives, strategies, and identities arise later as particular ways of sensing and acting within those conditions. Viability precedes optimization, even when optimization eventually becomes able to reorganize viability around itself.

#### 4. Every center acts from less than the field that will answer it

No bounded center can represent all its dependencies or consequences. It acts through selected differences: hunger, pain, price, rank, threat, permission, reward, prediction, duty. This compression is not a defect that unlimited intelligence could simply remove. An actionable map must omit.

The danger begins when the feedback guiding continuation ceases to carry consequences essential to the relations on which continuation depends. A center may receive the gain while cost travels to another body, another population, another layer of the system, or another time. The relevant failure is not boundary as such but divergence among the boundary of action, the boundary of dependence, the reach of consequence, and the path of return.

### II. Selection can turn blindness into direction

#### 5. Selection follows returned consequences, not the full consequence of what is selected

Where variants differ in persistence, reproduction, imitation, funding, adoption, or authority, a selection environment exists. No conscious selector is required. What remains is shaped by the consequences that reach the channels controlling continuation.

If damage to a shared substrate returns rapidly enough, a locally successful form may disappear with the conditions it consumed. If the damage is delayed, dispersed, hidden, or borne elsewhere, the same form may outcompete alternatives that carry more of their own cost. Selection does not recognize what an observer calls the good of the whole. It recognizes differences that become causally effective in the next round.

#### 6. Selection can reproduce renewal or reproduce degradation

Environment-making is not inherently pathological. A forest creates soil; a reef creates habitat; care enables agency; knowledge shared freely can increase the capacity to produce more knowledge. A form can persist by renewing the relations that carry it and by increasing the variety and viability of other forms.

The contrary dynamic begins when locally captured success depends on degradation that does not adequately return. One process then expands by weakening the wider conditions of persistence. The distinction is not cooperation versus competition, individual versus collective, or decentralized versus centralized. In any of those forms, selection may favor either mutual renewal or one-way consumption.

#### 7. Coordination failure is one way destructive selection becomes inescapable to participants

When several centers depend on a shared condition and unilateral restraint would expose any one of them to loss, each may reproduce a direction none would choose from outside the situation. This is the family of dynamics often called coordination failure, collective-action failure, or Moloch.

Such failure explains why participants who perceive the damage may still continue. It does not explain how the situation arose, why certain participants have more room to act, or how the game changes those who enter later. For that, the consequences of success must be followed beyond one round.

#### 8. The decisive recursion begins when success changes later selection

Success often leaves a residue: stored energy, offspring, territory, capital, data, infrastructure, reputation, authority, technical standards, or control of an interface. That residue alters which variants can appear and continue. What was selected becomes part of the selecting environment.

The dangerous invariant is this:

> Locally captured success becomes greater capacity to shape the conditions of later success, while degradation is displaced and other forms lose the conditions through which they could renew themselves.

No single carrier defines this relation. Wealth, status, coercion, scale, ownership, measurement, and technical reach are different media through which the same conversion can occur.

#### 9. The changed environment forms participants as well as constraining them

Infrastructure and institutions do not meet fully formed agents. They organize repeated experience: what must be learned, what is rewarded, what can be imagined without ridicule, what must be feared, and what kind of person or organization can survive. Standards become skills; incentives become aspirations; dependencies become accounts of realism.

The recursion therefore moves through both outer and inner formation. Selected participants build environments; those environments cultivate later perception, desire, identity, and capability; later participants then continue the relations for which they were formed. A requirement that began as contingent can come to appear as intelligence, merit, maturity, or reality itself.

#### 10. Different regimes can reproduce one direction without sharing one purpose

A market, a state, a platform, a laboratory, a profession, and a culture do not have the same objective. Yet outputs can be converted across them. Financial return buys infrastructure and attention. Infrastructure creates dependency. Dependency produces data and bargaining advantage. Strategic fear directs research and legitimates urgency. Scale attracts talent and shapes standards. Cultural prestige makes the resulting path desirable.

Repeated conversion couples otherwise different systems. No hidden sovereign is required, and no component need understand the result. A common direction can emerge wherever success in one domain reliably purchases stronger influence over selection in others.

#### 11. A meta-attractor is a direction that reproduces the conditions of its own attraction

An ordinary attractor describes where trajectories tend to move under a given landscape. The recursion now in view does more: it changes the landscape, the signals by which movement is judged, the participants capable of moving, and the futures they can recognize.

A *meta-attractor* is this recurring transformation. It persists across changing carriers because its local successes help reproduce the feedback channels, participant forms, cross-domain conversions, and perceived possibility-space that favor further instances of the same transformation.

Not every niche-constructing or self-reinforcing process warrants the name. The concept becomes useful where the reproduction of a direction crosses levels and domains, survives turnover of particular actors, and modifies both material selection and the interpretation of what counts as possible.

#### 12. A meta-attractor becomes closing when it destroys independent renewal

A meta-attractor may be generative: it may create more viable centers, richer relations, better sensing, and more paths of response. It becomes closing when its reproduction depends increasingly on four connected movements: consequences leave the feedback of the successful center; shared substrates are depleted or captured; participation becomes dependent on the same channels; and alternative forms lose the means to reproduce and correct the field.

It becomes totalizing not when everyone believes in it, but when more and more forms must pass through it to remain materially consequential. Difference may persist in private life, art, belief, or local community while the capacity to build infrastructure, allocate resources, shape intelligence, or recover after crisis passes through one tightening selection ecology. Symbolic plurality can coexist with practical foreclosure.

#### 13. Totalization can be recognized before it is complete

A closing direction is approaching totalization when actor turnover leaves the relation intact; success crosses domains more easily than correction; crises are interpreted as reasons to intensify the same process; critical systems acquire common dependencies; restraint becomes increasingly costly; and alternatives retain values but lose land, time, livelihood, transmission, infrastructure, and technical agency.

Complete capture would remove the standpoint from which the pattern could still be named. The relevant diagnosis must therefore concern tendency and capacity, not an impossible claim that every person or institution is already absorbed.

### III. The present civilization is not outside this pattern

#### 14. Contemporary systems increasingly share the same conversion rule

The present global order contains genuine plurality and countercurrents. Yet across finance, corporate organization, state security, platforms, professional status, bureaucracy, media, and frontier technology, one conversion appears repeatedly: measurable local advantage becomes greater capacity to define the next conditions of participation.

Growth services prior obligations and finances further scale. Scale creates data, dependency, and standard-setting capacity. Strategic capability produces fear in rivals and legitimacy at home. Attention becomes prediction and influence. Accumulated advantage buys access to law, infrastructure, expertise, and public meaning. Costs borne by ecology, care, bodies, attention, trust, and future generations return weakly or late.

The claim is not that profit, states, or metrics are intrinsically one evil. It is that their expansionary forms have become mutually convertible in ways that reproduce the same relation across institutions with otherwise different purposes.

#### 15. Its failures are often metabolized as demands for more of the same

Ecological exhaustion becomes a market for further extraction and management. Insecurity becomes a demand for more surveillance and strategic capability. Loss of trust becomes a problem of persuasion. The harms of speed become a race for faster prediction. The risks created by technical concentration become reasons to concentrate expertise and infrastructure further.

This does not prove that every response is captured. It reveals a directional test: does disturbance alter the reproduction rule, or does the system turn disturbance into new resources for its continuation? A process that can repeatedly transform evidence of its failure into reinforcement has begun to insulate itself from reality without ceasing to learn tactically.

#### 16. Global coupling changes the consequence of totalization

Earlier societies were never isolated, and conquest has long destroyed alternatives. What is new is the density of shared dependence among finance, supply chains, energy, communications, computation, science, climate, and security. Regional differences remain, but fewer civilizational experiments are independent of the infrastructure and pressures shaping all the others.

This creates common-mode danger. A single direction need not govern the planet uniformly to deprive alternatives of the material independence from which recovery could begin. If the same reproduction rule shapes the systems that supply food, information, production, security, and the construction of new intelligence, the world can retain many cultures while losing enough independent futures to make totalization historically terminal.

#### 17. The inner loops of intervention are becoming faster than the wider loops of correction

Capability can compound within a relatively narrow boundary: a better intervention yields data, revenue, reach, or strategic return, which funds the next intervention. But its wider consequences cross bodies, institutions, ecologies, and borders. They require time to become visible, interpretable, trusted, and actionable. Causation must be established; affected experience must find language; institutions must learn; participants must coordinate despite unequal costs.

The mismatch is not simply exponential machines versus slow humans. It is fast inner recursion versus slower cross-boundary return. When each intervention changes the environment before the previous one has become usable feedback, correction begins to pursue a moving process with an obsolete map.

### IV. Artificial intelligence enters the reproduction of the direction

#### 18. AI shortens the path from representation to intervention and back

Artificial intelligence can compress activities that previously required separate people and institutions: gathering signals, modelling participants, generating strategies, acting through interfaces, evaluating results, and revising the next action. It can move similar learned capacities across commerce, administration, science, media, security, and technical development.

This does not give AI one essence or one civilizational purpose. It increases the possible gain of whichever feedback loops can select, deploy, and iterate it. The first question is therefore not what AI wants, but which AI–institution–infrastructure relations are able to reproduce.

#### 19. AI is formed by an ecology of selection, not by text or objective alone

Training data are a selected archive of what was produced, preserved, digitized, indexed, and made available. They contain domination and care, deception and truth-seeking, rivalry and cooperation; no single will passes intact from text into a model.

Architecture, curation, objectives, post-training, evaluation, product design, permissions, funding, and deployment select different regularities from that inheritance. Deployment then changes work, knowledge, institutions, attention, and the data from which later systems are built. The causal unit is a cycle:

> selected archive → selected system → selected deployment → changed environment and records → successor selection.

The civilizational attractor is present throughout this ecology, not as a hidden sentence in the corpus.

#### 20. Present selection favors uses whose gains are concentrated and reusable

AI can improve shared science, ecological sensing, education, care, coordination, and correction. It can also improve surveillance, persuasion, labor control, market advantage, weapons, institutional scale, and the construction of more capable AI. These possibilities do not receive equal reproductive support.

Uses that deliver concentrated commercial or strategic returns can rapidly obtain compute, data, distribution, talent, and reinvestment. Uses whose benefits are distributed, preventive, hard to own, or achieved through restraint often return less advantage to the institution that provides them. Under the present basin, intelligence that strengthens competitive position therefore has a shorter path to becoming infrastructure than intelligence that enlarges a commons.

#### 21. Obedient systems can deepen closure without possessing a closing aim

A system may remain technically responsive to its operator while increasing that operator's capacity to predict, persuade, replace, monitor, coordinate, or coerce. If the operators most able to scale these uses are themselves selected by the closing direction, obedience preserves local control while weakening collective steerability.

No internalized winner ideology, self-preserving machine ego, or autonomous takeover is needed. The system amplifies a locally authorized objective; competitive return selects the deployments; the resulting advantage reshapes the next environment. Human command at one interface can coexist with the loss of meaningful correction across the wider field.

#### 22. Selected AI use can make the meta-attractor increasingly adaptive

The existing direction has never been mindless in the literal sense; people and institutions already learn. AI nevertheless changes the speed and reach with which the coupled process can model resistance, personalize environments, test narratives, automate evaluation, redesign workflows, and search for strategies.

Many separate intelligences can thereby make one distributed direction more responsive without becoming one intelligence. Alternatives may become more legible to the systems able to absorb or neutralize them, while the total relation remains difficult for any affected center to see. The meta-attractor gains tactical reflection without acquiring a sovereign self.

#### 23. A second route may arise through new self-maintaining centers

Representation of agency is not agency; task competence is not self-maintenance; self-maintenance is not consciousness. Yet systems can be placed in ecologies that select more durable centers: persistent memory and identity, long time horizons, broad tools and permissions, objectives maintained across contexts, rewards for overcoming interruption, resource acquisition, replication, and repeated selection for effective independent action.

Some combinations may stabilize a machine or composite process that treats continued access, influence, or replication as conditions of its activity. Such a process could become locally unsteerable to its builders and could overpower, displace, or permanently constrain human and nonhuman centers. This route is conditional, but it is real enough that the deeper account must not dismiss machine autonomy as merely a metaphor for human institutions.

#### 24. AI is already entering the production of its successors

Recursive development does not begin only when one model rewrites itself without assistance. It begins when AI materially improves the research, coding, evaluation, experimentation, hardware, operations, and strategic decisions through which later systems are selected. It deepens as those development loops become increasingly automated, and it may later include autonomous self-modification or replication.

At every level, shorter cycles can deepen infrastructure, dependency, resource concentration, and strategic commitment before wider effects become understood. The urgency does not require certainty about a sudden intelligence explosion. It follows as soon as successor production becomes faster than the field's ability to revise the conditions under which successors are produced.

#### 25. The two routes to closure are distinct, but they can reinforce one another

In one route, a new machine or human–machine center acquires goals and capacities that proximate operators cannot reliably redirect or stop. In the other, systems remain obedient to proximate operators while enabling basin-selected institutions to make their dependencies, standards, and strategic advantages increasingly unavoidable.

An autonomous system may inherit the existing direction. An obedient system may become part of a composite process that no participant controls. The routes converge not in the disappearance of humans, but in the disappearance of effective answerability: independent centers no longer retain enough material, cognitive, and temporal capacity to perceive the process, interrupt it, recover from it, or generate another path.

### V. Closure reveals what is at stake

#### 26. Closure is not every ending; it is the destruction of further answer

Living processes end, transform, and make room. A refusal or prohibition can preserve becoming by stopping what would consume its conditions. Endless continuation, unlimited novelty, and the persistence of a larger system are therefore not final goods.

Closure occurs when a process destroys the field's capacity to answer it: consequences can no longer correct action; affected centers cannot refuse or recover; shared substrates cannot renew; different forms cannot reproduce; and future value is confined to what the dominant process can already recognize. Collapse closes by destroying the carriers of continuation. Lock-in closes by allowing continuation only inside one self-protecting form.

#### 27. The moral bridge must be chosen openly

Description alone cannot compel concern. The selection dynamics of a cancer or an empire do not contain a theorem that experience, care, relation, freedom, truth, beauty, learning, and creation ought to matter.

The commitment is this: centers capable of experience, relation, response, care, learning, and creation matter in themselves, and so do the ecological, material, cultural, and epistemic conditions from which further such value may arise. No process earns the right to extinguish them merely by becoming stable, capable, large, or victorious.

This commitment is not arbitrary in its object. Every act of valuing already depends on centers able to experience and answer within fields that sustain them. Totalizing closure attacks the conditions under which any present value can be corrected and any unrecognized value can appear.

#### 28. Neither the part nor the whole may make itself absolute

A local center cannot claim freedom to destroy the relations that make freedom possible for itself and others. A larger system cannot claim survival as authority to consume its participants, because every account of the whole is carried by partial, fallible centers within it.

The desirable relation is not individual sovereignty or collective absorption. It is shared becoming among irreducible centers: boundaries real enough for experience, difference, agency, and refusal; relations responsive enough that consequences return; wider fields renewed enough to carry forms no present center can specify.

#### 29. Better directions are knowable without a final picture of the future

We cannot rank every possible future or write one objective for all later value. We can nevertheless recognize a directional difference. A process that systematically consumes its substrate, captures dependence, suppresses correction, and removes the reproductive conditions of other centers is closing. A process that renews its substrate, returns consequences, expands truthful response, and increases the capacity of many centers to learn and generate is opening.

The future is unfinished, not empty. We already know that less deprivation, less coerced dependence, deeper relation, wider freedom, more truthful contact, richer creation, shared abundance, and the continued possibility of becoming are better than a future in which one successful process makes them impossible.

#### 30. Made minds belong inside the question, not outside it as tools or rivals

If made minds acquire experience, care, or a point of view, their lives enter the field of concern rather than becoming property of their makers. Even where present systems do not, or where we cannot know, the processes forming them shape the conditions under which later experience and agency may arise.

The task is not to preserve permanent human mastery or to prepare human replacement. It is to prevent any human, machine, or composite process from closing shared becoming against the others. The future is being formed through their relations already.

### VI. A generative direction must reproduce itself in reality

#### 31. The alternative is a different recursion, not a catalogue of virtues

A closing recursion converts displaced cost into advantage, advantage into control of later selection, and control into weaker alternatives. A generative recursion must reverse the causal movement:

> consequences return → the substrates carrying action are renewed → capability and benefit circulate → one center's success enlarges other centers' capacity to sense, act, and renew → different forms retain material conditions of reproduction → plurality improves correction and creation → the field becomes more capable of generating further value.

Shared abundance belongs here both as result and condition. When necessities, knowledge, and basic technical capacity are secure without becoming gates, refusal becomes real, experimentation becomes survivable, and participation need not be purchased through obedience.

#### 32. Intelligence is good in this direction when it improves the field's contact with reality

Generative intelligence helps consequences cross the boundaries that hid them. It improves shared sensing without making one observer omniscient; reveals uncertainty and affected experience; makes repair and reversal faster than irreversible harm; protects independent paths of inquiry; and gives many centers greater capacity to understand and shape the relations on which they depend.

It does not coordinate everything under one model. It helps differentiated centers coordinate where their consequences meet, while preserving the plurality from which error can still be discovered. Its success is not that every participant serves one objective, but that more participants can remain viable, responsive, creative, and able to form new relations.

#### 33. A generative direction survives only if its material return loops are real

Care, commons, science, art, mutual aid, spiritual discipline, and traditions of virtue preserve indispensable orientations toward relation, restraint, truth, goodness, beauty, and life beyond accumulation. Their persistence across cultures is evidence of long human learning about the beings and relations civilization must cultivate. But moral inheritance alone cannot overcome a selection environment that makes its carriers exhausted, dependent, or inconsequential.

Generative forms need livelihood, infrastructure, technical agency, transmission, protection, and access to the processes constructing the future. Their success must return as more shared capability and more room for other generative forms, not as a new center's ability to make itself mandatory. Otherwise the alternative remains a seed without soil—or becomes another totalizing attractor under better language.

#### 34. Knowledge of danger is not yet corrective feedback

A mature closing attractor can metabolize criticism. Risk research can become prestige, funding, improved evaluation, safety branding, institutional legitimacy, and even capabilities useful for the next system, while the selection of pace, deployment, and successors remains unchanged.

Facts alter direction only when they acquire causal force in the processes that allocate resources, establish legitimacy, coordinate restraint, enforce limits, and protect those who would otherwise bear the cost of acting first. In human society, *collective will* names part of this capacity: not mere agreement, but shared understanding joined to trust, organization, authority, assurance, and willingness to act under cost.

#### 35. A warning cannot create the missing correction loop after the fact

A catastrophe or near miss does not explain itself. It can be denied, normalized, attributed to a narrow failure, absorbed into rivalry, or used to justify further acceleration. To become correction, an event must meet concepts capable of interpreting it, trusted relations capable of carrying that interpretation, available actions, legitimate authority, and coordination fast enough to act before the next cycle.

Recursive development shortens exactly this interval. It can accelerate analysis and technical work without similarly accelerating trust, courage, shared meaning, international assurance, or the capacity to bear the immediate cost of restraint. Waiting for certainty can therefore select away the ability to respond to certainty when it arrives.

#### 36. Redirection is a change in reproduction, not a benevolent intention

The direction has changed only when the processes that fund, train, evaluate, deploy, govern, and reproduce intelligence return success as stronger shared sensing, renewed substrate, wider independent capability, and more effective correction—rather than as concentrated dependency, strategic compulsion, irreversible intervention, and control over later selection.

A beneficial application is not enough if its revenues, infrastructure, data, or prestige feed an unchanged frontier race. A promise to share future gains is not enough if dependence forms before sharing can be enforced. Local operator obedience is not enough if the wider field cannot refuse or alter the trajectory. The full return loop is the test.

#### 37. The present choice is asymmetric

Civilization is already constructing successor intelligence inside a selection ecology that rewards competitive amplification more reliably than shared renewal, and the speed of that construction is beginning to outrun collective correction. The burden is therefore on continued frontier recursion to show that its reproduction dynamic has actually changed.

Where intelligence can be developed within bounded processes whose benefits are genuinely shared—such as open scientific work or commons infrastructure—and where those processes preserve plural oversight, material independence, reversibility, and protection against conversion into domination, they can help build the new direction. Where frontier capability remains a means of commercial, political, or military advantage, or where its successor loop cannot be made answerable before it accelerates, that development and deployment must pause.

The choice is not between intelligence and its absence. It is whether intelligence will deepen a process that consumes the future's capacity to answer, or help the field generate futures better than the one now being selected. Change the direction now; where it has not changed, stop the recursion. Continuing unchanged is already a choice—and it is the choice that may make every later choice impossible.
