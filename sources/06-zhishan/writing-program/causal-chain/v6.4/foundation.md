# V6.4 foundation — the v6.2 / v6.3 comparison

Written 2026-07-13 after the author judged v6.2 the better overall read despite v6.3's higher correctness. This file is the basis for v6.4; it is a diagnosis and a method, not a draft.

## The core diagnosis

V6.3 fixed almost every real hole the two six-lane adversarial rounds found. But the fixes are all one kind of sentence: qualifiers, subordinate clauses, and hedges added to block an *imagined hostile citer*. Individually correct; in aggregate they turned main-zh into a second independent-zh.

This is a live recurrence of the discipline set at the v6 stop and recorded in project memory (`zhishan-manifesto-turn`: treat the review as a *map of rebuttals* — make the text dare to speak, not grow longer). The round-two audit reports were executed as an obligation checklist instead of a rebuttal map. The three-draft division of labor already assigns the defensive load: **main-zh is the public architecture and compression boundary; independent-zh carries the full qualification set.** V6.3 back-flooded independent-zh's job into main-zh. That back-flooding is what reads as "dead."

Quantified: +27% length, distributed very unevenly — Section Six went from 6 paragraphs to 9 and absorbed most of the growth. Section Six was already "three treaty-annex paragraphs in a row" in v6.2; v6.3 expanded it into treaty body + annex + protocol. As the *ending of the essay*, where the reader wants to accelerate to the final line, it now delivers a clause-by-clause disclaimer. This is the single largest source of "v6.2 reads better."

## The example: footbinding → ozone

The footbinding paragraph swelled because the *example itself is miscast*, so v6.3 rescued it with qualifiers:

- Chinese-context only → needs cultural annotation;
- it is a **coordination / convention** game (in-group reciprocity self-suffices), while the essay's core threat is the **security-dilemma / arms-race** type → forced the type-tagging patch ("this is a convention…; the bridge in domination and security competition is harder to build");
- the bound daughters were not at the covenant table (the affected are objects) → forced the ethical-limit concession;
- causation is academically contested (Bossen & Gates 2017 vs. Mackie 1996) → forced the "weights are still argued" concession.

**Ozone (the Montreal Protocol) makes three of those four patches vanish and hands back three things footbinding cannot:**

1. **A genuine success of the security-dilemma type.** Every state had an incentive to keep using CFCs (substitutes cost more; if others don't cut, cutting alone loses), yet coordination held and the ozone layer is recovering. This is the same structural class as the essay's core threat — it fills the exact gap both systems-lane rounds flagged (v6.3 fills it with "arms control," which is precisely the audit's own half-success weak example; ozone is a recognized full success).
2. **The Multilateral Fund = a historical proof of "first movers pay first."** Developed countries (most of the historical emissions) funded technology transition in developing countries. This directly instantiates the Section-Six B9 principle ("the first mover who profited most bears restraint's cost first") — so it can let Section Six *spend fewer words*.
3. **Compliance and verification machinery** (trade restrictions on CFCs with non-parties), showing cross-border verification is not pure subjunctive — a partial answer to the national-security realist's "verification doesn't exist."

And its three disanalogies, stated, *strengthen* rather than weaken: CFCs have a clear industrial signature (AI weights can be stolen/hidden/offshored); substitutes were commercially viable (giving up the frontier may mean giving up a decisive advantage); harm was visible with clear consensus (AI harm "may lag detection"). Even ozone — a security dilemma with every favorable condition present — took a decade-plus plus a fund plus trade sanctions to bridge; AI lacks all three favorable conditions, so it is harder and more urgent. The bridge has been built (refuting "impossible"); the conditions that built it are absent for AI (hence "build the conditions now" = the essay's conclusion). Footbinding cannot do this double duty because it is not even the security-dilemma type.

Net: **the ozone paragraph can be shorter than the footbinding paragraph while carrying more** — the model for the whole v6.4 pass.

### Ozone's honest weakness

Section Four's argumentative spine is "the intrinsic demands of moral traditions (ren/yi/li/zhi, compassion, nonduality) pass through a material bridge to become causal force." Ozone is driven by **scientific recognition + prudent interest coordination**, not a moral tradition's power of refusal. Footbinding is at least a "moral recognition (binding is wrong) → action" shape, matching the section's title work. Ozone proves "the security-dilemma bridge can be built" but not "moral recognition can become causal force." Real mismatch.

**Author ruling 2026-07-13: straight swap to ozone.** Absorb the mismatch and seam it with one line — scientific recognition is also a form of *seeing consequence*, kin to moral recognition, both an inner direction crossing a material bridge; the section then carries two complementary shapes of that crossing (moral recognition in the ren/yi paragraph, consequence-recognition in the ozone bridge) rather than one. Do NOT re-add a second historical case (that was the rejected two-example route; it re-thickens against the breathe-again goal). The seam sentence is the whole cost of the swap; keep it to one line.

## Example evaluation framework (for any candidate)

1. **Game structure isomorphic** — security-dilemma/arms-race (the core threat), not convention/coordination (footbinding's weakness).
2. **Universally readable** — no cultural annotation needed.
3. **Mechanism complete** — recognition → material bridge (changes incentive/permission/return) → the selector actually changed.
4. **The affected are at the table** — not "those who can covenant switch the track on behalf of the absent."
5. **Honesty of the success** — real or partial; how large is the attack surface.
6. **Proximity to the thesis** — distance from "convert advantage into successor-selection power / give up relative advantage."

| Candidate | 1 struct | 2 universal | 3 mechanism | 4 at table | 5 honest | 6 proximity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Footbinding | ✗ convention | ✗ Chinese-only | ✓ | ✗ daughters absent | ~ contested cause | ~ |
| **Ozone / Montreal** | ✓ security dilemma | ✓ | ✓✓ fund + sanctions | ~ developing states funded in | ✓✓ recognized success | ✓ |
| Arms control (NPT/BWC) | ✓✓ purest | ✓ | ✓ but ✗ enforcement | — | ~ mixed (BWC no verification) | ✓ |
| Smallpox eradication | △ pure public good | ✓ | ✓✓ | — | ✓✓ | ✗ not restraint/advantage |
| Slave-trade abolition | ~ British sea-power imposition | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ compensated owners | ~ ethical stain | ~ |

Ozone dominates footbinding on four of six axes; its only losses are the moral-tradition mismatch (Section-Four spine) and a residual "at the table" gap (future generations, ecosystems).

## The v6.4 method

For every v6.3 repair, ask: **who falls into this hole — a real reader, or an imagined hostile citer?**

- Real-reader hole → keep it in main-zh, in fewer words.
- Hostile-citer-only hole → move it to independent-zh (which exists for exactly this and has the room), trust the reader.

main-zh breathes again; independent-zh wears the armor. This is the standing three-draft division, not a new decision. Ozone is the first application: the right example deletes patches, restores breath, and strengthens the argument at once.

### Targets, in priority order

1. **Section Six give-back** — the biggest lever. Keep the load-bearing turns (generativity as anti-closure constraint; the honest "adjudication process does not yet fully exist"; restraint must answer the same direction; the closing four-beat) in main-zh; move the three-condition adjudication detail, the executor-indexing clause, the first-payer-verification clause, the freeze-review clause to independent-zh. The ending must be able to be read aloud.
2. **Section Four example swap** — footbinding → ozone (route per author ruling); the swap alone recovers most of Section Four's breath and lets B9 shrink.
3. **Section Three rhythm** — keep the meta-attractor definition (author ruling) but break the six-thing paragraph so the abstract valley has air.
4. **Section Five compression** — keep the controllability-inversion keystone; shorten the METR long sentence and the recallability counter-side.
5. Leave in main-zh only the qualifiers a real reader needs to not misread; everything the hostile citer needs goes to independent-zh with a one-line pointer.

Length target: recover most of the +27%, back toward v6.2's footprint, without losing the two author rulings or the recovered keystone.
