# V6.1 revision contract

## Decision

The external review confirms the v6 stopping decision but identifies one local logic error, one leaked editorial instruction, and several bounded consolidation tasks in `v6/main-zh.md`. V6.1 repairs those problems in a new file rather than altering the accepted v6 artifacts.

## Required repairs

1. **Remove the false binary in the criticism/absorption test.** Criticism can be absorbed, inert, partially corrective, or selector-changing. It is absorbed when it improves the dominant loop while deepening alternatives' dependence; it becomes effective counter-selection only when it reduces forced dependence or changes permission, return paths, independent correction, infrastructure, or successor production.
2. **Remove the leaked instruction to the writer.** Preservation, reproduction, and selector change must be expressed as a distinction in the world, not as “正文不能……” guidance.
3. **Repair the awkward contemporary-diagnosis sentence.** The loops possess conditions that convert present advantage into later selection influence; “conditions” do not themselves “表现出” decline.

## Public-text consolidation

- Replace avoidable English terms in the Chinese text using the already-tested vocabulary of `v6/independent-zh.md`.
- Keep `main-zh.md`'s six movements and approximate length rather than importing the full independent draft.
- Let current evidence enter through claims and examples, not a report-style sentence followed by bare links.
- Preserve the conditionality of the struggle loop, the non-compensability test for independent correction, and the distinction among five levels of recursive development.
- State explicitly that withdrawal of expansion's default legitimacy does not automatically authorize an existing sovereign.
- Keep the Chinese title's conditional openness: civilization is selecting the future; the argument must show when that selection is narrowing it.
- End on the thesis that intelligence must change direction so the future remains able to answer.

## Out of scope

- no new theory family;
- no new three-draft generation cycle;
- no universal numerical threshold for pause;
- no complete international enforcement design;
- no claim that a global meta-attractor, aggregate AI acceleration, autonomous recursive self-improvement, or a present made-mind subject has already been proved;
- no silent modification of the v6 source and control texts.

## Acceptance test

V6.1 succeeds if the two sentence-level defects are gone, every compression preserves the v6 conditional chain, no editorial language remains in the public prose, and the Chinese candidate can be read without unexplained English technical vocabulary.
