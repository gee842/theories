## Author rulings

1. **元吸引过程 stays, fully defined.** The name is retained against the reader-lane deletion advice, and the thin definition is replaced by a complete one: ordinary attractor vs. landscape-rewriting movement; "被选择的，开始选择选择者"; explicitly not a hidden subject; explicitly direction-neutral (a commons can self-reinforce by producing more independent participants); explicitly falsifiable; explicitly tied to 总体化 as its malignant direction only under the non-compensability condition. This simultaneously discharges logic-r1 F12 (direction-neutral definition), systems-r2 F18 (commons guard at the definition site), and the reader lanes' temperature complaint about "可失败的跨领域耦合假说".
2a. **Opening rewritten for possibility space and collective irrationality (ruling three, same day).** "越来越难以走向真正不同的未来" was judged too vague; a bare "自取灭亡" framing was rejected as violating the text's own tense discipline and covering only one of totalization's two faces. The opening now carries the two elements the author named: the narrowing of possible futures ("未来却越来越窄" — incidentally aligning the Chinese opening with the English title's "narrowing") and the collective-irrationality scandal, phrased with the incentive/interest split made explicit: no one chose the direction, almost no one — not even its biggest winner — would choose it if choosing together were possible ("赢家想要的是赢，不是赢完之后的那个世界"), yet every step extending it has its own reasons from where each actor stands ("站在各自的位置上，都有它的道理"). The 道理 wording replaces an earlier 算得过账 draft (register clash) and threads the rationality motif from the opening through Section One's title to Section Two's "局部胜出的策略拿走了理性的名字".

2b. **Section One reframed from 克制 to 理性.** New title "理性为什么会成为劣势"; new thesis paragraph: options reasonable and necessary at the global scale (maintenance, verification, fidelity, time for correction, conditions for successors) are selected out at the local scale — not because unseen, but because those who see them cannot carry their cost; restores the control-stone sentence "把更多现实纳入考量的行动者，可能最先被淘汰"; 克制 remains the sharpest instance, so the section's two paths, the coupling loop, and Section Six's restraint argument are unchanged. Section Two's formation paragraph now closes the irony: "全局需要的理性在局部出局，局部胜出的策略拿走了理性的名字。" The causal chain is generalized, not altered: cost displacement and relative vulnerability still do the mechanical work.
