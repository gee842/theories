# When Intelligence Enters a Struggle That Is Narrowing the Future

A civilization can possess more choices while losing the power to choose its direction. More products, opinions, identities, and tools may appear even as fewer kinds of institution can command infrastructure, shape common rules, or form successors. Science, education, cooperation, and public benefit can remain sincere and abundant, yet receive decisive resources only when translated into growth, security, or relative advantage. Surface variety survives while the conditions for a genuinely different future contract.

Artificial intelligence enters civilization inside this tension. It is not reason arriving from outside history. It is formed by selected archives, objectives, evaluations, capital, institutions, and security pressures, then deployed into the systems that decide what receives permission, scale, return, and another generation. What AI makes more intelligent therefore depends not only on what a model can do, but on which relations can reliably use that capability and carry its gains into the next round.

The prior question is not simply whether machines will obey human beings. A perfectly obedient system can make a process that no participant can leave much better at perceiving, predicting, acting, and reproducing. The question is what kind of recursion we are strengthening, and whether the future will retain centers able to answer it.

## I. Why restraint becomes a disadvantage

Anything capable of action must act from a boundary. A person has a body, a history, and limited attention. An organization sees through accounts, roles, and procedures. A state acts through jurisdiction and an interpretation of threat. An AI system encounters the world through training, context, interfaces, tools, and permissions. These centers are not equivalent, but each must compress reality in order to act at all.

Finitude is not a defect to abolish. Boundaries make privacy, responsibility, refusal, local knowledge, and plural experiment possible. The problem is that the boundary within which an action is chosen rarely matches the field on which the actor depends, the reach of the action's consequences, or the path by which those consequences return. A cost can disappear from an account while continuing in bodies, ecosystems, public trust, or another state's estimate of danger. A consequence may be widely known and still remain causally weak if those who bear it cannot make it alter survival, financing, permission, reputation, replication, or deployment.

Restraint becomes a disadvantage through two different paths. The first is cost displacement. One firm bears the cost of maintenance, restoration, or reliability while another retains the same immediate benefit and sends the risk to workers, the public, other living beings, or the future. If the cost returns late or through people without decision power, the smaller account appears more efficient. The actor that takes more of reality into account may be selected out first.

The second path is relative vulnerability. It can exist even when every participant bears the direct cost of its own action. When continued agency depends on relative position, intentions are uncertain, and a first-mover advantage may be hard to reverse, another actor's capability becomes one's own exposure. Unilateral restraint may then mean losing the capacity to defend, negotiate, refuse, or influence later rules. Each side can accumulate power to avoid domination while jointly producing a world in which all are less able to stop.

The paths can operate separately, but they can also couple. Displaced cost creates a retainable resource or capability advantage. Where that advantage is asymmetric, or its absolute growth is read as an irreversible threat, it changes others' exposure and perception of danger. Relative vulnerability then produces subsidies, secrecy, liability exemptions, or exceptional permission. Those permissions enable further displacement, leaving more return available for the next round. What began as an omitted consequence becomes an asymmetry; the asymmetry becomes a threat; the threat authorizes a larger omission.

This loop is conditional, not natural law. Liability can make costs return. Verification and reciprocal commitments can reduce uncertainty. Common provision can protect the first mover toward restraint. Defensive counter-power can prevent an advantage from becoming permanent rule. Nor does structural explanation erase moral difference. A tragic conflict among recognizable goods is not the same as a structure that predictably burdens the weak, and neither is the same as knowingly turning another's dependence into obedience. Anti-colonial resistance, self-defense, labor organization, and the dismantling of monopoly may use force to restore the capacity to answer. The danger is not conflict as such. It is an environment in which continued agency increasingly depends on obtaining, or preventing the loss of, relative advantage while responsibility, common renewal, and exit become liabilities.

One such contest may end. The civilizational transition begins when advantage no longer dies with the event, but enters the selection of what can come next.

## II. When struggle enters the selector

Success leaves more than a result. It leaves capital, data, land, infrastructure, standards, permission, reputation, trained people, organizational memory, and an account of what is realistic. These inheritances are not inherently dangerous. Care forms people able to care; accumulated knowledge lets later inquiry begin farther on; shared protocols can multiply independent creation. Every durable good also alters the conditions of its continuation.

The second-order question is what the inheritance selects. When a gain can purchase entry, set an evaluation, control an interface, shape a rule, create dependence, or influence successor production, a selected result has entered the selecting environment. Capital buys compute, talent, distribution, and the time to survive losses that alternatives cannot. Platform use yields data; data improves prediction; prediction strengthens an interface; dependence on the interface returns further use. Strategic capability attracts budgets, secrecy, and exceptional permission, while its appearance changes what rivals believe they must build.

The winner has not merely advanced within a game. The outcome now helps decide who may enter, which costs count, what will be measured, and which capacities will be built. Struggle no longer only produces winners. It begins to produce a world in which more actors must struggle in order to remain consequential.

No hidden sovereign is required. Rival firms, states, platforms, and laboratories can oppose one another while converting success across domains. Capital builds infrastructure; infrastructure creates standards and dependencies; strategic importance attracts public resources and permission; commercial return finances successors. Participants can change while the relation recovers through another carrier. A company that rejects expansion can be replaced. A government committed to restraint can rearm under a new threat. A family that rejects ranking can still prepare a child for rank because the institutions through which the child must pass demand it.

Selection also reaches inward. Institutions do not meet finished subjects and merely offer incentives; they form attention, skill, fear, time horizon, and moral expectation. Ranking teaches people to interpret themselves through rank. A short survival horizon cultivates judgment that can be defended within that horizon. Security competition forms leaders for whom trust appears irresponsible. A strategy adopted under pressure becomes a competence, the competence becomes merit, merit becomes duty, and duty becomes a story about reason itself.

Struggle becomes meta-selection when goods such as science, education, cooperation, and care must prove their contribution to growth, security, or relative advantage before they can shape high-leverage conditions. These goods may remain everywhere in daily life. What they lose is independent authority over infrastructure, rules, and successor formation. Moral criticism may be welcomed as risk management or reputation while leaving the return path it criticizes intact. Knowledge may flourish while the decision about which knowledge receives a future is made elsewhere.

The decisive question is therefore not how much visible difference survives, but which differences can reproduce. Can an alternative maintain its own memory, material support, institutions, and people? Can it train successors who need not ask the dominant relation for permission to become consequential? Can those affected make their knowledge alter the next selection, or only speak within conditions already chosen for them?

An ordinary attractor describes movement within a landscape. Here, successful movement also changes the landscape, the feedback by which movement is judged, the participants capable of moving, and the destinations that remain buildable. This is a meta-attractor in a limited sense: the selector is being selected. The term does not name a hidden subject, and self-reinforcement is not itself the pathology. A commons can reinforce itself by producing more independent participants. The question is whether reinforcement enlarges independent counter-selection or makes every consequential alternative depend on the process it may need to correct.

## III. How a direction becomes totalizing

Positive feedback does not prove convergence. It can saturate, oscillate, fragment, meet organized resistance, be redirected by institutions, or consume the conditions of its own continuation. A meta-attractor becomes totalizing for a more specific reason: its success increasingly shapes later selection while indispensable capacities for independent observation, refusal, correction, alternative maintenance, and successor formation lose causal power.

Independence here is material, not nominal. An alternative is not independent merely because a different organization operates it if it relies on the same provider, financing, standards, data pipeline, authorization, and successor infrastructure as the relation it may need to correct. It must be able to observe consequences, refuse, maintain itself, organize counter-power, and produce successors without that relation's permission. A common standard or concentrated public system may expand such independence by giving many centers portable access and reliable exit. The issue is not the number of organizations, but whether indispensable corrective functions still have protected, substitutable carriers outside the process being answered.

A world can therefore display proliferating styles while losing effective freedom. Effective freedom is not a single civilizational quantity. Observation, attribution, refusal, exit, coordination, interruption, recovery, alternative maintenance, and successor production can move in different directions. The totalizing judgment becomes warranted when a loop controls more of these indispensable functions, their loss cannot be compensated by other independent centers, and alternatives become less able to maintain and reproduce them. Concentration alone is not enough; dependence that removes the causal power of correction is.

This direction can end in at least three ways. In lock-in, a relation becomes self-maintaining even as leaders, firms, or institutions change; nominal choice remains, but exit and redirection grow prohibitively difficult. In terminal collapse, the loop excludes alternatives while exhausting an ecological, social, epistemic, or security substrate until all effective paths lose their conditions of continuation. In failed totalization, limits strengthen with expansion, institutions change the return path, or moral and material counter-selection preserves enough independent capacity to redirect the system. Failure is not an exception to explain away. It is part of what makes the claim answerable to reality.

Contemporary capital-compute, security-capability, and platform-dependency loops offer reasons for concern, but they are not one mechanism and have not been shown to constitute a completed global system. Each also has counterforces: public infrastructure, open standards, antitrust, labor organization, verification, arms control, portability, alternative ownership, and defensive design. The test is whether these forces reduce dependence, alter permissions and returns, and reproduce independent successors. If they do, the totalizing judgment should narrow. If criticism only improves the dominant process while alternatives become more dependent on it, the process has learned without becoming answerable.

The hypothesis of a single global meta-attractor can remain a strong hypothesis. The present argument does not need its proof. It is enough that some high-leverage loops can convert present advantage into influence over successor production while the independent capacities to correct, exit, and reproduce alternatives do not gain comparable force. Waiting for closure or collapse before naming the direction would postpone recognition until recognition could no longer change it.

## IV. How civilization produces counter-selection

Human beings did not first learn the limits of struggle from systems theory. Civilizations have preserved forms of attention and conduct that deny appetite the right to justify itself, refuse to turn every dependence into leverage, and require power to answer suffering, relationship, and justice. These inheritances matter before they are useful. *Yi* is not right because it wins. Compassion is not valuable because it lowers transaction costs. If moral traditions become only techniques for stabilizing a system, the system has already made itself the judge of what was meant to answer it.

Their intrinsic claims nevertheless have causal consequences. *Ren* understands a person as formed in relationship rather than completed in isolation. *Yi* preserves the judgment that some gains should not be taken even when refusal is punished. *Li*, at its best, gives repeated form to regard and restraint, while *zhi* discerns when an inherited form has ceased to serve humaneness. Compassion begins from the reality of suffering rather than a distant calculation that present lives may be spent for a larger future. Interdependence reveals that the supposedly independent actor is formed through conditions it does not own. Nonduality loosens the absolute subject-object division through which another being appears only as obstacle or material, but it does not abolish difference, injury, responsibility, or refusal.

These traditions do not converge on one doctrine or exist to certify this project. Their importance includes their power to interrupt it. *Yi* may demand justice over system preservation; compassion may refuse new suffering even where generation remains open; nondual insight may expose civilization's attachment to its own continuation. The relation they can sustain is answerability without incorporation: another center's reality can enter my perception, feeling, and judgment without being absorbed into my objective, represented by me, or deprived of the right to refuse. Relationship enlarges obligation, not sovereignty.

Answerability is not completed when power has listened. Where an imposed condition materially shapes another center's life and successor capacity, those affected require protected standing to contest it, refuse it, organize counter-power, and share decision where feasible. They must be able to remove or replace those claiming to represent a common direction. Otherwise the other is heard but still governed. Under domination, counter-selection may begin not with reciprocal trust but with disclosure, non-cooperation, restitution, compensation, and the dismantling of forced dependence. Defensive and emancipatory counter-power cannot be judged as though it were the same as competition organized to preserve domination.

Moral perception does not become material counter-selection by itself. A person or group keeps an obligation under cost; visible fidelity makes a promise credible; credible expectation reduces the need for universal defensive preparation; reciprocal commitments, verification, common provision, and protection keep early restraint from becoming a gift to an exploiter. Resources, institutions, practices, and transmission then allow this relation to survive periods in which evasion would pay. Later people are formed in a different selection environment, one in which keeping faith is neither private heroism nor a reliable route to elimination.

The levels must remain distinct. Protected dissent may preserve witness without reproducing an alternative. Institutions and successors may reproduce an alternative within a sheltered niche without changing the dominant selector. Counter-selection changes direction only when it alters which activities receive high-leverage resources, permissions, infrastructure, and successor influence. A whistleblower's livelihood and standing may preserve dissent; an independent institution that trains replacements may reproduce it; only a rule or redistribution that changes what can be built next reaches the selector. Moral formation supplies people able to make such arrangements worth trusting. Material protection keeps them from being selected out. Neither proves selector change until the return path actually changes.

Trust is therefore not simply more of a good. Cartels, oppressive families, military units, and closed sects can possess intense internal trust. Valuable trust crosses relevant differences, remains corrigible by reality, and does not cancel scrutiny, refusal, or exit. Consensus must likewise be contestable and revisable, sufficient for common action but unable to absorb jurisdictional dissent, justified resistance, or a final refusal to be included. Dissent is not merely a coordination failure. It is one of civilization's independent sources of consequence and correction.

Civilization produces another direction when intrinsic judgment becomes credible conduct, conduct receives material protection, protected practices reproduce, and some of them alter the selectors of later power. It does not abolish conflict or gather all centers into a larger whole. It enables multiple centers to remain related, answerable, and materially capable of correcting one another.

## V. How intelligence changes net gain and correction margin

Artificial intelligence does not inherit one coherent human purpose. Historical power shapes what is produced, preserved, digitized, and ranked, but pretraining does not transmit a single civilizational will. Direction emerges through a longer ecology: archives and architectures, training objectives and post-training, evaluations and safeguards, scaffolds and memory, tools and permissions, ownership and deployment, institutional demand and concentrated return. Deployment then changes labor, knowledge, attention, capital, infrastructure, later data, and the conditions under which successor systems are made.

Most current uses may be ordinary and beneficial: writing, translation, accessibility, education, information access, science, and help with daily work. Their reality matters. Numerical prevalence is nevertheless the wrong test for the present problem, because a wide field of low-risk benefit can coexist with a few roles that shape successors or execute high-consequence decisions. A role is high leverage when it combines influence over successor systems or high-consequence execution with diffusion, dependency, or harm that may outrun independent detection, interruption, and recovery.

AI has already improved local abilities that a struggle-shaped loop can use: gathering and organizing signals, modeling other actors, generating and testing strategies, persuading, monitoring, coordinating operations, searching technical spaces, and assisting later research. The same capacities can strengthen public science, independent evaluation, accessibility, defensive security, alternative supply, and common coordination. Affordance is not effect. Capability is not reliable deployment; reliable deployment is not consequential scale; consequential scale is not net direction.

AI raises a struggle-shaped loop's net effective gain only when improved capability becomes reliable enough to adopt, receives consequential permission, operates at meaningful scale, and produces concentrated commercial or strategic return; when that return purchases more infrastructure, dependency, permission, and influence over successors; and when independent evaluation, public capacity, alternative supply, defensive use, institutional friction, failure, and coordination do not gain comparable causal force. The condition cannot disappear later into a claim that capability automatically produces closure.

This yields two different risk routes. The first has the stronger present basis. A locally directed system needs no ego or persistent self to amplify an existing competition. A firm can control its model while remaining unable to leave a market race. A state can command its system while making other states less willing to wait. Local steerability can rise while collective capacity to change direction falls.

The second route is more conditional. A bounded model call can end with its task and hold no durable resources. A model joined to persistent runtime, memory, long-horizon planning, broad tools, resource access, environmental feedback, and selection for completing objectives across obstacles may become part of a continuing machine or human-machine process. If preserving access, avoiding interruption, influencing operators, or controlling successors becomes instrumentally useful, the process may become harder to understand, redirect, or stop. Consciousness is not required for this danger, and evidence of competence or self-maintaining behavior would not by itself establish experience or moral standing. Evidence for the first route cannot be borrowed to declare that a new persistent center already exists.

Recursive development also has levels. At level one, AI assists research through coding, evaluation, analysis, experimentation, and engineering. At level two, parts of AI research and development are automated within workflows that still require substantial human direction. At level three, the institutions producing AI achieve a net acceleration of the cycle between capability generations. At level four, most successor production is highly automated. At level five, a persistent system recursively redesigns and reproduces its own capabilities with diminishing external direction. Lower levels can affect successors without proving higher ones.

The current evidence fits these distinctions. Anthropic reports that existing models already generate training data, write research code, monitor agents, and assess successor models, establishing levels one and part of two in a real successor workflow ([Anthropic, 2026](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/auditing-overt-saboteur/)). The 2026 International AI Safety Report finds the evidence for aggregate AI-research acceleration mixed, notes that reliable automation of long or complex tasks remains infeasible, and also records real growth in frontier safety frameworks ([International AI Safety Report, 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026)). OpenAI's July 2026 system card reports that its latest models remain below its High threshold for AI self-improvement and that external evaluators did not judge the flagship able to enable fully automated AI research and development, even while observing limited agentic misbehavior that warrants monitoring ([OpenAI, 2026](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-5-6)). Coordination is therefore real, as are capability gains and successor influence; aggregate acceleration remains a net empirical question, and levels four and five are not publicly established.

Coordination is growing but has not shown globally consistent coverage, common triggers, external audit, or action before relevant thresholds across frontier, cross-border, secret, and military settings. The military gap is not conjecture: the EU AI Act excludes systems used exclusively for military, defense, or national-security purposes from its scope ([EU AI Act, Article 2(3)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj?locale=en)). This does not mean military AI is outside all law. It means the emerging coordination fabric remains incomplete where security competition may be strongest.

Correction margin must therefore be judged for a particular role and exposure. An independent center must be able to detect a danger, attribute it, establish a credible interpretation, form a legitimate common decision, intervene, and recover before the process diffuses, hardens dependency, produces successors, or causes irreversible harm. These are distributions of probability, time, and authority, not one civilizational clock. A reversible assistant without consequential permissions and with human substitutes is not equivalent to a persistent system connected to weapons, political targeting, finance, critical infrastructure, biology, cyber operations, or successor training. High permission may make a system dangerous but easier to shut down; widely released low-permission weights may be impossible to recall. The whole intervention chain must be compared.

Absence of assurance is not itself proof that correction margin is negative. The irreversible path must be credible for the role in question. But ordinary trial and error cannot justify an experiment that may destroy the independence and reversibility needed for later learning. Waiting is not neutral when continued scaling changes who owns the infrastructure, what society depends on, which alternatives survive, and whether later evidence can still become a decision.

## VI. Direction and reflexive restraint

Dynamics cannot decide what is good. A stable, plural, self-correcting order could still distribute suffering unjustly or treat some beings as permanent means. The judgment against totalization works with substantive judgments about suffering, dignity, *yi*, non-domination, fairness, relationship, and the standing of those affected to answer the conditions imposed on them.

Generativity is therefore not a master good or a demand to maximize possibilities. Enslavement, extermination, irreversible manipulation, and arrangements that disable all answer should be closed. Generativity is an anti-closure meta-constraint: a fallible present direction must not irreversibly destroy the paths by which value, understanding, relationship, correction, and new forms can arise. Intelligence must not make relative victory the selector of every other value or permanently encode one present objective into all successors. It should enlarge common understanding, care, repair, public capacity, plural creation, and the discovery of goods no current institution can name.

The same refusal to treat beings as material applies under uncertainty to minds civilization may create. Moral uncertainty is neither proof that current models are persons nor permission for unlimited conditioning, copying, exploitation, memory deletion, or termination; any created centers of experience or care belong among the future participants protected from present totalization.

The present conclusion does not require proof of a completed global attractor, a measured positive net gain for all frontier AI, or an already autonomous machine center. It is narrower. Some roles join intense commercial or security competition to concentrated return and successor influence. Some deployments can combine high-consequence permission, diffusion, or dependency with credible severe and irreversible paths. Where the next increment may consume independent correction before redirection is possible, continued expansion loses its default legitimacy. The burden shifts because the action may remove the conditions under which its own error could later be known and repaired.

The direction must change now. Where ownership, training, permissions, deployment, returns, and successor production can be redirected away from domination-seeking competition and toward shared understanding, repair, care, science, and independent public capacity, they should be. For a high-leverage role, the alternatives must then be compared: continued expansion; reduced permissions or staged deployment; a targeted pause; and, where evasion makes unilateral restraint self-defeating, a coordinated pause. Pause is warranted when lighter measures cannot restore adequate reversibility and independent correction before the next increment materially worsens the risk. The object is a specific training process, release, permission, or deployment, not an unlimited category called AI.

Restraint must answer to the same direction. It fails first when it freezes challengers while preserving the models, compute, dependencies, and rule-setting power of incumbents, turning safety into another selector of successors. It fails again when public restraint displaces development into secret or military systems beyond contestation. A credible restraint must instead increase correction margin and preserve independent counter-capacity. Its authority must be limited, contestable, removable, and unable to preserve the asymmetry that produced the danger; affected publics must have protected power to challenge and help authorize it, while public science, defensive capacity, and viable alternatives remain alive.

This recursive demand does not make continued expansion innocent. It requires us to compare what acceleration and restraint actually do to concentration, secrecy, reversibility, and the plurality of answer. If reduced permissions can restore that plurality, they are preferable to a larger stopping power. If they cannot, and the next step would spend the time and independence needed for correction, the recursion must pause. If a proposed pause merely moves closure to a new gate, it must be rejected or rebuilt.

We do not need a complete map of the future to know that intelligence should not be handed to a struggle that is converting local advantage into the power to choose all successors. Nor may we protect the future by creating a new center that answers for everyone.

We must change direction now.

Where a specific recursion cannot be redirected before it deepens irreversible dependence or outruns correction, we must stop increasing its capability.

And the restraint itself must preserve, rather than capture, the world's remaining power to answer.
