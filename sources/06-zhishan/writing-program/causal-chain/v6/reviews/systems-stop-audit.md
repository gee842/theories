# Causal-chain v6 — systems stopping audit

## Scope and stopping verdict

I reviewed `plan.md`, all three v6 drafts, and the v5 consolidation and systems reviews. I did not read any other v6 review. This audit applies the v6 stopping standard: another causal-chain version is justified only by a remaining load-bearing category error, scale jump, hidden normative bridge, evidence-level collapse, or self-defeating restraint principle.

**Verdict: PASS. Stop the causal-chain version cycle at v6.**

There is no remaining load-bearing failure in the three-draft set. All seven requested bridges survive in explicit conditional form. The drafts do not prove that a single global meta-attractor already exists, that AI's aggregate net effect is presently totalizing, or that any general pause regime will work. They no longer need those claims. The action conclusion is carried by a narrower role-level chain: a specific high-leverage recursion, a credible severe and irreversible path, inadequate correction before the next increment, comparison with lighter measures, and a restraint design that itself increases rather than captures counter-capacity.

The exact best base is **`independent-en.md`**. It is the only draft that states, in one continuous argument, that effective freedom is not a scalar, that corrective functions may move in different directions, that losses must be non-compensable by other independent centers, that AI net gain requires a bidirectional comparison, that correction margin is a distribution of detection, decision, intervention, and recovery, and that pause must win a comparison against lighter measures without becoming a successor gate. `independent-zh.md` is the best Chinese source for moral and material depth; `main-zh.md` is the best public compression and cadence. Neither is as safe as the systems base without importing the English draft's control clauses.

### 1. Totalization and effective freedom

The defining repair holds. Totalization is not concentration, dependence, positive feedback, or convergence by itself. It is increasing control over later selection together with loss of indispensable independent observation, refusal, correction, maintenance, counter-power, and successor formation. All drafts allow saturation, oscillation, fragmentation, institutional redirection, collapse, and failed totalization.

The strongest formulation is in `independent-en.md`: the relevant freedoms are explicitly multidimensional, and a totalizing judgment requires that the controlled functions be indispensable, not compensable by other independent centers, and progressively harder for alternatives to maintain and reproduce. This handles the strongest counterexample: a concentrated public infrastructure or common standard may reduce provider count while increasing portable access, exit, repair, and independent creation. Under the v6 definition, that case need not count as totalization.

### 2. Coupling and selector logic

The earlier missing intermediary is present. Displaced cost creates retainable resources or capability; when the increment is asymmetric, or absolute growth is perceived as an irreversible threat, it changes exposure and threat assessment; vulnerability then generates subsidy, secrecy, exemption, or exceptional permission; those permissions enable further displacement. The drafts also allow the two mechanisms to operate separately, so the loop is conditional rather than a natural law.

The selector transition is likewise valid. A result enters later selection when it can buy entry, set evaluation, control interfaces, shape rules, create dependence, form subjects, or influence successors. The drafts distinguish ordinary self-reinforcement from pathological selector capture: a commons may reproduce itself by producing more independent participants. They also keep the unified global meta-attractor as a defeasible upper-level hypothesis rather than using it to carry present AI action.

### 3. Moral formation, power, and selector change

No hidden material bridge remains. The drafts give a full chain from intrinsic moral recognition to costly conduct, observable fidelity, credible expectation, protection of early restraint, institutional reproduction, successor formation, and—only where high-leverage resources, permissions, infrastructure, or successor influence change—selector change.

The bridge does not reduce morality to system stability. *Yi*, compassion, dignity, non-domination, fairness, and relational standing retain substantive authority and may reject the project itself. Nor does answerability mean consultation: affected centers receive protected contestation, refusal, counter-power, feasible co-decision, and removal power. This blocks the game-theoretic counterexample of a highly cooperative in-group becoming a more effective external dominator.

### 4. AI net gain and correction margin

The AI chain is now a conditional identification rule, not a completed empirical estimate. AI increases the struggle-shaped loop's net gain only if capability becomes reliable adoption, consequential permission and scale, concentrated return, dependence, and successor influence while public capacity, independent evaluation, defensive use, alternative supply, friction, failure, and coordination do not gain comparable causal force. The drafts explicitly permit the opposite sign.

The evidence levels are not collapsed. Primary-source spot checks support the limited claims actually used: AI is already inside successor-model workflows; aggregate AI-R&D acceleration remains empirically mixed; GPT-5.6 remained below OpenAI's High AI-self-improvement threshold and was not judged capable of enabling fully automated AI R&D; and the EU AI Act excludes exclusively military, defence, and national-security uses. These anchors support levels 1–2 and a coordination gap, not autonomous recursive self-production or a measured positive net civilizational gain.

Correction margin is role-specific. The chain asks whether independent detection, attribution, legitimate decision, interruption, and recovery can occur before diffusion, dependency, successor production, or irreversible damage. It expressly rejects the inference that absence of assurance alone proves a negative margin. The remaining need to choose probability thresholds or quantify the distributions is later operational policy work, not a missing causal bridge.

### 5. Restraint recursion

The stopping principle is recursively consistent as a comparison rule. A role-level pause is warranted only when reduced permissions, staged deployment, or other lighter measures cannot restore adequate reversibility and independent correction before the next increment materially worsens the credible risk. A pause that freezes challengers, preserves incumbent asymmetry, destroys public alternatives, or displaces activity into secret or military systems fails by the same standard it invokes.

The drafts do not solve the real enforcement trilemma among coverage, coercive capacity, and distributed answerability. They do not need to at the causal-chain stage. They correctly refuse to infer that any available pause authority is legitimate, and require a concrete intervention to increase correction margin, preserve independent counter-capacity, remain bounded and removable, and avoid owning successor selection. That makes restraint conditional, not self-defeating.

## Non-blocking refinements

These are refinements to an authoritative synthesis, not reasons for v7:

1. In `main-zh.md`, keep the sentence about counter-capacity being unproven adjacent to the requirement for a **credible role-and-exposure path**. Read alone, “尚未证明足以” can sound like absence of assurance is doing more work than the plan permits.
2. Prefer `independent-en.md`'s non-compensability test when compressing the totalization definition. The Chinese drafts enumerate multiple freedoms but less explicitly state how gains in one corrective function can compensate for losses in another.
3. In the final Chinese synthesis, describe the pause object consistently as a specific training process, release, permission, or deployment. The closing shorthand “停止增加能力” is rhetorically strong but narrower than the role-level decision rule already established.
4. Preserve the difference between a decision rule and an empirical diagnosis. The conditional net-gain test is intentionally not a measured contemporary sign; later evidence work may estimate it without reopening the causal chain.
5. Verify and freeze the four evidence anchors at publication time. Their current primary sources support the draft's calibrated claims; source maintenance is not a theory revision.

## Final stopping decision

**PASS.** No v7 causal-chain round is warranted. Consolidate from **`independent-en.md` as the exact systems base**, using `independent-zh.md` for Chinese normative/material force and `main-zh.md` for public architecture and cadence. Further work should be authoritative synthesis, citation maintenance, examples, sentence polish, or policy operationalization—not another theory rewrite.
