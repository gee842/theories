# Causal-chain v6 — Cross-version stopping audit

## Verdict

**PASS.** V6 contains no remaining load-bearing category error, scale jump, hidden normative bridge, evidence-level collapse, or self-defeating restraint principle. On the causal-chain standard, another free rewrite is more likely to loosen an already completed bridge than to repair one.

This is not a claim that the eventual essay is finished. Citations can be strengthened, Chinese diction can be made more natural, and policy implications can be developed later. None of those needs warrants a v7 causal-chain round.

## 1. The chain that now survives

The three drafts all establish the same sequence without silently changing levels:

1. finite centers act through partial feedback;
2. cost displacement and relative vulnerability can make restraint locally disabling;
3. retained asymmetry couples these mechanisms into a self-reinforcing struggle;
4. success then changes later resources, permissions, standards, subjects, and successor production;
5. struggle thereby enters the selector rather than remaining one strategy within it;
6. a direction becomes totalizing when its influence over later selection increases while indispensable independent capacities to sense, refuse, correct, maintain alternatives, and form successors lose causal power;
7. moral insight becomes counter-selection only when commitment gains protection, reproduction, and eventually selector-level leverage without incorporating every other center into one sovereign whole;
8. AI changes the trajectory conditionally, when capability becomes reliable high-consequence use, concentrated return, dependency, and successor influence faster than counter-capacity grows;
9. where the next concrete increment can consume the conditions of later correction before redirection is possible, continued expansion loses its default legitimacy;
10. redirection, restriction, or pause must itself preserve independent answer, or it reproduces the same closing direction.

No step requires a completed global attractor, inevitable convergence, a unified human subject, an already autonomous machine center, or the claim that all conflict is domination.

## 2. Audit by failure class

### Category error — passed

The drafts no longer treat a system boundary as a uniquely real subject, positive feedback as a hidden agent, stability as goodness, diversity of products as effective freedom, capability as deployment, or current AI assistance as autonomous recursive self-improvement.

`independent-en.md` states the distinctions most explicitly. `main-zh.md` carries them with less exposition but does not collapse them.

### Scale jump — passed

The movement from local advantage to civilization is conditional: retained gains must alter later entry, permission, infrastructure, standards, subject formation, or successor production. The contemporary diagnosis is presented as several mutually reinforcing loops and a serious directional hypothesis, not proof that one global meta-attractor already controls everything.

The claim therefore remains falsifiable. Strong counter-selection, restored independent alternatives, changed return paths, or constraints that strengthen with scale would narrow or defeat it.

### Hidden normative bridge — passed

The texts do not derive the good from system persistence. They say directly that suffering, dignity, *yi*, fairness, non-domination, relationship, and the standing of affected centers supply substantive judgments. Generativity is limited to an anti-closure meta-constraint; it cannot legitimate every possible future or convert moral traditions into techniques for system maintenance.

They also preserve the distinction among moral preservation, institutional reproduction, and actual selector change. Answerability includes protected refusal, counter-power, co-decision, and removal, not only consultation.

### Evidence-level collapse — passed

All three drafts separate:

- capability from reliable adoption, permission, scale, return, and net direction;
- AI-assisted research from partial workflow automation, aggregate institutional acceleration, highly automated successor production, and autonomous recursive self-production;
- operator amplification from the more conditional formation of a persistent hard-to-redirect process;
- real coordination growth from adequate threshold-time coverage.

`independent-zh.md` and `independent-en.md` provide the fullest evidence ladder. `main-zh.md` compresses it to the minimum needed for the causal chain. No conclusion depends on promoting a lower-level observation into a higher-level fact.

### Self-defeating restraint — passed

The intervention is role-level, not a ban on an unlimited category called intelligence. It compares continued expansion, reduced permission or staged deployment, targeted pause, and coordinated pause. It also subjects stopping power to the same test: incumbent preservation, secret or military displacement, single-gate capture, and destruction of public alternatives can all deepen closure.

The drafts therefore do not answer a totalizing process by installing a new unanswerable center. They require restraint to increase correction margin, remain contestable and removable, protect independent counter-capacity, and recede when its conditions no longer hold.

## 3. Differences among the drafts

| Draft | Strongest use | Residual non-blocking weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`main-zh.md`](../main-zh.md) | Best Chinese public spine: concise, cumulative, and forceful; every finite v5 repair is present | English technical terms and compressed empirical ladder should be revised during essay composition, not by reopening the chain |
| [`independent-zh.md`](../independent-zh.md) | Best Chinese source for normative nuance, empirical calibration, and the distinction between preservation, reproduction, and selector change | Greater length and evidentiary detail slightly interrupt the main movement |
| [`independent-en.md`](../independent-en.md) | Best English control text and most explicit scientific audit trail | Repeated qualifications make it a reference foundation rather than the final public cadence |

The three are not rival theories. They are compatible views of one completed foundation.

## 4. What remains, but belongs later

The following are legitimate future tasks, not reasons for v7:

- replace avoidable English terms in the Chinese public version;
- verify and format every empirical citation during the essay research pass;
- decide how much contemporary social evidence the final essay needs;
- derive domain-specific policy from the role-level decision rule;
- test examples against readers from different moral and civilizational traditions;
- restore the elegance and narrative flow of the strongest earlier essay sections.

Those tasks concern evidence architecture, rhetoric, application, and finished prose. Reopening the foundational chain would confuse incompleteness of the essay with incompleteness of the reasoning.

## 5. Recommendation

Stop causal-chain iteration at v6 unless an independent audit identifies a specific load-bearing failure and can name the exact invalid inference. Treat:

- `main-zh.md` as the Chinese public architecture and compression boundary;
- `independent-zh.md` as the Chinese normative and empirical reasoning source;
- `independent-en.md` as the English and systems reasoning control.

The next artifact should be built from this foundation rather than replacing it.
