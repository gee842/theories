# Causal-chain v6 — Stopping synthesis

## Decision

**Stop the causal-chain version cycle at v6. Do not generate v7.**

Four independent review lanes—cross-version, systems/game theory, normative/traditional/justice, and AI engineering/evidence—returned PASS under the stopping standard in `plan.md`. None identified a remaining load-bearing category error, scale jump, hidden normative bridge, evidence-level collapse, or self-defeating restraint principle.

This is the first round in which the reviews no longer disagree about whether a bridge exists. They disagree only about which draft preserves each bridge most safely and which refinements belong in the later authoritative text.

## What is now established

The defensible causal chain is:

> Finite centers act through partial feedback. Cost displacement and relative vulnerability can make restraint locally disabling; retained asymmetry couples them into a self-reinforcing struggle. When the gains of that struggle reshape entry, resources, permission, standards, subject formation, and successor production, struggle enters the selector. A direction becomes totalizing not because positive feedback guarantees one endpoint, but because it gains influence over later selection while indispensable independent capacities to sense, refuse, correct, maintain alternatives, and form successors lose causal power. Moral insight becomes counter-selection only when commitment receives material protection, reproduces itself, and eventually alters high-leverage selectors without absorbing every center into one whole. AI strengthens a struggle-shaped direction only conditionally: capability must become reliable high-consequence use, concentrated return, dependency, and successor influence faster than counter-capacity grows. Where a concrete next increment can consume the conditions of correction before redirection is possible, continued expansion loses its default legitimacy. Redirection, restriction, or pause must itself preserve independent answer, or it repeats the closing direction it was meant to interrupt.

This conclusion does **not** require:

- a single proven global meta-attractor;
- inevitable convergence;
- the claim that all conflict is wrong;
- a unified human subject or uniquely real system boundary;
- a measured positive net effect for all AI;
- an already autonomous, self-maintaining machine center;
- blanket authority for an existing state, firm, or institution to stop everyone else.

## Source-of-truth architecture

No single draft should be made to perform every function.

- [`independent-zh.md`](../independent-zh.md) is the **Chinese reasoning source**: it preserves the fullest normative, material, empirical, and authorization bridges.
- [`independent-en.md`](../independent-en.md) is the **systems and evidence control** and the source for the eventual authoritative English chain.
- [`main-zh.md`](../main-zh.md) is the **public architecture and compression boundary**: its six movements, cumulative cadence, and ending should govern later Chinese composition.

This division resolves the reviewers' different “best base” judgments. It is not an averaging instruction. Later writing should inherit the public movement from `main-zh.md`, test every compression against `independent-zh.md`, and use `independent-en.md` to catch scientific or systems-level collapse.

## Why the remaining issues do not justify v7

The audits recorded several real improvements:

- make explicit that withdrawal of expansion legitimacy does not automatically authorize an available sovereign;
- keep the credible role-and-exposure path adjacent to any burden-shifting language;
- state the non-compensability test when compressing effective freedom;
- add a development-justice principle for who bears pause costs;
- prevent “where feasible” from becoming an escape from affected-public participation;
- upgrade several citations and add the missing concentration and coordination anchors to a short public version;
- operationalize “adequate reversibility,” “material worsening,” and review/exit triggers for particular roles;
- replace avoidable English terms and recover finished literary flow.

These do not alter the causal chain. They are bounded tasks for authoritative consolidation, evidence verification, essay writing, or policy design. Creating v7 to perform them would again mix different layers of work and risk reintroducing settled errors.

## Stopping rule going forward

Reopen the causal-chain series only if a critic can identify an exact invalid inference in the established sequence and show that it belongs to one of the five stopping categories. A request for another example, more evidence, stronger prose, a specific governance design, or a complete moral theory is not sufficient.

V6 is therefore accepted as the causal foundation. The next work should build from it, not replace it.
