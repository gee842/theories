# Causal Chain v6 — Consolidation Plan

## Objective

V6 is not a new theory round. It consolidates v5 into a candidate authoritative chain. Use the six-movement shape and public cadence of `v5/main-zh.md`, the normative/material depth of `v5/independent-zh.md`, and the scientific discipline of `v5/independent-en.md`.

The draft must remain compact and repair only the finite residual bridges identified in `v5/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-consolidation-direction.md`.

## The argument that must survive

> Struggle can enter successor selection. A direction becomes totalizing when the process selecting it increasingly shapes later selection while indispensable capacities for independent sensing, refusal, correction, alternative reproduction, and successor formation lose causal power. Moral traditions can form counter-selection when intrinsic commitments become credible conduct, receive material protection, and alter later selectors without absorbing other centers into one whole. AI raises a struggle-shaped loop's net gain only when capability joins reliable adoption, consequential permission, concentrated return, dependency, and successor influence while counter-capacity does not gain comparable force. Where a concrete recursion may consume correction before it can be redirected, continued expansion loses its default legitimacy. Restraint must then preserve the plurality of answer rather than capture it.

## Six movements

1. **Why restraint becomes a disadvantage** — finite feedback; cost displacement; relative vulnerability; retained asymmetry as the coupling intermediary.
2. **When struggle enters the selector** — success changes resources, rules, subjects, and successor production.
3. **How a direction becomes totalizing** — multidimensional loss of independent correction; lock-in, terminal collapse, and failed totalization; no guaranteed convergence.
4. **How civilization produces counter-selection** — intrinsic moral claims; answerability without incorporation; commitment, protection, reproduction, selector change; dissent and counter-power.
5. **How intelligence changes net gain and correction margin** — high-leverage roles; conditional effective gain; two risk routes; five recursion levels; current coordination and evidence boundaries.
6. **Direction and reflexive restraint** — generative meta-constraint; made minds; withdrawal of default expansion legitimacy; role-level redirect/restriction/pause; restraint remains answerable.

## Finite repairs

### 1. Make independence concrete

An alternative is not independent merely because another organization runs it. It must be able to observe, refuse, maintain itself, organize counter-power, and produce successors without permission from the process it may need to correct. Do this in one paragraph, not a taxonomy.

### 2. Add the missing coupling intermediary

Write the loop explicitly:

> displaced cost creates retainable resource or capability asymmetry → asymmetry changes others' exposure and threat perception → relative vulnerability produces exceptional permission and renewed competition → permission enables further displacement.

### 3. Separate three moral levels

- preservation of dissent;
- reproduction through institutions and successors;
- actual change to high-leverage selectors.

Do not claim selector change when only preservation is shown.

### 4. Give affected centers material standing

Answerability must include protected contestation, refusal, counter-power, co-decision where feasible, and removal or replacement of representatives. Under domination, counter-selection may begin through disclosure, non-cooperation, compensation, and dismantling forced dependence rather than immediate trust.

### 5. Preserve made minds

Add one sentence: moral uncertainty is neither proof that current models are persons nor permission for unlimited conditioning, copying, exploitation, memory deletion, or termination; any created centers of experience or care belong among the future participants protected from present totalization.

### 6. Insert no more than four evidence anchors

Use current primary/official evidence only for:

- frontier/successor-production concentration or influence;
- AI already entering successor-model workflows;
- current evidence limits on aggregate acceleration and autonomous self-improvement;
- real but incomplete coordination, especially military/secret gaps.

Do not turn the chain into a report.

### 7. Define a high-leverage AI role

A role is high leverage when it combines successor influence or high-consequence execution with diffusion, dependency, or harm that may outrun independent detection, interruption, and recovery.

### 8. Make pause a role-level comparison

Compare continued expansion, reduced permissions/staged deployment, targeted pause, and coordinated pause. Pause when lighter measures cannot restore adequate reversibility and independent correction before the next increment materially worsens the risk.

### 9. Keep restraint recursive

State only the essential failure modes: incumbent freeze and secret/military displacement; one-gate capture and destruction of public alternatives can be folded into them. Restraint must increase correction margin and preserve independent counter-capacity.

## Evidence discipline

- `capability ≠ reliable deployment ≠ consequential scale ≠ net direction`;
- first AI route has stronger evidence than the second;
- level 1 is established, level 2 partly established, level 3 remains a net empirical question, levels 4–5 are not publicly established;
- coordination is growing but has not shown globally consistent threshold-time coverage;
- absence of assurance is not itself proof of negative correction margin; the role and exposure must make the irreversible path credible.

## Normative discipline

- systems stability does not define good;
- generativity is an anti-closure meta-constraint, not a master good;
- suffering, dignity, *yi*, non-domination, fairness, and relational standing remain substantive judgments;
- traditions may challenge the project rather than merely serve it;
- answerability transfers some power, not only information;
- defensive and emancipatory counter-power must not be equated with domination-seeking competition;
- restraint authority must be limited, contestable, removable, and unable to preserve the asymmetry that produced the danger.

## Prose discipline

- six sections only;
- use `v5/main-zh.md` as the length boundary, not a quota;
- let concepts appear after the phenomenon they name;
- one concrete scene or contrast per major bridge;
- no repeated lists of safeguards, AI use cases, traditions, or metaphors;
- reduce English terms in Chinese where a precise Chinese phrase exists;
- keep the conclusion strong because its conditions have been established, not because caveats disappear.

## Stopping standard

V6 is good enough if independent reviewers find no remaining load-bearing category error, scale jump, hidden normative bridge, evidence-level collapse, or self-defeating restraint principle. Residual needs for citations, examples, sentence polish, or later policy design do not justify another causal-chain version.
