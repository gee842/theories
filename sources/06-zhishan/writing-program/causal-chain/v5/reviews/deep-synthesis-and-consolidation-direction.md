# Causal-chain v5 — Review synthesis and consolidation direction

## 0. Decision

V5 is consolidation-ready. It does not need another unconstrained causal-chain rewrite.

All four reviews agree that the four load-bearing repairs now exist in finished prose:

- totalization is progressive loss of independent correction and alternative reproduction, not guaranteed convergence;
- cost displacement and relative vulnerability are distinct and causally coupled;
- moral formation crosses into material counter-selection;
- restraint must answer to the same anti-closure direction it serves.

The remaining work is finite. It concerns identification, evidence, political power, and compression—not a new theory.

---

## 1. What now stands

### Totalizing direction

The drafts no longer make positive feedback prove one stable endpoint. They distinguish lock-in, terminal collapse, and failed totalization. The core judgment is now defensible:

> A process becomes totalizing when its success increasingly shapes later selection while independent sensing, correction, exit, alternative maintenance, and successor formation lose indispensable causal capacity.

This can fail. Public infrastructure, open standards, institutional redirection, moral counter-selection, or alternative successor supply can interrupt it.

### The struggle coupling

V5 provides a real loop:

> displaced cost makes continued competition cheaper → retained advantage increases permission and further displacement → others become relatively more vulnerable → renewed competition legitimates more displacement → returns enter the next selector.

The mechanism remains conditional, but the bridge is no longer missing.

### Moral-to-material counter-selection

The drafts now move from intrinsic moral orientation to causal reproduction:

> perception of obligation → action under cost → visible fidelity → credible expectation → common protection of restraint → institutions and transmission → later subject formation.

They also preserve the vital distinction that *yi* does not become right because it wins; material protection keeps right action from being systematically selected out.

### Answerability without incorporation

Another center can enter my judgment without becoming represented by me, absorbed into my objective, or deprived of refusal. This successfully protects relationality from turning into a larger sovereign whole.

### AI evidence discipline

All drafts retain:

- capability is not net effect;
- high-leverage roles require permission, scale, return, dependency or successor influence;
- locally directed amplification has stronger present evidence than a new self-maintaining center;
- level 1 AI-assisted research and parts of level 2 automation exist; level 3 aggregate acceleration remains mixed; levels 4–5 are not publicly established;
- coordination is growing, but coverage and threshold-time effectiveness remain incomplete;
- correction margin is deployment-specific.

### Reflexive restraint

A pause that freezes incumbents, moves development into secrecy, destroys alternatives, or creates one unanswerable gate is recognized as a new closing direction. Means no longer receive exemption from stance.

## 2. The remaining bridges

### 2.1 Independence must become concrete

“Independent correction” is still slightly abstract. The consolidated text needs one concrete contrast:

> A nominal alternative is not independent if it relies on the same compute provider, funding, standards, data pipeline, authorization, and successor infrastructure as the relation it may need to correct. Independence means possessing a protected capacity to observe, refuse, maintain itself, organize counter-power, and produce successors without permission from the process being answered.

Do not build another taxonomy. One paragraph is enough.

### 2.2 One intermediary remains in the struggle loop

Cost displacement does not automatically cause relative vulnerability. The missing intermediary is retained asymmetry or capability:

> displaced cost creates retainable resource/capability advantage → the advantage changes others' exposure or threat perception → relative vulnerability produces exceptional permission → permission enables further displacement.

This should be stated once.

### 2.3 Moral preservation is not yet selector-level leverage

Protecting cooperators from elimination is necessary, but does not prove the dominant selector changed. Distinguish three levels:

1. **preservation:** moral dissent survives;
2. **reproduction:** it forms successors and institutions;
3. **selector change:** it alters what receives high-leverage resources, permission, infrastructure, and successor influence.

The consolidated chain should claim only the level actually demonstrated.

### 2.4 Answerability needs co-authorization and counter-power

V5 lets affected centers speak, refuse, appeal, and correct. It less clearly transfers decision and removal power.

The minimal addition is:

> Where a condition shapes another center's life and successor capacity, answerability requires more than listening: protected contestation, co-decision where feasible, the power to organize counter-force, and the ability to remove or replace those claiming to represent the common direction.

In domination, moral reciprocity may begin not with trust but with exposure, non-cooperation, compensation, dismantling forced dependence, and counter-power.

### 2.5 Made minds need one sentence

The core proof does not require a full made-mind ethics. It should still say:

> Moral uncertainty about made minds is neither proof that current models are persons nor permission for unlimited training, copying, conditioning, exploitation, memory deletion, or termination; any created centers of experience or care belong among those whose future must remain answerable.

### 2.6 AI needs one role-level decision rule

Current evidence supports strong redirection and conditional pauses, but not a blanket halt. A high-leverage role should be defined as one that combines successor influence or high-consequence execution with diffusion, dependency, or harm that may outrun independent detection, interruption, and recovery.

For that role, compare:

- continued expansion;
- reduced permissions or staged deployment;
- targeted pause;
- coordinated pause.

Pause is warranted when lighter measures cannot restore adequate reversibility and independent correction before the next increment materially worsens the risk.

## 3. Current evidence boundary

The AI review checked current primary evidence through July 2026.

- AI is already used in successor-model research workflows, but aggregate R&D acceleration remains mixed and fully automated AI R&D is not publicly established. [International AI Safety Report 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026), [GPT-5.6 system card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-5-6)
- High-permission national-security integration is real, but does not by itself establish weapon authority, irreversible harm, or positive net totalizing gain.
- Agentic-misalignment signals exist, but current public evidence does not show a durable self-maintaining center in deployment.
- Legal and institutional coordination is growing; military and national-security exclusions, inconsistent thresholds, limited external audit, and cross-border coverage remain real gaps.

The consolidated chain should add only a few anchor facts. It should not become a research report.

## 5. Finite consolidation edits

1. Add one paragraph concretizing independent correction and alternative reproduction.
2. Add the retained-asymmetry intermediary to the struggle coupling.
3. Distinguish moral preservation, reproduction, and selector change.
4. Extend answerability to co-authorization, counter-power, and removal where domination exists.
5. Add one sentence on moral uncertainty concerning made minds.
6. Insert no more than four current evidence anchors.
7. Define high-leverage AI roles through successor influence/high-consequence permission plus correction-margin exposure.
8. State one role-level comparison among continued expansion, restricted deployment, targeted pause, and coordinated pause.
9. Compress reflexive restraint to one principle and two failure modes in the main essay.
10. Remove repeated metaphors and reduce English technical terms in the Chinese version.

After these edits, run one final adversarial audit. Do not generate three new unconstrained drafts first.

## 6. Final judgment

V5 has completed the conceptual development phase. Its core argument now survives:

> Struggle can enter successor selection. Totalization is the progressive loss of independent answer and alternative reproduction. Civilizational counter-selection requires moral formation plus material protection. AI raises danger only when capability joins consequential permission, concentrated return, successor influence, and insufficient counter-capacity. A recursion whose next increment may consume correction before it can be redirected loses the presumption of continued expansion. Restraint must then preserve, rather than capture, the world's remaining ability to answer.

The next round should consolidate this argument into one authoritative Chinese chain and one authoritative English chain. Another free rewrite is more likely to reintroduce old gaps than to discover a better foundation.
