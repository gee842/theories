# Post-draft load-bearing audit: independent English draft

## 1. The burden shift names the bearer but not the full proof required

The section “The old direction must stop” correctly derives an asymmetry between an erroneous continuation and a revisable pause, but “the burden therefore belongs to continuation” remains one step too abstract. A continuer could answer it with another safety evaluation while leaving the commercial or strategic selector intact. The draft must say that continuation has to demonstrate both a substantive change in what selects and receives the capability, and a correction window that remains ahead of diffusion, dependence, successor production, and hard-to-reverse harm. Otherwise the burden risks collapsing back into “show us more evidence of safety” instead of the claimed change of default.

## 2. The commons correction gate is missing its capability floor and temporal priority

The two tests near the end are independent in name, but the second asks only for discoverability, interruption, recovery, and redirection. It does not require the least capability, permissions, and deployment scale sufficient for the public purpose, nor does it require independent correction to become effective before diffusion, dependence, irreversible damage, or successor production. A nominally interruptible public system could therefore continue expanding until interruption is no longer practicable. The correction gate needs these two conditions in the prose, without becoming a technical checklist.

## 3. The commercial rule leaves a private-surplus and cross-subsidy loophole

The draft permits payment for labor, cost recovery, and common maintenance, then forbids converting “AI profit and valuation into competitive frontier expansion.” That still appears to permit AI revenue to become private surplus or capital appreciation when the recipient does not visibly reinvest it in a frontier model. It also attaches the prohibition mainly to frontier expansion, allowing revenue, data, dependence, or valuation generated by a nominally bounded product to cross-subsidize the same race elsewhere. The rule must follow final flow: charging is allowed where needed so only incumbents and states do not monopolize provision, but AI returns may not become private surplus, capital appreciation, strategic advantage, or successor control, regardless of the label on the sold system.

## 4. The strategic carrier is asserted more strongly than it is presently evidenced

Movement V gives a concrete contractual observation for the commercial cloud–model loop but describes government procurement only generically. The later carrier-neutral prohibition therefore rests on a less visible national-security chain. Add one narrow current observation: the United States publicly frames AI leadership in economic and military terms and has created frontier-model procurement agreements, while preserving the distinction between contract ceilings and funds actually obligated. At the same time, change “deployment creates revenue, valuation, budget, procurement, and relative position” to a non-deterministic formulation. Deployment can yield those returns; the complete feedback loop has not been directly measured.

## 5. Carrier neutrality needs a boundary between domination and bounded contest or defense

The top rule properly includes commercial, strategic, and military selectors, but the draft does not explicitly preserve bounded, exitable comparison whose victory cannot buy control of the next round. Its national-security paragraph also acknowledges real defense needs without distinguishing a proportionate countermeasure that restores another center’s ability to answer and relinquishes exceptional powers from a security competition that reproduces dominance. Without that distinction, the carrier-neutral rule can be read either as a blanket prohibition on contest or as an invitation to relabel escalation as defense.

## 6. Defensive repetition is beginning to interrupt discovery in two load-bearing transitions

The meta-attractor and totalization appear as adjacent definition paragraphs even though the second is simply the first process deepening. They should be one short naming movement. In Movement V, three successive passages separately say that the complete loop is unproved, that AI R&D autonomy is unproved, and that acceleration is an inference. All three boundaries are necessary, but repeated audit language slows the passage from real resource return to time compression and present loss. Preserve each distinct boundary once, tighten repeated disclaimers, and let the next question—whether correction remains independently faster than dependence—arrive sooner.

## Revision disposition

`independent-en.md` now states the substantive burden on continuation, the least-sufficient and temporally prior correction gate, the final-flow rule for all AI returns, current evidence for the strategic carrier, and the boundaries of bounded contest and proportionate defense. Repeated audit language was compressed without merging the two danger paths. The revised draft passes this audit; the findings above remain as a trace of the first draft's vulnerabilities.
