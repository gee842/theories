# Wamotopia 2026 – Call for Camps

> Beyond Broken Systems, Towards Human Flourishing
> 

A two-week, decentralized pop-up city in Chiang Mai, where communities from all over the world build their own camps, experiences, and temporary homes together.

<aside>
🔥 Dates: Jan 21 – Feb 4

**Location:** Chiang Mai, Thailand

</aside>

We are living in a strange overlap of time:

- **Stone-age emotions.**
- **Medieval institutions.**
- **God-like technology.**

Technology is sprinting forward with a blindfold on. Our feeds are full, but our **shared reality is fracturing**.

**We face a choice:** let the old games of extraction and rivalry win, or build a new protocol of flourishing. We all carry our own small version of “how things could work better” — a different way to live, gather, collaborate.

**Wamotopia is an invitation:**

Just come, share your version, and help us rebuild a shared future for humanity together.

---

## 1. What is Wamotopia?

Wamotopia is **not** a conference or a single event.

It is a **connected archipelago of camps** — a **living protocol** for how we might relate differently. It obeys one prime directive: **Positive Externality**. If we thrive, the network thrives.

For two weeks in Chiang Mai, dozens of small, self-organized spaces appear across the city:

apartments, cafés, rooftops, studios, villas. Each becomes a **Wamo Camp (“Waca”)** with its own story, rituals, and gatherings. Together they form one connected map and timeline.

In a world where:

- algorithms mediate our attention,
- institutions feel slow and distant,
- and many of us feel increasingly isolated,

Wamotopia asks a simple question:

> What happens if we rebuild reality from the bottom up, camp by camp, person by person?
> 

Each camp is a **micro–proposal** for a different way to be together —

a philosophy tavern, a rooftop dojo, a living room studio, a kitchen commons.

Across the city, this looks like:

- **Community co-creation** – every camp designs its own vibe and programming.
- **Open, low-barrier gatherings** – from intimate circles to late-night jam sessions.
- **Experiments in how we live and meet** – beyond tickets and stages.
- **Positive externalities** – people leave more connected, resourced, and inspired.

**Why Chiang Mai?**

- A thriving hub where **many communities** are exploring new ways of life and the future.
- A place where **pop-up cities and new ideas thrive**, connecting intellectuals and like-minded builders.
- Walkable neighborhoods, cozy cafés, and countless “hackable” third places.
- Soft January weather: cool nights, gentle days.
- A city where local and international cultures already mix — perfect for cross-pollination.

Wamotopia is our yearly attempt to **prototype another mode of civilizational operation** —

not on slides, but on actual streets, for just long enough to feel like a new kind of home.

## 2. Who is this for?

We’re inviting **communities and individuals** who want to **build the bridge between the world as it is and the world we know is possible.**

We are calling for those who refuse to let the future be colonized by the past, who are ready to **suspend the logic of competition and practice a new operating system based on shared flourishing.**

### Great fits

The world is at a crossroad. We gather because we believe it is our responsibility to fix it.

We are calling for **builders, visionaries, and anyone determined to ensure a flourishing future.**

We want you here if:
- You see what is broken in the current world and refuse to look away.
- You have solutions, or you are seeking allies to co-create them.
- You want to find like-minded people who share your vision and goals.
- You are ready to join forces to build a reality that works.

Specifically, you might be a:

- Builders of new systems (Web3, AI, Crypto, Network States)
- Thinkers & Philosophers (Systems thinking, Human psychology, Social change, Futurism)
- Explorers of new narratives (Metamodernism, Game-B, Solarpunk)
- Research groups & Visionaries (DeSci, Governance, Future Studies)
- Maker / Hacker / Developer collectives
- Communities exploring new ways of living (Co-living, Eco-villages)
- Art, music, and performance crews
- Wellness / movement / meditation groups
- Local Chiang Mai creative & social communities

We’re especially excited about camps that:

- Have **2–3 committed core organizers**
- Are willing to **design their own camp experience** (theme, format, vibe)
- Care about **The Gifting Gravity** – you understand that to build abundance, you must first give to the commons (people leave better than they arrived)
- Align with our basic values: **respect, openness, consent, non-extractive behavior**

### Not a fit

We’re **not** the right place for:

- Purely extractive “sell your course / funnel building / aggressive lead-gen” setups
- Brands that only want a billboard, not a genuine community presence
- Activities that ignore local context, neighbors, or safety

If you’re not sure whether you fit, talk to us — we’d rather explore together than gatekeep.

## 3. How can you participate?

### 1) Host a Camp

**“This is our base in Wamotopia.”**

Your community hosts its own **Wamo Camp** (“Waca”): a physical base + series of gatherings over a few days or the whole two weeks.

**You bring:**

- A core organizing crew (min. 2–3 people)
- A camp theme / intention (“AI philosophy tavern”, “Night walk radio”, “Yoga & hackers”)
- Programming: talks, workshops, dinners, jams, rituals…

**We support you with:**

- Venue matchmaking & negotiation support
- Integration into the shared map & schedule
- Visibility and cross-community connections

### 2) Co-host Events / Sessions

**“We can’t run a whole camp, but we want to host something.”**

You host **1–2 sessions** as part of an existing camp or in a shared space.

**You bring:**

- A clear idea for a session (topic, format, who it’s for)
- 1–2 organizers to run it smoothly

**We support you with:**

- Matching you with a host camp or venue
- Listing your session on the map & schedule
- Helping you reach the right people

### 3) Join & Explore

**“I just want to be part of the magic.”**

You don’t have to host anything to be a vital part of Wamotopia. We need curious souls, active participants, and cross-pollinators.

**You bring:**

- Your presence, curiosity, and vibe
- Participation in sessions, workshops, and rituals
- Support for others’ camps (buy a drink, help set up, share the word)

**We support you with:**

- A **City Map & Schedule** to navigate the chaos
- **Open events** every day
- A community of new friends waiting to be met

### 4) Partner / Sponsor

**“We want to empower the ecosystem.”**

If you’re a project, DAO, foundation, studio, or brand that wants to support:

- Sponsor a camp / theme day
- Provide venues, equipment, food & drinks, art, or micro-grants
- Support open public programming that aligns with our values

We’ll co-design how your contribution **adds to the commons** instead of taking from it.

## 4. What we provide

We’re here to make it **easier and richer** for your camp to exist inside the city.

### Information & Coordination

- Local intelligence on **coworking spaces, villas, cafés, bars, studios, rooftops**
- Intros to local partners, service providers, and collaborators
- 1:1 support for camp organizers on:
    - Choosing a neighborhood & venue type
    - Designing schedules & flows
    - Basic budgeting & logistics questions

### Infrastructure

- A **shared city map** of all camps & venues
- A **public schedule** with filters by date, area, and theme
- Info Desk / HQ and volunteers to:
    - Help people find your camp
    - Assist with translation & local context
    - Respond in basic on-the-ground situations

### Visibility

- Presence on the **main website, map, and social channels**
- Highlighted “camp spotlights” and featured days
- Intros to other communities that might love your vibe

### What we do **NOT** provide

To keep expectations honest:

- We do **not** book flights or individual accommodation for your members
- We do **not** guarantee profit, fundraising, or ticket sales
- We do **not** take responsibility for your internal finances or HR
- We **avoid** extractive or misleading activations — we’re building a commons, not a funnel

## 7. FAQ

- **We’ve never hosted a camp before. Can we still build a wamo camp?**
    
    Yes. Experience helps, but what matters more is **care** and **commitment**. If you have a clear intention and a few people willing to organize, we can support you with structure and examples.
    
- **Do we have to stay for the entire Jan 21 – Feb 4 period?**
    
    No. Camps can be:
    
    - A few key days,
    - A long weekend, or
    - Active across the full two weeks.
    
    We’ll work with you to place your camp in the city-wide timeline so it complements others.
    
- **Will you book accommodation and venues for us?**
    
    We don’t book individual accommodation.
    
    For venues, we:
    
    - Share a vetted list of options
    - Make intros and help with negotiation
    - In some cases, help secure shared hubs or co-hosted spaces
    
    Final contracts and payments are usually handled by camps themselves (or by specific agreements if we co-host).
    
- **Any visa advice?**
    
    Most participants will come on **tourist visas** or visa-exempt entries, depending on passport. Please check the latest rules for Thailand for your nationality. We can share a simple info page and what previous participants have done, but we are **not** an immigration authority.
    
- **Can we run paid events? Will you take a cut?**
    
    You can run **free or paid** events, as long as they respect local laws and our Code of Conduct.
    
    - Wamotopia does **not** automatically take a cut from your ticket sales.
    - If we co-produce something financially, we’ll make a clear, separate agreement.
    
    We encourage non-extractive pricing and at least some accessible / open activities.
    
- **We just want to attend and explore, not host anything. Is that okay?**
    
    Absolutely. Wamotopia is also for **wanderers and participants**.
    
    We’ll share a separate **Participant Guide** with:
    
    - How to navigate the city and map
    - How to join open sessions
    - How to contribute in small ways to the commons

## 9. Past moments

> If you’re also curious where Wamo began…
> 

### The Origin: Dali, 2022

In August 2022, a loose constellation of Web3 builders and communities gathered in Dali with a simple set of principles: **positive externalities, openness, commons, open source**. Fueled by curiosity about DAOs, self-governance, public goods and the future of community, they launched **“Summer of Wamo｜Dali Web3 Summer Fest.”**

Then things broke.

Amid sudden lockdown policies and shifting rules, the original plan dissolved — and the experiment went fully decentralized. There was no “main venue” left, so participants simply started weaving the festival into the city itself. Dozens of cafés, bars and public corners turned into spontaneous camps. Conversations spilled onto streets. New sessions appeared on handwritten posters and in group chats. People who had never met acted as if they were co-hosts of the same invisible event.

By the end, there were **dozens of camps, hundreds of sessions, thousands of co-builders**, and countless encounters between the Cangshan mountains and Erhai lake. Web3 didn’t stay on slides or in Discord servers; it flowed into Dali’s alleys and rooftops.

No centralized organizer could have “delivered” this. It was trust, shared values and stubborn optimism doing the work.

To everyone who came from around the world to co-create this **non-existent conference** — thank you. Its ripples continue, on-chain and off.

> The center dissolved.
> 
> 
> Wamo didn’t.
