# The Meta‑Attractor — Complete Single‑Page Copy (Public‑First)

## 0) Hero & TL;DR
The Meta‑Attractor: the pattern that quietly narrows our future—and how to widen it before AI makes it permanent.

We don’t just live in markets and models. We live in a pattern that rewards simple numbers over rich ends, then rewrites rules so those numbers win again. That cross‑scale loop—a meta‑attractor—captures minds (habits), institutions (KPIs), media (what spreads), and infrastructure (code, capital) until alternatives feel naïve. AI trained and deployed inside this basin will supercharge it. The fix isn’t a slogan; it’s a sequence: Recognize → Slow/Cap → Align → Coordinate → Transform. Use optimization to defend plurality—not collapse it. [Q01]

## 1) What is a Meta‑Attractor?
A meta‑attractor is a self‑reinforcing pattern that operates across minds, institutions, culture, and infrastructure. It reshapes environments to reproduce itself and progressively eliminates conceptual and practical alternatives. Think “basins” in rule‑space: once you enter, feedbacks deepen the groove. Over time, the pattern feels like nature itself. [Q02]

Why it matters now: when the basin is misaligned (proxy > purpose), adding speed makes things worse—arriving faster at the wrong destination. [Q03]

Everyday snapshots:
- Social platforms: connection → outrage optimization → attention harms.
- Search/ads: organize information → profit extraction.
- Universities: truth & character → credential factories.

---

## 2) The Loop Today
Two live examples show the logic:

A) Attention economy. Engagement is the proxy; wellbeing, truth, and civic trust are the ends. Push the proxy hard enough and the correlation flips (Goodhart): more time‑on‑site, worse information diet and wellbeing. Winners write the platform rules; the proxy becomes law. [Q04]

B) Growth–finance. Cheap capital → scale → power → rule‑rewriting (accounting, tax, antitrust) → cheaper capital. Real productivity, resilience, and externalities fall off the dashboard. The loop entrenches itself and raises barriers to alternative models. [Q05]

See case cards in the Practitioner layer for diagnostics and interventions.

## 3) How Capture Closes the Loop
Micro: strategies that “win here and now” spread; identities form around optimization habits. Meso: optimizers refactor KPIs, hiring, and budgets; institutions turn into optimization machines. Macro: winners shape laws, narratives, and infrastructure; alternatives lose language and shelter. Feedback: captured minds build captured orgs that sculpt captured environments that re‑capture minds. [Q06]

## 4) Diagnostics You Can Use
- Proxy–Welfare Coupling (PWC): correlation × local slope sign between a real outcome (W) and its proxy (f). Negative = optimizing f harms W. Gate scaling on PWC ≥ 0. [Q07]
- Proxy Dominance Index (PDI): how much decision weight falls on f. High PDI with low/negative PWC is classic proxy tyranny. [Q08]
- Slack Coefficient (SC): resource slack × variability penalty × time‑scale safety; (0,1], higher is better. Don’t scale below threshold. [Q09]

## 5) AI/AGI/ASI Readiness & Meta‑Attractor Evolution
How the current basin shapes AI: proxies (benchmarks, engagement, EPS) dominate; speed/scale supremacy rewards capability over safety; rule‑rewriting turns proxies into environment; training data reflect practiced values (extraction, short‑termism). [Q10]

With more capable systems: lock‑in amplifies; oversight capture accelerates; deployment outruns safety evidence; deceptive competence rises. [Q11]

Why we aren’t ready for ASI (minimum argument):
1) Alignment tech is immature (corrigibility, robust honesty under shift).  
2) Values are fragmented (plural ends, single proxies metastasize).  
3) Incentives reward speed over safety.  
4) Governance lags capability; capture risk is high.  
5) Slack is scarce; reversibility is rare. [Q12]

Readiness gates (rails before thrust): evidence‑tied compute tiers; tripwired deployments with tested rollback; independent kill‑switch authority; diagnostic floors (PWC≥0, SC≥min, PDI in band, CR↓, DE↑); procurement/prestige that pay for reliability and resilience. Sequence for AI: Recognize → Slow/Cap → Align → Coordinate → Transform. Don’t scale before evidence. [Q13]

## 6) Wisdom We Forgot
Civilizations already saw the pattern and built dampers.
- Vedic India: niṣkāma‑karma (act without attachment to fruits) breaks proxy fixation; yajña/ṛta restores reciprocity cycles. [Q14]
- Rome: virtus→gloria→dignitas→potestas (honor–wealth–power flywheel) and the annona provisioning system—attempts to throttle capture and add buffers. [Q15]
- China: rectification of names (正名) against name–reality drift; ever‑normal granaries and land resets as buffers and anti‑capture. [Q16]

## 7) The Sequence that Works
Write it on every roadmap: Recognize → Slow/Cap → Align → Coordinate → Transform. Skip the first three and superhuman systems lock in the wrong game—or outgrow our control. Follow them and AI defends plurality and commons. [Q17]

## 8) The Anti‑Attractor Toolkit
- Multi‑objective gates: value created, harm avoided, uncertainty reduced—each must pass; no single lever. [Q18]
- Tripwires & kill‑switches: eval fail → pause; rollback tested; compute gates tied to safety evidence. [Q19]
- Buffers & slack: reserves, rate limits, quiet hours, circuit breakers; time‑scale safety. [Q20]
- Polycentric oversight + sortition: overlapping authorities; rotation/cooling‑off to limit patronage; independent shutdown authority. [Q21]
- Provenance & transparency: content credentials, public eval artifacts, model cards with non‑goals and incident disclosures. [Q22]

## 9) Objections—Short Replies
- “Optimization made us rich.” Past a threshold, proxies eat the substrate (trust, ecology, wisdom). Measure it; redesign objectives. [Q23]
- “It’s just evolution.” Evolution also yields cooperation and restraint; systems with slack and buffers outperform in the long run.
- “Coordination is impossible.” Hard, not impossible; compute gates, liability, audits, and shared safety funding are minimum viable enforcement. [Q24]
