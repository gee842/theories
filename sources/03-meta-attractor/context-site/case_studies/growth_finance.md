# Case Study: Growth–Finance Loop (Stub)

## Objective
Show how cheap capital → scale → market power → rule‑rewriting → entrenchment, with proxy dominance over real productivity and welfare.

## Proxy vs. Welfare
- Proxy (f): EPS, valuation, revenue growth, AUM.
- Welfare (W): long‑term productivity, resilience (buffers), innovation diversity, externalities (carbon, attention, safety).

## Evidence/Data (to source)
- Sector panels linking capital costs, concentration indices (HHI), lobbying spend, and regulatory outcomes; productivity vs. valuation gaps.
- Data stub: firm‑year panel with EPS/EVA, R&D intensity, HHI, lobbying/standard‑setting involvement, externality proxies.

## Diagnostics
- PWC: correlation and local slope W vs. f (watch for declining coupling in mature scale regimes).
- PDI: decision weight on f (compensation plans, shareholder letters) vs. non‑proxy components.
- SC: reserves, redundancy, and time‑to‑decision vs. optimization cadence.
- CR (Capture Risk): lobbying intensity + revolving door + standard control normalized by share.

## Interventions (Anti‑Attractor Toolkit)
- Multi‑objective gates in procurement and listings; interoperability and data portability to reduce lock‑in.
- Taxes/caps/pacing where externalities and arms‑race dynamics dominate.
- Steward‑ownership, purpose trusts; rotation/cooling‑off; sortition boards for high‑impact decisions.

## Expected Outcomes
- Restore PWC; lower PDI; SC above threshold; CR trending down; resilience up without killing innovation.
