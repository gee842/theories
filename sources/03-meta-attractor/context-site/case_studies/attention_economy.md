# Case Study: Attention Economy Loop (Stub)

## Objective
Explain how engagement‑optimization induces metric collapse, capture, and lock‑in—and how to redesign for health and legitimacy.

## Proxy vs. Welfare
- Proxy (f): engagement minutes, CTR, session length.
- Welfare (W): attention health (time‑use quality), epistemic quality (accuracy/diversity), civic trust.

## Evidence/Data (to source)
- Time‑use and wellbeing correlations; news exposure diversity metrics; misinformation spread measures; platform policy changes and outcomes.
- Data stub: panel by account/region over time with engagement, quality flags, and survey‑based wellbeing.

## Diagnostics
- PWC: rolling correlation and local slope of W vs. f (expect decay/negative at high f).
- PDI: decision weight on f in ranking/product OKRs (text mining + comp plan proxies).
- SC: buffer measures (rate limits, review cadences) and decision latency vs. optimization cadence.

## Interventions (Anti‑Attractor Toolkit)
- Multi‑objective gates: quality + equity + resilience with non‑tradeable floors.
- Tripwires: virality speed‑bumps; incident‑driven pauses.
- Buffers: default‑off for cross‑network forwarding; quiet hours.
- Oversight: external audits, red‑team bounties, provenance for high‑reach content.

## Expected Outcomes
- Raise PWC to non‑negative; reduce PDI into safe band; SC above threshold.
