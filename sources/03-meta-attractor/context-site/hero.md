# Hero Variants

## Public‑First (general audience)
Title: The Meta‑Attractor
Subhead: The pattern that quietly narrows our future—and how to widen it before AI makes it permanent.

Body (~150 words)
We don’t live inside “the market” or “the algorithm.” We live inside a pattern that rewards simple numbers over rich ends—and then rewrites rules so the numbers win again. That’s a meta‑attractor: a cross‑scale loop that captures minds, institutions, media, and infrastructure until alternatives feel naïve. AI trained and deployed inside this loop will supercharge it. The fix isn’t a slogan; it’s a sequence: Recognize the loop, Slow/Cap where risk runs hot, Align objectives with real‑world harms and goods, Coordinate oversight with teeth, and only then Transform at speed.

This page maps the loop in plain language, shows today’s examples, offers diagnostics you can use, and outlines design patterns that keep ends bigger than their measures. The goal is simple: use optimization to defend plurality—not collapse it.

## Policy‑First (governance emphasis)
Title: Rails Before Thrust
Subhead: A readiness blueprint for AI that won’t lock in the wrong game.

Body (~150 words)
Frontier capability is outpacing safety. In systems already optimized for proxies (EPS, engagement), more thrust without rails deepens lock‑in and brittleness. Readiness means gating compute on evidence, tripwired deployments with tested rollback, independent kill‑switch authority, diagnostic floors (PWC≥0, SC≥min, PDI in band), and polycentric oversight with sortition to reduce capture. These are minimum viable constraints, not utopian wishes. They create a meta‑attractor in rule space where fast dynamics fall into safe attractors—and where the rules themselves return when they drift. Change the basin; then add speed.
