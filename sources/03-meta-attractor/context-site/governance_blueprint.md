# Governance Blueprint (Policy‑Maker Track)

Objective: Minimum viable enforcement to prevent proxy tyranny and capture while enabling beneficial AI.

Pacing & caps
- Monitored compute thresholds tied to demonstrated safety; tiered gating for capability scaling.
- Incident‑driven pauses: automatic slow/stop when evals fail or externality budgets breach.

Facility & deployment controls
- Facility registration and audits; red‑team bounties; disclosure windows for incidents.
- Kill‑switch authority independent of operators; rollback paths tested before high‑impact changes.

Accountability & incentives
- Liability for negligent deployment; risk‑proportional insurance.
- Shared funding for alignment/safety research; procurement that pays for reliability and resilience, not just speed.

Anti‑capture architecture
- Polycentric oversight with sortition on high‑impact boards; rotation and cooling‑off to reduce patronage.
- Interoperability and data portability mandates to limit lock‑in.

Transparency & provenance
- Content credentials for high‑reach media; model cards with non‑goals stated; evaluation artifacts public by default.

Gating checklist
- SC ≥ threshold; PWC ≥ 0; PDI below risk band; CR trending down; DE above floor. If not, scale only after mitigation.
