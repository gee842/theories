# Policy One‑Pager: Rails Before Thrust

Goal: Minimum viable enforcement to prevent proxy tyranny and capture while enabling beneficial AI.

Gates & pacing
- Compute tiers unlock only with external safety evidence; incident‑driven pauses by default.
- Tested rollback paths for high‑impact changes; capability scaling tied to safety maturity.

Oversight & accountability
- Polycentric oversight with sortition on high‑impact boards; rotation and cooling‑off.
- Independent kill‑switch authority; facility registration, audits, and red‑team bounties.
- Liability for negligent deployment; risk‑proportional insurance.

Diagnostics floors (don’t scale without them)
- PWC ≥ 0; Slack Coefficient ≥ threshold; PDI within safe band; Capture Risk trending down; Diversity Exposure above floor.

Transparency & provenance
- Content credentials for high‑reach media; model cards with non‑goals; public evaluation artifacts.

Procurement & prestige
- Pay for reliability, resilience, and safety evidence over raw speed; reward stewardship.

Sequence
- Recognize → Slow/Cap → Align → Coordinate → Transform. Rails first, then thrust.
