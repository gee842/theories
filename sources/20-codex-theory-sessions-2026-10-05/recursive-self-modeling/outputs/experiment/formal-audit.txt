Independent formal audit of the proposed recursive self-modeling experiment
Date: 2026-10-05
Status: design audit and pre-development implementation review

Scope
The conclusions here are algebraic consequences of the proposed scalar synthetic
system. They are not empirical claims about feelings, introspection, consciousness,
or biological implementation. No external theory workspace was modified.

1. Ordinary observations already make the constant bias observable

Let s_t=(x_t,b_t)^T with constant b, A=diag(a,1), a=.8,
known input B*u=(u,0)^T, and observation H*s with H=(1,1).
The two-row observability matrix is O=[[1,1],[a,1]]. Its determinant is
1-a=.2, so O has full rank. In the noiseless system:
  y_(t+1)-a*y_t-u_t = (1-a)*b,
  b = [y_(t+1)-a*y_t-u_t]/(1-a), x_t=y_t-b.
The known applied action must be used, including its clipping. A controller's
choice of feedback does not change this rank calculation when its action is
observed and subtracted.

With w~N(0,.05^2), eps~N(0,.25^2), the numerator's variance is
Q+(1+a^2)*R_y=.0025+1.64*.0625=.105. The one-step bias estimate has
variance .105/.04=2.625 and SD about 1.620. Observability is not a claim
of useful instantaneous precision. Consecutive estimates also have correlated
sensor noise, so naive independent averaging is not a correct variance model.
A temporal estimator can combine many steps. Include a joint x,b estimator
which never buys z; otherwise ordinary dynamics information is omitted from
the apparent comparison between 'external' evidence and self-calibration.
At a=1 the rank is one: an absolute offset is then unidentifiable from y
and known inputs alone. As a approaches one, separation is poorly conditioned.
The known .8 relaxation toward absolute zero is an epistemic anchor.

2. A biased reference identifies relative bias directly, but need not impose
   a fundamental identification limit under the supplied dynamics

At a reference step r=y-z=b-d+eps-eta. A policy treating z as unbiased
therefore estimates b-d, leaving d in its corrected state estimate. This is
a real failure mode of that specified policy and its assumptions.
It is not an impossibility result for every policy in this world.

If d is an unknown constant, augment the state to (x,b,d), with
A=diag(a,1,1), H_y=(1,1,0), H_z=(1,0,1). Rows H_y,H_z,H_y*A form
[[1,1,0],[1,0,1],[a,1,0]], whose determinant is a-1=-.2.
Thus one simultaneous y,z observation and a subsequent y observation
suffice for noiseless identification given known dynamics and actions.
Intermittent z is enough, although finite-horizon noisy estimation may be poor.
A 2D Kalman filter with z=x+eta in the biased-reference world is deliberately
misspecified. Either add a 3D x,b,d sensitivity comparator or explicitly
limit the result to reference-trusting implementations.

The boundary is instructive. If an unknown constant process offset c is also
allowed, x_next=a*x+u+c+w, the transformation
  x'=x+delta, b'=b-delta, d'=d-delta, c'=c+(1-a)*delta
preserves every y,z and the dynamics. Absolute location then cannot be
identified without another anchor. With fixed c=0 and the specified zero-mean
noise law, this transformation changes the process-noise distribution, so it
does not establish nonidentifiability in the currently proposed model.

3. Retained bias correction equals direct fusion when a reference arrives

For h_new=(1-alpha)*h_old+alpha*(y-z), using xhat=y-h_new yields
  xhat=(1-alpha)*(y-h_old)+alpha*z.
On purchase steps this is an ordinary measurement-fusion rule; its distinctive
functional feature is retaining h for later ordinary-only steps. Equivalent
flat and nested implementations must agree in actions, forecasts, acquisitions,
and all numerical state given identical random inputs, including clipping.
Their semantic labels alone do not predict a behavioral difference.

Do not compute uncertainty at reference steps by adding R_y+Var(h_new):
h_new contains current eps and is correlated with y. If h_old-b has variance
P_h and is independent of fresh eps,eta, the estimation-error variance after
the current update (for d=0) is
  (1-alpha)^2*(P_h+R_y)+alpha^2*R_z.
At ordinary-only steps it is P_h+R_y under those assumptions. With d!=0,
unmodeled mean error is also present. A residual-adaptive alpha selected from
the current residual makes simple unconditional Gaussian covariance formulas
approximate; label its probabilities accordingly and evaluate calibration.

4. Fair contrasts

Keep the action law fixed across estimators, e.g. u=clip(-.8*xhat,-2,2).
Different control gains can otherwise confound estimation with control tuning.
Report raw y and switch-to-z-at-purchase as weak transparent baselines, but
include a dynamic no-reference joint estimator. A direct reference controller
that discards z immediately after acquisition is not the strongest simpler
alternative; an estimator can retain/predict x without a named nested layer.
Choose 'best simpler comparator' on development seeds only and freeze it.
If a separate comparator is chosen for each world, disclose that the evaluation
contrast is world-specific. Policies must not receive the world's identity,
actual b,d, the sign-switch time, or hidden state unless every arm is explicitly
given that same information as a separate oracle experiment.

Stage 1: all policies should have the same acquisition schedule and sensing
charge, whether they use or ignore z. Equal opportunities alone do not establish
equal acquired information or equal expense. Disaggregate purchased evidence,
retained sufficient state, update algorithm, and resulting control.
Stage 2: budgets are equal hard ceilings, not equal realized costs. Decide
whether to acquire using y and retained history before revealing current z.
A residual y-z cannot trigger the purchase that makes z visible. It may change
the update after purchase. The price, computation-price conversion, and any
practical improvement threshold are supplied design choices.

Pair exogenous draws x0,b0,w,eps,eta across policies. Once their actions differ,
their x,y,z values differ causally even when the disturbance draws are the same.
Do not replay one policy's realized y trajectory into another controlled plant.
Generate all disturbance arrays in advance so acquisition and computation
counts cannot alter the random stream. Initialize the Gaussian bias prior with
variance 2/3 if moment-matching the specified uniform {-1,0,1} prior; acknowledge
that Gaussian filtering is an approximation to the discrete prior. Do not use
a different zero-bias prior for the no-bias world without disclosing that
additional regime knowledge.

5. Forecasting and outcome timing

At step t: observe y; optionally buy z; update; choose u; record the forecast
for x_(t+1) conditional on available history and this chosen u; then realize
w_t and score against x_(t+1). For an approximately Gaussian current state
posterior N(xhat,P_x), the action-conditioned next mean/variance are
mu=a*xhat+u and V=a^2*P_x+Q, and
P(|x_next|>.5)=Phi((- .5-mu)/sqrt(V))+1-Phi((.5-mu)/sqrt(V)).
Check deterministic/zero-variance edges. A controller can drive its predicted
mean near zero, so a good event forecast requires honest uncertainty too.
For an estimator without a valid posterior, its Gaussian plug-in is a forecast
heuristic, not a true conditional distribution. Report proper scores (Brier,
and log score if probabilities are safeguarded), calibration/coverage and
prediction error separately. Avoid inferring calibration from one scalar score.
Record xhat error against x_t before the action. Declare whether cumulative
state loss sums t=0..59 or t=1..60 and use the same convention everywhere.

6. Change-world and statistical interpretation

The proposed switch at t=30 coincides with an offered reference. That is a
relatively favorable change-detection schedule. Preserve it as the planned
main world, and if feasible add an explicitly labeled nonaligned switch
sensitivity (e.g. t=32). Do not quietly change the main world after evaluation.
One third of sign-switch runs begin with b=0 and do not change. Report the full
world primary outcome, but assess recovery on |b0|=1 runs or explicitly mark
the others as no change. Recovery times need a frozen criterion and censoring
for runs failing to recover by the horizon. Avoid assigning zero recovery time
to no-change runs or dropping non-recoveries without disclosure.

Use a paired per-seed difference for each contrast. Give a mean paired
difference and uncertainty separately in all four declared worlds. Do not select
the favorable world after seeing evaluation results. A selected-best arm on the
held-out data invalidates an ordinary unadjusted pairwise interval for that
selection. Within-world intervals are not simultaneous confidence guarantees.
Seeds are simulation replicates under this supplied generator, not human cases.
Resource-adjusted losses need frozen weights and separate raw components.
Count modeled arithmetic, retained scalar state and realized measurements with
explicit conventions. Wall-clock microbenchmarks and Python object memory
are machine/implementation measurements, not universal cognitive costs.
Repeated reformulation may alter arithmetic or numerical rounding, but creates
no new evidence; call a repeated unchanged calculation a negative control only
after verifying no hidden state or information was added.

7. Suggested exact implementation checks

* Verify det(O)=.2 and noiseless reconstruction for multiple x,b,u.
* Verify the x,b,d rank and the unknown-offset gauge transformation algebra.
* Verify bias-update/fusion equality and Monte Carlo error variance at fixed alpha.
* Verify nested/flat trajectories and forecasts exactly or at justified floating
  tolerance for every world and both acquisition regimes.
* Verify purchase decisions do not read z, b,d,x or future disturbances.
* Verify no arm gets scenario labels, true switch time or hidden noise.
* Verify paired exogenous arrays are unaffected by policy RNG paths.
* Verify price/budget bookkeeping and pre-action forecast timing.
* Verify confidence-probability range and nonnegative covariance.
* Verify saturation uses the applied action in estimator prediction.

Overall judgment
The experiment can test benefits of retained calibration and adaptive updating
under supplied information, dynamics and costs. It cannot establish that another
semantic meta-level is uniquely necessary. The two most important safeguards
are a no-reference temporal estimator and explicit acknowledgment that biased
reference failure is conditional on the assumed anchoring and estimator model.

Implementation review: experiment.py, filter_model.py and protocol.json
Reviewed before development/evaluation on 2026-10-05. No implementation files
were edited by this auditor. The runner's common gain is .5 (rather than the .8
example used above); this is explicitly frozen in the protocol and shared by
all policies. It does not change observability or the measurement-fusion proof.

Sound features found by inspection:
* Ground truth, world labels and future observations remain outside Policy.
* All exogenous arrays are generated before policy execution and seed choices
  do not depend on acquisition or action histories.
* Purchase decisions precede reference-value construction. Past residuals may
  influence retained variances, but an unpurchased current residual is not read.
* The joint filter predicts using the preceding clipped, actually applied action.
* The simultaneous observation correction handles shared current epsilon in
  h_new and xhat; audit-selected gains remain explicitly approximate forecasts.
* Actions and forecasts are computed before the next physical noise/outcome.
* All matched policies acquire/pay for 12 packets, even ignored ones. Adaptive
  policies have a six-packet ceiling; gates continue to be charged after exhaustion.
* Gate, audit, repeated estimate and joint-update blocks are included in the
  declared abstract work accounting. They are not measured floating-point costs.
* Baseline and policy tuning use only development seeds and four primary worlds.
* The primary critical value corresponds to two-sided Bonferroni familywise
  95% coverage for eight normal-approximation intervals. Seed pairing is used.
* Zero-bias sign-switch episodes are excluded from recovery calculations.

Independent executable checks completed without using held-out seeds:
* Noiseless two-y joint-filter identification at three distinct x,b,u tuples:
  maximum estimate error approximately 3e-15.
* 400 policy runs = five development seeds {0,1,4,11,22}, five worlds, two
  acquisition regimes and eight policy implementations.
* Every matched run bought 12 packets; every adaptive run bought at most six.
* Actions stayed within +/-2, variances stayed nonnegative/positive where needed,
  and event probabilities stayed within [0,1].
* Replacing all unpurchased reference-noise entries with 1e6 left each entire
  trace unchanged. Purchased entries were preserved. This checks an important
  acquisition boundary beyond merely inspecting the method signature.
* Total loss equaled control loss plus sensing cost plus work cost.
* Nested and flat retained estimators had identical traces.
* Four repeated estimate calculations had identical traces to retained estimation
  and exactly .018 greater episode work cost under the supplied work price.
No violations were found in these checks.

Issues and caveats sent to the implementing researcher before freeze:
1. switch32 is the descriptive timing control, but its contrast label was still
   'primary' even though the code correctly used ordinary descriptive 95% bounds.
   Change the label or reporting to descriptive.
2. implementation_seconds times complete run() simulations and metric collection,
   not policy-only deliberation. Describe it as end-to-end simulation runtime.
3. PERSISTENT_SCALARS is analytical retained belief size, not measured peak memory.
   Policy objects additionally allocate unused alternate representations and
   bookkeeping. Do not claim those figures are actual process memory.
4. Recovery initially reported the start j+1 of a five-step successful window,
   while assigning unconfirmed cases delay L (remaining horizon). Start times
   after L-4 cannot be assessed. Prefer confirmed recovery at completion j+5,
   censored at L; alternatively censor onset at L-4 or explicitly present a capped
   heuristic score rather than ordinary time-to-event censoring.

The biased-reference result remains a misspecification experiment. The current
two-dimensional filter intentionally assumes d=0; its failure cannot establish
that estimating reference bias is impossible in the supplied anchored world.
