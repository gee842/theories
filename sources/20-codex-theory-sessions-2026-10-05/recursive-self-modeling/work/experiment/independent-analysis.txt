INTERPRETIVE FINDINGS (observed after freeze)

The original claim of conditional usefulness relative to a weak immediate comparator survives; a stronger claim that retention was the best frozen approach is rejected. joint_y_only retains its own joint state and bias, so it is not a no-memory or necessarily non-meta comparator.

These were two prespecified timing cases, not representative samples from a timing distribution. The mechanism includes unavailable residual checks between purchases and long gaps caused by the covariance-only gate.

Biased-reference joint filtering achieves better control than joint_y_only (1.020496 versus1.162361) while state-estimation MSE is similar (.024795 versus.024304; paired95% difference interval[-.000873,.001856]) and nominal90% coverage is much worse (.532433 versus.899283).
