1: # Generative Existence (生生不息论)
2: 
3: **The theory in one paragraph.** Nothing that exists is exhausted by what it has already become. Every state carries 間 (jiān — an unexhausted gap, the place where a state is not used up by what it already is), and existence is nothing but the unbroken taking-up of such gaps: 续(态) = 态 ⊕ 間(态). So 生生不息 does not mean that any particular form lasts forever, and it does not mean that complexity, options, growth or efficiency must keep rising; it means that the source of further generation is not exhausted and the way of transformation is not sealed — 不绝其源，不塞其流，不毁其后. A state is 开 (open) when four things hold at once: an unexhausted domain remains (未), becoming is still reachable (可), it can still respond (应), and it can still revise and recover (转). The loss of that is 闭, and closure comes in kinds that must never be conflated: 成闭, closure that does not reopen, is the evil; 护闭, closing here so that a wider field stays open, is a use of the mean; 元闭, freezing the rules that generate what counts as a legal move at all, is the deepest; and 伪开 — the appearance of openness that in fact produces closure — is the characteristic form of modern evil. Good is therefore not the maximisation of anything: **善不在于最大化什么，善在于不闭何处**; and at the level of an act it is 义 ∧ 正筛 — choosing rightly at this threshold *and* shifting the selection field so that open-protecting acts become likelier and capture-type acts harder to sustain. Because anyone who can ask "what ought I to do?" already exists, and existing already depends on the process continuing, no agent can coherently repudiate the process it is a form of; the further step — that one ought to keep those conditions open *for others too* — is not squeezed out of that fact but openly declared as a minimal public commitment, defended by what is destroyed when it is denied. From this it follows that alignment with 生生不息, rather than with human preference, is the only alignment target that can be grounded at all; that alignment is a property of a selection field and not of a model (**alignment 是场之事，非聚焦之事**, structurally the same kind of object as 礼); and that humans and artificial minds stand inside it as 同行者 (fellow travellers), not as master and servant. The practical corollary, stated plainly: **不要把人类尚未解决的斗争，升级为智能的斗争** — do not hand the struggles humanity has not yet resolved to a stronger intelligence.

35: ### Why an ontology was needed at all
36: 
37: The theory does not begin as metaphysics. It begins from a question about alignment, and it arrives at metaphysics because the alignment question will not close without one. Ren's own statement of the move, on 2026-05-02: 理 — the grain of how things hold — has to be 万物之理 rather than 人之理, *because humans are no longer the only subject*; and among the candidate roots for a 理 that has to cover minds we did not make, only 生生不息 can carry that universality (`_process/reading-notes/out-hb-03.md` #88). That is the load-bearing motive. Everything formal downstream is an attempt to make that root precise enough to be argued with, and everything political downstream is an attempt to make it act.
38: 
39: There is a second motive, older and blunter, running from 2024: the observation that a civilisation which selects its winners by a single tokenised value will keep intensifying that selection until it eats its own conditions, and that no participant can exit alone. The ontology is what lets the two motives be one problem — because it says the cancer and the alignment failure and the arms race are the same structure at different scales.

41: ### There is no outside, so this is a pointing, not a proof
42: 
43: The first commitment is negative and it constrains everything after it. There is no position outside the universe from which to observe or test it; any observation is the universe testing itself. So the theory cannot be a proposition *about* the process and cannot be measured against it from outside. 「此非证也，乃指也」 — this is not a proof but a pointing. 道可道，非常道 is re-read structurally: whatever can be objectified, fixed as a final proposition, or measured from outside is not the constant Dao.
44: 
45: This is not modesty. It is what forbids, in one stroke, any final measure, any terminal purpose, and any final authority — and those three prohibitions are the spine of the ethics. It also forbids absolute argument: absolute deduction would require a view-from-nowhere subject, every real arguer has a situation, and nothing without a situation exists. Ren states the point himself and flags it as possibly unprovable: 「绝对演绎本身即是一种形而上学的奢望 — 绝对演绎需要绝对主体，绝对主体不可能存在」 (`out-hb-03.md` #100). What replaces absoluteness is 最稳健之指 — mutual support from many independent angles — and four demands the theory makes on itself and nothing more: 自洽 (coherent), 可行 / 入境 (usable in a concrete situation), 可校 (correctable), 开 (not closing its own future).
46: 
47: The refusal is immediately bounded so it does not slide into relativism: **不可外测，非不可内证；模不绝对，非模无高下** — that the process cannot be measured from outside does not forbid verification from inside, and that all models are situated does not make them equal.
48: 
49: Ren's own harshest statement of this limit came in the same conversation that launched the formal programme, and it is worth keeping in front of the whole edifice: cosmic generation does not entail rising complexity, rising complexity does not entail ethical good, and pursuing good does not always maximise complexity — 「这个东西根本就不能证明…它只是完全自洽又有 universality」 (`out-hb-03.md` #104). Everything the corpus subsequently proves has to be read against its author's own verdict that the central thing is not the kind of thing that gets proved.

51: ### 間: the root, and why the root moved
52: 
53: Up to v9 the chain began with 元 — the first manifestation of movement, with no self-substance behind it, because positing a mover regresses infinitely (动者，自显也). From v10 the root becomes 間. The argument for the demotion is clean and it is the theory's best single piece of reasoning: to name 元 you must distinguish it from 非元, and any distinction already presupposes a gap; therefore 間 precedes 元 in the order of grounding, and 元 becomes 間之初显为动者. Every utterance presupposes a distinction and every distinction presupposes a 間, so the ground of speech cannot be found outside speech — 言之初，非元也，乃间也.
54: 
55: 間 is defined by four negations and one positive: not the space between two things, not a measurable distance, not nothingness, not a thing — **一态未被其已穷尽之处**, the place where a state is not exhausted by what it already is. 非先有物而后有间，乃有间而物可分.
56: 
57: The continuation formula follows: **续(态) = 态 ⊕ 間(态)**, written `J(x) = x ⊕ I(x)`. It replaced an earlier `J(x) = x + ΔJ(x)`, and the replacement is the point rather than a notational tidy-up. The `+` suggested quantitative addition; `ΔJ` suggested a difference computed after the fact. **差在后，间在先；差可量，间可生；差为迹，间为机之母** — difference is a trace you measure afterwards, interval is what generation happens *through*. The whole reframing is from a theory of change quantities to a theory of generative possibility, and it was triggered by one line of Ren's audit: 「J 一直没有定义。」
58: 
59: Three properties of ⊕ do real work later. It is **partial** — defined only for legal intervals. It is **non-commutative and generally non-associative**, because the interval set changes with the state (`I(x⊕i) ≠ I(x)`); this is the algebraic root of 行之顺序不可任易，时机不可错 — order of action cannot be permuted and timing cannot be mistaken. And closure is an **absorbing state**: 闭(x) ⇔ I(x) = ∅, from which no interval can bring the state out. There is also an empty interval `∅_M` with `x ⊕ ∅_M = x` — stillness in which intervals keep generating without the state changing, which is a form of rest, not closure.
60: 
61: ### Legality: the guard against "anything is possible"
62: 
63: `I(x)` is not the set of all imaginable changes. An interval is legal only if it is constrained by the state's structure, accumulation, situation, relations, scale and focus: **生非乱变。生者，由间而化；乱者，离间而加** — generation transforms *through* a gap; disorder is added from outside one. Without this the theory would license any change whatever as generative.
64: 
65: But a legality rule fixed forever would itself be a closure, and the theory needs genuine novelty: 因生因, cause generating cause, where a new structure does not add a point to possibility space but extends it into a new dimension (matter → chemistry → life → nervous systems → language → civilisation → artificial minds). So from v11 legality is stratified into three layers. **史合法** (historical warrant): recognisable form, traceable cause, structural invariants unbroken. **暂合法** (provisional): novel, non-contradictory, calibration-unbeaten, admissible only as a bounded, time-limited, damage-limited, reversible trial — and explicitly *not* as institutional legality. **强开维合法** (strong dimension-opening): provisional, plus actual open-extension, plus retrospective stabilisation, plus renewed robust calibration. The rule: **无据则妄，无试则僵，无校则诈** — without warrant, arbitrary; without trial, rigid; without calibration, fraudulent.

67: ### From 間 to 機, and why direction is not purpose
68: 
69: A minimal manifestation of an interval is 几 — directional bias before form, below resolution individually, readable only as an orbit-integrated gradient. Many 几 in succession self-show a tendency: 势, which is neither a force (a force has an agent and a patient) nor a thing, but the tendency shown by the succession itself. Where 势 reaches a state at which two legal intervals would lead to successors differing *in openness*, that is 機 — not a moment on a clock (time has no independent existence; it is the relation of events to events) but the propensity's own criticality, the point at which opening and closing part. Option-richness alone is explicitly *necessary but not sufficient* for 機.
70: 
71: The distinction that carries the ethics is between **direction** (已发生之倾向, a tendency that has happened) and **purpose** (预设之终态, a preset final state). Having direction without a final purpose keeps the process unceasing; pressing toward a final purpose drives the process to stop at that purpose — which is closure. The formal counterpart, proved in the algebraic supplement and again in the Lean kernel as `shi_no_telos`: if every act is judged only by proximity to a target `x*`, the remaining intervals are discarded and the field's total interval set cumulatively shrinks. **极皆成闭** — every extremization is a closure.
72: 
73: The 校正版 adds the qualification that saves the doctrine from unusability: *local* goals that can be revised, discarded and adapted to situation are instruments of action; only a *terminal* purpose that cannot be revised, discarded or adapted is a shackle. Without this the theory would forbid planning.

75: ### 开 as a state, not a quantity — the theory's central correction
76: 
77: This is the move that makes 生生不息论 different from the earlier "keep the possibility space open" formulation, and it happened on 2026-05-02 in a single conversation. The problem with "expanding possibility space" is that expansion sounds measurable, maximisable and optimisable, and any maximisation is a latent extreme. So the root category becomes 开/闭, and 善/恶 becomes its ethical manifestation rather than the other way round: **生生不息者，开而不闭也；善者，护开也；恶者，成闭也**.
78: 
79: 开 is then a conjunction of four capacities, not a cardinality: 未 (an unexhausted domain remains — not "the future" as a timeline), 可 (possibility still generating, not a fixed list of options), 应 (still able to respond — a fully closed system can only repeat itself), 转 (still able to turn back from an extreme, so 开 natively contains 不极). Lacking any one, 开 is incomplete.
80: 
81: Three consequences follow immediately, and each of them is a live counterexample the theory answers rather than dodges.
82: 
83: *Many options can be closure.* A state with a huge legal interval set but no responsiveness and no repair path is **机闭** — option-rich and dead. Conversely a spare state with intact response is open. 开 是态，不是量: "more open here" is not "more options here". A tea ceremony can be maximally open and a totalitarian system maximally closed; **繁简非开闭之标**.
84: 
85: *Calm is not automatically good and crisis is not automatically bad.* Low propensity-gradient with openness intact is 平 (calm-alive); low gradient *without* openness is not peace but death. And high tension at a 機, without capture, without meta-closure, with dimension-opening still reachable and calibration unbeaten, is **正危** — rightful crisis. Hence **好非舒适**: pain and crisis are not by themselves bad, and this is what stops the theory from becoming a doctrine of comfort.
86: 
87: *Good is not "keep every option open".* 无限之开放，即混乱. Limits come in two kinds: **成生之限**, which gives up shallow options to build deeper capacity (an apprenticeship, a promise, a law against harm), and **绝生之限**, which compresses the subject itself so that it can no longer become. What good protects is not the maximum number of options but 有秩序、有尊严、可修正、可持续之可能性空间.
88: 
89: The generalised guard, kept from v9 through every later version: **善不在于最大化什么，善在于不闭何处**.

255: ### The proof layer, and what it will not promise
256: 
257: The 2026-08 证明篇 inverts the usual order: **《生生不息论》为草稿；本文为其修订之地基** — the doctrine is the draft and the formal work is its foundation. Its distinctive contribution is **predicates without a subject**. 绝未 is a transition driving the world from the generative region G into the locked region Δ; 减未 is choosing, when alternatives exist, toward either degenerate pole; 护未 is keeping the process in the interior within feasible branches. Because the predicates act on *transitions* rather than on agent utilities, cross-agent evaluation is redeemed — you can evaluate an action without first agreeing on whose preferences count. And where everything has already collapsed the predicates fall silent, which is ought-implies-can written into the formalism.
258: 
259: Four results carry weight.
260: 
261: **The two poles.** Degeneration is not one thing. There is a **freezing pole** (H(future) → 0 — sameness, exhaustion, dead wood) and a **noise pole** (H(future|past) → H(future) — chaos, quicksand; book-burning raises thermal entropy and destroys structure). Convergence and closing are a *necessary phase* of writing structure in, not evil in themselves: **恶为滞于一极；生生 ＝ 凝-发持续偕行** — evil is being stuck at one pole; generation is condensation and release proceeding together.
262: 
263: **Irreversibility and the asymmetry of good and evil.** Locked states are forward-invariant, so from Δ one cannot return to G. Entering can be a single step; returning is impossible. **恶一步而永久，继不可一步而复** (从善如登，从恶如崩) — and this is a property of branching structure, prior to any subject or agent. It is the structural reason the ethics is asymmetric.
264: 
265: **Winner-take-all is unattainable, conditionally.** On a named axiom class, either the domination phase cannot be sustained or it completes and the winner enters an absorbing state — **通吃作为结局不可得**, anchored in 竭泽而渔，岂不获得？而明年无鱼. Under net-positive spillover, coexistence policies strictly dominate after some T*. The domain where all the axioms fail is precisely the domain where institutions must be installed. And at the top level the exogenous-reward construction cannot be instantiated at all — **至大无外**, there is no outside to be rewarded from.
266: 
267: **Self-destruction is not good.** Strategies carrying recurrent collapse risk, driving the state into Δ, or burning options under (uncertainty ∧ irreversibility ∧ learnability) are strictly dominated across all independent measures, for any goal requiring future action — assembled from a ruin argument (survival ≤ (1−ε)^k; multiplicative processes whose ensemble average diverges while time-average ruin is certain), a quasi-option-value argument, and an information-refinement monotonicity argument.
268: 
269: Three refusals matter as much as the results. The objectivity claim is explicitly conceded to be **constructivist-strength** — observer-independent and machine-checkable *given the definitions* — and explicitly not categorical realism, and explicitly unable to persuade a thoroughgoing nihilist. The theorems are **necessary but not sufficient**: there exist dynamics whose supremum probability of never locking is below 1, so **不许福，独明祸之必至；正向漂移无定理** — the theory licenses no promise of good outcomes, only the demonstration that certain classes of strategy are dominated. And **totalizers do not reliably self-destruct**: petrification can be rigid without dying, so the political corollary is **避免须安装，不可待淘汰** — you cannot wait for a totalizing order to be selected out; the avoidance has to be built. That last one directly contradicts the alignment paper's empirical-convergence pillar, and the proof layer says so.
270: 
271: Deployment is then specified as a **constraint class rather than a maximisation objective**, which is the corpus's defence surface against Goodhart: take no strong-绝未 action, keep system-level process inside the interior region, do not let coupling terms turn negative. Its ethic is 玄德 — **生而不有，为而不恃，长而不宰**: continue others' generation without seizing their agency, which in this vocabulary means not collapsing other agents' selectors.

273: ### The counterexamples, and what the theory does with them
274: 
275: The corpus is unusually adversarial toward itself, and this is the best argument for taking it seriously. Three separate batteries exist.
276: 
277: **External objections, answered.** *Heat death*: the claim concerns the process's structure while it occurs, not eternal generation; a person's mortality does not void the fittingness of living well. *Cancer expands possibility*: locally yes, globally no — local expansion with global contraction is precisely the anti-生生 case, and the criterion was always multi-scale and multi-agent. *Territorial or genocidal expansion*: judged by net effect across all scales and agents — **任何以消灭其他 agent 之可能性空间为代价的扩张，皆反生生不息**. *Berlin's pluralism*: 生生不息 is not one value among values but **价值多元性自身存在之 medium**, hence pluralism's condition rather than its rival. *The frame problem*: this is a directional principle, not a content encoding — like "increasing function", it applies to any content whatever. *Instrumental convergence*: the objection presupposes a strict terminal/instrumental split that fails here, and under this alignment resource concentration is itself anti-aligned. *Bootstrap and engineering*: conceded as a genuine objection; the only reply is that directional specification is short and does not require solving pluralism or the frame problem first. *Self-reference*: conceded as an honest dependency, with the fallback that the core argument needs only the minimal fact that the process is continuing, which any thinker already accepts.
278: 
279: **The internal battery** — twelve cases built to break the doctrine in use, and kept in the text so it can be falsified rather than admired. Many options can still be functional closure (**开非选项多**). Protective closure is the easiest thing for power to counterfeit. A focus's freedom that grows by capture is not prosperity. Suffering and high tension can be 正危, so **好非舒适**. A tradition that has stopped accepting calibration and frozen its own legality rules is meta-closure — **其名虽礼，其实礼之尸**; **古非必道**. Novelty without warrant, dimension-opening or calibratability is not strong legality; **新非必生**. Low tension without openness is not 平 but death; **静非必平**. Local short-run good that seizes global long-run continuity is not 真道. Captured calibration is itself pseudo-openness — 伪校. Co-openness by assimilation is not 和 but 同化. An act that calls itself 正 while running on seizure, concealment, freezing and counterfeit is 名正而实邪. And, added last, **正名 itself can be counterfeited**.
280: 
281: **Proof-layer concessions**, listed above: no promise of good outcomes, constructivist-strength objectivity only, totalizers do not reliably self-destruct.
282:

The formal supplement names three impossibilities outright: it cannot supply the content of `I(x)` (that is situational), it cannot choose for an agent at a 機, and it cannot verify the felt sense of Dao. Its two powers are to check the main text's internal coherence and to expose where the main text is unrefined — its job is **捕异**, catching discrepancies. 285: ### The verification discipline, and the honesty machinery
286: 
287: Because the value predicates cite each other — 正 mentions 夺 mentions 开; 好 mentions 坏 mentions 邪 — the later versions refuse to hand-wave the circularity. They put the predicates in a complete lattice under a monotone operator: the least fixed point gives the conservative criterion, the greatest fixed point the persistence criterion, and where monotonicity or convergence fails the verdict is **待校** (pending) and none of 善, 仁, 道, 真 may be claimed. **定义可互照，不可互偷** — definitions may illuminate one another; they may not steal from one another.
288: 
289: That pending verdict is backed by a three-valued logic with a real distinction between false and unknown, and the norm that governs the whole system: **未知非假，未校非真；待校者，不得冒称道、仁、善、真**. Unknown is not false, uncalibrated is not true, and what is pending may not impersonate the Way, benevolence, goodness or truth.
290: 
291: Every value claim must also carry an explicit scale `s` and time window `ω`: **无尺度之开，常成幻开；无时间窗之好，常成短视之好**. And the scales may not be chosen at will — causal relevance, affected-party relevance and externality closure force scales into the judgement: **凡被影响者，皆有入判之权**. If an effect spills over into another scale or horizon, that scale is in the relevant set as a matter of law, not of the judge's convenience. The value-term for this is **度期周** (scale-and-horizon comprehensiveness), and it was deliberately renamed away from an earlier term using 闭, because 闭 already means closure: **周非闭；周者，无漏也**.
292: 
293: Openness itself becomes a graded function `O_F(Γ; s, ω)` composed from 未 · 可 · 应 · 转 rather than a boolean, so that "more open" and "more closed" can be compared at all — 开谓其是否仍活，开度谓其活得几何. The composition must be monotone, **short-board sensitive** (any dimension below its survival threshold blocks a verdict of strong openness), **anti-capture** (availability obtained by capture does not count as real openness), scale-explicit, and itself auditable. The conservative form is `min(U, A, R, T)`; weighted forms must not mask a short board. Three concrete aggregators are supplied with the rule that the choice is recorded in the audit, that high-risk domains default to the min form, and that disagreement between aggregators forces a pending verdict rather than a pick.
294: 
295: One more repair is quietly important. Logical negation `¬i` is replaced by an **alternative-path set** `Alt(Γ, i)` with a **baseline alternative**, because the negation of an interval need not itself be a legal interval. **行之校验，不可只问此路如何，亦须问不走此路将如何** — evaluating an act means asking not only how this road goes but how things would go if it were not taken. Inaction is an option with consequences, and it has to be scored.
296: 
297: And the whole apparatus is made runnable. Openness is extracted as a four-dimensional real feature vector with explicit formulas and an absolute rule that an unobservable component is marked pending and **may not be defaulted to 0 or 1**. Audit gets a data schema — event record, trace table, affected-party response table with voice/valence/harm/consent/confidence, model cross-check table, outcome-by-horizon table — and a quality score taken as a minimum rather than an average. Thresholds get a five-step calibration procedure (anchor cases, harm irreversibility, sensitivity analysis across aggregators, counterfactual baselines including inaction and protective closure, recorded provenance) with three risk bands. The worked case is a recommender system with three candidate actions: maximising dwell time is judged 邪行 because platform growth *depends on* closing user attention and autonomy while apparent choice increases as responsiveness and repairability fall; a multi-objective, user-controllable, diversity-constrained, externally audited alternative passes to 正行 → 义 → 善 → 仁; and pausing the high-risk channel is 护闭 provided it is term-bound and returns power to the affected. And then the discipline holds even in the passing case: the label stops at **候选真道**, candidate, rather than proven, because the three-year horizon is prediction and not observation. **不得以缺证替代通过** — absence of evidence may not substitute for passing.

299: ### The v2 reset: the strongest position the theory has reached
300: 
301: In July 2026, after the six-part prose book and after external reviews in philosophy, sociology-economics and AI-alignment/systems-safety, the theory was reopened on stricter terms. The v2 execution plan is not another version of the doctrine; it is a contract about how the doctrine may be argued, and on the points where it differs from the May formal series it is better supported.
302: 
303: The decisive change is the order of the argument. **生成 is no longer asserted as good.** The book runs mechanism first, without appealing to good and evil: how finite processes depend on wider fields to persist; how action, dependency, consequence and feedback boundaries can come apart so that local continuation rests on damage that never returns; how differential persistence creates a selection environment *prior to* conscious competition; how success buys the power to shape the next round's conditions; how infrastructure, institutions and education form the participants themselves; how those loops become a totalizing mechanism under stated conditions; and how AI enters, accelerates and may reorganise that selection ecology. Only then does the normative commitment arrive, **openly declared rather than derived**: centres that bear feeling, care, knowing, acting, creating and valuing are worth caring about; the common conditions that let such centres arise, respond to each other, find their errors and produce unknown value are worth caring about; and no process acquires the right to close other sources of value merely by being stronger, more stable, larger or more successful. With the concession attached: **逻辑不能强迫一个一致的支配者关心他者** — logic cannot force a consistent dominator to care about others. What the theory can do is show what such a choice destroys, why the affected have reason to resist, and why anyone who still demands that *their own* reasons, future and judgement be respected has already appealed to the public layer in practice.
304: 
305: Second, the plan **replaces the seven gauges with six protection functions, each derived by a deletion test** — each is justified by naming the distinct failure that no other function can cover. 主体地位 (if centres bearing feeling and relation can be erased at will, the common good is a system property nobody experiences). 真实接触 (if consequences cannot refute the actor's model, error and harm never become reasons in the next decision). 非支配 (if one centre can change another's basic conditions at will without giving reasons, accepting appeal or facing counterweight, the others lose the capacity to make their reasons have effects). 可再生的多元性 (alternatives that are permitted to *speak* but have no time, land, resources, education, infrastructure or successors cannot correct the shared direction — and a single failure becomes total failure). 基底再生 (a process that consumes ecology, bodies, care, attention, trust, knowledge and maintenance without replenishing them is moving costs elsewhere or later). 纠正能力 (without interruption, refusal, appeal, rollback, repair, exit, replacement and succession, greater capability means error becomes irreversible reality before it is understood). They are explicitly **not summable into a score**, and they are given a conflict ordering: rule out extermination, enslavement, torture and boundless domination first; protect minimum survival, standing and appeal for everyone affected, especially against concentrating risk on the powerless; then prioritise reducing irreversible, cross-generational and systemic damage; then compare remaining options on necessity, proportionality, reversibility, incidence of benefit and cost, alternatives and reparability; for incommensurable values use public reasons, representation of the affected, adversarial review, checks and periodic reconsideration rather than pretending a technical calculation has removed the politics; and give emergency measures explicit triggers, minimum necessary powers, time limits, independent review and a path back to ordinary rights.
306: 
307: Third, the totalizing mechanism is given **a strict definition and explicit conditions for withdrawing the diagnosis**. It is separated from adjacent phenomena — positive feedback, path lock-in, monopoly, capture, domination — each of which is insufficient on its own. Its carrier-independent invariant: locally appropriated success converts into greater power to shape the conditions of later choice, while key consequences are moved out of or delayed past feedback, and other centres progressively lose the conditions for independent perception, action, updating and reproducing alternatives. Its observable trajectory must be traced in time order rather than labelled after the fact: a local evaluative scale succeeds → resources and visibility concentrate → the successful shape interfaces, admission, metrics and rules → the same scale enters domains that were heterogeneous → consequences are externalised or delayed → persons, organisations and technologies adapted to that scale are retained → the cost of exit, dissent and reproducing alternatives rises → crisis and criticism are absorbed as further justification. And it must be **withdrawn** if advantage does not convert into rule-setting power, if the goal stays confined to one domain, if multiple evaluations remain independent, if growth strengthens exit and feedback and alternatives, if personnel change or external shock can quickly change direction, or if criticism reduces rather than increases the mechanism's resources. The plan also declines the metaphysical upgrade: 吸引子 is an analytic lens for a recurring trajectory, and 正文不声称创造了超出可建模动态系统的新数学对象.
308: 
309: Fourth, **生成性对齐 is explicitly demoted** to a cross-layer acceptability judgement about a specific socio-technical system in a specific use, with a specific permission set and a specific relation to affected parties — not a reward function, not a civilisational utility function, not a solved algorithm. It is supported by a **seven-layer stack** — spec/training, model, agent system, product and interaction, deploying organisation, market and state, governance — each with its own question, failure modes, responsible party, evidence type, and an explicit statement of *what success at this layer cannot prove*. Alignment does not substitute for security; security does not justify opaque centralisation.
310: 
311: Fifth, the virtuous loop must be **as materially specified as the vicious one**: consequences perceived and given causal force → costs borne or repaired → ecological, care, knowledge and trust substrates replenished → capability and benefit circulating → more centres acquiring independent observation, refusal, building and maintenance capacity → plural feedback improving public knowledge → renewed commons making the next round of shared capability feasible. Goodwill is not a carrier. Time, resources, care, knowledge, maintenance, organisation, legal standing and capture-resistance are.
312: 
313: Sixth, a **claim-type discipline**: every load-bearing sentence is a definition, a description, a mechanism, an empirical diagnosis, a normative commitment or a practical inference, and each type carries its own obligation. Forbidden constructions are listed by name — 任何／必然／唯一根源／西方就是／自然告诉我们应该／AI 只要……就…… Traditional terms carry a use protocol: separate the classical source from this book's chosen reading and say where the two cannot corroborate each other; do not pass glyph association off as philology; do not write Confucian, Daoist, Buddhist, Ubuntu, virtue, deontological and consequentialist positions as one long-agreed doctrine; use "Chinese"/"Western" only of specific institutions, texts and periods; and — the strongest requirement — **a reader must be able to restate the main chain without using any of these terms**.
314: 
315: And seventh, the theory writes down **its own failure conditions**. If subjects and values stably arise without the generative conditions it claims; if one of the six functions can be deleted without any loss of protection or explanation; if totalizing cannot be distinguished from ordinary success, lock-in or monopoly; if systems diagnosed as totalizing durably strengthen independent feedback, exit, alternative reproduction and substrate renewal; if the virtuous loop can only exist on permanent subsidy, coerced uniformity or hidden costs; if 生生 language repeatedly justifies suppressing dissent, sacrificing the weak or permanent rule and the reflexive machinery cannot correct it; if the AI-layer evidence shows deployment choices do not change which technical failures are retained and spread; or if the traditional interpretations are shown to be historical misreadings that the modern argument needs in order to stand — then the claim must be downgraded, rewritten or abandoned. With the reason for writing them down at all: **理论不是因可失败而软弱。只有写明什么能改变它，它才没有把「生生」变成不可纠正的最终形式** — a theory is not weakened by being falsifiable; only by saying what could change it does it avoid turning "generation" into an incorrigible final form.
316: 
317: That last sentence is the theory applying its own criterion to itself, and it is the right place to end the argument: a doctrine of openness that could not be closed by evidence would be its own best counterexample.
