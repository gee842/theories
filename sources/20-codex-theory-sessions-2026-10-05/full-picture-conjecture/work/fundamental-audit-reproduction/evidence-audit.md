# Evidence audit: do we have a fundamental theory?

5 October 2026. Independent audit of the actual local reports, frozen protocol, implementation, calculations, and claim register. No frozen artifact was modified. This note evaluates what the existing work establishes; it does not adjudicate physics history or assert a new empirical result.

## Finding

**We do not yet have evidence for a complete root theory in the sense of a compact, independently validated dynamical account that unifies the target domains. We have a defensible formal framework, several exact conditional constraints, and checked model-specific consequences.** The strongest coherent result is a theory of *when a description preserves attainable correction under a declared family of future mechanisms*. That is substantive. It is not equivalent to having identified the governing dynamics of becoming, cognition, institutions, selection, and ethical alignment.

The decisive distinction is visible in the code. The core `M=(Z,A,F,h)` supplies places for states, actions, dynamics, and observation, but leaves `F` and `h` arbitrary. In the concrete study, `Config` and `production()` separately supply the damage probabilities, stock law, diagnostic reliability, fault evolution, income, and repair technology. The optimizer derives consequences of those supplied laws. The common core does not derive them or constrain their cross-domain values beyond the added assumptions.

This is not criticism merely because the model needs initial conditions or domain-specific parameters; any useful dynamical theory can need those. The issue is that the substantive dynamic mechanisms are currently chosen separately, and there is no completed cross-implementation test showing that the common formulation predicts them, compresses them, or predicts integrated outcomes more successfully than the established control/decision models already containing those mechanisms.

If “fundamental” means a general conditional mathematical restriction—such as prefix affordability or the impossibility of distinguishing identical observation laws—some results qualify. If it means a complete account of the actual causal mechanisms and legitimate ends of all the intended domains, the current evidence does not. The reports themselves mostly preserve this distinction correctly.

## Concrete constitutive commitments in the implemented study

The access model uses a particularly definite family, visible in `work/access-study/model.py`:

- Stock is capped at three, zero is absorbing, and its update is `clip(x+1+repair−2·damage,0,3)`.
- Current fault follows supplied onset `.1` and clearance `.05`; damage probability is `.1` if healthy and `.8` if faulty.
- Repair always clears the present fault and adds one stock increment. It is available directly without diagnosis.
- Output is exactly twice next stock and arrives after every current bill. The natural productive increment and technology are supplied; “endogenous revenue” does not mean physical causal closure.
- Readiness has a known one-round rebuild lag. Tests have fixed sensitivity/specificity `.9`, and the information filter knows those likelihoods.
- Access is fixed as barred, immediate, or a known one-step countdown. There is no causal account of authority formation, capture, bargaining, rule selection, or compliance.
- A sufficient posterior is maintained without memory cost. The action grammar, hidden-state family, response technology, evaluation coordinates and weights remain fixed.

These are precisely the mechanisms generating the numerical consequences. Calling them instances of a common transformation structure does not explain why these laws rather than other laws hold. Replacing them freely after each failed application would retain expressiveness while losing predictive constraint.

The larger conceptual model can include policy/model/evaluator *carriers* in state. That is a legitimate construction showing self-inclusion is possible. The access experiment does not implement unrestricted revision of those carriers or its own state grammar. Its exact optimizer is an analytical benchmark, not a model of how a resource-limited institution discovers or executes the optimum.

## Predictions and tests: the distinctions that matter here

The main protocol was locally frozen before its numerical grid. That prevents silently changing inputs to improve the main outcome. It was not external preregistration, and the central H2 witness had already been analytically derived. The H4 counts and controls are prospective calculations conditional on the frozen kernel, not independent outcomes generated by a separate real process.

Several predictions are near-direct consequences of construction: immediate-access partitions are explicitly normalized to the same state; weak improvement follows when a policy class contains an emulation of the old class; the no-fault/no-damage control has no modeled need to repair. Those are useful implementation checks. They should not be counted as independent corroborations of a newly discovered causal law.

The diagnosis nonuse was an informative limitation because it showed the chosen integration did not need an available subsystem. Its exploratory reversal under a different evaluation is equally informative: the equations do not determine a unique “corrective” direction independent of the criterion. The common expense ceiling does not make actual spending equal; the no-test optimum spends.6 and the test policy4.5. The separate .9/.9 versus .5/.5 sensor comparison holds the test-positive prescription and expected4.5 expense fixed and isolates a modeled information effect.

The inherited record contains genuine computational surprises and corrections. It retains a failed predicted escape in a two-frame system (U12), a corrected nonhyperbolic invasion join (U09), and a failed earlier authority pilot (H02). These constrain specific claims and strengthen the audit trail. They are not evidence that the umbrella theory has survived a representative empirical falsification program. The inherited S01's256 held-out seed clusters are materially stronger generalization evidence for that bounded learner than selecting examples after seeing them, but still do not test transport beyond the supplied generator.

Successful packaged reproduction demonstrates executable provenance and stable calculation. Independent latent-event implementations and exact optimization checks reduce the risk of coding mistakes. Neither is independent confirmation of the shared physical, social or ethical assumptions. Arithmetic universality over indefinitely many instances is not the same proposition as empirical universality across all real systems.

## Strong conventional explanations remain sufficient

The best rival account currently explains the results without a new fundamental law:

1. **Resource-constrained partially observed control:** all finite access outcomes follow from the supplied transition/observation kernel, branchwise financing, and dynamic programming.
2. **Sufficiency, aggregation and controller refinement:** adequate summaries preserve selected transition/decision structure; hidden access or temporal distinctions break them; normalization restores the declared interface under its assumptions.
3. **Statistical decision theory:** richer information can help under one loss/burden criterion and have no net value under another. The original seven probes missing the diagnostic point is an ordinary finite-frontier issue, not a contradiction needing new dynamics.
4. **Ruin and accounting:** bounded accessible buffers with recurrent shortfall, unpaid restart, and positive ongoing expense can fail despite positive uncapped returns. No novel ethical or evolutionary force is needed.
5. **Conditional probability:** the proposed two-gate factorization is an ordinary multiplicative conditional structure once its no-alternative-route and modularity assumptions hold.

These are not mutually exclusive external competitors; they are the frameworks actually used in the proofs and programs. A new integration must demonstrate additional explanatory compression, useful transported restrictions, or a hard-to-obtain cross-mechanism prediction. Beating a model that deliberately omits cash access is insufficient: a standard control model with the same measured access constraint predicts that effect too.

The primary-source comparisons in the synthesis establish limited interpretive connections, with disagreements retained. They are not independent datasets showing that traditions converge on the same latent formal object. Similar vocabulary cannot supply the missing transport evidence.

## Overclaims to reject if “fundamental” is strengthened

The written reports generally **do not** make these overclaims. They would arise from strengthening the interpretation beyond the documented record:

- **“The root equations have been found.”** No common closed dynamic law derives the separately supplied production, damage, selection, observation, authority or evaluative mechanisms. `F` is a placeholder until those laws are provided.
- **“The full picture is proved because pictures are included.”** Including a representation's carrier and operations does not establish correct semantics, exhaust all relevant variables, price the observer's resources, or justify its standard.
- **“The law covers infinitely many cases, so completeness is established.”** The pooling theorem quantifies over a stipulated class and every finite horizon. That is real mathematical scope, not a proof that all intended domains satisfy its assumptions.
- **“The experiments verified a root account of inquiry or selection.”** The access experiment has no selected paid tests, and it contains no competition/reproduction process selecting its policies or institutions.
- **“A funding–enactment product law is supported by this repair experiment.”** The current model permits direct repair; its selected main policies all use no diagnosis. The strict two-gate formula's assumption that recovery requires a recommendation is not satisfied here.
- **“More accessible options must improve actual or ethical outcomes.”** The set-inclusion theorem concerns best achievable scalar values under emulation. Actual policies, distributions, legitimacy and burden can behave differently.
- **“A complete common score follows from causal embedding.”** The same kernel supports different evaluative orderings, and the follow-up diagnostic result concretely changes with the supplied criterion. No moral conclusion is entailed by causal realization alone.

The absence of a Maxwell-like complete system does not mean the only achievement is loose metaphor. Exact abstraction/refinement contracts, action-relevant information limits, prefix funding constraints, and counterexamples to inappropriate aggregation are genuine structural results. Their maturity should be described accurately: general conditional mathematics plus a developing applied research program.

## Strongest defensible claim

> For declared controlled processes, the attainable prospects for a specified correction depend on jointly preserving action-relevant information, payment access and timing, implementable responses, and the protected outcome. We can prove when particular abstractions preserve those prospects and exhibit exact failures when an omitted distinction becomes causally relevant under an admitted future mechanism. Several such results are independently checked. Whether a compact common account predicts correction across independently measured biological, technical, and institutional implementations remains an untested transport hypothesis.

This claim links the exact access result, inherited model-relative sufficiency, observation limits, and renewal constraints without asserting that they reduce to a single discovered empirical force. It also separates feasibility of legitimate correction from the legitimacy that still needs argument.

## Decisive next tests

### 1. Freeze a target and challenge the proposed state, not merely its code

The most useful immediate target is the independently specified task-processing service already mentioned in the research record. It should have queues/deadlines, paid diagnosis, restarts that interrupt work, earned operating resources, and observed authorization. Its physical transition logic must be specified independently of the reservoir model, rather than copying that table with new names.

Before integrated target outcomes are exposed, freeze the candidate mapping and the following measurements from local calibration trials: service/arrival dynamics, fault effects, observation likelihoods, payment timing, restart duration and interruption cost, and authorization delay. Declare which variables the proposed shared description retains. A serious rival is an ordinary queueing/control model with exactly the same calibration access and parameter budget; a current-output/total-money baseline is only a weak secondary comparator.

Use matched initial output/resources with different queue backlog or pending work. If the proposed state omits a quantity that changes post-restart losses or deadline feasibility, the same projected state should yield different outcomes under the same admissible intervention. That verified difference rejects the frozen abstraction. A refined state can be a new hypothesis, but must be evaluated on untouched target conditions. Do not reinterpret the discovery of a missing variable as the original account's successful prediction unless it forecast that missing-variable effect beforehand.

The target should predict four intervention cells, recovery times and deadline failure using independently calibrated local quantities. Do not fit the integrated recovery probabilities and then report them as a transfer. Freeze tolerances and a seed-cluster uncertainty procedure first; compare both predictive error and calibration burden against the serious conventional model. Equal performance at equal assumptions supports useful organization of existing theory. Equal or better prediction with fewer independent mechanisms across targets would support a stronger integration claim. Failure is evidence against the registered mapping/model, not grounds for adding another free law while preserving the original success label.

A second simulator remains a synthetic transport test. After success there, an independently measured physical or institutional application is still required for empirical generality.

### 2. Test the two-gate factorization only where its mechanisms are justified

`outputs/next-test.md` proposes `R_fa=p_f q_a`. With positive probabilities this imposes, among other restrictions, `R_00 R_11=R_01 R_10`. The equality is a testable consequence of the restricted rank-one probability table; it is not a new foundational law. Independently calibrated `p_f` and `q_a` make the prediction stronger than fitting that table after the fact.

The current access model cannot validate this subclaim because direct repair bypasses the diagnostic gate. A new target must document a real reason that a timely valid recommendation is necessary, or include the direct-repair route in a different frozen equation. Merely prohibiting direct repair to make the product formula true would test an imposed architecture, not discover an institutional law. Likewise, finance can change which recommendations are produced and their quality; representative conditional calibration of enactment cannot silently hold those changes fixed.

If the factorization survives an independently specified target with the required modularity, that is scoped transfer of a useful conditional mechanism. It still does not supply the dynamic laws of unconceived model formation, conflicting purposes, evaluator succession, or ethics. Those require separately discriminating questions rather than attaching new labels to the product.

## What would warrant upgrading the claim

A stronger “fundamental theory” interpretation would need an explicit common law that restricts dynamics beyond allowing arbitrary state transitions; independently grounded measurement/interpretation maps; failed as well as successful domain applications; prospective integrated predictions against strong standard comparators; and a clear account of what remains irreducible or normative. The current work has begun the map/proof discipline. It has not yet completed the decisive transport step, much less established ontological or ethical completeness.
