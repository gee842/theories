# Fundamental theory audit evidence

Investigation5October2026. Companion to fundamental-theory-audit.md in the delivered outputs. The assessment distinguishes philosophical grounding, registry completeness, faithful formal representation, and empirical dynamical unification. It does not claim a full corpus audit, a new physical experiment, or an independent Lean rebuild. Reference repositories were read only.

Contents:
- formal-audit.md: four-root audit with exact source anchors, strongest representation results and their scope.
- evidence-audit.md: supplied assumptions versus derived/checked consequences in the research record.
- formal_countermodels.py/json: exact finite countermodels and saved verification.
- inspected-source-manifest.json and inspected-source-excerpts.md: actual formal-source hashes, line ranges and excerpts.
- sources/: selected canonical-theory and formal-plan source records, with full-source and excerpt hashes.
- primary-sources.md: primary physics/control source locators. No remote book or paper body is reproduced.
- report-formal-review.md: final focused formal review.
- MANIFEST.json: SHA-256 for all payload files except itself.

To reproduce the finite checks, extract and run with Python3 (standard library only):

    python3 formal_countermodels.py

The script overwrites its local JSON. It checks288 legal transitions across three72-state repair models; all16 deterministic two-state/two-action plant tables and128 projected carrier transitions; and a distinguishable frozen-state example. These are logical countermodels, not empirical data, and do not claim that a fundamental constraint theory must select every engineering detail.

The broader experiment and proof records remain in the earlier delivered archives. Neither the independent reproduction of those computations nor the source-level formal audit establishes empirical correspondence or universal semantic adequacy. The primary-source comparison in the companion report treats Maxwell as a substantive unification within classical electromagnetism, not a theory of everything.
