## Local interpretive basis

`outputs/conscience-and-morality-core.md` was read in full. Its distinctions—moral standing versus responsibility, fallible conscience versus infallible answers, and shared protection versus endless production—guide the proposed integration. The present note is an analysis of that revised core and the supplied traditions. It does not edit the authoritative theory repository.

## Evidence types

The texts supply normative commitments, interpretive descriptions of experience, reported institutional procedures, and candidate functional relations. They are not a random sample of traditions, independent experiments, a proof of statistical convergence, or proof that every named mechanism is universally necessary. Their nonconvergent positions were retained to constrain the synthesis.
