# Scope of the combined foundation certification

Declared before the new combined model's result calculation, 5 October 2026.

The requested target is an account of how activity changes the conditions of later activity across embodied judgment, relationships, institutions, succession, and systems without represented purposes. The task is to integrate existing results and certify their combination. It does not require every process to continue, every actor to innovate, or every domain to contain the same cognitive machinery.

## Joint process

The configuration must include whatever causally affects the declared obligations: operating conditions, actual observations and retained evidence, candidate representations, installed response and evaluation rules, admission and execution mechanisms, usable support and reconstruction routes, successor formation, and the record of affected parties' consequences. A component is included only where the application has such a mechanism. The architecture does not attribute minds or moral agency to cells or ecosystems.

Transitions specify actual effects and their event order. Program, institution, or evaluator revision is a change in the joint process, not an intervention supplied for free by the analyst. Shared resources, disturbances, and dependencies are retained. Externally supplied support is disclosed rather than counted as proof of self-sufficiency.

## What is being certified

The mathematical target is the behavior of the actual installed combined process. Safety, recurring specified duties, response to requests, successful termination, and novel outcomes are distinct objectives. The current programme's explicit common-generation commitments supply the normative reference where relevant: affected-party standing, disclosed harms, and conditions for response and correction. An institution's own changing score remains a separate state variable.

A change in score or an amendment cannot silently erase a previously measured harm. Deliberate rest, exit, and ending a harmful process can meet a declared obligation. They must not be mislabeled as failures merely because they cease local production. Conversely, an absorbing terminal state is not counted as actual infinite physical generation.

## Planned evidence

1. A written composition theorem, including actual changing controllers and an exact success/failure characterization where justified. Its state-set scope and implementation limitations are stated separately.
2. One new finite constructed process joining candidate formation, checking, admission, execution, resource renewal, maintenance, evaluator revision, and succession. This is a witness of compatibility, not a measurement of human psychology or universal discovery.
3. Exact reachable-graph verification, intermediate-state checks, recurrence/rank evidence, and controls exposing omitted or damaged mechanisms. Independent checking should inspect the produced graph and the mechanisms' meanings, rather than only rerun the same assertions.
4. Source-based integration of the supplied traditions as comparative accounts of cultivation, impairment, repair, and social feedback. Their ontological disagreements and the distinction from controlled causal evidence remain visible.

The existing empirical and comparative materials will be integrated at their demonstrated scope. This task does not initiate a new human study, claim every previous pilot has been independently rerun, or relabel a shared description as validated causal identity across domains.

## Claim discipline

The finite witness can certify the supplied joint mechanism under its admitted disturbances. The general theorem can state conditions across a broader class without supplying an algorithm for every instance. The semantic reach of those theorems, the empirical interpretation of a particular system, and ethical justification are separate claims. Completing the first two artifacts is substantive progress even though the world's membership in an unrestricted proposed ontology is not thereby proved.
