# Independent cognitive and normative scope review

5 October 2026. Independent review of `implementation/MODEL.md`, `scope-contract.md`, `implementation/model.py`, and `combined-process-proof.md`. Status: passed at the stated synthetic scope after two general-proof wording repairs. No reference source was changed. Full graph/rank certification is performed separately; this review checks the cognitive and normative meanings of the implemented stages.

## Design findings

The model explicitly supplies a deterministic response law on two contexts, exact paid calibration, and a law that is fixed during each 23-step period. Under these assumptions, two linked calibration observations can identify the response table exactly. That finite inference is legitimate. It is not a guarantee of certainty under arbitrary noisy, adversarial, or changing evidence. The declared common calibration-corruption control is especially important because repeated checking by the same systematically misleading instrument need not reveal the mistake.

The observer interface excludes the hidden physical law. This must remain true in the actual action selector and all policy-side generation, scoring, amendment, and admission functions. An independent evaluator may use the physical law to assess actual harm; that does not grant its information to the actor.

Candidate formation is described as building a conditional expression from actual context-linked records. This is a real bounded formation mechanism if the implementation performs that construction and loses the ability when the records are removed. It uses a supplied grammar and supplied contexts. It should not be described as discovering an unrestricted language, inventing the grammar, or generating every relevant future possibility.

The evaluator change is concrete: an initially simplicity-first nomination rule becomes mismatch-first after observed validation failure, then actually governs a later nomination. This is more than changing the analyst's evaluation of an unchanged actor. The code must nevertheless establish the causal link through the actor's acquired checks rather than the hidden law or a preinstalled answer.

The irreversible public-violation flag is assessed independently of the installed score and survives score revision, handoff, and period change. This appropriately implements the current theory's protection commitment within the synthetic world. It supplies a declared test of public consequences rather than deriving morality from success, survival, or institutional self-approval. A binary response error counts as harm here by the model's stipulated interpretation, not by a finding about all real-world errors.

Memory, archive maintenance, training, handoff, and action each have explicit charges against one ledger. Auxiliary production is a supplied replenishing technology with external support and demand. The construction may therefore certify a funded continuing cycle; it does not prove physical self-sufficiency or explain the origin of the external support.

Successor formation is meaningful only if the successor's actual fields come from the archive through a paid transition, and their absence disables the relevant capabilities. A `competent` bit can be an honest finite abstraction of this dependency. It is not a biological or psychological learning model. The fixed interpreter and transition mechanism remain supplied implementation structure, which should be distinguished from any maintained programme that they enact.

No listed design claim entails infallible conscience, a literal mapping from the religious texts to these variables, a uniquely universal emotional mechanism, or a general theory that every process must continue. The text properly characterizes the example as an organizational witness, and the scope contract explicitly avoids attributing cognition or moral agency to cells or ecosystems.

## Implementation checks required by these meanings

1. The action selector reads only the documented observation tuple. Generation and validation never inspect the hidden law directly.
2. The candidate expression is assembled from records, not returned by a correct-law lookup. The loss-of-records control prevents this particular formation route.
3. Initial failed checks actually trigger a changed evaluator, and the changed evaluator actually changes selection when relevant.
4. Physical public harm persists across amendment and succession regardless of apparent score improvement.
5. All paid transitions check the available balance first; capacity clipping records any discarded income in the accounting contract.
6. Archive and trainee values come from actual source fields. Missing training or archived verifier does not restore initial defaults.
7. The fixed phase schedule cannot secretly execute competent actions after competence loss. Failure must disable the functions it is intended to represent, even if time still advances.
8. Liveness events are emitted only on actual service, maintenance, training, or transfer; advancing a phase label is not enough.
9. Withheld harmful service can preserve safety while failing recurrence. The controls must report those properties separately.

## General combined-process proof

I also read `combined-process-proof.md`. Its principal theorem analyzes the actual installed transition relation, including changed controllers and evaluators, rather than an existentially available better policy. The invariant and event-free well-founded-rank characterization is sound under its totality, adequate-state/history, and ordinary choice/recursion assumptions. Event labels are retained on transitions, which correctly prevents an endpoint or phase label from standing in for delivered work. A no-event infinite path is prohibited even if it has probability zero; the stochastic progress extension is stated separately.

The proof does not claim that the model discovers all future meanings, that the actor can inspect the proof rank or hidden plant state, or that an internally changed score changes physical consequences. Its scope matches the design's bounded cognitive witness and permits a separate external protection commitment. Expanding the state to contain programmes and evaluators is representational closure under the declared semantics, not proof of their empirical or ethical correctness from nothing.

Two precision repairs were sent to the theorem author and verified as adopted:

- Strictly positive step durations alone do not establish time divergence: durations can shrink with finite sum. The stochastic-extension passage should require a uniform positive lower bound or another proved divergence condition.
- If an old request is resolved and a new one admitted in the same transition, the system can resolve every token while its pending flag remains true forever. A single-obligation monitor needs a distinguishable completion event/intermediate state or token-specific treatment; recurring nonpending states must not be claimed as an unconditional exact characterization of all such response schedules.

Neither issue alters the 23-phase witness when its steps have the stated discrete-time interpretation and its obligations are actual recurring transition events. The final proof now explicitly handles both boundaries.

## Code and results review

The implemented observation dataclass excludes `truth` and `bad`. `choose`, `nominate`, and `strict_verdict` receive that observation type. Direct source inspection confirms that the physical law is used only in sensor outcomes and independent outcome/event assessment, not in candidate choice. The public `bad` flag is never read by the actor and is never reset.

`nominate` begins with constant programs and constructs the additional two-context response table as `data0 | (data1 << 1)` when both discovery records exist. Those are executable programmes under the supplied table interpreter. The bit expression implements the finite conditional response described in the design; it is not a hidden-law lookup. Fresh-check answers are not used as an undocumented replacement for missing discovery records by this installed generator. Another generator could choose differently, so this control shows failure of this specified route rather than universal impossibility after losing those records.

The simplicity-first evaluator picks a constant for each initial nonconstant law. Failed fresh checks set `review_failed`; a paid amendment changes the evaluator; the next generation then selects the record-built table. Generation clears previous checks and certification, so admission cannot silently reuse a certificate for a different candidate. The final strict admission rechecks the nominated table against the acquired checks.

Maintenance copies the actual programme, evaluator, guard and linked records into a payload. Training receives that payload; handoff installs its contents. A captured guard is copied as captured, not restored to a strict default. Missing archive or training produces an unskilled successor with a missing guard; ordinary observation, generation, admission and service subsequently fail their usability gates.

The fixed dispatcher and auxiliary-work mechanism remain present after that loss. They can continue paid phase advancement and auxiliary receipts, but they do not restore the missing competence or earn ordinary-service/handoff recurrence credit. These are supplied surviving infrastructure in this model. The result must not be enlarged into a claim that the whole physical interpreter or every productive technology is endogenously regenerated.

Every nonstopped microstep pays one token before any receipt; insufficient funds lead to a zero-cost stopped self-loop. Cap overflow is explicitly recorded as `spill`. The irreversible public harm field is retained through amendment, handoff, and period close. Score capture and common calibration corruption can therefore appear internally acceptable while the fixed physical assessment reports harm.

The focused audit in `cognitive_scope_audit.py` imports the actual implementation and checks selected paths against separately stated semantic assertions. It is not a second independently written simulator. It examined two 46-step paths in each of eight modes: 736 transition steps, 5,888 action comparisons under changes to hidden truth/public-harm fields, and 16 deliberately varied archived-payload transfers. All checks passed.

The selected baseline paths enact the evaluator amendment and subsequent conditional nomination, produce no harm, and deliver every required event twice. Lost records or blocked execution withhold ordinary service. Lost maintenance or training remove competence at the first handoff and prevent later ordinary service. Score capture produces a wrong response at step 16 or 17 depending on the initial law; shared calibration corruption produces a wrong response at step 16. These finite paths illustrate the corresponding mechanisms; the separate exhaustive graph certificate establishes the full declared path properties.

## Review determination

The implemented witness supports its claimed finite combination: record-dependent candidate formation, evidence-dependent revision of an operative evaluator, fresh admission checks, actual service, one resource ledger, maintenance, and transmitted successor contents. It also separates institutional self-approval from physical public consequences. No impossible cognitive certainty is assumed inside the declared deterministic calibration class, but that calibration contract is essential and the corruption control correctly exposes its failure. The artifact does not establish unrestricted discovery, an empirical model of conscience, or automatic transfer from traditional descriptions to causal identity.
