## Exact certificate

Let `H` be the 486 states in the exported baseline graph. Both initial states lie in `H`. The exhaustive successor enumeration is nonempty at each state and every successor lies in `H`. Every state satisfies the fixed public protection predicate; every debit prefix is payable; every retained strict certificate is physically correct. Ordinary recipient harm is assessed outside the revisable score. The supplied isolated calibration technology creates no other recipient harm in this model.

For each required event at phase `p`—corrective installation (15), actual delivery in contexts zero and one (16 and 17), archive maintenance (18), actual successor training (19), and competent handoff (20)—put `rho=(p-phase) mod 23`. On every edge without that actual semantic event, `rho` strictly decreases. Its values lie in `{0,...,22}`. Thus every infinite path contains the event infinitely often, with at most 23 unit-duration microsteps between events. This checks delivery, not just the availability of a action or arrival at a phase label. The event-deleted graph checks establish the same qualitative conclusion by a second graph algorithm.

The initial evaluator demonstrably chooses a simpler wrong program, pays for fresh evidence of failure, changes its selection rule, generates the evidence-supported conditional program, validates and admits it, and delivers correct service. The working evaluator, verifier and operating program then survive actual paid archive/training/handoff. Every admitted period change receives a new corrected operating program through those same stages. The environment's four-rule grammar, exact isolated calibration and continuing productive auxiliary inputs are premises. They are not supplied by the certificate itself.

The seven adverse controls separate the mechanisms. Missing execution authority defeats delivery even with a correct installed program. Missing renewal stops the shared paid process. Missing archive maintenance or training prevents competent succession. Record loss defeats the installed discovery-record interface even though fresh checks can still reject wrong candidates; an alternative generator could use other records, so this is not a universal necessity result. Captured evaluation and misleading calibration can harm the fixed public objective. Some correct outcomes can still recur in the capture condition, demonstrating why recurrence must be combined with protection.

## What gap this closes

It does not show that every successful system requires this architecture, that moral criteria are derived from physical facts, that arbitrary open-ended discovery is decidable, or that this synthetic organization has been validated across cultures or biological scales. Those are different claims. The model repairs evaluator selection between simplicity and evidence fit; it does not construct moral standards from scratch.
