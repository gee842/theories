## Reading Rule

Use the strongest claim allowed by the theorem type, not by the file title or surrounding doctrine prose.

- **closed** means there is a non-placeholder Lean theorem/definition whose statement carries the claimed content.
- **scoped** means the theorem is real but restricted by base, carrier level, or supplied structure fields.
- **schema** means the Lean object fixes the shape of a claim while leaving fields or hypotheses to instantiation sites.
- **external route** means a standard mathematical theorem suggests a reduction, but this repository has not stated and proved the exact bridge theorem.
- **sketch** means the document names a plausible research direction without a theorem-grade reduction.
- **placeholder** means the statement is intentionally `True`, `coherent := True`, or otherwise vacuous at the load-bearing point.
- **open** means the repository states the obligation but does not provide the proof.
