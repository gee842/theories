# Declared checks for a restricted generative basis

5 October 2026. Fixed before the new computations below. These are mathematical software checks and constructed counterexamples, not empirical validation or a search for favorable parameter regimes. Earlier theory and examples are already known.

## Scope

Study finite controlled stochastic transition systems with exact rational probabilities, named action/boundary interfaces, and a vector of protected labels. A deterministic state partition is exact when it preserves those labels and gives identical probability of every successor block for every declared action from each member of a block. Actual decision-makers receive only their specified observation histories; the partition is an analyst's representation, not a new information grant.

The first target is the necessary-and-sufficient quotient criterion and the unique coarsest stable refinement of the declared labels. The second target is an explicit bounded compiler using Boolean guards, single-bit assignments, rational coin choices and sequencing. The compiler observes states only at atomic macrostep boundaries; its private bookkeeping does not model paid physical institutional work.

## Checks fixed before results

1. Independently enumerate all partitions for small systems and compare the coarsest stable answer with iterative refinement. Include deterministic, rational stochastic, deliberately paired-block and state-dependent availability examples. Use deterministic seed `20261005` to generate supplementary kernels; no parameter selection based on outputs.
2. Confirm an observation-hidden bit can be immaterial under the original intervention interface and necessary after that interface expands. Confirm a new protected refusal/cost-incidence label can split a physically sufficient quotient even when transition kernels do not change.
3. Produce a concrete pair whose immediate labels and next-label probabilities agree but whose longer behavior differs, requiring recursive refinement rather than a one-step output check.
4. Check exact compilation of each declared rational kernel, including a probability1/3 row, into guards, biased coins and bit writes. Verify the one-third row cannot be implemented by a bounded-depth fair-coin tree because every such probability is dyadic. This is a formal arithmetic obstruction, not a numerical search over all programs.
5. Verify composition under common quotients for sequencing and quotient-measurable mixtures. Include failures of hidden-state-dependent guards and of composing correlated subsystems as if their noise were independent.
6. Verify monotonic refinement when adding actions or labels. Preserve a stable-state safe rule as a counterexample to a universal need for active renewal. Check distinguishability of arbitrarily many counter states under repeated decrements, illustrating the separate proof that no finite exact quotient covers an unbounded counter.
7. Keep the protected outcome vector explicit. A compact representation and a functioning closed loop do not choose normative requirements or establish desirable outcomes.

## Interpretation fixed in advance

A successful compiler proves expressiveness for the finite rational class. It does not prove that the biological and social mechanisms named by the framework are a unique or irreducible basis, that source transition tables have been explained, that missing observations can be recovered, or that finite exact representations cover open-ended systems. Partition minimality is only within deterministic state partitions under the specified interface. Counterexamples delimit the claim rather than being discarded.
