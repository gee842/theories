# A defensible constitutive bridge for protected generation

5 October 2026. Read-only source audit and mathematical analysis. No reference repository was changed or built.

## Finding

The strongest completed core is a theory of **attainable protected continuation under an independently specified process and challenge class**, together with an openly defended public protection commitment. It can be representation independent without deriving its purpose or the world's mechanisms from the mere existence of distinctions. The six protection functions can be defended as distinct dimensions of that public commitment and tested as mechanisms of robustness. They are not necessary physical ingredients of every process that generates something.

This distinction is already explicit in the strongest canonical source. The v2 plan calls itself a normative process–relation philosophy and a middle-range theory (§2.1); disclaims a complete physical ontology and deduction of a unique morality from natural facts (§2.2); openly states the public ethical commitment (§2.3); and says the six protections are not cosmic commandments (§5). Moving to a stronger ontology is new work, not something silently supplied by those passages.

## 2. The exact strength of a deletion test

Let `G(M)` mean the independently declared protected generativity objective is attainable in model `M`. Let `del_i(M)` remove every implementation of function `i` while retaining the rest of the stated intervention contract.

A successful deletion witness proves

`for every named i, there exists M_i such that G(M_i) and not G(del_i(M_i))`.

That establishes that no named function is uniformly dispensable across the model class. It does **not** prove

`for every M, G(M) implies all six named functions are present`.

The first quantifier pattern is appropriate for showing that a public agenda has distinct, nonredundant concerns. The second would require showing that every attainable instance needs each function. An institution that supplies redundant implementations of a function must not count as a counterexample to the function merely because one implementation can be removed. Conversely, adding arbitrary function labels to a successful model does not establish their causal necessity.

There is a clean necessary-and-sufficient test inside a specified decision model. Let `W_G` be its set of executable controllers that satisfy objective `G`, assume `W_G` is nonempty before deletion, and let `Pi_without_i` be the executable controller set when the complete implementation of function `i` is removed. Then loss under deletion is exactly

`W_G ∩ Pi_without_i = empty`,

provided deletion leaves outcome laws of the retained controllers unchanged. If it changes the plant, observations, timing, or disturbances, compute the winning controllers in the changed model instead. This expression alone is definitional; the substantive result is obtaining `W_G` by the already established local fixed-point calculation or another independently checkable certificate.

## 3. Functions and circumstances: exact countermodels

### Information can be essential or dispensable

There are two hidden conditions, `L` and `R`, and actions `l`, `r`. Only `l` protects under `L`; only `r` protects under `R`. A perfect observation permits protection in both cases. With one indistinguishable observation, no common action does. This proves that informative observation is essential in this model.

Add a third action `s` that protects under both conditions. The actor can now protect with no discriminating observation. More information remains potentially useful but is not necessary for this objective. The invariant is **compatibility of a common executable decision with all currently admitted possibilities**, not a requirement to purchase information in every successful case. This matches the earlier financing examples, where direct repair sometimes made diagnosis unnecessary.

### Recurrence does not always need intervention or plurality

A stipulated deterministic process with one action and rule `n ↦ n+1`, with all states protected and each increment an independently declared output, continues and produces distinct outputs. It has no alternate route, no intervention channel, and no changing belief. This is a mathematical counterexample to their unqualified necessity for all generation. It is not a claim that such an unbounded process can be physically implemented for infinite time.

If the admitted environment can destroy the sole implementation, then a recovery route or another implementation may become necessary for robust continuation. Two implementations still do not suffice against an admitted common-mode failure that destroys both. Robust necessity is relative to the challenge class; this should be stated before observing success or failure.

### Substrate renewal needs a consumption premise

Let a resource stock start at 1. At step `t≥1`, consume `2^{-t}` and never replenish. After `n` steps the stock is `2^{-n}>0`. This stipulated model can perform infinitely many transitions without replenishment. Thus continued resource use alone does not prove that renewal is necessary.

With nonnegative stock, finite initial stock, no external supply, and consumption at least `ε>0` on every progress event, the number of such events is at most `floor(initial_stock/ε)`. More generally, cumulative consumption cannot exceed initial stock plus cumulative receipts. This is the right universal accounting constraint. A positive lower cost per qualifying progress event, unbounded cumulative demand, or equivalent physical premise makes replenishment necessary. The source's ecological and institutional diagnoses require measured depletion/renewal laws, not an inference from the word "process."

### Agency does not logically entail a commitment to indefinite self-preservation

A controller can have the finite objective "complete this task, then stop" and satisfy it by terminating after completion. Its action at a time requires the relevant action-producing mechanism then; it does not logically require a preference for its indefinite continuation. For an agent that does adopt a continuing objective, the means necessary to that objective have conditional instrumental importance. Concern for other persons requires the separate public premise the v2 text explicitly supplies.

This observation does not reject endings, sacrifice, or protective closure. It helps represent them without disguising a public ethical choice as a consequence of being an agent.

## 4. A positive bridge that is universal in a declared class

The following formulation captures the useful root without tying it to one social or physical implementation:

> Given independently specified protected subjects/functions, qualifying progress, feasible intervention and information interfaces, a challenge class, and an implementation standard, continued protected generation is attainable exactly when a compatible realizable controller exists that satisfies those obligations against the admitted challenges. In the finite sure-winning case, existence is characterized constructively by a protected invariant region and a progress certificate.

The first sentence specifies the problem. The fixed-point and progress-certificate theorems solve it from the local mechanism description, rather than simply renaming success. The theorem's conclusion is unchanged by a representation change that preserves observations, executable decisions, possible consequences, and the protected/progress labels. It is therefore carrier independent in the precise sense of implementation refinement. It does not entail that arbitrary physical replacements preserve generation.

The same root yields meaningful institutional interpretations:

- Standing specifies whose protected conditions cannot be omitted from the objective.
- Reality contact controls whether decisions can depend on consequential differences that matter to attaining that objective.
- Non-domination restricts which rule changes count as authorized and which external interventions must be guarded against.
- Renewable plurality supplies continuing alternative implementations against some failure classes.
- Substrate renewal changes which action sequences remain executable through time.
- Correction provides ways to return to, or remain within, attainable protected continuation after admitted disturbances.

These roles overlap causally but not conceptually. None can be collapsed into a single success score without an additional normative aggregation rule. They should be implemented as separately declared constraints and mechanisms. A jointly winning controller must exist: merely providing each ingredient somewhere is insufficient.

## 5. What the totalizing pattern can prove

When an actor's success expands its power to remove others' executable strategies, hide outcome-relevant information, or impose additional admitted adverse consequences, it can contract others' attainable protected futures. Under an exact intervention contract that makes those changes pure restrictions, the relevant winning region cannot expand. If an essential controller or distinction is removed and no substitute remains, some previously attainable objective becomes unattainable.

That is a rigorous conditional causal principle. Strict loss does not follow from power concentration alone: the removed options may have been irrelevant, new options may be added, or compensatory observations may appear. Authority may instead remove adversarial harms, changing the intervention contract in a beneficial direction. Restricting environmental harms is different from restricting the protected actor's controller choices. The canonical downgrade criteria correctly recognize these cases.

The arrows `success → rule power`, `rule power → restriction`, and `restriction → loss without substitution` therefore remain individually testable mechanisms. Calling the entire chain a definition of totalizing creates a coherent classification. Predicting that the chain will arise or recur in particular institutions requires independent evidence for its arrows. No label or universal coding construction provides that evidence.

## 6. What can be completed now, and what cannot be discharged by notation

Completed or completable by proof within the chosen model class:

1. A semantics of protected continuation, recurring progress, novelty, and plural-agent obligations that keeps them distinct.
2. Exact existence/certificate results and valid implementation invariance.
3. Assumption independence, substitution examples, and failure certificates.
4. An explicit public protection commitment and reasons for its six dimensions.

Still a substantive bridge, rather than a missing symbolic manipulation:

1. Which real processes satisfy the mechanism/interface description, and with what uncertainty.
2. Whether the theory's distinctive causal chain predicts more than a conventional mechanism model supplied with the same inputs.
3. Why the public normative commitment ought to be accepted; mathematical consistency cannot compel every coherent opponent to accept it.
4. Whether the actual world's continued generation is inexhaustible, and what "meaningfully new" means independently of the proposed root.

A completed structural theory can retain these as explicit empirical and normative interfaces. Calling it a complete ontology that derives all mechanisms, values, and indefinite existence would erase the interfaces instead of closing them.
