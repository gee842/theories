# Finding the strongest operational root

5 October 2026. Focused continuation of the philosophical and evidential audit, with a completed independently implemented finite service-queue challenge. Reference repositories were read only. This is neither an empirical service study nor a full philosophical-corpus audit.

## 1. The most charitable central claim

The strongest reading is not “every process survives” or “more options are always better.” The canonical account explicitly allows local closure, protective restrictions, scarce resources, unsuccessful correction and changing situations. Its four-part openness concerns an unexhausted domain, attainable transformation, responsiveness and revision/recovery. Its stronger v2 formulation also separates mechanism from the public commitment to protect plural centres of experience and agency.

The corresponding operational claim is:

> A process remains capable of protected generation when its situated mechanisms admit an executable strategy that preserves the relevant conditions while repeatedly realizing the specified responses, corrections or developments. A common characterization should explain both the cases where such a strategy exists and the cases where it cannot exist.

This is a proposed reconstruction, not a claim that these English terms exhaust 間 or 生生. It makes the user's ambition a positive mathematical target: for a declared class and independently specified protection/progress criteria, characterize exactly the states from which protected continuation is attainable. Neither success in every situation nor a context-independent best action is required.

The canonical v2 is especially important here. The source describes six protection functions, explicit deletion tests, a causal account of how success may purchase power over later rules, and conditions requiring that a totalizing diagnosis be withdrawn. These are more discriminating proposals than saying everything changes. The later account also says generation is not asserted to be good merely because it occurs; concern for others is an explicit commitment. A faithful continuation should test that stronger version rather than force a derivation the source itself has abandoned.

## 2. What the distinction → gap → generation bridge can and cannot establish

Separate four propositions:

1. Articulation requires a distinction.
2. A described state has at least one physically or institutionally realizable continuation.
3. A realizable policy can maintain protected continuation through every admitted future challenge.
4. That continuation repeatedly produces novelty or protects other affected agents.

The first does not entail the others without additional structure. This is not just an abstract objection: two labelled terminal states satisfy distinction but have no continuation. Conversely, the fixed rule `n→n+1` produces endlessly new integer states with only one successor at each state. Thus branching alternatives are not necessary for continued change, while the existence of distinguishable alternatives is not sufficient for it. If 間 means something richer than branching, its richer requirements need an explicit bridge.

The formula `J(x)={x⊕i : i∈I(x)}` is useful as a typed description. But every transition relation T has this form by choosing `I(x)={y : (x,y)∈T}` and `x⊕y=y`. Consequently the bare formula admits beneficial, harmful, static and terminating processes. The predictive content must lie in restrictions on I, on enactment, or on the protected query. This expressiveness result also preserves situationality: the same form can describe quite different laws, but sharing the form does not select those laws.

There is a second important bridge. Fixed formal rules need not imply exhausted novelty. A fixed machine can emit an unbounded sequence or execute programs that construct new representations. Object-level grammar expansion can occur under a fixed metalevel rule. The inherited U21 gives a sharper valid limit: ordinary Bayesian updating inside a fixed finite hypothesis family cannot introduce an excluded hypothesis. That does not prove every fixed physical law, invariant or formal rule prevents novelty. The operational issue is whether the **implemented revision mechanism** can introduce the distinctions needed for the admitted future tasks.

Similarly, putting four capacities into an openness vector does not by itself establish joint indefinite feasibility. The possibilities can require incompatible actions, consume the same scarce enabling condition, or arrive too late. Separately proving “there exists a response,” “there exists a repair” and “there exists an experiment” does not prove “there exists one information-admissible strategy that carries out the required combination.” The newly completed queue example below makes the timing conflict explicit.

If “open” is instead defined to mean exactly that such a joint strategy exists indefinitely, sufficiency follows by definition. That can be a useful semantic target, but it leaves the central work: derive an independently checkable structural characterization from the mechanism.

## 3. An actual necessity-and-sufficiency answer in a controlled class

Fix protected states K independently of the continuation calculation, and let P mark a declared progress or successful response event. “Progress” is a task-specific label here; it is not automatically unlimited originality or moral improvement.

In a finite fully observed **deterministic** controlled graph, there is a policy producing an infinite path that stays in K and visits P infinitely often **iff** there is a finite path inside K to a directed cycle inside K that intersects P. Every edge must represent an executable action.

Sufficiency is constructive: follow the path, then repeat the cycle. Necessity follows because an infinite path through finitely many states with infinitely many P-visits repeats some P-state; the intervening segment supplies a reachable cycle. This characterizes both closure and ongoing protected progress without assuming all systems continue. It also yields a witness controller and an explicit obstruction when no qualifying cycle exists.

Under nondeterministic disturbances, a reachable good cycle alone is insufficient: the environment may select another successor. For a fixed finite-memory controller, form the finite product graph of plant state and controller memory. Assuming every live node has a successor, robust safety plus recurring P holds on **every** admitted path iff all reachable nodes are protected and the reachable subgraph outside P has no directed cycle. If an outside-P cycle exists, an allowed path can repeat it forever. If a path avoids P forever, finiteness implies such a cycle. Existence of a suitable controller is then a synthesis problem; partial observations require the same executable decision on indistinguishable histories.

These are established finite-state safety/liveness ideas, not a newly discovered law of generation. They nevertheless provide a real answer within a clear class. The mathematical object to preserve is the set of attainable protected response/progress behaviours, with the actor's information and implementation restrictions included. Common representations can then be proved to preserve it under suitable refinement contracts.

This answer does not silently equate different infinite claims. A finite cycle gives recurring specified achievement, not infinitely many distinct achievements. Infinite-horizon survival is also different from arbitrarily long finite survival: choosing a finite countdown N at an initial state gives a policy for each finite horizon but no one indefinitely surviving policy. Probability-one recurrence differs from every-path recurrence; a possible exceptional infinite path may have probability zero. The desired criterion must choose between these meanings.

## 4. Does the philosophical core add a restrictive law?

At present it adds a promising **selection of questions and protected structures**, not a demonstrated new equation beyond supplied dynamics. It insists that apparent success be examined together with the conditions for later response, correction, replacement, independent alternatives and affected agents. That can reveal failures hidden by current output or a single score. The service result is one concrete instance of why the relevant structure cannot be replaced by a generic amount of “capacity.”

The more distinctive empirical proposal lies in the v2 account of totalization: locally appropriated success must convert into power over later choice conditions, while consequences escape or lag feedback and other centres lose independent perception, action and reproduction. This proposes an ordered causal mechanism with withdrawal conditions. It is stronger than “positive feedback exists,” and could be false in a system with positive feedback that expands exit and effective challenge. It should be tested with separately measured conversion, feedback and alternative-reproduction mechanisms. None of those causal conversion laws follows merely from the existence of distinctions.

The six protection functions likewise support a serious deletion-test program. A function cannot be called universally necessary merely because deleting it was defined to count as failure. Specify an independently meaningful protected outcome and an intervention that removes the candidate mechanism while holding plausible substitute mechanisms visible. If protection persists through the declared challenge set, the claimed necessity was too strong or the removed implementation was replaceable. If failure occurs, distinguish the mechanism's causal contribution from a definition that relabelled the outcome.

The canonical account explicitly allows abstraction levels and situational judgment. That is compatible with universal structural laws, but it does not license changing the protected criterion after each outcome. The strongest bridge would fix the criterion and intervention map before the target test, then demonstrate that the common characterization predicts which situations sustain it.

The substantive counterexample is independent of coding agreement. Fix known initial fault, one job, cash3, barred finance and enabled enactment. Only the job deadline changes:

| Initial deadline | Always serve: failure | Restart first, then serve: failure | Better of these two on failure |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 slot | 3/4 | 1 | Serve |
| 3 slots | 27/64 | 9/64 | Restart first |

With the urgent job, restart consumes the only slot and necessarily misses the deadline. With three slots, it can clear the fault before two service attempts. The latter failure is `(1/4)(3/4)^2=9/64`: restart must fail, then both service attempts must fail. Without restart, three failed attempts have probability `(3/4)^3=27/64`. Thus the same affordable intervention, technology and current fault belief change decision value with the unfinished task's temporal structure. Situationality has a concrete causal meaning here.

Across the three deadline-blind policies, 132 of 144 same-coarse-state/policy groups differ across omitted deadlines. There are 834 differing failure pairs among 1,296 queue pairs; 1,020 differ in at least one outcome coordinate. Across 48 coarse initial-state groups, 18 exhibit a strict reversal between serve and restart-first failure rankings. The fourth policy directly reads deadlines, so it is excluded from those primary common-policy counts. Its results remain in the complete 864-cell comparison.

This rejects the particular sufficient-state claim that job count, belief, accounts, regime and time can omit unfinished deadlines while preserving these outcomes. The coarse model was expected to fail; this was an intentionally chosen diagnostic test, not a surprise discovery. A conventional full-queue control composition given the same local laws matches exactly. Therefore the result supports accurate composition and a specific representation requirement. It does not show explanatory compression, a new physical law, indefinite protected recurrence, or empirical institutional applicability. The original F11 two-gate transport protocol remains distinct.

## 6. What resolves the remaining question

There is now a sharper answer than either “the theory is complete” or “everything is conditional.” We have an operational characterization of protected continuation in declared transition classes, and the new target shows that its representation contracts exclude a concrete tempting abstraction. The distinctively philosophical interpretation can organize this work, but its further universal content must be stated as a restriction that the supplied transition tables do not already guarantee.

The next useful empirical candidate is the canonical mechanism connecting success to rule-setting power and declining independent correction. Freeze measurable variables for those links, intervention timing and protected outcomes. Compare systems where local success does and does not acquire such power; separately vary whether adverse consequences reach a decision mechanism with effective authority. A finding that concentration durably increases independent appeal, correction and reproduction of alternatives would challenge a registered totalization claim. This follows the source's own withdrawal conditions and would test a distinctive mechanism beyond relabelling queue dynamics.

For the mathematical route, instantiate the four capacities or six protection functions as independently specified interfaces, then prove a joint protected-response/progress characterization or give a countermodel. A promising proof target must quantify over **one compatible executable strategy**, not separate existential witnesses for each capacity. Safety, recurring response, actual novelty and plural protection should remain separate until their implication is proved.

For the global philosophical claim that existence never exhausts its source of further generation, neither the finite terminal-state countermodel nor the queue experiment decides what the actual universe does. A mathematically expressible restriction on global transitions and an independently supported bridge to the world are still required. Declaring that restriction as an ontological commitment is coherent; presenting it as a consequence of descriptive distinction alone is not established by this record.
