## Target conjecture

In a declared class of processes where current production supports diagnosis and effective repair, a fixed model of resource access, information, timing, and enactment can predict recovery in a second implementation using independently measured parameters, without adding mechanisms after viewing its integrated recovery outcomes.

This is a restricted transport conjecture. “A process can be written as states and transitions” is not its prediction. We seek an informative structure shared by different processes. The universal ambition remains open; passing this test would establish a limited transfer, while failing it would reject the registered model for this target class.

## A quantitative subclaim

Let finance intervention \(f\in\{0,1\}\) alter whether a valid recommendation can be produced in time, and enactment intervention \(a\in\{0,1\}\) alter whether a timely recommendation produces an effective repair by the deadline. Let \(p_f\) be the first probability and \(q_a\) the second conditional probability.

Under the following assumptions,

1. recovery requires both events, with no spontaneous recovery or alternative repair route;
2. finance does not change conditional repair efficacy or the distribution of recommendations relevant to enactment;
3. the enactment intervention does not change proposal production, diagnostic behavior, or the first gate;
4. timing and the protected function are the same in calibration and evaluation,

the joint recovery probability is

\[
R_{fa}=p_fq_a,
\]

and the financing-by-enactment interaction is

\[
\Delta=R_{11}-R_{01}-R_{10}+R_{00}
=(p_1-p_0)(q_1-q_0).
\]

This is elementary probability, not a proposed new theorem. The risky proposition is that independently calibrated gate probabilities and the same conditional structure predict the target setting's integrated outcomes. Calibration of \(q_a\) must use representative standardized recommendations, not the desired joint success rate disguised as an input.

For an arithmetic illustration only, \(p_0=.25,p_1=.75,q_0=.2,q_1=.8\) predict:

| Finance | Enactment | Recovery |
|---|---|---:|
| Late or restricted | Restricted | .05 |
| Timely and accessible | Restricted | .15 |
| Late or restricted | Enabled | .20 |
| Timely and accessible | Enabled | .60 |

The interaction is .30. These are chosen values, not estimates or experimental results. A large benefit from finance when enactment is blocked would contradict this particular factorization, unless a scope violation such as a direct repair route had already been documented. Such a violation is an informative failure of this registered application; it cannot be counted as successful prediction by adding a mechanism afterward.

## Stage one checks the abstraction

*Completed scope:* the access-only H4 study retains belief and readiness, uses barred/immediate/delayed access, and tests seven declared outcome probes. It supplies negative witnesses and a conditional pooling proof. Its complete frozen specification and results are in the linked reproduction materials. The following paragraphs record the original plan; larger horizons and additional modules have not been run.

Begin with an access-only extension to the finite recoverable-inquiry model described in the frozen prior snapshot. Keep its stock, cash, readiness, paid diagnosis and rebuilding, ordinary production evidence, and repair dynamics. Add only a second treasury account and a permission bit controlling transfers between the accounts. Transfers conserve their sum. Postpone record-format and separate admission/enactment modules until this first projection is understood. Every payment precedes the income or service it enables. Invalid actions enter an explicit failure state or are excluded by an observed availability rule. Ordinary observations, including stock changes and visible investment, must be available to every compared policy.

The complete extended transition table, parameter grid, initial states, safety criteria, observation interface, and allowed controller memory must be frozen before this stage runs. At the time this plan was written they were not specified at that level here; the separate frozen access-study protocol subsequently supplied them before the main run. Start at horizon \(H=4\); predeclare the larger evaluations \(H=8,12\) before examining their outcomes and report any additional horizon as a later study.

Compare three descriptions:

- **Aggregate:** current output, productive stock, and total cash.
- **Typed operational:** additionally accessible cash, observation history or an actually sufficient summary, readiness, pending work, and enactment access.
- **Full implementation:** all simulator variables and the allowed memory and controller update mechanisms.

For each, explicitly state the state map, action map, observation map, preserved predicates, and discarded variables. Compute feasible outcome vectors rather than assuming a single ethical score. The primary components are probability of crossing the productive floor, probability of timely recovery, expected inquiry expenditure, and declared unwanted-intervention burden. Report protected participation or appeal as a separate predicate if included; never infer it from stock trajectories.

Search exhaustively for states in one projected fiber with different attainable vectors or different safe-policy existence. A discrepancy refutes the exact abstraction. Add only the missing relation and rerun the entire declared intervention family, including failed cases. Restoration of equivalence supports that repaired finite map. It does not establish empirical adequacy or prove all future questions are preserved.

Use exact rational enumeration or certified finite optimization where feasible. Treat floating-point differences below a stated numerical tolerance as numerical questions, not findings. Preserve proofs or witness policies for claimed attainability and explicit merged-state witnesses for failures.

## Stage two tests transfer between implementations

Use two independently specified implementations rather than changing the labels on one transition table. One can represent a cooperative maintaining an irrigation supply, with stock dynamics and a collective decision delay. The other can represent an automated service, with discrete failures, diagnostic jobs, and a deployment permission gate. They are initially simulators; neither is a validated model of its named real-world setting.

Fit or identify the shared mechanism structure on the first implementation. Define the bridge measurements for the second using local trials: productive loss, observation reliability, payment timing, rebuilding time, and conditional enactment success. Lock the common equations, measurement rules, and mapping before revealing any integrated recovery outcomes from the second implementation. Calibration trials must not reveal the very recovery quantity to be predicted.

In the target, cross finance and enactment interventions in a two-by-two design. Hold initial output, total endowment, diagnostic quality, hazard law, and repair technology fixed across the four cells. Finance changes accessibility or timing; enactment changes whether a validated repair can be applied before its deadline. Measure actual spending, cash access, time, and every relevant information channel separately. Equal nominal endowment is not a claim of matched expenditure.

The primary prediction is the four recovery probabilities and their interaction. Secondary predictions are recovery times and floor-crossing probabilities. Freeze tolerances and uncertainty procedures before sampling. Prefer exact finite evaluation; if simulation is needed, use 1,024 independent target seeds, pair primitive shocks across cells, and analyze uncertainty at seed-cluster level. Do not count the four outcomes from one seed as independent replications. Use a simultaneous 95% uncertainty procedure for the four primary probability differences, specified before the held-out run.

Compare the transported mechanism model with a current-output/total-resource predictor, a model with fixed diagnostic access, and a serious domain-specific control model that includes the same measured constraints. The latter is essential: beating a deliberately incomplete predictor cannot establish a new unification. Compare calibration error, held-out predictive error, and the number of independently estimated quantities; do not select a complexity penalty after seeing which model wins.

## Stage three tests the causal role of sustained inquiry

Only after the first two stages, let recognition of observed outcomes affect later diagnostic funding. Recognition must be a rule applied to observable records; hidden true benefit cannot determine the grant. Charge record retention, validation, readiness, rebuilding, and enactment where applicable, and retain all passive evidence.

Compare the feedback policy to a schedule selected independently of the target run's outcomes. A schedule copied from that same run's future feedback would introduce foreknowledge and is invalid. Two admissible options are an ex ante lottery over fixed schedules, or a schedule estimated on independent training trajectories and frozen before evaluation. If matching expected expenditure, choose the comparator using the declared model or training data, not by adjusting it after the target outcomes are known. Record both expected and realized spending.

This stage must distinguish two questions. Whether feedback predicts recovery at the same initial endowment is one question. Whether feedback improves the best achievable outcome at matched expected expenditure is another. Neither comparison grants a pathwise equal-spending guarantee. If exact matching is infeasible, show the attainable cost–outcome frontier and compare at overlapping expenditure levels.

If changing the schedule changes what agents can infer from visible funding decisions, that is part of the intervention unless held fixed through an explicit observation design. A finance-only interpretation then requires another control separating signaling from physical availability. Do not erase informative silence or visible readiness to force the desired interpretation. A donor replay is only one nonadaptive comparator; compare it with the best ex ante calendar lottery before claiming an advantage over nonadaptive allocation generally.

### A completed check of the spending comparison

An exact two-period example has already been checked as a design guard. Both policies start with readiness and cash six. A fair public signal arrives after the calendar policy commits: one signal implies no later need; the other implies need probability .2. Keeping readiness costs .1, a later perfect test costs one, treatment costs four, and untreated need costs ten. Rebuilding would finish after the decision deadline. The feedback policy retains and tests only after the informative-risk signal; both policies may otherwise adapt diagnosis and treatment.

Feedback spends .55 on inquiry and has operating loss .40. For a calendar lottery with retain probability \(q\), let \(u\le q\) be the unconditional probability of retaining and then testing if the risk signal occurs. Inquiry expense is \(.1q+.5u\ge .6u\), while operating loss is \(1-.6u\). At expense allowance .55, the best calendar has \(q=u=11/12\), spends .55, and has operating loss .45. All paths can be financed from the same initial cash; the largest bill is 5.1.

Replaying feedback's marginal retain frequency .5 on an independent recipient instead spends only .30. It is not a spending match. This constructed result checks the comparison logic; it does not include recurrent production, institution selection, or unrestricted discovery. The larger experiment remains unrun.

## Controls and refusal to overgeneralize

Include all of the following in the frozen evaluation set:

| Control | What it can expose |
|---|---|
| No useful new hazard or proposal | Costs and burdens of unnecessary continued inquiry. |
| Diagnostic independent of the hidden fault | Apparent information benefit produced by bookkeeping or optimization error. |
| Direct repair without diagnosis | A failure of the strict two-gate factorization; sometimes information is unnecessary. |
| Free immediate rebuilding | Loss of option value when maintained readiness is no longer needed. |
| Nonbinding enactment access | Whether the model spuriously predicts an authority effect everywhere. |
| Informative passive production | Whether paid diagnosis is credited with information already available for free. |
| A poorer diagnostic with the same positive support | Whether a possibilistic safety map is being misused to preserve probabilities. |
| Suppressed participation but unchanged stock outcomes | Whether a scalar performance projection erases the declared normative relation. |

Do not assume funding and enactment are always independent or complementary. Institutional anticipation, recommendation quality, strategic behavior, and heterogeneous repair efficacy can violate the simple factorization. Those possibilities are targets for diagnosis, not automatic permissions to rescue its failed prediction.

## Decision rules

Reject an exact projection claim after one verified counterexample within its registered domain. Reject the registered transport model for the target when its exact predictions fail beyond numerical tolerance, or its sampled primary differences exclude zero under the prespecified simultaneous procedure. A systematic reversal of the predicted interaction is particularly diagnostic.

Report a narrower success if the shared model transports only after an independently identified boundary restriction. Label changes suggested by target outcomes as a new exploratory model and evaluate them on a new held-out target. Do not redefine the original success criterion.

If a conventional control model matches the transported model using the same assumptions, conclude that the common formulation organizes established mechanisms; do not infer a new law. If the shared model predicts equally well with fewer independently calibrated mechanisms across implementations, that supports explanatory compression and scoped integration. Neither outcome settles universal ontology or ethical adequacy.

Real empirical application remains a separate stage requiring independently observed mechanisms and outcomes, appropriate permissions, and no reliance on simulator labels as evidence about people. No external study or publication is authorized or performed by this protocol.
