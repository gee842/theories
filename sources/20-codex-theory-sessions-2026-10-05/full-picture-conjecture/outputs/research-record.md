## Decisions and rejected formulations

1. **Retain the unification ambition, but do not substitute a catalog for its achievement.** A family of models must share checked interfaces and transfer a useful result. The final conjecture was strengthened after review from “may predict some cases” to a frozen target prediction that can fail.
2. **Do not collapse world change, model revision, and abstraction.** Their different preservation questions are central, including when a representation's carrier is itself an object in the world.
3. **Reject unrestricted invariance.** If arbitrary replacement is allowed, a nontrivial property can be replaced by its violation. Declare the transformation class.
4. **Reject one scalar resource ontology.** Money, energy, information, access, authority, and burdens have different units and operations. Shared notation is not a conservation law.
5. **Reject necessary-bounds-as-sufficiency.** The two-observation diagnostic counterexample demonstrates the gap exactly.
6. **Reject self-inclusion-as-omniscience.** The construction can represent fallible model-making. Its actual premises, interpretations, and controller resources still need assessment.
7. **Reject persistence-as-ethics.** Coercive stability, manipulative evaluation, and unequal options remain possible under viable dynamics.
8. **Reject cultural sameness and priority claims.** Only limited interpretations are defended; Chinese wording has a provisional own gloss and a specialist translation comparison remains open.
9. **Reject replay with future information.** A target run's own future feedback cannot be used to select its supposedly ex ante comparison schedule. Equal nominal allocation and equal realized spending are different.
10. **Keep failed claims visible.** The inherited failed frame-escape prediction, corrected nonhyperbolic case, signaling correction, and limited S06 trace remain in the audit rather than being presented as uninterrupted progress.
