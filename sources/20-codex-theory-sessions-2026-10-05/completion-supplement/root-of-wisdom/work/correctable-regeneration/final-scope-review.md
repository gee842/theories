# Final source and ethical-scope review

Reviewed `outputs/regeneration-and-effective-correction.md` as available on 5 October 2026, before the evidence package was created. This review concerns claims, sources, and completion scope; the independent mathematical audit and exact transition checks carry verification of the recurrence and thresholds.

## Verdict

The report has no material ethical or source-scope blocker. Its bounded completion claim is warranted once the cited evidence package and independent verification are finalized. The requested study supplies a causal mechanism, matched comparisons, an indefinitely recurrent protection witness, and separate obstructions. It does not need to solve the origin of authority or universal morality to complete this declared construction.

The draft explicitly identifies automatic filing, reserved funding, binding authority, and the replacement rule as supplied protocol features. It does not conflate affordability with voluntary participation or strategic compliance. It restricts the protected stock to one participant capacity, distinguishes end-of-round protection from intraround prefunding, and restricts sharpness to the specified temporary-mandate protocol. The permanently fixed restorative rule is properly presented as a different implementation assumption.

The two primary citations are appropriately used as predecessors for costly institutional support and governance choice. The draft neither attributes its particular equations to them nor claims to replicate their empirical findings. Earlier participation and plural-requirements mathematics is acknowledged rather than presented as new.

## Two small precision edits recommended

1. Opening: replace “Protecting that participant requires a separate causal route” with “Under the specified replacement rule, protecting that participant requires a causal route responsive to that harm.” The unrestricted original could be read as a universal necessity; the report's own externally fixed restorative rule makes clear that the necessity is conditional on the mechanism being tested.

2. Ethical interpretation: replace “Giving them standing in the model requires an explicit protection criterion and a working path through which that criterion can affect outcomes” with “Making the declared protection effective in the model requires an explicit protection criterion and a working path through which that criterion can affect outcomes.” Moral standing need not disappear when protection is unenforceable. The construction explains effectiveness of one supplied protection rather than creation of the participant's value.

Neither edit changes the mechanism or requires rerunning numerical checks. These are scope refinements, not requests for another research phase.

## Completion boundary

The defensible finished claim is that a specified material channel can repeatedly protect a participant whom aggregate production could otherwise disregard, provided its operation, timely use, and next use are funded. The report correctly leaves strategic acceptance, legitimacy, additional threats, and a universal derivation of 善 outside that result. Those exclusions do not make the bounded study incomplete.
