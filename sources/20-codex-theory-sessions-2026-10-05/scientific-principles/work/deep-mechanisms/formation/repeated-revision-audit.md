# Audit of repeated bidirectional rule revision

5 October 2026. Independently inspected and replayed `repeated_revision.py` against `repeated-revision-results.json`; core single-study source was not edited. `audit_repeated_revision.py` and `repeated-revision-audit-results.json` preserve the audit.

**Verdict:** no implementation error found. The installed admission rule is actually carried across phases and executed after each decision. A symmetric supplied toggle can be adopted in both directions from observed evidence. The result is stronger than choosing a fresh preferred policy independently at each phase, but remains a bounded exercise with externally scheduled opportunities and task changes.

## Carry, freshness, and enactment

The phase function receives the previous installed `Rule`, creates exactly one candidate by toggling `require_improving_prefix`, and runs incumbent/candidate pairs before any assignment changes `current`. The closure in `collect` therefore refers to the correct entering incumbent throughout discovery and checking. It is not called again after `current` can change. Main assigns the returned rule to the next phase's installed state.

Instrumented calls verify every discovery/checking pair uses entering rule then its toggle, and all 32 post-decision tasks use the returned final rule. The observed sequence is True→False→True→False. All three complete phase records replay exactly.

Discovery, checking, and post-decision tasks use separate predeclared seed offsets, and phases use separate offsets as well. Each policy obtains its own paid training and local-validation outcomes. Only observed reports enter revision. The phase profile belongs to the environment, not the acceptance function. The externally chosen schedule and larger second-phase sample are design information, however; this is not autonomous detection of a distribution shift.

## Ledger and limits

Worst-case reserve is `(2*(128+n)+32)*20.06`, including all post-decision tasks. It is 16048 for each interaction phase and 334440.32 for the null phase. Independently summing recorded expense for both study arms plus post-decision execution yields 11704.96, 232716.12, and 11704.96, total 256126.04. Every amount is below its assigned phase budget. An instrumented zero-budget phase acquires no observations and leaves the rule unchanged.

Budgets are external phase grants, not money regenerated by the learned rule. Outcome feedback, editing, and meta-arithmetic retain the earlier explicit free-cost conventions. The large null-phase checking expense is a consequence of this particular conservative testing design, not a universal price of reversing scientific standards. Matched-service estimates reuse study reports and retain their earlier limits; they are not a second independent inference about economic benefit.

## Conditional error control

Each pair difference remains in [-75.31,75.31], including when the incumbent is the broader rule and the candidate restores the gate. For each phase alpha=1/60. The one-sided radii are 13.469139684372903 at n=256 and 2.3810300018922286 at n=8192. These independently reproduce the saved values and positive lower bounds.

Let H_(t-1) contain all history before phase t's checking data. Conditional on this history and the discovery screen, the proposed rule and configuration are fixed. Under fresh independent within-phase pairs and the stated unchanged within-phase task law, the probability of accepting a nonpositive-mean-gain change is at most alpha_t. Therefore

P(any such accepted change in the three declared phases) ≤ sum_t alpha_t = .05.

Independence between the three adoption decisions is not required. Fresh conditional sampling is required. This guarantee covers nonpositive *within-phase expected objective gain*, not every future result, changing laws after the check, perpetual continuation, or legitimacy of the objective. It also does not require that every beneficial change be detected.

## Interpretation that the implementation warrants

The learner does not receive the instruction "switch to the correct rule now". Its supplied opportunities lead it to compare consequences, accept a costly-search expansion in interaction tasks, accept contraction in fair-label tasks, and accept expansion when interaction tasks return. Actual post-decision behavior follows those revisions.

The goal, loss conversion, representation language, toggle grammar, phase boundaries, budgets, probe schedule, sample sizes, and error allocation remain supplied. The experiment therefore implements **repeated evidence-sensitive revision of one operational admission standard**, rather than unrestricted evolution of goals, autonomous discovery of when inquiry must reopen, or a fully self-maintaining scientific institution. Preserving this boundary makes the constructive result credible.
