# Integrated inquiry: bounded mechanism protocol

5 October 2026. This protocol records the final development design before the held-out seed evaluation. It describes one persistent, resource-bearing institutional process. Mathematical constraints, synthetic observations, governance choices, and real-world conclusions remain distinct.

## What the loop implements

Ordinary prediction services produce observed successes/failures and settled contractual income. The institution retains those outcomes separately for two observable stakeholder groups. Experienced group shortfalls, current-mandate errors, or time without examination can prompt a review. Income and expenditure determine whether the review can acquire evidence. A maintained checking facility makes the required prospective linked records available. The institution can rebuild that facility after losing it. A paid hearing can change which group's errors count in the institutional target. A generic rule edit is then judged against the actual installed predictor under that enacted target using entirely fresh evidence. Accepted edits change both the installed search rule and compiled predictions used in later services, changing outcomes, refund liabilities, and resources.

This is a finite constructive example, not a complete account of science. The institution uses a supplied behavioral controller, finite representation and rule grammar, fixed stakeholder concerns, a supplied voting constitution, given contracts, and a defined physical checking protocol. The operative institutional mandate changes; the participants' ultimate values and the meta-level error criterion do not emerge from nothing.

## State, ordinary work, and information

State contains integer cash, functioning/nonfunctioning checking capability, an installed prefix-admission rule, two compiled predictor tables, institutional mandate, separate rolling group error histories, lifetime group service counts, review/attempt timestamps, a continuing error-budget index, and chronological events/ledger records.

Each ordinary round has exactly one request from group A and one from group B. This fixed request population remains unchanged across mandates and arms. A contract is a two-group service bundle. If the institution cannot finance its prediction work and the maximum possible refund, both requests are unserved. Counts distinguish requested, served, correct, errors, and unserved. No absence of service is recorded as success or low error.

An ordinary context contains three binary fields. The installed predictor is committed as a complete table before any new outcome is observed. Context arrives, a prediction is made, and the outcome and contractual invoice settle synchronously later in that atomic service operation. Fees become spendable only after settlement. There are no overlapping unpaid service contracts or future fees in the cash balance. This synchronous timing is an explicit simplification, not a model of extended payment delays.

The retained ordinary record contains group error counts/history, coverage, cash, and settled invoices. Full contextual rows are discarded. Already compiled predictors remain usable without the checking facility. New prospective linked training/evaluation records require that facility under the declared action protocol. This does not establish that every conceivable check is impossible without the facility: some aggregate comparisons, alternative instruments, or different institutional procedures might be available in a richer model. The loss concerns this priced review route, not a metaphysical disappearance of all information.

The world supplies contexts, outcomes, and observable maintenance/contract quotes. Its scenario identity and hidden law do not enter the controller's review, ballot, fitting, checking, or financing decisions. Exogenous random streams are keyed by seed, scenario, ordinary round, group, acquisition stage, review number, and sample index, without arm identity. Ordinary cases are consequently matched across arms even when their inquiry histories differ. Review data match when the corresponding acquisition keys match. Source code exposes the simulation laws to the external researcher; the implemented controller does not use those laws as decision inputs.

## Supplied prices and resource transitions

All monetary values in saved machine records are integer thousandths of one unit. Initial capital is 20 units. Two fees of .40 can be earned per completed ordinary bundle. A wrong prediction incurs a .05 contractual refund. There is no payment from an inaccessible true-risk score. Poor-income scenarios change the publicly quoted fee to .01 per group after round 40.

Ordinary operating expense is .01 per prediction, .005 per field read, and a .14 per-bundle retainer when the expanded search rule is installed. The latter is a supplied ongoing method-support tariff. A simpler implementation could have different economics; this tariff is not a scientific constant. The future rule comparison includes the full known difference in the packages' standalone running expenses.

Checking upkeep normally costs .05 each ordinary round before inquiry. Missing an upkeep payment makes the facility unavailable. Rebuilding costs 8, plus the current upkeep charge. The full controller rebuilds when observable inquiry pressure exists and cash covers rebuilding, upkeep, the next service bundle's operating/refund needs, and a 5-unit reserve. No credit or external replenishment is supplied after initial capital. All further funds come from settled ordinary service contracts. Thus the accounting is self-financed within an externally supported service market, not materially closed or capable of creating resources from nothing.

Research prices are .01 per acquired label, .005 per retained linked row, .005 per observed field, .01 per fitted table, a 1-unit opening fee, and a .25 hearing fee when a hearing occurs. A paired exploratory or checking round pays four forecast computations, one shared .14 shadow-engine charge, and one linked observed outcome per group. Labels and contexts may be shared by the two passive predictors because neither predictor changes the outcome; their two forecast computations are both charged. The current prices give .24 per paired round. Coarse fitting, singleton fitting, pair fitting, inner validation, exploratory pilot computation, and final checking computation are charged before their named actions occur.

These are all costs named in this model. Meta-level bookkeeping, the institutional controller itself, physical construction of initial instruments, elapsed laboratory time, and alternative uses of resources receive no additional price. Reviews occur atomically inside one ordinary tick with the environment held fixed during the acquisition batch; opportunity costs and maintenance during a long real experiment are therefore not represented. This is not a complete physical cost model.

## When the institution questions its procedures

After at least 16 settled observations per group, the full controller can register:

* weighted current-mandate error at least .20;
* an eligible B grievance: B error at least .25 and at least .125 above A error;
* 128 ordinary rounds without a paid examination of the procedure.

An attempt must also be at least 64 rounds after the previous attempt. The age threshold is a supplied exploratory safeguard; its trigger does not inspect hidden world change dates. Actual starts depend jointly on accessible experience, capability, and funds. Review timing is endogenous under this controller, but the controller's trigger grammar is supplied rather than learned.

The opening affordability gate covers only the worst possible opening, candidate construction, inner validation, exploratory pilot, possible hearing, and reserve. At current prices that is 15.19 units. It does not demand funding for a maximum-sized final study before the candidate and mandate are known. After the pilot and any hearing, the actual fresh checking size is fixed and separately financed. An unfunded check ends without predictor adoption; money already spent is recorded, and its error-budget index is consumed. A mandate may remain changed after that interruption.

## Bounded representation and rule construction

The language contains constant, singleton-field, and pair-field majority tables. Training is a paid balanced grid of eight contexts for each group. A table's score is empirical classification error plus 1/32 for each cell beyond the constant table. The incumbent rule requires a useful singleton before considering pairs containing that singleton. Its generated edit toggles this requirement, without receiving the correct field pair or hidden target function.

The edited procedure fits its candidate tables from the paid records, then uses eight different fresh outcomes per group for a local acceptance requirement of at least seven correct. Otherwise that group's candidate package uses its fitted constant. These local checks do not justify the final rule change alone. The actual installed incumbent package is frozen throughout; it is not silently refitted or replaced during a review.

The final object of comparison is a **rule-plus-compiled-predictor package under the current case distribution**. Successful checking does not demonstrate that its search algorithm will dominate on every future fitting task. After acceptance, both the proposed rule and its compiled models are installed coherently. If the rule change is rejected or remains unfunded, the actual old package remains in use.

## Operative purpose change through a supplied council

The initial mandate counts A error only: weights `(1,0)`. A candidate inclusive mandate weights errors `(.5,.5)`. Services continue to serve both groups; changes of purpose do not change the reporting denominator. The weights do change the objective authorizing a package edit, which can change actual subsequent prediction behavior.

A separate paid exploratory pilot supplies 32 paired outcomes per group. A hearing uses those observed pilot error reductions, known running-cost changes, current service summaries, cash, and checker status. The implementation centrally represents three distinct interests through explicit votes:

* A's delegate favors its observed error reduction minus half of the extra running expense.
* B's delegate requires an eligible grievance and its own pilot error reduction minus half the expense to exceed .125.
* The steward favors reduced maximum group error after total extra expense, subject to an empirical no-A-harm guard and liquidity/capability requirements.

A two-of-three majority with the operational guards can enact inclusion. Individual concerns, thresholds, constituency standing, and voting authority are supplied normative and institutional choices. The model does not derive them from reliability or model independently adapting human minds. Events preserve each vote and its reason. The purpose decision is fallible: a favorable pilot can change a mandate even when a subsequent predictor check fails or cannot be paid. Purpose adoption is not counted as validated predictive improvement.

For the main example, A's old predictor remains correct while B becomes predictable only through a field combination. A pair package's prospective objective can be unfavorable under `(1,0)` and favorable under `(.5,.5)` on exactly the same comparison data. This is an operative target change rather than merely relabeling a report. Old and inclusive scores are retained side by side as diagnostics; only the enacted target determines the final test.

## Fresh checking and its exact scope

After any hearing the mandate, incumbent, candidate, price difference `delta`, and future sampling protocol are frozen. If the enacted pilot improvement is nonpositive, the institution rejects the edit at the exploratory stage and does not buy confirmation. This screening is not final validation.

Review attempt `j` receives `alpha_j=.05/[j(j+1)]`, including attempts that end before final checking. For the frozen packages, let `W` be the sum of enacted group weights whose prediction vectors differ on at least one allowed context. The paired weighted error advantage less known running-cost difference lies in `[-W-delta,W-delta]`; its range width is `2W`. `W` is computed from fixed forecasts, not hidden outcomes.

If `W=0`, the objective difference is exactly `-delta` for every possible outcome, so a price-dominance comparison requires no statistical sample. Otherwise the sample count is fixed before final outcomes:

`n = min(512, max(64, ceil((2W)^2 log(1/alpha_j)/(2*.075^2))))`.

The full known checking bill and reserve must then be available. Exactly `n` fresh paired rounds are purchased; there is no extension after inspecting their results. Acceptance requires the mean observed enacted-target advantage to exceed `2W sqrt(log(1/alpha_j)/(2n))`.

Conditional on the past, pilot, enacted mandate, and frozen predictors, fresh checking cases are independent from the current atomic review distribution. The bounded-sum inequality therefore controls acceptance of a nonpositive expected-advantage package at `alpha_j`. Summing the allowances bounds the probability of any such false acceptance over the run by .05. Independence of different review decisions is not needed. The statement does not cover changing the loss after looking at final outcomes, arbitrary within-batch drift, unpriced errors, or future advantage after the world changes. Seeded pseudo-random paths implement a reproducible simulation; independent sampling is a declared model condition, not an empirical inference from those paths.

## Frozen arms and constructed environments

Five arms differ only at the declared mechanisms:

1. `full`: all controller, council, renewal, and enactment operations active.
2. `fixed_mandate`: complaints, paid hearings, pilot evidence, and ballots remain; only mandate enactment is clamped.
3. `fixed_rule`: proposed packages may be evaluated, but installation is clamped. This is an intervention on the mechanism, not an optimized agent that knows adaptation is prohibited.
4. `no_renewal`: initial compiled models are built from paid data, then upkeep/rebuilding is deliberately withheld. This is an ablation, not a claim that never maintaining checks is optimal.
5. `incumbent_only_opening`: only errors under the current mandate can trigger review; B grievances and exploratory age cannot open one. Initial A correctness therefore blocks examination even when B suffers.

Every environment has 700 ordinary request rounds. A's relation is stable and simple. B initially has the same simple relation. At round 80, `emerging` gives B a deterministic pair interaction, `noise` gives B independent fair outcomes, and `transient` gives the interaction only until round 150. `simple` never changes B's simple relation. `material_stress` uses the interaction from round 80 to 399, quotes upkeep of 20 per round at rounds 200–209, then returns B to a simple relation at round 400. `poor_income` uses the emerging relation but drops group fees from .40 to .01 at round 40. These exogenous world trajectories are supplied tests, not controller schedules or representative samples of science.

## Evidence, controls, and limits

Development used only seeds 1103, 2207, 3301, and 4409. The held-out evaluation is separately specified by the root validation plan: all 64 seeds `800001+7919*i`, `i=0..63`, across every arm and scenario, after source/protocol/evaluator hashes are frozen. No held-out results were used in designing this document.

Every run checks cash identity, nonnegative balances, full request denominators, and actual post-change behavior. The chronological ledger records per-round credits, payments, minimum interim balance, transaction counts, and a rolling hash; optional full transactions support a predeclared trace audit. Fresh-check events retain frozen prediction vectors, weights, support bound, sample size, error allowance, and separate stage keys. All named expenses, failed/unfinished reviews, mandate changes without rule adoption, checker losses/rebuilds, and old/new diagnostic scores remain visible.

The mechanism can work while its costs outweigh its benefits under other criteria. Development already exposed costly fruitless inquiry, transient opportunities missed because confirmation was unaffordable, fallible mandate expansion under noise, and poor-income runs in which spending on inquiry/upkeep prevented later services that a nonrenewing arm still provided. Those are retained failure cases. The protocol does not establish optimal governance, optimal review timing, an optimal exploration budget, or an ethical reason to value only the represented groups.
