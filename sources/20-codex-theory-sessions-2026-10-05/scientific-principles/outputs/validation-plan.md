# Validation plan for integrated inquiry

Recorded before the integrated implementation and before validation outcomes are available, 5 October 2026. This is a computational mechanism study, not a preregistered human experiment. Final parameter choices and implementation hashes must be recorded before the held-out run.

## Required integration

One persistent community state must contain cash, checking capability, installed admission rule, adopted institutional mandate, and accessible histories. Ordinary work generates observable outcomes and contractual income. Those receipts and all named expenses enter the same ledger. No future income is spendable before receipt.

Review starts must depend on accessible experience, capability, and affordability rather than hidden world labels or externally prescribed change dates. A proposed rule is generated from a declared grammar and assessed with fresh observations. Mandate changes must arise from a specified proposal and authority process, affect actual assessment or action, and retain old and new outcome measures separately.

Maintenance and rebuilding must affect the same checking operation used for rule reviews. Ordinary retained data must not secretly permit an unpriced equivalent replay. If a substitute route is included, it must be priced and reported.

## Development and evaluation separation

Development seeds are 1103, 2207, 3301, and 4409. Numerical prices, horizons, trigger thresholds, and scenario timings may be revised during development, with material changes and failed predictions recorded. Code correctness failures can be fixed before validation.

The held-out seed rule is 800001 + 7919*i for i from 0 through 63. Do not run these seeds until the protocol, scenario definitions, behavioral arms, and source hashes are frozen. These are 64 independent seed clusters, with matched exogenous input streams across the compared arms where feasible. Arm-dependent observations and decisions need not match.

No seed searching or dropping unsuccessful runs. After freezing, a correctness defect requires a new version and a full rerun with the defect and scope disclosed. A failed substantive prediction stays a failed result; it does not authorize tuning on the held-out records.

## Comparisons

At minimum compare the complete implementation with a fixed mandate, a fixed admission rule, and a restricted route for preserving or rebuilding checks. Additional controls can isolate entry to reviews or remove review cost; label altered resource conditions accurately.

Use an environment in which an initially excluded group's outcomes become improvable through a combined representation, alongside a simple no-need environment and an irreducibly noisy environment. Include at least one material stress condition affecting maintenance/rebuilding or ongoing income. A later return to simpler tasks can test whether review remains useful after an earlier successful revision; adaptation is not assumed.

Freeze the actual arm and scenario list before validation. Do not describe these exploratory constructed worlds as a representative sample of science.

## Required measurements

- Cash identity, every payment's affordability, total receipts and categorized expense, and any dividends or external transfers.
- Review proposals, starts, blocked starts, paid evidence, terminations, and accepted edits with start and decision times.
- Checking capacity, upkeep, loss, rebuilding, and any substitute use.
- Purpose petitions, hearing cost, individual votes, adopted mandate, and changes in the criterion applied to candidate rules.
- Outcomes and error rates separately by group, under old and new reporting weights, without allowing a new target to erase old failure.
- Actual behavior after a rule or mandate change, not just a changed status label.
- Independent evaluator measurements clearly separated from observations available to the community.

Report all scenario-arm records, denominators and failed or unfinished reviews. Seed-cluster comparisons concern this constructed model only. A self-financing ledger means that paid expenses are covered by initial resources and observable contractual receipts; it does not mean a closed system produces resources from nothing, nor that every possible cognitive or opportunity cost was priced.

## Discriminating expectations and limits

The integration should make it possible for experience to change the mandate, the mandate to change which rule comparison is pursued or accepted, the revised rule to change service outcomes and income, and income/allocation to affect future checking. We will test whether these links occur rather than infer them from the presence of state variables.

A fixed-mandate arm can reveal whether the purpose transition is causally active. A fixed-rule arm can reveal whether service gains depend on the rule transition. Restricting maintenance or rebuilding can reveal whether preserved check access matters. Effects may disappear with substitutes, no later need, or unaffordable prices. Broad search and repeated checking may impose costs without benefit in the noisy or simple controls.

The error-control statement must match the implementation. If a sequential betting test is used, its guarantee concerns reviews whose conditional expected net gain is nonpositive throughout; it does not certify future advantage under arbitrary drift. If a fresh fixed batch is used, its target law and error allowance must be specified. Adaptive starts do not remove the need for fresh data and an error allowance across reviews.
