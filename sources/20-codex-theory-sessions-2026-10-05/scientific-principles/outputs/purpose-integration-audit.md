## The consequential chain is present

The institution serves both groups and retains separate group errors while the initial mandate gives B zero weight in rule acceptance. A B grievance can therefore initiate a review even when the A-only error score is quiet. This opening is an explicitly supplied constitutional entitlement, rather than a result inferred from the institution's previous objective.

The council helper consumes observed ordinary-service summaries, an independently acquired exploratory pilot, declared expenses, actual cash, and checker status. It does not receive a scenario identity or true risk. Separate concern functions can disagree: in the first emerging-case hearing, A votes no, B and the steward vote yes. The resulting mandate update occurs before the final rule check. A successful vote does not itself install the edited rule.

The enacted mandate feeds `weights(self.mandate)`, which determines the fresh comparison's gain, its effective uncertainty width, and its acceptance decision. An accepted edit changes `self.package`; later ordinary service uses that package's compiled predictions and pays its operating costs. The purpose change is therefore operative, rather than a reporting label or a retrospective change of score.

The stable emerging development run provides a concrete trace:

- At round 92, both arms have the same pilot, the same cash after the paid hearing, and the same ballots. A votes no; B and the steward vote yes. The full arm enacts inclusion; the control records the decision but clamps enactment.
- The full arm has 74.41 cash after the hearing, below the 83.72 required for its planned fresh check and reserve. It records an unfunded check, acquires no confirmation sample for that review, and installs no edited predictor. Its new mandate persists. Adopting an institutional target and financing a particular method are distinct transitions.
- At round 156, a later paid review purchases 426 fresh paired observations per group. Its observed reductions are A=0 and B=201/426. Relative to the actually installed two single-field predictors, the candidate costs an additional .145 per service bundle in the specified normalized objective.
- On those same fresh observations, A-only gain is −.145, whereas inclusive gain is +.0909155. The inclusive lower bound is +.0159547, so the rule is accepted. The A-only criterion would reject from the known cost increase and identical A forecasts alone.
- The full arm installs an A single-field predictor and a B two-field predictor. The subsequent ordinary-service trace actually uses this rule, retains inclusion, and accumulates no further B errors in this scenario. Across 700 ordinary rounds, the full arm has 36 B errors and the fixed-mandate arm 303, with A error zero in both. These are development results, not held-out estimates.
- The full arm also ends with less cash: 280.11 versus 405.71. These outcomes must be reported separately. Lower B error does not establish universal welfare improvement or financial dominance.

The fixed-mandate arm preserves the grievance, pilot, and ballots at the first divergence. It subsequently incurs different costs and has different observations because its actual policy differs. Such downstream divergence is a consequence of the intervention, not an initial information blackout. Its unpromising A-only proposal fails the pilot screen; identical spending across arms would impose a different policy comparison. The structural old-score rejection on the successful full-arm record needs no further sample because its A predictions agree and its known cost is higher.

## Resource and evidence boundaries

Cash is persistent and ordinary contracts generate receipts. Acquisition calls pay before obtaining a linked observation; review entry, hearing, fresh-check affordability, routine operations, checker upkeep, and rebuilding have explicit conditions. A blocked review cannot inspect fresh labels. The final check uses a separate keyed stream from training, inner validation, and the purpose pilot. The mandate and two predictor vectors are frozen before that stream is acquired. The old/inclusive fresh scores are both preserved as diagnostics; only the prospectively enacted score drives acceptance.

The final independent replay verified every transaction prefix: 5,057 full-arm transactions and 4,655 fixed-mandate transactions. Every recorded before/after balance agrees with the actual credit/debit, no intermediate balance is negative, and both final balances reconcile with initial capital plus settled receipts minus expenses. Fitting charges execute before the coarse, singleton, and pair computations they finance. This is a check of the declared financial mechanism, not proof that its price schedule models real scientific work.

Opening affordability covers only construction, inner validation, pilot, a possible hearing, and the reserve. The later fresh-check size and cost are determined separately. Both the next paired-case obligation used in the hearing and the complete confirmation bill derive from `Config` through `paired_case_price`; the default paired-case price is .240. The hearing guard therefore does not promise that a whole confirmation study is already affordable. The round-92 trace exercises that limitation explicitly.

Routine service keeps errors but discards full contextual records. The checker thus represents a specified prospective linked-measurement facility, not an arbitrary permission bit sitting on top of already available reusable training data. Other means of recovering those records are absent from this finite model by assumption. This is a modeled dependency, not a claim that such a facility is necessary for every real scientific community.

An enacted mandate may survive a failed or unaffordable final rule check. That outcome means the institutional target changed while the proposed method remained unaccepted. It must not be counted as a successful method discovery. Conversely, a later resolved B problem should not automatically remove B's standing; the implemented constitution allows inclusion but supplies no revocation procedure.

## Closed corrections and exact participant scope

The earlier missing pilot prediction charge is fixed. Pilot and confirmation use the same configured paired-computation prices. The earlier hardcoded hearing payment is replaced by the configured price helper. Real debits now execute through explicit mandatory-payment calls; they no longer depend on Python assertions being enabled. The final fitting callback moves actual payment before computation; the purpose decisions, paid totals, and service outcomes above remain unchanged on replay. The audit found no remaining purpose/payment integration defect within the stated model.

The three voters remain centrally represented stakeholder concern functions operating on separately retained group records. Their different votes have real causal authority under the supplied majority rule, but they are not autonomous minds, strategic agents, or independently evolving organizations. The final account should describe represented interests and a council procedure, not claim that this version implements a distributed community with separate actor beliefs. The more ambitious actor-local registry proposed in `purpose-mechanism.md` was not implemented and is not needed for the narrower operative-purpose result.

## Claims this implementation can support

This is a finite constructive model of changing an institution's adopted evaluative standing through experience, paid inquiry, different represented interests, and a supplied voting constitution. The resulting target changes what counts as a useful rule and thereby changes service, finances, and later investigative opportunities in the development trace.

The underlying concerns, permissible groups, candidate mandates, voting rule, protected hearing route, value-to-expense conversion, empirical A guard, and available rule edits remain supplied. The model does not originate fundamental values, prove the constitution legitimate, establish group noninferiority statistically, discover all possible purposes, or explain an entire real scientific institution. The pilot's fallible governance choice and the fresh check's conditional statistical claim must remain distinct.
