# Independent audit of the integrated inquiry model

5 October 2026. This audit distinguishes code correctness, conditional statistical guarantees, and the explanatory reach of the constructed model. The pre-validation source hash was `c220ff0d128dee5de0305f7f448671eddd206dda18fbe3f323421b390352623d`. This auditor did not run any held-out seed before the freeze.

## Pre-freeze verification

`audit_implementation.py` instrumented all 30 combinations of the six scenarios and five arms at development seed 1103. It checked each acquired experimental label against an already completed payment, each fitted table against prepaid computation credit, and every chronological cash transaction against the available balance. It independently reconstructed comparison scores, price differences, disagreement bounds, alpha assignments, and sample sizes from saved frozen proposals. The source also produced identical behavioral summaries under normal and optimized Python, verifying that financial debits and decisions do not depend on assert side effects.

Boundary probes confirmed that an unfunded acquisition, missing checker, unaffordable service contract, or blocked review obtains no outcome. A blocked review that never starts a paid probe does not consume an error allowance; a paid attempt does, including one that later fails screening or cannot finance its check. Complete results and source hashes are in `implementation-audit-results.json`.

Earlier development issues were repaired before freezing: uncharged pilot predictions; updating the deployed incumbent through an unlogged refit before acceptance; computation charged after execution; and overly broad upfront financing that priced a future check before the proposal and mandate were known. The current implementation freezes the actual installed package, prepays the next construction/observation operation, stages opening and final-check financing, and changes the deployed package only on acceptance. These are correctness and protocol repairs, not held-out optimization.

## What the checker establishes

A review generates a candidate rule-plus-fitted-predictor package, compares a separate exploratory pilot, possibly changes the operative mandate through a hearing, and then freezes the resulting comparison before drawing final-check data. Training, inner validation, pilot, and final checking have distinct sample-key namespaces. Pilot outcomes may select the proposal or mandate but are not counted as fresh confirmation.

For one final-check round,

G = sum_g w_g(error_incumbent,g - error_candidate,g) - delta,

where delta is the known difference in recurring deployment price. If W is the sum of weights of groups whose compiled forecasts differ on at least one allowed context, G lies in [-W-delta,W-delta]. Its width is 2W. The constant price shift affects expected benefit without increasing uncertainty. The reported Hoeffding radius `2W sqrt(log(1/alpha)/(2n))` is therefore correct under independent rounds from the declared stationary review distribution.

The check sample size is calculated before the final sample; the source does not stop on an early favorable prefix or extend a disappointing checked batch. If it cannot afford the selected final check, it does not accept. If W=0, weighted errors agree for every possible allowed outcome and the comparison reduces to the known cost difference; no uncertain empirical claim is inferred from a zero-width interval.

Each paid review j uses alpha_j=.05/[j(j+1)]. Conditional on all previous observations and decisions, its final candidate, mandate, and n are fixed and the check is fresh. Thus a check of a package whose true expected comparison gain is nonpositive has false-pass probability at most alpha_j. A countable union bound yields total probability at most .05 across such reviews, within one institution's declared stochastic process. Independence between review decisions is unnecessary. Clamping deployment in the fixed-rule arm cannot increase its probability of enacting an erroneous package.

This guarantee concerns current-distribution package comparison. It does not certify the proposal grammar, the purpose vote, future advantage after drift, the economic value of the entire research program, or a collection of different simulated institutions jointly at .05. The mathematical sampling assumption is an ideal independent context/noise process. Keyed hash streams are a reproducible pseudorandom implementation; a fixed deterministic hash tape is not itself a proof of IID randomness.

## Finance, timing, and capacity

The same integer cash account pays model work, experimental evidence, hearings, upkeep, restoration, operation, and refunds. Income consists of settled external contract fees. The model does not borrow against future receipts. Cash conservation and nonnegative intermediate balances are independently checked. Reducing prediction loss is not automatically credited as money; only the specified contract terms affect cash.

Ordinary service settles synchronously within a round: a context is received, a previously installed predictor supplies the prediction, then the outcome and fee/refund settle. The root explicitly retained this simplifying assumption. There is no implemented delayed-feedback queue or concurrent obligation system. Atomic reviews hold the world at the current service-round regime while paid experiments execute; their elapsed time and opportunity costs are not modeled. All named cash prices are included, not all conceivable real-world costs.

Review timing depends on recorded group errors, complaints, a fixed age-based curiosity rule, cooldown, capability, and available cash. It is adaptive under supplied trigger rules. It is not autonomous invention of when to inquire. Maintenance and rebuilding affect the actual prospective linked-record and shadow-evaluation operation used for reviews.

Ordinary service retains error/fee summaries rather than linked raw contexts. The supplied review facility enables richer prospective records and candidate evaluation. This explains a particular action dependency; it does not prove that every alternative form of checking is physically impossible without that facility. Other aggregate or informal checks, substitute infrastructures, and alternative constitutions remain outside the specified action set.

## Purpose and causal interpretation

The mandate genuinely changes the weights used by future package comparisons. The same fresh record is reported under both A-only and inclusive criteria, while only the frozen operative criterion controls that check. A new score does not erase old group outcomes. The fixed-mandate arm preserves records and deliberation while clamping enactment authority, which makes it a useful control for that specific mechanism.

The ballot represents three distinct, supplied interests through separate vote calculations. It is centrally implemented; it does not instantiate independently learning or autonomous stakeholders. Their concerns, representation, majority rule, thresholds, and eligibility are fixed. Votes are fallible pilot-based governance decisions, not statistically certified welfare or ethical legitimacy.

Adoption changes a composite admission-rule and fitted-predictor package. An observed gain is not automatically attributable solely to the admission clause: candidate fitting also uses new data. A separate same-rule-retraining comparison would be needed to isolate that component generally. In the declared feature-interaction mechanism, the broader grammar makes the useful pair available, but the implementation's general inference target remains the package.

The model contains a real chain from observations to proposals, purpose choice, evidence, action, expense/income, and future access. It does not implement unrestricted concept creation, new sensing primitives, new ultimate values, self-chosen contracts, or an autonomous social constitution. A coherent bounded integration is a constructive result; it is not completion of every explanatory debt in a theory of science.

## Independent validation record audit

`audit_recorded_integration.py` reads saved records without running seeds or communicating evaluator knowledge back to the learner. It independently encodes the six declared world laws, enumerates exact per-group risks from each frozen forecast table, adds exact integer-derived deployment prices, and evaluates true current-distribution gain under both mandates. It checks every completed comparison and counts nonpositive formal passes and enacted changes separately. It also verifies all cash identities, per-round ledger continuity, sample provenance, predeclared n, confidence radii, and score arithmetic.

The reserved validation completed on all 1,920 records: 64 predeclared seed clusters, six scenarios, and five arms. The saved-record auditor verified 460 completed comparisons and 188 enacted package changes. Exact evaluator-only enumeration found zero nonpositive-current-distribution formal passes and zero nonpositive enacted changes. The source-integrity report confirms that all ten frozen source/protocol files remained unchanged. These counts are reproducible finite-run diagnostics, not proof of the theorem, a familywise .05 guarantee across all institutions, or evidence of robustness outside the specified scenarios.

All 64 full-arm emerging-interaction runs enacted one package change. In material stress, all 64 lost and rebuilt checking capacity; 61 enacted two changes, while three enacted none. Capacity restoration therefore occurred without guaranteeing successful epistemic adaptation on every run. In noise, 26 of 64 full runs broadened their mandate, but none changed the deployed package. This separates fallible pilot-based purpose enactment from fresh package confirmation: the statistical guarantee covers the latter, not the former. A broader mandate in noise is not a statistical false acceptance under the package-check theorem, nor is it thereby established to be a good governance decision.

The strongest adverse finding is the first predeclared transient seed, 800001. Its full institution accepted the interaction package at round 106. The exact gain under the then-current atomic-review law was +0.105 per service round under the inclusive mandate, while its A-only gain was -0.145. The world reverted to the simple relationship at round 150. Four subsequent reversal attempts, at rounds 170, 234, 298, and 362, could afford opening/pilot costs but could not finance their required final check. Reversal was finally accepted at round 426, with exact current-law gain +0.395. Over the 700 requested B services, the full arm made 145 errors, compared with 31 in each of its four ablations; all five served all 700, so this difference is not an artifact of excluding unmet demand. The other 63 transient full runs enacted no package change, though all broadened the mandate.

Both accepted changes in the adverse transient run were locally valid under their frozen current distributions. Their validity did not protect the institution from costly future mismatch, the burden of paying to establish reversal, or greater realized error than a less adaptive institution. The result is a concrete warning against equating accurate evidence at a review with beneficial whole-trajectory adaptation. It also exhibits the proposed interaction between evidence policy and resources: supplied confidence requirements and repeated opening costs can delay a warranted reversal after the world changes. That mechanism is established within this price/trigger/check design; the experiment does not establish that this particular threshold or review policy is optimal.

The held-out failure was retained without changing the frozen model or replacing the seed. The defensible result is a bounded, causally connected model whose information, purpose, checking, deployment, and material-access mechanisms can be audited together, including an observed failure of beneficial adaptation. It supports explanatory investigation of such mechanisms, not a universal claim that reflexive revision improves science or welfare.
