Turing's 1939 ordinal-logics paper was located through its [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-45.1.161) and a reproduced opening. Its technical results were not used without a verified full-text reading. Dewey's Logic was located but the selected full-text retrieval failed; no Dewey-specific claim appears in the synthesis. These are literature gaps, not evidence of absence of relevant precedents.

The [completed pilot report](reasoning-pilot-findings.md) and [evidence bundle](reasoning-pilot-data.json) record the later synthetic comparison. They add no new historical or doctrinal claim. ## Evidence from the active-inquiry continuation

The [active-inquiry report](active-inquiry-findings.md) and [evidence bundle](active-inquiry-data.json) preserve six finite environments, three treatment instructions, two matched reverse orders, exact private draw provenance, all 18 participant response texts and mechanical feedback rounds, final states, scores, pre-run corrections, and independent audits. Reproduction code and frozen-file content are embedded in the bundle. The superseded paired-draw draft remains identifiable as a pre-run design version.

An independent reconstruction from the public tables agreed with the exact evaluator over all 74 reachable ordered histories, retaining all optimal ties. Engine and scorer fixtures checked malformed responses, missing/duplicate commands, prerequisites, deadlines, sunk fees, cap violations, and cash boundaries. The actual cash caps do not bind in the six evaluated episodes. The complete result audit replayed every feedback round and final state, checked all 25 frozen input hashes and the three initial treatment hashes, and independently recounted every choice.

Each condition achieved 12/12 optimal initial and 22/22 optimal visited commands, with no strictly unnecessary inquiry, invalid command, or cap violation. These are six fresh contexts containing 36 episodes and 66 decisions, not independent item-level replications. The complete menu of models and query mechanisms was given, so successful active selection does not establish conceptual discovery or model adequacy. Stipulated scalar losses do not establish ethically justified purposes.

Bellman's first two page images were retrieved from PMC's image archive after several publisher or full-PDF access attempts failed. They are retained under this chat's work directory. No unexamined theorem from those failed retrievals is relied upon. This continuation adds no new historical claim about 道 or 慧根.
