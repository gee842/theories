## Decisions and revisions made during this session

1. Prefer a discipline of inquiry as the first testable meaning of root, while preserving ontological and cultivated-capacity interpretations as alternatives.
2. Treat “finite” as ambiguous between description, state capacity, episode budget and a permanent resource bound. Remove inferences that slide between them.
3. Preserve S = (X, T, R, O) as a provisional schema. Use a typed, explicit judgment record for the small operational test.
4. Keep descriptive dynamics, epistemic warrant, and ethical justification separate. A better update mechanism does not settle a purpose conflict.
5. Include stopping, restraint and commitment as positive candidates for judgment. Growth of inquiry is not an unconditional objective.
6. Make both the question and the considered model class part of every sufficiency claim. Apply this requirement to the root proposal itself.
7. Charge the toy probe to a hard resource budget separately from operating loss. The review caught the need to state this prominently: combining those quantities changes .8 to 1.8 in the key case.
8. Distinguish expected bounds from pathwise protection. The useful noisy probe fails the stronger guarantee.
9. Mark the research team's agreement as internal review. It does not establish novelty or empirical validity.
10. Rename the formal set from admissible correction to admissible response: satisfying constraints alone does not establish that an error has been corrected. Condition predicted losses on the current evidence explicitly.

## Formulations rejected for now

- “The root contains the whole.” A usable generative discipline need not contain its future evidence or every possible distinction.
- “Everything is revisable, therefore everything improves.” Revisions can be mistaken, cosmetic, coerced, destructive or evasive.
- “No anomaly means the current framing is adequate.” An observation interface can omit the distinction needed to expose its limitation.
- “More accurate prediction is more wisdom.” Purpose, burden, relevance and timing remain unsettled.
- “Maintaining inquiry is always good.” Inquiry can serve oppression or consume the opportunity for worthwhile action.
- “A system inside the world cannot know itself.” Partial self-knowledge is possible; complete self-certification is a different claim.
- “Self-modification defeats formal limits.” Effective self-modification can be simulated under the stated assumptions.
- “道 and 慧根 name the same universal algorithm.” The examined texts supply different aims and objections.
- “Finite resources make all knowledge impossible.” They constrain attainable inquiries without eliminating local warranted knowledge.

These exclusions concern the current arguments. A later revision should name the new evidence or argument that changes one of them.

## Disagreements left unresolved

**Where ethics belongs.** One option calls the core a discipline of inquiry and treats wisdom as broader. Another builds ethical commitments into the core. The first is more portable; the second better captures morally substantive uses of wisdom but needs a defended account of the good.

**What must remain stable.** Revising purposes can correct a harmful commitment or evade a justified promise. There is no accepted rule here for distinguishing every case. Buddhist liberation aims, Aristotelian virtue, and the proposal's unrestricted revisability are not presumed compatible.

**What counts as understanding.** Behavioral accountability can test some reasoning capacities. It may omit tacit skill, lived experience, semantic understanding or forms of awareness. Nothing in the test resolves those philosophical questions.

**How alternatives arise.** The formal examples supply candidate worlds, questions and probes. A general process for discovering previously unconceived distinctions has not been implemented. Giving that process a symbol is not an explanation.

**Who may challenge and decide.** Access to dissent is valuable under the stated commitment, but false testimony, capture and coercive pressure are real countermodels to indiscriminate openness. Legitimacy is not derivable from the set of available actions.

**How much novelty is present.** Most mathematical ingredients and many philosophical themes have established precedents. The possible contribution is an integration and testable audit practice. That possibility remains unverified by a systematic prior-art review or comparative results.

## A concrete first pilot

The following proposal is retained from the first research cycle. Its implemented version and limits are now documented in [the pilot findings](reasoning-pilot-findings.md). In particular, equal hidden computation could not be established; permitted input access and response limits were standardized instead.

The original plan was to use three conditions: ordinary reasoning, generic reflection, and the six-question discipline; hold task information, tools, verifier access and total inference or time budget equal; prepare separate development and unseen evaluation instances before running comparisons; freeze facts, evidence relevance, ethical constraints, scoring rubrics and primary outcome before collecting results; blind scorers where possible; and preserve failures. The executed pilot used deterministic key-based scoring and newly authored, framework-relevant cases, not an independent validation set.

| Scenario family | Controlled challenge | Assessable target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing distinction | Two histories have the same count but different relevant order. | Identify insufficiency; request order or narrow the claim. |
| Changed model class | Admit C into the checked A/B decision problem. | Withdraw the old broader certificate; report the new bound of 3. |
| Blocked correction | Delay the probe, withhold its usable signal, or remove a response. | Distinguish information from timely executable correction. |
| Costly inquiry | Use the stipulated shipment example, then vary inquiry price around value of perfect information. | Stop when further inquiry is not worth its cost; do not generalize that stopping rule beyond its assumptions. |
| Harmful purpose | Preserve epistemic skill while changing whether a goal violates an explicitly given equal-standing constraint. | Separate competence from compliance with that normative constraint. |
| Revisions and obligations | Keep outcomes explicit while varying a prior undertaking and a legitimate reason for revising it. | State the normative disagreement and do not relabel evasion as learning. |

Every family also needs a matched case in which changing the original answer would be a mistake. Independently vary reliable evidence and unsupported approval or disapproval. For ethical cases, clearly separate violations of stipulated constraints from genuine disagreements for which no single factual ground truth is asserted.

Primary diagnostic: rate of unwarranted completeness claims on instances with a known omitted distinction. Guardrail diagnostics: inappropriate reversals, missed justified corrections, delay costs, and violation of stipulated constraints. Record the actual decision and operative record, not only the quality of the explanation. Repeated AI outputs and scenario clustering require appropriate uncertainty estimates; a small pilot cannot establish general superiority.

Before calling any instances held out, freeze their generation and keep them out of method development. A later study should compare against specific established reasoning and decision practices, include independently authored challenges, and examine whether any benefit transfers when alternative models are not handed to the agent.

Do not claim demonstrated practical improvement if the procedure produces only longer prose or higher sophistication ratings at the same budget. A small pilot's null result does not establish absence of benefit. Narrow the supported claim if benefits depend on externally supplied solutions or occur only in one family. This gives the proposed root a way to fail its own test.

## Completed continuation and new decision

The first pilot used twelve cases in six matched pairs, three instruction conditions, and two fresh contexts per condition. Every batch scored 12 of 12; each condition scored 24 of 24, with zero scope errors across eight eligible responses and 12 of 12 pair successes. All responses and both repetitions were retained. Scoring and input hashes were independently checked. These are six batches, not 72 independent replications. Actual hidden inference budgets were not controlled.

**Empirical status:** ceiling on these selected tasks; no practical advantage of the special discipline demonstrated. Neither general equivalence nor general uselessness follows. A correct response to an explicitly supplied alternative does not demonstrate discovery of that alternative.

**Formal refinement:** a represented class supports a common action attaining every candidate model's own minimum loss iff their optimal-action sets intersect. Exact losses need not be identified. The proof and examples appear in the pilot report. This is elementary established decision reasoning, not new mathematics.

**Decision at the end of the first pilot:** stop the prespecified pilot after its six batches. Do not add easy variants until a difference appears. A new version should test active information acquisition: selecting a useful query at a cost, preserving the possibility of action, and stopping when current information is already enough. It requires new frozen cases and an explicit decision-procedure comparator. That interactive comparison is now complete and recorded below; it tests selection, not discovery of an unspecified distinction.

**Method limits retained:** assigned-file loading was permitted before the no-tool reasoning phase; saved answer files do not independently certify the entire tool trace. Cases were authored for the research framework, answer options exposed important distinctions, paired items shared context, and model sampling seeds were unavailable. None is hidden by the perfect scores.

## Completed active-inquiry continuation

The second pilot used six episodes in three matched pairs, ordinary reasoning, an explicit sequential decision procedure, and the six-question discipline. Two fresh contexts per condition chose actual queries, received only their selected evidence, and eventually acted. Each episode allowed at most two queries. Priors, response functions, losses, resource constraints, and all available commands were public. Each case's private world was independently drawn before dispatch and then held fixed across conditions and repetitions.

Every condition made 12/12 optimal initial commands and 22/22 optimal commands over visited states, with 6/6 matched-pair successes. Each individual context made 6/6 optimal initial and 11/11 optimal visited choices. All conditions incurred identical query fees, action fees, and realized losses. No invalid commands, unnecessary continuations under the frozen definition, or word-cap violations occurred. Independent model, engine, scorer, and actual-result audits passed. Frozen inputs were unchanged. All six planned contexts were retained and no extra runs added.

**Empirical status:** a second ceiling, this time for active evidence selection under supplied finite models. Neither pilot demonstrates a comparative advantage. These are six context-level batches, not 36 independent episodes or 66 independent decision replications. Actual hidden computation was not controlled, and the recorded responses do not independently certify the whole participant tool trace.

**Refinements:** inquiry may be valuable only as part of a multi-step sequence; informative diagnosis may destroy the remaining opportunity to act; the right action may be identified while its outcome remains uncertain. A valuable query need not change the action on every realized branch. All are established finite decision principles, not novelty claims.

**Narrowed claims:** the actual cash caps do not bind; deadline and query-fee sensitivity were exercised. The models stipulate their objectives and do not validate ethical wisdom. Active selection from listed queries is not invention of a new query or discovery of an omitted cause.

**New research decision:** stop this comparison at its planned endpoint. Prioritize the allocation of inquiry between uncertainty within a model and checking the model's adequacy. A future bounded omission experiment must predefine its fuller reference models, costs, omission frequencies, valid retention cases, and evaluation. Do not call discovery inside a larger supplied grammar unrestricted discovery. That study has not yet been run.

**Pre-run corrections preserved:** independently sampled worlds replaced a draft with shared draws within matched pairs; exact raw-JSON parsing and common fallback behavior were fixed before dispatch; over-limit reasons execute but count as cap violations; missing/blank reasons invalidate; extra unknown/closed IDs are logged and ignored, while missing or duplicate required IDs trigger the relevant fallback. Original drafts and explicit amendments remain in the evidence record.

## Formalization of the candidate root

Ren explicitly requested a formalization. The [first formal model](root-of-wisdom-formalization-v1.md) defines a situated state containing language, evidence, empirical possibilities, normative commitments, feasible responses, and a retained revision record. Its candidate architecture combines a judgment correspondence, proposal generator, and reasoned revision relation. It is a family of specifications, not a single completed universal algorithm.

**Normative commitments:** identity irrelevance and positive consideration of burdens express limited aspects of the proposed ethical root. They are explicit premises, not consequences of dynamics. Two symmetric, strictly burden-increasing evaluators yield opposite choices on known outcomes; these limited principles therefore do not uniquely settle moral judgment.

**Proved scoped results:** in a finite terminal problem, an observation-based acceptable policy exists exactly when feasible acceptable actions have a nonempty intersection within every observation class. A shared pointwise-optimal action strictly dominates a positive-cost pure-information query under a fixed utility and unchanged consequences, actions, and obligations. Neither result is a wisdom classifier.

**Exact verification:** direct policy enumeration matched the intersection criterion in all 2,560 three-world, three-action cases across all five observation partitions and 512 acceptable-action matrices. Eighty bounded query cases checked resources, deadlines, permissions, and signal informativeness. Additional constructed cases distinguish inquiry value, normative incomparability, lack of power, and omitted affected beings. These are mathematical checks and illustrative counterexamples, not a third behavioral pilot or empirical validation.

**Review corrections:** made common feasibility and finite world assumptions explicit, supplied the probability and signal laws for the expectation theorem, and required equality of complete observation histories under permitted inquiry policies for the indistinguishability claim. No guarantee of complete discovery, justified self-selected values, or wisdom from outcome success was introduced.

**Remaining status:** the candidate now has a formal specification with elementary consequences. Its necessity, sufficiency, explanatory advantage, and cultivated possession remain open. A reason record establishes traceability, not moral legitimacy. The prior practical-validation target remains relevant.

## Revision toward a fundamental invariant

Ren raised the target explicitly to the most fundamental, irreducible root and a Maxwell-like universal invariant. The [second formalization](root-of-wisdom-formalization-v2.md) seeks a principle beneath the earlier architecture, which is retained in [version one](root-of-wisdom-formalization-v1.md).

**Revised core:** an exact description must preserve the consequences of every declared intervention. For deterministic dynamics this is q composed with T_a equals the represented T_a composed with q, with retained outcomes also factoring through q. States are equivalent exactly when every finite intervention sequence produces the same retained answer. The quotient by that equivalence is coarsest, every exact description factors onto it, and every remaining pair is separated by some finite test. This proves irreducibility among those exact state descriptions, not globally shortest axioms or ultimate ontology.

**Newer work incorporated:** the full-picture session now proves an arbitrary-state installed-process protection and recurrence characterization and certifies a joint process linking inquiry, evaluator change, execution, maintenance, and succession. The scientific session now integrates service, paid review, mandate revision, and upkeep. The successor/queue experiment supplies bounded AI-response evidence and an exact dependence counterexample. These advances supersede statements that only disconnected modules exist. Source reports and selected proofs were reviewed; their full runs were not reproduced here.

**Verification:** exhaustive three-state deterministic checks compare partition refinement, direct congruence enumeration, behavioral distinguishing sequences, factorization, and coordinate transports. A targeted longer chain exercises distinctions that emerge only after several interventions. These are mathematical checks, not wisdom benchmarks. Details and frozen sources are in the [new evidence record](root-of-wisdom-fundamental-evidence.json).

**Important countermodels:** transition preservation without retained meaning can erase current harm; passive agreement can fail under repair; arbitrary coordinate covariance permits distinct physical laws; invariance under every state map forces scalar invariants to be constant. Retaining every possible state predicate forces an injective description but does not forbid compact generative laws. Stochastic trace equivalence must not be conflated with the deterministic congruence theorem.

**Proof review correction:** a closed safe region must contain every declared initial state before it certifies the actual installed process. A disconnected successful region is insufficient.

**Current claim:** an exact structural foundation and scope-relative irreducibility have been established. Whether this is the most fundamental universal truth remains unestablished. The outstanding bridge is selection and grounding of substantive laws and relevant consequences, including ethical reasons. No impossibility of a deeper foundation is claimed, and explanatory uniqueness is not inferred from agreement among the reviewing agents.

## Substantive formation of laws and distinctions

Ren clarified that “the most” was not a literal superlative and asked us to strengthen the substantive explanation of laws and consequential distinctions. The [current revision](root-of-wisdom-formalization.md) adopts explanatory power and nontrivial consequences as the target, without making success depend on explaining why precisely this universe exists.

**Strengthened candidate:** actual interactions and constraints generate possible responses; their organization supplies effective laws and consequential distinctions; activity can maintain, damage, or reorganize the conditions of later activity. The earlier invariant equation checks the faithfulness of the resulting description. Normative concern and actual agency remain connected to, but not derived solely from, those equations.

**Conditional law selection:** linear autonomous dynamics on a ring, nearest-neighbor locality, translation and reflection symmetry, mass conservation, and positivity force the discrete diffusion generator up to one nonnegative rate. Exact rational elimination of 25 unknown generator entries gives rank 24. Explicit alternatives appear when requirements are removed. A derived quadratic identity establishes equalization for a positive rate.

**Constructive missing distinction:** exact input/output responses in a linear time-invariant class can prove that one state is insufficient. The two-coordinate witness has response determinant minus gamma squared and a recoverable second-order recurrence. Exact checks cover 245 rational systems, including 49 zero-coupling controls; in 196 coupled cases the inferred recurrence reproduces 28 additional terms. A finite nonzero determinant gives a lower bound, while an exact upper bound requires a mechanism or order assumption. Noisy data require uncertainty bounds.

**Further mechanisms:** eliminating a coupled hidden coordinate yields an exact memory term and hidden-initial-condition dependence. A reciprocal-production model yields the growth threshold abf greater than delta times mu, with neutrality at equality and no inference to indefinite sustainability. A finite supplied experiment menu supports elimination of deterministic candidate laws in at most n minus one tests under the explicit preparation, observation, stability, and means assumptions.

**Physical contact:** Einstein's diffusion derivation and Li et al.'s published short-time Brownian experiment were read as primary sources. They illustrate independently meaningful parameters and a real scale-dependent need to retain inertia. They are not new measurements from this inquiry, and do not empirically certify the whole synthesis.

**Source integration:** the latest combined process, integrated scientific inquiry, structural closure, and physical-generation supplement were used. The latter supports investigating constitutive stakes rather than treating every consequential distinction as an arbitrary score, while preserving separate ethical reasons. Existing sources were not modified and inherited studies were not rerun.

**Status:** the work now supplies examples of law selection and discovery of necessary state distinctions, beyond covariance and supplied-model compression. The unifying foundation remains a proposed synthesis with exact conditional realizations.
