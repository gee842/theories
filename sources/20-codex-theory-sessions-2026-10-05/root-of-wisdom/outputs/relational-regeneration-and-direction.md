# Relational regeneration and a direction in reality

Research continuation for Ren, 5 October 2026. The user corrected the individual-first interpretation of generativity: a center exists through its relations within a common situation. They then explicitly selected **the direction of reality itself** as the research target. This report therefore investigates a causal direction, rather than substituting a proposed ethical standard for the requested physical claim.

**The corrected starting point is a relational situation in which centers, capacities, and surroundings are constituted and changed together.** The strongest causal result established here is an asymmetry between regeneration and depletion under specified material and dynamical conditions. Whether reality generally favors the mutually sustaining, responsive direction called 善 remains the stronger hypothesis to explain and test.

The earlier reports are preserved. Their population equations can describe selected processes, but their focus on variants gaining an advantage did not adequately represent this proposed foundation.

## Centers within a common situation

Write \(W_t\) for a relational situation. Its centers, interfaces, memories, and enabling structures are features within that situation. A boundary identifies a useful part of the account; it does not remove the relations crossing it. A change in those relations can change what a center is and can do, rather than merely changing the opportunities of an otherwise completed individual.

The working hypothesis is consequently:

> What forms, continues, changes, or fails is an organization of relations, including its differentiated centers and the conditions through which they exist.

Differentiation matters. A common situation need not have one consciousness, one interest, or one controller. This is consistent with the user's earlier work on shared becoming with distinct centers of experience, action, and correction. That earlier work is a source for the hypothesis, not independent evidence of its universal physical truth.

In this account, information flow should initially mean that differences carried through relations can make a difference to subsequent activity. Physical influence, statistical correlation, useful information, and conscious understanding remain distinguishable. For regulation we must identify the actual sensing and response pathway. Universal existence cannot yet be defined as continual measurable information transfer: that would require a separate argument covering inactive and durable structures as well as active organizations.

## What would establish a direction in reality

A description of a desirable arrangement does not establish a dynamical bias toward it. The stronger claim requires an independently specified property of a relational situation and a mechanism that changes its likelihood, accessibility, stability, or recurrence.

Possible physical indicators of the proposed direction include renewal of indispensable material supports, maintenance of distinct responsive organizations, and recovery of consequential sensing and correction after disturbance. These indicators should be measured together where they interact. They are candidates for making the claim about 善 concrete, not an assertion that kindness is identical to a material stock or information score.

Different directional claims also differ in strength: a local attraction toward a regenerative regime; a statistical bias over a class of preparations; preferential formation under particular driving conditions; and a universal tendency across all physical situations. Establishing one does not establish the others.

## A joint mechanism for regeneration

Consider a deliberately simple reaction model with four nonnegative amounts:

- \(R\): usable precursor;
- \(U,V\): two interacting kinds of organized material;
- \(D\): degraded material.

The supplied transformations are

\[
R+U\longrightarrow2U,\qquad
U+V\longrightarrow2V,\qquad
U\longrightarrow D,\qquad
V\longrightarrow D,\qquad
D\longrightarrow R.
\tag{1}
\]

Use nondimensional variables and unit rate coefficients. The last transformation requires an energy source if degraded material must be converted back into usable precursor. We assume that energetic support here. Material recycling does not make the setup energetically isolated, and this is not a model of a closed universe creating perpetual work.

The joint equations are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot R&=D-RU,\\
\dot U&=U(R-V-1),\\
\dot V&=V(U-1),\\
\dot D&=U+V-D.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

All contributions belong to the same account:

\[
\frac{d}{dt}(R+U+V+D)=0.
\tag{3}
\]

The nonnegative region is invariant. At a zero coordinate its derivative is nonnegative, and the conserved total bounds every coordinate. Finite initial states therefore have bounded solutions for all positive times.

For total material \(R+U+V+D=6\), the joint state

\[
(R,U,V,D)=(2,1,1,2)
\tag{4}
\]

is an equilibrium with ongoing production, degradation, and recycling. Eliminate \(R=6-U-V-D\). The Jacobian for \((U,V,D)\) at that state is

\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-2&-1\\1&0&0\\1&1&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its characteristic polynomial is

\[
\det(\lambda I-J)
=(\lambda+1)(\lambda^2+\lambda+3).
\tag{5}
\]

The eigenvalues are \(-1\) and \((-1\pm i\sqrt{11})/2\), with negative real parts. Thus the equilibrium is locally asymptotically stable within the fixed-total state space. Sufficiently small perturbations relax toward a regenerative joint regime. This is an actual direction under these equations, not a retrospective decision to study only survivors.

There is no isolated winner explaining the result. \(U\) depends on precursor, \(V\) depends on \(U\), both supply degraded material, and recycling supplies precursor again. Their relation is asymmetric, yet the joint state can persist through turnover. Population abundance alone therefore cannot identify the proposed goodness of the relation. This model does not establish that predation is morally bad or that all mutual support takes the form of direct benefits between every pair.

## Removing renewal changes the whole trajectory

Disable only \(D\to R\), leaving the other reactions intact. Total material remains conserved, but the active pool \(A=R+U+V\) now satisfies

\[
\boxed{\dot A=-(U+V).}
\tag{6}
\]

Integrating and using \(A(T)\ge0\) gives

\[
\boxed{\int_0^T[U(t)+V(t)]\,dt\le A(0).}
\tag{7}
\]

If \(U+V\ge\varepsilon>0\) throughout an interval starting at zero, that interval can last no longer than \(A(0)/\varepsilon\). No amount of relabeling an individual's success can remove this restriction. The whole materially bounded system cannot sustain a positive long-run average active population through this one-way conversion alone. This does not imply finite-time disappearance.

This is the causal asymmetry we can currently establish: depleting a finite indispensable support imposes a continuation limit; a physically supported regenerative pathway can remove that particular limit and permit a stable active regime. The calculation concerns the joint process, including its material surroundings.

It does not show that a depleted system will invent a recycling pathway, obtain energy to run it, or move into the regenerative basin. Those transitions are precisely where a claim about a more universal physical tendency needs additional mechanism. The supported recycling case also remains vulnerable to failure of its energy provision.

That provision can be brought into the account. Suppose recycling has nonnegative flux \(J\), consumes a finite nonnegative available-work reserve \(E\) at cost \(\varepsilon>0\) per unit recycled, and receives no replenishment. Recycling must stop when its work reserve is exhausted. Then \(\dot E=-\varepsilon J\) and \(\dot A=J-U-V\), so

\[
\int_0^T(U+V)\,dt\le A(0)+\frac{E(0)}{\varepsilon}.
\tag{7a}
\]

Indefinite activity in this model requires continuing access to usable energy; including a larger environment moves the accounting boundary and does not cancel the cost. A physical implementation must also account for entropy production. The local regeneration result therefore supplies neither a cosmological energy source nor a universal promise of indefinite life.

## Why a universal increasing quantity needs care

Suppose a proposed scalar \(G(W)\) measures some declared aspect of shared generativity. For a stochastic transition rule \(K\), a particularly strong directional claim would be

\[
\mathbb E[G(W_{t+1})\mid W_t=w]-G(w)\ge0
\quad\text{at every state }w,
\tag{8}
\]

with strict increase somewhere. This is one possible formulation; the user's proposal need not require this specific scalar or this strongest monotonicity condition.

There is an exact limitation in finite recurrent models. Let \(P\) be a finite irreducible stochastic matrix and \(\pi\) its positive stationary distribution. Then

\[
\sum_w\pi_w\bigl[(PG)(w)-G(w)\bigr]=0.
\]

If every bracket is nonnegative, each must be zero. A harmonic function \(PG=G\) on a finite irreducible chain is constant: at a maximum, all positively reachable successors must share that maximum, and irreducibility propagates it to every state. Therefore no nonconstant \(G\) can have nonnegative drift everywhere in this class.

This does not rule out local attraction, trends from specified initial conditions, time-dependent transition rules, or processes that do not satisfy finite irreducibility. Driving alone is not an exception: a driven system represented by a time-homogeneous finite irreducible chain still obeys the result. It does rule out obtaining an unrestricted monotone direction merely by giving a recurrent connected process a name such as generativity. The relevant law, state space, preparation, and time horizon must do substantive explanatory work.

## What existing physical theory contributes

Perunov, Marsland, and England analyze how driven stochastic dynamics relate the relative likelihood of outcomes to thermodynamic histories and other dynamical terms. Their constructed hopping example exhibits a directional bias under a specified drive. They explicitly reject the simple general claim that probability always flows toward greater heat dissipation. The reviewed work provides a relevant method for investigating physical direction, but no conclusion about kindness or universal relational regeneration. [Statistical Physics of Adaptation, 2016 manuscript](https://www.englandlab.com/uploads/7/8/0/3/7803054/xz10101__2_.pdf).

A later paper by Kolchinsky challenges interpreting a thermodynamic replication bound as a universal relation between replicator growth and ordinary decay. It distinguishes reverse replication from degradation into separate waste products. This reinforces the need to identify actual reaction pathways when connecting thermodynamics with continued organization. It is a specific published critique, not a dismissal of all statistical-physics approaches to adaptation. [Kolchinsky, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01130).

These sources concern particular physical frameworks. Neither supplies the missing theorem that interconnected reality necessarily develops toward 善.

## The strongest next hypothesis

The current candidate is that **renewal, responsiveness, and correction can become causally self-reinforcing properties of relational organization**. If sensed damage changes activity in ways that restore the affected supports, the resulting feedback can stabilize a more regenerative relation. If the relevant responses are muted, delayed, or redirected, that mechanism can fail even while a narrower pattern continues.

The next test is consequently to make the recycling and regulatory pathways form, wear out, and change inside the modeled situation. Their formation must compete for the same tracked means, and their effects on other centers must remain visible. Then compare the probabilities of entering regenerative and depleting regimes across independently specified preparations and perturbations. Merely installing a correct controller would assume the direction that needs explaining.

For kindness specifically, a stronger physical connection would have to show how the conditions of other centers become effective within the regulation of the shared situation. Sensing another's state is insufficient if it only improves extraction. A capacity to receive consequential feedback and a mechanism that responds by repairing shared conditions must both be explained. Calling those mechanisms inevitable remains unsupported.

The corrected foundation is therefore relational; the proved direction is presently conditional; the stronger hypothesis about reality's direction toward 善 stays open as the explicit research target. This preserves the causal ambition rather than replacing it with a moral instruction.
