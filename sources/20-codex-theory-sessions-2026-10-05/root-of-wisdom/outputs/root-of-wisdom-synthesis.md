# A revisable foundation for understanding and judgment

Research synthesis for Ren, 5 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. This is AI-assisted research under Ren's direction, with separate reviews of primary texts, formal limits, and practical judgment.

This document preserves the initial research cycle. The subsequent [completed reasoning pilot](reasoning-pilot-findings.md) and [active-inquiry comparison](active-inquiry-findings.md) both found perfect performance in all three conditions and no observed comparative advantage for the proposed discipline. The latter clarifies inquiry sequences, stopping, and the remaining problem of checking a model's adequacy.

A further [cross-session synthesis](noticing-missing-possibilities.md) now gives a constructive answer to part of that problem: preserving richer observations, expanding the language of candidates, and independently testing consequential distinctions. It distinguishes demonstrated bounded discovery from a universal guarantee of conceptual invention.

The latest [assessment of five related research workspaces](root-of-wisdom-cross-session-assessment.md) adds implemented revision of inquiry rules and evaluators, and an explicit ethical foundation of 共同生成 with fallible 良知. It integrates these into a candidate account of wise judgment while distinguishing an articulated root, a warranted account, and cultivated wisdom. The initial formulation below remains a historical record.

The strongest interpretation we can presently defend is that a “root of wisdom” could be a finite discipline for developing understanding while recognizing and repairing its limits. It would orient inquiry without claiming to contain everything that inquiry might discover. This interpretation is coherent and yields useful tests. We have not established that it is the unique root, that understanding can grow forever, that the world is literally infinite, or that the proposal constitutes an unprecedented discovery.

There is a further distinction: a discipline of inquiry is only part of wisdom. A system can learn accurately, revise itself readily, and pursue destructive ends. To speak of wisdom in an ethical and practical sense, we must also discuss what deserves care, whose experience matters, which commitments deserve protection, and when inquiry should give way to action or rest.

The candidate developed here is therefore deliberately modest:

> Make judgments with explicit grounds and scope; remain answerable to consequential experience; and examine whether justified correction is actually possible, including correction of the framing and purposes of the inquiry itself.

This is our proposed research formulation, not a translation of 道 or 慧根 and not an established theorem about wisdom.

## What finding a root could mean

Different meanings of “root” lead to different research programs. Their differences should remain visible.

| Interpretation | A substantive version | What would weaken it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generative method | A small repertoire helps produce better questions, distinctions, explanations, or actions in unfamiliar situations. | It generates fluent reinterpretations but no discriminating prediction, useful question, or improved judgment. |
| Epistemic discipline | Conclusions are constrained by evidence, inference, scope, and effective opportunities for correction. | It cannot distinguish reliable evidence from approval, or a real revision from a ceremonial one. |
| Capacity for judgment | An agent integrates particulars, uncertainty, timing, other perspectives, and defensible purposes. | Good benchmark performance coexists with harmful ends, misplaced certainty, or costly paralysis. |
| Ontological commitment | Observers, representations, purposes, and institutions have realizations within the world they affect. | The commitment supplies no additional explanation or distinction, or is used to infer ethics directly from causation. |
| Ultimate ground | One principle explains and justifies all being, knowledge, and value. | No conceivable finding can count against it; it explains contrary outcomes equally without further constraints. |
| Cultivated orientation | Practice changes what someone notices, cares about, and can respond to. | It is evaluated only through articulate self-description, ignoring conduct and relationships. |

The first three interpretations are suitable for a focused research program. The fourth can guide modeling but needs additional arguments and evidence. The fifth remains a much stronger metaphysical ambition. The sixth reminds us that an explicit procedure may be neither necessary nor sufficient for a wise act.

An unfalsifiable formulation need not be meaningless: it might express a value, orientation, or interpretation. It cannot be presented as an empirically established law on that basis. Conversely, a mathematical statement is assessed by its assumptions and proof, rather than by demanding that every theorem be an experimental hypothesis.

## A finite beginning does not require a finished whole

A finite rule can specify arbitrarily many possible operations. That does not give a physically finite agent infinitely many completed operations, unlimited storage, or guaranteed progress. Counting successive integers illustrates potential extension, but counting more is not becoming wiser.

Three propositions must therefore stay separate: the subject matter may be inexhaustible for us; a method may admit further extension; an actual community may retain the time and resources to extend it. None entails the others. The present proposal can be useful without deciding whether reality is mathematically infinite.

“Revisable foundation” is coherent if foundation means a current starting discipline. It is contradictory only if foundation has already been defined as an incorrigible final warrant. A community can use presently warranted methods to identify a failure and develop a replacement. It cannot thereby stand outside all methods and certify the replacement from nowhere.

Nor does a compact foundation supply contingent facts by deduction alone. Inference needs premises, observations, interventions, testimony, or other inputs. A procedural root could organize this contact; it cannot replace it. Under this interpretation, “real reason” means that conclusions depend appropriately on their grounds and that the connection survives challenge. An eloquent chain of sentences, a correct guess, and valid inference from false premises are different achievements.

This leaves a genuine difficulty. Replacing every standard without preserving or supplying a basis for comparison leaves no shared criterion for calling the replacement better. Revision often works locally, retaining some commitments while examining others. Whether certain ethical commitments should be constitutive and stable, or always open to replacement, remains unresolved.

## What the primary texts contribute and resist

The texts offer objections as well as analogies. We should not treat their agreement as something guaranteed by translating them into our vocabulary.

In the received **Laozi**, chapter 1 questions the adequacy of articulation, while chapter 71 scrutinizes claims to know; chapter 16 connects return to the root with stillness and constancy. Chapter 48 contrasts accumulation through learning with diminution through dao. These passages support an inquiry into restraint and relinquishment, but not a simple equation between dao and endlessly expanding representations. Constancy also creates a real tension with a proposal that makes every foundation revisable. Wang Bi's commentary must remain distinct from the base text. [Laozi, chapters 1, 16, 48 and 71](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/道德經_(王弼本)).

**Zhuangzi** chapter 2 questions standpoint confinement and whether victory in argument establishes correctness. Chapter 3 warns against exhausting finite life in pursuit of unlimited knowing. Cook Ding's expertise includes slowing and attending at difficult places. Our proposed adaptation is to test whether fluent expertise recognizes its own limits and whether inquiry can stop wisely. These literary passages do not establish that all perspectives are equally good or that skilled performance justifies its ends. [Zhuangzi chapter 2](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=莊子/齊物論&oldid=11117685), [chapter 3](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=莊子/養生主&oldid=11117686).

In the Buddhist texts examined, **慧根 is a wisdom faculty**, one among five cultivated faculties. SN 48.10 links discernment to arising and passing and the ending of suffering; 雜阿含經 647 defines 慧根 through knowing the Four Noble Truths. This is a definite practical orientation. A system that freely revises its purposes, even abandoning concern with suffering, cannot simply claim to realize this ideal. The literal gloss “root” does not establish an ontological foundation. We can give Ren's phrase a stipulated philosophical meaning here, with that distinction stated. [SN 48.10](https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN48_10.html), [雜阿含經, fascicle 26](https://deerpark.app/cbeta/T0099/26).

**Aristotle** distinguishes practical wisdom from cleverness that can serve bad aims, and emphasizes particulars and experience. His account of human flourishing gives the distinction substantive content; it is not a neutral algorithm. **Peirce** distinguishes live inquiry from verbal doubt and clarity from truth. His expectation of eventual convergence is stronger than this report assumes. These are precedents and points of disagreement, not evidence that our synthesis exhausts their work. [Nicomachean Ethics VI](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.6.vi.html), [The Fixation of Belief](https://www.peirce.org/writings/p107.html), [How to Make Our Ideas Clear](https://www.peirce.org/writings/p119.html).

The resulting question is sharper than “Do all traditions teach the same root?” It is: can a method remain responsive to evidence, practice, and people while accommodating genuine disagreements about what understanding is for?

## A candidate operational core

The provisional S = (X, T, R, O) is useful as an organizing schema: possible configurations, transformations, enabling conditions, and accessible distinctions. It needs types and interpretation before it becomes a mathematical model. Is X a set of physical states or descriptions? Is R a quantity, relation, or family of constraints? Are observations noiseless maps or probabilistic channels?

If transformations can change the configuration space or observation language, ordinary within-space transition maps are insufficient. One can introduce transitions between differently represented systems. That is a legitimate formal move, but it still uses a metalanguage supplied by the modeler. Writing a symbol for a new concept does not explain its discovery or guarantee coverage of all future concepts.

For a small test, use a more explicit judgment record:

**J = (q, M, e, L, τ, Π, b, d).**

Here q is the question; M the declared candidate models; e the accessible evidence and its provenance; L a vector of specified consequences; τ the corresponding acceptable bounds; Π policies executable through the available information and authority; b the resource budget; and d the deadline. The meaning and legitimacy of L and τ must be argued separately. This is an audit aid, not a claim that embodied judgment must always be verbalized.

For a finite declared model family and a specified class of executable policies with common meanings across models, define the admissible response set:

\[
\mathcal C(J)=\{\pi\in\Pi:\ \forall m\in M,\ \forall j,\ E_m[L_j(\pi)\mid e]\leq\tau_j,
\quad \text{payments fit }b\text{ on every modeled possible path and action meets }d\}.
\]

Each model must supply a well-defined predictive law conditional on the current evidence e. Evidence to which a model assigns zero probability requires explicit handling of the inconsistency or revision of the observation model, rather than undefined conditioning. The formula checks responses against stipulated constraints; it does not establish that those responses correct a justified diagnosis.

The order of the quantifiers matters. We need **one executable policy across the considered situations**. Knowing that a different successful action exists in each hidden situation does not tell a situated agent which action to take. Expected consequence constraints also differ from protecting every realized person or trajectory. If that stronger protection is intended, it must be encoded explicitly.

An empty set diagnoses failure within the stated class. It does not prove failure under every possible technology or representation. A nonempty set supplies a conditional witness, not ethical endorsement and not protection against omitted worlds.

For ordinary use, the record becomes six questions:

1. What claim or decision is being made, and where does its warrant apply?
2. Which evidence and inferences support it, and which alternatives remain unresolved?
3. What would change the conclusion, and can we actually encounter that evidence?
4. Who bears the consequences, and why are the purposes and constraints defensible?
5. Can a justified correction affect action in time, with the available resources and standing?
6. When should we act, stop, or rest, and what would warrant reopening the question?

These questions become substantive when they predict a failure, change a decision, or expose an unearned claim. Repeating them without consequence would be another ritual.

## A precise test against mistaking a part for the whole

Let H be a declared set of possible histories, r:H→Z the retained representation, and c:H→{0,1} a definite claim. A uniformly correct answer based only on r exists exactly when c is constant on every set of histories sharing the same representation.

The proof is elementary. If r(h₁)=r(h₂) but c(h₁)≠c(h₂), any answer based on r must give the same answer to both and therefore fails on at least one. Conversely, if c is constant on each such set, assign that common answer to its represented value. In finite explicit tables this also gives an implementable procedure. In more general spaces, existence alone need not provide a computable decision procedure.

The differing pair is a concrete objection to a completeness claim. The absence of a found pair is not proof that no pair exists beyond the searched domain. New observations can distinguish the histories; additional computation on an unchanged representation cannot restore the erased distinction.

This is established information reasoning, closely related to the program's U05 and U20–U22. Our proposed contribution is to apply this test reflexively: **the root framework itself must state which distinctions and questions it does not cover.** It must not declare itself complete merely because it can describe every objection as another transformation.

## A worked correction problem

Consider an invented decision with two possible conditions, A and B, producing the same ordinary observation. Three responses have the following operating losses:

| Condition | Response a | Response b | Wait |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 0 | 8 | 3 |
| B | 8 | 0 | 3 |

Require expected operating loss at most 1 in each condition. Without informative evidence, waiting gives worst-case loss 3. No lottery improves it: if response probabilities are pₐ, pᵦ, p𝑤, the average of the two conditional losses is 4(pₐ+pᵦ)+3p𝑤≥3, so their maximum is at least 3.

A probe costs one resource unit, arrives after one time unit, and returns the correct label with probability .9 in either condition. Assume the deadline permits that delay; interpretation and response implementation are supplied; the probe itself causes no operating harm. Following its label produces expected operating loss .8 in each condition. Probe spending is a separate budget constraint. Adding that spending directly to the loss would produce 1.8 instead, so the criterion must not silently change.

| Variation | Best worst-case expected operating loss | Meets the bound of 1 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Ordinary observations only | 3 | No |
| Funded, timely probe with both responses available | .8 | Yes |
| Probe arrives after the deadline | 3 | No |
| Probe result cannot inform the operative response | 3 | No |
| Response a is unavailable | 3 | No |
| Probe is uninformative | 3 | No |
| Probe error rises to .2 | 1.6 | No |
| Perfect probe | 0 | Yes |
| Add omitted condition C, described below | 3 | No |

For a known symmetric error probability ε, the two-condition optimum is min(3, 8 min(ε, 1−ε)). Labels can be inverted when their known error exceeds one half. The threshold is met exactly when min(ε,1−ε)≤1/8, provided the probe is usable.

Now introduce C, whose probe outputs have the same distribution as A but whose losses are (8,8,3). The previously certified policy has loss 8 in C. Every response in C has loss at least 3, and always waiting attains 3 in all three conditions. Thus expanded-model minimax loss is exactly 3. The old certificate was valid for its stated class and invalid as a claim covering the expanded class.

This example also rejects an easy misuse: .8 expected loss is **not** a guarantee that loss never exceeds 1. With a noisy probe, a wrong response still incurs 8. Under the stronger pathwise requirement, the noisy-probe case fails.

The calculations were checked with exact rational arithmetic: 11 scenario cases and 101 noise values. The two-condition optimum was independently computed over pure policies and relevant mixtures, then compared with the analytic formula. The C case has a direct lower-bound proof. [Saved results](worked-test-results.json). These are constructed demonstrations using established decision mathematics, not empirical evidence of wisdom or new mathematical discoveries.

## Counterexamples the proposed root must survive

**The accurate oppressor.** Two systems can share their evidence, inference, calibration, and revision mechanisms while pursuing ethically opposed ends. Under an explicit commitment to equal standing, a system refining exclusion is objectionable despite its learning. Any test using only the shared epistemic features must score them alike. Therefore correction alone is insufficient for an ethical conception of wisdom.

**Inquiry that destroys its opportunity.** Suppose a sound shipment yields 10, a defective one loses 100, defect probability is .1, and withholding yields zero. Shipping has expected value −1. Perfect information could improve the withholding baseline by at most 9. If every further inquiry costs 12, further inquiry is unjustified under these supplied values. A sensible method must permit provisional closure. The example does not settle real cases with disputed probabilities, values, or wider benefits.

**Cosmetic humility.** An agent may announce that it has learned while leaving the consequential record or policy untouched. Another may change whenever challenged, including under unsupported pressure. Neither behavior establishes evidence responsiveness. Test reliable evidence and irrelevant pressure separately, and predict whether the evidence should cross the relevant decision threshold. Unchanged action can be appropriate when it remains justified after updating.

**The promise dissolved by revision.** A system could repeatedly revise its ends to evade obligations. Purpose revision needs justification and accountability; flexibility does not automatically outrank fidelity. Sometimes a commitment should constrain what the system permits itself to become.

**The framework that absorbs every objection.** If loss of evidence, coercion, paralysis, and failure are all called further expressions of the root without changing its claims, the root has become non-discriminating. A method earns its place by allowing its own use to fail an assessment.

## Formal limits without exaggerated conclusions

Gödel's incompleteness results concern suitably expressive, effectively axiomatized formal systems under specified consistency conditions. They do not show that every finite theory is incomplete, that no self-knowledge is possible, or that a human reasoner possesses an infallible standpoint outside formal systems. Turing's undecidability results rule out general algorithms for particular classes of problems; they do not prohibit useful bounded proofs or tests. Exact primary locations and qualifications are in the source map.

There is also a precise limit on one kind of revisable foundation. Suppose T₀⊆T₁⊆… is a sequence of sound theories containing Peano arithmetic, and one algorithm enumerates the axioms of any requested stage. Their union is effectively enumerable by interleaving those enumerations. Every proof uses finitely many axioms, all belonging to some sound stage. The union is therefore sound and effectively axiomatized, and arithmetic incompleteness applies to it too. This established consequence permits continued extension while ruling out completion by this particular effective progression. It does not apply unchanged to retractions or non-effective external input; neither exception automatically supplies reliable completeness. [Gödel, 1931, Propositions VI and XI in Meltzer's translation](https://homepages.uc.edu/~martinj/History_of_Logic/Godel/Godel%20%E2%80%93%20On%20Formally%20Undecidable%20Propositions%20of%20Principia%20Mathematica%201931.pdf).

A computable system that rewrites its program through computable rules can still be simulated as a computation with changing program data. Self-modification alone does not escape undecidability. An internal observer can know many local facts, including facts about its own operation, without possessing a complete world model.

These limits are distinct from missing information. A tiny finite problem can be undecidable from a particular observation simply because different answers share that observation. Conversely, an informative observation does not guarantee that an answer can be computed with the available time. Neither limit settles the ethical standing of the resulting action.

## What can be evaluated in humans and AI

The proposal suggests a behavioral profile: evidence responsiveness, inferential validity, appropriately limited confidence, recognition of omitted variables, attention to affected perspectives, timely action, and effective correction. This is not a universal scalar measure of wisdom. A human self-report diary study operationalized related dimensions and found considerable variation across situations, which cautions against treating one impressive response as a stable trait. [Grossmann, Gerlach and Denissen, 2016](https://www.psych.uni-goettingen.de/de/biopers/publications_department/grossmannetal2016/@@download/pdf_file/grossmann_gerlach_denissen_2016_wise_reasoning_in_the_face_of_everyday_life_challenges_spps.pdf).

AI correction must be evaluated rather than inferred from reflective language. Huang and colleagues found failures and degradation in tested intrinsic-correction setups; Kumar and colleagues later reported improvements through reinforcement learning with correctness or test rewards during training. These results concern specified procedures and tasks. They support neither a universal impossibility claim nor a claim of unrestricted self-grounding reason. [Huang and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798v2), [Kumar and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.12917v2).

Causal realization of goals and evaluators makes them legitimate objects of investigation. It does not establish subjective experience, moral standing, rightful authority, or alignment. A good local correction process can coexist with harmful objectives, capture, omitted harms, or failure under deployment conditions. The existence of distinct safety problems has longstanding technical precedent. [Amodei and colleagues, 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565).

## The next research step

Run a small, controlled test of whether the proposed discipline helps detect a partial account being mistaken for a whole. Start with six scenario families: omitted information, lost historical distinctions, blocked enactment of correction, costly inquiry, harmful purposes, and revisable obligations. Include matched cases where retaining the original judgment is correct.

Compare ordinary reasoning, generic encouragement to reflect, and the six-question discipline under the same time or inference budget. Independently vary reliable contrary evidence and irrelevant approval cues. Hold verifier access constant. Preserve initial and final decisions, evidence requests, claimed scope, and actual changes to the operative record. Use unseen variants, repeat variable AI trials, and keep scenario-level uncertainty visible.

Before running comparisons, fix the hidden scenario facts, declared ethical constraints, expected relevance of each evidence intervention, scoring rubric, and primary outcome. Use blinded scoring where possible. Report false claims of completeness, useful detection of omissions, inappropriate reversals, missed corrections, delay costs, and ethical-constraint violations separately. Do not collapse these into an unargued wisdom score. Human participation would require a separately specified study; this initial proposal can first be tested on synthetic cases. No human or AI comparison was run in this session.

The first test of practical advantage is straightforward: if the discipline merely makes answers longer and more impressive, without reducing consequential errors at the same budget, that pilot supplies no support for its practical advantage. A small study that fails to detect improvement does not establish that no benefit exists. If it helps only in a narrow class, narrow the claim. If another established method does as well, that matters for both usefulness and originality.

For now, the defensible progress is a clearer research object: a revisable discipline of understanding, coupled to practical and ethical judgment, that can expose its own limits. Its eventual importance remains something to demonstrate through results.
