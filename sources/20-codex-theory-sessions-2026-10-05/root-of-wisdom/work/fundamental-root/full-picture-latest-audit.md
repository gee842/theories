## Strongest candidate for the requested equation

For a concrete finite controlled process with kernel \(K_{u,d}\), retained labels \(L\), and a proposed surjective description \(q:S\to Q\), let \(C(s,a)=1\{q(s)=a\}\). The elementary but substantive preservation equation is

\[
\boxed{K_{u,d}C=C\bar K_{u,d}\quad\text{for every admitted }(u,d),\qquad L=\bar L\circ q.}
\]

**Taking the consequences of an intervention and then changing description must agree with changing description first and then applying the represented intervention.** This is a precise invariance condition, with supplied information and permission maps preserved separately. It is a stronger root for exact representation than naming three faculties such as judgment, generation, and revision.

It forbids a description that merges situations whose retained consequences differ under an admitted intervention. It preserves complete retained path laws under the same admissible observation-based policies, not just agreement on a sample or on passive averages. It composes through sequential mechanisms, compatible guards, and actually independent parallel mechanisms. Shared noise, hidden guards, costs, and intermediate harms require their own retained interfaces.

### Exact relative irreducibility

Start by grouping states with the same retained labels. Repeatedly split a block whenever states in it have different transition probabilities into any current block under an admitted input. For a finite model this process terminates. Every faithful partition refines every stage, by induction: a coarse block is a union of its blocks, so equality of probabilities to its own blocks implies equality to their unions. The terminal partition is itself faithful and therefore is the unique coarsest faithful partition.

The result supplies a mathematically exact sense of “nothing further can be removed”: merging any two remaining quotient blocks breaks this state-level contract. The scope is deterministic partitions of this finite state model, with these labels and interventions. It is not minimality among all imaginable concepts, stochastic latent models, all coding languages, or all true theories. Combining all coordinates into one variable shows why counting primitive names cannot establish ontology-level irreducibility.

### Minimal countermodel

Let two states \(s_0,s_1\) have the same present label. Under `wait`, each stays unchanged. Under `repair`, \(s_0\to g\) and \(s_1\to b\), where \(g,b\) carry different retained labels. Passive histories cannot distinguish the initial pair, but a quotient merging them would require the single repair row to assign both probability one and probability zero to the good block. The equation fails. This is an exact falsifier of an inadequate proposed root.

If `repair` was absent from the original intervention contract, merging the pair could have been valid there. Adding it refines the coarsest faithful partition. This directly connects irreducibility to our missing-possibility question: a description may be irreducible and adequate for its current questions yet omit a distinction exposed by a newly admitted intervention or affected party's outcome. Relative completeness and openness to revision are compatible.

\[
\boxed{B\subseteq A\subseteq K,\quad \Delta(A)\subseteq A,\quad
\neg E_j(\gamma)\Rightarrow\rho_j(z')<\rho_j(z).}

The implication is restricted to actual transitions from \(A\). Rank can reset on genuine delivery. Sufficiency is induction plus impossibility of infinite descending ordinals. ## What universal invariance can and cannot mean

Three notions must be distinguished.

1. **Covariance under relabeling.** A bijective change of state coordinates can transport any transition law. An absorbing two-state law and a toggling two-state law both admit perfectly covariant descriptions. Covariance alone does not choose either law or predict whether the process settles or alternates.
2. **Faithful reduction.** A noninvertible description must obey the displayed intertwining equation. Many proposed reductions fail. This is a real restriction on descriptions, though every model admits the identity description.
3. **A substantive world law.** A proposed family of mechanisms excludes other coherent possible mechanisms and derives independently testable quantitative relationships. The supplied research does not yet identify one universal family whose empirical coverage and unique foundational status have been established.

There is also a simple boundary theorem. Let every map \(g:X\to X\) count as an allowed transformation, and demand \(f(g(x))=f(x)\) for all of them. For any \(x,y\), use the constant map \(g(\cdot)=y\); then \(f(y)=f(x)\). Thus every such invariant is constant. Even all bijections force constant scalar functions when their action is transitive. An informative universal-invariance claim must identify what changes and what structure is preserved. This is not an objection to ambitious unification; it is how to make the ambition nonempty.
