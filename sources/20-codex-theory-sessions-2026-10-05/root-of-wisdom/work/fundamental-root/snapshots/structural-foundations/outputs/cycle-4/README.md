# Adoption learning and transmission of continuation capacity

Continuation: the [fifth cycle](../cycle-5/README.md) now implements the proposed population selection experiment with funded births, paid evaluation, and explicit joint copying. The record below preserves the fourth cycle's results and scope.

The [synthesis](synthesis.md) connects feasible continuation to the actual mechanisms choosing, learning, and passing on responses. A locally successful repair can eliminate all later sustainable policies, while an inherited apparatus can let the same short-horizon rule continue successfully.

## Results

- [Synthesis](synthesis.md): connected findings and the next explanatory bridge.
- [Adoption dynamics](adoption-dynamics.md): rolling planning, paid installation, exact viability, capacity loss, inherited assets, and replication.
- [Paid learning](paid-learning.md): actual finite registers, Bayesian regime updates, costs, maintenance adoption, and risk versus guaranteed repair.
- [Selection and scale](selection-and-scale.md): actual-path and probability-law transfer, transmission failures, approximation bounds, and correspondence of planning decisions.
- [Selection audit](selection-audit.md): independent checks, primary-source connections, controls, and remaining assumptions.
- [Claim register](CLAIMS.md): definitions, conditional proofs, examples, and open empirical hypotheses.

The three models share an operational framework and retain their separate duties and environmental assumptions. Periodic repair, finite Bayesian deployment, and stochastic copying are not silently combined into one solved population process.
