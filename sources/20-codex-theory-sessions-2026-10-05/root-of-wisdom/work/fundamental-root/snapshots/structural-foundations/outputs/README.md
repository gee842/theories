## Principal checked example

With an unread diagnostic archive and a cheap authorization service intact, minimum reserve is one for successful physical execution with full information, two for authorized execution with full information, and three for an authorized observation-based guarantee. A routine action destroys both cheap routes; correction through substitutes then requires five additional units, or six including the initial routine action.

The example is a supplied finite mechanism. It does not generate unconceived concepts, model contested values, or empirically establish institutional behavior. The report also preserves separate failures of aggregate resource balance, individual prerequisite feasibility, ordinary trace equivalence, and unpriced policy memory.

## Reproduce the checks

Both scripts use only the Python standard library and write their results beside the script. Run from this `outputs` directory:

```text
python3 verification/check_revision_model.py
python3 verification/check_foundation_examples.py
```

The [revision results](verification/revision-model-results.json) record 32 threshold cases, 24 second-implementation policy-tree comparisons, eight routine-first cases, and 15,624 ledger cases. The [foundation results](verification/foundation-example-results.json) record trace/control, memory, and shared-reserve countermodels. All assertions passed in the agent runs and the coordinator's rerun on 5 October 2026.

The policy computations share their transition function and recursive logic. Independent analytic lower bounds and witness policies justify the main thresholds; repeated program agreement does not validate the causal assumptions empirically. The foundation checker enumerates its finite examples and does not machine-prove the general refinement theorem.
