## Decisions retained

1. Treat the proposed `S=(X,T,R,O)` as a useful checklist, not a complete dynamics. It omits actual configuration, transition outcomes, environmental quantifiers, event timing, and whose information or authority makes a response usable.
2. Reuse established controlled transition semantics rather than invent a new all-purpose formalism. Keep resources, institutional relationships, and representations typed, even when all are stored as state.
3. Separate physical possibility, institutional permission, feasible observation-based policies, and the behavior of the implemented selector. A best possible controller is not an explanation of how the system produces that controller.
4. Preserve the parent record's distinction between identities, impossibility results, empirical hypotheses, operational definitions, and normative commitments. Existing U01, U04, U05, U16, and U22 already contain core accounting and information constraints.
5. Explicitly audit policy memory. Charging for an archive does not charge for a solver's free perfect recall. The finite example destroys an unread archive, so acquired knowledge is not falsely erased.
6. Keep substitution routes in the worked example. Loss of a cheap route may raise the reserve threshold without implying permanent impossibility.
7. Treat rule and representation changes as endogenous only when their generation, interpretation, authorization, execution, and costs are specified. A state variable named “new frame” does not explain discovery.
8. Do not equate continued operation, novelty, or more feasible actions with ethical improvement. The protected functions and legitimate revision procedures require explicit substantive commitments.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Summing money, knowledge, permission, and trust into one conserved “resource” without a justified conversion model.
- Proving each repair prerequisite separately and calling their conjunction a feasible sequence.
- Treating the analyst's knowledge of a hidden state as information available to an actor.
- Treating physical trace equivalence as preservation of control against an environment.
- Treating a fixed universal description language as evidence that unrestricted concept formation has been explained.
- Calling a simulation of a supplied access gate an empirical law of institutional closure.
- Calling established conditional constraints mathematically novel because they appear together in one vocabulary.
