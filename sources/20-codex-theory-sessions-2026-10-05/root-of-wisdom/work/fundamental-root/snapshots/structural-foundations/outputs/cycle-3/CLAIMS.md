# Claim register for constraints across scale

Date: 5 October 2026. This register distinguishes a formal definition, a proved implication under assumptions, an exact constructed result, and an empirical research hypothesis. None establishes mathematical novelty or an ethical conclusion from persistence.

## Remaining explanatory gaps

The formal architecture permits represented changes of rules, selectors, and interfaces. It does not explain unrestricted concept formation, guarantee adoption of a winning controller, or derive legitimate values. The paid handoff experiment uses external per-epoch finance; an endogenous productive process with repeated succession and costly memory remains an integration task. Positive-error finite predictions do not imply indefinite correct service.
