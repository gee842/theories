## Claims not established

The session does not establish unrestricted concept formation, mathematical novelty, a universal social law, a single ethical measure, inevitable degeneration under selection, universal benefits from inquiry, or an alignment solution. It does not establish indefinitely recurrent correction in the proposed next model. Its installed renewal loop remains conditional on an explicitly supplied external input.
