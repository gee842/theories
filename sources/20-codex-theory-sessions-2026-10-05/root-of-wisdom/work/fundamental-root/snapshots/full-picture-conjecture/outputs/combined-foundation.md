# A combined foundation for shared generation

5 October 2026. Integration of the existing programme, moral cultivation, and a newly certified joint process.

**The constructed combined process is now certified under its declared conditions, and the general characterization covers processes that change their own controllers and evaluators.** This closes the previous gap between individually demonstrated modules and one process that actually connects inquiry, judgment, execution, maintenance, and succession. The broader foundation also incorporates conscience, relationships, institutional power, and the revision of commitments, while keeping their substantive evidence distinct from the mathematical certificate.

The intended scope is a theory of how possibilities and capacities are formed, sustained, damaged, corrected, and transmitted—including when a process should end. It does not require every process to survive, every action to innovate, or every situation to need inquiry.

## The integrated root

The central proposition is:

> What happens changes the conditions of what can happen next. For agents and institutions, this includes changes to how they notice, remember, judge, choose, authorize, and teach. Shared generation asks whether those changes preserve and develop the conditions under which affected beings can live, respond, and form value.

This proposition organizes several kinds of work that can now be connected precisely:

| Part of the foundation | Its substantive question | Its current basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distinctions and dependencies | Which differences alter possible actions or consequences? What belongs inside the relevant boundary? | Existing faithful-representation and dependency-closure results; include hidden guards, shared disturbances, and permissions. |
| Material continuation | Can the actual process pay, act, maintain its capacities, and renew required support? | Resource and access theorems, actual transition models, and the new joint certificate. |
| Psychological formation | How do attention, emotion, memory, practice, and social experience shape later judgment? | The supplied traditions contribute mechanism descriptions and rival explanations; specific causal laws require evidence in the relevant setting. |
| Conscience and ethical direction | Whose harms, needs, and possibilities must count? How can judgment correct itself? | The already adopted [normative core](conscience-and-morality-core.md): fallible conscience and publicly defended commitments to shared generation. |
| Relationships and institutions | Who can be heard, refuse, authorize, act, challenge a rule, and obtain repair? | Explicit authority and intervention interfaces; consequences assessed beyond the operator's own success score. |
| Succession and self-revision | Can acquired knowledge and revised judgment survive replacement? Can the rules themselves change responsibly? | Actual archive/training/handoff in the new model; changing code, evaluators, and commitments within the general theorem. |

These roles need not appear identically in every system. A cell can instantiate resource use, feedback, and reproduction without conscience. A person's habits and a government's appeal procedure can share a functional question while having different mechanisms. A cancerous process can expand locally while destroying wider conditions of life. That is a substantive scale conflict the foundation should explain.

The structural boundary is chosen by the consequences and interventions under investigation. Existing results show how to retain the dependencies needed for an exact description. Where a finite quotient preserves observations, permissions, protected outcomes, progress, and action-dependent transitions, the prior [completeness bridge](completeness-bridge.md) transfers the relevant policy conclusions in both directions. Merely calling two situations “feedback” does not establish that correspondence.

## What the traditions add

Their contribution reaches beyond repair financing. They direct attention to the **formation of the decision maker**, including dispositions that shape which facts and people are even recognized.

Mencius describes nourishment and repeated loss of moral capacities; Xunzi emphasizes deliberate formation through learning and practice and disputes Mencius's explanation. Both belong in the framework without making their theories of human nature identical. [Mencius, Gaozi I](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%AD%9F%E5%AD%90/%E5%91%8A%E5%AD%90%E4%B8%8A); [Xunzi, Xing e](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%8D%80%E5%AD%90/%E6%80%A7%E6%83%A1%E7%AF%87).

The Gita supplies an ordered account connecting attention, attachment, anger, disturbed recollection, and judgment. AN 3.69 connects harmful dispositions with domination and refusal to acknowledge facts. These suggest distinct causal links to investigate rather than one undifferentiated variable called badness. [Gita 2.62](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/62/), [2.63](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/63/); [AN 3.69](https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/an/an03/an03.069.than.html).

The Catholic account explicitly connects repeated conduct, impaired judgment, and social structures that encourage further wrongdoing. The Qur'anic passage links moral orientation with cultivation and corruption, while supplying less of a stepwise mechanism. These remain theological accounts as well as materials for functional comparison. [Catechism 1865–1869](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_one/article_8/v_the_proliferation_of_sin.html); [Qur'an 91:7–10](https://legacy.quran.com/91/7-10).

Freida J. Jacques's Good Mind account connects attention and choice with working through hurt, including the danger of suppressing feelings. The Onondaga Nation describes governance through ordered deliberation and reconsideration with responsibility toward future generations. This brings interpersonal and institutional conditions into the account of cultivation. [Jacques's essay](https://peacecouncil.net/2025/01/15/january-2025-discipline-of-the-good-mind/); [Onondaga government](https://www.onondaganation.org/government/).

The resulting common research structure is:

**Experience and support → attention and interpretation → judgment and conduct → relationships and institutions → the conditions of subsequent experience and judgment.**

Cultivation can intervene throughout this loop. Better concentration alone might train obedience; truthful recognition might lack authority to act; anger might expose a real injury. Consequently, the relevant test is whether the practice improves warranted recognition and response while respecting affected parties. The [source-based companion](cultivation-and-shared-generation.md) preserves these countercases and ontological differences. Textual convergence supports this mechanism catalogue; it does not supply independent experimental replications or a single established psychology for everyone.

## The combined-process theorem

Let a configuration record the actual environment, installed programs, memories, available evidence, evaluators, commitments, permissions, resources, and succession state. Every transition includes the installed process's actual behavior and all admitted environmental outcomes. No hypothetical better controller is substituted when the installed controller fails.

Let **K** be the stated protection requirements and **E₁,…,E_q** the actual outcomes required to recur. Then, for an arbitrary set of configurations:

> Every admitted execution remains in K and delivers every required E infinitely often **if and only if** the reachable process lies in a safe closed region and, for each E, admits a well-founded rank that strictly decreases on every transition that does not deliver E.

A well-founded rank cannot decrease forever. An indefinitely deferred outcome would require precisely such an impossible descent. Closure supplies protection throughout the intervening steps. Conversely, if every actual execution is protected and each outcome recurs, the reachable region and ordinal ranks exist. This is an exact semantic characterization, not a promise of a terminating algorithm for arbitrary programs.

The usable certificate proves the conditions on actual component transitions: initial resources and competence, dispatch, evidence acquisition, formation, checking, admission, execution, replenishment, maintenance, training, handoff, and permitted revision. They share one resource account and one treatment of interference. Mutual support is legitimate when an initial base and every succeeding transition are verified; merely assuming that each component will rescue the others is insufficient.

The state set may be infinite. Code, representations, evaluators, and even the procedure for revising commitments may change. Those changes must satisfy the same joint contract. The theorem needs no external actor continually choosing an ideal policy. Its interpretation and admitted mechanisms still have to be specified correctly.

For continuing physical activity, elapsed time must diverge. For finite duties, rest, refusal, or exit, use the appropriate completion or request-resolution condition. Infinite recurring service is one specification, not the definition of every good life. The [full proof](combined-process-proof.md) treats these cases and separates all-path guarantees from probability-one progress.

## One actual process now satisfies the certificate

The new executable witness has a hidden response law over two contexts. It acquires paid observations, constructs a candidate from the linked records, buys fresh checks, revises a failing simplicity-first evaluator to prioritize fit, installs a validated program, performs ordinary service, and pays to archive and teach its program and evaluator to a successor.

Each of 23 microsteps costs one token before its effects occur. Productive auxiliary work supplies later receipts to the same capped account. The environment can switch to any different response law between periods. The controller cannot read that hidden law; calibration supplies its observations. Harm is assessed separately and persists despite later changes to the evaluator.

Exact enumeration finds **486 reachable states and 506 transitions**. Every baseline prefix is protected. The minimum account balance is two tokens at state boundaries and one immediately after payment. All six required outcomes—corrective installation, correct service in each context, archive maintenance, training, and competent handoff—occur at least once in every 23 transitions on every admitted infinite execution.

The initial evaluator actually changes, and the successor receives its archived replacement. Continual rewriting is unnecessary once the evaluator works. Knowledge, acceptance, installation, delivered response, and competent succession are separately checked events.

| Intervention in the model | Protection preserved? | Correct service in both contexts recurs on every run? | Competent succession recurs? |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Baseline | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Remove linked discovery records | Yes | No | Yes |
| Block ordinary execution | Yes | No | Yes |
| Remove renewal receipts | Yes | No | No |
| Remove archive maintenance | Yes | No | No |
| Remove successor training | Yes | No | No |
| Capture evaluator and admission standard | No | Yes | No |
| Corrupt both discovery and checking observations | No | No | Yes |

Here competent succession means actual transfer of the retained strict-checker program and operating skill; it does not certify the sensor in the corrupted-calibration condition. Carrier labels alternate in a finite turnover abstraction.

The capture result is especially informative: some correct service keeps recurring while harmful responses also occur. Recurrence alone would misclassify it. Correlated corruption produces 122 reachable states with an internally strict but physically false certificate. More checking through the same compromised instrument cannot establish truth.

Across all eight conditions, an independently written graph reader checked **2,455 states and 2,587 transitions**, recomputed physical event meanings and payment identities, and used depth-first cycle detection and computed ranks to verify recurrence. It agrees with the producer. Separate reviews checked the mathematics, observation restrictions, enacted evaluator change, and actual archive contents. These are AI-assisted mathematical and software checks of a synthetic process, not fresh human experiments or proof-assistant certification.

The witness supplies a finite grammar, reliable baseline calibration, within-period stability, continuing productive inputs, and a persistent paid scheduler and auxiliary apparatus. Its training is executable payload transmission. Its evaluator revision concerns simplicity versus evidence fit. Human moral development, the invention of unlimited concepts, and the origin of the apparatus are not demonstrated by those operations. The general framework can represent these questions; each needs its own adequate mechanism and evidence. The [certification report](combined-process-certification.md) records the detailed contract and reproduction instructions.

## What “complete” now means

Three achievements can be asserted together:

1. **An integrated scope:** material support, psychological formation, ethical direction, institutional action, succession, and self-revision fit within one account of changes to future possibilities.
2. **An exact general process characterization:** for a declared actual transition system, the protection-and-recurrence condition is necessary and sufficient; finiteness of the state space is unnecessary.
3. **A checked joint realization:** the bounded executable process satisfies those conditions, with explicit controls showing which mechanisms its success depends on.

The normative direction is already part of this theory: affected parties cannot be excluded merely because the operator benefits from excluding them. Norm revision must address facts, standing, reasons, and existing obligations; changing the success label cannot erase a harm. That direction is openly defended rather than inferred from the fact that some process persists.

This yields concrete grounds for rejecting unreasonable claims: spending the same reserve twice; presenting inaccessible information as usable knowledge; calling unexecuted repair successful; treating a score change as a repaired injury; counting expansion while excluding those whose conditions it destroys; or assuming a successor inherits capacities that were never maintained and transmitted. Which option is best in a difficult conflict can still require substantive judgment.

**The combined operational foundation is established at the stated level.** Calling it the complete fundamental theory of reality would add a further claim: that its substantive mechanisms and interpretation cover every intended domain. Neither the general transition notation nor the textual convergence proves that claim. A Maxwell-like comparison would require comparably specific substantive laws and evidence in a declared domain, alongside this structural foundation.

The foundation is therefore broader and more complete than a theory of financing repair. Its completed contribution is the connection between formation, action, consequences, and the renewal or corruption of the conditions of future action—including the conditions under which that connection succeeds or fails.
