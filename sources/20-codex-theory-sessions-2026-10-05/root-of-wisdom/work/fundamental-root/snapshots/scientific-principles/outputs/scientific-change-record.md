# Decisions and verification for the scientific change continuation

5 October 2026. Ren asked to pursue the possibility of a theory explaining the formation and change of scientific questions, concepts, methods, and standards. The preceding research's protocol-reliability and instrument-calibration projects remain distinct. This continuation does not label their unrun proposals completed.

## Main decisions

- Broaden the candidate theory from error correction to the production, appraisal, and revision of empirical knowledge and its enabling conditions.
- Treat discovery of a finer conditional relation as potentially compatible with a correct earlier aggregate account.
- Require explicit transition mechanisms and counterfactual predictions for a theory of scientific change. A state vocabulary or causal loop diagram alone is insufficient.
- Distinguish candidate generation, question admission, validation, and propagation.
- Keep concept construction within a grammar separate from creation of new primitives, goals, instruments, or semantics beyond that grammar.
- Keep actual institutional reward, epistemic performance, available inquiry capacity, and ethical legitimacy separate.
- Preserve close prior art rather than claim that multiple search spaces, resource costs, or revisable standards are new.

It is a calculation of selection pressure under an assumed propagation law, not a simulation of autonomous standards evolution.

## Interpretation corrections retained

The full balanced training grid is informative about the joint rule. The greedy procedure's failure is a search restriction, not an information impossibility. An unlinked IID marginal observation interface supplies a separate indistinguishability construction; these settings are not interchangeable.

The code learns a predictive table. The analyst's description as mismatch does not mean the learner invents an XOR primitive or its semantic interpretation. The fixed prediction goal and generic operators remain inputs.

Fresh validation gives the stated 9/256 conditional pass probability only for independent fair labels and one selected candidate in one episode. It does not certify every nonbeneficial alternative, eliminate cost, or control lifetime error.

The signed institutional scores do not demonstrate financial survival. Detection, enforcement, cost, equal opportunities, and a monotone propagation rule are explicit assumptions. The final heading says method spread under fixed standards; it does not imply the model already explains standard formation.
