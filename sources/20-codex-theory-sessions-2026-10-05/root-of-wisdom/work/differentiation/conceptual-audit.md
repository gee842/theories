# Differentiation within unity: conceptual and source audit

Prepared 5 October 2026. Read-only audit of the retained nonseparation and joint-organization reports, selected other-session material, and five primary research sources. This note supplies explanatory criteria and limits; it does not claim a completed theory of consciousness or new physical experiments.

## Strongest positive construction

One common process can generate several relatively persistent organizations, each coupling to a different part of its surroundings, retaining a different history, and regulating different consequences. Their common origin and law do not require identical states, identical access, or interchangeable interests. The explanation becomes substantive when the process accounts for the formation, maintenance, and eventual failure of those organizations and their interfaces.

The useful progression is a set of explanatory burdens, not a claim that all actual evolution followed one simple linear sequence:

| Target | What would explain it | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| A physical distinction | A mechanism generates or preserves a difference under specified conditions. | Giving two names to an undifferentiated state. |
| A relatively persistent unit | A localized organization or robust collective variable survives relevant disturbances for a stated time scale. | Several alternative attractors of one scalar variable. |
| A maintained boundary | The organization contributes causally to producing or repairing the interface, and that interface preserves enabling conditions. | Choosing a partition or finding conditional independence in one probability model. |
| A functional standpoint | A locally implemented observation, memory, and action organization has particular accessible distinctions and response possibilities. | Assuming a subsystem has the entire analyst's state description. |
| An agent in the stronger biological sense | The system regulates its coupling in ways tied to its own organization and continuing functioning. | Mere persistence, passive correlation, or the analyst's assigned reward. |
| Experienced subjectivity | An independently justified account connects the organization to experience and explains the relevant evidence. | Renaming a sensor or attractor a subject. |

An object need not satisfy all rows. A crystal can be a differentiated physical unit without being an organism. The phrase “differentiated beings” should retain this range. The functional standpoint can be studied precisely without asserting or denying that every instance has experience.

## A necessary symmetry qualification

Let a deterministic flow satisfy uniqueness and be equivariant under a symmetry group: \(\phi_t(gz)=g\phi_t(z)\). If \(gz_0=z_0\), then \(g\phi_t(z_0)=\phi_t(z_0)\). Thus the trajectory preserves every exact initial symmetry shared by the dynamics. An unstable homogeneous state does not leave itself merely because it is unstable.

Small initial differences, fluctuations, heterogeneous conditions, or symmetry-breaking inputs can be amplified by an instability. A stochastic law may preserve a symmetry in the distribution while individual realized trajectories differ. This makes differentiated outcomes compatible with one symmetric law, but the fluctuation law or perturbation is still part of the explanation. Numerical roundoff must not be presented as a derivation of physical asymmetry.

Turing's original discussion explicitly makes this point: deviations matter even when their exact forms do not determine the same macroscopic details. The paper supplies a pattern-generating mechanism with assumed reaction and diffusion rates, rather than a deduction of those rates from unity.

## Endogenous boundaries require more than clustering

A useful experimental criterion is a reciprocal causal dependence between boundary maintenance and the activity it supports:

1. Changing boundary integrity changes retention, exchange, and the persistence of the internal organization.
2. Interrupting the relevant productive organization changes boundary replenishment or repair.
3. Restoring that production or supplying the missing support rescues the boundary under specified conditions.
4. Changes in external feed, load, temperature, or disturbance have measurable effects on whether the combined regime continues.

This is compatible with extensive dependence on the environment. “Endogenous” here means the organization is an active causal contributor to the interface's persistence; it does not mean that the system supplies all its own matter, energy, history, or enabling conditions.

Several simultaneous units must be shown if the target is plurality. Two basins of attraction can merely describe two possible states of the same whole. A field with several localized patterns, or a common medium supporting several compartments, addresses coexistence directly. Distinct units may overlap functionally or be nested, so an adequate account need not produce one uniquely correct partition at every scale.

The previous four-site slow-mode result explains useful units for a given coupling structure. The present burden is stronger: show what produces or maintains that coupling structure. The previous two-component replacement model explains maintained abundances; a boundary variable or spatial formation process must be added if the new claim concerns boundary production.

## Functional viewpoints are differences in access and capability

For a declared global causal model, let \(H_i\) be the locally available observation-action history of organization \(i\), and let \(\Pi_i(H_i)\) be its implementable policies. The relevant viewpoint is specified by the actual observation channels, retained memory, available interventions, and consequences it can distinguish and influence. The global process contains the machinery implementing all these restrictions.

Two organizations can inhabit the same world while receiving different signals, reaching different states, and facing different continuation conditions. Even identical sensors can acquire different histories. A signal can bear different functional significance when it has different effects on two organizations' maintenance or actions. These differences require no additional substance outside their common dynamics.

Three tests prevent this account from becoming a relabeling:

- **Access test:** exhibit two possible global states that the local history cannot distinguish, while a larger observation channel can. The local agent must use one policy across the unresolved alternatives.
- **Use test:** remove or scramble a particular signal, while controlling other modeled changes, and determine which feasible responses or maintained functions are lost. Correlation alone does not establish functional significance.
- **Maintenance test:** interrupt the channel's physical or organizational support and measure the loss of discrimination or executable response. Repair should restore the corresponding capacity when the model predicts that it can.

The continuing-realizability work from the other session gives an especially useful extension: channels and execution reserves consume maintained support. A viewpoint is not merely a projection written by an analyst; its projection and its actions are themselves physically or institutionally implemented processes.

This explains functional situatedness. It does not by itself explain why experience feels like anything, why a particular organization has experience, or how experiences individuate. Those require an explicit bridge theory and evidence beyond the present mathematical definitions. Likewise, imperfect local information explains partiality but does not make every local belief true.

## Counterexamples that a strong root must survive

1. **One stable homogeneous medium:** common reality and uniform dynamics may erase differences. Unity alone does not entail differentiation.
2. **Several stable whole-system states:** multistability can explain history-dependent alternatives without creating coexisting individuals.
3. **A gas in an externally maintained container:** physical compartmentalization can exist without the contents producing their own boundary or acting as an agent.
4. **A perfect passive recorder:** high mutual information may have no role in action or maintenance. Information quantity alone does not establish a functional viewpoint of the stronger kind.
5. **A thermostat or simple controller:** a sensor-action loop can implement limited regulation. Such a demonstration establishes its stipulated functional capacities, not consciousness or biological autonomy.
6. **A durable catalyst:** persistent functioning can occur without renewing that particular support. Renewal theorems need their finite-lifetime or productive-vulnerability premises.
7. **Two exactly coupled copies:** two physical locations or records need not produce different accessible consequences. Distinct viewpoint claims require a relevant difference in coupling, history, capacity, or consequences.

The constructive root should therefore identify conditions under which differentiation is generated and maintained, rather than assume that every difference, persistence, and action is the same phenomenon.

## Five primary research sources and read scopes

Each source summary below is deliberately short. The first four concern models, experiments, or a proposed research definition; the fifth is a primary conceptual critique. None alone establishes metaphysical monism.

1. **A. M. Turing, “The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis” (1952).** [Primary paper](https://www.dna.caltech.edu/courses/cs191/paperscs191/turing.pdf). Read the symmetry/instability discussion on journal pages 41–43 and related passages on perturbations. It explains amplification of small differences under reaction–diffusion dynamics. Scope: a mechanism and illustrative models, with explicit rates and perturbations. Its symmetry argument supports the qualification above. The claim that actual cosmic differentiation arose by this particular mechanism would exceed the paper.

2. **M. M. Hanczyc, S. M. Fujikawa, J. W. Szostak, “Experimental Models of Primitive Cellular Compartments: Encapsulation, Growth, and Division” (2003).** [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4484575/); [journal PDF mirror](https://www.microeco.ethz.ch/uni/kurs/mikevol03/scinews/Science_302_2003_618.pdf). Read indexed primary full-text passages on mineral-assisted assembly, RNA retention, growth, and division. Clay accelerated vesicle formation; encapsulated free RNA showed no observed leakage over 24 hours; supplied fatty acids supported membrane growth. Division was driven by pressure through filters, which the authors explicitly call artificial. Evidence supports physically generated compartments, not complete autonomous living cells or a demonstrated history of life's origin. Direct PMC opening encountered a browser challenge; the research tool's indexed text supplied the cited sections.

3. **X. E. Barandiaran, E. Di Paolo, M. Rohde, “Defining Agency” (2009).** [Author manuscript](https://xabier.barandiaran.net/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/barandiaran_dipaolo_rohde_-_defining_agency_v_1_0_-_jab_20091.pdf). Read sections 2–3, especially the three proposed conditions and modulation of coupling. Their proposal joins individuality, interactional asymmetry, and norms related to organization. It supplies criteria for distinguishing agency from passive dynamics; it is a theoretical definition, not a universally established empirical test or a theory of phenomenal consciousness.

4. **A. Kolchinsky, D. H. Wolpert, “Semantic information, autonomous agency, and nonequilibrium statistical physics” (2018).** [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.08053). Read sections IV–V, including limitations of entropy-based viability and intervention-based information measures. They test information's contribution by scrambling correlations and measuring effects on a specified viability function. The authors explicitly say that function is selected by the scientist in their framework. This supports intervention-based relevance tests but does not automatically derive an organism's interests, identity, or experience.

5. **J. Bruineberg, K. Dołęga, J. Dewhurst, M. Baltieri, “The Emperor's New Markov Blankets” (2022; accepted manuscript 2021).** [Author manuscript](https://manuelbaltieri.com/assets/pdf/EmperorMarkovBlanketsAccepted.pdf). Read abstract, introduction, and sections 5.1–5.2. They distinguish statistical inference boundaries from claims about agent boundaries and give counterexamples involving alternative causal sources of a movement. This is an argued methodological critique, not a proof that every use of blankets fails. It supports requiring independently warranted mechanisms and interventions when making ontological claims.

## Audit verdict

The proposed unity becomes explanatory when it includes mechanisms that produce different persistent organizations and maintain different interfaces to their surroundings. Common laws then explain why different histories and couplings yield different capacities and concerns. A strong result would exhibit coexisting units, perturbable boundary maintenance, and a local observation–action restriction with a provable consequence. That is a substantive explanation of functional differentiation within one process; a metaphysical grounding claim and phenomenal experience remain distinct explanatory targets.
