# Focused primary-source check: consequences become conditions

Prepared 5 October 2026. This is a source note for the current generativity discussion, not a historical novelty claim or empirical test of a universal theory. The existing `outputs/generative-becoming.md` was read before this search; it already explicitly states that products can become conditions of further change. The current user's emphasis adds a useful focus on changed surroundings and on generation retaining or producing the capacity for subsequent generation.

## 1. Produced structure changes the effect of later inputs

**Primary study:** Alan Puttock, Hugh A. Graham, Andrew M. Cunliffe, Mark Elliott, Richard E. Brazier (2017), “Eurasian beaver activity increases water storage, attenuates flow and mitigates diffuse pollution from intensively-managed grasslands,” *Science of the Total Environment* 576:430–443. DOI: 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2016.10.122.

- Stable citation: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27792958/
- Full primary paper inspected: https://www.ecotoneinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/eurasain-beaver-attenuate-flow-2017.pdf
- Read scope: abstract, site/design sections, and results 3.1–3.2, PDF pages 1–6. Search result supplied PubMed abstract; direct PubMed open returned a minimal page. Full PDF text successfully opened. This is one controlled reintroduction site with temporal surveys and upstream/downstream hydrological comparisons, not a randomized cross-site causal experiment.

Reusable paraphrase (under 90 words):

> In one monitored English site, beavers constructed 13 dams and created ponds storing about 1,000 cubic metres of water. The resulting landscape changed how subsequent rainfall became downstream flow: peaks were smaller and delayed. Here an activity's product becomes a lasting condition governing later interactions. This is evidence for that particular mechanism; it does not imply that every environmental alteration increases diversity, persistence, or the number of accessible futures.

Avoid adding unmeasured species diversification or improved beaver reproductive fitness as a demonstrated outcome of this study. It establishes a concrete change in subsequent hydrological response. A greater future repertoire is our conceptual inference only if a repertoire and comparison are explicitly defined.

## 2. Nonliving product becomes part of production

**Primary experiment:** Kenso Soai, Takanori Shibata, Hiroshi Morioka, Kaori Choji (1995), “Asymmetric autocatalysis and amplification of enantiomeric excess of a chiral molecule,” *Nature* 378:767–768.

- Primary publisher abstract successfully inspected: https://www.nature.com/articles/378767a0
- DOI: 10.1038/378767a0.
- Read scope: complete abstract only; full article is subscription content and was not read. No claim to have checked the detailed experimental protocol or yields.

Reusable paraphrase (under 90 words):

> Soai and colleagues experimentally showed that an autocatalytic reaction could amplify a small initial imbalance between mirror-image molecules. Material produced by the reaction contributes to the catalytic process that makes further product. This supplies a nonliving example of generation feeding subsequent generation, under specified reagents and conditions. It establishes neither indefinite production without replenishment nor the invention of indefinitely many new chemical capabilities.

## 3. Classical resonance: 生生

**Primary passage:** *Zhouyi*, *Xici* I, section 5: “生生之謂易”.

- Primary-text locator: https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=46918&show=parallel
- General section: https://ctext.org/book-of-changes/xi-ci-shang
- Read scope: the indexed primary Chinese text was returned in full for the relevant passage. Direct page open returned 403. The wording includes “日新之謂盛德。生生之謂易”; the user's exact phrase “生生不息” should not be presented as this passage's verbatim wording.

Reusable interpretive note (under 90 words):

> The *Xici* says “生生之謂易”: a possible working rendering is “generation continuing into generation is called change.” This makes the user's 生生不息 a fitting philosophical resonance for production that carries forward further production. The rendering is interpretive; the passage is not a mathematical statement about growing option counts, and its context includes a larger account of the Changes. It should not be collapsed into a single doctrine identical to the *Daodejing*.

## 4. Classical resonance: 道法自然

**Primary passage:** *Daodejing*, chapter 25, ends “人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然”.

- Primary text: https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=11616&show=parallel (indexed passage read; direct open 403).
- Independent primary commentary opened successfully: Wang Bi's commentary, https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/SK2153/chapter/1l9o8e03atwl2 . Relevant lines 13–15, with 27th chapter commentary also returned at lines 35–54.
- Wang Bi treats following 自然 through noncontravention and attending to a thing's own way, rather than positing an additional named object that legislates above the Dao. This is one historically situated interpretation, not an uncontested reading shared by all traditions.

Reusable interpretive note (under 100 words):

> 道法自然 can resonate with processes unfolding through their own conditions without a separate external director. Here 自然 is usefully glossed as “self-so” or “of itself,” rather than simply the modern collective noun “Nature.” Wang Bi's commentary supports attending to how things are instead of imposing a fixed form upon them. Applying this to a dynamical model is our philosophical comparison; it is not proof that the classical text asserted that model or that the model establishes Daoist metaphysics.

## Synthesis boundary

Evidence supports: a produced structure or material can alter the mechanism and conditions of later transformation; some such loops renew or amplify productive activity. The wider claim that reality is intrinsically and endlessly generative remains a proposed philosophical generalization. Sustained generativity, increasing accessible possibilities, and increasing occurrence of a generative organization are distinct claims with separate resource, dynamical, and selection requirements. Use the classical terms to sharpen the question, not to bypass those requirements.
