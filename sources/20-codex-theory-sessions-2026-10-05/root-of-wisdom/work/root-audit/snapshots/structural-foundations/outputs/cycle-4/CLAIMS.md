## Remaining bridge

Selection among selectors requires explicit contribution, appointment, imitation, financing, or reproduction mechanisms and a population update. The next proposal is to let early versus delayed measured outcomes affect successor composition while accounting for absolute survival and paid observation/transmission. This population experiment has not been run in the current cycle.
