## Decisions made in this pass

Use scientific principles as conditional requirements and mechanisms rather than treat a single maxim as the essence of science. Keep discrimination, explanation, exploration, validation, and implementation distinct. Use simple examples for proofs and a broader next experiment to test their conjunction. Prefer exact arithmetic for the finite examples so simulation variation cannot obscure the logic.

Keep the supplied framework workspace read only. Work within this chat's directory; do not coordinate with its other sessions. Use three research agents for formal, philosophical, and replication/resource contributions. Their review can expose mistakes but is not independent empirical evidence or human peer review.

Use the NASEM reproducibility/replicability terminology and say that conventions vary. Treat philosophical texts as distinct positions. Mark incomplete readings rather than filling gaps from reputation or secondary summaries. The full Laudan chapter, Neyman–Pearson paper, and Blackwell paper remain incomplete primary readings.

The exact examples are illustrations of proved conditional claims. The proposed instrument-laboratory comparison is not reported as run. No external publication, messaging, or automation was created.
