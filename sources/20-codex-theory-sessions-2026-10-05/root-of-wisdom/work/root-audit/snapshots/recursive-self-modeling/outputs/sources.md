# Primary sources for recursive self modeling

Focused source map, checked 5 October 2026. This is a targeted investigation rather than a systematic review or an assertion of current consensus. Entries distinguish original experiments, formal work, original theoretical positions, and reviews. Papers support the claims stated beside them, not an overall theory of consciousness. Source copies and detailed working notes remain in this chat's work directory.

## Metacognition and introspective access

**Nelson and Narens 1990. Metamemory A theoretical framework and new findings.** Original framework with empirical material. Checked the chapter's monitoring/control definitions and discussion of levels, reports, and study allocation, especially pages 125–137. It supports separate measurement of monitoring and regulation. It does not establish anatomical tiers or accuracy at every level. [Author chapter](https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~lnarens/1990/Nelson%26Narens_Book_Chapter_1990.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0079-7421(08)60053-5).

**Fleming and Daw 2017. Self Evaluation of Decision Making A General Bayesian Framework for Metacognitive Computation.** Original computational theory with simulations. Confidence is modeled as inference about a decision, potentially using different evidence across time or space; the actor's choice can itself be evidence. Checked the introductory model and model comparisons. This supports functional separation without proving separate brain structures for successive semantic levels. It is not a new experiment establishing the proposed architecture. [Full paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5178868/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/rev0000045).

**Nisbett and Wilson 1977. Telling more than we can know Verbal reports on mental processes.** Critical review and theoretical argument. Checked the distinction between access to contents and explanations of the processes producing them, including pages 255–256. The paper supports caution about causal introspective explanations while acknowledging informational advantages from private contents and history. It does not establish that all introspection is false. [Original paper mirror](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/cognitive-bias/illusion-of-depth/1977-nisbett.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.84.3.231).

**Ericsson and Simon 1980. Verbal reports as data.** Methodological theory and evidence review. It distinguishes reporting attended information from generating explanations and treats report validity as dependent on instructions and mechanisms. Access limitation: the original PDF was available but image-only extraction did not support a full-text audit; the authors' abstract and indexed excerpts were checked. This is a qualification of blanket skepticism, not a guarantee of transparent reporting. [Original PDF](https://acs.ist.psu.edu/ist597/papers/ericssonS80.pdf), [Abstract record](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ231273), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.87.3.215).

**Jameson, Narens, Goldfarb and Nelson 1990. The influence of near threshold priming on metamemory and recall.** Original experiment; abstract checked. Near-threshold answer primes improved recall without corresponding sensitivity in feeling-of-knowing judgments. This is a specific dissociation between a performance change and its monitor, not evidence that monitoring never helps. [Author paper](https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~lnarens/1990/JamesonNarensGoldfarbNelson_Acta_Psychologica_1990.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-6918(90)90058-N).

**Maniscalco and Lau 2012. A signal detection theoretic approach for estimating metacognitive sensitivity from confidence ratings.** Original measurement method and application; abstract and introductory material checked. Meta-d′ estimates how confidence distinguishes correct from incorrect responses in task-comparable signal-detection units. It helps separate sensitivity from response bias. It is not a measure of wisdom or a test of whether a system is conscious; its modeling assumptions matter. [Corrected author PDF](https://brianmaniscalco.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Maniscalco-Lau-2012-Consc-Cog-corrected.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2011.09.021).

## Competing consciousness accounts

**Lau and Rosenthal 2011. Empirical support for higher order theories of conscious awareness.** Theory-led review advocating a higher-order account. Checked its introduction, theory comparison, and diagram discussion. Performance/report dissociations and prefrontal findings are interpreted as support, but do not uniquely identify the account. This is a theoretical position supported by selected evidence, not a decisive original experiment. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2011.05.009).

**Rosenthal 2012. Higher order awareness misrepresentation and function.** Original philosophical argument; abstract and central sections checked. Explicitly distinguishes higher-order awareness proposed to explain consciousness from regulatory metacognition. Both may involve higher-order content and error, yet need not have the same function. The proposed limited utility of consciousness is the author's argument, not an established conclusion adopted here. [Full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3318760/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2011.0353).

**Lamme 2010. How neuroscience will change our view on consciousness.** Original theoretical target article drawing on earlier experiments, with published commentaries. Checked its distinctions among feedforward, local recurrent, and widespread recurrent processing and its concluding argument. It allows phenomenality without the higher-order access emphasized by rival accounts. Neural recurrence is not automatically a representation of a representation. The disagreement remains unresolved in this report. [Paper and commentaries](https://people.uncw.edu/tothj/PSY595/Lamme-How%20Neurosci%20Will%20Change%20Our%20View%20of%20Cs-CN-2010.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/17588921003731586).

The central disagreement concerns what explains experience, as distinct from reportability, confidence, or behavioral control. A task that measures one of the latter cannot be assumed to settle the former. This methodological conclusion is our synthesis of the competing positions.

## Observation can alter emotional processing

**Lieberman and colleagues 2007. Putting Feelings Into Words.** Original fMRI experiment with 30 participants. Full author-hosted paper checked, including methods and comparison conditions. Participants labeled expressions on faces; the study reports differences in amygdala activity across tasks. This is relevant to feedback from representational activity but does not test unbounded recursive self-modeling or provide a direct measure of subjective feeling. The proposed regional pathway draws partly on correlation and mediation. [Author paper](https://teams.semel.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/publications/May%202007%20-%20Putting%20Feelings%20Into%20Words.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.01916.x).

**Levy-Gigi and Shamay-Tsoory 2022. Affect labeling The role of timing and intensity.** Original experiments; full paper checked. Experiment 2 compares labeling and control under two image-intensity conditions. Its outcome is reported distress, and generalization beyond the laboratory remains open. It provides a counterweight to universal downregulation claims. Labeling one's feelings here differs from labeling expressions on other faces in the preceding study. [Paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0279303).

## Computation self reference and deliberation

**Turing 1936–37. On Computable Numbers with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem.** Original formal work, with correction in the inspected copy. Universal simulation and diagonal arguments delimit effective procedures over a specified machine class. The original formulation concerns computable sequences and circle-free machines. It does not show that particular introspections are necessarily wrong or that finite bounded control problems are undecidable. [Stanford copy](https://theory.stanford.edu/~trevisan/cs172-07/turing36.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-42.1.230).

**Jones 2013. A Swiss Pocket Knife for Computability.** Primary research on implementations, with a constructive exposition of Kleene's older self-reference theorem. Checked acceptable-language assumptions, the construction, and overhead discussion. The fixed point equates computed partial functions, not necessarily source text or terminating executions. Original Kleene scans were not successfully inspected, so Jones is identified as the checked exposition rather than the origin of the theorem. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.5128), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4204/EPTCS.129.1).

**Russell and Wefald 1991. Principles of metareasoning.** Original normative computational framework. Publisher abstract checked; full article not inspected. Supports the historical attribution of evaluating computational actions through their effects on external decisions. The framework is not an empirical claim that people optimize expected computation value. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C).

**Hay, Russell, Tolpin and Shimony 2012. Selecting Computations Theory and Applications.** Original formal research; definitions, Theorems 4–7, and Example 3 checked. In its static selection model, bounded terminal utilities and constant positive computation cost yield a bound on optimal expected computation count and almost-sure stopping. There need be no uniform finite worst-case deadline. A positive myopic value supports continuing; the converse does not hold. The model does not directly cover reflection changing the target itself. [Full paper](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/papers/uai12-meta.pdf), [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5879).

**Wolpert 2008. Physical limits of inference.** Original formal research on inference devices defined over worldlines. Definitions and selected diagonal claims were checked, not every theorem. Universal success is obstructed under specified question, setup, and correctness conditions. Accurate restricted inference remains possible. Applying it to cognition requires defending those conditions; “embedded” alone supplies no universal failure theorem. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.1362), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physd.2008.03.040).

**Lawvere 1969. Diagonal Arguments and Cartesian Closed Categories.** Original formal work; fixed-point argument checked in the scan. Under a strong representability premise, diagonalization gives a fixed point for each suitable endomap. Boolean negation then exposes a limit to that representability. This is not a convergence theorem for reflective psychology. [Original scan](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~matilde/LawvereDiagonalArgCartesianClosedCats.pdf), [Official reprint](https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/15/tr15abs.html).

## Information and abstraction

**Polyanskiy and Wu 2017. Strong data processing inequalities for channels and Bayesian networks.** Primary research developing stronger channel-dependent bounds. The checked abstract states the ordinary Markov-chain inequality used here; the synthesis supplies its elementary finite-variable proof directly. We do not apply the paper's stronger results to cognition. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06025).

**Abel, Hershkowitz and Littman 2016. Near Optimal Behavior via Approximate State Abstraction.** Primary formal research on four abstraction families in Markov decision processes and corresponding performance bounds. This is relevant prior art for requiring assumptions before transferring performance across a representation. Our elementary deterministic closure proof is self-contained; their quantitative guarantees have not been imported into the feeling example. [Proceedings paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/abel16.html).

## Observation access and model uncertainty: second continuation

**Banerjee and Veeravalli 2012. Data-Efficient Quickest Change Detection with On–Off Observation Control.** Author-hosted full paper checked, especially the model, skipped-observation update (2.3), and limits of exact two-threshold optimality. It treats a geometric change time, specified pre/post-change densities, false alarms, delay and observation cost. Its guarantees are not transferred to our controlled finite-budget experiment. [Author paper](https://vvv.ece.illinois.edu/papers/journal/bane-veer-sqa-2012.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/07474946.2012.651981).

**Klenske and Hennig 2016. Dual Control for Approximate Bayesian Reinforcement Learning.** Original computational research; publisher abstract inspected only. It situates action and future information in finite-horizon control with uncertain dynamics. No specific algorithm or theorem is used in this experiment. [Publisher record](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v17/15-162.html).

The new scheduling, estimation-error and displaced-coordinate proofs are self-contained arguments, not claims of new theorems in the literature. Their explicit assumptions and numerical checks are in the [formal note](correction-access/formal-note.md) and [anchor explanation](correction-access/anchor-mechanism.md).
