# Audit: structural foundations and scientific principles

Read-only audit of the copied session outputs, 5 October 2026. No source files or other chats were modified. This memo checks what the other sessions actually achieved and what that supports about reaching a “root of wisdom.” Source locators refer to the local snapshots under `work/root-audit/snapshots/`.

## Verdict

Neither session reports having established a necessary-and-sufficient root of wisdom. They do, however, supply a considerably stronger foundation than the earlier description “we know how to correct supplied beliefs.” The scientific-principles session implements experience-driven revision of a rule governing which inquiries may proceed. Structural cycle 6 implements a physically paid revision of the evaluator itself and checks whether its corrected decision can actually reach a funded successor. Together these establish local mechanisms for **changing what one can investigate and assess, while preserving the ability to act on the assessment**.

This is a substantial completed advance. It is not a derivation of what deserves care, unrestricted conceptual invention, or reliable wise conduct. The latest records explicitly preserve those boundaries.

## What is completed

**1. An investigator can revise a rule that blocks its own discovery.** The scientific learner initially examines feature pairs only after a singleton improves prediction. It generates a syntactic edit removing that prerequisite, assesses the edited procedure using observed errors and paid expenses, validates on fresh tasks, installs the selected rule, and executes it on later tasks. For the noiseless interaction task the incumbent loses 40.75 units per episode and the edited procedure 20.06; the 20.69 gain is exactly checked. A later three-phase implementation removes the prerequisite, restores it in an independent-outcome environment, and removes it again when the interaction environment returns. Thus the claim is actual rule revision, not just a variable named “standards” or an adoption flag without changed behavior.

Status: **completed implementation and independent audit; conditional evidence-assessment guarantee**. Scope: one editable admission/search condition, supplied feature semantics, fixed prediction objective, supplied fresh-check rule, external study schedule and funding. The learner does not invent its ultimate purpose or autonomously detect when the next review is due. The three-check error allowance is finite, not a lifetime guarantee.

Sources: `scientific-principles/outputs/mechanisms-of-scientific-change.md:71–118`; `scientific-change-formal-results.md:106–139`; `scientific-change-deepening-record.md:9–36`. The last record at lines 55–61 explicitly supersedes the older statement that standards revision is wholly unimplemented, within this narrow scope. Do not treat `theory-of-scientific-change.md`'s earlier “proposed, not implemented” paragraph as the latest status.

**2. Correcting an evaluator and benefiting from its correction are different achievements.** Structural cycle 6 makes query acquisition, retention, evaluator rewrite, stored alternative programs, installation, and successor finance explicit. In its constructed population the paid route still succeeds at epoch 27. At epoch 28 the evaluator is correct but the population cannot fund its next carrier. The unchanged population remains visibly alive until epoch 32. In a separate noisy handoff example, three probes improve regime classification to 27/32 but, under insufficient execution reserve, reduce delivered success to 27/64. A cost-aware one-probe policy achieves 3/4 in that state.

Status: **completed exact constructed mechanisms and conditional proofs**. These are separate models with declared interfaces, not one solved adaptive community. Their dates and numerical thresholds are protocol-relative; alternative signals, credit, blind revision, or different resource access can alter them.

Sources: `structural-foundations/outputs/cycle-6/synthesis.md:13–26,49–87`; `cycle-6/CLAIMS.md:7–23`.

**3. Inquiry is sometimes unnecessary.** A separately endowed mixed population can persist through either of two regimes and one unknown change using an explicit funded quota allocation rule, without identifying the regime. At a final decision with no further information, a sure acceptable action exists exactly when the acceptable action sets across all indistinguishable states have nonempty intersection. This prevents “always learn more,” “always revise,” or “always preserve every distinction” from being credible roots.

Status: **completed countercontrol and exact conditional local criterion**. The local intersection test does not certify a longer protocol; information, time, authority, and financing must remain jointly feasible along the path. The mixed population is differently endowed, not evidence that the original failing population already had the same escape route.

Sources: `structural-foundations/outputs/cycle-6/synthesis.md:89–108`; `cycle-6/CLAIMS.md:15–16,21–25`.

**4. The supports of assessment are part of the explanation.** Inquiry may lose the measurement, records, training, or access needed to examine its own failures. A supplied maintenance/reconstruction model demonstrates that restoring current funding need not restore a lost capability: maintenance can be affordable while reconstruction is not. The effective-testability result bounds attainable error using the full feasible protocol class, including access, time, and budget. Repeated agreement is weaker when checks share a failure mechanism; a group of critics with no informative test or uptake route adds no automatic warrant.

Status: **completed conditional proofs and exact examples**, with empirical applications still requiring measured mechanisms. Neither source says preserve all apparatus forever. Substitution, restart, closure, and costly irrelevant inquiry are explicit counterweights.

Sources: `scientific-principles/outputs/scientific-change-formal-results.md:35–92`; `mechanisms-of-scientific-change.md:120–152`; `scientific-principles-investigation.md:197–213,229–247`.

## The strongest defensible general foundation

The two sessions support the following synthesis, which is an interpretation rather than a new theorem:

> A finite inquirer can become better oriented by making its own distinctions, methods, and standards available for examination, while keeping that examination connected to the world and to a feasible path of response.

“Available for examination” must include generation, admission, evidence, assessment, authority, execution, and retention. Collapsing these into “openness” erases different failure mechanisms. A true objection that cannot be heard, an admitted proposal that cannot be tested, a corrected belief with no means of action, and a propagated standard lacking evidential warrant are distinct cases.

The structure goes beyond introspection: a person's current perspective cannot supply all missing distinctions merely by inspecting itself. Other records, instruments, people, and practical encounters can be necessary. Conversely, social acceptance does not manufacture truth. The response of the world constrains a specified comparison, while the channels and interpretation remain revisable. Sources: `mechanisms-of-scientific-change.md:11–19,41–69,144–162`.

The foundation is conditional on something worth orienting action toward. Structural persistence is insufficient: a stable coercive arrangement can persist, and increased reachability can add ways to harm. Formal permission also does not establish legitimate authority. These are explicit counterexamples and normative distinctions in the source, not external objections invented by this audit. Sources: `structural-foundations/outputs/synthesis.md:190–214`; `outputs/CLAIMS.md:7,20–24`; `scientific-principles-investigation.md:241–249`.

## How to reach a useful root without an endless sequence of toy tests

The sensible target is a finite, explicit account whose generative use and limits we understand, rather than a guarantee that no future encounter can revise it. “Root” should identify what gives rise to wise judgment across cases; “understood” should name an assessable level of achievement. The sources support these concrete completion criteria:

1. **State the candidate and its scope.** Separate an epistemic capacity for revisable understanding, a practical capacity to carry warranted changes into action, and commitments about what and whom action should serve. Do not pretend the first two logically entail the third.
2. **Defend its discriminations.** It must explain why an accurate oppressor, an endlessly inquisitive but incapacitated agent, an uncritically adaptable institution, and a rigid but appropriately settled procedure receive different judgments. Existing source counterexamples already do much of this work; another simple choice quiz is unnecessary.
3. **Show generative use in a consequential real case.** Record the prior understanding, whose consequences and objections counted, what evidence could actually arrive, which distinctions or standards changed, and what feasible action followed. Include a reason for stopping or reopening. The goal is one complete accountable practice, not ever more isolated numerical demonstrations.
4. **Make its own revision possible.** Identify an objection or outcome that would narrow or overturn the candidate account, rather than redefining every failure as a missing ingredient. Provisionally fixed standards may be reconsidered later; infinite prior certification is not required. Keep results under old and changed aims separate where meaningful.
5. **Use an honest stopping statement.** We may claim to have reached and understood a *working root* when we can state it clearly, explain its mechanisms and exceptions, use it reliably enough within a declared domain, and preserve a feasible route for correction. Universal necessity, sufficiency, and ethical consensus remain stronger claims requiring separate arguments. Possessing the explanation does not itself establish wise character or conduct.

These are proposed criteria for the user's project, not a theorem or an already satisfied certification. They provide a route to completion that uses the completed work and does not require postponing every conclusion until all philosophy and all future discovery are solved. In particular, aims and standing need an explicit defensible position, not an impossible derivation from causal structure alone.
