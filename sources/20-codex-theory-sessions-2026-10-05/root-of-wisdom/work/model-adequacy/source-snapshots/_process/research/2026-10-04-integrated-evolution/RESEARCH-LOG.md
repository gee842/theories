## 4 October 2026 Universality becomes the explicit target

**User correction.** Ren clarified that the aim is a universal law or invariant that remains a useful framing despite complexity and incomplete observability, and that helps humanity coordinate and develop AI. This changes the ambition from merely integrating models to identifying restrictions that apply across implementations.

**Decision D01.** Keep universality as the research target while preserving distinctions among established identities, derived conditional results, hypotheses, and normative commitments. The inability to predict every trajectory does not by itself defeat a structural law.

**Decision D02.** Seek constraints on admissible histories and effective interventions. Do not assume a conserved scalar, a universally ascending objective, or that evolution inevitably approaches regeneration.

**Finding U01.** An independently identified indispensable stock obeying a balance has a cumulative deficit bound. With fixed positive deficit, reserve divided by deficit gives a deadline. The result permits temporary depletion, imports, and substitution; it must not be strengthened into a demand that every old stock be preserved forever.

**Finding U02.** A common multiplicative change to all abundances leaves shares unchanged. Accompanied by the corresponding common growth-rate shift, it gives an exact identification limit in pure-selection models: relative success alone cannot universally certify absolute viability.

**Insight U03.** An invariant can concern the relationship between activities and their enabling functions across replacement of carriers. This is different from a conserved amount. Montévil and Mossio's organizational closure is close prior art; novelty cannot be claimed for this observation alone.

**Decision D03.** Analyze the dependency network. A beneficiary can depend on maintenance supplied by others. Neither individual self-sufficiency nor moral virtue of every carrier follows from a renewal constraint. Imported support remains a boundary condition whose reliability can be investigated.

**Decision D04.** Treat agency, legitimate revision, and responsibilities to others as explicit commitments. A self-maintaining coercive system is a counterexample to inferring ethical adequacy from persistence.

**Research question.** Can reserve bounds, information requirements, intervention latency, and the renewal of corrective capacity yield useful restrictions without a fully observed model?

**Decision D14.** Charge rewards and overlap to identified resources. A fee is not proof that enforcement works; a total budget is not proof of enough peak capacity. Keep these implementation conditions visible.
