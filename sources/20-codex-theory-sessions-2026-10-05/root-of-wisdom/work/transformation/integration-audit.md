### A functioning present can have no executable successor

Source: `/Users/ren/theories/_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-health/ANSWER.md`, with proof in `MATHEMATICAL-ANALYSIS.md`.

The incumbent obeys `c'=alpha*u-delta*c`, successor progress obeys `x'=beta*(1-u)`, effort is in `[0,1]`, and current service requires `c>=c_*`. With `p=delta*c_*/alpha<=1` and `t0=log(c0/c_*)/delta`, its exact maximum construction by deadline T is

`X_max(T)=beta*[min(T,t0)+(1-p)*max(T-t0,0)]`.

At `c0=c_*` and `p=1`, current service can last indefinitely but no positive successor project can be installed. An inherited reserve can finance one passage without financing repeated passages. This is a paid transition theorem for an already specified workable successor, not a theory of its discovery. The deterministic optimum spends reserve first under stated assumptions; it is not a general rule for safe real transitions.

### Inquiry has to maintain its own means and still afford installation

## What remains open and how to avoid an artificial win

The discovery critical analysis explicitly separates learning, representational invention, functional invention, and formation of purposes. Existing situated-discovery models use a supplied predicate grammar, actions, and purpose. Therefore constructing a new classifier is not automatically a new effective action, and changing a numerical target is not evidence that a purpose emerged.

A meaningful new toy model should state the fixed comparison class precisely. Showing a selected bad controller fails is weaker than proving no controller with the original interface and budget can succeed. Conversely, a universally equipped controller may succeed while violating the same physical cost constraints: compare equivalent installed means and actual budgets.

If cochange is claimed necessary, perform separate restrictions on each candidate change and show where necessity holds. It may be that sensing alone, a robust action alone, or environmental reorganization alone also works in some regimes. Such alternatives sharpen the mechanism rather than refute transformation generally.

If a new arrangement preserves a service but changes who bears loss, no equivalence of service aggregates proves identity of the consequences for affected participants. Historical obligations and causal succession need explicit records if they are part of the requirement. A preserved response profile under a declared question cannot be promoted into all truth.

## Directly checked primary precursor

[Di Paolo, *Autopoiesis, adaptivity, teleology, agency* (2005)](https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/autopoiesis_teleology_2005.pdf), especially pp. 435–440 and notes on pp. 449–450, argues that continuing self-production alone does not imply a system detects worsening conditions or regulates them. Adaptivity adds distinction and correction of trends toward viability loss, potentially with restructuring. Regulation can be limited and need not amount to agency that changes environmental encounters. This is a conceptual biological account, not an ethical derivation or guarantee under arbitrary change. The primary text was opened and its adaptivity definition and caveats directly checked during this audit.
