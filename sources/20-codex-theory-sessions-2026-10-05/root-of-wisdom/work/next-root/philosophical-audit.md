# The next root: constrained individuation and the route toward monism

Independent philosophical audit, 5 October 2026. This is a new research direction. The existing formalization is preserved and remains useful.

## Positive recommendation

The strongest next step is **constrained individuation**: investigate how identifiable parts, capacities, and perspectives arise within organized processes, and establish when these parts can actually be treated as independent.

Proposed formulation:

> A part is a real differentiation within an organized reality. Its identity, possible states, and capacities are constrained by how it belongs to that organization. Independence is a property to establish, rather than an assumption supplied by naming the part.

This is a proposed explanatory principle. Its rigor must come from particular mechanisms of individuation, explicit criteria of independence, and failures of proposed decompositions. The universal quantifier in “a part” is philosophical extrapolation; the mathematical results can establish only their specified domains. This direction should therefore begin with the sharper question: **when does the joint process permit a sufficient description in terms of independently specifiable parts?**

Why it is a deeper step: the current account explains consequences once interactions, states, and distinctions have been identified. The next question concerns the origin and validity of those units themselves. The earlier account need not be abandoned: law selection explains the dynamics, response tests detect missing distinctions, and the present direction asks which decompositions preserve that explanatory content.

The most useful compact wording is **distinguishability without assumed separability**. The fact that two features are distinguishable does not imply that each can exist, vary, or act independently of the other. Conversely, a dependency does not make the features numerically identical or unreal.

## Exact distinctions needed before claiming monism

The following are working definitions for our investigation, not claims that every author uses the vocabulary identically.

| Claim | What it asserts | What would support it |
|---|---|---|
| Descriptive unity | One formal description collects the variables | A joint state space or history model |
| Dynamical coupling | Changing one component can alter another under a specified intervention | An identified mechanism and intervention-sensitive dynamics |
| Failure of free recombination | Some individually possible component states cannot jointly occur in the specified regime | A proper subset of the Cartesian product of local possibilities |
| Informational nonseparability | Specified local descriptions fail to determine the joint description | Two distinct joint states with identical local descriptions |
| Constitutive dependence | A thing has a given capacity or identity because of its organization or role | A warranted account of what constitutes that capacity or thing |
| Ontological priority | One entity grounds another entity's existence or nature | An argument for an asymmetric grounding relation, beyond prediction or representation |
| Priority monism | Exactly one concrete entity is fundamental, with other concrete entities dependent on it | A defended priority relation whose ultimate ground is the whole |
| Existence monism | Exactly one concrete object exists | A further argument that apparently distinct objects are not genuine parts or objects |
| Substance monism | There is exactly one substance in the specified sense of substance | A substantive account of substance and an argument establishing uniqueness |

These do not form an automatic implication chain. A single equation can describe two uncoupled oscillators. Coupled oscillators can be interpreted as two genuine objects linked by a force. A correlated preparation can remain correlated after interaction ceases. And a model that requires a joint state does not itself determine which entity metaphysically grounds which.

## Primary philosophical anchors

**Schaffer.** In *Monism: The Priority of the Whole*, Schaffer distinguishes the existence of many parts from the question of what is fundamental. His framework assumes a cosmos with proper parts; composition is not identity; metaphysical priority forms a well-founded strict order. The last assumption excludes both endless grounding chains and circular grounding. He then argues for the whole's priority using physical and modal considerations. These are explicit substantive assumptions, not conclusions supplied by writing a global state vector. [Schaffer, 2010, §§1.1–1.3, printed pp. 33–40](https://www.jonathanschaffer.org/monism.pdf).

**Spinoza.** *Ethics* I defines substance through existence and conception in itself, and modes through another. Proposition 14 concludes substance uniqueness using the absolutely infinite substance's existence and the claim that substances cannot share an attribute; Proposition 15 places everything in that substance. Thus the argument contains an account of substance, modal dependence, existence, and attribute individuation. An experiment demonstrating coupling does not establish that entire package. [Spinoza, *Ethics* I, definitions 3–6 and propositions 5, 11, 14–15, Elwes translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3800/pg3800-images.html#chap01).

**Whitehead.** *Process and Reality* treats concrete actualities as occasions of becoming, with a plurality of actual entities and relations by which antecedent actualities enter a new occasion. This supplies a serious process-oriented alternative: becoming and interdependence can be fundamental without replacing the plurality by one basic concrete object. Our candidate should be compared with this alternative rather than treating process language as already establishing monism. [Whitehead, *Process and Reality*, Part I, chapter II, corrected edition, printed pp. 18–23](https://pubhtml5.com/vmmz/lthw/basic/).

**Rovelli.** The original relational-quantum-mechanics paper proposes interpreting physical states relative to other systems, without a privileged observer/observed division. That is an interpretive proposal about quantum theory. It supports taking relational descriptions seriously; it is not an experimental proof that the cosmos is the uniquely grounding object. [Rovelli, 1996](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002).

Read scope: Schaffer's primary paper, particularly its opening framework and explicit assumptions; Spinoza's cited definitions and proofs; Whitehead's opening categoreal exposition; Rovelli's original abstract and statement of interpretive aim. This audit does not claim to have reconstructed all four systems or settled their controversies. External summaries above remain short; subsequent sections are our analysis and constructed examples.

## A constructive argument that does not overreach

Consider two physically interpreted quantities A and B, each individually admitting values 0 and 1. Let the jointly permitted set be

\[
R=\{(0,0),(1,1)\}.
\]

Both local projections are \(\{0,1\}\), but their product contains four possibilities while R contains only two. Therefore a catalogue of what each quantity can individually do loses a real joint constraint. Assuming free recombination introduces possibilities the actual organization forbids.

This is already a positive root-level lesson: **possibility is organized jointly**. The local inventory is not always a sufficient basis for reconstructing what can occur. The independent-parts model is a special case where the joint constraint factors as a product.

But three interpretations remain:

1. One common process has two distinguishable manifestations.
2. Two objects are tied by a fundamental relation.
3. Two objects inherit a constraint from a common preparation or additional variable.

All three can represent this example. A common latent bit L with A=L and B=L realizes the third. The construction proves failure of free recombination for the specified preparation, not uniqueness of substance. Releasing the physical constraint could make the previously forbidden pairs accessible. The ontological interpretation must therefore distinguish a regime-dependent constraint from a metaphysical impossibility.

A corresponding informational example assigns equal probability to 00 and 11, versus equal probability to 01 and 10. Each bit is individually uniform in both cases, while joint correlation differs. Marginals omit relational information. This occurs in classical probability, so the bare inference “marginals fail, therefore quantum monism” would already be invalid. Quantum results may establish stronger nonseparability under their own physical assumptions, but that additional content must be stated and independently sourced.

The next advance is to derive the joint restriction from a warranted mechanism, show why a particular decomposition loses predictions, and identify the smallest correction required. Simply renaming R “the whole” gives no further explanation.

## Candidate roots compared

| Candidate | Positive explanatory potential | Main burden or weakness | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composition and constrained individuation | Explains why some apparently separate properties, objects, or capacities cannot be specified independently; invites explicit factorization and closure tests | A common representation alone is cheap; physical decomposition must be justified | Strongest next research direction |
| Mutual determination | Captures capacities or patterns sustained by reciprocal dependencies | Excludes asymmetric cases if made universal; dynamical reciprocity is not circular grounding | Use as a mechanism within the broader direction |
| Generativity | Explains how activity forms conditions of future activity | Already central to the retained theory; unconstrained generative functions are empty | Preserve and connect to formation of parts |
| Consistency or compatibility | Explains why locally plausible claims or actions cannot always coexist | Mere logical consistency admits indefinitely many incompatible worlds and laws | Treat as a necessary test, not the complete new root |
| One substance or one fundamental whole | Offers an ultimate answer to what differentiation belongs to | Requires a justified grounding/substance account and comparison with relational pluralism | Keep as a downstream hypothesis |

The direction should retain the possibility of several roots because these answer different questions. Compatibility asks which descriptions can coexist; generation asks how states arise; individuation asks which entities and boundaries are real and explanatory; grounding asks what depends on what in being. Compressing them into “everything is one” before their roles are understood would remove useful explanatory distinctions.

## Adversarial checks

**1. The global-coordinate trick.** Any finite collection of variables can be written as one vector. This compression of notation does not establish a single physical object, interaction, shared substance, or priority. Remedy: require the proposed whole to impose testable joint constraints or yield independently justified derivations unavailable from the stated local inventory.

**2. The product-coordinate trick in reverse.** A finite set can sometimes be bijectively relabeled as a product. Coordinate factorization alone does not establish physical separability either. Observable access, interventions, admissible preparations, and composition rules must anchor the coordinates.

**3. Knowledge is not being.** Needing to know B to predict A establishes a limitation of the selected information or a dependency of the prediction. It does not show A exists in virtue of B. Likewise, finding a smaller sufficient predictor does not prove that omitted entities fail to exist.

**4. Correlation is not current interaction.** Shared preparation can cause enduring correlation in two otherwise uncoupled processes. A dependence in a probability distribution and a causal influence under interventions are distinct claims. A common-cause explanation is a competitor, not a trivial nuisance to exclude by definition.

**5. Coupling is not constitutive identity.** A planet's trajectory can depend on another planet without either being constituted by the other. A cell's metabolic capacity may depend constitutively on a network. Which kind of dependence is present requires additional biological or physical analysis.

**6. Formation is not metaphysical grounding.** A factory's earlier production of a component and that component's present constitution of a machine are different relations. Reciprocally sustaining processes do not violate an asymmetric grounding theory merely because their causal graph has a cycle.

**7. Exactness must not erase useful independence.** A tiny interaction can make strict independence false while a bounded-error autonomous model remains excellent. The root must accommodate approximation, observation horizon, and interventions. Otherwise it turns every connected environment into a demand to model the entire cosmos.

**8. Universal connectedness is not yet established.** Results in one bounded process do not prove every actual concrete thing is linked into the same irreducible component. Even such connectedness would not settle how many fundamental entities exist, because a connected relational plurality is logically possible.

**9. Compatibility needs a justified domain.** In a classical model a joint assignment can be required. Do not silently require simultaneous values for every incompatible quantum observable and then treat the resulting contradiction as a defect of reality. Mathematical composition rules are substantive physical assumptions.

**10. Non-independence does not imply dispensability of individuals.** Dependence can coexist with real agency, distinct experience, and morally significant interests. “One whole” cannot by itself license sacrificing a person to an alleged collective good. Neither irreducibility nor persistence derives an ethical ordering.

## Strongest defensible next claim

> Where a process has irreducible joint constraints, its parts cannot be understood exhaustively as independently specifiable bearers of properties. A faithful account must include the organization that makes those differentiated parts and their capacities possible.

This statement has testable conditional content. It preserves real differences while rejecting unwarranted independence. It also makes monism a precise research direction: determine whether explanatory dependence is merely local and contextual, or whether apparently separate domains can be derived as differentiations of one more comprehensive organization.

The immediate deliverable should therefore establish one step—when and how individuation depends on joint organization—and leave the stronger grounding hypothesis explicit. The next discriminating question is not merely “can we name a whole?” It is: **what explanatory work does the proposed whole do that independently existing relata plus their relations cannot do equally well?** A successful answer would be progress toward priority monism. An answer that only reiterates correlation would be progress in modeling, but would leave that metaphysical contest open.
