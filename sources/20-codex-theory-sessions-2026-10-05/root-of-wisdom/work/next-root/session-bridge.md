## Recommendation

The strongest next question is **what makes a distinction into a relatively autonomous thing**. Earlier work begins with interactions, their constraints, and the responses they generate. This step asks which units of interaction the dynamics warrant treating as parts, and at what scale. A useful candidate principle is:

> A part is not made independent by our naming it. Its identity is supported by patterns of internal coherence and selective interaction that remain usable across an explicitly stated range of changes.

This can take the inquiry toward a process interpretation of unity while preserving real differences. It does not require saying that every connected system is one substance, that the universe has exactly one causal component, or that mathematical representation determines physical ontology.

The latest other-session results strengthen the demand that observation, evaluation, maintenance, and refusal themselves have physical or institutional implementation. They do not yet derive the relevant decomposition into components. That is a real remaining problem, and therefore a substantive new direction.

### 1. Mechanisms generate persistence or collapse

Source: `/Users/ren/theories/_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/final-synthesis/MECHANISM-MATH.md`, especially sections 2–7 and 9–11; core model lines 20–49.

This strengthens earlier two-stock and practice-transmission results to a class of increasing, strictly concave, bounded production laws. Capacities carried by high- and low-renewal practices obtain support, expend it on replacement, decay, and switch practices in response to immediate savings. The derived aggregate equations are

\[
\dot E=P(C)-\delta E-\bar u EC,\qquad
\dot C=(\eta\bar u E-\mu)C,
\qquad \dot x=(\eta-\kappa)E(u_H-u_L)x(1-x).
\]

Writing \(m=\mu/\eta\) and \(H=\max_{C\ge0}[P(C)-mC]\), the positive-equilibrium threshold is \(u_c=\delta m/H\). Away from the equality cases, the selection-favored effort is high when \(\kappa<\eta\), low when \(\kappa>\eta\). A positive hyperbolic locally attracting equilibrium exists exactly when the selected effort exceeds \(u_c\). If it is strictly subcritical, the companion proves collapse from every mixed initial population. The proof uses the actual accumulated support exposure, so it does not silently assume selection finishes before collapse.

This is meaningful mathematics: continuation is obtained or lost through specified local replacement mechanisms. The report also distinguishes local attraction from rescue from arbitrary depleted states and deterministic attraction from guaranteed survival of finite stochastic populations.

**Bridge to new direction:** a carrier can persist while its material instances are replaced. Identity over time can therefore be a maintained organization or transmitted response pattern. But the proof **supplies** the carrier types, support/capacity decomposition, production technology, and copying rule. It does not show how these are first individuated. The source explicitly says, at line 49, that their historical origin and the implementation of an institution controlling copying remain unmodeled.

## A concrete mathematical continuation from the current diffusion result

This is a proposed new illustration developed in this survey, not a theorem imported from the other chats or an empirical result.

Take four quantities in two pairs. Within each pair, exchange occurs at rate \(k>0\). Each site exchanges with the mean of the other pair at rate \(\varepsilon>0\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot x_1&=k(x_2-x_1)+\varepsilon[(x_3+x_4)/2-x_1],\\
\dot x_2&=k(x_1-x_2)+\varepsilon[(x_3+x_4)/2-x_2],\\
\dot x_3&=k(x_4-x_3)+\varepsilon[(x_1+x_2)/2-x_3],\\
\dot x_4&=k(x_3-x_4)+\varepsilon[(x_1+x_2)/2-x_4].
\end{aligned}
\]

The full graph is connected. With pair means \(a=(x_1+x_2)/2\), \(b=(x_3+x_4)/2\), exact addition and subtraction give

\[
\dot a=\varepsilon(b-a),\quad
\dot b=\varepsilon(a-b),\quad
\frac{d}{dt}(x_1-x_2)=-(2k+\varepsilon)(x_1-x_2),
\]

and the corresponding within-second-pair equation. Thus within-pair differences decay as \(e^{-(2k+\varepsilon)t}\), while between-pair difference decays as \(e^{-2\varepsilon t}\).

If \(k\gg\varepsilon\), there is a range of times in which each pair has internally equilibrated but the two pair means still differ. More precisely, for a tolerance \(0<\delta<1\), internal differences have fallen to at most \(\delta\) of their initial magnitude and pair contrast retains at least \(1-\delta\) when

\[
\frac{\log(1/\delta)}{2k+\varepsilon}\le t\le
\frac{-\log(1-\delta)}{2\varepsilon}.
\]

The interval is nonempty exactly when the lower bound does not exceed the upper bound. No arbitrary word “slow” is needed; the approximation has a stated tolerance and horizon. If initial between-pair contrast is zero, there is no observed contrast to preserve, although the mode and its response to an allowed preparation remain present.

This demonstrates a precise candidate advance: **one interacting process can support relatively autonomous, distinguishable units at a scale**, with unity and differentiation simultaneously measurable. It does not derive the origin of the coupling pattern; that would require a model of formation or selection of the couplings. It does not show a unique decomposition at every scale or imply that distinct units are illusory.

## Scope and unclosed gaps

1. Representation, physical realization, and ontological unity remain different claims. A common encoding proves neither a universal physical substrate nor an independently observed universal law.
2. The new mathematical target should identify exact or approximate closure, persistence, controllability, and memory across a proposed boundary; “everything is connected” alone specifies none of these.
3. When parts appear through a timescale separation, verify robustness to changed preparations and interventions, including perturbations that reveal a previously hidden coupling.
4. Generating and sustaining a boundary is stronger than recognizing one in a supplied coupling matrix. The first new step can solve the recognition problem and explicitly retain formation as the next burden.
5. Unity of process does not erase plural needs, distinct observation positions, consent, or moral standing. The fresh participation result is a useful constraint on any philosophical synthesis.
6. The source corpus offers a credible process-oriented route toward monism, not an already completed proof of monism. “Differentiated unity” is a defensible research interpretation once its particular mechanisms are shown; “one substance is logically necessary” is not established by these results.
