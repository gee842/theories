# From faithful representation to substantive explanation

5 October 2026. Read-only audit of the recent full-picture work and generative-foundations programme, plus constructive mathematical synthesis. The proposals below are not claims of a new experimentally established law of physics.

## 1. Correct the target before extending it

The previous demand that a root explain *which exact fundamental laws the world supplies* was unnecessarily stronger than the user's Maxwell analogy. A substantive theory can explain a broad family of phenomena through fixed restrictions, mechanisms, and independent measurements while leaving constitutive parameters and boundary conditions to each application. It need not derive the universe's unique microscopic dynamics from logic.

The right next target is: **a common principle which determines some previously unconstrained consequences from independently specified conditions, and says how experience distinguishes competing realizations.** An invariant representation equation alone does not do this. Neither does stating a universal transition function without restrictions on its mechanisms.

## 2. Strongest constructive principle available from the recent work

> Realized processes form, consume, maintain, and reorganize the conditions of subsequent processes. A difference has causal significance when changing it changes realizable consequences. For a self-maintaining being, some of these consequences concern the continuation of the very capacities by which it senses, acts, and responds.

This combines three claims that should retain separate status:

1. **Mechanism:** local interactions under material constraints generate outcomes and alter later effective possibilities. This is a substantive modeling hypothesis when the mechanisms and constraints are independently measured.
2. **Relevance:** consequential distinctions are forced by differences in possible responses to interventions, not merely by chosen descriptive vocabulary. The equivalence/quotient results provide its exact mathematical form.
3. **Constitutive stakes:** a process's conditions of living and acting give some differences functional significance. Biological/psychological interpretation needs evidence; moral standing is not equivalent to competitive persistence.

This is considerably stronger than “everything is interconnected” or “all is change.” It requires specifying the dependency, its sign and magnitude, its time scale, the material support, the permitted intervention, and the outcome. It permits collapse, rest, transformation, and termination; no automatic growth or immortality is presumed.

## 4. A genuinely substantive miniature law

The following reciprocal-production mechanism shows what fixed explanatory content would look like. Let two maintained capacities have nonnegative amounts x and y. Let f be a supplied resource level held constant over the modeled interval. Positive constants a, b, delta, and mu describe formation and loss:

\[
\dot x=afy-\delta x,\qquad
\dot y=bx-\mu y.
\]

This represents y-dependent production of x using the supplied resource, x-dependent production of y, and loss of both capacities. It is an idealized local linear mechanism, not a complete chemical or biological system. The external support and finite range over which the linear approximation applies must be stated.

The characteristic polynomial is

\[
\lambda^2+(\delta+\mu)\lambda+\delta\mu-abf=0,
\]

so its larger eigenvalue is

\[
\lambda_+=-\frac{\delta+\mu}{2}
+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(\delta-\mu)^2+4abf}.
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\lambda_+>0\iff abf>\delta\mu.}
\]

Below the threshold both eigenvalues are negative and every trajectory tends toward loss of the capacities. At equality there is a neutral mode; this does not imply robustness. Above it there is a growing positive mode. For positive initial amounts that mode has positive coefficient. Nonlinear resource depletion or saturation must be added before predicting long-run persistence or a stable positive state.

This derives a nontrivial difference between *a dependency cycle* and *effective mutual support*: the very same cycle can decay or replenish, depending on independently measurable rates and resource conditions. For example, with a=b=delta=mu=1, the boundary is f=1. A reduction of f below one changes the sign of the leading growth rate without altering the dependency graph.

No new physics has been derived. The advance is an explicit mechanism-to-consequence bridge: rates calibrated separately imply a withheld threshold prediction. Removing either production link predicts decay. Raising loss shifts the resource threshold. Those predictions could fail in an actual target, exposing missing mechanisms or an invalid approximation.

## 5. A constructive principle for selecting laws by contact with the world

Let Lambda be a finite class of deterministic candidate mechanisms. Each admitted experiment e includes its preparation, intervention, elapsed time, and observation, with a predicted outcome r_lambda(e). Assume:

- the actual mechanism belongs to Lambda;
- experiments can be implemented with the required preparations and truthful observations;
- its response law remains stable over the experiment sequence;
- any claimed resource, protection, and resetting conditions actually hold.

Define empirical equivalence within the available experiment family by

\[
\lambda\equiv_E\lambda'
\iff \forall e\in E,\quad r_\lambda(e)=r_{\lambda'}(e).
\]

**Finite identification theorem.** The actual E-equivalence class can be identified by at most |Lambda|-1 separating experiments.

**Proof.** Maintain candidates consistent with every observed result. If they occupy more than one E-equivalence class, choose two inequivalent candidates. Some experiment separates their predictions. Its actual result eliminates at least one of that pair, while retaining the actual mechanism. Thus every performed query strictly reduces the candidate count. After at most |Lambda|-1 reductions, all remaining candidates are E-equivalent. No available experiment can distinguish members of that final class. Conversely, if two candidates are E-equivalent, every adaptive experiment procedure sees the same transcript under them, so exact identification between those two is impossible through E alone. QED.

This theorem directly answers a narrower, useful form of “which law does the world supply?”: **world response selects an equivalence class of candidate laws, and the available interventions determine its achievable resolution.** It does not infer truth from internal consistency. A future disagreement eliminates the current class or exposes a changed mechanism or broken observation contract.

The theorem is deliberately deterministic and finite. No fixed finite bound follows for noisy observations without separation and error assumptions. Unknown mechanisms outside Lambda require expanding the language. Actions that change the mechanism or prevent reset require sequential experiment design rather than pretending that independent queries remain available. Experiments should not be undertaken merely because they distinguish laws: their costs and consequences remain part of the decision.

Existing invariant causal prediction is an established route from variation across environments to mechanism identification under explicit assumptions. It is relevant evidence that intervention stability can do more than redescribe a known law; it is not a proof that every invariant predictor is causal. [Peters, Bühlmann, and Meinshausen](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.01332).

## 6. Where consequential distinctions come from

There are at least three distinct sources:

1. **Dynamical:** a change yields a different physically realizable future under an admitted intervention. This is independent of whether the observer presently measures it.
2. **Constitutive:** the change enables or disables a capacity implicated in the system's maintained organization: sensing, metabolism, repair, movement, or response. The organization and capacity can be investigated rather than treated as a label.
3. **Ethical:** an affected being's suffering, agency, commitments, or other interests give reasons for responding. Their causal basis can be investigated, but respecting them is not reducible to a system's replication rate.

One must avoid saying that all meaning was arbitrarily selected merely because a formalization declares its system boundary. The physical effects of loss of circulation, for example, are not created by writing a utility function. Conversely, finding a self-maintaining process does not show that maximizing its duration is morally obligatory. Cancer and exploitative institutions prevent that shortcut.

Relevant primary research, checked through web tools on 5 October 2026:

- Montévil and Mossio characterize biological organization through interdependent constraints that contribute to each other's maintenance at stated time scales. This is a theoretical framework, not a derivation of all biological organization from pure logic. [Author-hosted paper](https://montevil.org/publications/articles/2015-mm-organisation-closure-constraints/).
- Kolchinsky and Wolpert define a form of semantic information through counterfactual scrambling of system–environment correlations and their contribution to the system's continued low-entropy existence. Their chosen viability measure, boundary, and horizon remain part of the formalization. This supplies a rigorous route to causal significance, not a universal ethics or completed account of consciousness. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08053).

## 7. A candidate compact foundation

The strongest appropriate synthesis is:

> **Actual dependencies constrain possibilities; realized activity changes those dependencies; truthful understanding preserves the consequential dependencies; wise response takes responsibility for the beings and capacities altered by its own participation.**

The first two clauses need mechanisms and contact with experience. The third admits exact invariance/minimality theorems. The last introduces substantive practical and ethical reasons. This does not merely append arbitrary values to an otherwise value-free machine: constitutive and experienced stakes are part of the world's processes. It also does not erase the work of defending obligations among beings.

For a concise final response, emphasize the first substantial correction: we need not explain why precisely this universe rather than every other. We can establish a foundational law of **how actual conditions generate, maintain, and change possibilities**, and make its implementations answerable to observations and interventions. The miniature threshold illustrates the missing kind of content, while the identification theorem supplies a concrete route to discovering which realization is ours.

## 8. Evidence caution from the existing empirical work

The existing microbial external-data study is mixed after a preprocessing repair. Adding composition reduced primary condition-held-out RMSE by 7.3% but slightly worsened MAE; the stronger source-held-out sensitivity was favorable. The source-block uncertainty is coarse with four sources. Its target was ordinary next-cycle biomass, not recovery or self-maintenance under a novel shock. This is evidence for some predictive content of composition summaries in one setting, not confirmation of a universal generative law. No new numerical audit of that study was performed here.
