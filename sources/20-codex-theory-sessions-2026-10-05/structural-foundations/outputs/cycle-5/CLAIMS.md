# Claim register: funded selection and population renewal

5 October 2026. These are supplied synthetic mechanisms, conditional proofs, and exact finite computations. Numerical prices, grants, program menus, and copying institutions are assumptions. No empirical universality, evolutionary inevitability, ethical ranking, or mathematical priority is claimed.

| Claim | Status | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A contribution score does not itself finance a successor. Absolute funded counts must precede share calculations. | Accounting and probabilistic distinction | [Population formalism](population-and-scale.md). Random expected share generally differs from the ratio of expected counts; shares at extinction are undefined. |
| Actual early winner copying drives S to fixation at epoch one and the population to extinction at epoch 23. | Exact constructed outcome with finite-potential proof | [Funded selection](funded-selection.md). Same two-cycle generation for all treatments; compulsory retirement, fresh plant, grant 150/parent, audit 2/parent, birth packet 260, four sites, treasury cap 259. |
| Late winner copying selects L and sustains nonempty renewal under the supplied inputs. | Exact invariant | Every L returns 136 and receives net grant 148, covering one 260-unit birth. [Results](verification/funded-selection-results.json). Reaches four sites at epoch 11 in the base run; persistence does not imply growth under every treasury cap. |
| Renewal potential satisfies `Φ'=Φ−12N_S+24N_L−spill`, with `Φ=260N+R`. | Exact identity | [Proof and separate cash ledger](funded-selection.md). Replacement potential is not literal cash; the child's 240 is a transfer, not destroyed expenditure. |
| Pure-L and pure-S per-parent replacement grant thresholds are 126 and 162 respectively. | Conditional exact financing thresholds | Derived from final returns, audit cost, and birth packet. They concern this retirement/funding mechanism, not individual operating viability. No continuing grant makes both base treatments fail to reproduce. |
| A calibrated early report yields the same population kernel as late observation. | Exact controlled equality | 3,900 state comparisons plus equality of scores used for copying. This is not a useful practical extinction-time estimate. |
