## What remains outside this cycle

Paid long-term controller memory, unreliable diagnostics, unrestricted candidate or concept generation, selection that endogenously produces the winning controller, strategic conflict, and legitimate authority remain open. The recurrent model permits temporary loss of production during repair; its protected duty is solvency plus completion by each deadline, not uninterrupted service. Continued solvency or larger correction capacity does not establish ethical goodness.
