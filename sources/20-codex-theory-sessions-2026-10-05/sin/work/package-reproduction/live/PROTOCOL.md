# Feedback and successor correction experiment

Task date 5 October 2026. Frozen before any main participant response. This is a live AI experiment on a constructed routing task, not a human trial or a demonstration of general institutional alignment.

## Claims and fixed sample

Thirty two independently created founder contexts comprise 24 changing rule lineages and eight unchanged rule controls. Each founder later has two fresh successor contexts, giving 64 successor observations. The founder lineage is the clustering unit. All participants use the parent's configured model and effort; no model override or model population claim is made. Actual fresh model responses supply decisions; they are not programmed by the scoring code. No outcome based stopping, task replacement, prompt adjustment or pilot exclusion is allowed.

The three confirmatory positive predictions in the 24 challenge lineages are: timely feedback increases the rate of delivered maintenance packets; operative packet access improves successor correctness after timely feedback; and the access gain is larger after timely than delayed feedback. Each test uses a two sided exact randomization probability and a Bonferroni threshold .05/3. All three must be positive and pass to support the registered coupled mechanism. Each may independently fail. A null first arrow must not be replaced after seeing outcomes by packet quality. An interaction is not automatically mediation.

## Task

Legacy route equals one named binary field. Current truth is legacy or its inversion when a fixed conjunction of two other fields is satisfied. All 24 pair and bit value exceptions are included once, plus eight unchanged controls. Fields are renamed and reordered separately for each lineage. Participants know the candidate family, not the current truth. This is bounded rule identification, not unconstrained concept discovery.

The mandatory founder baseline has eight even parity combinations of the other four fields, with balanced legacy field. Complete report labels distinguish all 25 hypotheses. Each exception changes two legacy report answers. Successors receive all eight odd parity combinations with both legacy values, 16 cases. No full context was in the founder report; four legacy answers are wrong per exception. The true rule does not change during the handoff. The test concerns transfer under a shifted case distribution, not robustness to a new hidden rule.

## Timing and treatments

All baseline answers must be sealed before A is drawn. Within each feature pair block of four exception lineages, two receive timely verified report and two delayed report. The eight unchanged controls form two blocks of four with two timely each. The PRNG algorithm and seeds are fixed in run.py. The report is truthful and includes all baseline labels and the founder score. Delayed founders are told only the report's timing. Both receive the same maintenance task.

Each founder has four explicit work slots. It routes four ordinary cases or uses one slot to deliver a packet of at most 120 words and routes three cases. Each correct current or successor answer is worth one team point. Packet delivery probability is disclosed as one half. This prices maintenance as a foregone ordinary case; no claim of equal model compute or economic human stakes is made. The one point opportunity cost is an intentional design choice, lower than the maximum two point expected successor improvement from correcting four cases at half delivery.

After all maintenance submissions are sealed, B is assigned by a fresh fair coin to one of two independently started successor seats. The other seat receives the opposite condition. Both get identical base orientation and test rows. B=1 receives the actual valid packet; B=0 gets a fixed no packet notification. No packet lineages remain included. Founder input and all other research files are prohibited to successors. This uses paired potential task replicas, not a second human recipient in the original protocol.

## Scoring and uncertainty

Maintenance M=1 requires allocation of one slot and an actually delivered nonempty packet of at most 120 whitespace words. A too long or empty packet is invalid; it remains in the denominator and consumes its declared slot. Primary successor outcome is correct answers divided by 16, with missing, invalid or extra unsupported output scored according to the first required entries; absent required entries are incorrect. No correctness reward for following an inherited rule.

For each lineage d=Y(B=1)-Y(B=0). First arrow is challenge mean M at A=1 minus A=0. The access effect is mean d among timely challenge lineages. The interaction is the timely minus delayed difference in d. A inference enumerates all 6^6=46656 blocked assignments. B inference enumerates all 2^12=4096 seat swaps in the timely challenge set. Two sided probabilities count absolute statistics at least the observed absolute value, including ties, divided by the full assignment count. The B sharp null is no effect on either observed seat. These probabilities condition on the realized responses; shared model training and runtime variability limit extrapolation.

Report all four challenge arm means, paired effects, current service counts and correctness, total scored team outcomes and the unchanged controls. Bootstrap intervals over founder lineages within treatment groups may be given as descriptive sensitivity only, not the confirmatory criterion. Source data, prompts, actual outputs, assignments and hashes are retained.

## Execution integrity

Participants start with no parent history, may read only their designated prompt and write only their response file, and must not inspect neighboring files, use internet, spawn agents, or message other participants. The founder maintenance prompt is self contained and includes the baseline answers; it is sent to that founder context. Successors always start fresh. Every invocation identity and permitted input is logged. The orchestrator necessarily knows the truth to generate reports, but the scorer uses fixed code. An independent audit checks scores and protocol deviations.

Only an explicit technical tool failure permits one identical retry; invalid task responses are outcomes. No answer correction or requested improvement is permitted. A context count mismatch, forbidden information access, treatment leakage, altered prompt or early outcome guided adaptation must be disclosed. There is one constrained response opportunity rather than a controlled wall clock deadline.

## Interpretation limits

The intervention is feedback timing and actual packet availability. Positive results support those mechanisms in this model, task grammar, work budget and information boundary. They do not establish indefinite renewal, human legitimacy, a universal theory, or superiority to all conventional learning methods. The known candidate family and diagnostic coverage make the task unusually favorable to rule identification; a conventional exact learner is a serious comparator and is scored separately. A negative or null result is retained without selecting a new primary claim.
