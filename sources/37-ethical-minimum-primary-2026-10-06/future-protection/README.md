**Bounded source synopsis:** Pummer develops a person-affecting account using requiring and permitting reasons. Components of future welfare, rather than only a life's total, inform prospective complaints. Section 2 defines suffering broadly as negative welfare and supplies a nonconsequentialist framework; neither follows from generative dynamics. The account distinguishes favoring happy formation from requiring it. Page 19 n. 17 states an extension from creating lives to allowing them to arise, permitting different reason strengths; it does not separately prove obligations for uninvolved helpers. Page 26 n. 26 distinguishes formation choices from rescuing independently existing people, whose particular claims matter. Silencing and unresolved aggregation questions further constrain application. This is a philosophical proposal, not a completed foundational derivation or empirical result.
