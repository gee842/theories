**Bounded source synopsis:** Kumar grounds future protections in generic reasons associated with representative individual standpoints. Particular people, not abstract standpoints, hold claims by instantiating relevant circumstances. General permissions must be justifiable from affected standpoints, including expected future people; identities need not already be fixed. His account separates ex ante risk from realized harm and later endorsement.
