# TCLab physical benchmark sources

Original author preprint and source archive for Park, Martin, Kelly and Hedengren (2020), *Benchmark Temperature Microcontroller for Process Dynamics and Control*, DOI [10.1016/j.compchemeng.2020.106736](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compchemeng.2020.106736).

Downloaded from the [authors' APMonitor repository](https://github.com/APMonitor/arduino/tree/f36e65a70dd7122d1829883899e40e56bf6c4279), pinned to that exact revision. [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) records URLs, byte counts, hashes and all selected archive members. The archive and 14 extracted originals retain their bytes and line endings. Their duplication is preservation, not independent evidence. The preprint is the authors' version, not a claim to preserve the final publisher layout.

The selected members support physical controller-log replay, separate model identification/validation and inspection of the optimizer's objective discrepancy. They are data and historical source code; do not run their hardware/remote-solver operations as part of the research checker. Our derived numerical code/results live in [the experiment directory](../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/inquiry-correspondence), and interpretation is in [the research record](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-JUDGMENT-EMPIRICAL.md).

This is archival reanalysis, not a new hardware experiment, independent biological evidence or empirical validation of an ethical premise. No modification of upstream originals is claimed.


The [timing continuation](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/TIMED-FEEDBACK-INTEGRATION.md) preserves eleven further MPC source/log members in [TIMING-PROVENANCE.json](TIMING-PROVENANCE.json). This additive manifest leaves the original fourteen-member record unchanged. Physics/ANN Summary copies are duplicates; the ARX Summary and illustration logs are distinct. Initialized and unacquired terminal rows remain byte-preserved and are identified in the derived analysis. Exact issue times and hardware power are not present in these logs.
