## Sahyoun and colleagues, 2019

Nadine R. Sahyoun, Zeina Jamaluddine, Jowel Choufani, Sandra Mesmar, Amelia Reese-Masterson and Hala Ghattas. *A mixed-methods evaluation of community-based healthy kitchens as social enterprises for refugee women*. BMC Public Health 19, 1590. [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12889-019-7950-3), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-019-7950-3).

- [Original full-text XML](sahyoun-2019.xml), retrieved from the Europe PMC public full-text API; [download metadata and SHA-256](sahyoun-2019-provenance.json).
- Read the complete main article through the publisher, including methods, results, discussion, limitations and availability statement; inspected both tables in the XML. References were not independently reread. The figure was not visually audited and supplies no new extracted values.
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), copyright the authors, 2019. This archive preserves the source XML without changes; research notes and aggregate extraction are separately marked adaptations. The article also identifies its published data under CC0 unless otherwise stated.
- The PMC browser page returned a browser-check page; the publisher full text and separate Europe PMC API were accessible. No browser check was bypassed.
- Participant records are not publicly available according to the article. Only published aggregates were checked; no corresponding-author request, participant contact, new field collection, statistical-test replication or contemporary programme-status verification was performed.

## Loopstra and Tarasuk, 2013

Rachel Loopstra and Valerie Tarasuk. *Perspectives on Community Gardens, Community Kitchens and the Good Food Box Program in a Community-based Sample of Low-income Families*. Canadian Journal of Public Health 104, e55–e59. [Publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03405655), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23618120/).

- [Original bibliographic/abstract XML response](loopstra-2013-record.xml), retrieved from Europe PMC; [download metadata and SHA-256](loopstra-2013-provenance.json).
- Reading scope is the original article's abstract, cross-checked at the publisher and PubMed, **not full text**. The publisher exposes subscription-only main text. The public full-text API returned HTTP 500; no full-text copy was saved or inferred from that response.
- The 485-family baseline, 371-family follow-up and 16 kitchen users are abstract-level reported counts. Selection details beyond the abstract, response coding, food-insecurity subgroup results and any kitchen-specific causal effect remain unverified here.
- Copyright remains with the original rightsholder. The archive stores the public bibliographic response for provenance; the study paraphrases only the limited findings it actually read.
