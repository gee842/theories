### Gloss (assistant)

- **Transition.** Survival is the minimum requirement of every actor's game. Because the theory can predict the expected outcome of the present trajectory, that prediction is the bridge from race to cooperation.
- **Against "make falling behind survivable".** If the winner's logic is cancerous, falling behind cannot be made survivable. Everyone should therefore first oppose the logic itself.
- **If cooperation fails, in order:**
  1. Do not embed AI, or the use of AI, in that logic, and do not let AI become it. AI must not exist as struggle or serve as its instrument. Otherwise the game converges and dominance passes to AI.
  2. If even that fails, have the humility not to build ASI or RSI. Technical conditions are met, but social and conscious conditions are not. Pause, so that cooperation and rational thought become possible, then build more complex AI. Or slow development below the speed at which cognition, and the response that cognition makes possible, can follow.
- **Everything else** is a dead end for humanity.

### Gloss (assistant)

- **Correction cannot keep up.** Human intelligence is bounded and does not grow exponentially, so cognition and actual understanding of the world grow linearly or more slowly. That correction falls behind rests on the fact that response, observation and feedback mechanisms cannot grow exponentially. This holds for every institution in society. It is a mismatch between the growth of real complexity and the growth of cognition, and society faces it already.
- **The winner is cancer-like.** Two replies:
  - the meta-attractor theory holds that an agent's cognitive model is selected;
  - environment and positive feedback construct cognitive modelling, and present society is one vast instrumental convergence and reward hacking.
- **Status of the limits.** Under real conditions, these limits can be proved further, or at least have answers rational judgment can give.
- **Where the dynamics come from.** These are not problems of the theory. The arms race itself creates the dynamics that drive these limits to one, and the present trajectory is an arms race.
