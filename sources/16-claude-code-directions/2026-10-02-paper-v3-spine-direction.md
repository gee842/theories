## Gloss (assistant)

Ren widens the paper's target. It is no longer a result about control over evaluation. It is the theory of why the present trajectory fails, with three requirements:
- the argument must be hard for a careful academic reader to refute, so every claim is derived under stated conditions;
- it must state the invariant constraints that a subsystem (the field of AI development) has to respect as part of a larger system on whose conditions it depends;
- it must show, by game theory and the present dynamics, that the trajectory of winning undermines the conditions of everyone who plays it, the winners included.

"Demonstrate" means shown by derivation, not by experiment. That agrees with his earlier ruling against experiments for this version. He authorised subagents where they help. Message 3 names the conclusion the paper must reach: cooperation is required, as a shared future of humankind. "My new conversation" was read as the 2026-10-02 conversation with Alex, which he wants to say this clearly as well.
