# Foundation — Principles (concise)

Claims sorted by kind: what we observe, the trap, the one value we choose (marked), what it requires, how we reason, what is unsolved, and what would defeat it. The empirical lines fail if the dynamics are wrong. Full grounding in `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES.md`.

## Observations

1. Things persist by exchange across a boundary, so treating an agent as separate mispredicts wherever the couplings dominate. Interdependence is a lens for accuracy, not a source of values — a parasite is coupled to its host too.
2. A system absorbs only the shocks it has the variety to answer, and only where its parts fail independently — variety enough that no single shock is fatal, not the most possible. Variety is also the raw material of new forms.
3. Every useful model is coarser than its system; the variables it drops bite when conditions move. This one included.
4. At the extreme many quantities reverse; runaway feedback instead accelerates to a limit or a lock-in. Which one holds must be checked.
5. Dependency is universal: every node begins unable to maintain itself, and every node ends — renewal runs through the ending. A persisting state is not a persisting process.
6. Societies that last converge on the same functions — sufficiency, reciprocity, trust, the forms that hold relationships — and non-rivalrous, polycentric coordination is observed to last at scale. A single competitive axis hides both.

## The trap

7. Where winning buys more power to win, leads compound and the axis selects the fastest climber — no intent needed; the dynamic is in the structure, and runs in profit and in power between states alike. It runs away only where returns increase; the danger is a compounding axis whose balancing comes too slowly or never forms.
8. In a coordination problem belief is causal and a credible alternative moves the system; where defection is the best response, only changed payoffs or credible reciprocity do — repetition can supply that. Confusing the two is the recurring error.
9. Compounding on one axis thins the system toward one — worth collapsed to a single measure, the unpriceable shed, the substrate drawn down — until correlated parts fail together and one shock takes all. (Irreversibility is not the danger; correlation is — decorrelated death is how the whole renews.)
10. Two old limits are gone: harm now outruns its correction loop and locks in before it is undone, and one global system leaves few independent parts to fail apart.
11. AI is the hinge: it sharpens the strongest strategies past human correction speed, so aligning each system to its operator while the game between operators stays unaligned reaches the bad equilibrium sooner — per-agent alignment does not reach inter-agent dynamics.

## The one thing we choose

12. Description settles no aim; we add one premise — that it continue, that the generating of new forms go on. *(The one underivable line: a single value-choice, over a process the observations fix.)* Continue means the process, not a state — it ends by freezing (the generating stops, brittle) or by burning out (the substrate outrun); neither is its going on.

## What it requires

*(Each follows from the premise and the dynamics above.)*

13. Keep variety and decorrelation high enough that no single shock is fatal, and the substrate renewed faster than it is drawn down.
14. Keep correction loops intact and faster than the harms — exit, voice, the power to undo; lost correction, more than change, lets a harm complete.
15. Maintain the parts that cannot yet maintain themselves — the seedbed of new forms — as a default held by the test, not the propping-up of spent parts, which still end.
16. Keep many measures of worth; no single scoreboard, price included, holds all the variety adaptive capacity draws on.
17. Channel the drives rather than suppress them — suppressed they re-route, unshaped they rebuild the race inside.
18. Change the game, do not win it (winning it and ending it both lock in): alter payoffs until the axis stops compounding. Exit plus free association turns domination into worth — but only while what is joined has non-increasing returns, else a dominant destination re-forms, held off only by the constraint below; it needs affordable exit (the abundance question) and destinations that do not re-concentrate.
19. Hold sensing in common as far as function needs (harm must be sensed faster than it spreads); one logic for all parts — a made mind that comes to bear variety and to sense is a participant, not an instrument.
20. Hold one constraint near-absolute: keep the system from closing onto a single locked-in, monocultural, or exhausted state — not a second axiom but the operational form of the one value, the rule that keeps every other line revisable (none crowned). A part may freely close only itself; the constraint fires only where that would close the world for others.

## How we reason

21. Reason from how things behave; treat any structure — central or local, strong or soft — as a means judged by what it does, since siding with a pole is itself the thinning reflex.
22. The test, contestable against a declared scale: does an act raise or lower variety and decorrelation, draw the substrate past renewal, keep or disable correction — judged at the scale the dynamics run, the whole web across generations? Proxies for continuation, not its definition.
23. Build for resilience, not the prevention of all failure (which assumes a complete model and is brittle); against a determined defector the only admissible defense is bounded, accountable, and able to stand down.
24. Act on the landscape before the crisis: vision moves a system only where a better basin is already adjacent, and where defection still pays, only changed payoffs reshape it.

## What we have not solved

25. The determined adversary who prefers to win: belief-change is no defense, yet the power to stop a superhuman defector is itself the concentration we constrain — with no model that guarantees it stands down.
26. The transition: whoever leaves alone is outcompeted; conditional commitment, thresholds, and shared vision are real but individually insufficient, and where the cascade first catches is unknown.
27. The abundance layer and its ownership: real exit needs cheap necessities, but a self-reproducing means of supply is either a commons or a collar — keeping it common is the central open problem.

## What would defeat this

28. The empirical spine fails if more independent variety is not more robust to novel shocks; if increasing-returns axes reliably self-balance before lock-in; if individually aligned systems compose without changing the game; or if lives go durably well with no substrate of care and unpriced worth. The derivation fails if any requirement does not follow from continuation. A model held to be tested.
