# Speculative Future

A project to make better human futures *thinkable* again — and, by making them thinkable, to help make them possible.

## The premise

Our technological possibility space is expanding exponentially, yet our political and
moral imagination has contracted. We can analyze our traps with great precision — we name
them Moloch, the metacrisis, capitalist realism — but naming them has not freed us.
Coordination beyond competition has become almost unthinkable, and the unthinkability is
itself part of the trap.

This project starts from a wager: that the current order is a *totalizing attractor* held
in place partly by our belief in its inevitability — and that escaping it is therefore both
an **objective** problem (mechanisms, institutions, technology) and a **perceptual** one
(how we see, what we can imagine and expect of one another). The two are coupled. Changing
what is thinkable is not commentary on the dynamics; it is an intervention in them.

We build in the era of AI deliberately: the first technology that is at once the maximal
accelerant of our traps and a candidate solvent for them.

The working core is sharper still: **stop using AI to compete with other people.** If
intelligence is poured into rivalrous games, even perfectly obedient systems can intensify a
destructive civilizational dynamic. The project therefore treats AI alignment not only as an
inner technical problem, but as an outer social one: what game is aligned intelligence serving?

## Method

**Think first.** The eventual artifact is a presentation — most likely a website — but the
substance has to be earned before it's designed. This repo begins as a *thinking corpus*:
numbered documents in `thinking/` that develop the diagnosis, the frame, the veins of
existing thought, and the seeds of a better future. The website is the last 10%, not the first.
