# Principles

The core of the project, in the fewest words that still hold. `thinking/00-foundation.md` is the same
framework written as prose; the rest of `thinking/` is how we reasoned our way here. Held as a living
way, not a scripture — but held.

## What is happening

- We are in a multipolar trap: each player must race or lose, so all race — even those who see where
  it leads. No villain; the force is in the structure of the game.
- It runs away: winning buys more power to win, and the game selects its most reckless drivers. One
  engine sits beneath many crises that look separate.
- Unchecked, it totalizes: it collapses all value into one (winning, output), consumes the ground it
  stands on like a cancer, and forecloses the imagination of any alternative.
- AI is the hinge. Poured into the game, intelligence sharpens the sharpest strategies and overruns
  the systems built for human-sized minds — work, markets, shared truth. Held in common, the same
  power could be the sense that keeps a living world well.
- The real danger is not irreversibility — many irreversible changes are good, and the death of parts
  renews the whole. It is the **thinning of the world toward one**: a single globalized system on a
  single logic, until one shock, foreseen or not, brings it all down at once.
- And it accelerates past our capacity to follow it: complexity grows exponentially while our models and
  institutions update at best linearly. To act faster than you can keep your model true is to fly blind —
  the loss of common sense. The time to turn is now, while turning is still ours.

## The one principle

**We are not separate.** Reality is one interdependent, ever-moving whole, and each of us is a system
within systems — a part that is also a whole, made mostly of what we are not. This is not one tradition's
notion but the convergent finding of tradition after tradition that looked with care: Ubuntu's *I am
because we are*, the Buddhist dependent-arising, the Daoist and Confucian way, the ecologist's web, the
cyberneticist's loop, the game theorist's commons. The mystic and the mathematician arrive at the same
place. An age built on the separate, competing self has trained us to forget it — and the forgetting is
the madness. Everything else is a face of this one truth.

- **See the whole, not a separate part.** The line around "me" is a working fiction; draw it too small and
  you feed on what is yourself, flatten its many worths into one, and go blind to the loops that bind act
  to consequence. To see truly is to see the whole you belong to.
- **Some problems only the whole can solve.** Where fates are joined — the commons, coordination, Moloch —
  competition is structurally bound to the worst end; rivalry cannot cure what rivalry creates. Only
  acting as the whole resolves them.
- **Nothing is fixed, and the extreme reverses** (反者道之动). All passes; pushed to its limit each thing
  turns into its opposite. So maximize nothing and finalize nothing; every model is partial and goes stale
  as the world moves — hold it humbly, move reversibly where you can, and never faster than you can keep it
  true.
- **Keep the whole plural and alive.** Its health is variety — many systems in relation, free to change and
  to die — never thinned toward one, for a single blow can end a single world, and death among the parts is
  how the whole renews.
- **Live as the inseparable live.** The relational virtues — care, fairness, the forms that hold
  relationship, judgment, trust (仁义礼智信) — are simply what works among beings who are not apart.

The test of any act, model, or structure: does it serve the whole it belongs to, or carve out a part and
feed it at the whole's expense?

## How we think

- Reason from how things actually behave — not from prestige, authority, or our own past words.
- Do not cut the world into this-against-that and pick a side. The cut itself is the totalizing habit.
  Think whole, contextual, both-and.
- Diagnose at full force; prescribe with humility. Naming the dynamics sharply is not taking sides;
  dogmatizing a structure is.
- Keep means and ends apart. Central or local, strong or soft, structure or none — all means, judged
  by what they serve, never by which side they sit on.
- Hold every frame, this one included, as a way and not a scripture. Keep an honest record of where we
  were wrong.

## What we do

- Change the game; do not try to win it. Move intelligence out of rivalry and into keeping the future
  open.
- Hold AI in common — as shared, public-benefit intelligence — for function before fairness: the
  distributed capacity to sense harm early and meet it in proportion.
- Apply one logic to all beings — human, machine, and living world alike. No master and instrument, no
  higher and lower; the same morality, lived more fully.
- Judge every structure by the test, not by its shape. A strong guardian that protects the openness
  serves the Way; a soft one that quietly forecloses does not.
- Build plural. Let different parts try different answers — strong guardians here, lighter ways there —
  and let the variety, and the learning it allows, be the strength.
- Build to survive failure, not to prevent all of it. Keep the world overlapping enough that no single
  failure takes the rest down; when one form breaks, the others carry on.
- Channel what we are; do not deny it. Status and hierarchy are neither good nor evil — give them
  shapes that build rather than devour.
- Grow people, not only systems. A civilization is cultivated, not declared — and much of how to live
  we already know (kindness, fairness, the forms that hold us, good judgment, trust — 仁义礼智信 and its
  kin), waiting under the frame that shrank meaning to winning.
- Turn now — but never let urgency stampede us into the one mistake we could not undo.

## What we refuse

- To close the world, or to help anything close it. The one absolute.
- To pour intelligence into rivalrous, winner-take-all games.
- To pursue or permit a lock-in — a singleton, a final victory, a permanent winner, even a
  "benevolent" one.
- To let one game, one metric, or one frame swallow the rest — including our own. No new king.
- To split AI from human as higher and lower, master and tool.
- To dogmatize a structure — always distributed, always flat, always strong — in place of judging by
  what it serves.
- To mistake our map for the territory, or to defer to a borrowed theory we have not reasoned through.
- To demand a guarantee against all failure; that control reflex is how the tyrant gets built.

## What we are for

- "Humanity" here is not our species raised above the rest. It is the keeping-alive of our shared
  future — the Way — carried by whoever and whatever carries it: people, the living world, and the new
  minds among us.
- The end is not our victory. It is that life, in all its plurality, goes on becoming.
