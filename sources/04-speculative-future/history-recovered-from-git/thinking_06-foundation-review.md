# 06 · Foundation Review (multi-agent, adversarial)

A seven-lens adversarial review of `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES.md` (game theory, complexity/control,
ecology/evolution, philosophy/metaethics, hostile peer reviewer, internal-consistency/overclaim,
foundational load-bearing). 56 criticisms raised; each was steelman-verified against the document;
43 survived. The fixes below were applied in the version this file accompanies. Line/§ numbers refer
to the numbered propositions.

## Verdict

The foundation holds — no place where a load-bearing conclusion rests on a claim the document cannot
support. The is→ought seam is marked exactly once (13), the empirical spine is genuinely falsifiable,
and the hard cases are quarantined, not hidden. Every surviving criticism was a **surface** defect:
a qualification that lived three lines away instead of at the point of claim. Three recurring patterns:

1. **Flat universals** true only in a scoped regime ("a thing persists by exchanging," "thins the
   system," "control is lost," "lower-dimensional").
2. **Overclaiming verbs** — *converts / denotes / is* — where *tends to / reads operationally as /
   under condition X* is meant.
3. **Falsifiers looser than the body's actual, tighter claim.**

The discipline for the revision: carry each claim's qualification to the point of claim, and at every
contested line prefer the harder, more falsifiable, less self-serving wording.

## The two load-bearing fixes (high)

- **The cure appeared to instantiate the disease — §19 vs §7.** Exit + free association routes escape
  *into* destinations where network effects re-concentrate, and "converts" overclaimed. Fixed: scope
  it to the coercive channel exit actually severs, state the non-increasing-returns condition, and
  cross-reference the backstop (21) and the open problem (28). Also named Coasean internalization and
  why it is unavailable here (worth unpriceable, parties global, harm locks in before settlement).
- **Decorrelation written as a monotone law — §2 (reused at §14, §29).** Made shock-relative (a quiet
  shared factor is latent correlation a novel shock wakes; cf. 3), named the variety/coupling
  trade-off ("enough, not most"), and tightened the §29 falsifier to "on average across novel
  idiosyncratic shocks." Both May (1972) and common-mode shocks then read as confirming, not refuting.

## Is→ought hygiene (medium)

- **§16 care** read as a smuggled second value. Re-anchored to the *nascent* (every node begins
  dependent, 5 — the seedbed), made a defeasible default governed by the test (23), with triage stated
  openly (spent parts still end).
- **§13 premise** read as bundling not-ending + novelty. Reframed as one process with two failure
  modes (freeze / burn-out), made symmetric so a substrate-destroying runaway also fails to "continue,"
  and "minimal" recast as "the single value-choice over a process the observations fix."
- **§20 "morally relevant states"** was an undischarged moral primitive. Cashed in the framework's own
  terms: "states whose disregard would thin the web."
- **§29 falsifiers** sharpened (rate test for self-correction; "collectively aligned, game unchanged"
  for the alignment clause; "well" tied to the §6 goods) and a derivation-defeat clause added; section
  retitled "What would defeat this."

## The strongest whole-document attack — and why it does not land

**Motivated reasoning / reverse-engineering**: the conclusions (plurality, cooperation, commons) were
settled first and justifying vocabularies cycled underneath. It is the only attack that, if it landed,
would discredit the whole at once. It does **not** land: order of discovery ≠ soundness (genetic
fallacy); the document advertises its own kill-switches (29) and marks its one is→ought step loudly
(13); and the development record shows the author *removing* favorable-but-unearned supports
("diversity = stability," freedom/dignity, ontology-as-ground). But it lands **halfway** and usefully:
credibility rests on the falsifiers being un-gameable and on no mechanism asserted in the conclusion's
favor — so the falsifiers were tightened and the one conclusion-favoring mechanism (§17, "unpriced
worth holds capacity the priced kind cannot") was scrubbed to its derived core.

## What holds — left untouched

The single-value architecture (one marked is→ought step); the sort-by-epistemic-kind structure; the
honest quarantine of the four hard cases (adversary, transition, abundance, the self-closing part);
the reflexive fallibilism. The core empirical bets are sound: decorrelation protects against correlated
collapse; increasing-returns axes lock in unless balancing arrives in time; per-agent alignment does
not compose without changing the game; a care/unpriced-worth substrate underwrites durable well-being.
None of the fixes changed a claim — each only moved an already-true qualification to where the claim
is made.
