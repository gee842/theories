# Agent Instructions

## Required Pre-Work

Before starting any work in this repository, read
`FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT.md` in full.

Do not treat that file as background flavor. It is the governing context for the
project. Every change, review, recommendation, or generated artifact should be
checked against it before work begins.

## Operating Frame

When working in this repo:

- Keep the shared future open; do not narrow the work toward a single locked-in
  frame, metric, winner, or final blueprint.
- Treat AI as part of a larger social, economic, technical, and ecological
  system, not as an isolated tool.
- Do not use AI to compete for advantage over other people. In this repo,
  competition means AI used for relative advantage where gains compound into
  power over others: coercive dependence, capture, manipulation, replacement,
  war, or foreclosure of exit.
- Preserve plurality, variety, decorrelation, local autonomy, redundancy, and
  separate failure domains.
- Draw system boundaries honestly. Do not hide harms, dependencies, or duties as
  externalities just because they fall outside a chosen accounting frame.
- Prefer shared agency, shared abundance, shared sensing, and shared
  coordination over private leverage.
- Keep abundance, intelligence, and governance anti-capture in practice, not
  merely in name.
- Hold the governance paradox in view: power strong enough to prevent capture
  can itself capture. Favor answerability, appeal, audit, exit, and stand-down
  mechanisms.
- Keep understanding faster than change. Do not recommend acceleration where the
  model, institution, or correction loop cannot keep up.

## Writing And Editing

- Preserve the draft status of `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT.md` unless the user
  explicitly asks to remove it.
- Keep language confident without pretending the document is a closed doctrine.
- Do not reintroduce the old `chosen premise` or `what would defeat this`
  framing unless the user explicitly requests an academic proof-sketch version.
- When proposing changes, prefer edits that make the foundation harder to
  misread without diluting its hard guardrails.
- If a change would weaken the core line `Stop using AI to compete with other
  people`, pause and explain the tradeoff before editing.

## Repository Shape

This repository is intentionally minimal. At the time these instructions were
written, the project consists of:

- `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES-DRAFT.md`
- `AGENTS.md`

Do not recreate removed `thinking/` files or old foundation variants unless the
user explicitly asks for them.
