[retrieval.json](metadata/retrieval.json) gives original byte counts, SHA-256 hashes, access methods, rights statements and recorded HTTP metadata. For the Carrillo and Schneider article HTML, the independent reviewer supplied exact previously fetched bodies. Their final redirect URL, headers and HTTP status were not recorded; the receipt preserves those omissions rather than inventing them. For newly fetched XML and dataset landing HTML, response headers, status, final URL and retrieval time are retained.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) binds every other package file. [availability.json](metadata/availability.json) distinguishes public documents from unavailable recordings and uninspected dataset schemas. [verification.json](metadata/verification.json) records byte/hash and local-link checks.

Wu's article is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Carrillo's article, correction and identified dataset are CC BY 4.0, with scope and site notices retained. No explicit open reuse license was observed for the Schneider author manuscript. An article license does not establish a license for raw data that have not been supplied.

No author was contacted. No raw electrophysiology, video, supplement archive or bulk dataset was downloaded; no model, data-analysis script or experimental action was run. HTML/XML may reference external assets; these are exact response bodies, not standalone rendered documents.

The [Wu identification audit](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SHARED-AFFECT-ACTION-EVIDENCE.md) separates its short source summary from methodological assessment. Companion papers have an independent review; none of the separate experiments is treated as one complete causal history.
