## Context (AI-authored)

Everything below is the assistant's, not Ren's.

**Where the name appears.** `IdealWarrant.lean` is a periphery module of the completion line (`mathematical-abstraction/completion/formal/`, namespace `CompletionWarrant`). The I4 classification table lists it as a join candidate for `Becoming.Horizon`. Ren's I4 decisions placed the general mathematical line outside the spine for now ([source](2026-10-16-i4-decisions.md)). So when I4b-3 landed (study §145, record `what-a-finite-history-refutes`), IdealWarrant's order form stayed outside, still a candidate. No work on it is under way.

**What the module states.** Take any partial order, and read its ideals (directed lower sets) as completed histories given by their finite stages. A property of ideals is refuted at a finite stage exactly when it is Scott-closed. That holds exactly when the property is "every stage lies in Q" for one downward-closed set of stages Q. That set is unique: it is the set of stages whose own principal ideal has the property.

**Why the name misleads.**
- "Warrant", together with the module's `Admits` and `Permitted`, frames a history as needing permission from an authority outside it, a judge apart from what unfolds.
- In this repository, "Ideal" also reads as a normative ideal.
- Nothing in the theorem grants or permits anything. It is the order form of `Becoming.Horizon`'s finite refutation, and Horizon already names that structure: `Refutable`, and `Adheres`, a run's every finite stage staying within.

**How the instruction is read.**
1. **When it applies.** Whenever a module outside the spine is joined or reworked, its new names describe the structure in the core's existing vocabulary. This covers three things:
   - the core law;
   - the periphery instance or correspondence module;
   - the record's title.

   A dualist or limited-scope name is not carried into the new ones. A correspondence module is named after the core law it instantiates, not `<Original>Correspondence` when the original's name is of this kind.
2. **What the rule does not touch.** The original file keeps its path, its namespace and its bytes. Receipts and record hashes read those bytes, and originals are preserved. The new instance module's docstring and the record name the original, so the connection stays traceable.
3. **A first proposal for IdealWarrant**, which is the assistant's and not adopted. It would be fixed at the join, which waits on the general mathematical line coming into scope.
   - Place it as a section of `Becoming.Horizon`: what finite stages refute, over any order.
   - Rename its terms:
     - `Admits` becomes `Adheres`, Horizon's word;
     - `Permitted` becomes the stages that leave the property open;
     - `Content` becomes the reached stages;
     - an ideal becomes a history given by its stages.
   - Name the instance module after the law, for example `HorizonOrderCorrespondence`.
   - Title the record "What finite stages refute, over any order", paired with `what-a-finite-history-refutes`.
4. **Other join candidates with names of this kind.** The rule applies to these when they are worked on; they are flagged here and not renamed now:
   - `Admission`, `FamilyAdmission` and `FiniteAdmission`, which use gatekeeping words;
   - `ControllerRevision` and `ControlledRefinement`, which split a controller from what it controls;
   - `RenewalAccounting` and the three `Valuation` modules, which use accounting words.
