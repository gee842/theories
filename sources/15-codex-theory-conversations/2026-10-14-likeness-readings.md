2. **Equality and registration.** Yes, and already a theorem in form: equality is likeness where the claims name the configurations (determinacy-up-to-likeness, law 1), so a law that needs equality is one that holds under the registration naming every configuration and fails under the given claims. 3. **Identity.** Yes, with three precisions: likeness is relative to what is realized; it is carried as a relation, not a quotient; and it is fixed by the whole continuation, so it can outrun finite tests (constructive on finite decided frames, exactly the principle of law 9 where each move has listed outcomes, and not at all where a move has infinitely many outcomes).
