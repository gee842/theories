## Operational reading — AI-authored

**Structure.** The theory is stated in two parts.

- **Part I** is the strongest claim that holds with no axiom. Ren names it the most important and elegant part. It holds of every unfolding as represented, and states the laws that follow under each disclosed premise, with those premises appearing only as hypotheses of conditional theorems. Its candidates are:
  - the premise-free laws;
  - the map of premises;
  - the exact laws of counting;
  - enclosure, rest and openness;
  - the extent a continuing formation must count;
  - the point where the dynamics stop deciding.
- **Part II** adds one axiom and states what it yields. Which axiom that is remains Ren's choice. The center of the [continuation-grounding proposal](2026-10-10-wanting-continuation-one-axiom.md) was the earlier candidate; Ren's later answers in the continuation session bear on whether the center belongs to Part I instead.

"Without axiom" keeps its strong sense. Part I uses neither ethical nor substantive axioms, and it does not shrink to "no ethical axiom". The representation (discrete steps, natural-number amounts, the shared index, decidability where counts are taken) stays disclosed. Whether actual unfoldings meet a premise remains an empirical question.

**Names.** Ren now delegates the choice of names, with criteria:

- the most precise word;
- in the zero-axiom part, formal words that describe the abstract dynamics;
- scientific and systems words;
- social and financial words not as the first choice.

Readings stay Ren's to choose.

The first application came before P35 was committed: the draft names `Budgeted`, `Budget`/`budgetQueue`, `countSince` and `change_within` became `ChangeBound`, `EnclosedQueue`/`enclosedQueue`, `occurrences` and `enclosure_bounds_change`. The other social or financial names in the core, and their move to a systems vocabulary, are left to a naming pass planned with the Part I/Part II restructure. Two examples are `Spending` and `tendedQueue`/`Tended`.

The older rule still applies: no formal part is named after Ren's own words.
