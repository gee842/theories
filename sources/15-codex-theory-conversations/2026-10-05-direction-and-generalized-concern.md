## The research development

The [exploration](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-direction-frontier/EXPLORATION.md) distinguishes monotonic, eventual, statistical, accessible, and normative meanings of direction. It separates representation of another's situation, private consequences of that situation, direct concern, effective action, and inheritance of the capacity to revise.

A three-action counterexample shows why stronger returned consequences need not yield protection when bypass is cheaper. A resource-accounted successor model derives an exact condition for responsive protection to spread and provides paid first formation from a supplied construction repertoire. It does not derive universal values, general invention, or a universal law of moral progress. A separate conceptual analysis proposes learning and generalization of valuation, including concern for unfamiliar recipients without retaliatory or productive leverage.

## Current status

The strongest new formal result concerns relative propagation of specified protective responses with actual resource costs and two-way trials. Formation recipes, environments, and trial allocation are premises. The broader hypothesis predicts increased costly protection of unfamiliar powerless recipients after independently measured improvements in cross-position experience and representation transfer, under fixed scope and controls. It remains untested.

The existing [canonical theory](../../theories/becoming/README.md) records this as the current research frontier. Universal direction remains open; the exploration replaces an undifferentiated question with separable mechanisms and possible failures. Three collaborating AI agents assisted the research. This is internal development, not external peer review or new empirical evidence.
