## AI interpretation and scope

The working name **responsive transformation / 因应转化** is proposed by the assistant, not quoted from Ren. It names the reorganization of understanding, purposes and action through changing relations and consequences. Its capacity can form, persist, change or deteriorate; the term does not define every response as good.

The instruction changes the governing question of this study: investigate boundary conditions and ethically warranted directions through changing cognition and circumstances, without presenting an unconditional guarantee as the completion requirement. Existing local conditional proofs and counterexamples remain valid at their declared scope. The AI-alignment term “corrigibility” is retained as a reader bridge, with no claim that every use of that term implies ontological dualism or that the two concepts are fully equivalent.
