This authorizes a contextual language revision. The shared account should explain how activities and differentiated positions form and change the conditions of further activity. Historical contribution, actual participation, effective choice and justified responsibility are related questions whose answers must not be supplied by one ambiguous label.

The correction preserves actual differentiated effects, legitimate choice and the need to justify normative conclusions. It does not establish universal ethics, remove the capacities described by an older term, or authorize changing quotations and source originals. Documentary authorship, attributed philosophical terminology and names identifying existing formal declarations retain their provenance and scope.
