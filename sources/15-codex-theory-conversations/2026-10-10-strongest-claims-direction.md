**The assistant's frame.** Two checked results bear on all of them.

**The assistant's answers.**
1. The feeding closure is exact for supply but misses protection, such as wolves holding back the deer that would strip a forest. The general object is the closure under every influence on what a formation requires. The system one is in is larger again: in `Clearing` the dweller is in the clearer's system but outside its dependence. So there are four levels: self, dependence, system and whole.
2. Route (b) is not enough for universal ethics; `Clearing` shows it. Without any axiom it shows the cancer pattern to be a mistake by the center's own lights, and it agrees with the axiom wherever a feeding closure is the whole. Keep both: route (b) as the zero-axiom result, and the axiom as the one addition whose content is stated exactly.
3. `bounded_whole` is the endpoint, not the process: the axiom is route (b) taken from the whole's standpoint. How parts come to represent the whole is not yet modelled. Since feedback reaches at most what can affect a formation, the whole realizes itself only through parts that seek truth beyond their own feedback and through communication across standpoints.
4. `Unrestricted` reads the axiom: significance without qualification is significance from every standpoint, a predicate, not a weight. "Anything" is kept.
5. "Not harm" is retaining, the phase-boundary reading Ren gave. The stepwise reading permits preemption.
6. Both members and lineages count, since "anything" covers every level. Continuation beyond one's cycle of life is visible only if lineages are individuated. Under the axiom the whole retains only if every region does (`Web.Retains.within`).
