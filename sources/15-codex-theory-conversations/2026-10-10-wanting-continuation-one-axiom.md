## Context — AI-authored

**First reply (9 October).** The assistant read the [characterization record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md) as saying that, on a connected whole, G0 is a seed plus two boundary neutralities, and that every rival tested is the same rule kept inside a formed boundary. It pushed back on three readings: life supplies the seed and also the boundaries; the laws that hold of every unfolding exclude nothing, so explanation lives in the premises; reasons why things happen and reasons for supporting them are two questions about one unfolding. It proposed a bridge theorem joining “continuing generation draws in from beyond” to the formed relations along which N_rel carries significance.

**Second reply (10 October), which Ren's “exactly” answers.**

1. It restated the proposal in five steps: wanting confers significance on one's own continuation; “the whole” a formation counts is perspective-dependent in extent and center; a closed self cannot continue; the meta-attractor builds a totalizing, rigid self that does not count the dynamics beyond what it sees; one axiom closes the account.
2. It matched steps 3–4 to checked results: under balance and dissipation a closed system comes to rest and a continuing finite family draws in from beyond itself (without dissipation a closed family can generate forever, `Recycle`); no finite horizon separates renewal from consumption, though taking no more than is renewed at each step is safe; under the supplied wear law consumption forms more at first, a one-step count prefers it, and it ends what shares its conditions, even when each part is within renewal and only the parts jointly exceed it; more without end needs replenishment without bound.
3. It named the new step: a formation that wants its continuation, following its own want, must count what its continuation depends on, look far enough ahead, and keep the joint draw within renewal. The extent of the self is then derived, not chosen.
4. It named the limits of that derivation: it runs one way (to what the self depends on), and it reaches others as conditions, not as beings that want; a careful totalizer satisfies all of it. So the dynamics fixes the extent of a system, not its center; the axiom's one job is to put a center at every point.
5. It set out two choices: weak or strong form and at which level (suggesting the weak form for each formation and the strong form for the whole, which is G0's own shape built from below), and what counts as harm, given that almost all life continues by ending other life.
6. It said the axiom closes the grounding, while non-ethical premises remain (physical premises, the step from wanting an end to reasons for what it requires, the representation) and practical judgment between conflicting continuations still needs commitments such as J1–J4.

## AI interpretation and operational scope

**Status.** This records Ren's proposal and the authorization to prepare its implementation. It is not a new result. No Lean has been added to the core by this record; existing results keep their scope. G0 remains the adopted axiom of record until the restatement is implemented, checked and reviewed, and Ren accepts it.

**What “exactly” settles.** Ren's reply affirms the assistant's restatement and its analysis: the dynamics can derive the extent of what a wanting formation must count, and the one axiom fixes the center. The assistant's suggestion of the weak form for each formation and the strong form for the whole is taken as the working structure for implementation; Ren may revise it.

**What remains Ren's choice.** Whether the axiom ranges over anything that wants its continuation or over anything at all; at which level harm is read (individual, lineage or the whole); the reading of “more” in the strong form; and the names of the new formal parts.

**“Does not require any axiom.”** This is read as: no ethical axiom. Physical premises (balance, dissipation, embodiment), correspondence premises (which relations count as formed), the step from wanting an end to having reason for what it requires, and the representation itself stay disclosed premises, as the [Becoming priority](2026-10-08-theory-of-becoming-priority.md) requires when it separates zero normative axioms from zero substantive premises.
