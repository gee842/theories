## How the AI reads it (AI-authored)

- **"把中华文化中所有在theory of becoming上有映射，或者对应的，找出来"**: every complete judgment in the 脉络 is read against the spine, not a sample. A correspondence is structural (the same relation, condition and direction), not a shared word; each is agreement, exact scope or divergence, the kinds of the RO map, and the divergences are kept.
- **"非同于英文系统的一套符号系统 … 在历史长河中一直在演变和迭代"**: a controlled vocabulary of classical characters and compounds, one name for each concept of the theory, each name attested in context (mostly before 秦), with its attested sense, what in the theory it fits, what it does not carry, and how its use changed in later layers (汉, 魏晋, the Buddhist translations, 宋明). By `CrossSituational.stands_iff` such a vocabulary is an agreement among the theory's users, constrained by the historical senses; it is not a recovery of what the ancient texts meant.
- **"它自然就是一元的，关系的，动态的"** is a thesis to test, not a premise ([Ren, 17 October](2026-10-17-ren-words-not-authority.md)). RO found it true at the root of many lines and in many passages, and also found four non-relational layers in the same traditions (a prior ground, a fixed nature or value, a will or single center, contest and rank). The vocabulary chooses senses that carry the relational structure and says, for each character, where its other senses carry one of those layers.
- **"现在不用改英文版本，我们只是做中华版本"**: the English chapter and its modern Chinese edition are not changed. The 中华 edition is a new document, in Chinese only, stating the theory in that vocabulary; its claims say exactly what the checked laws say, and its readings keep the AI label.
- **"把我做的这个脉络，也整合进我们的theories"**: the release's canonical model, its documents and the frozen texts its quotations rest on enter `sources/` byte for byte, bound by SHA-256, and a chapter places the 脉络 within the theory with every correspondence found.
- **Not read into it.** That classical Chinese is in itself a better language for the theory than English, or that the ancient texts meant the theory's laws: a symbol system fits where its names keep the theory's distinctions and relations, which is checked name by name.
