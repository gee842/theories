## Context (AI-authored)

The assistant's reply to the first message set out what is proved.

- **Decidability.** The core's logic is constructive: it does not assume that every statement is true or false. Decidability adds that a particular question, such as whether a relatum is active at a step, is settled yes or no. The representation assumed it wherever something is counted. The laws of change do not need it: laws 1, 3 and 4 decide no predicate of the world, their proofs comparing only numbers (P48), and R6 proves revisiting and the stopping of a marked change with no decidability (`revisits_within`, `Marks.change_stops`). Only counting and naming a last step need it, and provably so: without decidability, the finite-world law is equivalent to excluded middle (`World.law_iff_em`), and an explicit step after which nothing arises gives the limited principle of omniscience (`World.lpo_of_finite_ends`, `Web.lpo_of_ending`, `Listed.lpo_of_few`). The weaker forms, “not at arbitrarily late steps” and “not unboundedly many ever holding”, need none. Three options were given: keep it as a commitment; drop it, losing exact counts and named end steps; or make it local, with no global commitment and each counting law stating “decided at these steps and relata” as its own condition.
- **Re-foundings.** Counting an imbalance as a relatum would make balance hold by construction, as R4's counted refinement did for the unit (`World.rests_refinement_counted`). The general construction was not yet proved; the condition would move to what the new relata do rather than vanish, and it could hide unexplained change. Reading embodiment as a definition would merge the two roles of a relatum and make proved witnesses unrepresentable (`World.seedbank_world`, `Drowsy`, `World.sourcedQueue_holds`), while embodiment is already localizable.

The assistant suggested: decidability, the local option; imbalance as a relatum, proved first as a representation equivalent to the current one before any adoption; embodiment as a definition, not adopted.

The assistant's readings of “please”, which Ren may correct:

- **Decidability is made local.** It is no longer a commitment of the representation. Each law that counts or names a step states decidedness at the steps and relata it counts as a condition, as balance and the other premises now are, and every law that does not need it is stated without it. Reading Ren's “too dualist” as the yes/no split this imposes on becoming is the AI's.
- **The imbalance relatum is proved before it is adopted.** The construction is to be stated and checked as a representation equivalent to the current one; whether to adopt it stays with Ren.
- **Embodiment stays a premise, made local** at the relata that arise or cease, as the zero-premise inventory found; it is not read as a definition.
