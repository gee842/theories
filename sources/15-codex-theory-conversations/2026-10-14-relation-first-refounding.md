## Context (AI-authored)

Everything below is the assistant's, not Ren's.

**The correction conceded.** The second answer had used `World.independent` (each of the eight premises fails in some world meeting the other seven) as evidence that the substantive laws are not necessary. The third answer withdrew that: `World` is eight separately chosen fields (who takes part, take, give, level, active, requires, need, draws) over natural-number steps and amounts, so a premise tying two fields together can be switched off by choosing the fields differently. The independence measures the freedom of the representation, not the contingency of becoming; the repository's method already says that freely toggled predicates in a weaker model do not refute the full account. Ren's non-Euclidean point was taken as the right model: the zero-premise redirect of 11 October made each premise a local condition inside its law, as Riemann made the parallel postulate a curvature, and a necessary law does not need necessary conditions. The analogues of causality named were the order of joined exchanges (`Footprinted.sorts_iff`) and the confinement of a difference to what it reaches (`Flow.Balances.comparison_within_difference`).

**The proposal Ren answered.** The answer distinguished glue (balance, only active relata giving or taking, activity needing support: premises tying two readings of one exchange) from local states (closure, loss, dormancy, finiteness, bounded transmission: real differences between parts), and proposed a re-founding that starts from relation, in three steps landed through the work board:

1. state the laws without the speculative conditions on the base layer: a finite part that gains nothing returns, only what gains is new, a finite whole forgets;
2. adopt the rest of the whole as a relatum (the re-founding proved equivalent in `Becoming.Imbalance`, adoption Ren's since 11 October), read activity and dependence off the exchange, and re-prove the glue premises as definitions or theorems, so that what remains of the eight premises is the local states;
3. one theorem shape for unity, with contextual readings as the general case and `World` as the case where readings glue; and, by the same standard of invariance, the one axiom as a dichotomy.

It said that steps 2 and 3 re-found the representation, which the zero-premise rules leave to Ren. "please, finish all" is read here as Ren's approval of all three steps, including the adoption of the rest as a relatum.
