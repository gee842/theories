## Context (AI-authored)

Everything below is the assistant's, not Ren's.

**The question.**
- The spine counts amounts in whole units, so its dissipation ("a formation gives a closed family less than it takes") loses at least one unit at every exchange. The rest theorem, "continued arising needs openness", rests on that.
- R1 (`Becoming.Resolution`) showed that if the unit may split at each step, the spine's other premises no longer bring rest (witnesses `Asymptote`, `Emission`, `Circulation`, `Alternation`).
- So something must replace the whole unit, or rest stays tied to the representation.

**The options offered.**
- **A.** Keep the whole unit: every exchange loses at least one least amount.
- **B.** Each transfer gives back at most `q/(q+1)` of what it takes.
- **C.** Each enclosed family gets back at most `q/(q+1)` of what is taken from it (the premise of R2), giving the amount law (R3).
- **D.** A transfer of at least `1/p` of the unit loses at least `1/m` of it, which gives only the exchange law (`Trickle`).
- **E.** An enclosed family exchanges a bounded total.
- **F.** No new premise: only the loss law holds.

The assistant recommended C. The fifteenth independent review then found that a least loss on the family's totals at each size (between D and C) already gives the growth law.

**The assistant's reading, pending Ren** (the reading of "the case of 0" was confirmed by the fifth message, below).
- "A is right" is read as: the least difference per exchange belongs to becoming, not to the representation. Every passing is a change, and its undoing is another change, so no exchange makes no difference.
- "The case of 0" is read as the zero-premise case: rest at every resolution with no premise added. Ren uses "zero axiom" (零公理) for the strongest claim without a supplied premise. The alternative reading, a lossless exchange, would contradict the first paragraph.
- The elegant way proposed: count the refinement itself.
  - Balance at a refining unit is the spine's balance with one more formation, the refinement, that gives each condition the finer units the splitting adds, and takes nothing.
  - Counted so, the refinement gives without taking, which the spine's dissipation excludes.
  - So where it gives nothing, enclosed change comes to rest with no premise beyond the spine's. The R1 witnesses are fed by the refinement rather than counterexamples to rest.
  - The commitment is Ren's ontology (an amount is a difference made, so a finer distinction is itself a becoming), not a proved theorem.
- "The rest" (options B to F, the amount law and the review's premises) are kept as research notes, separate from this line.
