## Conversational context — AI-authored

The assistant interpreted the proposal as an encompassing Theory of Becoming: relations form, persist, transform and dissolve within one unfolding, including both generation and degeneration. Life, evolution and evaluative capacities are to be explained through their particular formed relationships. G0 supplies an affirmative ethical orientation within this same reality; explaining becoming need not begin by assuming that becoming is good.

This instruction makes the Theory of Becoming the current first research priority for the continuing foundation programme. Preserve the idea in repository context, the directive register and the scoped goals, and display it first among ongoing research directions. **当前首要研究方向：生成／变化之论（Theory of Becoming）。** 从同一关系展开说明形成、维持、转化、退化与消失，以及这些过程怎样改变后续活动的条件。生命、演化、认识与评价在其中分化；不先把整体限定成伦理上值得肯定的生成，也不先假定整体必然改善。

Preserve these distinctions in subsequent work:

1. **One unfolding, inclusive explanatory scope.** Generation and degeneration concern actual relational changes, not two independent substances or necessarily disjoint classes. The same transformation can enable some continuations and destroy others. Retain the real losses, mixed outcomes and ending of particular continuations.
2. **Becoming, life and evolution.** The broad account includes formation before inheritance exists. Evolutionary explanations must retain their specific transmission, reconstruction, variation and selection relationships; merely calling every change evolution does not supply those explanations. This organization of scopes is the assistant's working interpretation, not a claim that Ren supplied three separate ontologies or settled their names.
3. **Ethical orientation within the whole.** G0 and the additional practical commitments state how living and further generation receive positive normative significance and support judgment. They need not be assumed in every explanation of dynamics. Ethical evaluation remains within the same unfolding, can concern mixed consequences, and is not reduced to selecting a preclassified good subset. The formation of an evaluation and its warranted authority retain distinct explanatory tasks.
4. **The strong zero-axiom hypothesis remains a priority.** Investigate whether becoming can be grounded without added substantive axioms. Do not silently replace this target with the narrower absence of ethical axioms, an empty Lean axiom report, or premises hidden in definitions. Disclose logical machinery, methodological commitments, model hypotheses, correspondence assumptions and normative premises separately. Their role in a local representation does not by itself establish their ultimate necessity or externality to reality. Neither success nor impossibility is asserted by this directive.
5. **Reuse and discriminate.** Reuse the completed conditional foundation and evolutionary mechanisms at their actual scope. The next foundational connection is from those explicit relational mechanisms to an account of which structures becoming itself requires, which regularities form within it, and which premises remain unexplained. Inclusion of all outcome types must lead to an explanation of actual possibilities and constraints, not an unconstrained catalogue. A genuine case missing a claimed necessary relationship would require revision; a representation omitting that relationship is not automatically such a case.

This entry records a priority and research hypothesis, not a new scientific result or proof. Existing conditional results, adverse cases and stronger independent goals remain intact. New mathematical claims retain the repository's actual Lean-check requirement.
