## Assistant interpretation of the preceding exchange

The assistant proposed distinguishing a capacity or orientation toward good, discernment developed through an encounter, and good actually realized in responding. These are differentiated aspects of one unfolding rather than independent inner and outer substances. A situation may reveal an existing significance, cultivate a previously undeveloped capacity, and participate in creating a new relationship or good. No claim that every person already possesses complete or infallible goodness follows.

The preceding reply compared Wang Yangming's “道必體而後見” and his discussion of 良知 and 見聞, Zhang Zai's 天人合一 and differentiated 感應, and Cook Ding's slowing and vigilance at difficulty. It distinguished attunement from a subjective feeling of fluency. These comparisons are the assistant's research and interpretation, not classical citations supplied by Ren, and do not identify the three thinkers' positions with one another.

The assistant also distinguished the conditions for recognizing or realizing a good from the time at which it first matters: an unrecognized harm need not wait for discovery to become adverse. Participation can disclose existing significance and form new goods without making every endorsement authoritative.
