> 这是一个很好的方向，同时，研究一下汉字的单字，是不是和我们理论树的概念有很多有一一映射？有的可能也是双字，或者某一种组合，例如武就是止戈的组合

## Context and scope

This follows the [request to extend research into pre-Qin and literary Chinese](2026-10-06-pre-qin-and-wenyan-scope.md). Ren proposes studying correspondences between the theory's concepts and individual characters, multi-character terms or combinations, giving 武 and 止戈 as an example. The question does not require all characters to have one meaning, nor does it establish a historical or mathematical bijection in advance.

The resulting [study](../../theories/becoming/chapters/HANZI-AND-THEORY-TREE.md) distinguishes graphic composition, historical senses, philosophical reinterpretation and a deliberately defined theoretical vocabulary. The earlier subset proposal and earlier recordings of the 武 example remain at their original repository locations.
