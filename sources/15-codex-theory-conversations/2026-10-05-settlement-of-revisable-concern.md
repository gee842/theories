# Seeking a full settlement of revisable concern

5 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. This records Ren's direction and the resulting AI-assisted research. The quoted requests are his words; the mechanisms, arguments, proofs, and review conclusions are proposed elaborations.

## Requests

> for what does not settle, can they be settled?

> please spawn a team of agent to archive full settlement

The second request was interpreted in the context of the first as authorization to assemble a team to pursue and preserve the strongest justified settlement. It was not treated as permission to label unresolved questions complete. The existing research repository remains the primary home.

## Work and result

Three research agents developed formal revision, ethical scope, and evidence limits. Two further agents independently reviewed the construction and the normative/evidence branches. The coordinating agent integrated the contributions and repaired the empirical protocol in response to review. All are AI collaborators; this is internal review.

The [integrated chapter](../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md) joins the [joint-formation result](../../theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/JOINT-FORMATION.md) with [revisable concern](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/THEORY.md). A new conditional game selects and funds audit, preserved grounds, admission and retraction, effective strategic protection, and successor reconstruction. Independent review found a missing gate-price assumption, which was repaired while retaining the counterexample. Two harms to unadmitted patients remain explicitly counted.

The ethical branch defends positive reasons from subjects' losses and agency without deriving them from survival. It proves finite robust-comparison and symmetric-allocation results, while preserving incomparability and the distinction between ethical justification and an actor's motivation. Finite feature construction is supplied as a separate bounded refinement, with authored labels and paid operations rather than unrestricted concept invention.

An existing AI experiment was independently rescored with 160 source hashes checked and no new observations. A conventional rule learner solves its fixtures given the complete verified report. The future human protocol remains unrun and unregistered; review strengthened behavioral selectivity, confidence coverage, and the scope of the pooled claim. No new human or animal study was performed.

The [study record](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/README.md), [baseline](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/BASELINE.json), and [review record](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/review/INTEGRATION.md) preserve premises, exact checks, rejected formulations, and remaining obligations. Empirical universality, subjective experience, comparative optimality, and unrestricted invention of ethical meanings remain unresolved. The result is a stronger coherent candidate with scoped settlements, not a declaration that every original aspiration has been achieved.
