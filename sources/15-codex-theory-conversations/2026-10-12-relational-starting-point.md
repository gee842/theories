## Context (AI-authored)

Everything below is the assistant's, not Ren's.

**The answer given: partly.** L0 and L1 posit no subject and object, no actor, patient, observer, owner or value type; [DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT §10](../../theories/becoming/chapters/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) already records that. Their form nevertheless takes four splits as given rather than derived, which the [nondual method](../../theories/becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md#make-analytical-separations-accountable) asks to be accounted for:

1. **Configuration and move.** `step : S → A → S → Prop` reads change as a configuration under an action becoming another, with the actions drawn from an alphabet supplied from outside. Where the core does not need the label it sets `A := Unit` (`Recurrence`'s `process next`, `Web.paths`, `Roles`' `bearing`). Where it uses it, in `Joint` and `Experiment`, it reads it as moves ("varying one's own act"), and nothing in the configuration says how the mover or its choice forms: an input from outside against a responding world.
2. **Law and history.** `Process` declares what is admissible and `Run` what happens. The law is supplied before the history, fixed for all time, and never forms, holds or changes.
3. **Configuration and claim.** `Frame.realized : S → C → Prop` is a vocabulary of claims read off each configuration, formed by no relation in the unfolding. `Becoming.Testing` (识) later makes registration a relation within the dynamics; L1 does not.
4. **Carrier and relation.** The configurations `S` exist before `step`: relata before their relations.

The shared clock `Nat` is a fifth, already disclosed as a commitment of the representation.

**The observation.** Most of Part I does not use L0. `World` (`Becoming.Space`) has one type of relatum with its flow and web at each step, and no `S`, no `A`, no admissibility relation; formation and condition are roles of one relatum (`Roles`); identity is carried by the pattern across steps (`Identity`); laws are theorems holding of every world (the zero-premise line). So the core has two starting points, the inherited labelled transition system (L0–L2, `GenerativeCore`, whose bytes the receipts pin) and the relational `World`, joined by a reading (`Dependence.along`) rather than one primitive.

**The proposal Ren accepted.** Present `World` as the starting point and the process and frame as its readings; prove in the pure core that (1) the move is the relata's own activity and an outside label is dispensable with an exact loss, (2) a history fixes the law it shows, (3) configurations are alike exactly when they relate alike to every continuation, as an equivalence relation since `Quot.sound` is an axiom, and (4) claims are relations realized among relata. `GenerativeCore` stays byte for byte; the change is in the order of presentation and in bridging theorems. It is P69 on the work board.
