## AI implementation interpretation

Pursue the dialectical route from the monist relational ontology as an affirmative attempt at completion. Start with an evaluating position formed within one unfolding. Its own living and further generation are identified by formed, graded relations, not by a primitive owner. The aim is to derive G0, or as much of it as can be derived, from a commitment the evaluator already holds, namely its own self-good, which Ren [already includes in the whole](2026-10-07-self-good-within-the-whole.md). The derivation should reach beyond the self without routing through return to the self. Ren's 8 October formulation, ["从个体开始，把自己的future向所有的relationship做积分"](2026-10-08-positive-generative-direction.md), is the direct antecedent.

"尝试完成" asks for a completion attempt that reports where the route succeeds and where it hits a wall. It does not authorize presenting the route as complete beyond its premises.
