## Operational reading — AI-authored

This continues Ren's earlier instructions in the same session. Those instructions asked the session to update the plan first and then complete it step by step, keeping the code and the overall structure elegant ([one-theory directive](2026-10-09-one-theory-unification.md)). They also asked it to keep the core pure ([pure core](2026-10-09-pure-core.md)) and to "pursue the theory of becoming, and prove the necessary proofs".

The session reads "a complete theory of becoming" against Ren's [priority note](2026-10-08-theory-of-becoming-priority.md). That note proposed that, counting the whole space, generative and degenerative, the theory of becoming may need no axiom, and that adding axioms defines a part of the whole space. The note also named the theory's scope: formation, persistence, transformation, degeneration and dissolution within one relational unfolding, and how these change the conditions of later activity. Life, evolution and evaluation differentiate within that unfolding.

"Complete" is read as a target with four parts. The plan's [section 9](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-UNIFICATION-PLAN.md#9-走向完整的生成之论--toward-a-complete-theory-of-becoming) states these as checkable phases.

1. Every mode in that scope is stated on the one formal spine.
2. The laws that hold of every unfolding are proved with no premise, and the premise of every other law is named.
3. Each such premise is shown to pick out a proper, non-empty part of the whole space, not an axiom of the whole.
4. Everything left open is classified: as Ren's choice, as philosophical warrant, as empirical correspondence, or as a further proof.

"Related work" is read as:
- integrating the independent reviews;
- the records, study, chapter, plan and catalogue that the new results touch;
- connecting results already checked elsewhere, such as the evolutionary mechanisms, to the spine.

This reading does not decide the following. They remain Ren's:
- the reading of 生 and 生生;
- whether embodied relata are 生;
- how succession is read;
- whether living on and descent count for N_rel;
- the level at which 生生不息 is read;
- names.

It does not claim that formal completeness of a representation establishes the deductive necessity of the whole for reality. The [foundation record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/theory-of-becoming-foundation.md) keeps that target open until its relational-constitution step is argued. Nor does it replace the strong zero-axiom hypothesis with the absence of Lean axioms: methodological commitments of the representation, such as discrete steps, natural-number amounts and decidability where counting needs it, are disclosed separately.
