# Market names and the port's continuation

7 October 2026 (Asia/Bangkok; 6 October 19:22 UTC). Source: Ren's message in the Claude project thread on the dependency map. The quotation is Ren's wording; the context and interpretation below are AI-authored.

> fuel, spent and income are clearly bad names, belongs to a paradigm that already distorted, I mean thinking in the market way
>
> please elegantly connect them

Ren wrote this after the assistant explained that the native packet port had not been proved to satisfy the conditional account law U1, and described the port's bookkeeping as fuel, spent and income. The message rejects that exchange vocabulary as a distorting frame. It also asks for the connection between the port and the conditional laws to be made.

The [native-port continuation module](../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/conditional/native-port/README.md) answers the request. It reads the same coordinates as aspects of one continuing activity: held (存), enacted (化) and arrived (来). It proves the port's conservation and its membership in U1. The inherited source field names are preserved unchanged, as provenance requires; only the new interface uses the new reading. The upstream U1 vocabulary (reserve, demand, credit) is also market-inflected and is left unchanged here; renaming it would be a separate, wider edit.

## Follow-up: rename them

Later the same day (6 October UTC), after the assistant offered to rename the remaining exchange-inflected Lean names, Ren replied:

> yes, rename all these "not elegant" names, be precise

The paragraph above records the first step, when the inherited names were kept. Following this reply, the Lean names themselves were changed: the U1 package now reads `SustainLaw` over `held`, `enacted` and `arrived`, and the native port's fields and operations are `held`/`enacted`/`arrived`/`dissipated`, `draw`, `support`, `enact` and `arrivalAmount`. The Python original keeps its names. The pre-rename sources, the full old → new list and a check that only names changed are preserved in the [rename record](../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/verification-history/2026-10-06-before-vocabulary-rename/README.md).
