## Who said what

Neither text tells the speakers apart. The transcript says so on purpose and does not assign turns. **This corpus therefore attributes nothing in these recordings to Ren individually.** Asked on 2026-10-03, Ren answered: "not remembering which line is mine, but my talk is generally aligned with my theories". The line-by-line attribution cannot be recovered. Where a passage uses vocabulary Ren has used for years, the theory documents say so and still leave it unattributed. Examples are *cosmic cancer*, *the game of winning*, "这个逻辑需要扩张它本身" and "当然不止这两个，我能跟你讲出来几十个". The friend is unnamed. One of the two recalls biopunk ideas from "他儿子", the son of a former boss (the name is uncertain: "前老板／钱老板").

## Identifications the texts leave open

These are the assistant's readings, made while filing. They are not checked against the audio.

- **"Cloud 5.5"** (story1 04:52), named as the tool to make the film with, is most likely *Claude 5.5*, the Claude model family current on that date.
- **"Sylos"** (生态文明 about 02:37) is most likely *Silo*, the Apple TV+ series after Hugh Howey's *Wool*. Its people live in an underground silo, but they are not an elite. The comparison in the talk is to mood and setting.
- **Rain for thousands of years** at very high water-vapour levels (生态文明 01:11). The speakers were unsure of the name; "全新世" (Holocene) is certainly wrong. They were probably thinking of the Carnian Pluvial Episode, about 234 to 232 million years ago in the Late Triassic. It is popularly described as rain lasting a million years or more, not thousands.
- **Greg Egan** (生态文明 03:45). Egan's "Luminous" (1995) and "Dark Integers" (2007) turn on a conflict between two incompatible mathematics, which matches the "数学战争" between worlds. An ASI persuading a world government to freeze humanity in order to free compute is not something I can place in Egan. Unverified.
- **刘慈欣《吞食者》** (*The Devourer*) is named correctly. **"戴达罗斯之翼"** has no referent I can identify.
- **"青藏高原那个"**, the other idea proposed as a line animation, appears nowhere else in this corpus.

The transcript's own open items still stand. The cabin safety announcement covers 生态文明 02:54–03:45. The passage at 02:26–02:37 is unconfirmed. The words "corporate interest" at story1 01:38–01:40 need listening to. A place name, the moon's adjective at story1 04:52 and several closing sentences are also unresolved.
