# Final conceptual and ethical integration review

5 October 2026. Review by a collaborating AI agent, not external peer review. Scope: the formation report, updated theory and reader guide, ethical foundation, claim register, and the insulated-regeneration countermodel. This review does not replace the separate mathematical and executable audits.

## Verdict

**Accept, with three small precision edits recommended below. No blocking conceptual or ethical finding.** The new mechanism advances the unified foundation while preserving the distinction between a conditional construction, a general causal hypothesis, a philosophical interpretation, and ethical justification.

## Findings

The report accurately locates the advance beyond the earlier supplied binding gate: a participant chooses to build and operate a rival alternative, and an operator chooses its response. Physical control, observed implementation, available technology, and interference costs remain supplied premises. Neither report nor overview claims to explain the origin of every element of the institution.

The persistent-operator example preserves the consequential distinction between physical construction and activation. It explicitly allows early dormant construction and describes delayed operation rather than claiming a unique building date. Its extra-reserve result is framed as removal of one particular two-period financing attack. The report does not infer general superiority of turnover or a universal advantage of additional cash from its chosen grid.

The integration strengthens the broad wisdom account through the future conditions of understanding, choice, and correction. Bargaining remains one application. The cognition paragraph is explicitly an application inference rather than a tested cognitive result; the next epistemic research problem remains open. The guide preserves the negative reasoning-pilot finding. This prevents the new model from being presented as demonstrated superiority of the whole wisdom theory.

The positive ethical account remains premise-relative. Its argument invokes standing, relevant consistency, answerability, and feasible implementation. It does not derive standing from productive usefulness, control, survival, or equal bargaining power. Its distinctions among standing, influence, and legitimacy are retained in the main report and theory. Consent, uncertainty about moral patients, conflict, infeasibility, and justified limits on challenge are treated rather than silently settled.

The revised insulated-regeneration countermodel is sound as a deliberately specified causal counterexample. Production now actually requires R>=Rmin>0; initial stock and the target are in this region, so the convex update preserves operability. With replenishment prescribed, productive support converges to its maintained value. Since each safeguard carries positive private cost and no selector-relevant continuation effect, the no-safeguard sequence uniquely maximizes private receipts over a finite positive horizon or an infinite horizon discounted with a strictly positive factor below one. The model supplies the production opportunity and renewal rule; it does not claim energetic closure, general realism, or that actual institutions necessarily satisfy insulation. It disproves an implication from regeneration and perfect information alone, not a qualified empirical tendency that explicitly excludes its assumptions.

## Precise recommended edits

1. **CLAIMS.md, introductory paragraph.** The current sentence says that every proved claim uses assumptions displayed in FORMAL-CORE, while the new A-series results appear in FORMATION-MATH. Replace its first sentence after the date with: `“Proved” below always means under the displayed assumptions in the [formal core](FORMAL-CORE.md) or [formation proofs](FORMATION-MATH.md), as indicated.` This is a navigation/scope clarification, not a mathematical defect.

2. **THEORY.md, Section 8, paragraph beginning “The distinction reaches the foundation”.** Replace `A present improvement can be chosen for its future effects on another actor's corrective means.` with `A costly present intervention can be chosen because it reduces another actor's future corrective means.` The existing phrase “present improvement” obscures the specific result: blocking is more expensive now than safeguarding, but gains future control. The proposed wording tracks that mechanism directly.

3. **FORMATION.md, insulated-regeneration paragraph.** The revised productive-region assumptions are sufficient and correct. State `h>0` when introducing harm and change `an infinite discounted horizon` to `an infinite horizon with discount factor 0<delta<1`. Use the document's existing math markup for these symbols. The current standard interpretation is sound; these additions make the unique-maximization claim fully explicit and exclude zero-discount indifference about future actions.

None of these edits changes the argument or requires a new experiment. No additional normative expansion is needed for this release.
