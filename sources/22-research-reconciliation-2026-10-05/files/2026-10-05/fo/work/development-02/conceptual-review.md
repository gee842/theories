# Joint formation as a conditional result

5 October 2026. Conceptual review of `experiments/joint-formation/model.py`, `PROTOCOL.md`, and `TIMING-CLARIFICATION.md`. This is an internal AI review of the model's meaning, preference structure, evidence assumptions, and relation to the unified foundation. Mathematical certification and independent finite-game reconstruction are separate tasks.

## Verdict and the exact achievement

**Conceptually acceptable as a conditional joint-formation model.** The candidate implements the central choices in one sequential process: an initially untrained controller chooses whether and how to learn; its later self chooses whether to fund observation and physical control; a persistent operator chooses whether to interfere or protect; all actual payments pass through the same account; and each replacement controller must acquire its competence again. The patient supplies neither a productive input nor a reward or sanction.

The positive interpretation depends on proving the claimed protected region and the operator's continuation incentives, not merely observing a successful finite run. Once those proofs and independent checks pass, the proper conclusion is that the declared joint-formation problem has a conditional solution. It would move the goalposts to deny that achievement solely because the physical technology and the existence of an institutional role remain premises. It would overstate the result to call those premises themselves explained.

The new result is stronger than placing three successful schedules beside one another. Learning, inspection, control, interference, and replacement can conflict over the same money. Here their choices and costs actually meet in one game. The positive and adverse cases can therefore explain why the complete arrangement forms in one stated environment and fails in another.

## Preference change is explicit and consequential

The cold and warm controller are two dated decision selves. The cold self initially maximizes the episode's closing account balance. It anticipates that paid shared learning will create another-directed coefficient and that its warm successor will spend money differently. The warm self maximizes closing cash minus the represented patient's harm weighted by its acquired coefficient. The operator has a different objective: discounted cash over subsequent episodes.

This is a specified game with changing preferences. It is not a stationary utility-maximization theorem, and the source learning equations do not establish that every learner should or will adopt this convention. The clarification is important: anticipated preference change is accounted for rather than smuggled into an allegedly unchanged maximizer.

The concrete bootstrap works through an initially valued commercial return. With the primary parameters, calibration costs two and earns eight. Shared learning then leads to a three-unit verified arrangement. Its combined extra expenditure is five against an eight-unit return. An identity filter costs four instead of the subsequent three-unit verified arrangement. Because its fee arrives after the commercial receipt, the identity candidate is genuinely affordable at the primary opening reserve. Shared learning wins by the stated comparison, rather than by excluding the rival through timing.

The rival menu contains shared and identity-gated learning. That is enough for a conditional architecture-selection result within this menu. A free identity gate can reverse it and must remain a reported control. The model does not prove that all physically possible identity gates have an irreducible positive price, or that an unrestricted architecture search selects shared evaluation.

The controllers should be described as **dated account-cash maximizers or trustees**, rather than as fully modeled private owners pursuing personal consumption. The commercial receipt enters a continuing account, and the controller's objective over its closing balance is supplied. No personal distribution, voluntary donation, theft opportunity, or departing owner's bequest is modeled. Even when learned patient value is exactly zero, the specified cold objective still favors the account's money. This objective is distinct from benevolence toward the patient, but stewardship of the account remains an institutional primitive rather than a derived personal motive.

## Concern is acquired, while its enabling route remains specified

Each incoming controller starts with zero learned adverse value and zero recognition. A paid learning event acquires w=eta*beta and g=kappa*G; shared evaluation produces v=wg. In the primary witness this yields a patient-harm weight of 3.5. With zero alignment or an identity gate, the patient does not acquire that action-relevant weight, even though the commercial skill can still be learned.

This is a conditional acquisition mechanism rather than an initially positive fixed concern coefficient. Its primitives remain explicit: self valence, informative correspondence, update machinery, and the candidate shared route. An observed attack can preempt alignment without destroying the private learning return. Thus concern is a vulnerable product of the process, not an invulnerable label applied after the desired behavior occurs.

The coefficient has a behavioral meaning within the model. It does not prove compassion, subjective experience, justified similarity between all possible patients, or a complete ethical disposition. The patient's moral standing is neither created nor canceled when the coefficient changes. The represented harm is independently specified; the coefficient determines whether and how this actor responds to it.

## Evidence is selected and purchased, not conjured by an audit label

The model makes a useful distinction between an unauthenticated cue and a paid technical hazard test. A cheap mask defeats the cue. The verified gate depends on a specified nondestructive preproduction assay and physical input control, both of which have positive prices. The warm controller must prefer and afford their joint purchase.

This is **formation of reliable inspection through acquisition of a supplied reliable technology**. It is not a theorem deriving the truthfulness of a sensor from payments, learning its reliability from potentially corrupted reports, or identifying an unknown real-world causal effect. The assay's relation to the technical configuration is a physical premise. Its zero direct patient exposure is justified only because this constructed assay reads the configuration before the patient is exposed.

The interface also models an attack that bypasses verified control and creates actual patient harm. The effective arrangement therefore depends on both information and power: knowing the hazard is insufficient if a bypass makes the knowledge unable to constrain production. Conversely, paying for physical control without credible observation can leave a cue-based rule vulnerable to masking.

Because the game and technologies are common knowledge, the controller can anticipate operator responses when choosing its profile. This complete-information strategic calculation should not be described as discovery of an unknown danger. The dynamic program calculates choices within the declared game; it is not the controller's empirical learning algorithm.

A proposed physical refinement can make the exact assay more explicit without changing the solved game. Let its raw signal be y=h XOR theta, with an initially unknown polarity theta in {0,1}. Paid observations of known nondestructive fixtures h=0 and h=1 identify theta from the first reading and check complementary response with the second. The controller then decodes the current configuration by h_hat=y XOR theta. Under faithful fixtures and one stationary polarity from calibration through operation, this exactly identifies the binary hazard without giving the controller theta in advance. A constant-zero cheap cue fails the fixture check. Paid bypass must remain a separately charged breach or forgery action, rather than an unpriced failure of the sensor premise.

This is a genuine finite identification problem, but its scope remains narrow. Known fixture labels, faithful transfer from fixtures to the actual configuration, stationarity during that episode, and protection against undeclared manipulation are premises. Two fixtures do not validate an arbitrary sensor or prove robustness to noise, changing mappings, strategic fixture substitution, or unknown patient outcomes. If the independent refinement checks reproduce the complete action/cost/outcome ledger, this strengthens the implementation explanation of the assay without changing the strategic theorem. At review time this refinement is a proposal; its executable verification should be reported separately when completed.

## Strategic interference and paid renewal

The high-resource witness is informative because continuing finance is checked against the operator's responses, not just the intended safeguard path. The proposed condition R+o>=ell+a+D makes even the low receipt compatible with rebuilding the selected apparatus, once the opening reserve is adequate. If continuation remains in a region where future incentives are unchanged, a persistent operator cannot gain merely by moving the account between already sufficient balances.

A destructive reserve raid differs from a current bypass. It can prevent the next learning event or control purchase and thus alter future choices. The claimed expensive-raid argument appropriately compares its price with the entire discounted gain available from removing safeguards, rather than with one immediate expense alone. The exact proof and its scope need to accompany the interpretation.

The cheap-raid control is an especially strong negative case: starting at five, destruction of two leaves only the three-unit subsistence bill. Learning becomes unaffordable; untrained open production returns the account to five. A recurring raid can therefore reproduce the same harmful state while the underlying productive process continues. This is a stable failure of joint formation inside the same model, not an exception discarded for lacking wisdom.

Succession is reconstructed, not merely announced. Every controller starts untrained; every episode again pays for its apparatus. No learned coefficient or sensor survives by assumption. The continuing account, incoming role, available commercial transaction, authority over the productive input, and physical access to the technologies do survive as premises. There is no production during the modeled setup stages, so this model does not silently credit protection during an omitted exposure interval. It also does not claim that a real handoff takes no time.

## What this settles within the foundation

The model supplies a candidate answer to the question left open by the static interface: concern, evidence, and effective control can be jointly selected and financed, rather than separately declared feasible. A patient without personal bargaining power can be protected because another controller acquires a valuation of the patient's condition and then chooses to use that controller's actual power. The patient need not become productive to become causally relevant to action.

The same process can fail through an inexpensive gate on concern, corruptible cues, cheap physical bypass, or attacks on the money needed to become competent. The theory's causal object becomes precise: **the relations that let affected consequences influence conduct are themselves acquired, purchased, attacked, and rebuilt.** Understanding those relations explains more than equating knowledge with concern or finance with protection.

A successful proof therefore completes one bounded developmental mechanism. It does not establish the prevalence of its commercial ecology, stewardship convention, routing costs, assay technologies, or inherited control in human institutions. Nor does it justify every represented protection or exhaust the goods of an affected being. Those limits delimit application; they do not erase the completed conditional explanation.

## Wording safeguards for the report

Use “initially money-focused controller” and “account return” where a claim of private ownership would be misleading. Describe evidence as purchased reliable inspection under the specified technical assay, rather than learned truth in general. Describe shared architecture as selected among the admitted candidates. State that renewal reconstructs competence and apparatus while continuing account possession and control remain supplied. Preserve actual patient harm, harmless withdrawal, protected production, and nonoperation as separate outcomes.

With these qualifications, the study advances the unified theory without converting an authored prototype into evidence of inevitable moral progress or human prevalence.
