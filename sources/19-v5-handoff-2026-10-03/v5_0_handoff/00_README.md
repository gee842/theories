# v4.5 review package and v5.0 direction

Prepared 3 October 2026. This is a research and writing handoff, not a finished v5.0 manuscript or submission bundle.

## Read first

Start with `01_v5_0_direction_and_authorial_brief.md`. It incorporates the latest authorial clarifications, including the perfect-AI-alignment case, the human winning-oriented game, AI's planetary purpose, and why a training pause without a relevant change of course is not itself readiness to resume. It includes a proposed v5.0 structure, repair priorities, evidence requirements, and two explicitly scoped mathematical demonstrations.

The author’s intended thesis is situational: explain why the present AI arms-race trajectory warrants a cooperative pivot. The task is not to prove that every imaginable bounded competition is impossible. Authority can serve public flourishing; governance tensions are mechanism-design problems, not an assumed law of inevitable corruption. Existing meta-attractor results must be credited. Mathematical and empirical objections remain applicable where their target claims are retained.

## Precedence and status

The latest authorial brief governs the intended argument. The corrected roadmap governs interpretation of the prospective panel; the individual memoranda remain useful challenges, not author-approved premises. The historical v4.5 review's precise mathematical objections are not overridden by a change of framing. New conditional demonstrations are labeled and do not establish that their empirical premises hold in the world.

The package contains no older manuscript versions, no v1/v2 reviews, and no superseded editions of the corrected roadmap or panel. The complete working manuscript repository and the author's original checks were not available for this handoff. The baseline is a reading copy.

The disciplinary reviews were generated by AI agents, not independent human referees. Source URLs and inspection limits are recorded in the reports; some original chat citation markers are preserved in the historical v4.5 review. The archive has not been submitted or published.
