# Supplementary assay methods

The main article points to [Figshare collection 7736484](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.7736484). The unchanged collection listing and two article metadata responses are preserved with the original supplementary PDF in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Its published MD5 matches the downloaded bytes. Both article records specify CC BY 4.0.

Reading for the mat-conditions study covers **PDF page 2, Supplementary Note 1, lines 969–987**. The page was rendered and visually inspected to confirm the extracted methods. They specify recording mat presence at the end of phase I, followed by phenotype-count measurements. That endpoint does not by itself distinguish failure to form from formation followed by loss. This reading does not certify every supplement figure, genotype count, inference procedure or genomic reconstruction.

The original supplement heading names the manuscript as 2024; the main journal article was published in 2025. Both are preserved. The [integration](../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MAT-CONDITIONS-INTEGRATION.md) uses the methods to constrain what the existing assay can identify, without changing the prior empirical reconstruction or proof scope.

The separate 35,106,336-byte supplementary ZIP was also retrieved and its published MD5 verified. [Byte and member comparison](archive-comparison.json) finds **430 common code/data files all byte-identical** to the already preserved Zenodo archive, with no missing or changed common members. Its only addition is `public.tar.gz`, containing exported analysis outputs. Eight CSV headers were inspected. The four lineage tables' `time` values are phase indices 1–20, explicitly matched to cycle and phase; they do not add within-phase mat trajectories. Their processed row counts differ from our raw-record reconstruction and are not substituted for it. Other exported contents have not been substantively audited.

To avoid importing a redundant bundle, the downloaded ZIP stays outside the repository; original API metadata, complete added-member inventory, hashes, CSV headers and phase-index checks are preserved here. The [inspector](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/ecological-selection/mat-conditions/inspect_supplement.py) reproduces the comparison from the public download. This establishes the inspected data scope, not that no relevant time-course data exist elsewhere.
