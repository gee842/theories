The earlier record 11170876 is linked in the article's analysis section; the final data citation identifies 13244627. Our calculations use only **13244627**. Its large precomputed-output archive was not downloaded. The code/data ZIP includes the two raw workbooks and genomic alignment outputs. The initial reconstruction inspected the selected genealogy/fitness workbooks, README and two processing notebooks; preservation of the whole original ZIP is not a claim to have read or rerun every component.

Reading covers methods §§2a, 2b, 2d; results §§3a–d; their embedded captions, data availability and license. `01_genealogies.ipynb` and `06_fitness_assays.ipynb` were read as data, not executed. The latter computes overall performance by multiplying three component frequencies. No genome-alignment, Bayesian mutation propagation, author-software installation or independent genetic manipulation was performed.

The genealogy workbook has 3,840 rows, including 32 marked `MISS`. After excluding only those explicitly missing rows, 3,808 unique phase/culture observations remain. Eight missing rows in the WT-L sheet repeat an earlier cycle index; they are preserved unchanged and excluded as missing, not silently relabelled. The raw missing treatment label differs from the Figure 3 caption's L-lcs+ reference: in the workbook and author conversion it is WT-L, mapped to L-lcs−. Our reading edition records the original worksheet and row and follows those explicit labels. After gaps, ancestry is left unresolved until supported by subsequent recorded donor relationships.

The fitness workbook includes assays from multiple dates and differing designs. The directly linked comparison uses 2 June 2020 because both focal genotypes have all 72 initial cultures identified, and their phase-II rows correspond exactly to the phase-I survivors with no recorded replacements. Earlier unmatched or incompletely followed batches are not pooled into that denominator. They remain in the original archive; a separate ancestral first-phase correspondence audit explicitly retains one contaminated, missing-count record.

## Dated-assay follow-up

The [developmental transfer study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DEVELOPMENTAL-TRANSFER-INTEGRATION.md) inventories all 3,802 raw assay rows into 139 cohorts, then independently checks 485 selected rows from five focal date/genotype batches. It revisits article §3d and the existing assay notebook; [reading scope and original-byte bindings](transfer-reading.json) distinguish this from a new experiment or replication of the author's statistical analysis. The February 2020 batch has incomplete second-phase follow-up, while the 2018 evolved-strain batch permits following 40 original survivors separately from eight replacement destinations. These distinctions are preserved rather than pooling all dates into one fitness value.

The [newly preserved supplementary methods](supplement/README.md) confirm that mat presence is scored at the phase-I endpoint. Their selected reading constrains the next mechanistic test: initial absence and formation followed by loss are not distinguished by that endpoint alone. Published PDF checksum, original Figshare metadata and visual verification are recorded separately from the earlier unchanged source manifest.
