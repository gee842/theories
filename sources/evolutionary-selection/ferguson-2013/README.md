## Read scope and provenance limitations

Read the imaging, reconstruction, complementation and competition methods; FS niche revision; pH experiments; WS mat-integrity results; and Figures 2 and 5. The article reports a genetic intervention and a prior ecological interpretation corrected by temporal observations. Those are the original authors' results. This repository has re-extracted and inspected media, not repeated their experiments.

The method specifies 6 ml KB broth, approximately 5 × 10^5 initial bacteria and static incubation at 28°C. Photographs were taken at 5- or 10-minute intervals over 24–72 hours and compiled in Movie Maker. The four MOV files are 640 × 480 at an encoded 30000/1001 fps. Their frame counts, playback timestamps and movie durations do **not** establish the capture times of the original photographs. No biological-time axis, transition rate or independent replicate count is inferred from encoded frames. The manifest's reported spans come from legends, not reconstructed clocks.

**S6 discrepancy:** the legend and result paragraph name WS and WS-fuzY T443G. The actual tube labels read LSWS and LSWS with a deletion-like “Δ…478” notation. The article identifies fuzY as pflu0478 and separately studies point-mutant and deletion constructs. This does not license silently choosing one exact genotype for the movie. We retain the legend, visible label and unresolved correspondence separately. The material contrast remains viewable; allele-specific quantitative interpretation is withheld.

The BioStudies metadata date 2013-01-01 is not used as the publication date. The movie's 1970 creation metadata is not an experimental date. Table 4B's heading and footnote also differ in genotype attribution; this round does not extract that subsection into a quantitative model. Nonsignificant 72-hour titers in Table 4A do not establish identical growth trajectories.
