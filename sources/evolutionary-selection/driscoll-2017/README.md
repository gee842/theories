# Cluster interactions and coexistence: preserved primary source

William W. Driscoll and Michael Travisano, *Synergistic cooperation promotes multicellular performance and unicellular free-rider persistence*, Nature Communications 8, 15707 (2017), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms15707). Original article and six supplementary workbooks retrieved 8 October 2026. Article XML states CC BY 4.0, with the usual source-specific exceptions for third-party content.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) records exact URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 fingerprints. These are local integrity checks, not publisher-supplied checksums. The XML comes from Europe PMC; publisher HTML supplies the original Springer Nature workbook links. All eight files retain retrieved bytes. The workbooks were opened read-only and never resaved.

| Original file suffix | Publisher designation | Contents and current reading scope |
|---|---|---|
| MOESM76_ESM.xlsx | Supplementary Data 1 | Isolate size/shape summaries; schema inspected, not reconstructed from microscopy. |
| MOESM77_ESM.xlsx | Supplementary Data 2 | Growth-parameter estimates and comparisons; schema inspected, no raw growth curves reanalysed. |
| MOESM78_ESM.xlsx | Supplementary Data 3 | Growth/settling performance, competition coefficients, mutual-invasion fit and contrasts. Complete tables read; these are summary outputs, not the underlying population time series. |
| MOESM79_ESM.xlsx | Supplementary Data 4 | Unicell enrichment regression on mixture volumes. Complete table read; underlying 37 observations absent from this workbook. |
| MOESM80_ESM.xlsx | Supplementary Data 5 | 54 video mean velocities, with treatment and technical-replicate IDs; separately, structural-equation model output. Velocity worksheet fully reanalysed; particle measurements and model resampling not reconstructed. |
| MOESM81_ESM.xlsx | Supplementary Data 6 | Culture-settling fit/contrast summaries, including heat-fixed preparations. Complete tables read, no original image/time-series reconstruction. |

Article reading covers the four results subsections, settling-selection, invasion, short-term settling, video and culture-imaging methods, relevant figure captions and data availability. The publication reports 10 evolved populations and two mutual-invasion experiments. Those biological experiments must not be confused with the **three technical replicates per video treatment**. The public workbooks retrieved here do not supply a common particle-to-offspring record. The article says underlying supporting data are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request; no request has been sent.

The main text and Figure 7 caption give different nonsignificant Tukey values for the S. cerevisiae density comparison. Our study does not select one as a newly verified significance result; it recomputes observed means and technical-replicate sensitivity only. Supplementary video and PDF files have not been downloaded or independently inspected. No visual tracking replication is claimed.
