L99@P3: and continues to be, fair and transparent.

L1182@P30: In flight income compliance reviews
L1183@P30: 3.106. Services Australia uses the term ‘income compliance review’ to refer to activity by its
L1184@P30: compliance staff to ‘review’ discrepancies between people’s income reported to
L1185@P30: Services Australia and information obtained from the ATO to determine if a debt might exist.
L1186@P30: 3.107. Services Australia advised that at the time of the Australian Government’s 19 November 2019
L1187@P30: announcement, there were approximately 240,000 income compliance reviews ‘in flight’ (that
L1188@P30: had commenced but had not yet been finalised).30 On 10 September 2020, it reported the
L1189@P30: number of ‘in flight’ reviews had decreased to 199,065 (i.e. a reduction of approximately
L1190@P30: 41,000 reviews since changes were made to the IC Program).

L1192@P30: following the 19 November 2019 announcement, without relying on averaged ATO income
L1193@P30: information in the process. It provided the following information about what it has been doing,
L1194@P30: and what it intends to do with these reviews:

L1196@P30: confirmation that an individual had received an initiation letter, staff have contacted
L1197@P30: individuals to ask for information or seek clarification on information previously
L1198@P30: provided. If officers have been unable to contact individuals, they have been
L1199@P30: instructed to use ‘information already provided’ to finalise the review.31

L1201@P30: reviews between 3 April and 2 November 2020. During this time, Services Australia

L1203@P30: of the review is a debt, it postponed raising and recovering any debts until the end of
L1204@P30: the debt pause.
