# A purpose-built building for shared augmented worlds

Four original 1536 × 1024 landscape concept illustrations, created with built-in `image_gen` on 7 September 2026. All were visually inspected. The images here are unchanged copies of the generated PNGs, with embedded provenance intact; original Codex paths and output hints are retained in [manifest.json](manifest.json). [prompts.json](prompts.json) preserves the exact prompts.

The series uses a common architectural vocabulary: a monumental terraced rectilinear megaframe, immense stacked world halls, graphite lift cores and amber service bands. It explores a building conceived from the beginning for embodied shared augmented experience. The independent generated views develop that vocabulary; they are not geometrically registered views of a single CAD model or evidence that the proposed engineering is solved.

## Visual inspection notes

- **Arrival:** Monumental single megaframe clearly dominates a human-scale arriving plaza. Tall open volumes, dark lift cores and amber service bands read consistently. No readable labels or branding.
- **World hall:** Shared cloud-ocean realm fills the image. Tangible curved walkway, handrail, bench and residents in lightweight glasses preserve bodily anchors. The immense impossible landscape is an experience layer.
- **Reality pair:** Exactly two equal panels preserve the same camera, travelers, clothing, poses, curved walkway, bench and rail. The first reveals the high engineered hall; the second shows the corresponding immersive cloud harbor. Matching floor and touchable geometry make the relation immediately legible.
- **Section:** Three large world halls are visible above a ground logistics level, with amber service bands, elevator cores and secondary stairs. The original prompt requested four halls; the usable output instead shows three plus logistics. Accompanying labels should follow the visible image. Blue transparent layers indicate perceptual design and are not literal physical holograms hanging in the halls.

Use the source PNGs intact. Captions and any diagram labels belong in the layout outside these images. Physical routes, structural spans, services, accessibility, tracking and emergency operations require further design studies; the images illustrate a setting and design proposition.
