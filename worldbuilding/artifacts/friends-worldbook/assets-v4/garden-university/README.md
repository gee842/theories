# Embodied Garden and Open University views

Four original **1536 x 1024** landscape concept images for the fourth friend-worldbook edition. Created with the **built-in image_gen tool**, with one independent generation call per requested image. No CLI/API fallback was used. University calls were retried after connection failures.

These are AI-rendered spatial proposals derived from the Living Garden and Open University briefs, not records of constructed facilities or engineering validation.

## Assets

- **garden-walk.png** - Eye-height garden route: hand on cool stone, continuous accessible path, water-garden play, planted reusable steel housing and autonomous garden maintenance.
- **garden-section.png** - A continuous inhabited section-perspective: garden homes above, rainwater storage, shared cup return/washing hub, service access, utility runs and robot maintenance below.
- **university-cosmos.png** - Shared AR astronomical inquiry from a participant's perspective: hands on tangible controllers, room-scale orbital visualization, companions and an observable roof telescope.
- **university-workshop.png** - Public instrument foundry with guarded robotic fabrication, hands-on optical-instrument assembly, public design station, parts library, service carrier and ordinary courtyard life.

## Prompts and provenance

The exact four generation prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json). [generation-metadata.json](generation-metadata.json) records original generated-image paths, selected outputs, dimensions and two unsuccessful workshop cleanup attempts. Original generated PNGs remain at their tool-provided locations. The selected project PNGs preserve the generated pixels; no postprocessing or resizing was performed.

## Visual review

All four outputs were inspected in their native 1536 x 1024 display.

- Garden walk: strong tactile foreground and coherent architectural depth. The primary path stays clear. Nature and active maintenance share the environment.
- Garden section: spatial relationships are legible across a continuous cut. Root zone, cistern, utility routes and service bays are visible. This is a concept section: pipe connectivity, drainage, loads, fire strategy and hygiene arrangements require engineering. A human is present in the washing area; the image does not establish whether a depicted activity is required labor or freely chosen participation.
- University cosmos: the floor and benches retain bodily orientation while AR lines occupy the shared visual field. Text in the booklet should explicitly identify the starfield as a visualization. The telescope and loft are concept architecture.
- University workshop: machine guarding, parts handling, public observation and optical assembly are visible. There are no explanatory captions or readable display labels. **A tiny manufacturer-like monitor-bezel mark remains**; two built-in cleanup attempts did not convincingly remove it. This is recorded rather than describing the image as completely free of marks.

No image is intended to substitute for the worldbook's distinction between current demonstrated capabilities and speculative integrated arrangements.
