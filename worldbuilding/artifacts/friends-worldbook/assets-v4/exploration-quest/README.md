## Visual review

All four outputs were inspected inline at their generated resolution. All contain distinct compositions and no captions, logos or readable interface text. All PNG dimensions were checked. The launch view provides clear visual distance, though it does not establish a measured launch-safety design. The two cutaways make spatial relationships legible; their exposed edges are illustration conventions, not missing finished guardrails. They are speculative architectural illustrations and do not establish validated structure, circulation, cleanroom performance, fire protection, or launch engineering.

The exploration section contains an uncrewed probe and instrument assembly, with launch absent from the inhabited campus. The quest bridge view renders augmented imagery from a participant's perspective. The backstage view similarly shows the authored perceptual layer alongside the tangible city. Human activity in service and creative rooms expresses voluntary participation; these images do not by themselves establish the project's assumed degree of automation.
