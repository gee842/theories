ABOUT THIS ATLAS / EDITION 04 / 07 SEPTEMBER 2026
Seeing a possibility
is the beginning
The vision and this edition
Ren’s dialogue joins autonomous productive capacity, shared
abundance, freedom from compulsory labor, generative culture, and
plural ways of living. This visual edition answers his request for
embodied experiences and a megabuilding designed expressly for AR.
Scenes, architectural forms, sensory details, and layouts are
AI-developed proposals.
What the images show
Twenty distinct concept illustrations, each placed once. Twelve are
new: embodied experiences, architectural sections, and the
purpose-built AR megastructure. They share a design vocabulary
within each case; they are not views derived from one geometrically
resolved 3D model. Cutaways make spatial ideas visible without
establishing structural or engineering feasibility.
How to read the AR worlds
Bodies move through real floors, paths, lifts, and tactile settings.
Perceptual environments are aligned to that space. Large visual
replacement requires a proposed occlusive display or passthrough
system; ordinary transparent glasses alone are not assumed to erase
the building. Immense apparent skies and distances are experienced,
not literal additional floor area.
The argument remains available
The atlas retains the technical, generative, and material foundations in
compact form. Edition 03 and the project’s linked foundations retain
the fuller argument, evidence notes, and original source passages.
Ren’s April 30 quotation and 生生不息 anchor the generative
orientation.
