# Concept illustration review

Date: 2026-09-07. Method: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, three independent new-image calls, with no input images. Exact prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json); tool output paths are preserved in [generation-metadata.json](generation-metadata.json). Original outputs remain in their generated-images directory. Each project asset was inspected using `view_image`.

All three outputs are 1536 × 1024 PNGs. They are illustrative proposals, not records of built places or evidence of engineering feasibility.

- **house-of-worlds.png:** A coherent daylight timber atrium with lift, clear ordinary circulation, refuge bench, adjacent movement studio, and a discrete immersive doorway. Optional glasses coexist with people without glasses. No text or branding. The projected cloud harbor is deliberately fantastical, contained within a real architectural threshold.
- **open-university.png:** A credible courtyard connects library, workshop, learning table, movement practice, shared kitchen and rest. Human activities and materials read naturally. No text or branding.
- **material-lives.png:** Metal cup return and clean supply shelves, a washing utility area, separated component storage/inspection space, compact wheeled robot and common dining courtyard. No molten metal, sparks, or dangerous work crosses the public path. No text or branding. Separation is visually legible at concept scale; food hygiene, circulation, structure and fire safety still require actual design review.

The images use warm neutral materials and ordinary near-future spaces. They illustrate varied daily life and care rather than claims of perfect automation, zero material loss, or a completed transition.
