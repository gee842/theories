# Embodied worldbook copy · Edition 04

Proposed reader-facing captions, developed with AI from Ren’s worldbuilding project. Each caption accompanies a concept image; sensory details describe an imagined visit. Root production may adapt wording to the final images. Section titles below are stable content IDs, not intended page titles.

## garden-arrival

You arrive while breakfast is still happening. Voices travel across the water; someone waves from a table under the trees. Your home is close enough that joining can be an impulse. Shared kitchens, maintained paths, water systems, and quiet service routes support this ease. The garden is a place you can live, with room for an ordinary afternoon.

## garden-walk

You follow the cooler edge of the garden, where leaves move above the path. A friend stops to show you something small growing beside the water. You can linger without blocking the way. Planting, shade, seating, and accessible routes are composed together; automated care supports the landscape while people develop their own attachments to it.

## garden-section

You can follow your day through the building: from a private room to a shaded terrace, a shared meal, and the garden beyond. Beneath that ease are water, power, repair access, and routes for returning useful things. Housing and common space share one working structure. A secure place to withdraw makes the invitation to join feel different.

## university-arrival

You hear a conversation before you know its subject. A table has gathered around a question, and there is space beside someone who is learning too. Across the courtyard are books, instruments, and places to make an idea tangible. Public knowledge and shared equipment give curiosity somewhere to go. You can stay an afternoon or begin a life here.

## university-cosmos

You stand beside friends inside a model of something too vast to encounter directly. A gesture changes the scale; a familiar point becomes a landscape of relationships. Someone asks what the model leaves out. Shared visualization, scientific instruments, and AI guidance bring difficult ideas into reach. Understanding grows through moving, questioning, and comparing what each of you notices.

## university-workshop

You turn a part in your hands while a more experienced visitor shows you how it connects. Nearby, a machine prepares the next piece. The question from yesterday is becoming something you can test. Shared fabrication, repair benches, and public design records let discoveries travel beyond this room. You learn enough to change the design, then leave that knowledge available.

## exploration-arrival

You enter the spacecraft hall and instinctively lower your voice. Above the gathering, a vessel is taking shape; someone beside you recognizes a component they helped imagine. Robots and specialized tools carry out the heavy assembly. This community gives a shared dream a physical home, where learning, making, and the patient life between missions belong together.

## exploration-launch

You wait with people who have followed this mission through years of questions and revisions. The distant launch lights the horizon; conversation gives way to watching. Transport, launch infrastructure, automated preparation, and a continuing flow of knowledge connect this moment to the settlement. Tomorrow, the same community will make breakfast, study what happened, and begin thinking further.

## exploration-section

You can trace a mission through its home on Earth: a conversation becomes an experiment, a component, an assembled craft, and eventually a journey. Workshops and gathering places remain connected while heavy operations have their own routes. Energy, materials, maintenance, and shared learning sustain the adventure. Exploration becomes a culture people inhabit across the long intervals between departures.

## quest-arrival

You arrive as lanterns appear along the canal. A friend greets you in a costume they have been making all week; supper is waiting nearby. Through shared AR, the city carries creatures, histories, and invitations its residents have composed. The story continues between quests, held in real homes, workshops, friendships, and the places where people keep meeting.

## quest-bridge

You cross the bridge with someone who knows a different version of the tale. Above the roofs, the shared world answers a choice your group has made. The bridge remains solid beneath your hand. Spatially anchored AR gives many people one unfolding encounter; the physical city supplies distance, weather, companions, and somewhere to sit when the adventure pauses.

## quest-backstage

You come looking for a missing costume piece and stay to help invent a creature. Here, the city’s magic has a workshop: fabric, tools, digital models, spare parts, and people exchanging ideas. Public assets and shared fabrication let new storytellers begin. A guest can become a maker, and a familiar world can grow beyond what its founders imagined.

## ar-megastructure

You approach a building designed to contain worlds. Its enormous halls and terraces give those worlds real depth, while circulation threads through places to gather, live, and make things. Architecture supplies the body of the experience; shared AR, light, and sound can give it different identities. Communities can create whole environments here, with space for cultures to take root.

## ar-portal

You enter the cabin with a friend. The doors close; the atmosphere changes. When they open, the next world seems to have been waiting on the other side. The lift has carried your body to another floor. Coordinated sound, shared AR, and an arrival designed around the doorway turn that ordinary movement into the felt experience of crossing realities.

## ar-worldhall

You step into the cloud harbor and feel the scale of it before you speak. Friends wait beside a railing; their voices belong to the same space as yours. A vast physical hall gives the realm room to breathe. Walkways, touchable surfaces, spatial sound, and shared AR let an imagined place become somewhere people can gather and develop traditions.

## ar-reality-pair

You lift the glasses. The harbor becomes a hall, yet your hand still rests on the same rail and your friend stands where they stood before. Put them on, and the world returns around those anchors. Both views share one physical geometry. The building is designed so that appearance can change while bodies, surfaces, and ordinary navigation remain coherent.

## ar-section

You can trace a passage from a garden world to a cloud harbor and onward to a quiet home. Large experience halls sit around transport, workshops, service spaces, and places to pause. Stable structure supports changing worlds; reusable assemblies give new communities material to work with. This building makes collective imagination an ongoing practice with somewhere to live.
