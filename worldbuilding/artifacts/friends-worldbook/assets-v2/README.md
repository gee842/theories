# Expanded worldbook illustrations

Four original concept illustrations created on 7 September 2026 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Each is 1536 × 1024 pixels (3:2). The files here are unchanged copies of the generated PNGs, including embedded provenance metadata. Originals remain in Codex's generated-image directory; their paths and tool output hints are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Exact generation prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json).

These are proposals for the speculative setting. They are not photographs of existing places or evidence that the illustrated technologies are currently available.

## Visual review

All four were viewed at generation completion and checked for the requested subjects, spatial readability, and absence of readable lettering or branding. No earlier image was reused. The cover presents people as active participants in shaping possibilities; the quest city combines ordinary inhabitation and a shared narrative. The exploration image conveys civilian cooperative ambition and places the launch complex visibly beyond the immediate commons. The elevator image preserves recognizable cabin anchors and the three people across the two panels, making the transformation of experience legible. Its fantasy destination needs the accompanying perceptual-experience caption so it is not mistaken for a claim of physical teleportation.

The illustrations establish atmosphere and lived possibilities. They are not detailed construction, launch-safety, accessibility, or circulation plans; those belong in subsequent studies.
