# Worlds we can inhabit

2026-09-07 · **Edition 04: An atlas of lived futures** · A worldbuilding project by Ren, developed with AI.

**Read or share:** [Worlds we can inhabit — visual atlas](output/pdf/worlds-we-can-inhabit-v4.pdf). Twenty-five pages, approximately 15.5 MB, with twenty distinct illustrations and seventeen pages devoted to inhabiting the worlds. Every illustration appears once, in its complete frame. Selectable captions, bookmarks, embedded text fonts, and clickable sources accompany the images.

Ren's [latest direction](../../archive/008-ren-embodied-worlds-and-ar-megastructure.txt) asks readers to feel the future through what they see: the environment around a body, companionship and activity, and the technology and engineering that make a life possible. This edition develops each world as a visual journey, giving images most of the page.

## Later independent review

Ren requested a full review by fresh agents after delivery. The [2026-09-07 review](reviews/2026-09-07-v4/README.md) includes four independent verdicts and complete page coverage. It confirms the strong embodied imagery and export quality while identifying substantive gaps in productive recursion, dependable shared abundance, practiced generative understanding, and dynamic material transformation. The earlier broad content pass should not be read as establishing full standalone coverage of the vision. Edition 04 remains unchanged; the review records priorities for a future revision.

## What changed

Garden, university, exploration, and quests each receive three views: arrival, participation, and a supporting section or workshop. The House of Worlds receives five: the megastructure, passage through a portal lift, sustained life in a cloud harbor, physical and experienced space side by side, and the building's working section. Twelve new images expand eight selected earlier images.

The [House of Worlds brief](../../worlds/02-house-of-worlds.md) now starts with a **megastructure conceived expressly for shared AR inhabitation**. Enormous world halls, portal lift groups, real terrain and tactile anchors, and thick technical floors belong to its primary purpose. The paired view preserves the people, walkway, rail, and bench while transforming their experienced surroundings. The earlier mixed-use brief is [preserved](../../worlds/02-house-of-worlds-edition-03.md).

The [embodied-journey guide](../../worlds/embodied-journeys.md) expands all five worlds in prose and supplies thirty future frame directions. The world template, possibility map, and project guidance now carry this method forward. Particular buildings, sensory details, and visual styles remain proposed developments of Ren's direction.

## The foundation remains part of the world

The atlas retains the argument **existing knowledge and machinery → capacity that helps build more capacity → conditional compounding → abundant shared provision and creative means → freedom from compulsory labor → generative civilization-building**. Its opening connects pictured futures to operating capabilities and a practical construction program. Full autonomous industrial reproduction remains a thesis to develop and test; no complete supply-network autonomy or doubling time is established by the reviewed evidence.

The generative page shows the reciprocal formation of people and environments. It carries Ren's April 30 quotation, principles for further becoming, and the meaning of 生生不息 used in this project. Material continuity connects transformation to public knowledge, recoverable stock, useful parts, and ecological renewal.

The [third edition](output/pdf/worlds-we-can-inhabit-v3.pdf) remains the fuller text companion, with six opening technical and social chapters. Its [notes](edition-03-README.md), [automation foundation](../../foundations/automation-to-shared-abundance.md), [generative foundation](../../foundations/generative-worldmaking.md), and [material foundation](../../foundations/regenerative-material-civilization.md) retain the developed argument. These are concept illustrations. Within each case they share a design vocabulary; they are not projections from one geometrically resolved 3D model. Sections explain proposed spatial relationships without validating structural or engineering performance. Large AR visual replacement uses a proposed occlusive display or passthrough approach; ordinary transparent glasses alone are not assumed to erase the building. Experienced apparent distances do not create additional physical floor area.

## History and rebuilding

[Edition 01](output/pdf/worlds-we-can-inhabit.pdf) and [its notes](edition-01-README.md) preserve the first introduction. [Edition 02](output/pdf/worlds-we-can-inhabit-v2.pdf) and [its notes](edition-02-README.md) restored the meta-level and wider worlds. To rebuild from this folder, run `build-v4.py` with Python, ReportLab, and Pillow. The default font paths use the discovered runtime's Noto fonts and the host's Arial Unicode font; pass `--font-dir` and `--cjk-font` to use another compatible installation. Render the PDF with Poppler, inspect every changed page, and run `verify-v4.py` before refreshing the review record. Fonts are embedded in the PDF and are not separately copied into the project.

A PDF edit-operation marker was recorded before authoring this edition. This artifact is ready for Ren to share; it has not been sent to other people or published.
