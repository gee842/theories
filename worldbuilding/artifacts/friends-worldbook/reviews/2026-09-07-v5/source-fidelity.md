## Verdict

The atlas preserves the full causal argument and is substantively suitable as an illustrated invitation to the project after the small consistency corrections below. It represents a practical construction direction with declared uncertainties. It does not quietly replace Ren's strong automation thesis with arbitrary fictional abundance, free association alone, a generic garden aesthetic, or a demand that everyone perform creativity.

The strongest improvements are meaningful sequences rather than a checklist of themes: production and knowledge each return to improve later capacity; Mira changes commitments while retaining her means of life; a guild changes a rule that shaped authorship; a recognizable frame changes use without multiplying; and the AR building contains a recurring shared activity with real bodily anchors.

## What the actual sequences establish

**Pages 2–4, 33: technical starting point.** The factory image puts bodies, fixtures, mobile machines, and production equipment into one understandable scene. Page 3's upper return makes the larger productive base contribute to useful production again; its lower return makes deployment teach later operation. The coupling arrows connect the physical and knowledge loops. Inputs, maintenance, retirement, commissioning, and local validation remain visible. Page 4 states the full-automation engineering thesis in Ren's name while separating it from the checked pieces and the proposed construction program. The evidence page preserves bounded task/deployment language and continuing human assistance. This is faithful attribution and scope framing; it is not an independent finding that those external sources prove full automation.

**Pages 5–6: abundant means and labor freedom.** Mira remains recognizably the same person through a private breakfast, the commons, and the instrument library. Her paused commitment does not withdraw her home, care, food, or access to a new inquiry. The atlas therefore conveys more than a survival allowance: private space, shared instruments, workshops, mobility, cultural life, and ambitious undertakings are available. Page 6 joins productive/maintenance capacity **with** common access. Its text explicitly makes free association one result of sustained civilizational systems. The option to rest is credible because belonging is not tied to the next productive project.

**Pages 7 and 17–20: generative understanding in practice.** The general principles protect further beginnings, material continuity, plural lives, and revision without maximizing output or possibilities. The guild sequence then exposes a concrete feedback: winning brings authoring time; repeated advantage narrows what can be imagined; a newcomer's unused sketches reveal the consequence; access rules change while honors remain; later participation becomes something the guild can inspect and revise. This makes inhabitants coauthors of conditions rather than merely consumers of an attractive game. The image shows the newcomer moving from the edge of the table into shared authorship. The prose supplies the social rule, which need not be literally legible on an illustrated board.

**Pages 8–21: plural lived worlds.** Garden attachment, shared inquiry, instrument-making, a community's long commitment to spacecraft, a launch watched together, persistent fantasy culture, and embodied expressive life are distinct pursuits. The expressive image includes movement, instruments, a wheelchair user participating, and a person resting at the edge. It supplies a valuable counterweight to cultures organized around understanding or missions. Its one-page depth is less than the other developed journeys; that is an accepted scope limit for this additional world, not evidence that it stands for the whole civilization.

**Pages 22–27: a megabuilding made for embodied AR.** Exterior and section agree at the level claimed: three broad halls, ground logistics, amber technical bands, passenger core left, service core right. The elevator makes actual vertical travel feel like passage between realities. Local captions on pages 23–25 state the future occlusive/passthrough display assumption. The middle-hall gathering has a seasonal ritual and continuing shared musical activity. The matched views preserve the table, near canopy, doorway, bench, rail, walkway, and participants while the apparent distant extent changes. This gives the reader real footing and reachable companions without suggesting the sky supplies extra floor area. Page 27 connects arrival, gathering, ordinary return, and a separate module change. The mixed-use interpretation has not returned.

**Pages 28–31: regenerative material and amplified will.** The cup/parts scene, then the indigo-ended frame across pavilion, disassembly/inspection, and music room, makes successive usefulness visible. Page 30 explicitly distinguishes circulation from simultaneous expansion and material reuse from living ecological regeneration. It acknowledges losses, replacement, energy, and system-specific evaluation. Page 31 makes restraint positive: people reshape a building proposal around continuity and an existing tree while the tools of rapid transformation are visible. This sustains Ren's material freedom argument without treating recovery as perfect or a recast beam as an ecosystem.

## Accepted scope and remaining limits

- Names, institutions, rituals, hall arrangements, and machinery sequences remain AI design proposals. They do not become Ren's settled canon merely because they are compatible with his direction. Page 34 makes that status explicit.
- The atlas is a shareable conceptual worldbook. It does not need structural spans, evacuation calculations, robotic disassembly validation, a complete automation audit, a measured recovery yield, or a growth forecast to succeed at that task.
- The production sequence is a readable mechanism diagram and construction program, not a measured two-generation industrial demonstration. The distinction is clear and appropriate.
- General cutaways locate supporting functions and route strips state intended flow; the images do not resolve every lift, pipe, or component transfer. The AR sequence is coordinated in major relationships, not a single resolved 3D model. Its local and closing captions accurately limit that claim.
- Pages 24–25 deliberately revisit one gathering for the physical/experienced comparison. They use distinct illustrations and a clear explanatory purpose; this is not the accidental reuse of a decorative picture that Ren objected to.
- The expressive world remains an invitation rather than a full multi-page architectural journey. The six-world breadth nevertheless survives.
- The atlas says a readable companion and ZIP travel with it. This review did not verify the companion, archive contents, bookmarks, link behavior, or final sharing route; those remain export/package checks for the editor.

No doctrinal regression requiring a rewrite was found. The remaining requested work is a small number of local consistency fixes and verification of the final export.
