This is an editorial judgment about a concept atlas, not audience testing or evidence that the proposed civilization has been engineered.

## Fresh review method

Both reviewers were spawned with no inherited conversation. The [cold reader](cold-reader.md) read only the current PDF and renders before judging whether it could explain itself to a friend. The [source-fidelity reviewer](source-fidelity.md) read the archived directions and connected foundations and checked the visual/textual correspondence. Following project orientation exposed that reviewer to general summaries of earlier review history; the actual earlier review files and QA were not opened. Their report records that limitation.

The reviewers each inspected all 34 pages. They reviewed changing drafts, then checked corrections or reported them pending for the lead agent’s final check. The lead agent inspected the complete sequence in page contact sheets, enlarged the new story and diagram pages, and re-inspected all six pages that changed after the second assembled draft. All other final page render hashes are identical to that reviewed draft. Individual image creators also inspected their selected assets, but those inspections are not substituted for the independent editorial passes.

- Page 24 places the quiet observer beside the rail, matching the image.

## What remains open

The sources do not demonstrate universal present-day AI competence, a fully autonomous industrial supply network or a measured doubling time. The atlas clearly proposes the integration and social program. Architecture, display performance, recovery yields and operating institutions still require their own design and testing.

A future cultural episode could include a disagreement that persists, or a scarce workshop allocation showing how a newcomer challenges a decision. The present stories show successful revision more than durable conflict. The Expressive Commons can gain a fuller architectural sequence. These are directions to deepen an already intelligible atlas; they are not concealed proofs or unresolved layout failures.
