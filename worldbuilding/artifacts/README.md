## Creating further artifacts

For a substantial artifact, create a descriptive subfolder, for example `living-garden-courtyard/`, with:

- A short `README.md` linking to the world brief, the question being explored, and relevant map IDs.
- Editable source files or the script that generates the artifact.
- Viewable exports where useful, with their relationship to the source clear.
- A short note on assumptions, scale, what was inspected, and what remains unresolved.

For a first 3D study, settle the spatial question and the simplest useful representation before choosing software. A plan, section, adjacency diagram, or rough massing model may answer more than a polished render. When 3D is useful, retain an editable source and consider a portable export such as glTF/GLB; choose the actual formats with the task.

Make physical geometry, infrastructure, and perceptual layers intelligible in their relationship. The [House of Worlds](../worlds/02-house-of-worlds.md) is now a megastructure purpose-built for shared embodied AR, as [Ren explicitly directed](../archive/008-ren-embodied-worlds-and-ar-megastructure.txt). Its proposed world halls, lift/portal groups, sensing, environmental systems, service strata, and material handling belong to one dedicated architecture. The [earlier mixed-use interpretation](../worlds/02-house-of-worlds-edition-03.md) and [edition-four megastructure proposal](../worlds/02-house-of-worlds-edition-04.md) are historical stages. Edition five uses three broad stacked halls, a left passenger core, a right service core, and recurring cloud-harbor anchors as one concept reference. Supporting rooms for rest, care, equipment, and unmediated movement serve the AR building’s primary purpose.

Where relevant, show an ordinary day, an unusual load or disagreement, and a failure of automated or mediated systems. Include a person who pauses, declines, or leaves the featured experience. These scenes should deepen understanding of the place while leaving its positive purpose visible.

Represent the culture making the place. A spacecraft hall can show public inquiry and the ambition joining generations; a quest city can show shared authorship and relationships that persist between festivals; a portal sequence can show people actually arriving in a different experienced world. Pair images with enough of the underlying understanding to explain how stories, institutions, and environments form one another. Give shared creation, learning, pleasure, and continuing becoming space alongside material dependencies and disagreement.

A substantial world chapter needs a connected visual journey. Use the [embodied-journey brief](../worlds/embodied-journeys.md) to show approach and arrival, a close interaction with people or materials, sustained inhabitation, and the supporting architecture and technology. The sequence should make a reader able to imagine their own body there: where a foot lands, what a hand meets, whom they encounter, and how the day continues. Let mood and human activity carry meaning alongside concise explanatory copy.

Coordinate establishing views, human viewpoints, and sections. Keep the same main halls, paths, cores, structural vocabulary, and physical anchors recognizable. For AR, identify the view convention and distinguish apparent distant scenery from real contact geometry; the engineered building must account for shared bodies, sensory equipment, air, sound, power, data, maintenance, and movement of actual materials. Do not imply that a render of a vast world creates physically unbounded floor area. The purpose-built megastructure is user direction; particular dimensions, systems, and performance remain proposals unless separately established.

A project overview should convey the plurality of worlds and the generative orientation that relates them. Avoid using one attractive garden scene as a substitute for the entire vision. Distinct chapters need purposeful imagery; check for accidental reuse of an image even when crops differ. An intentional comparison or matched AR-on/off pair should be explicitly identified and serve a named question.

It must also explain why these are practical directions to pursue. Show the existing technical starting point, productive equipment contributing to further equipment, the conditions under which capacity can compound, and how common access turns output into a generous life and means of creation. A production diagram should show both feedback loops—equipment contributing to further equipment, and operational learning spreading through public knowledge—and identify inputs and what becomes usable after commissioning; it must not disguise a missing dependency or invent a growth rate. Show how a person retains essentials and usable creative means when declining or leaving an activity, with remaining human work explicitly arranged. Link empirical statements to the bounded evidence notes, and keep the stronger engineering thesis distinct from what has actually been demonstrated. The production commons, cultural worlds, and regenerative material loops belong to the same civilization.

For material or architectural studies, preserve a view of the next use: how a component is reached, separated, assessed, stored, and made available again. Mark parts that cannot yet follow that route. Include the design/repair information needed by another maker, distinguishing a public design from a qualified physical part. Link the relevant choices to the [regenerative-material foundation](../foundations/regenerative-material-civilization.md).

Record dimensions as design choices or researched constraints, with units. An attractive concept is not evidence of structural, ecological, medical, or regulatory feasibility. Commission those investigations when the design depends on them.

Keep each significant revision traceable. Preserve abandoned alternatives when they explain a discovery; do not accumulate unexplained copies named `final-final`.

An edition’s planned counts and commissioned images belong in an “in preparation” status until the assembled export has been checked. Preserve the previous reviewed artifact while preparing a revision, and verify each image’s actual use once in the new PDF unless an explicitly labeled comparison requires repetition.

Provide a usable reading route alongside an image-led overview: complete narrative, meaningful image descriptions, and links that travel with the shared package. A companion supplements the atlas; the essential technical, generative, and material argument must remain legible within the atlas itself. Verify companion links in the actual exported package before calling that route complete.
