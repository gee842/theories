# Working in this project

This is Ren’s new worldbuilding project, explicitly requested in the conversation archived here. Its separate folder is intentional. Keep edits within this project unless the user asks to change the surrounding theories collection.

## Orientation

- Start with `README.md`, `foundations/charter.md`, and the recent entries in `process/decision-log.md`. For conceptual or representational work, read the connected foundations: `automation-to-shared-abundance.md`, `generative-worldmaking.md`, and `regenerative-material-civilization.md` under `foundations/`. Read relevant world, source, map, and evidence entries; avoid rereading the entire corpus by default.
- Preserve an inspired, concrete, hopeful exploration of possible life after compulsory economic labor, alongside careful treatment of physical constraints, power, uncertainty, and pluralism.
- Preserve the causal argument in overviews and revisions: existing AI and industrial knowledge → productive capacity contributing to further capacity → conditional compounding → generous shared provision and creative means → freedom from compelled/exploited labor → people constructing further generative systems. Free association is one resulting capability, not the whole theory of civilization. Do not replace the technical account with an unexplained assumption of abundance.
- Keep the meta level visible: inhabitants help author the stories, practices, institutions, and environments that in turn shape their understanding and conduct. Show the positive cultures and constructive feedback through which further lives, relationships, and undertakings become possible. Generativity concerns enabling environments; it is not compulsory personal creativity or a target to maximize the number of possibilities.
- Preserve the actual breadth of Ren's world vision. Rocket/exploration communities, persistent MMORPG-like cultures, and AR elevators experienced as portals belong alongside gardens, universities, expressive communities, and ordinary lives. Do not let one comfortable garden aesthetic stand for all of them.
- Follow Ren’s explicit AR clarification in `archive/008-ren-embodied-worlds-and-ar-megastructure.txt`: House of Worlds is a megastructure built specifically for embodied AR. World halls, passenger portals, sensory systems, and service space are designed around that purpose. The previous mixed-use interpretation is preserved in `worlds/02-house-of-worlds-edition-03.md`; do not silently restore it. Dedicated AR purpose is user direction; specific structural, optical, spatial, and operational choices remain design proposals.
- Treat the user’s instructions and continuing conversation as steering. Progress with reasonable reversible choices; do not add approval gates to ordinary drafting, research, or local modeling.

## Creating and connecting artifacts

- Use `templates/world-brief.md` for substantial world proposals and `templates/inquiry.md` for bounded investigations. Adapt them when the task benefits; completeness of thought matters more than filling every heading.
- Use stable node IDs in `foundations/possibility-map.json`; do not recycle or renumber existing IDs. Explain the relation and direction of each new edge. Keep the Markdown explanation consistent with the structured graph.
- Connect physical or visual artifacts to their world brief, dependencies, assumptions, and design question. Store editable sources with exports under `artifacts/`; follow `artifacts/README.md`.
- Give each featured world an embodied visual journey: approach and arrival, close human contact or activity, time spent inhabiting it, and an intelligible view of the architecture and technology that sustain it. Use `worlds/embodied-journeys.md` with the world brief. An establishing image plus a short descriptive paragraph is insufficient for a substantial friends-facing world overview when it leaves readers unable to imagine being there.
- Keep architecture coherent across views: major volumes, passenger and service routes, lift locations, physical contact surfaces, and recurring materials should correspond between the section and experienced scenes. In AR, distinguish the actual supporting geometry from rendered apparent extent and shared sensory effects. A bodily viewpoint should show real footing, reachable surfaces, and other people, with the device/view convention clear when it matters.
- Include ordinary life, accessibility, consent, maintenance, resource flows, and ways to refuse or leave when they matter to a design. Do not turn every scene into a checklist or make productivity a condition of belonging.
- Give cultures and shared ambitions enough positive detail to be inhabitable: knowledge, play, exploration, expression, friendship, rituals, and coauthorship should not be displaced by pages of generic guardrails. Explore how people can keep learning about the consequences of their own arrangements.
- Model uncertainty through variants and failure branches. Do not imply that automation alone produces equality, cultural harmony, or infinite resources.
- Explain productive recursion positively: added capacity can contribute to later additions, including relief of a limiting upstream process. Identify required inputs, maintenance, retirement, and commissioning. A conditional exponential model supplies no measured doubling time, and expanding material stock differs from circulation. Shared access must be built alongside productive capability for automation to become actual freedom.
- Carry Ren’s regenerative-material direction into relevant work: public knowledge, durable shared provision, maintainable and recoverable components, and freedom to reconfigure while sustaining the shared material stock. Distinguish desired recoverability from measured recovery, finite-stock circulation from net expansion, and material reuse from ecological regeneration. Treat standards and material-return arrangements as proposals that must preserve autonomy and diversity.
- Make drawings and renders legible as concepts; do not present them as engineering-validated designs. Do not add precise dates, dimensions, budgets, or probabilities without a reason and a stated basis.
- Record substantive choices and discoveries in `process/decision-log.md`, with their source and status. Preserve a concise project navigation path as files accumulate.

## Review

Check source attribution, links, graph references, and the actual behavior or appearance of an artifact where relevant. Use proportionate verification. Run `python3 process/validate.py` after changes to the archive, graph, or Markdown links; it verifies archive integrity against the captured manifest, not the historical accuracy of a claim. No software dependency stack is required for this initial document collection.

For an illustrated overview, check the complete technical-to-civilizational argument, breadth of worlds, and depth of embodied experience before treating the artifact as successful. Verify that each developed case lets a reader see arrival, inhabitation, and supporting engineering as a coherent place. A revision that restores one foundation must retain the others: available knowledge and engineering program, generous common means, generative mindset, material continuity, and lived cultures. Inspect every rendered page and check for accidental repeated images, including different crops of the same asset. Keep physical concepts, experienced overlays, and engineering claims distinguishable. Preserve significant earlier editions when revising a shared artifact.

A substantive review should follow at least one complete causal or lived sequence, not only confirm that themes are mentioned. Check that the reader can see productive and public-knowledge feedback, a changed commitment with secure access, a rule revised through its consequences, and material through successive uses when those are the revision’s aims. Give an image-led overview a complete readable companion with meaningful image descriptions and a verified sharing route; retain the essential argument within the atlas itself.

Report a new edition as in preparation until its actual exports, page count, image placements, complete copy, links, and rendered appearance have been checked. A plan for new images or a successful image-generation call is not a completed atlas. A visually condensed edition may retain an earlier fuller text edition as a companion; preserve the technical, generative, and material argument in the new artifact itself as well.
