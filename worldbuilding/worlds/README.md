# Worlds in development

These briefs develop **user-directed world concepts through AI-proposed architecture, cultures, and journeys**. Ren’s embodied-worlds direction establishes that the AR setting is a megabuilding constructed specifically for AR; the latest authorization asks for the reviewed artifact to be fixed and upgraded. Its detailed layout, hall types, materials, dimensions, sensory systems, and social arrangements remain proposals. The worlds explore life with broadly automated provision alongside partial-automation variants: starting points for spatial design and 3D creation, not engineered designs or demonstrated capabilities.

## Shared principles, different cultures

The worlds are ways to investigate how a civilization understands and shapes itself. Each asks what habits its arrangements cultivate, whose choices they amplify, what they make easier for the next person, and how inhabitants can correct an appealing story when its consequences depart from its purpose. Material security supports voluntary participation; public knowledge lets experiments become resources for others; recoverable construction supports change; consent and revision protect the variety of lives these powers should serve.

These principles do not prescribe one aesthetic, one life mission, or a ranking of residents by contribution. A quiet garden, a scientific expedition, an embodied imaginary world, and an uneventful afternoon can belong to the same generative civilization. Architectural images should make this range visible and connect a striking scene to the culture and systems producing it.

## Material continuity across worlds

The September 7 expansion asks how civilization could preserve material usefulness while continually changing its forms. See [Regenerative material civilization](../foundations/regenerative-material-civilization.md) for the shared foundation. The briefs translate this user direction into **AI-proposed spatial arrangements**: convenient return points, repair rooms, inspectable component stores, public design libraries, and routes for recovery as well as delivery. Shared provision coexists with private possessions and long attachments.

Each next blockout follows something through use, return, inspection, repair or preparation, storage, and another use. It also shows where that path fails, where material leaves the local system, and who decides whether change proceeds. Reuse capacity is a design question; energy, losses, contamination, transport, and time remain visible. Ecological recovery needs its own account. Faster construction expands both creative agency and the capacity to damage a place; the ability to keep something matters alongside the ability to transform it.

The [possibility map](../foundations/possibility-map.md) keeps all six world IDs stable. The Expressive Commons develops the former `W04` seed into its own way of life, distinct from the House’s dedicated AR purpose. A neighborhood with no shared mission remains a worthwhile further branch.

Use the [world-brief template](../templates/world-brief.md) to extend or challenge them. Before modeling, declare a site hypothesis and assumptions. Attractive imagery does not settle resource allocation, consent, maintenance, or governance.
