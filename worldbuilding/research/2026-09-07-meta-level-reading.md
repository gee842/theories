## What the new foundation contributes

The wording, six-part organization, causal-loop expression, library/friendship examples, and learning scenes are new assistant synthesis. They connect source ideas rather than purport to transcribe a previously finished Ren essay.

The foundation imports three distinctions that matter to the artifact: inhabiting a game versus sharing responsibility for its conditions; producing something versus sustaining an environment from which further things can arise; greater capability versus wiser consequences. It keeps correction and consent within a positive account of making worlds. It does not reduce the vision to defensive administration or require every inhabitant to perform personal generativity.

Empirical work remains needed to establish particular behavioral expectations, mechanisms of shared provision, automation capabilities, and the material feasibility of any proposed architecture. Philosophical provenance shows why these questions belong in the project; it does not answer them by itself.
