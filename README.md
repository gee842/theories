# Ren 的理论：成果与进度 / Ren’s theories: results and progress

我们正在建立一套**一元、关系性的生成理论**：活动形成并改变后续活动的条件，组织、生命、认识、学习、关怀与智慧在同一展开中分化。它既解释建设性与破坏性的过程，也追问生生为什么具有伦理意义，以及这种意义如何成为实践理由。

We are developing a **monist, relational theory of generativity**: activity forms and changes the conditions of further activity, within which organization, life, knowing, learning, concern and wisdom differentiate. The account investigates constructive and destructive processes, ethical significance and practical reasons.

**当前成果：有条件的统一基础已通过既定五项完成审查；普遍规范根据、无假设基础和唯一必要性仍在研究。** Ren 已批准从明确的生成伦理起点继续推导情境理由；这条路线及起点自身的更强辩护各有完成范围。更新：2026-10-08。

**Current position: the conditional unified foundation meets its five reviewed completion criteria. Universal normative warrant, assumption-free grounding and exclusive necessity remain open.** Ren has approved continued derivation of situated reasons from an explicit generative ethical starting point; that route and the stronger justification of its premise retain separate scopes. Updated: 2026-10-08.

## 目前继续探索什么 / Current research frontier

1. **普遍规范根据，第一优先。** 研究整体生成及实际生命为何有正面伦理意义，怎样形成差异化实践理由；继续[获批准的显式起点路线](sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-generative-premise-route-approved.md)，保留起点的独立辩护。 / **Universal normative warrant:** explain ethical quality and differentiated reasons while developing the approved explicit-premise route and its stronger grounding.
2. **真实冲突与回应范围。** 研究目的变化、参与退出、照料、承诺、损失和建设性结束，并校准形成的辨别。 / **Conflict and response:** investigate changed purposes, participation, care, commitment, loss and constructive endings, including calibration.
3. **数学与现实的普遍范围。** 独立解释更丰富数学范围的接纳与真实生命的依存，以能改变解释的对应和实验推进。 / **Mathematical and empirical scope:** warrant richer domains and real living dependencies through discriminating tests.
4. **实践与治理。** 将研究用于选择制度、合作供给、社区软件与生活环境，说明每项应用怎样检验共同理论。 / **Practice and governance:** test the account through institutions, shared provision, community software and living environments.

[完整路线](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/results/ROADMAP.md)区分已有成果、正在探索与建议下一步。普遍规范有效、实际遵循和当前表述的唯一必要性需要各自的论证。

The [roadmap](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/results/ROADMAP.md) distinguishes achievements, ongoing inquiry and proposed tests. Universal validity, actual compliance and exclusive necessity require separate arguments.

## 阅读与研究方法 / Reading and research method

遵循[非二元方法](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md)：从同一生成展开说明角色、边界、认识和评价的分化，保留实际差异、冲突与损失。中英文同为主要语言；Ren 原话、AI 重构、证明、经验结果与猜想分别标注。生生的正面方向研究更多生命、可能性、多样性与复杂组织的发展，不预先把“生”改成另行判定为好的事物，也不预设每步单调增长或标量最大化。

Follow the [nondual method](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md): explain roles, boundaries, knowing and evaluation within one unfolding, retaining difference, conflict and loss. Chinese and English are primary languages; quotations, reconstructions, proofs, evidence and conjectures are attributed separately. The positive generative direction does not redefine 生 as what was independently judged good or require stepwise growth or scalar maximization.
