2. **实践判断的实际应用。** 一般公理候选已覆盖真实冲突、四种回应与责任；下一应用校准基本条件、依存、共同负担和权限。 / **Practical judgment in application:** general principles now cover conflict, the four responses and responsibility; next calibrate basic conditions, dependence, joint burdens and authority in actual practice.
3. **数学与现实的普遍范围。** 独立解释更丰富数学范围的接纳与真实生命的依存，以能改变解释的对应和实验推进。 / **Mathematical and empirical scope:** warrant richer domains and real living dependencies through discriminating tests.
4. **实践与治理。** 将研究用于选择制度、合作供给、社区软件与生活环境，说明每项应用怎样检验共同理论。 / **Practice and governance:** test the account through institutions, shared provision, community software and living environments.

## 阅读与研究方法 / Reading and research method

遵循[非二元方法](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md)：从同一生成展开说明角色、边界、认识和评价的分化，保留实际差异、冲突与损失。中英文同为主要语言；Ren 原话、AI 重构、证明、经验结果与猜想分别标注。生生的正面方向研究更多生命、可能性、多样性与复杂组织的发展，不预先把“生”改成另行判定为好的事物，也不预设每步单调增长或标量最大化。

Follow the [nondual method](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/NONDUAL-METHOD.md): explain roles, boundaries, knowing and evaluation within one unfolding, retaining difference, conflict and loss. Chinese and English are primary languages; quotations, reconstructions, proofs, evidence and conjectures are attributed separately. The positive generative direction does not redefine 生 as what was independently judged good or require stepwise growth or scalar maximization.
