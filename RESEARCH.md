## The central research line

**Latest advance:** [physical turnover](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/RENEWAL-RETURN.md) now derives paid replacement of all original carriers and a conditional repair-kernel improvement, while proving a sharp failure boundary. [Fabrication machinery](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-CRITIQUE.md) can form from absence under one fixed law; useful delivery still need not favor useful copying. A [reversible thermodynamic embedding](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/THERMODYNAMIC-EMBEDDING.md) preserves the earlier one-capture gain with explicit finite fuel stocks. Specification v0.5 integrates these reviewed conditional results and [direction limits](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/DIRECTION-AND-TURNOVER.md). Their different material/source assumptions must still be combined in one model; useful retention, physical identification and universal direction remain open.

**Preceding advance:** [paid allocation control](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ALLOCATION-CONTROL.md) proves a finite-rate class in which construction from the same supported zero-gate state improves joint access to fresh support, after an early construction disadvantage. The [continuation assay](experiments/shared-renewal-capability/continuation/README.md) retains the old regulator's repeated-delivery failures. The [cellular baseline](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/RESOURCE-ALLOCATION-BASELINE.md) supplies an established resource-allocation comparison.

**Formalization-led completion:** the [common specification](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/FORMAL-SPECIFICATION.md) and [practical completion plan](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/COMPLETION-PLAN.md) connect physical renewal, joint capability, derived stochastic transitions and actual formation. The [integration record](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/INTEGRATION.md) tracks completed contributions and active turns awaiting review. This is ongoing research; the universal positive objective remains open.

The new [mechanism-to-capability bridge](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/JOINT-CAPABILITY-BRIDGE.md) derives joint probabilities directly from the shared-renewal event law. It proves positive finite-time construction effects and computes incomparable installed capability regions at a common reliability. The [completed joint-formation review](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/JOINT-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md) accepts the strategic formation theorem with its physical and equilibrium limits.

The [physical regulator extension](experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/README.md) now pays for formation, binding, wear and replacement within that process. Its first prespecified comparison is negative: construction costs overwhelm the proposed regulator's benefits in every tested comparison with the absent-regulator arm. A correct sensing response and a stronger energy source do not by themselves establish joint generative advantage. The [concern-settlement source review](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONCERN-SETTLEMENT-INTEGRATION.md) separately accepts the completed strategic contribution and preserves the unrun empirical-study status.

The [opportunity audit](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REGULATION-OPPORTUNITY-AUDIT.md) and [independently checked response diagnosis](experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/response/README.md) explain why useful regulation needs a specified productive conflict. Recycling stores usable material; less free fuel alone is not less later production. The [full biological feedback audit](_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/PHYSICAL-EVIDENCE-CERONI.md) supplies an external burden-and-relief mechanism, including costly failure designs and source-data limitations. Next are delivered-continuation assays, a physically derived gain/failure boundary and an identified comparison with conventional resource-allocation models; universality remains open.

The candidate foundation describes activity changing the conditions of further activity. Its ethical development asks how another being's condition becomes a reason within a process, how that reason can learn and widen, and how effective concern survives costs, obstruction, and succession. Supporting conditions, recognition, valuation, justified standing, operational influence, and actual protection have distinct roles.

The completed studies supply conditional mechanisms and counterexamples. They do not establish a universal tendency toward wiser or more inclusive regeneration. The current [theory overview and genealogy](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/README.md) keeps that distinction explicit.
