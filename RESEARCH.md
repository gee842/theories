## The central research line

**Governing research position:** [generative grounding](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) develops a universal account for system-forming reality from minimal properties of consequential differences, structured interaction and composition. Organization, learning, significance and concern are phenomena to explain within it. Positive anchors and favorable institutions remain local model assumptions, not foundational givens. Universal explanatory scope is the research target; it does not assert inevitable moral progress or a completed universal proof.

**The goal we are currently pursuing:** identify the smallest adequate generative foundation and derive its substantive consequences, with actual conditions made explicit. The intended progression connects interacting differences, organization, significance, learning and selection over selectors, models of shared futures, concern, and wisdom. Each transition must explain how the relevant capacity develops and when it fails. The [derivation map](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/unified-2026-10-05/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#the-derivation-and-the-existing-work) places the current studies within that task. A connected derivation remains open; completing a supplied game or one renewal mechanism does not finish it.

### Significance, learning and concern

The candidate foundation describes activity changing the conditions of further activity. Its ethical development asks how another being's condition becomes a reason within a process, how that reason can learn and widen, and how effective concern survives costs, obstruction, and succession. Supporting conditions, recognition, valuation, justified standing, operational influence, and actual protection have distinct roles. Their origins and connections are central derivation tasks under the governing position above.

The completed studies supply conditional mechanisms and counterexamples. They do not establish a universal tendency toward wiser or more inclusive regeneration. The current [theory overview and genealogy](theories/shengsheng-buxi-generative-existence/README.md) keeps that distinction explicit.
