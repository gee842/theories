## Title

**Generative Chiang Mai 2045: A City of Many Beginnings**

Shared abundance, mutual care and a regenerative culture give diverse people and communities the means to cultivate a flourishing world together.

## World / Situation / Scenario, 2045

By 2045, AI, robotics and clean energy sustain shared abundance. Climate change is being brought under control, and ecosystems are recovering. Society has shifted from extraction, consumption and waste toward generative, regenerative living. Chiang Mai embraces shared prosperity and mutual care: everyone is provided for, care extends beyond one's own circle, and diverse communities cultivate a world where life can keep flourishing.

## Adaptability / Flourishability

Automated public infrastructure gives everyone the means to learn, create, care and explore. Diverse communities share discoveries while keeping different ways of life. Materials circulate, knowledge remains open, and human activity renews the ecosystems it depends on. Generativity becomes a shared practice: sustaining the conditions for others and future generations to flourish. People can rest or change direction without losing necessities.

## Becoming / How This Capability Grew

Residents, cooperatives, universities and public services pooled resources to build common capacity. Automation expanded provision; open designs travelled globally and evolved locally. Schools and shared projects cultivated an understanding of interdependence. People learned to judge progress by the life it enabled, the damage it repaired and what it left for others. Care, repair and regeneration became ordinary civic practices.

## Story

*As told from 2045, looking back.*

- **2026-2029 | Planting the seeds.** We began by asking what kind of life our city made possible. Shared kitchens, repair spaces, gardens and watershed projects became places to practise cooperation. We learned how our habits, institutions and technologies shape the lives around us.
- **2030-2034 | Building common capacity.** We connected clean power, automated growing, fabrication and delivery. Factories supplied robots and parts for further factories; public investment expanded shared provision. Growing capacity supported clean energy, carbon removal and ecosystem restoration. Designs and lessons circulated between cities.
- **2035-2038 | Abundance changes everyday life.** Good homes, food, care and transport became universally accessible. Shared workshops and public knowledge gave people the means to create together. Materials returned to use, buildings became adaptable, and streets became gardens. Care for our common world entered everyday learning and practice.
- **2039-2042 | Many worlds take root.** Lanna craft guilds, contemplative communities, open universities, forest gardens, AR story realms and space exploration collectives flourished. People moved between them and shared discoveries. Different pursuits enriched a common life; the freedom of each rested on the flourishing of others.
- **2043-2045 | An ordinary abundant day.** Climate recovery is underway. Beside the regenerating Ping River, a child follows a question to an open laboratory; an elder teaches weaving after breakfast. Robots maintain the common infrastructure. People garden, invent, love, play or rest. Our city keeps renewing the conditions for life.
