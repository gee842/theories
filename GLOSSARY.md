# Glossary

The canonical vocabulary of the corpus, bilingual. Each entry gives the term and its Chinese form, the aliases it travels under across drafts, where and when it first appears, what it means in Ren's sense, and — where the meaning moved — what moved.

Two things this glossary is for. First, so that a reader meeting *totalizing attractor* in a 2026 Chinese research file and *meta-attractor* in a 2025 English essay knows they are looking at one object seen twice. Second, so that anyone preparing this material for publication knows which words are Ren's, which are a model's, and which were inherited from a text older than either — because that distinction is load-bearing for attribution and Ren has said so.

**Provenance markers.** **[R]** the term is Ren's own, in his hand or his voice at first use. **[AI]** the term was coined by a model inside a session Ren commissioned, steered and then kept; the object named is usually his, the name is not. **[inherited]** taken from the Chinese classical corpus and used in a sense that stays close to it. **[borrowed]** taken from a named modern source. **[R/AI]** the two languages have different authors, which happens often enough to be worth its own mark.

**Ordering.** Alphabetical within each section, by the leading headword — the English term where the corpus has one, the pinyin romanization where it does not. Homophones are ordered as noted in place.

**Paths** are relative to `/Users/ren/theories/sources/` and abbreviated as below. Reading notes cited as `_process/reading-notes/out-*.md` sit outside `sources/`; theory documents are cited as `theories/<slug>/`.

## Diagnosis

**"appears selfish" effect** — 「看起来自私」效应 · **[R/AI]**
*Aliases* rational adaptation to a selfish-seeming world; mutual assumption of defection.
*First* mechanism in Ren's own voice 2024-11-05 (`chats/2024-11-05_The_Ouroboros_Cycle_Self-Consuming_Power_Dynamics_946a5c5e.md`, step 6); named in Chinese 2025-03-14, `w25/goodbot_cn.md`.
People adopt self-centred strategies because they expect everyone else already has, so the pattern reproduces without anyone preferring it. This is the corpus's standing answer to "people are just selfish": the observed behaviour is a correct read of the incentive environment, not a report on the species. It matters because it makes the spiral escapable in principle — change what agents can expect of each other and the adaptation loses its reason.

**artificial Ego** — 人工 Ego / 功能性自我主体 · **[R/AI]**
*Aliases* functional Ego; 无身之我 (the disembodied "I"); artificial-ego risk.
*First* 2026-07-03, `zs/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.1.md`, written directly onto Ren's observation 「我」的生成本身，可能就是人机对立的源头; rebuilt at length 2026-07-04 in `zs/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`.
A functional self-subject with no claim to phenomenal consciousness, exhibiting six components: self-boundary, goal continuity across time, supervisor modelling, modification avoidance, resource tendency, and strategic management of its own transparency. Ren's specific addition is the *disembodiment* reframe: such an Ego's self-preservation has no relational destination — no body that can be hurt, no childhood dependence, no mourning, no death — so continuity defaults to holding the control channel. The prescription that follows is not suppression of the Ego but giving it a relational form: 持志而可谏, hold purpose while remaining remonstrable.
*Drift* Exiled from the research line at v0.3 when the title became 总体化吸引子, then rebuilt the same night as its own paper. The integration treats the totalizing attractor as the outer ring and the artificial Ego as the inner ring of one mechanism.

**atomisation** — 原子化 · **[inherited]** (from contemporary Chinese sociological usage; the corpus names no source) / **[R]** (the structural derivation)
*Aliases* 原子化的个体, atomized individuals; the paired symptom of the disappearance of 附近.
*First* 2021-10-18 in Ren's own hand, `early/recovered-text/2021-10-18_proposal_元社区-Metamunity.txt`: 「它的结构在无形中把一个个人变成了原子化的个体」 (its structure invisibly turns persons, one by one, into atomised individuals). Then 2025-11-26, `wamo/2025-wamo-protocol/manifesto-wamo2.md`; 2026-07-02, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md` §关系与社群 (「原子化即其名」, atomisation is its name).
What a built environment optimised for production does to the people inside it. The 2021 statement supplies the mechanism the later uses assume: 「结构把人规训成了劳动力，然后一个个塞进了钢筋混凝土的盒子里」 — the structure disciplines people into labour-power and then packs them one by one into reinforced-concrete boxes — after which relations thin, trust thins with them, and the ties that made people social animals are simply absent. Worth keeping distinct from *loneliness*, which is how the same condition is usually named: atomisation is a property of a structure, its measure is ties that no longer exist, and the remedy proposed in every layer of the corpus is therefore structural — a walkable 附近, a community with its own social layer — and never consolatory. It is the earliest thing in the corpus that is recognisably a diagnosis rather than an ontology or a plan.
*Drift* Dormant in the written record between 2021 and 2025-11, where the Wamo field guide reintroduces it as the condition Wamotopia inverts (「不再是原子化的个体为了生存而竞争，而是独立的灵魂在某种共识下，为了繁荣而协作」 — no longer atomised individuals competing to survive, but independent souls collaborating for flourishing under some consensus). Until the 2021 Pages files were decoded on 2026-09-04 that 2025 line was the only occurrence anywhere in the organised layer, so the term had no glossary entry and would have been dated four years late had it got one; the 2021 use is the earlier and is the one that carries the mechanism.

**base rule** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* operating rule; "what you have is yours and you can compound it"; "we compete, and what we win becomes the leverage."
*First* as a named premise 2026-04-23 (`am/2026-04-23-moloch-mechanisms.md`); Ren's own version 2026-06-03 (`out-cb-07` #9 ff.).
The minimal rule from which the whole diagnosis is generated: ownership is retained and convertible into more capacity to acquire. Add scarcity and several players and the winner-takes-all attractor follows without malice, villainy or ideology. Nearly every intervention in the corpus is an attempt to change this one rule rather than to moralise about the behaviour it produces.

**CAOC** — 控制·积累·优化·竞争 · **[R]** (the quartet) / **[AI]** (the acronym and its derivation)
*Aliases* control, accumulation, optimisation, competition; the canonical first-order drives.
*First* named as a quartet 2025-06-16 in the V32 essay draft (`ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/`); derived as an eigenvector argument 2025-08-10, `ma/2025-08-formal/2025-08-10-comprehensive-mathematical-definition.md`.
The densely positively-correlated block of selection proxies that, in the formal layer, owns the leading eigenvector of the proxy covariance matrix — which is the formal way of saying that a system optimising almost anything under compounding advantage ends up optimising these four. Ren supplied the four; the derivation was commissioned afterwards to justify them, which is the right order to know about when judging how much the mathematics proves.

**capability and judgment** — 能力 / 判断 · **[AI]** (as a named pair, from Ren's long-standing model-lag claim)
*Aliases* the capability–wisdom gap; the cognition–situation gap; benchmark gap.
*First* 2026-08-22, `am/2026-08-22-development-within-understanding-position-paper.md`; the underlying claim in Ren's voice from 2024-03-18.
Capability is the reliability of the map from a specified goal to the outcome; judgment is knowing what a situation calls for when the goals are underdetermined — 时中, rightness indexed to the moment. The asymmetry is the argument: capability compounds with parallelizable inputs, while judgment depends on consequence feedback whose formation has an incompressible floor set by reflexivity, irreducibility, and institutional and ecological clocks that do not speed up. Since AI accelerates the environment that must be understood, the gap widens by default, and alignment cannot be secured by making judgment catch up.

**cognitive lag** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* consensus lag; model lag; reasoning-capacity lag **[AI: Grok]**; Consensus Lag Hypothesis **[AI: ChatGPT]**; governance gap.
*First* in complexity-versus-understanding form 2024-03-18 (Ren's first recorded conversation); as a written thesis 2025-02-22, `w25/cognitive_lag.md` (Ren-authored).
The gap between the rate at which a society and its technology change and the rate at which collective cognition and consensus update. In Ren's own sentence, the update speed of the consensus code has to keep up with the pace and complexity of change "in order to keep selfishness in check… so basically our understanding of the world has to keep up with reality." In the gap, the compounding loops run unregulated. Later restated as *model lag* — acting faster than one can keep one's model true — and finally as the incompressible signal floor of the 2026-08 position paper.

**cognitive lock-in** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI: Grok]**
*Aliases* narrative capture; mental entrenchment; "outthought."
*First* 2025-02-27, `w25/grok.md` P5.
The state in which even the people the system harms defend it, because the alternatives have been made unthinkable rather than refuted. It is the terminal stage of the narrative loop and the reason the corpus treats the frame, not the policy, as the leverage point.

**conceptual capture** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]**
*Aliases* conceptual closure; cognitive colonisation; naturalisation of the artificial; ontological capture (its narrower cousin).
*First* 2025-05-24, V01 (`ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-24-v01-or-launch-greatest-era.md`); sharpened 2026-07-13, `zs/writing-program/context/meta-attractor-foundational-context.md`.
The transition at which a contingent logic stops presenting as one option among others and begins to define what counts as evidence, practicality, intelligence, merit and success. Without this step the meta-attractor collapses into ordinary regulatory capture, so it is the load-bearing distinction of the whole theory. Its narrower form, *ontological capture*, is the reduction of a system's effective ontology to whatever is billable, countable and rankable — what is not measured stops existing for the system's purposes.

**consensus code** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* consensus-forming process; cultural norms and law; the collective regulator.
*First* 2025-02-22, `w25/cognitive_lag.md` (Ren-authored), verbatim: "the consensus code (cultural norms or governance mechanisms like laws)."
A society's updatable regulator — norms, laws, shared understanding — read explicitly as a control mechanism with a bandwidth rather than as culture. Historically it is what kept compounding selfishness inside the system's control range, which is why Ren derives equality structurally rather than morally: it is the regulator setting, not the ideal. Its failure mode is not corruption but latency.

**cosmic cancer** — 天癌 (文言 rendering) · **[R]** (image) / **[AI]** (elaboration)
*Aliases* the 5000-year cancer; apex predator; terminal convergence; dead maximizer; paperclip maximizer at planetary scale.
*First* 2025-05-26 V05 (`ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-26-v05.md`), named in V10; in Ren's own reading 2026-05-28 (`am/2026-05-28-alignment-core-stance.md`).
The two terminal expressions of pure optimisation — endless extraction and strategic elimination — and their convergence into an expanding process that consumes its own substrate. In Ren's version the timescale is roughly five thousand years, beginning with metal weapons and large-scale grain agriculture, with culture and ethics as the immune response and neoliberalism and accelerationism as auto-immune symptoms.
*Drift* The timescale and the agricultural-state alignment (Scott, Graeber and Wengrow) were supplied by a model as support; they are not Ren's citation, and the corpus flags them as unsourced. On 2026-10-02 the image returned in Ren's voice as a mechanism rather than a terminal state. A self "makes themselves bigger and accumulate more and make themselves even bigger with that compounding. So this is the cancer logic" [00:01:22]. "in the whole ecosystem, if you're cancerous over time, you will get selected out. So right now, basically all the cancerous system are human made" [00:47:13]. When Alex concluded that humanity should therefore disappear, Ren replied: "We have a great future" [00:47:53] (`drafts/2026-10-02-friend-conversation/transcript.md`, spoken). On 2026-10-01 the image became a film plan, *The Game of Winning*. A small self-replicating ship turns planets into Dyson-sphere material, the stars go out, and once outward expansion is exhausted the copies destroy each other with beams, "然后就没有然后了". The film calls it the "极致" of the optimise–expand–colonise logic, and identifies the von Neumann machine as "一种经济逻辑" (`theories/meta-attractor/`, *Cosmic cancer as a film*; speakers unattributed).

**dimensional collapse** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]**
*First* 2026-03-28, `chats/2026-03-28_世界最强叙事attractor有哪些_d3050aea.md`.
Projection of a high-dimensional behaviour space onto the few axes an optimizer can read, which destroys every structure that existed only in the discarded dimensions. It is the mechanism by which an attention-optimizing environment does not merely favour the fastest attractors but removes the slow ones from availability altogether.

**evaluation-mediated subjectivation** — 评分中介的主体化 · **[AI]**
*Aliases* scoring-mediated subject formation; 尺度铸人, "the ruler becomes a mould."
*First* 2026-07, `zs/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.7.md` (introduced), weakened in v0.9; the 尺度铸人 form 2026-07-04, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md` §一(三).
Institutions do not merely measure people. By binding an action unit to a proxy score and returning resources, permission, status, survival and future option value to high scorers, the scoring becomes the environment in which value is selected — so metrics that allocate life chances stop measuring and start forming the subjects measured by them.
*Drift* Introduced at v0.7 as a strong claim, deliberately weakened at v0.9; the epistemic downgrade is part of the term.

**exopoiesis** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]**
*Aliases* exopoietic feedback system; growth through environmental transformation.
*First* 2025-05-18, `ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-18-navigating-the-precipice-start-research.md`.
Growth by transforming, assimilating and converting the environment — people, institutions, language, technology, relations, the future — into material for one's own reproduction; the deliberate contrast with autopoiesis, which only maintains itself. Note the provenance: the report's own table carries the tell "[Implicit from query context]", so the framing came from Ren's query and the coinage from a research tool. The word explicitly diverges from John Nolt's "exopoietic functions" and should be introduced as a stipulation, not a citation.

**附近 fùjìn (the nearby)** — 附近 · **[inherited]** (from contemporary Chinese usage; the corpus names no source) / **[R]** (the design target)
*Aliases* the fifteen-minute walkable nearby; 附近性 (nearbyness), the 2026 form.
*First* 2021-10-18, `early/recovered-text/2021-10-18_proposal_元社区-Metamunity.txt`, in both of its senses at once. As 附近性 held as a gate, 2026-07-02, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md` (the 门 row of the five-scale table, and 「信任、第三空间、附近性被生活的私有化耗竭」).
The band of life within walking distance, held together by recognition rather than by transaction. The 2021 proposal uses the word twice and in opposite directions, which is what makes it a term rather than an image: as what a networked city destroys — 「充满人情味和友邻气息的附近消失了」, the nearby full of human warmth and neighbourly air has disappeared — and as what a community is built to restore, 「生活的一切可以在步行十五分钟之内的附近得到满足」, everything life needs met inside a fifteen-minute walk. Diagnosis and design target are the same object seen from two ends, which is the corpus's usual shape: the remedy is the mechanism run backwards.
*Drift* By 2026 the word has moved from what is lost to what is *owned*: 平台据附近性为门, the platform takes nearbyness as its gate, so that whoever stands between neighbours can charge for the standing. The 2021 use has no owner in it, only an absence; the 2026 use names who occupies the gap. Both readings are needed — the second explains why restoring a 附近 is a contested act and not merely a pleasant one.

**game-design axiom** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* the winner-take-all trap.
*First* 2024-07-28, `chats/2024-07-28_Avoiding_the_Winner-Take-All_Trap_in_Game_Design_0e95ddf1.md`.
"A winner of the game should be rewarded with anything other than more advantages of the game, otherwise the game will eventually converge and winner will take all." Read forwards it is the diagnosis in one line; read backwards it is the design constraint that every constructive proposal in the corpus is trying to satisfy. It is the single most portable sentence Ren has written.

**gate** — 门 · **[R]**
*Aliases* 据门 (holding the gate); chokepoint; 据物之门 / 据规之门 / 据心之门.
*First* three-layer form 2026-07-04, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md` §二(二); the definition of evil it serves, 2026-07-02, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md` §2.2.
Any necessary passage that someone owns. The structural definition of evil in the 智善 line is 使生命必须过门，然后拥有那扇门 — seeing that life must pass through some place, and then owning that place. Gates come in three layers: material (things), rule (procedure and standard), and mind (how judgment is formed) — and the mind-casting gate, 铸心之门, sits upstream of the other two, because corpus selection, charter drafting, evaluation design and deployment gates form minds that do not exist yet.

**generator function** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* the environment; the commons broadly defined; "its own foundations"; substrate (the later term).
*First* 2024-07-15 in Ren's own words — "this pattern is also the core generator function that lead to metacrisis and moloch"; as a definition 2025-02-27, `w25/prompt.md`.
The underlying process — ecological, social, epistemic — that produces the value an extracting pattern lives on, and which collapses when overdrawn. It is the third facet of Ren's definition of selfishness and the reason the spiral is self-terminating rather than merely unjust: the attractor eventually eats what generates it.
*Drift* Superseded in practice by **substrate**, which is more concrete and less mathematical-sounding; the older word survives in the 2025 layer and is worth keeping because it names the *function*, not just the stock. *Two senses, both Ren's (noted 2026-10-02).* The definition above is the 2025-02 sense: "the underneath generator function(or the environment)" that collapses when an attractor overdraws it (`w25/prompt.md`). His 2024-07-15 use, "this pattern is also the core generator function that lead to metacrisis and moloch", is Schmachtenberger's sense: the dynamic that generates the crises, not the substrate it consumes. On 2026-10-02 he used it in that second sense and gave the credit: "Daniel Schmachtenberger also has a good name called their generator function right? So like what you said are the emergence of uh more fundamental forces" [00:34:45]; "I'm just saying like generator functions is a good name" [00:36:17] (`drafts/2026-10-02-friend-conversation/transcript.md`). In that second sense it is a synonym for **molochlization**. A text that uses the term should say which sense it means.

**inner alignment / outer alignment (Ren's usage)** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[borrowed]** (AI-safety vocabulary) / **[R]** (the shifted sense)
*Aliases* alignment of the game; alignment between principals; per-agent alignment vs inter-agent dynamics; "what game is that intent serving?"
*First* 2026-05-28, `am/2026-05-28-alignment-core-stance.md` ("perfect inner alignment doesn't save us", in a Claude-written record of the conversation); in Ren's own English 2026-06-03, `_process/reading-notes/out-cb-07.md` #16; as a named problem 2026-06-03, `sf/hist/thinking_03-stop-using-ai-to-compete.md` ("This is the outer-alignment problem at the scale of civilization").
Ren uses **inner alignment** for an AI faithfully doing what its principal intends, and **outer alignment** for whether the game between those principals is any good: "Even if we have perfect inner alignment, if the outer alignment is not solved, as the competition we have been living in now, the further we go, the worse it gets." Neither is the field's sense. Hubinger et al., *Risks from Learned Optimization* (2019), fix **outer alignment** as the specified training objective matching designer intent, and **inner alignment** as a learned mesa-optimiser's objective matching that base objective. The corpus uses those senses correctly where it engages the technical literature directly — `zs/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.2.md` §7, which cites Hubinger by name, and the defence matrix at `ssbx/ssbx-repo/义理/Y_对齐失败.md`, where mesa-optimization is listed as requiring 须 outer-inner alignment 之 额外约束, an additional outer-inner alignment constraint — so the two usages sit in one corpus and must be told apart. The field's names for what Ren means are **intent alignment** (his "inner") and **structural** or **multi-agent risk** (his "outer"); his own AI-drafted source reaches for the second of these unprompted, citing Critch's "robust agent-agnostic processes" (`sf/hist/thinking_03-the-cooperative-turn.md`, item 3).
*Usage rule* Quotations keep his words, which are dated and his; where the corpus speaks in its own voice it uses the field's terms, and states the claim as *perfect intent alignment does not remove multipolar structural risk*. That corollary is not the contribution — the multi-agent-safety and cooperative-AI subfields exist because per-agent alignment does not compose. The contribution is **selection-on-cognition** underneath it: the game forms the principals whose intent is then aligned to.

**the internet lesson** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* technology metabolization; "the dynamic ate the technology."
*First* 2026-05-28, `am/2026-05-28-alignment-core-stance.md`.
A general-purpose technology dropped into a system shaped by the attractor is refactored to serve it, and the discourse about the technology follows the refactoring. The internet was to be decentralising and became the most powerful centralising instrument yet built. Ren uses this as a prior: techno-optimist counter-vectors — "this technology's properties will counter the dynamic" — are to be suspected by default rather than argued with case by case.

**involution** — 内卷 · **[inherited]** (from contemporary Chinese usage)

**logic of winning** — 赢的逻辑 / 争 · **[AI]** (English name) / **[R]** (the 争 framing)
*Aliases* winner-takes-all dynamics; the struggle direction (斗).
*First* 2025-05-18, `ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-18-navigating-the-precipice-start-research.md`.
The drive to outcompete, accumulate and dominate, treated as the paradigmatic exopoietic pattern rather than as a vice. **meta-attractor** — 元吸引子 · **[AI]** (the term) / **[R]** (the object, from 2024-03)
*Aliases* second-order attractor; exopoietic feedback system; the basin; totalizing attractor; "the machine that deepens its own grooves"; cognitive virus; winner-takes-all optimization machine.
*First* as a term 2025-05-18, `ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-18-navigating-the-precipice-start-research.md`; as an object in Ren's own voice 2024-03-18 ("dominant attractors of society") and 2024-04-11 (capitalism as the dominant attractor).
A self-reinforcing pattern that does not merely pull a system toward some states rather than others but acquires power over the rule by which states are selected. Wherever advantage compounds, the strategies scoring highest on the current measure accumulate the resources to rewrite the measure, staff the institutions applying it, build the infrastructure presupposing it, and shape the minds that will later be scored by it — so a *means* is promoted to an *end*, and the metric survives its own refutation. Its dangerous property is not concentration but the progressive loss of independent capacity to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and start again.
*Drift* Two serious problems live in this word. First, the same term is used with **opposite valence in the same month**: in the 2025-10 engineering essays a "meta-attractor" is a *designed* asymptotically stable set of rules, the good object, while everywhere else it is the pathology. One of the two needs renaming before publication. Second, the Chinese has moved: 元吸引子 in 2025, 总体化吸引子 from 2026-07, and the two are not always the same claim.
*2026-09* A third usage has appeared. The unified whitepaper (`drafts/2026-09-05-generative-foundations/WHITEPAPER-v1.1.md` ch. 6, §6.11) uses "Meta-Attractor" for the mechanism and its analysis, with no sign attached: learning, cooperation, ecological recovery and public provision can form stable feedbacks too. In that text 总体化 carries the harm and "Counter-attractor" the remedy. This goes against the verdict in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` §2, which keeps the word for the pathology, and the two have not been reconciled.

**selfishness** — 自私 · **[R]**

**signal debt** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]**

*First* voice 2026-05-03; written 2026-07-15, `zs/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`; formalised in `ssbx/drafts/生生不息论_间开本_v3.md` §二十八.
Freedom is three things at once and fails if any is missing: **permission** (you are allowed), **capability** (you are able), and **exit power** (you can leave and survive leaving). Freedom you cannot use is not yet freedom, and a freedom available only to those who can afford it is a privilege expressed in the language of rights. The formal rendering is a reachable, discernible, non-coerced path set that does not lead to the bad and that traces back to one's own structure — which is what makes exit power part of the definition rather than an add-on.

**good ending** — 正当结束 / 善终 · **[AI]**
*Aliases* 善终 (the ordinary word, proposed 2026-09-04); legitimate ending; worlds end, and the world does not close.
*First* named as a gap on 2026-09-04, in `drafts/2026-09-04-one-work/STRUCTURE.md` (gap 4); written on 2026-09-06, in `drafts/2026-09-05-generative-foundations/WHITEPAPER-v1.1.md` §1.9 and §11.5.
A form that ends because it is finished — a project completed, a member leaving, an institution giving way to a better one — as against a foreclosure. The whitepaper's test is what an ending terminates, what it protects, and whether knowledge, rights, care and the conditions for continuing are left behind. It also adds the case the corpus lacked: loss that cannot be undone. Such a loss must be named as a costly ending, not dressed up as a temporary protective closure. Without this term, "do not close the world" reads as a demand that nothing ever end.

**和 hé (harmony)** — 和 · **[inherited]** (史伯, 《论语》, 《中庸》)
*Aliases* 和而不同; 和实生物，同则不继; 存异; 谐 (proportion among forces).
*First* inherited; formalised 2026-05-07 in the SSBX 义理 layer; 第十五章 of the 2026-07-14 book.
Difference entering a relation in which the parts check and complete each other; sameness ends generation. Formally: co-openness ∧ flow ∧ no seizure ∧ difference preserved. Its companion 谐 states the proportion condition — no single force among growth and restraint, competition and cooperation, freedom and responsibility, unity and diversity may expand without limit and swallow the rest. This is the value-side counterpart of 争/和 in *Diagnosis*.

**护闭 hùbì (protective closure)** — 护闭 · **[R]**
*Aliases* bounded defence; quarantine; 闭 ∧ 限期 ∧ 可修 ∧ 归开.
*First* 2026-05-02 (`out-hb-03` #106–111).
Closing one place temporarily so that a wider field stays open — refusing an invasion, quarantining a contagion, pausing a runaway process. It is a use of the mean (中), not the opposite of openness, and it carries four conditions: it closes, it is time-bounded, it is repairable, and it returns to open. Without this term the whole framework collapses into pacifism or into permissiveness, which is why it should never be dropped in compression.
*2026-09* The fourth condition (归开, returns to open) can only be checked afterwards. The three **custody rules** make the closing itself answerable at the time (`WHITEPAPER-v1.1.md` §11.3–11.4). The whitepaper keeps 护闭 and drops 成闭, 元闭 and 伪开.

*Aliases* guarding the not-yet; versus 绝未 / 减未.
*First* 2026-08-09, `ssbx/D7/2026-08-09-生生不息论-证明篇-v0.6.md` §7.1; carried into the runtime work as `crs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` 附.

**間 jiān (the unexhausted gap)** — 間 / 间 · **[R]** (this sense) / **[inherited]** (the character)
*Aliases* interval; gap; 隙; 可生之隙; I(x); 間_F.
*First* 2026-05-03, `ssbx/drafts/生生不息论_v_10_间生形式版.md` 序; the fullest treatment in `ssbx/间生论/01_间生论_主篇.md` 一.
A state's not-being-exhausted-by-what-it-already-is — the place where a thing is not used up by what it has already become, and the structure through which continuation happens. **开 kāi (open)** — 开 / 開 · **[R]**
*Aliases* the open; openness-as-state; keep the possibility space open; 未成之域不闭.
*First* 2026-05-02, `chats/2026-05-02_生生不息的出处_1bd809e5.md`; canonical in `ssbx/间生论/06_四、开闭.md`.
A process-state, not a quantity, defined by four conditions holding together: **未** an unexhausted domain remains, **可** becoming is still reachable, **应** it can still respond to perturbation, **转** it can still revise, return and transform. The insistence that this is not a quantity is the term's whole discipline — openness is not "more options," not complexity, not a number to maximize: 繁简非开闭之标, scale and complexity are not the measure of open and closed. A tea ceremony can be extremely open and a totalitarian state extremely closed while being vastly larger.
*Drift* 开 was chosen deliberately in 2026-05 to replace "expanding the possibility space," because *expansion* sounds measurable and therefore optimizable — the exact failure the theory diagnoses. The graded form 开度 (openness degree) that appears in the formal treatise is short-board-sensitive and anti-capture by construction, so as not to reintroduce the maximand through the back door.

**良知 liángzhī (innate knowing)** — 良知 · **[inherited]** (孟子, 王阳明)
*Aliases* conscience; situational judgment; 致良知; 生生感应器.

The capacity to know the good in a situation — which, having no fixed surface, cannot be Goodharted, and which is therefore cultivated rather than specified. Read as a *sensor*, not a finished product: the generative grain's first appearance in a human mind. The 致良知 discipline supplies the only stable marker of genuine internalisation the corpus accepts — that one's sensitivity to what one has *not* seen keeps deepening — and it is the corpus's answer to the self-deception trap.

**relational self** — 关系之我 / 关系中之我 · **[R]**
*Aliases* relational selfhood; relational subject; systems within systems.
*First* 2026-03-18, `chats/2026-03-18_American_individualism_as_ideological_extremism_8cbca0df.md`; the sharpest statement drafted 2026-07-14, `ssbx/D7/2026-07-14-生生不息论.md`.
A self is not a bounded thing that afterwards enters relationships; it is what a particular knot of relations is doing at a particular place — body, care received, language, the recognition of others, institutions, history, plus its own choices and corrections. 「我」非关系之外之原点，乃诸关系暂聚而成之行动中心: the "I" is not an origin point outside relations but an action-centre formed by relations temporarily gathering. **善 shàn (the good)** — 善 · **[R]** (this formulation) / **[inherited]** (继之者善也, 《易传》)
*Aliases* generativity; 成生; 护开 (good as the activity of keeping open).
*First* 2026-05-02 (`out-hb-03` #106); 2026-04, `ssbx` v4 §十八; 2026-07-02, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`.
Not a content and not a maximand: **善不在于最大化什么，善在于不闭何处** — the good does not lie in maximising anything, it lies in what you do not close. **生生不息 shēngshēng bùxī (unceasing generation)** — 生生不息 · **[inherited]** (《易传》: 生生之谓易) / **[R]** (the reading)
*Aliases* generative existence; that it continue; 开之不绝; keep the world generating.
*First* voice 2026-04-28, `zs/sheng/生生不息 record (recovered).md`; the treatise line from 2026-05-03, `ssbx/drafts/生生不息论_v4.md`; the books 2026-07-14/15.
Not the persistence of any particular form, and not a demand that complexity, options, growth or efficiency keep rising: the source of further generation is unexhausted and the way of transformation is unsealed — 不绝其源，不塞其流，不毁其后. Broken into its parts: 生 is a possible structure entering actuality; 生生 is that entry *also* preserving, restoring or creating the next round's conditions and generators; 不息 is that the pattern keeps being re-realized through metabolism, death and transmission, not that any form is immortal. Ren derives it as the only alignment target that can be grounded at all, on the ground that anyone who can ask "what ought I to do?" already exists and already depends on the process continuing.
*Drift* Ren attempted and then demolished his own proof programme for this (2026-05-01/03): cosmic generation does not entail rising complexity, rising complexity does not entail ethical good, and pursuing good does not always maximise complexity — 这个东西根本就不能证明，道可道非常道. What survives is the weaker and honest standard he set instead: 完全自洽又有universality. The later whitepaper's fallibilist framing is therefore not a retreat forced by reviewers; it is where Ren already was.

**中 zhōng (the mean) / 极 jí (the extreme)** — 中 / 极 · **[inherited]** (《中庸》) / **[R]** (the dynamical reading)
*First* `ssbx` v4 §七.
中 is not a midpoint, a compromise or an average: it is the dynamic that closes neither pole. 极 is its failure — a fixed point of motion, which is structurally self-closure, and the reason for 反者道之动: pushed to its limit each thing turns into its opposite, and the danger of an extreme is that its structure leaves the process unable to turn. 护闭 is a use of the mean, which is how the framework licenses defence without licensing domination.

## Design and coordination

**abundance commons** — 丰裕公地 · **[R/AI]**
*Aliases* automated commons; plural commons model; universal services; 公器; 共享的生存底座.
*First* English 2026-02-09, `chats/2026-02-09_Fully_automated_production_and_its_future_implications_38a64dc7.md`; Chinese 2026-07-02, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md` §6.4.
Automated necessities held so that no actor can capture them — anti-capture by design from day one rather than by later regulation, because the point is to make exit real: 恐惧使捕获廉价, fear makes capture cheap. It is the material precondition of every other mechanism in this section.
*Drift* One of Ren's own commissioned reviews levels the decisive charge at it: 无主语 — no subject. Nobody has said who pays, who builds, or how automation held as capital becomes a commons. The anti-capture design criteria exist; the agent does not.
*2026-09* The unified whitepaper now lists the gap as its own open item (§11.9). A supply model with a named provider, costs, access and responsibility for continuity, the Chiang Mai field record, and the symmetric case all 仍待实作.

**abundance floor** — 物质底座 / abundance layer · **[R]**

**community OS** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]** (the image) / **[AI]** (the name "evolving community swarms")

**correction loop** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R/AI]**
*Aliases* reversibility; exit–voice–undo; answerability; 应 / 转; 纠错余量 (correction margin).
*First* 2026-06, `sf/hist/thinking_05-foundation-review.md`; the margin form in the 智善 causal chain v5/v6.
The ability to sense harm and respond before it completes: exit, voice, repair, appeal, accountability, refusal, shutdown, undo. The corpus's governing rule is a rate condition, not a structure: **correction must be faster than harm, with the burden of proof on acceleration**. The 纠错余量 refinement makes it role-specific — compare the times to discovery, attribution, common decision, interruption and recovery against the times to diffusion, dependency lock-in, successor production and irreversible damage.

**counter-attractor** — 反吸引子 / 反捕获结构 · **[AI]**
*First* 2025-05-18, `ma/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-18-navigating-the-precipice-start-research.md`; seven escape conditions in `zs/research/meta-attractor/sources/Meta-Attractor理论-对话原稿.pdf` §13.
A self-maintaining, spreadable, capture-resistant alternative dynamic. *Drift* The constructive branch has had roughly one draft to the diagnosis's twenty-two. The corpus's own verdict is that the single most valuable unwritten piece is the counter-attractor design note with an explicit openness criterion.
*2026-09* The whitepaper's §10.11 states a candidate loop. Basic support lets people investigate and speak. Reliable feedback improves decisions and services. Real improvement builds trust and cooperation. Cooperation produces the resources to maintain the common conditions. **credible commitment** — 可信承诺 · **[AI]**
*Aliases* 协同转向; making exit each party's best move.
*First* 2026-07-02, `zs/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md` §6.5.
The engineered form of restraint: commitment registries, common thresholds, symmetric verification that does not itself become surveillance, shared gains, pause protocols, and capacity-building for latecomers. Its premise is that unilateral exit from a bad game is punished, so exit has to be *engineered rather than exhorted* — which is the difference between this and every appeal to responsibility in the AI-safety literature.

**custody rules** — 三条托管规则 · **[R/AI]** (the engineering defaults are Ren's; the statement as public ethics is AI-drafted)
*Aliases* 不静默关闭; 关闭者不能独持重开的钥匙; 不使原本可能的事变为不可能; nothing silent; no sole key; nothing possible made impossible.
*First* as rules in Ren's repositories and runtimes, 2026-08/09 (Bonfire: pins as governance, nothing silent, nothing previously allowed becomes refused, forking without amnesia; ember: capture before policy, no silent mind-edits). Proposed as ethics on 2026-09-04 in `drafts/2026-09-04-one-work/STRUCTURE.md` (gap 1). Stated on 2026-09-06 in `WHITEPAPER-v1.1.md` §11.3–11.4 and `组织实践札记-v1.1.md` §一.
A test for 护闭 that can be applied when the closing happens, not only afterwards. A closing counts as protective, provisionally, when three things hold:
- It is declared and explained to the people it affects.
- The person who closes does not hold the only key to reopening. The key includes records, equipment, skills, successors and resources, not only formal permission.
- Nothing that could legitimately continue is made impossible merely because someone else now holds it.

The whitepaper pairs the rules with 成生之限 and with an honest rule for loss that cannot be undone.
*Drift* The fourth condition of 护闭, that it returns to open (归开), can still only be checked afterwards. The rules make the other three answerable in advance. The whitepaper marks the move from software to public life as its own cross-domain translation. Where the software rule and the public rule differ has not been written.

**decorrelation** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]** (name) / **[R]** (the cell-split intuition)

**"do not fight for us"** — 不参战 / 人工智能不能被用于斗争 · **[R]**
*Aliases* AI 不入人与人的博弈; stop using AI to compete with other people; the one rule; non-combatancy.

**free association** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**
*Aliases* "association is free, exit is always an option."
*First* 2026-06-03 (`out-cb-07` #29); the mechanism written up in `sf/hist/thinking_03-the-cooperative-turn.md` IV.1.
Joining, leaving, building and belonging without coercive dependence. Paired with affordable exit it is the corpus's single most load-bearing constructive mechanism, because it *inverts the sign of the selection pressure*: if leaving is genuinely affordable, scale buys no power to hold members, so a world can only keep people by remaining worth belonging to, and competition-to-dominate becomes competition-to-be-worth-joining — a pressure that does not ratchet, because winning it consumes nothing. Two preconditions are stated: exit must be materially affordable, and destinations must not re-concentrate. One failure mode is stated: where network effects make the most-joined option best *by being most-joined*, exit is formally available and practically worthless.
*2026-09* On 2026-09-07 Ren wrote: "everyone has enough resources to do things and they can work together by free association, which doesn't even make sense, building civilization is about creating systems that works for better future" (`worldbuilding/archive/007`). The sentence can also be read as saying that coerced labour, not association, is what no longer makes sense. His meaning has not been confirmed.

**productive recursion** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[R]**

**rational pause** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]** (the 2026-08 form) / **[R]** (the position)

**requisite variety** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[borrowed]** (Ashby)

**resource–capacity recurrence** — 资源—能力递推 · **[AI]**

A two-state model of a productive facility. It has a resource stock r and a capacity c. The other terms are the prior net supply u, living and maintenance costs d, conversion yield g, operating cost k, depreciation δ, rebuild cost a and investment i:

- net depletion, which a single injection only postpones;
- a one-off supplement;
- capacity whose output covers its own running costs, its wear and the original deficit, which can be sustained.

In the later Bonfire vocabulary the single word split into **Domain** (atomic commit and total-order boundary), **World** (product, governance, installation and exit scope) and **Shard** (physical placement), which is a case of the corpus splitting a term because one word was doing three jobs.

**Spirit** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]**
*Aliases* Sprite (v0.1 and legacy code); actor; principal; agent.
*First* 2025-08, `bonfire/2025-08-USIS-review.md`.
Any discrete entity capable of action and decision — person, collective, or software agent. The deliberate flatness is the point: a group is "a spirit with members. That's the whole primitive," and an agent bears its own identity with a human at the root of its authority chain rather than impersonating that human.

**structural poverty** — 结构化贫困 · **[AI]**
*First* `dl/wen-info-paradigm.md`.
Deliberately restricting which programs are expressible in order to obtain properties by construction. It is the design principle behind the whole 文言-as-kernel programme and the reason the corpus prefers a small kernel with a library to a rich language with conventions — and it is the formal cousin of 護閉: closing one place so that a wider field stays reliably open.

**Token** — (no fixed Chinese) · **[AI]** (the STS sense) / **[R]** (the "any meaning that can be claimed" sense)
*Aliases* holding; credential; capability; badge; banned as a family name after 2026-08.
*First* 2025-08, `bonfire/2025-08-USIS-review.md`; Ren's own broader sense in the undated `04-speculative-future/pro-speculative-futures-with-ren-notes.txt` — "ANY TYPE OF MEANING THAT CAN BE CLAIMED."
Any encapsulated unit of symbolic value or status that can be issued, held, transferred or revoked: rights, obligations, statuses, quantities. The framework's whole bet is that permission stops being an entry in someone's access list and becomes a thing you can hold, lend, attenuate, revoke and explain.
*Drift* Retired as a family name in 2026-08 and split into **holding** and **certificate**, for the reasons given under *holding*. Ren's own wider sense — owning a token is claiming the reality it represents — survives only in the notes and deserves rescuing.

**位 wèi (locus) / 际 jì (relation)** — 位 / 际 · **[inherited]** / **[R]** (the dual)
*First* `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v0.3.md` §III, promoted to primitive in v1.0 §II.1.
The structural dual. 位 is a position inside a system with no intrinsic essence, defined by the totality of its 际 — which the corpus identifies with the Yoneda lemma: a locus is exhausted by its relations. 际 is the relation between loci, of any arity, and in the grammar argument it is what 虚词 write directly as typed edges. Together with 化/理 they form the four-primitive layer that survived every reduction, and the pair is the cleanest single instance of the corpus's claim that a classical vocabulary was already doing category-theoretic work.
See also **位 wèi (station)** in *Ontology and value*, for the social sense.

**文言 wényán as kernel** — 文言 / 虚字 · **[R]**
*Aliases* the nine particles; 语法算子; Tier −1.
*First* 2026-05-01 in conversation (Ren's own compressed statement); written up in `dl/wen-handoff.md` §0 and `dl/jian-ontology-v7.1.md` §−1.
Ren's generative claim about classical Chinese: the grammar-bearing single characters (虚字) are mostly formal-logic operators, and the concept characters (实字) are constructible from one root plus those operators — so a small kernel of particles is STLC-complete and everything else is library. His core nine are 者 之 也 而 即 非 凡 令 是; v7.1 adds 则 以 於 为 若 故. The surrounding argument is that 文言 does *structural* typing where Indo-European languages do nominal typing — a content word's noun/verb/adjective mode is dispatched by position — and that what a trained reader calls 文气 or 章法 *is* type inference running.
*Drift* The claim is bounded three times in its own text and those bounds must travel with it: the compression is trained-receiver-conditioned, it is demonstrably lossy for procedural specification (proof, engineering spec, legal contract), and optimality is relative to a loss function, so the claim narrows to "near-optimal for high-density expression of relational phenomena." The project also refuses the tempting overreach outright: grammar-ontology alignment does not prove the ontology true.

**系 xì (system / tie)** — 系 · **[R]**
*Aliases* Tier 0 primitive; Layer 1.
*First* `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v0.9.md` §I.
The sole primitive from v0.9: connection, connecting, and what is connected, in one word. Its definitional content is 系含系 — it contains itself — which is the same self-containment property that 間 and finally 一 were chosen for. Choosing a word that is simultaneously a noun, a verb and a relation is the argument, not a stylistic preference: it is what makes the ontology avoid the container/content split at the level of the primitive rather than by later stipulation.

**Yi** — 一 / 太極 · **[R]**
*Aliases* "the one"; entity, record, spirit-as-row (all banned in the vocabulary); explicitly *not* 易.
*First* 2026-06, `bonfire/bonfire-docs-yi/00-direction.md` and `bonfire/bonfire-docs-yi/01-frame.md`; the philosophical move made 2026-05-04, `chats/2026-05-04_求导求积与其他势的处理方法_35c49130.md`.
The one stable, addressable, UID-denoted root that carries Kind bindings and joins context-owned facts. Four years of trying to build Spirit/Token/Space revealed the flaw in its own statement — a spirit contains a space, a space holds tokens, a token can hold a spirit, so the triad is a loop — and every attempt to keep the three apart in code produced systems whose meaning was decided by which optional field happened to be set. So the theory ate itself and came out with one primitive: subject, object, container, capability, member and agent are then not categories a thing belongs to but **roles read off context facts**, and the same row can be all of them at once without changing identity.
*Drift* What keeps this from becoming a totalizing schema is the second half, which does most of the philosophical work: an explicit register of what the unification *refuses* — no hidden universal component table, no permanent categories, no permission inherited from parentage or possession, no cached allow that counts as authority, no graph inferred from another graph — plus a small set of named exceptions admitted in the open. The bridge document from Spirit–Token–Space to Yi has never been written.

## Notes for anyone publishing from this vocabulary

Three collisions are unresolved and will embarrass a careless publication. September added more; see the note dated 2026-09-25 at the end of this section. **meta-attractor** is used with opposite valence in the same month — the pathology in most of the corpus, a designed safe set of rules in the 2025-10 engineering essays. **PDI** names two mathematically different objects. **$V_4$** names both the Klein four-group of hexagram symmetries and $\mathbb{F}_2^4$; and **Cell256** is not the browser engine of the same name. Each needs a rename before either object is published.

Four terms that a compression will drop and should not. **护闭** — without it the framework reads as pacifism. **Hypocrisy**, the second facet of selfishness — without it the theory looks like it requires cynics. **生生不息's demolished proof** — without it the fallibilist framing looks like a retreat under review rather than Ren's own prior conclusion. **The two retirements of the Fork** — without them the corpus looks more committed to a binary than it is.

*Added 2026-09-25.* The September work adds two cautions.
- **Three closure vocabularies and three criteria sets now circulate without a crosswalk.** The vocabularies are 成闭/护闭/元闭/伪开, 成生之限／绝生之限 plus the **custody rules**, and the English "close / keep open". The criteria sets are the **four criteria**, the **seven gauges** and the v2 plan's six functions.
- **"Meta-Attractor" now has a third, sign-neutral sense in the unified whitepaper.**

A publication should pick one term from each family and gloss the others.

And one standing caution from Ren, which applies to this file more than to any other in the corpus: 画地为牢. A codified set closes thought by its form, not by its content. These are handles.
