# Opening, version 7: scope edits proposed for Ren

AI proposal, 19 October 2026. Not applied. [OPENING.md](../../theories/becoming/OPENING.md) is in Ren's voice and changes only through a proposal to him. Board item R20 (fixes from the outside review of 19 October) lists "a proposal to Ren for OPENING's body"; this is that proposal's draft, offered to R20's owner.

**What prompted it.** An outside reader's critique, which Ren received on 19 October, read the opening as claiming universal, empirical and ethical truth from proofs inside a model. The critique's own evidence was the footnotes, which already mark every such step as open, a reading or a judgment. So the reader did not misread the body: several body sentences assert what their notes take back. The v6 proposal ([history](history/OPENING-V6-PROPOSAL.md)) aimed that "no sentence of the main text says more than its footnote" and left the thesis sentence to Ren. This proposal takes up that sentence and the remaining ones.

**Second review, 19 October.** A further round, aiming for the strongest claims the work supports, corrected several edits below and added others; see [the second review](#second-review-19-october-corrections-and-the-strongest-supported-forms). Its integrated text at the end supersedes the tables wherever they differ.

**The aim.** Each body sentence claims no more than its note supports, and a reader who never opens a note can still tell what is proved, what is proved under conditions, and what is a reading or a judgment. The edits keep the voice and the thesis, and add the scope in the fewest words. None of them adds or removes a result.

> *Each note says how far its sentence is proved: proved, proved under conditions, a reading, or a judgment.*
>
> There is a set of universal invariants, a law of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away. In every universe, becoming happens, and wherever it can be told in steps and counted amounts, the law follows.
>
> Some of it holds of anything so told, however finely its steps are drawn, by counting alone: in anything finite that can be counted, coming to be and ceasing to be keep pace, and arising recurs exactly where ceasing does.
>
> The rest holds wherever nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without a least loss: every change of an amount is what is taken from it and given to it, and within anything closed, nothing gives back more than it takes, and whatever takes gives back at least a least amount less. There, arising and ceasing are one change, seen from its two sides: nothing rises or comes to stand but something else falls in the same exchange. Whatever is closed, where what stands is listed and acts while it stands, cannot go on forever without coming to rest, though when it rests no general method can tell; and whatever keeps taking cannot go on forever unfed from beyond itself. Many parts, each drawing within what is renewed, can together exceed it, and end what depends on it. And a part can go on while it closes the world of others it does not depend on.
>
> None of this needs an axiom. Each law is proved from how the theory represents a world and from the conditions it names, stated openly as its premises, in a form any mind, human or machine, can check for itself.
>
> Under these conditions, nothing that keeps arising or taking can stay closed. There is no absolute self, and the continuation goes beyond one's cycle of life, beyond one's death. What one is goes on, not as a self kept apart, but in the larger life one was always part of, which, made of lives that each end, lasts only as ever new lives arise.
>
> The law shows what anything that wants to go on must count: everything that bears on what it depends on, at any remove, far beyond the boundary it draws around itself; nothing beyond that can touch whether it goes on. What it does not show is that others count too, nor that only oneself does: a part can go on while closing their world, and no law of the dynamics says whether that matters. That takes one step, which the work states as a condition and does not assert: for anything, not just oneself, its continuation into the future has significance. Given that each holds its own continuation to matter, the step is exactly this, and all of it: what matters from one standpoint matters from every other. Whether the law takes this step for us too is our first open question.
>
> In history, Laozi said parts of it, Confucius said parts of it, the Buddha said parts of it, Jesus said parts of it, Muhammad said parts of it. Where they agree most is the one step the law has not yet taken for us: do not do to others what you would not want done to yourself (己所不欲，勿施于人); love your neighbor as yourself; love for your brother what you love for yourself.
>
> This law, with that one step, is offered as the basis of all ethics, for all beings, in all universes. I take it to be the truth, the dharma, the Dao. It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures, and it draws the line between good and evil exactly. What is good opens without closing: with it, all that could form without it still can, and more. What closes draws more from what others need than it gives back, beyond what there was to spare; evil is a part that enlarges itself so, closing the world of others. The law shows what each does, and the one step makes all of it count, the same from every standpoint. What neither yet does is weigh a part that does both.

What an act does beyond that never comes back to the selection, whatever it selects for, and a pool of selectors reaches it only through a member who couples into it. This holds of any understanding, this one included: no map couples into everything, and what lies beyond it is reached only through others who couple into it. That is why this work is laid open for everyone to check and to challenge. To achieve alignment, we need a foundation that makes coordination possible, and this foundation supplies that too. Coordination needs understanding to be shared first, and what anyone can check for themselves needs no guardian: everyone, human and AI, can check that each law follows from its stated premises, and judge in the open whether the premises hold and whether the words say what the Lean says.

It keeps the first thing every living being acts on: that its own continuation matters. It drops the rest: what that continuation depends on, and that the continuation of others matters too. What remains are incoherent narratives, which drive some of the population into an "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole". Where the whole gains nothing, every rise of a part is paid from the rest of it, and nothing the part senses through what it couples into shows how near the break is.

>
> This influence is deeply rooted in our history. Within Western thinking, it has become the dominant logic in how reality is shaped. > It is clear that we need to shape society away from the logic of competition, accumulation, colonization and domination.
