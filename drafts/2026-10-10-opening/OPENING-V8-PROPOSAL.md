# Opening, version 8: the capture paragraph and "It is clear"

AI proposal, 20 October 2026. Not applied. [OPENING.md](../../theories/becoming/OPENING.md) is in Ren's voice and changes only through a proposal to him. Two edits, each independent of the other; neither adds or removes a result, and no Lean changes.

**What prompted it.** Ren asked whether the opening is too spicy. The assessment: most of its boldness is the thesis, and each bold sentence carries its tag ("Truth exists", "in all universes", "the dharma, the Dao", the line between good and evil, cancer, godlike technology). Two places carry more heat than their notes support: the sentence that names three doctrines as captured, and the four "It is clear".

**The history this reopens.** Version 1 replaced "Western thinking" with a line of Ren's from 4 October, 「这种斗争并不是西方的本色，更不是人类的本色」 (this struggle is not the West's true nature, still less humanity's), and kept the three doctrines, each with the feature it takes for law. It also dropped the four "It is clear". Version 2 restored Ren's original wording, because version 1 had changed it without his approval ([notes](history/OPENING-NOTES.md), the table row on the doctrines). Ren then rejected version 2's AI line "The logic is older than the West, and it is no one's nature": he holds that the logic is part of nature, hijacked by what people were nurtured into ([source](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-truth-exists-opening.md#nature-nurture-and-无明)). Versions 6 and 7 left the paragraph as it is and marked it a diagnosis in its note. Nothing below brings back the rejected line: no edit says what is or is not anyone's nature.

**Why reopen it now.** Version 7 set the opening's standard: each body sentence claims no more than its note supports. Under that standard, these sentences are the ones still out of line, and the sentence on the doctrines differs in kind from the other judgments, because it is aimed at named people's views.
1. *The note does not support the diagnosis.* `[^no-law]` says the theory "does not test those theories against the evidence they cite". The body says their holders' system is captured. What the theory does support is narrower and checkable: the features those doctrines take for laws of nature are not among the laws.
2. *The opening's own argument.* It says that coordination needs understanding to be shared first, that what anyone can check needs no guardian, and that "we need to stop seeing each other as enemies". A label on named camps is the one sentence a reader in those camps cannot check, only accept or refuse, and they meet it before the laws. The same point, stated as what the laws contain, is one they can check.
3. *The method.* The theory's diagnosis is a selection that any system can fall into, and the paragraph itself says so ("Any system can be captured by such a rule"). Placing the logic in one civilization's thinking turns a dynamic into a pair of parties, which the nondual method asks to rebuild from relations.
4. *Reception.* The opening echoes 人类命运共同体 on purpose ("mankind as one community with a shared future"), and the echo stays. A reader who knows the phrase as a state slogan, meeting it a few paragraphs after a critique addressed to "Western thinking", hears a political alignment, and reads the rest through it. The pairing does this, not either part alone.

A correction to the assessment given in conversation: it said the Laozi quotation argues against the Western locus. That overstated it. The paragraph says the logic is old (Laozi named it) and that within Western thinking it became the dominant logic, which is a historical claim, not a claim that the logic is Western only. The reasons above do not depend on that point.

## Edit B: the four "It is clear"

The four paragraphs carry claims of three kinds, all stated with the same certainty:
- "the whole Earth has become a highly entangled system": a judgment of the present world (`[^present]`);
- "we need to see mankind as one community…": a call whose ground is the continuation condition with comparison by widening (C), which the opening offers and does not assert (`[^ethics]`, open);
- "we need to shape society away from…; it has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises…; an exponential pace": a call and a judgment (`[^present]`);
- "the incentives… are aligned wherever we share what the race draws down": proved under conditions, with the judgment that the Earth meets them (`[^aligned]`).

The opening's credibility rests on its tags, and "It is clear" is the one place where the voice claims more certainty than the note beneath it.

## Left as they are

- **"Truth exists", "in all universes", "the truth, the dharma, the Dao", "offered as the basis of all ethics".** They are the thesis, and versions 6 and 7 gave each its scope and tag.
- **The sages.** The paragraph locates their agreement at the reciprocity of the one step, and `[^sages]` marks it as a reading.
- **The line between good and evil, and "evil is a part that enlarges itself so".** The body already says what it cannot weigh ("a part that does both"), and `[^good-evil]` marks the naming as a reading.
- **Cancer.** It is defined as dynamics, "not growth itself, but growth that has stopped answering to the body", not as a group of people, and `[^cancer]` and `[^transitions]` give the mechanism and the literature.
- **"It has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises".** It says "our" and describes a selection, not a camp.
- **Godlike technology, and "the most important matter at this moment".** These are judgments their notes mark, and they are common in the field.
- **The shared-future echo.** Kept on purpose; edit A removes the pairing that makes it read as alignment.
