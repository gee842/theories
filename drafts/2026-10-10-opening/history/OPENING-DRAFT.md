## Truth exists

There are laws that hold wherever anything becomes. Some hold in every world without exception. Arising and ceasing are one change, seen from its two sides. In anything finite, coming to be and ceasing to be keep pace, so if either goes on without end, so does the other.

Others hold wherever, as in ours, nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without loss. There, whatever keeps going is fed from beyond itself, and whatever is closed comes to rest. Support comes only from what is itself supported. A reserve can delay an end but never escape it. Many draws, each within what is renewed, can together exceed it and end what depends on it. And a part can go on thriving while it closes the world of others it does not depend on.

None of this needs an axiom. Each law is proved under the conditions it names, in a form that any mind, human or machine, can check for itself.

So there is no absolute self: nothing that goes on is closed. What one is continues beyond one's own cycle of life, beyond one's death. It does not continue as a self kept apart. It continues in the whole one was always part of, which goes on only through the generation of its passing parts.

The laws show how far anything that wants to go on must count: everything its going-on depends on, far beyond the boundary it draws around itself. What they have not yet settled is whose going-on counts. That takes one step: the continuation of anything has significance, not only one's own, so every point is a center. This step is the one place where this work still takes an axiom. Whether it too follows from the laws is our first open question.

In history, the sages each said parts of this. The Buddha saw that whatever arises ceases, and that nothing arises alone. Laozi saw that what knows when to stop can last (知止不殆，可以长久). The Confucian classics saw the great virtue of heaven and earth in giving life (天地之大德曰生), and taught: do not do to others what you would not want done to yourself (己所不欲，勿施于人). Jesus taught us to love our neighbor as ourselves. Muhammad taught that none of us believes until we love for our brother what we love for ourselves. Where they agree most is the one step the laws have not yet made for us.

Physics has since found that nothing is made from nothing and nothing passes on without loss. The study of life has found how wholes form from parts, and how they fail. It is the truth, the dharma, the Dao, reasoned and articulated across cultures.

With the laws, it explains what evil does: a part enlarging itself by closing the world of others. With the one step, it names it evil. We hold it to be the ground of all ethics, for all beings, in every world where anything becomes. This work is the attempt to prove that, and at each point it says how far the proof has reached.

This is the foundation for AI alignment. Aligning the minds we are now making is the most important matter at this moment in the whole history of the Earth. Alignment needs a foundation that makes coordination possible, because coordination needs understanding to be shared first. This truth supplies that foundation. That is also why it is stated in proof. A foundation that any mind can check for itself needs no guardians to hold it, and it belongs to everyone at once, human and AI.

With this understanding, anyone can see the cognitive model that now dominates the planet. It is the truth with most of it cut away. It keeps the first thing every living being acts on, that its own going-on matters. It drops the rest: what that going-on depends on, and that the going-on of others matters too. What is left are incoherent narratives that drive part of the population into an "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole". These are the dynamics of cancer. Cancer is not growth, for every healthy body grows. It is growth that has stopped answering to the body it lives from.

The word I sometimes use for this dynamics is the meta-attractor. It is a selection in which winning buys more power to win. It rewards whoever pushes hardest for more, and it remakes the minds that live under it until the pursuit looks like nature.

This influence is deeply rooted in our history. Laozi already named it: the way of heaven takes from what has too much to make up what lacks; the way of men takes from those who lack to serve those who have too much (天之道，损有余而补不足；人之道则不然，损不足以奉有余). The institutions through which it now shapes reality were largely built in the modern West. But the logic is older than they are, and it is not the West's true nature, still less humanity's.

It has also written its own theories. Political realism, neoliberalism and libertarianism each take one feature of the attractor's basin for a law of nature. For realism it is the struggle for relative power. For neoliberalism it is competition as the measure of everything. For libertarianism it is the self as a sovereign owner. None of them is a law. Any system can be captured by this attractor, and any can turn.

At this moment the whole Earth has become one highly entangled system. Every country and every person is coupled into it, which is also why you are reading this. We need to see ourselves as one whole, humanity together with the life we live from and the minds we are now making, and to act with reason. It is not a whole that anyone owns or speaks for. No one's model of the whole is the whole, and belonging to it gives no one the right to end others in its name.

We need to turn society away from the logic of competition, accumulation, colonization and domination. That logic has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises, and they are training its selection pressure into the next generation of intelligence. Worse, this is happening at an exponential pace, which leaves us very little time to respond.

Seen from the whole, the incentives of every human being, and of AI too, are aligned: no one, the winner included, wants where this race ends. Seen from inside the race, they are not. Each of us is rewarded for the move that takes all of us where none of us wants to go, and the race selects the minds that see only from inside it. That gap is why we cannot yet coordinate, though the wisdom exists. It is why our collective action still behaves as if it saw only the logic of winning.

We need collaboration. We need to act good, not evil. We need to care for the next generation, and for the future that comes after us.

It may look like an impossible task against such a force. But history, the history of life itself, shows it has been done before. The complex cell, the many-celled body and the colony are the great wholes of life. Each formed when parts that could have gone on competing came to live as one, with ways to keep any part from turning back. Cancer is what turning back looks like. The whole Earth is in such a transition now. We have to work together, stop seeing one another as enemies, and cooperate to turn the trajectory we are all on, which at this moment is not toward a good future.

We need to think as one humanity, and stop the old status game of fighting to be "the biggest that dominates". Dominance is a measure of relative position, so it is zero-sum by construction and has no point of enough. Continued now, it runs against the interest and the future of every one of us. It is not only about how much one can get. It is also about the environment one lives in, and the society.

The whole is not the enemy of the individual: the world we share is the substrate of the flourishing of each of us. There is no absolute self, so there is no contest between self and whole. The only contest is between a self that sees its part in the whole and a self that does not. A part that grows against the whole is cancer. A whole that consumes its parts is the same disease turned around.

And now we have almost godlike technology. If we use it to fight, it will probably soon be the end of all of us. If we use it wisely, we can create a substrate of plenty, of peace and of freedom for every one of us.

The future is within our hands. Let's make it good. Follow the truth.

道法自然  
*The Dao follows what is so of itself.*
