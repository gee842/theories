## Truth exists

There is a set of universal invariants, a law of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away. In every universe, becoming happens, and the law follows.[^law]

Some of it holds in every universe. Arising and ceasing are one change, seen from its two sides. In anything finite they keep pace: if either goes on without end, so does the other.

The rest holds wherever, as in ours, nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without loss.[^conditions] There, whatever is closed comes to rest, and whatever keeps going is fed from beyond itself. Support comes only from what is itself supported. A reserve can delay an end, but never escape it. Many parts, each drawing within what is renewed, can together exceed it, and end what depends on it. And a part can go on thriving while it closes the world of others it does not depend on.

None of this needs an axiom. Each law is proved under the conditions it names, in a form any mind, human or machine, can check for itself.[^proof]

Nothing that goes on is closed. There is no absolute self, and the continuation goes beyond one's cycle of life, beyond one's death. What one is goes on, not as a self kept apart, but in the larger life one was always part of, which lasts only as new lives arise and old ones pass.[^self]

The law shows what anything that wants to go on must count: everything it depends on to go on, far beyond the boundary it draws around itself.[^count] What it does not show is that others count too: a part can thrive while closing their world. That takes one step: for anything, not just oneself, its continuation into the future has significance. This is the one place where the work still rests on an axiom.[^axiom] Whether the law takes this step for us too is our first open question.

In history, Laozi said parts of it: what knows when to stop can last (知止不殆，可以长久). Confucius said parts of it: do not do to others what you would not want done to yourself (己所不欲，勿施于人). The Buddha said parts of it: whatever arises ceases, and nothing arises alone. Jesus said parts of it: love your neighbor as yourself. Muhammad said parts of it: none of us believes until we love for our brother what we love for ourselves. Where they agree most is the one step the law has not yet taken for us.[^sages]

Physics has since found that nothing comes from nothing, and nothing passes on without loss. The study of life has shown how larger lives form from smaller ones, and how they fail.[^science]

This law is the basis of all ethics, for all beings, in all universes.[^ethics] It is the truth, the dharma, the Dao. It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures,[^dao] and it explains good and evil. What is good opens: its existence creates more possibilities of becoming. What is evil closes: a part enlarges itself by closing the world of others. The law shows what each does, and the one step shows why it matters.[^good-evil]

This is the foundation for AI alignment, the most important matter at this moment in the whole history of the Earth.[^alignment] To achieve alignment, we need a foundation that makes coordination possible, and this foundation supplies that too. Coordination needs understanding to be shared first. That is why the work is written so that any mind can check it. What anyone can check for themselves needs no guardian, and everyone, human and AI, can reach it directly.[^guardian]

By one's own understanding, one can see the cognitive model that dominates at the planetary scale. It is the truth with much of it abstracted away. It keeps the first thing every living being acts on: that its own continuation matters.[^own] It drops the rest: what that continuation depends on, and that the continuation of others matters too. What remains are incoherent narratives, which drive some of the population into an "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole". These are the dynamics of cancer.[^cancer] The trouble is not growth: every healthy body grows. Cancer is growth that has stopped answering to the body that feeds it.

The word I sometimes use for these dynamics is the meta-attractor: a selection in which winning buys more power to win. It rewards whoever pushes hardest for more, and it remakes the minds that live under it until the pursuit looks like nature.[^meta]

This influence is deeply rooted in our history. Laozi already named it: the way of heaven takes from what has too much to make up what lacks, but the human way takes from those who lack to serve those who have too much (天之道，损有余而补不足；人之道则不然，损不足以奉有余). Within Western thinking, it has become the dominant logic in how reality is shaped. Political realism, neoliberalism and libertarianism are all manifestations of a system captured by the meta-attractor. Each takes one feature of life under it for a law of nature: the struggle for relative power, competition as the measure of everything, the self as a sovereign owner. None of these is a law.[^no-law] The logic is older than the West, and it is no one's nature. Any system can be captured by this attractor, and any can turn.[^turn]

It is clear that, at this moment, the whole Earth has become a highly entangled system. Every country and every person is heavily coupled into it, which is also why you are reading this.

It is clear that we need to see mankind as one community with a shared future, together with the life we live from and the minds we are now making, and to act with reason.

It is clear that we need to shape society away from the logic of competition, accumulation, colonization and domination. Unfortunately, it has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises, and they are training its evolutionary pressure into the next generation of intelligence. What is worse, this is happening at an exponential pace, which leaves us very little time to react.[^present]

It is clear that, seen from the shared future of mankind, the incentives of every human being, and even of AI, are aligned: no one, not even the winner, wants to be where this race ends.[^aligned] Seen from inside the race, they are not. Each of us is rewarded for the move that takes all of us where none of us wants to go, and the race selects the minds that see only from inside it. That is why our collective action, at this moment, still behaves as if it saw only the logic of winning. The wisdom exists, but we cannot coordinate.

We need collaboration. We need to do good, not evil. We need to care for the next generation, and for the future that comes after us.

It may look like an impossible task, confronting such a force. But history tells us that we need to work together, and that it can be done. Life has done it before. The complex cell, the many-celled body and the colony each formed when parts that could have gone on competing came to live as one, with ways to keep any part from turning back. Cancer is what turning back looks like.[^transitions] The whole Earth is in such a transition now. We need to stop seeing each other as enemies, and to cooperate, to turn the trajectory of us all toward a good future. At this moment, unfortunately, it is not headed there.

We need to think of humanity as a whole. We need to stop the current mammalian way of fighting to be "the biggest that dominates". Dominance is always a measure of relative position, so it is always zero-sum, and it never reaches enough. If we continue it now, it runs against the interest and the future of every one of us.[^aligned] It is not only about how much one can get. It is also about the environment you live in, and the society you live in.

The collective is not the enemy of the individual. In fact, the world we are in together is the substrate of the flourishing of each of us. A part that grows against the body it lives from is cancer. A collective that consumes the lives it is made of consumes what it goes on through: the same disease, turned around.[^collective]

And now we have almost godlike technology. If we use it to fight, it will probably soon be the end of all of us. If we use it wisely, we can create a substrate of plenty, of peace and of freedom, for every one of us.[^present]

The future is within our hands. Let's make it good. Follow the truth.

[^law]: **Proved, for every world as the theory represents it:** in discrete steps, with whole-number amounts, and with what is counted decided at each step. In every such world, arising and ceasing, a dependence forming and dissolving, and a condition coming to stand and ceasing to stand are one change (`change_readings`), and in a finite family coming to be and ceasing to be keep pace (`keep_pace`). Asked of every finite world without that decision, the law that arising recurs exactly when ceasing does is equivalent to excluded middle (`World.law_iff_em`). **Open:** whether every becoming can be so represented, and the deduction that the whole theory is necessary with no added premise ([Part I stated whole](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#第一部分的总陈述--part-i-stated-whole); [the open target](../../theories/becoming/results/records/theory-of-becoming-foundation.md)).

[^conditions]: **Proved under conditions.** "Nothing comes from nothing" is the premise of balance: every change of a level is what is taken from it and given to it. "Nothing passes on without loss" is dissipation: within a closed family nothing gives back more than it takes, and whatever takes gives back less. Under these, with further premises named in each theorem: a finite family that keeps taking cannot, enclosed around, go on fed from nothing beyond it (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`); a world with a census whose relata are all embodied comes to rest once enclosed (`World.rests_enclosed`); support is gained only from the supported (`Flow.gains_from_supported`); a reserve defers the end (`Carries.lasts`); draws each within renewal can together exceed it and, where the renewers depend on what they renew, end them and what depends on the condition (`Pair.own_not_joint`, `Flow.joint_excess_ends`); and a part can thrive while closing what lies beyond its dependence (`Clearing.loses_beyond`). Whether actual unfoldings meet these premises is an empirical question ([the premise card](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#0-前提卡--premise-card)).

[^proof]: **Proved.** The theorems are checked in Lean 4 on a core that imports nothing outside itself, and every declaration has an empty axiom report: beyond Lean's own logic, no axiom, mathematical or ethical, is assumed ([the formal spine](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#9-形式脊柱--the-formal-spine)). Premises such as balance enter only as hypotheses of the theorems that use them, and the commitments of the representation are stated, not assumed silently.

[^self]: **Proved under conditions, with a reading.** Under balance and dissipation, whatever keeps taking or arising cannot go on closed (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`, `World.arising_opens`); an absolute self would be one that goes on closed. A whole active exactly while some part is persists only through the generation of its parts (`Becoming.Wholes`, [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)), and what a formation gives to continues after it ends (descent, `Becoming.Evolution`). That what one is continues in this larger life is a reading of identity: the theory carries identity across steps by the pattern of names, and leaves which pattern is "one" to the representation (`Becoming.Identity`).

[^count]: **Proved under conditions, with limits.** Under balance and dissipation, what goes on is fed from beyond (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`), and draws each within renewal can together end what depends on the shared condition (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`), so a continuation wanted beyond what a condition holds must count the joint draw. A boundary that omits something one requires lets one's own draw end one's own support (`Omitted.loses_own`, [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/represented-boundary-against-dependence.md)). **Limits:** what is so derived runs one way, to what one's own continuation depends on, and ends with the want (`Downstream.one_way`, `Mortal.after_its_end`). "Must" means "if it is to go on"; the step from wanting an end to having reason for what it needs is a premise, not a theorem ([record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/derived-extent-limits.md)).

[^axiom]: **The one axiom, and where it stands.** No theorem of Part I, the part without axiom, mentions significance, and where nothing has significance nothing is ordered (`Web.significance_undetermined`). Part II states the axiom as: from every standpoint, the continuation of anything has significance at every step (`Unrestricted`). From it the work derives G0, its adopted axiom (that living and further generation within the whole have basic positive significance), together with each standpoint's own significance and both neutralities, assuming no connection (`Valuation.premises_of_unrestricted`; [Part II, section 6.8](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#68-一条公理推出什么p39-what-the-one-axiom-yields-p39); [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/one-axiom-yields-g0.md)). This is checked, not yet independently reviewed, and G0 remains the adopted axiom until this one takes its place. A significance bounded by a self or a region is no less an axiom (`bounded_rival`). Acting on significance, through reasons, rankings and choices, also uses disclosed practical premises (A, L and J1–J4 on [the premise card](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#0-前提卡--premise-card)). **Open:** the attempt with no axiom at all, which would explain how recognizing this step forms within the dynamics, has not yet begun.

[^sages]: **A reading.** Sources: Daodejing 44, 77 and 25; Analects 12.2 and 15.24; Saṃyutta Nikāya 56.11, on whatever arises being bound to cease, and the teaching of dependent arising; Mark 12:31; Sahih al-Bukhari 13. That the three teachings of reciprocity are the one step, extending to others the significance one gives one's own continuation, is the author's interpretation. Their agreement is offered as a meeting of traditions, not as proof.

[^science]: **Correspondence, not proof.** Balance and dissipation are read from conservation and the second law of thermodynamics; whether a given system meets them as represented is checked case by case, not here. On how larger lives form and fail, see the note on the history of life below.

[^ethics]: **Open: this is the claim the work sets out to prove.** Where it stands: Part I holds with no axiom; Part II derives G0, and exact forms of harm and generativity, from one axiom ([Part II](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#第二部分一条公理--part-ii-one-axiom)); judgment between conflicting continuations still needs the disclosed practical premises ([the premise card](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#0-前提卡--premise-card)). That all ethics rests on this ground is not proved: a rival that bounds significance within each standpoint's region is consistent with every law, and only the axiom rules it out (`bounded_rival`).

[^dao]: **A reading.** Calling it the dharma and the Dao names what the author takes it to share with those traditions: whatever arises ceases, nothing arises alone, and the Dao follows what is so of itself. The theory does not prove that the traditions name one thing.

[^good-evil]: **Proved under the one axiom; calling it good and evil is a reading.** With no axiom, a part compared with its absence widens what can form, narrows it, keeps it the same, or is mixed; it closes only through a net draw on what others require, and opens only through a net give ([four cases](../../theories/becoming/results/records/four-cases-of-a-part.md), [net draw](../../theories/becoming/results/records/closing-needs-net-draw.md)). Under the axiom, a part loses something with significance exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin, and adds something exactly where its net giving lets form what could not form without it (`Flow.Balances.loses_iff_beyond_margin`, `Flow.Balances.adds_iff_within_margin`, [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/significance-retained-within-margin.md)). So "not harm" is an exact boundary, and being generative is adding. A free rider, whose part closes another's world though its own continuation does not need that part, loses from every standpoint (`Clearing.free_rider_loses`). **Open:** a mixed part, opening some possibilities while closing others, is ordered only by a further measure (`Transfer.unordered`), and the step from significance to reasons, rankings and duties rests on the premises A, L and J1–J4.

[^alignment]: **Proved under conditions, as a direction; not shown to be the only foundation.** With G0, the practical commitments J1–J4 and warranted correspondence to actual AI systems, the work states a direction: align AI formation, learning, action and revision with actual living and further generation, governed by real losses, basic living conditions, bounded responsibility and legitimate participation, and open to correction ([AI alignment](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#7-ai-对齐--ai-alignment); [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/alignment-direction-g0-j.md), a reviewed argument). It proves neither compliance nor reliability in deployment. That this is the most important matter of the moment is the author's judgment.

[^guardian]: **Proved for the derivations only.** Anyone who runs Lean can recheck every theorem without trusting the author. What remains for judgment cannot be checked this way: whether the premises hold, whether the readings fit, and whether the representation fits the world. That shared understanding makes coordination possible is the author's thesis about how coordination forms, not a theorem.

[^own]: **A reading.** Under the one axiom, each standpoint's own living continuation has significance for it (S0, `Valuation.premises_of_unrestricted`). That every living being acts on this describes what living beings do; how recognition and motivation form within the dynamics belongs to the attempt without axiom, not yet begun.

[^cancer]: **The mechanism is proved under conditions; the diagnosis is a judgment.** Draws each within what is renewed can together exceed it, ending the renewal and what depends on the condition, the takers among them where they so depend (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`); and a part can thrive while closing what lies beyond its dependence (`Clearing.loses_beyond`). That the dominant cognitive model drives these dynamics today is the author's diagnosis.

[^meta]: **A theory with formal results at its own scope; its present reach is a judgment.** [The Meta-Attractor](../../theories/meta-attractor/README.md) develops how a pattern of selection gains power over the rule that selects, so that the winners of one round write the next round's scorecard. In this theory's [collective dynamics](../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#4-集体动力学--collective-dynamics), joint consumption has no single consumer, and "my share makes no difference" does not remove a drawer from consumption ([record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/joint-difference-making.md)). Its calibration to today's world is not established.

[^no-law]: **Proved, of this theory's laws; the diagnosis is a judgment.** The laws with no premise exclude no unfolding beyond what the representation excludes, and every premise of the conditional laws is met by some worlds and not by others (`World.contingent`): no struggle, competition or sovereign self is among the laws. No theorem of Part I mentions significance, so a significance bounded by a self is supplied as a premise, just as the axiom is (`bounded_rival`). Where each center's significance stops at its own dependence, centers disagree and the free rider is licensed (`Clearing.centers_disagree`, [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-first-and-bounded-centers.md)). That this logic dominates how Western thinking shapes reality, and that the three theories are its manifestations, is the author's diagnosis.

[^turn]: **A conviction, with a proved limit.** With nothing given from beyond and requirements not loosening, once nothing in a family is supported, nothing in it is supported again: the end absorbs (`Flow.unsupported_stays`, `Flow.nothing_supported_stays`, [record](../../theories/becoming/results/records/support-from-the-supported.md)). Turning has to come before that end. That every captured system can still turn is the author's conviction.

[^present]: **Judgments of the present world, not theorems.** That the Earth has become one entangled system, that this logic has captured academic systems and enterprises and is being trained into AI, that its pace is exponential, and that almost godlike technology could soon end us or provide for all, are the author's assessment of where we stand. The theory gives the dynamics such claims describe, not their measurement.

[^aligned]: **Proved under conditions; not universal.** Incentives align where continuations share the conditions a race draws down: joint excess ends the renewal and every formation depending on the condition at such a need, the takers among them where they so depend (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`). They need not align where a part's continuation does not depend on what it closes: a part can thrive while ending another (`Clearing.undermines_within_renewal`), and only the one axiom makes that loss count from every standpoint (`Clearing.free_rider_loses`). The entanglement above is what makes dependence shared. That the present race ends badly even for its winner, and selects the minds that see only from inside it, is the author's judgment.

[^transitions]: **Empirical, with a formal counterpart.** On the major transitions, see John Maynard Smith and Eörs Szathmáry, *The Major Transitions in Evolution* (1995); on cancer as cheating within multicellular cooperation, Athena Aktipis and colleagues, "Cancer across the tree of life" (*Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*, 2015). The formal counterpart is that a whole of brief parts persists only through their generation (`Becoming.Wholes`). That the Earth is in such a transition now is the author's judgment.

[^collective]: **Proved, partly under the one axiom.** A whole of brief parts persists only through their generation (`Becoming.Wholes`): a collective goes on through the lives it is made of. Under the axiom, what retains what has significance in the whole retains it within every region, a system's or a self's, and not conversely (`Web.Retains.within`). So a collective that consumes its members loses what has significance within its own region, while a part that keeps everything within its own boundary can still lose in the whole, as the free rider does (`Clearing.centers_disagree`).
