# Truth exists: independent review and revision

10 October 2026. This is an independent editorial review (Claude) of the AI-edited opening in [the opening draft](OPENING-DRAFT.md), read against Ren's original in [the source record](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-truth-exists-opening.md). It judges the writing only. Evidence, proofs and sources were not checked. The draft's table of where each of Ren's points went, now kept in [the notes on the draft](OPENING-NOTES.md), was treated as a set of constraints. Ren's three decisions of 10 October, relayed while the review was in progress, override that table where the two differ (see Notes). Nothing here is accepted, and every change is Ren's to keep or revert.

## 1. Review

### Main problems, most important first

**1. The opening hands a cold reader eight laws before it gives a foothold.**

"There are laws that hold wherever anything becomes" stops a newcomer at the intransitive verb: becomes what? Then one pair gets two names ("Arising and ceasing", then "coming to be and ceasing to be"), so the reader looks for a difference that is not there. "Many draws" reads as ties or lotteries. The second paragraph is six free-standing sentences, so it reads as a list of results, a technical summary, rather than a movement. And the umbrella sentence says the laws hold "wherever anything becomes", while the next paragraph says some hold only under two conditions. A careful reader will catch that.

Fix: name becoming and gloss it once in plain words: "There are laws of becoming: of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away." The split that follows then carries the universality exactly ("Some hold in every world." "Others hold wherever, as in our world, …"). Keep one pair of words, "arising and ceasing". Turn the noun into a verb: "Many parts, each drawing within what is renewed, …". Order the conditional laws so each sets up the next. What is closed comes to rest, and what keeps going is fed from beyond itself. Support comes only from the supported. A reserve only delays. Many parts can together exceed what each alone stays within. And one part can thrive while it closes the world of others. That last sentence is the seed of the later account of evil, so it should close the paragraph, as it does. Drop "without exception", which "every" already says.

**2. The second half alternates diagnosis and exhortation, so the argument doubles back.**

The paragraph beginning "At this moment the whole Earth has become one highly entangled system" joins the entanglement, a call ("We need to see ourselves as one whole") and the safeguard. It sits between the doctrines and the captured institutions. The next paragraph opens with a call ("We need to turn society away …") and gives the diagnosis behind it only afterwards. Four paragraphs later, "We need to think as one humanity" repeats the earlier call, and the zero-sum mechanism arrives after the call it supports.

Fix: run the diagnosis as one line: the cognitive model, the meta-attractor, its roots, its theories, the entangled Earth and the institutions already captured, then the incentive gap. Then turn: what we need, why it looks impossible and why it is not, mankind as one community with a shared future, the status game to stop and why, the collective and the individual, the technology, the close. The two "one whole" calls become one. Ren's own sequence (the incentives, then "we need collaboration", then "it may look like an impossible task") survives intact inside the new order.

**3. Pronouns drift exactly where the largest claims are made.**

"The study of life has found how wholes form from parts, and how they fail. It is the truth, the dharma, the Dao" reads as if the study of life were the Dao. "With the laws, it explains what evil does … With the one step, it names it evil. We hold it to be the ground of all ethics" has four uses of "it" with three referents, and "We hold it" lands right after "names it evil". "This is the foundation for AI alignment" follows "This work is the attempt to prove that", so "this" can be read as the work.

Fix: end the science paragraph on Ren's own two sentences: "This is the truth, the dharma, the Dao. It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures, and it explains good and evil." Then name each subject: "The laws show what evil does … The one step names it evil. We hold this truth to be … This truth is also the foundation for AI alignment".

**4. Repetition wears down the strongest images.**

Cancer is defined twice ("growth that has stopped answering to the body it lives from"; "A part that grows against the whole is cancer") and invoked a third time. "Foundation" occurs four times in one paragraph and "at this moment" three times in the text. "The minds we are now making" and "any mind can check for itself" each occur twice. One premise appears in two wordings ("nothing comes from nothing", then "nothing is made from nothing"), which makes a reader wonder whether physics found something else. "The whole" does at least four jobs: the totality of what becomes, humanity with life and AI, the larger life one belongs to, and the body a part lives from. (Ren's second decision now removes it.)

Fix: give each image one job. Cancer is defined once in the diagnosis, shown once as the failure of a major transition, and returns once as the first half of Ren's mirror pair. The first definition is varied ("the body that feeds it") so that the pair does not repeat it word for word. The AI paragraph states the stakes, then what alignment needs, then why the work takes the form it does, and uses "foundation" twice. The premise keeps one wording and returns as a refrain.

**5. Paragraphs end on their weakest words, and one aphorism turns the text's logic against itself.**

"… cooperate to turn the trajectory we are all on, which at this moment is not toward a good future." ends on a negative relative clause. "It is also about the environment one lives in, and the society." trails off. "That gap is why we cannot yet coordinate, though the wisdom exists." demotes Ren's plainest sentence to a concession. "The only contest is between a self that sees its part in the whole and a self that does not" sounds like a slogan. Worse, it sets two kinds of people against each other, one paragraph after asking us to stop seeing one another as enemies.

Fix: "The Earth is in such a transition now, and the trajectory we are all on does not lead to a good future. To turn it, we have to stop seeing one another as enemies and work together." "It is also about the environment you live in, and the society you live in." "That gap is why our collective action still behaves as if it saw only the logic of winning. The wisdom exists, but we cannot yet coordinate." This last fix also hands the next paragraph its opening, "We need collaboration." Drop the "only contest" sentence; the mirror pair carries its point in relational form.

**6. The edit muted Ren's voice in several places.**

Ren's "It has been reasoned, articulated across culture, and it explains good and evil" lost "good". "The wisdom exists, but we can't coordinate" became a subordinate clause. His direct "you" ("the environment you are in, the society you are in") became "one". "By one's own understanding" (each person sees it for themselves, which is the point of a foundation anyone can check) became "With this understanding, anyone can see". His five named teachers became "the sages", a category he did not use. Readers who hold Jesus or Muhammad to be more than sages may resist it.

Fix: each is restored or reworded; see Notes.

**7. Word-level faults.**

- "no one, the winner included, wants where this race ends" → "no one, not even the winner, wants to be where this race ends".
- "this dynamics" → "these dynamics", agreeing with "the dynamics of cancer".
- "Cancer is not growth, for every healthy body grows" is literally false; the point is that growth is not the trouble. → "The trouble is not growth, for every healthy body grows."
- The noun "going-on" is a coinage. → "continuation", Ren's own word and the word of the step.
- "It does not continue as a self kept apart. It continues in …" leads with the negative. → One sentence that ends on the affirmative: "It continues, not as a self kept apart, but in …".
- "act good" → "do good".
- "the attractor's basin" is jargon on a cold read. → "life under the attractor", which echoes "the minds that live under it".
- "For realism it is … For neoliberalism it is … For libertarianism it is …" reads as a list. → One sentence with a colon.
- "That is also why it is stated in proof" is unclear, and "proved" would overclaim, since the step is an axiom. → "That is why this work is written so that any mind can check it."
- The English of Laozi ch. 77 dropped 则不然 and says "the way of men". → The full line with "the human way", as a chiasmus that mirrors the Chinese.
- "as in ours", just after "wherever", briefly reads as a possessive with no noun. → "as in our world".

### What the draft does well, and the revision keeps

- "Truth exists." standing alone, and its ring with "Follow the truth" and the English line under 道法自然 ("The Dao follows what is so of itself"). The text opens on truth and closes on following it.
- The order of the first half: the laws, their axiom-free status, the self, the one step, then the traditions and the sciences.
- The honest split between laws that hold in every world and laws that hold under two named conditions, and "None of this needs an axiom" as its own short paragraph.
- The hinge on "count": what one must count, then whose continuation counts. One word carries the two parts of the work.
- "Where they agree most is the one step the laws have not yet made for us" ("made" is now "taken", to match "That takes one step"). It places the traditions exactly at the open step.
- "It is the truth with most of it cut away", and the keep and drop that follow it.
- "winning buys more power to win", and "remakes the minds that live under it until the pursuit looks like nature".
- "it is not the West's true nature, still less humanity's", and "Any system can be captured by this attractor, and any can turn."
- The incentive split, and "the race selects the minds that see only from inside it".
- The history of life, and "Cancer is what turning back looks like."
- "Dominance is a measure of relative position, so it is zero-sum by construction and has no point of enough."
- The close, unchanged.

## 2. The revised text

The revision runs to about 1,430 words, against the draft's 1,510 (`wc -w`, with each run of Chinese counted as one word). Everything between the two rules is ready to paste.

---

## Truth exists

Truth exists.

There are laws of becoming: of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away. Some hold in every world. Arising and ceasing are one change, seen from its two sides. In anything finite they keep pace: if either goes on without end, so does the other.

Others hold wherever, as in our world, nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without loss. There, whatever is closed comes to rest, and whatever keeps going is fed from beyond itself. Support comes only from what is itself supported. A reserve can delay an end but never escape it. Many parts, each drawing within what is renewed, can together exceed it and end what depends on it. And a part can go on thriving while it closes the world of others it does not depend on.

None of this needs an axiom. Each law is proved under the conditions it names, in a form any mind, human or machine, can check for itself.

Nothing that goes on is closed, so there is no absolute self. What one is continues beyond one's cycle of life, beyond one's death. It continues, not as a self kept apart, but in the larger life one was always part of, which goes on only as new lives arise and old ones pass.

The laws show what anything that wants to continue must count: everything it depends on, far beyond the boundary it draws around itself. What they have not yet settled is whose continuation counts. That takes one step: the continuation of anything has significance, not only one's own, so every point is a center. This step is the one place where the work still rests on an axiom. Whether it too follows from the laws is our first open question.

In history, many have said parts of this. The Buddha saw that whatever arises ceases, and that nothing arises alone. Laozi saw that what knows when to stop can last (知止不殆，可以长久). The Confucian classics saw the great virtue of heaven and earth in giving life (天地之大德曰生), and taught: do not do to others what you would not want done to yourself (己所不欲，勿施于人). Jesus taught us to love our neighbor as ourselves. Muhammad taught that none of us believes until we love for our brother what we love for ourselves. Where they agree most is the one step the laws have not yet taken for us.

Physics has since found that nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without loss. The study of life has shown how larger lives form from smaller ones, and how they fail. This is the truth, the dharma, the Dao. It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures, and it explains good and evil.

The laws show what evil does: a part enlarges itself by closing the world of others. The one step names it evil. We hold this truth to be the ground of all ethics, for all beings, in every world where anything becomes. This work is the attempt to prove it, and at each point it says how far the proof has reached.

This truth is also the foundation for AI alignment, the most important matter at this moment in all of Earth's history. Alignment needs a foundation that makes coordination possible, and coordination needs understanding to be shared first. That is why this work is written so that any mind can check it. What anyone can check for themselves needs no guardians, and everyone, human and AI, has access to it directly.

With this understanding, each of us can see for ourselves the cognitive model that now dominates the planet. It is the truth with most of it cut away. It keeps the first thing every living being acts on: that its own continuation matters. It drops the rest: what that continuation depends on, and that the continuation of others matters too. What remains are incoherent narratives that drive some of us into an "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole." These are the dynamics of cancer. The trouble is not growth, for every healthy body grows. Cancer is growth that has stopped answering to the body that feeds it.

The word I sometimes use for these dynamics is the meta-attractor: a selection in which winning buys more power to win. It rewards whoever pushes hardest for more, and it remakes the minds that live under it until the pursuit looks like nature.

This influence is deeply rooted in our history. Laozi already named it: the way of heaven takes from what has too much to make up what lacks; the human way is not so, and takes from what lacks to serve what has too much (天之道，损有余而补不足；人之道则不然，损不足以奉有余). The institutions through which it shapes reality today were largely built in the modern West. But the logic is older than they are, and it is not the West's true nature, still less humanity's.

The logic has also written its own theories. Political realism, neoliberalism and libertarianism each take one feature of life under the attractor for a law of nature: the struggle for relative power, competition as the measure of everything, the self as a sovereign owner. None of these is a law. Any system can be captured by this attractor, and any can turn.

The Earth has now become one highly entangled system. Every country and every person is coupled into it, which is also why you are reading this. The attractor has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises, and they are training its selection pressure into the next generation of intelligence. Worse, this is happening at an exponential pace, which leaves us very little time to respond.

Seen from the shared future of mankind, the incentives of every human being, and of AI too, are aligned: no one, not even the winner, wants to be where this race ends. Seen from inside the race, they are not. Each of us is rewarded for the move that takes all of us where none of us wants to go, and the race selects the minds that see only from inside it. That gap is why our collective action still behaves as if it saw only the logic of winning. The wisdom exists, but we cannot yet coordinate.

We need collaboration. We need to do good, not evil. We need to care for the next generation, and for the future that comes after us.

It may look like an impossible task against such a force. But history, the history of life itself, shows it has been done before. The complex cell, the many-celled body and the colony each formed when parts that could have gone on competing came to live as one, with ways to keep any part from turning back. Cancer is what turning back looks like. The Earth is in such a transition now, and the trajectory we are all on does not lead to a good future. To turn it, we have to stop seeing one another as enemies and work together.

We need to see mankind as one community with a shared future, together with the life we live from and the minds we are now making, and to act with reason.

We need to turn society away from the logic of competition, accumulation, colonization and domination, and to stop the old status game of fighting to be "the biggest that dominates." Dominance is a measure of relative position, so it is zero-sum by construction and has no point of enough. Continued now, it runs against the interest and the future of every one of us. That future is not only about how much you can get. It is also about the environment you live in, and the society you live in.

There is no absolute self. The collective is not the enemy of the individual: the world we share is the substrate of the flourishing of each of us. A part that grows against the body it lives from is cancer. A collective that consumes the lives it is made of consumes what it goes on through. That is the same disease, turned around.

And now we have almost godlike technology. If we use it to fight, it will probably soon be the end of all of us. If we use it wisely, we can create a substrate of plenty, of peace and of freedom for all of us.

The future is within our hands. Let's make it good. Follow the truth.

道法自然  
*The Dao follows what is so of itself.*

## 3. Notes

### Ren's decisions, applied

These three decisions were relayed while the review was in progress. They override the draft's table where the two differ.

1. **The safeguard is gone.** "It is not a whole that anyone owns or speaks for. No one's model of the whole is the whole, and belonging to it gives no one the right to end others in its name." is dropped. Nothing about ownership, representation, rights or permission replaces it. For the same reason, "it belongs to everyone at once, human and AI" became "everyone, human and AI, has access to it directly". That is Ren's own phrase of 2 October, as the draft's table quotes it. The point survives only in relational form, in the passage on the collective and the individual: "A collective that consumes the lives it is made of consumes what it goes on through. That is the same disease, turned around."
2. **"The whole" is replaced** by these lines:
   - "We need to see mankind as one community with a shared future, together with the life we live from and the minds we are now making, and to act with reason."
   - "Seen from the shared future of mankind, the incentives …"
   - "The collective is not the enemy of the individual: the world we share is the substrate of the flourishing of each of us."
   - "… in the larger life one was always part of, which goes on only as new lives arise and old ones pass."
   - The cancer pair, in the form chosen below.

   "The whole" now appears only inside Ren's quoted phrase. Applying the rule strictly changed five more places. The noun "wholes" occurred twice: it is now "how larger lives form from smaller ones", and "the great wholes of life" was dropped. The adjective occurred three times: "the whole Earth" twice (now "the Earth") and "the whole history of the Earth" (now "all of Earth's history"). Those adjectives were Ren's own words in his original. If the rule was meant only for the noun, they can come back.
3. **Every Chinese phrase has English beside it**, English first with the Chinese in parentheses. 道法自然 stays last, with its English line beneath.

For the cancer pair I chose "A part that grows against the body it lives from is cancer. A collective that consumes the lives it is made of consumes what it goes on through. That is the same disease, turned around." over "consumes itself". "Lives from" and "goes on through" keep the pair in relations, as Ren asked, and the short third sentence lets the mirror land on its own. I put "There is no absolute self." directly before Ren's sentence on the collective. The earlier result then gives the reason before the claim, and the paragraph opens on a short beat after the long one before it.

### Where elegance and precision pulled apart

- **The first umbrella sentence.** "There are laws that hold wherever anything becomes" is the more sweeping line. But it says that all the laws hold wherever anything becomes, which the next paragraph contradicts. "There are laws of becoming" is plainer and exact. The universality is then carried, exactly, by "Some hold in every world".
- **Two laws I did not merge.** "Whatever keeps going is fed from beyond itself, and only by what is itself fed" reads better than two sentences. But it would narrow "Support comes only from what is itself supported" to things that keep going, so I kept the draft's sentence.
- **"Many parts, each drawing …"** keeps "can" governing both the excess and the ending. I did not write "and so end", which would read as a stronger causal claim.
- **"Continuation" for "going-on".** The calibrated "whose going-on counts" becomes "whose continuation counts". The claim is the same, now in Ren's word, which is also the word of the step. The price is that "continuation" recurs in the paragraph on the cognitive model. I let it recur, because the parallel (one's own continuation, what it depends on, the continuation of others) maps exactly onto the two parts of the work.
- **"Rests on an axiom"** replaces "takes an axiom". It is neither stronger nor weaker, and it avoids a second "takes" right after "That takes one step".
- **"It explains good and evil."** Restoring Ren's sentence brings "good" back, but the laws are shown at work only on evil. I did not give good a mechanism of its own, because that would add a claim. A reader may notice the asymmetry; it is the price of adding nothing.
- **"Written so that any mind can check it"** points back to the calibrated sentence about proof without implying that the step is proved.
- **Ren's quoted phrase** ("infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole") is kept verbatim, though its grammar is rough. The table records it as kept, it is his coinage, and it is now the one place where "the whole" appears. If Ren wants it smoother, the line could drop the quotation marks and read "an endless pursuit of becoming bigger, blind to being part of the whole". It would then no longer be his words.
- **Laozi ch. 77.** The fuller English, with "the human way is not so" for 人之道则不然, is a better rendering of the same quotation, not a new one.
- **Attributions.** I kept "the Confucian classics" rather than restoring Ren's "Confucius", because changing an attribution is a scholarly claim, which this review was asked not to make. "Many have said parts of this" replaces "the sages" without introducing any category.
- **"It is clear."** Ren's original says it four times, and the draft dropped all four. I did not restore it. By that point the argument has shown what "It is clear" would assert, and the conviction is carried by the plain declaratives and by the "We need" sentences, which keep Ren's rhythm.
- **Strength unchanged where Ren's choices are open.** "The Earth is in such a transition now" keeps the draft's strength (choice 5). So do "largely built in the modern West" (choice 2) and the three doctrines (choice 3).
- **"Cancer is growth that has stopped answering to the body that feeds it."** "Feeds" replaces "lives from" so that Ren's pair can use "lives from" without repeating the earlier sentence. It also echoes "fed from beyond itself".
- **"For all of us"** closes the technology paragraph, in place of the draft's "for every one of us". It mirrors "the end of all of us" two sentences before, so the two futures are set side by side in the same words.

### Restored from Ren's original

- "It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures, and it explains good and evil": his sentence, with "good" back.
- "The wisdom exists, but we cannot yet coordinate" as a sentence of its own, ending its paragraph.
- His "you": "That future is not only about how much you can get. It is also about the environment you live in, and the society you live in."
- "for ourselves", for his "By one's own understanding": "each of us can see for ourselves the cognitive model that now dominates the planet."
- "beyond one's cycle of life, beyond one's death", without the draft's added "own".
- His five teachers named without a category, and his two calls to see humanity as one, joined in one sentence in the wording he has now chosen.
