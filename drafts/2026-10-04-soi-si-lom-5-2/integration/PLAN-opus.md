## Summary

**What is new or sharper, ranked by what it does for the theory.**
1. **Selection runs at the speed of power** (I81, I91; 33:11, 36:08). A race that makes everyone run faster selects against decision models, and the faster power changes hands, the stronger the effect. This reframes MA open item 31, which holds that gain levers move only the date, not the destination. Once correction is limited by its own rate, tempo decides where the system ends. Construction (character education, I82) and selection (power's rewards, I90) now come in Ren's own sequence. That is a first answer to DR open questions 8 and 14.
2. **Trust and verification are coupled** (I71, I115, I118, I134; 30:17, 41:57, 43:09, 47:25). The line 验证放大信任，但不产生信任 ("verification amplifies trust but does not create it") was in a pasted text of 10-03 whose author was not stated. Here Ren says it in his own voice and extends it to transparency. Trust in turn rests on credible information. With "there is no safe way to win" (46:20, which makes T < R), the three lines line up with the 10-03 formal sketch.
3. **Asymmetric proposals are read as the logic of winning** (I138, I139; 48:33, 49:09). The other side reads a "win safely" discourse as hegemony, and confrontation then looks obvious to it. This turns symmetric application (善不姓国, the blade) from an ethical clause into a condition of being believed.
4. **A new reading of "no self"** (I12, I13; 05:26). 逍遥 is a cognitive model that fits reality, and in that state there is no independent self. RS's open question holds that "beyond the drive, there is no self" must be either deflationary or mystical. This is a third, epistemic reading: the separate self is a model's boundary error. It is also the positive opposite of the boundary error (V61 Proposition 3.6).
5. **心理 as 心 and 理, and the frame that calls correction violence** (I4, I8, I97; 00:39, 02:57, 37:15).
   - 理 is the negative feedback inside a person that keeps 心's positive feedback from running away. It names the function the 2026-04 Moloch essays said had "no name left".
   - The jungle frame recodes rules imposed by others as violence. That is a named channel by which the constraint is corrupted before it acts.
   - Also sharper, in brief: the training ground (44:20), relational reasoning as constraint-first (07:59), acceleration needs a direction (36:49, 24:19), Moloch and Leviathan as one frame (46:38), self-alignment as the competence in question (37:35), the legitimating narrative as a ledger (18:05), 天子 (26:35), and why the copies fight (27:56, Ren's answer to MA open question 46).

**The three most important tensions** (argued in §5).
1. **"We do need to win" against "oppose the logic first."** 21:32 and 40:22 sit against Ren's 10-03 ruling. Read as a description, they are the trap told from inside, a live case of V61's "an actor with a wider objective still races if it believes its rivals hold Ψ". Read as a stance, they are an adaptation that the ruling forbids as a remedy. The theory should carry them as the first reading. It should also separate the capacity not to be beaten (护闭, bounded and checked by the custody rules) from winning.
2. **State narrative read as legitimate direction-setting.** 17:27, 18:05, 19:03, 38:55 and 39:22 go beyond Ren's own rule of 10-02, "don't read them as a believer", and beyond RS claim 30. The plan holds every polity's legitimating narrative to one standard, defined in §5(b):
   - its direction, judged by the corpus's own definitions;
   - a ledger that its sponsor's conduct can be held to, not evidence of that conduct;
   - whether the people it shapes can contest it.
3. **The China–West frame.** The 10-03 ruling says to argue about the dynamic, not a pair of countries, and 49:59–50:12 say the same in this recording. The plan states the mechanism generically in every argument body, keeps "西方" in the own-words entries, and uses the recording's own evidence that the frame runs on both sides: 21:32 enacts it, and its own image of 护城墙 at 35:15 is the 护城河 the corpus records as Chinese business-speak (DR claim 62).

### 3.1 `theories/relational-self-and-datong/README.md`

**(1) Argument.** *Anchor:* in `## The argument`, after the paragraph that begins "Register two returns as well, as what he calls "a new theory"." and ends "…the etymology should not be offered as evidence for it.", and before the `---` that precedes `## Claims`.

> **2026-10-04: the relational self as a way of deciding, and what fit with the world is like.** [Provenance paragraph, full form.] The recording adds three things to this theory. It also needs a ruling from the theory, on the state's narratives.
>
> **A procedure for deciding.** The theory has said what a relational self is. The talk says how such a self decides, and it begins from the situation, not from a goal:
> - 「当下，我与周围环境的关系是什么样的？我的约束条件是什么？在这样的情境之中，我可以做什么？」 [07:59] (*what is my relation to my surroundings now, what are my constraints, what can I do in this situation?*);
> - 「而不是围绕某一个价值，去优化一个既定的目标。」 [07:59] (*rather than optimising a fixed target around one value*);
> - 「你的能力越大，你在这种“因应情境而行动”的过程中所占的权重也就越大。」 [11:04] (*the greater your capability, the more your acting on the situation weighs*). This is claim 24's 力之所及，责之所在 restated as the weight of a decision.
>
> The difference between the two procedures is a mechanism, not a temperament.
> - A decision built around one value and its holder draws a boundary around what it counts. Everything outside the boundary becomes a constraint or a resource.
> - A decision built from relations puts the effects on the web inside the problem.
>
> The first is the bounded self that the 2026-10-02 conversation named as the disease (claim 41), seen as a way of posing problems.
>
> This gives the theory a joint that its own critique below says is missing ("joined by assertion, not derivation"). Where winning compounds, a target-first decider is rewarded for the costs it leaves outside its boundary, so selection favours it, while a relations-first decider bears those costs. The 2026-10-03 paper proves the formal version: a self-model narrower than the real coupling raises intensity and lowers its holder's own welfare, and selection favours it anyway (`drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v6.1/THEORY.md`, Proposition 3.6).
>

> 1. *Rounds.* Selection acts each time positions of power are contested again. For a fixed advantage per round, more rounds per year means more selection per year on whatever wins rounds.

> - The first step of trust is to stop presuming the other's type: 「所以，为什么一开始就认定对方不可信？我们先尝试接触」 [41:57] (*so why decide from the start that the other side cannot be trusted? Let us first try making contact*).
>

> - Verification can make messages credible about what it can see.
> - It cannot see future commitments or intentions.
> - It cannot even be installed without some prior belief about the other, nor can its reports be read as information rather than as moves.
>
> The formal sketch Ren pasted on 10-03 (its author is not stated) says the same:
> - Cooperation is a best response only when the belief that the other will cooperate exceeds a threshold p*.
> - Verification lowers that threshold, by lowering the cost of being the one who cooperated, but it cannot carry anyone across it.
> - If winning is believed to be worth more than cooperating (T > R), no level of trust is enough.
>

> - 「没有办法这样安全地赢。」 [46:20] (*there is no way to win safely like this*) is the claim that moves T below R;
> - 41:57 refuses to start the belief at zero;
> - 47:25 says verification cannot stand in for the belief.

### (b) The official narratives, under one standard

**What the talk says.**

**What the corpus already holds.**
- *Loop 3 of the spiral.* Winners buy discourse until their logic reads as human nature.
- *The drives theory.* Narrative reaches the drive system ahead of evaluation; "Propaganda does not work because people are stupid".
- *Ren's own limit of 2026-10-02.* "don't read them as a believer … on the meta level, what are they trying to do?"
- *RS claims 29–30.* Accept 人类命运共同体 on its merits, regardless of sponsor, and articulate it apart from state framing.
- *RS's critique of 大道之行.* It argues from one state's institutional capacity in a corpus whose doctrine is the danger of concentrated, weakly checked power; "力愈大者，受调当愈严".
- *The asymmetric-scrutiny rule (RS claim 35).* Western political vocabulary should not travel unmarked while non-Western terms are flagged.

**The standard has to be symmetric in both directions.** It must not scrutinise Chinese state narrative more than Western narratives, and it must not scrutinise it less. That rules out two easy moves.
- Treating 39:22 as disqualifying because it praises a state narrative would apply to Chinese narrative a suspicion the corpus does not apply to "freedom", "democracy" or "abundance through progress". The corpus does criticise those, but as claims, not as contaminated vocabulary.
- Adopting 39:22 into the argument because it is Ren's would exempt one state's narrative from the capture loop the corpus applies to every winner's discourse.

**The plan proposes one standard with three parts, applied to every polity alike.** It is drafted in RS and OQ §2.
1. *Direction.* Judge a narrative's content by the corpus's own definitions. By SB claim 343, AI向善 and AI普惠 pass as direction: 普惠 is "真正扩大所有人的未来", and 向善 is not resting on "剥削、殖民、操纵、欺骗、压缩认知和关闭未来". The talk's gloss, "不能断了子孙后代的路", reads the slogans the way the corpus does. Western formulas such as "AI for the benefit of all humanity" pass or fail by the same definitions.
2. *Ledger, not evidence.* A legitimating narrative supplies the terms in which power must justify itself. That makes it a lever: conduct can be demanded in those terms, which is the useful half of 18:05. It is not evidence of conduct, and it can be emptied into cover (伪开). This is the corpus's existing treatment of the San Francisco abundance narrative (SS 2026-10-02), applied to every state.
3. *Contestability.* Narrative works ahead of evaluation, so the legitimacy of shaping expectations comes from whether the people shaped can contest and correct it. That is the corpus's V3 and its 公开可纠. The same test applies to platforms, advertising and Western security narratives. On this test, 39:22's "most legitimacy and historical height" is a claim the corpus cannot carry as stated for any state. It can carry a narrower one: a legitimating narrative that the governed can invoke against the governors is a real constraint, wherever it is found.

**How to record it.** Record 39:22 faithfully, as Ren's view and in his words (RS own words 72), with his hedge at 38:55. Carry it in the argument only under the standard above. Name the persons at 17:27 only in the own-words entry (RS 71).

**Where it lands.** The symmetric case the corpus keeps deferring (ZS found by reading 4 and open question 25; OQ §4.6) is no longer only an honesty debt. By the talk's own argument at 49:09, it is what makes any of this hearable.

### (c) "In this order we do need to win"

**The passages.**
- 21:32: 「当下我们处在一个西方的秩序里面，而这个秩序本身就在斗争，落后就要挨打。」 [21:32] (*we are now inside a Western order, and that order is itself a struggle; fall behind and you get beaten*), and then 「在这个过程中，我们确实需要赢。」 [21:32] (*in this process, we really do need to win*).
- 40:22: struggle with compounding grows fastest 「在余量足够的时候」 [40:22] (*when there is enough slack*), so a participant must enter its logic.

**The ruling of 10-03:** "如果胜利者的逻辑是cancer式的，落后并不可以承受，因此所有人都应该去首先反对这个逻辑".

**The two share a premise.** Both say falling behind cannot be borne. 落后就要挨打 and 落后并不可以承受 are nearly the same sentence. They diverge on the conclusion: adapt and win, or oppose the logic. Ren gave the ruling against an assistant's proposal to make falling behind survivable. The ruling says that against a cancer-like winner nothing makes falling behind survivable, so the only remedy is to change the logic for everyone.

**Description or concession.** The passages can be read three ways.
1. *As description,* they are the trap told from inside. 40:48 adds that this is not the best the tradition recognises, and 22:14 turns at once to 止戈 as "the other direction". This is the corpus's central mechanism in the first person: an actor whose own objective is wider still races, because it reads the order as struggle and believes falling behind is fatal. V61 §3.8 states it as "Ψ shapes conduct where it is held and where it is attributed". Taken this way, the passages are evidence for the theory, and among the best it has, because they come from the theory's author.
2. *As a stance,* they are an adaptation. The 10-03 ruling forbids adaptation as a remedy, and the corpus's own diagnosis predicts its result: the participant who enters the logic to survive is selected by it (molochlization), with "good values as the upper structures" (MA claim 279).
3. *A charitable middle reading* separates competence from aim. Understanding Legalism and the 孙子 is not the same as adopting winning as the aim. A tradition can know the logic of struggle in order not to be exploited by it.

**What the theory should do.** Carry the first reading and make the third explicit, as drafted in PS and OQ §2.
- *The capacity not to be beaten* is 护闭: closing here so that a wider field stays open. It is bounded, it reopens, it is answerable to the custody rules, and it is never aimed at decisive advantage.
- *Winning* is the logic to be opposed.

The speech runs the two together, and the theory has to keep them apart.

**The slack condition at 40:22 helps.** It concedes that the fastest growth is the struggler's share while the host has reserves. 35:15 then says what follows when the reserves run out. Read together, 40:22 and 35:15 are the cancer image made into a claim: struggle grows fastest while there is something left to consume.

**One fact to record.** The 10-03 ruling has already dropped out of the v6.1 draft. Only half of it survives in DISCUSSION X1 (`v6.1/reviews/R-v6.1-fidelity.md`). The recording is a reason to restore it (§6).

### (d) Trust against verification, and how trust is built

**The positions.**
- *47:25:* 「无论你是建立核查机制（verification），还是提高透明度（transparency），只要没有信任，这些都白搭。」 [47:25] (*whether you set up verification or raise transparency, without trust none of it helps*).
- *Ren, 2026-10-02, on open models:* the direction is right only "if … there are verification uh processes that you cannot fine tune those models or train those models to do other dangerous things" (AS claim 210).
- *OCR §4, from Ren's 10-03 critique:* verification changes q, the probability of being caught, not G, the value of winning.
- *V61 thesis 9:* "Trust is an outcome of a staged transition" (§1.2). The Chinese guide adds "信任是分阶段过渡的结果，不是前提". The §11 claim box: "Trust is mainly an outcome of the transition, not its precondition."

**What verification can and cannot do.** Verification establishes present facts on dimensions it can see: capabilities, custody, compliance now. It cannot establish future commitments or intentions. Realism's strongest reply to verified conduct is that "conduct verified today does not settle uncertainty about a rival's future intentions" (V61 DISCUSSION X1). In Ren's own two-part definition of 10-03, trust is that "其他方的消息可信" (the other side's messages are credible) "并可守承诺" (and its commitments will be kept). Verification can supply the first part. It cannot supply the second.

**What trust adds.**
1. *A belief about the remainder* that verification cannot see: intentions, future conduct, and the dimensions where verification is impossible or would itself become a capture surface.
2. *The first step.* Installing verification needs prior trust: the rival must accept instruments in its own facilities, and their reports must be believed not to be staged. V61 §11.1 already says this: installing instruments "is a first step taken under ϖ". The 10-03 pasted text says it too: "验证设施本身也需要事先的信任".
3. *A bound on verification.* The less trust there is, the more intrusive the verification demanded, and verification demanded at low trust is the kind that becomes surveillance, the corpus's "assurance without surveillance" frontier. Trust is what keeps verification from turning into a capture surface. This mechanism is not stated anywhere in the corpus. It is drafted in PS and GL *trust*.

**How trust is built, if not partly by verification?** Partly by verification. The talk says so itself: trust 「当然，这也要建立在信息可信的基础上。」 [30:17] (*naturally, this too has to rest on information being credible*). The honest synthesis is that the two are coupled, not ranked. The other routes, all in the corpus or the talk:
- *Change what winning is worth.* Shared understanding that no one can win safely (46:20) pushes T below R, and that lowers the trust any cooperation requires.
- *Costly, reciprocated, bounded steps.* Examples are GRIT-style ladders (OCR's proposed fix for Track 1) and V61 Proposition 11.2, where verifiably and durably removing AI from one's own strategic decision loops separates types.
- *Symmetric proposals* (49:09).
- *Correcting misattribution* by understanding the other side's frame (19:49, 41:03).
- *Forming actors who keep promises* (34:03), and stopping a selection that removes them (36:08). In the long run, the supply of trustworthy actors depends on what power selects.

**The 10-02 open-model condition is consistent with 47:25.** That condition verifies a property of an artifact. It also had a second clause (AS claim 210: "only if the compounding dynamics are resolved"), and that clause plays the role trust plays in 47:25.

**V61 thesis 9 and the recording fit if "precondition" is read relatively.** Trust is not a precondition of coordination, because shared seeing produces it (the 2025 position, SS claim 121). It is a precondition of verification working. The thesis is right about the first and should say the second.

### (e) Empirical claims that need evidence or tiering

Each claim is tiered below, with what would test it. The test rows are drafted for OQ §5.

### (f) 止戈为武

### (g) Other tensions, including tensions with earlier spoken positions

1. **Legalism as competence.** At 15:42 and 21:32 the talk presents Legalism and the 孙子 as the tradition's competence for struggle. Elsewhere the corpus treats them otherwise:

   The way through: as a theory of how status and fear move conduct, Legalism is evidence for the corpus; as a programme for winning, it is the logic opposed (DR open question 18).
2. **Not hostile to power** (21:00). Relational thought, the talk says, does not always meet power with fear, hostility and confrontation. This is consistent with V61 V4, "Authority is a productive capacity". It is in tension with the corpus's structural suspicion of concentrated, weakly checked power ("力愈大者，受调当愈严"). The two can be held together if attitude and structure are separated: no reflexive hostility to authority, and checks in proportion to reach. The talk's own 36:08, that power selects dark traits, applies to every power system without national exemption, including the one whose documents it praises.
3. **Relational thought does not license domination** (14:07). This sits against the corpus's own historical concessions: "dynastic China extracted at scale" (RS claim 34); Confucian logic "became the value system *of whoever held power*". It also sits against MA's claim that selection acts on cognition, which would include relational cognition (RS Relations, the 《间》 objection). The talk itself concedes the selection at 15:42. The way through is that the frame does not license domination, but the frame alone does not prevent selection either (RS open question).
4. **天子 against the guardian test** (26:35). This is drafted in RS and GL: 天子 is legitimate as a ledger, and a better guardian as a person. The status ladder at 33:11 sits under the 天子.
5. **Political realism must be put down** (43:09). V61 describes itself as "defensive realism with one more variable" (§7.2 and X1). The v6.1 fidelity review already proposes "nearest neighbour in defensive realism, which it extends by a hazard that endangers its holder and departs from by treating self-help as a selected institution". The recording supports that change (§6).
6. **The conditional opening at 48:06.** "Unless AI has the capability and is aligned with us" sits between two earlier positions: the 2025 delegation hedge ("only in theory") and Ren's 10-03 demand that the theory derive that AI-assisted oversight cannot keep coverage. It is closed by the talk's own 37:35, 44:44 and 45:06, as drafted in AS. If it is quoted alone, it will be read as permission.

8. **The removal of 善不姓国 from the community document against 49:09.** On 2026-09-26 Ren asked that phrases such as 善不姓国 be avoided for political reasons. The AI draft kept the function under 天下为公 and 己所不欲. The recording argues that asymmetric proposals defeat themselves. 9. **Western categories for "ontology"** (22:46) against Ren's own "use the the pre-Qin ontology as the basis" (10-02). This is a minor tension. The asymmetric-scrutiny rule cuts both ways, and the corpus should say what the Western category loses (JW open question 65).
