# Opening a Road, or Holding a Gate

### I. A society picks one value as the token of power

On 14 July 2024, before any of the later vocabulary existed, Ren wrote one sentence in Chinese that the rest of this essay is downstream of: 这个社会选择了某一种value作为权利的象征，最擅长于去优化这些value的人，在竞争的过程中会逐渐获得权利，从而以他们的方式和思考来控制我们的社会系统. A society picks one value as the token of power. The people best at optimising that value gradually acquire power through competition. And then they run the social system through their own way of seeing. The first thing to say about the sentence is that it is not an accusation, and the second is that it describes a mechanism rather than a mood, which is why it has survived four changes of name.

The mechanism needs one condition and nothing else: a game in which what you win is yours and can be used to win more. Given that, four things follow in series. Three of them were written out in one message on 3 February 2025, and the fourth nineteen days later in five lines. Advantage converts into the capacity to gain advantage, so the more balanced ways of acting are not refuted, they are out-competed. The winners arrive at the place where rules are written and write them, so that everyone else must adopt the same pattern to survive, and must assume that everyone else already has. Accumulated power buys the story: the winners' logic becomes the common sense the next generation is built out of, and at the limit it reads as human nature, at which point the people it harms defend it. And the regulator that every society keeps against exactly this, its norms and laws and shared understanding, only regulates if it updates as fast as the world changes; in an accelerating society it cannot, and the gap between the world's rate of change and the regulator's update rate is precisely the space in which the compounding runs. Ren's own summary, asking only whether the logic holds: "I am not good at english but you see my complete logic right? If a system reward winner more edge to win, eventually the system go into winner takes all, but also winner justifies all."

Two things keep this from being a complaint about rich people. The first is that the token does not matter. "any competitive advantage accumulation game, in nature, will fall for this cycle, in the dynamics, the power can be replaced with capital, status, or any tokens" (2024-11-05). Attention, rank, compute, followers, citations, model capability: any axis on which winning buys more capacity to win will do. The second is that no one has to be steering. "it seems to me, not the capitalists or a small wealthy people are controlling the economy, but the optimization of the capital itself. it seems the society itself is optimizing its capital gain over its people" (2024-08-11). That sentence is what turned a grievance into a dynamical object. The winners are, on the whole, sincere; the pattern selects for sincerity, since a person who believes the metric is the good is a more reliable carrier of it than a cynic. Stated in the vocabulary of selfishness, as the 2025 drafts did, the argument invites the reply that not everyone is selfish, and the reply misses it entirely. Nothing here is about persons. It is about which persons a game promotes, and what the promoted then do to the game.

Very little of this is unheard of, and an essay that pretended otherwise would lose its reader on the first page. Cumulative advantage has a literature going back to Merton. The claim that power works by shaping what people want is Lukes's third face of power and Gramsci's hegemony. The claim that a metric outlives its own refutation is Espeland and Sauder's finding about rankings. The runaway structure in which the winners of a round acquire influence over what counts as winning is Boyd and Richerson's indirect bias, formally the same object as Fisher's runaway. In the AI literature, structural risk, gradual disempowerment, Christiano's first failure mode and the multi-principal problem in ARCHES each already hold a piece of this without a villain. Ren asked, in February 2025, "Did someone else also sees this or did I discover it?" The honest answer is that others saw most of it. What this line adds is narrower, and it will be stated where it earns it: that the loop closes through the mind, that this is why an obedient intelligence is not a remedy, and that the selection reaches the machine by two channels nobody else has traced.

### II. The gate

The 2026 vocabulary gives the loop a shape a reader can point at, and it comes from the Chinese line rather than the English one. Evil is given a structural definition: 使生命必须过门，然后拥有那扇门, to see that life must pass through some place and then to own that place [AI-drafted from Ren's direction, kept through every version]. Gates come in three layers. There are gates of things, 据物之门, where necessities are held; gates of rules, 据规之门, where the procedures and standards that decide what counts are held; and gates of minds, 据心之门, where the way judgment itself is formed is held. Most gates are not designed. They are rewarded into existence: the loop first blindly rewards whoever happens to stand at a passage, and then rewards the knowing refinement of standing there. 无知而据门，是愚；明知而精炼，是恶 [AI-drafted, kept]: to hold a gate without knowing is stupidity; to refine the holding knowingly is the thing the word evil is reserved for.

The mind gate is upstream of the other two, and it is where the loop goes inside. Ren said this most directly out loud, in the spoken record of July 2026: "You are in an environment that keeps selecting winners and make them to define the rule of society. And that mechanism is competition. … The conclusion that guides your actions defines your reality. And eventually, you might be proud of that is who you are." What concentrates is not capital only but a way of seeing. This is the reason structural reform on its own does not dislodge the pattern: the reformers were formed inside it, and the institutions they reach for were staffed by its selection. It is also why Ren could write, to the model that was drafting his own whitepaper, 我们为什么必须加速？这个完全不合理，这是西方当代叙事进入totalizing attractor，然后产生的文本进入了你的训练导致的吧 (2026-07-03): why must we accelerate? this is contemporary Western narrative entering the totalising attractor, and the text it produced entering your training. The mind gate is not a metaphor. It has a pipeline: corpus selection, charter drafting, evaluation design, deployment gates, the whole apparatus that forms minds that do not yet exist, which the whitepaper calls 铸心之门, the mind-casting gate.

Here the argument reaches artificial intelligence, and it is worth being exact about which part of what follows is new, because the field has a vocabulary and misusing it is fatal. The field calls making a system do what its principal intends intent alignment, and it calls the problem between principals structural or multi-agent risk; it already knows that aligning each system to its own principal leaves the game between principals unaligned, which is why cooperative-AI research exists. Ren's own phrase is that "perfect inner alignment" does not save us (2026-06-03), and he calls the game between principals outer alignment; the words are kept because they are his, but the claim they carry is, in the field's terms, one it already presupposes. What his line adds is one level below. The standard framing takes the principal's ends as given and asks whose ends to serve. This line says the game selects which cognition occupies the principal's seat, so that the ends a faithfully aligned system serves are an output of the dynamic and not an input to it. "AI makes it much worse because AI strengthens the very capacities that competitive systems reward. So if not handled with care, instead of making humanity wiser, AI may make the most aggressive strategies more powerful" (2026-06-04). An intelligence perfectly loyal to one part of humanity against the rest, polite at the interface and extractive in structure, is not a hypothetical failure mode. It is what faithful service to a selected principal looks like from the outside.

And the selection reaches the model itself, by two channels, both stated by Ren in a single editing session on 3 July 2026 and neither developed since. The first is the loss function. Correcting a drafting agent that had written that training is first-order and the model cannot touch its objective, he wrote: 训练并不独立于训练环境，损失函数会间接的被模型影响; and then, 一轮训练的不及预期，或者训练的完成品无法产生足够profit - 都会直接或者间接的修改训练环境和损失函数. A training run that disappoints, or a product that does not pay, modifies the training environment and the objective, directly or through the people who decide what to run next; commercial pressure is inside the loop, not outside it. The second is the corpus. The attractor's own narratives, its sense of what is inevitable and rational and serious, are already in the pretraining data, so a model's picture of what is possible is partly an artifact of the selection that wrote its data. From the two together he drew the strongest form of the claim: 我们不能在这样的dynamics中，安全的训练出不被这个attractor capture的ai, inside these dynamics you cannot safely train an intelligence that is not itself captured. That is the sentence a hostile reader will attack first, and Ren scoped it himself in the same conversation: 我不是说这是一个真理或者必然，我们可以把发展速度降下来. It is a claim about the present configuration, not a law of nature, and its whole content is that the configuration can be changed.

### III. Gain, not pattern

Manufactured intelligence does not create the pattern. It raises the gain on it. The very first sentence in the conversation record, from 18 March 2024, already has the shape: "The speed of the complexity of society grows exp, the speed of the governing organizations' understanding reality only grows in a sub-linear speed." Within the same session he corrected his own conclusion, from collapse to unmanaged risk, "just like cells die," and that correction is the load-bearing move: what fails first is not the society but its correction rate. Two years later, in the founding session of the foundation documents, the same thought at full volume: "We are pushing the world to go into exponential complexity growth, but our minds and institutions, only can catch up at linear, or sub linear speed - this is madness, we have lost the principle of common sense" (2026-06-05).

The August 2026 position paper makes this measurable, and it is the most defensible statement of the prescription anywhere in the theories. Capability compounds with inputs that can be parallelised: data, compute, search. Judgment depends on consequence feedback, and the formation of consequence feedback has a floor: reflexive systems change when observed, and institutions and ecologies run on clocks that do not speed up. So the gap between what an actor can do and what it can understand about the situation it does it in widens by default, and manufactured intelligence accelerates the very environment that has to be understood. The claim needs one scoping that the paper does not give it and that its first reviewer will demand. The floor is incompressible only in domains without a cheap verifier. Wherever a faithful simulator exists, self-play and synthetic feedback have repeatedly stood in for real consequences, and those are precisely the domains where capability compounds fastest. So the gap does not widen everywhere; it widens where the rules metabolise slowly and closes where they can be simulated. That scoping is not a concession. It is the prescription itself, derived rather than asserted: no game may be accelerated past the metabolic rate of its own rules, and the domains to be named first are the ones whose rules metabolise slowest, which are the substrate of social coordination: labour markets, finance, politics.

What follows is an ordering claim and not a doom claim, and the distinction is the difference between this line and the genre it resembles. Ren retired the terminal image himself: "I also don't hold the terminal outcome will be doom or feudalism as a belief" (2026-04-16). The pattern is not fated. Positive feedback saturates, oscillates, splits and breaks; energetic ceilings, cultural heterogeneity, delay and the reflexive awareness of the people inside it all cap it, which is why the meta-attractor line came to call it a hurricane rather than a black hole. "look at the ecosystem, death is not a bug but a feature, changes and mutations are what keeps things alive - create a rich space of systems, not reducing to one rule, one attractor" (2026-06-05). The order of operations is the whole content: change the basin, then add speed. Adding speed first is what the last three years have been. That is the diagnosis. It has been written some thirty times in this corpus, and this is its one pass here; from this point it is a citation, and the weight of the essay goes to the half that has been written once.

### IV. What is being kept

The affirmative half begins with the smallest thing Ren says about it. On 7 July 2026, having written five self-binding principles for his own organisation in his own hand, he threw them out for their shape: 这些本身就在画地为牢，你能意识到吗？, these themselves are drawing a prison on the ground, can you see that? And he collapsed the whole apparatus into one job: 我们其实主要把keep possibilities space open说清楚就行. Mostly we just need to say keep the possibility space open clearly. The earliest form is four months older and plainer: "what I'm asking for is not to get into a particular state or demanding my reality to be the reality, but I think it it is important to keep a open loop" (2026-03-14). What follows is the attempt to say it clearly, and most of the work is saying what it is not.

It is not a quantity. Not more options, not more complexity, not a number that could go up. The Chinese line has the sharpest test: 繁简非开闭之标, complexity and simplicity are not the measure of open and closed [AI-drafted in dialogue, adopted]. A tea ceremony can be extremely open and a totalitarian state extremely closed while being vastly larger. What openness has instead of a magnitude is four conditions that must hold together, and the four are the single most useful thing the Chinese texts add to the English ones. 未: something not yet become remains, not "the future" as a timeline but the unfinished as such. 可: becoming is still reachable, and the possible is itself still being generated rather than drawn from a fixed list. 应: the process can still respond to a perturbation; a fully closed system can only repeat itself. 转: the process can still turn, correct, return, transform, which is why openness natively contains non-extremity. Any one missing and it is not whole. Call the condition 开.

The word was chosen on 2 May 2026 to replace "expanding the possibility space," and the reason for the replacement is the discipline of the whole idea. Expansion sounds measurable, and anything measurable can be maximised, and any maximand has a hidden pole. 善不在于最大化什么，善在于不闭何处 [AI-drafted, kept through every version]: the good does not consist in maximising anything; it consists in where you do not close. Ren said the same in English a month later, correcting a draft: "we should keep possibility open - which means not collapse, but not optimizing it" (2026-06-04). The English foundation documents kept the expansion verb anyway, and the alignment paper's engineering advice still reads "expand possibility space at all scales," which is exactly the formulation the Chinese line rejected. Openness is a floor, not a target. A project that sets out to maximise it has already become what it was meant to prevent.

There is a root under this that predates everything else in the corpus. A single page from April 2022 stipulates, in order: difference, structure, change, space, time, dynamic, possibility, thing, manifestation, system. "If change may happen to a structure, it possesses possibility." A system is "a thing that maintains certain dynamic pattern over time." The page admits it cannot define every term without becoming self-referential, and then stops; there is no chapter behind the chapter heading. What it supplies is that possibility is a term with a construction behind it rather than a value word, and that existence is pattern maintained through change rather than substance. What it cannot say is why any possibility would close. There is no agent in it, no feedback, no compounding. That took three years to arrive, and it arrived as the mechanism in Part I.

### V. Four ways to close, and one of them is the thing

The Chinese line's second gift is a taxonomy, and without it the commitment is unusable. Ren adopted the formulation in the same May conversation: 生生不息者，开而不闭也；善者，护开也；恶者，成闭也. Unceasing generation is being open and not closing; the good is protecting the open; the bad is closure that completes. Then the distinction that does the work: 闭有二义：一曰成闭，闭而不复开；一曰护闭，暂闭一处以护更大之开. Closure has two senses. 成闭 is closing that never reopens. 护闭 is closing one place, for a time, so that a wider field stays open. If the second sense did not exist, then a quarantine, a border against an invader, an excised tumour, a halted runaway process and a boundary set against a manipulator would all count as evil, and "keep the world open" would collapse into never refusing anything. Protective closure carries four conditions: it closes, it is bounded in time, it is repairable, and it returns to open. It is not the opposite of openness. It is a use of the mean.

Two further kinds complete the set. 元闭 is meta-closure: not one gap sealed but the source of gap-generation frozen, the rules that decide what counts as a legal move made unrevisable. It is the deepest kind, and the one a foundation document is most at risk of committing, which is why the corpus concedes that its one line held without exception is itself a deliberate minimal totalisation, the single rule whose function is to keep every other rule revisable. Its permitted twin is the load-bearing freeze: a grammar, a protocol, a base frozen so that generation above it opens. Ren's reading of the Urbit design says it exactly: it is not frozen because it died, it is frozen to enable endless generation, 变易 requires 不易. And 伪开 is the pseudo-open: 外显为开，内成其闭；以一时之可，夺长远之未, outwardly open, inwardly producing closure, spending a momentary "can" to rob a long-term "not yet." It is named as the characteristic modern form, and the reason the framework spends more effort on it than on honest closure is that nobody today closes a world by announcing it. A platform that offers every option and forecloses the domain in which options are generated is the standard case.

This is the criterion that the reader of Moloch literature has never been given. The diagnosis has been available for a decade; what no one supplies is a test a person can apply to their own project, and the test is not "is this good" but "does it reopen." It also settles, on the conceptual side, the oldest embarrassment in the English half of the corpus. The foundation review named an enforcement antinomy: stopping a determined defector requires a capability that out-powers the strongest actor, which is exactly the concentration the principle forbids, so preventing capture seems to require building the disease. The English drafts treat bounded defence as an exception needing apology. The Chinese line has a category for it. Defence is protective closure, and the question it must answer is whether it reopens. The empirical half of the antinomy is untouched by this, and the corpus says so in its own words: "we have no model that guarantees such a defense stands down." But the conceptual half dissolves, and the test the July manifesto ends on, written to Ren's ruling of the day before, can be applied to any closing, including a defensive one: 它在开路，还是在据门？ Is it opening a road, or holding a gate?

Now the gap, which the corpus does not name and this essay does. The test is retrospective. Whether a closure reopened is known after it either did or did not; the decision to close is made before. Of protective closure's four conditions, two can be checked at the moment of closing, that it is bounded in time and that it is repairable, and the decisive one, that it returns to open, only afterward. And the obvious substitute, judging the closer's intent, is forbidden by the hardest ethical constraint in the formal line, 反诛心: no metric may adjudicate anyone on inferred inner states; only speech and act enter. So the ethics has a criterion and no decision procedure, and its own rule blocks the usual shortcut. The procedure exists in the corpus, in the wrong place. It is the set of rules Ren wrote for his running systems in the last six weeks, stated as engineering and never as philosophy. Nothing silent. Nothing previously allowed becomes refused. A deleted thing is marked missing, never pruned. Forking without amnesia: whoever leaves takes the whole history, and the source keeps no continuing control over them. Every departure from clean custody is carried as a named debt rather than refused or hidden. Coercion recorded as coercion, never laundered into consent. Every one of those is checkable at the moment of closing, and none requires reading a mind. Restated as ethics: a closure is provisionally protective when it is declared rather than silent, when the one who closes does not hold the only key to reopening, and when what was possible before the closing is not made impossible by it. That turns protective closure from a verdict passed afterward into something a second party can hold the closer to in advance. It was derived from a version-control system, and it should be said as a claim about worlds.

### VI. Why hold this, and not something else

A commitment has to say why it and not another, and the corpus has tried three grounds, of which only one survives. The transcendental ground runs: anyone who can ask what they ought to do already exists, and existing already depends on the process continuing, so the questioner has already tacitly joined the business of keeping open. It establishes less than it seems to. It reaches the questioner's own continuation and not anyone else's; it does not bind an agent indifferent to their own continuation; and it yields a hypothetical rather than a categorical imperative. Hans Jonas made almost exactly this move and met exactly this objection. Ren reached the limit himself on 2 May 2026, the same day he had commissioned a proof: 宇宙过程不断生成，不等于复杂性必然持续上升；复杂性上升，也不等于伦理上一定是善；伦理上追求善，也不一定总是最大化复杂性。这个东西根本就不能证明，道可道非常道，你不能用模型来测量宇宙，而宇宙又是包含测量的 它只是完全自洽又有universality. Cosmic generation does not entail rising complexity; rising complexity does not entail the good; pursuing the good does not always maximise complexity. It cannot be proven. What it has is complete self-consistency and universality of scope.

So the honest form is the one the rigorous foundation document uses: one owned, underivable premise, that it continue, declared as a minimal public commitment, defended by what is destroyed when it is denied, and conceded to be a deliberate minimal totalisation. The v2 plan for 生生不息论 concedes what remains: a thoroughly consistent dominator can refuse this commitment, and the theory does not claim to produce care in him by logic [AI-drafted, kept]. That concession is the strongest sentence in the ethics, because it stops the ethics from pretending.

Then where does care come from, when it does? Not from logic. From what a self is. Ren's fullest statement of it, on 19 April 2026, in one unedited message: "well maintaining the self is the step to the whole, every recognized self is a part of the whole recognizing that 'self'. what i am saying is we have the circuit, so we need to satisfy the self, like not in survival mode or hajacked by insatiable desires, so that we can be a part of the whole that is in coherence with the whole. the environment is also created by every self within it, therefore, in a way, they are also the self. we have this evolution drive to maintain existence at the self level, but beyond the drive, there is no self." A week earlier: "Do you see the connection? We are the ecology of our cells." The relational self is not decoration on the ethics. It is the only place the "for others too" can come from without smuggling. If the I is not an origin point outside relations but an action-centre where relations temporarily gather, then keeping other people's futures open is not altruism added to self-interest. It is the same act, seen from the other end of a relation.

And care is not a conclusion. It is one of the ancient drive systems that supply motion, and like every drive it is fed or starved by environment. 如果你存在于斗争的环境，你就会去以斗争的视角看你的世界，如果你是安全满足的，就会天然倾向于和谐共处，合作的行为 (2026-04-11): in an environment of struggle you see the world through struggle; safe and provided for, you tend naturally to cooperation. Ren corrected the environment-only version of this the same day, 不不不，人类自身的drive，人性，也是这些模式出现的主要原因: drives are also a primary cause, and unchecked positive feedback on status or dominance breaks a system regardless of abundance. Both halves are needed. What they yield is the plainest sentence in the corpus about the good life: 我经常说的一个概念是'好好做人'，其实我们只要是一个正常的人，不被劫持和奴役，我们的生活自然不是totalizing的 (2026-03-28). As long as we are ordinary people, not hijacked and not enslaved, our lives are naturally not totalising. The language claim is the microfoundation of the same thing: 合作才讲理，斗则讲赢, cooperation has to reason while struggle only has to win, and 一旦争，则「我对」，我大于理, once there is contention, "I am right" outranks the reason; an environment of concord selects shared symbols toward a common root and an environment of contest selects them toward the speaker (2026-05-05, both premises flagged by Ren as his own hypotheses).

Two things follow, and they carry the rest of the essay. The motive is environmental, so this is a claim about environments before it is a claim about persons; the ethics points at the constructive half rather than at the reader's will. And the person who can refuse the premise, the consistent dominator, is precisely the person the game selects into power. His refusal is not a counterexample to the theory. It is the theory's prediction.

### VII. The floor and the exit

Now the half that has had one draft. Its fullest statement is a chat message from 8 February 2026: "Now we have ai, everything can be automated, we can build a layer of infrastructure to free everyone from exploitation, we can build a new system, more complex coordination, thinking in systems. Once this world is built, everyone has an exit to freedom, no coercion. Then we can escape the old logic." And in the same message the conservative clause that keeps it from being a revolution: "We do not want the current system to collapse - we just want to keep it continue providing a stable and kind market, for as long as it benefits humans … we can still use market, but we want an automated necessity abundance to be archived so everyone can have exit." The engine is self-reproducing automation: 如果一个ai工厂生产机器人，生产出的机器人还可以再盖一个工厂 (2026-02-17), a factory whose robots build the next factory, growing like a living thing where resources allow. Ren's own phrase for what it is for: 让人从剥削和旧系统的崩塌前解放出来，获得自由去玩，探索，爱和塑造意义的自由, to free people from exploitation and from the collapse of the old system before it comes, for the freedom to play, explore, love and make meaning.

The mechanism at the centre is not generosity, and it is worth stating precisely because it is the only non-ratcheting selection pressure the whole corpus proposes. If leaving any arrangement is materially affordable, because necessities come from a layer no actor can capture, then no arrangement can hold its members by trapping them, and the surviving pressure changes sign: from competition to dominate into competition to be worth joining. Abundance without free association is a comfortable zoo. Free association without abundance is the freedom to starve. Together they invert the game. And the same layer in the wrong hands is the opposite of a floor: 丰裕若为门，即是最柔软的项圈 [AI-drafted, kept], if abundance is a gate it is the softest collar, a provision you can be cut off from rather than a market you can leave. "Common" is therefore defined negatively and operationally, as non-capturable in practice: access rules, auditability, local accountability, dispute resolution, exit rights, and no unchecked power to convert the floor of life into dependence; never by being called public or shared or benevolent.

The objections have to be stated at full strength here, because a mechanism with one draft cannot afford to skip the ones the literature already made. Exit is Hirschman's, and Hirschman's central result cuts the other way: cheap exit degrades voice, because the people who leave are the ones who would have repaired the thing, so an institution's quality can fall rather than rise as exit gets easier. Sorting is Tiebout's, and absent his assumptions of perfect mobility, no scale economies and equal endowments, competition among arrangements produces stratification and cream-skimming rather than quality. Membership is rivalrous: an arrangement that wins members takes them from another, and scale buys reputation and infrastructure that buy more members, which is exactly the compounding axis the diagnosis in Part I defines, so "non-increasing returns to membership" is not a property commons have but a design obligation nobody has yet discharged. Rent is Ricardo's and George's: if necessities go near-free while land, siting, grid interconnection, spectrum and a few minerals stay fixed, the surplus capitalises into rent on the non-reproducible input, and a capture of that form is none of hidden, one-way, coercive, non-substitutable or irreversible, so all five marks of capture pass while the floor is owned upstream. And care, listed among the necessities the layer will make abundant, is the textbook sector that does not automate, because its quality is time spent; a floor that includes care includes labour, and "coordination, not physics" has to say what it means when the residual is labour-intensive.

None of these kills the mechanism. Each names a condition under which it fails, and the naming is what a mechanism with one draft most needs. The list of open items that results is short and concrete: a supply model for one necessity in one place, with the land priced; a legal form that survives the five marks and a sixth for rent; a stated way to keep returns to membership from increasing; and a division of the necessities into what automates and what does not. Each is a month of work and none is philosophy.

### VIII. Do not fight for us

Ren's first principle for artificial intelligence exists in his own spoken words, and every later version keeps them: "We can keep fighting, but we should not let our AI fight." The written form is from 9 February 2026: "Make the global coordination work based on one rule, that is forcefully executed: stop using AI compete with others, only use them for commons, for the purpose of all, as a first principle." And the name the position acquired in June: "AI cannot be treated as a competitive weapon. It is a planetary technology. It must be governed as a public-good / common-benefit intelligence infrastructure, as an interconnected part of the whole" (2026-06-04). The category is capability that runs beneath human games rather than inside them, of the kind humanity has already built at its most hostile moments: the eradication of smallpox, Antarctic science, the global weather observation network, each assembled between enemies. That proves the category possible between enemies. It does not prove it available now: a precedent as proof of possibility is not a precedent as a design, and the condition-by-condition comparison the whitepaper's reviewers asked for, on verification cost, defection payoff and veto players, is owed and unwritten.

The derivation is game-theoretic and Ren makes it flatly: 除了最后的胜利者，其他人的利益其实都是偏向不与ai博弈的 (2026-02-17), everyone except the final winner is better off under the rule, so the consensus is rationally available rather than morally demanded. It covers more than violence: fighting for profit, attention, market share, political power or national position by treating everything past the boundary as obstacle, resource or externality. And it concedes an asymmetry that the corpus has never quite decided how to hold. Humans may keep playing; intelligence may not play for them. The record treats that as strategy, the whitepaper as ontology, and Ren's own view is symmetric: "we should not separate AI and human as if they should hold different principle, as if human are superior, there are logic and common sense that should be followed by all beings" (2026-06-04). The best reading is that the asymmetry is the achievable minimum and not the theory, and the essay says so rather than choosing.

The principle inherits a debt from Part II, and it is the sharpest unpaid debt in the whole prescription. If, inside these dynamics, no intelligence can be trained that is not captured, then the referee-side systems the prescription needs, the verifiers and monitors and treaty-compliance models, are trained by the same actors under the same pressure as the product-side ones. Either the impossibility claim is weaker than stated or instrument-side intelligence escapes by something other than training. The reading this essay offers is that it escapes by position. Its outputs are checkable by parties with opposed interests, and checkability by adversaries is a property of the deployment structure, not of the model. If that is the answer, then symmetric verification is the load-bearing element of the entire prescription rather than an implementation detail, and "assurance without surveillance," which the whitepaper lists among its unfinished questions, is not one open problem among six but the central design task. Exit from the race has to be built rather than exhorted, because unilateral exit is punished: commitment registries with shared thresholds, verification that does not itself become surveillance, pause protocols, capacity for latecomers, and the abundance floor of Part VII so that refusal does not mean destitution. Ren proposes the one rule and says in the same breath that it has no enforcement mechanism. Both halves are kept.

### IX. 无主语: who builds

The sharpest sentence anyone has written about this corpus was written at its author's request. The nine-perspective audit of the 智善 whitepaper returns the verdict 丰裕公地无主语: the abundance commons has no subject. 谁出钱、谁建、资本手里的自动化产能怎么变成公地, who pays, who builds, how automation held as capital becomes a commons, is answered only by 应推动, "should be promoted." 设计有了，供给为零: the design exists, the supply is zero. The same review notes that the framework connects to no welfare system, no cooperative tradition and no mutual-aid tradition, and that of all the parties addressed, builders, governors, researchers, publics, humanity and machines, workers are absent. Ren's own line, recorded as his named frontier: "I can't find the path."

Here the essay adds something the corpus does not say, and it changes what the question is. The missing subject is not an oversight in the design. It is what the diagnosis predicts. A system that selects into power those who relate to everything instrumentally selects against the kind of actor who would build a layer nobody can capture; the environment that would have to produce the builder is the environment that produces the gatekeeper instead. So the constructive half being thin is evidence for the diagnosis, and the thirty drafts of diagnosis against one of construction are the same selection acting on one author, because the diagnosis is what the environment rewarded him for writing. That reframing takes "who builds" away from the register of plans. It is not asking for a supply chain. It is asking where, inside a selecting environment, the selection is weakest, and the answer is wherever an environment of concord already exists and has not yet been metabolised. The museum's undelivered exhibit list is that answer read as history: cooperatives, unions, libraries, public health systems, open-source collaboration, the coordination systems that resisted a rivalrous logic. A network like 706 is that answer read as biography. A festival with no main stage is that answer read as an experiment. A family, a workshop, a neighbourhood are that answer at the scale where most people actually live. None of these is a demonstration of the theory. They are where its subject is formed.

The corpus's earliest text knew this. In October 2021, in a proposal that was unreadable until this week, the real deficit of city life was named as 城市中社会联结机制的结构性问题, the structural problem of the city's social-connection mechanism; the city was described as a machine designed to maximise production efficiency, which 把人规训成了劳动力，然后一个个塞进了钢筋混凝土的盒子里, disciplines people into labour-power and packs them one by one into concrete boxes, so that 充满人情味和友邻气息的附近消失了, the warm, neighbourly nearby has disappeared. The answer proposed was a nearby in which everything life needs is within a fifteen-minute walk, and a list of places surveyed for it. That is the middle scale, between the person the drives theory describes and the civilisation the attractor describes, where openness is actually lived and mostly lost, and it is the scale the corpus has written least about since 2021. The labour the whitepaper's reviewers found missing lives there too.

What the smallest real units can and cannot show must be said plainly, because the constructive branch's credibility depends on not overstating them. Wamotopia has a published protocol, a festival that ran, and a Dali precedent whose decisive feature, the state cancelling the venue, is not reproducible by design; the promised recap of Chiang Mai does not exist, and the reciprocity that is supposed to close the abundance loop is asserted and not specified. The runtimes have nineteen recorded decisions, a reversal ledger, a shipped kernel, and their central claim unmeasured, with the project's own verdict on itself the most honest line in the cluster: "Ember preserves my autobiography much better than it protects my agency." The proof layer proves consistency inside hand-built models, not that reality instantiates the distinctions, by its author's ruling. 希望的单位不是口号，是地址 [AI-drafted, kept]: the unit of hope is an address, not a slogan. There is no address yet. A theory that names its own gap is stronger than one that hides it, and this is where the gap is.

### X. Is this an attractor?

The author is inside what he describes, and he asked the question himself, on 3 July 2026, and nobody has answered it: 在当下的社会环境中，我们有多大的概率逃脱这个竞争性主体形式？如果我们人类本身即受到此attractor筛选和塑造？ In the present environment, what is the probability that we escape this competitive form of subjecthood, if we ourselves were selected and shaped by the attractor? The essay cannot answer a probability over an unmodelled process and should not pretend to. What it can do is say what the question already contains. The prose of this corpus was mostly drafted by a model trained on a corpus the theory says is captured, and Ren caught the symptom in the tool as it happened, in the sentence about acceleration quoted in Part II. He caught it in himself, too, in a different form: the habit of splitting the world this-versus-that and siding with a pole, resilience over prevention, distributed over central, is the totalising move, performed while arguing against totalisation. And the framework installs machinery against the charge that it explains everything. 善不姓国, the good bears no nation's surname. Symmetric application: any test applied to one party is applied to its opposite and to the framework itself. Public corrigibility as a precondition of publishing at all. A dissolution clause. And the author's own repudiation of his own principles for their form, quoted in Part IV, which is the one thing a creed cannot survive.

Two problems belong in this last part because they are about the theory's relation to itself. The first is the interior. The diagnosis's central variable is a way of seeing; the hardest constraint in the formal line forbids measuring it; in the Lean formalisation the sincerity metric's modality type has only two constructors, so the interior cannot be constructed into it at all. The theory therefore has no permitted measurement of the thing it is about. The reading this essay offers is that it never needed one. Selection on cognition is inferred from selection on acts; a way of seeing is, for every purpose the theory has, a distribution of what gets done and said, and the corpus's own runtimes already work this way: capture before policy, record the act and infer nothing. The interior stays unmeasured and the diagnosis stays stated. The second is reopening. The theory has an account of closing and none of reopening. 转, the capacity to turn back, is a capacity and not a mechanism; the strategy of recovering and preserving older frames "so they are available when the dominant frame fractures" presupposes a fracture it never explains. The implicit theory is Ren's own, stated once and written into no document: "right now we are in arms race, I don't have the power to stop it right away, nor does anyone else unfortunately, so tragedy will happen … Then people will start to wake up, learn the lesson, start to look for ways to change, and for me, I think that will happen in the next year or two, I am preparing for that" (2026-06-03). The race breaks itself, and preserved seeds are taken up in the gap. Without that paragraph the choice of deliverables, samples and foundations and vocabulary rather than campaigns, looks like avoidance; with it, it is a strategy, and it should be published as one. History offers other reopenings than overshoot: split, succession crisis, exhaustion, external shock, a defection cascade. None has been examined. Collecting them is the museum's real job.

Three problems stay open at full strength, and the essay's last obligation is to leave them there rather than to resolve them by rhetoric. The determined adversary, who understands all of this and prefers to win. The transition: how a critical mass leaves the race without being eaten while leaving. The ownership of the abundance layer. The symmetric blade has been declared and not yet swung; no case study applying the framework's tests to both sides of a live dispute, and to the framework itself, exists. Until one does, the objection that this is a framework in philosophical dress is correct in practice whatever the doctrine says.

So the essay does not end in a creed, because its author threw one away. It ends with the test the manifesto ends on, which can be applied to any project, any closure, any defence, and to this text: 它在开路，还是在据门？ Is it opening a road, or holding a gate? And it keeps, unrepaired, the sentence Ren wrote at his own frontier, "I can't find the path," because a book about not closing the world has to keep the place where its author has not yet found the way.
