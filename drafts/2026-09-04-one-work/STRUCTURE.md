# One work, not five

*Answer to Ren's question of 2026-09-04: can the theories become an essay or a short book, how would it be structured, and what is missing. Companion to [ESSAY.md](ESSAY.md), the first draft of that essay. This file is the plan and the gap list; the essay is the thing itself.*

## The short answer

Yes. The honest form is one essay now and one short book later, on the same spine. The essay is drafted beside this file: about 7,000 words in English, Ren's own words verbatim and dated, AI-drafted lines marked in place, and three additions the corpus does not contain, flagged in the text. The short book is the same ten movements at four to five times the length. It should not be written until three things exist that the essay cannot supply: an address (one worked supply model for one necessity in one place), a field record (Chiang Mai, January–February 2026), and one symmetric case study. Written now, a short book would be the thirty-first diagnosis with a thin constructive tail, which is the corpus's own diagnosed shape.

## Why one work is possible without the reduction Ren forbids

`BOOKS.md` refuses a single volume, on the ground that collapsing five readers and two languages into one book would be lossy consolidation. That ruling is right for the five books and this plan does not overturn it. A single *work* has a different job: not to contain the theories but to state the one commitment once, with the diagnosis as its shadow, the metaphysical floor as its motive, the constructive half at the thinness it actually has, and the unsolved problems at full strength. The essay does not assert the three-layer identity (metaphysical, coordination, civilisational) that Claude proposed on 2026-04-16 and Ren never ratified. It uses only the four-name identity of the diagnosis, which the drafts support, and lets the relational self, the runtimes and 大同 appear as what the commitment presupposes, runs on, and points toward. Plurality is kept by keeping the gaps, not by resolving them.

## What is missing

Three kinds, in order of how much they change the work.

### Holes the corpus has not named

These are this reading's findings. Each carries what would fill it. Push back on any that the corpus already answers; the check below lists what was looked for.

1. **The protective-closure test has no decision procedure.** 护闭 is judged by whether it reopens, which is known afterward; the closing is decided before; 反诛心 forbids judging the closer's intent. Of 护闭's four conditions (closes, time-bounded, repairable, returns to open), the first three are prospective and the decisive fourth is not. *Fill:* lift the runtime custody rules into the ethics. A closure is provisionally protective when it is declared rather than silent, when the closer does not hold the only key to reopening, and when nothing previously possible is made impossible by it. Those rules were written for git, Bonfire and ember in August–September 2026 and have never been stated as claims about worlds. Confidence: high that the gap is real; the fill is a proposal.
2. **无主语 is predicted, not overlooked.** A system that selects instrumental cognition into power selects against the actor who would build a layer no one can capture. The thin constructive half is evidence for the diagnosis, and the same selection acting on the author: the diagnosis is what the environment rewarded him for writing. *Consequence:* the constructive task is subject-formation, not demonstration; the only sources of a subject are the places where the selection is weakest, existing 和 environments that have not been metabolised. The museum's exhibit list, the 706 network, a festival with no main stage, a neighbourhood become subject-formation sites rather than proofs. Confidence: high; nothing in the corpus says it.
3. **A theory of closing, and no theory of reopening.** 转 is a capacity, not a mechanism. "Preserve the frame so it is available when the dominant frame fractures" presupposes a fracture it never explains. The implicit theory, overshoot plus preserved seed, is the crash-window strategy Ren stated on 2026-06-03 and no document carries. *Fill:* publish that paragraph as strategy; then collect historical reopenings (split, succession crisis, exhaustion, external shock, defection cascade) as the museum's reopening wing. Confidence: high.
4. **No word for a good ending.** The English "close" does double duty for foreclosure and completion. The corpus has death-as-feature for ecosystems (Ren, 2026-06-05), 终止如传承 for machines, and a dissolution clause for the organisation, but no term for a form ending because it is finished, at the scale of a project, a tradition, a book or a movement. Without one, "do not close the world" reads as immortalism, which is acceleration grammar. *Fill:* name it (the 传承 vocabulary is a start; 善终 is the ordinary word) and state once that worlds end and the world does not close. Confidence: medium; part of this is a naming gap.
5. **The scale index exists and is never applied where it is needed.** The formal layer indexes openness by scale (开度 must be scale-explicit and short-board sensitive; the cancer-at-cell-scale counterexample). The one place the ethics needs it, the 2024 permissiveness ("you can have a society where it violent like mad max") against the 2026 floors, is never adjudicated with it. *Fill:* one paragraph stating which scale's openness the floors protect and why the min form makes exit a floor rather than a value imposed on plurality. Confidence: high (a connection not drawn, not a hole).
6. **The middle scale is absent.** Household, workplace, school, neighbourhood: the scale between the drives theory's person and the attractor's civilisation, where 伪开 is actually lived. The 2021 proposal (附近, 原子化, the fifteen-minute nearby) is its seed and its only text; labour, which the whitepaper's reviewers found missing, is its named instance. Confidence: high.
7. **The motive is in pieces.** The transcendental argument reaches only one's own continuation; the owned premise is motiveless by design; the relational self (Ren 2026-04-19: "beyond the drive, there is no self"), care as a drive, and 和/争 selection supply the motive and have never been assembled. The essay assembles them in §VI. Confidence: medium-high; Ren may hold that the motive is simply declared, in which case §VI is optional.
