# Mathematical development for V7.3

The new contribution is a bounded transition into the already proved S16 region, not a proof of political reform in general. The candidate `reformpath` couples the existing one-domain capture field to two-type load selection and the forgiving host. It starts outside S16, allows correction to take time to build, pays all transition operating costs from a finite budget, and ends its temporary booster before entry is complete. A persistent reduction in incoming conversion lets ordinary correction maintain the result.

The planned proof uses elementary comparison equations. A lower bound on correction during installation bounds accumulated rule support. An explicit support-decay time under the booster then produces a negative capture-growth margin; integrating the capture logit gives a finite time to the liability-safe capture threshold. A second logit bound gives the remaining time for load to enter the host-compatible region. The host compared with maximal load throughout this interval supplies a conservative reserve condition. S16 then supplies continuation under the maintained baseline arrangement.

Feasibility is stated, not smuggled in: peak correction, build rate, installation delay, capital cost, all operating costs until entry and recurring maintenance are separate constraints. Reform authority, enforceable nominal liability, the persistent conversion cut and the subsequent revenue stream remain implementation premises. The model contains no endogenous political adoption, coalition persistence, or general O2/human-agency proof. An uncoupled human-capacity variable would add appearance rather than a causal bridge and is omitted.

The proof is a reach-and-stay construction in the standard control-theoretic sense (Meng et al. 2021), but does not invoke a converse theorem to claim implementability. The initial state and policy are explicit; the nonempty parameter witness is initially above the capture, support and load bounds of S16. Counterexamples will distinguish structural repair from temporary damping, sufficient certificate failure from actual failure, and feasibility of the destination from adequate transition time.

Numerical verification will use independent integrators: joint RK4 and a midpoint method that updates capture/load through their exact logits at frozen midpoint support/effective liability, support by its frozen-source linear solution, and the host by its exact constant-load solution. Refinement agreement, invariant continuation, policy withdrawal, insufficient structural repair, excess delay, and full rather than incremental cost accounting will be checked. All claims remain dimensionless model statements, not calibrated empirical estimates.

Implemented `reformpath` with one new stable declaration. The positive case begins at field gain 2, capture 0.9, support 1.8 and load above the host-compatible bound. Its certified entry deadline is 2.606503, after booster release at 1.643742; full transition cost 10.630740 and recurring cost 0.6 fit the stated separate resources. The actual model path has a much larger host reserve than the sufficient comparison certificate.

The fresh mathematical review prompted explicit positive parameter domains, invariant state-domain justification, and explicit permission for rapid downward deactivation (the build-rate limit is upward only). Two draft numerical claims were corrected rather than defended: order convergence must be checked at common fixed times rather than extrema sampled on different grids; and defunding after very deep suppression need not cause recapture in this self-coupled model. The latter is retained as a counterexample to inferring actual failure from losing a sufficient certificate. A separate no-repair/short-pulse case and a slow-construction case demonstrate actual adverse trajectories. The two omitted reforms in the combined case are not claimed individually necessary.

The standard-library numerical module now has six passing groups. Joint RK4 refinement differs by about 1.1e-10 in the reported quantities; the independent exponential-midpoint implementation differs by about 1.2e-6 and its common-endpoint error falls by a factor of 3.99 on halving the step. The method agreement verifies the model calculations, not implementation in any real institution.
