# Integrating correction through institutional change

Ren asked for full integration after a candid assessment of V7.1's significance and open causal joins, and explicitly requested a team to think through the integration first. The supplied proposal is preserved in USER-PROPOSAL.txt. V7.1 is the immutable baseline; this working edition is V7.2.

## Decision after the first team pass

The constructive question is whether increasing capability preserves shared viability and the real means of correction through changes to strategies, successors and institutions. This strengthens the existing generative alternative, rather than introducing a second theory based on three gradients. Selection, protected conditions and intervention effects remain distinct questions.

The main unresolved step is from an available correcting policy to institutions that retain the information, resources, authority and time to execute it. A definition of a safe region, a local incentive derivative, or an externally supplied controller cannot establish that step. Preserving a corrective function does not require freezing the institution performing it; legitimate challenge and replacement are protected commitments.

The integration therefore has three evidential levels:

1. An explicit constructive target in the main argument, with institutional implementation and preservation under selection left open.
2. One reduced joint certificate in S16, composing the field and host models through a maintained liability relation. Its initial-state and all-time incoming-gain bounds are sufficient for a specified invariant region. It is a conditional composition, not a general solution or proof of novel endogenous governance.
3. A proposed, unrun Test N that measures detection, authorization and effective execution through institutional changes, with matched resources and genuine alternatives. Its outcomes cannot establish a catastrophe floor without an independent harm model.

- Does any use of correction silently assume durable access, resources, authority or truthful measurement?
- Are selection resistance, physical viability, legitimate contestability and reachability kept distinct?
- Does the reduced certificate state every maintained coupling and initial condition, and does its failure example actually fail?
- Can Test N reject the proposed mechanism without treating an experimental scoring instrument as a demonstrated permanent external corrector?
- Do the new citations support only the component claims actually read?
