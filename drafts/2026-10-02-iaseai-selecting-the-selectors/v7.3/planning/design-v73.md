# V7.3 institutional design work

Status: illustrative design and proposed test; no institutional pilot has run.

## Constructive addition

X10 now contains a worked transition for a public agency's AI-assisted routing and drafting of routine service requests, with staff retaining final decision authority. It starts from imperfect evaluation and supplier dependence. It does not assume a new worldwide authority or an indefinitely protected budget.

The initiating coalition comprises the service owner, an authorized procurement officer and the budget holder. Each required authority is a premise to verify from the applicable instruments. Missing authority or resources stop the dependent step and become a named prerequisite; a proposed commitment is never treated as an enacted one.

The sequence is:

1. Register service and agency floors, feasible powers, total resources and credible comparators; address known errors under existing authority.
2. Acquire enforceable evidence rights, separately executable access and a finite migration reserve through the next procurement or permitted renewal.
3. Exercise portable records, qualified staff and a viable alternative before expanding dependence.
4. Drill finding → authorization → independent operation → verified outcome under supplier resistance, including actual joint timing.
5. Exercise legitimate appeal and correction of an abusive evaluator, with delegated reversal authority and deadlines.
6. Expose coalition formation, budget and supplier responses to failure rather than assume adoption.
7. Require operational overlap and resources through succession, with a smaller protected service or an explicit failed-transition determination when continuation is infeasible.

## Relationship to the models

The operational mechanism is temporary transition investment followed by a structural reduction in dependence and ordinary maintenance. It is analogous to, but does not estimate, the reduced model's temporary correction and structural-conversion changes. More audit expenditure is not identified with $d$; supplier market share is not identified with capture; legal terms are not identified with effective actuation. A certificate's mathematical assumptions remain distinct from these empirical observables.

Successful protection also requires that mistaken or abusive correction be reversible. The relevant object is the continuity of protected service, agency and executed correction through institutional change, not the survival of the original body or charter.

## Coordination with Test N

The Test N author is using the same procurement-service unit and temporary reserve/portable evidence/independent executor mechanism. X10 deliberately does not prescribe rounds or synthetic parameters. Test N owns the proposed experimental timing and comparison plan. X10 supplies the actual-authority questions and operational interpretation.

Comparators are ordinary continuation and extra audit/training under matched total resources without altered dependencies. Relevant rival explanations are total expenditure, unusually easy or narrow tasks, initial competence, observation effects and disappearance of the benefit after funding or leadership changes. Institutional adoption, supplier acceptance and budget renewal are possible failures, not events filtered out before evaluating performance.

## Scope and unresolved claims

- The example is generic and illustrative; it does not assert facts or legal powers for a named jurisdiction.
- It covers one enrolled agency service and its credentials, not control of model weights, a supplier's other uses, states or a frontier race.
- It does not establish a model-parameter estimate, catastrophe-risk bound, indefinite durability or superiority of the full compact.
- The initiating delegation, lawful access, qualified staff, resources and fallback capacity must exist or be acquired; where they cannot be, the proposed step is not yet feasible.
- Independent reviewers and budget holders remain potential failure points. Additional bodies provide operational hypotheses to test rather than trusted endpoints.

## Review focus

The most useful falsification is a complete correction path that works initially but fails after the temporary reserve ends, a supplier becomes harder to replace, leadership changes or a valid appeal challenges the evaluator. The most useful positive evidence is retained improvement through those changes, with service and agency floors intact and all costs counted. Neither outcome should be generalized beyond the registered envelope.
