## What would warrant the upgrade

1. A substantive, bounded constructive result that adds an achievable transition to V7.2's protected destination, with explicit starting state, implementation conditions, costs or delays and continuation requirements.
2. A worked institutional path that names its initiating actor, prerequisites, change in actual operating conditions, corrective execution, continuity, succession and valid appeals. Its relationship to the reduced model must be candid and non-equivalent.
3. A proposed empirical comparison that could reject the reform's claimed advantage. Model checks must not be reported as an institutional experiment or empirical validation.
4. Preserved prior claims and declaration identities, reproducible assembly and appropriate numerical checks, and fresh bounded review of the added mathematics and institutional claims. Findings must be resolved or constrain the release assessment.

The full endogenous political implementation problem, the real-world causal interlock, general correctable kernel and residual irreversible-risk floor are not required to be solved to make a useful bounded contribution; they must remain explicitly open. Standard comparison and viability techniques must be credited as standard. No claim of historical mathematical priority is planned.
