Each entry refutes a stronger version of a claim or marks where a claim stops; the result named states the weaker claim that survives. The last column names the block of S15 that computes the entry, part by part; *closed form* marks an entry whose arithmetic is in the proof cited, and *evidence* one that rests on an evidence row. Entries marked (O) are the boundary cases of the outside review of V6.1: every default run of the checks ends with their index and asserts that the eight it requires hold (S15).
