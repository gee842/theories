# V7.1 preservation and revision ledger

V7.1 is a separate edition of V7, made at Ren's request after the [reviewer-team readiness assessment](../v7/reviews/readiness/REVIEW-v7-readiness.md). V7 and its review remain the historical record. Earlier review dispositions and authoring records are linked in [V7 LEDGER](../v7/LEDGER.md), [V7 CLAIMS](../v7/CLAIMS.md) and [V7 CHANGELOG](../v7/CHANGELOG.md).

## Structure and author record

The revision retains the twelve-section core, corresponding Supplement and Discussion chapters, and the distinction among mechanism, empirical diagnosis and practical judgment. Its comparison with V7 checks **128 stable declarations: 112 numbered results/tables and 16 equations**. Keys, kinds, numbers and source files are preserved; statements and line numbers can change.

CLAIMS §§8–9 are preserved verbatim as a historical provenance record, including Ren's confirmed decisions and model renderings still awaiting him. They are not silently converted into new endorsements. CLAIMS §10 explains the current treatment of renderings affected by this revision: concentration is not equivalent to a narrower objective, shared cognitive understanding is not the only possible source of protective institutions, and preference for broad E1 is distinct from satisfying the independent hazard-coverage condition.

The normative preference remains broad E1 before fallback responses to continuing competition; narrow E1 remains a first step. Hazardous activity must satisfy the coverage and effective-control conditions on every branch. This is a correction to the models' applicability, not a new quotation or an attributed change in the author's values. The shared-fate standpoint and trust and verification together are retained.

## Changes and ownership

[CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) gives the readable account; [CLAIMS §10](CLAIMS.md) records the disposition of each readiness finding. Parallel implementation notes are in [mathematics](planning/math-v71.md), [systems](planning/systems-v71.md) and [evidence](planning/evidence-v71.md). The coordinating pass revised the English and Chinese openings, entry, claim register and release documentation.

The revision narrows R1's selection class, qualifies scope comparative statics, and preserves analytical counterexamples in the Supplement and new RP checks. It separates the compact's lifecycle states and the strongest forms of competing arrangements. It corrects the literature comparisons and synchronizes safeguard-tax and zero-tax conditions. The institutional production relations and post-construction fallback remain explicit open cases where they have not been demonstrated.

Historical drafting plans and old verification outputs have not been copied into this edition as if they were current results. V7.1 carries the reusable source and check code and produces its own assembled documents, numerical log and verification record.

## Verification

The initial [V7 baseline manifest](verification/v7-baseline-manifest.json) hashes **87 V7 files**, including its readiness review. The final audit checks that they remain unchanged. It also compares declaration identities and CLAIMS §§8–9, records the exact revised input hashes, and checks that assembly reproduces the delivered documents.

The new RP block checks analytical counterexamples and valid special cases. The inherited numerical suite is run once against the completed revision; further reruns are limited to changes or unresolved failures. Structural and cross-reference checks cover unresolved keys, declaration order, citations and the English/Chinese guide's registered correspondence. Source diffs and focused rereading check the qualifications those mechanical checks cannot establish.

Final results are recorded in [verification/summary.json](verification/summary.json), [verification/xref.log](verification/xref.log) and [checks_v71.log](checks_v71.log). The [release review](RELEASE-REVIEW.md) is separate from the implementation notes and tests. The completed default run passed 239 counted checks across 44 blocks; three fresh focused reviews found no remaining blocker within their stated scopes. Passing checks do not certify every proof, empirical premise, source or institutional proposal. Separate AI review contexts do not replace independent specialist or human review.
