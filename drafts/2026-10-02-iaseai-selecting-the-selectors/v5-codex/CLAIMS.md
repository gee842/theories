# v5 claim and revision record

3 October 2026. This file records the scope of [THEORY.md](THEORY.md), its relation to v4.5, and what has actually been verified. It is a companion to the manuscript, not a replacement for its argument. This version was written separately in `v5-codex/` at the user's request to proceed independently of another agent.

| Level | What this version asserts | Support and limits |

| Institutional proposal | A bounded compact can separate productive rewards from rule control, support correction, and protect essential services through disputes | §§7–8 are a worked hypothetical design; feasibility, legitimacy and durability are not empirically established |
| Practical judgment | Begin a cooperative pivot through reciprocal, revisable commitments and shared useful provision | Explicit normative premises, local mechanisms, dated evidence, and comparison; no computed optimal global policy or universal catastrophe theorem |

## Verification and empirical status

The standard-library `checks_v5.py` exercises thirteen groups of identities, boundary cases, and counterexamples. The saved output is `checks_v5.log`. It includes perfect-alignment game profiles, the control optimum, correction-rate and concentration comparisons, field thresholds, correction architectures, hazard/summability cases, freeze integrals, finite delegation, exact risk and multiplayer assurance, BIC/participation, and host-transient checks. Passing these checks is not proof of empirical applicability or certification of every proof.

The evidence pass inspected twelve primary documents or research reports on 3 October 2026. It did not rerun external experiments or obtain confidential institutional records. Announced policies and diplomatic agreements are not described as verified implementation. The review of proofs and narrative in this writing pass was performed by AI agents, not independent human referees.

The earlier rubric-editing simulation remains a separate historical experiment: [the recorded pilot](../experiment/pilot.log) used 20 seeds, and [its revised prediction record](../experiment/PREDICTIONS-2.md) reports P1 failed while P2–P4 held. Design 2 has no saved run in the inspected project. No new empirical capture experiment was performed for v5. The proposed study in §9.3 is not a result.

## What remains open after this draft

The full present-world recursive-capture chain, the comparative growth of effective correction and exposure, any nonsummable irreversible-risk floor, and the feasibility of a durable global arrangement remain unmeasured here. The manuscript's institutional proposal must still face real budget, authority, legitimacy, and defensive constraints. A fuller literature comparison could alter its contribution claims. These are substantive limits on the strength of the conclusion, and the draft states them where they matter.
