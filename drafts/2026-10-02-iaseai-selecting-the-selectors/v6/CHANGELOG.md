### Architecture

- **One pressure, not one root.** The selection pressure of winning (Definition 2.1) replaces "the trajectory has one root, Ψ". Ψ becomes the configuration of self-model and doctrine that the pressure selects (Definition 2.5): locally rational, systemically false, partly made true.
- **Meta-attractor, in three layers** (Definition 2.2). Why not "generator functions": they name causes of risk but lack reflexivity (selection rewriting its own criteria), persistence, and the layer of mind and language. They are also a borrowed term. The meta-attractor is Ren's own concept. It is substrate-free, and its generative counterpart has the same structure (Remark 2.4).
- **Overlapping mechanisms** (§2.3), not an independent basis. Three objects (values, beliefs, institutions) are measured separately, following GPT-6. Three products are named: lag is produced, paradigms converge, harm and capture reinforce each other.
- **Substrate independence** (§2.8): cells, firms, states, federations, science, training loops; another planet.
- **"One pressure" made falsifiable** (§2.7): five predictions and refutation conditions.
- **One system, no outside** (§3): Lemma 3.3(2), universal constraints invisible to selection; Proposition 3.5, two-channel accounting; Lemma 3.10, one group, so integration is anticipatory; Proposition 3.11, clubs and blocs; §3.8, what "we are one" means, in five parts, none of them θ = n−1.
- **Three erosions and the scissors** (§5) replace the three zones: enforcement (Proposition 5.7, Corollary 5.8), correction (Propositions 5.11, Theorem 5.12), shared understanding (Proposition 5.14). Theorem 5.16, the dilemma of decisiveness, states when oneness is a requirement rather than a gain.
- **AI as instrument and incarnation** (§6): Proposition 6.7, the nested reward loop.
- **Design by function** (§8.6) replaces ideological axes; federations as illustration; the Compact as one candidate.
- **The generative meta-attractor** (§8): the purpose, the generative loop with four strands, the correctable kernel, the point of enough, the guardian test.
- **Claims raised to the level of game and selection** rather than withdrawn: THEORY Appendix F and CLAIMS §7.
