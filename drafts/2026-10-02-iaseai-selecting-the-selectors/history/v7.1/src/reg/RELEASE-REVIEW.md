# V7.1 release assessment

**V7.1 is ready for public circulation as an explicitly conditional working paper.** The V7 readiness findings have been addressed, the revised arguments have received fresh focused reviews, and no release blocker remains within those reviewed scopes. This is not a claim that the present-path diagnosis is empirically established, every mathematical extension has been independently proved, or the proposed institutions are operationally ready.

V7.1 was prepared on 4 October 2026 at Ren's request. [V7 and its readiness review](../v7/reviews/readiness/REVIEW-v7-readiness.md) remain unchanged. Start reading with [START-HERE](START-HERE.md), continue to [THEORY](THEORY.md), and use [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) and [CLAIMS §10](CLAIMS.md) for the revision record.

## What changed

The formal blocker was R1's extension of a necessary condition to a selection class that did not justify it when payoff gaps changed sign. R1 now uses constant-speed replicator dynamics and its exact signed-gap identity. The earlier cumulative-selection result retains its positive-gap condition. The excluded-class counterexample remains in the Supplement and regression checks rather than disappearing from the record.

Two related mathematical scope claims are corrected: objective narrowing does not by itself imply concentration, and diversification at fixed total activity does not always increase the required correction rate. Functional institutions that protect common dependencies are also distinguished from shared cognitive understanding as a proposed route to coordination.

The policy and design revisions preserve broad E1 as Ren's preferred social arrangement while applying the independent coverage/control condition on every branch. The compact distinguishes withdrawal before and after capability construction, states a candidate disposition and executor for the latter, and includes retained knowledge, hidden copies and reconstruction in its unresolved risks. Institutional correction and technical hazard coverage have separate funding obligations; d* does not compute a coverage budget.

Alternatives now receive their own strongest assessment. A sprint followed by relinquishing superiority is distinct from a permanent monopoly freeze. Durable verified containment remains a conditional endpoint if it maintains the relevant bounds. The readiness and construction gates use the same functional conditions, allowing effective custody or equally effective containment. Common custody remains the preferred proposal, not an inferred necessity for every arrangement.

The source and test-design corrections remove unsupported rival predictions from Tests D/G, restore the joint tax/sanction condition, attribute context-dependent misalignment to the training intervention rather than evaluation, and distinguish dated benchmark gaps from leadership duration or effective-response windows. Zero tax alone no longer warrants withdrawal of admission constraints.

The revising agents and release reviewers are AI systems in the same model family. Fresh contexts reduce dependence on the drafting conversation but do not provide independent human certification.

## Limits of release readiness

The empirical interlock, residual-hazard floor, decisive-capability conditions and conversion into control remain questions for measurement. The complete strategic continuation problem and joint institutional basin are not closed by these edits. Safe execution after construction, independent recertification, budget-to-performance relations and comparative whole-design risks remain to be demonstrated.

Those limits are compatible with a conditional working paper that states its mechanisms, assumptions, values and possible counterevidence clearly. Independent human specialist review and empirical work are the next basis for stronger claims. No publication or external distribution was performed as part of this revision.
