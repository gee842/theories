### 1. P2, resolved — Carry the verified-containment alternative into E2's literal governance gate

**Source anchor:** [src/core/b0_s11.md:44](/Users/ren/theories/drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.1/src/core/b0_s11.md:44).

At the start of this pass, the first sentence defined E2 as not building the decisive class while its governance conditions were absent, enumerating those conditions as “an accurate model of the coupling, the shared prediction, and common custody.” That unqualified list made common custody a prerequisite to construction. But the revised readiness paragraph explicitly permitted “common custody or equally effective verified containment” at [src/core/b0_s11.md:52](/Users/ren/theories/drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.1/src/core/b0_s11.md:52), as did Q7 at [src/supp/11_S11.md:65](/Users/ren/theories/drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.1/src/supp/11_S11.md:65). The alternatives section and judgment correctly accepted a durable containment endpoint.

A programme under effective verified containment could therefore satisfy the revised Q7 and every applicable functional requirement yet still fail the literal E2 build gate for lacking common custody. The issue was not whether containment is feasible; the paper rightly leaves that empirical. It was which conditions the policy definition asked a feasible alternative to satisfy.

**Minimal repair:** state E2's governing condition functionally, referencing the applicable requirements and the enforcement bound, and allow common custody **or equally effective durable containment**. Keep an accurate shared understanding and common custody as the preferred route where intended, rather than making the particular custody form mandatory for every admitted endpoint. Reconcile the source paragraph with its own readiness paragraph and reassemble.

**Verification and release effect:** the coordinator changed line 44 to independent evidence adequate to bound coupling/hazards, binding purposes/constraints, and effective custody **or equally effective durable containment** satisfying the enforcement bound. The shared-prediction/common-custody route is expressly preferred rather than uniquely required, and permitted construction also needs Q7's disposition and independent execution plan. I re-read that exact source change. **Closed; no hold remains.**

## Comparative fairness and what remains legitimately open

The revised comparison applies the same pre-loss and surviving-independent-executor test to reversals on continuation and on the proposed design. X8 includes the preferred design's late/failed correction, masked hazards, standby failure, coercive escalation and captured custody, and treats documented remedies as proposals rather than evidence of successful recovery (`src/disc/08_X8.md:132,166–175`). Section 12 explicitly says that named corrective routes do not establish lower loss probabilities, and requires estimates on both sides (`src/core/c0_s12.md:35–39`). That closes the main risk-side asymmetry.

The benefit comparison remains a declared normative choice. The paper counts forgone benefits, including irreversible deaths due to delay, and says that preferring reversibility is about the option set rather than the size of the losses (`src/core/80_s8.md:13`; `src/core/b0_s11.md:44`; C10/N1). A reader can disagree with that weighting without identifying a proof error. Conversely, the claim that a candidate design realizes useful common value does not establish that its full benefit/risk tradeoff wins; the text now leaves the whole-design ranking provisional.

The post-construction executor is a specified candidate with a requirement to install and demonstrate effective access/authority, **not an already existing independent institution**. That is sufficient to repair the missing lifecycle case in a conditional working paper. It does not establish operational readiness. The same applies to independent correction certificates, current-state capture measurements, budget production functions, breakdown rates, common-cause errors and every all-deviation enforcement bound.

With the narrow E2 gate and the table labels verified as corrected, I recommend no systems-specific hold on a clearly labelled public working paper. No remaining actionable source correction emerged from this bounded pass. Independent specialist review, empirical measurement and the paper's own C4 stress tests remain necessary for stronger claims.
