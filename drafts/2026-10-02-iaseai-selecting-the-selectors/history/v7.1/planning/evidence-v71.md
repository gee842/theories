# V7.1 evidence and test-design revision

Completed 4 October 2026 by the evidence reviewer/reviser. This is a targeted repair for public working-paper circulation, following the fresh [V7 evidence readiness review](../../v7/reviews/readiness/evidence/REVIEW.md). It is not an audit of every inherited citation, proof replication, empirical certification, or independent human review. V7 and its review records were left unchanged. Only the assigned source files and this note were written; no generated document was assembled.

## Changes implemented

- **Test D:** [TESTS:123](../src/reg/TESTS.md:123) now registers the specified model's phase ordering, dwell and recruitable-alternative predictions. It no longer attributes “no re-absorption after levels fall” to Pierson, or presents excluding sunk costs as excluding path dependence. Learning, coordination, sunk costs and continuing capacity are possible competing causes to measure. Confirmation is not declared discriminating without a matched comparator.
- **Test G:** [TESTS:126](../src/reg/TESTS.md:126) preserves the stated game and its numerical predictions, but removes the unsupported claim that structural realism predicts no objective-dependent gap at intermediate breakout probability. A comparator must face the same objective intervention, payoffs, information and enforcement. [The register's introduction](../src/reg/TESTS.md:116) now distinguishes a model prediction, a possible contrary observation and discrimination among specified accounts. No new named rival model or citation is needed for this narrower status.
- **Masked-divergence condition:** [X1:153](../src/disc/01_X1.md:153) restores the positive lower bound on the safeguard tax for the bounded-sanction, vanishing-detection conclusion. If the tax also falls, expected enforceable sanction must be compared jointly with it. EV6 and TESTS already carried this qualification; the omission was local.
- **EV2 policy:** [EVIDENCE:52](../src/reg/EVIDENCE.md:52) corrects the competitor clause to §4.3, printed p. 12 (PDF page 13), paraphrases its full conditions, replaces secondary reporting of the Frontier Governance Framework with the primary announcement, and removes the unsupported inference that version 2 is unchanged today. Written permission to adjust is not evidence that the clause was invoked; that existing distinction and contrary safeguards remain.
- **EV5/C5 benchmark inference:** [EVIDENCE:106–118](../src/reg/EVIDENCE.md:106) separates benchmark-equivalent gap size from leadership duration, preference scores and effective response time. Public measures neither establish decisive advantage nor exclude it through unmeasured capabilities and complements. [TESTS:17](../src/reg/TESTS.md:17), [91](../src/reg/TESTS.md:91), [146](../src/reg/TESTS.md:146) and [199](../src/reg/TESTS.md:199) reflect that scope. C5 withdrawal requires positive evidence that balancing prevents decisive leads; lack of a measured decisive lead is not itself a release condition. Coverage-based grounds remain independent.
- **EV6 experiment and monitorability:** [EVIDENCE:131](../src/reg/EVIDENCE.md:131) identifies the constructed reward-hacking setup and corrects the causal attribution: the context dependence follows the training intervention; evaluation reveals it. It includes the paper's contrary production-model result. [EVIDENCE:138–139](../src/reg/EVIDENCE.md:138) and [X1:139](../src/disc/01_X1.md:139) separate a small capability trade-off at equal compute from higher inference cost and unmeasured latency; they do not assert a uniformly small total monitoring tax.
- **EV7 source scope:** [EVIDENCE:150](../src/reg/EVIDENCE.md:150) links the UN report landing page and release announcement and accurately labels indexed primary excerpts. The longer catastrophic-risk quotation was removed because this pass could not verify its full primary context. [TESTS C1](../src/reg/TESTS.md:12) distinguishes the international report's documented safeguard limits from the UN announcement's stronger lag statement.
- **Systems conditions in TESTS:** [C1/introduction](../src/reg/TESTS.md:7) and [E3 activation](../src/reg/TESTS.md:142) apply the coverage/control/tempo condition on every institutional branch, including broad E1. The E3 indicator uses C1's cumulative-risk/summability criterion; a falling trend alone is insufficient. [C4](../src/reg/TESTS.md:16) and [release](../src/reg/TESTS.md:154) distinguish pre-construction from post-construction D1: after authorized construction, weights, compute, keys, knowledge, the executor after exit and the residual breakout bound must be specified. No-built-class reasoning applies only before construction. [C9a](../src/reg/TESTS.md:20), [item 7](../src/reg/TESTS.md:74), [withdrawal](../src/reg/TESTS.md:95) and [joint measurement](../src/reg/TESTS.md:168) require evidence of safeguard persistence at zero tax, including relevant replacement and entry; neutral selection alone gives no restoring force.
- **Edition/provenance:** Current titles and script reference now identify V7.1 and `checks_v71.py`. [TESTS §9](../src/reg/TESTS.md:206) preserves historical reviews and records the V7 readiness/V7.1 revision sequence without claiming human certification. Other contributors' C7 response-clock edits in TESTS were preserved. Final S11 propagation distinguishes binding purposes/rules from private preferences, requires the enforcement bound for every relevant deviation, and admits equally effective durable containment alongside custody, with post-construction disposition specified (TESTS C7 and §§6.1–6.2).

## Validation and remaining limits

Exact-match edits were checked; declaration keys in all three owned files match V7. Markdown table structure and source changes were inspected. No numerical model or script changed in this work; assembly and whole-manuscript checks belong to the lead editor. No claimed experimental results were invented or recomputed.

The central empirical gaps remain C0/C1/C2/C4/C5/C12: shared-parameter coupling in the world, hazardous variety and coverage, residual loss, conversion into correction, institutional durability, decisiveness and verifiability. They are research commitments rather than new barriers to a conditional working paper. The source evidence supports particular mechanisms, policy declarations and illustrative risks; it does not establish the full present-path diagnosis or certify the proposed institutional remedy. A matched rival for D/G, the UN full report and a clause-by-clause current-policy audit remain outside this completed targeted pass.
