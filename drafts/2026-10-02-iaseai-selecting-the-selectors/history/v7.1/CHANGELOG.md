# Change record

## V7.1, 4 October 2026

V7.1 follows Ren's instruction to revise V7 before public release. Its immediate basis is the [V7 readiness review](../v7/reviews/readiness/REVIEW-v7-readiness.md), which recommended specialist circulation but identified corrections needed for a public working paper. V7 and its numerical and review records are preserved. The prior edition's history remains in [V7 CHANGELOG](../v7/CHANGELOG.md).

### Formal scope

- **R1 and cumulative selection:** the time-average and integrated-payoff necessary conditions now require constant-speed replicator dynamics when the liability gap changes sign. General cumulative payoff monotonicity is insufficient: short periods of stronger selection for restraint can outweigh longer periods of advantage for loading. S9 and S14 include the all-time viable counterexample. The original positive-gap load result remains.
- **Concentration and objective narrowing:** the opening and Chinese guide no longer identify them. A fixed-prize contest can equalize stocks while the local continuation calculation favors standing over common gains. Their equivalence remains only where the special model proves it.
- **Scope and correction:** at fixed total size and cost factors, increasing scope raises the correction requirement when it raises the return to cultivating influence without lowering the evaluation channel's conversion factor. Cross-domain conversion determines the sign; diversification across uncoupled domains can reduce the requirement.
- **Understanding:** the common-host result establishes that a common benefit alone supplies no relative selective advantage and that prevention cannot be learned from the irreversible failure afterward. Functional institutions that count common dependencies are distinguished from shared cognitive understanding; the latter remains the proposed route to deliberate coordination, not the only possible origin of protection.

### Judgment and institutions

- **Broad E1 and independent hazards:** the preference for broad E1 is preserved. It no longer serves as an exemption from coverage and effective-control conditions. A cooperative programme can still outgrow correction; E3's relevant conditions apply on every branch.
- **Withdrawal after construction:** the compact now distinguishes withdrawal before the decisive class is built from withdrawal after authorized construction. The latter must account for existing capability, access and retained knowledge, a surviving independent executor and time to act. A proposed disposition is not a proved safe fallback, and the earlier low-overhang rationale cannot be carried across those states.
- **Finite sprint:** a sprint followed by relinquishing superiority is separated from a sprint followed by a permanent monopoly freeze. Peak exposure, commitment and renewed competition apply to the first; unequal imposed restriction and coercive maintenance apply to the second.
- **Verified containment:** a durable agreement that keeps deviations within the enforceable bound remains a conditional endpoint. Retaining competitive motives alone does not prove that enforcement must fail. Its empirical durability is tested under the same standard as the proposed generative arrangement.
- **Funding:** institutional correction and technical hazard coverage are separate obligations. The threshold d* does not compute a hazard-coverage budget. The resource-to-performance relation, including access, authority and response latency, remains to be established.
- **Withdrawal at zero tax:** zero payoff difference makes selection neutral. Operational summaries now retain the main recommendation's requirement for evidence that safeguards persist before admission constraints are withdrawn at zero tax.

### Evidence and comparison

Tests D and G no longer claim that broad rival literatures predict the contrary of the model's result without a matched derivation. They retain within-model intervention predictions; a discriminating test needs a specified comparator with matched primitives. The source comparison separates Pierson's several feedback mechanisms from a narrow sunk-cost account and does not infer objective-invariance from Powell's fixed-preference model.

X1's masking summary now compares expected enforceable sanctions with the changing safeguard tax. EV6 attributes the experimental production of context-dependent misalignment to safety training; evaluation reveals or misses it. Experimental construction and uncertain deployment prevalence remain explicit. Public benchmark gaps are dated measurements, not durations of leadership, internal capability bounds or response windows. Source locators and contemporary policy qualifications are corrected where checked.

### Verification and release scope

The edition uses `src/assemble_v71.py`, `xref_v71.py` and `checks_v71.py`. It retains V7's **RV** regression block and adds **RP** for this review's counterexamples and retained cases. S15 maps each calculation; [verification/summary.json](verification/summary.json) records the final numerical and structural results and preservation checks.

The release is a conditional working paper. The empirical interlock, residual-hazard floor, full strategic continuation extension, comparative institutional risks and post-construction fallback are not established by this revision. Acknowledging those questions is compatible with circulation; treating them as solved would not be. The author record is preserved without attributing new confirmations to Ren.
