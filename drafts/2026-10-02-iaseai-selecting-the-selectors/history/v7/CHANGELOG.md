### Corrections

- **Standing and continuation values:** distinguished the local deterministic price, the linearized expected-deviation calculation and finite-jump lottery values. The nonlinear infinite-horizon interpretation now requires an explicit discounted-tail condition; the fixed-prize and proportional-prize ratio cases remain. Strategic continuation derivatives, including responses in costs and shared losses, remain an open interface.
- **Capture certificates:** carried eventual removal, the initial-state restriction and strict-gain conditions into rival verification and downstream requirements. Reliance on a corrector now also requires evidence about its present captured share, inherited support, residence and effective authority. A channel certificate alone does not establish present independence.
- **Masked divergences:** propagated the distinction between failed assurance and actual residual hazard into S6 and the interfaces. Coverage failure requires its own residual-floor premise. The sanction ceiling holds a positive tax fixed; changing taxes require comparison against detection times the enforceable sanction, with cumulative payoff gaps stated for limiting-share conclusions.
- **A point of enough:** separated bounded contest effort from finite satiation and actual cessation of hazardous growth. The positive-survival result still assumes that hazardous variety actually stops and coverage continues to grow.
- **Narrow E1 and timing:** preserving detection preserves breakout probability only when the full detection-and-effective-response clock is preserved. Human authorization latency enters C7. The static certain-lock result and Ren's preferred branch remain intact.
- **Pacing literature:** checked the relevant full-text result of Fudenberg and Koh. The stated high-hazard region supports the constructed pacing equilibrium and excludes the everywhere-racing profile; it establishes neither uniqueness among all Markov equilibria nor safe pacing from every initial state. Source scope is synchronized across the comparison, evidence and research-status entries.
- **Reversibility:** applied the same before-loss and surviving-independent-corrector conditions to every path. The design's failed standby, masked hazard and late repair can also cause irreversible loss. A named remedy establishes no comparative probability advantage, and a possible catastrophic branch alone does not establish a probability above the tolerance.
- **Remaining mathematical boundaries:** complete pooling neutralizes relative payoffs instead of preserving strict ordering; other sharing conventions retain their own effective-prize conditions. The common-benefit selection statement assumes a common background payoff. Dwell is an infimum with successful release beyond the boundary. Trust and participation comparative statics state what is held fixed.

The first two V6.2 review rounds' successful repairs are retained, including cumulative selection, the correction-rate tracking and cost-floor conditions, conditional continuation values for detection timing, layer independence in oversight, zero-tax neutrality, and readiness tests at the actual threshold. CLAIMS records the current disposition by result.
