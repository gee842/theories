# V6.1 change record

## V6.1, 3 October 2026

**Decisions taken by Ren before drafting.** Quality comes before any deadline, so IASEAI'27 is not the target. The core is stated as a set of interconnected conditions unless "one pressure" can be proved elegantly; the mathematical planning showed that only a part can be (one loop gain), so the name "selection pressure of winning" is kept for what the conditions produce together, with the loop gain as its strength. Planning tasks for important work ran the same prompt on two models in parallel and compared the results, rather than having one check the other. The structure is the four-Part layout by level of claim, with renumbering and stable keys; two companions (Supplement and Discussion); and the Compact reduced in the core to the function-to-form table and a short summary, with the full split design in the Discussion.

**Why a new structure.** v6.0 was long and unstructured: one file carried manifesto, argument, formal paper, literature review, policy analysis, research programme and version ledger; the argument was restated about nine times; Section 2 listed mechanisms, signatures, predictions and evidence before any mechanism was proved, with most of its result references pointing forward; there were eleven differently worded lists of what an adequate arrangement must do; and many symbols had several meanings. V6.1 separates the layers. The core argues; the Supplement proves; the Discussion holds lineage, alternatives, design and transition in full; the registers hold evidence, tests, claims and the ledger. Each point has one home. No result is used before it is stated. Results are numbered by section and keyed by stable names.

**What changed in the argument.**
- *Part I.* The conditions are interconnected and combine near equal standing into one loop gain (Proposition 2.2), which also prices standing and so manufactures the relative objective (Proposition 3.9). Compounding gains provision not won in the contest, the formal content of trees (Proposition 2.3). The enforcement condition replaces v6.0's Proposition 5.7, with an exact enforceable bound and the finding that reversion to a race can fail to punish (Proposition 5.4); v6.0's Theorem 5.16 is replaced by what restraint needs where breakouts are possible (Corollary 5.6) and what the premise of the race cannot justify (Corollary 7.3). Lemma 3.3 gains incidence and evasion; Lemma 3.10 holds for every partition and names the transmission term; talk among three or more forms blocs; Section 4's cost structure is explicit; AI's incarnation is weak and strong, under the alignment-tax premise.
- *Part II.* Diagnosis has its own Part, after the proofs: the shared-parameter table, the chain as four loops, the contest branches, and signatures joined to their evidence.
- *Part III.* One protection standard with a rule for the range of uncertainty; profiles that record the direction of selection; the alternatives at their strongest; one requirements table by kind of support; the generative loop closing through what all share, not through its winners.
- *Part IV.* E2's cost counted without redefining O1 (crux C10); the judgment in three tiers, with custody prepared now and activated on registered indicators, and the ranking of whole designs stated as a judgment.

**Narrative moved here from the text.** v6.0 §5.5 noted that Theorem 5.16 was "the correct form of an earlier claim, 'the premise that motivates the race is what makes the race ruinous'"; V6.1 replaces it in turn by the enforcement condition, Corollary 5.6 and Corollary 7.3. v6.0 §4.5 reported that in the first version of the field model a liability reform at development unwound capture while development was a keystone (now SUPPLEMENT S4). v6.0 §10.5 carried two predictions over from earlier versions (now TESTS §5). v6.0's status line and file paths are in README.

**Length.** The structure plan set the core at about 26,000 words with a cap of 27,000. The assembled core is about 30,500 words, of which Parts III and IV are 37%. The excess comes from the repaired and added results (the loop gain and the price of standing, the five-part enforcement condition and its corollaries, the alignment-tax premise and the classes of divergence, requirements by kind), from the content restored at the fidelity review's request, and from a dozen points restored by the paragraph check of LEDGER §6. Cutting further would mean moving more results to one-line mentions; that choice is left to Ren.
