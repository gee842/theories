## S9. Requirements

### Proposition {{#require}}, row by row

Each necessary row is the cited result read as a necessary condition in its model and over its horizon; the last column of the core's table states when the cited result does not apply. The derivations and the counterexamples that fix the scope follow.

- **R1, consequences return.** Theorem {{#safeguards}}(3): under (reach), with a race carrier present, viability at every horizon needs long-run average effective liability at least the private advantage. *Not at a finite horizon:* a zero-liability trajectory need not cross the floor before $H$. With the host starting at full capacity, floor 0.2, $\rho=0.01$, $h_V=0.02$ and zero liability, even full load from the start keeps $V(50)\ge0.435$, and crosses the floor only at $t=109.9$, although (reach) holds (HO; Codex). At a finite horizon the necessary condition is weaker: liability must keep the cumulative advantage below the path that just reaches the floor at $H$, by monotone comparison.
- **R2, coverage.** Propositions {{#load}} and {{#nounilateral}}(1): an uncovered race carrier with unbounded cumulative advantage takes the field. *Not needed* when harms are local, or when the uncovered carriers' combined maximal load cannot reach the floor, $h_Vp_{\rm unc}\bar e<\rho(1-V_{\min})$, or within a horizon shorter than the crossing time.
- **R3a, the rate law.** Proposition {{#corrrate}}(2): in the field model with $\mathcal C$ captured, a cultivation channel and $[H_\gamma]$, the certificate holds at every date only if $\alpha_{\rm cor}\ge\alpha_{\rm ctl}/(1+\eta)$. *Not needed* with $\chi=0$ or channels removed, with a fixed cost of control that does not fall, with nothing captured, or within a horizon shorter than the failure time.
- **R3b, no core.** Proposition {{#certificate}}(4): no capture from every initial state needs an empty core of mutually sustaining capture. *Not needed* if only the states actually reached must be excluded: with a row sum of two, the low initial state $(0.1,0.1)$ stays in the invariant rectangle $[0,0.1]\times[0,0.2]$ and converges to no capture (FA). A full row sum below one is the stronger, sufficient certificate.
- **R4, enforceable breakouts.** Proposition {{#enforce}} with $D\ge C$; Corollary {{#restraint}}. *Not needed* in Branch III, above the saturating level of Branch I′, in Branch II with timely detection, when $D<C$, or when objectives and sanctions give $\bar p_d\ge1$.
- **R5, summable uncovered share.** Theorem {{#coverage}} and its converse: if any of (variety grows), (coverage lags), (residual floor) fails, the theorem does not apply; at finite horizons the exposure of Proposition {{#freeze}} replaces it.
- **R6, independent observation.** Remark {{#alarm}} and Proposition {{#talk}}: sufficient for alarms that fire before the floor and for trust without good will; not necessary where channels that observe actions directly suffice.
- **R7, standing.** V3. Proposition {{#club}} shows that harm to outsiders is over-produced relative to the system optimum; it does not show that O1 or O2 is reached, so the requirement is normative.
- **R8, admission constraints.** Proposition {{#nested}}(3) and Lemma {{#retain}}: under relative, cumulative selection among developers and $(A_\tau)$, a divergence with common costs persists unless a constraint common to all, of equal incidence and sanctioned against evasion, covers it. *Not needed* where developers are selected by public mandate, where the net private cost of the safeguard is zero or negative, where decisions are not retained by contest performance; and admission reaches only to release.

**Sufficient safeguards.** Full row sums below one certify each corrector domain against every state (Proposition {{#certificate}}(2)); the safeguards of Section 10.3 give viability on the forgiving host and, with a margin, on harsher ones (Theorem {{#safeguards}}); not building, custody and detection faster than breakout lower $p_d$ (Corollary {{#restraint}}(a)); verification with $\upsilon F$ at least the net exit gain deters irreversible exit (Proposition {{#overhang}}).

### Proof of Proposition {{#nounilateral}}

(1) While $\tau_{\rm eff}<\nu$, a carrier's unilateral contribution to the safeguards is a cost borne by it alone, selected against (Lemma {{#blind}}(2); Proposition {{#noinside}}(1)). (2) With rule-makers as players in a contest of standing, unilateral imposition of the liability is restraint in Proposition {{#wow}}, not chosen when $\lambda_s>(\Upsilon_s-n_s)/(n_s-1)$; whether an agreement among them is an equilibrium is governed by Proposition {{#assure}}. (3) Under $\tau(1-x_{\rm rule})>\nu$ the private payoff of load is negative. $\square$
