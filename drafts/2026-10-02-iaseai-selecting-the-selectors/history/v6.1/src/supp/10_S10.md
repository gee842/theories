## S10. The generative alternative

### A common frontier

**Proposition {{#commonfront}} in full.** *If the value of capability is common, $\pi_i=\Omega(\mathcal E)/n-ce_i-\ell\mathcal E$ with $\Omega$ concave, private incentives give $\Omega'(\mathcal E)=n(c+\ell)$ against the joint optimum $\Omega'(\mathcal E)=c+n\ell$, so total intensity is at or below the joint optimum: the residual failure is too little speed, not too much, and the joint optimum must also respect the host's floor.* (Compare first-order conditions.)

**Proposition {{#commonfrontn}} in full.** *Of $n$ actors, cooperators hold effort $e_c$ and racers choose theirs; whoever wins the decisive contest gains $\Omega$, and every loser bears $L_b$. Under separate frontiers every effort enters the contest separately; under a common frontier cooperators pool effort into one entity of strength $(me_c)^r$, which yields a common value $\Omega_c$ split equally and confers no dominance among them. With $n=4$, $\Omega=\Omega_c=L_b=c=1$, $\ell=0.3$, $e_c=0.05$, $r=1.3$: (1) under separate frontiers the unique pure equilibrium is all racing; (2) under a common frontier all cooperating ($R=0.140>T=0.002$) and all racing are both strict equilibria, cooperating being a best response iff trust in each rival exceeds about 0.61, or 0.82 if a deviator best-responds to fixed intensities; (3) treating the others as a coalition maximizing their summed payoff, the coalition's cooperation is dominant; (4) a small pooled effort ($e_c=0.03$) leaves only all racing, and with $n=2$ all cooperating is an equilibrium for $r$ up to about 1.2; (5) holding $\Omega_c$ and $L_b$ fixed, the pooled effort needed to keep $T\le R$ is 0.0054, 0.035, 0.141 and 0.49 for $\Omega=0.5,1,2,4$.* (`../v4/checks_v45.py`, N22: racers' intensities are the symmetric fixed point of best responses, each verified as a global best response; cases without a symmetric pure equilibrium are excluded.) A common frontier turns a dilemma into a coordination problem while the value of winning is moderate; when it is large, keeping ahead of defectors is the race moved up a level.

### Proof of Proposition {{#share}}

(1) Pooled payoffs $\pi'_j=(1-\beta)\pi_j+\beta\bar\pi$ give $J_i=[(1-\beta)+\beta(1+\theta_0)/n]\pi_i+[\theta_0(1-\beta)+\beta(1+\theta_0)(n-1)/n]\bar\pi_{-i}$; dividing by the first coefficient gives $\theta_{\rm eff}$, increasing in $\beta$ for $\theta_0<n-1$ with value $n-1$ at $\beta=1$; at $\theta_0=-1$, $J_i=(1-\beta)(\pi_i-\bar\pi_{-i})$, and under relative selection $\pi'_k-\pi'_l=(1-\beta)(\pi_k-\pi_l)$. (2) With the promise kept with probability $\varrho$, each payoff is $w_i[(1-\beta\varrho)\Omega_e+\Omega_s]+\varrho\beta\Omega_e/n-ce_i-\ell\mathcal E$; the shared term cancels from relative selection; a mutant at $y$ among residents at $x$ has relative payoff $\Omega_{\rm eff}(y^r-x^r)/(y^r+(n-1)x^r)-c(y-x)$, with zero slope at $x^\ast=r\Omega_{\rm eff}/(nc)$, concave for $r=1$, and no profitable mutant on a grid for $r<1$ (SH). (3) is Proposition {{#compound}} with Proposition {{#loopgain}}'s reflexive channel removed when $\chi=0$; then the return to influence is zero (Proposition {{#influence}}) and $d^\ast=A/c_{\rm ev}$ (Proposition {{#corrrate}}(1)). $\square$ *Numerics:* with $n=4$, $r=1$, $c=1$, $\Omega_e=1$, the uninvadable intensity is 0.25 without a positional part, and 0.125 with half the divisible prize shared; with $\Omega_s=1$, sharing all of $\Omega_e$ only halves it from 0.5 to 0.25; in a common field of intensities $(0.2,0.05,0.1,0.1)$ the higher-intensity carrier out-earns the lower by 0.017 with half shared, and the difference turns negative only beyond 55%. An earlier version said that only changing what winning converts into reverses the direction; in this model none of the three levers makes the selected intensity zero while a positional prize remains.

**Remark {{#twoorders}} in full.** At the first order, more contenders make the race fiercer (Proposition {{#join}}(4)) and fragmentation raises speed and conditional risk (Bueno de Mesquita, Dziuda and Polborn 2026); at the second, cultivated influence rises with $\sum_kp_k^{1+(2-\xi_v)/\eta}$ (Proposition {{#corrrate}}(4)). A merged frontier also raises connectivity and with it common-mode failure, so it keeps modules of independent lineage inside it (Proposition {{#topology}}).

### Proof of Theorem {{#safeguards}}, and the margin on harsher hosts

(1) (I2) with margin in rule-making gives a full row sum below one, so $x_{\rm rule}\to0$ (Proposition {{#certificate}}(2)); once (I1) is enacted, $\tau_{\rm eff}>\nu$ after a finite time, load has negative private payoff, $E\to e_{\min}$, and $\liminf V\ge1-h_Ve_{\min}/\rho>V_{\min}$ on the host {{eq:host}}. For unanticipated harms, (I2) in evaluation gives $x_{\rm ev}\to0$, so masking falls to $m_{\rm out}<m_{\rm fl}$, or the conservative rule applies, and the alarm fires above the floor. (2) Without (I1), Proposition {{#load}} applies; without (I2) in rule-making, $x_{\rm rule}\to1$ and $\tau_{\rm eff}\to0$; without (I3), silent crossing follows; with rule-making captured first, the floor can be crossed when rule-making adapts slowly. (3) If the long-run average is below $\nu$, there are $\epsilon>0$ and $T_0$ with average at most $\nu-\epsilon$ beyond $T_0$; the high-load carrier then grows relative to every lower-load carrier and the argument of Proposition {{#load}} applies. $\square$

*The transient and the margin (HO).* On the logistic host, with liability $\Delta=\tau_{\rm eff}-\nu=0.08$ from the start and the race strategy holding 99% of activity, the host dips to 0.1397 before recovering. On the pointwise harsher threshold host $\rho_A(V/A-1)(1-V)$ with $e_{\min}=0.1$ and the same initial composition, the minimum margin $\Delta_{\min}$ that keeps the transient above the host's own threshold $V_u(e_{\min})$ is 0.26, 0.31, 0.36, 0.42 and 0.51 for $A=0.05,0.10,0.15,0.20,0.25$, with $V_u$ equal to 0.25, 0.31, 0.36, 0.42 and 0.48. For $A\ge0.10$, $V_u$ lies above a normative floor of 0.3, so the floor must be set no lower than the host's threshold for crossing it to be the first loss. This is the first computable instance of the correctable kernel of Definition {{#kernel}}.

### Proofs of Propositions {{#rivalverify}} and {{#participate}}

*Rival verification.* Apply Proposition {{#certificate}}(2) to the verifier's full row. A verifier taking 0.10 from the verified party and 0.95 from shared sources is captured once those sources are. A verifier biased toward finding violations is an uninformative sender of unverifiable claims (Proposition {{#talk}}), so it must rest on evidence the other side can check.

**Proposition {{#participate}} in full.** *Two actors have private types, strong or weak, with costs $c_s<c_w$, each strong with probability $p_s$ independently. Outside the arrangement types are revealed in a complete-information all-pay contest for $\Omega+L_b$: a strong actor against a weak one gets $U=(\Omega+L_b)(1-c_s/c_w)-L_b$, otherwise $-L_b$; the race's survival is $\Phi^{\rm race}$. The arrangement yields a common value net of costs $S_a$, divided in shares $\mathsf s_i$, with survival $\Phi^R$; utility is $\Phi_i(V_i+\bar V)$. (1) Under complete information and transferable utility, a pair gains iff $\Phi^R(S_a+2\bar V)\ge\sum_i\Phi^{\rm race}_i(U_i+\bar V)$. (2) With private independent types and participation decided before types are revealed, Bayesian incentive compatibility makes each type's interim expected share independent of its report, budget balance makes the interim shares constants summing to one, and the arrangement is feasible without verification iff $\Phi^R(S_a+2\bar V)\ge\sum_i\sup_cE[\Phi^{\rm race}(c,t_j)(U_i+\bar V)\mid c]$; ex-post shares may still vary with reports. (3) Verification revealing types removes the information rents, distinct from timely detection of defection. (4) The information condition relaxes as $\Phi^{\rm race}/\Phi^R$ falls. (5) Under complete information an overwhelmingly strong actor takes part only for a share near its race payoff; raising the joint value, lowering the value of winning, compressing survival or changing cost asymmetry can still make equal shares feasible.* *Numerics:* with $c_s/c_w=0.1$, $\Omega=1$, $L_b=0$, $S_a=1$ and every $\Phi=1$, the sum of suprema is 0.36, 0.90, 1.44 and 1.71 at $p_s=0.8,0.5,0.2,0.05$: feasible without verification in the first two cases and not in the last two, although cooperation is efficient for every pair; with $\Phi^R=0.95$, $\bar V=1$ and $p_s=0.2$, equal shares become feasible once $\Phi^{\rm race}/\Phi^R\le0.872$ (`../v5/checks_v5.py`, K7). Which mechanism is second-best depends on the mechanisms allowed and on commitment, not solved here.
