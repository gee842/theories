## 9. What any adequate arrangement must do

> **Claim.** Before designs are compared, what every adequate design must do can be stated, each requirement with the kind of support it has: necessary in a named model over a named horizon, sufficient, or normative. Several requirements cannot be met by any actor alone, and competition among actors does not supply them.
>
> **Why.** A requirement stated with its model and horizon can be checked against any design, including regulated competition, which can then be found to meet it.

**{{!Proposition:require}} (Requirements, by the kind of support each has) [thm∣prem].** *Each requirement in the table is necessary only in the model and over the horizon stated, and the last column names the routes by which an arrangement can do without it; safeguards marked sufficient are not necessary; requirements marked normative rest on values. The proposition organizes results proved elsewhere and does not rank designs.*

| Requirement | Kind | Needed for, in which model | Basis | Not needed when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** Consequences return: long-run effective liability at least the private advantage of load | necessary | viability at every horizon; host, selection, a race carrier present | Theorem {{>safeguards}}(3) | finite horizon before the floor is reached; (reach) fails; cumulative advantage bounded |
| **R2** Coverage of every actor whose share can grow | necessary | the same | Propositions {{#load}}, {{#join}}; Lemma {{#blind}} | harms local; the uncovered cannot reach the floor in the horizon |
| **R3a** Independent correction growing at least $\alpha_{\rm ctl}/(1+\eta)$, or the conversion channels removed | necessary | the evaluation certificate at every date; field model, capture seeded, $[H_\gamma]$ | Proposition {{#corrrate}} | $\chi=0$; a fixed cost of control AI does not lower; nothing captured; horizon shorter than the failure time |
| **R3b** No core of mutually sustaining capture among the corrector domains | necessary | no capture from every initial state; field model | Proposition {{#certificate}}(4) | only the states actually reached need be excluded |
| **R4** Breakout probability within the enforceable bound, $p_d\le\bar p_d$ | necessary | enforced restraint where breakouts are possible and $D\ge C$ | Proposition {{#enforce}}; Corollary {{#restraint}} | no position beyond punishment; saturation; timely detection; $D<C$; objectives and sanctions with $\bar p_d\ge1$ |

The requirements constrain functions, not institutional form: one institution can meet them, and so can mutually recognized institutions of several jurisdictions, a federation, or a polycentric complex. Four further safeguards are sufficient and not necessary: full inbound row sums below one for every corrector (Proposition {{#certificate}}(2)); the safeguards of Section 10.3, with a margin on harsher hosts; not building, custody, or detection faster than breakout, which lower $p_d$; and verification with a sanction at least the net gain from exit.

**{{!Proposition:nounilateral}} (No unilateral solution) [thm].** Correction and coverage are not supplied by any one carrier or rule-maker. *Assume (reach) and replicator selection with a race carrier present. While effective liability is below the private advantage, no carrier's unilateral contribution to the safeguards is favoured by selection. If no single rule-maker reaches every carrier whose combined load satisfies (reach), and the rule-makers are themselves in a contest of standing with weight $\lambda_s>(\Upsilon_s-n_s)/(n_s-1)$, no rule-maker imposes the liability alone in equilibrium. And restraint is each carrier's best response only once effective liability, $\tau(1-x_{\rm rule})$, exceeds the advantage: enacting $\tau>\nu$ is not enough while rule-making is captured.*

*Proof:* S9.

R2 needs more than one party by definition. R3 is a second-order public good that competitors do not supply (Proposition {{#noinside}}). Of the means in R4, custody needs more than one party, and not building binds only the one that refrains. On branches where the mechanisms are active, a minimum of cooperation is therefore necessary: binding commitments covering every capable actor, with a layer of correction that no competitor controls. This is a conditional result combined with evidence and a stated standard, not a proof from first principles.

**Coverage can unravel.** The protection of Lemma {{#blind}}(2) holds only under full coverage. When a large participant leaves, the constraint borne by those who remain becomes unilateral, is selected against, and can trigger further exits (compare minimum participation in Barrett 2003). An arrangement therefore states its participation threshold, the coverage at which its constraints remain neutral among compliers, and the conditions of access that keep coverage above it. A design that cannot state its threshold has not shown that it can last.
