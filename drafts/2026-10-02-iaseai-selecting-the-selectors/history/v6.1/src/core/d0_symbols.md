## Symbols and labels

Each symbol below has one meaning throughout the core and both companions; any other symbol is local to the model in which it is declared. Supplement S0 gives the full table and the maintained assumptions.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $n$; $i,j$; $k$ | number of actors; actors; carriers |
| $p_k$; $e_k$, $E$ | share; load or intensity, and the field's load |
| $\pi_i$, $J_i$, $W_i$, $\Phi_i$ | material payoff; objective acted on; true welfare; survival (O1, O2) |
| $\theta$; $\theta_\Gamma$; $\lambda$; $\kappa$ | weight on others in the self-model; its loop-induced working value; weight on winning; unpriced coupling |
| $\beta$; $\kappa_c$, $\kappa_j$; $k_0$ | share divided equally; convertible share of what is won (always subscripted); inflow not won in the contest |
| $r$; $d$; $\delta_f$; $\delta$ | decisiveness; decay or correction rate; diffusion; discount factor only |
| $\Gamma$, $\Gamma_\delta$; $\omega_j$ | loop gain and its discounted form; won share of stock $j$ |
| $p_d$, $\bar p_d$; $C_i,D_i,v_i,g_i$ | breakout probability and its enforceable bound; values of cooperation, decisive position, punishment, and the deviation gain |
| $V$, $V_{\min}$; $\rho$, $h_V$; $\nu$, $\tau_{\rm eff}$ | host and floor; regeneration and load coefficient; private advantage and effective liability |
| $\chi$; $b_{ij}$, $G_{ij}$; $m$ | appropriability of influence over correctors; conversion and conversion gain; masking |
| $\alpha_{\rm ctl}$, $\alpha_{\rm cor}$; $\eta$ | rates at which the cost of control falls and independent correction grows; curvature of the cost of capacity |
| $Y$, $U$; $\Upsilon$; $\Theta$ | hazardous variety and contestable coverage; value of restraint to the field over its private cost; the justified range of uncertainty |
