**What changed in the argument.** The review round's corrections became the spine rather than caveats. Six tightenings (plan §1):
1. **Compounding prices standing** wherever reinvestment's relative return exceeds its common return (Proposition 3.9). With a fixed prize this holds wherever the discounted loop gain is positive; with a prize proportional to capacity, and neither provision nor diffusion, exactly where leads compound, so that $(1-\beta)r>1$ is both conditions. Each actor computes correctly; the game is wrong (Remark 3.10). V6.1's "where the loop gain is high, every forward-looking actor" and "compounding manufactures the relative objective" are withdrawn.
2. **The levers act channel by channel** (Proposition S2.4): sharing and diffusion lower the gain in every channel; provision not won in the contest where decisiveness rises less than in proportion to the stock, as with capacity; correction where it rises at least in proportion, as with rule support. Correction that only speeds a decay common to all does not lower the gain, since a contest of ratios does not see it. Trees for capacity, correction for rule support, sharing and openness for all: "change what winning confers", made exact.
3. **The scissors turn on one pivot**, the share of capacity that has to be won (Proposition 5.17). Each arm is proved in its own model; a reduced joint model closes the first two together [num] (Remark S5.2); their co-movement in the world is conjecture C0, linked to verification by $p_d=1-\upsilon(T_{\rm dec})$ (Remark 5.5).
4. **The rate law has two halves** (Proposition 4.5): sufficient at any speed of adaptation, necessary where capacity tracks its selected level. Correction resting on human capacity that delegation erodes needs $+w\delta_h$, and capture that makes delegation look safe is hysteretic (Proposition 4.6).
5. **Masked divergences**, a fourth class, make coordination necessary but not sufficient, and are the strongest alignment reason for stopping (E2) and slowing (E3) (Remark 6.8).
6. **The thresholds table** integrates the paper (Table 7.1): nine thresholds, one inequality each, with the result that proves it, what pushes toward danger, the lever that reverses it, and the side of the enforcement condition (14) it moves. A few parameters recur across rows; that is the formal sense of "interconnected conditions".

No result is used before it is stated. Results are numbered by section and keyed by stable names.

**What changed in the argument.**

Lemma 3.3 gains incidence and evasion; Lemma 3.10 holds for every partition and names the transmission term; talk among three or more forms blocs; Section 4's cost structure is explicit; AI's incarnation is weak and strong, under the alignment-tax premise.
