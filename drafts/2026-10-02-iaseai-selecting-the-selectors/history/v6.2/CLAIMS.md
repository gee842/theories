## 1. Thesis

The thesis is stated as the abstract states it, each part with its provenance.

Where whatever can gain or lose share is selected by relative success, what is won converts into the capacity to win again, and capacity converts decisively into winning, the carriers are under a selection pressure of winning (R; Definition 2.1); where success can also buy control over the selection itself (the rules, the correctors, the formation of minds and the language), the pressure closes on itself: the meta-attractor of winning (R, A; Definition 2.4). In the author's account it accelerates the contest, makes it harder and harder to regulate, and alienates language (R; THEORY §12.4).

In specified models, each under the conditions it states (D):
- near equal standing, with the prize and conduct fixed, the conditions multiply into one structural loop gain, and leads compound where it exceeds one (Proposition 2.2);
- to first order, an actor that looks ahead weighs standing above what all share wherever reinvestment's relative return exceeds its common return: with a fixed prize, wherever the discounted loop gain is positive; with a prize proportional to capacity and neither provision nor diffusion, exactly where leads compound. Each actor computes correctly; the game is wrong (Proposition 3.9; Remark 3.10);
- influence over correctors that can be kept is selected in the largest carriers, and where AI cheapens control, independent correction keeps evaluation uncaptured from a certified start if it grows at a stated fraction of that rate (Proposition 4.3; Proposition 4.5);
- restraint is enforceable only while each deviation's chance of becoming decisive stays below a threshold; the share of capacity that has to be won raises that chance where leads compound and lowers the threshold where looking ahead narrows objectives, each arm proved in its own model, and whether they close together in the world is what the diagnosis tests (Proposition 5.4; Proposition 5.17; C0);
- where contestable coverage falls ever further behind hazardous variety, the chance of irreversible loss is at least a fixed fraction of the share left uncovered, and failure is shared, survival tends to zero whoever races (Theorem 5.13); what decides whether growth stops where need is met is the objective: growth is not the danger; the mind is (R, rendered D13);
- aligning each AI system to its principal does not align the game among principals, and divergences masked under evaluation can escape even a common constraint conditioned on evaluated behaviour (Proposition 6.1; Proposition 6.7; Remark 6.8).

With one host and no outside, integration against global failures that cannot be undone has to be anticipated: understanding that we are one is a long-term requirement if growth and change are favoured (R, D; §9). The way out is not to abolish competition, growth or selection but to change what winning confers, so that the same reflexive structure selects for contribution and persists through what all share, not through its winners (R, A). Under a standard that protects irreversible losses first, and on the present evidence, the change is warranted in three tiers: steps taken now, on every reading of the cruxes or until a named crux is measured favourable; stopping the decisive class, common custody and pace, prepared now and activated by a mechanism committed in advance; and whole designs, the coercive restriction of frontier inputs among them, compared and not settled (D). The meaning is affirmative, and comes before the commitment: we are not only our separate lives but the larger process of becoming, going on through us, and that is good (R, in the rendering D34 of §8); what has to be opposed is one logic, a part that forgets it belongs to a body, not growth, competition or any people (R).

**What is not claimed.** That competition, growth, technology or any people are the danger; that every path through a race ends at zero survival; that decisive advantage is attainable now; that the thresholds move together in the world (C0); that the signatures load on one factor; that relative actors enforce or talk as blocs; that the structural loop gain has been estimated for any channel; that the generative order attracts (the name is for the mechanism, and attraction is shown only in the field model of Section 4); that one institutional blueprint is uniquely feasible or best; anything about any actor's motives; that understanding suffices; or that AI should not be built: stopping (E2) concerns the decisive class only, and slowing (E3) concerns pace.

## 2. Definitions fixed before comparison

- **The selection pressure of winning** (Definition 2.1): three conditions held together, each to a degree: relative selection, with a share $\beta$ divided equally; convertibility of winnings into stocks, beside *provision not won in the contest*, called in prose *provision beneath the game*; and decisiveness $r$. *Carriers* are whatever selection acts on. The reflexive closure, success buying control over the selection itself, is the meta-attractor. Three conditions plus the closure, not four (CHANGELOG records the departure from the V6.1 plan).
- **The meta-attractor** (Definition 2.4): three objects kept apart, the mechanism, an attracting configuration in a specified model, and a long transient; a claim of the second kind about the world is a conjecture. **Pathological and generative** (Remark 2.5).
- **The loop gain.** The *structural loop gain* $\Gamma$ of (2); the *equilibrium gain* $\Gamma^{\rm eq}$ where rivals respond (Proposition 2.2(4)); the discounted gain $\Gamma_\delta$ and the common gain $\Gamma^{\rm c}_\delta$ (Proposition 3.9); the *won share* $\omega$, the stakes set against provision beneath the game, the pivot of Proposition 5.17. The *drive*, the belief that one is in a struggle for survival, is not a factor of $\Gamma$ (THEORY §7.1).
- **Two errors** (Remark 3.10): the *held self-model*, which can draw its boundary inside the system, and the *working objective* that compounding sets, which is accurate for the actor and collectively self-defeating. **The price of standing**: the weight compounding puts on relative position.
- **Ψ, the winning model** (Definition 3.13): individually rational where compounding prices standing, collectively self-defeating, and false as a doctrine about nature.
- **Capture** (Definition 4.1): three things separately documented, in two directions, accommodation and barriers. A domain is *certified* when its full inbound row sum is below one.
- **Coverage** (THEORY §5.3): hazardous variety $Y_t$ and coverage $U_t$, both counted in predefined hazard modes of a class, $U_t$ those for which correction meeting the four conditions of contestable correction (legible, overrulable, exercised, resourced) is in place at a stated catch rate; the uncovered share $u_t=(1-U_t/Y_t)^+$.
- **Divergences** (THEORY §6.4): privately visible, common-cost, delayed and masked; the alignment-tax premise $(A_\tau)$; incarnation, weak and strong; principal alignment and purpose alignment (§6.1).
- **Signatures** T1–T6, in groups W, C and N (THEORY §7.3).
- **Values** V1–V5, **outcomes** O1–O4 and **the protection standard** with the rule for $\Theta$ (THEORY §1.3, §8.1); **the correctable kernel** (Definition 8.1); **profiles** (Definition 8.3); **durability tests** D1–D8 (Definition 8.4); **the marks** G1–G7 of a generative direction (THEORY §8.2).
- **Requirements** R1–R9, R3 and R8 in two parts (Table 9.1); R9 confirmed by Ren on 4 October 2026.
- **The arrangements**: E1, narrow (decisions whose delegation would lock with near certainty) and broad (AI not an instrument in any contest that tends toward dominance or concentration), broad being the scope and narrow the first step; E2, stopping the decisive class; E3, development at the pace of understanding, class by class. Stopping is not slowing.
- **The tiers** (THEORY §12.1), sorted by kind of justification: 1(a), on every reading of the cruxes; 1(b), withdrawn only where a named crux is measured favourable to withdrawal; 2, prepared now and activated by mechanism; 3, compared, not settled.
- **Readiness** (Definition 11.2), seven dimensions with residence and a sequential decision rule; the transition's **tracks** 1–5 (THEORY §11.4).
- **Labels** are listed at the end of THEORY and in SUPPLEMENT S0, with every rename of this version.
