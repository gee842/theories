## 2. Disagreements and errors resolved by derivation or log

1. **The box's correction sentence.** The plan has "rises with the stock's level"; A follows it, B has "in proportion". By chansign(2), correction lowers Γ iff ξ_r > δ_f/(d+δ_f), and lowers Γ_δ iff ξ_r > (1−δ+δδ_f)/(1−δ+δd+δδ_f). Both thresholds are below 1, so "rises at least in proportion" (ξ≥1) is an exact sufficient condition. "Rises with the level" (ξ>0) fails with diffusion, and for Γ_δ even without it: at ξ=0.1, δ=0.9, d=0.2, δ_f=0 the threshold is 0.357, so correction *raises* Γ_δ. Final text: provision for "less than in proportion" (an iff), correction for "at least in proportion". A random check of all four parts of chansign found 0 sign mismatches in 79,995 derivatives.
2. **The cap-equality boundary.** A has 1<Γ≤n/(n−1); B has strict. At Γ=n/(n−1), φ′(K*)=0, so first order is undecided. Strict is kept.
3. **Continuity at Γ_δ≥1.** Both proofs gave only the lower bound PV ≥ cε^a. An upper bound is added: the sum up to T′ is at most ηδ^{T′}, the tail at most δ^{T′}, both O(ε^{ln(1/δ)/lnΛ′})→0. A's "positive on both sides" is dropped, since PV is negative for ε<0.
4. **The spectral radius.** V6.1 and A have "for Λ>max D_jj the right side falls from +∞". That fails when the largest diagonal entry belongs to a channel with u_jv_j=0. B's version is correct and kept.
5. **Talk among three or more.** V6.1 and A have "not a function of Γ". Under the unified talk result (plan; D2-opus §13) the effective bias b₀(n−1−θ)/(n−1+θ) decreases in θ, and θ=θ_Γ. So the clause is now false. It is dropped, and the S2 table row reads "Yes, through the working self-model".
6. **The diffusion-only stall.** Both drafts have "0.36 of the cap". The CE log ([finding]) shows the stall is at K=0.36, which is 0.16 of the cap (cap 2.25). Corrected.
7. **The minimum of φ in the reviewer's diffusion case.** B has "0.0007", which depends on the grid: 0.0001 on m04's grid and 0.000075 exact. Now "about 10⁻⁴".
8. **"Restore the cap in every case checked"** (A's compound Scope) is inaccurate, because cases at equality fall away by design. B's "agree with (2)(i)" is kept. The simulation moves every carrier (code checked).
9. **Bench.** A's "no rank-one choice reproduces it" is false: any 2×2 nonnegative matrix splits as diagonal plus rank one. Dropped. The distinction kept is structural, a pair fed directly versus every stock fed through one win share (Sys m1).
10. **Price magnitudes in the loopgain Scope** (A: "up to about three times"). This understates the log (×4.75 with a shared loss; ×7.87 with patience) and would make a second home for §3's standprice Scope, where both U03 drafts carry the range. The core Scope keeps stability numbers only. All price numbers are in S2 `eqgain`.
11. **"A smaller gain shrinks the region"** (A, multiplicity). m01 F shows multiplicity at Γ=0.2 and 0.3 (d=0.05), and its absence only at coarser rounds. Unsupported, so dropped.
12. **The `eqgain` tag.** A has [thm∣prem; num], B [thm; num]. The structural claims are proved in 2.2(4) and the static factor is exact, so [thm; num] with per-item tags.
13. **r^eq/r range.** B has "2.5 to 5". The MP log gives 5.09 at Γ=0.8 (d=0.1) and 5.41 at Γ=0.9 (r=1), so "about 2.5 to 5.4 at structural gains from 0.45 to 0.9".
14. **The corpus key.** A's [corpus: keep-the-possibility-space-open] becomes [corpus: possibility-space], the key used throughout V6.1/V6.2. The quotation is verified at `keep-the-possibility-space-open/README.md:262`, with "Then the game collapses".
15. **chansign(4).** A has "ratio only when k₀>0; level always". B gives the iff 1+(ξ−1)ω>0, derived as the elasticity of the term in κ, and A's version is its two special cases. B is kept. B's chansign(3) clause "ξ>1 raises" is confirmed by CH (the X² channel, 1.75→2.25).
16. **The off-parity reflexive gain** (B: "about 3.5" from the systems review text, with no script). Recomputed from corrrate(4) with η=1, ξ_v=0, n=4: Σp³ = 0.2231 against 0.0625, so 3.57. Now "about 3.6", with the arithmetic in the text.
17. **Leaders at K̄=K* with diffusion** (B's open issue). The leader's inflow (x/n)(d_KΓ₀−δ_f) is verified numerically to 10⁻⁹. It is kept in the S2 proof, and the Scope says the bound fails there.
18. **The proportional-prize multiplier** m = [(1−d−δ_f)+κg(1−β)r]/G is verified by one-step simulation (1.013333 against 1.013333).
19. **delayosc.** Modulus 1.00747, period 51.56, net gain 0.25: recomputed.
