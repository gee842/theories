## 2. Disagreements decided by derivation

a. **Topology budget.** A said that two or three checks per layer "catch more" than six checks across the gap. Two per layer catch 0.941 < 0.989 [SM(13)], so the sentence now states three per layer: 18 layer-checks against 36, 0.994 against 0.989.

b. **Guan et al. 2025.** A read it as showing that the capability gap of legibility at fixed compute is "zero or negative". The verified EV6 row says something else: reinforcement learning "does not materially decrease monitorability", and a smaller model at higher effort matches capability with more monitorability *at higher inference cost*. That is a positive tax paid in compute. B's reading is taken: the instance stays conditional.

c. **Tax paid from a lead.** Under $(A_\tau)$ the matched variant shares every other advantage, so a lead cannot pay the tax against it. CI(j) and AL(6.7) run the lead against an unmatched rival. B's wording ("against rivals that lack its other advantages") is correct; A's "a developer with an independent lead pays the tax…" is not, under the stated premise.

d. **Compliance at equality.** `blind`(3): the evading variant grows iff $c_k>\bar F_k$. So at $\bar F_k=c_k$ evasion is neutral, not selected out. The S6 proof now says "does not grow iff $\ge$; selected out if strict". The core keeps "keeps the divergence out if … at least", which matches `blind`'s "compliance survives".

e. **`masked`: Proposition or Remark.** A's (1) is `blind`(3) with $\bar F=p_{\rm det}F$ plus the premise that masking bounds $p_{\rm det}$. Its (2) says that a protocol safe against an adversarial system of that capability bounds the hazard, which is true by definition of "safe". Its (3) holds by construction. Under the plan's rule ("Proposition if a statement is proved"), the honest kind is Remark, with the formal part in italics and tagged [thm∣prem]. Number 6.8 goes to `masked`, and `topology` stays 6.9, as in V6.1.

f. **Where the capability bound comes from.** B states admission "in capability" without saying how capability is measured. A capability evaluation is a lower bound, and sandbagging lowers it (EV6, van der Weij). A threshold on shown capability is therefore gamed by the very masking it guards against. A's point is kept: the bound comes from compute, training data and fine-tuning elicitation (`alarm`).

g. **`delegatelock`(3) for $p<1$.** A's "the ratio can cross one from above, which excludes both corners and leaves an interior number…" needs the ratio to be monotone. B's condition (decreasing in $k$, crossing between $m-1$ and $m$) is taken. The proof is spelled out: $m$ delegators is an equilibrium iff ratio$(m-1)\ge1\ge$ ratio$(m)$, so a decreasing ratio gives exactly one $m$.

h. **Bounded-speed survival.** B's S6 bound holds for every $p$, not just $p=1$: $\Phi_N=Q_N[1-p+pw_N]\ge Q_Nw_N$.

i. **`opaquecorr` summability.** B gave only a necessary condition, $\sum(1-c_{b,t})<\infty$; A said "iff summable". With $h_0>0$ and non-negative terms, the exact condition is $\sum(1-c_{b,t})<\infty$ *and* $\sum h_{c,t}<\infty$, with a per-period hazard below one for `nonsum`. Both the core sentence and S6.4 state it.

j. **Common cause.** $0.940=0.95\times0.989$ [SM(13)], so for a common cause independent of the checks the factor is exact. "Times $1-p_{\rm cc}$" is right; B's "at most" is too weak for the statement.

k. **`nearlock`.** Re-derived: $w_N\le w_D\le(1+s)w_N$, so $(1-p+pw_D)/(1-p+pw_N)\in[1,1+s]$ and $\Phi_D/\Phi_N\le(1-q_L)a(1+s)$. Tight as $n\to\infty$ with $k=0$, $p=1$. B's script (`planning/drafts/U06/B/checks/dl_nearlock.py`) was re-run: 0 violations in 200,000 draws, and ratio/bound 0.29, 0.67, 0.95, 0.9995 at $n=2,10,100,10^4$.

l. **`homeostasis`(2) roots.** Recomputed in this merge, independently of v5 HO. The two roots of $(x+b_s)(1-\psi_0-x)=K_h$ are $\iota=0.153$ and $0.937$, and both, with the corner, are best responses on a 2,001-point grid.

m. **`boundedopt`.** $\frac{d}{dR}(1-e^{-R}-cR)=0\Rightarrow R^\ast=\ln(1/c)$, which needs $c<1$; the premise is added.

n. **`delegate`(1).** "If for some $k$ it does not [exceed]": the gain is strictly below its limit at every finite $s$, so equality also fails. B's "falls short" missed the equality case.

o. **Symbols.**
   - $h_d$, $\beta_h$ (D1-opus).
   - $f\to p_\ell$: $f$ is the global fixed cost of capacity.
   - $q_{\rm cc}\to p_{\rm cc}$; $q_0,q_c\to h_0,h_{c,t}$; homeostasis's $q\to1-\psi_0$: $q$ is the global conversion capacity.
   - Checks per layer $m\to k_c$ (D1-opus); layer ratio $m_\ell\to\mathsf m$: $m$ is masking.
   - Attention $Y\to\mathsf A$: $Y$ is hazardous variety.
   - B's detection probability $\pi\to p_{\rm det}$: $\pi_i$ is payoff.
   - "$k$-fold" → "tenfold" (core).
