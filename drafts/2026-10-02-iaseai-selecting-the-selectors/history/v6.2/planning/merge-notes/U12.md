## 2. Disagreements resolved by derivation, not by vote

- **(a) Admission (item 7): "for classes whose tax is measured positive" (A, the plan's literal wording) against "for every common-cost class, withdrawn where measured ≤ 0" (B).**
  - The plan's own header for tier 1(b) is "withdrawn only if a named crux is measured favourable".
  - `nested`(3) and `blind`(3) make the constraint necessary exactly where $(A_\tau)$ holds. An unmeasured tax therefore falls under the burden of proof.
  - A's item was also internally inconsistent: it said both "measured positive" and "lapses where it pays privately".
  - Taken: B. Table 9.1 R8a ("for divergences whose costs fall on common terms") agrees.
- **(b) The `signal` claim.** A said the step "is a costly signal that separates" the two types. `signal` gives a condition: separation holds iff $c_{\mathsf S}\le\Pi_{\mathsf S}$ and $c_{\mathsf R}\ge\Pi_{\mathsf R}$. The merged text states the binding half: durability must make the step cost a relative actor at least what the induced restraint is worth to it.
- **(c) "Each of the design's failures has a reversal" (A; B in part).** X8's table marks two of the design's modes irreversible: the coercive fallback's escalation, and capture of custody. The text now reads "each … but two", and the box excepts the escalation.
- **(d) Which indicators activate what.** A gave one list of indicators for all three arrangements. Under the definitions of §5.3 and §11.3, E2 is not building the decisive class, so its trigger is time to decisiveness (C5, C7). E3 is per-class pace, so its trigger is coverage (C1, C3a, C12). B's split is taken.
- **(e) C12, favourable case.** B wrote "admission on evaluated behaviour". `masked` bounds $p_{\rm det}$ by how often the divergence shows *under evaluation*. The admission that C12 can license is on evidence the behaviour does not control (weights, activations, data; §6.4 and `alarm`). Corrected in item 7 and the C12 row.
- **(f) Delays (#9).** A wrote "E3 lengthens the time correction has". `tempo` bounds $a\tau_c$, and slowing lowers $a$, so the delay becomes short *relative* to the change. B's wording is taken.
- **(g) "Neither moves a threshold" (A).** E3 is G7 and moves the tempo and coverage thresholds of Table 7.1. The true statement is "neither changes what winning confers".
- **(h) Why measurement continues after C2 is favourable.** A: "fails later wherever control keeps cheapening faster than correction grows". The rate-law threshold is $\alpha_{\rm ctl}/(1+\eta)$ ($+w\delta_h$), and the necessary half needs capacity to track its selected level (`corrrate`(2)). The text now says both.
- **(i) Published research "spreads without coordination" (A, unconditionally).** By the Scope of `nested`, a safeguard spreads without coordination only where the tax is ≤ 0. B's form is taken.
- **(j) "Each condition the reverse of / set by a mechanism of Part I" (A, B).** Chamber consent is V3/R7 [val]. The text now says "two by mechanisms, one by value".
- **(k) The basis of "understanding acts only through agreement".** Both drafts cited `boundary`(3) for "transmission is selected against". `boundary`(3) is selection on intensities toward $\theta=-1$; it says nothing about transmission. The claim rests on `twoerrors`: with $\Gamma_\delta$ above $n\kappa/((n-1)(1+\kappa))$, even an actor holding $\theta_0=\kappa$ acts narrow, and accuracy does not correct it. Restated so.
- **(l) "The blades of {{scissors}} are closing" (B).** Each arm is proved; their co-movement is C0 [conj]. At the trigger only the breakout arm is implied (a shorter $T_{\rm dec}$ raises $p_d$). Restated so.
- **(o) A's `wingives` clause.** A wrote "What is shared as it arises lowers the gain from deviating". `wingives` shows instead that a promise for after victory is worth breaking by $\beta\Omega(n-1-\theta)/(n-1)$. The supported claim is that sharing as gains arise does not rest on the winner's restraint.
- **(p) C9a "zero or negative ⇒ item 7 withdrawn" (B).** Withdrawal turns on the total private tax, not on the capability part alone. Hence "net of C9b and C9c".
- **(q) "Continuation's failures are reversible … only by slowing or stopping" (A).** X8 marks the capture of national evaluators reversible by reform, and absorption by bounding racing. "Only by slowing or stopping" holds for the coverage row alone, so it is not carried.
