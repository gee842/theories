## 2. Disagreements decided by derivation

1. **`share`(2) under the $n-1$ convention.** $\Omega_{\rm eff}=(1-\beta\varrho\,n/(n-1))\Omega_e+\Omega_s$. At $\beta\varrho=1$ it is $\Omega_s-\Omega_e/(n-1)$. So it is positive for every $\beta$ iff $\Omega_s>\Omega_e/(n-1)$, and $\Omega_{\rm eff}\ge\Omega_s$ only while $\beta\varrho\le(n-1)/n$. A's core statement is right. B's core statement gave only the all-$n$ case, and V6.1's "for every $\beta$ when $\Omega_s>0$" holds only under that convention.
2. **`share`(3): non-convertibility with diffusion.** Equal standing gives $d_KK^\ast=\kappa_c\Omega/n+k_0$, so $S^\ast=(\kappa_c\Omega+nk_0)/d_K$. With $k_0=0$, `compound`(1) becomes $(1-\beta)r-1<\delta_f/d_K$, and $\kappa_c$ cancels. Non-convertibility stabilizes only with provision (B, V6.1, §2.2), not "with provision or diffusion" (A).
3. **Scope of $r\le1$.** A put $r\le1$ in `share`'s preamble. Then $(1-\beta)r-1\le0$ and part (3)'s instability condition can never hold. $r\le1$ belongs to (2) only (B).
4. **`commonfront` proof.** $n(c+\ell)-(c+n\ell)=(n-1)c\ge0$, with equality iff $n=1$ or $c=0$ (B). A's "iff $c\ge\ell$" is false.
5. **`dissipate`.** "Without bound at $\theta=-1$" is part (2), which needs the premise that power scales stakes and shared loss together. A cited it without the premise; the core uses part (1)'s monotonicity in $r$, $\ell$ and $\theta$, with the $\ell$-derivative $c(n-1-\theta)>0$ checked.
6. **`participate` numerics.** The feasibility sums (0.36/0.90/1.44/1.71), the survival compression (0.872) and the minimum shares (0.925/0.700) are in `v4/checks_v45.log` K7. The interim shares are in `v5` K7. B is right, and V6.1 and A misattributed them.
7. **Margin monotonicity.** B's comparison proof was stated for two loads. For a general load distribution under replicator selection with constant $\Delta$, the distribution at $t$ is the exponential tilt $p_0(e)e^{-s\Delta et}/Z$, whose mean is decreasing in $s\Delta t$ (derivative $-\mathrm{Var}$). The proof now covers the theorem's premise.
8. **$\theta_0$ range in `share`(1).** At $\theta_0=-1$, $\theta_{\rm eff}\equiv-1$, so "strictly increasing" needs $\theta_0\in(-1,n-1)$ (B).

All figures in the three files were checked against `checks_v62.log` (HO, SH) and the legacy logs (`v4/checks_v45.log` N22, K7; `v5` K7).
