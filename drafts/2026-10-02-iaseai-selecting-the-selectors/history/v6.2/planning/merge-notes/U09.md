## 2. Disagreements resolved by derivation

1. **R5's exceptions (B: "(coverage lags) or (residual floor) fails"; A: only (residual floor)).**
   - Necessity of $\sum u_t<\infty$ uses (residual floor) alone. If the uncovered share is at least $\underline u_t$ on every surviving history, the hazard is at least $h\underline u_t$, and `nonsum` gives $\Pr(\Phi_H)\le\exp(-h\sum\underline u_t)\to0$.
   - (coverage lags) implies $u_t\to1$. It is therefore a condition under which R5 *fails*, not one under which R5 is unneeded.
   - (variety grows) only motivates (coverage lags) (§5.3; S5: "The growth of $Y_t$ is never used").
   - A is right. D2-fable §14 suggested that R5's list "keeps" (variety grows); by this derivation it does not. Its failure with coverage growing appears as "met, not excused" (`enough`).
2. **The R4 detection form: R4 (B) or R6's kind (A: "necessary for R4").**
   - `detectlink` is (14) with $p_d=1-\upsilon(T_{\rm dec})$, so the form belongs to R4.
   - R6 (independence of observation) is not necessary for timely detection: direct observation of actions is R6's own exception.
   - B's placement taken; R6 stays "sufficient".
3. **R4, Branch II: "met without design" (B) or "met, not excused" (A).**
   - Detection and response faster than breakout is a designed means (`restraint`(a); the sufficient-safeguards list), so Branch II is not "without design".
   - Branches III and I′ ($p_d=0$ by the world) are not needed.
   - A's classification taken.
4. **R4, $D_i\le v_i$ (B: "the condition is then weakest at $p_d=0$").**
   - (14) $\Leftrightarrow(1-\delta)g\le\delta(C-v)-\delta p_d(D-v)$. For $D\le v$ the right side is non-decreasing in $p_d$, so the condition is *hardest* at $p_d=0$, and ordinary repetition decides.
   - B's conclusion stands; its word "weakest" was inverted and is corrected.
5. **Gradual selection in R3a (B: "gradient selection whose variance grows with capacity tracks").**
   - `capdyn`(2)(b): capacity stays bounded wherever $\int dq/V_q$ diverges. That covers constant variance *and* variance proportional to $q$.
   - Only variance rising faster than capacity ($\propto q^2$) can track (2)(c).
   - A's "its variance not rising faster than capacity" is right and matches `corrrate`'s Scope; B's wording is not carried.
   - B's GA bound $q_0+V\chi\hat Yp^{2-\xi_v}/(d_0\alpha_{\rm cor})$ also carried a spurious factor ($\hat Y$ already contains $p^2$; `capdyn` gives $q(0)+V_q\chi\hat Y/(d_0\alpha_{\rm cor})$). Not carried.
6. **R8a's kind (A: "necessary [thm∣prem]" for a cell that includes "each type's sanction at least its gain").**
   - `nested`(3) and `blind`(3): the sanction condition is necessary within a type and sufficient, not necessary, across types. In one field with equal baselines, compliance takes the field iff the best-paid variant complies. CI m05(B): sanctions (1.0, 0.15) against costs (0.1, 0.2) give compliance; (1.0, 0.05) gives evasion.
   - The cell now says so in its kind column.
7. **`unravel` across types (A: "bistable … participation threshold $1-\min_kx_k^\dagger$" without the multi-type argument).**
   - Within a type the result is `blind`(3) with exit for evasion.
   - Across types with aggregate $x$: if $x<\min_kx_k^\dagger$ then $s_k(x)>c_k$ for every $k$ ($s_k$ decreasing), so every type's outside share falls. The minimum is thus the edge of the basin of full participation.
   - This requires $s_k(0)>c_k$ for every type. A type that leaves at every share makes full participation impossible.
   - The statement now owns both conditions.
8. **R1 tag (B [thm∣prem], A [thm]).** `load` is [thm], with (reach) as its antecedent, and `safeguards` is [thm]. Taken: [thm].
9. **R3a necessary-half tag (B [thm∣prem], A [thm]).** `corrrate`(2) is [thm] and states the tracking condition as an antecedent. Taken: [thm].
