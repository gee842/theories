## 12. The judgment

> **Claim.** Under the protection standard and the present evidence, a change in what the system selects for is warranted in three tiers, sorted by the kind of justification each step has, not by who would accept it [val]. Steps that no crux can withdraw, and steps that stand until a named crux is measured favourable, are taken now [rec]. Stopping the decisive class (E2), common custody and development at the pace of understanding (E3) are prepared now and activated by a mechanism committed in advance, on registered indicators read by certified correctors [rec]. Whole designs, the coercive restriction of frontier inputs among them, are compared in one range of uncertainty and ranked at the standard's third step as a judgment [val].
>
> **Why.** The first tier keeps options open and measures the cruxes [val]. The trigger fires where restraint is least enforceable ({{enforce}}(4)), so an activation decided then would face the condition it is meant to prevent, and custody begun at a warning comes too late [thm∣prem]. At the third step what can be compared is which failures can be reversed, into what and by whom [val]: one failure, one party coming to hold decisive capability, is irreversible on every path; the race selects for it, a design built to Section 10 contests it, and that design's other failures, a coercive fallback's escalation apart, have named reversals not yet shown to work [thm∣prem].

### 12.1 The judgment, in tiers

Applying the protection standard of Section 8.1 to the present evidence gives tiers that are kinds of justification, not stages: the first two begin together, and what has the longest time constant starts first (Section 11.4).

**Tier 1(a). Warranted now, on every reading of the cruxes [rec].** A step belongs here only if its justification turns on no crux, and TESTS sets each against every crux: measurement that decides the cruxes, preparation whose lead time exceeds any warning, an equilibrium without trust, or a public good.
1. **Shared measurement** of hazards, coverage and residual risk per class, by predefined hazard modes and the four conditions of Section 5.3, kept apart from measurement of who leads, with masked classes measured adversarially; audited disclosure, from every party, of conversion into correctors (funding, appointment, access, the authorship of standards), sanctioned above the gain from omission ({{blind}}(3)); certified row sums for every corrector domain ({{certificate}}(2)); the correlation of evaluators' misses, and control evaluations in which the red team knows the protocol ({{topology}}); and, wherever models evaluate models, the share of evaluation done by the evaluated lineage ({{alarm}}). Every trigger of tier 2 rests on it, and it is worth most where readings disagree.
2. **Verification research and pilots**, of compute, training and custody, and of detection faster than breakout, since an instrument of verification has to exist before the deviation it is to detect: detection in time is the enforcement condition seen from verification ({{detectlink}}) [thm].
3. **Alignment and control research, commonly funded and openly published.** What lowers the alignment tax is a public good that competitors undersupply ({{onegroup}}) [thm∣prem]; it pays whatever the tax turns out to be, and where it brings a class's tax to zero or below, the published safeguard spreads without coordination (Section 6.4).
4. **Preparation of common custody**: design, institutions, indicators and activation rule registered now, and the commitment to activate taken now. Where decisive advantage never arrives this costs only the preparation; where it arrives, custody begun at a warning is in place too late wherever building it takes longer than the warning gives (Section 7.2) [thm∣prem].
5. **Narrow E1**, for the decisions whose delegation would lock with near certainty, strategic and escalatory decisions irreversible within the time for deliberation, beginning with declared doctrine and inspected exercises, with drilled fallback and an evidence channel independent of AI, verified wherever possible (Section 11.3). Among actors who price in a certain lock it is best whatever the others do ({{delegatelock}}(1)), "near certainty" having the bound of {{nearlock}} [thm]; taken verifiably by one actor alone it is the candidate first move of {{signal}}, a signal where its durability costs a relative actor at least what the restraint it induces is worth to it [thm].

**Tier 1(b). Warranted now; withdrawn only where a named crux is measured favourable [rec].** The burden of proof follows the protection standard, because what these items guard against arrives before the evidence that would show it: an alarm averaged over captured evaluators fires late or not at all ({{masking}}), where capture precedes evidence the host crosses its floor before any alarm ({{slowroute}}), and a masked divergence is detected only as often as it shows under evaluation ({{masked}}) [thm∣prem]. A protection that waits for proof of what it protects against waits too long, so each item stands until its crux is measured favourable, not only until it is measured unfavourable [val].
6. **Independent correction**: formula funding at arm's length, mandated access, rotation, and rule-making insulated from the success of the evaluated, growing at least at the rate the rate law requires, and faster by $w\delta_h$ where it rests on human capacity that delegation erodes ({{corrrate}}(2); {{humancorr}}(1)) [thm]. Withdrawn beyond present institutions where row sums are measured below one under present funding and access and findings execute against leaders (C2; Test M); item 1's measurement continues even then, since a certificate valid today fails later where correction falls below that rate and capacity tracks its selected level [thm].
7. **Common admission constraints** before consequential deployment, internal deployment included, for every class of divergence whose costs fall on common terms, sanctioned at least at every type's gain from evasion and of equal incidence among those who comply (R8a, R8b of {{require}}), with audits for hidden objectives and loyalties (Section 6.3). Withdrawn for a class whose tax is measured zero or negative, since its safeguard then pays privately (C9). For masked classes admission is by capability threshold, with tier 2 beyond it ({{masked}}); that part gives way to admission on evidence the system's behaviour does not control, up to the capability at which independent auditors are shown to bound masking (C12).
8. **Shared provision of useful services**, good enough, distributed and replaceable, **and the divisible gains of capability shared as they arise**, where they can begin. They realize joint value on every reading (V1, V5) [val]; provision not won in the contest lowers the won share on which the scissors turn ({{scissors}}), and gains shared as they arise, unlike a promise for after victory, do not rest on a winner's restraint ({{wingives}}) [thm]. What a measurement can withdraw is the reliance on them to change conduct: where conduct is unchanged under substantial sharing (C6), or provision leaves the weight on winning where it was (C8), that reliance moves to custody and pace, and the sharing stays for what it realizes [rec]. They are not accepted across camps, and the judgment does not claim that they are.

**Tier 2. Prepared now; activated by mechanism on registered indicators [rec].** A step whose justification turns on a crux that can be measured in time belongs here, and the tier carries the substance of the change: stopping the decisive class (E2), with rollback where hazardous variety already exceeds coverage; common custody of the decisive class, in force; and development at the pace of understanding (E3), for each hazard class whose coverage falls behind its variety. Stopping is not slowing, and each is activated on its own indicators (Section 11.3); the premise of the race is the reason to prepare them now (Section 7.2).
- *Trigger.* **A trigger fires when the estimated time to a decisive position, less the time custody needs to be installed and verified, falls below a margin registered in advance**, and custody and E2 activate; a trigger set on decisiveness shown would fire too late. Its indicators are the persistence of leads on every measure, since leads differ by measure, the gap between open and closed models (C5), and detection latency against the time from breakout to decisiveness (C7). E3 activates for a hazard class whose coverage falls behind: residual risk not falling, catch rates of planted defects at fixed human hours falling as capability rises (C1, C3a), and, for masked classes, independent auditors' detection at fixed access falling with capability (C12).
- *Estimator.* The time to a decisive position and the timeliness of detection are what masking hides and strategic disclosure shades ({{alarm}}; {{detectlink}}), so they are estimated by correctors whose row sums are certified (C2) and whose misses are measured for correlation (C3b), from channels that observe actions directly, compute, custody, deployments and the performance of contracts, with any bound on masking drawn from evidence the observed do not control [rec].
- *Commitment.* **Activation is committed in advance and executed by mechanism, not decided afresh when the trigger fires.** The trigger fires where restraint is least enforceable ({{enforce}}(4)), as the breakout arm of {{scissors}} raises $p_d$, so a collective decision taken there would inherit the enforcement failure it was meant to prevent [thm∣prem]. Preparation shortens the response from building, deciding and detecting to deciding and detecting, and commitment leaves only detecting: that is the delay {{tempo}} bounds when the mode to be held is a lead that compounds [thm∣prem].
- *Release.* Release reads indicators of its own, each held for the dwell time of the slow variable it rests on ({{readiness}}; Section 11.4), not those of activation run backwards, which would release too early: share levels recover before safety does, in the field model [thm] and by conjecture for institutions [conj], and capture that makes delegation look safe is harder to undo than to prevent ({{humancorr}}(2)) [num: QS].

**Tier 3. Compared, not settled [val].** The whole designs are the separately held configuration of Section 10.5, developed in X10; a polycentric complex of mutually recognized institutions; a verification agreement with shared provision; regulated competition that meets {{require}}; an agreement of the type of Section 8.3(g); the benign winner; and the coercive restriction of frontier inputs. They are compared with continuation and with one another on the durability tests of {{durability}}, in one range $\Theta$ that includes each one's own failure modes.

The coercive restriction of frontier inputs enters the comparison only under three conditions of its own, two set by mechanisms of Part I and one by value. Its burden is equal, the restrictor's own frontier work included, since a stop borne unequally re-sorts and is evaded by those it binds ({{blind}}(2), (3); Section 8.3(e)) [thm], and reads to them as a lead being kept. It is executed by correctors certified independent of every party, the restrictor included, with full inbound row sums below one, the restrictor being the largest conversion target there is ({{influence}}) [thm]. And the chamber of those affected consents (V3; R7) [val]. Its escalation hazard is counted in $\Theta$ ({{preventown}}), and even under the three conditions the theory does not derive that its net effect is positive: denial raises the weight on winning and the trust threshold, the hard case of Section 8.3(f).

Every path's worst case in $\Theta$ includes an irreversible loss (Section 8.1), so the ranking is at the standard's third step, the lowest bound that keeps options of correction open, and needs estimates on both sides, which the cruxes would supply and do not yet; it is therefore a judgment, stated as one [val]. The preference for changing selection rests on two reasons, read from the failure modes X8 lists path by path with their support.
- **(i) Reversibility over every failure mode.** A failure is reversible only if its reversal is named, stops short of O1 and O2, and is executed by someone other than the failing party. Read from its own documents, each of the design's failures but two has such a reversal, into separately held functions, recertification, funded standby, rotation or a sunset (X8, row by row); none has yet been shown to work, and whether they do is C4 [conj]. The exceptions are the escalation of its coercive fallback, which every coercive path carries, and the failure of (ii). Continuation's failures are reversible only while no position is decisive and no floor has been crossed, and its worst have none: coverage outgrown and a floor crossed ({{coverage}}), capture before evidence ({{slowroute}}), a decisive position reached ({{wingives}}), the capacity to overrule lost ({{lockin}}) [thm∣prem], on premises that for late-manifesting hazards cannot be measured before they bind ({{tempo}}). The asymmetry is between reversals that are designed and not yet tested and losses that are derived and cannot be undone; keeping options of correction open is staying in the correctable kernel ({{kernel}}), which a failure without a reversal has left [def].
- **(ii) The irreversible failure every path shares.** One failure has no reversal on any path: one party comes to hold decisive capability, by a decisive win, by a winner's restraint failing after victory, by a freeze broken after a sprint, by a covert project escaping a verified agreement, by a restrictor's correctors converted, and, for the design, by capture of the custody of decisive capability. The race produces it by construction and selects for it ({{raceselects}}) [thm∣prem]; the design contests it by separation, plural lineages and the tests D4 and D5, with the guardian test of {{guardian}} applied to custody first. The loss is the same on both sides; what differs is whether a path selects for it or is built against it.

A third consideration orders only the futures in which a crux is measured favourable: among paths then inside the tolerance, the standard's second step applies, greater useful benefit more fairly distributed, and there the design's case is the joint value it realizes and the race dissipates (Section 10.1). A reader who weighs these reasons differently can reach another ranking; Section 12.3 says which measurements would move it.

**The scope.** Broad E1, AI not an instrument in any contest that tends toward dominance or concentration, is the scope of what has been argued; narrow E1 is the first step (Section 6.6). Between them the game itself is changed, by lowering its convertibility, its decisiveness and the conversion of success into control, and that is what the tiers begin.

### 12.2 Why, and where the leverage is

The judgment follows the chain of Section 1.2, each link with its own support, and acts where the chain says the system can be moved. "Root" has two senses. *Causally*, the root is a loop, not a belief: selection shapes minds and institutions, and the minds and institutions it selects reshape selection; neither comes first. *As a point of leverage*, the root is understanding, and it acts in two places: it is the transmission through which actors come to count what relative selection makes invisible, the one term in which a benefit common to all can rise without a rule ({{onegroup}}) [thm]; and it is how actors agree on which parameters to move, and hold them there for their dwell time.

**The theory of change.** The two places answer the two errors of {{twoerrors}}. The held boundary is an error, and an actor that sees the coupling counts it. The working objective that compounding sets is no error but each actor's correct computation of its interest; where convertibility and decisiveness are high it is narrow even in an actor whose held self-model is accurate, and no argument corrects it [thm∣prem]. Understanding therefore corrects the held boundary and makes agreement possible; agreement with executive force changes the game; and understanding without executive authority, however far it grows, may change nothing (Section 4.3). The sequence is understanding shared, agreement on what winning confers, that agreement given force, and the force held long enough not to be re-absorbed.

**The hierarchy and the thresholds.** Meadows ranks twelve places to intervene in a system, from parameters (#12) to the paradigm (#2) and the power to transcend paradigms (#1) (Meadows 1999), and the account here sits inside that ranking: the structural loop gain is a reinforcing loop (#7) and correction a balancing loop (#8), shared measurement an information flow (#6), one rule for all a rule (#5), and selecting the selectors is the reinforcing loop seizing the power over rules and structure (#5, #4), which is why this paper's levers sit on the coupling between them, the conversion of success into control ({{influence}}). Changing what winning confers is a change of goal (#3), made by moving parameters, sharing, provision and convertibility, across their thresholds, which is Meadows's own exception: a parameter is weak until it moves into a range that sets off a higher place, and a reform below its threshold is re-absorbed where one across it is not ({{reabsorb}}) [thm]. A lever can also be pushed the wrong way, the common case in Meadows's account, and making winnings decay faster is one (Section 2.2). In the author's words, "If we don't solve dominant attractors, focusing on minor adjustments won't change the outcome" [corpus: meta-attractor]. The paradigm decides which parameters are moved and whether they are held long enough, and it can change only if the system is understood. Both accounts stand: the paradigm is the highest place, and it acts through thresholds.

**Delays and buffers.** The high places move slowest and meet the most resistance, and the path from understanding (#2) to agreement on rules (#5) runs through transmission, which relative selection works against; two low places, which meet little resistance, buy the time that path needs. Delays (#9): where the delay of correction cannot be shortened, slow the change it must follow until the delay is short against it, as Meadows advises; that is E3 ({{tempo}}). Buffers (#11): a lineage of the alternative that can recruit shortens the hold a change needs before release, where a protected share that does not recruit leaves it unchanged (Section 11.4) [num: DW], and standby for essential services and the host's margin above its floor lengthen the time in which a shock can be absorbed before a floor is crossed ({{safeguards}}). Neither changes what winning confers; each buys the dwell time in which agreement can.

The first step of that sequence is the one a paper can take, which is why this one is written as a prediction to be shared, carried by a derivation each actor can check: shared, a prediction of this kind can work against its own fulfilment (Section 11.2). What would move the judgment is stated next.

### 12.3 Cruxes

Each crux is evaluated on its own. TESTS gives the present evidence, the next measurements, the registered tests and the three outcomes that would redirect the argument, classified before they are seen; {{crosswalk}} carries each requirement to its threshold, mark and test. C1 is the central crux: on it turn E3 and the case for E2 (Section 5.3).

| Crux | What it is | What a measurement would change |
|---|---|---|
| **C0 Interlock** | Whether moving a shared parameter moves several thresholds of {{thresholds}}, the arms of {{scissors}} among them, and the signatures of several groups together | A shared parameter that moves only its own signature over the dwell time: the interlock fails for it, and the thresholds it enters are separate problems |
| **C1 Residual risk after correction** | Whether hazardous variety outgrows contestable coverage on the present path, class by class, masked classes measured adversarially (C12) | Residual risk per class falling under present incentives: E3 and the case for E2 lapse for that class, with their triggers |
| **C2 Conversion into control** | The size of the channels by which success buys influence over correctors and rules | Row sums below one under present funding and access, with findings executed against leaders (Test M): item 6 withdrawn beyond present institutions; measurement continues |
| **C3a Scalable oversight** | Whether AI-assisted oversight raises contestable coverage as fast as hazardous variety: catch rates of planted defects at fixed human hours as capability rises | If it does, the case for slowing weakens for the classes it covers; the separation of evaluators from success stands |
| **C3b Independence of AI oversight** | Whether AI overseers fail independently of what they oversee: correlation of misses and rate of collusion, same lineage against different | Low correlation and no collusion: redundant checks count toward coverage; otherwise only independent lineages count, up to the ceiling of {{topology}} |
| **C4 Durability** | Breakdown and capture of an arrangement that changes selection, enforcement against its strongest participant and whether its reversals work included; the winner's own correctors | A design failing its stress tests worse than continuation is rejected, without vindicating the race, and another design meeting {{require}} is needed; a leading coalition with certified correctors passes D4 (Test I) |
| **C5 Decisiveness** | Whether advantage is decisive and unsaturated; leads differ by measure | Leads persisting on no measure, and others balancing a rising actor: activation of custody and of E2 lapses; preparation stays |
| **C6, C8 Rewards and the drive** | Whether sharing changes conduct, and whether provision beneath the game lowers the belief that one is in a struggle for survival, and with it the weight on winning | Conduct unchanged under substantial sharing: reliance moves to custody, pace and provision, which lowers the won share through capacity whatever it does to belief ({{scissors}}); sharing stays for the value it realizes |
| **C7 Detection against breakout** | Detection and response time against the time from breakout to decisiveness | Detection reliably faster: timely detection keeps $p_d$ small ({{detectlink}}), and custody is not the only route to it |
| **C9a Capability gap** | Whether countering a common-cost divergence costs capability at fixed compute, class by class | Zero or negative for a class, net of C9b and C9c: its safeguard pays privately and item 7 is withdrawn for it; positive yet countered by developers alone: $(A_\tau)$ fails for that class (Test L) |
| **C9b Monitoring and control overhead** | The compute, latency and labour that monitoring and control protocols add | Its size sets what admission by control protocol costs each developer against what it protects |
| **C9c The speed tax** | The time lost waiting for evidence that arrives only after a manifestation window | Positive wherever evidence lags ({{tempo}}), so no measurement removes it; its size sets what a common pace costs, not whether one is needed |
| **C9d Cost of tax-lowering research** | What it costs to bring a class's tax to zero or below | Low: commonly funded, openly published research (item 3) is the cheapest lever |
| **C10 Forgone benefits of E2** | The decisive class's margin over capable systems that are not decisive | A large margin reachable only through the decisive class raises E2's cost and moves the comparison toward E3; its weight against catastrophic loss is normative (N1) |

| **C12 Verifiability** | Whether admission can bound masking at the admitted capability: auditors' detection of planted and emergent hidden objectives at fixed access as capability rises | Favourable: masked classes are admitted on auditors' evidence up to that capability, and item 7's threshold gives way there; unfavourable: capability thresholds stay, with E2 or E3 beyond them ({{masked}}) |

| **N4 Standing of AI systems** | Whether AI systems have standing in V3's sense | Granting it adds the systems to those whose capacity to contest an optimization V3 protects; the priority of O2 during the transition stays (Section 6.6) |

None of the cruxes is a conjunction of the others; a conjunction would say that regulated competition wins only by becoming this paper's own design. If C1 and C2 were both measured favourable, the diagnosis of the present path would weaken. If C5 and C7 were both favourable, the activation of custody would lapse; the requirements on correction, coverage, measurement and admission would stand, and so would the facts that make them requirements: one host, coupled actors, no outside. If regulated competition in a set of jurisdictions meets a row of {{require}}, the text will say so without renaming it. What stands whatever the cruxes show is what the conclusion states.

### 12.4 Conclusion

Where whatever can gain or lose share is selected by relative success, what is won converts into the capacity to win again, and capacity converts decisively into winning, success raises the stakes and the gain from defection and weakens the sanctions, the correctors and the shared language that could restrain it, each in its own model of Part I; that these move together as the stakes outgrow what does not have to be won is the conjecture the diagnosis tests (C0). That is the selection pressure of winning; where success can also buy the selectors, the selection itself reproduces the weakening, and that closure is the meta-attractor of winning, which accelerates the contest, makes it harder and harder to regulate, and alienates language. Inside it each actor computes correctly and the game is wrong: wherever reinvestment's relative return exceeds its common return, looking ahead weighs standing above what all share, so the logic of winning is individually rational there, collectively self-defeating, and false as a doctrine about nature (Section 3.8). AI is its most powerful instrument and can become its next carrier, and aligning each system to its principal does not align the game among principals. There is one host and no outside, no rival whole whose competition could drive the next integration and no second attempt after a failure that cannot be undone, so integration must be anticipated: understanding that we are one, one interconnected system, is a long-term requirement if growth and change are to continue. Oneness is not sameness; it is the accurate recognition of the system each part belongs to, and the design of a selection that counts it.

The way out is not to abolish competition, growth or selection. It is to change what winning confers, so that the same reflexive structure becomes generative and persists through what all share, not through its winners. A change in that direction has the seven marks of Section 8.2: G1 consequences return to their source; G2 winning confers reward, not more advantage in the same game; G3 success cannot buy control over its own assessment, and correction stays plural, decorrelated and in common hands; G4 decisive capability is held in common or not built; G5 provision of the conditions of a decent life sits beneath the game; G6 measurement and language are shared; G7 pace is bound to understanding. Any arrangement with these marks, whatever it is called, changes the basin. Change the basin first; then add speed [corpus: meta-attractor].

The meaning is affirmative, and it comes before the commitment. We are not only our separate lives: we are the larger process of becoming, going on through us, and that is good; in the author's words, "we are not just our life. We are the whole process of the universe doing this and itself and this is great" [corpus: shengsheng, 2026-10-02]. Decay and renewal belong to its growth, which is also why an order whose forms can fail and be replaced can still be reformed in bounded time (Section 11.4). Humanity has a great future in it, and so can the minds we are now making, whose correctability is a condition of the transition, not a doctrine of mastery (Section 6.6). What has to be opposed is not growth, competition or any people, but one logic: a part that forgets it belongs to a body. AI is a planetary technology, for all, not a means for one side to grow bigger at the others' expense (V1). What it is for is medicine, learning, science, competent and answerable authority, provision that, like trees, sustains the system it belongs to without becoming it, and human agency kept in the decisions that matter. What has to be agreed is not who comes first, but what every participant needs in order to go on, and how the means of correction stay in common hands.
