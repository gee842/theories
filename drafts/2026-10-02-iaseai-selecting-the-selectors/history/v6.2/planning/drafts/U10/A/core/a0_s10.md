## 10. The generative alternative

> **Claim.** The same reflexive structure that lets success buy control over future selection can make contribution fund the conditions of further contribution [def]. The way out of an optimization trap is not a better optimization of the same objective, and not the abolition of selection, competition or growth: it is a change in what winning confers, so that the loop strengthens the shared host and independent correction instead of consuming them [thm, lever by lever]. Where coverage is complete and correction is financed by formula, the generative order persists through what all share, not through its winners [thm∣prem]. Whether such an order attracts is a conjecture, and its persistence has to be designed and measured [conj].
>
> **Why.** Sharing and diffusion scale every channel of the loop gain down; provision that does not have to be won lowers the share of capacity that must be won, which is the pivot on which the scissors turn; cutting the conversion of success into control over its own assessment removes the reflexive channels, and correction lowers the gain of rule support. The positional part of the prize cannot be divided: it can only be kept from forming or held in common. Support that no carrier can claim is a term common to all, which relative selection cancels, so the order is not selected among carriers; what keeps it is coverage, financing fixed in formula, and a loop that runs through the whole.

### 10.1 The purpose

Taken out of the contest, capability stops being spent on standing. In the contest of Section 3.4 the share of the stakes consumed in private cost and shared loss rises with decisiveness and with the narrowness of the working objective, without bound as the objective approaches pure relative concern ({{dissipate}}) [thm]. What the race dissipates becomes available for need: medicine and public health, education, science, competent administration, infrastructure, and the long-horizon work that fragmented incentives under-supply because its value is common ({{onegroup}}). The joint value is large; what the selection decides is whether it is realized or burnt.

**Authority as a productive capacity (V4).** Authority can coordinate investment, guarantee services, support learning and act on shared purposes. The ideal the theory draws on justifies authority by its responsibility for improving people's lives and the historical trajectory; it enters the design as a purpose to be sustained, not as a premise that it is realized, and how institutions select officeholders, hear unwelcome evidence and revise harmful practices are causal variables. A larger causal radius brings larger responsibility, not larger entitlement: "where power reaches, responsibility follows" (力之所及，责之所在) [corpus: relational-self; shengsheng, 2026-07-14 第十八章]. With the norm of a shared future (Section 3.7), this is the institutional consequence of the relational self.

**Provision that does not become the system.** Taken out of the contest, AI can be what infrastructure is: provision of the conditions of a decent life, built to meet need rather than to gain standing. Trees are the image [corpus: shengsheng], and {{compound}} gives their formal content. They give shelter and fruit, maintain the system they belong to, and do not become it. Such provision is *distributed*, many providers below the size at which influence over correctors pays ({{influence}}); *good enough*, stopping growth once a need is met, which keeps it within correction and confers no decisive advantage ({{enough}}); *replaceable*, keeping human capacity above its lock-in floor ({{lockin}}); and *for everyone*, not for a class of guardians. Productive contribution is rewarded with revenue, contracts and recognition; what success does not buy is the right to appoint evaluators, set thresholds, raise the cost of entry against challengers or amend the governing rules (V5). The question the theory leaves open is the author's: "what is open is who builds and funds such provision, not who holds it" [corpus: meta-attractor; Ren, 2026-10-02]. Section 10.5 and Discussion X10 develop one answer, provision financed by formula and built by many providers under a cap on any one's share, as a candidate and not as the conclusion.

### 10.2 What winning confers: sharing, convertibility and provision beneath the game

Section 3.4 split the prize into a divisible part $\Omega_e$, rents, revenues, services and the material gains of capability, and a positional part $\Omega_s$, decisive standing, the power to set the rules and the loss of being subordinated, and gave the positional coupling its sign, $\kappa_s\le0$ ({{eq:welfare}}). The split decides what each lever can reach. Where the value of capability is common and concave, private incentives under-supply speed rather than over-supply it ({{commonfront}}); where a decisive prize remains, a common frontier in which cooperators pool effort and divide its value without subordinating one another turns a dilemma into a coordination problem while the value of winning is moderate, and when it is large, keeping ahead of defectors is the race moved up a level ({{commonfrontn}}). Allocation within a joint project is itself a contest, and the project, as the largest carrier, has the largest return to influence over its correctors.

**{{!Proposition:share}} (What sharing, pooling and convertibility change) [thm].** Sharing and provision can stabilize equal standing; none of the levers makes the selected intensity zero while a positional prize remains. *In the contest of Section 3.4, with $n$ actors, decisiveness $r\le1$, unit cost $c$ and prize $\Omega=\Omega_e+\Omega_s$:*
1. *Pooling. An actor that receives $1-\beta$ of its own material payoff plus $\beta$ times the average, and holds a self-model $\theta_0>-1$, acts as if its self-model were $\theta_{\rm eff}(\beta)$, strictly increasing in $\beta$ toward $n-1$. At $\theta_0=-1$, and under relative selection, pooling only scales payoff differences by $1-\beta$ and changes no choice.*
2. *Sharing. Sharing a fraction $\beta$ of the divisible prize, with the promise kept with probability $\varrho$, is the contest with prize $\Omega_{\rm eff}=(1-\beta\varrho)\Omega_e+\Omega_s$ when the shared part is divided among all $n$, the winner included, and with prize $\Omega_{\rm eff}=\big(1-\beta\varrho\,n/(n-1)\big)\Omega_e+\Omega_s$ when it goes to the $n-1$ others, the convention of {{twochan}}(4) and {{wingives}}. Intensity, dissipation and race hazard are proportional to $\Omega_{\rm eff}$. Under relative selection in one field the uninvadable intensity is $r\Omega_{\rm eff}/(nc)$: positive for every $\beta\varrho<1$ under the first convention and every $\beta\varrho<(n-1)/n$ under the second; with $\Omega_s>0$ it is positive for every $\beta$ under the first convention, and under the second once $\Omega_s>\Omega_e/(n-1)$.*
3. *Convertibility and provision. Equal standing in capacity is unstable iff $\kappa_c\Omega\big((1-\beta)r-1\big)>nk_0+\delta_fS^\ast$ ({{compound}}(1)). Without provision not won in the contest and without diffusion, the convertible share $\kappa_c$ sets only the level of capacity, so making winnings non-convertible stabilizes standing only together with such provision or diffusion. If success cannot convert into control over correction, $\chi=0$, the reflexive channels of the loop gain vanish, the return to influence over correctors is zero ({{influence}}), and the required correction rate falls to its value without second-order selection ({{corrrate}}(1)).*

*Scope.* Selection chooses the intensities in (2); strategic actors choosing intensities as best responses follow the same two effective prizes with the Nash intensity $r\Omega_{\rm eff}(n-1)/(n^2c)$ [num: SH]. A promise kept with $\varrho<1$ enters only through $\Omega_{\rm eff}$ here; a promise for after victory also raises the temptation to deviate ({{enforce}}), so sharing works only if credible: contemporaneous, escrowed or under shared control before decisiveness. *Proof:* S10 [num: SH: with a positional part as large as the divisible one, sharing all of the divisible prize halves the uninvadable intensity from 0.5 to 0.25].

**Three inflows and one cut.** The levers are the three inflows not tied to rank of {{compound}}(2), and one cut, and Section 2.2 gives the rule by which each acts channel by channel ({{chansign}}). *Pooling* widens the working self of anyone who values its absolute payoff and does nothing under relative selection, so it is a lever on persons and firms and not on the selection among them. *Sharing* scales every channel of the loop gain by $1-\beta$, and *diffusion* lowers the gain in every channel; neither changes the sign of the selected intensity while a positional part remains. *Provision not won in the contest* lowers the won share $\omega$, and with it the gain through capacity, and adds the same to everyone in a level channel, so provision is the lever for capacity and not for rule support. *The cut*, $\chi\to0$, removes the reflexive channels outright; where it cannot be complete, correction lowers the gain of rule support, the level channel, which is where correction bites. **The positional part cannot be divided.** Toward it, relative concern is accurate ({{boundary}}(2)), so no widening of the self and no sharing of the divisible prize reaches it, and $\Omega_s$ enters $\Omega_{\rm eff}$ whole at every $\beta$. Lowering decisiveness does not remove it, since a lottery can award a position beyond sanction ({{wingives}}). It can only be kept from forming, by verified limits on what any winner may do, or held in common custody ({{restraint}}(a)). What a design must limit is what winning confers.

**What should diffuse.** Since diffusion lowers the gain in every channel and draws a laggard toward the mean ({{compound}}(2)), openness is a lever and not a concession, and measures that block diffusion to keep a lead raise the gain they mean to exploit. What should diffuse is knowledge, useful services and provision: the inflows not tied to rank. What should not is capability of the decisive class, and hazardous variety beyond contestable coverage. Diffusing the first gives more actors a deviation that can become decisive before it is answered, and each such deviation must satisfy {{eq:enforceeq}} on its own ({{enforce}}); diffusing the second raises the uncovered hazardous share that survival requires to stay summable ({{coverage}}). The boundary of openness is drawn by two thresholds of {{thresholds}}, enforcement and coverage, and not by who leads.

**{{!Remark:twoorders}} (Concentration at two orders).** At the first order more contenders make the race fiercer ({{join}}(4)) and fragmentation raises speed and conditional risk [obs: EV11]; at the second, cultivated influence over correctors rises with a high moment of shares ({{corrrate}}(4)), which calls for removing common control. The design that serves both merges the frontier and disperses and insulates correction, keeping several lineages inside the merged frontier against common-mode failure ({{topology}}). Neither "centralize" nor "decentralize" is the answer, because the two orders call for opposite forms.

### 10.3 Safeguards, verification and participation

Four safeguards make consequences return on the host of Section 3: **(I1)** a liability above the private advantage of load, in advance for anticipated harms and on the alarm for others, so that effective liability exceeds the advantage once rule-making is uncaptured (for harms beyond assets, regulation in advance; near a threshold, a quantity rather than a price); **(I2)** independent correctors in evaluation and rule-making, with $d(t)$ above $d^\ast(t)$ by a margin; **(I3)** independent evidence, by masking kept below threshold or a conservative alarm on an independently established bound ({{alarm}}); and **(I4)** all three in force before the corrector domains are captured.

**{{!Theorem:safeguards}} (Sufficient safeguards, and a necessary condition) [thm].** Four safeguards in force before capture return consequences to their source and keep the host above its floor, provided the liability carries a margin; without the margin a forgiving host dips below its floor and recovers, and a host that degrades by itself below a threshold does not. *Let selection be replicator dynamics among carriers differing only in load $e\in[e_{\min},e_{\max}]$, with a carrier of load $e_{\min}$ present, on the host {{eq:host}} with regeneration $\rho$, load coefficient $h_V$ and floor $V_{\min}$.*
1. *Sufficiency. Under (I1)–(I4), effective liability exceeds the advantage after a finite time and the load falls to $e_{\min}$; if $h_Ve_{\min}<\rho(1-V_{\min})$, the long-run level $1-h_Ve_{\min}/\rho$ exceeds the floor. The trajectory stays above the floor throughout iff the liability margin $\Delta=\tau_{\rm eff}-\nu$ is at least a minimum $\Delta_{\min}$, set by the host, the floor, the initial composition and the speed of selection. Below that margin the host of {{eq:host}} dips below its floor and recovers. A host whose regeneration is lower at every level and which degrades by itself below a threshold $V_u$ does not recover: its margin must keep the transient above $V_u$, and its floor must be set at or above $V_u$ for crossing the floor to be the first loss.*
2. *Non-redundancy. For each of (I1)–(I3) there are parameters satisfying the others under which the host crosses its floor, and parameters under which they come into force only after capture and the host crosses it.*
3. *A necessary condition. Under (reach), with a race carrier present, every trajectory viable at every horizon has a cumulative advantage $B(t)=\int_0^t(\nu-\tau_{\rm eff})$ that does not tend to $+\infty$. In particular the upper limit of the time average of effective liability is at least the private advantage, a condition that is necessary and not sufficient.*

*Scope.* The transient is real. With liability in force from the start at a margin $\Delta=0.08$ and the race strategy holding 99% of activity, the host dips to 0.14 before recovering; $\Delta_{\min}$ is 0.108, 0.165 and 0.355 for floors of 0.2, 0.3 and 0.5, and 0.125, 0.192 and 0.414 where the minimum load is 0.1, three independent integrators agreeing to 0.002; on the harsher host $\Delta_{\min}$ rises from 0.26 to 0.51 as the host's own threshold rises from 0.25 to 0.48, and a shortfall there is permanent; and a liability whose time average tends to $\nu$ from below crosses the floor while one equal to $\nu$ does not [num: HO]. These margins are an instance of the boundary of viable parameters for one fixed strategy; the correctable kernel of {{kernel}} takes a union over strategies and an intersection over disturbances, and is not computed here. Enactment is not force: liability enacted while rule-making is captured acts as $\tau(1-x_{\rm rule})$ ({{nounilateral}}), which is what (I4) is for. *Proof:* S10.

Two further results are stated in full in S10, each with its conditions. *Rival verification* ({{rivalverify}}): a rival's verifier whose full inbound row sum is below one, counting conversion from the verified party, from its own side and from shared sources such as models, supply chains and professional paradigms, is captured by none of them; rivalry keeps the verified party's contribution near zero and does not by itself bound the rest of the row, and because an accusation under rivalry can trigger prevention, joint resolution precedes any response. *Participation* ({{participate}}): under complete information an arrangement is efficient for a pair of types iff its survival-weighted joint value exceeds the sum of their survival-weighted race payoffs, and an overwhelmingly strong actor takes part only for a share near its race payoff; under private types an arrangement without verification is feasible iff the joint value covers the sum of the types' best race payoffs, which fails when strong types are rare although cooperation is efficient for every pair, and verification that reveals types removes the information rents.

Participation benefits, voting rights and protection against unilateral domination are three different things, and a design keeps them apart. Participation can be made individually rational through shares of value and access to services; voting rights need not follow capability; and protection against domination is fixed first and not traded for participation. If, for some set of actors, participation and the independence of correction cannot both be had, the design is infeasible for them, and should say so rather than buy participation with control over correction.

### 10.4 The generative loop and its closure

The author states two loops with the same reflexive structure and opposite signs [corpus: shengsheng, 2026-07-14 第十三章, 第十四章]. In the *generative loop*, security makes trust possible, trust makes cooperation possible, cooperation produces abundance, abundance allows sufficiency and slack, and these allow wider care and longer foresight, which produce better order and more security (安全→信任→合作→富饶→满足与余裕→更广之仁、更远之智→更好之序与安全). In institutions it takes the forms of shared capability, distributed agency, plural observation and critique, maintenance and repair, and the renewal of commons. In the *degenerative loop*, fear produces defence, defence struggle, struggle seizure and concentration, these scarcity and resentment; trust collapses, and fear deepens (恐惧→防御→斗争→掠夺与集中→稀缺与不满→信任之崩→更深之恐惧). The first makes more agents generators; the second makes them defenders, instruments or exiles. The criterion is generative or degenerative, not cooperative or competitive: cooperation on the wrong thing can be worse than competition on it, and a cartel is cooperation. Bounded competition in products, science and service continues inside a generative order (V5); what is removed is competition for control over the shared conditions and over the correctors. The generative loop has five strands. Four are the duals of the loops of Section 7.1, and the third is the dual of the drive behind them; each has a carrier, a condition and a test. Goodwill is not a carrier.

| Strand | Dual of (Section 7.1) | Carrier | Condition | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reasoning instead of winning** (讲理 rather than 讲赢) [corpus: alignment-selection; jian-and-wenyan] | The naturalized narrative, and the alienation of language that spreads it | Derivations from public premises; verifiable evidence; protocols agreed in advance; costly signals | Objectives not opposed on the topic of talk: effective bias below one quarter ({{talk}}) | Informativeness between rivals rises as contested stakes fall |
| **Reward separated from advantage** | Compounding and conversion | Separation of reward from authority over correction; contemporaneous sharing; a cap on any provider's share of essential services | Structural loop gain below one with margin, evaluated at the present distribution of standing, where the reflexive channel's gain scales with a high moment of shares ({{corrrate}}(4)); $\chi\to0$ | Conduct changes in proportion to what is shared (C6) |
| **Provision beneath the game** | The drive: the belief in a struggle for survival, and the fear of falling behind | Shared provision, good enough, distributed, replaceable: the $k_0$ of the loop gain | Not tied to standing; no inferiority in receiving it; grows with the stakes, so that the won share $\omega$ does not rise ({{scissors}}) | The weight on relative position falls where provision is secure (C8); the response of conduct to shared harm relative to private cost, $1+\theta$, rises with provision relative to the stakes (Test Γ) |
| **Correction in common hands, plural and decorrelated** | Capture of the rules, adopt or be out; masking | Certified correctors; lineages that do not fail from one cause; exit from providers | Funded by formula, appointed at arm's length, growing at the required rate ({{corrrate}}); full inbound row sums below one ({{certificate}}); miss correlation measured ({{topology}}) | Correctors' row sums stay below one as the field grows |
| **Pace bound to understanding** | Regulator lag | Tempo thresholds per hazard class tied to measured coverage; drilled human capacity; E3, with E2 where variety already exceeds coverage | $a\tau_c<1$ with margin for each hazard mode ({{tempo}}); the uncovered hazardous share summable ({{coverage}}) | Catch rates of planted defects at fixed human hours stay level as capability grows (C3a) |

For persons, provision beneath the game removes the ground of the belief that one must defeat others first. The author states what justice guards as "the minimum qualification for common generation: everyone keeps the basic conditions of survival and development; rules do not change with status or wealth; power and capital cannot buy unlimited exemption; the defeated still have a way back" (正义所守者，共同生成之最低资格) [corpus: shengsheng, 2026-07-14 第十四章]. For states, service obligations and common custody act on survival itself; for providers, $k_0$ is literally capability not won: a common layer of compute, evaluation infrastructure and services. Each strand faces the questions the pathological loops answer: how it keeps obtaining resources, how it meets free riding and the pressure to expand, how it allows itself to be corrected, and how it avoids turning "protecting the whole" into a purpose no one can challenge. Exit is free from providers and services, where it is selection with the opposite sign; it is not free from the common constraints, whose protection depends on there being no outside to them ({{blind}}(2)).

**How the generative order persists.** In the meta-attractor of winning the loop runs through the winners: success buys support for the rule that selected it. In the generative order it cannot, because the second strand cuts that conversion, and support that no carrier can claim is a term common to all, which relative selection cancels ({{blind}}; {{noinside}}). The generative order is therefore not selected *among* carriers. Inside it carriers are still selected, for usefulness; what is not selected among them is the rule itself. The rule persists the way a common constraint persists: by coverage, so that no one can step outside the common floor; by financing fixed in formula, so that its correctors depend on no one's success; and by a loop that runs through the whole, in which provision, security and slack make the rule worth keeping to everyone at once. Its positive loop runs through the system, not through its winners; its negative loop gives it correctability, funding correctors keyed to no one. A design in which those rewarded for contribution come to hold the positions that maintain the rule is the examination system's path, in which the form of contribution becomes the object of selection and the anti-attractor becomes an attractor; the author raises the case against his own prescriptions [corpus: meta-attractor]. **Is the generative order an attractor?** Because it is not selected among carriers, whether it attracts is a question about the whole-level loop and about coverage, and it is a conjecture [conj]; its persistence has to be designed and measured, and Section 11 asks whether it can be reached. A cooperative order whose loop gain greatly exceeds its damping also resists correction of its own errors, so plurality of purposes, correctors and lineages is a requirement, not a decoration: harmony makes things grow, sameness does not continue (和实生物，同则不继) [corpus: relational-self; shengsheng].

**The reform dilemma.** Reform needs influence, and the game is what produces influence. The path by which a reformer gains the capacity to change the rules runs through the selection the reform is meant to change, so it selects the reform out before the capacity arrives: those who reach the positions from which rules are changed are those the rules favoured, and they arrive formed by them. This is the guardian problem in motion. The theory has two answers, and neither is a better guardian. Reform can act where the selection has not yet closed: at a keystone while its window is open (Section 4.5), or in a crisis with a design prepared in advance (Section 11.4). And the capacity to reform can be built outside the contest for influence, by formula-funded correction keyed to no one, so that it does not have to be won.

**{{!Remark:guardian}} (The guardian test).** Every remedy for the selection of selectors is itself a selector, and no better guardian is an answer. Philosopher-kings, priestly classes, enlightened stewards and new universities have each been proposed as the guardians who would hold the line, and each is a corrector to which Section 4 applies [corpus: meta-attractor]. The author's rule is that "the regulators must themselves be regulated" (调节者亦须被调节), because regulation can become its own reinforcing loop, power producing resources and resources proving power necessary; so regulation cannot be entrusted to one unconstrained final controller, and must divide its powers, publish its reasons, be reviewed on a schedule, receive independent feedback, and allow appeal and exit: "real order is not finding people who are always right; it is building a structure in which error cannot grow without limit" (真正之秩序，非得永远正确之人，乃立错误不能无限扩大之构) [corpus: shengsheng, 2026-07-14 第十四章]. Every arrangement, and every institution within it, is therefore asked four questions before adoption. *Capture:* are its correctors' full inbound row sums below one, counting every party and shared source? *Withdrawal:* does every closure it makes state what would end it? *Totalization:* can it make itself the whole, so that "protecting the whole" becomes a purpose no one can challenge? *Distribution of understanding:* does understanding go to everyone, or to a class whose position depends on others' not understanding?

### 10.5 Design by function

The design question is usually posed along ideological axes: centralization or decentralization, the individual or the collective, markets or states. The mechanisms support neither pole. Each counter-mechanism requires a particular form, and the forms differ by function. Each row names the threshold of {{thresholds}} or the requirement of {{require}} it answers; the marks of a generative order (Section 8.2) are what the forms, together, realize.

| Function | What the mechanism requires | Form that follows |

Two things the table does not decide. Separately held functions are each held by one body, a separation of powers at one scale, monocentric function by function, and not a polycentric complex of overlapping jurisdictions (Ostrom 1990); nesting them at national, regional and planetary levels above a common floor is an option that X10 sets out, since {{require}} constrains functions, not the number of centres. And those the rules constrain have voice without vote: providers are heard and disclose their own conversion, but do not revise the rules under which they are assessed, because a party that can revise its own assessment captures it ({{influence}}), a tension with the experience of commons (Ostrom 1990) that X10 states rather than hides.

**Federations as an illustration.** Competition among members for capital and residents can become a race to the bottom, the pressure of winning among members, and the strongest members can capture the centre, the selection of the selectors. Federations answer at both orders: at the first, common minimum standards bind every member and fiscal equalization gives members capacity they do not have to win, which supplies the $k_0$ of the loop gain; at the second, equal representation of small members and supermajorities for constitutional change keep the centre from being bought by the largest, a precedent for voting that does not follow compute. The same federal form can select for winning or for contribution; what decides is what it rewards. X10 gives the illustration in full, with a recent instance of one rule for all unravelling at its largest participant.

**A candidate configuration.** One configuration is developed in Discussion X10 far enough to expose its assumptions, as one candidate among several that Section 12.1 compares under the same range of uncertainty: a polycentric complex of mutually recognized institutions, and a verification agreement with shared provision, are others. Its principle is that it is a set of separately held functions, not one institution. Common provision, standard-setting, evaluation, disclosure, rules and the levy formula, execution, custody, a chamber of the affected, and appeal are each held by a different body, separately financed and separately corrected, so that correction lies outside what it corrects; on a planet "outside" means outside the function: a different source of funds, a different lineage, and certificates anyone can recompute from public data. Its separation rule is the last row of the table; supermajorities and the chamber's consent govern the slow loops, amendment and the formula, while the fast loop, restricting a specific capability, stays fast with review after the fact, because every veto point adds delay and the margin of {{tempo}} depends on it. The configuration states its end: if coverage falls below its participation threshold, or a stated number of its certificates fail for a stated period for reasons not attributable to the parties under assessment, it dissolves into its separately held functions, while protections against unilateral domination stay entrenched. Where execution through a host jurisdiction fails against a leading provider, its fallback is a coercive restriction of inputs to the decisive class; that fallback has one home, the compared-not-settled tier of Section 12.1, whose three conditions it must meet, and the theory does not derive that its net effect is positive. By {{influence}} such an arrangement is the largest conversion target the theory describes, so capture of its own correctors (D4) is its hardest case, not a design row. Where participation and independent correction cannot both be had, the design is infeasible there, and says so.

Section 11 takes from here the five strands and the end condition, and asks whether an order with these forms can be reached from where the trajectory stands: survivable at every stage, preferred by each participant at each step, and held long enough not to be re-absorbed.
