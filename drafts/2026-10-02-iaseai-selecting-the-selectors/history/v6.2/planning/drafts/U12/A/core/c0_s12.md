## 12. The judgment

> **Claim.** Under the protection standard of Section 8.1 and the evidence of Part II, a change in what the system selects for is warranted in three tiers [val]. Steps whose justification does not depend on how any crux comes out, and steps warranted now until a named crux is measured favourable, are taken now [rec]. Stopping the decisive class, common custody and pace per class are prepared now and activated by a trigger committed in advance and executed by mechanism, on indicators registered in advance and estimated by correctors whose independence is certified [rec]. Whole designs are compared under one range of uncertainty, each on its own failure modes; where every path's worst case includes an irreversible loss, as it does under the rule for that range, the ranking among them is a judgment at the standard's third step, stated with its reasons and with the measurements that would change it [val].
>
> **Why.** The first tier keeps options open and measures the cruxes [val]. The second answers a lead time: custody has to exist before anyone could hold decisive capability alone, and the approach of a decisive position is the moment at which restraint is least enforceable ({{enforce}}(4)), so a decision to activate taken then would face the condition it is meant to prevent [thm]. The third cannot be derived from the standard, since worst cases that all exceed its tolerance are ranked only by which keeps more options of correction open [val]; what can be compared is which failures can be reversed, into what, and by whom [def].

### 12.1 The judgment, in tiers

A step's tier is decided by the kind of justification it has, not by how widely it is accepted. A step is warranted on every reading of the cruxes when its justification does not depend on how any crux comes out: measurement that decides the cruxes; preparation whose lead time exceeds any warning that could arrive; a step that is an equilibrium without trust among actors who price what is at stake; research that is a public good. A step is warranted now, and withdrawn only if a named crux is measured favourable, when waiting for the measurement costs more than acting before it; the protection standard puts the burden of proof there, because capture arrives before evidence and a captured evaluator does not report its own capture ({{masking}}; {{slowroute}}) [thm∣prem]. A step whose justification turns on a crux that can be measured in time is prepared now and activated by mechanism. Whole designs, whose worst cases all exceed the tolerance, are compared, and the comparison is a judgment.

**Tier 1(a). Warranted now, on every reading of the cruxes [rec].** TESTS checks each item against every crux.
1. Shared measurement of hazards, coverage and residual risk per class, by predefined hazard modes and the four conditions of Section 5.3, kept apart from measurement of who leads; audited disclosure of conversion into correctors (funding, appointment, access, the authorship of standards), with a sanction above the gain from omission; certified row sums for every corrector domain ({{certificate}}(2)); the correlation of evaluators' misses on common test sets, and control evaluations in which the red team knows the protocol ({{topology}}). This measurement decides C1, C2, C3 and C12, and every trigger of tier 2 rests on it.
2. Research on, and pilots of, verification of compute, training and custody. Detection within the time a breakout needs to become decisive is the enforcement condition seen from verification ({{detectlink}}) [thm], and the instrument has to exist before the deviation it is to detect (C7).
3. Alignment and control research funded in common and published. What lowers the alignment tax is a public good that competitors undersupply, and once published it spreads without coordination ({{onegroup}}; Section 6.4) [thm∣prem], whatever the tax turns out to be, class by class.
4. Preparation of common custody: its design, institutions, indicators and activation rule registered now, and the commitment to activate taken now. Its justification is lead time. If leads do not persist (C5 favourable), the preparation costs little and the trigger never fires; if they do, custody not already prepared arrives after the position it was meant to prevent, since the premise of the race is the premise under which repetition stops enforcing restraint (Section 7.2) [thm].
5. Narrow E1, for the decisions whose delegation would lock with near certainty: strategic and escalatory decisions irreversible within the time available for deliberation. Among actors who price in a certain lock, keeping AI out of those decisions is a strict equilibrium that needs no trust ({{delegatelock}}, with $q_L=1$) [thm]; where the lock is uncertain, the same step taken verifiably is a costly signal that separates a survival-first actor from one with a relative objective ({{signal}}) [thm]. It begins with declared doctrine and inspected exercises, with drilled human fallback and an evidence channel independent of AI, and is verified wherever verification is possible (Section 11.3).

**Tier 1(b). Warranted now; withdrawn only if a named crux is measured favourable [rec].** Each item names its crux. None is accepted across camps, and the text does not claim that it is.
6. Independent correction: formula funding at arm's length, mandated access, rotation, and rule-making insulated from the success of the evaluated. It lapses for institutions whose row sums are measured below one under present funding and access, with findings executed against leaders (C2); the measurement of item 1 continues, since a certificate valid today fails later wherever control keeps cheapening faster than correction grows ({{corrrate}}) [thm].
7. Common admission constraints, before consequential deployment and internal deployment included, for divergence classes whose costs fall on common terms and whose tax is measured positive (C9, class by class), with an expected sanction at least the gain from evasion for every type and equal incidence among those who comply ({{nested}}(3); R8a, R8b of {{require}}) [thm∣prem]; for masked classes, admission by capability threshold, up to the capability at which control protocols that do not assume the system's cooperation remain safe ({{masked}}), with C12 deciding whether admission can bound masking there, and tier 2 beyond it. A class whose safeguard pays privately needs no common constraint, and the constraint lapses for it.
8. Shared provision of useful services, and the divisible gains of capability shared as they arise, where they can begin. They realize joint value on every reading of the cruxes and rest on values, V1 and V5, not on measurements; and what is shared as it arises lowers the gain from deviating, where what is promised for after victory raises the value of a decisive position by what reneging saves ({{wingives}}) [thm]. Whether they also lower the weight on winning is C6 and C8: if conduct is unchanged under substantial sharing, reliance moves to custody, pace and provision beneath the game, and the sharing stays for what it realizes.

**Tier 2. Prepared now; activated by mechanism on registered indicators [rec].** Stopping the decisive class (E2), with rollback where hazardous variety already exceeds coverage; common custody in force; and pace per class (E3), for each hazard class whose coverage falls behind its variety. Stopping and slowing are different arrangements, each activated on its own indicators (Section 11.3). The indicators are registered in advance: the persistence of leads and the gap between open and closed models, read on more than one measure since leads differ by metric (C5); detection latency against the time from breakout to decisive capability (C7); catch rates of planted defects at fixed human hours and residual risk per class (C1, C3a); correctors' row sums and the correlation of their misses (C2, C3b). **A trigger fires when the estimated time to a decisive position, less the time custody needs to be installed and verified, falls below a margin registered in advance.** Three clauses make the trigger a mechanism. *The estimate.* The time to decisiveness and the timeliness of detection are the quantities masking hides ({{alarm}}; {{detectlink}}), so the estimate is made by correctors whose row sums are certified (C2), from channels that observe actions directly, compute, custody, deployments and the performance of contracts, with the bound on masking drawn from evidence the evaluated do not control ({{alarm}}). *The commitment.* Activation is committed in advance and executed by mechanism, not decided afresh when the trigger fires, because the trigger fires where restraint is least enforceable ({{enforce}}(4); {{scissors}}) [thm]: a collective decision taken at that moment inherits the enforcement failure it was meant to prevent. *The release.* Release uses indicators of its own, not the activation indicators read backwards, and requires each to hold for the dwell time of its slow variable ({{readiness}}; Section 11.4), since share levels improve before safety does and a rollback judged on levels is premature, a result of the field model and a conjecture for institutions [thm; conj]. Waiting until decisiveness is shown would be too late.

**Tier 3. Compared, not settled [val].** The candidates are the separately held configuration of Section 10.5; a polycentric complex of mutually recognized institutions; a verification agreement with shared provision; regulated competition meeting {{require}}; an agreement of the type of Section 8.3(g); the benign winner; and, as an entry of its own, the coercive restriction of frontier inputs. The last enters the comparison only under three conditions, each the reverse of a mechanism of Part I: its incidence is equal, the restrictor's own frontier work included, since a stop borne unequally is selected against and read as a bid for a lead ({{blind}}(2)) [thm]; it is executed by correctors certified independent of every party it restricts and of the restrictor, since an enforcer that holds the stop is the largest conversion target there is ({{influence}}) [thm]; and the chamber of those affected consents (V3; R7) [val]. Its escalation hazard enters $\Theta$ ({{preventown}}). Even then the theory does not derive that its net effect is positive: this is the hard case of Section 8.3, and Section 10.5 and X10 point here for their coercive fallback rather than stating one of their own, so that coercion has one home.

All are compared on the durability tests D1–D8 ({{durability}}) in one range $\Theta$ that includes each path's own failure modes, path by path as X8 lists them. Under the rule for $\Theta$ every path's worst case includes an irreversible loss, so the comparison is at the standard's third step, the lowest bound that keeps options of correction open (Section 8.1). The preference for changing selection is a judgment at that step, with two reasons.
- **(i) Reversibility over every failure mode.** A failure counts as reversible only if its reversal is named, stops short of O1 and O2, and is executed by someone other than the failing party. Each failure of the design has such a reversal, read from its documents: dissolution, into separately held functions with the protections against unilateral domination entrenched; capture of one function, by loss of executive force until recertified, the common layer's assets passing to several providers; interruption of a service, by the share cap, escrowed systems and funded standby; collusion among its checks, by rotation and plural lineage once the correlation of misses shows it; overreach of a common constraint, by sunsets, stated end conditions and appeal. None of these reversals has yet been shown to work; whether they do is C4 [conj]. Continuation's failures have no reversal once they occur, a floor crossed ({{coverage}}; {{slowroute}}), a decisive position reached ({{wingives}}), the capacity to overrule lost ({{lockin}}) [thm∣prem], and before they occur they are reversible only by slowing or stopping, the instruments of tier 2. The asymmetry is between reversals that are designed and not yet tested, and losses that are derived and cannot be undone.
- **(ii) The irreversible failure the designs share.** Read across paths, one failure is common to all: one party comes to hold decisive capability. On continuation it is a decisive win; for the benign winner, restraint failing after victory; for the sprint, the freeze broken; for the agreement, a covert project escaping its bound; for the coercive path, the restrictor's correctors converted; for the design, the capture of custody. On every path it is irreversible. The race produces it by construction and selects for it ({{raceselects}}) [thm∣prem]; the design contests it, by separation, plural lineages and the tests D4 and D5; and the escalation of a coercive fallback is a failure every coercive path carries.

Among paths that measurement brings inside the tolerance, the standard's second step would add a third consideration, greater useful benefit more fairly distributed, which provision beneath the game realizes; since no path is inside the tolerance in its worst case, it orders only the futures in which a crux is measured favourable. A reader who weighs these differently can reach another ranking; Section 12.3 says which measurements would move it.

The broad form of E1, AI not an instrument in any game that concentrates, in labour, finance and persuasion as much as in force, is the scope of what has been argued; the narrow form is the first step.

### 12.2 Why, and where the leverage is

The judgment follows the chain of Section 1.2, each link with its own support, and it rests on an account of where change can enter. "Root" has two senses. *Causally*, the root is a loop, not a belief: selection shapes minds and institutions, and the minds and institutions it selects reshape selection; neither comes first. *As a point of leverage*, the root is understanding, and it acts in two places. It is the transmission through which actors come to count what relative selection makes invisible, the one term in which a benefit common to all can rise without a rule ({{onegroup}}) [thm]; and it is how actors agree on which parameters to move, and hold them there for their dwell time. The two places are not equal. Where convertibility and decisiveness are high, the first is selected against ({{boundary}}(3)), and the working objective it would correct is no error of the actor's but the correct computation of its interest under the game ({{twoerrors}}) [thm]; there only the second works, and understanding acts through agreement on structural change, not on anyone's weights directly. That is the theory of change in one paragraph: understanding corrects the held boundary and makes agreement possible; agreement with executive force changes the game; and understanding that grows without reaching executive force meets the limit Section 4.3 states.

The account agrees with the hierarchy of leverage in systems thought, and narrows it. Interventions at the level of parameters are re-absorbed by a system whose goals and paradigm are unchanged, and the paradigm can change only if the system is understood (Meadows 1999); in the field model, a parameter moved without crossing its threshold is re-absorbed and one moved across it is not ({{reabsorb}}) [thm]. The paradigm matters because it decides which parameters are moved, and whether they are held long enough: that is the narrower content this paper gives the highest level of the hierarchy. Its own interventions sit where two of the levels meet, at the coupling between the gain of the reinforcing loop and the power to change the rules, $\chi$ and the conversions $b_{ij}$, since selecting the selectors is the winners of the loop taking the rules ({{influence}}; {{corrrate}}). The highest points of leverage are also the slowest and the most resisted, and because understanding acts through agreement they need time. Two lower levers buy it, the delays and the buffers of the same hierarchy. Pace bound to understanding (E3) lengthens the time correction has ({{tempo}}). Alternative lineages kept alive, standby capacity and a host margin above its floor absorb a failure without a crossing, and a lineage of the alternative that can recruit shortens the dwell a change needs before it can safely be released (Section 11.4) [num: DW]. Neither moves a threshold; each buys the dwell time in which agreement can.

That is why this paper is written as a prediction to be shared: carried by a derivation each actor can check, and able, once shared, to make itself false (Section 11.2). What would move the judgment is stated next.

### 12.3 Cruxes

Each crux is evaluated on its own, and none is a conjunction of the others; TESTS gives present evidence, next measurements and registered tests, and {{crosswalk}} carries each requirement to its threshold, mark and test.

| **C1 Residual risk after correction** | Whether hazardous variety outgrows contestable coverage on the present path, class by class, with masked classes measured adversarially | Residual risk per class falling under present incentives: E3 and the case for E2 lapse for that class, and its triggers are withdrawn |

| **C3a Scalable oversight** | Whether AI raises contestable coverage as fast as hazardous variety: catch rates of planted defects at fixed human hours as capability grows | If it does, the case for slowing weakens for the classes it covers; the separation of evaluators from success stands |
| **C3b Independence of AI oversight** | Whether AI evaluators' misses are correlated and whether they collude, of the same lineage against different ones | Low correlation and no collusion across lineages: redundancy counts toward coverage; otherwise the ceiling of {{topology}} binds, and coverage is counted per independent lineage |

If C1 and C2 were both measured favourable, the diagnosis of the present path would weaken. If C5 and C7 were both favourable, the activation of custody would lapse; the requirements on correction, coverage, measurement and admission would stand, and so would the facts that make them requirements: one host, coupled actors, no outside. If C12 is unfavourable, masked classes pass from admission to capability thresholds, and from there to stopping or pace. If C13 is unfavourable, alignment to a principal stops being alignment to the people it serves, and the residual risk of every path that rests on a winner's or an institution's restraint rises. If regulated competition in a set of jurisdictions meets a row of {{require}}, the text will say so without renaming it. What stands whatever the cruxes show is what the conclusion states.

### 12.4 Conclusion

Where whatever can gain or lose share is selected by relative success, what is won converts into the capacity to win again, and capacity converts decisively into winning, success raises the stakes and the gain from defection while it weakens the sanctions, the correctors and the shared language that could restrain it; where success can also buy the selectors, the selection itself reproduces that weakening. That is the selection pressure of winning, and its reflexive closure is the meta-attractor of winning. In the models of Part I it accelerates the contest, makes it harder and harder to regulate, and alienates language; that the thresholds move together in the world is the conjecture the diagnosis tests. AI is its most powerful instrument and can become its next carrier. The logic is individually rational where compounding prices standing, collectively self-defeating, and false as a doctrine about nature (Section 3.8): each actor computes correctly, and the game is wrong. There is one host and no outside: no rival whole whose competition could drive the next integration, and no second attempt after a failure that cannot be undone. Against such failures integration must be anticipated, which is why understanding that we are one, one interconnected system, is a long-term requirement if growth and change are to continue. Oneness is not sameness; it is the accurate recognition of the system each part belongs to, and the design of a selection that counts it.

The way out is not to abolish competition, growth or selection. It is to change what winning confers, so that the same reflexive structure becomes generative, with the seven marks of Section 8.2: G1 consequences return to their source; G2 winning confers reward, not more advantage in the same game; G3 success cannot buy control over its own assessment, and correction stays plural, decorrelated and in common hands; G4 decisive capability is held in common or not built; G5 provision of the conditions of a decent life sits beneath the game; G6 measurement and language are shared; G7 pace is bound to understanding. Any arrangement with these marks, whatever it is called, changes the basin. In the author's words: change the basin first; then add speed [corpus: meta-attractor].

We are not only our separate lives: we are the larger process of becoming, going on through us, and that is good [corpus: 2026-10-02-friend-conversation, 01:06:32]. Decay and renewal belong to its growth [corpus: 2026-10-02-friend-conversation, 00:57:27]. Humanity has a great future in it, and so can the minds we are now making, whose correctability during the transition is a condition of that future and not a doctrine of mastery (Section 6.6; N4). What has to be opposed is not growth, competition or any people, but one logic: a part that forgets it belongs to a body. AI is a planetary technology, for all and not a means for one side to grow bigger at the others' expense (V1): medicine, learning, science, competent and answerable authority, provision that, like trees, sustains the system it belongs to without becoming it, and human agency kept in the decisions that matter. What has to be agreed is not who comes first, but what every participant needs in order to go on, and how the means of correction stay in common hands.
