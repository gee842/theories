## What remains unestablished

The new result supplies a conditional invariant subset in a reduced model. It does not construct an institution whose funding, information, authority and execution persist under the full selection process. The general correctable kernel and safe route into it remain uncomputed. The institutional Test N is proposed and unrun. The present-path interlock, the residual irreversible-risk floor and whole-design comparative performance still require independent evidence.

Fresh AI review contexts are bounded checks, not independent human certification. The numerical suite checks specified calculations and witnesses; it does not validate the empirical diagnosis, every inherited proof or the feasibility of a public institution.
