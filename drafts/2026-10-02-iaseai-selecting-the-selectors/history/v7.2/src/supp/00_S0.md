## S0. Conventions

**Maintained assumptions.** Each is listed with what changes if it is dropped.
- (A1) Exposure to the host is shared, so $G(V)$ is common to all payoffs. {{unequal}} treats unequal exposure.
- (A2) Field size is fixed. With entry driven by absolute profit, the host settles where rents dissipate, and (reach) must hold at that equilibrium.
- (A3) Conversion is monotone ($b_{ij}\ge0$), so countervailing pressure enters only through correction $d$ ({{countervail}}). A countervailing channel is not a negative term of $\Gamma$, and one that answers with delay can oscillate with growing amplitude while the net gain is below one ({{delayosc}}).
- (A4) The cost of conversion capacity is that of {{costq}}, with $[H_\gamma]$ and $[H_f]$ stated as hypotheses wherever used.
- (A5) Reports are averaged and non-strategic. {{alarm}} treats other alarm rules.
- (A6) In the field model, one practice and its alternative per domain, with payoffs linear in the stated terms; parameters uncalibrated. Contest prizes and stock dynamics have the forms stated in their own results. No loop gain is estimated for any channel; {{gammarange}} shows at illustrative values what a gain above one asks of each parameter.
- (A7) Contest models are symmetric unless stated.
- (A8) In the bargaining model of {{preventattack}}, after a shift in power the side that gained the advantage sets the terms each period, and the other accepts or fights.
- (A9) Unless a statement says that rivals respond, the others' conduct is held fixed, and each statement says so. With responsive rivals, the equilibrium win-share elasticity determines local stability; in the deterministic share model the private elasticity enters the local price calculation, together with any additional payoff effects ({{loopgain}}(4)). A lottery's finite-jump continuation value is a separate object, computed in {{eqgain}}, and is not obtained by that substitution. The computed price corrections can be severalfold; the reported catch-up reversal concerns win-share elasticities, not the total continuation price [num: MP; held for outside review].

**Renamed in V6.2.** To give each shared symbol one meaning, the following were renamed.
- *S2.* The Jacobian $J\to\mathcal J$ and its diagonal $D\to\mathcal D$, with the vectors written $\mathbf u$, $\mathbf v$; an eigenvalue $\lambda\to\Lambda$, the Perron root $\Lambda^\ast$; the Perron vector $w\to\mathbf z$; the deviations of {{bench}} $\to u$, $w$.
- *Definitions 2.4 and 3.2.* $\sigma$, $F$, $G\to\mathcal S$, $\mathcal F$, $\mathcal G$.
- *S3.* The costs of a constraint $\kappa_k\to c_k$; the expected sanction $s_k\to\bar F_k$, $\bar F(x)$; the host's payoff coefficient $\varpi\to g_V$, so that $\varpi$ is trust only; the number of non-contestants $\to m_o$.
- *S4.* The cost of capacity $\to\mathrm{Cost}(q;p)$, and the "fixed cost" $f$ is now the per-unit cost floor, a marginal cost that every unit pays; locals $k\to\kappa_q$ (adjustment rate), $\rho\to s_0$, $c\to c_1$, $V\to V_q$ (selection variance), $g\to g_q$, $F\to F_q$ (lump sum), $D\to D_h$ (delegated share), $v_d\to k_V$, and the number of domains $D\to N_D$.
- *S5.* In {{enforce}}(5), $q\to q_b$ and the value of subordination $\to V^{\rm sub}_i$; locals $w_p$, $\tau_{\rm det}$.
- *S6.* In (15), the harm $d\to h_d$ and its exponent $\beta_d\to\beta_h$; in {{topology}}, the one-layer reliability $f\to p_\ell$, the checks per layer $m\to k_c$, $q_{\rm cc}\to p_{\rm cc}$, the layer ratio $m_\ell\to\mathsf m$, and attention $Y\to\mathsf A$, $\mathsf B$; in {{opaquecorr}}, $q_0$, $q_c\to h_0$, $h_{c,t}$; in {{homeostasis}}, $q\to1-\psi_0$; the detection probability $\pi\to p_{\rm det}$.
- *S8.* $U_t\to\mathsf u_t$ in the proof of {{fallback}}; $X$, $X_{\rm stay}\to V^{\rm exit}$, $V^{\rm stay}$.
- *S10.* The cooperators' number $m\to n_c$; the winner's keep $\to A_w$; in {{participate}}, $U^{\rm race}$ and $\mathsf v_i$, clear of the host $V$.
- *S11.* In {{signal}}, $k_{\mathsf t}$, $G_{\mathsf t}\to c_{\mathsf t}$, $\Pi_{\mathsf t}$.

Two families are still separated by subscript only: $\kappa$ alone is the unpriced coupling, while $\kappa_s$ is the positional weight, $\kappa_c$ and $\kappa_j$ are convertible shares and $\kappa_W$ a winner's coupling; $\delta$ alone is the discount factor, while $\delta_f$ (diffusion) and $\delta_h$ (erosion of human capacity) are rates.

**Numbering and keys.** The core numbers results by section with one counter per section, shared by all kinds, tables included ({{thresholds}} is the first result of Section 7). The Supplement uses the same numbers for full statements and adds S-numbered results, among them results moved from the core; the Discussion has no numbered results. Equations are numbered (1), (2), … in the core and (S1), (S2), … here. Every result has a stable key that never changes (for example `enforce`, `loopgain`); CLAIMS, LEDGER and `checks_v72.py` are indexed by key, and `src/keymap.json` maps keys to displayed numbers.

**Claim tags.** [def] definition; [val] value, written [val: V1–V3] where it names the values it rests on; [thm] proved in its model; [thm∣prem] proved given premises the statement names; [prem] premise; [obs] observation, with its evidence row ([obs: EV5]); [num: X] a number reproduced by block X of `checks_v72.py`; [identity: X] an algebraic identity or threshold formula that block X evaluates against its own statement, which guards against slips and is not independent evidence; [num: legacy …] a number carried from an earlier version and reproduced by its legacy block (`checks_v72.py --legacy`); [conj] conjecture; [rec] recommendation. "Held for outside review" marks a computation not yet checked outside the drafting family. A table of results tagged [def, with support] organizes results proved elsewhere, each row carrying the tag of the result it cites; in {{thresholds}} a dagger marks a sign that crosses models through an interface of S13 that is not proved. [corpus: key] marks the source in the author's corpus, the folder that the key names (X1 gives the sources); a quoted line is his own words unless it is introduced as the corpus's, which marks a line whose authorship the corpus does not settle; the English renderings of his Chinese, and the paraphrases a drafting model made of his spoken words, are the drafters' until he confirms them (CLAIMS).
