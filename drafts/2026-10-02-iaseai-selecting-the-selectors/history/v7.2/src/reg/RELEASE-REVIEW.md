## What remains open

The full institutional result must derive how funding, access, appointment, authority and measurement remain effective under the relevant successor and rule changes. The reduced certificate assumes bounds on those consequences; it does not construct their durable implementation. The general correctable kernel and a safe route into it remain uncomputed.

The present-path interlock, residual irreversible-hazard floor, stock-to-strategic-payoff connection and comparative whole-design performance still require further theory and independent evidence. An empirical Test N, specialist human review and operational tests would support stronger conclusions. This revision clarifies those obligations rather than reporting them as achieved.
