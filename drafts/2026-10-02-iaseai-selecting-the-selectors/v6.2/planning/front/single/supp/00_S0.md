## S0. Conventions

**Maintained assumptions.** Each is listed with what changes if it is dropped.
- (A1) Exposure to the host is shared, so $G(V)$ is common to all payoffs. {{unequal}} treats unequal exposure.
- (A2) Field size is fixed. With entry driven by absolute profit, the host settles where rents dissipate, and (reach) must hold at that equilibrium.
- (A3) Conversion is monotone ($b_{ij}\ge0$), so countervailing pressure enters only through correction $d$ ({{countervail}}). A countervailing channel is not a negative term of $\Gamma$, and one that answers with delay can oscillate with growing amplitude while the net gain is below one ({{delayosc}}).
- (A4) The cost of conversion capacity is that of {{costq}}, with $[H_\gamma]$ and $[H_f]$ stated as hypotheses wherever used.
- (A5) Reports are averaged and non-strategic. {{alarm}} treats other alarm rules.
- (A6) One practice and its alternative per domain; payoffs linear; parameters uncalibrated. No loop gain is estimated for any channel; {{gammarange}} shows at illustrative values what a gain above one asks of each parameter.
- (A7) Contest models are symmetric unless stated.
- (A8) In the bargaining model of {{preventattack}}, after a shift in power the side that gained the advantage sets the terms each period, and the other accepts or fights.
- (A9) Unless a statement says that rivals respond, the others' conduct is held fixed, and each statement says so. Where rivals respond, the structural loop gain and the price of standing keep their form with an equilibrium decisiveness in place of the structural one ({{loopgain}}(4); {{eqgain}}), which changes the numbers, by several times in the contests computed where a lead discourages the follower, and in one case of catch-up their sign.

**Reuses.** A statement or proof that reuses a letter of the core's table for a different quantity declares it there. The cases are: in S2, the proof of {{loopgain}}(3) writes $\eta$ for a small radius and $f$ for the map of the deviation; the one-stock computations of S2 and S3 write $\kappa$ for $\kappa_c$, and with a prize that grows with capacity $G$ for the growth factor of total capacity and $m$ for the multiplier of a share deviation; {{scope}} writes $Y$ for a carrier's total share across domains; {{freeze}} in full writes $\tau$ for the date at which hazardous variety is frozen; {{delegatelock}} and its proof write $p$ for the probability that losing means subordination; {{raceselects}} and its proof write $E$, $W$ and $X$ for events and $\pi$ for $\Pr(E)$; {{fallback}} and its proof write $A$ and $C$ for an arrangement and continuation; S8's reading of Jones (2024) writes $\rho$ for the discount rate. Where an index is dropped, as in $C-v$ for $C_i-v_i$ in symmetric statements, the symbol keeps its meaning.

**Numbering and keys.** The core numbers results by section with one counter per section, shared by all kinds, tables included ({{thresholds}} is the first result of Section 7). The Supplement uses the same numbers for full statements and adds S-numbered results, among them results moved from the core; the Discussion has no numbered results. Equations are numbered (1), (2), … in the core and (S1), (S2), … here. Every result has a stable key that never changes (for example `enforce`, `loopgain`); CLAIMS, LEDGER and `checks_v62.py` are indexed by key, and `src/keymap.json` maps keys to displayed numbers.

**Claim tags.** [def] definition; [val] value, written [val: V1–V3] where it names the values it rests on; [thm] proved in its model; [thm∣prem] proved given premises the statement names; [prem] premise; [obs] observation, with its evidence row ([obs: EV5]); [num: X] a number reproduced by block X of `checks_v62.py`; [identity: X] an algebraic identity or threshold formula that block X evaluates against its own statement, which guards against slips and is not independent evidence; [num: legacy …] a number carried from an earlier version and reproduced by that version's suite (`checks_v62.py --legacy`); [conj] conjecture; [rec] recommendation. "Held for outside review" marks a computation not yet checked outside the drafting family. A table of results tagged [def, with support] organizes results proved elsewhere, each row carrying the tag of the result it cites; in {{thresholds}} a dagger marks a sign that crosses models through an interface of S13 that is not proved. [corpus: key] marks the author's words, quoted from the folder of his corpus that the key names (X1 gives the source and date of each).
