## Symbols and labels

Each symbol in this table keeps the meaning given here wherever it appears in the core and in both companions; indices ($i$, $j$, $k$) name actors, carriers, stocks or domains as each model declares. Every other symbol, and a letter of the table with a subscript or superscript the table does not give, is local to the model that declares it. A statement or proof that reuses a letter of the table for another quantity declares the reuse; Supplement S0 lists the reuses, the other symbols shared between chapters and the renames of this version.
