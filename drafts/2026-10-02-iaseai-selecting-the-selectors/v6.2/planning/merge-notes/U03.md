## 2. Disagreements resolved by derivation

1. **`blind`(3), weak or strict.** If $\bar F_k=c_k$, evaders and compliers of type $k$ earn the same, so their ratio is constant: compliance survives but does not prevail. Hence "survives if $\ge$; share tends to one if every inequality is strict". A had only $>$ with "prevails"; B had both.
2. **`blind`(3), falling sanction.** Existence of $x^\dagger$ needs continuity (intermediate value theorem); uniqueness and instability need strict decrease. B's statement omitted both, and A's omitted continuity. Merged: "continuous and strictly decreasing", one type.
3. **`standprice`(1) at $\Gamma_\delta=0$.** $\theta_\Gamma=\theta_0$, so "always narrows on $0\le\Gamma_\delta<1$" (B) is false at zero. Merged: "whenever $\Gamma_\delta>0$".
4. **Signs of the common channel.** From `twochan`: host visibility $(1+\theta)/n$ and the point of enough $(1+\theta)H/n$ fall as $\theta$ falls; the rebated levy $\tau(n-1-\theta)/n$ and the promise-breaking gain $\beta\Omega(n-1-\theta)/(n-1)$ rise. V6.1, both D2 memos and B listed all four under "falls". A's correction is taken, in core and S3.
5. **`boundary`(1) proof.** At a symmetric profile $W=(1+\kappa)\pi^{\rm e}+(1+\kappa_s)\Omega_s/n$, not $(1+\kappa)\pi$ (B). The conclusion that $W$ rises with $\theta$ is unchanged.
6. **`onegroup` headline.** "Selected against at every level of grouping" (B, V6.1) asserts a non-positive between-group term, which fails for non-affine fitness (PR ext: 3–346 of 3,000). A's "overall and within every group, however grouped" is what is proved.
7. **`growprize`(1).** A's $(1-\beta)r(1+\Omega_{20}/(gS))$ and B's $(1-\beta)r\Omega_2/(nKg)$ coincide ($S=nK$). A's form is kept because it shows that a fixed part of the stakes adds only to the relative return.
8. **`growprize`, the gap.** $\delta_f/(\kappa_cg)-\delta\delta_f/(1-\delta(1-d_K))=\delta_f(1-\Gamma^{\rm c}_\delta)/(\kappa_cg)$, positive inside the domain. Verified; SP(F) gives 1.1421 against 1.2000.
9. **Two errors, $n=2$, $\kappa=1$.** $\kappa=n-1$ lies outside $[0,n-1)$, where $\theta_0=n-1$ never narrows. B's remark is degenerate and is dropped. The threshold $n\kappa/((n-1)(1+\kappa))$ is below one for every admissible $\kappa$ (stated).
10. **S3 Bounds.** A said the infinite horizon with a mixed prize and depreciation "is not closed". At a steady state the general form covers any $\Omega(S)$; what is not closed is the time-varying case (a mixed prize while capacity grows). Reworded.
11. **`club`(1) corner.** If the club's optimum is zero, the system's is zero too, and nothing is over-produced. Over-production needs $m_o\ell_o>0$ and a positive club choice; proof: $\Omega'(\mathcal E)\le c+n\ell<c+n\ell+m_o\ell_o$ for $\mathcal E\ge\mathcal E_c$.
12. **The strategic form.** `loopgain`(4) establishes the relative price with $r^{\rm pr}$. Nothing computes the common return when rivals respond to a growing prize. Both drafts said (9) "survives" generally; the merge says so with a fixed prize, and S3 states what is not computed.
13. **Amplification ranges.** B's "1.05 to 3.15 with small jumps" includes a large-jump case (r=1, Γ=0.9 at $d=0.1$). Relabelled "at $d=0.1$". The core keeps "about 1.05 to 7.9", which MP's s2b assertion quotes.
14. **Identifying θ with perceived share ψ.** The first ratio, $(1+\theta)\psi$, reads a true $\theta=0$ as $-0.5$ at $\psi=\tfrac12$; the second, $n(1+\theta)\psi/(n-1-\theta)$, reads it as $-0.43$ (SM(3)). V6.1 and A attached $-0.43$ to "what is identified is $(1+\theta)\psi$", which conflated the two. Both are now stated.
15. **The cusp.** B proved it again in S3. Merged S2 (U02) carries the statement in `loopgain`(3) and the proof with PD, so S3 points there.
16. **Notation for prize growth.** A used $\Omega'$, which collides with the prime for "next period" in (1). B used $g$, $G$, $m$; $G$ (host function, conversion gain) and $m$ (masking) are global. Merged S2 writes $\Omega=gS$ with $\kappa_c$ and $d_K$. Merged: $g_j=\partial\Omega/\partial S^j$ and $g$ in S3 only (the core uses words), with multipliers $\Lambda_{\rm rel}$, $\Lambda_{\rm com}$.

Every number kept was checked against `checks_v62.log`: CI (a)–(j), TC, TH, SM(1)–(3), CB, PR (a)–(h), RH, NB(a), PD(a)–(c), SP(A)–(J) and MP(a)–(i).
