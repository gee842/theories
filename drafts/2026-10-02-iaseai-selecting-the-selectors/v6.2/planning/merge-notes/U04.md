## 2. Disagreements decided by derivation

1. **Necessity in S4.2: "iff $\liminf q/q^\ast>0$" (A; D2-fable §27; the QS block's comment) or "whenever" (B).** If $q/q^\ast\to0$ at a rate below $(\alpha_{\rm ctl}-(1+\eta)\alpha_{\rm cor})/\eta$, $q/d$ still grows without bound and the certificate fails. Only "if" holds. B is right.
2. **Does gradual selection with variance rising in $q$ track the selected level?** Both drafts (and D2-fable §9, §27; the f5 and m06 docstrings) say yes for variance $\propto q^2$ [num], and A's core statement said the necessary half holds "for selection whose variance grows with capacity". Derivation: for $\dot q=V_q(q)\,\partial_q\Delta\pi$ with $f=0$, $\partial_q\Delta\pi\le\chi\hat Y/d(t)$, so $\int_{q(0)}^{q(t)}dq/V_q\le\chi\hat Y/(d_0\alpha_{\rm cor})$ for all $t$. Where the integral diverges (constant variance, variance $\propto q$) capacity is bounded and the certificate holds eventually for every $\alpha_{\rm cor}>0$. Where variance grows faster than $q$ ($\propto q^2$) capacity can escape only if $\chi\hat Y/(d_0\alpha_{\rm cor})>\int_{q(0)}^\infty dq/V_q$, and once near $q^\ast$ it tracks (relaxation rate $\propto q^\ast/d$, rising below the rate law). The logs confirm every part: m06's "multiplicative" dynamics (variance $\propto q$) fails at 23.8 and ends with row sum $8.6\times10^{-8}$; Math-O s4(c)'s "log" dynamics likewise (fails at 28.8, ends at $4.6\times10^{-8}$); Sys GM(0.1) is captured only for $\alpha_{\rm cor}\le0.063$ at every horizon; m5 at speeds $\le0.1$ never fails. Stated in S4.2(2)(b)–(c), with one sentence in the core Scope. It strengthens the plan's reading: the necessary half is about optimizing (strategic) carriers.
3. **Capture follows certificate failure by "12 to 75 periods"** (both drafts, from D2-opus m06). True for m06's runs only. Across the Sys s4 runs the lag is 8.3 (GM(0.1), $\alpha_{\rm cor}=0$) to 84.7 (R(0.1), $\alpha_{\rm cor}=0.09$). The core and S4 say 8 to 85, longer as correction approaches the rate law.
4. **$[H_f]$ at $\alpha_{\rm cor}=\alpha_f$** (A's FF numerics). A wrote that $f_0=0.17$ "holds" under "$\alpha_{\rm cor}=\alpha_f$", citing a run at 0.051. f5(B) and Math-O s7: at $\alpha_{\rm cor}=\alpha_f=0.05$ both $f_0=0.1$ and $f_0=0.17$ start with cultivation and fail (37.1, 58.0); only $f_0=0.3$, with none at the start, holds. B is right. f5's printed note mislabels $f_0=0.17$; the FF block already flags it.
5. **"The certificate holds eventually in three cases"** (A; D2-fable §9 wording). In two of the three cases the rate law decides, so the certificate may fail. Rewritten as "three cases decide whether the certificate holds eventually".
6. **Pause length "at least"** (A). The formula $\ln(G/S(x_p))/(\varepsilon d)$ is the exact minimal length from captured support $b/d$ (exponential decay to the separatrix height), so "must last" is the precise form (B).
7. **$T_A$** (A: "the date at which the host under the race load first reaches $V_{\rm alarm}$"). The proof needs the path's own first entry into the band; the closed form is a lower bound under full load (comparison). B's definition kept; the T_A formula is checked in SR(d).
8. **Clock version coverage.** A restricted (b) to $f=0$; V6.1 had "$f=0$ or $[H_f]$". Under $[H_f]$ with $\alpha_{\rm cor}<\alpha_f$, (3) gives selected capacity asymptotically at its floorless level, so the necessary half applies. B's form kept, so nothing of V6.1 is lost.
9. **Symbol clashes** resolved by renaming locals: adjustment rate $k\to\kappa_q$ ($k$ is the number of parts); fraction $\rho\to s_0$ (host $\rho$); constant $c\to c_1$; selection variance $V\to V_q$ (host $V$); growth $g\to g_q$ (table $g$); lump sum $F\to F_q$ (sanction $F$); delegated share $D\to D_h$ (decisive value $D$); $v_d\to k_V$; number of domains $D\to N_D$.
