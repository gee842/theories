## 2. Disagreements resolved by derivation

1. **`assure`(2) needs $T-P+F>0$.** $g_C=R-\upsilon(P-F)-(1-\upsilon)T=(R-T)+\upsilon(T-P+F)$. With $T>R$, $g_C>0$ iff $\upsilon(T-P+F)>T-R$, which is $\upsilon>(T-R)/(T-P+F)$ only if $T-P+F>0$; if $T-P+F\le0$ no $\upsilon$ suffices. B's condition kept; A and V6.1 omitted it.
2. **`preventown`(1): the bound tends to one only where $s_P\Phi^R>0$.** $1-\max(\Phi^S,\Phi^P)/(s_P\Phi^R+(1-s_P)\Phi^P)\to1$ as $\Phi^S,\Phi^P\to0$ iff the denominator stays positive. B's condition kept.
3. **The sprint's freeze under `blind`.** Merged `blind`(2): a constraint "borne by a single carrier is selected against"; one whose cost differs "re-sorts the compliers toward those it costs least". The freeze is borne by all but one, so it re-sorts those it binds and, by (3), is evaded wherever $c_k>\bar F_k$. A's "selected against … (`blind`(2))" misapplies the single-carrier clause; B's reading kept.
4. **`verify`(3) tag.** The bound's dependence on effective compute is an identity [def]; whether the coalition's compute advantage keeps pace is unproved [conj] (plan Tags: Thm 8.11's last clause [conj]; D2-opus §18 for the identity). A tagged the whole clause [conj]; B split it; B kept.
5. **`survassure` example parameters.** Recomputed from the closed forms: $(0.90,0.224,0.134,0.28)$, dominance; low delegation 0.156; leader 0.424 and 0.110. They match `../v4/checks_v44.log` (SV). B's parameters kept.
6. **Block labels.** In `checks_v62.py`, FS is the frontier-switch toy and SR the silent route; A's "[num: FS]" for `preventown` and "SR" for the horizon result point to the wrong v6.2 blocks. Labelled "legacy v5, FS/SR/N13/CMP", "legacy v4.4, SV", "legacy v4.5, LD", each checked against its log.
7. **Who stops the sprinter's freeze.** "The freeze is not held" (A: reversible, back to the race) conflates the holder breaking it (others' O2 at its discretion: irreversible) with rivals catching up (a contest again: reversible). Split into two rows.
8. **Fallback and the durability tests.** Bounded own risk concerns the arrangement's own hazards while it lasts (capture, restriction, collusion), not its hazard of breakdown (D2); only "the first condition is D1" is stated.
9. **G7 and delegation.** Table 7.1's delegation row is reversed by narrow E1 and kept human capacity, which is pace bound to understanding applied to decisions. A's sentence that the marks do not protect a generative order from delegating its decisions away contradicts "each reverses a threshold" (D1-opus) and is not carried; E1 remains an arrangement of §11.3.

Evidence decisions (not derivations): Jones's γ=2 figure (EV11); EV10's 48%; Bueno de Mesquita et al. local commitment result (EV11, D4 F1–F2); Abecassis moved (D4 G1); EV2 wording (D4 E3, E4, Verification #2).
