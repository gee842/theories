## 2. Disagreements resolved by derivation

1. **`raceselects`(2) needs conditional independence.** With $X\perp W\mid E$, $\Pr(X\mid W)-\Pr(X)=[\Pr(X\mid E)-\Pr(X\mid\neg E)][\Pr(E\mid W)-\Pr(E)]$. Without it the correlation premise is not enough: with $\Pr(E)=\frac12$, $\Pr(W\mid E)=0.8$, $\Pr(W\mid\neg E)=0.2$, $\Pr(X\mid E,W)=0.1$, $\Pr(X\mid E,\neg W)=1$, $\Pr(X\mid\neg E)=0.1$, the premise holds ($0.28>0.1$) but $\Pr(X\mid W)=0.1<\Pr(X)=0.19$. A's statement is kept; B's (V6.1's) omits the premise.
2. **"Near parity is where standing is priced highest."** Checked against MP (c), (g), (h), (i) in `checks_v62.log`. The summed price at parity exceeds the summed price at a 3:1 split in all 14 settings of MP (c) (closest: $r=1$, $\Gamma=0.3$: 0.387 against 0.371). The leader's own price at 0.75 exceeds parity in one setting ($r=1$, $\Gamma=0.3$: 0.2703 against 0.1934). Total effort peaks at parity (MP (i)). B's support (amplification and "a laggard at a quarter share prices standing at 25 to 57 per cent") does not establish "highest", and the 25–57% range holds only for $r=1$ (for $r=4$ it is 5–8%). Merged core states the summed form, effort and laggard, with the condition "a lead discourages the follower"; S7 records the leader exception.
3. **Branch I′.** At $p_d=0$, (14) reduces to $(1-\delta)g_i\le\delta(C_i-v_i)$; B's "$D_i\le C_i$ above the level" is not needed and not implied.
4. **Branch II, delegation.** In `delegate`'s model the gain from delegating is $L_e[w_i(1,k)-w_i(0,k)]$, proportional to $L_e$, and the cost does not depend on $L_e$, so the dominance speed rises as $L_e/L_c$ falls (A). B's "the gain from speed is bounded by the time a decision has before it is answered" has no counterpart in that model.
5. **`racepremise`(1).** `join` gives the best response only where $S_i<P_i$ (B omitted it; A wrote "can be"). With merged `enforce` per deviation, repetition restores restraint only where $p_d(a)\le\bar p_{d,i}(a)$ for every actor and deviation.
6. **Talk at $\theta=-1$.** Merged `talk`(1)–(2): effective bias $b_0(n-1-\theta)/(n-1+\theta)$, unbounded at $n=2$, $b_0n/(n-2)$ for $n\ge3$. The table row states both cases (A).
7. **Capture danger side.** `corrrate`(1): certified iff $d>d^\ast$, so the side that is not certified is $d\le d^\ast$ (both drafts wrote $d<d^\ast$).
8. **Turnover of capacity in enforcement (P11).** $\Lambda^\ast-1=(d_K+\delta_f)(\Gamma-1)$ rises with $d_K$ iff $\Gamma>1$, so the sign is ± (A), not ↑ where $\Gamma>1$ only (B).
9. **Load column for $r$ and the stakes.** B marked Load ↑ for decisiveness and stakes (intensity ∝ $r\Omega$). The Load threshold ($\tau_{\rm eff}<\nu$, `load`) contains neither; intensity belongs to the race channel of Coverage. A's "—" kept.
10. **$\chi$ in Table 7.1.** Counted in the merged table: every row but delegation, five of eight through †. A's "a parameter moved one way moves every threshold it enters toward the same side" fails for P8 (diffusion of weights: Comp ↓, Tax ↑) and patience, so B's weaker sentence is used.
11. **Wider held self-model (P20, Standing).** By (9), $(1+\theta)/(n-1-\theta)$ is proportional to $(1+\theta_0)/(n-1-\theta_0)$, so the working self-model is wider at every $\Gamma_\delta$; the sign ↓ is for the level, not for the size of the narrowing (U03 item: not monotone).
12. **The drive and provision.** B's overlap sentence said only the held part is a question for provision. Provision lowers the endogenous part as a theorem (it lowers $\Gamma_\delta$ through the won share; SP(H)); only for the held part is it a crux (C8). Corrected.
13. **`delayosc`.** CH (e): modulus 1.0075, period 51.6, $|y(600)|=292$. The V6.1 systems review's "111×" is superseded by the log.
14. **Laggard's quarter-share price** and **LG** (0.8 + 0.3 = 1.1; gap 3.96 after 4,000 periods), **MP** ($\Gamma^{\rm eq}$ 1.11 at 0.45, 2.83 at 0.6), **CH** (1.2857 → 1.0000; 1.125 → 0.750; 1.75 → 2.25; 0.857 → 0.964; $\Gamma_\delta$ 0.853, 1.157, 1.409), **SP (H)** (0.5846 → 0.8058), **growprize** threshold: all checked against `checks_v62.log` and the merged S2–S3.
