## 2. Disagreements decided by derivation

1. **`signal`(2).** V6.1 and B: "if $k_R<G_R$ … the receiver restrains after $E$ only if $1-\mu\ge\varpi^\ast$". A: false. Check: with $1-\mu<\varpi^\ast$, let $\mathsf S$ always send, $\mathsf R$ send with $\sigma_{\mathsf R}=(1-\mu)(1-\varpi^\ast)/(\mu\varpi^\ast)<1$ (which needs exactly $1-\mu<\varpi^\ast$). The posterior after $\mathsf E$ is $\varpi^\ast$, so the receiver may mix; $\rho_{\mathsf E}=c_{\mathsf R}/\Pi_{\mathsf R}$ makes $\mathsf R$ indifferent; after $\mathsf N$ the posterior is 0. $\mathsf S$ sends iff $c_{\mathsf S}\le\Pi_{\mathsf S}c_{\mathsf R}/\Pi_{\mathsf R}$. So restraint after the step has positive probability although prior trust falls short. The upper bound $\rho_{\mathsf E}\le c_{\mathsf R}/\Pi_{\mathsf R}$ in every equilibrium was checked case by case (posteriors average to the prior; if $\rho_{\mathsf E}$ exceeded the bound, $\mathsf R$ would always send and the posterior after $\mathsf E$ would be at most $1-\mu$). V6.1's clause holds only among pure-strategy equilibria. A's statement taken, with the attainment condition added to (2).
2. **Dwell in depth.** A: monotonicity "observed in every case computed, not proved". B: the insulated $\dot u$ does not depend on $u$, so a deeper start stays deeper by the same $\Delta u$. Completed here: $\tilde z(t)$ does not depend on $u_0$, the field is cooperative (`fieldlemma`), so the basin of absence is a down-set (as S4 uses for `pausefield`); hence $T_{\min}$ is nondecreasing in $u_0$. The down-set contains no state with $u>\operatorname{logit}x_\sigma$, and $u(T)\ge u_0-\zeta cT$, so $T_{\min}\ge(u_0-\operatorname{logit}x_\sigma)/(\zeta c)$: unbounded in depth, and depth grows at $\zeta c(G-1)$ with age. Under a floor the capped path dominates every path from below, so the dwell is bounded. And for $x_0>x_\sigma$, the state when shares are back at $x_0$ (and at every earlier time) has $u\ge u_0>\operatorname{logit}x_\sigma$, so it is outside the basin of absence: "levels improve before safety does" is now [thm], the numbers [num: DW]. Checked against DW(a), (b), (e): 0.99 exceeds $x_\sigma$ (0.984 at most) in every case printed, and every dwell exceeds its $s^\ast$.
3. **`commonfrontn` tag** in Table 11.5: B [thm], A [num]. Merged §10 states it as [num] (one parameterization). A.
4. **Non-proliferation row.** B: "a stop imposed by a leading coalition ends others' O2". A: "need not end their O2". Merged X8 l.53 relies on this row saying the latter, and Adv-O made that point. A, with B's "does not end the contest among its members".
5. **Frozen-share pause length.** B states it in `pausevalue` and in `dwell`(4); merged `pausefield` Scope (U04) and S4 already hold it with the same DW numbers. One home: pointer only (A).
6. **E3 and `corrrate`.** B cites `corrrate` for "correction whose productivity AI raises as fast as it lowers the cost of control" as a route to E3. `corrrate` concerns the capture of correctors; coverage of hazardous variety is §5.3's channel, "symmetric with `corrrate`". Replaced by crux C3a.
7. **The staying-out identity.** B dropped it as unchecked. Substituting $q_{\rm II}=q_W=0$, $q_{\rm I}=p_{\rm dec}=1-\varepsilon$, $n=2$, $p_{\rm ext}=1$ into `survassure`'s closed forms gives $\Phi^P=\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)/2$ and $\Phi^S=1-p^S_{\rm dec}$; $\varepsilon^2<\varepsilon(1+\varepsilon)/2$ for $\varepsilon<1$. Kept in X11 as [thm, by substitution; num: TR] (TR(h) checks it).
8. **`readrule`(2).** A: Wald's bounds hold "whatever the noise". They hold where the log-likelihood ratio is that of the readings' distribution; TR's SPRT uses the true σ. Condition added.
9. **`relaxtrust`(3).** A: exit deterred while $\upsilon F\ge\Delta V$. That is the irreversible public exit of `overhang`(1); covert preparation needs $\upsilon F\ge(1-\upsilon)\Delta V$. Statement names the case; proof gives both.
10. **A's "safe step" claim** in §11.1 (see the table): too broad as stated; narrowed.
