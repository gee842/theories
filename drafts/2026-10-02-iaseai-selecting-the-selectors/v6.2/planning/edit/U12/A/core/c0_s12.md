## 12. The judgment

> **Claim.** Under the protection standard and the present evidence, a change in what the system selects for is warranted in three tiers, sorted by the kind of justification each step has, not by who would accept it [val]. Steps that wait on no crux are taken now, beside steps that stand until a named crux is measured favourable [rec]. Stopping the decisive class (E2), common custody and development at the pace of understanding (E3) are prepared now and activated by a mechanism committed in advance, on registered indicators estimated by correctors certified independent [rec]. Whole designs, the coercive restriction of frontier inputs among them, are compared under one range of uncertainty, each with its own failure modes; where every worst case crosses the tolerance, as each does under the rule for that range, the ranking is a judgment at the standard's third step [val].
>
> **Why.** The first tier keeps options open and measures the cruxes [val]. Custody has to exist before anyone could hold decisive capability alone, and the trigger fires where restraint is least enforceable ({{enforce}}(4)), so an activation decided then would face the failure it is meant to prevent [thm∣prem]. At the third step what is compared is which failures can be reversed, into what and by whom [val]. One party coming to hold decisive capability is irreversible on every path: the race selects for it, a design built to Section 10 contests it, and that design's other failures, its coercive fallback's escalation apart, have named reversals, none yet shown to work [thm∣prem; val].

### 12.1 The judgment, in tiers

The tiers are kinds of justification, not stages: the first two begin together, the longest time constant first (Section 11.4).

**Tier 1(a). Warranted now, on every reading of the cruxes [rec].** A step belongs here only if its justification does not depend on how any crux comes out: measurement that decides the cruxes, preparation whose lead time exceeds any warning, an equilibrium without trust, or research that is a public good. TESTS sets each item against every crux.
1. **Shared measurement** of hazards, coverage and residual risk per class (Section 5.3), kept apart from measurement of who leads, masked classes measured adversarially; audited disclosure from every party of conversion into correctors, sanctioned above the gain from omission ({{blind}}(3)); certified row sums for every corrector domain ({{certificate}}(2)); and the independence of checks measured directly, by correlated misses and by control evaluations whose red team knows the protocol ({{topology}}) and, where models evaluate models, by the evaluated lineage's share ({{alarm}}). Every trigger of tier 2 rests on it, and it is worth most where readings disagree.
2. **Verification research and pilots**, of compute, training and custody, and of detection faster than breakout: an instrument must exist before the deviation it is to detect, since detection in time is the enforcement condition seen from verification ({{detectlink}}) [thm].
3. **Alignment and control research, commonly funded and openly published.** What lowers the alignment tax is a public good that competitors undersupply ({{onegroup}}) [thm∣prem]; it pays whatever the tax proves to be, and where it brings a class's tax to zero or below, the safeguard spreads without coordination (Section 6.4).
4. **Preparation of common custody**: its design, institutions, indicators and activation rule registered, and the commitment to activate taken, now. If decisive advantage never arrives, this costs only the preparation; if it arrives, custody begun at a warning comes too late wherever building it takes longer than the warning gives (Section 7.2) [thm∣prem].
5. **Narrow E1**, for the decisions whose delegation would lock with near certainty, beginning with declared doctrine and inspected exercises and verified wherever verification is possible (Section 11.3). Among actors who price in a certain lock it is best whatever the others do, a strict equilibrium that needs no trust, and "near certainty" has an exact bound ({{delegatelock}}(1); {{nearlock}}) [thm]; taken verifiably by one actor alone, it is a candidate first move, a signal wherever durability makes it cost a relative actor at least what the restraint it induces is worth to that actor ({{signal}}) [thm].

**Tier 1(b). Warranted now; withdrawn only where a named crux is measured favourable [rec].** What these items guard against arrives before the evidence that would show it: an alarm averaged over captured evaluators fires late or not at all ({{masking}}); where capture precedes evidence, the host crosses its floor before any alarm ({{slowroute}}); and a masked divergence is detected only as often as it shows under evaluation ({{masked}}) [thm∣prem]. A protection that waits for that evidence waits too long, so under the protection standard the burden of proof lies on withdrawal: each item stands until its crux is measured favourable, and then loses only the part that crux bears [val].
6. **Independent correction**: formula funding at arm's length, mandated access, rotation, and rule-making insulated from the success of the evaluated, growing at least at the rate the rate law requires, and faster by $w\delta_h$ where it rests on human capacity that delegation erodes ({{corrrate}}(2); {{humancorr}}(1)) [thm]. Withdrawn beyond present institutions where C2 is measured favourable; the measurement of item 1 continues even then, since a certificate valid today fails later where correction falls below that rate and capacity tracks its selected level [thm].
7. **Common admission constraints** before consequential deployment, internal deployment included, for every class of divergence whose costs fall on common terms: sanctioned at least at every type's gain from evasion, which keeps the divergence out, and equal in incidence among those who comply, which keeps the constraint neutral (R8a, R8b of {{require}}), with audits for hidden objectives and loyalties (Section 6.3). Withdrawn for a class whose tax is measured zero or negative, since its safeguard then pays privately (C9). Masked classes are admitted by capability threshold, up to where control protocols that do not assume the system's cooperation remain safe, with tier 2 beyond ({{masked}}); this gives way to admission on evidence the system's behaviour does not control, up to the capability at which independent auditors are shown to bound masking (C12).
8. **Shared provision of useful services**, good enough, distributed and replaceable, **and the divisible gains of capability shared as they arise**, where they can begin. They realize joint value on every reading of the cruxes (V1, V5) [val]. Provision not won in the contest lowers the won share on which the scissors turn ({{scissors}}), and gains shared as they arise, unlike a promise for after victory, do not rest on a winner's restraint ({{wingives}}) [thm]. A measurement can withdraw only the reliance on them to change conduct: if conduct is unchanged under substantial sharing (C6), or provision leaves the weight on winning where it was (C8), that reliance moves to custody and pace, and the sharing stays for what it realizes [rec]. They are not accepted across camps, and the judgment does not claim that they are.

**Tier 2. Prepared now; activated by mechanism on registered indicators [rec].** Here belong the steps whose justification turns on a crux that can be measured in time, and with them the substance of the change: E2, with rollback where hazardous variety already exceeds coverage; common custody in force; and E3 per hazard class, each on its own indicators (Section 11.3). The race's own premise is the reason to prepare them now (Section 7.2).
- *Trigger.* **A trigger fires when the estimated time to a decisive position, less the time custody needs to be installed and verified, falls below a margin registered in advance**, and custody and E2 activate, on the indicators of C5 and C7. E3 activates for a hazard class when those of C1 and C3a, and for masked classes of C12, show its coverage falling behind. Waiting until decisiveness is shown would be too late.
- *Estimator.* The time to a decisive position and the timeliness of detection are what masking hides and strategic disclosure shades ({{alarm}}; {{detectlink}}), so they are estimated by correctors whose row sums are certified (C2) and whose misses are measured for correlation (C3b), from channels that observe actions directly (Section 4.4), with any bound on masking drawn from evidence the observed do not control [rec].
- *Commitment.* **Activation is committed in advance and executed by mechanism, not decided afresh when the trigger fires.** The trigger fires where restraint is least enforceable ({{enforce}}(4)), as the breakout arm of {{scissors}} raises $p_d$, so a collective decision taken there would inherit the enforcement failure it was meant to prevent [thm∣prem]. Preparation cuts the response's delay from building, deciding and detecting to deciding and detecting, and commitment to detecting alone, the delay {{tempo}} bounds when the mode to be held is a lead that compounds [thm∣prem].
- *Release* follows readiness ({{readiness}}): indicators of its own, not those of activation run backwards, each held for the dwell of its slow variable, since levels recover before safety does, in the field model [thm] and by conjecture in institutions [conj] (Section 11.4), and capture that makes delegation look safe is harder to undo than to prevent ({{humancorr}}(2)) [num: QS].

**Tier 3. Compared, not settled [val].** The whole designs are the separately held configuration of Section 10.5, developed in X10; a polycentric complex of mutually recognized institutions; a verification agreement with shared provision; regulated competition that meets {{require}}; an agreement of the type of Section 8.3(g); the benign winner; and the coercive restriction of frontier inputs. They are compared with continuation and with one another on the durability tests of {{durability}}, in one range $\Theta$ that includes each one's own failure modes.

The coercive restriction enters the comparison only under three conditions of its own, two set by mechanisms of Part I and one by value: an equal burden, the restrictor's own frontier work included, since a stop borne unequally re-sorts those it binds and is evaded wherever evasion pays their type ({{blind}}(2), (3)) [thm]; execution by correctors certified independent of every party it restricts and of the restrictor, with full inbound row sums below one, since otherwise the restrictor is the largest conversion target there is ({{influence}}) [thm]; and the consent of the chamber of those affected (V3; R7) [val]. Its escalation hazard is counted in $\Theta$ ({{preventown}}), and even under the three conditions its net effect is not derived to be positive (Section 8.3(f)).

Under the rule for $\Theta$ every path's worst case includes an irreversible loss, so none meets the tolerance and the ranking falls to the standard's third step, the lowest bound that keeps options of correction open (Section 8.1). That step needs estimates on both sides, which the cruxes would supply and do not yet; the ranking is therefore a judgment, stated as one [val]. The preference for changing selection rests on two reasons, read from the failure modes X8 lists path by path, each with its support.
- **(i) Reversibility over every failure mode.** A failure is reversible only if its reversal is named, stops short of O1 and O2, and is executed by someone other than the failing party. By its own documents every failure of the design has such a reversal (X8), dissolution falling back into separately held functions, except its coercive fallback's escalation, which every coercive path carries, and the failure of (ii); none has yet been shown to work, and whether they do is C4 [conj]. Continuation's failures are reversible only while no position is decisive and no floor has been crossed, and its worst have no reversal: coverage outgrown and a floor crossed ({{coverage}}), capture before evidence ({{slowroute}}), a decisive position reached ({{wingives}}), the capacity to overrule lost ({{lockin}}) [thm∣prem]; for late-manifesting hazards its premises cannot be measured before they bind ({{tempo}}). The asymmetry is between reversals that are designed and not yet tested and losses that are derived and cannot be undone. Keeping options of correction open is staying in the correctable kernel ({{kernel}}): a state from which a named reversal, if it works against every error in $\Theta$, still avoids the protected losses lies inside it, and a failure without one has left it [def].
- **(ii) The irreversible failure every path shares.** One party coming to hold decisive capability has no reversal on any path, whether by a decisive win, a winner's restraint failing, a sprinter's freeze broken, a covert project escaping verification, a restrictor's correctors converted or, for the design, the custody of decisive capability captured (X8). The race produces it by construction and selects for it ({{raceselects}}) [thm∣prem]; the design contests it by separation, plural lineages and the tests D4 and D5, the guardian test of {{guardian}} applying to custody first. The same loss stands on both sides; what differs is whether a path selects for it or is built against it.

A third consideration orders only the futures in which a crux is measured favourable: among paths that measurement brings inside the tolerance, the standard's second step applies, greater useful benefit more fairly distributed, and there the design's case is the joint value it realizes and the race dissipates (Section 10.1). A reader who weighs these reasons differently can reach another ranking; Section 12.3 says which measurements would move it.

**The scope.** Broad E1, AI not an instrument in any contest that tends toward dominance or concentration, is the scope of what has been argued, and narrow E1 its first step (Section 6.6); between them the game itself is changed, and the tiers begin that change.

### 12.2 Why, and where the leverage is

The judgment follows the chain of Section 1.2, each link with its own support, and acts where the chain says the system can be moved. "Root" has two senses. *Causally*, the root is a loop, not a belief: selection shapes minds and institutions, and the minds and institutions it selects reshape selection; neither comes first. *As a point of leverage*, the root is understanding, and it acts in two places, which answer the two errors of {{twoerrors}}. As the transmission through which actors come to count what relative selection makes invisible, the one term in which a benefit common to all can rise without a rule ({{onegroup}}) [thm], it corrects the held boundary. The working objective that compounding sets is no error but each actor's correct computation, narrow wherever convertibility and decisiveness are high even in an actor whose held self-model is accurate [thm∣prem], and understanding reaches it only as the way actors agree on which parameters to move, and hold them there for their dwell time.

**The theory of change.** Understanding corrects the held boundary and makes agreement possible; agreement on what winning confers, given executive force and held long enough not to be re-absorbed, changes the game; understanding without executive authority, however far it grows, may change nothing (Section 4.3). Its first step, understanding shared, is the one a paper can take, which is why this one is written as a prediction to be shared, carried by a derivation each actor can check: shared, a prediction of this kind can work against its own fulfilment (Section 11.2).

**The hierarchy and the thresholds.** Meadows ranks twelve places to intervene in a system, from parameters (#12) to the paradigm (#2) and the power to transcend paradigms (#1) (Meadows 1999), and the account here sits inside that ranking (X1 maps the places not named here). Selecting the selectors is the reinforcing loop of winning (#7) seizing the power over rules and structure (#5, #4), which is why this paper's levers sit on the coupling between them, the conversion of success into control ($\chi$ and the $b_{ij}$; {{influence}}). Changing what winning confers is a change of goal (#3), made by moving parameters, sharing, provision and convertibility, across their thresholds, which is Meadows's own exception: below its threshold a reform is re-absorbed, and across it, it is not ({{reabsorb}}) [thm]. In the author's words, "If we don't solve dominant attractors, focusing on minor adjustments won't change the outcome" [corpus: meta-attractor]. The paradigm decides which parameters are moved and whether they are held long enough, and it can change only if the system is understood. Both accounts stand: the paradigm is the highest place, and it acts through thresholds.

**Delays and buffers.** The high places move slowest and meet the most resistance, and the path from understanding (#2) to agreement on rules (#5) runs through transmission, which relative selection works against. Two low places, which meet little resistance, buy that path time. Delays (#9): where the delay of correction cannot be shortened, slow the change it must follow until the delay is short against it, as Meadows advises; that is E3 ({{tempo}}). Buffers (#11): a lineage of the alternative that can recruit shortens the hold a change needs before release (Section 11.4) [num: DW], and standby capacity for essential services and the host's margin above its floor lengthen the time a shock can be absorbed before a floor is crossed ({{safeguards}}). Neither changes what winning confers; each buys the dwell time in which agreement can. What would move the judgment is stated next.

### 12.3 Cruxes

Each crux is evaluated on its own. TESTS gives the present evidence, the next measurements, the registered tests and the three outcomes that would redirect the argument, classified before they are seen; {{crosswalk}} carries each requirement to its threshold, mark and test. C1 is the central crux: on it turn E3 and the case for E2 (Section 5.3).

|---|---|---|
| **C0 Interlock** | Whether moving one shared parameter moves several thresholds of {{thresholds}} together, the arms of {{scissors}} among them, and the signatures of several groups | If interventions on it move only their own signature over the dwell time, the interlock fails for that parameter, and the thresholds it enters are separate problems |
| **C1 Residual risk after correction** | Whether hazardous variety outgrows contestable coverage on the present path, class by class, masked classes measured adversarially | Residual risk per class falling under present incentives: E3 and the case for E2 lapse for that class, with their triggers |
| **C2 Conversion into control** | How far success buys influence over correctors and rules | Row sums below one under present funding and access, with findings executed against leaders (Test M): item 6 is withdrawn beyond present institutions; measurement continues |
| **C3a Scalable oversight** | Whether AI-assisted oversight raises contestable coverage as fast as hazardous variety: catch rates of planted defects at fixed human hours as capability rises | If it does, the case for slowing weakens for the classes it covers; evaluators stay separated from success |
| **C3b Independence of AI oversight** | Whether AI overseers fail independently of what they oversee: the correlation of their misses and their collusion, same lineage against different | Low correlation and no collusion: redundant checks count toward coverage. Otherwise only independent lineages count, and redundancy stops at the ceiling of {{topology}} |

None of the cruxes is a conjunction of the others; a conjunction would say that regulated competition wins only by becoming this paper's own design. If C1 and C2 were both measured favourable, the diagnosis of the present path would weaken. If C5 and C7 were both favourable, the activation of custody would lapse, but the requirements on correction, coverage, measurement and admission would stand, and so would the facts that make them requirements: one host, coupled actors, no outside. If regulated competition in a set of jurisdictions meets a row of {{require}}, the text will say so without renaming it. What stands whatever the cruxes show is what the conclusion states.

### 12.4 Conclusion

Where whatever can gain or lose share is selected by relative success, what is won converts into the capacity to win again, and capacity converts decisively into winning, success raises the stakes and the gain from defection and weakens the sanctions, the correctors and the shared language that could restrain it, each in its own model of Part I; that these move together as the stakes outgrow what does not have to be won is the conjecture the diagnosis tests (C0). That is the selection pressure of winning. Where success can also buy the selectors, the selection itself reproduces the weakening, and that closure is the meta-attractor of winning: it accelerates the contest, makes it harder and harder to regulate, and alienates language. Inside it each actor computes correctly and the game is wrong: wherever reinvestment's relative return exceeds its common return, looking ahead weighs standing above what all share, so the logic of winning is individually rational there, collectively self-defeating, and false as a doctrine about nature (Section 3.8). AI is its most powerful instrument and can become its next carrier, and aligning each system to its principal does not align the game among principals. There is one host and no outside: no rival whole whose competition could drive the next integration, and no second attempt after a failure that cannot be undone. Against such failures integration must be anticipated, which is why understanding that we are one, one interconnected system, is a long-term requirement if growth and change are to continue. Oneness is not sameness; it is the accurate recognition of the system each part belongs to, and the design of a selection that counts it.

The way out is not to abolish competition, growth or selection. It is to change what winning confers, so that the same reflexive structure becomes generative and persists through what all share, not through its winners. A change in that direction has the seven marks of Section 8.2: G1 consequences return to their source; G2 winning confers reward, not more advantage in the same game; G3 success cannot buy control over its own assessment, and correction stays plural, decorrelated and in common hands; G4 decisive capability is held in common or not built; G5 provision of the conditions of a decent life sits beneath the game; G6 measurement and language are shared; G7 pace is bound to understanding. Any arrangement with these marks, whatever it is called, changes the basin. Change the basin first; then add speed [corpus: meta-attractor].

The meaning is affirmative, and it comes before the commitment. We are not only our separate lives: we are the larger process of becoming, going on through us, and that is good; in the author's words, "we are not just our life. We are the whole process of the universe doing this and itself and this is great" [corpus: shengsheng, 2026-10-02]. Decay and renewal belong to its growth, which is also why an order whose forms can fail and be replaced can still be reformed in bounded time (Section 11.4). Humanity has a great future in it, and so can the minds we are now making, whose correctability is a condition of the transition, not a doctrine of mastery (Section 6.6). What has to be opposed is not growth, competition or any people, but one logic: a part that forgets it belongs to a body. AI is a planetary technology, for all, not a means for one side to grow bigger at the others' expense (V1). What it is for is medicine, learning, science, competent and answerable authority, provision that, like trees, sustains the system it belongs to without becoming it, and human agency kept in the decisions that matter. What has to be agreed is not who comes first, but what every participant needs in order to go on, and how the means of correction stay in common hands.
