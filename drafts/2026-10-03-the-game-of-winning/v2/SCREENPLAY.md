# THE GAME OF WINNING

*Screenplay for v2. Five minutes, no narration. From the conversation Ren recorded on a flight on 2026-10-01 (`sources/18-friend-conversations/2026-10-01-airplane/`). Every date and number on screen comes from `../trajectory.py`.*

---

## What the film says

**Logline.** If we keep playing the game we are playing now, the winning logic becomes self-replicating through automation. It consumes the Earth, then the Sun, then the galaxy, and then itself. Nobody wins.

**The question the film poses.** Each step is a victory: more compute, more power, more reach. The film asks what all this winning is for. It never says the answer. The instruments keep reporting success, the sky shows the cost, and in the end nothing is left to keep score.

**What it must never become.** It must not be a story about an evil AI. No machine face, no robot, no villain. Ren's distinction is that superintelligence does not have to lead here ("superintelligence 不一定 lead to……"). The protagonist is the logic. The opening word is the promise made in its name.

## The five threads

The film holds together because five things run through it from the first minute to the last. Each one is introduced, transformed, and paid off.

| Thread | Introduced | Transformed | Paid off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The line**: the logic, drawn as thin amber light. Everything artificial is line; the world is rendered realistically. | The underline of the opening word becomes the edge of the Earth. | Transmission lines, launch tracks, mass-driver streams, the relay beam, probe paths, the galactic front | The weapons: beams between stars. The last two beams cross, and then there is no line. |
| **The panel**: the game's instruments, YEAR, POWER and POPULATION | Appears at *today*, 2026, with a TODAY tick where real data ends and the projection begins | POPULATION falls, then leaves the panel. POWER climbs to its maximum. PLAYERS appears at the split. | PLAYERS 0, POWER 0 W. The panel switches off. |
| **The sky**: the same events seen from below, by people | 2050: satellites crowd out the stars above a campus | 2113: a dome under steam columns. 2291: two suns. | The last sky: the stars go out one by one until it is black. |
| **The shape**: one cancer, the same branching growth front at every scale | Data-centre clusters spread across the continents at night | A lesion across Mercury; a lesion across an alien world | A front crossing the whole galaxy |
| **The sound**: the compounding clock, one click per doubling of POWER | Single clicks, 2026 | The clicks quicken as the years compress, and a tone rises under them with the power. In the galaxy the clicks stop and the tone flattens into a drone. | It fractures at the split, then beams, then silence. |

**The word.** "Super intelligence" opens the film and returns once, over the dead galaxy. *(The real clip comes from Trump's address to the UN General Assembly, 22 September 2026. Until Ren sources it, the word appears as a caption, with no imitated voice.)*

## ACT ONE · THE WORD

### 1. THE WORD
**0:00–0:12 · BLACK**

Black. The low room tone of a vast hall.

A caption fades in, small, lower third: *United Nations General Assembly · 22 September 2026*. Then the words, centred, as a speech caption: **super intelligence**. *(When the real clip arrives, his voice says it here and the caption goes.)*

A thin amber underline draws itself beneath the words, left to right. The words fade. The line keeps going and curves, and the curve becomes the night edge of the Earth. We are in orbit.

SOUND: room tone, then the first click of the clock as the line becomes the planet.

> From the talk: "从开头，你就直接把前几帧剪成川普在那里说：'Superintelligence。'"

---

## ACT TWO · EARTH

### 2. TODAY
**0:12–0:20 · ORBIT, NIGHT SIDE · 2026**

The real Earth at night: Black Marble city lights, the real coastlines. The camera drifts east over North America. Among the city lights, the real data-centre regions pulse amber: Northern Virginia, Dallas, Phoenix, Abilene, Columbus, Oregon. Then Dublin, Frankfurt, Singapore, Beijing and Mumbai as the planet turns.

The panel fades in at the frame's edges:

PANEL: YEAR **2026** ▸ TODAY · POWER **62 GW** · POPULATION **8.2 bn**

SOUND: click. Five seconds. Click.

> "然后逐渐地，你就从这个地球视角向外放，然后你就逐渐地可以看到地球上，突然……出现了各种各样的东西。"

### 3. THE COLONY
**0:20–0:34 · ORBIT · 2026 → 2046**

Time starts to run. Amber lines grow out of each region along the real grids and highways and branch like a colony on a dish. New nodes light up where gas plants and reactors appear beside them. Three shots, each wider and faster than the last:
- North America;
- Eurasia from above the Arctic;
- the whole night side, laced with amber.

CARD (large, centred, one second): **2046 · 1 TW**

SOUND: the clicks come every three seconds.

> "然后地球的表面，出现了各种各样的这种，就是 super data center。"

### 4. THE SKY · I
**0:34–0:39 · GROUND, NIGHT · 2050**

Ground level. The black silhouette of a hyperscale campus to the horizon, with red aviation lights blinking on its roofs. Above it, a hazy, washed-out sky with few stars and dozens of moving satellite lights in trains. A launch climbs from the horizon on a column of light.

SOUND: a distant rumble under the clock.

### 5. ORBIT
**0:39–0:48 · LOW ORBIT · 2050 → 2068**

Launch tracks stitch the surface to orbit. Compute constellations thicken from scattered points into shells of light around the planet, with radiators glinting as they pass the terminator.

CARD: **2068 · 19 TW** *(compute alone equals all the energy humanity uses today)*

> "然后在这个过程中，有一天它就开始向外发射一些东西。" "从马斯克的轨道数据站开始。"

### 6. THE HEAT
**0:48–0:58 · ORBIT, THERMAL VIEW · 2068 → 2097**

The image switches to a thermal camera. The night side glows: dull red, then orange around the colonies, with isotherms creeping toward the poles. The amber lines still grow over it.

The POPULATION figure turns and starts to fall.

CARD: **2097 · waste heat 2 W/m²**

> "随着能源的使用的上升，然后随着地球需要的降温的能力越来越高，……没有办法再用这种，就是环境控温，然后改变二氧化碳浓度之类的方式去保持散热了。"

### 7. THE OCEANS
**0:58–1:12 · ORBIT, THE LIMB · 2097 → 2113**

On the coasts, lines of mass drivers fire. White plumes of seawater arc off the planet at the limb, dozens at once, in a slow rhythm. Below, rain belts wrap the planet. The shorelines retreat; the dark blue of the seas shrinks to grey basins.

PANEL: POPULATION falls faster: 6.1 bn … 3.4 bn … 0.9 bn.

CARD: **2113 · 10 PW**

> "然后这种超级智能会开始通过把海洋，然后变成高温蒸汽，喷射到外太空的方式，来给整个地球降温。" "这种地质现象是因为这个逻辑需要扩张它本身，所以说它在 consume Earth。"

### 8. THE SKY · II
**1:12–1:22 · A DRIED SEABED · 2120**

A glass dome on the cracked floor of what was a sea. Inside: a garden, warm light, a few figures, beautiful. Outside: compute fields to the horizon under a white sky, with steam columns rising out of sight.

One word appears in the panel's type, quiet, lower third: **PROGRESS**

PANEL: POPULATION **42 million**

SOUND: the clock muffled, as if heard through glass.

> "而人类只能生活在一些小的泡泡的温室里面。然后这就是 progress。" "还有一小帮人住在一个非常优美的玻璃罩子里面。"

### 9. NOT ENOUGH
**1:22–1:30 · HIGH ORBIT · 2132**

The whole Earth from far away: amber-laced, its seas grey, wrapped in light. Two launch tracks leave it and reach inward, toward Mercury and Venus.

CARD: **2132 · all the sunlight the Earth receives**

---

## ACT THREE · THE SUN

### 10. MERCURY
**1:30–1:44 · MERCURY · 2132 → 2170**

The real Mercury, MESSENGER's cratered grey. A seed lands. From the landing point a lesion spreads, dark and scarred inside, bright amber at its branching edge: the colony shape again. Mass drivers along the scar fire streams of ore into orbit as lines. The planet thins and unravels into a ring of debris around the Sun.

> "然后它首先射到了水星和金星，然后开始在这两个行星上面，然后去制造大量的自动化工程能力，然后不断地把这两颗行星拆解。"

### 11. VENUS
**1:44–1:50 · VENUS**

The pale cloud ball. The same lesion tears the clouds open. A second ring.

### 12. THE SWARM
**1:50–2:04 · THE SUN · 2170 → 2291**

Collectors pour out of the rings into orbit around the Sun, glittering, hundreds of thousands of them. The Sun's light dims behind them. The image switches to infrared: the Sun is no longer white but a dull red ember, glowing with waste heat.

CARD: **2291 · 3.8 × 10²⁶ W · the Sun**

SOUND: the clicks come fast now, with the tone at its height under them.

> "拆解了之后开始造戴森球。"

### 13. THE RELAY
**2:04–2:10 · CISLUNAR SPACE**

A beam leaves the swarm and crosses to the Moon. The Moon's near side blazes white.

> "然后把一部分的能量逐渐地直接以光速地往月球传输，做一个中转站。"

### 14. THE SKY · III
**2:10–2:17 · THE DOME, DAY · 2291**

From beside the dome: a daytime sky with two suns. High up, the Moon blazes, too bright to look at. Lower, the real Sun, dull red behind a faint shimmer of collectors. Two figures by the glass look up.

The POPULATION line fades from the panel and does not return.

> "然后月球就是，比如说在白天也有了一颗太阳般的……"

### 15. LAUNCH
**2:17–2:25 · THE SOLAR SYSTEM → THE ORION ARM**

From the swarm, thousands of probe lines radiate in every direction. The camera pulls back fast. The solar system shrinks to an amber point in a spiral arm of the galaxy.

> "然后再从那里开始，它就把各种各样的这种飞船，这种 space probe，射进了银河系。"

---

## ACT FOUR · THE GALAXY

### 16. THE CELL
**2:25–2:48 · ANOTHER STAR · +9,000 YEARS → +12,000**

The film's founding image, held longest and drawn most closely.

A small craft falls toward a yellow star, braking on a faint magnetic glow. It lands on a rocky planet. It spreads, like a cell or a virus: the same lesion, faster now. Its automated swarms lift the crust away; the planet comes apart into a ring. The ring becomes collectors around the star. The star dims until it is gone from the sky.

CARD: **a few thousand years**

SOUND: one landing thud. Then the tone again, rising.

> "我想到就是一种，就是 cosmic cancer 的真正的存在形式。然后它本身就是一个小的飞船……当它落到一个星球的时候，它就会开始使用当地的资源，去像一个生物、像一个细胞、一个病毒一样，开始在那个表面开始扩张。" "然后整个把那个，就是太阳，在几千年内就完全地吃掉，就不见了。"

### 17. THE FRONT
**2:48–3:15 · THE MILKY WAY · +12,000 → +2,000,000 YEARS**

One continuous pull-back across the whole galaxy, a hundred thousand light years. From the Orion Arm a front spreads along the probe lines. In visible light, star after star goes out. At the end the image switches to infrared, and the galaxy glows a dull, even red.

PANEL: POWER **10³⁷ W**

> "然后在几百万年的时间的长河之中，然后整个银河就一个个全都灭掉。"

### 18. THE SKY · IV
**3:15–3:22 · THE DOME, NIGHT**

The dome again, empty, its glass cracked, under a night sky. The stars go out one by one, near ones first, until the sky is black.

SOUND: nothing but a low drone.

### 19. EFFICIENT
**3:22–3:35 · THE MILKY WAY, INFRARED**

The dark galaxy, perfectly efficient, silent, turning. The POWER figure holds, flat, at its maximum. Nothing grows and nothing happens.

The word returns: **super intelligence**, faint, in the opening's caption type. *(With the real clip: the same voice, slowed and far away.)*

> "对，但是这些能源体虽然说更高效率，但是它也没有在做什么。它是为了 corporate interest 服务的。"

## ACT FIVE · THE GAME

### 20. THE EDGE
**3:35–3:43 · THE RIM OF THE GALAXY**

Probes reach the rim. Beyond it is the intergalactic dark. The nearest galaxies are faint smudges millions of light years away. One probe line reaches out into the nothing and fades.

> "因为它是一种向外斗争扩张式的，当这种斗争扩张没有了之后……"

### 21. THE SPLIT
**3:43–3:52 · THE MILKY WAY**

The even red glow cracks into domains. Neighbouring swarms separate along bright seams, dozens of them, then hundreds.

PANEL: a new line, PLAYERS, appears: **1** → **2** → **14** → **212**

SOUND: the drone fractures into dissonance.

> "然后它就开始，比如说一个个的戴森球之间，它又会开始分裂。"

### 22. THE BEAMS
**3:52–4:22 · THE MILKY WAY · +2,000,000 → +2,600,000 YEARS**

The swarms focus their stars into beams. One, then many, cross the galaxy as lines of white light. Each takes millennia to arrive, so the YEAR jumps with every volley. Struck swarms flare and go dark. From the far side, one beam sweeps across the galaxy, and everything it passes goes out.

PANEL: PLAYERS **212** → **40** → **6** → **2**. POWER falls.

SOUND: impacts arrive late, out of step with the shots, because light takes millennia to get there.

> "就像是它们还可以直接把恒星作为一个动力源。" "当这些星星都差不多逐渐灭掉的时候，然后从另外一边，然后你就可以看到互相毁灭的超能光束，在彼此从这个银河系统扫了一遍。"

### 23. LAST LIGHT
**4:22–4:32 · THE MILKY WAY**

The last two swarms fire at each other at the same moment. The two beams pass each other in the middle of the galaxy and travel on for fifty thousand years. Both arrive. Both swarms go dark.

PANEL: PLAYERS **0** · POWER **0 W**. The panel flickers and switches off.

### 24. NOTHING
**4:32–4:40 · BLACK**

Black. Silence.

> "然后就没有然后了。"

**THE GAME OF WINNING**

Held. Then black.

## Notes for the build

- **Words on screen:** the opening caption, the panel, the chapter cards, "PROGRESS", the reprise, the title. Nothing else.
- **Cut rhythm:** the outward shots accelerate. The four sky shots and THE CELL are the only places the film holds still.
- **Left out on purpose:** the frozen-humanity setting (borrowed from Greg Egan) and any coda. No company or person is named on screen except in the real opening clip.
- **To check before release:** the real clip's exact words and rights (reporting on the UN speech notes that transcriptions differ); the data-centre regions; every number against `trajectory.py`.
- **POPULATION** follows the UN *World Population Prospects 2024* medium projection up to its peak (10.3 bn in the 2080s). After the heat wall the falling figures are the film's illustration of Ren's "人类的数量大幅下降", not a computation.
