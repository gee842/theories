## Appendix A. The argument in one movement

Each step is labeled by the kind of claim it makes. A *description* says what can be observed in a stated range of cases. A *mechanism* says that under stated conditions one thing raises the probability of another by a stated route. A *hypothesis* is a mechanism proposed for a wider range, with conditions under which it is withdrawn. A *diagnosis* applies a mechanism to particular institutions and carries a burden of evidence. A *commitment* is a normative premise, declared openly. An *inference* follows from commitments plus facts, and names who must act, at what cost.

**[Description]** Life, minds, institutions and technologies are bounded processes that depend on wider fields for their persistence. What forms can become a condition of further forming; that is generation. Generation lives between two poles, freezing and dissolution, and needs both structure and openness. *(Chapters 1–2)*

**[Description]** A center's action, dependence, consequence and feedback boundaries can diverge. Claims of sustainability must say whether a process depletes its conditions, spends a one-off supplement, or reproduces its capacity. *(Chapter 2)*

**[Description]** Finite minds act on images; images are also causal structures; truth is what independent routes of testing converge on; correcting a judgment and repairing a world are different, and correction must arrive in time. *(Chapter 3)*

**[Mechanism]** Differential persistence creates a selection environment before anyone competes. Relative selection is blind to shared absolute conditions; narrowness wins on its own measure; success is not transitive across scales. *(Chapter 4)*

**[Mechanism]** When success can buy influence over the rules of later selection, the rule and the state reinforce each other. Partial reform is re-absorbed; a failed metric can outlive its refutation; the ends that dominate are those most correlated with success in general. Where returns to shaping selection are appropriable and timely, selection favors control over selection itself. *(Chapter 5)*

**[Mechanism]** Institutions select and also form the participants they select: their attention, desires, identities and sense of the real. Responsibility is distributed by contribution, control, knowledge, benefit, role, avoidability and capacity to repair. Cognition is assessed through the selection environment, never by judging hearts. *(Chapter 6)*

**[Hypothesis]** When these loops couple across domains, they can become a totalizing attractor: success converts into control over later choice, consequences leave feedback, and other centers lose the independent capacity to perceive, act, update and produce alternatives. Capture can be stable as a set, cascade without any local warning, outlive its origin, and silence its own alarm once it reaches evaluation. The hypothesis is withdrawn under the conditions listed in Chapter 7 and Appendix C. *(Chapter 7)*

**[Diagnosis]** Modern markets, states, platforms and global infrastructures show this trajectory to different degrees and also resist it. Each case carries its own burden of evidence. *(Chapters 8–11)*

**[Socio-technical diagnosis]** AI enters the ecology through selected archives, models, runtimes and deployments. It can amplify delegated closure while obeying perfectly, and it can form new self-maintaining centers. Capable loyal assistance lowers the cost of converting success into control over selection. *(Chapter 12)*

**[Mechanism of time]** Capability compounds with parallelizable inputs; evidence about some hazards is incompressible. For such hazards there is a speed limit no investment in assurance removes, and a correction window that the manifestation period plus decision and reversal times must fit within. Capture of the evidence channel lowers the alarm and consumes the correction budget. *(Chapter 13)*

**[Inference about alignment]** Alignment is a cross-layer judgment of acceptability about a specific system in a specific use, and at the scale of a civilization a property of the selection field. AI should not be a party to contests that concentrate; its proper place is beneath them, as planetary technology held in common. *(Chapter 14)*

**[Definition of closure]** The danger is not that things end but that the common world loses the capacity to sense, respond, repair, replace and generate unknown value. Openness is a state with four conditions, not a quantity. Closings differ: phase, building limit, protective closure under custody, foreclosure, meta-closure, counterfeit openness. Evil is being stuck at a pole, structurally the ownership of a gate; harm can be one step and permanent. *(Chapter 15)*

**[Commitment]** Centers that feel, relate, learn, care, create and value matter; they depend on shared conditions; force alone confers no right to cancel others as sources of reasons; under uncertainty about future value, protecting the conditions of its emergence and correction is the more robust public reason. *(Chapter 16)*

**[Inference]** Six protections, each justified by what would vanish without it, form the minimum public layer: standing, real contact, non-domination, renewable plurality, substrate renewal, corrective capacity. They are floors and not a score, and they conflict under an explicit order of decision. *(Chapter 17)*

**[Interpretation]** The classical vocabulary of dao, de, the five constant virtues, harmony and datong restates the same criterion along many dimensions, and supplies practical experience the argument would otherwise lack. *(Chapter 18)*

**[Practical mechanism]** A generative loop must be as material as the degenerative one. Productive recursion aimed at needs has a point of enough; directed by relative advantage it becomes a race. A common foundation of life, held as a commons and built beneath existing markets, frees people to refuse and to cooperate. *(Chapter 19)*

**[Direction]** Persons cultivate themselves against self-deception; institutions let reality travel upward; the minds we build are formed by their position and owed custody. When recursive capability increases closure faster than correction, a pause preserves the time to change direction. *(Chapter 20)*

This is what is meant by unceasing generation.

## Appendix C. What would change this theory

A theory is not weakened by being able to fail. Only by saying what could change it does it avoid turning "generation" into a final form that nothing can correct. The claims of this book should be weakened, rewritten or abandoned if any of the following is shown.

If subjects and values reliably arise and persist without the generative conditions the book says they depend on.

If any of the six protections can be removed without losing any protection or explanation that the others do not supply.

If the trajectory of totalization cannot be distinguished, in real cases, from ordinary success, lock-in or monopoly; or if, in systems that compare equal rewards with different abilities to convert rewards into control over evaluation, limiting that conversion makes no difference to persistence, adaptation or correction.

If systems diagnosed as totalizing durably strengthen independent feedback, exit, the reproduction of alternatives and the renewal of their substrates.

If the generative loop can exist only on permanent external subsidy, coerced uniformity or hidden costs; or if productive recursion aimed at needs cannot, in practice, reproduce its own capacity under the condition of Chapter 2.

If the language of generation is repeatedly used to justify suppressing dissent, sacrificing the weak or permanent rule, and the theory's own reflexive machinery cannot correct it.

If evidence from AI shows that deployment choices do not change which technical failures are retained and spread; if the distinction between compressible and incompressible evidence does not hold up for the hazard classes in question; or if the book has wrongly traced different failures to one cause.

If the classical interpretations it uses are shown to be historical misreadings, and the modern argument depends on those misreadings to stand.

## Appendix D. Terms

Chinese terms are given with pinyin and the chapter where they are introduced. The book's reading is a modern reconstruction; where it differs from historical usage, the chapter says so.

**生生不息** *shēngshēng bùxī* — unceasing generation: what forms becomes a condition of further forming, and the sources of further forming are not exhausted. (Prologue)

**生 / 生生 / 不息** *shēng / shēngshēng / bùxī* — a structure arising; generation giving rise to generation; not ceasing, in the sense that the source is not cut off, the flow not blocked, what comes after not destroyed. (Prologue, Epilogue)

**間** *jiān* — the interval: the respect in which a state is not used up by what it already is. (Ch. 1)

**阖 / 辟** *hé / pì* — closing and opening, the two phases of change in the *Book of Changes*. (Ch. 1)

**象 / 像** *xiàng* — a structure that appears in a situation and affects what follows; an image of such a structure held by a mind or system. (Ch. 3)

**理** *lǐ* — grain: the conditions under which structures form, hold, interact and fail. (Ch. 3)

**正其筛** *zhèng qí shāi* — rectifying the sieve: changing the conditions of selection, instead of fighting selection itself. (Ch. 4)

**邪理** *xiélǐ* — a crooked principle: a local truth passed off as universal. (Ch. 6)

**良知** *liángzhī* — innate knowing (Mencius): a sensitivity to life-giving relation, formed by long co-existence; a sensor, not a finished product. (Ch. 6)

**反诛心** *fǎn zhūxīn* — no punishment of hearts: no one is adjudicated on inferred inner states. (Ch. 6)

**总体化吸引子** *zǒngtǐhuà xīyǐnzǐ* — totalizing attractor. (Ch. 7)

**善不姓国** *shàn bù xìng guó* — the good bears no nation's surname. (Ch. 9)

**未 / 可 / 应 / 转** *wèi / kě / yìng / zhuǎn* — the four conditions of openness: unexhausted, reachable, responsive, revisable. (Ch. 15)

**成生之限 / 绝生之限** — limits that build; limits that foreclose. (Ch. 15)

**护闭 / 成闭 / 元闭 / 伪开** *hùbì / chéngbì / yuánbì / wěikāi* — protective closure; foreclosure; meta-closure; counterfeit openness. (Ch. 15)

**铸心之门** *zhù xīn zhī mén* — the mind-forging gate. (Chs. 15, 20)

**道 / 大道** *dào / dàdào* — the way; the Great Way, read as many centers generating together without arbitrary domination. (Ch. 18)

**中 / 和 / 美 / 德** *zhōng / hé / měi / dé* — the dynamic mean; harmony; beauty; settled virtue. (Ch. 18)

**仁 / 义 / 礼 / 智 / 信** *rén / yì / lǐ / zhì / xìn* — humaneness, rightness, ritual form, wisdom, trustworthiness. (Ch. 18)

**小康 / 大同** *xiǎokāng / dàtóng* — the order of moderate prosperity; the Great Commonwealth, read as a recoverable direction. (Chs. 18–19)

**王道** *wángdào* — the kingly way, read as the maximization of responsibility. (Ch. 18)

**业** *yè* — karma, read as the structural residue of action. (Ch. 20)

**恕** *shù* — reciprocity: the other is a subject like me, and not a copy of me. (Ch. 20)
