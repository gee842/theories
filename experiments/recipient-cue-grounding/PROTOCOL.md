## Units, parsing and validation

The focal rat is the paired unit. Retain source row/cell strings and parsed values with explicit missingness. Require exactly one `neutral` and one `cooperating` row per `ratID`, no duplicated row identifier, nonnegative integer `pull_f`, and valid numeric or explicit missing latency/timing cells. Report missing and invalid fields and all incomplete pairs. Never convert `NA` to zero. Unexpected structure or invalid primary counts pauses numerical acceptance for a documented protocol amendment; do not silently exclude rows. Training, apparatus, repeated exposure and treatment assignment are supplied by the experiment.

Use all valid complete count pairs, including zero counts and zero differences. Sort focal identifiers lexicographically for reproducible outputs and resampling. Define d_i = cooperating pulls minus neutral pulls. Pulls within sessions are outcomes, not independent subjects. Donor/recipient identifiers, sequence/order and session dates are absent from this preview, so clustering beyond focal animal, period effects and carryover cannot be estimated from it.

## Primary descriptive estimate and assumption-conditional inference

Report all raw paired counts and differences; n; treatment totals and means; mean and median paired difference; count of positive, negative and zero differences; and the sample standard deviation of differences. Units are additional pulls per focal rat per seven-minute session. Counts indicate physical food-access actions, not verified ingestion, welfare, energy expenditure or future survival.

Supply an animal-paired percentile bootstrap interval for the mean difference: Python standard-library `random.Random(20261006)`, 20,000 resamples, each selecting n complete pairs with replacement using `randrange(n)`. For sorted bootstrap means use linearly interpolated empirical quantiles at .025 and .975, with index (B−1)p. State that interpretation as a sampling interval assumes the animals approximate independent comparable sampling units; reused partners or common experimental influences could violate that assumption. This interval is exploratory and unadjusted for historical selection and multiple endpoints.

Calculate the two-sided equiprobable sign-flip reference probability for the absolute sum of paired differences, including equality at the tail boundary. Enumerate its exact integer distribution by dynamic programming over ±d_i, retaining zero-pair multiplicities. Report numerator, denominator 2^n and probability. This is an exact calculation under an assumed reference distribution, **not a recovered assignment-exact randomization test**: randomized order is documented, but actual assignment probabilities, restrictions, carryover and inter-animal independence are not recoverable. Its null requires the justified sign symmetry/exchangeability or corresponding sharp-null randomization assumptions, rather than merely zero average effect.

As a secondary robustness description, supply a two-sided binomial sign reference probability among nonzero differences: min(1, 2·Pr[Binomial(m,.5) ≤ min(n_positive,n_negative)]). Report ties separately. Conditional equiprobable independent signs are assumed; this is not an assumption-free causal result. No threshold determines conceptual acceptance, no model selection is performed, and no equivalence claim follows from a large probability.

The same donor was used across the two situations, but pulling access, exertion and other state changes were not independently matched. Online cue potentiation, generalized helping and richer recipient models remain observationally compatible. A fixed identity-only response is a narrower comparator. Experiment 4 provides no delay separating cue from action and no alternative beneficiary choice; do not infer retained evidence, cognitive misbinding or recipient-specific reasons.

The next bridge is identifying how acquired information is bound to an affected center and used through effective action despite cue/source/recipient mismatch. This study can rule out an overstrong interpretation of helpful behavior. It cannot derive the training regime, internal representation, calibration, moral concern, authority, human governance or general AI alignment.
