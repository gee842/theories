# Experiments

**Cost-sensitive learning and causal identification, 6 October 2026:** the [public-data analysis](motor-learning-cost/README.md) independently reconstructs fixed paired summaries for forty participants from an unchanged source workbook. The primary contrast's interval crosses zero; all secondary comparisons remain visible. Exact synthetic countermodels separately distinguish altered behavior from an identified operative updater change. Source cells, protocol history, numerical receipts, figures and reproduction commands are preserved. This is a retrospective empirical analysis and identification study, not a new human experiment or validation of the whole generative foundation.

**External evidence and learned-agent action, 6 October 2026:** the [AgentDojo retrospective audit](agentdojo-evidence-boundary/README.md) independently reconstructs 320 saved traces and separates reported task/attack labels from simulated effects and authority. Complete replay covers 315 trajectories; five missing receipts remain unknown. A corrected identity-disclosure proxy and its first outputs are preserved. This is historical model-behavior evidence, not a new model run, real banking activity or a test of the proposed generative mechanism.

**Paid abstraction acquisition, 6 October 2026:** the [new mathematical-abstraction continuation](integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/README.md) connects an actual resource history to an observation-only learner, an acquired continuation distinction, a minimal model and productive unqueried use. Twelve controls and 234 finite target checks retain explicit cartridge/algorithm/interface inputs and failures of funding, bounds, reset and calibration. It is a conditional synthetic mechanism with standard active-learning ancestry, not empirical mathematical cognition.

Applied experiments and prototypes informed by Ren's theories and values. These projects explore what those ideas might imply in practice; they are not part of the canonical theory corpus.

Each experiment owns its assumptions, implementation choices, evidence and limitations. Links back to theories describe intellectual provenance, not proof. A result changes the experiment; incorporating it into theory requires a separate explicit decision.
