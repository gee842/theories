# Experiments

Applied experiments and prototypes informed by Ren's theories and values. These projects explore what those ideas might imply in practice; they are not part of the canonical theory corpus.

Each experiment owns its assumptions, implementation choices, evidence and limitations. Links back to theories describe intellectual provenance, not proof. A result changes the experiment; incorporating it into theory requires a separate explicit decision.

| Experiment | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Circular construction](circular-construction/README.md) | A speculative, regenerative construction system demonstrated with Blender, robotics dynamics, structural analysis and an interactive 3D construction sequence. |
| [Evaluator succession](evaluator-succession/README.md) | A conditional authority model and a reproducible synthetic benchmark testing success-dependent control of evaluation, beneficial revision, and effective correction. Includes contradicted predictions and preserved raw results. |
| [Prospective preservation and questions without anomalies](prospective-revision/README.md) | Exact paid archive, admission, and fresh-validation workflows, with a counterexample to anomaly-required investigation and choices made before a later question is known. |
| [Renewal of inquiry](inquiry-renewal/README.md) | Locally generated candidates, independent validation and monitoring, paid finite reserves, selective credit, and restart, with positive-return shutdown and no-benefit controls. |
| [Recoverable inquiry](recoverable-inquiry/README.md) | Exact finite decisions about paid diagnosis, lagged rebuilding and direct repair, funded by productive stock, under an explicit expected burden constraint. |
| [Plural requirements for correction](plural-correction/README.md) | Common-policy feasibility under distinct expected requirements, with separating certificates, controlled authority restrictions, and funding/permission complements on unchanged productive dynamics. |
| [Continuing participation and funded correction](continuing-participation/README.md) | Personal loss incidence, public collective veto, and compensation paid from shared funds, separating insufficient gains from distribution and payment timing. |
| [Constructive succession under paid inheritance](constructive-succession/README.md) | Program construction through four paid handoffs, comparing inherited solutions and intermediate materials; an additional matched-package test separates preservation from active use in search. |
