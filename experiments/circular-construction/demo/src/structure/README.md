# Stage-by-stage axial truss screening

`assessConstructionStages(model, stages)` solves each declared stage as an independent three-dimensional, pin-jointed, linear-elastic truss. Coordinates are metres, loads and reactions newtons, member stresses pascals, and Z points up. A member carries **axial force only**. Its axial stiffness is `EA/L`; tension is positive and compression is negative. Ideal supports restrain selected translations at nodes. The caller supplies every stage's active member IDs, active support IDs, and nodal loads, including self-weight if relevant. Inactive, unloaded nodes are omitted from that stage's stiffness system.

The solver builds the free-degree stiffness matrix, checks its eigenvalue rank for mechanisms, estimates its spectral condition number, solves stable systems, and checks member forces against caller-supplied tension and compression capacities. The compression capacity must already account for buckling if buckling matters. A stage's `canTransferLoad` means only that **this axial truss screen** has a reliable equilibrium solution and the stated axial capacities are not exceeded. Results deliberately carry `scope: 'axial-truss-screening-only'`.

`createBracedTripodExample()` supplies four inspectable stages: two legs held by a temporary restraint, premature restraint release (a mechanism), a completed three-leg brace, and a static overload. The numbers are illustrative and do not represent the demonstrator's existing beam.

`loadPaths` give one route through members with significant solved axial force to each reacting support. They are a compact witness of transmission, while the member-force and reaction arrays show the full split. A disconnected load has no topological route to a restraint; a connected load in a mechanism remains unresolved. Self-equilibrating loads may have no reacting-support path even when the stiffness system is stable.

The model assumes small displacement, straight concentric bars, perfect pins, rigid translational restraints, and static loading. It does not calculate beam bending, shear, torsion, joint stiffness or slip, local or global buckling, foundations, dynamics, geometric nonlinearity, erection history, or code load combinations. The current demonstrator's unbraced beam between fixtures is a bending-member system and is **outside this solver's scope**; its structural load transfer remains unverified here. These results are research screening, not building-code certification.
