# Carrier construction prototype

The shared design is `../contracts/carrier-construction.json`, regenerated with `python3 production/contracts/make_carrier_contract.py` from the demo directory. The 20-storey architecture uses a 6 by 4 grid of 3 m bays, 3.2 m storeys, pale steel, light dry floor cassettes, glazing, warm soffits and demountable terrace assemblies. No heavy precast core or 6 m heavy beam is silently assigned to a climbing carrier.

Run `blender --background --python production/blender/build_carrier_construction.py` to generate the two editable `.blend` libraries here and `public/assets/construction-{kit,robots}.glb`. Run `preview_carrier_construction.py` after generation to render the saved loaded carrier and a jaw-closeup inspection coupon. Blender 5.2.1 was used. All libraries are reusable templates at their local origins; they are not an erected site or stored inventory. The runtime must create independent component identities, stored transforms and installed transforms and must carry the same instance through that history.

All modeling helpers accept glTF-style Y-up XYZ, convert to Blender Z-up `(x,-z,y)` and export glTF with Blender's Y-up option, giving canonical SI source `(x,y,z) → glTF(x,z,-y)`. There is no hidden scale. Local root scale is one. Kit roots have physical center origins, except the terrace adds its guard above the centered floor slab. `carrier` and `assembler` origins are chassis center; travel height is 0.8 m. `ground_builder` and `cargo_rover` roots are at the ground plane.

The exact root names are in `public/assets/construction-assets.json`. A column body is centered on its 3.2 m length, a beam runs along local X, floor cassettes span local X by Z, and facade/core modules are vertical X by Y. The brace template is already a diagonal from `(-1.5,-1.6,0)` to `(1.5,1.6,0)` in glTF; do not rotate it as though it were a straight X-axis rod. Stairs are half-storey flights. Foundation caps and 2 m pile segments are separate handled components. Pile flights are simplified rings; they are not a toolpath or ground mechanics solution.

The carrier has 0.65 m upper and lower links, hips at source `(0,±0.32,±0.48)` and source Y-axis joints. Named pivots are `limb_{fl,fr,rl,rr}_{hip,knee,wrist}`. Every link is authored along source +X; knee and wrist origins are 0.65 m along their parent's +X. Initial source angles are hip −0.8, knee +1.6, wrist −0.8 radians. Drive absolute joint angles, rather than adding solved angles to these defaults. Blender may append `.001` to equivalent assembler object names because Blender names are globally unique; strip numeric suffixes within each cloned role or match the prefix.

The cradle is outboard from the frame, its payload center at source `(−0.61,0,0)`. A carried beam must have its long axis along source Y (glTF Z), so rotate the beam template accordingly. Four cradle fingers, four retainers, actual articulated clamp jaws, removable-looking batteries, sensors and ground wheels are visible. The assembler replaces the cradle with a supported two-finger receiving tool and a fastening spindle. Its tool pivots are `tool_shoulder`, `tool_elbow`, `tool_wrist` and `tool_tip`. The ground builder contains a slewing base, boom, stick, rotary pile head, stabilizing feet and crawler tracks; cargo rovers carry a separate cargo mount.

Twin climbing rails are permanently part of each column assembly, at source X +0.18 m, source Y ±0.32 m relative to the column axis, with 0.32 m receiver pitch. Receiver heights are 0.16 through 3.04 m above each segment bottom. These parts are included in column handling mass and arrive with that column; they do not appear independently. They remain as proposed structural maintenance interfaces at completion. A representative physics carrier at source `(0,0,1.28)` facing a column axis X=0.57 sees receiver rail X=0.75 and initially aligned grip heights 0.80/1.76 m.

Geometric masses include explicit material densities and separately named assembly allowances. They are engineering estimates rather than weighing results, and accessory topology is simplified where the mass is an allowance. The column is 327.858 kg including rails/brackets/connection plates, the beam 92.928 kg, floor 139.542 kg, facade 254.928 kg. No part exceeds the proposed 350 kg single-carrier envelope. Carrier dry mass is specified as 320 kg, not derived from visual mesh volume: 160 kg chassis and batteries, four 30 kg complete limbs/gear, 40 kg cradle. Hip 3000 Nm, knee 2000 Nm and grip 16 kN are provisional hardware targets. They are not verified ratings.

Structural adequacy of this 20-storey frame, foundations and connections, loaded contact-safe climbing, wind handling, joint collision clearance, fire/acoustics/weathering and MEP commissioning remain open. A completed visible planned construction sequence does not imply qualified autonomous construction or an occupiable building. Any detailed physics result must identify this contract revision, actual payload and boundary assumptions.


## Manufactured detail and contact limitations

The refined machine library uses rounded casing edges and weighted normals; coaxial reducer housings, hinge shafts, encoder covers and captive fasteners; guided opposed-leadscrew jaws with replaceable pads; screw-driven cradle retainers; battery latches and vents; camera lenses and a lidar window; and wheel steering, suspension and retraction pivots. Cable conduits stay on their parent links and terminate at sealed joints, rather than crossing moving hinges as rigid decorative hoses. Cosmetic parts remain within the existing design envelope and assigned assembly mass; these details are not additional rated hardware evidence. Fixed meshes are consolidated by parent and material, preserving every articulated pivot and interface while limiting fleet draw calls.

New `limb_*_grip_contact` empties locate the actual jaw throat at wrist-local source `(0.135,0,0)`. Open jaw centers are ±0.15 m laterally; capture around the 0.05 m rail needs centers near ±0.045 m, a 0.105 m stroke. Existing ideal physics anchors constrain the wrist pivot, which is occupied by its reducer, and do not establish real jaw contact at this offset. A closed-jaw preview with a short independent rail coupon is a static geometry inspection only.

For a front facade at source Y=−6 m, rotate the column source yaw −π/2 to present its rails outward (−Y), and face the carrier source yaw +π/2 toward it. A carrier center at Y=−6.93 gives 0.75 m reach to the near-side rail at Y=−6.18. For the standalone +X-facing fixture, use column axis X=0.93 with column yaw π to place the near rail at X=0.75. Clearance at brackets, beams, floor transitions and the complete swept robot volume still needs collision testing.

The first ideal-constraint loaded dynamics work found a materially different outcome between a 92.928 kg beam-shaped load and 327.858 kg ballast at the same outboard center: the heavier case aborts. This is not proof that a 327 kg carrier is impossible, and it does not qualify the 350 kg design target. See the current physics result files in `production/physics/carrier/`; they supersede any visual impression of payload feasibility. Candidate next designs are shorter handled column segments and a closer cradle center, each requiring new assembly dependencies and loaded contact dynamics.

## Circulation kit revision

The staircase reserves x=[−3,0], z=[−3,3] in the viewer's Y-up site coordinates. Six 3×1 m floor cassettes are omitted at each elevated occupied level; a 3×1.5 m near landing fills z=[−3,−1.5]. A corresponding half-storey landing fills z=[1.5,3]. The paired 1.1 m-wide flights occupy x centers −2.15 and −0.85, run 3 m between landings and rise 1.6 m through ten 160 mm risers and 300 mm goings; the returning flight is rotated π. Handrails are integral. This is a circulation geometry prototype, not qualified egress, guard infill, accessibility or fire separation.

`kit_stair` now weighs 147.688 kg. Its root remains the rise/run midpoint; guards increase its bounding height to 2.69 m, so do not position it by the bounding-box center. `kit_landing` is 190.409 kg. `kit_core_half` is a 0.75 m-wide partition at 122.106 kg; two flank the 1.5 m-wide `kit_entry` (264.672 kg). `entry_door_hinge` is closed for storage/transport and open for the installed scene. The ground deck supplies a walking datum of +0.16 m. For storey f, flight roots are Y=f×3.2+0.96 and f×3.2+2.56, mid-landing root Y=f×3.2+1.68 and next full-landing root Y=(f+1)×3.2+0.08.

Full-level beams across the shaft at z=0 and z=3 are omitted because they intersect the flights or obstruct headroom above the mid-landing. Added full-level headers support the near landing at z=−1.5; mid-level headers at z=1.5 and z=3 join side trimmers at x=−3 and x=0. These new members require explicit structural modeling and qualified connections. Twenty occupied storeys comprise the ground level plus nineteen upper levels: use nineteen pairs of flights, and close the final 64 m roof deck. Roof access is not part of this circulation scope.

`preview_circulation.py` renders a two-storey cutaway from the same reusable kit and saves `construction-circulation-preview.blend` plus `public/assets/construction-circulation-preview.png`. The cutaway deliberately omits two enclosure sides to inspect the openings and landing relationships.

Short stair-side trimmers use the dedicated `kit_beam_half` (1.5 m, 55.059 kg). Only its RHS shaft is shorter; its two connection plates and four pins match the 3 m beam at full size. Scaling the entire 3 m beam would incorrectly shrink the connection hardware and misstate its mass.
