BLENDER CONSTRUCTION ASSET PIPELINE

Rebuild from the demo directory:
  blender --background --python production/blender/build_assets.py

Blender 5.2.1 LTS was used. There are no downloaded or generative-image assets.
All architecture, steel sections and mechanical parts are editable mesh geometry.
Build output includes Cycles stills, not screenshots of a browser approximation.

The generator copies assembly-preview.blend and planned-architecture.blend into
public/assets/ as browser-downloadable sources. The planned design reuses the
36 x 24m steel grid and closes the level-12 beam gap. It fills all 20 levels
with floor cassettes, facade modules, core modules and concept finishes, while
omitting the crane, yard, grip rails, robot and hoist. It is not a construction
or occupancy simulation. The public architecture.html viewer labels that scope.

SI dimensions / axes
Blender and MuJoCo use X/Y ground plane, Z up, metres.
glTF exporter maps world position (x,y,z) to browser (x,z,-y).
The tower footprint is 36 x 24 m, with 6 m structural bays.
Twenty 3.6 m storeys reach 72 m; crane mast reaches 87 m.
The model intentionally shows the steel skeleton to level 20, floor cassettes
through level 11 with partial deck at level 12, and facade modules through 8.
This is a construction-stage design scenario, not an erection-sequence signoff.

Beam center starts Blender (9,-12,44.7), browser (9,44.7,12).
Seated center is (9,-12,43.425); bearing-seat top is 43.2 m.
Beam total length is 5.6 m, center at x9 between columns at x6 and x12.
I-section 450 mm deep x 300 mm wide, web 9 mm, flanges 18 mm.
Nominal physical beam mass is 638.563 kg (density 7850 kg/m3).
Beam sling/lug centers are local (-1.6,0,.225) and (1.6,0,.225).
Carrier sling sites are local (-1.6,0,0) and (1.6,0,0).
The ActiveHoist GLB node contains only carrier hardware. Runtime viewer draws
dynamic slings and vertical cable from actual moving attachment sites.
PreviewRigging_NotExported exists only in the assembly Blender scene/stills.
Rail centers: x6.06, y=-12 +/- .25, z43.9; dimensions .06 x .09 x 2.8 m.
TemporaryYBraces runs X diagonals at x+/-18, in y bays [-12,-6] and [6,12],
for every 3.6m storey from ground to level 20, in a subdued green safety finish.
End plates include four actual cylindrical through-holes each; bolted seats,
washers, hex heads, web, flanges and lifting lugs are separate solid features.
GLB asset metadata is descriptive; the physics engine owns mass and inertia.

  limb_rr_upper                     (0,+0.25,-0.32)
  limb_{fl,fr,rl,rr}_lower           relative to upper: (+0.32,0,0)
  limb_{fl,fr,rl,rr}_grip            relative to lower: (+0.32,0,0)
  robot_tool                        (+0.25,0,-0.55), bit tip +0.12 m X
Upper/lower links extend along local +X from the proximal joint.
Upper bind rotation is -0.35 rad about Y; lower bind rotation is +0.35 rad.
GLB's converted joint axis is negative browser Z.
Paired jaws are separate named nodes and can slide along local Y.
The viewer can replace these world poses with physics-recorded body poses.

Model content
Open I/H sections, column splices, paired fin plates, fasteners, cross-bracing,
floor cassette underside ribs, slab joints, facade mullions/spandrels, concrete
core panel cassettes and stair modules, crane lattice/rails/cab/counterweight, carrier crossbar/pulley housing,
external cage hoist, staged beam stacks and cassettes, containers, barriers,
road markings, four-limb robot housing, motors, hydraulic links, wiring,
gripper actuators, opposed jaws, stereo sensors and a torque tool.

Demountable core and recovery boundary
ConcreteCore preserves the original 8.4 x 7.6m wall-center footprint and uses
sixteen 3.6m assemblies reaching 57.6m. Each CoreModule_XX contains framed
precast wall panels, separate doorway lintel, visible physical seams, accessible
bolted splice plates, lifting sockets, stair treads/stringers and landings.
Panels and stair modules are designed here as recoverable/reusable components
subject to inspection of panels, inserts, frames and connections before reuse.
This is not a claim of complete concrete recycling or proven recovery yield.
Foundations, crane pads and other site-bound concrete remain residual site scope.
The existing OpenSees limitation is unchanged: NO CORE STIFFNESS CREDIT is taken.
The modular core is visual/architectural scope; its connections are not analyzed
by the current steel-frame structural model.

Architectural finish and completed design
The active-site GLB has removable visual finish through level 8. The planned
GLB has FinishModule_01 ... FinishModule_20 and core modules through level 20.
The facade retains a 6m structural-bay rhythm and recessed south glazing.
Terrace depth is 3m on most levels and 6m at levels 3-4, 10-11 and 17-18;
warm soffits, pale fins, planters, garden-room trees and planted forecourt
are actual authored meshes. A 72m height / 36m width ratio limits the slender
appearance; narrowing the grid needs new engineering rather than rendering.
These finish pieces are outside the structural analysis, robot collision
model, individual crane-lift inventory and materials passport. Their
attachments, loads, fabrication and recovery remain future engineering work.

Limitations
Geometry is a dimensioned design concept, not manufacturer CAD or certified
shop drawings. The entire 20-storey skeleton is visible for site context.
The tower GLB itself contains no animation: the viewer must load physics output.
Robot meshes do not establish contact/friction/fastener verification by themselves.
The assembly-preview poses are presentation poses; the runtime trace is the
source of motion truth. Fine bolt threads, full tool-change racks, and an interior
fit-out are outside this asset slice.

never overwrites the beam-receiving robot or tower assets.

Climb robot GLB root is at the origin; trace supplies the chassis world pose.
Preview chassis starts at Blender (0,0,1.2). Each link is 0.45m along local +X.
Shoulder bind angle is -0.9133327704 rad; elbow is +1.8266655408 rad about Y.
Hips retain y+/-0.25,z+/-0.32. Fixture rails are x0.55,y+/-0.25, extending to5.7m.
All 52 receiver positions are read individually from the contract: front and
rear receiver grids have separate vertical offsets and each has0.3m pitch.
The root ClimbRailFixture and receiver IDs rail_{fl,fr,rl,rr}_{0..12} are stable.
These visible parts represent the ideal-lock fixture in the climb simulation;
mesh detail does not imply modeled jaw engagement or certified attachment design.
