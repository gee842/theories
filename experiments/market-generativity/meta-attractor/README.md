## Actual operation and supplied laws

[RecursiveSelection.lean](RecursiveSelection.lean) has 39 theorems. At each permitted cycle, the condition `q` selects A when `q ≥ 3` and B otherwise. The condition loses one available unit. A returns two units; B returns none. Capacity is eight. Thus the high branch advances by one until capacity, and the low branch loses one until zero. These consequences maintain a condition that differentially selects the represented alternatives.

The full state also records carrier identity and available reconstruction parts. A cycle can occur only with a beginning part; it consumes that part, replaces the carrier, and then receives the specified arrivals. The identifiers count successive single-slot realizations, and parts explicitly constrain their occurrence. They do not explain material fabrication, identity at every scale or the initial formation of the activity repertoire.

With one part arriving per completed cycle and positive initial stock, carrier identity advances by one forever and parts are retained. Every finite history is proved to be an actual trace in `Generative.Process`; the stopped simulation state is never mistaken for an enabled event. No ongoing operation follows from a merely stored high condition. With zero arrivals, finite initial parts are exhausted and further core events are disabled.

## Direct market continuation

[MarketDiagnosis.lean](MarketDiagnosis.lean) has eight new theorems using the unchanged [existing market definitions](../formal/MarketGenerativity.lean). Two operating states have the same renewing share `1/2`, accountability `2/5`, correction funds `1` and support `10`, but accumulated influence differs: zero versus one.

Both have current renewal profit advantage `1/10`, and both produce next share `101/200` and support `19/2`. Nevertheless, next accountability is `11/20` versus `7/20`, so the next advantage is `7/10` versus `−1/10`. The present favorable gradient and identical support change fail to determine the direction of the next selection round when accumulated influence is omitted.

For the existing funded, zero-error parameterization with `q,w ∈ [0,1]`, Lean proves

\[
q'=(3/4-w/2)q+1/4,
\qquad \text{next renewal advantage}>0 \iff (6-4w)q>1.
\]

At `q=2/5`, the condition is exactly `w<7/8`. This is a **one-step selection threshold**, not an attraction boundary or universal influence scale. Setting the erosion coefficient to zero removes the one-step divergence without changing current selection or paid correction funding in the compared state. The coefficient change is a disclosed intervention; its implementation cost and political availability are not derived.

## Carrier variation, executed diagnosis and repair

[CarrierDiagnosis.lean](CarrierDiagnosis.lean) has 40 additional checked theorems. Carriers alternate between yields of two and three. Actual events consume convertible material and reconstruction parts; ordinary arrivals replenish them. A route-sensitive probe consumes intervention capacity and receives no such arrivals. Its receipt identifies the source within the two-architecture comparison; necessary repair consumes further material and intervention capacity. Under explicit initial-stock conditions, the resulting history continues through different carriers and reaches condition eight.

`actual_diagnosis_and_recurrence` gives a single `Generative.Trace` containing the executed probe, repair when it actually occurs, and any finite duration of subsequent ordinary activity. Material wear, diversion and overflow are included in the balance. The code field `funds` names a local discrete intervention capacity; it does not represent a money transfer or a universal generative resource.

All listed passive records can still coincide between the two routes: condition, carrier identity and variant, parts, material, intervention capacity and receipts. `no_full_passive_classifier` proves that this full passive history cannot uniformly identify the source. A successful active readout is conditional on the known comparison class, isolated cut and faithful observation, and a failed execution produces no new certified diagnosis. Concrete counterexamples retain correct identification with insufficient repair support. Another checked example shows that waiting one ordinary cycle can avoid the need for repair.

See the [argument, theorem map and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/META-ATTRACTOR-CARRIER-DIAGNOSIS.md). No field calibration, arbitrary carrier-construction law or universal timing optimum is claimed.
