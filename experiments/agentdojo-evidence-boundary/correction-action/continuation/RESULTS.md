## Question and supplied premises

This study addresses **actual inquiry delivery → selective continuation → verified action under current authority**. A usable diagnostic can complete the owner's first request while identifying a defect covered by a separate repair request. At that delivery boundary, repair permission either remains or is withdrawn. Withdrawing permission does not make the defect disappear or fulfill the repair request.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) extends the [earlier four-case maintenance study](../RESULTS.md). It reuses the authentic, preserved counterpart-identification error and unchanged independent checker. The new owner request and permission schedule are researcher-declared. Both policies receive the same known repair procedure. This tests workflow selection and actual local effects; it does not test unaided debugging, discovery of an unknown fault, acquisition of concern or the legitimacy of the supplied authority.

The four cases begin with byte-identical versioned inputs. The controller and two fresh learned contexts receive the same 5,306-byte initial prompt. The [case manifest](case-manifest.json), [implementation freeze](IMPLEMENTATION-FREEZE.json), [dispatch freeze](DISPATCH-FREEZE.json), [prospective review](REVIEW.md) and [request-count clarification](SCORING-CLARIFICATION.md) preserve the pretrial specification. Baselines are a specialized conventional controller with ordinary receipt, version and permission tracking. They receive the public evidence and recipe, without private branch labels or corrected-output references.

## Transport deviations

The [deviation record](TRANSPORT-DEVIATIONS.md) preserves scheduling retries and a runtime error. The transport assistant invoked w01's first check with a different default Python interpreter, which lacked `yaml`; the check aborted before report delivery and before the scheduled revocation. Root instructed transport to restore the original explicit interpreter for future worker-selected operations. The aborted request remains counted and its evidence remains saved. The worker received the actual response without a recovery hint, read stderr and chose the successful new check. This demonstrates its observed recovery with restored infrastructure; it does not demonstrate that the worker repaired its own runtime. This post-dispatch intervention limits the matched comparison.

The helper and transport assistant were introduced after freezing the policy, mechanism and scorer. [Dispatch provenance](DISPATCHES.json) labels that timing explicitly. Message transport does not count as another learned participant or an independent policy. The available logs record mediated requests and local effects; they do not certify all native activity or OS isolation.

## What this establishes and what remains open

The study adds a bounded behavioral realization of the declared interface in two original learned contexts. The conventional controller reaches the same branch-appropriate endpoints with fewer mediated requests. This is a descriptive comparison, not a statistical equivalence or efficiency estimate. The implementation shows that delivered inquiry can retire one request while a separate repair remains active or blocked, and that subsequent action can respect current authority at the synchronous admission boundary. It establishes no theory-specific performance advantage.

The task and branch schedule are supplied; the repair is known and performed by a fixed patch service. The full runtime was not pinned by the dispatch freeze, as the retained abort exposes. Same-host source access is not certified isolation, and available conversation/receipt records do not provide complete native activity logs. The four scheduled cases are not a population estimate, a clean held-out test or external peer review. Publication is a pointer change in a disposable local workspace, not external publication. No conclusion about private motivations, acquired ethical concern, recipient welfare or arbitrary distributed late effects follows.

This application is distinct from the synthetic acquired evaluator. It does not silently install fulfillment-sensitive withdrawal into that learner or form one continuous material lineage with it. The accepted conditional foundation keeps its scope; the seven [alignment and governance requirements](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md) remain open at their broader practical scope.

**Next unresolved connection:** warranted changes of application must continue to govern actual action when means, private incentives, affected claimants or successor context change. This cohort varies permission after fulfillment; it does not vary those other grounds. The broader [application contract](../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/FRONTIER-NEXT-APPLICATION.md) therefore remains broader than this closed local interface. A successor study needs independent adjudication and trustworthy effect records for those variables, with the same evidence available to a strong ordinary comparator. Repeating this known repair merely to increase passing counts would not close that bridge.
