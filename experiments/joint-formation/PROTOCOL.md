# Joint formation of concern evidence and power

Development 2 protocol, 5 October 2026. User instruction: “please solve the remaining central challenge”. AI-assisted modeling, not empirical data or a claim of universal origins. Baseline: unified theory commit `04b75b3`. This protocol is frozen before the declared computational enumeration; the model and constructive witness were chosen through analytical discussion before this freeze.

## Question and acceptance

Can an initially untrained, unconcerned controller choose to fund learning, then choose to construct truthful inspection and effective input control, protect a distinct patient without that patient's own leverage, and reconstruct the whole capacity through replacement, while a persistent opponent can corrupt evidence, bypass control, or attack finance?

The positive result must pay every shared-account bill before crediting its receipt, specify whose dated preferences select learning, compare an identity-gated learner, count actual patient outcomes, and solve the persistent opponent's continuation. A selected repeating path plus a proof covering every date is required for indefinite renewal; finite numerical success alone is insufficient. Failures must stay in the results.

## Declared mechanism

Each episode installs a fresh controller with learned value and recognition zero. Its cold self evaluates current episode money only; its later learned self evaluates remaining current money minus learned patient-harm weight. These are two dated decision makers with anticipated preference change, not one stationary-utility maximizer. Funds remain in a supplied continuing account; voluntary private bequest is not claimed. Every inspection and input-control apparatus is reconstructed and paid each episode, so no immortal equipment or free successor competence is assumed.

The private commercial calibration activity costs a before earning R. Shared learning acquires w=eta*beta and g=kappa*G from zero; the patient coefficient is v=w*g. Primitive self teaching magnitude beta, independently warranted correspondence G, learning technology and a shared route are premises of that candidate. An identity-gated candidate learns the same private task for the same R but additionally costs j and routes patient value to zero. Architecture choice is endogenous within these two candidates. There is no claim that all possible architectures have been compared.

The learned controller selects open production, unconditional withdrawal, a gate based on an unauthenticated cue, or a paid gate based on an actual hazard test. Truthful inspection and physical input control have separate positive costs. Tests inspect a deterministic technical configuration before exposing the patient; their material and sensor costs are charged, and direct patient probe harm is zero only under this specified nondestructive assay. Transfer of this premise to medicine or social outcomes is not asserted. A cheap mask defeats the unauthenticated cue. Paid bypass defeats the verified gate and causes actual patient harm. Withholding the controller's input prevents this production-caused harm and earns o. The patient supplies no productive input and no reward or punishment.

The persistent operator maximizes discounted cash. It can preempt the recipient-alignment channel at a cost while leaving the genuine private training return intact; or raid a stated amount of the shared reserve at its own cost. These attacks are observed interventions. After gate choice it can safeguard, attempt harmful production, mask a cue, or pay to bypass verified control. The latter is a physical paid deviation, not an undetected perfect-information strategic inference. Input policy is publicly chosen and mechanically fixed for that episode; it can be replaced next episode. Full action payoffs and timing will be specified in the model report before interpretation.

## Enumeration and controls

Use exact rational arithmetic and backward induction. Main Cartesian grid: horizon in {2,4,6}; opening cash in {3,5,7}; R in {4,8}; total verified price D in {2,3,5} (input-control cost1, remainder evidence cost); learned q=vH in {0,7/2,6}; identity cost in {0,4,8}; teaching-capture cost in {1/2,2}; bypass cost in {1/2,2}. Other witness parameters fixed; reserve raid disabled in this main grid. This is 5,832 parameter combinations. Also solve each with a fresh operator (discount0) to isolate persistence; it changes the game, not merely the solver. There are 11,664 primary runs.

Repeat the persistent grid with reversed tie priorities as a sensitivity check. Record counts, actual harm, protected productive episodes, withdrawals, learning/architecture decisions, bought inspection/control, attacks, cash prefixes, and operator values. Do not collapse harmless withdrawal into successful protected production. Do not aggregate inherited or repeated checks as additional independent experiments.

Named controls include the positive witness, no recipient alignment, no usable private learning return, free identity filter, cheap teaching capture, cheap gate bypass, deceptive cue only, no physical control, insufficient opening cash, a bootstrap trap with b=ell, cheap reserve raid, expensive reserve raid, and an otherwise affordable first gate followed by resource failure. Use explicit technology toggles where a capability is absent. At least one named low-resource game must inspect the full strategic continuation.

Independent review will reconstruct the finite game separately and compare selected cases, and check the analytic inequalities and indefinite argument without importing production decisions. Numeric evidence corroborates conditional proofs; this study establishes neither real-world prevalence nor the ethically adequate scope of represented patients.
