# What the recently completed work contributes to a generative basis

Research integration, 5 October 2026. AI-generated analysis under Ren's direction. ## 1. Main conclusion

The recent work supplies a substantial **test suite for a proposed generative basis**. A basis should generate the examples and retain their counterexamples under the relevant interventions. A representation that erases them has lost explanatory content even if it reproduces aggregate output.

The most consequential common result is that adequate state is relative to what must remain answerable and executable. A sufficient state for physical prediction can be insufficient for model revision, successor inference, or continuing participation. Consequently, a coverage theorem needs an explicit interface of observations, interventions, feasible actions, and evaluations. Preserving only an uncontrolled transition law is too weak.

None of the recent work derives every institutional update, every new concept, the formation of evaluative purposes, or the reproduction of enforcement. Nor does it establish that every system requires all of the distinctions below as independent mathematical primitives. They are distinctions the proposed language must be able to express; a lower-level construction could derive several jointly.

## 3. Six compact witnesses for testing the new language

These examples are small enough to use as translation checks. The proposed representation should preserve their premises and conclusions, rather than restating each conclusion as a primitive law.

**A. Relative success does not determine absolute support.** In a pure-selection description, replacing absolute populations by `a(t) n_i(t)` and adding `d log(a)/dt` to all growth rates leaves shares unchanged. A representation containing only shares cannot answer an absolute viability question over that model class. Additional observations or causal restrictions can break the ambiguity. This is an identification obstruction, not a claim that abundance is always an independent primitive.

**B. Identical current summary, opposite revision.** Histories `00010` and `01000` both have final state zero, two switches and four transitions. These statistics suffice for the supplied unknown homogeneous switching-rate family. After equal-prior slow-then-fast and fast-then-slow models are admitted, next-need probabilities become approximately `.44835` and `.05165`; the response at threshold `.3` differs. A candidate abstraction must therefore declare the family of future questions and interventions under which it is sufficient. Universal preservation of arbitrary distinctions in a finite record requires an injective encoding; a cheaper summary can still be sufficient for a restricted task.

**C. No old anomaly, useful new question.** In the prospective-revision construction, E and U have the same entire permitted four-switch archive law, while a new linkage from order to later need is useful only in U. For any gate based on that archive, admission rates in the two worlds coincide. More generally, their difference is at most the total variation of the available data laws. Fresh linked observations can discriminate. A generating scheme in which all candidate formation must be triggered by a detectable old residual would exclude this example.

**D. Positive average receipt, eventual outage.** Let operating expense be one, accessible reserve at most two, and credited receipts independently be three or zero with equal probability. The uncapped mean net receipt is `+.5`, but two consecutive zero receipts cause absorbing shutdown. Mean lifetime from full reserve is six. This requires the buffer bound and absence of restart: unbounded saving or an actual replenishment route changes the conclusion. It is not a universal indictment of success-linked support or a claim that practical short-horizon reliability must be poor.

**E. Initial agreement, later refusal.** In the one-period participation model, passive loss is `−.65` for each half-owner. A fair initial repair assignment yields expected loss `−.80` each, but the revealed worker faces `−.30` and refuses. Shared-pool compensation `k` produces worker loss `−.30−k/2` and other-owner loss `−1.30+k/2`, so both accept exactly for `.7 ≤ k ≤ 1.3`. Repair costs three; divisible prefunding needs at least `3.7`, while the supplied integer payout grid needs four. These numbers depend on ownership, timing, fallback and commitment. For another terminal belief, aggregate gains are negative and no payment solves the objection.

**F. Correction after the deadline.** V7.3 S17 supplies a finite construction whose temporary intervention and durable structural cut reach a protected region while a conservative comparison trajectory preserves the host. Increasing installation delay in its adverse numerical case crosses the floor despite eventual execution of the same destination. The theorem does not explain who supplies enforceable authority, initial funds, recurring revenue or their continuation. A primitive that only records a final favorable equilibrium loses the safety question.

## 4. Composition rules suggested by these results

1. **Preserve causal chronology.** Paid acquisition precedes evidence, evidence precedes its permissible use, and due expenses precede future receipts unless credit is explicitly available. An intervention can change both information and later resources.
2. **Preserve actual information.** A sender's silence, visible readiness, public lotteries and disclosed recommendations can all carry evidence. Removing a payload is not sufficient to establish absence of communication. A physical-state compression must not silently erase decision-relevant promises or observations.
3. **Keep feasibility distinct from actual behavior.** A policy witness establishes that a route exists under its permissions and resources. It does not derive the update, adoption, selection, authority or commitment processes that implement it.
4. **Keep plural evaluations outside mechanical identities.** Nonnegative separating weights certify the infeasibility of stated expected constraints; they do not discover a shared social utility. Actual preferences and rules may be represented as evolving state, while the legitimacy of their change requires an explicit evaluative standpoint.
5. **Treat maintenance and replacement as different paths to function.** An outage followed by feasible replacement may preserve a long-run function while violating uninterrupted-service requirements. Preserving the original provider is not a universal requirement.
6. **Distinguish a property from its certificate.** A failed sufficient inequality does not prove impossibility. Conversely, a local derivative, average return or favorable long-run equilibrium does not certify every transient or policy.
7. **Close boundary conditions only where needed by the claim.** Internalizing a corrector's funding but leaving productive output exogenous closes one dependence, not every dependence. Neither arbitrary regress nor a declaration that everything is endogenous establishes generative completeness.

These are requirements on a useful interpretation and abstraction, not a proof that exactly seven irreducible generating operations exist. Low-level implementations can realize several requirements with a common state-transition mechanism. Their explanatory value lies in retaining the intervention distinctions.

## 5. Status of the integrated ambition

**Already available:** conditional theorems for balance, observability, recurrence, sufficiency, finite feasibility, participation and one safe transition; checked constructed counterexamples; bounded candidate generation and validation; several exact finite computations. Their common methods are established mathematics. Numerical verification does not establish cross-domain applicability.

**Candidate synthesis:** activities alter the conditions under which later activity, inquiry, transmission and correction can occur. A situation-specific generative account can include multiple goals, no single scalar optimizer, changing representations and feedback between levels. The [generative-capacity memo](../../../../../_process/history/2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/generative-capacity.md) already proposes a relational profile of achievable protected functions, not a universal scalar or a uniquely complete taxonomy.

**Now formally established for a restricted supplied class:** [FORMAL.md](../formal/FORMAL.md) constructs every finite rational controlled kernel from bounded programs of bit tests, bit writes, rational coins and sequencing at atomic macrostep boundaries. It also gives the necessary-and-sufficient faithful-quotient condition and a coarsest-partition algorithm preserving the declared intervention and label contract. The construction supplies expressiveness and reduction; it can encode row probabilities without explaining their origin.

**Additional structural step:** [STRUCTURAL-CLOSURE.md](../formal/STRUCTURAL-CLOSURE.md) proves that closing a protected seed under all declared local read dependencies gives a faithful coordinate projection, with the full joint noise and observation restrictions preserved. Its explicit ten-coordinate system derives a nine-coordinate boundary from short local equations; a new badge-dependent authority mechanism expands that boundary. This supplies a useful sufficient decomposition rule beyond row-table encoding, while leaving the local equations and protected seed supplied. The [independent review](../checks/REVIEW.md) checks the proof and its complete finite example.

**Still unproved:** that the substantive biological and institutional domain has the required finite representation; that a restricted explanatory basis derives its transition mechanisms rather than encoding them separately; that the chosen semantic modules are minimal or unique; that their restrictions predict real institutional dynamics; that unrestricted concept or purpose formation has been explained. Existing finite grammars merely make particular forms of novelty executable.

**Normative premise, not mechanical deduction:** stable coercive systems can reproduce their conditions. Viability, reproduction, correction, novelty and consent relative to a worsened fallback are therefore not individually sufficient for ethical alignment. N01 explicitly protects shared agency and legitimate revision. A complete descriptive language would also describe domination; it would not thereby select the acceptable use of that language.

## 6. What to carry into the first completeness theorem

Use a declared class of systems and a declared experimental interface. Require an encoding/decoding to preserve relevant joint histories under each allowed intervention, including information available to decision makers and the feasible choices they can actually execute. Specify whether the theorem is exact, approximate, finite-horizon or asymptotic. Treat changing parameters, models, permissions and evaluative rules as explicit objects that can themselves be changed by declared mechanisms; representing such changes is distinct from deriving their empirical update laws.

The examples above can then serve three roles: instances generated by the language; counterexamples to overaggressive abstraction; and counterexamples to claims that one abstract property alone ensures alignment. This makes recent work cumulative without presenting the collection of successful translations as a universal completeness proof.
