# Protocol: distinguishing causes of failed correction

5 October 2026 task date. AI-generated research under Ren's direction. This protocol precedes the new computations but follows inspection of the existing closure-study results. It is a theory-directed extension, not a blind prediction or external validation.

## Fixed inputs and questions

Use the unchanged 72-state model in `../closure-study/analyze.py`, its event order, parameters, and initial state. Do not fit or retune it. The known equality of routine and capacity outcomes between blocked admission and blocked execution motivates the study.

1. Does this equality extend to the full projected path law, not merely a selected outcome mean?
2. Which specified restoration interventions distinguish the failure mechanisms?
3. Can individually ineffective restorations jointly improve the chosen outcome?
4. Does the sign depend on evidence quality, and does the diagnostic endpoint depend on the presence of outcome-induced attrition?
5. How does irreversible loss before restoration limit later restoration?

## Comparisons

Hypotheses are the baseline, admission blocked, execution blocked, both blocked, and training blocked. These are stipulated mechanisms, not inferred real institutions. Interventions enable admission, execution, training, admission plus execution, or all three, beginning immediately. They preserve all other parameters. Restoration is externally supplied for this diagnostic study: these interventions do not explain who can enact them.

Primary endpoint: probability that skilled capacity has remained positive through 200 periods. Separate endpoint: mean routine adequacy over those periods. Retain the earlier plural-outcome caveat: higher retention or adequacy does not automatically dominate total completed or adequate-case counts.

Use evidence accuracy 0, .5, .95, and 1 for the two-gate factorial interaction. This tests a supplied information mechanism; accuracy 0 is systematically misleading, not a plausible estimate of every real inquiry process. Recompute intervention signatures with failure-induced attrition zero to determine when capacity survival cannot reveal the information/implementation mechanism.

For delayed restoration, evolve the admission-blocked or execution-blocked system for 0, 25, 100, or 200 periods, then enable all gates, following it for another 200 periods. Report absolute-time and post-restoration horizons explicitly. Since no external entry is supplied and zero skill is absorbing, success over the entire horizon cannot exceed survival up to the restoration time. Do not condition away systems that failed before intervention.

## Verification

Prove the observational equality by coupling and check the event-labeled projected transition distributions on every related reachable-state pair. The projection includes context, routine, installed practice, and skill; observed events exclude admission and memory records. The first two are different from each other only in an internal causal gate and the resulting stored records. If admitted records are independently observable, observational indistinguishability no longer applies.

Check all kernels are stochastic, reproduce previous principal results, report maximum transition-equivalence residual, and check the delayed-restoration bound. Preserve the unmodified parent model hash. No significance tests on these enumerated model probabilities are appropriate.

## Transfer claim

Use the existing finite-horizon total-variation coupling bound only under matched, implementable interventions and event-preserving state maps. Observational equivalence does not establish this condition. A published ecological intervention may illustrate one relevant causal mechanism; it cannot establish a shared intervention model with the institutional dataset without testing the required map.
