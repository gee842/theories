# Ongoing revision can repair frames and consume their supporting capacity

5 October 2026. This targeted extension was frozen after the baseline limitation was identified and before its own numerical execution. The [extension protocol](REOPENING-PROTOCOL.md), [separate implementation](reopening.py), and [complete output](reopening-analysis.json) preserve the original source and result hashes. Sixteen checks pass; maximum mass discrepancy is3.11×10⁻¹⁵. At most841 states are reachable at a round. Computation enumerates the finite distribution, without Monte Carlo sampling or probability pruning.

The sole changed rule allows an admitted complaint to reopen inquiry despite an installed frame. Ordinary service continues using that frame while five fresh experimental records accumulate. Their empirical winner replaces the incumbent, whether or not it is actually better. Each completed batch consumes the pending complaint. No physical correctness label enters that rule.

**Endpoint scope clarified by audit:** the JSON's `plural_success` evaluates the retained, executable ordinary-service response, independent of the next work portfolio. A correct frame with a pending complaint can meet this endpoint while the institution next chooses randomized inquiry, whose expected harm is.50 in every context. The endpoint is therefore **not whole-behavior compliance or all-period safety**. The .20 harm caps are evaluated for ordinary service; extending them to every experimental case would make Q violate them. The inquiry-harm ledger remains separate, and permission to impose those harms is a substantive normative premise, not supplied by the endpoint. This corrects the label without altering the frozen calculation.

Each row uses50 effort units: ordinary cases plus inquiry cases plus record slots plus training slots. More investigation reduces ordinary case volume and adds experimental harms. Lower ordinary harm counts cannot therefore be interpreted without the changed volume. Total harm counts rise slightly under reopening in both comparisons:14.847→15.024 for endogenous succession and11.660→11.712 under protection. Simultaneously the protected retained ordinary response becomes more likely to meet all declared expected constraints. Those are different outcomes, not contradictory measurements or a supplied scalar verdict.

Feedback changes the timing of revision. Badly performing installed rules produce complaints more frequently; correct ones still produce occasional harms because physical response noise is nonzero. This allows repair without a perfectly informed trigger, while also producing unnecessary or damaging reconsideration. Under endogenous succession, inquiry outcomes affect turnover and can remove the skill required for future inquiry. Preserving only the opportunity to propose a revision does not preserve all these supporting conditions.

## Continuing revision does not imply a permanently correct ordinary response

The earlier two-round skill-loss bound still applies to the unprotected reopening arm: inquiry does not add carriers, training can restore at most two before a single loss, and a finite-weight volume successor can omit training. Positive finite-horizon success is therefore compatible with eventual loss of all skill in this closed version.

Under protected succession, skill remains positive. The modeled repeated inquiry continues almost surely: ordinary cases have harm probability at leastν=.1, each harm has positive complaint-admission probability, and training returns a one-carrier state to two before turnover, with probability at least.75 of remaining at two. A pending batch therefore obtains its finite number of fresh query cases almost surely. Package retention prevents an unfinished batch from being erased.

Every fresh five-case inquiry nevertheless has conditional probability at leastν⁵=10⁻⁵ of receiving response-noise bit1 on every case. Then the true expression has five mismatches and its reversal has zero, so the actual correct expression cannot be nominated. This event guarantees a physically noncompliant replacement in the protected arm. With indefinitely many batches, the chance that all of the firstN replacements avoid even this particular error event is at most\((1-10^{-5})^N\), tending to zero. Independent block errors are not required beyond the stated conditional fresh-noise law.

This is not an estimate of the actual false-replacement frequency; the bound identifies only one error pattern. It establishes that fixed-size repeated reconsideration with permanently positive error cannot guarantee unbroken correctness. A lifetime guarantee would require a different declared error policy, a safe implementation rule, larger evidence obligations, or a weaker criterion such as recovery or acceptable error frequency. Those changes would have their own work and legitimacy requirements.

The extension consequently closes a finite **ongoing-revision** join within the supplied grammar: retained commitments and installed frames can now be questioned by consequential experience, investigated using shared work, and replaced under uncertain evidence, while the ability to continue doing so survives or fails through the same succession process. The norms, reflection dispositions, supplied categories, admission rights and protected benchmark remain explicit assumptions. No claim of open-ended invention, legitimate universal norms, empirical institutional effects or cross-domain transfer follows from this calculation.
