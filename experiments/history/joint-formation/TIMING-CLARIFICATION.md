# Timing clarification before enumeration

After protocol commit `8a5bf6f`, the proof discussion identified a necessary clarification: the pre-learning controller chooses its architecture in advance, but an optional identity filter is paid and installed **after** the commercial calibration receipt R, before the learned action decision. The training expense a and subsistence ell still precede R. This lets both architectures be affordable at the primary opening reserve W=5, so shared routing wins on the compared private costs D=3 versus j=4, rather than because the identity alternative is infeasible. This clarification is recorded before running the frozen grid; none of its parameter values or declared comparisons changes.

Each cold and learned self evaluates only its own current episode. The persistent operator evaluates actual continuation, including future controllers and their money. Continuing possession of the shared account, the calibration trade, the fresh controller's current-money objective, and the ability to install the declared input gate are explicit environmental and architectural premises. Incoming reconstruction is paid before the first consequential operation. The model does not infer voluntary outgoing bequest or continuous protection during an omitted handoff interval; there is no production during its modeled setup stages.
