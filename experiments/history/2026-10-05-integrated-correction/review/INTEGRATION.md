# Integration review: a fee-funded, selected correction package

5 October 2026. Mechanism and integration review by an AI research agent. This is a bounded extension of the existing programme, not an external scientific review, empirical validation, or originality claim. This memo's model is being coordinated with the mathematical lead; the final checked model statement takes precedence where notation or details change.

## The missing connection and the smallest useful join

V7.3 already connects capture, effective liability, selection of harmful activity and a host viability bound (S16), then constructs a finite funded transition into that region (S17). It assumes executable reform authority and continuing maintenance. The renewal programme separately makes corrective capacity depend on expenditures and makes maintenance practices spread through specified transmission channels. The situated-correction note distinguishes evidence, interpretation, interests and implementation rights.

The smallest additional result should join **paid information and implementation capacity, actual production, and selection of the rule that funds those capacities**. Adding a universal social objective, unrestricted cognitive novelty or every political mechanism would obscure this test. The proposed join is deliberately smaller: a correction package can produce additional useful output, pay its own maintenance, and remain attractive to adopt; the same parameters can also permit collapse from depleted initial conditions.

## A task that supplies a benefit without an external corrective proposal

Each local task has an independently balanced latent condition, L or R. A correctly matched action produces b+Δ and an incorrect action produces b, with b≥0 and Δ>0. Every uninformed fixed action or mixture yields y₀=b+Δ/2. A funded test supplies a signal that identifies the condition with probability α∈(1/2,1]; the signal is not a perfect oracle unless α=1. Acting on that information also requires a functioning authorization-and-execution channel. If either is unavailable, the operator uses an uninformed response. The analyst represents the condition in order to define the generative process; the operator obtains information only through the test.

Let C measure a **paired infrastructure package**. Each of its two functions is available with probability C/(1+C), conditionally independently. Consequently, the probability that both are usable is

q(C) = [C/(1+C)]².

The usable signal adds B=Δ(α−1/2), so expected incremental output is Bq(C). The canonical binary task therefore restricts 0<B≤y₀. This is a stated finite task technology, not an oracle for unrepresented future hazards. The model does not yet distinguish different forms of evidence or permit discovery of a new model class. The sign and size of B must be tested in an application; α=1/2, hence B=0, is an essential negative control. Proofs and examples for the canonical model should retain A=χB≤B≤y₀; a larger-context task could change that domain but is not part of this result.

C is one maintained *package*, not two independently financed copies of the same resource. The conversion efficiency η must account for the cost of renewing both functions. Independent physical stocks would require two funding channels and a larger model.

## Output, funding and selection

Let x be the prevalence of the package-using and package-funding arrangement, y₀>0 baseline output, and χ∈[0,1] the recoverable private fraction of the incremental output. The remainder is a common spillover. An adopter produces private gross receipts

r(C) = y₀ + χ B q(C),

pays fraction τ∈(0,1) of those receipts into infrastructure, and retains (1−τ)r(C). A nonadopter retains y₀. The common spillover (1−χ)xBq(C) enters both returns equally and therefore cancels from their comparison. Adoption responds to realized retained returns:

xdot = κ x(1−x)[(1−τ)r(C)−y₀],

Cdot = η τ x r(C)−μC.

κ,η,μ>0. The second equation has no external infrastructure subsidy. At x=0 capacity decays; at x=C=0 the model cannot recreate the package spontaneously. The rule deciding whether to fund the package is endogenous through x. The correctness of receipts, the ability to debit the agreed fee and the exclusion of nonpayers from the private package remain implementation assumptions.

The accounting closes exactly. Across the population, retained private receipts, common spillovers and maintenance expenditures sum to gross output:

x(1−τ)r(C) + (1−x)y₀ + (1−χ)xBq(C) + τxr(C)
= y₀ + xBq(C).

Thus the maintenance advantage is neither a free bonus nor a transfer counted twice. The productive feedstock and baseline opportunities still lie outside the boundary. This is not a resource-complete ecosystem or a model of the creation of legitimate property rights.

## Joint adoption and funding feasibility

Write A=χB>0. Adoption ceases to decline when

q(C) ≥ q_s := τy₀/[(1−τ)A].

If 0<q_s<1, set z=√q_s and C_s=z/(1−z). At C_s, the capacity-maintenance requirement is x≥x_s, where

x_s = μ C_s(1−τ)/(ητy₀).

The quadrant x≥x_s, C≥C_s is forward invariant whenever x_s≤1. At the x boundary, the adoption derivative is nonnegative; at the C boundary, the capacity derivative is nonnegative. Strict x_s<1 gives a nonempty region with slack. This is a sufficient protected set, not the maximal viable set or an empirically calibrated safety bound.

Substituting τ=Az²/(y₀+Az²) yields the simpler expression

x_s = μ/[ηA z(1−z)].

Because max z(1−z)=1/4, **some fee supports a strict invariant region exactly when ηχB>4μ**, in the normalization where the availability half-scale is one capacity unit. With q(C)=[C/(K+C)]², the corresponding threshold is ηχB>4μK.

When the inequality is strict, permissible z lie between

z_± = [1±√(1−4μ/(ηχB))]/2,

and permissible fees lie between the images τ(z_−) and τ(z_+). At equality the slack vanishes; it should not be presented as robust persistence. The midpoint z=1/2 minimizes the sufficient prevalence threshold. It does not optimize welfare, protected outcomes, recovery time or institutional legitimacy.

The distinction has substantive meaning: stronger appropriation can make self-funding selection feasible, but neither maximizing fees nor maximizing private retention follows. Too little financing leaves renewal unsupported; too much deduction eliminates adoption's relative advantage. χ=0 selects against costly maintenance in this model, even though common output can benefit from it. That is a failure condition, not a universal recommendation to privatize public goods.

An admissible witness is b=0, Δ=2, α=.9, y₀=1, B=.8, χ=1, η=10 and μ=K=κ=1. Choosing τ=1/6 gives z=1/2, C_s=1 and x_s=1/2. This respects the binary-task bound and has a strict self-maintenance margin. The final verification record supplies the numerical trajectories and root checks.

## A finite funded initiation, without assuming spontaneous recovery

A one-time external intervention can be distinguished from an inexhaustible controller. Suppose a positive seed population already uses the package, x₀>0, and the selected fee has x_s<1. Pick C₁>C_s and a target x_T∈(x_s,1). Put

g₁=(1−τ)r(C₁)−y₀>0,

T=max{0,[logit(x_T)−logit(x₀)]/(κg₁)}.

Inject capacity once so that its post-injection level satisfies C_seed≥C₁ exp(μT). Under the stated conversion technology, the one-time external expenditure is max(0,C_seed−C_initial)/η. This assumes capacity can be purchased or installed at that rate without delay; an actual construction delay or imported expertise would need its own constraint.

Because Cdot≥−μC, capacity remains at least C₁ until T. During that interval the adoption equation is bounded below by the logistic equation with advantage g₁. Therefore x(T)≥x_T and C(T)≥C₁. The state has entered the invariant rectangle, and no further subsidy is required under the remaining model premises.

This is a sufficient construction, not the least-cost or fastest intervention. Its expenditure can be very large from a tiny adoption seed. It does not explain why an external funder undertakes the injection or why the rights to enact it exist. If x₀=0, then x remains zero and every finite capacity injection eventually decays: founding or introducing adopters is a separate requirement. Changing τ alone similarly leaves the exact origin absorbing and, for each fixed admissible positive fee, leaves a local collapse basin. Better parameters do not by themselves reconstruct the lost institutions.

## Distinguishing evidence from authority empirically

The common capacity C imposes balanced joint investment. Its square success probability encodes serial complementarity but does not identify distinct political and epistemic dynamics. For a diagnostic, split evidence capacity C_e and implementation capacity C_a, debit both from a fixed total maintenance expenditure, and let q=f(C_e)f(C_a). With equal depreciation and conversion and a balanced expenditure split, the paired-stock model is an invariant special case after accounting for both renewal costs.

A factorial intervention can vary informative evidence and executable decision rights while matching total resources. Record three intermediate outcomes separately: accuracy of the proposed response, the fraction of correct proposals that can be enacted, and realized output. The serial mechanism predicts that informative evidence can improve proposals without improving output when implementation is blocked. Restoring implementation with no informative evidence permits uninformed action. Joint restoration yields the task benefit. These are possible experimental predictions, not observations from a new experiment.

Holding only total capital fixed and comparing balanced with skewed investment is weaker: many ordinary productive complements can give the same outcome pattern. The evidence/authority interpretation earns content from the observed proposal and enactment dissociation. Even that result would not establish the legitimacy of the decision rights or explain how those rights survive political amendment.

## Interpretation limits to retain in the final result

- The model permits competing rules **within a declared choice set**, not arbitrary corrupt amendments. A nonpaying type that can retain private access would need its own state and payoff; the current exclusion premise prevents that invasion by assumption.
- Effective test and response availability vary endogenously with a paid stock. The constitutional mechanism supporting conditional access and fee collection does not.
- Aggregate public benefits are not silently taxed a second time. Adding a public levy would change both the resource equation and the mechanism whose authority needs explanation.
- A stable or invariant correction package is not necessarily legitimate. The local task establishes one productive benefit; it does not settle conflicts over social objectives, rights or coercive use.
- The model joins part of the chain. It does not yet couple shared ecological support, changing representation, political resistance, arbitrary successor types and unknown harms in one system.
- Proofs and numerical checks can establish what these equations imply. The reproduction of the same phenomenon under different sampling rules, task distributions and access mechanisms, and its external predictive value, remain separate work.

The candidate central claim is therefore bounded: **a socially useful correction procedure can exist but lack a self-maintaining, selectively viable implementation; under explicit recoverability, funding and initial-capacity conditions, the same procedure can instead finance the information and execution capacity needed for its own continued use.**

## Independent implementation and proof spot-check

I subsequently read the completed `model/CONSTRUCTION.md` and root `verify_model.py`. No blocking discrepancy was found between the stated canonical model, its proofs and the implementation. This is a bounded internal AI review, not independent human certification.

An independent import and calculation reproduced C_s≈1, x_s≈.5, C_R≈2.317295406891308 and the stated fee interval. Centered finite differences of the two-dimensional vector field matched the analytic Jacobian with maximum discrepancy approximately 1.13×10⁻¹⁰ at an interior state. Direct task enumeration gives proposal accuracy .7 with the evidence channel and .5 without; at C=1, both channels yield output 1.2, while either disabled channel yields 1.0. The accounting of public spillovers and actual fees is consistent.

The implementation integrates prevalence in log-odds coordinates, avoiding artificial loss of a rare practice through clipping at x=0 or x=1. Displayed prevalence can round to a boundary, but the saved log odds retain the state used in continued integration. Capacity can acquire small numerical negative errors near zero within the declared tolerance; there is no observed consequential crossing or physical negative-capacity regime. The analytic nonnegative invariance, rather than rounded terminal values, establishes the intended limiting claim.

The disabled-evidence and disabled-execution controls retain the fee, and free access removes the private participation advantage. Poor private recoverability and fees outside the viable interval are also checked. The externally held-capacity control deliberately violates self-financing by providing capacity from outside; its success must be read as the consequence of that added subsidy, not as an alternative arrangement with the same resource budget. Setting task informativeness to chance or B=0 would duplicate the no-incremental-benefit dynamics already checked through disabled channels, though it would remain a useful empirical control in a later task experiment.
