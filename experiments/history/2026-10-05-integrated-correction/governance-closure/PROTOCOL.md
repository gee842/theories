# Testing paid observation and executable rule revision

5 October 2026. Internal AI-assisted mathematical and simulation protocol, written after deriving the candidate construction and before running its trajectory comparisons. This is not an independent preregistration or empirical study. Earlier viability-control results are known. The new experiment is kept separate from those artifacts.

## Question

Can the specified institution finance observation, shared computation and rule actuation from its existing revenue, while its governance workers can shirk, report arbitrarily or issue arbitrary commands? Which residual physical and institutional assumptions are necessary? The target is conditional closure of operating costs and a finite threat class, not an assumption-free account of legitimate authority.

## Frozen construction

Divert gamma=.02 of fees F to governance W=gamma F. Multiply both old operating allocations by 1−gamma. Use p(G)=G/(1.1+G), with 1.1 an assumed extra inspection-congestion parameter rather than an empirically measured labor surcharge. No other operating conversion coefficient improves.

Three governance practices are truthful maintainers a, lying maintainers b, and shirkers c. Let z=a+b and theta=a/z. Both maintainers spend d=.3 of their budget on the governance service and earn (1−d)W; shirkers earn (1−D)W when existing effective checks D reject false delivery claims. Thus zdot=100*z*(1−z)*W*(D−d), and theta is conserved within the specified imitation dynamics. Lying maintainers are neutral competitors, not detected semantic liars. Canonical theta=.9.

Governance capacity J obeys Jdot=2500*d*z*W−J. This paid package includes measurement, aggregation, computation, communication and actuator maintenance. Honest instrument error is bounded by .002/J for J>0, with no informative guarantee at J=0. Actuation capacity is R(J)=J/(1+J); requested commands are clipped to its magnitude. Seed J is an initial endowment, not generated freely.

Authenticated weighted medians aggregate G and m reports; honest controllers compute a common robust command from those aggregates; another weighted median selects the command. An honest reporting/command mass theta*z>1/2 bounds the observation error and selects the honest command despite arbitrary other messages. Identity weights and the nonbypassable aggregation/actuation interface remain declared architecture. This is not a theorem about unconstrained distributed consensus or political legitimacy.

The certified region uses x,h≥.9, C≥2.5, G≥2.1, m≥.48, .9*G/(1.1+G)−m−.02≥0, z≥.9, J≥1. On it, sensor error≤.002, actuation capacity≥.5, and truthful command mass≥.81. The robust filter uses the lower operating budget implied by the region, not an oracle observation of h. Alpha=4; maximum requested rate=.5. The proof will show joint feasibility and invariance under the unchanged finite practice repertoire.

## Evidence and interpretation

Check the full flow ledger, selected derivatives against the earlier model after declared parameter changes, median containment with arbitrary minority packets, feasibility inequalities, and selected trajectories using a second numerical solver. Keep operating-floor breaches, loss of truthful majority, loss of financed capacity, target shortfall and operating depletion separate. Record source and protocol hashes.

The error technology is a deterministic conditional model assumption, not a statistical confidence interval inferred from finite audits. Continuous observation and the error bound do not automatically validate sampled or delayed control. A bounded-latency extension needs an explicit rate/error budget. Any additions after examining results will be recorded as exploratory.

Closure claims must retain initial conditions, majority preservation within the modeled dynamics, physical calibration, authentic delivery evidence, settlement, membership and command-path assumptions. Removing all such assumptions is not an available theorem. Separate impossibility results concern indistinguishable states that require incompatible actions, not a blanket claim that every safe fixed policy needs fresh observations.
