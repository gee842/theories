# Declared protocol: paid bound-fuel access and full succession

5 October 2026. Declared before the new state enumeration or probability calculation. This is an AI-assisted model test, not an experiment with physical molecules. The old model and results remain unchanged.

Use the complete N=4 CF-storage parent in [Useful retention](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/USEFUL-RETENTION-MECHANISM.md), with exactly its three proposed new channel families:

- D+CF -> R+C, at mass-action rate h D CF;
- R+CF -> A_i+C, at rate s_i R CF for i=0,1;
- A_i+R+CF -> A_i+G_i+C, at rate q_i A_i R CF for i=0,1.

Retain every original reaction and both competing fabricator/gate types. Do not add CF -> C+F or bound-packet copying A_i+R+CF -> 2A_i+C. CF does not catalyze free-packet recycling D+F+CF -> R+CF in this specified version; the parent explicitly left that optional channel undecided. The new D+CF reaction supplies the declared access to its packet.

All positive rate constants are set to one, including both fabricator-copy rates, free and bound-packet leakage, source capture, and all three new channel constants. Type0 has zeta0=0, type1 has zeta1=1. This is one fixed moderate-rate generator; no rates or target are to be tuned in response to the sign. Changing rates or adding reactions requires a separately labeled follow-up protocol.

The common initial state is Z0: R3,Y1,F2, with all other species absent. The externally maintained source can charge an empty C via C -> CF; this supplied condition is retained throughout the reference assay. At the first fresh capture, require entry into some P_ij: A_i=YG_j=CF=1, all other species zero. Mark the four A,C,G,Y carriers then present. At the seventh fresh capture, require return to that same count/type state with all four marked carriers having worn. Both branches face this same type-neutral target. Let I=i mean the first actual reaction forms A_i through its template-free free-packet channel; compare J_i(H)=P(I=i, full event by H).

Primary declared deadline: H=10 in the model's time units. Also permit analytic short-time leading-order comparison and an undiscounted hitting comparison as distinctly labeled questions; neither substitutes for the H=10 result. All failed preparations and competing reactions remain in their probability mass. Tags and counters are analyst observations, not actor controls. No success-conditioned averaging is allowed.

Before the seventh capture, total packets U=F+CF+GF0+GF1 are at most eight. N=4 and capture/entry/carrier markers make the stopped process finite. The exact work ledger can reject states that can no longer attain the minimum-cost target; each rejection must be justified, not chosen because the path is inconvenient. A finite numerical result must disclose its approximation/error method. An exact path lower bound establishes only that event's probability, not the whole comparison.

The structural questions are: whether the new channels actually permit full carrier turnover; what its minimum capture cost is; whether the original retained-scaffold advantage survives this broader event; and which use of reproduction makes any favorable or adverse sign possible. Even a positive result would not establish total architecture prevalence, reliable indefinite succession, thermodynamic realization of this CF extension, or feasible molecular kinetics.
