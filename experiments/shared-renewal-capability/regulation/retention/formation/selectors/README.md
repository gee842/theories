## What was executed

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) predates the numerical comparisons. [model.py](model.py) represents a persistent compartment, finite inherited reserve, an initially absent selector, an external finite packet stream and a hidden changing condition. A selector maps cue b to response b XOR m. Actual captured fuel pays template-preserving renewal; failures consume fuel and selector lifetime. Expiry permits paid reconstruction of either mapping. Observation-only metrics do not drive transitions.

[check.py](check.py) evaluates all histories with exact rational arithmetic: **11 cases × 5 arms**, plus **945** deterministic-capture recovery diagnostics. [results.json](results.json) preserves exact values, decimal readings, every law, reachable-state counts and source hashes. The conventional random-restart automaton must equal the proposed material law as a complete distribution. Directed feedback is an additional strong comparator, with the same observations, replacement opportunities, feasible orientations and prices. Fixed mapping and outcome-blind maintenance are ablations.

The primary case gives expected captures 4.5 for material/random-automaton, 6 for directed feedback, 2 for fixed mapping and approximately 3.358391 for outcome-blind maintenance. The probability of a pre-change capture, paid mapping revision and subsequent post-change capture in the same compartment is 3/8 for material/random-automaton and 1/2 for directed feedback. Each statistic includes failed initial formation and insolvency. Final readiness is an inventory measure, not evidence of survival after the finite source ends.

The material law loses to fixed mapping on capture count in the two-reversal case (3.75 versus 4) and to the outcome-blind intervention at initial reserve three (2 versus approximately 2.59082). No superiority claim is supported. Successful upkeep itself can consume reserve needed for later revision.

## Verification and scope

[independent_check.py](independent_check.py) imports no producer transition code. It propagates additive moments over a compact state, whereas the producer stores counters in the full state. It reproduces all 13 metrics in all 55 arms and checks the recovery formula over 85,932 parameter combinations. Its saved record hashes sources and results. These are exact finite-model checks, not independent biological observations.

The [formal package](formal/README.md) checks 21 new Lean declarations for the noiseless event law, actual resource ledger, a within-compartment revision witness, a connection to the existing causal-root trace, and deterministic recovery costs. Analytical probability, Python refinement, the stochastic scheduler, noisy cases and ablations remain outside this certificate.
