6 October 2026. A finite continuation of the actual formed-predictor process:
**local graph formation → use → paid retention → consequence-funded guide
copying → a second local graph formation**. The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) precedes
the first run and records its pre-execution additions. The
[mathematical account](../../../../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-MATH.md)

## What forms

The original declaration supplies actor fuel30 and source121, plus finite typed
side materials and a separate recipient reserve4. The unchanged precursor
process commissions a predictor with probability2/9. Eight actual coupling
exposures leave four packets for this study; eligibility is1/6 over all
original histories. Eligibility reads live hardware and acquired confident
memory, never hidden truth. All failures and unused stocks remain accounted.

Each round fabricates receptor R and identity comparator D from actual parts.
Local bonds can connect R→P, R→D or D→P, where P is the inherited predictor.
R has one outgoing port. D passes a cue for the actor and blocks the recipient.
D→P can form before either R bond, and its expense remains even when unused.
Runtime activity propagates through these actual edges. Completed programme
labels are never sampled or consulted by construction.

A private capture pays for copying an actual P-docking guide; the added guide
changes the next round's primitive bond rates. With adequate parts and funds,
direct routing rises from1/2 to2/3 after one such copy. Spending exactly the
same fuel and blank on an inert guide keeps it1/2. Copying a D guide instead
makes it1/3. The latter countercase exposes the supplied return-to-template
chemistry: feedback can narrow as well as broaden later production.

This is a changed **production distribution**, not proof that broad routing
is favored by every subsequent retention process. Recipient opportunities
send no private receipt. Their expenses can instead reduce later maintenance.
