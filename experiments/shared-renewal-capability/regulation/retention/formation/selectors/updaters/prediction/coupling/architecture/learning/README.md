# Recipient-specific revision on a locally formed architecture

This exact finite continuation establishes a restricted connection: an actually formed retained graph can pay for a recipient return wire and a new use of its existing memory, revise a recipient candidate from costly evidence, preserve its actor record, then act again and remain retained. It does not derive faithful sensing, memory-address chemistry or selection for recipient benefit. The actual earlier motor-revision histories do **not** complete this entire continuation with the declared resources.

The [prospective protocol](PROTOCOL.md) precedes execution. [Protocol provenance](protocol-freeze.json) records both prospective versions and one later wording clarification: the scalar fuel bound also requires the direct recipient-transmitting path already enforced in code. The [model](model.py) imports the frozen [architecture parent](../README.md), preserves every preparation failure and full terminal state, and adds four inert packets at the original start. No actor fuel is added at handoff. Original total energy, including the separate recipient reserve, is 195.

## Mechanism and outcome

The fixed last graph/site 1 operates on A0, R1, A1, R2. After A0, one actual spare connector and one fuel make a feedback wire; two more fuel recode the inherited ten-state `q × last` carrier into eight states for actor bit, recipient candidate and qualification. The actual coupler remains in command generation and evidence decoding. A filtered graph still blocks recipient work and evidence. A partial retrofit can leave the old read-only response operational, but gives no learning evidence.

An admitted recipient trial prepays work, observation and write. A real zero while unqualified flips a candidate; it does not establish the opposite law under outages. A positive recipient receipt qualifies the issued command, subject to channel fidelity and continued target stability. Actor income never stands in for recipient receipt in the primary law. Both retained old graphs require a final paid hold.

Paid recoding has mass 47/810 and first recipient candidate change 31/810; full validated-and-retained continuation has smaller mass 22/810 = 11/405. Faithful feedback changes recipient outcomes without changing private fuel or retention relative to the matched absent channel. This model therefore supplies no private selection explanation for faithful recipient feedback. The private-zero control reproduces the primary behavior, but has no real-recipient-evidence witness. It damages the same-law response: faithful captures are 1/10, private-zero captures 59/810.

The prespecified counterregions matter. With 3/4 recipient availability, primary strict mass falls to 11/540. Under the same efficacy with outages, the faithful exploratory rule obtains 151/2160 captures, below the no-feedback 3/40: an unqualified good candidate can be overturned by an outage. Under reversal after a positive first receipt, qualification can become stale: faithful captures are 1/18, while the private-zero proxy happens to obtain 67/810. Observation or write cost 5, original reserve 20, no original connectors, and no architecture construction all give zero strict witnesses. Copying filter-compatible guides upstream lowers primary strict mass to 5/324. These are supplied finite regimes, not estimates of a universal advantage.

`195 = 4 preparation + 1 adapter + 68 prefix spending + 10 prefix spill + 37 architecture spending + 2 earlier recipient operations + 31 learning spending + 10 learning spill + 1 recipient operation + 1 damage + 0 actor remainder + 30 recipient remainder`.

For a direct site 1, correct inherited actor command, two retained graphs, spare connector, stable opposite recipient law and default prices, paid first revision requires entry fuel at least 6; full strict continuation requires at least 11. The latter ends at `F−11`. A solvent actual endpoint with fuel 13 but no spare connector cannot retrofit. Different obstructions must not be erased by handing a later stage nominally correct bits and fresh resources.

The full-history mass joining **earlier motor-coupling revision** to this strict recipient-learning witness is zero in the primary experiment. The exhibited success starts with an actually formed normal coupler whose motor revision flag is false. This joins formation and recipient correction; it does not join every earlier favorable mechanism into one successful history.

## Reproduction and outgoing interface

Run `python3 model.py` for exact results and `python3 check.py` for the complete witness and hand-derived boundaries. [The independent suffix checker](independent_check.py) and [handoff checker](handoff_check.py) are separate implementations; their receipts record exact coverage and source stability. The independent suffix audit passed 3,108 reachable comparisons (4,980 exogenous-draw branches), 72 complete aggregate and terminal-law comparisons, 15,120 configured comparisons, 24 missing-interface checks and 21 sharp-threshold checks with 14 stable source hashes. [Results](results.json) contain all twelve cases and six arms, full terminal laws, preparation failures and dependency hashes. See also the [math note](../../../../../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING-MATH.md).

Public interfaces are `Law.architecture_law()`, `parent_distribution(law)`, `entrance(law, architecture_state)`, `suffix(law, architecture_state, current_regime, arm)` and `compare(law)`. `State.parent` retains the entire predecessor state, including histories, graphs, guides and stocks; learning counters are separate. For indexed states, `parent.q = 2*a+r` and `parent.last = qualification`. A successor must preserve `indexed`, `wire`, coupler and retention, and use the new addressed command contract. Passing packed `q,last` straight to the old parent command decoder would be wrong. The older copied guides predate the recipient record and do not themselves copy or transmit this new relation.

Further derivation must explain production and retention of the feedback/recoding recipe, reliable target addressing and sensing, adaptive response to outages and reversals, and resource-feasible composition with previous motor revision. This study supplies a finite mechanism and its limits, not completion of that derivation.
