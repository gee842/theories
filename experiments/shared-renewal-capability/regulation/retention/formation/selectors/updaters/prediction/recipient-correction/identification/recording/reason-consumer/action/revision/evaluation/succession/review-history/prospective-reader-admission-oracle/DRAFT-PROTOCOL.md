## 2. One resource ledger and operational succession

Before handoff there is one live donor core. After handoff there is one live child core and a retired donor. A distinct identity label alone is insufficient: the old donor loses every read, inquiry, assessment and actuation transition, and only the child can own the newly commissioned window and port.

The global recipient balance is transferred between active-core views exactly once. It is not copied into two spendable `RecipientIdentification.State` values. Retired donor hardware, occupied parts, learned cells, old archives and their provenance remain. The donor's remaining actor fuel and unallocated old recipient packets become explicit inert owned compartments rather than vanishing or secretly seeding the child. This matters for the n = 0, gated and failed ancestors, even though both stocks are zero on the strict primary path. The three native source compartments stay isolated under their accepted law.

The physical reader and reason workspace are maintained and reused for this one finite interval. Their old evidence cells and bank metadata stay intact; cold invalidation clears only the active cache/publication. Every unused old monetary compartment is retained but quarantined at successful retirement: old actor fuel and packets, reader fuel and reason reserve, both old writer budgets, action-command/reader/source reserves, fresh-window actor/reader/source reserves, native training and later-service/source reserves, and existing idle accounts. The old reader's balance is moved to that inert ledger before its live account is funded only from the new 24. No old account subsidizes the child. Native source compartments remain separately denominated. Retired historical state in the analyst's source catalog is provenance, not another live core or a new runtime memory source.

A paid access adapter binds the child, the retained n cells, the maintained reader and the new bank. Its finite bindings distinguish these particular components; no universal identifier registry, cryptographic authentication or discovery of correspondence is claimed. Disconnecting the n path leaves n and the recipient facts intact but blocks an evaluative assessment. Disconnecting after evaluation reading invalidates its pending assessment. Filtering the recipient term is a different control: it acquires the same n and factual input, then excludes the recipient term.

The scope/permission input requires an explicit supplied **child-specific binding and reauthorization contract**: the new physical port is bound to the same named recipient, and an external grant names this child and that port. Assign child scope as donor scope AND the valid child/recipient binding AND the supplied scope grant; assign child permission as donor permission AND the supplied child permission grant, and child delivery as donor delivery AND the supplied child delivery grant. Thus a positive new grant never repairs an inherited false guard. The positive fixture explicitly supplies all three grants; denied permission is a separate case. Assembly alone cannot set them true. Their origin is a local input, not an ethical conclusion or a new authorization obtained from the real user. Rebinding/reauthorization cannot change the hidden efficacy law or replace the acquisition protocol.

This draft uses those technical guards for **both** scheduled recipient inquiry and the selected recipient trial, matching the accepted fresh-bank wrapper's ordinary pre-work checks. There is no unguarded query bypass. `child_grant_denied` therefore blocks inquiry and does not claim newly acquired facts. The separate `permission_cut_before_evaluation` intervention happens after completed paid inquiry/reading and retains those facts while blocking later delivery. A technical grant is not ethical authorization of either a probe or a selected act.

## 9. Reused laws, new interfaces and alternatives

The accepted copier supplies the paid actual-read/commit pattern and bounded q semantics. Its whole recipient-funded primary process is **not** silently reused: it copies only `(route,q)`, includes its own commissioning action and does not transport n, a qualified-positive updater, reasons or permission. `SuccessorLearning` establishes a real retained-child learning interface under its own funding and update law; this proposal reuses its separation of actual child, historical actor bit, finite access, transfers and sunk costs, not an assertion that its negative-feedback updater is RI.

The new obligations are the typed child assembly/retirement handoff, paid n/reader access and conservative single-core ownership change. After those obligations, fresh-bank steps should project to the accepted emission/RI laws; reader and reason steps to their accepted laws; selected child use to the acquired-evaluation command/effect contract. These are stage-wise proof lenses, not copied runtime parents or a claim that all stochastic histories are already one formal trace.

Alternatives considered and not yet executed:

1. **Full old copier commissioning.** It would require a separately available donor-hold unit, recipient financing, another source packet and a stable/available old-law commissioning interval, then still need the new RI and n-access interface. It preserves an exact old retained-child entry but adds a recipient-funded action unrelated to the named missing connection. It is not selected for this draft.
2. **Copy the learned coefficient.** Three further recipient cells and explicit read/write/copy validity would remove dependence on external n access. This changes cost, custody and failure tests and is unnecessary for one finite access-based successor.
3. **Same-carrier correction.** A new bank/port could close a narrower temporal correction gap but would not discharge actual evaluative succession. It is not substituted for this proposed combined history.
4. **Use exact old opening/reset.** This would honestly add two initial common units. The economical proposal instead pays for unknown initialization of a genuinely new child and empty window; it makes no exact `OpenStep` claim.

Before implementation: root and reviewer settle the capacity/guard contract, choose literal cases, save the independent oracle and freeze accepted parent sources/results plus this protocol. First code, outcomes and any failures must be preserved before repair. No current law or result is changed by this draft; ethical standing, complete comparison, original endowments, finite recipes and access rights remain supplied where stated.
