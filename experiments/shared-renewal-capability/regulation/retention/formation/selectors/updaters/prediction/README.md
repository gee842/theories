6 October 2026. Exact finite extension under the [canonical account](../../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/PREDICTION-ACTION.md). The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) was fixed before primary comparisons. This is a synthetic construction using supplied assembly, observation, writing and routing laws.

## The inherited process

Reuse the preceding updater study's actual `prepare` function: original F24 and source121, no A/H or module installed. Successful preparation and adapter supply fuel20 and twelve packets10 with probability2/9; failed histories retain their actual stocks. Old updater and selector formation cost 2+1, followed by a paid three-unit predictive retrofit in the same two slots. Initial operative fuel is 14. Slow/Fast algorithms are added functionality, not renamed old R/D semantics.

The circuit retains a five-valued q and last-action bit. Its command follows q above/below2 and last at the tie. Its actual capture receipt and action generate a paid teaching bit. Fast overwrites, Slow increments/decrements; the receipt decoder is exact only in the available noiseless complementary plant. Record retention, work/observation, writing, renewal and all replacements cost actual fuel. Replacement of the updater preserves circuit memory; replacement of the circuit resets it.

## Primary results

All values below include unsuccessful preparation. Expected trials are8/3 for the first five arms,13/9 for the frozen register and2/3 for each inverted arm.

| Arm | Captures | Strict correction-and-use probability | Trial-weighted Brier loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

| conventional | 19/9 | 2/9 | 109/768 |
| fixed_fast | 7/3 | 2/9 | 5/48 |
| fixed_slow | 17/9 | 2/9 | 35/192 |

| frozen_register | 2/3 | 0 | 1/4 |
| inverted_record | 1/9 | 0 | 65/96 |

The normal variable controller and conventional finite-state realization have identical full distributions. Fixed Fast is the equally priced strong comparator. `direct_record` has lower supplied retrofit cost1 and consequently retains more fuel; its observation, write and persistent-record retention remain paid. No costless memory comparator is used.

Forecasts are recorded before the receipt. Brier loss concerns the actual executed action's capture, including in the inverted-action arm. The reported ratio is total expected loss divided by expected scored trials, accompanied by trial coverage. It is neither an all-history accuracy probability nor a common-policy held-out test. Inactive arms have undefined conditional loss, not perfect accuracy.

The strict witness requires a previously used acquired prediction, a later unsuccessful inherited command, a paid q-write that changes the issued command while last is fixed, and productive use of that changed command. Circuit destruction clears unfinished witness flags; replacing only the updater preserves them. A completed historical witness is retained. These readouts never govern actions.

## Failures and accounting

The low-reserve12 case commissions the expensive hardware but cannot pay a first trial. The cheaper direct-record circuit obtains 11/9 expected captures. At yield6 the variable learner never completes the strict correction witness. At availability3/4 the unchanged receipt decoder mistakes some source outages for an unsuitable action; fixed Slow slightly exceeds fixed Fast in captures. All eight cases remain in the result file.

The inverted-action arm predicts many failures accurately while obtaining little support. Its lower conditional Brier score has lower sampling coverage and does not establish better control. An accurate predictor's evaluative/action coupling remains a separate mechanism.

`reserve` covers commissioned compartments. `uncommissioned_energy` retains failed preparation stocks. Whole energy is reserve + source remainder + uncommissioned energy + preparation expenditure + adapter expenditure + new expenditure + packet waste. It equals the original common and source energy for every comparison. The twelve-step clock starts after commissioning, so the calculation is not a shared physical-time deadline including preparation.

A specific ten-exposure joined witness starts from145 original energy units and finishes with fuel18 and two packets10. Its ledger is **145=4 old preparation+1 adapter+102 operational costs/waste+38 remaining**. It has probability 1/72 of all initial histories; other paths also contribute to the primary correction probability2/9.
