## Semantic and operational contract

Interpret a reason with its bearer, relevant deliberator or role scope, context/generation, purpose/ground, proposition kind and action meaning. These distinctions may be carried by an enclosing trusted setup rather than every individual serialized record. No theorem should require that all fields always be physically stored, or mistake matching labels for evidence of their calibration.

Export a command only from an actual completed current parent publication with the corresponding acquired/inferred support. Root-only, unknown, contradictory, partial or invalidated publications cannot supply a uniquely supported command. Do not replace the parent's correlated evidence with independently sampled Boolean edge labels.

An explicit supplied transport policy states which source scopes imply which destination scopes and how action meanings map. A same-role operational successor may be admitted despite a different process identity. Actor-specific reasons need an independently warranted actor transport before a broader addressed-reason claim; copying does not supply it. Patient, purpose or proposition changes cannot be inferred from equal masks. A semantic refinement can be implication rather than exact equality when the destination deliberately asserts less; state its scope.

The producer's G remains a supplied support objective. Instrumental compatibility means that the declared command produces the modeled receipt whenever the opportunity is present. It is not a proof that G is ethically justified, that receipt prevents harm, or that this action settles conflicting claims. Justified grounds, calibrated physical meanings and legitimate authority remain distinct premises.

Keep these outputs separate:

1. A currently supported instrumental proposition in its declared scope.
2. A scope-compatible command recommendation for the same recipient.
3. A technically permitted, funded and deliverable request under the supplied controller relation.
4. An actual event and recipient consequence, which this metadata adapter alone does not execute.

Loss of an enforcement channel must not invalidate the reason or imply that every voluntary act of help is impossible. Distinguish an enforced route from an independently available voluntary route. A permission flag only implements a supplied policy; it does not prove rightful authority.

## Required positive and negative cases

Positive coverage must make the contract nonvacuous: every authentic current uniquely supported command in an explicitly admitted scope should pass semantic dispatch. Always refusing is insufficient. Test both recipient orientations, ordinary same-role use, replacement of a process within the same role, and a separately supplied justified scope transformation. A competent conventional typed dispatcher on the same information must be compared exactly.

Test fixed-patient orientation changes, cross-patient transplant, wrong purpose/ground, changed proposition kind, unauthorized role transport, context change, same-label reopening with changed generation, incomplete/currentness-failed publication, and source substitution. Test valid scoped advice with unavailable funds, blocked delivery or unavailable enforcement; preserve the recommendation while distinguishing the unavailable route from a permitted voluntary one. Do not manufacture a violation of either upstream implementation by omitting its declared enclosing context.

Test the limitation of the adapter: consistent but wrongly calibrated source tags can pass syntactic binding. Hashes or signatures authenticate bytes at most; they do not prove the source/actuator mapping, normative transport or physical effects. Without an independently supplied receipt-to-harm relation, there is no protection conclusion. If two valid claims cannot both be funded, preserve both claims and the infeasibility rather than silently dropping a patient.
