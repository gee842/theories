## Publication counts, costs and a funding trap

The audit counts actual `publish` edges, including publications subsequently invalidated. It does not count terminal flags or assume occurrences of a schedule word. Strongly connected components contain no publication edge; the maximum number of actual publications in this domain is 2.

For each initial unread state, `24*N ≤ spent`; for a full factual handoff, `24*N ≤ spent+14`. Partial handoffs use their actual already-paid credit. The graph maximizes this expression over admitted paths, preserving all zero-cost loops. Payment bounds hold independently of whether archived facts are true.

These are the selected kernel's prices. Its guards force a paid reread before another reason cycle. The parent's separately declared five-unit lookup circuit and other lawful cache-reuse designs prevent reading this bound as a universal minimum cost of reasoning.

A genuine partial handoff has reader 3, reserve 28, token=current and next 2. It cannot pay the next read 4, and `fund` requires a complete reader. Exhausting all its continuations gives 21 states and 82 edges with **no current publication**. Aggregate money 31 therefore does not guarantee executable completion. This is distinct from a total-budget lower bound; changing the transfer guard would change the law.

Free context changes also give a zero-cost self-loop with no publication. Safety and sufficient payment do not establish liveness.

## Python/Lean boundary

The parent Python `reason_cycle` helper can be called again directly on a published state, paying 10 more without another factual decode. Lean reason activation requires phase 0 and rejects that one-step call from phase 9. The parent also transfers reserve 28 into a partial reader's fuel 3 in its scheduled control, whereas Lean `fund` requires completion and rejects the transfer. Both discrepancies are preserved as executable counterexamples to a **stronger all-interleavings equivalence claim**. The accepted scheduled positive composition remains intact.

This audit translates the guarded Lean kernel; it neither silently modifies the parent helpers nor treats arbitrary dictionary mutation as an allowed event. The independent checker reconstructs the relation separately. The Lean proofs concern arbitrary admitted traces under their premises; finite enumeration supplies concrete coverage and countermodels, not an unrestricted proof by itself.
