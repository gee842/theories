## Precise formal scope

The new relation represents the **deterministic-capture subprocess in which retention, work and writing can all be paid and the circuit persists**. Its explicit phases are ready, retention paid, actual receipt acquired, and memory written; completion then wears or renews the hardware. Updater replacement is represented and retains the existing circuit's score and last action. A reversal changes only the hidden plant condition.

The full executable program additionally handles unaffordable writes, loss of memory from unpaid retention, circuit destruction/rebuilding, inactive opportunity expiry, and stochastic outages. Those extra branches are not claimed to refine into this Lean relation. Their all-history accounting and probabilities are computational claims with a separate independent checker.

These results establish a particular operational record/update/action route, not calibration of every finite-counter prediction or a normative value of the forecast.

## Affordability and proof boundaries

The deterministic cost result checks sequential payment of the three-failed-write correction route, including its intervening updater formation and the corrected action's startup. Its pre-return price is `3*(rent+work+writing)+gate+rent+work`, equal to21 for the primary parameters. A future capture cannot finance this prefix. Separate finite counter equations show the Slow three-write versus Fast one-write update behavior; the mathematical note supplies the decision-lag, lifetime and source conditions needed to use those equations as an actual route.

The package preserves explicit paid event granularity. It does not use the controlled-refinement theorem to replace rent/work/write by a free atomic update. Old-prefix embedding plus paid functional retooling is a different claim from observational equivalence of unchanged controllers. There is no proved full Python refinement or Lean probability theorem here.

The two-slot sensor/register functionality, interpretation of receipts, algorithms, maintenance, actuator rule and staged external clock remain supplied local laws. Their natural formation, empirical identification, general calibration, cross-center evaluation and concern are not proved.
