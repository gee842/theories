# Paid route revision and actual succession in Lean

This package formalizes the [frozen protocol](../PROTOCOL.md) and its [prospective clarifications](../PROSPECTIVE-CLARIFICATIONS.md). It composes the existing physical monitor, ordinary successor learner and transmission process. It does not replace their event laws with a score-based retention rule.

## Checked interface

[RouteRevision.lean](RouteRevision.lean) wraps the actual `FormedRouteMonitor.State`. A first complete, bound monitor frame can release previously separate fuel5 and probe source20; copy source10 remains separately reserved. A new finite head costs1 to assemble, actual diagnostic access costs1, and an authorized replacement costs1. Assembly and reading precede the verdict test, including when the result is unresolved. All four proposed routes have the same guard. The paid swap preserves the actual packed record, original actor and carrier, consumes one replacement part, retains the old part in a retired inventory, and invalidates the old monitor evidence. A known binding break cancels authorization.

The head supplies a finite one-shot permission/phase and a counter bounded by three completed probes. Its functional wiring, proposal repertoire, finite schedule, protected provisions and material inputs are premises. The hidden plant law is read by the inherited work event, not by the head's diagnostic rule or proposal guard. This is conditional physical revision of a learning mechanism, not a derivation of the replacement chemistry or an optimal selector.

Ordinary probes are the imported `SuccessorLearning` funding and performance events, with their real intermediate cash, observations, acquired record, costs and packet guard. General trace theorems preserve energy, new head stock, replacement plus retired plus installed routing parts, child parts, old monitor material and the original actor. They preserve recipient floor1, bounded packed payload and the three-probe counter.

[RevisionSuccession.lean](RevisionSuccession.lean) uses the imported donor-funding bridge and the unchanged `FormedRecipientRoute` copier. Its input template and law are the actual learner successor. Its source is the separately retained copy packet. Any unused probe source stays in `outsideEnergy`, together with the original actor and unreleased provisions; this explicit partition is not a resource reset. The copied module retains partial records, consumed material, seeded cash and failed work. Imported trace accounts give the whole energy and child-material equations.

## Concrete witnesses and limits

The primary ordinary witness starts at the monitor's typed retained CLOSED boundary with reserve25 and wrong recipient bit after reversal. Actual monitor assembly and its first paid frame leave reserve11 and an `unchanged` diagnosis. Release and the three revision fees leave13. TARGET's unchanged probes give receipts011, finish at12, and fund the actual copy/use/hold process. The retained child has reserve11, the acquired TARGET payload, zero carrier cash and zero copy source. The local typed ledger is:

- actor8 + initial recipient25 + monitor source20 + protected grant5 + probe source20 + copy source10 = 88;
- final actor8 + recipient11 + dissipation49 + lost packets20 = 88.

This ledger starts at the typed retained boundary. It does not re-prove or erase earlier ancestry costs. The separately checked numerical mixture and monitor handoff export establish which saved histories realize those boundary fields; Lean does not prove the full Python distribution or its preparation probability.

The controlled PRIVATE_ZERO ancestry uses its own reserve15 stable-plant monitor boundary. Its successful first monitored frame identifies the proxy, leaves11 and a wrong record, and TARGET replacement leads to the corresponding paid child at11. It is not treated as an ordinary PRIVATE_ZERO ancestor at reserve25.

The same law supplies useful failures:

- PRIVATE_ZERO's three probes finish at2. It can fund and pay donor hold, then recipient own-use is blocked at1. The new child remains absent; its blank, routing part and copy packet remain unused.
- CLOSED/FROZEN block after actual second-probe work, at recipient1 with the third probe packet unused. Their partial learner phase cannot be reset to donor-funding readiness.
- A reversal immediately before probe3 can leave TARGET corrected but unable to copy, while PRIVATE_ZERO retains a wrong child at reserve1 after losing the child's packet. Retention therefore does not imply truth or productive continuation.
- A larger supplied buffer can retain the same proxy. Immediate copying under an explicitly different zero-probe schedule can retain a wrong record. Under the two-probe schedule, both writer routes pay for a copied template but cannot afford its seed.
- With no copy packet, the target can still pay through the child's target-sensing phase. Work is blocked, preserving the seeded cash and actual copied child; the missing source is not modeled as a free failed packet.
- An unresolved first monitor frame still pays head assembly and diagnostic access, then cannot swap. Successful swap cannot reuse the spent authorization.

The zero/two-probe schedules are explicit alternatives, not branches inferred by the three-probe head. The guard relation does not prove a stochastic proposal distribution, liveness, unlimited adaptation, repair under arbitrary channel faults, ethical standing or universal favorable selection. The physical head/route/copy recipes, calibrated monitor contacts, recipient funding role, supplied plant, source allocation and finite control remain local premises.

## Verification

Run `python3 check.py` from this directory or use its repository path. [verification.json](verification.json) records the result, source and output hashes, explicit toolchain, compiler binary hash and checker hash. The checker snapshots every imported source into a fresh temporary directory, builds them in dependency order with `lean +leanprover/lean4:v4.29.1`, and verifies that protocol, clarification and all source bytes stayed unchanged during the build. It imports the monitor's source without editing that concurrently owned package.

Every theorem in both new modules and every imported dependency is explicitly audited with `#print axioms`. The permitted trust base is Lean's kernel plus the standard `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice` axioms when reported. The checker rejects proof holes, custom axiom declarations, unsafe declarations, native decision shortcuts, warnings, missing audits and stale source bytes. Concrete finite event witnesses use kernel-checked `decide`; general accounting and trace statements use ordinary proofs.

This package supplies a typed conditional composition and its counterexamples. The numerical all-ancestry replay, independent implementation comparison and saved-distribution handoff have separate validation scopes.
