# Formation and revision of the prediction-to-action coupling

6 October 2026. AI-authored protocol saved before executing this extension's
results. This is a finite conditional construction within the generative
programme. The named connection is an acquired prediction becoming causally
usable through a formed, costly and replaceable action coupling.

## Producer and new functional recipe

Import the actual prediction module's unchanged preparation, including every
unsuccessful original formation history, predecessor energy and paid adapter.
Record hashes of all imported source modules before and after each run because
parallel work may change them. This extension does not edit its predecessors.

The prediction circuit and updater are formed exactly as in the parent study's
fixed-Fast arm: two units for a D updater, one for the old selector, three for
the predictive retrofit. The existing updater slot then uses Fast writes and
its actual lifetime, upkeep and replacement costs. Holding this type fixed
isolates the new action coupling; this does not derive the update algorithm.

A new one-unit **coupler retrofit** arranges a binary normal/inverted output
switch inside the declared composite circuit. Both orientations begin absent;
formation draws them fairly. Its replacement costs the same unit and retains
q, last action, prior acquired-record status and the continuing circuit. No
additional material unit or third backing slot is claimed: the additional
switch capacity and retooling chemistry inside the old composite circuit are
explicit new local premises. Energy payment does not derive this capacity.
Rebuilding the entire circuit resets the switch and its pending witness.

Before receiving its outcome, the circuit commands the parent's threshold
action XOR the actual switch orientation. The plant alone reads hidden z.
The register uses the actual issued action and receipt to decode the parent's
teaching bit. Fast writes set q to 0 or 4; no hidden target or performance score
chooses the switch. Forecasts are computed for the action actually issued.

## Lifecycle and accounting

At each exposure: replace an expired Fast updater if affordable; build and
retrofit an absent circuit if affordable; form an absent or expired coupler
if affordable; pay record retention; attempt work if every component is active
and its trial cost is affordable; receive or miss the actual packet; pay
renewals; pay an acquired-record write if affordable. Payments are not refunded
when later stages fail. Source packets are lost at inactive exposures as well.

Capture renews the circuit, updater and coupler at one unit each. Failure wears
each by one. Primary lifetimes are six, two and two exposures respectively.
A missing record-retention payment destroys accessible circuit memory and its
coupler. Merely lacking a trial payment does not additionally destroy memory.
The post-assembly exposure clock and predecessor preparation time retain the
parent study's distinction.

For every operational successor check

    old fuel + packet = new fuel + paid expense + inaccessible packet loss.

Join this to all preparation costs, adapter cost, failed-preparation retained
energy and any source remainder. The two backing material units remain in the
same inherited slots throughout operation; this finite model assumes their
retooling does not require additional consumable material or recovery energy
beyond the declared prices.

## Controls, cases and witness

Use five arms with equal initial resources, observation access and basic prices:

- Variable paid switch with capture-gated renewal.
- Conventional finite-state redescription of that exact mechanism.
- Fixed normal switch, still paying formation, upkeep and replacement.
- Fixed inverted switch with the same bills.
- Outcome-blind switch renewal: after every executed trial it pays the same
  switch-renewal price if affordable and resets its lifetime irrespective of
  receipt. Otherwise the switch wears. The circuit/updater keep their normal
  capture coupling. The extra realized renewal expense is part of this
  intervention, not held equal by an artificial refund.

Primary: initial common reserve 30; eight packets of ten; reversal after four
exposures; noiseless capture. Also retain no reversal; packet availability
3/4; initial common reserve 12; packet yield six; circuit lifetime two; switch
lifetime one. These are declared finite fixtures, not calibrated biology.

The strict coupling witness requires an old switch to command a failed trial
from an already acquired confident q, with that paid write leaving q unchanged;
the same continuing circuit then pays to form the opposite switch while q
retains that value; the replacement issues the different command, counterfactually
holding q and last fixed, and captures a packet that pays its own renewal.
The old switch must therefore have been used; a newly renamed idle component
cannot qualify. Circuit rebuilding clears an unfinished witness. Completed
witnesses remain historical facts. Diagnostic flags never govern behavior.

Report unconditional preparation success, captures, trials, writes, decoder
errors, switch formations/changes/renewals, paid expense, inaccessible source,
retained fuel, active written memory, strict witness probability and forecast
error together with actual trial coverage. Do not equate the witness frequency
with optimality or concern. Fixed normal can succeed without ever revising its
switch and therefore need not pass this witness.
