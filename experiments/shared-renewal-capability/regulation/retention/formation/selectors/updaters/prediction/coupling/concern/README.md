## One actual inherited history

The process starts with thirty common fuel units and 111 source units. Eleven ten-unit opportunities and the original preparation source are specified at the beginning. It runs the [parent coupling model](../README.md)'s actual preparation and first eight exposures, including a reversal after four. Preparation succeeds with probability 2/9; failed histories remain in every unconditional result. Three originally specified packets remain for this continuation. No reserve, prediction or component lifetime is reset.

An eligible circuit must be active, written and confident, with a live updater and coupler. A two-unit retrofit gives it a common target input. One later opportunity can deliver ten units only to the recipient; two later opportunities can deliver ten each only to the actor. Both later actor opportunities also require this new interface. The old private circuit cannot address their new site. This is an explicit environmental and wiring condition, not a reward from the recipient.

At the recipient phase, retention costs one, observing the deficit costs one and work costs three before any receipt. The inherited prediction and coupling issue the command. A successful command deposits ten into a separate recipient reserve. One subsequent recipient operation consumes one unit, leaving nine. The actor gets zero units from that delivery. The actor's two subsequent trials each cost retention1 + work3 before their own receipts. This finite suffix uses a supplied read-only retention law; parent writing, wear and replacement events are suspended for these three exposures.

The saved reachable state has reserve11, q4, normal coupling, component lifetimes6/2/2 and a **previous actual coupling-revision witness**. Its unconditional state mass is 1/36; this is a merged terminal-state probability, not the probability of one specified event word. A symmetric state contributes another 1/36.

## Counterregions and selection boundary

There are five cases and eleven intervention programmes for four upstream producers, giving **220 exact result rows**. Programmes include no construction, identity gating, concealment, temporary or permanent disabling, budget locking and switch reversal with or without restoration. Failed payments consume no future receipt and never refund earlier bills.

- A one-unit identity gate leaves the saved actor with nineteen units and the recipient with zero. A free temporary block leaves twenty and zero. Both outperform shared operation on actor reserve while preventing recipient use.
- A wrong transfer map or absent recipient source leaves the recipient unsupported even though the actor can obtain the same later private receipts. Private success does not certify recipient success.
- Without the two future private opportunities, declining the retrofit preserves more actor fuel. Past private learning cannot make optional recipient-only construction privately advantageous.
- Seven inherited units suffice for the recipient bill; eleven are required to fund that bill and the first subsequent actor trial. At ten, recipient support can succeed while the actor cannot use its next opportunity. These reserve interventions condition on the displayed capability; they are not all asserted to be reachable predecessor states.

A finite maintenance tail spends one unit per hold of the installed target circuit until fuel is exhausted. It gives actual paid holding duration, without new offspring, components or support. `no_build` preserves the old private circuit and reserve; its zero target-interface holding duration means the new interface was never constructed, not that all older machinery died.

The comparison does not choose a programme by inspecting final reserve. Upstream motor-coupling replacement is consequence-mediated; downstream target programmes are interventions. A theorem about the private optimum over a stated menu likewise requires a specified selection criterion. Neither establishes natural population prevalence or an autonomous origin of ethical concern.
