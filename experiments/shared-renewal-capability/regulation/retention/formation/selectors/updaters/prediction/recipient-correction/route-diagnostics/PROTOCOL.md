# Evidence interface for revising a learning route

6 October 2026. AI-authored bounded formal and saved-evidence study, complementary to the successor-learning owner's route-revision implementation. **This is a conditional diagnostic interface, not a newly constructed meta-selector or sensor.** Its direction is formed learner → evidence about exercised update behavior → accountable revision of that behavior under the [generative foundation](../../../../../../../../../../theories/becoming/history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md).

The accepted [successor-learning handoff](../successor-learning/HANDOFF.md) changes the acquired response `q` while its route `gamma` stays fixed. This study identifies what later evidence can and cannot establish about that route. It does not alter the parent, successor, renewal, recipient-recording or reason/protection laws. Costs and route semantics are already known when designing this synthetic analysis; no preregistration or empirical novelty is claimed.

## Supplied semantics and two observation contracts

Use the actual packed-record and formed-route functions, with recipient action equal to the current recipient bit. The four configurations are TARGET, PRIVATE_ZERO, CLOSED and FROZEN. Readable configuration `gamma` identifies a nominal supplied recipe; that is different from inferring its behavior through performance. No result will assert that two physically different `gamma` values are identical complete states.

The strongest proposed diagnostic interface contains the issued action, a genuinely acquired actual recipient capture report, the pre-write and post-write recipient bits, and confirmation that the contemplated write completed. Costs, context and access conditions must be retained by any physical consumer. The routed decoded label is not automatically an independent report of actual capture; `receipt_source`, decoder-error and successful-witness fields in saved traces are analyst outputs, not newly available sensors.

For one unchanged route, bounded code `q < 4`, correctly addressed recipient action equal to its current bit, and a completed recipient write with the external outcome channel available before route-specific routing, let `flip` mean that the post-write recipient bit differs from the issued action. The expected signatures to verify from the actual functions are TARGET: flip exactly after nonreceipt; PRIVATE_ZERO: flip after either outcome; CLOSED/FROZEN: no flip. A success/failure pair of such observations identifies three behavior classes. It does not distinguish CLOSED from FROZEN from bits alone.

The weaker proposed interface records only the independently acquired recipient outcome word from repeated ordinary actions. Under a stable available binary plant and an initially wrong recipient bit, the first three expected receipts are TARGET `011`, PRIVATE_ZERO `010`, CLOSED/FROZEN `000`. TARGET and PRIVATE_ZERO agree for the first two receipts; a paid post-write bit read can distinguish them earlier. These are alternative observation contracts, not claims that the existing runtime supplies the additional report or read apparatus.

## Boundaries to retain

- With a correct initial bit in a stable available plant, TARGET, CLOSED and FROZEN keep producing the correct command and same returns. Passive success need not exercise their different responses to failure.
- CLOSED and FROZEN have identical recipient record/action behavior for arbitrary sequences of externally supplied receipt values. Their input-presence behavior differs. A paid observation of that difference, or a read of `gamma`, changes the observation contract.
- An unpaid write leaves a bit unchanged without identifying a nonwriting recipe. Channel outage can suppress a TARGET update. Unavailable plant supply can give nonreceipt even when the issued action matches the productive law. Update-behavior classification does not infer the hidden law or the ecological cause of a failure.
- The initially wrong-record condition and stable available plant are hypotheses of the proposed distinguishing sequence. A deployment needs an actual acquired change or a calibrated, paid challenge to establish them. Neither the desired bit nor a notified environmental-change flag may be inserted into the ordinary learner.
- Diagnostic affordability, construction and permissions remain separate. Repeated-function evaluation is not a paid physical experiment. A downstream implementation must supply acquisition, reads, write confirmation, probe resources and any challenge apparatus, preserve partial failures, and account for route replacement parts.

## Evidence and acceptance

The executable audit will enumerate bounded recipient codes, route alternatives, capture outcomes, channel presence and paid/unpaid writes using frozen actual functions. It will also inspect the accepted successor distributions for matched stable TARGET/CLOSED/FROZEN descendants, preserving actual ancestry, original-actor reserves, recipient reserves, occupied components and mass. Parent preparation and learning experiments are not rerun. Compact references and hashes suffice; duplicate large result archives are unnecessary.

The separate Lean module will import the actual formed-route semantics and establish the signatures, behavioral aliases and finite distinguishing sequences. Its proof does not certify a new physical diagnostic device or all Python stochastic histories. Existing controller-history and outage impossibility results supply antecedents; this study should add the route-specific observation contract rather than rename those general results.

An independent internal review will check source correspondence, the availability/write hypotheses, distinction between actual reports and audit fields, and the limits of inferring route quality. Acceptance closes only this evidence interface. Actual paid route revision remains the successor branch's separate construction; recipe origins, generality and justified concern remain further foundation obligations.
