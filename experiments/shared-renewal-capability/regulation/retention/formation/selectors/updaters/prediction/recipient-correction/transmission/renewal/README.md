## What is carried forward

Each generation receives the actual previous child's acquired record, route, cash, action capability and retention status. The original actor's account remains separate, every old donor's residual cash remains accounted, and each new occupied carrier has a distinct parent identity. The copier's result supplies the next record; an observer's knowledge of the new target does not initialize it.

The complete finite horizon declares its packets and receiver bundles at entry. Every allocation moves stock from this bank into the current block. An insufficient whole-block packet allocation stops that generation before it starts; this is an added, supplied scheduling rule. An insufficient bundle is passed into the ordinary copying law, retaining its paid partial failure. Later bank stock remains unused after a failed lineage.

## Exact conditional result

For the primary faithful, calibrated, noiseless route with two warm-ups, each complete generation pays for four probes and generic retransmission. It acquires a response after a target reversal, uses the changed response productively, and copies that actual state into a productive retained successor. The recipient reserve obeys `B_next = B + 1`.

The general n-warm-up formula `B_next = B + 3n - 5` requires an admitted TARGET learner with zero learner cash and an inherited recipient response that is correct at entry, together with the declared parts, faithful evidence and stable target during descendant use. Its exact ordered-payment guard is `(n = 0 or B >= 6) and B + 3n >= 15`. When n is positive, the first warm-up must be funded before its return is available. A retained child with an incorrect inherited response is outside this recurrence, even if it remains admitted by the simulator; nonzero inherited cash also requires the full account calculation.

## Failures change the interpretation

- Zero warm-ups leave the primary recipient at 1 without a retained descendant. One warm-up allows actual correction but leaves 4 and cannot finish the generic transmission. Resource order matters before a favorable final balance can be used.
- Frozen-record controls match under stable conditions. The actual `same_law` parent mixture also includes genuinely formed and retained frozen/disconnected routes; their formation histories are preserved.
- Copying an old record can retain one wrong child, then fail in the next generation. Retention and productive learned use remain separate measures.
- A second target reversal before the descendant acts changes the comparison: copying the old record can outperform copying the newly learned one. The environmental schedule is a substantive premise.
- With probe availability 3/4, the faithful G2 full strict mass falls to 81/16384 for variable/conventional and 81/8192 for fixed-normal. Nonreceipt can misidentify the productive law.
- Ten packets support two primary blocks and then stop at the whole-block allocation guard. Two receiver bundles support two copies and leave the later paid learning/attempted copying visible.

The learner's routing algorithm is inherited unchanged. The result exercises and transmits learning; it does not establish revision of the learning algorithm, rightful control of recipient resources, justified concern, protection against strategic exploitation, empirical calibration, or uniqueness over schedule-aware alternatives. Those remain separate derivation and identification obligations.
