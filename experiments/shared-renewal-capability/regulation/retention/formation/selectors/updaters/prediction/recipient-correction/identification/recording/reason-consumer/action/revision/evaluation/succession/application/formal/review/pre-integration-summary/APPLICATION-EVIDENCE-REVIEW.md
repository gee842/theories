# Independent review: application evidence and the one-service boundary

AI-authored independent internal review, 6 October 2026. **Accepted at the bounded statement below.** Source inspection and a separate fresh Lean 4.29.1 build pass. There is no unresolved material proof or scope finding. No numerical experiment, scientific outcome or inherited transition was rerun or changed.

The [prospective contract][contract] is satisfied by [ApplicationEvidence.lean][source]. This is a theorem about an existing paid history and its interpretation. It does not implement a service-completion register, a changed evaluator or a withdrawal policy.

## 1. What is established

For a fixed recipient-production law, take an actual producer trace starting ready with an empty archive, and an actual initialized paid reader/reason trace using exactly that resulting archive. If its endpoint is current and supports a non-root command, the proof establishes this chain:

| Step | Actual evidence or invariant | Result and scope |
|---|---|---|
| Publication → accessible frame | Exact phase/closure and selected-input invariants, completed cache agreement, receipt-only decoder | A current, present, positive frame exists at an accessible index. Semantic `Assured` alone is insufficient. |
| Accessible frame → historical acquisition | Consumer archive preservation and empty-bank emission provenance | The **same index and frame** occurs at an actual recipient acquisition, with before/after states and prefix/suffix retained. No arbitrary payload injection or other bank is substituted. |
| Acquisition → prior delivery | Actual producer-step induction and ready initialization | A work event crediting the recipient ten physical units occurs in **that acquisition's own prefix**. A later unrelated delivery cannot establish this conclusion. |
| Delivery → this service met | Separately supplied `EpisodeBound` and `ServiceMeaning` | The credited event fulfills the independently specified service only under those interpretations. |
| Service met → not soundly pending | `Pending → ¬Met` | The proposed pending-one-service entrance is incompatible with the above conditions. This is not a selected policy or a universal abstention theorem. |

The first three steps need no ethical root `G`. The final two are explicit application premises, rather than consequences of physical credit alone. The generic theorem is conditional on actual histories and endpoint support; the concrete inherited instance supplies those histories. Neither the existence of this proof nor an abstract semantically sound mask alone proves that every reason system has this receipt-based origin.

## 2. Hypotheses and proof audit

The relevant result is `entrance_has_receipt_with_prior_delivery`, not merely `positive_emission_is_recipient_acquisition`. The latter identifies an acquired positive worked phase; the former also establishes its history.

`ReceiptOnlyEntrance` contains the fixed law, backing/address limits, initially ready world, actual empty-bank producer trace, initialized paid consumer over the resulting records, and current supported endpoint. It does **not** assume positive evidence, correct instrumental command, fulfillment, an ethical root, or warranted selected action at the initial boundary. The proof derives the required factual soundness from the imported ready-state and archive-preservation laws.

The proof retains exact index/frame provenance throughout `stored_from_empty_has_located_origin`, `positive_origin_shape` and `AcquiredAfterDelivery`. A decoded Boolean `false` must not be confused with a failed delivery: in this decoder either decoded orientation requires a present **positive raw receipt**, with the Boolean identifying the command relation. The conclusion is existential; it does not claim a unique record or work event.

The production law is fixed over the admitted trace. This theorem does not add robustness to a changed law between observation and use, a new sensor calibration, or arbitrary recipient/episode identification. Its trace relation includes all imported physical and payment guards. The independently justified service interpretation is stronger than merely knowing that the final command is efficacious.

## 3. Findings preserved and addressed

### Positive phase versus actual historical work

The early shape argument allowed one to inspect `worked recipient true`; that alone cannot show a work event occurred in the inspected history. An initial state could already carry that phase. The final proof explicitly preserves this alternative in `worked_trace_has_delivery_or_initial_receipt`, then rules it out using the ready initial state.

`AcquiredAfterDelivery` places `DeliveredOnTrace` in the identified acquisition's own prefix. The final theorem therefore proves historical ordering instead of assuming that an acquired receipt implies an earlier event by definition. The ready hypothesis is a sufficient reviewed restriction; this review does not claim it is the weakest possible one.

### Context and episode binding

A Boolean context match is not proof of episode, recipient or service identity. The imported recorder's `contextChange` clears its query and sets `live=false`; ordinary core events do not restore it, and positive emission requires a live recorder. Thus that event cannot silently relabel an earlier work event and then emit a positive frame.

More importantly, the application theorem does not use that engineering safeguard as ethical identification. `EpisodeBound` quantifies the actual work position, before-state, successor and surrounding trace. `ServiceMeaning` supplies the meaning of its credited effect. The interpretation must be justified independently for the declared window. The theorem does not infer it from a context bit, an arbitrary status flag, or an agent's confidence. The generic binding is deliberately strong: it binds the declared trace's work events to the specified episode.

Generic reader currentness is relative to its supplied initialized context. `ReceiptOnlyEntrance` does not additionally equate that parameter with the terminal producer world's context or supply an independent current-time sensor. This does not invalidate the historical delivery result; it limits what “current” alone certifies. The actual witness uses its actual fresh-bank context, while episode/service applicability remains the separate interpretation above.

### Cost wording

The initial README wording conflated an arbitrary-trace consumer ledger with the concrete 24-unit cycle. The author corrected the prose before acceptance: the generic conclusion is terminal consumer energy plus actual charges equals its initial fuel plus reserve; the concrete cycle spends 24. No proof or price changed.

Producer work, acquisition and archive writing remain charged under the imported `RecipientIdentification` and `EmissionArchive` laws. The new file introduces neither a combined replacement energy law nor a new tariff. It also creates no refunded fuel, source, material, stored frame or operating capacity.

### Independent review harness

The first independent build compiled the first two modules successfully but falsely rejected `FormedSelection` because its unqualified `#print axioms` requests were compared with Lean's qualified output names. [That first source, receipt, logs and finding are preserved][first]. Only the review harness was repaired: it now compares unique short names and additionally requires exact matches for every explicitly qualified request. The subsequent build started from a new temporary directory. This was not a proof repair, an experimental rerun, or an author-receipt replacement.

## 4. Concrete inherited instance

`actualEntrance` uses `SuccessionWitness.second_probe_trace`, the actual fresh bank and `actual_consumer_trace`. It stops at `consumed`, before the already accepted later selected action. Its initially empty bank is the new sparse bank, not the donor's historical archive. The first unrecorded probe is prior accepted history; it is not silently turned into a stored frame.

The second query credits the recipient from **60 to 70**. Its retained frame is `⟨true,true,true,true⟩` at query index 1. The paid reader/reason cycle consumes **24** and leaves the recipient at 70, actor fuel **10** and one source packet. These are actual inherited constants and traces, not a new configured example or numerical population result.

The further selected use is still physically feasible. The theorem does not alter that action, the learned coefficient or the recipient account. It establishes why, *if* the identified query already completes one independently specified service, a proposed comparison demanding both this informative entrance and that same service still pending is unavailable. Repeated assistance, recurring protection, another episode and independent grounds are outside that restricted inference.
