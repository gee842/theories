## A genuinely unaudited alternative

It keeps the same constructed apparatus, full archive, actual actor/recipient state and all original reserves. It skips both reason cycles and the intervening cold reread; their unspent charges remain in the reader/protected accounts. No current reason is fabricated for this alternative.

This is a real alternative to purchasing instrumental reasoning, not a claim that the recorder/learner itself originated without an information source. If a later paid reading/reason cycle is requested, it is the first such cycle in this arm and is labeled accordingly; the old records remain its only archive evidence.

The earlier route can tie or outperform the reason path in this fixed-law task. The interface's claim is valid composition, not a manufactured algorithmic advantage.

## Scope and currentness are actual interface conditions

The source and destination lineage/recipient fields in saved experiment rows describe the intended physical coupling; the destination is fixed independently of an incoming token. The port model's trusted binding and invalidation interlock are explicit supplied functional premises. It does not construct cryptographic authentication, an unbounded identity database or a free sensor proving that a claimed identity is true.

Currentness is maintained by causal invalidation events: reopening evidence, changing context, replacing publication or changing destination invalidates the current command latch. A Boolean context label alone cannot restore it. Scope faults can break that supplied premise; a consistently miscalibrated but faithfully routed source remains an accepted fault case and may deliver a wrong command. The pure semantic contract and concrete physical preservation checks keep these assumptions separate from their consequences.
