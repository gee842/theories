# First committed observation: an actual input to route replacement

6 October 2026. This package exports the existing monitor's first completed frame, keeping all incomplete histories. It adds no dynamics, parts or funding. The accepted [monitor](../README.md) already constructs sensing and retained evidence under its declared cell/head/contact law; the separate [revision protocol](../../revision/PROTOCOL.md) specifies the subsequent construction.

## The boundary

[boundary.json](boundary.json) contains 66 distinct boundaries across nine full mixtures: reversed ordinary ancestry, stable ordinary controls and the separate stable prior-clamp ancestry, each under three producers. Forty boundaries complete a frame and 26 retain failed prefixes. Every exported trace is checked as an exact prefix of the accepted two-frame trace. All 128 distinct incoming source rows and their exact probabilities remain referenced.

| Completed first frame | Recipient reserve | Stored summaries (success, failure) | What the evidence identifies |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Reversed CLOSED/FROZEN | 11 | (unseen, unchanged) | Common nonwriting class |
| Reversed TARGET | 11 | (unseen, flipped) | Still unresolved after one failure |
| Stable controlled PRIVATE_ZERO | 11 | (flipped, unseen) | Private-zero class |
| Stable ordinary TARGET/CLOSED/FROZEN | 21 | (unchanged, unseen) | Still unresolved |

Every completed boundary has one unused ten-unit monitor packet, zero carrier cash, eight occupied monitor cells and one occupied head. The altered prior-clamp ancestry is not a reserve reset into the ordinary cohort. Incomplete histories preserve their own stocks and absence of valid commitment.

The earlier two-frame final endpoints are not these first-frame inputs. Waiting for the second frame can reduce a diagnosed nonwriter from 11 to the one-unit floor and consume the remaining packet. Choosing the earlier boundary is an explicit change of continuation schedule; it is not a refund of an already executed second frame.

## Import without inventing access

`entries` preserves the exact incoming plant/history references. `boundaries[id].analyst_state` is the full physical boundary, including the unchanged route and current record. The inherited productive law belongs to the plant and is obtained as `entry.base_law XOR cases[boundary.case].reverse`; it is not inferred from the record or given to the revision controller.

`public_interface.stored_cells` describes the eight backed cells at valid commitment. `head_contacts` and `acquired_target_report` describe the declared finite contact/report state. The receiving controller must pay for access under its own event law. The export is not a free read or a message-delivery event. Nominal route, full packed record, hidden plant law, analyst diagnosis, metrics and lineage are excluded from this evidence interface. An incomplete frame supplies no completed cell port.

The exact commit state uses `stage='commit'`. The checked one-frame stop only changes that marker to `episode_complete`, recorded in `prefix.one_frame_stop_marker`. An importer expecting the latter may perform that verified marker normalization; it must not add a new event, change money or silently complete a failed frame. Other old stock remains in the immutable ancestor entry; its audit copy grants neither duplicate material nor access to another account.

## Parts and funding still have to be supplied

The [resource derivation](../../../../../../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ROUTE-REVISION-RESOURCE-MATH.md) checks the declared next construction from the informative reserve-11 boundary. It requires a revision head, a replacement routing part, a blank successor carrier and its separate routing part. The protocol adds five accessible funding units, twenty units of probe source and a separate ten-unit copying packet at the original extended boundary. Their release is a transfer, not creation of resources. No such provision is added by this export.

For the specified stable three-probe/copy sequence, five is the sharp minimum *additional accessible funding* for TARGET; the new thirty units of packet energy and all required parts are separate conditions. A four-unit grant fails before the second return despite a sufficient hypothetical final net balance. Larger reserves can retain faulty routes too. These cash-flow statements do not derive component recipes, demonstrate optimal revision or claim its full implementation has passed review.

## Checks and scope

[export.py](export.py) freezes the accepted monitor sources and receipts, executes only its existing one-frame prefix, and verifies exact equality to accepted traces, full probability mass and inherited energy/material accounts. The [independent reconstruction](independent_check.py) and [receipt](independent-verification.json) separately check the exported boundary and ordered resource words; the [review](../../../../../../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ROUTE-REVISION-RESOURCE-REVIEW.md) records their final status and scope.

```sh
python3 export.py
python3 independent_check.py
```

Run from this directory. These commands overwrite their own receipts. They do not construct the replacement or rerun earlier formation experiments. The replacement mechanism must still pay for evidence access, form its head, replace the actual route while retaining its record, invalidate old evidence, and exercise/copy the result under actual consequences. Its separate implementation and review determine whether those operations compose.
