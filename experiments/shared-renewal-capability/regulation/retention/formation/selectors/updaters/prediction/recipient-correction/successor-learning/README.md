## Result and limits

The primary route carries recipient stock 10 → 13 → 16 → 9 → 12 during two successful warmups, a failed corrective probe and corrected productive use. A paid donor-read transfer and unchanged copy law produce a corrected grandchild, ending at recipient 11 and original actor 8. The strict full-chain probability is 1/36 including original failures and route formation.

For n actually successful warmups from B, B + 3n ≥ 13 funds correction and use; B + 3n ≥ 15 funds the later copy prefix through its operational seed. Positive n also requires an initially affordable warmup. One warmup at B = 10 can commit a copied record while failing its seed: copied does not imply operational.

The conventional table is exactly equivalent. Correct TARGET/FROZEN carriers from actual same-law ancestry tie when conditions stay stable. An obsolete copied response can remain retained using surplus funds; another target reversal can favor that old response. Missing evidence, outages, absent parts and failed preparations remain counted. Actual `gamma` is fixed; the acquired record `q` changes.
