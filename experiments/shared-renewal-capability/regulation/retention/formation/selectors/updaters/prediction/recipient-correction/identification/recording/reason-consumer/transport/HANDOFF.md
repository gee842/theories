- A current semantic recommendation is not an executed or protective event. `local_prerequisites_met` lists necessary exposure conditions, not construction of the missing binding hardware. The actual endpoint has actor4, recipient20, reader0 and source0. The existing exposure requires actor8 before work and a ten-unit source packet. Recipient funds cannot silently rescue this join.
- An actual compatible command supports receipt when the modeled opportunity is present. No receipt-to-welfare or receipt-to-harm-prevention relation has been derived. Ethical justification of `G`, legitimate authority and plural conflicts remain separate obligations.

Proposed independent comparison with the retained controller's acquired `q` and qualification flag is being investigated here as a **calibration interface**, not an implemented paid action route. Agreement between jointly corrupted channels is not evidence of correct grounding.
