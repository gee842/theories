## Event and callable interfaces

- `initialize` reconstructs an exact donor with isolated new provisions; `admission` checks its structural quietness without demanding n7, correctness or a positive publication.
- `assemble`, `copy_candidate`, and `bind_access` execute the declared ordered construction, paid copy, retirement and binding. Writer5 moves into `bank.budget` at `accounts.release_child`, alongside the once-only actor/source transfer. Reader24 remains protected until `bank.lock_and_release_reader`.
- `trial(..., kind='probe0'|'probe1'|'selected')` emits each RI payment and outcome separately: rent, recipient sense, reserve, work, acquire and write, or blocked/miss states. `probe_cursor` consumes each scheduled probe opportunity even if blocked or missed; `bank.next` advances only on actual sensing. A repeated named probe is disabled.
- `bind_frame`, `latch_frame`, and `write_frame` preserve the fixed second-query frame and fully reserved writer escrow. Only actual acquisition supplies frame contents.
- `read_reasons` locks the new bank after both attempts, transfers reader24 once, and emits factual14 and reason10 from that single account. It cannot repair an interrupted RI core.
- `evaluate` pays read/write, checks current bank/owner/command/n access, and applies the shared, gated or literal-table comparison. Unknown support consumes an enabled read without creating n0. `intervene` only applies declared cuts; it offers no restoration to true.
- `run_case` is the frozen schedule; `metrics` is observer reporting. Only physical `trial.work` receives efficacy/availability. Policy choice never receives the observer's law.

The selected port is one use. Its consumed assessment can authorize only an already started RI sequence; completed write/miss or a denied start leaves no reusable permission. An unpaid evaluation write remains a pending paid read. A cut after rent retains the actual rented phase. A retired donor, exhausted probe opportunity, used copy latch or completed account transfer cannot be restarted.

`continuation.complete` and `status == 'finished'` mean that the fixed scheduler has exhausted its listed opportunities. They do not assert physical completion, cancel a pending transaction, or establish a ready continuation boundary. Inspect the actual RI phase, port mode, escrow, `pending_read` and `core_ready`; a paid read or rented trial can remain pending at that marker. A general completed-protocol result cannot be applied solely from this schedule label.

Probe receipts and final assessment-selected receipts are reported separately. The same facts and retained n reach the shared/gated/table comparison before their routing differs. A probe may already fulfill a warranted claim; another physical increment does not by itself establish another obligation. The paid query1 frame genuinely retains and supplies access to its receipt. Under independently justified recipient, episode and service meanings, it can support historical fulfillment evidence.
