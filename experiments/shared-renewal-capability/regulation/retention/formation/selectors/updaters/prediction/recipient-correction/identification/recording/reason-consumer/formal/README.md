## Exact positive interface

A cached `Some b` at address `i` states `b=(Z==designated i)`, with designated
vector `(0,1,0)`. Paid normalization maps true to the designated orientation,
false to its complement and unknown to unknown. Every sound known entry
therefore identifies the same `Z`. Conflicting entries produce a separate
conflict flag and no positive derived edge; consistency alone does not
establish authenticity or calibration.

The derived cache is `[Some(!Z), Some Z, None]` when an orientation is known.
The graph is `root→p`, `root→q`, `root→p`; the third edge is unused. The meaning
of `p` or `q` is `G` and compatibility of action0 or action1 with the actual
receipt law. Compatibility means `receipt(action,Z,availability)=availability`
for every availability bit. The code proves relevance from this law instead
of supplying an arbitrary edge truth vector. It cannot prove the objective
`G`, an ethical root, available future source, or successful delivery.

The arbitrary finite-list theorem concerns logical normalization only. The
physical consumer remains three-entry hardware with declared finite capacity
and tariff. Its star produces one direct route; observational redundancy is
not a second reason path or a new multi-hop result.

The bridge uses the unchanged factual reader's actual twenty-unit trace.
A new formed reason pair and reserve28 have been carried alongside the old
producer since preparation. At the factual boundary, its remaining reader6
receives the reserve, then pays reason10, leaving24. Cold turnover clears
operating cache and reason state, retaining the full actual archive, pending
state, hardware and accounts. It pays factual14 plus reason10 again, leaving0.
The consumer has no replacement evidence oracle and does not invoke the old
raw-Bool `Assayed` or `decodeAt` interface.

## What the semantic theorems require

`actual_archive_to_paid_reasons` starts from an empty archive and a genuine
recipient-event trace. It derives factual `Facts` and `CacheAgrees` through
the accepted archive/reader proofs, then interprets the completed prescribed
reason word. `actual_archive_to_cold_replayed_reasons` carries that provenance
through the enabled continuation and the second factual/reason cycle.
`funded_reason_trace`, `funded_two_cycle_tail` and `funded_cycle_trace` prove
that the stated words can execute at their actual budgets.

These are semantic guarantees for the **prescribed completed all-access
reason words**. The general event relation also permits access cuts and
reader/reset interleavings; the configured finite controls are separately checked by the
executable. No theorem here certifies every arbitrarily fabricated state
whose publication flag is true. `CurrentPublication` requires current phase,
factual completeness and matching publication, but authenticity still comes
from the proved history. Pure `run` endpoint equations, including
`reason_run_complete`, are calculations; enabled trace theorems separately
justify execution rather than treating saturated subtraction as payment.

Every reader reopening invalidates active/current reasoning, including a
same-tag context reopen. The supplied finite interlock avoids a free
unbounded version counter. Cache/record integrity, context sensing, fixed
recipient law, binary exclusivity, truthful acquisition, stable meaning,
functional recipe, physical retention and objective interpretation remain
explicit premises. A uniformly corrupted archive may support a consistently
wrong route; no universal corruption detector is constructed.
