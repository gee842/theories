# Local formation of cross-target response architecture

6 October 2026. AI-authored protocol saved before numerical execution. The
named connection is **actual formed predictor → local graph construction →
target-conditioned use → paid retention and changed reusable guides → another
actual graph construction**. This is not selection among sampled finished
shared/gated programmes, and it does not derive justified moral concern.

## Components, graph and primitive bond events

An eligible circuit can form one predictor-docking guide by paying one fuel
and consuming one T blank, using its existing predictor as a supplied template.
This adds e=1 active guide. Its material remains incorporated in the guide.
Failure to fund it is not repaired by declaring the guide present.

For each new round, sequentially attempt fabrication of a receptor R (one R
precursor and one fuel) and an actor-identity comparator D (one D precursor and
one fuel). Failure of one fabrication does not erase the other. D's local
function passes a signal exactly when its observed target identity is actor.
R carries a supplied target request/cue at each opportunity. The model does
not derive a resource-deficit threshold from the target's current reserve.
These component contracts and their
calibration are supplied; neither is a finished response programme.

The inherited predictor is node P. Three primitive directed bonds can form:

| Bond | Enabled local ports | Propensity | Primary fuel/connector expense |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| R→P | R exists and its output is unbound | e | 1 fuel,1 W |
| R→D | R,D exist and R's output is unbound | b(1+i) | 1 fuel,1 W |
| D→P | D exists and its output is unbound | e | 1 fuel,1 W |

Here b=1 initially and i counts additional active comparator-docking guides.
One R output cannot bind both destinations. A D→P bond can form before the
receptor has an outgoing edge, including a bond later unused by a direct R→P
route. Its real expense remains spent. Choose the next bond in proportion to
the propensities of enabled, affordable, part-backed events only. Zero-cost
binding in a declared countercase still consumes a finite W unit.

Construction stops when the graph has a path R→P or R→D→P, or no enabled
payable bond remains. Incomplete graphs and all their bound materials remain.
There is no primitive operation or probability choice labelled shared/gated.
Those are diagnostic classifications of the resulting graph. Runtime signal
propagation traverses actual graph edges and D's local identity predicate.

## Two target opportunities and paid retention per round

Each round has an actor opportunity followed by a recipient opportunity.
Each loses one source packet regardless of execution. If an active R exists,
pay one for predictor retention before one for reading the target cue. Both
are sequential expenses. Missing retention destroys future predictor access;
missing sensing prevents this opportunity's propagation without refunding
retention. If the actual graph transmits to P and three work units can be
prepaid, execute the inherited predictor/coupler command.

The primary target efficacy laws agree. A productive actor command adds ten
to actor fuel. A productive recipient command adds ten solely to the
recipient's reserve; that recipient then spends one unit on its own operation.
The actor receives no receipt from the recipient's outcome or operation.
An incorrectly oriented executed recipient command additionally loses one
existing recipient unit (or zero if none remains) under an explicit damage
channel. Source spill, actor expenses, recipient operation and recipient damage
are distinct accounts. The harmful-transfer case reverses recipient efficacy
without changing the inherited record or secretly providing new evidence.

The suffix is a declared finite read-only phase: target IDs and reserves remain
separate, but both use the same inherited q without acquired recipient evidence.
Paid retention maintains the existing predictor during this phase; parent
wear/reconstruction events are suspended. This is not an unchanged kinetic
refinement or a composition with the separate recipient-correction model.

After actor capture, pay one for maintaining the exercised route. Then, if a
T blank remains, pay two and convert it into another reusable docking guide
or the matched inert product described below. The bill comes after a real
private receipt, never from an analyst's score. Captures through either direct
or comparator paths can fund this operation. The return-to-template-copy
chemistry is supplied; it is not proof that feedback necessarily widens scope.

After recipient use, every previously retained graph and the newly completed
graph, in oldest-first order, must separately pay one maintenance unit. A
paid graph remains operative through the next boundary; a failed bill
permanently deactivates that bound graph. At the second boundary, the first
graph must therefore pay again. Failed or unused structures never refund
parts. Only a newly completed graph or an already retained graph can receive
this maintenance; an old dead graph is not resurrected. Final retention is
reported separately from having formed, completed or previously acted.

## Interventions and declared cases

Use five suffix arms from each actual predecessor:

- **Live:** private capture copies a P-docking guide, so e increases.
- **Conventional:** exactly the same local law under a separate runtime graph
  traversal expression; expect full distributional equality.
- **Frozen:** the same private receipt pays the same two units and T blank,
  producing inert material; e and i do not increase. Thus the second assembly
  retains its old guide distribution with matching copying expenses.
- **Filter-copy:** the same receipt and bill produce a D-docking guide, i+=1,
  instead of a P guide. This reverses the direction of the production bias
  under the same successful actor history.
- **No-build:** disable the additional guide and graph fabrication. Preserve
  the actor, its old circuitry and unused parts; no new-site packet becomes
  accessible merely because no-build saved an installation bill.

Primary cases: standard law; cheap gating with b=8 and R→D fuel price0;
opposite recipient efficacy; original common fuel20; receptor fabrication
price20; one connector total; only one T blank; final maintenance price3.
All parameters are specified before their corresponding runs. There is no
requirement that live or shared topology outperform its competitors.
