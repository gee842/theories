## Proved chain

1. The initialized paid reader/reason trace supplies the exact `PublicationSafety.Invariant`. A current non-root command requires a nonempty normalized input, then a nonempty completed cache cell. The receipt-only decoder makes that cell an accessible current positive frame. `Assured` alone does not supply this physical provenance.
2. Consumer steps preserve the archive. The same frame at the same index is therefore stored in the terminal producer bank. The accepted empty-bank provenance theorem places its emission in the actual producer trace; the new location theorem retains the exact before/after states and trace prefix/suffix.
3. Positive emission is specifically a present recipient acquisition following a positive worked phase. An induction on actual producer steps shows that phase either was already present initially or was generated by a recipient work event crediting ten physical units. The ready-initialized entrance excludes the first case. `AcquiredAfterDelivery` locates the credited work in this acquisition's own prefix, not merely somewhere later in the history.
4. `EpisodeBound` separately binds actual work positions, before states and successors to a predeclared episode and recipient/service scope. `ServiceMeaning` separately interprets that episode-bound physical credit as `Met`. Under these premises, a current command implies `Met`; a sound `Pending → ¬Met` assertion is impossible in this fixed entrance class.

The central declarations are `entrance_has_receipt_with_prior_delivery`, `current_command_implies_interpreted_service_met` and `no_sound_pending_one_service_entrance` in [ApplicationEvidence.lean](ApplicationEvidence.lean). Their `ReceiptOnlyEntrance` requires an actual ready-initialized empty-bank producer trace, an actual initialized paid consumer trace, and current command support at its endpoint. It does not assume a correct command, positive frame, fulfillment or ethical warrant at the entrance.

## Premises and limits

Here “current” means a current publication in the declared initialized reader context. The generic `ReceiptOnlyEntrance` does not equate that context with the terminal producer bank’s `world.context`, and supplies no empirical freshness or episode sensor. The actual witness matches its fresh bank. Historical fulfillment of one fixed service can persist after context changes under the independently justified interpretation.

- Physical receipt and credited resources do not intrinsically mean welfare, standing, service completion or ethical authority. No ethical `G` is needed for the physical provenance result. A warranted ethical application needs its independent grounds.
- `EpisodeBound` is a separately justified temporal/address/recipient interpretation of the predeclared bank window. Equality of Boolean context tags is insufficient. The file creates no runtime episode flag and does not infer a new service obligation from a new context or child.
- The no-pending result applies only where the positive credited work completes this one declared service. It does not apply automatically to recurring assistance, continuous protection, a different recipient/episode, independent obligations, qualitative service requirements, or broader banks with alternative grounds.
- The result rules out a proposed reachable comparison entrance; it supplies no deliberation policy, proves no global abstention theorem and does not assume selected action warranted.
- Missing, stale or zero observations do not become inverse facts or pending-need certificates. The existence of a current supported command here depends on the actual positive-only decoder and exact initialized publication invariant.
