# Paid measurement and predictive learning

6 October 2026. Exact finite constructions for the [consequence-to-learning
interface](../../../../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-MATH.md).
They work backward from the teaching assumptions of the concern model, alongside
the separate selector-revision study. The learner, sensor and actuator designs
remain explicit premises. These are mathematical specimens, not empirical
measurements or a derivation of felt valence and ethical concern.

## What the checks establish

The gate specimen starts damaged, with no acquired record or written coefficient.
Two worlds have identical accessible initial states but differ in source
availability. That source determines the independently defined gate-restoration
contrast. A paid probe reveals it; a separate paid write stores it. The supplied
controller uses only its declared observation, and a coefficient clamp changes
its repair command while holding the physical plant fixed.

| Initial common reserve | Probe and write before first repair | A cheaper route |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Source-present world can probe → write → repair → capture | Direct use of the record also works |
| 2 | Probe and write exhaust reserve before repair | Probe → repair → capture → write can succeed |
| 1 | Cannot both probe and write | A blind repair can still enable capture |

Capture consumes an actual source packet and transfers it to common fuel. It
does not refund earlier costs before they are paid. Exhaustive reachability
checks cover twelve source/reserve preparations. The three-unit obstruction is
specific to the stated order and memory route. The direct-record controller
preserves a counterexample to any universal three-unit learning-cost claim.

The same accurate coefficient can be connected to an inverted actuator that
does not deliver support. Prediction accuracy therefore does not determine
action direction. After capture, the old target remains historically
identifiable from the source/receipt ledger, while the current restoration
contrast is zero. Reuse of that old target requires a new justification.

## Funding changes the learning calculation

A separate stochastic circuit prepays operation and observation/write costs,
then measures the actual return. A counted finite source funds receipts. Its
teacher is the observed net common-fuel loss divided by two. The physical return
law gives mean target 1/2; the learner does not receive an independent reward.

With gain 1/4, zero initial coefficient and three opportunities:

| Quantity | Initial common reserve 6 | Initial common reserve 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean retained coefficient over all histories | 37/128 | 7/32 |
| Probability all three updates are paid | 1 | 1/4 |
| Probability common reserve remains at least 2 | 7/8 | 1/8 |
| Mean coefficient among histories with reserve at least 2 | 111/448 | 0 |
| Probability coefficient is strictly above 1/4 | 1/2 | 0 |
| Expected held-out squared prediction loss | 2653/8192 | 43/128 |

Both prediction losses are below the initial untrained loss 1/2 under the same
specified Bernoulli target law. They are analytic evaluation quantities, not
additional physically executed tests or runtime rewards. Retained memory in a
stopped process is not a functioning learner. Common reserve alone is not full
continuation: in the scarce all-success history, the incident source is spent.

The exact identity is

\[
Ew_{t+1}=(1-\delta)Ew_t+\eta E[u_tY_t]-\eta E[u_tw_t].
\]

Conditional calibration can give `E[uY] = β E[u]` while affordability still
correlates `u` with the existing coefficient. The ordinary mean recurrence is
valid under the stated fully funded schedule, not under arbitrary stopping or
survivor selection. Even when that mean exceeds a decision threshold, individual
histories can fail it. In the funded fixture the mean exceeds 1/4 but only half
the histories pass the strict threshold.

## Verification and limits

[check.py](check.py) checks the event ledgers, reachable order obstruction,
counterpaths, observation-only controllers, clamp intervention and exact moment
identities. It covers four primary stochastic cases and 390 additional finite
parameter cases. [results.json](results.json) records source hashes. The
[protocol](PROTOCOL.md) identifies the declared comparisons and the additional
threshold check introduced during interface review.

The [independent checker](independent_check.py) passed all of those cases and
45 further decay cases using full innovation words and closed-form weights,
rather than the producer's probability recursion. Its
[saved record](independent-check.json) and
[review](../../../../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-TO-LEARNING-REVIEW.md)
record the exact scope and hashes. These Python calculations are
not Lean proofs or a verified implementation of the earlier chemical process.
The gate and prediction circuit are new interface specimens. Their repertoire,
measurement accuracy, costs, update rule and policy remain supplied; their
outputs have not been composed into an actual concern mechanism.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/check.py
python3 experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/learning-interface/independent_check.py
```
