# Paid contextual certificates and successor replay

6 October 2026. AI-authored protocol agreed by the formal and independent-model branches before numerical computation, with the explicitly marked publication-freshness clarification below added after the first run. The named connection is **actual retained observations → context-specific certificates → rooted reason revision → charged successor replay**. The existing [recorder](../PROTOCOL.md) and the settlement's [R1](../../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/formal/REPORT.md) are predecessors, not proof that this new connection already works.

This is a finite deterministic construction. It supplies an implemented audit/replay interface under explicit assay, relevance and positive-anchor premises. It does not derive ethical standing, calibrate an arbitrary instrument, implement the whole protective game, or establish the prevalence of caring institutions.

## Fixed finite language and query binding

Use three nodes: root `a=0`, patient-case `p=1`, and patient-case `q=2`. Each is a particular case–reason pair, not a complete value assigned to a person. A valid positive root and the interpretation of candidate transfers remain supplied. There are three candidate edge identities. Their primary endpoints are, in order,

1. `a → p`;
2. `p → q`;
3. `a → q`.

There are two context versions, `false` and `true`. Six record addresses are bound before outcomes: positions 0–2 query edges 0–2 in the first context, and positions 3–5 query edges 0–2 in the second. Each address has its declared assay identity and polarity. This finite address layout is part of the reader's supplied recipe, not a learned semantic mapping or unbounded free metadata store. Each edge identity/context pair occurs once. Parallel edges with equal endpoints remain distinct certificates; conflicting duplicate observations for the same query are outside the positive protocol.

The underlying `RecordedEvidence` Boolean keeps its old physical meaning: `false` transfers two source units to common fuel, while `true` transfers none. A separate, declared deterministic assay/calibration law makes the observed Boolean the truth bit of this edge's particular factual applicability premise. The positive relevance rule has the form `Fact(context, edge) → Reason(source) → Reason(target)`. Thus `true` can license this transfer under that relevance rule. A calibrated `false` defeats this particular factual premise; it is not the logical negation of the entire implication from the source's reason to the target's reason. Distinct parallel edges may have different factual premises and remain independent grounds. The bit does not become ethically meaningful merely because it was stored or paid for.

Queries are admitted according to the prebound address plan and actual context before their outcome. Neither the plan nor the query is chosen after seeing the Boolean. Context cannot change during a reserved/latched recording attempt in the positive protocol. This is a stated stability condition; the model does not silently relabel an interrupted observation.

## Actual material producer

Start with material capacity 15, common fuel 80 and source 20, no formed A/H, an empty resident and a damaged site. The unchanged `FormedSelection` law performs:

**Form seven H → form eight A → associate A → repair.**

The result has seven free A and seven free H, one A resident, common fuel 63, source 20 and no local fuel. Preparation spends seventeen units. All formation precedes repair.

Six unchanged recorder assemblies each bind one A/H pair and cost one common unit. They leave six blank slots, one free A/H pair and fuel 57. A new one-unit reader/workspace assembly consumes the remaining free pair, leaving fuel 56. Total matter is twelve constituents in record slots, two in the reader/workspace, and the one resident.

The reader recipe supplies the bounded query map, certificate cache, context token, three-node working/published masks and four-path checking circuit. Their functional capacity is declared, not inferred from a two-constituent count. The combined reader remains physically present through the three-audit witness. Each use pays service/activation; no fresh reader or copied archive is given to a successor. There is no hard three-session limit unless the formal model explicitly adds one. This study claims a finite three-audit history, not indefinite maintenance.

Recording continues through the unchanged reserve/observe/commit protocol and its escrow. Two common units are secured before an outcome. All pending states, unused slots, receipts and spent costs remain represented. There is no overwrite, slot reclamation, conversion of inaccessible source into free upfront cash or free addition of a new record slot.

## Audit operations and charges

The operational certificate cache distinguishes **positive**, **negative** and **unknown**. Unknown includes absent current evidence; a stale or mismatched record cannot supply a current positive or current negative. Lack of access blocks reading instead of refuting the stored premise. A false edge defeats that particular transfer and does not declare its target node or patient false.

Each audit consists of the following actual payments from current common fuel:

| Operation | Charge | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Activate/service | 1 | Clear working admissions and certificate cache; install the supplied root as the working root. |
| Sense current context | 1 | Obtain a context token through the declared calibrated channel. |
| Decode each of three edge queries | 1 each | Read its bound record for that context, or retain unknown when absent. |
| Check path `a→p` | 1 | Add its endpoint only if every required edge has a current positive certificate. |
| Check path `a→q` | 1 | Same rule. |
| Check path `a→p→q` | 2 | Same rule, charging both relation checks even when the path fails. |
| Check path `a→q→p` | 2 | Same rule. |

The audit bill is **eleven**, with every path prepaid before checking it. This fixed catalogue contains all nonempty simple rooted paths on three nodes. Root `a` is already present after paid activation. Missing, negative or unknown edges do not cause unsupported paths to be admitted. The four checks are charged even when they add nothing. Publication occurs only after all required decodes and path checks; an interrupted audit remains a partial working state rather than a completed claim of exact closure.

The edge endpoint graph may vary in the general three-node checks. A path transition is supported if one candidate edge with those endpoints is currently positive. Cycles disconnected from the root cannot bootstrap; the supplied catalogue does not seed itself from old non-root admissions. The result is scoped to this finite language, not a proof of arbitrary-size graph closure at constant material or fuel cost.

A context-change event retains the archive and the old published mask with its version. A declared reliable interlock invalidates any active audit. A new paid activation and context sensing are necessary for a current completed result. Changing context alone does not silently update beliefs or establish that an old certificate has become a current negative. Current-soundness statements require matching context and completion. The sensing and invalidation mechanisms are supplied local laws, not derived from paying their fees.

**Supplemental publication-freshness clarification.** Records can only be appended in dormant phase 0; an active audit or completed publication in phase 10 prevents recording. Reopening with a context event resets the phase even when its context tag equals the existing tag. Thus an old published mask and matching context tag can coexist with a later, larger archive, but do not constitute a current completed audit. The old mask can remain sound while incomplete. Exactness against currently available records requires a completed audit with no subsequent reopening; a new paid audit is required after an append. A version label alone does not guarantee evidence freshness.

Successor turnover clears the operating/working admissions while retaining the real archive, reader backing, source and remaining fuel. The successor must activate, sense, decode and check again. It receives neither an already installed reason mask nor replenished funds. Reuse of the same archive is not copying it to newly created storage.

No later-context observations are preloaded before the first audit. There is one `false` recording outcome, so two source units move to common fuel. Recording spends twelve support units in all. This is a feasible observation history under the supplied sound assay law, not its probability. A current negative for `a→p` does not remove `q`'s independent direct ground. No claim follows that `p` lacks moral standing.

## Prespecified independent checks

The independent program must implement the recorder/reader event table without importing the new Lean conclusions or executable producer code. Compare paid replay with a separately computed rooted closure and independent simple-path reasoning. Keep the following scopes distinct:

- Enumerate every ordered triple of non-self directed candidate edges on three nodes, including repeated endpoints, and every Boolean certificate assignment. Check the four-path result against closure.
- Enumerate the two contexts, all six-bit archive words and every available prefix length, including absent current records. Check decoded positive/negative/unknown and completed replay. Use the actual primary graph for the full resource and archive grid.
- Vary common fuel around the eleven-unit audit requirement with source unchanged. An unaffordable stage retains earlier payments, the archive and the partial workspace. Construct a low-fuel recording predecessor by changing its **original** endowment, not its endpoint.
- Replay the complete preparation, interleaved recordings, old/new audits and successor account. Check total material, source, common fuel, escrow and historical bytes at each step.
- Compare an ideal current-bit/free-audit R1 oracle with the actual paid reader. The ideal closure is a comparator, not information supplied to the physical process.

Adversarial cases must cover missing records, stale context, visibly mismatched query labels, absent assay/polarity agreement, and corrupted trusted binding. Observable mismatch may produce unknown; an undetectably forged binding or false calibration may produce a wrong certificate. Do not claim universal corruption detection. That last control deliberately violates a positive soundness premise and demonstrates why it is needed.

Also test a fresh negative with surviving alternative support, an unsupported `p↔q` cycle, an interrupted recording/context change, a context change during replay, blocked archive access, a delivery/actuator cut that does not change warrant, cold successor without enough replay fuel, and the distinction between unknown and false. A new record must use a remaining slot. Evidence or working masks may not be silently copied, repaired or made current.

The publication-freshness check was added by root/reviewer review after the original numerical run: audit a one-record prefix, reopen under the same context, append the next positive record, and compare the old installed mask with the enlarged available closure before and after another paid audit. This is a supplemental diagnostic, not part of the original prespecified grid. It clarifies the existing event guards without changing their law.
