The formal checker passes 58 new audits and 94 inherited audits under pinned Lean 4.29.1 with frozen dependency sources in a fresh build. Three new declarations have empty dependencies; the other 55 use only standard `propext`, `Quot.sound` and/or `Classical.choice`. No custom axioms, proof holes or native proof shortcuts are added. Consult the manifest for the exact declaration and dependency records. The finite checker independently implements the event table; it does not import the producer's transition implementation or Lean conclusions. Its saved results distinguish configured grids from actual formation histories.

The fixed three-node language has four nonempty simple rooted paths. A complete audit costs eleven common units: activation, context sensing, three decodes and six path-relation checks. Unknown, negative and positive certificates remain distinct. Sound negative factual interpretation requires its own calibration assumption; no negative result by itself denies a patient's standing.

The main history declares capacity 15, common fuel 80 and source 20 before preparation. It forms six record slots and one reader/workspace pair, records `111`, audits, changes context, records `011`, revises while preserving an alternative ground, and replays for a cold working successor. The checked whole ledger is `100 = 17 + 6 + 1 + 12 + 33 + 31`. This finite recipe supplies its functional memory/decoder capacity; counting constituents does not derive that capacity.

Independent checks pass 1,728 graph/certificate cases, 23,296 configured audits, fourteen actual low-fuel histories, sixteen original boundaries and one supplemental diagnostic. All recorded final source hashes match.

Completed publication is phase-gated. Reopening even the same context withdraws completed status, so appending a previously missing record cannot make an old result automatically complete for the enlarged archive. The supplemental diagnostic and its post-computation origin are preserved separately from the original protocol.

This supplies a finite paid-audit interface, not an empirical instrument calibration, arbitrary-size graph algorithm, replacement organism, protective strategic equilibrium or complete theory of wisdom. The eleven-unit physical bill cannot silently replace the older settlement's monetary audit bill. A recipient's zero receipt during outages also cannot be reinterpreted as this protocol's sound negative assay.
