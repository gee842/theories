The [existing reader](../README.md) is the implemented predecessor. Its semantic grounds and its eleven-unit physical bill remain unchanged. The purpose is to supply an actual action interface to the existing concern settlement. A physically executable word, a selected action in a specified game and continuing renewal are different claims. The formal branch addresses the first and its general conditions. The independent executable branch also solves a finite game over the actual residual states. Neither claims to derive ethical standing from an energy ledger, or to identify a particular natural actuator merely by assigning it constituents.
