# Addressed claims through actual paid action

6 October 2026. AI-authored formal interface study. This supporting continuation leaves the accepted protection process unchanged. It addresses the existing composition obligation between partial warranted evidence, actual action and claims that remain unfulfilled. It does not construct concern or select a new ethical objective.

## Question and prior results

The repository already establishes a finite undominated action remainder, robust feasibility under indistinguishable evidence, and sound semantic action transport. Those are inputs and precedents, not new results of this study. The proposed addition is a verification interface for actual paid traces and an explicit addressed-claims ledger.

Every claim retains its patient, reason, context and addressee through its type or declared interpretation. Independently admitted claims K are separate from action permissions, certified fulfillment D, actual harm and the unresolved set K minus D. An unknown or unperformed action does not defeat its patient's reason. Conversely, lack of a certificate does not prove actual harm. The ledger is mathematical verification state; this study claims no newly constructed storage, reader or deliberation apparatus.

## Formal contracts

1. The retained evidence class contains the actual world. A chosen action's normative permission is supplied separately from its actual enabled paid execution. A sound certificate can claim only the addressed effect that execution establishes. Partial evidence need not certify every claim or every candidate edge.
2. Execution preserves the admitted ledger for the fixed claim/context under study. Evidence or argument can separately revise admission, but mere unaffordability, obstruction, absent records or failed execution is not such a revision rule.
3. Combining certified fulfillments requires a shared assessment horizon and preservation of the earlier fulfilled condition. A generic union law must expose that persistence premise.
4. For the unchanged protection law, prove each patient's accumulated harm never decreases along any actual trace. Derive the exact condition for no new harm across two concatenated traces: the same patient must have no new harm in both. Continuous protective coverage therefore composes by intersection, rather than by union of differently timed successful acts.
5. Instantiate the interface using the existing reached protection states and actual histories, retaining the energy account, both patients and the distinction between historical protection and a later period's harm. Include delivery obstruction, uncertified harmless withdrawal, and insufficient audit funds where the parent permits the relevant statements. Do not infer global infeasibility from the cost of one armed protocol; voluntary protection and withdrawal remain alternatives.

## Checking and scope

Use Lean 4.29.1, a fresh temporary dependency build and explicit axiom reports for every new theorem. Preserve the imported parent files byte for byte. An independent collaborating agent reviews proof statements, supplied premises, type/time attribution and concrete inherited witnesses. This is internal mathematical review, not external peer review or empirical evidence. No new stochastic experiment or broad benchmark is proposed.

The result would be rejected or restricted if a certificate changes its patient or horizon unnoticed, treats unknown as false, erases a claim on nonpayment, promotes an intended act to a reached trace, or counts an earlier protected interval as later protection. Grounding standing, legitimate authority, forming evaluative uptake, selecting among incomparable claims and constructing renewable support remain separate obligations of the existing foundation.
