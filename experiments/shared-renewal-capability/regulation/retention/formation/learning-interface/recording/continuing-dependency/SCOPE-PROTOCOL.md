# Prospective activity-scope protocol

Status: fixed before the first execution of `scope.py`. This extends the actual
rich trained ancestor of `criterion.py`; it does not modify that frozen law or
its results. The named connection is: formed material reaction → acquired
item-specific continuation scope → operative revision when that reaction ends.

## Inherited history and resources

Replay the saved original 94-event FormedSelection ancestor, then the exact
`criterion.TRAINING` word with faithful receipt and `later_repair=False`. The
trained endpoint is `(29,30,1,1,1,274,215)`, with nine A/H module pairs, the actual
common-channel receipt, and the earlier historical count completion. The old
criterion is inactive. The new configuration contains no later-repair flag.
The old whole state and both new expenditure ledgers are retained in every row.

Three further A/H pairs are assembled for the two records and their reader.
Each assembly consumes one common unit. Each activity is one further A/H pair:
phase 0 absent; 1 coupled; 2 primed; 3 product; 4 dissociated. Phases 1, 2 and 3
continue to occupy the pair; phase 4 returns it to the free pools. No lifecycle
restarts. The native capacity is always `78 - 2*(oldParts + readerParts + heldA
+ heldB)`. No repair/capture law is changed and no common fuel is replenished.

## Physical activity law, distinct from its records

Formation consumes one free A, one free H and one common unit. A's first use
requires the existing local unit, consumes it and leaves the site damaged;
it changes phase 1 to phase 2. A's final conversion likewise consumes a local
unit and leaves the site damaged, but now produces phase 3. Thus after first
use, in the actual empty-local damaged state, completing A requires the native
repair→capture connection. This need is produced by activity, rather than by
an externally stipulated future damage event. The final A product does not
remove the damage it has caused.

B's first use and final conversion each consume a common unit. B can continue
when the site is damaged and local support is empty. Dissociating an unfinished
A or B costs one common unit and returns its pair; it produces no product and
does not undo damage or expenditure. Completion, dissolution and continuing
B are different physical and acquired contents. No claim that every permitted
dissociation is a justified release from a commitment is made.

All reactions are available according to these physical guards independently
of any reader. The supplied recipes are a new explicit local-law extension.

## Acquisition and consumption

A contact costs one common unit and emits the actual phase plus one into its
item record. Zero means no received contact. Records 4 and 5 therefore preserve
the difference between product and dissolution. The first phase-2 records are
also retained as snapshots for the controls. `refreshA` uses the same contact
and cost: faithful copies current phase; missing emits zero and leaves the old
record; stale emits the earlier stored snapshot; false-absent emits code 1.
These faulty modes are explicit interventions, not faithful native observations.

Acquired pending items are records 2 or 3. A charged selector uses these acquired
records, the inherited learned common-channel cost, and supplied current plant
support/feasibility feedback. It does not inspect A or B's actual phase. For a
recorded primed A it selects a physically support-feasible repair/capture/final
conversion plan; otherwise it selects a recorded primed B if support permits.
A has supplied priority when both are feasible. A source-starved pending item
is retained in the scope even when its plan is unavailable. The reader clamp
uses the earlier snapshots despite receiving the current faithful records.

`select` costs one common unit and stores a plan code. Each subsequent `act`
executes one stored primitive and advances a program counter. Each primitive
has its actual guard and expenditure; no skipped/blocked reaction is inserted
as a native event, and no failed plan is silently redirected. Thus a stale A
record can cause an actual unnecessary repair, followed by capture, and then
fail on the unavailable final A reaction. The current actual phase is used
only by the physical reaction guard, not as an oracle correcting the record.

## Fixed histories and controls

The shared new prefix is: assemble reader three times; contact absent A;
form A; prime A; contact A; form B; prime B; contact B. This reaches common 265,
A and B both primed, site damaged and local empty. Acquired scope expands from
empty to A to A+B. Branches then run as follows:

- A completion: native repair, capture, finish A (common 264, source 214).
- A dissolution: dissociate A (common 264, source 215).
- A left unfinished: no additional reaction (common 265, source 215).
- A source unavailable: repair, then 215 actual capture/leak-local pairs, then
  native damage (common 264, source 0); A remains primed.

Every branch refreshes A at its declared mode and cost. Before the terminal
selection, fixed leak counts are 261 for completion/dissolution/source-empty,
and 262 for unchanged A. All therefore reach common 2; selection leaves 1.
These counts and all control modes are fixed before execution.

The nine cases are completion faithful/stale/missing/clamped; dissolution
faithful/stale; unfinished faithful/false-absent; and source-empty faithful.
Run the selected plan until finished or its next true guard rejects it.
Retain denied events and every full state. Expected contrasts are conditional:
faithful completed/dissociated A leaves the unit for actual B product; stale
or clamped A spends it on repair, then cannot produce A again or finish B.
For unfinished A, faithful evidence selects its actual completion whereas
false absence selects B and leaves A unfinished. Source exhaustion does not
make A complete or erase its pending acquired relation; B can still finish.

The endpoint audit is not a new runtime observation. In particular after B
physically finishes, its last acquired record is still primed until a later
contact; this finite history ends without pretending a free final update.

## Claims and boundaries

This tests conditional formation and revision of operational activity scope:
new occupied structures add real continuations, received contacts put their
different dependencies into a reader, and actual products/dissolution change
which item the reader continues. Damage, products, material, sunk support and
other ongoing activity remain distinct. It does not derive why any activity
ought to be initiated or continued, legitimate withdrawal, a unique priority,
or universal noninstrumental concern. Reaction/observation laws, finite feature
semantics, availability of source and prior learning, the schedule and selector
priority remain declared local premises. A fixed policy informed of these laws
is still a comparator; the experiment does not establish learning is necessary.
