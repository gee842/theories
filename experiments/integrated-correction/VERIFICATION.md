# Verification plan for a funded correction practice

5 October 2026. This is a mathematical construction and numerical audit, not an empirical experiment or a registered prediction. The team chose parameters after deriving the mechanism; the illustrations cannot establish prevalence in real institutions.

## Claim to check

A practice can finance an informative, executable correction service from its own realized production and remain favored by payoff-based adoption. There can be both a self-maintaining regime and a depleted regime under the same parameters. Financing, selection, and initiation impose different constraints.

The construction fixes the task, fee rule, collection and service-access rights, output attribution, and adoption process. It does not derive the legitimacy or political survival of these rules. See the mathematical construction and independent integration/adversarial reviews.

## Checks specified before the root verification run

1. Derive production from a balanced two-context task, noisy information, and executable action access. Check the intermediate proposal and enactment outcomes, not only total production.
2. Check that fees subtract exactly what capacity renewal receives, after the declared conversion. Include common spillovers without crediting them twice or changing relative payoffs.
3. Independently calculate fixed points and linear stability. Across varied admissible parameters, compare the analytic fee-window criterion with the selection margin at a numerically solved full-participation equilibrium. This checks algebra; it is not statistical evidence.
4. Under one parameter set, integrate a healthy initial state and an explicit depleted rectangle. Check the two invariant-region bounds and limiting behavior.
5. Preserve all costs while separately removing informative evidence and executable action access. Both must defeat the productive correction mechanism. An uninformative signal is not cost-free.
6. Demonstrate failure with inadequate private benefit capture and fees outside the feasible interval. The existence of a benefit does not suffice to fund a selected practice.
7. Check a finite capacity-only seed: adoption must follow its ordinary equation after the intervention, with no forced membership reset or permanent subsidy. Report the sufficient seed cost as a conservative bound, not the minimum recovery cost.
8. Show that an externally fixed capacity can mask a financing failure. This is a deliberately different model, included to expose why funding must be in the model.

Numerical integration will use a log-odds coordinate for interior participation, so floating-point rounding near full participation does not create a false absorbing state. The actual zero-participation and full-participation boundaries will be handled analytically. Proofs, rather than finite integration horizons, support asymptotic conclusions.

An exploratory extension was added after the initial verification run: compare two initial states with exactly equal current gross output, and inspect whether their trajectories enter different proved attraction regions. Current retained output and upkeep are also reported so the comparison cannot hide differing expenditures. This diagnostic was chosen after the model and its basins were known; it is not an independent or prospective prediction.

## Interpretation boundaries

- The aggregate equations could also describe an ordinary productive club. Distinct evidence and action measurements are necessary to test the correction interpretation.
- One capacity stock bundles two channels. Removing a channel probes the assumed causal mechanism; it does not establish independent stock dynamics or endogenous political authority.
- A finite external seed is an intervention, not spontaneous recovery. With no adopters, this selection equation cannot invent an institution.
- Output is a stipulated local task success measure. It does not define a universal objective, legitimate social ordering, or an AI alignment solution.
- Familiar public-goods, coordination, institutional evolution, and ecological-feedback mechanisms are close prior art. Novelty is not established by these checks.
