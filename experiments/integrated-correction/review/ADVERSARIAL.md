# Review of the integrated correction model

This review assesses the bounded claim that a specified correction service can produce useful responses, pay for its own information and implementation capacity, and persist through selection among funding practices. It does not assess the whole meta-attractor corpus or establish novelty. The review was conducted by a separate AI research agent on 5 October 2026, with targeted primary-source comparison. It is not independent human peer review.

The important distinction is between a valid conditional join and a completed theory of self-maintaining institutions. Actual service funding and practice selection can be made endogenous while access rules, revenue attribution, and the available alternatives remain fixed. That is a useful boundary if it is stated. Renaming those remaining assumptions as capacity would not close the institutional question.

## Acceptance conditions

The following conditions apply to the result, its implementation, and the interpretation of its examples.

1. **Derive useful correction from a task.** The improvement in output must follow from specified observations and feasible actions. It cannot be a bonus awarded to a practice because it is labelled corrective. A noisy binary task suffices for a restricted result. It does not model discovery of previously unrepresented concepts.
2. **Charge the full resource flow.** Fees or investments must be subtracted from the output or reserves that pay them. A conversion coefficient must pay for the complete information and implementation package, including both stages when their success probabilities are multiplied.
3. **Make access explicit.** If noncontributors can use the same direct service, they obtain the same service benefit without paying. Restricting their access is a substantive mechanism assumption. It must not be hidden inside the payoff formula.
4. **Derive selection from specified returns.** The copying or replacement advantage must use the declared observable net returns. A shared benefit received equally by both practices cancels from this comparison. Prospective foresight, political legitimacy, or deliberate public-interest choice cannot be inferred from payoff-biased copying.
5. **Distinguish infrastructure from authority.** Physical capacity to execute an action is not automatically legitimate authority to choose it or the ability to preserve that right against attack. The result must state which object it models.
6. **Show both continuation and failure.** An invariant functioning region is evidence for maintenance under stated initial conditions. A genuine failure basin under the same parameters demonstrates that those initial conditions matter. Neither result by itself certifies an affordable transition between basins.
7. **Check all fixed assumptions in the survival claim.** The budget commitment, direct-benefit attribution, access mechanism, observation rule, task family, and allowed successor practices must be listed. Survival means resistance to the represented alternatives, not every strategically possible attack.
8. **Use meaningful controls.** Disable information, disable implementation, remove private benefit recovery, and allow nonpayers equal access while retaining costs. These interventions test the actual causal links rather than reproducing the theorem's algebra.
9. **Do not upgrade sufficient conditions to necessities.** Failure of a protected-region certificate is not proof of collapse. A stable equilibrium is not a global recovery guarantee. A numerical example is not a proof of a phase boundary.
10. **Keep the contribution bounded.** A finite task with a known observation technology can establish production of usable information. It cannot establish open-ended cognition, legitimate preference formation, or a universal alignment objective.

## Resource and access audit

For the proposed two-practice model, let the funding-practice share be \(x\), its privately recoverable service return be \(r(C)=y_0+\chi Bq(C)\), and its reinvestment fraction be \(\tau\). The proposed direct net returns are \((1-\tau)r(C)\) and \(y_0\). A common spillover \((1-\chi)xBq(C)\) can be added to both without changing the selection difference.

Under this interpretation, total gross output is \(y_0+xBq(C)\), the investment flow is \(\tau x r(C)\), and total consumption plus investment equals gross output. There is no external corrective bonus. Capacity production \(\eta\tau xr(C)\) is legitimate bookkeeping only if \(\eta\) converts that complete paid input into the declared joint service package.

The access interpretation remains essential. A nonpaying practice receives the common spillover but does not receive the direct correction service. This can represent membership in an excludable service or use of privately accessible equipment. It cannot simultaneously represent a fully public service available equally to everybody.

The decisive counterfactual gives nonpayers the same direct service. Their direct return then becomes \(r(C)\), and the funding practice's relative return is exactly \(-\tau r(C)<0\). With positive baseline output and depreciation, the funding share and capacity eventually disappear. Any successful result must acknowledge that maintaining appropriable access is one of its premises.

Likewise, setting \(\chi=0\) makes all additional benefits public. Under the stipulated current-return copying rule, the funding practice pays \(\tau y_0\) without a relative benefit. This is a negative control, not a universal theorem that public goods cannot persist under other selection, governance, or commitment mechanisms.

## Information and implementation audit

A useful microfoundation is a balanced latent binary task. Without a distinguishing observation, either chosen action is correct with probability one half. A purchased signal has accuracy \(\alpha>1/2\), and applying its recommendation requires a separate successful implementation stage. If both stages operate with probability \(C/(K+C)\), conditional independence gives \(q(C)=[C/(K+C)]^2\).

For a nonnegative baseline payoff \(b\) and correct-action increment \(\Delta>0\), the default mean return is \(y_0=b+\Delta/2\), and the improvement coefficient is \(B=\Delta(\alpha-1/2)\). Thus \(0<B\le y_0\). A noisy signal avoids assuming an infallible answer, although the task family and signal technology still remain specified from outside.

The two-stage product is a technological assumption. Correlated breakdowns, biased signals, communication failure, malicious reports, and a policy refusing to follow a valid recommendation would change it. A single stock can represent a jointly purchased package, but cannot establish independent dynamics of evidence institutions and political authority.

The task-derived bound also matters mathematically. With arbitrarily large \(\chi B/y_0\), the full-funding capacity equation can have several positive equilibria. The unrestricted example \(y_0=1,\chi B=100,\tau=.2,\eta=1,\mu=4,K=1\) has three. Claims of a unique positive equilibrium therefore require the task-derived coefficient restriction or another stated condition. This is a repair achieved by the microfoundation rather than an assumption that the desired root is unique.

## Closest primary work

[Andrews, Clark, Hillis and Borgerhoff Mulder, The cultural evolution of collective property rights for sustainable resource governance](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-024-01290-1), published 19 February 2024, already combine political resource-use rules, costly monitoring funded from payoffs, resource harvesting, and cultural selection. The [primary open copy in the Max Planck repository](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3571303_1/component/file_3571304/content) was read directly: its model description and access-rights sections specify policy aggregation, paid monitoring, seizures, and payoff-biased transmission. This directly precedes a general claim to integrate funding and institutional evolution. Our serial information-to-action service and exact financing threshold concern a narrower object, not demonstrated superiority.

[Lee, Iwasa, Dieckmann and Sigmund, Social evolution leads to persistent corruption](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1900078116), published 13 June 2019, model costly monitoring of enforcement institutions and the interaction of vigilance, corruption, and cooperation. The publisher's full article was read directly. Its Results specify paid information about honest or corrupt umpires, service fees, and imitation based on payoffs; its analysis includes persistent and damped oscillations. Thus costly maintenance of trustworthy institutions has close predecessors. Our two-practice construction does not model corrupt officials or their strategic responses.

[Lerch, Smith, Koffel, Bagby and Abbott, How public can public goods be? Environmental context shapes the evolutionary ecology of partially private goods](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010666), published 1 November 2022, use resource-explicit public-goods models to derive conditions under which producers persist through partially retained benefits. The publisher's full text was read directly, including the single-resource formulation, results, and colimitation analysis. Recoverable benefits exceeding production costs is established territory. Our task's information and implementation stages supply a specific interpretation, not a first general explanation of maintained cooperation.

These comparisons support using the model as a transparent conditional synthesis. They do not support a claim that its components or its basic threshold coordination structure are unprecedented. A stronger contribution would need a result that these neighboring models do not provide, plus evidence that its extra distinction changes explanation or intervention.

## Proof review

The [completed construction](../model/CONSTRUCTION.md) was reviewed independently. No mathematical blocker was found in the following claims under its stated assumptions.

| Claim | Review conclusion |
|---|---|
| Unique capacity equilibrium at fixed positive prevalence | Valid using the task-derived bound \(A=\chi B\le y_0\); the quotient \(h_x(C)/C\) decreases strictly from positive infinity to a negative limit |
| Exact financing interval | Valid for strict resistance of the full-funding equilibrium to the represented nonfunding practice; the condition is \(\eta A>4\mu K\) |
| Protected rectangle | Both active lower boundaries point inward; bounded capacity supplies global continuation |
| Convergence within the rectangle | Valid apart from its saddle corner; monotonic prevalence, the logit identity, and scalar comparison suffice |
| Same-parameter failure basin | The explicit depleted rectangle is invariant and forces exponential loss of the funding practice followed by capacity decay |
| Global loss below the strict return threshold | Valid for every fixed fee and every initial \(x<1\); the invariant full-funding boundary and equality cases are correctly excluded |
| Finite capacity-only initiation | Valid as a sufficient construction with an actual external installation budget, inherited positive adoption, instantaneous authorized installation, and no omitted installation charge |
| Free-access and disabled-channel controls | Their negative return differences force the declared loss result while costs remain charged |

The strict threshold also has a simple independent derivation. At a full-funding equilibrium, replacement costs consume \(\mu C/\eta\) units of output. Selection favors the practice exactly when recoverable benefit exceeds that expense. The maximum benefit per capacity unit is

\[
\max_{C>0}\frac{Aq(C)}{C}
=\max_{C>0}\frac{AC}{(K+C)^2}
=\frac{A}{4K}.
\]

This proves the feasibility threshold for the specified fixed-fee family. The number four depends on the paired saturation technology. For a single-stage alternative \(q=C/(K+C)\), the corresponding supremum gives threshold \(\eta A>\mu K\). Cross-technology comparison would also need to charge the different production costs. The two-stage result is not a universal bound on all ways to finance correction.

Independent arithmetic checks reproduced the example's positive capacity root \(2.31729540689132\), net selection advantage \(0.158647703445655\), and fee interval \((0.0168678804505472,0.368225287251316)\). The depleted rectangle's upper-edge funding limit is \(27/98\), above the chosen prevalence bound \(1/4\). These are equation checks, not empirical observations or a substitute for the coordinating agent's trajectory audit.

The requested wording repair was incorporated: with the exact stated conversion and no additional installation expense, the chosen seed's charge is \([C_{\rm seed}-C_{\rm initial}]_+/\eta\). This is a charge for the sufficient construction, not a claim to the cheapest successful intervention.

The completed [implementation](../verify_model.py) and [saved verification record](../results/verification.json) were also inspected. The task enumeration, exact payment accounting, log-odds integration, 600 admissible parameter comparisons, free-access control, and finite-seed calculation match the construction. The reported cross-integrator discrepancy for the seeded trajectory is about \(1.54\times10^{-7}\). Some raw depleted-capacity values are slightly negative, around \(10^{-10}\) or smaller, within the declared numerical tolerance; the mathematical flow preserves nonnegativity. These values mean numerical zero, not physically negative capacity. No implementation blocker was found in this review.

## Verdict and remaining boundary

**Accepted for the declared bounded claim.** Under explicit service access and copying assumptions, correction can generate the return paying for its maintenance, while the practice that pays for it remains selected. The same parameters admit a depleted failure basin, and an explicitly funded capacity-only intervention can reach a protected region from a positive adoption seed. This joins useful function, funding, practice persistence, and one finite transition rather than assuming a permanent external correction budget.

Loss here means disappearance of the additional correction service; baseline output continues. Moreover, the aggregate equations could describe an ordinary productive club. The correction interpretation earns its specific content from the task's independently represented signal and action stages. Proposal accuracy and the enactment of correct proposals are therefore important observations for any external application.

Endogenous constitutional survival remains unestablished. Access enforcement, revenue attribution, the rule governing practice replacement, and the admissible alternatives remain outside the two-state dynamics. The task repertoire and signal technology remain fixed as well. The proof does not establish open-ended cognitive renewal, a universal ethical criterion, or a solution to AI alignment. Its defensible contribution is a transparent conditional join with exact thresholds, contrasting basins, and an explicit initiation cost. Originality and external predictive value remain separate questions.
