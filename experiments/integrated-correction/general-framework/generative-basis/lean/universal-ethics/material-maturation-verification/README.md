The [source](../MaterialMaturation.lean), [checker](check.py) and [receipt](verification.json) cover **28 new theorems / 44 new declaration audits**. Lean 4.29.1 uses a fresh temporary build and isolated `LEAN_PATH`; source snapshots and compiler/axiom logs are retained with hashes. Only standard logical dependencies are allowed, and warnings or proof placeholders fail the check.

The checked interface derives growth probabilities from uniformly sampled actual components, verifies precursor/support accounts, and removes actual tokens into a supplied slot recipe. Failed routing retains unused components and support. Joined contact/growth/routing expectations reverse the previous partition ranking under same-type production and change again under complementary production. Complete preparation is equivalent to the recipe's required composition, not a freely assigned reset probability.

Native catalyst laws, routing capabilities and recipe, slot geometry, partition formation and initial stocks remain explicit. The finite protocol does not establish renewed supplies, invasion, biological calibration or superiority over the same conventional mechanism.
