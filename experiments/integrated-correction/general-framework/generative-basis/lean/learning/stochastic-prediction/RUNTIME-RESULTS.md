## Two diagnostic boundaries

With primary prices, initial fuel4, a live Fast circuit, q0/last0, fixed regime0 and availability1/2:

- failure leaves fuel0, spends4, wastes10, and cannot pay writing;
- capture leaves fuel7, spends7, wastes0, and pays writing.

Thus capture and paid writing each have unconditional probability1/2, while capture conditional on paid writing has probability1. Reporting only payable-write trials would select successful receipts. This example does not change q and does not demonstrate correction.

With fuel8 and write cost6, the same first failure leaves fuel4. A later capture can fund a write and ends at fuel2. The two-step first-failure/second-capture path has probability1/4; total second-capture mass is1/2. Treating the first unpaid write as absorbing would remove that1/4 continuing recovery mass. Again q stays0: the result is later paid use/write, not learned correction.

The broader grid includes active-circuit rent when the gate is expired, rent failure with memory destruction, sunk prototype costs, inactive/no-work packet expiry, last-action tie handling, end-of-trial expiry and the alternative record/action controls. Matching costs does not make the outage decoder correct; its erroneous labels remain in the actual output and metrics.
