# Pre-comparison scope clarification

6 October 2026. Added after the numerical protocol/source snapshot and **before any new outcome comparison**.

The frozen CHECK-PROTOCOL phrase “all composed endpoint/moment distributions” means the exact data structure actually returned by the inherited `evolve`: endpoint probability mass and cumulative **mass-weighted first moments conditional on each endpoint**. It is not a joint distribution of complete cumulative seventeen-metric vectors. This clarification narrows the description, not the comparison or returned fields.

The new checker compares the full one-exposure joint law of `(State, seventeen-metric increment)` at every reached entry. It then independently propagates the actual inherited endpoint/weighted-first-moment recursion and compares it directly with callable `evolve` and all saved aggregate metrics. The composition retains hidden orientation, real resource/state successors, actual reversal timing and all probability mass. No claim of a full cumulative-cost distribution, covariance or higher-moment reconstruction is made.

The formal evaluator source was still undergoing proof/audit finalization when the first source snapshot was taken. Its operational definitions were declared frozen by the owner before this comparison; the final compilation receipt binds the final bytes separately. Both source snapshots remain preserved. The parent source, parent results and original formal protocol hashes remain unchanged.
