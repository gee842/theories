## Actual stages and costs

A full opportunity executes this declared order:

1. If the gate is expired and its assembly is affordable, pay assembly and select its type. Otherwise preserve the existing state.
2. If the source base-formation guard holds, pay base construction and select a fair prior orientation. This resets the circuit's in-progress acquisition flags but preserves any historical completed witness.
3. If a prototype and live gate have affordable retrofit, pay it and install the predictive circuit.
4. An installed live circuit owes rent even if the gate is unavailable. Unaffordable rent destroys its accessible circuit fields according to `advance`; it does not erase a completed historical witness.
5. With a live gate and affordable work, issue the existing memory-decoded action, pay work, and sample physical capture. The capture probability is availability if action equals the retained hidden orientation, zero otherwise. A capture pays maintenance from its packet return.
6. Pay writing only if the actual post-work fuel permits it. Otherwise retain `q`. Paid frozen-register writes are sham writes, not unpaid events.
7. Whether or not writing was possible, an executed trial updates last action and renews or wears both lifetimes. An unpaid write is a **continuing boundary outcome**, not absorbing deadlock. A later opportunity may reform hardware.
8. Every uncaptured exposure loses its scheduled packet, including exposures with no work. No sunk construction or rent is refunded.

The independent interpreter exposes these stages and retains an in-flight scheduled packet until actual capture or expiry. At each prefix, current fuel plus that packet's remaining energy plus cumulative expenditure and waste equals entry fuel plus one packet. Work failure and no-work expiry are distinct; an interruption before completion retains its actual intermediate state and bill. This prefix representation is an interpretation of the declared sequential code, not an added interruption API or physical circuit construction proved for Python.
