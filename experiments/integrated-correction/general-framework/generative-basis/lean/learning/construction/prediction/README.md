# Typed material for acquired prediction and action

This package joins actual constituent preparation and `ConstructedUpdater` machinery to the unchanged deterministic payable-write `PredictionAction` law. The paid retrofit consumes one free A/H pair into a separately retained five-state register. Later updater formation competes for the remaining pairs; writes reuse the register and updater replacement preserves its acquired value.

The formal checker uses pinned Lean 4.29.1, builds the exact dependencies in a fresh temporary directory, records hashes and audits 36 new declarations plus 180 inherited declarations. Three new declarations have empty axiom dependencies; the other 33 use only standard `propext` and/or `Quot.sound`. Some inherited proofs also use `Classical.choice`. There are no new proof holes, custom axioms or native proof shortcuts.

For an installed register, a specified predictive word and endpoint lift exactly when the original word is feasible and its updater-formation count fits both initial free stocks. All physical microevents, acquired memory, leases, packet counts, phases and payment guards remain intact. Material and energy are conserved with explicit expenditure and incorporated/spent parts.

An actual capacity-eleven preparation constructs both machinery and memory, learns a productive orientation, suffers reversal, replaces the updater while retaining the acquired record, corrects the next command and captures again. Its final material is one resident plus bound A3/H5 plus the separate register pair. The whole energy account is `154 = 13 + 1 + 3 + 3 + 96 + 38`; final fuel is 18 and two ten-unit packets remain. The earlier thirteen-material updater-revision endpoint is also retained as an actual obstruction: it can afford the coarse retrofit but has no free register pair.

The finite checker independently implements the declared laws and checks 972 retrofit configurations, 302,508 short-word/inventory/register cases, nine whole-witness inventory pairs, 387 prefix/inventory cases and seven boundaries. A smaller four-packet graph closes at 180 states and 222 enabled labelled edges. The saved record includes actual ten-material preparation shortages and a paid-work/write-insolvency case.

This is a finite conditional construction. Component recipes, five-state memory, decoder, predictive wiring and staging remain supplied. No probabilities are transported from the coarse construction. The full stochastic Python predictor, erased or rebuilt memory, append-only evidence, action-coupler construction, recipient concern, indefinite material renewal and empirical chemistry require further compatible models.
