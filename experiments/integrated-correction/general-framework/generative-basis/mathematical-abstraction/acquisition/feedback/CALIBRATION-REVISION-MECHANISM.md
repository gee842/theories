## Operative evidence, consumption, and preserved failures

The evidence-preserving clamp forms the same full record, filters the same

inquiry proposal. Its commit withholds activation. All state and charges agree
through proposal, and at commit apart from activation. It therefore retains

matches the ordinary consumer's complete trace for all three principal parents.

The fixed adverse cases preserve distinctions that an unqualified recovery
claim would erase:

- A false full indication can make the old failed prediction appear supported,
  suppressing inquiry. A false fresh weak indication can produce the wrong
  diagnosis. True-map retention is conditional on faithful, correctly bound new
  contacts; the controller never consults hidden truth to repair corrupt input.
- Missing or misbound contact remains unknown. A real old context change cannot
  supply same-context full evidence. A source-denied old operation is ineligible
  before installation and is not recoded as an observed full failure.
- One unit of incurred waiting still allows the four-model diagnosis, but final
  retention fails. Two units allow acquisition of the fresh weak record but not
  the following filter. Three units prevent the weak challenge after its command.
  Each denied charge leaves the current state intact; subsequent retention is
  attempted and may also fail. No price was tuned after observing these results.

This closes the named particular interface through an actual further inquiry.
It leaves the untested full coordinate unresolved and supplies no second service
or universal calibration guarantee. The new contact recipe, stationary weak

this realization. The separate material-location dependency and its support
obstruction remain separate histories. Independent reconstruction and formal
receipts establish their own additional scopes when available.
