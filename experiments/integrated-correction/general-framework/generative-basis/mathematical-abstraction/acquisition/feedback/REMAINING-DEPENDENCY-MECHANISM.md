relay setting supports a separately specified later load, uses that acquired
relation to revise what must remain operative, and then performs a distinct
full-load transfer. The prior productive-use component remains fulfilled in
every valid handoff. The broader load undertaking, the test schedule, and the
construction/calibration contracts are supplied; the acquired object is the
relay-to-load relation, not the undertaking's ultimate value.

## Causal use and actual conservation

The dependent primary takes 16 snapshots. At the second record write it has
fuel `260833/32768` and acquired pair `[0,1]`. The charged read and commit select
setting 1, after which a new relay command and one-transfer hold occur. The
separate full-load operation debits the remaining 10-unit source packet and
deposits it in the load. The hold expires after that operation. Retention then
leaves fuel `214009/32768`; total new consumption is `142497/32768`.

There is no actor replenishment from this load. The exact whole-history account
is

`145 = 2033587/16384 inherited consumption + 142497/32768 new consumption + 214009/32768 actor fuel + 10 load stock`.

Each snapshot verifies this identity with whatever source, load stock, or waste
is present. Original H, the old table, and historical fulfillment are preserved.
Every cell formed, rewrite, temporary workspace, interface action and final
retention uses the inherited account tariff. The extra relay/reader recipe and
boundary load contract remain supplied; capacity accounting is not a claim
that their material origin has been derived.

The eight new cells are **bounded registers, not eight binary bits**. Their
staged encoding is part of the supplied recipe. Two hold outcome values and
two hold context tags. One relay register has three states: bypass,
engaged-unheld, engaged-held; `relay` and `held` are its projections. One
transient register holds an empty value or one bounded `(context,setting,bit)`
datum, moving from native `pending` to acquired `contact` after the charged
contact. They are never simultaneously occupied. One mode register encodes the
finite control phase together with the pending decision category: `mode` and
`read_mode` are projections. One route register stores no route, 0, or 1;
`candidate` is its retained content, while `selected` becomes active only at a
non-clamped commit, as specified by the mode. Thus a clamp can retain a candidate
without an active selection. `candidate` and `read_mode` really are retained
inputs to commit; they are not discarded as observer metadata. These finite
encodings explain the Python projections without claiming that every printed
field is a separate cell, or that the charged eight-register recipe is an
optimal bit-level implementation.

The matched clamped consumer receives the same contacts and records and incurs
the same read and commit charges. Only its consumer retains the old component
stop. It leaves source 10 and load 0, ending at `240229/32768`. The traces agree
through read and at commit apart from the selected setting and consumer mode.
An ordinary finite-state consumer using the same acquired pair exactly matches
the learned consumer's complete trace and final state for all four laws. This
locates the result in acquired information and its operative use, not a special
vocabulary or an advantage over equivalent conventional control.

## Failures preserved

Missing or misbound contacts leave an unknown slot. In particular, `[unknown,1]`
still contains a known productive setting, but the supplied complete-pair
identification policy withholds service. Complete dependency identification is
not thereby proved necessary for every useful response.

Undetected false bits can select bypass under an actually relay-dependent law,
or certify apparent dependence under `00`; the actual packet then becomes waste.
A changed full-load law or context likewise breaks the supplied correspondence.
The controller does not acquire a final load receipt here. It knows only that
its selected operation was issued; the saved failure is not a newly learned
diagnosis of calibration failure.

The source-exhaustion case uses the actual retained `later_ten` predecessor;
its zero remaining source is not manufactured at handoff. It acquires `[0,1]`
but cannot issue full transfer. Explicit incurred waits of 8, 9, or 10 units
produce record-write, contact, or installation failure respectively; the first
two also cannot retain the new interface. The capacity-40 fixture blocks the new
installation peak 42, but cannot be a joined history: the earlier acquisition
needed peak 114. It is preserved and explicitly excluded from the accepted
ancestral cases. The original unfulfilled
`blocked` predecessor is denied entry by its actual controller location.

The result is a positive inquiry continuation at this actual interface. It is
not a learner for the separate original-law common-support/source-location
dependency in the concurrent continuing-dependency study, and does not remove
that study's one-unit-support reader obstruction. Those histories and laws
remain distinct.
