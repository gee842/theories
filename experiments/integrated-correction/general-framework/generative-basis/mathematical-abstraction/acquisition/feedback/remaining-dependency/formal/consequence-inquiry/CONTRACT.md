# Continuing access to a completed operation's effect

6 October 2026. AI-authored verification contract for an additive proof about
the unchanged [relay law](../RemainingDependency.lean). This contract follows
source inspection and proof development; it records the acceptance boundary,
not a blind prospective prediction or a new transition law.

The question is whether any remaining operation in that law can make the
ordinary-success and calibration-failure endpoints distinguishable to their
controller. Both endpoints have source zero, identical weak responses and
contact configuration, identical inherited knowledge and identical controller
and actor accounts. They differ physically in delivered load versus waste and
in the full-load response law. Those actual differences must remain preserved.

The proof's representation retains every state coordinate except the full
table and the allocation of discharged stock between load and waste. It keeps
their sum. It is richer than the actual controller's accessible input, not a
claim that the controller reads all retained coordinates. Canonicalizing a
stock allocation is a mathematical projection; no physical transformation,
repayment or erasure of the operation's effects is performed.

Acceptance requires:

1. Source zero and the whole physical stock triple remain invariant under
   every admitted event attempt, including denials, repeated installation,
   waits, changed context, arbitrary contact index and repeated retention.
2. The representation commutes with every such step. Equal representations
   at source-zero states stay equal through every common finite request word.
3. Finite feedback policies using only this representation and their own
   identically initialized memory make identical requests and preserve equal
   representations. This excludes a selective success-versus-failure trigger
   from that evidence; it does not prohibit common inquiry or abstention.
4. Source-bound generated fixtures instantiate the result at the two exact
   saved endpoints, preserve distinct actual outcomes and original account,
   and retain the positive alternate-contact distinction for the different
   false-dependence case.
5. A fresh isolated Lean build compiles unchanged prerequisites and the new
   proof/fixtures. Audit every explicit new declaration, record actual logical
   dependencies, preserve failures and reject proof holes or custom axioms.

The unchanged law supplies no source renewal or load-effect contact. A new
interaction, a different law or newly available support can change the scope.
The existing [calibration extension](../../../CALIBRATION-REVISION-MECHANISM.md)
forms an actual full-effect contact under additional laws; its independent
runtime acceptance and separate formal status must be credited. The earlier
acquired-applicability learner also already selects inquiries from acquired
failure; no general inquiry-selection gap is inferred.

The result is about accessible distinctions inside a specific unfolding. It
does not refute the fuller generative account, identify all cognition with this
projection, or derive the worth of the original undertaking. Physical outcome,
acquired knowledge, diagnosis, remaining service feasibility and ethical
warrant retain their different consequences within the same history.
