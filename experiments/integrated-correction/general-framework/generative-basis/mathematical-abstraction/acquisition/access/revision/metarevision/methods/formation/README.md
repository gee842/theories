6 October 2026. Finite conditional construction extending the [method-switch study](../README.md). The selector edge starts absent. A supplied generic head forms a receipt-addressed connection to a proposed generator, tests the generator against an internally retained six-bit record, removes a contradicted connection and forms an untried alternative. A later read of the accepted edge regenerates the family and supplies the model for paid forward use.

The receipt addresses the row; it does not choose G0/G1. First proposals are uniform over the two supplied output ports. The six sensor bits drive rejection. The generator recipes, typed component functions, constructor, rejection rule, calibration and finite schedule remain supplied. This establishes endogenous connection formation at a declared boundary, not autonomous origin of every algorithm or law.

## Checks and preserved counterexamples

The producer checks35 fixtures,448 model/phase/fault/proposal configurations and24 state-renaming cases. All448 admitted configurations retain correct future reading models;128 actually reject and replace an edge. These are exhaustive declared configurations, not empirical probabilities. Independent reconstruction includes the saved fixtures and additional stage-boundary denials.

The record belongs to its acquisition epoch. The later paid action changes the current state, which receives its own cursor commit. Replaying the old record alone would recover the earlier cursor; indefinite replay after arbitrary future actions is not implemented.

Clamped formation, a mismatched source port and a broken accepted edge block traversal without fallback. Swapping the destination changes the generator invoked. A five-state process can fail at later use; a delayed ten-state process passes the six-bit archive and one further reading while leaving a wrong model. Missing source, short reserve, insufficient scratch and denied commits remain in the results.

Logical deallocation reuses backed writable cells under the declared storage law. It refunds no energy. External retired-edge records preserve discarded connections and their costs; they never provide learner evidence. Denied maintenance is not modeled physical bit erasure.
