## Separate information audit

Consider four logical histories consisting of two independent bits, x and y. A one-bit deterministic retained record is any map from these four histories to two memory states:16 encoders. A decoder for each possible future question (ask x or ask y) maps the retained bit to an answer bit:four possible decoders per question. Enumerate all256 encoder/decoder designs. Check whether any reconstructs both bits on every history, and record the attainable exact-answer counts for each question. Preserving x and preserving y give crossed advantages.

Uniform history counts and any future-demand weighting used to describe this comparison are analyst-defined mathematical criteria. They are not an empirical task distribution, native feedback, a resource receipt or a reward driving the controller. No choice among these designs is enacted in the physical continuation. The negative claim is failure of universally exact reconstruction for these incompatible demands within one-bit memory, not impossibility of useful competitive, minimax, average-case or domain-specific retention.

Also enumerate six-bit signatures of the previous14 current behavioral possibilities to confirm their conditional distinguishability. Explain separately why an unrestricted family can always add a delayed disagreement beyond finite tests; do not mistake a restricted-family certificate for a universal generator-selection theorem.

## Acceptance and scope

Independent checks must reconstruct the actual endpoints, resource carry, complete histories and installed-routine behavior without importing the evaluator's target into its predictor. Recalculate installation bills and every later-use prefix. Verify the256 logical designs independently of the controller experiment. Source hashes and original results remain preserved.

This closes a feasibility/claim boundary only. A positive metaselection mechanism still needs available alternative procedures, an accessible difference in their operational consequences, a paid route from those consequences into a changed selector, and sufficient support for the next operation. An analyst's correctness score cannot supply that missing route. If support is replenished or work is instead branched prospectively from an earlier checkpoint, that new intervention must be declared; it cannot silently repair the present exhausted trajectory.
