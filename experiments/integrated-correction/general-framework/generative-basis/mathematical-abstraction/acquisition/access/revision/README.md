## What remains open

The achieved mechanism is conservative reacquisition of current sensed behavior within a generated finite class. A selected H4 is not a retrospective explanation of when or why an earlier apparatus changed. The general proof gives the criterion for exact reinterpretation from retained evidence, and describes a possible lossless record, but this implementation does not retain that record.

Future work must explain how candidate generators and retention policies form and are selected by consequences under unknown future questions. It must also supply a paid archive if later reinterpretation is claimed. Nothing here establishes unrestricted self-calibration, a new general learning algorithm, human mathematical cognition or unique metaphysical grounding.
