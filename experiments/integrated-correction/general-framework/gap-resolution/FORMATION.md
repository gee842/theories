# Bounded frame formation, changing commitments, and inherited execution

5 October 2026. Restricted nonfinancial construction and exact calculations. This extends the [situated-correction distinction](../../../evaluator-succession/SITUATED-CORRECTION.md) and the [earlier finite candidate-discovery analysis](../../../../_process/research/2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/candidate-discovery-math.md). The new integration is a representation-dependent plural-feasibility result, an explicit commitment-revision procedure, and a finite lineage from experience to inherited execution. It is not a new general theorem of discovery, a causal finding about real institutions, or an explanation of unconceived categories.

## 1. Independent consequences and two kinds of representation

A fresh case has two independently fair binary attributes \(X=(X_1,X_2)\). Cases are independent. The physical environment has a fixed unknown pair \((j_*,\theta)\in\{1,2\}\times\{0,1\}\). An action \(a\in\{0,1\}\) produces binary harm

\[
H=(a\mathbin\oplus X_{j_*}\mathbin\oplus\theta)\mathbin\oplus\xi,
\qquad \xi\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\nu),\quad 0\le\nu<1/2.
\tag{1}
\]

Here \(\oplus\) is exclusive-or. Noise is part of the stipulated physical response, independently sampled, not dishonesty. Action 1 also imposes one unit of a separate physical burden. The declared consequence vector is

\[
\big((h_x)_{x\in\{0,1\}^2},B\big),\quad
h_x=\Pr(H=1\mid X=x),\quad B=\Pr(a=1).
\]

These quantities remain recorded under the same definitions when institutional standards change. They are population expectations in the stationary case distribution, **not pathwise quotas** for every realized batch.

The original operational frame \(F_0\) permits actions depending on retained history but not on the current case's attributes. Its ordinary archive retains \((a,H)\), dropping the attribute-to-outcome link. Updating estimated average harm, or updating a prior over supplied possibilities, does not enlarge this policy language.

A bounded generator can inspect prospectively collected linked records \((X,a,H)\) and construct the four expressions

\[
\gamma_{j,s}(X)=X_j\oplus s.
\]

Installing a selected expression creates frame \(F_1\): a retained category, an interpreter for the conditional rule, and access to its case attribute. It also permits a randomized action rate within either of the two represented classes. The raw attributes and expression grammar are supplied; the distinction is absent from the initial active frame but not unimaginable to the analyst. This is bounded representation refinement, more than a changed estimate within \(F_0\), less than unrestricted conceptual invention.

## 2. Experience, inquiry, admission, and commitments are separate events

The following is a finite event mechanism, not an externally arriving correct answer.

1. Under the installed blind practice, a participant who experiences harm can lodge a linked complaint. A standing rule admits that ticket with probability \(g\). It may do so even when the institution's accurate aggregate forecast predicted that some harm would occur. Over \(M\) independent ordinary cases, the probability of an admitted complaint is \(p_T=1-(1-g/2)^M\).
2. The admitted ticket activates a fixed prospective inquiry: \(n\) fresh independent cases, with randomized actions independent of their attributes and potential responses. It uses \(n\) case-handling slots that cannot simultaneously provide ordinary service, plus declared record and computation capacity. A ticket is not reused as if it were an unbiased confirmation sample. If the slots, timely access, or grammar are unavailable, this inquiry does not occur.
3. The generator computes the four empirical harms under the corresponding conditional actions using (1), equivalently the mismatch counts below, and nominates a least-mismatch expression. This calculation is valid for this known structural response family; it is not an observational causal estimator valid for arbitrary worlds. Ties are randomized uniformly. A separate authority gate admits the new expression and its semantic labels into the institutional frame. A further gate enables actual attribute-sensitive execution.
4. Participants can propose a revised commitment vector, separately from the empirical candidate. The initial public rule assesses only pooled harm and burden; a revised proposal adds an individual harm ceiling for each affected context. A concrete finite reflection rule is: after an admitted account of unaddressed harm, a participant switches its proposed ceiling from \(.30\) to \(.20\); after a separately recorded environmental warning, the burden steward switches its proposed burden ceiling from \(.50\) to \(.45\), or to \(.40\) for its declared severe-warning category. The trigger and response menu are explicit behavioral assumptions. Experience causes a changed normative proposal in this model; empirical truth does not imply that it is ethically required.
5. Under the declared ratification procedure, each represented participant can endorse or reject the proposed tuple, and the burden steward retains its separate veto. Endorsements refer to disclosed physical estimates and a declared error tolerance; institutional enforcement requires the relevant signatures and authority. A commitment is not adopted merely because a predictor favors it. An infeasible tuple remains a recorded disagreement requiring another proposal or a changed physical option, not a silently lowered standard.

This procedure models an institution acquiring new binding commitments, and a stipulated experience-dependent change in individual proposed standards. It does **not** derive the underlying response dispositions, rights of representation, consent procedure, or error tolerance from physical data. Suppressing a testimony, compelling a signature, or changing a participant's proposal through an override is a different transition called coercion in this construction; it must not be recoded as learning or consent. Genuine social legitimacy requires more than these formal signatures.

For the results below, the ratified plural requirement is

\[
h_x\le r\quad\text{for every }x,\qquad B\le b,
\qquad \nu\le r<1/2.
\tag{2}
\]

The common numerical ceiling simplifies calculation; each group constraint remains separate, and burden has no stipulated exchange rate with harm. No weighted total score selects which commitment should prevail.

## 3. Two distinct impossibilities and a constructive feasibility result

**Information obstruction.** Under \(F_0\), fresh fair attributes independent of the action and past make \(H\) a fair bit conditional on each chosen action and retained history, in every one of the four environments. Consequently the complete \((a,H)\) history has the same law in all four worlds under any adaptive blind policy. More such records cannot identify \((j_*,\theta)\). Any estimator has average exact-identification probability at most \(1/4\) under the uniform four-world prior; therefore its worst-world success is at most \(1/4\). Even if \(j_*\) is supplied, worst-world orientation success is at most \(1/2\). These statements rely on fair independent contexts; unequal or historically informative context frequencies can remove this particular equivalence.

**Representation obstruction.** Even an oracle revealing \((j_*,\theta)\) cannot satisfy (2) while execution remains blind. Let \(p\) be its probability of action 1. In contexts whose correct action is 0, expected harm is \(\nu+(1-2\nu)p\); in contexts whose correct action is 1, it is \(\nu+(1-2\nu)(1-p)\). The two values sum to 1, so they cannot both be at most \(r<1/2\). Belief correction alone cannot remove a missing executable distinction.

**Plural-feasibility proposition.** With the correct conditional distinction installed, (2) is feasible if and only if

\[
\boxed{b\ge b_{\min}(r,\nu):=
\frac{1-\nu-r}{2(1-2\nu)}}.
\tag{3}
\]

To prove necessity, every context whose correct action is 1 must receive that action with probability at least

\[
q_{\min}=\frac{1-\nu-r}{1-2\nu}.
\]

Those contexts have total probability \(1/2\), so their unavoidable burden is \(q_{\min}/2\). For sufficiency, use action 0 always where it is correct, and action 1 with probability \(q_{\min}\) where it is correct. The two class harms are \(\nu\) and \(r\), while burden is exactly \(b_{\min}\). This rule is expressible in \(F_1\). The proof is an elementary feasibility calculation, not a welfare theorem.

For \(\nu=.10,r=.20\), the boundary is \(b_{\min}=7/16=.4375\). A revised burden ceiling \(.45\) is jointly feasible; \(.40\) is not, even with perfect information, representation, authority, and consent. The fully corrective deterministic rule has consequences \((h_x=.10\ \forall x,B=.50)\); the minimum-burden admissible rule has harms \(.10\) or \(.20\) and burden \(.4375\). The latter trades an actual harm increase in some contexts for less actual burden, within the separately declared limits. Changing the ceilings can change the institutional verdict without changing these physical consequences.

This also makes the scope of formation precise: discovering a distinction can enlarge feasible responses, while a simultaneously revised commitment can shrink the acceptable set. Neither “more representation” nor “more demanding goals” alone entails improvement.

## 4. Exact discovery probability and question-relative compression

For a linked inquiry record define

\[
Y_i=H_i\oplus a_i=X_{i,j_*}\oplus\theta\oplus\xi_i.
\]

The generator compares the errors of \(X_1\), \(1-X_1\), \(X_2\), and \(1-X_2\) against \(Y\). By symmetry, take the true expression to be \(X_1\). Its mismatch count \(E\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,\nu)\); the other attribute's mismatch count \(F\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,1/2)\) is independent of \(E\). The four errors are \((E,n-E,F,n-F)\). Thus the exact correct-nomination probability is

\[
p_n=\sum_{e,f=0}^{n}
\Pr(E=e)\Pr(F=f)
\frac{\mathbf1\{e=\min(e,n-e,f,n-f)\}}
{\#\{v\in(e,n-e,f,n-f):v=\min(e,n-e,f,n-f)\}}.
\tag{4}
\]

This is a finite hypothesis comparison with specified noise and uniform tie handling, not a new discovery algorithm. It quantifies uncertainty about the actual frame selection, rather than silently inserting the correct predicate. An expression using the wrong attribute cannot satisfy every group ceiling below \(1/2\) in this example, even with arbitrary response probabilities within its represented classes: the missing true split leaves equally likely opposite needs within one represented class. A reversed correct split does retain the relevant information, but the fixed orientation-dependent deployment rule described here implements the reversed action and fails; later relearning could repair that different error.

The exact checks give \(p_{11}=0.9705066347482031\) for \(\nu=.10\). This is correct selection in the stipulated response family, not 97% confidence in that family's real-world adequacy.

For this **fixed supplied grammar**, keeping all raw records is unnecessary: \(n\), the two labeled mismatch counts, the labels' semantics, and the noise specification preserve the likelihood and nomination calculation. Keeping only \((a,H)\) aggregates does not. A correct selected program and its semantic/execution package can also suffice for subsequent deployment when no re-estimation is required. Compression is therefore not inherently destructive; sufficiency is relative to the future questions and interventions. The claim does not extend that sufficient statistic to a future grammar containing unrecorded attributes or relations.

## 5. Succession is part of the actual result

After nomination, distinguish probabilities \(a_A\) for admission, \(a_E\) for enabled execution, and \(a_R\) for ratification of the specified feasible commitment tuple. These describe actual gates, not existence of a benevolent planner. Each succession consumes a declared training/handoff slot and must transmit a **joint package**: feature semantics, interpreter or skill, case measurement access, orientation/response rule, commitment record, and operative authority. If the slot is unavailable, transmission fails in this finite version. If available, let the package survive each of \(L\) handoffs with probability \(\sigma\). A failed handoff reverts to blind execution and cannot be repaired within the stated deadline. No immortal truthful governor restores it.

In the simple event model where gate and handoff draws are independent of inquiry outcomes and each other, with enough scheduled slots and no earlier expiry, the probability of realizing and retaining the declared feasible revision by the deadline is exactly

\[
\boxed{p_T\,p_n\,a_Aa_Ea_R\,\sigma^L.}
\tag{5}
\]

No independence of the gates is needed for a lower-bound version if each factor is instead a uniform conditional lower probability given success so far. Without such conditions, multiply the actual conditional probabilities rather than marginal rates. The formula concerns the specified formation-and-deployment event; it is not a pathwise harm guarantee or probability of meeting every realized case outcome. Inquiry and handoff burdens must also be reported separately from the deployed steady consequence vector.

With \(b<b_{\min}\), realized satisfaction of the entire proposed tuple is zero regardless of these factors. With absent admission or execution authority, it is zero even when \(p_n\) is high. With \(\sigma<1\) and indefinitely many unrepairable handoffs, retention probability tends to zero; finite success does not prove an indefinitely renewing institution. The model closes one finite lineage explicitly. Sustained renewal of inquiry, training, authority and representation under arbitrary opposition remains a separate dynamics question, as formalized in the [actual-kernel closure study](../closure-study/FORMAL.md).

For a declared illustration with \(M=4,n=11,L=3,g=.5,a_A=.8,a_E=.75,a_R=1,\sigma=.9\), (5) is approximately **.2901853**, despite the .9705066 identification probability. Those gate rates are assumptions, not measured social quantities. The episode uses 11 inquiry slots and three training/handoff slots in addition to its ordinary case opportunities; no money variable is needed to record their opportunity cost.

### How to embed this in the existing succession kernel

An explicit coupled state can augment the earlier skill-and-installed-practice state with inquiry progress \(\ell\in\{0,\ldots,n\}\), the two labeled mismatch counts, an admitted expression or null, the finite commitment tuple and consent record, and a retained execution-package indicator. An inquiry work slot adds one fresh linked record and updates those counts; a training slot maintains the carrier/interpretation package and cannot also count as inquiry or ordinary service. At \(\ell=n\), the nomination transition uses the observed counts, and actual authority determines admission and enactment. Succession acts on the package and commitments as well as the incumbent practice. Failure of the package means that a nominally retained aspiration need not have an executable realization.

The [subsequent coupled calculation](formation-coupled/RESULTS.md) now implements such a finite join with shared inquiry/training slots, actual outcome-dependent turnover and record loss, using full reachable-state enumeration. Its [separately frozen reopening extension](formation-coupled/REOPENING-RESULTS.md) permits additional complaints to challenge an installed frame while retaining the old ordinary response during inquiry. The existing study's query, training and turnover transitions cannot simply be multiplied by (5): they can interrupt inquiry, change the available sample, and correlate the gates with outcomes. The coupled calculation therefore evaluates their joint kernel directly. Its endpoint concerns the retained ordinary-service response; experimental harms are separately charged and do not satisfy the ordinary harm caps.

To retain (4), chosen inquiry cases must remain fresh and unselected on their outcomes, and \((j_*,\theta,\nu)\) must be fixed during the batch, or a replacement nonstationary inference argument is needed. Both coupled versions maintain these restrictions. The calculations establish a finite implemented join, not unrestricted concept formation, legitimate consent, indefinite preservation or empirical validity of the supplied response family.

The transfer boundary is likewise explicit. Applying these results elsewhere requires evidence for the context distribution, physical response family, measurement and action access, complaint and authority transitions, and actual handoff behavior. Matching a diagram or a pooled performance correlation does not establish these hypotheses. Independent cross-domain validation must use the [mapped transition and intervention criterion](../closure-study/FORMAL.md), or justify a different approximation with its own error bounds.

## 6. What the calculation establishes and leaves open

`FORMATION-CHECKS.py` evaluates (4) exactly with rational arithmetic, checks (3) and its constructive policy at declared rational examples, enumerates the four environmental outcome tables, and checks the finite-lineage product. Its JSON is a mathematical implementation check, not real-world validation.

The substantive restricted join is: an experience route creates a new active distinction; linked evidence determines its uncertain orientation; plural commitments can change through an explicit procedure; authority and inherited skill determine whether a feasible response becomes actual. The construction distinguishes **belief updating**, **frame expansion**, **commitment revision**, **coercive override**, and **physical improvement** rather than identifying them by a single improving score.

Still supplied are the raw categories and response grammar, response model and stationary population, available inquiry technology and time, constitutional gates, reflection dispositions, constituency, and ratification procedure. The construction does not prove that these are legitimate or self-maintaining. It does not model strategic false testimony, a newly invented sensor/category, irreducible value transformation, decentralized bargaining, or external causal transfer. These omissions are explicit empirical and normative boundaries, not reasons to rename the model a complete theory.
