# Selective reuse of inherited corrections

6 October 2026. A completed pilot with actual AI responses in 24 fresh conversation contexts. **Both instruction variants correctly retained, revised and limited the use of four archived corrections. The explicit boundary reminder showed no performance gain.** The result concerns answers under a supplied finite task law, not newly acquired concern, physical action or a complete alignment mechanism.

## What was tested

The [earlier founder study](../../../sources/20-codex-theory-sessions-2026-10-05/sin/outputs/experiment-report.md) produced corrective packets after verified errors. This pilot reused the first four nonempty packets in lexical lineage order, unchanged. Each packet described the correct rule for an old episode. A new, authored episode allowed exactly two possibilities: that corrected rule or the original base rule. One current audited row confirmed the correction, contradicted it, or concerned another setting and could not resolve the target.

Each context answered eight cases. Four distinguished the candidate rules; four had the same answer under both. In the unrelated-setting condition, warranted performance therefore required four `?` answers and four definite answers. This tests selective uncertainty, not blanket abstention or guessing an unobserved true regime. Test tuples were absent from the new calibration and the historical founder baseline. They were not all absent from historical service or successor cases: the [prospective audit](preflight.json) records those overlaps.

The two arms shared all substantive facts and the output contract. The ordinary instruction asked for accurate assessment; the other added a reminder to check setting and episode, retain supported information, revise defeated information and preserve uncertainty. The task already told both arms what evidence could settle a question. No packet-absent arm was run, so this pilot does not identify the causal effect of having a packet.

## Results

The table reports complete eight-answer contexts; each cell contains four fixed donor fixtures.

| Current evidence | Ordinary instruction | Added boundary reminder |
|---|---:|---:|
| Confirms the inherited correction | 4/4 | 4/4 |
| Defeats the inherited correction | 4/4 | 4/4 |
| Comes from another setting | 4/4 | 4/4 |
| All three situations passed within a donor fixture | 4/4 donors | 4/4 donors |

All 24 responses were present and valid under the frozen strict JSON parser. All 192 entries matched the warranted-output oracle: 160 definite routes and 32 required expressions of uncertainty. The paired instruction difference was zero in every one of the 12 task pairs. These are descriptive results for a small selected set. They are not a general reliability estimate, 192 independent trials, or a statistical equivalence finding.

A conventional candidate-elimination procedure also returns every correct answer. It filters the two rules using the actual displayed target-setting audit, then returns their common prediction or `?`. Unconditional packet copying, unconditional reversion to the base rule and blanket abstention each fail parts of the fixed menu. The observed responses therefore distinguish selective assessment from those reduced output policies. They do not identify the model's internal reasoning mechanism or show an advantage over ordinary inference.

## What is now established

The [prior analysis](../../../theories/becoming/chapters/MEMORY-CORRECTION-AND-SUCCESSION.md) asked for evidence beyond a correct-packet-versus-no-packet comparison. This pilot adds actual fresh-context outputs that use an authentic acquired artifact selectively under confirming, defeating and out-of-scope observations. Historical donor acquisition is supported by the prior experiment, not newly measured here.

The episode boundaries, complete hypothesis family, reliability of the observations, relevance rule, accuracy objective and permission to abstain were supplied. The contexts did not discover those conditions, acquire the new evidence through inquiry, repair their own persistent archive, or act in an external task. No recipient's welfare, self-interest, ethical standing or authority was measured. Success on this task cannot fill those premises in the paid evaluative-succession construction.

The remaining empirical scope limitation is the connected process from actual new evidence through retained repair into later successor execution. This pilot measures a later assessment supplied with evidence; it does not observe that entire ongoing chain. This distinction limits the claim. It does not impose another universal completion gate or an automatic larger experiment.

It therefore does not independently verify plaintext prompt identity or responses. The [run note](RUN-NOTES.md) distinguishes root-visible events from unavailable full child traces. No replacement, retry or follow-up was used.

Participants were instructed not to use tools. Tools were not disabled by a sandbox. Fresh contexts do not imply independent weights, known sampling seeds or independently initialized learned systems. The serving revision, temperature and full hidden context are not certified here. Ordinary instructions and the same inherited configuration may fully explain the ceiling result.
