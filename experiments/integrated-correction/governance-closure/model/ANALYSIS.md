# Funding the means of observation and executable rule revision

This extension charges the observation, communication, computation and execution service used by the previous controller to the same receipts financing the operating system. Providers can retain that payment without delivering the service. Existing paid verification, with an explicitly increased workload, supplies the payment consequence. Providers can also deliver the purchased hardware while issuing false reports and commands. A preserved initial truthful majority makes arbitrary minority reports and commands harmless within the stated interface; the payment mechanism selects delivery, but does not select truthfulness among those who deliver.

The result is a conditional closure of one operating gap. It does not derive trustworthy instruments, authenticated membership or every source of legitimate authority from incentives alone. It proves that a specified physical and procedural interface can remain funded, maintained and usable despite a particular selected alternative, while accounting for its opportunity cost. The interface and threat boundaries are identified before the theorem.

## Resource accounts and practices

Retain the user task, capacities and contracts of the [constitutional extension](../../constitutional-extension/model/CONSTRUCTION.md). Write

\[
q(C)=\left(\frac{C}{1+C}\right)^2,\qquad
r(C)=1+.8q(C),\qquad F=\tau x r(C).
\]

The new governance allocation is a fraction \(\gamma\) of actual fees. Split the remainder as before:

\[
V_G=(1-\gamma)(1-\lambda)F,\qquad
W=\gamma F,\qquad
\text{useful-capacity allocation}=(1-\gamma)\lambda F.
\tag{1}
\]

The original contractor population has genuine-maintenance share \(h\), maintenance fraction \(m\), and verification stock \(G\). Its payment and maintenance equations now use the reduced budget \(V_G\). The checking technology is

\[
p(G)=\frac{G}{L+G},\qquad D=hp(G),\qquad L=1.1.
\tag{2}
\]

The previous normalization was \(L=1\). The increase explicitly represents the additional governance-completion checking workload. Its numerical size is a declared technology assumption, not a measurement. Governance checks are not added to a costless unlimited audit channel.

A third, separate carrier population supplies governance. It has three practices with shares \(a,b,c\), summing to one. A truthful maintainer, share \(a\), spends fraction \(d\in(0,1)\) of its assigned governance budget on actual service, retains the rest, reports its instrument readings, and issues the prescribed command. A lying maintainer, share \(b\), delivers the same actual service but may issue arbitrary reports and commands. A shirker, share \(c\), spends nothing on service, files a false completion claim, and may also issue arbitrary reports and commands. Existing paid checks discover false completion before settlement with probability \(D\); they do not magically detect every semantic lie from a provider who delivered the hardware. Thus actual private returns are

\[
U_a=U_b=(1-d)W,\qquad U_c=(1-D)W,
\qquad U_a-U_c=W(D-d).
\tag{3}
\]

Let \(z=a+b\) be the total maintaining share and \(\theta=a/z\) the truthful share among maintainers when \(z>0\). Under the three-practice replicator equation, \(z\) follows the two-practice maintenance equation below and

\[
\dot\theta=0,
\]

because \(U_a=U_b\). Truthful maintenance is not positively selected against lying maintenance. The theorem consequently needs an initially sufficient truthful composition and excludes unmodeled forces that change \(\theta\). This neutral hybrid is retained rather than disguised as an easily detected shirker.

The service stock \(J\) is a **joint functional package**: calibrated sensing of \(G,m\), a shared communication and aggregation service, computation, and an actuator able to implement a specified range of rule-change rates. Its conversion coefficient prices the whole package, including operation at its rated capacity. It is not one expenditure counted separately as several complete stocks. This continuous service model does not additionally claim to price an arbitrary number of discrete messages, unknown communication delays or unbounded computation.

Actual governance spending divides as

\[
dzW+(1-d)zW+(1-z)(1-D)W+(1-z)DW=W.
\tag{4}
\]

These terms are delivered service, legitimate retention, undetected diversion, and withheld reserve. Combining them with the reduced useful and verification budgets gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
F={}&(1-\gamma)\lambda F\\
&+mhV_G+(1-m)hV_G+(1-h)(1-D)V_G+(1-h)DV_G\\
&+dzW+(1-d)zW+(1-z)(1-D)W+(1-z)DW.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]

The two withheld terms have no productive credit. Their sum can accumulate in an inert reserve. User retention remains gross user output minus \(F\); the same fees are not also available to the old capacities at their previous rates. Separate populations make explicit which income enters each imitation process.

## A finite observation and authority interface

The interface admits arbitrary values in the reports and commands of the nonfollowing minority. It does not assume that a dishonest report is automatically rejected. Instead, it uses the following declared implementation properties.

1. Membership weights are authenticated, sum to one, and cannot be multiplied by a provider creating false identities. The measure of truthful protocol-following weight is \(a=\theta z\).
2. Each truthful instrument reports the actual observable pair \((G,m)\) with componentwise error at most
   \[
   e(J)=.002/J\quad(J>0).
   \tag{6}
   \]
   At \(J=0\), no informative observation is promised. This is a calibrated physical error envelope, not an inference that spending automatically creates truth. Hidden shares \(h,z\) are not observed by the controller.
3. A common pair of coordinatewise weighted-median reports is made available to the truthful providers. They apply the same deterministic command rule to that common pair and the public requested target. Their commands therefore agree.
4. The command executed by the plant is the weighted median of submitted commands, saturated by the actually available actuator rate
   \[
   R(J)=J/(1+J).
   \tag{7}
   \]
   A missing report or command may be represented by an arbitrary fixed value; the proof tolerates arbitrary minority values. If usable command delivery disappears, the interface can hold the last setting. The absence of a command is not an instantaneous reset to a safe rule.
5. The actuator channel cannot be bypassed within this threat class, and \(m\) has the physical contract range \([0,1]\). The prescribed range restriction has no effect inside the certified region below.

These assumptions distinguish service availability from the correctness of its physical mapping. Maintaining and operating the service is charged to \(J\); the bounded-error instrument law, authenticated identities, common aggregation semantics, actual execution through that channel and conditional settlement remain primitives. This is not a protocol for obtaining Byzantine consensus over an arbitrary unreliable network. A forged majority, a bypassable actuator, a sensor whose claimed calibration is false, or a changed settlement constitution is outside the theorem.

The practice repertoire is explicit. Maintaining but lying is admitted and can persist indefinitely. The selected share \(z\) concerns service delivery, not a moral property or agreement on legitimate purposes. Truthful reporting and prescribed command production remain bundled in practice \(a\), while practice \(b\) may violate either or both. Existing \(D\) must test real delivered work, rather than accepting a report of completion as delivery. New preferences for sabotage, side payments or mutation between truthful and lying maintenance are not hidden inside monetary imitation; they require a changed dynamic for \(\theta\).

**Median lemma.** If more than half of authenticated weight reports within \([s-e,s+e]\), every weighted median lies in that interval, regardless of all other report values. Otherwise at least half of the mass would lie beyond an endpoint although less than half can do so. If more than half submits the same command, that command is the unique weighted median. Thus arbitrary minority behavior is controlled even while a lying-maintainer minority persists; a long-run selection argument alone would not give this pathwise protection.

## Closed operating equations

Use state order \((x,h,z,C,G,J,m)\). The equations are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot x&=\kappa x(1-x)[.8q(C)D-\tau r(C)],\\
\dot h&=\nu h(1-h)V_G(D-m),\\
\dot z&=\nu_Zz(1-z)W(D-d),\\
\dot C&=\eta_C(1-\gamma)\lambda F-C,\\
\dot G&=\eta_GmhV_G-G,\\
\dot J&=\eta_JdzW-J,\\
\dot m&=\operatorname{sat}_{R(J)}(\operatorname{med}\{u_i\}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]

The physical range restriction at \(m=0,1\) is understood if an out-of-certificate adversarial trajectory reaches those endpoints. Inside the theorem the endpoints are never reached. The reserve can be recorded with \(\dot Q=(1-h)DV_G+(1-z)DW\), without feedback into (8).

Canonical coefficients are

\[
\tau=.15,\ \lambda=.5,\ \gamma=.02,\ d=.3,
\quad\eta_C=800/29,\ \eta_G=1600/29,\ \eta_J=2500,
\quad\kappa=1,\ \nu=10,\ \nu_Z=100,\quad\theta=.9.
\tag{9}
\]

All three depreciation rates are one. The magnitude of \(\eta_J\) specifies the price of the normalized service unit \(J\); its feasibility implications are shown below rather than assumed to hold for any real institution. Initial capacities and the initial majority are inherited states. Their creation and political authorization are not proved by a preservation theorem. Under the declared linear conversion, an initial stock \(J_0\) alone costs \(J_0/\eta_J\) units of delivered construction expenditure, before any additional startup costs.

## A controller using only the purchased observations

Let

\[
\bar x=\bar h=\bar z=.9,\quad
\bar C=2.5,\ \bar G=2.1,\ \bar J=1,
\quad m_\ell=.48,\quad\delta=.02.
\]

The proposed protected set is

\[
\mathcal S_G=\{x\ge\bar x,\ h\ge\bar h,\ z\ge\bar z,
C\ge\bar C,\ G\ge\bar G,\ J\ge\bar J,
m\ge m_\ell,\ b_0:=\bar h p(G)-m-\delta\ge0\}.
\tag{10}
\]

The physical share upper bounds are implicit. In particular \(b_0\ge0\) implies \(m\le\bar h-\delta=.88\). The controller need not continuously observe \(h,z,J,x,C\): their initial bounds and the model establish the bounds that will be maintained. This avoids pretending a sensor directly reads hidden honesty. It does not make an unobserved shock violating those bounds harmless.

Inside \(\mathcal S_G\), truthful reporting weight is at least \(\theta\bar z=.81>1/2\). The median observation \((y_G,y_m)\) therefore has error at most \(\epsilon=.002\), and available rate is at least \(R(1)=.5\). More generally the required majority condition is \(\theta\bar z>1/2\). Define

\[
\underline F=\tau\bar x r(\bar C),\qquad
\underline V_G=(1-\gamma)(1-\lambda)\underline F,
\qquad k=\eta_G\bar h\,\underline V_G.
\]

Actual verification production is at least \(km\). Hence a lower bound on the derivative of \(\bar h p(G)\) is \(\bar h L(km-G)/(L+G)^2\). With \(\alpha=4\), define

\[
f(G,m)=\bar h\frac{L}{(L+G)^2}(km-G)
+\alpha[\bar h p(G)-m-\delta].
\tag{11}
\]

Using the purchased median observations and certified side information, set

\[
G_- =\max\{\bar G,y_G-\epsilon\},\qquad
m_- =\max\{m_\ell,y_m-\epsilon\},\qquad
m_+ =\min\{1,y_m+\epsilon\}.
\]

The truthful providers' common command is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\ell(y)&=\max\{-.5,-\alpha(m_- -m_\ell)\},\\
v(y)&=\min\{.5,f(G_-,m_+)\},\\
u_H&=\min\{v(y),\max\{\ell(y),u_0(t,y_G,y_m)\}\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]

For example \(u_0=\varepsilon_c y_m(1-y_m)[T(t)-y_G]\) pursues a requested verification target. The safety result does not rely on that particular objective. The nominal rule is common to all truthful providers; it is not read from a dishonest provider's preferred command. Interval reversal is an explicit certification failure outside the proved set, never an instruction to silently exchange the endpoints.

## Robust feasibility calculation

On \(G\ge2.1\), \(.48\le m\le1\), the function (11) increases in \(G\) and decreases in \(m\). In fact

\[
\begin{aligned}
f_m&=\frac{\bar h Lk}{(L+G)^2}-\alpha<0,\\
f_G&=\frac{\bar h L}{(L+G)^3}
[(\alpha-1)L+(\alpha+1)G-2km]>0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]

With the canonical values, the second bracket is positive even at \(G=2.1,m=1\). Consequently

\[
f(G_-,m_+)\le f(G,m),\qquad
-\alpha(m_- -m_\ell)\ge-\alpha(m-m_\ell).
\tag{14}
\]

These inequalities make the estimated command bounds conservative for the actual state. They still need a proof of joint feasibility.

On the same domain,

\[
|f_m|\le\alpha=4,\qquad
0<f_G\le M_G:=\frac{\bar h L(\alpha+1)}{(L+\bar G)^2}
=.4833984375.
\]

The actual-to-estimated endpoint differences are at most \(2\epsilon\) in each coordinate. Thus the upper bound loses at most \(E_f=2\epsilon(\alpha+M_G)\), and the lower bound rises by at most \(E_\ell=2\alpha\epsilon\). At the actual state,

\[
\begin{aligned}
f(G,m)+\alpha(m-m_\ell)
&\ge-\bar h/4
+\alpha[\bar h p(G)-\delta-m_\ell]\\
&\ge-\bar h/4+\alpha\Delta,
\qquad \Delta=\bar h p(\bar G)-\delta-m_\ell.
\end{aligned}
\]

Here \(LG/(L+G)^2\le1/4\) bounds the fastest unsupported verification decline. Numerically,

\[
\Delta=.090625,\quad E_f=.01793359375,\quad E_\ell=.016,
\]

\[
\boxed{f(G_-,m_+)+\alpha(m_- -m_\ell)
\ge .10356640625>0.}
\tag{15}
\]

The estimated upper raw bound is at least \(-\bar h/4-E_f=-.24293359375>-.5\), and the estimated lower raw bound is at most zero. Therefore their intersection with \([-.5,.5]\) is always nonempty. This establishes feasibility despite measurement errors and the actual finite actuator limit.

## Funded preservation theorem

**Proposition.** With the canonical \(\theta=.9\), every initial state in \(\mathcal S_G\) remains in \(\mathcal S_G\) under (8) and (12), for arbitrary reports and commands from lying maintainers and shirkers within the authenticated interface, and arbitrary admissible bounded-error truthful measurements. The delivered controller obeys its actual capacity limit. Moreover

\[
x,h,z\longrightarrow1,\qquad
C\longrightarrow C_R\simeq2.9290487362,\qquad
J\longrightarrow J_R\simeq3.2503474496.
\tag{16}
\]

Verification remains at least 2.1, and rule revision remains available at rate at least .5. No general target-tracking or \(G,m\) convergence claim is made for changing requests.

**Proof.** Since \(\theta\) is invariant and \(\theta z\ge.81>1/2\), the median lemma bounds both purchased observations. Every truthful provider computes the same command; the command median therefore equals \(u_H\), regardless of the others' behavior. Because \(J\ge1\), actuator saturation does not alter that command.

The robust endpoint inequalities give

\[
\dot m\ge-\alpha(m-m_\ell),\qquad
\dot b_0\ge-\alpha b_0.
\]

Hence neither the maintenance floor nor the return reserve can be crossed. Since \(h\ge\bar h\), the actual margin satisfies \(D-m\ge\delta\), so \(h\) is nondecreasing. At the minimum corner, the numerical inward and selection margins are

| Condition | Strict margin |
|---|---:|
| User return advantage | 0.0298469388 |
| Useful-capacity derivative at its floor | 0.0696551724 |
| Verification-capacity derivative at its floor | 0.1201820690 |
| Governance return difference divided by \(W\): \(D-d\) | 0.2906250000 |
| Governance-capacity derivative at its floor | 1.5663775510 |

They use \(\underline F=.1901020408163\), \(\underline V_G=.09315\), \(\underline W=\gamma\underline F=.00380204081633\), and \(k=4.625379310345\). Every remaining lower face points inward. In particular, governance maintenance is selected at the \(z\) floor, and the same fee flow renews \(J\) at its floor. This closes the first-exit argument without assuming that availability survives independently of its funding and provider population. The truthful composition among maintainers survives through payoff neutrality, not a positive reward for truth.

All production inputs are bounded, the shares stay in their physical ranges, and \(m\le.88\) in the protected set, giving global bounded solutions. The strictly positive user, verification-contractor and governance-provider margins imply logistic lower comparisons for \(x,h,z\), respectively. Hence all three shares approach one. The useful-capacity equation has its unique positive attracting limiting root

\[
\eta_C(1-\gamma)\lambda\tau r(C_R)=C_R,
\]

by the same decreasing \(r(C)/C\) argument as in the baseline. Finally \(J\)'s linear equation has the limiting input \(\eta_Jd\gamma\tau r(C_R)\), proving (16). \(\square\)

The guarantee is for the deterministic population model and the declared continuous observation/actuation interface. It applies to every admissible error and minority-command realization for which an absolutely continuous solution exists; standard measurable-in-time, locally Lipschitz-in-state nominal rules and error inputs give the usual Carathéodory formulation. It is not a high-probability theorem for a finite stochastic population. In particular, neutral \(\theta\) need not be fixed in a finite stochastic population, and this mean-field result does not bound the probability of losing the truthful majority through random replacement.

## The cost is visible in both feasibility and targets

At the minimum fee base, governance renewal requires

\[
\eta_Jd\bar z\gamma\underline F\ge\bar J.
\tag{17}
\]

For the canonical price, this needs \(\gamma\ge.0077930856\). Keeping the same useful-capacity floor requires \(\gamma\le.0465646806\); the corresponding verification funding inequality alone allows \(\gamma\le.0730489950\). These are individual funding requirements for this certificate, not a complete universal feasibility interval: the audit workload, provider margin, observation envelope and robust control inequalities must also hold. They exhibit the actual competition for receipts. Arbitrarily expensive governance cannot be added while retaining all the old operating floors.

At full participation and delivered maintenance, the new relation is \(G=2C_Rm\), with \(C_R\) below the old value 3. Lying maintainers can still occupy weight \(1-\theta=.1\); the median tolerates them. The conservative observation controller also uses only a certified lower bound on the hidden original contractor share. Therefore an operating region that stays safe need not permit every requested verification target. For example, the error-free nominal target 3.6 has a strictly positive upper-command margin at its clean operating equilibrium, whereas target 4 has upper margin approximately \(-.0197705\) under (12), even before adding conservative measurement endpoints. Exact tracking of target 4 is rejected by this sufficient controller. That is a real cost and conservatism tradeoff, not evidence of operating failure.

The theorem does not establish global tracking even for target 3.6. A common conservative fixed rule may preserve operation without purchasing active governance. A resource-matched comparison must therefore report both what active revision enables and what its diverted budget reduces; merely charging for a governor does not demonstrate that it is preferable.

## Removing the consequence for false governance completion

Suppose governance false claims receive the full assigned payment even when discovered, while genuine providers still spend fraction \(d\). Their selected share then obeys

\[
\dot z=-\nu_ZdWz(1-z).
\tag{18}
\]

For \(z(0)<1\),

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\log(1-z)=\nu_ZdWz,
\qquad
\int_0^\infty zW\,dt\le
\frac{-\log(1-z(0))}{\nu_Zd}<\infty.
\tag{19}
\]

Consequently the input to \(J\) has finite integral, \(J\to0\), and \(\int_0^\infty J\,dt<\infty\). Actual rate capacity \(R(J)\to0\), the promised observation envelope becomes uninformative, and

\[
\int_0^\infty|\dot m|\,dt
\le\int_0^\infty R(J)\,dt
\le\int_0^\infty J\,dt<\infty.
\]

The executable rule therefore approaches a frozen limit. This proves loss of observation-and-revision capability even if some underlying productive service continues. It does **not** imply that every operating stock must collapse or that the frozen rule must be unsafe. A no-governance architecture can be viable for some purposes. If \(z(0)=0\), imitation cannot recreate the missing practice; if \(z(0)=1\), an absent alternative cannot invade without reintroduction. Those boundary facts are not evidence of general takeover resistance.

## Where closure stops

The result internalizes the operating cost, depreciation, selective maintenance and finite execution capacity of one governance service. It also handles arbitrary minority reports and commands even when the lying-maintainer minority persists. It retains a starting authenticated truthful majority and a payoff model preserving its composition, a correct bounded-error physical instrument technology, a functioning nonbypassable command channel, actual completion verification, and enforceable conditional settlement. These are named anchors, not additional hidden populations asserted to be perfectly honest.

A particularly informative negative control transfers mass from truthful maintainers to lying maintainers at fixed \(z,J\), without changing the ledger. If \(\theta z<1/2\), arbitrary reporters can control the median even though every observation and actuation resource is still funded. Thus resource sufficiency and maintenance selection do not imply truth selection. This is a distinct vulnerability from loss of the service stock.

No model can obtain a useful guarantee from payment alone when available evidence is identical across situations requiring incompatible actions, or when the physical command can be overwritten arbitrarily. The [independent boundary analysis](../review/BOUNDARIES.md) gives the precise observation obstruction and distinguishes it from the failure of a conservative controller. The [institutional audit](../review/INSTITUTIONAL-AUDIT.md) records further checks on payment, observation, execution and comparison design.

The remaining question is therefore narrower and testable: which anchors can be implemented, at what cost, under which attack and uncertainty classes, and with which acceptable operating constraints? This construction does not settle political legitimacy, endogenous goal formation or unrestricted constitutional capture. It supplies one resource- and incentive-consistent architecture whose previously unpriced feedback service is maintained within an explicit threat model.
