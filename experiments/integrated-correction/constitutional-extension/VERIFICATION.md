# Verification plan for paid verification and accountable custodians

5 October 2026. This extends the prior funded correction model. It is a mathematical construction and illustrative numerical audit, not an empirical experiment or novelty claim.

The extension represents two populations: service users choose paying or bypassing; maintenance custodians choose doing the paid work or submitting a false completion claim. The information and execution service has capacity C. A separate paid verification package has capacity G. An audit reaches an honest peer with probability h, the honest custodian share, and succeeds with probability p(G). Neither peer honesty nor verification capacity is held fixed.

## Checks before the implementation run

1. Account for gross task output, user retention, service upkeep, honest custodian work and earnings, successful diversion, and withheld funds. Withheld funds enter an inert reserve and cannot silently finance recovery.
2. Verify the four-state equations and Jacobian, the exact reference equilibrium and its two invasion margins, and a strict invariant region leading to that equilibrium.
3. Compare the derived joint design threshold against independently optimized costs. Check cases in which the custodian incentive constraint is active and inactive. Feasible co-design changes the fixed fee and budget split; it does not imply the population itself discovers the best design.
4. Demonstrate persistence with both payer and custodian alternatives initially present, a low-service depletion basin, and loss from insufficient honest custodians under the same parameters.
5. Disable payment withholding while preserving paid inspections. Unaccountable diversion should defeat maintenance even if paying users would initially resist bypassing.
6. Disable verification, charge its budget but do not deliver its work, or remove the useful signal. These controls should expose the separate necessary links.
7. Check that adding infrastructure alone cannot always recover an institution whose honest custodian share is too small. Do not label initial conditions or an imposed replacement of personnel as endogenous reform.
8. Compare a second numerical integration method and preserve log-odds for both populations so rounding near full participation cannot erase rare invaders.

An exploratory repair comparison was added after the first implementation run. From the same low honest share, compare a changed maintenance contract alone, increased initial capacities alone, and the combined intervention. The contract changes m from .5 to .2; the combined case is checked for entry into an explicit region satisfying the proved recovery inequalities. These are imposed design and stock changes, not an endogenous account of political reform or a cost-matched comparison. No installation budget or optimal transition is claimed.

## Boundaries

The mechanism relies on payments being deferred until an audit decision, restricted transfer of withheld funds, and peer assignment not chosen by the claimant. Detection denotes verifiable evidence under a specified noisy technology. Honest peers perform that protocol; corrupt peers may approve false claims. Bribing otherwise honest peers, changing the assignment law, forging the final audit decision, seizing the reserve, overriding escrow and arbitrary constitutional amendments are outside the threat family.

The same paid G package supports access verification and inspection of maintenance receipts. Its stated conversion pays for those functions jointly. The model does not charge one resource twice, assume unpaid higher auditors, introduce unlimited fines, or infer legitimate authority from physical exclusion.
