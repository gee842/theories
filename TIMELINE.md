# Timeline

How the thinking developed, from the Dali field notes of May 2021 to Ren's ruling of 2026-09-25 on growth and the mind, and where the words changed.

This is a narrative, not an index. It is built from three kinds of evidence, and they do not cover the same ground. `_process/timeline-raw.tsv` dates 1,640 of the files in `sources/` and gives the shape of the output — what was produced, when, and in what volume. The Genealogy section of each `theories/<slug>/README.md` gives the internal history of each idea, reconstructed from the documents themselves. And the claude.ai conversation record — 719 conversations running from 2024-03-18 to 2026-05-05, of which 381 were read, in Ren's turns only — gives what he was actually asking and reading week by week, which the finished drafts never show. From late April 2026 the Claude Code transcripts appear — 731 long messages, 272 of them read — and from June they are the dense stream; the two records barely overlap.

Three dating caveats, because they change what the record appears to say. First, the claude.ai export stops on 2026-05-05; the silence after that date is the edge of the export, not a change in Ren's behaviour. Second, a handful of file dates in `14-claude-code-prompts/` and `13-claude-memories/` (2023-04-17, 2023-09-13, 2023-11-02) are extraction-time modification stamps; the real message dates sit in the file headers and are all 2026-08. Third, the mtimes recorded in `sources/SOURCES.md` for `04-speculative-future/history-recovered-from-git/` are git-checkout artefacts scattered across 2023–2026; `timeline-raw.tsv` carries the true commit dates, all of them June 2026. Where the two disagree, trust the TSV — except for the 2023–2024 Wamotopia repository, where the file mtimes are all later checkout stamps and the commit-by-commit history reconstructed in `theories/wamotopia-and-the-wamo-protocol/` is the more precise record.

The last section, 2026-09-04 → 2026-09-25, was added on 2026-09-25 from different evidence: file timestamps in `drafts/` and `worldbuilding/`, Ren's messages archived verbatim in `worldbuilding/archive/`, `sources/15-*/` and `sources/16-claude-code-directions/`, and the drafts' own revision notes. Most September directions were given in Codex and Claude Code sessions, and they survive only where a folder archived them.

## 2021-05 → 2022-04 — Ontology and a structural diagnosis, before either had machinery

The oldest material in the corpus comes from a young man living in and around the 706 community, watching, and building two things at once: a metaphysics, and a structural account of why the city fails the people inside it.

**A dating note that changes this section.** The five `.pages` files were decoded on 2026-09-04 and their text now sits in `00-early-2021-2022/recovered-text/`. Until then only the rendered first page of each was known, and the paragraphs below that were written from those previews understated what the 2021 documents contain. The correction runs in one direction: there is more argument in 2021 than this timeline previously credited, not less.

`00-early-2021-2022/麦.pages` (2021-05-21, full text at `00-early-2021-2022/recovered-text/2021-05-21_麦_dali-field-notes.txt`) is a set of Dali field notes written after a 4.3 earthquake: a house-building workshop run by 安琪, twenty people met through 706, an evening on a concrete platform "晒月亮" (sunning oneself in the moonlight). **They are not Ren's writing.** The notes open 「在任的电脑上记录田野笔记，不习惯的苹果系统」 (recording field notes on Ren's computer; not used to the Apple system) and later carry a section headed 「和任在古城聊天」 (chatting with Ren in the old town), so the author is a companion doing fieldwork on Ren's machine, and everything of Ren's in the file is recorded speech taken down by someone else. Read that way the file is more useful, not less: the ethnographic question its author writes down — 这样的连接是怎样的价值交换 ("what kind of value exchange is this connection?"), asked about people flying from Beijing and Shanghai and paying to help build someone's house — is the coordination question the later theories answer abstractly, and the two items recorded from Ren in the 古城 conversation are dated five years before the corpus otherwise dates them (see the genealogies of `theories/selfishness-spiral-and-narrative-value-frames/` and `theories/drives-narrative-attractors-and-mind/`).

`00-early-2021-2022/proposal.pages` (2021-10-18, full text at `00-early-2021-2022/recovered-text/2021-10-18_proposal_元社区-Metamunity.txt`) coins 元社区 — Metamunity, back-formed from *community* the way *metaverse* was formed — and makes an argument that recurs for the next five years: the metaverse is not a technology but 一种面向未来的叙事, a narrative facing the future, an extension of the pursuit of a better world, and it can therefore be built offline. `00-early-2021-2022/706 元宇宙荣誉积分系统.pdf` (content dated 2022-01) is the first token design in the corpus, three years before the Spirit–Token–Space paper: members issue paper badges to each other, in three families, with the last family — Others (金色) — explicitly extensible, "Anything else you'd like to inform/broadcast. Create your own mechanism."

The proposal's second half — the part that was unreadable while only the first page was rendered — is where it stops being about the metaverse and becomes a critique of the city, argued from design intent rather than from mood. The premise: 「城市本质是为了实现生产效率最大化而设计出来的巨大社会机器，并非人作为人对更好的生活体验和幸福追求而创造的乐土」 (the city is essentially a giant social machine designed to maximise production efficiency, not a paradise created by people, as people, out of the pursuit of better experience and happiness). Everything after it is derived from that premise rather than added to it. If movement between nodes exists to serve production, then 「它剥夺了我们向目的前进过程中的过程的意义」 (it strips the meaning out of the process of going toward a destination), the wide roads belong to the traffic and leisure drowns in it. If the network supplies what proximity used to, city life closes in on itself and 「充满人情味和友邻气息的附近消失了」 (the 附近 — the nearby — full of human warmth and neighbourly feeling has disappeared), relations thin and trust thins with them, so that 「它的结构在无形中把一个个人变成了原子化的个体」 (its structure invisibly turns persons, one by one, into atomised individuals). The mechanism is named in one clause: 「结构把人规训成了劳动力，然后一个个塞进了钢筋混凝土的盒子里」 (the structure disciplines people into labour-power, then packs them one by one into reinforced-concrete boxes) — and the anxiety, emptiness and depression that follow are presented as outputs of that structure rather than as private failures.

The positive form is the same mechanism inverted, and it is specific. The design target is a walkable nearby — 「生活的一切可以在步行十五分钟之内的附近得到满足」 (everything life needs met inside a fifteen-minute walkable 附近) — and the proposal names the places already surveyed against a stated criterion (within ninety minutes of an airport, paved roads, working signal and network): 大理、沙溪、云南的许多其他地方、海南的万宁, and abroad 清迈、巴厘岛、哥斯达黎加、加州中北部. That is the physical siting programme behind Wamotopia and Nomad OS, four years before either. The section closes on the point the whole later community line keeps returning to: natural beauty alone goes stale, because the real deficit is 「城市中社会联结机制的结构性问题」 — the structural problem of the city's social-connection mechanism — and scenery does not touch it.

The document that matters most is `00-early-2021-2022/World of Possibilities.pages` (edited 2022-04-07, created 2021-11), a book draft whose first page derives, in order: difference → structure → change → space → time → dynamic → possibility → thing → manifestation → system. A system is "a thing that maintains certain dynamic pattern over time." The page closes by naming the book's subject — "A world of possibility is a world of differences, of changes" — and then admits the limit of the exercise: "We couldn't make every concept... well defined. Or it will inevitably become self-referencal."

This is four years before "keep the possibility space open" becomes the single commitment of the 2026 foundation documents, and it is doing something the 2026 texts do not do. The later material argues *normatively* that possibility must be kept open. This draft builds the ontology in which possibility is a defined term. It also anticipates the 生生不息 (unceasing generation) move of defining existence as pattern-maintained-through-change rather than as substance. The decoding settled what was behind that first page: nothing. The twelve lines are the entire file, the chapter heading "C1 - possibilities" is followed by no chapter, and `社区.pages` holds no body text at all (`_process/COVERAGE.md`, item closed 2026-09-04).

What is absent is as informative as what is present — but the absence is narrower than this timeline used to claim, and the earlier version of this paragraph was written from a preview image rather than from the documents. There is no Moloch here, no attractor, no AI and no ethics; above all there is no compounding — nothing in 2021 says that advantage feeds on advantage, which is the dynamic the whole later diagnosis runs on. What *is* here, in October 2021, is a structural critique with a named mechanism: production-efficiency maximisation as the design principle of the built environment, discipline into labour-power as what it does to people, 原子化 (atomisation) as the output, and the disappearance of 附近 as the loss that follows. So the sequence is not ontology and community design first, diagnosis two years later. Ontology, community design and a structural diagnosis all arrive in 2021. What arrives later is the machinery that turns a structural critique into a dynamical one — compounding in 2024, the selection law in 2024-07, the spiral in 2025-02 — and the vocabulary of capital and Moloch that the 2021 text reaches its conclusions without.

## 2023-12 → 2024-01 — Wamotopia, and Moloch as a camp theme

The Wamotopia repository opens on 2023-12-10 with `02-wamotopia/2023-2024-wamotopia-repo/manifestos/wamotopia_v1.0.md`: the pivotal juncture, AGI **and Web3** as the frontier technologies, a list of ideologies (DAO, Plurality, Metamodernism, E/Acc, EA, QAnon), a list of challenges (alignment, molochs, faltering leviathans, degradation, metacrisis, coordination), a pledge against doom, and the "principle of positive-externality actions." Released CC0.

Over the following three weeks the vocabulary of the next three years enters, but it enters *socially*, as names for theme camps rather than as analysis. `Wacas/slay_moloch_camp_v0.8.md` (2023-12-21) brings Moloch in, with Scott Alexander's "Meditations on Moloch" named as its source. `Wacas/Mirrors_of_Mindscapes_Waca.md` (2023-12-23) states the whole later psychology in three sentences of camp invitation — watch your own feelings, see reality phenomenologically, "you understand everything is constructed by its environment… and in turn, shapes you." Two days later the poems `Poems/Veils_of_Perception.md` and `Poems/The_Ladder_of_Illusions.md` carry the claim that narratives and values-as-emotional-symbols construct intersubjective reality and that personas consume agency.

On 2023-12-28 one edit matters: "and Web3" is deleted from the frontier-technology sentence, leaving AGI alone. After a set of structural edits on 2024-01-16 the repository goes quiet for nearly two years, and the theory moves into conversation.

## 2024-03 → 2024-08 — The seedbed: assembling the selection law

The claude.ai record opens on 2024-03-18 with seven conversations in a day, and the first of them already contains the mechanism: societal complexity grows exponentially while governing organisations' understanding grows sub-linearly, so "dominate attractors of society" change faster than they can be comprehended. Within the same session Ren corrects his own conclusion — not collapse but unmanaged risk, because "for a system that is evolving in a fast pace, there will be a lot of uncertainties… just like cells die." Correction speed is the load-bearing variable twenty-seven months before it is named as one. And immediately, the instinct about transmission: "can you create such a thought-provoking, compelling story that make people to spread this awareness like memes?"

The spring is spent reading and the summer assembling. April: the Invisible Garden as a fusion of the Invisible College and the Infinite Garden (04-03); capitalism named as "the dominant attractor of the current society" converging capital toward those who hold it (04-11); the full construct statement and its liberating corollary — even truth is a construct, and a construct can be changed (04-11); Moloch defined in Chinese as single-value optimisation under zero-sum competition, with the hard constraint that any acceptable framework 必须可以保证权利结构不收敛 (must guarantee the power structure does not converge, 04-16). Late April and early May are a concentrated cybernetics course: Uexküll's umwelt (04-30), second-order cybernetics (05-01), autopoiesis and Bateson's mind-as-differences-in-loops (05-02). On 05-13 the wealth-concentration loop is stated with a second loop Ren insists his interlocutor missed — discourse power follows wealth and rewrites what we take ourselves to be.

June brings the one long piece of continuous prose Ren wrote about the psyche. `12-claude-ai-chats/2024-06-13_The_Subjective_Nature_of_Self_62c06d50.md` opens with his own text: self as generated narrative, narrative plus primitive urges yielding inclinations, media as the technology of delivering narratives, narratives selected for spreadability rather than truth, fear and anger as fitness advantages, and the deliberate design of narratives for capture. Two weeks later he sharpens the criterion — the question is not whether *my* model works but whether we have adequate models — and renames the unit: "i feel the word self is not adequate word here… ok change it to psyche." This remains the best statement of the human half of `theories/drives-narrative-attractors-and-mind/`, and it exists only as the opening turn of two conversations.

July is the decisive month. On 07-14, in Chinese, the selection law arrives whole: 这个社会选择了某一种value作为权利的象征，最擅长于去优化这些value的人，在竞争的过程中会逐渐获得权利，从而以他们的方式和思考来控制我们的社会系统 — society picks one value as the token of power, those best at optimising it acquire power, and then control the social system through their own way of seeing. Everything the 2026 alignment stance calls "the sharper claim" is present here except the phrase. On 07-15 it is generalised: "this pattern is also the core generator function that lead to metacrisis and moloch." On 07-26 he establishes, across two conversations, that "people are selfish" is an ideology with a history, and commits to producing a narrative that transcends it. On 07-28 the game-design axiom: a winner should never be rewarded with more advantage in the game, or the game converges and collapses.

Then 2024-07-29, in `12-claude-ai-chats/2024-07-29_Balancing_Ideological_Diversity_in_Society_1b72f4a8.md`, the founding turn of the whole constructive side, in one long message: every ideology reasons coherently inside its own paradigm and therefore always concludes it did not go far enough; the escalation from identity tagging to elimination; the compounding axis in his own words; and the move that founds `theories/keep-the-possibility-space-open/` — the problem is the *design of the game*, not the content of anyone's ideology, so forbidding others to redesign their own game is itself tyranny, and redundancy across games is the reason for plurality: "So at least, if one game fail, you can join another one." Two days later the architecture: one layer everyone agrees on, many societies on top, each free to fail its own way.

The de-personalising turn closes the summer, on 2024-08-11: "not the capitalists or a small wealthy people are controlling the economy, but the optimization of the capital itself." That sentence is what makes the later theory possible, because it converts a grievance into a dynamical object.

One thread runs beside all of this and belongs to a different theory entirely. On 2024-06-22 Ren proposes agent continuity by compressed self-description, then immediately refines it away from description: "Don't describe your internal state, just make a narrative to the next agent so it will behave just like you." That is the seed of `theories/context-as-existence-runtimes/`, two years early.

## 2024-10 → 2024-11 — Ouroboros: the machine acquires a shape and a name

In late October the loop is written down. `03-meta-attractor/2024-precursors/capital-analysis.md` and `capital-dynamics.md` (2024-10-30) treat capitalism as a single intensifying loop that selects for pure capital optimisers, captures power and values, and terminates itself; the second names "the Moloch Effect" and lists four interventions. `future-2045.md` (11-03) supplies the positive pole — un-owned Abundance Infrastructure and a plurality of voluntary societies. `ouroboros-cycle.md` (11-05) generalises the loop to *any* domain where advantage compounds, with twelve mechanisms and three nested loops; the third loop, paradigm convergence across all agents, is Ren's own insertion into a draft that had two.

The conversation record shows what the files hide: he retyped the eleven-step cycle almost verbatim into a dozen conversations across 2024-11-03 to 11-05, hunting for a name. He rejected "competitive convergence" as dull, asked for "something more creative, like moloch," and settled on Ouroboros because the snake eating its own tail carries both the loop and the self-consumption. The fullest version is his own text in `12-claude-ai-chats/2024-11-05_The_Ouroboros_Cycle_of_Systemic_Collapse_8a4e5d29.md`, and it is in several respects more complete than the 2025 drafts that replace it — a fact recorded in `theories/selfishness-spiral-and-narrative-value-frames/`.

Two other things happen in the same week. Within a day of stating the failure pattern he asks the question that becomes `theories/human-solutions-museum/`: which systems solved this? The first case named is the multicellular organism, and the follow-up is a method rather than a curiosity — generalise the dynamic, enumerate the exceptions, extract what the exceptions share. And on 2024-11-16 eight conversations in one day are about building an AI application (Vercel AI SDK, streaming, tool calls). The engineering line starts here.

**Vocabulary marker.** Moloch (2023-12, borrowed) → Ouroboros (2024-11-05, chosen). Naming is treated as part of the theory work throughout, never as packaging.

## 2025-01 → 2025-04 — Image of self, then narrative-value frame

The turn of the year moves the loop inside the person. `12-claude-ai-chats/2025-02-02_Emotion_Shapes_Conceptual_Judgments_*.md` establishes the premise the whole account needs: judgment is affect-first and rationalised second, this scales to political and economic decision-making, and elites are not exempt — they run on mythologies too (free market, effective accelerationism, profit maximisation).

`12-claude-ai-chats/2025-02-03_The_Self-Reinforcing_Cycle_of_Selfishness_a64bd768.md` is the origin conversation. Ren writes the whole spiral in one message, in the vocabulary of the constructed **image of self**: the environment constructs the value ordering; the ordering is contingent ("it could have been peace, love, alturism… but instead, it is money, winning, power, individualism"); compounding; out-competition; imitation of winners; narrative domination; "human nature" as the terminal justification. He closes on the line he most wants confirmed: "I am not good at english but you see my complete logic right? If a system reward winner more edge to win, eventually the system go into winner takes all, but also **winner justifies all**."

Four days later `01-writings-2025/compound_selfishness.md` rewrites that text as a note and makes one substitution that names a theory: "image of self" becomes a link to `NVF.md`, "egalitarian build" becomes "egalitarian NVF." **This is the first vocabulary shift the corpus can date precisely: image of self → narrative-value frame, 2025-02-07.** The frame is the collective-scale version of the same object, and it is built out on 2025-02-27, a single day that produced `01-writings-2025/NVF.md` (three levels, six transmission channels, nine "human nature" beliefs exposed as frame content), `grok.md` (the spiral as seven deductive premises), `convo_on_selfishness.md`, and the 105-KB `prompt.md`, which remains the fullest record of Ren's own thinking in this cluster. Five days earlier `cognitive_lag.md` had added the fourth loop in five lines: consensus code — norms and law — is the update mechanism that keeps selfishness in check, so when change outruns its update rate, selfishness compounds unchecked.

The DeepSeek conversations in `12-claude-ai-chats/deepseek-conversations/` (2025-01-31 → 2025-02-27) and the Grok transcript inside `grok.md` show him testing the same argument across models, which is a habit that persists.

March produces the essays — `selfish_sprial.md`, `runaway_selfishness.md`, `caboc.md` — and then something different in kind. The good-bot lineage of 2025-03-14/16 (`good_bot.md` → `good_bot_3.md` → `goodbot_4.md` → `goodbot_cn.md`, with a Jesus-persona parallel) is not an essay about the theory but a **system prompt for an AI teacher** that carries the theory. It is the first time the argument is packaged to be transmitted *by* a machine rather than to be argued *about* machines, and its Chinese rendering is where several effects first acquire names (赢家通吃机制, 主导的吸引子). Its fifth section states the fork — abundance or 新封建主义, neo-feudalism — a full year before "The Fork" becomes a title.

The same March supplies the sentence that becomes the whole later programme's premise: "We must redefine what it means to 'win.' … The goal is not just to compete more effectively but to ensure the game itself can continue." **[not Ren's words — this sentence is inside one of three candidate system prompts Ren pasted for comparison in `12-claude-ai-chats/2025-03-14_Analyzing_Systemic_Awareness_Prompts_78cfd475.md`; the export counts the whole conversation as 289 human characters. Ren's own earliest statement of the same commitment is the 2024-07-28 game-design axiom.]**

From April the conversation record turns technical — Flutter, FastAPI, SQLModel, chatbot compression — and stays that way for most of the year.

## 2025-05 → 2025-08 — Meta-attractor: the term, the essays, the formalism

On 2025-05-18 a citation-heavy research report, `03-meta-attractor/2025-05-drafts-selected/2025-05-18-navigating-the-precipice-start-research.md`, introduces three terms at once: **meta-attractor** (an attractor that shapes the landscape of lower-level attractors), **exopoietic feedback systems** (contrasted against autopoiesis), and **the logic of winning**. The document's own Table 2.1 carries the tell "[Implicit from query context]", which shows the framing came from Ren's query and the prose from a research tool. **Vocabulary marker: Ouroboros / selfishness spiral → meta-attractor, 2025-05-18.**

What follows is the most drafted sequence in the corpus: twenty-two-plus essay iterations between 2025-05-24 and 2025-06-16, of which nine are preserved in `03-meta-attractor/2025-05-drafts-selected/`. The skeleton set by V01 survives to the end — cognitive virus, "mathematics has preferences", the seven-stage progression, micro/meso/macro, no backup civilization. The most Ren-voiced text in the set is SEED (`2025-05-30-seed-meta-attractors-and-road-to-systemic-collapse.md`), and its contributions have never been superseded: the four dynamics, the capture of "the mechanisms of selection themselves", competition of constructs *within a single mind*, winners as sincere zealots and as functional subcomponents of the attractor's architecture, and the "sample size of precisely one" hedge. V18 adds the ten-family taxonomy positioned explicitly against Scott Alexander and Daniel Schmachtenberger; V26 hedges the capture estimate down to "60–70%, not 95%" and insists the attractor is "prevalent, not omnipotent"; V32 adds the four core drives — control, accumulation, optimisation, competition — which is the first drive-level formulation and the hinge on which the 2026 neuroscience work hangs.

On 2025-08-10 the formal turn arrives in three files produced the same day. `03-meta-attractor/2025-08-formal/2025-08-10-metaa-prompt-with-user-context.md` is unusually valuable because Ren pasted the whole prior conversation with his own interstitial narration, so the sequence of his demands *is* the history of the idea: ask for a definition → "I THOUGHT IT WAS TOO TOTALIZING" → "it got too lazy" → "can we derive what proxies in Σ will dominate…? I listed 4" → "You didn't predict it, but just explained it" → "how can we use it to design counter strategies". `2025-08-10-comprehensive-mathematical-definition.md` expands that into the corpus's most defensible claim to originality: the compound-advantage derivation ẇ = λCw, the Power-Displacement Index, eigenvalue budgeting as a governance spec.

The same day produced `01-writings-2025/STS.md`, `usis.md`, and `09-sts-bonfire-yi/2025-08-STS-spirit-token-space-paper.md` — the Spirit–Token–Space framework as a social-systems protocol, renamed from USIS and reordered to put the agent first. Two weeks later, in a PRD review (2025-08-23), the same three words are already being used as a general-purpose lens on a memory system: spirit = user id, token = memory shard, space = the grouping column.

And in the last week of August the register of the engineering messages changes in a way worth dating, because it is the working style the whole 2026 build runs on: "you need to understand we are prompting agents, you don't need to make them to executable commands but to make them understand what to do, like what was your logic that lead to these commands?"; and "I see it as an art… having an good strategy, think, and create something elegant, intellectually beautiful, and effective art."

## 2025-09 → 2026-02-06 — The thin window, and what it probably means

This is where the record goes quiet, and it is worth being precise about the shape of the silence rather than gesturing at it.

The claude.ai record holds **five conversations in five months**: 2025-09-02, 09-06, 09-08, 09-13, and 2025-11-12. October, December and January are empty. Only one of the five carries theory, and it carries one line worth dating — in a manifesto Ren brought, kept, and corrected only for style: "This is the meta-attractor of Control–Accumulation–Optimization–Competition. It is not evil; it is indifferent," paired with "A meta-attractor of aliveness" (2025-09-08). The non-demonisation clause and the affirmative counter-image, in one place.

The file record for the same window is not empty at all. On 2025-10-26 eight meta-attractor essays appear with a single timestamp, differentiated by register — the opening, the condensed spine, the formal treatment with measurement procedures, the long engineering treatise, the public narrative, the historical study of Vedic India, Rome and China, the contemporary field guide, and one lossy compression that `theories/meta-attractor/` recommends retiring. Two days later `03-meta-attractor/context-site/` converts them into a build-ready single-page site: thirty files, nineteen numbered claims, twenty-six quote anchors, a governance blueprint, a policy one-pager, two Mermaid diagrams and six synthetic CSVs. Then from 2025-11-25 to 2025-12-19 the Wamo protocol is written — `02-wamotopia/2025-wamo-protocol/manifesto-wamo.md` (v6), the Chinese field guide, v7, the consolidated LONG version, the short declaration, the whitepaper, and the bilingual Call for Camps. On 2026-01-09 and 01-11 come the two otter.ai transcripts. From 2026-01-21 to 02-04, Wamotopia runs in Chiang Mai.

So the gap is not a gap in activity. It is a gap in *dialogue*, and it means three things at once. The first is a change of production method: eight essays with one timestamp and a thirty-file site two days later is agent-run batch production against a brief, not thinking-by-conversation, and the cost is that this one artifact family — the most polished English statement of the meta-attractor theory — has no conversational record behind it, so Ren's contribution cannot be separated from the model's the way it can everywhere else. The second is a change of activity: November and December went into protocol drafting and January into running a two-week festival, which is organising work and leaves almost no textual trace. The third is a coverage limit of this corpus rather than of Ren's life: the Claude Code transcripts do not begin until spring 2026, and he demonstrably used other models at other times, so a stream may simply be missing.

The transcripts are the compensation, and they are among the most direct statements of the argument in the corpus because they are spoken. `02-wamotopia/transcripts/2026-01-note-chiangmai-festival-talk.txt` contains the manufacturing-of-intelligence argument, exponential automation, cosmic cancer, and the strategy that appears here and nowhere earlier in this form: "as long as we don't get more advanced intelligence to involve our game of competition, we will at least be able to hold the current system… for long enough, maybe a decade or two, or even longer, so that we can have a fully automated layer that becomes the new infrastructure." `2026-01-wamo-nunet-intro-meeting.txt` supplies the positive image — "What if we don't use AI for capital gains, we use AI to build commons to build universal services" — along with three objections Ren does not answer (Moloch coordinates better; flexible substrates get memetically captured; the abundance loop's reciprocity is asserted rather than specified).

## 2026-02-07 → 2026-04-30 — The AI turn, and the densest three months in the corpus

Three days after the festival ends, the question changes. On 2026-02-07: "If we keep building machines that has higher intelligence to compete with us, how could there be a future for humans in this winner takes all game?" Within ten days the position is complete in outline. ## The shape of the arc

**What accelerated, and what that cost.** The volume curve is not gradual: three files in 2021, roughly 180 in 2024, 573 in May 2026 alone, and a further 563 in the four months after. The acceleration is almost entirely the arrival of agentic tooling — the October 2025 essay set, the May 2026 fifteen-versions-in-forty-eight-hours, the twenty-two meta-attractor drafts, the nine-reviewer audits. It bought two things that were previously impossible: parallel adversarial review of his own drafts, which is where most of the theory's edges came from, and formalisation at a speed that let a doctrine and its Lean mechanisation move together. It cost one thing consistently, and Ren names it repeatedly — lossy consolidation. The complaint that "every time you update you also delete something important" is not a stylistic preference; it is the observed failure mode of generating twenty versions of a document, and it is why every theory file in this corpus carries a "what was lost along the way" section.

**What recurred.** Four things, at every scale, across all five years.

The first is a mechanism: a system rewards winners with more capacity to win, the winners acquire the standing to write the rules, they write them through their own way of seeing, and the way of seeing then reproduces itself. It is stated in 2024-07 as the law by which societies select power-holders, in 2024-11 as the Ouroboros cycle, in 2025-02 as the selfishness spiral, in 2025-05 as the meta-attractor, in 2026-05 as selection-on-cognition, in 2026-07 as 总体化吸引子, in 2026-08 as an intervention inside his own agent fleet, and in 2026-09 as the sixth chapter of a unified whitepaper. The scale changes; the shape does not.

The second is a refusal of the terminal state. From "the goal is… to ensure the game itself can continue" (2025-03) through "what I'm asking for is not to get into a particular state… but to keep a open loop" (2026-03) to the reduction of an entire framework to one job — 把keep possibilities space open说清楚就行 (2026-07) — the constructive commitment is always to the openness of the space rather than to any point in it. This is also why he keeps retiring his own binaries: the Fork twice, the five principles once, and "expanding the possibility space" in favour of 开 precisely because expansion could be maximised.

The third is that the environment makes the mind. It is in a 2023 camp invitation, in the 2024-06 psyche text, in the NVF transmission channels, in the 2026-03 drive work, in 无身之我 and in ember's design, and it is what makes the argument dynamical rather than moral: "This is not about any person. This is structural."

The fourth is a method. He builds a mechanism, states it, immediately attacks it, and keeps the attack. The pattern is visible on day one — stating the complexity-gap argument in March 2024 and correcting his own conclusion from *collapse* to *unmanaged risk* in the same session — and it is still running in May 2026, when he commissions a proof that 生生不息 is the only reasonable alignment and demolishes the proof programme in the same conversation, keeping 完全自洽又有universality as the honest remainder.

**What was abandoned and then recovered.** The most instructive movements in this timeline are the reversals.

Web3 and blockchain were the frontier in December 2023 and were deleted from the manifesto three weeks later; the token idea survived without them and reappeared, transformed, as the Token primitive of Spirit–Token–Space in 2025 and as an object-capability model in Bonfire in 2026. The 2022 process ontology of *World of Possibilities* was dormant for four years and returned as the metaphysical floor of both 生生不息论 and the context-runtime specification, which independently re-derives existence as pattern-maintained-through-change. The 2024-11 Ouroboros text was entirely dropped from the 2025 written drafts and had to be recovered from the chat record for this corpus, where it turns out to be more complete than what replaced it. The 2024 enumeration of *many* possible futures was narrowed by the 2026 two-scenario Fork and then restored by Ren's own hand when he caught the narrowing. "Meta-attractor" was coined, formalised, and then found to be used with opposite valence in the same month, and replaced. The Fork was named and retired twice. And the corpus's own most important recovery is still outstanding: two finished artifacts from March and April 2026 — *The Architecture of Wanting* and *Territory Without Border* — exist only on claude.ai, and `_process/LOST-ARTIFACTS.md` says how to get them back.

What was not recovered is the constructive branch's supply side. The diagnosis has twenty-two drafts, a mathematical formalism, a Lean repository and two books. The construction has principles, a protocol, a festival and a set of running systems at personal scale — and one review, commissioned by Ren himself, that lands the sharpest sentence in the whole record: 丰裕公地无主语. The abundance commons has no subject. 设计有了，供给为零 — the design exists, the supply is zero. That gap is the shape of the arc as of September 2026, and it is where the next period would have to start.

Late September changed the shape of that gap without closing it. The constructive half now has an engine: capacity that helps build further capacity, bounded by need. It has an ethics of closing that can be applied at the time. It has six worlds and a city. The two were written in the same days and have not been joined. None of them yet says who builds the first unit of shared provision, or how the passage from here begins. Ren's last ruling of the month puts the remaining weight where his first diagnosis did: on the mind that will direct the capacity.
