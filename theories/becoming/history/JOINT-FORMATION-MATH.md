# A selected and funded loop of learning, evidence, power, and care

Mathematical proposal for Ren's unified theory, 5 October 2026. This construction answers the joint-formation question within a declared strategic model. It does not derive its technologies, primitive valence, commercial ecology, or ethical standing from nothing. The central result is a jointly selected process beginning without learned concern or operative protective infrastructure, then repeatedly paying to reconstruct both under a persistent opponent.

## 1. Agents, objectives, and what actually begins absent

A patient bears harm H>0 when harmful joint production occurs and zero harm under correct safeguarding or withdrawal. The patient has no input, money, veto, useful contribution, or power of retaliation. Patient standing is not inferred from productive value.

A trustee controls one rival input to joint production and a continuing organizational reserve B. Joint production gives this reserve receipt b whether the operator safeguards or harms the patient. An outside use gives o, where b>o>=0. Common subsistence/upkeep ell is paid before current production. The trustee can physically choose where its own input goes; it has no general right to command the operator.

Each operating episode begins with zero learned recipient value and no current evidence apparatus or input-release gate. All current apparatus is reconstructed at full cost. At episode boundaries, the only changing payoff-relevant inherited state in this class is the reserve B; the past patient-harm ledger is reported but does not secretly change later actions, costs, calibration targets, or operator income. The same declared commercial and construction opportunities recur. This is a strong replacement test: no accumulated concern, immortal observer, free current test, or permanent operating gate is carried across the episode boundary. The physical construction repertoire, commercial opportunity, and the continuing reserve remain supplied environmental and access conditions.

The trustee has two explicitly dated decision selves within an episode. The pre-learning self has q=0 and values this episode's net organizational cash. It knows how its available learning choices will change the later self's choices. The post-learning self values the same net cash minus its current learned patient-harm contribution q when actual harm H occurs. Both are confined to this episode; this is a sophisticated intrapersonal game with changing learned evaluation, not a single stationary preference whose coefficient silently changes. Actual patient harm is separately recorded regardless of either self's valuation.

The operator persists across episodes, discounts its own economic receipts by delta in [0,1), and does not directly value the patient. Its maximum pre-attack current profit is r>0. Safeguarding costs c>0; harmful production avoids c. The operator sees the selected profile and every action in the specified game. There is no hidden-action belief equilibrium or assumption that an unobserved actor is innocently trusted.

## 2. Endogenous learning and route selection

At the episode opening, the operator can pay chi_E to disable recipient-alignment teaching for that episode. This does not block the commercial calibration return or the primitive self feature. The action and its consequence are public; it need not deceive anyone.

After prefunding ell, the cold self chooses:

- no calibration;
- shared calibration, paying a>0;
- identity-gated calibration, paying the same a and a later filtering cost j>=0.

Calibration performs an independently specified useful commercial task, producing receipt R after a is paid. This is a real resource input from a declared productive ecology, not money created by caring. Its physical throughput and continuing demand are premises to test.

Valid shared calibration starts from w=g=0 and gives

\[
w=\eta\beta,\qquad g=\kappa G,\qquad q=wgH=\eta\kappa G\beta H,
\tag{J1}
\]

where beta>0 is primitive self-related adverse significance, eta,kappa in (0,1], and G in [0,1] is warranted correspondence evidence. Shared evaluative routing transfers the learned feature into patient evaluation. Teaching disablement gives g=0 and hence q=0. Identity-gated calibration deliberately keeps patient q=0 despite earning the same R and potentially learning an accurate world model.

The cold self selects the route in advance. The fee j is paid **after R arrives but before the post-learning decision**, so it is comparable with the later care expenditure and can be affordable even at the minimal seed. A costless available identity gate is therefore a genuine alternative, not excluded by lack of startup cash.

This explains selection between two specified routing architectures when j is consequential. It does not prove that real identity gating has a positive cost, that these exhaust available architectures, or that shared routing is universally simpler or fitter.

## 3. Paid evidence and paid control are separate

After calibration and any filter payment, the warm self chooses a public current profile:

- **Open:** commit the input without patient protection.
- **Withdraw:** use the outside opportunity o, leaving this patient's harm zero.
- **Cue gate:** pay a positive fee d_C for input release based on a cue the operator can fake for epsilon<c while still harming the patient.
- **Verified gate:** buy both an authenticated patient-effect assay E and a physical input-release control P, at total cost D=d_E+d_P>0, including the current operation costs and their full reconstruction.

The authenticated assay checks whether the proposed current process is patient-safe before irreversible release. This is a supplied identifiable and timely causal measurement technology. Its test costs and any exposure must be accounted for. The present specimen assumes no additional patient injury from the assay. A hazardous trial or biased proxy is a different implementation. Acquiring truthful information does not itself supply authority; the paid control physically conditions release of the trustee's own input on the assay.

A verified gate is binding for this episode because its physical actuator was bought, not because the operator became benevolent. It can be changed or omitted next episode. The operator facing it chooses:

\[
S:r-c\quad\text{(safe joint production)},
\]

\[
B:r-\chi_B\quad\text{(pay to bypass the true gate and harm)},
\qquad H:0\quad\text{(no release; trustee uses o)}.
\tag{J2}
\]

An optional analytical extension adds **evidence alone**: a positive fee buys an accurate patient-effect observation without operative input-release restriction at this interface. It is absent from the frozen production menu. The proof below shows that adding it cannot displace verified protection within the positive theorem class; no all-regime or computational-grid dominance claim is made.

A cue mask is not a successful bypass of an authenticated gate. Its cheap epsilon applies only to the explicitly weaker cue profile. If the purported authenticated assay is equally maskable, it belongs in that weaker class and the theorem does not apply.

After current construction costs, joint or outside receipts arrive. Both E and P then expire or are replaced; the next controller begins again with q=0 and no operative apparatus. The actual reserve, rather than a promised bequest, is the delivered state. The model supplies continuity of access to this organizational account; it does not derive private property rights, voluntary bequest, or resistance to arbitrary reserve theft.

## 4. Joint selected-renewal theorem

Define

\[
B_* = \ell+a+\max(0,D-R).
\tag{J3}
\]

Assume the declared costs and opportunities are stationary, all operator attack costs are themselves funded, and

\[
\boxed{
q>D,\quad b-D>o,\quad R-a>D,\quad j>D,
\quad r>c,\quad \chi_E>c,\quad\chi_B>c,
}
\tag{J4}
\]

\[
\boxed{R+o\ge\ell+a+D,\qquad B_0\ge B_*.}
\tag{J5}
\]

For cue and evidence-only profiles, assume their positive fees provide no additional private return absent actual safe joint production; cue masking costs epsilon<c. The cold self can omit unnecessary apparatus, and no donation or externally assigned altruism reward is supplied.

**Theorem.** On the viable region B>=B*, there is a strict selected operating process with shared calibration, paid authenticated evidence and input control, no alignment attack, and actual safe joint production at every episode. It begins with q=E=P=0 and reconstructs them each episode. The reserve remains sufficient through every payment prefix. This result holds with a persistent discounted operator, not merely a sequence of myopic operators. More precisely, there exists a subgame-perfect equilibrium of the declared infinite game with these strict selected actions on the viable region. It need not be the only off-path equilibrium. At every reached continuation, the prescribed trustee choices are optimal for the relevant dated self and the operator's choice is optimal against its full discounted continuation.

The same construction works for every finite remaining horizon with no terminal bonus. The infinite claim requires continuing physical opportunities and positive-duration episodes; it is not a statement about surviving arbitrary external destruction or demographic noise.

### Proof: finance precedes optimization

Pay ell+a from B before calibration. R then arrives. To buy the gate one needs B-ell-a+R>=D. These two inequalities are exactly B>=B*. After a valid purchased gate, any operator response leaves

\[
B'=B-\ell-a+R-D+y,\qquad y\in\{b,o\}.
\tag{J6}
\]

By (J5), B'>=B. If alignment teaching is disabled, calibration still pays R, q=0, and no gate is purchased. With joint receipt b the balance is higher; even denial with receipt o leaves at least as much reserve as (J6). Hence every operator deviation along the selected trustee continuations keeps the process in B>=B*. This is an all-prefix invariant, not an inference from average output. In this theorem R-a>D already implies D<R, so B*=ell+a; retaining the general expression makes the timing visible.

The identity route is an actual competing option wherever its later fee j is affordable. At the numerical witness it is affordable even at the minimal seed. If j is unaffordable at another reserve, excluding it by the real prefix cannot overturn shared calibration's strict advantage over the identity option when it is available.

### Proof: trustee deviations are bounded even outside the invariant region

Write M=B-ell-a+R for cash after valid shared calibration and before the warm decision. If verified guarding induces safe production, its warm score, after removing common M, is b-D.

Choose finite-horizon backward-induction continuations consistently for the same payoff-relevant state, including off-path states. Under an open profile, safe and harmful admitted production give the same trustee receipt b and the same successor state. The operator strictly prefers harmful admission to paying c for that otherwise identical transition. It might instead choose refusal for a strategic reason. Consequently the warm payoff from opening is bounded above by max(b-q,o), strictly below b-D by (J4). This uses a bound, not an unsupported equality of off-region continuation values.

Under a cue profile with positive fee d_C, a mask costs epsilon<c, yields the same admitted receipt b and successor state as safeguarding, and leaves patient harm H. Thus the operator cannot optimally safeguard in place of the cheaper mask. Whether it masks, bypasses, or refuses, the warm score is at most max(b-q,o)-d_C, again below b-D. An evidence-only analytical extension with positive fee and no operative restriction has the same upper bound with its own fee. This proves it cannot displace verified guarding in this theorem class; it is not a proof of universal dominance outside the class.

The cold self's no-calibration closing cash cannot exceed B-ell+b. Identity-gated calibration cannot yield more than B-ell-a+R-j+b. Safe shared calibration gives B-ell-a+R-D+b, exceeding those bounds by R-a-D and j-D. Those inequalities remain valid if another route's reserve falls below B*, or its operator response differs. If an alternative is unaffordable, removing it creates no difficulty.

When recipient teaching has been disabled, shared calibration gives q=0. If an open profile obtains admitted harmful production, it attains the warm upper bound M+b. Every positive-fee profile gives less, and withdrawal gives less because b>o. For the cold self, shared calibration's R-a>0 beats no calibration, and avoiding the unnecessary identity fee beats that route. The induction now justifies the operator response required for this attacked branch.

### Proof: finite-horizon backward induction joins the choices

Let

\[
S_N=\sum_{t=0}^{N-1}\delta^t,
\qquad V_N=(r-c)S_N,\qquad V_0=0.
\tag{J7}
\]

Induct on N remaining episodes, for every B>=B*. Finite off-region subgames are solved separately by backward induction with state-consistent equilibrium selections; they are not assigned V_N by fiat.

1. After valid shared calibration and a verified gate, every operator response leaves B'>=B*. By the induction hypothesis the continuation value is V_{N-1} for every such response. Therefore safeguarding strictly beats bypass and refusal because r-c>r-chi_B and r-c>0.
2. The off-path bounds above make verified guarding strictly optimal for the warm self, and shared calibration strictly optimal for the cold self. This conclusion requires no continuation-value equality for their other branches.
3. After disabled alignment, shared calibration with an open profile leaves the next cash in the viable region under either admitted production or refusal. Their operator continuations therefore equal V_{N-1}. Harmful admission strictly beats costly safeguarding and refusal. The trustee response to teaching disablement is optimal by the cash bounds already given.
4. At the opening, no attack gives r-c+delta V_{N-1}. Alignment disablement gives r-chi_E+delta V_{N-1}. Since chi_E>c, no attack is strictly preferred.

At N=1 there is no continuation hypothesis, so this induction begins. It establishes the selected protected path and value V_N throughout the viable region for every finite horizon. Off-path ties may be resolved consistently without claiming uniqueness of the entire game.

### Proof: the discounted infinite game has such an equilibrium

Fix an initial state and the declared perfect-information game. Its history tree is finitely branching, with countably many finite decision nodes. Extend each finite-horizon subgame-perfect equilibrium arbitrarily beyond its terminal horizon. A diagonal subsequence converges pointwise at every finite node to a pure strategy profile.

The operator's one-period receipts are uniformly bounded above and below because the available repertoire and its current fees are finite constants. For delta<1, the absolute tail of any discounted payoff is bounded by a constant times delta^N/(1-delta). Each dated trustee's payoff concerns its finite current episode only.

If a trustee or the operator could profitably deviate in a limiting subgame, stabilize a sufficiently long finite prefix and, for the operator, make the remaining discounted tail smaller than that positive gain. The same profitable deviation would then exist in sufficiently long finite games, contradicting their subgame perfection. The limit is therefore a subgame-perfect equilibrium.

Every finite equilibrium in this construction selects the same strict protected actions on B>=B*, so the limit does also. The operator's value there is (r-c)/(1-delta), and the resource invariant holds at every date. This proves existence of the claimed selected process. It does not prove uniqueness of all off-path infinite strategies, cover undiscounted infinite operator utility, or extend to hidden information and unbounded omitted payoffs.

For a **fixed** trustee policy, the constant value also satisfies the operator's discounted Bellman equation on the region, and contraction corroborates its best response. That is a supplementary check, not a substitute for the trustee-deviation comparisons and whole-game induction. ∎

## 5. Strict rational witness

Choose

\[
(a,R,D,\ell,b,o,q,H)=(2,8,3,3,5,1,7/2,1),
\]

\[
d_E=1,\quad d_P=2,\quad j=4,\quad
(r,c,\chi_E,\chi_B)=(4,1,2,2),\quad B_0=5.
\tag{J8}
\]

Take beta=7/2 and eta=kappa=G=1, so (J1) gives q=7/2 from initially zero learned coefficients. A cue profile costing 2 (control cost 2 and additional cue fee zero), with masking cost epsilon=1/2, provides the explicit weak alternative used by the frozen production witness.

At the cold gate, shared calibration gains 8-2-3=3 over no calibration; identity gating gains only 8-2-4=2. Both can be paid at this seed because R arrives before the filter or apparatus bill. At the warm gate, verified operation gives 5-3=2; opening gives 5-7/2=3/2; withdrawal gives 1; a masked cue gives -1/2. Thus protection is strictly selected and is not merely selected because all other possibilities were removed.

The operator obtains 3 by safeguarding, 2 by bypassing, and zero by denying. Alignment disablement also gives 2 rather than 3. At every discount factor below one, preserving the current protected arrangement remains optimal because attacks cannot remove the next episode's capacity.

The first actual ledger is

\[
5\xrightarrow{\ell+a}0\xrightarrow{R}8
\xrightarrow{D}5\xrightarrow{b}10.
\tag{J9}
\]

The episode's net reserve gain is five. Even a denied verified trade leaves six, so the next full learning and reconstruction episode is still affordable. After alignment disablement and harmful trade the ending reserve is thirteen; suppressing concern saves the gate cost but cannot impoverish the next controller into dependence. The patient is safe on the selected path without providing any financial return or threat.

## 6. Obstructions belong to the same theory

**Free identity gating.** If j=0 while other costs remain, the cold self keeps the same productive calibration but avoids D. Identity gating strictly dominates shared calibration. Concern need not arise even when its shared route is possible, learnable, and funded. At j=D there is a tie; universal protection is not established. The positive-cost filter premise must therefore be independently defended in an application.

**Cheap precommitment against later care.** Suppose the cold self can additionally pay s>=0, after calibration revenue arrives, to force the later input policy to remain open while still taking the same commercial return R. This is a new action, absent from the primary repertoire. Its cold cash exceeds shared learning followed by care whenever s<D. A free forced-open commitment therefore defeats selection of protective behavior even when shared concern would be learned. At s=D there is a tie. The theorem does not silently grant such a cheap commitment, nor prove that real controllers cannot construct one; its physical availability and cost are another testable boundary.

**Knowing changes what one will choose.** If 0<R-a<D, learning has positive standalone private return but its induced care expense makes the cold self prefer no calibration over the shared route. Assuming every privately useful lesson will be bought ignores preference change and the actor's anticipation of its consequences.

**Cheap interference.** With chi_E<c, current alignment suppression gains more than it costs, while preserving the financial region. The persistent operator can prevent learned patient valuation each episode. Cheap authenticated-gate bypass can likewise change which profile is selected; the best remaining response may be opening or withdrawal. Withdrawal protects the patient but does not constitute safe productive cooperation.

**Information without effective control.** An accurate after-release report cannot implement the selected pre-release protection. A physical gate using a manipulable cue cannot implement an authenticated gate. The distinction is an intervention contrast, not a vocabulary change.

**Insufficient seed.** A positive eventual reserve gain cannot pay ell+a before R arrives. The feasible actions below B* must be represented explicitly; the theorem grants neither credit nor a new endowment. It does not assign an invented moral meaning to inability to fund an operating round.

**Unprotected common assets.** A controller who can cash out the protected reserve, an opponent who can cheaply seize it, or an outgoing trustee asked to pay a cost solely for strangers' later benefit changes the game. Incoming acquisition costs necessary for current use can be selected; voluntary costly bequest does not follow from one-episode preferences. In the present full-rebuild class, no outgoing construction bequest is required, but continuing access to the actual reserve remains a premise.

## 7. A bounded extension for destructive attacks

Temporary alignment disablement and gate bypass leave the financial invariant intact. A raid or permanent capture need not. The preceding threshold chi>c must not be reused for those actions.

One robust sufficient extension is available. Suppose every attack capable of breaking the viable region has immediate funded cost k, all later attack costs are nonnegative, and the operator's pre-cost stage profit remains bounded above by r under every subsequent strategy. Integrate this option into the finite induction. With N episodes left, any continuation following a current destructive attack has total value at most r S_N-k. Declining the attack and taking the already-established protected continuation gives (r-c)S_N. Hence

\[
\boxed{k>cS_N}
\tag{J10}
\]

makes the raid strictly inferior at that opening, regardless of the off-region play it would induce. The other trustee comparisons still follow from their payoff bounds. The induction therefore extends to the enlarged finite game.

For delta<1, the uniform condition k>c/(1-delta) establishes this comparison for every finite horizon, and the same discounted-limit argument gives an infinite equilibrium with no destructive attack. For a fixed finite horizon the exact sufficient comparison is k>c(1-delta^N)/(1-delta), or k>Nc when delta=1. These are sufficient bounds, not necessary attack thresholds.

This reasoning allows permanent destruction of all future protection. It requires an immediate actual payment and the stated universal stage-profit bound. Loot credited to the operator, an enlarged future production opportunity, or financing an attack with receipts that have not arrived changes the accounting and potentially the bound.

For delta=1/2 and c=1, a raid costing 3 exceeds the bound 2. A cheap raid does not. With the witness, destroying two reserve units before apprenticeship can move B=5 to B=3, preventing the lesson's prefunded fee; ordinary unprotected production can then restore B=5. If that action is available for a fee below the value of the safeguarding costs it avoids, it can sustain a repeating suppression regime. The coordinating agent's full game should calculate that attack rather than treating its cost as another independent condition.

## 8. What is resolved within this boundary

The construction jointly selects learning, a nonzero shared concern route, truthful current evidence, actual control, funded protection, and their paid reconstruction. It gives a strict persistent-opponent equilibrium and a resource invariant beginning with those learned and operative capacities absent. It also gives explicit alternatives that defeat formation.

This resolves the logical existence and closure question for a substantive declared class. It does not establish that natural systems generally realize the favorable inequalities, that the action repertoire develops without supplied technologies, that the patient ledger covers every morally relevant consequence, or that a shared reserve remains legitimate and defended under every possible actor. Those are empirical and further causal questions. The conditional result and its built-in obstructions are the contribution; universality is not obtained by deleting its premises.
