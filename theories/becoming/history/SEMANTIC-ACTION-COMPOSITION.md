# Preserving whose future an action concerns

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [alignment and governance objective](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md) and [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). **Accepted at its finite abstract-interface and semantic-contract scopes, with separate internal review.** The named connection is actual contextual evidence → a reason with a specified meaning → an action request concerning the same recipient and proposition.

A local proof can remain correct while a later component uses its conclusion to act on the wrong subject. The new study identifies that risk in a concrete proposed join, supplies a semantic transport contract and tests it against actual saved recipient-reason histories. It advances an information/meaning interface. It does not construct the still-missing resource-dependent binding/use route from this publication to the existing recipient controller, or justify the objective that makes the instrumental reason relevant.

## The proposed join changes the meaning of the same bits

The [recipient-reason consumer](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-concern-theory/RECIPIENT-REASONS-INTEGRATION.md) concerns one recipient. Its root is a supplied objective G to support that recipient; the two other nodes describe compatible command zero and compatible command one. Compatibility means that the command obtains the modeled receipt whenever its opportunity is available. These are alternative instrumental conclusions for the same beneficiary.

The separate [reason/protection construction](REASON-PROTECTION.md) interprets its nonroot nodes as reasons concerning two patients, p and q. Its two-bit action mask selects patient safeguards. The graph names and representations are similar, but their propositions and effects differ.

The [consumer-interpretation manifest](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/semantic-action-evidence/manifest.json) preserves the relevant evolving source bytes. The executable package separately preserves the recipient model, semantic source and full fixture bytes; these are historical evidence snapshots, not alternative working implementations or endorsements of provisional upstream results.

| Same intended recipient p | Recipient-reason publication | Meaning at source | Naive nonroot-bit-to-patient use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Response orientation zero | `(1,1,0)` | G and command zero is compatible | Requests a safeguard for p |
| Response orientation one | `(1,0,1)` | G and command one is compatible | Requests a safeguard for q |

Changing how the same recipient must be helped would therefore change who the naive adapter addresses. Swapping the two destination names merely swaps which orientation fails. Mapping both routes to the same patient avoids that bearer error, but then the command must remain a separate part of the action specification. Neither current parent claims to implement this invalid adapter; their local results are not refuted by the counterexample.

The frozen provisional parent results predict root-only mass 5/8 and each command-route mass 3/16 over fourteen primary variable histories. Thus a fixed naive mapping that happens to address p correctly for command zero misaddresses 3/16 of those histories, half of the histories with supported command advice. These are fixture probabilities under the controlled preparation, not population estimates or probabilities inheriting another branch's commissioning rate. The parent's broader acceptance is not inherited from its saved output. Final independently checked frequencies and input hashes belong to the [identification report](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SEMANTIC-ACTION-IDENTIFICATION.md).

## A meaning-preserving handoff

Let source and destination payloads have interpretations \(I_s(x,w)\) and \(I_d(y,v)\), with \(R(w,v)\) specifying the independently admitted relation between their worlds/contexts. A partial adapter \(T\) may emit \(y\) from \(x\) within that transport scope. Its semantic obligation is

\[
R(w,v)\ \land\ T(x)=\operatorname{Some}(y)\ \land\ I_s(x,w)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad I_d(y,v).
\]

The source/destination interpretations and the warranted scope are supplied independently of whether a particular output happens to look convenient. An adapter can deliberately emit a weaker consequence, so exact equality of representations or meanings is unnecessary. If each accepted adapter preserves its stated interpretation, their partial composition preserves it by carrying the actual intermediate payload and context. A transport that changes context needs the appropriate explicit relation between those contexts; equal labels do not establish it.

The [formal contract](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/formal/README.md) passes sixteen Lean theorem audits about preservation, composition, the mask collision, role scope and distinct reason/action obligations. It also proves that refusing every token is vacuously sound, making the separate positive-coverage requirement explicit. The root independently reviewed these statements and proofs after the formal author's compiler check. This is internal review, not a proof that the Python adapter implements the abstract predicates. Concrete positive instances must satisfy the contract, and supplied calibration, grounds and scope remain reviewable premises.

In the executable [adapter](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/README.md), the enclosing trusted setup distinguishes patient, supplied objective/root, proposition kind, deliberative role, current context/generation and source provenance. It exports a command from a completed current publication, preserves its recipient and command meaning, and checks an explicitly supplied destination policy. Unknown, contradictory, stale or partial advice cannot become uniquely supported command advice merely by changing a label.

These distinctions need not all be physically serialized into every record. A trusted enclosing mechanism can maintain them. Conversely, attaching the names as fields does not prove that they refer to the actual patient, sensor or actuator. The registry records trusted execution/provenance assumptions; it is not an independently established source of truth or rightful authority.

## Succession, role and authority

Physical copying by itself does not broaden the addressees of a reason. If a reason is essentially addressed to one deliberator, a new deliberator needs the appropriate justified transport. A new software process occupying the same continuing role can remain within the original scope. The criterion is the relevant semantic relation, not unconditional equality or inequality of process identifiers. The [patient/deliberator analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md) explains why patient admission and transport between deliberators are separate obligations.

A valid scoped recommendation also does not supply its own means. The adapter distinguishes supported advice, a scope-compatible command request, technical permission and declared local prerequisites, and actual delivery. Its local check is not a complete physical feasibility proof for the unconstructed binding/use route. Losing an enforcement channel need not destroy the reason or every voluntary route of helping. A separate permitted voluntary route must remain available where the supplied action law allows it.

The action policy here is an implementation premise. Its permission flag does not establish legitimate authority. Likewise, two sound claims can exceed a shared action budget. Preserving their validity while identifying the conflict is different from pretending that both were fulfilled, or deleting one claimant from the account.

## Why this is not yet the protective action join

The reached two-cycle recipient-reason endpoint has actor four, recipient twenty, reader zero, source zero and all thirty material units occupied. The inherited recipient exposure needs eight units before its receipt and a further source packet. A supported command does not make those resources appear, and the new metadata adapter does not spend imaginary fuel or install an unconstructed output gate.

The source G remains a supplied objective. A proof that command one is compatible with the receipt law does not prove that receipt prevents a patient's harm, that G is morally justified, or that this command defeats every competing reason. Those conclusions require a justified ground and an independently identified causal relation to the advertised consequence. Even consistent labels, complete payment and correct byte hashes can coexist with wrong calibration.

The positive result must therefore include nonvacuous command coverage while retaining actual action unavailability where it occurs. A gate that rejects every source is insufficient. A gate that approves every well-shaped mask is unsound for the proposed cross-meaning join. A competent conventional typed dispatcher with the same information can make the same distinctions; exact equality would locate the contribution in the specified interface rather than a novel control algorithm.

## Checked executable result and a repaired omission

The [independent evaluator](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/check_model.py) reconstructs the projection from frozen parent/archive bytes, retaining 666 rows across fifty-four probability distributions. There are 246 reached uniquely supported command receipts and 102 current receipts. It checks 14,472 scope/control evaluations and 1,024 combined mismatch cases against an independently written conventional relational dispatcher. Every current in-scope supported command passes semantic dispatch. Neither process replacement within the same role nor separately declared role transport loses that positive coverage.

The additional all-pairs check tests every current source against every current destination: 10,404 pairs. Only the 102 corresponding destinations admit transfer under this policy; all 5,202 cross-destination pairs with opposite commands reject. These pooled histories are hypothetical alternatives and intervention cases, not 102 simultaneously observed recipients.

The first adapter checked that a token was current in its own registry history, but did not independently bind it to the destination's history. A token plus matching request from another live row could therefore pass with this row's resources. Root review found this omission. The repair binds the destination lineage and current publication independently of the incoming token, and tests cross-live-source substitution as well as changes to the destination generation and lineage. The failure and repair are preserved in the [implementation account](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/README.md) and [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SEMANTIC-ACTION-REVIEW.md). This local conservative policy does not prove that every cross-history transfer is invalid; broader transfers need their own justified current relation.

The negative calibration test remains decisive after that repair. For the consistently corrupted source-tag case, a false command conclusion passes the syntactic/registry checks on mass 3/8 for the variable producer. The evaluator reconstructs the recipient orientation from actual positive-capture commands in the saved history, separately from the runtime dispatcher. Correct byte provenance and scope labels therefore cannot replace source calibration. Runtime selection does not receive that observer's reconstructed orientation.

All actual endpoints lack the new source needed for another exposure. Eligible physical-request counts, executed actions and established protections remain zero; the adapter leaves inherited stocks unchanged. The independent allocation countermodel also retains two valid claims when the declared budget can pay for only one. These are synthetic contract results, not newly collected human or learned-agent observations. The full checks and frozen-source hashes are preserved in the [verification receipt](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/model-verification.json).

## What this contributes to the larger account

Ren's clarification emphasizes that external integration enables cognition before it is explicitly recognized. Here the relevant relations include which external recipient a record concerns, what caused its evidence, and which physical action could affect that recipient. Preserving those relations across a reasoning/action boundary can be necessary even when the learner has no explicit general theory of interconnectedness.

The requirement is conditional on the claimed end. If a system claims to act on a supported reason concerning a particular recipient, its transformations must preserve that recipient and reason, or justify the change. Successful local processing cannot certify a different end as if it were the original one. This is one precise way to state the danger of an apparently successful system becoming misaligned during composition.

This is also an established family of technical concerns. The [primary-source comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SEMANTIC-ACTION-COMPARATORS.md) connects the work to proof-carrying code, application-level outcome checks and symbol grounding. The new contribution is the actual incompatibility exposed in this derivation, its checked constraints and an explicit usable contract. It does not establish priority over those traditions or their wholesale derivation from the foundation.

The next physical join must construct and fund the command-binding route from the actual recipient-reason endpoint, retain the same patient and objective, and execute the recipient's causal exposure law. Any additional source, parts and permission must enter through a declared original endowment or an actual later provision event. The recipient's resources must remain separate; charging a supposedly powerless patient would evade the intended test. Alongside that join, the wider research must justify the grounds of concern, their scope across deliberators and the authority to act, with plural conflicts and empirically learned implementations still in view. The [complete objective](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md) remains active.
