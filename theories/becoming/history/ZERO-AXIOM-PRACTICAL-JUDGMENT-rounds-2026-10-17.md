## 2. 肯定路线：实践判断是否已经在承担其根据 / Affirmative route

本节保留早期以行动相关判断为对象的路线。Ren 在第10节的后续纠正明确：这不是所有规范结构的通用定义；第11–12节从整体活动重新构造更宽的问题。 / This preserves the earlier route concerning action-related judgment. The later correction in §10 withdraws its use as a definition of every normative structure; §§11–12 reconstruct the broader question within whole activity.

当前最值得直接检验的路线是：**判断不是外在观察，而是形成行动的活动；有根据的判断须能够回应自己的内容、后果及其所依赖的生活关系，因此实践的规范性可能内在于这一活动。** 这个路线比“我想某件事，所以它好”更强；它试图解释判断作为判断的成立条件。

The affirmative route treats judging as an activity forming action. A warranted judgment must answer to its content, consequences and relations of dependence; practical normativity may therefore be internal to this activity. This seeks constitutive warrant, rather than treating a desire as its own ethical validation.

逐步展开后必须分别检查：

## 5. 反审与目前剩余连接 / Counterassessment and remaining bridge

**Strongest affirmative reply:** the finite-weight rule may omit a non-substitutable relation of living participation and the constitution of judging itself. The local theorem does not settle that richer question. The next account must independently specify the relation, explain its non-compensable practical standing and address lives unable to deliberate. Naming it irreplaceable or defining genuine judgment to endorse J2 does not discharge the work.

**构成路线的实质对手：**它接受整体关系、真实损失、判断的可错性及自己的参与，却认为判断成立只要求理由内容的可靠把握与适当行动，不已经包含生生的普遍正面方向、基本条件的词典序或所有关系性责任。不能仅称它为二元论或缺乏智慧。它也须解释自己的伦理内容；双方都不是由对方尚未证明而自动胜出。

## 9. 规范如何成为有作用的结构 / How a norm becomes an operative structure

*(Excerpt from this section; the rest of it stays in the [live chapter](../chapters/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md#9-规范如何成为有作用的结构--how-a-norm-becomes-an-operative-structure).)*

### 自我强化与有根据的坚持 / Reinforcement and warranted persistence

概念案例：一个共同制作活动以“报告缺陷，使使用者免受已知故障”为要求。初期报告改变检查、修复和训练。后来，组织可能把低报告数当成成功，并把每次报告当作不忠；报告减少、口号更一致，实际缺陷却可能失去进入纠正的路径。要求的词语保持相同，实际组织已变。这个变化不能靠统计口号的复制数量充分描述。

Conceptual case: a shared making practice asks participants to report defects so that users are protected from known failures. Reports initially change inspection, repair and training. Later, the organization might treat low report counts as success and reports themselves as disloyalty. Reports decline and slogans become more uniform while defects lose a route into correction. The wording persists while its operative organization changes. Counting copies of the slogan misses that change.

这提供有内容的内部批评：若该要求仍以辨明并修复缺陷为其理由，压低报告不能替代缺陷已修复的证据。批评所诉诸的制作、缺陷、报告与受影响生活都在同一过程内。它不必引入过程之外的裁判；但此处对“保护使用者”的认可仍是该实践明确承担的目的，尚未由这个案例独自得到普遍伦理根据。

An internal criticism can identify the mismatch: while detecting and repairing defects remains the stated reason, reduced reporting is not evidence that defects were repaired. Making, defects, reports and affected living all belong to the process. The criticism needs no judge outside it. However, this case still credits the practice's declared purpose of protecting users; it does not independently establish that purpose's universal ethical warrant.

有根据的坚持也可能拒绝错误报告，因此“受批评就改变”并非判准。具体检验应比较：指控得到独立支持还是被反驳；同样充分的证据是否因提出者不同而被排除；复查是否真的改变行动；故障被修复还是只改了记录；原目的是否公开改变，改变的后果是否仍被承认。实际认知和组织成本也必须计入，不要求每次活动重开全部判断。

Warranted persistence can reject a false report. Changing whenever challenged is therefore no criterion of correctness. The proposed checks concern corroboration or rebuttal, exclusion of equally adequate evidence by its source, actual changes in action, repair versus altered records, and openly acknowledged changes of purpose and their consequences. Cognitive and organizational costs matter; every action need not reopen every judgment.
