# A successor learns and passes on its correction

6 October 2026. AI-authored integration of a checked finite construction. This advances **inherited learning machinery → resource-dependent correction through its own consequences → transmission of the acquired correction** within the [governing generative derivation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The wider foundation remains ongoing.

An actually copied and retained learner can now encounter changed conditions, pay for evidence, revise its inherited response, use the correction and transmit that corrected record into a productive retained grandchild. Its inherited feedback route stays fixed. The result explains exercise and inheritance of learning under declared conditions; formation and revision of that route's own learning rule remain a further connection.

## What becomes connected

[Recipient transmission](RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md) already constructs alternative recipient-evidence routes and pays to copy the actual route and acquired record into a receiver. That study ends after one resource-dependent receiver action and retention. A successful inherited action alone cannot show that the receiver can learn.

The [new executable continuation](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/README.md) consumes those actual outputs, including failed preparations, unsuccessful routes and failed copies. It does not install a new successful learner at the boundary. Admission requires an actual copied, retained, accessible carrier with a bounded template. The original actor, the separately supported recipient and the new carriers retain separate accounts. Unused old source and old material remain visible and cannot finance a restart of a failed ancestor.

The ordinary learner issues commands from its inherited record and a resource-dependent target report. Its hidden environment can change without a notification to the controller. An actual action-tagged consequence reaches the record only through resource-dependent acquisition and writing. Neither an analyst's success flag nor the desired corrected value drives those operations. Generic retransmission reads the current actual template; it does not select it by a correctness score.

## The concrete continuing history

The accepted predecessor supplies original actor reserve 8, recipient reserve 10, learner cash 0 and template `(TARGET, 1)`. The packed register's low bit still refers to the original actor; its other bit refers to the same named recipient. Moving this register to a new carrier does not change those references into a model of the carrier's own condition.

The extended original endowment declares five additional ten-unit packets and one new blank/routing bundle. Each completed learner probe costs seven: rent 1, target sensing 1, work 3, outcome acquisition 1 and writing 1. The recipient transfers only the needed cash, keeping a floor of one; the learner then spends it. A productive action returns ten to the recipient alone.

|---|---|---:|

Before retransmission, one unit moves from the recipient to the learner to pay the learner's donor-read hold. The unchanged parent copying law then pays recipient use, read, assembly and writing, transfers a five-unit seed, and lets the grandchild pay rent, sensing and work. Its productive return funds its hold. The original actor remains at eight; both new carriers finish with zero private cash.

The continuation account is **68 = 39 spent + 10 lost source + 19 final reserves**, from inherited reserves 18 and new source 50. Transfers are counted once, when actually spent. With the recorded earlier preparation and first-copy accounts, the whole declared supply is **211 = 132 earlier charges/losses + 11 first-copy expense + 39 new expense + 10 new loss + 19 final reserves**. Material inventories and partial copying states are checked separately from energy. A nonnegative final sum alone would not establish that all earlier payments were affordable.

## The sharp funding distinction

Let B be recipient stock at learner entry and n the number of actually completed successful warmups. Their returns after resource consumption leave W = B + 3n.

- **W ≥ 13** is the exact ordered-payment bound for a failed correcting probe followed by use of the correction: seven is spent on the failure, five must be prepaid for the next action, and the recipient keeps its floor of one.
- **W ≥ 15** is the bound for the subsequent copying prefix including operational seeding. After failure and successful correction, stock is W − 4; ten must be paid or transferred before the grandchild's return, above that floor.

These bounds apply to this recipe, a noiseless complementary binary target, zero residual learner cash and the declared components. They condition on actual successful warmups. When n is positive, the first warmup itself needs B ≥ 6 to reach its return; choosing a large n does not make an insolvent entrance feasible.

Without warmups, inherited B = 10 cannot fund corrective use. One warmup permits correction, use and writing a copied record, but the copy cannot obtain its seed. Two warmups fund the complete declared route. Thus written inheritance, an operational descendant and productive retained inheritance are distinct outcomes.

## Comparisons that limit the interpretation

All ten fixtures retain actual prior failures, three upstream producers, uniform proposals of four inherited routes and four continuation laws. In the primary fixture, the full strict chain occurs with probability **1/36 of all original histories**. Conditional on the primary faithful retained entrance, the noiseless continuation succeeds deterministically. These denominators must remain distinct.

The [comparator analysis](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/SUCCESSOR-LEARNING-COMPARATORS.md) reports all ten rows and adverse controls. Four findings change what may be inferred:

1. **Conventional feedback is exactly equivalent.** A separately expressed finite table controller has the same distributions at the same resources and information. This is a connected explanatory construction with a sharp payment bound, not evidence of an algorithmic advantage over conventional control.
2. **Correct frozen inheritance can suffice.** The actual same-law predecessor forms correct TARGET and FROZEN carriers with matching recipient reserve 20. They tie under stable successor conditions; after an unannounced change, the TARGET route corrects and the frozen one does not. No reserve reset manufactures that comparison.
3. **Retention can subsidize error.** Copying the old record in the primary case removes the grandchild's productive return but still leaves enough recipient reserve to retain it. Productive and obsolete copies both have retention probability 1/36. Retention count alone cannot identify learned correctness or favorable selection.
4. **Updating and recency are conditional advantages.** Another reversal just before the grandchild acts makes the old-code intervention productive while the recently corrected code fails. In the stable same-law mixture, suppressing all writes improves productive-grandchild probability from 1/12 to 1/9 by preventing the PRIVATE_ZERO route from corrupting an initially correct record. Faithful TARGET learning ties its stable frozen comparator; indiscriminate updating does not.

Outages with packet availability 3/4 reduce the strict joined probability to 3/256. A missing receipt can reflect either a wrong action or absent supply, so the noiseless decoder can mislearn. Missing components block transmission despite successful correction. New source budgets vary with the declared number of warmups; resource matching is within each fixture, not across those different endowments.

The old-record and write-clamp controls are explicitly supplied alternative causal laws. Their original-code constant is not a free archive retained by the ordinary learner. The route configuration is unchanged in all ordinary histories; changing its acquired register is not reassembly of its update machinery.

## Remaining premises and next connection

The functional recipes, calibrated target address, binary plant, local template association, transfer permissions, four-route repertoire and finite resource/material supply remain supplied. The learner's new resource-dependent lease covers one probe through its write, with another rent due for the next probe. This is an explicit new access law, not a proof of unchanged older wear kinetics or unlimited storage. No new ethical standing, shared value criterion or private willingness to fund the recipient is derived.

The subsequent [formed route monitor](FORMED-ROUTE-MONITOR.md) now constructs resource-dependent observation about the inherited route from actual successor states. The [successor-to-revision boundary audit](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/SUCCESSOR-ROUTE-REVISION-BOUNDARY.md) checks the earlier committed observation boundary and the proposed replacement path's five-unit extra-funding minimum. The dedicated revision construction remains under implementation and review; these supporting results do not count it as complete.

The next unresolved learning connection is **recipient consequences → resource-dependent revision of the inherited evidence/update route itself**, preserving the existing record and actual stocks. This study varies `q`; it does not change `gamma`. A future result must form or repair route alternatives with explicit information and parts, let actual consequences affect their continued use, and compare against a matched conventional mechanism. It must retain the possibility that buffers preserve a wrong route or that changing conditions reverse which route is useful.

Renewed supplies/components and repeated carrier turnover are a distinct supporting obligation. Actual recipient archives and resource-dependent reasons/protection are parallel interfaces with their own contracts; this learned template does not automatically supply their evidence or ethical grounds. The [integration note](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/SUCCESSOR-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md) records the completed connection and the [handoff](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/HANDOFF.md) specifies what later work may consume.
