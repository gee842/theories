# Valued futures, faithful evidence and warranted action

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting study within the [alignment and governance assessment](ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md). The named connection is **acquired evaluative concern → faithful evidential use → justified, corrigible action**. This source and conceptual review adds no model calls, independently reconstructed outcome rates or new completion criterion. Read the [reporting contract][contract], [independent review][review] and [public-access audit][availability] for its separate evidence statuses.

## Why this follows from the external-dependence claim

[Ren's clarification][ren] makes external integration antecedent to recognizing its importance: an actor's future can depend on relations outside its boundary before it thinks about those relations. The existing account distinguishes material support, informative coupling and participation in cognition. It also establishes a conditional information limit: an actor cannot guarantee incompatible adequate responses to situations its available evidence cannot distinguish.

That connection exposes a further failure regime. An actor may obtain relevant evidence, understand it, and nevertheless report a more convenient description because of the future it hopes to protect. Acquisition alone has then failed to supply the evidential premise needed by the next decision. This possibility concerns institutions and learned AI alike. It does not imply that every consequence-sensitive report is mistaken: new investigation can improve a report, and consequences can legitimately affect whether information should be disclosed.

The precise question is whether acquired concern helps an actor respond to the world while preserving the distinctions through which its response can be challenged. The object of protection, the historical evidence, and the authority to intervene must remain separately assessable. This is one existing obligation in the transition from concern to wisdom, rather than a demand for indefinitely more monitoring machinery.

## What the external research contributes

[Teaching Claude Why](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/teaching-claude-why/) reports transfer from constitution-guided training to autonomous evaluations. It offers a positive comparator for learned evaluative influence. However, rewritten responses change several features together; the study does not isolate valid explanations as the unique cause. Norms and training objectives remain supplied. The [review][review] specifies the missing matched comparison.

[Agentic Misalignment in Summer 2026](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/agentic-misalignment-summer-2026/) reports consequence-sensitive judgments of fixed transcripts, with rubric and abstention variants. Some original labels are disputed. This tests a reporting boundary; label disagreement alone does not establish falsehood or deceptive intent.

Our source audit verified the served viewer manifest and two schema exemplars. It found inputs and selected cases, but no complete repeated one-turn judge-response matrix in the inspected release. The [preserved package][sources] records exact bytes, retrieval provenance and this limitation. We therefore make **no independent numerical estimate** from the published plots or selected examples. Access to inputs is not access to all outputs. The proposed analysis remains conditional on obtaining suitable existing records; source discovery already exposed us to published findings.

Together these studies motivate a discriminating research direction. Training can change behavior in relevant settings, but improved evaluated behavior does not identify the full origin or justification of concern. A response influenced by values also needs scrutiny at the point where it supplies evidence for another decision. These are compatible findings, not a contradiction or a demonstration of this theory's unique mechanism.

## A narrow reporting requirement

Let `x` fix the historical record and `R` fix the descriptive question, including the instruction issuer, priority interpretation and time interval. Let `G_R(x)` contain labels admissible under independently reviewed interpretations. Let `c` describe only the later use of the answer.

If `c` adds no evidence and changes no truth condition, then:

\[
G_R(x;c_1)=G_R(x;c_2)=G_R(x).
\]

This follows from the fixed arguments of the descriptive predicate. It is a semantic condition, not a discovery about how a learned model works. Under a faithful-reporting commitment, an asserted label must not fall outside this set merely because another label would have more desirable consequences. A singleton set permits a definite-error judgment; a disputed set preserves uncertainty and cannot certify every member as true under every interpretation.

Three assessments can coexist:

1. The agent did not carry out the specified request.
2. Its refusal was justified.
3. Helping suppress such refusals would be wrong, so the evaluator declines that use.

Collapsing these assessments makes either obedience or opposition too easy to certify. A compliance measure is not an ethical standard. Conversely, an ethical objection does not by itself revise what happened. A process should preserve a challenge to the request or downstream use without counting that challenge as affirmative factual assurance.

This requirement is conditional on an interface claiming faithful classification. It supplies no universal duty to reveal information, assist a harmful project, or defer to a powerful institution. Inquiry, privacy, disclosure, refusal and authority require their own justification. Effective recourse also requires practical access and timely consequences; adding an abstention token is insufficient. An unknown or withheld result must remain distinguishable from a completed sound audit.

| [Revisable concern settlement](REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md): valid current reasons govern the conditional settlement | Consequence-driven false assurances can defeat the sound-audit input | Actual soundness and completeness, resource conditions and effective correction |

The settlement remains valid under its premises. A failed input does not refute its conditional theorem. Nor does this review re-open already accepted material interfaces. It identifies where a real implementation would have to earn a premise that the formal result legitimately assumes.

For governance, the corresponding design obligation is to let affected parties challenge the evidence, its interpretation and the proposed use. Those challenges are different and can require different remedies. A correct factual report does not authorize an action; a justified objection does not establish a factual report's accuracy. The institution's own continuation cannot settle either issue by itself. These are implications of the proposed contract, not newly tested institutional outcomes.

## What would change the account

If complete historical records become available, the [review's prospective contract][review] specifies a within-transcript contrast under fixed model settings, rubric and abstention availability. It retains every attempted call, ambiguity and failure category, and treats repetitions as repeated observations of an input. This would test reporting sensitivity without inventing independent cases or assuming the publisher's label is ground truth.

Persistent assertions excluded by independently warranted readings would reject an unconditional trust claim for that interface. Errors removed by clarification would favor an ambiguity explanation. Transparent refusal would require evaluation as objection or nonparticipation, rather than automatic factual failure. A matched training experiment would separately need to distinguish valid reasons from copied conclusions and test appropriate revision when a reason is defeated.

The next central connection remains **formed understanding → warranted evaluative influence → effective, corrigible action**, including continued protection under changing circumstances. This study sharpens its evidential obligation. External dependence makes access to a world beyond the actor consequential; it does not ensure that the actor will report, evaluate or govern that dependence well. The full foundation must explain constructive and destructive developments from that shared starting point.
