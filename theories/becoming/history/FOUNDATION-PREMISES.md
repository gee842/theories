## 1. The common claim

The candidate begins with activity forming and changing the conditions of further activity. Organizations, observers, retained models and evaluations are formations within that unfolding. A model that changes action belongs in the same explanation as the action's consequences. Where a mechanism is said to form, persist or change, its enabling relations, formation history and effects on later possibilities have to be identified.

The [deeper-root analysis](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md) gives the current bilingual formulation and its genealogy. This explanatory commitment does not identify every center with one subject, make every change beneficial, or imply that every organization learns. Different formations and opposed consequences remain within the account. Acting and undergoing distinguish relations within particular histories; neither requires a permanently active giver or passive receiver. Where a local model identifies a learner, responder or recipient, those names specify roles in that model. They do not by themselves explain the formation of the beings involved or erase differences in experience, agency and consent.

For the discrete finite-history results, a compact common mathematical presentation is

\[
P=(S,A,E),\qquad E\subseteq S\times A\times S.
\]

The states and event relation are supplied model data. Events need not be intentional, deterministic or controllable. A state must retain the conditions needed by the claim; otherwise the omitted history must be restored or an adequate history-state representation used. Observable distinctions and causally consequential distinctions need not coincide.

Given this data, finite histories and their concatenation through an actual shared state are constructed inductively. The checked least-closure result derives finite endpoint reachability; it does not derive the physical event relation. A changing difference becomes a condition of further change when the actual successor participates in later events. Particular feedback loops, organizations and learners require further properties of that relation.

This presentation alone has little predictive content. An arbitrary transition system can encode almost any proposed history language. The foundation earns explanatory force only when independently interpretable restrictions on its laws imply consequences, when mechanisms satisfy those restrictions, and when observations test those identifications. Adding unmeasured hidden states after every contrary observation would preserve representability while abandoning a testable explanation.

## 2. Five distinct kinds of commitment

| Kind | What is supplied | What it can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Formal presentation | States, events, transition relation, definitions and the proof system | Exact consequences of stated hypotheses; representation and composition results |
| Interpretable law conditions | Identified demand/provision accounts, construction constraints, observations, consequential feedback, timing | Conditional theorems over every process satisfying those conditions |
| Particular realization | Components, recipes, rates, sensors, initial stocks, available interventions and declared schedules or policies | Feasible or impossible histories, probabilities or strategic conclusions at that model's stated scope |
| Empirical identification | Measurements, interventions, variable-to-world correspondence and justified boundaries | Evidence that a real system instantiates the premises, or that the mapping fails |
| Ethical grounds | Reasons concerning affected beings, relevance, agency and answerability | Arguments about warranted concern and action; causal origin alone cannot establish their validity |

These are not interchangeable. A kernel-checked theorem validates an implication in its formal interpretation. It does not measure a sensor's calibration or establish the ethical standing of the subject represented by a variable. A successful physical experiment can support an implementation without proving universal historical convergence. An ethical reason can remain valid even when the modeled actor fails to recognize or act on it.

The phrase *no custom axioms* in a proof report belongs to the first row. Model data, theorem hypotheses and ordinary proof rules remain explicit. Reports must disclose standard dependencies rather than treating their absence or presence as an ontological result.

**Mathematical-grounding clarification, 6 October 2026:** the [completed argument assessment](MATHEMATICAL-TRUTH-AND-THE-ROOT.md#resolution-of-the-remaining-bridges) distinguishes a canonical construction from a uniquely ultimate explanation of mathematics. Formation rules, equality conditions, admitted domains, background existence principles and logical semantics remain explicit inputs when used. Least closure and initiality give precise relative uniqueness; they do not select their own rule system, and exact truth determination does not by itself establish metaphysical priority. A generative structural identity interpretation is defended as a scoped philosophical extension, not inserted as a proved consequence of the causal presentation. This clarification adds no empirical law or ethical ground and does not complete the unified foundation.

## 4. Where a derivation may legitimately stop

**Scope after the no-assumptions directive:** the stopping points in this section are legitimate for conditional studies. They are not final stopping points for Ren's stronger foundation requirement; any remaining independently supplied explanatory premise leaves that requirement unmet.

A conditional foundation can begin with an explicit class of physical or informational laws. It need not explain why every law of our universe has its actual value. A construction recipe is a legitimate local premise when its inputs, outputs, costs and functionality are stated independently and the advertised mechanism is actually built and used through them.

The stopping point becomes inadequate when the premise already supplies the central capacity being explained. Examples include assuming an active learner when claiming to derive its formation, assuming a correct target label when explaining evidence acquisition, or assuming other-directed evaluation when explaining how another center first enters evaluation. Naming the hidden capacity a physical law does not discharge that explanatory obligation.

Conversely, a fixed finite repertoire can establish a conditional instance of selection over selectors. It need not generate every possible algorithm to count as that result. A general account of architectural change must then identify the structural conditions shared across different repertoires and test alternative realizations. It cannot obtain generality merely by repeating one binary fixture.

Variation of higher-order mechanisms can itself consist of ordinary physical events. No infinitely descending tower of external selectors is required by the formal presentation. Whether a particular law produces, retains or replaces those mechanisms is a substantive question about its dynamics and starting conditions. The existence of one useful event word, its likelihood, its selection by a policy, and its indefinite reliability are different conclusions.

If a claim requires indefinitely many costly reconstructions, it must supply continuing components and support. Finite-stock obstructions do not imply that all learning requires unlimited matter: already formed finite hardware can perform repeated updates under an appropriate law. Claims about ever-growing records, architectural novelty or ongoing maintenance carry their own capacity and support obligations.

## 5. How constructive and destructive regimes share the root

The comparison must name the affected centers, their enabling conditions, the available interventions and the horizon. Where one organization acts and another undergoes its effects, their conditions of continuation must be distinguished. A local model may represent these through the acting organization's reserve, the other being's functioning, retained evidence, authority to correct and means of reconstruction. An aggregate increase cannot by itself establish that each center's possibilities improved.

The [formal core](FORMAL-CORE.md) already distinguishes effective capacity from unrestricted reachability. Under fixed laws, a successor's all-time reachable states were also reachable from its predecessor by concatenation. Creating a tool can nevertheless enlarge immediate or budget-limited achievable tasks. These distinct claims must not be exchanged when describing “opening possibilities.”

The common account currently identifies particular positive and adverse mechanisms: repair can restore access; learning can correct a stale command; a faithful record can support an inference; shared construction can also exhaust future components; selective recording can preserve a false model; private success can enable exclusion; an identity gate can suppress protection. A completed regime account must connect these mechanisms within compatible histories and specify interventions that move the process between them.

These contrasts separate four questions: whether a capacity exists, whether a mechanism selects its use, whether that use actually benefits the addressed center, and whether it is warranted in the relevant comparison. A favorable answer to one does not settle the others. The proposed common explanation is that actual interactions can form, sustain, redirect or destroy the conditions for each subsequent capacity. Its useful content is the identified dependency and failure boundary; mere persistence or a favorable terminal score is insufficient.

Historical domination, cooperation and civilizational development require their own mappings and evidence. Neither exactly two attractors nor inevitable favorable direction is a premise here. Proving an attractor requires a specified dynamical system and the relevant approach or invariance property, beyond naming a feedback pattern.

## 6. Tests of adequacy and possible revision

The following failures have different implications:

1. If a measured material, sensor or coupling fails the declared recipe, that realization must be revised or rejected. Other realizations of the abstract law remain separate possibilities.
2. If independently established sufficient premises hold but the claimed consequence fails, the theorem, its implementation, or its empirical correspondence is wrong. An unmeasured rescue condition cannot be added without recording the changed claim.
3. If the proposed common root contributes only a relabeling of arbitrary transitions, its explanatory adequacy is unestablished even when the representation theorem remains correct. Useful constraints and checked causal connections must do work beyond that representation.
4. If a simpler account matches the established explanatory reach with fewer independent commitments, a claim of minimality must be weakened or the additional commitments justified. Unique minimality is not currently established.
5. If a self-renewing, accurate system is exploitative or indifferent, favorable or ethical entailments fail. Such a system is compatible with an account explicitly intended to explain constructive and destructive regimes.

The [cybernetics comparison](CYBERNETICS.md) records exact conventional counterparts for several mechanisms and earlier work on adaptation and observers. Those counterparts are useful checks. An integrated account can contribute through explicit formation, resource, information and cross-center connections without claiming that established controllers cease to apply. Scientific distinctiveness requires identified claims that competing explanations answer differently, rather than a new name for the same transition kernel.

Completion therefore requires the connected derivation and identified scope in the governing goal. This ledger advances the explicit-grounding obligation by fixing the levels and relative dependencies; it leaves the remaining construction, shared-significance and empirical identification work visible.

## 7. The connected explanatory argument

The present candidate combines **constraints on every admitted history** with **constructions of particular developmental histories**. The first part explains why some proposed continuations are impossible or require additional support or information. The second explains how a capacity can arise, operate and change under an explicit local law. Neither part alone implies that every system learns or that successful learning becomes concern. This section draws together existing results; it is not a new all-in-one formal theorem.

### From organization to operational significance

Begin with a specified activity and identify the structures and relations that actually enable it. In the repair-and-capture construction, a usable relation to the source changes which later operations and reconstructions are possible. The [local-store bound](LEARNING-INTERFACE.md#5-joining-the-general-account-to-actual-formation) makes this dependence substantive: with an initially empty local store, a local expenditure requires an actual preceding capture, and arbitrarily much common fuel cannot substitute for the missing access relation under that law. Thus aggregate stock does not determine the organization's operational possibilities.

Calling this dependency *operationally significant* identifies an actual difference to the organization's continuation. It does not yet say that the organization detects it, that a reward signal exists, or that its continuation is morally good. Regulation requires an additional causal path: the dependency changes accessible conditions, these conditions alter a response, and the response changes the enabling relation or its support. The [accepted regulation construction](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REGULATION-RETENTION-INTEGRATION.md) supplies that path with actual formation and costs. The reference activity identifies what the explanation concerns; an analyst's preferred score does not enter the process as a hidden selector.

The general support theorem then constrains the entire history, including formation and repair. For every identified account satisfying the local law, each prefix obeys `demand + remaining reserve ≤ initial reserve + provision`. Where an operation consumes support before replenishment occurs, the event law or its finer protocol must retain that order; the prefix inequality alone does not specify the order of consumption and provision within one primitive event. Under the stated resource guards, an operation requires sufficient incoming support. Later replenishment cannot supply it retrospectively. Different indispensable supports must be represented separately. This connects local operational significance to an informative common restriction without assuming a universal utility or treating a particular chemistry as universal.

Useful regulation also need not select its producer. In the [shared-return population analysis](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md), repair can increase total reproduction while equal per-capita copy and loss rates give zero drift in the useful type's stopped expected fraction. Actual differential access to captured support can change that drift; catalytic loss and turnover can remove or reverse the advantage. This is a separately reviewed analytical result for its stated population and stopped-frequency statistic, not a Lean theorem or a claim about the later formed-variant model's expected-count statistic. It identifies the missing causal step between producing useful consequences and being differentially retained.

### From consequential experience to learning and changed selectors

Learning from an earlier consequence after its stimulus is no longer directly present requires an effective retained difference. Immediate feedback can operate while the stimulus is present without durable memory. The [accessible-revision theorem](LEARNING-INTERFACE.md#3-indistinguishability-constrains-revision) states a necessary condition: the same deterministic update mechanism, inherited memory and complete observation history yield the same commands. If two situations require incompatible responses for a declared continuation, a mechanism cannot reliably address both while those inputs remain identical. Complete input includes every material channel that affects the update; matching visible reports alone does not establish the premise.

The formation, [prediction/action](PREDICTION-ACTION.md) and [learning-route revision](LEARNING-ROUTE-REVISION.md) constructions supply positive instances beyond this obstruction. Actual consequences change stored responses; diagnostic evidence can also trigger replacement of the mechanism that changes those responses, with the local construction and resource requirements satisfied. The replacement operates later and can be copied into a successor. The organizing explanation is that an updater is itself an implemented, vulnerable formation whose changes occur through the same process as other events. It need not be controlled by an unexplained external chooser at each successive level. Which replacements are proposed, usable and retained still follows from the supplied local law, evidence and selection conditions.

The [acquired-record continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ACQUIRED-RECORD-INTEGRATION.md) derives an additional interface under that same law: the current native score equals replay of the actual consumed writes from its inherited score, and the resulting command becomes the issued action whenever work is enabled. A concrete acquired prefix establishes provenance rather than assuming a freshly correct record at the join. The extracted transcript is not an added installed log. Outcome-sensitive continuation semantics preserve effects that feasible-word summaries erase; neither the native command record nor a possibility profile is a complete future model. The sensing, initial prior, update recipe, command coupling and schedule remain supplied. This is local premise discharge, not their assumption-free origin.

The [receipt-identification continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECEIPT-IDENTIFICATION-INTEGRATION.md) now derives the decoder's exact validity condition and the existing protocol's local freshness guarantee. A successful receipt identifies the event-time regime; a failed receipt with unknown availability permits both regimes. The existing work law supplies availability true, and its observed phase allows only write as an enabled next event, so an actual next write uses evidence still current under that law. Neither affordability nor occurrence follows. After finish, a legal reversal retains the record and changes its target. Thus correctness and freshness can be discharged locally from earlier actual steps, while the origin of the faithful receipt law, guaranteed availability and phase guards remains supplied. Empty reports for all 32 new theorems do not discharge those physical premises.

The [nondual continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CORE-TREE-CONTINUATION.md) now derives the receipt, retained revision, fuel/packet effects and support renewal from one actual episode. Their relationship was already present in the local process and was obscured by foregrounding the isolated decoder. The existing law's origin remains unexplained by that model; this is not proof of an external ontological source. A further proof transforms generated-branch reuse into its original derivations while retaining every local condition. Such rearticulation discharges no unproved physical or ethical premise, but it rules out treating root and branch status as independently justified layers of the formal theory.

These claims retain distinct strengths. A possible replacement history establishes construction; a selected or differentially retained replacement requires the corresponding choice or retention result; sustained operation requires its continuing support contract. A fixed underlying law is compatible with changing implemented selectors, but does not guarantee that useful selectors form. The actual constructions and adverse comparisons establish which changes occur in their declared classes.

Joining descriptions requires more than matching event possibilities and total costs. [Controlled refinement](CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md) preserves the complete observations and inherited memory needed for actual controlled histories; its counterexample preserves world possibilities and fuel costs while erasing a distinction that changes a command. Likewise, correspondence between completed operations does not establish that an issued operation returns. [Protocol completion](PROTOCOL-COMPLETION.md) supplies a sufficient scoped contract using actual enabledness, preservation of an admitted domain and strict internal progress. The [composition map](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) identifies which present connections are actual joined histories, which use conditional transport and which remain separate realizations. These interface results do not assert that every accepted branch can be concatenated unchanged.

### From learned consequences to concern and wisdom

The same causal account can include several centers with different consequences and enabling conditions. Another center's loss need not impair the actor's private continuation. Therefore the preceding support and learning results cannot, by themselves, force other-directed evaluation. This is a substantive boundary of the derivation, demonstrated by the matched filtering and exclusion cases, rather than a reason to remove the other center from the account.

[Evaluative uptake](EVALUATIVE-UPTAKE.md) adds a specific developmental connection: a coefficient acquired from the consequences of resource-consuming operations enters the learner’s assessment of action through a specified help route. It changes selected action while the exclusion control retains the same factual information. [Evaluative succession](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) carries that acquired influence through retained supports to a distinct child, fresh evidence about effects on the modeled recipient, and actual use under the stated resource conditions. These results explain a finite causal route to consequential influence. The transfer relation, sign and scale remain supplied; the studies do not identify the coefficient with welfare or establish the origin of every evaluative connection.

The accepted [plasticity construction](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) now removes one of those supplied operative inputs in a sibling continuation of the same acquired factual boundary. Paired operations and record acquisition bind initially absent signed components under the specified construction and resource rules. Their occupancy and sign then determine how acquired influence enters assessment. Further acquired data can guide retirement and replacement of a component when the required means are available. This is formation and change of the operative connection under fixed socket, coding, calibration, plasticity and gain laws. It does not derive those laws or automatically inherit the separate successor contract. The matched replacement preserves new observations, native updating, resource consumption and the means to perform the service, yet can suppress a command that would still produce its intended effect on the identified recipient. Thus change in evaluative influence and warranted revision require different explanations.

The [application argument](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md#when-the-application-is-functionally-recognized) supplies the next positive implication at a different level. An independently justified, actor-addressed patient ground can combine with sound acquired means knowledge that actually controls assessment and action. With applicable authority, the complete relevant comparison and feasible execution, this can constitute bounded functional recognition and realization of the ground. The patient ground cannot be inferred from the mechanism's persistence, and an observer's approval of a coincidentally helpful output does not establish recognition by the actor.

Wisdom, in the proposed account, adds revisable understanding and maintenance of effective correction. Means, a claim's present application, authority and competing reasons may change separately. The [existing correction witness](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md#an-existing-witness-of-correction-and-feasible-choice) shows a premise revision, with its resource requirements met, affecting feasible choice while an independent reason survives. Its physical and strategic contracts are distinct from the acquired evaluator's lineage. Their conceptual connection is warranted dependency-sensitive action; their full physical combination is not established by this paragraph. The ethical defense of the grounds and the broader acquisition and selection of wise inquiry remain substantive tasks.

This explains why the same general architecture admits constructive and destructive paths. A process can preserve its own operation while blocking another center's influence, maintain a false record, sustain a mistaken successor, or continue an efficacious action after a particular claim has been fulfilled. An intervention must change the relevant causal or practical dependency, preserve unaffected grounds, and remain executable. More reserve addresses a resource shortage; it does not correct a false observation or justify authority. More accurate means knowledge does not by itself settle whether acting is still called for. The contrasts in §5 identify these different intervention targets.

### What this adds to simpler descriptions

| Description | What it answers | What must be added for the present developmental question |
|---|---|---|
| Bare state-transition presentation | Which histories a supplied relation admits | Independently identified constraints, actual mechanisms and a tested interpretation; representability alone predicts no formation or concern |
| A supplied feedback controller with a fixed objective | How its installed observation, update and action rules respond | The formation, conditions of operation, replacement, inherited state and evaluative application of those rules wherever their origin or continuation is claimed |
| The current accountable developmental account | How specified structures acquire, use, revise and transmit consequential distinctions, and where information, material access or application fails; plasticity now supplies a conditional origin of operative connection occupancy/sign | The conditional formed-influence-to-warranted-repair argument in §8 is accepted; the whole candidate has passed the fixed-criterion assessment at its declared scope. Additional composition and empirical identification are needed for stronger claims that rely on them. Explicit local-law and normative premises, conventional equivalence and counterexamples remain |

An ordinary control or dynamical model extended to preserve all these same structures and laws can be equivalent to the construction. Several exact conventional comparators already are. The proposed contribution is the connected explanation and the dependencies it makes testable, not a claim that the same transition kernel computes something different when renamed. A simpler account that preserves the required explanation should be preferred or incorporated.

Universality therefore attaches to the independently specified implications and their faithful realizations. Concrete histories establish nonempty constructive subclasses; they do not establish universal prevalence or inevitable favorable selection. The [grounding argument](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md), summarized in §8, now closes a conditional connection from formed influence to an independently warranted role-bound response. It does not establish general acquisition of ethical validity or reliable reason tracking by this associative recipe. The [whole-account assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) now accepts this conditional candidate under the unchanged five criteria. That assessment preserves the independently stated premises, actual composition boundaries and measured evidence scopes; it does not infer universal empirical validity or generally reliable moral learning.

## 8. Formed influence and independently justified application

The [accepted grounding argument](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) now joins actual link formation to the positive application result. A justified responsibility for an identifiable uncompensated deprivation supplies a repair reason; adequate means, legitimate authority and a complete priority comparison can warrant the service independently of what the learner chooses. The selected physical history separately derives positive-link formation, acquired command use and delivery under the specified resource conditions. Nonconducting and filtered controls establish causal mediation. Under that interpretation, a generated disposition therefore realizes a warranted role-bound response. Responsibility may belong to a learner, deploying operator or institution; the supplied schedule alone does not decide this, and any delegation must be justified.

This closes a conditional explanatory connection, not a derivation of ethical priority from conditioning. Its matched replacement also supplies a failure: the same acquired observations and resource use can change the effective sign and suppress a remedy whose required means remain available while the independently justified ground persists. Valid technical invalidation can preserve the ground while blocking one use; an own-association change alone supplies no normative defeater. The bounded wisdom and generic correction results retain their established scopes and separate realizations. The common account can consequently explain generated appropriate response and mislearning without assuming every acquired evaluator becomes wise.

The normative repair premise, its real application, local formation law, correspondence and authority inputs remain explicit. The [source assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING-ASSESSMENT.md) and [separate review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING-REVIEW.md) record what the connection establishes. No new physical or empirical result follows from this synthesis, and the unchanged whole-foundation conditions require their own final assessment.

## 9. Selection, communication and maintained correction

The [V7.3 integration](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) extends the explanatory map through the social continuation of formed capacities. Its six mechanisms are inherited conditional results and interpretive connections, with the following additional local inputs. None is a new premise that every system must instantiate.

| Local input | Conditional explanatory consequence | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|
| Additive identical host benefit and a translation-invariant relative selection law | Shared support drops out of relative-share change; costly provision needs a differential route | Actual survival and opportunities still depend on shared conditions; unequal exposure and correlated advantages can change selection |
| Specified deterministic reinvestment and finite differentiable continuation returns near the symmetric reference | Relative return exceeding common return narrows the working objective despite fixed held concern | Does not identify factual belief, acquired value or ethical reason; reverse returns can widen the objective |
| Specified conversion network, correction rates and admissible initial states | Full incoming-gain bounds certify eventual domain recovery | Inherited support can create unsafe transients; exactly absent alternatives, actual funding and legitimate authority remain separate |
| Identified disuse/recovery law and a safe competence threshold | Delegation can make safe reversal temporarily unavailable | Parallel maintenance or recovery can prevent permanent loss; delegation of authority is a different relation |
| Defined sender/receiver utilities, information and cheap-talk equilibrium | Relative accounting can reduce informative communication | Verified channels and costly signals are outside the theorem; strategic credibility does not establish truth or moral validity |
| Declared slow field dynamics, recruitable alternatives, executable reform and sufficient transition/maintenance support | A sufficiently long intervention can enter and maintain a protected region | Safe endpoint, safe path, selection of the intervention and legitimacy are distinct; failure of a sufficient certificate is not impossibility |

The current market model already joins paid influence, rule erosion, correction funding and recovery; the shared-return analysis already separates collective production from producer selection. The additional integration preserves these contributions and connects them to working objectives, multi-domain control, competence and communication. It does not identify continuous fields, discrete market rounds and material histories with explicit resource use merely through shared notation.

For a further composition, map actual carriers, material returns, held evaluations, information, clocks, funds, authority and affected centers. In particular, show how an acquired evaluative state enters choice under changed continuation returns without replacing the inherited state or silently supplying a new reward. The [mechanism audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/V73-MECHANISM-AUDIT.md) and [evidence/interface assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/V73-EVIDENCE-AND-INTERFACES.md) specify remaining identification tasks and counterexamples. Ethical grounding concerns why a patient supplies a reason; these mechanisms explain how such a reason can gain or lose practical influence. Neither question can substitute for the other.

## 10. Noninstrumental concern in a concrete response process

The [F14 extension](FORMAL-CORE.md#f14-noninstrumental-patient-reasons-and-paid-response) explicitly connects [the patient-ground candidate](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md) to the shared process and resource core. C1 interprets an independently identified patient burden as locally bad; C2 interprets eligible relief as a contributory moral merit for the actual responder without requiring prior private adoption. These are separately defended hypotheses, not consequences of a reward signal, successful delivery or an empty custom-axiom list.

The concrete process supplies one responder, two patients, faithful reads, current-condition checks, permission interpretation, a formation recipe, costs and a specified response plan. Its new proofs give a legal trace, relief of the same patient in the same episode and an exact resource account. Private metadata changes preserve the C2-supported baseline under fixed operative conditions. The causal law also admits a zero-merit interpretation without C2, preserving the distinction between implementation and justification.

An arbitrary current cache does not supply its acquisition provenance; explicit empty-cache histories do. Plan cost is sufficient and exact for the designated plan, not a minimum for all typed states. Supplied scheduling proves possible execution, not universal helping. The model does not acquire C2 as an internal reason or automatically compose with the earlier plasticity history. The [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN-REVIEW.md) and [integration record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md) accept this conditional connection while retaining patient realization, normative sufficiency and mechanism formation as root obligations. The stronger assumption-elimination requirement remains unmet.

The [subsequent elimination attempt](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md#9-实际尝试从根基消除-c2) proves that event-anchor coverage and fixed-event operator locality together are equivalent to C2. Their conjunction therefore cannot be counted as fewer independent normative premises merely because the old parameter disappears. Generated relation records can preserve full-history relabeling while restricting reasons, and event-only factorization still permits all reasons to be false. The new actual-service adapter preserves explicit same-event compatibility across local instances without making them one joint resource history. Twenty-four new declaration audits and independent finite checks establish this dependency analysis, not the patient-ground or its universal practical scope. Those grounds, experiential realization, supplied formation laws and the formal background remain obligations.
