# 生生不息 (Shēngshēng-Bùxī) — Canon and History (part b: 史料 archive, 六表, 史 archive, repo READMEs)

> A process is "open" (开) when it still has an unexhausted gap it can generate through (未尽 → 间), can actually reach some legal continuation (可), can respond to disturbance (应), and can repair or regenerate after damage (转). Everything else in the theory is a specialization of this one predicate: 闭 is its loss (in seven distinguished modes), 正 is action that stays open without seizing (夺), faking (伪), freezing (冻) or hiding (蔽) and that survives four-fold audit; 邪 is partial self-continuation purchased by another's closure; 义 is choosing 正 at a branch point (机); 善 is reshaping the selection field so that 正 becomes easier and 邪 harder to sustain; 仁 is one's own continuation being genuinely contained in the shared continuation; 道 is continuing rightly without seizing the shared continuation; 真道 is 道 that holds across all *relevant* scales and horizons and survives audit. The canon claims this whole ladder can be generated from single Chinese characters (single-character root law), typed, given three-valued judgments with an explicit "pending" verdict, closed under least/greatest fixed points, run as a decision protocol on real data, stress-tested against eleven counterexamples, and — in its formal wing — anchored in an (Z/2)⁸ algebra of 256 cells (64 hexagrams × four V₄ time-modes) in which 道 is literally the group identity.

## 2. Mechanism

**The one loop.** A field state Γ = (A, R, σ, ρ, 𝒞) always has some manifested-but-unexhausted region (未尽 = 显 ∧ ¬尽). Wherever that holds there are *gaps that can be generated through* (间, "interval" — the theory's primitive). A gap becomes a **candidate interval** (候间) if it is realizably generative (可生, with a finite path, each step legal, key traces checkable, audit unbeaten), and a **legal interval** (i ∈ I_F) if it additionally does not contradict the field (合) and can be admitted under some law (法). Admission runs the partial operator 生合: Γ' = Γ ⊕_F i, defined only for legal intervals, non-commutative and non-associative, with closure (I_F = ∅) as an absorbing state. That loop — field → gap → law → generation → new field, with traces accumulating into 积 and 史, out of which 史法 is re-extracted — is the whole engine. Everything else is a predicate on it.

**Genesis inside the loop.** Within one field, activation weight w_Γ crossing a manifestation threshold gives 元 (a stirring, not a substance); a directional gradient in that weight gives 几; the accumulated gradient over an orbit gives 势 (direction *without* purpose — a fixed target would terminate the process, which is why the theory's Lean kernel proves `shi_no_telos`); and a state from which two legal intervals lead to *different openness profiles* is 机, a bifurcation. Choice at 机 is where agency enters. 聚散 is the same dynamics seen as form condensing and dissolving; only when a form's invariants enter no successor at all is it 真坍.

**The judgment.** 开 is a four-part conjunction — 未尽 ∧ 可 ∧ 应 ∧ 转 — deliberately *not* an option count. 闭 is its failure in seven named modes, the deepest being 元闭, freezing of the law that generates legality. The moral vocabulary is then read as openness under increasingly demanding conditions: 正 adds non-seizure, non-fakery, non-freezing and audit survival; 义 localizes 正 to a bifurcation; 善 lifts it from a single act to a change in the *selection field* (正筛: raises P(正行), lowers P(邪行), itself audited); 仁 states the containment 己续 ⊆_实 共续 without 夺依; 道 states persistent generation that does not seize the shared continuation; 真道 quantifies 道 over all *relevant* scales and horizons and requires audit survival. Value therefore has an interval structure, not a point: 好 = 平 ∨ 和 ∨ 正危, bounded below by the death of stagnation and above by capture-by-violence.

**Why audit is constitutive, not decorative.** Every one of these predicates is only as good as its evidence, and the theory refuses to let evidence be optional. 四校 (dissent admitted, affected parties heard, traces checkable, independent corroboration) plus 审 (source independence, no expulsion of the affected, no concealed trace, no captured corroboration) plus 审校² (the audit process must itself survive audit) is what turns 开/正/善/道 from rhetoric into checkable claims. And a fifth thing keeps the whole thing honest: three-valued logic with a real ⊥ ≠ U distinction, so that unproven never becomes proven — 「未知非假，未校非真」.

**Recursion and its danger.** The value predicates cite each other (正 mentions 夺 mentions 开; 好 mentions 坏 mentions 邪). Rather than hand-waving, v13.2 lifts them into a complete lattice with a monotone operator and takes lfp for conservative judgment, gfp for persistence judgment, and *neither* when monotonicity fails: then the verdict is 未定 and no value claim may be made. The polarity table records for each predicate which dependencies are positive and which are negative, and 分层修正律 gives three ways to break negative cycles — stratify (facts first, values second), assign U, or import new external evidence.

**How it runs.** 补篇〇 makes the whole thing executable: openness becomes a four-dimensional real feature vector; three Ω models and three Ω_B models are offered with an explicit rule that the choice is recorded and disagreement forces U; Π_开 becomes a projection *package* with double thresholds and an explicit "otherwise U" band; audits get a data schema and a min-based quality score; thresholds get a five-step calibration process with three risk bands. The recommender-system case shows the whole chain running from Γ through I_F and ⊕_F to labels — and then refuses the strongest label, holding at 候选真道 because the three-year horizon is prediction, not observation.

**The two counterexample families the sources raise against themselves.**
*(a) "Openness is just more options."* The theory's own answer is 机闭: many legal intervals, none responsive, repairable or recoverable — option-rich and dead. And symmetrically, 平静之死: low tension with no openness is not calm, it is a corpse.
*(b) "Then all closure is evil, and all novelty good."* Answered by 护闭 (bounded, reversible, non-seizing, audited closure is 正) and by 创新之妄 (novelty without evidence, trial-bounds, reversibility or audit does not qualify as 开维法). The pair is what keeps the theory from collapsing into either conservatism or accelerationism.
Two more: *(c)* "co-openness" that erases difference is 同化, not 和 — which is why 存异 (差异可显 ∧ 可通 ∧ ¬同化) is written into 和's definition; *(d)* an audit whose sources are captured, whose affected parties are expelled, whose traces are concealed and whose dissent is inadmissible is 伪校 — four boxes ticked, nothing checked.

This is claimed *complete* only for the static abelian closure; the moment the same substrate becomes a machine (BaguaTuring's 12-instruction ISA), unbounded iteration reintroduces halting undecidability. That boundary is the exact content of 道/理二分: 理 is the encodable, runnable, semi-decidable object layer; 道 is the meta-layer that can talk about 理's totality and its edge — and 理 provably cannot internalize 道's full discriminative power as a single universal judge (`daoJudge_not_universal`).

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised inside the corpus

1. **v13.2 alone is content-incomplete.** The 编校说明 itself lists ~18 things v13.2 weakened or dropped relative to the two supplementary texts; that is why the collation preserves all three rather than merging. `00_编校说明/01_编校说明.md`
2. **The collation is not yet a book.** 合订版结语 names two remaining tasks: single-text coherence (deduplicate, unify terminology, re-order) and empirical calibration of ℋ_t, thresholds, and the stability of the three Ω / three Ω_B families across scenarios. `05_合订版结语/00_合订版结语.md`
3. **Six things stay pending by design.** 邪续, 开势投影, 审校数据, 正邪阈值, 度期计算, 经验校准 — none may be promoted to truth without empirical calibration. `01_正篇…/21_附录甲_形式项总清.md`; `README.md`
4. **Four v10-era theorems were never formalized.** 绝闭 ⇒ 非开 (non-converse), 护闭 ≠ 成闭, 自由 ⇏ 任意, 正危 without four-fold audit degrades to 乱 — the appendix marks these as "尚未在 Lean 中逐条形式化…是开放课题". `00_编校说明/02_词汇迁移与早期方向.md`
5. **Eight-domain application testing is still open.** Life, mind, community, market, civilization, AI, ritual/institutions, art/beauty — only the recommender case and some Lean stubs exist. Same source.
6. **27 characters lack sense-splitting.** Including the most load-bearing ones (生, 开, 闭, 正, 邪, 真, 道, 心, 理, 法, 性, 礼, 信); the current claim is only that they have a core and a path back to the root, not that context fixes which sense applies. `史料/atom-derivation-report.md`
7. **The kernel's Lean layers stop short of four named layers.** 序之上升 (emergent levels), 因生因, 宇宙即理, 是为吾性 remain candidate layers 17–20 in the kernel plan. `daoli-v12-yi-zi.md`
8. **The kernel's monads are unproved.** Effect monad laws (令…则…), aspect monad laws (既/方/将), explicit β-like reduction rules, the formal 自指 closure proof, the 一元 trace-back unification proof, the categorical adjoint proof for the 生生 cycle, a Lean encoding of 名实合一, and a working 文言 interpreter are all listed as 待 complete. `daoli-v12-he.md`
9. **Lean cannot verify 法则 62.** The lean-verifier document states plainly that internal coherence and cross-frame connectedness are verifiable but empirical correspondence is not, so the framework's own truth condition is only two-thirds machine-checkable. `daoli-v12-lean-verifier.md`
10. **法则 53's 56 frame-transformation directions are not all verified.** Only 5 are worked; the rest rest on the general claim. `daoli-v12-frames.md`
11. **間's feature character is unsettled.** v12 commits 間 → 关系 with a note that 关系 overlaps the aspect itself; candidates 通 / 络 / 距 are flagged for v12.1. `daoli-v12-main.md`; `史/daoli旧版/daoli-v12.md`
12. **The 動 reframe is incomplete.** v12 commits 動 = continuous transformation with 势, but all earlier v8–v11 readings of 動 have not been systematically updated. Same sources.
13. **The necessity type is undistinguished.** The Urbit document leaves standing the question of whether "necessary form of generation" means logical, metaphysical, or structural necessity — and notes that P1 (a taxonomy) and P5 (proving 不易 *emerges* from generation necessity rather than being an added commitment) are the highest-leverage open items. `urbit-jian-isomorphism.md`
14. **The homoiconicity claim is aspirational.** `KernelDanZi` / `Field` / `ZhongOrbit` are three types with mappings, not a collapse; the honest verdict recorded is "我们在这个维度上也是 1.5-D — Urbit 至少有 'everything is a noun' 的工程现实, 我们目前有的是 philosophical aspiration". Same source.
15. **Uniqueness of the frozen-kernel insight is weakened by the corpus's own review.** ZFC frozen + mathematics generating, and the Lean kernel frozen + Mathlib growing, are the same pattern — so "frozen rule + endless generation" is the general pattern of formal foundations, not a distinctive discovery. Same source.
16. **The repository's headline numbers were stale and are flagged as such.** ~150 sorries and ≥1 axiom against a README that once claimed 0/0. `README.md`; `README.en.md`
17. **Heisenberg-Weyl is unbridged.** The G11 group is closed abstractly but not connected to Cell256 / R₈ / hexagrams, with the narrative document still in draft. `README.md`
18. **The 三轴 27 格 has 7 uncovered cells.** ~26% of the modal × temporal × epistemic product needs compounds; the gaps cluster in negated-modal and past-几 regions. `六表_实虚史真/表五_三轴27格.md`

### New (my reading)

19. **Two version numbering systems collide and no reconciliation document exists in this cluster.** The 生生不息论 track (v5…v14) and the 道理 track (v11, v11.1, v12) run in parallel and are merged only implicitly through the kernel documents. A reader arriving at "v12" cannot tell which v12 is meant without checking the directory. The 史料 README's supersession table is the closest thing to a bridge and it does not mention that the two v12s are different objects.
20. **v5 is cited but not archived here.** `daoli-v12-yi-zi.md` quotes v5 with line numbers throughout (v5 §二 l. 59, §七 l. 189, §二十一 l. 645, §二十四 l. 904), and v5's section numbering matches neither included supplement. The Lean kernel layering is derived *from v5*, so the text that grounds the machine-checked chain is not in this cluster.
21. **The "one truth, many articulations" claim (法则 53 strict) and the "audit can defeat any claim" norm are in tension.** If frame transformations preserve truth-content exactly, then a claim refuted in one frame is refuted in all — which is a strong falsifiability commitment the framework never cashes out. Conversely, if a derivation survives in one frame and fails in another, 法则 53 strict is false. No procedure is given for detecting this case.
22. **The theory-mapping claims are unfalsifiable as stated.** "Newton ≡ 阳明心学 as specific cell selections" and the sample transform (F = ma → "environment + mind-state → progress of cultivation") are analogies presented as truth-equivalences. The verifications document supplies real content for the Fourier case (Parseval) but the other 18 theory mappings have no comparable test. This is exactly the failure mode the theory's own 伪校 counterexample warns about (four boxes ticked, nothing checked).
23. **The audit machinery has no cost model.** 四校 + 审 + 审校² + 度期周 is expensive; a system that cannot afford full audit will systematically produce U, which is safe but paralyzing. The threshold document handles risk bands but not audit *budget*, and there is no account of when partial audit licenses a provisional non-U verdict.
24. **Conjecture 2 (大同 as historical residue) is where the corpus is least careful.** It infers a long selection history from the precision of a symbol system, but the precision is largely a property of the *reconstruction* (Cell256, OXNotation, the 43 CoreAtoms) rather than of the transmitted classical texts. The corpus's own norm — 「未校非真」 — cuts against stating it as strongly as the README does, and the README does correctly tier it as conjecture; the risk is that the surrounding prose does not read as conjectural.
25. **The "元 is not first" move is stated but not enforced everywhere.** 间生论 and 致知版 both argue that 元 depends on 间, yet the Lean kernel takes 動 as the primitive axiom and derives 元 from it, and the atom roster keeps 元 as one of the 43 CoreAtoms directly under 一元. Three different roots (间, 動, 一元) are in play across the layers without an explicit reconciliation.
26. **好 as an interval assumes a single well-ordered tension magnitude ‖势‖.** For a field with multiple polarities whose tensions differ in kind (not just degree), 好 = 平 ∨ 和 ∨ 正危 would have to be evaluated per-polarity, and no aggregation rule is given — the same failure mode Ω was given three models to avoid.
27. **The 256-cell hexagram glosses do real conceptual work but have no derivation.** Assignments like 需 = Future T, 师 = quotient, 泰 = g∘f, 中孚 = bisimulation are proposed per-cell with a prose justification each; nothing constrains them beyond plausibility, and nothing checks them for global coherence (e.g. that the V₄ involutions act on the *concepts* the way they act on the bits).
28. **The three-valued logic is stated but the decision protocols sometimes collapse it.** 卷十四's step lists say "判待校" in the right places, but 补篇〇's pseudocode has `label[i] = U` as the fall-through *else* branch, which conflates "genuinely indeterminate" with "not matched by any rule" — two different U's.

## 9. Publication readiness

**Publishable / teachable now.**
- **《生生不息论·间开本·致知版》** (`03_补篇一…/`, 13 files) — the single most publishable artifact in the cluster: complete, self-aware about its provisionality, with the three-phase presentation that makes it readable by philosophical, analytic and formal audiences at once, and a worked AI-alignment application. It would need only light editing (deduplication against 补篇二, consistent notation) to stand as a book.
- **《间生论·主篇》** (`04_补篇二…/`, 16 files) — publishable as-is as a short philosophical essay (~10k characters). It carries the whole doctrine with minimal machinery, has the best sentences, and answers the is/ought objection in a page.
- **卷十五 反例校验 + 补篇〇** — publishable together as a standalone methods paper: "eleven ways to break an openness criterion, and a protocol that survives them". This is the most defensible, most novel, most immediately useful piece.
- **《六表》表一–表五** — publishable as a compact linguistic/logical study: the modal / temporal / epistemic three-axis decomposition of classical Chinese, the 27-cell coverage analysis with its 74% single-character result and the seven named gaps, and the argument that 疑 was never collapsed into bivalence. This does not depend on any of the theory's larger claims.
- **The v9→v10 transition document** — publishable as a methodological case study in how a philosophical text was made formal: 32 named repairs, each with the defect, the fix and the reason.

**What is missing.**
- A **single coherent volume**. The collation is explicitly a 校勘合订本, not a book; the 合订版结语 says so. Terminology is not unified across 正篇 and the two supplements (焦/著, 场/境, 四校's two formulations, 好's two definitions), and the 编校说明's own table is the reader's only map.
- **Empirical calibration.** No ℋ_t is filled with real data; thresholds are anchored only by construction; the three Ω and three Ω_B families have never been compared on real scenarios. Every value verdict in the corpus is therefore either constructed or U.
- **The four v10 theorems**, formalized.
- **Eight-domain application tests** beyond the recommender case.
- **A reconciliation of the three roots** (间 / 動 / 一元) and of the two version tracks.
- **A falsification procedure for 法则 53 strict** — what observation would show two frames articulating different truths.
- **A cost model for audit**, so that resource-bounded agents can act on partial audit without either faking completion or freezing on U.

**The one piece of writing that would most advance this.**
A ~40-page paper, in English with Chinese terms preserved, titled something like *"Openness as a Judgment: a four-part criterion, seven closure modes, and an audit protocol"*. It would: (i) define 开 = 未尽 ∧ 可 ∧ 应 ∧ 转 and argue against the option-count reading; (ii) give the seven closure modes with real-world instances, with 元闭 and 伪开's three types as the payload; (iii) give 护闭 with its four binding conditions, so the criterion cannot be read as anti-conservative; (iv) run the eleven counterexamples as the robustness argument; (v) give the three-valued discipline and its 「未校非真」 norm; (vi) give 补篇〇's runnable protocol with one worked case; and (vii) close by deriving the alignment consequence (naive preference-alignment as type-III pseudo-openness; alignment as a field-level 正筛 problem; responsibility's third layer). This piece is *entirely* extractable from what already exists, needs no new philosophy, carries the cluster's most defensible and most useful content, and would give every other part of the corpus — meta-attractor, keep-the-possibility-space-open, alignment-selection-on-cognition, the-fork — a citable formal core. The 易 algebra, the 62 法则, the cross-cultural mappings and the two conjectures should all stay out of it; they are separate publications with separate evidentiary standards.
