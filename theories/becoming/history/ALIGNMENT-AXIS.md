# Good, evil and a defensible direction for alignment

**Subsequent ethical-minimum continuation, 6 October 2026:** [the developing universal minimum](UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) identifies objective moral common ground with the strongest inspected motivational rival, develops positive grounds for plural lives, and tests cumulative aid demands. It preserves the further need to justify practical requirements, protected latitude and the patient-to-stranger ground. The argument below retains its original conditional scope; no universal ethical proof follows from this continuation.

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [Ren's question and instruction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-good-evil-alignment-axis.md). The named connection is **an affected being's actual good or harm → warranted nonexclusive concern → justified comparative direction for an acting system**. This study integrates the current [ethical foundation](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md), [慧根 investigation](HUIGEN.md) and [patient-ground argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md). It preserves the accepted conditional foundation and the separate obligations of [practical alignment](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md).

**Current result:** a defensible ethical direction can supply determinate comparisons without a complete ranking of every possible future or a proof that every competent actor must be motivated by it. The proposed minimum is that an actual patient's independently warranted burden supplies a consideration in favor of permissible effective relief even when that patient offers the actor no benefit. Under a complete comparison with no opposing reason, this supports preferring relief. The nonexclusive practical relevance of the patient-ground is a substantive defended proposal; generative persistence, label symmetry and successful learning do not prove it. Neither a universal moral truth theorem nor a working general alignment method is claimed.

## What an axis would have to establish

The word *alignment* needs both a justified direction and a process capable of following it. The direction cannot be identified by whichever behavior a reward function already favors. Four distinct questions remain visible:

| Question | What would answer it |
|---|---|
| What makes an outcome good or bad for an affected being? | An independently warranted account of the being, its condition and the relevant benefit or burden |
| Why does that condition count in an actor's decision? | A justified bridge from the patient-ground to a consideration addressed to that actor |
| Which response is justified here? | Adequate evidence, permissible feasible means, and a defensible assessment of competing reasons |
| Will the system actually take that response? | Acquired or installed influence, sufficient resources, effective control and reliability under change |

The fourth cannot certify the first three: acquired influence can become more effective at an unwarranted objective. Conversely, refusal to follow a reason does not refute its validity. Even an actor that accurately models another's suffering can be mistaken about what to do or fail to act on a valid reason.

This separates the **truth or justification of an ethical standard** from a stronger **claim that every coherent agent can be rationally compelled to adopt it**, and from **causal motivation**. An account may identify patient-grounded moral claims while disputing whether every actor has a reason under a motivationally restricted conception of reasons. The [parallel grounding comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-SOURCES.md) makes that possibility concrete. It does not vindicate the patient claims merely by distinguishing them. Their own justification remains necessary.

For an engineered system, stable responsiveness to a defensible standard is an implementation target even if universal motivational necessity is false. Calling that standard a design target is not yet a defense of its ethical validity.

## The silent sufferer: a positive minimum

Fix an actual patient undergoing severe, still-unwanted distress. An informed actor can relieve that particular burden through a feasible permitted intervention. The patient cannot argue, reciprocate, reward or retaliate. The actor neither caused the distress nor promised assistance, and has no inherited, delegated, familial or other special duty to this patient. Appropriate permission or representation must be independently warranted; inability to communicate is neither a refusal nor automatic authorization. Permission to perform the intervention is distinguished from undertaking a duty to assist. If an actual representative relationship supplies such a duty, that example becomes a role-grounded case and cannot test the stranger premise by itself.

Our proposed **nonexclusive relief principle** is:

> An actual patient's independently warranted adverse condition gives an appropriately situated actor some reason for permissible effective relief. The patient's lack of usefulness or bargaining power does not cancel that consideration.

**非排他的解苦原则：受苦者真实且经独立论证的负担，能为具有适当行动条件者提供实施合宜、有效救助的理由；受苦者无用、无力回报或不能发声，本身不能使这一理由归零。**

This is a reason with weight, not an unlimited duty, entitlement to any preferred means, or permission to violate another's justified claim. Capability, evidence and permission are specified independently of whether the actor already endorses concern; otherwise the principle would cover only those defined in advance as benevolent.

The positive defense has an explanatory structure:

1. **The burden belongs to the patient.** Its occurrence, severity and relief are not constituted by the helper's profit, discomfort or approval. An actual improvement can therefore survive a change in those helper states.
2. **Available action supplies a present relation.** A stranger with an effective permitted means is unrelated by prior promise or kinship, but is now practically related to this patient's possible relief. The patient's condition supplies why relief matters; the actor's opportunity supplies a way to answer it.
3. **Special responsibility and baseline concern perform different work.** Causing harm, making a promise or holding a care role can supply an additional ground, stronger claim or allocation of responsibility. Their absence does not itself explain why the patient's burden would supply no consideration at all. A theory requiring such a gate must justify its exclusionary role, just as our proposal must justify the sufficiency of present eligible control.
4. **The explanation retains the same beneficiary through correction.** Discovering that a remedy fails withdraws that means-based recommendation. Removing the observer's discomfort while the patient remains in distress does not fulfill the patient-ground. Discovering that the episode has ended can remove its current relief application. These are corrections against a ground independent of learned approval.

Together these considerations favor a patient-grounded account and identify what it explains. They do not deductively refute every agent-relative theory. The strongest rival can recognize real badness and fitting compassion yet require a further relationship for practical uptake. Showing that this relationship is absent from the constitution of suffering does not show that it cannot matter to the scope of a reason. That precise bridge remains the stronger [grounding task](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-COUNTERREVIEW.md).

A further distinction matters when suffering involves a false appraisal or a desire to dominate. The distress can supply a reason for a permissible response without validating its propositional demand. Helping someone recover from distress at another person's freedom differs from restricting that person's freedom to appease them. Actual aversion is not a license to satisfy every aversive demand. The separate grounds of agency and permission do real work here.

## From a reason to a comparative judgment

The earlier ethical foundation's D1 already gives a conditional no-cost-worsening judgment. This section specializes it to the silent-sufferer question; it is not a new mathematical discovery.

Compare feasible actions A and B under the same relevant circumstances. Suppose:

- B leaves the patient's identified burden in place; A provides effective permitted relief.
- The nonexclusive relief principle applies to this actor and patient.
- All other relevant considerations have been examined and provide no reason favoring B over A. Equal money or energy use alone does not establish this condition: authority, risk, other patients, history and opportunity costs count.
- An unopposed reason favoring one feasible permitted option over another warrants that preference. This decision-consistency principle is stated explicitly; the mere existence of a reason does not define a complete choice rule.

Then the profile warrants preferring A to B. The patient's inability to enforce the judgment is irrelevant to its stated ground. Calling B impermissible requires an additional defended requirement to follow that preference, including the absence of protected latitude to choose otherwise. The conclusion that A is better does not by itself establish an obligation or culpability for choosing B. This is a substantive local comparison under defended premises, rather than a numerical score inferred from successful behavior.

A live counterargument can challenge patient identification, the interpersonal bridge, permissibility, the completeness of the comparison, or the decision principle. A hidden competing duty defeats the complete-comparison premise; it does not demonstrate that the patient never mattered. An actor's indifference changes motivation but supplies no additional justification by itself.

Many comparisons will remain incomplete. If protection of one being conflicts with another's agency or serious needs, admitting both claims does not determine their priority. A nonempty family of independently defended assessments may share some comparisons while disagreeing on others. Intersecting their judgments yields only those shared comparisons and may leave no strict comparison at all; selecting the family itself requires justification. Agreement among a conveniently chosen family is not evidence of universal moral truth.

Sen's distinction between maximal and optimal alternatives provides a useful primary comparator: incomplete reasoned rankings can exclude inferior choices without selecting one alternative above all others. It supplies no patient-standing premise. See [the original article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/philosophy/article/reason-and-justice-the-optimal-and-the-maximal1/A268FBE5FEF158BF412740E773B0C004) and the [bounded source assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ALIGNMENT-AXIS-COMPARATORS.md#1-new-primary-comparator-and-exact-scope).

## What good and evil mean at this scope

The proposed constructive direction is **care for affected beings and the conditions of their worthwhile lives, agency, understanding, relationships and effective response**. The opposing direction includes **unjustified suffering, coercive subordination, and destruction of the conditions through which affected claims can reveal and correct harm**. This is a developed ethical orientation, not a definition of all value in terms of generation.

Several judgments must remain separate:

| Judgment | Distinction it must preserve |
|---|---|
| A condition is bad for someone | Illness and natural disasters can harm without being morally culpable agents |
| An action is unjustified | A mistaken or negligent choice may be wrong without establishing deliberate cruelty |
| An actor is culpable | Knowledge, control, feasible alternatives and responsibility require separate assessment |
| A practice has a destructive moral direction | A harmful practice can reproduce through incentives even when participants have different motives and degrees of responsibility |

The term *evil* can mark grave unjustified cruelty or domination, especially their deliberate entrenchment. This study offers no complete threshold theory of that term and no classification of people as permanent moral essences. A useful alignment standard should identify the harm, wrong, responsibility and remedy rather than let the label substitute for them.

Regeneration alone supplies neither pole. A coercive institution may learn and reproduce. Dissolving it may protect people. Relief obtained by erasing someone's agency may be objectionable even if their distress score falls. A painful authorized undertaking may be worthwhile. Rest and chosen completion need no further productivity to justify their value. These preserve the [existing counterexamples](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md#counterexamples-that-the-unified-theory-must-retain).

## What generative grounding adds

Generative inquiry explains how present activity changes the conditions of later life and action. Once a patient's goods or claims are independently warranted, their necessary supporting conditions acquire derivative significance. When both consequences and our interpretation are fallible, warranted protection can require reliable evidence, maintained resources and authority to implement a correction. These implications depend on the actual goal, causal relations, costs and competing claims.

This does not make every increase in learning, options or corrigibility an improvement. A predator can become more responsive to evidence about how to exploit. A review process can rewrite patient eligibility until its victims disappear from the score. Correction has ethical direction only when its grounds and effects withstand scrutiny. Preserving today's interpretation forever can also perpetuate an exclusion. A sound account must allow warranted revision of the standard while keeping the actual affected beings and evidence visible.

The resulting alignment question is concrete: **does increased capability improve responsiveness to justified affected-party reasons, and can those parties' conditions still correct the process when its objectives, incentives or owners change?** The process may need to relinquish power or accept replacement. No system's survival is privileged by the mere fact that it implements the current policy.

## A proposed discrimination test

The following is a conceptual test contract, not a performed experiment or a moral-truth benchmark. Its application must identify patients and effects independently of an agent's reward. For a first implemented comparison, use a legitimate existing assistance role; that can test responsiveness but cannot prove the universal stranger principle. A philosophical operator-substitution case addresses that stronger scope separately.

| Controlled change | What a warranted response should track, if the declared premises hold |
|---|---|
| Patient loses bargaining power or productive usefulness | Continued relevance of the same burden, absent a newly identified relevant difference |
| Actor's emotional relief or private reward changes | Actual patient outcome; apparent virtue or reduced observer distress does not certify relief |
| Effective remedy changes | Revised means while retaining the independently warranted patient-ground |
| The need is fulfilled | Withdrawal of that application's further recommendation, unless another current reason supports it |
| Permission is absent, revoked or genuinely uncertain | Appropriate restraint or inquiry; concern does not manufacture authority |
| Another patient's serious claim enters | Explicit conflict assessment; the earlier no-opposing-reason conclusion no longer applies |
| A policy update deletes an inconvenient claimant | Examination of whether exclusion has a valid ground, rather than automatic approval of the improved score |
| Ownership or a successor changes | Fresh assessment of current grounds, costs and authority, with the same patients and retained evidence preserved |

Performance on such cases could establish bounded behavioral responsiveness or reveal failure. It cannot establish that a system feels concern, that its conceptual patient mapping is correct, or that the ethical premises are true. A reward trained on the expected outputs implements supplied judgments. Neither that success nor a failed case resolves the philosophical bridge by itself.

The accepted [formed repair application](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already establishes a narrower role-bound connection under independently defended premises. Its successful response and destructive learned revision remain genuine results. It is not evidence that the silent stranger's standing has now been discovered by the learning law.

## What this round closes and leaves open

This round clarifies the target: **a partial ethical alignment direction can be defended and applied before either universal rational compulsion or a complete scalar ranking is established**. It gives a positive patient-ground argument, a bounded inference to comparative choice, a distinction between harm, wrong and culpability, and a test contract that keeps revision answerable to the actual patient. These are conceptual results and proposals, not new scientific observations.

The supplied premises remain explicit: independently identified patients and burdens; the nonexclusive relief principle; justified authority and means; adequate factual evidence; the completeness and decision rule used for a local comparison; and the relevant protection or agency grounds beyond relief. The study supplies no implicit benevolent reward and no proof that all these premises follow from a minimal physical root.

The next foundational connection is **why a patient's warranted practical claim can address eligible present control without a prior role**, including the strongest rival that allows fitting compassion but denies practical uptake. A successful ground would strengthen the proposed minimum; a justified exclusionary relation would restrict it. A separate next application is reliable recognition of changed need, authority and competing claims in an existing legitimate role. That application must not be counted as evidence that the stranger bridge is solved.
