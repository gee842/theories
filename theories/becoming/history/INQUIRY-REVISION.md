## The concrete change

In the original continuing history, four encounters leave two occupied positive links, the learned coefficient at four, available fuel at fourteen and the affected support stock at thirty-six. The apparatus still requires six encounters before opening its response pathway. Its retained configuration already determines a favorable response score, but the assessment event remains unavailable before closure. It cannot use those consequences to revise that prerequisite. The [previous application](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md#completing-the-application-when-the-protocol-obstructs-repair) correctly established that stopping further teaching and initiating service are incompatible under that unchanged law.

The new extension continues from **that same state with the six-encounter requirement still active**. It then performs three actual operations:

1. One inherited spare component pair forms a contact for revision, consuming one fuel unit. No new material is introduced; the pair is no longer available for later link replacement.
2. The contact reads the occupied sockets, consuming one unit and recording the result in two existing cleared cells. Earlier encounters formed those sockets. Under the completed-prefix correspondence, two occupied sockets identify four units of unrecovered depletion. The read does not consult an observer's verdict, hidden efficacy or desired outcome.
3. When both acquired bits are present and the declared technical guards hold, a one-unit rewrite changes the **operative continuation parameter from six encounters to four**. It preserves the history, encounter count, learned coefficient, link signs, retained sources and affected stock.

The original admission and response laws now use that changed parameter. Another teaching encounter is disabled because the count has reached the new limit. Closure and the existing response pathway become enabled. The unchanged assessment costs two units; the existing successful response costs eight and restores ten units to the affected stock.

## What makes this evidence-dependent revision

A matched sham consumes the same three units and component pair but leaves the continuation parameter at six. Early response remains unavailable. Omitting the read, receiving missing or wrongly bound evidence, or acquiring a report below the installed threshold also leaves the old requirement in force. A subsequent inherited event invalidates a read; restoring permission or scope does not silently restore evidence. Fresh acquisition can make revision possible again, with its additional expense retained.

The reader is not an oracle. A coherent false-positive channel can produce the two bits on a genuine zero-depletion history and cause a schedule change unsupported by the claimed depletion evidence. The mechanism checks its acquired record and technical context, not hidden truth. Faithful calibration matters to the evidential interpretation; absence of this depletion does not exclude every other legitimate reason to stop.

Actual opposed and mixed histories provide a second limit. They form the same number of occupied sockets and produce the same depletion. Their evidence therefore causes the same schedule revision, but their unchanged learned link signs lead the existing evaluator to abstain. This extension changes access and inquiry; it does not guarantee that every learned response repairs the situation. Low fuel can likewise permit rewriting while leaving successful response unaffordable. If a sham or interrupted revision resumes teaching, the consumed spare component can prevent the old link replacement. These failures remain part of the result.

The finite enumeration covers all 256 four-encounter own/affected shortfall schedules. Of these, 176 reach a complete ready prefix; 80 are interrupted when a third required link cannot be formed. The latter are not misclassified using the completed-prefix evidence relation. Among the 176 complete histories, 96 have two occupied sockets and undergo the installed rewrite. The saved traces retain actual response successes, abstentions and resource failures separately.

## What this contributes to the theory

Earlier activity changes later possibilities twice in this construction. Its effects first participate in forming a retained arrangement. A subsequently formed contact makes that arrangement available for use in revising how activity continues. The consequence is no longer confined to a score used within an unchanged procedure: it changes the procedure's admission condition and opens an earlier response.

The distinction between acting, undergoing, retaining, reading and revising is therefore traceable within one continuing history. The separate registers let us inspect differentiated consequences; they do not posit independent actors and objects as universal primitives. Resource accounting is a property of this local realization, not the foundational meaning of activity or memory.

This closes the specific representational and implementation omission under the declared new laws. It does not overturn the old impossibility result, which concerns the frozen law. Nor does it re-open the already answered generic questions about learning, selected correction or renewal.

## Supplied relations and the next stronger question

The local extension supplies the component-formation recipe, contact calibration, validity discipline, two-occupied trigger and parameter-rewrite law. The capacity is formed from inherited material, and its use depends on an acquired report; the report does not invent its own interpretation or criterion. An independently defended mandate to install and use this rule remains an application premise. The inherited delivery-permission bit is a service interlock, not evidence of consent to teaching or self-created moral authority. A one-socket history can still carry a real claim even though this sufficient trigger does not fire.

These supplied relations are within the modeled activity; calling them supplied does not place them outside generative reality. Their acquisition or selection through a wider connected history is a stronger explanatory target. The [inquiry-interface reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-INTERFACE-ARGUMENT.md) already distinguished changing inquiry under a fixed rule from revising an operative criterion. This extension changes the actual encounter requirement under its declared evidence-to-rewrite rule. It does not yet show how competing rewrite criteria are generated and selected through their consequences in this same concern history.

That stronger question must preserve the present positive result and its adverse cases. A wider account would improve on it by forming or revising the criterion's scope while detecting misleading evidence, recognizing consequential cases below the current threshold and preserving independently grounded limits on intervention. The result does not establish universal favorable direction, ethical necessity or the stronger no-assumptions foundation.
