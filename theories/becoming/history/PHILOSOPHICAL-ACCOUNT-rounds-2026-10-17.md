## 30. Realized good, alternatives and the necessity boundary

Something can genuinely sustain a life even when another arrangement could have done so equally well. Its actual good is not erased by that alternative. But identifying this good does not prove that adding another arrangement improves the whole, or that a particular person must supply it. **Realization, comparative improvement and duty require distinct judgments within the same history.** The existing G0/J/E account supplies their disclosed grounds.

A serious welfare-based philosophy can accept this distinction too. Our disagreement cannot be established merely by showing equal totals or replaceable benefits. At biological scope, the direct question is whether actual nonconscious living has basic positive significance when it supplies no conscious welfare at all. G0 says it does: the living is already realized, rather than only awaiting someone else's appreciation. This need not assign welfare to every significant relationship or add a second value bonus to each conscious good.

## 33. Why a reason can outlast an inclination

The [practical-authority comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/SCIENTIFIC-COMPATIBILITY-NECESSITY.md#31-practical-authority-is-a-separate-compatibility-test) tests another commitment of the entire account. Recognizing a living good, having a reason to respond, wanting to respond and being authorized to require a response are distinct questions within the same formed relationship.

Where a justified bounded undertaking has changed another life's possibilities, losing interest does not itself restore those possibilities, fulfill the undertaking or arrange a replacement. Under J3, a reason for fulfillment, justified renegotiation or transition can persist. Actual replacement, changed capacity, conflicting basic needs or legitimate release can change what is required. The contributor's life remains included; responsibility does not become indefinite service. These are applications of the completed practical account, not new obligations inferred from causal connection alone.

The stronger rival can understand this history and still contest the reason's authority independently of antecedent concern. Present reluctance does not settle the issue: some accounts locate a continuing reason in deeper purposes, interests or sound reflection. They are not automatically incompatible with our conclusion. The relevant disagreement occurs when a rival denies a practical requirement that our account warrants at the same scope.

Defending reasons for belief therefore does not yet establish every practical reason. Even a shared kind of normative relation still needs its concrete instances justified. At the same time, our affirmative arguments deserve assessment together: living realization, actual loss, dependence, contributors' self-good and legitimate endings form one connected proposal. Their cumulative support is not erased because no single component proves the entire theory. Nor may their shared premises be counted repeatedly as independent evidence.

The test closes another route that cannot presently complete the universal claim: inquiry's authority alone has not established the entire practical package. The remaining work concerns the substantive grounds of actual denials, not mandatory use of our vocabulary or universal agreement. G0/J/E, the two completed answers and existing checked consequences retain their scopes.

**意愿变化不等于承担已经履行；真实关系变化可以改变责任。认识理由与实践理由同属一个展开，具体的规范根据仍须说明。肯定论证可以相互支持，不能以重复前提冒充普遍证明。**
