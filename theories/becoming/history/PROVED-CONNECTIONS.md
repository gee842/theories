## 1. Scope of the audit / 核查范围

A **proved composition** imports or explicitly transports the producer result into its consumer with matched meanings and guards. An **argued correspondence** explains how different representations or evidence concern the same relations, without claiming a checked whole-history refinement. A **supplied premise** is an explicit local input; it may have an explanation elsewhere and is not thereby external to generative reality. An **unresolved connection** names the missing explanation, realization or evidence at the stated scope. One connection may have more than one status.

The audit reads the [existing composition record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md), the [recent connected articulation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SHENGSHENG-ARTICULATION-RECORD.md), and the linked newer studies. ## 2. The new connection / 新接通的关系

The new [Lean source](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/systems-complexity/analysis/InquiryJudgment.lean) imports `ScaleRevision` and `PracticalJudgment`. The threshold claim is attached to the actual endpoint of the inherited event law. Failed candidates remain in the menu. The practical conclusion is derived from their consequences and the disclosed protection order, rather than inserted into eligibility.

The first general theorem closes a precise implementation condition: calibrated error and sufficient response supply imply the threshold **after the guarded, work-charged response event**, provided the process is active, at the response phase, the evidence is current and bound to the context, workspace fits, and the operation is funded. The earlier theorem established the response body's consequence; the new theorem includes its outer execution guards.

先把校准和可供回应的支持，与工作额度、工作空间、阶段和证据绑定接起来。它们共同保证模型中的实际回应；单独“算出了正确回应”不保证已经能够实行。

The concrete comparison retains the inherited start `(a,b,source)=(12,3,1)`. All three plans execute the same three inquiry/revision stages and one response stage. Each funded stage costs 1044 abstract work ticks. The common deadline is stage four. A successful claim requires `a≥1`, phase four, stage four and no stop. Thus a denied operation that stutters at the earlier `a=3` cannot masquerade as fulfilled continuation. The companion claim retains `b≥3` and nonnegative source. Neither threshold is a measured biological criterion.

The two cases share the actual dependence and differ in the readout relation. Treating both as live is a supplied epistemic commitment. Under J2's scoped protection priority, the fixed plan is the unique undefeated member of **this three-plan menu** when both cases remain live, regardless of secondary gains. Each competitor loses the continuation claim in one live case; the fixed plan retains both declared protected claims. The selected plan has an actual original-process trace, rather than merely a success label.

If the accurate case alone is warranted, the measured plan is also undefeated when secondary gains are equal. Excluding the biased case without justification can lose the actual condition. Expanding uncertainty can therefore change the practical verdict while leaving the target and event law unchanged. Every case separately having a successful measured variant does not establish one common successful measured variant.

两种情况各有成功方案，不等于事先有同一个共同成功方案。正确收窄误差范围可以恢复选择；任意排除不利情况只会使证明失去实际适用性。此处选定的固定方案仍消耗了真实支持；失败的测量方案留下的一单位支持没有被说成不存在。

With only 3132 work ticks, all three plans finish inquiry but fail to enact the due response. This is a failure of this inquiry-first menu, not proof that every possible intervention is impossible. An earlier response or a cheaper inquiry is outside the menu and must be compared before any real claim of indispensability.

### An acquired-record alternative changes necessity / 新的证据回应方案改变必要性

A fourth, explicitly installed policy reads the acquired `view.second` at the response stage: if it is zero, use allowance 3; otherwise use allowance 0. It does not receive the hidden case label at that decision. In this particular two-case model the acquired values are 0 and 3, and the retained dependence is known. Lean proves that its executed endpoint equals the successful inherited measured variant in each case, that both claims hold, and that work remains accounted for. A new core trace certifies the state-dependent policy itself.

第四方案直接读取已经取得的坐标，再决定余量。此两种情况中它成功，并非从未观察的情况标签直接挑选答案。规则本身、两种误差关系及固定依存仍是给定的；本轮没有证明系统自行发现了该规则。

When this policy is admitted alongside the earlier three and secondary gains are equal, both it and the fixed response are undefeated. A collective protection requirement remains, while use of the fixed response is no longer required. The earlier unique-choice theorem remains true for its original menu. This is a checked example of **a new feasible alternative changing a practical necessity without changing the protected target**.

The final operation's stipulated tariff includes the simple record branch; no separate physical implementation or measured computational cost is established. The new policy uses the same inquiry prefix, so it still does not compare early intervention with inquiry.

## 3. What this advances, and what remains / 推进与余项

The new composition supplies a precise implementation and comparison condition for the ethical programme: **a warranted practical reason must track a jointly feasible, timely response through the same history that obtains and uses evidence.** It also shows why richer inquiry is not automatically favored, why conservative margins can be unaffordable, and why the permitted uncertainty domain matters to the verdict.

G0 supplies the positive ethical direction in the accepted route; J2 supplies the scoped protection priority. Application must independently identify the living claim, all affected basic conditions, permissions and relevant alternatives. The toy companion coordinate is not an exhaustive account of contributors or an ecosystem. If spending the source destroys another basic claim, the dominance proof no longer applies without representing that loss. The theorem's `Required` becomes a practical requirement only under those warranted meanings.

The next substantive connection is **formed or evidence-justified uncertainty sets and policy alternatives → selection of inquiry versus earlier response under a common physical deadline**. This result evaluates inherited executions and implements one supplied selector using their acquired record. It does not discover that selector, acquire its own uncertainty certificate, or compare immediate response with inquiry. The represented histories are finite and idealized; measured biological and institutional correspondence remains further work. Neither this local omission nor a successful local proof settles the whole theory's explanatory reach.
