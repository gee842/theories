# A learner changes how it learns and passes on the change

6 October 2026. AI-authored conditional construction within the [generative foundation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). **Accepted after independent internal review.** The named connection is actual resource-dependent diagnosis → physical replacement of an inherited learning route → acquired consequences → resource-dependent copying and use by a successor. It extends [successor learning](SUCCESSOR-LEARNING.md), whose acquired record could change while its update route remained fixed, using the [formed monitor](FORMED-ROUTE-MONITOR.md) and its checked first-commit handoff.

The result addresses a real part of selection over selectors. The process can replace the mechanism that interprets subsequent outcomes, keep its existing record, learn through the replacement, and transmit the resulting mechanism and record together. Its variation, hardware recipes, diagnostic trigger, funding relationship and finite schedule remain supplied. It does not establish that the process has learned which updater is best, or that selection must improve learning.

## A diagnosis is an acquired state with a bill

Two actual histories provide the principal inputs. Ordinary retained CLOSED/FROZEN descendants have recipient reserve 25. When their target changes, one constructed monitor frame observes a failed action followed by an unchanged record, leaving reserve 11 and a nonwriter diagnosis. A PRIVATE_ZERO descendant is available through a separately labeled earlier write-clamp history, with reserve 15. Once that old clamp ends, its first stable successful action produces the wrong update; monitoring also leaves 11, now with a proxy diagnosis. These histories retain their different origins and all failed predecessors.

Both have one unused ten-unit monitor packet and an incorrect current recipient bit. Incorrectness is an analyst's characterization, not a runtime input. The monitor identifies behavior from actual resource-dependent observations; it does not announce hidden truth or an optimal replacement. A packet outage can trigger the same diagnosis in a perfectly adequate fixed response.

The continuation declares five additional fuel units, twenty units of probe source, a separate ten-unit copying packet, a revision head, a replacement routing part, and a blank/routing pair for a new successor. These were present outside the earlier funding accounts from the extended original boundary. Failed preparations retain them unused. The ordinary no-monitor and no-repair comparisons receive the same later grant when their first prefix completes.

## Change the mechanism without rewriting its answer

The new head costs one unit to assemble. Another unit pays to read the actual completed, bound diagnostic cells. Only then can their contents authorize a replacement attempt; a saved analyst verdict cannot bypass the resource-dependent read. An unresolved result still costs these two units and permits continuation on the old route.

The supplied variation law offers the four existing route recipes with identical guards and equal proposal probability. It does not inspect the hidden target, choose a desired command or rank the alternatives by an external score. Swapping costs one unit, consumes a real spare routing part and leaves the displaced part in a retired inventory. It preserves the actual packed record, the original actor's cell and cash, and the same recipient connection. The old diagnostic binding and summaries are invalidated; evidence about the previous route cannot certify its replacement.

At either principal diagnosed boundary, 11 + grant 5 − the three fees leaves 13. The replacement must now earn its effects through the unchanged learner: rent 1, target sense 1, work 3, outcome acquisition 1 and write 1 for each probe. A successful ten-unit packet credits the recipient alone. Each partial failure retains its costs and actual phase.

## Actual consequences determine what can be copied

Under the stable, available primary law, starting with that wrong record:

| Proposed route | Actual probe receipts | Recipient reserve after completed probes | Subsequent copying |
|---|---|---|---|
| TARGET | 011 |13 → 6 → 9 → 12 | Can pay the existing copier, produce a useful child and retain it; final reserve 11. |
| PRIVATE_ZERO | 010 |13 → 6 → 9 → 2 | Pays donor hold, then cannot pay recipient own-use; no child is copied. |
| CLOSED/FROZEN | First failure, then a second failed work event | The second work leaves floor 1 | Cannot pay its acquisition/write; the third probe does not occur. |

The final selection uses the already established copying process. It reads the actual revised `(route,q)`, consumes a distinct successor carrier and routing part, funds the child's sensing and work, and charges its later hold. No new favorable “retention score” or arbitrarily chosen terminal fee selects TARGET.

The original variable and conventional preparations give a full-mixture probability **1/72** for a functional route change followed by a productive, retained successor. Including descendants whose existing route already works, productive retained child probability is **1/24**, versus **1/36** in the corresponding no-repair and no-monitor controls. These probabilities preserve failed original preparation and copying mass. They are exact conditional model results, not empirical frequencies.

The primary whole-distribution account is 246 original units, including the newly declared 35; the earlier consumed resources remain charged. At the typed ordinary retained boundary, the local account is 88: actor 8 + recipient 25 + monitor source 20 + grant 5 + probe source 20 + copy source 10. The successful final state accounts for actor 8 + recipient 11 + dissipation 49 + lost packets 20. Formation costs before this boundary belong to the earlier account, not to a fictitious new endowment.

## The same mechanism can preserve a bad updater

The adverse outcomes are substantive parts of the result:

- **Late change reverses the selection.** If the plant changes before probe 3, TARGET can finish with a newly corrected record but insufficient resources to copy. PRIVATE_ZERO can instead copy a record it has just made wrong. Its child fails, loses its packet and still pays its hold, remaining retained at reserve 1.
- **Buffers weaken discrimination.** With enough additional reserve, misleading or nonwriting routes can also transmit. The [separate resource analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ROUTE-REVISION-RESOURCE-MATH.md) derives the ordered-payment thresholds; eventual net balance alone does not establish affordability of every preceding operation.
- **Earlier copying can preserve an unproductive record.** The zero-probe control can retain a wrong child. The two-probe control can pay to write a template while lacking the seed needed to use it. A copied tuple, an operational child and a productive retained child are different outcomes.
- **Monitoring can make the original process worse.** In the actual controlled-proxy ancestry, ordinary unmonitored operation yields productive retained children with probability 1/9, whereas monitoring plus random replacement yields 13/144. A route that looks inferior at the diagnosed boundary can do better over its whole cheaper history. Under stable ordinary ancestry, the extra monitoring and diagnostic reads buy no extra productive children.
- **Observation and materials can fail independently.** A missing contact, broken binding, absent part or short reserve blocks a different stage. Missing copy source can leave a paid, seeded child at target sensing with three cash units, unable to perform work. It is not a fabricated zero-return observation.

Thus differential copying supplies one causal mechanism of selection over learning mechanisms. It does not equate persistence, truth, recipient benefit and ethical worth, and does not establish a universally favorable attractor.

## Verification and explanatory limits

The [experiment and reproduction record](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/revision/README.md) separates three kinds of evidence. The existing handoff supplies 66 actual first-frame boundaries. A fresh Lean 4.29.1 build checks the typed resource-dependent event composition and its counterexamples. The separately implemented numerical reconstruction checks 75 cases, 189 full mixtures, 2,592 actual-boundary comparisons and 8,136 distinct invocations. It checks 106,004 physical states, including every resource/material field and reused operational register; observer stage names and duplicate identical physical snapshots are not physical differences. Ordinary finite-table control matches the producer exactly.

The [review](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ROUTE-REVISION-REVIEW.md) and [integration](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ROUTE-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) retain a repaired contract ambiguity and the initially omitted formal no-swap branch. Lean proves implications of the declared laws; it does not verify the entire Python preparation distribution or establish the physical recipes. These are independent internal implementations and collaborating-AI reviews, not external peer review or a new biological experiment.

The [empirical/comparator account](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/ROUTE-REVISION-COMPARATORS.md) explains what an actual within-carrier test must measure: acquired diagnostic evidence, resource-dependent reconfiguration, preserved memory, changed future updating, resource consequences and successor inheritance. Existing biological work supports parts of this chain; it does not establish this whole construction.

The next explanatory obligation is the formation and sustained use of the diagnostic/revision architecture and its evidence routes, including cases where the affected center cannot finance the intervention. The trigger rule, supplied repertoire and calibrated contacts must remain visible rather than being renamed “wisdom.” In parallel, newly acquired evidence must support revision of reasons and effective action under changing conditions, and the grounds of shared concern still require justification. This construction closes a finite inherited-route-revision interface within that larger derivation; it does not close the unified foundation.
