## 主张 Claim

塔（Tower）：每一层有一个网与一个流，第 n 层是第 n+1 层在严格递增的时刻 refine n 读出（web_sample、flow_sample）。最粗一层的步 s 是第 n 层的 reads n s；最粗一层是第 n 层在这些时刻读出（Tower.web_zero、Tower.level_zero），这些时刻严格递增（Tower.reads_strict）。每一层的规律：每个关系项只列出一次的平衡与耗散传到更粗一层（Tower.balances_coarser、Tower.dissipative_coarser）。分得再细，变化也有界：最粗一层从 S 起 N 步的一段在第 n 层读出，第 n 层有所立者的普查、所立者在其中各细步不被否定地下降时，总水平下降的细步（即有水平改变的步）不多于最粗一层在 S 的总水平（Tower.changes_within），界与 n 无关。 / A tower (Tower): at each level a web and a flow, level n being level n+1 read at the strictly increasing times refine n (web_sample, flow_sample). Step s of the coarsest level is step reads n s of level n; the coarsest level is level n read at those times (Tower.web_zero, Tower.level_zero), which are strictly increasing (Tower.reads_strict). Laws at every level: balance with each relatum listed once, and dissipation, pass to the coarser level (Tower.balances_coarser, Tower.dissipative_coarser). Change bounded however finely divided: a stretch of N steps of the coarsest level from S, read at level n, under a census of what stands at level n and with what stands not refuted to run down at each of its fine steps, has at most as many fine steps at which the total standing falls, the steps at which some standing changes, as the coarsest level's total standing at S (Tower.changes_within); the bound does not depend on n.
