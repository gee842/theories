# The warrant for living significance: how its starting commitment is supported

Written on 2026-10-09 (Asia/Bangkok) by the AI (Claude), executing [Ren's instruction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-lean-evidence-roles.md). Ren's own words and the AI's reconstruction are labelled separately. The [external-perspective review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/UNIVERSAL-METAETHICS-OF-AGENCY-REVIEW.md) and the [discrimination integration](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-DISCRIMINATION-INTEGRATION.md) record why this chapter was written and the new proofs.

This chapter does not prove living significance or convert it into an unconditional theorem. The theory does not assume living significance: it follows under [the continuation condition](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#615-若任何东西的延续都有意义--if-the-continuation-of-anything-has-significance), whose own warrant and necessity are open. The chapter organizes the kinds of support appropriate to a starting commitment of this kind, states what each formal check contributes, and names results that would revise it.

<a id="1-地位--status"></a>
## 1. Status

**Living significance: every living claim has significance from every standpoint (`Valuation.Significant`).** It gives significance, not a positive valence, and the `living` readings do not include further generation. It is not assumed; it follows under the continuation condition, the starting commitment whose warrant is compared here. Every normative foundation has a starting commitment; what remains open is comparative: what living significance explains, how much the condition assumes, which positions it addresses, and what would revise it.

Why a starting commitment is needed (AI reconstruction, from the revision of review §3.1): the connection between a normative predicate and a descriptive relation either does not exist, in which case description cannot yield a normative conclusion, or it is supplied by a premise, a definition or a constitutive identity, in which case that connection is itself the commitment. The review's appendix checks the formal core of this dilemma in Lean. The theory meets it without an axiom: it states the commitment as a condition, the continuation condition, which it does not assert. Kant, Gewirth, Parfit, utilitarianism and contractualism each have a starting point of their own; having a starting commitment is therefore not in itself a defect relative to rivals. Provenance: the point was first put on 2026-10-08 in Ren's words “当然，这种地方需要有axiom才行，不要继续白费功夫” ([source](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-alignment-grounding-axiom-correction.md); gloss, AI: "Of course, a place like this needs an axiom; don't keep wasting effort").

The object of the ethical direction is 生生, not any new organization: 生生 is read as generation generating generation, and its direction is compared by widening what can form ([D1](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md#d1-生生的读法--how-生生-is-read)). As relations, generation and degeneration do not overlap, although one occurrence can take part in both, so reading `generative` in the formal inventory as "any realized new organization" is a broad reading the direction of 生生 does not support. Provenance: the question was first put in Ren's words “我G0是什么？generative和degenerate虽然都在一个becoming model上，但是他们不overlap吧” ([source](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-renewing-consuming-without-axioms.md#ren-follow-up); gloss, AI: "What is my G0? Generative and degenerate are both in one becoming model, but they do not overlap, right?").

Living significance supplies significance only, not a valence: every living claim has significance from every standpoint. This chapter, written on 9 October 2026, argues for a stronger reading: that realized living and further generation have positive significance, which is living significance read together with the direction of 生生 (D1). Where the sections below speak of positive significance, or of 生生 as the object of living significance, they concern that reading; the formal content does not carry it. Turning that reading into a reason for a given position rests on the direct practical articulation D/P; turning reasons into comparisons and requirements rests on the practical commitments; applying them to a concrete situation rests on the corresponding condition E. The sections below explain, one by one, how these commitments are supported.

<a id="2-支持的四种方式--four-kinds-of-support"></a>
## 2. Four kinds of support

<a id="21-解释统一--explanatory-unification"></a>
### 2.1 Explanatory unification

Care, repair, creation, learning, renewal, rest, finite fulfillment and constructive ending can all be explained from the same ground, without assuming a separate intrinsic value for each ([premise derivation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-PREMISE-DERIVATION.md) §§1, 3; [alignment direction](ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION.md) §1). Self-care is included without first having to produce external benefit; ending is included without first having to prove the old arrangement permanently useless. If a rival needs more mutually independent starting points to explain the same range, that is a comparative reason in favour of living significance.

<a id="22-区分力--discriminating-content"></a>
### 2.2 Discriminating content

If the inventory of the formal predicate is read broadly, so that every realized new organization counts as generative content, the formal predicate gives the same positive significance to processes that keep forming new organization while destroying the conditions they depend on. Tumour-like proliferation is of this kind, and so is competitive expansion that externalizes its costs; the latter is not in the law class below, see "Scope of this separation" in this section.

This is a problem of the broad reading, not of the direction of 生生 (§1). That direction is a whole one: generation generating generation, compared by widening what can form (D1), not a filter of possibilities already judged good independently. Provenance: Ren's wording of it, “一种整体的，不断创造更多生的方向” (gloss, AI: "a whole direction that keeps creating more life"), with his refusal to shrink it to "possibilities already judged good independently" ([original words](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md)).

The question is therefore: can one recognize, without a prior good/bad filter and by checkable descriptive content, which formations belong to 生生? [RenewingFormation.lean](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/RenewingFormation.lean) answers "yes", in one explicit law class of irrecoverable wear and with two formal readings by the AI:

- When formation pressure stays within renewal, formation occurs at every step (`within_renewal_ceaseless`) and renewal capacity stays unchanged; this holds also when rest is allowed (`keeps_capacity_iff`);
- When the pressure persistently exceeds the current renewal rate, the cumulative formation of the whole unfolding is bounded by `stock + tri renew` and stops before that moment (`beyond_current_bounded`, `beyond_current_ceases`); from the first step of excess, renewal capacity falls permanently (`beyond_loses_capacity_at_once`);
- Other formation sharing these conditions stops with it, however the pressure is distributed (`consuming_part_ends_otherwise_ceaseless_formation`). The same holds when two parts are each within renewal and only jointly exceed it (`jointly_exceeding_parts_end_shared_formation`);
- Consuming formation can at first form more, and by a one-step count it ranks ahead; after the budget, every within-renewal continuation exceeds every beyond-renewal continuation (`consuming_forms_more_first`, `within_eventually_exceeds_every_beyond`);
- The event-by-event reading of living significance acknowledges the formation events of both, and the no-loss comparison C does not apply between them either, since neither extends the other in generation. What separates them are the dynamical properties `Ceaseless` and `KeepsCapacity` (`event_reading_recognizes_both`, `comparison_does_not_apply`, `ceaseless_separates`, `capacity_separates`).

These distinctions are consequences of the supplied law, and the law itself carries most of the content. The theorems are sign-neutral by construction: which side lies within the object of living significance, Lean does not provide.

- That 生生 has positive significance is this chapter's reading of living significance with the direction of 生生 (§1), not a further premise and not a Lean result: the formal content gives significance, not a valence.
- Two things are not Lean results and must be carried separately. First, that the two formal readings do capture 生生: this is the AI's reading and a correspondence. Second, the comparison "consuming is worse": this is carried by C. Under this module's event-by-event broad reading, C does not apply. Under a relational reading it was checked in another session's [relational-reading-comparison](../results/records/relational-reading-comparison.md): in the law family, every beyond-renewal draw is ranked strictly below its continuation in which it does not occur, by C.

The two formal readings each have defects. `Ceaseless` (formation at every step) captures "unceasing", but a single rest makes it fail, while a formation that surges first and then continues at a lowered rate permanently loses renewal capacity and nevertheless satisfies it (`rest_keeps_capacity_not_ceaseless`, `wears_yet_ceaseless`). `KeepsCapacity` (the renewing organization keeps its rate) permits rest and decides at the first step of excess. Neither expresses "more" or rising complexity, since this law does not allow the renewal rate to grow. Stopping itself is not judged bad: finite fulfillment, rest and fitting ending remain life that living significance acknowledges. This representation, however, omits the restorative role of rest: under the event-by-event reading, C even ranks a rest strictly below sustained formation (`event_comparison_ranks_rest_below`). This is a relation omitted by the representation, not a refutation of the theory. The ethically sharper relation is ending shared formation that does not itself exceed renewal; judging that still goes through full comparison, bounded responsibility and the corresponding conditions. Whether real processes satisfy the supplied wear law must be established by evidence in each case.

**Scope of this separation.** In the law class above, the consumer itself depends on the condition it consumes, and therefore ends itself in the end. Another case is outside it: a formation draws on a condition that another relation depends on, without itself depending on it. It can be present at every step and form without bound, while that relation permanently loses its condition; the externalizing "winner" expansion, that is, the meta-attractor case, is of this kind. Here neither the focal formation's own `Ceaseless` nor the `Ceaseless` of total formation detects this consumption. What decides is a relational property: whether some dependent relation permanently loses its condition. `relational-renewal-classification` on another branch (in registration) gives this classification on arbitrary unfoldings, and proves that the focal formation's own activity and formation do not determine its class. The "whole direction" must therefore be read as the continuation of every dependent relation, not as the persistence of any one formation or of the total. `ceaseless_separates` above uses the persistence of total formation; it suffices because this law class has a single shared condition on which the consumer also depends, so that persistence of total formation and continuation of the dependent relations coincide. Outside this law class they do not coincide.

<a id="23-与对手的判别--discrimination-against-rivals"></a>
### 2.3 Discrimination against rivals

**Natural-number scores and bounded scores.** [ProtectionPriorityRival.lean](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/ProtectionPriorityRival.lean) checks a constructed protection-versus-gain situation:

- Living significance, the no-loss extension comparison C and the ordering of basic-condition priority can hold together, and that priority never trades the basic condition for any number of gains (`g0_leaves_ordering_open`, `priority_never_trades`);
- Living significance constrains only positive relevance, not ordering: it equally coexists with an ordering that satisfies C yet does trade. Basic-condition priority therefore does not follow from C; that it does "not follow from living significance" is trivial (`comparison_does_not_yield_priority`);
- Every natural-number score satisfying C trades, at the `score 0 + 2` gains (`every_nat_score_trades`). What does the work is that a strictly increasing natural-number score adds at least one at each step;
- A bounded rational score (protection is 1, `k` gains are `k/(k+1)`) satisfies C, never trades, and agrees with basic-condition priority on every pair of options (`ratio_agrees_with_priority`, `bounded_score_represents_priority`).

This situation therefore separates basic-condition priority from natural-number-valued (unit-step) scores, but not from bounded, diminishing-marginal scores. The latter exactly represent basic-condition priority here and remain a substantive rival; separating them needs another situation. That basic-condition priority does not follow from C is an instance, in the protection-versus-gain situation, of the existing result that "C is silent on trade-offs": `both_appraisals_respect_all_extensions`, `tradeoff_has_opposite_strict_appraisals` and `loss_sensitivity_alone_supplies_no_cutoff` already have this sense, and `compatible_cutoffs_disagree` and `both_tradeoff_options_survive` are related. `no_protection_trade_by_count` gives the non-trading property of basic-condition priority. Three things are new: it satisfies C here; natural-number scores cannot represent it; bounded scores can.

**Later endorsement as the final measure.** In the rival-one claim of the [generative-alignment revision](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-ALIGNMENT-REVISION.md), later sufficient endorsement is enough to justify a formative process. This framework rejects its sufficiency: later absence of objection does not make a loss that occurred not have occurred, and basic-condition priority does not permit trading more endorsement for a basic deprivation that still exists.

**Gewirth and the dialectical route.** Gewirth tried to derive a universal principle from commitments every purposive agent already has, without positing a value axiom ([source study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/NORMATIVE-WARRANT-SOURCES.md) §1). His argument is acknowledged to be contested at the step from "I need X" to "others also ought to respect everyone's X". Review §3.1 proposes a research hypothesis: under a monist relational ontology, the evaluator's own evaluation and continuation are formed in, and depend on, relations with others, so the self-boundary that blocks this step is not given. If this route succeeds, the premise needed for living significance could be lowered to something close to "the continuation of the generative relations that constitute my evaluation", and what it would support is precisely the renewing formation of §2.2. This is a target to be proved (`universal-practical-warrant`, open), not a result.

**Partially completed on 2026-10-09, narrowed after independent review** ([record](../results/records/self-good-reachable-g0.md), [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/EVALUATOR-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md), [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/EVALUATOR-GROUNDING-REVIEW.md)).

The starting point becomes the evaluator's own self-significance (`SelfGood` in Lean): its own living claims have significance from it.
- **First step.** Self-significance plus unrestricted monotonicity (across self and other) reaches others at least as connected. Unrestricted monotonicity is equivalent to supervenience, a profile-monotone valuation and BI; BI is contested. A rival who acknowledges only "mine" is strictly inconsistent only if it accepts that "degree of connection is the complete ground".
- **Second step.** Adding tolerance and graded connectivity reaches all reachable life. The result is a **standpoint-indexed, single-history instance** of living significance (`G0 (reach s) (sig s)` in Lean), and everything beyond self-significance is carried by tolerance.
- **A bridge is still needed.** The unindexed living significance, across continuations, still needs a bridge from standpoint-relative to normative significance.
- **Binding force depends on endorsement.** Whether a reason binds a position depends on whether that position endorses self-significance, tolerance (and BI); it is not independent of endorsement.
- **The wall.** An evaluator of present purposes without self-significance is not bound; tolerance has not been defended against rivals with closed coordinates and weights set to zero; what is obtained is significance, not weight.

**Content characterization, 2026-10-09** ([record](../results/records/metaethical-core-characterization.md), [Lean](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Metaethics.lean)). On a whole in which formed relations connect living content together, living significance is equivalent to the conjunction of three items:
- some living claim has significance from some standpoint (the seed; every position that holds self-significance with its own living content provides one);
- significance is transmitted along nonzero formed relations to living content (relational neutrality);
- the significance of living content is not cancelled by standpoint (standpoint neutrality).

D follows from the externalist articulation A. The seed, relational neutrality, standpoint neutrality, connectivity and A each have a consistent countermodel; every normative rival is exactly the same rule plus a formed boundary: its own region, its own standpoint, the endorsers.

This is not a proof of living significance and does not replace it. It shows that what living significance contains beyond what the evaluator already holds falls exactly on the premise that "a formed boundary cannot cancel normative significance". The tolerance and BI of the evaluator route above, and the "bridge from standpoint to norm" demanded by the review, are rules of this shape. Sidgwick's dualism is not dissolved, only located here.

**Constitutivism and shmagency.** No argument can bind a process that evaluates nothing, or that is indifferent to every continuation. So even if the strongest dialectical route succeeds, what results is "universal over all evaluators", not "universal over all possible beings". Living significance, with the continuation condition that yields it, is stated openly and acknowledges this limit.

<a id="24-对齐上的丰饶性--fruitfulness-for-alignment"></a>
### 2.4 Fruitfulness for alignment

Under living significance, the practical commitments and E, human instructions, institutional aims and the AI's present purposes are all open to ethical evaluation; one concrete case has already shown which changes in fact would change the judgment ([alignment direction](ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION.md) §5). The result of §2.2 adds a checked caution for alignment: by a one-step count of formation, a consuming continuation ranks ahead of sustained formation, while under the same law such consumption later ends the shared formation. Reading the whole direction by the continuation of every dependent relation is meant to avoid this error, and the distinction needed is supplied by `relational-renewal-classification`. Which optimization objective avoids it has no checked conclusion yet: no optimizer is formalized here. Looking only at whether the system itself can persist misses expansion that externalizes its costs, and the case that stabilizes after wearing once.

## 4. What would revise living significance

1. In a faithfully represented case, a 生生不息 formation that stays within renewal and has no reverse loss is, after adequate review, still judged to have no positive significance at all, and not merely to be outweighed by other reasons.
2. In a case, the judgment given by the whole-direction reading is shown by the same faithful facts to be wrong, and the practical commitments cannot account for the difference.
3. A rival explains the same range as §2.1 with fewer or weaker commitments, and does at least as well in the discriminating cases of §2.2–§2.3.
4. The dialectical route (§2.3) succeeds, so that living significance is lowered to a premise the evaluator already holds. This would not overturn living significance but change its status. So far only this is obtained: the standpoint-indexed single-history instance follows from self-significance, unrestricted monotonicity (including BI), tolerance and the correspondence condition; the unindexed living significance additionally needs standpoint neutrality (§2.3 content characterization), which is itself a normative premise.

<a id="5-仍未完成--open"></a>
## 5. Open

The universal practical warrant (`universal-practical-warrant`) remains an open target. [Position-indexed positive reasons](../results/records/positioned-positive-reason.md) have been checked: every position that can reach a response has the reason, and endorsement-independence is the content of D, not a consequence of living significance. "Universal" therefore has a formal counterpart, but it depends on D. The content characterization further splits D into the externalist articulation A and standpoint neutrality; both are explicit premises, and neither has been argued from the agent's own standpoint. The law class of §2.2 has no recovery of the renewing organization, no restorative role of rest, no "more" or rising complexity, and no externalized consumption; the surge-then-decline case is recognized only by `KeepsCapacity`, not by `Ceaseless`. The situation of §2.3 does not separate basic-condition priority from bounded-sensitivity rivals. Each correspondence still needs concrete evidence.
