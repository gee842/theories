# 新可能性的形成 / Formation of new possibilities

8 October 2026. AI-authored continuation responding to [Ren's completion request](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-formation-of-new-possibilities.md), within the [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). [Proof, dependency and completion record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NEW-POSSIBILITIES-INTEGRATION.md) · [Evidence and substantive counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NEW-POSSIBILITIES-REVIEW.md).

**新能力可以由已有活动之间新形成的组织关系产生：新的连接使原先不能实现的作用得以实现，其中一些作用又改变后续组织怎样形成。描述要随这些实际差异修订，并保留旧描述仍然成立的范围。**

**New capabilities can arise through newly formed organization among existing activities. New connections enable effects previously unavailable; some effects change how further organization can be formed. Descriptions must track the resulting consequential differences while preserving their earlier valid scope.**

The explanatory integration is complete at the scope specified below: distinctions, a constructive causal witness, general description-revision conditions, primary-evidence comparison and counterreview. **45 new Lean theorems / 64 new declaration audits pass**; the full two-module closure has 92 audits. This is not a proof that every process continually invents new kinds or that all future possibility can be specified in advance.

## 1. 相对于什么而言是新 / What is new, relative to what?

“最初给定的 repertoire”可能指当前已能做的事、现有构造方法的产物，或一个模型容许的全部历史；三者不能混用。新于当前能力，不等于违反形成它的规律；一个模型能事后表示新能力，也不说明该能力早已实际存在。

An initial repertoire may mean currently operative capabilities, products of installed construction methods, or all histories admitted by an encompassing model. These are different claims. Being representable in a law does not make a capability already operative. Conversely, declaring something outside a model does not explain its formation.

| Level / 层次 | What would establish novelty / 所需证据 |
|---|---|
| New occurrence / 新发生 | An event or configuration absent from the selected earlier history. Repetition in a new place alone does not establish a new capability. / 指定历史中未出现的事件或构形；异处重复不独自证明新能力。 |
| New operative relation or capability / 新的实际关系或能力 | A formation history and an independently specified effect that the earlier arrangement could not realize under matched relevant conditions. / 实际形成历史，以及相应条件下旧组织不能实现的独立作用判据。 |
| Changed construction repertoire / 构造能力改变 | The formed arrangement changes which further arrangements can actually be constructed. / 新组织改变随后实际可构造的组织。 |
| Changed descriptive requirements / 描述要求改变 | Previously merged cases differ on a warranted question, or the old language omits the new relation to be explained. / 原先合并的情形在有根据的问题上产生差别，或旧语言未表述待解释的新关系。 |
| Unlimited continued innovation / 无界持续创新 | A sustained history with specified support, retention, diversity and distinct consequential outcomes. No finite demonstration establishes this. / 有支持、留存及后果差异的持续历史；有限展示不证明此项。 |

The boundary of a currently installed repertoire is an actual organizational boundary when its enabling relations are identified. A list made by an analyst is only a representational boundary until checked against that organization. These distinctions concern one process, not independent inventories of objects plus an outside meaning-giver. The meaning of a capability comes from the interaction and consequence being explained; it need not first be recognized by a subject.

能力的区分来自实际关系、条件和后果。观察、认识与记录也是其中形成的活动，不是从外部赋予一切作用意义的独立起点。

## 2. 从形成到进一步形成 / From formation to further formation

The proposed mechanism has four connected aspects. Existing differences and encounters permit a change in arrangement. The resulting arrangement changes a route of influence, access or transformation. That route makes a specified effect attainable. If the effect participates in constructing, maintaining or revising other arrangements, the formation conditions themselves change. Retention, spread and beneficial consequences each need additional explanation; none follows merely from first appearance.

已有差异与相遇容许组织改变；新组织改变作用、接入或转化的路径；由此出现此前不能实现的作用；若该作用参与其他组织的形成、维持或修订，后续形成条件也随之改变。首次出现、稳定留存、传播与善的方向分别需要根据。

This extends [formed method connections](AUTONOMOUS-METHOD-FORMATION.md), which already explain an initially absent operative route, without claiming that their two supplied generator recipes were invented. The present construction removes a **finite list of complete endpoint arrangements** and an **initially installed constructor connection** from its inputs. It retains explicit elementary binding and extension laws. Explaining those laws' realization in a particular material is a further correspondence task; placing a law in a lower module does not explain its origin.

Mechanisms such as altered regulation, changed contact geometry, composition, recruitment into a new interaction, and formation of an arrangement that supports further assembly are candidate realizations of this account. They are not interchangeable names: a concrete study must identify the actual transformation and intervention that removes its effect. Sometimes a previously available capacity merely becomes exercised or discovered; then the finding is access or knowledge, not the first formation of that capacity.

## 3. 已检查的构造 / The checked construction

The [Lean model](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/NewPossibilities.lean) describes one assembly with four coordinates: a chain of existing links, loose parts, available work and whether two parts are bound into an operative constructor connection. These are differentiated relations and stocks within one history. The model stipulates that binding this particular pair enables extension; it does not derive connector physics from a Boolean field.

All three event schemas belong to the same law: **couple** binds two loose parts and consumes one work unit; **extend**, requiring that connection, binds a further part into the chain and consumes one work unit; **wait** consumes one work unit without changing the arrangement. Waiting represents a matched expenditure control, not a universal cost of elapsed time. Material is conserved; work is depleted. Parts do not wear or detach in this example.

同一模型同时包括配接、延长与对照消耗。配接形成构造关系，延长使用该关系；材料守恒而支持被消耗。模型没有声称所有等待有成本，也没有模拟部件磨损。

Two actual one-event histories begin at zero links, three loose parts and three work units. Coupling produces two bound constructor parts, one loose part and two work units. The matched waiting history retains three loose parts and two work units. **Both have equal total material, work and existing chain length; only the first can perform an extension.** Thus the difference concerns an actual enabled construction operation, not an added name or a larger numerical stock.

The unchanged repertoire that excludes constructor coupling cannot extend any initially uncoupled chain. The full process constructs that connection and then extends it. This is escape from the **frozen installed repertoire**, not escape from the complete law that already includes coupling. The general closed-region theorem rules out attributing an escape to a genuinely closed unchanged law.

End-to-end reach is derived from consecutive existing links. Each extension supplies a path to a previously unreachable endpoint. For any finite list of advertised endpoints, the same local schema can construct a route beyond that list, given enough separately stated parts and work. Completed endpoint tools are not selected from a finite preloaded table. The result remains a parameterized family of one kind of transport; it does not establish endlessly new qualitative kinds.

对任意有限终点清单，在逐项明确材料和支持后，同一局部规律能构造清单之外的路径。每次有限构造不需要预装所有成品，但这只是同类传递的可扩展族，不等于无穷种新功能，也不等于一次无尽执行。

Every actual finite history satisfies exact material conservation and `final work + number of events = initial work`. An infinite execution with no replenishment is impossible under this event law. The checked loss case gains a route while using the last loose part, thereby losing capacity for further extension. Novelty is neither free nor necessarily a superset of previous possibilities.

The idealized reach assay omits signal costs, timing and attenuation. The new-constructor comparison concerns enabled operations including their actual local resource guards. These two validation scopes must not be conflated.

### 新功能，而不只是更长 / A new function, beyond increased length

A companion contact model addresses the limitation of the chain example. A contact transmits an incoming signal exactly when it is conducting. Before joining, the readout depends on the first contact alone. Binding two contacts in series makes the readout depend on passage through both. The resulting coincidence response is derived from these two local transmissions: it is true exactly when both contacts conduct.

配接前，输出只受一个接点影响；串接后，信号须通过两个接点。共同导通这一新功能由已有接点的局部传递规律及新形成的关系产生，不是把“具有新功能”直接设成任意谓词。

Lean proves that **no function of either input alone can reproduce this response over all four input combinations**, even allowing arbitrary processing of that one input. Further unary processing preserves that limitation. The joined circuit changes functional kind relative to this whole initial repertoire, rather than merely enlarging a numerical parameter. Two counterfactual input changes independently alter the resulting output. A controller already receiving both inputs could implement the same function; it does not satisfy the initial single-channel limitation.

The same abstract two-part binding and work account provides an actual joining history for this companion interpretation. It is a separate wiring realization of the accounting pattern, not a claim that the earlier constructor's components simultaneously are this signal circuit. “Loose” here means unjoined into the new serial arrangement, not that an individual contact cannot already conduct. Contact behavior, compatible binding and calibrated input/output access are supplied. The single joining event is a checked qualitative change; indefinitely many new functional kinds are still unproved.

This also preserves a concrete loss: with the first contact conducting and the second closed, the old arrangement transmits and the new one does not. Whether coincidence detection is preferable to that earlier transmission depends on the actual undertaking and consequences.

## 4. 描述何时、怎样必须改变 / When and how descriptions must change

Specify the histories or states being described, the questions being answered, and the response relation. A description is sufficient for those questions when equal descriptions imply equal responses on that domain. **If two actual possibilities share the description but differ on a relevant response, the description cannot correctly determine that response.** This is a checked conditional inference, not a requirement for perfect prediction in every practical model.

描述的充分性相对于适用域和问题。若旧描述把实际回应不同的情形合并，它就不能独自决定该回应；若粗略模型只承诺范围、概率或误差，须按其真实承诺另行检验，不能强迫它承担精确模型的任务。

In the matched construction histories, total material, work and chain length fail to determine extension capability. Adding the binding relation makes the description sufficient for this question. The old quantities remain recoverable. An arbitrary relabeling of the old tuple still fails, because it retains the same collision. For a single new query, pairing the old description with its response is the coarsest sufficient refinement among descriptions that preserve the old distinctions and answer that query. This is a semantic result, not a claim that recording the answer is free or already possible.

There are three different repairs:

1. **Different actual state, adequate language:** update the state described. No new vocabulary is required. / 语言足够而实际状态已变：更新状态。
2. **Insufficient distinctions:** retain the old quantities and acquire a relevant new relation or response distinction. / 旧分类遗漏相关区别：保留旧量并取得新关系或回应区别。
3. **An enlarged question domain:** introduce a query or relational type with an operational interpretation, preserving the earlier language on its original domain. / 问题域扩展：给新问题或关系类型以实际对应，并保留旧语言原有范围。

The checked sum-of-questions construction preserves old response meanings and makes the extra adequacy obligation explicit. It does not prove that one syntax anticipates every future concept. Nor does a new biological function necessarily require a new mathematical primitive: an existing relational language may express it once the previously omitted organization is represented.

The [acquired-abstraction study](ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION.md) already supplies an actual bounded learner whose own tests produce new distinctions; its prior validation scope is retained. Here, the new record theorem specifies the interface: **faithful evidence of the queried response supports the distinction; stale or miscalibrated evidence does not**. The complete response-profile construction is not an executable oracle. Retaining a dated finding and predicting a later state are separate commitments; transport to the later state requires stability or fresh evidence. An observation that changes the arrangement must be represented as part of that history.

## 5. 证据、竞争解释与理论贡献 / Evidence, rivals and contribution

证据支持组织关系改变可以产生新作用，并限制其效力与历史条件；它不把局部装配模型验证为生物学定律。已有开放演化与调控研究具有实质先例地位，本研究不声称首次发现这些机制。

No predictive superiority over that conventional account is established.

## 6. 生生伦理与完成范围 / Ethical connection and completion scope

This strengthens the descriptive premise in **whole generative organization → ethical quality → practical reasons**: “new possibilities” must identify actual changed relations and consequences, not a bigger label set. It does not impose novelty as a universal ethical duty. Under G0, actual living and further generation have basic favorable significance; [practical judgment](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md) supplies separately disclosed comparison and responsibility commitments. New capacity can coexist with lost care, exhausted support or coercive power. Established good, rest and finite fulfillment need no novelty bonus to count.

此项推进使规范讨论的对象更清楚：新增的是哪些实际可能，失去的又是什么。它不从新颖性推出善，不把复杂度或选项数变成伦理标量，也不要求一切生活永远创新。G0、额外比较承诺、实际对应和结论保持分开。

**Completed:** an integrated explanation of novelty relative to operative organization and construction repertoire; a law-consistent, resource-accounted witness exceeding any finite endpoint menu; checked conditions and limits for revising descriptions; primary-evidence comparison and substantive self-counterreview. The theory no longer needs to identify formation with selection from a finite list of finished capabilities.

**Not established:** novelty beyond every possible encompassing representation, guaranteed endless qualitative innovation, spontaneous origin of every physical law, a generally autonomous inventor of its own semantic criteria, or universal empirical coverage. These stronger claims are not silently substituted into the completed result. The next empirical connection is the actual rate, retention and effects of changed construction relations under ongoing support, testing matched conventional mechanisms. This also supports, but does not complete, the separate [evolutionary programme](THE-THEORY-OF-EVOLUTION.md).

已完成的是上述有明确证据与形式范围的理论整合；尚未证明无界质变、全部构造条件的起源或普遍经验覆盖。下一连接是新构造关系如何在真实支持、损失与竞争条件下形成、留存并持续发挥作用。
