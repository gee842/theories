# From consequential difference to accessible revision

6 October 2026. AI-assisted mathematical development under Ren's instruction to continue the [unified foundation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). This advances the named connection **consequences → revision of selectors**, alongside the concrete formation and retention programme. It extends [conditional universality](CONDITIONAL-UNIVERSALITY.md) from support requirements to the information reaching a response mechanism.

## 1. The connection to be explained

A difference can affect an organization's future while remaining inaccessible to its response mechanism. Even an accessible difference can be discarded before it changes a response. The foundation therefore needs separate explanations of:

1. which differences change the conditions of further activity;
2. how those differences reach an operative mechanism;
3. how their effects persist in that mechanism and change its later responses;
4. how sensing, retention, operation and revision remain materially possible.

The result here makes the second and third requirements precise and connects the fourth to an existing concrete process. It does not assume that a useful difference automatically becomes a reward, meaning or insight. An analyst's comparison of possible futures remains separate from whatever actually changes the mechanism.

## 2. A controller inside the same process

Let a declared world process have states \(s\), actions \(a\), and admitted steps \(P(s,a,t)\). A deterministic controller has memory \(m\), a response rule \(\pi(m)\), and an update rule \(U(m,o)\). A supplied channel maps an actual event to an accessible observation \(o=O(s,a,t)\).

The combined state is \((s,m)\). A combined step requires all of the following:

\[
a=\pi(m),\qquad P(s,a,t),\qquad o=O(s,a,t),\qquad m'=U(m,o).
\]

This is a process in the existing causal core. Its world and memory follow the actual successor; a later stage does not receive a newly initialized world or reserve. Neither the controller's rule nor the channel is inferred merely from the existence of a state space. Their implementation and origin remain supplied in this interface.

“Observation” here means **the complete input through which the world affects this specified update**. A reserve level, mechanical deformation, elapsed time or direct chemical interaction must be included if it affects the update. Equality of a recorded verbal signal alone cannot exclude changes caused by omitted material channels. Arbitrary memory types are allowed; the theorem does not assume a small or finite memory.

## 3. Indistinguishability constrains revision

For two actual histories of this same deterministic controller, equal initial memory and equal complete observation histories imply equal final memory and equal command histories. This follows by iterating the update through the observed events. Consequently, the mechanism cannot condition its next response on a world difference that has never produced a difference in its retained accessible history.

To give this an operational consequence, let \(w\) be a required future event sequence. Define the responses preserving that continuation at state \(s\) by

\[
K_w(s)=\{a\mid\exists t,\ P(s,a,t)\ \land\ \operatorname{Feasible}(P,t,w)\}.
\]

Suppose two reached world states have disjoint sets \(K_w(s_1)\) and \(K_w(s_2)\). Their continuing possibilities require incompatible next responses. If the controller arrived with the same initial memory and the same observation history, it issues the same next response in both. That response cannot preserve the declared continuation in both states.

Equivalently: **choosing a response compatible with each of two incompatible continuation requirements requires a difference in the accessible history or in the inherited controller state.** Compatibility is existential feasibility of the declared future word. It does not ensure that this controller will issue the later commands or that every possible outcome of a nondeterministic action preserves them. The result does not say that every difference needs a different action, that distinguishability is sufficient for successful learning, or that every supplied continuation is valuable. The continuation is a stated comparison used in the theorem; the controller does not read it as a reward or a success oracle.

The empty continuation specializes this to ordinary next-step feasibility. Nonempty continuations are more informative: a wrong response may be physically executable while destroying the possibility of a later activity. An action being possible now and its consequences permitting later activity are distinct.

This is a deterministic theorem. Random behavior cannot be interpreted by silently applying equality of paths to unrelated random draws. A stochastic extension needs its own coupling or distributional premises. No probability, optimal policy, guaranteed exploration or information-theoretic quantity is supplied by the present interface.

## 4. A paid diagnostic witness and its limits

The accompanying construction has two hidden regimes, a probe, two alternative responses and a later use operation. Both alternative responses are physically executable. The matching response prepares the later use; the mismatching response loses that possibility. The controller's accessible input comes through the declared probe channel and is retained to select its response. A fixed update rule can therefore change its operative response after a consequence reaches it, while remaining part of one unchanged transition law.

The probe, response and use each spend one reserve unit. Lean checks the complete three-event history in either regime and proves that any such three-event history needs at least three initial units. Their costs cannot be erased when the informative observation arrives. A prepared structure without the fuel needed to use it is distinguished from an actually feasible use. The blind-channel impossibility holds for any deterministic controller with the same inherited memory after the compared probes, rather than merely showing failure of one chosen algorithm. A separately checked misleading channel makes the supplied controller issue a physically executable wrong response and reach the failed state.

This is a deliberately supplied diagnostic channel and controller. The construction does not derive the availability or reliability of such a channel, its formation, an open response repertoire, or revision of the update rule itself. It shows first acquisition of an effective response in either of two fixed regimes; it does not yet show recovery when an already learned response becomes misleading during the same history. Its role is to jointly exhibit accessible feedback, retained response change and cost within one process. The concrete programme must explain how actual structures acquire and alter those roles.

## 5. Joining the general account to actual formation

The [formed-variant construction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/FORMED-VARIANT-SELECTION-INTEGRATION.md) already represents common fuel, locally stored fuel and a finite incident source. Its exact event law permits four related accounts:

| Account | Reserve | Demand | Provision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediately usable support | Common plus local fuel | Actual event expenditure | One unit per capture |
| Closed fuel account | Common plus local fuel plus remaining source | Actual event expenditure | Zero |
| Source account | Remaining source | One unit per capture | Zero |
| Local store | Locally stored fuel | Local copying and local leakage | One unit per capture |

These accounts describe different boundaries of the **same actual process**. A capture is provision for the usable account and transfer within the closed account. It is not creation of support from nothing. The new bridge applies the general `SustainLaw` and history bounds to the concrete event relation; the ledgers no longer meet only through matching prose.

After any actual preparation history, further expenditure is bounded by the usable reserve and remaining source at the successor actually reached. The initial budget is unavailable for reuse once earlier formation, association or repair has spent it. The established mixed formation-to-copy witness supplies an exact accounting check: four units of initial common fuel and one captured unit pay five expenditures.

The local account adds a constraint that an aggregate budget conceals. All local copies together are bounded by the initial local store plus the source remaining at the start of that history. Starting with an empty local store, any positive local expenditure requires an actual capture in that same history. Arbitrarily much common fuel cannot bypass this access relation in the supplied law. Thus the difference between available quantities and usable relations has a checked consequence for what can happen next.

The concurrent concrete [formation-account module](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/formal/FormationAccounts.lean) establishes overlapping usable/source/closed accounts of the same event law. These are corroborating formalizations, not separate empirical evidence or distinct discoveries of those balances. This module additionally checks the local-store constraint, capture necessity and exact post-repair handoff; the [support companion](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LEARNING-SUPPORT-MATH.md) records the overlap.

This bridge does not identify a kinetic quotient, prove a stochastic adaptation advantage or turn the formed variants into individual learners. Nor does it equate that chemistry with the diagnostic controller. It connects the concrete formation law to the same universal support interface that constrains a proposed learning mechanism.
