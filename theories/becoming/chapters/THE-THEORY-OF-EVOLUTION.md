## One unfolding and differentiated evolutionary roles

The starting orientation is [activity forming and transforming the conditions of further activity](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). A living organization, its supporting relations and its effects on later activity are differentiated within that history. Their analytical separation allows questions about ancestry, development, resource access and population composition; it does not posit independent organism and environment substances.

An evolutionary explanation must identify what is reconstructed, what changes, how descendants arise and how their consequences affect later continuation. Selection describes one part of that account. Variation, stochastic sampling, movement, changed transmission and developmental reconstruction cannot all be relabeled selection. The emergence of an organized individual also cannot be assumed to have been explained merely by changing a partition of an already supplied population.

The proposed advantage is a connected explanation of **organization formation → inherited change → changed continuation → changed conditions of evolution**, including the later emergence of learning and evaluation. Some links already have conditional proofs and empirical correspondences. A general account recovering the relevant established theories and earning a comparative advantage remains to be completed.
