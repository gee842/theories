## The positive account

**生的延续依赖那些使其实际发生的关系持续有效；形成的条件可以保留并在后来起作用，而更新或转化的必要性，要从这些关系在实际展开中的变化来说明。** This is an AI-authored formulation. Historical formation, current exercise, retained viability and recurrent reconstruction are connected but different claims.

An organism's relative boundary, material exchanges and regulatory activity form within one history. The boundary does not make its supporting conditions independent of it. Earlier formed structures can remain operative during rest or dormancy; activity elsewhere can participate in their provision. Conversely, naming a wider whole does not explain how support is formed, transferred or exhausted. The account must follow the relevant relationships.

The common explanatory task is to identify an independently recognized living realization, reconstruct its enabling relations and inherited provision, and explain when actual interactions make that provision insufficient. An adequate response may preserve a relation, restore it, recruit another realization or transform the ongoing activity. Mere recurrence does not require ever-increasing novelty or replacement of every part.

## A derived maintenance mechanism

The [first mechanism-based derivation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md) starts with transport and the support needed for active compensation. Within a specified regime, net inward transport changes an amount that must remain bounded. Export offsets that change and has a declared work cost. The new Lean proof derives the earlier general support account by composing those relationships.

This explains a meaningful distinction. Available physiological tolerance can accommodate some transport without immediate compensation. Retained work can fund compensation without new input. Once actual cumulative load exceeds both, further support is required for that maintained continuation. Including a finite supplying store preserves its depletion rather than manufacturing a new source through redescription.

Fresh Lean 4.29.1 verification passes **10 new named theorems and 20 new declaration audits**, with standard logical dependencies exposed. This is a checked conditional derivation for a class of maintained transport processes. The transport law, operative bound, units, work coupling and actual load still require correspondence to a living realization.

## Disturbance, passive relief and renewed activity

The [8 October extension](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#7-extension-passive-relief-and-actual-bounded-endpoints) allows passive transport in both directions and temporary departures from a selected amount range. A general account over the actual history now derives what compensation and work input are necessary to attain an endpoint bound. Earlier tolerance, retained work and passive relief all count; an excursion alone does not entail a need for fresh input. A finite included provision store remains depleted by what it supplies.

**暂时失调并不等于生的中断；某项量恢复，也不等于使生继续的组织已经恢复。** This AI-authored distinction connects the formal result to the evidence. The recovery assay reports nutrient-assisted repolarization and resumed growth in some previously depolarized cells. It does not establish return to the old starvation regime. A successor activity may be the relevant living endpoint, and the relations enabling it must be followed through the disturbance.

Fresh Lean checking adds **15 new named theorems / 20 new declaration audits**, with 107 audits across the full five-module dependency closure. The earlier maintained result is preserved. The extension proves a necessary transport/support account for actual bounded endpoints; it does not construct recovery, establish its physiological sufficiency or guarantee recurrent successful endpoints. Its passive-return and retained-work examples test the model's scope; they are not biological rescue experiments.

## Organization enabling further activity

The [next integrated connection](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#8-organizing-relationships-and-actual-resumed-activity) explains why available material and operative organization cannot be identified. **物质仍在，不等于组织关系仍有效；不活动，也不等于其后续作用已经丧失。** The biological assessment uses an independently measured output and structural differences, preserving partial activity and the supplied experimental conditions.

A new general reaction-history theorem covers arbitrary declared species and reaction families. If an actual operation requires a member of a selected organizing class, that class was present initially or an actual preceding reaction introduced it. After a known empty reached state, later activity requires actual intervening entry. Joint participant counts and the reached intermediate states prevent a later product from supplying its own earlier prerequisite. The result does not require a new proof for each finite reaction list, but each application must establish its relevant recipes and their adequacy.

Initial presence is weaker than survival or causal ancestry of a particular component. Separate checked histories show retained protected structures being reactivated, and dissociated parts being assembled before productive activity. Assembly from parts is an admitted alternative to retaining an intact complex. A restricted absence result therefore cannot establish universal physical impossibility.

Fresh Lean checking adds **22 named theorems / 47 declaration audits**, with **75 audits** in the two-module closure. The [proof](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-ORGANIZATION-PROOF.md), [primary evidence](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-ORGANIZATION-EVIDENCE.md) and [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-ORGANIZATION-REVIEW.md) retain their separate scopes. This establishes a general conditional origin requirement and a new functional correspondence test, not a proof of complete biological recovery.

## Partial competence and jointly supported activity

The [fourth integrated connection](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#9-joint-participation-and-continuing-productive-organization) explains how activities compete for the participants that also enable changes in later organization. **当前活动与组织恢复共同依赖现有条件；后续能力不能倒过来充当自身形成的前提。** Available organization, actual engagement and retained output require distinct observations within the same process.

A general allocation theorem aggregates the input requirements of arbitrary declared reactions. For admitted recipes, if the combined requirements fit the same inventory and the aggregate balance holds, an admissible sequence of the original reactions reaches that endpoint without inserting support. Input multiplicities also bound counted participant uses; across batches, actual increases in the selected count are credited only to later allocations. Other required support must still be available. Returned catalysts remain reusable and are not counted as new organization merely because they appear among outputs.

Checked histories preserve positive partial competence while activation competes with current production for participants and support. Activation enables more later work within the declared batch comparison. The finite supplies are exhausted; neither indefinite self-maintenance nor faster physical growth follows. Another checked history shows why legitimate sequential reuse can exceed what one joint reservation admits.

Fresh Lean checking adds **16 named theorems / 32 declaration audits**, with **107 audits** across the source closure. The [proof and premises](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-ALLOCATION-PROOF.md), [capacity evidence](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-CAPACITY-EVIDENCE.md) and [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-ALLOCATION-REVIEW.md) distinguish this untimed result from measured biological throughput. Joint reservation applies to exclusive participants; shared contextual support need not be exclusively occupied. An inferred productive fraction is not automatically a census of all recruitable organization.

## Retained organization through a changing transition

The [next correspondence study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#10-retained-organization-through-a-changing-living-transition) examines a measured nutrient transition before introducing another timing theorem. Its [evidence assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-TRANSITION-EVIDENCE.md) and [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-TRANSITION-REVIEW.md) distinguish a causal initial-condition intervention, coordinated transient observations and model-dependent interpretation.

**旧有组织仍可有效，后续活动所需的组织关系却可以改变。** Retaining constituents and changing the organization of subsequent activity are compatible. Which response is necessary depends on what the specified continuation now requires and what inherited provision can still do. A smaller relative abundance is not by itself destruction or functional failure. The new assessment therefore strengthens the connection between inherited conditions and later activity while preserving successful reserve-based alternatives.

This is an empirical and explanatory advance, with no new mathematical proof claim or repeated Lean run. The existing allocation proof supplies no physical clock, and these observations do not identify individual occupation/release histories or a hard upper bound on inherited capacity. Coordinated experiments can support a causal account without measuring every molecule in one preparation; a formal refinement or an exclusive-necessity claim needs its additional correspondence.

## Expanding provision and retained copies

The [next integrated argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#11-expanding-provision-without-assuming-destruction) concerns the organization that coexisting continuations require together. **旧有组织可以继续存在，却不足以同时支持扩展后的全部需要；先后复用则不能算作同时占有。** Retention, redistribution and new formation are differentiated possibilities within the same history.

The [general proof](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-EXPANSION-PROOF.md) uses explicit assignments of distinct present copies to variable current requirements. It derives the bound on total provision from those assignments. If actually supplied requirements exceed the initial count, an increasing reaction must occur in the actual prefix. A repertoire with no such increase bounds the current requirements by inherited provision. The result concerns coexisting requirements, not cumulative births, a fixed generation clock or consumption of a catalyst on each use.

The [evidence assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-EXPANSION-EVIDENCE.md) tests retained function and interrupted formation through an expanding lineage, and contrasts this with changing demand inside a growing compartment. The [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-EXPANSION-REVIEW.md) preserves the difference between a general structural proof and its biological interpretation. Additional effective organization can be assembly, activation or import. Neither a count increase nor a division count proves physiological success, molecular genealogy, novelty or ethical priority.

The three connected modes are now maintenance under actual compensating demand, retained organization and functional recovery, and expansion or change in jointly required provision. Their general explanation credits sufficient inheritance before requiring a response. A response is adequate only when it supports the independently identified subsequent function.

Fresh Lean 4.29.1 checking adds **14 named theorems / 19 declaration audits**, with **94 audits** in the three-module closure. The proof note and review record exact hypotheses, source bindings and logical dependencies. This is a general current-provision result, not a calibrated biological copy threshold.

## Evidence and scope

The [primary biological assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-MECHANISMS.md) investigates nongrowing maintenance, ion homeostasis and recovery from dormant inheritance. Its interventions support particular mechanisms while retaining alternatives and recovery. The [coverage review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-COVERAGE-REVIEW.md) also preserves persistent constituents and heterogeneous replacement. A specific repair route, constant local production or universal molecular expiry is not established as necessary for all life.

The formal maintained bound is not an irreversible death threshold. The extension now represents intermediate excursions and both directions of finite passive transfer, while [the deeper recovery assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-RECOVERY-EVIDENCE.md) distinguishes signal restoration from resumed function. Changing geometry, physiological organization and correspondence to the paper's stochastic model remain open. Expected biological losses cannot be treated as guaranteed losses on every history. Nor does a theoretical physical-scale estimate establish a positive maintenance constant shared by all life.

These are limits of the current representation and correspondence, with evidence constraining stronger claims. They are not demonstrations that the fuller generative account fails or that living conditions originate outside the unfolding.

## What remains to establish

The general necessity proof is already available. Its new mechanistic realization advances one applicability connection. Broader coverage must explain which still-required relationships become insufficient through actual use, loss, changed demand or transformation, while preserving supported alternatives and the same history. Different living stages can have different relevant dependencies; no fixed list or uniform clock is assumed.

The inherited-provision test now has a general current-allocation argument and targeted interrupted-formation evidence. This advances the expansion branch without requiring old material first to disappear. Correspondence still has to identify actual requirements, alternative provision and the resulting function. A chosen batch partition supplies no physical clock, a mean rate is not a deterministic bound, and a protein ratio cannot define life. If retained provision suffices, no necessity of new formation follows for that interval.

The [coverage audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#synthesis-of-the-living-dependency-modes) retains four requirements: independently identify continuation, establish actual insufficiency where claimed, connect an effective response to subsequent functioning, and warrant the scope across stages and scales. These require adequate mechanism and evidence, not an exhaustive organism catalogue or one completely calibrated molecular model. The [completion audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md#12-full-goal-completion-audit) finds those obligations met for the general account and its declared coverage. Further applications can use existing rate, composition and succession results. Universal eventual insufficiency, a universal maintenance minimum, all-law derivation and ethical authority remain stronger open questions; they are not claimed as consequences of this completion.

The [integrated argument and validation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-DEPENDENCY-INTEGRATION.md), [general continuation results](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md), and [research index](../../../RESEARCH.md) preserve the connection between what is proved, what is empirically supported and what remains open.
