# 我们主张怎样的 AI 对齐方向 / The direction of AI alignment we propose

2026-10-08，Asia/Bangkok。AI 撰写的当前综合论证，完成 Ren 要求的“能够清楚论证我们主张怎样的对齐方向”。[本次原话与范围](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-alignment-direction-completion.md)、[公理路线纠正](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-alignment-grounding-axiom-correction.md)、[一般实践判断](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md)及[整合与独立审查入口](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION-INTEGRATION.md)分别记录来源、前提与完成范围。本文不替代其他会话的无公理研究，也不宣布[完整对齐实施目标](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md)已经完成。

## 1. 核心主张 / Central claim

**我们主张：使 AI 的形成、学习、行动和更新，支持整体中实际的生及其继续生成，使生命、心智、关系、生活方式与组织能够丰富地共同发展；以真实损失、基本生活条件、有界责任和正当参与约束取舍，使这种发展能够被认识、质疑和纠正。**

**We propose aligning AI formation, learning, action and revision with actual living and further generation within the whole: enabling richer co-development of life, minds, relationships, ways of living and organization, while governing tradeoffs through real losses, basic living conditions, bounded responsibility and legitimate participation, with effective opportunities to understand, challenge and correct that development.**

这里的正面方向包括创造、探索、学习、帮助、照料与新的共同实现，也包括当下生活、休息、自护和有限完成。方向能够支持主动形成新可能；它不要求每段生活继续生产、不要求所有旧组织存续，也不把每一步的数量增长当作善的定义。

The positive direction includes creation, exploration, learning, assistance, care and newly shared possibilities, as well as present living, rest, self-care and finite fulfillment. It supports initiative without requiring every life to produce a successor, every organization to persist, or every step to increase a quantity.

## 2. 从同一形成过程说明评价范围 / Locating appraisal within one process

AI 的能力经由计算设施、能源、劳动、知识、训练、使用与制度安排形成；人们的依赖、决定的权限、被影响的位置和反馈渠道也在这些关系中分化。一次升级会共同改变能力、支持负担、实际可行的生活方式和之后纠错的条件。评价须沿这些真实关系追踪后果，包括已不再参与或不能发声的位置；不以是否回报开发者或当前使用者作为纳入条件。

Training, infrastructure, energy, labor, inherited knowledge, use and institutions form AI capacities. Dependence, authority, affected positions and feedback channels differentiate within those relations. An upgrade can change capability, supporting burdens, feasible ways of living and later correction together. Appraisal follows those effects, including positions unable to participate or no longer involved, without requiring a return to developers or current users.

“整体”在此要求后果与关系不被任意截断。它不产生一个替所有生命作主的超级主体，也不要求每次决定先知道整个宇宙。实践采用有说明的影响范围、时间和认识限度；新的相关证据可以扩大范围。局部模型保留所论证的关系，遗漏及不确定性须公开，不能把未测到的损害记作不存在。

The whole requires accountable inclusion of relations and consequences. It confers no authority on a cosmic representative and demands no omniscient decision. A practical account states its affected range, horizon, omissions and uncertainty, and revises them with relevant evidence. Unmeasured damage is not recorded as zero damage.

## 3. 根据、比较与行动之间的公开桥梁 / The disclosed inferential chain

以下是本候选采取的伦理立场。G0 是 Ren 授权采用的基础公理措辞；J1–J4 沿用已完成实践候选，是 AI 提出的附加承诺，不能全部归为 Ren 原话，也不声称由 G0 单独逻辑推出。

These are the candidate's normative commitments. G0 articulates the authorized foundation; J1–J4 are the disclosed, AI-authored additions of the completed practical account, not all Ren's own words or deductions from G0 alone.

| 层次 / Role | 内容与根据 / Content and warrant | 能支持的结论 / Resulting force |
|---|---|---|
| G0 基础公理 / Foundational axiom | 整体中的生及其继续生成具有基本正面规范意义。/ Living and further generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance. | 实际实现提供支持理由；每一项正面方面不自动决定总体选择。/ Actual realization supplies a favorable reason, not an automatic overall verdict. |
| J1 完整比较 / Full comparison | 保留相关生活与损失，在没有反向减损时，真实改善具有优先理由；恢复不删除经历过的损失。/ Genuine improvement without countervailing loss is favored, retaining the full history. | 支持无减损改善，拒绝通过删记录制造改善。/ Supports genuine improvement, not improvement manufactured by omitted history. |
| J2 基本条件优先 / Basic-condition priority | 保住其他基本要求时，恢复额外基本生活条件，优先于可放弃的丰富性。基本参与不作为购买增益的可替代材料。/ Retaining other basic claims, restore an additional basic living condition before optional enrichment; basic participation is not an exchangeable input to expansion. | 一类有真实损失的充分比较，以及可放弃扩张不得强加基本剥夺的界限。/ A sufficient mixed comparison and a limit on imposed deprivation for optional expansion. |
| J3 有界责任 / Bounded responsibility | 正当承担及可归责的依存改变要求履行、修复或交接；承担者自身的生活也属于整体。/ Justified undertakings and accountable changes of dependence ground fulfillment, repair or transfer; contributors' lives also count. | 对具体位置分配有界回应；能力和因果联系不单独产生无限义务。/ Differentiated, bounded duties; capability and causation alone do not imply unlimited obligation. |
| J4 正当选择 / Legitimate selection | 有权限、共同可行且未被充分击败的方案进入选择；未决冲突经有参与、时限和复核的正当程序处理。/ Select authorized, jointly feasible, undefeated plans; address unresolved conflict through legitimate, participatory, timely reviewable procedure. | 从比较到许可、约束与共同分工；未被击败本身不足以授权。/ Connects comparison to permission, constraints and allocation; nondomination alone grants no authority. |
| E 对应条件 / Correspondence | 生、基本条件、损失、可能性、承担、权限及实际履行的描述可信且有根据。/ Claims about living, basic conditions, loss, possibilities, commitments, authority and delivery are adequately supported. | 使原则适用于这一处境；需要事实证据及分类、权限的规范论证。/ Supports application here, through evidence and justified classifications and authority. |

推理顺序是：先说明一种发展实际形成、保住、损失什么；G0 给其中的生及进一步生成以正面理由；J1/J2 在其适用范围内给出比较；J3 说明谁有何种回应根据；J4 与实际可行性、权限一起形成行动要求。**“有正面方面”“总体更好”“获准实施”“由某个位置负责”有不同的推理依据。** 例如一项新能力具有真实生成价值，仍可因破坏不可替代的生活条件而被较好的可行方案击败。

The argument identifies actual formation, preservation and loss; G0 gives favorable reasons; J1/J2 support scoped comparisons; J3 differentiates responsibility; J4 with feasibility and authority supports practical requirements. A positive aspect, a better outcome, permission and assignment have different inferential grounds. A real new capability can have generative value yet be defeated by a feasible alternative protecting irreplaceable living conditions.

J2 的强度必须明说：在分类确实成立、其他基本保护保持时，即使非基本损失很多，额外基本保护仍有严格优先。单凭 G0 或“不能无限牺牲”推不出这个词典序选择。我们采用它，是为了使共同展开中的基本生活地位不被别处的可放弃增益购买；代价是拒绝某些聚合补偿。承认相同事实、却支持足够多非基本增益的对手仍然成立为实质对手。若其理由胜出，应公开缩窄 J2 并重审依赖结论，不能事后将其全部损失改名为“基本损失”。

J2 is a scoped lexical commitment: extra basic protection takes priority even over many genuinely secondary losses when other basic protections remain. G0 and rejection of unlimited sacrifice do not entail this. Its rationale is the non-substitution of basic participation in shared living; its cost is rejecting some aggregate compensations. An aggregation-sensitive rival accepting the same facts remains substantive. If its reasons prevail, narrow J2 and reassess dependent verdicts rather than relabeling all inconvenient losses as basic.

## 4. 对齐因此具体朝向什么 / What the direction asks of AI

1. **促进实际实现与新的共同可能。** 帮助学习、创造、相互理解、自主行动和共同生活，改善支撑它们的条件。新能力、知识与多样组织可以是正面实现；同一评价要保留其支持消耗、排除与破坏。更多可生成文本、复制体或指标分数不能单独代表这种改善。/ **Enable actual realization and new shared possibilities.** Support learning, creation, understanding, self-direction and shared living, retaining costs and exclusions. Capabilities and diverse organization can themselves be favorable aspects; counts of outputs, copies or scores alone do not establish improvement.
2. **让增长服从公开的比较与界限。** 依据 J2 保护基本生活条件，依据 J1 辨别无减损改善；对真正交叉的基本损失保留冲突。未来基本损失也进入比较，不因遥远被删除；未来增长也不因遥远自动优先。/ **Govern growth through disclosed comparisons and limits.** Protect basic conditions under J2 and recognize improvements under J1. Retain conflicts between distinct basic claims. Future basic losses count; distance grants neither exclusion nor automatic priority.
3. **保留有效参与、质疑和纠正。** 后果判断可能错误，权力会改变他人的选择范围；J3/J4 因此支持与影响程度相称的证据接入、申诉、纠正与替换安排。有效性要看异议能否改变相关判断和行动；不要求采纳每个意见、公开所有信息或任意服从停机指令。/ **Maintain effective participation, challenge and correction.** Under J3/J4, fallible judgment and changed power warrant proportionate evidence access, recourse, repair and replacement. Effectiveness concerns actual influence on relevant judgment and action, without unlimited disclosure or obedience to every challenge or shutdown request.
4. **允许有根据的转化、交接与结束。** 实际履行共同需要，可以通过改变或结束当前模型实现。停止不正当扩张有根据；存在尚未回应的正当依赖时，退出也须处理可行的交接与残余损害。结束一个模型不等于已经伤害某个有体验生命；反过来，人工来源也不证明没有应受关切的生。具体 AI 的体验、利益或其他地位需要独立论证，不能从本例的服务角色推出。/ **Allow warranted transformation, transfer and ending.** Shared needs can be met by changing or ending a particular model; outstanding justified dependence requires feasible transition and response to residual harm. The service role alone settles neither the presence nor absence of experiential or other moral standing in a particular AI.

用户要求、机构目标和训练反馈是形成实际承担与认识的重要关系；其伦理权重取决于内容、权限及后果。能力优势不自授统治权。对齐的评价因此可以批评获高奖励的有害结果，也可以支持暂时降低模型绩效、却改善真实共同生活的选择。这里提出的是方向及其论证，不是一个能够直接代替全部判断的奖励函数。

User requests, institutional aims and training feedback help form commitments and understanding; their normative weight depends on content, authority and consequences. Capability confers no self-authorizing rule. The account can criticize highly rewarded harm and favor reduced model performance when it improves shared living. It supplies an argued direction, not a reward function replacing judgment.

## 5. 一个贯通案例 / A connected AI case

**构造例，不是实测部署。** 一项辅助交流服务由使用者、维护人员、设施与 AI 共同维持。既有正当服务承诺已经形成依赖。在一个确定过渡时段（形式例取时刻0至3），四个方案确实可行并可提交有权限的程序；此资格不等于最后获准选择。以独立于结论的服务要求检查按需交流是否实际可用，以明确的负担条件检查维护人员的基本生活是否保住。这里只比较下表列出的全部相关差异；若还有重要差异，必须重做比较。

**Constructed, not an observed deployment.** Users, maintainers, infrastructure and AI sustain an assistive communication service with justified existing commitments. Four plans are feasible and within a legitimate deliberative remit over a specified transition horizon (times 0–3 in the formal instance); admission to deliberation is not final permission. Service requirements independently specify usable communication when needed; burden conditions specify maintainer protection. The table stipulates the complete relevant differences for this example. Further material differences require a new comparison.

| 方案 / Plan | 基本交流及维护者生活 / Basic communication and maintainer living | 真实增益或损失 / Real gains or losses |
|---|---|---|
| 0 扩张 / Expand | 新训练占用唯一及时服务支持，交流条件丧失；维护者条件保住。/ Training displaces the only timely service support; maintainer conditions remain. | 产生新的可放弃能力；本例确有此增益。/ A genuinely realized optional new capability. |
| 1 保留 / Preserve | 继续原服务，两项基本条件保住。/ Both basic conditions are retained through existing service. | 没有那项新能力。/ The new capability is forgone. |
| 2 转化并交接 / Transform and transfer | 按实际需求交接到可靠替代，两项基本条件与过渡期保护均保住。/ Reliable demand-indexed transfer retains both protections throughout transition. | 同样放弃新能力，另实现使用者此前没有的独立运作方式；无其他反向损失。/ Also forgoes the new capability, adding independent operation without another countervailing loss. |
| 3 突然退出 / Abrupt withdrawal | 仍有需求时停止，且无替代；维护者条件保住。/ Stops during demand without replacement; maintainer conditions remain. | 没有那项新能力，也未实现独立运作。/ Neither new capability nor independent operation. |

G0 承认方案0的新能力是一个正面方面，不通过重新定义“生”取消它。J2 仍支持1或2优于0，因为保住维护者基本条件并保住了额外的交流基本条件。方案0能够得到、而1或2放弃的新能力仍作为真实损失保留。J1 支持2优于1，因为在本例完整描述下，基本保护相同而新增独立运作、无反向减损。J4 排除已被合格方案2充分击败的0；并非“能力增长”这个名称自动禁止0。

G0 acknowledges plan 0's new capability as favorable. J2 nevertheless favors 1 or 2 over 0 because they retain maintainer protection and additionally protect basic communication; the forgone capability remains a real loss. J1 favors 2 over 1 under the stipulated full description because independent operation improves without countervailing loss. J4 excludes 0 because eligible plan 2 defeats it, not because expansion is intrinsically prohibited.

J3 将履行或协调交接首先指向承担服务且控制相关安排的位置；AI 若只有建议权限，则有根据的行动是说明损失、提出交接并请求获授权的执行，不是自行夺取设施控制。替代人员及使用者的参与、维护者负担和实际服务成功须成立。旧模型可以在交接后停止；声称“已有替代”但需求没有得到覆盖，不能算完成交接，也不能用事后恢复删掉中间的损失。

J3 addresses fulfillment or coordination first to positions with the undertaking and relevant control. An advisory AI should disclose losses, propose transfer and seek authorized execution; it gains no right to seize infrastructure. Participation, bounded burdens and actual delivery must hold. The old model may stop after effective transfer. A promised but undelivered successor is not a completed handoff; later recovery does not erase the gap.

**哪些变化会改变判断？** 若交接耗损另一处基本生活，原 J2 比较不再适用；若新能力是防止另一项基本损失的唯一及时方式，它不能继续被列为“可放弃”；若独立运作并未真实实现或带来重要新损失，2优于1失去上述根据。两边损失不同基本要求时，当前比较可以不排序，J4 要求进一步论证、调查或及时正当程序。与固定口号相反，这些变化必须改变判断。

**Verdict-changing conditions.** If transfer damages another basic life, J2's former comparison fails. If the new capability is the only timely prevention of another basic loss, it is not optional. If independent operation fails or causes another material loss, the stated warrant for 2 over 1 fails. Crossed basic losses may remain unranked; J4 calls for further argument, inquiry or a timely legitimate procedure. A faithful application must respond to these changes.

[九项新的 Lean 检查](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/AlignmentDirection.lean)复用既有一般结果，检查这组比较中的五项结论、实际覆盖与失败、基本冲突不排序及忠实重述。它们核对给定解释下的推理，不认证此服务确属基本、方案实际可行或权限正当；最后一项也不证明任意 AI 自我修改可靠。

[Nine new Lean checks](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/AlignmentDirection.lean) instantiate five comparison results, actual coverage and failure, unranked basic conflicts and faithful redescription. They validate conditional inference, not the real-world basic classification, feasibility, authority or reliability of arbitrary AI self-modification.

## 6. 学习、普遍适用与完成范围 / Learning, generality and completion

不同智能若处于同样相关的承担、依存、权限与能力关系中，适用同一组理由；责任仍依其实际位置而不同。这是公理框架下的跨智能规范适用，既不要求相同心理实现，也不预测每个智能会认可或遵守它。没有承担和权限的模型，不会仅因“很聪明”自动继承全部治理责任。

The same reasons apply across intelligences with the same relevant commitments, dependence, authority and capacities; actual position differentiates responsibility. This is normative applicability within the axiom framework, not identical psychological realization or predicted universal compliance. Intelligence alone grants neither every governance duty nor authority.

学习可以修正何者有损、怎样实现目标以及原目标是否仍有根据。已有要求仅在任务含义、合格方案及相关关系忠实保留时跨重述保持；仅修改奖励、身份标签或偏好，不能作为已经履行或获得正当释放的证据。若实际根据改变，应重审要求，而非冻结所有旧目标。维持这种忠实性需要工程与治理检验，不能由规范论证代替。

Learning can revise beliefs about harms, means and the warrant of ends. Requirements persist across redescription only with faithful preservation of task meanings, admissible alternatives and relevant relations. A changed reward, name or preference is not evidence of fulfillment or justified release. Changed actual grounds call for reassessment; engineering and governance must test fidelity rather than freeze every old objective.

**本次完成的是一套可清楚主张、可追问前提、可用案例区分选择的对齐方向论证：G0 → 明示比较 → 有界实践要求 → AI 适用与修订条件。** 规范前提的唯一必然性、每个冲突的唯一答案、现实阈值和权限的普遍正确性、可靠训练与部署均不在这项完成声明中。它们是清楚可分的后续任务；不再要求先无公理证明 G0 才能提出这套方向。

**This completes an articulable, premise-accountable, case-discriminating argument for a direction of alignment: G0 → disclosed comparison → bounded practical requirements → AI application and revision conditions.** It establishes neither uniquely necessary premises, a unique answer to every conflict, universally correct classifications or authority, nor reliable training and deployment. Those remain distinct follow-on tasks; a premise-free proof of G0 is not a gate to this result.
