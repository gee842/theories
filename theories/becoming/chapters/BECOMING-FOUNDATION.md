9 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. Direction: Yongxu Ren. This formulation and its formal interpretation are AI-authored, following [Ren's proposal and priority instruction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-theory-of-becoming-priority.md) and [completion continuation](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-theory-of-becoming-continuation.md). **The candidate explains how activity forms, sustains, transforms and dissolves operative relations within one unfolding. Where their effects continue, those effects participate in the conditions of further activity.** Generation, loss and mixed consequences belong to that unfolding. Relatively enduring organization, life, inherited change, knowing and evaluation are differentiated achievements within it. Their actual relationships must be explained; naming them does not establish separate substances or derive their existence. The temporal order appropriate to an explanation must also be warranted, rather than inferred from the order in which a model lists events.

**核心候选：活动在同一展开中形成、维持、转化或消解作用关系；其后果若继续起作用，便参与后续活动的条件。** 生成、损失及混合后果都属于同一展开；组织、生命、遗传变化、认识与评价通过具体关系分化。时间次序也须说明根据，不能从模型的书写次序直接取得。理论不先把变化限定为善，也不预设普遍增长、无限延续或固定整体效用。

## The whole and its generative forms / 整体与其中的生成形态

[Ren clarifies the claim](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-becoming-whole-generative-manifold.md): the Theory of Becoming as a whole is deductively necessary; generativity is part of its manifold. **The foundational deduction concerns Becoming as a whole. It must not require every becoming to be generative.** The earlier conversational demand for a universally generative relationship imposed the narrower theme on the larger account.

The whole is the relational unfolding through which these forms differentiate, not an independently constituted container with separate processes inserted into it. Generation, persistence, transformation, degeneration and dissolution can overlap in one activity. A loss need not become beneficial or produce a successor to belong within Becoming. Whether a particular change is generative remains a substantive question about its relationships and effects.

**整体的演绎必然性是根基主张；生成性是其 manifold 中的部分。** 不把局部生成规律普遍化作为整体成立的门槛，也不把真实退化或终止重新命名为生成以保全整体。这里的形态区分不预设互斥、穷尽分类或微分几何结构。须先明确整体有哪些必要内容，再说明其中生成形态的具体条件。

This corrects the scope of the deduction. Ren's necessity claim is recorded as stated; the existing proof record does not yet establish that entire claim, and the correction is not introduced as a new axiom. The conditional results below keep their supplied laws and correspondence. Their boundaries test their particular formulations; failure of one generative mode is not by itself a failure of the encompassing account.

## 1. The explanatory whole and its ethical orientation

Ren's proposal removes a requirement that would distort this explanatory task: a becoming need not deserve affirmation to be real or explicable. Destruction, failed learning, exhaustion and cessation are therefore positive explanatory obligations, not exceptions to be screened out. A loss can contribute to another formation without ceasing to be a real loss at the relationship concerned.

G0 gives living and further generation positive normative significance; the disclosed J commitments develop comparison and responsibility. Those completed ethical results concern this same reality. Their axioms need not be used to prove how a relation forms or disappears. Explaining how a standard acquires influence, recognizing its target, justifying its authority and actually acting on it remain distinct tasks. A formed standard can be wrong.

This is an organization of questions within one account. It does not split facts and values into independent worlds. Nor does it make ethical judgment a filter selecting a previously fixed good subset: consequences can be mixed and their relationships can change.

## 2. What the whole possibility space must retain

“Include generation and degeneration” concerns the scope of the explanation. A second question concerns how its possible histories fit together. A family of permitted possibilities cannot in general be turned into one actual process simply by pooling its displayed steps and forgetting which relationships support each one. This is a test of one proposed representation, not an attribution of that mistake to Ren and not a disproof of monism.

The [new checked result](../results/records/becoming-context-coherence.md) establishes the exact finite boundary. For a nonempty family of processes using the same state and event interpretations, pooling their steps preserves exactly their union of endpoint histories **if and only if that union is closed under joining histories through their shared represented boundary**. Otherwise the pooling invents a history that no included process supports. The represented boundary has omitted a consequential distinction.

The positive construction retains the operative context alongside the displayed conditions. It includes each original history exactly, without requiring different contexts to be separate substances. It then allows context itself to change through a joint relation. The possible successor contexts follow from the contexts actually supported by the incoming history and the permitted joint event. No fresh selection from all contexts is inserted between steps.

**包容全部结果，不等于可以任意拼接结果的成立条件。** 已证有限条件：合并步骤恰好保留原历史并集，当且仅当该并集对共同边界接合封闭。保留作用情境可以精确保留原历史；情境也能在同一活动中真实改变。接续成立所需的是关系上的实际相容，既不要求情境永恒不变，也不要求变化总是有利。

The finite control includes a context-changing event, its enabled continuation and a terminal configuration. The mathematics supplies no probability distribution, implemented observer, physical mechanism or ethical verdict. A context variable is an analytical description of relevant relations within the history, not an independently existing controller. Whether an actual situation is represented by these fields must be warranted separately.

## 4. The zero-axiom target and its present boundary

The strong target remains: can the common account obtain its substantive foundational warrant without added substantive axioms? Absence of ethical axioms is one relevant distinction, not a replacement objective. The proof language, inferential rules, definitions, supplied relations, actual correspondence and normative commitments must each remain visible.

The new coherence result has a necessary conditional form: **given the represented histories, their composition cannot acquire support by erasing an incompatibility.** Its proof uses finite inductive traces and their checked composition. It does not assume G0, inevitable improvement, totality or infinite continuation. This advances the account's necessary treatment of whole histories.

The further inference concerns the necessary content of **Becoming as a whole**. The candidate must establish how its differentiated determinations belong to one relational unfolding, including persistence and loss; it must also warrant the inference and interpretation used to establish that content. This does not require a productive effect or successor for every occurrence. The [earlier assumption-free inquiry](ASSUMPTION-FREE-FOUNDATION.md) investigated several stronger operative claims. Its completed local reductions, direct constitutive arguments and failed promotions remain useful at their actual scopes; their failure cannot simply become the whole theory's failure criterion. In particular, this task does not begin by assuming an inert space of independent things and demand that a relation be added from outside it.

Nor does the target automatically require deriving the numerical value of every contingent local law. The obligation is to warrant whatever universal content the theory actually asserts, and to keep the particular content used in an application explicit. Calling an input immanent does not discharge it; finding an input in a local model does not establish that it is unexplained throughout the theory.

## 7. Operative relations and causal order

The whole is articulated through the relationships that actually sustain, connect or interrupt activity. An account of their formation must preserve the operations being explained and their joint consequences. It must also distinguish the order of its calculation, the temporal organization of an implementation, and the causal order attributed to the identified operations. These are analytical distinctions within the same unfolding, with potentially different resolutions.

The [primary-source assessment and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#17-operative-relations-without-a-presupposed-global-order) examines a substantive comparison: the quantum switch and the loss of its control coherence. The published account distinguishes a coherent arrangement from a classical mixture while retaining the two order alternatives. Our inference for this foundation is that recording those alternatives alone does not identify the operative relationship or explain its loss. This applies the earlier requirement to preserve consequences to a specific physical-theory interface; it is not a new quantum theorem or experimental result. [Branciard, sections II.2 and IV.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.00043v1).

The resulting scope is precise. The candidate must not require a definite causal order at **every** identified operational interface. It may use an enlarged sequential description where a justified correspondence preserves the original operations and their joint responses. Conversely, listing numerical calculation steps is not that correspondence. The existing `Trace` results remain valid mathematical results and may still apply at an appropriate descriptive level; no counterexample to those theorems is claimed.

**A continuing effect is an explanatory relationship to establish, not a universal memory postulate.** Some relations persist, others are transformed or lost; an ending need not leave a surviving operative successor. For temporally ordered mechanisms, the existing formation-to-use constructions already establish particular continuing effects. For other interfaces, an account must earn the relevant composition before applying that temporal explanation.

**作用关系、实现时序与模型次序须分清。** 同一展开中的关系怎样成立、怎样改变共同后果，是共同问题；不能把每个操作都具有确定全局因果次序作为未经论证的前提。既有有限历史证明继续有效。量子比较要求保留操作与联合响应，并区分关系丧失和仅仅删去一个选项。这里修订的是候选论述的适用范围；它没有用“凡有持续后果就有持续后果”替代实质解释，也没有把更强零公理目标缩成无伦理公理。实际作用关系及其普遍构成根据仍须建立。

## 8. Local loss within a retained whole

The [checked continuation](../results/records/control-coherence-loss.md) now derives one operative comparison rather than leaving it as a literature analogy. A specified preparation, controlled X/Z order, interaction with a blank register and reduction produce different control responses within one composed account. The operations and quantum interpretation remain supplied; their consequences are checked by Lean.

| Stage / 阶段 | Control matrix numerator / 控制矩阵分子 | Probability of the same plus outcome / 同一正向测量结果的概率 |
|---|---|---|
| Prepared control / 已制备控制 | `[[1, 1], [1, 1]]` | 1 |
| After controlled X/Z order / 经受控X/Z次序 | `[[1, -1], [-1, 1]]` | 0 |
| After complete dephasing / 完全退相干后 | `[[1, 0], [0, 1]]` | 1/2 |

Each displayed matrix is divided by 2 for its density interpretation. Both branch weights remain 1/2. The response difference is obtained from the off-diagonal contribution, which a branch-weight-only description omits. Retaining that contribution exactly recovers this particular measurement response; it is not claimed sufficient for every possible measurement.

The loss has an explicit relational realization. A reversible controlled interaction correlates the control with an initially blank register. Restricting access to the control gives exactly the dephased matrix. No function of that reduced matrix alone can recover both different original preparations, because both have the same reduced output. Retaining the joint interaction permits its reversal and restores the original control. The proof therefore distinguishes unavailable local discrimination, retained wider relations, and the capacity to use those relations for restoration.

**局部退化可以和更广关系的形成发生在同一活动中。** 本例中局部测量区别丧失，但在保留的共同关系上反向操作可以恢复。不能据此说局部损失不真实，也不能把约化后的不可恢复升格为整个现实的绝对毁灭；实际是否仍可控制相关环境，决定恢复论述的范围。退化与恢复在此是明确操作关系的变化，不是先验伦理判定。

## 9. The content of the whole deduction

Relations form, hold, change and cease within the unfolding being investigated. An account of a lost relationship can preserve its loss without requiring that loss to produce a new capacity. An account of persistence can explain the relevant continuing relationships without asserting that every determination changes at every moment. This leaves the whole genuinely wider than its generative forms.

The [direct argument and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#19-the-whole-deduction-and-the-passage-from-inclusion-to-relation) distinguish three claims often compressed into “the whole”:

| Claim / 主张 | What its warrant must establish / 根据须建立什么 |
|---|---|
| Inclusive scope / 包容范围 | A whole account undertakes to include the occurrence of inquiry, loss and persistence as well as generation. This states its undertaking; it does not yet establish its adequacy. |
| Relational unity and differentiation / 关系整体与分化 | The determinations being explained have their constitution within the same unfolding. Shared inclusion in a description cannot replace the argument for this substantive claim. |
| Particular operative forms / 具体作用形态 | Explain the relationships through which a claimed formation, persistence, loss or restoration occurs. Their conditions and consequences may differ, and no single generative mechanism is required everywhere. |

The affirmative route begins with actual determination and its articulation, including how this inquiry identifies and relates its content. For an established relationship, suppressing its constitutive role changes the phenomenon to be explained. This credits the existing constitutive argument. The next step would establish the relational constitution of the whole's determinations, rather than merely collect established instances. That step remains to be demonstrated: an inclusive description can admit an occurrence without yet explaining how it is constituted, and describing a contrast does not by itself establish every relation attributed to its referent. These distinctions remain within the same inquiry and reality.

The primary comparison now also distinguishes a philosophical category of becoming from a supplied causal mechanism. The [study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#primary-comparison-the-category-does-not-already-supply-a-cause) examines Hegel's explicit restriction on that inference, and his treatment of coming-to-be, ceasing-to-be and determinate stability. It does not identify his argument with Ren's theory or import its conclusions as premises.

**整体不是“所有情况”这个名称本身。** 包容范围说明理论要解释什么；关系整体主张说明这些规定怎样在同一展开中成立；具体作用说明某种形态怎样发生。三者须接通，不能用包容替代根据，也不能以每种变化都有生成后效作为整体的必要条件。此前把共同生成法则处处成立作为完成门槛的用法撤回，相关条件定理与历史尝试保留原范围。

The strong zero-added-substantive-axiom objective remains unchanged. This assessment supplies neither a new mathematical proof nor a proof that the objective is impossible. Its substantive correction is to locate the missing deduction in relational unity and differentiation at the whole scope, while preserving the explanatory work already earned for particular forms.
