## 1. What the scope question actually requires

Universal explanatory ambition does not mean that one local mechanism, boundary or collection of test cases applies to every system. Nor does it mean every attribution made in the theory is already correct. The earlier account already excluded exhaustive inspection of every case and one mandatory architecture. The present distinction clarifies its universal-scope question; it does not claim to discover or retract an explicit earlier demand for infallibility.

There are two continuing obligations: establish what the general account explains, and establish why a particular application instantiates the relations it attributes. The former cannot be replaced by a list of successful applications. The latter need not await a complete inventory of the universe. Warrant can be local and substantive without certifying an unlimited claim.

An operation can constitute the relationship being investigated. If a preparation copies an operative value and the subsequent operation actually reads that copy, the same history supplies evidence for correspondence and use. A second, correlated record may predict the operation equally well without participating in it. The evidence must distinguish those roles when the claim concerns constitution, even if they are interchangeable for a selected prediction.

## 2. Match the grounds to the claim

| Claim | Required kind of support | What a successful observed run alone does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| This joint variation occurred | A warranted record or encounter with that preparation and continuation | That it can be repeated now |
| This continuation is currently possible | A supported realization from the current conditions, retaining the required organization, access, timing and provision | That historical conditions still hold |
| This relation is sufficient for the selected answer | Coverage of the relevant admitted cases, or a supported general argument that preserves the answer across them | That no untested joint effect exists |
| This continuation is excluded | Constraints covering every relevant route to it | That failure to observe or attempt it makes it impossible |
| This is exactly the range of possibilities | Both positive realization and exclusion of additional possibilities at the claimed scope | That the observed cases exhaust the range |

These are different questions about one activity, not separate kinds of reality. A witness supports an existential claim. A justified conservative range can support a universal claim even if it contains some possibilities not actually realized. A precise statement of every possibility requires both kinds of support. This distinction prevents treating complete identification of a domain as a prerequisite for every useful explanation.

The same distinction constrains criticism. One actual counterexample can defeat a universal claim about an adequate description. The absence of a counterexample in a finite protocol cannot alone certify it. Relevant alternatives must preserve the target and the available evidence or explain why an attribution is mistaken; mere imaginable disagreement does not automatically defeat ordinary warrant.

## 3. Derive variations through the organization being explained

Begin with the preparation and operations actually under consideration. Follow which joint conditions they form and which remain reachable. State the supporting conditions for any transfer from an earlier preparation to a present capability. Such an account earns a variation range through its relation to the organization; it does not assume that independently named coordinates are independently changeable.

Preparation and intervention are one route to warrant. Observational, historical and independently justified constraint evidence can also warrant relations where manipulation is unavailable. No ability to experiment on every system is required.

For the preceding Boolean example, the initial comparison domain had been written as equality of the two coordinates. The new [Lean continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/VARIATION-WARRANT-FORMAL.md) proves that this domain is exactly what can be reached after the actual `correlate` event of the unchanged formal process, from every initial pair. Every reached endpoint has equal coordinates. Conversely, either equal-coordinate endpoint is reached by a preparation needing at most one subsequent `flip`.

Thus the equal-coordinate domain is no longer an independently selected input for that prepared process. Its exclusion and realization directions are both derived. The connection extends to arbitrary finite continuations; the formal study records the exact endpoint and history scope. The proof does not replace a long history by a short preparation when the question concerns provenance or costs. Equal endpoints can retain different histories.

**19 new Lean theorems / 21 explicit named declarations** pass with empty axiom dependencies; 59 inherited declaration audits retain their previous dependency maps. The [integration and checks](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/VARIATION-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md) record the fresh compiler run and [independent assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/VARIATION-WARRANT-REVIEW.md). This closes a definite local dependency. It does not prove that every actual system has Boolean coordinates or these operations. A law appearing in a local model is not thereby external to generative reality, but its actual applicability still needs evidence. The formal result earns the domain relative to the process it analyzes; it does not earn the entire process's physical interpretation by itself.

## 4. Actual evidence can warrant participation and joint variation

The [primary-source assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/VARIATION-WARRANT-REVIEW.md) reexamines existing cases at this more precise interface. The documented TCLab program passes its retained integral into the next computation. Source order and the already checked archived records support actual use at that software interface. A complete account of alternate thermal responses requires further apparatus correspondence; changing recorded input numbers does not perform a heater experiment.

The bacterial oxygen study specifies whole experimental preparations, including medium, vessel, atmospheric conditions, handling and measurement. Its performed comparisons support particular joint variations and outcomes. They do not establish that every coordinate in an analyst's description can be varied independently, that oxygen is the sole mediator of every effect, or that related assays form one fully tracked culture history. The original ecological explanation already earns these contributions and receives credit.

The [source-reliance study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SOURCE-RELIANCE-INTEGRATION.md) also provides an established warning: a concrete omitted joint effect passes the supplied calibration and then produces a wrong definite answer. That result concerns its declared model and is not a general defeat of evidence. It shows why separate successful perturbations cannot silently certify their combination. The present account preserves this finding rather than declaring model coverage solved by more agreement.

## 5. Why this can justify an attribution without defining it true

The target and evidence constrain the attribution independently of the preferred vocabulary. A retained quantity must be shown to enter the actual operation if the claim concerns its use. A preparation must provide the organization it is said to provide. An alleged invariant must survive every step covered by its justification. An unperformed manipulation needs an account of its means and preservation conditions. The account is revised when one of these connections fails.

The resulting warrant comes from the content and specificity of these connections, not from naming a model “adequate.” The inquiry itself also has formed access, retained evidence, interpretive practices and failure possibilities. Their inclusion in the unfolding makes them investigable; it does not automatically make their conclusions true. Common causes, redundant sources and errors shared by a record and its check remain substantive alternatives where supported.

This is a general form of accountable explanation with case-specific grounds. It can justify more than a single observed output: a supported mechanism or invariant can carry a conclusion across a stated class. Which class is warranted remains answerable to its construction and evidence. Conventional scientific accounts that preserve these connections are successful articulations, not competitors defeated by different terminology.

## 6. What is resolved and what remains

**Resolved at the local formal scope:** the formerly supplied post-formation domain is derived from the unchanged operation and its continuations, including positive reachability and exclusion. The reason its variations are admitted is their proved realizability in that process, not membership in an arbitrary predicate.

**Resolved as a scope clarification:** an exact inventory of all physical variations is not required for every claim. Occurrence, present capability, invariance, exclusion and exact characterization require different grounds. A past witness does not automatically transfer to current capability; a conservative universal argument need not identify every possibility exactly.

**Still unresolved at the stronger foundational scope:** the general warrant for the actual relations, recognition and inferential practices attributed throughout system-forming reality. A useful warrant procedure is not itself proof that all its applications are correct, or that the three proposed root commitments have thereby been established without remaining assumptions. The new proof and evidence assessment do not certify that stronger passage. No impossibility result is asserted.

The next warranted investigation should identify a specific claimed extension and test whether its actual preparation, continuing mechanism and joint constraints support it. Where those are already established, reuse them. Where they are absent, leave that extension open rather than making every completed mechanism wait for an unrelated global guarantee.

**中文结论。** 这次不再把“可接纳变化”一律作为外加集合：在既有形式机制中，由实际形成与接续完整导出该域；同时把发生见证、当前实现、普遍约束与精确范围分清。对应可以取得有内容的局部根据，不必先穷尽一切可能。但同一过程中的观察与推理并不因此自动无误，完整范围的无假设奠基仍不能冒称完成。
