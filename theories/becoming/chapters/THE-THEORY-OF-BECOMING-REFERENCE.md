## From “In one sentence” (original English, line 32)

**The Theory of Becoming explains formation, persistence, transformation, degeneration and dissolution within one relational unfolding, and how life, evolution, knowing and evaluation differentiate within it, without first assuming that becoming is good.** The theory uses no axiom (Part I): it states what holds of every unfolding, what each premise adds, where the dynamics stop deciding, and how evaluation holds for any significance (sections 5–6), the generative space included: there a part widens what can form, and with “better” read as widening, C is a law (section 6.13). The former Part II asserted one axiom and nothing else, Ren's proposed “for anything, its continuation into the future has significance”; on 16 October 2026 Ren folded it into Part I as one conditional section after the generative space (section 6.15): the theory does not assert the axiom, and states what follows with it as a condition. With it as a condition, the Universal Metaethical Core's G0, that living and further generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance, is a theorem (sections 6.8 and 6.15). G0 is no longer an axiom: Ren folded it on 11 October 2026, and Part I states its content with no axiom and shows that the dynamics do not decide it (section 5). With explicit practical premises, G0 supplies reasons addressed to every situated intelligence and a normative framework for AI alignment. Without the axiom (route (b), section 6.9), what a center has its own reason to count reaches only its feeding closure, what its continuation depends on: a loss there can return through a feeder, and one beyond it need not. So that route reaches the self and what it depends on, and the whole only where the whole is one feeding closure. A center's own feedback registers at most what can affect it, its influence closure, while the comparison with and without its own part is decided within what the part's exchange reaches (section 6.10). The axiom decomposes into the self's presupposition, that the continuation of what lies within its boundary has significance, and one step: that the presupposition does not change with how the self's boundary is drawn (section 6.11). The step carries all of the axiom's content beyond the presupposition, so the decomposition locates it and does not lower the axiom. These readings are the AI's, adopted on Ren's delegation of 15 October 2026.

**Correction notes.**

- “C is a law” (section 6.13): C's no-loss extension on level paths is exactly widening by definition (`Better := Widens`, `Web.paths_comparison_widens`); that widening is better is the open warrant (review A12).
- “G0 … is a theorem”: with the condition, the step is immediate; `g0_of_unrestricted` and `relevance_least` unpack the definitions. Living significance is the continuation condition restricted to living claims, and the condition gives significance to every claim, not a positive valence (review A2).
- “the dynamics do not decide it”: `Web.significance_undetermined` toggles a free significance parameter; it shows that the represented dynamics, which contain no significance, do not entail living significance (review A12).
- The sentences from “Without the axiom (route (b), section 6.9)” to the end are moved, not deleted: their content belongs to sections 6.9–6.11 and 6.15 (outside part 1).

## The theory stated in full: introduction (original English, line 95)

This section states the theory once as a whole: the whole and its laws, then the parts carved by premises, the unity of coming to be and ceasing, the conditions of further activity, and the places of evolution, knowing and evaluation. Items 1–9 and the structural half of item 10 belong to Part I, which uses no axiom and closes with [Part I stated whole](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#第一部分的总陈述--part-i-stated-whole); of the normative half of item 10, G0, the content and standing belong to Part I, with no axiom (sections 5–6), and it holds under the one axiom, the condition of section 6.15, which the theory does not assert. Each item is marked as **representation** (how a world is represented: discrete steps, natural-number amounts and a shared index identifying conditions with relata; that what is counted is decided is not a commitment of the representation but the own condition of the laws that count or name a step, stated inside them, which classically always holds and, asked of every world, is excluded middle, `Becoming.Decidedness`), a **law** (no premise, holds of every world so represented, checked), a **theorem** (holds under disclosed premises, checked), an **axiom** (Ren's proposed one axiom, which enters only as the condition of section 6.15 and is not asserted; G0 is folded and no longer an axiom), a **reading** (decided by the AI on Ren's delegation of 15 October 2026, which Ren may reverse; what stays Ren's is in section 8, [decision record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md)) or **open**.

## Item 1. The space (original English, line 99)

1. **The space.** The whole is every world: relata read with one kind, the flow among them and their web (`World`). Formations and conditions are two roles of one relatum, and the two-kind representation embeds in the one-kind reading (`Becoming.Roles`, `World.ofRoles`). ## Item 2. Laws of the whole (original English, lines 100–105)

   - [arithmetic identity] Over any stretch, an amount at the end with the falls between is the amount at the start with the rises between (`over_stretch`).
   - [consequence of two-valuedness and finiteness] With the determinations counted decided, in a finite family coming to be and ceasing to be keep pace, each recurring exactly when the other does (`keep_pace`, `coming_recurs_iff`); in a finite world whose activity is decided, arising recurs exactly when ceasing does (`World.arising_iff_ceasing`); asked of every finite world without decidability, this law is equivalent to excluded middle (`World.law_decides`, `World.law_iff_em`); with excluded middle as a hypothesis it holds of every finite world (`World.law_of_em`); classically every activity is decided. Without a model no finite record refutes a claim about what follows it (`Web.tail_unexcluded`), and refutation relative to a model can err (`Stationary.model_error`). The blindness below a threshold and beyond the registration closure is conditional on supplied response hypotheses; where others respond to the stock itself a higher stock can end the formation first (`Attracting.higher_breaks_first`). What an act does is testable only relative to a model of the history without it (`Flow.beyond_needs_model`).
- **Controls.** Which projection the step factors through is decided neither by the dynamics nor by the record: a wrong projection is never refuted while the moves follow what it leaves out, and is refuted only by moves varied against it (`Confound.confounded`, `Confound.varied_refutes`).
- **Targets.** Which targets actual formations hold, and how targets form, are hijacked or transmitted, are not decided by the dynamics; nor is it derived that a seeker pursues its target (`Becoming.Seeking`).
- **Descriptions.** Which description a formation uses is not decided by the dynamics; the description that distinguishes nothing always carries a law (`goAlike_const`), carrying a law is monotone neither toward coarser nor toward finer descriptions (`Coarse`, `Switching`), and a true mechanism is not fixed by the record at a projected configuration not yet taken (`Run.not_fixesThrough_of_goAlike`).
- **Where moves and laws come from.** Whether a move is formed within the unfolding, and by what, is not decided by the dynamics: the law only leaves the next move open (`withMove_open`). Nor is which law a formation takes its history to show: where a history revisits, the law it shows admits a history it never took (`Splice.spliced`). Determinacy as equality depends on how configurations are named (`Twins.alike_one`).
- **Hypotheses and classes.** Which hypotheses, classes or lumpings a formation holds or can reach, whether its registration tells values apart and whether its world is held by a list are not decided by the dynamics; data alone fix no untried value (`every_determines_nothing`), a generalization is right on condition that the world goes alike across the lumping (`lumped_right_iff`), and a learner's record never shows that it has come to rest (`no_prefix_fixes_next_finite`).
- **Registration and context.** What a formation registers, and so which split makes sense for it, is not decided by the dynamics; given what is registered, the split that makes sense is unique in what it identifies (`Becoming.Unfolding`, `Becoming.Description`). Whether claims are context-free is a commitment of the representation, not decided by the dynamics: the frame indexed by context always fits, a context-free one only where the registrations glue, and quantum registrations do not glue (`Contextual.indexed_fits`, `Contextual.fitsFree_iff`, `no_context_free_values`). From within, no registration holds every reading about itself at once (`Frame.self_incomplete`); any given reading can be registered at the next step (`Diagonal.Unfolds`).
- **The bound.** How many states a situation has is not decided by the dynamics; the situation supplies it. Given it, everything registered is derived from a finite record, and a learner not given it is right from a step it gives on, without knowing when; not given, no finite record suffices (`Becoming.Learning`, `Becoming.Identification`). This holds as far as runs do not part, from 2(T + N) − 1 on where they part only before step T, and not parting is exactly where a learner given the world can be right: a branching that shows in what is registered is fixed by neither the record nor the law but by the situation, and no record shows that a world will not part; weights are numbers of situations, most situations of a kind give frequencies near the ratio of the weights, and which situation happens is not a count (`Becoming.Parting`, `Becoming.Typicality`, `Becoming.Situations`).
- **Significance.** The laws and theorems give no part significance, and do not say which part should continue: on any web with an event that lives, living significance holds where everything has significance and fails where nothing has (`Web.significance_undetermined`): the represented dynamics contain no significance, so they do not entail it. Here living significance is a condition, not an axiom (section 5). With no axiom its content is characterized exactly: on a connected whole with a living claim it is a seed with both boundary neutralities (`Valuation.significant_iff`); where every living claim is held it is self-significance at every standpoint with standpoint neutrality (`Valuation.significant_iff_held`); on level paths, with every claim living and positive relevance read as significance from every standpoint, it is the continuation condition (`Web.unrestricted_iff_paths_g0`). Reading a part's widening, narrowing and keeping the same as Ren's generative, degenerative and neutral is an AI reading (section 2, [D2](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md)). Section 6.15 takes the continuation condition as a hypothesis: under it living significance is a theorem, immediately, being the condition restricted to living claims (the condition gives significance to every claim, not a positive valence), retaining what has significance is inclusion, and a part loses a continuation with significance exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin (section 6.8).

**IV. How evaluation holds for any significance (sections 5–6).** With no axiom, for any significance and valuation; the continuation condition enters only as a hypothesis:
- **What living significance is.** On a connected whole with a living claim, living significance is exactly a seed with both boundary neutralities, and every rival accepts the same rule inside a formed boundary (`Valuation.significant_iff`, sections 6.1–6.2). Where every living claim is held, it is exactly self-significance at every standpoint with standpoint neutrality (`Valuation.significant_iff_held`, section 6.7). Positive relevance being read from significance, living significance says that every realized living claim's continuation has significance from every standpoint (sections 5 and 6.12).
- **Connection and reasons.** Under the correspondence discipline a common condition connects the whole, and under relational neutrality significance passes along co-participation (section 6.3); D follows from A, and a sound inquiry gives every position with access a contributory reason (`universal_reason`, section 6.4).
- **Retaining and adding.** For any significance, the levels with a part retain what has significance without it exactly when the part's net draw stays within the margin, and losing and adding have equally exact boundaries, with balance needed only at the conditions the continuations compared require, across the steps before each (`Flow.retains_iff_within_margin_at`, `Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`, `Flow.adds_iff_within_margin_at`, sections 6.8 and 6.13). The fewer continuations have significance, the more is retained (`Web.Retains.of_fewer`); centers stopping at their own dependence disagree and license the free rider (`Clearing.centers_disagree`).
- **The generative space.** The levels with a part widen those without it exactly when the part's net draw stays within the margin for every formation the levels without it admit and, at some step, brings within the margin one they do not admit, again with balance only where it is used (`Flow.widens_iff_within_margin_at`); widening goes one way, is transitive, and excludes keeping the same and being mixed; the comparison over the whole is the comparison within every region at once (`Web.includes_iff_within_every`, section 6.13).
- **Reach.** What a continuation depends on is its feeding closure (section 6.9); any significance covering what a part reaches gives the comparison by inclusion (`Flow.Balances.comparison_within_reach`, section 6.10).
- **The condition itself.** On frames whose claims are formations at steps, each realized somewhere, the continuation condition is living significance for anything (`unrestricted_iff_g0`; on level paths with no further condition, `Web.unrestricted_iff_paths_g0`); where the whole is an admissible boundary of every self it is exactly the presupposition sub-true and boundary-invariant at every standpoint, route (b) being the same presupposition at narrow scope (`unrestricted_iff_presupposed`, `presupposition_scopes`, section 6.11); significance bounded by the whole is the condition (`bounded_whole`).
- **Comparison.** What C ranks varies with the reading compared (section 6.5); courses are compared on possibilities, which with the condition as a hypothesis is the condition's comparison (`Ranking.possible_extends_iff`, section 6.12); on level paths, C's no-loss extension is exactly widening, which holds by definition, so with “better” read as widening what can form C holds with no axiom (`Web.paths_comparison_widens`, section 6.13); that widening is better is the open question of warrant.

This is the completeness of a formal statement in the sense of the four checkable criteria of [plan section 9](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-UNIFICATION-PLAN.md#9-走向完整的生成之论--toward-a-complete-theory-of-becoming), not a proof of necessity. The theory aims at the strongest claim with no axiom; that it is the strongest has not been shown, and the formal questions still open are in section 8. The families of the spine meet through correspondence theorems, not by definition: in the theorem-level measurement of 16 October 2026, 1,019 of 7,214 spine theorems stand apart from both base families (792 on the extended base), in 65 components ([THEOREM-CONNECTIVITY](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/THEOREM-CONNECTIVITY.md)). The laws hold of worlds so represented, and whether every becoming can be so represented is part of the warrant still needed (section 8).
