## 1 The foundation

**Nondual core, 6 October 2026:** following [Ren's clarification](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-nondual-core-and-subject-extensions.md), subject/object and actor/patient distinctions belong to extensions whose formation must be explained within generative activity. The [core reconstruction](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) makes this architectural requirement explicit, preserves the existing role-free `Process` and derives conditional, overlapping effective ranges from actual connection histories. A role annotation is not yet an acquired self-model, and a common process description does not establish common experience or moral direction. The local patient-grounded results below retain their scopes without making their subject indices foundational primitives.

**Governing research position:** [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) now makes the intended universal scope explicit: explain system formation, significance, learning and their divergent regimes from minimal properties of system-forming reality. The positive anchors and learning architectures supplied in local theorems are further explanatory targets of this foundation. This clarification changes the direction of further derivation; it does not retroactively enlarge the scope of the proofs below.

Activity forms and changes the conditions of further activity. Some effects persist, enter new relations, and become conditions of later transformation. They may renew a support, reveal a consequential difference, or leave a successor able to act; they may also deplete a support, obstruct a relation, or prevent later action. The account must explain how these differences arise, including how centers of experience and response form within the unfolding situation.

The strongest common foundation supported by the work is therefore:

> **Understand activity through the conditions that enable it, the conditions it changes, and the further activity those changes make effectively possible. Where continuation matters, explain how its indispensable conditions are renewed, replaced, or transformed—including the conditions for understanding and correcting that very process.**

This connects the ontological question of becoming to the practical question of wisdom without declaring them identical. It says what a causal account must include when those dependencies matter. It does not assume that every actual process regenerates its conditions, that all possibilities should remain active, or that reality has already been shown to favor kindness.

The concise proposed formulation is:

**生生：活动生成后续活动的条件。**  
**续生：在损耗与变化中，使必要的条件得以更新、替代或转化。**  
**智慧：在其中辨明关键差异，修正理解与行动，并维护这种辨明与修正得以继续的条件。**

These are contemporary formulations for this research, not quotations from classical texts. The ethical extension is stated separately in Section 10.

The unity is relational. A person and their surroundings, a lineage and its habitat, or a scientific community and its instruments belong inside the relevant explanation together. Distinct centers still have different observations, needs, powers, and consequences. A good account of the whole preserves the differences that change what can happen. [S1–S4](SOURCE-MAP.md)

## 2 What has been unified

**Core and tree as one unfolding:** the [project-wide nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md#worked-continuation-knowledge-action-and-support-in-one-history) now has a worked reconstruction in the existing history of learning and resource use. A real outcome participates in the organization's resources, retained action-tagged evidence and later command changes; the tree's knowledge, action and support descriptions are accountable to that same history. Their distinctions are useful without becoming independent starting realms. The outcome-sensitive branch also constrains what the core's abstraction must preserve. This continuation reuses the established mechanism and repairs the scope of earlier root-audit language; it adds no theorem or empirical result.

The separate investigations exposed successive gaps in treating current achievement as evidence of continued capacity. A functioning organization may be exhausting its material supports. An organization that renews itself may undermine another being's continuation. That being may recognize what is happening yet lack an effective way to respond. A corrector may work once but consume the reserve needed to work again. A successor may inherit all the material but use it in a way that prevents a new construction. A model may accurately summarize old questions while making a new one impossible to answer.

These findings have one common structure: **a present state or success is insufficient when the consequential object is a course of activity and what it leaves possible afterward.** The relevant conditions are causal, temporal, and situated. [S5–S13](SOURCE-MAP.md)

The formal unity is one language of situated processes, capacities, and continuation conditions, together with proved results for specified classes. The chemical models, scientific simulations, and institutional models retain their own assumptions. They are not being relabeled as one empirically fitted equation.

## 3 A situation with differentiated centers

Let \(z_t\) describe the modeled joint situation at time \(t\). Depending on the application it can include material states, relations, tools, organisms, records, observation interfaces, institutions, installed rules, and the means of replacing them. This is a choice of representation, not a declaration that a universal list of substances has been discovered.

For an extension in which organized centers have already been identified, the mathematical interface is

\[
z_{t+1}\in F(z_t,a_t,w_t),\qquad
o_t^i=O_i(z_t),\qquad
a_t^i=\pi_i(h_t^i).
\tag{1}
\]

Here \(w_t\) denotes admitted disturbances; \(h_t^i\) is the observation and action history actually available to center \(i\); and \(\pi_i\) is its installed response mechanism. Stochastic models replace the successor relation by a transition law. Continuous models use their corresponding differential equations. Rates, noise, observation, and available interventions require justification in each application.

Equation (1) describes an organizational and observational extension, not a primitive division of the root into subjects and objects. The formation of its centers, indexing and observation/action relations is further content to explain through the [role-free process and derived-range interface](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions). It remains legitimate to supply a center partition in a scoped local model, provided that this dependency is retained rather than counted as derived.

An organization can alter an instrument, a practice, a boundary, or a rule. Those alterations change the future observation and action structure because their physical or social carriers occur inside \(z\). A new possible description is not automatically an available intervention. Construction, consent, installation, interpretation, and enforcement can all take time and consume resources.

A center is a relatively organized locus of response within this situation. Acting and undergoing distinguish relations within particular histories: the same organized activity can affect other activity and be changed by it. These relations do not erase differences in experience, control or consent. The theory permits centers to overlap, divide, merge, disappear, or be replaced. A biological interpretation needs biological criteria; a conscious standpoint requires more than a dynamical coordinate. The present mathematics does not derive phenomenal experience or settle personal identity.

An accounting boundary is useful when its omitted dependencies are either represented as inputs or shown irrelevant at the stated resolution. For a conserved transfer quantity,

\[
\frac{dX_S}{dt}
=\sum_{i\in S}s_i+
\sum_{i\in S,\,j\notin S}j_{ij},\qquad j_{ij}=-j_{ji}.
\tag{2}
\]

Internal currents cancel; boundary currents remain. Enlarging the region changes which currents are internal, not whether the complete account contains their effects. This gives a precise model of consistency across boundaries. It does not establish that every quantity is conserved or that a regional total suffices to predict its future. [Formal result F1](FORMAL-CORE.md)

The observer belongs within the same inquiry. Instruments, concepts, attention, evidence access, and capacities to intervene form and change through activity. Their enabling conditions and effects require explanation, including resource use where it occurs. This places knowing within the account without granting complete access to the whole.

## 4 From becoming to maintained regeneration

Generation is broader than regeneration. A process generates when its activity produces changes that enter subsequent activity. It regenerates a specified capacity when its causal effects replenish, reconstruct, or replace the conditions of that capacity. A process can do both for one center while damaging another. The reference function, boundary, horizon, and comparison must be stated independently of the observed outcome.

For a justified support stock \(C\), renewal \(R\), loss \(L\), and required floor \(C_{\min}\),

\[
C(t)=C(0)+\int_0^t(R-L)\,ds.
\]

Continuation above the floor requires, at **every prefix** of the trajectory,

\[
\int_0^t R\,ds\ge
\int_0^t L\,ds-[C(0)-C_{\min}].
\tag{3}
\]

This elementary balance has a substantial implication: later replenishment cannot prevent an earlier breach of the required floor. A successor that becomes capable after its predecessor fails can arrive too late. A diagnostic that becomes usable after irreversible damage can no longer prevent that damage. Resources that cannot be converted into the needed function in time are not substitutes merely because both have a number attached. [F2](FORMAL-CORE.md)

Not all supports are stocks. Permission, competence, an observation distinction, or a compatible interface may enter as a logical or dynamical condition. They should not be added to energy or money in an invented universal currency.

The newest work derives the formation of a renewing organization in a small joint system. Let \(R\) be usable material, \(C\) renewal machinery, and \(D\) degraded material:

\[
\dot R=kCD-aR,\qquad
\dot C=aR-bC,\qquad
\dot D=bC-kCD.
\tag{4}
\]

Assembly makes machinery; machinery wears; powered machinery recycles degraded material. Material is conserved, \(R+C+D=M\), while maintained energetic driving is a premise of \(k\). The machinery can start absent if some usable material is present.

For positive rates, the exact threshold is \(kM>b\). Above it, every initial condition with \(R+C>0\) converges to

\[
(R_*,C_*,D_*)=
\left(\frac b{a+b}(M-b/k),\frac a{a+b}(M-b/k),\frac bk\right).
\tag{5}
\]

At or below the threshold, activity fades. The all-degraded boundary remains inactive even above threshold. Formation, maintained abundance, and rudimentary regulation follow from the supplied reactions: \(D-b/k\) contracts while machinery operates. The model explains more than assuming an already successful organization, but its reaction repertoire and energetic driving are still assumptions. [S3](SOURCE-MAP.md), [F3](FORMAL-CORE.md)

The wider network result strengthens the obstruction. For nonnegative capacities satisfying a uniform production bound \(\dot z\le(B-L)z\), with positive diagonal losses and no independent supply, \(\rho(L^{-1}B)<1\) implies exponential decay. An acyclic production graph is subcritical in this class. A productive cycle is consequently necessary for endogenous persistence here; it is not sufficient without adequate gain, resources, and dynamics. External supply, nondecaying capacities, or other production laws change the result. [F4](FORMAL-CORE.md)

These statements express a direction under particular conditions: trajectories converge toward a maintained joint regime. They do not yet establish a universal tendency of reality to produce increasingly inclusive regeneration.

## 5 Effective possibilities and the role of lenses

A possibility matters to continuation only through its conditions of realization. We therefore distinguish:

1. **Physical possibility:** the represented process admits a route.
2. **Situated capacity:** a route can be executed with actual information, means, rights, and time.
3. **Selection and installation:** the process brings a particular route into operation.
4. **Delivered continuation:** its execution leaves the relevant successor conditions available.

The difference is causal. A repair may be technically possible but require inaccessible evidence. The evidence may be available while the means of repair are missing. Those means may exist while someone with control prevents their use. A repair may also succeed while destroying the machinery needed to repeat it.

Fix an explicit evaluation specification \(\mathcal E\): affected centers or reference functions, tasks, acceptable consequences, disturbances, information, budgets, deadlines, and permitted amendments. Let \(I\) be the actual information state. Define the situated capacity profile

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathcal E}(I)
=\{\tau:\text{one admissible observation policy can satisfy test }\tau
\text{ from }I\text{ within its stated conditions}\}.
\tag{6}
\]

Each test keeps its own scale, reliability, and resource conditions. We do not replace different affected futures by the count of task labels. Inclusion can compare profiles under a common specification; many profiles remain incomparable. [S5, S8](SOURCE-MAP.md)

A **lens** is a representation used to answer a declared family of questions. It is faithful when it preserves the answers needed for those questions, including consequential intervention differences. Two histories can be equivalent for one lens and different for another. Their aggregate production may be identical while their participant reserves differ; their old prediction summary may coincide while their time ordering differs.

For response profile \(\mathscr R_{\mathcal Q}(h)\), define

\[
h\sim_{\mathcal Q}h'
\iff \mathscr R_{\mathcal Q}(h)=\mathscr R_{\mathcal Q}(h').
\tag{7}
\]

The equivalence classes give the coarsest exact deterministic summary for those responses: any exact summary must distinguish histories with different responses. This is a precise optimality result. It is conditional on the supplied questions and causal response model, and does not prove that either is adequate. Established causal-state and predictive-representation work already develops this kind of minimality. [F5](FORMAL-CORE.md), [Shalizi and Crutchfield](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9907176)

Combining lenses means retaining the distinctions needed by their joint questions. It does not mean averaging their outputs. Questions about joint action can also require correlations and shared budgets absent from each marginal view. A representation sufficient for a previous task can cease to be sufficient when the question changes. No decoder can recover a distinction that its input has erased unless it obtains additional information.

The inheritance experiments make this concrete. With the same inherited material and resources, activating every extra component solved 17 possible next tasks that a smaller active repertoire missed; leaving extras dormant solved 15 that the larger search missed. The full 256-task comparison also included 50 solved by both and 174 by neither. These are exact results for the supplied construction algorithms, not findings about all learning systems. They show why storage, access, activation, execution, and transmission cannot be collapsed. [S9](SOURCE-MAP.md)

This does not contradict the elementary fact that **free, genuinely optional capabilities cannot reduce the optimum when all old policies remain available**. The experiment changes a fixed search procedure or imposes specified resource requirements. A larger theoretical choice set and the performance of its installed organization are different objects.

There is a second precision about possibilities. Under fixed dynamics, the complete set reachable from a later state is contained in the set reachable from its predecessor: concatenate the path to the later state with any path from it. Building a tool can nevertheless enlarge what is executable now, within a particular budget, deadline, or interface. The theory uses this effective sense of capacity, rather than claiming that an unfolding trajectory enlarges its own unrestricted all-time reachability set.

## 6 Wisdom as situated and revisable understanding

Later constructive studies give the following discussion an actual developmental sequence. Acquired observations can reject a candidate generator, form a replacement connection and later regenerate through that retained connection after the successful trial model has been discarded. In a separate matched history, an acquired signal changes native inquiry duration. The [organization reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSEQUENCE-ORGANIZATION-INTEGRATION.md) and [method chapter](METHOD-REVISION.md#what-subsequent-inquiry-actually-changes) preserve their separate ancestry, misleading evidence and supplied formation rules.

Inquiry itself also changes the questions its evidence can answer. Two candidates can correctly agree about the next event, both survive that event, and then disagree about what now comes next. Conversely, a probe can consume the condition needed for further identification. The [sufficiency argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-INTEGRATION.md) and [identifying-inquiry result](INQUIRY-AND-MATHEMATICAL-IDENTITY.md) make these relations exact. Hypothesis correction, changed sampling, newly formed access and revision of the sufficiency criterion are differentiated changes within continuing activity. Their separate proofs do not yet make one acquired, justified criterion of wisdom.

The inquiry began with a difficult question: how can one recognize a consequential possibility missing from the current picture? The combined answer is a capacity with several possible mechanisms, rather than one introspective trick.

A model organizes what can be noticed, asked, tested, and acted upon. Some failures produce an anomaly within that model. Others remain invisible because the instrument, archive, question, or criterion does not preserve the relevant distinction. Repeating reasoning within the same information can improve an inference without overcoming that information limit.

Suppose two still-possible situations have identical observation histories but require incompatible actions before a deadline. A controller restricted to those histories has the same decision distribution in both. If no safe probe can distinguish them in time, it cannot guarantee success in both. This obstruction applies regardless of the controller's computational sophistication. [F6](FORMAL-CORE.md)

The new aggregate-feedback model gives a continuous example. Two supports can deviate in opposite directions while their measured total stays fixed. A controller stabilizing that total can leave an unobserved difference growing exponentially. Additional responsiveness to the difference restores local stability only when its gain and timing meet specified thresholds. Understanding the whole therefore requires keeping consequential differences available to effective response. It does not require reconstructing every hidden detail. [S3](SOURCE-MAP.md)

Incomplete knowledge can still be sufficient for a decision when all compatible situations share a best action. Conversely, a summary sufficient for every admitted future prediction can omit a true fact about ancestry or uninterrupted past functioning. Decision adequacy, prediction, and historical knowledge are different questions. Their differences prevent both demands for impossible omniscience and the dismissal of facts that the current model does not retain. [F5](FORMAL-CORE.md)

The proposed root of wisdom is **the capacity to recognize and revise consequential dependencies in one's understanding and conduct, while remaining answerable to the situation and maintaining workable conditions for further correction**. “Answerable” here includes evidence and effects; its moral use additionally invokes the commitments in Section 10. This formulation has four practical consequences.

First, inquiry can change the interface, not only the estimate. A different measurement, a protected experiment, access to another standpoint, or construction of a new tool may make a previously invisible difference actionable. These changes are interventions in the same world being understood.

Second, good inquiry can sometimes begin without an anomaly. A present summary may be accurate for all questions previously asked while lacking the relation needed for a future action. The work supplies exact cases of this, but no general oracle for which new questions will matter. Search, alternative models, external criticism, and protected exploratory work all have costs and failure modes. [S6–S7, S11](SOURCE-MAP.md)

Third, revision has different objects. Belief revision updates expectations within an admitted model family. Model revision changes that family. Interface revision changes what can be observed or done. Institutional revision changes admission, finance, or authority. Commitment revision changes what is protected or valued. They can interact, but a posterior update alone cannot perform all five. New standards should not retroactively make old outcomes true or acceptable under the old standards.

Fourth, inquiry must be supportable. Testing can deplete the activity it is intended to improve. In the scientific-inquiry simulations, the full inquiry policy sometimes consumes resources needed for ordinary service and performs substantially worse than a no-renewal policy. In another case, a change correctly judged beneficial under current conditions later causes harm because reversal is delayed and unaffordable. Stable simple tasks show no accuracy benefit from the extra inquiry. These are failures of specified policies in synthetic environments, not proofs that every supported inquiry policy must fail. They show why warranted, maintainable responsiveness needs a resource and transition account. [S10](SOURCE-MAP.md)

The later [evaluative plasticity construction](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) makes the distinction between acquired influence and justified revision concrete. The consequences of paired operations contribute to forming initially absent signed connections, which then affect whether the learner acts through the modeled help route. Formation and later replacement consume the resources specified by the local law. A replacement can suppress the same effective service while the means to perform it remain available, even though both comparison arms acquire the same new data and preserve factual command knowledge. Thus learning changes the conditions of later evaluation within the common causal account, but the change is not itself evidence of improved judgment. The [positive application account](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md#from-acquired-understanding-to-warranted-revisable-choice) separately derives what is warranted from defended patient-related grounds, the actor's role, legitimate authority and the complete comparison, then checks causal recognition and execution. Formed influence can contribute to wisdom only through such a justified application; fixed plasticity and primitive coding do not establish it.

The [historical-delivery proof](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/APPLICATION-EVIDENCE-MATH.md) gives a second concrete distinction. In its specified receipt-only reader class, present command support can be traced through the same retained acquisition to an actual earlier delivery. Under independently justified episode and service meanings, inquiry may already have fulfilled the one-service claim before the later action. The accepted proof establishes that provenance and conditional obstruction; it adds no fulfillment-sensitive choice policy. Accurate means knowledge, a still-effective disposition and an outstanding practical claim therefore cannot be treated as the same condition.

On this account, wisdom is compatible with commitment, rest, selective closure, and decisive action. Its opposite is not simply insufficient information. It can be an organization of attention, incentives, and authority that makes correction progressively less attainable. An accurate and adaptive oppressor demonstrates that epistemic competence alone does not establish ethical wisdom; the stronger interpretation includes the defended concern for affected beings in Section 10.

The proposed account remains broader than what has been empirically validated. Both small reasoning pilots in the source work reached ceiling performance in every comparison condition and demonstrated no special-method advantage. The formal obstructions and constructive models remain valid on their own terms; a practical advantage for this proposed discipline of wisdom remains to be shown. [S1](SOURCE-MAP.md)

## 7 Selection changes the conditions of selection

Renewal is not automatically selected. Different mechanisms transmit practices for different reasons. Reproduction can favor productive maintenance while imitation favors savings in visible private cost. Rewards can support contributors, yet rewards drawn from the same production can also exhaust shared renewal. A favorable equilibrium can coexist with a collapse basin. These are separate mechanisms and parameter conditions. [S12](SOURCE-MAP.md)

The latest retention example is sharper still: everyone contributes, but higher private retention can spread while shared support declines. Contribution alone therefore does not establish a positive contribution to the continuation of the whole situation. Its allocation relations matter. [S4](SOURCE-MAP.md)

The earlier meta-selection theory belongs here. Success can increase productive capacity and control over later activity, including how success is judged, who can challenge that judgment, and which alternatives receive support. If the latter channels close corrective routes, an organization's success can undermine the process by which its success is assessed. Whether this feedback occurs and how strong it is are empirical questions, not consequences of competition alone. [S14](SOURCE-MAP.md)

This yields a common investigation across evolution, institutions, science, and AI: identify the actual transmission and allocation events, their private and shared consequences, and the ways those consequences alter later selection. Replicator dynamics, imitation, institutional appointments, and deliberate choice must not be treated as interchangeable laws.

It also clarifies the concern about acceleration. In a specified workload model, a finite correction buffer cannot absorb cumulative excess of incoming hazardous work over effective processing once that excess exceeds its reserve. A sufficient long-run processing rate can still arrive too late for an individual irreversible event. Faster capability growth can improve or worsen these margins depending on the actual mechanisms. There is no conclusion that every acceleration is harmful or that every slowdown is corrective. [S15](SOURCE-MAP.md)

### Six mechanisms connecting formed capacities to their social continuation

The [explicit V7.3 integration](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) develops the preceding meta-selection connection through six conditional mechanisms. It credits the newer paid market, shared-return, learning and concern results; these mechanisms have overlapping explanatory roles but are not one jointly proved state law.

**Shared dependence can be invisible to selection.** If a host benefit enters every payoff additively and identically, a translation-invariant relative rule cancels it from share dynamics. A costly contribution therefore needs an actual differential retention, transmission, liability or constraint route. The current shared-return model already separates increased total reproduction from increased producer frequency. This sharper interface prevents the analyst's recognition of useful regulation from silently becoming its reward.

**Compounding can change effective priorities while held concern stays fixed.** In V7.3's local continuation calculation, the working self-model narrows when the return to a relative gain exceeds the return to a common gain; it can widen under the reverse inequality. Accurate understanding of dependence does not itself remove this incentive. Factual belief, acquired evaluation and operative priority must therefore remain distinguishable. The result retains its deterministic local reference, fixed-opponent and discounted-return conditions; it is not a general formula for ethical learning.

**Independence is a maintained relation within a network.** Success can alter appointments, funding, access, standards and enforcement across several domains. Repairing one evaluator can leave other channels supporting its capture. V7.3's full inbound-gain certificates sharpen the present scalar market and paid-interference results, while distinguishing eventual recovery from safety during the transient. Capture also requires impaired corrective purpose or protected interest; concentration and legitimate revision alone do not establish it.

**Delegation can consume independent competence.** Under a supplied disuse and recovery law, replacing an activity can erode the capacity needed to resume it or overrule its substitute. Formal authority may persist after its safe exercise becomes unavailable. Maintaining parallel competence can prevent that particular loss when its costs can be met. The mechanism extends the current account of depleted correction and succession without identifying every delegation with permanent loss of agency.

**Communication is itself affected by selection.** In the predecessor's specified cheap-talk game, relative objectives increase sender–receiver conflict and restrict informative communication. Shared hazard does not certify a warning's credibility. This joins the current faithful-reporting problem to strategic communication among centers. Costly signals, independent verification and broader narrative effects retain separate premises; a derived working objective cannot simply be inserted into another equilibrium without checking the interface.

**Reform must outlast the state that reproduces failure.** V7.3 adds delayed influence decay, a required intervention duration, recruitable alternatives, and a finite funded transition to the current safe-path and recovery results. Visible improvement can precede escape from a capture basin. Preserved material that cannot recruit into the new opportunity is not an operative alternative. A viable destination therefore needs a survivable path, while neither its incumbents nor every existing possibility must persist indefinitely.

Together these mechanisms explain how already formed understanding and concern can acquire or lose causal influence when activity changes the conditions of further selection and correction. They also explain constructive responses under the corresponding laws: change consequence allocation, reduce appropriable control, maintain independent capacities, make evidence usable and sustain the required transition. Which response is warranted still depends on affected beings, justified authority and competing claims. Selected success supplies neither ethical standing nor a universal favorable direction. The [chapter's composition contract](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#7-their-place-in-the-common-derivation) preserves different carriers, meanings, clocks, resources and successor states; its [next connection](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#8-ethical-direction-scope-and-the-next-connection) concerns acquired evaluation under changed continuation incentives.

## 8 Correction has to regenerate its own use

The newest institutional construction joins three quantities that the earlier work often examined separately: shared productive support, a participant's protected capacity, and the resources for effective correction. Production can continue while the participant is depleted; the participant is deliberately not required for aggregate production. This prevents the model from hiding moral standing inside system usefulness. [S4](SOURCE-MAP.md)

Production is ten units per round. Both allocation rules devote two to shared maintenance and \(f\) to correction. The restorative rule gives the participant three; the extractive rule gives one. The participant needs two, so the reserve gains or loses one respectively. The incumbent gets two more units under extraction, and the supplied replacement rule selects that rule whenever no binding correction constrains it.

The participant can file at reserve four, paying one unit. The correction carrier pays one per round for upkeep and two more when a claim is initiated, all before current funding arrives. A binding mandate takes two rounds to alter allocation. It restores the participant to six, then expires. The exact reserve cycle is

\[
6\to5\to4\to2\to1\to2\to3\to4\to5\to6.
\tag{8}
\]

Nine rounds require nine upkeep units and two intervention units. Indefinite repetition under this protocol therefore requires \(f\ge11/9\). Capacity \(K\ge3\) is also necessary to pay the prefunded claim bill. At the boundary, \(K=3\) and initial reserve \(Q_0=23/9\) suffice from the stated starting phase. Filing threshold \(h\), filing cost \(c\), and delay \(d\) must satisfy \(h\ge1+c+d\). [F7](FORMAL-CORE.md)

These requirements are independent in the relevant sense. Money arriving later cannot pay an earlier bill. Carrier funding cannot erase the participant's delay. Accurate detection cannot change allocation without the binding gate. Funding upkeep alone can finance a first rescue and still fail on later claims.

The common implication is that correction is itself an activity whose operation changes its future conditions. A claim to continuing protection must cover its repeated use, not just its installation or existence.

This original model supplies the funding earmark, complaint protocol, and binding authority. It does not explain why those institutions form, why participants accept them, or how they survive strategic attack and constitutional change. Moving these mechanisms inside a larger model was the formation challenge addressed by the following studies. Their conditional constructions do not retroactively make F7's supplied gate self-forming or settle the wider origin of authority.

The [completed formation study](FORMATION.md) now derives a route to effective protection from the participant's construction and use of an alternative. The participant allocates a rival input between shared production and that alternative. With public implemented choices, a self-interested operator safeguards when the safeguard costs less than both the rent lost through refusal and the cost of blocking the alternative. Building, operating, and transmitting the alternative have separate financial and incentive conditions. Physical control of the input, observability, and interference costs remain supplied premises. [S17](SOURCE-MAP.md)

This advances the earlier supplied correction rule, and also reveals a further obstruction. A persistent operator can spend more on a present blockade than on a safeguard because the blockade leaves the participant unable to finance the next corrective operation. In a proved two-period class, the reserve needed to start is \(K+m+\ell\); the reserve removing this particular strategic attack is \(K+2m+\ell\). Between those thresholds, the participant anticipates the attack and defers activation. There may be no observed blockade on the selected path. These are conditional results, not a universal claim about incumbent horizons or cash. [Formation proofs](FORMATION-MATH.md)

The distinction reaches the foundation: current action can change both the world's condition and who retains the capacity to affect its later condition. A costly present intervention can be chosen because it reduces another actor's future corrective means. Long-term optimization alone therefore does not identify ethical wisdom. The whole-process certificate must include those actual strategic responses and all their cash prefixes.

The parallel concern branch now derives one learned route through which another patient's condition enters an actor's evaluation. A [new one-period interface](CONCERN-AND-CORRECTION.md) joins that route with an active participant-controlled alternative. For safeguard cost c, blocking cost chi, participation rent r, current concern coefficient v, patient harm H, and known safeguard efficacy z, the exact conditions are c<chi+zvH and c+(1−z)vH<r. The first comparison joins concern with resistance to interference; the second preserves the option of harmless exit. A separate third-party patient keeps the participant's previously specified preferences unchanged. This is a precisely defined choice interface, not yet a composition of the two full developmental dynamics. [S19](SOURCE-MAP.md)

The [joint-formation development](JOINT-FORMATION.md) now closes one such common process. A fresh controller with zero learned recipient valuation chooses a useful calibration task while anticipating its later protective expense. Shared learning competes with identity-gated learning. The learned controller then chooses and pays for current inspection and actual input control, while a persistent operator can suppress correspondence, disguise a cue, breach control, or attack the reserve. The patient contributes nothing to either actor's production. In a proved strict region, the selected choices finance and reconstruct the whole protective process at every replacement. [S22](SOURCE-MAP.md)

Its proof joins dated incentives with every payment prefix. Even a refused trade after paid guarding leaves the next learning and reconstruction affordable; this invariant prevents temporary interference from creating a profitable unprotected future. Finite-horizon induction checks all decision makers' alternatives, and a discounted-limit argument establishes an infinite equilibrium. Cheap identity filtering, a cheap commitment against later care, or recurring attacks on the learning budget defeat the construction. This resolves a conditional joint-existence question and rejects automatic moral direction. The continuing account, primitive teaching, physical repertoire, and represented patient stakes remain declared premises. [Joint proof](JOINT-FORMATION-MATH.md)

The later [revisable-concern settlement](REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md) adds paid admission and retraction of case–reason relations to a common strategic process. Recomputing valid derivations from a supplied positive root removes unsupported conclusions while preserving independent grounds. A sound finite audit, known contexts, monotone protection prices with a specified tie rule, costly joint exclusion, and sufficient reserve support repeated audit and protection of the admitted set. The price and tie conditions are essential repairs to the initial proof. Equilibrium existence does not establish attraction or universal ethical coverage: the six-episode witness records two actual harms before or outside current admission. [S23](SOURCE-MAP.md)

A separate finite grammar constructs a useful composite feature with charged costs; it does not discover unrestricted meanings or ethical reasons. The ethical branch proves scoped decision results under supplied grounded accounts, while the evidence audit adds no new observations and leaves its human study unrun. These contributions have an [accepted integration review](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONCERN-SETTLEMENT-INTEGRATION.md). Realizing their audit, learning, incentives, and succession in a common physical process remains an explanatory obligation.

## 9 The integrated formal statement

The separate results meet in a common certification problem. Specify the joint state, actual observations, installed decision mechanisms, resource and authority gates, permissible disturbances, protected conditions, and required services. Include replacement and revision transitions in that same process. Then ask whether the process can continue satisfying those obligations.

For a finite partially observed safety problem, augment the information state with resources, deadlines, and relevant monitors. Let \(S\) contain information states whose compatible physical states meet the requirements. Starting from \(W_0=S\), iterate

\[
W_{n+1}=\{I\in W_n:\exists a\in A(I),\ 
\operatorname{Post}(I,a,o)\in W_n\text{ for every possible observation }o\}.
\tag{9}
\]

The finite fixed point gives exactly the states admitting an observation-history policy that guarantees safety under the declared model, with exact information updates and action availability. A physical implementation must also sustain the policy's memory and computation, including their actual resource requirements. The existential action precedes the unresolved outcomes: one action must handle the possibilities that the actor cannot yet distinguish. Decentralized actors need their actual information structure; silently sharing every observation computes a different problem. [F8](FORMAL-CORE.md)

Possibility still differs from actual operation. Fix the installed policy, including its revision procedure and any admitted refusals. In its finite, total reachable transition graph, perpetual safety and every specified recurring service hold on **all** admitted runs exactly when:

* every reachable state satisfies the protection conditions; and
* for each required service, no reachable directed cycle can avoid that service forever.

Equivalently, within a reachable protected invariant, a nonnegative integer rank strictly decreases on every transition that does not deliver the particular service. It may reset when that service occurs. The rank proves that waiting cannot continue indefinitely. Separate ranks accommodate several required functions. Costs, handoff delays, and rule changes are already transitions inside the graph. There is no unaccounted supervisor stepping in from outside it. [F9](FORMAL-CORE.md)

This is the central formal integration: **continuation is certified for the coupled process that actually reproduces, observes, selects, repairs, and revises—not inferred from isolated proofs that each component could work.** Two modules certified separately can demand the same scarce resource, hide information from one another, or rely on incompatible actions. Their joint certificate must exclude those conflicts. The recurrence claim uses actual transition events, retains every admitted event-free alternative, and requires physical time to diverge for an indefinite physical interpretation.

The results are exact within their finite classes and constructive. Feasibility analysis supplies a policy witness or a failure-rank argument against every admissible policy. Verification of a fixed installed process supplies its protected region or a failing path or cycle; one such failure does not prove that all alternative policies fail. These results use established viability, dynamic programming, and verification ideas. The research contribution sought is their disciplined application to the renewal of the enabling and corrective process itself. Existing compositional-control research provides direct precedent. [Chen and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10636)

Safety does not require constant activity. A supported dormant capacity can be part of a good policy if it can be restored before need. Nor does recurrence prove endless novelty. A two-state cycle repeats forever without producing infinitely many distinct responses. A stronger novelty claim requires an explicit novelty criterion, an executable extension mechanism, adequate resources and memory, and physical time that does not compress infinitely many episodes into a finite interval. [S8, S9](SOURCE-MAP.md)

Stochastic conclusions are also different. A finite Markov chain can hit every required service almost surely while admitting probability-zero infinite paths that avoid one. Conversely, the finite-particle renewal model eventually reaches an absorbing inactive state even though its deterministic concentration model has a stable regenerative equilibrium. “Sure,” “almost sure,” “high probability over a stated horizon,” and “locally stable” must remain distinct. [F10](FORMAL-CORE.md)

## 10 Shared generativity and 善

**Current positive criterion, clarified by Ren on 7 October 2026:** [生生不息 as the whole's continuing creation of more 生](UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#shengsheng-as-the-positive-ethical-direction), with richer generated possibilities and diversity and the capacity for increasingly complex organization. Ren accepts multiple scopes of 生, including biological/ecological life, life/mind/relationships and general organization-forming processes. Their relation and universal ethical significance are now the substantive investigation. This does not assert necessary physical progress or a single complexity maximum. The older application formulation below remains credited, but its word “worthwhile” cannot silently supply this new foundational identification.

**一体之仁 as coformation.** The [current account](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md#一体之仁的生成解释) explains concern through actual undergoing, acquired understanding and response changing one another within generative activity. Undergoing can persist without recognition; understanding may also genuinely transform it. Response changes the situation to which future concern must answer, including whether to stop, withdraw control or support a new development. This constrains the core's representation: it must preserve the relevant intermediate consequences and changing relations, not merely an endpoint success score. The noninstrumental ethical claim concerns the significance of the actual transformation; neither a common trace nor an increased resource quantity proves its universal warrant.

The primary foundational question is how interacting differences form organizations and how some organizations develop significance, regulation, learning and the capacity to revise their own selection mechanisms. Within that account, a further directional question asks whether, how and under which conditions reality generates increasingly shared and responsive regeneration. The present work establishes conditional mechanisms of renewal and responsiveness, their failures, and constraints on their operation. The [governing position](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) makes both the universal explanatory ambition and the unfinished derivations explicit.

Five different propositions should therefore remain visible:

| Proposition | Present status |
|---|---|
| One generative account explains organization, significance, learning and their constructive and destructive regimes wherever systems form | Governing research target; the minimal premises and connected derivation remain under development |
| An indispensable finite support cannot cover unbounded cumulative loss without replacement, provision, or changed dependence | Proved under the stated balance assumptions |
| Specified coupled dynamics can form a maintained regenerative organization | Proved for the supplied reaction model |
| Dynamics generally favor regeneration that becomes responsive to more affected centers | Research hypothesis; no general selection or formation theorem supplied |
| Affected beings' suffering, agency, and worthwhile futures provide reasons for how to act | Explicit ethical commitment requiring ethical justification |

The fourth proposition is the stronger direction-of-reality hypothesis within the explanatory program. Interconnection alone does not prove it: the same relations can transmit depletion, coercion, or collapse. Demonstrating it would require a specified mechanism of formation and spread, a justified scope of environments, and evidence against plausible alternatives. Restricting attention to surviving regenerative systems would introduce survivor selection rather than establish the direction of all becoming.

The earlier application-oriented ethical formulation is:

> **善 concerns how activity supports, repairs, and enables the worthwhile possibilities of affected beings, including their effective capacity to understand, contest, and change the conditions shaping those possibilities.**

**善：照料并成全受影响者继续生活、行动、理解与改变的条件，也使纠正损害这些条件的力量能够继续。**

This formulation expresses the program's commitment. The causal theory explains what may make that commitment effective, expose a contradiction among its requirements, or reveal that an apparent improvement merely shifts harm out of view. Its current proofs do not derive the commitment from survival. Explaining how another center acquires significance within a process, and why that recognition is warranted, remains part of the foundational task. Affected beings can matter even when the larger system can persist without them.

Shared existence gives no warrant to erase distinct centers. A whole can survive while some are deprived. Formal permission can coexist with unaffordable exercise. Agreement can rise because cooperation improves or because refusal becomes worse. The participation work accordingly separates initial agreement, continued willingness, total benefit, and cash available at the required moment. Effective alternatives and the legitimacy of their conditions remain part of the ethical and institutional question. [S13](SOURCE-MAP.md)

A partial comparison can use separately stated consequences and protections. Where one feasible arrangement improves every declared concern under the same circumstances, it supplies a sufficient comparison without a universal scalar. Where concerns conflict, the correct output can be an incompatibility certificate or a frontier of tradeoffs. It is not a mathematical license to select whose suffering ceases to count.

The [ethical foundation](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) now gives a positive argument for these commitments: admitted subject-relative harms supply reasons; relevant consistency prevents their force disappearing solely with a change of identity or analytical boundary; answerability requires consequential power to respond to affected claims. These are explicit normative premises and limited deductions, not conclusions forced on an unqualified egoist by physical persistence. Standing, influence, and legitimacy remain different: the first concerns whose claims deserve consideration, the second whether selectors respond, and the third whether the arrangement and its powers can be justified. [S18](SOURCE-MAP.md)

The formation study adds an exact countermodel in which productive support renews while every costly patient safeguard remains privately disadvantageous. The patient's harm changes neither the selector's rewards nor its future constraints, and direct concern is absent. This isolates the missing causal connection: continued production and accurate information do not, on their own, make that harm consequential for selection. It does not establish that actual reality must have such preferences or institutions. [FORMATION](FORMATION.md)

The [noninstrumental-concern integration](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md), developed from the comparison with 一体之仁, now locates a direct patient-grounded option within the same formal process. The independently interpreted C1/C2 contracts connect a realized patient burden and an eligible response to a contributory moral merit without requiring private benefit, prior role or antecedent adoption. A history of carrier formation, observation, selection and action realizes the designated response under the specified resource conditions. It reduces the same patient's modeled burden and preserves the resource changes at each step. [F14](FORMAL-CORE.md#f14-noninstrumental-patient-reasons-and-paid-response) states the conditional composition and counterinterpretation. Patient identification, ethical sufficiency, faithful reading, current-condition checks, recipes and scheduling remain explicit; a correctly supplied program has not thereby learned the ground or demonstrated autonomous moral development.

The [attempt to remove C2](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md#9-实际尝试从根基消除-c2) now locates its content in a patient-event reason anchor and preservation across eligible execution positions. Their conjunction is equivalent to C2, so a change of parameters is not a derivation. A generated historical relation can restrict reasons while preserving whole-history relabeling; removing a primitive owner therefore does not by itself eliminate every restrictive relation. The positive route remains to explain the patient's actual experiential stake and why its permissible relief supplies a reason across those positions, including for patients who never could act. New formal checks delimit this attempt without converting the supplied normative ground into an established consequence of generation.

This gives 非工具性关切 a precise current role: the patient's actual condition can be the reason's object and the response's beneficiary even when the responder receives no material return. Ground, acquired evidence, particular means, selected action and delivered effect remain distinct. Loss of permission or unavailable means cannot erase the patient condition; real fulfillment can end that episode's pending application. Two patient-directed reasons need not admit a jointly feasible response or a single numerical priority. The positive grounding argument and its remaining necessity dispute therefore constrain the model without being replaced by its successful trace.

The classical terms remain valuable lenses. 生生 emphasizes generation through generation; 間 can point to an unresolved or unrealized possibility relative to a frame; 开, 闭, and 护 distinguish availability, closure, and protective restriction. Their mathematical counterparts depend on the question. A temporary closure can preserve future response, while an apparently open contest can eliminate it. No claim is made that Chinese, Buddhist, or Western traditions express one identical doctrine or that classical language supplies the physical equations. [S1, S16](SOURCE-MAP.md)

## 11 Coherence and optimality

An elegant foundation should make its distinctions earn their place. This one uses a single causal question—what an activity does to effective further activity—and retains the distinctions required to answer it. The formal core then supplies specific obstructions, constructions, and equivalences. This is stronger than gathering everything under a word and more modest than claiming the word determines every law.

There are several legitimate meanings of optimality:

* The coarsest faithful representation is optimal for a specified response family.
* A viability construction identifies the full feasible region in its declared finite class.
* A least-cost policy is optimal only after the model, objectives, hard protections, uncertainty, and available policies are fixed.
* A scientific theory is preferable when it explains or predicts more with justified structure and survives comparison with alternatives. That claim needs evidence.

These optima need not coincide. A smaller model can erase an intervention distinction. An optimal policy for one objective can violate another center's requirements. A high-performing current method can leave successors unable to meet its continuing requirements. The archive experiment even supplies opposite task-specific rankings under identical material resources. Therefore the current work does not establish a universally best vocabulary, policy, institution, retention level, or direction of intervention. [F5, F11–F12](FORMAL-CORE.md)

Its present coherence rests on explicit compatibility rules. Descriptive and ethical assertions have different warrants. Model states do not become the world merely by definition. A change of boundary retains its supporting inputs. A change of question can require a richer representation. A revision changes future action without rewriting past evidence. A replacement must meet its transition requirements and preserve the specified obligations. A claim about all possible policies does not become a claim about the installed one.

These rules resolve the main tensions among the sessions while preserving their discoveries. They also expose where no reconciliation is available: a universal moral tendency, unlimited novelty, a complete effective evaluator for arbitrary programs, and a common ranking of conflicting future possibilities do not follow from the proved core. The program remains able to investigate stronger claims without treating them as completed results.

## 12 The resolved construction and remaining explanatory obligations

The governing obligation is the [connected derivation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#the-derivation-and-the-existing-work): explain organization, consequence-sensitive significance, learning and selection over selectors, then shared concern and wisdom within the same generative account. The [accepted regulation result](../../../_process/history/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REGULATION-RETENTION-INTEGRATION.md) already closes the earlier dependency-to-formed-response-and-retention bridge at its declared law. Later results connect acquired prediction, updater revision with explicit resource requirements, evidence about effects on another center, acquired influence and actual succession; [plasticity](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) now forms and changes an initially absent operative evaluative connection. The [common argument](FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md#7-the-connected-explanatory-argument) and [composition map](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) preserve their actual interfaces and incompatible realizations. The accepted [grounding argument](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) now connects actual formed influence to warranted role-bound repair under independently defended responsibility, patient-good, authority and comparison premises; the same learning law can fail that ground after replacement. The [five-condition assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) now accepts the combined conditional candidate at its declared explanatory scope. Generally learning ethical validity, universal empirical truth and unique metaphysical necessity remain unestablished stronger claims. The earlier joint-formation results discussed below retain their own scopes and supplied laws.

The first development has completed one bounded formation mechanism: a participant can construct an alternative that makes protection an operator's best response, and a strategic counterexample shows when the operator can prevent its early use by attacking future operating cash. The [report](FORMATION.md) contains the exact regime boundaries, all declared limitations, and the completed numerical checks. The broader prospective study in the [synthesis record](SYNTHESIS-RECORD.md) remains unrun where it requires uncertain sensing and the discovery of new interventions.

The parallel concern theory supplies a conditional acquisition mechanism: learned significance and recognition begin at zero and acquire an effective product under declared teaching and shared routing, with resource use specified for evidence acquisition. It also derives maintenance and succession conditions in a favorable stationary ecology. This advances beyond merely assigning a positive patient-loss coefficient, while leaving primitive valence, evaluative routing, legitimate evidence access, and their formation under competition unresolved.

The previously central joint-formation question now has a [constructive conditional solution](JOINT-FORMATION.md). The model selects the first learning expenditure before concern exists, compares deliberate identity gating, funds actual inspection and input control from the same account, computes strategic interference, and pays for complete reconstruction by fresh controllers. The proof establishes a nonempty strict region with indefinite discounted-equilibrium renewal. Its failure cases show why merely combining useful modules or increasing aggregate wealth cannot guarantee that result.

The [later settlement](REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md) also conditionally resolves joint paid revision and renewal for a finite supplied reason repertoire. It adds selective retraction and preserves independent grounds without treating obstruction as refutation. Wider patient scope, the validity of its positive root and audit, and legitimate representation still require their own arguments.

The remaining causal questions concern the formation and prevalence of these declared enabling conditions: the commercial opportunity, warranted feature transfer, sufficiently costly self-insulation and capture, continuing account access, and the relevant physical technologies. These are not missing steps inside the proved constructions; they determine where the constructions apply and what a more general account must explain. A strategic equilibrium and a physically realized process are different claims; their common information, event timing, resource use, and successor capabilities still need a faithful connection.

The epistemic frontier proceeds alongside it: how a process discovers that the supplied questions, action repertoire, or criteria are inadequate. The current strategic model grants public observation and known technology; it cannot be cited as an account of discovering unknown harms. A stronger study must test useful new distinctions and interventions on withheld problems, include the conditions and resource use of their construction and transmission, and permit simpler alternatives to succeed.

The program's universal explanatory target remains open, as does its further hypothesis of broadly favorable direction. The completed work offers more discriminating mechanisms: renewal can persist without concern, concern can be learned under explicit architectural conditions, effective refusal can induce protection, and forward-looking control can destroy the resources required for later correction. A general theory must explain how these divergent relations form and which prevail under specified conditions. It must also explain the development of significance and justify its ethical conclusions.

The candidate foundation now connects the conditions of further activity to the formation and contestability of the means that understand and alter those conditions. Where continued responsiveness matters, wisdom tracks how present action changes the conditions under which later understanding, choice, and correction can occur.
