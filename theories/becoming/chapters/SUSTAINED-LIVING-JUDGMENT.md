# 持续的生活组织与共同实践判断 / Sustained living organization and collective practical judgment

2026-10-08，Asia/Bangkok。AI 撰写，落实 Ren 在本会话设定的目标：connect sustained living organization to collective practical judgment。承接[生命依存](LIVING-DEPENDENCIES.md)、[实践判断](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md)与[非二元方法](NONDUAL-METHOD.md)。[研究整合、证明对应与完成核验](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SUSTAINED-LIVING-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md)保留范围与评审。

**主张：当一项生活的继续具有已说明的基本地位，而且确有正当、共同可行、保住其他基本要求的实现途径时，共同判断应保住或重建使它实际继续的组织关系，并按时提供所需支持。具体由谁承担，另由正当承担、权限、能力与完整负担决定。** 所需的是生活的实际接续，不是原组织、原提供者或原技术永远存续。

**Claim.** When a continuation has a justified basic standing and an authorized, jointly feasible route preserves the other basic claims, collective judgment should retain or reconstruct the relations that actually enable it and provide support at the relevant times. Who undertakes which contribution depends additionally on justified commitments, authority, capacities and cumulative burdens. The protected continuation need not preserve the original institution, provider or technique.

## 1. 一段历史中的三个关系 / Three relations within one history

植物、人的生活、供水设施、操作技能与共同规则都在相互作用中形成。渠首与渠尾、维护者与承受后果者是水流、位置、劳动、知识及规则形成的不同关系；不是两个先在独立的领域。认识、讨论和判断也改变这段历史中可发生的事。

Plants, human lives, infrastructure, skills and shared rules form through interaction. Upstream/downstream and maintaining/undergoing name positions differentiated by water flow, location, work, knowledge and rules. Inquiry and deliberation also change the ensuing possibilities. These are distinctions within one unfolding, not independent domains.

本连接分别检查：**实际组织使什么能够继续；这种继续及其损失为什么具有伦理地位；哪些共同安排有权限而且能真正完成。** 第一问约束后两问的实际对应，不能代替 G0 或 J2–J4。后两问也不能用“已批准”“有人负责”把未恢复的功能说成已经恢复。

The connection checks what organized activity can sustain, why its continuation and losses have ethical standing, and which authorized joint arrangements can enact it. Mechanism constrains correspondence without replacing G0 or J2–J4. Conversely, approval and assigned responsibility cannot turn an unsuccessful restoration into a successful one.

## 2. 从组织到持续实现 / From organization to sustained realization

已有核糖体研究把保留组织与恢复产出联系起来，但没有证明完整细胞的长期恢复。本轮不把较低残余功能改称完全缺失；用明确的时间窗口区分**有部分能力、能完成一项操作、能及时完成整个要求**。一个准备充分的反应可以连续发生，仍可能因速率不足错过窗口。

The existing ribosome evidence distinguishes retained organization from mere material presence, without establishing complete long-term cellular recovery. This connection retains partial competence: possessing some capability, performing an operation, and meeting the whole demand on time differ. A well-provisioned reaction may still have insufficient throughput for its deadline.

[新增 Lean 源码](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/SustainedJudgment.lean)直接导入已有 `LivingOrganization` 和 `PracticalJudgment`。沿原来的实际催化配方，组织参与并保留，每次操作消耗一单位底物和支持。对任意有限操作数，构造同一库存历史、推导总消耗，并明确不能把上一步已经消耗的存量重置。

The new Lean module imports the two existing accounts. Its constructive witness reuses the actual catalytic recipe: the organizing component participates and remains, while each operation consumes one substrate and one support unit. For arbitrary finite operation counts, it constructs a single inventory history and derives consumption without resetting a spent stock.

时间扩展公开添加一个局部速率条件：每个时间段每单位有效组织至多完成一次操作；并且，在已检查的输入充足且没有额外延迟、占用或干扰的模型中，每个符合该上限的反应序列均可在该段完成。后一项是额外的时段可实现性假设，不能由速率上限单独推出。需求列表中允许零需求。形式结果区分每段能力上限、整个期间的底物及支持总量，并构造保留各段标签的历史；例如同样两单位供给，一单位有效组织能够完成两个连续的单次需求，却不能在单段内完成双次需求。这个速率是**待校准的局部规律**，不是从计量配方推出的生物定律。

The temporal extension explicitly supplies a local throughput law: at most one operation per competent unit per epoch. It additionally assumes that each admitted reaction sequence within that bound can finish in the epoch when the checked inputs are available and no further delay, occupancy or interference is represented. This epoch-realization assumption does not follow from an upper bound alone. Demand lists permit zero-demand epochs. The proof separates each epoch's competence limit from cumulative substrate and support and constructs a history retaining epoch labels. With the same two units of provision, one competent unit meets two successive single demands but misses a double demand in one epoch. This rate is a supplied calibration obligation, not a biological law derived from stoichiometry.

实际生活可能需要连续流、不同损失率、损伤修复、多种共同条件或需求改变。应用时必须替换或丰富本配方，并保留真正的时间和相互影响；不能直接把作物、照护者或机构代入这些单位。已有组织来源定理另支持：已到达真正缺失的状态后，后来的必要操作须有实际重新进入组织类的过程，不能仅宣布恢复。

Living applications may involve continuous flow, changing losses, repair, several joint conditions or changing demands. They require an appropriate law retaining timing and interaction; the toy units cannot be identified directly with crops, carers or institutions. The inherited organization-origin theorem separately requires actual entry after an established empty state before a later operation needing that organization; announcing recovery is insufficient.

并行完成的[一般共同分配结果](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-ALLOCATION-PROOF.md)已直接接入本轮形式证明：任意已声明反应族中，必要参与次数受继承组织及此前实际增加的组织约束，并保留损失和后续重用。共同判断由此能够要求实际组织改变，而非只增加名义供给。这些批次仍不自带物理时钟；本轮时间模型的额外速率条件不能倒称为该一般定理的结论。

The concurrently completed general allocation result is now directly composed with judgment in the proof. Across arbitrary declared reaction families, necessary participant uses are bounded by inherited organization and actual earlier increases, retaining losses and later reuse. Judgment can thereby require real organizational change rather than nominal extra provision. Those batches remain untimed; the separate temporal model's supplied rate is not a consequence of the general allocation theorem.

## 3. 从持续实现到共同要求 / From sustained realization to collective requirements

本轮的普遍推理连接是**有根据的基本生活保护 → 排除可避免且无其他基本代价的失保方案 → 保留共同实现所必需的条件 → 有范围的分工**。它对任何智能或实践位置都具有同样的带前提形式，而其实际承担随形成的关系不同；不声称所有智能都会认识或执行它。

The general inference is justified basic protection → exclusion of avoidable failure when restoration preserves other basic claims → necessary conditions of joint realization → bounded allocation. Its conditional form applies across intelligences and practical positions, while actual responsibilities depend on formed relationships. Recognition and compliance are further questions.

| 层 / Layer | 必须公开什么 / What remains explicit |
|---|---|
| G0 | 生及其继续生成的基本正面意义。 / The basic favorable significance of living and further generation. |
| J2 | 保住其他基本要求时，基本保护优先于可放弃的丰富性；这是明示比较承诺。 / Priority of basic protection over optional enrichment when other basic claims are retained; an explicit comparative commitment. |
| E：组织及生活对应 / Correspondence | 何种活动体现该生活、哪些关系实际必要、整个期间的后果、损伤与替代；功能产出不自动等于生活良好。 / Which activity realizes the living claim, its actual dependencies, whole-period consequences, impairments and alternatives; functional output alone does not establish living well. |
| E：共同可行及保护 / Joint feasibility and protection | 每一恢复方案确实可执行；保留所有相关基本要求，包括承担者及未参与者。 / The restoration is executable and preserves every relevant basic claim, including contributors and nonparticipants. |
| J3、J4 | 谁可决定、谁正当承担、完整负担、有限期限、被影响者参与及纠错。 / Authority, justified assignment, cumulative burdens, finite scope, affected participation and correction. |
| L | 以上前提下的严格比较、存在、必要条件及有界分工推论；由 Lean 检查。 / Checked implications for comparison, existence, necessary means and bounded allocation. |

形式连接没有直接把“必须继续”写进所有合格方案。先允许失败方案进入待比较集合，再要求：对其中每个失败方案，都存在一个合格恢复方案，保留它所保护的其他基本要求。由 J2 的比较结构和 J4 的选择承诺，失败方案被击败。另行保证未被击败集合非空，随后才推出共同需要的支持、组织及时间条件。有限非空候选的存在使用已完成的前沿定理；无限候选不自动满足。

The formal connection does not define every eligible plan as successful. Failure plans enter comparison. The substantive premise is that each such plan has an eligible restoration retaining its other protected basic claims. J2's ordering and J4's selection then exclude the failed plan. Nonempty undefeated alternatives are required separately before necessary support, organization and timing become collective requirements. Existing finite-frontier results can establish existence for finite nonempty menus; unrestricted infinite menus need more.

不同方案的需求列表或参与次数可以因真实替代技术而不同，但必须指向同一个独立说明的生活要求；改变目标必须另有根据。把需求改为零、推迟有害的期限或删掉受影响者，不能靠形式上的“现在可行”认证同一保护已经成功。

Plan-specific demand lists and participant counts can differ with genuine alternative means, but must serve the same independently identified living claim unless a target revision is separately justified. Setting demand to zero, postponing a deadline harmfully or deleting affected positions cannot certify the original protection by making a weaker target feasible.

**前提不成立时结论改变。** 恢复若夺走他人的基本生活、只有不正当强迫才能实现、或者真正没有及时途径，这个充分推论不适用。进入既有的真实冲突、共同增援、目标修订、减损或正当结束程序；不得在定义里删掉受损者来保住定理。

**Failure of a premise changes the conclusion.** Restoration that deprives another of basic living, requires unjustified coercion or lacks a timely route falls outside this sufficient inference. Use the existing conflict, additional-support, revision, mitigation or justified-ending procedures; do not remove affected claims to manufacture applicability.

## 4. 分工、交接与有限完成 / Allocation, handoff and finite completion

两个已有正当支持途径都能完成同一实际反应历史时，必需的是至少一个共同实现，不是每位贡献者都必需。Lean 中的具体现证允许“左方提供”“右方提供”“不提供”三个候选；不提供仍实现一个真实的可放弃活动。前两项在公开的剩余容量条件下保留三项基本保护，不提供失去其中一项。比较排除失保而保留两个分工，不删除放弃活动的损失。

The concrete checked comparison admits left provision, right provision and withholding. Withholding realizes an optional activity that the support choices forgo. Under disclosed spare-capacity assumptions, both provision routes protect the living claim and both contributors; withholding loses the first protection. The comparison therefore preserves two allocations while retaining the optional loss. Neither contributor is individually indispensable across those alternatives.

仅剩一种有界正当路径时，条件性特定要求可以成立；容量总和不够时，空方案集不产生无限义务。一般责任仍须 J3 的关系根据，不能仅把有能力者列入一个库存表就认定他应被征用。共同池的配方不推导所有权、同意或劳动分工。

When only one bounded legitimate route remains, a conditional specific requirement can arise. Insufficient joint capacity produces no unlimited obligation from an empty plan set. J3's relational grounds remain necessary; counting another's capacity does not authorize requisition. A shared-stock recipe derives neither ownership nor consent nor a division of labor.

交接须保持实际到达的状态及下一时段的共同能力；换一个名义负责人没有恢复被消耗的物资、组织或技能。需求空档可以容许先撤后接；发生实际需要时须核对支持。需求结束、知情拒绝、承担已完成或有根据的终结可改变继续要求；此前未补救的损失另行保留。这里复用[实践判断的交接与释放结果](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md#6-目的变化学习与结束责任--changed-purposes-learning-and-discharge)，不重新定义其完成范围。

Handoff must preserve the state actually reached and the next interval's joint capability. A new nominal provider does not restore consumed material, organization or skill. A no-demand gap may permit withdrawal before replacement; actual demand requires actual support. Ended demand, informed refusal, fulfilled undertakings or warranted closure can change the continuation requirement, while unresolved earlier harms remain distinct. Existing handoff and discharge results retain their scope.

## 5. 有证据的共同实践对应 / An evidence-informed collective application

[来源评估](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SUSTAINED-LIVING-JUDGMENT-EVIDENCE.md)选取灌溉共同实践，连接作物生长时段、实际送达、设施与协调的保留。它是有资料约束的应用分析，不是对任何现实社群作出未经调查的分水命令，也不是本项目的新干预。

The evidence assessment uses irrigation to connect living stages, actual delivery, infrastructure and coordination. This is a documented application analysis, not an instruction allocating water to an uninvestigated community or a new intervention by this project.

对一个具体待决安排，必须先查明：每块地所处阶段及可受损程度、哪些家庭生活依赖收成、替代供给、生态与下游要求、渠道真实能力、维护者的健康与完整负担、既有正当规则及申诉权限。只知道总水量或预期产量不足。维护队伍的训练、接班与参与能是生活继续的必要条件，同时这些人的生活也具有自身保护地位。

For a concrete decision, establish field stages and vulnerability, household dependence, alternative provision, ecological and downstream claims, actual delivery capacity, maintainers' health and cumulative burdens, and justified rules and appeal. Total water or expected yield is insufficient. Skills, succession and participation can enable continuation while contributors' own lives retain protection in their own right.

可检验的三种结论为：若有及时修复且保住其他基本要求，失保加可放弃增益的方案被击败；若修复仅靠损害维护者基本生活，原充分结论撤回并进入冲突程序；若名义供给增加而实际送达或生长继续未改善，则修正对应，不能以“投入已完成”报告保护成功。以上是本理论从既有 G0/J2–J4 作出的应用推理，不归给经验文献作者。

Three testable consequences follow conditionally: timely repair preserving other basic claims defeats avoidable failure plus optional gain; repair dependent on basic deprivation of maintainers requires conflict reassessment; increased nominal provision without actual delivery or living continuation defeats the claimed correspondence. These are our applications of G0/J2–J4, not ethical findings attributed to the empirical authors.

## 6. 范围、对手与下一检验 / Scope, rivals and the next test

**实际产出至上**承认组织与依存，却只按总产出选择。它可以真实维持某些活动，同时毁掉维护者或下游生活；本理论以 J2–J4 明示拒绝这种无限优先，不假装它在逻辑上不可能。**总量足够论**忽略时序和能力，已被模型中同供给、不同时间窗口的反例限制。**原组织永存论**把可替代的手段变成最终目的；重新组织和正当结束保留。**无限照护论**把共同需要推成每人无限义务；实际替代与容量反例排除这一步。

Output maximization can retain real organization while depriving maintainers or downstream lives; J2–J4 explicitly contest that priority. Bulk sufficiency fails in the checked equal-provision/different-deadline witness. Institutional perpetuity confuses replaceable means with the living realization. Unlimited care confuses collective need with unlimited individual duty; alternatives and capacity boundaries block that inference.

本轮完成目标是把**持续生活的实际条件、明确伦理根据与共同决定**连成可审查的论证，并以一般条件定理、真实反例和来源约束检查。它不宣称全部生命域、完整自生组织、无限持续、普遍遵循或全部现实分配已经完成。下一实证连接是一个具体共同实践中“测得的组织／送达能力与阶段需求 → 有权限的实际安排 → 实际保护及承担者负担”的前瞻核验；未经实际校准不能升级为经验验证。

This connection joins sustained living conditions, explicit ethical grounds and collective decisions in an inspectable argument with general conditional proofs, genuine adverse cases and source constraints. It does not complete all living-domain coverage, autonomous organization, infinite continuation, universal compliance or every real allocation. The next empirical connection is prospective testing in a specified shared practice: measured competence/delivery and stage demand → authorized enactment → actual protection and contributor burden. Correspondence cannot be promoted to empirical validation before that test.
