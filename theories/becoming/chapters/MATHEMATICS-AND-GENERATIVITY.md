## Abstraction and Ren's earlier question

On 4 May 2026, Ren wrote:

> 对过程进行抽象可以产生一些“绝对真理”，对物也可，数学不就是干这个的吗

An English rendering is: “Abstraction from processes can produce some ‘absolute truths’; so can abstraction from things—isn't that what mathematics does?” The Chinese is Ren's verbatim wording; the English is an AI translation. [Original conversation](../../../sources/12-claude-ai-chats/2026-05-04_绝对哲学真理的定义_a62c2a92.md).

A precise development of this thought is that a mathematical conclusion can be independent of the material realizing a structure while remaining conditional on that structure. Given explicit assumptions \(A\), a proof establishes a consequence \(G\):

\[
\forall P,\quad A(P)\Rightarrow G(P).
\]

This is the form of the current [conditional-universality programme](CONDITIONAL-UNIVERSALITY.md). It lets one argument constrain many processes. Whether a particular organism, institution or machine satisfies \(A\) remains a question about that application.

Mathematics also constructs structures with no known physical realization. The proposed relationship therefore cannot require every mathematical object to be abstracted directly from an observed object. Abstraction, idealization, invention and investigation of consequences can work together. “Mathematics studies structures and their consequences under explicit rules” is a useful perspective here, rather than a settled account of all mathematical truth.

## What mathematics already contributes

The [formal core](FORMAL-CORE.md) uses several mathematical languages because the explanatory questions differ.

| Question in the theory | Mathematical contribution | Inputs still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Which events can follow which others? | Relations, inductively generated histories and reachability | Conditions, event laws and physical interpretation |
| Which differences may a model forget? | Exact abstraction and refinement | The questions, observations and outcomes to preserve |
| Can an activity continue with its actual supports? | Accounting inequalities, dependency graphs and invariant sets | Identified stocks, losses, provision and support laws |
| How do organizations form, persist or disappear? | Dynamical systems and stochastic processes | Reaction laws, parameters and initial conditions |
| Can one mechanism supply another's inputs? | Composition and implementation correspondence | Compatible information, resources, timing and affected centers |
| When is a conclusion warranted by its premises? | Logic, proof objects and proof checking | Definitions, inference rules and the interpretation of premises |

These tools can expose a missing explanation. A theorem that begins with a calibrated sensor makes calibration an input. A controller maximizing a supplied reward does not thereby explain the origin of that reward. Formalization becomes especially valuable when it identifies precisely which assumption the next generative mechanism must supply.

For example, the existing support account has the form

\[
r_{t+1}+d_t\le r_t+c_t,
\]

where reserves \(r_t\), demands \(d_t\) and provision \(c_t\) are nonnegative. Summing gives

\[
r_n+\sum_{t<n}d_t\le r_0+\sum_{t<n}c_t.
\]

With finite initial reserve, unbounded cumulative demand requires unbounded cumulative provision. This conclusion depends on the account, not on whether its realization is chemical or institutional. Identifying a faithful account in either domain requires further evidence. Several supports can have separate accounts; the argument supplies no universal scalar of value.

## A small bridge from repetition to number

Take one formal event symbol \(a\), and permit every finite word:

\[
\varepsilon,\ a,\ aa,\ aaa,\ldots
\]

Here \(\varepsilon\) is the empty word. Concatenation combines words. Their lengths obey

\[
\ell(uv)=\ell(u)+\ell(v),\qquad \ell(\varepsilon)=0.
\]

Every word over this one-symbol alphabet is uniquely determined by its length. Consequently these words with concatenation have the structure of the natural numbers with addition. A hand proof follows by induction on the first word: appending to the empty word leaves the second unchanged; prepending one symbol adds one to the combined length. Each \(n\) is represented by the word of \(n\) copies of \(a\).

This is an elementary mathematical construction, not a new result or a derivation of arithmetic from premise-free existence. Finite words, equality and induction are supplied. With several symbols, length still preserves concatenation and reaches every natural number when the alphabet is nonempty, but it forgets order and symbol identity.

The physical distinction matters. A switch may return to its starting state after two toggles. Two events and zero events then have the same endpoint while remaining different histories. A system with a finite unreplenished reserve may be unable to realize arbitrarily long words at all. Formal repeatability, distinct histories and distinct physical endpoints are three different things.

This example locates a plausible connection: operations and repetition provide material for mathematical abstraction; the abstraction states exactly which differences it retains.

## When an abstraction supports composition

Let \(W=A^*\) be all finite words over an alphabet, and let \(\sim\) be an equivalence relation expressing which words an abstraction identifies. We would like to define an operation on equivalence classes by

\[
[u]\cdot[v]=[uv].
\]

This operation is independent of the chosen representatives **if and only if**

\[
u\sim u',\quad v\sim v'
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
uv\sim u'v'.
\]

An equivalence with this property is a congruence for concatenation.

**Proof.** If the displayed implication holds, replacing either representative leaves the resulting class unchanged, so the operation is well defined. Conversely, if class multiplication is well defined, equal input classes must give equal output classes, yielding the implication. Associativity and the identity class then follow from concatenation. This proof concerns total formal concatenation; physical paths need their endpoints and admissibility preserved as well.

Length gives a good example: words of equal lengths continue to have equal lengths when concatenated with words of equal lengths.

A counterexample is the summary “short” for lengths zero or one, and “long” for lengths at least two. It identifies \(\varepsilon\) with \(a\). But appending \(a\) produces \(a\) from the first and \(aa\) from the second. The same summarized input and operation produce different summaries. Exact multiplication of these classes cannot be defined by concatenation.

The result identifies a useful requirement: **when an abstraction is intended to support exact composition on equivalence classes, it must respect the composition it is being asked to support.** Approximate models and richer representations need their own criteria.

The theory already has a stronger, differently scoped result for actual processes. Its [Lean-checked abstraction theorem](GENERATIVE-CORE-LEAN.md#exact-representation-has-a-complete-local-test) requires exact projected one-step possibilities from every concrete starting state. This is equivalent to preserving every finite event word and its projected endpoint. The quotient argument above does not substitute for that lifting requirement.

In the existing example, a depleted organization and a revised organization both lack current support. Only the revised organization permits restoration. A summary recording only support presence therefore loses a consequential distinction. Recording organization as well as stocks changes what can be predicted.

## Composition gives category theory a concrete role

Category theory is relevant where structures and the transformations between them must compose coherently. For our work, its useful question is whether a translation preserves the operations and interfaces claimed by a model.

There is an established comparator close to the material branch. Baez and Pollard construct categories of open reaction networks and open dynamical systems, with a functor relating them; their composition connects specified inputs and outputs. They also give a separate steady-state abstraction. This shows a concrete way to organize translations between process descriptions. It does not identify their steady-state interface with our finite-history, resource or controller interfaces. [Primary paper, introduction and Sections 3–8](https://arxiv.org/html/1704.02051v5).

A categorical presentation could make some of our composition obligations easier to state and reuse. It would still need the actual resource laws, information channels and physical coupling. A compositional diagram earns explanatory force when its mappings preserve these features; matching terminology is insufficient.

The same applies to type theory. Lean represents propositions and proofs within dependent type theory, giving explicit objects whose validity can be checked. This provides a precise setting for many of our conditional derivations. The chosen logic and theorem premises remain part of that setting. [Official account](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Propositions-and-Proofs/).

## What the theory might explain about mathematical activity

The reverse direction is a research proposal:

\[
\text{consequential distinctions}
\longrightarrow
\text{retained representations}
\longrightarrow
\text{reusable operations}
\longrightarrow
\text{explicit rules and checked inference}.
\]

A learner can first distinguish situations by their consequences. It may then retain a representation that groups situations together, discover when those groupings support repeated operations, and revise a grouping when composition fails. Mathematical practice further requires capacities to formulate general claims, distinguish examples from proofs, check dependencies and transmit reliable methods.

The threshold counterexample gives this proposal a concrete target. A learner that groups empty and one-event histories together can encounter incompatible predictions after appending an event. Recovering exact composition requires refining that distinction or changing the representation and question. Why the learner detects the failure, has access to suitable evidence, pays for a richer representation and retains the correction must be explained.

The proposed mechanism connects to the existing acquired-prediction and selector-revision branches. It currently borrows the symbol interpretation, available operations, question to be answered, calibrated evidence, candidate representations and update route. The next advance would construct or identify part of that acquisition and revision under an inherited material history, compared with a fixed representation and a conventional adaptive learner.

The stronger philosophical questions remain distinct:

| Question | What the present exploration supports |
|---|---|
| How do mathematical beliefs and practices arise? | A candidate generative research path involving representation, composition failures and revision |
| Why is a particular conclusion mathematically valid? | A proof from specified definitions and premises; causal success alone does not establish validity |
| Why does mathematics apply to a physical process? | A justified mapping that preserves the relations relevant to the claim |
| What ultimately grounds mathematical truth or existence? | An open philosophical question; the present construction supplies no unique answer |

A copied invalid proof can persist. A valid proof can be destroyed. The activity of proving belongs within the world, while the validity of its conclusion cannot be identified with the activity's survival. This is the same distinction between formation and warrant already developed in [scientific philosophy](SCIENTIFIC-PHILOSOPHY.md) and the [generative-closure study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-GENERATIVE-CLOSURE.md).

## What this changes in the research programme

The exploration gives the root an additional explanatory obligation: account for the formation of abstractions that retain relevant distinctions across operations, including the discovery that an abstraction fails. It also supplies a precise mathematical test for one elementary version of that obligation.

The [deeper-root result](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md) remains binding. Distinctions and product constructions alone do not determine dynamics. Likewise, naming generation as the root does not derive logic, arithmetic or all mathematical structures without additional assumptions.

Three kinds of finding would change this proposal. A composition failure would refute a claimed exact abstraction. A learner whose retained representation changes without improving its declared inference would separate reproduction from mathematical progress. An alternative mechanism explaining the same acquisition at lower cost or with fewer supplied capacities would weaken the claimed explanatory contribution of our implementation.

The initial conceptual checkpoint established a two-way relationship, an elementary construction, a composition criterion and explicit counterexamples. The deeper results below strengthen that interface. The acquisition mechanism, empirical reach and any metaphysical foundation of mathematics remain open. Their next connection is **formed learning → acquired and revisable abstraction → warranted general inference**, supporting the existing path toward models, reasons and wisdom without replacing it. The deeper investigation distinguishes six increasingly strong claims.

|---|---|---|
| Mathematical construction and deduction can have exact generative presentations | Established for the stated finitary structures and calculi | Operations, inference rules, interpretation and background mathematics |
| Some reusable concepts have a canonical minimum of necessary distinctions | Proved for deterministic processes relative to fixed actions and outputs | Correct law, question interface and exactness requirement |

## A canonical structure for consequential distinctions

Let \(S\) be the states of a deterministic process, \(A\) its allowed action alphabet, \(\delta\) its transition function and \(o\) its observable output. The output can be a tuple retaining several questions. Define

\[
s\sim t
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every finite action sequence gives the same output from }s\text{ and }t.
\]

This relation groups together states that no permitted continuation distinguishes. Its classes form a process: applying an action to a class gives the class of the resulting state. That operation is well defined because equivalent states have equivalent successors.

The important result is that **this is the uniquely coarsest exact abstraction that preserves the outputs and can update recursively**. Any other representation answering every such continuation correctly must retain at least these distinctions. In the finite case, any minimal exact recursive representation has the same structure up to renaming. [Structural results, M1–M3](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-STRUCTURAL-RESULTS.md#2-the-canonical-exact-abstraction).

This gives a precise sense in which a concept can be objective without being independent of every question. The actions, law and observed consequences determine the required distinctions. Different learners may use different symbols, but they cannot merge a distinguishable pair and still answer all those questions exactly.

Changing the question can force a finer concept. If a summary retains only parity, it cannot generally answer an exact-count question. If it retains only an actor's return, it may erase changes in another center's condition. The theorem requires the new distinctions once the richer question is specified; it does not determine which questions have ethical standing.

The result belongs to established automata and transition-system mathematics. It gives our theory a precise target for concept acquisition and revision. It is not a new discovery of behavioral equivalence, and it applies neither to every human concept nor automatically to stochastic, nondeterministic or approximate models.

## A finite certificate for all finite continuations

There is a constructive answer to the objection that finite learning can only support finite examples.

Suppose an unknown deterministic process has at most \(N\ge1\) reachable states, with a known finite action/output interface. Suppose a test can reset it faithfully to the same initial condition, execute a requested word and read the exact output. The bound and stationarity must be justified independently; trial costs and failures remain in the larger account.

Test every action word of length at most \(2N-2\). **Every candidate with at most \(N\) states that matches these tests has the same observable behavior on every finite word.**

The reason is finite distinguishability. Comparing two candidates uses at most \(2N\) states. If their behavior differs, successive distinctions must expose it by length \(2N-2\). Agreement throughout that horizon therefore excludes every later disagreement within the declared class. This is a sufficient exhaustive procedure, not an efficiency claim. [Structural results, M4–M5](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-STRUCTURAL-RESULTS.md#4-when-finite-acquired-evidence-can-identify-the-whole-behavior).

For \(N=3\) and two actions, there are 31 words through length four. The finite check enumerates all 5,898 Boolean-output machines with one to three states and two actions: these query profiles determine their 1,054 distinct initial behaviors. The broader verification covers 9,994 machines by also including all four-state unary cases; it independently compares refinement with shortest-witness search. These are exhaustive constructed model checks, not observations of natural learners or a proof-assistant certification. [Executable coverage and receipt](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/README.md).

The bound is essential. For any completed testing horizon \(H\), a constant-zero process and a longer chain that first outputs one at \(H+1\) agree throughout the tests and later differ. Without a justified bound or other restricting structure, that finite transcript cannot certify unrestricted agreement.

This is a stronger and more useful conclusion than either unqualified induction or a blanket denial of generalization: **finite evidence can support an unbounded conditional conclusion when a proved structural restriction closes the alternatives.** The structural restriction is part of the justification, not an observed fact supplied by the transcript itself.

An actual learner must still acquire the records, construct or select its candidate model, preserve the relevant labels, pay for checking and use the model later. The present theorem supplies the information and certification interface. It does not derive the complete learner, its experimental access or its supported construction from the causal root.

## What generation explains and what it leaves supplied

The positive structural result extends beyond counting. A term grammar supplies generators and operations; an interpretation assigns them meanings; recursive evaluation uniquely determines the meaning of each compound expression. Declared equations identify expressions while preserving the operations. Finitary proof rules similarly generate a closure of judgments; soundness carries truth from premises to conclusions throughout the intended class.

This gives the research programme three distinct explanatory relations to join:

1. **Construction:** how a representation, operation or proof token is produced and retained.
2. **Structural consequence:** what follows from specified operations, equations and inference rules.
3. **Warranted application:** why the interpretation and premises fit the intended subject.

The [presentation study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-GENERATIVE-PRESENTATIONS.md) proves these local interfaces and preserves counterexamples. One generator can yield ordinary counting, arithmetic modulo a chosen integer, or idempotent saturation under different equations. The same inscription can state a truth in one interpretation and a falsehood in another. Generation alone does not choose among those structures.

Mathematical reasoning can therefore be included within a generative account without being reduced to survival or causal efficacy. The account can explain how a mathematician or proof checker forms and functions while also using proof to establish why its conclusions follow. Using mathematics in this explanation is not automatically a vicious circle. Claiming that the explanation has thereby justified its own entire logic would require another argument.

## The strongest foundation claim that survives comparison

A defensible current formulation is:

> **Mathematical practices are realizable forms of generative activity. Their constructions and inferences can be specified by structural rules whose consequences hold across faithful interpretations. A generative account can investigate how systems acquire, support and revise the representations that make such reasoning possible.**

This is an AI-authored research formulation. It is stronger than an analogy and more limited than a proof that generativity constitutes every mathematical truth.

Hilbert already connected reliable inference to distinguishable signs and their arrangement; Brouwer grounded mathematics in a different conception of constructive activity. Structuralist and realist positions can accept that mathematical knowledge develops while disagreeing about whether development constitutes its objects or truths. The [foundation audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-FOUNDATION-AUDIT.md) compares the primary texts and states its access limits. Compatibility with these positions is not evidence that our framework uniquely subsumes them.

Two exact limits matter. First, generating arbitrarily long finite expressions does not by itself justify a completed infinity or all arbitrary infinite objects. Finite descriptions over a countable alphabet are countable, while classical infinite binary sequences are not. Second, no sound effective axiom system strong enough for ordinary arithmetic yields all standard arithmetic truths. A uniformly effective sound extension process remains subject to that limit. This permits continued mathematical development; it excludes the stronger promise of one effective, exhaustive, self-authorizing truth generator.

These limits do not refute the repository's governing objective. A universal explanation of the conditions and mechanisms of mathematical activity need not decide every mathematical proposition, just as a theory of computation need not solve every computation. They require the proposed foundation to state which kind of universality it seeks.

## Empirical reach and the next connected construction

The [evidence review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-LEARNING-EVIDENCE.md) inspects five primary reports and existing computational comparators. Accessible exact matching, retained cardinal representations, productive counting, formal arithmetic expressions and compositional transfer can come apart. Some studies associate exact performance with usable symbolic range; none establishes one indispensable physical or linguistic carrier for all mathematics.

Established Bayesian concept learning and neural meta-learning already implement important parts of the proposed acquisition route. Their supplied primitives, curricula and transfer failures make good comparison points. No distinctive empirical prediction for the whole generative theory is established by this study.

The next connection is now more precise:

**A formed learner retains real diagnostic evidence, refines an initially inadequate abstraction, and passes its interpreted result to a sound checking procedure that it can afford to use.**

The construction must distinguish an ordinary good prediction, a certificate valid within a known model class, and confidence that the real target belongs to that class. Its inherited resources and reader must remain compatible with the existing prediction/recording work. Conventional state-learning and compositional learners must receive matched evidence and budgets.

Failure to discover the distinguishing continuation would identify an access problem. A correctly refined abstraction that cannot be stored or read would identify a support problem. A formally sound certificate applied outside its class would identify an interpretation problem. If a conventional mechanism meets all these interfaces with equal or fewer assumptions, the local comparison should record equivalence rather than assert novelty.

This completes the stronger claim audit and its mathematical supporting study. It leaves the acquisition-to-certification mechanism and broader empirical/philosophical grounding open, preserving the full derivation toward significance, learning, concern and wisdom.
