# A philosophy of becoming for knowing and living / 认识与生活的生成哲学

8 October 2026. A connected, AI-authored philosophical proposal responding to [Ren's request to pursue the account to its finish line](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-knowledge-content-continuation.md#finish-line-request). It draws together the [knowledge account](KNOWLEDGE.md), [practical judgment](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md), [shared inquiry](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SHARED-INQUIRY-AND-STANDING.md) and the [generative foundation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). [Completion assessment and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/KNOWLEDGE-PHILOSOPHY-COMPLETION.md).

**The resulting proposal is complete as a connected, defended account of knowing, inquiry and their ethical place in living. Its universal truth, exhaustive semantics and exclusive necessity are not established.** This is a completion of the present synthesis, not a claim that every philosophical problem or the repository's stronger foundational objectives have been solved.

**本轮完成的是相连而有辩护的哲学候选：认识如何在生成中形成、怎样有根据、如何共同纠正，以及怎样进入有责任的生活。不是宣告全部哲学已解，也不是证明所有科学哲学必须接受本理论的每项命题。**

## 1. The common beginning

Activity forms and changes the conditions of further activity. Effects can persist, interact and become conditions for later formation. Relatively enduring organizations, living capacities and differentiated perspectives form within this unfolding. This is the theory's positive explanatory starting point, with existing conditional mechanisms and evidence; the sentence alone does not derive all laws, all life or all possible minds.

A knowing perspective and what it investigates are differentiated through actual access and activity within this history. They are not fundamentally independent substances. Their difference nevertheless matters: what someone believes can fail to describe what obtains, and one participant can know something another does not. A common reality does not imply one universal knower, identical experiences or unrestricted access.

The account accordingly gives analytical distinctions a task. Memory concerns how earlier effects remain available; agency concerns organized capacities to respond; a model concerns selected relations represented within activity; evaluation concerns how continuation is assessed. These relations can develop together without becoming interchangeable. Calling them generative does not discharge the need to explain their particular formation.

## 2. How a difference becomes something known

Not every trace is a representation, and not every representation is understood. A distinction acquires content through sufficiently determinate relations of formation and use: acquired discrimination, preserved reference, calibration, language, testimony or inference can make something available as concerning a particular question. Those relations must constrain what the representation concerns independently of whether this instance succeeds.

Content therefore survives some mistakes. An inaccurate report of present conditions can still be about those conditions. A true report about an earlier sample can be mistakenly used as evidence of the present. If every output were defined as meaning whatever makes it true, the account would erase error instead of explaining it.

For someone to possess a propositional judgment, its content must be available within their cognitive activity: for acceptance, inference, anticipation, qualification or response. A sentence stored somewhere inaccessible is not automatically that person's knowledge. These capacities may be socially supported and need not all be exercised now. They require no further inner observer who interprets the interpretation.

The defended candidate is:

> Propositional knowledge is a true commitment whose formation or present maintenance is competently connected to what makes it correct, and whose correctness is not accidental relative to the relevant possibilities of error.

Truth, content, competence, access and relevant error each require an account. The proposal does not derive them from the ethical axiom. It distinguishes knowing that, explanatory understanding and knowing how; the latter two concern organized dependencies and practical capacities respectively, and need not reduce to a stock of asserted sentences.

## 3. Why a reason is more than a cause of belief

An epistemic reason supports a claim through a defensible relationship to its correctness. It becomes someone's operative reason when it participates in their acceptance or maintenance of that claim. Admiration can cause trust while relevant access supports trust; both can coexist, but neither explains the other merely by being present.

The account explicitly adopts four philosophical commitments: assertion is answerable to accuracy; evidence must bear on the stated claim; competent fallible reliance need not await proof of every enabling condition; and reflective claims of justification must answer pertinent challenges. These commitments are defended through the phenomena of error, correction and inquiry. They are not unannounced consequences of G0 or new verbatim axioms attributed to Ren.

Grounds can be acquired, lost or replaced. A later sound investigation can establish a new basis for a formerly lucky belief without repairing its original epistemic history. An earlier well-grounded belief can become unwarranted when its sustaining conditions change. A serious undercutter can defeat reliance without proving the opposite claim.

Standards can also be investigated. A rule of trust can be challenged by evidence about source access, dependent reports or changed calibration. Correction uses capacities that remain in place while testing a specified failure. It does not certify all cognition from nowhere. This fallibilist position makes ordinary inquiry possible while retaining the general skeptical challenge as a substantive unresolved dispute.

## 4. Objectivity through differentiated access and correction

Objectivity concerns answerability to the relations a claim describes across relevant differences in access and perspective. Agreement can reflect this achievement or merely reproduce one mistake. Differently vulnerable checks can expose a shared error without being causally isolated from the common reality.

Revisions need not discard every earlier achievement. A finer concept can preserve a supported partial claim; a new route can preserve an answer with better grounds. But a false universal claim does not become past knowledge simply because a later theory extracts a useful approximation. Where meanings or records cannot be adequately connected, continuity remains unsettled.

Projection into new conditions likewise needs grounds for relevant continuity. Sampling, causal organization, calibration and theoretical explanation offer different routes. Belonging to one generative world does not guarantee that its local conditions remain unchanged. A warranted extension states its conditions and vulnerabilities; its precision inside a model cannot establish the model's empirical range.

An explanation must add a supported dependence: why a phenomenon occurs, what constitutes a capacity, or what structural relationships establish a consequence. The appropriate evidence differs for empirical, mathematical, constitutive and normative claims. Their integration depends on justified correspondence, not on transferring one kind of authority indiscriminately to the others.

## 5. The field of inquiry can itself be corrected

Shared inquiry is accountable for what it admits and excludes. A question selects a target; evidence is acquired through particular routes; a model admits possibilities; a practical comparison includes particular effects. Those selections require reasons appropriate to the undertaking.

The crucial distinction is between a reason to investigate and a reason to believe. A puzzling report can justify examining an omitted condition before its preferred explanation is accepted. A difficulty in existing language can justify work on the categories before a complete new vocabulary exists. The present framework cannot require every challenge to fit its final conclusions before allowing the challenge to bear on them.

Relevant access, affected standing and decision authority are different grounds for participation. Competence supports credibility in a scope. Being affected gives reasons for inclusion in practical comparison and, under the declared practical commitments, standing to be heard and challenge. A justified undertaking or procedure can authorize a bounded decision. None of these grounds automatically supplies the others.

An adequate hearing requires a real possibility of changing the assessment when the contribution warrants it. It does not require accepting every objection, equal confidence in every report, disclosure of every private experience or an unlimited veto. Nonverbal and absent affected lives require supported representation rather than erasure. Control over the inquiry does not make its controller the voice of the whole.

The remaining fallibility is real. A community can overlook a relevant possibility despite good procedures. The account provides grounds for revisable scope and corrective practice, not a certificate that all future surprises have been enumerated.

## 6. G0 and the ethical place of knowledge

G0 states: **整体中的生及其继续生成具有基本正面规范意义。** Living and its further generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance.

This affirms present life as well as future formation: rest, understanding, friendship, self-care and finite fulfillment need not justify themselves solely by producing successors. A particular institution need not persist forever. Ending a damaging arrangement can participate in a better continuation, while its actual losses remain real.

Knowledge can contribute by making dependencies, possibilities, harm and effective responses available. Understanding can also be a present realization of life. Yet a true and competently grounded discovery can facilitate destruction, and a false belief can accidentally produce a benefit. These cases require the distinction between epistemic adequacy and ethical quality within the same activity.

“Good is generative” therefore needs substantive content. Mere multiplication, complexity, persistence or information growth does not settle the whole comparison. G0 supplies the positive direction; the declared J1–J4 commitments address improvement and loss, basic protection, bounded responsibility, and authorized feasible choice. E requires actual correspondence. Neither arbitrary weights nor universal obligations may be concealed in the meaning of generation.

J2's scoped priority of basic protection is an additional substantive commitment. J3's responsibility and participatory standing are also substantive. They can be defended and disputed. Someone may accept G0 while contesting these elaborations; that disagreement is not automatically a logical inconsistency.

## 7. Wisdom joins understanding, concern and response

Wisdom concerns sufficiently grounded understanding of what matters in this situation, warranted concern for the lives and possibilities involved, and a feasible, authorized response. It includes judgment about the scope and limits of inquiry itself.

Some uncertainties change which responses are appropriate; others can remain open because a justified response fits the remaining cases. A truthful investigation can improve a decision, or consume its opportunity. More information therefore does not automatically improve the whole activity of obtaining and using it.

The preceding checked components make only their stated mathematical claims: twenty theorems concern exact correspondence under supplied domains and meanings; eight further theorems concern common admissible responses, faithful refinement and deadlines. They do not prove that an actual domain is complete, a requirement ethical, or a person understands the result. This synthesis adds no new mathematical theorem and does not recount those twenty-eight as new results.

Under G0/J1–J4/E, inquiry can become a bounded duty when a justified responsibility, material uncertainty and proportionate available investigation make it necessary. It can also end when a response is adequately grounded or further inquiry would defeat a stronger requirement. Closure preserves what remains unknown and what would warrant reopening. It is distinct from certainty, successful implementation and final settlement of the underlying scientific question.

## 8. Non-self preserves the particular lives in helping

Ren's helping example concerns a course that benefits both participants overall but changes the helper's life. The wider perspective includes the formation of their capacities, relationship and future possibilities. The helper's change can be part of a new shared realization, rather than an exchange between two permanently fixed selves.

That explanation does not make the change costless. A lost opportunity, a changed relationship or an abandoned way of living remains part of the actual history even if the participants later endorse what develops. The helped life and the helping life are differentiated realizations within the whole, not interchangeable units whose losses vanish in an aggregate description.

The ethical question is therefore what this relationship actually sustains, forms and forecloses, which responsibilities are justified, and what alternatives and limits apply. Shared formation can transform the reasons; it cannot replace the comparison with the slogan that everything is one. Genuine self-good needs no independent outward payoff to count.

## 9. What scientific philosophy must answer to

The account supports a requirement of explanatory adequacy: a philosophy claiming to explain scientific knowing must preserve, or better explain, the relevant achievements of meaningful judgment, warranted support, genuine error and correction. An account that simply identifies communal acceptance with truth misdescribes cases of common error. One that ignores formation may omit a question rather than refute a formation account; its stated scope matters.

This requirement does not establish that every adequate philosophy must adopt our entire formulation. Rival competence theories and social epistemologies can preserve many of the same relationships. Ethical pluralism can dispute J2 without thereby destroying an account of calibrated measurement. These are substantive competitors and disagreements, not merely arbitrary predicates switched in a weaker model.

The original conjecture therefore separates into two claims. **Adequate accounts must answer to the relationships required by their target phenomena** is a defensible, scoped methodological claim. **Every scientific philosophy must agree with our whole theory, including G0 and all further commitments** is not established. Compatibility, explanatory contribution and unique necessity require different arguments.

Our theory must accept the same demands. Its premises remain visible; its source comparisons retain their limits; formal proof cannot certify its empirical interpretation; and a relevant counterexample can require revision of a preferred claim. Coherence with our theory is not independent evidence that our theory is true.

## 10. The endpoint reached

The connected account now runs from generative formation through differentiated knowing, meaningful content, error, grounds, revisable standards, objectivity, shared inquiry and practical significance to wisdom. It includes its own exposure to criticism. Each connection has a proposed explanation and identified conditions; the latest study addresses the previously unanswered question of relevant scope and standing.

The synthesis is ready to be assessed as a philosophical position. Its completion does not depend on manufacturing one more mechanism example, proving G0 without axioms, or solving every remaining dispute before stating the account. Equally, completing the position does not prove its universal adequacy. Conscious experience, exhaustive semantics, the general problem of induction and exclusive necessity remain unsettled; actual institutional applications need their own evidence and justification.

**认识，是生成中的某些活动形成了对实际关系有根据的把握；智慧，是这种把握与对生及继续生成的关切相连，进入有限、负责而可纠正的回应。** Here “grasp” summarizes knowing and understanding broadly; it does not add explanatory understanding as a requirement for every item of propositional knowledge.

The endpoint is a defended proposal with clear routes for correction, not an immunity from further inquiry. ## 18. Completed answers on scope and further formation

The [completed two-question account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIVING-VALUE-SCOPE.md#10-completion-of-the-two-questions) states the theory's position under G0. **Consciousness differentiates significance within living relations; it is not their sole admission condition.** Experiences of pain, enjoyment, understanding and participation matter in their specificity. Biological and ecological living can also have basic significance beyond their contribution to conscious experience. Whole appraisal preserves these differences and their relations; it supplies neither identical treatment nor a numerical ranking. The broadest extension to all organizational generation remains a declared research scope.

**Further living formation can itself count positively, even without improving an already existing beneficiary.** This is G0's explicit commitment, not an inference solely from the standing of existing lives. New living and relationships can form new purposes and forms of fulfillment. A possible person need not already be deprived for there to be a favorable reason concerning a possible realization. Future lives' basic conditions still count when affected.

The favorable aspect, overall comparison, permission and assigned duty remain distinct. J1–J4 and faithful correspondence supply additional comparison and practical requirements. Optional enrichment does not automatically override basic protection; new living does not make existing lives replaceable. There is no universal duty to reproduce or maximize population, complexity or possibilities. Present living, rest and finite fulfillment have significance without needing successors. This also connects with [developing concern](CONCERN-AND-NEW-REASONS.md), without claiming guaranteed discovery or motivation.

The comparison with Frick's primary argument concerning conditional welfare-related reasons makes the remaining disagreement exact: G0 affirms a further positive ground concerning formation. The report answers the objection without declaring the rival incoherent or scientifically disqualified. **Both questions are complete as a defended position under the authorized foundation; whole-theory necessity remains unproved.** No new theorem or empirical result is claimed.

**意识使生的意义分化，但不穷尽其范围；进一步的生有正面意义，但不自动成为某个人无限创造的义务。公理、比较承诺与实际对应各自明示；这一哲学回答的完成，不冒充整套理论普遍必要性的证明。**
