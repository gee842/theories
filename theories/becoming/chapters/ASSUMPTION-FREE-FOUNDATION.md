## 1. The question concerns warrant, not the absence of an `axiom` command

Three different achievements must be distinguished. A proof can use no separately declared Lean axioms. An explanation can make every local premise explicit. A foundation can justify its starting commitments without leaving them merely supplied. The first two do not establish the third. The stronger request also asks about the logical rules, interpretation and actual applicability through which justification is expressed.

Conversely, a supplied premise is not necessarily an arbitrary belief. A proposition supported by an actual occurrence has a different status from one admitted solely to obtain a desired conclusion. We should investigate that support rather than either call every input groundless or call every observed input completely self-justifying.

Here “assumption-free” retains Ren's stronger objective. We do not silently replace it with “no hidden assumptions,” “no ethical axioms,” “constructive Lean,” or “no hypothesis parameters.” A report of progress must say which commitment has acquired a ground and what that ground itself requires.

## 2. Begin with the inquiry that is occurring

The first candidate is situated:

> **此问正在发生；对它的怀疑若实际进行，也在发生之中。**
> This inquiry is occurring; an enacted doubt about it is itself an occurrence.

This formulation does not begin by positing an independently constituted thinker who produces a separate representation of an independently constituted world. It points to the activity in which these descriptions are being made. Nor does it derive the occurrence from the truth of whatever the inquiry asserts. An actually expressed falsehood is still an expression; recognizing that occurrence does not endorse its content.

This is an evidential asymmetry between **the occurrence of the denial** and **what the denial says**. It is not the assertion that whatever cannot be doubted is universally true. Silent suspension, an unexpressed possibility and a later cessation of inquiry must not be redescribed as further actual denials just to keep the argument going.

The [earlier root argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONINSTRUMENTAL-ROOT-ARGUMENT.md#2-第一次尝试从发生中取得最小起点) already established the scope of this route. The present work credits it. Its uneliminated obligations are the recognition of the actual occurrence, the interpretation of the denial, and the inferential connection between them. The occurrence is a candidate direct ground for the claim; calling it a ground is not yet a presupposition-free justification of every operation of recognition.

Our [Lean companion](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/EnactedFoundation.lean) consequently takes both an enacted token and its occurrence correspondence as explicit inputs. It checks the inference; it does not create an actual token from the empty context. This is a faithful audit of the argument's logical form, not its ultimate grounding.

## 3. From occurrence to activity that forms further conditions

Bare occurrence does not yet contain the full content of 生生. The proposed advance concerns the actual relations within an unfolding inquiry:

1. A question becomes available for a response.
2. Some consequence of that question persists in the response's formation.
3. A response, correction or newly available demonstration changes what a subsequent inquiry can address or use.

These are claims to investigate, not three axioms hidden inside the word “inquiry.” Mere chronological succession does not show that the earlier activity mattered. A repeated string does not by itself establish memory, and writing a more elaborate description does not by itself create a new operative capacity. The earlier consequence must participate in the later activity in the relevant way.

The current work supplies a modest actual instance: a source was written, the compiler rejected specific proof terms, those terms were revised, and the revised source received a successful check. The saved sources and logs distinguish a produced assertion from a checked derivation. They support that tool-mediated history under the stated compiler and record correspondence. They do not prove the infallibility of the compiler, the accuracy of every surrounding report, or the ultimate origin of inference. This instance is evidence within the account, not a new universal experiment.

The existing [joined formation–learning history](SHENGSHENG.md) gives a richer conditional mechanism: an enacted consequence changes evidence, retained organization and subsequent action within one represented process. It earns its causal claim through its event laws and connected history. It does not obtain those laws from the bare fact of inquiry.

The positive foundation candidate can therefore be stated more carefully:

> **发生留下的实际关系，参与构成后续发生的条件；认识也在这种承接与转化中形成。**
> Relations retained through activity participate in the conditions of subsequent activity; knowing itself forms through this continuation and transformation.

This is our contemporary explanatory candidate, not a quotation from Ren or a classical text. Where the operative relationships are established, it articulates more than “something happens.” Its universal scope and ultimate warrant remain the work to be done. In particular, an occurrence's addition to a written history must not be substituted for an actual change in its possible consequences.

## 4. Why a real finite inquiry does not by itself establish 不息

Three questions differ: what has happened; what is now permitted by the operative relations; and what will actually happen. A successful history gives the first. Further admissibility needs a continuation argument. Actual indefinite continuation needs still more than the bare presence of permitted successors.

The new formal audit preserves the declared finite history instead of replacing inquiry with an unrelated inert state. Given any process \(P\), construct a bounded representation \(B(P)\) whose configurations are pairs \((s,n)\), with

\[
(s,n)\xrightarrow{a}_{B(P)}(t,m)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
s\xrightarrow{a}_{P}t\;\land\;n=m+1.
\]

The count is part of this explicit comparison construction. It is not a primitive cosmic time, a claim that all activity consumes a stock, or a newly discovered condition of the original process.

Lean checks the following exact characterization, with \(w\) a finite event list:

\[
\operatorname{Trace}_{B(P)}((s,n),w,(t,m))
\iff
\operatorname{Trace}_{P}(s,w,t)\land n=m+|w|.
\]

At every positive count, an action label is enabled exactly when it is enabled at the original configuration. Every replayed step projects to its original step. Every original finite history lifts with the same labels and original intermediate configurations. Starting the count at \(|w|\) yields the original history and an endpoint with no further enabled action.

Thus all records and relations already carried by those original configurations can remain present along the witnessed history while this enlarged process terminates. The result is general over finite histories; it is not one hand-picked two-state example. There are **11 new checked theorems and 18 rechecked inherited declarations**, all with empty reported axiom dependencies in the successful [receipt](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/enacted-foundation-verification.json).

The limitation of the comparison is decisive. It preserves the finite history and its projected transitions, not every unperformed future, full modal theory, or independent law of indefinite renewal. A retained sentence about an unbounded future need not remain true when interpreted as a claim about the bounded process. A model of a local inquiry is not automatically a model of the whole of reality. Consequently this result tests the inference **from finite enactment alone**; it does not refute a richer constitutive argument or prove that reality can cease.

Nor does 生生 have to mean endless operation of each particular organization. Transfer, transformation and finite fulfillment already belong to the fuller account. The outstanding stronger issue is why continuation of the whole would be necessary. Ending one represented process does not settle that issue. Conversely, mentioning an unspecified larger whole does not establish its continuation.

## 5. Can the developed whole justify its own beginning?

A serious alternative to a linear stack of axioms is a reconstruction in which the developed account explains the beginning, the means of inquiry and its own standards together. That is compatible with the nondual method. An explanation can include its own production without making its conclusions true merely because they were produced.

Hegel explicitly examines a beginning whose fuller ground is disclosed through the development of the whole. In *Science of Logic*, “With What Must Science Begin?”, §§93–99 discuss mediated pure knowing and an immediate beginning; §§101–107 develop the return to the ground. This supplies a substantive precedent for retrospective grounding, not a ready-made proof of 生生. The required transitions and their necessity still have to be assessed. [Primary text](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hl/hlbegin.htm).

For this investigation the positive test is: does the developed account provide additional warranted connections that explain why the starting commitment is adequate, while preserving alternatives and actual failure? The unsuccessful shortcut is: begin by granting universal generativity, derive a theory that calls all inquiry generative, then use that classification as the only reason for granting universal generativity. Our existing [core/tree theorem](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/CoreTreeRearticulation.lean) already shows that replacing a basis by its derived branches preserves its deductive content. Rearticulation alone does not discharge its initial warrant.

Wittgenstein's *On Certainty*, §§110–115 and §204, locates doubt and justification within a practice of acting rather than treating each as an independent proposition awaiting another proof. This challenges the assumption that ultimate grounding must consist only of an endless chain of propositional proofs. It does not establish our universal ontology or supply its generative laws. [German primary text](https://www.wittgensteinproject.org/w/index.php/%C3%9Cber_Gewi%C3%9Fheit).

These passages motivate investigating immanent warrant. Neither author is recruited as having proved the present proposal, and this comparison is not a full assessment of either philosophical system.

## 6. How the mathematical core should now be situated

The route is **actual inquiry → identified operative relationships → a justified representation of those relationships → consequences checked within that representation**. Each arrow has its own obligation. The route is not a premise-free deduction of types and a transition relation from the word 生生.

| Commitment | Present ground or role | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| This inquiry occurs | Its actual enactment is the situated evidential candidate | Ultimate justification of recognition and interpretation |
| Activity forms further conditions here | Retained effects and their operative participation, where identified | Actual correspondence and the reach of this explanation |
| Configuration/event distinctions | Analytical summaries of those relationships for a specified question | Adequacy, omitted relations and the formation of the chosen interface |
| The transition relation | Declared local laws, or laws established by further inquiry | Their more fundamental origin and universal applicability |
| Finite path consequences | Checked within Lean from the declared interface | Warrant of the adopted logic; physical realization is separate |
| Generation necessarily continues | Not established by finite enactment or replay | Independent modal and whole-process grounding |
| Life and further generation have positive normative significance | Explicit G0 in the independently authorized ethical route | That route is not an assumption-free derivation from occurrence |

“Configuration” and “event” need not name two independent ingredients of reality. They can name retained configurations of relations and distinguishable changes within the same activity. That interpretation still needs justification; a programming type declaration does not establish it. Likewise, avoiding primitive subject/object fields is a methodological achievement, not a proof that every possible ontology has been excluded.

The official [Lean account of axioms and computation](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Axioms-and-Computation/) explains the logical foundation and additional axioms available to proofs. Our empty audits concern the latter dependencies. They do not audit away type theory, inductive construction, meanings or the occurrence assumptions displayed in theorem signatures.

## 7. The next foundational obligation

The most promising surviving candidate is an **immanent account of warranted differentiation**: identify the relationships an actual inquiry must retain to be this inquiry, explain how they sustain meaningful discrimination and correction, and test whether alternative accounts preserve them. The target is not a primitive isolated self and not a universal process postulate placed beyond criticism.

The next result must go beyond making the current machinery more elaborate. It must justify a particular constitutive relationship without relying solely on a definition containing the desired conclusion. For example, can a rival preserve actual content, its uptake, correction and their independently identified dependencies while denying that prior activity participates in forming further conditions? If it can, we must determine whether it is an equivalent articulation, a substantively different ground, or evidence against the proposed connection. If it cannot, the argument must identify the precise relationship lost; “it is nongenerative” is not enough.

Even a successful result there would have to disclose its scope. Necessity for meaningful inquiry is not automatically necessity for everything that exists. No presupposition-free derivation of all logic, actual laws, whole continuation or universal ethics has been established here. Equally, no theorem has established that every richer grounding attempt must fail.

## 8. Earning an inference by constructing its ground

The next affirmative route asks whether an inferential rule can be justified through the ground it actually constructs. Pairing grounds and recovering a member makes one such justification explicit. A constructed function applied to an appropriate ground makes another explicit. The claim is about these actual transformations under their stated interpretation; it does not grant authority to every rule that happens to produce an assertion.

The new [typed inference companion](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/InferentialGrounds.lean) implements atomic grounds, conjunction, implication and contexts. Its interpreter recursively returns a ground of the conclusion. Three checked equations show what introduction followed by elimination actually returns. Its closed atomic terms exist exactly when their corresponding atomic ground type is inhabited. Applying this to actual claims still requires a warranted correspondence from those grounds to the claims.

The result does not establish that inference yields no new knowledge: type inhabitance is not a historical inventory of what someone already possesses. Nor does logical duplication establish that an activity can physically be performed twice. The exact duplication theorem returns two references to one ground. Actual realization and joint feasibility remain questions about the operative process.

This earns a relative explanation of the selected rules while displaying what remains supplied: atomic interpretation, admissible constructions, structural reuse and the metatheory. The source imports only `Std`; **eight new theorems and two definitions pass empty axiom-dependency audits** in the [source-bound check](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/inferential-grounds-verification.json). ## 9. Direct ground is not disqualified by being noninferential

A formal parameter is not automatically an arbitrary assumption. A displayed construction can supply a witness; an exhibited occurrence can supply evidence. Requiring every first ground to be an earlier proved proposition would itself impose an additional theory of justification. This investigation therefore takes direct presentation seriously rather than declaring ultimate grounding impossible from the shape of ordinary proofs.

The affirmative candidate is to identify what the inquiry actually exhibits, including its differentiated relations, without first positing an isolated thinker or an inert instantaneous datum. Recognizing a current record and establishing the history it represents remain different warrant obligations within that same inquiry. The mere word “immediate” does not certify every richer interpretation.

The [new study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md) examines this route alongside primary passages from proof-theoretic semantics and Nishida. It also assesses three proposed shortcuts: every challenge is generated; nothing actual lies outside the whole; and an ending is itself activity. Each needs a further specific argument before it can establish universal generative necessity. No general impossibility of such grounding is inferred.

The strongest remaining task is now more precise: **how can an exhibited ground justify the content attributed to it and the permitted transformations of that content, with neither a hidden semantic guarantee nor an endlessly postponed earlier proof?** That connection is pursued as part of the fully assumption-free target, with universal scope still to be earned.

## 10. The foundation's actual content fixes its completion test

The [canonical minimal-grounding statement](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope) includes consequential differentiation, structured interaction/change, and composition with possible recurrence. Its scope includes finite, static and destructive processes. The earlier focus on necessary endlessness must therefore be kept at its proper scope: the finite-replay result tests an additional claim of indefinite continuation, not a required property of every instance of the core.

Assumption freedom does not require one uniquely privileged notation or determination of every contingent empirical fact. It requires that the actual foundational commitments, their logical and interpretive warrant, and the scope claimed for them are justified rather than merely supplied. A local law cannot be moved into a parameter and thereby declared grounded. An actually evidenced application condition, conversely, is not ungrounded merely because a theorem takes it as an input.

The [corrected work sequence](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#8-correct-the-scope-before-making-a-stronger-claim-a-completion-gate) now tests those three root commitments directly. The task is to establish the consequences, dependence and composability attributed to them, then earn their foundational scope. ## 11. Mutual determination deserves a real test

A whole arrangement can determine content without independently fixing the truth of each starting symbol. The new [checked example](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/MutualGrounding.lean) imposes \(P\leftrightarrow\neg Q\) and \(Q\leftrightarrow(P\land Q)\), and derives the unique truth profile \(P\land\neg Q\). This gives genuine mathematical credit to mutual determination. The specified constraints and their interpretation remain visible.

Reading \(P\) as occurrence and \(Q\) as its absence then yields an exact audit: the whole constraint package is equivalent to \(P\). Under that interpretation the starting occurrence has been repackaged, not independently justified. This does not rule out a better whole-account argument. It locates what this candidate must still earn.

A further theorem shows why coherent implicit meaning and factual restriction differ. If every old case admits an extension satisfying the new constraints, those constraints establish an old-case claim uniformly exactly when it already held across all old cases. Unique new meanings can coexist with this conservativity. The supplied case range is not automatically a representation of all reality.

**Ten additional theorems pass**, with exact hypotheses and empty dependency audits in the [new receipt](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/mutual-grounding-verification.json). The [argument and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#9-a-direct-test-of-mutual-grounding) preserve both the positive result and its limit. The remaining central task is to justify the constitutive constraints and their intended reference, without declaring a linear chain the only possible foundation or granting a circle authority merely because it closes.

## 12. What an actual consequence establishes

An account of this inquiry must preserve its established consequential differences. That requirement does not automatically select a unique dependence behind them. The [new checked comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#10-consequential-difference-and-the-warrant-of-a-particular-dependence) preserves every finite ordinary record in two changing, generative routes. A specified reset separates their responses, and its result identifies the route within that declared class. What the reset actually changes, what it retains and whether the class covers the real case remain explicit correspondence obligations.

Both routes are generative. Their agreement therefore limits this dependency-identification argument; it does not refute the core. Nor is a record of values automatically the full relational target. If an operative connection is itself encountered, an adequate comparison must preserve or contest that connection rather than quietly discard it. Its warrant must be explained instead of inserted by calling the record complete.

A simultaneous whole-relation presentation is also checked to preserve the complete declared interface, including the reset. It counts as an equivalent articulation. Grounding the core does not require proving that only a sequential narrative can express its content.

The three root commitments now have more precise obligations:

| Commitment | Ground already investigated | Remaining foundational obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Consequential differentiation | Actual inquiry and checked constructions exhibit differences with distinct consequences. | Justify the recognition and relevant individuation of those differences; earn the claimed general scope. |
| Structured interaction | Specific dependencies can be constructed, tested and discriminated within explicit interfaces. | Ground the operative relationship and its applicability; ordinary co-occurrence does not settle every dependency attribution. |

These are obligations within one account, not separations into independently existing kinds. The present result makes a particular failed shortcut and a positive discrimination route precise. It leaves the stronger task intact: **发生怎样在自身之中成为有根据的分化、承接与转化，而其说明不再把这些联系仅仅当作预设？** How does actual activity ground its own differentiation, continuation and transformation, with those relationships justified rather than merely supplied?

## 13. A constructive answer to part of the grounding problem

**构成能够给出关系的根据；递推能够传递这个根据，却不因此证明一切起点与整个实在的对应。** A construction can ground a relation; further construction can preserve that ground, without thereby justifying every starting correspondence or its reach across reality.

The [new checked argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GROUNDING-INFERENCE-AND-PRESENTATION.md#11-derive-correspondence-through-formation-and-state-its-exact-remainder) makes the first part concrete. Form a representation `(x, x)` from a source value `x`. Its two projections return that value because of the operation just performed. Those equalities are proved, not added as an assumed correctness field. The correspondence then extends through arbitrary pairing, projection, abstraction and application in the existing typed-term language. The two presentations are related positions in this construction, not independently existing kinds of reality.

The general result also gives an exact limit: correspondence for **every** closed term holds if and only if the source transformations satisfy their atomic correspondence. This does not say that every application needs every possible source. It says that an all-terms claim already includes the single-source terms. A longer derivation cannot by itself remove those obligations.

Accordingly, the earlier question has a positive partial answer. A relation need not always be imposed by an unexplained interpretation after construction; a specified operation can constitute and exhibit it. The formal composition of such grounds is now checked, with **four new theorems and fourteen total declaration audits** in the [source-bound receipt](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/deeper/ground-relation-transport-verification.json). Ten of those audits are inherited. This is a standard mathematical method applied here, not a newly discovered universal logic.

The original stronger problem remains: establish the logic, recognition and reference through which the actual generative unfolding is understood, and justify the proposed scope wherever systems form. A proof about a constructed representation does not automatically answer those questions. Nor does the persistence of those questions make the constructed relation unreal or its proof worthless. The precise gain is a warranted instance and a complete relative propagation result; neither universal assumption freedom nor its impossibility has been established.

## Direct constitutive-warrant assessment

The [direct foundational argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-WARRANT-ARGUMENT.md) traces independently identified formation through the same arrangement's actual retention, use, interruption or replacement. This supplies a positive warrant for a constitutive relationship where that continuation is established, and for requiring an adequate account to preserve it. A whole-history or atemporal account can preserve the same content.

One excess completion gate is removed: the root does not require every unfolding to implement `FormedGrounds` or any other single architecture. Its common content concerns consequential differentiation, operative relations and compatible composition; static, finite and destructive regimes remain included. The previously completed conditional candidate is not reopened.

The stronger foundation nevertheless remains unproved. Neither an exhibited instance nor the universal requirement to preserve established relationships supplies the warrant that the proposed root adequately covers its full intended class, including recognition and inferential use. An enacted denial may demonstrate participation in inquiry without establishing that scope. An exhaustive division into participation and its absence can broaden representation without proving universal positive participation. These are limits of the proposed arguments, not a proof that the full core is false or that assumption-free grounding is impossible.

This round adds no mathematical theorem or empirical replication. Existing Lean results keep their exact scopes. The remaining work concerns the attribution and generality of the foundational relationships themselves; another exact-description theorem is not by itself a solution.

## General constitutive warrant and the exact remaining inference

The [direct general investigation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERAL-FOUNDATION-INTEGRATION.md) corrects the undifferentiated statement that actual applicability and all three root commitments remain ungrounded. Their permissive content has actual evidence: consequential differentiation and recurrent composition occur in identified histories. No induction to every system is needed to support that existence claim.

The stronger constitutive argument concerns actual joint realization. Contributions sharing conditions must fit together at the timing of the organization being explained; incompatible separately successful arrangements do not establish that organization. Continued organization can retain, transform or replace its realizing relations. This is a general necessary constraint grounded in independently identified organization, not a definition asserting that prior activity necessarily produces every future.

The [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERAL-FOUNDATION-REVIEW.md) accepts this constitutive component and direct grounds within the same unfolding. It does not accept the stronger inference from necessary realization to sufficient common explanation of formation, transformation and changing possibilities.
