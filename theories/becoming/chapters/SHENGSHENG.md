# 生生：活动怎样形成进一步活动 / How activity forms further activity

8 October 2026. A connected articulation requested by Yongxu Ren. The exposition is AI-authored; [Ren's wording and objective](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-shengsheng-articulation.md) are preserved separately. This is the conceptual and mechanistic companion to the [mathematical core](MATHEMATICAL-CORE.md), integrating the [generative-grounding programme](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md), its existing checked constructions and the current explicit-axiom ethical account. [Evidence, counterreview and completion audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SHENGSHENG-ARTICULATION-RECORD.md) identify the validation scope.

## 1. The principle and its content / 原则及其内容

> **Activity forms and transforms the conditions of further activity.**
>
> **活动形成并转化后续活动的条件。**

In this account, 生生 names the unfolding in which activity produces differences that participate in subsequent activity, including the formation and transformation of the relations through which further activity becomes possible. Some effects dissipate; others persist, combine or reconstruct enabling relations. Through those histories, relatively enduring organizations and differentiated capacities can form. Some organizations develop sensing, learning, evaluation and ways of revising their own activity. Each development requires its particular explanation.

**生生指向这样的展开：活动产生的差异持续或转化，参与后续活动，并能形成、维持或改变进一步活动赖以发生的关系。组织、生命、认识与关怀在其中分化；它们的形成、持续及善的方向分别需要说明。**

“Activity” does not require intention or an already constituted actor. “Conditions” names what enables or constrains a specified continuation: an arrangement, contact, material availability, temporal relation, retained distinction, acquired skill or established practice, where such a relation is actually identified. These examples are not a universal inventory of substances. Activity and conditions describe aspects of the same unfolding: an interaction can create an arrangement through which another interaction occurs, and that later interaction can change the arrangement again.

“Further” includes effects that continue while other activity is still occurring. The account does not assume that reality advances by one global sequence of isolated steps. Discrete traces are one selected mathematical resolution; concurrent and continuous processes require appropriate laws and correspondence.

The central explanatory demand is **to trace what an activity leaves operative, and how that changes subsequent occurrence or possibility**. Naming a successor state does not yet explain this. Naming a relation “enabling” does not establish that it operates. The account needs the actual route of influence, formation history and relevant alternatives.

Three commitments organize the investigation: consequential differences can occur; their interactions have structure; and interactions can combine, sometimes into recurrent arrangements. These are the [provisional foundational premises](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope), not a proved uniquely minimal axiom set. The [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md) asks us to explain distinctions within this unfolding. It does not certify monism by definition or require erasing differentiated consequences.

The descriptive account includes constructive and destructive activity. Depletion, exclusion, disintegration and misleading learning also change what follows. The normative 生生不息 orientation adds the positive significance of living and further generation; its explicit premise appears in §8. Neither account requires every particular organization to last forever.

## 2. How differentiation and continuity arise / 分化与持续

An organization is identified through relations that continue to perform an independently specified role across change. Components may be replaced while those relations recur; components may remain while the organization ceases to function. A history must therefore say what continues, by which processes, and at what resolution. An unchanged label is insufficient evidence of identity; a changed component is insufficient evidence of total discontinuity.

A boundary is a description of those effective relations at a stated scale. We can distinguish an acting position, an affected position and an observation channel when the history warrants them. The positions can overlap and change. The same activity can participate in producing an effect and undergo other effects. Neither physical connectedness nor membership in a larger organization makes experience, control or loss equal across its differentiated centers.

The [derived-range construction](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) gives one conditional way to form overlapping effective ranges from a connection history. Its reachability roles do not establish consciousness or personal identity. A biological, experiential or institutional application needs its own criteria. When a local model begins with an indexed center, that is a supplied analytical boundary whose wider formation remains relevant; it is not thereby a separate fundamental substance.

This yields two kinds of continuity to investigate. **Retained consequence** concerns an earlier effect still making a difference. **Regeneration** concerns activity reconstructing, replacing or renewing conditions needed for a specified continuation. A scar, an exhausted reserve and a working memory can all retain history; only their actual relations tell us whether they help regenerate anything. Retention, regeneration and beneficial continuation are distinct claims.

**持续要说明实际延续了什么，分化要说明关系怎样形成。部件相同不保证组织相同，整体相连也不抹平不同位置的真实损失。**

## 3. The connected explanation / 相连的解释

The following connections organize the account. They are conditional mechanisms, not an inevitable ladder every process ascends. Later capacities can affect earlier conditions: inquiry can redirect repair; a learned practice can change future training; justified concern can alter shared arrangements.

| Connection | What makes the transition explanatory | Failure retained in the account |
|---|---|---|
| Activity → operative conditions | An effect changes a relation through which subsequent activity occurs. | An inert product or unused report fails to establish the claimed influence. |
| Operative conditions → organization | A formation history establishes relations that jointly sustain an independently identified activity. | A nominal assembly may lack access, compatible parts or a required source. |
| Organization → operational significance | Disruption and restoration have different consequences for that organization's activity. | Something can support one organization while damaging another; organizational benefit is not yet ethical priority. |
| Consequence → regulation | The effect alters a response, and that response changes the relevant continuation conditions. | A dependency may exist without an effective, affordable or timely response. |
| Retained consequence → learning | Acquired effects change later response through an identified retained mechanism. | A stored label may never guide response; a used record may be stale or misleading. |
| Learning → revision of learning or selection | Consequences change the operative relation that forms, updates, routes or retains later responses. | An updated answer can leave its updater unchanged; a changed updater can learn worse. |
| Formed connections → new capabilities and joint possibilities | Changed organization enables independently specified effects or compatible co-realization previously unavailable under matched conditions. | A new capability can consume another; separate possibilities can compete for the same support. |
| Acquired understanding → concern and practical judgment | Another center's consequences acquire effective influence, and the resulting response answers warranted grounds under adequate evidence and authority. | Accurate understanding can be excluded from action; acquired motivation can oppose a still-valid reason. |

This sequence contains no analyst-supplied success number doing the causal work unnoticed. If a particular mechanism contains a reward, threshold, copying rule or evaluative connection, its installation and role must be stated. A score used afterward to assess an experiment is not automatically information available inside the process.

The connection between continuation and learning is especially important. An outcome can simultaneously change available support, supply a retained distinction and change the machinery's ability to respond again. An account that separates those into independent stories can lose the very relation 生生 asks us to explain. The next section reconstructs that relation in one actual model history.

## 4. One worked formation–learning history / 一段实际相连的形成与学习历史

The [prediction–action construction](PREDICTION-ACTION.md) is a synthetic, finite, resource-accounted example. It begins with two material units, the required A/H components absent, a damaged site, 24 common fuel units and 121 source-energy units. Formation, repair and capture make the earlier apparatus usable. Its adapter leaves 20 common units, twelve source packets of ten units each, and two recyclable backing slots. Forming an updater costs two common units, forming a selector costs one, and the predictive retrofit costs three. The first predictive circuit therefore starts with 14 common units, rather than a new unexplained endowment.

These reactions, backing capacities and retrofit functions are supplied local laws. The result derives actual construction and later operation under those laws; it does not derive a molecular implementation of a sensor or register from a two-slot description.

The formed circuit has a five-valued register `q`, initially `2`, and retains the previous action. Its current register determines which of two routes it tries. The action's actual result produces a receipt; that same result changes fuel and the lifetimes of the machinery. A subsequent supported write updates the register. Completion clears the temporary receipt while retaining the acquired register.

The [joint-episode result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CORE-TREE-CONTINUATION.md) reconstructs these as aspects of the same admitted sequence:

```text
formed machinery and retained register
                  ↓
             issued action
                  ↓
actual contact with the current route and source
                  ↓
        one actual outcome participates in
        receipt formation, fuel change and renewal/wear
                  ↓
        supported write of that receipt
                  ↓
   changed register, available support and usable machinery
                  ↓
              later activity
```

The diagram is a correspondence map of this implemented mechanism, not an additional theorem or a universal event schedule. Receipt and resource consequence are tied by the work law. Arbitrarily changing only the receipt while claiming to preserve that whole law would misdescribe the example. Outages and channel errors can be modeled, but require their actual changed conditions.

In the checked continuation, successful trials first produce `q4`. The productive route then reverses while the circuit's acquired register persists. Two failed trials and their writes bring the register to `q2`. The old updater expires; a newly formed Fast updater retains that circuit's register. A further failure receipt changes it to `q0`, which changes the next command. That supported command succeeds. The endpoint retains 18 common fuel units and two source packets.

The formal account preserves the original material and records all energy: **145 initial units = 4 preparation + 1 adapter + 3 machinery + 3 retrofit + 96 later costs/waste + 38 remaining**. These are units of this model, not a universal price of learning. The proved witness includes the intermediate payments; a later successful return cannot retroactively fund an unaffordable earlier stage.

Several distinctions now have concrete content:

- **Formation:** the circuit that responds was actually built in the preceding history.
- **Historical consequence:** its current register results from actual earlier action–outcome pairs.
- **Operational significance:** route changes affect capture, support and hardware continuation.
- **Learning:** a write changes the later command through the retained register.
- **Changed learning organization:** the replacement updater changes how subsequent receipts update that same register; its particular recipe is supplied.
- **Reflexive consequence:** responding and learning consume or replenish conditions for another episode.

The controls constrain the interpretation. Frozen-register comparisons retain charged sham writes; in the reported primary finite computation they lose the strict correction-and-use outcome. An inverted action route can acquire accurate forecasts of its own failures. A failed return with unknown source availability can be ambiguous. These show why retained information, correspondence, response routing and support must all be identified.

Validation has two scopes. [Lean checks](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/formal/README.md) cover the deterministic subprocess, actual joined witness, relevant bounds and accounts. The full stochastic program, rebuilding branches and intervention comparisons have separate independent computational checks. The full simulator is not claimed to be a proved refinement of the Lean subprocess. Neither is an observation of a natural organism or an ethical proof.

**此处生生的实质是：同一活动的后果进入记录、支持及组织的后续变化，并通过实际关系改变下一轮活动。形式定理验证了指定机制中的连接；具体规律、材料实现与伦理意义仍有各自的对应要求。**

## 5. How further formation becomes possible / 进一步形成怎样成为可能

The predictor illustrates one connected development. The broader account also needs to explain changing constructive capacity, shared possibility, method revision and evaluative formation. The existing studies supply the following connections at their stated scopes.

**Formation of new operative capacities.** In [New possibilities](NEW-POSSIBILITIES.md), binding two parts forms a constructor connection; later extension requires that connection. A matched expenditure leaves the same total material, work and existing chain length without enabling extension. The differing outcome therefore depends on organization, not merely a larger stock. A separate serial-contact interpretation derives a joint-input response unavailable to any response depending on either input alone. These are checked changes relative to a specified earlier repertoire. They do not show that physical laws are violated or that endless qualitative innovation follows. Extension can also consume the last loose part and remove further extension capacity.

**Joint realization and shared conditions.** The [generative-capacity study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-CAPACITY-INTEGRATION.md) includes a formed reusable connection that changes whether two constructions can both be completed with the same support. The formation cost is included. This explains a relational gain: what is possible together can change even when the separate desired products are unchanged. Shared formation, specialization, maintenance and lost alternatives must be assessed through the whole relevant history. A count of possibilities does not establish compatibility or ethical superiority.

**Consequences change how later knowledge is produced.** In [autonomous method formation](AUTONOMOUS-METHOD-FORMATION.md), acquired observations contradict every candidate produced through a first formed generator connection. That connection is replaced. The successful trial result is discarded; later generation must use the retained replacement to reconstruct the result. Breaking that connection blocks generation despite the historical acceptance label. This is actual use of a changed method, not only a changed answer. The two generator recipes, tests and assembly rules remain supplied. Its reviewed executable histories are a separate realization from the Lean predictor, with their own validation scope.

**Selection arrangements have formation histories.** [Generative selection](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) and the [evolutionary continuation](THE-THEORY-OF-EVOLUTION.md) investigate how reconstruction, inheritance and relative success change which arrangements persist and influence further selection. A useful activity need not preferentially reproduce its producer; a shared return can cancel from a relative comparison. Actual associations, conversion paths and continued support matter. These conditional results locate an endogenous causal route for selection over selectors, while retaining supplied reconstruction laws and the uncompleted stronger evolutionary objective. Moral favor is not inferred from differential reproduction.

**Another center's condition can acquire practical influence.** In [evaluative plasticity](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md), actual paired consequences form conducting links that were initially absent. In the selected history, the affected stock changes from 40 to 36 during the learning operations; the resulting active links enable an action raising it to 46. Matched inert or filtered routes preserve relevant facts and teaching bills but leave it at 36. A later matched replacement can suppress an affordable service while factual competence remains. Thus acquired influence is causally real and fallible. The numbers do not themselves identify welfare, legitimate responsibility or a warranted repair.

**Succession and warranted correction can preserve differentiated grounds.** [Evaluative succession](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) carries acquired influence into a separately supported child and connects fresh evidence to its later action. [Evaluative grounding](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) supplies a defended, conditional repair interpretation of the plasticity history's actual loss. The [practical application account](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) and its separate correction realization show how a defeated dependence can be withdrawn while an independent ground survives. Their contribution is bounded practical wisdom under stated premises; they do not establish a generally wise autonomous learner or automatically revise every ethical standard.

These examples explain different relations within the common proposal. Their exact composition matters. The [composition record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) distinguishes:

| Connection status | What is established |
|---|---|
| Joined history | An actual output, with its retained state and costs, is consumed by the demonstrated continuation. |
| Conditional transport or application | A result applies if the consumer's full input and interpretation conditions hold. This does not produce those inputs. |
| Separate realization | Another law or implementation demonstrates a complementary connection. No material handoff is asserted. |
| Unresolved advertised connection | A stronger specifically claimed continuation still lacks its actual input, use or meaning. |

One preserved case has positive recipient-correction probability but zero joint probability of that correction together with the earlier strict motor revision. Another pair uses opposite bit encodings for actor and recipient. Equal variable names, positive marginals and plausible narratives cannot join them. These limits constrain stronger composition claims without erasing the genuine mechanisms already established.

## 6. From 生生 to the mathematical core / 从生生到数学核心

The route is **interpretation → representation → specified laws and correspondence → checked consequences**. 生生 does not uniquely dictate one formal language. We selected a relation `step : State → Action → State → Prop` and finite traces because it expresses which modeled activities can follow which configurations and how their histories compose. The logical/type-theoretic background is explicit in the [mathematical-core document](MATHEMATICAL-CORE.md#the-mathematical-background-we-explicitly-use).

This interface can represent all the aspects above when its state, events and laws retain their relevant relations. Its generic path results establish sequence, feasibility, invariance and faithful abstraction under supplied premises. They do not identify what a state coordinate means, create a missing mechanism, or prove that every real process satisfies a selected law.

There is no contradiction between a fixed encompassing law and changing operative organization. An event can build or replace a connection represented in the state, and later enabled operations can depend on that connection. The model's law stays fixed while the organization through which activity proceeds changes. The supplied binding and operation laws still require justification in an application.

Likewise, novelty relative to a current working repertoire is different from novelty relative to every history admitted by the encompassing model. A new tool can become usable now through its actual construction. Representability before construction does not make the tool already operative. The [new-possibilities results](NEW-POSSIBILITIES.md#1-相对于什么而言是新--what-is-new-relative-to-what) state these distinctions and the limits on unbounded claims.

Calling this a mathematical representation of “one aspect” therefore requires precision: **the bare core establishes general relations between changes and their composition; substantive mechanisms give those changes their generative content**. The other aspects are also mathematically investigable. They are not placed outside mathematics merely because the first generic theorems do not derive them.

## 7. What correspondence requires / 对应需要什么

A model articulates this proposal when its mathematical distinctions preserve the relations needed for its explanatory claim. The following are application obligations, not additional entities in reality.

| Correspondence | What must be identified | A decisive failure |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration and activity | What the coordinates and events describe; boundary, resolution, timing and law assumptions. | Omitted history changes the claimed response; an apparent atomic step conceals an unavailable intermediate operation. |
| Operative condition | The actual relation through which an effect changes a specified continuation, including formation and later use. | Removing only a success label changes the report but leaves the alleged mechanism unaffected. |
| Capability | Independently specified effects, available means, coupled constraints and relevant horizon. | A possible endpoint is counted as an accessible response despite a missing route, permission or earlier support. |
| Information and understanding | How an event forms a distinction, what it identifies, what errors remain possible, and how it guides the declared inquiry or action. | Accurate dated evidence is used after its applicability changes; an answer is inserted by the observer. |
| Cross-model connection | Actual predecessor, consumer guards, encoding, retained records, resource accounts and relevant observation/action structure. | A handoff resets fuel, silently changes the target encoding or joins mutually exclusive branches. |
| Ethical application | Actual life and loss, the scope of the normative premise, justified comparative commitments, authority, responsibility and competing effects. | A stock increment is declared welfare or a permission flag declared legitimate without the needed argument. |

Countercomparisons must preserve the relevant rival's explanatory conditions. To test retained influence, compare appropriate matched histories or intervene on the operative route while accounting for side effects. Demanding identical complete states while expecting memory-dependent differences would remove the very retained difference at issue. Conversely, equal external reports do not establish identical internal information. A redundant route may preserve an effect when one connection is cut.

There is empirical contact with these questions. In an experimental *E. coli* lineage, a duplication formed a regulatory connection enabling aerobic expression of a citrate transporter; the published genetic and expression comparisons connect changed organization to a new ecological function. This supports a particular formation-to-capability relation, with history and culture conditions retained. It neither identifies the synthetic constructor with that genetic mechanism nor establishes unlimited innovation. [Blount and colleagues' primary study](https://www.blount-lab.org/uploads/2/3/0/3/23037222/blount_et_al_2012.pdf); [the scoped evidence review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NEW-POSSIBILITIES-REVIEW.md).

The broader [empirical ledger](EMPIRICAL-SCOPE.md) contains regulation, retained-record, beneficiary-related learning and correction comparisons, each with its alternatives and measurement limits. No single observed development from unformed organization to acquired, justified wisdom is claimed. The [knowledge account](KNOWLEDGE.md) likewise keeps true correspondence, competent knowing, explanatory understanding and skill distinct; its stronger formation and error-scope investigation remains open.

The proposal earns content through these identified dependencies, conditional constraints and discriminating comparisons. Conventional feedback, assembly or learning descriptions can reproduce a mechanism when given the same laws and information. Such equality limits claims of mathematical novelty or predictive superiority. It does not remove the usefulness of a connected explanation, and it must not be concealed by renaming the same mechanism.

## 8. 生生 as an ethical direction / 生生的伦理方向

The descriptive explanation makes the object of evaluation clearer: actual living, organization, differentiated experience, shared dependence and changing possibilities. The current ethical route states its normative starting point openly:

> **G0：整体中的生及其继续生成具有基本正面规范意义。**
>
> **Living and its continued generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance.**

This includes current life, self-care, rest, relationships and finite fulfillment, as well as future formation. Biological/ecological life, mental and relational life, and broader organization remain distinguishable ranges. “生” is not first redefined as whatever an independent theory has already judged good. Nor is every generative effect an overriding reason: genuine formation can accompany coercion, deprivation and loss.

G0 is the [authorized explicit axiom](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-alignment-grounding-axiom-correction.md). The [practical-judgment account](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md) adds disclosed commitments: full comparison preserving real losses (J1); a scoped priority for basic living conditions over optional enrichment (J2); bounded responsibilities arising through warranted undertakings and accountable dependencies (J3); and authorized, jointly feasible practical selection and conflict procedures (J4). These are not theorems of the bare G0 sentence.

The resulting orientation can support preservation, transformation, effective transfer or appropriate ending, depending on the actual history and justified comparison. A larger aggregate does not erase a differentiated loss. Restoration does not undo the fact that a loss occurred. Persistence of an institution is not automatically continuation of the lives it affects. Ending an exhausted or damaging form can participate in a better whole continuation; the actual comparison must establish that claim.

This also gives concern and wisdom their place in the account. **Concern involves responsiveness to affected life; its formation, epistemic adequacy and warranted scope must be explained. Wisdom involves warranted, situated and revisable judgment about those connected consequences, including the conditions for correcting that judgment.** Acquired motivation, justification, knowledge and implementation can support one another without being equivalent. The plasticity example establishes formed influence; its repair application adds defended premises. No ethical validity is inferred merely from association, survival or successful prediction.

Axiomatic reasoning takes place within the same reality as learning and action. Distinguishing a premise's warrant from a mechanism's causal operation does not divide facts and values into separate substances. It makes the argument answerable for both what occurs and why a practical conclusion follows.

Normative understanding itself can form and change through attention, memory, feeling, expression, mutual recognition and correction. The [current cognitive and informational investigation](ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md#9-规范如何成为有作用的结构--how-a-norm-becomes-an-operative-structure) treats a normative structure as an operative organization within those relations. Its wording can persist while its actual use changes, and its own reinforcement can coexist with losses to the lives it concerns. This is a developing account of formation and warrant, with a separately authorized stronger grounding objective; it does not turn a spreading narrative into a justified norm or cancel the explicit-axiom route used here.

## 9. What this articulation establishes / 本说明完成什么

The resulting account has a connected center: **activity can form the relations through which its consequences continue; those relations can sustain and transform organization, change what becomes jointly possible, and participate in further sensing, learning, selection and judgment.** Each claimed development is accountable to its actual mechanism and correspondence. The ethical orientation is explicit and its practical consequences retain their additional commitments.

This completes the requested articulation through mechanisms and correspondence. It includes a worked joined history, the wider causal and normative connections, constructive and destructive cases, the relation to the mathematical core, application requirements and a source-bound assessment. It reuses the [accepted conditional foundation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) rather than presenting earlier completed bridges as absent.

This is an explanatory synthesis, not a new omnibus theorem, empirical experiment or claim of historical priority. The inherited mathematics keeps its checked hypotheses and original validation scope; this document introduces no new proof claim. Universal empirical coverage, the origin of all physical laws, phenomenal experience, guaranteed favorable evolution, autonomous ethical discovery and a generally wise artificial intelligence are not established. They remain substantive questions wherever the research claims them, rather than being hidden in the word 生生.

The articulation can be revised substantively: a proposed formative relation might prove inert; an alleged learned influence might instead be preinstalled; a handoff might fail its contract; a normative application might lack authority or misdescribe the loss; a simpler account might preserve all explanatory content. Such findings change the relevant claim and its scope. A failed local model is first assessed against the relationship it actually represents, while a genuine counterexample to a stated general claim remains a counterexample.

**生生的完整表述，不是把一切变化重新命名，而是说明：哪些活动形成了哪些实际关系，这些关系怎样使后续活动成为可能、改变其组织，并怎样在共同展开中形成可理解、可检验、可修正的生活与实践。**
