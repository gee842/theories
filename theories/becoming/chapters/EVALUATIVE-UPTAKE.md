The named transition is **actual consequence acquisition → retained evaluative influence → selected, resource-dependent action for the same recipient**. The accepted [fresh-evidence construction](RECIPIENT-REASON-REVISION.md) already acquires a changed command relation, revises its instrumental reasons and pays for its use. This study asks what additional causal connection lets acquired content change whether the actor uses that knowledge for another center.

## The difference that the experiment must isolate

Two controllers can know the same recipient facts, receive the same private training and face the same costs, yet give that recipient different practical influence. To identify that difference, the principal comparison preserves recipient perception and changes the subsequent evaluative route. The earlier [architecture-learning comparison](ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) changed access to evidence itself; it cannot serve as this equal-information control.

The new register begins at zero. Three resource-dependent own-resource operations produce a retained estimate of operation shortfall. Each operation spends two units and can return two; a separate read and write cost one each. The learning datum excludes these later acquisition fees, while the whole-history account includes them. Thus a zero operation shortfall still permits net lesson expenditure.

The supplied finite update is

\[
n' = \lfloor n/2\rfloor+4Y,\qquad n\in\{0,\ldots,7\},\quad Y\in\{0,1\}.
\]

The shared route compares `10*n > 8*C`, where `C` is the actual specified recipient-trial charge, normally eight. The identity-filtered route has the same acquired `n` and recipient knowledge but excludes this influence from its choice. A conventional table supplies the same decision law independently. Missing or inverted measurement, disabled updating, a supplied constant valuation and a resource-dependent suppression route test distinct causal alternatives. These comparisons were fixed before the results reported below.

Neither `n` nor the packet quantity measures experienced harm or moral standing. The update, sign, scale and cross-center transfer are declared local recipes. Private self-calibration alone cannot select their uniquely correct extension to another center: positive, zero and negative recipient influence can agree on all the self examples.

## Causal influence and warranted application

The [ethical application contract](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) identifies what is additionally needed to interpret the same process as warranted concern: an independently supported patient-condition relation, a defended ground, legitimate authority, an adequate comparison of competing claims, and actual addressed fulfillment. A resource receipt is evidence about one physical consequence; it cannot silently supply all of those meanings.

This leaves a specific joint obligation. Acquired evidence must change recognition of a ground's applicability, the retained evaluation must make a difference to selection, and the selected event must actually address that ground under its declared authority and costs. An observer's favorable interpretation alone does not demonstrate this causal recognition. Equally, a failed or indifferent controller does not refute an independently justified patient claim.

The present study targets the acquired-influence part using the already formed factual core. The parallel application study supplies its precise normative and semantic interface. Any accepted interpretation will state which interface premises are established, independently defended, supplied or still unknown.

The accepted application argument permits bounded functional recognition of instrumental relevance under fixed, independently justified grounds. Here the parent contains two actual successful acquisition histories supporting opposite recipient commands with the same entry fuel and recipient stocks. The saved new continuations use the corresponding commands; these are reached histories rather than edited register counterfactuals. The unknown-fact control withholds an assessment despite acquired `n=7`. These tests address how acquired evidence directs the response. They do not show that the controller acquired the patient's standing, the benefit interpretation, legitimate authority or a new reason for the second offered opportunity.

The bounded application now has an explicit interface:

| Required input | What this joined result supplies | Remaining premise |
|---|---|---|
| Acquired instrumental understanding | Actual fresh acquisition, a retained supported command, blocked unknown/mismatched support and reached opposite-command histories | Calibration and stability through use |
| Practical evaluative influence | Resource-dependent acquisition from zero, equal-information filtered controls and an assessment that affects actual dispatch | The transfer recipe, sign and scale |
| Addressed effect | The current command directs a resource-dependent trace to the same recipient; no recipient receipt finances the actor | Evidence that this receipt serves the relevant patient condition |
| Warranted response | The accepted application argument transports a fixed warranted ground through sound, causally used means knowledge | Independently defended patient ground, legitimate authority and the complete relevant comparison |
| Continued responsiveness | Retained data supplies a second resource-dependent port and another actual action | A valid application for that later episode; full learner succession and continuing provision |

Under those independent application premises, the first reached action can instantiate the accepted bounded functional recognition of instrumental applicability. This closes a conditional semantic/causal join. It does not establish that the synthetic recipient has a demonstrated ethical need, or that a second packet has the same justification. Acquiring or revising the patient ground, the transfer's justified scope, and the priority of costly competing claims remain broader obligations.

## Contribution to the fixed completion conditions

The checked result removes an externally installed coefficient and prescribed recipient action at this concrete implementation boundary. It exposes conditions for acquired influence, exclusion, mistaken transfer and unaffordable use within one resource history. It does not prove that self-preservation selects inclusive concern, that every system learns, or that the whole foundation has been completed.

The next central connection is warranted evaluative correction and actual learner succession: preserve or reacquire the learned payload, its target meaning, applicable grounds and resources in a genuinely continuing lineage. The existing successor, semantic-action and renewal contracts should supply the parts they already establish; replacing the present port alone does not meet that interface. Its general claims must retain their law conditions and empirical identification requirements. The [fixed finish-line audit][finish] remains the acceptance standard; the number of further components is not a measure of completion.
