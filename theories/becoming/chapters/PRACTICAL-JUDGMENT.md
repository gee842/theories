## 1. 共同起点、附加承诺、对应与推论 / Premises and their roles

G0：**整体中的生及其继续生成具有基本正面规范意义。** 当下生活、休息、自护、有限完成和未来形成都属于这个起点；不以生产后继作为每段生命的资格。生物／生态、心智／生活关系、广义组织生成均保留，不预先折为一个数值。

G0 recognizes living and further generation within the whole as basically favorable. Current life, rest, self-care and finite fulfillment count without first serving later production. Biological/ecological, mental/relational and broader organizational ranges remain distinguishable. The following commitments make G0 practically more determinate; **they are proposed additional normative commitments, not logical consequences of the bare sentence G0 or newly attributed instructions from Ren.**

## 2. 什么能够构成第一次有损比较 / A substantive mixed comparison

J2 的根据是：基本生活和可继续形成条件不仅是更多产出的材料，也是在 G0 中已被肯定的实际实现。为了可放弃的增益而毁掉这些条件，会把其中一部分生固定为其他部分可无限消耗的条件。J2 拒绝这种优先安排，同时允许为恢复真实生活承受不跨越基本剥夺界限的损失。这个理由解释我们为什么采用它；不把不同意 J2 的对手说成违反逻辑。

J2 treats the conditions of living and further formation as realizations within G0, not merely consumable inputs to extra output. It rejects granting optional enrichment an unlimited claim over those conditions. This supports a restricted priority for restoration, while retaining losses that remain below the independently defended protection boundary. This is an argument for adopting J2, not a proof that every G0-compatible view must adopt it.

**“基本”须在比较之前说明。** 指具体生命得以持续、避免严重损伤、维持最低实际参与／自护能力或关键生态接续所需的条件；“这家组织想永远存在”“新增形式更多”不够。要说明谁受什么影响、持续多久、是否可恢复、会失去哪种生活，是否有替代及条件间的依存。严重告别损失、强制、排除参与、累积耗竭不能藏在“可放弃”一栏。数值阈值若用于实际研究，须另有校准；这里不提供普遍小时数、交换率或人数权重。

**Classify basic conditions before choosing the winner.** Identify threatened life, serious impairment, minimally effective participation/self-care, or a critical ecological continuation, together with intensity, duration, reversibility, alternatives and dependencies. Institutional persistence and added complexity do not qualify merely by being described as essential. Unique farewells, coercion, exclusion and cumulative exhaustion cannot be silently classified as optional. Operational thresholds require independent calibration; no universal exchange rate is supplied here.

比较完整接续中的两层内容：第一层列出被保住的基本要求，按实际受影响的位置及历史索引；第二层保留其他生活、丰富性和真实损失。A 若保住 B 的全部第一层要求并多保住至少一项，则在 J2 的适用范围内优于 B，哪怕 A 有已公开的第二层损失。第一层相同，再用 J1 的无减损改善。两边各损伤不同基本要求，或第二层各有不可包含的得失时，规则不制造总排序。对某项基本要求的“保住”包含事先确定的全部实质内容；若程度不同足以影响比较，应细分或重做描述，不能只保留一个通过位。

Compare two layers of the whole history: indexed basic claims protected, then other realizations, enrichments and losses. Within J2's scope, retaining every protection of B and restoring another favors A despite disclosed secondary losses. With equivalent protections, apply J1. Different sacrificed basic claims, or crossed secondary gains, can remain incomparable. A protection predicate must preserve its independently specified content; material degrees of impairment require a richer description. This is a sufficient partial ordering, not a complete scalar ranking.

**J2 的确切强度：有范围的词典序承诺。** 在上述独立分类真实成立的范围内，本候选采用基本保护相对第二层的严格优先：即使第二层损失数量很大，只要它们确实仍是第二层、全部其他基本保护不变，恢复额外基本条件仍胜出。单凭“不能无限牺牲”并不能推出这个更强结论；这里明确另行承担，不藏入“基本”的定义。它不是说第二层毫无价值：其损失仍构成减轻、分担及补救的理由，在第一层等效时决定比较；它是拒绝用这些实现的增加，购买另一处基本生活条件的丧失。

**Exact strength: a scoped lexical commitment.** Where the independent classification holds, this candidate gives basic protection strict priority over the secondary layer: restoration wins even against very many genuinely secondary losses, provided all other basic protections remain unchanged. Rejecting unlimited sacrifice alone does **not** entail this stronger claim. J2 explicitly adopts it instead of concealing it in the meaning of “basic.” Secondary losses still supply reasons for mitigation, sharing and repair and can determine comparisons at equivalent protection. The commitment denies that accumulation of such realization purchases the loss of another basic living condition.

正面辩护是：基本条件指某种生活继续展开所不可替代的地位，而第二层的增加仍在各处这种地位获保留的条件下发生；保住这层共同展开的资格，是本理论对“整体”所作的非替代性承诺。一个有力对手可以承认全部这些关系，仍认为数量足够大的、确实未越界的失落应当胜过一次基本恢复。本账户没有证明该对手自相矛盾；选择 J2 是在 G0 下明确的、可争论的伦理立场，付出的代价就是不作这种跨层补偿。不能把对手的损失事后全部重分类为基本损失来逃避它。若进一步判断支持该对手，应公开撤回或缩窄 J2，并重新检查依赖它的结论；J1、分工、交接、释放和有限候选的一般结果可分别保留。当前完成的是这个已公开承担上述代价的公理候选，不是所有 G0 理论的唯一必然答案。

The affirmative defense is non-substitution: the basic layer concerns an irreplaceable standing for a particular form of living to continue, while secondary expansion occurs with such standing retained elsewhere. Protecting that participation in the whole is an explicit interpretation of the shared direction. A strong rival can accept these relations and still favor sufficiently extensive, genuinely below-boundary losses over one basic restoration. We have not shown that rival inconsistent. J2 is a contestable ethical commitment under G0, with the stated cost of rejecting cross-layer compensation. We cannot evade the rival by reclassifying its losses after the fact. If further judgment favors it, retract or narrow J2 and reassess dependent verdicts; the separate J1, allocation, handoff, discharge and general finite-menu results can survive. The completed candidate openly bears this commitment; it is not the unique necessary answer of every G0 theory.

**真正的混合锚点。** 一次可重排的普通研习与唯一及时修复相冲突。维修使已有生活与共同活动的基本条件实际恢复，代价是失去当次研习；完成活动却使那些条件丧失。若维修可行、负担有界、有权限、其他基本要求完整保住，则 J2 支持维修，J4 排除被它击败的方案。研习损失仍真实存在。若那次相聚是最后告别，维修永久伤害另一处生活，或设施保住却生活没有恢复，前提改变，不能沿用结论。这比旧 K 的“一项可反驳理由”更强，但强度来自公开 J2，非形式计算暗中升级。

**Mixed anchor.** A reschedulable ordinary meeting conflicts with the last timely repair opportunity. Repair actually restores basic conditions of living and shared activity but sacrifices that meeting; holding it loses those conditions. With feasible, authorized, bounded repair and no other basic deprivation, J2 favors repair and J4 excludes the defeated alternative. The missed meeting remains a loss. A final farewell, serious displaced harm, or an empty repaired facility changes the premises. The sufficient comparison is stronger than the earlier defeasible K because it explicitly adds J2.

**牺牲边界。** 固定的可放弃增益不能要求任意加重负担。一旦额外负担破坏了当事人的基本生活条件，原 J2 比较失效，并可被保住这些条件的替代方案击败。此处得到的是有内容的限制，不是从 G0 推出某个神奇数值。若两个方案都面临基本损失，就进入真正冲突程序；不能为规避界限，把每一项组织扩张改名为基本需要。有限而不跨界的损失仍须进入第二层比较和公平分担；“未越界”本身不是许可。

**Sacrifice boundary.** Fixed optional enrichment cannot demand arbitrary imposed deterioration. Once burdens undermine basic living conditions, the earlier priority argument fails and a protective alternative may defeat it. This is a substantive limit, not a numerical bound derived from G0. Competing basic losses require the conflict procedure below. Below-boundary losses still require comparison and fair allocation; being below a ceiling is not itself permission.

### 2.1 支承为何取得理由：在需要时共同接续 / Why support acquires a reason: continuing together when needed

2026-10-13，P95。AI 撰写的应用论证；复用 G0/J 及现有动力学证明，不新增公理、数学定理或独立成果。详见[原研究 §85](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ETHICAL-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md#timely-continuation-and-support)。

本节沿用已完成成果的 G0/J 条件接口。按[当前统一结构](../../../GOALS.md#theory-of-becoming)，G0 已折入意义：第一部分将其作为条件，第二部分的单一意义公理使其成立；本节不把历史上的 G0 公理表述重新加为第二条公理。/ This section reuses the completed G0/J conditional interface. In the current unified structure, G0 is folded into significance: Part I treats it conditionally and the single significance axiom in Part II yields it. The historical axiom wording is not reinstated as a second axiom.

先看同一展开：活动改变随后活动的条件，维持这些条件的活动本身也依赖其中的支持；新的支持可以形成，旧的支持可以失效。在这样的关系中才区分某段生活、它的所需、支持它的活动及能够改变这些关系的行动位置。所谓支承的意义，指它在这段实际共同形成中的作用；不是先给一个独立“资源所有者”分配价值，再从外面接到另一种生活。

Begin with activities changing the conditions of subsequent activity, including the support needed by the activities that maintain those conditions. New support can form and old support can fail. A life, its needs, its supporting activities and positions capable of changing them are differentiated within that unfolding. Support matters through this actual relation of formation, without first assigning separate values to independent owners.

**正面论证。** 生及继续生成在 G0 下有正面意义。实现它的活动若依赖某种共同支持，那么维持或形成有效支持，就是使这一实现成为实际的组成部分。因此，这段生活的正面理由也及于它的支承、修复或适当替代。这是 G0/J 路线中对具体实践理由的说明；因果关系给出理由所指向的实际联系，未单凭一个因果命题证明规范意义，更未推出每种手段均可采用。支承若同时损害其他生活，这些损害进入同一次判断。

**Affirmative argument.** G0 gives living and further generation positive significance. Where their realization depends on common support, maintaining or forming effective support participates in making that realization actual. The favorable reason therefore extends to support, repair or an adequate replacement. This explains a concrete practical reason within the G0/J account. Causation supplies its actual connection; a causal proposition alone establishes neither normative significance nor permission for every means. Damage caused by the supporting arrangement enters the same judgment.

理由所及的是**实际接续**，不能直接认同为某个旧条件、机构或角色的永久存续。若数条路线均能接续，须比较它们共同造成的后果；不必保存每条旧路线。若替代尚未形成，或者只能支持改变者而不能支持原来一起展开的生活，则“已有替代”的说明仍不充分。新增共同能力可以是更好的回应；其规模或集中程度本身不决定答案。

The reason concerns **actual continuation**, not the perpetual survival of a particular condition, institution or role. Where several routes can sustain it, compare their whole effects rather than preserve every old route. A replacement that is not yet available, or supports only the changing formation while abandoning the living relations previously supported together, does not establish adequate succession. Forming stronger common capacities can be the better response; size or concentration alone settles nothing.

应用时须依次说明以下内容。这是对“保住”“恢复”“替代”的对应要求，不是一张给方案加分的清单。

For application, establish the following content. These are correspondence requirements for preservation, recovery and replacement, not a scoring checklist.

| 内容 / Content | 必须说明什么 / What must be established |
|---|---|
| 被接续的具体生活 / What continues | 实际生活、休息、自护、形成或有限完成，以及变化前后保留、改变和失去的内容；不以某种组织继续存在替代它。 / Actual living, rest, self-care, formation or finite fulfillment, with what is retained, changed and lost; organizational survival is not a substitute. |
| 需要的时段 / When support is needed | 需要何时发生、能够间断多久、是否有真实储备；期限来自这些关系的实际变化，不能为选定赢家任意指定。 / When need arises, tolerable interruptions and actual reserves; timing comes from the changing relations, not a deadline selected to choose a winner. |
| 过渡中的接续 / Continuation through transition | 到达恢复时刻之前如何得到支持；允许无需求的空档，但不能用后来恢复删除期间的生活损失。 / How support persists until recovery; a gap without demand can be harmless, while later recovery does not erase loss during actual need. |
| 共同实现与替代 / Joint realization and replacement | 相关生活能否一起接续，替代依靠什么，是否把中断移到别处；各自有一条可能路线不足以证明共同可行。 / Whether relevant living relations can continue together, what supports the replacement and whether interruption is displaced elsewhere; separate possible routes do not establish joint feasibility. |

现有证明直接支持这些区别。[回程等级](../results/records/becoming-return-grades.md)给出总会恢复、却不存在统一恢复周期的展开；它不证明每次恢复都迟于实际需要，所以应用仍须说明相应需要与时段。[替代中的消耗](../results/records/substitution-preserves-consumption.md)给出改变依存后焦点形成持续活跃、维持者却永久失去支持的展开；它不证明全部替代均不好，而是排除“我能继续，所以共同条件已被保住”的捷径。这两个例子均保留其原模型范围，不是一个完整转型方案的联合证明。

Existing checks support these distinctions. [Return grades](../results/records/becoming-return-grades.md) supplies a course that always recovers without any uniform recovery period; it does not show that each recovery misses an actual need, whose timing must still be established. [Consumption under substitution](../results/records/substitution-preserves-consumption.md) supplies a course where the focal formation remains active after changing its dependence while the sustainer permanently loses support. It excludes certifying common preservation by focal persistence, not all substitution. The two witnesses retain their original model scopes and are not a joint proof of a complete transition.

**怎样产生比较。** 若一条路线真实保留另一条路线的相关生活，并增加继续形成的能力，且全部相关过渡损失已进入比较、没有抵消损失，可用 J1。若修复保住额外基本生活条件，保留其他全部基本要求，却牺牲了真正可放弃的丰富性，则按已公开的 J2 作有损比较。若两条路线各失去不同基本生活，支承的依存关系本身仍未提供交换率；须继续实质比较。需要满足的时点可以使“稍后同样恢复”的说法失效，但迫近本身不赋予一项生活压倒其他生活的地位。

**How comparison follows.** J1 applies where a route retains the other route's relevant living realizations and adds capacity for further formation, with all relevant transition losses included and no countervailing loss. J2 applies where repair protects an additional basic living condition, retains all other basic protections and sacrifices only genuinely optional enrichment. Where each route loses different basic living relations, dependence alone still supplies no exchange rate. An actual need can defeat a claim that later restoration is equivalent; urgency alone does not establish overriding priority.

据此，对齐方向可以更具体地表述为：**在整体中支持生及继续生成，形成、维持和改善使生活能够共同延续、修复、转化与合宜完成的实际关系；以这些关系中真实发生的所得与损失修订目标和行动。** 这同时支持创造新能力与改变旧形式。它不要求当下生活先靠未来产出取得资格；无后继生产的休息和有限完成也在 G0 的正面范围内。它也不把同一段生活经由多个支承关系重复记成多份价值。

The alignment direction can therefore be stated concretely: **support living and further generation within the whole by forming, sustaining and improving the actual relations through which lives can continue together, recover, transform and reach fitting completion; revise ends and actions through their real gains and losses.** This supports creating capacities and changing inherited forms. Present life need not earn its standing through future production: rest and finite fulfillment remain within G0. Nor should one living realization be counted repeatedly merely because several supporting relations contribute to it.

## 3. 四种回应及其转变条件 / Four responses

| 回应 / Response | 有根据的条件 / Grounds | 改变判断的条件 / What changes the judgment |
|---|---|---|
| 保留 / Preserve | 既有关系实际支持生活；改变没有更充分根据，或会造成可避免损失 / Existing relations sustain living; alteration lacks sufficient advantage or causes avoidable loss | 失效、支配性封闭、不可持续负担或更好的真实替代 / Failure, domination, unsustainable burdens or a better real alternative |
| 转化 / Transform | 原方式失效或损害共同条件；可行的改变修复生活，或在同等保护下改善实现 / The arrangement fails or damages shared conditions; feasible change restores living or improves realization with equivalent protections | 只是提高产出，转嫁剥夺，恢复不成立，或有损失更小的方案 / Mere output growth, displaced deprivation, failed restoration or a less damaging alternative |
| 交接 / Transfer | 需求仍正当，原参与可以退出；后继获正当接受、具备能力、信息与支持，并按实际需求及时承接 / Need remains warranted, exit is justified, and an accepted successor has capacity, information and support in time for demand | 名义交接、无人接管、过载、对后继强加负担或关键时段失接 / Nominal succession, vacancy, overload, imposed service or a gap during actual need |
| 结束 / End | 活动已完成、得到正当释放、目标不再有根据，或继续只会施加不正当负担；残余损失与依存已获适当回应 / Fulfillment, justified release, loss of the target's warrant or unjustifiable continuation burdens, with appropriate response to residual losses and dependence | 仅换名字、删记录、改变偏好、隐瞒仍有效承担 / Mere renaming, deleted records, changed preference or hidden outstanding undertakings |

四种回应可组合：保留生活而转化机构，经交接结束旧角色；或没有接续生产的有限完成。**生生不要求每个组织存续，也不要求每段好生活产生后继。** 接续有必要条件只说明怎样实现已成立的要求，不证明该要求永远成立。没有任何可实现的继续时，实际责任转为诚实说明、减轻承受、合宜收尾及仍可行的补救；不能命令不可能的救援，也不能假装未救成就是完成救援。

Responses can combine: preserve living through institutional transformation and transfer that ends an old role, or permit finite fulfillment with no productive successor. Generativity requires neither every organization to persist nor every good life to produce more life. Necessary conditions explain an already warranted continuation; they do not make it perpetual. When continuation is impossible, feasible responsibilities concern honest acknowledgment, relief, appropriate closure and remaining repair. Failure to save is not fulfillment of saving.

无需求空档可以容许先撤再接；有实际需求而无人支持则不能称为完整交接。实际交接终止的是原来那项履行责任，不自动抹掉早前造成的损失、欺骗或修复责任。许可退出也不等于受影响的生活已经接续；二者分别检查。

A no-demand gap may permit withdrawal before replacement; unsupported actual demand defeats a claim of complete transfer. Successful transfer can discharge an old service duty while leaving responsibility for earlier harm intact. Permission to exit and successful continuation are distinct judgments.

## 4. 谁承担什么 / Allocating responsibilities

J3 的正面理由是：共同生成依赖可靠协调，同时协调者自身也属于所保护的生。邀请合理依赖、封闭替代或承担某项职责，会改变其他活动的实际可行路径。让这些改变后的负担自动落在受影响者身上，会只计算发起位置的生成而删除同一关系中的后果。但所有关联都产生无限偿付，则又删除了承担者自身及其他关系。因此责任须指向具体恢复、履行、协调或交接，有容量与结束条件。

J3 is grounded in reliable coordination and the inclusion of contributors' own lives. Inviting justified reliance, displacing alternatives or undertaking a role changes others' actual possibilities. Automatically externalizing the resulting burden omits those consequences from the same generative relation. Unlimited liability for every causal connection would instead erase contributors and their other relations. Responsibilities therefore specify fulfillment, repair, coordination or transition, with capacity limits and discharge conditions.

| 关系根据 / Relational ground | 初步责任 / Presumptive responsibility | 范围 / Limit |
|---|---|---|
| 正当明确承担、共同安排 / Justified undertaking or shared arrangement | 依范围履行，及时通知变化，组织适当交接 / Fulfill within scope, give timely notice and arrange appropriate succession | 无效承诺不被依赖追认为正当；履行、释放或正当重议可终止 / Reliance does not validate an unjust undertaking; fulfillment, release or justified renegotiation can end it |
| 可归责地制造损害、依存或撤掉替代 / Accountable creation of harm/dependence or removal of alternatives | 停止加重，修复或支持过渡；说明所作改变 / Stop aggravation, repair or support transition, account for the change | 因果贡献并非自动过错；受操纵的依赖不授权继续控制 / Causation is not automatically fault; manufactured dependency does not authorize continued control |
| 控制关键共同条件 / Control of critical shared conditions | 不任意阻断，维护或开放合理替代，说明限制 / Avoid arbitrary obstruction, maintain conditions or enable justified alternatives, explain restrictions | 控制能力不自授统治权，正式职位也不保证实际能力 / Control gives no self-authorizing sovereignty; title does not ensure capacity |
| 及时、有效、有界的应急能力 / Timely, effective, bounded emergency capacity | 在严重损害可防、无相当反向损害且无及时替代时提供援助或联络 / Assist or summon support when serious harm is preventable without comparable harm and no timely alternative exists | 这是 J3 的救助承诺，不由“有能力”单独推出；不要求无限牺牲 / An explicit J3 rescue commitment, not capability alone or unlimited sacrifice |
| 获益且能参与共同支持 / Benefit with capacity for shared provision | 按正当共同安排分担维护、补偿或制度重建 / Contribute to maintenance, repair or institutional reconstruction under a justified shared arrangement | 获益本身不产生任意债额；不能强加未获认可的礼物来制造债 / Benefit alone sets no debt amount; unsolicited benefits cannot manufacture arbitrary debt |
| 直接受影响、拥有相关认识 / Affected participation and relevant knowledge | 具有被听取、质疑及参与相关决定的地位 / Standing to be heard, challenge and participate in relevant decisions | 不是必须公开全部私人生活，也非对所有他者的无限否决 / No duty of total disclosure or unlimited veto over others |

先确定须完成的共同任务，再检验**同一分工方案**能否同时完成：时间、支持、技能、既有任务、自护和实际同意不能逐项分别可行后直接相加。原承担者有优先履行／协调责任；可归责的制造者有优先补救责任；其他有能力者在急迫缺口下可负暂时援助责任，但不因此继承永久服务。共同资源管理者负责组织公平分担，不能把协调失败永久转嫁给最可靠的参与者。急救与之后补偿分开处理，不能等过错调查完才回应可防的严重损害。

Identify the collective task, then test one jointly feasible allocation: time, support, skills, existing duties, self-care and actual consent. Separate per-task feasibility is insufficient. Undertakers have presumptive fulfillment/coordination responsibilities; accountable contributors have presumptive repair responsibilities; capable others may have temporary emergency responsibilities without inheriting perpetual service. Those managing shared provision must organize fair burdens. Immediate response need not await a final account of fault; later repair and redistribution remain due where justified.

**“任务必须有人做”不推出“每个人都必须做”。** 多个合格分工保留替代；没有任何正当方案可避开某人的贡献时，才在这些前提下形成该人的特定要求。经过有权限、可执行、公平的分工选择，参与者须履行被正当分配的任务，或及时提出无法承担并进入调整；不能仅凭“不唯一必要”逃避已成立承担。若所有方案都要求过载，应修订目标、期限、支持或分工，承认无法全满足，不能用空方案集推出人人无限义务。

**A collective task need not bind every individual.** Alternative legitimate allocations preserve latitude. A particular contribution is indispensable only when every admissible allocation requires it. Once an authorized, feasible and fair allocation is settled, its participants must fulfill assigned undertakings or promptly seek justified revision; prior alternatives do not erase a settled commitment. If all allocations overload someone, revise goals, timing, support or allocation and acknowledge unmet claims. An empty plan set cannot generate unlimited duties by vacuous logic.

## 5. 真实冲突、有限认识与及时决定 / Conflict and uncertainty

完整一般原则必须说明未被比较规则唯一决定时怎样实践。不可比不等于同样好，更不等于任何选择都获许可。J4 增加下列有范围程序承诺；它们与强制权限不是同一件事。

A general account must address cases where comparison does not yield a unique answer. Incomparability is neither equality nor unrestricted permission. J4 adds the following procedural commitments; it does not supply unrestricted coercive authority.

1. **组成完整可行方案。** 包括维持、转化、交接、结束、分阶段、协作和及时调查；无行动与延迟也有后果。说明时间窗口及未能覆盖的范围。/ **Form whole feasible plans**, including combinations and timely inquiry. Inaction and delay have consequences; state the decision window and omitted scope.
2. **先独立查明约束。** 权限、非任意强加、基本保护、自护与总体负担进入资格检验。若无法全满足，不把这些要求默默删除：明确冲突、紧迫性及可降低的违背，寻求增援和重排；紧急权限只及必要范围。/ **Establish constraints independently.** Include authority, protection, self-care and cumulative burden. If jointly impossible, acknowledge conflicts, urgency and reducible infringements; seek support or rescheduling. Emergency authority remains limited to necessary scope.
3. **排除充分被击败方案。** 在固定描述中采用 J1/J2，保留尚未排序的要求。若两种方案牺牲不同生命基本条件，仅数人数、复杂度或未来产量不足以决定。严重度、迫近性、可防性与既有承担可提供进一步公开论证，但不能用标签预定赢家。/ **Exclude sufficiently defeated plans.** Use J1/J2 with fixed meanings. Distinct basic sacrifices are not resolved by counting output or complexity. Severity, urgency, preventability and undertakings may support further disclosed argument; they are not unexamined winner labels.
4. **未定且来得及：有目的调查。** 只调查能够及时改变决定的关键对应，计算调查自身的负担和延误。保留真实处境是校准条件，信念集合缩小不自证更可靠。/ **If unresolved and time permits, investigate decision-relevant uncertainty**, including the inquiry's costs and delays. A smaller belief set is not better if it excludes actuality.
5. **必须及时决定：使用已有正当授权或共同建立程序。** 给实际受影响者合理参与与质疑机会，公开理由、利益关系及未满足要求，优先选择可纠正且不把重大不可逆负担集中给无参与者的方案。若这些理由仍不能区分且机会可以公平分配，可用轮换或公开抽签；抽签不能把明显更重的损害说成等同。严重紧急情境下允许范围受限的救助判断，随后复核，不能以紧急为名建立永久控制。/ **When a decision is due, use existing legitimate authority or establish a shared procedure.** Allow affected participation and challenge, disclose reasons and conflicts, prefer corrigible action without concentrating grave irreversible burdens on excluded positions. Rotation or a public lottery is permissible for otherwise unresolved fairly allocable chances; it does not equate unequal harms. Urgent protective decisions require narrow scope and subsequent review, not permanent control.
6. **记录残余责任与重新打开条件。** 许可选择并不抹掉牺牲；明确谁监测、补救、承担下一轮支持，何种新事实需撤回决定。/ **Retain residual responsibilities and reopening conditions.** A permissible choice leaves real losses; specify monitoring, repair, further support and evidence that would reopen judgment.

这是有确定输出类型的原则：充分支持的优先、可经正当程序选择的多个方案、需要调查、需要救助性临时决定，或当前要求不可共同实现。它不保证对任意现实都有唯一答案。一个有限、非空的合格方案集在上述严格传递比较下至少有未被击败者，已经 Lean 检查；无限改善链可能没有最终最优，保留旧反例。程序资格及事实识别并未因此自动得到认证。

The account gives definite kinds of practical conclusion: sufficient priority, procedural latitude among alternatives, warranted inquiry, a provisional urgent decision, or acknowledged joint infeasibility. It does not promise a unique verdict in every real case. Lean verifies an undefeated alternative for any finite nonempty menu under the stated strict transitive comparison. Infinite improvement chains may lack a final optimum. Formal existence does not certify authorization or correspondence.

## 6. 目的变化、学习与结束责任 / Changed purposes, learning and discharge

目的形成和修订都是整体中的真实变化。纠正手段误认可以保留原目的；知情拒绝可能要求停止介入；旧目的不再成立可以撤回该目的的未来生产要求。但已经形成的正当依赖、承诺与实际损害不能被新偏好或自我改名直接删掉。另一方面，被迫的旧角色、无效承诺和不正当目标不因“曾经存在”而永远有效。

Ends and their revision form within the same history. Correcting means may preserve an end; informed refusal may warrant ending intervention; a superseded end may remove a future production requirement. New preferences or names do not erase justified reliance, undertakings or actual harm. Conversely, coercive roles, invalid promises and unjustified targets do not gain permanence from their past existence.

完成的条件是实际履行、有效交接、相关要求的正当释放／撤回或适当终结。释放必须由有地位的参与者或有根据的程序作出，不是模型随意设定 `released=true`。对已完成任务继续要求同样服务没有原根据；仍未补救的旧损失可以产生另一项责任。生活需求结束，不能被“为生生永远运行”覆盖；有限完成与终末安宁可以自身实现 G0 的生。

Discharge requires actual fulfillment, effective transfer, justified release/revision or appropriate closure. Release needs standing and warranted procedure; it is not an arbitrary flag. Fulfilled service no longer has the same outstanding ground, while earlier unrepaired harm can support a distinct responsibility. Ending demand cannot be overridden by an injunction to keep producing forever; finite fulfillment and terminal peace can themselves realize living within G0.

忠实重述若保持所有合格方案、任务含义及实际关系，已成立要求保持；新认识真实改变可行性、依存、许可或目标时，必须重新判断。Lean 检查前者，不保证任意学习或自我修改忠实，也不把行为服从、动机形成与规范有效等同。对不同智能适用的是同一带前提的推理；实际责任随其形成的位置和能力而异。

Faithful redescription preserves requirements when admissible alternatives, task meanings and relevant relations are preserved. Actual changes in feasibility, dependence, permission or ends require reassessment. Lean checks the former conditional result, not the fidelity of arbitrary learning or self-modification. Normative validity, motivation and compliance remain distinct across intelligences.

## 7. 有内容的反对及边界 / Substantive rivals and limits

**接续无限优先的对手。** 它可以完整承认当前损失，却认为持续自我更新值得一切有限牺牲。G0 单独未排除它。本账户明确以 J2 反对：可放弃的更新不得凭时间无限压过基本生活；发生在未来不是额外的生命内容。若它主张真正基本的未来损失，须在基本要求层比较，不能直接称为可放弃，也不因遥远而删除。

**Unlimited continuation priority.** A rival can acknowledge present losses while giving recurrence priority over every finite sacrifice. G0 alone does not refute it. J2 explicitly rejects that priority for optional continuation; future basic losses must instead be included among basic claims and not erased because they are distant.

**固定阈值的对手。** 基本生活不是到某个点突然有价值；阈值可能僵化并被操纵。本账户承认连续差别与边缘不确定，要求完整记录、提前分类、公开修订；无法证成分类时不能使用 J2 强结论。J1 与有界程序仍可用。这个限制是原则的适用条件，不是任意把反例重新分类的自由。

**Rigid thresholds.** Living differences do not become valuable only at a cutoff. Thresholds can be manipulated. Preserve continuous differences and uncertainty, classify independently and allow challenge. An unsupported classification cannot authorize J2's strong verdict. This is an application limit, not a license to reclassify counterexamples until a favored answer wins.

**有产出的支配。** 强制安排确能生成复杂组织；不要把它改称“不是生”。本理论承认该正面方面，却以 J2/J3/J4 另行评估剥夺、非任意参与及权限。强制可有正面方面，并不自授实施权。确有范围受限的紧急保护或承担执行时，须说明具体根据，不能用“控制”一词先验否决。

**Productive domination.** Coercion can genuinely create organization; those gains are not redefined out of 生. J2–J4 separately assess deprivation, participation and authority. Favorable effects do not confer unlimited permission; limited protective intervention requires its own grounds rather than categorical approval or rejection of “control.”

**无任何替代的照护。** 此时依存仍真实，承担者也是真实生命。结论是公开冲突、动员共同支持、调整范围并保留实际损失；不是照护者无限服务，也不是拒绝一句便使所有责任消失。基本需求都无法满足时，原则能约束程序、负担和补救，却不虚构无损结局。

**Irreplaceable care.** Dependence and the contributor's life both remain real. Acknowledge conflict, seek shared support, revise scope and retain losses. Neither unlimited service nor automatic discharge follows. Principles constrain process, burden and repair without inventing a lossless outcome.

若实际 J2 应用反复以可争议的“基本”分类遮蔽更严重损失，应修订该比较；若公平程序持续把负担集中给无话语位置，应撤回其正当性。被证明仅有局部产出而无所声称生活恢复的实例，推翻对应 E。承认这些修订条件，不把当前形式化宣布为唯一伦理语言。

Repeated concealment of more serious losses under a “basic” classification defeats that application and can require revision of the principle. Persistent exclusion or burden concentration defeats procedural legitimacy. Output without the claimed restored living defeats correspondence. No exclusive necessity of this vocabulary is asserted.

## 8. 完成范围 / Completion scope

本章完成的是**明确公理下的一般实践判断候选**：第一次有损比较的充分规则、有内容的牺牲限制、四种回应、差异化责任、共同分工、真实交接、合法终结、冲突程序及认识／自我修改范围。它保留不可比的实质选择；普遍总排序、从 G0 单独推出 J2–J4、任意阈值的正确性、现实全面实施及无公理的第一根据均没有被伪称为本次结果。

The completed candidate supplies a sufficient mixed comparison, substantive sacrifice limits, the four responses, differentiated responsibilities, joint allocation, actual succession, legitimate discharge, conflict procedure and epistemic/revision scope **under explicit axioms**. It retains genuine incomparability. It does not claim a universal total ranking, derivation of J2–J4 from G0 alone, correctness of arbitrary thresholds, universal implementation or assumption-free grounding. The integration and independent review record the evidence supporting this scoped completion.
