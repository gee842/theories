## 1. 要建立的内容 / What must be established

实际判断形成于同一生成展开：活动形成可回应的位置、理解及受影响的生活，判断又改变它们的后续条件。这个完整关系必须保留。零公理路线所需的正面结果是解释为什么这些关系自身具有实践上的支持／反对力度，并给出竞争需求的比较、保留、转化、交接、结束、责任及其范围。仅解释判断怎样形成或后来怎样被保留不足以完成它。

Judgment forms within the same unfolding as capacities, understanding and affected living, and participates in changing their further conditions. The required result must explain why these actual relations have practical force and how that force differentiates comparison, preservation, transformation, transfer, ending and responsibilities. Formation of a judgment and preservation of its effects do not by themselves establish its warrant.

零公理不等于没有任何句子或区分；但一个句子若承担规范结论的根据，就不能以“定义”“构成”“合格”“正当”之名免于说明。逻辑与证明框架、所指范围和事实对应仍保留其地位；本次不擅自宣布它们获得了零假设豁免。我们也不能仅从表征分成两类，就宣布真实世界分成事实域与价值域。

Zero-axiom grounding cannot be achieved by placing a normative premise inside a definition of adequacy, constitution or legitimacy. Formal background, domain and actual correspondence remain visible dependencies. Neither separate representations nor a failed local entailment establishes a fundamental fact/value dualism.

## 2. 肯定路线：实践判断是否已经在承担其根据 / Affirmative route

当前最值得直接检验的路线是：**判断不是外在观察，而是形成行动的活动；有根据的判断须能够回应自己的内容、后果及其所依赖的生活关系，因此实践的规范性可能内在于这一活动。** 这个路线比“我想某件事，所以它好”更强；它试图解释判断作为判断的成立条件。

The affirmative route treats judging as an activity forming action. A warranted judgment must answer to its content, consequences and relations of dependence; practical normativity may therefore be internal to this activity. This seeks constitutive warrant, rather than treating a desire as its own ethical validation.

The question here is whether the first identification has been established, not whether it needs to be authorized twice.

## 3. J2 的具体新检验 / New test of J2

保留同一关系描述：一项基本生活条件是否保住，以及明确限定范围内的次级实现数量。后者只是这一数学检验的同类计数，不是普遍幸福量或把生命折价。词典序规则始终优先保住基本条件；另一规则给该条件一个有限正权重，同时也肯定每项次级实现。两个规则均能忠实记录发生了什么、在同等其他条件下肯定两类增益，并保持比较传递。

The restricted test keeps one history description: one protection status and a count of specified secondary realizations. The count is not universal welfare. One rule gives protection lexical priority; another gives it a positive finite weight while favoring each secondary realization. Both retain the described outcomes and can be transitive and positive in each coordinate. The two rules are openly specified rival interpretations, not evidence that either is ethically correct.

**新实查结果：**对任意有限样本，可构造一个有限正权重，使两规则在样本内所有成对比较上完全一致，却在一个未观察的更大次级实现比较上反向。样本次级计数上限为 M 时，权重 M+1 即可；未观察案例的次级计数 M+2 足以暴露不同。任一固定有限权重都无法与词典序在整个给定域一致。量词不能交换：每个有限样本各有匹配规则，不等于存在一个有限权重全域匹配。

**New checked result:** every finite sample admits a positive finite weight agreeing with the lexical rule on every pair in that sample, yet reversing a comparison outside it. If the maximum secondary count is M, weight M+1 agrees on the bounded domain; a case with M+2 secondary realizations exposes the disagreement. No fixed finite weight agrees globally. The quantifier order matters.

这个结果让零公理路线更加精确：有限的正面案例、排序一致和完整记录还不能把 J2 的无限强度变成已识别事实。它不证明 J2 错误，也不证明任何经验都无关，更不反驳尚未被该简化模型完整表达的关系理论。若真实关系本身排除有限补偿，应说明究竟哪一构成关系排除了它；不能只说样本都支持保护，也不能在见到反例后任意把次级损失改成基本损失。

Thus finite agreement, coherent ordering and retained descriptions do not identify J2's unrestricted priority. This does not disprove J2, make empirical evidence irrelevant or refute a fuller relational constitution omitted from the model. A successful derivation must identify the substantive relation excluding finite compensation. It cannot simply appeal to favorable samples or retrospectively reclassify the rival's secondary losses.

## 4. 责任的具体新检验 / Responsibility and symmetry

两个由同一协作形成的位置若在相关依存、能力、承担、控制和负担方面完全对称，仅凭共同任务与可行性不能无任意差别地唯一选中其中一个。形式结果表明：互换角色后仍不变的分配不能同时恰好指定其中一个。具体承诺、先前轮值、某种实际不对称，或有根据的抽签／共同选择可以打破对称；每一种的事实及正当性仍须说明。

In a history with exactly symmetric relevant dependence, capacity, undertakings, control and burdens, task need and feasibility do not uniquely distinguish one position. A swap-invariant assignment cannot select exactly one of two roles. Actual undertakings, earlier rotations, another relevant asymmetry or a warranted selection procedure can differentiate the result. Their existence and normative force remain to be established.

这不否定关系性责任，反而精确说明它应由哪种差异支撑。它也不要求普遍伦理必须给每个人不同命令；“二者之一承担”本身可以是合适的共同要求。问题仍在于共同要求及决定程序为何具有规范力度，而不是制造一个与真实位置无关的唯一名字。

This identifies what could ground differentiated responsibility without denying relational ethics. A collective requirement that one of the two contribute may be appropriate; a universal account need not name a unique individual. The open question concerns the warrant of that requirement and of the deciding procedure.

## 5. 反审与目前剩余连接 / Counterassessment and remaining bridge

**Substantive rival:** it accepts shared relations, losses, fallibility and participation, while denying that judging already entails G0, J2 or the full responsibility system. It cannot be dismissed as dualist merely for disputing that entailment. Its alternative ethical account also needs warrant. Neither side wins solely because the other has an unresolved explanation.

**下一中心连接：**在没有把善、正当或基本保护写入“判断”的定义时，说明实际的判断／承担关系是否已经构成某种可被辨明的实践支持力；再检验其范围能否到达不依赖愿望的责任与有损比较。本轮没有得到这一步的充分证明。14项新 Lean 定理／21项审计检查的是识别与分配限制，不是零公理伦理。首次研究轮已有实质新检验；不能据此宣布目标完成或普遍不可能，也不重开先前已验收的有条件成果。

**Next central bridge:** without defining judgment through goodness, legitimacy or basic protection, determine whether actual judging/undertaking already constitutes identifiable practical force, and whether its scope reaches desire-independent responsibility and mixed comparison. This round has not proved that step. Fourteen new Lean theorems and 21 audits establish identification/allocation limits, not zero-axiom ethics. 以下是 AI 重构：一个要求可能通过注意、预期、情感、记忆、相互纠正及行动组织而发挥作用；话语和文本可以参与它的保留与转变。神经、认知和交往层面的具体对应仍需查明，不能用“一个规范就是一组神经元”替代研究，也不把信息另设为脱离实现关系的实体。此处与既有[“像亦象”的认知研究](COGNITION-LI-AND-RECIPROCITY.md#1-认知理论与理不是同一层的三个名称)相接：表征本身也发生在现实中。

The AI reconstruction investigates how a demand operates through attention, anticipation, affect, memory, mutual correction and organized action, with speech and records participating in retention and transformation. The neural, cognitive and communicative correspondences require investigation; no specific neural mechanism is claimed here. Information is not introduced as a separate substance. This continues the existing account in which representations themselves occur within reality.

“模因”的比喻突出传播和留存；当前目标还包括未被说出的辨别、一次性的判断、相互承担，以及判断实际改变的生活关系。因此不能让可复制单位预先决定研究边界。也不能因为某种生命没有表述一个要求，就把它受到的影响排除在判断之外。

The meme analogy highlights transmission and retention. The proposed target also includes tacit discrimination, a judgment made once, mutual undertaking, and the living relationships changed by judgment. Replicable units therefore cannot fix its boundaries in advance. The absence of an articulated demand does not remove an affected life from the relations being investigated.

为进行检验，可以在同一过程中区分：一种要求怎样形成；怎样使人感到应当如此并组织行动；怎样影响相互承认、争议与纠正；怎样改变其所涉及的生活与后续可能。这些是关系的不同侧面，不是事实与价值的两种独立世界。其正当性也应在这些实际关系及其充分说明中研究，不再把它措辞成等待过程之外赋予的某种力度。与此同时，“它有作用”仍未穷尽“它所声称的理由成立”。

Within this one process we can investigate formation, felt demand and action, mutual recognition and correction, and effects on living and further possibilities. These are aspects of connected relations, not separate factual and evaluative worlds. Warrant must likewise be investigated through those actual relations and their adequate explanation, rather than phrased as force awaiting an external source. A demand's efficacy does not exhaust what its claim to warrant concerns.

**退化提供具体研究切口。** 一个叙事可能通过把所有反证重新解释为自身正确来强化留存，同时削弱参与者辨错、回应彼此和改变共同安排的能力。这是概念性案例，不是神经科学实验。此时某种信息组织的扩展与更广关系的退化可以并存；不可因其有害便事后说它“不是真正的生成”，也不能因其流行便说它正当。反过来，纠正可能削弱一个叙事的传播，却恢复这些关系。说明“退化”必须指明哪种能力／关系实际丧失，而非仅表示我们不赞同。

**Degeneration supplies a concrete investigation.** A narrative might reinforce its retention by interpreting every challenge as confirmation while impairing participants' capacities to detect error, respond to each other and change shared arrangements. This is a conceptual case, not a neural finding. Expansion of one informational organization can coexist with degeneration of wider relations. Correction might reduce the narrative's transmission while restoring those relations. Calling something degenerative must identify an actual loss of capacity or relationship rather than merely express disagreement.

下一检验据此改为：具体说明一个判断结构如何形成、维持、修正或封闭其与所涉及生活的关系，再检验这种完整关系能否说明其实践正当性及范围。不能只证明传播成功，也不能预先把正当判断定义成会促进生生的判断。本次是理论对象与解释方向的修正；尚无新数学定理、经验验证或零公理完成声明。

The revised test specifies how a judgment structure forms, sustains, corrects or closes its relations with the lives it concerns, then tests whether that complete relationship explains its practical warrant and scope. Transmission alone is insufficient, and warranted judgment must not be defined in advance as whatever promotes generativity. This revises the explanatory target; it introduces no new mathematical theorem, empirical verification or completed zero-axiom grounding.

## 9. 规范如何成为有作用的结构 / How a norm becomes an operative structure

**经 Ren 纠正的工作候选：在所研究的认知与信息结构中，规范结构组织关于何为正确、合宜、有价值、被允许或被要求的标准与区分。** 它可以涉及信念、感受、解释、关系或行动，不必产生行动、动机或一个正在生效的命令。词语可以表达并重构它，但不穷尽它；已被表达或实行的标准也不因此得到正当性证明。此前以“处境成为行动要求”定义全部规范结构的表述过窄，现明确撤回，保留为下述行动相关案例的局部说明。

**Revised working candidate following Ren’s correction: within the cognitive and informational structures under investigation, a normative structure organizes standards and distinctions concerning correctness, fittingness, value, permission or requirement.** Its object can be belief, feeling, interpretation, relationship or action. Producing action, motivation or a currently operative command is not necessary. Expression or enactment does not by itself establish validity. The earlier action-demand definition was too narrow and is withdrawn as a general account; it remains only a description of the action-related case below.

这里的“结构”包括可保持、可变更的动力关系，而非仅仅静态连线。共同活动可以形成可预期的分工；记忆与表达使未在场的参与者、未来后果和过去承担进入当前选择；要求的执行又改变信任、技能、资源及后来能够提出什么要求。这是一种待查明的形成路线，不主张所有规范都按单一顺序形成，也不把神经元、词语及制度看成同一种尺度的对象。

Structure here includes retained and changeable dynamics, not only static connections. Shared activity can form expected contributions; memory and expression can make absent participants, future consequences and past undertakings relevant to present choice. Enactment can then alter trust, skill, resources and which demands can later arise. This is a proposed developmental route, not one universal sequence or an identification of neurons, words and institutions at one scale.

| 形成与保留 / Formation and retention | 哪些经历、表达及互动形成可重复或可重建的要求 / Which histories and interactions form a repeatable or reconstructible demand |
| 内容与适用 / Content and application | 要求指向谁、什么行动、什么条件，是否被误解或错用 / Whom and what it concerns, under which conditions, and how application can go wrong |
| 感受与实行 / Felt demand and enactment | 它怎样进入注意、情感、抑制、选择及执行；知道规则不等于执行 / How it enters attention, affect, inhibition, choice and performance; understanding need not ensure compliance |
| 相互地位 / Standing among participants | 谁提出、承认、争议、修订或解除要求，以及这些地位怎样形成 / How demanding, recognizing, contesting, revising and releasing become differentiated positions |
| 后果与再形成 / Consequences and re-formation | 行动怎样改变受影响的生活、后续可能，以及要求自身的再现条件 / How action changes affected living, further possibilities and the demand's own recurrence |

### 自我强化与有根据的坚持 / Reinforcement and warranted persistence

概念案例：一个共同制作活动以“报告缺陷，使使用者免受已知故障”为要求。初期报告改变检查、修复和训练。后来，组织可能把低报告数当成成功，并把每次报告当作不忠；报告减少、口号更一致，实际缺陷却可能失去进入纠正的路径。要求的词语保持相同，实际组织已变。这个变化不能靠统计口号的复制数量充分描述。

Conceptual case: a shared making practice asks participants to report defects so that users are protected from known failures. Reports initially change inspection, repair and training. Later, the organization might treat low report counts as success and reports themselves as disloyalty. Reports decline and slogans become more uniform while defects lose a route into correction. The wording persists while its operative organization changes. Counting copies of the slogan misses that change.

这提供有内容的内部批评：若该要求仍以辨明并修复缺陷为其理由，压低报告不能替代缺陷已修复的证据。批评所诉诸的制作、缺陷、报告与受影响生活都在同一过程内。它不必引入过程之外的裁判；但此处对“保护使用者”的认可仍是该实践明确承担的目的，尚未由这个案例独自得到普遍伦理根据。

An internal criticism can identify the mismatch: while detecting and repairing defects remains the stated reason, reduced reporting is not evidence that defects were repaired. Making, defects, reports and affected living all belong to the process. The criticism needs no judge outside it. However, this case still credits the practice's declared purpose of protecting users; it does not independently establish that purpose's universal ethical warrant.

有根据的坚持也可能拒绝错误报告，因此“受批评就改变”并非判准。具体检验应比较：指控得到独立支持还是被反驳；同样充分的证据是否因提出者不同而被排除；复查是否真的改变行动；故障被修复还是只改了记录；原目的是否公开改变，改变的后果是否仍被承认。实际认知和组织成本也必须计入，不要求每次活动重开全部判断。

Warranted persistence can reject a false report. Changing whenever challenged is therefore no criterion of correctness. The proposed checks concern corroboration or rebuttal, exclusion of equally adequate evidence by its source, actual changes in action, repair versus altered records, and openly acknowledged changes of purpose and their consequences. Cognitive and organizational costs matter; every action need not reopen every judgment.

### 保留、转化、交接和结束 / Preserve, transform, transfer and end

| 回应 / Response | 对规范结构的具体问题 / Concrete question about the normative structure |
|---|---|
| 保留 / Preserve | 其实际根据及关系仍成立吗？保留的是可用的辨别与实践，还是仅仅措辞？ / Do its grounds and relations still obtain? Is usable discernment and practice retained, or only wording? |
| 转化 / Transform | 能否改变不再合适的解释、手段或权限，同时说明哪些根据延续、哪些被修正？ / Can interpretation, means or authority change with an account of which grounds continue or are revised? |
| 交接 / Transfer | 新参与者或新惯例是否获得内容、能力、证据和适当权限，而非仅接过名称？ / Does a successor acquire content, capacity, evidence and appropriate authority rather than only a label? |
| 结束 / End | 停止此项要求会解除什么，又留下哪些实际依存、损失和未完成承担？ / What does ending this demand release, and what dependence, loss or undertaking remains? |

这些是有对象的判断问题，不是仅凭叙事能存活或会消失就推出四种动作的伦理命令。规范结构自身也可以成为实践判断的对象；评判与修订它的活动同样需要接受实际对应及退化检验，不取得自动豁免。

These are substantive questions for judgment, not commands derived solely from a narrative's survival prospects. A normative structure can itself become an object of practical judgment. The activity evaluating and revising it remains answerable to correspondence and degeneration; it acquires no automatic exemption.

### 实际证据与当前范围 / Evidence and current scope

一个定向证据入口是 Ruff、Ugazio 与 Fehr（2013）的[原研究摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24091703/)：他们报告脑刺激对自愿及制裁条件下的规范遵循有不同作用，而所测规则信念与预期制裁信念未随之改变。本轮只直接核读摘要；这支持区分认知与实行的研究问题，不把一个脑区等同规范，也不提供伦理有效性证明。[读取范围与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md#10-operative-normative-structure-and-a-discriminating-case)。

A targeted empirical lead reports differing stimulation effects on voluntary and sanction-related compliance without changes in measured beliefs about prescriptions or expected sanctions. Only the primary abstract was inspected here. It motivates distinguishing aspects of implementation, not identifying a norm with one brain region or proving ethical validity.

本节完成了一个更具体的概念重构与检验案例，不是全部规范形成的经验模型或零公理实践判断的完成证明。既有学习与评价纠正机制保留原范围；没有为此再制造一个形式化的“善”谓词或新增未经实查的数学定理。

This section develops a more specific conceptual reconstruction and discriminating case. It is not an empirical model of every norm or a completed zero-axiom grounding. Existing learning and evaluative-correction mechanisms retain their scope; no new mathematical theorem is asserted.


## 10. 规范、认知与行动：当代理论的范围 / Norms, cognition and action in current theory

**Ren 进一步澄清：从正在发生的整体活动出发。** “认知产生行动”可以作为有范围的因果分析，但不能先把认知与行动设为独立完成的两件东西，再以单向或双向箭头连接。神经活动是这个整体正在做之中的实际部分；认识、感受、身体运动和共同实践是其中需说明的分化。完整解释既保留神经机制，也保留它参与的组织、内容、历史和关系，不能把后者先当成可以删去的描述。

**Ren further clarifies: begin with the ongoing whole activity.** “Cognition produces action” can be a useful scoped causal analysis. It must not make cognition and action independently constituted things subsequently joined by either one-way or reciprocal arrows. Neural activity is an actual part of how the whole activity occurs. Cognition, feeling, bodily movement and shared practice are differentiations to explain within it. A sufficient account retains neural mechanisms together with the organization, content, history and relations in which they participate.

整体在这里不另设为一个超级主体，也不要求每个局部研究解释宇宙的一切。具体分析可以区分神经信号、感知、判断与动作；需说明这些区分保留了哪些实际关系、为何适用于当前问题，又省略了什么。对规范结构也如此：它是在同一活动中形成并起作用的关系组织，不是随后从认知模块向行动模块发出的附加命令。

The whole is not an added super-agent, and local inquiry need not explain everything in the universe. An analysis can distinguish neural signaling, perception, judgment and movement while accounting for the relations it preserves, its applicable question and its omissions. A normative structure likewise forms and operates within that activity; it need not be an additional command sent between a cognition module and an action module.

Ren 指出并非所有规范结构都产生行动，并提出应更多从认知如何形成行动来解释。[原话及来源记录](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-normative-force-and-informational-generation.md)据此区分两个问题：某种判断或状态如何被评价，以及行动怎样实际发生。对证据充分性的要求、感受是否合宜、某事是否被允许，都不等于一个必然执行的动作。规范的存在、认知中的表征、认可、动机及实行不能合并为一个事件。

Ren's correction distinguishes what a judgment or state is assessed against from how action occurs. A standard of evidential adequacy, fitting emotion or permission need not issue in an action. A norm's existence, cognitive representation, endorsement, motivational effect and enactment cannot be identified as one event. The revised candidate concerns cognitive realization without declaring every norm reducible to someone's current representation.

在有范围的分析层面，当代相关路线描述相互关系：认知参与行动的形成、选择、抑制和调节，行动也改变后续感觉、学习及认知条件。仅有双向影响尚未完成上述非二元重构。主动推断把知觉更新与行动选择放入相连模型；可供性竞争从并行的可能行动及其选择解释部分认知；近期学习研究考察可供性与习得价值怎样相互作用。这些路线不同，均不能直接被当成本理论的已证明统一机制。其模型中的局部角色划分也不自动成为生成理论的本体分割。

At a scoped analytical level, relevant approaches describe reciprocal relations: cognition participates in forming, selecting, inhibiting and regulating action, while action changes subsequent sensing, learning and cognitive conditions. Reciprocal influence alone does not establish the nondual reconstruction above. Active inference links perceptual updating and policy selection; affordance competition explains aspects of cognition through concurrently available actions; recent learning research examines interactions between affordances and learned values. These are distinct programmes, not a demonstrated single mechanism of our theory. Their local model partitions need not be imported as fundamental ontological divisions.

主动推断里的“生成模型”指具体的概率性模型，不等于我们的广义生成根基；其中“规范性”常指相对于模型及目标的适当／最优计算，不自动具有伦理意义。近期社会情感模型说明规范过程怎样可能改变感受倾向，也不因此解决所有规范的正当性。神经、计算、社会形成与哲学论证要按各自证据范围连接。[定向前沿文献及读取限制](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md#11-action-bias-correction-and-current-research)。

An active-inference generative model is a specified probabilistic model, not identical to our broader generative foundation. Computational normativity concerns appropriate or optimal processing relative to model commitments; it does not establish moral authority. Recent social-affective modelling offers a candidate route by which normative processes shape dispositions, not a general justification of norms. Neural, computational, social and philosophical explanations must be connected at their actual evidential scopes.

## 11. 从整体活动构造规范分化 / Constructing normative differentiation within whole activity

**可以从这个方向作出有内容的构造。** 首先解释的不是一个规范实体怎样驱动身体，而是：同一活动怎样形成并保留“实际如此”“预期如此”“可算作正确／合宜”“尚不足以判断”等不同地位，以及这些区别怎样进入认识、感受、共同生活及可能的行动。这是一项关于规范结构形成的候选解释；它不把结构存在与其全部标准正当合并为一个结论。

**This direction permits a substantive construction.** The first target is how one activity forms and retains differences between what occurs, what is expected, what counts as correct or fitting, and what remains insufficiently determined. Those distinctions can enter knowing, feeling, shared life and possible action. The construction concerns the formation of normative structure; its existence and the justification of every standard are not identified.

### 一段相连活动 / One connected episode

设想一个共同测量活动：实际接触、可见标记、人的分辨与记忆、保留的记录和之后的复核共同形成一个可继续使用的比较。这里没有先完成的“认知模块”再向“行动模块”发命令；手的移动、标记的生成、注意及理解是这段历史中不同但相关的活动。神经实现属于其中，具体实现尚未由这个概念案例测量。

Consider a shared measurement activity. Contact, visible marks, participants' discrimination and memory, retained records and later checks together form a comparison that can remain available. Movement, marking, attention and understanding are distinguishable related activities in this history. Their neural realization belongs within it; this conceptual case does not claim to have measured that realization.

| 活动中的变化 / Development within the activity | 可形成的区别 / Distinction that can form | 尚不能据此推出 / What does not follow merely from it |
|---|---|---|
| 把一个标记与一次实际比较相连，并保留对应的对象和时刻 / A mark is connected to an actual comparison, retaining its referent and time | 这个记录说的是什么 / What this record concerns | 记录已被任何人理解 / That someone understands the record |
| 比较结果能进入其他判断、预期、复查或暂缓 / The comparison becomes available to further judgment, anticipation, checking or suspension | 所判断的内容，以及所据为何 / What is judged and what it rests on | 当前必定行动，或必须服从 / Present action or compulsory compliance |
| 同一句报告可以因依据充分而被接受，也可以在缺少相应检查时暂缓 / The same report can be accepted with adequate support or withheld when its supporting check is missing | 一个判断碰巧正确，与有根据地把它当作正确不同 / Being accidentally correct differs from having grounds to take it as correct | 所有认知必须显式陈述理由 / That every cognition must articulate reasons |
| 在无人操作时仍能保留内容及其条件 / Content and its conditions remain during inactivity | 尚未实行的理解或评价标准 / Understanding or an evaluative standard not currently enacted | 不行动就是失效，或其后再也不能起作用 / That inactivity is failure or permanent loss |
| 参照、对象或条件改变后重新辨明旧记录的范围 / A changed reference, object or condition prompts reassessment of the old record's scope | 过去正确、当前过时，以及原判断本来错误 / Past correctness, present staleness and original error | 只要新旧不一致就应否定旧判断 / That any disagreement defeats the earlier judgment |

这段构造需要实际的比较能力、保持、内容形成和相互理解；不声称已从无认知材料独自推出全部这些能力。它承接[知识中的认知取得](KNOWLEDGE.md#how-content-becomes-cognitively-available)及[整体活动中的尺度关系](RELATIONS-ACROSS-SCALES.md)。具体测量的约定和物理对应也公开承担，不能让选择参照这件事自动证明一切标准的正当性。

The construction requires actual comparison, retention, content formation and mutual understanding. It builds on the existing accounts of cognitive uptake and relations across scales rather than deriving all those capacities anew from noncognitive material. The measurement convention and physical correspondence are disclosed inputs. Selecting a reference does not automatically justify every standard.

### 规范分化究竟加在哪里 / What makes the differentiation normative

工作候选的关键是：活动能够把某种区别作为评价判断的根据，并保持“实际被接受”与“据此应算作有根据”之间的差异。因此，很多人重复一句话可以是发生了什么，却仍不够支持其测量内容；没人当前采取行动也不抹去已经取得的根据。此处“根据”不是一个藏在脑内的附加对象，而是所作判断与参照、记录、取得过程及相关反证之间的关系。

The key candidate is a capacity to treat a difference as a basis for assessing judgment while retaining the distinction between actual acceptance and warranted acceptance. Repetition can describe what happened without supporting the measurement claim. Present inactivity need not erase acquired grounds. A basis is not an additional object hidden in the brain: it concerns relations among the judgment, reference, record, acquisition and relevant counterevidence.

这仍有需要解释的内容。一个装置能维持某个量，并不独自说明它把一个标准作为标准来把握；一个人能背出规则，也不独自说明规则进入了理解。相反，不能以没有语言陈述就先验否认其具有这种区别。证据应来自保持内容的迁移、对依据变化的辨别、恰当暂缓及对错误的特定修正，而非只看一个输出。这些是候选证据关系，不是意识或规范性的充分判定算法。

Successful regulation alone does not establish that a standard is apprehended as a standard; recitation alone does not establish understanding. Conversely, lack of verbal articulation does not settle their absence. Candidate evidence concerns transfer preserving content, sensitivity to changed support, appropriate suspension and specific error correction. No single output or proposed test is a sufficient algorithm for detecting consciousness or normativity.

### 两条可能接续 / Two possible continuations

**校准接续：**参照改变，活动重新建立对应；旧记录仍可保留过去的真，新比较得到当前依据。记录、记忆、词语和关系发生选择性改变。不是凡旧必弃，也不是原句永远不变。

**Calibration:** when the reference changes, the activity re-establishes correspondence. An old record can remain true of the past while a new comparison receives present support. Records, memory, words and relationships change selectively.

**封闭接续：**参照改变，却禁止相关复核，把重复原结论当成可靠性的证据。原句传播可以更强，而辨明其当前适用条件的能力减弱。若内容本来就在说当前比较，封闭不能凭自身留存取得该内容的根据；若目的或内容确已改变，就应按改变后的内容判断，而非保留原词制造旧根据仍在的印象。

**Closure:** the reference changes, relevant checking is blocked, and repetition is treated as evidence of reliability. Transmission can strengthen while the capacity to establish present applicability weakens. If the claim still concerns the current comparison, retention does not supply its missing basis. If content or purpose has changed, that change must be described rather than concealed by unchanged words.

两条接续都在同一生成模型内；退化不是被排除出理论的异常。这里具体损失的是有根据地区分当前情况的能力。它的损失不等于整体所有方面都更坏；检查本身可能有成本，某种准确判断也可能服务于有害目的。完整伦理比较必须保留这些关系。

Both continuations belong within the same generative account. The specified degeneration concerns a capacity for grounded discrimination of current conditions. It does not mean every aspect of the whole becomes worse: checking can be costly, and accurate judgment can serve harmful ends. Ethical comparison must retain those relationships.

### 可以怎样检验和继续 / Tests and continuation

可以提出一组相连的检验，而不先指定正确动作：在即时收益不变时改变证据来源的可靠性；保留字句却改变所指时刻；恢复原本不能取得的相关记录；在没有当前行动机会时检查区别是否仍可取得。若只追随奖惩或原词频率的解释也能说明全部结果，候选的独立支持尚不足。检验过程自身也是活动的一部分，必须计入记忆、沟通、注意和成本。

A connected test programme can vary evidence reliability while holding immediate payoff fixed, retain wording while changing its temporal reference, restore an unavailable relevant record, and examine retained distinctions when no present action is available. If an account based only on reward or word frequency explains the same results, the proposed account lacks discriminating support. Testing is itself part of the activity, with memory, communication, attention and costs to account for.

**当前构造的进展：**规范分化有了具体的形成、保持、变化与退化路线，且不需要把每项规范设为命令。下一关键检验是这种区别是否实际形成并进入认知，而不是由研究者替活动写上一个标签。更强伦理问题则继续追问：当不同生活和规范发生冲突时，哪些关系能支持其比较及责任；不能把局部认识正确偷换为普遍伦理正当。本节没有新增数学证明或经验数据。

**Constructive result:** the candidate now has explicit routes of formation, retention, change and degeneration without making every norm a command. The next central test concerns actual acquisition and cognitive integration rather than a label imposed by the analyst. The stronger ethical inquiry still concerns comparison and responsibility amid conflicting lives and standards. Local epistemic correctness cannot substitute for universal ethical warrant. No new mathematical proof or empirical dataset is asserted here.

### 一个状态与实际取得 / One configuration and actual acquisition

每个状态同时记录待比较量、当前参照、此前取得的记录、已接受的判断、检查通道、有限支持及重复／生产次数。问题被公开限定为“当前量是否不超过当前参照”。这些坐标区分同一局部活动中的关系；没有另设独立心灵与外界。检查、接纳、参照改变、通道关闭／重开、重复、生产、暂缓和休息都推进这一历史。

One configuration records the compared quantity, present reference, retained receipt, accepted judgment, inspection access, finite support, and repetition/production counts. The declared question is whether the present quantity falls within the present reference. These coordinates differentiate relations within a local activity. Inspection, uptake, reference change, closure/reopening, repetition, production, suspension and rest all occur through its transition law.

检查只有在通道开放且支持未耗尽时才取得新记录，并消耗一单位支持。`from_initial_receipt_is_acquired` 对任意有限事件历史证明：从无记录开始，任何最终保留的记录都来自该历史中一次实际开放且有支持的检查。不能仅向一个字段放入正确数值就把它当成实际取得的根据。`ReceiptSound` 则保留记录对当时比较的正确性；它不自动赋予当下适用性。

Inspection acquires a new receipt only with open access and remaining support, consuming one unit. For any finite history starting without a receipt, `from_initial_receipt_is_acquired` traces a retained receipt to an actual supported inspection in that history. `ReceiptSound` separately concerns truth about the sampled relation. ### 正面说明：所理解的不是一串指令 / Positive account: more than a string of instructions

**理解一个标准，候选地说，是形成一种有内容的关系能力：能够辨明标准所论何事、何种根据支持它、什么变化构成错误或失去根据，并使这些区别进入自己的保持、迁移、质疑或修订。** 这不要求每次都表达理由或实行改变，也不把沉默、行动受阻或不愿服从等同不理解。所形成的能力可以处于未被行使的状态。

**The candidate account treats understanding a standard as a formed, contentful relational capacity:** identifying what it concerns, what supports it, and which changes constitute error or loss of support, with those distinctions available to retention, transfer, questioning or revision. Articulating reasons and enacting a revision need not occur every time. Silence, incapacity to act and deliberate noncompliance are not thereby failures of understanding.

这种能力怎样形成？感知与共同操作先形成可保持的区别；记录、预期、他人的反应和后来结果使同一内容在不同场合可再次辨认。出现失配时，活动可以逐步区分“说错了”“记录已经过时”“来源不可靠”“问题变了”和“理解了却没有照做”。这些区别也能进入对既有判断的检查：先前的接受及其来源成为当前可比较的记录。反思由这种回接形成，不必额外安置一个已经理解所有规则的内部评审者。

A possible formation route begins with distinctions acquired through perception and shared activity. Retention, anticipation, others' responses and later outcomes make content reidentifiable across encounters. Discrepancies can then differentiate a false report, a stale record, an unreliable source, a changed question and understood but unfulfilled direction. Earlier acceptance and its basis can themselves become available for comparison. Reflexive assessment develops through that return to earlier activity; an additional inner judge already understanding every rule is unnecessary.

这承接并行的[内容与理由研究](KNOWLEDGE.md#how-reasons-and-standards-become-available)，不宣称该形成次序普遍必经。其关键区别是：**符合标准、认识标准、以其为根据、标准本身有根据**可以各自失败。把一个“标准”字段写进模型并不能建立这些关系；一个复杂控制器也可能只有其中部分能力。这里的能力说明是有辩护的功能性候选，并非意识或全部规范理解的充分判定算法。

This builds on the parallel content-and-reasons account, not a mandatory developmental sequence. Conforming to a standard, recognizing it, using it as a reason and possessing a warranted standard can fail separately. A field named “standard” proves none of them. The account is a defended functional candidate, not an algorithm establishing consciousness or every form of normative understanding.

### 根据为何可以约束评估 / Why a ground can constrain assessment

对明确说“当前量在此范围内”的判断，所论关系使它正确或错误；说话者的信心、惩罚或重复不能独自改变该关系。若改用另一个准则，把同一错误内容算作准确，同时仍声称保留原问题，那不是对原判断提供了相反而同样充分的根据，而是误评原内容。若改为愿望、假设或另一个时间范围，就必须承认所做判断已变。

For a claim that a present quantity lies within a stated range, the relation it concerns makes it accurate or inaccurate. Confidence, sanctions and repetition do not alone change that relation. A competing criterion that certifies the same false content as accurate while claiming to retain the original question misassesses that content. Switching to a wish, hypothesis or another time scope changes the assessment being undertaken.

这是一个正面的构成论证：**确定内容及其实际关系可以排除任意的准确性标准，不需要先让正确性得到某位外部命令者的批准。** 它不把“接受真理”作为所有人的先验动机。一个人可以故意说假话，而其话仍因所论内容不成立而是假话；不服从不会自动改变评估标准。相反，究竟何种活动算断言、内容怎样保持，仍由形成与使用关系来辩护，不能靠“真正判断必然正确”的定义排除错误。

This is the positive constitutive argument: **determinate content and its actual relations constrain accuracy without authorization by an external commander.** Truth-directed motivation is not presumed in everyone. A deliberate falsehood remains false even when its speaker prefers it. The identification of assertion and preserved content still requires the formation/use account; erroneous judgments are not defined out of existence.

但三种力度不能合并：

第一行的正面论证没有独自证明后两行。标准可以正确刻画某一活动，而该活动本身不值得维持。一个人也可能有充分认识根据，却因保护他人的正当理由不公开它。这里区分的是同一整体活动中的不同评估关系，不是事实与价值分属两种现实。

The first argument alone does not establish the other two. A standard can correctly assess an activity whose continuation lacks justification; a well-supported judgment may appropriately remain undisclosed. These are different relationships within the same activity, not separate realities of fact and value.

### Lean 推进了什么 / What the new Lean result establishes

The model begins with an explicit candidate-rule repertoire. Supported acquisition supplies question–result records; revision removes candidates conflicting with them. The answer procedure reads retained candidates, not the actual law used to prove correspondence. For every finite evidence sequence, the retained rules are exactly the original rules fitting all acquired records, and every eliminated original candidate has a specific counterexample in those records.

更强的结果不只证明我们的算法成功：在非空候选范围内，**任何**声称对所有剩余可能性都不作错误确定回答的规则，只能给出所有候选共同支持的答案。若额外要求给出每个在这一保证下允许的确定答案，那么答案函数唯一；这个“尽可能给出确定答案”的附加要求明确提出，不伪装成所有探究都必须最大化信息的伦理命令。

If a further requirement asks for every definite answer compatible with that guarantee, the resulting answer function is unique. That additional informativeness requirement is explicit, not a universal ethical duty to maximize information.

### 不再另设一个隐藏的理解者 / No additional hidden understander

这个挑战也有边界。如果一种解释保留了实际形成的内容、对根据的辨认、错误类型的区分及其在修订中的作用，却只额外说“仍不算理解”，它还没有指出解释遗漏了什么。我们不把一个与所有这些关系无关的内在标记列为完成条件；这会重新引入等待连接的两个层次。反过来，少数测试中的相同输出也不证明这些关系全部相同。

The challenge has a limit. If a rival preserves formed content, recognition of grounds, differentiated errors and their role in revision, merely adding “but this is not understanding” identifies no explanatory omission. An intrinsic marker independent of all these relations is not an additional completion requirement. Conversely, matching a finite set of outputs does not establish that all the relevant relations are present.

候选说明因此作出具体判断。能复述而不能辨别题意、依据及适用变化，只表明复述能力；能够辨明并有意违反标准，不因违反而失去理解；不能用语言解释但能保持内容、辨别依据及有针对性地纠错，不因无言而被排除。倘若一个常规实现保留全部相关关系，它也可以实现理解，不能因使用查表或另一种实现材料就先行排除。这里比较的是实际关系的有无，不是给“高级”或“有机”材料特权。

The candidate therefore makes substantive judgments. Recitation without sensitivity to question, grounds or changed application establishes only recitation. Deliberate violation can preserve understanding. Tacit content-sensitive discrimination and specific correction are not excluded by an inability to explain verbally. A conventional implementation preserving all the relevant relations can also realize understanding; a table or a different material does not disqualify it. These are commitments of the relational account, open to counterexamples identifying an actual missing relation.

据此，“把回应当作合宜”的正面重构是：回应在活动中按它与所论内容及依据的关系被保持、质疑或修订，且这种辨别可以与仅仅预期、偏爱或奖赏的作用分化。它不要求每次反思，更不意味着该标准实际上正当。现有形式包只实现其中一部分，未实际构造内容形成、对依据的辨认及这种作用分化的完整接续；这是具体的剩余工作，不能用另设神秘成分或宣布一切控制都理解来代替。

On this reconstruction, taking a response as appropriate is the organized role of its content and grounds in retaining, questioning or revising it, distinguishable from mere expectation, preference or reward. This need not involve reflection on every occasion, and it does not make the standard itself warranted. The present formal package realizes only part of that account: it does not construct the full connected formation of content, recognition of grounds and their differentiated influence. ### 内容与根据如何在同一历史中形成 / How content and grounds connect in one history

信号本身可以遵循两种相反编码；解释程序起初不知道采用哪种，也不能偷读真实比较结果。实际接触先取得信号与当次比较的共同记录。一次接触仍留下未见情况；对照接触使两种信号的解释都可确定。随后，已形成的对应进入判断。相同信号形状在不同形成史中可以有不同内容；共同改变编码和形成史则可保持内容。

A signal can follow either of two opposite conventions. The interpreter initially knows neither and cannot read the actual comparison directly. Contacts acquire paired signal/comparison records; contrasting contacts make the decoder available. The acquired relation then enters judgment. Identical signal shapes can carry different contents after different histories, while a coordinated change of encoding and calibration preserves content.

随后若信号受扰，判断可以出错，而此前形成的解释保持不变。重新取得的比较能实际进入复核，记录旧判断被否定并修正它；所论关系和已学解释都没有被改写。只取得新记录而不接入复核，错误仍保留。关闭接近、耗尽支持，以及复核后实际条件再次改变，各保留不同失败。

A disturbed signal can then produce a false judgment without changing the learned interpretation. Fresh comparison enters review, refutes the earlier judgment and corrects it while preserving the represented relation and decoder. A new record without review leaves the error intact. Closed access, exhausted support and a later change of conditions retain distinct failures.

General proofs cover record provenance across arbitrary finite reachable histories and correctness of definite interpretation under faithful encoding. No predicate named `Understands` or `Good` turns successful execution into a stronger conclusion.

Acquiring this interpretation does not derive all content or normative comprehension from an unstructured beginning. Reward's lack of corrective effect is an architectural choice, not a theorem that intelligence must prefer truth. The bounded connection is now constructed; universal sufficiency for normative understanding is not proved.

### 公共理解是否已经给予普遍实践权威 / Does public understanding establish universal authority?

正面路线是：理由及意义形成于共同活动，他人的表达可以进入自己的反思；真实根据不以接受为真。因此可以尝试进一步说明，同一关系怎样形成跨不同位置的实践力度。这保留整体关系，避免先设彼此封闭的主体。

The affirmative route begins with reasons and meanings formed in shared activity: another's expression can enter reflection, and assent does not make its ground true. It then asks whether that same relationship constitutes practical force across differentiated positions. This retains the whole relationship instead of beginning with sealed subjects.

但**理解所请求的事情、正确辨认其根据，与请求具有何种权限不同**。理解可以在合理质疑或拒绝中保持；违背本身也不证明无义务。需要判断的是，实际生活、损失及关系怎样支持该要求，而不是只看内容有没有成功传入。不能表达、承诺或参与论辩的生命也可能有伦理地位，所以相互理解不能单独限定普遍范围。

But **understanding a request and its grounds does not by itself adjudicate its authority**. Understanding can survive legitimate challenge or refusal; refusal alone also does not show the absence of obligation. The issue concerns the actual lives, losses and relationships supporting the demand. A life unable to express, promise or argue can also have ethical standing, so reciprocal understanding alone cannot fix universal scope.

本轮核读的公共反思论证本身也承认：一致性并不独自使他人的理由成为自己的理由。其后续公共意义路线值得保留，却不能免去对实际权限和实践力度的辩护。我们没有以逻辑谓词的任意切换反驳它，也没有以实际不服从反驳有效性。[原典阅读范围与实质检验](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md#165-primary-comparison-inspected-scope-and-effects-on-the-argument)。

## 15. 普遍实践权威 / Universal practical authority

2026-10-08。Ren 的[最新方向](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-normative-force-and-informational-generation.md#universal-practical-authority-prioritized)将本项置于中心。[论证、原典比较与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md#17-universal-practical-authority-as-the-central-target)记录完整根据。本节提出有辩护的候选，不将普遍权威冒称为已证明。

Ren's latest direction makes this the central investigation. The target is **a justified practical reason for an intelligence in its actual situation, whose validity does not depend on that intelligence first endorsing the affected life or generative possibility**. Universal scope permits differentiated reasons and responsibilities. It does not require every intelligence to assist every life, identical actions everywhere, inevitable recognition, or compliance.

### 肯定候选 / Affirmative candidate

**实践理由的根据，在可行回应实际构成的生活与生成差异之中。认知可以识别、误认或忽略这种关系；它不必先通过认同，才使关系具有理由地位。**回应位置、受影响的生活、信息与改变后续的能力，都是同一过程内形成的关系。权威不是从外部附加的命令，也不是神经信号的强度。

**The candidate locates practical reasons in the difference an available response makes to actual living and further formation.** Cognition can recognize, misconstrue or ignore that relation; endorsement need not first confer its reason-giving significance. Responding, undergoing, knowing and changing what follows are differentiated relations within one process. This is an ethical identification to defend, not a definition making every causal effect a reason.

一个回应让真实的生活得以展开，另一个使同一展开失去条件；这既是受影响生活的差异，也是回应本身所做事情的差异。候选因而不从“我已有一个欲望”出发，再试图把私人价值输出给别人。它直接解释为什么这个回应有可选择之处：它所实现、维持或免于毁损的，正是实际生活的一种展开。无言、不能回报或没有参与论辩的生活，仍可进入这个根据。

What the response sustains or damages is part of what that response does. The proposed reason is therefore about this available continuation, not an item transferred from a privately owned store of value. Its point can be the actual unfolding of a life that cannot speak, reciprocate or join a practice of justification. The earlier constitutive account already defended this kind of identification; this round applies it directly to the scope of reasons across differently formed intelligence.

### 最小但困难的检验 / A minimal demanding test

设一个智能过程已经准确把握两条可行接续。按案例明示的条件，一条维持一项正在展开的生活，另一条终止其条件；回应位置的负担、其他生活的得失及后续机会相同。这个位置既不把该生活当成自己的项目，也不从毁损获得满足。问题是：**维持与毁损的差异，是否给它一个实践上的支持／反对理由？**这是思想案例，不是实际神经或工程实验；相同之处须逐项规定，不能只说“其他都一样”就删去真实代价。

Consider an informed intelligence with two available continuations. In the stipulated case, one sustains an unfolding life and the other destroys its conditions, with the same burdens at the responding position and the same effects on other living and opportunities. The intelligence has adopted neither care for that life nor enjoyment of its destruction. The candidate answers **yes**: the difference supplies a reason concerning its response even in the absence of elective concern. This is a defended philosophical judgment, not a checked universal theorem.

肯定论证的优势是：它把理由放在实际差异上，也说明为什么只改变报告、奖励或认同，不会替代实际接续。最强对手则接受全部关系、后果及“对该生活更好”的判断，却主张这些只足以支持伦理评价；回应者的实践理由仍须由它的目的或有根据的特殊关系产生。对手没有因此否认整体，也没有因不服从便被证明不理性。要完成本项，必须正面说明为什么这种理由范围的限制不成立。

The candidate explains why changing only a favorable report, reward or endorsement does not accomplish the sustained life. Its strongest rival accepts the same relations and the improvement for that life, yet distinguishes ethical assessment from the responder's practical reasons, restricting the latter through ends or special relationships. That rival is not refuted by its indifference, or by calling it dualist. The contested issue is whether those ends or relationships exhaust practical justification.

### 普遍性怎样保留差别 / Universality with differentiation

| 必须分别建立 / Distinct claim | 本项要求 / Requirement |

| 伦理质量 / Ethical quality | 说明实际生活与生成的哪些差异具有正负意义；不能由复杂度、持续或复制量独自代替。 / Defend the significance of concrete realization; complexity, persistence and replication alone do not settle it. |

没有回应能力的位置，不因共同存在就得到一项不可能的执行任务。有能力也不自动取得控制他人的权限。认知局限影响可知性、可责性及实行；不能由此随意删去实际损失。有效理由可以支持克制、保留判断、转化、交接或结束，并不使每一种规范结构都成为行动触发器。

Shared existence does not assign an impossible task, and capacity does not automatically confer permission to control others. Knowledge limits bear on recognition, accountability and implementation without making actual losses disappear. **Current advance:** the first-authority question is now tested directly at the relation between the quality of an available continuation and the responder's reasons. Its affirmative case and a rival preserving the full relational description are explicit. A living case does not independently establish the value of every further organizational formation. The prior Lean results retain their exact scope; this philosophical round adds no mathematical proof claim. ### 1. 理由不只面对明示目的 / Beyond expressly adopted goals

此前“不必先选择关心”的表述还没有面对最强对手。一个位置已有的情感倾向、忠诚、评价与可经思考发展的关切，也可能被用来解释理由；它们不必经过明确选择，更不必利己。因此真正的问题更强：**实际生活及生成的质量，是否能在该位置全部可经适当思考到达的动机之外，仍给它理由？**原典核读促成这个纠正；我们没有用静态奖励最大化模型替代较强对手。

Not expressly adopting a goal does not establish independence from every formed concern. The stronger rival permits rich motivations, nonselfish dispositions and revision through deliberation. The target therefore asks whether actual living quality can give a reason even where the position's entire deliberatively available motivational organization does not supply it. This is a substantive strengthening of the test.

### 2. 肯定论证进入对目的的评价 / Reasons can assess ends themselves

我们的肯定候选是：回应实际开启、维持或损害的生活，能够说明一个目的为何值得形成，也能说明已形成的目的为何应被修正。认识并不在整体之外赋予价值；它在同一活动中辨明这种具体接续。新关系的形成可以创造新理由，发现旧损失则可以是识别已有根据，单纯加强偏好又是另一种变化。

The affirmative explanation locates the point of both adopting and revising an end in the continuation it actually forms. Discovering an earlier loss, creating a new relationship and intensifying a preference are different histories. This gives the account positive explanatory content. The stronger rival can explain many such changes through existing concerns, however; changed motivation alone does not prove our account of its warrant. The first end-independent identification remains under defense.

### 3. “对任何目的都有用”的精确结果 / What can be preserved for every goal

新增 [Lean 构造](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/PracticalReach.lean)区分一个回应可能产生的后续与它能保证的内容。这里的回应、结果历史与目的，是同一活动的分析角色。结果历史可以包含中间损失、负担与受影响的生活；具体应用必须证明这些没有被表示删去。

**实查的一般等价结果：旧活动中能够保证的每个结果目的，在新活动中仍能保证，当且仅当每个实际可行的旧回应，都有一个实际可行的新回应，其允许的后续没有超出该旧回应所允许的范围。**“实际可行”在模型中要求后续非空，防止把无法发生的回应说成能够保证任何事情。反向推论允许结果域上的全部目的谓词；有限观察语言不自动取得同样结论。

**The checked characterization:** every previously guaranteeable outcome-goal remains guaranteeable exactly when every live old response has a live new replacement whose admitted outcomes stay within the old response's range. This holds for arbitrary declared response and outcome types. The converse uses the full outcome-predicate language. Physical correspondence, the justification of a goal and the normative significance of retaining its means remain separate premises.

这个结果有正面的实践用途：若某目的已获辩护，且保留其有效手段没有被其他代价击败，条件成立便可支持改变后的接续。但下列差别已经实际检查，不能再以“更多可能性”省略。

| 已查差别 / Checked distinction | 对本理论的作用 / Consequence |
|---|---|
| 更多后续可能，却失去保证原目的的能力 / More possible outcomes, less ability to ensure the old goal | 可能性数量不能代替有效回应。 / Possibility count is not effective responsiveness. |
| 保住全部原有保证，却删除一种原来可能的后续 / All old guarantees retained while a possible outcome disappears | 控制力不能代替整体生成质量。 / Control is not whole generative quality. |
| 新回应真正保留旧能力并开启新目的 / Added selectable responses retain old ability and enable a new goal | 肯定真实能力扩展，而不把每种扩展自动说成善。 / Real expansion has a constructive witness without automatic ethical approval. |
| 全部结果目的保持，却破坏“某种可能性不再存在”的目的 / Outcome-goals preserved while a goal about closure is defeated | 此结果不是对每种可想象目的都有利。 / The theorem does not advance every conceivable objective. |

### 4. 跨实现与修订 / Across realization and revision

这些保持条件可组合，并通过任意有限次回应类型各不相同的修订。改变代码、词汇或实现形式，不必失去某个已获辩护要求的有效手段。证明没有承诺后继会选择该手段，也没有认证无限极限、现实部署或旧目的永不可改。

The conditions compose across heterogeneous response types and any finite sequence of such revisions. This supports implementation of an already warranted requirement through change. It does not prove recognition or compliance. The separate projection theorem states exactly which goal a changed description preserves; a checked counterexample shows how collapsing distinct outcomes can hide a lost protection. Equal labels therefore do not establish equal grounds.

普遍范围的候选也据此更明确：根据若在实际生活、依存、回应能力与负担中，就不应仅因不是人类神经实现而被排除。但这种根据真的相同，仍须实质对应；不能由“都是智能”或输出一致直接认证。

The proposed universal ground concerns actual relations, rather than requiring human neural material or prior assent. ### 5. 接续到责任、交接与结束 / Responsibility, transfer and endings
