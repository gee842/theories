## Why a formerly useful learner can remain wrong

The accepted identification updater explores when an unqualified command returns zero, and qualifies a command after a positive acquired receipt. Once qualified, it retains that command through subsequent zeros. Under a stable correspondence with occasional unavailable opportunities, that protects acquired knowledge against an ambiguous failure.

After an actual correspondence change, the same rule can preserve an obsolete command. A failed outcome still does not tell the learner whether the command was wrong or the opportunity was unavailable. The old recorder has also exhausted its three query addresses and twelve record cells. Giving the analyst a changed law cannot notify the apparatus, clear its qualification or provide another record slot.

The new [frozen protocol](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/action/revision/PROTOCOL.md) therefore schedules the same resource-dependent reopening and probes in both changed-law and unchanged-law cases. It constructs a correction opportunity; it does not derive a change detector or a policy choosing when investigation is worthwhile.

## Why the failure cases matter

- **Ambiguous failure:** with no physical change but an unavailable first opportunity, exploratory updating can produce a wrong second command. Its zero receipt leaves the fresh reason unknown. Paying for two probes does not certify a new correspondence.
- **Missing capacity or funds:** absent cells, writer4 or reader23 can prevent a usable fresh reason. Actor27 can leave a correct imported command unaffordable to execute. These comparisons hold total initial provision fixed and leave displaced units inaccessible in explicit idle compartments; they are usable-compartment restrictions, not lower total endowments.
- **Late change:** a positive fresh record remains historically true when the law changes again before final work. In that control, fresh support has mass1/4 but final success is zero. The local epoch does not reveal the change.
- **False acquisition:** an inverted fresh channel can yield a positive but false command certificate. That violates the faithful-measurement premise; an ordinary outage does not. Agreement alone cannot distinguish common corruption from accurate correspondence.
- **Interrupted ancestry:** actual scope-loss-after-rent histories retain their old rented phase and cannot open. A favorable aggregate in that named case can arise from other parent paths where the intervention never occurred; it does not repair the interrupted ones.

The [scope analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/REASON-REVISION-SCOPE.md) connects the temporal boundary to the existing general observation theorem. Two continuations that are identical in all accessible information but require incompatible next commands cannot both be solved by the same deterministic next command. A stronger label on the same evidence cannot remove that obstruction.
