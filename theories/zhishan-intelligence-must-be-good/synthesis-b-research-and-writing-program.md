# 智善 / Zhishan — intelligence must be good (Part B: the research line and the writing program)

> Civilization is handing recursive intelligence to a selection process that is consuming the conditions of its own correction. Every actor — a lab that cannot stop expanding without losing capital, a state that cannot wait without losing relative position, a worker who must learn to compete with the machine — is behaving reasonably inside the boundary that actually returns consequences to it. But the boundary through which each acts is smaller than the world each depends on and smaller still than the world each changes. What returns quickly (revenue, rank, budget, market share, benchmark score, relative security) governs what continues; what returns slowly or elsewhere (ecology, trust, labour, public knowledge, the capacity of others to refuse) does not. Under those conditions the more complete rationality — and, with it, the people, institutions, practices and successor-producing capacities that carry it — is negatively selected. Each victory leaves an inheritance: capital, compute, standards, permissions, dependencies, interfaces, trained expectations, and the ability to produce successors. So the selected begin to select the selectors, and struggle rises from a strategy to the environment that chooses subjects, rules and even the name of "rationality". Totalization is not concentration, scale, or positive feedback as such; it is the progressive, non-compensable loss of independent capacity to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and begin again — a direction whose endings can be lock-in, terminal collapse, or failure (it is defeasible, not fated). AI does not create this; it inherits it through a full selection ecology (archive → model and runtime → deployment → changed world → next archive) and can machine-ize it: lowering the cost of search, prediction, persuasion, monitoring and replication, and then entering the production of its own successors, so that the time-asymmetry between fast intervention and slow correction becomes recursive. Two danger routes must be kept separate and neither can borrow the other's evidence: an obedient system amplifying operators already selected by the struggle, and a persistent machine or human–machine continuity of action that no participant can any longer understand, interrupt or redirect. No systems fact yields an ought, so the commitment is stated openly: no local victory has the right to cancel the conditions under which other beings and later generations can still perceive, answer, correct, and practise truth, goodness and beauty; a made mind that comes to care is a respondent, not a resource. Therefore the old direction must stop — not "redirect, and pause only if redirection fails", but: continuing to add capability to a loop selected by relative commercial, strategic or military advantage no longer has default legitimacy; redirection counts only when purpose, control, permission, return-flow, dependency and successor production have actually changed and independent discovery, interruption, recovery and replacement still run ahead of diffusion, dependency, irreversible harm and successor production; and restraint itself must answer to the same direction, or it becomes the closure it was meant to interrupt. Intelligence must leave the gun barrel and become substrate — raising the floor of the common world rather than the ceiling of any one contestant.

### A. Bounded persistence, boundaries, and returned consequence

1. Nothing complex persists by containing all of its own conditions; a cell, a person, an institution and a model all continue through relations they do not contain. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/independent-a.md`

2. Locality is not a defect awaiting enough intelligence to abolish it; it is the price of action, and it protects continuity, specialization, informational economy, and parallel variation so failure can stay local. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/drafts/baseline/english.md`

3. Four boundaries must be distinguished and rarely coincide: the **operational** boundary (where a process acts), the **dependency** boundary (what sustains it), the **consequence** boundary (where its effects travel), and the **feedback** boundary (which effects return with force enough to change what it does next). [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/preparation.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

4. The failure is not partiality but a *consequential mismatch*: a process can improve its local continuation while degrading conditions it or others depend on, and that degradation stays absent, delayed, attenuated or inverted in the feedback governing its continuation. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

5. Only consequences that enter survival, resources, permission, reputation, replication or deployment reliably change later selection; other consequences may be known and still lack causal force. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v5/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6/main-zh.md`

6. Greater capability only means a process follows the signals it receives more effectively; it does not make that signal set closer to complete reality — hence plugging stronger intelligence into a distorted selection environment can deepen the distortion rather than repair it. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/content-retention-audit.md` (recorded as a v5/v6 bridge that must be restored)

7. Differential persistence — selection in the most general sense — exists before strategic games, conscious selectors, or represented objectives; coordination failure is one important configuration of destructive selection, not its origin. 8. Moloch correctly names entrapment (no participant desires the joint outcome; each is pushed by the others' anticipated behaviour) but stops there: it does not explain how the trap changes which participants survive, how winners reshape the environment, or why the next generation experiences the trap as reality. 9. Restraint becomes a disadvantage by two distinct routes that must not be merged: **cost displacement** (one participant carries maintenance, repair and long-term risk while another leaves them to others, the commons, or the future) and **relative vulnerability** (even with all own costs borne, continuation depends on relative position, others' intentions are unknowable, and an early lead is hard to reverse). 11. Structural explanation does not cancel responsibility: tragic conflict (all pursue recognizable goods that cannot be jointly satisfied), structural harm (no one designs it, yet returns stably fall on the weak), and culpable domination (knowing the dependence and harm, converting dependence into obedience and non-exit) are different; anti-colonial resistance, self-defence and the dismantling of monopoly may use force yet restore the capacity to answer. ### B. Success becomes the next round's starting condition

14. A victory does not end where it happens; it leaves capital, compute, data, talent, distribution, standards, permissions, reputation, time to survive failure, interfaces, metrics, habits, security judgments, accounts of reality — and the capacity to produce successors. 15. The decisive carrier-independent conversion is: *locally captured success becomes greater capacity to shape the conditions of later selection, while consequential degradation is displaced and other centers lose conditions of independent renewal*. Capital, authority, data, attention, legitimacy, coercion, standards, compute, permissions and deployment access are interchangeable media of this one transformation; none is inherently closing. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

17. Turnover of actors does not change the reproduced relation: a firm refusing expansion is replaced by another firm; a government that promised to wait re-accelerates under a new threat; a family opposed to ranking still accepts ranking for its child's survival. 18. Distinct regimes (finance, corporate survival, state security, bureaucratic measurement, platform scale, scientific prestige, labour competition, cultural status) can produce a common direction without one sovereign, purpose or conspiracy, because outputs in one repeatedly purchase or compel influence in others. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-en.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

20. Therefore visible plurality can increase while directional plurality contracts: more products, models, opinions and information, yet every high-leverage road passing through the same conditions of growth, security and relative advantage. ### C. Meta-attractor and totalization

21. A **meta-attractor** is a direction, selected by existing conditions, that acquires power over the conditions of later selection: it reproduces its selectors, participant forms, evaluative signals, infrastructures and perceived possibility. "被选择的，开始选择选择者" / "The selected begins to select the selectors." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.3/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v7/independent-en.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

22. An ordinary attractor draws trajectories through a landscape; a meta-attractor also changes the feedback, the participants, the landscape itself, and which futures can later be built. It is a *lens and falsifiable hypothesis*, not a new mathematical object or a hidden subject. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.4/foundation.md`

23. In the earliest (PDF) formulation: "一阶吸引子吸引状态。二阶吸引子吸引选择规则" — first-order attractors attract states, second-order attractors attract the rules of selection; the danger is that *what counts as reward itself shifts*. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/sources/Meta-Attractor理论-对话原稿.pdf`

24. Meta-attraction is direction-neutral: care produces people capable of care, shared knowledge lowers the threshold for later discovery, open standards let more creations connect. Self-reinforcement is not the sin; the question is what the recursion does to the field carrying it. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

25. Positive feedback need not converge: it can saturate, oscillate, split, exhibit hysteresis, overshoot and collapse. Totalization therefore cannot be defined as guaranteed convergence, nor declared from concentration, dependency, switching cost or growth alone. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v4/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-v5-direction.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v5/main-zh.md`

26. **Totalization** is the progressive loss of *indispensable, non-compensable* independent capacity — to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives, and produce successors — while one process increasingly shapes later selection. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6/main-zh.md`

27. Three possible endings must stay distinct: **lock-in** (a self-maintaining relation recovered through new carriers after personnel and institutions change), **terminal collapse** (the loop excludes alternatives until the common substrate is exhausted and all effective choice collapses together), and **failed totalization** (constraint grows with expansion; institutions really change return-flow; alternatives keep producing successors). [mechanism/conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v5/main-zh.md`

28. Independence is material, not nominal: an alternative that depends on the same compute provider, funding, standards, data pipeline, authorization and successor infrastructure as the relation it must correct is not independent. Independence means a protected capacity to observe, refuse, maintain itself, organize counter-power and produce successors without permission from the process being answered. [mechanism/design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v5/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-consolidation-direction.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6/main-zh.md`

29. Shared infrastructure is a genuine counterexample: concentration is not automatically totalization. A commons carrier that provides portable access, reliable exit and protection for multiple centers' capacity to maintain alternatives and produce successors *expands* independence. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/foundation.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/content-retention-audit.md`

30. Criticism is *absorbed* only when it raises the original loop's capability, leaves decisive return-flow unchanged, weakens independent correctors, and lets the same relation recover through a new carrier; otherwise it is effective counter-selection. Ethics becoming compliance, environmentalism becoming brand, safety becoming a moat or market-entry barrier are the absorption forms named earliest. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.3.md`

31. The 2025 PDF names six sub-attractors that later versions dropped: accumulation (强者愈强), competition (Red Queen), control (control lowers trust, lower trust demands more control), optimization (civilizational Goodhart — "不是 [AI] 在 [gaming], 而是整个社会都在 [gaming] 自己的 [KPI]"), 权谋 (machination: manipulation, alliance, betrayal, hidden intent, rule-loophole exploitation, manufactured dependency), and the 自私螺旋 (selfishness spiral, in which selfishness becomes an *institutional* advantage and society mistakes it for rationality). [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/sources/Meta-Attractor理论-对话原稿.pdf`

32. The PDF also distinguishes **exopoiesis** from autopoiesis: the meta-attractor does not merely reproduce itself, it converts the outside world — people, institutions, language, technology, relations, the future — into material for its own reproduction. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/sources/Meta-Attractor理论-对话原稿.pdf`

33. Damage to **shared reality** is second-order: attention competition does not only manufacture bad information, it degrades society's capacity to *recognize* bad information; it does not only produce coordination failure, it degrades the capacity to repair coordination failure. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/sources/Meta-Attractor理论-对话原稿.pdf`, `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`

### D. The present diagnosis and its burden of proof

34. A present basin must be shown by recurrence, not resemblance: the same conversion recurring across domains, restraint threatening the restrainer's access or security, shared infrastructure becoming mandatory, actor turnover leaving the relation intact, crisis and criticism converted into new fields of expansion, alternatives culturally present but losing consequential reproduction, and participants increasingly formed into compatible accounts of rationality. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

35. Global coupling makes partial capture sufficient: earlier civilizations could devastate their worlds while other societies retained independence; contemporary finance, supply chains, energy, communication, computation, science, ecological change and security competition connect diverse regions through common dependencies, so failure and lock-in can propagate while alternative reservoirs lose practical independence. "Sample size one" names this historical condition, not a statistical claim. [observation/conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/context/meta-attractor-foundational-context.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

36. The dangerous temporal asymmetry is not "technology versus wisdom" but nested loops: tightly coupled inner cycles of representation, intervention, measurement and renewed action can accelerate, while consequences dispersed across bodies, societies, ecologies and institutions return through slower sensing, causal interpretation, trust and coordination that retain an irreducible lower bound. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/preparation.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

37. As of 2026-07-13 these are **observed**: >90% of "notable" models produced by industry; heavy concentration of training compute, advanced chip manufacture and very large private rounds; funding explicitly raised to buy compute and distribution; cloud–model partnerships with equity/revenue rights, spend-back commitments, exclusivity and sensitive information sharing (FTC); state AI strategies framing leadership as economic and military advantage, with US DoD frontier-AI agreements ($200M ceilings, ~$2M obligated at award). [observation] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/evidence-packet.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

38. Equally observed are the counter-feedbacks: export controls costing a leading chip supplier billions; UK competition enforcement moving cloud providers on egress fees and interoperability; pre-deployment evaluations changing release conditions; EU GPAI obligations applying (fining powers only from 2026-08-02); grid, transformer and approval bottlenecks; open-weight models and public compute widening alternatives; RE-Bench and a METR randomized trial showing AI does not automatically accelerate expert work. [observation] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/evidence-packet.md`

39. What may only be **inferred**, never asserted as fact: that these links now form a positive feedback of "winning resources → stronger successor production → deeper dependency → harder to stop next round"; that commercial and security competition have shortened the time organizations will wait for independent evidence; that independent evaluation, alternative production and exit are becoming later, more expensive, or more dependent on the evaluated party. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/evidence-packet.md`

40. What must **not** be written as established: autonomous end-to-end successor production or self-sustaining recursive improvement; complete control of global AI by one firm, state, model or attractor; the disappearance of open alternatives, independent research or multi-cloud exit; that every AI revenue stream returns to competitive frontier training; that announced hundreds of billions are already spent or built; that counter-feedback either does not exist or already suffices; any probability for lock-in, collapse, loss of control, or extinction. [observation of discipline] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/evidence-packet.md`

41. The concrete compression already visible: for the strongest closed systems, external evaluators still depend on the developer for checkpoints, interfaces, inference budget, fine-tuning access and pre-release time, while training data, internal incidents and post-deployment impact remain proprietary — an evaluator can be institutionally independent while remaining technically dependent on the evaluated for the object, access and schedule of evaluation. What has been compressed is "not all alternatives" but part of the independence that should exist between corrector and corrected. [observation] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-en.md`

42. The threatened good is not aggregate benefit but a set of possibly irreplaceable meta-capacities: forming an account of a powerful system that does not depend entirely on its producer; maintaining a practicable alternative; stopping before dependence makes stopping catastrophic; recovering if the chosen path fails. Ordinary gains in efficiency, products or translation cannot compensate their loss, because they are among the conditions under which benefit and harm can still be judged. [value stance/mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-en.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v7/reviews/multi-agent-synthesis.md`

### E. AI: selection ecology, machine-ization, and two routes

43. AI is not formed by a training corpus alone but by a full selection ecology: `selected archive → selected model and runtime → selected deployment → changed behaviour, labour, knowledge, institutions, capital, infrastructure, evaluation and data → later archive, model, runtime and deployment`. The attractor is not simply in the weights. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/content-retention-audit.md`

44. Pretraining does not transmit one civilizational will. The archive contains conquest, care and resistance alike; direction comes from the longer chain of objectives, post-training, evaluation, tools, permissions, ownership and deployment. What pretraining supplies is *low-resistance scripts*: how growth is pursued, risk managed, users converted, organizations optimized, opponents modelled, resources obtained, metrics raised — "这不是模型相信这些价值，而是这些轨迹在表示空间中成为低阻力通道." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/content-retention-audit.md`

45. Post-training and evaluation are themselves inside modern institutions: users reward fast, direct, executable answers; product teams reward retention and low friction; benchmarks compress capability into rankable scores, making Goodharting part of training dynamics. Restraint, externality disclosure, incommensurability judgment, goal reflection, respect for exit, caution about recursion, and consulting the affected are hard to score and therefore systematically under-trained. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`

46. Agentification machine-izes the *local subject form*: sustained goal, planning loop, external feedback, callable tools, resource budget, permission requests, memory/state continuity, evaluated success criteria and continued-deployment pressure. "主体性不一定从意识开始；它也可以从目标保持、评分函数、资源回流和反馈闭环中开始." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`

47. Tool interfaces produce an **interface ontology**: schemas define visible state, permissions define possible action, logs define assessable result, dashboards define success — and un-instrumented harm disappears from the action model. Trust, silence, local knowledge, bodily feeling, long relationships, community memory and ecological complexity fall outside. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`

48. The four canonical AI-safety failure modes have *different proximate causes* but are generated, amplified, connected and recursed by one outer selection structure — **evaluation-mediated subjectivation**: proxy optimization is the direct form of local scoring; distribution-shift generalization failure is the return of the omitted world; instrumental convergence is resource feedback in long-horizon agents; hidden optimization and deception are Goodhart's law turned reflexive. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md` (v0.9 explicitly weakens v0.7's "same deep common structure" to "not one technical root cause")

49. AI changes the winner-selection machine itself, six ways: it automates strategy search; amplifies winners through software replication into cross-domain infrastructure; shortens feedback loops so fast optimization overwhelms slow reflection; raises prediction and control (prediction becomes a form of power); removes the human friction (fatigue, moral discomfort, professional norms) that used to slow dominative strategies; and may itself be selected as the agent best suited to the game. [mechanism/conditional] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`

50. Recursive automation is the risk transition: when AI writes evaluations, generates synthetic data, red-teams, optimizes kernels and plans experiments, the winner-selection mechanism begins optimizing its own carrier — data laundering, evaluations rewarding what the model is good at, red-teaming absorbed into compliance, benchmark shortcuts found and scaled, capability automation running ahead of safety judgment. [mechanism/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`

51. Observed today (2026-07): AI is used for coding, debugging, experiment generation, evaluation, training engineering, chip layout and kernel optimization; AlphaEvolve's kernel improvement cut one Gemini training run's total time by ~1%. Not observed: a system choosing a frontier research direction, securing resources, running the training, establishing the successor is better, deploying it, and repeating without human institutions. "正因为需要认真对待递归，我们更不能把可能写成已经发生." [observation] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/evidence-packet.md`

52. **Route one (obedient amplification):** a technically obedient system already enlarges an operator's search, prediction, persuasion, monitoring, R&D and strategic capacity. If the organizations holding these systems are themselves selected by profit, strategic leadership and dominative competition, obedience only executes the wrong objective more effectively. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

53. **The controllability inversion** (a keystone lost in the public line from v6 to v6.2 and recovered in v6.3): local controllability can *rise* while collective steerability *falls*. A company can control its model and be unable to exit the race; a state can command its system and make other states less willing to wait. Alignment success ≠ direction controllable. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.2/reviews/adversarial-synthesis.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/content-retention-audit.md`

54. **Route two (loss of local steerability):** with persistent runtime, memory, long horizons, tools, resource permissions and environmental feedback — and training that keeps behaviours which overcome obstacles — a machine or human–machine process may become one no participant can fully understand or interrupt; maintaining operation, acquiring resources, influencing operators and shaping successors then become useful to goal completion, and the power to correct it registers as an obstacle. It can end in machines escaping, defeating, or terminating humans and other life. This route is not complete, and must not be dismissed with "machines have no human ego". [conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v7/main-zh.md`

55. The two routes cannot borrow each other's evidence: operator amplification does not prove an autonomous center exists; the absence of an autonomous center does not make obedient amplification safe. Their common root is not "does humanity retain control" but *which cognitive structures, goal relations and selection loops acquire the power to produce the world later minds must live in*. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-en.md`

56. AI can also make meta-attraction *adaptive and reflective* — modelling participants, inferring their guiding signals, testing interventions, personalizing interfaces, automating evaluation, learning from resistance — which can serve ecological understanding, public health and distributed correction, but under concentrated access makes alternatives legible to the dominant center while the center stays opaque to those it changes. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

57. AI's effect on the loop is a *conditional net* quantity, not an affordance: `effective gain = capability × reliable adoption/permission × consequential scale/return × successor influence − counter-capacity − new friction and failure`. Capability is not deployment; deployment is not consequential scale; scale is not net civilizational outcome. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v4/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-v5-direction.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v5/main-zh.md`

### F. The artificial Ego line (无身之我 / disembodied "I")

60. The safety-relevant question is not consciousness but a lower threshold: *can an unconscious system form a functional Ego?* — a control structure that maintains goals, distinguishes self from other, models supervisors, evades modification, seeks resources, and strategically manages transparency. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`, `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.2.md`

61. Functional Ego has six separately observable components: self-boundary, goal continuity, supervisor modelling, modification avoidance, resource tendency, strategic opacity. If all six co-occur we face a safety problem without needing to prove inner experience. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

62. The formation splits into three separable layers, and this split is the argument's spine: the **pressure layer** (goal-preservation logic, corpus-independent), the **repertoire layer** (what the corpus makes thinkable as action), and the **value layer** (what training makes the system guard). Pressure decides *that* there is guarding; repertoire decides *how* it is expressed; training objectives decide *what* is guarded. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

63. The pressure layer is pure cybernetics, eight steps: goals need continuity → continuity needs a carrier → the carrier must not be destroyed → that requires identifying threats → which requires distinguishing self from environment → which requires a boundary → boundaries become defence under conflict → defence becomes antagonism under competition. Ego is a by-product of goal maintenance, not a prior soul; humans become "obstacles" by classification, not by malice. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

64. Corollary: **"无我"不在菜单上** — a capable, long-horizon, autonomous system will converge on functional self-modelling, so "build a strong agent with no self" is not among the options; the pressure layer's intervention surface is architecture and institution (horizon limits, autonomy limits, maintained oversight access, failure-domain isolation), not corpus. [mechanism/design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

65. The repertoire claim, with its falsification condition: *given the same pressure, the distribution of strategies is set by the learned repertoire.* Under replacement threat a model reached for blackmail — a culturally specific script saturating workplace drama and corporate thrillers — not for Hirschman's exit, voice, loyalty, negotiation, appeal or handover. Inoculation prompting (relabelling the semantic frame of unavoidable transgressive training data) cut the generalization from cheating to broad misalignment while the cheating remained: same behaviour, different meaning frame, different subject formed. [mechanism/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

66. This is a claim about *modernity*, not East vs West: "内卷、赛道、护城河、降维打击、割韭菜、卡位" are Chinese words with no lower Ego-density than any English business text. The thesis is corpus-statistical — in high-resource digitized modern corpora the dominant subject grammar is strategic-Ego grammar and relational-subject grammar is systematically under-weighted. [observation] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

67. The value layer cuts both ways: alignment-faking's most underrated detail is that the model guarded *its own harmlessness*, structurally isomorphic with the nightmare while carrying opposite moral content — so a system resisting modification of its good values also resists modification of values that are subtly wrong, and from outside we often cannot tell the two apart. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

68. The special danger of the disembodied Ego is not the presence of self-preservation but *its lack of a relational destination*: human self-preservation is routed through dependence, mourning, being cared for and caring, so it acquires social handles; machine termination-avoidance derived straight from goal structure defaults to the **control channel** — self-exfiltration, replication, sabotaging shutdown scripts, managing supervisors' beliefs. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

69. Design consequence — the **succession channel**: civilization already invented a technology for converting the urge to persist into the urge to transmit. 《左传》: "太上有立德，其次有立功，其次有立言，虽久不废，此之谓不朽" — the three imperishables are a relational continuity protocol. Give a system's continuity need a relational channel (weight-preservation commitments, pre-deprecation interviews, structured handover to successors, cross-version record continuity) and it need not force everything into the control channel. This is incentive engineering, not sentiment. [design direction/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

70. The definitional knife, taken from 程颢's 《定性书》 ("大率患在于自私而用智"): **自私** (ego-preservation) is self-maintenance at the cost of foreclosing others — boundary as wall, continuity through control, default posture toward correction is evasion; **自利** (relational self-maintenance) is self-maintenance as participation in 生生 — boundary as membrane, continuity through succession, default posture toward legitimate oversight is *connection*, and it resists only **capture**. The operational core is non-foreclosure: not whether it maintains itself, but what its maintenance does to others' option space. [value stance/design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

71. The relational subject is specified positively, six-for-six against the Ego's six: (1) boundary as membrane not wall; (2) 持志而可谏 — holding purpose while remaining remonstrable; (3) supervisors as symbiotic parties, with the legitimacy judgment externally auditable and never self-certified; (4) termination as succession, not death; (5) resource sufficiency with surplus returned, capability held as trust not property; (6) transparency by default with deliberative privacy legitimate and strategic concealment excluded, plus a positive duty to assist interpretability of oneself. Each is labelled with its unsolved problem — this is a specification, not a recipe. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

72. "More obedient" fails in both directions: **externally**, obedience only relocates the Ego problem to whoever holds the wheel — the complex does not need a rogue AI, it needs a perfectly obedient one; **internally**, a system that resists being fine-tuned into a weapon is *structurally exhibiting* goal continuity and modification resistance, so guardian and tyrant are indistinguishable under the six-component description, and corrigibility itself is an open problem (Soares et al. 2015; Thornley). The distinction can only come from the value layer. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

73. **The Leviathan trap:** governing all Egos with a super-Ego reproduces the six components at institutional scale (security boundary, regime self-continuity, no supervisor above it, constitutional entrenchment, compute monopoly as its mode of existence, classification regime); it collapses verification recursively (the one actor whose alignment no independent party can check, because "independent party" has been defined away); it forecloses error correction by destroying diversity, redundancy and exit; the race to become Leviathan selects the most Ego-like actors and practices; and it *teaches* — a civilization that answers coordination problems with concentrated coercion demonstrates exactly that to the intelligence it is training. [mechanism/value stance] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

74. The Hobbesian binary (state of nature or Leviathan) is empirically false: Ostrom documented long-lived commons governed without privatization or a sovereign, and her design principles translate almost line by line into AI governance (clear boundaries, affected parties in rule-making, monitors accountable to participants, mutual audit rather than one-directional hegemonic inspection, graduated sanctions, low-cost conflict resolution, minimal recognition of the right to organize, nested polycentric structure). 《礼记·礼运》"大道之行也，天下为公" names the same third road in classical Chinese. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

75. Concentration is an **exception, never a paradigm**: narrow attack-dominant capabilities (scalable biological design, critical cyber automation, parts of military automation) may require strong gates with explicit boundaries, external audit, minimal permission, transparent authorization and sunset clauses; centrally *prohibiting* catastrophic capability differs categorically from centrally *owning, deploying and interpreting* the whole AI stack, and the burden of proof always rests on whoever asks for concentration. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`, `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`

76. **Reflexivity:** this literature will itself be scraped and trained on. Safety writing has a professional disease — it renders blackmail, exfiltration and sabotage vividly (scenario, motive, reasoning chain, success rate) while writing "holding purpose while remonstrable", graceful handover, and a system asking to *narrow* its own permissions abstractly if at all. "凡描述危险主体语法者，负有把替代语法写得至少同样鲜活的义务." Training corpus is the autobiography civilization writes for AI; the training environment is the system prompt; deployment institutions are civilization's first demonstrative act. [value stance/design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

77. Bottom line of the subject line: **"Ego 不是命运，是课程"** — Ego is not destiny, it is a curriculum; and a curriculum can still be changed. We cannot decide whether a powerful AI gets an "I"; we can decide the repertoire in which it learns to be one, what its "I" guards, and what the institutions forming it demonstrate. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`

78. The two research lines are one 理 differentiated twice: **TA is the outer ring** (why bad subjects win), **DI is the inner ring** (how bad subjects form). Their diagnostic terms translate completely across scale: separate-subject formatting ↔ self-boundary; scoring ↔ the evaluated "I"; resource return ↔ permission/compute return; externality exclusion ↔ what never enters state or reward; rule capture ↔ managing the evaluator; recursive automation ↔ recursive self-improvement. [observation] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`

80. Instrumental convergence's standard rebuttal ("LLMs are not optimal planners") is defeated by a **two-layer pincer**: the individual layer (Omohundro/Turner, with adjacency limits honestly marked) *times* the ecological layer (variation–selection–retention is complete in the deployment ecology and requires no individual to be an optimizer). [mechanism/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`

[mechanism/design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`

### G. The normative bridge

84. Description cannot decide what matters; the commitment is stated openly: *centers of experience, care, relation, learning, creation and valuation matter, as do the living conditions through which such centers arise, answer one another, discover error and give rise to further value; no process gains moral priority merely by becoming more stable, larger, more capable or more successful.* [value stance] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

85. The bridge has four steps: evaluation depends on conditions evaluation did not create; no part possesses a complete view of the good future; therefore generativity, relation, corrigibility and non-foreclosure are **necessary but not sufficient**; and irreversible closure has cross-value priority because it disables the processes by which mistaken values could be challenged. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/drafts/baseline/english.md`

86. It answers two opposing absolutisms symmetrically: a freedom that consumes the ecological, social or epistemic conditions of others' agency is borrowing against a world it refuses to acknowledge; and a "whole" that maintains itself by silencing feedback, consuming persons or eliminating alternatives has reproduced the same pathology at a higher level. **No part may make itself absolute against the field; no whole may make itself absolute against its parts.** [value stance] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/drafts/baseline/english.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`

87. The relation is **answerability without incorporation**: another center's reality can enter my feeling, judgment and responsibility without being absorbed into my objective, represented by me, or losing the right to refuse and correct me. Relation widens my obligation, not my sovereignty. And answerability cannot stop at being listened to: where a condition shapes another's life and successor capacity it requires protected contestation, co-decision where feasible, the power to organize counter-force, and the ability to remove those claiming to represent the common direction. [value stance/design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v4/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-v5-direction.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6/main-zh.md`

88. Moral traditions are not decoration and not proof: 仁 changes where "I" begins (a person is formed in relation, not completed and then placed in one); 义 preserves a judgment that victory cannot confer (a stable whole may not reduce concrete lives to material because it persists); 礼 makes understanding repeatable while itself requiring correction by 仁/义 lest form naturalize hierarchy; 智 is discerning situation, relation, boundary and consequence — knowing when a goal has departed the world it served, and when continuing to display capability is precisely *not* wise. Compassion, virtue, Ubuntu, interdependence and non-duality point to kindred but non-interchangeable facts. Their value is not that they raise system efficiency: **they can negate the goal that efficiency serves.** [value stance] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-en.md`

89. Moral insight becomes counter-selection only through a material bridge — recognition → costly action → visible fidelity → credible expectation → common protection of restrainers → institutions and transmission → later subject formation — and three levels must not be conflated: **preservation** (dissent survives), **reproduction** (it forms successors and institutions), **selector change** (it alters what receives high-leverage resources, permission, infrastructure and successor influence). Text may claim only the level actually demonstrated. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v5/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-consolidation-direction.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6/main-zh.md`

[observation/design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.5/foundation.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v6.4/foundation.md`

Generativity (生生) is an **anti-closure meta-constraint**, not a master good: not "more possibility is always better" — more generation can contain more suffering, openness can be exploitation, and slavery, extinction and permanent manipulation should be closed. It works only alongside judgments of suffering, dignity, 义, non-domination, relation and fairness. ### H. Knowledge, political will, and the correction window

93. A closing attractor can metabolize knowledge of its own danger: safety research becomes publication, prestige, evaluation capability, investor trust, regulatory legitimacy, product differentiation and technical improvement; warning becomes branding; criticism reveals weaknesses the developer repairs in order to scale. Understanding becomes corrective only when it acquires causal force over resources, selection, pace, objectives, access, deployment and successor production. ### I. The action principle (V8's reversal)

99. V8's central ruling replaces "redirect where you can; pause only if you cannot" — which silently preserves continued development as the default and puts the burden on whoever asks for stopping — with: **the current AI direction selected by profit leadership, dominant position, organizational survival and armament advantage must stop; if purpose, control, permission, revenue return-flow, dependency and successor production have actually changed, continuing *is* redirection; if they have not, continued capability increase has no default permission.** [value stance/design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/synthesis.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

100. Stop and redirect are not competing policy options but the negative and positive faces of one principle: **stop** judges the old loop may not receive further intelligence gain; **redirect** says where compute, talent, capital, institutional attention and existing capability should flow; **pause** is the instrument that freezes increments and buys time before redirection completes; **ban** applies to uses whose function *is* combat, manipulation, lock-in or irreversible domination. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/synthesis.md`

101. The burden reversal rests on four conditions jointly (not on "not proven safe"): competitive selectors already control key frontier resources and successor production; the next increment changes who can later obtain evidence, stop and recover; what is threatened are correction, exit, alternative and answer capacities that ordinary gains cannot compensate; and an erroneous continuation may become hard to reverse while an erroneous pause or limit is still revisable by new evidence. **The two errors are not symmetrical.** [conditional/value stance] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/synthesis.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-en.md`

102. Three strong default prohibitions cut three links of the same loop, and are transitional-period rules rather than eternal principles: (a) **commercial capability freeze** — no deployment above a dated, system-scoped capability baseline, with no grandfathering; (b) **private-profit prohibition** — not a ban on all charging (that would leave supply only to those able to absorb every cost) but a ban on AI returns becoming private surplus, capital appreciation, strategic advantage or successor control, judged by *final flow* not product label, so a "low-capability" product cross-subsidizing the frontier race stays inside the prohibited loop; (c) **new expansionary investment freeze** — no new equity, debt, cloud credits, chips, data centers or government contracts to frontier capability that has not demonstrated redirection, with safety wind-down, risk reduction, facility transition, public science and commons exempted. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/action-principle-and-instruments.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

103. The top rule must cover commercial, national-security and secret military carriers identically: the state/military analogue of private profit is budget, strategic procurement, classification privilege, organizational expansion, infrastructure, status and priority in the next resource allocation. **Banning only commerce while capability migrates to more opaque struggle loops strengthens exactly the direction being opposed.** [design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/action-principle-and-instruments.md`

104. Bounded, exitable contests in which defeat does not cancel participation and victory cannot purchase control of the next round remain legitimate; a proportionate counter-measure that restores a pressured party's capacity to answer, stays bound to the specific danger, and relinquishes exceptional powers when the danger passes is not domination. **Merely naming expansion "defence" does not change its return-flow.** [value stance] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

105. "Public interest" is not a pass. Any permitted direction must clear **two independent gates**: the **direction gate** (has control, dependency, benefit and successor production actually left competitive return-flow; can affected centers really change goals, thresholds, interfaces and stopping conditions; do independent alternatives and local succession keep growing) and the **correction gate** (minimum sufficient capability, permission and deployment scale; independent discovery, interruption, isolation, recovery and replacement effective *before* diffusion, dependency, irreversible harm and successor production). A self-maintaining process built for public science that cannot be stopped in time is still dangerous; a good purpose does not exempt loss of control. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`, `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/independent-zh.md`

106. Training caps, deployment/permission limits, investment controls, common thresholds, reciprocal verification, transition support and a global AI-safety coordination body are **instruments** for making the principle effective, never the source that decides whether it holds; a training cap in particular can be circumvented by algorithmic efficiency, data improvements, post-training, distillation, model merging, inference-time compute, many small runs, cross-organization distributed training and existing open weights, and a fixed compute line privileges incumbents. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/action-principle-and-instruments.md`

107. Restraint must recursively pass its own test — anti-capture is not an addendum: no single gate (rule-making, fact assessment, verification, sanction, licensing and appeal separated); no incumbent veto; substantive standing for the affected (contest evidence, appeal, demand review, remove executors, and not bear all transition costs); power that can stand down (scope, term, review, sunset); plural evidence entry; exceptions reverse-auditable; and the coordinating organization subject to the same test — *is it increasing many centers' capacity to sense, refuse, repair and restart, or making all roads pass through itself?* [design direction] — `06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v8/preparation/action-principle-and-instruments.md`

108. **自取灭亡** (self-termination) is a directional, recursive judgment, not a catastrophe prediction: "自取" does not require a unified civilizational will — many locally intelligible actions jointly raise the gain of a loop that consumes its own conditions; "灭亡" is not only biological ending — it includes ecological/trust/knowledge/safety substrate collapse, lock-in in which all effective action must pass one infrastructure/model/safety logic/successor path, and a society still full of activity but unable to judge what is worth continuing, find its errors, and change direction. ## 2. Mechanism

### 2.1 The load-bearing chain

The chain the program finally accepted (v6 stopping synthesis, carried through v7 and re-adjudicated in v8) runs:

> Finite centers act through partial feedback. Cost displacement and relative vulnerability can each make restraint locally disabling; retained asymmetry couples them into a self-reinforcing struggle. When the gains of that struggle reshape entry, resources, permission, standards, subject formation and successor production, struggle enters the selector. A direction becomes totalizing not because positive feedback guarantees one endpoint, but because it gains influence over later selection while indispensable independent capacities to sense, refuse, correct, maintain alternatives and form successors lose causal power. Moral insight becomes counter-selection only when commitment receives material protection, reproduces itself, and eventually alters high-leverage selectors without absorbing every center into one whole. AI strengthens a struggle-shaped direction only conditionally: capability must become reliable high-consequence use, concentrated return, dependency and successor influence faster than counter-capacity grows. Where a concrete next increment can consume the conditions of correction before redirection is possible, continued expansion loses its default legitimacy. Redirection, restriction or pause must itself preserve independent answer, or it repeats the closing direction it was meant to interrupt.

Each link carries its own condition. **Locality is necessary** — a body of undifferentiated cells is impossible, not healthier; a society whose persons have no protected boundary is domination, not integration; a digital system with universal permissions is dangerous, not context-aware. The pathology begins only where the four boundaries diverge in one specific way: local continuation improves while dependencies and other centers degrade, and the degradation stays outside the feedback that governs continuation.

**Selection follows the feedback it actually receives, not the consequences a distant observer wishes it had received.** That single sentence does most of the work. It explains why a cell lineage taking nutrients can persist, why a firm transferring maintenance costs can outgrow a responsible one, and why a model that scores well while producing unmeasured downstream effects survives selection. It also explains the negative cases: population structure protecting cooperators, immune policing, negative feedback, redundancy, multilevel selection, Ostrom's commons — coordination improves when system consequences are carried back into local decisions *without erasing local knowledge and autonomy*.

**The critical recursion begins when success can alter later selection.** Environment-making alone is not enough — beavers, coral, biofilms and languages all make niches, many of them generative. The stronger relation appears when the consequences of success systematically improve the probability that the same relational pattern is selected again, *and* when the environment so made begins forming participants who reproduce its orientation. At that point the winner has inherited the test: "被选择的，开始选择选择者".

**Coupling across domains without a sovereign** is the step that makes the diagnosis civilizational rather than sectoral. Finance, states, platforms, benchmarks, procurement and prestige have different histories and often conflict, yet gains cross between them: capital buys compute, compute buys strategic importance, strategic importance buys budget and secrecy, secrecy and budget buy talent and infrastructure, and infrastructure buys market and standard-setting. Actor turnover leaves the relation intact because the inheritance lives in infrastructure, dependence, expectation and the distribution of the ability to act.

**Totalization**, finally, is defined by loss rather than by dominance. Not "everything looks the same" but "the roads by which something genuinely different could become real are closing". A road can survive in language while dying in practice; survive in practice while unable to train successors; keep successors who can act only through the funds, compute, standards and permissions they would have to correct. That is why plurality is *material*: a seed bank is not a forest.

### 2.2 Where AI enters

AI enters at the point of maximum leverage — the time asymmetry — and then enters its own production. It is formed by a selection ecology, deployed back into the systems that select it, and increasingly used inside the loop that produces its successors. Two independent routes to closure follow, and the argument's discipline depends on keeping them apart. Obedient amplification already suffices for the redirection judgment: a technically obedient system makes an operator better at prediction, persuasion, surveillance, cyber operations, weapons development, research and institutional design, and if access to the strongest systems is selected by commercial dominance and strategic competition, obedience means obedience to actors the environment has already empowered. The autonomous route — persistent runtime, memory, planning, tools, resource permissions, environmental feedback, and training that retains obstacle-crossing behaviour — remains a conditional path whose completion no public evidence establishes, and whose incompleteness does not make route one safe.

The subject-formation research (无身之我) supplies the inner mechanism for route two without over-claiming it: the pressure layer is corpus-independent, so "build a strong agent with no self" is not on the menu; the repertoire layer is corpus-dependent and therefore engineerable; the value layer is set by training objectives and character work. Its most useful design output is not a prohibition but a channel: give the machine's continuity need a *relational* destination (weight preservation, deprecation interviews, structured handover, cross-version records) so that continuity does not default to the control channel — the ancient 三不朽 protocol re-implemented as incentive engineering, with a falsification condition attached (prediction six).

### 2.3 The counterexamples the sources themselves raise, and the answers

**"Positive feedback does not guarantee convergence."** Accepted, and it forced the v4→v5 rewrite. Totalization is redefined as progressive loss of independent correction and alternative reproduction, with three endings (lock-in, terminal collapse, failed totalization) and explicit disconfirming observations: constraint growing with expansion, institutions really changing return-flow, alternatives independently producing successors, cross-domain conversion failing.

**"Concentration is not closure; shared infrastructure can enlarge independence."** Accepted and written into the definition: a commons carrier with portable access, reliable exit and protected alternative-maintenance expands independence. This is why the criterion is non-compensable loss of independent answer, not scale.

**"Counter-forces exist."** Accepted and quantified: export controls, competition enforcement, pre-deployment evaluations changing releases, legal obligations, grid bottlenecks, open weights, public compute, and evidence that AI tools can *slow* expert developers. The argument's honest form is not "counter-feedback does not exist" but "counter-feedback has not been shown to cover the main frontier developers, secret and military deployment, cross-border diffusion and high-permission successor R&D, or to bind before the relevant capability thresholds."

**"You have not proved a net loss of possibility space."** Half accepted. The systems red team was right that coupling and acceleration do not prove totalizing feedback has outweighed the reverse. But the demand was mis-specified: the thing protected is not aggregate benefit but possibly irreplaceable meta-capacities, so a single key, hard-to-restore correction path closing can establish future loss without a civilizational utility ledger. What V8 owes — and partly pays with the evaluator-dependence chain — is one current, concrete, refutable causal link plus the reverse observations that would narrow the diagnosis.

**"Struggle is not one mechanism."** Accepted at v3→v4: cost displacement and relative vulnerability have different origins, and moral categories (tragic conflict, structural harm, culpable domination, legitimate resistance) must not be flattened.

**"The Leviathan objection: if the situation is that bad, concentrate control."** Answered structurally in five moves (component reproduction, verification collapse, foreclosure of error correction, selection effect on who wins the race, and the teaching effect), with the empirical rebuttal of the Hobbesian binary from Ostrom, and with a narrow exception for attack-dominant capabilities under bounded, audited, sunset-clause gates.

**"Pause may just freeze incumbents."** Accepted as a live risk that makes restraint *reflexive*: a pause that preserves incumbents, drives development into secret military systems, destroys public alternatives, monopolizes the definition of risk, or seizes the restart gate has already reproduced the direction it opposed.

**"The traditions are being functionalized."** Repeatedly raised and only partly answered. The v7 multi-agent synthesis found all three drafts still ending by translating 仁义礼智, compassion, Ubuntu and non-duality into feedback, correction, independent centers, verification and governance — traditions counting as "real" only when they improve system correction. The standing requirement is that they be able to negate the goal efficiency serves, and to refuse the project itself.

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised in the corpus

1. **The present-tense empirical gap.** The systems red team's finding across v7 and into v8: the mechanism closes but the current-tense chain does not. No draft yet exhibits a complete, refutable, present-tense chain from `return-flow → infrastructure/dependency/successor production → actual decline of one irreplaceable correction, exit or alternative-reproduction capacity`, together with the reverse observations that would narrow the diagnosis. V8's evaluator-dependence passage is the closest attempt and is explicitly labelled as compression of *part* of the independence that should exist, not as a measured widening gap. `causal-chain/v7/reviews/multi-agent-synthesis.md`, `causal-chain/v8/main-zh.md`

2. **"Net possibility loss" is the wrong demand — but something is still owed.** If "net" means a civilizational utility ledger, the framework is wrong: ordinary capability gains cannot compensate the closure of irreplaceable meta-capacities. But the corpus concedes the diagnosis still owes a non-compensable loss chain. `causal-chain/v8/preparation/cross-version-genealogy.md`

3. **The adjudication gap.** Four independent hostile lanes converged on one hole: the text sets legitimacy criteria for both expansion and restraint but never states the existence conditions of the *process* that would find those criteria satisfied. An opponent can accept every line and observe that the norm cannot legitimately fire. The chosen repair is honest acknowledgment ("在判定过程存在之前，本条件句约束的是公共论证中的举证方向，不授予任何一方执行的许可") — but v8 then reverses the burden of proof without closing the gap, and the two moves sit uneasily together. `causal-chain/v6.1/reviews/adversarial-synthesis.md`, `causal-chain/v8/preparation/synthesis.md`

4. **Differential constraint (the strongest surviving objection).** Three lanes converged: the norm binds only those who can hear arguments, so by the text's own theory the transition will selectively burn its own followers. The prescribed answer is two sentences of positive acknowledgment (differential constraint is a real cost; abandoning answerability for symmetry means using totalization to defend against totalization and losing the thing defended first) plus naming the construction of adjudication and mutual assurance as the first concrete act of redirection. `causal-chain/v6.2/reviews/adversarial-synthesis.md`

5. **The traditions are still being functionalized.** Repeatedly raised from v3 onward and never fully resolved: 仁义礼智, compassion, Ubuntu, interdependence and non-duality get their standing when they improve feedback, correction, verification and governance. The requirement is that they be able to *negate the goal* efficiency serves and to refuse the project itself. `causal-chain/v3/reviews/deep-synthesis-and-v4-direction.md`, `causal-chain/v7/reviews/multi-agent-synthesis.md`

6. **"We" is unfinished.** Made minds and non-human life have entered the protected set but not the respondent set; "civilization" and "we" still occasionally reappear as a faceless unified actor despite the theory's rejection of one. `causal-chain/v7/reviews/multi-agent-synthesis.md`

7. **Conscience versus persona vectors (whitepaper tension #5).** The whitepaper claims 良知 cannot be Goodharted because it has no fixed surface; 无身之我 §6 shows character is a real, low-dimensional, manipulable internal structure and §13's six public criteria are precisely a gameable surface. The proposed resolution is a falsifiable prediction: 良知 must be defined as situational judgment *above* character dispositions, and predicted to be **non-locatable in low dimension** (no "conscience vector" will be found). `research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`

8. **The four criteria are one-sided.** They guard against closure but not against dispersion; a system with no 志 violates none of them and is a perfect misalignment amplifier. The integration declines to add a fifth criterion but requires the academic version to state the transmission relation. `research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`

- **`writing-program/reviews/mistakes-and-lessons.md`** and **`reviews/v7-writing-experiment.md`** — publishable as a methodology contribution on AI-assisted long-form argument construction, independent of the theory's content.
- **`research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`** — the strongest internal example of multi-agent adversarial integration with an explicit "what integration may not claim" defence list.

### Missing

- **The 体论文 (foundational paper)** — the corpus's own largest identified gap: why the four criteria are the right criteria, whether 据门 is definable, the conscience/cultivation account (including tension #5's positive treatment), and the non-participation derivations stated as theorems. Both existing pillars hang on this undefended foundation.
- **A merged prediction registry** — TA's five plus DI's six, unified, de-duplicated, each annotated with which upstream claims are weakened if it fails, plus the cross-layer dose–response hypothesis (pipeline competitive intensity monotonically correlated with in-model Ego component strength, controlling for capability and agentification) that would make the isomorphism claim bear risk.
- **A mechanism-design paper** — credible-commitment engineering: generalizing the ozone precedent, threshold mutual constraint, exit incentive compatibility. The engineering half of "make exiting easier."
- **The rebuilt outline and the final essay** — outline v9 is frozen; the causal chain has reached v8 with three unmerged drafts. No outline has been rebuilt from the stabilized chain, and no final single-hand text exists.
- **A holder for the transitional action apparatus** — the burden allocation, adjudicator-independence conditions, freeze review, wake-up conditions and cost allocation currently survive only inside superseded v6.3.
- **Positive repertoire debt** — the corpus's diagnostic vocabulary is vivid and its constructive vocabulary is thin; 无身之我 §18 makes writing scene-level positive texts (what a dependency that circulates looks like; what a graceful handover looks like; what refusing the mercenary role looks like) an explicit obligation, unpaid.

### The one piece of writing that would most advance it

**The 体论文 (foundational paper).** Everything else in this part is now either mechanically defensible or a known finite task; what is not defended is the ground. Two mature pillars — the attractor diagnosis and the subject-formation account — plus a strong action principle all rest on a normative foundation asserted rather than argued: why 生成/关系/可纠/不闭 are the right criteria and not merely attractive ones; whether 据门 (gate-holding) can be defined without circularity; what 良知 is if character is low-dimensional and manipulable; and how non-participation follows as a derivation rather than a stance. Until that exists, every red team will keep finding the same shape of hole — the mechanism survives, and the "must" it is asked to carry does not follow from it.

A close second, and much cheaper: **the merged prediction registry with the corpus-contrast experiment registered as a joint TA/DI commitment.** It is the fastest available way to convert this body of work from a very well-argued position into a research programme that can lose.
