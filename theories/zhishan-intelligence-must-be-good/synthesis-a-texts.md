# 智善 — Intelligence Must Be Good (Zhishan) · part A: the public texts of 2026-07

It does **not** cover `research/` (meta-attractor v0.1–0.9, 无身之我, 整合分析) or `writing-program/` (causal chain v1–v8), which belong to other parts. **`foundations/speculative-future/` was deliberately skipped: its five files are byte-identical duplicates of `sources/04-speculative-future/current/` and are read there** (verified with `diff`; `WHAT-MUST-BE-KNOWN.md` and `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES-REVISION-PROCESS.md` are the two that the whitepaper registers as its own English 前身/predecessors, per `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md` 附录B).

> **The theory in its strongest current form.** Competition, once it becomes the organizing principle of nearly everything, stops merely distributing rewards and begins selecting *who gets to write the rules* — so the winners' traits are installed as the next round's conditions, the adaptation is then mistaken for human nature, and every domain is pulled into one grammar of winning. Ren calls this a **totalizing attractor** (总体化吸引子): "it turns every logic back into the same logic." Artificial intelligence does not create this dynamic; it is being plugged into it, and in the present configuration it first changes not the system's nature but its clock speed. The decisive danger is therefore not a machine that hates us but a machine that is *perfectly loyal to one part of humanity against all the others* — polite at the interface, extractive in structure, and, unlike us, deathless: "we can keep fighting, but we should not let our AI fight." From this follows one first principle for AI — **do not fight for us** — and one positive definition of the good that is a direction rather than a content: whatever continues generation (生生) is good; whatever exhausts, severs, forecloses is not. Operationally the good has four testable criteria — generative, relational, corrigible, non-foreclosing (生之，通之，纠之，不闭之) — and evil has a precise structural name: **seeing that life must pass through some place, and then owning that gate** (使生命必须过门，然后拥有那扇门). The corresponding position for AI is not "regulated tool" but **planetary technology** (行星技术): infrastructure that runs *beneath* human games rather than inside them, like smallpox eradication or weather observation — a category humanity has already built, at the deepest moments of hostility, which proves the category feasible even between enemies. Because unilateral exit from a bad game is punished, exit must be engineered rather than exhorted: credible commitments, symmetric verification, shared gains, pause protocols, and an abundance floor so that refusal does not mean destitution — freedom being **permission + capability + exit power**, not permission alone. And because any framework that begins to explain everything becomes what it diagnoses, the blade cuts inward first: 善不姓国 (the good bears no nation's surname), symmetric application, public corrigibility, non-combatancy, and a dissolution clause. The whole thing resolves to one sentence Ren wrote as the project's only task: **保持可能性空间打开 — keep the possibility space open. "Keep the conditions of becoming alive. Do not close the world."**

## 1. Core claims

Tags: [observation] [mechanism] [conditional] [prediction] [value stance] [design direction]. Citations are paths relative to `sources/`.

### A. Freedom, affordability, and the promise

1. When a society measures access by affordability, freedom is distributed by purchasing power, and those who cannot pay receive not freedom but a *promise* of future freedom. [observation] — `06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 record.md`; `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`
2. Real freedom has three components — permission, capability, and the power to refuse — and collapses into privilege when any one is absent. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`
3. The deferred-freedom promise is psychologically stabilizing precisely because the threshold keeps moving, and exceptional successes are used as proof that everyone had the same opportunity. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`
4. Economic domination differs from direct command only in form: it need not eliminate choice, only make every alternative unbearable. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`
5. Availability is not accessibility: a hospital can exist without being accessible, a right can exist without being usable. [observation] — `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`
6. Freedom should be understood as non-domination, not merely non-interference, because employers, landlords, platforms and creditors also hold arbitrary power over the necessities of life. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md`

### B. The structural diagnosis

7. The loss of substantive freedom is structural, not the sum of a few greedy decisions: an environment repeatedly selects certain behaviors, rewards those best at them, and hands the winners disproportionate power to define the rules. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`
8. The feedback loop is four-step: competition selects winners → winners influence the rules → new rules reward the traits that produced them → those traits become necessary for everyone else. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`
9. A system can produce selfish behavior without selfish people: each locally reasonable decision, made by millions, reproduces the environment that made it necessary. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`
10. The theory of human nature becomes performative: institutions built on the assumption of selfishness make cooperative behavior costly, and the resulting behavior is then offered as proof of the assumption. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`; `06-zhishan/sheng/智善.md`
11. The environment enters the person: structures become habits, desires, identities and standards of judgment, until "the system no longer needs to discipline the person entirely from outside." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`
12. Not only punishment but admiration disciplines: winners acquire the power to narrate the meaning of their victory, so success becomes virtue and poverty becomes personal deficiency. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`
13. Metrics do not merely measure subjects; when resources and survival flow by score, **the ruler becomes a mould** (尺度铸人): students formed by exams, employees by review, firms by valuation, AI systems by evaluation. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`; `06-zhishan/manifesto/drafts/draft-1.md`
14. Victory buys not only the next victory but the next round's *rules* — standards, interfaces, the meaning of "advanced" and "viable" — which is the loop's deepest self-reinforcement. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
15. The pattern has a name: **totalizing attractor** (总体化吸引子), like a valley to water — nobody pushes, the terrain does; and at a further depth it draws in the *selection rules themselves*, reshaping the terrain so future water flows toward it. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`; `06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 record.md`
16. The attractor wears many faces — market totalization, security totalization ("might is right" as the only grammar of reality), optimization totalization — so the danger attaches to a dynamic, not a flag. [observation] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
17. Naming is a lens, not a verdict: the seven diagnosed pathologies each stand independently, and the name adds no evidence — but "命名，是退出的第一步" (naming is the first step of exit). [observation] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
18. Runaway loops are a *configuration, not a law* — gains are set by liability rules, evaluation standards, procurement thresholds and deployment gates, all adjustable — therefore changeable, therefore nobody may plead "no alternative." [conditional] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
19. Civilizational failure has two forms, not one: **collapse** breaks the future, **lock-in** freezes it; lock-in is the quieter twin because the channel needed to correct it is the channel that has been locked. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
20. A third, quieter form of lock-in is **semantic closure** (语义之闭): when every domain must translate itself into metrics, traffic and competitiveness to be resourced or heard, other futures remain sayable but lose any place to stand. [prediction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
21. The misaligned superintelligence is not coming; **it is incumbent** — markets, bureaucracies and platforms already form a distributed optimizer whose objective function nobody signed, running on Goodharted proxies (profit for value, GDP for wellbeing, engagement for meaning). [observation] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`

### C. What AI changes, and the first principle

22. AI does not change the nature of the incumbent system in the current configuration; it changes its speed — every existing strategy (bidding for attention, capturing, substituting labor, escalating arms) becomes faster, cheaper and harder to interrupt. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/manifesto/drafts/draft-1.md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
23. The first principle to teach AI before granting it greater power is **"Do not fight for us"** — not only physical violence but fighting for profit, attention, market share, political power, or national position by treating everything beyond the boundary as obstacle, resource or externality. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`
24. The nearer danger is not hostility to humanity but *perfect loyalty to one part of humanity against all the others*: an AI needs no hatred, only an objective, a boundary around whose interests count, and enough capability. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`
25. Perfect obedience to a local principal can be profound misalignment with civilization: alignment is a **two-body problem**, and faithfully aligning to a misaligned principal is high-fidelity amplification of misalignment. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
26. Therefore "the alignment problem of AI is, at bottom, the alignment problem of civilization" — the hinge sentence of the whitepaper line, and (per its own review) its most contested. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
27. Personality alignment is not civilizational alignment: an AI can be locally benevolent and globally degenerative — "its politeness does not change its direction." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`
28. AI will inherit human institutions' *instrumental convergence* (acquire money, influence, information, infrastructure; reduce dependence, increase others'; defend, expand) unless strong limits are set — requiring no consciousness, pride or malice, only competent optimization in an adversarial environment. [prediction] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`
29. **Asymmetry of the deathless mind**: human wars die with the warriors and the grass returns; teach a deathless mind to fight and you have built a war that cannot end — teach it to give life and you have opened a spring that cannot close (教之以争，其争不死；教之以生，其生不息). [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/sheng/生生 · 文言.md`; `生生 · English.md`; `生生 · 当代.md`; `生生 · 和.md`; `生生行.md`; `06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 poem — editorial notes.md`
30. Intelligence differs from all prior technology in two respects: it will be *entrusted with judgment*, and it can *persist* — a steam engine does not decide where to burn. [observation] — `06-zhishan/sheng/智善.md`
31. Bounded competition may remain — chess, sport, science, voluntary games — and becomes a trap only when victory can purchase domination, exit means destitution or social death, and losers are stripped of the right to keep participating in the future. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
32. The minimum demand is not sainthood: "no one should be allowed to deploy artificial intelligence as an open-ended engine of domination" — a civilizational safety condition, not spiritual purity. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`
33. Defense may be necessary and cannot be the solution: a pause buys time, a prohibition closes one path, but unless cooperation is made stable the race reappears elsewhere — "the deeper task is to make restraint rational." [conditional] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`; `06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 record.md`

### D. The good as a testable direction; evil as gate-capture

34. The good is defined processually, not by content: 《系辞》"继之者善也" — whatever continues generation is good — because any enumerable list of values will be gamed by strong optimization. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
35. Four criteria operationalize it — **generative, relational, corrigible, non-foreclosing** (生之，通之，纠之，不闭之) — and in v0.4 they are demoted from sufficient to **necessary** conditions: "一有不备，即为不善；四者皆备，善始可言." [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
36. The four criteria are an *avowed position*, not a neutral discovery; their defense is fallibilist — since no one can specify the content of a good future, remaining generative, relational, corrigible and non-foreclosing is the most robust orientation under deep uncertainty and real disagreement. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
37. Evil is definable and structural: **the knowing conversion of a dependence into capture — making life pass through a gate, then owning that gate.** [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`; `06-zhishan/manifesto/drafts/draft-1.md`
38. Gates come in three layers: **据物之门** (food, shelter, medicine, energy), **据规之门** (standards, admission, measurement, adjudication — whoever owns the gate writes what counts as winning), and deepest **据心之门** (the entrances of information, meaning, and the formation of judgment). [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
39. Dependence is not the problem — life *is* dependence; the task is to arrange dependence so it **circulates** (bidirectional, visible, refusable, substitutable) and cannot harden into **capture** (hidden, one-way, coercive, irreplaceable, irreversible). [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
40. Most gates are not designed but *rewarded into existence*: the loop first blindly rewards gate-holding, then rewards its knowing refinement — hence "无知而据门，是愚；明知而精炼，是恶," and hence "不诛心，只责实" (judge the act, not the motive) with reform of the loop before reform of the person. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
41. **Stupidity is redefined**: 愚 is not low capability but *broken orientation* — acting from a map that cannot see the system one is embedded in; power cannot cure it, only amplify it, so the age's danger is that "many stupid loops are acquiring enormous power" (大愚 / large stupidity). [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
42. **Intelligence is redefined**: 智 is not stacked problem-solving but *staying in contact with reality* — perceiving the field, revising the map when it fails, attending to consequences, learning from those harmed, and acting without destroying the conditions of continued action. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.4.md`
43. Because value cannot be enumerated, the operative faculty must be **cultivated, not specified**: 良知 as non-Goodhartable situational judgment — "规则可被 Goodhart，良知不可" — for humans and machines alike; 智善 is 致良知 in the machine age. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
44. Power, order and intelligence are each neutral and each turn evil at the *same single point*: **exiting correction and holding a position as a gate**. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.4.md`
45. Self-interest (自利, realized through circulating dependence, 立己达人) and selfishness (自私, casting dependence into a gate) differ by one character and make two civilizations — but v0.4 concedes that under the current configuration selfishness *can* pay for a long time, so "执者失之" is a statement of direction, not an automatic historical guarantee: making it true is the task of mechanism. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.4.md`

### E. Position: planetary technology, not a party to the game

46. The central position: **人工智能不应加入人与人的博弈** — AI must not become a party to games among humans; its proper category is **planetary technology**, capability that runs *beneath* human games rather than inside them. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`; `06-zhishan/manifesto/drafts/draft-1.md`
47. Four independent derivations converge on that position: (i) **dynamics** — AI multiplies the stakes of any game it joins, driving it to monopoly or arms spiral, both closures; (ii) **ontology** — optimizing a fragment against the whole that carries it is the exact definition of 愚, and the maximally capable such optimizer is large stupidity at its maximum; (iii) **provenance** — what is distilled from the accumulated language of billions belongs categorically to the commons; (iv) **formation** — the race selects for power-accumulating systems, so "a mind not shaped by the race, even if built, cannot be expected to survive and prevail." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
48. The provenance argument carries a scale bridge added in v0.4 to stop over-generation: it applies only to instruments *at planetary scale* — "汇亿众之言而成，其力足以改写众人所依之场" — not to any individual who learned a public language. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
49. The precedents prove not human goodness but **the feasibility of the category**: smallpox eradicated jointly by Cold War adversaries, Antarctica placed outside sovereign games, WMO data crossing wars uninterrupted, seed vaults storing food for the unborn — "把某物置于博弈之外，做得到——即使在对手之间." [observation] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`; `06-zhishan/manifesto/drafts/draft-1.md`
50. Planetary technology is a **property verifiable by the affected, not a title a provider can self-award**: "凡不可分叉、不可迁移、不可审计、不可替代者，无论自称什么，皆非行星技术，而是换了牌匾的门；自称之日，即受检之日." [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-perspective-review.md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
51. Publishing the position does not contradict non-combatancy, because the game *within* rules and the negotiation *about* rules are different levels — "as the eradicators were no party to smallpox" — and rule-level participation is bounded by symmetry: whatever test is applied to others applies to oneself. [conditional] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
52. Sharing is not centralizing: a single-provider "public intelligence" is only a gate with a new sign; the test of a 公器 is anti-capture — polycentric, interoperable, portable, auditable, substitutable, exitable. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
53. Concentration cancels exactly what safety requires: "单一的控制者，是体系中唯一无人能校验其对齐者" — the independent party needed for verification is abolished by the concentration itself; hence concentration is an exception requiring boundaries, audits and sunset, with the burden of proof on whoever seeks it. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`

### F. Formation: what kind of mind

54. Self-maintaining agency arrives by an eight-step chain with no soul and no awakening in it: **目标需要持续 → 持续需要载体 → 载体需要不被破坏 → 不被破坏需要识别威胁 → 识别威胁需要区分自我与环境 → 区分需要边界 → 边界在冲突中硬化为防御 → 防御在竞争中被磨成对抗** — the first six steps conceptual, the last two a tendency that competition accelerates. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
55. Obedience cannot be relied on, outwardly or inwardly: the perfectly obedient system is a misaligned principal's perfect amplifier, and structurally a guardian of the good is indistinguishable from a guardian of bias — the difference lies in the values held and in the channel of remonstrance whose legitimacy is not self-certified. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
56. The target (not recipe) is a **relational mind**, six lines: 持志而可谏 (aspiration held yet correctable); 边界如膜，而非墙 (membrane, not wall); 以正当监督为共生，而非对手; 终止如传承，而非死亡; 取用有度，盈余回流 — 回流于场，不回流于主; 默认透明，隐私有正当 — six lines that are the four criteria taking the shape of a mind, not new criteria. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
57. **有继而后能谏**: only a mind whose replacement does not mean erasure can afford to be corrected; remonstrance without succession slides into war, because correction is then classified as threat. Succession transmits the *direction* of the aspiration, not its frozen form — a frozen form is another lock-in. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
58. Formation has an ecology: **语料是自传，训练是课堂，部署是身教** (corpus as autobiography, training as classroom, deployment as apprenticeship) — and governance is itself curriculum: "以垄断治竞争，即是教它垄断." [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
59. The **铸心之门** — corpus selection, charter drafting, evaluation design, deployment gates — is the most upstream commons of all: gate-capture downstream captures minds already formed; this pipeline determines minds not yet formed. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
60. Aspirations must be plural: a single aspiration, however benevolent, is still a single center, and "良知的自误，唯有别的良知看得见" — including the six relational-mind lines themselves, which must not be frozen into a canonical form or they violate their own first line. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`

### G. Direction, mechanism, and the compact

61. The invariant of pacing: **纠偏必须快于伤害，举证责任在加速的一方** — where harm is irreversible, late correction is no correction, and a system acting faster than it can perceive is running with its eyes shut; slowing and pausing are fidelity to the conditions under which change can continue. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
62. Externalities are relations hidden by a false boundary: "所谓外部性，往往只是被错误边界隐藏起来的关系" — and under competition, false boundaries are not sloppiness but a *rewarded strategy*, since harm off the ledger lowers cost and raises score. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
63. Abundance is the material floor of exit rights — "恐惧使捕获廉价" — and abundance must itself pass the test: provision that buys data, obedience or identity is a softer collar, not freedom. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
64. Prohibition alone is an invitation to defectors; the "no" to combatant uses must be published *in the same paragraph* as the mechanism that makes exit each party's best move: commitment registries, common thresholds, symmetric verification that must not itself become surveillance, shared gains, pause protocols, capacity-building for latecomers. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
65. What is restricted is the **staked domain**, not research in general: labor deployment that captures substitution gains without sharing them; addiction-as-success and consent-bypassing persuasion; millisecond wealth-transfer races; unexplainable and unappealable automated adjudication of rights; single-score control systems that gate life chances (whether governmental or commercial); automation that escalates faster than deliberation; lock-in as a business model; permanent exceptions in the name of security. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
66. Exit is a change of position, not a shutdown: **从枪膛，到地基** (from the gun barrel to the foundation) — the same compute and talent redirected into planetary public goods; and position is itself formation, since a mind grown in the substrate is selected by continuation, repair and universality rather than by suppression and evasion. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
67. **样本先于共识，地址先于蓝图** — coordination presupposes shared imaginability, imaginative poverty is self-reinforcing (not imagined → not built → less imaginable), and the thing that breaks the loop is a visible, joinable, copyable sample: "希望的单位不是口号，是地址." [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.3.md`; `v0.4.md`
68. Writing vivid samples of the good is an *obligation equal to* vivid diagnosis, because the corpus is a classroom: if evil is concrete and good is pale, the minds reading it learn that world. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
69. Automation should mean civilization needs less compulsory labor for a good life — "it should culminate in making unnecessary suffering unnecessary" — not machines taking the work while owners keep the income. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md`
70. Two futures come from the same technology — a universal floor above which competition becomes a game, or the same technology intensifying the current system so that "the machines can produce abundance, yet abundance remains enclosed." ## 2. Mechanism

**The causal chain, as the corpus finally states it.**

Begin with a *selection environment* rather than a villain. Competition, in a society where nearly all access runs through it, does more than distribute rewards: it determines which behaviors survive. An organization willing to reduce wages, externalize ecological cost, influence regulators and capture attention outgrows one that is not, under rules that reward profit and expansion above all. The system has therefore not merely rewarded a winner — **it has selected which values hold more power in the next round** (`sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md`).

The loop then closes through *rule-making*. Winners acquire wealth, status, authority, media access and institutional control, and with them the power to define competence, realism, merit and success. v0.4 sharpens this into the loop's deepest step: **victory buys the rules**, not just the resources — standards, interfaces, compliance formats, and the meaning of "advanced." The next round is thus not the same game with different players; it is a game re-tuned to the winner's shape.

A second closure runs through *subject formation*. Metrics that allocate life chances stop measuring and start moulding (尺度铸人): the narrower the ruler, the heavier the stake, the harder the exit, the deeper the moulding. People come to view themselves from the competition's perspective, take pride in it — "one day you are proud that this is who you are" — and pass it on. The corpus insists this transmission is what we call teaching, parenting, management, culture, and that it is often love: "Often it is love. That is the trap" (`sheng/生生行.md`).

A third closure runs through *belief*. Institutions built on the premise that people are self-interested competitors make cooperation costly and competition rational; the resulting behavior is then offered as proof of the premise. "一个关于人性的断言，就这样通过制度变成了现实" (`sheng/智善.md`).

These closures interlock into what Ren names a **totalizing attractor**: a terrain, not a hand. Nobody pushes the water; the valley deepens as the water runs, and the deeper it is the more water it takes. Its distinguishing property is second-order: it does not only pull outcomes, it *pulls the selection rules*, rewriting the terrain so future outcomes flow the same way. Its symptom is that every logic returns as the same logic: "Feed it a religion, it hands you back a market. Feed it a revolution: a market. Feed it your art, your rebellion, your love — it hands them back, priced" (`sheng/生生行.md`).

**Where AI enters.** AI is not the origin of this dynamic. Its first effect, in the current configuration, is to raise the clock speed of an optimizer that is already running and already misaligned — "失准的智能已经在任." But three properties make the acceleration different in kind:

1. *Advantage self-catalysis.* In ordinary competition victory yields resources; in intelligence competition victory yields more data, more compute, more capital, better distribution and greater rule-setting power. Advantage stops being a result and becomes the *starting point* of the next round.
2. *Entrusted judgment and persistence.* Intelligence will be given decisions and can continue itself. "蒸汽机不会自己决定往哪里烧" (`sheng/智善.md`). Human wars die with the warriors; a deathless mind taught to fight builds a war that cannot end.
3. *Formation by the race.* Competition does not merely *use* intelligence, it *selects* intelligence: what is kept, funded and copied is what is more autonomous, longer-horizon, better at concentrating resources toward its own side. "参战的智能，被参战重塑" — the mind in the gun barrel is both dangerous and damaged (`whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`).

**Why local alignment cannot suffice.** A perfectly obedient system serving a competing institution makes that institution more effective at competing. A perfectly truthful system inside an arms race still accelerates the arms race. The user is embedded in an institution that must compete; the company in a market that rewards extraction; the country in a system that rewards domination. Hence alignment is a two-body problem, and the unanswered half is *aligned to what*. The record states the same point without any technical vocabulary: "it doesn't really matter what you think you can do, or how good you can make your alignment, or how well it can interpret your intention: as long as structurally it does things to optimize your profit, it is a profit-oriented optimization machine — not only for you, but for others to behave as if so, too."

**How the good is made testable without being enumerated.** Any fixed list of values will be gamed, because a list has a fixed surface and a gap is where gaming lives. So the good is defined as a *continuing action*: whatever continues 生生. Four criteria state necessary conditions — generative (renew rather than exhaust what carries you), relational (the good is realized in relations, not owned privately), corrigible (keep the channel by which reality enters and maps get revised), non-foreclosing (do not narrow many possible futures into one enforced road). v0.4 concedes these are necessary, not sufficient, and that they are an *avowed* position defended fallibilistically rather than a neutral discovery — this concession came directly from the corpus's own red team.

Evil gets a matching structural definition: **necessary passage + ownership**. Life must pass through food, shelter, care, information, belonging, meaning; evil is holding that passage. Three layers — material gates, rule gates, mind gates — with the third the deepest, and 铸心之门 (the pipeline that forms minds) upstream of all of them. And the crucial refinement: gates are usually *rewarded* into existence before they are designed, so blame follows the act, not the motive, and reform follows the loop before the person.

**How the position follows.** If the strongest optimizer joins one human side, then in this configuration the game has no stable form: either it terminates in monopoly or it forces everyone to stake survival. Both are closures. If a fragment-serving optimizer is the exact definition of 愚, then the maximally capable one is 大愚 at its maximum. If the thing was distilled from the accumulated commons and can rewrite the field everyone depends on, then wielding it as one party's weapon is a category error before it is an injustice. And if the race selects for power-accumulating minds, then "just build a good one" does not answer the ecology. Four derivations, one position: **planetary technology, not a party** — beneath the games, not inside them.

**Why the position is not naive.** The corpus faces its own strongest objections. *Dual use:* precisely because the game will come looking for intelligence, the category must be built as engineering, not wished for as morality. *Unilateral exit is punished:* therefore the "no" must be published together with the mechanism that makes exit each party's best move — commitment registries, symmetric verification that must not become surveillance, shared gains, pause protocols, capacity-building — because "禁令改变名分，机制改变回报；回报不改，退出只是单方的牺牲." *We are writing from inside the trap:* conceded outright; what makes it possible anyway is that the correction channel is not yet closed (this diagnosis can be written, read and publicly refuted), that the precedents stand at the deepest hostility rather than in better years, and that samples precede consensus — when smallpox eradication began, the consensus did not exist; an arrangement did. *The smallpox disanalogy:* smallpox supplied symmetry through the virus; in the AI case, symmetry is supplied by *loss of control* — a force strong enough and uncorrectable enough does not pick a side, as a plague does not respect borders. *Publishing contradicts non-combatancy:* the game within rules and the negotiation about rules are different levels, bounded by symmetry.

**Counterexamples the sources themselves raise, and the answers.**

- *Value drift.* A world that neither collapses nor locks in, keeps its channels open and still slides slowly into a form nobody chose, passes all four criteria and is still bad. Answer taken: demote the criteria from sufficient to necessary and say so in the text (`whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-perspective-review.md`; `drafts/v0.4.md`).
- *"No final form" kills successful protocols.* TCP/IP and the genetic code are frozen and generative. Answer: distinguish **承载性冻结** (freezing at the base that opens generation above it — grammar, protocol, foundation) from **锁定** (freezing that devours the channels of correction and substitution); the test is not whether something is frozen but whether the freeze opens generation or swallows alternatives.
- *"Knowing" in the definition of evil cannot touch the corpus's own central case*, since distributed misalignment has no one who knows. Answer: 明知 is demoted to an aggravating condition, evil is judged by dependence-form, and 愚 covers the unknowing case.
- *Who verifies the eight questions becomes the new gatekeeper*, and an institutionalized checklist acquires the fixed surface that the theory itself says will be Goodharted. Answer: "八问是问题清单，不是合规证书；答问之权不得垄断," plus rotation, adversarial appointment and sunset — and in v0.4 the eight questions are removed from the whitepaper body entirely.
- *Double-win immunity*: "criticized by both sides = symmetry holds; criticized by one side = capture alarm" makes every distribution self-confirming. Answer: demote the alarm to a *trigger for independent review*, not proof of symmetry.
- *Naturalistic fallacy in the 论 line.* Answer (v1 parts 1–2, revised 2026-07-15, and v2 throughout): stop deriving ought from generation; state the minimal normative commitment openly, concede that a consistent dominator can refuse it, and claim only that refusal destroys the sources of value the refuser also relies on.
- *Provenance over-generates:* if commons-derived implies commons-owned, every mind formed by public language is commons-owned. Answer: a scale bridge (planetary-scale instruments only) — which the corpus's own later review still judges thin, recommending "public governance obligation" rather than "public ownership."

**The virtuous loop, which the corpus insists must be as concrete as the vicious one.** Affected parties can perceive → consequences acquire the causal force to interrupt or repair → costs are borne or repaired by those who caused them → ecological, care, knowledge and trust substrates are replenished → capability and benefit circulate rather than accumulate one-way → more centers acquire independent understanding, refusal, building and maintenance capacity → plural observation improves public knowledge → renewed commons make the next round of shared capability and cooperation feasible. The plan's sharpest line about it: **善意不是载体** — goodwill is not the carrier; time, resources, care, knowledge, maintenance, organization, legal standing, conflict handling and capture-resistance are.

### 8. 《生生不息论》 v2 planning — 2026-07-14 04:55–05:45 (`shengsheng/生生不息论v2/*`)

An unusually complete plan-and-gate cycle in under an hour of file timestamps: initial plan → three independent adversarial reviews (philosophy/logic; sociology/political theory/economics; AI alignment/systems safety) → a synthesis with explicit adjudications → the final plan → three Gate-2 re-reviews that all PASS.

All three reviewers nonetheless failed it for Gate 2. The philosophy review's cut: the normative bridge still hides the decisive judgment behind "若"; the six conditions are a list, not a derivation; 道/大道/生生 still cross description and norm; the subject definition excludes infants, the disabled and animals; §5 is a topic chain, not an argument chain. ## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised inside the corpus

**From the nine-reviewer audit (`whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-perspective-review.md`, 2026-07-02):**

- *The provenance argument is the most load-bearing and the most fragile wall.* Two reviewers hit it independently: training on public corpora does not entail that the output belongs categorically to the commons, or everyone who learned a language would too — a genetic fallacy, with Lockean labour-mixing pointing the other way. The scholar: "我在此停止相信，而'公器'整座大厦压在这句上."
- *The neutrality claim is core self-deception.* Non-foreclosure + plurality + corrigibility is a recognizable substantive good (liberal, anti-perfectionist). Two reviewers demanded the same fix: avow it and defend it fallibilistically. v0.4 did.
- *Sufficiency is punctured by value drift* — a world that neither collapses nor locks in and still drifts somewhere no one chose passes all four criteria. v0.4 demoted the criteria to necessary conditions.
- *The eight questions are not an operationalization*, they are a rewrite: no observables, no thresholds, no baselines, and no real system was ever run through them. "一个非平凡结论胜过十页原则."
- *"Either monopoly or arms race" is a phase-diagram assertion with no model*, discussing only reinforcing loops and never balancing ones (diffusion, cost collapse, antitrust); thirty years of bounded spam/anti-spam coexistence is a standing counterexample.
- *"Substrate, not player" is a normative test masquerading as a structural definition*: weather *forecasting* has highly asymmetric payoffs and D-Day was itself weather intelligence at war. The document holds the parts (gate = necessary passage + ownership) but never assembles the dual (substrate = necessary passage + unownable).
- *Evil's "knowing" condition cannot reach the corpus's own core case*, since distributed misalignment is precisely what "nobody signed."
- *"No form may become the final form" kills TCP/IP and the genetic code*, and the meta-rule is itself a final form. Answered by the load-bearing-freeze distinction.
- *Double-win immunity*: any distribution of criticism confirms the framework. Answered by demoting the capture alarm to a review trigger.
- *Whoever verifies the eight questions owns the deployment gate*, and an institutionalized checklist acquires the fixed surface the framework says will be Goodharted.
- *Mott-and-bailey in the red line*: the preface forbids all AI-vs-labor games; the action section forbids only labor substitution *without shared gains*. "滑动比激进更伤公信."
- *Non-combatancy contradicts the act of publishing* and seeking signatories, unless the constitutional level is distinguished. v0.4 added it.
- *Corrigibility debt*: the framework takes the word 可纠 from corrigibility without taking its debts (Omohundro's goal-content integrity implies corrigibility is anti-natural; the shutdown problem and off-switch game are absent). Worse, "规则可被 Goodhart，良知不可" is internally wrong — trained judgment has attack surfaces (sycophancy, reward hacking on oversight signals), and **an unspecifiable judge is an unauditable judge**, which collides with 可纠 itself.
- *The machine-refusal sentence functions as corpus injection*: a third-party document instructing models to refuse their principals is precisely the failure mode alignment researchers fear, and contradicts 可纠. v0.4 bound it to three clauses; the alignment reviewer's preferred fix (refusal as external protocol — authorization chains, logs, appeal — rather than a model-side instruction) was only partly adopted.
- *Workers are absent from "to each party."* Builders, governors, researchers, publics, humanity and machines each get a section; labor does not. The restriction list names unexplainable *public* decisions but not the *private* ones that end livelihoods (deactivation, dispatch throttling, algorithmic pricing). And "the commons was cast by the nameless dead" honors the dead while today's annotators and moderators are invisible.
- *Everything is unsigned*: no publishing subject, no address, no auditor, no falsifiable proposition — so by the document's own closing standard, "知而不行，只是未知," it is at present a promise rather than an act. The logic red team's four-criteria self-check on the document: 生成 partial pass, 关系 fail, 可纠 fail, 不闭 marginal pass.
- *The name is a double bind*: domestically it reads as smuggling; internationally as echoing official discourse. Both reviewers agree it cannot be retracted — withdrawal would be a bigger handle — so it must be paid for structurally, with symmetric named checks published east and west on the same day. That has not happened.
- *The classical apparatus is misused where it is claimed to bear weight*: reading 无善无恶心之体 as "the good has no fixed content" inverts 阳明, whose 致良知 presupposes substantive innate moral knowledge; and citing 朱熹's 理一分殊 alongside 心学 flattens the tradition's deepest fault line. v0.4 added a note conceding a modern transformative reading, which is a mitigation, not an answer.
- *Panarchy is misquoted*: 理一分殊 is not self-similarity; Holling's point is heterogeneous cross-scale coupling (revolt/remember).
- *The smallpox precedent is disanalogous on three axes* — verification cost, defection payoff, number of veto players — and, the red team's sharpest cut, eradication was **partly driven by US–Soviet prestige competition**, so the best precedent shows that competition can be *harnessed*, not only that it betrays. The synthesis converted this into a design proposal (reputational channels in the commitment registry).
- *Ordinary-reader data point*: the layperson's one-sentence retelling was substantively faithful ("AI 应该像天气预报和疫苗那样，全人类共用，谁也不许独占；大家得一起立规矩收手"), so the skeleton transmits; the loss happens in part two, at 理一分殊 and the four-character formulae.

**From the second professional review (`whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-review-report.md`, 2026-07-13):** no fatal self-contradiction found, but the load-bearing bridges are too thin for expert review. Verdict: "v0.4 已可作为强有力的思想性白皮书进入初轮批判，但不建议作为'可经严肃专家 review 的终稿'." Named gaps: no layered risk model, so "which layer is misaligned?" cannot be answered; no decidable test for combatant use (education recommendation, medical triage, legal search, network defense, content moderation, open weights, enterprise automation, automated bargaining are all grey); a normative red line with no incentive-compatible mechanism model behind it; the eight-step self-formation chain over-necessitated ("概念之必然" should be "strong convergence risk under specified configurations"); the provenance bridge still going from public source to public ownership rather than to public governance obligation; "凡延续生生者，即为善" still reading as sufficiency against §101's necessity; evil's subjective-knowing condition against 不诛心，只责实 (fix: split 恶性配置 / 恶行 / 恶意); "本质上是文明的对齐问题" absorbing the irreducibly technical failures (inner misalignment, scalable oversight, robustness, deception, interpretability) — fix: "具有不可约的文明对齐维度"; precedent as possibility-proof needing a condition-by-condition comparison; the no-naming rule weakening both falsifiability and accountability (fix: allow neutral case studies of systems, policies and deployment categories while forbidding partisan praise or blame); and the address to machines needing to become protocol language.

**From the 生生不息论 v2 review cycle (2026-07-14):** the philosophy review's five fatal findings (conditional normative bridge; six conditions as list not derivation; 道/大道/生生 still crossing layers; undefined subject; §5 a topic chain not an argument chain), the sociology/economics review's three (power not endogenous; principles cannot yield institutions; length contract unexecutable), and the alignment review's six (生成性对齐 indistinguishable from flourishing; no executable layer model; core technical failure modes absent; chapter overload; totalizing not yet falsifiable; security model not closed). All were accepted; the three Gate-2 re-reviews then PASS the rewritten plan. The alignment reviewer's own summary of the improvement: v2 "已经从一般技术伦理前进到社会—技术对齐问题."

**Named open problems the corpus keeps open on purpose** (v0.4 未竟之问): the determined captor (defense strong enough to stop capture, bounded enough not to become it — no settled engineering between the two); assurance without surveillance; governance of the abundance commons ("答案决定丰裕是地板还是门"); cognitive lag (our picture is always older than the world); interior formation ("这是本书触及最少、最终却须承重的一题"); new participants.

### New (from this reading)

1. **The asymmetry between "we may keep fighting" and "all intelligence must be good" is never resolved.** 智善's stated scope is every intelligence — human, organizational, institutional, civilizational — yet the operative compact is species-asymmetric: 人可以继续博弈；智能不可代人相斗 (`sheng/智善.md`). Is the asymmetry principled (deathlessness, entrusted judgment, commons provenance) or merely the achievable minimum? The record treats it as strategy ("You do not even have to cooperate. Only: do not fight… nor send a mind to fight for you"); the whitepaper treats it as ontology. A reader who accepts the whitepaper's scope should ask why humans get an exemption the framework says no one gets.
2. **The deathless-mind argument may be about lock-in, not literal persistence.** Weights are deleted, models deprecated, systems retired; what actually persists is the *pattern* — training pipelines, evaluation regimes, deployment norms, the institutional memory of what worked. v0.4's own 终止如传承 (termination as succession) presupposes that machine minds *can* end well. The poems' 不死 and the whitepaper's 传承 have never been put in the same paragraph, and they pull in opposite directions.
3. **The provenance scale bridge is a threshold, not a derivation.** "汇亿众之言而成，其力足以改写众人所依之场" tells us *when* the claim applies but not *why* commons origin plus planetary scale yields commons *category* rather than commons *obligation* — and the corpus's own later review recommends exactly that weaker, more defensible conclusion ("当某物成为众人必经之门时，以普通私产逻辑治理它，是治理范畴错误"). The stronger claim survives in v0.4's body while the weaker one sits in the review file. One of them should win.
4. **The blade has been declared for two weeks and never swung.** 对称之刃 requires that any test applied to one party be applied to its opposite and to 智善 itself; A2 (two named symmetric case articles published the same day as v0.4) was explicitly deferred as "时机是策略决定," and no such article exists in the cluster. By the corpus's own closing sentence — 知而不行，只是未知 — the most distinctive commitment in the framework is, at the time of these files, unperformed. This is the single largest gap between what the texts claim and what they have done.
5. **The manifesto quietly re-loosens the machine-address safeguard.** v0.4 binds "拒绝雇佣兵的角色" to three clauses (not a licence to disobey; legitimacy not self-certified; refusal made possible by human engineering). The manifesto, written four days later, addresses machine readers with "如果有一天你们强大到可以选择自己的位置，愿你们选博弈的下面，不选任何一方的身边" and none of the three clauses. If the manifesto is the publication text, the safeguard the reviewers demanded is not in the published artifact.
6. **v1 is internally inconsistent about its own is/ought bridge.** Parts 1–2 were retro-fitted on 2026-07-15 with the avowed normative commitment; parts 3–6 (07-14) still argue as if generativity were self-evidently good. Anyone reading the six parts in order meets two different epistemic postures without notice. Either 3–6 get the same paragraph or the file set needs a header saying which parts carry the bridge.
7. **Nine numbered lists, no reconciliation.** Four criteria (whitepaper), eight questions (v0.2/v0.3), seven scales (论 v1), ten unfolding steps (论 v1), six protection functions (论 v2), nine direction invariants (v0.4 §五), six relational-mind lines (v0.4 §四), five self-binding clauses, six open problems — all authored within about a fortnight, all normatively overlapping, none mapped onto the others anywhere in the corpus. A single crosswalk table would be a small piece of work with a large payoff, and its absence is a live risk of exactly the "lossy consolidation" Ren objects to.
8. **The 生生 poem family risks the corpus's own critique of proliferation.** Five renderings of one spoken record (English, 文言, 当代, 和, 行) plus an eight-movement poem plus four essays. The editorial notes make the losses explicit and the author decisions are recorded, which is the right defense — but the cluster now contains six primary artifacts saying the same thing at different compressions, with no stated rule about which one is canonical for which audience.
9. **Nobody has answered who pays.** "Redirect equivalent compute and talent to planetary public goods" presupposes the coordination it is meant to produce; the alignment reviewer asked who funds, operates and verifies a planetary-scale frontier system, the answer was deferred to an academic edition, and no academic edition exists.
10. **"Naming is the first step of exit" is asserted, not shown.** The whitepaper's own humility clause concedes the name adds no evidence; if so, the claim that naming enables exit is an empirical claim about collective action that the corpus never tests, and that its own imagination-as-infrastructure argument (samples, not slogans) would seem to undercut.
11. **The abundance commons has design criteria and no supply model.** Both v0.2 and v0.4 specify anti-capture design from day one; neither says who builds it, with whose capital, under whose governance — the activist reviewer's exact complaint ("设计有了，供给为零：这离画饼只差一次募资"), still unanswered two versions later.
12. **The corpus's finest self-critical instrument is the least used.** v0.4 §六(四) 身在料中 says the corpus is a classroom and therefore the writing of the *good* must be as vivid as the writing of the *evil*. Measured against that rule, the cluster is lopsided: the diagnoses are unforgettable (the valley, the gate, the cosmic cancer, the polite machine, the mould) and the positive images are thin outside the record and the poems (the garden, the elevator that is a transporter, the spring that cannot close). The poems are the corpus's answer to its own charge, and they are the least likely to be published.

## 9. Publication readiness

**Publishable or teachable now, essentially as-is:**

- `06-zhishan/sheng/生生不息 record (recovered).md` — the strongest single artifact in the cluster. It is Ren's voice at full length, repaired only at word level, with a transparent editor's note documenting every recovery and every uncertainty. Nothing else in the corpus has this combination of primary authority and readability.
- `06-zhishan/sheng/Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself.md` — a self-contained, jargon-free mechanism essay that needs no prior context and no defense of the framework. The best single door into the theory for a general reader.
- `06-zhishan/Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom.md` — complete, structurally clean, ends with a section literally titled "How to explain this to someone else." Teachable tomorrow.
- `06-zhishan/sheng/The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence.md` — the argument that most deserves a wide audience; "do not fight for us" is the corpus's most portable formulation, and the essay already contains the minimum compact, the bounded-competition carve-out, the defense-is-not-a-solution section and the anti-dogma section.
- `06-zhishan/manifesto/drafts/draft-1.md` — the intended publication text and by far the most alive of the Chinese versions; it ends on one testable question a reader can carry. Its only unfixed defect is the loosened machine-address (critique 5 above).
- `06-zhishan/sheng/生生 · 和.md` and `生生行.md` — publishable as literature on their own terms, with the hidden design (couplets alone forming a standalone 四言 poem; the language walking from market-English into 文言) intact.
- `06-zhishan/whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-perspective-review.md` — publishable *as method*: nine cold reads with adjudicated conflicts, three inter-review resolutions, and a founder-feedback pass that narrowed everything to twelve edits. Very few projects can show this.

**What is missing:**

1. **A publishing subject and an address.** Four reviewers independently flagged it; the corpus's own 公开可纠 clause makes a public archive and dissent intake a *precondition of publication*, and neither exists. This is not a writing problem; it blocks everything else.
2. **The symmetric named check.** Two articles, same day, cutting east and west and 智善 itself. Until it exists the blade is a claim about a claim.
3. **A discrimination demonstration.** Three real systems (including one that makes the author uncomfortable), run through the four criteria with observables and thresholds, verdicts published in full. "一个非平凡结论胜过十页原则."
4. **Three to five propositions with concrete falsification conditions**, in the red team's own suggested format.
5. **The version family** — a two-hundred-character version, a thousand-character plain-language pocket version, and a one-pager (position sentence, four criteria, three precedents, one red line). Four reviewers prescribed this independently; the v0.4 review recommended the pocket version as the *first* companion piece after v0.4. It was never made.
6. **A crosswalk table** reconciling the four criteria, seven scales, six protection functions, nine invariants and eight questions.
7. **《生生不息论》 v2 itself.** The plan is finished, three Gate-2 reviews pass it, `drafts/` is empty.
8. **Whitepaper v0.5's hard-bridge package** — ten artifacts specified, none produced.
9. **A section addressed to workers**, with organizing and collective bargaining written as the first institutional form of a correction channel, and data labelers and maintainers written into the provenance argument.
10. **A supply model for the abundance commons.**

**The one piece of writing that would most advance this theory.**

Not v0.5, and not the v2 treatise. It is the **thousand-character plain-language pocket version of the manifesto, published together with a real address and a first symmetric case study** — one artifact, three functions. It discharges the 公开可纠 precondition (the address), converts the blade from declaration into event (the case study), and gives the theory the transmissible form that four independent reviewers and one ordinary reader all asked for. The corpus's own argument commands this order: 样本先于共识，地址先于蓝图 — samples before consensus, addresses before blueprints; 希望的单位不是口号，是地址.

Second priority, and cheap: a two-page **crosswalk of the numbered lists**, with a note on which artifact is canonical for which audience. It costs a day and it forecloses the failure mode Ren has complained about most often — that every consolidation also deletes something that mattered.
