# Human Solutions Museum (人类解决问题的方法博物馆 / 文明方法博物馆)

> Civilization is not a sequence of clever objects; it is an accumulated stock of *methods* by which humans converted real problems into workable mechanisms. Most of that stock is unreadable today, because we archive inventions as results rather than as problem-solving processes — so creators, activists, policy designers and founders keep re-deriving structures humanity already worked out, and keep failing in the same two ways: asserting a value without designing a mechanism, or shipping a mechanism without understanding the problem it lands in. The Human Solutions Museum is a living online museum that re-archives tangible and intangible inventions alike as seven-part dossiers — the real problem, how people at the time misread it, the key insight, the mechanism it became, how it diffused, what it solved, what new problems it created, and the method you can borrow today. Its interaction thesis is one sentence: 用户带着一个问题进入，沿着人类历史上的相似问题旅行，最后带走一种可迁移的方法 ("the user arrives with a problem, travels through structurally similar problems in human history, and leaves with a transferable method"). It refuses to sanctify inventions (every dossier must show the harms it produced), refuses to be an encyclopedia (the exit of every exhibit is a method, not a fact), and refuses to launder unreviewed prototype copy as authoritative history (a machine-enforced boundary between `prototypeReady` and `publicationReady`). Its governance is an open, thresholded loop — 开放提名、公共讨论、策展审核、持续修订 — and its deepest users are not visitors but people who intend to change something.

## 1. Core claims

**Positioning and diagnosis**

1. The project is not an "invention encyclopedia" (发明大全) nor an encyclopedic website displaying great inventions; it is 一座收录人类曾经如何解决真实问题的活博物馆 — "a living museum collecting how humanity once solved real problems." [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
2. The selection question is not how novel an invention was but whether it 推动了人类人文文明往前一步 — moved humanistic civilization one step forward. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
3. Therefore the essence of the museum is 一个"文明方法库" — a civilizational method library, not a collection. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
4. The reason it is needed: many social movements, public campaigns, product innovations and inventions today are 重复造轮子 — reinventing the wheel. [observation] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
5. Failure mode A: people propose good value claims but design no landable mechanism (提出了很好的价值主张，但没有设计出可以落地的机制). [observation] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
6. Failure mode B: people build new tools without actually understanding the social problem they face. [observation] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
7. Failure mode C: people believe they face a new problem when humanity has repeatedly met problems of *similar structure* and developed solutions at several different levels. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
8. The corrective is to reorganize historical experience so people see not only 结果 (results) but 问题、洞察、机制、扩散、代价和可迁移方法 (problem, insight, mechanism, diffusion, cost, transferable method). [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
9. The intervention point is *before* action: help people think more clearly before inventing something, pushing a social change, designing an institution, or proposing a new idea. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`

**What counts as an invention**

10. Tangible and intangible creations are collected on equal terms; the intangible list (文字、学校、图书馆、法律、货币、民主制度、科学方法、心理治疗、版权、公共卫生制度、合作社、开源协作、无障碍设计、人权观念) are 同样是文明发明 — "equally civilizational inventions." [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
11. Intangible inventions count because they changed how humans organize society, preserve knowledge, build trust, express pain, coordinate action and distribute rights. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
12. Tangible objects are admitted not because they are objects but because 它们把某类人类问题转化成了更可处理、更可传播、更可使用的形式 — they converted a class of human problem into a more tractable, transmissible, usable form. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
13. Five admission criteria: does it answer a real and important human problem; does it change the basic way humans handle that problem; does it expand 表达、选择、尊严、自由、安全、协作或理解能力 (expression, choice, dignity, freedom, safety, cooperation, or understanding); does it create a method later people can transfer, reuse or reflect on; did it have durable influence on later institutions, tools, ideas or social structures. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
14. Admission is explicitly not restricted to "wholly positive" inventions: ambivalent ones may enter, but must be presented completely — both what they solved and what new problems they made. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`

**Exhibit form: the dossier**

15. Exhibits are displayed as 问题解决档案 (problem-solving dossiers), never as 物品说明书 (object spec sheets). [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
16. Reframing thesis: the value of many great inventions lies not only in giving an answer but in 重新定义了问题 — redefining the problem; so the museum must record how the problem was previously misunderstood, suppressed, ignored, or moralized. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
17. What pushes civilization forward is usually not the technology itself but a new insight (真正推动文明前进的，往往不是技术本身，而是一个新的洞察). [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
18. Two named insight archetypes: some grand social problems must be converted into tools ordinary people can use autonomously; some problems treated as personal failures actually require public institutions to carry them. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
19. The museum foregrounds mechanism rather than story, and enumerates the possible mechanism substrates: 材料、制度、教育、标准化、传播网络、法律保护、社会运动、商业化 (materials, institutions, education, standardization, diffusion networks, legal protection, social movements, commercialization). [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
20. 一个好想法不等于一个有效改变 — a good idea is not an effective change; diffusion must be interrogated separately, including resistance and whether spread came from the state, the market, grassroots transmission, or was forced by a crisis. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
21. Any civilizational advance may have side effects; the museum must not sanctify inventions but show their complexity. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
22. The transferable-method module is 本馆最重要的部分 — the most important part; each exhibit must end in a method useful today, not merely historical knowledge. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
23. Exhibit titles must carry explanatory power rather than only a name, e.g. 避孕套：把宏大的公共卫生问题，变成一个人可以随身携带的选择. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
24. The web spec fixes the dossier at eight modules: 它遇到的难题 / 当时人们误以为问题是什么 / 关键洞察 / 它把问题变成了什么机制 / 它如何扩散 / 它解决了什么 / 它制造了什么新问题 / 今天可以借走的方法. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
25. Those eight become machine-enforced canonical section keys — `problem, misreading, insight, mechanism, diffusion, solved, new-problems, method` — with `normalizeContent` required to backfill them and the validator required to block any exhibit missing one. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`

**The anchor case: the condom**

26. The condom is treated not as an object invention but as a response to a bundle of problems: the tight binding of sex to pregnancy, STI risk, women's bodily autonomy, power asymmetry in intimacy, family structure and fertility choice, and 公共卫生系统难以进入的私人场景 (private scenes public-health systems cannot enter). [observation] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
27. Its civilizational significance is the conversion move: it turned a complex problem spanning body, desire, disease, shame, intimacy and public health into 一个低成本、可携带、可复制、可个人自主使用的工具 — a low-cost, portable, replicable, individually self-usable tool. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
28. The transferable method extracted from it is stated as a conditional design rule: 如果一个问题发生在高度私人、羞耻、分散、难以监管的场景里，那么解决方案必须足够低成本、私密、可自主使用，并且不要求人先改变成理想中的样子 — if a problem occurs in highly private, shameful, dispersed, hard-to-regulate settings, the solution must be low-cost, private, autonomously usable, and must not require people to first become their ideal selves. [conditional] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
29. General value stance derived from it: good civilizational tools usually do not demand that people first become more noble; they offer a better option 在真实的人性、真实的处境、真实的限制中 — within real human nature, real situations, real constraints. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
30. The condom is designated the v0.1 气质锚点 (temperament anchor) and the quality baseline for information density and visual treatment; `condom` must reach anchor quality before v0.1 is done. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`

**Information architecture**

31. The museum is organized by 人类问题 (human problems), not by timeline, country, discipline or technology class; the structure is a 问题星图 / 文明地图 (problem star map / civilization map). [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
32. The concept doc proposes twelve thematic halls, including 人类如何保存记忆、传播知识、降低暴力、扩大信任、组织大规模协作、保护身体自主权、让弱者也能发声、把羞耻问题变成公共问题、把道德诉求变成制度流程、治疗痛苦、承认差异、把专家能力封装成普通人可用的工具. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
33. The web spec narrows v0.1 to eight galleries: 记忆、传播、身体、信任、协作、发声、照护、公平 (memory, transmission, body, trust, cooperation, voice, care, fairness). [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
34. One exhibit may appear in several problem galleries; the data must distinguish `primaryGalleryId` from `galleryAppearances` to keep routing, filtering and breadcrumb semantics coherent. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
35. Galleries are linked to each other by *method relations* rather than by category — 低成本复制、私人场景接口、公共制度承接、专家能力普及、陌生人协作、风险转移、权利重新定义 — so users see deep structure instead of isolated inventions. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
36. Adjacency between galleries is not to be authored as data in v0.1 but derived in the domain layer: shared method first, shared exhibit second, an exhibit's `similarStructures` reaching another gallery third. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
37. Cross-exhibit recommendations are made on *problem structure*, not discipline — the condom links to vaccines, anonymous help hotlines, accessible design and public-health systems 不是因为它们属于同一学科，而是因为它们处理了相似的问题结构. [mechanism] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
38. Similarity links must be typed, not loose: every `similarStructures` entry needs `relationType`, `dimension` and `confidence`, checked by the validator. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/content-engineering-audit.md`

**The interaction thesis and the site**

39. The site must not be an ordinary "online museum homepage" nor an "inventions list" but 一件可交互的文明思考工具 — an interactive civilizational thinking tool. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
40. The core experience is defined in one sentence: 用户带着一个问题进入，沿着人类历史上的相似问题旅行，最后带走一种可迁移的方法. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
41. The intended felt result is de-isolation: 原来我现在面对的问题，并不是孤立的。人类以前也在类似结构里挣扎过。我可以从历史里借一种方法，而不是从零开始乱撞 — "my problem is not isolated; humans have struggled inside similar structures; I can borrow a method from history instead of blundering from zero." [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
42. The axis is 问题到方法 (problem → method), explicitly not 发明到知识点 (invention → factoid). [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
43. The homepage's first line must be 你现在想解决什么问题？ ("what problem do you want to solve right now?") — never a "welcome to the museum" opener; marketing-style heroes, long project blurbs and passive-visitor entrances are forbidden. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
44. The user is framed as 带着问题进入博物馆的人 — someone entering the museum carrying a problem — not a passive visitor. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`
45. What users collect in the 文明工具箱 (civilizational toolbox) is *methods*, not exhibits, because the intent is that they leave with thinking tools rather than historical knowledge; engineering red line: favorites store only `method.id`. 46. Reverse search — entering from one's own problem description, e.g. 我想解决一个羞耻、隐私、难以公开求助的问题 — is realized in v0.1 only by preset entry prompts, with free-text, semantic and AI-recommended search explicitly deferred. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/content-engineering-audit.md`
47. The nomination form is itself a thinking tool: it must demand problem, common misreading, key insight, mechanism, observable impact, side effects and transferable method rather than name + blurb. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md`

The method library is a first-class object, not a by-product: the methods page's primary entity is `methods`, not exhibits, and methods must read as borrowable action principles, not keywords mechanically extracted from exhibit titles. **Living museum and governance**

55. The museum is not finished once: it must be open, continuously updated, nominable and discussable — 任何人都可以自荐或推荐某个发明、制度、方法、观念或社会创造. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
56. 但开放不等于无门槛 — openness does not mean no threshold; every candidate must answer the six dossier questions to be considered. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
57. The governance loop is 开放提名、公共讨论、策展审核、持续修订 (open nomination, public discussion, curatorial review, continuous revision), producing something that is neither a rigid authority nor an arbitrary submission platform but 一个不断生长的文明知识系统 — a continuously growing civilizational knowledge system. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`
58. Publication is a state machine, not a boolean: `proposed → drafted → fact-checking → sensitivity-review → published → revised`. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`
59. Content status is split so prototype completeness cannot be mistaken for publication readiness: `contentStatus.prototypeReady: true`, `contentStatus.publicationReady: false`. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/content-engineering-audit.md`
60. Content without sources, review and visual licensing may be used only as prototype copy and must never be published in the voice of a formal factual archive. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`, `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
61. The prototype/public boundary is machine-enforced: `VITE_CONTENT_MODE=public` with `contentStatus.publicationReady !== true` must *fail the build*, rather than emit an empty site or a formal-looking site carrying unreviewed content. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
62. `public` mode admits an exhibit only if `publicationReady === true`, `reviewStatus.facts === "approved"`, `reviewStatus.sensitivity === "approved"`, at least two reliable sources exist, and any displayed key visual has `src`, `alt`, `credit`, `license`; reliable means `primary`/`secondary`/`reference`, and `uncertain` sources do not count toward the threshold. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
63. Prototype wording rules: may say 原型内容 / 种子展品 / 待补来源; must not say 权威档案 / 已考据 / 正式馆藏; sensitive exhibits must use restrained public-discussion language. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
64. The nomination form must not simulate a real submission pipeline: no server, no review promise, local draft only, with the UI stating that it is a thinking-tool form; likewise the living-museum page must not imply a review backend already exists. [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md`
65. Sources must live in a top-level `sources` table referenced by `evidence.sourceIds`, 不要把来源散落在正文里 — never scattered inside prose. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`
66. Before public release each exhibit needs key historical nodes, reliable sources, points of dispute, over-generalizations to avoid, and citable further reading. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`
67. The admission criteria must eventually be rewritten as executable curation rules (real problem / changed the basic handling / expanded capability / yields a transferable method / fully presents side effects and disputes). [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/content-assets-production.md`

## 2. Mechanism

**The underlying model of how civilization advances.** The museum encodes a specific theory, not just a format. Its claim is that a civilizational advance has a canonical shape, and that shape has seven stages: a *real problem* exists in some historical, social, bodily, technical or institutional setting; people hold a *misreading* of it — they moralize it, privatize it, suppress it, or take it as fate; someone produces an *insight* that redefines the problem rather than answering the old version of it; that insight is converted into a *mechanism* with a concrete substrate (material, institution, education, standardization, diffusion network, legal protection, social movement, commercialization); the mechanism *diffuses* through a force that must be named (state promotion, market, grassroots transmission, or crisis-forced adoption); it *solves* some things by changing behaviors, relationships, institutions, risks or capabilities; and it *creates new problems* — side effects, misuse, controversy, new inequalities. The seventh stage is not historical at all: the *transferable method* is what a present-day actor can carry out of the dossier. The claim that makes the museum more than a taxonomy is claim 17: what advances civilization is usually not the technology but the insight — and specifically the reframing insight. Two archetypes are named: converting a grand, unregulatable problem into a tool ordinary people can use autonomously (the condom, eyeglasses, paper), and converting what was treated as personal failure into something a public institution carries (public health systems, libraries, accessible design).

**The retrieval mechanism.** For that stock of methods to be usable, the museum needs a way to route a present problem to structurally analogous past problems. It does this by refusing the two conventional indices — chronology and discipline — and indexing on the problem itself. Galleries are named by human problems (memory, transmission, body, trust, cooperation, voice, care, fairness); exhibits can sit in several galleries at once; and the edges between exhibits are typed by problem structure, not subject matter. The seven named method relations (低成本复制 low-cost replication, 私人场景接口 private-scene interface, 公共制度承接 public-institution catchment, 专家能力普及 expert-capability popularization, 陌生人协作 stranger cooperation, 风险转移 risk transfer, 权利重新定义 rights redefinition) are the actual analogy vocabulary: they are what makes the condom → vaccine → anonymous hotline → accessible design → public-health-system path a single trail rather than a category error. The user-facing loop closes on the toolbox: what you can save is a method, not an exhibit, so the artifact you leave with is transferable rather than decorative.

**The anti-degeneration mechanism.** The documents spend more words on failure prevention than on features, because the project's characteristic failure is not technical but epistemic. Four guards are installed. (1) *Anti-sanctification*: the "new problems" module is mandatory, and ambivalent inventions are admissible precisely so long as their harms are shown (claims 14, 21). (2) *Anti-sloganization*: methods must be action principles rather than title keywords, must reserve `steps`/`antiPatterns`/`notApplicableWhen`/`failureSignals` even when empty, and a core method should be supported by exhibits from multiple domains (claims 49, 72, 73). (3) *Anti-authority-laundering*: prototype copy must never speak in the voice of a verified archive, and the guard is not a policy but a build failure — `VITE_CONTENT_MODE=public` against `publicationReady: false` must break the build; publication requires approved facts and sensitivity review, two reliable sources, and licensed visuals (claims 60–63). (4) *Anti-fake-governance*: the nomination form must not look like a real submission pipeline and the living-museum page must not imply a review backend exists (claim 64). Engineering discipline here is not separate from the theory: the reason the content layer is the only reader of the JSON, and the reason components may not hardcode exhibit copy, is that a museum whose claims live in scattered component strings cannot be reviewed, sourced, corrected or de-accessioned.

**Counterexamples the sources themselves raise, and their answers.**

- *"This is just a demo dressed up as a knowledge system."* Raised directly by the audit: 审计前的内容资产更接近静态 demo 文案，不足以支撑长期活博物馆. The answer is the data contract — schema, validator, publication states, evidence table, sensitivity fields, search fields — so that content can be checked by machine and upgraded without a rewrite.
- *"`mvpReady` will be read as 'ready to publish.'"* Raised as a named bottleneck; answered by splitting the flag into `prototypeReady` and `publicationReady` and by making `published` (a per-exhibit workflow state) explicitly not equal to site-level `publicationReady`.
- *"The collection is a set of pretty examples."* Raised in both the audit and the content doc: the ten seeds skew to knowledge diffusion, body tools and digital collaboration. The answer is a required expansion into institutional cases — law, contracts, money, hospitals, psychotherapy, schools, unions, human rights, public-health systems, sewage treatment — i.e. exactly the exhibits where mechanism, diffusion and cost are messiest.
- *"Methods will drift into slogans."* Raised as P1 (方法库去口号化); answered by the reserved fields plus the multi-domain support rule.
- *"Scope will slide from prototype-complete to the whole vision."* Raised as a bottleneck; answered by the explicit v0.1 exclusion list, the phase plan with completion criteria, and the adversarial review checklist.
- *"Structural similarity is a soft claim."* Answered partially, by requiring `relationType`, `dimension` and `confidence` on every similarity link and by forbidding low-confidence AI-generated relations from public display — but the sources never define the space of dimensions (see §8).

## 5. What earlier versions had that later ones lost

1. **Four of the twelve thematic halls.** 方向定稿 §七 lists twelve; 可交互网页 §四 keeps eight. Dropped as galleries: 人类如何降低暴力 (how humans reduced violence), 人类如何把道德诉求变成制度流程 (turning moral demands into institutional process), 人类如何承认差异 (recognizing difference), 人类如何把专家能力封装成普通人可用的工具 (packaging expert capability into lay-usable tools), 人类如何把羞耻问题变成公共问题 (turning shame into a public question). Two survive demoted — as methods (把道德呼吁变成制度流程; 把专家能力封装成普通人可用工具) and one as a homepage entry prompt (如何把私人痛苦变成公共议题). **Violence reduction survives nowhere** — no gallery, no method, no seed exhibit, no expansion list. That is the single largest silent loss in the cluster.
2. **Tangible exhibits named then dropped.** 方向定稿 §三 names 显微镜 (microscope), 乐器 (musical instruments), 摄影机 (camera), 个人电脑 (personal computer), 手机 (mobile phone), 太阳能板 (solar panel), 轮椅 (wheelchair). Of these only 轮椅 reappears (as a 身体 gallery candidate) and 摄影 (photography) reappears as a 发声 candidate. Musical instruments and solar panels vanish entirely — losing, respectively, the expressive/aesthetic dimension of civilizational invention and the ecological/energy dimension.
3. **Intangible exhibits named then dropped.** 版权 (copyright) and 民主制度 (democratic institutions) appear in the concept doc's intangible list and then disappear: 版权 appears in no later list at all; 民主制度 survives only obliquely as 选票 (the ballot) among 公平 gallery candidates. Copyright's loss is notable because it is one of the few named inventions whose "new problems created" module would be sharply contested — exactly the kind of exhibit the museum's anti-sanctification stance calls for.
4. **The seven method relations as a controlled vocabulary.** 可交互网页 §六.1 names them (低成本复制、私人场景接口、公共制度承接、专家能力普及、陌生人协作、风险转移、权利重新定义). The later data specs require `relationType`, `dimension` and `confidence` on similarity links but never re-list this vocabulary, so the enumeration exists only in prose in one document and is at risk of being re-invented ad hoc in the data.
5. **The four user questions.** 方向定稿 §九 gives the actor's arrival questions in the actor's own voice: 我面对的问题属于哪一类？人类过去处理过类似结构的问题吗？当时成功或失败的关键机制是什么？我今天能不能借用、改造或避免这些方法？ Note "失败" (failure) and "避免" (avoid) — the concept doc explicitly wants *failed* mechanisms and methods to avoid. No later document specifies a place in the dossier or the data for failed attempts, or for methods marked "do not reuse." The dossier's `newProblems` is not the same thing.
6. **The full list of naming candidates.** Seven candidates in 方向定稿 §十一 narrow to a three-way naming policy; 人类发明与社会问题博物馆, 活的文明博物馆 and 人类问题解决史 drop out without a recorded reason.
7. **Simplicity of the first implementation.** 可交互网页 §十二 proposed `index.html` + `styles.css` + `app.js` with 暂时不需要引入复杂框架, justified by speed and by the fact that phase 1 validates narrative and temperament rather than engineering. The engineering spec overrides this and argues the case, but the original rationale — that shipping fast matters more than structure while the concept is unvalidated — is a real position that the later document answers rather than refutes.
8. **`mvpReady`.** The single flag is gone, replaced by a better pair; but the audit records only the misreading risk, not whether anything the single flag expressed (a simple "is the MVP done" signal) still has a home.
9. **The public-facing register.** 方向定稿 §九 insists the museum must remain readable by 普通公众 so they understand 文明不是自然发生的，而是一代代人解决问题的结果 ("civilization did not happen naturally; it is the result of generation after generation solving problems"). Later documents are all written for the builder; nothing downstream specifies how the general-public reading level is preserved, and the anti-marketing, anti-welcome rules push in the opposite direction.
10. **"活的更新脉冲" (living update pulse).** Listed among visual keywords in 可交互网页 §七 — a visible sign that the museum is alive and changing. It appears in the permitted-visual list of the engineering spec only as generic elements; the pulse itself, which is the one visual encoding of the living-museum thesis, is not carried into any page spec.

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised in the corpus

1. **The content assets were demo copy.** 审计前的内容资产更接近静态 demo 文案，不足以支撑长期活博物馆 (`content-engineering-audit.md`). Partially answered by the data contract; the substantive content debt remains.
2. **No machine-executable data contract, no source/review/sensitivity/publication status** — both fixed in the hardening pass, but the fix is described, not verified in this corpus (the files do not exist here).
3. **`mvpReady` invites misreading as publication-ready** — fixed by the split flags.
4. **Routing, detail structure, gallery membership, similarity and search fields were unstable** — fixed by `slug`/`routePath`/`primaryGalleryId`/`galleryAppearances`/`sections`.
5. **Scope creep from "prototype complete" to "full product vision"** — mitigated by the exclusion list, phase plan and adversarial review, but the same document also expands scope considerably (seven phases, four validation layers, three viewports, axe checks) for a project with no built page yet.
6. **The collection is too narrow and skews to pretty examples** — flagged twice, unresolved: the institutional exhibits (法律、合同、货币、医院、心理治疗、学校、工会、人权、公共卫生制度、污水处理) remain unwritten.
7. **Methods risk becoming slogans** — flagged as P1; the structural remedy (four reserved fields, multi-domain support) is specified but every one of the fourteen methods currently ships with those fields empty.
8. **Over-abstraction from a single case** — 一个核心方法最好由多个不同领域展品支撑; the anchor exhibit is one case (condom) and the flagship method is derived from it alone.
9. **No two-source floor met yet** — 正式公开前，每个展品至少需要两个可靠来源; currently zero exhibits meet this, so the entire site is prototype-only by its own rule.

### New (my reading)

10. **No theory of structural similarity.** The whole retrieval mechanism rests on "structurally similar problems," yet nothing defines what a problem *structure* is or enumerates the `dimension` values a similarity link may take. The seven method relations in `可交互网页` §六.1 are the only candidate vocabulary and were not carried into the data specs. Without this, similarity becomes curator intuition, and the museum's central promise — that your problem has a structural ancestor — is unfalsifiable.
11. **No falsification or failure test for a transferable method.** Methods are extracted post hoc from successes. There is no mechanism for recording that a borrowed method was tried and failed, no feedback path from a user's outcome back into the dossier, and — per §5 item 5 — the concept doc's interest in *failed* historical mechanisms and methods-to-avoid has no home in the data model. A method library with no failure record is a survivorship machine.
12. **Survivorship and Whig-history risk.** The museum indexes what worked and diffused. Problems humanity never solved, solutions that were suppressed, and mechanisms that worked and were destroyed are structurally invisible. The "new problems" module mitigates triumphalism about individual exhibits but not the selection bias of the collection as a whole. A 未解决 (unsolved) or 失败的解决方案 (failed solutions) wing would be the direct fix.
13. **The anchor method can be read as a dark-pattern license.** "解决方案… 不要求人先改变成理想中的样子" is a genuine insight about designing for real humans, but stated as a general transferable method it also describes engagement-optimized products that meet people where they are and keep them there. The museum has anti-sanctification guards for *exhibits* but no ethics guard on *method transfer* — no `antiPatterns` entry yet exists for any method, which is precisely where this would live.
14. **Curatorial legitimacy is undefined.** 策展审核 (curatorial review) appears in every governance statement, but no document says who curates, how curators are selected, how disputes between nominator and curator resolve, whether decisions are appealable, or whether an admitted exhibit can be *de-accessioned* when later found wrong. The workflow ends at `revised`; there is no `withdrawn` or `contested` state.
15. **The living museum has no discussion surface.** 公共讨论 (public discussion) is one of the four governance verbs, yet v0.1 ships no comments, no forum, no visible nomination queue, and the nomination form deliberately does not submit anywhere. The most distinctive governance claim is the least implemented, and the engineering spec explicitly forbids anything that would imply otherwise — which is honest, but leaves the model entirely on paper.
16. **Language scope contradicts the object.** The site is specified `html lang="zh-CN"`, multi-language is excluded from v0.1, and yet the object of study is human civilization and the English naming is already explored. The sourcing risk is sharper than the UI risk: a Chinese-only sourcing and review pipeline will silently narrow which civilizations' solutions get archived.
17. **Sensitivity handling is asserted, not specified.** `sensitivity` carries 等级、类别和措辞提醒 (level, category, wording reminders) and sensitive exhibits must use 克制的公共讨论语言, but no document defines the levels, the categories, or who adjudicates. For a collection whose anchor exhibit is the condom and whose expansion list includes psychotherapy and human rights, this is load-bearing.
18. **The museum's own content licensing is unspecified.** Image credit and license are required for exhibit visuals, and nominators must declare copyright, but nothing states the license of the museum's own dossiers and methods. For a project whose thesis is that methods should be borrowable, an explicit open license would be a substantive design decision, not a legal footnote.
19. **Preset entry prompts partly contradict the arrival thesis.** "用户带着一个问题进入" is the core promise, but v0.1 admits only preset problem entrances and forbids free text. A user whose problem does not match one of roughly seven prompts cannot in fact arrive with their problem. The deferral is defensible for v0.1; the tension should be named rather than left as a scope note.
20. **Engineering weight versus content weight.** Roughly 24 KB of the cluster's 66 KB is engineering specification and about 4 KB is audit, against a single worked exhibit example (the condom, appearing three times in overlapping form). The most likely failure mode of the project as written is a beautifully validated, well-tested, accessible shell around nine one-line exhibits — precisely the risk the audit named as demo copy, displaced from the content layer to the app layer.
21. **The museum never uses its own method on itself.** The obvious self-application — "the online museum / open knowledge commons" as an exhibit, with its own problem, misreading, insight, mechanism, diffusion, new problems and transferable method (Wikipedia is already listed as a 协作 candidate) — is never taken up, and the project also never cites Ren's other frameworks. Doing so would both test the format and connect this cluster to the rest of the corpus.
22. **Unverifiable artifacts.** All field-level, schema-level and validator-level claims in these documents describe files that are not in this corpus. Anyone reading this synthesis later should treat `data/museum-content.json`, the schema, the validator, `schemaVersion 0.2.0` and the ten seed exhibits as *specified*, not as *confirmed to exist*.

## 9. Publication readiness

**Publishable or teachable now.**

- `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` — the strongest standalone text in the cluster and essentially publishable as an essay/manifesto today. It argues its position, defines the format, works one case, and states its governance without requiring any of the software to exist. Minimal edits needed: a note that it is a project direction rather than a description of a live museum.
- The seven-question dossier schema (方向定稿 §五) and its eight-module web form (可交互网页 §五) — immediately teachable as a reusable analytic template, independent of the museum. It is usable as a worksheet in a workshop for founders, organizers, or policy designers on the day it is extracted.
- The nomination form's question set (可交互网页 §六.6, extended with the governance fields in `content-engineering-audit.md`) — publishable as a standalone thinking tool: eight questions that force a proposal to state its problem, misreading, insight, mechanism, evidence, harms and transferable method.
- The condom case (方向定稿 §六 + 可交互网页 §十) — publishable as a short essay demonstrating the format, provided sources are added and the presentation stays in the restrained register the documents themselves require.
- The publication-governance model (prototype vs publication readiness, the six-state workflow, the build-failure gate, the two-source floor, the "reliable source" definition excluding `uncertain`) — publishable as a short, genuinely useful piece for anyone building a public knowledge site with AI-assisted drafting. It is the most transferable thing in the cluster and is currently buried across three documents.
- `11-human-solutions-museum/agent.md` — small but directly reusable as an agent-boundary pattern.

**Missing.**

- Any sourced exhibit. Zero of the ten seeds meet the stated two-reliable-source floor, so by the project's own rule nothing here may be published as fact.
- The content data, schema, validator and any built page — not present in this corpus at all.
- The institutional half of the collection (law, contracts, money, hospitals, psychotherapy, schools, unions, human rights, public-health systems, sewage treatment), without which the thesis is demonstrated only on easy cases.
- Any filled `steps`/`antiPatterns`/`notApplicableWhen`/`failureSignals` — the anti-slogan interface is empty everywhere.
- A definition of structural similarity and its `dimension` vocabulary.
- The curatorial layer: who curates, how disputes resolve, how an exhibit is contested or withdrawn.
- A place in the model for failed solutions and methods-to-avoid.

**The one piece of writing that would most advance it.** Write the 避孕套 anchor dossier in full, to the eight canonical sections, at publication standard: two or more reliable sources with an `evidence` block, a timeline of key historical nodes, an explicit statement of disputes and of the over-generalizations to avoid, a sensitivity note in the restrained register, and — critically — the borrowable method written out with `steps`, `antiPatterns`, `notApplicableWhen` and `failureSignals` filled in, plus at least two structurally similar exhibits from *different* domains supporting the same method so it is not abstracted from a single case. This single document would (a) prove the format end to end, (b) convert the flagship method from an elegant sentence into a tested tool, (c) exercise the entire publication gate, and (d) directly attack the three largest open risks — sloganization, single-case abstraction, and prototype copy masquerading as archive. A close second, if the aim is to advance the *theory* rather than the museum, is a short essay extracting the general thesis — 文明是一代代人解决问题的结果，而我们把结果存成了物品，没有存成方法 ("civilization is the accumulated result of generations solving problems, and we archived the results as objects rather than as methods") — which is currently implicit across the cluster and stated nowhere in full.
