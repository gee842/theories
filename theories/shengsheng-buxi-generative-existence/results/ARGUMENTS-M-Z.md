## 市场更新、选择与俘获的数学模型 / Market renewal, selection and capture

**编号 / ID:** `market-dynamics-math` · **类别 / Category:** 数学模型 / mathematical model

**来源 / Source:** [MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md](../unified-2026-10-05/MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md)

**状态 / Status:** 有条件的形式结果已检查 / Checked conditional formal result

**中文论证：** 在明确的收益、支承更新、征收和干预规律下，实际责任成本改变两类实践的相对收益；获利形成的影响力又改变后续规则。充分的不变区域条件可同时维持更新、问责和分项支持，并识别恢复及崩溃区域。

**English argument:** Given explicit returns, renewal, collection and intervention laws, actual liability changes relative returns, while accumulated influence changes later selection. Sufficient invariant-region conditions jointly maintain renewal, accountability and separate supports; the model also identifies recovery and collapse regions.

**意义：** 把“何者胜出”与“胜者怎样改变下一轮规则”放在一个可检查过程里。

**Significance:** Connects what wins today with how winners change tomorrow’s rules.

**范围：** 39项 Lean 结果检查有理数转移、不变性与账户；实数吸引极限为历史手证，未全部 Lean 化。制度合法性、校准和市场对应仍给定。

**Scope:** 39 Lean results cover rational transitions, invariance and accounts; real-valued attraction limits retain historical hand-proof status. Institutional legitimacy, calibration and market correspondence are supplied.

**后续：** 正在：保留条件模型。建议：选择真实市场，检验进入退出、价格形成、错误征责与规则俘获。

**Next:** Ongoing: preserve the conditional model. Proposed: identify a real market and test entry, price formation, miscalibrated liability and capture.
