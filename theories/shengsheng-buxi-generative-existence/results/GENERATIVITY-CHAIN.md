## 核心论断 / The central claim

> 新形成的关系使原来不能共同发生的生活与进一步形成条件能够共同实现时，整体获得了新的实现方式；其正面意义可以就在这种共同形成中，而不必先变成发起者的收益。

> When newly formed relations enable forms of living and conditions for further formation that could not previously occur together to be jointly realized, the whole acquires a new mode of realization; its favorable significance may lie in this co-formation itself, without first becoming a return to its initiator.
