## 1. 普遍规范根据与共同伦理起点 / Universal warrant and a shared ethical starting point

**正在；第一优先 / Ongoing; first priority.** [统一伦理](../unified-2026-10-05/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md)已经提出整体生成、分化的生命与实际后果之间的正面联系。[基础接续](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERAL-GENERATIVE-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md#direct-foundational-continuation-and-an-explicit-starting-point)把生成及继续生成的正面规范意义作为显式候选起点。Ren 随后[批准沿此路线继续](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-generative-premise-route-approved.md)；[最新推导整合](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-PREMISE-INTEGRATION.md)以正面论证、独立审查及11项 Lean 定理区分支持理由、有限比较与更强选择约束。伦理起点自身的更强根据继续研究，不成为禁止这条条件路线的前置门槛。

The current account connects whole generation, differentiated living and actual consequences. Ren has approved further derivation from the explicit normative starting point. The latest argument, independent review and eleven checked theorems distinguish supporting reasons, limited comparisons and stronger choice constraints. Stronger justification of the starting point remains a parallel task, not a prerequisite blocking this conditional route.

**意义 / Significance.** 一个共同根据可以贯通多种情境，而不必每次另设无关的道德起点。它也使争议集中到规范起点的根据、实践强度和适用范围。 / A common ground can connect different situations without an unrelated moral premise for each; its warrant, practical force and domain become explicit questions.

**建议 / Proposed.** 眼前连接是两条可行接续各有真实生成实现及损失时，怎样形成有根据的取舍、容许范围或暂不决定，并通过新认识校准。更强的并行工作继续对“整体实际生成 → 正面伦理质量 → 差异化实践理由”给出实质充分性论证或能改变它的反例。保留生物／生态、心智／关系／生活方式、广义组织生成的不同范围及相互损失，分别论证普遍有效、认识与践行、唯一必要性。 / The immediate bridge concerns justified choice, latitude or suspension when feasible continuations each involve real generation and loss, with calibration through new understanding. In parallel, develop a substantive sufficiency argument or discriminating challenge for whole generation → ethical quality → practical reasons. Retain the different ranges of 生, their conflicts and separate questions of validity, recognition, compliance and exclusive necessity.

## 2. 情境理由、冲突与建设性结束 / Situated reasons, conflict and constructive endings

**最新推进 / Latest development.** [真实取舍与校准](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-TRADEOFF-INTEGRATION.md)已提出可反驳的 K 比较候选，并以11项取舍定理和28项审计检查必要损失及认识对应。恢复共同生活不抹去中断，未来无自动无限优先；K 的伦理力度不是这些定理证明的结论。下一步须解释损失的内容、持续和影响范围怎样改变比较。 / The tradeoff study develops a defeasible K comparison and checks necessary loss and epistemic correspondence in eleven theorems and twenty-eight audits. Recovery does not erase interruption or give every future unlimited priority. K’s ethical force remains to be justified through the content, duration and scope of loss.

**正在 / Ongoing.** [生成能力整合](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-CAPACITY-INTEGRATION.md)已经比较同一历史中的形成、共同实现、重组与结束；[情境根据](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SITUATED-WARRANT-INTEGRATION.md)考察回应怎样同时改变生活、依赖和以后判断的条件。这些研究容纳休息、接受照料、有限完成与真实损失。

Existing studies compare formation, joint realization, reconfiguration and endings within one history, and examine how responses change lives, dependencies and later judgment. They accommodate rest, receiving care, finite fulfillment and real loss.

**意义 / Significance.** 生生开始具备处理真实变化的内容；某一组织继续存在、每一步选项增加或总复杂度增长，都不能代替完整情境的评价。 / Generativity acquires content for real changes; persistence, stepwise option growth or aggregate complexity cannot substitute for whole-situation appraisal.

**建议 / Proposed.** 对同时包含真实好处与损失的案例，说明何种回应有理由、理由落在哪里、何时被履行或撤回；进一步检验目的变化、参与退出、无替代和长期依存。改变结论的证据应保留相同历史及真实后果，而非只切换一个未解释的“善”标签。 / Establish reasons, their addressees and conditions of fulfillment or withdrawal in mixed cases, including changed purposes, exit, irreplaceability and lasting dependence. Tests must preserve the relevant history and consequences, rather than merely toggle an unexplained goodness label.

## 3. 良知的形成、校准与发展 / Liangzhi: formation, calibration and development

**正在 / Ongoing.** [良知演化](../unified-2026-10-05/LIANGZHI-EVOLUTION.md)已有传递、发展、实践交接和校准研究；各轮 Lean 检查、原典核读及幼儿研究保持各自范围。[实践整合](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIANGZHI-PRACTICAL-INTEGRATION.md)包含失败、恢复、先撤后接的成功对照与有限结束。[校准整合](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIANGZHI-CALIBRATION-INTEGRATION.md)继续追问如何辨明所形成的回应。

Transmission, development, practical handoff and calibration have been studied with separately scoped Lean checks, classical interpretation and developmental evidence. Practical results retain failure, recovery, successful withdrawal-before-replacement and finite endings. Calibration asks how formed responses can be assessed.

**意义 / Significance.** 将“应当如何回应”与辨别和动机怎样生成接合起来，同时使伦理有效性保持可检验。 / Connects how responses ought to be assessed with the formation of discrimination and motivation, keeping ethical validity examinable.

**建议 / Proposed.** 研究目的／参与变化与既有依存冲突时的理由范围；区分事实误认、目的改变、知情拒绝和仍有效的承担。发展上的形成、非工具性动机与伦理正确不可互相代替。 / Test the scope of reasons when changed purposes or participation conflict with existing dependence, distinguishing factual error, changed ends, informed refusal and continuing undertakings. Developmental formation, noninstrumental motivation and ethical correctness remain different claims.

**建议 / Proposed.** 独立说明无限域、完整高阶范围及更丰富语言的接纳根据；区别能够表示、保持真理、解释其成立和唯一构成基础。更多有限实例只有在排除一个明确限制时才推进普遍目标。 / Independently warrant infinite domains, full higher ranges and richer languages. Separate representation, truth preservation, explanation and unique constitution. Further finite instances advance the universal target only when they remove a named limitation.

## 5. 生成、学习与自我修正的持续实现 / Continued realization of formation, learning and revision

**条件连接已完成；更强实现继续 / Conditional connections complete; stronger realizations remain open.** [五条件审查](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate)已接受有条件的连接基础，包括评价形成、正当应用、一般纠错与有限实践智慧。[评价接续](../unified-2026-10-05/EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md)和[学习路线修正](../unified-2026-10-05/LEARNING-ROUTE-REVISION.md)给出具体接续；失败与常规实现的等价性同时保留。

The five-condition assessment accepts the conditional connected foundation, including formed evaluation, warranted application, generic correction and bounded practical wisdom. Evaluative succession and route revision provide concrete continuations, retaining failures and conventional equivalence.

**意义 / Significance.** 关键能力已不只是列在框图中的名称，而有保持信息、消耗和失败历史的实现。 / Key capacities have implementations retaining information, expenditures and failed histories.

**建议 / Proposed.** 选择一个仍由局部模型提供的形成、校准或持续支持条件，给出相容的更深解释或真实对应。注明它怎样支持规范研究；不把已关闭的机制接口重新列为全局缺口。 / Explain or identify a remaining supplied formation, calibration or support condition and state its contribution to normative inquiry. Do not reopen accepted interfaces as missing global prerequisites.

## 6. 科学对应与有区分力的检验 / Scientific correspondence and discriminating tests

**新完成的局部连接 / Newly completed local connection.** [生命依存](../unified-2026-10-05/LIVING-DEPENDENCIES.md)把输运平衡、补偿所需的功及操作界组合为一般支持账户，记录10项 Lean 定理与20项审计。它解释某类维持过程，保留休眠、已有容限与恢复；下一步是双向被动输运及可恢复转化，生命全域对应仍开放。 / Living-dependency work derives a support account from transport balance, compensation work and an operative bound, with ten checked theorems and twenty audits. Its maintained-process scope retains dormancy, tolerance and recovery; signed transport and recoverable transformation are next, with universal living coverage open.

**已有证据整合；进一步检验有待开展 / Existing evidence integrated; further tests proposed.** [经验范围](../unified-2026-10-05/EMPIRICAL-SCOPE.md)区分模型内部检查、跨领域对应及真实观察；[实验接入](../unified-2026-10-05/EXPERIMENTAL-ACCESS.md)列出数据、操纵和识别的限制。既有实验或文献阅读不等于本项目做过新干预。

The evidence account separates internal model checks, cross-domain correspondence and observations of real processes. Access studies retain limitations in data, interventions and identification. Existing experiments or readings do not count as new interventions by this project.

**意义 / Significance.** 决定形式关系是否确实解释所指现象，并允许更简单的替代解释获得支持。 / Determines whether formal relationships explain their proposed targets and allows simpler competing accounts to succeed.

**建议 / Proposed.** 预先固定可测变量、操纵与替代模型；选能改变某个依存对应或机制判断的试验。对良知与选择演化分别标注来源质量、干预范围及因果限度。 / Fix measurements, interventions and comparators prospectively; choose tests capable of changing a correspondence or mechanism judgment. Keep source quality and causal limits explicit in developmental and evolutionary work.
