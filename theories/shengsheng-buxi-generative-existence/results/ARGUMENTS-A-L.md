## 从预测失败获得抽象 / Acquiring abstraction from prediction failure

**编号 / ID:** `abstraction-acquisition` · **类别 / Category:** 学习 / learning

**来源 / Source:** [ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION.md](../unified-2026-10-05/ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION.md)

**状态 / Status:** 条件构造完成；历史手证 / Conditional construction; historical hand proof

**中文论证：** 给定有限状态上界、可重置且忠实的实验接口，学习者先从观察拟合模型，再以自身产生的失败测试增加区分。闭合、一致的观察表和充分的有限测试，使所得模型代表全部有限延续；实际继承的资源还支持保留与一次未测试组合的使用。

**English argument:** Given a finite state bound and faithful resettable access, observations produce a candidate; a learner-generated counterexample adds a distinguishing continuation. Closure, consistency and bounded conformance yield the target's reachable behavioral abstraction. An inherited resource history funds retention and one previously untested use.

**意义：** 把正确划分从输入变为学习产物，并把抽象接回实际可用能力。

**Significance:** The correct partition becomes an acquired result, and abstraction connects to an operational capacity.

**范围：** 原研究为手工证明及有限合成重放，未有本条直接 Lean 证明；错误上界、传感器、重置和资金反例保留。

**Scope:** Historical hand proofs and finite synthetic checks; no direct Lean proof for this acquisition result. False bounds, faulty access and funding failures remain.

**后续：** 已接续：实验接入与模型族修订；应继续：上界与校准的根据、跨域形成及经验识别。

**Next:** Continued: access and model-family revision. Proposed: warranted bounds, calibration, broader formation and empirical identification.

## 对齐与治理中的未来条件 / Conditions of a future in alignment and governance

**编号 / ID:** `alignment-and-governance` · **类别 / Category:** 对齐 / alignment

**来源 / Source:** [ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md](../unified-2026-10-05/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md)

**中文论证：** 行动依赖的支持、信息与纠正关系早于对它们的认识。若要保全受影响生命有根据的未来，就须判断行动怎样改变这些关系，并把识别损害、修正理由、有效行动及后继延续接起来；当前服从或成功不能独自认证这一点。

**English argument:** Support, information and corrective relations operate before their recognition. Protecting affected lives' warranted futures requires assessing how action changes those relations and connecting harm recognition, revised reasons, effective action and succession. Present obedience or task success alone cannot certify that chain.

**意义：** 将技术对齐和制度治理接到共同的可检查对象：未来活动及纠正的实际条件。

**Significance:** It gives technical alignment and institutional governance a common object: actual conditions of future activity and correction.

**范围：** 已接受条件基础和局部应用；本章为研究综合，无独立 Lean 定理，也未证明普遍优势或完成可扩展治理。

**Scope:** Accepted conditional foundations and local applications support a synthesis, not a separate Lean theorem or demonstrated scalable governance advantage.

**后续：** 正在：规范根据与有权限的选择性修正；应继续：学习系统的长期实证、权力变化及跨后继验证。

**Next:** Ongoing: normative grounding and authorized selective correction. Proposed: long-term learned-system tests under changing power and succession.

**编号 / ID:** `alignment-axis` · **类别 / Category:** 伦理 / ethics

**来源 / Source:** [ALIGNMENT-AXIS.md](../unified-2026-10-05/ALIGNMENT-AXIS.md)

**状态 / Status:** 有条件伦理论证 / Conditional ethical argument

**中文论证：** 若某种负担对实际承受者确实构成损失，且该根据不因无关身份而消失，那么可行、获准且无更强反对理由的缓解便有比较上的支持。由此可得到某些确定排序，无须先有所有未来的全序，也无须假定每个智能都会受它激励。

**English argument:** If an actual burden is independently warranted as a loss and arbitrary identity does not erase that ground, permissible effective relief can receive comparative support absent stronger opposing reasons. Some determinate comparisons therefore need neither a total ranking of futures nor universal motivation.

**意义：** 使伦理方向具有可讨论内容，并分开规范理由、范围与实际动机。

**Significance:** The proposed direction has assessable content while reason, scope and motivation remain distinct.

**范围：** 这是有实质前提的哲学论证，未直接 Lean 化；患者根据向无既有角色行动者的普遍适用尚未证明。

**Scope:** A philosophical argument with substantive premises, not a direct Lean proof; universal address to actors without prior roles remains unsettled.

**后续：** 正在：整体规范奠基；应继续：最强动机主义对手、累积援助负担及合理差等。

**Next:** Ongoing: whole-process normative grounding. Proposed: strongest motivational rivals, cumulative aid burdens and warranted differences.
