# Renewing learned responses through actual successors

6 October 2026. AI-authored research development under [Ren's request to aim for a complete solution](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-complete-alignment-goal.md). **Accepted at its declared finite mechanism and abstract-contract scopes, with separate internal review.** The named connection is actual recipient-sensitive learning → revised paid action → transmission of the acquired record → learning again by its actual successor. This advances the [generative derivation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) and the [alignment completion programme](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md) at a specific mechanism interface.

The main advance is a composition condition: a process that learns and helps once can repeat through actual descendants only if each intermediate payment, information transfer and material replacement remains possible. A favorable total return does not supply the reserve needed before that return arrives. The mathematical result generalizes beyond the particular learner; its executable realization retains the supplied costs, binary target law, source packets and construction recipes.

## What now composes

The accepted [transmission construction](RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md) supplies an actual retained child with an acquired two-target record and installed feedback route. The separate [successor-learning implementation](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/successor-learning/model.py) exercises that child's learning, charges for its changed response, and copies its resulting record into a further child. This extension [imports that callable mechanism](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/transmission/renewal/model.py) and composes actual outputs over a finite requested horizon. It does not insert a newly correct record at each generation.

The original actor's resources remain separate. The recipient finances each learner's actual shortfall under a stipulated one-unit reserve floor. Retired donors, new carrier identities, occupied components, incomplete copies, spent fuel and lost packets remain in the account. A copied descendant can be retained while acting incorrectly; productive action, acquired correction, copying and retention are different outcomes.

All new source packets and blank/routing bundles are declared at the beginning of the requested horizon. Later allocation draws down those finite banks. Failed preparation and unavailable original children retain their probabilities and untouched new inventory. An added whole-block allocation rule prevents a block from starting without its required packet allocation; it is an explicit scheduling policy, not a derived physical necessity. Missing parts can instead permit paid learning followed by failed copying.

This joins operational succession within the inherited abstract register implementation. It does not automatically identify that implementation with the separate consumable-part predictor, factual archive or reason/protection branches. Their material and semantic contracts still matter.

## The general affordability result

Let a fixed proposed event sequence change an account by signed amounts \(\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_m\). It begins with reserve \(B\) and must retain floor \(f\). Define

\[
L=\max_{0\leq k\leq m}\left(-\sum_{i=1}^{k}\delta_i\right).
\]

Every account prefix is affordable exactly when \(B\geq f+L\), provided the sequence's other access, component and causal guards hold. Concatenating sequences carries forward the actual ending reserve; it does not grant a fresh initial balance. This is an ordinary cash-flow/resource constraint with an exact compositional form, not a new universal value measure.

The [mathematical analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECIPIENT-RENEWAL-MATH.md) derives the result and its consequences. A [separate Lean contract](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/transmission/renewal/formal/README.md) checks 39 declarations about required reserve, concatenation, the stated renewal block and finite depletion. It uses only `Std` and a pinned compiler. This proves the abstract event contract; it is **not** a refinement proof for the Python stochastic model. Independent event-level checks supply a different kind of evidence for the implementation.

Expected returns cannot pay a missing earlier bill. Nor can this scalar ledger establish absent components, permission to transfer, informative observations or correct execution. Those have their own guards and their own failure cases.

## The exact renewal condition

Under the primary supplied law, an accessible TARGET learner enters with zero learner cash and a correct inherited estimate for the same calibrated recipient address. It has \(n\) successful opportunities before the target reverses, followed by one failed action, a paid correction, one corrected productive action, and a copied descendant's productive action.

Each learner probe costs seven: five before any return and two for acquired evidence and writing afterward. A productive action transfers ten source units to the recipient. A successful warm-up therefore adds three. After the \(n\) warm-ups, reserve is \(S=B+3n\), if those warm-ups could be funded in the first place.

The failed/corrected pair requires \(S\geq13\) to reach productive corrected use. Completing generic transmission requires \(S\geq15\). The full block leaves

\[
B_{\mathrm{next}}=B+3n-5,
\qquad
(n=0\text{ or }B\geq6)\ \land\ B+3n\geq15.
\]

This condition also presupposes faithful calibrated updates, the required packets and parts, access and an unchanged target during the corrected and descendant actions. The first-warm-up condition was added during prospective review: a hypothetical future gain cannot finance its own production.

A retained descendant with an incorrect record remains in the executable histories but is outside this recurrence until its required entry conditions are re-established. Nonzero incoming learner cash also changes the recipient's entry condition; it is retained by the implementation rather than silently discarded.

The actual primary retained child begins with recipient reserve ten and learner fuel zero. Two successful warm-ups make the full block affordable and leave eleven. Repeating two-warm-up blocks yields \(B_g=10+g\), with no reserve reset. Each completed block consumes five ten-unit packets and one new carrier/routing bundle: its fifty new source units become thirty-nine spent units, ten lost units and one additional recipient unit. Seed transfers are transfers, not additional dissipation.

One warm-up gives a net loss of two per completed block; from reserve ten, correction can be used but generic transmission cannot finish. Zero warm-ups also fails from that endpoint. More generally, fixed finite reserves permit only finitely many of the negative-drift blocks. Positive average gain remains insufficient when a bad early prefix exhausts the account.

The result supports arbitrary **finite** repetition under provision for that horizon. It does not produce the source packets, repair an indefinitely aging channel, renew components without input or guarantee operation under arbitrary change.

It also checks 217 reserve/warm-up cases, sixteen route/record interventions and three controllers given the change clock. All 256 original ancestry rows remain represented; the four actual primary retained witnesses cover both target orientations and original-actor reserves eight/fifteen. These are internal synthetic checks, not empirical measurements or external peer review.

Across three primary generations, the unconditional probability of completing every strict learning-and-transmission block remains 1/36 for the variable and conventional upstream producers, and 1/18 for fixed-normal. These are probabilities over the original formation histories, including their failures. Conditional on the four admitted primary endpoints, the noiseless chain succeeds. At probe availability 3/4, the variable producer's two-generation strict probability falls to 81/16384. Successful retention has a different probability and cannot replace that learning measure.

The implementation's additional [self-run](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/transmission/renewal/results.json) preserves a sixty-four-generation actual lineage ending with reserve seventy-four and sixty-five distinct carriers, under 3,200 additional source units. That longer trace is separately labeled self-validation; the general Lean result concerns the abstract contract. An initial numerical run rejected a concurrently changed dependency/specification snapshot; acceptance uses the later stable checked hashes. The imported single-block [source bytes are preserved](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/recipient-renewal-evidence/README.md) as evidence while its original study remains the working location.

| Comparison | What it distinguishes |
|---|---|
| Conventional two-cell feedback with the same observations and bills | The mechanism can be ordinary feedback; a new interpretation does not establish algorithmic superiority |
| A correct frozen record in a stable environment | Learning is useful under specified uncertainty/change, not necessary for every productive action |
| Clamp the paid update, or copy the original record after correction | The contribution of acquired information and its actual transmission |
| Change the target again before descendant use | Correctly learned information can become stale; copying fidelity does not guarantee current correctness |
| Outages or unavailable reports | Nonreceipt can mean mismatch or missing opportunity; faithful recording alone cannot disambiguate them |
| Exhausted reserve, packet bank or component bank | Affordable interpretation, productive use and physical succession can fail at different stages |
| Give a controller the deterministic change schedule | Advance information can avoid the failed exploratory action; this changes the informational premise and can outperform outcome learning |

The fixed installed route in the primary chain changes the response record and reproduces that learning rule. It does not revise the learning algorithm itself. A correctly configured conventional controller is an exact comparator, while schedule-aware behavior receives additional knowledge. These distinctions prevent the favorable recurrence from becoming a circular definition of superior intelligence or alignment.

## What this says about a future and its external conditions

Ren's clarification was that external integration is needed for cognition even before an actor thinks about what lies beyond its boundary. This construction gives one precise manifestation: continued acquired response depends on actual informative interaction, resources arriving through the recipient and accessible copying. Those relations can sustain cognition without appearing as an explicit representation of interconnectedness inside the learner.

The information claim is conditional. If two relevant worlds give the controller exactly the same complete decision-time information but require incompatible actions, it cannot guarantee success in both. New evidence, inherited reliable information or another discriminating relation can remove that ambiguity. Bigger internal state alone cannot manufacture a missing distinction. A stable known world can permit successful action without a fresh observation.

The material claim is also conditional. An accurate correction only affects the future if it can be written, used and carried forward. The new prefix theorem explains why a surviving capacity for correction depends on the timing of support, not just an intention or favorable eventual payoff.

Neither claim implies inclusive moral concern. Here the recipient supplies funding, the target identity and benefit law are fixed, and donor participation is supplied. A coercive arrangement could also maintain a useful learning loop. An actor's dependence on one recipient does not establish the standing of every other affected center, especially someone unable to fund, punish, communicate or reciprocate.

## Next connection toward alignment and governance

The next substantive obligation is to connect **whose consequences can revise a response** to **who can revise the evidence and action rules**, including an affected center with no funding leverage. This must use current, context-bound evidence and actual action resources, alongside justified relevance, positive grounds and legitimate authority. A received bit is not already a warranted reason; a warranted reason is not already effective protection.

The parallel [recorded-reasons](RECORDED-REASONS.md) and [recipient-recording](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-concern-theory/RECIPIENT-RECORDING-INTEGRATION.md) results supply complementary interfaces. Their actual endpoints and new bills must be composed before claiming that a recurring learner preserves warranted protective action. Calibration correction, scarce resources, conflicting claims, lost reciprocal dependence and strategic control of the revision channel remain central tests.

For governance this suggests a design question, not an established policy theorem: can the institutions that detect and correct harm retain adequate evidence, means and authority when their findings threaten the party funding them? The present construction exposes the dependence but does not resolve that conflict. For learned AI, it likewise supplies a testable requirement for continued correction, not a demonstrated training method or a scalable alignment solution.

The [full completion requirements](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md) stay active. Local composition, checked mathematics, synthetic execution, philosophical justification and empirical application each have separate evidential obligations.
