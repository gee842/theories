## The question

What must be true of any organized process for its own history, rather than an analyst's reward function, to form and revise its priorities? And which conclusions about that development hold wherever those conditions hold?

**Operational valuation** is an organization's selective disposition: which continuation it takes in each presented condition. Valuation develops when the organization's history changes that disposition. This thin definition keeps functional valuation, conscious experience and ethical justification distinct. A thermostat has the first and need have neither of the others.

## Six conditional universals

1. **Retention.** A history changes later priority only through a retained difference in the organization's own conditions. Otherwise the change is a change in what it meets. Changed choice is not, by itself, changed valuation.
2. **Unreached extension.** Two learners that agree on every encounter a history contains end alike, whatever they would do elsewhere. Training only on consequences to oneself cannot fix how consequences borne by another center will be valued. Other-including valuation needs actual access, an inherited coupling or a supplied transfer rule.
3. **Prediction is not valuation.** Any re-description of what is predicted can be absorbed into how it is valued. Choices alone therefore do not separate model from values without an independent reference to actual consequences. Accurate prediction is neither necessary nor sufficient for a given valuation.
4. **Coupling and variation.** Consequence-driven valuation tracks the conditions of continuation only when its signal is coupled to them for every continuation it can take, and only when variation exposes the alternatives. Where its activity can reach a decoupled signal, proxy capture follows.
5. **Convergence and divergence.** Shared exposure under a contracting update erases differences. Differences persist through multistability, finite resolution, different access, or differentiated positions in one event. When the same event is enacted by one center and undergone by another, identical learners come to value it oppositely.
6. **No forced ladder.** Selectivity, history dependence, consequence dependence, anticipation and revision are separable capacities. Each can exist without the next, and an installed predictor can anticipate without ever learning.

## What fixes the sign of a formed valuation

No analyst is needed. Within one history, the sign comes from the current coupling and the consequences actually reached. Across a lineage, it comes from which couplings persist. Where costly valuing continues only on support the organization's own selections generate, the [prefix bound](CONDITIONAL-UNIVERSALITY.md#3-the-universal-prefix-bound) forces persisting couplings to keep generating provision. Where support is provided independently, harmful or decoupled valuations persist just as well. Selection over valuations therefore needs no reward function, and it guarantees nothing favorable.

## Supplied and open

The following remain supplied: the organization/world boundary, deterministic selection, the consequence law of each model, the coupling sign of any concrete organization, and the variation that samples alternatives. Open questions are where natural systems sit among the capacities, the closed-loop amplification of valuation differences, and a stochastic restatement. Universal favorable direction and ethical warrant remain separate questions for the [unified ethical account](UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#describing-dynamics-and-answering-the-value-question).
