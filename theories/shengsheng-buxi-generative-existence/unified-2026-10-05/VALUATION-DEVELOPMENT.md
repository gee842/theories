A separate internal AI review accepted it with corrections, which have been applied. It makes no empirical or ethical claim. It joins step 2 (operational significance) to step 3 (learning and revision of selectors) of the [accepted work sequence](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#accepted-work-sequence).

## The question

What must be true of any organized process for its own history, rather than an analyst's reward function, to form and revise its priorities? And which conclusions about that development hold wherever those conditions hold?

**Operational valuation** is an organization's selective disposition: which continuation it takes in each presented condition. Valuation develops when the organization's history changes that disposition. This thin definition keeps functional valuation, conscious experience and ethical justification distinct. A thermostat has the first and need have neither of the others.

## Six results

1. **Retention.** A history changes later priority only through a retained difference in the organization's own conditions. Otherwise the change is a change in what it meets. Changed choice is not, by itself, changed valuation.
2. **Unreached extension.** Two learners that select alike and retain alike on a class of encounters end every history within that class alike, whatever they would do outside it. Self-history therefore does not fix how consequences borne by another center will be valued, independently of the organization's own law. Other-including valuation forms through actual access, a feature of the organization's law linking the channels, or an inherited coupling.
3. **Prediction does not fix choice.** Any re-description of what is predicted can be absorbed into how predicted consequences are scored. Choices alone therefore do not separate model from scoring without an independent reference to actual consequences.
4. **Coupling and variation.** Consequence-driven valuation can be captured wherever its activity reaches a signal decoupled from the conditions of its continuation, and without variation it can deplete those conditions even when the signal is coupled. These are proved possibilities, not a necessity claim about all learners.
5. **Convergence and divergence.** Shared exposure under a contracting update erases differences. Differences can persist through multistability, finite resolution, different access, or being presented different consequences of one event. Read through the nondual method, an event enacted by one center and undergone by another can lead identical learners to value it oppositely.
6. **No forced ladder.** Selectivity, history dependence, consequence dependence and anticipation are separable capacities. Each can exist without the next, and a retained model can anticipate without ever being revised. Revision of the valuing route and other-including valuation are not formalized here.

## Closed loops: selection is the amplifier

When an organization's own selection decides what it meets next, the question is whether its activity can amplify valuation differences that shared exposure would erase. Under retention that contracts per encounter, two organizations that select alike at every step stay under shared exposure, and their difference shrinks. Any amplification must therefore pass through a step at which they select differently. Variation set by what is presented, rather than by what is retained, turns the loop back into shared exposure. The 11 further Lean results in §12 of the argument include witnesses for these points:

- Even the strongest per-encounter contraction amplifies a one-unit difference that straddles a selection boundary.
- A greedy estimator that never tries the better option keeps a self-confirming underestimate, and the gap in what it meets grows without bound.
- The same one-unit difference leads to exhaustion of one learner when only one option restores support. When both options do, it leads to sustained specialization of both.

Amplification is thus neither generative nor degenerative in itself. Which it becomes depends on how the signals each selection reaches relate to the support it restores. Exploration that depends on retained conditions remains open.

## What constrains the sign of a formed valuation

No analyst is needed. Within one history, the sign is constrained by the current coupling and the consequences actually reached. Across continuing histories, the [prefix bound](CONDITIONAL-UNIVERSALITY.md#3-the-universal-prefix-bound) gives a necessary condition at the level of the whole ledger. Where every event makes a positive demand on support, continuation needs provision recurring arbitrarily late. Attributing that provision to one coupling, or applying the bound across successors, needs further accounts that are not built here. Where support arrives independently, harmful or decoupled valuations continue just as well. Constraint on valuations needs no reward function, and it guarantees nothing favorable.

## Supplied and open

The following remain supplied: the organization/world boundary, deterministic selection, the consequence law of each model, the coupling sign of any concrete organization, and the variation that samples alternatives. Open questions are where natural systems sit among the capacities, exploration that depends on retained conditions, a stochastic restatement, and formal witnesses for revising the valuing route and for other-including valuation. Universal favorable direction and ethical warrant remain separate questions for the [unified ethical account](UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#describing-dynamics-and-answering-the-value-question).
