# Cognition, learning and evolution within generative unfolding

8 October 2026. AI-authored research account under [Ren's continuing objective](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-cognition-learning-evolution-goal.md). **The full objective remains active:** resolve the incomplete explanation of cognition, learning and evolutionary psychology. A completed local construction or the present exposition does not complete it. [Integration, evidence, formal scope and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/COGNITION-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-INTEGRATION.md) record the acquisition round. The [formation and repair continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/COMPOSITIONAL-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md) adds an operative construction and its correction.

**Central candidate.** Cognitive activity forms when differentiated organization makes consequential relationships available in discrimination, skilled response, anticipation and, in some cases, representation and reflection. Learning changes that organization through its history. Development reconstructs and transforms the capacities through inherited organization and encountered activity. Evolution changes the distributions and relations through which development and transmission recur. Their coupling explains how a way of learning can itself develop, how a learned distinction can acquire new uses, and how cognition can alter its own future conditions. These are distinguishable relationships within one process; none is an independent substance or an automatic ethical achievement.

## 1. The full target and its completion obligations

The requested scope concerns cognition, learning and evolutionary psychology together. A satisfactory theory need not predict every particular thought from a bare ontology, but must explain the relevant kinds of capacity, their formation and limits without silently supplying the very capacity at issue. Local law inputs remain legitimate when identified and connected to the relevant biological, developmental or social account.

## 2. What the higher orders actually explain

Begin with activity whose effects change later activity. Some relations recur sufficiently to distinguish an organized range of access and response. Within that history, changing a connection can change which differences are available, how long their effects persist, or which subsequent activity they influence. A body, learned model and shared practice are distinguished by these organized relationships and their histories, not installed as independent starting worlds. The [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md) governs the decomposition.

Ordinary regulation changes an ongoing condition through a response. Learning changes a disposition or relation involved in responding. Meta-learning changes how such modifications occur: which encounters are selected, what is retained, which errors trigger revision, or how a new procedure is constructed. Reflective inquiry can additionally make some of these formative relationships available as content. Evolutionary and institutional changes can alter which learning organizations form and continue. The same change may participate in several descriptions.

This classification is functional and relational. A slower timescale is not sufficient to establish a higher-order mechanism, and a meta-level need not occupy a separate physical component. Conversely, writing one encompassing transition function does not explain how its effective learning relations arise. The explanation has to identify the changed organization and the actual route through which the change affects later activity.

The [existing dynamics audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SYSTEMS-HIGHER-DYNAMICS.md) credits updater formation, method revision, acquired distinctions, recurrent revision and changing shared conditions. The [current evolution account](THE-THEORY-OF-EVOLUTION.md#the-next-theoretical-connection) now also credits acquired calibration and reference renewal. None should be reopened as wholly absent.

## 3. From organization to cognitive activity

The constructive explanation has several linked transformations.

**Differentiated access.** A dependency can affect continuation without being detected. A formed route makes some changes available to later activity through contact, signal propagation, retained effects or testimony. Access remains selective: the activity establishing a measurement can itself change the measured relation, and exclusion from a reporting route can conceal a real consequence. This connects attention to what encounters and records can occur; it does not yet explain every attentional phenomenon.

**Skilled coordination.** Retained organization can guide action without an explicit sentence, a detached inner observer or a reflective theory. The corresponding claim concerns an acquired capacity and the conditions of exercise. Accurate performance can depend on bodily and social supports that a narrow neural description omits. Habit and flexible skill require different explanations when consequences change.

**Representational content.** In cases where a distinction stands for a relation across encounters, acquisition, differential use and calibration constrain what it concerns. Content can remain the same when a report becomes false. A question, imagined possibility and assertion can concern the same condition while taking different roles in judgment. The [knowledge account](KNOWLEDGE.md) has developed these distinctions and the mechanisms of selective revision and acquired source dependence; this chapter reuses them.

**Understanding and inference.** A relationship becomes available for more than one trained output when the organization supports its implications, challenges and changed applications. Whether someone can revise a source's credibility, preserve the claim's referent, or separate contrary evidence from a changed desire supplies different evidence about that organization. This is the current affirmative account of understanding through differentiated answerability. Its universal sufficiency is not settled by a finite task battery.

**Combination and imagination.** Acquired components can participate in new organized combinations. An expression can be evaluated without its described action being performed, and the resulting anticipated trace can later guide a choice. The distinction between representing and enacting must be implemented by the actual access and execution paths. The new result below supplies a restricted compositional connection; richer counterfactual alternatives require an acquired model of the relevant dependencies and the limits of transfer.

## 4. New connection: acquired interpretation of unencountered combinations

The [new Lean module](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/CompositionalAcquisition.lean) joins actual probe records, acquired interpretation and later execution. Its grammar contains two atomic signs, a binary composition and repetition. A local law determines the two primitive effects and whether composition preserves or reverses the order of its arguments. The law is uniform across the admitted grammar; that uniformity is an explicit premise.

Three encounters expose the primitive effects and one ordered pair. With distinguishable primitive effects, the acquired code recovers the local law. **The checked implication then covers every finite expression in that grammar, including combinations never used during acquisition.** This is a symbolic consequence of the identified compositional law, rather than a claim that testing finitely many examples validates every possible law. A coordinated renaming also preserves the interpreted result.

The learner starts without an installed interpretation. Training records the actual probe outputs and consumes support under a declared convention. A later novel expression uses the acquired code and consumes additional support. One successful history spends eleven units in total; with seven it acquires the code but cannot execute the novel response. The numerical charges are this construction's accounting, not biological constants or a universal cost of thinking.

The result preserves four limits. Primitive-only observations cannot identify argument order in the distinguishable family. A later change of the operative order defeats transfer. A nonuniform law can agree with all three training encounters and disagree on the new expression. If the primitive effects coincide, unresolved internal order need not prevent a correct novel answer; full internal identification is not a universal prerequisite for competent use.

This advances the larger theory by connecting **acquired distinctions → reusable operations → new combinations → finite supported response**. It does not derive the grammar, parser, repetition operation, training curriculum or truth of the uniform-law assumption. A conventional compositional interpreter can realize the same relationships. Neural and symbolic realizations remain serious candidates; this proof establishes neither architectural exclusivity nor algorithmic novelty.

**新增连接。** 三次实际接触形成两个基本效果及组合次序的可用解释；在公开的递归法则范围内，后续解释适用于任意有限的新组合。形成出的解释须有剩余支持才能实际执行。原子效果不可区分时，内部次序未辨明也可能不妨碍当前新回答。语法、部件、接触安排与适用域仍有前提；这不是全部语言或认知已经证明。

### Formation and repair of the operative connection

The [second construction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/COMPOSITIONAL-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md) carries the acquired records into an apparatus whose composition route is initially absent. Generic connectors form candidate routes. A candidate is tested against the retained encounters, rejected when it fails, and replaced; the installed route then controls recursive use on new expressions. Failed construction consumes its declared support and parts. Retained approval cannot replace a damaged route.

Construction admits additional alternatives: a route may duplicate one argument instead of joining both. Consequently, the original examples can cease to identify an adequate operation. With identical primitive effects, a duplicate route passes the old records but fails on an asymmetric combination. A further reference encounter exposes the error and changes actual reconstruction. The repaired route supports another untrained expression. Without spare parts, the error can be recorded without successful repair.

**The expanded evidence theorem covers every finite expression within this four-route construction family, even when primitive effects coincide.** It requires the original records plus the asymmetric demonstration and the uniform recursive law. Exact internal order need not be known when both orders have identical effects. This extends the earlier result; it does not infer unrestricted induction from four examples.

The [checked package](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/compositional-formation-verification.json) contains 37 new theorems / 65 new declaration audits and separately rebuilds 86 inherited audits. Grammar, recursion, connectors, query choice, faithful demonstration access, search procedure and local charges remain explicit premises. The model forms the operative join route; it does not explain the origin of the entire apparatus or identify a neural implementation. The four-route repertoire and search procedure are supplied; this extension does not newly prove their autonomous revision.

**形成与修复。** 实际记录进入连接的构造、检验、失败与替换，新的组合确实经过形成出的连接。构造范围扩大能够使旧证据不足；新的不对称接触重新支持范围内的任意有限表达，而未必辨明所有内部次序。已消耗的支持与部件保留，发现错误不等于已能修复。37项新 Lean 定理是局部连接的完成，不是整体认知目标的完成。

## 5. Learning content, acquiring motives and changing ends

A learned relation can have several uses. It can guide a prediction, support an explanation, help obtain a wanted outcome, or disclose why a previously wanted outcome should be questioned. These roles depend on its actual connections to activity. A neutral relation can be acquired before it becomes motivationally significant; changing a motive can change action while some learned content remains correct.

The new compositional model contains only a minimal example of that distinction: the same acquired interpreter can support different choices under two supplied preferences. It does not form those preferences. Their formation and reflective revision are supplied by the existing [evaluative](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md), [goal-formation](GOAL-FORMATION-AND-REVISION.md) and [ethical-learning](ETHICAL-LEARNING.md) studies at their own scopes. No physical composition of every module is claimed merely by linking them.

A fuller affective explanation must connect bodily regulation, anticipation, salience, felt quality and social history where they actually occur. It must preserve cases of wanting something inaccurately represented, accurately recognizing a reason without acting, and care that persists without private return. Neither a reward variable nor retention success exhausts these questions.

Ethical justification follows the already explicit G0/J route. The generative account can explain how a motive develops and how consequences alter it; that history does not independently certify the motive. There is no requirement to restart an axiom-free proof of G0 to continue this cognitive investigation.

## 6. Development and evolutionary psychology

An inherited organization does not carry a complete adult psychology independently of development. Its causal contribution may be a particular response, a biased range of attention, a sensitive developmental period, an available learning operation, or a way of obtaining later support. Which contribution is present must be established. Equally, calling something learned does not explain the formation of its learning architecture.

The proposed connection is that transmitted organization changes how subsequent encounters reconstruct cognitive and motivational capacities. Those capacities change later encounters, relationships, care and transmission. Their effects can then alter which organizations recur. The roles of inheritance, development and social conditions are identified through these pathways within the common history, with their actual differences retained.

For a psychological disposition, ask four linked questions: what mechanism produces it now; how it develops; what effects it has on continuation and reproduction; and how its historical distribution arose. A present benefit does not establish its historical origin, and an evolutionary history does not reveal every current motive. Selection, drift, developmental constraint, byproducts and cultural transmission must be distinguished where they are live alternatives. The [evolutionary-organization account](EVOLUTIONARY-ORGANIZATION.md) supplies mechanisms for changing evolutionary organization, not a license to assign one adaptive story to every behavior.

This supports several concrete explanatory possibilities. Selection can favor a capacity for flexible acquisition without specifying every later belief. A reliably recurring disposition can be reconstructed through care or social practice. The same learning organization can give different outcomes after different histories. An acquired practice can modify the conditions under which its successors learn. These are conditional routes to investigate, not a claim that all psychological traits take one route.

The [Liangzhi evidence assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LIANGZHI-EVOLUTION-SOURCES.md) already preserves developmental and transmission examples with important limitations. Their experiments must not be spliced into a single observed genealogy. The complete psychological explanation still requires an identified chain joining the relevant inherited/developmental contribution to acquired discrimination, motivation, action and subsequent transmission in the claimed domain.

**演化心理的相连任务。** 说明某种心理能力现在怎样运行、实际怎样长成、改变什么接续条件、历史上怎样分布。遗传、发育与社会形成是同一过程中的不同关系，不能凭当下表现互相替代；当前适应成功不独自证明历史适应来源，更不证明伦理正当。

## 7. What evidence would advance or revise this account

The [source assessment and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/COGNITION-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-INTEGRATION.md#5-primary-evidence-and-comparisons) compare learned composition, biological meta-learning and developmental social response. They constrain different links. They are not a joint empirical validation or new experiments by this project.

Formation and revision of the operative composition route is now a completed conditional connection, as described above. The next selected connection is **formed cognitive operations changing the encounters and structures through which others or successors learn**. It must distinguish within-person revision, communicative coordination and transmission, reuse existing care/transmission and evolutionary-organization mechanisms through explicit interfaces, and preserve cases in which successful transmission loses distinctions.

The [new source comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/COMPOSITIONAL-FORMATION-INTEGRATION.md#5-developmental-and-evolutionary-evidence) identifies multiple routes to culturally shared structure and distinguishes adult cultural change from the development of the capacities it presupposes. Our inference is that the same mature structure cannot by itself identify its formative history. This is an explanatory obligation for evolutionary psychology, not a claim that all inherited contributions are absent.

In parallel within the full account, the evolutionary-psychology burden is to identify which developmental or transmitted changes account for a concrete psychological capacity. A candidate that merely reproduces the same adult behavior while leaving its developmental and historical origin arbitrary does not finish that requirement. Conversely, if an established developmental or computational theory supplies the same mechanism, its explanatory credit must be retained.

For the full objective, consciousness, broad affective organization, natural-language and social understanding, and the joint developmental/evolutionary correspondences remain active requirements. They are recorded here so that progress on composition cannot silently replace the requested whole.
