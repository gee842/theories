# Paying for independent evidence before command import

AI-authored research, 6 October 2026. Named connection: **actual acquired recipient memory → constructed and paid independent inspection → scoped command use → actual recipient consequence**. This extends [semantic calibration](SEMANTIC-CALIBRATION.md) and the accepted [recipient-reason/action interface](RECIPIENT-REASON-ACTION.md), within [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). Independent numerical reconstruction and the fresh formal audit are complete; the [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PREIMPORT-EVIDENCE-REVIEW.md) records acceptance at this conditional scope.

The new construction turns the earlier proposed comparison into a paid operation in the same continuing physical history. It blocks a corrupt archive's command when the original acquired controller remains intact. It also prevents a later read from treating an imported recommendation as independently acquired evidence. The result retains a strict boundary: a recent read of old memory is not a new observation of the recipient. Common corruption, silent change and faulty downstream execution still defeat it.

## The provenance problem is a real state transition

The original recipient updater acquires a command in its recipient component of `q`. A positive receipt can set `last=true`; under faithful acquisition and an unchanged recipient law, that qualified command is sound. Earlier proofs establish this implication from the actual updater's histories, not from arbitrary values of those two fields.

The action port has another route to those same values. Its import operation writes the recommended command into `q` and sets `last=true` from the certificate. This is useful for executing a justified recommendation, but it changes the evidential origin of the stored value. Reading the new value and comparing it with the recommendation would compare a claim with its own copy.

The physical join therefore needs more than another equality test. It needs an actual read before overwrite and an origin permission that cannot silently be renewed by that overwrite. The new module begins at the verified reason endpoint **before the first action import**, with that provenance supplied by the preserved predecessor history. A newly initialized flag on an already imported controller would not meet this boundary.

## A constructed, paid and single-use inspection

The [frozen experiment](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/calibration/pre-import/PROTOCOL.md) adds one A/H inspection module to the actual command port. Its finite functional recipe is supplied explicitly; counting two constituents does not derive its electronics, tariffs or meaning.

The controlled precursor starts with 34 constituents, common reserve 161 and source 154. Seventeen H formations and four A formations precede the inherited association, repair and thirteen capture/copy pairs plus final capture. The actual assembly now includes the old cells, readers and command port plus the inspection module. Construction costs 53. The remaining common reserve of 122 is partitioned into unchanged parent 89, command 6, later-reader 24 and inspection 3. Source 140 retains the old parent 130 and the action packet 10. The inspection reserve is actor-owned, stays separate and cannot rescue an unaffordable action. No recipient funds are taken.

The schedule is explicit:

1. The existing port pays 1 to read a current supported publication and bind its command to the recipient and lineage.
2. The new module pays 1 to read the retained recipient command, 1 to read its qualification and 1 to compare those latched values with the paid source-command latch.
3. Qualified agreement issues a one-use import permission. Disagreement remains unresolved; absence of a usable basis remains unknown. Those outcomes withhold this certified route under its declared policy, without declaring inaction universally best.
4. Before any import attempt, the module consumes origin permission. The existing import then pays 1 and the existing recipient trial pays 8, using a complete source packet 10 when work is attempted.
5. The protected reader reserve can reconstruct reasons from the old archive. That reconstruction does not obtain new evidence or rearm the inspector.

A damaged candidate read consumes its 1 and returns unknown. A damaged qualification read preserves the earlier payment and consumes its own 1. Insufficient reserve retains incomplete work. Missing assembly preserves its unused components and the actual saved assembly payment; formation is not refunded.

The two reads must describe one uninterrupted register state. A supplied causal interlock invalidates the partial latches on a controller write, publication change, same-context reopening, context change or destination replacement. Every instrumented write retires origin permission permanently in this one-shot mechanism. It cannot clear the latches and then let a retry authenticate the newly written flag as original acquisition. Faults that bypass that interlock remain outside its integrity premise and are tested separately.

The finite strict witness balances the whole original account:

\[
315=53+78+141+3+2+8+30.
\]

These terms are construction, earlier prefix expenditure/loss, inherited archive/reason expenditure, inspection, command read/import, physical trial and retained recipient stock. The prefix 78 includes 68 expenditure and 10 loss. The recipient gains 10 in the new trial and ends with 30; actor, source, reader and inspection operating reserves end at zero. This is an accounted model witness, not a measurement of welfare or a physical currency conversion.

The strict inherited success event reaches its additional receipt with probability 9/64. That event has a different scope from the aggregate capture mass 3/16. Some retained knowledge is readable while the controller remains inactive, so comparison approval does not imply affordable effective action.

An ordinary monitor using the same latched information, guards and charges matches every computed transition. The stronger direct learned-register and genuinely unaudited alternatives deliver more resource in this fixed comparison, while wasting more source. They omit unnecessary assemblies through actual original preparation, retaining unused parts and saved payments. No algorithmic advantage or unique practical necessity of explicit reasons follows from the guarded route.

The corruption case is nevertheless a substantive interface result. With a sound acquired anchor, a corrupt recommendation is prevented from overwriting the controller and triggering source loss. Both withholding and its cost are explicit. It does not follow that this guarded route dominates direct use of the intact acquired command, or that refusal is a complete response when the recipient needs timely support.

## The failures delimit the explanation

The old abstract copying counterexample now has a paid physical realization. In the naive post-import corruption case, the comparison still agrees on mass 3/8 after paying its inspection bill, but its independent-origin marker is false. It supplies no second evidential basis. The proper early-import control rejects later inspection while allowing the already bypassed ordinary action to execute; detecting lost provenance does not undo an import that already occurred.

Runtime origin markers must also be interpreted conditionally. The common-fault intervention flips the acquired recipient register before inspection while using the actual corrupt source history. It leaves the qualification flag unchanged and does not alter the registered publication. The runtime monitor cannot observe that the protected-register integrity premise has been violated. Its nominal pre-import agreement is therefore not proof of actual truth or globally independent causal errors.

The silent reversal changes only the physical recipient law used at work. It supplies no notification to the controller. Identical visible evidence produces the same decision and a different consequence. The inverted-import control isolates another failure: the checked command is correct but the actuator is made to install the other command. The pre-import proof consequently needs faithful subsequent import/actuation. The formal parent's consistency predicate is not smuggled into the implementation as a free live-register check.

A one-unit loss from the command reserve also matters. The reason can be compared and imported correctly, while the existing trial can no longer be funded and the scheduled opportunity is lost. Losing the third inspection unit instead leaves an incomplete comparison and no certified action. The separate costs distinguish knowing, approving, importing and doing.

Correct new evidence can contradict old qualified memory. That remains a semantic countermodel to a universal old-memory veto; this construction does not fabricate a new acquisition to make its guard appear adaptive. Its unresolved branch is a real remaining obligation.

## Verification scope and established comparisons

The numerical implementation covers 52 frozen cases, 156 producer/case arms, 1,976 continuing histories and 50,352 intermediate states. The [independent checker](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/calibration/pre-import/check_model.py) separately executes the new formation guards, inspection schedule and accounts. It uses the accepted parent's independently authored oracle for old observations, physical trial and archive replay, without importing the new producer's transition functions. It compares the complete continuing producer, reasons, identity, paid latches, physical phases and 63,232 per-history metrics. Eight deliberate corruptions test whether the checker rejects wrong read values, unpaid reads, rearmed origin, substituted destinations/targets, wrong phases and an omitted comparison.

The [formal package](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/calibration/pre-import/formal/README.md) passed a fresh Lean 4.29.1 build of 21 modules with 29 new theorem audits and no proof gaps or additional axioms. Its construction proof uses actual formation and assembly transitions, then projects the prepared M34 state to the accepted M32 parent while retaining the separate inspection reserve. Its event proof establishes paid coherent reads, irreversible origin consumption and conditional faithful import/recipient receipt. Its concrete witness begins with the accepted actual three-record producer/replay endpoint before import, reaches exactly the parent's imported state and inherits its feasible trial, increasing recipient stock from 20 to 30.

These proofs supply an exclusive inspection interval and faithful invalidation. They abstract the executable's content identity, installed/recoded flags and destination lineage rather than proving authentication or hardware isolation. Their read-feasibility conditions can allow an unsupported inspection that the Python readiness guards stop; successful agreement and the concrete witness have the stated narrower scope. The `retire replacement` event proves origin retirement for an arbitrary replacement state; it is not a global energy theorem about arbitrary external replacement. The receipt theorem requires sound acquired qualification, a stable recipient law, a feasible paid inspection, an enabled charged faithful import and a feasible actual trial. A conditional event-law proof, a concrete witness and the independently reconstructed finite Python distribution remain distinct evidence; there is no formal refinement proof connecting every Python transition to Lean.

## Connection to cognition, a future and governance

[Ren's clarification](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-alignment-governance-and-future.md) concerns a dependency that already operates before the actor thinks beyond its boundary. The [focused cognition source audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PREIMPORT-COGNITION-CONNECTION.md) distinguishes this conditional claim from stronger representation and connectivity claims. The acquired controller illustrates that distinction: earlier recipient consequences have changed an internal capacity, and that capacity later affects action without a reflective representation of interconnectedness. Its apparent internal sufficiency depends on a retained history of external interaction, material support and calibration. This functional example does not establish consciousness or identify every process of cognition with this controller.

The study adds a precise constraint to the [broader assessment](ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md). Integrating an external statement into action requires distinguishing the statement from evidence that warrants it. An imported claim can erase the very independence needed to assess it. Preserving the earlier basis can help under stable conditions; preserving it forever cannot substitute for learning when conditions change. Thus external dependence requires appropriately acquired, retained and revisable information where different external situations call for different actions. It does not require constant online connection, full representation of the external world or dissolution of useful boundaries.

An institutional analogue is an audit whose supposed corroborating record was generated from the decision being audited. Paying an auditor and obtaining agreement would not make that record independent. This analogy yields a testable governance design requirement: trace acquisition, authority, resources, revision and actual consequences through the check/use sequence. It is not evidence that this finite controller implements a legitimate institution. Nor does an agent's need for external resources or information make every other being indispensable or establish their standing. Explaining and justifying concern beyond usefulness remains a central bridge within the same account.
