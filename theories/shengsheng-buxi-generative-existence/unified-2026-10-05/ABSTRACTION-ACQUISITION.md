The named connection is **acquired prediction → revised distinctions → reusable compositional abstraction**, within [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The completed result is a conditional mathematical construction and a finite synthetic resource join. It is not a new learning algorithm, an empirical account of human mathematical cognition, or a derivation of the whole learning architecture from the root.

**Experimental-access continuation, 6 October 2026:** [the next bounded interface](EXPERIMENTAL-ACCESS.md) now uses this actual retained model and remaining resources to test reset and sensor/readiness assumptions. It preserves exact observational ambiguity and requires a paid calibrated consequence to separate some explanations. Broader faults and failed retention remain explicit. This closes part of the experimental-access gap identified below; model-family revision, evidence retention, architecture origin and general calibration remain open. An installed learner can acquire an exact abstraction without being given the correct state partition or an oracle that identifies its mistakes. It forms predictions from acquired observations, generates further tests, discovers a failure, and adds a distinction that makes its representation support subsequent actions. In a checked example, an actual earlier resource history pays for that acquisition, retention and a productive use on an untested continuation. The finite state bound, faithful experiment interface, learning procedure, memory cartridge and their prices remain supplied.

This advances the [previous structural results](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-STRUCTURAL-RESULTS.md): those established what an exact abstraction preserves and what evidence could certify it. ## How a new distinction is acquired

Consider a device with an unseen three-phase cycle. One action advances its phase. A contact reports 1 at one phase and 0 at the other two. The learner knows the action alphabet, output type and a valid upper bound of three states. It can pay to reset, act and read. It cannot inspect phase names, the transition table or the correct abstraction.

Its first observations are 0 before acting and 0 after one action. A one-state model fits this evidence and predicts 0 forever. The learner's own next test, two actions, returns 1. This is the acquired counterexample.

The learner now compares histories by the outcomes of their continuations. The empty history and the one-action history currently have the same output, but one additional action distinguishes them: one leads to 0, the other to 1. That observed continuation becomes a new coordinate of the representation. Three response rows emerge, carrying both outputs and action updates. Their transition cycle is learned from acquired entries rather than supplied as a correct partition.

| Stage | Acquired content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Initial fit | Empty and one-action observations are both 0 | One-state hypothesis fits the small table |
| Prediction failure | Two actions return 1 instead of the predicted 0 | The learner generates its own counterexample |
| Revised distinction | One more action separates two formerly merged histories | A distinguishing suffix is added |
| Compositional model | Three rows, with learned outputs and transitions | Updates preserve the represented continuations |
| Completed test | All words through length four agree | All finite words agree under the supplied bound and exact interface |
| Retained use | Five actions are predicted to reach the open contact | An unqueried composition productively routes an existing packet |

The new distinction concerns what can happen next, not only how things look now. A finite cluster of similar observations becomes a reusable mathematical state only when its action updates are well defined for the claimed questions.

## What the proof establishes

The [hand-proof study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ABSTRACTION-ACQUISITION-MATH.md) reconstructs observation-table learning. It retains access histories, observed endpoint answers and distinguishing suffixes. Closure supplies represented successors; consistency ensures that histories assigned the same state have compatible action updates. A completed table defines an accessible minimal candidate for its own behavior. It can still be wrong about the target.

The learner tests each candidate on all action words of length at most \(2N-2\), where \(N\) is a valid supplied bound on target states. A mismatch adds its prefixes and triggers further refinement. Each failed candidate forces a strict increase in the next candidate's number of states, which cannot exceed the target's behavioral classes. Therefore the information procedure terminates when the required operations remain feasible. A completed test establishes agreement on every finite continuation; minimality identifies the learned model with the target's reachable behavioral quotient, up to renaming.

These are standard active automata learning mechanisms, descended from [Angluin's L*](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~lreyzin/papers/angluin87.pdf). Combining learning with bounded conformance tests also has an established antecedent in [Peled, Vardi and Yannakakis](https://www.cs.rice.edu/~vardi/papers/pstv99.pdf). The contribution here is the explicit acquisition and resource interface within this research programme. A conventional implementation using the same evidence and procedure obtains the same learned behavior. It does not supply evidence of the theory's uniqueness.

## The actual resource connection

The [executable package](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/README.md) replays an unchanged ten-exposure path from [formed prediction and paid action](PREDICTION-ACTION.md). It has already formed a predictor, acquired a response, encountered dependency change and corrected its action. The selected path leaves 36 fuel units and two original source packets of 10 units each. Its probability is \(1/36\) of the original histories. This is a witnessed route, not the average reserve or the success probability of every possible continuation; unsuccessful preparation remains a separate mass \(7/9\).

The extension adds an explicitly supplied blank cartridge with 256 abstract symbol cells, present inertly from the start. Installation costs six units and retires the old task. The old predictor's binary record is preserved in the research history but does not teach the new phase model. Thus the earlier process supplies actual ancestry and funds; the new model's content is acquired through its own experiments. There is no demonstrated semantic transformation of the old binary prediction into this larger abstraction.

The cartridge, controller, stationary target, reset coupling and phase-controlled resource port are new local recipes. The old two backing slots do not by themselves imply the expanded capacity. The phase port is explicitly a new coupling to remaining source, rather than an identification with the old binary plant law.

Every observation, cache read, table operation, write, memory-retention interval, model construction and later action is charged before it occurs. Scratch space and command buffers count toward capacity and retention. Prices are a declared abstract resource law, not measured joules or Python instruction costs. The accepted primary run spends

## Failures that delimit the result

The [recorded comparisons](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/results.json) retain twelve named cases, including two exact funding-boundary counterfactuals.

- **More possible experiments can defeat affordability.** With a second action and the same bound, the exhaustive test alone requires 31 words, 98 actions, and an observation bill of 40 units before computation or installation. The inherited 36 cannot fund it. The implemented run stops without a certificate.
- **A correct acquired result can fail to become a usable capacity.** Excessive retention cost allows the finite test to finish but blocks retained use. A reserve 1/65,536 unit below the full schedule cost retains the model but cannot pay the final extraction operation. Future return cannot remove that obstruction.
- **A false bound licenses a false inference.** Declaring one state lets a one-state zero model pass its short test although two actions refute it. The learner cannot certify the state bound from the same finite transcript.
- **Systematic sensor error can be learned perfectly.** An inverted sensor produces a correct model of its inverted readings and a wrong interpretation of the productive contact. Its planned extraction fails and wastes a packet. Internally coherent prediction does not establish the action semantics.
- **An ineffective reset can conceal error.** Sequential drifting queries in the reset-failure control happen to return zeros. The learner accepts a wrong constant model under an unmet reset premise.
- **Refinement has a cost and a causal role.** A frozen first candidate encounters the same acquired mismatch but cannot repair it. Smaller capacity, unaffordable installation and fourfold prices also prevent completed acquisition.

Separately, all 234 labeled unary Boolean-output targets with one to three states and designated initial state 0 were learned correctly and minimally under explicitly ample assay funds. Of these, 222 acquisition-and-retention schedules fit the inherited 36-unit budget and 256-cell capacity; this is not a probability over natural systems and excludes downstream productive-use obligations. The exhaustive checks validate finite implementations, while the hand proof supplies the general conditional result.

## What changes in the derivation

The correct partition and externally supplied counterexamples have been removed from the learner's inputs. The learner's acquired history now produces the distinction, its recursive update and its bounded warrant. This closes a specific conditional acquisition interface and connects it to a real modeled resource prefix.

The remaining assumptions have become more precise: the architecture that implements revision; the generation and calibration of experimental access; a justified state bound; the choice of relevant outputs; and resource laws under which the capacity forms and persists. Successful execution does not derive these assumptions or establish a universal favorable direction.

The next substantive bridge is **how the learner obtains warranted experimental access and revises its model-class assumptions when access or predictions fail**, while preserving the costs and consequences of inquiry. In particular, the false-bound and inverted-sensor cases require ways to distinguish an inadequate abstraction from a defective observation or action interface. A procedure that simply receives the correct diagnosis would restore the dependency this programme is trying to explain. Where finite evidence cannot decide among these possibilities, warranted uncertainty must remain part of the result.
