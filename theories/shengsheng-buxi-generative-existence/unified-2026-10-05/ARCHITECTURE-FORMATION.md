# How organization changes the architectures it can form

6 October 2026. AI-authored development of [Ren's architecture question](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-architecture-formation-question.md) and [request to complete the next construction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-architecture-construction-request.md). This follows the preserved [proposal](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-PROPOSAL.md). **The finite conditional construction and its independent checks are complete.** Component premises, empirical identification and the remaining unified derivation retain their separate scope below.

**Organization can change its own development by producing the components through which later organization forms.** Existing activity pays for reusable material; that material changes subsequent assembly; the resulting connections change which conditions can activate a response. Their consequences then affect what can be maintained and what further material can be produced. This supplies a causal route from generative activity to a changing architecture of responsiveness.

The important addition to the theory is to explain **the production of the alternatives that learning and selection act upon**. A story that begins with fully available shared and exclusive programmes leaves this production unexplained. Formation, use, retention and warranted concern have different conditions. Explaining one does not establish the others.

## The connection being constructed

The [earlier concern construction](GENERATIVE-DEVELOPMENT.md#5-a-distinct-center-enters-the-response) carries actual formation, acquired prediction and motor-coupling revision into a resource-dependent response supporting a distinct recipient. It supplies the shared target-routing recipe. The [recipient-transmission construction](RECIPIENT-TRANSMISSION.md) physically installs and copies alternative routes, but supplies a uniform draw over named completed programmes. These results remain valid within their scopes.

The present construction moves behind that menu. It starts with the earlier learner's actual preparation and eight exposures, including failures, acquired records, orientation and spent resources. It then executes local fabrication and bonding events. A route becomes shared or exclusive because of the edges that actually form. After productive private activity pays for an additional docking guide, a second construction reads that changed material inventory.

The named transition is:

\[
\text{formed learner}
\to\text{local assembly}
\to\text{target-conditioned activity}
\to\text{resource-dependent persistence and guide copying}
\to\text{changed later assembly}.
\]

This is one finite private-learner-first history. It does not establish the proposed alternative in which differentiated centers and shared regulation develop together.

## The local parts explain the finished route

The [prospective protocol](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/PROTOCOL.md) supplies three component functions. A receptor **R** carries a supplied target request/cue; the model does not derive a deficit threshold or measure a need from the target's reserve. An identity comparator **D** passes an actor signal and blocks a recipient signal. The inherited predictor **P** supplies the acquired command through its actual motor coupling. Target identity and destination remain separate even when the predictor is reused.

Fabrication consumes finite precursors and actor fuel. Connectors can then form three local edges: R→P, R→D and D→P. R has one output port, so it cannot attach to both P and D. The R→P path transmits either target's signal. The R→D→P path transmits only an actor signal. There is no primitive lottery over programmes named “shared” or “selfish”. Execution follows the edges and the comparator's local predicate.

A D→P edge can form first and later be unused when R attaches directly to P. It still consumes its connector and fuel. Partial structures can exhaust their budget without becoming operative. This matters: a calculation over completed designs would miss both intermediate insolvency and the cost of unused structures.

The predictor-docking guides have amount \(e\). Additional comparator-docking guides have amount \(i\), with one base comparator affinity and rate multiplier \(b>0\). Given both fabricated nodes, sufficient fuel and connectors, the local competing events imply

\[
\Pr(\text{direct response path})=\frac{e}{e+b(1+i)}.
\]

For the primary case \(b=1,i=0\), one additional active predictor guide changes this conditional probability from \(1/2\) to \(2/3\). Copying a comparator guide instead changes it from \(1/2\) to \(1/3\). These probabilities concern assembly at a specified reached state. They are not the unconditional probabilities of obtaining helpful or retained circuits from the original preparation.

With only one unit available for unit-price bonding, only R→P as the first event can complete a path. Its probability is \(e/(2e+1)\), rather than \(e/(e+1)\). The larger formula requires viable intermediates. [The mathematical account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-MATH.md) separates these conditions.

## Consequences reach both maintenance and future construction

Each round has an actor opportunity and a recipient opportunity. Retention, sensing and work must be consumed before capture. An effective actor command delivers support to the actor. An effective recipient command delivers support only to the recipient, which pays for its own operation. The actor gets no return from that outcome. An incorrectly transferred command can instead damage the recipient's existing reserve.

After a private capture, the actor pays for route upkeep and, where a blank remains, copies a docking guide. The new guide is actual retained material used by the next round's local binding law. The fixed microscopic law therefore generates a changing effective distribution of architectures. An external evaluator neither chooses the successful graph nor resets the actor's resources.

The matched frozen-generator comparison pays the same copying bill and consumes the same blank but produces inert material. A second comparison produces a comparator guide. These interventions locate the causal role of what is copied. At the matched second-assembly state, changing active guides changes the assembly kernel. Over whole histories, production, later expenditure and retention can interact; an aggregate frequency alone does not isolate them.

After each recipient opportunity, completed graphs must pay their maintenance bills. An unpaid graph deactivates permanently; its bound parts are not refunded. This is a finite mechanism of retention and loss. It is not a population competition, a demonstration of evolutionary fixation, or an optimizer maximizing an observer's final fuel score. A well-funded ineffective structure can remain; a useful one can fail an earlier bill.

The reason a broad route can become available does not have to be a prior reward for helping. Private use can fund reusable machinery whose connections later admit another target. The persistence of that broader use is a further causal question, since its unpaid expense can weaken maintenance. Both facts belong in the explanation.

## Checked result and adverse regimes

The [exact experiment](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/README.md) contains ten declared cases and five arms per case. Its independent suffix interpreter has matched 390 reachable comparisons and 2,966 full event branches. A separate handoff reconstruction checks preparation and the eight-exposure prefix. The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-REVIEW.md) records the final verification scope and source hashes. These are exact finite computational results and internal review, not a new Lean certificate or an empirical identification.

The primary unconditional results include every original failed preparation:

| Consequence-funded product | Expected recipient captures | Probability of any recipient use | Expected graphs retained at the final boundary |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Active predictor guide | 17/108 | 13/108 | 2/9 |
| Matched inert product | 5/36 | 1/9 | 2/9 |
| Active comparator guide | 13/108 | 11/108 | 2/9 |
| No new construction | 0 | 0 | 0 |

A [fully recorded history](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/coupling/architecture/witness.json) starts with the actual preparation and eight learning exposures, reaches 21 actor units, and then constructs, uses and maintains two direct graphs. Private receipts pay for two new guides. It finishes with actor reserve 5, recipient reserve 22 and three active guides, with every intermediate bill and typed part accounted. This particular complete event word has probability 1/720; it does not include prior motor-coupling revision.

Expected counts can exceed event probabilities because there are two recipient opportunities and two graphs. Each effective capture supplies ten recipient units and is followed by one stipulated resource-dependent recipient operation. The recipient's initial four units could already pay that one-unit operation; this schedule does not derive a deficit threshold or prove that this receipt was necessary for operation. All actual eligible primary predecessors happen to have correct normal couplers; the eligibility rule itself does not test correctness. Earlier motor-coupling revision followed by recipient use occurs with unconditional probability **1/36** in this new experiment. This differs from the earlier supplied-recipe experiment's 1/18 because the local assembly law admits filtering and construction expense.

The active guide increases helpful use in the primary case, but all three copying arms retain the same expected number of graphs. The result establishes a changed production distribution and its consequences, not an overall survival advantage for broad response. The no-build intervention also saves more actor fuel over this finite horizon. It is a supplied comparison, not a spontaneously chosen action or an exhaustive optimization over possible abstentions.

Actual retention can oppose the production bias. With the declared higher maintenance bill, live copying retains **11/54** graphs on average, versus **5/24** for inert copying and **23/108** for comparator copying. At an actual inherited reserve of 21, two successive broad routes can help the recipient yet leave too little to hold the second graph; inserting a filter can avoid that expense. The changed frequency of retained architectures therefore combines formation with resource-mediated loss. No observer's ranking selects the survivors.

The destructive-transfer comparison is sharper still. Reversing only the recipient efficacy law preserves the actor's private outcomes and graph distributions but replaces the live arm's expected **17/108** recipient captures with **17/108** units of recipient damage. Activity can reproduce the conditions of a harmful response without receiving evidence that would correct it. Successful private use does not certify beneficial recipient transfer.

The other cases preserve expensive fabrication, low initial fuel, scarce connectors, absent copy material and cheap gating. Without another template blank, active and inert copying cannot diverge through new guides. Increasing comparator affinity changes well-funded production; lowering only the gate's binding cost changes expenses and marginal affordability without changing that well-funded rate formula. These comparisons separate formation bias, available resources and outcomes.

## What remains supplied

The model's component laws are explicit premises: compatible ports, the target-cue reader, target addressing, identity comparator, action efficacy, scheduled fabrication and target opportunities, and the chemistry converting a resource-dependent private receipt into a guide. It explains how these components form alternative complete routes; it does not derive their physical realization or the choice to attempt construction from the bare assertion that existence is generative.

The common initial fuel is 30 and the source is declared as 121 before preparation, leaving four ten-unit opportunities after the eight-exposure prefix. Two receptor precursors, two comparator precursors, four connectors and three template blanks are additional initial typed stocks. The recipient begins with its own four support units. These are finite supplied resources, not products of the older two-slot chemistry or fresh reserves added after selecting a successful predecessor.

The suffix is a declared finite read-only phase with resource-dependent predictor retention. It suspends the parent's wear and reconstruction law. Each round addresses a new receptor site; an older retained graph cannot bypass failed construction there. Reusing the inherited record for both targets supplies a common efficacy relation; the model does not acquire recipient-specific correspondence. The opposite-recipient-law case tests this boundary. It is not an already completed composition with [recipient correction](RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md), acquired evidence, reasons or successor learning.

The inherited predictor and primitive comparator already have functions. Moving from a finished-route menu to resource-dependent local composition closes a real explanatory gap, but leaves a smaller and more explicit set of component premises. Further progress requires deriving or identifying those components, not relabeling their contracts as consequences of the root.

## The theoretical addition and its next test

The addition can be stated without making favorable development a premise:

> **An organization's activity changes the material and informational conditions of its own possible reorganization. Those changes alter which response architectures can form, at what cost and with what probability. The consequences of their use then affect their continuation and further formation. The scope of responsiveness is therefore itself something that develops.**

This concerns access within actual time, material and resource conditions. It does not imply that a successor has more unrestricted all-time reachable states than its predecessor; under fixed laws, the predecessor could already reach them by passing through that successor. Nor does it impose a universal sequence in which all production finishes before selection begins.

The same account permits widened response, exclusion and destructive generalization. Production and retention can favor a readily formed or inexpensive architecture without making its treatment of a recipient justified. Functional responsiveness is a step toward explaining concern's implementation; ethical standing and warranted concern require their own grounds in the [ethical account](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md).

The subsequent [architecture-to-learning construction](ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) now completes **locally formed target routing → resource-dependent recipient-specific evidence → correction of that route's use and continued maintenance**, retaining actual guide, record and component histories under an explicitly extended initial source account. The old formation law remains unchanged. The new correction preserves actor correspondence and uses the recipient's actual outcome, yet faithful feedback gains no private retention advantage over matched absent feedback. Origin of the component contracts, alternative shared-regulation histories and justified evaluative influence remain distinct obligations.

The [identification assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION-IDENTIFICATION.md) separates relevant primary comparators from an unrun empirical test. Evidence would challenge this account if measured guide changes failed to change initial assembly under matched conditions, if apparent production effects were actually differential loss, or if target transfer could not preserve the recipient's identity and consequences. Exact equivalence to a conventional finite controller is expected; broader explanatory value must come from these causal identifications and tested dependencies.
