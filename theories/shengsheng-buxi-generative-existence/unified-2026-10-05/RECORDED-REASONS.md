## 1. The explanatory gap

The [revisable-concern settlement](REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md) gives a reason-revision procedure but supplies the sound current audit on which it operates. The [recording construction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-concern-theory/RECORDING-FIDELITY-INTEGRATION.md) supplies actual retained outcome words, but those words do not interpret themselves. A complete physical record can be associated with the wrong question, used after its context changes, or remain inaccessible to a successor.

This continuation constructs a finite producer for part of that audit premise. A paid reader interprets prebound record positions, checks the current context and reconstructs rooted support. The retained bytes, their meaning, the reasons they support and the ability to operate the procedure remain separate relations.

The checked semantic argument is explicit. Let \(F(c,e)\) state an edge-specific factual premise in context \(c\), and let \(R(c,v)\) state that the particular reason at node \(v\) holds. A calibrated positive assay supplies \(F(c,e)\). A separately warranted relevance premise supplies

\[
F(c,e)\;\Longrightarrow\;
\bigl(R(c,\operatorname{src}(e))\Longrightarrow
R(c,\operatorname{dst}(e))\bigr).
\]

Together with a valid root reason, a rooted chain of such certificates supports its endpoint. This does not obtain ethical standing from successful persistence. A negative assay defeats that edge's factual premise only under the separate calibration condition that its negative output implies the premise is false. Positive-output soundness alone does not provide this condition. Even a sound negative does not establish that the recipient has no standing or that no other ground reaches the same endpoint. Missing or inaccessible evidence is not a negative assay.

## 2. An actual finite producer

The declared preparation begins with capacity fifteen, common fuel eighty and source twenty, no formed constituents and a damaged site. The unchanged formation law produces seven H and eight A, associates one A and repairs. Six pairs become record slots, and the remaining pair becomes the reader/workspace. All construction is paid from the original common account.

The reader recipe explicitly supplies a finite query map, context token, certificate cache, working and published masks, and a path-checking circuit. Two constituents alone do not imply these functions. The map binds six addresses before outcomes: three candidate edge questions in each of two contexts. The actual recordings occur in their respective contexts, interleaved with audits. Future observations are not installed in advance.

The old recorder's fuel receipts keep their original physical interpretation. The new assay law separately relates its observed bits to the declared factual questions. That calibration and the reliable context interlock remain local premises. The recorder is not automatically an implementation of every recipient sensor.

An audit costs eleven common units: one activation/service, one current-context read, three decodes, and six relation checks over the four nonempty simple paths rooted at one of three nodes. Each path is prepaid before checking; all four are checked before publication. The finite language is explicit. No constant-cost closure result for arbitrarily large reason graphs is claimed.

The primary graph has transfers \(a\to p\), \(p\to q\), and \(a\to q\). The verified continuing history records `111` in the first context and reconstructs all three nodes. After a context change, it records `011` in three new slots. Re-auditing removes the first route to \(p\), while \(q\) retains the independent direct ground. A cold operational successor uses the same physical archive and reader, paying for a third audit from the remaining account. This is reconstruction of working state on retained hardware, not the formation of an entire new organism or a free copy of its archive.

The checked complete account is

\[
100=17\text{ preparation}+6\text{ slots}+1\text{ reader}
+12\text{ recording}+33\text{ audits}+31\text{ remaining}.
\]

The remaining thirty-one units are thirteen common units and eighteen source units. A fourth audit can spend eleven of the common units; after that, another cold replay cannot complete with only two, despite the eighteen source units. Later source cannot pay an earlier reader charge merely because the total is sufficient. For a valid dormant state with its reader installed, the writer idle and archive access available, the fixed audit is feasible exactly when common fuel is at least eleven.

## 3. Revision, access and freshness

The mechanism begins each reconstruction from its supplied root, rather than reusing unsupported old admissions as new roots. A cycle with no rooted connection cannot authorize itself. Independent alternative support survives the defeat of a different edge.

Context change preserves the archive and the old result's context tag while invalidating active use as a completed current audit. The context interlock is supplied physical behavior. It is not a theorem that paying for a reader makes arbitrary context changes detectable.

Freshness also concerns evidence growth within one context. A previously missing record may later arrive. An old published result can remain sound while being incomplete relative to the enlarged archive. The declared phase law requires leaving completed publication before recording can resume, and then paying for a new audit. A matching context tag alone is insufficient to establish current completed closure.

Blocking archive access can stop reconstruction without refuting the stored premise. Blocking an actuator can prevent protection without changing a reason's validity. These distinctions matter for an account of wisdom: a reason can survive a failure of recognition or power, while justified action still requires means and a response to conflicts.

## 5. The required next compositions

The [recipient-identification result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-concern-theory/RECIPIENT-IDENTIFICATION-INTEGRATION.md) illustrates an important limitation. Under its no-false-positive, stable-target law, a positive receipt qualifies an action. A zero during outages leaves the target unknown. That zero cannot be mapped to this construction's sound negative assay. A compatible producer must deliver qualified positive evidence and leave ambiguity unknown, or separately identify an actual defeater channel.

Its reusable registers and external diagnostic traces are also different from this physically retained archive. Any join must preserve actual receipt availability, query identity, component storage and payment. Reusing the old recorder's source-to-actor transfer on another center's receipt could double-count resources or invent a private return. The parallel evidence-producer work is addressing that explicit interface.

The new audit bill likewise cannot replace the settlement's monetary audit cost while preserving its old strategic inequalities. Effective authority, protection, suppression alternatives and subsequent renewal must be recomputed in a compatible process. The existing [concern/wisdom input analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONCERN-WISDOM-INPUTS.md) records these obligations.

This construction supplies a finite audit from actual retained evidence, with paid revision and reconstruction. Its calibration, relevance grounds, finite apparatus and positive anchor remain explicit. It closes this input-production interface without claiming that those grounds, the protective action or continued funding have thereby been derived.
