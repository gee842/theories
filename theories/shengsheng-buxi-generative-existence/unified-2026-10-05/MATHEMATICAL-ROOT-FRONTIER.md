## Formation and truth have different dependencies

The [new formation study](AUTONOMOUS-METHOD-FORMATION.md) asks how a learner can construct and replace the connections that select its mathematical procedures. Its causal question concerns the production, acquisition and subsequent use of real organization. A process can be autonomous in this relative sense while its component laws and environment remain specified. Requiring the organization to create every law making its creation possible would be a substantially different claim.

Truth constitution asks what fixes a mathematical proposition's truth, including propositions no learner ever encounters. Even a complete causal history of autonomous mathematical practice would not, by itself, specify every intended domain, identity condition, quantifier range or logical interpretation. The earlier [shared-root countermodels](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICS-CLOSURE-RESOLUTION.md#8-what-is-now-settled-for-the-root-claim) make that dependence precise: the same causal process and arithmetic base can coexist with different admitted subset domains and different answers to the designated closure question.

Adding causal machinery leaves that counterexample available whenever both semantic alternatives can still receive the same added machinery. Autonomous formation can improve the account of mathematical access and activity; eliminating the semantic alternatives requires an additional, independently justified bridge. This is a limit on that inference, not a proof that mathematical truth and reality are unrelated.

## A positive reconstruction of operations

Fix a finitary equational class of algebras \(\mathcal V\), including all its homomorphisms and its underlying-set assignment \(U\). For \(n\geq1\), consider a family of operations

\[
\eta_A:U(A)^n\longrightarrow U(A)
\]

that commutes with every homomorphism \(h:A\to B\):

\[
h\bigl(\eta_A(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\bigr)
=\eta_B\bigl(h(a_1),\ldots,h(a_n)\bigr).
\]

**Every such operation is a term operation of the given algebraic theory, uniquely up to its equations.** To see why, apply \(\eta\) to the free generators of the free algebra on n generators. Its result is represented by a term t. Every tuple in every other algebra is the image of those free generators under a unique homomorphism. The displayed compatibility forces \(\eta\) at that tuple to equal the evaluation of t. Conversely, every term operation commutes with homomorphisms. Two terms give the same operation everywhere exactly when the equational class validates their equality.

This is a reverse construction. Once the entire concrete class and its maps are fixed, they recover the operations and equational identities, rather than merely receiving an arbitrary chosen list of operations. It gives a meaningful conditional uniqueness claim: presentations with equivalent concrete realization data recover the same algebraic operations and identities. Here the equivalence includes a specified natural isomorphism of their underlying-set functors; abstract category equivalence alone is insufficient. Primitive notation need not be unique. The [proof and primary antecedent](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICAL-ROOT-FRONTIER-MATH.md) explain the precise reconstruction scope.

There is a direct specialization to the process root. Fix action labels A, take every deterministic system with one total transition per action, and include all maps commuting with those transitions. Every unary state operation natural across that whole class is **one unique finite action word**, including the empty word for identity. The proof evaluates the operation on the empty word in the free system of all finite words, whose transitions append an action. Mapping that system into any other forces the same word to act there. Chronological concatenation satisfies \(\delta_{uv}=\delta_v\circ\delta_u\). Thus finite generativity exhausts the uniform unary operations of this precisely stated process class; it is not merely one way to represent them.

For the universal proposal, the remaining burden is substantive. The causal root has not yet independently selected every relevant realization class, admitted map and underlying-set interpretation. Supplying those choices already supplies mathematical content. Compatibility with isomorphisms alone is also weaker than compatibility with all homomorphisms. General quantified or negated truths are not simply additional homomorphism-preserved operations. The deterministic specialization excludes partial and nondeterministic transitions unless a further interface is proved. These results establish forms of structural reconstruction, not a derivation of all mathematics from bare causal distinctions.

## Unique determination requires definability in a first order account

Let L describe the proposed root and let P be a new mathematical relation on its existing domains. Suppose an ordinary first-order theory \(T\) in \(L\cup\{P\}\) permits at most one interpretation of P for each fixed L-structure. Then Beth's definability theorem supplies an L-formula \(\theta\) such that

\[
T\models\forall\bar x\,
\bigl(P(\bar x)\leftrightarrow\theta(\bar x)\bigr).
\]

The proposed relation must already be expressible from the root vocabulary, under those assumptions. This strengthens the earlier information criterion into a reconstruction obligation. It does not say every intended root structure actually has an admissible expansion; existence must also be established. Nor does uniqueness in one preferred model establish the hypothesis: the requirement concerns every pair of admitted models with exactly the same root reduct. The [primary source assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICAL-ROOT-FRONTIER-SOURCES.md) records this distinction.

A successful first-order root argument can use this constructively: state the root language and admissibility conditions independently, prove existence and uniqueness of the required mathematical relations, and obtain definitions. Two admitted expansions with exactly the same L-reduct but different P refute the proposed implicit-uniqueness claim. Merely calling the added relation an expression of the root does not establish the missing condition.

## A single level cannot supply its own uniquely determined full truth predicate

Here the same test yields a sharper result. Let L have effective syntax coding, and let a consistent first-order theory T in \(L\cup\{\operatorname{Tr}\}\) contain an arithmetic base proving the diagonal lemma for every L-formula. Suppose that for every L-sentence \(\varphi\), T includes the adequacy condition

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner)\leftrightarrow\varphi.
\]

If T implicitly determined Tr uniquely over all identical L-reducts, Beth would give an L-formula \(\theta(x)\) defining Tr. Diagonalization supplies an L-sentence \(\lambda\) with

\[
\lambda\leftrightarrow\neg\theta(\ulcorner\lambda\urcorner).
\]

But the truth condition for that same sentence, together with the definition of Tr, gives

\[
\theta(\ulcorner\lambda\urcorner)\leftrightarrow\lambda.
\]

Together they contradict consistency. **These assumptions cannot all hold.** The argument does not assume that T's axioms are effectively enumerable; it is not merely another statement that a computable prover misses some truths.

The scope is crucial. This excludes a same-level, uniformly first-order, uniquely determined truth expansion for the whole coded root language. It permits a richer set-theoretic framework to define arithmetic truth, truth for restricted fragments, and a stratified sequence of semantic languages. It also permits a fixed intended structure to have a definite truth set in the surrounding mathematics. It does not disprove every metaphysical constitutive relation, nor contradict the earlier initial-algebra construction of arithmetic and its fixed semantic truths.

## The root already contains a substantive alternative to first order definition

Least finite reachability illustrates an alternative already present in the theory. Given an event graph, the relation “there is a finite path in the given graph” is uniquely determined by inductive closure. Yet no single ordinary first-order formula in the edge relation defines finite reachability on all graphs.

A compactness argument explains the difference. If such a formula existed, require it of two designated vertices while separately forbidding every finite path length. Every finite part of those requirements has a model: a sufficiently long chain. Compactness would then give a graph satisfying all the requirements, contradicting the formula's alleged meaning. Thus uniqueness by externally fixed least closure is stronger than ordinary first-order expressibility over arbitrary graphs.

The lesson is constructive: generative constitution can employ actual leastness, induction, typed domains and semantic levels. Each choice carries content that must be defended. Replacing ordinary semantics with “all intended possibilities” does not settle the question unless intended possibility, identity and closure are independently specified. Full powersets and unrestricted domains retain the separate obligations identified in the earlier closure study.

## What we can now claim and what would change the result

The positive frontier claim is:

> **A generative account can reconstruct a subject's algebraic operations and identities from adequately specified realizations and their structure-preserving maps. With independently fixed formation, identity and interpretation, its truths can be determinate even when no effective procedure exhausts them.**

This is stronger than universal encodability. It remains relative to substantive realization and semantic conditions. Exclusive metaphysical grounding would additionally require an argument that those conditions follow from our root, cover the intended mathematical scope, and make rival accounts equivalent or inadequate under a justified comparison. A universal common account may preserve many mathematical subjects; plurality itself is not a refutation.

The no-go result changes the form of the ambition. A consistently stated universal proposal must distinguish the root's constitutive scope from the expressive and truth-defining reach of any one arithmetically expressive first-order presentation. A layered or stronger semantic account remains a live research direction. It is not yet uniquely selected by the causal root, and adding it would be a substantive extension requiring argument.

The [mathematical development](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICAL-ROOT-FRONTIER-MATH.md) and [source review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/MATHEMATICAL-ROOT-FRONTIER-SOURCES.md) separate theorems, standard antecedents and philosophical inference. An independently justified reconstruction of the currently supplied realization/closure data would strengthen the root claim. A proposed model that preserves all stated root commitments while changing the same designated truth would defeat that version. These are specific tests; neither a successful autonomous learner nor a failed total truth predicate answers the entire metaphysical question by itself.
