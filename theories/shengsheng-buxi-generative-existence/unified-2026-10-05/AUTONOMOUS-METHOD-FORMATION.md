# Forming and replacing a mathematical method connection

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [method selection](METHOD-REVISION.md), following [Ren's stronger request](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-autonomy-and-mathematical-root-frontier.md). The named connection is **absent method connection → paid local formation → an actual trial → rejection from retained evidence → paid replacement → later enacted procedure**.

This finite connection now has a positive construction. The learner begins without an operative connection selecting either candidate generator. It forms a connection, tries the connected generator, and can remove and replace that connection when its candidates contradict acquired observations. A later invocation must traverse the resulting retained connection. The generic assembly/testing head and two generator recipes remain supplied. The demonstrated autonomy is the local formation and revision of their operative connection, not the autonomous origin of every component or learning law.

This is a stronger supporting study under the already [accepted conditional foundation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate). ## An absent connection has to become operative

As in the preceding method study, this is an explicitly alternative continuation from the actual acquired-H3 checkpoint. Fuel is `1030367/65536`, source10 and live storage15 cells. The complete earlier preparation/acquisition charges remain in the original145-unit account. No fuel, source or later diagnostic knowledge is restored from the more depleted continuations.

A paid generic head has two typed receipt input ports and callable output ports for G0 and G1. G0 generates the learned three-state machine's six phase/parity candidates. G1 constructs its declared four-state stutter alternative and generates fourteen candidates across the two models. Initially neither input has a bound route to an output. A complete route requires three stored fields: source port, destination port and bound marker. Reading a missing, broken or wrongly bound connection cannot fall back to direct dispatch.

The learner derives the positive word `aa` from its retained model, pays to reset and execute it, and attempts the remaining ten-unit resource packet. It pays separately to acquire and store the arrival receipt. Actual arrival, not the receipt symbol, determines resource credit. It then acquires six readings separated by five actions, pays for each stored bit, and maintains the resulting record. The older acquisition transcript remains unavailable.

The receipt now determines which input row is active. **The six acquired sensor observations drive rejection and replacement.** This differs from the earlier supplied rule that directly translated a zero receipt into broadening. An inverted receipt can change the addressed row without changing which model family fits the sensor record.

## Evidence replaces an actually tried procedure

The first output proposal is an unbiased local draw over the two ports. Both outcomes are included in the results. No hidden plant identity, desired-method label or external accuracy score chooses it. The learner pays to form the proposed edge, reads that edge, marks its output as tried, and invokes its generator.

Each candidate is replayed against the actually retained six-bit record. Both generator recipes are tested against that same previously acquired record; fresh native sensing resumes at the final forward-use test. Descriptor creation, record reads, predicted outputs, comparisons, simulated transitions and survivor writes all have explicit costs. A candidate stops at its first mismatch. The family is rejected only if every candidate mismatches. The six-reading profiles in the declared family are distinct, so a surviving result is unique; a multiple-survivor result is an explicit failure rather than an overwritten record.

If the family is empty, the learner pays to remove the edge and discard its trial storage. The tried mask excludes that output; a new paid proposal and formation connect the untried port. Logical deallocation releases rewritable cartridge cells under a stated storage law. It refunds no fuel and establishes no recycling chemistry. The external audit preserves every removed edge and its charges.

If the family is nonempty, the edge receives paid maintenance. The trial model and result are discarded. A later paid read must traverse the retained edge, **regenerate** its candidate family, and replay the stored evidence again. Thus the accepted edge is used after its formation test, and the controller cannot substitute the external audit's saved verdict. The selected model and current cursor are then retained, followed by a fresh forward action/read prediction and final maintenance.

The original H3 remains stored even when H4 is selected, because the retained generator descriptor refers to that original model. The receipt, six-bit archive and edge also remain stored. The unobserved receipt row stays absent on ordinary paths; one formed row does not supply a complete policy for every future receipt.

## The controls locate both the autonomy and its limits

Clamping formation still pays for its allocation but produces no bound edge; dispatch stops. Binding the wrong source port also stops dispatch. Damaging an accepted edge prevents its later invocation, even though its earlier evidence test completed. Swapping the first destination changes which generator actually runs. These interventions identify the operative connection's role rather than attributing every result to a stored label.

The [independent reference](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/methods/formation/independent_check.py) reconstructs the new execution schedule, candidate replay and every exact payment/cell prefix, using the previous independently written native and accounting laws. It matches448 admitted environment/phase/fault/proposal histories,35 saved fixtures and24 state-renaming cases, with eight additional strict payment-boundary controls. All448 complete with the correct future reading model within the admitted class;128 include actual rejection and replacement. The [proofs](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/AUTONOMOUS-METHOD-FORMATION-MATH.md), [protocol and results](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/mathematical-abstraction/acquisition/access/revision/metarevision/methods/formation/README.md), and [adversarial review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/AUTONOMOUS-METHOD-FORMATION-REVIEW.md) retain their different scopes. These are synthetic execution and internal review, not empirical identification or a new Lean certificate.

The counterexamples are consequential:

- **No cost optimum is learned.** H3 accepts G1 even though G0 is cheaper. The rule rejects inconsistency; it does not compare every acceptable method's efficiency.
- **Finite evidence does not establish unrestricted correctness.** A ten-state process agrees with the paid archive and the next prediction, so a wrong H3 model is retained. The five-state control instead contradicts its accepted model at the next use. Both outcomes are preserved.
- **Receipt and sensor evidence have different roles.** Correct reading-model acquisition does not diagnose transfer blockage, establish the readiness channel, or repair the reason the packet failed.
- **A formed route can be unaffordable to finish or use.** Exact funding and denied-stage fixtures distinguish acquired observations, stored bits, maintained archives, formed edges, completed tests, accepted connections and actual later invocation. A physical action can occur before an unaffordable cursor write. Denied maintenance is not modeled erasure.
- **The retained record has an acquisition time.** It locates the phase at the end of its six readings. The later physical action requires the separately committed current cursor. Replaying the old archive alone after further action would not establish the new current phase. No indefinite re-invocation loop or renewable source is implemented.

## The stronger autonomy claim we have earned

A fixed underlying law can produce a connection that was absent, expose it to acquired evidence, remove it and form a replacement. The completed connection need not be an initial component merely because its primitive formation laws are specified. This is the level at which the present construction advances the explanation.

It does not make the entire architecture self-originating. Typed ports, component meanings, G0/G1 code, the generic test-and-replace machinery, six-reading schedule and resource tariffs remain local premises. The result removes an initially supplied operative connection; it does not prove that the replacement specification is smaller or more fundamental. It must not be redescribed as autonomous invention of those generator recipes or as acquisition of the very testing law it executes. Their formation is a stronger, now more precisely identified problem.

The next substantive origin claim would have to replace a named supplied component with an actual, paid generative history while preserving this working interface. Merely moving its code into a lower-level “primitive” would not discharge that obligation. Conversely, requiring freedom from every law or initial condition would replace the present causal question with creation from nothing.

For the theory–mathematics relationship, we now have an explicit sequence from a learned representation through locally formed, tried and evidence-revised method selection to further mathematical model use. The [separate reconstruction and truth analysis](MATHEMATICAL-ROOT-FRONTIER.md) explains why this stronger causal autonomy still does not, by itself, establish that our root uniquely constitutes every mathematical truth.
