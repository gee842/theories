## Recursive generative selection across scales

7 October 2026. [Ren proposes that generative organizations are favored through evolution, that degeneration undermines continuation, and that this pressure propagates between wholes and parts](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-recursive-generative-selection.md). The [integrated investigation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) develops this as a positive mechanism: consequences for shared renewal change actual successor formation; successors retain or reconstruct the relevant organization; and the relationships connecting renewal to transmission can themselves recur or change.

This is a stronger proposal than shared dependence alone. Parts and wholes must be identified through actual coupling, formation and inheritance; overlapping dependencies need not form a fixed hierarchy. A locally costly contribution can spread through the greater contribution of its collective to later populations. The [primary-source assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION-SOURCES.md) gives experimental support for that route and for changes in collective life cycles, while retaining a conditional evolutionary-extinction model. Local disadvantage does not refute the proposed wider selection, and eventual local collapse does not by itself eliminate a transmissible trait elsewhere.

The [new Lean audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION-MATH.md) makes one inference exact. Under a supplied support-production and faithful-transmission law, a support-rich collective can produce more descendants while its producers lose relative frequency both within collectives and globally. Stronger production-mediated support under otherwise matched conditions reverses the global result while leaving the within-collective disadvantage. Thus collective success can favor generativity, but greater collective success alone does not prove that favorable pressure reaches its contributors. The theorem's exact threshold is local to this law; neither long-run recursion nor biology is derived from it.

**Stronger hypothesis retained:** selection can favor arrangements that keep future selection connected to renewal. The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RECURSIVE-GENERATIVE-SELECTION-REVIEW.md) specifies its next test: compare recruitment before depletion consequences with recruitment through actually restored support, and compare preserved association with mixing. Then allow those recruitment arrangements themselves to vary and be reconstructed. Their cost, bypass, lost variation and possible total failure must remain in the account.

**Universal ethical connection:** these mechanisms can explain formation and stabilization of a generative orientation. Ren's positive direction supplies the candidate practical conclusion that human activity should renew the ecological and social conditions of further differentiated life. Evolutionary success alone does not establish its ethical authority or require every individual to be productive. A single connected ecosystem's persistence also does not establish reproduction among competing wholes. The [current ethical account](UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#recursive-selection-and-the-generative-direction) retains that universal grounding task.

## 1. Shared dependence and the selection of its maintenance

An indispensable condition need not confer a relative advantage on the actor maintaining it. In V7.3 §3.2, material returns have the form

\[
\pi_i=\phi_i+(\nu-\tau_{\mathrm{eff}})e_i+G(V).
\]

Here \(e_i\) is a practice's harmful load, \(\nu\) its private marginal advantage, \(\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\) effective liability, and \(G(V)\) an additive benefit of the shared host received identically by all carriers. A relative selection law satisfies

\[
\mathcal F(p,\pi+\xi\mathbf 1)=\mathcal F(p,\pi).
\]

Consequently the common term does not alter the relative-share dynamics. This does not remove its physical importance or make participants indifferent to it: absolute survival and available opportunities can still depend on the host. Unequal exposure or a change in the selection law can defeat the additive cancellation. It separates three objects: actual support, what an actor values, and the rule distributing continuation among practices. Under the further payoff-monotonicity and equal-background conditions, load is favored while its private advantage exceeds liability. Stronger convergence claims require cumulative selection and the additional hypotheses of Proposition 3.4.

The existing [shared-return analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md) already makes this distinction concrete: useful repair can raise total reproduction without raising its producer's expected frequency under the declared equal per-capita rates. The two results use different statistics and laws; neither is a proof of the other. Together they locate the same missing causal interface: **how a supporting consequence changes actual retention, transmission, reward, liability or an enforceable constraint**.

There is a constructive counterpart. A common rule with equal additive incidence need not disadvantage one compliant type relative to another. That neutrality does not stop evasion: expected enforceable sanctions must separately meet the evasion incentives in the declared model. Likewise, common benefit alone gives no selection advantage to a costly protective trait among otherwise alike carriers, even when grouped. Correlated private benefits, biased transmission, locally shared benefits and changed constraints can change the outcome. This is the scope of V7.3 Lemma 3.11, not a theorem that voluntary cooperation or selection between groups never works.

**Integration:** organizational significance can ground a task for regulation, but it does not secretly install the selection law that rewards regulation. The account now carries that distinction explicitly from formed material repair to social maintenance. Primary derivations: [V7.3 §3](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/30_s3.md), [Supplement S3](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/03_S3.md). Existing paid social realization: [market selection and liability](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md).

## 2. Effective priorities under compounding

The current learning studies distinguish factual knowledge from acquired evaluative influence. V7.3 adds a further distinction: an actor's held concern can remain unchanged while the relative value of securing future capacity changes its present conduct.

Proposition 3.9 derives a local working self-model \(\theta\) from a held weight \(-1<\theta_0<n-1\) in \(J_i=\pi_i+\theta_0\bar\pi_{-i}\), where \(\bar\pi_{-i}\) is the other carriers' average material return. The continuation returns of a relative and a common gain then give

\[
\frac{1+\theta}{n-1-\theta}
=
\frac{1+\theta_0}{n-1-\theta_0}
\cdot\frac{1+\lambda_{\mathrm{com}}}{1+\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}}.
\]

For interior held weights, the working weight narrows when \(\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}>\lambda_{\mathrm{com}}\), widens when the inequality reverses, and is unchanged at equality. The result is first order near the specified symmetric steady state or balanced path, with fixed rival conduct, constant linearized coefficients in the admitted case, and the stated finite-return and differentiation conditions. It is not a global equilibrium or a general formula for every learning system.

This working weight is a representation of incentives, not evidence that the actor has acquired a new moral belief or lost factual knowledge. V7.3 Remark 3.10 separates an incorrect held boundary from a strategically induced working objective. Better understanding can correct the first directly; changing the second requires changing relevant returns, constraints or feasible alternatives. Narrowing by itself also does not prove concentration: diffusion can keep shares convergent while the working objective narrows.

**Integration:** distinguish factual belief, held evaluation, and effective priority under future selection. The [plasticity construction](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) changes an operative evaluative connection; the V7.3 calculation changes the incentive interpretation while holding the underlying concern fixed. These are complementary causal routes and cannot be identified from the same observed action without further evidence. A reason's validity remains separate from both. Primary derivation: [Proposition 3.9 and Remark 3.10](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/30_s3.md), with [S3's convergence and strategic limits](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/03_S3.md).

## 3. Selection over the network of correctors

The unified account already includes costly interference, paid correction and endogenous rule erosion. The [market model](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md) gives a genuine loop from practice selection through paid accumulated influence to collectible liability and back to selection. V7.3 adds several interacting domains: evaluation, rule-making, funding, appointments, access and formation of future judges.

In its supplied field law,

\[
\dot x_i=\zeta_i x_i(1-x_i)(a_i z_i-c_i),\qquad
\dot z_i=\varepsilon_i\left(\sum_j b_{ij}x_j-d_i z_i\right).
\]

\(x_i\) is the share of consequential decisions following the dominant practice in domain \(i\); \(z_i\) is supporting influence; \(b_{ij}\) is conversion from success in domain \(j\); and \(d_i\) removes that support. The normalized gains are \(G_{ij}=a_i b_{ij}/(c_i d_i)\). Under the source assumptions, a full inbound row sum below one certifies eventual recovery for that domain from \(x_i(0)<1\) and finite initial support. It does not certify safety throughout recovery. Large inherited support can produce a dangerous transient, and the exactly absent alternative at \(x_i=1\) cannot regenerate through this share law alone.

A locally improved evaluator may therefore remain dependent on unreformed funding or appointment channels. Conversely, limiting the relevant conversion or maintaining independent support can prevent the reinforcing path within the stated law. The important unit is the actual dependency network; counting nominally separate reviewers does not establish independence. The model's correction rate is still a supplied parameter until a funded, authorized mechanism realizes it.

V7.3 also gives a local selection argument for appropriable influence. Share enters both the production of influence and the decisions it favors, giving a squared-share return under its specified cost and transmission law. That result does not prove that every large organization captures oversight. Capture requires separately established success-driven control, persistent private advantage, and impairment of an independently specified entrusted purpose, protected interest or corrective function. Concentration and legitimate revision alone are insufficient.

**Integration:** maintaining correction now includes explaining the dependencies that maintain its independence, alongside its resources and authority. Joining this network to the market model would require a declared mapping from domains and influence to liability, with compatible timing and payments; a single accountability variable does not preserve every network path. Even the share symbols differ: V7.3's \(x_i\) follows the captured practice, whereas the market model's \(x\) follows renewing practices. Primary derivations: [V7.3 §4](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/40_s4.md), [S4](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/04_S4.md), and [S16's bounded host/capture composition](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/16_S16.md).

## 4. Delegation and the renewal of independent competence

A process can improve current performance while consuming the capacity needed to question or replace it. V7.3 Lemma 6.3 identifies one mechanism: independent task competence decays when displaced and recovers when exercised. With competence \(M\), the local laws are

\[
\dot M=-\delta_h M\quad\text{under delegation},\qquad
\dot M=\varrho_h(\bar M-M)\quad\text{without it},
\]

and operation without the delegate requires \(M\ge M_{\min}\), with \(\bar M>M_{\min}\). From \(M_0\ge M_{\min}\), continuous delegation reaches the threshold after

\[
T_{\mathrm{lock}}=\frac{\log(M_0/M_{\min})}{\delta_h}.
\]

Below the threshold, restoration takes time during which unaided operation falls below the specified floor. Parallel maintenance can avoid this loss but consumes resources or, in the contest model, competitive strength. This is a conditional competence mechanism, not a universal empirical law of automation.

The associated strategic model explains why participants may nevertheless delegate when the private speed advantage outweighs the added shared control loss. Other assumptions, including pricing a certain loss of authority, can instead favor nondelegation. The model therefore preserves constructive and destructive choices under different conditions. Loss of competence, valid delegation of authority, and irreversible loss of control remain distinct. Capacity loss need not be permanent when recovery time and support exist.

**Integration:** inherited or nominal authority must be accompanied by usable competence, evidence, time and feasible fallback wherever exercising it requires them. This gives a specific realization of the current principle that action changes future agency. It extends [strategic depletion of correction](FORMATION.md) and [succession with actual resources](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md); it does not establish that their actors literally obey a human disuse law. Primary derivations: [V7.3 §6.3](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/60_s6.md), [S6](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/06_S6.md).

## 5. Selection and the conditions of shared understanding

Evidence is generated and communicated by actors whose opportunities may depend on what others believe. A sender can benefit from a receiver's restraint without intending reciprocal restraint. Shared exposure therefore does not alone certify the credibility of a warning.

V7.3 Proposition 5.16 makes one part of this mechanism exact in a specified uniform-quadratic cheap-talk game. With a common objective weight \(\theta\) and the stated third-party payoff and concavity conditions, the effective sender–receiver bias is

\[
b_{\mathrm{eff}}=b_0\frac{n-1-\theta}{n-1+\theta}.
\]

Within that model, narrowing objectives increases conflict and limits informative communication. At the two-actor purely relative boundary, the separate zero-sum argument gives payoff-equivalence to a no-talk equilibrium; it does not require that no words or information are ever transmitted. Costly signals and independently verified channels lie outside the cheap-talk theorem. Narrative selection, professed doctrine and broader claims about language retain their distinct evidential or conjectural statuses in the predecessor.

The current [faithful-reporting account](EVALUATION-AND-FAITHFUL-REPORTING.md) already shows why a valued future can motivate a convenient report rather than a faithful one. The new connection concerns strategic informativeness between centers as well as the reliability of an individual report. More data, intelligence or communication bandwidth alone does not establish either property.

**Integration:** shared understanding needs a causal account of acquisition, communication, calibration, incentives, independent preservation and usable response. Verification and observable commitments can change that account when their costs and institutions are supplied; truthful communication is not installed by calling a channel shared. Substituting §2's locally derived working weight into this communication game is a proposed interface, not an established dynamic composition: its actors, utilities, equilibrium and changing information must first be made compatible. Primary derivations: [V7.3 §5.4](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/50_s5.md), [S5](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/05_S5.md); constructive proposals: [§11.1–11.2](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/b0_s11.md).

## 6. Reform, time and the availability of alternatives

The current [market recovery theorem](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md#recovery-before-support-failure) already distinguishes a protected invariant region from a funded path into it, preserving every intermediate support floor. V7.3 adds slow institutional state, temporary interventions, and the availability of an alternative that can expand.

In the specified field model, insulating rules can first reduce supporting influence while the dominant share continues rising. An improved visible share can also precede departure from the basin of capture. Intervention must persist beyond the model's recovery boundary, with a margin; release exactly on the boundary approaches a saddle. An archive or a fixed protected share outside selection is different from an alternative that persists and recruits when conditions change. The latter can bound recovery time under the stated model, while loss of all alternatives defeats replacement through that selection law. This is a reason to examine particular alternative capacities, their costs and their actual routes into use; it is no unconditional demand to preserve every possibility.

V7.3 Proposition S17.1 supplies a finite transition in a reduced joint model: an installation delay, a temporary correction increase, a structural reduction of incoming influence, then ordinary maintenance. Its sufficient conditions account for transition time, initial reserve, finite construction resources and continuing revenue. Executable authority, durable reform, effective liability, the conversion from expenditure to correction, and the declared fixed-trait selection law remain premises. S17 does not itself preserve every dimension of agency or hazardous-capability control discussed elsewhere in the paper. Failure of its sufficient certificate does not prove that all reform is impossible.

Irreversibility constrains the entire path. Recovery after a protected loss is too late for that loss; eventual correction does not substitute for timely response. The existing backlog and prefix-resource results, and V7.3's separate delay/coverage results, retain their different variables. Workload, hazardous variety and a residual-risk floor cannot be identified merely because all concern correction. In particular, failed assurance does not establish a positive catastrophe probability without additional premises.

**Integration:** a generative alternative must satisfy its justified support and protection requirements throughout an executable transition. Its incumbent forms may be replaced; indefinite survival of the institution is not the protected end. Primary derivations: [V7.3 §11.4–11.6](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/core/b0_s11.md), [S11](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/11_S11.md), [S17](../../../drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/v7.3/src/supp/17_S17.md). ## 7. Their place in the common derivation

These mechanisms explain how capacities already formed within a process become conditions of other processes, and how those relations feed back into their own continuation. They enter after and alongside the existing formation and learning results, without becoming new axioms about all reality.

| Existing transition | Integrated mechanism | What the connection requires |
|---|---|---|
| Dependency → regulation → retention | Shared support can remain invisible to relative selection | Identify the actual differential retention or constraint route; usefulness alone cannot supply it |
| Learning → evaluation → action | Compounding can alter effective priorities with held concern fixed | Distinguish acquired valuation from a local continuation-price effect and from justified reasons |
| Selection → revision of selectors | Success changes a network of evaluation and authority | Identify control, its conversion law, the impaired function, maintained independence and paid intervention |
| Implemented action → continued agency | Delegation can erode the competence needed to revise it | Identify disuse/recovery laws, task floors, support, permission and an executable alternative |
| Individual evidence → shared understanding | Strategic incentives change informative communication | Preserve distinct actors, truth conditions, information, incentives and actual verification |
| Corrective possibility → durable transition | Slow state and scarce alternatives determine whether reform persists | Retain intermediate floors, sufficient duration, recruitable alternatives, construction and recurring resources |

The integrated explanatory route is thus: **activity changes shared supports and relative opportunities; those opportunities affect which practices persist and which actions gain support or confer a selective advantage; success can alter future evaluation, evidence and authority; their condition determines whether consequential learning and warranted concern can still guide action.** The same account permits restoration when the relevant feedback, access and maintenance conditions change.

This is a connection of conditional mechanisms, not a theorem that every arrow operates together. V7.3's conjunction of erosions remains its empirical conjecture; the current material, market, strategic and communication laws are not one implementation. The [mechanism audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/V73-MECHANISM-AUDIT.md) and [evidence/interface assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/V73-EVIDENCE-AND-INTERFACES.md) record these boundaries. Neither common mathematical vocabulary nor compatible-looking signs prove a causal handoff.

Before claiming a combined realization, identify the same carriers and affected centers; distinguish material return, held objective, welfare and survival; map actual producer outputs to consumer inputs; preserve clocks, intermediate reserves, information and authority; and show which local laws remain true under the coupling. The [controlled-refinement account](CONTROLLED-REFINEMENT.md) and [existing composition map](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FOUNDATION-COMPOSITION.md) already impose these obligations. This chapter does not add a new completion gate to the accepted conditional foundation.

## 8. Ethical direction, scope and the next connection

The six mechanisms explain the practical influence of already formed concern: whether it affects action and remains effective. Its formation is supplied by the separately credited developmental studies. These mechanisms do not establish its ethical ground. V7.3 declared affected-party standing separately from its dynamics; the [current ethical grounding](UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md), [strongest-claim investigation](STRONGEST-FOUNDATION-CLAIM.md) and [ethical-minimum study](UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) continue the justificatory work. A patient's reason does not disappear merely because relative selection ignores it; a profitable or stable concern does not certify that reason's validity.

The integration strengthens the account of wisdom by locating the conditions under which correct understanding can become ineffective. Where independently warranted responsibilities require continued correction, wisdom may require changing incentives, protecting evidence, maintaining competence, funding a transition or relinquishing control. Which response is warranted still depends on the actual patient, permission, competing claims and feasible means. The six mechanisms do not privilege any institution's survival, mandate one universal score, establish exactly two attractors, or derive V7.3's AI policy preferences from minimal causal premises.

**Completed here:** integration of all six conditional mechanisms into the derivation, their existing overlaps, constructive/destructive regimes, inherited claims and remaining interfaces. The [independent internal review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/V73-INTEGRATION-REVIEW.md) and [integration record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/V73-INTEGRATION.md) distinguish this completion from a new joint model, empirical validation or universal ethical proof.

**Next causal connection:** in a justified application, connect an actually acquired evaluative influence to a specified continuation-return and retention law, then show whether it remains effective when its private advantage changes. Keep factual knowledge, held evaluation, effective opportunity and institutional constraints separately identifiable. An equally informed ordinary controller is a relevant comparator; behavioral agreement alone cannot identify which mechanism operated. A wider network or communication join should be claimed only when its additional interfaces are realized.

**The stronger foundational connection remains:** why an affected being's actual good supplies eligible actors reasons independently of their prior desire or role, and what justified requirements follow under competing goods and cumulative burdens. The causal integration supports that continuing programme without substituting selected behavior for justification.

Results that would change the account include a failed source implication under its stated assumptions; an identified system in which a proposed mapping misdescribes selection or control; a supposed composition that resets a reserve or conceals a changed beneficiary; or a simpler explanation that accounts for the observations without the proposed channel. Correlated private benefits, affordable independent competence, informative verified communication and successful alternative transition paths are also informative outcomes, not exceptions to suppress. The aim is a common explanation of the divergent regimes with each premise visible.

### Chronological prediction from inherited history

The [new forecast test](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LINEAGE-FORECAST-INTEGRATION.md) uses only earlier observations to predict phase viability and the viable descendants of already assigned donors. Across 3,424 scored outcomes, a conventional Bayesian lineage model improves on a changing stage-average model in all four treatments; stable-treatment subsets preserve advantages of a constant predictor. This makes the predictive relevance of inherited relations concrete, but supplies no separate victory for the generative interpretation of the same model. The actual reconstruction history, supplied medium and replacement activity remain one connected process. The next connection is independently calibrated switching/development → transition law → prediction under genetic or transfer changes. Priors and phenomenological beta transitions remain supplied; no new Lean theorem or ethical authority follows.

### Shared conditions and transfer of developmental estimates

The [follow-up comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DEVELOPMENTAL-TRANSFER-INTEGRATION.md) tests two conventional changing-block alternatives. A separate dated-assay test shows the focal genotypes' first-phase rank reversing between February and June; the incomplete February follow-up cannot calibrate their whole-cycle contrast. A single favorable genotype fitness cannot supply that explanation. The prior genetic-reversion result and conventional lineage mechanism remain credited; no new theorem or general superiority follows.
