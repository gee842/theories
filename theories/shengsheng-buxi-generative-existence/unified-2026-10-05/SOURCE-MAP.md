# Source map, provenance, and evidence boundaries

This map identifies the sources of the [unified theory](THEORY.md) and [formal core](FORMAL-CORE.md). The synthesis prioritizes the completed October 2026 research branches and their latest corrections. It is a targeted integration, **not a new exhaustive reading of every document in the older corpus**. Earlier canonical syntheses provide genealogy and preserve material outside the present scope.

Research direction and the motivating ideas are Yongxu Ren's. Most reports, mathematical elaborations, reviews, and the present synthesis are AI-assisted or AI-written. Their prose must not be presented as Ren's verbatim statements. User messages in preserved conversation records can establish his requests and formulations; retained AI elaboration is a separate authorship category. The Chinese formulations in this edition are contemporary proposals, not classical quotations. See the repository's [reading brief](../../../_process/BRIEF.md) and [source catalogue](../../../sources/SOURCES.md).

## S13 — Plural requirements and continuing participation

[Plural-correction mathematics](../../../_process/research/2026-10-04-integrated-evolution/plural-correction-math.md) asks whether one executable policy meets several requirements and separates joint feasibility from pairwise compatibility. These models use declared interests, loss bounds, and information stages. A feasible policy is not thereby legitimate, and an incompatibility certificate does not decide whose claims should yield. Raising consent by worsening refusal is different from improving participants' situation.
