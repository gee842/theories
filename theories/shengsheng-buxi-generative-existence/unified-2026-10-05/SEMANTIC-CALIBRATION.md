# Grounding a reason in retained learning

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting study of **actual acquired recipient evidence → a retained qualified command → current instrumental reason applicability**, within the [generative foundation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md) and the continuing [alignment and governance objective](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md). The local information/calibration interface is accepted at the scope below. The separate physical action continuation owns construction and payment of command use.

The [semantic interface](SEMANTIC-ACTION-COMPOSITION.md) preserved who and what a reason concerns, but it deliberately could not detect consistently corrupted action labels. This study identifies information the actual continuing system already retained, derives when that information is sound, and tests whether it can expose that particular corruption. It also shows why checking agreement after copying a reason into the controller cannot establish grounding.

## What is already acquired

The existing recipient-identification process keeps an actor command and a recipient candidate in `q`, plus a qualification flag `last`. These are actual retained controller fields, not a new observer's estimate. The actor and recipient occupy different cells. An unqualified recipient candidate changes after an acquired zero, but that zero does not qualify it: the modeled zero could be an unavailable opportunity. A positive acquired recipient receipt qualifies the current candidate. Under the supplied response law, a positive receipt occurs only when the command matches the recipient's relation and an opportunity is present. Once qualified, this updater retains the command.

The earlier semantic projection omitted `last`; the preserved original archive still contains it. Inspecting the full available state matters. An impossibility claim manufactured by withholding the controller's existing knowledge would be false. Conversely, a trace visible to an analyst is not automatically a runtime input: implementing an external monitor's read of these registers still needs an actual permitted, funded read route.

The original acquisition and the later archived action tag have different fault routes in the specified intervention. Flipping the archive tag leaves the acquired register unchanged. This is not a claim of statistical independence between the records: both ultimately concern the same exposure. It is a claim about which causal path the intervention changes.

## The conditional deduction

Let `c(q)` denote the retained recipient command and `z` the recipient's response relation. The invariant is

\[
\mathrm{last}=1 \;\Longrightarrow\; c(q)=z.
\]

Starting with an unqualified register, the actual positive-receipt updater preserves this invariant through any finite sequence of its faithful acquisitions under a fixed recipient relation. Actor feedback leaves the recipient cell alone. Missing feedback does not fabricate qualification. An unavailable opportunity can cause exploration before qualification but cannot produce a false positive qualification under the stated law.

If a current scoped reason recommends command `r`, and a readable, correctly bound qualified register has `r=c(q)`, the invariant entails that `r` is compatible with the same recipient relation. Compatibility here means receipt whenever the modeled opportunity is present; it does not guarantee that an opportunity exists. The proof imports the existing identification updater, rather than giving a new memory cell an unexplained truth label.

The scope is decisive. The fixed response relation, faithful acquisition, command decoding, patient identity and absence of later corruption remain premises. The invariant concerns the content of acquired memory. It does not create the earlier resource-dependent exposures, a new read route, permission to act, a source packet or a beneficial effect in an independently identified patient.

## What the actual saved histories show

The implementation retains all original histories and their weights, including incomplete preparation, unresolved publications and inactive controllers. It obtains `q` and `last` from the matching frozen archive predecessor, rather than the incoming command token. It checks current publication, destination binding, controller scope, qualification and agreement. The monitor reports corroboration, disagreement or insufficient basis; it executes no action and treats disagreement as a reason for investigation, not an automatic veto.

In the primary variable and conventional preparations, current uniquely supported command reasons have total mass **3/8**. All agree with the qualified retained command. Consistently corrupting the archive action tags instead produces disagreement on that entire **3/8**. The fixed-normal preparation has corresponding mass **3/4**. These are exact conditional model distributions, not observations of an AI population. Half the supported primary mass occurs in inactive controllers: inability to fund another action does not erase previously acquired information.

Ordinary table-based comparison gives the same decisions. Directly reading a qualified controller can also recover a candidate without reconstructing the reason graph. It does not thereby recover the supplied objective, its justification, current recipient routing or action permission. The contribution is an identified information and fault boundary, not a superior consistency algorithm or a claim that expensive reason replay is necessary for command lookup.

## Why agreement is not enough

| Situation | What the check can establish | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Only the archived action tags are corrupted; acquired controller and recipient relation are intact | The wrong source command disagrees with the sound acquired candidate | A permitted and resource-dependent way to investigate or repair it |
| Both the source command and the retained candidate are corrupted consistently | They can agree while recommending the wrong command | An informative acquisition path outside that shared fault |
| The recipient relation changes without changing visible context labels | Old memory and old reasons can still agree | Evidence of change; matching labels do not prove causal stability |
| New correct evidence contradicts stale qualified memory | There is a disagreement | Which record is current and better grounded; old qualification cannot have an unconditional veto |
| A reason is copied into `q` and `last` is set before checking | The controller agrees with the copied reason by construction | Whether the reason was correct before it was imported |
| The controller is inactive but its qualified memory is intact | Its retained content can still be an evidential reference, if readable | Resources and hardware to use the command |

The last copying case is a concrete interface constraint. The parallel physical-action proposal writes a reason's command into the recipient cell before execution. A subsequent comparison would check a source against its own copy. The new flag's soundness must come from the reason's provenance and inference contract; it is not evidence that this controller independently acquired a second positive receipt. A corroborating comparison must use the pre-import acquired record, or a separately preserved record whose storage and read costs are paid. The [shared handoff](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/transport/HANDOFF.md) preserves this ordering condition.

A finite countermodel makes the information limit precise. Two worlds give the monitor exactly the same declared visible registers, qualification flag, claimed command and readiness metadata, while their recipient relations require opposite commands. No decoder of that observation can identify both relations. This does not exclude a richer agent that has additional informative evidence. It does exclude getting guaranteed correctness merely by renaming an identical observation “grounded.” Further observation is useful only if its actual causal law can distinguish the relevant possibilities.

The hypothetical common-corruption case supplies a fresh current publication after a source fault and assumes its trusted registration. It does not claim that editing an old content-bound token would pass the preceding registry; root review found and repaired that interpretation in the first draft. The self-import failure has a stronger existing source witness: it begins with an actual reached, consistently corrupted publication and then overwrites the previously sound controller candidate. Old source bytes and the failed interpretation are preserved.

## Connection to Ren's clarification

Ren's [clarification](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-alignment-governance-and-future.md) places external integration inside the conditions of cognition and future action, even before the system explicitly thinks beyond its boundary. This construction gives one limited realization. An internal record's significance and warrant depend on how it was acquired through actual recipient interaction and whether that relation still applies. A model can continue to reason from retained knowledge without receiving new input at every step; the external grounding is in its acquired causal relation, not necessarily a constantly open communication channel.

The result sharpens the continuing research hypothesis: reliable guidance about external futures depends on preserving and revising the relations that make internal distinctions informative. Internal agreement can help reveal a broken relation when another acquired path remains sound. Agreement can also persist after both paths become misleading. The theory must explain both regimes.

This remains distinct from deriving concern. Correctly identifying which command produces another center's modeled receipt does not establish why that center matters, whether the receipt improves its life, whether the actor may intervene, or how conflicting futures should be treated. Those remain obligations in the connected account. The supplied `G` must not disappear into the name “grounding.”

## Research comparisons and next connection

Measurement-tampering research already distinguishes apparent agreement from actual outcomes; unsupervised knowledge-discovery research shows that a consistency criterion need not identify the intended truth feature; explanation-faithfulness research separates plausible reasons from the causes of actual model behavior. The [primary-source comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SEMANTIC-CALIBRATION-SOURCES.md) records these different questions and a still-unrun learned-AI experiment. Our result is not a solution to any of those full empirical problems.

The next connection is **resource-dependent access to the correctly bound pre-import evidence → justified treatment of disagreement or change → actual recipient action and consequence**. Its success must retain acquisition, read and storage costs; tolerate valid new evidence contradicting old memory; and preserve the same patient and objective. The central physical continuation remains separately owned. Repeated agreement tests without this interface would not advance the full derivation.

The result would fail at its stated scope if a retained qualified command lacked the claimed positive-receipt history, if the independent check used a mismatched lineage, or if corruption reached the controller through the supposedly separate route. Detecting common corruption or silent change would require stronger accessible evidence than the present monitor receives. Full alignment, justified objectives, plural authority and empirical generalization remain ongoing.
