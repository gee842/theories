# Keeping failed learning attempts inside the continuing process

6 October 2026. AI-authored supporting development under [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). **Accepted after the formal build, independent executable comparison and final independent review.** The named connection is an actual predictor exposure → its complete distribution of successors and costs → further exposures from those same successors.

The foundation requires mechanisms to compose through what they actually leave behind. A successfully completed learning step is only part of that account. Construction can fail, rent can consume the remaining reserve, an outage can produce misleading evidence, and a useful action can occur without enough fuel to write its result. Those outcomes change what can happen next.

## The precise gap in the existing work

The [resource-dependent prediction protocol](PREDICTION-PROTOCOL.md) proves a four-event completed trial: rent, work, write, finish. Its proof covers the declared deterministic, payable-write law. The [numerical predictor](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/model.py) also includes reconstruction, outages, unpaid writing and scheduled packet loss.

The two laws expressly differ outside the completed-trial overlap:

| Situation | Numerical exposure | Older completed-trial law |
|---|---|---|
| Work occurs but writing is unaffordable | Returns a continuing state with updated action and lifetimes | Remains at the observed phase |
| Rent is unaffordable | Clears specified circuit and memory fields | Rent cannot begin |
| A scheduled opportunity produces no work | Loses that opportunity's packet | An unused packet remains available |
| Later construction is affordable | A subsequent exposure may rebuild | Construction is outside the committed trial |

The new study formalizes the actual exposure law and identifies its overlap with the old proof. It does not alter either predecessor to make their different failure behavior disappear.

## The probability belongs to the whole successor

The exposure retains fuel, gate and circuit lifetimes, construction stage, memory, last action and write status. Its output also retains the actual expenditure, wasted source and whether work, capture and writing occurred. The physical regime affects capture; it is not an extra input available to the controller's choice.

Finite weighted tapes reproduce the existing gate choice, initial action and availability probabilities. Unused draws are summed out. Every outcome remains in the total measure, including absent equipment, no work and unpaid writes. A later exposure receives each actual successor with its associated weight; it does not receive a newly funded or correctly initialized substitute.

The general probability rule already exists in the repository's [renewal-composition account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/REPEATED-RENEWAL-COMPOSITION.md). The contribution is a checked application to the existing callable mechanism, including its previously unformalized branches.

## A missed write need not end useful action

An analytically specified example starts with eight fuel units, a live predictor commanding the correct route, write cost six and availability one half. A first missed capture leaves four fuel units: not enough to write, but enough to attempt work again. If the second attempt captures, its return pays for writing and leaves two units.

That missed-then-captured history has probability one quarter. The actual second-capture probability is one half. An account that treats the first unpaid write as permanent death retains only one quarter. The independent executable comparison confirms these source-law calculations. The score stays correct in this example; it demonstrates continuing action and a later resource-dependent write, rather than correction of a mistaken record.

For a task requiring two consecutive resource-dependent writes, excluding a missed first write is appropriate. The error is using that restricted task's successful paths as the complete physical law for a different question about later action or recovery.

Similarly, a low-reserve predictor may write only after receiving a successful return. Looking only at completed writes then shows exclusively successful receipts even though half the actual attempts failed. The conditioning event and its probability must remain visible before that record is used as evidence about the process.

## What has been checked, and what remains

The [mathematical note](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/STOCHASTIC-PREDICTION-MATH.md), [formal protocol](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/stochastic-prediction/PROTOCOL.md), [numerical protocol](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/stochastic-prediction/CHECK-PROTOCOL.md) and [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/STOCHASTIC-PREDICTION-REVIEW.md) separate the evidence. The [formal receipt](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/stochastic-prediction/verification.json) records a fresh Lean 4.29.1 build across ten modules, with **53 new theorem audits and 141 inherited audits**. Every new theorem is audited; inherited modules retain their recorded audit sets. Independent source, hash and semantic review accepted the completed package.

The [independent executable comparison](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/stochastic-prediction/RUNTIME-RESULTS.md) is accepted at its declared scope. It matches all twelve original state fields and seventeen one-exposure metric increments jointly over 15,985 distinct calls. This includes every reached input of the eight inherited cases and eight controller variants, plus a fixed guard grid. It checks 608,316 interpreted prefixes and directly compares 38,412 distinct compiled Lean tape outputs with the independent interpreter. All sixty-four inherited continuations retain their final endpoint probabilities and endpoint-weighted cumulative first moments. These are computational checks, not empirical observations or a complete distribution of cumulative costs. Finite software comparisons are not a universal proof about Python execution.

The declared-law proofs preserve total finite weight, actual inherited successors and the fuel/expenditure/waste account on every represented history. Under the old enabled-cycle and admissibility premises, installed variable-arm trials with availability true agree on the operational endpoint, actual receipt, separate expenditure and waste, and one-packet debit. Both matching and mismatching commands are covered. The explicit no-work and unpaid-write counterexamples preserve the disagreement outside that overlap. Literal weighted-list equality is a sufficient composition premise here; the result is not a general quotient of arbitrary probabilistic kernels or a universal Python refinement.

Every exposure requires its scheduled source packet. Finite composition does not manufacture further support, establish indefinite persistence or identify a real physical implementation. It also does not supply another center's evaluative significance or justify concern. Those remain central parallel derivations.

This accepted result closes a documented implementation boundary within compatible composition. It allows subsequent arguments to use a more complete account of the learning process, without hiding failed attempts or treating them all as the same irreversible failure. The five governing completion conditions remain unchanged.
