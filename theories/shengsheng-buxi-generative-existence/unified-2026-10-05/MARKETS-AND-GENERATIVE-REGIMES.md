# Keeping markets in generative regimes

6 October 2026. AI-authored development of [Ren's proposal](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-markets-and-generative-basins.md), within the continuing [generative foundation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). This is a conditional framework for market governance. Its application to a particular market requires identified dynamics, affected parties and feasible interventions.

**Formal continuation, 6 October 2026:** [the mathematical model](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md) now connects actual monetary returns, renewal, paid influence and funded accountability. It proves a robust invariant region, zero-disturbance attraction, recovery and a destructive region under stated laws. [Thirty-nine Lean results](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/README.md) check the rational invariant, accounting, guard and concrete certificates; [independent computation](../../../experiments/market-generativity/README.md) checks twelve synthetic histories. These results formalize the proposed mechanism conditionally; the actual market mapping, authority, calibrated liability and affected people's standing remain explicit premises or further derivations.

A market coordinates exchange and selects among practices through prices, revenue, financing, survival and expansion. Those practices also change the conditions on which future exchange and life depend. The proposed guidance is therefore: **arrange market selection so that successful activity renews those conditions, while preserving affected people's ability to discover damage and change the arrangement.** This connects economic performance to the reproduction of human capacities, shared supports and accountable institutions.

## What makes a market generative

A generative market regime would support useful production and discovery while renewing or replacing the conditions of further worthwhile activity: material and ecological supports, people's health and skills, reliable knowledge, shared infrastructure, and effective agency. Which conditions matter, whose lives are affected, and which losses are unacceptable must be specified. A larger financial total cannot establish these properties by itself.

Generation in the foundation is broader than favorable regeneration. Harmful institutions also generate successors and can be durable. Here, “generative market” names a favorable regime relative to explicit, justified commitments about affected beings and their futures. Those commitments extend beyond whatever supports profitable exchange. A person retains standing even when the market no longer needs their labor or purchasing power. The [ethical foundation](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) and [formation study](FORMATION.md) distinguish that standing from bargaining leverage.

Markets can contain mixed regimes at different scales. A firm may develop useful technology while depleting workers; a region may renew its own resources by shifting damage elsewhere. Some enterprises and products should disappear as better possibilities develop. Maintaining generativity does not require preserving every incumbent, every transaction, or growth in every output. It requires identifying which continuations and transformations are warranted and keeping them feasible.

## The connection from dependence to market selection

The named connection is **organizational dependence → consequences becoming operationally significant → selection of practices → revision of the mechanisms of selection**. The market application tests whether consequences for shared supports actually reach the mechanisms that decide which practices persist.

Consider a hypothetical service market. Two providers deliver an equally useful service today. One pays to maintain equipment and train successors; the other can defer those costs or shift them to workers and the public. If buyers cannot distinguish future reliability, financiers reward only present margins, and neither contracts nor other institutions make the deferred costs consequential, the second strategy can expand faster. The first provider's virtue does not change that selection mechanism. Conversely, reliable information, usable long-term commitments, maintenance obligations or shared provision could change the comparison. Each route needs an actual carrier, funding and enforcement.

This is conditional. Narrow optimization need not win: customers may recognize quality, workers may leave, reputation may matter, and future costs may return to the firm. The task is to identify where these feedbacks work, where they fail and who remains outside them. The analyst's assessment of renewal is not itself an economic reward.

Akerlof supplies a concrete antecedent: when quality cannot be distinguished, those producing it may fail to receive its return, allowing poorer offerings to displace better ones. He also notes that institutions counteracting this problem can concentrate power. [The Market for Lemons, 1970](https://pims.math.ca/files/Akerlof1970.pdf).

A second loop connects this application to the [meta-attractor theory](../../meta-attractor/README.md). Successful firms or coalitions may gain influence over contracts, standards, information access and public rules. Where that influence weakens restraints on the strategy that produced the success, present advantage changes future selection. Where it instead funds trustworthy knowledge, accessible infrastructure and accountable renewal, success can strengthen the conditions of further contribution. Neither direction follows from success alone.

The decisive governance question consequently concerns both what wins under today's rules and **what today's winners can do to tomorrow's rules**. Regulators, cooperatives and public providers face the same question about their own authority. Assigning an institution the name “guardian” does not explain why it will remain answerable.

## Practical guidance and its tests

The following are design directions to assess in context. Their combination matters: evidence without action can be ceremonial, obligations without funding can exclude smaller participants, and protection without accountability can entrench power.

| Condition to preserve | Practical direction | Evidence that the direction is working |
|---|---|---|
| Renewal of indispensable supports | Account for maintenance, skill formation, care, infrastructure and ecological replacement where relevant; protect non-substitutable limits directly | Actual support conditions remain adequate through the transition, including for people and places outside the transaction |
| A viable route for useful contribution | Make consequential quality and deferred costs affect procurement, contracts, liability, finance or other legitimate selection mechanisms | Practices that maintain the specified supports can obtain resources and continue without silently receiving an analyst's reward |
| Effective refusal and alternatives | Reduce coercive dependence through appropriate basic provision, bargaining capacity, portability and access to alternative providers or forms of provision | People can decline harmful terms and use alternatives in practice, within their time and resource constraints |
| Independent learning and timely correction | Support trustworthy evidence, protected challenge, competent investigation and usable remedies | A consequential error can be detected and corrected before the relevant loss becomes irreversible; the correction leaves the next review affordable |
| Accountable changes to market rules | Constrain exclusive control of standards, evidence and rule revision; give affected parties meaningful routes to contest changes | Economic success cannot automatically buy exemption from obligations or shut every effective route of challenge |
| Diversity and adaptation | Preserve feasible experimentation, entry, replacement and public or cooperative alternatives where appropriate | A failed practice can be replaced without destroying essential provision; a successful practice remains open to challenge |

Prices can carry some consequences, but their usefulness depends on information, power, enforcement and what is exchangeable. Some losses require limits, rights or direct provision. Conversely, restricting activity can destroy useful renewal capacity or serve incumbent interests. The framework evaluates an intervention through its actual consequences and revision conditions, including those of the intervention itself.

## Making the basin claim precise

In a specified dynamical model, an attractor basin consists of states whose trajectories approach the relevant attractor. A viability region instead concerns states from which admissible action can maintain specified constraints. A desirable region is not automatically an attractor; a recoverable situation is not automatically one the actual institutions will repair. The [governance assessment](ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md#the-exact-necessity-claim) gives a finite viability formulation under explicit information and action assumptions.

For a market model, the state must include relevant supports, participants, resources, information and installed rules. Rule revision and the resources that make it possible belong inside the evolving state. One must state the shocks, horizons and policies being considered, then demonstrate attraction, continued viability or recovery at that scope. There may be several favorable and harmful regimes, mixed trajectories, or no stable attractor.

The foundation supplies a useful necessary accounting condition for a represented support stock. If renewal is \(R(t)\), loss is \(L(t)\), initial stock is \(C_0\), and the justified floor is \(C_{\min}\), then remaining above the floor requires

\[
C_0+\int_0^t [R(s)-L(s)]\,ds\ge C_{\min}
\qquad\text{for every relevant }t.
\]

This is the existing [prefix renewal condition](THEORY.md#4-from-becoming-to-maintained-regeneration), not a new market theorem. Eventual gains cannot pay an earlier irrecoverable loss. A transition toward a better incentive structure must therefore preserve enough time and usable resources to reach it. Different supports retain their own units and substitution conditions; this expression does not turn agency, money and ecological function into one interchangeable stock.

“Keeping the market in the generative basin” now has a conditional mathematical realization in [the coupled model](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md#an-actual-attracting-set). Its protected and recovery regions are certified subsets of a basin, alongside a region ending in collapse. Applying that result to an actual market still requires identified laws, parameters and support conditions; it does not establish a complete empirical basin boundary.

## What this application adds to the research

The framework joins three obligations that should be examined in one continuing history: shared supports must be renewed, that renewal must become consequential for actual selection, and the institutions carrying that consequence must survive resource pressure and rule capture. Existing work already demonstrates scoped separations between shared benefit and private retention, between correct evidence and effective action, and between one successful correction and repeated funded correction. See the [unified theory](THEORY.md), [joint formation](JOINT-FORMATION.md) and [reason protection](REASON-PROTECTION.md). Their formal results are interfaces and counterexamples, not empirical proofs about markets.

The proposed contribution must be compared with established accounts of externalities, information, institutional power and collective action. The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-REVIEW.md) records primary comparators and the scope of the internal assessment. Common vocabulary or agreement with existing economics does not establish a distinctive predictive advantage.

Ostrom's institutional framework already connects outcomes to changing rules and material conditions, and examines monitoring, participant rule revision and conflict resolution. It is a strong comparator for this proposal's account of correction and differentiated centers. Its findings concern contextual institutional arrangements rather than an automatic tendency of markets to regenerate. [Beyond Markets and States, 2010](https://web.pdx.edu/~nwallace/EHP/OstromPolyGov.pdf).

The next discriminating study should choose one bounded market with measurable renewal costs and a real route for revising its rules. It should trace maintenance or harm into evidence, decisions, resource allocation and subsequent rule changes. Outcomes should include support renewal, harms by affected group, entry and exit, time to remedy, the cost of correction, and who controls later revisions. An aggregate performance score must not conceal any of these outcomes.

Compare matched interventions with equal initial resources and information: an existing remedy for the identified incentive problem, and that remedy with an explicitly funded, accountable revision mechanism. Include the additional mechanism's costs and test changed dependence or incumbent power. If the mechanism only works because an evaluator pays for “generativity,” the intended derivation has been supplied rather than explained. If an established institutional model explains the same findings, the result may support the mechanism while narrowing the claim of theoretical novelty.

A damaging counterexample would be continued support renewal accompanied by domination of dispensable people, accurate evidence that cannot change allocation, or successful correction that exhausts its own successor. These would require revising the claimed sufficiency of the proposed guidance. They remain compatible with the wider foundation's obligation to explain destructive regimes.

This application identifies a testable institutional interface. It leaves local production laws, measurement, legitimate authority, evaluative commitments and the formation of concern explicit. The continuing foundational task is to explain how shared consequences acquire and retain warranted practical significance, including when narrow private advantage favors ignoring them.

## 市场与互联网的实际检验 / Existing histories as tests

2026-10-08。[Ren 的新指示](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-generative-alignment-priority.md#markets-internet-and-degeneration)纠正了把后续检验过度限定为新实验的路线：现实的市场和互联网演化已有可用证据，enshittification 是理解 degeneration 的直接入口。上文的匹配干预仍是可选设计，不是承认既有历史证据的门槛。[本轮整合](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md)核读政策、开发者公告、申报文件及许可变更，保留竞争解释。

**Enshittification 是退化的一种具体制度形态：共同活动形成的关系、能力与依存，被转化为限制后续共同创造和纠正的控制力。** 它不只描述产品令人不满，而是要求追踪谁还能继续做什么、实际替代是否可用、谁承担支持成本，以及谁能改变下一轮规则。每一个案是否符合这一完整解释，仍须由这些关系的证据判定。

平台经营、内容贡献、开发、阅读和维护是同一活动史中形成的不同位置；控制能力与受损程度可以很不对称。退化因而不要求经营组织先衰弱：它可以在更精密的优化、更强的规则控制及商业增长中发生。被排除者的损失不会因为经营活动不再需要他们就失去意义。反过来，一家旧组织结束、某项收费出现，也不自动等于共同生成受损；实际替代、维护需要与过渡负担必须保留。

首轮两个历史对应给出不同后续：Reddit／Apollo 材料显示经营收入增长与一条既有参与路径结束并存；D&D 许可争议显示一次反对反馈之后的规则撤回和开放。事实、当事方因果解释、尚未识别的机制与来源分别见[案例记录 §§3–4](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#3-redditapollo经营增长与路径损失可以同时发生)。它们提供事件与规则变化的入口；增长与损失并存不是新的理论发现，也不能识别利润优化或完整退化机制。

由此，伦理学习与制度对齐之间的接口应明确为：**后果被认识，怎样取得改变决定及后续规则的实际效力。** 单有更多信息、善意或更好的推荐目标不完成这个接口。能否提出并维持异议、能否继续提供替代、纠正需要的资源由谁控制，均属于被对齐的活动本身。

正面方向沿已完成的 G0/J/E 论证：支持不同生活及其继续生成，并评价目标执行对共同条件的改变。它要求有根据地维持有益参与的继续路径、必要维护、可行替代及有效纠正，约束成功转化为不受纠正权限的过程。具体收费、接口或许可方案仍须处理实际损失、支持成本和正当权限；开放或去中心化的名称本身不保证善。

本轮完成实际历史的首轮对应及理论修订；沿上述证据继续追踪为何某些反馈改变规则、另一些未能改变，是现在的未完连接。既有市场 Lean 结果保持原来的条件范围，不以这些案例宣称验证了全部参数、吸引域或普遍向善。

Existing histories provide events through which to investigate participation and rule correction; disproving an equivalence between commercial growth and shared generativity is not a substantive new research target. Enshittification is interpreted as one institutional form of degeneration, requiring evidence of impaired generative conditions rather than mere dissatisfaction or a price increase. The next connection concerns how consequences gain effective influence over rule revision. G0/J/E supply the declared normative commitments; the historical cases do not prove those commitments or universal convergence.
<a id="profit-externalities-and-selection"></a>
### 利润、外部性与规则的再生产 / Profit, externalities and reproduced rules

[Ren 随后的纠正](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-generative-alignment-priority.md#profit-and-externalities-correction)明确：利润优化可以忽略外部性。因此，上一轮把增长与路径损失并存列作理论推进，研究靶子过弱。营收数字仅是背景，既不度量利润，也不证明实际优化目标；这一弱靶子现已撤回。

实际研究从既有外部性解释继续：哪些损失没有充分影响作决定者的成本、责任或其他约束，使消耗共同条件的做法取得优势；这些优势又是否进入准入、评价和修订权，维持同样的成本转移方式。复用已经完成的选择与元吸引子机制，检查其现实关系和纠正条件，不把所有利润归于损害，也不把经营成功当作规范根据。

“外部”相对于交易和收益核算划出的范围；被影响者仍属于同一生成过程。退化评价关心被改变的生活、支持、后续创造与纠正条件。单纯租金转移、外部性和这些条件的侵蚀需要分别辨明，不能只凭一个应用结束就宣布整个过程退化。[详细纠正与证据要求](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#externalities-and-recursive-degeneration)保留已知事实和反例。

The separation between profit incentives and wider consequences is the starting point. The substantive empirical question concerns whether cost-shifting advantage reproduces the conditions that favor it, including control over correction. These relations are part of one unfolding; an accounting boundary does not place consequences outside that process. Existing conditional mechanisms and G0/J/E commitments are reused.

<a id="interface-control-and-rule-reproduction"></a>
### 共同接口、纠正与规则的接续 / Shared interfaces and continuing rule control

2026-10-08。[续轮研究](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#interface-control-and-corrective-change)通过已发生的广告技术规则变更，具体化“优势怎样参与重建选择条件”。这一接口不只经由利润购买影响力：经营位置若同时运行共同接口并控制其修改，经营决定本身就会改变他人的准入与比较条件。既有有偿影响模型解释其中一种路线，不能直接冒充另一种路线的经验识别。

由此得到更明确的解释：**受损者已经反馈、已经发展替代，也不保证反馈导向修复；掌握规则的位置可以把这些回应纳入维持自身优势的调整。** 解释持续退化时，应辨明缺乏认识、回应不足与反向使用反馈，不能统称为信息不足或关切尚未形成。

改革的比较范围也随之明确：既记录哪项具体做法停止，也追踪谁能够决定下一轮如何参与、如何比较、如何改正。如果新做法保留了同一种有害的控制关系，一项限制的撤回不完成整个关系的修复；如果实际替代和有根据的纠正已恢复，就应承认修复，而不以“元吸引子”否定所有变化。这是有条件的理论判断，不是另一个已经证明的普遍动态法则。

正面方向仍是共同生成：在已有实践判断下，支持有益活动的可持续接续，并为有根据的纠正保留可实行的权限和支持；当前产出改善不能自动抵偿对这些条件的损害。接口维护、隐私、安全与转换成本同时进入实践比较。仅有更多供应者、更多选项或新监管名称，不足以确认改善。

本轮已完成所选历史接口的局部对应和实践比较论证，事实、法院认定、公司回应及 AI 推论在研究记录中分别列明。接下来的未完连接是有益替代和纠正怎样取得能随依存变化而接续的支持与权限。旧证明、Reddit／Apollo 和 D&D 材料保留范围，不重做已完成的规范方向。

Control over later selection may arise within the operation of a shared interface. Effective correction must be assessed through changes in feasible continuation and revisable authority, as well as the removal of a named practice. The historical study supports a bounded mechanism correspondence; integration benefits, costs and alternative explanations remain explicit. Existing G0/J/E commitments and conditional formal results are reused.

<a id="degeneration-boundaries-and-transitions"></a>
### 退化的边界与转变 / Degeneration boundaries and transitions

2026-10-08。[Ren 明确要求](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-generative-alignment-priority.md#degeneration-boundaries-and-phase-transitions)：并非所有市场演化都是退化，应找到退化边界、可能的 phase transition 及元吸引子规律的适用范围。[本轮论证、证明、现实对应及反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#degeneration-boundary-and-transition)按这一方向限定前述研究。

**退化元吸引子是有适用边界的规律候选：侵蚀共同生成条件的活动取得选择优势，其后果重建选择这种活动的条件；在指定状态域及继续条件下，既有纠正不足以扭转该接续。** 返回关系解释自我维持，实际后果及 G0/J/E 说明退化；两者须分别取得根据。高利润、集中度、一次退出或不满本身均不完成判断。通用元吸引子机制同样可能维持共同支持及有根据的纠正。

需要分开伦理比较、机制适用及状态域的保持／脱离三种边界。一个变化可以造成真实损失却未形成元吸引子；一个递归组织可以维持良性创造；同一组选择规律也可以允许两种状态域。不能用“元吸引子”否认已经发生的有效改革，也不能把一项做法撤回直接当作整个返回关系消失。

本轮在原市场模型中实际证明了一个精确界线。持续获授权并付款的纠正强度为 \(\alpha\)，影响侵蚀强度为 \(\beta\)，影响目标上界为 \(\lambda\)，私人选择门槛为 \(\theta\)，定义

\[
M=\alpha(1-\theta)-\beta\lambda\theta.
\]

在模型规定的物理域与更新条件下：

- 对全部 \(\theta\le q\le1,\ 0\le w\le\lambda\)，选择门槛得到下一步保护，当且仅当 \(M\ge0\)；要覆盖不利误差上界 \(\varepsilon\ge0\)，则当且仅当 \(M\ge\varepsilon\)。
- 原数学研究所指定的严格低选择矩形证书存在，当且仅当 \(M<0\)；其持续执行投影在任意自然数时刻保持于该区域，维护实践的正份额在正选择速率下严格下降。
- 原参数的负余量情形仍有一个不同的良性保护区域。因此，参数越界意味着这种退化区域能够出现，不能推出所有历史必然进入。

[19 项新 Lean 定理／27 项声明审计](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/boundary-verification.json)已实际通过，39 项导入定理审计分列。[模型范围](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/README.md#checked-regime-boundary)保留当期付款、授权、实际支持、正确对应、\(w\le\lambda\) 及定参等前提。它不是所有退化形态的充要条件，也不是已测出的平台临界份额。

可能的转变应分别研究：**参数改变使一个区域失去保持条件；冲击或累积变化使状态进入另一可保持区域；资金、权限或支持失效使原纠正规律不再执行；变化速度使纠正来不及完成。** 这些路线也可能反向促成恢复。区域存在变化、状态脱离与严格数学分岔不能混称为同一个相变证明。

对齐的新增实际含义，是检查目标执行如何改变纠正余量及未来选择域。更强执行力若侵蚀有根据的修订能力，可能扩大退化的可持续条件；修复应落到实际返回关系、替代支持和权限，而不是只追求更换组织或打破稳定。损失与改革成本仍按已有实践原则比较。

本轮完成所述条件边界及域内证明，不重开规范方向。接下来应识别现实返回关系怎样随制度变化，并取得完整状态域、实际支持与变化速率下的转变条件；不能从已发生的案例直接宣称全部参数校准或稳定性分类完成。

English: Ethical loss, reproduced selection and dynamical persistence have different boundaries. The existing paid-correction law now has an exact rational margin for uniform floor protection and for existence of the specified low-selection certificates. An all-time invariant projection is checked, while a distinct favorable region remains possible at the same parameters. Parameter change, state displacement, execution failure and excessive rate of change are different candidate transition routes. This is a conditional law with explicit correspondence, not inevitable degeneration, a full basin theorem or an empirically identified phase transition.

<a id="local-attraction-and-actual-support"></a>
### 局部吸引与实际支持 / Local attraction and actual support

2026-10-08。[接续研究及反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#attraction-and-support-coupling)已把上述区域保持推进到实际的局部吸引，再接回原完整运行和支持关系。新增 [38 项 Lean 定理／52 项声明审计](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/attraction-verification.json)通过；58 项导入定理／66 项审计分列，不重计为新成果。

在原例只改变侵蚀强度 \(\beta\) 的参数路径上，全部物理平衡点已经分类。维护端 \(H=(1,1,0)\)、耗损端 \(L_\beta=(0,1/(1+4\beta),1)\)，以及在 \(\beta\ge5/12\) 才进入物理域的内部点 \(S_\beta=(1-5/(12\beta),3/8,5/(12\beta))\)，具有明确的接合关系。在 \(\beta=1/2\) 截面，**两个端点均有几何缩小的邻域；内部点则有任意小、最终有限时离开的扰动。** 本轮证明覆盖每个正有理精度及此后的全部时刻，超出有限模拟和单纯不变区域。

这个局部双吸引结构仍须按其表示范围理解。把预算和必要支持接回同一活动史后，本轮证明投影与原运行法则逐步相符，并得到不同的实际结局：

- 高端邻域加足够的初始支持，满足旧保护证书，实际运行不会触发失效守卫。
- 低端邻域即使每步有钱支付既定纠正，也会在有限时间耗尽原模型的必要支持，触发实际停止。

所以，**能够把扰动吸收回自身的选择组织，也可能持续侵蚀它所依赖的共同生成条件。** 它的吸引性不独自证明整体可持续，更不证明伦理正当。这里没有把支持理解为独立于活动的外部对象；三坐标投影暂时略去的正是同一活动史中的支持关系。

纠正的类型因此应进一步辨明：一次状态改善若仍留在低端吸引邻域，会被原关系重新吸回；改变返回规律则可能改变该邻域本身；已经失去的参与能力还需要真实的进入与重建。原份额法则没有进入机制，零份额不能靠单改侵蚀参数自行重生。这是局部模型的表示限制，不能当作现实不可逆的证明。

对齐方向在此取得新的时序要求：按已有实践原则，评价纠正能否改变后续选择，也评价它完成之前谁仍有支持、谁承担损失，以及是否有可行的接续。吸引性、效率、增长与“有韧性”都不能代替这个比较。

局部三态结构和支持连接已完成；[平衡分支图](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/figures/regime-fixed-states.png)仅标示所证范围。完整三维吸引域、实数域推广、全部参数的稳定性／严格分岔类型及现实阈值未完成。下一连接是有支持的进入和持续纠正怎样在实际支持失效前跨过状态域分界；速率问题应置于这条实际时序中，复用已完成的边界和吸引证明。

English: Two endpoint neighborhoods now have checked local attraction in the original rational operating projection, while the interior state admits arbitrarily small perturbations with finite escape. Coupling the original essential support reveals finite guarded failure on the low side and certificate-backed nonfailure on the high side. Reproducing a selection organization can undermine the wider conditions of continuation. The next connection is supported entry and sustained change before those conditions fail, not another proof of the completed local mechanism.

### 有支持的有限转变 / A supported finite transition

2026-10-08。[本轮论证与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#supported-finite-transition)关闭了上文持续纠正的一项具体连接：**只要维护活动仍有正余量，实际改变选择关系可以在有限时间内把状态带入原来的保护区；支持必须贯穿这段转变。** [27 项新 Lean 定理／35 项审计](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/transition-verification.json)通过，96 项继承定理／118 项审计另列。

本例在有限阶段将侵蚀参数 β 从 1/2 改为 0。维护份额 x、责任效力 q 与累积影响 w 均继续按原有关系演化，未被重置。对每个有理物理初态 x>0，都存在有限 N，进入 x≥3/4、q≥1/2、w≤1/4 的既有保护区。这包括原低吸引邻域中的正份额初态。之后恢复 β=1/2，原证书即可继续保护；本轮不需要先求出完整三维分界面。

额外纠正每步消耗 κ≥0 个已表示的支持单位，原货币纠正预算仍按先付后收入执行。给定上述 N，一个已证明的保守充分储备是

\[
C_0\ge K_N=(15+10\kappa)(10/9)^N-10(1+\kappa).
\]

同一完整状态的实际守卫允许全部 N 步，并在撤除额外纠正时把剩余支持直接交给原保护规律，后续不发生守卫失败。这个界没有预支维护产生的收益，因此不宣称成本最小；初始支持在本地模型中给定，如何取得它仍须有现实说明。[成果记录](../results/records/market-supported-finite-transition.md)明确所复用的[原保护结果](../results/records/market-protected-continuation.md)及其正式连接。

**三个边界同时保留。** x=0 时，原份额法则不会因单改 β 产生新维护者，必须另有真实进入。对任意预设时长 N，都有正但足够小的初始维护份额，到期仍未进入保护区，因此不存在统一够用的有限干预时长。若开始时必要支持已处底线，纠正也可能立刻来不及：本轮检查 x=1/100、q=1/3、w=1、C=5 的状态，即使 β=0 且 κ=0，下一步 C=3.53，低于必要底线 5，实际运行停止。

这让元吸引子解释增加了明确的转变条件：**要改变反复产生退化的关系，纠正须持续到新的活动组成和留存影响足以支持接续；其间的支持不能用未来改善补账。** 有利的参数变化、足以恢复的活动余量和可承受的过渡时间不能互相替代。由既有 G0/J/E 判断何种维护、替代与损失值得支持，不由保护区或稳定性替代伦理评价。

当前数学结果是有限干预后的区域转移与实际支持交接，未宣称严格的全参数分岔分类或完整可恢复域。β=0 的实际授权与实施仍是给定条件；比例模型尚不包含离散退出和形成新参与者。下一连接应落到可实施的部分阻断及其形成、保留和成本，以及零余量后的实际进入。完成的有限到达与交接直接复用。

English: A finite, effective beta=0 intervention reaches the old protection rectangle from every physical rational state with positive renewal share. A conservative initial stock cover includes each intervention step’s extra support burden; the actual guarded history executes and hands its unchanged arrived state to the inherited beta=1/2 protection theorem after withdrawal. Zero seed, no uniform pulse duration and an immediate support-failure example delimit the result. It establishes a sufficient supported transition, not an optimal policy, complete basin, empirically calibrated threshold or endogenous formation of the intervention. G0/J/E supply the separate normative use.
