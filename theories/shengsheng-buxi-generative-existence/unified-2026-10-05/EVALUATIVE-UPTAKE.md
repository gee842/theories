# When acquired consequences change recipient-directed choice

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). **The first numerical run has passed its internal account checks; independent reconstruction and final Lean verification are pending. The construction is not yet accepted.** The [protocol][protocol], fixed cases and prospective independent oracle belong to the existing recipient-revision study. The first result is preserved before subsequent review; its provisional observations below are distinct from an independently checked finding.

The named transition is **actual consequence acquisition → retained evaluative influence → selected, paid action for the same recipient**. The accepted [fresh-evidence construction](RECIPIENT-REASON-REVISION.md) already acquires a changed command relation, revises its instrumental reasons and pays for its use. This study asks what additional causal connection lets acquired content change whether the actor uses that knowledge for another center.

## The difference that the experiment must isolate

Two controllers can know the same recipient facts, receive the same private training and face the same costs, yet give that recipient different practical influence. To identify that difference, the principal comparison preserves recipient perception and changes the subsequent evaluative route. The earlier [architecture-learning comparison](ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) changed access to evidence itself; it cannot serve as this equal-information control.

The proposed new register begins at zero. Three paid own-resource operations produce a retained estimate of operation shortfall. Each operation spends two units and can return two; a separate read and write cost one each. The learning datum excludes these later acquisition fees, while the whole-history account includes them. Thus a zero operation shortfall still permits net lesson expenditure.

The supplied finite update is

\[
n' = \lfloor n/2\rfloor+4Y,\qquad n\in\{0,\ldots,7\},\quad Y\in\{0,1\}.
\]

The shared route compares `10*n > 8*C`, where `C` is the actual specified recipient-trial charge, normally eight. The identity-filtered route has the same acquired `n` and recipient knowledge but excludes this influence from its choice. A conventional table supplies the same decision law independently. Missing or inverted measurement, disabled updating, a supplied constant valuation and a paid suppression route test distinct causal alternatives. These comparisons were fixed before the provisional results reported below.

Neither `n` nor the packet quantity measures experienced harm or moral standing. The update, sign, scale and cross-center transfer are declared local recipes. Private self-calibration alone cannot select their uniquely correct extension to another center: positive, zero and negative recipient influence can agree on all the self examples.

## One continuing material history

The construction must begin with an enlarged version of the parent's actual precursor and project to the unchanged parent history. The intended entry is the saved prefix before the old command read, corresponding to `RevisionWitness.consumed`; the old final endpoint is not reset or refunded. Failed inherited histories and their probability remain included.

Six binary cells back the acquired value, lesson count and reused outcome bit. A separately specified finite adapter and evaluation port supply control capacity. Every native operation, observation, write, evaluation and recipient action has an explicit bill. An assessment can initiate one action only. Permission loss can prevent execution without erasing factual knowledge or acquired value. Unused source stocks and interrupted phases remain in the state.

A later paid port replacement tests reconstruction of active influence from retained cells. It does not reconstruct the whole learner, repeat teaching, discover a new recipient need or establish indefinite renewal. Existing [successor learning](SUCCESSOR-LEARNING.md) and [recipient renewal](RECIPIENT-RENEWAL.md) remain separate accepted contracts until their state and resource requirements are actually connected.

The precise formation, source isolation, conditional entry, opportunity schedule, finite capacities and proposed accounts are in the [protocol][protocol]. Their verification is part of the work in progress. The [scope review][scope] fixes the intended interpretation before outcomes.

## First-run observations, pending independent acceptance

The saved [first-run receipt][first] records 29 cases, three evaluative arms and three inherited producer modes: 261 complete mixtures, 3,306 continuing histories and 40,200 snapshots. Failed parent histories retain their original probability. These are synthetic executions under a declared law, not empirical observations or a newly derived unrestricted formation distribution.

For the variable inherited producer, the first and second receipt probabilities are each `1/4` under the all-loss shared and conventional routes, and zero under the equally informed identity-filtered route. The mean additional recipient receipt is five resource units across all parent histories. Conditional on an admitted successful all-loss history, the actual register reaches seven and each of the two actions delivers ten. The aggregate numerator `7/4` is a probability-weighted summary, not a possible register value or an ethical magnitude.

| Prespecified comparison | First-run observation | What it can distinguish if independently verified |
|---|---|---|
| Same all-loss lessons; shared versus identity-filtered route | Same acquired value and facts; positive receipt versus abstention | Practical influence after perception |
| All-loss lessons; updating disabled | The paid lessons leave `n=0`; the controller abstains | Acquired content rather than elapsed teaching alone |
| All-return lessons; learned versus fixed-seven evaluation | Both learn `n=0`; only the supplied constant evaluator acts | Acquired evaluation versus an installed preference |
| Permission withdrawn | Positive assessments remain; both actions are blocked | Evaluation and factual knowledge remain distinct from permission |

| Later allocation reduced by one | First capture stays `1/4`; second falls to `1/16` | Actual later payment prefixes matter |
| Paid suppression | The learned register and facts persist while recipient influence is excluded | Affordable exclusion remains a causal alternative |

In the strict shared witness the continuing actor begins this layer with fuel ten and recipient stock forty. It ends with actor fuel zero, recipient sixty and six inaccessible native source units. The complete prospective account is realized by the first run:

No-return native packets retain their stock; it is never silently spent to manufacture a teaching loss. A later-port certificate covers reconstruction of active evaluation from the retained register, not a new organism or a new normative ground.

## Causal influence and warranted application

The [ethical application contract](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) identifies what is additionally needed to interpret the same process as warranted concern: an independently supported patient-condition relation, a defended ground, legitimate authority, an adequate comparison of competing claims, and actual addressed fulfillment. A resource receipt is evidence about one physical consequence; it cannot silently supply all of those meanings.

This leaves a specific joint obligation. Acquired evidence must change recognition of a ground's applicability, the retained evaluation must make a difference to selection, and the selected event must actually address that ground under its declared authority and costs. An observer's favorable interpretation alone does not demonstrate this causal recognition. Equally, a failed or indifferent controller does not refute an independently justified patient claim.

The present study targets the acquired-influence part using the already formed factual core. The parallel application study supplies its precise normative and semantic interface. Any accepted interpretation will state which interface premises are established, independently defended, supplied or still unknown.

## Contribution to the fixed completion conditions

A successful checked result would remove an externally installed coefficient and prescribed recipient action at this concrete implementation boundary. It would expose conditions for acquired influence, exclusion, mistaken transfer and unaffordable use within one resource history. It would not prove that self-preservation selects inclusive concern, that every system learns, or that the whole foundation has been completed.

The remaining synthesis must connect this interface to justified applicability, continuing correction and actual successors, using the existing results where their contracts suffice. Its general claims must retain their law conditions and empirical identification requirements. The [fixed finish-line audit][finish] remains the acceptance standard; the number of further components is not a measure of completion.
