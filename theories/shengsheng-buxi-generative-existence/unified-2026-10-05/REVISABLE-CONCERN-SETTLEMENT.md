# Toward a settlement of revisable concern

5 October 2026. Research direction from Ren; AI-assisted theory, proofs, evidence audit, and independent internal review. This advances the [unified foundation](THEORY.md), [joint formation](JOINT-FORMATION.md), and [revisable-concern study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/THEORY.md). The [research record](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/README.md) preserves the full branches, checks, repairs, and remaining obligations.

**The central constructive question now has a conditional answer:** one process can select and pay for learning, revising the application of its reasons, preserving their grounds, exercising effective protection, and rebuilding these capacities through succession. This is stronger than showing that those functions work separately. It is still a specified process with supplied ethical anchors, a sound finite audit, useful commercial opportunities, and bounded means of obstruction.

The broader aspiration remains larger than this result. Neither the process's successful renewal nor the mathematics establishes that reality generally becomes wiser, that every ethically relevant case will be represented, or why subjective experience exists. These claims need different grounds. Calling their absence an impossibility theorem would also overstate what has been established.

## 1. What another being's condition must change

In the proposed account, another being's condition matters functionally when a difference in that condition changes the process's evaluation and conduct because of the stake for that being. Direct concern counts the other's benefit in favor of action and harm against it, apart from private consequences. A response driven entirely by the observer's reward or punishment does not establish this kind of concern. A declaration, an internal coefficient, and an effective benefit are different observations.

The integrated account therefore keeps six questions distinct:

| Question | What must be supplied or established |
|---|---|
| What happens to the other? | A description of the affected being, condition, context, actual action, and outcome; uncertainty remains explicit. |
| Why should it count? | A defended reason, including the ethical bridge from a subject's loss to a reason in shared deliberation. |
| Does this reason apply here? | A warranted interpretation or comparison, with its dependencies and possible defeaters. |
| Does the process admit and retain it? | An implemented admission and revision procedure, memory, and paid access to relevant evidence. |
| Does it change what happens? | Motivation, a feasible action, timely authority, resources, and resistance to interference. |
| Can correction continue? | A successor able to inherit or reconstruct the reasons and rebuild the necessary capacities. |

These are connected parts of one explanation. They cannot substitute for one another. A representation may be accurate without motivating care; a caring action may fail; a profitable procedure may misidentify the relevant reason. A being's standing does not wait for the process to notice it.

## 2. A process that actually chooses revision and renewal

The [new formal construction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/formal/REPORT.md) places all the following choices in the same game. A successor initially has no learned recipient valuation or current protective actuator. Its earlier self chooses among no learning, private-only learning, an ephemeral audit, a durable audit, deliberate identity filtering, and a commitment forcing unguarded trade. It anticipates the later costs of learned concern. Without that binding commitment, the later self may withdraw, trade without protection, or buy protection for a subset of patients. A persistent operator may safeguard, refuse, or attack teaching and enforcement.

The durable audit has a separately useful commercial output. Its net receipt must exceed the best cheaper product by more than the entire protection bill. Self-insulation must also cost more than that bill. These conditions make a cold earlier self select the audit despite its anticipated protective expenditure. The receipt is a declared feature of this environment, not a reward patients provide or evidence that profitable judgments are ethically true.

The audit checks and operationally installs a finite graph of case–reason transfers. The context sequence is supplied and known for strategic evaluation; this theorem does not establish endogenous discovery under partial information. Starting from a positive anchor, the procedure admits exactly the cases with a valid rooted derivation. On revision it recomputes from the surviving grounds. A defeated premise retracts a conclusion only if no independent derivation remains. An unsupported cycle cannot certify itself. Cutting a delivery channel does not refute retained evidence.

Protection requires more than a sufficiently large aggregate concern weight. Each admitted patient's concern margin must exceed the maximum full protection bill in this sufficient construction. Gate prices must not decrease when additional patients are included; the equilibrium selects the gate for precisely the admitted set among relevant ties. Every jointly excluding attack must cost more than all the safeguards it avoids. Otherwise a shared bottleneck can defeat claims that separately look secure.

Finally, the reserve must pay upkeep and audit before their receipt arrives, and pay the gate before trade. A worst-case closing-balance inequality preserves that reserve even after temporary interference or refusal. Finite backward induction and a discounted infinite-game argument then establish an equilibrium that repeatedly selects the audit and protects each admitted patient. Existence is the claim; universal selection across all equilibria, technologies, or environments is not.

In the exact six-episode witness, admitted sets are `{p}`, `{p,q}`, `{q}`, then `{p,q}` for the remaining episodes. The third audit defeats one derivation while preserving another. An initial reserve of five grows to 67, with every payment made when due. **Two actual harms remain in the ledger:** one before q's admission and one when p's particular support is retracted. After the supplied valid relation stabilizes with complete coverage, both patients are protected. The model does not turn nonadmission into nonexistence or lack of standing.

The audit can completely reconstruct the current finite record at its charged price. Durable provenance is selected and retained, but this model does not establish that inherited storage is indispensable: a successor can use the paid reconstruction route. Nor does finite graph closure invent unrestricted new meanings. These distinctions prevent the achieved theorem from quietly assuming its broader research targets.

A [bounded feature refinement](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/formal/FEATURE-CONSTRUCTION.md) goes beyond a fixed primitive classifier. From three binary measurements it assembles 231 candidate expressions. A cheap identity proxy fits the discovery rows but fails every validation row; a constructed four-literal expression fits all eight cases. The separate check charges for assembly, labels, evaluation, and replay within the game's audit allowance. This is construction within a finite supplied grammar and authored assay table. It supplies neither real-world validation nor a new ethical reason.

## 3. Why the scope should widen

The [ethical branch](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/normative/FOUNDATION.md) supplies a positive argument rather than asking causal persistence to generate an obligation. Severe unwanted avoidable loss supplies a reason because it is a loss to its subject. Shared deliberation that claims to justify conduct to those affected owes an answer to that loss. The observer's convenience, the subject's lack of leverage, and a bare change of identity do not answer it. Agency and informed refusal supply additional reasons; reducing suffering alone does not justify controlling another's life.

This bridge is a substantive ethical commitment defended by the argument. A consistently indifferent or egoistic process can deny it without a formal contradiction. That possibility does not establish that the denial is justified: logical consistency, ethical justification, motivation, and universal persuasion are distinct. If the actor already accepts the reason and its applicability to a matched case, retaining a bare identity exclusion conflicts with those accepted commitments. If it instead rejects the bridge, the dispute concerns that bridge and its grounds.

The claim transferred is a particular reason in context. It is not a whole person's numerical worth or an all-things-considered ranking. Special responsibilities, consent, different needs, and conflicting claims can change the action without erasing the reason. Nor must an unfamiliar subject resemble an accepted beneficiary before its claim can be heard. The broader architecture requires direct intake and possible new reasons; the finite graph theorem covers only the supplied repertoire.

## 4. Acting before every disagreement is resolved

Incomplete ethical knowledge need not make every action equally defensible. The [decision results](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/normative/decision-math.md) use a finite set of feasible actions and a finite set of grounded factual and normative accounts. Each account may leave some actions incomparable.

An action is excluded when another is at least as good under every retained account and strictly better under at least one. With reflexive, transitive account-level comparisons, this robust dominance relation is a strict partial order. A nonempty remainder exists, and every excluded action has an undominated dominating witness. No common cardinal scale or probability of patienthood is needed for this result. Accepting dominance as a reason for exclusion remains a weak normative rule; the mathematics does not validate the supplied accounts.

This yields a narrow but useful resolution under uncertain patienthood. If protection introduces no adverse reason in any retained account and avoids harm in a warranted account where the candidate is a patient, the harmful alternative is excluded. Genuine costs, consent, or competing claims can break the premise. Mere speculative possibilities do not automatically enter the model set, and one possible benefit does not override every contrary burden.

When genuinely symmetric claimants compete for exactly k of n indivisible places, there is no deterministic choice invariant under every relevant renaming. If a lottery is ethically permissible and implementable, full symmetry selects the uniform lottery over the k-person subsets, giving each claimant probability k/n. This settles the symmetry question under those premises. It does not settle asymmetric need, the legitimacy of the lottery, or the realized loss of those unselected.

A remaining set is not a completed decision. Where more than one action survives, further grounds, an explicit priority, or a permissible fair procedure are needed. Leaving the actual choice to unexamined power would recreate the concern problem at the final step.

## 5. Where the causal and ethical parts meet

The formal game's audit certificates and the ethical decision models have different jobs. A certificate records a particular transferred reason. A decision model considers the relevant reasons for actual actions. An admitted injury claim cannot by itself authorize coercion, determine a rescue priority, or prove that all affected beings are represented.

Before interpreting an implemented equilibrium as justified concern, an application must independently examine the relevant patients and harms, the accuracy of its certificates, competing agency and other claims, the legitimacy of its powers, and the actual outcomes. The mathematical gate prices measure private expenditure; they are not ethical weights. A money surplus does not prove that spending has no competing ethical cost.

Conversely, filtering an action menu through an ethical rule can change the strategic game and its financing. One cannot append the normative remainder to the proved game and assume the same equilibrium survives. The formal theorem applies to its stated menu and preferences. The ethical results evaluate declared feasible choices. A proof of their general automatic compatibility has not been supplied.

The positive integration is nevertheless substantive. It shows how warranted revision and paid renewal can coexist, why justified scope is not exhausted by acquired motivation, and how some practical choices can be resolved without pretending that all values form a complete numerical order. These results constrain the next implementation rather than merely naming its aspirations.

## 6. What evidence can and cannot settle

The [evidence audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/evidence/report.md) independently rescored preserved outputs from an earlier AI handoff experiment. It reproduced the benefit of timely feedback and usable corrective packets in that task. It also found that a conventional candidate-elimination learner with the complete verified report solves every fixture. The experiment supports a particular correction-and-transmission mechanism; it does not identify newly learned concern for another being or establish comparative superiority of this theory. The audit checked 160 source hashes and added zero observations.

The evidence branch develops an explicitly unrun human study comparing a reason challenge with equally informative factual and recipient-perspective conditions. It separates actual costly transfer, the applicability of the stated reason, delayed generalization, missing outcomes, and active alternatives. Prospective thresholds and a study design are research preparations, not favorable findings. Implementation, power assessment, registration, and actual independent observations remain necessary.

A universal guarantee and a population tendency also have different quantifiers. Cheap self-insulation, missing evidence, unaffordable starts, or permanent capture refute an unrestricted favorable guarantee within the declared model classes. They do not measure how often those conditions arise in nature. A prevalence claim needs a defined population, time scale, outcome criterion, and denominator that includes failed and closed processes. An intervention that improves concern does not by itself show an unaided natural tendency.

Finally, the functional account describes recognition, valuation, behavior, and reconstruction. It does not explain why there is something it is like to undergo a condition. A proposed bridge to experience must add discriminating content. If competing accounts give exactly the same distribution for every permitted observation and intervention, those observations cannot choose between them. That conditional limit is not a proof that experience is forever inaccessible to inquiry.

## 7. The present settlement

| Target | Current answer | What remains |
|---|---|---|
| Can revision, retained reasons, protection, and renewal coexist in one selected process? | Yes, under the explicit finite audit, preference, technology, price, attack, and reserve conditions. | Application-specific validity and prevalence of those conditions; more general repertoires. |
| Can retraction preserve independent support and distinguish obstruction? | Yes; rooted recomputation supplies an exact finite procedure. | Sound and timely adjudication in fallible, contested settings. |
| Can the process build a useful feature absent among its initial primitives? | Yes, in a separately checked finite grammar with explicit costs. | Unrestricted novelty, fallible sparse evidence, and the ethical relevance of new concepts. |
| Can costly action be justified under incomplete ethical agreement? | Some choices can: robust dominance and fully symmetric permitted allocation have scoped solutions. | Other conflicts, model admission, priorities, and actual institutional legitimacy. |
| Does consistency alone generate inclusive concern? | No. Indifference is a counterexample; positive ethical grounds are required. | Continued argument about those grounds, not another consistency calculation. |
| Does the framework outperform established alternatives? | No demonstrated general advantage. | Matched-information, matched-resource, independent comparisons. |
| Does reality generally grow wiser and more inclusive? | Unresolved as an empirical population claim; unrestricted guarantees fail in the modeled classes. | A precise population hypothesis and discriminating evidence. |
| Does the theory explain subjective experience or every possible new reason? | No. | Additional explanatory mechanisms and independent tests. |

The proposed root of wisdom can now be stated more exactly: a capacity to let consequential reality and affected beings correct what counts as a reason, to make justified reasons effective under actual constraints, and to preserve or rebuild the means of further correction. This is the candidate defended by the combined research. It is not established as the unique foundation, an optimal theory among all possible theories, or a universal destination of reality.
