The studied connection is **actual paired consequences → formation of an initially absent evaluative connection → changed choice with factual knowledge preserved**. It aims to reduce one supplied input in the accepted evaluator. It is not an additional universal completion requirement, a reopening of completed local studies, or an explanation of ethical validity through conditioning.

## Accepted numerical scope

The [saved construction and reproduction record][construction] now preserve eleven cases and twenty-two local variants across all three inherited producer distributions. Every one of the 836 retained histories is independently reconstructed; failed ancestors remain in the denominator. This is a controlled formation lift, not a new probability law for unrestricted enlarged chemistry.

The strict paid history starts with no conducting links and actor10/recipient40. Matched aligned teaching forms two positive links and leaves n4; its selected service ends at actor4/recipient46. Inert and identity-filtered controls preserve the same acquired facts and teaching costs but abstain, ending at actor12/recipient36. The recipient's four-unit teaching loss is retained in each account. Opposed and mixed pairings produce negative or cancelling conductance. Conventional implementation matches exactly.

In the revision comparison, both arms acquire the same further data, continue native learning to n5, retire and replace the same component, and can afford the final service. The acquired replacement changes conductance and leads to abstention; the frozen-sign replacement still selects the effective service. Thus a learned change can restrict another center's delivered possibilities while knowledge of effective means remains intact. This causal contrast does not establish which outcome is ethically justified.

The result reduces the supplied **operative occupancy and sign** of the connection. It does not derive the entire architecture's capacity to acquire, interpret or select such connections. The [contribution audit][contribution] distinguishes that remaining content from the specific formation history now established. Formal law proofs and selected correspondence remain pending; this numerical acceptance does not complete the unified foundation.

## The input to be explained

[Architecture formation](ARCHITECTURE-FORMATION.md) constructs broad and filtered response paths from local parts. [Architecture learning](ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) uses another center's actual consequences to correct a response. [Evaluative uptake](EVALUATIVE-UPTAKE.md) acquires a coefficient from zero and makes that coefficient consequential for recipient choice. Its post-perception transfer route, sign and scale remain supplied.

Conditional acquisition and selection of shared evaluation already exist in the [concern learning account][learning] and [joint-formation mathematics](JOINT-FORMATION-MATH.md). Those results retain their declared correspondence, learning, prices and selection premises. The new question concerns a narrower implementation boundary: whether an operative cross-channel connection itself can begin absent and be built or changed by actual paid observations.

Merely learning another number and inserting it into a preinstalled helpful evaluator would leave that boundary unresolved. Here the acquired link must carry the signal used in assessment. A later instruction to set a controller's route to “shared” cannot be the mechanism.

## The proposed causal test

The design starts from actual saved histories before the old recipient-command import. It preserves acquired command knowledge, failed ancestry and spent resources. Any additional material, sensing capacity and provision must be isolated from the original preparation onward, with an exact projection to that unchanged preparation. A depleted endpoint receives no retrospective refund.

Native and recipient operations produce actual paid loss/return events. Paid meters acquire their outcomes separately. When an acquired recipient signal is active, a declared local plasticity law can bind an initially unbound coupling into the native evaluation pathway. The native outcome determines its excitatory or inhibitory sign. A generic transmission rule then reads the actual links during assessment; no operational route tag is set to shared after training.

The primary pairing comparisons keep both native and recipient outcome marginals fixed while changing their sequence pairing. Within each sequence, controls preserve the complete available observations, native learning and factual recipient knowledge while preventing conducting-link formation, blocking its evaluative use, or implementing the same transition law conventionally. The inert control still forms and pays for physically retained nonconducting products. Separate accounts retain actor fuel, recipient stock, source returns, sensing and writing expenses, formed and retired parts, and unused provision.

An optional revision branch changes a link before the single final assessment. The corresponding frozen-link control continues the same native learning and pays the same declared services. A preliminary design with only one extra return lesson was rejected during planning: its smaller native coefficient would make both routes abstain, so it would not isolate the changed connection's effect on choice. The revised sequence must preserve a feasible comparison in which the retained old connection still changes selection. These are prospective design constraints, not experimental results.

## What a successful result would and would not supply

Success would replace the initially operative cross-target link with a paid formation history and demonstrate its later causal use. It would explain one way acquired consequences can change the scope of evaluation, alongside nonconducting, opposing and filtered connections. The revision comparison tests whether changed influence can suppress a command whose physical efficacy and affordability remain intact; it does not independently identify a false analogy or an ethical error.

The input coding, plasticity chemistry, compatible sockets, native evaluation machinery, finite controller capacities, measurement calibration, addressing and gain remain local law premises. The paired small operations and the inherited recipient command are distinct modalities: their relation in a later assessment must be stated, not inferred from a shared recipient label. A relation can be acquired under those laws without being uniquely selected by predictive accuracy or private persistence.

The recipient's condition, an observer's learned influence, and a justified reason remain different quantities. Conditioning can preserve a false analogy or generate an opposed response. The separate [application account](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) must still defend patient-good, grounds, authority, competing claims and fulfillment; neither paired losses nor an excitatory link establishes them. The [separate actual-succession continuation](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) is now accepted at its finite causal scope (commit `dc4a16c`); this proposal neither replaces it nor automatically transports its conclusions to a differently formed link.

The study would fail at its stated boundary if the operative route were installed before acquisition, if observations came from inaccessible outcome metadata, if memory or parts appeared without backing, if costs or failed ancestors were reset, or if a claimed revision contrast were actually explained by changed native value or affordability. An exactly equivalent conventional learner is expected to match. The explanatory contribution would be the explicit acquisition and use of the connection, not an advantage obtained from new terminology.
