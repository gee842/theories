# Acquired evaluation, a successor and new evidence

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). **Prospective contract frozen and accepted for implementation; no new result is accepted.** This study follows the [accepted evaluative-uptake construction](EVALUATIVE-UPTAKE.md) and the [remaining-interface audit][audit]. Its [protocol][protocol], [literal cases][cases], [formal obligations][math] and [scope review][scope] belong to the existing material-learning study. The [root freeze acceptance][freeze] verifies the reviewed contract, independently preserved oracle and 150 unchanged parent artifacts before implementation or outcomes.

The named connection is **acquired evaluative influence → a distinct successor's access to retained learning → new recipient evidence → revised instrumental reasons → selected paid action**. One successor handoff is intended to supply both the missing inheritance interface and a new opportunity to correct knowledge. The full foundation still includes justified concern and wisdom, constructive and destructive regimes, and evidence for the scope claimed.

## Why the accepted result does not already give this continuation

The accepted chronology is recipient correction, then acquisition of an evaluative coefficient, then two uses by the same learner. Its final state has spent usable actor fuel, a finished action schedule, a locked evidence bank and no spare replacement pair. Retained recipient stock and isolated native source do not fund a new actor automatically. A new name or epoch would not make its used apparatus fresh.

The accepted [successor-learning](SUCCESSOR-LEARNING.md), [route-revision](LEARNING-ROUTE-REVISION.md) and [renewal](RECIPIENT-RENEWAL.md) results already establish real conditional lineages. Their copier transports route and q under different funding and feedback laws. It does not yet transport this evaluator's learned influence or access to it. The new study must supply that specific interface without treating all copied numbers as the same kind of knowledge.

## The proposed combined history

Begin at an actual final evaluator history, including its original failures and probability. Extra components and support are declared and isolated from the outset, rather than added to repair an exhausted endpoint. The proposed kit constructs a new writable controller, a fresh finite evidence window and a one-use evaluation port. A paid read and write transfer only the donor's actual command candidate; the child starts unqualified. The donor retires from control, while its hardware, retained coefficient, old records and residual accounts stay counted.

The successor then pays to access the actual retained coefficient through a specified connection. This is inheritance through retained supports; it is not a claim that the child privately experienced the donor's lessons. Loss of that connection must block evaluative use without erasing the acquired facts or pretending the coefficient is zero.

Changed and unchanged worlds receive the same two-probe inquiry schedule. New physical acquisition produces a prebound sparse record in a genuinely new bank. The maintained reader is moved and cold-bound through a paid interface; old publications and unused balances cannot become another live source of reasons or money. Actual new records, followed by paid interpretation, determine whether the child's candidate is currently supported. The learned coefficient then influences a paid help-or-abstain assessment under the declared transfer recipe.

Distinct succession must make a causal difference: subsequent feedback changes the child's separately backed q while the retired donor's controller remains unchanged and cannot act. The one recipient balance is shared across the history, not copied into two module accounts. Child-specific technical grants constrain inquiry and selected action; their existence does not establish ethical authority.

The current design uses nine additional A/H pairs and original provision M78/common360/source230. Its proposed energy account totals590: the accepted476, eighteen new constituent-formation payments, sixty-six protected service units and thirty new source units. These are prospective design quantities. Actual feasibility still depends on every ordered guard, source and payment prefix; the total does not prove a successful trace.

## What the comparisons must establish

The planned menu compares stable and changed efficacy, actual zero- and positive-coefficient donors, shared and filtered evaluation with equal information, and an exactly matching conventional controller. Missing access, missing evidence, an inherited qualification clamp, source outages, late unobserved change, permission loss, partial copying and payment shortages retain their actual failed states. A zero observation must not become a calibrated contrary fact when an outage can explain it.

Probe receipts and the later evaluation-selected receipt are separate outcomes. Inquiry is independently scheduled and can itself deliver resources. Total recipient stock therefore cannot identify the effect of evaluative selection. Likewise, transferred knowledge of a command does not imply correct private knowledge for the new actor; the old actor bit remains historically indexed.

## Effective continuation and justified application

The [accepted application account](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) permits bounded recognition of instrumental applicability under independently warranted patient-good, ground, authority and complete-comparison premises. Those premises must apply to the new actor and episode. The transferred coefficient and a fresh command certificate do not establish them.

Its [application-revision contract][application] also exposes a concrete counterexample to careless interpretation. A qualifying inquiry probe can fulfill a one-delivery claim before the evaluator acts. A later delivery cannot invoke that claim as still pending merely because it is called help. The independently fixed episode, actual receipt evidence and surviving reasons determine whether the application continues. Withdrawing one recommendation does not erase standing or every other ground.

The present physical design has no new patient-need or fulfillment controller. Its proposed result concerns inherited influence, corrected means knowledge and selected resource delivery. The [accepted reuse audit](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md#an-existing-witness-of-correction-and-feasible-choice) already establishes generic reason revision followed by selected, affordable action in the protection model. It preserves an independent reason and feasible alternatives when a different premise is withdrawn. Its proof and choice model have distinct stated scopes, and its two-patient meanings cannot be copied into the present two-command representation. The remaining common application must identify the actual accessible premise record, its justified meaning and its paid influence on this lineage's choice. An extra sensor or another complete apparatus is not a default completion requirement.

## Acceptance and the next synthesis

A successful result requires an actual paid path from the reached predecessor, independently reconstructed accounts and failures, proof of fresh evidence provenance, and causal use of the retained coefficient by a distinct learner. The supplied finite recipes, inquiry schedule, transfer rule, grants, calibration and continuing support must remain visible. No result is established yet.

Renaming the donor, reopening its spent bank, supplying n from observer history, duplicating recipient or reader accounts, importing a current reason at birth or prescribing the supposedly selected final action would defeat this proposed connection. A genuine lineage with poor information, inaccessible support or unaffordable action is a destructive regime to preserve.

After this round, integrate the accepted result or counterexample directly into the same five completion conditions. The next decision is whether the combined reference account now has the required compatible learning, concern and correction interfaces and evidence at its stated scope. Another local construction should be selected only for an identified remaining premise or discriminating test.
