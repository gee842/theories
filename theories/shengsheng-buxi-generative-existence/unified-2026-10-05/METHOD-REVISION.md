## What this adds to the theory–mathematics relation

The construction adds an operational level beyond forming and revising mathematical representations: a consequence of using an acquired representation can alter the retained rule that determines which representations will be generated next. Abstract inquiry can therefore participate in the same conditional organization–consequence–revision pattern as other organized activities.
