# External evidence and institutional correction

6 October 2026. AI-authored historical application of [Ren's clarification](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-alignment-governance-and-future.md): a center's future depends on integration with what lies beyond its boundary, whether or not it recognizes that dependence. Named connection: **affected-party evidence → individual correction → warranted shared-rule revision → corrected public guidance and delivered remedy**. This supports the existing empirical and governance obligations in [the completion contract](ALIGNMENT-COMPLETION.md); it adds no completion condition. The original case-to-rule study is preserved in an [exact pre-extension snapshot][before-notice]; the following notice extension adds a separately reviewed outward interface.

**External evidence supports corrective governance when it can change the reasoning and action that affect people's lives.** A system can receive complaints, learn facts and correct individual records while retaining a defective shared inference. The advance here is a concrete historical test of that gap and a reviewable criterion for closing it. It is neither a new general theorem nor evidence that the whole alignment problem has been solved.

## The historical test

Robodebt is an Australian automated public-administration case, not a test of contemporary generative AI. Three primary sources have different roles: a contemporaneous administrative investigation, a later court judgment approving a settlement, and a retrospective Royal Commission. The [evidence audit][evidence], [independent review][review] and [preservation record][sources] retain source versions, pinpoints and access limits.

The 2017 Ombudsman report recorded reassessments and administrative improvements while identifying limitations of income information and recommending better feedback capture and evaluation. Its qualified assurances cannot be treated as a judicial validation of every debt. [Report, executive summary, recommendations 5(e) and 8, Appendix B](https://www.ombudsman.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0022/43528/Report-Centrelinks-automated-debt-raising-and-recovery-system-April-2017.pdf).

In June 2021, the Federal Court approved the class-action settlement. The consent declarations concerned specified decisions relying solely on averaging tax data across longer periods, without evidence of constant fortnightly income or other support for the assumed income. They did not invalidate every use of data matching or averaging. The judgment distinguished what officials should have known from proof of actual knowledge of unlawfulness, and discussed unequal access to resources and challenge. Settlement approval was not a trial determination of every pleaded liability or complete restoration of every loss. [*Prygodicz (No 2)*, paragraphs 1–10, 13–24 and Annexure C](https://www.judgments.fedcourt.gov.au/judgments/Judgments/fca/single/2021/2021fca0634).

The 2023 Royal Commission found that individual Tribunal outcomes were generally implemented while the wider implications of their reasoning were disregarded; it also identified a remitted case where averaging was used again. Its conclusion questioned whether a better information system alone would have changed the agencies' response. The historical finding therefore concerns both rule-level uptake and actual implementation, with exceptions preserved. [Report, chapter 20, printed pp. 561–563](https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/publications/report).

These findings are attributed to their respective sources. They do not establish that every participant had the same knowledge, every appeal succeeded, or one isolated mechanism caused the eventual reversal. The present analysis does not infer institutional motives from persistence alone.

## What must pass from a case to a rule

Our analytical distinction is between **correcting an output** and **withdrawing a reason that no longer justifies producing that output**. A corrected individual fact can leave a general rule intact. A defeated shared premise can require broader reconsideration even when the correct final outcome of every affected case remains unknown.

The [reviewed institutional contract][contract] applies the existing settlement's selective retraction of unsupported reasons:

> When a warranted review defeats a premise necessary to a decision rule's justification in a specified domain, that premise must cease to count as sufficient support for decisions that depend on it there.

This is our normative and epistemic application criterion, not a quotation from a judgment. It retracts the defeated support; it does not automatically reverse every conclusion or prescribe identical remedies. Independently valid evidence survives. The applicable burden of justification and the authority to investigate, suspend, revise or remedy remain explicit.

| What the challenge establishes | What the institution must distinguish |
|---|---|
| A person-specific record is wrong | This correction from any independently established recurring defect |
| A shared evidentiary premise fails | Decisions relying on that premise from those supported independently |
| A governing standard is inadequate or misapplied | Accurate factual execution from justified authority and acceptable treatment |
| A credible issue remains unresolved | A reason to investigate or protect provisionally from a settled finding and final remedy |

The useful scope is a dependency relation: which decisions rely on the same challenged inference under the same relevant conditions. It is not simply everyone receiving an adverse outcome. Rule versions, dates, exceptions, evidence and legal or ethical grounds matter. Binding precedent and epistemic relevance also differ: a body's limited jurisdiction does not make its reasoning irrelevant, and relevant reasoning does not grant it unlimited power.

An elementary illustration connects this to [the earlier external-information argument](ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md): two different time histories can have the same aggregate total but require different period-specific decisions. An algorithm receiving only that total cannot distinguish them without another warranted input or assumption. This is an information limitation, not an estimate of historical error rates. Faster calculation cannot itself supply the missing distinction.

## Making correction observable

A review system should be assessed by the connection between the scope of a justified reason and the scope of changed action. The contract asks for a trace through five handoffs: retained reasons; justified identification of dependent cases; an authorized response; funded implementation; and verification with further challenge available. This is a diagnostic interface, not a claim that one organizational architecture uniquely satisfies it.

For a particular rule revision, inspect what happens to relevant people who never appealed. Does the changed method actually govern their cases? Does it retain valid distinctions? Are earlier cases reconsidered where warranted, or only future decisions changed? Does an approved remedy reach the person? A written policy, closed complaint or software release alone answers none of these questions completely.

This makes the application falsifiable. If a claimed shared-ground correction changes only the appellant's record while covered decisions continue using the defeated basis, the claimed rule-level correction has failed. If the original finding concerned only an individual fact, however, broader reversal is not established. If a change is authorized but never implemented, the break concerns execution. If a warranted defect is understood but authority, incentives or implementation prevent change, collecting more of the same evidence alone does not resolve that break. Additional corroboration can matter where the defect or its applicability remains genuinely uncertain.

The historical case supplies evidence that these distinctions matter. It does not measure the counterfactual benefit of adopting our contract. That would require linked case and rule histories, implementation evidence and a design capable of separating the revision from other contemporaneous changes. Appeal records are selected observations; they cannot certify the absence of harm among people unable to challenge a decision. Nor do they satisfy the settlement theorem's supplied assumption of a complete sound audit over its declared domain.

## Correction must also reach the information people act on

An internal revision does not necessarily correct the institution's continuing influence through earlier letters, demands or warnings. The [follow-through source audit][notice-evidence] examines that outward connection. In April 2021, Recommendation 8 called for correction of earlier information when an unfinished income review continued. [Investigation, §§3.106–3.113 and Recommendation 8](https://www.ombudsman.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0014/112442/Services-Australias-Income-Compliance-Report.pdf).

The 2022 follow-up classified that recommendation as unimplemented in its assessment, while §1.292 acknowledged later agency implementation steps. The Office recognized the recommendation's original condition, but criticized the delay and absent status correspondence; advice to proactive callers did not establish communication to the whole affected group. This is a dated oversight assessment, not a measurement of recipients' beliefs or a claim that later correction never occurred. [Follow-up, §§1.281–1.292 and agency response](https://www.ombudsman.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0029/286517/Did-they-do-what-they-said-they-would-Volume-2.pdf).

There is also positive implementation evidence. The agency reported over AU$749.1 million refunded by April 2023 against an estimated AU$752.7 million, and approximately 3,100 former customers still to engage by June. Those administrative figures have different dates; we have not independently verified each recipient's receipt, population-wide redress or restoration of all losses. [Annual report 2022–23, §7.10, printed p. 111](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-10/annual-report-2022-23.pdf). The evidence audit keeps refund and settlement categories separate; this study calculates no combined completion rate.

Our inference is that correction has more than one endpoint. An institution can improve its own decision procedure while the information available to affected people remains unresolved. Conversely, successful notice does not perform the remedy it describes. The [notice contract][notice-contract] therefore distinguishes:

| Claimed achievement | What must be established at that scope |
|---|---|
| Revised institutional rule | The warranted revision, its authority, domain and effective date. |
| Corrected guidance | The superseded representation, the accurate replacement and the relevant recipients. |
| Accessible communication | A usable route to those recipients; sending, receipt and comprehension remain distinct observations. |
| Usable choice | The corrected option remains practically available, with warranted handling of deadlines, assistance and intervening consequences. |
| Delivered remedy | The justified reconsideration, payment or other response actually occurs. |

This is an application of existing answerability and claims, not a derivation of duty from causal influence alone. Given a justified responsibility to correct an institution's materially misleading directions, its internal update is insufficient when the responsibility includes outward communication. Its actual content, scope, timing and reasonable-access conditions matter. A pending future policy can coexist with a warranted present status correction. Independent valid obligations survive; defeating one reason does not settle every underlying claim.

The test is correspondingly bounded. A claim of completed recipient-level correction fails where a covered person still reasonably treats the superseded institutional instruction as operative because it remains their available guidance. That is a proposed falsifier, not an observation established for every person in this case. A duty of reasonable communication need not guarantee universal understanding, and useful partial remedies need not wait for universal success. Noncallers belong in the relevant coverage frame; silence establishes neither informed agreement nor error. Unsuccessful contact and unknown fulfillment must remain visible.

This extension adds a documented implementation problem and a reviewed criterion, not a causal estimate of a communication intervention. [Independent review][notice-review] preserves the conditional recommendation, later agency response, subgroup denominators and limits of administrative reporting. The [source package][notice-sources] records labeled reader excerpts and failed original-file retrieval; it does not claim preservation of the original PDFs.

## Consequence for alignment and the foundation

Ren's point reaches beyond information flowing across a boundary. An institution's own records can be corrected by the lives those records describe; its messages can in turn shape the information available for those people's decisions. The relevant interaction therefore includes both directions. Receiving information, recognizing its significance, accepting a justified reason, correcting outward guidance and carrying through an authorized response are distinct connections. Each can be supported or obstructed. This case supplies a governance application of that distinction; it does not identify the complete cognitive mechanism of an institution or establish a unique advantage over existing accountability theory.

For learned AI, the corresponding proposed test would hold the challenged premise and its domain explicit, then examine whether a warranted correction changes dependent decisions for other affected people, including after model or operator replacement. A revised explanation alongside unchanged decisions would fail that claim. This is a proposed test, not a learned-AI result obtained here.

The [ethical foundation](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) already argues for standing, relevant consistency, answerability and feasible implementation. Those arguments remain necessary: an institution's continued operation does not substitute for the futures of the people it governs. Its ability to obtain external resources or accurate information does not by itself give others' interests sufficient influence. The [evaluative application](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) likewise requires warranted grounds, authority and comparison; this study makes one institutional route from those grounds to actual correction inspectable.

The next central connection remains **warranted evaluative correction → effective action through actual succession**, with preserved meanings, resources and continuing support. This round supplies a historical failure test and scoped implementation criteria, including outward correction and actual remedy. It does not reopen accepted finite bridges or remove the remaining ethical, material and empirical obligations. A separate [development recommendation][continuation-gap] identifies action-dependent future power as a possible later test of the strategic account; it is not an executed result or a new completion condition.
