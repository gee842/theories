**Another center's condition becomes a warranted reason for this action when acquired evidence establishes that an independently defended ground applies to that center, and that the action answers it under the relevant authority and competing claims.** Learning can change the recognized application of a reason without creating the patient's standing. A mechanism that gives a consequence weight and an argument that warrants that weight have different jobs in this connection.

The [architecture-to-learning result](ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) establishes actual target-specific correction. The accepted causal study shows how an initially absent evaluative coefficient can be acquired from consequences, admitted into a recipient choice and retained through later use under its declared law. This application study makes the next inference inspectable: what those physical fields must mean for the chosen action to instantiate warranted concern.

## Start with the actual learned quantities

The accepted evaluator receives the existing recipient facts and command correspondence. Its additional register begins at zero. An own-support operation either recovers its two-unit expense or loses those two units; separate observation and writing expenses are excluded from the teaching datum. With that operational shortfall encoded as \(Y\in\{0,1\}\), the supplied update is

\[
n' = \lfloor n/2\rfloor+4Y,\qquad w=n/8.
\]

Three loss observations produce \(n=7\). At the normal trial charge, a shared transfer path uses the declared comparison \(10n>64\), corresponding to \(10w>8\), to choose a recipient trial over abstention. The filtered comparison retains the same factual recipient information and own-operation outcomes while withholding their evaluative influence. These are now checked causal specifications; the [field contract](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-CONTRACT.md) records their source and scope.

The numerical meanings are limited. \(w\) is a retained response to the actor's operational shortfall. It is not an identified feeling, the recipient's welfare, a confidence level, or a justified interpersonal exchange rate. Ten is a modeled recipient resource receipt. Eight is a trial expense in the supplied resource law. Their appearance in one comparison does not establish the ethical interpretation of the comparison.

The [mathematical application audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-MATH.md) makes this precise without changing the mechanism. From zero, three binary observations yield \(n=Y_1+2Y_2+4Y_3\); only the all-loss word passes this particular threshold. This identifies exactly what changes the implemented behavior. It does not identify why that behavior is warranted.

## Two bounded positive applications

The first application uses the existing qualitative dominance argument. Suppose independent evidence identifies a recipient condition that this command improves, a defended reason applies to that improvement, and the intervention has settled permission. Suppose further that the command is feasible and, compared with every distinct alternative in the declared feasible, permitted menu, is no worse on every other relevant ground and strictly better on the admitted patient ground. Under the defended dominance rule, the command should be preferred. This conclusion needs no measurement of concern in fuel units.

The operation can still cost eight physical units. That expense must be funded, and its consequences for the actor, other affected parties, later obligations and future correction must be included. Calling money or fuel “spare,” paying the expense in advance, or ending the measurement window does not establish absence of a competing burden. Thus this is a conditional positive application, not an assertion that the physical witness already satisfies those ethical premises.

The second application concerns a genuine competing loss. Here the patient retains a reason in favor of support, but the action requires a separately defended priority or comparative margin. If that justification ranks the particular intervention above every feasible permitted alternative, and the physical history executes it, the result can instantiate warranted costly concern. A numerical implementation can represent that judgment only after its calibration is defended. The learned \(w=7/8\) does not itself supply that defense. The [existing transfer argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-FULL-THEORY-AUDIT.md#applicability-transfer-and-costly-choice) already proves why positive standing leaves a costly threshold underdetermined; this study applies that result to the acquired register.

Both applications preserve the distinction between an externally warranted action and an actor recognizing a reason. An observer can justify an action that a controller performs for an unrelated cause. To claim recognition of the particular ground, the causal study must show how acquired evidence of its applicability enters the retained evaluation and changes selection. A coefficient responding only to own-stock loss cannot establish this further achievement by itself.

## Uncertainty must retain the right claim

The existing [finite decision account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/normative/decision-math.md) retains factual and normative interpretations with documented grounds. Its robust remainder contains actions not excluded by the declared comparisons. Membership does not mean that every retained interpretation licenses that action, or that it is uniquely right.

For this application, report a stronger guarantee separately when it is requested: let \(L_m\) be the actions licensed by interpretation \(m\), including its authority and comparison premises. For a nonempty, justified retained set \(M\), an action licensed in every retained interpretation belongs to

\[
L_{\mathrm{all}}=\bigcap_{m\in M} L_m.
\]

This is a guarantee relative to the retained interpretations, not a new universal decision rule. If one interpretation licenses helping and another licenses abstaining, with neither licensing the other's choice, both can survive the earlier robust comparison while \(L_{\mathrm{all}}\) is empty. Calling them undominated cannot repair that disagreement. Nor does an empty intersection establish that abstention is justified, that action is impossible, or that the patient has no standing. Decisions under unresolved uncertainty require their own defended policy.

An empty interpretation set cannot certify anything through a vacuous universal claim. Nor may the investigator retain only interpretations that make the observed choice look good. The grounds for retaining or excluding each interpretation must be independent of the desired verdict. Actual-world coverage is a substantive soundness premise, not something established by enumerating a convenient list.

## What new evidence can change

Suppose recipient facts correctly predict that the command supplies a packet. In one admissible interpretation that packet relieves the relevant deficit; in another it is irrelevant or worsens a different condition. Equal receipt prediction leaves these interpretations unresolved. Evidence about the recipient's actual condition can distinguish them. If that evidence defeats the claimed benefit, it withdraws this instrumental application while leaving the independently admitted patient ground intact.

Authority can also differ across histories with the same final receipt and apparent benefit. A technical permission bit or agreeable outcome cannot reconstruct a missing valid authorization. The [authorship argument](HUIGEN.md) owns that substantive distinction. The application record must preserve the relevant history rather than infer permission from success.

Evidence must remain applicable through use. A record about one recipient or an earlier context cannot silently become a reason for another recipient or later changed circumstances. Likewise, a certificate of one successful episode cannot certify continuous protection or a replacement learner. The existing [claims/action result](CLAIMS-AND-PAID-ACTION.md) already distinguishes admitted claims, actual fulfillment and unresolved claims, and supplies the relevant paid-execution interface. It is reused here; no new claim ledger is constructed.

## When the application is functionally recognized

The next inference has a bounded positive answer. **A fixed, independently warranted ground can combine with acquired evidence to yield functional recognition of which action serves that ground.** The ground need not be learned anew on every occasion or stored as an explicit sentence. What must be learned and used in this case is the command-specific instrumental relation. This is a sufficient functional criterion for the present study, not a claim about conscious understanding or the only possible account of recognizing reasons.

Fix one recipient episode. Independently establish that the relevant resource delivery improves this recipient's specified condition, that the condition supplies an actor-addressed reason, that the intervention has legitimate authority, and that the proposed choice answers the full relevant comparison. These premises must be fixed before interpreting the favorable output and remain applicable across the declared comparison cases. The actual learner then acquires and retains evidence of which command delivers that resource. Its reader, evaluation and action must use that evidence as an operative condition for the recipient-directed response.

This can be more than an observer approving the final behavior. The needed causal relation is an acquired record controlling a retained assessment or command, with appropriate differences across supported, unsupported and coherently revised cases. The same target binding, fixed ground, active evaluative disposition and relevant payment conditions must be preserved when isolating that relation. A constant policy that happens to help, or a controller reading the analyst's hidden target law, would not establish this evidence-mediated recognition. The claim does not require every input bit individually to change action; redundant evidence can preserve a sound conclusion.

The [existing reason meanings](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/formal/RecipientReasons.lean) already express the narrow inference: a supplied ground \(G\), together with acquired command compatibility, supports the corresponding command-specific proposition. The accepted [fresh-revision construction](RECIPIENT-REASON-REVISION.md) supplies actual acquisition and revision under that ground. Sound acquired provenance and current command support are required for the interpretation; the final runtime law does not add a separate check of the old qualification flag. Its own-consequence coefficient governs evaluative influence; it does not supply the patient-ground premises above.

The smallest positive application uses a funded first episode with sufficient acquired influence, sound current recipient evidence and a supported stored command. Under the fixed warranted interpretation, the retained command-specific conclusion establishes whether that candidate is a supported means of answering this patient's admitted ground. A paid assessment and matching execution can then realize that answer. This establishes instrumental application of the fixed ground if the actual mediation and trace meet the contract. It does not establish the acquisition of standing, a newly discovered need, authority or an ethical priority rule.

This supplies the bounded causal mediation; the ethical interpretation remains independently warranted.

Lost or inconsistent support should remove the affected authorization without deleting the patient's standing. A coherent false channel or an unnoticed change in real efficacy can instead leave apparently matching records and produce mistaken action. The evaluator has no general reopening process during this phase and no new reader of patient needs or normative truth. ## Correct command, withdrawn application

The next change can concern the reason for another action while leaving the command correct. Fix one independently warranted claim addressing this actor: provide one specified service to this recipient in this episode. Define in advance what counts as completing that service and why it answers the admitted ground. When sound, accessible evidence establishes its completion, that claim no longer supplies the same reason for another delivery. The recipient's standing, the command's efficacy and the acquired coefficient can all remain unchanged.

The persistence rule is bounded by the claim's content. Within its admitted scope and absent a separate defeater, a requirement to complete one historical service supplies a pending reason until that service is completed; successful completion satisfies that demand and does not automatically create a second instance. Requiring another delivery on exactly the same ground would silently change the admitted demand. This is a conditional rule for a separately justified finite service claim. A continuing need, a maintenance obligation or a newly arising episode has different content and can warrant further action. One receipt alone proves neither satiation nor the absence of other claims.

The episode and completion condition must be fixed before outcomes. Every actual receipt meeting that condition counts, including one produced by a learning probe before the later action labeled “help.” If the inquiry has already delivered the admitted service, its results cannot be used to keep that same claim pending until a preferred demonstration. This applies to the accepted parent as well: for a claim active before a qualifying fresh probe, the first evaluator-selected action can already be a repetition. The entrance state must be classified accordingly. A later episode must have an independently warranted onset and claim.

| What changes | What the correction may withdraw | What remains distinct |
|---|---|---|
| This episode's service is fulfilled, or its claimed benefit is defeated | Support for another action derived from this particular application | Standing, correct means knowledge and other grounds |
| Legitimate authority is revoked | Permission to act | A positive benefit reason may remain |
| A competing claim takes priority | The comparative license for this action | Its pro tanto reason may remain |
| Funds become unavailable | Executability under the actual resource law | Warrant and permission need not change |

Together they specify the logical correction. They do not install a fulfillment memory or produce the patient interpretation.

There is already a concrete evidence opportunity. In the accepted evaluator's paid recipient trial, the acquisition step temporarily holds the observed capture in `physical.acquired_record`. The following write updates the factual core and clears that record. A continuation can use this existing observation before it disappears, or account for preserving and transferring its episode meaning. The observer's outcome log, a consumed port and a closed opportunity are not substitutes: each may exist without the actor knowing that this service succeeded.

To realize the correction, the continuation must expose the actual recipient-and-episode binding, soundness of the completion observation, its accessible lifetime, and the route by which it changes the next recommendation. It must retain a feasible, permitted later choice under compatible funding; depletion or a consumed one-use port alone cannot demonstrate revised evaluation. The interface needs to distinguish supported pending status, certified fulfillment and unknown status, with an explicitly justified policy for each. These are semantic distinctions, not a demand for three new hardware fields. Actual fulfillment can precede its recognition, so an unknown record cannot simply be read as an unmet need.

This adds a specific relation to the generative account: **an action can change the conditions that made that action worth repeating**. For this bounded service, regulation must track the consequences of its own success as well as its failure.
