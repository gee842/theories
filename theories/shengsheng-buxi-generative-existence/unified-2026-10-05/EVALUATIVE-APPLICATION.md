# When acquired understanding warrants a particular action

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of [Ren's request](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-evaluative-application-continuation.md). **The conditional application audit is complete and independently reviewed.** It uses the already defended ethical premises and accepted evidence/action interfaces to interpret the ongoing evaluative-uptake construction. It adds no physical apparatus or empirical result. The prospective evaluator remains separately owned and reviewed; its implementation is not certified here.

**Another center's condition becomes a warranted reason for this action when acquired evidence establishes that an independently defended ground applies to that center, and that the action answers it under the relevant authority and competing claims.** Learning can change the recognized application of a reason without creating the patient's standing. A mechanism that gives a consequence weight and an argument that warrants that weight have different jobs in this connection.

The [architecture-to-learning result](ARCHITECTURE-LEARNING.md) establishes actual target-specific correction. The parallel causal study asks how an initially absent evaluative coefficient can be acquired from consequences, admitted into a recipient choice and retained through later use. This application study makes the next inference inspectable: what those physical fields would have to mean for the chosen action to instantiate warranted concern.

## Start with the actual learned quantities

The proposed evaluator receives the existing recipient facts and command correspondence. Its additional register begins at zero. An own-support operation either recovers its two-unit expense or loses those two units; separate observation and writing expenses are excluded from the teaching datum. With that operational shortfall encoded as \(Y\in\{0,1\}\), the proposed update is

\[
n' = \lfloor n/2\rfloor+4Y,\qquad w=n/8.
\]

Three loss observations produce \(n=7\). A shared transfer path then uses the declared comparison \(10n>64\), corresponding to \(10w>8\), to choose a recipient trial over abstention. The filtered comparison can retain the same factual recipient information and own-operation outcomes while withholding their evaluative influence. These are prospective causal specifications; the [field contract](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-CONTRACT.md) records their source and status.

The numerical meanings are limited. \(w\) is a retained response to the actor's operational shortfall. It is not an identified feeling, the recipient's welfare, a confidence level, or a justified interpersonal exchange rate. Ten is a modeled recipient resource receipt. Eight is a trial expense in the supplied resource law. Their appearance in one comparison does not establish the ethical interpretation of the comparison.

The [mathematical application audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-MATH.md) makes this precise without changing the mechanism. From zero, three binary observations yield \(n=Y_1+2Y_2+4Y_3\); only the all-loss word passes this particular threshold. This identifies exactly what changes the proposed behavior. It does not identify why that behavior is warranted.

## A recipient receipt needs an application relation

For the same actor, recipient, command and relevant time window, distinguish the following entries:

| Entry | What the physical construction can supply | What the interpretation still needs |
|---|---|---|
| Consequence | A command and an actual or conditionally predicted recipient receipt | Evidence that this receipt changes an independently significant condition of this recipient |
| Learning | A retained coefficient and the observations that changed it | A justified account of when its transfer is relevant; resemblance to the actor is insufficient |
| Ground | A factual premise to which a standing rule could apply | An independently defended reason addressing this actor, with its scope and defeaters |
| Permission | A live scope, delivery route and technical authorization | Legitimate authority for this intervention, including relevant prior decisions and history |
| Comparison | The implemented score and physical action alternatives | The relevant competing claims, resource consequences and a defended ordering or conflict rule |
| Use | A funded execution for the same recipient | Evidence of the promised effect and the precise duration for which it is claimed |

The [ethical foundation](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) and [bounded patient-ground argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-FULL-THEORY-AUDIT.md#9-finite-interface-with-formed-understanding-and-action) supply positive arguments for standing, relevant consistency and a permissible response to independently identified adversity. They do not identify a resource counter with experience. A resource-support interpretation can be appropriate on its own terms; an experiential interpretation needs additional evidence.

The recipient may be dispensable to the actor and unable to communicate. Neither fact defeats an independently established patient ground. Conversely, supplying a packet does not establish that ground merely because the recipient cannot dispute the interpretation. Communication, relevant preferences, causal evidence and justified representation can inform the application, with their actual limits retained.

## Two bounded positive applications

The first application uses the existing qualitative dominance argument. Suppose independent evidence identifies a recipient condition that this command improves, a defended reason applies to that improvement, and the intervention has settled permission. Suppose further that the command is feasible and, compared with every distinct alternative in the declared feasible, permitted menu, is no worse on every other relevant ground and strictly better on the admitted patient ground. Under the defended dominance rule, the command should be preferred. This conclusion needs no measurement of concern in fuel units.

The operation can still cost eight physical units. That expense must be funded, and its consequences for the actor, other affected parties, later obligations and future correction must be included. Calling money or fuel “spare,” paying the expense in advance, or ending the measurement window does not establish absence of a competing burden. Thus this is a conditional positive application, not an assertion that the proposed physical witness already satisfies it.

The second application concerns a genuine competing loss. Here the patient retains a reason in favor of support, but the action requires a separately defended priority or comparative margin. If that justification ranks the particular intervention above every feasible permitted alternative, and the physical history executes it, the result can instantiate warranted costly concern. A numerical implementation can represent that judgment only after its calibration is defended. The learned \(w=7/8\) does not itself supply that defense. The [existing transfer argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-FULL-THEORY-AUDIT.md#applicability-transfer-and-costly-choice) already proves why positive standing leaves a costly threshold underdetermined; this study applies that result to the proposed register.

Both applications preserve the distinction between an externally warranted action and an actor recognizing a reason. An observer can justify an action that a controller performs for an unrelated cause. To claim recognition of the particular ground, the causal study must show how acquired evidence of its applicability enters the retained evaluation and changes selection. A coefficient responding only to own-stock loss cannot establish this further achievement by itself.

## Uncertainty must retain the right claim

The existing [finite decision account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-revisable-concern/settlement/normative/decision-math.md) retains factual and normative interpretations with documented grounds. Its robust remainder contains actions not excluded by the declared comparisons. Membership does not mean that every retained interpretation licenses that action, or that it is uniquely right.

For this application, report a stronger guarantee separately when it is requested: let \(L_m\) be the actions licensed by interpretation \(m\), including its authority and comparison premises. For a nonempty, justified retained set \(M\), an action licensed in every retained interpretation belongs to

\[
L_{\mathrm{all}}=\bigcap_{m\in M} L_m.
\]

This is a guarantee relative to the retained interpretations, not a new universal decision rule. If one interpretation licenses helping and another licenses abstaining, with neither licensing the other's choice, both can survive the earlier robust comparison while \(L_{\mathrm{all}}\) is empty. Calling them undominated cannot repair that disagreement. Nor does an empty intersection establish that abstention is justified, that action is impossible, or that the patient has no standing. Decisions under unresolved uncertainty require their own defended policy.

An empty interpretation set cannot certify anything through a vacuous universal claim. Nor may the investigator retain only interpretations that make the observed choice look good. The grounds for retaining or excluding each interpretation must be independent of the desired verdict. Actual-world coverage is a substantive soundness premise, not something established by enumerating a convenient list.

## What new evidence can change

Suppose recipient facts correctly predict that the command supplies a packet. In one admissible interpretation that packet relieves the relevant deficit; in another it is irrelevant or worsens a different condition. Equal receipt prediction leaves these interpretations unresolved. Evidence about the recipient's actual condition can distinguish them. If that evidence defeats the claimed benefit, it withdraws this instrumental application while leaving the independently admitted patient ground intact.

Authority can also differ across histories with the same final receipt and apparent benefit. A technical permission bit or agreeable outcome cannot reconstruct a missing valid authorization. The [authorship argument](HUIGEN.md) owns that substantive distinction. The application record must preserve the relevant history rather than infer permission from success.

Evidence must remain applicable through use. A record about one recipient or an earlier context cannot silently become a reason for another recipient or later changed circumstances. Likewise, a certificate of one successful episode cannot certify continuous protection or a replacement learner. The existing [claims/action result](CLAIMS-AND-PAID-ACTION.md) already distinguishes admitted claims, actual fulfillment and unresolved claims, and supplies the relevant paid-execution interface. It is reused here; no new claim ledger is constructed.

## When the application is functionally recognized

The next inference has a bounded positive answer. **A fixed, independently warranted ground can combine with acquired evidence to yield functional recognition of which action serves that ground.** The ground need not be learned anew on every occasion or stored as an explicit sentence. What must be learned and used in this case is the command-specific instrumental relation. This is a sufficient functional criterion for the present study, not a claim about conscious understanding or the only possible account of recognizing reasons.

Fix one recipient episode. Independently establish that the relevant resource delivery improves this recipient's specified condition, that the condition supplies an actor-addressed reason, that the intervention has legitimate authority, and that the proposed choice answers the full relevant comparison. These premises must be fixed before interpreting the favorable output and remain applicable across the declared comparison cases. The actual learner then acquires and retains evidence of which command delivers that resource. Its reader, evaluation and action must use that evidence as an operative condition for the recipient-directed response.

This can be more than an observer approving the final behavior. The needed causal relation is an acquired record controlling a retained assessment or command, with appropriate differences across supported, unsupported and coherently revised cases. The same target binding, fixed ground, active evaluative disposition and relevant payment conditions must be preserved when isolating that relation. A constant policy that happens to help, or a controller reading the analyst's hidden target law, would not establish this evidence-mediated recognition. The claim does not require every input bit individually to change action; redundant evidence can preserve a sound conclusion.

The [existing reason meanings](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/identification/recording/reason-consumer/formal/RecipientReasons.lean) already express the narrow inference: a supplied ground \(G\), together with acquired command compatibility, supports the corresponding command-specific proposition. The accepted [fresh-revision construction](RECIPIENT-REASON-REVISION.md) supplies actual acquisition and revision under that ground. The now-frozen evaluator protocol proposes using the retained publication and candidate to gate its new paid assessment. Its own-consequence coefficient governs evaluative influence; it does not supply the patient-ground premises above.

The smallest positive application uses a funded first episode with sufficient acquired influence, sound current recipient evidence and a supported stored command. Under the fixed warranted interpretation, the retained command-specific conclusion establishes whether that candidate is a supported means of answering this patient's admitted ground. A paid assessment and matching execution can then realize that answer. This establishes instrumental application of the fixed ground if the actual mediation and trace meet the contract. It does not establish the acquisition of standing, a newly discovered need, authority or an ethical priority rule.

The precise counterfactuals matter. At one read, the live choice is to execute the stored candidate or abstain. Changing the publication to support the opposite command while leaving the candidate unchanged must block the mismatch. Redirecting action requires a coherently acquired alternative candidate and publication, such as a compatible history of the parent learning process. Editing both fields by hand demonstrates a structural possibility, not a reached paid learning history. The [expanded contract](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-CONTRACT.md#5-the-smallest-functional-recognition-of-instrumental-applicability) and [mathematical argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-MATH.md#7-functional-recognition-of-instrumental-applicability-under-a-fixed-ground) keep those scopes distinct.

The accepted parent already supplies such acquired command diversity: two saved histories reach current support for opposite recipient commands with the same displayed actor, recipient and source balances, then complete their respective paid commands successfully. The [source-bound inspection](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-CONTRACT.md#reached-command-diversity-in-the-accepted-parent) preserves their different histories. This supplies real predecessors for the comparison; it does not substitute for checking the new evaluator's mediation or its ethical interpretation.

Lost or inconsistent support should remove the affected authorization without deleting the patient's standing. A coherent false channel or an unnoticed change in real efficacy can instead leave apparently matching records and produce mistaken action. Such a controller may act on a false instrumental belief; it does not thereby acquire warrant. The evaluator has no general reopening process during this phase and no new reader of patient needs or normative truth. Correcting the ground's application beyond its fixed domain remains outside this bounded recognition claim.

One successful episode can also discharge the original need. A second offered packet, retained coefficient or replaced port does not establish that another delivery is warranted. A later episode needs an independently applicable patient-good, authority and comparison interpretation of its own. This limits the claim without requiring a new apparatus for every repeated action.

The conceptual implication is therefore closed conditionally: static warranted premises plus sound, acquired and causally used instrumental evidence can constitute this limited functional recognition. Its realization in the new evaluator still requires the producer's actual acquisition, mediation and paid-use checks. The ethical premises remain substantive inputs, and the broader formation of revisable concern remains a separate research obligation.

## The exact completion boundary

This study specifies how the proposed evaluator's actual quantities enter an ethical application and where they cannot do so without additional grounds. Its [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION-REVIEW.md) checks those meanings, the uncertainty distinction and the supplied premises. The mathematical arguments are elementary applications of existing results; no new Lean build, empirical validation or completed causal evaluator is claimed.

The contribution to the unified account is a **conditional application contract: acquired consequence → warranted recognition of a particular ground's applicability → justified comparison → actual same-recipient response**, with factual, normative and operational premises kept distinct. The parallel causal construction can supply the acquired influence and execution; the ethical arguments can supply a defended ground. Completing their bounded join requires the concrete recipient-good, authority and competing-burden entries above to be satisfied, or the delivered claim to remain recipient-sensitive resource control. Broader concern, its continued correction and the foundation's full explanatory scope retain their existing completion conditions.
