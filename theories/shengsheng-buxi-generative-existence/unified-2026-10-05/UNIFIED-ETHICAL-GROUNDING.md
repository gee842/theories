### Completion and further enrichment

The [concrete completion comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#17-completion-and-the-claim-of-further-enrichment) gives the previous saturation rival a positive account. Their pleasure-centered commitments are not adopted as the foundation's criterion.

Both sides owe an explanation. Completing a task or reporting calm does not establish saturation of whole quality. A genuinely new appreciation does not establish strict enrichment. Factual accuracy about an encounter also differs from ethical fittingness: correctly identifying what one welcomes does not by itself justify welcoming it. The affirmative appraisal of a richer lived relationship remains substantive, and actual ease, other appreciations and affected relations must be retained.

Crucially, the strongest completion rival grants that appreciative realization is good. Its dispute concerns further improvement over another worthwhile continuation. Defeating it is therefore not a prerequisite for recognizing that good, and it need not conflict with the claim that appreciative formation can improve other continuations. The account must distinguish realizing good, sustaining it and making a particular future better. The next foundational comparison concerns the good-making grounds of the actual completed activity. Pleasure-based, relational and compatible composite explanations remain to be assessed at their real scope; their disagreement over some increments does not by itself establish disagreement over every good. Universal ethical identification and practical authority remain unfinished.

### Fulfilled activity and the future it forms

The [next grounding comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#18-fulfilled-activity-and-the-future-it-forms) distinguishes successful functioning, realized experiential quality and whole ethical goodness. This sharpens the positive candidate while preserving the possibility of harmful fulfillment.

An encounter's completion does not close its consequences. A finished collaboration can leave altered relationships, burdens and opportunities in the continuing world. Its real quality must remain in the account, alongside those effects. Conversely, the whole need not be one larger experience with a single final satisfaction. Its differentiated activities and their consequences require assessment within the same unfolding.

The maintaining arrangement also differs from the realization it enables. If reciprocal expression retains its quality when competent asymmetric provision replaces reciprocal maintenance, mutual maintenance cannot be that quality's necessary ground. Actual changes in dependence, control or burdens can defeat the proposed match and must be examined. Renewal remains causally consequential without becoming an automatic ethical certificate.

The candidate concerns **what activity realizes and how that realization participates in forming the future of the whole**. The inherited hostile-appreciation comparison already asks whether context transforms a favorable aspect. The next test must examine a different relation: damage that supports, serves as a means to, or follows from the fulfilled activity. Preserve the specified expressive and appreciative features while allowing that changed relation to affect what is realized. Identifying an immanent quality is progress toward specifying the proposed ground; why it has the claimed ethical significance remains unresolved. ### Damaging support and correction

The [concrete support test](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#19-damaging-support-and-the-organization-of-correction) distinguishes a damaging process supplying an activity, damage itself serving as a means, and the activity sustaining that process. Stopping an activity may leave an independently maintained damaging process in place; preventing a damaging byproduct may preserve the activity.

Stopping that work then interrupts different activities. This constructed comparison establishes a difference in immediate correction consequences under fixed routing. It does not establish equal complete histories, different complete sets of possibilities, or an ethical ranking. The already examined sacrifice and concern-formation cases are retained, not restarted as new results. ### A real difference in the whole

The following descriptions retain their original local scopes. The current governing criterion and continuation test are stated immediately above and in [renewal and actual selection](#renewal-and-actual-selection).

The whole includes the formation of experiencing lives, dependencies, capacities, purposes and evaluative practices, together with their continuing effects. It is not a larger subject, a single utility total, or the current institution's survival. Inquiry itself participates in these relations and has limited access. A local representation must therefore state its boundary and revise it when omitted consequences matter; the whole perspective does not grant omniscience.

Suppose production and the controller's future prospects remain unchanged while an affected life undergoes additional suffering. **The whole has changed:** the undergoing is itself an occurrence within it. Its existence does not depend on the controller's recognition or a return path to the controller. An aggregate showing no change has omitted a relevant difference. Conversely, genuine relief changes an aspect of that same unfolding. Calling it an aspect preserves the distinction between a real local good and an assessment of the complete arrangement; it does not infer that everything is good because one good occurs.

This resolves a representational error before evaluating anything: controller invariance is not whole invariance. The additional ethical proposal is that actual realization, impairment and repair of differentiated life can constitute qualities of the whole itself. They need not first become a private owner's reasons and then be transferred outward. Their ethical character nevertheless requires a substantive account; describing all effects accurately does not automatically rank them.

### Joint realization and repair as the candidate

Our candidate concerns **how generative activity realizes, obstructs and transforms differentiated lives and possibilities together, including the conditions through which further realization and needed repair can occur**. It evaluates the relation and its unfolding. It does not begin by adding independent private preferences, maximize activity, preserve every incumbent form, or make later production a condition of present enjoyment or relief.

Three structural requirements constrain this proposal without pretending to prove its value:

- **Retain affected consequences.** Absence of bargaining power, feedback or private return cannot make an occurrence disappear from an account of the whole.
- **Check joint realization.** Claimed continuations must be feasible together, with actual access, time, support and transition effects retained. Two separately successful branches may require the same unavailable condition.
- **Make needed repair real.** Where the defended good calls for correction, there must be a usable way for relevant consequences to change understanding and activity. A preserved formula or unused reform path is insufficient.

The [existing formal and philosophical resources](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-SCOPE.md#2-three-constructive-resources) already distinguish joint feasibility, actual installed revision and comparison without a universal sum. Their protected conditions and ethical grounds retain their premises. Reusing those results does not discharge the new constitutive grounding question.

A concrete comparison deepens the [already preserved no-return example](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md#test-the-whole-affected-continuation). Care enables later care after its initiator withdraws, but the arrangement displaces a serious burden onto another life. Those benefits and that burden are all actual. Suppose a feasible reorganization preserves the benefits and other warranted goods, including legitimate authority and means constraints, while removing the displaced burden without another countervailing loss. The defended judgment favors that changed configuration. Its advantage does not require any reward for the initiator or privileged observer.

This comparison is substantive ethical reasoning, not a theorem obtained from feasibility alone. If the goods cannot coexist, a genuine conflict remains inside the whole. It calls for examining the source of the incompatibility, possible changes in dependence and warranted priorities while preserving the losses. A scalar total or an appeal to the whole cannot settle that work by itself.

Stable regenerative domination and ecological replacement supply harder tests. A regime may accurately track harms, renew itself and suppress worthwhile developments; complete description and persistent reproduction then fail to establish its goodness. New life may also arise through irreversible endings, so goodness cannot require every current form to survive. These challenges address the candidate's own character and comparisons, rather than asking whether every change benefits its controller.

### The origin of a need and the value of its repair

A further test concerns **good-making activity → the goodness of the history that generates it**. Genuine care can continue through a harmful arrangement. Its beneficial episodes do not by themselves vindicate the arrangement producing the need for them. This is a direct whole-history question, independent of whether any particular helper gains.

Consider two feasible continuations from a common situation. One sustains an unburdened life. The other introduces an avoidable burden and then genuinely relieves it. Compare the actual lives, supporting conditions, timing, means and other consequences, including any learning or relationships gained along the way. In the restricted case where the detour supplies no countervailing good or justified requirement, the defended ethical comparison favors avoiding it. Equal relevant endpoints do not make the lived histories identical. This is a conceptual comparison under stated ethical grounds, not an empirical claim that every injury can be prevented without cost.

Two judgments can therefore hold together:

- **Once the burden exists**, real relief can improve the continuation compared with leaving it in place.
- **Before producing the burden**, introducing it and then relieving it can make the unfolding worse than preserving the unburdened continuation.

These judgments compare different alternatives. The later repair's benefit does not retrospectively justify creating its occasion. Conversely, a harmful origin does not erase the point of appropriate relief now. The whole perspective must preserve both, including any further responsibility for the harm and for changing the arrangement.

The distinction has an [existing causal and ethical genealogy](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-SCOPE.md#5-continuation-repair-comparisons-and-prior-scope). A previous material construction also supplies an exact operational comparison: from an actually reached intact site, damaging and repairing it restores the intact condition while consuming one common fuel unit. More completed repairs therefore need not mean more retained support. The restored site can instead support other activity without another repair. These are inherited results about a modeled site and resources, not evidence of experienced suffering or a new ethical theorem. The present extension concerns what such histories permit us to claim about generative goodness.

This yields a limited but definite constraint. Suppose a measure depends only on the number of genuine repairs and strictly increases with that number. One admissible burden-and-repair detour increases that measure even when it worsens the whole continuation on the defended comparison. Such a measure cannot represent that comparison. Repeated inflation requires genuinely repeatable conditions; the argument assumes no unlimited stock of free cycles. The [hand argument and review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#7-continuation-the-origin-of-a-need-and-the-value-of-its-repair) do not reject every quantitative assessment, or derive ethical badness from a numerical resource decrease.

The affirmative consequence for **good generating further good** is precise: trace whether activity sustains and develops fulfilled lives and their possibilities, including by reducing the need for repeated rescue. A succession with fewer repairs may be better because earlier care succeeded more deeply. Continuing benefit can consist in living, learning, resting or choosing freely without needing the original intervention again. Neither the number of helpful acts nor the survival of the helping institution is its ethical measure.

The comparison must change when the supposed detour actually constitutes a worthwhile achievement, enables otherwise unavailable learning, respects a refusal, or protects another life. Those relations require examination; calling them interference with a cleaner result would assume the conclusion. The [inherited contextual-value analysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PERMITTED-RELIEF-AND-DESERT-INTEGRATION.md#3-a-correction-to-the-binary-framing) also shows why a unified account need not first assign every isolated adverse episode an invariant moral value. It can assess transformations within their actual relations. That earlier insight and the existing history-sensitive representation are resources for this test, not discoveries of this continuation.

The revised candidate thus concerns **actual fulfillment and how its unfolding forms conditions for further fulfillment, with the burdens, dependencies and alternatives involved in producing those conditions retained**. The defective inference from beneficial episodes to beneficial generation is now identified. The positive ethical significance of fulfillment and appropriate repair remains substantively defended rather than deduced from generic causation. Whole quality, a particular responder's reasons and the formation of effective concern remain connected but distinct explanatory tasks. The full grounding goal stays active.

### When improvement transforms a valuable relation

The [realization continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md) tests the preceding comparison against a stronger relational objection. A response can be valuable partly because of the actual situation it answers. Removing that situation may transform or end this particular responsive good. The earlier comparison that removes an impairment without countervailing warranted loss remains sufficient under its premises. Relational change alone does not show that its no-loss condition fails. The question is whether ending or changing this response fulfills its point, sacrifices an independently warranted good, or does both.

Take a shared history in which an actual burden receives a genuine, authorized compassionate response. The compassionate response to this present burden may itself be valuable. If relief succeeds, the same response cannot keep occurring toward that same presently existing burden unchanged. The earlier care and what it accomplished remain in the history. Gratitude, ordinary participation, prevention or a different form of care may follow. These possibilities are transformations to identify; calling them care does not make all their relations identical.

This case does not show that losing any activity called care is harmless. It distinguishes keeping a response available in precisely its old form from preserving or fulfilling its value. If completion realizes the point of the response without another warranted loss, the existing no-loss comparison may apply. If an independently valuable relation is actually forfeited, the conflict must be acknowledged. A good response's value does not by itself warrant perpetuating or recreating its occasion. The [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md) uses Moore's distinction between responding well to existing suffering and defending suffering's existence, without adopting his arithmetic of value or his wider conclusions. The burden-origin and repair-count results immediately above are credited as a separate concurrent continuation.

Four relations need different explanations. A burden may have contributed historically to a retained understanding; that does not establish a present need to repeat it. It may currently enable a practice under one arrangement; a feasible changed arrangement may alter that dependency. It may partly constitute the very activity being valued; removing it then changes the object of comparison. Or it may be the condition a response is meant to repair, whose reduction changes that response's applicability. These roles can overlap in one history. None automatically transfers a favorable ethical judgment backward through the entire dependency.

A second test retains both the felt manner and the actual role of activity. Enjoyment in freely shared play and enjoyment dependent on another's imposed humiliation both contain enjoyment, while differing in their relations. A freely undertaken difficult activity and an imposed futile burden can both contain aversive undergoing, while differing in their purposes, authority and consequences. These are conceptual comparisons, not phenomenal-identification experiments. If the defended ethical assessments differ, felt quality or successful target attainment alone cannot determine them. A whole account must explain the relevant relations and their significance together.

The strongest rival can accept every lived occurrence while disputing an invariant ethical contribution from it: a challenge might partly constitute achievement, or a response might be fitting because of its relation to an existing wrong. Descriptive preservation of the experience does not answer that rival. It must identify the relation, the available alternatives and why its particular burden is warranted; our proposal must establish whether relieving that burden preserves the relation, transforms it beneficially, or forfeits a real good. Permission, meaning and other affected lives cannot be silently held fixed by the words “no warranted loss.”

The positive correction is **joint realization through accountable transformation**, with room for valuable activities to finish or change as their grounds change. Continuing goods may involve completion of an earlier undertaking and reduced need for its repair. This sharpens the ethical candidate's content by distinguishing continuation of an activity's form from fulfillment of its point. It does not establish that all such transformations involve an ethical loss or fall outside the earlier no-loss comparison. It does not yet establish the full ethical identity, an ordering of every conflicting continuation, or a new formal result. The [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-REVIEW.md) states the remaining constitutive and practical tests.

### When assistance preserves the activity

The [worked participation comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#8-continuation-6-october-2026-what-supported-participation-actually-preserves) gives the preceding question a concrete test. Consider an agreed duet whose point includes the musicians actually interpreting and responding to each other. Reducing intrusive noise can make that activity easier. An assistive control can also preserve it if the musicians' relevant timing and phrasing still shape the shared performance. A preset rendering can reproduce the sound while replacing that present exercise. These are hypothetical alternatives; the existence and effects of any real support require evidence.

Identify the activity before judging its preservation. Which choices actually enter the music, over what interpretive range, and how does the other participant take them up? Records of the operative route, actual listening and adjustment, and admissible changes in phrasing can distinguish participation from its appearance. A gesture that changes a decorative feature cannot establish control over an otherwise predetermined interpretation. Conversely, a backup or another sufficient contribution can mask a participant's effect without erasing their actual exercise. This applies the repository's existing mediation and participation analyses; it introduces no new universal causal criterion.

Assistance and replacement are relative to the role being examined. A device may support expressive choice while replacing one bodily technique. Live mixing can itself be skilled interpretation; a recording retains earlier creative contributions; shared listening can be valuable. None is automatically the same as present instrumental execution. If a specific unaided technique constitutes the agreed achievement, replacing it changes that achievement even when it enables worthwhile music. The account must retain both descriptions.

The comparison therefore separates **task demand, available capacity, felt effort and unwanted burden**. Mastery may make the same performance easier while preserving its relevant exercise. Extra interference may demand effort without contributing to the task. But an effort-based account can still defend exertion as an additional good, and a chosen challenge can make a particular demand part of the activity's point. The [primary comparisons and surviving rivals](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#6-continuation-difficulty-support-and-actual-participation) keep that disagreement explicit. Preserved skill and reduced strain do not alone prove that nothing valuable changed.

One harder case makes the limit concrete: the available aid preserves mutual phrasing and reduces strain but removes a timbre range the musicians value. There is no equally accessible alternative retaining that range in this session. The support preserves one good while restricting another. Its classification is now clearer; its overall appraisal still depends on the actual burden, expressive loss, legitimate choices and other consequences. Neither better sound nor lower effort settles that conflict.

The positive candidate is **actual shared expression as a present good**: differentiated contributions are heard, developed and answered within the same activity. Its proposed value concerns that realization, without requiring a later product or return to its initiator. This is a substantive ethical claim. Competence and consent do not make every shared activity good; a rival can also acknowledge the causal facts while locating welfare entirely in experience or satisfied attitudes. The worked test improves identification of the disputed relation, rather than settling that rival by definition.

For the foundation, this means that a claim to support worthwhile life must identify what the support enables people actually to do or undergo, including the relations through which they participate. Similar outputs can conceal changed realization; easier activity can preserve it. This is one application of joint realization, not a demand that every good involve active participation in shaping activity. Rest, receptive enjoyment and completion retain their place. The remaining grounding question concerns why and when the identified participation has ethical significance, including its weight when genuine goods conflict. No new formal or empirical result is claimed.

### When formed understanding can correct its source

The whole account needs a positive connection from good realized in a shared activity to the reasons governing its continuation. **Understanding formed through guidance can require correction of a particular aim before it removes the need for guidance generally.** This gives differentiated development an actual role in what the process becomes, without making its initiating position the permanent owner of its warrant.

Consider a mathematical apprenticeship whose independently defended point is truthful inquiry. A mentor proposes establishing a conjecture; the learner develops a verified counterexample within the same domain and interpretation. For a concrete arithmetic illustration, the conjecture that `n² + n + 41` is prime for every natural `n` fails at `n = 40`, where its value is `1681 = 41 × 41`. This is an elementary illustration, not a new mathematical result or an observed teaching case.

The learner can still need extensive guidance about generalization, proof, resources and communication. Nevertheless, the valid correction changes what this practice can responsibly seek to establish. The mentor's greater competence elsewhere and the learner's dependent role do not restore the defeated conjecture. The mentor may retain it as an explicitly false conjecture for an exercise; that changes the purpose rather than vindicating the original truth claim.

The positive relation is that the shared activity has formed understanding whose exercise can now correct that activity. Where guidance was justified by enabling such understanding, excluding its valid consequence solely to protect the initial aim frustrates the very good offered as the guidance's ground. A practice can instead realize that good through acknowledgment, reassessment and a changed inquiry while retaining other justified guidance. The reason comes from the actual relationship between understanding, its object and the shared undertaking; no private return to either participant is required.

Several distinctions make this a bounded argument. Being correct here does not confer general expertise, unilateral decision power, public credit or permission to use every implementation method. Institutional procedures can govern verification and subsequent action; other people and obligations remain relevant. A rival defending hierarchy or disciplined performance on another ground must state and justify it. The present argument defeats a role-based dismissal under the stated truth-oriented ground, rather than every possible account of authority.

The [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md#8-continuation-formed-understanding-can-correct-its-source), [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-REVIEW.md#7-continuation-correction-before-general-independence) and [genealogy](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-SCOPE.md#6-continuation-formed-correction-and-its-remaining-ground) credit the existing content-consistency and successful-support results. The extension concerns locally warranted influence arising before general independence. It explains a practical consequence of a defended relational good; it does not derive that good's ethical worth from arithmetic or generic formation. Ethical applications must independently establish the alleged good, the correction and the scope of authority. In contested cases, calling a new purpose enlightened would not establish any of these.

### Actual participation and lived concern

The [further value inquiry](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#9-continuation-6-october-2026-actual-engagement-and-the-grounds-of-its-value) narrows the inference available from the musical comparison. **Dependence on what actually happens does not establish value independent of every participant's concern.** Someone can care about genuinely making music together. An account based on fulfillment of that world-directed concern can distinguish actual collaboration from a convincing substitute, even when relevant experiences match. The previous comparison therefore does not uniquely support a separate intrinsic value of participation.

The affirmative candidate can have joint constituents: **lived concern for a relationship and the relationship's actual realization can together constitute a present good**. In the duet, participants' contributions genuinely receive and shape one another's responses. Their good is proposed to include actually living that valued engagement. It need not be divided into one benefit for desire satisfaction and another for participation. Concern, capacities and encounter form within the same unfolding; no outside evaluator is needed simply to make them related. This applies the [earlier relational account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#44-conscious-stakes-and-actual-relations), rather than introducing a newly proved foundation.

An informed concern for genuine collaboration is different from knowing that this particular episode realizes it. A person can understand what they care about while being mistaken about its occurrence. The proposed good therefore depends on more than a feeling or report of fulfillment. Several desire-based or hybrid accounts can share this structure. A purely experiential account can still accept all the descriptive differences and deny an additional welfare difference; identifying the real relation does not defeat that rival by itself. The [source comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-SOURCES.md#7-continuation-actual-participation-and-attitude-sensitive-value) records the existing genealogy and the limited primary recheck.

A stronger claim would make actual mutual participation beneficial even when nobody has an intrinsic concern for that participation. Testing it requires a different comparison. The proposed routing case preserves intentional execution, relevant experience, desire fulfillment, commitments and future effects while varying present mutual influence. Participants authorize either route and would remain indifferent after receiving accurate descriptive information. Those are demanding conditions to establish, not a completed experiment or an assumed ability to engineer perfect duplicates. If intrinsic concern is necessary for the activity under examination, removing it changes the target; that comparison cannot refute the narrower joint-constituent account. Even a coherent contrast would still need a defended welfare judgment.

This separates five claims: an activity occurs; it benefits a participant; it has impersonal value; there is a reason to preserve it; and a particular intervention is permissible. Admiration of a performance cannot establish all five. Nor does the good of a shared activity, by itself, authorize forcing participation on someone who wants rest or a different form of engagement.

The present result is a scoped reconciliation and inference audit. An independent welfare contribution from participation remains unestablished, and the joint-realization candidate does not require that stronger claim. Its ethical grounding remains substantive: a concern can be formed and fulfilled within cruelty or manipulation, and a local benefit does not settle the whole arrangement's quality. We must assess the content, formation and consequences of concerns alongside the lives they affect. This case does not make reflective endorsement a condition of every good. Rest, relief, receptive enjoyment and completion retain their distinct grounds. No new formal or empirical result is claimed.

### Significance through potential future goods

[Ren proposes](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-significance-as-future-goods-integral.md) constructing significance as the integral of potential future goods through the end of time. The [developed candidate](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md) concerns how a present formation changes the whole affected continuation. Its reach need not return to an initiating subject.

For specified feasible alternatives a and b following actual history h, let their causally supported distributions of continuations be μ_a and μ_b. Where a scalar history assessment G_T and finite expectations are justified, comparative significance is

\[
\Delta_T(a,b\mid h)=\mathbb E_{\mu_a}[G_T(H)]-\mathbb E_{\mu_b}[G_T(H)].
\]

If temporal addition is also warranted, G_T can be represented by an integral of realized contributions, with any separately needed terminal contribution. The integrand concerns actual fulfillment, burdens, relations and their formation; it is not an already accumulated future-value score counted again. The whole-history formulation does not require summing independent private utilities. Nonadditive goods or unresolved comparisons can instead require a richer functional or partial ordering.

Potential continuations must be jointly feasible within each history. Mutually exclusive histories receive justified probabilities rather than being counted as jointly realized; where probabilities are unsupported, retain the alternatives and uncertainty. More possible branches, more repairs or longer persistence do not alone establish more good. Present enjoyment and terminal relief retain value without requiring another productive sequel.

“To the end of time” requires a defined finite horizon or a justified limiting comparison. Even divergent total values can have convergent finite-horizon differences; other differences oscillate indefinitely. Discounting is an additional modeling and ethical choice, not an automatic implication of concern for the whole future.

The elementary recursion test identifies the grounding limit. With a Markov expectation operator P, bounded V and 0≤γ<1, V=γPV admits only V=0. Adding a bounded realized contribution g yields the familiar continuation sum; at γ=1, constants alone already prevent V=PV from fixing a scale or sign. These are scoped mathematical facts, not an impossibility proof for every immanent value theory. Actual good can be constituted within the unfolding; the integral then expresses its generative reach. Establishing that constitutive good remains necessary to the stronger foundation.

### Future fields and their causal organization

[Ren's field proposal](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-future-field-and-causal-generativity.md) extends the future-good account: activity changes both its outcomes and the conditions generating later possibilities. The present is a realized continuation of the past and a condition of the next field. Selves, evaluators and response capacities differentiate within this unfolding. An ordinary probability representation is sufficient here; literal quantum superposition is not established.

For two feasible arrangements, their difference F_{a:b}=Φ_a−Φ_b describes a causal redistribution of possible whole continuations on a common history space. This is a precise provisional meaning of the force of the future, rather than a mechanical force or a future entity pulling backward. Costs, means and changed relationships belong in the histories. A changed forecast alone does not establish a changed causal arrangement.

The [conditional result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#future-fields-and-changes-in-causal-organization) shows that transferring probability ε from a defensibly worse history H_- to a better one H_+, with other probabilities unchanged, changes every compatible finite numerical appraisal by ε[v(H_+)−v(H_-)]>0. No unique cardinal measure is selected. The better–worse judgment and the ethical principle favoring this probability improvement remain substantive; permission and universal necessity do not follow from the identity.

The [next-question exploration](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#can-field-organization-explain-better-and-worse) rejects probability diversity as a sufficient ethical measure: equal-entropy distributions can assign very different chances to the same better and worse histories. It also shows how equal usual outcomes can conceal different actual capacities to pause or revise an activity. The field must retain those response relations and their formation, not merely counts or probabilities of endpoints.

The positive candidate concerns the ethical quality of actual realization together with its organization of continuation, correction and completion. Actual relations can constitute capacities without an additional organizational benefit being counted. Their ethical role still depends on what those capacities realize, preserve or obstruct; more control and more possibilities are not universally favorable. Self-care and terminal fulfillment need no productive sequel. Explaining that ethical character remains the next grounding task, within the whole account.

### Fair chances can be part of what the whole realizes

The [fair-chance continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#fair-chances-as-a-quality-of-the-organized-future) gives the field proposal a positive constitutive candidate. Within a legitimate undertaking, two equally situated claimants may receive an effective impartial chance at one indivisible opportunity. The proposed good concerns their actual relation to its selection, even though only one receives it. A chance is not the benefit itself, and losing does not erase other grounds for concern.

Under a defended requirement of neutral treatment between the equal roles, their selection probabilities must be equal. This mathematical implication does not establish the ethical requirement. The account locates that requirement in the formed relationship without making membership or contribution prerequisites for all moral standing.

An endpoint appraisal assigning the same value to either recipient cannot distinguish the fair procedure from a direct allocation. The whole history can retain how access was actually organized. A false appearance of randomness or a draw that will not be honored does not establish that relation. No separate additive fairness quantity or duplication of the eventual benefit is required.

The positive conclusion is that **how futures are made available can partly constitute a relational good**. A serious rival can accept the same causal structure while regarding any permissible tie-break as sufficient. The fair-chance argument is defended at the specified scope, not proved universally necessary. The next grounding question concerns why those forms of standing and treatment are warranted within the whole, including outside prior undertakings. Entropy, equal probabilities and successful implementation alone do not answer it.

### Future fields and the formation of particular claims

The [selection-and-delivery comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#a-future-field-can-generate-differentiated-claims) gives the field account a history-sensitive continuation. Equal standing need not require equal chances after every event. Within a warranted procedure that settles an allocation, both participants initially have a claim to the specified fair selection. Its actual result can establish a particular winner's claim to delivery. The other participant's basic standing and continuing need remain; a new independent opportunity can still give a reason to help.

The finite comparison holds initial recipient probabilities fixed at 1/2 under honoring the draw, reversing it and independently redrawing. Honoring and reversal also have the same joint entropy and mutual information between the public signal and delivery. Those summaries therefore cannot identify performance of the actual undertaking. A sufficiently described whole-history field can retain it. A different rule genuinely established beforehand can change what the same numerical signal-to-delivery mapping performs; this changes an actual relation, not merely its ethical label.

The positive conclusion is conditional: **activity can form differentiated practical claims within an initially equal relationship, and fulfilling those claims can rightly close possibilities.** The procedure's ethical warrant supplies the force of the claim; causal implementation alone does not. A rival preserving the same recipient distribution can dispute whether the particular undertaking has that authority. The result explains the claim's changing addressee once the settlement warrant is defended; it does not derive initial equal standing or certify every adopted rule.

This develops the proposal that good concerns the whole unfolding and the futures it organizes. Equal probabilities, more openness and stronger statistical dependence are insufficient ethical criteria. The next bridge concerns why a formed relationship warrants its particular continuation or revision, with self-good and concern outside prior undertakings retained.

### Whole appraisal and temporal contributions

The integral need not begin with independently assigned ethical values at each instant. The [whole-first construction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#7-whole-appraisal-can-precede-its-temporal-expression) starts with a warranted scalar appraisal F of each history so far. Define the next contribution by d_n=F(h_{n+1})−F(h_n). Then

\[
F(h_N)=F(h_0)+\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}d_n.
\]

These contributions depend on the actual developing relationship. They need not be context-free ethical atoms. The construction represents F; it does not establish why F is warranted.

Different temporal and terminal terms can also preserve the same completed-history appraisal. The elementary telescoping identity makes that freedom precise. It changes how a total is expressed, without changing the history or making its lived adversity arbitrary. A term explicitly identified with goodness realized at that time has a stronger interpretation that an arbitrary redistribution may fail to preserve.

A terminal expression must retain whatever history the appraisal needs. It can distinguish an unburdened continuation from introduced adversity followed by repair even when selected endpoint capacities match. An endpoint-only summary that erases the interval cannot substitute for it. At an infinite horizon, a remaining boundary term cannot be discarded without the required convergence conditions.

Finally, temporal contribution and practical significance are different comparisons. Relief may prevent further adversity without adding a positive rate afterward; it can still improve on continued suffering. New information may raise an expected-value estimate without producing the benefit it reports. The whole account must explain these relations, rather than identifying them through the same symbol.

This strengthens the nondual construction: ethical identification may concern the whole relation, with its temporal expression subsequently derived. Neither the integral nor the earlier bounded-recursion argument requires an independently existing local reward to be the foundation. The substantive warrant of the whole appraisal remains unresolved.

### Relative importance within mutual production

The [production continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-GOOD-INTEGRAL.md#9-mutual-production-can-determine-relative-importance) gives a positive result beyond the bounded Markov-recursion test. Under an independently justified finite irreducible production law K with positive spectral radius, relative contribution weights can satisfy Kv=ρ(K)v, with v>0.

The production relations determine the positive ray of weights. No separately assigned reward for each type is required. With β=1/ρ(K), this becomes v=βKv. Unlike a probability transition, production can enable several successors together. Its linearity, actual resource support and jointly feasible outputs remain substantive conditions.

This derives contribution to the represented continuation. It does not yet identify favorable ethical value. Mutually supporting activities can maintain a harmful arrangement; their importance can give reasons to transform it. A purely successor-based measure also assigns zero to a terminal good without productive descendants. Extending the matrix to such terminal types leaves the irreducible case and exposes that limit. The existing good of final relief or enjoyment therefore needs a history appraisal or another warranted realization term. Such content can belong to the whole relationship without becoming an independent local ethical atom.

Nor is the recursive weight already an integral: at the balancing normalization, every βⁿKⁿv equals v, so their sum grows without bound. A contribution weight, a stream of realized goods and a whole-history appraisal retain different meanings.

The advance is that relations can jointly determine nonzero relative importance. The remaining transition is from the specified process and its actual realizations to their ethical quality, including when continuation should change or end. The result limits this reproductive representation, without refuting a richer generative account or establishing an external ethical principle.

The question is whether that history changes the good realized by relief or its moral importance. Both participants retain their proposed relief-ground, while scarcity leaves a conflict outside the earlier no-countervailing-loss test. Unresolved priority proves neither a tie nor permission to use a lottery. A completed episode does not make the continuing life unalterable, but giving its next improvement additional priority requires a defended history-sensitive ground. This is a supporting extension of G, not a prerequisite for the minimum that both rivals already grant in this comparison. The governing next derivation below retains the whole-process question.

Its proposed adaptive history is not established. It blocks a shortcut from the existence of judgments to their truth; it does not independently defeat every direct evaluative warrant. The specific selected content and any alleged unreliability require explanation.

The account provisionally retains C1 while testing genuine contextual error. Fitting grief or remorse is a stronger challenge than protective success alone: does the painful manner constitute a good that eliminates its local adversity, or does that good coexist with adversity? Neither fittingness nor unpleasantness decides this by itself. This remains a local comparison. [Ren's subsequent correction](#the-future-of-the-whole-is-the-criterion) redirects the governing investigation toward the future of the whole; this affective question is not its mandatory first ground. C1's necessity, its full generative constitution and C2's practical reach remain unproved.

### The next derivation

Following [the future-of-the-whole correction](#the-future-of-the-whole-is-the-criterion), the governing connection is **generative unfolding → changed conditions and realizations of its possible continuations → ethical quality of the whole future → responsive transformation within that same activity**. The earlier differentiated-undergoing route remains a supporting account. Particular subjects, objects, practical reasons and evaluators must be explained within the unfolding. The target includes the formation and correction of the standards they use, not merely more effective service of an inherited private end.

A whole-order account that recognizes actual needs, learning and warranted revision may be an instance of this broad candidate, rather than its refutation. Disagreement about the priority of participation or legitimate roles does not by itself defeat every universal ethical claim. Distinguish actual occurrence, ethical significance, comparative priority and practical responsibility without treating them as independent ontological starting points. The next grounding test must identify a particular realized relation whose ethical significance is disputed and defend its significance within the actual whole relation, without requiring a complete ranking of every case.

An account that recognizes an adverse ethical aspect but gives another good priority already grants that aspect's significance. A rival to this significance must contest the proposed constitutive connection within its specified context, rather than merely its comparative weight. Describing an experience as unwanted does not by itself settle that dispute; nor may we assume away the rival by calling every proposed restriction unwarranted.

The grounds of a particular inquiry can require revising its aims or methods as those grounds change. This does not by itself establish that every revisable activity or newly formed purpose is good. The [scope review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-GROUNDING-INTEGRATION.md#continuation-a-common-candidate-is-not-yet-a-discriminating-ground) found no new necessity derivation here. It clarifies the target; it does not replace the remaining ethical explanation with a procedural requirement.

The established correction is that a local beneficiary's advantage or insulation cannot serve as the whole's ethical standard. The proposed positive ground is joint realization and repair as a quality of the unfolding. Its necessity and conflict-resolving reach remain to be defended through actual constitutive comparisons; they are not supplied by the word “whole.” This is an unresolved explanation within the reconstructed theory, not evidence for an ethical realm outside generation. The new goal remains active. The completed conditional foundation retains its scope, and no new theorem, empirical result or universal favorable-direction claim is made in this round.

## Goodness beyond return to an individual

[Ren corrects the latest investigation](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-goodness-without-return-to-self.md): **good does not need to come back to the self; its realization and the further good it enables are what matter.** The assistant had narrowed the next test to the initiating subgroup's future dependence on those it excluded. That is a possible practical connection, not the foundation's criterion of goodness or its governing route.

The broader [relational-goodness account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md) already supplies the intended direction: **activity can realize good for differentiated lives and form conditions through which further good can arise, across lives and generations, whether or not its initiator benefits.** Selves and purposes form within that unfolding. There is no need to route significance through a primitive owner or enlarge one individual's interest until it represents everybody. Present relief and other lived goods also need no subsequent product to matter.

**善在其实际成全的生命与可能性，以及如何使后续之善成为可能；不以回报发起者为根据。果为后因，善的延续可以越过个体，也可以通过改变原有做法而继续。** This formulation is AI-authored, following Ren's correction and the existing genealogy.

The [new integration](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md) distinguishes a transmission path from a return path. After a usable handoff, later participants may adapt retained knowledge to a changed need while the initiator no longer acts. Compare that continuation with persistence of an unchanged formula that fails the later need. The relevant assessment concerns actual later benefits, the conditions carrying them and affected burdens. The initiator's reward, reputation or continued control is not the measure.

The [checked continuation interface](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GOODNESS-WITHOUT-RETURN-INTEGRATION.md#which-inherited-effects-remain-operative) now distinguishes a dormant description of the donor from inherited effects that still guide the successor. Under the unchanged succession law, replacing only that historical field preserves available finite continuations, resource use and live outcomes; effective knowledge, access and support remain. This is a precise abstraction within the existing history, not physical removal of its initiator or an identification of operational equivalence with ethical equality.

This also changes the challenge to the foundation. The absence of private advantage is not a refutation of generative goodness. A substantive challenge can instead grant actual good and its onward effects while identifying a displaced burden or an ineffective transmission. A beneficial path does not by itself vindicate every branch or means. These are differentiated consequences within the same unfolding, not values assigned by an outside subject.

The shared-stakes argument below remains one conditional application of this broader account. Its exposure and capture tests do not determine the standing of all affected lives. The next governing investigation follows actual good into the conditions of further good without requiring a return to its originator. This restores the intended foundation; it does not announce inevitable moral progress or a new axiom-free proof of universal ethical necessity.

## Forming those who will live in the future

Generation can change who comes to exist. The [new comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) therefore examines two feasible continuations that would form different future subjects, each with a worthwhile life. One includes an independently identified severe burden; the other supports comparable worthwhile possibilities without it. Existing lives, legitimate claims, means and risks remain in view. Neither future subject is treated as presently experiencing, and nonexistence receives no welfare score.

The positive candidate is that foreseeable adversity can count against choosing the formation that would produce it, even though that formation also enables its bearer's worthwhile existence. The alternative need not make that same individual better off. Origin can ground a responsibility concerning formation without making the resulting good a repayment to its source. This is a defended ethical consideration, not a deduction from causal involvement.

Identify who each possible history would form and what they would undergo before comparing those histories. Actual goods can arise in new lives; the beneficiary need not be fixed independently of the activity. The [primary comparisons](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-SOURCES.md) preserve the difference between prospective good, a reason favoring an option and a requirement to choose it.

The comparison leaves creation versus noncreation, population totals and replacement of existing people unsettled. A rival can accept generated selves while requiring same-person worsening; its restriction needs substantive adjudication. Particular fixed-patient service proofs omit this formation relation, while generic action comparisons can represent it under supplied grounds. Neither formal compatibility nor this limited argument completes universal ethical necessity.

### Prospective concern without prior responsibility

The [next comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md#7-prospective-concern-at-the-last-effective-window) removes prior participation in formation as well. An independently proceeding process would form A's future life. An uninvolved participant can redirect it toward a different worthwhile life, B, before either bearer exists and before effective intervention becomes unavailable. The specified severe burden in A is absent from B; other goods, existing claims, means and risks require independent assessment.

The proposed reason concerns the lived conditions this activity can enable, without return to its source. Taking part would create a formative role, but that subsequent responsibility cannot alone supply the reason to enter it. Nor does urgency establish ethical force. A rival can accept prospective reasons for initiators and assistance to existing strangers yet deny this combined extension. The unresolved question is why warranted future good and adversity favor an eligible nonoriginator's present action. Neither a creation duty nor a complete ethical ordering has been established.

The [stronger comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md#8-conditional-concern-and-selection-across-future-lives) adds R, which protects the same future person who would arise under A. A rival can favor R while denying B: it then accepts uninvolved prevention before the beneficiary exists, while disputing selection across different lives. Present nonexistence is not its decisive boundary. Making B available also need not erase the unchanged A/R ground merely because their beneficiary no longer exists under every option.

The positive selection argument must assess both protective grounds and constraints. A rival can deny a reason for B over A without preferring A, opposing B or asserting a future person's right to exist. Ending an existing life, destroying a relationship or evading an obligation supplies real objections; showing these absent does not itself establish favor for B. The positive candidate concerns what the changed activity would realize for future lives.

### Protection when its consequences change who will exist

The [worked continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md#9-protective-activity-when-formation-changes-identity) compares removing an environmental source of severe adversity with and without a further effect on formative timing. When identity remains independent, protection can help whoever forms through separate same-person comparisons. When the timing effect changes identity, the claim that the same person is made better off across the alternatives must be withdrawn. The intervention can nevertheless retain its positive point: worthwhile life develops without the identified adverse condition. An ineffective change with the same timing effect lacks that protective contribution.

This is the defended prospective ground; it requires no return to its source and does not assign another possible person's benefit to the person who actually forms. Calling the identity consequence incidental does not settle its significance. An independently warranted undertaking protecting an existing family's access can constrain the chosen method, even where no future person has a claim to be created. The [primary comparisons](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-SOURCES.md#future-standpoints-kumar-and-martin) preserve generic description, actual claims and unknown versus changed identity. Universal participant scope, duties and a complete ordering remain further claims.

The [prospective-significance connection](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FUTURE-BEARER-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md#connection-to-prospective-significance) places those possible lives within the whole histories assessed by [G_T](#significance-through-potential-future-goods). It needs neither a fixed beneficiary across alternatives nor welfare assigned to nonexistence. Temporal terms may express a whole appraisal without serving as independently given ethical atoms. A protective contribution does not by itself establish a positive difference for the complete history, and choosing a favorable G_T cannot prove the judgment encoded in that choice. The ethical comparison and its practical scope remain to justify within the unfolding.


## Others' wellbeing and the world in which we continue

[Ren's correction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-shared-world-grounding.md) opens a substantial route beyond the preceding direct-relief test: **others' wellbeing can sustain and constitute the world through which my present activity and future possibilities develop. Existing reasons concerning that world can therefore already support concern beyond my present personal boundaries.** The prior stranger-relief bridge is one ethical route, not the mandatory gateway for every unified grounding.

Begin with activity and its shared conditions. A situated life forms through relationships, language, practices, ecological dependencies and other beings' activity. In particular cases, another's health, understanding, security or ability to correct mistakes can change what the continuing relation makes possible. These are proposed dependencies to identify, not consequences proved by putting everyone inside one system boundary. “My world” names the world I participate in; it is neither my possession nor everything reduced to my experience.

Two forms of dependence matter. In instrumental dependence, others' wellbeing sustains valued conditions such as reliable cooperation or available care. In constitutive dependence, some goods partly consist in others' participation and condition: the quality of a worthwhile friendship, for example, concerns how both lives actually fare. Others' wellbeing can also affect how my capacities and purposes develop. The future person is formed within the continuing relations, rather than remaining a finished beneficiary receiving later returns. Formation alone does not justify a practice or a purpose; harmful dependencies must remain recognizable.

The [argument and review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SHARED-WORLD-GROUNDING.md) support the following conditional inference:

1. There are already warranted reasons concerning my continuing life, relationships or shared world.
2. Others' wellbeing actually sustains, constitutes or materially affects the valued conditions at issue.
3. An available response credibly supports those conditions through that wellbeing, with costs, authority and competing consequences assessed.
4. Those existing stakes can supply a practical reason for the response.

This conclusion needs no separate prior adoption of universal altruism. It uses existing practical grounds and warranted reasoning about their realization or support. That practical inference remains explicit: we have not derived all normativity from causation. Mere causal contact also supplies no favorable sign. The argument identifies what changes, in which direction, for which continuing possibilities.

Actual uncertainty about future participation can broaden the ground. I may later depend on care, knowledge, cooperation or institutions supplied by people I cannot now identify. Where access, resilience and future exposure are real, supporting broadly available conditions can be justified before a particular exchange or personal benefit is predictable. Uncertainty does not imply equal chances of becoming anyone, universal dependence on every individual, or an obligation to act on any remote possibility. Evidence, magnitude, alternatives and foreseeable burdens matter.

This gives **果为后因** a more direct practical role. Activity changes other participants and shared conditions; those changes can shape the world in which my own further activity becomes possible. Under the stated beneficial dependencies, concern for others and concern for one's future world are already related. Relief remains a real experiential change within that world, but the grounding need not begin or end with relief alone.

The [shared-selection analysis](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#1-shared-dependence-and-the-selection-of-its-maintenance) preserves an important distinction: common conditions may be indispensable while their maintenance brings no relative selection advantage. A reason to protect a common condition, motivation to do so and selection of the protective practice are different claims. Conversely, the [older isolated-harm model](FORMATION.md#standing-and-influence-have-different-grounds) explicitly supplies no feedback from the patient's loss. It limits inference from mere production and renewal; it does not refute the positive dependence proposed here.

Three tests set the new route's reach. Insulation can leave some harms outside the actor's relevant exposure. A connected actor can suffer some shared damage while capturing a larger private gain from an exploitative arrangement. Another's competence can support that arrangement while their freedom threatens its control. These cases respectively challenge coverage, decisive priority and identification of wellbeing with usefulness; they do not erase a genuine contributory reason where the positive dependency exists. Conflicting uses of shared conditions also require justified comparison.

The constructive result is **a shared-world ground of concern through already warranted stakes and identified dependencies**, including the formation of future capacities and relations. For this subsidiary route, the next investigation should identify those dependencies, their horizons, conflicts and transformations. Universal concern for every affected life remains a stronger question. The account need not settle the earlier direct-relief argument before advancing this route, and should not silently restrict a being's standing to its usefulness to an observer. No new empirical result or inevitable favorable-direction theorem is claimed.

### Shared stakes, actual contribution and usable correction

[Ren's instruction to continue](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-shared-world-continuation.md) advances the shared-world route in two connected ways: from valuing a supportive world to identifying an effective contribution, and from preserving others' present usefulness to protecting their capacity to correct our own activity. The [argument and review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SHARED-WORLD-GROUNDING.md#continuation-broad-conditions-and-real-contribution) keep both in the same continuing relations.

Future participation can depend on capacities whose eventual providers, users and failure points are not now identifiable. Where that uncertainty is real, preserving broadly usable conditions can protect warranted possibilities without naming the eventual beneficiary. The relevant good can include the ability to discover needs and revise purposes, rather than fulfilling a fixed list of present preferences. This is conditional on actual exposure, worthwhile possibilities and usable access; merely imaginable futures do not all carry equal practical weight.

A precise sufficient comparison is available. Suppose one arrangement preserves every independently worthwhile continuation supported by another, also preserves additional credibly relevant continuations, and introduces no countervailing difference in cost, authority, protection or other warranted concerns. Those additional possibilities give a reason to favor the first arrangement. Its breadth does not establish the comparison: the actual support and absence of offsetting losses must be shown. This is a limited practical dominance argument under stated value premises, not a new causal theorem, a demand to maximize options, or a rule treating all futures as equally probable. Where the arrangements trade off genuine concerns, it does not decide between them.

But a reason for a supportive arrangement to exist does not establish a reason for every proposed contribution. Assess the actual action against its available alternatives. It might maintain a necessary component, improve reliability, have a credible chance of completing a response, or help form a justified and effective joint provision. If isolated action accomplishes nothing but coordination can succeed, an invitation, agreement or preparation may be the relevant first contribution. Participation in a jointly sufficient scheme can matter even when a simplistic single-action removal test misses its role; that participation and the scheme's legitimacy must be established rather than assumed.

Conversely, if a proposed act changes neither support, reliability, coordination nor any constitutive good, the common-world argument alone has not justified that act. An obligation to contribute a fair share may have another ground; merely receiving a benefit does not establish its full content. Comparing a world where everyone contributes with one where nobody does is not yet a comparison of this person's live options. Shared dependence therefore calls for identifying contributions and their conditions rather than installing collective action by definition.

The second advance concerns independent correction. Where valued future activity depends on information and capabilities distributed among others, preserving their relevant capacity to investigate, report failures and challenge a practice can have practical support through one's own warranted stakes. Such correction must be receivable and able to affect what happens. It may need to change the organizer's purpose, not only improve execution of that purpose. Present obedience and competence can coexist with the destruction of this future capacity.

Consider two conceptual arrangements producing the same useful output now. One filters out evidence that challenges its controlling purpose. The other preserves competent inquiry and a usable route for warranted challenge to change that purpose. Where the second preserves warranted future possibilities that the first forecloses, without defeating the relevant safeguards, concern for the shared world favors that corrective capacity. This is a conditional comparison, not an empirical claim that all dissent helps or every restriction harms. Functional independence relevant to correction does not by itself establish unrestricted authority, equal standing or every dimension of personal autonomy.

The existing [selection account](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md#3-selection-over-the-network-of-correctors) identifies supporting mechanisms: actual independence depends on access and support; competence can erode despite improved current performance; nominal alternatives may lack a route into use. Its distinct conditional laws are not one newly demonstrated history. The older [consequence-selection result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/CONSEQUENCE-RETENTION-SELECTION.md) also separates useful contribution from sufficient support for its producer to continue contributing. These mechanisms constrain how justified concern can become effective; they do not supply its ethical validity.

The resulting principle is: **where warranted continuation depends on broadly usable and revisable shared capacities, an effective, legitimate contribution to those capacities can be justified before a particular future return is known.** This includes protecting capacities through which others can change what we recognize as worth pursuing. Free riding tests the contribution relation; insulation tests exposure; captured gains and incompatible futures test priority and the content of the valued continuation. For this subsidiary route, the next connection is to identify and compare actual contributions and corrective capacities, with their support, permissions and effects preserved. Universal contribution, equal burdens and inevitable cooperation remain unproved.

## The unified account

Following [Ren's request to finish the unified grounding](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-finish-unified-grounding.md), this section brings the account into one statement. Its central thesis is:

> **Ethics concerns generative activity becoming knowingly responsive to the actual good and harm it can make for differentiated lives, including transforming the conditions from which further response becomes possible.**

This is our defended philosophical candidate. The [five-condition assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FIXED-FINISH-LINE-AUDIT.md#15-completion-assessment-of-the-conditional-candidate) already accepted the connected foundation at its conditional scope. The present synthesis closes this exposition and evaluates its stronger ethical claim; it does not relabel that claim as a proved consequence of the minimal root. [Argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GOODNESS-CLOSURE-ARGUMENT.md), [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GOODNESS-CLOSURE-REVIEW.md) and [scope map](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GOODNESS-CLOSURE-SCOPE.md) distinguish these achievements.

### One unfolding, with real differentiation

Activity forms and transforms the conditions of subsequent activity. Relatively enduring organizations, their boundaries and their dependencies can develop within that history. Some effects participate in subsequent regulation; some organizations acquire distinctions, revise responses and change how they learn. These developments require identifiable conditions and mechanisms. They are neither guaranteed stages of every process nor separate substances assembled by an outside designer.

Acting, undergoing, knowing and evaluating describe differentiated relations within this unfolding. Their boundaries need not coincide: one life can undergo what several others can help change; a temporary coordination can act without becoming a single experiencer. Subject/object distinctions can develop through situated access and action without establishing fundamentally independent worlds. Their differentiation preserves real differences in experience, control, consent and consequence. The common account must explain destructive formations alongside caring ones.

The explanatory connections have different warrants:

| Connection | Content and present warrant |
|---|---|
| Activity → organization and operational significance | Conditions can enable or threaten an organization; consequence-dependent regulation changes what follows. Existing conditional constructions identify these relations under explicit laws. Operational significance alone does not identify conscious suffering or ethical value. |
| Continuing effects → learning and revision of selectors | Acquired distinctions can change later action and the means of selecting it. Accepted mechanisms include formation, retention, correction and changed learning routes; misleading evidence and retained error remain possible. |
| Differentiated experience and possibilities → good and harm | Actual suffering, relief, agency, enjoyment and damaged or restored possibilities are the ethical subject matter. Their identification requires substantive interpretation and evidence; a resource score or a report alone cannot certify them. A universal realization account is not supplied. |
| Good and a present response relation → practical reason | The constitutive proposal below identifies what a response can genuinely realize as a consideration favoring it before private adoption. Its universal sufficiency is the disputed ethical claim. |
| Acquired understanding and concern → effective, revisable action | The completed conditional foundation includes acquired evaluative mediation, independently warranted role-bound repair, compatible action and generic correction. It does not claim that conditioning creates ethical validity. |
| Response and its consequences → wisdom and further formation | Bounded wisdom connects warranted grounds, evidence, feasible response and correction. The wider proposal includes revising purposes, methods and institutions through their effects. Neither universal wisdom nor inevitable moral improvement follows. |

These are analytical distinctions accountable to their connections. A local model's supplied law remains an explanatory input, not a law outside generative reality. Accepted interfaces remain completed at their own scope; the table does not assert that every listed realization already forms one physical history.

### Goodness, reason and concern

Our [formulation of 善](THEORY.md#10-shared-generativity-and-善) concerns supporting, repairing and enabling affected beings' worthwhile possibilities, including their effective understanding and capacity to contest and change their conditions. It includes lived goods that need no further product: relief, rest, enjoyment and completion. Formation, persistence or increased complexity alone does not establish goodness.

The narrow ethical claim can now be stated precisely. Consider actual severe, still-unwanted experiential adversity; an available response credibly capable of relieving that same being; and means and a choice context independently assessed as permissible and without a justified exclusion of this ground. The candidate holds that **the prospective relief gives the responder a contributory moral reason before any prior bond, helping purpose or expected private return**. These conditions describe the intended application; they do not include acceptance of the conclusion as a condition of eligibility. Their realizations and restrictions require their own warrants.

The positive argument begins with what gives first care its point. A response that genuinely relieves this burden and an equally gratifying performance that changes nothing for the sufferer differ in a way relevant to choosing between them. We defend the appraisal that the actual relief can justify beginning care. The same affected life then explains why concern should change its means when they fail and stop pursuing an already fulfilled intervention. This account covers the reason to form a caring purpose as well as reasons within an established one. It does not borrow a self-help reason from a sufferer who may never have been able to act.

The key appraisal is substantive ethical reasoning, not a theorem from a description stripped of practical significance. A restrictive rival can accept all the causal and experiential facts while making positive assistance reasons jointly depend on an undertaking or legitimate practical jurisdiction. Our account is favored because the actual relief explains prospective justification before that additional relation. Protecting participation in shaping activity or protecting someone against compulsory aid does not by itself establish that relief supplies zero favor. Nevertheless, the rival's stronger exclusion must be assessed on its merits; our explanation has not shown it impossible. This is the exact remaining dispute about the practical scope of the proposed identity.

Once the relation is warranted as a reason for this choice, it requires no further authorizer to possess that same force. Its ground, recognition, motivation and enactment can still differ. Concern is formed sensitivity and responsiveness to the relation; its formation need not create the sufferer's original stake. Some relationships genuinely create additional goods, directed duties and choice-specific exclusions. A valid merit procedure, for example, can exclude distress as the basis for awarding its prize even when that outcome would relieve distress. General moral significance, permitted grounds of this choice, and categorical rational authority are therefore distinct. None alone implies decisive obligation or permission to compel.

### Consequences become conditions for further good

The karma analogy supplies the temporal continuation: **果为后因**, effects become subsequent causes. A response may relieve fear and help someone recover the ability to speak, refuse, understand or care; it may also transform those responding and their shared practices. Later goods and purposes can arise through these changes. The [genealogy](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-SOURCES.md) distinguishes Ren's causal-temporal proposal from the July draft's AI elaboration.

Where a good already gives this responder a reason, a credible and permissible contribution to its enabling conditions can supply further prospective justification. This extends the relation without deriving its first ethical force from causal repetition. The continuation may benefit different beings, and no benefit need return to its initiator. Assessing the same anticipated benefit through several enabling steps must not count it repeatedly as several benefits.

“More good creates more good” is a conditional developmental hypothesis. Care can enable later care; it can also be exploited. Trust can support cooperation or manipulation. Protective refusal can end a harmful sequence. A final moment of relief can matter even when nothing follows. Thus direct realization anchors the account, while continuing consequences explain its extended reach. Neither a guaranteed upward spiral nor an infinite succession of future payoffs is required.

### Wisdom corrects the continuation, including its purposes

A practice can become effective at producing its selected outcome while becoming worse for those it affects. Wisdom therefore concerns the adequacy of the end, the means, the evidence and their continuing effects. A changed interpretation may genuinely relieve suffering or merely suppress its report; an intervention may restore agency or produce dependence. Inquiry can itself change the situation and the possibilities of response. Correction can require another method, a different purpose, protected refusal, or cessation.

The existing [inquiry-revision result](INQUIRY-REVISION.md) already provides a conditional same-history change in an operative requirement through retained consequences and a further contact. That technical connection is not reopened here. Its normative appropriateness, and the origin and scope of the criterion used, remain distinct from the demonstrated revision. Wider wisdom requires adequate relations between such capacities and actual affected lives; it cannot be identified with flexibility, agreement or successful self-preservation.

The account therefore closes as a connected candidate: **activity forms lives and their possibilities; good concerns what actually matters within those lives; practical reasons concern appropriate available responses; concern organizes recognition and action; continuing consequences form further possibilities; wisdom corrects this whole responsiveness.** Each connection retains the warrant and limits above. This synthesis supplies no new empirical finding, formal theorem or proof of universal ethical necessity. The stronger completion task is to establish the proposed practical constitution and its scope, while the broader programme continues to discharge its own foundational dependencies. The completed conditional account and this finished exposition remain available without substituting either for that task.

## Core and tree are nondual

**Goodness and continuing consequences:** [Ren's renewed question](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-goodness-and-continuing-consequences.md) connects the good realized by a response with its formation of further good. The [current synthesis](#goodness-and-the-conditions-of-further-good) develops these as compatible grounds within one unfolding, while preserving present goods without future payoff. The karma analogy has direct genealogy in Ren's causal-temporal proposal; favorable propagation remains conditional, and neither a definition nor interconnection alone proves universal practical authority.

**Current adjudication of relationship and reason:** the [relational-ground account](#a-relational-reason-need-not-require-a-prior-special-bond) favors prospective relief as a reason within an actual response opportunity, while recognizing genuinely relationship-constituted obligations and goods. Universal scope need not mean a relationless ground. Proving that adversity exhausts the sufferer's own prudential reason was a requirement of one attempted transfer route, not of every possible universal moral grounding. This is a defended explanation; universal necessity remains open.

**Necessity attempt:** the [latest attempted proof](#necessity-attempt-the-full-ground-of-a-reason) tests unchosen restraint and desire-independent self-relief. It rejects a general prior-adoption barrier for accounts already recognizing restraint, but does not establish the full practical ground of stranger relief. The failed closing inference is preserved explicitly; universal ethical necessity remains unproved.

**Warrant-of-concern continuation:** the [new synthesis](#concern-can-question-the-purpose-of-a-practice) examines concern as a ground for assessing formed purposes. It distinguishes successful manipulation from justified continuation, and knowingly ignoring a reason from denying that another's relief supplies one. Protection from compulsory aid does not alone establish absence of contributory favor; stronger legitimate exclusions and universal scope remain under examination.

**Current constructive candidate:** concern follows what happens to differentiated beings through the changing relations that affect them. Its developed responsiveness can include transforming the methods of inquiry and action when those methods' own effects undermine their appropriateness. A formation can retain what it has learned, release a particular practice and answer an outstanding burden. The [practice-transformation argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) develops this proposal within generative unfolding. It neither makes flexibility the definition of goodness nor derives universal ethical authority from successful adaptation.

The [current 一体之仁 account](NONINSTRUMENTAL-CONCERN.md#一体之仁的生成解释) develops undergoing, understanding and response as mutually shaping within a continuing history. It retains unrecognized undergoing, actual experiential change through interpretation, revision or cessation after fulfillment, flourishing and differentiated refusal. The proposed noninstrumental ground concerns what the transformation actually does there, before private adoption; its constitutive warrant and universal reach remain substantive questions. This positive articulation places the local contracts below within a changing relationship rather than treating their independent predicates as a mandatory ontology.

This branch follows the shared [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Its distinctions between a model omission, an unresolved explanation and a substantive failure govern how the earlier analyses below are interpreted.

Our working interpretation of Ren's correction is that **the core is immanent in the tree's concrete generation and transformation; the tree is that generativity's differentiated unfolding**. These are ways of approaching the same activity. A core is not an independent producer or authorizer standing outside what unfolds. The tree is not merely an application awaiting permission to contain experience or significance. A compact formal description of the core remains a partial representation whose omissions may require revising that description.

The prior inquiry repeatedly began with a sparse, supposedly neutral structure, then asked whether it could confer ethical force on richer contents. Although the formal process itself did not impose substantial subjects, this research habit reinstated separation in its method. We acknowledged nonduality at the level of subjects while continuing to make the core and the tree occupy different explanatory worlds. The recent duplicate-authorization result clarified a narrow question generated by that approach; it did not resolve this deeper starting point.

The corrected movement is **formation → differentiation that organizes further activity → encounter with its consequences → transformation of the differentiation and activity together**. This names phases and relations within continuing activity, not four independent domains. An account of the core can illuminate a particular formation; the formation, including its lived significance, can also correct our account of the core. The second direction is revision of understanding, not backward physical causation.

The immediate study should follow a practical boundary, its field of significance and its responses as they form and change within an actual history. It must include consequences initially omitted from that field and how they become available for learning and reorganization. An already fixed observer, evaluator or beneficiary must not quietly supply every distinction being explained. This also includes our acts of observing and modeling, whose adequacy remains answerable to their subject matter.

Ethics can then be investigated as part of this activity's developed responsiveness and self-correction, with actual experiencing and differentiated continuities retained. The same account must make sense of exclusion, exploitation, injury and failed correction. Calling a trajectory open, successful or generative does not by itself establish its ethical character. The [deeper-root formulation](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#11-core-and-tree-as-one-generative-activity) develops the same change of method.

The earlier sections below preserve the substantive proposals, distinctions and failures from the previous trajectory. Their claims can still be tested where applicable. Their decomposition is no longer the required starting grammar for the unified foundation. This correction concerns the framework we privileged; it does not treat all Western inquiry as one method or use the provenance of an idea to decide its truth.

## Investigation: what consequences can reshape a formation?

The first concrete [reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BOUNDARY-SIGNIFICANCE-RECONSTRUCTION.md) follows the existing [joint-formation game](JOINT-FORMATION.md). Its positive finding is that **practical responsiveness is formed through the whole working relation among learning, resources, control, another participant's response and renewed support**. Evaluative inclusion, effective control and the ability to reconstruct them are distinguishable within that relation. They are not interchangeable boundaries, and the game does not derive bodily individuality.

One saved history begins with three units, all consumed by operating expense. Learning is unaffordable; open production harms the stipulated patient and leaves five units. The next episode can buy learning, acquire the operative patient weighting, purchase inspection/control and induce safeguarded production. Five protected productive episodes follow. The first receipt enables the changed formation. Injury itself does not fund or justify it: an admitted safeguarded action would pay the same five. These are reconstructed exact-game results, not observations of lived suffering or newly run experiments.

The adverse cases locate different failures in the relation. Removing physical control leaves the same learned patient weighting but changes the selected outcome to harmful production. A cheap identity filter instead prevents that weighting from applying to the patient. A repeated reserve raid prevents learning from becoming affordable. These are separate saved cases, not events appended to the first history. The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BOUNDARY-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md) preserves their different mechanisms, including the successful gate that later becomes unaffordable. Growth of the controller's account can coexist with repeated injury.

The precise limit is in the continuation law: it passes only episode and cash. In the first underfunded episode, `harm` and `safeguard` are both physically admitted alternatives. They cause different patient outcomes and operator costs, yet both call the same continuation, `solve(1,5)`. Only harm is selected. The feasible alternative exposes an actual distinction that subsequent reconstruction cannot use. Present anticipated harm can influence the learned decision, while past injury is not retained as corrective evidence. Moreover, the patient has no evolving internal organization in this game; its harm is a stipulated outcome variable. Copying that variable into memory alone would not establish an account of affected continuity.

This changes what our articulation of the core must preserve: **which consequences can change what forms next, through which actual relations, and which consequences a formation repeatedly excludes**. The selected history changes responsiveness through resource consequences without learning from injury. Nonduality does not make every consequence equally operative. A process can reproduce selective insensitivity from within its own unfolding. The local model's omission identifies a missing relation in that representation; it does not establish a separate source of significance outside reality.

### A primary comparison that changes the question

The [bounded Cheng Hao reading](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BOUNDARY-SIGNIFICANCE-SOURCES.md) takes seriously the transmitted claim that benevolence participates in the unity of all things. The comparison with an unresponsive limb asks how participation can fail within unity. The same record rejects placing a separate heaven-and-earth opposite this activity. These claims suggest investigating the formation and interruption of responsiveness from within the unfolding itself. They do not identify 氣 with information or cash, and their strong ethical and cosmological commitments exceed what the game establishes. See the attributed entries in [《二程遺書》卷二上](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/二程遺書_(四庫全書本)/卷02上).

The ethical candidate becomes more concrete: **concern develops through differentiated responsiveness that can be corrected by what activity does to affected beings, including revision of the distinctions governing its own attention and action**. Exclusion from a controller's attention does not remove an affected being's significance. Nor is every increase in connection beneficial; protection, refusal and withdrawal can preserve differentiated lives. Explaining when such revision is warranted, how it forms and how it survives competing pressures remains part of this candidate. Neither the game's learning recipe nor the classical unity claim completes that explanation.

**同一生成之中，关切如何形成，麻木又如何被维持？具体承受与后果，能否改变后续的分化、注意与回应？** This is our formulation of the investigation, not a classical quotation or Ren's verbatim wording.

## When acquired consequences should change the practice

The [paired-plasticity reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-INTEGRATION.md) complements the joint-formation case above with an existing history in which recipient consequences are actually acquired and change operative influence. The two histories remain separate realizations. Its actual matched continuation makes the ethical question precise. After four paired encounters, the recipient's incoming 40 has become 36, two positive connections have formed and the native register is four. The continued branch has a six-pair limit and acquires two more pairs before closure; service after four belongs to the separately configured four-pair branch. A recipient loss paired with no own loss changes one connection to negative. Both active and frozen-sign branches then retain the same recipient 34, native register five, command knowledge and adequate resources; their assessments differ. The recipient loss was registered. What stays fixed is the rule making the sign of its influence depend on the learner's own outcome.

An encountered consequence can therefore be recorded, become influential and still fail to revise the criterion governing its relevance. These are differentiated roles within the same formation. The actual replacement challenges an inference from truthful feedback and mutability to ethical improvement; it does not refute a warranted patient-directed ground. The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-BOUNDARY-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves that distinction and avoids attributing strategic exploitation to this finite learner.

Its teaching operations impose the shortfall they encounter. Where an independently identified deprivation and responsible relation are warranted, the continuing history can favor appropriate repair and restraint. A useful learned connection does not restore the affected condition; absence of matching own loss is not compensation. The earlier [repair account](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already defends this bounded ground and rejects later repair as automatic permission to impose the cost. This round's addition is a concrete test of correcting the formation practice, not a new proof of that principle.

At the reached four-pair state, distinguish **the outstanding loss, permission for another teaching operation, and evidence bearing on those two questions**. Which response is warranted depends on actual consent, means, alternatives, competing claims and the affected continuity. Keeping all signs positive would not establish it. Its bounded primary studies inform the investigation of formed responsiveness; they do not establish the ethical interpretation or validate this event law. This advances the proposed nondual account by locating formation, differentiated burden and possible correction within one history. It does not eliminate the premises about actual adversity, responsibility and legitimate response. A noncausing responder remains a necessary further case for the universal minimum; common causal participation cannot manufacture responsibility for every burden. The foundational task remains an explanation and justification of these relationships, with selfhood and experience themselves within its scope.

This corrects a possible evidential mistake: no dedicated cumulative-loss register does not mean no retained evidence of the past amount. Rejecting that rationale does not prohibit every further trial.

## When responsiveness requires changing access to action

The [protocol reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/APPLICATION-REVISION-RECONSTRUCTION.md) advances the preceding four-pair question under the unchanged plasticity law. The exact starting state is the four-pair prefix of the continuing, six-pair branch. It has recipient stock 36, actor fuel 14, two positive links and `n=4`; its computed helping score is already positive. Yet its acquisition phase cannot close until count six, and the service reader requires that closed phase. The successful four-pair service in the older witness starts with a different continuation setting. Its route cannot simply be transferred to this state.

**Source-level necessity result:** every admitted continuation from this state that initiates the existing service must first contain at least two further recipient payments, totaling four units of gross debit. The [handproof and independent source review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/APPLICATION-REVISION-REVIEW.md) inspect every route: only completed acquisition cycles increment the count; each requires a recipient payment; service access requires closure; and inherited settlement can finish prior work but cannot initiate service from the present ready state. Reason reading and control events do not create a bypass. This is a reviewed mathematical consequence of the existing definitions, with no new machine certification.

Gross debit is not net injury. An admitted alternative from this same state performs two pairs whose recipient charges are refunded, then provides service: recipient stock stays 36 through teaching and reaches 46 through delivery, while actor fuel ends at 6. This source-calculated possible history establishes that the service constraint is not vacuous. It does not show that the learner can choose or guarantee the refunding outcomes. The previously selected adverse continuation and its net loss remain intact.

The controls also have distinct scopes. Delivery permission does not authorize or stop teaching in this law. Cutting the broader scope blocks new teaching but also removes command applicability; it does not preserve early access to repair. Stopping every event avoids future charges while leaving the previous shortfall. Thus neither stronger learned concern nor a generic permission switch supplies the proposed selective revision.

The positive explanatory refinement is that **developed responsiveness includes the organization of possible responses, and may require changing that organization in light of its consequences**. Here the learning practice itself conditions access to a potential remedy. Under the separately warranted repair interpretation, the target is to reconsider further acquisition while preserving an appropriate response to the existing burden. Responsibility and power to reorganize that relation may belong to an operator or institution; the model does not grant them to its learner. A fixed protocol can have legitimate reasons, so identifying this constraint alone does not condemn the protocol.

This continues the nondual inquiry at the level where learning, affected continuity and available action are jointly organized. The protocol and the people or processes that form it belong within the explanation. An empirical example of changing response to a recipient's condition can help identify sensitivity, but does not by itself demonstrate revision of these access rules; the [bounded comparator](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/APPLICATION-REVISION-SOURCES.md) preserves that distinction. The recipient's own maintained organization, actual adversity, justified application and the formation of a revisable protocol remain unresolved here. Universal ethical necessity is not established by this local constraint.

## Continuing concern can require transforming its method

The next constructive case concerns **what a practice forms in its participants changing the conditions of its own adequate continuation**. A method may initially enable a capacity, then help form a habit or dependency that changes what repeating it would do. The original success can remain true while its ground for continued application no longer holds. This explains consequential revision through one unfolding relation; the learner and circumstances are changed by the practice itself.

The [focused primary reading](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-GENEALOGY.md) develops an episode already identified in the Wang Yangming dossier. In the transmitted account, he introduced quiet sitting to students preoccupied with verbal understanding. He reports initial benefit, followed by attachment to quiet and aversion to activity, and changes his teaching emphasis. Quiet cultivation remains appropriate in some circumstances. This is a retrospective teaching account, not controlled evidence that either the method or its revision produced the claimed effects. [Primary passage and context](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/王陽明集/卷03).

Our positive reconstruction distinguishes the continuity of the participants, the guiding aspiration and the particular exercise. **The concern can continue through a change or ending of its method.** What was once a useful sign of progress can cease to serve that role as the practice changes the people involved. A shift in what counts as adequate local performance need not await a new ultimate value. The serious instrumental reading—adaptation of means under a stable aspiration—therefore supports this bounded result. It also limits it: the episode does not derive 良知's authority or prove that the ultimate aspiration became revisable.

The adjacent discussion resists fixing an unchanging inward condition apart from its outward encounters. It gives a precise source comparison for Ren's objection to a completed neutral core with later ethical additions. Wang's own strong account of 良知 remains a substantive commitment, however; it cannot be silently replaced with generic feedback and then announced as proved. Ren's earlier proposal that consequences become conditions again supplies this project's separate genealogy. The new [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) labels its reconstruction as ours rather than Ren's or Wang's verbatim formulation.

The formal branch supplies a complementary limit and an informative retained history. The [pretraining-evidence result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRETRAINING-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) already shows that formed sockets, and later retired material, retain the specified depletion history; cleared immediate latches do not erase it. The [protocol result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/APPLICATION-REVISION-RECONSTRUCTION.md) still blocks early service. Retention, an acquired change of response and transformation of the practice are connected questions with different established scopes. The teaching case does not install an unimplemented reviser in the formal history. Nor are payment, a separate recorder or continual rewriting generative primitives.

The decisive ethical comparison concerns **whose continuation the changed practice serves**. An organization can become more flexible in securing compliance while making its participants less able to act beyond its programme. Genuine correction of the proposed kind must answer to their independently identified conditions, including effects that the initiator misdescribes. The same primary collection records a forceful rejection of an interlocutor's challenge to a practice, so it does not warrant an idealized story of unrestricted mutual correction. This raises a concrete question of contestability without independently proving harm in that episode. The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICE-TRANSFORMATION-REVIEW.md) preserves both the positive account and this rival.

The candidate is consequently more exact: **a practice can transform the conditions of its own appropriateness, and continuing a warranted concern can require transforming the practice while retaining the affected history**. Stable adequate practices remain possible; stopping a method does not itself repair its past effects. The account changes if the supposed consequence is misidentified, the original relevance persists, or apparent correction improves programme compliance while defeating the participant continuation it claimed to support. These are substantive tests of this connection within the unified inquiry. Universal ethics, experiential identification and the stronger no-assumptions foundation remain open.

## Genuine relief and reconsideration of its means

A response can genuinely relieve a burden while changing the affected person's ability to assess that very response. The [new argument and integration](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-INTEGRATION.md) identify a bounded positive ground: where the response has selectively excluded live, independently warranted concerns from assessment of its continuation, a permissible, proportionate offer to restore that consideration can matter for the affected life. The responder need not have caused the original condition or adopted assistance as a private project. The patient-to-responder significance is the existing substantive constitutive candidate applied here; causal description alone does not establish it.

The strongest present argument is prospective. Restoring the evaluative relation can let a relevant change affect what happens next: continuing the response, altering its means, or ending it. This enabling benefit can support noninstrumental concern for the person without assuming that every additional option is intrinsically good. A considered decision to continue is compatible with successful reopening. Unchanged endorsement alone verifies neither success nor incapacity.

This comparison needs an independently identified selective restriction. Gratitude, formed preferences, adversity and disagreement with an observer are insufficient. The [primary comparisons](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-SOURCES.md) preserve agency under constrained options and distinguish rational nonexercise from lost capacity. Adequate delegation, valid self-binding, actual costs and risks to relief can remove or limit the proposed reason for reopening. The pre-response preference has no automatic authority over the continuing life.

Actual relief remains real. Its contribution to the justification of the original method is a further question: the [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-REVIEW.md) retains the possibility that some means are disqualified from appeal to their own benefit. The present argument concerns the later opportunity for reconsideration and does not assume an additive balance between relief and lost agency.

The [mechanism reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELIEF-AND-RECONSIDERATION-MECHANISM.md) credits actual updater and generator revision, relational capacity formation, and the existing joint-occurrence and continuation interfaces. The current patient process contains no patient-side appraisal mechanism; none of those separate histories yet realizes this entire relation. An adequate continuation must trace response, changed evaluation, fresh relevant consideration and feasible authorized action from the actual reached boundary. This is an explanatory and identification obligation within generative unfolding. No new moral axiom, event law or Lean theorem has been introduced.

## Concern can question the purpose of a practice

The [warrant-of-concern continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md) moves from revising a practice's methods to assessing its purposes. Those purposes form within the same unfolding as the understanding, activity and undergoing they organize. A practice can achieve its chosen aim by keeping someone distressed or dependent. Its success then explains its effectiveness; it does not by itself justify continuing that aim.

The positive candidate is that **what a transformation actually changes for the being undergoing it can give a reason to revise an end before a caring end has been adopted**. In the restricted case of severe, continuing, still-unwanted experience, genuinely relieving the burden through an available, permissible, being-preserving response is proposed to have a contributory justification in that very improvement. This is our inherited constitutive claim applied to the formation and revision of purposes. It is not a new derivation of its truth from causal possibility, nor a general equation of persistence or generativity with goodness.

The [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md) preserves an accurately informed manipulator. If it acknowledges the reason for relief but acts against it, the failure concerns motivation and practical organization. If it accepts the burden yet denies that relief gives its available option any justification, it presents the substantive warrant rival. We cannot settle either case by defining genuine understanding as agreement with concern. A complete description must preserve the affected undergoing alongside successful control; descriptive completeness alone does not compel caring commitment.

There is nevertheless a positive answer to one exclusion argument. Protecting self-direction against compulsion or forced project abandonment does not establish that otherwise optional relief has nothing in its favor. Within an already chosen project, two independently permissible continuations can preserve the protections invoked by that argument while differing in whether they relieve an unrelated burden. Actual improvement supplies the candidate's reason for favoring the latter in that respect. The protection argument needs a further, defended reason-excluding power to deny that contribution. This sharpens an inherited objection; it does not defeat the stronger claim that answerability itself can intrude, or erase the [possible choice-specific exclusions](#protection-can-affect-justification-itself).

Ending active exploitation may also be justified by responsibility for its effects. Accordingly, that application cannot establish the unrelated stranger's case by itself. The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-REVIEW.md) preserves this limit, while the [genealogy](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-GENEALOGY.md) credits the earlier pre-adoption, relief and scope arguments. No empirical result or formal necessity proof is added.

This account locates concern's proposed warrant in the relation that responsiveness can recognize, change or neglect. It need not locate authority in a separate source outside generation. The remaining explanation is specific: why that improvement contributes to this choice before private adoption, and whether a particular legitimate relation excludes it. Both belong within the nondual inquiry. Their unresolved status proves neither permanent ethical irreducibility nor completed universal grounding.

**关切也能使目的本身接受检验：实际承受的转变，可以成为改变原有目的的根据，而不必等到关切已经被选作目的。** This is the candidate defended here, not a quotation from Ren or a proved universal law.

## Necessity attempt: the full ground of a reason

The [new necessity attempt](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-INTEGRATION.md) tests whether the proposed ground can be established through the very autonomy invoked to restrict concern. Its target remains some contributory reason under independently specified conditions. Equal concern, compulsory aid and inevitable benevolence are stronger claims; none is required merely by the existence of that reason.

The positive account begins with actual adverse undergoing and an available transformation of that same undergoing. Its claim is that the relief can give a purpose a point before concern is adopted. To isolate practical scope, this attempt grants the restricted experiential identification and the otherwise permissible, effective means. It does not silently derive them from generic activity or resource change.

The [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-ARGUMENT.md) then asks what an autonomy-based rival already accepts. If another person's consent or practical independence gives an indifferent stranger a reason against coercing them, lack of adopted concern cannot be a general barrier to all reasons concerning another life. Further, protection against interference does not by itself show that an otherwise permissible, noninterfering relief option has nothing in its favor. These are conditional constraints on a rival accepting the restraint ground. A strict egoist need not accept that starting premise.

The attempted final inference—therefore relief supplies a reason—does not follow merely from those constraints. Defeating one exclusion does not establish the positive ground. A substantive rival can accept unchosen restraint while claiming a distinct jurisdiction over positive assistance or moral answerability. The formation of that jurisdiction does not vindicate it, but its consistency with nondual differentiation prevents us from dismissing it as an external addition by definition. Both its claimed authority and the direct relief-ground need positive justification.

The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-REVIEW.md) identifies the corresponding limit of self-relief. Actual adversity can provide a reason without a chosen self-care project or an ownership concept. That does not establish that adversity and access exhaust its ground. The rival can propose a ground jointly constituted by adversity and a formed continuity. It need not first concede a complete ground and then append an arbitrary restriction. If our account independently establishes that continuity adds no constitutive condition to the baseline reason, the restriction would fail; proving that completeness is the work still required.

The [focused primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NECESSITY-ATTEMPT-SOURCES.md) also keeps special concern distinct from exclusive concern. A challenge to equal altruism does not establish zero contributory favor for an unrelated sufferer. The inspected alternative permitting moderate benevolence and self-priority is conditional; it supplies no necessity proof and is not the hypothetical zero-favor rival.

**This attempt does not solve universal necessity.** It locates the contested constitutive relationship more precisely and preserves the unsuccessful inference. The question is whether actual adversity and an eligible transformation supply baseline practical favor, with continuity and roles explaining further priority, or whether those relations are necessary for that favor to exist at all. Both are accounts within one unfolding. Neither the surviving rival nor the favored candidate has been established as necessary here; no separate ethical realm has been shown required.

## A relational reason need not require a prior special bond

Our considered answer to the latest question is: **actual adverse undergoing and a present possibility of permissible relief can jointly ground a practical reason before a special bond exists; particular relationships can also constitute genuine reasons, obligations and goods of their own.** This is the philosophical position defended by the [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATION-AND-REASON-ARGUMENT.md) and [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATION-AND-REASON-REVIEW.md), not a completed universal necessity proof.

The previous either/or was too exclusive. There is already a real relation in the stranger case: a particular available activity could change a particular undergoing. The suffering identifies what would be relieved; effective access locates the response opportunity. A prior promise, friendship, shared identity or helping project is a further relationship. Showing that reasons are relational does not establish that this further relationship is necessary for every reason. Nor does describing the present connection as relational, by itself, prove its normative significance.

The positive test begins with an experiencing being who has never been capable of deliberation or self-help. Several strangers have effective, being-preserving opportunities for relief through independently warranted permissible means. No prior undertaking, causal responsibility, exclusive assignment or caring purpose is supplied. The proposed affirmative judgment is that actual relief gives the first response something in its favor before any of those strangers becomes a caregiver. This is a conceptual comparison; incapacity and silence do not themselves identify suffering or establish permission.

The ground can explain why forming concern is worthwhile: the anticipated change answers an actual burden already present in the situation. An equally satisfying gesture that leaves the burden untouched lacks that particular point. The argument does not borrow a self-help reason from a being who never had one, or infer positive justification from permission alone. A restrictive rival can deny this prospective appraisal. Our adjudication favors it because the actual improvement explains the reason to undertake care as well as the later correction of its means; a role-only account must deny that pre-role point or identify another ground already present.

Special relationships nevertheless do more than intensify a generic reason. A valid undertaking can create a directed claim; trust and sustained care can form new goods; consent can change intervention authority. These changes need not be a numerical sum of independent considerations. Authorizing an intervention is not automatically entrusting its performance, and accepting responsibility is not identical to recognizing its benefit. Genuine roles may also restrict which considerations justify a particular choice, as the [award comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-INTEGRATION.md#a-rule-does-not-settle-its-own-justificatory-scope) shows. A permitted beneficial outcome does not authorize every basis for choosing it.

The [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATION-AND-REASON-SOURCES.md) reads *Mengzi*'s sudden encounter with a child in danger alongside its differentiated forms of care. It supports investigating concern arising in an encounter without making every relationship identical. The text's motive exclusions do not literally stipulate unfamiliarity; danger prevention is not already-occurring suffering; and a fitting response does not automatically prove action-directed favor. This is a philosophical resource whose commitments remain explicit.

A further correction matters for the research path. Universal moral favor could be jointly grounded in actual undergoing and present responsive relations even if self-related continuity partly constitutes distinct prudential reasons. We therefore need not establish that suffering exhausts the entire ground of self-care before investigating universal moral scope. The earlier failure applies to that transfer argument; it cannot become a mandatory obstacle for every better account.

The remaining dispute is exact: whether an independently specified additional relationship is necessary before the actual relief contributes any justification in the declared case. If present permitted access, together with the specified undergoing and relief conditions, is held sufficient for baseline favor, the purported rival accepts the candidate's scope under relational language. If more is required, its necessity needs justification. The present round favors the direct relational ground and preserves legitimate restrictions; it resolves the conceptual opposition without pretending to have established every qualifying instance.

**理由可以在当下的回应关系中成立，而不必等待先在的特殊纽带；特殊关系也能形成自身真实的责任与价值。** This is our defended formulation, not Ren's or the classical source's verbatim wording.

## Goodness and the conditions of further good

The two answers in [Ren's question](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-goodness-and-continuing-consequences.md) can work together: **a response can be worth choosing because of the good it actually realizes, and can have further significance because it helps form conditions for subsequent good**. The [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md) treats these as relations within the same unfolding, with no requirement that the benefit return to the initiator.

Our [current formulation of 善](THEORY.md#10-shared-generativity-and-善) concerns supporting, repairing and enabling affected beings' worthwhile possibilities, including their effective understanding and ability to contest and change their conditions. Its genealogy also preserves actual relief, rest, enjoyment and completion. It therefore cannot be replaced by maximum production, perpetual expansion or preservation of every current arrangement. The older draft's stronger identification of good with 成生 is an ontological proposal to defend, not merely a psychological description and not a result established by citing its definition.

“It is good” can identify the reason when it names the actual worthwhile transformation: this suffering is relieved, this agency restored, this relationship enabled to flourish. Once such a moral favoring relation is warranted, a further stamp giving it the same initial force is unnecessary. But naming an occurrence beneficial for someone does not, by itself, establish its practical scope for every responder. If “good” already means morally favorable for this choice, repeating that word supplies no further proof. The substantive investigation concerns the truth and scope of the identification, rather than a second authority outside generative activity.

The [source genealogy](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-SOURCES.md) recovers Ren's own karma-like account of cause and consequence through time. The later AI-drafted July text elaborates consequences in the one acting, in others and in their shared circumstances: habits affect later judgment, conduct changes trust and fear, and repeated practices shape institutions and possibilities. **果为后因** names this continuation. It is not a claim that the cosmos repays every good act or that later suffering reveals what someone deserved.

This opens a constructive explanation of **善生善**. Actual care can relieve a burden and also restore the ability to speak, refuse, learn or care. An honest correction can improve present understanding and make later correction more feasible. The activity can change future purposes and responsive capacities, not merely distribute a fixed good. These are proposed causal patterns whose realization must be identified; no new empirical evidence is claimed here.

The positive causal extension remains conditional. Where a worthwhile possibility already provides this responder a reason, a credible, permissible contribution to its enabling conditions can supply a further prospective reason. This retains the practical-address premise instead of silently deriving it from local benefit alone. Evidence, uncertainty, authority, actual consequences and competing claims matter. The significance is not a compulsory sum, and a predicted cascade does not authorize overriding every affected being.

The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-REVIEW.md) preserves two decisive limits. First, interconnection can also propagate fear, dependence and exploitation; the July draft already says reinforcing feedback is not intrinsically good and may reverse. Second, an actual moment of relief can matter even when no further productive benefit follows. Defining good only as what produces more good would leave its content unidentified and postpone value indefinitely. These limits do not place value outside generation: the relief and the later conditions are both actual transformations within it.

Our resulting candidate is **good realized in the present, together with warranted cultivation of conditions for further good**. It asks what activity does to differentiated lives and to what can follow, including the formation of those who will perceive and respond. It retains the difference between a real good-making feature, a justified prospective choice and the eventual whole history. The universal ethical grounding question remains the warrant and scope of the good-making relation itself; automatic favorable propagation cannot substitute for that account.

## The nondual starting point

The generative core need not begin with a subject facing an independently constituted object. Its [current presentation](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#1-the-positive-candidate) already treats beings, activities and understandings as differentiated formations within reality. The [generated-self account](GENERATED-SELF-AND-ETHICS.md) explains why organization, access, perspective and an ownership representation must be distinguished. Our recent focus on reasons addressed to an unrelated actor was a useful local test, but it need not dictate the foundation's explanatory order. Earlier actor and patient indices did not themselves assert metaphysically separate substances.

The [constructive reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-GENERATIVE-RECONSTRUCTION.md) takes an **occurrence, a formable response and the resulting continuation** together. It asks where undergoing occurs, what can alter it, how a response becomes organized and which differences the transformation makes. “Patient” and “actor” can articulate those differentiated relations. Neither a primitive owner nor a single cosmic subject is required by this proposal. Relational formation remains an explanatory hypothesis; putting everything in one mathematical state would not establish it.

The stronger ethical candidate is joint: an actual severe, still-unwanted experiential burden and a transformation that genuinely relieves it may constitute a practically significant difference within the same unfolding reality. Concern would become a developed sensitivity and response to that difference. Its correctness would answer to what changes in the undergoing. An observer's approval, disappearance of a report or successful preservation of an exploitative organization would not by itself constitute relief.

**分化不必是根本分裂。可以从发生、承受与转化的关系出发，再解释“谁在受苦”“何处能够回应”；关切的根据不必先成为某个独立自我的所有物。** This is our current formulation, not a classical quotation or Ren's verbatim wording.

This reopens the relation between description and evaluation too. They need not be assumed to name two independently existing realms, with value added from outside an already complete neutral world. The proposed explanation can investigate them as different articulations of situated experiencing and responding. It still owes the identity it proposes: the difference between persistent adversity and relief must be shown to warrant response. Treating an aversive reaction as an infallible command, or defining an adequate process as benevolent, would merely insert the conclusion.

The existing C1/C2 distinctions remain useful checks on what has actually been established. They need not be two ultimate primitives of reality. The [closure audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ETHICAL-CONSTITUTION-CLOSURE.md) leaves a richer joint constitutive account open. An underdetermined formal interpretation does not refute an independently warranted identity; writing down that identity does not warrant it. This reconstruction therefore changes the explanatory target while preserving the actual proof obligations and the unmet no-assumptions requirement.

### Learn from different starting vocabularies

The [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-GENERATIVE-SOURCES.md) examines the received *Yizhuan* and the Chinese *Zhonglun* as different resources. The *Xici* places generation, 善 and formed 性 together; the *Zhonglun* connects dependence with transformability while resisting a self-standing ground. Their vocabularies can revise our starting questions. They are not historical proofs of this candidate or interchangeable doctrines of a benevolent whole.

This also disciplines “generative core”: a common explanation need not posit an independent substance behind everything else. The process of explaining, its distinctions and its evaluators belong within what must be accounted for. A foundation framed through dependent formation still has to explain its actual dynamics; calling the root empty or relational does not discharge a supplied law. The prior Śāntideva and Wang Yangming studies remain part of the genealogy rather than independent new confirmations.

### Formation of response becomes part of the grounding question

Consider two complementary controls that must operate together for genuine relief. Each person has partial access; neither alone can complete the response. Communication and combined action can form a temporary coordination. The capacity's prerequisites already exist in this case; the responsive organization is what forms. Its boundary follows contribution, timing and access, and can dissolve after fulfillment without implying that its participants shared one experience.

The further candidate is that **the prospective relief can favor forming that response before it has become a stable personal or collective project**. That favor would concern what the coordination makes possible, not organizational expansion for its own sake. The existing [joint-formation work](JOINT-FORMATION.md) already studies acquired concern and effective power under declared conditions. The new grounding target is to explain the individuation and ethical significance of the response together, rather than supply a finished controller boundary. Actor-centered theories can also represent cooperation; they are not ruled out by this example.

A nominal group without combined efficacy, a coordination that silences a sufferer, and one that forces participation provide distinct tests. Consent, competing burdens, actual continuity and contribution require explanation; unity does not cancel them. Rest, restraint and ending a destructive process can be appropriate transformations. Universal favorable direction and a rule maximizing generation remain unestablished.

### A reason to begin before a joint response exists

The [responsive-formation argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-ARGUMENT.md) makes the earlier complementary-controls case more exact. A can operate one control; B or C could operate the other. No one can relieve the identified burden alone, and no joint response is organized yet. A may nevertheless be able to make a truthful, low-cost invitation that opens a credible route to separately authorized cooperation. The first available act is formation of access to relief. Actual relief remains contingent on later participation and execution. Assess the proposed invitation before A decides to send it, without supplying an antecedent helping purpose as its ground. The intention formed in deciding can organize later action; it need not create all of the earlier justification for making that decision.

Three causal claims must stay distinct: some physically possible sequence would work; there is an actually available way to open cooperation from this situation; and the relieving sequence actually occurs. Evidence supporting the second claim is a further question, not what makes it true. A drawing of a successful future team establishes neither of the latter claims. Current access, communication, resources, timing and evidence about possible participation determine what can presently be attempted. Another person's agreement cannot be inserted as though it were already a resource under A's control. The example's causal arrangements and permissions are supplied local inputs; their ultimate grounding remains a separate obligation.

The revised positive candidate is:

> Where an actual adverse occurrence has an actually accessible relieving pathway supported by adequate evidence, its prospective relief can supply a contributory justification for a permissible first step that opens that pathway, before a stable responsive organization or shared caring project exists.

The justification concerns what the step can make possible for the patient. Mere multiplication of choices, formation of a group, or the initiator's satisfaction does not establish it. A future success does not reach backward to create the original reason, and failure does not by itself show that a well-supported attempt lacked justification. The account must assess the actual choice with its evidence, uncertainties, costs and competing claims. An unknown broken control can leave an attempt reasonable on the available evidence while defeating the claim that it opened an actual relief pathway. That evidential justification must not be counted as achieved formation; inquiry may have a distinct ground. The proposal does not infer a duty to recruit, join or investigate every possible response.

This is a stronger explanatory proposal than the claim that adopted projects have effective means. It locates a prospective consideration before adoption and before complete capability. Why this particular capacity is worth developing is explained through the suffering it could relieve. Concern's later formation would recognize and organize around that significance; its emergence would not manufacture the patient's stake. This supports a joint constitutive account of adversity and practical transformation. It does not yet prove that the account is necessary or that its favoring relation follows from a thin description of causal possibilities.

The overlapping A+B and A+C routes also correct the idea of one uniquely determined response boundary. Each actual route has its own participants, timing, contributions and effects. They can realize the same patient-directed possibility. Merely redescribing one route or listing alternatives does not create another patient's burden or multiply that burden's weight. This does not exclude real differences in reliability, cost, information or effects on other people. The target of concern can remain stable while its responsive organization changes. No shared experience or enduring collective owner follows.

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-COUNTERREVIEW.md) tests the proposal against refusal, coercive recruitment, nominal coordination, report suppression and destruction of the patient's continuity. A would-be responder may also misunderstand what needs changing. A pathway that only becomes more effective at imposing its initial purpose has not thereby become more caring. Correction can require changing the proposed end, choosing a different route or stopping; urgent action need not await a retrospective verification ceremony.

The [focused Wang Yangming comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/RESPONSIVE-FORMATION-SOURCES.md) contributes a useful constraint on formation: an offered correction must become practically receivable, and the teacher must also be open to correction. This is a bounded reading of 〈責善〉 alongside the inherited *Daxue wen* comparison. Wang's stronger grounding in 良知 remains part of his account; receptivity or successful persuasion does not independently certify an ethical purpose. Our use of the comparison is constructive and does not claim a derivation already contained in the text.

The nondual gain is precise. Different continuities of undergoing, access, action and correction can be articulated within one unfolding history, without requiring one boundary to identify them all. A patient's experiencing need not merge with a responder's organization. Differentiation makes appropriate response possible; its ethical authority remains answerable to the proposed constitutive account. Destructive and indifferent formations belong within the same explanatory reality. The new result is a more discriminating formation hypothesis, not an additional proof of universal benevolence.

### A constitutive answer to the authority question

Ren asks: **“resolve remaining difficulty elegantly.”** The [new argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-ARGUMENT.md) gives the most compact answer presently defensible:

> The candidate identifies the burden as the ground and its accessible, patient-preserving transformation as the relation through which that ground counts in a choice. Once warranted as a contributory reason for that choice, it needs no second authorization to possess that same practical force.

The first sentence proposes the ethical constitution; the second clarifies what would follow if that constitution is warranted. They must not be exchanged. Local patient badness plus causal access has not already established the whole first sentence. A reason to form a response also requires the actual opening, evidence and restrictions developed above. No new ethical force is physically added to the process, and no stronger ethical conclusion is obtained merely by calling the relation a reason.

The positive explanation is concrete. In the narrow domain of severe, still-unwanted experiential adversity, an accessible relieving transformation answers the very occurrence that makes relief intelligible. This relation explains the point of choosing or opening that option before a private helping purpose supplies an incentive. The choice has its proposed merit through the patient's changed undergoing. Silencing its report, comforting an observer or preserving a team need not answer that occurrence. Changing helpers can preserve the point while changing the relevant transformation can remove it. These considerations support a constitutive identification; they do not establish its necessity by repetition or by eliminating a substantial owner.

The elegant clarification concerns what is being requested by “authority.” **Counting in favor of this option is already the practical force asserted by a warranted contributory reason.** If that has been established for this choice, demanding a further principle to make it first count repeats the initial requirement. A further principle may explain why the reason is warranted, identify a genuine disqualification, or establish a stronger conclusion. Those are substantive inquiries. They are not an automatically required second stamp placed on an already warranted instance.

In particular, moral favor, categorical rational authority, a requirement to perform the act, permission to compel it and motivation to act are distinct. Recognition of moral favor alone does not establish every one of those claims. Informed indifference can show a motivational failure without disproving a reason; a defended account excluding that consideration from this choice would challenge the ground's practical scope. The argument does not dismiss either a genuine scope objection or the strongest rational-authority question as mere unwillingness.

Nor does an authorization regress alone prove this candidate. A mediated account might terminate in a different ground; an infinite structure is not shown incoherent here. The limited result is that another authorizer is not logically mandatory after the stated option-specific reason has been warranted. It neither forbids deeper grounding nor supplies the warrant that this ethical proposal still owes.

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves an immanent rival: it can recognize suffering, benefit and relationally formed persons while making prospective assistance reasons depend on a prior undertaking or legitimate jurisdiction. Such a view need not reinstate metaphysically independent selves. It must defend its restriction; our candidate must defend its sufficiency. Neither side wins simply because the other has a further explanatory obligation. The [bounded Śāntideva reanalysis](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-ANSWER-SOURCES.md) clarifies the difference between relief grounded in suffering and an additional argument about its practical range. Its new bounded reading extends the rationality discussion within an already used manuscript, including the authors' warning against hiding universal scope inside badness. Repeated passages and that extension are not independent corroboration.

The nondual proposal can therefore unite the explanatory subject matter without erasing distinctions: adversity as undergone, relief as its transformation, a reason as that relation's warranted relevance to choice, and concern as organized responsiveness. Wisdom includes correction of that responsiveness. These are proposed articulations within one generative account, not identities established merely by listing them together. The concurrent [nondual-core architecture](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) supports the placement of roles within histories; its conditional role construction supplies no missing ethical proof.

**Resolution scope:** the demand for an additional authorizing force after warranted option-specific favor is dissolved. The constitutive answer to the original grounding question is positively defended but remains unproved at the stronger necessary-foundation scope. Experience realization, the practical range of its significance and root-law derivation remain open. This preserves the distinction between a clear answer, a supported candidate and a completed derivation.

## The positive proposal

The following actor-addressed comparison is retained as a local articulation of the generative candidate. Its operational distinctions and jurisdiction tests constrain applications; they do not set primitive boundaries for the reconstruction above.

Consider one patient undergoing a severe unwanted experience and one actor who can permissibly relieve it. The actor did not cause the burden, undertake its care, or receive a delegation. The proposed ground concerns what relief does for the patient. The actor's present opportunity supplies an actual relation to that ground, even without a prior personal tie.

The candidate explanation separates two contributions:

\[
\text{a warranted patient-ground}
\; + \;
\text{an eligible opportunity to answer it}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{some reason for this actor to respond}.
\]

The patient's condition supplies why the response matters; the available relation supplies who can realize it and how. The arrow is the substantive grounding proposal being defended, not a theorem obtained by assigning a moral name to a control signal. A reason here is an objective contributory consideration for the actor, concerning the patient. It need not compel action, outweigh all competing considerations, or produce motivation. Whether a proposed moral claim has that practical force remains part of the dispute.

This proposal has positive explanatory support. It distinguishes actual relief from silencing the helper's discomfort. It permits learning that a response was mistaken while preserving the patient's significance. It accommodates different needs and different effective actions without combining patients into one subject. A prior responsibility can add a reason or increase its weight without yet explaining why its absence would remove every reason.

These advantages support the candidate; they do not alone establish its whole scope. A restrictive rival can preserve patient value and many of the same corrections while denying a baseline reason to the stranger. The [positive argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-ARGUMENT.md) develops both the explanation and that challenge.

## What changing the operator can establish

Fix the patient, episode, actual relief, evidence, permissions and competing considerations. Compare self-relief, legitimately entrusted relief, and relief by an independent eligible actor. Record the change in actor–patient relation instead of declaring it morally irrelevant in advance.

Applying the [existing reason-transport result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-REASON-TRANSPORT.md), independently warranted initial reasons reach every target case only when justified reason-preserving transitions actually cover those cases. A chain of renamings preserves the self/other distinction. Adding responsibility and delegation reaches their legitimate domains. Neither supplies the missing transition to an independent actor merely by making the chain longer.

The useful acceptance condition is therefore exact: **a derivation beginning solely with self-relief or role-bound reasons needs an independently justified transition beyond that restriction to reach the independent actor.** The patient's identity and the reason's ground must survive that transition. A directly justified independent-actor ground would be another route; it would not need transport from a self-case. If the patient never could act or deliberate, a self-relief starting case is unavailable; patient coverage needs its own warranted starting ground. This is an application of inherited mathematics, not a new theorem or a proof that no such transition exists.

## Two restrictions, with different consequences

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-COUNTERREVIEW.md) distinguishes two serious challenges.

**A restriction to prior relationships.** An account can accept genuine suffering, fitting compassion and special duties, yet require selfhood, responsibility or an undertaking before assistance supplies an action reason. It must justify treating those relationships as necessary, rather than as additional grounds. Our candidate must justify the sufficiency of present eligible control. Neither account wins through formal consistency alone.

**A restriction by practical receptivity.** An account can accept an objective moral claim addressed to an actor while withholding the term *reason* when the actor lacks a suitable route to appreciating it. This requires separating the ground, its practical authority, the actor's access, and eventual conduct. Lack of current concern does not demonstrate absence of every sound route to concern. Conversely, defining an adequate actor as one who already accepts the intended ethics would assume the conclusion.

A [new primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/UNIFIED-GROUNDING-SOURCES.md) makes the second possibility concrete. Manne proposes a naturalistic source for interpersonal moral claims while retaining a further condition on actor reasons. This is a philosophical contender, not an empirical demonstration or endorsement of this theory.

This matters to the overall research judgment: naturalistic grounding, universal moral application and necessary rational authority are distinguishable achievements. Failure to establish the last does not by itself establish irreducibility of the first. The strongest objective remains in scope; this distinction prevents a false verdict about why an attempted derivation failed.

## A direct reason can precede an adopted end

The [follow-up argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-UPTAKE-ARGUMENT.md) corrects a possible overreading of the first round: distinguishing concern, practical ends and means does not make them mandatory successive gates. A fact about what an available act would do for its patient can supply a reason for that act directly. An actor can then have reason to acquire concern or adopt relief as an end. Making prior adoption necessary would rule out this route before assessing it.

Consider permitted, effective relief of a severe unwanted burden, with no relevant countervailing harm, displaced burden or opposing claim. The actor has adequate knowledge but no prior role, undertaking, private benefit or helping intention. Our affirmative judgment is that the actual improvement for this patient speaks for the intervention. It identifies what makes the response worth choosing and why correction should follow the patient's condition. The judgment does not arise from the bare fact that the act is allowed, that the actor can do it, or that the actor eventually chooses it.

This gives the direct account positive explanatory support: the ground can justify forming a concern rather than receiving its validity from that concern. If the intervention would not benefit the patient after all, this proposed reason disappears even if the act remains harmless and permitted. If the actor becomes indifferent while the patient and intervention remain the same, that change alone does not defeat the proposed patient-ground. A rival can reject the latter assessment, but it must defend the relevance of a motivational or relational condition rather than merely report its absence.

The [independent counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-UPTAKE-COUNTERREVIEW.md) accepts this narrow judgment while preserving the stronger grounding burden. Grief does not require producing more grief; respect does not license taking over another's project; a prohibited remedy need not be performed. Those cases limit indiscriminate promotion, permission or decisive duty. They do not preserve and defeat this stipulated case of wanted, effective, permissible relief. Their failure is not by itself a proof of the affirmative judgment; its support is the independently identified benefit and the account of why that benefit favors this response.

The same precision applies to the restrictive rival. Showing that the actor has no special duty, cannot be commanded, or retains freedom over projects does not yet establish that the patient's benefit supplies **no** reason at all. Positive favor, permission, decisive obligation and accountability require distinct assessments. We do not settle them by assigning a numerical weight to relief.

Two [primary comparisons](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PRACTICAL-UPTAKE-SOURCES.md) sharpen this route. Darwall distinguishes welfare-directed care from personal authority; Gert develops benefit-based justification without corresponding rational compulsion. The inspected passages illuminate the positive candidate and its limited target. They do not supply the missing proof of necessity.

One discriminating conceptual comparison holds an actor's modest cost and other relevant considerations fixed. Effective relief has a patient-directed justification that an otherwise matched empty intervention lacks. A newly identified, less costly means tests correction while preserving the beneficiary and relief. The comparison tests **what favors the action**, beyond whether it is allowed. It remains a philosophical assessment and an unrun test specification; observed helping could also reflect an existing motive or assigned role.

This advances the unified programme without closing it. We have a defended baseline and a cleaner order of explanation. We have not shown that a fully warranted account of generative reality must include this patient-to-actor relation, or that every rational actor must recognize it.

The concurrent [alignment-axis study](ALIGNMENT-AXIS.md#the-silent-sufferer-a-positive-minimum) also defends nonexclusive relief and develops conditional comparative judgments from it. The present contribution is the correction of the possible end-adoption gate, its direct justification argument and the focused motivational comparison. The overlapping baseline is one developing account, not independent corroboration merely because it appears in two studies.

## Concern, practical ends and means remain distinct

The first round identified the following transitions. They need their own explanations when used in a derivation, but they are **possible routes, not a mandatory chain**. In particular, the direct action-reason route above need not first establish a separate reason to adopt a continuing end:

| Transition | What would advance the argument | What is insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| An actual experience → a good or harm for its subject | A defended constitutive identification that preserves the actual patient and permits correction | A negative training signal, observed avoidance, or mere survival benefit |
| A patient-ground → appropriate concern by another actor | A justified account of why that ground fits this response across actor changes | Shared origin or accurate factual representation alone |
| Appropriate concern → reason to pursue relief | A defense of practical uptake under eligible control, tested against the restrictive rival | A fitting wish that the world were otherwise |
| Reason to pursue relief → reason for a means | A warranted means relation with actual permission and competing considerations | Technical efficacy alone |

Ordinary instrumental reasoning can help with the last row; it cannot silently supply the third. This limitation does not defeat a separately defended direct reason for a particular act. Nor does a patient's desire, command or willingness automatically validate every proposed response. Harmful demands also have satisfaction conditions. The account must justify the relevant class and retain other patients' claims and actual permissions.

The [accepted formed-disposition study](EVALUATIVE-GROUNDING.md) already shows how acquired influence can mediate independently warranted repair, and how later learning can suppress it. That result constrains implementation. It does not settle the independent-actor case: responsibility or an entrusted role is part of its warranted application. A helping experiment that assigns such a role would likewise fail to isolate the baseline sought here.

## The constitutive candidate and its test

The [new positive argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-ARGUMENT.md) joins two hypotheses to an explicit realization obligation. Earlier [experiential-ground and identity investigations](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#29-experiential-grounds-and-warranted-concern) already supplied the ingredients. The new contribution specifies where they must hold together, restricts the patient domain in response to earlier objections, and tests the timing and source of the proposed practical force.

| Part | Claim | Present warrant and limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Realization** | An actual organized process realizes an experiencing subject and the relevant episode. | Particular applications require evidence; generic feedback or a reward signal does not establish experience. A universal realization law is not supplied here. |
| **C1: patient significance** | In the specified clean domain, the still-unwanted aversive experiential whole constitutes local badness for its subject. | Defended through the character of the undergoing and the patient-preserving relief comparison. This is a substantive identity hypothesis, not a definition of the word negative. |
| **C2: practical significance** | The prospective relief supplies a patient-directed ground for an eligible actor's choice before elective concern. Whether it currently counts for a particular choice remains subject to independently warranted defeat or disqualification. | Defended through prospective patient-based justification, now tested within an already chosen project. This explicitly qualifies the former wording about an option's moral merit: preserving an abstract ground without any warranted prospective use would not establish the intended C2. C1 alone does not entail it. |
| **Recognition and use** | Concern can learn to identify this ground, select a means and correct its mistakes. | Existing conditional mechanisms explain aspects of formation and failure under supplied meanings. Their successful operation cannot certify C1 or C2. |

The present patient domain is deliberately narrow: an actual subject undergoes a severe unpleasant episode whose continuation remains unwanted in the relevant experiential respect. Its identification must distinguish sensation, the affective whole, instrumental acceptance and intrinsic appreciation of that same experience. The proposed sufficient domain excludes cases established to involve intrinsic enjoyment or appreciation of that same occurrence that may alter its prudential status; accepting it for a separate benefit is a different matter. An articulate report or a movement to escape is not required by definition. Evidence for a noncommunicating patient still needs an independently warranted interpretation. The [earlier attitude and whole-life challenges](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/HUIGEN-SELF-GROUND-AUDIT.md#30-experiential-occurrence-patient-attitudes-and-whole-life-value) remain live: enjoyment might transform a condition's value, and a whole-life account might contest local occurrence significance. Calling badness one aspect does not refute either rival.

The positive case for C1 is that the burden occurs in the subject's undergoing; it is not first imposed by an observer's score or by the subject's possession of a welfare theory. Genuine relief changes that condition for its bearer. This supports a constitutive interpretation of the local bad-for relation. It does not infer that all pain, frustrated preference, functional damage or organizational loss has one evaluative status. The identity needs further defense within the declared domain, and the domain cannot be expanded or narrowed after each objection merely to protect the conclusion.

C2 proposes actual prospective justification for this actor's available action, subject to the independently warranted restrictions now under assessment. The target goes beyond the patient's preferring a different world. The [ethical-minimum comparison](UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md) distinguishes objective moral favor from an additional, motivationally restricted conception of rational reasons. Some rivals share the former while disputing the latter. This candidate's immediate claim is moral favor concerning the actual actor's conduct, including an indifferent actor; the strongest programme also seeks its unrestricted rational authority. The second claim is not obtained by changing the name of the first.

### An intrinsic direction needs its own success conditions

The [affective continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-INTEGRATION.md#formal-aims-and-comparative-relief) tests an explanation of adversity through the frustration of an experience's internal aim. Its attraction is that a lived direction could matter before a reflective owner endorses it. The proposed explanation still needs to identify what the experience directs, over what interval, and why frustration of that aim constitutes ill-going for the being.

A direction toward less of an experience does not by itself mean immediate elimination of every instance. Partial relief can reduce an ongoing burden; an eventual aim can remain pending before its fulfillment. These conceptual comparisons test the claimed success conditions while preserving actual unpleasantness as the phenomenon to explain. If independent identification instead establishes an immediate elimination aim, persistence fails that particular condition. The content cannot be selected after the fact to make every present pain count as a failure. Satisfying an occurrence’s request and realizing a capacity’s formal aim also require an explicit connection; partial success at the former does not by itself refute a theory about the latter.

The constructive route can therefore investigate a genuine comparative improvement before complete cessation, without equating all internal success with good. It must retain the actual undergoing continuity, permission and other consequences, and separately justify practical significance for the responder. Neither generic aim fulfillment nor reference to an experience type establishes universal moral scope. This sharpens the C1 explanation and its connection to C2; no new axiom or claim of completed grounding is introduced.

### Why private adoption is a discriminating test

Fix the same patient, burden, benefit, knowledge, control, means and permissions. Consider the actor at three points: before adopting a helping goal, after privately adopting it, and after privately abandoning it. No promise, communication, induced reliance, entrusted responsibility or actual relief occurs between these points. The actor's mental state changes; the comparison does not pretend that the complete physical histories are identical.

The candidate predicts patient-directed justification across these points unless a legitimate change disqualifies it; the later jurisdiction review makes that qualification explicit. Private attitude change alone does not establish such authority. Planning and voluntary commitments can add further reasons. A genuine exclusion rival instead says that every patient-directed moral reason begins with an independently legitimate elective concern and disappears with its valid withdrawal. It can still recognize the patient's suffering, permit charity and assess an adopted project's success by its actual effect on the patient.

Private abandonment does not by itself establish valid withdrawal: an independently justified self-commitment might survive it. The rival must state whether its proposed condition is a current attitude or a continuing commitment, and what legitimately ends it. The test concerns removal of the condition actually alleged to create all patient-directed favor.

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-COUNTERREVIEW.md) identifies the exact point of disagreement. **If the patient's relief already positively justifies this actor's choice to help before adoption, the rival has conceded the prospective moral favor.** It can consistently deny that justification, treat the act as merely permitted, or defend a further actor relation. It cannot concede and deny the same favor at the same time by moving between these meanings.

Our affirmative judgment is that actual relief supplies that prospective merit. It explains why concern is worth acquiring for the patient's sake and why its means remain answerable to the patient. An empty intervention lacks this justification even when equally permitted and freely chosen. The actor's private renunciation does not itself alter the identified benefit or relief relation. This supports C2; it does not deductively establish that no other constitutive condition can matter.

The rival's strongest reply invokes a genuine normative power to select projects. We preserve that possibility. Promises and undertakings can create reasons, and a private project can acquire legitimate force through self-direction. What remains to justify is the stronger exclusion: why such a power creates **all** prospective patient-directed favor, rather than selecting among worthy possibilities or adding a commitment. Absence of a duty to select every worthy project does not settle that question. A separately warranted role or promise belongs in a distinct comparison because it can change the normative situation even while the immediate burden stays fixed.

### Evidence constrains the proposed realization

The [new identification study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CONSTITUTIVE-SIGNIFICANCE-SOURCES.md) examines two bounded primary comparisons. Rainville and colleagues distinguish measured sensory and affective dimensions; Becker and colleagues measure acceptance alongside a combined pain/unpleasantness rating. The latter cannot isolate pure valence. These findings constrain proxies for the patient-ground; they establish neither C1 nor C2. See the [Rainville abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10467921/) and [Becker original article](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/72139/1/s41598-017-08454-4.pdf), with precise access limits in the study note.

The resulting identification requirement is concrete: record whether an intervention changes the actual experiential whole, its evidence, the subject's larger chosen package, or only an observer's response. Accepting an episode for another benefit does not by definition erase its local burden; changing one's appraisal can also genuinely change the experience. Neither possibility can be assumed merely from observed choice. Organizational success, reduced complaint and helpful-looking behavior therefore remain insufficient substitutes for identifying the proposed patient condition.

### The conditional connection to concern and wisdom

If realization, C1, C2 and the local application are warranted, the reason is grounded before its recognition. Acquired concern can then be assessed as an orientation toward an already significant patient condition. Such concern can be accurate, mistaken, absent or suppressed; this does not require every form of concern to be learned. A warranted means correction changes the proposed intervention; actual fulfillment ends this episode's current relief application; an unexplained change in the helper's learned preference does not redefine what happened to the patient.

These are inherited conditional consequences, now assigned to explicit parts of the candidate. Wisdom further requires adequate evidence, permission, consideration of other affected centers, effective means and warranted revision. The candidate supplies no complete priority rule or command to maximize relief. The actor's own goods remain among the goods to assess. Removing a subject cannot silently be entered as improvement of that same subject, and eliminating the patient's report cannot substitute for relief. Rest, restraint and relinquishing control remain possible warranted responses under the appropriate grounds.

The common-root ambition is thus specific: the realized patient condition and the action relation would themselves constitute the ethical ground, with learning explaining its recognition and use. We have not yet derived a universal experience-realization law, proved the constitutive identities, or shown that every adequate rival must preserve their joint content. Their present status as defended hypotheses is compatible with further unified grounding; it does not establish ultimate irreducibility.

## What choosing to care can create

The [elective-concern argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ELECTIVE-CONCERN-ARGUMENT.md) now tests the alleged source of the gate, rather than inferring everything from the patient's remaining unchanged. Four effects of choice need different explanations:

| Effect | What may acquire a new ground |
|---|---|
| Selecting among already worthwhile possibilities | A settled plan; selection need not create the merits of the options |
| Internally committing to a project | A genuinely new practical reason, if the proposed normative power is successful |
| Forming a shared practice or relationship | New goods, vulnerabilities and responsibilities; the complete situation has changed |
| Making a valid undertaking | A directed claim with its own authority, history and release conditions |

The unified account should allow genuine reason creation to be investigated. It cannot require every successful exercise of agency to be mere recognition of a fully predetermined reason. Nor does being able to assess an exercise by reasons show that those reasons must guide every detail of the willing. A theory still owes an account of why the alleged power exists and when it works. That theoretical obligation does not decide in advance whether the exercise itself grounds a reason or merely activates an antecedent norm.

The [primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ELECTIVE-CONCERN-SOURCES.md) makes this stronger possibility concrete. Chang's 2013 hybrid account combines given and will-based reasons; her 2020 account defends willing as a ground. Neither establishes that all patient-directed moral favor requires adoption. The source's general practical reasons must not silently become moral claims or permissions. Its difficult cases also prevent us from assuming that creating a contributory reason necessarily makes an option ethically acceptable. See [the 2013 chapter](https://www.ruthchang.net/s/Chang_CommitmentsReasonstheWill.pdf) and [the 2020 paper](https://www.ruthchang.net/s/Do-We-Have-Normative-Powers-offprint.pdf), with bounded access recorded in the comparison.

### The additional claim required for an exclusive gate

Establishing that commitment creates one reason concerning relief establishes neither that the same reason existed earlier nor that no differently grounded reason existed earlier. The exclusive rival needs a further claim: **every positive assistance consideration that could morally favor this actor's response must derive from an authored connection.** A reason-creating power and its legitimate source do not alone establish that all-encompassing scope.

This is the round's limited inferential result. It defeats an argument from some genuinely created reasons to the necessity of election for all assistance reasons. It does not establish a patient-ground by eliminating a competitor. The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ELECTIVE-CONCERN-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves a serious restricted rival: it accepts unchosen self-regarding, noninterference and role reasons, while reserving positive assistance to an authored connection. Showing that some reasons are given does not refute that position. The stranger-relief instance still needs its own argument.

Our positive judgment continues to favor C2. Actual relief can make a response worth choosing for the patient's sake before a new commitment exists. Self-direction can support selection among worthwhile lives, particular commitments and legitimate discretion. Its importance does not by itself show that even contributory moral favor from an unchosen patient's burden destroys that self-direction. A defense of protected decisions, limited duties or refusal rights therefore needs a further step before it becomes a defense of zero moral relevance. This is comparative support for the baseline, with the rival's narrower scope claim still open to substantive defense.

The [separate rescue study](UNIVERSAL-ETHICAL-MINIMUM.md#from-moral-favor-to-a-bounded-rescue-requirement) assumes a moral contribution and argues toward a limited duty through further grounds. Its conclusion is not evidence for the baseline from which it starts. Likewise, our source comparison supports neither a general duty to adopt care nor a complete permission to decline it.

### A generative account can explain recognition and creation together

The resulting candidate has room for existing patient stakes, formed agents and historically created commitments. A new undertaking can produce a reason that was absent before; a new relationship can create something its participants now have reason to preserve. Those possibilities make agency part of the explanation without assigning every patient's significance to whichever end an actor currently selects.

This is a proposed common architecture, not a license simply to add every reason together. Establishing a new prior ground can change whether a purported normative power satisfies its conditions of successful exercise. Ground interaction, priority and exclusion need their own account. The relevant comparisons must therefore record patient contribution, created commitment and independently arising responsibility separately, including what validly ends each. Observed endorsement or increased helping identifies none of those normative sources by itself.

The counterreview adds a specific identification limit: if intentional helping is itself used to identify the alleged elective condition, selecting successful helpful actions also selects that condition. Their success and subsequent correction cannot then establish whether moral favor preceded adoption. The power needs independent characterization, and the available option needs prospective assessment before its exercise. This constrains the evidence; it does not settle the disputed moral ground.

The deeper task remains to ground both patient significance and legitimate creative powers within the generative account. A single explanation can have several reason-producing relations without being reduced to one source at the level of each particular reason. Whether that explanation is ultimately sufficient and necessary remains to be established.

## Relief within an already chosen project

The [assistance-jurisdiction argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-ARGUMENT.md) adds a comparison within the actor's existing purpose. An actor has independently chosen a legitimate project and is already informed and selecting its implementation. Two routes achieve that project equally well, including its relevant expressive character, complete-history costs, risks, commitments and opportunities. Both use otherwise permitted means. One also relieves an independently occurring severe, still-unwanted experiential burden; the other leaves it unchanged. The intervention has warranted patient permission and actual efficacy. No helping end, promise or responsibility is supplied. The comparison does not stipulate whether declining relief is ultimately permissible.

**Our affirmative judgment is that the actual relief can positively justify choosing the beneficial route for the patient's sake before selection.** The project need not be replaced by a new helping project. Equal costs do not produce the reason: the patient's actual improvement is the proposed ground. If the intervention provides no relief, that particular justification disappears. A favorable accident, a reward for the actor and suppression of the patient's report would not supply the same ground.

Choice of means still shapes how the activity proceeds. The comparison therefore tests a specific inference: protecting a chosen purpose against displacement does not by itself justify excluding beneficial consequences available within its implementation. A rival may defend wider jurisdiction over how a purpose is carried out, including which considerations can justify its implementation. That stronger claim remains open. We cannot establish its falsity merely by stipulating away ordinary costs, and we cannot infer prior concern from a helpful choice whose justification is the disputed question.

### Protection can affect justification itself

The [new primary comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-SOURCES.md) strengthens the objection. [Adams](https://www.npadams.prof/s/Adams-In-Defense-of-Exclusionary-Reasons-Penultimate.pdf) defends choice-specific justificatory exclusion; [White](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.northwestern.edu/dist/5/764/files/2016/10/Responsibility-and-the-Demands-of-Morality-JMP-final-2016-2kmy4j2.pdf) treats answerability itself as a possible burden. Neither establishes blanket exclusion of stranger relief.

Our account must distinguish four effects of protection:

| Effect | Consequence for the disputed ground |
|---|---|
| Permission to decline or a limit on inquiry | Helping can remain justified without being required or continually investigated |
| Exclusion from justifying a particular kind of choice | An otherwise significant consideration may not currently count for that choice; this is more than limited attention |
| Valid termination of a particular commitment | That created ground can end while other grounds remain to be assessed |
| A universal condition requiring adoption before any practical favor | This is the stronger barrier to C2 and still needs its own justification |

The [counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ASSISTANCE-JURISDICTION-COUNTERREVIEW.md) accordingly preserves a genuine possible revision. A warranted choice-specific disqualification could defeat the proposed justification in one implementation even when the means are permitted and the benefit real. An account barring every uncommitted actor from ever choosing for the patient consideration would challenge C2's intended practical content. Retaining the name of a reason would not meet that challenge. Conversely, permission to refuse, protection from constant scrutiny and lack of responsibility for the original burden do not automatically establish either kind of exclusion.

Eligibility must not be defined as “a case in which our preferred reason survives.” Identify the relevant act, history, role or practice independently, then justify any claimed power to disqualify this ground. Assess whether the actual choice falls within its scope and whether the grounds for maintaining that scope hold. Changing the description to “my private choice” or “a rescue” cannot settle the matter. The clean project comparison supplies no established special procedure, but it leaves the general objection from self-direction available for argument.

### Test exclusion against the actual response

The [new fair-allocation comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PATIENT-GROUND-AFTER-FAIR-ALLOCATION.md) distinguishes taking a winner's relief resource, reopening a completed fair draw, and using a fresh independently permissible opportunity to relieve the person who lost. The last act need not undo either the procedure or its result. The affirmative explanation is the change in continuing adversity; a completed chance claim has a different satisfaction condition. A genuine restriction on the original allocation is not automatically a restriction on this new response. This is a positive local defense of patient-grounded favor, with legitimate wider exclusions still open to argument.

[The direct-justification study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DIRECT-GROUNDING-EPISTEMOLOGY.md) examines how a particular assessment could be warranted without a separately believed general bridge. It neither certifies this assessment nor removes the constitutive and universal grounding obligations. Recognition can be investigated within a formed history; its reliability cannot be established by scoring agreement with C2. The earlier invitation case additionally needs warranted evidence about the formable continuation rather than treating anticipated relief as already given.

The [completed local desert comparison](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/PERMITTED-RELIEF-AND-DESERT-INTEGRATION.md) now studies the particular adverse episode claimed as deserved, its history and a permitted relieving continuation together. The affirmative assessment concerns what that response accomplishes for the actual sufferer; the rival claims that desert changes or excludes this patient-directed significance. Neither is settled by permission alone. The study grants exact desert and permission locally and does not yet establish their coexistence in a realized case.

Its source comparison also corrects the earlier binary framing. A suffering component could retain bad-making significance alongside a desert consideration; context could change that component's moral value while remission has value; or patient-directed favor could be genuinely excluded. Valuable remission grounded only in a compassionate motive or special relationship would not establish a reason preceding that motive or relation. A negative desert ranking does not by itself decide among these accounts. These remain substantive tests within the differentiated configuration, not a claim that independently variable formal predicates disprove its proposed unity. The governing nondual formation study below retains priority.

### A practical boundary within the generative account

The developing [nondual reconstruction](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-GROUNDING-QUESTION.md) permits actor, patient and project to name differentiated continuities within one reality. Earlier operational distinctions did not require substantial owners. A formed boundary can protect agency, coordination or sustained activity; its formation alone proves neither authority to exclude reasons nor absence of such authority.

The positive proposal therefore connects actual undergoing, an available transformation and the continuing activity within which it can occur. It seeks to explain both the significance of relief and the legitimate organization of response. The current comparison supports a prospective patient justification without a new helping purpose. The further account of choice-specific protection is a substantive grounding obligation, not an optional label or a derivation already furnished by shared origin.

## The next decisive connection

The boundary/significance investigation closes one reconstruction within **co-formation and consequential revision of practical boundaries, significance and response**. Resource consequences alter what can be rebuilt in the selected game; recipient injury does not teach subsequent revision through its actual continuation. This is a local explanatory omission, not the project's discovery that recipient feedback is universally absent.

The existing [recipient-correction result](RECIPIENT-CORRECTION.md) already carries actual recipient evidence into a separately indexed command without extra private return. [Evaluative succession](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) already carries acquired evaluative influence to a distinct successor, whose fresh evidence corrects its means and informs resource-dependent delivery. These are credited conditional constructions with their own histories. They cannot be silently spliced into the joint-formation game. They also distinguish correction of means under a supplied service arrangement from revision of that arrangement's practical applicability: an inquiry can fulfill a fixed claim while the current policy still recommends another delivery.

The next focused connection is therefore **affected condition and retained consequence → revision of practical inclusion or applicability, with the supports for that revision accounted for in the same history**. Work backward from the existing constructions' supplied target meanings, acquisition routes and application rules before adding machinery. Affected continuity must be represented adequately for the claim; copying a harm label, correcting a command or assuming a benevolent selector cannot alone establish it. This is a provisional question within the nondual investigation, not a mandatory architecture for every kind of care.

Earlier patient-ground and jurisdiction questions remain local tests for developed ethical claims. They no longer determine this branch's governing research order. Formal entailment and premise tracking retain their proper scopes: a result about a representation must not automatically become a verdict about the reality whose richer unfolding the representation leaves out. The actual generative account still owes its laws, experiential identification, distinctions and claimed directions; the no-assumptions requirement is not relaxed or declared complete.

Fix the proposed connection and its scope before comparison. A collection of disconnected histories cannot establish that the claimed relations formed together. A changed proxy or report without the claimed experiential change requires revising that identification. Improved internal regulation with worsened affected continuities requires revising any claim that it established ethical improvement. These are possible revision conditions, not outcomes declared in advance; the relevant evidence and rival explanations still need investigation.

The [relief/reconsideration comparison](#genuine-relief-and-reconsideration-of-its-means) now specializes the participant-capacity test to a response that changes how its own continuation can be evaluated. The next connection is **the response-altered evaluative relation → acquisition and assessment of a relevant consideration → an available authorized continuation in that same history**. Distinguish an actual inability from missing information, constrained options, reasonable refusal and valid delegation. The ground may already exist before it is recognized; acquisition explains its uptake, not necessarily its creation. Universal practical significance and experiential realization remain unresolved.
