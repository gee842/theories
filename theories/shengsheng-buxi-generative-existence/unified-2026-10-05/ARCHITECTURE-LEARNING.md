## Why architecture and evidence must be joined

The architecture determines what can act and what can be encountered through a particular channel. Under an action-dependent observation law, an identity gate that prevents a recipient trial also prevents acquiring that trial's outcome. A missing observation cannot be treated as an observed failure. A favorable later learning rule cannot correct a distinction that never reaches it.

This is a conditional information boundary. Other channels—passive observation, testimony or a separately constructed probe—could provide recipient evidence. They would require their own access, calibration and costs. The present join must not silently add such a bypass to a filtered route.

Once evidence arrives, its target must remain identifiable. A recipient's productive action can differ from the actor's. Replacing one shared estimate after each trial can then make learning about one target spoil the response to the other. The existing recipient-correction theorem establishes the relevant distinguishability requirement and a paid indexed representation. That theorem does not identify the physical recoding chemistry or prove that target labels are calibrated.

The connection being addressed is:

\[
\text{actual formed route}
\to\text{paid target trial}
\to\text{accessible recipient outcome}
\to\text{retained target-specific correction}
\to\text{changed paid response and continued maintenance}.
\]

The actor's private receipt remains distinct from the recipient's outcome. A recipient success and a recipient failure can both give the actor zero. Learning from that private zero can accidentally produce the right first correction after failure and then corrupt the record after success. Behavioral improvement alone therefore does not identify which evidence caused it.

## Evidence can revise a candidate without already proving it

An accessible opportunity pays rent and target sensing before work. A recipient learning trial must also prepay observation and writing through an escrow. This ensures its immediate outcome transaction is funded whether the result is positive, zero or unavailable through the tested channel. Failed admission preserves the earlier sensing expense. A route without a completed retrofit can still perform its inherited read-only response, but acquires no learning evidence.

The faithful channel reports the actual recipient receipt. A positive report records the issued command through the actual coupler and marks the recipient candidate qualified. A zero while unqualified flips the candidate without qualifying it. A zero after qualification leaves the record unchanged. This is a supplied exploration and retention policy, not a general inference that every failure identifies the correct alternative.

For a stable, noiseless binary recipient law, the first failed action followed by a different successful action gives a concrete correction history. The second recipient action uses the revised candidate; its subsequent positive outcome qualifies the record. There is no third recipient action using that newly qualified record in the primary four-opportunity continuation. With uncertain source availability, the first zero is ambiguous. With a corrupted channel, even a positive report can qualify a false candidate. With a later efficacy change, an old qualification can preserve a stale response. Qualification records the controller's acquired status; its warrant depends on the channel and temporal conditions.

Actual recipient receipt increases only the recipient reserve. One stipulated recipient operation is charged afterward. The inherited reserve could already fund that operation; the schedule does not derive a need or prove that this receipt was necessary for activity. Incorrect recipient commands can deplete the recipient's reserve under the declared damage law. None of these recipient outcomes gives actor fuel.

## What this adds to the theory

There are two different ways responsiveness can change. Construction changes which targets and signals can enter an operative route. Learning changes what that route does with the distinctions it can access. Their interaction explains both development and its limits: a broad architecture can learn differentiated responses, while a restricted architecture can prevent the encounters that would expose its limits.

Recipient-specific correction is an operational precursor of understanding another center's conditions. It does not by itself establish experienced compassion, ethical standing or a justified priority among centers. A controller can acquire accurate recipient knowledge and still suppress its use; another can act broadly while relying on an inaccurate transfer. The [ethical foundation](ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) retains the separate grounds of warranted concern.

The model must change if its alleged correction uses inaccessible truth, if it mistakes absent evidence for a negative result, if a recipient write alters the actor's supposedly preserved correspondence, or if an operation uses resources already spent. Empirical identification must further establish that measured signals and action outcomes instantiate the declared relation. An exact conventional controller remains a necessary comparator for the finite law, rather than evidence against ordinary learning theory.
