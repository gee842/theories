# 系统怎样形成其边界与可能性
# Systems, constitutive relationships and possible change

8 October 2026. AI-authored development of [Ren's systems question](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-systems-foundation-question.md), under the [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The question concerns formation, persistence, interaction, transformation and dissolution. It is broader than the formation of an initially separate object. This account develops a substantive answer and tests its boundaries; it does not declare the stronger assumption-free foundation complete.

**A system can be identified as a distinguishable organization of relationships within an unfolding. Its organization matters insofar as it helps explain what can occur together, what can follow, and how those possibilities change.** Such identification must be warranted by the relationships actually present. Choosing a set of variables or calling a collection a system does not establish that warrant.

**系统是在同一展开中可辨认的关系组织。其组织参与说明哪些活动能够共同实现、哪些变化能够接续，以及这些可能性怎样改变。** 边界、组成与延续须由实际关系获得根据；把若干东西圈在一起，或把“生成”写入系统定义，都不完成这个说明。

## 1. Identify the phenomenon before selecting its boundary

Begin with what requires explanation: a held shape, a recurring motion, a coordinated response, a temporary reaction, a developing collective, or the breakdown of one of these. These targets can be specified through observations and operations without first asserting the generative thesis. The investigation then asks which arrangements, interactions and histories account for the identified phenomenon.

Systemhood has a broad descriptive use: a mathematical treatment may group independent processes and still call the result a system. A substantive claim of organized unity has more content. It must identify the relations through which the selected behavior or form occurs. We therefore do not make every legitimate use of “system” satisfy autonomy, feedback, self-maintenance, long duration, indivisibility or a uniquely sharp boundary. Those are further properties to establish where they occur.

This leaves room for differentiated systems without making the whole one organism. Shared participation does not imply shared experience, purpose, control or ethical authority. Conversely, dependence on ongoing support does not make an organization unreal. A locally maintained organization and the processes supporting it belong to the same investigated unfolding, with different contributions.

## 2. Constitutive participation differs from successful prediction

A relationship is constitutive **of a specified organization or capacity** when its operative contribution helps realize that organization or capacity. The attribution needs an account of what participates, how it participates and which conditions support it. No single removal test is universally decisive: redundant routes, compensating changes and destruction of the entire preparation can make such tests misleading. Construction, provenance, operation, interruption and replacement provide complementary evidence.

For example, the [documented controller](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/LAW-CORRESPONDENCE-EVIDENCE.md#1-documented-acquisition-retention-and-issued-action) retains a calculated integral and passes it into the next command calculation. Program order and archived records support that operative connection. A correlated external recording may predict the same command; that agreement alone does not show that the controller reads the recording. Both concern one physical history, but their roles differ.

Consequently two questions must remain distinct:

- Which retained distinctions suffice to answer the selected question?
- Through which actual relationships is the target organization realized?

The first concerns explanatory or predictive sufficiency. The second concerns constitution. An adequate account can use the first to simplify the second, provided it preserves the relationships needed for the claim. It cannot turn a convenient substitute into an actual participant solely through predictive success.

## 3. Boundaries articulate dependencies within the unfolding

A boundary records which organization is being followed and how the description treats its continuing relations with other activity. The relation comes first in the explanation; “inside” and “outside” articulate positions within it. The boundary can be physically realized, analytically selected, or both. A membrane, for example, needs its own explanation of formation, transport and maintenance; the word boundary supplies none of those mechanisms.

Different questions can warrant different extents. A command calculation, a heating arrangement and a laboratory apparatus need not share the same boundary. They can be nested or overlap. Extending a boundary may reveal omitted dependencies; extending it indefinitely may conceal the particular organization whose behavior is being explained. What matters is whether the account preserves the dependencies relevant to its claims.

This does not make every boundary equally good. If two admitted situations look identical within a proposed description but permit different relevant continuations, that description needs an additional distinction, a stated range restriction, or an account that preserves the alternatives. Existing [systems results](SYSTEMS-COMPLEXITY-CHAOS.md#lean-completion-of-the-mathematical-connections) already establish the exact autonomous-reduction criterion. Autonomous prediction remains a stronger property than being an open system.

The new [Lean study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md#2-new-mathematics-and-its-exact-inputs) checks **27 theorems / 41 named declarations**, all with empty axiom dependency lists. It proves when sufficient regions can be intersected and exhibits correlated states with two sufficient regions but no least one. Each coordinate preserves feasibility of every finite event word in the established correlated domain; one fails outside it. Minimality of each region is checked among decidable subregions, while the no-least result is unrestricted. These are results about sufficient descriptions, not a derivation of causal constitution from correlation.

**形式实查：**27项新定理／41项声明的依赖审计全空，另复查18项继承声明。实际可接纳的联合状态决定描述充分性的范围；两个充分区域不必有最小的共同充分区域。此结果不把“能预测”改写成“实际构成”，也不把空公理清单改写成无前提的现实根据。

## 4. How organization generates possibilities

An arrangement makes possibilities concrete through its actual operations and joint conditions. Separately available components or actions need not be jointly usable: their timing, contact, configuration or support can conflict. A formed connection can make a previously unrealizable operation feasible. Loss, obstruction or reorganization can remove it. These claims require identified mechanisms and admissible comparisons; imagining a rearrangement does not establish its physical possibility.

The [existing formed-range construction](DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) derives overlapping capabilities from newly formed connections. The [evolutionary account](EVOLUTIONARY-ORGANIZATION.md) explains how an inherited developmental change alters association and how actual descendants reconstruct it. These are already established conditional mechanisms, not new discoveries produced by the word “system.”

The general explanatory connection is:

> Activity forms or changes an arrangement; the achieved arrangement participates in later operations; those operations preserve, transform or end that organization and can form further arrangements.

Its force comes from tracing the continuing operative relations. It does not require a second law outside the process to connect formation to use. Neither does it infer use from mere earlier existence. A discarded or overwritten arrangement may cease to matter. An organization can end after a single operation. Stable organization can maintain a capacity without increasing its repertoire.

“Generates possibilities” therefore concerns **realizability from an achieved organization**, not a guarantee that possibilities grow, that a possible continuation occurs, or that its occurrence is good. A fixed encompassing law can describe changes in local organization and capability. A supported whole-history description can express the same relations without treating sequential notation as uniquely fundamental.

## 5. The systems question changes the foundation's presentation

The core should articulate **formation and transformation of relational organization and its possibilities**, including persistence and dissolution. The tree investigates the different relations through which this happens. A branch can reveal that a proposed summary loses a constitutive dependency, that a boundary combines different centers, or that a supposed common mechanism is absent. Those findings revise the core's articulation.

This is a reciprocal explanatory relationship. The general account helps connect particular investigations; the investigations determine what its general statements are entitled to say. Agreement with the vocabulary is not an independent test. An equally adequate ecological, developmental, dynamical or whole-history account receives credit for preserving the same relationships.

**核心与生成树是同一展开的不同说明。** 系统研究使一般表述接受具体关系的检验：哪些边界成立，哪些摘要失真，哪些组织依靠外部维持，哪些改变生成新的能力，哪些改变导致终结。共同用语不代替对应根据，也不赋予整体一个统一主体或唯一目的。

## 6. What would change this account

| Proposed explanation | Substantive test and possible revision |
|---|---|
| The selected parts constitute one coordinated organization | Test actual coupling and common support. If a common driver accounts for the coordination without the alleged internal coupling, revise the attributed relation and boundary. |
| A retained effect participates later | Examine actual uptake, overwrite, bypass and replacement. Mere retention or later correlation is insufficient. |
| A reduced description preserves relevant possibilities | Compare admitted situations sharing the description. A difference in the selected possibility requires more information, a narrower scope, or retained uncertainty. |
| The same system persists through change | Track the claimed continuity of organization and provenance. Similar replacement and actual continuation need not answer the same identity question. |
| Formation increases capability | Identify the newly feasible joint continuation and its supporting history, retaining lost capabilities and costs. More components alone do not establish an increase. |
| The explanation applies generally | Establish its content and warrant at the proposed scope. Defining every admitted case as already generative would evade this test. |

These tests do not impose one architecture on all systems. Static, transient, externally maintained, redundant and destructive organizations are legitimate cases. Their differences determine what can be claimed.

## 7. Completion and the stronger question

This answers the systems question with a connected explanatory candidate: identify organization through warranted relations; account for boundaries through the dependencies being followed; explain possibility and change through actual formation, operation and transformation. The [argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-ARGUMENT.md), [formal study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-FORMAL.md) and [independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-REVIEW.md) record its warrant and limits. The new mathematical contribution and checked scope are recorded in the [integration](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION-INTEGRATION.md).

The remaining stronger obligation is precise: **warrant the actual relationships, the range of admissible variation and the recognition/inference used to attribute them throughout the intended foundational scope.** “It is a system” supplies none of these automatically. This is not a demand to inspect every possible case, derive every physical constant, find one unique boundary, or reject equivalent formulations. A warranted general argument could answer it. The present systems formulation and checked consequences do not yet provide that argument.

No new foundational axiom is adopted. Mathematical definitions and explicitly conditional results are not relabeled an assumption-free foundation. The completed conditional foundation and separate G0 ethical route retain their scopes. No impossibility theorem about the stronger target is asserted.
