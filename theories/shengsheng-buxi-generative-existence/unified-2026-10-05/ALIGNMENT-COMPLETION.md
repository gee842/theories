## What would change the account

A valid counterexample within an accepted theorem’s stated premises would reopen that result. An empirical mismatch can instead defeat its mapping or a supplied local law without refuting the formal implication. An unpriced resource reset, changed beneficiary meaning or inaccessible evidence would defeat a claimed composition. A coherent exploitative survivor refutes an automatic favorable-direction inference; it is compatible with an explanatory account that includes destructive regimes.

For concern, an acquired response that depends entirely on an unmeasured private consequence does not identify newly acquired other-directed evaluation. For justified action, a sound means conclusion may remain true after its application expires. For governance, profitable continuation or a nominal appeal does not establish legitimate authority or delivered correction. Failed comparisons, unknown outcomes, noncallers and unrepresented harms must remain in the account.

Conversely, independent evidence that a declared mechanism supplies a missing input should close that local obligation. The five conditions do not require a uniquely necessary metaphysical vocabulary, unanimous ethical agreement, all capacities in one carrier, or an unlimited regress of new selectors and monitors. Those stronger claims need evidence only if the theory continues to assert them.
