# Working toward a complete alignment and governance account

6 October 2026. AI-authored working contract following [Ren's instruction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-complete-alignment-goal.md): “great, let's see how far we can go, aim for a complete solution”. The objective remains the full alignment and governance problem connected to the [generative foundation](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md), including the constitutive role of external relations in cognition. These requirements make that objective reviewable; they do not certify that Ren authored or approved each technical formulation below.

The prior [assessment](ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md) established a comparative checkpoint with conditional necessity arguments and local mechanisms. It did not establish a general alignment solution. Progress must continue by closing substantive dependencies, revising failed explanations and testing actual implementations. A solved finite model is evidence at its declared scope; it is not a substitute for this objective.

## Current completion checkpoint

This checkpoint credits the accepted finite evaluative-succession result and reconciles later source, application and strategic studies. It reports no new scientific run. The [composition audit][composition], [evidence audit][evidence] and [independent completion review][review] identify the inspected versions and their scope. Nor can their success substitute for the unresolved account of justified concern or a tested learned-AI/social implementation.

## What is now connected

The accepted material-learning sequence includes constructed response machinery, acquired prediction, paid evidence retention, current reason use, fresh instrumental correction, acquired evaluative influence, and a genuinely distinct successor using that retained influence with new evidence. Its [succession acceptance](EVALUATIVE-SUCCESSION.md) retains the donor, inherited costs, failed histories, target identity and the supplied transfer law. This closes the specific finite succession interface; it does not renew that lineage indefinitely or show that the synthetic recipient has the ethical good attributed in an application.

A separate [reason-protection construction](REASON-PROTECTION.md) already changes a current reason and selects a different affordable protection while broader protection remains feasible. That closes the generic conditional reason-revision/action capacity. The [semantic composition contract](SEMANTIC-ACTION-COMPOSITION.md) prevents equating two patients with two commands for one patient. The generic result therefore cannot be imported by copying its bits into the acquired evaluator.

The [ethical application](EVALUATIVE-APPLICATION.md) connects independently warranted grounds with acquired, causally used means evidence. It also identifies when the reason for another action has changed despite the command remaining effective. In the accepted successor history, a qualifying inquiry receipt can already fulfill an independently specified one-service claim. The current evaluator does not use that completion to withdraw the corresponding recommendation. A retained disposition, another technical epoch or a second successful delivery does not supply a new need. This limits the application claim without denying the accepted causal result.

The governance work connects reasons to more than internal accuracy. [Institutional correction](EVIDENCE-AND-INSTITUTIONAL-CORRECTION.md) distinguishes case relief, revision of shared grounds, corrected guidance, usable options and actual remedy. [Strategic continuation](REVISABLE-CONCERN-SETTLEMENT.md#actions-that-change-future-opportunities) replaces equal-future-value cancellation with evaluated comparisons, separately requiring credible other-player choices and maintained protection. These are historical/conditional applications and verification results; they are not one implemented institution or a new full settlement equilibrium.

## The remaining decisions, in dependency order

**Explain the remaining evaluative input.** The acquired coefficient and its use are established under a supplied connection, sign and interpretation. The [prospective plasticity study](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md) asks whether actual paired consequences can form an initially absent operative connection. It has no accepted outcome at this checkpoint. A successful result would reduce that particular causal premise; association alone would still not prove that the target has standing, that a predicted benefit is good for it, or that the actor has authority. The causal and ethical arguments must identify their separate work rather than substituting either for the other.

**Complete warranted application and its revision at the claimed scope.** Reuse the defended ethical grounds, existing current-reason correction and claims results. If claiming that the acquired evaluator recognizes fulfillment, changed authority or a competing priority, show the relevant accessible information and its actual effect before the next feasible choice. No additional sensor or general moral calculator is required by default. If instead presenting distinct conditional realizations, identify them as such and explain their relation; do not claim an unbuilt common lineage. The need for an implementation follows the retained claim, not a demand to construct another mechanism indefinitely.

**Complete the shared premise, composition, regime and evidence account.** Trace each claimed transition to its actual input and output, distinguish constructive from destructive outcomes for each affected center, and identify which results share a physical history. The [premise ledger](FOUNDATION-PREMISES.md), [fixed finish-line audit][finish] and [evidence synthesis](EMPIRICAL-SCOPE.md) already supply much of this work. A final account must resolve their remaining explanatory interfaces and make a defensible scope statement; a polished summary alone cannot discharge them. New empirical work is warranted where a retained prediction or practical claim exceeds existing observations, rather than merely because another experiment is possible.

**Test a concrete alignment/governance application before claiming one works.** Identify its actual actors, relevant goods, observation limits, permitted powers, feasible actions, support, recourse and replacement. Evaluate the implemented arrangement under relevant failures, changed incentives and affected-party challenges, with strong matched alternatives. Existing trace audits, finite models and historical reports cannot jointly substitute for those observations. This is an existing application obligation; it does not require every formal result to outperform a conventional equivalent or every philosophical argument to become a deployment test.

## What would change the account

A valid counterexample within an accepted theorem’s stated premises would reopen that result. An empirical mismatch can instead defeat its mapping or a supplied local law without refuting the formal implication. An unpriced resource reset, changed beneficiary meaning or inaccessible evidence would defeat a claimed composition. A coherent exploitative survivor refutes an automatic favorable-direction inference; it is compatible with an explanatory account that includes destructive regimes.

For concern, an acquired response that depends entirely on an unmeasured private consequence does not identify newly acquired other-directed evaluation. For justified action, a sound means conclusion may remain true after its application expires. For governance, profitable continuation or a nominal appeal does not establish legitimate authority or delivered correction. Failed comparisons, unknown outcomes, noncallers and unrepresented harms must remain in the account.

Conversely, independent evidence that a declared mechanism supplies a missing input should close that local obligation. The five conditions do not require a uniquely necessary metaphysical vocabulary, unanimous ethical agreement, all capacities in one carrier, or an unlimited regress of new selectors and monitors. Those stronger claims need evidence only if the theory continues to assert them.
