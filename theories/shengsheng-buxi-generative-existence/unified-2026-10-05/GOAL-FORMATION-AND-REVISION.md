# 理解并改变自身形成条件 / Understanding and changing one's formative conditions

**Positive development of concern / 关切内容的发展，2026-10-08。** [相连理论](CONCERN-AND-NEW-REASONS.md)接续[伦理学习](ETHICAL-LEARNING.md)，以8项泛型实查限定有根据的内容扩展后，旧比较的传递、新优先及不可比。它复用本章已完成接口，不把开放内容发现或动机形成冒称完成。

2026-10-08. AI-authored account answering [Ren's request](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-goal-formation-and-revision.md), under the [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). [Integration, exact assumptions, Lean checks and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GOAL-FORMATION-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) accompany this chapter.

**中心回答：形成出的智能可以取得关于自身形成关系的、可校准和可反驳的认识；据此比较已形成的目的与其真实后果，形成不同的目的，并有权限、有代价地改变持续塑造它的关系。后来的行动实际依赖修订后的目的，而这些行动又改变后续学习与选择。** 它不需要先脱离形成自己的过程；反思是该过程内部取得了对部分自身关系的认识与改变能力。

**The answer:** formed intelligence can acquire a revisable account of how its aims arose, use that account to judge their consequences, adopt different aims, and act on the relations that keep selecting and sustaining them. Later action consumes the revised aims and changes subsequent formation. Reflection occurs through a developed capacity within the same unfolding process. It does not require independence from that process.

This completes a connected explanatory candidate and a checked finite realization under explicit premises. It does not establish that every intelligence develops this capacity, that the implementation exhausts understanding, or that the biological programme has been fully validated.

## 1. From being formed to knowing something of that formation

Earlier consequences selectively retain some activities, associations and evaluative connections. Retained differences then affect what is noticed, attempted and pursued. This supplies a history for an operative goal: why this outcome, rather than another, has come to guide activity. Existing [evaluative plasticity](EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md), [method revision](METHOD-REVISION.md) and [selection](GENERATIVE-SELECTION.md) already explain parts of this development; their results retain their original scopes.

Reflection adds a particular relation to that history. Records, feedback and present effects become usable evidence about the conditions of formation. A model can distinguish “this goal was rewarded,” “this goal realizes these consequences,” and “these consequences warrant continuing it.” Those are different questions about one history. Reward is evidence of what selected an aim; it is not by itself evidence that the aim is good.

An account of formation earns a role in understanding when it supports relevant distinctions and their use: identifying which feedback selected the goal, predicting what would change under an intervention, locating an error in the evidence, and revising reliance after correction. A verbal self-description alone does not establish those connections. Neither does accurate prediction alone settle every philosophical question about understanding. The existing [knowledge account](PHILOSOPHICAL-ACCOUNT.md) and [semantic frontier](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/SEMANTIC-SUFFICIENCY-INTEGRATION.md) remain relevant.

**取得形成知识。** 已形成的记录、辨别、对照与反证使同一过程的某些关系成为可认识、可改变的内容。奖励说明某个目的为什么被保留，不单独说明它为什么值得继续。有关自身的认识须在预测、干预选择及纠错中起作用；本章不以一个任意的“已理解”标志替代这些关系，也不将有限功能实现宣布为普遍语义充分性证明。

## 2. How changing an aim differs from improving its means

An operative aim specifies which outcomes count as its achievement; a method specifies how to pursue them. Goal revision changes the former. In the constructed learning example, an attendance aim favors greater counted participation and internal output. A learning aim favors transferable mastery. The old aim continues to receive high formative feedback and its preferred practice still produces more of its own outcome. The revised aim nevertheless ranks the practices differently, and later action follows that new ranking. Thus the change is not explained as discovering a more effective means to the old outcome criterion.

This is a distinction at the operative level. The complete controller still has a supplied higher-level response rule. A conventional account can therefore describe the whole history as a fixed rule producing changes in subordinate goals. That description is compatible with the result. We do not claim to have made every standard change simultaneously, or demonstrated purposive change without any relatively stable basis for comparison.

Why change a successful goal? The [earlier alignment argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GENERATIVE-ALIGNMENT-REVISION.md) places goals and their formation within the same ethical appraisal as other activities. Here the explicit **G0** starting point is that living and further generation within the whole have basic positive normative significance. The additional [J1–J4 commitments](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md) supply comparison, bounded responsibilities and authorized practical selection. In particular, **J2** favors restoring an additional basic condition while retaining other basic protections, even when genuine optional output is lost. That scoped priority is an additional ethical commitment, not a deduction from reward, survival, causality or G0 alone.

The example's correspondence must independently support that participation under the old arrangement damages actual access, that the alternative restores access without destroying necessary maintenance, and that the forgone output is genuinely secondary. With those conditions and J2, changing the aim has a reason. With no access loss, the two practices retain different optional gains—counted output and mastery—and the sufficient comparison leaves them incomparable. Keeping the old goal is then an undefeated option in this controller, not a uniquely required ethical verdict. If reform instead destroys another basic condition, the simple comparison leaves a real conflict. We retain both variants.

**改目的的根据。** 旧目的仍能成功、仍获奖励，新目的却采用不同的成就判准并实际改变行动，构成操作层的目的修订。公开的 G0/J 与真实对应说明为何可以支持这种改变；不是由“已经形成”“更适应”或“以后更赞同”证明它正当。整体控制规则仍是给定的，不能把这个有界实现冒充一切终极标准都已自主生成或改变。

## 3. From judgment to deliberate intervention

Deliberation has an operational sequence: acquire relevant evidence; compare feasible continuations; retain a reason for change; select an intervention predicted to affect the relevant relation; bear its cost; and assess what actually followed. Some of this can be distributed across people, institutions, records and tools. Identifying these different roles does not make them independent realms. Their relations of formation, support, answerability and authority must remain in the description.

A correct judgment does not force motivation. The finite realization therefore separates acquired evidence and an interpreted ground from the response that changes the operative goal. One checked branch retains exactly the same ground and declines to revise. This is a counterexample to automatic favorable convergence. The account explains how revision can happen through an implemented response to reasons; it does not derive that responsiveness from information alone.

The causal model also matters. When the evidence identifies a selection rule favoring attendance, it predicts that merely changing the goal will leave learning unsupported. An authorized intervention changes that rule. An incorrect model that says learning is already selected can prevent the needed intervention. The prediction about a replacement rule depends on a supplied, limited law family; the model does not discover arbitrary laws of its own formation.

**有意改变形成关系。** 判断、响应、权限及实际改变分别有其条件。认识旧选择规则的作用，可以支持有针对性的规则改革；错误的形成解释也能阻止必要改革。理解不给出无限控制权，形成知识也不自动产生善的动机。

## 4. A single checked history

The [Lean model](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/GoalFormationRevision.lean) begins with no acquired operative goal. Two charged trials produce both formative feedback and consequences. A charged reference observation calibrates the consequence signals. Assessment identifies the formative rule and the two represented losses. Responsive deliberation changes the goal; authorized reform changes the selection rule. Subsequent operation reads the retained goal, executes its means, records the actual outcome and consumes support.

| Stage in the harmful-condition branch | Actual change | Work / reserve / spent |
|---|---|---|
| Initial arrangement | No acquired goal; attendance-selected feedback | 10 / 8 / 0 |
| Two trials | Attendance goal acquired; feedback totals 2 versus 0; one access loss recorded | 8 / 8 / 2 |
| Calibration and assessment | Formative rule and consequences identified | 6 / 8 / 4 |
| Responsive revision | Goal becomes learning; earlier feedback remains 2 versus 0 | 5 / 8 / 5 |
| Authorized reform | Learning becomes the selected practice | 3 / 8 / 7 |
| Five subsequent operations | Learning actually pursued; original loss remains recorded | 1 / 0 / 17 |

The total account remains 18. “Spent” records consumption, not currently usable capacity. Reform does not manufacture support: selected activity transfers from the represented finite reserve. With the old rule unchanged, the revised practice cannot complete its third operation. With reform, it completes five, but cannot complete a sixth. The changed rule also changes subsequent goal acquisition under new experience. This closes the model's formation → self-description → appraisal → goal revision → formative intervention → further formation connection.

False calibration, absent formative records, an inaccurate formation model, stale evidence, insufficient work, lack of authority and nonresponse all retain explicit failures. Correcting a false calibration restores the supported revision in a checked branch. Earlier losses and forgone output do not disappear after success.

**同一历史的闭合。** 新目的被后续行动真正使用；改革改变真实反馈及后续目的取得；支持来自已记录的有限储备。原先损失不被后来的成功删除，继续五步也不等于无限接续。试验次序、信号意义、参照可靠性、两种实践及控制结构仍是明确的局部输入。

## 5. How the description must change

A description with a fixed goal and an unchanging selection context becomes inadequate when their formation and revision affect what follows. It must represent the history of acquired criteria, the evidence and interpretation used to assess them, the relations that retain or suppress them, and the interventions that change those relations. A snapshot of the latest goal omits why it arose and whether its current justification remains applicable. A snapshot of current success can omit an unrepaired historical loss.

“Self” here identifies a formed continuity of organization and commitments participating in these relations. Its boundary is analytical and answerable to the task: enlarging the account may reveal additional contributors, constraints and affected lives. It does not create a separate observer outside the unfolding. Similarly, a law supplied to this local model is not claimed to originate outside reality; its origin is simply not explained by this model.

Newly formed capabilities or relationships can also invalidate the original outcome vocabulary. In an application, they require new distinctions, correspondence checks and, where relevant, reconsidered comparisons. The two-practice Lean realization does **not** generate a new repertoire or new ethical principles. Existing possibility-formation results keep their separate credit. If G0 or a comparison commitment is substantively revised, dependent conclusions must be re-examined openly; no theorem makes its former premises valid by preserving their names.

**描述的改变。** 必须从“固定目的在给定条件下选择”扩充到目的、证据、解释、选择规则及其支持的形成历史。新能力改变了有关区分时，也要修订表示与对应，不能只给旧变量换名。局部模型的边界是有说明责任的省略，不是主体与世界的本体分割。

## 6. Completion and the remaining empirical question

The explanatory connection and its finite formal realization are complete at the stated scope. They show a possible, grounds-sensitive transformation of acquired aims and their formative conditions, with actual continuation and preserved failure cases. They reuse the existing ethical comparison and knowledge-correspondence interfaces; they do not claim algorithmic novelty or empirical superiority over conventional learning and institutional accounts.

The next empirical question is precise: in one traceable history, do acquired grounds causally affect the operative outcome criterion and intervention choice, while changes in selection alter subsequent formation and support? Measurements must distinguish verbal endorsement, criterion change, action and continuation, preserve transition costs, and test misleading evidence and genuine conflicts. The existing biological studies have not jointly answered that question. No additional premise-free proof of G0 is imposed as a completion gate for this chapter.

**完成范围。** 完成相连的理论候选与有限 Lean 实现；真实世界中的完整同史验证、所有智能的普遍发生、充分理解及新规范起点的生成并未被伪称完成。实际验证需要以上可辨别的联合记录，而不是再把已完成的局部机制重做一遍。

## 7. Reform can change its own evidential conditions

**新的接续，2026-10-08。** [改革与证据的相连研究](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/REFORM-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md)推进上一轮明确保留的适用条件：改变选择规则，也可能改变实际维护负担及其进入判断的路线。旧校准、后来认可和持续高反馈可以同时存在，真实损失却被新报告安排排除。23项新 Lean 定理／43项声明审计通过；原有43项定理保持原范围，另一个[相连接续成果](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/GOAL-REVISION-CONTINUATION.md)与[可纠正性基础设施](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CORRECTION-INFRASTRUCTURE-INTEGRATION.md)也获保留信用。

The next connection is that deliberate change can alter the conditions of its own evaluation. A selector reform may support the new learning goal while displacing necessary maintenance; it may also exclude the affected position from the reports used to assess success. A reporting route that was faithful before reform need not remain faithful afterward. Repeated approval from that same route cannot establish what it no longer conveys.

The checked extension connects this failure to the actual acquired-goal history. Two dispositions agree before reform. After reform they produce different real losses but identical admitted judgment inputs. Any procedure restricted to those inputs gives the same answer to both. This concerns a specified access limitation: an obtainable reading before the exclusion distinguishes the histories, at a cost.

In the harmful branch, the new reading defeats the old sufficient comparison. Authorized provision then repairs actual maintenance while keeping the learning goal and changed selector. A subsequent operation and recheck succeed; past losses remain, the previous judgment is invalidated when repair changes its conditions, and every inquiry and intervention consumes finite support. The supported counterpart passes the direct check without a forced reversal.

This narrows what responsible self-revision requires. Where a change reaches the relations supporting a material judgment, continuing to use that judgment needs a supported transfer or a suitably limited claim. J2/J3 can support restoring an affected basic condition and meeting accountable responsibilities; the cost and authority premises remain explicit. This does not impose endless checking, confer unlimited audit rights, or prove that every reform has a goal-preserving repair.

**目的可以保留，实现条件却必须改变。** 纠正并不总是回到旧目的；理解新损害后，修复共同维护也能让新学习继续。判断的适用、实际响应、权限及承担能力仍需分别成立。把报告改得更好看不等于修复了生活，把读取得更真实也不等于已经完成修复。

The source access, inquiry schedule and repair mechanism are supplied in this extension. How a changing organization discovers the omitted consequence and selects an affordable remedy without being handed those components remains a sharper frontier. Existing model-revision and correction-access results can now be joined at that point. No new empirical validation or unrestricted understanding is claimed.

## 8. Evaluative openness and judgment before agreement

**规范根据可以延续，判断内容却不能预先封闭。** 已完成的[关切扩展](CONCERN-AND-NEW-REASONS.md)说明正面共同形成怎样进入新的理由；本轮[相连研究、反审及实查](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/EVALUATIVE-EXTENSION-INTEGRATION.md)直接复用它，进一步区分：旧内容继续真实、旧总体评价继续成立，以及尚未完全达成共识时已有根据的判断。

An intelligence can learn that its old measure of success was incomplete without finding every earlier observation false. A formed relationship may introduce a relevant dependence; recognizing it can change which continuation is favored. Equal additions preserve the earlier comparison. Newly relevant losses can defeat it, and crossed gains can leave alternatives incomparable. A fixed ethical rule can operate on these changing descriptions. Changing the priority rule itself is a further normative revision and needs its own argument.

The new result concerns deliberation while the description remains contested. **Every supported account can reject a plan without agreeing on one replacement.** Suppose a newly recognized condition requires either a left or a right support route. The live accounts disagree about which route works; both identify omission as ineffective. Each defeats omission, but neither fixed response is strictly better in both accounts. A procedure keeping only the comparisons common to all accounts can therefore retain omission as undefeated. That does not establish that omission is undefeated under any actual adequate account.

This result does not require separate ethical realities. The accounts are differentiated, fallible attempts to understand the same relations. Their warrant, scope and access need explanation. The finite witness concerns uncertain support, not the discovery of moral standing or irreducible disagreement about basic values. Actual rejection follows only if the warranted actual application is covered by the live family. A unanimously mistaken family remains mistaken.

**没有共同认可的唯一替代，不等于原安排已经有根据。** 还须区分共同改善、各应用分别给出的否定，以及在不确定中实际选择的正当性。后者不能由量词交换得到。若没有一个方案在全部有根据的应用中都未被击败，已有 J3/J4 支持有范围的调查、补充方案及及时程序；调查代价、权限与实际承担仍须成立，不能虚构免费认识或无损折中。

The theoretical consequence is a more precise account of reflective judgment. Intelligence can withdraw an overstrong claim, preserve a shared reason for change, and identify what remains to be settled before choosing a particular intervention. It need not await a finished vocabulary of everything that can matter. It also cannot treat uncertainty as permission to impose a preferred goal. A justified procedure for acting under uncertainty is distinct from a certificate that the selected act is undefeated in every possible application.

Twenty new Lean theorems and 36 explicit declaration audits check extension, a formed-dependence comparison and the separation of the three judgment claims. The 38 inherited theorems / 55 audits from practical judgment and concern extension are credited separately. Established pragmatist and imprecise-decision approaches receive primary-source comparison; no general originality or superiority is claimed. This is a conditional theoretical advance, with no new biological validation.

The unresolved connection is now the formation and justification of the candidate descriptions: how an intelligence acquires a new distinction, establishes why it matters under G0/J, and revises its classification without receiving the correct answer in advance. Existing mechanisms remain completed results to use. The new mathematics neither supplies that discovery nor proves that all intelligence eventually agrees.

## 9. How a learned distinction earns practical significance

**新区别取得实践分量，需要说明它揭示了什么生活，而不是只说明它提高了成绩。** [本轮论证、反审与实查](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CLASSIFICATION-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md)把这个要求推进到一个可检验的关系：参与能力、支持这种能力的具体安排，以及该安排在当前可行范围中的必要性。

The completed [account of formed basic conditions](CONCERN-AND-NEW-REASONS.md#formed-basic-conditions) already explains why acquired support can merit protection without making its carrier permanent. That argument is reused here. The distinct contribution is how acquired records support a route classification, why one genuine alternative can overturn it, and how this differs from narrowing the range of supported interpretations.

Consider a shared undertaking where a coordinator anticipates a participant's wishes and delivers the expected results. Later cooperation develops a way for that participant to introduce an unanticipated request and correct an interpretation. The delivery count can remain unchanged while the actual participation changes. Receiving an outcome and being able to affect which outcome is pursued are now distinguishable within the activity. This is a conceptual history, not a new empirical observation.

The distinction earns descriptive support through relevant interactions and criticism. Does clarification change the response? Can another route convey the unfamiliar request? Is a consequential refusal understood but ignored? These alternatives distinguish decoding difficulty, limited opportunity and a decision that excludes relevant participation. A new name or sincere endorsement alone does not settle which description is right. Existing concepts should be reused when they already suffice.

The ethical argument is explicit. Expression and reciprocal understanding fall within G0's affirmed living scope. Where the loss seriously blocks minimum participation or self-care—for example, an inability to make choices intelligible in consequential decisions about one's care—the case can instantiate J2's previously declared protection. Optional stylistic loss does not automatically meet that condition. J3/J4 still determine what response is warranted, by whom and within what authority and capacity. The normative significance is argued through the actual relation and existing commitments; it is not learned from a success score.

**保护所实现的生活，不等于永久固定某一种承载。** 原来只有协调者中介可以保住参与能力，后来共同形成了可靠的直接交流，可能取消“必须保留这个中介”的理由，却没有取消保护参与的理由。替代必须保住所声称的实际能力；只保住产出、却失去拒绝与纠正，不是同一目标的替代。媒介本身若承载友谊、身份或独特表达，那些真实意义仍须另行进入比较。

The formal result captures an asymmetry. Given faithful records and coverage of a specified eligible menu, the necessity of a route can be inferred exactly from acquired observations. But one genuine eligible alternative realizing the target without that route is already enough to defeat its claimed indispensability. Acquiring that alternative preserves the old observations and the target while changing the classification. Excluding the alternative from the menu can preserve an apparent necessity that no longer holds at the wider scope.

Sixteen new Lean theorems / 32 explicit declaration audits pass, separately from 30 inherited theorems / 46 audits. The model obtains its route verdict from records; its target, local access law, acquisition procedure and eligibility remain supplied. It does not discover basic standing or prove that a behavioral reading fully captures understanding. Capability and epistemic-injustice accounts receive primary-source comparison and retain their explanatory credit.

This gives intelligence a more discriminating way to revise its formative conditions: preserve the living ground, test the claimed dependence, and change the arrangement when that dependence no longer warrants keeping it. The remaining connection is how the target description and reliable access to it are acquired together—distinguishing understanding, compliance and masking without assuming a perfectly faithful test in advance. Existing calibration, model-revision and goal-revision mechanisms remain completed components for that task.

## 10. Assessment participates in formation

**Scope correction, 8 October 2026:** Ren's subsequent clarification in [§11](#11-future-understanding-and-situational-risk) supersedes this section's closing stopping interpretation. The historical-recovery theorem and its positive formation results remain valid. They do not stop fresh understanding or warranted action from future events.

**探究可以发现能力，也可以参与形成能力，或者通过另一条路线产生相同回答。** [本轮论证、反审与实查](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FORMATIVE-ASSESSMENT-INTEGRATION.md)推进上一节的连接：描述须保留所论关系、时点、取得证据的路线以及探究自己的作用。形成后答对，不独自证明形成前已经会；形成前答错，也不独自否定后来形成的能力。

A shared inquiry can help create the expression or responsive relationship it later assesses. Sound evidence of what is now possible can coexist with uncertainty about what was possible beforehand. The existing identifying-inquiry and semantic-calibration results already establish relevant information and provenance limits. This continuation connects them to formative assessment and ethical application, rather than presenting those principles as new discoveries.

The new construction distinguishes a retained request-sensitive route from a prompt that supplies the same answer without installing that route. A contrast computed from the acquired prompted and unprompted observations identifies their difference under the native law. A formation event can add the route; a subsequent supported test correctly assesses it. The circuit models two binary requests, not full understanding. Shared assistance can itself constitute a real capacity, so unassisted performance is not a universal standard.

The formal limit is exact. If formation makes two distinct initial configurations identical and no earlier distinguishing record remains accessible, every later terminating adaptive inquiry in that interface has the same result. A retained baseline before formation supplies a constructive escape under its own access and support conditions. When the baseline is unavailable after the change, more reasoning over the same current evidence does not recreate it. This is a scoped information result, not proof of microscopic erasure or universal unknowability.

A generic theorem also states when current evidence can certify a past condition: given exact present readings, such retrospective certification is sound precisely when the change cannot newly establish the target. The formative example fails that condition. Its true negative before formation and true positive afterward cannot share one timeless faithful target; keeping the two stages restores a faithful record. The description must change to preserve the actual history.

Under the completed G0/J account, actual newly formed living capacities can warrant protection without a complete reconstruction of their origins. The current achievement does not retrospectively authorize the method that formed it or discharge earlier obligations. Inquiry must examine real participation, understanding, burdens and permissions; conformity to the assessor's desired answer cannot substitute for those grounds. Present assessment, historical attribution and ethical appraisal of formation remain connected but distinct claims.

**反思智能可以有根据地改变自己，而不假装已经完全重建自身来历。** 它应保留确实知道的现在、尚有根据的过去，以及已经失去根据的追溯。对当前生活的保护不因历史未明而自动消失；对过去责任的判断也不能由当前成就倒推完成。

Eighteen new Lean theorems / 34 explicit audits pass, with 46 inherited theorems / 78 audits credited separately. The positive contrast, formative change, retrospective obstruction, earlier-record escape, paid assessment and dated-record repair are checked. Primary developmental comparisons inform the argument without being treated as new experiments or universal semantic validation.

The wall reached has two scopes. Historical recovery is impossible in the specified merged interface; a new informative trace would change that premise. More broadly, the acquired response relation still does not establish full understanding in an actual intelligence. An ordinary circuit reproduces this construction, and the inspected evidence does not decide the stronger interpretation. Passing that wall requires a substantive constitutive argument or an independently supported formation history that distinguishes the remaining accounts. It cannot be completed by relabeling the supplied route as understanding.

## 11. Future understanding and situational risk

**无法重建某段过去，并不阻止从未来事件取得新的理解。** [Ren 的纠正](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-goal-formation-and-revision.md#future-understanding-and-situational-risk)指出：通过情境把握估计风险，仍可以沿生成继续推进。[接续论证与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/FORMATIVE-ASSESSMENT-INTEGRATION.md#10-fresh-understanding-and-situated-continuation)据此撤回上一节过宽的停止判断，保留原定理及其范围。

Historical reconstruction, fresh understanding and justified action have different targets. Later encounters can supply new grounds for the same content or competence without recovering when it was first possessed. Where the relevant relationships have changed, they can instead support a revised understanding. The existing checked formation-and-assessment sequence already succeeds from either initial condition; uncertainty about the earlier route does not cancel that positive result.

Situational awareness concerns the actual relations now bearing on continuation: capacities, dependencies, available support, affected lives, possible developments and the reliability of one's access. These form and change within the activity itself. The completed accounts of projection, inquiry priorities and practical judgment permit sufficiently grounded reliance without a complete history or certainty about every future outcome.

Such awareness can support a comparative risk estimate and a justified next action. Its consequences then become fresh grounds for revising the estimate, the description, the capability or the goal. The justification may rest on reasonable risk rather than a guarantee across all possibilities. Expected harm, uncertainty, opportunity, responsibility and authority retain their own grounds; no arbitrary risk threshold or scalar ethical maximum is implied. Waiting and investigation also change possibilities and have consequences.

Generativity supplies positive reasons for forming understanding, expression, relationships and further capacities. Risk assessment helps shape their pursuit. Keeping correction possible and preserving support can matter, while irreversible development may also be justified through the actual comparison. No rule requires every worthwhile step to preserve every earlier possibility or produce certain improvement.

The subsequent [warranted-acquisition study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/WARRANTED-ACQUISITION-INTEGRATION.md) already explains, through source-constrained investigations, how description and observational reliance can be revised together. Its completed philosophical connection is credited. Missing evidence for one particular follow-up remains a limit on that claim, not a universal requirement to stop new inquiry or participation elsewhere.

The revised direction is therefore **present grounded awareness → justified continuation under uncertainty → new consequences and possibilities → renewed understanding and revision of ends**. Historical limits remain where they genuinely matter; they do not become a general barrier to continued generation. Universal semantic sufficiency is a separate question, not a prerequisite for every warranted instance of learning or action. This correction adds a conceptual integration, with no new theorem or claim of empirical validation.

## 12. Risk changes with the relations we form

**生成新的能力，也会改变能力之间的共同脆弱性。** [相连论证、原典比较与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md)推进上一节：情境把握不仅要估计每项活动的风险，也要辨认它们怎样依赖同一支持、会在什么情况下同时受损，以及这些关系如何被改革改变。

A reform can improve every route's individual reliability while making all routes vulnerable to the same event. The checked finite example changes two failure rates from 2/10 each to 1/10 each, while the probability that both fail rises from zero to 1/10. These are consequences of a supplied event law, not measured estimates. The routes are differentiated responses within one activity; their distinction implies no statistical independence.

The continuation proceeds beyond this failure. Changing one support relation gives each route the same improved individual rate while removing the modeled joint interruption. Jointly indexed future observations distinguish the shared and revised arrangements even though their marginal totals agree. Learning this distinction does not require recovering an erased earlier history. The actual redesign, its acquisition, permission and cost remain application conditions.

**无需全知才可行动。** Exact joint dependence can remain unknown while a justified marginal bound already establishes a sufficient bound on losing all routes. The new proofs also permit a positive risk within a supplied limit. The ethical acceptability of that limit requires its own grounds; neither the arithmetic nor the analyst's preferred threshold supplies permission. Old evidence can support a changed arrangement when a justified transfer bound applies, without pretending the old law automatically persists.

This changes what counts as successful self-revision. Lower individual error rates can remain real gains while failing to capture a new vulnerability of continued participation or learning. A revised goal can explicitly include the actual shared conditions those activities require. Evidence, descriptions, goals and support arrangements then change together within the same unfolding. Established reliability theory and ecological response-diversity research already explain relevant dependence effects; the contribution here is their scoped integration with reflective formation and the explicit G0/J account.

The repair also reveals a cost that must remain visible. In the example, reducing joint failure raises the chance of some route failing from 1/10 to 2/10. If the surviving route fully preserves every relevant realization and imposes no extra burden, reducing complete interruption has a clearer comparative justification. If each route carries distinct basic functions, or changing support imposes other basic losses, that interpretation fails. Preserving a current institution indefinitely is not itself the ethical end.

**新的墙是交叉基本风险的正当比较，而不是因不确定就停止。** After improving evidence, examining genuine alternatives and checking responsibilities and permission, a reduction in shared basic vulnerability may still increase other basic risks. The existing commitments do not yet supply a universal substantive priority for that case. An invented exchange rate, automatic priority for endless continuation, or a marginal performance total would decide by adding an unargued premise. Additional explicit commitments can be investigated; the axiom route remains open.

J4 already allows timely, authorized decisions under unresolved comparisons, with affected participation, disclosed losses and residual responsibilities. This boundary therefore limits a claim to a universally justified ranking; it does not forbid continued inquiry, new support formation, justified risk-taking or learning from what follows. The formal package contains **18 new Lean theorems and 30 declaration audits** under the stated finite assumptions. It certifies no biological validation, complete understanding or universal ethical convergence.

## 13. Sharing basic risk and revising the selection rule

**风险交叉未必只能停在程序未定；在同等基本要求之间，可以正面说明分担风险的理由。** [接续论证与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#9-continue-through-equal-basic-risk-sharing)复用已有公平机会、J3/J4及联合损失比较 LC，新增有范围的 **RS：同等基本风险分担**。它是公开的比较承诺，不是 G0 或概率计算自动推出的结论。

RS applies when the basic claims and possible losses are relevantly comparable, no warranted difference in rights, prior disadvantage, urgency or undertakings favors one position, and a feasible supported mixture reduces concentrated exposure while preserving the distribution of the number and gravity of losses and other joint consequences. It gives a fairness reason for that sharing. Implementation costs, participation and authority still require their own assessment. These positions are differentiated within a joint living activity; neither institutional labels nor equal numbers of boxes establish equal claims.

The affirmative reason is that equally grounded basic claims should affect how unavoidable exposure is organized. With no relevant priority, leaving one position much more exposed gives the other a protection privilege without a corresponding ground. Sharing can give effect to both claims before the danger is settled. This develops the earlier account of fair access into a reason concerning basic-risk burdens. A rival can dispute this reason while accepting the entire causal description; the mathematics does not refute it.

The checked example has two plans with risks of (10%, 40%) and (40%, 10%). A supported equal mixture produces (25%, 25%). In the specified family it uniquely achieves the lowest attainable bound on both exposures. The probability of some loss and its supplied gravity are unchanged. The previously favored position nevertheless faces more risk: a real cost remains, so no improvement for everyone is claimed. RS can support selecting the mixture where further reasons do not defeat it and legitimate permission is present; it supplies no blanket authority to impose risk.

**名义上各选一半，不等于实际风险已经公平分担。** In a second checked law, a selector chooses each plan half the time but is coupled adversely to the danger. The resulting risks are (50%, 50%). It fails the preserved-consequences condition. Situational awareness must concern the relation among choice, danger and actual loss, rather than count procedure labels alone. Dependence is assessed within the same process, without presupposing independently constituted actors and surroundings.

The continuation then passes beyond that failure. If a reliable, timely and permitted new distinction becomes available, an informed selector in the same native law prevents both losses. Each plan is again chosen half the time, now with (0%, 0%) exposure. Obtaining that distinction and bearing its costs remain application conditions. The result does not demand perfect knowledge before every justified action; it shows why a newly available capability can change which action is warranted.

**对生活根据的忠实，可以要求改变原来有根据的选择规则。** Maintaining an earlier fair procedure is not the final goal when supported prevention becomes possible. The rule, its evidence and its continuing justification can change together, while earlier reasonable decisions retain their historical context and actual losses remain grounds for response. This connects risk comparison directly to reflective intelligence changing the conditions and goals that govern its formation.

Nineteen new Lean theorems and 36 explicit declaration audits check the allocation families, individual costs, joint outcomes, correlated failure and informed response. The ethical RS commitment, actual probabilities, permission and acquisition of information remain distinct from those proofs. The earlier 18-theorem risk package is unchanged.

A further [checked test beyond symmetry](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#14-push-beyond-symmetry-fairer-exposure-can-add-basic-harm) keeps basic gravity equal but changes available protection to (10%, 40%) versus (80%, 10%). The mixture minimizing the greater exposure gives (31%, 31%), while the chance of some basic loss rises from 50% to 62%. RS's preserved-loss condition fails. The proved arithmetic optimum therefore supplies no automatic ethical verdict. The sharper open question is how much added basic risk can be justified to reduce concentrated exposure, even when the probabilities and equal stakes are known.

The remaining comparison concerns unequal basic stakes, overlapping affected lives, differing prior rights or fairness that changes the amount of loss. The symmetric case supplies a completed candidate and checked application; those broader cases require additional argument. J4 continues to support timely decisions without a universal numerical exchange rate.

## 14. Bounded fairness costs and the scope of revision

**有根据的公平分担，可以在部分情形中承受微小的额外风险；这项理由有范围，不能每改一次规则便自动重置。** [接续论证、反审与实查](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#15-a-limited-fair-sharing-reason-can-survive-some-added-risk)复用已完成的 RS、基本损失比较的局部延续 LR 及机会修订结果，推进上一节的非对称情形。

The new candidate **RSL: limited retention of warranted sharing** makes an explicit additional commitment. In some cases where the complete comparison already sufficiently favors fair sharing, a sufficiently slight increase in basic risk need not immediately defeat that judgment. Actual lives, gravity, recovery, time, rights, support and implementation must remain adequately described. “Slight” must be specified through those changes, not defined as whatever preserves the desired verdict. RS's fairness reason alone does not establish the required complete baseline judgment.

The affirmative argument is that equally grounded claims can have practical force in organizing exposure, including some cases with a small cost. Otherwise any positive additional risk would automatically erase that force, however small the change and however strong the previously warranted comparison. This is a substantive rejection of universal zero-cost priority, not a deduction from physical continuity or from G0 alone. A rival may give every positive addition to basic harm overriding force. RSL contests that position while retaining the actual added risk as a reason against the change. It supplies no universal radius, exchange rate or authority to impose risk.

**继续生成可以形成中间方案。** In the preceding supplied law, choosing favor-A 90% or 80% of the time gives exposure pairs (17%, 37%) or (24%, 34%), between (10%, 40%) and (31%, 31%). Their chances of some basic loss are 54% and 58%. The new checked frontier identifies exactly which mixtures avoid improvement in both total exposure and greatest exposure within this family. Those two diagnostics do not exhaust ethical comparison: differentiated claims, valid settlements and omitted relationships still matter. In particular, the completed [chance-revision result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/CHANCE-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md) prevents final-law equality from erasing a warranted prior allocation.

If a separately justified constraint permits at most 54% total exposure, the unique mixture attaining a greatest exposure of at most 37% is the 90% favor-A mixture. Lean checks that conditional calculation. **The permission to add four percentage points is not established here**, nor is the ethical superiority of that intermediate proposal or of full equality. The construction changes the practical question from a forced choice between two endpoints to assessment of a range of actual possibilities.

**自我修订还须携带理由的适用范围。** The same proposals expose a failure of repeated local assessment. Moving from 100% to 90%, then 80%, then 70% favor-A adds four percentage points at each step, but twelve relative to the original comparison. A justification that permits four in total cannot license all three changes merely because each uses the immediately preceding proposal as its reference. These are successive revisions of one future probability law, not three repeated trials or an addition of unrelated probabilities across time.

A new supported reason can legitimately revise the reference or permitted scope. The requirement is to assess that change explicitly, including inherited losses and still-valid claims; it does not freeze an initial rule or require recovering all of history. The scope of a practical reason becomes part of what developing intelligence must represent and reconsider together with its goals and methods.

Nine new Lean theorems and thirteen declaration audits check the intermediate frontier, a supplied constraint and composition of scope bounds. Nineteen inherited risk-sharing theorems and thirty-six audits were rebuilt separately. The ethical RSL candidate and the permitted extent of risk remain philosophical commitments and application questions, not checked conclusions. The concrete unfinished connection is **justifying an allowable departure in a fully specified case**. J4 and situated learning still permit warranted action and further generativity without awaiting a universal ranking.

## 15. Situational awareness and continuing convergence

**可以证明情境把握中的可靠估计、持续追踪和有条件收敛，并把它们接到有限时间内足够的行动根据。** [Ren 的追问](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-goal-formation-and-revision.md#situational-awareness-and-convergence)由[新论证、实查与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#20-situational-awareness-and-continued-convergence)具体推进。已有的变化反馈、根据取得与有限证据纠正成果直接复用。

Situational awareness concerns what is happening, how affected lives and support relations fit together, what the current intervention changes, and what may follow. Acquisition, retained descriptions and action differentiate within that same history. The new formalization selects one calibratable numerical aspect. It preserves reading, retention, subsequent change and error; it does not identify a scalar estimator with complete understanding, conscious awareness or ethical judgment.

Let rₜ describe that current aspect, yₜ its acquired reading and xₜ the retained estimate. For the supplied update xₜ₊₁=q xₜ+(1−q)yₜ with 0≤q<1, Lean proves

\[
e_{t+1}\le q e_t+w_t,\qquad
e_t=|x_t-r_t|,\quad
w_t=(1-q)|y_t-r_t|+|r_{t+1}-r_t|.
\]

The formula includes change between observation and use. It assumes no statistical independence. A risk estimate needs its own calibration: one observed outcome cannot simply be substituted for a precisely known probability. The coefficient, observation access and the aspect's relevance remain supplied conditions of this local explanation.

**收敛可以发生在变化之中。** If wₜ tends to zero and updating remains supported, the estimation error tends to zero; the represented situation itself need not have a limit. If w stays bounded by (1−q)B, the finite-prefix error is bounded by qⁿe₀+(1−qⁿ)B, and with continued updating it eventually stays below B+ε for every ε>0. Persistent disturbance permits a tracking guarantee without a promise of exact knowledge or improvement at every step.

**可信的误差上界也须成为实际保留的内容。** Given a warranted initial radius E₀ and accessible bounds bₜ on wₜ, the update Eₜ₊₁=qEₜ+bₜ retains a sound error bound. If those bounds tend to zero, the retained radius does too. This distinguishes improving warranted confidence from merely settling into a stable belief. The theorem does not obtain calibration, sensing or indefinite resources by assuming them available.

For a separately justified current limit Cₜ, xₜ+Eₜ≤Cₜ establishes that the actual quantity meets that limit. If the actual quantity stays inside it by a positive margin and E tends to zero, a checked theorem establishes that this certificate passes from some finite stage onward, even when Cₜ changes. A particular deadline still needs a quantitative bound and actual support. One passing inequality does not certify the whole ethical decision or supply its authority.

Three checked failures constrain the interpretation. Constant misleading readings can produce a permanently stable wrong estimate. Perfect but delayed readings can retain error when the situation keeps changing. Even shrinking error can fail to yield a conservative finite certificate exactly on a decision boundary. These results delimit a particular method; they do not require all inquiry or action to stop.

**情境把握的改进，可以使有根据的比较逐步明确，也可以显露新关系，从而修订目的及描述。** The previous numerical ethical allowance is not determined merely by convergence of an estimate. Learning can change its factual grounds, disclose affected lives or reveal alternatives; the comparison and legitimate revision of its scope still require reasons. Newly formed capabilities may require new projections and renewed correspondence rather than permanent adherence to one description.

Fifteen new Lean theorems and nineteen explicit declaration audits passed. They establish the conditional estimation, tracking and decision-evidence connection. The current further obligation is to warrant the observation/change bounds and relevant comparison margin in a specified evolving situation. Full semantic awareness, the complete biological loop and inevitable convergence of all intelligence to one goal are not certified. Continued generativity remains compatible with limited, revisable understanding.

## 16. Predicting our effects and expanding the question

**情境把握可以把自身行动造成的变化纳入预测，让持续变化与认识收敛进一步相容。** [接续论证、原典与反审](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#26-learn-the-effects-of-acting-within-the-situation)推进上一节的具体限制：不必把全部变化都当作不可解释的扰动。已有尺度修订、改革证据与根据取得成果继续作为输入。

In a supplied response family, a probe v changes the condition by θv plus a bounded disturbance. The retained description computes θ̂ from the two acquired readings: their difference divided by v. Lean checks a coefficient-error bound from the reading and disturbance bounds, and carries it into the predicted effect of a later action. The predictor receives the acquired estimate; it does not receive the hidden true coefficient. Transfer still requires the stated response relation to remain applicable between calibration and use. Probe permission, support and effects require their own assessment.

The update now combines its retained estimate, new reading and a predicted increment pₜ. Its error is bounded by

\[
e_{t+1}\le q e_t+(1-q)|y_t-r_t|
  +|p_t-(r_{t+1}-r_t)|.
\]

The last term is the part of the actual change that prediction misses. If the total residual tends to zero, the imported convergence result applies even while the actual change persists. A warranted residual bound also feeds the previous retained-radius update. Reading, command, response and prediction are related projections within the same history; independence is not assumed.

**新增的例子跨过了单纯读取的时滞。** An exact unit probe with readings 0 and 3 gives response estimate 3. In the supplied continuation, the condition increases by three each step. The response-aware predictor tracks it exactly from the correct initial value, while the previous immediate-reading rule remains three units behind. This is a checked ideal real-valued example, not a measured biological calibration or a claim that one probe identifies every actual response law.

**认识更准确，仍须与行动放大的后果一起判断。** If coefficient error is bounded by ρ and action magnitude by U, their contribution to effect error is bounded by ρU. A checked counterexample has parameter error 2⁻ⁿ and action magnitude 2ⁿ: precision improves, but this product remains one. For positive ρ, a supplied effect-error tolerance A therefore restricts U through ρU≤A, equivalently U≤A/ρ. A is an uncertainty tolerance for this component; it is not automatically an ethically permissible harm or a license to maximize action. Other uncertainties, actual losses, authority and the original comparison's whole scope remain active.

The description can also expand. A new theorem joins finitely many separately warranted error bounds and positive comparison margins: their certificates eventually pass together. It preserves distinct conditions rather than assigning them a common ethical weight. Yet another checked construction shows that every fixed aspect can eventually be learned while a growing collection always includes a new unlearned aspect. Learning each aspect eventually is weaker than having uniformly complete awareness of every current and future aspect.

**继续生成需要不断形成和修订足够的具体问题，不要求先完成所有可能问题。** The finite set must cover what actually matters to the current intervention; unfamiliar lives or consequences cannot be excluded merely because their measurement is unfinished. Inquiry, a provisional response, a changed action or restraint of a particular intervention can each be warranted by the situation. No universal stopping rule follows from an incomplete expanding description.

This supports a stronger account of self-revision: acquire how one's activity changes conditions, incorporate that relationship into prediction, adjust action to the precision and reasons actually available, and revise the question when new effects or relations matter. The formal index of aspects is a coverage test, not a mechanism generating arbitrary new concepts. The next specific connection concerns distinguishing calibration error, changed response and an omitted relation through feasible inquiry, without supplying that diagnosis in advance.

Seventeen new Lean theorems and twenty-two declaration audits passed; the earlier fifteen tracking theorems and nineteen audits were rebuilt as an unchanged dependency. The local mathematical connection is complete. Actual calibration, supported diagnosis, full semantic awareness and ethical convergence retain their separate evidential obligations.
