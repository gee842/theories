# Constructed material carries acquired prediction and correction

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [generative grounding](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). The named connection is **formed constituents and revisable machinery → constructed predictive memory → acquired correction → resource-dependent action**, with one continuing inventory and support history.

The [predictive-action construction](PREDICTION-ACTION.md) supplied a functional retrofit on coarse reusable material slots. The [material-updater construction](MATERIAL-UPDATER-COMPOSITION.md) supplied typed consumable parts for selectors and updaters. Their separate results did not establish that the same actual constituents could also build the predictive register and support later updater replacement. This study closes that finite interface under an explicit additional construction recipe.

## 1. What is constructed, and what remains a law

The preparation, adapter and first machinery use the existing `FormedSelection` and `ConstructedUpdater` relations. The parameters are those of the predictive-action law from the start; they are not changed after preparation. The adapter consumes a free A/H pair, converts the one captured local fuel unit once, and retains the resident, site, source remainder and unused precursor capacity.

A predictive retrofit requires the existing live machinery, its original fuel payment, and **one additional free A and one additional free H**. It incorporates that pair into a separately counted register. The register has five possible values, initially the neutral value two. Its pair is neither left free nor counted again among the previously bound parts. Subsequent writes reuse this hardware. Each later updater formation consumes another free A/H pair and pays its inherited fuel bill; it preserves the acquired register value.

The functional wiring remains a declared recipe. The new pair and payment do not by themselves explain a five-state memory, a receipt decoder or its action circuit. Before retrofit the selector field is the assembled static orientation. After retrofit that same inherited field records the last issued action; the new circuit can issue a different action from the learned score without another two-part selector assembly. The supplied retrofit is precisely the functional change that permits this use. The old static selector is not claimed to have changed its function for free.

All predictive events retain their existing meanings and guards: rent, work and acquisition, write, finish, environmental reversal and updater formation. The deterministic payable-write law is unchanged. The broader Python model's outages, failed retention, skipped writes and circuit rebuilding are not added by this material refinement. There is no new evidence archive, variable action coupler or recipient interface here.

## 2. Exact compatibility and competing uses

Let \(U(w)\) count updater formations in a predictive event word \(w\). For an inherited state with an installed register and free stocks \(A_0,H_0\), the checked criterion is

\[
\begin{split}
&\text{the same predictive word and endpoint have a typed-material realization}\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{the original predictive word and endpoint are feasible},\quad
U(w)\leq A_0,\quad U(w)\leq H_0.
\end{split}
\]

The original feasibility condition retains every intermediate source, lease, phase and payment guard. Nonnegative component consumption makes the two total stock bounds sufficient for all prefixes under this no-replenishment law. Installed register backing is a separate premise; a coarse material total alone cannot satisfy it.

The conserved material account is

\[
A_{\rm free}+H_{\rm free}+\text{resident}
+A_{\rm bound}+H_{\rm bound}+2\,\text{register units}
+\text{unformed precursor}=M.
\]

Used updater parts remain bound when their mechanism expires. Finite remaining stocks therefore bound later updater formations even if fuel is abundant. This does not bound the number of possible writes by the same count: writing reuses the register under its declared retention law. Continued component production, reclamation and degradation require further dynamics.

## 3. One actual preparation-to-correction history

The successful witness begins with capacity eleven, common fuel thirty-three and source energy 121, no formed constituents, no resident and a damaged site. These stocks are declared before any construction.

1. Form six H and five A, associate one A, repair, and capture one local unit. The result has four free A, six free H, one resident, twenty common fuel units, one local unit and 120 source units.
2. Pay the adapter and bind its pair. The local unit is converted once. Form the first Slow updater from another pair and the initial `true` selector from two H. Common fuel is seventeen; twelve ten-unit packets remain; two A and two H are free.
3. Pay three fuel units for the predictive retrofit and incorporate its pair. The initial register is two, fuel is fourteen, and one free A/H pair remains.
4. Six productive trials acquire value four. The same history then undergoes environmental reversal. Two failed Slow trials retain and revise the record to three and then two, leaving the updater expired and fuel twenty-two.
5. Form the Fast updater from the remaining pair, retaining value two. Its next actual failure is written as value zero. That write changes the **next** command while preserving the action already issued. The following command captures productively.

The endpoint has value zero, eighteen fuel units and two ten-unit packets. Its eleven material units are one resident, three bound A, five bound H and the separately retained two-part register. No free parts remain.

The whole energy ledger is

\[
154=13\text{ preparation}+1\text{ adapter}
+3\text{ first machinery}+3\text{ retrofit}
+96\text{ predictive operation and waste}+38\text{ remaining}.
\]

This is a feasible finite history under the declared laws. It does not assign that history a probability, derive a policy that selects it, or transfer the earlier stochastic success rate to the changed preparation and material law.

## 4. Failure cases belong to the result

The previously checked thirteen-material updater-revision history finishes with nine fuel units but no free components. Its original parameters are retained. It satisfies the coarse predictive retrofit's fuel and live-machinery conditions, yet cannot supply the new register pair. Successful revision and a larger total material count do not imply that prediction can subsequently be constructed.

Two independent ten-material preparations do reach the predictive entrance with the successful case's fuel and source stocks. One is short of a free A, the other of a free H. Both execute the acquired-memory and reversal prefix, arriving at value two, fuel twenty-two and four packets; neither can form the replacement updater. The shortage is the result of the actual preparation and preceding consumption.

Conversely, available parts cannot fund an unaffordable phase. The finite checker retains a resource-dependent work event whose next write is insolvent. A configured state with the same total material and operational fields but no register backing also cannot execute a typed predictive event. That last state is a guard counterexample, not an asserted reachable preparation.
