## 1. What changes within the organization

A selector routes a sensed bit through one of two response mappings. Which mapping obtains usable support depends on a hidden environmental condition. Neither the selector nor its updater can inspect that condition or the reversal schedule.

An updater governs reconstruction when the selector expires. There are two declared implementations:

- **R:** propose either response mapping with equal probability.
- **D:** propose the opposite of the expired selector's retained mapping. With no previous selector, choose fairly, as R does.

The retained mapping is an accessible physical residue. D's advantage in a stable noiseless binary environment comes from excluding the mapping whose failures exhausted its lease. Noise or another reversal can invalidate that inference. An observer's performance score does not choose the updater.

Both selector and updater start absent. Updater formation costs two fuel units; selector assembly costs one; each sensing/work trial costs two before any return. A usable source packet supplies six, of which one maintains the selector and one maintains the updater. A capture resets their lifetimes while preserving their types. Failure wears both structures. Their primary lifetimes are two and four exposures respectively.

An expired updater is replaced by a paid, fair R/D proposal. This proposal law is fixed. The formed updater changes the conditional law for subsequent selector assembly; it does not rewrite its own replacement chemistry. Every payment remains spent if a later payment is unaffordable. Every unsuccessful or inactive exposure loses its source packet. Reversal carries forward the actual remaining fuel, structures, residues and source.

Thus a change of updater is more than a change of response: from the same expired selector residue, R permits rebuilding the old mapping, whereas D excludes it. The [mathematical note](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/UPDATER-FORMATION-MATH.md) and formal law state this distinction exactly.

It spends that common unit before making the captured local unit accessible. One A and one H become backing material for two recyclable module slots; neither new module is installed by the adapter.

\[
F_{\rm in}=F-1+f,\qquad

e_{\rm rem}=E\bmod6.
\]

The remainder remains present but inaccessible to this packet interface. Consequently

\[
F_{\rm in}+6H_{\rm in}+e_{\rm rem}+1=F+f+E.
\]

The adapter adds a functional conversion law. This identity proves that its account creates no support; it does not establish that real A/H molecules implement digital updaters. The coarse recyclable slots, scaffold retooling and packet-access valve are additional premises.

This probability concerns the embedded jump chain through all absorbing preparation states. In this reference, successful adapter states have no competing positive-rate old reaction. ## 4. Comparisons and a sharper recovery bound

The [fixed protocol and executable results](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/README.md) compare four mechanisms over twelve declared cases. They have the same original resources, available cues, assembly prices and physical lifetimes. Fixed updater types still pay for hardware, upkeep and replacement.

The primary case reverses after four exposures, with sixteen post-commissioning exposures in total. These expectations include failed preparation histories:

| Mechanism | Expected captures | Functional updater-revision probability |
|---|---:|---:|
| Variable R/D updater | 335/144 ≈ 2.3264 | 1/72 ≈ 1.39% |
| Conventional hierarchical feedback realization | 335/144 | 1/72 |
| Fixed R updater | 61/36 ≈ 1.6944 | 0 |
| Fixed D updater | 26/9 ≈ 2.8889 | 0 |

The conventional hierarchy has exactly the same physical transition kernel. Fixed D obtains more captures in the primary case. These are limits on any advantage claim, not failed controls to discard. The construction explains one way updater turnover can occur; it does not outperform feedback theory merely by naming its states differently. The [comparator assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-support-and-shared-generativity/completion/UPDATER-FORMATION-COMPARATORS.md) situates the result against prior work without a historical-priority claim.

A separate synchronized-expiry calculation gives an exact boundary. Let both structures have remaining lifetime k after their selector becomes obsolete, equal maximum lifetimes L, inherited fuel F and H opportunities left. Let g, c and d be updater, selector and trial costs. Before the first return, success after j failed fresh bundles requires

\[
C_j=kd+g+c+d+j(g+c+Ld),\qquad T_j=k+1+jL.
\]

If N such bundle positions fit both F and H, recovery probability is **1−4⁻ᴺ**. When recovery occurs, **2/3** of the first successful fresh bundles use D. This conditional bias arises from an initially fair proposal: D succeeds with probability one on the retained obsolete mapping, while R succeeds with probability one-half. The result requires fixed noiseless conditions, an existing residue and a capture large enough to fund both renewal bills; it is not a universal updater-frequency law or a formula for the unequal-lifetime primary experiment.

Two additional diagnostics were specified **after** the primary results:

- Against a half-R, half-D mixture with type held fixed after its first fair draw, variable turnover gains **5/144** expected captures in the primary case, but loses **1/48** at original reserve twenty-four. Turnover has no universal benefit even in this finite family.
- With updater lifetime one, selector lifetime two and noisy capture, a new updater can be renewed by an inherited selector before governing any reconstruction. An explicit path has conditional probability **9/512** from its stated commissioned entrance and fixed regime. The strict witness excludes it. This supplemental combination was not one of the twelve primary cases.

The reported reserve statistic covers commissioned compartments only. Failed preparations retain separate common, local and source stocks; they are included in the whole-history energy account, not silently counted as destroyed fuel.
