# What evidence can diagnose a learning route?

6 October 2026. AI-authored companion to [successor learning](SUCCESSOR-LEARNING.md), addressing **acquired response → evidence about the update mechanism → accountable route revision**. **Accepted at its conditional diagnostic and saved-evidence scopes, with separate internal review.** This is a formal evidence interface and an audit of accepted saved histories; it constructs neither a new sensor nor the mechanism that replaces a learning route.

The successor can already correct and transmit its stored response. Its route through target feedback, private feedback, disconnected input or frozen writing remains inherited. Revising that route requires knowing what the available observations actually distinguish. A failed action alone does not identify whether an estimate, route or source availability caused the failure.

## The precise diagnostic result

Assume an unchanged installed route, a bounded two-target record, correctly addressed recipient action, an external outcome channel available before route-specific routing, and a completed write. The action uses the current recipient bit. Suppose a diagnostic also obtains a warranted report of actual recipient capture and reads the record before and after the write. Then the existing route functions give:

| Route | Bit changes after actual failure | Bit changes after actual success |
|---|---:|---:|
| Target feedback | Yes | No |
| Private-zero feedback | Yes | Yes |
| Disconnected recipient input | No | No |
| Frozen writing | No | No |

A genuine success/failure pair, bound to the same unchanged route and recipient context, identifies three update classes. If route replacement occurs between observations, a paid association to the relevant route episode is needed before combining them. The disconnected and frozen routes remain equivalent for this record-based recipient test. A single failed probe with no change already identifies their shared class within these assumptions; a single successful probe followed by a flip identifies the private-zero class. Both polarities are needed to observe the complete signature uniformly, not for every particular diagnosis.

The classifier does not inspect hidden target truth, an analyst's correctness score or the route's name. Reading the actual configuration `gamma` would distinguish nominal recipes directly, but trusting their semantics is different from testing behavior. The [mathematical account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ROUTE-DIAGNOSTICS-MATH.md) specifies these observation boundaries.

## Different observations need different experiments

With only independently acquired recipient outcomes, a stable available plant and an initially wrong recipient bit, three ordinary probes have the following patterns, where 1 means capture:

| Route | Outcome word |
|---|---|
| Target feedback | `011` |
| Private-zero feedback | `010` |
| Disconnected or frozen | `000` |

The first failure makes both updating routes flip toward the productive response. The second action succeeds for both; afterward, private-zero feedback flips away again. Outcomes alone separate them at the third probe. A paid record read after the second write can separate them earlier.

This result concerns the named probe sequence and observation interface. It does not prove the probes are affordable, that every history supplies both outcomes, or that no other calibrated experiment can distinguish the routes sooner. A changed target or paid challenge must actually supply the initially wrong-record condition; the ordinary learner is not handed a change notification or the desired answer.

If the initial bit is correct and the plant stays stable and available, target, disconnected and frozen routes instead produce the same correct commands and returns for every finite completed sequence. Passive success does not exercise their different responses to failure. Disconnected and frozen routes also have equal recipient record/action behavior under arbitrary receipt words, although route identity and acquired input-presence reports can distinguish them. Actor-target experiments are outside this equivalence.

## The distinction occurs in actual accepted descendants

The saved stable successor experiment contains retained target, disconnected and frozen grandchildren with the same record and physical-stock projection. Twelve matched groups cover the three producers, both original-actor reserves and both retained codes. Each group keeps its distinct ancestry and unconditional mass; no unsuccessful ancestor is replaced by a successful one.

Within these groups, recipient reserve is 25, original-actor reserve is 8 or 15, both new carriers have zero private cash, sources are exhausted and the new blank/routing pair is occupied. Each exact route/reserve/code endpoint has mass 1/144 for the variable and conventional producers, or 1/72 for fixed normal. The groupings establish matched saved endpoints and ancestry references, not equality of every audit field or fresh reconstruction of every earlier micro-event.

These are actual examples of successful inheritance leaving different latent update behaviors. Recipient resources alone do not supply free replacement parts, a calibrated monitor or authority to change the route.

## A missing observation is not a negative diagnosis

The prospective diagnostic's actual recipient report cannot be replaced by the potentially misleading routed label. Nor do saved `capture`, `receipt_source`, `decoder_errors` or witness fields install a persistent monitor on the carrier. A downstream consumer must supply the warranted report, reads, context and write-completion evidence through a real paid interface.

Three failures have different meanings:

- **Write unpaid:** unchanged memory does not identify a nonwriting route.
- **Route input absent:** a target route can now look like a disconnected or frozen route in the bit signature.
- **Plant supply unavailable:** genuine noncapture can occur despite a correct action. It still induces the target decoder's specified flip, but that flip can teach an incorrect estimate. Identifying update behavior does not identify ecological truth.

The formal package separates these obligations with a supplied four-stage inspection account: pre-read, independent outcome acquisition, writing, post-read. With no intermediate inflow, a complete diagnostic exists exactly when all four prices can be paid. Earlier charges remain sunk if a later stage fails; writing can complete while its post-read remains unaffordable. This abstract account is not yet a physical refinement of the existing seven-unit learner probe or a calculation of its exact extra diagnostic cost.

## Evidence and remaining work

The [finite audit](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/verification.json) checks the actual executable functions over 64 route/input cases, 128 paid/unpaid commitment cases, 32 three-probe replays and twelve stable fixed points. It also matches 36 ancestor and 36 terminal rows in the twelve saved groups. Twenty-three frozen hashes bind the functions and saved data to their accepted check records. These are finite semantic checks and saved-evidence queries; the parent experiments are not rerun.

The [Lean module](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/formal/RouteDiagnostics.lean) imports the actual formed-route and packed-record functions. A fresh pinned Lean 4.29.1 build checks fifteen new declarations across three frozen modules, with no custom axioms or proof holes and only the standard `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice` dependencies. Its separate [verification record](../../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/prediction/recipient-correction/route-diagnostics/formal/verification.json) and [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ROUTE-DIAGNOSTICS-REVIEW.md) define the final accepted scope. The existing controller-history and ambiguous-receipt theorems are antecedents; no new general impossibility principle or empirical advantage is claimed.

**The next construction remains paid revision of the learning route itself.** It must obtain usable evidence, preserve or acquire any report needed after reconstruction, pay replacement parts and continued probes, and show how actual consequences affect the retained alternative. The earlier learner's funding bounds cannot absorb these new bills without a new check. Discriminating a route does not establish that replacing it is affordable, useful in the next context or ethically warranted.

This sharpens the common [generative direction](GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md): derive the observer and revision machinery as part of the process, while retaining plural mechanisms where their differences have no demonstrated consequence. It complements route construction and the parallel reasons/protection work without claiming those branches already form one execution.
