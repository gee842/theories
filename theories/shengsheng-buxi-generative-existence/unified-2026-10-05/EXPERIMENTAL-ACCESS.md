The receipt reports an actual consequence, not a correct fault label. Its reliability remains a supplied premise.

The general rule is simple. Let \(B_t\) contain all current apparatus configurations compatible with the paid chronological observations. Update each through its own action/reset law and remove only incompatible predictions. For a nonempty set, a property \(P\) is warranted within the admitted class exactly when

\[
\forall w\in B_t,\quad P(w).
\]

An empty set signals failure of the family together with its trusted observation assumptions. A singleton establishes uniqueness within that family. Neither establishes that every physically possible explanation has been considered.
