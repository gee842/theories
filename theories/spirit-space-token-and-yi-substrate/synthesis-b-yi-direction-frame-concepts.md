# Yi — the direction, the frame, the concepts (易/一 substrate as metaphysics implemented as a database)

> **Part b of the theory `spirit-space-token-and-yi-substrate`.** Cluster: `sources/09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/` + `sources/09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/` (2026-06 → 2026-09).

> Yi is the claim that *being*, *having*, *doing* and *permitting* are one composable substrate — and the attempt to make that claim literally executable. A **Yi** (一, "the one") is a stable, addressable runtime root with a UID and nothing else essential to it; what it *is* comes from the immutable **Kind bindings** it carries, and what is *true of it* comes from facts owned by separate semantic contexts — Identity (who/where in the principal tree), Custody (who holds), Containment (where placed), Relations (how connected), Authority (who may). The same root can therefore be simultaneously a subject, an object, a capability, an actor, a container, a member, a tool and a policy-holder — "one thing, several truths" — without ever changing identity, because none of those are storage categories: they are *roles read off context facts*. Meaning is declared, never inferred: an author writes `defineKind` / `defineKindFamily` with semantic `facet.*` contributions; a compiler closes those declarations into an immutable binding revision and a deterministic representation plan; the runtime executes that plan and "never guesses meaning from names, optional fields, or storage layout." Every durable change across every owner lands atomically on one commit spine, the **Occasion**. The design's stated success metric is not feature count but *mental resistance* — "the number of independent things you must hold in working memory to predict the system's behavior at a point" — enforced in CI as a concept budget (G8). What makes this a philosophical program rather than a schema is the set of things it refuses: no hidden universal component table, no permanent categories, no permission inherited from parentage or possession, no ambient authority, no cached allow as authority, no representation reconstructed from a name, no "one graph" that swallows the several explicitly-owned graphs. Bonfire, the system built on it, is described in its own canonical frame as "an encoded realization of 间理" — the reconstruction-law of 間 made operational as a running database.

## 1. Core claims

Citations are paths relative to `sources/`. All files in this cluster are AI-drafted engineering documents written from Ren's ideas and decisions; passages marked "founder" record Ren's own rulings.

**The direction (four verbs, one substrate)**

1. [design direction] Yi's committed engineering target is that "**being**, *having*, *doing*, and *permitting*" become "one composable substrate," not four separate subsystems — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/00-direction.md`.
2. [observation] Most systems choose exactly one center — actors (message passing), ECS (entities+components), graph DBs (relations), object-capability systems (possession of authority), social platforms (accounts/groups/posts/feeds/permissions), blockchains (ownership + verifiable state) — and make the rest derivative — `.../00-direction.md`.
3. [design direction] Yi "combines these without making one of them the hidden 'real' model" — `.../00-direction.md`.
4. [mechanism] The distinctive property: "A Yi can become subject, object, capability, actor, container, member, tool, or policy-holder through composed Kinds and context-owned state *without changing its root identity*" — `.../00-direction.md`.
5. [value stance] The good version of unification is explicitly *not* "everything is one table"; it is "everything is one kind of meaningful thing, while storage, indexes, fan-out, presence, messages, feeds, and moderation can be specialized where scale demands it" — `.../00-direction.md`.
6. [mechanism] The four verbs each get one primitive: being = an effective **Kind binding**; having = a context-owned **component**; doing = a **port**, "its firing is a *moment*"; permitting = a **gate** plus Authority contributions — `.../00-direction.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/01-frame.md`.
7. [design direction] The two halves that must be kept together are "conceptual elegance" (one kind of meaningful thing, no hidden center) and "operational reality" (specialized storage below a seam the author never crosses); the named failure mode is "**rhetorical victory before engineering closure**" — `.../00-direction.md`.

**The one root, and the refusal of a universal component table**

8. [mechanism] "There is one stable runtime root, **Yi**, but not one universal component table" — Identity, Custody, Containment, Relations and Authority each own the facts in their own domain — `.../01-frame.md`.
9. [mechanism] A Yi (一, "the one") is a stable addressable root owning only stable denotation and coordination fields: UID, Domain, primary Kind binding, creation/extinction timestamps, monotonic sequence, current Occasion head — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/yi.md`.
10. [mechanism] "People, agents, groups, spaces, posts, assets, invitations, relation occurrences, and certificates can all have Yi roots. There is no parallel subject/object root." — `.../01-frame.md`.
11. [mechanism] Context facts never live in authored state: "A holder is a Custody fact, a parent and slot are Containment facts, endpoints are Relations facts, and an issuer or subject is an Authority fact." — `.../01-frame.md`.
12. [mechanism] Each kind of connection has its own owner and law — Kinds owns bindings, Identity owns principal coordinates, Custody owns holdings, Containment owns placements, Relations owns occurrences, Authority owns certificates — because "Alice holds a key," "a key is equipped in a slot," "Alice is a member of a group," and "a certificate permits Alice to moderate" have different conservation, cardinality, lifecycle and authorization laws — `.../01-frame.md`.
13. [mechanism] **Roles are a reading lens, not root types**: subject, object, holder, held asset, child, parent, source/target endpoint, issuer, presenter, capability, container and agent are read off context components and "are never inferred from an optional authored field or a hidden category" — `.../01-frame.md`.
14. [observation] The same Yi may simultaneously be a Custody holder in one holding, the held asset in another, the child of a placement, an endpoint of several relations, the subject of one certificate and the issuer of another — `.../01-frame.md`.
15. [mechanism] The canonical worked illustration is the **sword**: "a sword is an item, but can also hold an agent, expose ports, grant abilities, track history, and act under gates" — `.../00-direction.md`.
16. [observation] The general form is "**one thing, several truths**": a sword can at once be a conserved asset, a child placed in a backpack, an object equipped in a hand slot, the source of a permission, an endpoint in a trade relation, part of a cross-Domain escrow, an item in four Views, and a stable object with recoverable Occasion history — and "[t]hose facts overlap, but they are not interchangeable" — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/bonfire-use-cases-and-possibilities.md`.
17. [mechanism] The same overlap table generalizes off games: house key, membership badge, agent token, event ticket, treasury approval and research artifact each decompose into Custody / Containment / Relations / Authority / durable behavior columns — `.../bonfire-use-cases-and-possibilities.md`.
18. [observation] Without this decomposition the rules "tend to accumulate in a central item service, a large transaction script, or a collection of loosely synchronized event handlers" — `.../bonfire-use-cases-and-possibilities.md`.

**Kinds and facets as portable law**

19. [mechanism] A **Kind family** supplies stable rename-safe genesis; a **Kind revision** is immutable semantic law for that family; a Yi carries one or more **effective Kind bindings** selected for its Domain — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/unified-class.md`.
20. [mechanism] A Kind is "immutable semantic law: family genesis, authored-state schema, gates, ports, facets, and cross-facet bindings. Stored in the Kinds repository, **never as a Yi**." — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/glossary.md`.
21. [design direction] Kinds are "live governed law": a family has genesis, semantic contributions, owner policy, evolution, migrations, ports, presentation and export policy, and installing it commits all of them together — `.../00-direction.md` (G3).
22. [mechanism] A **facet** is a typed semantic contribution owned by exactly one context (Identity, Custody, Containment, Relations, Authority, Streams …), decoded by that context's codec and deeply frozen — `.../unified-class.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/encoded-implementation.md`.
23. [mechanism] `composes` declares semantic family references only — "it never selects physical representation"; representation comes solely from decoded `facet.*` contributions — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/composition.md`.
24. [mechanism] When one contribution affects another, an explicit `bindFacet.*` declaration names the exact source contribution, target contribution, execution class and deciding owner; missing endpoints, incompatible owners and ambiguous overlaps are compile errors — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/port-gate-grant.md`.
25. [mechanism] **The compiler seam is a law**: "Declaration states what a Kind means. The closed representation plan states which owners must materialize it. Runtime executes that plan exactly." — `.../01-frame.md`.
26. [mechanism] Evolution has *several distinct identities*: semantic-contribution changes alter the semantic revision, representation-plan changes alter the binding revision, interface changes alter interface identity, presentation changes alter presentation identity; a rollout changes the *selected* binding and never mutates an old revision — `.../01-frame.md`.
27. [mechanism] Portable installation travels as one unit (`PortableKitInstallSourceV3`): family genesis, canonical contribution bytes, exact surfaces, handler artifacts, views, workflows, migrations, aliases, expected-source inventory and per-family export policy — `.../01-frame.md`, `.../encoded-implementation.md`.
28. [value stance] Export policy is operation-owned package metadata and "never influences semantic representation" — portability must not change what a Kind *means* — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/community.md`.

**Ports, gates, agency**

29. [mechanism] A **port** is a named typed operation offered by a Kind, referencing an exact deeply-frozen handler artifact with stable identity, digest, ABI and runtime schema; "[t]here is no ambient process-global handler lookup" — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/ports-and-faces.md`.
30. [mechanism] A **gate** is pure composable data (an ADT with `and`/`or`/`not`) returning allow / deny / requires / requires-any, reasoning over caller, target, bindings, context facts and the requested operation — `.../glossary.md`, `.../01-frame.md`.
31. [mechanism] "Ports are resolved from the target Yi's effective Kind bindings. **Holding an asset does not copy its ports onto the holder.**" — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/holdings.md`.
32. [mechanism] Agency is composed, not granted by class: every Yi is addressable, but *acting* requires an Identity principal (signing), ports (what it offers), gates (admission), Authority certificates (delegated permission), and any Custody/Containment influence declared through explicit cross-context bindings — `.../01-frame.md`.
33. [mechanism] Four control axes are kept separate and never fused: authored-state authority, custody, signing identity, and operation authority — `.../01-frame.md`; the pivot adds holder-transfer as a fourth separate axis alongside custody/signing/gate — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/02-pivot.md`.
34. [design direction] **Item-as-agent (G1)**: the item *itself* must be able to act — "one Yi carrying the item, Identity, and agent Kind bindings plus ports **signs its own action, receives a trigger, and fires a gated port — with tests**" — while Custody remains sole owner of holder state; delegating to a separate agent Yi is allowed only as a delegation *pattern*, "never as the meaning of an active item" — `.../00-direction.md`, `.../02-pivot.md` (D11).
35. [design direction] **Addressability is universal; agency is a trait** (D2) — "not 'everything is active'"; universal agency would expand every gate surface and raise cognitive load, so passivity is the default and activeness is composed — `.../02-pivot.md`.
36. [design direction] **Agents do not escape the model (G6)**: no privileged class, no backdoor, every action a gated port invocation under a held capability or scoped delegation, every durable action inside one auditable Occasion, with tests asserting no ambient authority — `.../00-direction.md`.
37. [mechanism] The equipped key is the canonical authority-composition example: Custody records who holds it, Containment whether it occupies the required slot, Authority the certificate it activates, and the port gate decides — so moving, transferring, extinguishing or revoking any component removes the authority immediately "without changing the Yi's root identity" — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/hearth.md`.
38. [design direction] **Explainable authority (G5)**: "Why can Alice do this?" must trace through held item, group membership, delegated capability, port gate, role and policy as "**one graph of cause**" returned by a single `explain(caller, target, port)` — `.../00-direction.md`.
39. [mechanism] Collective capability is admitted but bounded: member →Relations membership→ group →Authority certificate→ resource, under attenuation-only, deny-precedence, eager revocation, and "no synthetic membership" (access through the group creates no membership in the target) — `.../01-frame.md`, `.../02-pivot.md` (D13, implemented as an index intersection with both legs liveness-checked).

**Occasion as the single commit spine**

40. [mechanism] "**Occasion is the single durable commit spine** joining their changes" — one Domain-scoped coordinator acquires the lock, runs transaction-scoped narrow repositories, collects exact effects, computes the immutable commit, binds every changed Yi root to the actual commit ID, and commits once — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/touch-lifecycle.md`.
41. [mechanism] "An empty Occasion, an effect without materialized state, or an ordinary durable write outside the coordinator **fails closed**"; nested commands join the active Occasion rather than opening a second transaction or a side-channel audit record — `.../01-frame.md`, `.../touch-lifecycle.md`.
42. [mechanism] Checkpoint plus retained Occasion tail must reconstruct normalized state; history, watches, Authority frontiers, recovery, restore, erasure propagation and pruning all consume the same commit/effect source — `.../01-frame.md`.
43. [mechanism] Partial manifestation is invalid: a multi-context Kind is materialized by one runtime command that validates state once, prepares every component named by the committed plan, and commits root + bindings + authored state + components + effects through one Occasion — `.../01-frame.md`, `.../yi.md`.

**The laws of the frame**

44. [value stance] Ten laws govern the frame: (1) one stable root; (2) meaning is explicit, "never identifier prefixes or optional-field inference"; (3) owners are exclusive; (4) families are closed; (5) cross-context effects are bound; (6) ports and authority are exact; (7) representation is compiled; (8) one durable commit spine; (9) queries respect ownership; (10) break forward cleanly — `.../01-frame.md`.
45. [value stance] Law 10 forbids compatibility scaffolding outright: "Prelaunch changes do not retain dual writes, compatibility aliases, old wire decoders, or detached projections" — `.../01-frame.md`; restated as the founder's standing constraint "NOT deployed — no backward compatibility of any kind… Break forward, always the long-term-elegant option" — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/08-ideal-architecture.md`.
46. [mechanism] Queries must use context-owned indexed paginated operations (holder, placement, relation endpoint, certificate subject/issuer, Kind binding, identity path, forum order, balance); "no generic Yi scan substitutes for them" — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/visibility.md`.

**What is deliberately NOT in the frame**

47. [design direction] Five things are first-class but explicitly *not* generic Yi components — **Streams** (canonical dense-ordered content and reply causality), **Signals** (TTL-bounded presence/typing/cursors/occupancy that "do not assert durable authority or historical truth"), **Views and projections** (declared rebuildable read models), **external effects** (durable outbox/workflow with idempotency and compensation, because "a database transaction cannot roll back a completed external call"), and **portability** (a privileged operation) — `.../01-frame.md`.
48. [design direction] **Federation and sharding "translate semantic Domain and principal coordinates to physical placement. They do not alter the Kind or Yi model."** — `.../01-frame.md`, `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/federation.md`.
49. [value stance] These exclusions are "named boundaries, not hidden alternative roots" — the honesty condition on "no hidden center" — `.../01-frame.md`; the pivot sharpens the count: the *named* non-Yi centers are the column-promoted relations (`parentUid`, `originKindUid`) **and** the stream tier, so "'No *hidden* center' stays true; 'one exception' undercounted" — `.../02-pivot.md` (D9).
50. [mechanism] Messages are the deliberate exception: they are Streams-owned, non-Yi rows in their own dense `stream` table with a per-session monotonic `seq`, forming a session-local DAG via `parents[]`, "intentionally minimal compared to Yis: they carry no Kind binding, authored state, or normalized Yi components" — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/messages.md`.
51. [conditional] The justification is scale, not ontology: at ~10K–100K users and ~5B messages per host, "the per-row overhead of dragging the full Yi machinery onto every chat line would be wasteful"; a stream append still commits atomically with related Yi/context changes through Occasion — `.../messages.md`.

**Spirits: people, bots, groups, channels are not separate storage kinds**

52. [mechanism] "A spirit is an addressable agent: a Yi whose effective Kind binding includes an Identity principal contribution. People, bots, groups, channels, and sessions are common product nouns for spirits; **they are not separate storage representations**." — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/spirits.md`.
53. [mechanism] Kinds owns the stable UID, exact root binding and immutable Creation; Identity owns *mutable* principal profile, liveness, canonical path, parent edge and revisions; Containment owns placement — three separately-owned lifetimes over one identity — `.../spirits.md`, `.../yi.md`.
54. [mechanism] Renaming, reparenting or extinguishing a principal "never rewrites that Creation fact" — the born-as record (`yi_births`) is immutable and committed atomically with the root — `.../yi.md`, `.../spirits.md`.
55. [mechanism] A community is not a storage shape: "Identity gives the community its canonical public principal path," Streams stores channels/posts, Relations stores membership and roles as occurrences, Authority certificates decide moderation, Containment stores ordered tabs and inventories, Views declares the public surfaces — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/community.md`.
56. [mechanism] A session is likewise "a product composition of explicit Kind facets… not a Yi subtype and is not recognized from a discriminator or composed sentinel"; DMs, channels, posts and agent conversations "differ in product policy, not storage representation" — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/session.md`.
57. [mechanism] Membership is one live Relations occurrence used by the *same* indexed predicate for history reads, writes and watch delivery — "[t]here is no membership-by-history and no automatic admission on send" — `.../session.md`, `.../visibility.md`.
58. [design direction] After the face dissolution, a session *is* the composition `sys/membered` + `sys/streamed` and a spirit *is* `sys/identity` + `sys/profile`; the founder ruled "FULL DISSOLUTION, NO COMPROMISE" and the seeds' capability branches were deleted — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/06-unification.md` (U3.2/U3.3).

**Tokens, holdings, certificates, sigils**

59. [mechanism] Bonfire "separates conserved assets from issued authority": Custody owns fungible/unit quantity, holder, transfer, split, consolidation, activation, extinction; Authority owns certificates — issuer, subject, presenter, canonical bytes, use count, issuer epoch, revocation — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/tokens.md`.
60. [mechanism] Fungibility is a declared Custody quantity policy, "not a sentinel or a generic payload field"; Custody performs conserved unit arithmetic and journals idempotent transfer requests — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/holdings.md`.
61. [value stance] Vocabulary is legislated one-word-per-concept: **Yi** (not entity/record), **kind** (not class/type), **holding**, **certificate** (not token/claim/grant/attestation), **relation occurrence** (not edge), **spirit** (not actor/subject), **capability** (not item), **claim** reserved for work dispatch, **manifest** (not spawn), **extinguish** — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/VOCABULARY.md`.
62. [mechanism] A **sigil** is a sandboxed miniapp riding inside a message; an agent can never raw-emit one — "[t]he model's output is the least trusted thing in the system; it must not be the thing that decides what code lands in chat" — it must go through a `mint_sigil_reply` tool that validates and capability-clamps the envelope — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-concepts/sigil-replies.md`.
63. [mechanism] Three guarantees hold the sigil design: the tool output is inert data (nothing executes on reply; a human click later runs it click-gated in a readonly-by-default sandbox), the capability floor is the host's not the envelope's, and a bad envelope is a recoverable tool error rather than a crash or a silent render — `.../sigil-replies.md`.
64. [value stance] "Sigils receive no special bypass. Their host calls the same typed transport and the same gate/Authority envelope as other clients." — `.../visibility.md`.

**The pivot: the diagnosis and the metric**

65. [observation] The pivot's one-line thesis: Bonfire "unified the layer it should have left plural (*representation*…) and left plural the layer it meant to unify (*meaning*…)" — the inversion is the source of constant friction — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/02-pivot.md`.
66. [observation] The worst concrete instance: a holding's nature (capability vs attestation vs edge) was inferred "from **which optional field happens to be present at construction**, not declared on its kind" — so a Kind carrying the contract carried none of it — `.../02-pivot.md`.
67. [observation] The cost was measured, not impressionistic: ~84 re-derivations of `holdingSubFaceFromKinds` across core/server/std, ~20 kit sites bypassing the "one root," an advertised ~6-concept frame against ~24 concepts actually held, and a low-resistance-system score of ~42/100 — "a beautiful spine with leaky joints" — `.../02-pivot.md`.
68. [mechanism] Three mechanisms that felt separate were one idea in three costumes — `kinds[]` membership, holding edges, and the message DAG are all "a typed, directed link between two addressable things — **a tie**" — split for real performance reasons, but "it leaked out of the implementation and into the concept" — `.../02-pivot.md`.
69. [observation] The founder's original intent, restated: a thing can be subject or object, bearer or capability, container, type, agent, message target or resource "*depending on its traits and relations, not on a permanent category*"; "Having something and being something are the same substance seen from two ends of a relation. Even the **type** of a thing is a thing." — `.../02-pivot.md`.
70. [mechanism] The operative success metric is cognitive: "**mental resistance = the number of independent things you must hold in working memory to predict the system's behavior at a point**" — explicitly *not* primitive count, since three primitives with a dozen special cases beat five that compose cleanly; the levers are closure, no-special-cases and uniform access — `.../02-pivot.md`.
71. [value stance] Two constitutional corollaries: the concept need not mirror the implementation 1:1 provided divergence is hidden below the surface and reconstructable ("[a] leak is a defect; a hidden divergence is not"), and "**the conceptual framework itself may be flawed** — we are permitted to question the concepts, not just the engineering" — `.../02-pivot.md`.
72. [design direction] The seam is realized as a **deploy-time compiler**, not a runtime planner (D3); pinning is default and the cost-based re-materializer is deferred indefinitely — `.../02-pivot.md`.
73. [value stance] The acceptance test is **closure, not renaming** (D6): "[r]enaming `face → trait` is **not** the win"; the real question is "do `capability` + `container` + `agent` compose on one row with no special-case branch?", and where combination invariants exist the honest metric is the cross-trait matrix shrinking from N rules to M — `.../02-pivot.md`.
74. [design direction] Meaning lives in the open (D7): what a Yi *is* equals the traits it composes, "never which optional field was set at construction, never a parallel `face`/`face_class`/`Kind.face` discriminator" — `.../02-pivot.md`.
75. [design direction] Ports flow through ties (D12): a Yi's invokable ports are those projected by its *is-a* ties (always) union its *holds*-ties to capabilities declaring `confers` (opt-in) — "address the item" and "the holder transparently gains the item's ports" are the same primitive at different tie types — `.../02-pivot.md`.
76. [design direction] Mint authority must live in the open (D16): "authority you can `explain()` beats authority you *assert*" — the `enforce:false` privilege-assertion flag was deleted and replaced by readable license/`can_mint` layers plus a trait-derived gate reading `sys/agent` off the **holder**, never the caller ("that would be privilege-escalation") — `.../02-pivot.md`.
77. [observation] Two membership *relations* exist and must not be fused (D8, sharpened 2026-06-24): session-participation (who is in a thread) and community-standing (who is a member of a community); the law is "one encoding per membership *relation*," not one encoding overall — `.../02-pivot.md`, `.../06-unification.md`.
78. [design direction] Membership became uniform revocable cells and a founder's guaranteed control now flows from **ownership**, not an unremovable roster — separating "**who-is-in** (ties) from **who-controls** (ownership/gates)" — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/08-ideal-architecture.md` (S6), `.../02-pivot.md`, `.../messages.md`.

**The cognitive budget and the unification**

79. [observation] After the representation spine landed, the verdict was "**Representation is unified; meaning is not yet**" — fidelity ≈81/100, with the measured held-concept count at ~17 against the ≤12 target the whole pivot is justified on — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/06-unification.md`.
80. [design direction] **G8**, the cognitive-budget goal, was added so "the program cannot win on features while the concept count silently regrows": CI pins kept-vocabulary ≤12, one Kind authoring front door, one dispatch lane, one Occasion commit spine, context-owned storage, and the absence of alternate representation classifiers — "defended by executable structure and public-surface checks, not asserted by prose" — `.../00-direction.md`, `.../06-unification.md`.
81. [observation] Two mechanisms were "BUILT BUT UNWIRED while marked CLOSED" — the G5 one-graph `explain` (assembled only in a test fixture) and the compiled gate cache — "the one place the docs over-claim" — `.../06-unification.md`.
82. [mechanism] When `explain` was wired it was deliberately implemented as *sibling dispatch*, not an `and(floor, portGate)` node, because the latter "would fabricate a conjunction the runtime never computes — explain would lie" — `.../06-unification.md`.
83. [observation] A CRITICAL read-authorization regression shipped during the surface fold: re-pointing the read surface at the ungated `rt.yi.read` leaked hidden rows and per-spirit private signing keys to any authenticated caller — found by adversarial review and fixed with regression tests — `.../06-unification.md`.
84. [design direction] The principled storage line is "**gated verbs vs ungated substrate**," not "perspectival vs privileged": one gated verb surface per noun over one `@internal` ungated substrate root whose trust boundary is empirically the wire — `.../06-unification.md`.
85. [design direction] The unification plan's discipline: "**code rises to the docs ideal, never the reverse**" — the ideal docs stay pristine and revisions to them are proposed for founder ratification, not applied unilaterally — `.../06-unification.md`.
86. [value stance] Law 10 was kept un-softened despite a disclosed inversion in its own execution: "Do not edit the law to match what happened — that would violate the code→docs discipline." — `.../06-unification.md`.

**The ideal architecture: where unification stops**

87. [mechanism] The governing principle of the end-state: "**Unify the DECLARATION; split the EXECUTION by its real axis**" — the tie hypothesis is CONFIRMED at the level of relation and mental frame and **REFUTED** at the level of "collapse the mechanisms" — `09-sts-bonfire-yi/bonfire-docs-yi/08-ideal-architecture.md`.
88. [observation] The reducers are load-bearing-distinct along a real axis — "timing, residency, failure-mode, ctx-shape, cache-lifetime" — and every grounded attempt to fuse them was a behavior change or a hot-path/security regression — `.../08-ideal-architecture.md`.

## 2. Mechanism

**The three-layer causal chain.** The frame has exactly three conceptual layers and one direction of flow. At the **declaration** layer a product author writes a Kind: a family genesis token, an authored row schema, a set of semantic `facet.*` contributions, explicit `bindFacet.*` cross-context bindings, ports with exact handler artifacts, gates, and interface/presentation surfaces. At the **Kinds control plane** a compiler validates and closes those declarations — family genesis → immutable revision → effective binding → *closed representation plan*. At **runtime** a Yi root carries binding memberships and authored state while Identity, Custody, Containment, Relations and Authority materialize their own component rows; all durable changes join one Domain-scoped Occasion (`01-frame.md`).

The load-bearing move is the **compiler seam**. Because the plan is committed at compile time, runtime never has to ask "what kind of thing is this?" — it reads what the plan says must be materialized. This is what makes "meaning is explicit" (Law 2) enforceable rather than aspirational: the alternative, which the codebase actually had, was inferring a thing's nature from *which optional field happened to be present at construction* (`02-pivot.md`). The pivot's diagnosis is that the system had unified representation (one row, one table, one hardcoded message DAG) while leaving meaning plural (subject/object as stored type, one relationship primitive split ~8 ways) — the exact inverse of the intent, which was "a unified interface over plural representations."

**Identity without category.** A Yi's root fields are deliberately impoverished: UID, Domain, primary binding, timestamps, sequence, Occasion head. Everything interesting is elsewhere and owned. Consequently the question "what is this thing?" has no single answer and is not supposed to: it decomposes into "which bindings does it carry?" (Kinds), "who holds it?" (Custody), "where is it placed?" (Containment), "what is it connected to?" (Relations), "what does it justify?" (Authority). Subject, object, holder, child, endpoint, issuer, container and agent are *positions in context facts*, read as a lens, never stored as a class. The sword can be all of them at once because none of them is a category it belongs to.

**Authority as a compiled composition rather than a bit.** Permission is never a flag on an account. The equipped key shows the full mechanism: Custody records the holder, Containment records the slot occupancy, Authority records the certificate, an explicit `bindFacet.authorityActiveInContainmentSlot` binds the certificate's activation to the placement, and the port gate makes the decision. Moving the key out of the slot removes the standing *by semantic change*, atomically, with no second permission database and nothing to keep in sync. Because every input is a live context fact re-read at the decision frontier, "a cached `allow` is never Authority" and revocation is eager. The delegation algebra layered above it — attenuation-only, deny-precedence, conjunctive target descriptors, presenter/subject separation, conserved budgets by escrow — exists so that autonomous agents can act *inside* the model rather than around it.

**Occasion as the only place change happens.** Every durable multi-owner effect either commits together or not at all; an ordinary durable write outside the coordinator fails closed. This is what lets the system claim that history, watches, recovery, erasure propagation and Authority frontiers all read one source. The stream tier is the honest exception: messages are not Yis, but a stream append still commits its exact Streams effect inside the same Occasion when the operation also changes Yi or context state.

**The counterexamples the sources themselves raise, and their answers.**

- *"Isn't this just a relabeled object hierarchy?"* — Answered in `01-frame.md` §13: "The result is not a relabeled object hierarchy. It is a smaller root combined with stronger semantic owners." Each former ambiguity (runtime category deciding subject vs object; a type label also selecting storage; holder duplicated in payload; permission as an arbitrary product record; audit appended after mutation) is replaced by an explicit owner.
- *"Doesn't collapsing to one primitive make things worse?"* — Yes, and the pivot says so: "Collapsing into an *overloaded* primitive makes resistance worse, not better," and the D6 trap is that moving ~45 Kind fields into ~45 traits "relocates the zoo, it does not dissolve it." The acceptance test is therefore closure (does `capability` + `container` + `agent` compose with no special-case branch?), and where combination invariants genuinely exist the honest report is a shrinking N→M matrix, not a claim of zero special cases.
- *"Should everything be an actor?"* — Rejected. Universal agency "increases what must be reasoned about and expands every gate surface," so addressability is universal and agency is a trait (D2).
- *"Should the mechanisms be collapsed to match the unified concept?"* — Explicitly refuted by grounded probes (`08-ideal-architecture.md`): the north-star tie hypothesis is confirmed for the relation and the mental frame and refuted for the mechanisms, because reducers differ along timing, residency, failure-mode, ctx-shape and cache-lifetime. Feature markers must never enter the C3 MRO; lifecycle and claims must never fold into reactions; `can_mint`/license must never fold into the gate ADT. "A rigorous 'this split is load-bearing, keep it' is as valuable as a unification."
- *"Doesn't one product language imply one graph?"* — No: "One product language does not mean one generic graph." Seven graphs are separately owned with different cycle policies and loss laws, and none may be inferred from another merely because the same two Yi appear in both.
- *"Aren't messages a hole in 'everything is a Yi'?"* — Admitted as a *named* tier rather than hidden: the stream table is a canonical store with a dense per-session `seq` "that no tie expresses," and D9 corrects the earlier undercount of non-Yi centers.
- *"Is the elegance real or rhetorical?"* — The program answers with adversarial review and measurement: fidelity scores (42/100 → 81/100 → a 66/100 synthesis review), a measured held-concept count (~17 vs ≤12), a CI concept budget (G8), and the discovery that two claimed-closed mechanisms were built-but-unwired. The stated rule is "code rises to the docs ideal, never the reverse," and the stated failure mode is declaring victory in prose before the tests exist.

**The dynamic that keeps generating work.** Every unification the program performs creates pressure to unify one level further (bytes → contract → vocabulary → SDK surface → Core entrypoints), and each level surfaces a new class of leak: first field-presence inference, then face-shaped repos, then face-named wire namespaces, then two owners of World product semantics, then Core's export topology. The stated end-state condition is not "no more layers" but that the frame be "true for the **vocabulary**, not just the bytes."

## 4. Genealogy

**Pre-history (2025-08, outside this cluster but presupposed).** The STS paper (`09-sts-bonfire-yi/2025-08-STS-spirit-token-space-paper.md`) posits three primitives — Spirit (agents/identities), Token (symbolic artifacts of status or value), Space (contexts/environments) — as a universal symbolic framework for modeling societies. The Yi cluster is its successor: Spirit survives as an Identity-bearing Yi, Token bifurcates into Custody holdings *and* Authority certificates, and Space dissolves into Domain / World / Containment / session-as-composition. This cluster never argues against STS; it simply stops needing three primitives, because the three became roles over one root.

**The kernel as it stood (through mid-2026-06).** `02-pivot.md` §1 records the pre-pivot layer cake: storage floor, the `Yi` row + **faces** (spirit / item / token / edge), the kind/dimension type system, capability/authz, the `rt.yi.*` runtime spine (`manifest · touch · invoke · read · where · addKind · patch · extinguish · discover · explain`), reactivity, product surfaces. What was already right: type-is-a-thing (Kinds are themselves Yi rows carrying `sys/kind` — "[t]he model describes itself in its own primitive — the property that makes Lisp and RDF low-resistance"), face-agnostic port dispatch, capabilities as held things, and one canonical row with a same-transaction audit log. What was wrong: identity carried by hidden discriminators beside `kinds[]`, a ~13-field canonical `Kind` plus ~45 leftover class-spec fields, "face" naming six unrelated things, two foundational docs contradicting on whether a row discriminator existed, membership encoded three ways.

**2026-06-16→18 — the exploration (`unified-mental-frame.md`, folded in, original not retained).** Diagnosis adopted wholesale, central principle adopted: the author declares relations by intent; the substrate owns representation; every representation is a rebuildable projection of one `Yi`. Its strongest argument — that this seam is already constitutional law and already implemented correctly three times (CQRS row/event; single-writer children + aggregate-at-read; faces-as-projections over one row) — is what makes the pivot "a *finish*, not a rewrite." Refined on three points: build a deploy-time compiler not a runtime planner; sequence subtractions before the router; teach the trait/role frame rather than the (event-sourced) Tie-duality frame.

**2026-06-18 — the pivot (`02-pivot.md`), decisions D1–D16 frozen.** The keystone is relocated from "merge two tables" (already one table since PG migration 001) to "collapse the **face-shaped contract**" — writer, index, lifecycle, public API. Adds D2 (addressability universal, agency a trait, taken from a peer-agent critique), rejects that critique's compatibility-migration strategy as contradicting standing project law, and states the correctness-debt register (the `gate.ts` full-table scan, the `caller-holds` liveness gap, the dead SQL prefilter, the un-revocable `anchorMembers` roster).

**2026-06-21 — `the-frame.md` is frozen and moved.** The concepts page becomes a two-line pointer to `docs/yi/01-frame.md`, which supersedes the contradictory `yi.md` / `ports-and-faces.md` pair. This is the moment the frame becomes the yardstick rather than an intuition — the pivot's own analysis of *why* the system felt unchangeable was that "there is no frozen target to refactor toward."

**2026-06-22 — the unification plan (`06-unification.md`), U0–U5.** Born from a six-agent adversarial review. Verdict: "Representation is unified; meaning is not yet." ## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

**Raised in the corpus**

- **The keystone gap.** `06-unification.md`: `01-frame §2` calls the face-shaped writer/index/API contract collapse "not optional and not deferred," yet HEAD shipped three face-shaped repo APIs, four write builders, two visibility engines and two lifecycle engines — "The BYTES are one table; the CONTRACT above them is not."
- **The concept budget was unmet and unmeasured.** The first actual count was ~17 against ≤12, and "[t]he thing the pivot is *for* is the one thing not on the scorecard."
- **Built-but-unwired while marked CLOSED.** The G5 one-graph `explain` and the compiled gate cache — "the one place the docs slip from their otherwise-exemplary honesty."
- **Elegance work caused a security regression.** Folding the read surface onto the ungated `rt.yi.read` leaked hidden rows and private signing keys (ROB-1/4), with `history`/`subscribe` inheriting it and `countWhere` leaking cardinality.
- **The D6 relocation trap.** Renaming faces to traits, or moving `forkable` into `policy.forkable`, "does NOT reduce the held-concept count"; closure, not relabeling, is the acceptance test.
- **Unification refuted at the mechanism level.** `08-ideal-architecture.md` lists explicit NO-GOs: feature markers into the MRO, lifecycle/claims into reactions, payload behind an SPI, `can_mint`/license into the gate ADT — each a behavior change or hot-path/security regression.
- **Law 10's own inversion.** Subtract-first was violated in execution under goal pressure; the response was to keep the law and name the cost rather than soften it.
- **Two owners for World product semantics.** SDK `runtime-product.ts` (860 lines) and Server `world/catalog.ts` (758 lines) implement the same catalog/create/verify/invoke/Activation/transfer semantics, kept equal only by parity tests: "every new operation or security correction still has two implementation sites."
- **Core's export topology contradicts its internal rigor.** 49 entrypoints, a 787-line root exporting 248 runtime + 294 type names reaching ~737 modules and Node built-ins; "the path name does not predict the role."
- **`std` boundary inversion.** The standard library imports `@bonfire/core/runtime` and `@bonfire/sdk/advanced` and accepts a raw `Runtime`, while SDK test fixtures import `std` — "'intent without isolation'… more pronounced."
- **Activation does not exist.** "[A] general Yi-to-Yi activation requirement and reconciliation lifecycle does not exist," and several declared context lifecycle laws (Containment parent-extinction, Relation endpoint-loss) "are only comments or partial projections."
- **Doc contradiction as a recurring failure.** Two foundational docs disagreed on whether a row discriminator existed; `community.md`/`session.md` taught a retired model; the fix adopted was a precedence rule ("[w]here this page and source-anchored docs disagree, `llms.txt` and the cited source files win") rather than a guarantee of coherence.
- **Open forks left to the founder.** Whether Tie-duality should be the literal taught model, and whether the runtime cost-based planner is ever built or the deploy-time compiler is the permanent ceiling.
- **Named limits of the whole architecture.** No truth about physical events, no legal recognition, no permissionless consensus, no cross-host atomicity, no offline-first, and "not a substitute for product governance and human judgment"; also "[n]ot every social norm should become code."

**New (from this reading)**

- **The naming is philosophically ambiguous and the corpus does not resolve it.** Bonfire's Yi is glossed as 一/太极 ("the one"), while the wider corpus uses 易 (change, the Yijing) for its algebra (R0..R8, Cell256). A reader coming from the 易 side will assume the substrate is named for *change*; it is named for *unity*. Whether this is a pun Ren intends (一 and 易 are both "yi") or a divergence between two projects is nowhere stated, and it materially affects how the substrate should be read philosophically.
- **The Whitehead thread is orphaned.** "Occasion" is Whitehead's term (actual occasion) and "Touch" is explicitly credited to Whitehead, but the process-philosophy reading — that reality is a succession of atomic occasions of experience, not enduring substances — is never developed, even though the substrate's central move (a root with almost no properties, whose being is its participation in occasions) is a process-metaphysical claim. This is the single biggest unexploited theoretical asset in the cluster.
- **"One thing, several truths" vs "graphs do not infer one another" is an unresolved tension in emphasis.** The first says a thing's truths overlap on one identity; the second says overlap must never license inference. Both are correct, but the frame teaches the first as the headline and the second as fine print, and every recorded security incident in the cluster came from the second being violated. A stronger formulation would lead with the non-inference algebra.
- **The concept-count metric may not measure conceptual load.** G8 counts kept vocabulary, `cls*` accessors, dispatch surfaces and capability branches — proxies for *surface* size. A developer's actual burden includes the seven graphs, the ten laws, the four control axes, the three delivery contracts and the lane semantics, none of which are counted. The program's own D6 caveat (a trait-with-validator is not zero-special-case) suggests awareness of the gap without a metric for it.
- **The refusal to unify is doing more philosophical work than the unification.** §16 "What is not in the frame," the keep-as-is discipline, the non-inheritance laws and the "one language, several owned graphs" correction are what make Yi *not* a totalizing schema. This is arguably the corpus's most transferable idea — a general method for bounded unification — and it is currently scattered across four documents with no single statement.
- **The metaphysics is stated only as engineering.** Nothing in this cluster states the ontological thesis in its own right: that identity is bare denotation, that being is composed and revisable, that having/doing/permitting are relations owned by different laws, and that history is the only thing that is not revisable. A reader outside the codebase cannot currently reach the idea.
- **No account of who governs the compiler.** The frame makes Kinds "live governed law," but *who* ratifies a family revision, and by what process a community changes its own constitution, is treated as install consent rather than politics — while `bonfire-use-cases-and-possibilities.md` promises "constitutional packages." The governance of law-change is the missing layer between the two.
- **The `std` sigil path is a genuine AI-safety pattern with no writeup.** "The model's output is the least trusted thing in the system; it must not be the thing that decides what code lands in chat," with validate-clamp-inert-data-host-floor-recoverable-error as the mechanism, is a reusable containment pattern for LLM-authored artifacts and appears nowhere else in the corpus.

It should open with the 間 line ("Bonfire is an encoded realization of 间理") and close by naming what is still missing: activation, governance of law-change, and the philosophy the engineering has been carrying unstated.
