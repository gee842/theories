# The Selfishness Spiral and Narrative-Value Frames (自私螺旋 / 叙事—价值框架 — no fixed Chinese name in these sources; goodbot_cn.md uses 赢家通吃机制, 权力最大化者的自我强化选择, 主导的吸引子)

> Ren's 2025-02..08 formulation of what later becomes the meta-attractor / totalizing-attractor program. Every actor carries a Narrative-Value Frame (NVF): a constructed hierarchy of what is worth pursuing, which values outrank others, and which strategies are acceptable. In a system that rewards selfishness (defined by Ren not as intent but as a behavior pattern that neglects negative externalities and throws values, consensus and morals "under the bus" whenever the self-centered ledger favors it), the reward compounds: winners gain leverage, out-compete cooperative patterns, force others to adopt their rules, rewrite the rules of the game, and finally capture the narrative so that the next generation is constructed to take the winners' logic as "human nature". Winner takes all, and winner justifies all. Once selfishness dominates both power and narrative the society enters a self-tightening dead-lock spiral that reasons on ever more mythology and eventually destroys the generator function (the environment, trust, commons) it feeds on. What makes the present uniquely dangerous is cognitive/consensus lag: the update speed of the "consensus code" (norms, laws, shared understanding) must keep pace with the speed and complexity of change to keep selfishness in check, and in an exponentially accelerating society it cannot, so humans cannot both remain in charge and keep the system stable. The theory's design responses are (a) accelerate collective seeing: an AI whose mission is to teach people how the loops work so that alignment produces trust and trust makes coordination rational (the "good bot"); (b) restructure incentives so accumulation has counter-forces; (c) rewrite the narrative ("fight myth with better myth", a "larger us", regenerative interdependence); (d) hedge against collapse by splitting into many independent smaller entities, like cells or city-states, so that when some collapse others keep evolving; and (e) the theoretically forced but explicitly unproven option of delegating coordination to exponentially growing intelligence.

### C. Narrative-Value Frame (NVF)

28. [mechanism][AI, Ren-edited] Definition: an NVF "is a structured framework through which an entity (individual, organization, or institution) interprets reality, constructs meaning, and justifies actions based on an embedded hierarchy of values. It defines what is valuable, which values and actions take precedence over others, and what strategies are acceptable or necessary to pursue those values—often reinforced through biology, stories, myths, institutional structures, and discourse power." — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md` (the `convo_on_selfishness.md` version lacks "biology").
29. [mechanism][AI] NVFs exist at three levels: individual (perception, justification, decision), collective (emerges when individual NVFs align into paradigms), and dominant collective NVFs that shape future individual NVFs through rituals, moral framing, education, socialization, institutions. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
30. [mechanism][AI] Power–NVF loop: entities embodying an NVF that gain power/wealth/legitimacy reshape institutions (codify laws and incentives), influence norms (make the NVF "natural"/"common sense"), and control discourse (frame rivals as irrational/dangerous); "If power accumulation and the Narrative-Value Frame reinforce each other in a feedback loop, the dominant NVF becomes increasingly entrenched... until a crisis, internal contradiction, or a new narrative disrupts the system." — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
31. [observation][AI] "The very idea of an immutable 'human nature' is itself part of an NVF"; selfishness, endless accumulation, zero-sum competition, hierarchical dominance are "products of historical, economic, and technological reinforcement"; if history had differed "we might take peace, mutual care, and sufficiency as obvious". — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
32. [observation][AI] Even reproduction can be reshaped by NVFs (monasticism, celibacy), "This suggests that no single NVF is inevitable." — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
33. [mechanism][AI] NVFs mutate like genes: "Every act of interpretation is an act of transformation"; drift can be organic (misunderstanding, cultural shift) or intentional (deception, propaganda); survival depends on alignment with external conditions and human cognition. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`, `01-writings-2025/convo_on_selfishness.md`.
34. [mechanism][AI] Individual NVFs shape perception (salience, classification, epistemic authority), justification (what counts as a good reason), and decision-making ("decision-making compass"); illustrated with capitalist/socialist, technocratic/eco-centric, meritocratic/collectivist, patriotic/pacifist, libertarian/social-welfare pairs. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
35. [mechanism][AI] Collective NVFs emerge in stages: (A) initial similarity and small-scale convergence via shared background, networks, language and symbols; (B) coordination and institutionalization when aligned individuals gain power; (C) mass adoption and social reinforcement so that those born into it internalize it as common sense, enforced by social rewards and punishments. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
36. [mechanism][AI] Six channels by which dominant NVFs shape future individuals: media and narrative control (propaganda, cultural production, algorithmic curation); rituals and traditions; law, policy and economic structures (e.g. shareholder-primacy corporate law making co-ops less viable); punishment and deterrence (ostracism, criminalization, career penalties); psychological/emotional conditioning (moral framing, guilt/shame, fear/security framing); information control (historical revisionism, curriculum, academic gatekeeping). — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
37. [observation][AI] Nine "human nature" assumptions exposed as NVF content: endless growth/accumulation; zero-sum competition; work as moral duty; hierarchy as inevitable; rational self-interest as default; transactional relationships; scarcity as unchangeable; reproduction/nuclear family as biological imperative; land/nature/resources as private property. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`.
38. [mechanism][AI, Ren-refined] "For an NVF to function, it requires a collective to embody it—whether that be people, institutions, or soon, AI." — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`, `01-writings-2025/convo_on_selfishness.md`.
39. [mechanism][AI, Ren-refined] Selection pressure on NVFs favors those that: spread fastest; target audiences; create strong identities; coordinate large groups/legitimize power; build positive reinforcement cycles and converge; justify power and convert it into discourse dominance; legitimize stable hierarchies; are hostile and expansionist ("often outcompete others... However, their dominance does not necessarily mean they are beneficial"); provide knowledge, truth and good strategies; align with productivity. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`, `01-writings-2025/convo_on_selfishness.md`.

Collapse modes: lack of sufficient intelligence to navigate complexity ("when environmental complexity outgrows the collective's ability to manage it"); simply outcompeted; alienation by other NVFs; dependency on expansion; overoptimization and resource depletion; loss of contact with reality; feedback loops leading to self-destruction; technological or social disruption. — `01-writings-2025/NVF.md`, `01-writings-2025/convo_on_selfishness.md`.

## 2. Mechanism

**Units and state.** The unit of selection is not the person but the *behavior pattern* carried by persons, institutions and (soon) AIs, and the pattern is organized by a Narrative-Value Frame (NVF): a ranked set of values plus the strategies licensed to pursue them. An NVF must be embodied by a collective to function (claim 38). Individual NVFs filter perception, justification and decision (34); when enough of them align they become a collective paradigm (35); a dominant collective paradigm then manufactures the next generation's individual NVFs through media, ritual, law, punishment, emotional conditioning and information control (36). NVFs mutate at every act of interpretation (33), so the population of NVFs is under selection (39) and subject to collapse modes (40).

**Selfishness as the selected pattern.** Ren's late definition (21–25) makes selfishness an *outcome pattern*, not a motive: an actor whose ledger counts only self-centered measures will externalize any cost (law, trust, consensus, environment) whenever gain exceeds personal cost; a hypocritical variant does the same while sincerely believing itself good; and at the system level the pattern is an attractor that reinforces itself faster than rival attractors and eventually exhausts the "generator function" it feeds on — the tragedy of the commons generalized to trust, norms and ecosystems, and no longer requiring a deciding subject. Two further facets — winning-over-others as the salient metric, which drives short-term optimization into a Nash-style coordination failure (24), and an us-vs-others lens that denies interconnection (25) — explain why the pattern is myopic as well as extractive.

**Loop 1 — compounding (accumulation).** In a system that rewards the selfish pattern, the reward is convertible into leverage (wealth → political influence → rule-writing → more wealth; 6, 18-P1, 19). The loop is positive: "the more you gain, the more leverage you have to gain even more". Cooperative patterns do not compound in the same way and are out-competed (7).

**Loop 2 — rule capture (incentive).** Winners "restructure institutions to reinforce their dominance" (15 stage 2; 30). Ren's own compression is the HTML comment "NVFs define rules of the game, and the rules of the game determine the rewards of the players" (9). Because the rules now pay out for selfish moves, rational observers adopt them: "in order to compete with them they also have to follow their rules" (8), so the system "starts to require" selfishness (18-P2). Ren's Chinese version names this the "appears selfish" effect (67): people are not selfish, they are behaving as if others are.

**Loop 3 — narrative capture (justification).** Accumulated power buys discourse dominance (10, 30). The winners' logic "pollutes narrative space" (14) and is transmitted as common sense to the next generation, which perceives it as "human nature" and therefore unchangeable (10, 31). This is the deepest lock because it removes the ability to imagine alternatives: "even those harmed by the system defend it" (17, 18-P5). The NVF document itemizes the nine "human nature" beliefs this produces (37) and the six transmission channels (36). Ren's formula for loops 1–3 together: winner takes all *and* winner justifies all (11).

**Loop 4 — lag (regulation failure).** Every society has a regulator: the "consensus code" of norms, laws and shared understanding that must be updated as fast as the world changes (43). Balancing loops (redistribution, honor ethics, religious sufficiency norms, shame for hoarding) historically kept compounding "beyond systems regulatory control" in check (12, 84). Acceleration widens the gap between change and the regulator's update rate; in the gap selfishness compounds unchecked (44, 46). Grok adds the reflexive twist: selfishness itself drives acceleration (more tech, higher stakes) and acceleration shields selfishness (18-P4). The NVF collapse list registers the same thing as a property of frames: an NVF fails when its collective lacks the intelligence to navigate complexity (40–41). Under exponential acceleration Ren concludes humans cannot both remain in charge and keep the system stable (45).

**Exhaustion and endgame.** Because the pattern externalizes costs onto the commons broadly defined, the base it depends on is hollowed out; collapse follows when the commons cannot regenerate (23, 26). Ren's endgame taxonomy (51): a MAD-style freeze among few rational survivors; a deliberately frozen power structure by consensus of survivors; a narrative change that lets the survivors see themselves "as a whole"; continued collapse; or winner-takes-all. AI-drafted versions collapse this into five macro futures (53).

**Interventions as loop-breakers.** Each proposed intervention targets a loop. (i) Against loop 3: rewrite the narrative — Meadows' paradigm lever (60), counter-myths (58, 87), the "larger us" (87, 88). (ii) Against loops 1–2: caps and counter-forces on accumulation (progressive tax, quadratic funding, reputation-weighted governance, anti-accumulation feedback loops, tokenized cooperative economies; 61–63). (iii) Against loop 4: raise the regulator's update speed — augment human cognition, slow acceleration, adaptive regulation, or delegate to exponentially growing intelligence (45, 48, 64). (iv) Against the trust deficit that blocks coordination: the good bot, which treats trust as the *output* of shared seeing rather than a precondition, and whose job is therefore to accelerate alignment by teaching people to see the loops (73–74). (v) Against irreversibility: increase sample size by splitting into many independent smaller entities so that evolution continues after partial collapse (52). (vi) Regenerative interdependence supplies the concrete replacement pattern — systems whose outputs feed other systems' inputs (89).

**Counterexamples and answers raised in the sources.**
- *Cooperation happens at scale* (smallpox, ozone, ISS, EU; 88). Answer in the corpus: these are cases where a shared enemy or superordinate goal temporarily re-aligned NVFs (Robbers Cave), i.e., evidence that the narrative lever works, not that the spiral is absent.
- *Some societies endured for centuries with modest inequality* (84). Answer: they contained selfishness internally *and* avoided catastrophic external competition; once drawn into global markets or colonization they collapsed — consistent with the "hostile expansionist NVFs outcompete" selection trait (39) and with Ren's observation that past societies "last longer, or even keep a equilibrium" (12).
- *Dominant NVFs do collapse on their own* (40). Answer: dominance and persistence are decoupled; collapse of a dominant selfish NVF is the spiral's stage 5, not a refutation.
- *AI could be the regulator* (45). Answer: AI trained on the winners' data may "turbocharge the spiral" (49); the cluster splits into AI-as-governor (cognitive_lag, caboc's "Platonic guardian") versus AI-as-teacher (good bot), with Ren's own hedge "Doesn't mean it can work, only in theory".
- *Slowing down avoids the lag* (48). Answer: "Any society that slows down risks being outcompeted by others that don't" — the multipolar trap re-enters at the level of interventions.
- *Facts and moral condemnation should suffice to shift narratives* (86). Answer: the manifesto argues that technocracy and moral-superiority contests reinforce the demagogue's frame; narrative change must address emotion and belonging.

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

**Raised in corpus**
- The central term was undefined for most of the cluster; Ren says so himself ("we didn't define it", `prompt.md`). His definition then shifts the theory from a motive to a systemic attractor, which strains the word "selfishness" — Grok keeps the word but says "This isn't just a human flaw—it's a systemic engine".
- AI as the way out of the lag is simultaneously the way the spiral could complete: "if implemented incorrectly, it could become the final stage of runaway selfishness" (`prompt.md` ChatGPT §5; `grok.md` P7). Ren's own hedge: "Doesn't mean it can work, only in theory".
- Slowing acceleration reintroduces the multipolar trap between societies ("Any society that slows down risks being outcompeted", `prompt.md`).
- Incumbent resistance: "selfish actors will resist any reforms that limit their advantage" (`selfish_sprial.md`); "Actors benefiting from the current spiral have strong motivation to resist or co-opt any movement toward deeper awareness" (`prompt.md` file_8).
- Narrative interventions that shame or lecture backfire; "you don't bring a policy memo to a culture war" (`new_narrative_manifesto.md`) — a critique applicable to the good bot's didactic register.
- ChatGPT's "you may have discovered a new way to frame the root cause" (`prompt.md`) is a flattering AI verdict; the same reply lists Gramsci, Foucault, Marx, Piketty, Axelrod and Moloch as partial prior art.
- NVF theory notes that dominance ≠ persistence and that expansionist NVFs win short-term — which implies a cooperative NVF must also solve the external-competition problem, something `hyperconnected.md`'s precedents only partially address.
- Enduring communal societies collapsed under external pressure (`prompt.md` file_8), a limit on "just change the narrative".

**New (from this reading)**
- Two incompatible AI roles coexist without adjudication: AI as coordinator/governor (`cognitive_lag.md`, `caboc.md`) versus AI as teacher that accelerates human alignment (good bots). The good-bot design is a response to consensus lag that keeps humans in charge, but this is never stated as a choice against delegation.
- "Winner takes all" is a strong claim; the endgame taxonomy (`prompt.md` file_5) itself lists MAD freeze and frozen consensus as alternative absorbing states, so winner-takes-all is one attractor among several. The essays flatten this.
- The cluster asks "is the root cause *just* selfishness?" — a single-root question that sits uneasily with Ren's later style rule "do not reduce plurality to one root" (BRIEF); the NVF apparatus already offers a plural, selection-based answer.
- Acceleration grammar ("exponential", "machine speed", "AI doubles every few years") is smuggled in by the AI drafts and by Ren's own "exponentially accelerating society"; the BRIEF flags this as a later stylistic commitment to avoid. The lag argument does not actually require exponential change, only change faster than the regulator's update — a cleaner formulation is available.
- The historical evidence (Rome 1%/16%, Maya despotism, executive psychopathy 12%, UK collapse-in-a-decade) is AI-sourced with web citations of uneven quality and is not Ren's; the theory's empirical base is essentially untested, as ChatGPT itself proposes ("Test it against real-world data").
- The definition's facet "outcome-driven, not intent-driven" plus the hypocrisy facet make selfishness hard to distinguish from any successful strategy in retrospect; a falsifiable boundary (which externalities, which measurement) is missing.
- "Equality" as the anti-compounding regulator is asserted, not argued: the mechanism says compounding must be capped, not that outcomes must be equal.
- The NVF selection list mixes traits that favor spread (speed, identity, hostility) with traits that favor truth ("provide knowledge, truth, good strategies") without asking when they conflict — exactly the case that produces "mythologies like reasoning".
- The good bots are persona prompts; whether an AI teaching people "how to see" changes behavior is an empirical question no file addresses, and the Jesus persona raises an authority/impersonation problem the manifesto's own critique of top-down narrative would flag.
- `regen.md` and `hyperconnected.md` are generic research reports; their link to the spiral is thematic (interconnection, regeneration) and they do not engage the lag or lock-in arguments.

**Publishable/teachable now**
- `01-writings-2025/grok.md` — the seven-premise deductive statement is coherent, short and mechanism-first; with Ren's definition of selfishness (`prompt.md`) spliced in as Premise 0, it is a teachable essay.

- `01-writings-2025/selfish_sprial.md` — a usable executive summary (five stages, consensus lag, futures, interventions).

**Missing**
- A single authored text in Ren's voice that (a) defines selfishness as attractor from the start, (b) states the NVF→rules→rewards→narrative loop with the lag as the fourth loop, (c) keeps the endgame taxonomy and the sample-size hedge, (d) adjudicates AI-as-governor vs AI-as-teacher, and (e) drops acceleration grammar and the single-root framing.
- Any empirical test (wealth-accumulation curves vs narrative shifts; comparison of societies with and without anti-compounding regulators).
- A Chinese-language version of the core argument (only the good-bot prompt exists in Chinese).
- Explicit connection to the later vocabulary (meta-attractor, totalizing attractor, selection-on-cognition) so the 2025 text reads as version 1 rather than a separate theory.

**One piece of writing that would most advance it**
A 3,000-word essay, "Winner takes all, winner justifies all: four loops and a lag", written mechanism-first in Ren's voice: define the pattern (externality neglect; hypocrisy; attractor exhausting its generator), lay out the four loops (compounding, rule capture, narrative capture, regulator lag), state the endgames including the MAD/frozen-consensus/narrative-change/split options, and end with the two candidate roles for AI (regulator vs teacher of seeing) and the reason the second keeps the possibility space open. Everything needed for it already exists across `compound_selfishness.md`, `cognitive_lag.md`, `prompt.md` (files 5, 6 and Ren's definition), `grok.md` and `NVF.md`.
