# 間 and 文 as formal language — Part B: the 間 ontology series and the wen-* language downloads (2026-05)

> Classical Chinese single characters, taken as they already are, can carry a formal system that European-language formalisms have to reassemble out of pieces. Two tracks develop this. The **間 track** takes 間 (*jian*, "between / interval / among") as a category *prior to* the space/time, container/content, structure/process splits — "事物在间中显现，间在事物的关系中成立" (things appear in 間; 間 is constituted in the relations among things) — and unfolds it in one continuous descent from metaphysics (《理》) through engineering correspondence (《气》), a process calculus (《式》), a component architecture (《构》), a wire protocol (《仪》) and reference implementations (《器》), holding one discipline throughout: what is relational must be *derived*, never stored, and there is no observation from outside. The **文 track** (the same project, explicitly renamed from 間 to 文) claims that the family $R_N = \mathbb{F}_2^N$ — generated from the minimal distinction and closed under composition, relation, operation-as-content, recursion and self-articulation — is the minimal universal normal form of formal articulation, into which the 易经 trigram/hexagram apparatus, the classical Chinese function-word kernel, quantum stabilizer algebra and information conservation all land natively; and that a programming language built on it makes *information conservation and dissipation* a first-class type-level property, which no existing language does. The strongest defensible form of the whole is a two-track program with one shared conviction — that a language whose atoms are already relational, already both-spatial-and-temporal, and already self-referential is not decoration on a formal system but the cheapest available carrier for one — plus an honest ladder of proof obligations that the corpus's own review document (r_family v0.8) sets out.

### B. The 理→气 correspondence discipline

31. 《气》 is explicitly **not** a spec or implementation guide but a *correspondence table*: every core concept of 理 gets both a "shows up as" and a "must not be confused with", the second half guarding against silent substantivization. Its motto: 「理者气之理，气者理之气；离气无理，离理无气」. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
32. Concrete telltale tests are given rather than principles alone: if your code can "create an empty jian" and then "add actors to it", that is container thinking and violates the ontology — a 間 is spawned at the moment its first internal interaction is about to occur; its birth and that interaction are the same event. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
33. **Four derived-not-stored rules**, each with correct form, wrong form, cost of violation, and implementation path: caveat (derived from capability composition + interaction state, not `caveat: AccessRule[]`), visibility (capability-graph reachability, not `visibility: PolicyConfig`), protocol legitimacy (distributed session-actor states, not a central state machine), and jian boundary (active liveness, not `members: Set[ActorRef]`). [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
34. General test for reification: is it an individuated entity with its own boundary, agency and history (reify) or a mode/pattern of ongoing activity (do not reify)? **In the grey zone, default to not reifying** — a reification error is nearly impossible to repair later because everything else builds on top of it. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
35. Explicitly *not* reifiable: the actor-side 心; **relations themselves** (`Relationship(A, B, type, …)` is a reification error); 理; the cosmic 間; and an actor's substantive identity — cross-間 sameness is worldline coherence via capability chain, and reifying it as a global ID / DID / blockchain identity is substance thinking. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
36. Events are the source of truth and state is a cache: "『改变 state』这件事 ontologically 不存在——只有『新 event 发生，导致 derived state 改变』"; event sourcing is therefore an **ontological commitment**, not an architectural choice made for audit/replay/debugging. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
37. **No privilege for "current"**: any moment is one observational cross-section; across 間 in a causal graph there is no universal "now". Do not assume a global clock; causal ordering (the prehension graph) is reliable, linear time ordering is not. [mechanism] (`…/气.md`)
38. Autopoietic health has precise criteria — is the 間 maintaining itself (ongoing internal interaction), producing components (new actors/events/capabilities emerging), and keeping productive structural coupling with its environment? — with three named pathologies: **凝固** (frozen: state unchanging for long periods), **耗散** (dissipating: activity without coherence), **自闭** (autistic: zero coupling). Monitoring should measure these, not just message rate and latency. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
39. Vocabulary discipline: say **spawn**, not create — "create 暗示 substance（creator 制造 created），spawn 暗示 process（activity 生成 new activity）". [design direction] (`…/气.md`)
40. Priority order is fixed and non-negotiable: **paradigm > structure > parameter**. If an optimization requires a paradigm violation, abandon the optimization. Do not allow shared memory to reduce message overhead; do not introduce a global state store for fast query; do not cache in ways that violate autopoiesis. [value stance] (`…/气.md`)
41. And the highest leverage point is *transcending* paradigm: 間 must not become another totalization — "在某些场景（比如纯 computational tasks、no inter-actor coordination）substance ontology 可能更 fit——这种时候不要 force jian". 間's value is in cooperative infrastructure and coordination problems; elsewhere it is unnecessarily heavy. [value stance] (`…/气.md`)
42. The 20-row 理 → 気 → 须避免 reference table plus a seven-question self-check list (reify / storage / observer / boundary / coupling / paradigm / 心) is the operational core of the discipline; failing several checks is **not** a pragmatism-vs-purity trade-off but a sign the design has drifted from the ground. [design direction] (`…/气.md`)

### C. 式 — the Jian Calculus (JC)

43. No existing calculus meets all seven requirements (actor-primary, first-class jian, native nesting, first-class events, capability-as-actor, derived predicates over configurations, perspectival equivalence): π-calculus makes names ontologically prior to processes; Agha's actor calculus lacks native nesting; Distributed π's locations are syntactic wrappers; Mobile Ambients move ambients rather than have them message; join calculus does not match emergent boundaries; E/vat ocap is implementation-level and falls back to π when formalised. Hence a new calculus. [observation] (`…/式.md`)
44. In JC, a configuration is a parallel composition of actors, jians, in-flight messages and events with `(νa)` name binding, and **contains no global state component** — the no-god-view commitment is refused at the level of syntax. Every reduction is local; there is no global reduction step. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
45. Messages do not disappear on receipt — they **reify into events** and enter history: "Message 不消失，只是从 in-flight 转为 historical occasion". Each event `⟨e | π; ξ; ℓ⟩` carries its own prehension set, its content and its location. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
46. Prehension makes history a **DAG, not a timeline**; any linear ordering is a topological sort — a projection, not the reality — and there is **no canonical linearization**, the formal counterpart of relativity of simultaneity. Two observers can disagree about which of two events came first and both be right. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
47. Prehension also carries information content, so a decision's justification can be reconstructed through the prehension graph **without any central audit log**. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
48. Capability is not a primitive but a **forwarder actor pattern**; attenuation is a filter in the forwarder's behaviour; revocation is terminating the forwarder actor — giving revocation a clean semantics with no extra primitive. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
49. `caveat`, `visible`, `legitimate`, `alive` are **predicates over configurations**, never syntactic constructs — and the formal test is stated: a property is "stored" iff it appears in the syntax of Actor/Jian, "derived" iff it is a function over Σ. Any formal system that introduces a "rule" construct should be redesigned. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
50. Liveness `alive(j, Σ, τ)` requires internal activity + self-production + boundary maintenance in a recency window; dissolution is itself a reduction leaving a final event, with the dissolved 間's history joining the parent's and its inner actors released (not killed). Critically, **autopoiesis cannot be simulated by a stored `alive` flag** — the predicate's type signature over Σ-and-recent-history forces derivation. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
51. Perspectival semantics: `Σ↾a ⊊ Σ` **always** (strictly), and reductions entirely inside the part invisible to `a` leave `a`'s view identical though Σ changed; bisimulation is relativized (`Σ₁ ≈_a Σ₂`) and does **not** transfer across observers. "This is more cumbersome — 但 cumbersomeness 是诚实的." [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)
52. Eight invariants — no name forging, causal coherence, local reduction, jian sovereignty, capability discipline, autopoietic preservation, no god view, derived-not-stored — form a **mutually reinforcing web** in which violating any one cascades into the others; they are facets of a single ontological commitment, not independent rules. [mechanism] (`…/式.md`)

### D. 构 / 仪 / 器 — architecture, protocol, instrument

53. Five layers L0 (medium) / L1 (actor substrate) / L2 (間核: jian manager, capability layer, event store, autopoiesis monitor) / L3 (symbiosis: bridges, federation, identity continuity) / L4 (applications), with up-only dependency; and **four conventional layers deliberately do not exist**: no global state layer, no central control layer, no central registry layer, no global monitoring layer. Those absences are derived from 式's invariants, not omissions. [design direction] (`…/构.md`)
54. L1 must be swappable (BEAM, Pony, Akka, Tokio) and deliberately does not know what a jian, a capability, a prehension or autopoiesis is; **L2 is where 間 begins**. If an L4 application bypasses L2 to use L1 actors directly, that is permitted but it is then merely using the actor model, not in the 間 ontology. [design direction] (`…/构.md`)
55. All cross-boundary messages are mediated by the jian process itself — expensive-looking, but honouring "no direct external access"; identity continuity is by persona forwarders to a root identity actor verified by cryptographic challenge, and **default unlinkability** between personas is a privacy commitment (linkability is a capability-disclosure choice). [design direction] (`…/构.md`)
56. 間's performance is explicitly bounded by ontology: capability indirection cost, no global optimizations, replicated-state cost, liveness-check overhead. "間 architecture 不是 universally most performant solution" — its sweet spot is coordination-heavy, relational systems. [observation] (`…/构.md`)
57. **仪 is ritual, not mechanism**: two jians communicating over a bridge are "enact 一段共生关系" — bridge establishment is ceremonial (explicit identification, mutual capability exchange, commitment), dissolution is ceremonial (recorded event, preserved history, cleanly revoked capabilities, no silent timeout), and cross-jian messages carry ritual structure (attested, capability-proven, causally anchored occasions). [value stance] (`…/仪.md`)
58. The wire-level `ActorReference` (`jian://j_outer/j_middle/j_inner/actor_id?cap=…`) is **path-based and perspectival** — different senders may have different paths to the same actor — and is explicitly **not a dereferenceable pointer**: "Reference 是『claim』+『proof』，不是『address』". [design direction] (`…/仪.md`)
59. Capability proofs are certificate chains with issuer/subject/authority/caveats/expiry; delegation never needs to contact the root and can only *narrow* authority; revocation combines expiry, perspectival revocation lists (**no global revocation registry**) and forwarder termination. [design direction] (`…/仪.md`)
60. `EventId` is a content hash, so any two parties holding the same id know they refer to the same event **without being connected**; prehension entries carry optional location hint and fetch capability, and when a fetch capability is revoked the prehension survives as a historical fact that is no longer traversable — "historical knowledge of relation 比 ongoing access 更 fundamental". [design direction] (`…/仪.md`)
61. Attestation has no canonical authority: different attestors record the same factual event from different perspectives and each attestation is valid. [mechanism] (`…/仪.md`)
62. 《器》 commits to **multiple reference implementations rather than "the" implementation**, for three reasons: to keep paradigm separable from technology, to make 仪's cross-implementation promise actually testable, and because different stacks are strong in different dimensions. The correct question about an implementation is never "is it 間" but "does it honour 間's commitments". [value stance] (`…/器.md`)
63. Primary references: **BEAM/Erlang** (native actors, supervision trees, lightweight processes), **Pony** (capability discipline in the type system — the strongest guarantee), **Rust + Tokio** (performance, memory safety); secondary Clojure, Akka/Pekko, Go. 64. Eight named pitfalls that "superficially look like reasonable engineering" but break the paradigm: stored caveat config; a global registry for cross-jian ID; synchronous cross-jian calls; a centralized event log ("even if just for this deployment"); capability as data rather than actor; stored "current state" as authoritative; bypassing the jian boundary for performance ("『Trusted』不是 ontological category——everyone is in some jian"); treating jian as a namespace rather than an actor. ## 2. Mechanism

There are two mechanisms in this cluster, and the interesting thing is that they are not the same mechanism and the corpus does not fully notice this.

**Mechanism 1 — 間 as ontological ground, unfolded by descent.** The claim is that a category exists which is prior to a set of distinctions European formal traditions treat as basic: space vs time, container vs content, structure vs process, subject vs object. The Chinese character 間 already denotes that category, as its ordinary compounds show (人间 the human world, 之间 between, 瞬间 an instant, 间不容发 with no gap even for a hair). If such a category exists, then a formal system anchored on it does not need the reconciliation machinery that substance-ontological systems require in distributed settings — and the corpus reads the endless "special cases, reconciliation work, and 'we have to add another primitive' pain" in distributed systems as symptomatic evidence that substance ontology is an ill fit there.

The causal chain runs downward through six documents, each claiming to be the *same reality in a different register* rather than a derivation:

> 理 (ground) → 气 (engineering correspondence) → 式 (formal reduction system) → 构 (component architecture) → 仪 (wire protocol) → 器 (reference implementations)

At each step the same two constraints propagate. First: **relational character is derived, never stored**. A 間's boundary, visibility, protocol legitimacy and membership are computed from the present capability graph and interaction history, because storing them would convert a relational fact into a substance attribute, and once one is stored the rest follow — the surrounding API accumulates "modify caveat" operations, the system slides back to substance ontology, and every property derived from the ground (autopoiesis, structural coupling, leverage) silently fails. Second: **there is no observation from outside**. Every query carries a source 間; every monitor is itself a 間; $\Sigma{\restriction}a \subsetneq \Sigma$ strictly and forever; bisimulation is relativized to an observer and does not transfer. In 式 these become eight mutually reinforcing invariants — violate one and the others cascade — which is precisely what one would expect if they are facets of a single ontological commitment rather than independent design rules.

The self-maintenance mechanism is autopoietic. A 間 does not exist because a record says so; it exists because its members are interacting, and if they stop it dissolves — not "is deleted" but *ceases as a process*. Dissolution is itself a reduction that leaves a final event, hands the history up to the parent through prehension, and releases the inner actors rather than killing them. This is what makes "no central registry" affordable: nothing needs to track existence, because existence is activity.

**Mechanism 2 — R-Family as forced minimal substrate, unfolded by closure.** Here the chain runs the other way: from a requirement to a structure. Ask what any universal formal substrate must have; get seven properties; observe that each property, followed through, admits essentially one minimal answer; observe that the resulting structure is $\{R_N = \mathbb{F}_2^N\}$ with direct sum, three relational layers, a squaring tower, Hom-as-content, a four-modality carrier and eight atomic operations; observe that this coincides layer by layer with the 易经 cosmological sequence. The feedback loop that makes this self-supporting is **Hom-as-content**: because $\mathrm{Hom}(R_n, R_m)$ is itself an R-Family object, operations on the substrate live inside the substrate, so nothing external is needed to talk about it — which is the precise sense given to 道法自然, and the exact respect in which ZFC (needing first-order logic), type theory (needing a kernel) and category theory (needing a metalanguage) are said to fall short.

The recursion has a natural termination point that is also its self-naming point. Below $R_8$ there is not enough structure to carry a symbol system; at $R_8$ = 256 = (situation, temporal modality) there is; so $R_8$ is where the substrate becomes able to articulate itself — and the project takes its name, 文, from that level. The information-theoretic mechanism sits on top: because the tower's even layers decompose canonically into $R_2$ blocks, information conservation holds in *two* senses (globally recoverable from basis pairings, and block-locally recoverable without cross-block communication), and only even layers have both. That is why odd ranks are IR-internal and never surface, and it is the algebraic root of the programming-language claim: which operations conserve and which dissipate is readable off the type, not proved case by case.

**Where the two mechanisms meet, and where they don't.** They meet at information: 間's "no god view" says every observer has a strictly partial view, and $V_8$'s Information Preservation Theorem quantifies exactly which questions survive a group of transformations ($H$-invariant functionals are exactly the annihilator $H^\circ$; preserved dimension is $8 - \dim H$). They meet at ⊠: gate-closure is 間's "you cannot do inner work before the gate opens" *and* the type-level enforcement of an irreversible information collapse. They also meet at ethics: preserving information is preserving possibility.

They do **not** meet on the status of 間 itself. In the 間 essays, 間 is *the* root category and the system's own name; in the v7 glyph ontology, 間 is one of four 本 under 事; in the R-Family docs the four 本 are 物/動/間/**事** with 心 absent from the Mian matrix entirely. Nor do they meet on totalization: 《气》 §8 explicitly warns "不要把间的 paradigm 当作 universal solution", while Claim Z says formality *is* R-Family articulability. The corpus contains its own answer to the second tension (the v0.8 notes), but not to the first.

**Counterexamples the sources themselves raise, and their answers.** (a) *"Continuous arithmetic isn't natively $\mathbb{F}_2$."* Answer: this conflates encoding with articulation; reals live in $\hat R$ (Cantor space), and IEEE-754's misbehaviour is a property of one encoding, not of the substrate. (b) *"Neural networks aren't R-Family."* Answer: NNs are an implementation technique; the cognitive *functions* computed are computable and hence R-Family — "the training method is engineering, not foundation". (c) *"Qualia aren't formal."* Answer: conceded and welcomed — the claim is delimited to formality; whether phenomenal experience is formally describable is a separate question. (d) *"The 5-fold and 8-fold mappings are arbitrary."* Answer: partially conceded — that there are eight atoms is argued as forced; the *identification* with the eight trigrams is a motivated modelling choice, and the 仁义礼智信 mapping is explicitly "a v0.1 attempt". (e) *"Other substrates might be equivalent."* Answer: fully conceded — any substrate with all seven properties *is* R-Family under isomorphism; "R-Family is the substrate; 'R-Family' is the name for it". (f) *"This is Chinese-philosophy bias."* Answer: the mathematics is independent of naming; the 易经 names are used for historical accuracy, semantic density and mnemonic efficiency, and equivalent Western names work. (g) *"You haven't proven completeness."* Answer: foundational claims are demonstrated by articulation, not proved as theorems — and here the v0.8 review disagrees with the v0.7 answer and demands the theorem be stated with its obligations.

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised in the corpus

1. **Claim strength exceeded proof strength** (`…/r_family_v_0_8_revision_notes.md` §1). The v0.7 substrate document repeatedly conflates "encodable", "interpretable", "usable as a normal form" and "the unique, forced formal foundation". The remedy is three-tier calibration plus the nine proof obligations D1, D2, T1–T8 — with T2 (structure-preserving articulation, not merely encoding), T3 (naturality), T4 (minimality) and T5 (uniqueness up to equivalence) identified as the load-bearing gaps.
2. **The biggest single hole, named in the corpus's own words** (§19.6): "为什么二值 distinction 必然通向 F2-vector-space family，而不是 set、type、Boolean algebra、semiring、category 或其他 foundation？" — why must the minimal distinction lead to $\mathbb{F}_2$-vector-space families rather than to sets, types, Boolean algebras, semirings or categories?
3. **Semantic overlays may be arbitrary** (§19.9, T7/T8). It is not enough that there are eight trigrams and eight atoms; the mapping must preserve independently meaningful structure — that 64 hexagrams ↔ $R_6$ preserves inner/outer trigrams, 错/综/互; that 本/征 preserves the categorical/analytical distinction; that the $R_4$ 16 characters preserve row and column structure rather than being names attached after the fact.
4. **Every cross-domain claim needs independent proof** (§19.10), with specific gaps listed: for UG, the treatment of movement, binding and agreement, plus cross-linguistic tense evidence and sign-language case studies; for phenomenology, why exactly 4 本 × 4 征 and why the 16 are irreducible; for physics, continuum representation, metric/topology enrichment, non-stabilizer quantum extension and any empirical prediction at all.
5. **Wen is pre-alpha and says so** (`…/wen-vs-qpls.md` §7): no standard library, IDE, package manager, tutorials, community, hardware backend or even simulator; PhD-level prerequisites; the formal system is correct on paper but untested at scale; and the cultural framing carries real costs in acceptance and adoption. "2026 年没有 production 工作应该用 Wen."
6. **Decidability is bounded** (`…/wen-info-paradigm.md` §6.1). Composition of first-class tracked operators is decidable; arbitrary extended programs with oracles or runtime reflection are not — that is the halting problem.
7. **$R_5$ has no traditional anchor** (`…/wen-handoff.md` §7.1). It is a provisional `Mian × Bool` placeholder, and a later retrofit could break lift/proj chain consistency.
8. **Lifting is dangerous** (`…/wen-handoff.md` §7.2). One layer promotion can send an entire 道-set into ⊠; whether lift/proj should be an explicit user-facing primitive is undecided.
9. **The 9-atom kernel's evaluators are undesigned** (`…/wen-handoff.md` §7.5). 之 (relational query), 凡 (universal), 是 (reference) are flagged as the non-trivial cases; hexagram bit encoding for cuo/zong/hu is likewise unfixed (§7.7).
10. **The wenyan ↔ Lean 1:1 mapping is unproven** (`…/jian-ontology-v7.1.md` §27.12). Its totality and faithfulness are deferred to Phase 7, with axiomatization as the fallback.
11. **法则 17 may not be provable** (`…/jian-ontology-v7.md` §30.2, Risk 4): 心's non-externalizability may have to be an axiom rather than a theorem — which would make the entire fourth-root justification assumed rather than derived.
12. **Terminology has caused real damage** (`…/wen-handoff.md` §§0, 3.4, 9). "V8" was read as the browser engine and produced an entire discarded WASM/emscripten plan; paper's $V_4$ (16 elements) collides with Klein $V_4$ (4 elements); an unqualified `V4` identifier is banned.
13. **Cross-implementation interop is untested** (`…/器.md` §6). 仪's promise is only validated by paired implementations in different stacks, and no such pair exists.
14. **The self-reference glyphs may not encode cleanly** (`…/jian-ontology-v7.md` §27.9). Three candidate Lean encodings for multi-tier glyphs, none chosen.
15. **The 64-hexagram $V_4$-orbit count is posed and never computed** (`…/v4-foundation (1).md` §10.4) despite being called "可执行的 calculation".

This is not a naming variation — it is a different ontology. Either 事 is the ground *or* it is one aspect among four; it cannot be both, and if 心 is genuinely irreducible (法则 1, 法则 17) then a 16-cell phenomenology matrix without it cannot be complete.
19. **The two tracks also disagree on the 征 set.** R-Family: 幾/勢/機/**時** (limit / momentum / event / halt). v7: 幾/勢/機 plus 萌/绪/韵, with **時** nowhere in the list (艮/fixpoint maps to 時 in R-Family but to nothing in v7). 20. **The atomic bases differ.** R-Family's atoms are the eight trigrams read as ⊤/⊥/rewrite/compose + momentum/fixpoint/limit/event; v7's algebraic bottom is 合/和/寂/空/无 with a semiring structure. Neither document acknowledges the other's atoms.
21. **The anti-totalization commitment and Claim Z are in direct tension.** 《气》 §8 insists 間's paradigm must not be treated as a universal solution and that substance ontology may fit some domains better — this is the corpus's own highest value (keeping the possibility space open, refusing totalizing attractors). Claim Z says formality *is* R-Family articulability by definition. The v0.8 notes fix the epistemic half of this, but nobody notes that the ethical half is a self-contradiction: a project whose stated ethics is anti-totalization has produced a totalizing claim about the space of formal systems.
22. **心 is simultaneously non-reifiable and a first-class type.** 《气》 §3: the actor-side 心 must have no first-class API, no data structure, no capability — "试图 represent 心，就是把它从 immediacy 中拉出来". jian-ontology v7 makes 心 an `OnticRoot` constructor with a reading operator 觉, an invariant 明, three manifestations, three dynamic marks, two internal operators and a canonical inhabitant 吾. 法则 17 (lossy projection to the other roots) is a weaker claim than 《气》's (no representation at all) and does not resolve the conflict.
23. **The 間 series' anti-registry commitment sits oddly with a universal substrate.** 《间》: no global registry, no master coordinator, no universal naming system, no god view. wen-substrate: one universal formal substrate into which everything formal translates. These are compatible in principle (a substrate is not a registry) but nothing in the corpus argues the compatibility, and the rhetoric of the two documents pulls hard in opposite directions.
24. **The Chinese-character thesis is not actually load-bearing in the R-Family core.** wen-algebra Rule 10 and wen-formal §0.2 exclude all traditional terminology from the core type system and freeze the surface as `o`/`x` strings; the characters return only as opt-in application-layer aliases. But the project's distinctive claim — the one that would make it more than "organized linear $\mathbb{F}_2$ algebra" — is that Chinese characters are the right carriers. The isolation rule is good epistemic hygiene *and* it quietly removes the thesis from the part of the system that is being built.
25. **Not one line of Lean in this cluster compiles.** Every code block is a skeleton with `sorry`, `...`, "伪码，需校对索引" or "需具体定".
