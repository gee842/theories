# JIAN / WENYAN as formal language — cluster synthesis A: the `jian` repo (間 → 系 → 文言)

> Classical Chinese (文言) is not a decorative vehicle for a new ontology — it *already is* one. Its grammar encodes a relational-processual ontology directly: content words (实词) are polymorphic primitives whose noun/verb/adjective mode is dispatched by position (structural typing, i.e. the 化⟷位 dual built into syntax), function words (虚词) are typed relational connectives (the 际 dual built into syntax), and the "type" of a passage (理) emerges from composition the way a type-inferred program does. On this reading the whole pre-Qin formal inheritance — 墨家's 同/异/类/故/法, 名家's type-token distinctions, 易传's 几, 五行's positive/negative feedback ring, 医家/兵家's control vocabulary, 道家's 自然, 儒家's recursive 修, 心学's 知行合一 — is not a set of proto-sciences awaiting Western completion but a single interrupted lineage whose native operational vocabulary can be recovered and shown structurally equivalent to type theory, cybernetics, calculus, complex systems, ecology, statistics, information theory, and the three modern mathematical foundations (ZFC / MLTT / ETCS). The project's own name for what it is doing is **名实之辨在数字时代的延伸** — the extension of the name-and-reality debate into the digital age — explicitly *not* a Greek-style ontology of "being qua being." One primitive: **系** (system/tie/relation), whose definitional content is that it contains 系 (重重无尽), with 时 not a parallel primitive but a periodic anchoring *inside* 系. Two working duals: **位 ⟷ 际** (structural; Yoneda: a locus is the sum of its relations) and **化 ⟷ 理** (dynamic; a trajectory and its attractor are one phenomenon under two views). Ten Layer-3 idioms derived from those. And a hard ethical constraint carried through every layer: **反诛心** — no metric may adjudicate anyone on inferred inner states; only speech (言/T) and act (行/E) enter alignment measurement. Above all of it stands the project's own disclaimer: **一切形式皆指月之指** — every formalism is a finger pointing at the moon, never the moon.

### C. Layer 2 — the two working duals

19. Two duals jointly span the ontology: **位 ⟷ 际** (structural) and **化 ⟷ 理** (dynamic). They are Layer 2 working vocabulary, not new primitives — they are 系's working differentiation. [mechanism] — `.../JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §II`; `.../WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §I.2`

20. **位 is not an entity but a position inside 系, with no intrinsic essence**: a locus is what it is entirely by its place — "凡位之是其位，由其际之凡然定," which is the Yoneda lemma (X is determined by Hom(−, X)) stated in 文言. "这不是隐喻——是同一结构." [mechanism] — `.../WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §I.2, §III.4.5`

21. **化 and 理 are not process-vs-structure but one phenomenon under two views**: a trajectory and its attractor, a dynamical system and its phase portrait, a chemical reaction and its rate equation. The Chinese word 化学 encodes it — 化 = process, 学 = pattern, so chemistry is literally "the discipline of the patterns of process." [mechanism] — `.../WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §I.2`; `.../JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §II.2`

22. The four concepts are **mutually defining but not mutually reducible**: 位 = the stable point of 化 / the endpoint of 际; 际 = between loci / the synchronisation structure of 化 / the 理 of 化; 化 = 位 evolving in time; 理 = the invariant of 际. Neither pair alone spans the ontology — (位/际) lacks time, (化/理) lacks structural sites — which is the evidence that the pair-of-pairs is minimum-completeness rather than redundancy. [mechanism] — `.../WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §I.2`; `.../JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §II.3`

### D. 文言 as the surface syntax of Layer 2 — the most novel claim

26. **This part is the most novel**: 文言 is not *stylistically* suited as a reasoning vehicle — it is the **natural surface syntax of the (位⟷际, 化⟷理) layer**. [mechanism] — `.../JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III` ("此部最 novel")

27. **实词 are polymorphic primitives dispatched by position**: 王天下 (王 in verb position = 化 = to-king-ify), 天下王 (王 in noun position = 位 = the king), 王道 (adjectival = 理). 君君臣臣: the first character of each pair is 位, the second is 化. ## 2. Mechanism

**The derivational spine.** Start with one primitive, **系** (xì — system / tie / connection / lineage; etymologically a hand holding silk threads, hence at once the connection, the act of connecting, and what is connected). Its definitional content is that a 系 contains 系: recursion is not an operator applied to the primitive but the primitive's own content (`凡系即系系`). Mathematically this is the same structure as W-types, the cumulative hierarchy, and a least fixed point μX. F(X). Because the recursion *is what it is* rather than being produced, the framework can refuse creation-verbs (造/生/元) without losing generativity.

**Time is folded inside, not laid alongside.** Inside a 系 there are many 化 (processes). One 化 is conventionally designated a standard — a sundial, an atomic oscillation, a heartbeat — and the others measure their pace against it. That designated 化 *is* 时. So time is a periodic anchoring internal to 系, situational rather than absolute: a coinductive stream, a Lamport clock, 庄子's 时机, not Newton's t. This single move is load-bearing: it is what lets two duals rather than three span the ontology.

**Two duals emerge as working vocabulary.** 系含系 plus internal 时 yields four names: 位 (a stable site inside 系), 际 (the generalised inclusion/relation between sites), 化 (系 evolving along 时), 理 (系's time-invariant). The structural dual 位⟷际 is Yoneda: a locus is exhausted by the totality of its relations, so nothing is an entity with an intrinsic essence. The dynamic dual 化⟷理 is not process-versus-structure but one phenomenon under two views — a trajectory and its attractor are the same physical fact, as the word 化学 itself records. The four are mutually defining but not mutually reducible: (位/际) has no time, (化/理) has no site. That is the argument that this pair-of-pairs is minimum-completeness rather than redundancy — an argument the text itself flags as slightly circular, breakable only by the well-foundedness at the bottom of 系含系.

**The grammar is the ontology's surface form.** This is the load-bearing novelty. Content words in 文言 carry latent noun/verb/adjective modes and are dispatched by position — 王天下 activates the verb mode (化), 天下王 the noun mode (位), 王道 the adjectival (理); 君君臣臣 puts both modes of one character adjacent. Function words carry relation type without semantic content — 之 is a genitive edge, 以 an instrumental edge, 則 a conditional edge — and so are 际 instantiated in grammar. The overall pattern of a passage (文气, 章法) is not declared but inferred by the reader, structurally identical to Hindley–Milner type inference. Hence: **实词 = polymorphic primitives typed structurally; 虚词 = typed edges; 理 = inferred type.** Indo-European languages instead lock role into morphology, which is nominal typing. The consequence the project draws is an information-theoretic one: if reality is relational-processual, a relational-processual encoding is minimal, and 文言 approaches the Kolmogorov floor for this task — subject to the trained-receiver prior and to being lossy for procedural specification.

**Idioms are library, not core.** Layer 2 alone is a Peano-minimum: correct but painful, since "修身" would have to expand into 化(位X, 自X, 时T) every time. Layer 3 restores compact working vocabulary while keeping every idiom explicitly reducible: 感 = 化 propagating along 际; 修 = 化 applied to self; 约 = 理 with a time bound, locked by the act of promising; 势 = d化/d时, whose shape is 理(化); 信/诚 = alignment(化(言), 化(行)); 命 = 类 = 理 under a propositional stance; 数 = a von Neumann construction over 系; 心/性/气 as restricted descriptive idioms. Each idiom carries a derivation, a classical citation, and a modern precisification, which is what stops the vocabulary from becoming aphorism.

**The ethical constraint is threaded through the mechanism, not bolted on.** 诚 in its full Confucian sense is the coherence of five modalities — word (T), act (E), body (B), relational standing (R), inner inclination (M) — equivalently, prediction error across modalities going to zero. But only T and E are third-party observable. Inferring the other three in order to judge is 诛心, which the Confucian, Legalist and Mohist traditions jointly condemned. So the engineering metric is confined to alignment(T, E), is graded rather than Boolean, keeps observation sovereignty with the observed locus, admits B/R/M only by consent and never into the judgement, and explicitly separates coherence from truth. Because misalignment has many legitimate causes, the sanctioned response to low alignment is a question, not a verdict. The slogan is that **信/诚 是基础设施，不是道德裁判** — trust measurement is infrastructure, not a moral tribunal.

**The domain layer is the payoff.** Twelve domains — 墨家, 虚字 as logical operators, 名家, 庄子, type theory, cybernetics, calculus, complex systems, ecology, statistics, information theory, 五行 — are each shown to instantiate (位/际, 化/理) with native Chinese operational vocabulary, and none requires a new Layer-2 primitive. The demonstration doubles as a historical argument: 墨经's 大故/小故 is necessary-versus-sufficient ~2,300 years early; 公孙龙's 白马非马 is subtyping; 墨经's 异 is Shannon's bit; 五行 is a mass-balanced 5-node feedback ring; 治未病 is early-warning-signal detection ~2,500 years early; 祖暅原理 is Cavalieri ~1,100 years early. Then §III shows the three modern foundations (ZFC, MLTT, ETCS) each articulable in 文言, which is presented as the deepest evidence for the monism: one structure generating all three.

**The monism closes the loop.** The claim is not "文言 plus mathematics plus systems theory" but "文言 already contains them as viewings" — 关系 = 际, 反馈 = 化's self-loop, 趋势 = 势, 因果 = 故, 系统性思考 = 系含系, 逻辑 = 类/故/法, 数学 = the formal facet. 孔子's 一以贯之, glossed by 曾子 as 忠恕, is read as Layer 2's own description: 忠 ≅ 修 (self-applied, centred), 恕 ≅ 感 (relational propagation). Cross-references between viewings are then evidence rather than repetition.

**Counterexamples the sources themselves raise, and their answers.**

- *"Isn't 'near-global-optimum' overreach?"* Raised in JIAN v1.0 §III.5 and WENYAN §I.3. Answer: three caveats are added rather than the claim defended — the compression offloads the dictionary onto a trained reader's prior (conditional optimum); the language is demonstrably lossy for procedural specification, with Ming–Qing philological conventions and modern bisyllabic technical vocabulary as historical evidence; and optimality depends on the loss function. The claim is narrowed to "near-global-optimum *for high-density expression of relational phenomena*."
- *"Aren't the 八象 just the 易经's eight trigrams smuggled in?"* Raised by the project against itself in v1.0 §IV.10 and WENYAN §I.4.10. Answer: yes, and it is confessed — 8 = 2³, from the doubling scaffold, not from ontological necessity. 八象 is demoted from foundation to vocabulary; only the four Layer-2 names are minimum-complete. v0.8 §III.4 had already admitted the strict minimum was four and that 修/约/势 were promoted for *idiomatic foregrounding*, not independence.
- *"Layer 2's four concepts define each other circularly."* Raised in WENYAN §I.2 限度. Answer: the circle is grounded by the well-foundedness of 系含系 (W-types, regularity, no infinite ∈-descent), and the cross-domain mappings must be verified case by case rather than taken as proof.
- *"Cross-domain isomorphism might be 六经注我 — reading one's own doctrine into everything."* Raised in WENYAN §I.2 and §II.13. Answer: the mappings are argued to be native rather than forced, but the text concedes that some Layer-2 cells admit several reasonable mappings, that the alignment is structural rather than a rigorous bijection, and that monism is a *stipulated position*, not a theorem.
- *"Does grammar-alignment prove the ontology is true?"* Raised in WENYAN §I.3 限度. Answer: explicitly no — "Ontology grammar alignment 不证 ontology truth；只证 文言 适合 articulate 此 ontology."
- *"Isn't a coherence metric a surveillance instrument?"* Raised at length as the 诛心 problem. Answer: the five invariants, graded output, observation sovereignty, consent-gating of B/R/M, the coherence≠truth separation, and the inquiry-not-verdict protocol.
- *"How would anyone know whether they have actually internalised this, or are just fluent in the jargon?"* Raised in WENYAN §0.6. Answer: they cannot know by introspection; the only stable marker is whether their sensitivity to their *own* unseen is increasing, and the direction is diagnostic rather than the level. The author is placed under the same test.
- *"Does 五行 commit the project to a cosmology?"* Raised in WENYAN §II.12. Answer: an explicit fence — the graph structure is kept, the cosmological content refused, and any "X belongs to 木/火/土/金/水" claim is declared idiomatic rather than ontological.
- *"Isn't this cultural chauvinism?"* Raised from v0.3 §XII.5 onwards and again in v1.0 §0.7 / WENYAN §V.2. Answer: the framework does not claim a view from nowhere, is offered as an extension of one tradition, and asks for co-authorship from other backgrounds; the misreading is expected and accepted as a cost.

**WENYAN v1.0 — the final reframe: the subject is 文言 itself.** Explicitly "Replaces `JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md` (now deprecated; §0 drafted content migrated; engineering layer & SST refs removed)." **Best formulation.** **`WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md` is the strongest current statement**, on three grounds: (a) it names the actual thesis, which is about 文言 rather than about a new framework, removing the last trace of "here is my system"; (b) §0, §I, §II and §III are substantively drafted with classical citations, modern precisifications and a per-section 限度 subsection, which is the discipline the earlier versions lacked; (c) it is the version that has performed the most self-correction — renouncing the 易经 scaffold, demoting the 八象, bounding the optimality claim, separating expressive from proof completeness, and installing both ontological and epistemic humility as structural commitments. ## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised inside the corpus

- **Minimality was never proved, only graded.** v0.6 flags it as limit 1; v0.7 answers with the graded architecture; v0.7 §F.3 and v0.8 §III.4 both concede that 修/约/势/理 are specialisations of 化 plus multiplicity, that "strict-minimal Tier 1 might be 4," and that 8 was chosen for idiomatic foregrounding. v1.0 finally accepts the concession and demotes 八象.
- **Derivations are not unique.** v0.6 flags it as limit 2; v0.7 reframes it as perspective and adds a canonical-form rule; v0.8 resolves it via polysemy splitting (unique derivation per *sense*). The reframe is philosophically coherent but means the framework cannot claim a unique decomposition of its own vocabulary.
- **Verification is empirical, not exhaustive** (v0.7 §VI.7): common usages were checked, edge cases may have been missed; derivations require the reader to have internalised the framework; and words like 心/性/情/志 carry millennia of cultural weight that an ontological derivation strips.
- **Quantity words resist pure compositional derivation** (v0.7 §VI.5): 计/量/序/比/平 need an operational rather than structural definition, which v0.8 papers over with 数 ⊂ 际.
- **Contemporary domains were never stress-tested** (v0.7 §XIV.6): the verification sample is the framework's own earlier vocabulary; bioengineering, AI training and quantum physics concepts may break it.

WENYAN §I.4.5 redefines them as belief / reasoning / motivation. "位者，所立之位也。*王阳明 拔本塞源论*", "性也者，与生俱生也。*韩非子*", "求其所以易，因其势也。*吕氏春秋 知度*" and "因之，势之，威之。*韩非子 八说*" all warrant verification. The project now has no worked example of its own central practice.

Under I-信-2 (never output "P is not sincere") and I-信-3 (observation sovereignty), that scenario is now disallowed — but no later version revisits it to say what replaces it. The reputation-Gini health indicator in §VI.10 raises the same question.
- **Structural typing is asserted as a categorical rather than gradient difference,** but classical Chinese does have some morphological and phonological role-marking (使动/意动 usage, 破读/四声别义, the 者/所 nominalisers themselves), and Indo-European languages permit substantial zero-derivation (English *water*, *table*, *fast*). A real corpus experiment — e.g. compressed-length comparison of the same content in 文言, modern Chinese and English, or a held-out prediction test — is both possible and absent, and would convert the project's most distinctive claim from assertion into evidence.

The framework's own criterion — that a formal system is judged by 功用 — has therefore never been applied to itself.

Recovery-framing plus priority claims is precisely the combination that invites the charge of retrojection.

Nothing in the `jian-repo` files says how the two lines relate — whether the v7.x ontology precedes or postdates v0.9, and whether the wen-* algebra is the formal core this series keeps promising.
