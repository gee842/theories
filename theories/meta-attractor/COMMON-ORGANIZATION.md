**Common organization forms when achieved relationships make activities possible together and let their consequences participate in further activity. Regulation differentiates when some of those relationships make a consequential difference elicit a response that changes its onward effects. Regulation remains revisable when consequences can also alter the relationships producing, retaining and selecting the response.** These are explanatory connections to investigate in actual histories; a definition does not guarantee that every organization realizes all three.

**共同组织形成于实际关系使活动能够共同实现、并使其后果参与后续活动。调节是在其中分化出来的关系作用：某种差异引出回应，回应改变该差异的后续影响。持续可修订则要求后果也能改变回应的生成、留存与选择关系。** 这不是预先分开主体、对象和监管者，也不把共同组织等同于共同意志、统一目标或必然的善。

## From formation to regulation

Begin with the activities and their achieved dependencies, following [system individuation](../becoming/chapters/SYSTEM-INDIVIDUATION.md) and the [nondual method](../becoming/chapters/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). A common organization is warranted by what its relationships help realize. Mere simultaneous occurrence, a common label or a shared scalar score is insufficient. Its boundaries express which relationships the explanation follows; they need not divide a ready-made controller from an independently constituted controlled object.

The existing material study provides a specific route. Damage permits formation from available precursor. Association gives a formed component a situated repair role. Repair changes whether source capture can occur. Capture makes support available for subsequent activity. These roles are distinguished by their contributions within the same event history. The analyst's copy-count statistic does not instruct the reactions. The supplied reaction rules explain this local possibility; they do not explain their own chemical origin or guarantee that the preparation occurs spontaneously.

Regulation is already present where damage, repair and renewed capture are coupled. It does not wait for a separate evaluator. Its scope also has to be stated: restoring occupied capacity need not restore the former type composition; repair can succeed while a depleted source prevents capture. These are retained findings of the [organizational-requirement study](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ORGANIZATIONAL-REQUIREMENT-INTEGRATION.md). They distinguish effective regulation of a relation from generic persistence.

A differentiated center can organize this coupling. A distributed arrangement can do so too. Their adequacy depends on delivery, timing, coverage, support and the possibilities for revision. Centralization, multiplication of offices and nominal independence do not settle these questions. Neither common organization nor differentiated centers imply identical purposes among participants.

## From regulation to its revision

Regulation can maintain a response that has become ineffective. What makes revision concrete is a further connection: changed consequences leave an available difference; that difference participates in later selection or construction; the resulting change alters actual response. Recording alone is insufficient. So is an analyst replacing a rule after reading the outcome.

The existing [updater-formation model](../../experiments/shared-renewal-capability/regulation/retention/formation/selectors/updaters/formal/UpdaterFormation.lean) already connects component formation to a paid retooling step, selector formation, renewal, wear and replacement of the mechanism that selects replacements. It establishes more than a sequence of unrelated controllers. However, its witness supplies the command to build the replacement updater. The present continuation makes that command depend on an acquired failure record.

The [new joined construction](../../experiments/market-generativity/meta-attractor/CommonOrganization.lean) uses the same formation law and declared retooling bridge, with a larger finite initial source. The following all occur in one admitted history:

1. Two component types form; one associates, repairs and enables capture.
2. A paid bridge allocates the material organization to the existing selector/updater implementation. No stock is reset or replenished at this interface.
3. An initially undirected updater and a response mapping form. Productive operation renews their lifetimes and releases accounted support.
4. The local response relation changes. Actual trials now reduce the response's lifetime. A retained record registers that reduction and costs one support unit per trial.
5. Repeated failure exhausts the updater's lifetime. Its replacement command reads the record and builds the directed alternative. That updater changes which response can next be assembled; the new response operates successfully.
6. Later productive activity funds another change. When the response relation changes again, the acquired updater assembles the opposite response, which succeeds. Recovery has not made the first successful response permanent.

The replacement command reads retained misses; it does not inspect the hidden response regime. The trial's actual consequences do depend on that regime, as they should. The construction treats lifetime reduction as a faithful observable consequence and includes the receipt apparatus in the supplied module recipe. It does not derive the receipt apparatus, the interpretation of every possible failure, or open-ended invention from unorganized matter.

Two comparisons give this connection substance. A rival implementation pays the same trial costs but erases each reading. Its preparation and pre-replacement material state agree with the successful case; it then rebuilds the old kind of updater, repeats the ineffective response and depletes its operating fuel. Separately, reducing available formation support below the actual assembly cost blocks revision even with the informative record intact. Evidence and the capacity to act on it contribute differently to the outcome.

**新的接通并非仅把局部定理并列：同一付费历史包含形成、修复、取得支持、调节、失败留痕、更新机制的取得和下一次实际修订。** 留痕被擦除的对手有相同前段组织，却再次产生无效回应；经费不足时，有正确记录也不能改组。具体反应律、重整桥接、记录装置、候选类型及事件安排仍是明确给定的局部前提。

## How meta-attraction enters this account

The generative account explains formation and transformation of the relationships. The meta-attractor question asks when the consequences of an organization help reproduce the conditions selecting its recurrence through changing carriers. Regulation and its revision can participate in that reproduction. They are not presumed to stand outside it.

For example, productive operation in the joined model renews the selector and updater. Changed consequences can instead wear them out, make replacement possible, and select a different replacement mechanism. Reproduction is therefore selective and transformable. The new finite history illustrates that relation; it does **not** prove a basin of attraction for this whole combined model. The earlier [recurrence, basin and carrier results](GENERATIVE-REGIMES.md#checked-realization-and-its-failures) retain their own explicit laws and scope.

A constructive regime may reproduce the conditions of its own corrigibility: support for inquiry, usable records, effective routes for changing response, and capacity to carry a transition through. A destructive regime may reproduce arrangements that sever those connections. Neither property follows from recurrence alone. Ethical evaluation remains connected to the established [generative-alignment account](../becoming/chapters/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md); successful renewal does not establish that what is renewed is good.

This changes how we identify a slide. Look for a developing mismatch between the consequences of activity and the organization's effective ability to recognize and revise the relationships producing them. The proposed diagnosis needs concrete evidence: are records still acquired and used; can the affected relations be changed; does support last through the transition; do response timing and coverage still fit the changed situation? A functioning current output can coexist with loss of these capacities. Loss of one capacity is a warning about that connection, not a universal scalar distance to a destructive attractor.

## The mathematical descriptions now have different jobs

Use one encompassing process description, with local coordinates justified by what they preserve. A division into operational state $x$ and regulatory state $r$ is an analytical articulation of that process. It is useful when it preserves the relevant effects of connections, records, support and possible changes. A tuple alone does not establish that adequacy. In particular, two histories with the same proposed rule label can have different available revisions because their support or retained evidence differs.

| Description | What it can represent | What must additionally be established |
|---|---|---|
| Actual event histories and resource accounts | Formation, payment, operation, replacement and failed continuation | Local event laws, interpretation and empirical correspondence |
| Joint update $(x',r')=(F(x,r),G(x,r))$ | Operational and regulatory change in a sufficient representation | A closed description, operative roles and the actual feedback route |
| Covariance-driven proxy weights | One specified mechanism for changing an already supplied score | Centering or a covariance update law, gain, observation population, weight constraints and causal implementation |
| A slow rule equation | A warranted approximation when regulatory change is sufficiently slow | Relevant averaging/tracking hypotheses, retained memory and scope of the approximation |
| Invariant sets, basins and recovery conditions | Stability, approach or reachable alternatives under stated dynamics | The full joint law, disturbances, costs, timing and actual implementation of revision |

The covariance equation can occupy a rule coordinate of the joint model **when that update law is supplied**. This is an embedding, not a derivation of covariance learning from general organization, nor a proof that an unbounded growing weight vector approaches a finite rule attractor. The slow averaged account is a further approximation, not a synonymous notation. The [ledger](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/META-ATTRACTOR-RECONCILIATION.md#mathematical-reconciliation) records the missing conditions and corrections.

The next substantive bridge is **how an organization constructs and revises the apparatus that makes consequences discriminable and replacement candidates available, while its ordinary activity and dependencies continue changing**. Existing method-formation and inquiry results constrain an answer; the joined receipt construction does not yet realize that broader connection in one material history. A credible next result would replace a supplied sensing or retooling interface with a formed, revisable connection and preserve its actual costs and failures. Merely adding another permanent supervisor would not advance it.
