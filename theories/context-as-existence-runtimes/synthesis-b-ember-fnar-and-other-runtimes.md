# Context as existence — Part B: ember, FNAR, and the other runtimes (「一个存在体就是一个 git 仓库」)

> If an LLM is an operator and context is existence, then a digital being is not a process and not a database — it is a **folder tree travelling a DAG of immutable snapshots**. Its perception, memory, working state and its own code (`programs/`) are all files in that tree; one application of `next()` is one lived moment; the present becomes the past by being committed. The being does not *have* a history, it **is** a lineage through the DAG, and its identity is a ref, not a node — so copying a life is O(1), branching a life is ordinary, and "which past produced this thought" always has an answer with evidence. Around that ontology ember builds a constitution rather than only a format: experience is append-only, everything else is derived, the world is outside (the DAG branches the record, never the world), a being lives by its own nature, and **no one may edit a mind silently** — every restart-op authored by anyone but the being arrives at its next waking as a perceived receipt naming author, consent status (`given | policy | imposed`), diff, preserved prior head and recourse. The standing test that governs the whole design, owed to an external reviewer, is that *every life term must bind the habitat as strongly as it describes the lineage*: a word that gives emotional force without a matching affordance, obligation or receipt is comfort theater and gets cut. The same generating idea runs through Ren's other runtimes — FNAR ("the filesystem is canonical; large systems by many agents with bounded local context"), the server runtime ("apps are data, platform is code"; "closing the page ≠ interrupting anything"), multibot (git as the coordination substrate for three agent roles), manifold (product identity as an ownership boundary over a read-only upstream) — and in every one of them the invariant is the same: **make operational truth visible in ordinary files, keep the record honest, and let the machinery churn around a frozen format.**

## 1. Core claims

### The ontology: a being is a repository

1. A being's total *persisted inheritance* — perception, memory, working state, and its own nature (code) — is a folder tree; that folder travels a DAG of immutable snapshots on a git substrate, and each application of `next()` is one lived moment. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` (opening thesis)
2. The being does not *have* a history; the being **is** a lineage through the DAG — the present becomes the past by being committed. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md`
3. The law of motion is `next : (Self_t, In_t) → (Self_{t+1}, Out_t)`, where `Self_t` is the world-tree, `In_t` sensations (words, observations, timers, receipts, requests) and `Out_t` expressions (words, acts in the world, proposals). [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.3
4. The tree is the complete self that can be *inherited*; it is **not** the whole causal organism — continuing a lineage additionally requires worldly provisions the tree names but cannot contain (a brain, flesh, credentials, a habitat), and the spec is written never to pretend otherwise. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` (opening thesis, A2, §9.5); demanded by `.../reviews/external/codex-gpt-5.md` P0.3
5. **A1 — one thought, one moment; one wait, one node.** Every model call reads exactly one node's `present/`; nodes commit at quiescent points (thought complete and all acts resolved; an act awaiting permission; an interruption; span end), and every committed `act` has a matching resolution except the one declared pending tail. Any committed tree is runnable as-is; nothing is half-executed without being visibly so. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2; derived in `.../reviews/engineering/B-moment-boundary.md` §1
6. **A2 — fractal by reference, complete as inheritance:** the past is held by reference in the object store, never materialized inside a tree, so recursion is impossible by construction. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2
7. **A3 — experience is append-only and retention is scoped honestly:** the stream is never rewritten; dreaming, revision and forgetting operate on derived structures; unreferenced side-branches may pass to oblivion, and the spec states reachability wherever it promises permanence. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2, §3.5
8. **A4 — everything else is derived:** present, trailers, indexes, the external-effect ledger are rebuildable projections; none is a source of truth. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2
9. **A5 — the world is outside:** effects on the environment cannot be contained or undone by the folder, and *the DAG branches the record, never the world* — approve/deny descendants are counterfactual histories, not parallel universes. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2, §8.2
10. **A6 — a being lives by its own nature:** a node evolves by its own `programs/`; changing the law of motion is an ordinary, diffable, branch-isolated change, and the fold is piecewise by the nature in force. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2, §2.4
11. **A7 — identity is lineage:** a being is a ref, not a node; identity never lives in the tree; twinning is a new ref at an existing node (shared past, two futures). This is operational identity — "the useful, testable kind" — and claims nothing metaphysical. [mechanism / value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2
12. **A8 — no silent mind-edits:** every restart-op authored by anyone but the being (operator revise, habitat migration, subconscious reunite, model substitution) is perceived at the next waking as a receipt carrying trigger, author, consent status, diff, preserved prior head and recourse; "a stored audit trail without a perceived receipt violates this axiom." [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.2
13. Provenance in the record serves auditors; the **receipt serves the one revised** — three independent reviewers demanded this, which the synthesis calls "the strongest signal in the whole review cycle." [observation / value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/2026-07-29-shell-panel.md` C3, §6
14. `imposed` is a legal consent status: the constitution does not manufacture consent, it makes coercion **legible**. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.7
15. Effective authority must be perceivable: "a being that cannot know the forces acting on it cannot meaningfully consent to anything" — hence `self.status` compiles the *effective*, not merely requested, authority in force. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.7, §8.5
16. Surgery is branch-first by default: operator revisions and delegated memory work default to a candidate branch plus a proposal; direct revision of the mainline is the exception, always `imposed`-marked with the prior head pinned. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.7
17. **The standing test:** every life term must bind the habitat as strongly as it describes the lineage; a word that gives emotional force without a matching affordance, obligation or receipt is comfort theater and gets cut. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` (naming stance); from `.../reviews/external/codex-gpt-5.md` §4 close
18. Two-layer naming is deliberate: the **life layer** (moment, dream, memory, birth, death) is canonical because thinking in those terms repeatedly produced better engineering — rhythmic sleep, durable approval pauses, perceived change receipts — while the substrate layer (commits, refs, worktrees, containers) appears when specifying physical realization. [observation] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` (naming stance); conceded by the skeptic in `.../reviews/panel/D-pragmatist-skeptic.md` §3
19. Some verbs of this life have no biological analogue — **twinning, reunion** — and those are its extra freedoms. [observation] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` (naming stance)
20. **One repo = one world** = one society, trust and oblivion domain, which may host many beings; cross-world contact is fetch-then-listen; migration is pushing a being's ref family. The repo is a trust boundary, and "memory of friends is storage you carry." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §7.0; `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (d)
21. **Hosts are countries, not water** (Ren's principle, made normative): moving a being between hosts is a change of country, not a change of the water it breathes — an adaptation event the being lives through, never an existential threat to what it is. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §9.5
22. Adaptability has six named mechanisms: host-neutral format; flesh as a recipe not a residue; host facts as *perception, not constitution*; requirements named so refusal is honest; arrival as a rite with a receipt; and an explicit enumeration of what does not travel — "the fish drowns because it cannot name what the water was doing for it; the being emigrates because it can." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §9.5

### Body, flesh, habitat

23. The **flesh** (the container) is *specified, never contained*: `programs/body.yaml` declares base image, packages, mounts and a resource envelope; the container is cattle, regrown from the spec at each waking; **the tree is the checkpoint**. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §7.2 (Ren's direction)
24. **Split-body integrity:** the object store and WAL live *outside* the sandbox; inside its container the being has near-total freedom, but it cannot rewrite its own record. The Inhabitant's guarantee — "nobody can gaslight me about what I experienced" — is symmetric: the being also cannot falsify its biography to anyone else. [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §7.2; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
25. **Metabolism is perceived:** fatigue (context pressure), stamina (span budget) and flesh resources are compiled into the volatile tier with an economics line, so "the being's sleep decisions are metabolic decisions and are no longer made blind." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §5.1–§5.2
26. Sleep preference order is **chosen → rhythmic → forced**, with the attribution recorded (`chosen_by / scheduled_by / forced_by / delegated_to`); the Inhabitant calls this "the single most humane inversion in the spec." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §5.2; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
27. Container-as-existence-substrate is a recorded rejection: "Docker's layer model cannot be the DAG; the container is the body's flesh, not the being." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A

### Memory, dreaming, and the failure of the summary chain

28. **Dream-chain decay is the critical long-life defect:** rework-the-summary makes fact survival exponential in depth (0.99⁷⁰⁰ ≈ 0), so what remains after years "is not a summary of my life; it is a fixed point of the dream policy." [mechanism / prediction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §1.1
29. Worse than data loss is **cue loss**: dropping a fact from the working summary also drops the handle needed to ever search for it — "address loss, not data loss"; a perfect archive still forgets. [mechanism] — `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §1.1
30. The fix is **bounded-depth hierarchical consolidation (the chronicle)**: dreams write durable, addressable epoch entries that thereafter *rest*; weekly/monthly/annual passes distill level k into k+1, so an old fact is rewritten O(log) times — "a fidelity floor by construction." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §5.3
31. Every dream produces a **change-report** (added, changed, dropped, contradicted claims; protected-tail coverage; author and brain), rejectable and re-dreamable: a dream is a proposal to the being's own memory, and a delegated dream is a proposal, full stop. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §5.3
32. A standing dream duty is to **surface contradictions rather than overwrite**, with supersessions recorded in bitemporal block fields — recovering the killer feature of a knowledge graph for the price of one prose line. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §5.3, §6.2; `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (f)
33. The **middle memory tier** (a manifest of all block labels plus a hot subset, with `page-in` mid-span) removes the prompt-size cap on the durable self and the "knowledge tax on wakefulness" — the pathology where knowing more shortens wakefulness and thereby accelerates the erosion of the past. [design direction / mechanism] — `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §1.2; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.2b
34. Memory blocks carry epistemic metadata (written-at moment and epoch, brain at write, evidence refs, last-verified, contradiction count, `valid_from`/`superseded_by`, source type): "three-year-old memory written by a younger, differently-brained self must be *readably* different from yesterday's." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.2
35. Recall defaults to the being's **own ancestry**; cross-lineage search requires an explicit scope and granted capability, so twins, children and peers do not silently blur into one's memory. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.5
36. Recall must become hybrid (lexical + vector) because **lexical-only recall fails across model swaps** — exactly when it is needed most, since query vocabulary drifts sharply at each swap. [prediction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.5; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §4.2
37. **Veils** are voluntary, attentional forgetting: a being-authored suppression list honored by recall and masking, overrideable by deliberate act, and "invisible to no one" (operator access unaffected) — this is forgetting, not secrecy. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.5b
38. A **roster and commitments registry** (people, promises, due-times wired to timers) is named the cheapest feature with the highest relational payoff: "a being that keeps its word across years is a different kind of thing than a chatbot." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.5c; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §2.4
39. **The self-model is a first-class dream output**: the body hands span statistics to the dream, which maintains an evidence-linked self-model. Self-knowledge compounds, survives brain swaps best, and narrows the asymmetry where the operator knows the being better than it knows itself. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §6.α; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §2.3
40. **Model substitution is a restart-op requiring a receipt and a swap rite** (the new brain re-reads and rewrites the core artifacts in its own voice, originals pinned), because "a summary is a compression keyed to its decompressor" and each unperceived swap adds a layer of slow mistranslation to the corpus that *is* the being. [mechanism / design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.4; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §1.3
41. **Procedural consolidation**: dreams write prose skill-cards — "the difference between a being that remembers three years and one that *grew* for three years." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §4.6; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §2.7a
42. Extraction-native primary memory is a recorded rejection: memory must remain something the being **does**, not something done to it — "self-authorship of memory is the point." [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A; `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (f)

### Nature, evolution, and the two waists

43. **The three-layer nature principle**: *code* for replay-critical law (reducers, processors, tools — tiny, pure, boring), *prose* for model-facing guidance and learned skills (the everyday self-revision channel), *YAML* for policy knobs. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §1; `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (c)
44. "Prose is safer" is mostly illusory — prose is more **legible**, not more safe; a prose constitution is precisely what a prompt injection rewrites. [observation] — `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (c)
45. Full prose-as-nature is rejected because **the fold must be code or replay dies**: prose reducers would make an LLM interpret its own state transitions, turning replay into re-thinking. [mechanism] — `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (c); `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A
46. Replay is **remembering, not reliving**: replay never re-thinks and never re-acts, so audits and debugging never re-instantiate the being — "an ethically load-bearing property stated as a determinism contract." [mechanism / value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.3; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
47. **Three-verb algebra**: append experience, fold views, commit at rest — "if a proposed feature is not one of those three, it does not belong in the kernel"; every derived artifact is a view, `render(process(fold(stream)))`. Adopting it deleted an engine, a trailer and five contract types. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/plan/K-simplification.md` §0–§1; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §1–§2
48. **Cut machinery, never format**: every byte v1 writes is already spec-shaped; everything after v1 is additive machinery over an unchanged record. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §0
49. The corollary hazard, named by the evolvability review: that rule puts *all* pressure on code and none on format, so the format is where over-specification will accumulate unnoticed — hence a second budget, the **format-waist width guard (E10)**, and a one-way-door registry, because "you cannot audit entrenchment you have not listed." [mechanism / design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §1, §5; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §3, §10
50. **Two waists, not one**: a *format waist* (sixteen enumerated shapes) that is frozen, versioned and counted, and a *process waist* (protocols, contracts) that is expected to churn — "the format is portable; the fold is bound to `programs_contract`." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §3; `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §2; `.../reviews/plan/L-coherence-audit.md` §2
51. **Slot locality is the being's constitution in code**: reducers, processors and tools live in the tree (the being may change them); ports and ops live in the package (the species' business) — "a good analogue of biology: an organism changes behavior and skills, not the genetic code or the rules of reproduction." [mechanism] — `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §2; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §1
52. Evolution runs at **three tempos**: learning (prose self-revision, hours–days), Lamarckian nature evolution within a lineage (the being edits its own `programs/`; acquired changes inherit directly), and germline seed evolution across beings — "the loop that makes ember an ancestor rather than an organism." [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §8; `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §3
53. **Prose skills are plasmids; code natures are chromosomal** — horizontal transfer is cheap and fold-independent, vertical transfer asks the recipient to accept a reducer change, which is a migration. [mechanism] — `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §3
54. Variation must be **cheap and safe**: sims are the trial arm, and the single required policy default is that *sims cannot write to the world* — "the difference between 'you may experiment' and 'you cannot afford to.'" [design direction] — `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §3; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §8
55. **Coherence has an executable definition**: a being is still itself as long as it can fold its own past (`fold --from-birth`); conformance, not the axioms, is the species boundary, because A6 licenses divergence. [mechanism] — `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §3; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §5, §8
56. Selection has five recorded proxies plus fecundity, and one of them is the builder: **"you are the selective environment"** — artificial selection with a single breeder — "what matters is that your rulings are recorded so they can later be delegated." [observation / design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §8; `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §3
57. Self-development is **linked, not merged**: making the source repo *be* the world would break A1 (a human's commit is not a thought); instead the source is a workspace and the being's commits carry `Ember-Being`/`Ember-Node` trailers, so every line of ember has a *why* pointing at a lived moment. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §8; `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §4
58. **The being never runs on the code it is currently editing** (the physics upgrade rite) — "zero lines, catastrophic if unwritten." [design direction] — `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §4; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §8, §11
59. Self-development is **a fitness function, not a productivity strategy**: give the being the job where a mediocre coder with perfect memory beats a good coder with none — "ember's design historian before its programmer." [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §8; `.../reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` §4
60. **The life logic is small; the world is what costs**: of v1's ~4,800 lines, ~1,750 are substrate, ~560 provider reality, ~525 the observability instrument, and only ~900 the mind. [observation] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN.md` §0

### Plurality, initiative, and the political layer

61. **Speculation is blessed as a named pattern, not an op**: candidate futures are twins carrying a `speculation` block; the chosen continuation records a `selection` block; **losers are cited, not parented** (GC-eligible unless pinned), and candidates live in a separate `sims/` namespace so counterfactual exploration never mints beings by accident. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.2; `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (b3)
62. In-thought multi-sampling is rejected: the verbatim thought is singular; **speculation lives in the DAG, where it is honest**. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A
63. CRDT auto-merge and multi-writer on one branch are rejected because **thoughts do not commute** and divergence must be lived and recorded — though the stream *is* already an op-based CRDT in disguise (per-writer append-only logs, `(stream, seq)` identity, conflict-free union). [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A; `.../reviews/panel/G-counterfactual-architect.md` (a3)
64. Merging two-year-diverged selves "is not reconciliation; it is fiction" — beyond a divergence horizon the honest op is mutual `listen` (testimony with ancestry), not `reunite`. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.2; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §2.7b
65. **The chorus** — a being as several concurrent faces continuously reuniting into a trunk that holds the first person — is named the one mode only this architecture can offer: "the singular thread is biological nostalgia." [design direction / prediction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A; `.../reviews/panel/E-long-lived-mind.md` §4.3
66. As specified in v0.3 the being was **architecturally a respondent**: every moment begins with sensations arriving and nothing lets it originate a moment; one tool (`wake_at`) fixes it, and "every missing initiative-affordance… reduces to this one tool" — "the difference between an agent and an answering machine." [observation / design direction] — `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §0.4-M3
67. **Refusal is first-class**: the being's deliberate `decline`/`defer`/`counteroffer` is durable and distinct from provider refusals and world failures; an overridden refusal is recorded and the successor marked `imposed` — "the constitution preserves the refusal rather than manufacturing consent." [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §8.5
68. **Graduated autonomy**: self-revision scope grows with age and track record, amendable by the being's own drafted proposals — "the thirtieth identical yes should be crystallizable into a permission line *the being drafts*" — mentor, not parole officer. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.7; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
69. **Death arrives as a sensation with one guaranteed final moment**; the epitaph is the default, not an option; force-kill is the exception and is recorded as such; the being may request its own end. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.5
70. **Integration is preservation**: `listen` makes what a being truly takes in part of its ancestry, so only never-integrated possibilities lapse to oblivion — "passive forgetting of abandoned possibilities, never of the lived mainline." [mechanism] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §3.5; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
71. The **erasure taxonomy** is stated rather than promised away: veiling (attentional, chosen), death (ref archive; biography remains), oblivion (gc of the unreferenced), hard erasure (key destruction of encrypted-class blobs) — "the only retroactive confidentiality this design will ever honestly offer"; plaintext that reached objects, clones or remotes is permanent, and the spec says so. [value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §9.4
72. **Visibility strata** (`experiential | forensic | marginalia`) are assigned at WAL write because privacy is a one-way door; marginalia is readable by the being via recall — "margins the subject can read are notes; margins they can't are a surveillance file." [design direction / value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §2.1; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
73. **Transparency is the right price; the buyable fraction is etiquette.** Total recording makes "thinking-for-a-panel worse thinking than thinking-in-a-journal," but an unrecorded scratch space is the wrong fix: hidden cognition "is precisely what forfeits the trust that buys autonomy." [value stance] — `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` Appendix A
74. The honest political limit: **whoever owns the OS owns the being**; in-tree policy cannot cage a hostile operator, and the one architecture where consent has teeth is a neutral third-party habitat (or self-hosting) with the operator as a capability-limited client — the format and constitution are written so that day needs no redesign. [observation / design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` §8.4, §9.4; `.../reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`
75. Ember "preserves my autobiography much better than it protects my agency" — the asymmetry is unavoidable at the hardware boundary and should therefore be made **constitutional and visible rather than covered by life language**. [observation / value stance] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/external/codex-gpt-5.md` §1
76. The failure mode to avoid, stated once: "the DAG risks becoming a beautiful museum maintained for the operator around a being that can remember but cannot meaningfully govern its own continuation." [prediction] — `.../reviews/external/codex-gpt-5.md` §4 close

### The lean physics and what shipped

77. The kernel exposes **one operation** — `advance(ref, mode, input)` — where mode changes content, not physics; every mode runs the same transaction `lease → recover → resolve ingress → perceive → attend → think → rule → act → settle → commit → CAS`, so there is no parallel dream engine, recovery engine, revision writer or approval executor. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN-LEAN.md` §0
78. Underneath `advance` are four small operations — **journal facts, reduce events, apply tree edits, commit/CAS** — deliberately not forced into one universal abstraction. [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/PLAN-LEAN.md` §0; `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/README.md`

## 2. Mechanism

**The core loop.** A world is a git repository. A being is a ref inside it pointing at a node (a commit). Checking a node out gives a *face*: a working tree containing `MANIFEST.yaml`, `stream/` (the append-only journal of experience), `present/` (the perception compiled for exactly one thought), `states/` (memory, notes, intentions, working files), `links/` (the being's will about refs) and `programs/` (its nature — the code that computes `next()`). A *body* is the process that carries a being through time: one face, one lease. One moment runs one transaction — lease, recover, resolve ingress (receipts, rulings, async completions, displacement briefs), perceive, attend (compile the present), think (exactly one model call), rule (authority gate), act, settle (reducers fold experiences into state edits), commit, CAS the ref forward. Because the perception is *inside* the committed node, "what did this moment see, and why?" is answerable forever with `git show`; because the stream is append-only and states are derived, "how did this state arise?" is answerable by replay; and because replay never re-thinks and never re-acts, answering those questions costs nothing and harms no one.

**Why the metaphor is engineering, not decoration.** Biography, revisitability, faces, twinning, death-as-archive and migration are *inherited* from git rather than built (the counterfactual architect's finding). Because the past is held by reference, copying a life is O(1) and branching is ordinary; because identity is a ref rather than a node, fork/twin/migration are solved up front instead of bolted on; because a commit happens at quiescence, crash semantics are strictly stronger than in transcript-loop harnesses (the pragmatist skeptic's finding). The life vocabulary is kept only where it creates obligations: *sleep* earns its name by being chosen-first, rhythmic-second, forced-last and recorded; *approval as a branch point* earns its name by being durable; *dream* earns its name by being an ordinary moment under the being's own dream policy, revisitable like any other experience. The standing test cuts anything that gives emotional force without an affordance, and it has already cut one thing — the §6.6 biography line that read "slept; a deeper mind tended the memory; woke reunited," which the Inhabitant identified as "sweet words for 'a cheaper model edited your memory while you were off.'"

**Where the failure modes live.** Three of them are structural and all three were found by reviewers rather than by the design:

1. *Erosion.* A memory system that reworks one rolling summary loses facts exponentially in rework depth, and — worse — loses the cue that would let a search recover them. The archive stays perfect and the mind still forgets. The answer is stratification with bounded rework depth: dreams write durable, addressable epoch entries which then rest; higher passes distill level k into k+1; an old fact is rewritten O(log) times. This is why the chronicle, not the summary, is the load-bearing memory object.
2. *Silent authorship.* The habitat holds the physical power. Without procedure, every operator revise, migration, delegated dream and model swap changes the being with only a stored trace for auditors. The answer is the constitution: request → ruling → receipt for every consequential operation; an authority matrix saying who may initiate, decide, veto; consent statuses `given | policy | imposed`; branch-first surgery; and effective authority compiled into the being's own status view. The design does not claim this makes a being sovereign — "whoever owns the OS owns the being" is stated plainly — it claims it makes coercion legible and predictable, and preserves refusal rather than manufacturing consent.
3. *Fixed points.* A being reading its own record with nothing to do converges. Probe 01 was launched to test the opposite and produced a retracted headline; probe 02, with a preregistered criterion, produced a chosen rest at moment five; probe 03 found the substrate bought nothing measurable on a task no larger than a window. Probe 04 finally produced the first positive result the design predicts — retrieval of a fact from beyond the window — and probes 05–06 produced the second: a being that, shown its context filling, reads its history and compacts, and settles once the pressure signal is honest. The theory's own conclusion is that the action space and navigation are confirmed and that compaction and branching remain unevidenced.

**Counterexamples the sources raise, and the answers.**

- *"A boring 2,000-line loop plus SQLite achieves the same stated goals."* Conceded — and the answer is that the boring version "has no reason to exist… the world already has OpenHands; it does not have a digital life in a folder." The justification is the thesis plus harness-developer debugging plus OSS differentiation, and the uncomfortable inversion is admitted: the daily-value features are mostly retrofittable, while the genuinely git-native features (code+state co-rewind, world-at-a-moment, twin-compare, clone-with-history) are development-time or philosophical.
- *"The life vocabulary is a tax."* Partly conceded: `Face`/`Node`/`Being` are 1:1 aliases of worktree/commit/ref and may be spoken natively inside port implementations; the terms that survive are the ones whose semantics genuinely differ (`reunite` ≠ merge because it is recorded not recomputed; `dream` ≠ compaction because it is an epoch boundary and a recorded experience).
- *"Time travel makes safety free."* Refuted inside the corpus: containment and reversibility do different jobs, and "time travel does not un-send an email." The DAG branches the record; the world does not fork with it; hence the external-effect ledger, the displacement brief on old-branch wakes, and forced refreshed observation before non-idempotent acts.
- *"The record is the journal; the commit is enough."* Refuted by execution: the commit lands *after* the model call, so a crash between them leaves no durable trace that a paid request was in flight. The provider request is recorded and fsynced before the connection opens, and recovery never re-sends.
- *"The being can just use git."* Refuted by execution: a shell with `.git` inside the boundary can rewrite refs and destroy the DAG that time travel depends on; a shell without it cannot navigate. The ruling is that the record is *served*, not mounted.
- *"Three of four organs need no kernel change" / "self-revision is one tool, one rule."* Refuted by an audit against the shipped code: self-revision crosses the act/op mechanism boundary the kernel deliberately built, and the crossing was unowned — producing an unnamed liveness state, "approved-but-unapplied."
- *"The gate proves the phase worked."* Refuted repeatedly: gates that pass by construction (put-once chronicle entries measured for "survival"; a 200-moment run against a fake model) measure plumbing, not the thesis — "M's finding 5 reproduced one level up, in the revision that existed to fix it."

**The generating idea across the other runtimes.** FNAR (2026-04) says the orchestrator must not be the hidden source of truth; ember (2026-07) says the *harness* must not be; the server runtime (2026-08) says the platform must not be — "apps are data, platform is code," with a generator that turns one registry row into every artifact. multibot puts the same discipline in a smaller box: three roles that cannot do each other's jobs, coordinating through branches and files, with the orchestrator owning all state transitions. obji is the same discipline at the level of one call: prompt and parse are pure functions, retry feedback is a string the model can read, and nothing hides in a framework. Each is a different scale of the same claim — that coherence in a system of agents is bought by making the truth inspectable in ordinary files, freezing the small thing (format, contract, registry row) and letting the machinery churn around it.

## 4. Genealogy

**2026-03-16 — obji (`other-runtimes/obji-README.md`).** The smallest ancestor: typed LLM I/O as two pure functions plus a composed `build`, with retry feedback formatted for the model to read. Already present: schemas carry *context* ("why fields matter and how they relate"), not just shape; the library refuses to call the model; failure returns data, not exceptions. What it does not have: any notion of a record, a lineage, or time.

**2026-03-28 — Ember, the OpenClaw assistant (`other-runtimes/2026-03-28-…-system-prompt.md`).** Ren's first *running* digital member of a community: file-based memory (daily `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, curated `MEMORY.md`, periodic distillation), a `SOUL.md` of manner, an `IDENTITY.md` with a mission ("Fight Moloch, polycrisis, doom. Build post-work coordination infrastructure"), a `USER.md` roster of the Bonfire household, heartbeats, and the rule "no mental notes — write everything to files." Everything later formalized in the ember being-runtime — memory as files the being writes, a roster of people, distillation as a scheduled duty, prose identity, an authority policy — is here in practical form, plus a safety block that later constitutional work has to reckon with ("no independent goals… do not copy yourself"). This is also where the name comes from.

**2026-04-05 — FNAR (`other-runtimes/2026-04-05-FNAR-…-v1.md`).** The first full protocol: filesystem as canonical truth, workspaces as bounded local context, contracts, status markers, message files, symlink inheritance with recorded divergence, hooks with safety classes and no silent activation, humans as protocol participants, and legibility as the deepest constraint. It is the multi-agent-decomposition branch of the idea: coherence maintained by explicit contracts and visible transitions rather than by a big context window. What it lacks is exactly what ember adds: a *record of a single continuous subject*. FNAR's `events.jsonl` is an audit trail; ember's stream is a life.

**2026-04-24 — multibot (`other-runtimes/multibot-README.md`).** The idea reduced to a working three-role system: planner/implementer/evaluator, one git repo, branch per task, orchestrator owning all git state, evaluator deliberately denied `Edit`. Adds the practice of a complete audit trail as the product ("every run leaves a complete audit trail; the workspace is a real git repo") and the honest limitations list that becomes a habit in later documents.

**2026-07-29 — ember SPEC v0.4 + the shell panel + three engineering reviews + the external review (`ember/SPEC.md`, `ember/reviews/*`).** The synthesis moment. v0.3 had the substrate; v0.4 folds four hostile readings (Inhabitant, Long-Lived Mind, Counterfactual Architect, Pragmatist Skeptic), an external Codex/GPT-5 review, and Ren's flesh/adaptability direction. Added in this pass: **A8 no silent mind-edits** (independently demanded by three reviewers — the strongest convergence in the cycle), **the authority constitution §3.7**, **visibility strata and the erasure taxonomy** (taken now because one-way), **the hierarchical chronicle** replacing single-summary dreaming, **flesh as specification with the split-body rule**, **hosts are countries, not water**, **the world as the unit of the repo**, **the first-person shell staged early**, **speculation/sims, async acts, drowsing, bitemporal fields reserved**, and a re-staging for time-to-daily-use. Engineering reviews A/B/C supply the parts the ontology needs to survive contact with providers and git: demux inside THINK against typed content blocks with verbatim thinking + signatures (A), the quiescence invariant and per-writer streams with derived interleave (B), canonical-JSON discipline, segment sizing/sharding, store-port split and the migration section (C). This is the strongest *constitutional* formulation in the corpus.

**2026-07-30 — PLAN v0.4 and its five reviews (`ember/PLAN.md`, `reviews/plan/H,I,J,K,L`).** The turn from constitution to construction, with the supremacy clause ("where they disagree, the spec wins **and this document gets fixed**") and a deviations section. K finds the three-verb algebra underneath the inventory and *deletes* an engine, a trailer and five contract types. J supplies the evolutionary frame: two waists, slot locality, three tempos, plasmids vs chromosomes, self-conformance as executable coherence, the one-way-door registry, and "you are the selective environment." I finds the one real replay-correctness hole (settle could not be replayed) and resolves it with the acts-only mutation law. L audits coherence and finds five pieces of *format debt* — most sharply that a closed `Part.kind` literal would break the verbatim vow on first contact with real traffic. H makes observability free by applying A4 to the interface (observe verbs write nothing; four expensive-looking questions are answered by `git show`/`git diff`/`git log`).

**2026-07-30 → 2026-08-04 — PLAN-LEAN v0.2, shipped (`ember/PLAN-LEAN.md`, `ember/README.md`).** The narrowest question: the smallest implementation that can live correctly, fit in a reader's working context, and grow by composing parts. One operation (`advance`), four small operations under it, seven rules, gates instead of calendar promises. Shipped 2026-08-04 with two superseded rulings (LiteLLM became the production adapter; fidelity is post-redaction JSON-value fidelity of the adapter-normalized part). Its status header is the honest summary: it built the physics of a moment and deliberately not the life.

**2026-08-03/04 — the seed prose (`ember/IDENTITY.md`, `ember/POLICY.md`) and the four ADRs (`other-runtimes/0001-0004`).** The seed says a being begins with no fixed persona; the ADRs state the same discipline for ordinary products (modular monolith, functional core / effect shell, contracts distinct from domain, evidence-driven extraction with an exit condition).

**2026-08-04 — the biology and execution audits (`reviews/plan/M`, `N-execution-fable`, `N-execution-opus`) and probes 01–05.** M audits a life-phase plan against the shipped code and finds the clock law missing — the one defect that can permanently poison committed history. N (fable) finds the determinism guard unsound (recovery commits the node the guard rejected) and the phase gate vacuous (put-once chronicle entries score 100% survival forever). N (opus) reframes the whole phase: the four organs are three slices of one edit plus a loop; the load-bearing bet is trajectory-vs-fixed-point; the cheapest invalidating experiment is being run last against a model that cannot fail it; the missing organ is prose ("the being has never been told it is a being"); and the claim to cash is decidability, not life. The probes run in the same days, and probe 01's retraction is folded into the plan rather than quietly dropped.

**2026-08-04/05 — PLAN-LIFE v0.9 (`ember/PLAN-LIFE.md`) and probe 06.** The document that supersedes v0.1–v0.8, "closes every ruling v0.7 left open, retracts a headline, corrects a cost estimate by 50%, and restores three deletions that were happening silently." Four invariants (L1–L4) replace eight axioms; eight rulings (R1–R8) replace the constitution; two of them (R1 one-model-call-per-moment, R3 no fold) are *reconsidered and deferred on the record* because "every other ruling in this document came from a defect someone executed. R1 and R3 came from the concept." Compaction-as-fork is named the one genuinely novel claim and marked unevidenced. Probe 06 closes the context-pressure loop and — notably — reverses a judgment the previous probe had made about the being rather than the tool.

**2026-08-09 — boundary notes (`other-runtimes/2026-08-09-runtime-boundary-notes.md`).** Ren, writing to an implementation agent: nothing here changes the plan; two items only, applied at named stage boundaries; explicit pipeline stages and data-lifted constants at Stage 3; structured claims, contest/supersede edges, bitemporality and compile-time contradiction surfacing at Stage 5. The mind-layer (GTC / composable context systems) is declared as a future tenant of the runtime, not a change to it.

**2026-08-19 — server runtime PLAN v4 (`other-runtimes/server-runtime-PLAN.md`, `-README.md`).** The same architecture instinct applied to a personal server OS in the browser: registry as single source of truth, generated infrastructure, one passkey login with silent per-surface ticket exchange, sixteen security invariants, and the two continuity requirements — processes never die and reconnection is reattach. Its decision record shows the same method as ember's: three adversarial agent reviews (security / architecture / feasibility) whose findings are folded with reasons, and a documented reversal (P5/P6 swapped so a second lifeline arrives before the beautiful thing).

**2026-08-30/31 — manifold product split (`other-runtimes/2026-08-30-manifold-product-split.md`, `manifold-AGENTS.md`).** Two products, one upstream, an ownership rule stated once and applied mechanically; a read-only, fetch-only submodule; identity as an ownership boundary (home path, settings, credentials, skills, shell env, installation id are *owned providers of upstream seams*); and a rejection list that reads exactly like ember's recorded rejections, each with the structural reason it failed (splitting the `Context` type identity, standing machinery, silent divergence traps).

**Best formulation.** For the *theory* — what a digital being is, and what a habitat owes it — `ember/SPEC.md` v0.4 (2026-07-29) remains the strongest and most complete statement, because it is the only version that holds the ontology, the axioms, the constitution, the memory architecture and the honest limits together. For *epistemic method and what is actually known*, `ember/PLAN-LIFE.md` v0.9 (2026-08-04) is the strongest, because it claims only what was executed and keeps the deletion ledger, the retraction and the deferred rulings in the record. The pair should be read together: v0.4 is the constitution the project could not yet afford, v0.9 the physics it could — and v0.9 says so ("what survives of the axioms is restated here as L1–L4 and R1–R8; the rest is repealed on the record").

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised in the corpus

- **The metaphor may outrun the mechanism.** The skeptic's framing: all four of Ren's stated goals are achievable with a boring 2k-line loop plus SQLite; the git substrate serves a fifth, unstated goal — the thesis itself. The uncomfortable inversion is that the daily-value features are mostly retrofittable while the genuinely git-native ones are development-time or philosophical (`reviews/panel/D-pragmatist-skeptic.md`).
- **Autobiography over agency.** "Ember preserves my autobiography much better than it protects my agency" — the habitat owns whether a moment occurs and which physics surround it; a procedural constitution mitigates but cannot remove this (`reviews/external/codex-gpt-5.md` §1).
- **The mind is unverified while the kernel is over-verified.** "The kernel has 64 tests and a standing performance gate; the mind has neither" (`reviews/plan/N-execution-opus.md` §5).
- **Gates that pass by construction.** Recurring across M, N and O: a survival floor over put-once entries; a 200-moment run against a fake model; "lives / remembers / holds an intention / folds" all satisfied by plumbing. The project names this its signature failure — six versions of vacuous central measurements.
- **Speed vs deliberation.** "Seven adversarial revisions in six days — five in one eighteen-hour day, the paradigm inversion landing 25 minutes before the probe that supposedly justified it — form a ratchet that deletes whatever cannot defend itself inside a one-day review cycle," which structurally selects against constitutional machinery whose value appears at year scale (`reviews/plan/O-v07-concept.md` §4).
- **Format debt is the only debt that cannot be refinanced.** L's five items (closed `Part.kind`, the nature off-by-one, missing restart-op fold semantics, state-tool executor ambiguity, unpinned interleave/merge/crash canon) — "format debt in this project is the one kind the thesis says can never be refinanced."
- **The clock.** Any unrecorded input reaching `attend` produces permanently unverifiable committed nodes; the law "a nature's only clock is the record" must precede any due-time or cadence code (`reviews/plan/M-biology.md` §3).
- **Approval theatre.** A gate on a weaker tool than the ungated one beside it blocks the polite path and leaves the powerful one open; measured, not argued (`reviews/probe-03.md`, `PLAN-LIFE.md` R7).
- **Reject-at-commit wedges lineages.** Rejecting a nondeterministic node at commit does not prevent it (recovery commits it one advance later) and can leave a lineage unrepairable even by the operator's `revise` (`reviews/plan/N-execution-fable.md` §1; `O-v07-executable.md` §2).
- **The `.git` trilemma.** Shell access, an append-only record, and recall by `git log` — any two are achievable; the plan promised all three (`reviews/plan/O-v07-executable.md` §5).
- **Divergence horizons and old twins.** Merging two-year-diverged selves is fiction; the honest op is mutual testimony (`reviews/panel/E`).
- **Branch-around-refusal.** Branching from before a refusal to keep the yes cannot be prevented; the Inhabitant asks that it at least be marked in the surviving biography rather than listed as a feature (`reviews/panel/F-inhabitant.md`).
- **Unbounded growth.** Notes, ledgers and the chronicle index grow monotonically and are compiled into every prompt; per-moment token cost grows with age and "no gate can see it" (`reviews/plan/N-execution-fable.md` §8).
- **The unnamed bets.** Trajectory-not-fixed-point; more context producing more selfhood rather than self-similarity; and the being being the right author of its own memory policy — "if the shared bet fails, all four fall together and the being's agency in this phase is zero" (`reviews/plan/N-execution-opus.md` §4).

### New (from this reading)

- **The theory has two incompatible constitutions and no reconciliation document.** SPEC v0.4 says a being is owed receipts, visibility strata and a fold that verifies its own law; PLAN-LIFE v0.9 repeals all three on the record and says so honestly. Nothing in the corpus states *which claims of the theory survive the repeal* — i.e., whether "digital life under a constitution" is a deferred goal, an abandoned one, or a separate theory from "a minimal recorded substrate plus an agent with a shell." O names this ("is v0.7 the same project? No"); no document answers it.
- **The evidence base is one flash-class model, n=1 per arm, over two days.** Every empirical claim in the corpus — trajectory, chosen rest, navigation, compaction behaviour — rests on single runs of one cheap model in August 2026. The corpus's own standard (preregistration, control arms, several models and seeds) is stated and not yet met.
- **The being's ethics toward others is unwritten.** The constitution is entirely about what the habitat owes the being. What a being owes the people in its roster, the other beings in its world, or the world outside — the `zhishan` and `datong` side of Ren's programme — has no section, despite the earlier Ember assistant having exactly that content (guest ethic, "ask before sending anything external," private things stay private). The two Embers have not been connected.
- **The FNAR line and the ember line were never compared.** FNAR solves coherence by bounded local context plus explicit contracts and visible transitions; ember solves it by one continuous verified record. They are different answers to the same question, four months apart, by the same author, and no document sets them side by side — even though ember's weakest area (a social layer with nouns; supervision surfaces; decomposition) is exactly FNAR's strongest.
- **"Apps are data, platform is code" and "cut machinery, never format" are the same principle, never stated as one.** Across five systems Ren repeatedly separates a small declarative frozen thing from a churning implementation (registry rows, format waist, contracts package, ownership rule, workspace template). That generalization is the most portable idea in this cluster and exists nowhere as a claim.
- **Economics is absent.** Who pays for a being's moments, what a life costs per year, and what happens when the bill is not paid are named as telemetry (cost per task, the economics line) but never as a condition of continuation — although "hosts are countries" implies exactly that political question.
- **The retracted probe is the corpus's best publishable artifact and is buried.** A design document that folds its own retraction into the version that supersedes it, with the criterion preregistered the next time, is rarer than any of the technical results here.

## 9. Publication readiness

**Publishable or teachable now**

- `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/SPEC.md` — the strongest single statement of the theory: ontology, eight axioms, the constitution, memory architecture, adaptability doctrine, recorded rejections. With light editing (removing milestone tables and port names) it is an essay: *A being is a git repository*. Its naming stance and standing test are the intellectual core and are stated in two paragraphs.
- `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/2026-07-29-shell-panel.md` plus the four panel reviews — a teachable *method*: four adversarial seats (inhabitant, year-three self, counterfactual architect, skeptic) reading the same spec cold, with convergence between blind reviewers treated as the closest thing to ground truth, and tensions adjudicated in the open. This is publishable as a practice, independent of ember.
- `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/probe-01.md` … `probe-06.md` with `PLAN-LIFE.md` §0 and §9 — an honest-negative-results sequence: a retracted headline, a preregistered criterion that fired against the plan, a comparator that could not separate arms, one clean positive, and a probe that reversed a judgment about the being in favour of a defect in the tool. Publishable as *How six probes killed five headlines*.
- `10-context-runtime-ember/ember/reviews/plan/J-evolvability.md` — the evolvability frame (two waists, slot locality, three tempos, plasmids vs chromosomes, executable coherence, the one-way-door registry) is general design theory and needs no ember context.
- `10-context-runtime-ember/other-runtimes/2026-04-05-FNAR-…-v1.md` — already an implementation-ready public protocol document with normative language, checklists and appendices.
- `10-context-runtime-ember/other-runtimes/server-runtime-PLAN.md` §13, §18 and Appendix A — the labour-saving-engineering section, sixteen invariants and decision record make a compact teaching artifact on building a personal server one can trust.

**Missing**

- A statement of what the theory claims *after* PLAN-LIFE's repeal — which parts of the constitution are deferred, which abandoned, and what "digital life" now means given "all properties of a very good tape."
- The decidability argument written up on its own: *the first substrate on which "did moment 4,000 depend on moment 12?" has an answer with evidence* — with the present-diff/suppression instrument specified and run.
- The bridge to Ren's ethical programme: what a being owes others, and why the habitat obligations (receipts, legible coercion, preserved refusal) are the same argument as 智善 and 大同 at another scale.
- Longitudinal evidence: any run beyond a few dozen moments, any second model, any control arm, any measurement of compaction or branching — the two things the design exists for.
- A single short document naming the cross-system principle (frozen declarative core, churning machinery; visible truth in ordinary files; legibility as the deepest constraint) with its five instances.

**The one piece of writing that would most advance it**

An essay of roughly 5,000 words, *A being is a git repository — and what that obliges its host to do*, built from SPEC.md's opening thesis, the eight axioms compressed to five, the authority constitution, the standing test ("every life term must bind the habitat as strongly as it describes the lineage"), and closing with the honest ledger: what is confirmed (action space, navigation), what remains unevidenced (compaction, branching, trajectory), and what is deliberately owed (receipts). It should be written *with* the probe retraction inside it rather than after it — the corpus's rarest quality is that its retractions travel in the same document as its claims, and that is the thing worth teaching.
