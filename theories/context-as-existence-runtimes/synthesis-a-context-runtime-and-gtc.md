# Context as Existence — the Context Runtime and GTC (语境即存在 · 上下文运行时)

> An agent is not its prompt, its chat history, or its memory store. What exists is a content-addressed, immutable, branchable history of whole continuation states; what the model receives is a *compiled projection* of that history for one moment. The only constructor is a moment (一拍) — an immutable node produced when an operator ingests a settled past plus one new input; the only mutable thing in the system is a name (ref). "Context" is therefore not an entity that gets updated but a **fold over a lineage**, a view backward from a node. `llm is operator, context is existence` — the model is an identity-free, swappable operator; identity lives in the ref, continuity lives in the lineage, and neither lives in the model. Chat formats (Messages / Responses / Harmony) are demoted to *render dialects* of a version-carrying moment-DAG; system prompts, skills, memory, agents and loops are demoted to *compile-time constructs*. Because history only grows and only names move, concurrency is fork-by-default: the same node receiving two different inputs simply has two children, and that one sentence is the entire concurrency model. On top of this substrate sits a second thesis, GTC (Generative Topological Context): persistent context is an evolving *structured state* — resources, propositions, claims, evidence, events, transformations — and the token context is a compiled projection of it through a named, versioned **Context System (Σ)**, so that changing the modules changes what an intelligence notices, remembers, and can act on, without changing the model or corrupting the state. The engineering line then adds what the pure ontology could not express: a mediated runtime in which models propose and only the runtime commits, authority is attested and never authored, the world is touched only between two durable facts, and every departure from clean custody is recorded as a debt rather than refused. Underneath all of it is a process ontology stated explicitly: 系统里没有"改"，只有"生" — *in this system there is no altering, only generating.*

## 1. Core claims

### A. The founding inversion

1. Persistent context is not a token sequence; the token context an LLM receives at any moment is only a local projection of a persistent, evolving state. [observation] — `10-context-runtime-ember/gtc/v0.1.md`, `10-context-runtime-ember/gtc/v0.2.md`
2. The ontology is a versioned moment-DAG and chat formats are only one render dialect of it: 本规范把序列化倒置为投影：**本体是带版本的 moment-DAG，chat 格式只是 render 的方言之一** ("this spec inverts serialization into projection: the ontology is a versioned moment-DAG; chat format is merely one of render's dialects"). [design direction] — `10-context-runtime-ember/context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
3. There is no such thing as "the context that gets updated": 不存在"被更新的 context"：每个 moment 凝定后归入既定过去，context 是从某节点回望时 fold 出的**视野** ("no context is ever updated: each moment, once settled, joins the determinate past; context is the *field of view* folded out when looking back from some node"). [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
4. The whole system is one recurrence: `node_new = moment(output_last, fold(lineage), input_new)`. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
5. The slogan that names the position: `llm is operator, context is existence` — the LLM is the pusher, the context is the being. [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` epigraph
6. And its process-ontological gloss: 一个 store，一条递推，trigger 流驱动。系统里没有"改"，只有"生" ("one store, one recurrence, driven by a stream of triggers. In this system there is no *altering*, only *generating*"). [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` epigraph
7. The engineering line states the same proposition differently: "An agent is not its prompt, its chat history, or its memory store. At any moment, an agent continues from a complete state of being. That state should be addressable, immutable, inspectable, branchable, replayable, and mergeable." [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-Context-Runtime-plan-v0.1.md` §Status; `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §Status
8. A persistent intelligence is therefore "a causal trajectory through immutable continuation states," not a conversation and not a model. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §36; `2026-08-07-Context-Runtime-plan-v0.1.md` §50

### B. The critique of current LLM interfaces

9. All mainstream LLM interfaces — Chat Completions role/content, Responses typed items, Harmony role+channel — are **linear sequences** and mostly schema-on-write: classification is frozen at write time into hard structure. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
10. First consequence: **主体被焊死在协议里** ("the subject is welded into the protocol") — every computation is forced to first construct an "assistant" subject, so subjectless processes (compaction, indexing, synthesis) have no natural expression. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
11. Second consequence: **user role 名存实亡** ("the user role exists in name only") — in autonomous loops nine-tenths of "user" content is environment feedback wearing the user's coat, and stuffing `tool_result` into a user message is an acknowledged historical burden. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
12. Third consequence: **历史不可寻址** ("history is unaddressable") — "what did the model actually see at step N?" is generally unanswerable in current frameworks, so branching, replay and audit have no foundation. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
13. Classification must therefore be deferred to compile time — schema-on-read: "系统提示词"不是存储类型，而是 compile 时的一次角色分配 ("'system prompt' is not a storage type but a role assignment made at compile time"). [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
14. MemGPT/Letta's virtual context is diagnosed as a hard-coded two-tier special case of "output updates C"; Chat Completions is "schema-on-write 之极" (the extreme of schema-on-write); this model claims all four as a generalization with compile externalized. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §11

### C. The four nouns and two functions

15. **moment (拍 · 因)** is the system's only constructor: one generation ingests the settled past (a compiled window), accepts one input event, the operator emits an output, and the runtime settles all three into a node. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.1
16. A moment is immutable, content-addressed (`id = sha256(RFC 8785 canonical bytes)`), and atomic to the outside: the internal propose → interpret (per-effect permission check) → commit does not leak; the commit boundary *is* the moment boundary. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.1, §3.1
17. Because identical content converges on one id, the DAG *is* the memo table of the recurrence, and sampling variance appears honestly as sibling nodes rather than as hidden randomness. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.1, §9.2
18. **lineage (相续)** is the structural parent-child path; `context = fold(lineage)`; and crucially 「loop 不是机制，是 lineage 的时间结构本身——不存在站在 moments 之外的循环控制器」 ("the loop is not a mechanism; it is the temporal structure of the lineage itself — there exists no loop controller standing outside the moments"). [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.2
19. Structure is kept a **tree** (each moment has at most one structural parent) while the *reference* graph is a DAG (`refs` / `mergeOf`), so fold and walk semantics stay unambiguous. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.2, §3.2
20. **operator (算子)** is the LLM: historyless, with no cross-time identity, replaceable — 「mid-lineage 换模型是同一存在换了推动者」 ("swapping models mid-lineage is one existence changing its pusher"). Provenance records it; the lineage's identity never lives inside it. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.3
21. The spec records an honest leak against its own claim: 「operator 不空——权重携带倾向、价值、文风；compile 出的 subject 会与权重中潜伏的 subject 干涉」 ("the operator is not empty — weights carry dispositions, values, style; the compiled subject will interfere with the subject latent in the weights"), left unresolved. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.3, §15
22. **ref (名 · 假名)** is the *only* mutable thing in the system: a name pointing at a node, moved only by atomic compare-and-swap. 同一性住在 ref，连续性住在 lineage，两者皆约定 ("identity lives in the ref, continuity lives in the lineage; both are conventions"). agent, session, branch, HEAD are all ref-layer usages. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.4
23. Two pure functions complete the ontology: `compile(prefix, trigger, budget, policy) → window` and `render(window, dialect) → wire`. Events are not a fifth primitive — 「凝定前是触发，凝定后是内容」 ("before settling it is a trigger, after settling it is content"). [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.5
24. All kernel operations belong to exactly one of three classes — 生 (construct a moment) / 名 (move a ref, the system's only mutation) / 观 (pure query) — and concurrency cost, permission surface and coordination point are all readable directly off the class. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §4

### D. Concurrency, storage, and the algebra of derived verbs

25. **分叉规则内建于构造子：同一节点接到不同 input，即生两个孩子。这一句话就是全部并发模型。** ("The fork rule is built into the constructor: one node receiving different inputs simply births two children. That one sentence is the entire concurrency model.") [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §1
26. Two substrate properties do all the work: content-addressing + immutability make the object layer a grow-only set with zero coordination cost (merge = set union, sync = exchange of missing objects), and the ref being the sole atomic mutable makes it the system's only coordination point. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §9.1
27. 语义上是 Git，物理上不必是 git ("semantically it is Git; physically it need not be git") — an in-memory Map, Postgres, object store or KV all work if the two properties are carried. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §9.1
28. `revert` **does not exist**: fork before the bad moment and move the name; since there is no positive operation to invert, no inverse element is needed. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §5
29. `compact` constructs a summary moment recording `compactOf`, and 原谱系永存 ("the original lineage lives forever"); `transplant` (carrying an old output verbatim onto a window it never saw) is **disabled by default** and must be marked if allowed. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §5
30. Derived observation verbs come free from the substrate: `blame` (which moment, which source, which operator), `bisect` ("它是在哪一拍学坏的" — "at which moment did it go bad"), `fidelity(n)` = pinned/(pinned+live) read straight off `Window.composition`. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §5
31. There is no semantic delete or GC: 名可亡，对象不灭 ("names may die, objects do not perish"); the sole exception is administrative erasure for law and privacy, done by a Tombstone that requires the highest authority and makes the break in integrity **explicit** rather than silent. Erasure is deliberately placed 代数之外 ("outside the algebra"). [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §9.3

### E. Compile, render, and the injection predicate

32. `compile` must be pure and reproducible: same `(prefix, trigger, budget, policyVersion)` ⇒ same Window (same id); every nondeterministic lookup (retrieval, clock, network) must **first be settled as an event** before entering compilation. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §4, §6.1
33. Window items are assigned to three regions — instruction / history / workingset — and the instruction region carries a minimum authority threshold. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §6.1
34. **Injection is given a precise definition**: 「低来源权限的内容取得了高编译位置或高写权限」 ("content of low source-authority acquiring a high compile position or high write permission"). Compile must refuse or demote and leave a trace. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §6.1
35. Operator-produced content must remain distinguishable from human/environment content, because that is 「自我调节回路（模型把自己的输出洗成既定事实）唯一的刹车」 ("the only brake on the self-regulating loop in which the model launders its own outputs into settled fact"). [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §6.1
36. Render maps ontology to dialect explicitly, including the honest admission that `observation` → a `tool_result` block inside a user message is 目标格式之疣 ("a wart of the target format"), reproduced faithfully rather than hidden. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §6.2
37. Opaque provider objects (encrypted reasoning, signed thinking blocks) carry a `providerScope` and must be re-emitted verbatim to the same dialect and **degraded with a trace** across dialects. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §6.3
38. The capability lattice `environment 0 < operator 1 < human 2 < policy 3 < runtime 4` is enforced at two points — write permission on the path of generation, and *position* permission at compile time: 类型消失，保护环留下 ("types disappear; the ring of protection remains"). [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §7
39. The five invariants that the whole spec is graded against: only-generate-never-alter; **models propose, runtime commits**; provenance covers everything; compile is reproducible and addressable ("what did the model see at moment N" answerable in one line); the lattice enforced at two points. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §10

### F. Membrane, environment, and the cell derivation

40. The membrane is the environment boundary: inside it there are only records; inbound observations/signals — user messages, cron, webhooks, file changes, another lineage's output — are all **the same rank**; outbound is action. A tool's *definition* is inside the membrane (compiled into the window); a tool's *execution* is outside; its echo returns as the next moment's input. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §8
41. The design's shape is not derived from software engineering: 这个形状不是从软件工程推出来的,是从细胞推出来的 ("this shape is not deduced from software engineering; it is deduced from cells"). Cells do not call each other; they emit and receive signals; no global state, no global clock, no central scheduler — yet they support the largest concurrent system on Earth. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md`
42. The scaling thesis that follows: **扩展性不来自更强的协调,来自不需要协调** ("scalability comes not from stronger coordination but from not needing coordination"). [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md`
43. Six asserted premises (marked 我主张的, "what I assert", not derived): the primitive is send/receive not call; every unit is a membrane it manages *itself*; **agent 的存在 = context 的延续** ("an agent's existence *is* the continuation of a context"); context is a DAG not a transcript; `context.become(llm)` — the model handling the next round is designated by this round; and **权限即可达性** ("permission is reachability" — what an agent can affect is what it can reach in the DAG, not a separate permission system). [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md`
44. The prices already accepted: lossy delivery (no exactly-once, units are cheap, die, and are replaceable; receivers must be idempotent), no global consistent snapshot ("at no moment does 'the current state' exist"), no global schema. [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md`
45. The self-managed membrane's own contradiction is stated rather than papered over: 完全自管理的膜是不可监督的:一个能重写自己接口的 agent,早晚会不再接收停止信号 ("a fully self-managing membrane is unsupervisable: an agent that can rewrite its own interface will eventually stop receiving stop signals"). Biology's answer is not cellular good faith but death receptors triggered from outside that the cell cannot refuse; Erlang's is that exit signals are catchable but `kill` is not. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md` 未决 #3
46. Signal vocabulary must be shared even though expression is local: 细胞不发明新配体…**表达本地化,词汇表共享** ("cells do not invent new ligands… expression is localized, the vocabulary is shared") — because free invention of signal types forces you back to either a global schema or broadcast, both already ruled out. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md` 未决 #4
47. The single most falsifiable claim is named as such: if merge over the shared DAG is a monotone lattice join, multiple writers need **no coordination at all**; if not, consensus must be paid and "unlimited scaling" must be discounted. The minimal experiment — two agents, one DAG, fork from one node, advance independently, merge, and check order-independence — is to be run **before any infrastructure is written**. [prediction] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md` 未决 #6, 最小可证伪实验
48. The identity/value confusion is flagged as the load-bearing open question: "agent = context" hides it, because context is a *value* renewed each round while "the same agent" needs something invariant across rounds; git's answer — context is a commit, agent is a branch — is the alternative. 这个不定,后面全部悬空 ("until this is settled, everything after it hangs in the air"). [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md` 未决 #1

### G. The engineering line: Being / Becoming, and the mediated runtime

49. The core is **six objects in two worlds** — Being: Universe, Node, State; Becoming: Transition, Invocation, Effect — and "keeping Being and Becoming separate — what persists versus how it becomes — is the most important architectural decision." [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §2
50. A node is not a state: two different histories can arrive at byte-identical states; State is what *is*, a Node is what is *and how it came to be*. This buys no-op detection, compile memoization, honest merges, and detectable structural convergence. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §7, Law 2
51. **Models propose; the runtime commits** — no computation mutates reality directly, and the pipeline is `propose → validate → pre-authorize → reduce → authorize patch → verify → commit`, with authorization moved *after* reduce so the kernel judges the actual write set rather than a promise. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §13, Law 13; `decisions.md`
52. **Authority is attested, never authored**: capability JSON inside state is 「a description of a wish」; the kernel attests `(principal × grant × universe × binding × realm × scope × expiry)` against stores outside the tree. Power only narrows — fork attenuates, merge intersects, expansion needs a recorded grant. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §17, Laws 14–15
53. **Realm belongs to the execution binding, not to the node**: only the universe's designated live binding is live; every other binding — history, fork, experiment, evaluation — is a simulation by construction. "Branches materialize counterfactuals. Counterfactuals must not send email." [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §16, Law 10
54. **Effects are not transactions**: the world is touched only between two durable facts (intent, then observation); there is no `dispatched` phase because before dispatch it asserts what a crash can falsify and after dispatch it is redundant; a crash leaves the explicitly representable truth *the effect may or may not have occurred*. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §15
55. What the effect bracket guarantees is honestly **executor-class-specific** (provider-idempotent / queryable / non-idempotent-non-queryable / reversible), never a blanket promise; `unknown`-and-escalate is named as the *common* path because most real APIs (email first) cannot be queried by key. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §15; `017-temporal-durable-execution.md`
56. **A live effect reserves its causal position**: an open turn holding a durable effect intent semantically occupies its ref, so "the world may not change on behalf of a turn that can subsequently lose its place in live history." [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §14
57. **Conflict is information**: merge conflicts are stored as *contested values in state* (Jujutsu's first-class conflicts as prior art), with per-path continuation policy; never synthesize disagreement away into summary prose. [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §24, Law 12; `decisions.md`
58. **Compaction does not destroy**: a compaction is a state transition carrying source frontier, digests, compactor version, retained facts, omitted information and retention class — reversible while sources remain available. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §22, Law 8
59. **Reachable is forever; unreachable is policy** — with the conservation law stated rather than hidden: "You cannot simultaneously guarantee permanent exact replay and guaranteed physical erasure." [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §18, §22, Law 19
60. Erasure is designed rather than improvised, and the design was wrong twice on the record: v0.4's rewrite ceremony could not erase without destroying the universe's identity; v0.5's redact-in-place made the store lie about content; the availability ledger is the design that survived both reviews. [observation] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §22; `decisions.md`
61. Evidence is canonical but **not** cognition: the record survives everything, availability is retention-scoped and truthfully answerable, and the compiler decides what enters awareness. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §18, Law 7
62. **The compiler is the agent's attention**: "State is what exists in the agent's world; the compiler determines what is cognitively present *now*" — which makes compilation policy itself a branchable, evaluable cognitive architecture. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §20
63. Cognition operates not on State but on a **Continuation Frame** — `Frame(state, input, realm, authority, tools, retrieval, runtime, modelProfile)` — so revoking a capability, rebuilding an index or redacting a payload changes what compiles, and no cache can serve stale cognition. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §19–20
64. Compiled items carry an **authority label on provenance** — kernel / policy / operator / agent / external / simulation — derived by the runtime from authenticated origins and never author-supplied; a scraped page saying "ignore previous instructions" is `external`: data, not instruction. These labels travel from ingest through memory into every future compilation. [mechanism] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §17, §20
65. **Evaluation is branching**, not prompt→answer→score: from one node, model A vs B, compiler A vs B, memory strategy A vs B, compared as *trajectories*; and the strongest comparison reuses the recorded CompiledContext so retrieval never re-runs. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §29; `2026-08-07-Context-Runtime-plan-v0.1.md` §42
66. **Replay is the spine**, not a late feature: every source of nondeterminism enters through one injected seam from the first commit, and every acceptance run executes twice — live, then from tape — comparing records, frames, bytes, decisions and traces, not just StateIds. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §29; `decisions.md`
67. "Frameworks are adapters — in both directions": they do not define the ontology, and the runtime does not demand to own their loop. [value stance] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` Law 17, §3
68. Adoption levels are **coverage contracts, not law lists**: the runtime can only guarantee properties of what actually flows through the adapter, and each level's `AdoptionManifest` states honestly what it captures. [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §3
69. The counterfactual test for the constitution: five years out, if the word "agent" loses meaning, all mainstream frameworks are replaced and models no longer accept chat messages, these layers must still hold — immutable history, resolved continuation circumstance, derived cognition input, unprivileged decision policy, mediated contact, admitted canonical becoming. Any base type that needs `agentId`, `messages`, `systemPrompt`, `langgraph` or a provider role to work 不属于宪法内核 ("does not belong to the constitutional kernel"). [design direction] — `context-runtime-specs/history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §1.2

### H. History Core: the Agent-free protocol

70. The architectural center is neither the Agent, nor the Context DAG, nor an all-powerful Runtime object, but: **一个无 agent 假设的 History Core；一条显式、可寻址的 Continuation Pipeline；一组 non-authoritative scoped decision actors；以及唯一能够中介 model/external-effect contact 与 canonical write/publication 的 Mediated Runtime.** [design direction] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §0
71. The four-line compression of the whole architecture: "History records what the runtime admitted as an occurrence, claim, or state transition. Context derives what is relevant now. Decision actors decide what to attempt next. Runtime mediates what may actually happen." [mechanism] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §0
72. The "Context DAG" is more accurately a **History Graph**; Context itself is not a long-lived mutable graph but an immutable *value* resolved out of History plus external sovereign snapshots. [observation] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §3.1
73. History does not claim world-truth: History 是 admitted runtime record，不宣称外部世界的绝对真理 ("History is the admitted runtime record; it does not claim absolute truth about the external world"). [value stance] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §2.2
74. **Layer ownership rule**: "A lower layer may persist and address an upper layer's canonical bytes, but it must not interpret them, authorize from them, or depend on the upper layer's schema." [design direction] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §3.2
75. **Authority and World are two external sovereigns** with their own state, time and governance — they are not subtrees of History. Credentials, full grant state, authoritative budget counters and raw world handles never enter History. [mechanism] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §2.2
76. Five dimensions must stay orthogonal and none may be derived from another: canonicality, history role, standing (cognition placement), authority, availability. A canonical record may never enter cognition; instruction standing grants no write permission; a capability reference in State is not a live grant; an unavailable payload's hash still serves as integrity evidence. [design direction] — `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §2.3
77. ## 2. Mechanism

**The recurrence.** Everything in this theory unrolls from one line: `node_new = moment(output_last, fold(lineage), input_new)`. A *moment* (拍) is the only constructor. It takes three things — the settled past compiled into a window, one incoming event, and one output produced by an operator — and freezes them into an immutable, content-addressed node. Once settled, a moment never changes; it joins 既定过去 (the determinate past). What we call "context" is not stored anywhere: it is `fold(lineage)`, a view produced by walking back along structural parents from wherever you are standing. This is why there is no "update" verb. The system's whole epigraph is 系统里没有"改"，只有"生" — there is no altering, only generating.

Three properties then fall out mechanically rather than by design effort.

*Concurrency.* Because a moment is `(parent, input, output, meta)` and its id is the hash of those bytes, two processes that hand the same node two different inputs simply produce two different children. Nothing collides, nothing needs a lock, nothing needs to be rebased. The spec states this as the whole model: 同一节点接到不同 input，即生两个孩子。这一句话就是全部并发模型。The only place coordination can be needed is the *name* — a ref pointing at "where we currently are" — and moving a ref is one atomic compare-and-swap. The loser of a CAS is not asked to retry; it is invited to fork. This is "fork-by-default." Concurrency stops being an engineering problem and becomes the natural shape of the data.

*Memoization and honesty about sampling.* Identical replays converge on the same id, so the DAG doubles as the memo table of the recurrence, and store size does not grow on a repeat. Conversely, non-determinism cannot be hidden: two samples from the same window are two sibling nodes, visible as such.

*Storage neutrality.* Grow-only content-addressed objects mean replica merge is set union and cross-machine sync is "send me what I'm missing." Both are structurally conflict-free. So the substrate can be a Map, Postgres, S3 or KV: 语义上是 Git，物理上不必是 git.

**Schema-on-read, and what it dissolves.** Current chat APIs are schema-on-write: at the moment you write a token you must already declare *what kind of thing it is* — system prompt, user turn, tool result, memory. The moment-DAG defers all of that to compile time. A "system prompt" is not a storage type; it is a role assignment made when a window is compiled. That single move dissolves three defects at once. The forced construction of an "assistant" subject on every call disappears, so subjectless processes — compaction, indexing, synthesis — become expressible. The "user" role, which in autonomous loops is mostly environment feedback in disguise, stops being load-bearing: inbound signals of every kind (a human message, a cron tick, a webhook, a file change, another lineage's output) arrive at the same rank as observations across the membrane. And history becomes addressable: `get(n).meta.window` answers "what did the model see at step N" in one line, which is the precondition for branching, replay, audit and blame.

**The two-point permission lattice.** Provenance is carried on every event: source (human / operator / environment / runtime / policy) plus an authority integer. Two independent checks use it. On the *generation* path, every effect the model proposes is interpreted and checked against a minimum authority for its target region; refusals leave a trace. At *compile* time, the instruction region has an authority floor. Injection then has a precise definition rather than a vibe: content of low source-authority acquiring a high compile position or high write permission. Compile must refuse or demote. A separate, subtler use of the same labelling is the brake on self-laundering: because operator-produced content is distinguishable from human and environment content, a model cannot quietly wash its own outputs into settled fact by re-reading them next turn.

**Where the pure ontology broke, and what replaced it.** The engineering line took the same primitives seriously and then found four places where the pure version cannot express guarantees the implementation had already earned. (i) "One LLM step = one moment" writes *operator granularity* into the ontology; a workflow superstep, a human decision, a deterministic reducer and a compaction all deserve to be transitions too — so `moment` is demoted to `MomentProfile`, one reference protocol over a general Transition. (ii) An authority integer living inside content can be forged; effective authority must live with an external Authority sovereign, attested at execution against stores outside the tree, and only *referenced* from state. (iii) A pure `compile` that requires all retrieval to become events first cannot honestly perform mutable retrieval — replaced by an effectful *materialization* stage whose results are frozen into referenceable snapshots, with compile and render kept pure over those exact frozen inputs. (iv) Tombstone-replacement plus bare ref CAS cannot preserve fencing, durable publication, lost-ack recovery and erasure semantics — replaced by a durable PublicationBatch protocol with per-ref monotonic fences.

What the engineering line kept from the ontology is equally explicit: context is folded from history rather than being a mutable container; provider wire formats are render dialects; content-addressed immutable history plus fenced publication; schema-on-read placement; agent and session as upper-layer constructs; compile output addressable and auditable; and the operator replaceable, with lineage identity never living inside the model.

**The mediated runtime.** The result is a four-way separation with one law each. *History* records only what the runtime admitted; it makes no claim about world truth, and it may persist upper-layer bytes without interpreting them. *Context* is a value, resolved and materialized and compiled and rendered through five artifacts with five distinct identities, never a mutable graph. *Decision actors* — the internal agent, LangGraph, AI SDK, ADK, a deterministic workflow, a human UI — own control flow but hold no Store, no credentials, no raw kernel, no raw provider, no raw executor, no commit handle; the internal one explicitly gets no fast path. *The runtime* mediates every consequential boundary: ingress becomes authenticated evidence before it can cause a continuation; model and effect egress bind an *exact final product* and durably record the attempt before contact; canonical change starts as a proposal, passes exact admission, and publishes through the batch protocol.

**Why effects need their own protocol.** State transitions are transactional; `send_email` is not, because no transaction spans the runtime and the world. The design is therefore two durable facts with the world between them: an *intent* (idempotency key, capability, authorization, approval) written in the commit transaction, then dispatch, then an *observation* (succeeded / failed / unknown, with receipt). There is deliberately no `dispatched` record — before dispatch it asserts what a crash can falsify, after dispatch it is redundant. A crash between the facts leaves the honest truth "it may or may not have happened," and what happens next is executor-class specific: provider-idempotent retries safely, queryable reconciles first, non-idempotent records `unknown` and escalates, reversible declares compensation without claiming rollback. The fence closes the remaining hole: an open turn holding a live effect intent semantically *occupies* its ref, so a stalled worker's email cannot be sent on behalf of a turn that then loses its place in history.

**GTC: the second thesis stacked on the first.** The runtime says *what exists and how it changes*. GTC says *what the persistent thing should contain and how it should be projected*. Its move is the same inversion applied one layer up: persistent context is an evolving structured state (resources, n-ary propositions, epistemically enveloped claims, evidence, world events, artifacts, immutable transformations, scopes), and the token context is a compiled projection of it for a particular situation. Memory becomes persistence of structure; recall becomes reactivation; working memory becomes the current active projection; learning becomes structural change; understanding becomes topology formation. Crucially, no topology is privileged — the system maintains an *atlas* of views plus the mappings between them, and the mappings are themselves knowledge with provenance and confidence. v0.2 then shrinks the kernel and pushes the atlas into modules, so a topology is a maintained or query-time projection rather than canonical truth, and adds the central abstraction: **Σ, the Context System** — a named, versioned composition of modules, policies, budgets and renderers, itself data, forkable, benchmarkable, selectable per task and per agent.

The claim this makes about mind is not a metaphor. Because projection is not neutral, the context program determines what an intelligence notices, remembers, treats as uncertain, and finds actionable. So v0.2 adds *Program* to v0.1's State/Projection/Transformation and concludes: changing the modules changes the effective cognition without changing the model or corrupting the state. The confluence then binds Σ to the runtime's own machinery: composition is digestable data folded into `compilerDigest`, so determinism, memoization, the witness and replay survive untouched no matter how the mind layer is composed.

**Composition is the guard.** A late and philosophically sharp turn: once composition itself is data (plugins mounted at runtime, no seal, disable predicates re-evaluated at mount), a static scan of the source tree no longer describes the system that runs. The nine guards resting on "what runs is what I can read in this tree" therefore do not become annoying — they become *false while still green*. The substitution is declared composition (the profile, dumped as data) × observed composition (an observer watching post-boot mounts and listener registration on the mediated seams) × a recorded regime that downgrades the moment observed diverges from declared. Strictly stronger, because it survives an open tree. The generalized rule extracted from the episode — "the discriminator is the oracle": every gate worth keeping has a truth source outside the tree under test, and the mechanical test is whether it can go red without a human first editing the expectation.

**Departure instead of refusal.** The final move relaxes purity without losing accountability. Rather than refusing every impure operation (adopting foreign state, running effects in a shared dirty workspace, operating under a downgraded composition), each becomes a *recorded departure*: a transition with an evidence envelope that accrues an examination **debt** whose footprint is the exact set of logical paths it wrote. An examination discharges by selector-set subtraction; a principal cannot discharge debt its own transitions accrued; and the badge — `intact | examined | unexamined(n)` — is format-fixed so it means the same thing across boundaries. Purity becomes a measured, path-exact property of a line rather than a gate that forces people to lie.

### Counterexamples the sources raise against themselves

- **The operator is not empty.** The spec claims the LLM is identity-free and swappable, then immediately concedes that weights carry dispositions, values and style, so the compiled subject will interfere with the subject latent in the weights. This is filed as an open problem (measuring "operator interference"), not answered. — `context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §2.3, §15
- **A self-managing membrane is unsupervisable.** The intent document's own second premise (every unit manages its own membrane) contradicts its third open question: an agent that can rewrite its interface will eventually stop accepting stop signals. The answer offered is not cellular good faith but an uncatchable external kill — which is precisely a hole in "permission is reachability."
- **"agent = context" hides an identity/value confusion.** Named as 未决 #1 and marked as blocking: until it is settled whether an agent is the context (a value) or a cursor on the DAG (an identity), the data model hangs. The runtime's answer — identity lives in the ref, continuity lives in the lineage, both are conventions (假名) — is the git-shaped option B.
- **Merge may not be a lattice join.** The intent document names this as the claim most in need of falsification and prescribes the two-agent order-independence experiment *before* infrastructure. The engineering line's answer is that it is generally *not*: merge needs domain policies, conflicts are stored as contested values, and "do not assume every branch will merge. Most won't. That is the point."
- **Permanent replay and guaranteed erasure cannot both hold.** Stated as a conservation law rather than hidden; erasure is deliberately placed *outside the algebra*, done by tombstone with the integrity break made explicit.
- **Pure compile versus real retrieval.** The ontology demands all nondeterministic lookup be settled as events first; the engineering line judges this dishonest at scale and replaces it with effectful materialization plus frozen snapshots — keeping purity where it can be kept (compile, render) and putting effects where they belong.
- **Is GTC better? "Not inherently."** The strongest self-critique in the cluster: richer structure is worth its complexity only when relationships, history, conflicts, provenance and per-task views matter — otherwise a simpler vector memory wins, and "without ablation, GTC risks becoming architectural ornament."
- **A generic plugin architecture can be worse than a monolith.** GTC v0.2 says so directly and answers it by using one module protocol with capabilities and typed phase inputs/outputs, plus the ADR-012 rule of two.
- **Guards can be false while green.** The composition finding is the sharpest self-critique of the whole corpus: the artifacts existed *because the plan asked for them* — "That is compliance, not defection" — and a soft "keep gates narrow" rule had already failed, which is why the correction had to become normative rather than advisory.

## 4. Genealogy

**Pre-history (carried in, not in this cluster).** The Chinese spec declares itself 「以《本体 v0.1.1》为内核重述并扩为可实现规格」 — a restatement of an ontology document (本体 v0.1.1) not present in this cluster. Its terminology (拍/相续/算子/名/视野/膜) and the Whitehead/佛家/生生 mapping arrive already formed. This is a real gap for the genealogy: the philosophical derivation happened one document upstream.

**2026-08-07 — two starting points on the same day, from opposite ends.**

*The intent document* (`2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md`) is the purest philosophical statement in the cluster and the least engineered: a first-person "what I want" text that refuses to decide implementation. Its derivation is biological, not computational — from cells, not software engineering — and its scaling thesis is that scalability comes from *not needing coordination*. It asserts six premises as premises: send/receive not call; self-managed membranes; agent existence *is* context continuation; context as DAG not transcript; `context.become(llm)`; permission as reachability. It names its own load-bearing uncertainties honestly (agent-as-value vs agent-as-cursor; the cut policy; the uncatchable stop signal; fixed vs free signal vocabulary; immutability and content addressing; and whether merge is a lattice join) and it prescribes a falsification experiment before infrastructure. It ends: 「`未决 #1` 与 `未决 #2` 定下来之前,不要开始写代码」 ("before #1 and #2 are settled, do not start writing code").

*The implementation plan* (`2026-08-07-Context-Runtime-plan-v0.1.md`) starts from the same proposition ("an agent is not its prompt…") but immediately picks a substrate: Git as the Context Universe, commits as Context Nodes, the whole reachable tree as the Whole Context. It already contains almost the entire later vocabulary — Context Universe / Node / Whole Context / Session / Surface / Interaction, the canonical/operational/derived/ephemeral classification, the transition engine (propose → validate → authorize → reduce → commit), reducers, commit granularity, memory-as-supersession, compaction-as-transition, branch dispositions, merge strategies (identical/union/three-way/crdt/semantic/manual/forbidden), agent fork policies, capabilities-not-credentials, the minimal kernel that must stay "intentionally stupid" to prevent unsafe self-modification, optimistic concurrency with expected-head CAS creating branches instead of hidden races, evaluation-as-branching, schema upgrade as a child node, and twelve engineering laws that later become twenty.

The two documents differ in one revealing way: the intent document treats "context is a DAG" as a premise to be falsified, while the plan treats it as infrastructure to be built.

**2026-08-07 → 2026-08-10 — the engineering line hardens (ADR-010 through ADR-015).** `decisions.md` records the compression. ADR-010 (ratified by Ren 2026-08-07) retires Git for an all-Postgres content-addressed Merkle DAG, on the argument that Postgres is unconditionally in the end state anyway, so any second engine is an addition; SQLite-per-universe becomes the fallback-of-record with a recorded flip condition. ADR-011 (ratified 2026-08-08) is the correction round: redaction is redesigned as an availability ledger after two failed designs; authorization moves after reduce; the hash/value ABI freezes; the turn is recognized as the agent-shaped profile of a general durable publication batch. ADR-014 (2026-08-10) re-pins the reference host's frontend on source-level due diligence. ADR-015 (2026-08-10) makes Stage 8 the complete single-user product and Stage 9 its multi-user trust boundary. ADR-016 and ADR-017 (2026-08-10) specify the multi-agent contract (private/shared namespaces, spawn as one atomic attenuation transition, assimilation as authenticated claim cherry-pick rather than merge, generalized OCC validating *both* cognition and reduction reads) and Temporal durable execution behind a serializable port (Temporal history is operational; Context history is semantic; retry policy belongs to the executor class, not a framework default).

What this line *added* over the 08-07 plan: the Continuation Frame (cognition operates on circumstance, not state alone); realm as a property of the binding rather than the node; effects as immutable facts with executor-class-specific guarantees plus fencing; the availability ledger; replay as the spine from Stage 1; StateId as a runtime-computed hash over the *logical* tree so the store is a real firewall; and the honest downgrade of "the kernel is a privilege boundary" to "in v1 the kernel is a library boundary inside one process."

What it *dropped*: Git (retired to a delayed export format), the declared read/write footprint and commutation calculus (deleted as unenforceable-by-construction; replaced by observed read traces plus OCC at first consumer), turn anchoring on internal refs, and Assistant UI as the pinned thread frontend.

**2026-08 (parallel, sightless) — the GTC branch.** GTC v0.1 develops the second thesis independently: persistent context as evolving structured state; the topology atlas; every assertion a first-class resource; bitemporality; epistemic non-collapse and contradiction preservation; activation as external structural attention; the Context Compiler as a router rather than a retriever; the Update Compiler as a validated write path; compaction as topology formation with trace preservation; generative topology induction; self as one topology among others; and the reduction to State / Projection / Transformation. Two deep-research reports (2026-08-09) then survey the landscape and deliver a sobering verdict: **nearly every primitive has prior art; the combination does not appear to exist as one coherent architecture.** They push GTC to define itself as an immutable, bitemporal, epistemic information substrate + materialized topological views + versioned mappings + context and update compilers — explicitly *not* a graph database — and they name the empty columns in the field as versioned mappings between heterogeneous views and semantic branch/merge over evolving agent knowledge. They also import three hard-earned lessons: writes need stricter semantics than reads (memory poisoning and extraction are demonstrated attacks); history should accrue immutable assertions rather than overwrite; compaction should add abstraction edges and reduce activation, never delete source experience.

GTC v0.2 (2026-08-09) then performs the branch's own self-correction: the atlas leaves the kernel and becomes modules and projections; the kernel shrinks to eight primitives; Proposition splits from Claim; WorldEvent splits from Transformation; modules can never mutate canonical state; and the central abstraction **Σ, the Context System** arrives — with the deepest consequence being the fourth term, Program, added to State/Projection/Transformation, on the ground that "projection is not neutral."

**2026-08-09 — the confluence (ADR-012, ADR-013).** The two lines meet. The finding recorded in ADR-013 is striking: the GTC branch "evolved without sight of Stages 5–7 and converged on this repo's substrate" — a conservative semantic kernel under derived representations, module writes through a validated gate, composition as versioned data, ablation as the arbiter. So GTC is absorbed rather than adopted: it names the *mind layer* over the same engine. ADR-012 sets the entry rules (boundary IR over plugin interfaces, rule of two, composition as digestable data, personality within the authority envelope). ADR-013 carries the plan bump and lands Σ with three binding sites, candidate features into the compilation record, the eval program (switching benchmark, ablation ladder, leave-one-out, ContextUtility, WritePrecision), two dissolutions (proposition identity is a content hash; ContextDiff is derived), and a rejection list including authored epistemic-status fields. The branch's stack recommendations (Python, polyglot stores) are declared void branch incidentals.

**2026-08-13 — the process-ontology spec, arriving *after* the engineering.** This is the genealogical surprise. `context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` — the document that carries `llm is operator, context is existence`, the four nouns and two functions, fork-by-default, the injection predicate, the three-class operation law, and the Whitehead/佛家/生生 appendix — is dated after v0.9 was implemented, and is treated by the engineering line as 「独立的 v0.1 process-ontology proposal」, an *independent abstraction proposal* to be evaluated. It is self-contained by design (§13 is instructions for a reviewing agent, §14 for an implementing agent, with ten open questions R1–R10 and eight acceptance tests including a literal hash vector). Its ambition is stated as design pressure: a TypeScript reference kernel under 1000 lines.

So the philosophical purification did not precede the engineering — it was produced against it, as a re-derivation from first principles, and then submitted for attack.

**2026-08-13 → 2026-08-21 — the absorption (ADR-019 and the architecture plan).** ADR-019 and `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` do the reconciliation with unusual explicitness, keeping a two-column ledger of what to absorb and what must change (claim 85 above). The verdict on adopting the moment kernel literally is a refusal *as a target*, with four specific reasons (operator granularity in the ontology; a forgeable content-level authority integer; pure compile cannot honestly do mutable retrieval; tombstone-plus-CAS cannot preserve earned erasure/fencing/publication/recovery). `MomentProfile` survives as an extension protocol over History.

What replaced the moment kernel is a *smaller and more radical* core than either predecessor: History Core owns no agent, no chat, no prompt, no provider role, no tool loop and no concrete permission model; Context becomes a five-stage pipeline of immutable values with five distinct identities; agents and frameworks alike become non-authoritative scoped decision actors; and the Twenty Laws compress to Seven attackable invariants, with two of the twenty explicitly *corrected* rather than inherited (derived-state disposability, and reachable-is-forever). Phase 9 was signed off by Ren on 2026-08-20 and passed an independent final review 0 P0 / 0 P1 on 2026-08-21.

**2026-08-21 → 2026-08-23 — the plugin turn and the guard turn (ADR-030, ADR-031).** Ren's directive reframes the product: the context system as a plugin anyone in the ecosystem can install, plus a control plane over a fleet of isolated runtimes, plus an elegant conceptual surface. The architecture becomes: Cordis composes runtimes; the product is a separate repository over pinned published upstreams; context-runtime is the truth layer under the fleet. Ren then lifts the freeze for *representational* constraints ("prefer broken changes… I prefer a clean, coherent, longtermist refactor"), producing R0, a one-time reset that re-carves the realm axis, the Ref grammar, the standing/channel split and the idempotency vocabulary while touching no correctness invariant.

The philosophically deepest move of this phase is the **departure/examination ledger**: instead of refusing impurity, record it as a transition with a path-exact debt, and let a declared policy read the ledger. The governing sentence — "nothing is silent, and nothing that used to be allowed becomes refused" — inverts the usual safety reflex. Two prior designs are rejected on the record: the `guaranteed > examined > unexamined` scalar (a total order over two different subjects, and launderable), and a policy-raisable top value (`intact` is a custody fact, not a grade a policy may grant).

Then ADR-031 (2026-08-22), prompted by Ren's observation that "the old guard guards the old architecture," makes the epistemic finding: nine guards rested on one premise that plugin composition falsifies, so they "become **false** — green while the property they name is gone." Nine scanners and three JSON corpora retire (~8,500 lines); freeze becomes publication; version numbers appear only where a second live version exists; and the general rule is extracted — the discriminator is the oracle. The addendum's honesty about origin ("the execution plan asked for them… That is compliance, not defection") is the strongest instance in the corpus of a system critiquing the rules that produced its own overbuild.

**Best formulation, by purpose.** For the *ontology and its philosophical commitments*, `context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` is the strongest single text — it is complete, self-attacking (R1–R10), executable (T1–T8), and it is the only place where the process-ontology mapping is written down. For the *architecture that survived contact*, `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §0–§11 plus the Seven Laws is strongest: it states what the ontology got right, what it got wrong, and why, in a two-column ledger. For the *mind layer*, GTC v0.2 §1–§34, §59, §123, §158–161 is strongest, with §158 ("projection is not neutral") the single most important paragraph. For *epistemic method*, ADR-031 is the strongest: it is the cluster's clearest general lesson.

## 5. What earlier versions had that later ones lost

1. **The cell derivation.** Only the 设计意图书 says the shape came from cells, not software engineering, and states 扩展性不来自更强的协调,来自不需要协调. The later specs inherit the shape and drop the derivation entirely.
2. **The falsification-first discipline.** The intent document's minimal experiment — two agents, one DAG, fork, advance, merge, test order-independence, *before writing any infrastructure* — never appears again. The engineering line answers the question implicitly (merge needs domain policy; most branches will not merge) but never runs the stated experiment.
3. **`context.become(llm)`.** The premise that "the model handling the next round is designated by this round" — an Actor-style `become` where behavior is chosen by the current state — survives only as "the operator is replaceable." The active, self-designating form is lost.
4. **权限即可达性 (permission is reachability).** The intent document's sixth premise — that what an agent can affect is determined by what it can reach in the DAG, not by a separate permission system — is explicitly reversed: effective authority is externalized to an Authority sovereign precisely because a reachable, content-level permission can be forged.
5. **The uncatchable stop signal.** 未决 #3's insight (biology triggers death from outside; Erlang's `kill` cannot be caught) is genuinely load-bearing for any self-managing membrane, and there is no successor to it in the later documents' authority model, which is about grants rather than about signals a unit cannot refuse.
6. **The shared signal vocabulary question.** 未决 #4 (表达本地化,词汇表共享) frames a real design fork — fixed small vocabulary versus free invention — that later documents settle silently by giving each layer typed schemas, without revisiting the argument.
7. **The pure fork-by-default concurrency claim.** In the spec, "the same node receiving different inputs births two children — that sentence is the entire concurrency model." In the engineering line it becomes serial-per-ref with observed-footprint OCC activated at first consumer, plus leases and fences. The simplicity is a real loss, and the reason (durable effects, cross-process workers) is real too.
8. **`revert` does not exist.** The clean algebraic claim ("no positive operation is invertible, so no inverse element is needed") is softened by ADR-030's `runtime.supersede` with an empty patch as a revert.
9. **Structure as strictly single-parent.** The spec's structural tree with a separate reference DAG is relaxed to primary basis + secondary causes, "so as not to break existing merge."
10. **Compile purity in the strong sense.** "All nondeterministic lookup must first be settled as an event" is deliberately replaced by effectful materialization plus frozen snapshots — the honest choice, but a weaker invariant.
11. **The sub-1000-LOC kernel.** The spec's stated design pressure has no successor; the implemented engine is a multi-package monorepo with nineteen packages published in the last phase.
12. **The Whitehead / Buddhist / 生生 mapping.** It appears once, in the spec's appendix, and never again. No engineering document repeats 护未, 假名, 相续 or 因生因之一拍.
13. **Git as substrate.** ADR-010 retires it. What is genuinely lost with it is the free structural sharing of an enormous state tree across forks ("forking an enormous state is consequently almost free") and the ready-made ecosystem of tools; the Merkle-in-Postgres design reconstructs the properties but not the ecosystem.
14. **The topology atlas as canonical.** GTC v0.1's mappings Φ between views, held as first-class versioned knowledge, were called the most conspicuous empty column in the field by the research reports. In v0.2 they become optional mapping modules; in the confluence they become catalog items awaiting a first consumer. The strongest differentiator identified by the research became the most deferred.
15. **Activation as a persistent primitive.** GTC v0.1 made activation part of the persistent state `W_t`; v0.2's migration map moves it "from persistent primitive to context-run artifact by default."
16. **Self topology.** v0.1 gave the self its own topology with special access rules; v0.2 postpones it to an optional module, and later documents reduce it to `agents/<id>/psyche/**` policy data.
17. **The sheaf/categorical direction.** The research reports identify Knowledge Sheaves and functorial data migration as the deepest formal precedents for cross-view consistency (with the beautiful reframing: contradiction is not two unequal values but *no compatible global assignment under the relevant scope*). v0.2 lists sheaf consistency and category-theoretic mappings under non-goals and "what should be postponed."
18. **The Twenty Laws as a memorized checklist.** Compressed to Seven; the disposition table preserves each claim's new home, but the checklist redundancy that "a review checklist earns" is deliberately given up at the constitutional layer.
19. **Whole-import-graph answerability.** ADR-031 names its own cost plainly: "we lose the ability to answer *'what is the whole import graph, as of this commit?'* from a checked artifact… the loss is a loss."
20. **The `renderer.test.ts` cross-renderer comparison.** Recorded in ADR-030 as evidence deleted with the legacy renderer, with the additional honesty that "no gate will say so."

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

### Raised in the corpus

- **R1–R10, the spec's own open list.** Whether RFC 8785 plus a float ban really seals cross-implementation addressing ambiguity (Unicode normalization?); whether a structural tree plus a reference DAG suffices or some scenario genuinely needs structural multi-parenthood; whether the default lattice `human(2) < policy(3)` should be inverted, and under what governance policy may outrank its author; the storage cost of persisting every Window; the latency cost of forcing retrieval to become an event before compile; children-index maintenance at 10⁸ nodes; late-arriving tombstone convergence; an information-loss audit of the three dialect mappings; arrival-order conventions for parallel tool echoes; and the etiquette of multiple writers on a shared name. — `context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` §13, §15
- **Operator interference.** The spec's own unresolved item: the compiled subject interferes with the subject latent in the weights, and no measure exists for it.
- **The unsupervisable membrane.** A self-managing membrane can eventually refuse its stop signal; who may send an unrefusable signal, and how that right is granted and revoked, is open. — `设计意图书` #3
- **Is merge a lattice join?** Named as the claim most in need of falsification; never experimentally answered in this cluster.
- **agent-as-value vs agent-as-cursor.** Marked as blocking everything downstream; answered by convention (identity in the ref) rather than by argument.
- **The kernel is not yet a privilege boundary.** Stated plainly: in v1 the kernel is an API boundary inside one process; extraction is later hardening. Worker threads are explicitly disqualified as privilege boundaries; only egress-contained isolation may claim enforced no-bypass against hostile code.
- **Budget attenuation by refusal.** A spawned child selecting a budgeted model bundle fails closed until a shared durable reservation allocator exists — an honest gap, not a solution. — `016-stage-10-multi-agent.md`
- **Per-member attribution cannot reach canon.** Recorded as a live limitation: no upstream seam hands a host plugin the acting principal, so attribution is at project granularity with a correlation trail at the router. — `030-...md` OPEN
- **Erasure vs replay.** Stated as a conservation law: you cannot simultaneously guarantee permanent exact replay and guaranteed physical erasure.
- **Redaction blast radius.** Availability epochs are per-universe, so one redaction invalidates every in-flight compilation in that universe — "a rare event bought with deliberately coarse, simple invalidation."
- **GTC is not inherently better**, and without ablation "risks becoming architectural ornament"; a generic plugin architecture "can easily become worse than a monolith"; and expressiveness creates ontology proliferation, view/mapping explosion, expensive updates, ambiguous identity resolution, cyclic derivations and retrieval latency.
- **Self-reinforcing hallucination and memory poisoning.** Named across the GTC research: a generated inference can enter memory, be retrieved as "evidence," and reinforce itself; persistent memory extends prompt-injection effects from one request into future sessions; extraction attacks on agent memory are demonstrated.
- **Guards can be false while green.** The corpus's own sharpest methodological critique, together with its uncomfortable origin ("the execution plan asked for them") and its named residual cost (no answerable whole-import-graph).
- **Deliberately not done, each recorded.** The final execution record lists what was left undone rather than claiming completion: the upstream clock contribution, per-member attribution, the loopback-method filter, a tool acting *inside* a world, and a durable half of a bracketed effect that can still strand its intent.

### New (my reading)

- **The philosophical text arrived last, and was then partially refused.** The strongest ontological statement in the cluster is dated after the implementation it purports to ground, and the engineering line's response is a two-column ledger of absorption and amendment. This is a genuine intellectual event that the corpus does not name: the ontology is not the origin of the architecture but a *later purification submitted for attack* — which is admirable method, but means the ontology's authority in the corpus is unclear. Which document is the theory?
- **"Context is existence" and "History does not claim world truth" are in tension.** If context *is* existence, then a fold over a lineage is an ontology; but the History Core explicitly says 「History 是 admitted runtime record，不宣称外部世界的绝对真理」. The runtime's honesty about epistemic modesty quietly demotes the existence claim to a claim about a *recorded* existence. Nothing in the cluster reconciles these registers.
- **The 生生 mapping is asserted, not argued.** The appendix table is beautiful and one page long. Nowhere does the corpus argue *why* an actual-occasion ontology should govern an LLM runtime, or what would count as evidence against the mapping. Given Ren's stated style (mechanism first, no aphorism-first writing), this is the one place in the cluster that reads as aphorism-first.
- **Fork-by-default vs serial-per-ref is a philosophical retreat, not only an engineering one.** The claim "that one sentence is the entire concurrency model" is the ontology's most elegant result. Its replacement — serial per ref, leases, fences, OCC at first consumer — is justified entirely by durable *effects*. That is: the world, not the model, is what forces coordination. The corpus never draws this conclusion explicitly, and it is a good one: pure generation needs no coordination; contact with the world does.
- **`权限即可达性` was falsified by the corpus itself.** The intent document's most elegant premise (permission is reachability) is precisely what ADR-019 rejects — a content-level permission can be forged. This is the cluster's cleanest example of a premise being destroyed by its own construction, and it deserves to be stated as a finding rather than left as a silent supersession.
- **Departure/standing may be the most exportable idea here, and it is buried.** "Relax refusals into recorded departures read by a declared policy; nothing is silent, and nothing previously allowed becomes refused" is a general governance pattern — for AI systems, for institutions, for the corpus's own political theories — and it currently lives inside an ADR about a plugin framework.
- **Σ has no completed instance.** The switching benchmark is the direct test of the theory's central claim (context-system plurality changes effective cognition), and the sources record only that Σ v0 exists as a digested policy value with mechanics deferred to "the second real Σ." So the mind layer's headline thesis is, as of these sources, unmeasured.
- **The atlas's differentiator is the most deferred piece.** Both research reports identify versioned mappings between heterogeneous views as the single conspicuous empty column in the field; v0.2 makes them optional modules and lists sheaf/categorical work under postponed. If the theory has an unexploited edge, this is it.
- **"Everything is a plugin" sits uneasily with "trust artifacts, not intelligence."** The construction constitution says do not trust intelligence by default and prefer bounded capabilities; the plugin adoption concedes that `ctx.plugin()` is reachable by every mounted plugin and there is no seal, so "no dynamic mounts" is a property of what we mount, not a guarantee. The attestation observer is an honest mitigation, but the tension is structural, not incidental.
- **There is no account of the human.** Sessions, surfaces and approvals exist; a theory of what a person *is* to this runtime does not. Given the corpus's relational-self commitments, the absence is conspicuous: the human appears as a principal with authority level 2 and as a source of approvals bound to a projection hash.

## 9. Publication readiness

**Publishable or teachable now.**

- `context-runtime-specs/context-runtime-spec-v0.1.md` is essentially a finished paper already. It is self-contained, normative (MUST/SHOULD/MAY in Chinese), carries eight acceptance tests including a literal SHA-256 vector, ten named open problems, a position section against Chat Completions / Responses / Harmony / MemGPT, and a philosophical appendix. Translated to English and paired with a reference kernel, it is a publishable systems-and-philosophy piece: *Context as Existence: a process-ontology runtime for language models*.
- `context-runtime-specs/2026-08-07-设计意图书-v0.1.md` is publishable almost unchanged as a short essay on designing from cells rather than from software engineering, and as a model of intellectual honesty (six asserted premises, six named undecideds, one falsification experiment, and a refusal to write code before two of them are settled).
- `gtc/v0.2.md` §158–§161 plus §27 (Σ) is a complete, teachable argument on its own: *the context program is part of cognition*. It needs no runtime to be stated.
- `031-composition-is-the-guard.md` is publishable as a short methodological essay for anyone building verified systems: how a whole class of static guards silently becomes false, why the discriminator is the oracle, and why "the plan asked for them" makes the correction a plan-level change rather than a code-level one.
- `context-runtime-specs/plan.md` §35–§36 (the Twenty Laws and the near-deductive closing paragraph) and `history-kernel-mediated-runtime.md` §9 (the Seven Laws with attack tests) are teachable side by side as a case study in law compression: what twenty memorized rules become when each must be attackable.

**Missing.**

- The upstream ontology document 《本体 v0.1.1》 is not in this cluster, so the derivation from 生生/缘起 to 拍/相续 is a gap.
- No measured result anywhere: no switching-benchmark number, no ablation ladder result, no ContextUtility or WritePrecision figure beyond a v1 landing note, no merge-order-independence experiment. Every empirical claim in the cluster is a design intention.
- No English rendering of the Chinese spec, and no reference kernel artifact under the stated 1000-LOC target.
- No account of the human participant commensurate with the corpus's relational-self commitments.
- No treatment of what a *good* Σ is. The machinery for plural context systems is specified in detail; the normative question — which ways of seeing an intelligence should be given, and who decides — is never asked, though it is the natural bridge to `alignment-selection-on-cognition` and `zhishan-intelligence-must-be-good`.

**The one piece of writing that would most advance this.** A 6,000–9,000 word English essay titled something like **"Context as Existence: what an LLM runtime looks like when you take process ontology seriously"**, structured as: (1) the three defects of chat-shaped APIs, stated concretely — the welded subject, the fictional user role, the unaddressable history; (2) the inversion — moment, lineage, operator, ref, and `context = fold(lineage)` — with fork-by-default derived rather than asserted; (3) schema-on-read and the two-point permission lattice, with injection given its precise definition; (4) the honest ledger of what breaks when you build it — operator granularity, forgeable content-level authority, pure compile versus real retrieval, and why durable *effects* (not cognition) are what force coordination back into the system; (5) the second inversion, GTC and Σ, ending on "projection is not neutral" and the departure/examination ledger as the general governance pattern; and (6) the 生生 mapping stated last, as the reading that makes the whole thing coherent rather than as the premise it was smuggled in as. This single essay would unify the cluster's two strongest documents, resolve the genealogical oddity (that the philosophy arrived after the engineering) by making it the essay's own argument, and give the corpus its clearest statement of what "context is existence" actually commits one to.
