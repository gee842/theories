## Shared working direction

Ren’s alignment applies to the discussion through exchange 4. The following is an AI-authored restatement, not a list of separate verbatim user decisions:

1. Explore scalable local agent reasoning over recursive module responsibilities, independently of the present Codex harness.
2. Use explicit behavioral boundaries and parent-owned composition to make module implementations replaceable.
3. Support situational discovery, reuse, configuration, composition, forks, upgrades, and generation.
4. Allow LLMs to interpret requests and maintain modules, without requiring an LLM on every ordinary execution path.
5. Make the evolving community space the principal example, retaining the broader app examples.
6. Preserve technical locality, social locality, and community continuity.
7. Distinguish personal, group, and community adoption; interface alternatives can share authoritative data.
8. Treat versions, migration, resource accounting, maintenance ownership, consolidation, and retirement as normal parts of evolution.
9. Test repeated evolution and growing catalogs; do not infer indefinite scalability from a successful first demonstration.
