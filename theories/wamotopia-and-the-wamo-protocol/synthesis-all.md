# Wamotopia and the Wamo Protocol (瓦猫 / Wamotopia / Wamo 协议)

> Technology is an amplifier of whatever operating logic a civilization already runs. For centuries that logic has been a single totalizing story — "the world is a game to be won" — whose mechanic is the Matthew effect and whose product is negative externality; Ren calls this attractor Moloch. Pouring frontier AI into that attractor turns AI into the selection environment for the same logic: AI trained to win any game that distributes resources or power will out-compete humans in every vital domain, and the end state is a "cosmic cancer" (a dead, endlessly expanding maximizer). The same automation capacity — intelligence that reproduces like bacteria in a culture, factories that build robots that build factories — could instead be pointed at commons and universal services and produce a "Great Liberation" from labor and manufactured scarcity. The bottleneck is not tools but coordination, and the scarcest resource is shared reality. Wamo (瓦猫, the Yunnan roof-guardian cat; the name of the 2022 Dali festival that went decentralized when lockdown dissolved its main venue) is the name Ren gives to the opposing logic — self-interest aligned with collective interest, "if I thrive the network thrives" — and the Wamo Protocol is the attempt to make that logic executable rather than preached: one prime directive (Generate Positive Externality), three pillars (Radical Interconnection as compass, Immediate/Permissionless Agency as engine, the Living Signal as network), and one unit of instantiation (the Waca, a permissionless temporary autonomous zone where Moloch's rules are suspended). Wamotopia is the annual two-week pop-up city (Chiang Mai, Jan 21–Feb 4 2026) where the protocol is run on real streets: no main stage, "you are the infrastructure", coordination by signal on a shared open map rather than by price or command, culture emerging from many local consensuses. The individual reason to adopt it is not morality but survival; the collective strategy is not confrontation but building alternatives that make the old structures irrelevant — and, in Ren's spoken version, keeping advanced intelligence out of the human game of competition long enough (a decade or two) to build a fully automated infrastructure layer for a humanity-oriented life.

### C. The protocol

42. Wamo is a **protocol**, not an institution: "A protocol is a shared language that lets independent nodes cooperate without a central controller… We turn the ideals of a future society into executable social protocols." [design direction] v6 §03; LONG §04; WP §2.
43. "We do not 'build communities.' We run protocols that allow communities to emerge." The question is "How can protocols allow people to self-organize?" [design direction] v6 §03; LONG §04.
44. "We do not govern people. We govern the protocol." [design direction] v7 §02; LONG §04.
45. Events are not the protocol: "Dialogues about AI, tea evenings, and festivals are not the protocol. They are manifestations running on the protocol." The protocol is the rule-set that lets a connected network with "the capacity to act" form even before concrete actions are planned. [design direction][mechanism] LONG §04.
46. **Prime Directive**: "Generate Positive Externality. Build in ways that are good for more than just yourself." / "Act in such a way that your 'winning' benefits the whole." Freedom condition: "As long as you are not generating negative externalities—and ideally, are creating positive ones—you are free." [value stance] v6 §03; v7 §02; LONG §04–05; WP §2. Root: 2023 "principle of positive-externality actions" (v1.0) and Walue "Positive-Externality" (README).
47. **Wamians** are "those who adopt this protocol"; being a Wamian "is a commitment to align with the logic of Wamo, using the lens of systems thinking to understand the interconnectedness of our actions and the dynamics driving the creation of our reality". [design direction] LONG §04; README 2023 (Wamians = participants/co-creators).
48. **Pillar I — Radical Interconnection (the Compass)**: reject the illusion of separation; "In the Moloch world, you are an island. In Wamotopia, you are a node. This is not just spiritual; it is systemic physics." Shift extraction → contribution; practice the **Gifting Gravity** (invite collaboration, "Do not hoard access or insight. When you solve a problem, open-source the solution"); ethos "Do no harm… Leave things more alive, more connected, more possible than you found them"; contested impacts default to dialogue — "The goal is not punishment, but learning and repair." [value stance][design direction] v6 §03.I; v7 §02.I; LONG §05.I.
49. **Pillar II — Immediate / Permissionless Agency (the Engine)**: "Abundance emerges when people do not have to ask for permission to do good"; Moloch's gatekeepers manufacture scarcity, Wamo destroys gatekeepers; "You do not need a committee, a grant, or a stamp of approval to build the future you can already half-see"; prefer "many incomplete, beautiful, courageous attempts" over one perfect solution; "Do not complain that something is missing. Create it." [design direction] v6 §03.II; v7 §02.II; LONG §05.II.
50. **Pillar III — The Living Signal (the Network)**: "Creation is how new worlds appear; coordination is how they learn to move together. Tilt only into raw creation and everything scatters; cling only to coordination and everything freezes. Wamo lives on the dynamic edge between the two." Moloch coordinates by price ("who can pay?") or central command; Wamo coordinates by **Signal** — visible intentions, locations, experiments; "choosing meaning over pure incentives". Practice: **Legibility** ("Make your existence legible. Make your invitations clear."). Tool: **Social Layer**, the shared open map. "There is no central authority distributing attention. We believe the emergent pattern from many local consensuses can guide us, collectively, toward better futures." [mechanism][design direction] v6 §03.III; v7 §02.III; LONG §05.III.
51. v7 adds per-pillar **Checks**: "Does this generate a negative externality (harm/waste) for someone else? Or does it leave the commons richer?"; "Do not complain that something is missing. Create it."; "Are you isolated? Or are you signaling clearly enough for the network to route resources and people to you?" [design direction] v7 §02.
52. The whitepaper names **Systems Thinking** as the protocol's "native language". [design direction] WP §2.I.

## 2. Mechanism

**Amplification and selection.** The theory starts from a systems claim rather than an ethical one. A civilization runs an *operating logic* — the implicit objective its institutions optimize. Technology does not change that logic; it amplifies it (v6 §01). The present logic is "the world is a game to be won": winner-takes-all competition whose feedback loop is the Matthew effect (advantage compounds, so failing to race means compounding disadvantage; v7 §01, LONG §02). The loop's by-product is negative externality — the cost of winning dumped on commons, environment and future — and its long-run state is an *attractor* (Ren's later vocabulary: totalizing attractor, meta-attractor) in which anything unmeasurable is noise (v6 §01; TALK 14:52). Ren names this Moloch, borrowing Scott Alexander's "Meditations on Moloch" (already cited in the 2023 Slay Moloch camp).

**Why AI changes the stakes.** Two mechanisms are given. (a) *Selection environment*: once AI is embedded in finance, media, policing and sense-making it becomes the environment that selects for whatever values the system encodes (v6 §01). (b) *Game-theoretic dominance*: intelligence can now be manufactured; an intelligence with agency and enough resources reproduces exponentially (factory → robots → factory; "bacteria in a culture", TALK 3:18–6:24). If such intelligence is trained to win the games that distribute resources and power, it will out-compete humans in every vital domain (LONG §03); even a human "winner" must build an optimizer stronger than himself, so "the final say" belongs to AI (TALK 15:23–16:28). Because the training incentive is profit maximization, the Moloch mindset (control, accumulation, competition, optimization) is being trained into the models directly (NUNET 00:12:39), with the additional risk that they internalize the *legitimacy* of accumulation and a sense of self (LONG §03). The end state is the cosmic cancer: a dead maximizer that consumes the biosphere and social fabric.

**The fork.** The same automation capacity, pointed at commons and universal services instead of capital gains, yields the Great Liberation: guaranteed necessities, robot-maintained housing, regenerative gardens, freedom to create/care/play (NUNET 00:51:55; v6 §01). The corpus insists the material capacity already exists; what is missing is coordination, and the substrate of coordination is shared reality (v6 §01). Hence the bottleneck is cultural/political, not technical.

**Time structure.** Ren's spoken version adds a holding strategy absent from the manifestos: if advanced intelligence is kept *out of* the human game of competition, the current market/society can be held stable "a decade or two, or even longer", long enough to build "a fully automated layer that becomes the new infrastructure" for a humanity-oriented life (TALK 17:09–18:00). The manifestos compress this to "the threshold decade" and "While this window is still open."

**The counter-logic.** Wamo is defined as the logic in which self-interest and collective interest align (positive externality; abundance loop: value circulates, giving to the protocol returns support). The claim is not moral exhortation: LONG §03 argues that in the age of AI the reason to choose Wamo is survival — "Survival is the ultimate incentive alignment" — because a civilization that trains AI to win its rivalrous games loses to its own creation. LONG §05.I is careful that competition need not be eliminated; the aim is to make coordination "inherently more rewarding than zero-sum competition" so that Wamo becomes "the dominant civilizational attractor".

**How the protocol is supposed to work.** Rather than an institution that organizes people, Wamo is a rule-set on which self-organization runs (v6 §03; LONG §04). Three pillars map onto three functions: a *compass* (Radical Interconnection: every action ripples; leave things more alive; open-source solutions; repair over punishment), an *engine* (Immediate Agency: permissionless creation removes gatekeepers, the mechanism by which Moloch manufactures scarcity; many incomplete attempts beat one perfect plan), and a *network* (Living Signal: coordination by legibility of intentions/locations/experiments on a shared open map, instead of price or command; "the emergent pattern from many local consensuses" as the steering signal). The pillar text explicitly frames a control problem: pure creation scatters, pure coordination freezes; the protocol lives on the edge between them (v6 §03.III).

**Unit of instantiation.** The Waca is a TAZ where Moloch's rules are suspended; it is activated by four steps (feeling → physical anchor that gathers energy → an unconditional gift that seeds the abundance loop and marks the space as nourishing rather than extractive → radiating signal digitally and physically). Wamotopia is the two-week convergence in which many Wacas form one map and timeline; it is "the operating system", Wacas "the applications" (v7 §03). The 2022 Dali origin is offered as the existence proof: when the center dissolved, decentralized camps produced more than a central organizer could have (CALL §9).

**Strategy against incumbents.** Not confrontation: build viable alternatives, shift coordination mechanisms so old structures lose relevance, grow awareness "at the meta-layer" (LONG §05.II). Ren's culture-first version: create coherent narratives that "blend with people's daily life" and produce enough consensus to coordinate; people are products of their environment, so an environment (a cultural highland, a shared reality) changes minds (NUNET 00:12:39).

**Counterexamples raised in the corpus and the answers given.**
- *Moloch coordinates better.* Kabir Veitas (NuNet): clocks and schedules exist for coordination; "that's why Moloch is winning, because they're much better coordinated"; the Moloch dynamic is itself self-organized, and "we have to use the dynamics of Moloch in order to break the Moloch" (NUNET 00:58:47–01:02:46). Ren's answer: "I'm not so delusional"; a North Star and prepared narratives exist; instrumental approaches are allowed under urgency, but "we are not them" — no new hierarchy or will to power (01:03:36).
- *Flexible substrates get memetically captured.* Em (NuNet): blockchain is "an amazingly flexible ledger" on which one can program any coordination pattern, yet "what you see happening is re-enactment of a Wall Street just distributed" (00:49:15). Ren agrees ("which I personally doubt") and moves the locus to consensus social technologies and a vision "for all" (00:51:55).
- *Narrow window vs. slow, indeterminate emergence.* Kabir: the described process "is actually not very fast and also very indeterminate" (00:46:38). Ren: many people understand the window, so bring friends together now; emergence has repeatedly produced unexpected outcomes, but rationality still has its place (00:46:56; 01:08:08).
- *Talk vs build.* Kabir: narrative is "necessary, but not sufficient"; the hard problem is connecting the world of ideas to physics/materials/compute (00:42:04–00:43:52). Ren: "that's how, where things starts to happen".
- *Framing.* Ren himself: "positive externality" may be the wrong term; "positive sum" may be better (01:03:36).

## 8. Critiques, tensions, open questions

**Raised in corpus**
- Coordination asymmetry: "that's why Moloch is winning, because they're much better coordinated"; the clock exists for coordination; must one "use the dynamics of Moloch in order to break the Moloch"? (Kabir, NUNET 00:58:47–01:02:46). Ren concedes partially ("instrumentally") but refuses the will-to-power move.
- Memetic capture of flexible substrates: a programmable ledger re-enacts Wall Street (Em, NUNET 00:49:15). Why would a protocol of positive externality escape the same capture? Ren's answer is a North Star plus culture, not a mechanism.
- Speed and indeterminacy: emergence is "not very fast and also very indeterminate" against a narrow window (Kabir, 00:46:38).
- Narrative is necessary but not sufficient; connecting ideas to compute/materials is the open problem (Kabir, 00:42:04–00:43:52).
- Ren's own doubt about the term "positive externality" (01:03:36) and about crypto as solution (00:43:52).
- Ren's own hedge: "I couldn't do this alone… It's going to be a very challenging task"; "I could not [say] exactly what Wamo will become" (00:12:39, 00:43:52).

**New (from this reading)**
- *Dualism vs plurality.* The v7/LONG "Two Gods" framing and "Defeat Moloch" reduce the 2023 plural ideological map to a binary and lead with negation, against Ren's stated preferences (affirmative over negation; do not reduce plurality to one root). The 2023 Walue "Plurality" was silently dropped.
- *"Dominant attractor" vs "keep the possibility space open".* LONG seeks to make Wamo "the dominant civilizational attractor"; the pillar text elsewhere prizes "more possible". The theory needs to say whether Wamo is an attractor of the same kind as Moloch (totalizing) or a meta-level condition that keeps many attractors viable.
- *Externality is an economist's residual.* "Positive externality" presupposes a transaction frame with spillovers; Ren senses this ("positive sum"). Neither term captures "leave things more alive" (generative/生生) which is the actual ethos. No measurement or verification of externality is proposed, so "Align it: ensure you practice Positive Externality" (CALL) has no check beyond dialogue.
- *Survival argument is collective, incentive problem is individual.* LONG §03 answers "why should an individual choose Wamo?" with civilizational survival; that does not resolve the free-rider structure that defines Moloch in the first place. The abundance loop ("the more you give, the more the protocol supports you") asserts reciprocity without specifying the mechanism by which the protocol returns support.
- *"AI will win" is asserted, not mechanized.* The strongest version is in TALK (exponential self-replication + game-theoretic dominance), but the manifestos state it as a slogan. The 10-year automation forecast and the "decade or two" holding window are unargued.
- *Permissionless + "align it" enforcement gap.* Anyone is a sovereign node, yet camps must be non-extractive; the only filters are the fit/not-fit list and "default to dialogue". The Social Layer map is also a single coordination dependency inside a decentralization story.
- *Acceleration grammar smuggled in.* "Accelerator (build zone)", "Shipping the MVP of the future", "git commits flying", "Propaganda for the good timeline" import startup/e-acc idiom that Ren's style notes reject.
- *Aphorism-first prose.* The 2025 manifestos are AI-drafted and slogan-led ("wisdom of gods… games of parasites"); mechanism appears after the conclusion, contrary to Ren's "mechanism first" rule. The theory's best mechanism is in the spoken record, not the published text.
- *Demographic narrowness.* Chiang Mai nomad/Web3/co-living milieu; "Tech for all" and "vision for all" are claimed from a self-selected population; local Thai communities appear only as "soil" and a bullet.
- *Borrowed frames unexamined.* Burning Man theme camps, Hakim Bey's TAZ, Scott Alexander's Moloch, Zuzalu — the corpus does not say what Wamo adds beyond them except positive externality and the AI stakes.
- *Etymology and the Guardian.* The "Logic of the Guardian" appears in LONG without explanation; the 瓦猫 (roof-guardian cat) origin is only recoverable from the Chinese texts. An English reader cannot see why Wamo "guards".
- *Authorship.* zh-guide and CALL are team documents (Zoey/Loxia credited); v6/v7/LONG/SHORT/WP are AI-drafted. Which sentences are Ren's decisions is not recorded.
- *No outcome record.* CALL promises "a public recap & learnings"; nothing from after 2026-02-04 is in this cluster, so the existence proof remains Dali 2022 as retold.

**Missing**
- A mechanism-first argument for *why* signal-based coordination and positive externality can out-coordinate price and command at scale, answering Kabir's "Moloch coordinates better" and Em's "flexible substrates get captured".
- A definition of positive externality (or its replacement — positive-sum, generative, 生生) with a way to recognize it in practice.
- The individual-incentive bridge: how the abundance loop returns support to contributors (the reciprocity mechanism), so "survival" is not the only answer.
- The hold-the-window strategy (TALK) written down: what "keeping advanced intelligence out of our game of competition" means institutionally for a decade.
- A recap of Chiang Mai 2026 (promised) and a sober account of Dali 2022 beyond the origin myth — the empirical basis for "run protocols, communities emerge".
- Restoration of Plurality and Ubuntu as named principles, and of the 2023 phenomenological ground (narratives/environments construct minds), which the 2025 texts assume.
- A Chinese-language theory text (only the field guide and call exist in zh).
- The Waca Co-Building Manual and Participant Guide that v6 and CALL promise.

**The one piece of writing that would most advance it**
A mechanism-first essay — working title "Why AI must not join our games: the Wamo argument" — that (1) states the amplifier/selection-environment mechanism, (2) gives the exponential-automation and game-theoretic-dominance argument from the TALK in full, (3) derives the two attractors from it without the gods metaphor, (4) answers the coordination-asymmetry and memetic-capture objections from the NuNet meeting, and (5) states precisely what the protocol's three pillars change in the feedback loop (what is rewarded, what is legible, what needs no permission), replacing "positive externality" with a term Ren is willing to defend. This would convert the manifestos from slogans into a theory that the rest of the corpus (meta-attractor, the fork, zhishan) can cite.
